## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has informed trading members about the admission of equity shares of Fusion CX Limited for trading on the Capital Market Segment under the "Permitted to Trade" category effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Fusion CX Limited
- **Symbol:** FUSIONCX
- **Series:** EQ
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **ISIN:** INE0N2201019
- **Effective Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Category:** Permitted to Trade

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

The company admitted for dealing under the "Permitted to Trade" category does not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore does not provide full disclosures.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members of the exchange to trade in equity shares of Fusion CX Limited under the permitted category.
