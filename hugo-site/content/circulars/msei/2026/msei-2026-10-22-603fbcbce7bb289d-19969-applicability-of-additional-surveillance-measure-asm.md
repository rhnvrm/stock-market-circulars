## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Framework Stage I to specific securities, effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I: 21ST Century Management Services Limited, Turtlemint Fintech Solutions Limited, and Srestha Finvest Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective October 27, 2026, on all open positions as on October 26, 2026, and new positions created from October 27, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely a market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the entities.

## Regulatory Changes

Shortlisting of securities under Long-Term ASM Stage I requiring 100% margins.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must collect 100% margins on open positions as of October 26, 2026, and all new positions created from October 27, 2026, onwards for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of ASM Inclusion: October 23, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date for Margin: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhanced margin requirements (100%) will increase capital requirements for traders and participants taking positions in the shortlisted securities.
