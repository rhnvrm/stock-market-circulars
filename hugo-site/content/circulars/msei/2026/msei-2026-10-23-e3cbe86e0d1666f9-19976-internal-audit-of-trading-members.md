## Summary

MSEI has issued procedural manuals detailing the step-by-step submission process for Internal Audit Reports (IAR) by trading members and internal auditors via the inspection portal.

## Key Points

- Trading members must register internal auditors through the Auditor Master section on the MSEI inspection portal (https://inspection-os.msei.in/MemberLogin.aspx).
- Auditors receive login credentials via email, update passwords, and input audit findings chapter-wise.
- Members must submit an undertaking regarding penalties for short collection or non-collection of client margins in accordance with circular MSE/INSP/11982/2022.
- Members review audit data, download the PDF report, affix a digital signature, and upload the signed document to complete the sign-off.
- Submission status updates to "Submitted to Exchange" upon successful member sign-off.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standardized digital workflow for registering auditors, entering audit data, and submitting digitally signed internal audit reports to the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and auditors must strictly adhere to the portal workflows for audit entry, comment acceptance, and digital sign-off.
- Members are required to submit required undertakings and ensure timely upload of signed audit reports.

## Important Dates

- Applicable for current and upcoming internal audit reporting cycles.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational transparency and efficiency in compliance reporting and document verification between trading members, auditors, and the exchange.
