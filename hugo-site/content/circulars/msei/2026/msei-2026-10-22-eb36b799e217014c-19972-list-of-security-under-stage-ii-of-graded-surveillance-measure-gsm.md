## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced that securities mentioned in the annexure will be moved to Stage II of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) with effect from October 23, 2026. Trading in GLOBUS CONSTRUCTORS & DEVELOPERS LTD will be restricted to the Trade for Trade segment with a 5% price band and additional deposits.

## Key Points

- Security affected: GLOBUS CONSTRUCTORS & DEVELOPERS LTD (Symbol: GLOBUSCON, ISIN: INE064L01015).
- Moved to Stage II of Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM).
- Effective date: October 23, 2026.
- Trading restricted to Trade for Trade segment (series: BE).
- Price band restricted to 5% or lower.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value collected from buying trading members.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities placed under GSM Stage II face stringent surveillance measures as part of market integrity and investor protection frameworks.
- Trading mechanism shifted to Trade for Trade, requiring physical delivery without netting off intraday positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adequate precautions and adequate capital/collateral allocation to account for the 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD).
- Clients trading in GLOBUSCON must be aware of the 5% price band and delivery-based settlement requirements.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026.
- Effective Date of GSM Stage II: October 23, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading volumes for GLOBUSCON are likely to moderate due to the Trade for Trade restriction and 50% ASD requirement.
- Buying members face increased capital blocking due to the 50% ASD retained till further notice.
