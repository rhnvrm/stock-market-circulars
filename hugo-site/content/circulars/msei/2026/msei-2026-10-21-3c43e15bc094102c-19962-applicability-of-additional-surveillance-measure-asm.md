## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular detailing the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Hiliks Technologies Limited has been shortlisted under the Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 26, 2026 on all open positions as on October 23, 2026 and new positions created from October 26, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisted security: Hiliks Technologies Limited (ISIN: INE966Q01010, Symbol: HILIKS) under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I.
- Measures are in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures and do not constitute an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure framework provisions.
- Imposition of 100% margin on open and new positions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin collection on all open positions as of October 23, 2026, and new positions created from October 26, 2026 onwards for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Action: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Higher margin requirements (100%) will increase capital requirements for market participants trading in the shortlisted security.
