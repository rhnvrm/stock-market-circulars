## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has notified the suspension of trading in equity shares of Regency World Consulting Limited (RWCL) pursuant to an NCLT order dated July 27, 2026, regarding corporate restructuring.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Regency World Consulting Limited
- **Symbol:** RWCL
- **ISIN:** INE419C01010
- **Circular Number:** MSE/LIST/19959/2026
- **Reason:** Suspension of trading on account of corporate restructuring pursuant to NCLT Order dated July 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the Metropolitan Stock Exchange (Capital Market Segment) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the suspension and ensure no trading takes place in the security from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026 (with effect from closing hours of trading on October 22, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in equity shares of Regency World Consulting Limited (RWCL) on the MSEI capital market segment is suspended due to corporate restructuring.
