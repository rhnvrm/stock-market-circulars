## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) issued circular no. 19952 regarding the record date for determining eligible shareholders under the Scheme of Amalgamation between Regency World Consulting Limited (Transferor Company) and Swen Holdings Limited (Transferee Company), following the NCLT New Delhi Bench order dated July 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Transferor Company: Regency World Consulting Limited (CIN: L74210DL1985PLC021863).
- Transferee Company / Successful Resolution Applicant: M/S Swen Holdings Limited.
- NCLT Approval: Order pronounced on July 27, 2026, by NCLT New Delhi Bench, Court-III in I.A. (IBC) (Plan) 26(ND) 2026 in IB-109(ND)/2025.
- CoC Approval: Approved with 99.95% voting share in the 6th CoC meeting held on March 2, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Approval of the resolution plan and scheme of amalgamation under Section 30(6) read with Regulation 39 of IBBI (CIRP Regulations), 2016 and Rule 11 of NCLT Rules, 2016.

## Compliance Requirements

Eligible shareholders of the transferor company are identified as per the specified record date under the approved amalgamation scheme and NCLT order.

## Important Dates

- CoC Meeting Date: March 2, 2026
- NCLT Order Date: July 27, 2026
- Circular Date: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates corporate restructuring and amalgamation under the corporate insolvency resolution process (CIRP).
