## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced a change in trading symbols for two exclusively listed companies, Capital Finvest Limited and Verum Properties Ltd, effective from October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Capital Finvest Limited: Symbol changed from CAPFIN to CAPITALFIN (ISIN: INE379R01014).
- Verum Properties Ltd: Symbol changed from VPL to VERUMPRO (ISIN: INE501R01013).
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the updated symbols for order routing and reporting.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Members must update their trading terminals and systems with the new symbols.
