## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued circular MSE/SURV/19964/2026 regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Certain securities have been shortlisted under ESM Stage I and Stage II, involving a shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) and price band restrictions.

## Key Points

- Comfort Intech Ltd (COMFINTE) is shortlisted under ESM Stage - I w.e.f. October 26, 2026, attracting 100% margin on open and new positions.
- Valiant Laboratories Limited (VALIANTLAB), ICDS Limited (ICDSLTD), Cubex Tubings Limited (CUBEXTUB), and Indus Finance Ltd (INDUSFINL) are moved from ESM Stage - I to Stage - II.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. October 23, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) stages (Stage I and Stage II) on specified securities based on market surveillance criteria.
- Mandatory shift of affected securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- Stage II securities attract a 2% price band with Periodic Call Auction.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must note that securities shortlisted under ESM Stage I will attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on October 23, 2026, and new positions created from October 26, 2026.
- Trading members must ensure compliance with Trade-for-Trade settlement and applicable price bands for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- October 21, 2026: Circular issuance date.
- October 23, 2026: Shifting of securities to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE); cut-off for open positions for ESM margin.
- October 26, 2026: Effective date for ESM Stage I applicability and Stage II movement (including 100% margin and 2% price band with Periodic Call Auction).

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements and trading restrictions (Trade-for-Trade segment, price bands, Periodic Call Auction) apply to the affected securities, impacting liquidity and trading mechanisms for market participants holding positions in these scrips.
