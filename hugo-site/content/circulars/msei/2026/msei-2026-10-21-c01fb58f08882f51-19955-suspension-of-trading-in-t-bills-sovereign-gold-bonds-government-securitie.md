## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the suspension of trading in Sovereign Gold Bond SGBOCT26 (ISIN: IN0020180249) on account of its redemption, effective from October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Symbol: SGBOCT26
- Series: GB
- ISIN: IN0020180249
- Reason: Suspension of trading on Primary Stock Exchange on account of redemption
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in the specified Sovereign Gold Bond is discontinued under the permitted to trade category on the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must take note of the trading suspension and ensure no fresh trades are executed in SGBOCT26.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Suspension Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact limited to holders of SGBOCT26 undergoing redemption as per primary issuance terms.
