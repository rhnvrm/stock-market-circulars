## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) will conduct a mock trading session in the Equity Capital Market segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026. Members and CTCL members are advised to actively participate and test their systems using Version 25.0.9.0 of the Trader Workstation (TWS) and Member Administrator Terminal (MAT).

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 24, 2026.
- Covers morning block deal window, pre-open, normal market, special pre-open session for IPO and relisted securities, closing auction, and post-close session.
- Trades resulting from mock trading do not attract margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out, and create no legal rights or liabilities.
- Detailed download instructions and configuration steps provided for both Leased Line and Internet (SSL VPN) users.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and CTCL members must update their TWS/MAT setup to Version 25.0.9.0 and participate actively in the mock trading session from all trading software.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 23, 2026
- Mock Trading Date: October 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures operational readiness of trading members and infrastructure without any financial or margin impact.
