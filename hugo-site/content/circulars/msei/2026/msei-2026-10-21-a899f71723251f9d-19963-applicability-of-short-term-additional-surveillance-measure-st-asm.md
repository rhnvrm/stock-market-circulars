## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Securities have been shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I, moved between stages, or excluded from the framework effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Additions:** Securities including Pashupati Cotspin Limited (PASHUPATI), Amal Ltd (AMAL), and Srestha Finvest Limited (SRESTHA) are included under ST-ASM Stage I.
- **Stage Transitions:** Balurghat Technologies Ltd (BALTE) has moved from Stage II to Stage I.
- **Exclusions:** Eight securities including Virat Industries Ltd, Dhanlaxmi Bank Limited, Brooks Laboratories Limited, Deepa Jewellers Limited, Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited, Panorama Studios International Limited, Modern Engineering And Projects Ltd, and My Money Securities Ltd have been excluded from the ST-ASM framework.
- **Margin Requirements:** Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. October 23, 2026, on open positions as on October 22, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework in continuation of previous exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Surveillance Action:** October 22, 2026
- **Effective Date of Margin Applicability:** October 23, 2026 (on open positions as on October 22, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities will attract stricter margin requirements, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips. Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and does not reflect adverse corporate action.
