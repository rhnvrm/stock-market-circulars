## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/SURV/19977/2026 dated October 23, 2026, announcing the applicability of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage I to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Stage I: Nintec Systems Limited (NINSYS), Rudrabhishek Enterprises Limited (REPL), and Samrat Pharmachem Ltd (SAMRATPH).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective October 28, 2026, on all open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions created from October 28, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) effective October 28, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

The exchange has updated the list of securities under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework, imposing stricter margin requirements to curb excessive volatility and speculation.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure that 100% margins are maintained for all open positions and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective from October 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: October 28, 2026 (on open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities (NINSYS, REPL, SAMRATPH) will require 100% upfront margins, significantly impacting liquidity and trading volumes for these counters.
