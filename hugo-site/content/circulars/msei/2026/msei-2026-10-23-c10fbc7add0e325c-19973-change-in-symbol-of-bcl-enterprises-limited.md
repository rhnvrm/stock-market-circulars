## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in the trading symbol of BCL Enterprises Limited effective from October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: BCL Enterprises Limited
- ISIN: INE368E01023
- Existing Symbol: BCL
- New Symbol: BCLENTERPR
- Effective Date: October 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members are required to update their trading systems and records with the new symbol for BCL Enterprises Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating trading terminal symbols and internal records for BCL Enterprises Limited.
