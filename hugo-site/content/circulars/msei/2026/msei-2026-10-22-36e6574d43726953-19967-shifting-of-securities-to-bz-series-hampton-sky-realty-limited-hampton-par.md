## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the shifting of securities of five companies—Hampton Sky Realty Limited (HAMPTON), Parsvnath Developers Limited (PARSVNATH), Spicejet Limited (SPICEJET), Star Housing Finance Ltd (STARHFL), and Vivimed Labs Limited (VIVIMEDLAB)—to the 'BZ' series, effective October 23, 2026. This action is taken due to non-compliance with Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (March 31, 2026, and June 30, 2026).

## Key Points

- Affected Companies: Hampton Sky Realty Limited (HAMPTON), Parsvnath Developers Limited (PARSVNATH), Spicejet Limited (SPICEJET), Star Housing Finance Ltd (STARHFL), and Vivimed Labs Limited (VIVIMEDLAB).
- Action: Transfer of trading in securities to 'BZ' series.
- Settlement Basis: Trades executed in the BZ category will be settled on a Trade for Trade basis.
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Action taken by the Primary Stock Exchange due to non-compliance of Regulation 33 (Submission of Financial Results) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies must ensure timely submission of financial results and rectify compliance failures to restore normal trading categories as per exchange and SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Non-compliance Quarters: March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the securities of HAMPTON, PARSVNATH, SPICEJET, STARHFL, and VIVIMEDLAB will be restricted to Trade for Trade settlement, meaning no netting off of trades will be allowed, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in these scrips.
