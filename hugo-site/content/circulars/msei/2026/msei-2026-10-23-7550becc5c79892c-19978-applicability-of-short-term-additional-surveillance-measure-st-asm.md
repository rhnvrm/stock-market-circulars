## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular no. MSE/SURV/19978/2026 dated October 23, 2026, announcing the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I, stage migrations, and exclusions effective October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Kanani Industries Ltd (KANANIIND), Emudhra Ltd (EMUDHRA), and Globalspace Technologies Ltd (GSTL).
- Securities moving from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II: Phoenix International Ltd (PHOENXINTL).
- Securities excluded from ST-ASM framework: W S Industries (I) Limited (WSI), Shringar House Of Mangalsutra Limited (SHRINGARMS), Abans Enterprises Limited (ABANS), Kabsons Industries Ltd (KABSON), Mitsu Chem Plast Ltd (MITSU), and Annvrridhhi Ventures Ltd (ANVRDHI).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100% w.e.f. October 27, 2026, on all open positions as on October 26, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Regulatory Changes

The shortlisting and surveillance actions are implemented in conjunction with existing ASM frameworks and prevailing exchange surveillance measures to protect market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities starting October 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Measures / Margins: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the listed securities will be subject to higher margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for these scrips.
