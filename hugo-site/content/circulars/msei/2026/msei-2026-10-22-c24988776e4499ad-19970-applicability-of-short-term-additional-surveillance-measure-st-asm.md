## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued circular MSE/SURV/19970/2026 regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) effective October 23, 2026. This circular details securities entering ST-ASM Stage I, moving between stages, and being excluded from the framework.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusions**: Securities such as Generic Engineering Construction And Projects Ltd (GENCON), Transworld Shipping Lines Limited (TRANSWORLD), Lagnam Spintex Limited (LAGNAM), Dcm Shriram Industries Ltd. (DCMSRIND), Spencer's Retail Limited (SPENCERS), and Achyut Healthcare Ltd (ACHYUT) have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I.
- **Stage Movements**: AVRO INDIA LIMITED (AVROIND) has moved from Short-Term ASM Framework Stage I to Stage II.
- **Exclusions**: Several securities including Turtlemint Fintech Solutions Limited (TURTLEMINT), Indiabulls Limited (IBULLSLTD), BMW Ventures Limited (BMWVENTLTD), BN Agrochem Ltd (BNAGROCHEM), Terai Tea Company Ltd (TERAI), and Onward Technologies Limited (ONWARDTEC) are excluded from the Short-Term ASM Framework.
- **Applicable Margin**: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. October 26, 2026, on all open positions as on October 23, 2026, and new positions created from October 26, 2026 onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of surveillance measures under the ST-ASM framework in continuation of previous exchange circulars (MSE/SURV/6932/2018, MSE/SURV/9816/2020, and MSE/SURV/11774/2022).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements of 50% or existing margin (capped at 100%) for securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I starting October 26, 2026, on open positions as of October 23, 2026, and subsequent new positions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Surveillance / Exclusions / Stage Shifts**: October 23, 2026
- **Effective Date of Margin Applicability**: October 26, 2026 (on open positions as on October 23, 2026, and new positions onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Trading members dealing in the affected securities must maintain higher margins (50% to 100%), which may impact liquidity and leverage for these specific equities. Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and does not reflect an adverse action against the companies.
