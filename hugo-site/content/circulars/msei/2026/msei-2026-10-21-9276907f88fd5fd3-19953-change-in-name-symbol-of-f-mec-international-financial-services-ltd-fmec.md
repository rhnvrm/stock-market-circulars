## Summary

The Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the change of name and trading symbol for F Mec International Financial Services Ltd. Effective October 22, 2026, the company will trade under its new name and symbol.

## Key Points

- Old Company Name: F Mec International Financial Services Ltd
- New Company Name: DHVIJA FINANCE LIMITED
- Old Symbol: FMEC
- New Symbol: DHVIJAFIN
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026
- Segment: Capital Market (Permitted to Trade category)

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable - Routine corporate identification update]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to update their trading systems and records with the new name and symbol for all orders and references effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact limited to updating internal trading systems, master files, and client portfolios to reflect the new name and symbol.
