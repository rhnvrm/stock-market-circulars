## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of SRIT India Limited for trading onوینت the Capital Market Segment under the "Permitted to Trade" category effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SRIT India Limited
- Symbol: SRIT
- Series: EQ
- Market Lot: 1
- ISIN: INE307K01028
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Companies admitted under the "Permitted to Trade" category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the admission and particulars of the security for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Trading: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members to trade in the equity shares of SRIT India Limited on the Exchange's Capital Market Segment under the permitted category.
