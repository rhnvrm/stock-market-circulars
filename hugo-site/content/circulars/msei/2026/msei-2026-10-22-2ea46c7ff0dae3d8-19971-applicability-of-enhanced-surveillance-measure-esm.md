## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for certain securities, effective October 26, 2026, based on criteria satisfaction. Securities under ESM are shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment with additional margin requirements.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I are included under ESM Stage - I w.e.f. October 26, 2026 (on open positions as on October 23, 2026 and new positions created from October 26, 2026).
- Affected securities attract a minimum 100% margin requirement.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. October 26, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework rules in conjunction with prevailing exchange measures.
- Shift of qualifying securities from Series EQ to Series BE (Trade-for-Trade segment).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for affected securities.
- Trading in these securities will be settled in the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margin/Segment Shift:** October 26, 2026 (open positions as on October 23, 2026)
- **Stage II / Periodic Call Auction provisions:** w.e.f. October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and operational processes for the shortlisted securities will be restricted due to the Trade-for-Trade settlement requirement and 100% margin obligations.
