## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the half-yearly submission of details of Associate/Subsidiary Entities under the Risk Based Supervision (RBS) framework. Based on representations from the Brokers Industry Standard Forum (ISF) and consultation with SEBI and other exchanges, associate and subsidiary details will be taken as a separate submission.

## Key Points

- Trading members must submit details of their Associate/Subsidiary Entities on a half-yearly basis.
- Submission for the half-year ended March 31, 2026, must be completed on or before November 15, 2026.
- Any changes (additions, deletions, or modifications) in details must be notified to the exchange within 30 calendar days of occurrence.
- Trading members of NSE who are registered with other exchanges (including MSE) are required to submit associate/subsidiary details to NSE, which will share them with the respective exchanges.
- Exclusive MSE members must submit their details directly to MSE at membership@mse.co.in in the prescribed format (Annexure A).

## Regulatory Changes

- Streamlining of Risk Based Supervision (RBS) submissions by separating associate/subsidiary disclosures from other filings.
- Technology-based sharing mechanism among exchanges for common submissions.

## Compliance Requirements

- All trading members must compile and submit associate/subsidiary entity details in Annexure A format.
- Exclusive MSE members must email submissions to membership@mse.co.in.
- Subsequent changes must be reported within 30 calendar days.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Submission Deadline (Half-year ended March 31, 2026):** November 15, 2026
- **Change Notification Window:** Within 30 calendar days of any modification

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure timely and accurate reporting of associate and subsidiary entities to comply with MSEI and SEBI compliance guidelines under the RBS framework, avoiding potential regulatory friction.
