## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has released a circular detailing the list of Investment Companies (ICs) and Investment Holding Companies (IHCs) under the permitted to trade category that are eligible for special call-auctions, in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Pursuant to SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD 3/P/CIR/2024/86 dated June 20, 2024.
- Applies to specified Investment Companies (ICs) and Investment Holding Companies (IHCs) under the permitted to trade category.
- Includes 8 companies with their respective symbols and ISINs.
- If the call auction is not successful on day 1, it shall continue on the next day until price discovery is achieved.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of special call-auctions for eligible investment holding companies under the permitted to trade category as per primary stock exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to note the eligible securities and auction continuation rules if price discovery is not completed on day one.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates price discovery for holding companies under permitted to trade category through structured special call-auctions.
