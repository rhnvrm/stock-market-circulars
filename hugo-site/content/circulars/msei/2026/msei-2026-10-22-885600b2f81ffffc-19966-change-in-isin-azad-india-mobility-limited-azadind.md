## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding the change in ISIN for Azad India Mobility Limited (AZADIND) effective October 23, 2026, following the sub-division of existing equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Azad India Mobility Limited
- Symbol: AZADIND
- Old ISIN: INE566M01017
- New ISIN: INE566M01025
- Purpose: Sub-division of existing equity shares from one equity share of Rs. 10/- each into five equity shares of Re. 2/- each.
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine ISIN update reflecting a corporate action (stock split/sub-division).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the new ISIN for trades executed on and from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Ex-Date / Effective Date for New ISIN: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating the security master with the new ISIN for Azad India Mobility Limited.
