## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the applicability and movement of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, effective from October 26 and October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 27, 2026 on all open positions as on October 26, 2026 and new positions created from October 27, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. October 27, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% with Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 26, 2026.
- Specific securities are moving between stages (e.g., Stage II to Stage I) and several securities are moving out of the ESM framework entirely.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures, higher margin requirements, and trading segment restrictions for identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement, price band restrictions, and trade-for-trade settlement for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 26, 2026 and October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Directly impacts liquidity and trading operations for securities listed under the ESM framework due to stricter margin norms and trade-for-trade settlement.
