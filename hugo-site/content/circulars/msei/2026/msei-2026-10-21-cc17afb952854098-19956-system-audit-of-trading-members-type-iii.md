## Summary

MSEI has issued a circular regarding the System Audit of Trading Members (Type – III) for the half-yearly period ending September 30, 2026. Trading members are required to conduct their system audit through Exchange-empanelled auditors, complete portal registrations, ensure geo-location tracking during physical audits, and submit reports within specified timelines.

## Key Points

- Applicable to Type – III Trading Members for the audit period April 2026 – September 2026.
- Mandatory appointment of System Auditors empanelled with MSEI.
- Submission of auditor registration and preliminary audit plan via the MSE Inspection.OS Portal by November 05, 2026.
- Mandatory geo-location capture and entry/exit time marking by auditors via the MSE Inspection Portal and mobile application during physical visits.
- Audit working papers, logs, screenshots, and records of audit team members must be preserved for a minimum of three years.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of established SEBI and Exchange frameworks for monitoring and supervision of system audits of stockbrokers through technology-based measures and mandatory empanelled auditor utilization.

## Compliance Requirements

- Appoint only MSEI empanelled system auditors.
- Register auditor details and submit audit plans via MSE Inspection.OS Portal by November 05, 2026.
- Ensure auditors mark entry/exit times and enable geo-location tracking via the MSE portal and mobile app during on-site visits.
- Submit the system audit report by November 30, 2026, and Action Taken Report (ATR) by February 28, 2027 (if applicable).
- Maintain comprehensive records of audit team members and working papers for at least three years.

## Important Dates

- **Audit Plan / Auditor Registration Due Date:** November 05, 2026
- **Audit Report Submission Due Date:** November 30, 2026
- **Action Taken Report (ATR) Due Date (If applicable):** February 28, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must strictly coordinate with empanelled auditors, comply with portal-based geo-location and time logging mandates during physical inspections, and meet strict filing deadlines to ensure regulatory compliance.
