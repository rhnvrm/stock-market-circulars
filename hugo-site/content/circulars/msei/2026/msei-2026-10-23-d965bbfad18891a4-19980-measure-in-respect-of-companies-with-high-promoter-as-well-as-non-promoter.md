## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India (MSEI) has issued a circular regarding surveillance measures for companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities newly shortlisted under Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin.

## Key Points

- **New Inclusions (Annexure I):** Indo Tech Transformers Limited (INDOTECH) and EUREKA FORBES LIMITED (EUREKAFORB) have been added to the framework.
- **Effective Date:** The 75% margin requirement is effective from October 28, 2026, applicable to all open positions as of October 27, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- **Exclusions (Annexure II):** Nil securities are moving out of the framework.
- **Consolidated List (Annexure III):** Includes FORBESCO, TOTEM, RACE, BPL, BRAHMINFRA, INDOTECH, and EUREKAFORB.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margin requirements under SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011 for companies exhibiting high encumbrance levels.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adequate margin collection (minimum 75%) on open and new positions for the specified securities starting October 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Measure:** October 28, 2026
- **Positions Evaluated From:** Open positions as on October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements will impact liquidity and capital allocation for trading members holding open positions in the shortlisted securities.
