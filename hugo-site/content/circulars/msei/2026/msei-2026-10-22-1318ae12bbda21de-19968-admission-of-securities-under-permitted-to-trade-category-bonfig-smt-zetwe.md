## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has announced the admission of equity shares of four companies under the "Permitted to Trade" category effective October 23, 2026. These companies do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and therefore do not provide full disclosures.

## Key Points

- Trading effective date: October 23, 2026.
- Segment: Capital Market Segment.
- Admitted companies:
  1. Bonfiglioli Transmissions Limited (Symbol: BONFIG, ISIN: INE28SO01013, Market Lot: 1)
  2. Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited (Symbol: SMT, ISIN: INE356Y01018, Market Lot: 1)
  3. Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited (Symbol: ZETWERK, ISIN: INE09PO01019, Market Lot: 1)
  4. Tonbo Imaging India Limited (Symbol: TONBO, ISIN: INE0LDG01026, Market Lot: 1)

## Regulatory Changes

Companies admitted under the "Permitted to Trade" category do not execute a listing agreement with the Exchange and do not provide full disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the admission of securities and ensure operational readiness for trading starting on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands trading choices for market participants on the MSEI Capital Market Segment with the inclusion of new equities under the permitted category.
