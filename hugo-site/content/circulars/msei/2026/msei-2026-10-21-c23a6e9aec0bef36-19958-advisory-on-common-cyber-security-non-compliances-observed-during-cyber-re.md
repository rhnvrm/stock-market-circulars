## Summary

Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI) has issued an advisory detailing common cyber security non-compliances observed across trading members' systems during the special purpose cyber revalidation audit and CERT-In Cyber Swachhta Kendra (CSK) monitoring conducted by SEBI and Stock Exchanges.

## Key Points

- Lack of clear classification and categorization of critical assets under the Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF).
- Insufficient granularity in asset inventories (missing end-of-life, end-of-support, and installation dates).
- Gaps in internal cyber security policies regarding exceptions and deviations, including lack of differentiation between them.
- CISO role, level, and grade not positioned equivalently to CTO/CIO, impacting governance independence.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiterates adherence requirements under the SEBI and Exchange Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must put in place necessary systems, controls, and procedures to address observed non-compliances and strengthen cyber security posture.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Helps trading members bridge gaps between requirements and interpretation, ensuring better compliance and enhanced cyber resilience.
