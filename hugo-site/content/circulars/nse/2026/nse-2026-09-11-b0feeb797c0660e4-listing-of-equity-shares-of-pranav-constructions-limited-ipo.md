## Summary

Pranav Constructions Limited has announced the listing of its equity shares on the exchange following its initial public offering (IPO), detailing shareholding patterns and specified securities.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Pranav Constructions Limited
- Total equity shares: 112,622,782
- Promoter & Promoter Group holding: 55,219,345 shares (49.03%)
- Public holding: 57,403,437 shares (50.97%)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 regarding the shareholding pattern submission.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 10.09.2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of equity shares of Pranav Constructions Limited on the exchange platform.
