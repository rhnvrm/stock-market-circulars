## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the empanelment of M/s Ratel Fintech Private Limited as an approved Algo Provider for providing Whitebox Algorithmic Trading Solutions to trading members of the Exchange, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Empaneled Vendor: M/s Ratel Fintech Private Limited
- Type of Algo: Whitebox
- Correspondence Address: 4th Floor, 6348/BL-7, Gali No.1, Dev Nagar, Karol Bagh, Central Delhi, Delhi, India, 110005
- Contact Details: +91 7501351937
- Applicability: Effective immediately

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this circular pertains solely to vendor empanelment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members intending to use algorithmic trading solutions from M/s Ratel Fintech Private Limited may utilize their empaneled services in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact; expands the list of approved algorithmic trading solution providers available to exchange trading members.
