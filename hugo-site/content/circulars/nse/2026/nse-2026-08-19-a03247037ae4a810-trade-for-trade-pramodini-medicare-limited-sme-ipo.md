## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Pramodini Medicare Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pramodini Medicare Limited
- Symbol: PRAMODINI
- Transfer from: Trade for trade segment (Series: ST)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Securities are moved from the trade-for-trade segment to the normal rolling segment as per SEBI guidelines and previous exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are requested to note the change in segment and series for trading in the shares of the company.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the shares of Pramodini Medicare Limited will now be permitted in the rolling segment, enabling normal settlement cycles.
