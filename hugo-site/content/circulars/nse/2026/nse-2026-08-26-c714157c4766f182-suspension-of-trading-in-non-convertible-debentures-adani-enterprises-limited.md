## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of Adani Enterprises Limited with effect from August 28, 2026, pursuant to maturity.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Adani Enterprises Limited
- Suspended Securities:
  - Symbol: 925AEL26, ISIN: INE423A07351 (Reason: Maturity)
  - Symbol: AEL120924, ISIN: INE423A07369 (Reason: Maturity)
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Action taken in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the suspension of trading in the specified NCDs of Adani Enterprises Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified NCDs will cease on the NSE from the effective date as the securities have reached maturity.
