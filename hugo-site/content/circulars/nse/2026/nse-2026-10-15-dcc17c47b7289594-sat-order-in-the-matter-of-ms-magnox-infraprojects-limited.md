## Summary

The Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT), Mumbai, in Appeal No. 108 of 2026, condoned a delay of 2,948 days and entertained an appeal filed by Mr. Jyotiranjan Panda, former Director of M/s Magnox Infraprojects Limited, challenging the SEBI Whole Time Member (WTM) order dated February 20, 2018.

## Key Points

- The appeal challenges SEBI's WTM order directing the refund of monies collected via redeemable preference shares (RPS).
- SEBI investigation revealed that Magnox Infraprojects Limited had mobilized ₹2.31 Crore from 2,110 persons during 2012-13 through RPS in violation of the Companies Act, 1956 and securities laws.
- Misc. Application No. 332 of 2026 was allowed, condoning the delay of 2,948 days in filing the appeal.
- The company's offer and allotment of RPS was deemed a 'public issue' under Section 67(3) of the Companies Act, 1956 without necessary statutory compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory framework introduced; this is a judicial/appellate tribunal order enforcing existing provisions of the Companies Act, 1956 and the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

- Strict compliance with public issue norms, prospectus filings, and investor protection regulations under company law and SEBI regulations is mandatory for all capital mobilization.

## Important Dates

- Order Pronouncement Date: October 15, 2026
- Hearing Date: September 24, 2026
- Impugned SEBI WTM Order Date: February 20, 2018

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal direct market impact, as the proceedings relate to historical compliance enforcement actions against an unlisted entity.
