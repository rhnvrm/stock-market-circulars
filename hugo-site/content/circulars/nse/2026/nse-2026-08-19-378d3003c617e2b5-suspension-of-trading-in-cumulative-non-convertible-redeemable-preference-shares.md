## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in Cumulative Non-Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares (TVSMNCRPS) of TVS Motor Company Limited effective from August 21, 2026, due to maturity.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TVS Motor Company Limited
- Security Symbol: TVSMNCRPS
- ISIN: INE494B04019
- Reason for Suspension: Maturity
- Effective Date of Suspension: August 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Action taken pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must note the suspension of trading in the specified security from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 19, 2026
- Effective Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the Cumulative Non-Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares of TVS Motor Company Limited will cease on the exchange from August 21, 2026, onwards.
