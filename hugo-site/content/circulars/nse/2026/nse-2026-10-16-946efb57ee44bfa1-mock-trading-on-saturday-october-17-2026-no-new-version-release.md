## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, October 17, 2026. There is no new software version release for this session (NEAT+ version 7.9.0 remains applicable).

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 17, 2026.
- Conducted across primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites.
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 is applicable with no new version release.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session; members using NNF software must manually clear orders.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. The mock session meets regulatory requirements for business continuity and disaster recovery testing as per SEBI circular guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to participate in the mock trading session to test connectivity and trading systems.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.
- UCC validation will be skipped during the mock session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 17, 2026
- Trading Session-1 from Primary Site starts at 09:45 IST
- Trading Session-2 from DR Site starts at 12:45 IST

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness check for trading members with zero financial obligation impact.
