## Summary

NSE has issued a revised list of approved securities and banks, outlining the equity shares that form part of the non-cash component of liquid assets along with their applicable haircuts and permitted quantities across segments.

## Key Points

- Contains the updated list of approved equity shares forming part of the non-cash component of liquid assets.
- Specifies overall permitted quantities across all segments and memberwise permitted quantities for the COM segment.
- Applicable haircuts for the securities are determined based on VAR.
- Value-based prudential norms specify that exclusion of a pledged security should not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the list of eligible securities accepted by the exchange as collateral or liquid assets.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing Members must ensure adherence to the permitted quantities and prudential norms regarding pledged securities and margin shortfalls.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects trading members and clearing members utilizing non-cash collateral and liquid assets for margin requirements.
