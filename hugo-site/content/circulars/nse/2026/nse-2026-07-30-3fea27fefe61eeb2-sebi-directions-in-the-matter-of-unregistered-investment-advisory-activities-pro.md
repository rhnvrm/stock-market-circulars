## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the SEBI directions in the matter of unregistered investment advisory activities of Mr. Patibandla Venkata Vasanta Kumar, proprietor of Profit Your Trade.In. Referring to the original SEBI order dated August 24, 2023, which debarred the noticee for one year or until the expiry of one year from the completion of refunds (whichever is later), SEBI has now confirmed that the noticee deposited the full refund amount on September 18, 2024. Consequently, the one-year debarment period has elapsed, and the exchange has been advised to comply with these directions.

## Key Points

- **Noticee Details:** Mr. Patibandla Venkata Vasanta Kumar (Proprietor - Profit Your Trade.In), PAN: ADEPV8903E.
- **Enforcement Action:** Originally debarred from accessing or dealing in the securities market directly or indirectly for one year or until refund completion, whichever is later.
- **Deposit of Funds:** The noticee deposited the full refund/balance amount with SEBI on September 18, 2024.
- **Debarment Completion:** Since more than one year has elapsed since the deposit date, the debarment period has completed.
- **Action Required:** Trading members and the exchange are to note the completion of the debarment and update their compliance databases.

## Regulatory Changes

No active regulatory rules or frameworks have been modified. This is an update on the enforcement and compliance status of a specific individual/entity under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance officers of trading members must update their internal list of debarred/restricted entities to reflect that the debarment period of Mr. Patibandla Venkata Vasanta Kumar has concluded.
- Verify and ensure that operational or trading bans linked to PAN ADEPV8903E are updated in line with SEBI's notification.

## Important Dates

- **August 24, 2023:** Date of SEBI's original debarment order (QJA/AA/SRO/SRO/28911/2023-24).
- **September 18, 2024:** Date of full refund/balance deposit with SEBI by the noticee.
- **September 18, 2025:** Completion date of the one-year debarment period (one year from deposit date).
- **July 30, 2026:** Date of SEBI's communication and the NSE circular.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Nil. The notification only concerns the status of a single individual and does not affect market trading systems, liquidity, or listed stocks.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Requires routine, non-disruptive compliance register updates by trading members.
