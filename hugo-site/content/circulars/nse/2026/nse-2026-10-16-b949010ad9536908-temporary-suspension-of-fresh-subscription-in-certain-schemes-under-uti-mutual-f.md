## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, lumpsum transactions (including switch-ins), and fresh registrations under Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) & Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) for UTI Gold ETF Fund of Fund under UTI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Scheme: UTI Gold ETF Fund of Fund.
- Restrictions: Temporary restriction on all fresh and additional lump sum, switch-in transactions, and fresh registrations in SIPs and Step up facility.
- Cut-off Time: Transactions timestamped on or before 3:00 PM on October 15, 2026, are accepted and processed at applicable NAV.
- Unaffected Operations: Redemptions (including switch-outs), Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), and existing SIPs/STPs remain fully operational.

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension implemented pursuant to instructions from AMFI / SEBI for the specific scheme under UTI Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and platform participants must update their systems to block fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and new SIP/STP registrations for UTI Gold ETF Fund of Fund with immediate effect.
- Ensure ongoing processing of redemptions and existing SIP/STP/SWP instructions without disruption.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: October 16, 2026.
- Cut-off Timestamp for Eligible Transactions: 3:00 PM on October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors cannot initiate new lumpsum investments or register fresh SIPs/STPs in UTI Gold ETF Fund of Fund until further notice.
- Existing investors retain full liquidity through redemptions, switch-outs, and continuing automated investment plans (SIP/STP/SWP).
