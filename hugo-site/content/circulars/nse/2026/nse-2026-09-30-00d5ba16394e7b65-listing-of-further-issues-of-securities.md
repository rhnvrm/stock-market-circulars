## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of securities for 12 companies, including Ather Energy Limited, EPL Limited, The Federal Bank Limited, Go Digit General Insurance Limited, Jana Small Finance Bank Limited, Kaynes Technology India Limited, Renaissance Global Limited, Sai Life Sciences Limited, Sindhu Trade Links Limited, STL Networks Limited, Tejas Networks Limited, and Veranda Learning Solutions Limited, effective October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listing of further issues of equity shares admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective October 01, 2026.
- Covers 12 companies including Ather Energy Limited, EPL Limited, The Federal Bank Limited, and Go Digit General Insurance Limited.
- Shares issued under preferential allotment and ESOP (Employee Stock Option Plan).
- Trading shall be identified by designated security codes and respective lot sizes as specified in the Annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure trading is conducted using designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026
- Allotment dates vary by company (e.g., August 25, 2026, and September 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates trading of newly allotted equity shares resulting from preferential issues and ESOP exercises across multiple listed entities.
