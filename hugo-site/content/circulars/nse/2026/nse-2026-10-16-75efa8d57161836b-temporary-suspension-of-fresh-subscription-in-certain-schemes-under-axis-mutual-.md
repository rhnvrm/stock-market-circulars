## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, lumpsum investments, switches into, and fresh registrations of Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) for Axis Gold Fund under Axis Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from the opening of business hours on October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Transactions: Lumpsum subscriptions, switches into the scheme, and fresh registrations of SIP and STP in Axis Gold Fund are temporarily suspended.
- Continued Transactions: Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, existing systematic transactions (SIP/STP), and fresh registrations of systematic transactions out of the scheme will continue without interruption.
- Effective Date: The suspension is effective from October 16, 2026, until further notice from Axis AMC.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational restriction on subscription flows into Axis Gold Fund implemented by Axis Mutual Fund and facilitated across NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must note the suspension of fresh subscription channels and ensure no new lumpsum or fresh SIP/STP applications are submitted for Axis Gold Fund during the suspension period.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding existing SIPs or wishing to redeem units are unaffected. New investors or existing investors looking to increase exposure via lumpsum or fresh SIP/STP in Axis Gold Fund will need to wait until the suspension is lifted by Axis AMC.
