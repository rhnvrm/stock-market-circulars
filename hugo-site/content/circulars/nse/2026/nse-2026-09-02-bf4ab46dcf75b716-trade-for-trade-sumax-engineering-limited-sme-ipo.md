## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the transfer of securities of Sumax Engineering Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (series: SM) effective September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sumax Engineering Limited
- Symbol: SUMAX
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: ST)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026
- Reference to previous exchange circular no. NSE/CML/76112 dated September 01, 2026 and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are moved from trade-for-trade settlement to normal rolling settlement in compliance with regulatory norms for SME IPOs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are required to note the change in series and trade accordingly from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the shares of Sumax Engineering Limited will now be permitted in the rolling segment, improving liquidity and normal settlement cycles.
