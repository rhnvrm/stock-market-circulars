## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the admission to dealings of privately placed securities for Prisma Global Limited on the capital market segment, effective from August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Prisma Global Limited
- Security Description: PGL 10% 2028
- Symbol: 10PGL28
- Series: N0
- Market Lot: 1
- Coupon Rate: 10%
- Date of Maturity: August 18, 2028
- ISIN Code: INE0B2O07011

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, further securities are admitted to dealings with designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the specified securities shall be conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes as per the Annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 21, 2026
- Maturity Date: August 18, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading and settlement of the newly listed privately placed securities on NSE's capital market segment for eligible market participants.
