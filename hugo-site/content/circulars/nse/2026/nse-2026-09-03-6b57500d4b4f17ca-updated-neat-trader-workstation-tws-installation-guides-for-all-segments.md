## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has released updated installation guides for the NEAT+ Trader Workstation (TWS) across all segments including CM, FO, CD, COM, SLB, and EGR. The revised guides detail updated prerequisites, system requirements, and installation procedures.

## Key Points

- Updated guides available on respective segment Extranet paths.
- Covers operating system support (Windows 10 and Windows 11) and Visual C++ Redistributable requirements.
- Provides guidelines for taking necessary data backups prior to software upgrades.
- Details instructions for downloading and updating contract, security, and participant details.

## Regulatory Changes

- No direct regulatory policy change; operational guideline update for member trading infrastructure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must refer to applicable segment-wise guides and ensure all prescribed steps and prerequisites are completed before installing upcoming new NEAT+ versions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members need to verify system compatibility (minimum RAM, OS version, and resolution) and execute installations after live trading hours and completion of end-of-day activities.
