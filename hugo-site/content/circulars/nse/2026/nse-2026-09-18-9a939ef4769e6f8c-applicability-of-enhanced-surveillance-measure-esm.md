## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability, movement, and exclusion of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities shortlisted in ESM Stage-I will attract a minimum 100% margin and shift from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment effective September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for ESM Stage-I inclusion attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 22, 2026, on open positions as on September 21, 2026, and new positions created from September 22, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage-II shall be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 21, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework measures in conjunction with existing surveillance mechanisms.
- Specific shortlistings and stage movements for listed securities as detailed in Annexures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and segment shifting for impacted scrips.
- Market participants to note price band restrictions under Periodic Call Auction for Stage-II securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage-II price band and stage movements: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date for margin requirements and segment shifting (EQ/SM to BE/ST): September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity for the affected scrips may be moderated due to the Trade-for-Trade settlement and 100% margin requirements.
