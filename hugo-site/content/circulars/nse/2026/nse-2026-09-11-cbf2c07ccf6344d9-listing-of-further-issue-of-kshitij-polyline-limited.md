## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further equity shares of Kshitij Polyline Limited pursuant to a rights issue. A total of 5,98,86,887 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each will be admitted to dealings with effect from September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kshitij Polyline Limited
- Symbol: KSHITIJPOL
- Security Description: Equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under Rights Issue
- Number of Securities: 59,886,887
- Distinctive Number Range: 154244875 to 214131761
- Issue Price: Rs. 3.17
- Date of Allotment: 10-Sep-2026
- Effective Date: September 15, 2026
- ISIN: INE013801027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares allotted in dematerialized mode have been credited under temporary ISIN in accordance with SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing of shares for Kshitij Polyline Limited on the NSE capital market segment.
