## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced changes in the constituents of various indices within the Futures & Options segment and broader market indices w.e.f. September 30, 2026 (close of September 29, 2026), based on the press release issued by NSE Indices Limited dated August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Replacements and exclusions apply to multiple indices effective September 30, 2026.
- Excluded stocks include Ambuja Cements, Bajaj Holdings, Bank of Baroda, ICICI Prudential Life, SBI Cards, BSE Limited, Fortis Healthcare, Hindustan Petroleum, Lodha Developers, REC Ltd, Shree Cement, and United Spirits.
- Included stocks include Ashok Leyland, Biocon, Lenskart Solutions, Hitachi Energy India, Vedanta Aluminium Metal, Federal Bank, GE Vernova T&D India, Indian Hotels, Multi Commodity Exchange (MCX), Vodafone Idea, Polycab India, and Wipro.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic rebalancing and constituent changes of NSE indices impacting derivative products and index-linked portfolios.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals, risk management systems, and index tracking portfolios to account for the revised index constituents.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2026 (based on close of September 29, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Moderate impact on trading strategies, index funds, ETFs, and F&O derivative contracts associated with the affected indices.
