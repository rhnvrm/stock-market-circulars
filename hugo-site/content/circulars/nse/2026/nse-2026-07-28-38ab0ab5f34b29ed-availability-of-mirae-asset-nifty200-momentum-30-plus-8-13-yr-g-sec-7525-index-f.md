## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular (Ref. No. 1521/2026) informing members that the Mirae Asset Nifty200 Momentum 30 Plus 8–13 yr G-Sec 7525 Index Fund NFO will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and other systematic facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Reopening for Transactions:** The Mirae Asset Nifty200 Momentum 30 Plus 8–13 yr G-Sec 7525 Index Fund NFO of Mirae Asset Mutual Fund will reopen for ongoing subscription, redemption, and systematic facilities (SIP, STP, SWP).
- **Effective Date:** Reopening is effective from July 29, 2026.
- **Platform Availability:** Transactions will be supported via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Scheme Plans & ISINs:** Six distinct plans (Direct and Regular across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment categories) are supported under the Equity category with mapped ISIN codes (e.g., INF769K01RG1, INF769K01RD8).

## Regulatory Changes

No specific regulatory or policy changes have been introduced by this circular. This is a standard operational announcement facilitating trading/subscription of an authorized mutual fund scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Mutual Fund Trading Members:** Members of the NMF TM Segment must ensure their systems are updated to support subscription, redemption, and systematic facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) for these schemes.
- **Support Contact:** Members can reach out to NSE Invest support at support@nseinvest.com or Toll-Free at 1800 2100 940 for any assistance.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 28, 2026
- **Effective Reopening Date:** July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine operational enablement of mutual fund schemes on the exchange's mutual fund platform, adding to existing offerings without affecting equity trading structures.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Members can process transactions for the specified schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting July 29, 2026.
