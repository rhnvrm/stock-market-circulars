## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has approved the listing and admission to dealings of several privately placed securities on its Debt Market Segment, effective August 04, 2026. This action is taken in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations. The listed securities include Commercial Papers (CPs) and Other Debt Securities issued by various companies such as IIFL Finance Limited, Tata Steel Limited, JM Financial Services Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Northern Arc Capital Limited, Dar Credit & Capital Limited, and Tata Projects Limited.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** August 04, 2026.
- **Trading Segment:** Debt Market Segment of the Exchange.
- **Regulation:** Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.
- **Issuers of Commercial Paper:**
  - **IIFL Finance Limited:** Issued two series of CPs (Sr 2632 and Sr 2633) worth 3,500 and 2,500 securities respectively, maturing in November 2026 and February 2027.
  - **Tata Steel Limited:** Issued two CPs maturing in September and October 2026, totaling 19,000 securities.
  - **JM Financial Services Limited:** Issued 500 securities of CP (Sr 16) maturing in November 2026.
  - **Kotak Securities Limited:** Issued 7,000 securities of CP (Sr 133) maturing in November 2026.
- **Issuers of Other Debt Securities:**
  - **Northern Arc Capital Limited:** Listing of NACL 9.04% 2028 debentures (7,500 securities) with maturity on July 31, 2028.
  - **Dar Credit & Capital Limited:** Listing of DCCL 12.9% 2028 debentures (1,000 securities) with maturity on July 31, 2028.
  - **Tata Projects Limited:** Listing of TPL 8.32% 2029 Sr A1 debentures (40,000 securities) with maturity on July 31, 2029.
  - **UltraTech Cement Limited:** Mentioned in list (truncated in source).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory framework is introduced. This is a routine enforcement of the existing Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members:** Trading members can deal in these securities on the Debt Market Segment of the exchange starting from August 04, 2026, under the designated security codes and ISINs specified in the circular.
- **For Issuers:** Issuers must comply with continuous disclosure and listing requirements for debt securities/commercial papers as mandated by SEBI and NSE guidelines.
