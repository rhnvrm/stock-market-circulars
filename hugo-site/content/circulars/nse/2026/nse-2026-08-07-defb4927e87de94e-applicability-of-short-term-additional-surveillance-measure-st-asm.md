## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Based on market surveillance, nine securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I, effective from August 11, 2026. No securities have been added to ST-ASM Stage II in this revision. This surveillance framework is implemented in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures and is purely based on market surveillance, meaning it should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusion:** Nine securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion in ST-ASM Stage I.
- **Applicable Margin Rate for Stage I:** The rate of margin shall be 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% on all open positions as of August 10, 2026, and new positions created from August 11, 2026.
- **ST-ASM Stage II Status:** No securities have been shortlisted for Stage II under this framework.
- **Price Band Restoration:** The price band of any scrip moving out of the framework will be reinstated to its prior applicable price band, subject to not being part of any other surveillance measures.
- **Nature of Action:** The shortlisting under ASM is a routine market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I:** Margin rate raised to 50% (or existing higher margin), capped at 100%, for the 9 shortlisted securities.
- **Previous Circular References:** This action is in continuation of Exchange circulars:
  - NSE/SURV/39265 (October 27, 2018)
  - NSE/SURV/46557 (December 04, 2020)
  - NSE/SURV/52144 (April 28, 2022)
  - NSE/SURV/58558 (September 25, 2023)
  - NSE/SURV/64066 (September 20, 2024)

## Compliance Requirements

- **NSE Members:** Trading members must implement and collect the applicable margin of 50% (or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%) on all open positions as of August 10, 2026, and new positions created starting August 11, 2026, for the shortlisted securities.
- **Information Access:** Market participants can refer to Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) at https://www.nseindia.com/regulations/additional-surveillance-measure.
- **Inquiries:** Contact Exchange surveillance at surveillance@nse.co.in for any queries.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Position Cut-off Date:** August 10, 2026 (open positions of this date will be affected)
- **Effective Date of Margin Changes:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** The implementation of a 50% minimum margin requirement will likely reduce leverage and may temporarily decrease the trading volumes/liquidity of the 9 shortlisted securities (Arabian Petroleum Limited, ASK Automotive Limited, Elantas Beck India Limited, Forge Auto International Limited, Kriti Nutrients Limited, Manaksia Steels Limited, Signet Industries Limited, Savita Oil Technologies Limited, and TechNVision Ventures Limited).
- **Operational Impact:** Clearing members and brokers must update their risk management systems to reflect the increased margin requirements for the affected securities starting August 11, 2026.
