## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has released a revised list of privately issued corporate bonds that are made eligible (flagged as 'a') or ineligible (flagged as 'd') under the DVP-3 settlement mechanism on the Institutional Platform of the Debt Segment. This update supersedes previous circulars regarding bond eligibility and comes into effect on September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Updates eligibility status for privately issued corporate bonds under DVP-3 settlement.
- Applicable to the Institutional Platform of the Debt Segment.
- References previous guidelines under circulars NSE/CML/23366 and NSE/DS/24832.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of eligible and ineligible securities list for institutional debt trading settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the updated eligibility list for smooth settlement operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 15, 2026
- Circular Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational alignment for institutional trading members dealing with corporate bond settlements under DVP-3.
