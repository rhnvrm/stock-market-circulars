## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for multiple companies, effective from September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities further admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective September 02, 2026.
- Includes companies such as Aavas Financiers Limited, Balrampur Chini Mills Limited, BLACKBUCK LIMITED, and Bliss GVS Pharma Limited, among others.
- Allotment of equity shares under ESOP with specified distinctive number ranges and market lot of 1.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in such securities shall be identified by designated codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital base for the respective listed entities.
