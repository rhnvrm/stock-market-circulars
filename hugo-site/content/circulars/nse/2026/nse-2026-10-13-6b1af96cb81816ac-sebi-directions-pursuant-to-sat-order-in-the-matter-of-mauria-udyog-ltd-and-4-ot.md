## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated September 7, 2026, in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips. SEBI has confirmed that Kanungo Financiers Limited has deposited the full penalty amount, leading to a stay of the earlier restraint order.

## Key Points

- Reference to NSE Circular No. NSE/INVG/74958 dated June 30, 2026, regarding SEBI interim order restraining certain entities from accessing the securities market.
- Kanungo Financiers Limited (PAN: AABCK1251B) was subjected to a 4-year restraint period under the initial SEBI order.
- Hon'ble SAT vide order dated September 7, 2026, granted a stay of the impugned order subject to the deposit of the full penalty within four weeks.
- SEBI confirmed via email on October 13, 2026, that Kanungo Financiers Limited paid the penalty amount in favor of SEBI, complying with the SAT directions.

## Regulatory Changes

Market access restraint previously imposed on Kanungo Financiers Limited has been stayed pursuant to the SAT order and fulfillment of penalty payment conditions.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must comply with the modified directions and stay orders as communicated by SEBI and exchange notifications.

## Important Dates

- Initial SEBI Order Date: June 30, 2026
- SAT Order Date: September 7, 2026
- Circular Date / SEBI Email Confirmation: October 13, 2026
- Next SAT Hearing Date: December 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables the appellant entity, Kanungo Financiers Limited, to resume market access in accordance with the SAT interim relief pending final disposal of Appeal No. 291 of 2026.
