## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued an update regarding the dissemination of the Unified Distilled File Format (UDiFF) Bhavcopy report for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment, following previous circulars dated September 3 and September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Exchange will commence dissemination of UDiFF Bhavcopy files for the EGR segment.
- Effective date for the commencement is September 25, 2026.
- Continuation of previous circulars NSE/EGR/76278 and NSE/EGR/76158.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; this is an operational rollout update for market data dissemination formats.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to take note of the scheduled dissemination date for system and operational readiness.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants dealing in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment can integrate and consume the UDiFF Bhavcopy files starting from the effective date.
