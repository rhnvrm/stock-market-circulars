## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the revocation of temporary suspension of subscription and change in maximum amount limits under certain schemes of HSBC Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Effective August 18, 2026, subscriptions are resumed through fresh/additional lumpsum, switch-in, Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) up to ₹2,00,000 per PAN, per month.

## Key Points

- Temporary suspension of subscription in designated overseas FOFs of HSBC Mutual Fund has been revoked.
- Resumption applies to fresh/additional purchases via lump sum, switch-ins, SIP, and STP.
- Investment limit is capped at ₹2,00,000 per PAN per month.
- Affected schemes include HSBC Asia Pacific (Ex Japan) Dividend Yield Fund, HSBC Brazil Fund, and HSBC Global Emerging Markets Fund.
- Subscriptions are allowed only up to the available headroom without breaching overseas investment limits as of February 1, 2022.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revocation of previous suspension notice dated December 3, 2025, for the designated overseas schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must adhere to the monthly investment cap of ₹2,00,000 per PAN for the specified schemes.
- AMC reserves the right to suspend processing if overseas investment limits are approached.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 18, 2026
- Circular Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors regain the ability to invest in specified HSBC overseas mutual fund schemes via SIP, STP, and lumpsum up to the specified cap, providing limited access to international asset exposure.
