## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the recommencement of trading in equity shares of Mcnally Bharat Engineering Company Limited effective from September 01, 2026, following a capital reduction pursuant to a Resolution Plan approved by the Hon'ble NCLT.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Mcnally Bharat Engineering Company Limited
- Symbol: MBECL
- Trading Effective Date: September 01, 2026
- Series: BE (Trade for Trade segment)
- ISIN: INE748A01024 (Temporary ISIN for demat shares: IN8*********)
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Total No. of Securities: 33,333,334
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Capital reduction and restructuring carried out pursuant to the NCLT order under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016.
- Entire shareholding of erstwhile promoters extinguished.
- Balance shareholding of public shareholders reduced as per the approved Resolution Plan.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities shall be available for trading in Series 'BE' and continue in the Trade-for-Trade segment pursuant to prior exchange notices.
- Newly allotted equity shares in dematerialized mode credited under temporary ISIN as per SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Allows investors and market participants to resume trading in the restructured equity shares of Mcnally Bharat Engineering Company Limited under the designated trade-for-trade segment.
