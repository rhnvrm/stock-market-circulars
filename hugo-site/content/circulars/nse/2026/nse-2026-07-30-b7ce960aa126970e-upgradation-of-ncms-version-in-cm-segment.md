## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the release of a new EXE version of the NSE Clearing Management System (NCMS), version 4.2.4, in the Capital Market segment. This release is a follow-up to the circular regarding OTP-based Two Factor Authentication and the NCMS version upgrade. The new version will be downloadable starting August 01, 2026, and will be effective from the trade date August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Version Upgrade:** NCMS is being upgraded to EXE version 4.2.4.
- **Availability:** The setup files will be available for download on August 01, 2026.
- **Effective Date:** The upgrade is mandatory and effective from trade date August 03, 2026.
- **Download Channels:** Available via the Connect2NSE portal (https://www.connect2nse.com/) and through the extranet path `/cmftp/common/NCMSSetup_424`.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory policy changes introduced in this circular. It is a technical implementation update relating to security standards (OTP-based 2FA) previously mandated.

## Compliance Requirements

- All Capital Market segment members must download and install the NCMS EXE version 4.2.4.
- Members should ensure deployment is complete before the start of trading operations on August 03, 2026, to prevent any login failures or clearing disruptions.

## Important Dates

- **August 01, 2026:** NCMS version 4.2.4 becomes available for download.
- **August 03, 2026:** New version becomes effective for trade operations.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Medium. Standard operations are unaffected provided the upgrade is performed, but failure to upgrade will result in inability to clear trades through NCMS.
- **Technology Impact:** Systems administrators must fetch and deploy the `NCMSSetup_424` installer across all active terminals using the NCMS system.
