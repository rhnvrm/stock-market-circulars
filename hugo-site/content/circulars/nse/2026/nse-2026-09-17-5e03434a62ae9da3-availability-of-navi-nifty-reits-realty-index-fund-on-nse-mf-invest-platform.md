## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing members about the availability of Navi Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Navi Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund of Navi Mutual Fund reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions.
- Facilities available include SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: September 18, 2026.
- Schemes included: Navi Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF959L01HT0) and Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF959L01HU8).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants are notified of the scheme availability for client transactions on the platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to transact in the Navi Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
