## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities listed in the annexures are subjected to specific surveillance actions including shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment, attracting a 100% margin, or moving across ESM stages.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (DCG, GFSTEELS, GRADIENTE, RADHIKAJWE) attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 16, 2026, on open positions as on September 15, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 15, 2026.
- Certain securities are eligible to move out of the framework or move between Stage I and Stage II (e.g., ATCENERGY, EUROTEXIND, KESORAMIND).
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Inclusion and movement of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework in conjunction with prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and segment shifts for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Margin/Segment Shift: September 16, 2026 (with open positions as on September 15, 2026)
- Effective Date for Stage II / Movement: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement mechanisms for the affected scrips are directly impacted due to Trade-for-Trade transfer and mandatory 100% margins.
