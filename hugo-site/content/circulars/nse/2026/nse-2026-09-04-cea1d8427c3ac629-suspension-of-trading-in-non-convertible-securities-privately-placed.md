## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) for two entities, effective September 7, 2026, pursuant to redemption.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects Non-Convertible Securities of Konkan Railway Corporation Limited and Small Industries Development Bank of India.
- Suspension Date: September 07, 2026.
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the suspension of trading in the specified securities under Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine cessation of trading for the specified ISINs due to redemption; no broader market impact.
