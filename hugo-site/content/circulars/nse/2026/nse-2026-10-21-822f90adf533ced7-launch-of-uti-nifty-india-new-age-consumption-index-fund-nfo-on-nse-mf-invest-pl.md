## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the launch of the UTI Nifty India New Age Consumption Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from October 22, 2026, to November 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: UTI Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: UTI Nifty India New Age Consumption Index Fund (Direct Plan - Growth & Regular Plan - Growth)
- NFO Open Date: October 22, 2026
- NFO Close Date: November 5, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- Allotment Date: November 11, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 11, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening: October 22, 2026
- NFO Closing: November 5, 2026
- Allotment Date: November 11, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: November 11, 2026 (03:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to invest in the new thematic index fund scheme through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
