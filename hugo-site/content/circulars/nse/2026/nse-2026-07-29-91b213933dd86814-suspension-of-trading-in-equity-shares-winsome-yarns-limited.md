## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in the equity shares of Winsome Yarns Limited (WINSOME) effective from July 31, 2026. This suspension is implemented in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- **Company Name**: Winsome Yarns Limited
- **Symbol**: WINSOME
- **Reason**: Suspension on account of capital reduction pursuant to the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) Order dated April 16, 2026.
- **Effective Date**: July 31, 2026 (i.e., from the closing hours of trading on July 30, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

No systemic regulatory changes have been made. The action is taken under the existing provisions of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A and is specific to Winsome Yarns Limited pursuant to an NCLT Order.

## Compliance Requirements

All trading members are instructed to take note of the suspension of trading in the security of Winsome Yarns Limited (WINSOME) and ensure that no transactions are executed in this symbol on or after the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **NCLT Order Date**: April 16, 2026
- **Circular Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Last Trading Day**: July 30, 2026
- **Effective Date of Suspension**: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Trading in the equity shares of Winsome Yarns Limited will be completely halted on the exchange.
- **Investor Impact**: Shareholders of Winsome Yarns Limited will be unable to trade or liquidate their positions on the exchange starting from July 31, 2026. This is a standard procedure following capital reduction processes approved by the NCLT.
