## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing members about a change in the ISIN code for equity shares of Shankara Buildpro Limited (BUILDPRO), effective from October 08, 2026. This change is due to the sub-division of face value from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shankara Buildpro Limited
- Symbol: BUILDPRO
- New ISIN: INE24OJ01029
- Reason: Sub-Division from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-
- Effective Date (Ex-date): October 08, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members and market participants must update their records and trading systems with the new ISIN code effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 05, 2026
- Ex-Date / Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update with minimal market impact, requiring standard ISIN updates across trading and settlement platforms.
