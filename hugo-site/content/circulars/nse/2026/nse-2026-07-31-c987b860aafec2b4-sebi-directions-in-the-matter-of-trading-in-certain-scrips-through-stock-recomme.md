## Summary

SEBI has issued an ex-parte interim order under Sections 11, 11B, and 11D of the SEBI Act, 1992, against seven individuals (Hemant Gupta, Rohan Gupta, Aniket Gupta, Sharon Gupta, Leana Gupta, Rajani Gupta, and Purvangi Gupta) in the matter of trading in certain scrips through unauthorized stock recommendations given on social media platforms (specifically 'X', formerly Twitter). During its internal surveillance, SEBI observed posts on accounts influencing the public to invest in various Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SME) platform scrips, accompanied by suspicious trading patterns.

## Key Points

- **Noticees Involved**: Hemant Gupta, Rohan Gupta, Aniket Gupta, Sharon Gupta, Leana Gupta, Rajani Gupta, and Purvangi Gupta.
- **Modus Operandi**: The operators used 'X' (Twitter) and other social media platforms to publish stock recommendations, influencing public investors to buy specific SME scrips. This artificially created volume and interest, allowing the operators and beneficiaries (Profit Makers) to make wrongful gains.
- **Regulatory Intervention**: Ex-parte interim order passed under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11B(1), and 11D of the SEBI Act, 1992.
- **Surveillance Observation**: SEBI's internal market surveillance highlighted artificial influence concentrated primarily on SME platform listed scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no direct systemic statutory or regulatory rule changes introduced by this specific enforcement order. However, it reinforces SEBI's regulatory stance and strict surveillance over unauthorized investment advice, market manipulation, and "finfluencers" on social media.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Investors & Market Intermediaries**: Must remain cautious of stock tips and recommendations circulated via unofficial channels, messaging apps, and social media platforms ('X', Telegram, etc.).
- **Noticees**: Must comply with the directions issued in the interim order, which typically include restrictions on accessing the securities market and freezing of bank/demat accounts up to the amount of wrongful gains.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: Immediate (ex-parte interim order issued August 12, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Heightened scrutiny and surveillance of SME platform listings and social media activity. It acts as a strong deterrent against pump-and-dump schemes orchestrated via social media platforms.
- **Operational Impact**: Stockbrokers and depository participants may be required to freeze demat accounts and trading terminals of the named Noticees in compliance with SEBI's directions.
