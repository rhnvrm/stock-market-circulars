## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the transfer of Sonaselection India Limited from the trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) pursuant to SEBI guidelines and further to exchange circular no. NSE/CML/76512.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sonaselection India Limited
- Symbol: SONA
- Transfer from Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE) to Rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: October 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, and previous Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76512 dated September 23, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the shift in trading segment for SONA effective from October 09, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for Sonaselection India Limited shares (SONA), improving trading liquidity and operational flexibility.
