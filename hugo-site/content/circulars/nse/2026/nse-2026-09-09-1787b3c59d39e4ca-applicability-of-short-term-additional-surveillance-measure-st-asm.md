## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I effective September 11, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%.
- The margin requirements apply to all open positions as on September 10, 2026, and new positions created from September 11, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is a surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I surveillance action with enhanced margin requirements for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure the applicable margin rates (50% or existing, capped at 100%) are maintained for open and new positions in the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I.
