## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Alufluoride Limited has been shortlisted under ESM Stage - I, attracting a minimum 100% margin and shifting from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage - I shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 14, 2026 on all open positions as on October 13, 2026 and new positions created from October 14, 2026.
- Affected securities shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 14, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) criteria leading to margin modifications and trading segment transfers for identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and monitor open positions and settlement shifts for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 14, 2026 (for margin applicability and trade-for-trade shift)

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities face heightened margin obligations and trade-for-trade settlement, which may impact short-term liquidity and trading volumes.
