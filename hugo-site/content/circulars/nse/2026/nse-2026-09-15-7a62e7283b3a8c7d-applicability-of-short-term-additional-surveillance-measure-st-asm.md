## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I, requiring a 50% margin or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) on open and new positions.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I include ASHOKAMET, CORDSCABLE, DEEP, FONEBOX, OSWALSEEDS, PARTYCRUS, SAWALIYA, SRGHFL, TBI, TCC, VEDAVAAG, and VGL.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on September 16, 2026, and new positions created from September 17, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is a surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I framework provisions for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure the applicable margin rates of 50% or higher (capped at 100%) are levied on all open and new positions for the listed securities effective from September 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2026
- Open Positions Evaluated As Of: September 16, 2026
- Circular Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for trading in the shortlisted securities, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for these counters.
