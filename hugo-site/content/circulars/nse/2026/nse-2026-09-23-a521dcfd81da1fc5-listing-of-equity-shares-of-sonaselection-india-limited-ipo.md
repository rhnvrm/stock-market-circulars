## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Sonaselection India Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO). Trading will commence from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: SONA
- Series: BE (will subsequently shift to Series EQ)
- ISIN: INE0LLZ01011
- Issue Price: Rs. 99/-
- Face Value/Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Number of Securities: 56,828,681
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: September 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment in Series 'BE' and will subsequently be shifted to Series 'EQ' as per SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to note the pre-open session applicability for IPO and other category scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Sonaselection India Limited equity shares on the NSE Capital Market segment.
