## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to various securities based on set criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage I is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. September 08, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage II is 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. September 08, 2026.
- Changes apply to all open positions as on September 07, 2026, and new positions created from September 08, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must maintain the applicable increased margin rates (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: September 08, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on September 07, 2026).
- Circular Date: September 04, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased capital requirements for trading in the shortlisted securities due to higher margin obligations, potentially affecting liquidity and trading volumes for these scrips.
