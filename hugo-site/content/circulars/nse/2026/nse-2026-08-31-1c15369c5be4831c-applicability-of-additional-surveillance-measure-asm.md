## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Certain securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) framework, attracting a 100% margin rate and potential shifts to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 03, 2026, on all open positions as on September 02, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV, such as HFCL) shall be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. September 03, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term ASM (LTASM) Stage - I for Allcargo Logistics Limited, Gayatri Projects Limited, and GVP Infotech Limited.
- Shift of HFCL Limited from Long-Term ASM Stage - I to Stage - IV (Criteria VII) with settlement movement to Trade-for-Trade (Series: BE) on a T+3 basis.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure 100% margin is maintained on open positions and new positions for the shortlisted securities as specified.
- Trading members must note the segment shift for applicable scrips from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 31, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions / Margins / Segment Shifts: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, higher margin requirements (100%), and trade-for-trade settlement where applicable, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for these counters.
