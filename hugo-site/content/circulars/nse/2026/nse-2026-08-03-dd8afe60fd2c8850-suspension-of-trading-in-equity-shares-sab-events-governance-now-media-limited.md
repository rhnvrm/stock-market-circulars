## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in the equity shares of **Sab Events & Governance Now Media Limited** (Symbol: SABEVENTS) effective from August 05, 2026 (closing hours of trading on August 04, 2026). This suspension is enacted on account of capital reduction pursuant to the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) Order dated July 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Sab Events & Governance Now Media Limited
- **Symbol:** SABEVENTS
- **Action:** Suspension of trading in equity shares.
- **Reason:** Capital reduction pursuant to NCLT Order dated July 10, 2026.
- **Regulatory Basis:** Issued under Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no systemic regulatory changes introduced. This is a security-specific suspension under Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A following NCLT-approved restructuring.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Must cease all trading activities and executions in the equity shares of SABEVENTS from the effective suspension date.
- **Investors:** Positions should be managed or closed prior to the close of trading hours on August 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NCLT Order Date:** July 10, 2026
- **Last Trading Day:** August 04, 2026
- **Effective Date of Suspension:** August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity & Trading:** Active public trading and liquidity for SABEVENTS will be unavailable on the NSE platform beginning August 05, 2026.
- **Shareholder Impact:** Existing equity shares are suspended from the exchange platform pending the implementation of the capital reduction scheme.
