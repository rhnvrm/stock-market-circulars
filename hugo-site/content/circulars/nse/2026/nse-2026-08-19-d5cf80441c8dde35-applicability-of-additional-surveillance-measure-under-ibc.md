## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria under ASM for companies as per IBC effective August 20, 2026, include Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited (KTL).
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned company.
- The ASM IBC framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code framework for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are required to note the updated list of securities under ASM IBC and ensure compliance with respective trading and surveillance restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 20, 2026
- Circular Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlists under ASM IBC is subject to enhanced surveillance monitoring and applicable margin/trading controls.
