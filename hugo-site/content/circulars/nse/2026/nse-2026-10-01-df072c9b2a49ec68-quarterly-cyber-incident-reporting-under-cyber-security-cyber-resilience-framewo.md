## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the Quarterly Cyber Incident reporting under the Cyber Security & Cyber Resilience Framework for Regulated Entities (REs). All trading members are required to report cyber incidents for the quarter ending September 30, 2026, on or before October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reporting period covers the quarter ending September 30, 2026.
- Submission deadline is October 15, 2026.
- Online submission path: ENIT > ENIT-NEW-TRADE > Trade > Incident Report > Quarterly Report Submission.
- Non-compliance may lead to financial disincentives, penalties, or disciplinary actions.
- For support, members can contact DL-SYSCYB@nse.co.in.

## Regulatory Changes

Adherence to the Cyber Security & Cyber Resilience framework mandated by SEBI circular SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_CSC_EXT/P/CIR/2024/113 and Exchange circular NSE/INSP/63502.

## Compliance Requirements

- Admin needs to assign the "ENIT New trade" role to a designated officer in the Member Portal.
- Confirm whether any breach was observed by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
- For 'No' breaches, complete 'Nil Submission'.
- Save details, generate PDF report, and upload it on the portal.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Submission Deadline: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure timely quarterly reporting of cyber incidents and nil submissions where applicable to avoid penalties and disciplinary actions.
