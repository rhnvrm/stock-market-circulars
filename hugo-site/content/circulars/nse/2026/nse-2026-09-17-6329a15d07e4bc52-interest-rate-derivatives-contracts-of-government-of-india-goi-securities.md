## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the introduction of Interest Rate Futures (FUTIRC) and Options (OPTIRC) contracts for specific Government of India (GOI) securities, effective September 21, 2026, based on SEBI master circular guidelines and exchange provisions.

## Key Points

- New Interest Rate Futures and Options contracts introduced for symbols: 653GS2031 (Coupon: 6.53%, Maturity: 07-Sep-31), 657GS2033 (Coupon: 6.57%, Maturity: 17-Aug-33), and 706GS2041 (Coupon: 7.06%, Maturity: 27-Jul-41).
- Lot Size: Rs 2 lakhs face value of GOI securities equivalent to 2000 units.
- Quantity Freeze: 1251 lots or greater (orders up to 1250 lots allowed).
- Members are required to download contract files from extranet paths and update local databases before trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of new derivative contracts in accordance with item no. 1.13 of Exchange consolidated circular ref. no. 67722 and SEBI master circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD2/PoD-2/CIR/P/2024/181.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must download cd_contract.gz, cd_spd_contract.gz, and MII contract & spd files from extranet paths (/cdsftp/cdscommon) and update local databases prior to the go-live date.
- Orders must be placed in terms of number of lots adhering to the specified quantity freeze limits.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands trading opportunities and hedging instruments for market participants dealing in Government of India securities within the currency derivatives segment.
