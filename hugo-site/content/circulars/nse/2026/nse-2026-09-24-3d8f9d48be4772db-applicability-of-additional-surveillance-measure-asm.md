## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for specified securities. Effective September 29, 2026, an applicable rate of margin of 100% will be levied on all open positions as on September 28, 2026, and new positions created thereafter. Furthermore, securities qualifying under criteria VII (shifted to Stage IV) are moved from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Key Points

- 100% margin applicable w.e.f. September 29, 2026, on open positions as of September 28, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Stage I: CRESTO TECHNO LIMITED (CRESTO), Pearl Polymers Limited (PEARLPOLY), and VARVEE GLOBAL LIMITED (VGL).
- Securities shifted from Long-Term ASM Stage I to Stage IV: Bodal Chemicals Limited (BODALCHEM).
- Securities under Stage IV shift from Rolling Settlement (EQ) to Trade-for-Trade (BE) on a T+3 basis.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework provisions to curb excessive price volatility and enhance market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure collection of 100% margin on open and new positions for the affected scrips and account for trade-for-trade settlement requirements where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin/Settlement Changes: September 29, 2026
- Inclusion/Movement Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High operational and liquidity impact for investors and trading members holding positions in the affected scrips due to 100% margin requirements and transfer to the trade-for-trade segment.
