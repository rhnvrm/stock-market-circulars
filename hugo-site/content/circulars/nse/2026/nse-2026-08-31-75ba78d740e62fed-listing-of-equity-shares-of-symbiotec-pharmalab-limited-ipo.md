## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Symbiotec Pharmalab Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), effective from September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** SYMBIOTEC
- **Name of the Company:** Symbiotec Pharmalab Limited
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE899I01028
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 2/- per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 988/- per share
- **No. of Securities:** 64,256,112
- **Effective Date:** September 01, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 01, 2026
- **Various Lock-in Expiry Dates:** September 26, 2026, November 25, 2026, February 28, 2027, and February 28, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of Symbiotec Pharmalab Limited equity shares on the NSE Capital Market segment.
