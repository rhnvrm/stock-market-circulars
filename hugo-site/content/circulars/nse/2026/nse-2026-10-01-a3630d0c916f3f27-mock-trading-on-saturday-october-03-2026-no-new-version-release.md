## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited is conducting a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives (F&O) segment on Saturday, October 03, 2026. The session includes trading from both the Primary Site and Disaster Recovery (DR) Site, with specific guidelines regarding NEAT+ software versions and order clearing.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, October 03, 2026.
- Conducted in two sessions: Trading Session-1 from Primary Site and Trading Session-2 from DR Site.
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 must be used explicitly for the revised Pre-Open framework (/faoftp/faocommon/NEATPlus790).
- Existing NEAT+ version 7.8.9 will be discontinued on October 10, 2026 (mock).
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- Mock trades will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- UCC/PAN validation will be skipped during contingency time for order entry.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory policy changes; routine exchange mock trading session conducted in compliance with exchange and regulatory requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are required to participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 05, 2026. Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 03, 2026
- NEAT+ v7.8.9 Discontinuation (Mock): October 10, 2026
- Live Resumption Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact; ensures system readiness, connectivity testing, and disaster recovery preparedness for trading members with zero financial obligations.
