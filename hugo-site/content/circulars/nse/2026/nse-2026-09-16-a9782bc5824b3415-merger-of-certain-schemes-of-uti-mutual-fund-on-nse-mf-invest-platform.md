## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund has announced the merger of UTI Children’s Hybrid Fund (Merging Scheme) into UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund (Surviving Scheme), effective October 23, 2026. This merger is undertaken pursuant to SEBI Master Circular guidelines requiring a single scheme per category.

## Key Points

- Merging Scheme: UTI Children’s Hybrid Fund
- Surviving Scheme: UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund
- Effective Date of Merger: October 23, 2026 (or immediately following Business Day)
- Exit Option Period: September 21, 2026 to October 23, 2026 (up to 3:00 P.M.)
- No new scheme is created as a result of the merger, and there are no changes to the fundamental attributes of the surviving scheme.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI Master Circular dated March 20, 2026, which permits only one scheme per category.
- Implemented under Regulation 22(9)(c) of the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026 relating to changes in fundamental attributes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Unitholders of both merging and surviving schemes who do not agree with the merger are provided an exit option without any exit load during the specified exit window.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice-cum-Addendum: September 11, 2026
- Exit Period Opening Date: September 21, 2026
- Exit Period Closing Date: October 23, 2026 (by 3:00 P.M.)
- Effective Date of Merger: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Unitholders have the option to exit without exit load or continue their investments seamlessly under the surviving scheme which maintains similar underlying asset allocation and portfolio characteristics.
