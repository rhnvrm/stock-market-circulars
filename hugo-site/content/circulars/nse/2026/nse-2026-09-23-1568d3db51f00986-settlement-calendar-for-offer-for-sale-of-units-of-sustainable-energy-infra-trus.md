## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar and timings for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of units of Sustainable Energy Infra Trust, referencing circular NCL/CMPT/73996 dated April 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement for securities in the OFS follows existing rules for secondary market transactions.
- Order date for both non-retail (IS) categories is 24-SEP-2026.
- Settlement date for both categories is 25-SEP-2026.
- Applicable securities transaction tax (STT) and stamp duty will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Follows established secondary market settlement protocols for OFS.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, custodians, and members must adhere to the specified order dates, custodial confirmation times, and pay-in/pay-out schedules.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: 24-SEP-2026
- Custodial Confirmation: 24-SEP-2026 (for 100% margin category) and 25-SEP-2026 (for 0% margin category)
- Funds Pay-in: T+1 day by 11:00 am (25-SEP-2026)
- Securities & Funds Pay-out: T+1 day by 01:30 pm (25-SEP-2026)
- Settlement Date: 25-SEP-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational execution and timeline clarity for members participating in the Sustainable Energy Infra Trust OFS.
