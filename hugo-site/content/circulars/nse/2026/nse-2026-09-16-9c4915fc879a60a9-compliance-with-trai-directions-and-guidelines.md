## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular advising all trading members to ensure strict compliance with various TRAI directions and guidelines regarding telecommunication communications and security.

## Key Points

- Integration with the Department of Telecommunications' (DoT) Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL) for periodic reconciliation and risk workflows.
- Mandatory use of the 1600 numbering series exclusively for service and transactional calls, prohibiting promotional calls.
- Compliance with TRAI's direction on pre-tagging variable fields in SMS content templates ahead of blocker mode activation.
- Undertaking consumer awareness campaigns on 1600 and 140 numbering series.

## Regulatory Changes

Adherence to TRAI guidelines concerning telecommunication resource usage, MNRL integration, and SMS scrubbing/pre-tagging.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must integrate MNRL checks into risk, authentication, and communication workflows, review and pre-tag SMS content templates used during the preceding 90 days, and use dedicated numbering series correctly.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 16, 2026
- Reference to TRAI Direction: November 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Entities must update their communication systems and SMS templates to prevent message delivery failures once blocker mode is activated.
