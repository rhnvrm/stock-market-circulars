## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the revision of Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL), Participant, Institutional, and Non-Institutional Client Limits for the symbol BUILDPRO under the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) Scheme for the month of October 2026. The revised limits come into effect from October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: BUILDPRO
- Scheme: Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLB)
- Effective Date: October 09, 2026
- Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) increased from 1,450,678 to 7,253,394 shares.
- Institutional Client Limit increased from 145,067 to 725,339 shares.
- Participant Limit increased from 145,067 to 725,339 shares.
- Non-Institutional Client Limit increased from 14,506 to 72,533 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

Position limits for symbol BUILDPRO in the SLB scheme have been revised upwards in pursuance of circular (NCL/CMPT/73999) dated April 30, 2026, and partial modification of circular (NCL/CMPT/76656) dated September 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians are advised to take note of the revised position limits and ensure compliance while executing transactions in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme for BUILDPRO.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The upward revision of MWPL and client position limits allows for higher trading and borrowing/lending capacity for BUILDPRO in the SLB segment, accommodating increased market interest.
