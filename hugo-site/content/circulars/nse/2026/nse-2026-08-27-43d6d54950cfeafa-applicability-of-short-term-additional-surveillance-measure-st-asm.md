## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) to certain securities. The framework includes securities shortlisted under Stage I, Stage II, and those moving between stages effective August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin for securities under ST-ASM Stage I shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100% w.e.f. August 31, 2026, on all open positions as on August 28, 2026, and new positions created from August 31, 2026.
- Applicable margin rate for securities under ST-ASM Stage II shall be 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. August 31, 2026, on all open positions as on August 28, 2026, and new positions created from August 31, 2026.
- Securities shortlisted in ST-ASM Stage I: Aries Agro Limited, Genesys International Corporation Limited, Manav Infra Projects Limited, Nephro Care India Limited, Oriental Rail Infrastructure Limited, Advit Jewels Limited (RAMBHAJO), and S&S Power Switchgears Limited.
- Securities moving from Stage I to Stage II: GSM Foils Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure framework to monitor excessive price volatility and market manipulation in designated stocks.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100% as applicable) for open and new positions in the specified securities effective from August 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance criteria: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin applicability: August 31, 2026 (on open positions as on August 28, 2026, and new positions created thereafter)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements will likely impact liquidity and trading volumes in the shortlisted securities by discouraging speculative trading.
