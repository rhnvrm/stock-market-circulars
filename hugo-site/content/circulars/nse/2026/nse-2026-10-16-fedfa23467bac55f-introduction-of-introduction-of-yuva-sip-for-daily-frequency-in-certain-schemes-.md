## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the introduction of 'YUVA SIP' for daily frequency and revision in minimum instalment amount and instalments under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) for eligible schemes of Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund, effective October 19, 2026 on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Key Points

- Introduction of YUVA SIP facility by Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund.
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026.
- Eligible Schemes: Baroda BNP Paribas Flexi Cap Fund and Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Advantage Fund (Growth Option).
- Revised Minimum Amount & Instalments for Daily Frequency: Rs. 10/- and 1300 Instalments (previously Rs. 500/- and 6 instalments).
- Minimum tenure commitment: 5 years.
- Available in both Statement of Account (SOA) mode and Demat mode.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of terms for the existing Daily SIP facility under eligible schemes of Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund.
- Minimum SIP instalment amount reduced to Rs. 10/- with a minimum commitment of 1300 instalments (5 years).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the updated minimum instalment amounts and tenure requirements for daily SIPs in the specified Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Encourages disciplined daily investing through small-value SIP instalments for retail investors in Baroda BNP Paribas Flexi Cap Fund and Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Advantage Fund.
