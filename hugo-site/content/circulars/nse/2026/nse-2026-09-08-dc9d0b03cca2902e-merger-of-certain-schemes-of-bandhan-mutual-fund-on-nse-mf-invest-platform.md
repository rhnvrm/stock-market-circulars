## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the merger of Bandhan CRISIL-IBX 10:90 Gilt + SDL Index – Dec 2029 Fund (Merging scheme) into Bandhan Short Duration Fund (Surviving scheme) of Bandhan Mutual Fund. The merger is effective after the close of business hours on September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Merging Scheme: Bandhan CRISIL-IBX 10:90 Gilt + SDL Index – Dec 2029 Fund
- Surviving Scheme: Bandhan Short Duration Fund
- Effective Date: After business hours on September 11, 2026.
- Exit Load Window: Investors not in favour of the merger can redeem or switch to other schemes without exit load from August 13, 2026, to September 11, 2026 (up to 3.00 pm on September 11, 2026) through the NSE MF Invest platform.
- SIP/XSIP/SWP handling: Existing SIP/XSIP/SWP will continue to be processed in the merging schemes during the exit load period and will shift to the surviving schemes after business hours on September 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

SEBI accorded its 'No Objection' for the merger vide communication dated July 21, 2026. The merger does not entail any change in the fundamental attribute of the Surviving Scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors must take note of the merger timeline, exit load-free window, and processing changes for SIP/SWP/STP/XSIP instructions.

## Important Dates

- Exit Load Free Period Start: August 13, 2026
- Exit Load Free Period End / Cut-off: September 11, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM)
- Effective Date of Merger: September 11, 2026 (after close of business hours)

## Impact Assessment

Unitholders of the merging scheme will have their investments transitioned to the surviving scheme unless redeemed or switched during the designated exit window without exit load.
