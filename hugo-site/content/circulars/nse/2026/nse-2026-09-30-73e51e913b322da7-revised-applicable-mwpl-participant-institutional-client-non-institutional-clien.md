## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the revised applicable Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL), Participant limits, Institutional Client limits, and Non-Institutional Client limits for various securities and ETFs under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS).

## Key Points

- Covers updated share quantity limits for MWPL, Participant, Institutional Client, and Non-Institutional Client categories.
- Applies to numerous listed equities and Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) including 360ONE, 3MINDIA, ADANIENT, ADANIPORTS, and others.
- Ensures compliance with regulatory position thresholds under SLBS framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic revision of position limits for securities participating in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) based on market capitalization and trading metrics.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members, clearing members, and institutional/non-institutional clients must adhere to the revised position limits and participant limits while executing transactions under SLBS.

## Important Dates

Effective immediately as specified in the circular.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly trading and borrowing/lending activities in derivatives and SLBS segments by updating allowable position sizes for market participants and clients.
