## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Rays of Belief Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) effective September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Rays of Belief Limited
- Symbol: MOMSBELIEF
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: September 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Movement of securities pursuant to SEBI guidelines and exchange circular reference no. NSE/CML/76229 dated September 07, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members need to note the change in series for trading in the security.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Allows normal rolling settlement for the stock, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility for market participants.
