## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the commencement of trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Jaykay Enterprises Limited. Trading will take place in the Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors from September 07, 2026, to September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Jaykay Enterprises Limited
- Symbol: JAYKAY-RE1
- ISIN: INE903A20025
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 20,517,196
- Trading Period for Renunciation: From September 07, 2026, to September 15, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 16, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs, highlighting their unique ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes must include a prominent disclaimer stating that purchase of REs grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before the issue closes.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the Issue Closing Date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Commencement of Trading: September 07, 2026
- Issue Closing Date / Renunciation End Date: September 15, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates active trading and renunciation of rights entitlements for eligible shareholders of Jaykay Enterprises Limited in the Trade to Trade segment.
