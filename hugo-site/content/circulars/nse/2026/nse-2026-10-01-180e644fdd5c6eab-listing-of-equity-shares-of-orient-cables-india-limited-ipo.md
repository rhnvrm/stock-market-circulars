## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and commencement of trading of equity shares of Orient Cables (India) Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO), effective October 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: Orient Cables (India) Limited
- Class of Security: Equity Shares
- Total Share Capital: 113,799,705 equity shares
- Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 91,851,184 shares (80.71%)
- Public Holding: 21,948,521 shares (19.29%)
- Shareholding Pattern filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a standard corporate listing action following the successful completion of an IPO.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company is required to comply with all continuous listing obligations under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity to shareholders and enables public trading of Orient Cables (India) Limited equity shares on the NSE exchange platform.
