## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced changes in price bands for 3,561 securities, effective from October 08, 2026, pursuant to Capital Market Segment Regulation Part-A, 2.5.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised across various categories: 42 securities at 2%, 566 securities at 5%, 204 securities at 10%, 545 securities with no band, 2,203 securities at 20%, and 1 security at 40%.
- Securities on which derivative products are available shall continue to have a daily operating range of 10%.
- Price bands of 10% (either way) will be applicable on all close ended Mutual Funds.
- Price bands in the Limited Physical Market shall remain identical to those applicable in the normal market.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revisions in daily operating price bands for specified securities under surveillance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trading systems are updated with the revised price bands effective from October 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 08, 2026
- **Circular Date:** October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants must adhere to the updated price limits when placing orders for the affected securities.
