## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for several companies, effective from September 01, 2026. These shares were allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026.
- Companies involved include Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, AU Small Finance Bank Limited, Godrej Agrovet Limited, HDB Financial Services Limited, IndusInd Bank Limited, Intellect Design Arena Limited, Laxmi Dental Limited, Piramal Finance Limited, Tejas Networks Limited, and RBL Bank Limited.
- Securities admitted: Further equity shares allotted under ESOP.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, further issues of securities are admitted to dealings and respective lot sizes are specified.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the listed securities shall be identified by designated codes and carried out in the specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action with minimal direct market impact, representing regular issuance and admission of ESOP shares.
