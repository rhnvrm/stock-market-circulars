## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for specified securities, including new inclusions and stage migrations effective September 29 and September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auro Impex & Chemicals Limited (AUROIMPEX) and The Ugar Sugar Works Limited (UGARSUGAR) are shortlisted under ESM Stage - I, attracting a 100% margin w.e.f. September 30, 2026, and shifting from Rolling Settlement (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST).
- Delphi World Money Limited (DELPHIFX) and Aion-Tech Solutions Limited (GOLDTECH) are moving from ESM Stage - I to Stage - II with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities qualifying under ESM are subjected to stricter trading controls, including margin requirements and movement to the trade-for-trade settlement segment or periodic call auctions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open positions and new positions created for shortlisted securities, and take note of the segment and price band changes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II restrictions and inclusion criteria: September 29, 2026 / September 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading operations for the affected scrips will be impacted due to trade-for-trade settlement and heightened margin requirements.
