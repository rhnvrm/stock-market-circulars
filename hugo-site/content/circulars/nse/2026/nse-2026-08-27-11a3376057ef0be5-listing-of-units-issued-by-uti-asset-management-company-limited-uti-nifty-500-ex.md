## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission to dealings of units issued by UTI Asset Management Company Limited under the UTI Nifty 500 Exchange Traded Fund, effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: UTI Nifty 500 Exchange Traded Fund
- Symbol: NIF500BETA
- ISIN: INF789F1AG76
- Number of Units: 1,922,950
- Date of Allotment: August 25, 2026
- Face Value / Paid Up Value: Rs. 10/- per unit
- Issue Price: Rs. 37.85
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to trading regulations Part A of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market).

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of the new ETF units on the NSE capital market segment.
