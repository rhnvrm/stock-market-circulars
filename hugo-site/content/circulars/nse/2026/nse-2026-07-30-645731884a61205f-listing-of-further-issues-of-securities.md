## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular notifying the admission and listing of further securities of 14 companies, effective from July 31, 2026. These additional shares have been primarily allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOPs) or other corporate actions. The listed companies include Affle 3i Limited, Asian Energy Services Limited, Axis Bank Limited, Bliss GVS Pharma Limited, Cartrade Tech Limited, and ESAF Small Finance Bank Limited, among others.

## Key Points

- **Admitted Securities**: The further issues of equity shares for 14 specified companies have been admitted to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment starting July 31, 2026.
- **Trading and Lot Size**: Trading in these securities will be conducted in the designated lot sizes and identified by their specific security/ISIN codes as detailed in the Annexure of the circular.
- **Pari Passu Status**: Newly allotted shares rank pari passu with the existing equity shares of the respective companies (e.g., Affle 3i Limited, Asian Energy Services Limited, Axis Bank Limited, Bliss GVS Pharma Limited, Cartrade Tech Limited).
- **Lock-in Details**: Most allotments have no lock-in requirements, with exceptions like Bliss GVS Pharma Limited, which has a lock-in period until January 06, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- No structural regulatory changes are introduced. This is a routine administrative listing under:
  - **Regulation 3.1.1** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
  - **Regulation 2.5.5** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members**: Ensure compliance with the designated security codes and respective market lot sizes for trading on the National Stock Exchange system.
- **For Issuers**: Ensure timely compliance with dispatch, allotment, and listing requirements as per statutory guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates (Selected)**:
  - Bliss GVS Pharma Limited: July 07, 2026
  - Asian Energy Services Limited: July 20, 2026
  - Cartrade Tech Limited: July 23, 2026
  - Affle 3i Limited: July 25, 2026
  - Axis Bank Limited: July 28, 2026
- **Circular Date**: July 30, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing**: July 31, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry (Bliss GVS Pharma Limited)**: January 06, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Extremely low. The introduction of additional shares under ESOPs increases the outstanding share count marginally, which might cause minor dilution but does not impact broader market operations or individual stock price trends significantly.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Trading systems will update to support the additional shares under the existing ISINs.
