## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, September 12, 2026. No new software version is being released for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, September 12, 2026.
- Trading session conducted from the primary site with specific timings for pre-open, normal market, T+0, auction, and closing sessions.
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 must be used explicitly for CAS, while version 7.8.9 is slated for discontinuation on October 10, 2026 (mock).
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to participate in the mock trading session to test connectivity and systems. Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- Mock Date: September 12, 2026
- NEAT+ 7.8.9 Discontinuation Date (Mock): October 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out. Members can utilize this session to test trading software and connectivity parameters.
