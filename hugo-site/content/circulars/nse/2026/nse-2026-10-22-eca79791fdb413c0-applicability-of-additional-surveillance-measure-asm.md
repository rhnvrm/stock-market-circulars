## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) framework. Specific securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Long-Term ASM Stage-I effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I effective October 23, 2026:
  - 21st Century Management Services Limited (Symbol: 21STCENMGM, ISIN: INE253B01015)
  - Turtlemint Fintech Solutions Limited (Symbol: TURTLEMINT, ISIN: INE0OC301013) - Moved from STASM to LTASM framework
  - Voler Car Limited (Symbol: VOLERCAR, ISIN: INE241X01014)
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 27, 2026, on all open positions as on October 26, 2026, and new positions created from October 27, 2026, onwards.
- Shortlisting of securities under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specified scrips under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) Stage-I.
- Imposition of 100% margin requirement on open positions and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open positions and new positions for the affected securities starting October 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ASM Shortlisting: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin Requirement: October 27, 2026 (on open positions as on October 26, 2026, and new positions onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants and investors dealing in the affected securities (21STCENMGM, TURTLEMINT, VOLERCAR) will face heightened margin requirements (100%), impacting liquidity and trading positioning in these scrips.
