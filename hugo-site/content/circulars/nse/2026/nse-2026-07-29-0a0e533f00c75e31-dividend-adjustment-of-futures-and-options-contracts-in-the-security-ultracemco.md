## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in the security UltraTech Cement Limited (ULTRACEMCO) on account of its dividend announcement. In accordance with SEBI guidelines, NSE will adjust the strike prices and base prices of all active F&O contracts of ULTRACEMCO starting from the ex-date, July 30, 2026. The corporate action is a dividend of Rs 240/- per share on a face value of Rs 10/-.

## Key Points

- **Underlying Security:** UltraTech Cement Limited (Symbol: ULTRACEMCO).
- **Corporate Action:** Dividend of Rs 240/- per share (Face Value of Rs 10/-).
- **Ex-date & Effective Date:** July 30, 2026.
- **Contract Adjustments:** Active options strike prices and futures base prices will be adjusted downward by the dividend amount of Rs 240/- on the ex-date.
- **Rounding Off:** Adjusted strike/base prices and lot sizes appearing in decimals will be rounded off to the nearest tick size and nearest integer respectively.
- **File Updates:** Members are required to load updated contract and spread files (NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz and NSE_FO_spdcontract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz) prior to trading on the ex-date.

## Regulatory Changes

The adjustments are performed in pursuance of SEBI guidelines prescribed from time to time for adjustments to futures and options contracts on the announcement of corporate actions. No new regulatory changes are introduced; this is an execution of existing regulatory frameworks for corporate action price adjustments.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Application Updates:** Members must load the updated contract.gz / MII contract file (NSE_FO_contract_30072026.csv.gz) and spread file (NSE_FO_spdcontract_30072026.csv.gz) on their trading applications before the market opens on July 30, 2026.
- **Extranet Server Access:** These files can be downloaded from the directory faoftp/faocommon on the Extranet server or from the NSE website path: https://www.nseindia.com/all-reports-derivatives.
- **Position Adjustments:** Clearing members and participants must follow the methodology for position adjustments as separately intimated by their respective Clearing Corporations.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Ex-date and Effective Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Adjustment Effective Date:** July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Impact:** F&O contracts for ULTRACEMCO will open with revised strike and base prices on July 30, 2026, reflecting the dividend reduction of Rs 240 per share. For example, the old strike of 9600.00 for expiry 25-AUG-2026 will be revised to 9360.00.
- **Pricing Impact:** All option strike prices across all available expiries (such as August 25, 2026) will be adjusted. Option holders/writers should review their positions and order books.
- **Operational Impact:** Brokerage systems and risk management applications must be updated with the revised contract files prior to trading hours to ensure accurate execution and margin calculations.
