## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) for Nomura Capital (India) Private Limited, effective from October 6, 2026, due to redemption.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Nomura Capital (India) Private Limited
- **ISIN:** INE357L07457
- **Suspension Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Reason for Suspension:** Redemption
- **Regulation:** Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; administrative suspension reflecting debt security redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the suspension of trading in the specified security.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 05, 2026
- **Suspension Date:** October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact as the suspension is due to the redemption of privately placed non-convertible securities.
