## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding SEBI's modifications to the regulatory framework for Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs). The updates incorporate stakeholder suggestions to promote ease of doing business by permitting OBPPs to offer IFSCA-regulated products and specific tax-saving bonds under Section 54EC of the Income Tax Act, 1961 or Section 85 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

## Key Points

- Reference to SEBI Circular No. HO/17/11/(2)2026-DDHS-POD1/I/18769/2026 dated August 14, 2026.
- OBPPs are now permitted to offer products or securities or services regulated by the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA).
- OBPPs are permitted to offer bonds issued under Section 54EC of the Income Tax Act, 1961 or Section 85 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.
- Clause 5.2 of Chapter XXI of the SEBI NCS Master Circular dated October 15, 2025, has been modified accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

- Chapter XXI of the SEBI Master Circular for issue and listing of Non-Convertible Securities, Securitised Debt Instruments, Security Receipts, Municipal Debt Securities and Commercial Paper is updated.
- Clause 5.2.5 expanded to include products regulated by financial sector regulators including SEBI, RBI, IRDAI, IFSCA, or PFRDA.
- Clause 5.2.6 added to include bonds issued under section 54EC of the Income Tax Act, 1961 or Section 85 of Income-tax Act, 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and entities operating as OBPPs must take note of the modified framework and ensure compliance.
- For IFSCA-regulated products, OBPPs shall offer them in the manner specified and ensure appropriate grievance redressal mechanisms are established.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 17, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational flexibility for Online Bond Platform Providers.
- Promotes ease of doing business by broadening the scope of permissible financial products available on online platforms.
