## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from October 06, 2026, following preferential warrant conversions and ESOP allotments.

## Key Points

- Further equity shares admitted to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment with effect from October 06, 2026.
- Includes companies such as Akme Fintrade (India) Limited, Angel One Limited, DCB Bank Limited, IndusInd Bank Limited, and Jaro Institute of Technology Management and Research Limited.
- Allotments comprise preferential warrant conversions and employee stock options (ESOPs).
- Market lot size is set to 1 with Pari Passu ranking across securities.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate securities listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must trade the listed securities using their designated security codes and respective lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026
- Allotment Dates: Spanning June to September 2026 depending on the company.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update enabling liquidity and trading for newly allotted shares.
