## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities of Power Finance Corporation Limited (ISIN: INE134E08II2) with effect from July 29, 2026, due to redemption.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Power Finance Corporation Limited
- **ISIN:** INE134E08II2
- **Suspension Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Reason for Suspension:** Redemption
- **Authority:** Issued under Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced in this circular. The action is taken in compliance with Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

All trading members are required to note the suspension and cease trading of the specified Non-Convertible Securities under ISIN INE134E08II2 effective July 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 28, 2026
- **Suspension Effective Date:** July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine operational suspension of matured/redeemed debt instruments.
- **Operational Impact:** No trading of ISIN INE134E08II2 will be permitted starting July 29, 2026.
