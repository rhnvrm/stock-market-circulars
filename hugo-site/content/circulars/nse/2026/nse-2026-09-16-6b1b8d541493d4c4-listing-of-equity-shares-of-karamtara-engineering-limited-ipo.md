## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Karamtara Engineering Limited under the IPO route.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Karamtara Engineering Limited
- Symbol: KARAMTARA
- ISIN: INE590T01012
- The specific date of listing will be announced through a separate circular.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the security details for their systems and client onboarding.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 16, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular

Enables trading members and investors to prepare for trading in the equity shares of Karamtara Engineering Limited once the listing date is announced.
