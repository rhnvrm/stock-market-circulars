## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced a change in the ISIN code for the equity shares of JSW Dulux Limited (JSWDULUX) effective from October 22, 2026, following a sub-division of shares from a face value of Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** JSW Dulux Limited
- **Symbol:** JSWDULUX
- **New ISIN:** INE133A01029
- **Reason:** Sub-Division from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-
- **Effective Date / Ex-date:** October 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes; routine updates to security identification numbers (ISIN) reflecting corporate restructuring.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to update their systems and ensure that trades executed on and from October 22, 2026, reference the new ISIN.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Effective / Ex-date:** October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Ensures accurate trade reporting and settlement under the new ISIN post sub-division.
