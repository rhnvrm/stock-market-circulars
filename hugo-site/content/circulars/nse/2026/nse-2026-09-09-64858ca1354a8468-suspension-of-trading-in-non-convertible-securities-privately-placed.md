## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed non-convertible securities for three companies, effective from September 10, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Royal Sundaram General Insurance Co. Limited (ISIN: INE499S08039) - Suspension Date: 10-Sep-2026 (Redemption for exercising call option).
- L&T Finance Limited (ISIN: INE027E07CP8) - Suspension Date: 10-Sep-2026 (Redemption).
- LIC Housing Finance Limited (ISIN: INE115A07KM9) - Suspension Date: 10-Sep-2026 (Redemption).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to note the suspension of trading in the aforementioned non-convertible securities.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact as the suspension is due to normal redemption and call option execution of debt securities.
