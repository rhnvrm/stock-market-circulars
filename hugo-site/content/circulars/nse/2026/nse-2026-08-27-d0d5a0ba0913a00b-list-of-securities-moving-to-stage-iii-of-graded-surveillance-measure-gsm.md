## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that securities shall be moved to Stage III of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework effective August 28, 2026. CLC Industries Limited (Symbol: CLCIND, ISIN: INE376C01038) is moving to Stage III.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026.
- Affected Security: CLC Industries Limited (CLCIND, INE376C01038).
- Trading Frequency: Permitted once a week (Every Monday / 1st trading day of the week).
- Price Band: 5% or lower as applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities under GSM Stage III are subjected to enhanced surveillance actions to protect market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading shall be available in Trade for Trade mode with no netting off allowed.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 100% of trade value to be deposited by the buyers.
- Members are requested to take adequate precaution while trading in the security.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity for the affected security will be significantly restricted due to weekly trading permissions and 100% ASD requirements, impacting short-term market participation.
