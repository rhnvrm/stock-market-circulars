## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Several securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I, effective from August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I: ARCHIDPLY, BURNPUR, DJML, EEPL, MAXESTATES, ONDOOR, PRESSTONIC, and RAPIDFLEET.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100% w.e.f. August 28, 2026, on all open positions as on August 27, 2026, and new positions created from August 28, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures under the Short-Term ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the specified securities starting August 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Inclusion: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: August 28, 2026 (on open positions as on August 27, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for listed securities under ST-ASM Stage I may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected stocks.
