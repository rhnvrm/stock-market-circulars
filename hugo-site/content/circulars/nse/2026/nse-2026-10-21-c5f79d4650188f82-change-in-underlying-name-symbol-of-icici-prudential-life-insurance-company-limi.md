## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a change in the underlying name and trading symbol for ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited in the Futures & Options segment, effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Old Symbol: ICICIPRULI
- New Symbol: ICICILIFE
- Old Company Name: ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited
- New Company Name: ICICI Life Insurance Limited
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

All contracts in the security will be traded under the new symbol and name effective October 23, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the change and update their trading and back-office systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on market operations; participants need to update systems to reflect the new symbol ICICILIFE.
