## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued instructions regarding the early pay-in (EPI) of securities for Taal Tech Limited (TAALTECH) due to a face value split (sub-division) from Rs 10/- per share to Rs 2/- per share, with the record date and ex-date set for September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security: Taal Tech Limited (TAALTECH)
- Existing/Old ISIN: INE524T01011
- Corporate Action: Face Value Split (Sub-Division) - From Rs 10/- Per Share to Rs 2/- Per Share
- Record Date & Ex-Date: 22-SEP-2026
- EPI Dates and Settlement Numbers: 22-SEP-2026 (Settlement No. 2026179), 23-SEP-2026 (Settlement Nos. 2026179 & 2026180)

## Regulatory Changes

- Market participants and custodians must execute early pay-in instructions in the old ISIN without adjusting for the conversion for the specified trade dates and settlement numbers.
- For early pay-in through the block mechanism, the client-wise early pay-in allocation file should not be uploaded.

## Compliance Requirements

- Custodians providing early pay-in through the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details as per the actual traded quantity to pass the benefit to clients.
- Pay-in and early pay-in instructions must be executed in the New ISIN after the record date in the depositories.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: 22-SEP-2026
- Record Date: 22-SEP-2026
- EPI Dates: 22-SEP-2026 and 23-SEP-2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and custodians need to carefully manage early pay-in processes using the old ISIN for the specified settlement numbers to ensure smooth clearing and settlement operations during the corporate action.
