## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding SEBI's final order dated June 30, 2026, in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips. SEBI has restrained various noticees from accessing the securities market and prohibited them from buying, selling, or dealing in securities for specified periods, alongside imposing monetary penalties.

## Key Points

- Reference to earlier NSE Circular no. NSE/INVG/57174 dated June 19, 2023, and SEBI Order no. WTM/SM/IVD/ID9/27532/2023-24.
- SEBI issued final order no. WTM/AS/IVD-2/ID16/32460/2026-27 dated June 30, 2026.
- Restraint periods range from 4 to 7 years depending on the noticee category, set off against the period of restraint already undergone.
- Monetary penalties ranging from ₹5 lakhs to ₹10 Crores imposed on respective noticees.
- Noticees with open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts as of the date of the order are permitted to close out / square off such open positions within 3 months.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of market restraint and financial penalties on entities involved in the Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips matter pursuant to SEBI's final order.

## Compliance Requirements

- NSE members are advised to take note of the debarred entities and ensure compliance with restrictions.
- Consolidated list of debarred entities is available on the Exchange website.
- Noticees having open derivative positions may square them off within 3 months and settle prior pay-in/pay-out obligations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: June 30, 2026
- Derivative square-off deadline: Within 3 months from the date of the order

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on named noticees and entities restricted from accessing the securities market or dealing in securities. Members must verify client status against debarred lists to prevent unauthorized trading.
