## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on its capital market segment, effective from October 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Alphagrep Securites Private Limited**: Admitted with symbol `105ASPL28`, Series N0, Market Lot 1, 10.5% coupon rate maturing on 06-Oct-2028 (ISIN: INE961U07028).
- **Summit Digitel Infrastructure Limited**: Admitted with symbol `SDIL29`, Series N0, Market Lot 1, Reset coupon rate maturing on 28-Sep-2029 (ISIN: INE507T07229).
- Trading will take place in the specified lot sizes and under designated security codes pursuant to NSE Trading Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory policy changes; admission pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular Annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: October 07, 2026
- **Maturity Dates**: 28-Sep-2029 (Summit Digitel) and 06-Oct-2028 (Alphagrep Securites)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational listing with minimal market impact, expanding debt instrument availability on the capital market segment.
