## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issue of 1,14,034 equity shares of TIL Limited consequent to the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares. These shares will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** TIL Limited
- **Symbol:** TIL
- **ISIN:** INE806C01018
- **Security Description:** Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares
- **Number of Securities:** 1,14,034 shares
- **Effective Date:** September 24, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/-
- **Distinctive Numbers:** From 82264460 to 82378493

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Equity shares allotted in dematerialized mode have been credited under temporary ISIN (IN8806C01017) pursuant to SEBI circulars dated August 02, 2012, and September 11, 2012, prior to listing under the permanent ISIN.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion and conversion event with minimal direct market impact.
