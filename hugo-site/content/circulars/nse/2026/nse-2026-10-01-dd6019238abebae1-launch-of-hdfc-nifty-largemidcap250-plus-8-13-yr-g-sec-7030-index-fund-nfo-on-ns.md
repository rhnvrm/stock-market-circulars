## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of the HDFC Nifty LargeMidcap250 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund NFO of HDFC Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from October 05, 2026, to October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: HDFC Nifty LargeMidcap250 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund (Direct and Regular plans across Growth and IDCW options).
- NFO Period: October 05, 2026 to October 19, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- Allotment Date: October 23, 2026.
- ISINs include INF179KC1LB0, INF179KC1LC8, INF179KC1LD6, INF179KC1LE4, INF179KC1LF1, and INF179KC1LG9.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 23, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open: October 05, 2026
- NFO Close: October 19, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: October 23, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to transact in the new HDFC Mutual Fund index scheme smoothly through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
