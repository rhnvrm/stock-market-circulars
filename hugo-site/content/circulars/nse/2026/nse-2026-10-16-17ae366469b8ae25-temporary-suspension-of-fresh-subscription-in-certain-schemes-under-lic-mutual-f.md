## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions and certain transaction facilities in LIC MF Gold ETF Fund of Fund (FoF) on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh purchase and subscription of units for LIC MF Gold ETF Fund of Fund are suspended.
- Switch-in transactions are disabled.
- Registration of new SIPs (including SIP Step Up, Micro SIP, Pocket SIP) and new STP-In transactions are suspended.
- New IDCW Transfer Facility registrations are suspended.
- Existing SIPs, STP-In, IDCW reinvestment/transfer mandates, redemptions, SWP, switch-out, and STP-out continue to be processed normally.

## Regulatory Changes

LIC Mutual Fund Asset Management Limited has updated its Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) to reflect the suspension of fresh subscriptions and specific transaction facilities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the suspension of fresh subscription channels and ensure no new SIP/STP registrations or fresh purchases are initiated for the affected scheme on the NSE MF Invest Platform until further notice.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Notice Date: October 15 / October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors looking to make fresh investments or start new SIPs/STPs in LIC MF Gold ETF Fund of Fund will be restricted. However, existing SIP/STP holders and unitholders seeking redemptions or switch-outs face no disruptions.
