## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the revision of Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL), Participant limits, Institutional client limits, and Non-Institutional client limits for the symbol OLAELEC under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for October 2026, effective from October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: OLAELEC
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026
- Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) revised from 289,378,643 to 294,689,016 shares.
- Institutional Client Limit revised from 28,937,864 to 29,468,901 shares.
- Participant Limit revised from 28,937,864 to 29,468,901 shares.
- Non-Institutional Client Limit revised from 2,893,786 to 2,946,890 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

Position limits under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for OLAELEC have been updated in partial modification of earlier circulars (NCL/CMPT/73999 dated April 30, 2026, and NCL/CMPT/76656 dated September 30, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

Participants, custodians, and trading members must take note of the revised position limits for OLAELEC and ensure compliance with SLBS position monitoring and reporting requirements effective from October 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective Date of Revised Limits: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The upward revision in position limits for OLAELEC expands the scope for lending and borrowing transactions in the scrip under SLBS, accommodating higher participation levels for institutional and non-institutional clients.
