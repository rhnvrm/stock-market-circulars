## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has approved the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed debt securities issued by Muthoot Finance Limited and Hero FinCorp Limited on its Capital Market segment. This admission is effective from August 04, 2026, pursuant to Regulations 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- **Admission of Securities:** Debt securities issued by Muthoot Finance Limited and Hero FinCorp Limited are admitted to trading on the NSE Capital Market segment.
- **Identification:** The securities will be identified solely by their designated symbols and traded in specified market lots.
- **Muthoot Finance Limited Security Details:**
  - **Symbol:** MFL29A
  - **Series:** N0
  - **Market Lot:** 1
  - **Issue Description:** MFL Tbill lkd 2029 (T-bill linked)
  - **Date of Maturity:** September 04, 2029
  - **ISIN Code:** INE414G07KC4
- **Hero FinCorp Limited Security Details:**
  - **Symbol:** 840HFL28
  - **Series:** N0
  - **Market Lot:** 1
  - **Issue Description:** HFL 8.40% 2028 Sr 80 (8.40% Fixed Coupon)
  - **Date of Maturity:** July 31, 2028
  - **ISIN Code:** INE957N07898

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes have been introduced. This action is a routine listing procedure carried out under Regulations 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Must ensure their trading systems, front-end software, and risk management parameters are updated to recognize the new symbols ("MFL29A", "840HFL28") and series ("N0") prior to the market opening on August 04, 2026.
- **Clearing and Settlement:** Clearing members must prepare to handle clearing and settlement activities for these securities in accordance with the specified lot size of 1.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 03, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** August 04, 2026
- **Maturity Date (Hero FinCorp):** July 31, 2028
- **Maturity Date (Muthoot Finance):** September 04, 2029

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low overall market impact as this is a routine debt security listing. However, it provides liquidity and secondary market access for holders of these privately placed instruments. Trading terminals must align with the new symbol definitions to prevent processing errors.
