## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of equity shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) for several companies, effective from August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities further admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective August 31, 2026.
- Companies involved include The Hi-Tech Gears Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, IIFL Capital Services Limited, Infosys Limited, Mahindra Logistics Limited, PG Electroplast Limited, and R Systems International Limited.
- Trading will be conducted using designated security codes and respective lot sizes as specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 28, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for market participants regarding the listing and trading of newly allotted ESOP shares.
