## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for certain securities. Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage I will attract a minimum 100% margin and shift from the Rolling Settlement segment (EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST) w.e.f. September 03, 2026. Additionally, securities moving to Stage II are subject to trade-for-trade with a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (Stage I) attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on September 02, 2026, and new positions created from September 03, 2026.
- Affected securities shift from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 03, 2026.
- Bafna Pharmaceuticals Limited shifts from ESM Stage I to Stage II w.e.f. September 02, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely for market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) guidelines as per previous exchange circulars, moving specified securities into Stage I and Stage II frameworks with corresponding margin and trading restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open and new positions for shortlisted securities and note the segment shifts to Trade-for-Trade.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II price band / movement: September 02, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and EQ/SM to BE/ST shift: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions apply to the impacted securities, potentially affecting their liquidity and trading volumes.
