## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, September 19, 2026, using NEATPlus Version 1.0.1. Trades during the mock session will not result in any financial obligations or fund pay-in/pay-out.

## Key Points

- Mock Date: September 19, 2026.
- Trading conducted from DR Site using NEATPlus Version 1.0.1.
- Pre-open session opens at 11:15 hours; Normal market open at 11:30 hours and close at 12:30 hours.
- Default login password is "Neat@EG1".
- Connect2NSE member portal and Extranet APIs will have restricted availability during specific evening hours.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine operational mock session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members participating in the EGR segment are advised to take note of the schedule, gateway parameters, and market data broadcast details to ensure proper testing.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness check for members trading in the Electronic Gold Receipts segment with no financial impact.
