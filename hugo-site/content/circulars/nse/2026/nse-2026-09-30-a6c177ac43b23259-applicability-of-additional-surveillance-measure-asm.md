## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for certain securities based on preset criteria, effective October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I include Akanksha Power and Infrastructure Limited, JHS Svendgaard Laboratories Limited, and Landsmill Green Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 06, 2026 on all open positions as on October 05, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term ASM Stage-I surveillance measures for specified scrips.
- 100% margin requirement on open and new positions for affected scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure 100% margin maintenance on open and new positions for the listed securities starting October 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of ASM Inclusion:** October 01, 2026
- **Applicability of 100% Margin:** October 06, 2026 (on open positions as on October 05, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for trading in shortlisted securities may affect liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
