## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh issue and re-issue of commercial papers (CPs) admitted to dealings on the debt market segment.
- Issuing entities include Trust Investment Advisors Private Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, JM Financial Services Limited, Tata Communications Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, and Aditya Birla Capital Limited.
- Face value across securities is ₹5,00,000 per security with varying issue prices, allotment dates in October 2026, and redemption dates ranging from November 2026 to January 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of privately placed debt securities/commercial papers pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update trading and settlement systems with the respective ISINs and security details.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Allotment Dates: October 22-23, 2026
- Redemption Dates: November 23, 2026 - January 29, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market trading and formal listing compliance for corporate commercial paper issuances.
