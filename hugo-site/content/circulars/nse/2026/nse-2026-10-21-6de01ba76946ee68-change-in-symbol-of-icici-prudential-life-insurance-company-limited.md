## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the change in symbol for futures and options contracts of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited. Effective October 23, 2026, all derivative positions under the current symbol ICICIPRULI will be carried forward under the new symbol ICICILIFE.

## Key Points

- Underlying security symbol changes from ICICIPRULI (ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited) to ICICILIFE (ICICI Life Insurance Limited).
- Effective date for the new symbol in F&O contracts and portfolio files is October 23, 2026.
- Reports pertaining to October 22, 2026, will continue to display the current symbol ICICIPRULI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Circular no. 143/2026 dated October 21, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to update their trading and clearing systems to reflect the new symbol ICICILIFE for all F&O positions and reports effective October 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date for Symbol Change: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact limited to updating internal trading systems, portfolio files, and report mappings for the new security symbol.
