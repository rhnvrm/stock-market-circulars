## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the upcoming Buyback Offer under the Tender Offer scheme for Gandhi Special Tubes Limited. The live bidding window is scheduled from August 27, 2026, to September 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Gandhi Special Tubes Limited
- **Security Symbol:** GANDHITUBE
- **Series:** BB
- **ISIN:** INE524B01027
- **Issue Size:** Up to 8,68,100 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 900/- per equity share
- **Face Value:** Rs. 5/- per equity share
- **Issue Period:** 27-August-2026 to 02-September-2026
- **Merchant Banker:** Prime Securities Limited
- **Registrar:** KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. The offer is conducted in accordance with the Tender Offer Scheme as per Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the Capital Market segment can participate using the web-based IPO bidding platform with existing user IDs or via the admin terminal by creating necessary branches and users.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Issue Closure Date:** September 2, 2026
- **Bidding Time:** 9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.
- **Custodial Confirmation on Closure:** 9:15 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders of Gandhi Special Tubes Limited to tender their equity shares through the exchange's web-based bidding platform during the specified buyback window.
