## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has admitted privately placed debt securities (Non-Convertible Debentures) issued by Trust Investment Advisors Private Limited to dealings on the Capital Market segment of the exchange. The listing and trading of these securities is scheduled to commence on August 03, 2026, under the designated symbols, series, and lot sizes specified in the announcement.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** Trust Investment Advisors Private Limited.
- **Securities Listed:** Two tranches of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs):
  - **885TIAPL30 (Series N0):** Coupon rate of 8.85%, maturing on July 30, 2030 (ISIN: INE723X07323).
  - **910TIAPL33 (Series N0):** Coupon rate of 9.10%, maturing on July 28, 2033 (ISIN: INE723X07315).
- **Market Lot Size:** The designated trading lot size for both series of securities is 1 unit.
- **Identification:** For system trading purposes, these securities will be identified exclusively by their assigned symbols and series.

## Regulatory Changes

The listing is initiated pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 (admission of securities to dealings) and Regulation 2.5.5 (lot sizes) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A. There are no changes to existing regulatory frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Terminals:** Trading members must update their back-office systems and security masters with the new symbols, ISINs, and market lot parameters prior to the commencement of trading.
- **Settlement Procedures:** Standard clearing and settlement protocols for the debt segment/capital market segment will apply.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Effective Date / Trading Commencement:** August 03, 2026
- **Maturity Date (885TIAPL30):** July 30, 2030
- **Maturity Date (910TIAPL33):** July 28, 2033

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine operational listing of private placements on the exchange's capital market segment.
- **System Impact:** Low. Requires routine database updates for member brokers to enable trading in the new symbols.
