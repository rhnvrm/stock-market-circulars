## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment with effect from October 21, 2026. The securities include issuances from Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) and Emerald Haven Life Spaces 3 Private Limited.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 21, 2026.
- **Securities Admitted:**
  - **Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI):** 8.05% 2029 Sr VII (Symbol: 805SIDBI29, ISIN: INE556F08LI7, Maturity: 10-Dec-2029, Market Lot: 1).
  - **Emerald Haven Life Spaces 3 Private Limited:** EHL3 7% 2046 (Symbol: 7EHL346, ISIN: INE0TV208145, Maturity: 16-Oct-2046, Market Lot: 1).
- **Trading Regulations:** Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, trading will be conducted using designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further privately placed securities to dealings on the capital market segment under NSE Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading participants must ensure orders are placed using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 21, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine market operations enabling secondary market trading for the newly listed privately placed debt securities.
