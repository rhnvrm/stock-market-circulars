## Summary

Pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement, National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that HEG Limited (HEG) will be shifted to the Trade for Trade segment with effect from September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: HEG Limited (HEG)
- Effective Date: September 07, 2026 (Monday)
- Trading Segment: Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE)
- Pre-open Session: Will be a part of the call auction in the pre-open session as per SEBI Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of the security to the Trade for Trade segment pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure that trades in HEG Limited are settled under the Trade for Trade mechanism where netting-off is not permitted and delivery of shares is mandatory for all transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in HEG Limited will require mandatory delivery of shares, which may impact short-term liquidity and intraday trading volumes for the stock.
