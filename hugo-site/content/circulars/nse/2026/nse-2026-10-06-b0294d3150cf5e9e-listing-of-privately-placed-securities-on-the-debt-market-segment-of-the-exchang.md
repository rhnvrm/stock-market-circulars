## Summary

National Stock Exchange has notified the listing of privately placed securities (commercial papers and debt issues) on the debt market segment of the exchange with effect from October 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Privately placed commercial papers and debt securities admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment effective October 06, 2026.
- Issuing companies include Godrej Agrovet Limited, NTPC Limited, Godrej Consumer Products Limited, L&T Finance Limited, Alphagrep Securities Private Limited, Summit Digitel Infrastructure Limited, and ICICI Securities Limited.
- Details including ISINs, face values, issue prices, allotment dates, and redemption dates are specified in the exchange annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of privately placed debt securities and commercial papers in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and trading members must ensure compliance with applicable debt segment regulations and settlement schedules.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 05, 2026 (and October 01, 2026 for specific debt issues)
- Effective Listing Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for newly listed commercial papers and debt instruments.
