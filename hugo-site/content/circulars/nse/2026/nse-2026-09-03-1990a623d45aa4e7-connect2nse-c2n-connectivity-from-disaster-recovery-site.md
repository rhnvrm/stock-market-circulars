## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited will conduct its operations for the Currency Derivatives Segment from its Disaster Recovery (DR) Site on September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Operations for the Currency Derivatives Segment will run from the DR Site on September 3 and 4, 2026.
- Members accessing N-MASS and NCMS applications do not need to make any configuration changes.
- N-MASS and NCMS applications will be unavailable for live operations or updates between 01:00 hrs and 08:00 hrs on September 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; operational notice regarding DR site switch.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the operational downtime for N-MASS and NCMS applications on September 5, 2026.

## Important Dates

- DR Site Operations: September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026
- Application Downtime: September 05, 2026 (01:00 hrs. to 08:00 hrs.)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on members as no connection parameter changes are required for C2N applications.
