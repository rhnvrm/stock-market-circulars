## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, September 05, 2026. This mock session coincides with the live implementation of modifications in the pre-open call auction framework, updated norms for Base Price, Price Bands, and Call Auction in pre-open sessions for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs), alongside the introduction of NEAT+ version 7.9.0.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, September 05, 2026.
- Introduction of NEAT+ version 7.9.0, which members are recommended to use for the revised pre-open call auction framework.
- Two trading sessions: Session-1 from Primary site and Session-2 from DR Site.
- Covers changes issued via prior circulars regarding ETF norms and pre-open framework updates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Go-live of modifications in the pre-open call auction framework.
- Changes in norms for Base Price, Price Bands, and Call Auction in pre-open sessions for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs).
- Introduction of NEAT+ 7.9.0 incorporating updated trading parameters.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to use NEAT+ version 7.9.0 from September 05, 2026.
- Members should participate in the mock trading session to test systems and connectivity with the new version.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 04, 2026
- Mock Trading / Effective Date: September 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness for trading members to ensure smooth adoption of NEAT+ 7.9.0 and updated ETF pre-open rules in live trading.
