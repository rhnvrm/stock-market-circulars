## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Elevate Campuses Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** ELEVATE
- **Name of the Company:** Elevate Campuses Limited
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE147X01013
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Re. 1/- each fully paid up
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 362/- per share
- **Total No. of Securities:** 168,531,037 equity shares
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips as per SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012. Lock-in details are specified as per Annexure I of the circular.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** September 30, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** Ranges vary from October 27, 2026, through September 28, 2029 (with certain shares free).

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of Elevate Campuses Limited equity shares on NSE, providing market liquidity to investors following its IPO.
