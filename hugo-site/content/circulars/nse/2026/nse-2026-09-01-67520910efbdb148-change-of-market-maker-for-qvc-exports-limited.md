## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the change of Market Maker for the SME company QVC Exports Limited (Symbol: QVCEL).

## Key Points

- Company Name: QVC EXPORTS LIMITED
- Symbol: QVCEL
- Old Market Maker: AFTERTRADE BROKING PRIVATE LIMITED (Member Code- 90113)
- New Market Maker: GIRIRAJ STOCK BROKING PRIVATE LIMITED (Member Code- 90318)
- Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members may take note of the change in Market Maker for QVC EXPORTS LIMITED.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 03-Sep-26

## Impact Assessment

Operational update affecting designated market making services for the specified SME stock.
