## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) is conducting a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, August 08, 2026. This session will be conducted from both the Primary site and the Disaster Recovery (DR) site to test system performance and connectivity. No new software version is being released for this session.

## Key Points

- **Mock Trading Date:** Saturday, August 08, 2026.
- **Trading Session 1 (Primary Site):** Pre-open starts at 11:00 AM, Normal Market opens at 11:15 AM and closes at 1:30 PM.
- **Trading Session 2 (Disaster Recovery Site):** Pre-open starts at 2:15 PM, Normal Market opens at 2:30 PM and closes at 3:30 PM.
- **NEATPlus Version Details:** Supported versions are 7.8.8 and 7.8.9. Version 7.8.8 is scheduled to be discontinued on September 05, 2026 (mock).
- **Live Re-login:** A live re-login window will be open on Saturday, August 08, 2026, from 5:00 PM to 5:30 PM to ensure system readiness for the next live trading day.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades executed during the mock sessions will not result in any financial pay-in or pay-out obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes are introduced in this circular. The mock session is held in line with standard regulatory requirements for periodic mock trading and system testing (NSE/MSD/46441 and SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Order Purge:** All outstanding orders will be cleared before each mock session. Members using Non-NEAT Front-end (NNF) software must manually clear outstanding orders in their internal systems.
- **UCC/PAN Validity:** Only valid and compliant UCC/PANs uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be permitted to participate in the mock trading session.
- **Software Testing:** Members may utilize this mock session or the simulated environment to satisfy regulatory requirements for software testing.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Session:** August 08, 2026
- **Live Re-login Window:** August 08, 2026, from 17:00 Hrs to 17:30 Hrs
- **Discontinuation of NEATPlus v7.8.8:** September 05, 2026 (mock session)

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Low/Medium. Trading members are advised to participate to verify connectivity, operational readiness, and disaster recovery site switchover performance.
- **Financial Impact:** None. Mock trades carry no settlement or financial obligations.
