## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular transitioning Grand Foundry Limited (GFSTEELS) and Univa Foods Limited (UNIVAFOODS) to Stage IV of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The restrictions will take effect from Wednesday, August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Securities Affected**:
  - Grand Foundry Limited (Symbol: GFSTEELS, ISIN: INE534A01028)
  - Univa Foods Limited (Symbol: UNIVAFOODS, ISIN: INE275F01019)
- **Surveillance Actions (Stage IV)**:
  - Trading will be available only in the Trade-for-Trade (T2T) segment.
  - Price band is restricted to 5% or lower as applicable.
  - Trading is permitted only once a week (every Monday or the first trading day of the week).
  - Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 100% of the trade value must be deposited by buyers.
  - No upward price movement is allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

This movement is governed by the existing Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, implementing specific actions on the designated securities under previously established guidelines (circular refs: NSE/SURV/34262, NSE/SURV/38389, NSE/SURV/42790, NSE/SURV/59425, NSE/SURV/64066, NSE/SURV/74254, and NSE/SURV/74795).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Precautions**: NSE trading members are advised to take adequate precautions prior to facilitating trades in these securities.
- **No Netting Off**: Since settlement is conducted on a trade-to-trade basis, no netting off of buy and sell transactions is permitted.
- **Deposit Mandate**: Buyers must deposit 100% of the trade value as Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD).

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 11, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Extremely low. The measures are stock-specific and do not represent a change in overall market policy.
- **Operational Impact**: High for brokers and retail platforms, which must configure their systems to enforce the once-a-week trading schedule, block netting-off, and require 100% upfront ASD for these particular stocks.
- **Liquidity Impact**: Critical. The affected securities will experience a severe reduction in liquidity due to restricted trading frequency and the prohibition on upward price movement.
