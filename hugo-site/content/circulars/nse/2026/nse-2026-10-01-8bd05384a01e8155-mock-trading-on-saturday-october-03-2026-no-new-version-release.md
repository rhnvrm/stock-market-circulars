## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) segment on Saturday, October 03, 2026. There is no new software version release associated with this mock session.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** Saturday, October 03, 2026.
- **Trading Session Timings:** Normal Market open time at 11:00 hrs, close time at 12:30 hrs. Live Re-login start time at 16:30 hrs, close time at 17:30 hrs.
- **Connectivity & Software:** NEAT Version 1.3.3. All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades executed during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- **UCC/PAN Validity:** Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.
- **NOTIS Availability:** Accessible during Trading Session-1 from the Primary Site.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; conducted as a routine operational exercise in compliance with standard market requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 05, 2026.
- Ensure successful telnet connectivity and verify Box ID setups and NNF application parameters.
- Set appropriate branch and user limits from the Corporate Manager Terminal prior to the mock session.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** October 03, 2026
- **Live Readiness Check:** October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. The session helps members verify system connectivity, user IDs, Box ID setups, and ensure readiness for live market operations.
