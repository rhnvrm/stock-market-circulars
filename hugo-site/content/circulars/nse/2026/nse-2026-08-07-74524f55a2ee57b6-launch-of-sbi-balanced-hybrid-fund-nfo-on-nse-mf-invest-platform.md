## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the availability of the SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) of SBI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The NFO will be open for transaction starting August 10, 2026, through August 24, 2026, until 11:59 PM. Standard clearance of funds must be completed by August 27, 2026, and units are scheduled to be allotted on August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Availability**: The SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund NFO will be available on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Transaction Window**: Transactions are permitted from August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026 (until 11:59 PM).
- **Schemes and ISINs Offered**:
  1. SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF200KB1CA5)
  2. SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF200KB1CB3)
  3. SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund - Regular Plan - IDCW (ISIN: INF200KB1BZ4)
  4. SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund - Direct Plan - IDCW (ISIN: INF200KB1CC1)
  5. SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF200KB1BX9)
  6. SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF200KB1BY7)
- **Allotment**: All schemes are scheduled for allotment on August 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no changes to existing regulations. This circular is purely operational to facilitate the listing and trading capability of the specified mutual fund schemes on the exchange's mutual fund platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Funds Pay-in**: Members must ensure that clear funds for transactions are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 27, 2026, by 03:00 PM.
- **Platform Operation**: Trading members must leverage the proper schemes and ISIN identifiers for order placement and settlement processing.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date**: August 10, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date**: August 24, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- **Clear Funds Cut-off**: August 27, 2026 (by 03:00 PM)
- **Allotment Date**: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Negligible. The introduction of this hybrid fund scheme expands product choices on the NSE MF Invest Platform but carries no wider systemic implications.
- **Operational Impact**: Routine. Distributors and members can accept and route customer subscriptions for the new NFO directly through the platform. Clearing and settlement protocols remain as standard for mutual fund segment transactions.
