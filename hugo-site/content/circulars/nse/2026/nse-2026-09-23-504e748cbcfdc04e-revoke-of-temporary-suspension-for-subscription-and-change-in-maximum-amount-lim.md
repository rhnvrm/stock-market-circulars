## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the revocation of temporary suspension for subscription and change in the maximum amount limit under certain schemes of Aditya Birla Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Subscriptions via fresh/additional lumpsum, switch-in, SIP, and STP have been resumed up to ₹1 crore per PAN per day effective September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Subscription and transactions (lumpsum, switch-in, SIP, STP) resumed for specific Aditya Birla Mutual Fund schemes.
- Maximum limit set at up to ₹1 Crore per PAN, per day.
- Effective Date: September 24, 2026.
- Applicable Schemes:
  - Aditya Birla Sun Life International Equity Fund
  - Aditya Birla Sun Life Global Emerging Opportunities Fund
  - Aditya Birla Sun Life Global Excellence Equity Fund of Fund

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the resumption of subscription limits and available transaction types on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors can resume investments in the specified global and international equity schemes of Aditya Birla Mutual Fund up to the prescribed limit of ₹1 crore per PAN per day.
