## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular granting listing and trading approval for further equity shares of multiple companies, effective July 29, 2026. These additional issues include shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and on a preferential basis. The securities will trade in the designated lot sizes and market segments as specified in the circular's annexures.

## Key Points

- **Trading Permission**: Further equity shares are admitted to dealings on the NSE (Capital Market segment) with effect from July 29, 2026.
- **Allied Digital Services Limited**: Allotted 76,800 equity shares of Rs. 5 each under ESOP on July 15, 2026.
- **Bandhan Bank Limited**: Allotted 23,165 equity shares of Rs. 10 each under ESOP on July 13, 2026.
- **DEE Development Engineers Limited**: Allotted 5,976,096 equity shares of Rs. 10 each on a preferential basis on July 8, 2026, at an issue price of Rs. 502.
- **Lock-in Details**: Locked-in shares are specified for DEE Development Engineers Limited, with 398,406 shares under lock-in until February 28, 2028.
- **Market Lot**: The market lot size for all listed securities is 1.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Regulations Compliance**: The listing is approved pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 (admission to dealings) and Regulation 2.5.5 (lot sizes) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Operations**: Trading members must note the new listings, designated security codes, and ISINs for their system operations.
- **Lock-in Administration**: Relevant depositories and intermediaries must ensure lock-in rules are implemented for the preferential allotment of DEE Development Engineers Limited.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates**:
  - DEE Development Engineers Limited: July 8, 2026
  - Bandhan Bank Limited: July 13, 2026
  - Allied Digital Services Limited: July 15, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date**: February 28, 2028 (for 398,406 shares of DEE Development Engineers Limited)

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity & Share Capital**: The listings will result in a minor increase in the outstanding shares and paid-up capital of Allied Digital Services, Bandhan Bank, and DEE Development Engineers, slightly improving their trading liquidity.
- **Dilution**: Minor non-disruptive dilution for ESOP shares. The preferential allotment for DEE Development Engineers represents a larger increase in outstanding equity, but is subject to standard lock-in rules to manage supply.
