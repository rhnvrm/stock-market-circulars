## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the availability of the Edelweiss Ultra Short Term Fund NFO from Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window runs from August 17, 2026 to August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Edelweiss Ultra Short Term Fund (Direct and Regular Plans across Growth and IDCW options)
- NFO Open Date: August 17, 2026
- NFO Close Date: August 24, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- Allotment Date: August 31, 2026
- Category: Debt Fund

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 27, 2026, till 06:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening: August 17, 2026
- NFO Closing: August 24, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: August 27, 2026
- Allotment Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investment access to Edelweiss Ultra Short Term Fund debt schemes for market participants through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
