## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, August 29, 2026. The session is designed to test modifications in the pre-open call auction framework and norms for Base Price, Price Bands, and Call Auction in the pre-open session specifically for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs).

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** August 29, 2026.
- **Software Update:** NEATPlus version 7.9.0 will be introduced exclusively for testing the pre-open call auction framework during Trading Session-1.
- **Version Rollback:** Members must reinstall and log in using earlier NEATPlus versions (7.8.8 or 7.8.9) for Trading Sessions 2 and 3.
- **Scope:** Covers multiple trading sessions from both Primary and DR sites, including Block Deal Windows, Pre-Open sessions, Special Pre-Open sessions for IPOs/relisted securities, T+0 markets, and Closing sessions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Testing modifications in the pre-open call auction framework as per circular no. NSE/CMTR/74969 (dated July 01, 2026).
- Testing Base Price, Price Bands, Call Auction norms, and Close-out procedures for ETFs as per circular no. NSE/CMTR/74767 (dated June 18, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must participate in the mock trading session, use NEATPlus version 7.9.0 for Trading Session-1, and revert to versions 7.8.8 or 7.8.9 for subsequent sessions as specified by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** August 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members need to ensure proper terminal version management during the mock sessions to successfully test the new pre-open call auction framework and ETF norms without disrupting normal operations.
