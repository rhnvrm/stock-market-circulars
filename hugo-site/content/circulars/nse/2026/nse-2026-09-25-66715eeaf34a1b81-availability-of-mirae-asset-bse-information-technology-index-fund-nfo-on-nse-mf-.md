## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of the Mirae Asset BSE Information Technology Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective September 28, 2026. The circular covers ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities for multiple direct and regular plans.

## Key Points

- Mirae Asset BSE Information Technology Index Fund is made available on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Applies to both Direct and Regular plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- Effective date for subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP is September 28, 2026.
- ISINs include INF769K01RO5, INF769K01RQ0, INF769K01RP2, INF769K01RL1, INF769K01RN7, and INF769K01RM9.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes or policy updates; this is a routine platform inclusion notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members facilitating mutual fund transactions need to update their systems to support the new scheme and its various plans.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to the Mirae Asset BSE Information Technology Index Fund through the NSE MF Invest platform, expanding investment options in the technology sector index fund.
