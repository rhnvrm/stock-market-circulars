## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the System Audit of Trading Members (Type - III) for the period ending September 30, 2026. The circular highlights the mandatory appointment of SEBI-empaneled system auditors, submission deadlines for audit plans, preliminary audit reports, and Action Taken Reports (ATRs), and outlines the usage of the 'NSE Member Portal' mobile application for capturing geo-location and visit details during physical audits.

## Key Points

- Applicable to Type - III trading members conducting half-yearly system audits for the period ending September 30, 2026 (April 2026 – September 2026).
- Only system auditors empaneled by the Exchange (as per NSE Circular Ref No. NSE/INSP/69631 dated August 12, 2025) must be appointed.
- Mandatory use of the 'NSE Member Portal' mobile application during physical visits by auditors to capture geo-location, entry/exit timestamps, and visit details.
- Submission deadlines: Audit Plan by November 5, 2026; Preliminary Audit Report by November 30, 2026; Action Taken Report (ATR) by February 28, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reinforces technology-based monitoring and supervision of system audits for stockbrokers and trading members utilizing algorithmic trading or offering algorithmic trading facilities.
- Enforces strict compliance with auditor empanelment norms and geo-location tracking via the NSE Member Portal mobile application.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must select only Exchange-empaneled system auditors.
- Submit complete audit plan details via the NSE ENIT portal during auditor registration by November 5, 2026.
- Ensure auditors log into the 'NSE Member Portal' app during physical site visits, marking exact entry and exit times.
- Submit preliminary audit reports and Action Taken Reports (ATRs) within the stipulated timelines.

## Important Dates

- **Audit Period:** April 2026 – September 2026 (Period ending September 30, 2026)
- **Submission of Audit Plan:** November 05, 2026
- **Submission of Preliminary Audit Report:** November 30, 2026
- **Submission of Action Taken Report (ATR):** February 28, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances regulatory oversight and audit transparency through geo-location tracking and digital verification.
- Ensures timely identification and remediation of system vulnerabilities among trading members utilizing algorithmic trading facilities.
