## Summary

SEBI has issued a final order in the matter of Alliance Research and its sole proprietor, Mr. Mudassir Hasan, following an inspection that revealed various violations including carrying on unregistered investment advisory activities, improper KYC, risk profiling and product suitability assessment, charging unfair fees, and promising assured returns to clients.

## Key Points

- Inspection conducted during February 11-15, 2020 revealed multiple violations of SEBI Act, IA Regulations, and PFUTP Regulations.
- Noticee engaged in unregistered investment advisory activities prior to obtaining formal SEBI registration.
- Violations included improper risk profiling, charging exorbitant/unfair fees, and promising assured returns to investors.
- Interim and confirmatory orders were previously passed to restrain the entity from accessing the securities market.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of regulatory restrictions and penalties under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1) and 11B(2) of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

- Registered investment advisers must strictly comply with SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, maintain proper KYC and risk profiling records, avoid unfair fee structures, and refrain from promising assured returns.

## Important Dates

- Date of Inspection: February 11-15, 2020
- Date of Interim Order: January 6, 2021
- Date of Confirmatory Order: July 29, 2022

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on investment advisers to protect retail investors from fraudulent advisory practices and unauthorized operations.
