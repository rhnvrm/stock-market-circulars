## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, August 01, 2026. This is a routine test session to ensure operational readiness, including switchover capabilities to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site. No new software version is being released for this session; members will continue using NEAT+ EGR Version 1.0.0.

## Key Points

- **Mock Trading Schedule**: The mock session is split into Session-1 (Primary Site) and Session-2 (Disaster Recovery Site) on August 01, 2026.
- **No New Version Release**: Active software version remains NEAT+ EGR Version 1.0.0 (path: `/egftp/egcommon/NEATPlus100`).
- **Technical Parameters**: Details for Gateway Router IP (`172.19.14.86`, Port `11887`) and Multicast IP parameters for EGR segment broadcast are provided in the circular annexures.
- **Facility Downtime**: Connect2NSE and Extranet facilities will be unavailable from 14:30 hours until the end of the mock session on Saturday.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced in this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to participate in the mock trading session to verify system connectivity and DR switchover procedures.
- Members must ensure that their systems are re-logged during the Live Re-login window between 14:00 and 15:00 hours on the mock trading day to ensure smooth operations on the next trading day.
- No financial obligations will arise from mock trading transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date**: August 01, 2026
  - **Session-1 (Primary Site)**: Normal Market open at 07:45, close at 10:00
  - **Session-2 (DR Site)**: Normal Market open at 10:45, close at 11:45
  - **Live Re-login**: 14:00 to 15:00

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Low. This is a standard non-disruptive testing process performed on a non-trading day (Saturday).
- **System Readiness**: High. Verifies that disaster recovery site configurations and primary site fallbacks are functioning normally for all members participating in the EGR segment.
