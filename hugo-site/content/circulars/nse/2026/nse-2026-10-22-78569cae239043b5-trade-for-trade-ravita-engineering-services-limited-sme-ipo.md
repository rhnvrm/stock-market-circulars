## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the transfer of securities of Ravita Engineering Services Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ravita Engineering Services Limited
- Symbol: RAVITA
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (series: ST) to Rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Reference Circulars: NSE/CML/77041 dated October 21, 2026, and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are moved from trade-for-trade settlement to normal rolling settlement pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members take note of the change in trading segment and settlement series for Ravita Engineering Services Limited.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates normal rolling settlement for the stock, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility for market participants.
