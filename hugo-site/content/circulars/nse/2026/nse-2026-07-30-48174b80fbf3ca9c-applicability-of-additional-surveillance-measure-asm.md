## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Specific securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion or transition within the Long-Term ASM Framework. Notable actions include the imposition of a 100% margin rate and shifting eligible securities under Criteria VII (Stage IV) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Key Points

- **Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I**: Capital Trust Limited (CAPTRUST) has been shortlisted under Stage-I w.e.f. July 31, 2026.
- **Stage Transitions**:
  - Tips Films Limited (TIPSFILMS) moves from Stage-I to Stage-II w.e.f. July 31, 2026.
  - Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DIACABS) moves from Stage-I to Stage-IV w.e.f. July 31, 2026.
- **Trading Restrictions**: Securities qualifying under Criteria VII (shifted to Stage IV, such as DIACABS) will be moved from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- **Margin Requirements**: An applicable margin rate of 100% will be effective from August 04, 2026, on all open positions as of August 03, 2026, and on new positions created from August 04, 2026 onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

No new broad regulatory framework changes are introduced; this is an operational update applying existing Long-Term ASM guidelines (pursuant to earlier circulars dating back to October 2018) to the listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must implement a 100% margin rate on all open positions of the affected securities (as of August 03, 2026) and new positions starting August 04, 2026.
- **Settlement Adjustment**: Trading members must transition the shortlisted Stage IV securities (such as DIACABS) to the Trade-for-Trade series (BE) on a T+3 basis starting August 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **July 31, 2026**: Effective date for shortlisting/transitions under the Long-Term ASM Framework.
- **August 03, 2026**: Date for determining open positions subject to the updated margin requirement.
- **August 04, 2026**: 100% margin rate becomes effective, and Stage IV securities are shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Affected stocks (CAPTRUST, TIPSFILMS, and DIACABS) will likely experience decreased liquidity due to higher margin requirements and/or Trade-for-Trade settlement restrictions.
- **Operational Impact**: Brokerages and trading members must update their risk management systems to enforce the 100% margin requirement and handle the settlement shift to Series BE for DIACABS.
