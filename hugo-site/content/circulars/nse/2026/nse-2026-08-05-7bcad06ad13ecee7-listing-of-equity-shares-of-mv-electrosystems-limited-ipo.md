## Summary

This circular disseminates the post-issue shareholding pattern of **MV Electrosystems Limited** (symbol: `MVELECTRO`) following its Initial Public Offering (IPO) on the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE). The shareholding pattern, filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, shows the distribution of ownership between the promoters and the public as of August 5, 2026, ahead of its listing.

## Key Points

- **Total Paid-Up Capital:** 27,282,728 fully paid-up equity shares held by 22,437 shareholders.
- **Promoter Shareholding:** 8 promoter/promoter group entities hold 15,737,200 shares, representing **57.68%** of the total equity capital.
- **Public Shareholding:** 22,429 public shareholders hold 11,545,528 shares, representing **42.32%** of the total equity capital.
- **Locked-in Shares:** A total of 23,529,787 shares (representing **86.24%** of the total shares) are locked in, which includes 100% of promoter shares and a significant portion of public shares post-IPO.
- **No Encumbrance:** No promoter shares are pledged, encumbered, or subject to non-disposal undertakings.
- **Other Securities:** The company has not issued any partly paid-up shares, convertible securities, warrants, ESOPs, or shares against depository receipts. No significant beneficial owner has been declared.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory guidelines are introduced. The pattern is filed in compliance with existing **Regulation 31(1)(a)** of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, which governs the disclosure format of shareholding patterns for listed entities.

## Compliance Requirements

MV Electrosystems Limited is required to maintain and disclose its shareholding pattern on a quarterly basis and upon any major corporate actions in accordance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- **Shareholding Reference Date:** August 5, 2026
- **Listing Date:** August 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Trading Impact:** High transparency is maintained for the newly listed entity. Providing the post-issue holding structure allows market participants to assess the float available for public trading (approximately 11.5 million shares, although a significant portion of public shares is under lock-in).
- **Ownership Stability:** With promoters holding a solid 57.68% majority and zero encumbrances (no pledges), the ownership structure demonstrates high stability.
