## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has approved the listing of 1,97,82,690 further equity shares of GVP Infotech Limited arising from the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares, effective from August 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company:** GVP Infotech Limited
- **Symbol:** GVPTECH
- **ISIN:** INE382T01030 (Temporary ISIN: IN9382T01012)
- **Number of Securities:** 1,97,82,690
- **Face Value:** Re. 2/-
- **Paid-up Value:** Re. 2/-
- **Security Description:** Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 162861591 to 182644280

## Regulatory Changes

None. The admission is carried out under standard NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A (Regulations 3.1.1 and 2.5.5).

## Compliance Requirements

None for general market participants. Standard system and code configuration updates apply to trading members dealing in GVPTECH.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 05, 2026
- **Effective Date for Listing:** August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low. Increases the outstanding fully paid-up equity shares and liquidity for GVP Infotech Limited without altering the core company operations or broader market dynamics.
