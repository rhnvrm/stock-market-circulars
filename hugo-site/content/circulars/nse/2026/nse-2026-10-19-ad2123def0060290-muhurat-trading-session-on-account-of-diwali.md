## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a special live trading session (Muhurat Trading) on account of Diwali on Sunday, November 08, 2026, for the Currency Derivatives segment.

## Key Points

- Special live trading session scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- Normal Market Open: 14:20 Hrs.
- Normal Market Close: 15:30 Hrs.
- Trade modification end time: 15:40 Hrs.
- All trades executed during this session result in regular settlement obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a scheduled operational notice for festive trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must align their systems and operations with the announced timings for the Diwali Muhurat trading session.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 19, 2026
- Muhurat Trading Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact limited to standard operational adjustments for the special festive trading window.
