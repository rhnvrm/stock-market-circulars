## Summary

Tata Mutual Fund has issued an addendum detailing changes to the scheme names, types, and categories for select schemes in accordance with Clauses 3.7 and 3.8 of the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20th, 2026, regarding the categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Tata ELSS Fund renamed to Tata ELSS – Tax Saver Fund with updated scheme type and category details.
- Tata Gilt Securities Fund renamed to Tata Gilt Fund with refined investment scheme type.
- Minor modifications to Scheme Types for Tata Balanced Advantage Fund and Tata Corporate Bond Fund.
- Changes are not considered fundamental attribute changes as per clause 3.8.9 of the SEBI Master Circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- Alignment of mutual fund nomenclature and parameters with categories listed under Clause 3.8 of the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20th, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- The addendum forms an integral part of the SID/KIM cum Application Form.
- All other terms, conditions, and risk-o-meters remain unchanged.

## Important Dates

- Addendum Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact on investors; purely operational and regulatory compliance alignment of scheme names and disclosures without altering fundamental scheme objectives.
