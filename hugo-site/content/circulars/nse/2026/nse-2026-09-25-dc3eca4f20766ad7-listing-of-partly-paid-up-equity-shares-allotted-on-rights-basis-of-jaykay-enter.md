## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and commencement of trading for partly paid-up equity shares of Jaykay Enterprises Limited allotted on a rights basis, effective from September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Jaykay Enterprises Limited
- **Symbol:** JAYKAYPP
- **ISIN:** IN9903A01015 (temporary ISIN IN8********* was initially credited)
- **Face Value:** Re. 1 per share
- **Paid-up Value:** Re. 0.50 per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 75 (Rs. 37.50 paid on application)
- **Number of Securities:** 20,571,642 equity shares
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 130287067 to 150858708
- **Effective Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Pari Passu:** Yes
- **Lock-in Details:** Not Applicable

## Regulatory Changes

Admissions of further securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 23, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables shareholders holding partly paid-up rights shares of Jaykay Enterprises Limited to trade on the NSE Capital Market segment starting September 29, 2026.
