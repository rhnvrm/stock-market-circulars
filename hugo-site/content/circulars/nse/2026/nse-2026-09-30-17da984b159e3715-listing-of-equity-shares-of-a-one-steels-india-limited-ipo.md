## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of A-One Steels India Limited on the Capital Market segment, effective from October 01, 2026. The security will be identified by the symbol AONESTEELS.

## Key Points

- Symbol: AONESTEELS
- Company Name: A-One Steels India Limited
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE0OTC01025
- Face Value: Rs. 10/-
- Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 405/-
- Total No. of Securities: 77,234,693
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the securities are admitted to dealings with designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips as per SEBI circular nos. CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective / Listing Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Commences public trading and market liquidity for equity shares of A-One Steels India Limited on the NSE Capital Market segment.
