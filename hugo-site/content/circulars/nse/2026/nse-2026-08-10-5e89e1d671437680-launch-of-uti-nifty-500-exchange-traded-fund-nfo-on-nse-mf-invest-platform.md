## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the UTI Nifty 500 Exchange Traded Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) of UTI Mutual Fund will be available for transaction on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription is open from August 10, 2026, until August 24, 2026, with unit allotment scheduled for August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name**: UTI Nifty 500 Exchange Traded Fund (Regular Plan - Growth & Direct Plan - Growth).
- **ISIN**: INF789F1AG76.
- **Category**: Equity.
- **Subscription Period**: August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- **Allotment Date**: August 25, 2026.
- **Funds Settlement**: Members must ensure clear funds are received in the Clearing Corporation account on or before August 24, 2026, by 11:59 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced in this circular. This is a routine operational notice regarding a scheme launch on the exchange's mutual fund platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants using the NSE MF Invest Platform must ensure that clear subscription funds, through any payment mode, are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 24, 2026, till 11:59 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date**: August 10, 2026
- **NFO Close Date**: August 24, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline**: August 24, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date**: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Adds a new passive investment product (UTI Nifty 500 ETF) on the NSE MF Invest platform, expanding product choice for investors.
- **Operational Impact**: Requires members to manage fund collections and timely transfers to meet the Clearing Corporation deadlines.
