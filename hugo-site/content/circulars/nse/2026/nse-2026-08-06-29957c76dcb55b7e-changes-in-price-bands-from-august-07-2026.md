## Summary

On August 06, 2026, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) issued circular SURV75620 (Ref No: 600/2026) announcing revised price bands for various securities. These revised bands take effect from August 07, 2026. This review is conducted in pursuance of Capital Market Segment Regulation Part-A, 2.5, to enforce appropriate daily operating price limits for listed securities, aligning them with the current market surveillance framework.

## Key Points

- **Price Band Adjustments**: Revised daily operating range limits are set for 3,332 securities in total.
- **Mutual Funds Price Bands**: A standard price band of 10% (either way) is declared applicable to all close-ended Mutual Funds.
- **Derivative Products**: For securities with derivative products available, a daily operating range of 10% will continue to apply (no specific static band).
- **Physical Market Alignment**: The price band for revised securities in the Limited Physical Market will be the same as the band applicable in the normal market.
- **Surveillance Oversight**: Managed by the Surveillance Department, with updates to be continuously reflected on the NSE website.

## Regulatory Changes

Under Capital Market Segment Regulation Part-A, 2.5, the daily price operating ranges are revised as follows:
1. **2% Band**: 45 securities (as listed in Annexure I, including ACCORD, AMANTA, ANSALAPI, APSISAERO, etc.).
2. **5% Band**: 622 securities (as listed in Annexure II).
3. **10% Band**: 151 securities (as listed in Annexure III).
4. **No Band**: 208 securities (with derivatives, operating with 10% daily range, as listed in Annexure IV).
5. **20% Band**: 2,306 securities (standard band for securities not listed in Annexures I to IV).
6. **40% Band**: 0 securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Operations**: Trading members and system administrators must update their trading terminals to reflect the new daily price ranges (upper and lower circuit limits) for all listed securities starting from August 07, 2026.
- **Order Validation**: Automatic order matching systems must restrict orders outside the newly designated bands.
- **Queries**: Members can contact the Surveillance Department at surveillance@nse.co.in for clarification.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Issue Date**: August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date of New Price Bands**: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity & Volatility Control**: The adjustment of price bands limits excessive volatility in the affected securities, especially micro-caps and highly volatile shares restricted to lower bands (2% or 5%).
- **Operational Alignment**: Market participants must align pre-trade risk management systems to prevent orders from being rejected or causing trade halts.
- **Securities List**: Clear visibility of risk categories (from 2% up to 20%) helps in collateral valuation and margin calculation.
