## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Based on the surveillance criteria, five securities have been shortlisted to be placed under ESM Stage I, effective from August 06, 2026. These securities will attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions from August 07, 2026, and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Key Points

- **ESM Stage I Inclusion**: Five securities (CAPTRUST, HMVL, MAJESAUT, ONYX, and TSC) have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage I.
- **Trading Segment Shift**: Shortlisted securities will shift from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 07, 2026.
- **Margin Requirements**: A minimum 100% margin will be applicable on all open positions as on August 06, 2026, and new positions created from August 07, 2026, onwards.
- **Stage II Price Band**: Securities moving to ESM Stage II will trade in the Trade-for-Trade segment with a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 06, 2026.
- **No Movement in Stage II**: No securities are moving between Stage I and Stage II, and no securities are being excluded from the ESM framework in this revision.
- **Nature of Action**: The shortlisting of securities under ESM is a market surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of listed securities under ESM Stage I to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).
- Application of 100% margin requirements for ESM Stage I securities on all open and new positions.
- Price band restriction of 2% under Periodic Call Auction for any securities shifting to ESM Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading and Operations**: NSE Members must ensure that the shortlisted securities are traded only in the Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST series) starting August 07, 2026.
- **Margin Collection**: Members must collect a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as of August 06, 2026, and on all new positions created from August 07, 2026.
- **Periodic Call Auction**: Members must comply with the 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction trading mechanisms for any securities in ESM Stage II.

## Important Dates

- **August 06, 2026**: Effective date for ESM Stage I classification; cut-off date for open positions subject to the margin requirement.
- **August 06, 2026**: Effective date for any ESM Stage II price band changes (2% under Periodic Call Auction).
- **August 07, 2026**: Shifting of EQ/SM series to BE/ST series for newly added Stage I securities; commencement of 100% margin requirement on new and existing open positions.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity**: The transition of CAPTRUST, HMVL, MAJESAUT, ONYX, and TSC to the Trade-for-Trade segment will likely reduce their daily trading volumes and liquidity, as intra-day squaring-off of positions is not permitted in the BE/ST series.
- **Capital and Margin**: Trading members and clients will experience increased capital requirements due to the mandatory 100% margin on all positions in these securities.
- **Price Volatility**: Being placed under ESM Stage I and Trade-for-Trade series acts as a stabilizing measure to curb excess volatility and speculative interest in these counters.
