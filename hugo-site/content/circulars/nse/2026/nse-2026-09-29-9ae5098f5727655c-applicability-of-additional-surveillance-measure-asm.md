## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long-Term framework. Specific securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Long-Term ASM Stage I effective September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Stage I: JHS Svendgaard Retail Ventures Limited (RETAIL), RTS Power Corporation Limited (RTSPOWR), and Tamilnadu Telecommunication Limited (TNTELE).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective October 05, 2026, on all open positions as on October 01, 2026, and new positions created from October 05, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the concerned entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term ASM Stage I framework measures for specified scrips.
- Imposition of 100% margin requirement on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open positions as of October 01, 2026, and all subsequent new positions starting October 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Shortlisting: September 30, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: October 01, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in the affected securities will experience higher margin blocking (100%), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the shorted scrips.
