## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names of several mutual fund schemes managed by ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026. This change is pursuant to SEBI's guidelines on Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Platform: NSE MF Invest platform.
- Fund House: ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund.
- Changes include renaming of various equity, debt, and hybrid schemes to align with SEBI's Master Circular guidelines dated March 20, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI Chapter for Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes under the Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026, along with subsequent FAQs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, trading members, and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for order placement and reporting on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The change is purely operational and administrative regarding scheme nomenclature; existing investments remain unaffected.
