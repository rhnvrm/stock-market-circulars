## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the revised list of approved securities, Government of India (GOI) securities, open-ended mutual funds, corporate bonds, and empaneled banks effective for the month of October 2026. This supersedes the previous circular dated August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective for the month of October 2026.
- Covers seven annexures detailing equity shares, ETFs, Sovereign Gold Bonds, GOI Securities/T-Bills, open-ended mutual funds, corporate bonds, and empaneled banks.
- Haircut rates and market-wide/member-specific limits are stipulated in the respective annexures.
- G-Sec/T Bills have a shut period of two days.

## Regulatory Changes

- Update to the list of approved securities eligible for non-cash and cash components of liquid assets along with applicable haircut rates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to release G-Sec/T Bills at least two days before maturity due to the two-day shut period.
- Members who are also banks must ensure that G-Sec provided as collaterals are not reckoned for SLR purposes and not used for trading.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 1, 2026
- Circular Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Clearing members and custodians must review the revised limits, haircut rates, and approved securities list to ensure compliance and adequate collateral management for October 2026.
