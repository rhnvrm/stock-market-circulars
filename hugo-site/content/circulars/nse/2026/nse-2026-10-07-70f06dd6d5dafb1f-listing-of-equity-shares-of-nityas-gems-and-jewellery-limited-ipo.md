## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and commencement of trading of equity shares of Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited under its Initial Public Offering (IPO), effective from October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited
- Symbol: NITYAS
- Series: BE (to be subsequently shifted to Series EQ)
- ISIN: INE211501023
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 75/-
- Total Securities: 57,593,248 equity shares
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

Securities are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment in Series 'BE' and will participate in the pre-open session for IPO scrips as per SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the designated security codes, lot sizes, and lock-in details as specified in Annexure I of the circular.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Lock-in periods expire on various dates including April 08, 2027 and April 08, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and liquidity for the equity shares of Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited on the NSE.
