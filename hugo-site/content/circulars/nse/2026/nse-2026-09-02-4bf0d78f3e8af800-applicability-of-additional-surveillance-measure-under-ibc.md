## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective from September 03, 2026. The circular details the list of securities satisfying the criteria under the ASM IBC framework.

## Key Points

- Securities have been shortlisted under ASM for Companies as per Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) Stage I effective September 03, 2026.
- Includes Teerth Gopicon Limited (Symbol: TGL) under Stage I.
- The ASM IBC framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under the IBC framework for identified companies.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance applicability and ensure compliance with trading restrictions in the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 03, 2026
- Circular Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the shortlisted securities will be subject to additional surveillance margins or checks as mandated by the ASM framework.
