## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for specific securities, effective October 06, 2026. Securities meeting the criteria are subjected to a 100% margin requirement and shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST) effective October 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage I: Goldstar Power Limited (GOLDSTAR), Infinium Pharmachem Limited (INFINIUM), and Landsmill Green Limited (LANDSMILL).
- Affected securities will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 07, 2026 on all open positions as on October 06, 2026, and new positions created from October 07, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 07, 2026.
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) Stage I for specified securities.
- Movement of securities from EQ/SM series to BE/ST series (Trade-for-Trade settlement).
- Imposition of 100% margin requirement on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin collection on open positions as on October 06, 2026, and new positions from October 07, 2026 for the shortlisted securities.
- Trading members need to update their systems regarding the segment shift from EQ/SM to BE/ST for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- **October 06, 2026**: Effective date for shortlisting under ESM Stage I.
- **October 06, 2026**: Cut-off date for open positions for margin applicability.
- **October 07, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin requirement and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST).

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and investors dealing in GOLDSTAR, INFINIUM, and LANDSMILL will face liquidity restrictions due to the Trade-for-Trade settlement and 100% upfront margin requirement, which aims to enhance market integrity and curb excessive volatility.
