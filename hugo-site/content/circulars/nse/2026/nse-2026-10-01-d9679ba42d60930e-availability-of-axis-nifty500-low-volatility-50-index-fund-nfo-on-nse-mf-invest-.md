## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of the Axis Nifty500 Low Volatility 50 Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 5, 2026. The scheme supports ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.

## Key Points

- Schemes: Axis Nifty500 Low Volatility 50 Index Fund Direct Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1614) and Regular Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1606).
- Category: Equity.
- Effective Date: October 5, 2026.
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational circular announcing platform availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants should note the ISIN details and ensure operational readiness for processing transactions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 1, 2026
- Effective Date: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to transact seamlessly in the Axis Nifty500 Low Volatility 50 Index Fund via the NSE MF Invest platform.
