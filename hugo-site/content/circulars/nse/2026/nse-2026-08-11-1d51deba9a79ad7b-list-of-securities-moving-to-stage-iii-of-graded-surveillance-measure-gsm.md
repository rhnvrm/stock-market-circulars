## Summary

In continuation of previous exchange circulars, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that Ortel Communications Limited (Symbol: ORTEL, ISIN: INE849L01019) will be moved to Stage III of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from August 12, 2026. This move imposes stringent surveillance and trading restrictions on the security, including trading on a trade-to-trade basis with limited weekly trading windows and mandatory high deposit margins for buyers.

## Key Points

- **Security Affected:** Ortel Communications Limited (Symbol: ORTEL, ISIN: INE849L01019) is moved to Stage III of the GSM framework.
- **Effective Date:** The transition is effective from August 12, 2026.
- **Trading Restrictions:** Trading in ORTEL will be conducted under the Trade for Trade segment with a price band of 5% or lower as applicable.
- **Trading Window:** Trading will be permitted only once a week (every Monday / 1st trading day of the week).
- **Additional Margin:** Buyers are required to deposit 100% of the trade value as an Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD).
- **Settlement:** Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis; no netting off of transactions is allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory framework changes are introduced; this is an operational surveillance action executed under the existing Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework guidelines originally established under circular NSE/SURV/34262 dated February 23, 2017, and subsequently updated.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members:** Trading members must take adequate precautions when executing orders in ORTEL, ensuring they collect 100% ASD from buyers prior to order execution and process settlements on a trade-to-trade basis. No netting off of buy/sell positions is permitted.
- **For Investors/Buyers:** Buyers of ORTEL must deposit 100% of the trade value as ASD. They must also note that trading is restricted to Mondays (or the first trading day of the week) only.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Effective Date of Movement:** August 12, 2026
- **Weekly Trading Days:** Every Monday (or first trading day of the week) starting post-effective date.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Stock Impact:** Highly restrictive for Ortel Communications Limited (ORTEL). The stock liquidity will drastically reduce due to the trading frequency being limited to once a week and the 100% ASD requirement for buyers. This is likely to exert downward pressure on the stock price and limit trading volume.
- **Operational Impact:** Broader market operations are unaffected, but brokerage back-office systems must enforce Stage III GSM controls (price band <= 5%, weekly trading window, trade-for-trade settlement, and 100% ASD collection) for ORTEL.
