## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed commercial papers and other debt securities on its debt market segment with effect from October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Admission of fresh issues and re-issues of commercial papers (CPs) and debt securities.
- Issuing entities include Kotak Securities Limited, Motilal Oswal Finvest, Ventura Securities Limited, Nabkisan Finance Limited, Sundaram Home Finance Limited, Western Capital Advisors Private Limited, Reliance Industries Limited, and HDB Financial Services Ltd.
- Designated security codes and ISINs allocated as per the exchange annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to trading and settlement guidelines stipulated for debt market segment instruments.

## Important Dates

- Effective Listing Date: October 01, 2026
- Issue-specific allotment and redemption dates range from September 2026 through October 2036.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for privately placed corporate debt and commercial papers.
