## Summary

This circular issued by NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) outlines the procedures for the early pay-in (EPI) of securities of Tembo Global Industries Limited (TEMBO) in light of its face value split (sub-division) from Rs 10/- per share to Re 1/- per share. It provides instructions for trading members, custodians, and PCMs on executing early pay-in instructions during the transition period across old and new ISINs.

## Key Points

- **Corporate Action**: Face value split (sub-division) of TEMBO from Rs 10/- per share to Re 1/- per share.
- **ISIN Transition**: The old ISIN is INE869Y01010. A new ISIN will be active after the record date.
- **EPI Process for Transition**:
  - For EPI on 05-AUG-2026 (Settlement 2026146), the existing/old ISIN must be used with unadjusted quantity (e.g., 1 share instead of 10 shares).
  - For EPI on 06-AUG-2026 (Settlement 2026146 & 2026147), the new ISIN must be used.
- **Block Mechanism**: For early pay-in through block mechanism, client-wise early pay-in allocation files should not be uploaded.
- **Pool Account EPI**: Custodians providing EPI through pool accounts must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details as per the actual traded quantity (e.g., 10 shares) to pass the benefits to clients.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory frameworks are established; this circular provides operational instructions under the existing SEBI and NCL guidelines (consolidated circular NCL/CMPT/73996 and SEBI block mechanism guidelines NCL/CMPT/53386).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Depository Instructions**: Execute early-pay-in instructions in the old ISIN for ex-date/record date (05-AUG-2026) in depository systems, without adjusting for conversion on the trade date.
- **EPI Quantity Adjustment**: In the old ISIN, depository instructions must reflect the adjusted quantity (e.g., 1 old share for 10 traded shares).
- **Client-wise Allocation File**: Avoid uploading client-wise allocation files when using block mechanisms. Ensure uploading them for pool account EPI using actual traded quantities.
- **Post-Record Date EPI**: Execute pay-in/early-pay-in in the New ISIN for instructions after the record date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 04, 2026
- **Record Date**: August 05, 2026
- **Ex-Date**: August 05, 2026
- **EPI Old ISIN Date**: August 05, 2026 (Settlement No: 2026146)
- **EPI New ISIN Date**: August 06, 2026 (Settlement No: 2026146 & 2026147)

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Trading members and custodians must carefully manage depository instructions and allocation files during the ex-date transition to prevent settlement shortages and ensure correct margin exemptions.
- **Client Impact**: Benefits of early pay-in will be correctly credited to clients, provided pool account custodians upload accurate client-allocation files based on traded quantities.
