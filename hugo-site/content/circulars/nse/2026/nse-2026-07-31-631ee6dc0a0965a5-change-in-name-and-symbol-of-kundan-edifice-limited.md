## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced a change in the name and trading symbol of Kundan Edifice Limited. Effective August 06, 2026, the company will be known as Visdem Technosys Limited, and its ticker symbol on the exchange will transition from KEL to VISDEM.

## Key Points

- **Name Change**: Kundan Edifice Limited is changing its name to Visdem Technosys Limited.
- **Symbol Change**: The trading symbol is being updated from KEL to VISDEM.
- **Effective Date**: The changes will take effect starting August 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory rule changes or modifications are introduced by this circular. It is a standard listing compliance update following a corporate name change.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Market Intermediaries and Brokers**: System databases, back-offices, and trading terminals should be updated to reflect the new name 'Visdem Technosys Limited' and symbol 'VISDEM' prior to the market open on August 06, 2026, to prevent any order execution issues.
- **Investors**: No action is required. Portfolio holdings and open positions in KEL will be automatically updated by brokers and depositories to the new ticker VISDEM.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Operations**: Low impact. Systems and market databases will transition smoothly with standard naming/symbol mappings.
- **Shareholders**: No impact on equity ownership, share count, or underlying value; securities will simply trade under the new symbol VISDEM.
