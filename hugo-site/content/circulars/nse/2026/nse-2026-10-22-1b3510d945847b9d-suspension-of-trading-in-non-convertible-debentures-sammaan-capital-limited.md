## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of Sammaan Capital Limited due to their maturity, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sammaan Capital Limited
- Effective Date of Suspension: October 23, 2026
- Reason: Maturity
- Affected ISINs and Symbols:
  - INE148I07QH0 (Symbol: NK)
  - INE148I07QI8 (Symbol: NL)
  - INE148I07QM0 (Symbol: NM)
  - INE148I07QK4 (Symbol: NN)
  - INE148I07QP3 (Symbol: NO)
  - INE148I07QO6 (Symbol: NP)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; procedural suspension upon debenture maturity in accordance with exchange regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members take note of the suspension of trading in the specified NCDs w.e.f. October 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for maturing debt securities with no broader market impact.
