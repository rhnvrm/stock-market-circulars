## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and trading details for Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on the capital market segment with effect from September 04, 2026, following reference circular NSE/CML/76126.

## Key Points

- Listing of three Treasury Bills: 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day maturities.
- Listing of four State Development Loans (SDLs) issued by Bihar (BR), Madhya Pradesh (MP), and Jharkhand (JH).
- All securities are available for trading in the capital market segment with a lot size of 100.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine securities listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants are requested to take note of the designated codes and ISINs for trading.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 04, 2026
- Maturity Dates ranging from December 03, 2026 to September 02, 2056 depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables market participants to trade in the newly issued T-bills and SDLs on the NSE capital market segment.
