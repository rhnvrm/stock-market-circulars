## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of securities for GP Eco Solutions India Limited and S J Logistics (India) Limited under the SME EMERGE segment, effective from August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- GP Eco Solutions India Limited: 499,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment (Distinctive Range: 11830801 to 12329800, ISIN: INE0S7E01015).
- S J Logistics (India) Limited: 530,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment (Distinctive Range: 15293131 to 15823130, ISIN: INE0F3301020).
- Trading in these securities will commence with effect from August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) - SME EMERGE in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities are subject to specified lock-in periods and market lot requirements.
- Equity shares allotted in dematerialized mode may use temporary ISINs as per applicable SEBI guidelines until activation.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 19, 2026
- GP Eco Solutions Allotment Date: July 18, 2026
- S J Logistics Allotment Date: June 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the paid-up capital base and liquidity of the respective SME companies on the exchange platform.
