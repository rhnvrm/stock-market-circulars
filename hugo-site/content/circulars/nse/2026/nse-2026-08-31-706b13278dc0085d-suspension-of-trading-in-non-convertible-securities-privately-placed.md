## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of NTPC Limited due to redemption, effective from September 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: NTPC Limited
- ISIN: INE733E07KF5
- Suspension Date: 01-September-2026
- Reason: Redemption
- Regulation: Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the suspension of trading for the specified ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: September 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No broader market impact; standard procedure for redeemed debt securities.
