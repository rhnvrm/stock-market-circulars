## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment for the month of October 2026. The circular outlines the schedule for Normal T+1 settlement type '6' and Auction settlement type '7'.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules for the Electronic Gold Receipt segment for October 2026.
- Details trade start dates, trade end dates, and settlement dates for Normal settlement type '6' (Annexure A).
- Details trade dates and settlement dates for Auction settlement type '7' (Annexure B).
- Auction of shortages for settlement number 2026111 will be conducted in 2026112, with no auction conducted in 2026111.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued in pursuance of Regulation 6.2 of the NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the trade and settlement dates for EGR segment operations during October 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: Sep 10, 2026
- Settlement Period: October 2026 (Starting from October 01, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Operational guidance for clearing and settlement participants trading in the Electronic Gold Receipt segment.
