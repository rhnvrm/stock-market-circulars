## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of Power Grid Corporation of India Limited due to redemption, effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Power Grid Corporation of India Limited
- ISIN: INE752E07NE2
- Suspension Date: 28-August-2026
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants should note the suspension of trading for the specified ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: 28-August-2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified non-convertible securities of Power Grid Corporation of India Limited is halted following their redemption.
