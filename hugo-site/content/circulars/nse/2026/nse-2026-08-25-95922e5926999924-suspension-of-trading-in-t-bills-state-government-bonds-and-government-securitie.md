## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the suspension of trading in several Treasury Bills (T-Bills), State Government Bonds (SDL), and Government Securities (G-Sec) due to scheduled maturity and redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading in specified T-Bills, State Government Bonds, and Government Securities will be suspended w.e.f. respective suspension dates.
- Suspension is on account of redemption/maturity of the securities.
- Covers 16 listed securities including various 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day T-Bills, state development loans, and dated government securities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Execution under Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the suspension dates and avoid trading in these suspended securities from the effective dates onwards.

## Important Dates

- Effective dates range from August 28, 2026, to September 25, 2026, depending on the specific security's maturity schedule.

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact as these are routine expirations and redemptions of sovereign and sub-sovereign debt instruments.
