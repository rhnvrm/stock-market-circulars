## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced that securities of Ashutosh Fibre Limited will be transferred from the trade-for-trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM) effective September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security affected: Ashutosh Fibre Limited (Symbol: ASHUTOSH).
- Transfer from trade-for-trade segment (series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM).
- Effective date: September 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants to note the change in trading segment for the security.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for Ashutosh Fibre Limited shares, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility.
