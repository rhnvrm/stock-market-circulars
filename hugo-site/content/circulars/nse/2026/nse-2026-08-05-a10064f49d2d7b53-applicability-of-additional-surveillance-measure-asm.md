## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued circular Ref. No: 592/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/SURV/75591) dated August 05, 2026, regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Based on criteria defined in prior surveillance circulars, several securities have been shortlisted for inclusion or transition within the Long-Term ASM framework. Specifically, Cambridge Technology Enterprises Limited and Vivo Collaboration Solutions Limited are shortlisted under Stage I, while Yasho Industries Limited is moving from Stage I to Stage II. The surveillance actions, including a 100% margin requirement on open and new positions, will become effective from August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Long-Term ASM Stage I Additions:** Cambridge Technology Enterprises Limited (CTE) and Vivo Collaboration Solutions Limited (VIVO) have been shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Stage I effective August 06, 2026.
- **Stage Progression:** Yasho Industries Limited (YASHO) has progressed from Long-Term ASM Stage I to Stage II effective August 06, 2026.
- **Margin Requirements:** An applicable margin rate of 100% will be enforced starting August 10, 2026, on all open positions as of August 07, 2026, and all new positions created from August 10, 2026, onwards.
- **Nature of Action:** NSE clarifies that shortlisting under ASM is purely for market surveillance purposes and does not represent any adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of shortlisted securities into the Long-Term ASM framework in accordance with the established joint surveillance criteria.
- Mandated 100% margin requirement for the affected securities in the ASM framework.
- Standard provisions of Stage IV (Criteria VII) regarding shifting from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) are referenced, although no securities were shifted to Stage IV in this specific circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members/Brokers:** Trading members must collect and maintain a 100% margin on all open positions (as of August 07, 2026) and new positions created from August 10, 2026, onwards for the affected securities (CTE, VIVO, YASHO).
- **Client Communication:** Members should inform clients about the increased margin requirements and potential liquidity changes for the shortlisted securities.
- **System Updates:** Trading systems and risk management portals must be updated to reflect the 100% margin parameter and Stage II status for YASHO, and Stage I status for CTE and VIVO.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 05, 2026
- **Effective Date for ASM Shortlisting:** August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margin Requirements:** August 10, 2026 (applicable on open positions as of August 07, 2026, and new positions from August 10, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity Impact:** Shortlisted securities (CTE, VIVO, and YASHO) are likely to experience a reduction in trading volume and liquidity due to the stringent 100% margin requirement.
- **Trading Cost:** Leveraged trading or intraday positions in these stocks will be constrained, as market participants must fund the complete value of their positions.
- **Market Sentiment:** While the exchange notes that ASM is not an adverse corporate action, the inclusion of stocks in the ASM framework typically leads to short-term downward price pressure due to restricted leverage and negative sentiment.
