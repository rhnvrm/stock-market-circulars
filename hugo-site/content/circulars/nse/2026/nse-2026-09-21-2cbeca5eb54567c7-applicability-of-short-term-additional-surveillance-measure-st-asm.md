## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specific securities satisfying the inclusion criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Asian Star Company Limited (ASTAR), Bhageria Industries Limited (BHAGERIA), Glass Wall Systems (India) Limited (GLASSWALL), GMM Pfaudler Limited (GMMPFAUDLR), HLE Glascoat Limited (HLEGLAS), Kaushalya Logistics Limited (KLL), Kopran Limited (KOPRAN), Patil Automation Limited (PATILAUTOM), and Valiant Organics Limited (VALIANTORG).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100% w.e.f. September 23, 2026 on all open positions as on September 22, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I framework provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open and new positions on the shortlisted securities starting September 23, 2026 based on positions as of September 22, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ST-ASM Stage I criteria: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for trading in the specified securities, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume.
