## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the compulsory delisting of equity shares of three companies—Poddar Housing and Development Limited, Suumaya Industries Limited, and SecUR Credentials Limited—effective from October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Equity shares of Poddar Housing and Development Limited (Symbol: PODDARHOUS), Suumaya Industries Limited (Symbol: SUULD), and SecUR Credentials Limited (Symbol: SECURCRED) are compulsorily delisted.
- The delisting withdraws the admission to dealings in equity shares on the exchange.
- Effective date of delisting is October 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Action taken in terms of Regulation 32(1) of Securities and Exchange Board of India (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021, Section 21A of the Securities Contract (Regulations) Act, 1956, Securities Contract (Regulations) Rules, 1957, and Bye-Laws and Regulations of National Stock Exchange of India Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the withdrawal of admission to dealings for the specified companies and adjust their systems and portfolios accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date of Delisting: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares in these companies will no longer be able to trade these securities on the NSE platform post the effective date, restricting liquidity on the exchange platform.
