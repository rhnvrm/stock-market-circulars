## Summary

NSE has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions for certain Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) plan options under JM Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. This suspension applies to lumpsum subscriptions, as well as fresh registrations for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP), effective from August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh subscriptions (Lumpsum, SIP, STP) are disabled for select IDCW plans of JM Mutual Fund.
- The suspension is effective starting August 12, 2026, on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- There are no restrictions on Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), Switch-outs, and STP-outs.
- Existing SIP and STP mandates will continue to be processed by the AMC/RTA without any changes.
- Affected schemes include various direct and regular IDCW options under JM Aggressive Hybrid Fund, JM Arbitrage Fund, and JM Large Cap Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

This is an operational action initiated by JM Financial Trustee Company Private Limited to suspend fresh subscriptions in specific plan options, which is being subsequently implemented by NSE on its MF Invest Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors utilizing the NSE MF Invest Platform must ensure that no fresh subscription requests (Lumpsum, SIP, or STP) are initiated for the suspended scheme options from the effective date to prevent transaction rejections.

## Important Dates

- **August 11, 2026:** Circular issued by NSE.
- **August 12, 2026:** Effective date for the suspension of fresh subscriptions in the specified schemes.

## Impact Assessment

The impact is limited to investors attempting to initiate new investments in the specified IDCW options of JM Mutual Fund via the NSE MF platform. Existing investors and their ongoing systematic investments (SIPs/STPs) remain completely unaffected, ensuring a very localized and low market impact.
