## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names of Bank of India Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines on categorization and rationalization of schemes.

## Key Points

- Notice-cum-addendum issued by Bank of India Mutual Fund introduces changes in nomenclature for various mutual fund schemes.
- Changes become effective on August 26, 2026.
- Affected platforms include the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates implemented in compliance with SEBI circular on categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for order placement and reporting.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; ensures scheme name consistency across platforms and regulatory filings.
