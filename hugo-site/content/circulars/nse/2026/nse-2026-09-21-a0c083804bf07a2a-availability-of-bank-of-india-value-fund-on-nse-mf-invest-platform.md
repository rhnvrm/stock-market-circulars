## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced that various schemes of the Bank of India Value Fund will be available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes include direct and regular plans for Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment of the Bank of India Value Fund.
- Effective date for availability is September 22, 2026.
- Covers facilities such as ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants can transact in the aforementioned schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform as per standard operating procedures.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment choices for market participants and investors utilizing the NSE MF Invest Platform for mutual fund transactions.
