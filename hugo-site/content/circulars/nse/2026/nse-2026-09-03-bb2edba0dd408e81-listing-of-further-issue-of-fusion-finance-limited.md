## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issue of equity shares of Fusion Finance Limited. A total of 2,27,856 further equity shares are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Fusion Finance Limited
- **Symbol:** FUSION
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE139R01012 (Temporary ISIN: IN8139R01011 for demat credit prior to listing)
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/-
- **Number of Securities:** 2,27,856 equity shares
- **Reason for Issue:** Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares
- **Effective Date:** September 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. The listing follows standard regulatory provisions under NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations and SEBI guidelines for additional issue of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes, lot sizes, and ISIN activation guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 03, 2026
- **Effective Date / Admission to Dealings:** September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of paid-up equity capital base for Fusion Finance Limited with negligible market impact.
