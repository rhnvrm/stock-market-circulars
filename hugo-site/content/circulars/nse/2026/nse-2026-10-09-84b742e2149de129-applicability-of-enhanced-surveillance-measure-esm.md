## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Specific securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under the measure, while others are eligible to move out or transition between stages effective October 12 and 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 13, 2026, on all open positions as on October 12, 2026, and new positions created from October 13, 2026.
- Qualifying securities under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 13, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework have their price bands reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) Stage - I and Stage - II transitions.
- Shift of short-listed securities to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) and imposition of 100% margins.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ESM Stage II price band / Periodic Call Auction: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin applicability and Segment shift (EQ/SM to BE/ST): October 13, 2026
- Open positions reference date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement mechanics for the shortlisted securities (including Infollion Research Services Limited, Kavveri Defence & Wireless Technologies Limited, and others) are affected due to increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
