## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular announcing the availability of Quant Income Plus Arbitrage Active FOF schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities effective from September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes of Quant Mutual Fund (Quant Income Plus Arbitrage Active FOF) are reopened for ongoing transactions.
- Facilities enabled include subscription, redemption, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective date of availability is September 07, 2026.
- Includes multiple plans (Direct and Regular plans with IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment, and Growth options).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants should note the availability of these schemes on the NSE MF Invest platform for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational convenience for investors and distributors utilizing the NSE MF Invest Platform to transact in debt fund schemes of Quant Mutual Fund.
