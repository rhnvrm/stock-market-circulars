## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities effective September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. September 29, 2026 on all open positions as on September 28, 2026 and new positions created from September 29, 2026.
- No securities were shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II.
- The ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework applied to selected scrips to monitor market volatility and curb excessive speculation.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that applicable margin rates (50% or existing margin, capped at 100%) are maintained for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date for ST-ASM: September 28, 2026
- Margin Requirements Effective Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the shortlisted securities will require higher margins, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
