## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of Piramal Finance Limited due to maturity, effective from October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Piramal Finance Limited
- **Symbol:** 905PFL26
- **ISIN:** INE140A07740
- **Reason for Suspension:** Maturity
- **Effective Date:** October 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the securities are suspended from trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members take note of the suspension of trading in the specified NCDs.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date / Suspension Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Circular Date:** October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine market operation reflecting the maturity of the listed NCDs. No adverse impact on market participants.
