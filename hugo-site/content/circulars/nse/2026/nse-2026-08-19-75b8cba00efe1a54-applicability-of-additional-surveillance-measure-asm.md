## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Filatex India Limited has been shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I.

## Key Points

- Securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. August 24, 2026 on all open positions as on August 21, 2026 and new positions created from August 24, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I for shortlisted securities.
- 100% margin requirement applicable on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin is maintained for applicable securities effective from August 24, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 19, 2026
- Effective Date of ASM Framework / Inclusion: August 20, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: August 24, 2026 (on open positions as on August 21, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in affected securities will attract a 100% margin requirement, which may impact liquidity and positioning for market participants.
