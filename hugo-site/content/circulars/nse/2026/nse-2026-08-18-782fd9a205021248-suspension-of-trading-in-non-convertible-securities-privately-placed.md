## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) for Sundaram Home Finance Limited and State Bank of India, effective August 19, 2026, pursuant to redemption and call option exercises.

## Key Points

- Sundaram Home Finance Limited (ISIN: INE667F07IT7) suspended due to redemption.
- State Bank of India (ISIN: INE062A08280) suspended due to redemption for exercising call option.
- Suspension Date: August 19, 2026.
- Regulations: Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the suspension of trading for the specified non-convertible securities.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified non-convertible securities of Sundaram Home Finance Limited and State Bank of India will be halted on the NSE debt segment.
