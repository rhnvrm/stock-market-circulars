## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced a change in the name and trading symbol of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited
- New Name: ICICI Life Insurance Limited
- Existing Symbol: ICICILIFE
- New Symbol: ICICIPRULI
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of company name and symbol across exchange trading and listing systems.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their records and trading terminals with the new symbol and name effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update only; no financial or structural impact on existing contracts beyond symbol and name referencing.
