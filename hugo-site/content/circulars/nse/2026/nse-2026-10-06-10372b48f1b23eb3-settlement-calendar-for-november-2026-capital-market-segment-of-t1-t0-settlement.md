## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedules for the Capital Market Segment covering T+1 and T+0 settlements for November 2026, in pursuance of Regulation 6.2 of the NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997).

## Key Points

- Normal Rolling (T+1) settlement type M schedule provided in Annexure A.
- Normal Auction settlement type A schedule provided in Annexure B.
- Trade for Trade (T+1) settlement type Z schedule provided in Annexure C.
- ITP for SME (T+1) settlement type B schedule provided in Annexure D.
- Normal Rolling (T+0) settlement type 5 schedule provided in Annexure E.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine monthly schedule issuance.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members, clearing members, and custodians must align their operational workflows, funds/securities pay-in/pay-out planning, and custodial confirmations with the specified calendar dates for November 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 06, 2026
- Settlement Period: November 2026 (starting from trade date November 02, 2026 through December 01, 2026 settlement).

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless operational planning and compliance for market participants across T+1, T+0, auction, and specialized trading segments.
