## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedule for the Debt Segment (Settlement Type ‘I’) and Debt Auction Settlement (Settlement Type ‘2’) for the month of November 2026 pursuant to Regulation 6.2 of NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997).

## Key Points

- Outlines rolling T+1 settlement calendar for the debt segment for November 2026.
- Annexure A details regular Debt Settlement (Settlement Type ‘I’) with trade dates spanning from November 02, 2026 to November 30, 2026.
- Annexure B details Debt Auction Settlement (Settlement Type ‘2’) with trade dates spanning from November 03, 2026 to December 01, 2026.
- Applicable to all members, custodians, and PCMs participating in the debt segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine monthly schedule notification.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure clearing and settlement activities align with the specified trade and daily settlement dates.

## Important Dates

- Trade Dates (Debt Segment): November 02, 2026 – November 30, 2026
- Trade Dates (Debt Auction): November 03, 2026 – December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational clarity for clearing and settlement operations in the debt market.
