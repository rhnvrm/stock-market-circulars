## Summary

ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Limited has announced the merger of specific schemes under ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Merging Scheme: ICICI Prudential Nifty SDL Sep 2026 Index Fund (Multiple plans/options including Growth, Annual IDCW, and Direct variants)
- Surviving Scheme: ICICI Prudential Corporate Bond Fund (Corresponding Growth, Quarterly IDCW, and Direct variants)
- Effective Date of Merger: September 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Scheme consolidation and merger approved by the Board of Directors of ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Limited and ICICI Prudential Trust Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors in the merging schemes are transitioned into the surviving scheme as per the approved ISIN mapping.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding units in ICICI Prudential Nifty SDL Sep 2026 Index Fund will receive units of ICICI Prudential Corporate Bond Fund based on the applicable terms and ISIN mappings.
