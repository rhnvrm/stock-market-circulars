## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) issued Circular Ref. No. 1328/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/CML/75697) on August 11, 2026. Under the provisions of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the circular notifies market participants of the listing and trading permission for further issues of equity shares across 17 companies, effective August 12, 2026. The further allotments predominantly stem from Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and corporate Schemes of Arrangement.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date**: The further securities are admitted to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment with effect from August 12, 2026.
- **Pari Passu Status**: All newly listed shares will rank pari passu with the existing equity shares of the respective companies.
- **Allotment Mechanisms**: Shares have been allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and Scheme of Arrangement.
- **Trading Parameters**: Securities will be identified by designated symbols and ISINs, and will trade in a market lot size of 1.
- **Specific Allotments Outlined**:
  - **AXISCADES Technologies Limited**: 2,500 shares allotted under ESOP on July 17, 2026.
  - **Bajaj Finserv Limited**: 1,511,358 shares allotted under ESOP on August 6, 2026.
  - **CL Educate Limited**: 68,989 shares allotted under ESOP on August 3, 2026.
  - **CREDITACCESS GRAMEEN LIMITED**: 101,320 shares allotted under ESOP on August 6, 2026.
  - **Hindustan Foods Limited**: 48,139,085 shares allotted on May 21, 2026, pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement.
- **Lock-in Period**: Not applicable to the highlighted allotments.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no structural or fundamental changes to existing market regulations. The listings are executed within the established framework of:
- **Regulation 3.1.1** of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A: Relates to the conditions and admission of further issues to dealings.
- **Regulation 2.5.5** of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A: Relates to the determination and enforcement of standard lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members & Brokers**:
  - Update securities reference master data and system-level configuration files to include the new shares and facilitate trading starting August 12, 2026.
  - Ensure trading activities align with the defined market lot of 1 and proper symbol identifier mappings.
- **Listed Entities**:
  - Complete required post-allotment registry updates, corporate actions, and depositary notifications.
  - Track and report the updated paid-up share capital structure to exchange and depository databases.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates**:

## Impact Assessment

- **Capital Dilution & Outstanding Shares**: 
  - **Hindustan Foods Limited (HNDFDS)**: Will see a substantial capital expansion of 48,139,085 equity shares (face value Rs. 2/- each) following the execution of the Scheme of Arrangement. This is a non-cash corporate action that might modify ownership distribution but is neutral to immediate stock valuations.
  - **ESOP Allotments**: Allotments for AXISCADES (2,500 shares), Bajaj Finserv (1,511,358 shares), CL Educate (68,989 shares), and CreditAccess Grameen (101,320 shares) represent a minimal dilution to existing equity bases. These are expected to have negligible market impact.
- **Fungibility**: These shares are fully fungible, have no lock-in restrictions, and can be traded on the exchanges without any additional administrative barriers.
