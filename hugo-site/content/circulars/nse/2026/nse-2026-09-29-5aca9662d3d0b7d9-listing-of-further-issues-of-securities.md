## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of equity shares for multiple companies including Almondz Global Securities Limited, Cipla Limited, Creditaccess Grameen Limited, Cyient Limited, Fractal Analytics Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, ITC Hotels Limited, JM Financial Limited, SBI Cards and Payment Services Limited, Xelpmoc Design And Tech Limited, and Sun Pharma Advanced Research Company Limited.

## Key Points

- Effective date for admission to dealings: September 30, 2026.
- Covers preferential issues and ESOP allotments across several listed entities.
- Trading will be conducted in designated lot sizes and security codes as specified in the annexure.
- Compliance with Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of further issues of securities to dealings on the National Stock Exchange Capital Market segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure trading is conducted using designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates liquidity and trading of newly allotted equity shares resulting from preferential issues and ESOPs for the listed companies.
