## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of the DSP Financial Services Sectoral Debt Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes are available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Includes both regular and direct plans across growth and IDCW options.
- Effective date: August 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants are notified of the scheme availability for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 31, 2026
- Circular Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to transact in the specified DSP Mutual Fund debt schemes via the NSE platform.
