## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced a face value split for Shankara Buildpro Limited (BUILDPRO). The face value and paid-up value of equity shares will be changed from Rs. 10 to Rs. 2 per share, effective from October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shankara Buildpro Limited
- Symbol: BUILDPRO
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 2
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Corporate action updating the face value and paid-up value of equity shares as per NSE listing guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants should update their systems to reflect the revised face value of BUILDPRO shares.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Share price and volume dynamics will adjust proportionately to reflect the new face value structure.
