## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the launch of the Carnelian Liquid Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from October 12, 2026, to October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Carnelian Liquid Fund NFO available for transaction on NSE MF Invest Platform from October 12, 2026 to October 14, 2026 (till 11:45 PM).
- Includes 6 schemes: Regular and Direct plans for Daily IDCW Reinvestment, Weekly IDCW Reinvestment, and Growth.
- All ISINs categorized under Liquid category with allotment date on October 16, 2026.
- Schemes include:
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund Regular Plan Daily IDCW Reinvestment (INF30CN01079)
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund Direct Plan Daily IDCW Reinvestment (INF30CN01046)
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund Regular Plan Weekly IDCW Reinvestment (INF30CN01087)
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund Direct Plan Weekly IDCW Reinvestment (INF30CN01053)
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund Regular Plan Growth (INF30CN01061)
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund Direct Plan Growth (INF30CN01038)

## Regulatory Changes

None. Operational circular regarding mutual fund NFO availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 16, 2026, till 12:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Subscription Start Date: October 12, 2026 (up to 11:45 PM)
- NFO Subscription End Date: October 14, 2026 (up to 11:45 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 16, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 16, 2026 (up to 12:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to transact in Carnelian Liquid Fund schemes via the NSE MF platform.
