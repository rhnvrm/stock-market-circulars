## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has released a user manual detailing the procedure for the submission of annual returns for the Financial Year 2025–26 on the new NCL member portal. Members across different constitution types (Corporate/LLP, Partnership Firm, and Individual) are required to submit their annual accounts, networth certificates, and other compliance details through the designated portal.

## Key Points

- **Portal Access:** Members must log in to the new NCL member portal via Internet Explorer using the specified URL (`https://ims.connect2nsccl.com/NCLMemberPortal/`).
- **Constitution-wise Modules:** Sub-modules and tabs vary based on the member's constitution (Corporate/LLP, Partnership Firm, Individual), covering annual accounts, networth, directors/partners details, shareholding/partnership sharing, and compliance officer details.
- **Annual Accounts Tab:** Members must select the financial statement format (IGAAP, IndAS, NBFC) and upload mandatory documents (Audited Balance Sheet, P&L Account, Schedule & Notes to Account, Auditor's Report, and Directors Report where applicable) following strict file nomenclature rules.
- **Statutory Auditor Changes:** If there is a change in the statutory auditor, members must indicate 'Yes' and provide reasons (with mandatory explanation if 'Resignation' or 'Others' is selected).
- **Networth Tab:** Members must report Fixed and Variable Net Worth and upload a CA-certified Networth Certificate with computation.
- **Saving vs. Submitting:** Clicking 'Save' generates a reference number and saves entered values/documents without submitting the form to NCL.

## Regulatory Changes

Migration of annual return submissions to the new NCL member portal with structured document nomenclature and constitution-specific sub-modules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Access the NCL member portal and navigate to Compliance > Annual Return Submission > New Submission.
- Fill out all mandatory fields across applicable tabs.
- Upload scanned PDF documents using the exact naming convention (`XXXXXDocumentName31MarYYYY`, where XXXXX is the 5-digit member code).
- Ensure proper disclosure of statutory auditor changes and CA-certified networth calculations.

## Important Dates

- Financial Year: 2025-26
- As on Date / Year reference in document nomenclature: 2026

## Impact Assessment

Streamlines compliance filing for trading members by consolidating annual return submissions onto the new NCL portal with standardized document naming conventions and validation checks.
