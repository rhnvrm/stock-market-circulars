## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the admission of privately placed securities (commercial papers and debt securities) to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from October 15, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 15, 2026.
- **Segment:** Debt Market Segment of the Exchange.
- **Issuers Include:**
  - The Ramco Cements Limited (Commercial Paper, Allotment: 14-Oct-2026, Redemption: 27-Nov-2026, Face Value: ₹5,00,000)
  - Kotak Securities Limited (Commercial Paper, Allotment: 14-Oct-2026, Redemption: 05-Jan-2027)
  - Alphagrep Securities Private Limited (Commercial Paper, Allotment: 14-Oct-2026, Redemption: 13-Oct-2027)
  - Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited (Commercial Paper, Allotment: 14-Oct-2026, Redemption: 29-Jan-2027)
  - Chambal Fertilizers & Chemicals Limited (Commercial Paper, Allotment: 14-Oct-2026, Redemption: 18-Dec-2026)
  - Reliance Retail Ventures Limited (Commercial Paper, Allotment: 14-Oct-2026, Redemption: 15-Dec-2026)
  - Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (Commercial Paper, Allotment: 14-Oct-2026, Redemption: 16-Nov-2026)
  - L&T Finance Limited (Commercial Papers, Allotments: 14-Oct-2026 and 15-Oct-2026)
  - Hero FinCorp Limited (Other debt securities, 9.25% 2037 Sr 82)

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of privately placed commercial papers and debt securities to dealings on the Debt Market Segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and trading members must ensure adherence to trading, clearing, and settlement guidelines specified by the Exchange for debt market segment transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Listing/Effective Date:** October 15, 2026
- **Allotment Dates:** October 14, 2026 and October 15, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Ranging from November 16, 2026 to October 13, 2027 (and Hero FinCorp 2037 debt security).

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances liquidity and trading opportunities in the debt market segment by admitting fresh commercial paper issues and debt securities from prominent corporate issuers.
