## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding measures in respect of companies with high Promoter as well as non-Promoter 'Encumbrance' pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Indo Tech Transformers Limited (INDOTECH) moves out of the framework, while Race Eco Chain Limited (RACE) remains on the consolidated list.

## Key Points

- No new securities are added to Annexure I for this review period.
- Indo Tech Transformers Limited (INDOTECH, ISIN: INE332H01014) is excluded from the framework effective October 01, 2026.
- Race Eco Chain Limited (RACE, ISIN: INE084Q01012) is included in the consolidated list of securities under the framework.
- This circular is in continuation of Exchange circular no NSE/SURV/51189 dated January 31, 2022.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic review of securities with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance under SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the security exclusions and consolidated list for proper margin and risk management.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for securities moving out of the framework: October 01, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding positions in affected securities must align their risk parameters and margin planning accordingly.
