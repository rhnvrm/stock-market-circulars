## Summary

Pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement, National Stock Exchange has announced that K.M. Sugar Mills Limited (KMSUGAR) will be available for trading in the Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE) starting October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: K.M. Sugar Mills Limited (KMSUGAR)
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026 (Thursday)
- Segment: Trade for Trade (Series: BE)
- Pre-open session: Part of call auction in pre-open session as per SEBI Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading segment change pursuant to Scheme of Arrangement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the change in trading segment for KMSUGAR and ensure compliance with trade for trade settlement rules.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in KMSUGAR will be settled on a trade-for-trade basis from the effective date, impacting liquidity and requiring mandatory delivery for trades.
