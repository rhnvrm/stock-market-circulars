## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the transfer of Inox Green Energy Services Limited (INOXGREEN) from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ) with effect from August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security**: Inox Green Energy Services Limited (INOXGREEN)
- **Transfer Details**: From trade for trade segment (series: BE) to rolling segment (series: EQ)
- **Effective Date**: August 14, 2026 (Friday)
- **Regulatory Authority**: Pursuant to SEBI guidelines under circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes. This is an entity-specific transition executed in accordance with existing SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their backend systems and trading terminals to reflect the shift from Series BE (trade-for-trade) to Series EQ (rolling segment) for INOXGREEN prior to the market open on August 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date of Transfer**: August 14, 2026 (Friday)

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity**: Significant increase in liquidity is expected for INOXGREEN as the rolling segment allows intraday netting, unlike the trade for trade segment which requires 100% upfront delivery/payment for all trades without netting.
- **Market/Operations**: Low overall systemic impact, but operationally essential for brokers and active traders of INOXGREEN.
