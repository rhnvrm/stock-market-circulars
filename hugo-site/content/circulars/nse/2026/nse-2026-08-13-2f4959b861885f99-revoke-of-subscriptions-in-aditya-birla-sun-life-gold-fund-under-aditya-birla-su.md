## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the resumption of fresh lumpsum subscriptions and switch-in applications for Aditya Birla Sun Life Gold Fund under Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh lumpsum subscriptions and switch-ins have been resumed without any restriction.
- Applicable to Aditya Birla Sun Life Gold Fund.
- Effective date: August 13, 2026.
- Based on Notice-cum-Addendum No. 45/2026 issued by Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational update regarding scheme subscription availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors can resume fresh lumpsum investments and switch-ins into the scheme on the platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors can now freely invest lumpsum amounts or switch into the Aditya Birla Sun Life Gold Fund through the NSE MF Invest Platform without prior restrictions.
