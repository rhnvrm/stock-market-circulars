## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited announced that operations for the Commodity Derivatives Segment will be conducted from its Disaster Recovery (DR) Site on September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Operations for the Commodity Derivatives Segment will run from the Disaster Recovery Site on September 3 and 4, 2026.
- Members accessing N-MASS and NCMS applications do not need to make any configuration changes or connection parameter updates.
- N-MASS and NCMS applications will be unavailable for live operations or updates during a scheduled downtime window.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; operational notice regarding DR site switchover.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must note the downtime window and ensure no live operations or updates are attempted during the restricted period.

## Important Dates

- DR Operations Dates: September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026.
- Application Downtime: From 01:00 hrs on September 05, 2026, to 08:00 hrs on September 05, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on members as no connection setting modifications are required, though applications will experience temporary unavailability during scheduled maintenance/downtime.
