## Summary

Helios Mutual Fund has issued a notice cum addendum announcing the introduction of the Monthly Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) Option under the existing Regular and Direct plans of Helios Balanced Advantage Fund, effective September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- **New Option:** Monthly IDCW Option introduced under Regular and Direct plans of Helios Balanced Advantage Fund.
- **Effective Date:** September 15, 2026.
- **Allotment Price:** Units created on the first day of subscription under the monthly IDCW option will be allotted at Rs. 10/- per unit and thereafter at NAV-based prices.
- **Sub-Options:** Re-investment of IDCW (default), Transfer of IDCW, and Pay-out of IDCW.
- **Record Date:** Last Wednesday of each month (or immediately succeeding business day if non-business day).

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a scheme-level addendum regarding product features.

## Compliance Requirements

- Unit holders are requested to update their PAN, KYC, email address, mobile number, and nominee details with the AMC and link their PAN with Aadhaar.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 01, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides investors with an additional payout/withdrawal frequency option under the scheme with no change to the underlying investment objective or expense ratio.
