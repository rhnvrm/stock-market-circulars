## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released a revised list of approved securities, GOI securities, open-ended mutual funds, corporate bonds, and empaneled banks for the commodity derivatives segment. This circular supersedes the previous circular dated August 21, 2026, and is effective for the month of October 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised list covers equity shares, ETFs, Sovereign Gold Bonds, G-Secs/T-Bills, open-ended mutual funds, and corporate bonds eligible as non-cash and cash components of liquid assets.
- Value-based prudential norms specify that exclusion of a pledged security should not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.
- G-Sec/T Bills have a shut period of two days; members are requested to release them at least two days before maturity.
- Banks acting as members should ensure G-Secs provided as collaterals are not reckoned for SLR purposes or used for trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated market-wide limits, member-specific limits, and haircut rates across multiple annexures for approved collaterals.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must take note of the revised limits, haircut rates, and release G-Sec/T-Bills prior to their shut/maturity period.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts clearing members and custodians trading in the commodity derivatives segment by updating acceptable collateral valuations and limits.
