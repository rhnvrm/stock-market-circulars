## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the proposed Offer for Sale (OFS) of Hindustan Copper Limited shares scheduled to take place on August 25, 2026, and August 26, 2026. Eligible trading members can participate using the eOFS platform.

## Key Points

- Dates of Offer: August 25, 2026, and August 26, 2026.
- Base Offer Size: Upto 2,90,10,721 Equity shares, with an equal Oversubscription Option of upto 2,90,10,721 Equity shares, and additionally 25,000 Equity shares for the Eligible Employee Option.
- Face Value: Rs. 5 each.
- Categories: IS (non-retail), RS (Retail category - Bid value <= Rs 2 lacs), and ES (Employee category - Bid value <= Rs 5 lacs).
- Platform: NSE eOFS platform (https://eofs.nseindia.com).

## Regulatory Changes

The Offer for Sale complies with the implementation of Interoperability among Clearing Corporations along with the Exchange Scheme circular NSE/CMTR/72975 dated February 24, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible trading members must use the eOFS platform for participation.
- Members requiring Admin user IDs must submit details on the ENIT portal under ENIT-NEW-TRADE→Membership→Enablement→eOFS.
- Compliant Unique Client Codes (UCC) of self (PRO) and clients must be updated in the Unique Client Identification (UCI) system a day prior to the OFS bidding session.
- Orders will not be accepted if the UCC becomes compliant on the day of the OFS bidding session.
- Members must ensure sufficiency of collaterals before initiating carry forward of orders on T+1 day.

## Important Dates

- Announcement/Intimation of Oversubscription: On or before 5:00 PM on August 25, 2026.
- Bidding Dates: August 25, 2026, and August 26, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates institutional and retail participation in the Hindustan Copper Limited share sale through streamlined electronic bidding infrastructure while enforcing strict UCC and collateral compliance.
