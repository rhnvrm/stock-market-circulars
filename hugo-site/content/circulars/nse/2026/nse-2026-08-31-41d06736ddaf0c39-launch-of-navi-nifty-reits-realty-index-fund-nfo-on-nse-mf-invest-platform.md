## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the Navi Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) by NAVI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from September 01, 2026, to September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: NAVI Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Navi Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund (Direct Plan - Growth & Regular Plan - Growth)
- ISINs: INF959L01HT0 (Direct), INF959L01HU8 (Regular)
- Category: Equity
- NFO Window: September 01, 2026, to September 10, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 11, 2026, till 3:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 01, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 10, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 11, 2026 (3:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors an opportunity to transact in the new Navi Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund NFO through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
