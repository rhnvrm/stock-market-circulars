## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a revised list of approved securities and banks under the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS), superseding circular no. 096/2026 dated September 21, 2026. This circular outlines the updated list of eligible securities, liquid assets, GOI securities, mutual funds, and empaneled banks effective for the month of November 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Month: November 2026.
- Supersedes: Circular No. 096/2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/CMPT/76469) dated Sep 21, 2026.
- Annexure 1: List of equity shares forming part of the non-cash component of liquid assets, along with market-wide limits, member-specific limits, and applicable haircut rates (VAR-based).
- Annexure 2 & 3: Lists of Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) acting as non-cash and cash components of liquid assets.
- Annexure 4: List of Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB), GOI Securities (G-Sec) / T-Bills with applicable haircut rates and a shut period of two days.
- Annexure 5: List of open-ended mutual funds acceptable as collateral with applicable limits and haircuts.
- Annexure 6: List of banks empaneled for issuing Bank Guarantees (BGs) and Fixed Deposit Receipts (FDRs).

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated permitted quantities, market-wide limits, memberwise limits, and haircut rates for approved equity shares, ETFs, G-Secs, and mutual funds used as collateral under SLBS.
- Reiteration of value-based prudential norms: Exclusion of pledged security should not result in margin shortfall exceeding 25% at a Clearing Member level.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and participants must verify the revised eligibility, permitted quantities, and haircut rates for securities pledged as collateral.
- G-Sec/T Bills have a shut period of two days; members must release G-Sec/T Bills at least two days before maturity.
- Member-banks must ensure that G-Secs provided as collateral are not reckoned for SLR purposes and are not used for trading.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026.
- Effective Date: Effective for the month of November 2026.
- G-Sec Maturity: Release at least two days before maturity due to the 2-day shut period.

## Impact Assessment

Clearing members, participants, and custodians must update their collateral management systems and risk parameters in alignment with the revised approved securities list, limits, and haircut schedules effective November 2026.
