## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Limited pursuant to its IPO, effective from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: ARCIL
- ISIN: INE148G01016
- Issue Price for current public issue: Rs. 139/-
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Total No. of Securities: 324,897,140
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be in the specified lot sizes and identified by its designated codes. Subject to pre-open session for IPO and other scrips as per relevant SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Limited equity shares on the NSE Capital Market segment.
