## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued an update regarding the streamlining of End-of-Day (EOD) information dissemination related to Bhavcopy reports. The Exchange is additionally disseminating certain Bhavcopy reports (.DAT format) on the Extranet and has extended the discontinuation date for legacy formats (.MS and .MD) and broadcast methods from October 12, 2026, to November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Additional dissemination of Bhavcopy reports in .DAT format on Extranet paths for various segments (F&O, CD, CO, CM, and Electronic Gold Receipts).
- Effective date for additional CM and EGR Bhavcopy reports dissemination is October 12, 2026.
- Discontinuation date for Extranet Bhavcopy reports in .MS and .MD formats and via broadcasts extended to November 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of timelines for phasing out legacy Bhavcopy report formats (.ms and .md) on the Extranet.
- Standardization of .DAT format reporting across segments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their retrieval systems and automated pipelines to ingest Bhavcopy reports from the updated Extranet paths and formats before the final discontinuation date.

## Important Dates

- October 12, 2026: Effective date for additional CM and EGR Bhavcopy reports dissemination on Extranet.
- November 16, 2026: Discontinuation date for legacy .MS and .MD Bhavcopy reports on Extranet and broadcast dissemination.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact for members relying on legacy .MS/.MD formats or broadcast data feeds, requiring migration to the specified Extranet .DAT file paths.
