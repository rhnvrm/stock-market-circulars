## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the update on the settlement calendar for October 2026 and November 2026 for the 'R3' series under the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS). The first leg settlement for the R3 series continues under Settlement Type 'L', while schedules for reverse leg settlement (Settlement Type 'P') and reverse leg auction (Settlement Type 'Q') have been detailed.

## Key Points

- First leg settlement for R3 series continues to be conducted under Settlement Type 'L'.
- No change in settlement pay-in timings for settlement types 'L', 'P', and 'Q'.
- Reverse Leg Settlement (Settlement Type 'P') schedules are specified in Annexure 'A' covering trade dates through October and November 2026.
- Reverse Leg Auction (Settlement Type 'Q') schedules are specified in Annexure 'B'.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this circular provides routine settlement calendar updates for the SLBS R3 series.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians must adhere to the specified settlement dates and pay-in timings for the R3 series reverse leg settlements and auctions in October and November 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 09, 2026
- Settlement calendar covers trade dates across October 2026 and November 2026 with respective reverse leg settlement dates extending into December 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Operational clarification ensuring smooth clearing and settlement processes for market participants engaging in SLBS transactions.
