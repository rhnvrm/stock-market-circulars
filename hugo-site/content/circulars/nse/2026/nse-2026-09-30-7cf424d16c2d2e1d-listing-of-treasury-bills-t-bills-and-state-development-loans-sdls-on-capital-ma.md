## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the admission to dealings of Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on its capital market segment with effect from October 01, 2026, pursuant to relevant trading regulations.

## Key Points

- Admission of new SDLs and T-bills to trading on the capital market segment effective October 01, 2026.
- Includes securities such as SDL SK 7.55% 2030, SDL UP 7.95% 2051, GOI T-Bills (91D, 182D, 364D), and SDL SK 7.98% 2042.
- Lot sizes for trading are specified as 100 for all listed securities in the annexure.
- Securities will be identified by their designated codes for trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, securities are admitted with specified lot sizes and designated codes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes when executing transactions in these listed T-bills and SDLs.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026
- Maturity Dates ranging from December 31, 2026 to September 30, 2051 across specific instruments.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market liquidity and trading for newly issued state development loans and government treasury bills.
