## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the movement of Next Mediaworks Limited to Stage I of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM), effective September 02, 2026. This measure applies specific trading restrictions and enhanced margins to safeguard investors.

## Key Points

- **Security Affected:** Next Mediaworks Limited (Symbol: NEXTMEDIA, ISIN: INE747B01016).
- **Effective Date:** September 02, 2026.
- **Applicable Restrictions:** 100% margin rate and a price band of 5% or lower.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities moved to Stage I of the GSM framework are subject to heightened monitoring and specific trading controls to prevent excessive volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities and ensure compliance with the mandated margin requirements.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 1, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in NEXTMEDIA will be restricted by a 5% price band and require a 100% margin rate, which may reduce liquidity and alter short-term trading dynamics for market participants.
