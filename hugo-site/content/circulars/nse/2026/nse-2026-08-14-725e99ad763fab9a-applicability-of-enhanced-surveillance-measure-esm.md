## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Certain securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion, shifting, or moving out of the ESM framework, attracting 100% margins and trade-for-trade settlement.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I are included under ESM Stage I and shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 18, 2026, on open positions as on August 17, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage I or Stage II are moved from Rolling Settlement segment (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST).
- Securities moving out of the framework have their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Application of 100% margin on open and new positions for shortlisted securities.
- Shifting of securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and segment shifts for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026 / August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement for shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
