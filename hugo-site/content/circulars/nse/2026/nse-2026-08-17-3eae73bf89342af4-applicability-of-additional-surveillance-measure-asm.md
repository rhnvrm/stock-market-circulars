## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Certain securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM).

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. August 20, 2026, on all open positions as on August 19, 2026, and new positions created from August 20, 2026 onwards.
- Securities listed in the Annexure include Ganga Forging Limited (GANGAFORGE) and Sansera Engineering Limited (SANSERA) under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I.
- Shortlisting of securities under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company/entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of 100% margin requirements for short-listed securities under the LTASM framework.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure that 100% margins are maintained for open and new positions on the affected securities starting August 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions / Margins: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (100%) for shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
