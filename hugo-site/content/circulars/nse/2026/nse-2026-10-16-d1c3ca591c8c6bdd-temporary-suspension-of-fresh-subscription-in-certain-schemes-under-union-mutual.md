## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that Union Mutual Fund has temporarily disabled certain schemes, specifically the Union Gold ETF Fund of Fund, for fresh subscriptions, lumpsum purchases, switch-ins, and fresh registrations of Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Scheme: Union Gold ETF Fund of Fund (Direct and Regular Plans, all available options).
- Suspended Activities: Fresh/additional subscriptions through lumpsum purchases, switches, fresh SIP/STP registrations, and top-ups of existing SIPs.
- Unaffected Activities: Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, STP-outs, and existing SIP/STP registrations prior to the effective date.
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026, till further notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary operational suspension of fresh capital inflows into the specified mutual fund scheme as requested by Union Asset Management Company.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and platform participants must immediately update their order routing systems to restrict fresh subscriptions and new systematic registrations for Union Gold ETF Fund of Fund while ensuring redemptions and existing SIP/STP processing remain unhindered.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors are restricted from making fresh investments into Union Gold ETF Fund of Fund through lumpsum or new SIP/STP mandates. Existing investors retain full liquidity via redemptions and SWPs.
