## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Several securities have been shortlisted for Stage I and Stage II of the ST-ASM framework, resulting in revised margin requirements to ensure market stability and prevent excessive volatility.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusion**: Five securities (AASTHA, ASIANTILES, IPHL, MBAPL, NILKAMAL) have satisfied the criteria for inclusion in Short-Term ASM Stage I w.e.f. July 30, 2026.
- **ST-ASM Stage I to II Movement**: Grand Oak Canyons Distillery Limited (GRANDOAK) is shortlisted to move from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II w.e.f. July 30, 2026.
- **Margin Action Stage I**: Margin rate is revised to 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%, effective July 31, 2026.
- **Margin Action Stage II**: Margin rate is revised to 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%, effective July 31, 2026.
- **Nature of ASM Action**: ASM selection is solely a surveillance measure to maintain market order and does not represent any adverse regulatory or punitive action against the shortlisted companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Short-Term ASM Stage I**: Margin requirements are adjusted to 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. July 31, 2026 on all open positions as of July 30, 2026, and new positions created from July 31, 2026.
- **Short-Term ASM Stage II**: Margin requirements are adjusted to 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. July 31, 2026 on all open positions as of July 30, 2026, and new positions created from July 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must update their risk management systems and margin collection processes to reflect the revised margin requirements (50% for Stage I, 100% for Stage II) for the shortlisted securities.
- **Position Monitoring**: Ensure the updated margins are applied w.e.f. July 31, 2026, for all outstanding open positions as of July 30, 2026, and any new positions created thereafter.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Shortlisting Effective Date**: July 30, 2026
- **Margin Revision Effective Date**: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Impact**: Medium. Shortlisted securities will face increased capital requirements, potentially reducing short-term trading liquidity and volume for the affected stocks. However, the measure does not impact other market segments.
- **Affected Securities**:
  - **Short-Term ASM Stage I**: Aastha Spintex Limited (AASTHA), Asian Granito India Limited (ASIANTILES), Indian Phosphate Limited (IPHL), Madhya Bharat Agro Products Limited (MBAPL), and Nilkamal Limited (NILKAMAL).
  - **Stage I to Stage II Movement**: Grand Oak Canyons Distillery Limited (GRANDOAK).
