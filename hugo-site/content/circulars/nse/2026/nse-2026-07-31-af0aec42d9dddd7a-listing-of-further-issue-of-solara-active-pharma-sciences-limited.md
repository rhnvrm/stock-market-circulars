## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the listing of further equity shares of Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited. A total of 5,999 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each, converted from partly paid-up to fully paid-up equity shares, will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment of the Exchange with effect from August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name**: Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited
- **Symbol**: SOLARA
- **Series**: EQ
- **ISIN**: INE624Z01016
- **Temporary ISIN**: IN8624Z01015 (credited in dematerialized mode in accordance with SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012)
- **Security Description**: Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares
- **Number of Securities**: 5,999 equity shares
- **Face Value**: Rs. 10/-
- **Paid-up Value**: Rs. 10/-
- **Market Lot**: 1
- **Pari Passu**: Yes
- **Lock-in Details**: Not Applicable
- **Distinctive Numbers**:
  - 46056075 to 46059212 (3,138 shares)
  - 47552760 to 47555620 (2,861 shares)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes. The admission of further securities is in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their databases and trading systems to recognize the new shares under the designated ISIN codes and market lot size for trading commencing August 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard operational listing procedure following the conversion of partly paid-up shares. The volume of shares listed (5,999) is extremely small and will have negligible impact on market liquidity or price volatility.
