## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement schedule for the Commodity Derivatives Segment for the month of September 2026. The circular provides a comprehensive list of trade dates and their corresponding settlement dates for market participants.

## Key Points

- The settlement calendar applies to all trades executed in the Commodity Derivatives Segment during September 2026.
- Includes trade dates starting from September 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Trades executed on September 11, 2026, and September 14, 2026, will both be settled on September 15, 2026.
- Standard trade settlement generally follows a next-day clearing cycle (T+1), adjusted for holidays.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced in this circular. It is a standard operational calendar release.

## Compliance Requirements

All NSE Clearing Members must ensure that their back-office systems, funds, and collateral allocations are aligned with the specified trade and settlement dates to ensure smooth operations and prevent settlement failures.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Trade Start Date**: September 01, 2026
- **Effective Trade End Date**: September 30, 2026
- **Double Settlement Date**: September 15, 2026 (for trades of September 11 and September 14)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Neutral. There is no disruption to standard trading procedures.
- **Operational Impact**: Low to medium. Clearing members need to plan for the consolidated settlement on September 15, 2026, to manage liquidity and margin requirements effectively.
