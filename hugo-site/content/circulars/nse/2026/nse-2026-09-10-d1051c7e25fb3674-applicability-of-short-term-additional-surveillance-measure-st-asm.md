## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for several securities based on market surveillance criteria effective September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been placed under ST-ASM Stage I w.e.f. September 11, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. September 15, 2026 on all open positions as on September 11, 2026 and new positions created from September 15, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.
- List of short-listed securities includes Anmol India Limited, Astec LifeSciences Limited, Avience Biomedicals Limited, Bal Pharma Limited, Chembond Material Technologies Limited, Indo Rama Synthetics (India) Limited, Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited, Novartis India Limited, Rbm Infracon Limited, Texmo Pipes and Products Limited, and Uday Jewellery Industries Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of stricter margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the increased margin requirements of 50% or existing margin (capped at 100%) on open and new positions starting September 15, 2026 for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of ST-ASM: September 11, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the short-listed securities.
