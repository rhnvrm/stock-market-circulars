## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the applicable Volatility Scan Range (VSR) for option contracts in the commodity derivatives segment for the month of October 2026, in continuation of earlier circulars and SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Applicable VSR for October 2026 across specified commodities:
  - COPPER: 5%
  - GOLD: 5%
  - SILVER: 6%
  - ZINC: 5%
  - CRUDEOIL: 30%
  - NATURALGAS: 6%
- Rates apply to the mentioned commodities including their variants.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic review and specification of Volatility Scan Range (VSR) for commodity derivatives options as per SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the specified VSR percentages for margin calculations and risk management.

## Important Dates

- Effective for the month of October 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts margin requirements and risk parameters for option contracts across specified commodity derivatives.
