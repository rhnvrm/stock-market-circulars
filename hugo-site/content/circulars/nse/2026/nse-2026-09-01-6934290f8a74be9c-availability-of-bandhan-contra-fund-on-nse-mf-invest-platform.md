## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that Bandhan Contra Fund of Bandhan Mutual Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective from September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available include regular and direct plans for growth and IDCW options.
- Facilities enabled: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: September 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with platform guidelines while executing orders for the listed schemes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to Bandhan Contra Fund schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
