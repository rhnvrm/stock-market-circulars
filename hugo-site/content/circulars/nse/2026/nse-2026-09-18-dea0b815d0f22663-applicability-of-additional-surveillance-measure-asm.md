## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for certain securities based on specified criteria. Applicable margin rates and movement between stages take effect from September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 23, 2026, on all open positions as on September 22, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term ASM framework provisions including 100% margin requirements and shifting of specific scrips to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on applicable open and new positions, and note segment shifts for qualifying securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margins and potential movement to the Trade-for-Trade segment increase trading friction and reduce liquidity for the affected securities.
