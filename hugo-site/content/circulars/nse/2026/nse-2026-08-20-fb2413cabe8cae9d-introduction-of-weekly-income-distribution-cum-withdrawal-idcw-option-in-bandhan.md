## Summary

Bandhan AMC Limited has issued a notice-cum-addendum announcing the introduction of the payout option under the weekly frequency of Income Distribution & Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) for select schemes, effective immediately.

## Key Points

- Weekly IDCW Payout option added to Bandhan Ultra Short Duration Fund, Bandhan Low Duration Fund, and Bandhan Money Market Fund.
- Affects existing IDCW options by enabling weekly payout capability alongside the existing weekly reinvestment option.
- All other features, terms, and conditions of the schemes remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Addition of weekly IDCW payout option under SID and KIM of select schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- The addendum forms an integral part of the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) of the respective schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: June 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides enhanced flexibility for investors opting for weekly IDCW frequencies across the specified debt and money market schemes.
