## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026. There is no new software version release associated with this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for October 24, 2026, with two trading sessions from the Primary Site and Disaster Recovery (DR) Site.
- NEAT+ version 1.0.2 is recommended, while version 1.0.1 discontinuation (mock) is scheduled for November 14, 2026.
- An additional mock trading session is scheduled for October 31, 2026.
- Trades executed during the mock session will not result in any financial obligations (fund pay-in or pay-out).
- Default password for login during mock sessions is "Neat@EG1".

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational mock trading circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the EGR segment are advised to note the schedule, gateway parameters, and connectivity details for testing.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 24, 2026
- Additional Mock Trading Date: October 31, 2026
- NEAT+ Version 1.0.1 Discontinuation (Mock): November 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact. Allows members to test connectivity, login parameters, and disaster recovery switchover procedures.
