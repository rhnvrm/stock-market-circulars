## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the suspension of trading in partly paid-up equity shares of Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited effective from August 19, 2026 (closing hours of trading on August 18, 2026), on account of the first & final call.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited
- **Symbol:** SSFLPP
- **ISIN No:** IN9572J01019
- **Reason for Suspension:** Suspension on account of first & final call

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the suspension of trading in the specified partly paid-up equity shares.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 19, 2026
- **Suspension from Trading (Closing Hours):** August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the partly paid-up shares (SSFLPP) will be halted on the exchange from the effective date pursuant to the call money process.
