## Summary

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, the Exchange has notified the admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment with effect from September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Debt Market Segment
- **Effective Date:** September 10, 2026
- **Issuers Include:** Godrej & Boyce Mfg Co Ltd, Aditya Birla Money Limited, LIC Housing Finance Limited, Hsbc Investdirect Financial Services (I) Limited, Tata Steel Limited, Axis Securities Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, Jm Financial Services Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, and L&T Finance Limited.
- **Instrument Type:** Commercial Papers (CP)

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; standard listing of debt securities pursuant to existing trading regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and issuers must ensure adherence to the specified ISINs, allotment dates, and redemption schedules.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates:** September 9, 2026 / September 10, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** September 10, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Various maturity dates ranging from October 2026 to September 2027.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market trading and settlement for the newly listed commercial papers.
