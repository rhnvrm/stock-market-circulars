## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities of Jio Credit Limited on the capital market segment with effect from September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Jio Credit Limited
- Security Description: JCL 8.08% 2029 Sr X
- Symbol: 808JCL29
- Series: N0
- Market Lot: 1
- Coupon Rate: 8.08%
- Date of Maturity: 31-Aug-2029
- ISIN Code: INE282H07109

## Regulatory Changes

None. The admission is granted pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and applicable lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 03, 2026
- Maturity Date: August 31, 2029

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt listing enabling secondary market trading for the specified NCD issue.
