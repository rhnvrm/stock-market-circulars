## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by Mirae Asset Investment Managers (India) Private Limited under the Mirae Asset BSE LargeMid (60:40) Stable Dividend 50 ETF scheme, effective from August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** LARGEMID50
- **ISIN:** INF769K01RK3
- **Number of Units:** 1,800,669
- **Face Value & Paid Up Value:** Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 36.94/-
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Date of Allotment:** August 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notification pursuant to NSE trading regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes specified for the ETF.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 14, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of the Mirae Asset BSE LargeMid (60:40) Stable Dividend 50 ETF units on the NSE Capital Market segment.
