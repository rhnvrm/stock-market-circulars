## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the extension of the Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) on Dated Brent Crude Oil (Platts) Futures for a further period of 3 months. The scheme will now remain in effect until December 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- The Liquidity Enhancement Scheme (LES) for Dated Brent Crude Oil (Platts) Futures is extended by 3 months.
- The scheme will now be valid until December 14, 2026.
- All other provisions from the previous circular (NSE/COM/74471 dated May 29, 2026) continue to apply.
- The Exchange retains the right to amend or discontinue the scheme with 15 days' advance notice.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an extension of an existing incentive scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

All members participating in commodity derivatives trading are required to note the extended validity of the LES program. No specific compliance actions are mandated.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Extension Period:** Up to December 14, 2026
- **Previous Reference Date:** May 29, 2026 (Circular No. NSE/COM/74471)

## Impact Assessment

Provides continued liquidity support and incentives for trading in Dated Brent Crude Oil (Platts) Futures contracts on the NSE commodity derivatives segment.
