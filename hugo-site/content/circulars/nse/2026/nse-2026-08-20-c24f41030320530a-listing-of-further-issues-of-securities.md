## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from August 21, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Covers further issuance of equity shares for companies including Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Anupam Rasayan India Limited, Arvind SmartSpaces Limited, Axis Bank Limited, Dabur India Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, Wipro Limited, and others.
- Shares are allotted primarily under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) or other corporate actions.
- Effective date for trading of these securities: August 21, 2026.
- Trading will take place in designated lot sizes with specific security codes as detailed in the exchange annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notification under National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A and Regulation 2.5.5.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that the securities are identified by their designated codes and traded in the specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion and equity listing for listed entities, resulting in minor increases in paid-up equity capital with negligible market impact.
