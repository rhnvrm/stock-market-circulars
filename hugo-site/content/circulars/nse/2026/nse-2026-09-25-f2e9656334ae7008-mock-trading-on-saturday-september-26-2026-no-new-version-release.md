## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, September 26, 2026. There is no new software version release for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: September 26, 2026.
- Segment: Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR).
- NEAT+ Version: 1.0.1 (/egftp/egcommon/NEATPlus101).
- Default password for mock session: Neat@EG1.
- No financial obligation: Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- Timings: Pre-open opens at 10:45, normal market opens at 11:00 and closes at 12:30, live re-login from 16:00 to 16:30.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine mock trading and system check session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the EGR segment are advised to participate in the mock session, ensure NEAT+ EGR Version 1.0.1 is installed as per circular NSE/MSD/76159, and verify login and broadcast parameters.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 26, 2026
- Live Re-login Window: September 26, 2026, 16:00 to 16:30 Hrs

## Impact Assessment

Operational readiness check for members in the EGR segment with zero financial impact.
