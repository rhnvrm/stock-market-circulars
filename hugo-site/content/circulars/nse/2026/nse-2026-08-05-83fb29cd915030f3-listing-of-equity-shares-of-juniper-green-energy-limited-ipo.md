## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Juniper Green Energy Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The exact listing date will be shared through a subsequent circular.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Juniper Green Energy Limited
- **Symbol:** JNPR
- **ISIN:** INE05C901015
- **Status:** Listing date to be communicated via a separate circular.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are established by this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no immediate compliance requirements for trading members, except for updating internal databases and trading terminals with the new symbol (JNPR) and ISIN (INE05C901015) in preparation for listing.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 05, 2026
- **Listing Date:** To be announced through a separate circular.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Introduces a new green energy equity security (JNPR) to the market.
- **Operational Impact:** Operations and back-office systems should be prepared to integrate the symbol JNPR and ISIN INE05C901015 once the official listing date is declared.
