## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the transfer of Bench Mark Infotech Services Limited from the trade-for-trade segment to the rolling segment.

## Key Points

- Company: Bench Mark Infotech Services Limited
- Symbol: BMISL
- Segment Change: Transferred from trade-for-trade segment (Series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer executed pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012 and exchange circular no. NSE/CML/76703 dated October 01, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must note the shift in trading segment and series from October 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates regular rolling settlement for BMISL shares, enhancing trading participation and liquidity.
