## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the reversal of action initiated against Quintegra Solutions Limited. Following the payment of outstanding Annual Listing Fees (ALF) by the company, trading in its securities will be shifted from the trade-for-trade category (BE series) to the normal trading segment (EQ series).

## Key Points

- Previous action against Quintegra Solutions Limited was taken due to non-payment of Annual Listing Fees (ALF).
- The company has successfully paid the outstanding dues to the Exchange.
- Trading category changes from BE series (trade-for-trade) to EQ series (normal trading).

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of trading restrictions associated with the trade-for-trade category for Quintegra Solutions Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- No further compliance action required regarding the past ALF dues as payment has been completed.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors can resume normal trading in the equity shares of Quintegra Solutions Limited effective September 28, 2026, improving liquidity and trading flexibility.
