## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that Wipro Ltd. (Symbol: WIPRO) will not be available for trading in the T+0 rolling settlement cycle on September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Affected:** Wipro Ltd. (WIPRO, Series: T0).
- **Date of Restriction:** September 30, 2026.
- **Reason:** Security is moving in/out of the index on which derivatives contracts are available for trading due to index rebalancing.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary exclusion of specific scrips from the T+0 rolling settlement cycle pursuant to capital market trading parameters.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the unavailability of T+0 settlement for Wipro Ltd. on the specified date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact; standard operational adjustment resulting from index rebalancing activities.
