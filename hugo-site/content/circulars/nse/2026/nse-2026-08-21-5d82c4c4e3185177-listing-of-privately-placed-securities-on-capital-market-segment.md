## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities of Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited on the capital market segment, effective from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited
- **Symbol:** 783TCHFL31
- **Series:** N0
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Issue Description:** TCHFL 7.83% 2031 Sr D
- **Coupon Rate:** 7.83%
- **Date of Maturity:** 20-Aug-2031
- **ISIN Code:** INE033L07IV6

## Regulatory Changes

None. The admission is pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the specified securities shall be conducted using the designated security codes and respective lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** August 20, 2031

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading for the newly listed privately placed non-convertible debentures of Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited.
