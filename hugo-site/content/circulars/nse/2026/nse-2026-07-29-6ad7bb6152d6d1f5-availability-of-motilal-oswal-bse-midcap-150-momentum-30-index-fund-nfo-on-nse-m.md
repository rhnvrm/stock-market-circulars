## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the reopening of the Motilal Oswal BSE Midcap 150 Momentum 30 Index Fund for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic transaction facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Reopening of Motilal Oswal BSE Midcap 150 Momentum 30 Index Fund for ongoing transactions.
- Facilities enabled include ongoing subscription, redemption, Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP).
- Applies to both Direct Plan Growth and Regular Plan Growth options on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or structural changes are introduced by this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

No active compliance actions are required for members, other than standard readiness to process these scheme transactions on the platform.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Effective Date:** July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Low. This is a routine operational enablement of specific mutual fund schemes, facilitating transaction access for registered platform users.
- **Enabled Schemes:**
  - **Direct Plan Growth:** ISIN INF247L01HE2
  - **Regular Plan Growth:** ISIN INF247L01HF9
