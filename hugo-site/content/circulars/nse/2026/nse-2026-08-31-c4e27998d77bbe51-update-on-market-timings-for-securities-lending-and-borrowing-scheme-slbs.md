## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued an update regarding the market timings for the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS). In partial modification of previous circular NCL/CMPT/38690, the market open time is being preponed to 09:00 AM.

## Key Points

- Market open time for SLBS is revised to 09:00 AM.
- Revised trading hours for the SLB Scheme are 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM.
- Applicable to all participants and custodians.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension / revision of trading timings for the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS).

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians are advised to take note of the revised market timings and align their operational systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 07, 2026
- Circular Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants engaged in securities lending and borrowing will have extended morning hours starting from 09:00 AM to execute transactions.
