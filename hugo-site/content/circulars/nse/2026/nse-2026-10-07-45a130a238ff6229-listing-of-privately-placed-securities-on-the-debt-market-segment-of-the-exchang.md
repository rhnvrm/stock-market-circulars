## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities, including commercial papers and other debt securities, on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from October 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fresh Issue - Commercial Papers:**
  - **Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited:** 70,000 CPs (HPCL CP 05/11/26 Sr 63), ISIN: INE094A14KS9, maturing on 05-Nov-2026.
  - **Godrej Agrovet Limited:** 1,000 CPs (GAL CP 30/11/26 Sr 23), ISIN: INE850D14WD5, maturing on 30-Nov-2026.

- **Fresh Issue - Other Debt Securities:**
  - **IIFL Finance Limited:** Market Linked Debentures (IIFL Mkt lkd 2030 Sr MLD 1), ISIN: INE530B07690, maturing on 08-Apr-2030.
  - **Jio Credit Limited:** 176,650 units of 8.59% 2031 Sr XI (ISIN: INE282H07125) and 85,000 units of 0% 2030 Sr XII (ISIN: INE282H07117).

- **Re-Issue - Commercial Papers:**
  - **Hdfc Securities Limited:** 6,000 CPs (HSL CP 11/12/2026), ISIN: INE700G14WN4, maturing on 11-Dec-2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuing entities must ensure adherence to ongoing disclosure and debt listing compliance standards.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 07, 2026
- **Allotment Dates:** October 05, 2026 to October 07, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Various dates ranging from November 05, 2026 to October 05, 2031

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing update facilitating secondary market trading for the newly issued debt instruments and commercial papers.
