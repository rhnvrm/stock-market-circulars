## Summary

360 ONE Asset Management Limited has issued a notice-cum-addendum announcing changes to the parameters, nomenclature, and asset allocation of its schemes (such as 360 ONE Focused Fund) to align with SEBI's revised Mutual Fund Scheme Categorization and Rationalization Framework dated February 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Updates made in accordance with SEBI circular on Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes dated February 26, 2026.
- Modifications apply to nomenclature, type, investment objective, and asset allocation pattern.
- Effective date for changes is August 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SEBI's revised scheme Categorization and Rationalization Framework to bring greater clarity, comparability, and investor protection.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMC and fund houses must update scheme parameters, SID, and KIM within the stipulated 6-month timeline from the SEBI circular issuance date.

## Important Dates

- SEBI Circular Date: February 26, 2026
- Effective Date of Changes: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors in 360 ONE Mutual Fund schemes will experience updated scheme classifications and nomenclature reflecting revised regulatory guidelines.
