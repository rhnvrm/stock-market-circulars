## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Certain securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under the framework and will attract a minimum 100% margin along with a shift to the trade-for-trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I (Aarti Surfactants Limited, Shekhawati Industries Limited, and Vivo Collaboration Solutions Limited) are shortlisted under ESM Stage - I.
- Securities qualifying under ESM will shift from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 17, 2026.
- A 100% margin will be levied on all open positions as on August 14, 2026, and new positions created from August 17, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 14, 2026.
- The shortlisting is strictly on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework rules, shifting specified scrips to Trade-for-Trade settlement and imposing strict margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for the affected securities, and note the shift to the trade-for-trade settlement segment.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Margin/Stage II Move:** August 14, 2026
- **Effective Date for Settlement Shift (EQ/SM to BE/ST):** August 17, 2026
- **Open Positions Evaluation Date:** August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected scrips (AARTISURF, SHEKHAWATI, VIVO) will experience reduced liquidity due to the trade-for-trade settlement restriction and higher capital requirements from the 100% margin mandate.
