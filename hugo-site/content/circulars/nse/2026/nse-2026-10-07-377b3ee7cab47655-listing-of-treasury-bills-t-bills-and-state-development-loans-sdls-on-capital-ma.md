## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of multiple Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on the capital market segment with effect from October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities will be available for trading starting October 09, 2026.
- Lot sizes for all listed T-bills and SDLs are set at 100.
- Trading will be conducted using designated security codes and ISINs as specified in the circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure orders are placed using the designated security codes and correct lot sizes (100).

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 07, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands trading opportunities in government securities and state development loans for market participants on the capital market segment.
