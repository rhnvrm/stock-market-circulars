## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for multiple companies, including Amagi Media Labs Limited, Carborundum Universal Limited, The Federal Bank Limited, Genesys International Corporation Limited, and others, effective from August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities further admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective August 27, 2026.
- Includes shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and other categories.
- Trading will take place under the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notification under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure trading is conducted using the designated security codes and applicable lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action facilitating the trading of newly allotted shares on the exchange capital market segment.
