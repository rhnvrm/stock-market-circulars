## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a change in the trading symbol for ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- The trading symbol changes from ICICIPRULI to ICICILIFE.
- Effective date for the symbol change is October 23, 2026.
- All contracts in the security will be traded under the new symbol from the effective date.
- Reference made to earlier circular NSE/CML/76967 dated October 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians are advised to take note of the symbol change and update their systems accordingly for SLBS transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for market participants dealing in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme to ensure smooth transition of contract identification.
