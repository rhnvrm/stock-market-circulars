## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced a face value split for TD Power Systems Limited (TDPOWERSYS). The face value and paid-up value of equity shares will be reduced from Rs. 2 to Re. 1 per share, effective from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TD Power Systems Limited
- Symbol: TDPOWERSYS
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/-
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Re. 1/-
- Effective Date: August 24, 2026

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the change in face value and paid-up value for TD Power Systems Limited in their trading and back-office systems.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The stock split alters the share price denomination and increases the number of outstanding shares proportionally, affecting trading lots and liquidity.
