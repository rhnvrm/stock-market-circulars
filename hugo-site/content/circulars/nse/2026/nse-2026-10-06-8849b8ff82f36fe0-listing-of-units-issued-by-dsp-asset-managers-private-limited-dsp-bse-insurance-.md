## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of units issued by DSP Asset Managers Private Limited under the DSP BSE Insurance ETF scheme, effective from October 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: DSP BSE Insurance ETF
- Trading Symbol: INSURADD
- ISIN: INF740KA1ZR8
- Number of Units: 7,206,699
- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: October 07, 2026
- Face Value / Paid Up Value: Rs. 10/- per unit
- Issue Price: Rs. 14.47/- per unit
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes specified for trading.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading of DSP BSE Insurance ETF units on the NSE capital market segment, providing liquidity and investment access for market participants in the insurance sector ETF.
