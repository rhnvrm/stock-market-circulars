## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, October 10, 2026. There is no new version release associated with this mock trading session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, October 10, 2026.
- Conducted across two trading sessions: Trading Session-1 from Primary Site and Trading Session-2 from DR Site.
- NEAT+ version 7.8.9 discontinuation date is October 10, 2026, while version 7.9.0 remains active.
- Testing of revision in tick size for block trading session (circular NSE/CMTR/76441) will be available, effective live from October 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; routine mock trading session for system verification and member testing.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to participate in the mock trading session to test their connectivity, order management, and DR switchover procedures. NEAT+ version 7.8.9 users must ensure migration/readiness as applicable.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 10, 2026
- NEAT+ 7.8.9 Discontinuation Date: October 10, 2026
- Tick Size Revision Effective Date (Live): October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal and routine. Members can validate trading application setups, disaster recovery procedures, and block trading tick size revisions in a simulated environment.
