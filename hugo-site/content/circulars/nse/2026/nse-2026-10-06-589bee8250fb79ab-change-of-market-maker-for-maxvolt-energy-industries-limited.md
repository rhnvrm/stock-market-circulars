## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular announcing the change of market maker for Maxvolt Energy Industries Limited (Symbol: MAXVOLT). The market maker responsibility has been transferred from Rikhav Securities Limited to Shreni Shares Limited, effective October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** MAXVOLT ENERGY INDUSTRIES LIMITED
- **Symbol:** MAXVOLT
- **Old Market Maker:** RIKHAV SECURITIES LIMITED (Member Code - 12804)
- **New Market Maker:** SHRENI SHARES LIMITED (Member Code - 14109)
- **Effective Date:** 08-Oct-26

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine administrative update under the SME segment market making framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the updated market maker details for MAXVOLT and update their systems and records accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 06, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Limited impact restricted to the liquidity support and market making operations of MAXVOLT in the SME segment.
