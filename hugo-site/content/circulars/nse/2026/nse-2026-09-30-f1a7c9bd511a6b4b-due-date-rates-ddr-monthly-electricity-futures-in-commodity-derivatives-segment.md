## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the Due Date Rates (DDR) for Monthly Electricity Futures contracts in the commodity derivatives segment that expired on September 29, 2026, pursuant to circular nos. NCL/COM/69054 and NCL/COM/76047.

## Key Points

- Commodity: ELECMBL (Monthly Electricity Futures)
- Expiry Date: September 29, 2026
- DDR Price: 4949.00
- Base Quantity: 1
- Base Unit: MWh

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine final settlement price notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to take note of the Due Date Rates for final settlement of expired contracts.

## Important Dates

- Expiry Date / DDR Date: September 29, 2026
- Circular Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine settlement price publication impacting clearing and settlement operations for market participants holding positions in the expired electricity futures contract.
