## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the merger of certain schemes of Axis Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Specifically, the Axis Nifty SDL September 2026 Debt Index Fund (merging scheme) is being merged into the Axis Banking & PSU Debt Fund (surviving scheme) effective after business hours on September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Merging Scheme:** Axis Nifty SDL September 2026 Debt Index Fund
- **Surviving Scheme:** Axis Banking & PSU Debt Fund
- **Effective Date:** Merger takes effect after close of business hours on September 30, 2026.
- **Exit Load Window:** Investors not in favour of the merger can redeem or switch their investments without paying an exit load from August 31, 2026, up to 5:30 PM on September 29, 2026.
- **SIP/SWP Handling:** Existing SIP/XSIP/SWP will continue to be processed in the merging scheme during the exit load period and will automatically shift to the surviving scheme after business hours on September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory policy changes; this is a fund house scheme merger communicated through exchange operational channels.

## Compliance Requirements

Investors and members must note the timeline for exit load-free redemption/switches and ensure instructions for ongoing systematic plans (SIP/SWP/STP) are managed accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Exit Load-Free Period Start:** August 31, 2026
- **Exit Load-Free Period End:** September 29, 2026 (up to 5:30 PM)
- **Merger Effective Date:** September 30, 2026 (after business hours)

## Impact Assessment

Existing unitholders of the merging scheme will have their units migrated to the surviving scheme or can exit fee-free during the specified window. Operations on the NSE MF Invest platform will reflect the merged scheme structure post-September 30, 2026.
