## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) is conducting a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment from the Business Continuity Plan (BCP) / Disaster Recovery (DR) site on Saturday, August 22, 2026. The session will be followed by a Re-Log in session from the Primary site to ensure seamless login for market participants on Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mock trading from BCP site is scheduled for Saturday, August 22, 2026, from 09:00 hrs to 17:00 hrs (Trade modification end time: 17:15 hrs).
- Re-Log in Session from Primary Site is scheduled from 18:30 hrs to 19:00 hrs.
- No changes in NEAT Adapter settings are required for connecting to the Primary/DR site.
- The exchange will execute a non-graceful shutdown from the primary site during the mock session to test switchover procedures.
- All outstanding orders will be purged before trading starts from the BCP site.
- Multicast TBT sequence numbers will start from '1' once trading commences from the BCP site.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory policy changes; the session complies with regulatory requirements for disaster recovery and business continuity testing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must participate using all trading software and re-login to the live environment to verify connectivity.
- Members using NNF software must manually clear outstanding orders of session 1 before trading commences from the BCP site.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 22, 2026
- Live Operations Resume: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures member system readiness, tests disaster recovery switchover mechanics, and prevents login issues for live trading sessions.
