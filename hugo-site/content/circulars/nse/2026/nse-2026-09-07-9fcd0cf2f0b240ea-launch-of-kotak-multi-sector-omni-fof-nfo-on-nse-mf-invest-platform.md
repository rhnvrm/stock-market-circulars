## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the launch and transaction dates for the Kotak Multi Sector Omni Fund of Funds (FOF) New Fund Offer (NFO) of Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Subscription period: September 08, 2026 to September 22, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- Multiple regular and direct plans (Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, IDCW Payout) are available with ISINs provided.
- Allotment date is scheduled for September 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 25, 2026 till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 08, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 22, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 25, 2026 (12:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading and investment participants to access the Kotak Multi Sector Omni FOF NFO through the NSE platform.
