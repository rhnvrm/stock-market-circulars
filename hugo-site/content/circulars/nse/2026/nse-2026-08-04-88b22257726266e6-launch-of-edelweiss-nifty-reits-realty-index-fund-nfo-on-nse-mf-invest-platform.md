## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the launch of the Edelweiss Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund NFO (New Fund Offer) on its NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription period for the NFO runs from August 05, 2026, to August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Edelweiss Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund (available in Direct and Regular plans with Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- **Platform Availability:** NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **NFO Period:** August 05, 2026, to August 19, 2026 (until 11:59 pm).
- **Allotment Date:** August 26, 2026.
- **Fund Clearance Deadline:** Members must ensure clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 24, 2026, by 06:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no direct regulatory changes introduced in this circular; it is an operational announcement regarding the availability of a new mutual fund scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Members/Distributors:** Ensure that clear funds for subscriptions are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account by the specified deadline of August 24, 2026, 06:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 05, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** August 19, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Clear Funds Deadline:** August 24, 2026 (till 06:00 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low. This is a routine launch of an NFO on the mutual fund platform, enabling trading/investment in a new index fund targeting the REITs and Realty segment.
