## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued an update regarding the listing of partly paid-up equity shares allotted on a rights basis of Party Cruisers Limited, amending the previous circular NSE/CML/76984 dated October 16, 2026. The market lot for the partly paid-up rights issue is now specified as 166 equity shares.

## Key Points

- Amends previous circular NSE/CML/76984 dated October 16, 2026.
- Market Lot for the Partly Paid-up Rights Issue of Party Cruisers Limited is revised to 166 equity shares.
- Effective date of implementation: October 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Market lot size for the partly paid-up equity shares of Party Cruisers Limited is clarified and set to 166 equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members of the Exchange must ensure orders are placed in accordance with the revised market lot size of 166 equity shares effective October 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures accurate order execution and clearing operations for trading members dealing in the partly paid-up shares of Party Cruisers Limited.
