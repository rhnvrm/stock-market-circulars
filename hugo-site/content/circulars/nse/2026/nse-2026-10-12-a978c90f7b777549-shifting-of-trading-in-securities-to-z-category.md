## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the transfer of trading in securities of companies to the 'Z' Category ('BZ' Series) due to non-compliance with Regulation 33 (Submission of Financial Results) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters ending March 31, 2026, and June 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Parsvnath Developers Limited (PARSVNATH), Vivimed Labs Limited (VIVIMEDLAB), and Hampton Sky Realty Limited (HAMPTON) have been identified as non-compliant with Regulation 33 for two consecutive quarters.
- Trading in securities of these companies will be transferred to 'Z' Category ('BZ' Series) with effect from October 23, 2026, if compliance is not met on or before October 20, 2026.
- 23 additional companies already trading in 'Z' Category pursuant to previous Exchange Circulars are also noted as eligible for transfer due to ongoing non-compliance under Regulation 33.
- Trades in the aforesaid securities executed in the Z category will be settled on a Trade for Trade basis.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement pursuant to SEBI Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 ("Master Circular") dated July 11, 2023, and last updated on January 30, 2026, alongside Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Affected companies must submit their pending financial results and comply with Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing Regulations) on or before October 20, 2026, to avoid the transfer of their securities to the 'Z' Category.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 12, 2026
- Compliance Deadline: October 20, 2026
- Effective Date of Transfer to 'Z' Category: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The transfer of securities to the 'Z' Category and settlement on a Trade for Trade basis significantly restricts liquidity, increases investor caution, and enforces stringent surveillance measures on non-compliant listed entities.
