## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular informing members about the change in the name of **Take Solutions Limited** to **TAKE Limited**, effective from August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name Change:** Take Solutions Limited is being renamed to TAKE Limited.
- **Symbol:** The existing symbol **TAKE** remains unchanged.
- **Effective Date:** The name change will take effect from August 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes. This is an administrative update following the company's name change process.

## Compliance Requirements

Members of the Exchange and market participants are advised to update their records, client-facing applications, and back-office systems to reflect the new company name (**TAKE Limited**) starting August 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date of Name Change:** August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Minimal. The equity shares of the company will continue to trade under the same symbol "TAKE".
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Systems registering the company name will need updating, but no trading disruption is expected.
