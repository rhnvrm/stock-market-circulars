## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the SBI Nifty Midcap 150 Momentum 50 ETF FOF (Fund of Funds) New Fund Offer (NFO) by SBI Mutual Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Additionally, systematic transaction options—Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP)—will be available for these schemes starting from August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Reopening of Schemes:** SBI Nifty Midcap 150 Momentum 50 ETF FOF schemes reopen for active transactions.
- **Transaction Types:** Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities will be fully supported.
- **Effective Date:** Transactions can be initiated starting August 12, 2026.
- **Scheme Details:** The specific schemes included are:
  1. SBI Nifty Midcap 150 Momentum 50 ETF FOF Direct Plan - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF200KB1BV3) - Category: Debt
  2. SBI Nifty Midcap 150 Momentum 50 ETF FOF Direct Growth (ISIN: INF200KB1BU5) - Category: Debt
  3. SBI Nifty Midcap 150 Momentum 50 ETF FOF Direct Plan - IDCW (ISIN: INF200KB1BW1) - Category: Debt
  4. SBI Nifty Midcap 150 Momentum 50 ETF FOF Regular Plan - IDCW (ISIN: INF200KB1BT7) - Category: Debt
  5. SBI Nifty Midcap 150 Momentum 50 ETF FOF Regular Plan - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF200KB1BS9) - Category: Debt
  6. SBI Nifty Midcap 150 Momentum 50 ETF FOF Regular Growth (ISIN: INF200KB1BR1) - Category: Debt

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or compliance policy changes are introduced by this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors utilizing the NSE MF Invest Platform should update their systems, catalogs, and customer-facing interfaces to enable transactions for these newly added/reopened ISINs starting August 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low. This is a routine operational enablement of mutual fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform. It provides mutual fund investors with convenient access to transact in SBI Mutual Fund's Nifty Midcap 150 Momentum 50 ETF FOF schemes through the exchange infrastructure.
