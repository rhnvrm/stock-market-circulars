## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the adjustment of market lot for Arihant Academy Limited (symbol: ARIHANTACA) on the SME platform on account of a bonus corporate action.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Arihant Academy Limited
- **Symbol:** ARIHANTACA
- **Corporate Action Type:** Bonus
- **Bonus Ratio:** 1:1
- **Adjustment Factor:** 2
- **Ex-Date / Effective Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Record Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Adjusted Market Lot:** 800

## Regulatory Changes

Market lot adjustments are implemented in accordance with corporate action guidelines for securities traded on the SME platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to load the updated security.gz, nnf_security.gz, or MII - Security File.gz files in their trading applications prior to trading on the effective date (October 01, 2026). These files can be obtained from the common/NTNEAT directory on the Extranet server or the NSE website reports section.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date / Ex-Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Record Date:** October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Traders and members dealing in ARIHANTACA on the SME platform must update their trading terminals with the revised market lot of 800 units to ensure smooth order placement and execution from the effective date onward.
