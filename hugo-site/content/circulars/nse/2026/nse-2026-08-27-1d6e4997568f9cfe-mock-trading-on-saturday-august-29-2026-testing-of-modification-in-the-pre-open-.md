## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a mock trading session in the Futures & Options (F&O) segment on Saturday, August 29, 2026. The primary objective is to test modifications in the pre-open call auction framework and evaluate NEATPlus version 7.9.0.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: August 29, 2026.
- Focus: Testing pre-open call auction framework as per circular NSE/FAOP/74790.
- NEATPlus version 7.9.0 introduction specifically for Trading Session-1 from the DR Site.
- Reinstallation of earlier versions (7.8.8 or 7.8.9) required for Trading Sessions 2 and 3.

## Regulatory Changes

- Testing of modifications in the pre-open call auction framework for the Futures & Options segment.
- Discontinuation of old broadcast parameters in Live effective September 05, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure correct NEATPlus versions are used across the respective trading sessions.
- Version 7.8.8 download path: /faoftp/faocommon/NEATPlus788 (discontinuation date September 05, 2026).
- Version 7.9.0 download path: /cmftp/common/Mock/FO/29AUGUST2026/NEATPlus790 (applicable for Trading Session-1).

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 29, 2026
- NEATPlus 7.8.8 Discontinuation Date: September 05, 2026
- Broadcast Parameters Change Effective Date: September 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness for trading members to verify trading terminal versions and compatibility with updated pre-open call auction frameworks.
