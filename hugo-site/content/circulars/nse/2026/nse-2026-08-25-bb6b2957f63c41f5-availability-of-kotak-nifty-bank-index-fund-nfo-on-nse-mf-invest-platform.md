## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular announcing the availability of the Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund NFO of Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes made available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Covers multiple plans including Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment for both Direct and Regular plans.
- Effective date of availability: August 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants looking to transact in the mentioned scheme plans can utilize the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update enabling platform access for investors and members subscribing to Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund.
