## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the launch of the Mirae Asset BSE LargeMid (60:40) Stable Dividend 50 ETF New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The scheme will be open for transactions from August 10, 2026, to August 12, 2026 (until 11:59 pm).

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name**: Mirae Asset BSE LargeMid (60:40) Stable Dividend 50 ETF (Direct Growth and Regular Growth options).
- **ISIN**: INF769K01RK3.
- **Category**: Equity.
- **Transaction Period**: August 10, 2026, to August 12, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- **Allotment Date**: August 14, 2026.
- **Funding Requirement**: Members must ensure clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 13, 2026, till 11:59 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced in this circular; it is an operational notice facilitating the transaction of a new fund offer.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members/trading participants must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account by August 13, 2026, till 11:59 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date**: August 10, 2026
- **NFO End Date**: August 12, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline**: August 13, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date**: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Low. Trading members and participants on the NSE MF Invest Platform will have access to a new ETF product for transaction during the specified NFO window.
- **Market Impact**: Minimal. The launch expands the product suite of available mutual funds and ETFs but does not introduce systemic market changes.
