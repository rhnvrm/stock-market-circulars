## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective August 21, 2026, with surveillance actions taking effect from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I.
- Applicable rate of margin is set at 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100% w.e.f. August 24, 2026.
- Margin provisions apply to all open positions as on August 21, 2026, and new positions created from August 24, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I framework in conjunction with existing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective August 24, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 20, 2026
- Inclusion Date / Effective Date: August 21, 2026 / August 24, 2026 (for margin applicability)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin obligations for trading members and investors dealing in the shortlisted securities, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in the short term.
