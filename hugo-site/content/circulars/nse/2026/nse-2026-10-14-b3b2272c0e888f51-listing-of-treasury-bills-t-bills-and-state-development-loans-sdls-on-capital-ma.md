## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing of various State Development Loans (SDLs) and Government Treasury Bills (T-bills) on its capital market segment, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Admission of 12 State Development Loans (SDLs) across various states (Uttarakhand, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, Assam, Kerala, Gujarat, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh) with varying coupon rates and maturity dates up to 2056.
- Admission of 3 Government Treasury Bills: 91-Day T-Bill (maturity Jan 14, 2027), 182-Day T-Bill (maturity Apr 15, 2027), and 364-Day T-Bill (maturity Oct 14, 2027).
- All listed securities have a designated trading lot size of 100.
- Securities will be identified exclusively by their designated codes on the trading system.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, securities are admitted to dealings and respective lot sizes are established.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure orders are placed using the correct designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants with additional sovereign and sub-sovereign debt instruments for trading and investment in the capital market segment.
