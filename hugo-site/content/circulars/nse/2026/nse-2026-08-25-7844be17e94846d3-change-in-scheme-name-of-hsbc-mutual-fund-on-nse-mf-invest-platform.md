## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names of several HSBC Mutual Fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026, pursuant to SEBI master circular guidelines on categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes.

## Key Points

- Scheme name changes implemented for multiple HSBC Mutual Fund debt schemes.
- Changes take effect from August 26, 2026.
- Changes are not considered a change in the fundamental attribute of the schemes.
- Examples of name changes include HSBC Ultra Short Duration Fund changing to HSBC Ultra Short Term Fund, and HSBC Low Duration Fund changing to HSBC Ultra Short to Short Term Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modifications pursuant to Part IV of Chapter 3 of SEBI Master Circular on Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026, on Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors are requested to note the revised scheme names and categories on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; informational update for investors and trading members regarding updated scheme nomenclature.
