## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives (F&O) segment on Saturday, October 17, 2026, across both primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites.

## Key Points

- Mock Date: October 17, 2026.
- Trading Session-1 (Primary Site): Pre-Open from 10:00 to 10:10 hrs; Normal Market open at 10:15 hrs and close at 12:00 hrs.
- Trading Session-2 (DR Site): Pre-Open from 12:50 to 12:55 hrs; Normal Market open at 13:00 hrs and close at 13:45 hrs.
- NEAT+ Version 7.9.0 is applicable.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- No financial obligations or fund pay-in/pay-out will arise from mock trades.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine operational drill.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are required to participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment between 15:30 hrs and 16:00 hrs on October 17, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 17, 2026
- Live Re-login: October 17, 2026 (15:30 hrs to 16:00 hrs)
- Live Trading resumption: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures seamless operational readiness, connectivity, and disaster recovery preparedness for trading members.
