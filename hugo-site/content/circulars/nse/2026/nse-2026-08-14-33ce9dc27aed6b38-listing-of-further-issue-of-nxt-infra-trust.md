## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issue of units of Nxt-Infra Trust, comprising 7,370,000 units allotted under a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP), effective from August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Nxt-Infra Trust
- **Symbol:** NXT-INFRA
- **Series:** IV
- **ISIN:** INE0SF023016
- **Issue Price:** 95
- **Number of Units:** 7,370,000
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 285000001 to 358700000
- **Date of Allotment:** August 13, 2026
- **Pari Passu:** Yes
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** NA

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure dealings are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 13, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion for Nxt-Infra Trust through QIP units admission to dealings on the Capital Market segment.
