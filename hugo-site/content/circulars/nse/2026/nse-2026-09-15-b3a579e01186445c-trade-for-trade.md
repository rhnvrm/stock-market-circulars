## Summary

In pursuance of surveillance review to ensure market safety and safeguard investor interests, the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the shifting of several securities to the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/ST) with a price band of 5% or lower, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure 1 will move from the rolling segment (series: EQ/SM) to the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/ST).
- A price band of 5% or lower will be applicable to these securities.
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Certain securities mentioned in Annexure 2 will continue in the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/BZ or ST/SZ).
- The transfer is strictly due to market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in specified securities shifted to series BE/ST under Capital Market Segment Trading Regulations Part - A, 2.6.
- Elimination of rolling segment availability (EQ/SM) for the newly shifted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities since settlement occurs on a gross trade-to-trade basis without netting off.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 15, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 18, 2026 (Friday)

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity for the affected securities may be impacted due to the removal of netting off and the imposition of a strict 5% price band.
