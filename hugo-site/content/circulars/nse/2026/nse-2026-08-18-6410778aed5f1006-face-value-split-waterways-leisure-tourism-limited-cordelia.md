## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced a face value split for the equity shares of Waterways Leisure Tourism Limited (Symbol: CORDELIA). The existing face value and paid-up value of Rs. 10 per share will be subdivided into Re. 1 per share, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Waterways Leisure Tourism Limited
- Symbol: CORDELIA
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Re. 1/-
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Subdivision of equity shares from a face value of Rs. 10 to Re. 1.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the face value change and update their trading and risk management systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The stock price of CORDELIA will be adjusted accordingly to reflect the face value split, which may impact retail participation and trading liquidity.
