## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the cancellation of trading restrictions on securities of Supreme Engineering Limited (Symbol: SUPREMEENG). This follows the company's payment of outstanding Annual Listing Fees (ALF) for the financial year ended 2025-26.

## Key Points

- Previous restrictions were notified via circular NSE/CML/76505 on September 23, 2026, due to non-payment of ALF.
- Supreme Engineering Limited has successfully paid the outstanding ALF for FY 2025-26.
- No further regulatory action will be taken against the company regarding this matter.
- Trading in the security will continue on a daily basis in series BZ effectively from October 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Lifting of trading restrictions previously imposed for non-compliance with annual listing fee payments.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company has fulfilled its compliance requirement by clearing the pending Annual Listing Fees.

## Important Dates

- **September 23, 2026:** Date of initial circular notifying restricted trading.
- **October 21, 2026:** Date of circular announcing cancellation of restrictions.
- **October 22, 2026:** Effective date for the cancellation of restrictions and continuation of trading.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors can trade in the securities of Supreme Engineering Limited on a daily basis under series BZ without previous restrictions, resolving uncertainty regarding listing status.
