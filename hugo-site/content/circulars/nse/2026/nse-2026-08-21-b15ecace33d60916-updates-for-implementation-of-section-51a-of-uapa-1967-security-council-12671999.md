## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding updates to the United Nations Security Council (UNSC) 1267(1999) Sanctions List concerning ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida, pursuant to Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967.

## Key Points

- Receipt of communication from SEBI regarding updates to the UNSC Sanctions List dated August 19, 2026, and August 20, 2026 (amendment of 21 entries).
- Reference to updated lists of individuals and entities subject to asset freezing and financial service denials.
- Requirement to scan existing and future accounts against the updated sanction lists.
- Instructions on handling de-listing requests and forwarding them to appropriate authorities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of updates to the UNSC 1267(1999) Sanctions List under Section 51A of UAPA, 1967.
- Compliance with SEBI guidelines on Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Standards and Combating the Financing of Terrorism (CFT).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take note of the UNSC press releases and amended entries.
- Follow procedures detailed in the Central Government Order dated February 2, 2021.
- Scan all existing and future accounts to ensure no linkage to listed entities or individuals.
- Forward de-listing requests electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA, and MEA Joint Secretary (UNP).

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 21, 2026
- UNSC List Updates: August 19, 2026 and August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High operational impact for trading members and intermediaries to ensure robust screening mechanisms are in place for client onboarding and ongoing monitoring against updated UN sanctions lists.
