## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement schedule for mark-to-market settlement in the Currency Derivatives Segment for the month of November 2026, outlining daily trade dates and their corresponding settlement dates.

## Key Points

- Covers trade dates ranging from November 2, 2026, to November 30, 2026.
- Specifies final settlement dates for specific contracts including 91-Day GOI T-Bill Futures, Cash Settled Interest Rate Contracts, Currency Futures and Options, and Overnight Call Rate (MIBOR) Contracts.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine operational schedule circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and participants must take note of the trade and settlement dates to manage their margin and fund requirements accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **November 02, 2026**: First trade date of the month.
- **November 26, 2026**: Final settlement for November 2026 91-Day GOI T-Bill Futures Contracts.
- **November 27, 2026**: Final settlement for November 2026 Cash Settled Interest Rate Contracts.
- **November 30, 2026**: Final settlement for November 2026 Currency Futures and Options Contracts and last regular trade date.
- **December 01, 2026**: Final settlement for November 2026 Overnight Call Rate (MIBOR) Contracts.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational workflow and clarity on settlement obligations for market participants trading in currency derivatives and interest rate instruments during November 2026.
