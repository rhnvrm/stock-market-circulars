## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the applicable market-wide position limits (MWPL), trading member-wise position limits, FII/FPI and mutual fund position limits, trading member proprietary limits, and client level limits for three newly introduced individual securities: ATHERENERG, MAHABANK, and SAGILITY.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Applicable to 3 individual securities: ATHERENERG, MAHABANK, and SAGILITY.
- Specifies limits across Market Wide Position Limits, Client Limits, Proprietary Limits, FPI Category I & II, and Mutual Funds.

## Regulatory Changes

- Position limits have been established for the newly introduced securities in the Futures & Options segment in pursuance of earlier circulars dated April 30, 2026 and June 22, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with the specified position limits for the listed securities starting from the effective trade date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures orderly trading and risk management for the newly introduced securities in the derivatives segment.
