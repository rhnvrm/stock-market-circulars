## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for five companies on the SME Emerge platform, effective from October 16, 2026. The securities were issued via Qualified Institutions Placement (QIP), preferential allotment, and Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP).

## Key Points

- **AVP Infracon Limited (AVPINFRA):** 4,424,000 equity shares issued under QIP at an issue price of Rs. 56.50 (Face Value: Rs. 10, Paid-up: Rs. 10). Allotment date: October 13, 2026. Lot size: 800. Lock-in: Not Applicable.
- **Committed Cargo Care Limited (COMMITTED):** 530,840 equity shares issued under preferential allotment at an issue price of Rs. 81.00 (Face Value: Rs. 10, Paid-up: Rs. 10). Allotment date: July 18, 2026. Lot size: 800. Lock-in: April 30, 2027.
- **Diensten Tech Limited (DTL):** 46,320 equity shares allotted under ESOP (Face Value: Rs. 10, Paid-up: Rs. 10). Allotment date: September 11, 2026. Lot size: 1,200. Lock-in: Not Applicable.
- **Kody Technolab Limited (KODYTECH):** 123,100 equity shares issued under preferential allotment at an issue price of Rs. 975.00 (Face Value: Rs. 10, Paid-up: Rs. 10). Allotment date: August 7, 2026. Lot size: 100. Lock-in: May 10, 2027.
- **Vinyas Innovative Technologies Limited (VINYAS):** 91,000 equity shares issued under preferential allotment at an issue price of Rs. 1,100.00 (Face Value: Rs. 10, Paid-up: Rs. 10). Allotment date: September 9, 2026. Lot size: 100. Lock-in: April 25, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the securities are admitted to dealings and designated lot sizes are established for trading on the SME Emerge segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in these securities shall be conducted using their designated security codes and lot sizes. Equity shares allotted in dematerialized mode have been credited under temporary ISINs where applicable in accordance with SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the respective SME Emerge listed entities with specific lock-in constraints where applicable.
