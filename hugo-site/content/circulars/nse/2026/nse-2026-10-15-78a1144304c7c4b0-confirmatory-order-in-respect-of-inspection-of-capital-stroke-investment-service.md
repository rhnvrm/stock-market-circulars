## Summary

SEBI has issued a final confirmatory order (No. QJA/BS/WRO/WRO/32771/2026-27 dated October 15, 2026) restraining Capital Stroke Investment Services Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAFCC1815M, SEBI Reg No: INA000001316) from accessing the securities market and prohibiting them from buying, selling, or dealing in securities for a period of 5 (five) years.

## Key Points

- Target Entity: Capital Stroke Investment Services Pvt. Ltd.
- Debarment Period: 5 years, taking into consideration and setting off the restraint period already undergone pursuant to the Interim Order and earlier Confirmatory Order.
- Findings: Violations included promising assured returns, website misrepresentations regarding the research team and performance, breach of fiduciary duties, qualification/certification lapses, risk-profiling deficiencies, and submission of forged documents to SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement action reinforcing regulatory standards and compliance under SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 for investment advisers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the order and ensure strict compliance that the debarred entity is not associated with or given access to the securities market.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High regulatory impact on the debarred entity and reinforces strict oversight on investment advisers engaging in fraudulent practices.
