## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective September 16, 2026. Setubandhan Infrastructure Limited has been shortlisted in ASM Stage I.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria under ASM for Companies as per Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) are listed effective September 16, 2026.
- Setubandhan Infrastructure Limited (Symbol: SETUINFRA, ISIN: INE023M01027) is shortlisted in ASM Stage I.
- The ASM IBC framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and updating of securities under the ASM IBC framework based on corporate announcements and disclosures under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance measures and ensure compliance with trading restrictions applicable to securities shortlisted under ASM IBC Stage I.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, higher margin requirements, or other applicable trading controls as mandated by the exchange's ASM framework.
