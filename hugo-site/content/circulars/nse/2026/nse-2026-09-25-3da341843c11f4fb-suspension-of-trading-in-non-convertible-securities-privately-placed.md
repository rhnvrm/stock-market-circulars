## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of Power Finance Corporation Limited with effect from September 28, 2026, pursuant to redemption.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Power Finance Corporation Limited
- ISIN: INE134E07125
- Suspension Date: September 28, 2026
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption
- Regulation: Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard operational suspension following the redemption of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to note the suspension of trading in the aforementioned security.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 25, 2026
- Suspension Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard administrative procedure for redeemed debt securities and has no broader market impact.
