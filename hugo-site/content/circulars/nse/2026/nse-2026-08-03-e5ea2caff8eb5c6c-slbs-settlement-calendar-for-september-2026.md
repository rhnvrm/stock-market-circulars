## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has notified all participants and custodians of the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) settlement calendar and schedules for September 2026. The schedules cover First Leg Settlement (Settlement Type 'L'), Reverse Leg Settlement (Settlement Type 'P'), and Reverse Leg Auction (Settlement Type 'Q'). This calendar is issued in pursuance of NCL circular NCL/CMPT/73999 dated April 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **First Leg Settlement (Type 'L')**: Detailed in Annexure A, with 21 scheduled settlement dates starting from September 1, 2026 (Settlement 2026165) and ending on September 30, 2026 (Settlement 2026185).
- **Reverse Leg Settlement (Type 'P')**: Detailed in Annexure B, specifying the reverse leg settlement dates across various series (e.g., Series 09/X9 expiring on September 1, 2026, to September 7, 2027).
- **Reverse Leg Auction (Type 'Q')**: Detailed in Annexure C.
- **New Series Launch**: The Series 09/X9 expiring on September 7, 2027, will be available for trading starting September 2, 2026, after the current 09/X9 series expires on September 1, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes introduced in this circular. It is a scheduled monthly operational publication following the framework of the earlier circular NCL/CMPT/73999 dated April 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- All market participants and custodians must adhere to the timeline (Trade Date, Custodial Confirmation, and Settlement Date) for SLB settlements during September 2026.
- Custodial confirmation of trades is required to be completed on the same day as the Trade Date for the First Leg Settlement (Type 'L').

## Important Dates

- **September 1, 2026**: First Leg Settlement 2026165 Trade Date; also the reverse leg settlement date for the current 09/X9 series (Settlement Number 2026509).
- **September 2, 2026**: Launch of the new 09/X9 series expiring on September 7, 2027.
- **September 30, 2026**: Trade Date for First Leg Settlement 2026185 (Settlement Date October 1, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. The announcement provides scheduled operational calendar details which facilitate normal SLBS operations and market liquidity.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Clear calendar dates assist operations and settlement teams in configuring systems and scheduling custodial confirmations on time.
