## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular (Ref. No: 114/2026, Download Ref No: NSE/FAOP/75644) regarding the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in the security INDUSTOWER (Indus Towers Limited) on account of its dividend announcement. SEBI guidelines dictate adjustments to derivative contracts on corporate actions like dividend payouts. Since Indus Towers Limited has declared a dividend of Rs. 14/- per share, option strike prices and futures base prices in INDUSTOWER will be adjusted on the ex-dividend date (August 10, 2026).

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Indus Towers Limited (Symbol: INDUSTOWER).
- **Corporate Action:** Dividend of Rs. 14/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-).
- **Ex-date and Effective Date:** August 10, 2026.
- **Adjustment of F&O Strike Prices:** Options strike prices will be adjusted downward by Rs. 14/- (the dividend amount). For example, a strike of 315.00 will be adjusted to 301.00, 320.00 to 306.00, etc.
- **Rounding Off:** Revised options strike/futures base prices and lot sizes will appear in decimal places and will be rounded off to the nearest tick size, while lot sizes will be rounded off to the nearest integer.
- **Contract and Spread Files:** Members must load updated files (`NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz` and `NSE_FO_spdcontract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) before trading on the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard operational adjustments in pursuance of SEBI guidelines for derivative contract adjustments during corporate actions. No new overarching regulatory policies are introduced, but existing SEBI formulas are applied to modify option strikes and futures bases for the security INDUSTOWER.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to load the updated contract and spread files (`NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz` and `NSE_FO_spdcontract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) on their trading applications before the start of trading on August 10, 2026.
- Updated files can be downloaded from the Extranet server under directory `faoftp/faocommon` or via the NSE website on the path: `https://www.nseindia.com/all-reports-derivatives`.
- Clearing members and trading participants should note that position adjustments methodology will be separately intimated by the respective Clearing Corporation.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular:** August 07, 2026
- **Ex-Date & Effective Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Expiry Date for Adjusted Contracts (Annexure 1):** August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Direct downward price adjustment of option strike prices and futures base price by Rs. 14/- on August 10, 2026, corresponding to the cash dividend payout. Open option positions will automatically adjust to the revised strikes listed in Annexure 1.
- **Operational Impact:** High importance for F&O desks, retail traders, and brokers to load the updated contract files prior to market open on August 10, 2026. Failure to update system contract files could lead to order routing errors or incorrect strike displays on trading terminals.
