## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The scheme will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities starting September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 - Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A575, Category: Equity)
- Scheme Name: ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A583, Category: Equity)
- Facilities Available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are informed to take note of the availability of the scheme on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform availability for investors and members to transact in the specified ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund schemes.
