## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has scheduled a mock trading session on Saturday, August 01, 2026, to test and facilitate the implementation of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Cash segment. This mock session will also introduce the new NEATPlus version 7.8.9, which is mandatory for CAS-related changes and must be used from August 01, 2026 onwards. The official live deployment of the CAS trading modalities is set for Monday, August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mock Trading Session:** Scheduled for Saturday, August 01, 2026, covering sessions from both the Primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites.
- **NEATPlus Version 7.8.9:** A new software version is being introduced, and members must adopt this version starting August 01, 2026, for compatibility with CAS changes.
- **Live Implementation:** The closing auction session modalities will go live on August 03, 2026.
- **References:** The changes continue work established in prior circulars (NSE/CMTR/73362, NSE/CMTR/73845, NSE/CMTR/74466) and FAQ guidance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of Closing Auction Session (CAS) modalities in the Equity Cash Segment.
- Modifications in Security Master Files in alignment with the introduction of the CAS.
- Adjustments in trading modalities in the Equity Derivatives segment on account of CAS.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Software Upgrade:** Members must upgrade to NEATPlus version 7.8.9 to support the CAS-related changes from August 01, 2026.
- **Mock Participation:** Trading members are strongly recommended to participate in the mock session on August 01, 2026, to test connection, systems, and the new trading software version.
- **System Verification:** Verify system behavior during the mock session for both Trading Session-1 (Primary site) and Trading Session-2 (DR site).

## Important Dates

- **July 30, 2026:** Date of circular issuance.
- **August 01, 2026:** Mock trading session date and mandatory go-live for NEATPlus version 7.8.9.
- **August 03, 2026:** Live effective date for CAS trading modalities in the Equity segment.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** High. Trading members must install and deploy a new frontend client version (NEATPlus 7.8.9). System integration testing is required during the Saturday mock session to ensure no disruptions on the live date.
- **Market Impact:** Medium. The introduction of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) changes the market closing dynamics in the Equity Cash segment and affects downstream pricing/settlement procedures.
- **Technical Impact:** Medium. Structural modifications in Security Master files require technical system adaptation by the back-office and trading software vendors.
