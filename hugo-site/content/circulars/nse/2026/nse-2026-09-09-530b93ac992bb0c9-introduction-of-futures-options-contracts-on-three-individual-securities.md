## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the introduction of Futures & Options (F&O) contracts on three individual securities: Anand Rathi Wealth Limited (ANANDRATHI), Siemens Energy India Limited (ENRIN), and Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Limited (UJJIVANSFB). These contracts will be available for trading starting September 30, 2026, subject to fulfilling the eligibility criteria of the Quarter sigma computation cycle of September 2026.

## Key Points

- F&O contracts introduced on three individual securities: Anand Rathi Wealth Limited, Siemens Energy India Limited, and Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Limited.
- Effective trading date for the new contracts is September 30, 2026.
- Based on stock selection criteria prescribed by SEBI master circular dated December 30, 2024.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SEBI stock selection criteria for derivatives expansion.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must load the updated contract.gz and NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz files in their trading applications prior to trading on the effective date.
- Files can be obtained from the faoftp/faocommon directory on the Extranet server or the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 09, 2026
- Market Lot and Scheme of Strikes Announcement: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants gain expanded trading capabilities and hedging instruments on ANANDRATHI, ENRIN, and UJJIVANSFB.
