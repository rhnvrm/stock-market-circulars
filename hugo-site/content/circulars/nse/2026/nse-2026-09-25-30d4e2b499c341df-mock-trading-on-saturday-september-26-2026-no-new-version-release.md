## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) segment on Saturday, September 26, 2026. There is no new software version release for this session.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date**: Saturday, September 26, 2026.
- **Trading Session Timings**: Normal Market open at 11:00 hrs and close at 12:30 hrs.
- **Live Re-login Timings**: Start time at 16:00 hrs and close time at 16:30 hrs.
- **Order Purge**: All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- **No Financial Obligation**: Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; routine mock trading session conducted as per exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, September 28, 2026.
- Ensure valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff.
- Set appropriate branch and user limits from the Corporate Manager Terminal before placing orders.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date**: September 26, 2026
- **Live Resume Date**: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct operational impact. Ensures system readiness and connectivity for members ahead of regular live trading.
