## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities. Effective October 19, 2026, applicable margin rate shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% on open and new positions.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Short-Term ASM Stage I include ADVANCE, AVANA, AVPINFRA, COREIN, FWSTC, SEJALLTD, VTMLTD, and WSTCSTPAPR.
- Applicable rate of margin is set at 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Effective date for surveillance actions is October 19, 2026, based on open positions as on October 16, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I on specified securities.
- Margin requirement increased to 50% (or existing margin, whichever is higher) with a cap of 100%.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure the applicable margin rate of 50% or higher is collected on all open positions as of October 16, 2026, and new positions created from October 19, 2026.
- Market participants must adhere to surveillance measures in conjunction with existing exchange rules.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 15, 2026
- Open positions snapshot date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (50%) for securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I may impact liquidity and trading volumes for affected scrips.
- Provides additional risk containment measures for market participants trading in designated volatile securities.
