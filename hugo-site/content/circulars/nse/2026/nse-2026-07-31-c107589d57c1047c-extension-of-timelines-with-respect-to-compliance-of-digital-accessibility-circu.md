## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the extension of timelines for compliance with Digital Accessibility Circulars. Following a SEBI circular issued on July 31, 2026, the deadline for conducting the accessibility audit of digital platforms and remediating audit findings has been extended to October 31, 2026. This compliance pertains to the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016, and the rules formulated thereunder.

## Key Points

- **Timeline Extension:** The deadline for 'Conduct of Accessibility Audit for the digital platforms and Remediation of findings from the audit' has been extended to October 31, 2026.
- **Regulatory Mandate:** All regulated entities must comply with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 and rules made thereunder.
- **Reference Circulars:** Mentions SEBI Circular ref. no. SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131 dated September 25, 2025, and Exchange circular no. NSE/COMP/75240 dated July 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No fundamental changes to the regulatory requirements themselves, but an extension of the compliance timeline to October 31, 2026, under SEBI Circular HO/(411)2026-ITD-5_DIV2/I/17922/2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Audit Conduct:** All trading members must conduct an Accessibility Audit for their digital platforms.
- **Remediation of Findings:** Trading members must remediate any accessibility issues and findings identified during the audit.
- **Adhere to Extended Deadline:** Take note of the revised timeline and ensure full compliance on or before October 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **September 25, 2025:** SEBI Circular ref. no. SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131.
- **July 16, 2026:** Exchange circular no. NSE/COMP/75240.
- **July 31, 2026:** SEBI extension circular and NSE compliance circular date.
- **October 31, 2026:** Extended compliance deadline for audit and remediation.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Medium. Trading members receive a three-month extension to ensure their websites, mobile applications, and other digital infrastructure meet mandatory accessibility requirements under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. This provides additional time to perform thorough audits and implement necessary technical adjustments.
- **Market Impact:** Low. The circular is an administrative timeline extension and has no direct impact on market trading, liquidity, or pricing structures.
