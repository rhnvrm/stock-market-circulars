## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 03, 2026. There is no new version release for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 03, 2026, in the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Normal Market open time: 11:00 hours; Normal Market close time: 12:30 hours; Trade Modification end time: 12:40 hours.
- Live Re-login start time: 16:30 hours; Live Re-login close time: 17:00 hours.
- NEAT Version: 1.5.5. All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational mock session conducted in accordance with regulatory requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 05, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.
- Set appropriate branch and user limits from the Corporate Manager Terminal before placing orders.

## Important Dates

- Mock Date: October 03, 2026
- Live Re-login: October 03, 2026 (16:30 - 17:00 Hrs)
- Live Trading resumption: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact. Enables members to verify connectivity, box ID setup, and NNF application settings ahead of live trading.
