## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of the Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund NFO from Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window runs from October 05, 2026, to October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund Name:** Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund (Regular and Direct plans, Growth and IDCW options)
- **NFO Open Date:** October 05, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** October 19, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 23, 2026, till 10:00 AM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening:** October 05, 2026
- **NFO Closing:** October 19, 2026
- **Cleared Funds Deadline:** October 23, 2026 (10:00 AM)
- **Allotment Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to transact in the Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund NFO through the NSE mutual fund platform.
