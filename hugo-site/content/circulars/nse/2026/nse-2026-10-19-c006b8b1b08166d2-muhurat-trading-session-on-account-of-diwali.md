## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that a special live trading session (Muhurat Trading) on account of Diwali will be held on Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Special live trading session scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- Pre-open order entry, modification, and cancellation period (limit orders): 14:05 hrs to 14:10 hrs.
- Pre-open order entry/modification/cancellation for limit orders only (no modification/cancellation for market orders, random closure in last 2 mins): 14:10 hrs to 14:15 hrs.
- Pre-open order matching & trade confirmation period: 14:15 hrs to 14:17 hrs.
- Pre-open buffer period: 14:17 hrs to 14:20 hrs.
- Normal Market (Continuous Trading Session - CTS): 14:20 hrs to 15:40 hrs.
- Update of stock & index future price bands for alignment with CAS in Equity Cash Market at 15:15 hrs.
- Cash Market trade modification time: 14:20 hrs to 15:50 hrs.
- All trades executed in the session result in settlement obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes; scheduling of customary festive trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are required to take note of the special trading timings and operational schedules for Diwali Muhurat trading.

## Important Dates

- **Trading Session Date:** November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification ensuring members are aware of special festive market timings.
