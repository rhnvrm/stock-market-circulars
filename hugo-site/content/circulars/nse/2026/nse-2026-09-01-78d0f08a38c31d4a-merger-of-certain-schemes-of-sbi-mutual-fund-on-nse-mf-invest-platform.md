## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the merger of SBI CPSE Bond Plus SDL Sep 2026 50:50 Index Fund (merging scheme) into SBI Banking & PSU Fund (surviving scheme) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The merger becomes effective on October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Merging Scheme:** SBI CPSE Bond Plus SDL Sep 2026 50:50 Index Fund
- **Surviving Scheme:** SBI Banking & PSU Fund
- **Effective Date:** October 01, 2026 (or next business day if non-business day).
- **Exit Load Free Window:** Investors not in favour of the merger can redeem or switch investments without exit load from 01.09.2026 to 30.09.2026 (upto 3.00 pm on September 30, 2026) through NSE MF Invest platform.
- **Transaction Restrictions:** Fresh/additional lumpsum purchases and fresh systematic transactions (SIP and STP) are not permitted in the merging scheme effective 01.09.2026.
- **Existing SIP/STP/SWP:** Existing registrations in the merging schemes continue during the exit load period and will shift to surviving schemes on 01.10.2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Scheme merger processed in accordance with notice cum addendum issued by SBI Mutual Fund and facilitated via NSE MF Invest platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the discontinuation of fresh purchases in the merging scheme, the exit load-free redemption/switch window, and the automated transition of existing folios/systematic instructions to the surviving scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Exit Load Free Period:** 01.09.2026 to 30.09.2026 (upto 3:00 PM on 30.09.2026)
- **Effective Merger Date:** 01.10.2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding units in the merging SBI CPSE Bond Plus SDL Sep 2026 50:50 Index Fund plans have the option to exit without load or transition automatically to the corresponding plans of SBI Banking & PSU Fund on the effective date.
