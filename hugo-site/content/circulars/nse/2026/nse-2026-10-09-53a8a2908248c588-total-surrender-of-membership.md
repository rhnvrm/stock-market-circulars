## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has approved the request for total surrender of membership submitted by MI Stock Broking Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: MI STOCK BROKING PRIVATE LIMITED
- SEBI Registration Number: INZ000263437
- Last Trade Date: Dec 30, 2025
- Surrender Approval Date: Sep 04, 2026
- Circular Reference No: 92/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/COMP76808)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an administrative approval of membership surrender.

## Compliance Requirements

All members are requested to take note of the surrender of membership.

## Important Dates

- Last Trade Date: December 30, 2025
- Surrender Approval Date: September 04, 2026
- Circular Date: October 09, 2026

Insignificant market or operational impact as this pertains to the surrender of membership by a single trading member.
