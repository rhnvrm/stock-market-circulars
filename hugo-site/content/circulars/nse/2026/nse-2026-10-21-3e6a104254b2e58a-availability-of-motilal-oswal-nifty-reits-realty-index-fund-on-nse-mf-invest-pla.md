## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that the Motilal Oswal Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available: Motilal Oswal Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF247L01HO1) and Regular Growth (ISIN: INF247L01HP8).
- Facilities enabled: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform (NMF TM Segment).
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can facilitate transactions in the specified schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform adhering to standard operational procedures.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to trade and manage investments in the Motilal Oswal Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund seamlessly through the NSE infrastructure.
