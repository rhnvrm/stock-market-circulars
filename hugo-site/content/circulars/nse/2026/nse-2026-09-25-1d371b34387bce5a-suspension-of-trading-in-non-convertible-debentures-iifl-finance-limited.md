## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of IIFL Finance Limited effective September 29, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading applies to three ISINs of IIFL Finance Limited (INE530B07161, INE530B07187, INE530B07179).
- Reason for suspension: Maturity of the securities.
- Effective date of suspension: September 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in specified NCDs of IIFL Finance Limited is halted under Regulation 3.1.2 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A due to maturity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the suspension and cease trading in the affected NCD symbols (842IIFL26, IIFLZC26, 875IIFL26) from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding these maturing NCDs will no longer be able to trade them on the NSE platform post the effective date as they reach maturity.
