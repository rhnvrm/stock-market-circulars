## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has published the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) settlement schedules for the month of September 2026. This notification has been issued in accordance with Regulation 6.2 of the NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997). The circular defines specific operational calendars for EGR Normal T+1 settlement (Settlement Type '6') and EGR Auction settlement (Settlement Type '7') to guide clearing members in their processing operations.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Schedules Defined:** Establishes clearing schedules for both Normal T+1 (Type '6') and Auction (Type '7') settlements during September 2026.
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Released under Regulation 6.2 of the Capital Market Regulations, 1997.
- **Support Channel:** The clearing help desk is accessible at 1800 266 0050 (IVR Option 2) or via email at securities_ops@nsccl.co.in.
- **Annexures:** Normal settlement calendar is placed in Annexure A and Auction settlement calendar in Annexure B.

## Regulatory Changes

No changes have been introduced to the underlying regulatory framework. This is a recurring operational calendar release to implement clearing schedules under the pre-existing capital market regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Operational Alignment:** Active clearing and trading participants in the Electronic Gold Receipt segment must configure their back-office systems with these specific trade and settlement dates.
- **Obligation Management:** Members are required to maintain sufficient EGR holdings and funds in their accounts to ensure timely settlement of obligations on the designated pay-in/pay-out dates.
- **Auction Monitoring:** Trading members must track the settlement schedules for Type '7' auctions to address and clear settlement shortages.

## Important Dates

### EGR Normal T+1 Settlement (Settlement Type '6')

- **First Trade & Settlement:** Trades on 01-Sep-2026 will settle on 02-Sep-2026 (Settlement No. 2026077).
- **Last Trade & Settlement:** Trades on 30-Sep-2026 will settle on 01-Oct-2026 (Settlement No. 2026098).
- **Extended Settlement Cycles:**
  - Trades on 04-Sep-2026 settle on 07-Sep-2026 (Settlement No. 2026080).
  - Trades on 11-Sep-2026 settle on 15-Sep-2026 (Settlement No. 2026085).
  - Trades on 18-Sep-2026 settle on 21-Sep-2026 (Settlement No. 2026090).
  - Trades on 25-Sep-2026 settle on 28-Sep-2026 (Settlement No. 2026095).

### EGR Auction Settlement (Settlement Type '7')

- **First Auction Trade & Settlement:** Auction on 02-Sep-2026 will settle on 03-Sep-2026 (Settlement No. 2026077).
- **Last Auction Trade & Settlement:** Auction on 24-Sep-2026 will settle on 25-Sep-2026 (Settlement No. 2026093).
- **Extended Auction Cycles:**
  - Auction on 04-Sep-2026 settles on 07-Sep-2026 (Settlement No. 2026079).
  - Auction on 11-Sep-2026 settles on 15-Sep-2026 (Settlement No. 2026084).
  - Auction on 18-Sep-2026 settles on 21-Sep-2026 (Settlement No. 2026089).

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Moderate. Operations and settlement divisions must verify and update their settlement databases with the specific dates to avoid mismatches or defaults.
- **Risk Management:** Facilitates a robust clearing framework by providing clear timelines for auctions, mitigating risks associated with trade settlement shortages.
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Adhering strictly to the published dates minimizes the risk of late-delivery penalties and compliance flags.
