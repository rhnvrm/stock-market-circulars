## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the movement of securities to Stage IV of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM). Effective September 09, 2026, CLC Industries Limited will be moved to Stage IV of the GSM framework.

## Key Points

- **Security Affected:** CLC Industries Limited (Symbol: CLCIND, ISIN: INE376C01038).
- **Effective Date:** September 09, 2026.
- **Trading Frequency:** Trading permitted once a week (every Monday / 1st trading day of the week).
- **Surveillance Action:** Trade for Trade with a price band of 5% or lower as applicable, and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 100% of the trade value to be deposited by the buyers with no upward movement.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities placed under Stage IV GSM are subjected to strict surveillance measures to safeguard market integrity and investor interests.
- No netting off shall be allowed as settlement is done on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to take adequate precautions while trading in the specified securities.
- Buyers must deposit 100% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD).

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 08, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on liquidity and trading accessibility for CLC Industries Limited due to weekly trading restrictions and 100% ASD requirements.
