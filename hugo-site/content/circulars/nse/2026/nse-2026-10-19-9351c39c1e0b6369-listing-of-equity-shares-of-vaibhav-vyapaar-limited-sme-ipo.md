## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Vaibhav Vyapaar Limited on its EMERGE SME Platform effective from October 21, 2026. The equity shares will be traded under the symbol LOANFRONT.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vaibhav Vyapaar Limited
- Symbol: LOANFRONT
- ISIN: INE1KAA01025
- Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/- per share
- Issue Price: Rs. 54/- per share
- Number of Securities: 28,182,423 equity shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 28,182,423
- Market Lot: 2,000 Equity Shares

## Regulatory Changes

The securities will be admitted to dealings on the EMERGE SME Platform in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A. Trading will commence in Series 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment – Settlement Type 'W') and will subsequently shift to Series 'SM' (Normal Rolling Segment – Settlement Type 'N') as per SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that the security will participate in the pre-open session for IPO and other scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012, and continuous market trading as per Exchange Circular dated March 14, 2018.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants with official trading access to Vaibhav Vyapaar Limited shares on the NSE SME platform under specified surveillance and lot size requirements.
