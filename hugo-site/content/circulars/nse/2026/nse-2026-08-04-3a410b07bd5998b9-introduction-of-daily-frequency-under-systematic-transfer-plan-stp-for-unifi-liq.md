## Summary

Unifi Mutual Fund has announced the introduction of a Daily Systematic Transfer Plan (Daily STP) facility under the Unifi Liquid Fund, effective August 5, 2026. This facility allows unit holders to transfer a fixed amount at daily intervals (on business days) from the Unifi Liquid Fund (Transferor Scheme) to eligible Transferee Schemes, which currently include the Unifi Dynamic Asset Allocation Fund and the Unifi Flexi Cap Fund.

## Key Points

- **Introduction of Daily STP**: Effective August 5, 2026, Daily STP is available under the Unifi Liquid Fund.
- **Eligible Transferee Schemes**: Daily STP inflows are enabled for Unifi Dynamic Asset Allocation Fund and Unifi Flexi Cap Fund.
- **Minimum Criteria**: The minimum transfer amount is Rs. 1,000 (and in multiples of Re. 1/-) with a minimum of 6 instalments.
- **Transfer Schedule**: Daily transfers occur from Monday to Friday (business days only). If a selected date falls on a non-business day, the transaction is processed on the immediate next business day.
- **Commencement**: Transfers begin on the 7th calendar day from the receipt of a valid request.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Scheme Documentation Modification**: Para XXIII regarding Special Products and Facilities in the Scheme Information Document (SID) of Unifi Liquid Fund stands modified to incorporate the Daily STP facility.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Operational Enablement**: NSE MF Invest Platform and distributors must update their systems to facilitate Daily STP requests for Unifi Liquid Fund.
- **Discontinuation Processing**: Daily STP will be automatically terminated upon liquidation, withdrawal, pledge, or death of the unit holder, or via a written termination request given 7 calendar days in advance.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: August 5, 2026 (for the introduction of the Daily STP facility).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: This is a routine operational update enhancing convenience and flexibility for investors on the NSE MF Platform. It allows better rupee-cost averaging and asset allocation from liquid funds to equity/dynamic schemes on a daily basis.
