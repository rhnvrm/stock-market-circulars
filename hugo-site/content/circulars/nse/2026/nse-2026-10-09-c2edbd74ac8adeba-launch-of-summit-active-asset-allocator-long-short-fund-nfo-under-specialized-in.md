## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular announcing the New Fund Offer (NFO) launch of the Summit Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund under Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) by Invesco India Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Invesco India Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Summit Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund (Direct and Regular plans across Growth, Payout, and Reinvestment options)
- NFO Transaction Window: October 12, 2026 to October 26, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 30, 2026
- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard product launch and platform availability notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 30, 2026 till 12:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 12, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: October 26, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 30, 2026 (12:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides members and investors access to transact in the new mutual fund scheme via the NSE MF platform within the designated subscription period.
