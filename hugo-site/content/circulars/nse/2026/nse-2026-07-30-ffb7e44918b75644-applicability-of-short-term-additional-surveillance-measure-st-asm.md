## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Based on the surveillance criteria, specific securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II, while certain other securities are excluded from the ASM framework, effective from July 31, 2026. The applicable surveillance margin changes will take effect from August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusion**: GSP Crop Science Limited (`GSPCROP`) is shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I.
- **ST-ASM Stage II Inclusion**: No new securities are listed under Stage II (Nil).
- **Stage Transitions**: No securities are moving between Stage I and Stage II (Nil).
- **Exclusions**: Ashima Limited (`ASHIMASYN`), Mrs. Bectors Food Specialities Limited (`BECTORFOOD`), C2C Advanced Systems Limited (`C2C`), and `GSTL` are excluded from the ASM framework.
- **Surveillance Nature**: The shortlisting of securities under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and does not represent any adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- **ST-ASM Stage I Margin Requirement**: The applicable rate of margin is increased to 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher (subject to a maximum cap of 100%), effective August 03, 2026, on all open positions as on July 31, 2026, and new positions created from August 03, 2026.
- **ST-ASM Stage II Margin Requirement**: The applicable rate of margin is increased to 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher (subject to a maximum cap of 100%), effective August 03, 2026, on all open positions as on July 31, 2026, and new positions created from August 03, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must implement and collect the revised higher margin rates (50% for Stage I, 100% for Stage II) for the shortlisted securities.
- The ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures imposed by the Exchanges.

## Important Dates

- **Shortlisting Effective Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Margin Changes Effective Date**: August 03, 2026 (applicable to all open positions as of July 31, 2026, and new positions created from August 03, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Impact**: The increased margin requirements (50% for Stage I) will increase funding costs and leverage requirements for traders holding positions in `GSPCROP`, which may lead to reduced trading volume and price volatility for this stock.
- **Operational Impact**: Brokerages and clearing members must update their risk management and margin computation systems to reflect the higher margin rate starting August 03, 2026.
- **Exclusion Relief**: For the excluded securities (`ASHIMASYN`, `BECTORFOOD`, `C2C`, `GSTL`), margin requirements return to normal levels, which may improve liquidity and trading participation.
