## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued an update regarding bulk and block reporting procedures, referencing consolidated circular NSE/CMTR/73927 dated April 28, 2026. The circular details the availability of indicative records on ENIT, member verification requirements, mismatch correction windows, and public dissemination timelines.

## Key Points

- Indicative bulk and block records will be made available on ENIT (Member Portal > ENIT-NEW-TRADE > Trade > Bulk/Block Reporting) on a best-effort basis within three hours after the close of trading hours.
- Members must verify downloaded records against their own books; matching records require no further action.
- In case of a mismatch, members must submit modification requests through ENIT on the same trading day.
- If no modification is submitted within the 60-minute verification window, the Exchange treats the data as verified without mismatches.
- Exchange will disseminate bulk and block trade information to the public via its website by 21:00 hrs on the same day.
- Bulk upload and deletion/update facilities remain available until the reporting window closes.

## Regulatory Changes

Refinement of operational timelines, verification protocols, and ENIT module facilities for bulk and block trade disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must verify bulk/block records downloaded from ENIT and submit any required correction requests or disclosures on the same trading day within the specified reporting window.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Streamlines the reporting and verification workflow for trading members executing bulk and block deals, ensuring timely public dissemination by 21:00 hrs daily.
