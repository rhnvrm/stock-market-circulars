## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, lump-sum investments, switch-ins, and fresh SIP/STP registrations for LIC MF Medium to Long Term Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Scheme: LIC MF Medium to Long Term Fund.
- Restricted Transactions: Lumpsum subscription, switch-in, and fresh registration under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) & Systematic Transfer Plan (STP).
- Unaffected Transactions: Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-out, STP-out, and existing SIP/STP registrations continue without changes.
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors must note the temporary discontinuation of fresh inflows and ensure no new SIP/STP registrations or lump-sum investments are submitted for the affected scheme through the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing investors can continue with redemptions and existing SIP/STP plans, while fresh investors cannot initiate new subscriptions or SIP/STP registrations in the scheme via NSE MF.
