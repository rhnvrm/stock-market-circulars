## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the listing of privately placed commercial papers on its Debt Market Segment, effective August 06, 2026. This is in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations. A total of 10 commercial papers issued by eight different companies, including Godrej Properties, Angel One, ICICI Securities, and IIFL Finance, have been admitted to dealings.

## Key Points

- The circular notifies the admission of privately placed commercial papers on the Debt Market Segment of NSE.
- The listing is effective from August 06, 2026, under the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations (Regulation 3.1.1).
- High-profile issuers include Godrej Properties Limited, Angel One Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, and IIFL Finance Limited.
- The total value of listed instruments is substantial, led by Export Import Bank of India's CP issue of 80,000 securities with a face value of ₹5,00,000 each.
- All listed commercial papers have a maturity/redemption date in November and December 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory policies or structural changes are introduced. This listing is conducted under the existing framework of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members of the Exchange and the issuing companies must adhere to the standard operating procedures and trading regulations of the NSE Debt Market Segment.
- Clearances and settlement of these privately placed securities will follow designated security codes and ISINs specified in the Annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date**: August 05, 2026 (for all listed commercial papers)
- **Effective Listing/Trading Date**: August 06, 2026
- **Redemption/Maturity Dates**:
  - November 03, 2026: Godrej Properties Limited (Series 61)
  - November 04, 2026: Tata Capital Housing Finance, Axis Securities, Godrej Properties (Series 62), Angel One, ICICI Securities, Export Import Bank, Hero FinCorp (HFL CP 04/11/26)
  - December 15, 2026: Hero FinCorp Limited (HFL CP 15/12/26)
  - December 28, 2026: IIFL Finance Limited

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. The listing of commercial papers is a routine financing activity for corporate issuers and does not impact the broader equity market.
- **Operational Impact**: High for debt market segment participants and institutional investors who deal in these specific short-term money market instruments, as it provides them with liquidity and trading venue for these ISINs.
