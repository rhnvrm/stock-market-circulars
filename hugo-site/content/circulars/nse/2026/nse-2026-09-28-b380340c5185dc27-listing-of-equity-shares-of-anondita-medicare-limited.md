## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Anondita Medicare Limited under a rights issue on the SME EMERGE segment with effect from September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Anondita Medicare Limited
- Symbol: ANONDITA
- ISIN: INE0VTV01012
- Issue Type: Rights Issue
- Number of Securities: 958,166 equity shares
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- each, Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 950/- per share
- Date of Allotment: 24-September-2026
- Effective Date: September 29, 2026
- Market Lot: 200 shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 18086619 to 19044784

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the NSE SME EMERGE segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the securities shall be identified by its designated code and conducted in specified lot sizes. Shares are credited under temporary ISIN in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 28, 2026
- Date of Allotment: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up capital base of Anondita Medicare Limited through rights issue allotment on the SME EMERGE platform, providing liquidity to eligible allottees.
