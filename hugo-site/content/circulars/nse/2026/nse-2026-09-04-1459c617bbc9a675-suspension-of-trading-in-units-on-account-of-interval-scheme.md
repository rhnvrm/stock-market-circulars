## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the temporary suspension of trading in units of multiple schemes under UTI Annual Interval Fund - I due to the interval scheme structure.

## Key Points

- Securities affected: UTI Annual Interval Fund - I (Symbols: UTFIAIPRGI, UTFIAIPRRI, UTFIAIPRPI, UTFIAIPIGI)
- Reason for suspension: Interval Scheme
- Suspension Date: 07-Sep-2026
- Closing hours of trading: 04-Sep-2026
- Trading in the above securities will be subject to revocation upon confirmation by the Asset Management Company (AMC) of the continuation of the said schemes post closure of Specified Transaction Period (STP).

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Asset Management Companies must confirm the continuation of the schemes post closure of the Specified Transaction Period (STP) for trading revocation.

## Important Dates

- Closing Hours of Trading: 04-Sep-2026
- Suspension Date: 07-Sep-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Temporary suspension of trading for unit holders of the specified UTI Annual Interval Fund schemes during the interval period.
