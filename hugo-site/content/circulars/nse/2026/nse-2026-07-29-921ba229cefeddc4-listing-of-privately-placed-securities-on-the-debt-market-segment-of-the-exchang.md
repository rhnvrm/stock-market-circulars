## Summary

Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has admitted several privately placed securities, including Commercial Papers (CPs) and Debt Securities (DBs), to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange. The admissions are effective from July 29, 2026. This includes fresh issues of CPs and non-convertible debentures/debt securities from nine corporate issuers.

## Key Points

- **Debt Market Segment Admission**: Under Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, privately placed CPs and debt securities listed in the Annexure are admitted to trading.
- **Corporate Issuers**: The circular covers issuances by Aditya Birla Money Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, JM Financial Services Limited, Hero FinCorp Limited, L&T Finance Limited, PNB Housing Finance Limited, Angel One Limited, Sundaram Finance Limited, and Summit Digitel Infrastructure Limited.
- **Instrument Types**:
  - **Commercial Papers (CPs)**: Fresh issues of short-term, zero-coupon discounted CPs with maturities ranging from September 2026 to December 2026.
  - **Debt Securities (DBs)**: Long-term interest-bearing coupon securities (Debentures/Bonds) with interest rates of 7.74% (Sundaram Finance) and 7.505% (Summit Digitel Infrastructure), with maturities in 2028 and 2031 respectively.

## Regulatory Changes

- **NSE Debt Market Regulations Compliance**: This action is executed in accordance with Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.
- No new macro-regulatory shifts are introduced; this is a routine enforcement of the existing listing framework for privately placed debt instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Listing and Trading Compliance**: Issuers must ensure compliance with ongoing disclosure requirements, timely payment of interest/coupon, and redemption under the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and NSE Debt Market guidelines.
- **Trading Members' Actions**: Trading members in the Debt Market Segment can commence dealings in these securities starting from July 29, 2026, under the designated security codes and ISINs specified in the Annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates**:
  - July 27, 2026 (Sundaram Finance, Summit Digitel Infrastructure)
  - July 28, 2026 (Aditya Birla Money, IIFL Finance, JM Financial Services, Hero FinCorp, L&T Finance, PNB Housing Finance)
  - July 29, 2026 (Angel One)
- **Effective Listing Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Maturity/Redemption Dates**:
  - JM Financial Services CP: September 11, 2026
  - L&T Finance CP: September 21, 2026
  - IIFL Finance CP: October 26, 2026
  - Aditya Birla Money CP & PNB Housing Finance CP: October 27, 2026
  - Angel One CP: October 28, 2026
  - Hero FinCorp CPs: December 1, 2026, and December 2, 2026
  - Sundaram Finance DB: July 27, 2028
  - Summit Digitel Infrastructure DB: 2031 (as implied by maturity/listing structure)

## Impact Assessment

- **Issuers**: Enables corporate issuers to access short-term and long-term liquidity through the secondary debt market segment, enhancing capital structure flexibility.
- **Investors/Market Participants**: Provides institutional and accredited debt investors with structured liquidity and exit routes for privately placed commercial papers and debentures on a regulated exchange trading segment.
- **Overall Market**: Routine expansion of the exchange's debt market segment volume with no broader systemic impact.
