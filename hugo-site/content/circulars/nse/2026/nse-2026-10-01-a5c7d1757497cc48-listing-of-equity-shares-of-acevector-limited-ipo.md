## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of AceVector Limited under its IPO. The equity shares will be admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) with effect from October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: ACEVECTOR
- ISIN: INE580P01029
- Issue Price: Rs. 32/-
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Re. 1/-
- No. of Securities: 544,186,770
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date / Trading Commencement: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of AceVector Limited equity shares on the NSE Capital Market segment, providing liquidity to investors.
