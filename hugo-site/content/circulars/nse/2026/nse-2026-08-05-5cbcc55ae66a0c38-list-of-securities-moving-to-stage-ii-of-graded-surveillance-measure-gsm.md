## Summary

In continuation to successive surveillance circulars from 2017 through 2026, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that CLC Industries Limited (CLCIND) will transition to Stage II of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The transition takes effect on August 06, 2026, imposing Trade for Trade trading rules, a 5% price band, and a requirement for a 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on buyers.

## Key Points

- **Security Affected**: CLC Industries Limited (Symbol: CLCIND, ISIN: INE376C01038).
- **Surveillance Action**: Shifted to Stage II of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.
- **Trading Format**: Trading in the security will be available under "Trade for Trade" rules.
- **Price Band**: Limited to a maximum price band of 5% or lower, as applicable.
- **Financial Requirements**: Buyers must deposit an Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) equivalent to 50% of the trade value.

## Regulatory Changes

No structural changes have been introduced. This action represents the execution of pre-established guidelines under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework originally established in February 2017 and subsequently amended.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Precautions**: Trading members are advised to take adequate precautions and exercise necessary diligence before executing transactions in CLC Industries Limited.
- **Deposit Enforcement**: Member brokers must collect and deposit the 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) from the purchasing client on all Stage II trades.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 05, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity**: The movement of CLCIND to GSM Stage II is expected to significantly reduce trading volume and liquidity, as transactions must be settled on a gross (Trade for Trade) basis.
- **Operational Burden**: Brokers and buyers face higher margin requirements and capital lockup due to the 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) mandate.
