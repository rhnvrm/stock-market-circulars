## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the launch of the Invesco India Pharma and Healthcare Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The scheme will be available for transactions from August 18, 2026, to September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund Name:** Invesco India Pharma and Healthcare Fund
- **Fund House:** Invesco Mutual Fund
- **NFO Open Date:** August 18, 2026 (until 11:59 PM)
- **NFO Close Date:** September 01, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** September 07, 2026
- **Available Options:** Direct and Regular plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 07, 2026, till 12:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Period:** August 18, 2026 to September 01, 2026
- **Fund Allotment Date:** September 07, 2026
- **Fund Clear Funds Deadline:** September 07, 2026 (12:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for trading members and investors participating in the Invesco India Pharma and Healthcare Fund NFO through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
