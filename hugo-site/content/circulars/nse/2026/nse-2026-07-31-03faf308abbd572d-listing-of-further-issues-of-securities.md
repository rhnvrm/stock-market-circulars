## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular notifying the list of further securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment, effective from August 03, 2026. The listing covers further equity shares issued under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and Qualified Institutional Placements (QIP) by several companies, including Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Adani Energy Solutions Limited, BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Limited, Eicher Motors Limited, and other listed entities.

## Key Points

- Standard listing of further issues of equity shares for multiple listed companies on the NSE Capital Market segment.
- Newly listed shares are admitted to dealings with effect from August 03, 2026, and will trade under the specified series and ISINs.
- Detailed allocations include:
  - **Aditya Birla Capital Limited**: 53,687 equity shares (under ESOP) allotted at face value of Rs. 10.
  - **Adani Energy Solutions Limited**: 21,671,826 equity shares (under QIP) allotted at Rs. 1615 per share (face value Rs. 10).
  - **BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Limited**: 4,853 equity shares (under ESOP) allotted at face value of Re. 1.
- Trading in these shares will be conducted in the designated market lot sizes as specified in the circular's Annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Issued in pursuance of **Regulation 3.1.1** (admission to dealings) and **Regulation 2.5.5** (market lot sizes) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- No major policy revisions; this represents standard compliance under existing listing and trading frameworks for further issues.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Ensure internal systems and back-office databases are updated to support trading of these newly listed securities with effect from August 03, 2026.
- **Depository Participants**: Ensure demat credit and distinct ranges are properly synchronized.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date**: August 03, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**:
  - BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Limited: July 20, 2026
  - Aditya Birla Capital Limited: July 27, 2026
  - Adani Energy Solutions Limited: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity**: Incremental increase in trading volume and liquidity for the affected securities.
- **Equity Dilution**:
  - **Aditya Birla Capital Limited**: Negligible dilution from 53,687 shares.
  - **Adani Energy Solutions Limited**: Placement of 21,671,826 shares under QIP represents a notable capital raise and dilution.
  - **BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Limited**: Negligible dilution from 4,853 shares.
- **Pari Passu**: All newly listed shares will rank pari passu with the existing equity shares of the respective companies.
