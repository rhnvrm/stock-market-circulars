## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the exclusion of AvenuesAI Limited (CCAVENUE) from the Securities Lending & Borrowing (SLB) segment, effective October 13, 2026, following the suspension of trading in its equity shares.

## Key Points

- Based on Circular No. (NSE/CML/76849) dated October 12, 2026 regarding trading suspension in equity shares of AvenuesAI Limited.
- CCAVENUE is unavailable for transactions in the SLB segment effective October 13, 2026.
- Applicable to all participants and custodians in the SLB segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- AvenuesAI Limited (CCAVENUE) has been removed from the list of eligible securities for Securities Lending & Borrowing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians must ensure no transactions or positions are initiated for CCAVENUE in the SLB segment.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Halts all SLB activities for CCAVENUE, mitigating counterparty and settlement risks associated with suspended securities.
