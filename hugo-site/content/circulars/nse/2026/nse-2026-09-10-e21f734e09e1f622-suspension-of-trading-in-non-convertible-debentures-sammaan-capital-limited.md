## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in several Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) issued by Sammaan Capital Limited, effective September 11, 2026, pursuant to their maturity.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sammaan Capital Limited
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 11, 2026
- Reason for Suspension: Maturity of securities
- Total Securities Suspended: 14 ISINs

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, trading in specified debt securities has been suspended.

## Compliance Requirements

No specific additional compliance actions are required by market participants other than noting the trading suspension of the specified ISINs.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operation reflecting the maturity and subsequent suspension of specific NCDs of Sammaan Capital Limited with no broader market impact.
