## Summary

NSE has released the manual for the submission of Internal Audit Reports by auditors for trading members through the NSE Member Portal. The circular outlines the step-by-step registration, conflict of interest submission, data sharing, sampling requirements, and compliance reporting procedures.

## Key Points

- **Auditor Registration & Login:** Trading members must register their internal auditor via the Member Portal, after which the auditor receives credentials and access links for submission.
- **Conflict of Interest (COI):** Auditors must submit conflict of interest declarations first, followed by confirmation and submission by the trading member from their login.
- **Prerequisites for Submission:** The 'Next' button remains hidden until the member submits the Undertaking on Penalty, Conflict of Interest, and certificate details.
- **Part A - Data Sharing:** The Exchange provides various data submissions for auditor verification, including holding statements, running account settlement reports UCC-wise, bank/DP details, registered Authorized Persons (AP) lists, and SEBI letters.
- **Part B - Sampling Files:** Specific audit checklist points require evaluating sample dates/clients such as date-wise client margins, top 100 client margins across 6 dates and audit period, top turnover dates, and total turnover for top 100 clients.
- **Previous Observations:** Auditors must provide compliance status and comments on observations from previous inspections and the last two half-yearly internal audit reports.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standardized workflow introduced for internal audit report submissions, mandatory conflict of interest declarations, and integration of exchange-provided sampling datasets.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and registered internal auditors must follow the multi-step portal submission process.
- Auditors must verify exchange-provided data and sampling files, report non-compliances, misuse of client funds/securities, and delays in client account settlements.
- Mandatory submission of undertaking on penalty, COI, and certificate details before final report submission.

## Important Dates

- Applicable for half-yearly internal audit report submissions as per exchange schedules (e.g., FY ended March 2026 audits).

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the internal audit verification and submission process for trading members, enhancing transparency, data consistency, and adherence to regulatory compliance standards.
