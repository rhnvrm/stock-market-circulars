## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the timelines for trading members to take disciplinary action and submit Action Taken Reports (ATRs) for observations reported during inspections of Authorised Persons (APs).

## Key Points

- Trading members must take necessary disciplinary action against APs within 2 months from the end of the quarter and submit the Action Taken Report (ATR) along with closure status to the Exchange within 3 months from the end of the quarter.
- For non-compliances where termination has been prescribed, trading members must take disciplinary action within 7 days from the receipt of the inspection report/identification of non-compliance.
- Applicable for AP inspection reports for the July–September 2026 quarter and onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes explicit timelines for disciplinary actions and ATR submissions following AP inspections.
- Implements a strict 7-day turnaround for cases warranting AP termination.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must review AP inspection findings and enforce appropriate disciplinary actions within the stipulated deadlines.
- ATRs and status of closure of observations must be submitted within 3 months from the end of the respective quarter.

## Important Dates

- Applicable from the July–September 2026 quarter and onwards.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the compliance monitoring process for trading members regarding their Authorised Persons and enforces timely accountability.
