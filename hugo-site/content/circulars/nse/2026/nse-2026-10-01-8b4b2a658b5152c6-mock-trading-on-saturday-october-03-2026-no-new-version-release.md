## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) is conducting a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives (CD) segment on Saturday, October 03, 2026. There is no new software version release for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock Trading Date: October 03, 2026.
- Segment: Currency Derivatives Segment.
- Trading Session-1 (Primary Site): Normal Market open at 11:00 hours, close at 12:30 hours; Trade Modification end time at 12:40 hours; Live Re-login start time at 16:30 hours and close at 17:00 hours.
- NEAT Version: NEAT 3.5.4.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session; NNF software users must manually clear orders.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- UCC/PAN validation rules apply as per existing guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; conducted as part of routine exchange operations and testing requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure valid and compliant UCC/PAN are uploaded and approved before cutoff.
- Corporate Manager Terminals must be set with appropriate branch and user limits before placing orders.
- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Mock Trading Date: October 03, 2026
- Live Environment Connectivity: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational drill for trading members to verify connectivity, box ID setup, and NNF application readiness with zero financial impact.
