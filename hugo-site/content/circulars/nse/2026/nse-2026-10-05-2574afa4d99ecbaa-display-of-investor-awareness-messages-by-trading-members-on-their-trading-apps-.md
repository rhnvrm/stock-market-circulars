## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the display of investor awareness messages by Trading Members on their trading apps and websites under SEBI's Project Jagrook and World Investor Week 2026. Trading members must adhere to specific timelines and formats for displaying Project Jagrook messages and risk disclosures.

## Key Points

- Part of SEBI's nationwide investor awareness initiative 'Project Jagrook' and World Investor Week 2026.
- Mandates specific investor awareness messages and risk disclosures on trading member websites and trading apps.
- From October 05, 2026, to October 31, 2026, display on trading apps is voluntary, and risk disclosure display on apps becomes optional if the awareness message is shown.
- From November 01, 2026, mandatory bilingual (Hindi-English) display on website landing pages and alternating display on trading apps comes into effect.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SEBI circular no. HO/38/24/(15)2026-MIRSD-PODMMC/I/22872/2026 dated October 01, 2026.
- Mandatory display of 'Samajh Se Investing Simple' messages covering understanding investments, regulated products, starting early, and avoiding investment frauds.

## Compliance Requirements

- **October 05, 2026 - October 31, 2026:** Display specific investor awareness messages on trading member websites. Voluntary display on trading apps.
- **November 01, 2026 onwards:** Mandatory bilingual display on website landing pages. Mandatory alternating display on trading apps on designated days.
- **Weekly Schedule from Nov 1:** Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Sunday for Project Jagrook investor awareness messages; Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday for Risk disclosure messages.

## Important Dates

- **October 05, 2026:** Circular issuance and commencement of voluntary/initial display period.
- **October 31, 2026:** End of initial voluntary app display period.
- **November 01, 2026:** Commencement of mandatory bilingual website landing page display and alternating schedule on trading apps.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must update their web portals and trading applications to automatically rotate or display the required Project Jagrook investor awareness messages and risk disclosures according to the mandated weekly schedule, ensuring compliance with SEBI and NSE guidelines.
