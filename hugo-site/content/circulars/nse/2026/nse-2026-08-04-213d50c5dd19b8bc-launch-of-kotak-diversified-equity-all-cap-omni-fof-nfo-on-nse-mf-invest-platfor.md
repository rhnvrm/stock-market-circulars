## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the New Fund Offer (NFO) of **Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF** by Kotak Mutual Fund will be available for subscription on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The transaction window is open from **August 05, 2026, to August 19, 2026, until 11:59 PM**. Allotment of units is scheduled for **August 24, 2026**.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** Kotak Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF
- **Category:** Equity / Fund of Funds (FOF)
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **Subscription Period:** August 05, 2026, to August 19, 2026, till 11:59 PM
- **Allotment Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Supported Plans & ISINs:**
  - Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF Regular Plan - Growth: `INF174KA1D59`
  - Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: `INF174KA1D75`
  - Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF Regular Plan - IDCW Payout: `INF174KA1D67`
  - Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF Direct Plan – Growth: `INF174KA1D26`
  - Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: `INF174KA1D42`
  - Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF Direct Plan - IDCW Payout: `INF174KA1D34`

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or compliance changes are introduced by this circular. This is a routine operational enablement of a new mutual fund product on the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members & Distributing Entities:**
  - Facilitate client transactions for the NFO within the specified period.
  - Ensure that clear subscription funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before **August 21, 2026, till 12:00 PM**.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a standard product expansion on the NSE MF platform that provides investors with a new diversified equity Fund of Funds (FOF) option.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Participating members need to configure the respective ISINs and ensure strict adherence to the clearing and fund pay-in deadline of August 21, 2026.
