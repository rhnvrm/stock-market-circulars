## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of Himalayan Solar Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (series: SM), effective from October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Himalayan Solar Limited
- Symbol: HIMALAYAN
- Segment Change: Transferred from trade for trade segment (ST) to rolling segment (SM)
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026
- Reference: Further to Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76699 dated October 01, 2026 and SEBI guidelines CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities are moved from the trade for trade segment to the rolling settlement segment as per standard regulatory guidelines and exchange circulars following SME IPO listing phases.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members need to note the change in segment and series for trading in Himalayan Solar Limited effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Date of Transfer: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for Himalayan Solar Limited shares, improving liquidity and trading flexibility for investors.
