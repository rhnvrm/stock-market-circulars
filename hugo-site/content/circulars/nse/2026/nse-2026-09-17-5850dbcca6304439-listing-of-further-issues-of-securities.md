## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for several companies effective from September 18, 2026. These shares were allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective September 18, 2026.
- Includes shares allotted under ESOP by companies such as Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Avalon Technologies Limited, Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited, Delhivery Limited, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited, Laxmi Dental Limited, MphasiS Limited, Nippon Life India Asset Management Limited, Paisalo Digital Limited, SPML Infra Limited, Tata Communications Limited, Tracxn Technologies Limited, Varun Beverages Limited, and Viceroy Hotels Limited.
- Trading will be conducted under the designated security codes and respective market lots specified in the exchange annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure trading is conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up capital of the respective listed entities with no major structural market impact.
