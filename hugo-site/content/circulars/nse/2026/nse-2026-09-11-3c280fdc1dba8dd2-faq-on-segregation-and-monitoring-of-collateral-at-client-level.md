## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released a circular containing frequently asked questions (FAQs) and clarifications regarding the segregation and monitoring of collateral at the client level, following reference to SEBI circulars and previous NCL guidelines.

## Key Points

- Error/own code reporting must be performed with account type "C".
- Segregated client collateral balances must be reported if collaterals are held, irrespective of peak or EOD margin obligations, to avoid non-submission penalties.
- Guidelines provided for collateral reporting during broker transitions between clearing members, inter-clearing corporation collaterals, debit balances with cut-off time fund receipts, and option value inclusions in ledger balances.

## Regulatory Changes

Clarifies existing compliance rules and reporting mechanics under SEBI/HO/MRD2_DCAP/CIR/2021/0598 and NCL/CMPL/50662.

## Compliance Requirements

All members and custodians must strictly adhere to accurate reporting standards for client collateral segregation to prevent violations and associated penalties.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and reduces common reporting errors by clearing members and custodians in daily client collateral segregation data.
