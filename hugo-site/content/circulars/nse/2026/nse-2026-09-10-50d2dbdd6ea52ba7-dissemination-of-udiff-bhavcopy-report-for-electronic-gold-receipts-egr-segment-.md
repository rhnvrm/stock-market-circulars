## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued an update regarding the dissemination of the Unified Distilled File Format (UDiFF) Bhavcopy report for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment, following up on the previous circular dated September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- The Exchange will start disseminating UDiFF Bhavcopy files for the EGR segment from September 18, 2026.
- The file structure and availability path remain as specified in the annexure of the earlier circular (Ref. No. NSE/EGR/76158).

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; operational implementation schedule confirmed.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the commencement date and update their systems accordingly to consume the UDiFF Bhavcopy files for the EGR segment.

## Important Dates

- **Previous Circular Date:** September 03, 2026 (NSE/EGR/76158)
- **Effective Date of Dissemination:** September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact on market participants; ensures smooth integration and availability of market data reports for the Electronic Gold Receipts segment.
