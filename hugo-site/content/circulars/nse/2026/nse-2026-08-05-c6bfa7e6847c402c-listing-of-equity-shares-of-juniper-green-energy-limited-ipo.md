## Summary

This circular relates to the listing of equity shares of Juniper Green Energy Limited (NSE: JNPR) post-IPO. It discloses the Share Holding Pattern format for specified securities filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, reflecting the post-issue shareholding structure as of August 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name**: Juniper Green Energy Limited (Post Issue)
- **Scrip Name/Class of Security**: Equity Shares (NSE Symbol: JNPR)
- **Filing Regulation**: Filed under Reg. 31(1)(a)
- **Total Shareholding Breakdown**:
  - **Promoter & Promoter Group**: Holds 488,989,292 shares (85.94% of total equity shares), fully dematerialized with 0% pledge/encumbrance.
  - **Public**: Holds 80,009,150 shares (14.06% of total equity shares).
- **Key Declarations**:
  - Outstanding ESOPs: Yes
  - Locked-in shares: Yes
  - Significant Beneficial Owner: Yes
  - Partly paid-up shares, Convertible Securities, Warrants, and Depository Receipts: No
  - Promoter shares encumbered or pledged: No

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, specifically Regulation 31 for listing newly issued equity shares post-IPO.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company is required to submit the specified format of holding of securities to stock exchanges upon listing.
- Shares subject to lock-in requirements post-IPO must remain locked-in as per SEBI regulations.

## Important Dates

- **Shareholding Pattern Filing Date**: August 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Medium. The listing introduces a new player (Juniper Green Energy Limited, Symbol: JNPR) in the renewable energy generation/distribution sector on NSE.
- **Operational Impact**: High subscription interest and initial trading volume are expected as trading commences post-IPO.
