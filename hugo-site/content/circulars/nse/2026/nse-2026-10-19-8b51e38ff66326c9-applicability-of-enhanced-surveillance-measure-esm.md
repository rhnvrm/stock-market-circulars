## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) effective from October 21, 2026. Securities qualifying under ESM attract a 100% margin and are shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Quintegra Solutions Limited (QUINTEGRA) is included under ESM Stage-I effective October 21, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 22, 2026, on all open positions as on October 21, 2026, and new positions created from October 22, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 22, 2026.
- Consolidated list includes securities such as ACS Technologies, Apsis Aerocom, Bafna Pharmaceuticals, and Bilcare under various stages.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specific securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Shift of qualifying securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Application of 100% margin on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must note the inclusion of securities under ESM and ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade settlement rules.
- Market participants must ensure appropriate risk management in line with surveillance measures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Implementation Date: October 21, 2026 / October 22, 2026.
- Margin Applicability: w.e.f. October 22, 2026 (on open positions as on October 21, 2026, and new positions).
- Shifting to Trade-for-Trade: w.e.f. October 22, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

High impact for investors and trading members holding positions in securities shortlisted under ESM due to 100% margin requirements and restrictions on intraday squaring off under Trade-for-Trade segment.
