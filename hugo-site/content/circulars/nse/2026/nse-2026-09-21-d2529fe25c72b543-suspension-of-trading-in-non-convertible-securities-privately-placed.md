## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of NHPC Limited due to redemption, effective from September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: NHPC Limited
- ISIN: INE848E07AP1
- Suspension Date: 22-Sep-2026
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants to note the suspension of trading in the specified security.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified non-convertible securities of NHPC Limited will cease on the exchange from the effective date.
