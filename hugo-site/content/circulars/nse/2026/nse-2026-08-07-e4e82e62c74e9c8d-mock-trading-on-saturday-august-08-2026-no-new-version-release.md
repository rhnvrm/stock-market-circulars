## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives segment on Saturday, August 08, 2026. This mock session will be held to test system performance and disaster recovery capabilities by switching trading from the Primary site to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site. No new software version is being released for this session.

## Key Points

- **Mock Trading Schedule**: To be held on Saturday, August 08, 2026.
- **Disaster Recovery (DR) Switchover**: Trading Session-1 will run from the Primary Site, while Trading Session-2 will run from the DR Site.
- **No Software Upgrade**: NEAT version remains at 3.5.4, and no new version is being released.
- **Service Disruption**: Connect2NSE and Extranet facilities will be unavailable from 12:30 to 21:00 hours. Extranet API will be unavailable from 12:30 to 14:00 hours.
- **No Financial Obligation**: Trades executed during the mock session will have no financial pay-in or pay-out obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes are introduced in this circular. Members are reminded of their ongoing obligation to participate in mock trading or use a simulated environment to meet regulatory guidelines under SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Order Purge**: Since all outstanding orders will be cleared before each session, members using Non-NEAT Frontend (NNF) software must manually clear orders in their systems.
- **UCC/PAN Compliance**: Only valid and compliant UCC/PANs uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed to participate. UCC validation is skipped during contingency order entry time.
- **Participation and Re-login**: Members should participate using all trading software and must perform a live re-login into the live environment between 16:30 and 17:00 hours on August 08, 2026, to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, August 10, 2026.
- **Pre-requisites**: Verify Telnet connectivity from the intended IP address to the Exchange host and ensure appropriate Box ID setup.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 07, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date**: August 08, 2026
  - **Session 1 (Primary Site)**: 11:00 to 13:30 hours
  - **Session 2 (DR Site)**: 14:15 to 15:30 hours
  - **Trade Modification End Time**: 15:40 hours
  - **Live Re-login window**: 16:30 to 17:00 hours
- **Live Market Resumption**: Monday, August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Low. Standard operational test to ensure robust business continuity and disaster recovery procedures. Requires coordination for the weekend re-login.
- **System Downtime**: Extranet, Connect2NSE, and Extranet APIs will face temporary downtime during the afternoon of the mock session.
- **Market Impact**: None. Mock trading has no impact on live markets or outstanding live orders.
