## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for several schemes of Franklin Templeton Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026.
- Applies to multiple debt and duration schemes of Franklin Templeton Mutual Fund.
- Changes include renaming schemes such as 'Franklin India Medium to Long Duration Fund' to 'Franklin India Medium to Long Term Fund', and 'Franklin India Corporate Debt Fund' to 'Franklin India Corporate Bond Fund'.
- Pursuant to SEBI Circular on Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes dated February 26, 2026, and SEBI Master circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with SEBI guidelines on categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

All references to the existing scheme names in Scheme Information Documents (SID), Key Information Memorandums (KIM), and Statement of Additional Information (SAI) shall be updated to the new corresponding names.

## Important Dates

- Addendum Date: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date of Name Change: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal impact on investors; existing units and folios remain unaffected apart from the updated nomenclature.
