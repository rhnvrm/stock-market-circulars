## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the discontinuation of weekly 'Trade for Trade' trading (Series "BZ") on the first trading day of every week for Shree Ram Proteins Limited (SRPL). This action is effective from August 11, 2026, following the completion of the mandated six-month weekly trading window as of August 10, 2026. This suspension stems from the company's non-compliance with Regulation 34 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, specifically the non-submission of Annual Reports for two consecutive financial years (ended March 31, 2024, and March 31, 2025).

## Key Points

- **Discontinuation of Trading:** Trading in the securities of Shree Ram Proteins Limited (SRPL) under Series "BZ" on the first trading day of each week will be discontinued.
- **Reason for Discontinuation:** Completion of the mandated six-month period of weekly trading (on the first trading day of every week) following the initial suspension.
- **Regulatory Violation:** Non-compliance with Regulation 34 (submission of Annual Report) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive financial years (FY 2023-24 and FY 2024-25).
- **Effective Date:** The discontinuation is effective from August 11, 2026, meaning trading will cease after the closing hours of trading on August 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Regulation 34 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015:** Mandates the submission of the Annual Report.
- **SEBI Master Circular (dated July 11, 2023, updated January 30, 2026):** Governs the treatment of non-compliant listed companies, which includes suspension of trading and a subsequent transition to limited weekly 'Trade for Trade' trading on the first day of every week for a mandatory period of six months before complete discontinuation.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For the Company (SRPL):** To resume trading, Shree Ram Proteins Limited must comply with Regulation 34 by submitting the outstanding Annual Reports for the financial years ended March 31, 2024, and March 31, 2025, and pay any applicable fines or penalties as prescribed by SEBI and the Exchange.
- **For Members/Investors:** Trading members should take note of the discontinuation of trading in the equity shares of Shree Ram Proteins Limited starting August 11, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **August 10, 2026:** Completion of the mandated six-month weekly trading period. Last trading day for the weekly series at closing hours.
- **August 11, 2026:** Effective date of discontinuation of weekly trading for SRPL.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** There will be no trading (including weekly 'Trade for Trade' on Series "BZ") in the securities of Shree Ram Proteins Limited (SRPL) on the NSE platform starting August 11, 2026. This complete suspension reduces liquidity to zero for this security until compliance is achieved.
- **Investor Impact:** Investors holding shares in SRPL will not be able to trade their holdings on the Exchange until the suspension is revoked upon compliance.
