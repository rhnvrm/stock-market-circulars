## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Steamhouse India Limited pursuant to its IPO, effective from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: STEAMHOUSE
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE0FRO01022
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 81/-
- Total No. of Securities: 276,406,311
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admission to dealings on the National Stock Exchange Capital Market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the security shall be identified by its designated codes and standard lot sizes. The security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2026
- Lock-in details vary from March 14, 2027 to September 14, 2029 as per the schedule in the circular.

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Steamhouse India Limited equity shares on the NSE main board.
