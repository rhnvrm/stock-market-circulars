## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities added to ST-ASM Stage I: Avonmore Capital & Management Services Limited, Boss Packaging Solutions Limited, California Software Company Limited, Finkurve Financial Services Limited, Metalic Technoforge Limited, Pakka Limited, PVP Ventures Limited, and Technocraft Industries (India) Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate capped at 100%.
- Surveillance measures are in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework provisions for the shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements on all open positions and new positions created from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 19, 2026
- Effective Date for ST-ASM Stage I Measures: August 21, 2026 (on all open positions as on August 20, 2026 and new positions created thereafter)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for specified securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected stocks.
