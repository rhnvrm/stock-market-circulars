## Summary

NSE Clearing Corporation (NCL) has released the revised applicable Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL), Participant limits, Institutional Client limits, and Non-Institutional Client limits for securities and Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS).

## Key Points

- **Eligible Instruments**: Covers 68 listed equity securities and ETFs eligible under the SLB Scheme.
- **Limit Metrics Defined**:
  - **Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL)**: The global threshold of open positions for each security across all participants.
  - **Participant Limit**: The individual borrowing/lending cap per registered SLB participant.
  - **Client Limits**: Segregated limits for Institutional and Non-Institutional clients to manage exposure and concentration risks.
- **Monitored Securities**: Includes major equities such as `3MINDIA`, `AARTIIND`, `ABFRL`, `ACC`, `APOLLOTYRE`, `ATGL`, etc.

## Regulatory Changes

- Recalculation and periodic revision of SLB position limits based on the underlying capitalization and volume metrics in the cash market.
- No changes to existing operational structures of the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Position Limit Adherence**: Clearing members, trading participants, and institutional/retail clients must ensure their outstanding borrow and lend positions do not breach the updated limits.
- **Risk Management Update**: Trading and risk systems of member firms must be updated to incorporate these new quantity-based thresholds.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Effective Date**: Immediate as per NCL SLB operational calendar.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity**: Periodic updates to limits prevent concentration risks while facilitating healthy short-selling, hedging, and arbitrage activities via SLBS.
- **Risk Management**: Ensures safe operational limits in SLB trades, mitigating systemic clearing and settlement risks.
