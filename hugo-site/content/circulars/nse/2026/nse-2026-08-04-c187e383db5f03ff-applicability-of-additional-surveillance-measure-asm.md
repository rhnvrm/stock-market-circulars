## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Based on the criteria for Long Term ASM, three securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under Stage I of the Long Term ASM framework. The applicable surveillance actions, including a 100% margin requirement, will take effect starting August 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three securities have been shortlisted and included in the Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I w.e.f. August 05, 2026:
  1. **Alphageo (India) Limited** (Symbol: ALPHAGEO, ISIN: INE137C01018)
  2. **Goldkart Jewels Limited** (Symbol: GOLDKART, ISIN: INE06MH01016)
  3. **Kapston Services Limited** (Symbol: KAPSTON, ISIN: INE542Z01028)
- Applicable rate of margin for the shortlisted securities shall be 100% w.e.f. August 07, 2026 on all open positions as on August 06, 2026, and new positions created from August 07, 2026 onwards.
- No securities were shortlisted for other stages or stage transitions (such as Stage-IV, moving from Stage-I to Stage-II, Stage-II to Stage-III, etc.) in this update.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely for market surveillance purposes and does not indicate adverse action against any concerned company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specific securities (ALPHAGEO, GOLDKART, KAPSTON) into the Long-Term ASM Stage-I framework.
- Implementation of 100% margin requirement on all open and new positions for these securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must enforce a 100% margin rate on all open positions as of August 06, 2026, and on all new positions created from August 07, 2026 onwards for the shortlisted securities.
- Members must monitor further updates and potential stage promotions or demotions for these securities under the ASM framework.

## Important Dates

- **August 04, 2026**: Circular issuance date.
- **August 05, 2026**: Shortlisting under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I takes effect.
- **August 06, 2026**: Reference date for existing open positions subject to the 100% margin requirement.
- **August 07, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin requirement on all open and new positions.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Selected securities (ALPHAGEO, GOLDKART, KAPSTON) may experience temporary reduction in liquidity and trading volumes due to the 100% margin requirement.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading members/brokers must update their risk management and margin systems to enforce the 100% margin limit on the affected scrips.
