## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of SRIT India Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO). Trading will commence effective October 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** SRIT
- **Name of the Company:** SRIT India Limited
- **Series:** BE (will subsequently shift to Series 'EQ' as per applicable SEBI circulars)
- **ISIN:** INE307K01028
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 5/-
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 130/- per share
- **Total No. of Securities:** 64,271,757 equity shares
- **Market Lot:** 1 share
- **Effective Date:** October 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings under the Capital Market segment in Series 'BE' with subsequent migration to Series 'EQ' as per SEBI guidelines.
- Subject to pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure orders are placed using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the exchange annexures.
- Compliance with lock-in details and promoter shareholding criteria as outlined in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 05, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** October 06, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** Various tranches ranging from March 2027 to April 2028 (with certain shares free from listing).

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of SRIT India Limited shares on the NSE Capital Market segment, providing liquidity to investors and expanding market participation in the IT/technology sector.
