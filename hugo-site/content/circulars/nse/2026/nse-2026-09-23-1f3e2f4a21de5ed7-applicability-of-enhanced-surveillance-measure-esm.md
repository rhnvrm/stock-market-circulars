## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Securities satisfying the criteria are shortlisted for inclusion under ESM Stage - I, moving across stages, and shifting from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment with applicable margins and price bands.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage - I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 25, 2026, on all open positions as on September 24, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 24, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company/entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 25, 2026.
- ESM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and segment migration for the impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage I inclusion and segment shift: September 25, 2026 (with positions assessed from September 24, 2026).
- Shifting to Stage II / Framework changes effective: September 24, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement for shortlisted securities may impact trading volumes and liquidity for the affected scrips.
