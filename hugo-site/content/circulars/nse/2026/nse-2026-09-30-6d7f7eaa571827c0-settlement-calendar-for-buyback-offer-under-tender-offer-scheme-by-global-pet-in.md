## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement calendar and operational guidelines for the buyback offer under the tender offer scheme by Global Pet Industries Limited (Settlement Type: X, Settlement No: 2026117). Trading for the offer is scheduled from October 1, 2026, to October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Type & Number:** X, 2026117
- **Trade Start Date:** 01-OCT-2026
- **Trade End Date:** 08-OCT-2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 13-OCT-2026
- **Settlement Date:** 14-OCT-2026
- **Demat Shares:** Must be tendered exclusively via Early Pay-in of Shares through depository mechanisms for Market Type Buyback.
- **Order Acceptance:** Early pay-in of shares must be completed well in advance on the trade end date for orders up to 03:30 p.m.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; follows standard NSE Clearing guidelines and previously issued circulars regarding tender offer buybacks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure early pay-in of shares is completed prior to the 03:30 p.m. cutoff on the trade end date.
- Securities and funds pay-out will be processed following the receipt of funds pay-in on the settlement day.
- Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the settlement day.
- Members should review the Tender Offer Acceptance Report for accepted, partially accepted, and rejected orders along with rejection reason codes provided in Annexure I.
