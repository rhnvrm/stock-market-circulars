## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the Muhurat Trading session on account of Diwali scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026. The circular outlines modified market timings for SLBS transactions, client code modifications, custodian confirmations, final obligations, multiple settlements, reverse leg settlements, VAR margins, and collateral operational timings.

## Key Points

- Muhurat Trading session will be held on Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- SLBS transaction session timings: 14:20 hrs – 15:30 hrs on November 08, 2026.
- Client Code/CP code modification: 15:40 hrs; Custodian confirmation: 16:15 hrs; Final Obligation: 17:00 hrs.
- Multiple settlements scheduled on November 09, 2026 with specific pay-in and pay-out times.
- Reverse Leg Settlement (Settlement Type ‘P’) for R3 series scheduled for November 12, 2026 (Trade Date: 08-Nov-26).
- Standard VAR margin rates applicable in the capital market segment shall apply to SLBS.
- Specific collateral timings defined for cash addition, allocation of collaterals, and securities/cash release requests.

## Regulatory Changes

Special trading sessions and modified settlement calendars implemented for the Diwali Muhurat trading holiday.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians must note the revised cut-off timings for pay-in at depositories and ensure adherence to modified operational schedules for November 08 and November 09, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Muhurat Trading Date: November 08, 2026
- Multiple Settlement Date: November 09, 2026
- Reverse Leg Settlement Date (Series R3): November 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth processing of trades, settlements, and collateral management during the special Diwali trading session without operational disruptions.
