## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on Bajaj Holdings & Investment Limited (BAJAJHLDNG) in accordance with SEBI circular guidelines and exchange eligibility criteria.

## Key Points

- New expiry month contracts for BAJAJHLDNG will not be issued upon the expiry of existing contract months.
- Existing unexpired contracts for August 2026, September 2026, and October 2026 will continue to be available for trading until their respective expiry dates.
- New strikes will continue to be introduced in the existing contract months until full phase-out.
- No F&O contracts will be available for trading in BAJAJHLDNG effective from October 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Action executed pursuant to SEBI Circular Ref. No: SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024, and chapter 3.5 of Exchange circular No. NSE/FAOP/73928 dated April 28, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the discontinuation schedule and manage open positions and new contract issuances accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **August 24, 2026:** Circular issuance date.
- **October 28, 2026:** Effective date from which no contracts shall be available for trading in BAJAJHLDNG.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding positions in BAJAJHLDNG F&O contracts must square off or settle their positions before the final expiry on October 28, 2026.
