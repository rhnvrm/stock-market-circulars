## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives (CD) Segment on Saturday, October 17, 2026. There is no new software version release for this session.

## Key Points

- Trading Session-1 from Primary Site: Normal Market open at 10:00 hrs, close at 12:00 hrs.
- Trading Session-2 from Disaster Recovery (DR) Site: Normal Market open at 12:45 hrs, close at 14:00 hrs.
- Trade Modification end time: 14:10 hrs.
- Live Re-login Timings: Start at 15:00 hrs, close at 15:30 hrs.
- Testing of pre-trade risk controls (pre-emptive order cancellation on account of NNF ID-Algo ID validations) as per circular NSE/CD/76841, effective in LIVE from October 19, 2026.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session. Members using NNF software must manually clear orders in their systems.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.
- NOTIS application will be accessible during the mock trading session.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes introduced; testing of pre-trade risk controls announced previously is enabled for verification.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment on October 17, 2026, to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 19, 2026. Ensure valid and compliant UCC/PAN and Box ID/telnet connectivity.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 17, 2026
- Effective Date for Pre-Trade Risk Controls in LIVE: October 19, 2026
- Live Trading Connectivity Check: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Allows members to test disaster recovery switchover, connectivity, and pre-trade risk controls prior to live implementation on Monday.
