## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the introduction of Futures & Options (F&O) contracts on three individual securities: Anand Rathi Wealth Limited (ANANDRATHI), Siemens Energy India Limited (ENRIN), and Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Limited (UJJIVANSFB), effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Anand Rathi Wealth Limited (ANANDRATHI)**: Market lot size is 250.
- **Siemens Energy India Limited (ENRIN)**: Market lot size is 175.
- **Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Limited (UJJIVANSFB)**: Market lot size is 8000.
- **Effective Date**: Contracts will be available for trading starting September 30, 2026.
- **Tick Size & Strikes**: Futures contracts share the CM segment tick size of the underlying, while options follow directions in circular NSE/FAOP/70382.

## Regulatory Changes

- Expansion of the Futures & Options segment to include ANANDRATHI, ENRIN, and UJJIVANSFB with defined strike schemes and lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must load the updated contract.gz and NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz files from faoftp/faocommon on the Extranet server or NSE website prior to trading on September 30, 2026.
- Quantity freeze limits apply as specified in the contract file.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances liquidity and hedging options in the F&O segment for the specified securities while necessitating operational readiness and contract file updates by trading members.
