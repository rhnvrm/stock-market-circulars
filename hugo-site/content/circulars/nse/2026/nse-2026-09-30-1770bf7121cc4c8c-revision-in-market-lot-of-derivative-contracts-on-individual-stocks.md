## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a revision in the market lots of derivative contracts on individual stocks in pursuance of SEBI guidelines (Circular SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024). The changes become effective from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Market lots have been revised downwards for 7 securities (Annexure 1) and upwards for 15+ securities (Annexure 2).
- Average closing prices of the underlying stocks during the period September 1 to September 30, 2026, were used for computation.
- Securities BAJAJHLDNG, IEX, and IREDA were excluded from the review due to their exclusion from the Futures & Options segment effective October 28, 2026.
- For contracts revised upwards or with non-multiple lots, existing market lots continue for November 2026 and December 2026 expiries, with revised lots applying to January 2027 and subsequent expiries.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic revision of derivative contract market lot sizes in compliance with SEBI periodic review framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to load the updated contract.gz, spd_contract.gz, NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz, and NSE_FO_spdcontract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz files into their trading applications before trading on October 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 28, 2026
- Computation Period: September 01 – September 30, 2026
- Expiries affected: November 2026, December 2026, January 2027 and beyond.

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants and market participants dealing in F&O segments must update their trading systems and risk management parameters with the new lot sizes effective October 28, 2026.
