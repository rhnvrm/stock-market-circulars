## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from August 27, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Listing of Commercial Papers (CPs) from various issuers including Nomura Capital (India) Private Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, HSBC Investdirect Financial Services (I) Limited, Vedanta Power Limited, Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited, Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited, and HDB Financial Services Ltd.
- Effective date of admission: August 27, 2026.
- Issued under respective ISINs with varying face values, issue prices, allotment dates, and redemption dates.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notification under NSE Debt Market Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to standard debt market listing and trading guidelines as stipulated by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 27, 2026
- Allotment Dates: August 25, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from September 18, 2026, to February 26, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for debt market participants regarding newly listed commercial papers.
