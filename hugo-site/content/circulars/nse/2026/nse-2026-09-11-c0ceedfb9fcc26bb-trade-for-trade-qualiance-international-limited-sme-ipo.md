## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that securities of Qualiance International Limited will be transferred from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM) with effect from September 28, 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines and previous exchange circulars.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Qualiance International Limited
- Symbol: QUALIANCE
- Segment Change: Transferred from trade for trade segment (ST) to rolling segment (SM)
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026
- Reference Circular: NSE/CML/76294 dated September 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are requested to note the transfer of the security from trade for trade segment to rolling segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normal trading in the rolling segment will resume for the security, allowing regular settlement of trades.
