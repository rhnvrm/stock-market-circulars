## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 07, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Issuance of Commercial Papers (CPs) by multiple companies including PNB Housing Finance Limited, Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Barclays Investments & Loans (India) Private Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Reliance Retail Ventures Limited, and Cholamandalam Securities Limited.
- Allotment dates for the securities are on September 04, 2026, with maturity/redemption dates ranging from December 2026 to March 2027.
- Standard face value across listings is ₹5,00,000 per security.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine listing notification under existing debt market regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Complies with Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Important Dates

- **Listing/Effective Date:** September 07, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** September 04, 2026
- **Date of Redemption:** Dec 2026 - Mar 2027 (varying by security)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital market activity enabling trading of listed commercial papers on the debt segment.
