## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of German Green Steel and Power Limited under the symbol GERMAN following its initial public offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Company Name: German Green Steel and Power Limited
- Symbol: GERMAN
- ISIN: INE0D4501011
- Listing specifics and trading commencement date to be announced separately.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing admission notification by the NSE Department of Listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals and systems with the new security details and ISIN (INE0D4501011).

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Listing Date: To be notified via a separate circular

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the inclusion and subsequent trading of German Green Steel and Power Limited equity shares on the NSE capital market segment.
