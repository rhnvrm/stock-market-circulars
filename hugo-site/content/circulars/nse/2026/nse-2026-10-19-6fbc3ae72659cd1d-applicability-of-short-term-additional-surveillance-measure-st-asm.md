## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specific securities pursuant to exchange surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Five securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I: BMISL, MOMSBELIEF, ONWARDTEC, SHREERAMA, and URBAN.
- No securities are listed under ST-ASM Stage II or moving between stages in this circular.
- Applicable margin rate for ST-ASM Stage I securities shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of securities under ST-ASM Stage I with enhanced margin requirements.
- Margin rate set at 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) effective October 22, 2026, on all open positions as on October 21, 2026, and new positions created from October 22, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (50% or existing, whichever is higher, up to 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective October 22, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Requirements: October 22, 2026 (on open positions as on October 21, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in the shortlisted securities will experience higher margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in these counters.
