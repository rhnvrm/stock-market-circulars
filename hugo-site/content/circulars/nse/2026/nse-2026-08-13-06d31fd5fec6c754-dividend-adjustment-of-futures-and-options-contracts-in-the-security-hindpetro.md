## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts for HINDUSTAN PETROLEUM CORPORATION LIMITED (HINDPETRO) due to a dividend of Rs 19.25/- with a face value of Rs 10/-. The effective date for the adjustment is August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: HINDUSTAN PETROLEUM CORPORATION LIMITED (Symbol: HINDPETRO)
- Corporate Action Type: Dividend
- Face Value: Rs 10/-
- Dividend Amount: Rs 19.25/-
- Ex-Date & Effective Date: 14-Aug-2026
- Revised option strike prices and futures base prices apply from the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of F&O contracts in pursuance of SEBI guidelines on corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to load the updated contract.gz/MII contract and spread files from the Extranet server or NSE website before trading on the ex-date.
- Revised strike prices and lot sizes will be rounded off according to exchange specifications.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date / Effective Date: 14-Aug-2026
- Expiry Dates in Annexure: 25-Aug-2026, 29-Sep-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Existing positions in HINDPETRO F&O contracts will be subject to revised strike prices and base prices to account for the dividend payout.
