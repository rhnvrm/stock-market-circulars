## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the merger of HDFC Nifty SDL Oct 2026 Index Fund (Merging Scheme) into HDFC Banking and PSU Debt Fund (Surviving Scheme) under HDFC Mutual Fund. The merger is effective from October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Merging Scheme: HDFC Nifty SDL Oct 2026 Index Fund
- Surviving Scheme: HDFC Banking and PSU Debt Fund
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026 (or next business day)
- Exit Load-free Window: Investors not favoring the merger can redeem or switch without exit load from September 11, 2026, to October 13, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM on October 13, 2026).
- SIP/XSIP/SWP Processing: Existing SIP/XSIP/SWP will continue to be processed in merging schemes during the exit load period and will automatically shift to surviving schemes on October 14, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- The proposed merger constitutes a change in the fundamental attribute of the Merging Scheme and requires compliance with relevant regulatory approvals.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the effective date and timeline for exit load-free redemption or switching through the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Start Date of Exit Load-free Window: September 11, 2026
- End Date/Time of Exit Load-free Window: October 13, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM)
- Effective Date of Merger: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Unitholders of the merging scheme are provided a seamless transition to the surviving debt fund or an option to exit without penalty, ensuring investor flexibility and tax efficiency.
