## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities of State Bank of India, Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, and Jharkhand Road Projects Implementation Company Limited with effect from October 1, 2026, pursuant to the redemption of these securities.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities under Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.
- Affected Issuers: State Bank of India, Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, and Jharkhand Road Projects Implementation Company Limited.
- Suspension Date: October 1, 2026.
- Reason: Redemption / maturity of ISINs INE062A08298, INE752E07OC4, INE746N07820, and INE746N07408.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Action taken in accordance with standard debt market trading regulations upon redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

No compliance actions required by market participants other than noting the trading suspension for the specified ISINs.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update reflecting the maturity and redemption of corporate debt instruments with no impact on equity trading or broader market operations.
