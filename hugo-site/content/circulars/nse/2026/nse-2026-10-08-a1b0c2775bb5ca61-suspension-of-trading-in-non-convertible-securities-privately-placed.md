## Summary

National Stock Exchange has notified the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities for three companies due to redemption, effective October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities affected belong to Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (ISIN: INE322J08040), Tata Capital Limited (ISIN: INE306N08201), and Sundaram Finance Limited (ISIN: INE660A07RQ0).
- Suspension date for all specified securities is October 09, 2026.
- The suspension is enacted due to the redemption of these securities.
- Action taken pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes; standard administrative suspension following security redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no transactions are executed in the suspended ISINs from the effective suspension date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 08, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operation with minimal impact, affecting only holders of the redeemed privately placed non-convertible securities.
