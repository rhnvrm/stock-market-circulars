## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for certain securities. Securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I and movement from Stage I to Stage II.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Basilic Fly Studio Limited (BASILIC), Brooks Laboratories Limited (BROOKS), Deepa Jewellers Limited (DEEPA), Dhanlaxmi Bank Limited (DHANBANK), OCCL Limited (OCCLLTD), and Virat Industries Limited (VIRAT).
- Securities moving from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II: Fascinate Textiles Limited (FASCINATE).
- Applicable margin rate shall be 50% (for Stage I) or 100% (for Stage II) or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to maximum 100% cap.

## Regulatory Changes

Surveillance actions under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework are imposed on specified securities based on objective criteria related to price variation, volatility, etc.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must apply the applicable margin rate of 50% or 100% (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) on all open positions as on October 13, 2026 and new positions created from October 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions: October 14, 2026 (on open positions as on October 13, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Traders and members holding open positions in the affected securities will be subject to higher margin requirements effective October 14, 2026, which may impact liquidity and position sizing in these scrips.
