## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective September 01, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Privately placed securities including Commercial Papers (CPs) and other debt securities are admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment.
- Effective date of listing: September 01, 2026.
- Issuers include Skoda Auto Volkswagen India Private Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD), LIC Housing Finance Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, and AVP Infracon Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine listing of approved debt securities under existing exchange regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers must comply with continuous listing obligations and disclosure norms applicable to debt securities and commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026
- Allotment Dates: August 31, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from November 2026 to December 2028 depending on the security.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for the listed commercial papers and debt instruments.
