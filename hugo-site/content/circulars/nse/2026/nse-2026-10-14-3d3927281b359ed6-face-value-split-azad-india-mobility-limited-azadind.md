## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the face value split of equity shares of Azad India Mobility Limited. The existing face value and paid-up value of Rs. 10 per share will be subdivided into Rs. 2 per share, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Azad India Mobility Limited
- Symbol: AZADIND
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 2
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Sub-division of equity shares processed in accordance with NSE listing guidelines and corporate actions framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure systems are updated to reflect the new face value of AZADIND equity shares from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The face value split adjusts the nominal share value and increases liquidity dynamics for shareholders of Azad India Mobility Limited.
