## Summary

Inox Green Energy Services Limited (INOXGREEN) will be available for trading in the Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE) on the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) with effect from July 31, 2026 (Friday). This arrangement is pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement. Additionally, the stock will participate in a call auction during the pre-open session.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Inox Green Energy Services Limited
- **Symbol:** INOXGREEN
- **Segment/Series:** Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE)
- **Effective Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Pre-open Session:** The stock will participate in the call auction in the pre-open session.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading and pre-open call auction will be conducted in accordance with SEBI Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must note that transactions in this security will be settled on a Trade for Trade basis, meaning 100% upfront margin is required, and no intraday netting or squaring-off of trades is allowed.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 28, 2026
- **Trading Commencement Date:** July 31, 2026 (Friday)

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity Impact:** Being in the Trade for Trade (Series: BE) segment will significantly reduce speculative and intraday trading volumes in INOXGREEN, as every trade requires mandatory physical delivery.
- **Price Discovery:** Participating in the call auction during the pre-open session will facilitate stable and efficient price discovery on the first day of trading post Scheme of Arrangement.
