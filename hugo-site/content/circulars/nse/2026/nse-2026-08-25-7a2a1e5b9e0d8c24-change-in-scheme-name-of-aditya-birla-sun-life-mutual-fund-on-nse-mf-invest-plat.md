## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for several schemes of Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Aditya Birla Sun Life Money Manager Fund is renamed to Aditya Birla Sun Life Money Market Fund.
- Aditya Birla Sun Life Medium Term Plan is renamed to Aditya Birla Sun Life Medium Term Fund.
- Aditya Birla Sun Life Income Fund is renamed to Aditya Birla Sun Life Medium to Long Term Fund.
- Aditya Birla Sun Life Floating Rate Fund is renamed to Aditya Birla Sun Life Floating Interest Rates Fund.
- Aditya Birla Sun Life Government Securities Fund is renamed to Aditya Birla Sun Life Gilt Fund.
- Aditya Birla Sun Life Equity Hybrid '95 Fund is renamed to Aditya Birla Sun Life Aggressive Hybrid Fund.
- Aditya Birla Sun Life Regular Savings Fund is renamed to Aditya Birla Sun Life Conservative Hybrid Fund.
- Aditya Birla Sun Life Savings Fund is renamed to Aditya Birla Sun Life Ultra Short Term Fund.
- Aditya Birla Sun Life Low Duration Fund is renamed to Aditya Birla Sun Life Ultra Short to Short Term Fund.
- Aditya Birla Sun Life Long Duration Fund is renamed to Aditya Birla Sun Life Long Term Fund.
- Aditya Birla Sun Life Dynamic Bond Fund is renamed to Aditya Birla Sun Life Dynamic Term Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

Changes are carried out pursuant to SEBI circular dated February 26, 2026, regarding Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors are advised to take note of the revised scheme names while transacting on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The changes are purely administrative and nomenclature-based to align with SEBI's categorization norms, with no direct financial impact on existing unitholders.
