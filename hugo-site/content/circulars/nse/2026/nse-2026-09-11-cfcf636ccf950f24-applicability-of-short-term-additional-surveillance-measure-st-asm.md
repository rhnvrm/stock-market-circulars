## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) to various securities effective September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I effective September 15, 2026.
- Included stocks: Almondz Global Securities Limited (ALMONDZ), Confidence Futuristic Energetech Limited (CFEL), Kavveri Defence & Wireless Technologies Limited (KAVDEFENCE), Molbio Diagnostics Limited (MOLBIO), Moneyboxx Finance Limited (MONEYBOXX), STL Networks Limited (STLNETWORK), and Sunlite Recycling Industries Limited (SUNLITE).
- No securities were reported under ST-ASM Stage II in this circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate capped at 100% w.e.f. September 16, 2026, on all open positions as on September 15, 2026, and new positions created from September 16, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities as per the ST-ASM framework.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 15, 2026 / September 16, 2026 for margin applicability.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for the shortlisted scrips may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities.
