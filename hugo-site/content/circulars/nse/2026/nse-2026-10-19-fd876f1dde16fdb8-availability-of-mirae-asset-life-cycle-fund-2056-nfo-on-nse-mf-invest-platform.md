## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the availability of Mirae Asset Life Cycle Fund 2056 on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 19, 2026, for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.

## Key Points

- Mirae Asset Life Cycle Fund 2056 of Mirae Asset Mutual Fund is available starting October 19, 2026.
- Facilities available include ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP).
- Covers multiple plans including Direct Plan and Regular Plan options (Growth, IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment) across respective ISINs.
