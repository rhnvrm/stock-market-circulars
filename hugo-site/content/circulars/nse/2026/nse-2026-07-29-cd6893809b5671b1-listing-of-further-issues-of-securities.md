## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular notifying the admission of additional securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment, effective from July 30, 2026. These further issues include equity shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and conversions for Gulf Oil Lubricants India Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, OnMobile Global Limited, SBI Cards and Payment Services Limited, Sudarshan Chemical Industries Limited, and Tech Mahindra Limited.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date**: The admission of further issues is effective from July 30, 2026.
- **Regulations**: The notification is made in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 (listing/admission of securities) and Regulation 2.5.5 (market lot sizes) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- **Further Issues Listed**:
  - **Gulf Oil Lubricants India Limited**: 38,751 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP on July 14, 2026.
  - **ICICI Bank Limited**: 948,479 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP on July 28, 2026.
  - **ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited**: 1,948 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP on July 28, 2026.
  - **OnMobile Global Limited**: 33,335 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP on July 21, 2026.
  - **SBI Cards and Payment Services Limited**: 27,984 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP on July 23, 2026.
  - **Sudarshan Chemical Industries Limited**: Equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted pursuant to conversion (issue price of Rs. 1019.75).
- **Trading Specifications**: Securities will be identified by their respective symbols and ISINs, and traded in the specified lot sizes, ranking pari passu with existing shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This notification is a routine enforcement of existing Listing and Trading Regulations (Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A) for the admission of further issues.

## Compliance Requirements

No general compliance requirements are imposed on members other than updating trading systems with the new securities, distinctive number ranges, and respective ISINs.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular**: July 29, 2026
- **Effective/Trading Commencement Date**: July 30, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**:
  - Gulf Oil Lubricants: July 14, 2026
  - ICICI Bank: July 28, 2026
  - ICICI Prudential Life: July 28, 2026
  - OnMobile Global: July 21, 2026
  - SBI Cards: July 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. The listing of additional shares increases the floating stock of the respective companies slightly, but is routine and unlikely to cause material price impact.
- **Operational Impact**: Standard operational updates are required by brokers and clearing members to support trading in the newly allotted shares.
