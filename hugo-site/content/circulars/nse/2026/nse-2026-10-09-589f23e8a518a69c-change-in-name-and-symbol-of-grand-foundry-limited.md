## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced a change in name and trading symbol for Grand Foundry Limited. Effective October 15, 2026, the company will be known as Tikona Communication Limited and traded under the new symbol TIKONA.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Grand Foundry Limited
- New Name: Tikona Communication Limited
- Existing Symbol: GFSTEELS
- New Symbol: TIKONA
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Exchange records, trading terminals, and indices will be updated to reflect the new name and symbol.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their internal systems, client databases, and trading terminals with the new symbol TIKONA and company name.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update with minimal market disruption; ensures seamless transition of trading under the new corporate identity.
