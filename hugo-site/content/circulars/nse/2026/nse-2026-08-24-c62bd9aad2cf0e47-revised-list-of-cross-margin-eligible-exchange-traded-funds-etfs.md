## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released a revised list of Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) eligible for cross-margin benefits, superseding circular no. 0273/2026 dated July 27, 2026. The revised list comes into effect from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Updates the list of cross-margin eligible ETFs along with their minimum required quantities.
- Supersedes the previous circular reference NCL/CMPT/75422 dated July 27, 2026.
- Effective date for the revised list is August 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Modification of the approved securities list eligible for cross-margining facilities across participating clearing members and clients.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must take note of the revised minimum quantities and eligible ETF symbols for cross-margining calculations starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and institutional clients utilizing cross-margining strategies involving ETFs must adjust their parameters based on the updated minimum quantity requirements.
