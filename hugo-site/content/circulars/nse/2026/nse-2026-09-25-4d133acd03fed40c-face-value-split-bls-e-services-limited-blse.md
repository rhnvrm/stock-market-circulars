## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced a face value and paid-up value split for equity shares of BLS E-Services Limited (BLSE). The face value will be reduced from Rs. 10 per share to Rs. 5 per share, effective from October 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** BLS E-Services Limited
- **Symbol:** BLSE
- **Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10
- **New Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 5
- **Effective Date:** October 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action implemented by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the change in face value and update their trading and risk management systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Splitting the face value enhances share liquidity and accessibility for retail investors while proportionately increasing the number of shares held by existing shareholders.
