## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from August 21, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Fresh issue of Commercial Papers (CPs) and Other Debt issues from various companies admitted for trading from August 21, 2026.
- Issuing companies include Kotak Securities Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, Astec LifeSciences Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, UltraTech Cement Limited, Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited, and ICICI Securities Limited.
- Re-issue of Commercial Papers also included for ICICI Securities Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with the operational parameters and settlement schedules specified by the Exchange for debt securities trading.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Allotment Dates:** August 20, 2026, and August 21, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Various redemption dates ranging from September 2026 to August 2031 depending on the security.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update providing liquidity and trading access for newly listed debt instruments and commercial papers.
