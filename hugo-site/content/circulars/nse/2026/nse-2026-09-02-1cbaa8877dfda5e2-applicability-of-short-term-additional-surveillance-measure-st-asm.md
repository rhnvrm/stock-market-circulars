## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specific securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria for ST-ASM Stage I have been listed in Annexure I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100%.
- Surveillance actions are effective from September 04, 2026, on all open positions as on September 03, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is a surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I framework provisions on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must maintain the applicable margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective September 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 02, 2026
- Effective Date for Open Positions: September 03, 2026 (open positions as on this date)
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for the shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected stocks, requiring participants to adjust capital allocations accordingly.
