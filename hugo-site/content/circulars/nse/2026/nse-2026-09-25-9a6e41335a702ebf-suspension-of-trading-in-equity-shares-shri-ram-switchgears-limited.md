## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the continued suspension of trading in equity shares of Shri Ram Switchgears Limited. The suspension is on account of a capital reduction pursuant to an NCLT Order dated May 08, 2026, effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shri Ram Switchgears Limited
- Symbol: SRIRAM
- Download Ref No: NSE/CML/76563
- Circular Ref. No: 1708/2026
- Reason for Suspension: Capital Reduction pursuant to NCLT Order dated May 08, 2026.
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026 (closing hours of trading on September 29, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

Trading suspension enforced under Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A following capital reduction.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note that trading in equity shares of Shri Ram Switchgears Limited remains suspended.

## Important Dates

- NCLT Order Date: May 08, 2026
- Circular Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares of Shri Ram Switchgears Limited will face continued restrictions on trading through the NSE platform until further notice or completion of capital restructuring processes.
