## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch and availability of the Navi Multi Asset Omni FOF NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The New Fund Offer (NFO) period runs from October 19, 2026, until November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Navi Multi Asset Omni FOF (Direct and Regular Plans - Growth)
- **NFO Window:** October 19, 2026 to November 02, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** November 04, 2026
- **ISINs:** INF959L01HV6 (Direct), INF959L01HW4 (Regular)
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational circular for mutual fund NFO listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 03, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** October 19, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** November 02, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- **Funds Clearance Deadline:** November 03, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to transact in the Navi Multi Asset Omni FOF NFO via the NSE MF Invest platform within the specified timeline.
