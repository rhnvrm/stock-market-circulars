## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that the New Fund Offer (NFO) for UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Exchange Traded Fund and UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Index Fund will be available for subscription on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 31, 2026, until September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available: UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Exchange Traded Fund (Regular/Direct Plan Growth) and UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Index Fund (Regular/Direct Plan Growth).
- NFO Window: August 31, 2026 to September 11, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- ISINs:
  - ETF Regular & Direct Plan Growth: INF789F1AH42
  - Index Fund Direct Plan Growth: INF789F1AH67
  - Index Fund Regular Plan Growth: INF789F1AH59
- Allotment Dates: September 15, 2026 (ETF) and September 17, 2026 (Index Fund).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 15, 2026 (for ETF) and September 17, 2026 (for Index Fund) till 3:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 31, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 11, 2026
- Fund Allotment Date (ETF): September 15, 2026
- Fund Allotment Date (Index Fund): September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational circular for trading members participating in mutual fund NFOs on the NSE MF platform.
