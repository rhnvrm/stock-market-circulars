## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Non-Convertible Debentures) issued by Power Finance Corporation Limited on the capital market segment, effective from September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Power Finance Corporation Limited
- **Effective Date:** September 09, 2026
- **Securities Admitted:** 
  - Symbol: 761PFCL36, ISIN: INE134E08OE9, Maturity: 08-Sep-2036, Coupon: 7.61%, Lot Size: 1
  - Symbol: 760PFCL31, ISIN: INE134E08OF6, Maturity: 08-Sep-2031, Coupon: 7.60%, Lot Size: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Admitted pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Lot sizes specified pursuant to Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the specified securities shall be conducted using the designated security codes and respective lot sizes only.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 09, 2026
- **Maturity Dates:** 08-Sep-2031 and 08-Sep-2036

## Impact Assessment

- Provides secondary market liquidity for the newly listed privately placed debt securities of Power Finance Corporation Limited.
