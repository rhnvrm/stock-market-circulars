## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of HD Fire Protect Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The circular includes post-issue shareholding pattern disclosures under Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: HD Fire Protect Limited
- Security Class: Equity Shares
- Shareholding Pattern Filed Under: Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015
- Date as on: October 17, 2026 (Post Issue)

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 regarding the submission of shareholding pattern post-IPO.

## Compliance Requirements

The listed entity must ensure continuous compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 and maintain disclosures regarding shareholding patterns, promoter holdings, and lock-in requirements as applicable.

## Important Dates

- Post Issue Date / Shareholding Pattern Date: October 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the formal trading and listing of HD Fire Protect Limited equity shares on the NSE, providing transparency into post-issue shareholding distribution.
