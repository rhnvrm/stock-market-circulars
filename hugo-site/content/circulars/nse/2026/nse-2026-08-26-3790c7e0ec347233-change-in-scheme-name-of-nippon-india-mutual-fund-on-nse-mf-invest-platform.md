## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names and categories of various debt schemes of Nippon India Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Change in names and categories for multiple Nippon India Mutual Fund debt schemes pursuant to SEBI Master Circular on Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026.
- Effective date of the changes is August 26, 2026.
- Schemes affected include Ultra Short Duration Fund, Low Duration Fund, Short Duration Fund, Floater Fund, Banking and PSU Fund, Medium to Long Duration Fund, Medium Duration Fund, Dynamic Bond Fund, and Nivesh Lakshya Long Duration Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI Master Circular on Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026, on Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and investors are required to note the revised scheme names and categories for transactions on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational alignment of scheme nomenclature across trading platforms in line with regulatory rationalization norms.
