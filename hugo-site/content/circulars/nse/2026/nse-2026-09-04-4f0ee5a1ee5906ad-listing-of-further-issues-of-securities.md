## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of securities for several companies under the SME Emerge segment, effective from September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers further issues of equity shares issued under preferential allotment.
- Effective date for trading: September 7, 2026.
- Applicable to SME Emerge segment companies including Grill Splendour Services Limited, Ganesh Infraworld Limited, Magson Retail and Distribution Limited, and Moxsh Overseas Educon Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Lot sizes specified pursuant to Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the specified securities shall be conducted using designated security codes and in the prescribed lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action for the listed SME entities increasing their paid-up capital via preferential issues.
