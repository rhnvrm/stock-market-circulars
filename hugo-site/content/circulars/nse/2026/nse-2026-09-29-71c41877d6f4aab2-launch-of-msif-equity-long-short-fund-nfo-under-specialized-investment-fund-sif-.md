## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the availability of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund under Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) of Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Scheme: MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund (Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund)
- NFO Period: September 30, 2026 to October 14, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 22, 2026
- Available options: Regular and Direct plans across Growth and IDCW (Reinvest / Payout) options.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard operational notification for mutual fund scheme launch.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 22, 2026, till 3:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: September 30, 2026
- NFO End Date: October 14, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Funds Clear Deadline: October 22, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates trading and subscription of Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund's MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund NFO for members and investors through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
