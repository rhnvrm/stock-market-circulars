## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued revised operating guidelines for Offer for Sale (OFS) in partial modification of its earlier circular no. NSE/CMTR/72975 dated February 24, 2026. Members, participants, and sellers are required to abide by the revised operating guidelines and terms and conditions detailed in Annexure A and associated annexures.

## Key Points

- Partial modification to previous OFS operating guidelines circular NSE/CMTR/72975.
- Incorporates SEBI's comprehensive framework and subsequent modifications for OFS through stock exchange mechanisms.
- Reaffirms requirements regarding interoperability among clearing corporations and compliant Unique Client Codes (UCCs).
- Restricts retail investor price bids in RS series on T+1 day below the cut-off price determined in the non-retail category.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates and consolidates operating guidelines and terms & conditions for Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares by promoters and eligible shareholders.
- Reinforces compliance with SEBI master frameworks and exchange circulars governing OFS participation and settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members, participants, and sellers must strictly adhere to the revised guidelines and terms & conditions in Annexure A and related annexures (Annexure I to V).
- Ensure only compliant UCCs are used for placing bids in OFS transactions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective: Immediate application in continuation of prior guidelines.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational clarity and standardization for market participants executing Offer for Sale transactions.
- Ensures regulatory alignment across stock exchanges and clearing corporations.
