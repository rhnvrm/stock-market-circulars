## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities have been shortlisted under ASM for Companies as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective August 26, 2026.
- Simbhaoli Sugars Limited (SIMBHALS) has been shortlisted under Stage I.
- Consolidated list includes Aban Offshore Limited (ABAN), Impex Ferro Tech Limited (IMPEXFERRO), and Reliance Infrastructure Limited (RELINFRA).
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of ASM IBC framework in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to note the list of securities under the ASM IBC framework effective August 26, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to surveillance measures as per the ASM IBC framework.
