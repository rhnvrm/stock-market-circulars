## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the exclusion of futures and options (F&O) contracts in Polycab India Limited (POLYCAB) due to its shortlisting under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework.

## Key Points

- Futures and options contracts for new expiry months shall not be issued on expiration of October 2026 contracts.
- Existing contracts with October 2026, November 2026, and December 2026 expiries will trade until their respective expiry dates.
- No F&O contracts will be available for trading in POLYCAB from December 30, 2026 onwards.
- A 100% margin shall be levied on all existing derivative contracts on a T+2-day basis (open positions as on October 13, 2026, and new positions from October 14, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Exclusion of F&O contracts under the IBC framework.
- 100% margin requirement on all existing and new derivative positions in POLYCAB.
- Subsequent surveillance actions (price band, gross settlement) effective January 01, 2027 after contract expiry.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new derivative positions in POLYCAB starting from October 13/14, 2026.
- Members and market participants must wind down or square off positions prior to contract expiration and final exclusion date of December 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **October 13, 2026**: 100% margin levy applicable on open positions.
- **October 14, 2026**: 100% margin levy applicable on new positions.
- **December 30, 2026**: No F&O contracts available for trading in POLYCAB onwards.
- **January 01, 2027**: Further surveillance actions (price band, gross settlement) effective under IBC framework.

## Impact Assessment

Derivatives traders and market participants holding positions in POLYCAB face mandatory 100% margin requirements and the systematic phase-out and final exclusion of all F&O contracts by December 30, 2026, requiring position adjustments or closeouts.
