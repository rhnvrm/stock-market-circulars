## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the revised trading hours for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment due to daylight saving time changes, effective from November 02, 2026, to March 12, 2027.

## Key Points

- Revised trading hours are applicable in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment due to daylight saving.
- Effective period: From November 02, 2026, to March 12, 2027 (both days inclusive).
- Trade Start Time: 9:00 AM.
- Trade End Time: 11:55 PM.
- Trade modification end time: 11:59 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of trading timings in reference to Chapter 2.1 (Trading hours) of SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2024/87 dated June 24, 2024.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to note and adhere to the revised timings for trading sessions and trade modifications in the EGR segment during the specified period.

## Important Dates

- Effective Start Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective End Date: March 12, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Operational alignment for market participants trading in the Electronic Gold Receipts segment during the daylight saving period.
