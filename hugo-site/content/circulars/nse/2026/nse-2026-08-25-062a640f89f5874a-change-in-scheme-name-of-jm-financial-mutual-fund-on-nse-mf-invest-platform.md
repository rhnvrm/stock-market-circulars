## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for JM Financial Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective August 26, 2026, following SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- JM Midcap Fund renamed to JM Mid Cap Fund
- JM Flexicap Fund renamed to JM Flexi Cap Fund
- JM ELSS Tax Saver Fund renamed to JM ELSS - Tax Saver Fund
- JM Low Duration Fund renamed to JM Ultra Short to Short Term Fund
- JM Dynamic Bond Fund renamed to JM Dynamic Term Fund
- JM Short Duration Fund renamed to JM Short Term Fund
- JM Medium to Long Duration Fund renamed to JM Medium to Long Term Fund

## Regulatory Changes

Changes executed pursuant to SEBI circular dated February 26, 2026, regarding Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes read with Chapter 3 of SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

All references to the existing scheme names in respective SIDs, KIMs, and SAIs shall be updated to their corresponding new names.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal impact on investors; existing folios and units will continue under the revised scheme names.
