## Summary

National Stock Exchange has issued guidelines and timelines for conducting the half-yearly Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Audit (CSCRF) for Trading Members, Qualified REs, and Mid-size/Small-size REs providing IBT or Algo Trading facility for the audit period ending September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Cyber audit must cover 100% of critical systems and 25% of non-critical systems on a sample basis with explicit rationale provided by the auditor.
- Unaudited periods due to category changes in the beginning of the financial year must be included in the current audit cycle.
- Categorization of REs holding multiple registrations must be reviewed and approved by the entity's Board, Proprietor, Partner, or Technical Advisory Committee.
- Auditors must verify and validate whether the categorization determined by the trading member complies with the SEBI CSCRF framework.
- Audit reports must provide compliance status for each Terms of Reference (TOR) item as Compliant, Non-Compliant, or Not Applicable with necessary justifications.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of SEBI CSCRF circular dated August 20, 2024, and subsequent clarification circulars regarding mandatory half-yearly cyber audits and reporting timelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Conduct cyber audit through a CERT-In empanelled auditing organization.
- Submit the Cyber Audit Report to the Exchange after approval from the respective IT Committee by December 31, 2026.
- Submit Action Taken Report (ATR) / Revalidation report through a CERT-In auditor providing closure status by March 31, 2027.

## Important Dates

- Audit Period Ending: September 30, 2026
- Submission of Cyber Audit Report: December 31, 2026
- Submission of ATR / Revalidation Report: March 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must engage eligible CERT-In auditors, perform technical and procedural validations, and ensure timely submissions to avoid regulatory non-compliance penalties.
