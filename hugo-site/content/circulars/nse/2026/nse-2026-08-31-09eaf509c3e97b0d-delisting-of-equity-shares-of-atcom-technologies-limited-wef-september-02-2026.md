## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the delisting of equity shares of Atcom Technologies Limited with effect from September 02, 2026, pursuant to its compulsory delisting by BSE Limited.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Atcom Technologies Limited
- Symbol: ATCOM
- Effective Date: September 02, 2026
- Basis: Rule 21(2)(b) of the Securities Contracts (Regulations) Rules 1957 following compulsory delisting by BSE Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Withdrawal of admission to dealings in equity shares of the company on the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the delisting and cease trading in the equity shares of Atcom Technologies Limited effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Delisting: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the equity shares of Atcom Technologies Limited will be permanently withdrawn on the exchange platform.
