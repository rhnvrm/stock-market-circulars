## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of further securities issued by several companies, including Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Alivus Life Sciences Limited, Apar Industries Limited, and Arvind Limited, with effect from August 10, 2026. These securities represent further shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- **Admitted to Dealings**: Further equity shares of multiple companies have been admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment of the NSE.
- **Allotted under ESOP**: The securities were issued and allotted under respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).
- **Specific Allotments (detailed in text)**:
  - **Aditya Birla Capital Limited**: 304,002 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each, allotted under ESOP on August 04, 2026 (ISIN: INE674K01013, Distinctive range: 2736160883 to 2736464884).
  - **Alivus Life Sciences Limited**: 32,000 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each, allotted under ESOP on July 30, 2026 (ISIN: INE03Q201024, Distinctive range: 122739449 to 122771448).
  - **Apar Industries Limited**: 9,484 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each, allotted under ESOP on July 24, 2026 (ISIN: INE372A01015, Distinctive range: 40402386 to 40411869).
  - **Arvind Limited**: Admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from August 10, 2026.
- **Effective Date**: This circular and the dealings are effective from August 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- The listing notifications are issued in pursuance of **Regulation 3.1.1** and **Regulation 2.5.5** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- No overarching regulatory changes are introduced; this is a procedural notification for corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- **System Updates**: Trading members must update their securities databases and systems with the newly listed symbols, ISINs, and allotment ranges to enable seamless trading.
- **Trading Parameters**: Trading in these shares must comply with the designated symbols, series, and lot sizes specified in the circular's Annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular**: August 07, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing**: August 10, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**:
  - Apar Industries Limited: July 24, 2026
  - Alivus Life Sciences Limited: July 30, 2026
  - Aditya Birla Capital Limited: August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. Listings of shares under ESOP are routine and have negligible direct impact on stock price or market liquidity.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Trading systems require standard updates for new distinctive number ranges and ISIN records to ensure precise settlement.
