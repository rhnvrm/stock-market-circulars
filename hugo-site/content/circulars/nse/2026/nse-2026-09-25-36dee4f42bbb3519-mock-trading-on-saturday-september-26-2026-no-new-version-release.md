## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) announced a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, September 26, 2026. The session involves trading from the primary site and DR site with scheduled timings for block deals, pre-open, normal market, and closing sessions. No new software version is released, but NEAT+ 7.9.0 is required for CAS while version 7.8.9 is scheduled for discontinuation on October 10, 2026 (mock).

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, September 26, 2026, in the Capital Market segment.
- Trading sessions conducted from both primary and DR sites with staggered timings.
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 is specified for CAS (/cmftp/common/NEATPlus790).
- Existing NEAT+ version 7.8.9 scheduled for discontinuation on October 10, 2026 (mock).
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session; NNF software users must clear orders manually.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational circular for exchange mock trading and system readiness.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must review connectivity parameters, installation guidelines, and ensure usage of recommended NEAT+ software versions.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 26, 2026
- NEAT+ 7.8.9 Discontinuation Date (Mock): October 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low impact; conducted on a non-business Saturday to test system stability, disaster recovery preparedness, and software compatibility.
