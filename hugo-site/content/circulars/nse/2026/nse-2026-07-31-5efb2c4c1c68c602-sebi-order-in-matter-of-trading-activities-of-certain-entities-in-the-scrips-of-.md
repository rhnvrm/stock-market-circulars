## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a final order against Mr. Suresh Venkatachari (Noticee No. 1), Mr. R S Ramani (Noticee No. 2), and Mr. M V Bhaskar (Noticee No. 3) in the matter of trading activities and financial misreporting in the scrip of SecureKloud Technologies Limited (formerly known as 8K Miles Software Services Limited). The order details severe findings of financial statement manipulation, revenue inflation from FY 2016-17 to FY 2018-19 through controlled front companies, and insider trading during the Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI) period. Consequently, SEBI has issued market bans, ordered the disgorgement of avoided losses, and imposed substantial monetary penalties under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Key Points

- **Parties Sanctioned:** Promoters and directors Suresh Venkatachari and R S Ramani, along with associate M V Bhaskar.
- **Fictitious Revenue Generation:** SecureKloud Technologies Ltd systematically inflated sales by booking fictitious transactions with Ensys Technologies Inc, Idol Solutions Inc, and Intuit Micro Technologies LLC—entities managed or controlled by the company's promoters.
- **Auditor Resignation:** The fraudulent practices led to corporate governance failures and the subsequent resignation of the statutory auditor, Deloitte Haskins and Sells.
- **Pledge Invocation as Trading:** The order confirms that pledging promoter shares and the subsequent invocation of those pledges by lenders during the UPSI period constitutes illegal trading under SEBI's Insider Trading regulations.
- **Disgorgement of Loss Avoided:** The promoters are held liable for disgorging the wrongful gains (losses avoided) arising from disposing/pledging shares while in possession of negative material information before it was disclosed to the public.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Interpretation of 'Trading':** Reaffirms and solidifies the regulatory interpretation that pledging of shares or the invocation of a pledge by insiders while in possession of UPSI is considered 'trading' and is subject to the Prohibition of Insider Trading (PIT) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Disgorgement and Interest:** The Noticees are required to disgorge the calculated wrongful gains (avoided losses) plus interest within the stipulated timeframe.
- **Market Prohibition:** Promoters and Noticees are prohibited from accessing the securities market, buying, selling, or otherwise dealing in securities, directly or indirectly, for specified ban periods.
- **Corporate Governance Standards:** Listed entities are reminded of the strict enforcement of related-party transactions, internal controls, and the absolute prohibition of booking fictitious sales through promoter-controlled foreign or domestic entities.

## Important Dates

- **August 4, 2022:** Date of the initial ad-interim order issued by SEBI.
- **FY 2016-17 to FY 2018-19:** The period during which the fictitious revenues were booked and financial reporting was manipulated.
- **August 12, 2026:** Date of the final order/directions under Sections 11 and 11B of the SEBI Act.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market & Entity Impact:** High. The confirmation of promoter-led accounting fraud and insider trading will severely impact investor confidence in SecureKloud Technologies Ltd. The long-term market exclusions of key promoter-directors will necessitate immediate leadership and board restructuring.
- **Legal Precedent on Pledges:** Clarifies the risk for lenders and promoters regarding share-pledging activities during UPSI periods, making it clear that promoters cannot use pledging to offload risk or monetize shares using inside information.
