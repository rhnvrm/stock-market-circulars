## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular regarding the review of position limits for clients and penalty provisions for violation or breach of position limits for the commodity derivatives segment, modifying provisions in Paragraph 3.4.3 and Paragraph 3.5.2 of the SEBI Master Circular dated August 04, 2023.

## Key Points

- Penal provisions for position limit violations at the client level are linked to the quantum/value of violation and charged per day.
- Violations more than 2% of prescribed limits attract penalties based on limit exceeded, closing price, number of days, and 2% or Rs. 2,00,000, whichever is lower.
- Violations up to 2% attract penalties based on limit exceeded, closing price, number of days, and 2% or Rs. 10,000, whichever is lower.
- Members must reduce positions by the next trading day after violation; otherwise, exchanges will square off excess positions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Annexure J under sub-para (ii) of Para 3.4.3 regarding monitoring of position limits is revised to update monetary penalties and square-off procedures for commodity derivatives.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must monitor client open interest positions closely to prevent breaches and ensure prompt reduction of excess positions within the next trading day.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 09, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines penalty structures and clarifies square-off enforcement for commodity derivatives segment participants.
