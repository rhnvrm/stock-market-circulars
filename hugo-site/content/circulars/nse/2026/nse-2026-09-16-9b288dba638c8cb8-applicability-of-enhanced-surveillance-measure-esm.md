## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Certain securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion and will attract a minimum 100% margin and shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities as per Annexure I (including AMD Industries Limited and IL&FS Engineering and Construction Company Limited) are shortlisted under ESM Stage-I w.e.f. September 17, 2026.
- Affected securities shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 18, 2026 on all open positions as on September 17, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shift from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 18, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework criteria on shortlisted securities in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions for the shortlisted securities effective from the specified dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage-I / Stage-II shifts / exclusions: September 17, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin and Settlement Segment change: September 18, 2026 (on open positions as on September 17, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for the affected securities will experience tightened controls due to trade-for-trade settlement and 100% margin mandates.
