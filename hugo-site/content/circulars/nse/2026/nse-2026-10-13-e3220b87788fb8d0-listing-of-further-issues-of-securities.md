## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a notification regarding the listing of further issues of securities for several companies, effective from October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities further admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment.
- Includes companies such as Ajax Engineering Limited, CreditAccess Grameen Limited, Avenue Supermarts Limited, GMR Airports Limited, and others.
- Shares allotted under ESOPs and pursuant to schemes of arrangement.
- Trading allowed in specified lot sizes and designated security codes.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action with minimal direct market impact.
