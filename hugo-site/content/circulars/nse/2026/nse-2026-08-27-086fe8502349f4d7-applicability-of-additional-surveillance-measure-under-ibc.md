## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective August 28, 2026, specific securities have met the criteria for shortlisting under the ASM IBC framework.

## Key Points

- Securities have been shortlisted w.r.t. receipt of corporate announcement/disclosure from the company effective August 28, 2026 (e.g., Owais Metal And Mineral Processing Limited).
- Securities shortlisted in ASM for Companies as per IBC Stage I effective August 28, 2026 (e.g., Ankit Metal & Power Limited).
- The ASM IBC framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely for market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ASM IBC surveillance criteria for specified companies under insolvency proceedings or corporate disclosures effective August 28, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and members must note the revised list of securities under the ASM IBC framework and adjust trading and surveillance monitoring accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026
- Circular Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced monitoring and potential trading restrictions apply to the shortlisted securities under the ASM framework.
