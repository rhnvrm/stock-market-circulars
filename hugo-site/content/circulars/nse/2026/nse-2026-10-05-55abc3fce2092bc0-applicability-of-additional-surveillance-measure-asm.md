## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for specified securities satisfying criteria for inclusion under Long-Term ASM and stage movements.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I effective October 06, 2026: AVATAR Industries Limited, G-TEC JAINX EDUCATION LIMITED, and Krebs Biochemicals and Industries Limited.
- Securities moved from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I to Stage-II effective October 06, 2026: D. P. Abhushan Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 08, 2026 on all open positions as on October 07, 2026 and new positions created from October 08, 2026 onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of securities under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework based on surveillance criteria.
- Application of 100% margin requirements on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on affected securities starting October 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM inclusion / stage movement: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin: October 08, 2026 (on open positions as on October 07, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities will be subject to strict margin requirements, impacting liquidity and trading behavior for the concerned scrips.
