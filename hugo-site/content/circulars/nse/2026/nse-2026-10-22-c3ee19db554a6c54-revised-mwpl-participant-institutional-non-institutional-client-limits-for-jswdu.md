## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a revision in the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL), as well as position limits for Participants, Institutional clients, and Non-Institutional clients for the symbol JSWDULUX under the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) Scheme. These revised limits take effect from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** JSWDULUX
- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Circular References:** Circular No. 109/2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/CMPT/77073), partially modifying previous circulars dated April 30, 2026, and September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The position limits under the SLB scheme have been revised as follows:

- **Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL):** Revised from 1,766,859 to 17,668,591 shares.
- **Institutional Client Limit:** Revised from 176,685 to 1,766,859 shares.
- **Participant Limit:** Revised from 176,685 to 1,766,859 shares.
- **Non-Institutional Client Limit:** Revised from 17,668 to 176,685 shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians are advised to take note of the revised position limits and ensure adherence while executing transactions in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme for JSWDULUX.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The upward revision expands the lending and borrowing capacity for JSWDULUX in the SLB segment, accommodating higher market participation.
