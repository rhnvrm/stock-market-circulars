## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Based on the criteria outlined in previous surveillance circulars, two securities—Scan Steels Limited and Sical Logistics Limited—have been shortlisted for inclusion under ESM Stage I. These securities will attract a minimum 100% margin requirement and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment. The circular also provides a consolidated list of securities currently under the ESM framework.

## Key Points

- **New Shortlisting (ESM Stage I)**: Two securities, Scan Steels Limited (Symbol: SCANSTL, ISIN: INE099G01011) and Sical Logistics Limited (Symbol: SICALLOG, ISIN: INE075B01020), have satisfied the criteria for inclusion in ESM Stage I.
- **Trading Segment Shift**: These newly shortlisted securities will shift from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 03, 2026.
- **Margin Requirements**: Shortlisted securities shall attract a minimum of 100% margin starting August 03, 2026, on all open positions as of July 31, 2026, and on new positions created from August 03, 2026.
- **Stage II Price Band**: Securities shifting to ESM Stage II will be under the Trade-for-Trade segment with a 2% price band under the Periodic Call Auction, effective July 31, 2026.
- **Exclusions**: No securities are eligible for exclusion from the ESM framework or shifting between stages in this review (Nil in Annexure II and other sections).
- **Consolidated List**: A consolidated list of all securities under the ESM framework is available, which includes Aarey Drugs & Pharmaceuticals Limited (Stage II) and Accord Synergy Limited (Stage II), among others.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) Framework**: Shifting of newly shortlisted securities to Stage I, moving them from rolling settlement series (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade series (BE/ST).
- **Price Band Limitations**: Price band is restricted to 2% under Periodic Call Auction for securities shifting to ESM Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members/Brokers**:
  - Must collect a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as of July 31, 2026, and on all new positions created from August 03, 2026, for SCANSTL and SICALLOG.
  - Must ensure that trading for SCANSTL and SICALLOG is conducted in the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) starting August 03, 2026.
  - Ensure trading terminals and systems are configured for Stage II price bands (2%) and periodic call auction mechanics for affected securities w.e.f. July 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **July 31, 2026**: Effective date for ESM Stage II price bands and Periodic Call Auction, and snapshot date for positions subject to the margin increase.
- **August 03, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin requirements on open/new positions and shifting of Stage I securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Liquidity**: Shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) is expected to significantly reduce trading volumes and intraday liquidity for SCANSTL and SICALLOG because day trading/speculative trading is barred.
- **Capital Cost**: A 100% margin requirement increases the cost of carrying positions in these stocks, forcing leverage reduction and potential squaring off of open positions.
- **Market Sentiment**: While the exchange notes that the ESM framework is purely for market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies, its application often leads to negative short-term pressure on stock prices due to reduced accessibility.
