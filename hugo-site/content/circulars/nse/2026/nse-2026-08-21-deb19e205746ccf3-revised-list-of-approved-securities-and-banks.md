## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a revised list of approved securities and banks, specifying equity shares that form part of the non-cash component of liquid assets along with applicable haircuts and permitted quantities.

## Key Points

- Contains the revised list of equity shares forming part of the non-cash component of liquid assets.
- Specifies overall permitted quantities across all segments and memberwise permitted quantities for the CM segment.
- Applicable haircut for the listed securities is set as VAR.
- Value-based prudential norms specify that exclusion of a pledged security should not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the list of eligible securities accepted as collateral and liquid assets under revised risk management frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing Members must ensure adherence to permitted quantities and value-based prudential norms regarding pledged securities and margin shortfalls.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular release.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects trading members and clearing members utilizing equity shares for non-cash component liquid assets and collateral management.
