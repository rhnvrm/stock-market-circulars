## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, September 05, 2026. The session involves trading from both the primary site and the Disaster Recovery (DR) site, with no new software version release.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** September 05, 2026.
- **Segments:** Commodity Derivatives segment.
- **Trading Sessions:** 
  - Trading Session-1 from Primary Site (Normal Market open at 08:45, close at 11:30).
  - Trading Session-2 from DR Site (Normal Market open at 12:15, close at 13:00).
- **Order Purge:** All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session. Members using NNF software must manually clear orders.
- **Financial Obligation:** Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- **UCC/PAN Validity:** Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed. UCC validation will be skipped during contingency time for order entry.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; conducted as a routine operational mock drill.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, September 07, 2026. Ensure NEATPlus installation procedures and connectivity parameters are followed as per referenced circulars.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Date:** September 05, 2026
- **Live Re-login Window:** September 05, 2026, from 14:30 to 15:00 Hrs
- **Live Market Readiness:** September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational drill to test member connectivity, system readiness, and switchover to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site with no direct financial impact.
