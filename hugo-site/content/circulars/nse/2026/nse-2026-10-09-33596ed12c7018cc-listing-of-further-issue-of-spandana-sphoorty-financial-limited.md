## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issue of equity shares of Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited, effective from October 12, 2026. The listing follows the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited
- Symbol: SPANDANA
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE572J01011 (Temporary ISIN: IN8572J01010 for demat credit)
- Number of Securities: 1,67,60,459 equity shares
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Pari Passu: Yes
- Lock-in Details: Not Applicable

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the securities shall be identified by designated codes and conducted in specified lot sizes. Shares allotted in dematerialized mode were credited under temporary ISIN in accordance with SEBI circulars dated August 02, 2012, and September 11, 2012.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 12, 2026
- Circular Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity capital base of Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited with the conversion of 1,67,60,459 partly paid-up shares into fully paid-up equity shares. Minimal direct market impact as a standard corporate capital adjustment.
