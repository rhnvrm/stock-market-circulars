## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the AlphaGrep Flexi Cap Fund of Alphagrep Mutual Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the NSE MF Invest Platform with effect from August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Reopening Date:** The schemes will be available for transactions starting August 13, 2026.
- **Available Facilities:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP).
- **Supported Schemes:** Six schemes are enabled, detailed below:
  1. AlphaGrep Flexi Cap Fund - Regular - Growth (ISIN: INF2VOA01103)
  2. AlphaGrep Flexi Cap Fund - Regular - IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2VOA01111)
  3. AlphaGrep Flexi Cap Fund - Regular - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF2VOA01129)
  4. AlphaGrep Flexi Cap Fund - Direct - Growth (ISIN: INF2VOA01079)
  5. AlphaGrep Flexi Cap Fund - Direct - IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2VOA01087)
  6. AlphaGrep Flexi Cap Fund - Direct - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF2VOA01095)

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes associated with this circular. It is a standard operational enablement of a mutual fund scheme on the NSE's mutual fund platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members and Distributors:** Ensure front-end and back-office systems are updated with the new scheme names and ISIN codes to facilitate smooth customer transactions.
- **For Investors:** No mandatory compliance action is required. Investors wishing to subscribe or transact in these schemes through the NSE MF Invest platform can do so starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine administrative update supporting the availability of a specific mutual fund scheme on the exchange platform.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Minor operational updates are required by members to support transactions under the listed ISINs.
