## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that Triveni Engineering & Industries Limited (TRIVENI) will be transferred from the trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) back to the rolling segment (series: EQ). This transfer is effective from August 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Triveni Engineering & Industries Limited (TRIVENI) is being shifted from the trade-for-trade segment (BE series) to the rolling segment (EQ series).
- The shift is pursuant to SEBI guidelines on Trade for Trade segment transitions (circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012) and follows a prior NSE circular (NSE/CML/75242) dated July 17, 2026.
- Normal trading and settlement rules (rolling settlement) will apply to the stock in the EQ series.

## Regulatory Changes

- The transfer aligns with SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, which governs guidelines for moving securities to and from the trade-for-trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members need to update their systems to reflect the change in trading series/segment for TRIVENI from BE to EQ.
- Normal rolling settlement and margin requirements will apply to TRIVENI from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **July 28, 2026**: Circular date.
- **August 05, 2026**: Effective date for transfer to rolling segment (series: EQ).

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity**: Transferring the security to the rolling segment (EQ series) will likely increase its liquidity, as transactions can now be netted and intra-day trading is permitted (unlike the trade-for-trade segment where every trade must result in actual delivery).
- **Operational**: Trading members must adjust trading filters and risk systems to allow normal rolling settlement for TRIVENI.
