## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I to certain securities, effective from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I.
- Applicable rate of margin is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. October 13, 2026, on all open positions as on October 12, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- List of securities included under Stage I: Alok Industries Limited (ALOKINDS), Inox Green Energy Services Limited (INOXGREEN), Phantom Digital Effects Limited (PHANTOMFX), PNGS Reva Diamond Jewellery Limited (PNGSREVA), Shanti Gold International Limited (SHANTIGOLD), Spunweb Nonwoven Limited (SPUNWEB), and V2 Retail Limited (V2RETAIL).
- No securities were listed under Stage II or moving from Stage I to Stage II in this circular.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures in accordance with the ST-ASM framework to monitor price volatility and market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective October 13, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date for ST-ASM Stage I: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in the affected scrips will be subject to higher margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in these specific securities.
