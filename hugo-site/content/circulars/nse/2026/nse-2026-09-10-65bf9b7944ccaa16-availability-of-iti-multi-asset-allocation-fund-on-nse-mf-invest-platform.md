## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund of ITI Mutual Fund is reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Effective Date: September 10, 2026.
- Covers multiple plans including Direct and Regular plans with Payout, Reinvestment, and Growth options across various ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members utilizing the NSE MF Invest Platform can access the newly available scheme variants for their clients as per standard operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members with expanded investment options on the NSE mutual fund platform.
