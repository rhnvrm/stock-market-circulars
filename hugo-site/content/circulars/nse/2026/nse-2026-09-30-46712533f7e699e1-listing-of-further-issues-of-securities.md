## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for Rox Hi Tech Limited with effect from October 01, 2026. A total of 1,030,926 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- issued under preferential allotment are admitted to dealings on the SME Emerge segment.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Rox Hi Tech Limited
- Symbol: ROXHITECH
- Security Description: Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under Preferential allotment.
- Number of Securities: 1,030,926
- Distinctive Number Range: 22937689 to 23968614
- Issue Price: Rs. 48.5 per share
- Date of Allotment: 24-Aug-2026
- ISIN: INE0PDJ01013 (Temporarily credited under temporary ISIN as per SEBI guidelines)
- Market Lot: 1,600

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing circular for further securities issued under preferential allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes. Shares allotted are subject to lock-in details until 15-Apr-2028.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: October 01, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry / Details: April 15, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Addition of further shares increases the paid-up capital base of Rox Hi Tech Limited on the SME Emerge segment, with standard trading lot sizes applicable.
