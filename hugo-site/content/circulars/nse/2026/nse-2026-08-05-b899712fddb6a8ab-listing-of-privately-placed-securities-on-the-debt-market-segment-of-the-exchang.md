## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has admitted several privately placed securities to dealings on its Debt Market Segment, effective August 05, 2026. This is issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations. The listings include a series of fresh issues of Commercial Papers (CPs) from major financial services and securities firms, as well as Other Debt Securities.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** The admitted securities are available for trading on the Debt Market Segment starting August 05, 2026.
- **Instrument Types:** Includes various fresh issues of Commercial Paper (CP) and Other Debt Securities.
- **Issuing Entities (Commercial Paper):**
  - Aditya Birla Money Limited
  - Axis Securities Limited
  - ICICI Securities Limited
  - Angel One Limited
  - Hdfc Securities Limited
  - Aditya Birla Capital Limited
  - Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited
  - Kotak Securities Limited
- **Issuing Entities (Other Debt Securities):**
  - SMFG India Credit Company Limited

## Regulatory Changes

This circular does not introduce new regulatory amendments. It is an administrative notification issued under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, facilitating the listing and trading of the specified privately placed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no direct compliance changes or action items for general market intermediaries, other than updating internal security master databases and trading systems with the newly listed ISINs and security codes to facilitate secondary market trading for interested clients.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates:** August 04, 2026 and August 05, 2026 (for various instruments as detailed below).
- **Listing Effective Date:** August 05, 2026.
- **Redemption/Maturity Dates:**
  - CP (Axis Securities Limited): September 17, 2026.
  - CP (Aditya Birla Money, ICICI Securities, Angel One, Hdfc Securities, Aditya Birla Capital, Motilal Oswal Financial Services): November 03, 2026.
  - CP (Kotak Securities, Hdfc Securities): November 04, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity:** The listing of these privately placed instruments provides secondary market liquidity on the exchange platform for these high-quality short-term corporate debt papers.
- **Operational Alignment:** Trading desks and operations teams should ensure that the designated security codes and ISINs are mapped correctly in their trading, risk management, and settlement systems.
