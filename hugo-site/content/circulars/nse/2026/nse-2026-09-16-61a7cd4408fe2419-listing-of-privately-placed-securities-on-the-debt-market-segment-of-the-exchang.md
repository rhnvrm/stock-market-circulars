## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Admission of commercial papers issued by companies such as Godrej Agrovet Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Blue Star Limited, CESC Limited, IDBI Capital Markets & Securities Limited, and HSBC Investdirect Financial Services (I) Limited.
- Effective date of listing: September 16, 2026.
- Allotment date: September 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard compliance and listing obligations for privately placed debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 15-Sep-2026
- Listing/Effective Date: 16-Sep-2026
- Redemption Dates ranging from October 2026 to December 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice with no direct impact on equity market segments.
