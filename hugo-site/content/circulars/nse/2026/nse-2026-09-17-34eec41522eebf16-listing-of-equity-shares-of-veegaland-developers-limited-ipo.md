## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of equity shares of Veegaland Developers Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), along with the disclosure of its post-issue shareholding pattern.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Veegaland Developers Limited
- Security Type: Equity Shares (Post-Issue)
- Date of Shareholding Pattern / As On: 17.09.2026
- Compliance under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard listing and disclosure formalities under SEBI LODR regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entity is required to adhere to ongoing disclosure and listing obligations as per SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 17.09.2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading of equity shares of Veegaland Developers Limited on the NSE platform.
