## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular informing members that HDFC Mutual Fund has temporarily restricted fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and fresh systematic registrations (SIP/STP) in select schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective from October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Affected Schemes**: HDFC Gold ETF Fund of Fund and HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF.
- **Restrictions**: Temporary restriction on fresh subscriptions, switch-in(s), and fresh systematic registrations (SIP/STP) received after the cut-off time (3:00 PM) on October 15, 2026.
- **Unrestricted Transactions**: Existing systematic transactions registered prior to the Effective Date, redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and STP-outs will continue without any changes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary operational restriction on mutual fund subscription services for specific fund-of-fund schemes on the platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must note the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions in the specified HDFC Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: October 15, 2026 (post 3:00 PM cut-off time).
- **Circular Date**: October 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors looking to make fresh investments or register new SIPs/STPs in HDFC Gold ETF FOF and HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF are temporarily restricted, while existing investments and redemption mechanisms remain fully operational.
