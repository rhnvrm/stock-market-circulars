## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, September 12, 2026. The session involves trading from both the Primary Site and the Disaster Recovery (DR) Site using NEATPlus EGR Version 1.0.1.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** September 12, 2026.
- **Trading Sessions:** Includes two trading sessions: Trading Session-1 from Primary Site (Pre-open 10:45 to Normal Close 13:30) and Trading Session-2 from DR Site (Pre-open 14:15 to Normal Close 14:45).
- **NEAT+ Version:** Version 1.0.1 (Download path: `/egftp/egcommon/NEATPlus101`).
- **Default Password:** “Neat@EG1”.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes; this is a routine market operations mock session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the EGR segment are advised to take note of the schedule, login parameters, and network connectivity details to test their systems successfully.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** September 12, 2026
- **Live Re-login Window:** 17:30 to 18:00 hours on September 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Members can verify system readiness, Disaster Recovery switchover procedures, and broadcast parameters for the EGR segment without any financial exposure.
