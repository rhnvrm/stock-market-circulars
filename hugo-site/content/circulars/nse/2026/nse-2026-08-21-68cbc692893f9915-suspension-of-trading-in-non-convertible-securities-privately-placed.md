## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of Power Finance Corporation Limited due to redemption, effective from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Power Finance Corporation Limited
- ISIN: INE134E08LS5
- Suspension Date: 24-August-2026
- Reason: Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants should note the suspension of trading for the specified ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact as this is a routine suspension following the redemption of privately placed non-convertible securities.
