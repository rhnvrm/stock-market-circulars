## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified members regarding a change in the name of the Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) scheme managed by Quantum Asset Management Company Private Limited. The name of the scheme will be updated from 'Quantum Gold Fund -Exchange Traded Fund (ETF)' to 'Quantum Gold ETF', effective August 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Asset Management Company:** Quantum Asset Management Company Private Limited
- **Symbol:** QGOLDHALF
- **ISIN:** INF082J01408
- **Existing Scheme Name:** Quantum Gold Fund -Exchange Traded Fund (ETF)
- **New Scheme Name:** Quantum Gold ETF
- **Effective Date:** August 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced by this circular. The modification is strictly a corporate name realignment for the ETF scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market intermediaries are required to update their databases and trading systems to display the new scheme name.
- No action is required from individual retail investors, as the trading symbol (QGOLDHALF) and ISIN (INF082J01408) remain unchanged.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a purely administrative change with no impact on trading operations or liquidity.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Systems mapping scheme names to ISINs/Symbols must be updated to ensure accurate reporting and client statements.
