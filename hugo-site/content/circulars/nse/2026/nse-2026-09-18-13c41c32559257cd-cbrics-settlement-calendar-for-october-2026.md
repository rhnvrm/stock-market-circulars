## Summary

NSE Clearing Ltd. has issued the settlement calendar for CBRICS (Corporate Bond Reporting and Integrated Clearing and Settlement platform) for the month of October 2026. Participants and custodians must adhere to the specified settlement dates and transfer securities using the 'On-market' settlement mode.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement dates and numbers for October 2026.
- Market Type: CORPORATE BOND.
- Clearing Member Name: NSE Clearing Ltd.
- CM-BP ID: IN568177.
- Reminder that previously used DP details (IN001002-10000617) are invalid for CBRICS settlement.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a routine operational schedule circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians are requested to transfer securities for CBRICS settlement based on the provided schedule using 'On-market' settlement mode for NSDL/CDSL transfers.

## Important Dates

- Effective period: October 2026 (Settlement dates range from 01-Oct-2026 to 30-Oct-2026).

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth and timely clearing and settlement of corporate bond trades on the CBRICS platform for October 2026.
