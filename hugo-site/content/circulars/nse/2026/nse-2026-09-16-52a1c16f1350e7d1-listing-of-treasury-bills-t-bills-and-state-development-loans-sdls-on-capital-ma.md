## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of various Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on its capital market segment, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted: 91D, 182D, 364D Treasury Bills and various State Development Loans (SDLs).
- Effective Date: September 18, 2026.
- Trading Lot Size: 100 for all listed securities.
- Identification: Securities will be identified by their designated codes and ISINs as specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes during order placement.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides secondary market liquidity and trading access for investors in government debt instruments (T-bills and SDLs).
