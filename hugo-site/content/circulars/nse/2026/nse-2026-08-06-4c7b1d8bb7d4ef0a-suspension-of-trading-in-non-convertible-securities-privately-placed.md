## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities of Sundaram Home Finance Limited and HDFC Bank Limited. This suspension, in accordance with Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A, will take effect on August 07, 2026, following the redemption of these securities.

## Key Points

- **Suspension of Trading:** Trading in the specified privately placed Non-Convertible Securities is suspended with effect from August 07, 2026.
- **Affected Entities:** Two securities are affected: Sundaram Home Finance Limited (ISIN: INE667F07IN0) and HDFC Bank Limited (ISIN: INE040A08484).
- **Reason for Suspension:** The suspension of trading is due to the redemption of the respective debt securities.

## Regulatory Changes

The suspension is implemented under the provisions of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A. No changes to the regulatory framework have been introduced.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are required to note the suspension of trading for these specific ISINs and update their internal tracking and trading systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 06, 2026
- **Suspension Effective Date:** August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Very Low. This is a routine administrative process for debt instruments that have reached maturity and been redeemed, and has no broader implications for the market or trading volumes.
- **Issuer Impact:** Low/Neutral. The suspension reflects the completion of redemption obligations by the respective issuers (Sundaram Home Finance Limited and HDFC Bank Limited) on maturity.
