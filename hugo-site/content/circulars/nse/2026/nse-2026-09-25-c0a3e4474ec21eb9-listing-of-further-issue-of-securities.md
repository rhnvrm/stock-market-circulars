## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issue of 50,388 equity shares of Lead Reclaim And Rubber Products Limited (Symbol: LRRPL) pursuant to the conversion of warrants issued on a preferential basis, effective from September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lead Reclaim And Rubber Products Limited
- Symbol: LRRPL
- Series: SM
- ISIN: INE0H4B01011 (Temporary ISIN applies for demat credit per SEBI guidelines)
- Number of Securities: 50,388 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 75/-
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Market Lot: 1,500 shares
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the SME EMERGE segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Lock-in details apply as specified in Annexure A (43,555 shares locked-in until March 31, 2028; 6,833 shares locked-in until March 31, 2027).
- Equity shares are credited under temporary ISIN in accordance with SEBI circulars dated August 02, 2012, and September 11, 2012.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: July 7, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the paid-up equity capital of Lead Reclaim And Rubber Products Limited through preferential warrant conversion with standard lock-in constraints.
