## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the face value split of equity shares for JSW Dulux Limited (JSWDULUX). The face value and paid-up value will be changed from Rs. 10 to Re. 1 per share effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: JSW Dulux Limited
- Symbol: JSWDULUX
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Re. 1
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Listing department updates reflecting the corporate action of face value split for the specified security.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading and settlement systems to reflect the new face value for JSW Dulux Limited.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The stock split increases the number of outstanding shares while proportionally reducing the share price, enhancing retail liquidity and accessibility.
