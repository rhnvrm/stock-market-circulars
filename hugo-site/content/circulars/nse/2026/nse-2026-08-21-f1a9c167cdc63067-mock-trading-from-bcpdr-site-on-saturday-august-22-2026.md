## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment from the BCP/DR site on Saturday, August 22, 2026. The mock session will be followed by a re-login session from the Primary site to ensure seamless operations for Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** August 22, 2026
- **Trading Session from DR Site:** Pre-open starts at 08:45 hrs, Normal Market open at 09:00 hrs, and Normal Market close at 17:00 hrs.
- **Re-login Timings from Primary Site:** 18:30 hrs to 19:00 hrs.
- **Adapter Settings:** No changes required in NEAT Adapter settings; live session settings of August 21, 2026, will be retained.
- **System Switchover:** Exchange will shift operations from primary to DR site during the mock session via a non-graceful shutdown.
- **Order Management:** All outstanding orders will be purged before trading starts from the BCP site.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes; this is a routine technical mock trading exercise.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should ensure systems are brought into a consistent state as per circular NSE/MSD/48662 in the event of a non-graceful shutdown.
- NNF software users must clear outstanding orders of session 1 prior to BCP site trading.
- Extranet and Member portal access will be unavailable from 10:00 am to 11:00 am and 06:00 pm to 09:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** August 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on live trading as the mock session is conducted on a non-business day (Saturday) to ensure system readiness and disaster recovery preparedness.
