## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Several securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I.

## Key Points

- Securities added to ST-ASM Stage I effective August 18, 2026 (based on positions as of August 17, 2026).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100%.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on August 17, 2026, and new positions created from August 18, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of enhanced margin requirements (50% to 100%) for securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance for the listed securities in compliance with the ST-ASM framework.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased capital requirements for trading in the affected securities due to higher margin rates, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes for the shortlisted scrips.
