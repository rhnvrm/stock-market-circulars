## Summary

In continuation of the Exchange circular NSE/SURV/51189 dated January 31, 2022, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued an update on surveillance measures for companies with high Promoter as well as non-Promoter 'Encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. 

The update details three exclusions from the surveillance framework and lists four companies that remain under the consolidated framework. No new securities have been added to the framework in this review cycle.

## Key Points

- **Inclusions:** No securities have been added to the framework for this period (Annexure I).
- **Exclusions:** Three securities are excluded from the framework effective August 03, 2026 (Annexure II).
- **Consolidated List:** Four securities continue to remain under the surveillance framework (Annexure III).
- **Margin Implication:** Securities added to this framework attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments on all open and new positions.
- **Clarification:** Shortlisting under this measure is a surveillance function and does not constitute adverse action against any concerned company or entity.

## Regulatory Changes

No structural regulatory changes have been made. The circular is an administrative and list-based update of securities subjected to surveillance under Regulation 28(3) of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Trading members must update their risk systems to reflect the exclusion of the three specified securities from the surveillance framework effective August 03, 2026.
- **Margin Collection:** Members must ensure that a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments is applied to any active or future securities listed under this framework.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Effective Date for Exclusions:** August 03, 2026
- **Effective Date for Inclusions (if any):** August 05, 2026, on all open positions as of August 04, 2026, and new positions created from August 05, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- **Excluded Securities (BLKASHYAP, JAYNECOIND, SHAREINDIA):** Exiting the framework will remove the 75% minimum margin requirement, likely improving trading volumes, market interest, and liquidity for these counters.
- **Consolidated Securities (AQYLON, STEELXIND, THYROCARE, ELPROINTL):** These securities remain under high promoter encumbrance surveillance and will continue to be subject to a minimum 75% margin requirement, which restricts trading leverage.
