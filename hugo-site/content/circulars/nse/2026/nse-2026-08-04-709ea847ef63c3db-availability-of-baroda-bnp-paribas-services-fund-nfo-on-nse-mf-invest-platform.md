## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the availability of the Baroda BNP Paribas Services Fund (reopened from its New Fund Offer phase) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The scheme will be open for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plans (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plans (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP) with effect from August 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Reopening**: The Baroda BNP Paribas Services Fund will be reopened for ongoing transactions from August 05, 2026.
- **Facilities Offered**: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities will be available.
- **Schemes Covered**: Six schemes of the fund are included, covering regular and direct plans with growth, IDCW payout, and IDCW reinvestment options.
- **ISIN Details**:
  - Baroda BNP Paribas Service Fund - Regular Plan Growth: `INF251K01VV1`
  - Baroda BNP Paribas Service Fund - Regular Plan IDCW Payout: `INF251K01VW9`
  - Baroda BNP Paribas Service Fund - Regular Plan IDCW Reinvestment: `INF251K01VX7`
  - Baroda BNP Paribas Service Fund - Direct Plan Growth: `INF251K01VY5`
  - Baroda BNP Paribas Service Fund - Direct Plan IDCW Payout: `INF251K01VU3`
  - Baroda BNP Paribas Service Fund - Direct Plan IDCW Reinvestment: `INF251K01VZ2`
- **Category**: All schemes are categorized under Equity.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no changes to existing regulatory frameworks. This is an operational enablement of a mutual fund scheme on the NSE MF platform under established guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members/Participants**: Members of the NSE MF Invest Platform should update their systems and back-office records to facilitate orders, subscriptions, and redemptions for these schemes from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 04, 2026
- **Effective/Reopening Date**: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Low. The addition/reopening of schemes on mutual fund platforms is a routine operational process. It provides investors on the NSE MF Invest platform with access to transactions in the Baroda BNP Paribas Services Fund, thereby increasing liquidity and investment avenues.
