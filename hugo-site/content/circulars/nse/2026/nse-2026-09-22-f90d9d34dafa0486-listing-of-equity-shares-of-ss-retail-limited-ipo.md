## Summary

NSE has announced the listing of equity shares of SS Retail Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The circular includes post-issue shareholding pattern details, showing promoter and public holding distributions as of September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: SS Retail Limited
- Scrip Code/Symbol: SSRETAIL (implied by context/IPO details)
- Date of Filing: 22.09.2026
- Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 48,232,659 equity shares (64.86%)
- Public Holding: 26,134,916 equity shares (35.14%)
- Total Paid-up Equity Shares: 74,367,575 equity shares
