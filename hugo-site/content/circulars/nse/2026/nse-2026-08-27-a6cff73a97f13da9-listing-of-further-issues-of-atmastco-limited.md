## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares of Atmastco Limited under the SME EMERGE segment, effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Atmastco Limited
- Symbol: ATMASTCO
- Series: SM
- ISIN: INE05DH01017
- Issue Price: Rs. 152
- Number of Securities: 1,423,600 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under Preferential Issue.
- Distinctive Number Range: 24734691 to 26158290
- Market Lot: 400
- Lock-in details: 30-Mar-2027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be in the specified lot sizes and identified by their designated codes.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 24-Jun-2026
- Effective Date: 28-Aug-2026

## Impact Assessment

Addition of further shares to trading on the SME EMERGE segment.
