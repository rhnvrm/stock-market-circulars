## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the Invesco India Nifty India Defence Index Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The NFO will be available for subscription from September 15, 2026, to September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Invesco India Nifty India Defence Index Fund
- Available Plans: Regular Plan (Growth, IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment) and Direct Plan (Growth, IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment).
- Subscription Window: September 15, 2026, to September 29, 2026, till 11:59 pm.
- Allotment Date: October 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 06, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 15, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 29, 2026
- Allotment Date / Clear Funds Deadline: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides members and investors access to transact in the new defence index fund scheme through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
