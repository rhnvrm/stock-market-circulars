## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited owing to maturity, effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited
- Affected Securities: NCDs with symbols 925IHFL25E (ISIN: INE121A07RF6) and CIFC26A (ISIN: INE121A07RD1)
- Reason for Suspension: Maturity
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, trading in the specified securities stands suspended.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance action is required by market participants other than noting the suspension of trading for the specified ISINs.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to the cessation of secondary market trading for the matured Non-Convertible Debentures of the company.
