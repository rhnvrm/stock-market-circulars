## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the implementation of a dedicated 1600 series number (1600322410) for outbound calls made by its Investor Service Centres and the Defaulter department, effective August 15, 2026. This initiative aims to help investors easily identify authentic calls from the Exchange and safeguard them against fraudulent or impersonation attempts.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 15, 2026.
- Dedicated Outbound Number: 1600322410 (used exclusively for outbound calls from NSE Investor Service Centres and Defaulter department).
- Inbound Restrictions: Investors and market participants cannot call or send messages back to this number.
- Existing Contact Channel: The existing Toll-Free number (1800 266 0050) remains active for contacting Exchange departments.
- Fraud Prevention Advisory: Investors are strongly advised not to disclose sensitive personal information (such as PAN, Aadhaar, bank/card details, login IDs, passwords, or OTPs) over phone calls.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of a standardized communication channel for outbound investor outreach by the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and investors should note the new outbound number and remain vigilant against unauthorized callers impersonating exchange officials.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances investor trust, security, and awareness by establishing a reliable mechanism to authenticate official communications from the NSE.
