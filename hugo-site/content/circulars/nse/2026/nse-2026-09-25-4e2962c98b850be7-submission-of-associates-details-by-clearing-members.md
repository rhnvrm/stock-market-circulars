## Summary

National Clearing Corporation Ltd. (NCL) has outlined the step-by-step procedure for Clearing Members and Trading Members to submit associate details via the NCL Member Portal. Members must configure sub-users with specific inspection roles to complete the submission under the compliance tab.

## Key Points

- Access the NCL Member Portal at https://ims.connect2nsccl.com/NCLMemberPortal/.
- Admin users can reset passwords and create sub-users under User Management by assigning the 'NCL INSPECTION' role.
- Navigate to the Compliance tab and select 'Associate Details Submission'.
- Submissions are categorized into CMS (Clearing Member for Self), CMA (Clearing Member for Associates), TMS (Trading Member for Self), and TMA (Trading Member for Associates).
- Mandatory disclosure of listed securities or listed debt instruments held by members or their group/associate entities, along with ISIN selection and PAN details or reasons for unavailability.

## Regulatory Changes

Establishment of a streamlined portal-based reporting mechanism for clearing and trading members to disclose associate and group entity holdings in listed instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing and Trading Members must create sub-users with 'NCL_Inspection' access.
- Accurate reporting of self and associate entity details, including listed ISINs, PAN numbers, and proper categorization.

## Important Dates

Refer to the NCL portal notifications for specific operational windows and submission deadlines.

## Impact Assessment

Improves regulatory monitoring and transparency of associate entities and group structures connected with clearing and trading members.
