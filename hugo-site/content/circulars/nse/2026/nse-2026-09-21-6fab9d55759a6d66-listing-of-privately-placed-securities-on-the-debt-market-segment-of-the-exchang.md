## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the admission of privately placed securities (commercial papers) to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 21, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026.
- Segment: Debt Market Segment.
- Issuing entities include Nomura Capital (India) Private Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Trust Investment Advisors Private Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, SRF Limited, and Reliance Retail Ventures Limited.
- Securities comprise commercial papers with varying tenures and ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Standard listing and depository compliance for privately placed commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 18, 2026
- Listing/Effective Date: September 21, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from October 19, 2026 to March 17, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing of debt instruments with no direct impact on secondary equity trading.
