## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers and other debt securities) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh issue of commercial papers and other debt securities admitted to dealings effective August 31, 2026.
- Includes issuances from entities such as Kotak Securities Limited, Cholamandalam Securities Limited, NABARD, Godrej Industries Limited, Apollo Tyres Limited, Vision Infra Equipment Solutions Limited, and IIFL Finance Limited.
- Re-issuance of commercial papers also included for select companies.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and issuers must comply with the standard listing and disclosure norms under the relevant Exchange regulations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: August 31, 2026
- Allotment Dates: August 27, 2026 - August 28, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Various dates ranging from September 2026 to August 2030

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update facilitating secondary market trading for newly listed debt instruments and commercial papers.
