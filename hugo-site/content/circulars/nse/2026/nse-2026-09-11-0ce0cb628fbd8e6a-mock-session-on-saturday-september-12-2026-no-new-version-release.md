## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, September 12, 2026, across primary and DR sites.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** September 12, 2026.
- **Trading Session-1 (Primary Site):** Normal Market open at 11:00 hours, close at 12:30 hours.
- **Trading Session-2 (DR Site):** Normal Market open at 13:15 hours, close at 13:30 hours.
- **Position Limit/Collateral Cut-off:** 13:40 hours.
- **Trade Modification End Time:** 13:40 hours.
- **Live Re-login Timings:** Start at 17:30 hours, close at 18:00 hours.
- **NEAT Version:** 1.5.5.
- **Order Purge:** All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment on September 12, 2026, to ensure smooth connectivity for Tuesday, September 15, 2026. Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Date:** September 12, 2026
- **Live Re-login Window:** September 12, 2026, from 17:30 to 18:00 hours

## Impact Assessment

Enables members to test connectivity and trading setups in the Commodity Derivatives segment ahead of live operations.
