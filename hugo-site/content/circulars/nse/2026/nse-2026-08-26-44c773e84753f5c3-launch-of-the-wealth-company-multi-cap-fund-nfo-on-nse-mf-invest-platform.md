## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the launch of The Wealth Company Multi Cap Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from August 27, 2026, to September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund NFO: The Wealth Company Multi Cap Fund
- Subscription Period: August 27, 2026 to September 10, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Available Plans: Direct and Regular options covering IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment, and Growth.
- Allotment Date: September 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 16, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 27, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 10, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 16, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to transact in the new multi-cap fund scheme through the NSE mutual fund platform.
