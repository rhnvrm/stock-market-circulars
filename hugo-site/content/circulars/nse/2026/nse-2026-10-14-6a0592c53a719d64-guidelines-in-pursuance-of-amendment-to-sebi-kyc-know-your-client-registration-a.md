## Summary

In pursuance of amendments to SEBI KRA Regulations, 2011 and relevant SEBI circulars, the National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued guidelines regarding trading restrictions on PANs with On-Hold, Not Available, or Demise KRA status, effective October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- PANs uploaded to KRAs till September 30, 2026, with Primary KYC status "On-Hold" are restricted from trading or squaring off open positions effective October 31, 2026.
- Members must review exchange-provided files (`<TMcode>_KRA_NOT_AVAILABLE` and `<TMcode>_Demise_Clients_by_KRA`) for necessary action.
- Trading members are required to exercise due diligence and mark relevant Unique Client Codes (UCCs) as "Closed" in the Exchange's UCC database for demised clients before October 31, 2026.
- Only clients with KRA status as either "KYC Registered" or "KYC Validated" are permitted to trade.
- PANs becoming KRA compliant subsequently will be permitted to trade on T+1 based on KRA updates.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of centralized mechanism for reporting investor demise and stringent validation of KYC status through KRAs, restricting trading access for non-compliant and demised accounts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must download non-compliant PAN lists from ENIT (ENIT > Log > Downloads).
- Block trading and square-off capabilities for On-Hold and Not Available KYC status PANs effective October 31, 2026.
- Mark UCCs of demised clients as "Closed" in the Exchange database following due diligence.

## Important Dates

- September 30, 2026: Cut-off date for KRA PAN status uploads.
- October 05, 2026: Exchange flagging date for KYC non-compliant PANs.
- October 31, 2026: Effective date for trading restrictions and closure of demised client UCCs.

## Impact Assessment

High operational impact for trading members and clients whose KYC records are incomplete, on-hold, or demised, resulting in immediate suspension of trading rights until compliance is restored.
