## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Securities of NTPC Limited, Power Finance Corporation Limited, and Union Bank of India are suspended from trading.
- Suspension is effective from August 31, 2026.
- Reasons for suspension include redemption and exercise of call options.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Action taken pursuant to existing debt market trading regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the suspension of trading for the specified ISINs.

## Important Dates

- **Suspension Date:** August 31, 2026
- **Circular Date:** August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for debt market participants holding or trading the specified non-convertible securities.
