## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced that the Quant Income Plus Arbitrage Active FOF NFO by Quant Mutual Fund will be available for transaction on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 17, 2026, to August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme NFO Window: August 17, 2026, to August 31, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- Available Plans: Direct Plan and Regular Plan (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- Asset Category: Debt.
- Allotment Date: September 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 04, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 17, 2026
- NFO Close Date: August 31, 2026
- Allotment Date: September 04, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 04, 2026 (03:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the new fund offer via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
