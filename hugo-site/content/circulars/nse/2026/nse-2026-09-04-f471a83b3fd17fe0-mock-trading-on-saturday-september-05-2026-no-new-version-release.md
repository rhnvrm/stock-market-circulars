## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives Segment on Saturday, September 05, 2026. The session will feature trading from both the primary site and the Disaster Recovery (DR) site with no new version release.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** September 05, 2026.
- **Segment:** Currency Derivatives Segment.
- **NEAT Version:** NEAT 3.5.4.
- **Order Purge:** All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session; members using NNF software must manually clear orders.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- **UCC/PAN Validation:** Valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded before the cutoff will be allowed, and UCC validation will be skipped during contingency time for order entry.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The session is conducted to meet regulatory testing requirements using mock trading or simulated environments.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, September 07, 2026. Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** September 05, 2026
- **Trading Session-1 Primary Site Normal Market Open:** 08:45 Hrs
- **Trading Session-2 DR Site Normal Market Open:** 12:15 Hrs
- **Live Re-login Close Time:** 15:00 Hrs
- **Live Environment Resume Date:** September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Ensures system readiness, connectivity, and switchover capabilities to the Disaster Recovery site for trading members ahead of the live market session.
