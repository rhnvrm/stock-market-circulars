## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding measures in respect of companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities meeting the criteria will attract a minimum 75% margin in the equity and equity derivatives segments.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments w.e.f. October 09, 2026, on all open positions as on October 08, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities in Annexure II are eligible to move out from the framework effective from October 07, 2026 (Nil listed in Annexure II).
- Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities under the framework, including BPL Limited and Race Eco Chain Limited.
- This measure is in conjunction with all other prevailing measures and is subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance margin requirements under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure the application of minimum 75% margin on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities starting October 09, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 06, 2026
- Exclusion Effective Date (Annexure II): October 07, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: October 08, 2026
- Margin Effective Date (Annexure I): October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants holding positions in affected securities (such as BPL and Race Eco Chain) will experience higher margin blocking (minimum 75%), increasing capital requirements for open and new positions.
