## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the **Bandhan Contra Fund** of Bandhan Mutual Fund will be open for transaction on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The transaction window is open from **August 10, 2026**, to **August 24, 2026** (till 11:59 pm).

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Bandhan Contra Fund
- **Subscription Window:** Available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026 (until 11:59 pm).
- **Available Options:** Both Regular and Direct plans are available with Growth and IDCW (Annual / Annual-Reinvestment) options.
- **ISINs Offered:** Six distinct ISINs covering Regular/Direct, Growth/IDCW options.
- **Funding Deadline:** Trading members must ensure that clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before **August 27, 2026** (until 11:59 pm).
- **Allotment Date:** The allotment of units is scheduled for **August 28, 2026**.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes are introduced by this circular. It represents a routine operational announcement for a product launch on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members transacting on the NSE MF Invest Platform on behalf of investors must:
- Facilitate client transactions during the specified subscription window.
- Ensure that clear funds, through any permitted payment mode, are successfully received in the Clearing Corporation's designated account on or before August 27, 2026, till 11:59 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 10, 2026
- **NFO End Date (Transaction Window):** August 24, 2026 (until 11:59 pm)
- **Funds Receipt Deadline:** August 27, 2026 (until 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a standard product launch (NFO) on the NSE's mutual fund platform and does not impact secondary market equity trading.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Operational impact is limited to mutual fund intermediaries and clearing members who need to process subscriptions and ensure timely settlement of funds within the specified deadlines.
