## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the proposed Offer for Sale (OFS) of units of Knowledge Realty Trust (Symbol: KRT) scheduled to take place on August 31, 2026, and September 01, 2026. The issue comprises up to 740,000,000 base offer units with an oversubscription option of an additional 370,000,000 units.

## Key Points

- **Issue Dates:** August 31, 2026, and September 01, 2026.
- **Base Offer Size:** Upto 740,000,000 units of REITs, with an additional oversubscription option of upto 370,000,000 units.
- **Floor Price:** Rs. 108/- per unit.
- **Tick Size:** Re. 0.01.
- **Market Lot:** 1 unit of REIT.
- **Platform:** NSE eOFS platform (https://eofs.nseindia.com).

## Regulatory Changes

- The Offer for Sale is conducted in accordance with the implementation of Interoperability among Clearing Corporations along with Exchange Scheme circular NSE/CMTR/72975 dated February 24, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible trading members of the Capital Market segment can participate using the eOFS platform.
- Members requiring Admin user IDs must request them via the ENIT portal (ENIT-NEW-TRADE→Membership→Enablement→eOFS) before the processing intervals.
- UCC of self (PRO) and clients must be compliant and updated in the Unique Client Identification (UCI) system a day prior to the bidding session.
- Orders will not be accepted if the UCC is updated or becomes compliant on the day of the bidding session.
- Members must ensure sufficiency of collaterals before carrying forward orders on T+1 day.

## Important Dates

- **OFS Bidding Dates:** August 31, 2026, and September 01, 2026.
- **Non-Retail Category Bidding Time:** August 31, 2026, from 09:15 hrs. to 15:30 hrs.
- **Oversubscription Intimation:** On or before 5:00 PM on August 31, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates institutional and retail participation in the Offer for Sale of Knowledge Realty Trust units via the NSE eOFS platform with established operational safeguards and timelines.
