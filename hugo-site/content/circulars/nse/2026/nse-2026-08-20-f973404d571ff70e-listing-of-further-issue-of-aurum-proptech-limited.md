## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further issue of 39,775 equity shares of Aurum PropTech Limited with effect from August 21, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aurum PropTech Limited
- Symbol: AURUM
- Security Description: Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares
- Number of Securities: 39,775
- Face Value: Rs. 5/-
- Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/-
- Effective Date: August 21, 2026
- ISIN: INE898S01029 (Temporary ISIN: IN8898S01010 for dematerialized allotment)

## Regulatory Changes

None. The listing is conducted in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be identified by the designated codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 20, 2026
- Effective Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion and conversion of shares for Aurum PropTech Limited with minimal market impact.
