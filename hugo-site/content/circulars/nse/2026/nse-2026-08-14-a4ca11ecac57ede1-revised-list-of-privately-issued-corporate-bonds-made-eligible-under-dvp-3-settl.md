## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a revised list of privately issued corporate bonds made eligible under the DVP-3 settlement mechanism on the Institutional Platform of the Debt Segment.

## Key Points

- Inclusion of various corporate bonds issued by prominent entities including Reliance Industries Limited, Larsen & Toubro Limited, REC Limited, Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited, HDFC Bank Limited, Indian Railway Finance Corporation (IRFC), State Bank of India (SBI), and ICICI Bank.
- Specifies instruments, ISIN codes, maturity dates, and issuing entities eligible for institutional debt trading and settlement.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the eligible securities roster for DVP-3 settlement on the debt platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure transactions in the listed corporate bonds adhere to the DVP-3 settlement guidelines on the Institutional Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates smoother clearing and settlement for secondary market transactions in specified corporate debt securities.
