## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar and timelines for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares of East India Drums and Barrels Manufacturing Ltd., outlining important dates and timings for retail and non-retail categories.

## Key Points

- Settlement calendar details for Offer for Sale of East India Drums and Barrels Manufacturing Ltd. shares.
- Covers both retail (RS) and non-retail (IS) investor categories across multiple settlement numbers (2026160 and 2026161).
- Funds pay-in is scheduled for T+1 day by 11:00 am, and securities & funds pay-out by 01:30 pm.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must ensure adherence to the specified settlement dates, margin requirements, and fund/securities pay-in and pay-out timelines.

## Important Dates

- Order Dates: August 25, 2026 and August 26, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 27, 2026
- Funds Pay-in: T+1 day by 11:00 am
- Securities & Funds Pay-out: T+1 day by 01:30 pm
