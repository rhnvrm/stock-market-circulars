## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective October 13, 2026. The circular details securities satisfying criteria under ASM for companies as per IBC, including corporate announcements and stage classifications.

## Key Points

- Polycab India Limited (POLYCAB) is shortlisted w.r.t. receipt of corporate announcement/disclosure under IBC with effect from October 13, 2026, attracting 100% margin on derivative contracts.
- Impex Ferro Tech Limited (IMPEXFERRO) continues under Stage II of ASM for Companies as per IBC.
- Shortlisting under ASM IBC is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned entity.
- ASM IBC framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures and margin requirements under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework for specified companies.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must note the 100% margin requirement on derivative contracts for applicable securities and ensure adherence to all prevailing surveillance measures.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 13, 2026
- Corporate Announcement Date (Polycab): October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities listed under the ASM IBC framework, particularly Polycab India Limited, will be subject to 100% margin requirements on derivative contracts, impacting liquidity and margin utilization.
