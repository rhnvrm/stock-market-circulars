## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular (Ref No: NSE/SURV/75716, Circular Ref No: 619/2026) regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Following market surveillance review, 13 securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I, effective August 13, 2026. The surveillance actions are preventive in nature and do not represent an adverse action against the shortlisted companies.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusion**: 13 securities have been added to the Short-Term ASM Stage I list.
- **ST-ASM Stage II Inclusion**: No securities were added to the Short-Term ASM Stage II list.
- **Stage Transition**: No securities have transitioned from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II in this circular.
- **Precautionary Measure**: The shortlisting of securities under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance parameters and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- **ST-ASM Stage I Margin Requirement**: A higher rate of margin—50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at a maximum of 100%—will apply to all open positions as of August 13, 2026, and all new positions created from August 14, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Collection**: Trading members must collect and maintain the enhanced margin rate of 50% (or higher, capped at 100%) for all clients holding positions in the 13 shortlisted securities.
- **Risk Management**: Risk Management Systems (RMS) must be updated by the trading members to implement the revised margin requirement on the effective dates.

## Important Dates

- **August 12, 2026**: Date of circular issuance.
- **August 13, 2026**: Shortlisting of securities under ST-ASM Stage I comes into effect.
- **August 14, 2026**: Enhanced margin requirements (50% or higher) become applicable on all open positions as of August 13, 2026, and on new positions created.

## Impact Assessment

- **Leverage & Volume**: Traders will experience increased margin requirements (minimum 50%) for the 13 affected stocks, which is likely to reduce trading leverage, decrease daily volumes, and impact market liquidity for these specific counters.
