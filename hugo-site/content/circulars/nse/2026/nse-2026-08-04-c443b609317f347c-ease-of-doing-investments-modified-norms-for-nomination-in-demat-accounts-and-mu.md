## Summary

Following SEBI's guidelines to improve the ease of investing, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has implemented modified norms for nomination in Demat accounts and mutual fund folios. These changes simplify the nomination process, reduce operational friction for investors, and specify the updated bulk upload Client Master Common file format (183-column) under the Mutual Fund Service System (MFSS).

## Key Points

- **Mandatory Nomination for Single Holders:** Nomination is mandatory for all new single-holder Demat accounts and mutual fund folios opened on or after September 1, 2026. Investors must either provide nominee details (up to three nominees) or submit a formal "Opt-out" declaration.
- **Optional for Joint Accounts:** Nomination remains optional for jointly held accounts and folios. However, any addition or change to a nominee requires the consent of all joint holders.
- **Data Requirements Simplified:** Only the nominee's name and relationship with the investor are mandatory. Other details, such as address and contact info, are now optional to streamline onboarding.
- **UCC Client Master Format:** Details the 183-column file structure for Client Master Common bulk upload on the Mutual Fund Service System (MFSS), including fields for client code, tax status, gender, holding nature, and other account holder details.

## Regulatory Changes

- **SEBI Alignment:** This circular implements SEBI circular SEBI/HO/OIAE/OIAE_IAD-3/P/CIR/2026/12676 dated May 29, 2026, to solve operational issues highlighted by stakeholders.
- **Equal Allocation Default:** If multiple nominees are added without specifying percentage shares, assets will be divided equally. Fractional balances will be assigned to the first-named nominee.
- **Removal of Witness Requirement:** For physical nomination forms, a witness signature is only required if a thumb impression is used instead of a signature.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Systems Upgrade:** Market intermediaries, depository participants, asset management companies (AMCs), and registrars (RTAs) must update their portals and onboarding platforms to support the modified nomination flow and simplified fields.
- **File Upload Compliance:** Members utilizing the MFSS bulk upload system must format their UCC registration files to strictly align with the Client Master Common 183-column layout, ensuring proper validation of mandatory and conditional fields.
- **Joint Holder Authorization:** Intermediaries must establish a process to secure consent from all joint account holders before any nominee modifications are registered.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Issue Date:** August 4, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Investor Convenience:** The elimination of redundant mandatory fields significantly reduces rejection rates and speeds up account opening and investment processes.
- **Operational Adjustments:** AMCs and broker-dealers must adapt their back-office systems and bulk file processors to accommodate the specified 183-column format for seamless synchronization with the MFSS.
