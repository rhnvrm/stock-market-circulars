## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and trading of equity shares of German Green Steel and Power Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), effective from October 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: German Green Steel and Power Limited
- Security Details: Equity shares post-issue effective October 1, 2026.
- Event: Initial Public Offering (IPO) listing on NSE.

## Regulatory Changes

- Listing of new equity shares on the Exchange platform under regulatory frameworks for IPOs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations and NSE trading guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 1, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading and liquidity for equity shares of German Green Steel and Power Limited on the NSE platform.
