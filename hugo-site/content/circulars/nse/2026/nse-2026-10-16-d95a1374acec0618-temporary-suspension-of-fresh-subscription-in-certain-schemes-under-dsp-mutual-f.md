## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, lump-sum investments (including switch-ins), and new registrations for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) in DSP Gold ETF Fund of Fund under DSP Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Temporary restriction applies to DSP Gold ETF Fund of Fund.
- Affected modes: Lump-sum subscription, switch-in, fresh SIP registrations (including modification with top-up), fresh STP-in, and IDCW TP fresh registrations.
- Applications received on or before 3:00 p.m. on October 15, 2026, will be processed; subsequent applications are liable to rejection and refund.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, STP-outs, and existing SIP/STP registrations made on or before October 15, 2026, remain unaffected.

## Regulatory Changes

- DSP Mutual Fund has issued an addendum to the SID and KIM of DSP Gold ETF Fund of Fund modifying subscription rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the suspension of fresh subscriptions and ensure no new SIP/STP registrations or lump-sum orders are placed for the affected scheme starting October 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Cut-off for prior applications: 3:00 p.m., October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding existing SIPs/STPs or looking for redemption/SWP can continue normal operations without interruption, while new investors cannot enter the scheme via fresh lump-sum or SIP/STP registrations until further notice.
