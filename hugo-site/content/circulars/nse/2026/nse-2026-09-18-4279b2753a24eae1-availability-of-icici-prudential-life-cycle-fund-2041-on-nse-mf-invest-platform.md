## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the availability of ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041 on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities effective from September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes involved: ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041 - Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A617) and ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041 - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A625).
- Category: Equity.
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure proper integration and awareness of the scheme availability on the platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update facilitating investor transactions for the specified mutual fund scheme on the NSE platform.
