## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the application of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to specific securities under the Long-Term ASM framework effective September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I: Anlon Healthcare Limited (AHCL), EKI Energy Services Limited (EKI), and Sahasra Electronic Solutions Limited (SAHASRA).
- Securities moving from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage II to Stage III: Kabra Extrusion Technik Limited (KABRAEXTRU).
- 100% margin requirement applicable on all open positions as on September 29, 2026, and new positions created from September 30, 2026 onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

Selected securities are subjected to heightened surveillance measures under the Long-Term ASM framework to curb excessive volatility and protect market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on all open and new positions for the affected securities starting from the specified effective dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ASM: September 28, 2026
- 100% Margin Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in AHCL, EKI, SAHASRA, and KABRAEXTRU will experience increased margin obligations, which may affect trading volumes and liquidity in these scrips.
