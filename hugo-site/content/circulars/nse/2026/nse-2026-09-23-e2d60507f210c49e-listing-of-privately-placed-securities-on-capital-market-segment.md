## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of privately placed securities on its capital market segment effective from September 24, 2026. The circular covers debt securities issued by Kisetsu Saison Finance (India) Private Limited and IndiaFirst Life Insurance Company Limited.

## Key Points

- **Kisetsu Saison Finance (India) Private Limited**: 7.70% 2028 Sr 02 (Symbol: 770KSFP28, ISIN: INE0DZE07077, Maturity: 21-Sep-2028).
- **IndiaFirst Life Insurance Company Limited**: 9.03% 2036 (Symbol: 903IFL36, ISIN: INE381Y08037, Maturity: 22-Sep-2036).
- Both securities are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with a market lot of 1.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The listing is pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in these securities must adhere to the designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: September 24, 2026
- **Maturity Dates**: September 21, 2028 (Kisetsu Saison) and September 22, 2036 (IndiaFirst Life)

## Impact Assessment

Provides secondary market liquidity for the newly listed privately placed debt securities.
