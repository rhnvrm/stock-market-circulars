## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of the Titanium Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund under the Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) of Tata Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Tata Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Titanium Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund (Regular and Direct Plans across IDCW Reinvestment, Growth, and IDCW Payout options)
- Facilities Available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Segment: NMF TM Segment
- Download Ref No: NSE/NMFTM/76910, Circular Ref. No: 1756/2026

### Scheme ISIN Details
- Regular Plan IDCW Reinvestment: INF277K30153 (Equity)
- Direct Plan IDCW Reinvestment: INF277K30187 (Equity)
- Regular Plan Growth: INF277K30138 (Equity)
- Direct Plan Growth: INF277K30161 (Equity)
- Regular Plan IDCW Payout: INF277K30146 (Equity)
- Direct Plan IDCW Payout: INF277K30179 (Equity)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; this is a routine operational circular regarding scheme availability on the trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants must ensure systems are updated with the correct ISINs and scheme parameters for smooth order execution on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors seamless access to transact in multiple options of the Titanium Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund through established platform facilities.
