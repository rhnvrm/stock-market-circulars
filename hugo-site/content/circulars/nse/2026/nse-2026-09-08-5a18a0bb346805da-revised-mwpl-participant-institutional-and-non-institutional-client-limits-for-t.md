## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a revision to the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) and position limits for participants, institutional clients, and non-institutional clients for the symbol TVSHLTD under the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) Scheme, effective September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: TVSHLTD
- Effective Date: September 09, 2026
- Market Wide Position Limit revised from 516,870 to 24,292,918 shares.
- Institutional Client Limit revised from 51,687 to 2,429,291 shares.
- Participant Limit revised from 51,687 to 2,429,291 shares.
- Non-Institutional Client Limit revised from 5,168 to 242,929 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial modification and update of position limits in pursuance of earlier circulars (NCL/CMPT/73999 and NCL/CMPT/76085).

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians are advised to take note of the revised limits and ensure compliance while executing trades under the SLB scheme for TVSHLTD.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Allows increased participation and higher borrowing/lending limits for TVSHLTD in the SLB segment matching the revised market metrics.
