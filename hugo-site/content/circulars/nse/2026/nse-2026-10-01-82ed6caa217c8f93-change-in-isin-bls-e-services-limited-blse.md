## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing members about the change in ISIN for BLS E-Services Limited (BLSE) resulting from a stock sub-division.

## Key Points

- Company Name: BLS E-Services Limited
- Symbol: BLSE
- New ISIN: INE0NLT01028
- Reason: Sub-Division of face value from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/-
- Effective Date / Ex-date: October 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The ISIN code for equity shares of BLS E-Services Limited is updated in the depository systems following the corporate action of stock sub-division.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure that their systems are updated with the new ISIN (INE0NLT01028) for all trades executed on and from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Ex-date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The new ISIN is applicable for all trades done on and from October 06, 2026. This is a standard administrative update associated with corporate stock splits.
