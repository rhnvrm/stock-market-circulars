## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Certain securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion or stage migration under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) framework.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. August 26, 2026, on all open positions as on August 25, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I: Cressanda Railway Solutions Limited (CRSL), Rajshree Sugars & Chemicals Limited (RAJSREESUG), Rana Sugars Limited (RANASUG), S.A.L. Steel Limited (SALSTEEL).
- Securities moving from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I to Stage II: Cambridge Technology Enterprises Limited (CTE).
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of 100% margin requirements and potential shifting of scrips to appropriate surveillance stages in accordance with the LTASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on applicable open and new positions effective August 26, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Surveillance/Margin Actions: August 26, 2026
- Circular Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in specified securities will be subject to strict margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for market participants trading these scrips.
