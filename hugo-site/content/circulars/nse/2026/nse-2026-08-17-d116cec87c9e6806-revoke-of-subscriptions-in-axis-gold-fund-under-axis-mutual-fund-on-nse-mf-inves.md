## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the resumption of lumpsum subscriptions and switch-ins for Axis Gold Fund and Axis Gold ETF under Axis Mutual Fund. The temporary restrictions introduced via notices dated June 05, 2026, and June 10, 2026, have been withdrawn with effect from August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Temporary restrictions on lump sum subscriptions in Axis Gold Fund and Axis Gold ETF are withdrawn.
- Axis Gold ETF: Subscription transactions by large investors exceeding Rs. 25 crores directly with Axis Mutual Fund are accepted starting August 18, 2026.
- Axis Gold Fund: Lump sum subscriptions and switch-ins are permitted without any restrictions effective August 18, 2026.
- All other terms and conditions of the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational update regarding scheme subscription limits.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors take note of the resumption of subscription services on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 18, 2026
- Notice Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors can resume lump sum investments and switch-ins in Axis Gold Fund and Axis Gold ETF without the previous temporary limitations.
