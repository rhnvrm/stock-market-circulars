## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and new registrations under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) for Groww Gold ETF FOF under Groww Mutual Fund, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Temporary discontinuation of fresh subscriptions, lumpsum, switch-ins, and new SIP/STP registrations in Groww Gold ETF FOF.
- Applications received up to October 15, 2026, will be processed normally.
- Existing SIPs and STPs will continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA end without changes.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and STP-outs remain fully operational.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary suspension implemented by Groww Asset Management Ltd. as per notice cum addendum no. 31/2026–2027 communicated via NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors must note the suspension of fresh subscriptions and guide investors accordingly regarding Groww Gold ETF FOF.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 16, 2026 (suspension commences).
- **Cut-off for Processing:** Applications received until October 15, 2026, will be honored.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors seeking fresh investments or new SIPs in Groww Gold ETF FOF will be restricted until further notice, while existing investors maintain normal exit/redemption and ongoing SIP processing facilities.
