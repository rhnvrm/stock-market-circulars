## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I w.e.f. October 09, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. October 12, 2026 on all open positions as on October 09, 2026 and new positions created from October 12, 2026.
- Shortlisting under the ASM framework is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned company or entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I on 12 listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements of 50% or existing margin (capped at 100%) for open positions as on October 09, 2026 and new positions created from October 12, 2026 in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date for ST-ASM Inclusion: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for the affected securities may lead to modified trading dynamics and reduced leverage for market participants trading in these scrips.
