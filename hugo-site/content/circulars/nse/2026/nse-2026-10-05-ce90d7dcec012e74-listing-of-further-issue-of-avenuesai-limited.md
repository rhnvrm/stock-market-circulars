## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issue of equity shares for AvenuesAI Limited (Symbol: CCAVENUE) effective from October 06, 2026. A total of 10,68,411 partly paid-up equity shares have been converted into fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** AvenuesAI Limited
- **Symbol:** CCAVENUE
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE483S01020 (Temporary ISIN: IN8483S01029)
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Re. 1/-
- **Corporate Action:** Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares
- **Number of Securities:** 10,68,411 shares
- **Distinctive Numbers:** From 3481469520 to 3482537930
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Pari Passu:** Yes
- **Effective Date:** October 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; processed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Equity shares allotted in dematerialized mode were credited under temporary ISIN (IN8483S01029) in accordance with SEBI circulars dated August 02, 2012, and September 11, 2012, and are now admitted to dealings under the permanent ISIN.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital structure update reflecting the conversion of partly paid-up shares to fully paid-up shares for AvenuesAI Limited, with no direct adverse market or trading impact.
