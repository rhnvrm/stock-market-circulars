## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for several schemes of Sundaram Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective August 25, 2026. These changes are implemented pursuant to SEBI circular dated February 26, 2026, on categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes.

## Key Points

- Changes affect multiple Sundaram Mutual Fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest platform.
- Effective date of the name changes is August 25, 2026.
- Modifications are in compliance with SEBI Circular No. HO/24/13/15(2)2026-IMD-RAC4/1/5764/2026 dated February 26, 2026.

### Scheme Name Changes:
- **Sundaram Ultra Short Duration Fund** $\rightarrow$ **Sundaram Ultra Short Term Fund**
- **Sundaram Low Duration Fund** $\rightarrow$ **Sundaram Ultra Short to Short Term Fund**
- **Sundaram Short Duration Fund** $\rightarrow$ **Sundaram Short Term Fund**
- **Sundaram Medium Duration Fund** $\rightarrow$ **Sundaram Medium Term Fund**
- **Sundaram Banking & PSU Fund** $\rightarrow$ **Sundaram Banking and PSU Debt Fund**
- **Sundaram Balanced Advantage Fund** $\rightarrow$ **Sundaram Dynamic Asset Allocation Fund**

## Regulatory Changes

- Complies with SEBI guidelines on categorization and rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes, superseding Clause 2.6 of the Master Circular for Mutual Funds to reinforce the "True-to-Label" principle and enhance transparency.

## Compliance Requirements

- The modifications do not constitute a change in the fundamental attributes of the respective schemes; investors need to note the revised nomenclature for future transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal, requiring updates to platform scheme descriptions and investor records to reflect the new nomenclature.
