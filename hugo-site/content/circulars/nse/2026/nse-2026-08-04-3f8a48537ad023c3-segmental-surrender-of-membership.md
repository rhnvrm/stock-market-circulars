## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has approved the request from **DB (International) Stock Brokers Ltd.** for the segmental surrender of its membership in the **Currency Derivatives & IRF (Interest Rate Futures)** segments, effective from **July 29, 2026**.

## Key Points

- **Trading Member:** DB (International) Stock Brokers Ltd.
- **SEBI Registration Number:** INZ000179035
- **Surrendered Segments:** Currency Derivatives & IRF (Interest Rate Futures)
- **Surrender Approval Date:** July 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced by this circular. It is an administrative notification of a specific member surrendering membership in selected segments.

## Compliance Requirements

- No action is required from other trading members.
- All members are requested to take note of the segmental surrender of membership by DB (International) Stock Brokers Ltd.

## Important Dates

- **Surrender Approval Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Circular Date:** August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** None. This is an isolated, member-specific administrative event and does not impact wider market operations or trading of currency derivatives and IRF.
- **Operational Impact:** DB (International) Stock Brokers Ltd. will no longer be permitted to trade in the Currency Derivatives and IRF segments on the NSE.
