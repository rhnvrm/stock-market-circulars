## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the shift of specified securities to the Trade for Trade segment as part of its periodic surveillance review to ensure market safety and safeguard investor interests.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure 1 are shifted from the rolling segment (series: EQ) to the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE) with a price band of 5% or lower.
- Effective date for the transfer is September 03, 2026 (Thursday).
- Securities shifted to Trade for Trade will not be available in the rolling segment (series: EQ/SM).
- Securities listed in Annexure 2 will continue in the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/BZ or ST/SZ).
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of securities for trading and settlement on a trade-to-trade basis based on market surveillance criteria including Price Earnings Multiple, Price Variation, and Market Capitalization.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to take adequate precautions while trading in these securities as no netting off is allowed.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity for the affected securities may be impacted due to the trade-to-trade settlement requirement and 5% price band.
