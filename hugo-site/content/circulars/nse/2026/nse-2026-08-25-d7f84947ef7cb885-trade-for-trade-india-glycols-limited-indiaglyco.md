## Summary

Pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement, National Stock Exchange has announced that India Glycols Limited (INDIAGLYCO) will be shifted to the Trade for Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Company Name: India Glycols Limited
- Ticker: INDIAGLYCO
- New Trading Segment: Trade for Trade (Series: BE)
- Effective Date: September 02, 2026
- Pre-open Session: Will be part of call auction in pre-open session as per SEBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in the security is restricted to the Trade for Trade segment pursuant to corporate restructuring under a Scheme of Arrangement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the Trade for Trade settlement requirements for INDIAGLYCO.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity in INDIAGLYCO shares may be impacted as intraday netting is not permitted in the Trade for Trade segment.
