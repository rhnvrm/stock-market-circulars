## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for specific securities. Shortlisted securities will attract a minimum 100% margin and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in ESM Stage I: Rox Hi Tech Limited (ROXHITECH) and Steel Strips Infrastructures Limited (STLSTRINF).
- 100% margin requirement effective October 09, 2026, on all open positions as on October 08, 2026, and new positions created from October 09, 2026.
- Shift from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 09, 2026.
- Consolidated list includes securities such as ACS Technologies Limited (ACSTECH) under Stage II.
- Action is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) Stage I for specified securities.
- Mandatory 100% margin on open and new positions.
- Migration of trading segment from Rolling Settlement (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade (BE/ST).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure applicable 100% margins are maintained for open positions as on October 08, 2026, and new positions from October 09, 2026.
- Trading members must account for segment shifts (EQ/SM to BE/ST) for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- October 08, 2026: Effective date for criteria applicability and open positions snapshot.
- October 09, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and shifting from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade settlement restrictions impact liquidity and trading operations for the shortlisted securities (ROXHITECH, STLSTRINF).
