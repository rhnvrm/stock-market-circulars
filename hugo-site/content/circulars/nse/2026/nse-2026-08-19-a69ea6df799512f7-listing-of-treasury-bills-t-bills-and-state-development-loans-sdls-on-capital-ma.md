## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the admission for dealing of Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on its capital market segment, effective from August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted include State Development Loans (SDLs) from Mizoram, Odisha, and Madhya Pradesh, alongside Government of India Treasury Bills (91D, 182D, and 364D).
- Designated security codes and respective lot sizes (standardized at 100) are specified for trading.
- Trading will be conducted exclusively via the designated security codes on the capital market segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adherence to the specified lot sizes and security codes for the listed instruments.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 21, 2026
- Circular Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market liquidity and trading for newly issued state development loans and treasury bills.
