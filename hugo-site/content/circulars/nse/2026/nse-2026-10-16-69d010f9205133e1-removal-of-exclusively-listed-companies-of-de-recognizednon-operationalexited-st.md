## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the removal of three exclusively listed companies of de-recognized, non-operational, or exited stock exchanges from its Dissemination Board pursuant to SEBI Circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD/DSA/CIR/P/2016/110 dated October 10, 2016.

## Key Points

- Elite Capital & Management Services Limited, Govinda Cotspin Limited, and Iggi Highway Motels Limited have been removed from the Dissemination Board.
- The removal is based on the status of these companies on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) website reflecting as 'strike off'.
- The companies will not be available on the Dissemination Board of NSE with effect from October 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Execution carried out pursuant to SEBI Circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD/DSA/CIR/P/2016/110 dated October 10, 2016, regarding companies placed on the Dissemination Board.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to take note that the specified companies are no longer available on the Dissemination Board of NSE.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: October 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market or operational impact, as the affected companies are non-operational and have been struck off by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.
