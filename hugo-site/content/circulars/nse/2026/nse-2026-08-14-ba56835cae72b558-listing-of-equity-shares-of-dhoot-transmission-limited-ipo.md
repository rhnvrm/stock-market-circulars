## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Dhoot Transmission Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The circular includes details of the share holding pattern post-issue.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Dhoot Transmission Limited
- Security: Equity Shares
- Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 82.78%
- Public Holding: 17.22%
- Total Paid-up Equity Shares: 204,548,057

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with standard listing regulations and submission of share holding pattern under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Date of Filing/Listing: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Dhoot Transmission Limited equity shares on the NSE platform, expanding investment opportunities for market participants.
