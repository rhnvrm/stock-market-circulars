## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified members regarding the communication from Nippon India Mutual Fund about the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and new Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) & Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) registrations in specific schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes affected: Nippon India Retirement Fund - Wealth Creation Scheme and Nippon India Retirement Fund - Income Generation Scheme.
- Suspension applies to fresh/additional lumpsum subscriptions, switch-ins, and new SIP/STP registrations w.e.f. October 16, 2026, until further notice.
- No restrictions on Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), Switch-out, and STP out.
- Existing SIPs and STPs registered prior to the effective date will continue to be processed without change.
- Transactions time-stamped on or before 3:00 PM on October 15, 2026, are accepted at applicable NAV.
- Restrictions do not apply to mandatory regulatory investments (e.g., employee interest alignment and mandatory AMC contributions).

## Regulatory Changes

[Not applicable - Operational investment restriction implemented by the AMC on specific scheme categories]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the subscription restrictions on the specified Nippon India Mutual Fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform and ensure no new SIP/STP or lumpsum orders are initiated for these schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Cut-off for prior orders: October 15, 2026 up to 3:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Investors are temporarily unable to make fresh investments or register new systematic plans in the specified Nippon India retirement schemes through the NSE platform, while liquidity and redemption channels remain fully operational.
