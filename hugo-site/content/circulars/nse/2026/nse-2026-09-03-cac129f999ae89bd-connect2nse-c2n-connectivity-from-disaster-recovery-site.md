## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited is conducting its operations for the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) from its Disaster Recovery (DR) Site on September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Operations for SLBS will be conducted from the DR Site on September 3 and 4, 2026.
- Members and Custodians accessing N-MASS, NCMS, and NCIS applications do not need to make any configuration changes or connection parameter updates.
- Applications will experience downtime for live operations or updates between 01:00 hrs and 08:00 hrs on September 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational continuity notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians should take note of the DR site operations schedule and application downtime.

## Important Dates

- DR Site Operations: September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026
- Application Downtime: September 05, 2026, from 01:00 hrs to 08:00 hrs

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on members as no configuration changes are required, though applications will be temporarily unavailable during the scheduled downtime on September 5, 2026.
