## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement calendar for the Mutual Fund Service System (MFSS) applicable for the month of November 2026. The circular outlines the settlement schedules for subscription, liquid subscription, and redemption orders, along with category codes and funds settlement days.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules for MFSS Subscription (Settlement Type 'S', Annexure A), Liquid Subscription (Settlement Type 'K', Annexure B), and Redemption (Settlement Type 'U', Annexure C).
- Specifies category codes for scheme categories and respective funds settlement days.
- Subscription orders funds settlement takes place depending on the fund's settlement day for the scheme category (T Day, where T is the transaction date).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational schedule notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants are requested to check the category of schemes in the scheme master to ensure correct funds settlement for subscription orders.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 05, 2026
- **Effective Period:** November 2026 settlement schedules

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification ensuring seamless clearing and settlement of mutual fund transactions through the MFSS platform for November 2026.
