## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Horizon Industrial Parks Limited (IPO) on the Capital Market segment with effect from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: HORIZONIND
- ISIN: INE685T01010
- Issue Price: Rs. 60/-
- Face Value: Rs. 10/-
- Total No. of Securities: 2,882,902,834
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: August 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange Capital Market segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.
- Respective lot sizes specified in pursuance of Regulation 2.5.5.

## Compliance Requirements

- Security will be part of pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Commencement of trading for Horizon Industrial Parks Limited equity shares on NSE.
