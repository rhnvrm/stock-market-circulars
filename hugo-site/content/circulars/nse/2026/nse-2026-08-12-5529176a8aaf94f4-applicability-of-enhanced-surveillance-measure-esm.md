## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Based on surveillance criteria, specific securities have been shortlisted for Stage I and Stage II of ESM. Securities qualifying under ESM will undergo changes in their settlement segments, price bands, and margin requirements. Additionally, consolidated lists have been updated.

## Key Points

- **Inclusion in Stage I**: Securities like 3i Infotech Limited (3IINFOLTD) and Speciality Restaurants Limited (SPECIALITY) have satisfied the criteria for ESM Stage I. They will attract a minimum 100% margin.
- **Stage II Migration**: Vipul Limited (VIPULLTD) is shortlisted to migrate from ESM Stage I to Stage II.
- **Trading Restrictions**: Securities in Stage I will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST). Stage II securities will trade under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% in the Periodic Call Auction.
- **Surveillance Nature**: The Exchange clarified that shortlisting under ESM is purely for market surveillance purposes and does not constitute adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Margin Requirements**: 100% margin on all open positions as of August 13, 2026, and new positions from August 14, 2026.
- **Segment Shifting**: Rolling Settlement segment (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST) for shortlisted Stage I securities.
- **Periodic Call Auction**: Price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction for securities shifting to Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must adjust margin collection to 100% for the affected securities from the specified dates.
- **System and Operations**: Trading systems must update trading series (from EQ/SM to BE/ST) and apply price band restrictions (2%) for Stage II securities.
- **Client Information**: Inform clients trading in these securities about the increased margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- **August 13, 2026**:
  - Implementation of ESM Stage II price band (2%) and Periodic Call Auction.
  - Calculation of open positions for 100% margin requirement.
  - Stage migration (Stage I to II, and II to I) becomes effective.
- **August 14, 2026**:
  - Shift of Stage I securities from EQ/SM to BE/ST (Trade-for-Trade) segment.
  - 100% margin requirement on all new positions.

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity Impact**: Affected securities shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment and Periodic Call Auction are likely to see a decrease in trading volume and liquidity.
- **Financial Impact**: Increased margin requirements (100%) will increase capital requirements for investors holding or establishing positions in these securities.
- **Risk Management**: Helps mitigate market volatility and speculative trading in these high-risk or highly volatile securities.
