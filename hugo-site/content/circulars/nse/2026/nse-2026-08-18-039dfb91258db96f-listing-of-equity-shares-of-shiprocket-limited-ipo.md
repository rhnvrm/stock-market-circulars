National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Shiprocket Limited under the IPO route.

- **Symbol:** SHIPROCKET
- **ISIN:** INE0FOO01011

None.

Members of the Exchange are informed about the forthcoming listing of security for their records and necessary compliance.
