## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding surveillance measures for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Race Eco Chain Limited has been included under this framework and will attract a minimum 75% margin.

## Key Points

- Race Eco Chain Limited (RACE) is included in Annexure I and attracts a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Enhanced margin requirements take effect from September 29, 2026, on all open positions as on September 28, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- No securities are excluded from the framework as per Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework (Annexure III) includes Indo Tech Transformers Limited (INDOTECH) and Race Eco Chain Limited (RACE).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures requiring minimum 75% margin on securities with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that applicable clients and positions in shortlisted securities maintain the mandated minimum 75% margin starting September 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 24, 2026
- Exclusion Effective Date: September 25, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: September 29, 2026 (on open positions as of September 28, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Stricter margin requirements increase funding and collateral obligations for trading participants holding positions in affected scrips, which may affect liquidity and price volatility.
