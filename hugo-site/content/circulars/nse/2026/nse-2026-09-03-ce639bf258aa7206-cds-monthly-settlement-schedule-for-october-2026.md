## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement schedule for Mark to Market (MTM) settlements in the Currency Derivatives Segment for the month of October 2026.

## Key Points

- Contains daily trade dates paired with corresponding settlement dates for October 2026.
- Outlines special final settlement dates for specific asset classes.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must adhere to the specified settlement dates and timelines for Mark to Market settlements in the currency derivatives segment.

## Important Dates

- First Trade Date: 01-Oct-2026 (Settlement: 05-Oct-2026)
- Final settlement for October 2026 91-Day GOI T-Bill Futures Contracts: 29-Oct-2026
- Final settlement for October 2026 Currency Futures and Options Contracts & Cash Settled Interest Rate Contracts: 30-Oct-2026
- Final settlement for October 2026 Overnight Call Rate (MIBOR) Contracts: 02-Nov-2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparent and orderly settlement operations for market participants trading in currency derivatives during October 2026.
