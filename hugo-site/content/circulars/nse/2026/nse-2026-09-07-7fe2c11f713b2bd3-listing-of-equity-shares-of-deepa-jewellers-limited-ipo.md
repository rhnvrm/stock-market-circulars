## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Deepa Jewellers Limited pursuant to its initial public offering (IPO), effective from September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: DEEPA
- Company Name: Deepa Jewellers Limited
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE1M2N01020
- Face Value: Rs. 2/-
- Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 177/-
- Total No. of Securities: 96,124,293
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: September 08, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 08, 2026
- Financial Year: April 2026 - March 2027

## Impact Assessment

Trading in equity shares of Deepa Jewellers Limited will commence on the National Stock Exchange capital market segment for members and investors.
