## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued circular ref. no. 1281/2026 notifying members about the suspension of trading in specific Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) issued by Shriram Finance Limited, effective from August 05, 2026. This action is taken due to the scheduled maturity of these debt securities.

## Key Points

- **Suspension of Trading**: Trading in the designated Non-Convertible Debentures of Shriram Finance Limited will be suspended starting August 05, 2026.
- **Securities Affected**: Three specific debentures are affected, identified by their symbols and ISINs:
  - Symbol: **931SFL26** (ISIN: **INE721A07PA8**)
  - Symbol: **97SFL26** (ISIN: **INE721A07PE0**)
  - Symbol: **SFLZC26A** (ISIN: **INE721A07PH3**)
- **Reason**: The suspension is due to the maturity of the securities.
- **Authority**: The suspension is executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory framework is introduced. This action is an enforcement of existing **Regulation 3.1.2** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A concerning the suspension of maturing securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: All trading members must update their trading systems and terminals to prevent any trading activity in the affected symbols starting August 05, 2026.
- **Clearance and Settlement**: Clearing and settlement operations for these securities will be closed post-suspension.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 04, 2026
- **Effective Date of Suspension**: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Extremely low. The maturity and subsequent suspension of NCDs are standard operational processes and do not represent any distress or unexpected events for the issuer or the market.
- **Issuer Impact**: Shriram Finance Limited's equity shares (Symbol: SHRIRAMFIN) and other active debt instruments remain unaffected by this routine suspension.
- **Operational Impact**: Minor adjustments required by brokers and exchange systems to update active security lists.
