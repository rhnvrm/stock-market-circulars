## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, including Axis Bank, CG Power and Industrial Solutions, Inox Green Energy Services, and Moschip Technologies, admitted to dealings effective from October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective date for trading: October 05, 2026.
- Axis Bank Limited: 116,086 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE238A01034).
- CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited: 18,250 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE067A01029).
- Inox Green Energy Services Limited: 18,110,473 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under QIP at an issue price of Rs. 165.65 (ISIN: INE510W01014).
- Moschip Technologies Limited: 135,409 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE935B01025).

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, securities are admitted with designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the specified securities shall be conducted using their designated codes and lot sizes as specified in the circular annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing of further shares through ESOP and QIP with no broad operational impact on the general market.
