## Summary

NSE has issued a circular reminding all Trading Members to submit their Annual Returns for the Financial Year 2025-26 on or before October 31, 2026, through the ENIT Portal. The submission includes audited financial statements, networth certificates, inventory of assets, and declarations on Politically Exposed Persons (PEP).

## Key Points

- Deadline for submission of Annual Returns for FY 2025-26 is October 31, 2026.
- Submissions must be done via the ENIT Portal (https://enit.nseindia.com/MemberPortal/).
- Required documents include Audited Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss Account (in XBRL format), shareholding details, director details, and auditor-certified Networth Certificate.
- Mandatory prerequisites before submitting Annual Returns include the submission of the Inventory of Assets as on March 31, 2026, and a declaration on Politically Exposed Persons (PEP).
- Financial statements must follow the format prescribed under the Companies Act, 2013, regardless of applicability.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of enhanced supervision guidelines requiring digital submission of audited Networth certificates and financial statements via ENIT.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must enter all details on the ENIT portal, have them verified by their auditor, and make the final submission by October 31, 2026.
- Mandatory submission of Inventory of Assets and PEP declaration prior to Annual Returns filing.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 05, 2026
- Due Date for Annual Returns & Prerequisite Submissions: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure timely completion of audits and prerequisite declarations (Inventory of Assets and PEP) to avoid disciplinary actions stipulated by the Exchange.
