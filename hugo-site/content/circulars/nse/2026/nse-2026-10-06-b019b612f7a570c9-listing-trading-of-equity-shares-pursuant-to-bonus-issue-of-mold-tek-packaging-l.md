## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and trading of 3,32,28,914 additional equity shares of Mold-Tek Packaging Limited pursuant to a bonus issue. The securities will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective from October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mold-Tek Packaging Limited
- Symbol: MOLDTKPAC
- ISIN: INE893J01029
- Record Date: October 9, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: October 12, 2026
- Number of Bonus Securities: 3,32,28,914
- Distinctive Number Range: 33228915 to 66457828

## Regulatory Changes

None. The circular pertains to standard listing of shares issued under corporate actions pursuant to NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that trades are executed using the designated security codes effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 9, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date for Listing and Trading: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Addition of bonus shares increases the total paid-up equity capital and liquidity of Mold-Tek Packaging Limited on the exchange.
