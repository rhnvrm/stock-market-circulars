## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a facility for the migration of member link(s) at the Mumbai BKC POP to Category I connectivity with an enhanced pool of IPs, following previous circulars regarding digital submission facilities for Category I connectivity.

## Key Points

- One-time migration window enabled for members to migrate existing self-service leased line connectivity availed through Mumbai BKC POP to Category I.
- Details of IP, address of termination, POP-service provider combination, and bandwidth remain unchanged post-migration.
- Exchange will manually allot child IPs against the Category I link request and issue fresh BOX IDs for parent and child IPs.
- Existing user status (Trading rights of Pro/Cli, User Limits, etc.) will get reset post-migration, requiring members to place appropriate requests before the next trading session.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of a streamlined migration process from Mumbai BKC POP to Category I connectivity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must submit applications detailing the links to be migrated, schedule the migration date, and subsequently submit activation/surrender requests via ENIT.
- Proper requests for resetting user status and trading rights must be placed prior to the commencement of the next trading session after migration.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational convenience for trading members wishing to upgrade their connectivity pool with minimal disruption through a structured one-time migration process.
