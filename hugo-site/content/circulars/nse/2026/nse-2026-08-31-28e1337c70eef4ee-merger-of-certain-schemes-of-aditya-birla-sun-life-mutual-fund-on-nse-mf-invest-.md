## Summary

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited has announced the merger of two of its mutual fund schemes into Aditya Birla Sun Life Corporate Bond Fund, as approved by the Board of Directors, Trustees, and noted by SEBI.

## Key Points

- Merging Schemes: Aditya Birla Sun Life Nifty SDL Plus PSU Bond Sep 2026 60:40 Index Fund and Aditya Birla Sun Life CRISIL-IBX AAA NBFC - HFC Index-Sep 2026 Fund.
- Surviving Scheme: Aditya Birla Sun Life Corporate Bond Fund.
- No changes in name, attributes, investment objective, or asset allocation of the Surviving Scheme post-merger.
- SEBI has noted the proposed merger vide its communication dated August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

SEBI noted the proposed merger of the schemes under communication dated August 19, 2026. All references to the merging schemes stand deleted from SID and KIM of the Fund post-merger.

## Compliance Requirements

- Unitholders of the merging schemes who agree with the merger must submit their consent between September 01, 2026, and September 30, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM).
- Unitholders who do not submit the duly filled consent form within the stated timelines will be deemed as not in agreement and will receive redemption proceeds based on applicable NAV.
- Unitholders of the Surviving Scheme opting to continue face no tax implications; however, redemption/switch during the exit option period results in capital gains/losses.

## Important Dates

- Consent Period Start Date: September 01, 2026
- Consent Period End Date: September 30, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM)
- Effective Date for Merger: After close of business hours on September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Unitholders in the merging schemes need to provide consent or face redemption. Surviving scheme unitholders experience no adverse effect or changes to scheme attributes.
