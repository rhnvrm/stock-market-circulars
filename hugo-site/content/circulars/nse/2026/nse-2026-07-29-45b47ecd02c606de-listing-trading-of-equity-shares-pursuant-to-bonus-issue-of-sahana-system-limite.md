## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the listing and trading of additional equity shares of Sahana System Limited. This is pursuant to a bonus issue by the company, admitting an additional 17,67,421 equity shares to dealings on the Capital Market segment, with trading commencing on August 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer**: Sahana System Limited
- **Symbol**: SAHANA
- **ISIN**: INE0LEX01011
- **Security Volume**: 17,67,421 equity shares
- **Distinctive Numbers**: 8837105 to 10604525
- **Trading Commencement**: August 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory framework changes. The listing follows standard procedure under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in these shares is subject to the provisions of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations.
- The newly admitted securities must be traded under the symbol 'SAHANA' and ISIN 'INE0LEX01011'.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Record Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Deemed Date of Allotment**: August 03, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. This action is company-specific, affecting only the capital structure and trading volume of Sahana System Limited without broader systemic effects.
- **Operational Impact**: Standard post-allotment listing updates for trading platforms, market makers, and custodians.
