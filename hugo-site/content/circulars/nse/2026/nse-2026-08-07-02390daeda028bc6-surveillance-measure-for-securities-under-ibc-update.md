## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE), in coordination with SEBI and other exchanges during a Joint Surveillance Meeting, has updated the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The revised framework introduces specific Stage 0 surveillance measures and defines clear rules for exiting the IBC framework based on Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) thresholds and court stay orders.

## Key Points

- **Framework Update**: Revision of the extant IBC surveillance framework following a Joint Surveillance Meeting of Exchanges and SEBI on August 7, 2026.
- **Stage 0 Criteria**: Applicable upon receipt of corporate announcements or disclosures of IBC from the company. It triggers gross settlement, a price band of ±5%, and a 100% margin requirement, effective from T+2 day.
- **Framework Interaction**: The revised IBC framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures imposed by the exchange.
- **Stay Orders**: In case of a court or NCLAT order directing specific actions on IBC surveillance, the exchange will abide by and execute court directives.

## Regulatory Changes

The amended framework modifies the transition and exit criteria for companies undergoing the IBC process:
- **Stage 0 Surveillance Action**: Immediate application of gross settlement, ±5% price band, and 100% margin on the T+2 day after corporate announcement.
- **Exit Conditions**: Securities can move out of the IBC framework post-attainment of Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) >= 25%, following the completion of 3 subsequent calendar months from the date of attaining the threshold.
- **NPH Verification**: Compliance is assessed via quarterly shareholding patterns or ad-hoc shareholding updates, whichever is reported earlier.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Market Participants**: Must adjust risk management systems to accommodate gross settlement, ±5% price bands, and 100% margin requirements for shortlisted IBC securities under Stage 0.
- **Listed Companies under IBC**: Ensure timely submission of quarterly or ad-hoc shareholding patterns to document compliance with the >= 25% Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) threshold for eventual exit from the framework.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 07, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 10, 2026 (for the amended framework application and publication of the shortlisted scrips)

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading & Liquidity**: Shortlisted securities under Stage 0 will face reduced liquidity due to the gross settlement (trade-for-trade), strict ±5% price bands, and 100% margin requirements.
- **Risk Management**: Trading members must monitor the updated shortlist of IBC-affected securities to ensure appropriate leverage and margin controls are applied.
