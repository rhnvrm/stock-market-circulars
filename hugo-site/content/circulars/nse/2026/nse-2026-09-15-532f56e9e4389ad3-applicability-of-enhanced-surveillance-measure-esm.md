## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the criteria have been shortlisted for inclusion under Stage I of the ESM framework, attracting a minimum 100% margin and shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I are included under Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) Stage - I w.e.f. September 16, 2026.
- Attracts a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 17, 2026, on all open positions as on September 16, 2026, and new positions created from September 17, 2026.
- Affected securities shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 17, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ESM framework measures including 100% margin requirements and shifting of specified securities to the trade-for-trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure appropriate margin collection (minimum 100%) on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities and account for their movement to the trade-for-trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ESM Stage - I inclusion / price band / movement: September 16, 2026 / September 17, 2026
- Margin applicability effective date: September 17, 2026 (on open positions as on September 16, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will face higher margin obligations and settlement restrictions, impacting liquidity and short-term volatility for the affected scrips.
