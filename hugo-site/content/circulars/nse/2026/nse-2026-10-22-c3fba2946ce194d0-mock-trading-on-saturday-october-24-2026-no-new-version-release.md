## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026. There is no new version release associated with this mock session.

## Key Points

- Mock Trading Date: October 24, 2026.
- Segment: Capital Market Segment.
- Trading Session-1 from Primary Site and Trading Session-2 from Disaster Recovery (DR) Site will be conducted with specific timings.
- NEAT+ version details: NEAT+ 7.9.0 (Download path: /cmftp/common/NEATPlus790).
- Reminder for NNF members regarding discontinuation of the existing encryption mechanism with old interactive port (10827) from December 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational mock trading procedure.

## Compliance Requirements

Members using NNF are requested to refer to circular NSE/MSD/76883 regarding the upcoming discontinuation of the existing encryption mechanism with old interactive port (10827) from December 07, 2026, and migrate accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 24, 2026
- NNF Encryption Migration Deadline / Old Port Discontinuation: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact on live trading days. Participants can test their systems, connectivity parameters, and DR switchover mechanisms during the mock session.
