## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding measures in respect of companies with high Promoter as well as non-Promoter 'Encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities satisfying the criteria are subjected to a minimum 75% margin.

## Key Points

- **Shortlisted Securities (Annexure I):** Eureka Forbes Limited (EUREKAFORB) and Indo Tech Transformers Limited (INDOTECH) have satisfied the criteria and will attract a minimum 75% margin.
- **Exclusions (Annexure II):** Nil securities are eligible to move out from the framework.
- **Consolidated List (Annexure III):** Includes BPL, Brahmaputra Infrastructure, Eureka Forbes, Indo Tech Transformers, and Race Eco Chain.
- **Margin Application:** Minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments on all open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions created from October 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, concerning high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 75% margin requirement on equity and equity derivative segments for the affected securities starting October 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Exclusion Effective Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Margin Effective Date:** October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants dealing in the shortlisted securities must maintain adequate margins (75%) across equity and derivatives segments, impacting liquidity and position sizing for these scrips.
