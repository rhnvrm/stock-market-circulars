## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives (F&O) segment on Saturday, September 19, 2026. No new software version release is scheduled for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading is scheduled for Saturday, September 19, 2026, from the DR Site.
- Pre-Open market session starts at 11:15 hours and Normal Market opens at 11:30 hours.
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 must be used explicitly for the revised Pre-Open framework.
- Existing NEAT+ version 7.8.9 will be discontinued on October 10, 2026.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised Pre-Open framework requiring NEAT+ version 7.9.0.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, September 21, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 19, 2026
- NEAT+ 7.8.9 Discontinuation Date: October 10, 2026
- Live Environment Resumption: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Zero financial impact as trades are simulated. Ensures broker and member system readiness for live market operations.
