## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the enablement of the Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The transaction window for the scheme is open to members from August 07, 2026, to August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Availability:** Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund NFO of Axis Mutual Fund will be available for transactions starting August 07, 2026, and ending on August 21, 2026, at 11:59 pm.
- **Scheme Details:**
  - Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund Direct Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1580, Category: Equity)
  - Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund Regular Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1572, Category: Equity)
- **Allotment Date:** The units of the schemes will be allotted on August 27, 2026.
- **Payment Cutoff:** Members must ensure clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 27, 2026, by 12:30 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no specific regulatory changes introduced in this circular; it serves as an operational announcement for a standard New Fund Offer (NFO) listing under the existing mutual fund platform rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members utilizing the platform must ensure customer transactions are completed within the designated operational dates (August 07, 2026 – August 21, 2026).
- Members must ensure that clear funds, via any permitted payment mode, are successfully received in the designated Clearing Corporation account prior to the deadline on August 27, 2026, at 12:30 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Transaction Start Date:** August 07, 2026
- **NFO Transaction End Date:** August 21, 2026 (until 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Clear Funds Cutoff:** August 27, 2026 (until 12:30 pm)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low impact. This is a routine operational enablement of an Axis Mutual Fund scheme on the NSE's mutual fund platform, allowing trading members to facilitate subscription transactions for clients.
