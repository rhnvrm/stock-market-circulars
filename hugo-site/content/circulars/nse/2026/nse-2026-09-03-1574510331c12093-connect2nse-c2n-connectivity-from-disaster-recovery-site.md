## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited will conduct its operations for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) Segment from its Disaster Recovery (DR) Site on September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026. Members accessing N-MASS, NCMS, and NCIS applications are not required to change any configuration settings.

## Key Points

- Operations for the Electronic Gold Receipt Segment will run from the Disaster Recovery (DR) Site on September 03 and September 04, 2026.
- No changes in configuration settings or connection parameters are required for members accessing N-MASS, NCMS, and NCIS applications.
- N-MASS, NCMS, and NCIS applications will be unavailable for live operations or updates between 01:00 hrs and 08:00 hrs on September 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians are advised to take note of the DR site operations and application unavailability windows.

## Important Dates

- **DR Site Operations:** September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026
- **Application Unavailability:** September 05, 2026 (01:00 hrs. to 08:00 hrs.)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact as no configuration updates are necessary, with a brief scheduled downtime for applications on September 05, 2026.
