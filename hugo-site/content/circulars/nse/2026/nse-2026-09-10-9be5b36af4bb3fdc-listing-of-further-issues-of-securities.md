## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing of further issues of securities for several companies under the SME Emerge segment with effect from September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Companies involved: Agarwal Toughened Glass India Limited, Beacon Trusteeship Limited, Rulka Electricals Limited, and Viaz Tyres Limited.
- Securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment – SME Emerge effective September 11, 2026.
- Includes equity shares issued under preferential allotment and ESOP.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notification pursuant to trading regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to designated security codes and market lot sizes specified in the circular annexure for trading.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for market participants trading in SME Emerge segment securities.
