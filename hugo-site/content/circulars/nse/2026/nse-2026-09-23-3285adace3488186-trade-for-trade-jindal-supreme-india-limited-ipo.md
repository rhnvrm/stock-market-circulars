## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Jindal Supreme (India) Limited (IPO) from the trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ), effective from October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Jindal Supreme (India) Limited
- Symbol: JSIPL
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Reference to prior circular: NSE/CML/76473 dated September 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, eligible securities are shifted to the rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the transfer of the security from trade for trade segment to rolling segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Allows normal rolling settlement for the equity shares of Jindal Supreme (India) Limited, facilitating regular trading operations for market participants.
