## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the admission of privately placed securities to dealings on its capital market segment with effect from October 06, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 06, 2026.
- Issuing entities include Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, Kaleidofin Capital Private Limited, Vikabh Securities Pvt Ltd, and Apollo Tyres Limited.
- All listed securities carry a market lot size of 1 and are identified by their respective designated codes and ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of new privately placed debt/securities to trading on the capital market segment under NSE Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must execute trades using the designated security codes and adhere to the specified market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026
- Maturity Dates: October 05, 2027 (Vikabh Securities), October 05, 2028 (Kaleidofin Capital), September 28, 2029 (Apollo Tyres), and October 25, 2029 (Motilal Oswal Financial Services).

## Impact Assessment

- Enables secondary market trading and liquidity for the newly listed privately placed securities.
