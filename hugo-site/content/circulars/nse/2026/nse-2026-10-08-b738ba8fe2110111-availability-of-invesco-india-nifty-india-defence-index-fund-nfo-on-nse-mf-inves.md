## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of the Invesco India Nifty India Defence Index Fund NFO along with ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Scheme availability effective from October 9, 2026.
- Covers Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- Associated ISINs range from INF205KA1CD5 to INF205KA1CI4.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should ensure correct scheme mapping and ISIN usage when executing transactions on the NSE MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 8, 2026
- Effective Date: October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members platform access to subscribe and transact in the defence sector index fund.
