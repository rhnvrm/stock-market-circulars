## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced a periodic review of securities under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework in coordination with other exchanges. Eligible securities will move to lower stages of surveillance or exit GSM effective October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Periodic review conducted under the Graded Surveillance Measure framework.
- Five securities identified for relaxation or removal from surveillance stages.
- Next Mediaworks Limited moves from Stage I to Stage 0 (removal).
- Blue Chip India Limited and Morarjee Textiles Limited move from Stage III to Stage II.
- CLC Industries Limited and Ortel Communications Limited move from Stage IV to Stage III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic relaxation of surveillance actions applied to eligible securities as per standard GSM provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the revised GSM stages and apply appropriate trading restrictions and margin requirements effective from October 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date for GSM Revision: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Eased trading restrictions and margin norms for eligible securities moving to lower surveillance stages, improving liquidity and operational clarity for investors.
