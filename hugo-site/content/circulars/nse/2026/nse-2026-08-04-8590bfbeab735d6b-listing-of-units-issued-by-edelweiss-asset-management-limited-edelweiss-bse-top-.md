## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the listing and commencement of trading of units issued by Edelweiss Asset Management Limited under two Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) on the Capital Market segment, effective from August 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Schemes Listed**:
  1. **Edelweiss BSE Top 10 Bank ETF** (Symbol: `EBANK10`, ISIN: `INF754K01XH9`) - 12,322,326 units allotted.
  2. **Edelweiss BSE LargeMid (60:40) Stable Dividend 50 ETF** (Symbol: `ELMDIV`, ISIN: `INF754K01XI7`) - 1,706,710 units allotted.
- **Trading Attributes**: Market lot size is 1. The face value and paid-up value per unit are Rs. 10.
- **Issue Prices**: Rs. 16.87 for EBANK10 and Rs. 36.61 for ELMDIV.
- **Market Makers**: Parwati Capital Market Private Limited, Irage Broking Services LLP, and Kanjolachana Finserve Private Limited are designated as Market Makers to facilitate liquidity.
- **Registrar and Share Transfer Agent**: Kfin Technologies Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes. This listing is processed under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Trading systems must be updated with the newly assigned symbols and ISINs. Member operations must align with the market lot size of 1 unit.
- **Asset Management Company (AMC)**: Edelweiss Asset Management Limited and its Trustee company must adhere to SEBI guidelines and NSE requirements regarding mutual fund disclosures and continuous listing obligations.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment**: July 31, 2026
- **Circular Date**: August 04, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. This is a standard procedural listing for newly launched ETFs and does not represent systemic market or regulatory changes.
- **Liquidity Facilitation**: The presence of three designated market makers should ensure sufficient liquidity and competitive spreads for investors wishing to trade these units from the listing date.
