## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the temporary suspension of trading in units of UTI Annual Interval Fund - I due to the interval scheme structure, effective from September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes affected: UTI Annual Interval Fund - I (Symbols: UTFDAIPIGI, UTFDAIPIPI; ISINs: INF789F01VW8, INF789F01VU2).
- Reason for suspension: Interval Scheme.
- Suspension Date: September 07, 2026.
- Closing hours of trading: September 04, 2026.
- Trading revocation is subject to AMC confirming continuation of the schemes post closure of the Specified Transaction Period (STP).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Asset Management Companies must confirm continuation of the schemes post closure of the Specified Transaction Period to revoke trading suspension.

## Important Dates

- Closing hours of trading: September 04, 2026
- Suspension date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified units will remain temporarily suspended until AMC confirmation following the Specified Transaction Period.
