## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switch-in transactions, fresh Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), and Systematic Transfer Plans (STPs) for certain schemes of Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from the close of business hours on September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes: Aditya Birla Sun Life International Equity Fund, Aditya Birla Sun Life Global Emerging Opportunities Fund, and Aditya Birla Sun Life Global Excellence Equity Fund of Fund.
- Lumpsum subscriptions, switch-ins, and fresh registrations under SIP and STP are disabled on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and outgoing STPs remain unrestricted.
- Existing SIPs and STPs registered prior to the effective date will continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA end without any changes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Notice-cum-Addendum No. 55/2026 issued by Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited regarding the suspension of fresh subscriptions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must note the discontinuation of fresh subscriptions and switch-ins for the specified global/international schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: Close of business hours on Thursday, September 24, 2026.
- Circular Date: September 25, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding or planning fresh investments in the specified international/global equity schemes of Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund will be unable to make fresh subscriptions or register new SIPs/STPs on the platform.
