## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to certain securities satisfying specified criteria for inclusion under Long Term ASM.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. August 27, 2026 on all open positions as on August 26, 2026 and new positions created from August 27, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. August 27, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of 100% margin requirements for securities under Long-Term ASM Stage-I.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin is maintained on applicable open and new positions effective August 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 27, 2026
- Circular Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for listed securities may impact trading liquidity and positions for market participants.
