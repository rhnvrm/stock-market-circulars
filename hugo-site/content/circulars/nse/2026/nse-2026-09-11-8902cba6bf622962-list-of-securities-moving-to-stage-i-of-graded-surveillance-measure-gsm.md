## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that Digjam Limited is moving to Stage I of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) effective September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security affected: Digjam Limited (Symbol: DIGJAMLMTD, ISIN: INE731U01028).
- Effective Date: September 15, 2026.
- Applicable Surveillance Action: 100% margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.

## Regulatory Changes

The security is subjected to Stage I of the GSM framework due to ongoing surveillance reviews.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to take adequate precautions while trading in the aforementioned security and adhere to the specified margin and price band requirements.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in Digjam Limited will be restricted with a 100% margin requirement and a 5% price band, affecting liquidity and price movement for market participants.
