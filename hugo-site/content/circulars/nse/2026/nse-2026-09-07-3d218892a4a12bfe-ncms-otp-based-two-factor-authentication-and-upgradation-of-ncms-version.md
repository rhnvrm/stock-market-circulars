## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the implementation of OTP-based Two Factor Authentication (2FA) for user logins in NCMS-FO (covering both admin and sub-users). Additionally, a new EXE version 4.2.5 will be made available for download starting September 12, 2026, with 2FA becoming effective from September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- **New Version Release:** NCMS-FO version 4.2.5 will be available from September 12, 2026, downloadable from the member extranet/portal (`faoftp/faocommon/NCMSSetup_425`).
- **Two Factor Authentication (2FA):** OTP-based 2FA implementation for NCMS-FO logins goes live on September 15, 2026.
- **First-Time Registration:** Members must register with their mobile number and email ID upon their first login after password validation.
- **OTP Mechanism:** OTPs will be sent to the registered mobile number and email ID. Invalid OTP entries can be regenerated after 30 seconds.
- **Account Locking:** Entering an invalid OTP 4 times will result in the user ID being disabled, requiring members to contact NSE Clearing Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Enhancement of security measures for user access in the Futures & Options segment through mandatory Two Factor Authentication.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must upgrade to NCMS version 4.2.5 and complete the initial registration process with valid mobile and email details during the first login to comply with the new 2FA mandate.

## Important Dates

- **Version Release Date:** September 12, 2026
- **Effective Date of 2FA:** September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational workflows will require user adaptation to the new 2FA login process and version upgrade. Failure to comply or multiple incorrect OTP entries will disable user access, requiring intervention from NSE Clearing Ltd support.
