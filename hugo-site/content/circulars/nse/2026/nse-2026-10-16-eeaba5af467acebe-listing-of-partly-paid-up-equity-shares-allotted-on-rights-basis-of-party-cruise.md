## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and commencement of trading for partly paid-up equity shares of Party Cruisers Limited allotted on a rights basis, effective October 21, 2026, under the SME EMERGE segment.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Party Cruisers Limited
- Symbol: PARTYPP
- Series: E1
- ISIN: IN906ZX01014 (Temporary ISIN)
- Face Value: Rs. 10 per share (Paid-up Value: Rs. 2.50 per share)
- Number of Securities: 1,876,676 equity shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 11989914 to 13866589
- Market Lot: 2,000 shares
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of partly paid-up equity shares to dealings on the NSE SME EMERGE segment pursuant to Regulations 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with security codes, lot sizes, and temporary ISIN guidelines as per SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing and Trading: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables shareholders of Party Cruisers Limited to trade their partly paid-up rights shares on the NSE SME EMERGE segment with specified market lots and temporary ISIN identification.
