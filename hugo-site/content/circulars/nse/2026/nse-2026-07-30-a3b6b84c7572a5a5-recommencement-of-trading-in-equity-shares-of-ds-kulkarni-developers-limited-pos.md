## Summary

Trading in the equity shares of DS Kulkarni Developers Limited (DSKULKARNI) is set to recommence post capital reduction. This capital restructuring was implemented pursuant to the Resolution Plan approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) vide its order dated June 23, 2023. As part of this process, the company submitted its post-capital-reduction Shareholding Pattern under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, detailing the restructured ownership as of June 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Trading Recommencement**: Equity shares of DS Kulkarni Developers Limited will resume trading following a capital restructuring process.
- **NCLT Approved Resolution**: The capital reduction is a key action taken under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) resolution plan approved on June 23, 2023.
- **Share Capital Structure**: Post-restructuring, the company has 10,000,000 (1 Crore) fully paid-up equity shares.
- **Promoter Shareholding**: 7 promoter entities hold 9,500,000 shares (95.00% of the equity), all of which are 100% locked-in and dematerialized.
- **Public Shareholding**: Public shareholding stands at 500,000 shares (5.00% of the equity) across 4,575 shareholders, all in dematerialized form.
- **No Encumbrances**: There are no promoter shares pledged or otherwise encumbered post-restructuring.

## Regulatory Changes

- Execution of capital reduction under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) and relevant NCLT orders, overriding standard capital change regulations.
- Submission of Shareholding Pattern in compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Disclosure Filings**: The company must maintain continuous compliance under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, including timely submission of periodic shareholding reports.
- **Lock-in Provisions**: Monitor and ensure adherence to the lock-in conditions applied to the 9,500,000 promoter-held shares.
- **Dematerialization**: Maintain 100% dematerialized status of all issued equity shares.

## Important Dates

- **June 23, 2023**: Approval date of the Resolution Plan by the Hon'ble NCLT.
- **June 30, 2026**: Date of the post-capital-reduction Shareholding Pattern filing.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity**: Resumption of trading provides liquidity and exit/entry options for investors after a prolonged period of restructuring.
- **Ownership Control**: Promoters hold a highly concentrated 95.00% stake, leaving a very small public float of 5.00%, which may result in high price volatility upon trading recommencement.
