## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed Government Securities on the Debt Market and Capital Market Segments, effective from September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: GOI Loan 6.53% 2031
- ISIN Code: IN0020260074
- Symbol: 653GS2031
- Coupon Rate: 6.53%
- Maturity Date: 07-Sep-2031
- Lot Size: 1

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that transactions in these securities are conducted using the designated security code and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 09, 2026
- Maturity Date: September 07, 2031

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing providing secondary market liquidity for the specified Government Security.
