## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the restriction in trading of securities of SPEL Semiconductor Limited under the Trade for Trade category, based on communication received from BSE.

## Key Points

- SPEL Semiconductor Limited (Symbol: SPELS) is under the Permitted to Trade category at NSE.
- Trading in the securities of the company will be suspended in the normal segment (EQ Series) w.e.f. September 10, 2026.
- W.e.f. September 15, 2026, trading will be allowed only on the first trading day of every week in the trade for trade category.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities moved to Trade for Trade category with weekly trading restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to the revised trading schedule and category restrictions for SPELS.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 10, 2026
- Trade for Trade Category Restriction Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading frequency for SPEL Semiconductor Limited will be restricted to once a week in the trade for trade segment.
