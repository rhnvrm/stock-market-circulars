## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular notifying market participants about the change in scheme names for HDFC Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026. This change is pursuant to SEBI's revised norms on categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes.

## Key Points

- Effective date for the scheme name changes: August 26, 2026.
- Applies to multiple HDFC Mutual Fund schemes listed on the NSE MF Invest platform.
- Changes align with SEBI's circular on scheme categorization and rationalization.
- Examples of name modifications include changes between 'HDFC Large and Mid Cap Fund' and 'HDFC Large & Mid Cap Fund', and 'HDFC Hybrid Debt Fund' to 'HDFC Conservative Hybrid Fund'.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SEBI's revised norms for "Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes" as noted in Clause 3.7 of the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must note the revised scheme names for all future transactions, reporting, and reference purposes on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Circular Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal, serving primarily as an administrative nomenclature update to maintain consistency across regulatory and trading platforms.
