## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the resumption of subscription transactions in Tata Gold Exchange Traded Fund (Tata Gold ETF) and Tata Gold ETF Fund of Fund (Tata Gold ETF FOF) under TATA Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Previous restrictions, including the Rs. 10 lakh per PAN per calendar month limit on lumpsum purchases/switch-ins and direct large investor restrictions (investments of Rs. 25 crores and above), have been removed with effect from August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Subscription transactions by large investors (investing Rs. 25 crores and above) directly with Tata Mutual Fund in Tata Gold ETF are resumed.
- Applications for lumpsum purchases and switch-ins to the Tata Gold ETF FOF will now be accepted without any investment limit restrictions.
- Replaces previous temporary limits introduced in June 2026 due to normalized market conditions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of the Rs. 10 lakh per PAN per calendar month restriction on lumpsum purchases and switch-ins for Tata Gold ETF FOF.
- Resumption of direct large investor subscriptions of Rs. 25 crores and above in Tata Gold ETF.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and investors can process transactions in the mentioned schemes without previous restriction caps.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 21, 2026
- Circular Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and distributors can resume unconstrained investments and switch-ins in Tata Gold ETF and Tata Gold ETF FOF through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
