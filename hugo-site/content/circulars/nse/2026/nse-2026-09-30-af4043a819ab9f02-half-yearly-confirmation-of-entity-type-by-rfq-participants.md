## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has mandated all existing Request for Quote (RFQ) participants to confirm their entity type for the half-year ending September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mandatory half-yearly confirmation for all existing RFQ participants.
- Confirmation must be performed via admin login on the CBRICS portal.
- Submission window runs from October 01, 2026, to October 31, 2026.
- Non-submission within the prescribed timeline results in revocation of RFQ access.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of mandatory half-yearly validation and confirmation of entity types for accurate regulatory records on the RFQ platform.

## Compliance Requirements

RFQ participant admins must log into the CBRICS portal between October 1, 2026, and October 31, 2026, review the pop-up, and confirm the entity type. If incorrect, submit an amendment request with relevant supporting documents (note that submitting an amendment revokes RFQ access until approved by the Exchange).

## Important Dates

- Start Date: October 01, 2026
- Deadline: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures accurate validation and compliance of entity types across the RFQ platform while posing potential temporary access restrictions for participants failing to meet the deadline.
