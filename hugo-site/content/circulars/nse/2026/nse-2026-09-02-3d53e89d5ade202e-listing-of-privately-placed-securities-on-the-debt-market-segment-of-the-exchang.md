## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has notified the admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers and debt issues) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted include commercial papers and other debt issues from various entities.
- Effective date of listing: September 02, 2026.
- Issuing entities include Axis Securities Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI), Aditya Birla Capital Limited, IDBI Capital Markets & Securities Limited, and Jio Credit Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Standard listing compliance for privately placed debt instruments.

## Important Dates

- Allotment dates range from August 31, 2026 to September 01, 2026.
- Effective listing date: September 02, 2026.
- Redemption dates range across various maturities up to 2029.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational notice facilitating secondary market trading for the listed debt instruments and commercial papers.
