## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in equity shares of Ashapura Intimates Fashion Limited (Symbol: AIFL) on account of a capital reduction pursuant to an NCLT Order dated June 23, 2025. The record date for the capital reduction is set for October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ashapura Intimates Fashion Limited
- Symbol: AIFL
- Reason for Suspension: Capital Reduction pursuant to NCLT Order dated June 23, 2025
- Record Date: October 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Action taken in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the trading suspension in the security as per the scheduled record date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Record Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in equity shares of Ashapura Intimates Fashion Limited is suspended reflecting the corporate action execution under the NCLT order.
