## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has approved the request of trading member Gogia Capital Growth Limited for the voluntary surrender of its membership in the Commodity Derivatives segment.

## Key Points

- **Trading Member Name**: Gogia Capital Growth Limited
- **SEBI Registration Number**: INZ000202733
- **Surrendered Segment**: Commodity Derivatives
- **Surrender Approval Date**: August 11, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

There are no systemic regulatory or policy changes introduced by this circular. This notification is an administrative approval of a voluntary segmental membership exit.

## Compliance Requirements

Other members and market participants are requested to take note of the segmental surrender of membership by Gogia Capital Growth Limited. No specific compliance actions are required from other trading members.

## Important Dates

- **Surrender Approval Date**: August 11, 2026
- **Circular Issue Date**: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Extremely low. The surrender of membership in the Commodity Derivatives segment by a single entity does not affect market liquidity, trading systems, or other market operations.
- **Operational Impact**: Gogia Capital Growth Limited will cease to operate as a trading member in the NSE Commodity Derivatives segment.
