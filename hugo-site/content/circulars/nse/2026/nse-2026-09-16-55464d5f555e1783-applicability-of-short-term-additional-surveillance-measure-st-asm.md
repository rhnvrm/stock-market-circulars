## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for specified securities. The surveillance actions include margin requirements capped at 50% or 100% depending on the stage, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Anlon Healthcare Limited (AHCL), Sunshine Pictures Limited (SUNSHINE), and VTM Limited (VTMLTD).
- Securities moving from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II: Skytech Infinite Platform Limited (SKYTECH).
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage I shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. September 18, 2026.
- Applicable margin rate for Stage II shall be 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. September 18, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measures (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II provisions on open positions and new positions created from the effective date.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that appropriate margin rates (50% or 100%) are levied on all open positions as on September 17, 2026, and new positions created from September 18, 2026, for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 16, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance / Margin Applicability: September 18, 2026 (on open positions as on September 17, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in the affected securities (AHCL, SUNSHINE, VTMLTD, SKYTECH) will experience increased margin obligations, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in those specific counters.
