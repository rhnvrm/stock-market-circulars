## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of Lakshya Overnight Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective September 25, 2026. Members can facilitate ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities for the scheme.

## Key Points

- Lakshya Overnight Fund made available on NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Supported facilities include ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Covered schemes: Lakshya Overnight Fund Direct Plan Growth (ISIN: INF2ZPR01014) and Lakshya Overnight Fund Regular Plan Growth (ISIN: INF2ZPR01022).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational notification regarding mutual fund scheme availability on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members facilitating mutual fund transactions must ensure systems are updated to support the new schemes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with additional overnight fund investment options on the NSE mutual fund platform.
