## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding surveillance measures for companies exhibiting high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrances, in accordance with Regulation 28(3) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011. This circular, which continues the framework set in previous circular NSE/SURV/51189, details the newly shortlisted securities, exclusions, and the consolidated active list under this framework, along with applicable margin adjustments.

## Key Points

- **Inclusion under Measure:** Securities meeting the high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance criteria under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011, are subject to surveillance restrictions.
- **Margin Requirements:** Shortlisted securities attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- **New Shortlisting:** Indo Tech Transformers Limited (INDOTECH) is newly shortlisted under this framework.
- **Exclusions:** No securities have been excluded from the framework in this cycle (Nil exclusion list).
- **Consolidated List:** A total of five securities are active under this surveillance framework, including newly added INDOTECH, plus AQYLON, STEELXIND, THYROCARE, and ELPROINTL.
- **Regulatory Clarification:** Shortlisting under this measure is a surveillance mechanism and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Framework Applicability:** Implemented in accordance with Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.
- **Margin Constraint:** A minimum margin of 75% is mandated for all open positions in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- **Complementary Measure:** This surveillance measure operates in conjunction with any other prevailing exchange-imposed surveillance controls.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Collection:** Trading members must collect and maintain a minimum 75% margin for transactions involving INDOTECH in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- **Existing Position Adjustment:** The 75% margin requirement applies to all open positions of INDOTECH as of the close of trading on August 13, 2026, and to all new positions created thereafter.
- **System Updates:** Trading systems and risk management processes must be updated to enforce the high margin limit for the shortlisted security within the specified timelines.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular:** August 11, 2026
- **Exclusion Effective Date:** August 12, 2026
- **New Shortlisting Inherent Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Open Positions Margin Evaluation Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Margin Enforcement Effective Date:** August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading and Leverage Impact:** The high 75% margin requirement for INDOTECH will significantly curb trading leverage, potentially leading to lower daily volumes and increased transaction costs for traders.
- **Liquidity Mitigation:** The ongoing inclusion of companies like AQYLON, STEELXIND, THYROCARE, and ELPROINTL keeps their trading activity constrained under the high margin framework.
- **Risk Containment:** This surveillance framework serves to manage systemic risk arising from high promoter share pledging (encumbrance), reducing volatility in the broader market.
