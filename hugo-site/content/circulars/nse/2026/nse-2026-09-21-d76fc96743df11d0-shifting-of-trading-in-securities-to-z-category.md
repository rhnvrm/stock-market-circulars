## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the shifting of trading in securities of certain companies to the 'Z' Category (‘BZ’ / ‘SZ’ Series) due to non-compliance with Regulation 76 (Submission of Reconciliation of Share Capital Audit Report) of SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018 for two consecutive quarters (March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2026).

## Key Points

- Osia Hyper Retail Limited (OSIAHYPER) and Wardwizard Innovations & Mobility Ltd (WARDINMOBI) have been identified as non-compliant for two consecutive quarters and will be transferred to ‘Z’ Series effective October 05, 2026, unless they comply on or before September 29, 2026.
- Several other companies which are already trading in the ‘Z’ Category pursuant to various Exchange Circulars remain subject to trade for trade settlement for the said non-compliance.
- Trades in the aforesaid securities executed in the Z category will be settled on a Trade for Trade basis.
- The circular takes effect from October 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of trading restrictions by transferring non-compliant securities to the 'Z' category pursuant to SEBI Master Circular dated July 11, 2023, and updated on January 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies must comply with Regulation 76 (Submission of Reconciliation of Share Capital Audit Report) of SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018 for the quarters ended March 31, 2026, and June 30, 2026, on or before September 29, 2026, to avoid transfer to the 'Z' category.

## Important Dates

- **Deadline for Compliance:** September 29, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Transferring securities to the 'Z' category and settling trades on a Trade for Trade basis severely impacts stock liquidity and investor trading capabilities for the affected companies.
