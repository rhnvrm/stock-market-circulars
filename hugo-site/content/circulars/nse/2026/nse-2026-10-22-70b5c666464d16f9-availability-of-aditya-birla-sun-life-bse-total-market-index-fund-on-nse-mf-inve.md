## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the availability of the Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market Index Fund-Regular Growth (ISIN: INF209KC1761)
- **Scheme Name:** Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market Index Fund-Direct Growth (ISIN: INF209KC1753)
- **Category:** Equity
- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Facilities Available:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP (Systematic Investment Plan), STP (Systematic Transfer Plan), and SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan).

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members must note the ISINs and scheme details for trading and operational processing on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to the Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market Index Fund on the NSE MF platform with full operational support.
