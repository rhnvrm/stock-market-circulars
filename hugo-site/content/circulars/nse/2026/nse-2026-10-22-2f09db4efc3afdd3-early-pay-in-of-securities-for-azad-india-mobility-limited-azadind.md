## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued operational guidelines regarding early pay-in (EPI) of securities for Azad India Mobility Limited (AZADIND) on account of its face value split (sub-division) from Rs 10/- per share to Rs 2/- per share. Members are instructed to execute early pay-in instructions in the old/existing ISIN (INE566M01017) without adjusting for the conversion for specified settlement dates around the record date.

## Key Points

- Company: Azad India Mobility Limited (AZADIND)
- Corporate Action: Face Value Split from Rs 10/- to Rs 2/- per share.
- Existing/Old ISIN: INE566M01017
- Record Date / Ex-Date: October 23, 2026.
- Applicable EPI Dates & Settlements: October 23, 2026 (Settlement No. 2026200) and October 26, 2026 (Settlement No. 2026200 & 2026201).

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory use of existing/old ISIN for early pay-in instructions on specified settlement dates without adjustment for the stock split.
- Subsequent pay-in and early pay-in instructions must be executed in the new ISIN after the record date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members/custodians doing EPI on October 23, 2026 and October 26, 2026 must execute early pay-in in the depository systems using the old/existing ISIN (INE566M01017).
- In case of early pay-in through block mechanism, client-wise early pay-in allocation file should not be uploaded.
- Custodians providing early pay-in through the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details as per the actual traded quantity to pass benefits to clients.
- Market participants/custodians must execute pay-in/early pay-in instructions in the New ISIN following the record date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 23, 2026
- Ex-Date: October 23, 2026
- EPI Date (Settlement 2026200): October 23, 2026
- EPI Date (Settlement 2026200 & 2026201): October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Clearing members, custodians, and market participants must correctly handle ISIN selection during early pay-in for AZADIND transactions around the record date to ensure smooth settlement and avoid clearing discrepancies.
