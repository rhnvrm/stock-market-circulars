## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long Term ASM framework. Effective October 14, 2026, specific securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Long-Term ASM Stage I.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long Term ASM Framework Stage I: Dev Accelerator Limited (DEVX), Manilam Industries India Limited (MANILAM), and Premier Polyfilm Limited (PREMIERPOL).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective October 16, 2026, on all open positions as on October 15, 2026, and new positions created from October 16, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of securities in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I.
- Imposition of 100% margin requirements on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must maintain 100% margin on open and new positions for the affected securities starting October 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM inclusion: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin requirement: October 16, 2026 (on open positions as of October 15, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements to 100% for DEVX, MANILAM, and PREMIERPOL will impact liquidity and trading volume for these securities in the near term.
