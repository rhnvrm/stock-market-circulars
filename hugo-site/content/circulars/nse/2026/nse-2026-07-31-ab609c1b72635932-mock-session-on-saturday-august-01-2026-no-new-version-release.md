## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, August 01, 2026. This session will include testing trading operations from the Primary site, switching over to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site, and a subsequent live re-login session. No new software version is being released for this session.

## Key Points

- **Mock Session Date**: Saturday, August 01, 2026.
- **No New Version**: The NEAT version remains at 1.5.5, with no new software release.
- **Disaster Recovery (DR) Test**: The session will involve a switchover from the Primary site to the DR site during Session-2.
- **Order Purge**: All outstanding orders will be cleared before each mock session. Non-NEAT Frontend (NNF) users must manually clear orders in their systems.
- **Financial Obligations**: No financial pay-in or pay-out obligations will result from trades executed during the mock session.
- **System Availability**: Connect2NSE and the Extranet facility will be unavailable from 14:30 hours until the end of the mock session.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes introduced in this circular. The session is conducted under existing guidelines including SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 and NSE circular NSE/MSD/46441.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Participation**: All members enabled for trading in the Commodity Derivatives segment are expected to participate using all trading software.
- **Re-login**: Members must re-login to the live environment between 14:00 and 15:00 hours on August 01, 2026, to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, August 03, 2026.
- **UCC/PAN Validity**: Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN details uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed. Note that UCC validation will be skipped during contingency time for order entry.
- **Manual Order Purge**: Members using NNF software must manually clear any outstanding orders from their systems as outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Mock Session Date**: August 01, 2026
  - **Session-1 (Primary Site)**: 07:45 to 10:00 hours
  - **Session-2 (DR Site)**: 10:30 to 11:55 hours
  - **Live Re-login Window**: August 01, 2026, from 14:00 to 15:00 hours
- **Next Live Trading Day**: Monday, August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: None. Mock trading sessions are non-disruptive and carry no financial settlement implications.
- **Operational Impact**: Low to medium. Members must allocate technical resources on a Saturday to complete the mock trading and ensure their systems can successfully switch over to the DR site and reconnect to the live environment prior to the Monday market open.
