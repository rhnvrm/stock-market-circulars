## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of the Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund NFO under the Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) category by Sundaram Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window runs from October 21, 2026, to November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** Sundaram Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund (Regular and Direct options across Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout)
- **NFO Period:** October 21, 2026 to November 04, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** November 06, 2026
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform (NMF TM Segment)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 04, 2026, up to 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** October 21, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** November 04, 2026 (03:00 pm for fund realization)
- **Allotment Date:** November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification facilitating member participation in the new mutual fund scheme offering on the NSE platform.
