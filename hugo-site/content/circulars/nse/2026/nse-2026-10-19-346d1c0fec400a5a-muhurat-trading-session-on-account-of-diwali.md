## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a special live trading session (Muhurat Trading) on account of Diwali on Sunday, November 08, 2026, detailing specific timings for pre-open, normal market, and block deal sessions.

## Key Points

- Special live trading session scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026 on account of Diwali Muhurat trading.
- Pre-open session timings: 14:05 to 14:13 hours (with system-driven random closure in the last 1 minute).
- Normal market session timings: 14:20 to 15:30 hours.
- Block Deal Session timings: 14:35 to 14:50 hours.
- Trade Modification cut-off time: 14:20 to 15:40 hours.
- All trades executed in this Diwali Muhurat trading session shall result in settlement obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a scheduled operational notification for festive trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure that trading systems and operational workflows align with the notified Muhurat trading schedule and settlement obligations.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 19, 2026
- Trading Date (Diwali Muhurat): November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates festive trading participation for market participants with standard settlement obligations applicable to all executed trades.
