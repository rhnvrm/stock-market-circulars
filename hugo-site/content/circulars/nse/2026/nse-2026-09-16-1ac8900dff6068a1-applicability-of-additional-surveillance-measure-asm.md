## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long-Term ASM framework. Specific securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion and will be subject to applicable surveillance actions effective September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I include ESDS Software Solution Limited and Steel Strips Infrastructures Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 21, 2026, on all open positions as on September 18, 2026, and new positions created from September 21, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework provisions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements applicable to open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 16, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Framework / Inclusion: September 17, 2026
- Open Positions Date: September 18, 2026
- Applicable Margin Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements (100%) for affected securities (ESDS and STLSTRINF), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for these scrips.
