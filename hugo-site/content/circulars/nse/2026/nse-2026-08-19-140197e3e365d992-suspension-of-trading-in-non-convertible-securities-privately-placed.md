## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) for two entities, SMFG India Home Finance Company Limited and Small Industries Development Bank of India, effective August 20, 2026, pursuant to redemption.

## Key Points

- Securities affected: Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed)
- SMFG India Home Finance Company Limited (ISIN: INE213W07269) - Suspension Date: 20-August-2026, Reason: Redemption
- Small Industries Development Bank of India (ISIN: INE556F08KI9) - Suspension Date: 20-August-2026, Reason: Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

None. Action taken pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

No specific compliance required by market participants other than noting the suspension of trading for the specified ISINs.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 19, 2026
- Suspension Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard procedure following redemption of debt securities; no wider market impact.
