## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited will conduct operations for the Capital Market Segment from its Disaster Recovery (DR) Site on September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026. Members and custodians are informed that no configuration changes are needed for C2N applications.

## Key Points

- Operations for the Capital Market Segment will run from the Disaster Recovery (DR) Site on September 03 and September 04, 2026.
- No changes in configuration settings or connection parameters are required for members/custodians accessing N-MASS, NCMS, and NCIS applications.
- N-MASS, NCMS, and NCIS applications will be unavailable for live operations or updates from 01:00 hrs to 08:00 hrs on September 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians are advised to take note of the temporary unavailability of specific applications during the specified downtime window.

## Important Dates

- DR Site Operations: September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026.
- Application Downtime: September 05, 2026, from 01:00 hrs to 08:00 hrs.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on operations as no configuration changes are required, and the scheduled downtime occurs during non-standard live operation hours.
