## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Non-Convertible Debentures) of Vision Infra Equipment Solutions Limited on the capital market segment starting September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vision Infra Equipment Solutions Limited
- Securities admitted:
  - Symbol: 1150VIES30, Series: N0, ISIN: INE0TR007022, Maturity: 27-Aug-2030, Coupon: 11.5%
  - Symbol: 1150VIES29, Series: N0, ISIN: INE0TR007014, Maturity: 20-Nov-2029, Coupon: 11.5%
- Market Lot: 1 for both securities.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine issuance governed under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify securities only by their designated codes and adhere to the specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital market listing expanding available debt instruments for trading.
