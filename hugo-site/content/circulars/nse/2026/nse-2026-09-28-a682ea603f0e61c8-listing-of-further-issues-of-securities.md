## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of equity shares for various companies including Aditya Birla Capital, Abans Financial Services, Arvind Fashions, AU Small Finance Bank, and Aye Finance, effective from September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Further issues of equity shares admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment.
- Shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) by respective companies.
- Trading will take place using designated security codes and lot sizes as specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2026
- Allotment Dates: Between September 10, 2026, and September 24, 2026 (varies by company).

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion via ESOP allotments with negligible immediate market impact.
