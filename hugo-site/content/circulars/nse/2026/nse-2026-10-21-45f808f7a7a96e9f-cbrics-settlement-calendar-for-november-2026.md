## Summary

NSE Clearing Ltd. has issued the settlement calendar for CBRICS (Corporate Bond Segment) for November 2026. Participants and custodians are required to transfer securities using the 'On-market' settlement mode based on specified NSDL/CDSL transfer dates.

## Key Points

- Market Type: CORPORATE BOND
- Clearing Member Name: NSE Clearing Ltd.
- CM-BP ID: IN568177
- Covers 19 settlement dates spanning from November 2, 2026, to November 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reminder that previously used DP details (IN001002-10000617) are not considered for settlement on the CBRICS platform effective September 3, 2019.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians must transfer securities for CBRICS settlement using the 'On-market' mode in accordance with the specified settlement calendar and member details.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: 21 October, 2026
- Settlement Period: November 2, 2026 to November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth and timely settlement operations for corporate bond transactions on the CBRICS platform for November 2026.
