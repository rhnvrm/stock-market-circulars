## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has approved the listing and admission of units of Mirae Asset Nifty200 Momentum 30 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 50:50 ETF on its Capital Market segment. The units, issued by Mirae Asset Investment Managers (India) Private Limited, will be admitted to dealings with effect from July 31, 2026, under the trading symbol HYBRIDETF.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Mirae Asset Investment Managers (India) Private Limited (AMC) / Mirae Asset Trustee Company Private Limited (Trustee).
- **Scheme Name:** Mirae Asset Nifty200 Momentum 30 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 50:50 ETF (under Mirae Asset Mutual Fund).
- **Symbol:** HYBRIDETF
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INF769K01RJ5
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Paid-Up Value:** Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Allotment Date:** July 27, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1 unit
- **Market Makers:** Multi-member panel including Mirae Asset Capital Markets, Kanjalochana Finserve, East India Securities, Parwati Capital Markets, Vaibhav Stock & Derivatives Broking, IRage Broking Services, Junomoneta Finsol, Motilal Oswal Financial Services, Obsidian Investment, Eureka Stock & Shares Broking Services, and Cholamandalam Securities.
- **Registrar & Transfer Agent:** KFin Technologies Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no changes to existing regulatory frameworks. The admission is executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure their front-office, risk management, and back-office systems are updated with the security code (HYBRIDETF), ISIN (INF769K01RJ5), and lot size (1) to facilitate trading from the effective date. Retail investors can trade the ETF units in the secondary market through registered stockbrokers.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** July 27, 2026
- **Circular Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine listing of a new ETF, expanding investment options in the passive hybrid-G-sec category.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Limited to market makers fulfilling liquidity obligations and brokerage/clearing systems updating their security master databases.
