## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Manika Plastech Limited pursuant to its IPO. The securities will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: MANIKA
- Company Name: Manika Plastech Limited
- Series: BE (to be subsequently shifted to 'EQ' as per SEBI circulars)
- ISIN: INE0KWF01020
- Face Value and Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 43/-
- Total No. of Securities: 116,511,627
- Market Lot: 1
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and lot sizes under Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Securities shall be part of pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per SEBI circular nos. CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012. Trading will be in Series 'BE' initially before shifting to Series 'EQ'.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026
- Lock-in periods vary across tranches (e.g., October 16, 2026; December 15, 2026; March 20, 2027; September 20, 2027; September 20, 2029; with certain shares free upon listing).

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Manika Plastech Limited shares on the NSE Capital Market segment, providing liquidity to investors subject to applicable lock-in restrictions and trading series controls.
