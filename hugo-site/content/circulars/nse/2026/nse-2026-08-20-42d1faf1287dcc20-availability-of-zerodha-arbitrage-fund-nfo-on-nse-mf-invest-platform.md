## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of the Zerodha Arbitrage Fund NFO of Zerodha Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities starting August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Zerodha Arbitrage Fund Direct Growth
- ISIN: INF0R8F01232
- Category: Equity
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members can facilitate transactions for clients through the NSE MF Invest Platform in accordance with existing operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides members and investors access to trade and invest in the Zerodha Arbitrage Fund via the NSE MF platform.
