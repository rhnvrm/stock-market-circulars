## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, October 10, 2026. The session will test primary and disaster recovery (DR) site operations and introduce the new NEAT+ version 1.0.2.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 10, 2026, comprising two trading sessions from Primary and DR Sites.
- Release of NEAT+ version 1.0.2 available for download from the NSE Extranet (/egftp/egcommon/NEATPlus102) starting October 09, 2026, at 18:00 hrs.
- Version migration timeline: Version 1.0.1 will be discontinued on November 14, 2026 (mock).
- Quantity Freeze Report for the EGR segment will be applicable during the mock and in LIVE from October 12, 2026 onwards.
- Trades executed during the mock session will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes; procedural circular for system testing and software version upgrade.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the EGR segment must download and install NEAT+ version 1.0.2, review the specified login parameters, and ensure readiness for the mock trading session.

## Important Dates

- NEAT+ v1.0.2 Release Date: October 09, 2026
- Mock Trading Date: October 10, 2026
- Quantity Freeze Report LIVE Date: October 12, 2026
- Version 1.0.1 Discontinuation Date: November 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact; operational preparedness exercise for trading members in the EGR segment with zero financial obligation.
