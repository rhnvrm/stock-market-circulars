## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions under the Income Distribution Cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) option in certain schemes of UTI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension applies to Lumpsum subscription including switch-In and fresh registration under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) & Systematic Transfer Plan (STP).
- No restrictions on Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), Switch-out, and STP-out.
- Existing SIP and STP registrations will continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA end without any change.
- Effective date of suspension: September 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Discontinuation of fresh subscriptions in IDCW option for schemes including UTI Short Term Fund and UTI Corporate Bond Fund as per UTI Mutual Fund addendum No. 38/2026-27.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the discontinuation of fresh investments in the specified IDCW option schemes through lump sum and new SIP/STP modes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact on general market participants; affects only investors attempting fresh subscriptions or new SIP/STP registrations in the specified UTI Mutual Fund IDCW schemes.
