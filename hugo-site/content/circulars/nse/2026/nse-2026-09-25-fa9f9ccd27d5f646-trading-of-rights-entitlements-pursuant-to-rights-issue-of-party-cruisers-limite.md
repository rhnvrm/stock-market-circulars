## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has notified the admission to dealings and trading schedule for Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Party Cruisers Limited.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Party Cruisers Limited
- **Symbol:** PARTY-RE
- **Series:** ST
- **ISIN:** INE06ZX20015
- **Number of Rights Entitlements:** 1,997,940
- **Trading Period:** September 29, 2026, to October 08, 2026
- **Suspension Date:** October 09, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 166
- **Segment:** Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat (SME EMERGE)

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs, highlighting the specific ISIN and clarifying that REs are distinct from ordinary shares. Contract notes must include a prominent disclaimer explaining that purchasing REs grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before the closing date, after which unexercised REs will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- **Trading Commencement Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Trading Renunciation Closure Date:** October 08, 2026
- **Suspension Date:** October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and transparent price discovery for shareholders and investors participating or renouncing their rights in the Party Cruisers Limited Rights Issue.
