## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment for Tata Capital Limited and Rare Asset Reconstruction Limited, effective from September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective September 10, 2026.
- Trading will be conducted using designated security codes and specified market lot sizes.
- Included securities:
  - Tata Capital Limited: TACA 8.05% 2029 Sr E (Symbol: 805TACA29, ISIN: INE976I07DH2, Maturity: 07-Sep-2029, Coupon: 8.05%)
  - Rare Asset Reconstruction Limited: RARL 16% 2029 Sr VI (Symbol: 16RARL29, ISIN: INE03UZ07049, Maturity: 31-Aug-2029, Coupon: 16%)

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are identified by the designated security codes and executed in the specified market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing notice facilitating secondary market trading for the specified privately placed debt securities.
