## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares of SPR Auto Technologies Limited (trading symbol: SHRIPISTON) issued under Qualified Institutions Placement (QIP). These securities are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SPR Auto Technologies Limited
- Symbol: SHRIPISTON
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE526E01018
- Issue Price: Rs. 4280
- Number of Securities: 2,336,448 equity shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 44399825 to 46736272
- Date of Allotment: 20-Aug-2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Equity shares allotted in dematerialized mode have been credited under temporary ISIN in accordance with applicable SEBI circulars regarding activation of ISIN for additional issues.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 20, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion via QIP for the company with no direct adverse market impact.
