## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for certain securities based on predefined market surveillance criteria, effective from August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I: Kamanwala Housing Construction Limited, Quicktouch Technologies Limited, and Superior Industrial Enterprises Limited.
- Securities moving from Stage-I to Stage-II: Raghav Productivity Enhancers Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. August 25, 2026, on all open positions as on August 24, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities satisfying criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. August 25, 2026.
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin requirements are maintained on open positions and new positions for applicable securities starting August 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ASM inclusion/movement: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin requirement: August 25, 2026 (on open positions as on August 24, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and potential trading segment shifts may impact liquidity and trading operations for the shortlisted securities.
