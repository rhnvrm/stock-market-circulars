## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the revision of the allotment date for the Nippon India Income Plus Arbitrage Omni Fund of Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform, following communication from Nippon India Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- The allotment date for Nippon India Income Plus Arbitrage Omni Fund of Fund NFO has been advanced from September 4, 2026, to September 3, 2026.
- Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account by September 01, 2026, 03:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure timely fund transfers into the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before the stipulated deadline.

## Important Dates

- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** September 01, 2026, 03:00 PM
- **Revised Allotment Date:** September 03, 2026
- **Previous Allotment Date:** September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for investors and members participating in the specific NFO subscription.
