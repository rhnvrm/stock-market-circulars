## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has admitted multiple privately placed commercial papers and other debt securities to trading on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange, effective August 10, 2026, in accordance with Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Admissions on Debt Market Segment**: Fresh issues of commercial papers and debt securities, as well as a re-issue of commercial papers, have been admitted to dealings on the exchange.
- **Issuing Companies**: Prominent corporate issuers include Godrej Agrovet Limited, Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited, National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD), Kisetsu Saison Finance, Godrej Consumer Products Limited, Nu Vista Limited, Hero Housing Finance Limited, Lloyds Metals And Energy Limited, and L&T Finance Limited.
- **Maturity & Coupon Details**: Short-term commercial papers feature maturities spanning from September to November 2026. The debt listing of Lloyds Metals And Energy Limited includes a long-term coupon rate of 9.02% with maturity in August 2036.
- **Instrument Identifications**: Detailed identifiers (such as ISIN, designated security code, series, issue size, face value, and issue price) are assigned to each listed instrument.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes are introduced. This is an operational listing action conducted under established exchange guidelines (Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations).

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance action is required from corporate entities or individual investors. Trading participants and institutional players wishing to execute deals in these instruments must update their back-office systems with the new ISIN and security identifier codes.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date**: August 7, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date**: August 10, 2026
- **Maturity Dates (CPs)**:
  - Godrej Agrovet Limited CP: September 7, 2026
  - L&T Finance Limited CP (Re-issue): August 25, 2026
  - Godrej Consumer Products Limited / Nu Vista Limited CP: November 5, 2026
  - Tata Capital Housing / NABARD / Kisetsu Saison / Hero Housing CP: November 6, 2026
- **Maturity Date (Debt Security)**:
  - Lloyds Metals And Energy Limited 9.02% Debt: August 6, 2036

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Liquidity Impact**: Standard operational listing of new corporate issuances. It facilitates direct market access and trading liquidity for primary market instruments, allowing qualified buyers to transact on the secondary exchange market.
- **Operational Impact**: Systems integration of newly admitted ISINs (including INE850D14VQ9, INE033L14PF0, INE261F14PY0, INE0DZE14438, INE102D14CI2, INE973U14318, INE800X14507, INE281B07021, and INE498L14FV3) is required for clearing and settlement.
