## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch and availability of the ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031 New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Scheme available for transaction: August 26, 2026 to September 09, 2026 (till 11:30 pm).
- Schemes included:
  1. ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031 - Regular Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A591, Category: Equity, Allotment Date: 15.09.2026)
  2. ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031 - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A609, Category: Equity, Allotment Date: 15.09.2026)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 11, 2026 till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 26, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 09, 2026 (11:30 PM)
- Allotment Date: September 15, 2026
- Clear Funds Submission Deadline: September 11, 2026 (12:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for trading members participating in the NSE Mutual Fund platform for the ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031 NFO.
