## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has scheduled a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, August 08, 2026. This routine testing event will run from both the Primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites to verify system performance and connectivity without introducing any new software version release.

## Key Points

- **Two Sessions**: Scheduled trading from the Primary site in the morning and from the DR site in the afternoon.
- **Software Details**: NEAT+ EGR Version 1.0.0 will continue to be used; no new version release is required.
- **Disaster Recovery (DR) Switchover**: Tests operational continuity during a system transition to the DR site.
- **No Financial Impact**: Transactions executed during this session are for mock purposes only, with no fund pay-in or pay-out obligations.
- **Downtime Details**: Connect2NSE, Member Portal, and Extranet applications (including APIs) will be offline for certain durations during the afternoon session.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory or policy changes associated with this mock trading circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Participation**: All members are encouraged to participate in the mock trading session to ensure smooth operation of their trading systems and verification of parameters.
- **Re-login Test**: Members must perform a live re-login between 16:30 hours and 17:00 hours on August 08, 2026, to confirm readiness for regular trading on Monday.
- **Access Details**: Default password for mock trading logins is `Neat@EG1`.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Session**: Saturday, August 08, 2026
  - **Session 1 (Primary Site)**: Pre-open starts at 10:45; Normal Market open at 11:00; Normal Market close at 13:30.
  - **Session 2 (DR Site)**: Pre-open starts at 14:15; Normal Market open at 14:30; Normal Market close at 15:30.
  - **Live Re-login Time**: August 08, 2026, from 16:30 to 17:00.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Infrastructure**: Validates the switchover resilience of the trading systems to the Disaster Recovery site, ensuring business continuity.
- **Participant Operational Impact**: Minor schedule interruption on a Saturday with no financial risk or exposure, serving as routine validation of member connectivity.
