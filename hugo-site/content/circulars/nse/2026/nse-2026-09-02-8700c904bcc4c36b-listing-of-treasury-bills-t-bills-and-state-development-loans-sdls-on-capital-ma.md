## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on its capital market segment, effective from September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted include 91D, 182D, and 364D Treasury Bills along with various State Development Loans (SDLs).
- Trading will take place using designated security codes and specified lot sizes pursuant to NSE Trading Regulations.
- Effective date for the commencement of trading is September 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure orders are placed using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 04, 2026
- Maturity Dates ranging from 2038 to 2056 for SDLs and late 2026 to 2027 for T-bills.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine market operations providing liquidity and trading access for government securities and state development loans.
