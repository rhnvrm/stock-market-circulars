## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of Veegaland Developers Limited's securities from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) following SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Security affected: Veegaland Developers Limited (Symbol: VEEGALAND).
- Transfer from trade for trade segment (series: BE) to rolling segment (Series: EQ).
- Reference made to previous exchange circular no. NSE/CML/76393 dated September 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of IPO shares from trade-for-trade settlement to regular rolling settlement pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members need to note the change in segment and settlement series for VEEGALAND.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 05, 2026
- Circular Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal trading in the rolling segment for Veegaland Developers Limited shares.
