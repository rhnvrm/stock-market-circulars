## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has introduced a digital representation and submission mechanism for appeal requests regarding penalty review decisions. Members can now submit appeals, track statuses, and upload supporting documents directly through the NCL Inspection Portal for portal-initiated requests such as short allocation, short margin reporting, client collateral segregation, and margin violations.

## Key Points

- Introduction of digital representation for penalty review appeal requests on the NCL Member Portal.
- Applies to portal-initiated actions including short allocation, short margin reporting, client collateral segregation, and margin violations.
- Appeals must be filed within 45 calendar days from the date of intimation of the Member Committee's decision.
- Mandatory declaration and supporting document upload (single file, max 10MB, formats: PDF, Excel, ZIP, JPEG, JPG).
- Appeal panel decisions will be communicated directly through the portal.

## Regulatory Changes

Digitization of the penalty appeal process, moving away from offline submissions to a streamlined workflow on the NCL Inspection Portal.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must access the portal via Inspection Portal -> Statement Upload -> Penalties at NCL -> Submission for Penalty Review Request.
- For cases with status 'Rejected- Rationalization' or 'Rejected- No Rationalization', click 'Raise Appeal'.
- Upload valid supporting documents conforming to size and format restrictions and complete the mandatory declaration before submission.

## Important Dates

- Effective with immediate effect (Circular dated October 15, 2026).
- Appeal filing timeline: Within 45 calendar days from the date of intimation.

## Impact Assessment

Enhances operational transparency and efficiency for clearing members and custodians by streamlining penalty appeal filings, document management, and status tracking.
