## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the transfer of trading in securities of non-compliant companies to the 'Z' Category ('BZ' series). Astron Paper & Board Mill Limited has been identified as non-compliant with Regulation 33 (Submission of Financial Results) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (December 31, 2025, and March 31, 2026). If the company fails to comply on or before August 05, 2026, its trading will be shifted to the 'Z' Series ("BZ") starting August 10, 2026. Additionally, 25 other companies already trading in the 'Z' Category have been identified as eligible for transfer due to the same non-compliance under Regulation 33.

## Key Points

- **Non-Compliance:** Astron Paper & Board Mill Limited (Symbol: ASTRON) failed to submit its financial results for two consecutive quarters ending December 31, 2025, and March 31, 2026, in violation of Regulation 33.
- **Shift to 'Z' Category:** If compliance is not met by August 05, 2026, trading in ASTRON securities will transfer to the 'Z' Series ('BZ') starting August 10, 2026.
- **Trade-for-Trade Basis:** All trades in the 'Z' category for the listed securities will be settled on a Trade for Trade basis.
- **Existing 'Z' Category Companies:** 25 other companies are already in the 'Z' category due to previous non-compliance and continue to be non-compliant under Regulation 33 for the same quarters.

## Regulatory Changes

- **SEBI LODR Regulation 33:** Relates to the mandatory submission of financial results by listed companies.
- **SEBI Master Circular:** Pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026, consecutive non-compliance for two quarters leads to shifting of securities to the 'Z' category.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Astron Paper & Board Mill Limited** must submit its outstanding financial results for the quarters ending December 31, 2025, and March 31, 2026, on or before August 05, 2026, to avoid being shifted to the 'Z' Series.
- **Already Shifted Companies** must address their non-compliance under Regulation 33 to initiate the process of moving out of the 'Z' category.

## Important Dates

- **July 28, 2026:** Date of issuance of the NSE circular.
- **August 05, 2026:** Deadline for Astron Paper & Board Mill Limited to submit financial results.
- **August 10, 2026:** Effective date of shifting trading to the 'Z' Category and commencement of Trade for Trade settlement.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Liquidity Impact:** Shifting to the 'Z' Series and Trade for Trade settlement significantly restricts liquidity, prevents intraday netting, and increases margin requirements, which negatively impacts trading volumes and investor interest in these stocks.
- **Regulatory Impact:** Demonstrates strict enforcement of listing regulations (Regulation 33) by the NSE and SEBI, protecting investor interest through transparency.
