## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) for several companies, effective September 15, 2026, due to redemption or call option exercises.

## Key Points

- Suspension affects multiple non-convertible securities across various companies including Bhilangana Hydro Power Limited, DME Development Limited, Oriental Nagpur Betul Highway Limited, Bank Of India, Star Health And Allied Insurance Company Limited, and REC Limited.
- Total of 10 ISINs suspended from trading.
- Reason for suspension is redemption or redemption for exercising call options.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the suspension dates and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified non-convertible securities will be halted on NSE debt segment from the effective date.
