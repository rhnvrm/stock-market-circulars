## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Capital Market segment on Saturday, September 19, 2026. The circular details schedule timings for various trading windows from the DR site and provides instructions regarding software versions, including the introduction of NEAT+ 7.9.0 and the upcoming discontinuation of version 7.8.9.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, September 19, 2026, in the Capital Market segment.
- Timings specified for normal market, pre-open, block deal windows, special pre-open session, and call auction illiquid sessions.
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 is introduced explicitly for CAS with download path `/cmftp/common/NEATPlus790`.
- Existing NEAT+ version 7.8.9 will be discontinued on October 10, 2026 (mock).
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; procedural circular regarding mock trading and software version upgrades.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to participate in the mock trading session, update to NEAT+ version 7.9.0 where applicable, and ensure valid UCC/PAN upload before cutoff.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** September 19, 2026
- **NEAT+ 7.8.9 Discontinuation Date:** October 10, 2026 (mock)

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness for trading members through testing of trading infrastructure and software upgrades without financial risk.
