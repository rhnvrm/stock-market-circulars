## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the Carnelian Small Cap Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) by Carnelian Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The NFO transaction window is open from October 16, 2026, to October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Carnelian Small Cap Fund (Regular and Direct plans for IDCW Reinvest, IDCW Payout, and Growth).
- NFO Period: October 16, 2026, to October 30, 2026 (till 11:45 PM).
- Category: Equity.
- Allotment Date: November 5, 2026.
- Designated Clearing Corporation funds clearance deadline: November 04, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that clear funds through any supported payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 04, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: October 16, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 30, 2026 (11:45 PM)
- Funds Realization Deadline: November 04, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscription and trading of Carnelian Small Cap Fund NFO units for members and investors through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
