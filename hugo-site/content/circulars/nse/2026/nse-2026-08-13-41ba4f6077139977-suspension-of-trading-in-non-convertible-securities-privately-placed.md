## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) for multiple entities effective from August 14, 2026, pursuant to redemption.

## Key Points

- Total of 6 securities suspended from trading on NSE.
- Suspension Date: August 14, 2026.
- Reason: Redemption of securities.
- Affected Issuers: Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, Export Import Bank of India, Power Finance Corporation Limited, Small Industries Development Bank of India, and Aseem Infrastructure Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must take note of the suspension of trading in the specified ISINs.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: 14-Aug-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational procedure following the redemption of privately placed debt securities; no direct equity market impact.
