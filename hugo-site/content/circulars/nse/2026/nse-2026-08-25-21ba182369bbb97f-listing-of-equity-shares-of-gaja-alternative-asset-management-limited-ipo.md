## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of equity shares of Gaja Alternative Asset Management Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The equity shares will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: GAJA
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE18UN01038
- Face Value/Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 160/-
- Total No. of Securities: 14,10,10,230
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the securities will be subject to National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A and pre-open session guidelines for IPOs.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note the designated security codes and lot sizes for trading. Lock-in details and distinctive numbers are specified in Annexure I of the circular.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Gaja Alternative Asset Management Limited shares are now available for trading on the NSE Capital Market segment, providing liquidity to investors and expanding the exchange's listed securities profile.
