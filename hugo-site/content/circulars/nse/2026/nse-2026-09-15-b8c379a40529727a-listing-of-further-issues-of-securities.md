## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for six companies, effective from September 16, 2026. The issuances include shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and Rights Issue.

## Key Points

- **Aptus Value Housing Finance India Limited**: 12,500 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE852O01025).
- **ICICI Bank Limited**: 336,265 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE090A01021).
- **Meesho Limited**: 1,352,253 equity shares of Re. 1/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE0VDM01015).
- **Ratnaveer Precision Engineering Limited**: 12,499,669 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under Rights Issue at an issue price of Rs. 264/- (ISIN: INE05CZ01011).
- **S.J.S. Enterprises Limited**: 222,875 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE284S01014).
- **Wipro Limited**: 264,143 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE075A01022).

## Regulatory Changes

None. The circular is issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

All designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure must be used for trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion for the respective companies through ESOP and Rights Issue exercises with no adverse market impact.
