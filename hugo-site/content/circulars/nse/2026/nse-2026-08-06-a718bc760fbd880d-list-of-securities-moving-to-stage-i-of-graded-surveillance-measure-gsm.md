## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular informing market participants that **Ortel Communications Limited (ORTEL)** will be moved to **Stage I of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM)** framework, effective from **August 07, 2026**.

## Key Points

- **Security Affected:** Ortel Communications Limited (Symbol: ORTEL, ISIN: INE849L01019).
- **Surveillance Action:** Movement to Stage I of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.
- **Trading Restrictions:** Trading in ORTEL will be subject to an applicable margin rate of 100% and a price band of 5% or lower, as applicable.
- **Precautionary Advice:** Members are requested to take adequate precaution while trading in the security.

## Regulatory Changes

- Ortel Communications Limited is placed under Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) Stage I, in continuation of previous surveillance circulars starting from February 23, 2017.
- Under Stage I actions, the applicable margin rate is set to 100% and the price band is limited to 5% or lower.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Precautions:** Members must exercise adequate precaution and implement system-level checks for orders in ORTEL to align with Stage I restrictions.
- **Margin & Price Bands:** Ensure 100% margin requirements and price band controls of 5% or lower are applied to trading activities in ORTEL starting August 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Security-specific Impact:** The 100% margin requirement and 5% price limit are expected to restrict price volatility and potentially reduce trading volumes for ORTEL.
- **Market Impact:** No broader market or systemic impact is anticipated, as this is a standard surveillance measure applied to a single security.
