## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited, effective from September 23, 2026, pursuant to the redemption of the securities.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited
- **ISIN:** INE246R07517
- **Suspension Date:** 23-Sep-2026
- **Reason for Suspension:** Redemption
- **Regulation:** Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the suspension of trading in the aforementioned securities.

## Important Dates

- **Suspension Date:** September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operation reflecting the completion of the security's lifecycle through redemption.
