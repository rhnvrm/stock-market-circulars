## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Effective September 01/02, 2026, specific securities have been shifted between ESM stages, and others have been excluded from the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 02, 2026, on open positions as on September 01, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 01, 2026.
- MIC Electronics Limited (MICEL) has moved from Stage I to Stage II.
- Owais Metal And Mineral Processing Limited (OWAIS) has been excluded due to inclusion in IBC Stage 1.

## Regulatory Changes

Shifting of applicable securities from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) and imposition of 100% margins and stricter price bands as per ESM guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements and trading segment shifts for the affected securities starting from the effective dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II shift and exclusions: September 01, 2026
- Effective Date for margin requirements and segment shifts: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience increased margin requirements and movement to the Trade-for-Trade segment, potentially impacting liquidity and trading mechanisms.
