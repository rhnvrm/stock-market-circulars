## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular detailing the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). The circular outlines securities shortlisted for inclusion in ST-ASM Stage I, movement from Stage I to Stage II, and exclusion from the ASM framework effective October 01, 2026, with surveillance actions taking effect from October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in ST-ASM Stage I: Manas Polymers and Energies Limited (MPEL) and Syncom Formulations (India) Limited (SYNCOMF).
- Securities moved from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II: Visaman Global Sales Limited (VISAMAN).
- Securities excluded from ASM Framework: ABS Marine Services Limited (ABSMARINE).
- Applicable margin rate: 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) for Stage I, and 100% for Stage II, effective October 05, 2026 on open positions as on October 01, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.

## Regulatory Changes

Updates to the list of securities under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) pursuant to ongoing market surveillance framework guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must implement enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) on all open positions as on October 01, 2026 and new positions created from October 05, 2026 onwards for the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Surveillance Criteria / Movement / Exclusion: October 01, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Actions / Margins: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding or trading positions in MPEL, SYNCOMF, and VISAMAN will be subject to higher margin obligations, restricting leverage, while ABSMARINE will have surveillance restrictions removed upon exclusion.
