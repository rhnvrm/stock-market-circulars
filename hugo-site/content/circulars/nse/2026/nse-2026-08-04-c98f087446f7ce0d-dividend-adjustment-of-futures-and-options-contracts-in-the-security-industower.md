## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding adjustments to Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in the security INDUSTOWER (Indus Towers Limited). This adjustment is in response to the company's dividend announcement of Rs 14/- per share and will take effect from the ex-date, August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Corporate Action Details:** Indus Towers Limited announced a dividend of Rs 14/- per share (Face Value Rs 10/-).
- **Adjustment Date:** The ex-date and effective date for contract adjustments is August 10, 2026.
- **Revised Parameters:** Revision of option strike prices, futures base prices, and lot sizes will be implemented.
- **Availability of Updates:** The revised options strike prices, futures prices, lot sizes, and quantity freeze limits will be provided in the contract.gz and spread files on the day prior to the ex-date.

## Regulatory Changes

These adjustments are carried out in pursuance of SEBI guidelines prescribed for adjustments to futures and options contracts on announcement of corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Load Updated Files:** Trading members must download and load the updated contract file (`NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) and spread file (`NSE_FO_spdcontract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) before trading begins on August 10, 2026.
- **File Access:** Files can be downloaded from the Extranet server under `faoftp/faocommon` or accessed via the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 04, 2026
- **Ex-Date / Effective Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Revised Files Release:** August 07, 2026 (one trading day prior to the ex-date)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Trading Impact:** F&O strike prices and futures base prices will be adjusted downward, rounded off to the nearest tick size. Lot sizes, if adjusted, will be rounded off to the nearest integer.
- **Operational Impact:** Clearing and trading applications must be updated to prevent discrepancies during the ex-date trading session. Position adjustments methodology will be separately shared by the clearing corporations.
