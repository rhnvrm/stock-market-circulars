## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Ashapuri Gold Ornament Limited (Symbol: AGOL) effective from October 12, 2026. A total of 33,33,14,666 equity shares with a face value of Re. 1/- each are admitted for trading in the Normal Market segment under rolling settlement.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ashapuri Gold Ornament Limited
- Symbol: AGOL
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE05FR01029
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Re. 1/- each fully paid up
- Total Number of Securities: 333314666
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 333314666
- Market Lot: 1
- Pari Passu: Yes
- Lock-in Details: Not Applicable
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the securities shall be in the Normal Market segment (Rolling Settlement) in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading and liquidity for equity shares of Ashapuri Gold Ornament Limited on the NSE platform.
