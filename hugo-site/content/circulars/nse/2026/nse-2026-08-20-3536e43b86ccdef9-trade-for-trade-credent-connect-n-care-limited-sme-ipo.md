## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Credent Connect N Care Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM) effective September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security affected: Credent Connect N Care Limited (Symbol: CREDENT).
- Transferred from Trade for Trade segment (series: ST) to Rolling segment (Series: SM).
- Effective Date: September 03, 2026.
- Pursuant to previous exchange circular and SEBI guidelines (CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012).

## Regulatory Changes

- None specific; implementation of standard guidelines for movement out of trade-for-trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members need to note the change in series for trading in Credent Connect N Care Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading will move from the trade-for-trade segment to the normal rolling segment, facilitating regular settlement for the stock.
