## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular (Ref. No: 589/2026) informing all members that **Impex Ferro Tech Limited (Symbol: IMPEXFERRO)** will be moved to **Stage I of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM)** framework. This surveillance action is a continuation of previous Exchange circulars on GSM and will take effect from **August 06, 2026**.

## Key Points

- **Affected Security:** Impex Ferro Tech Limited
- **Symbol:** IMPEXFERRO
- **ISIN:** INE691G01015
- **Surveillance Action:** Migration to Stage I of Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM).
- **Effective Date:** August 06, 2026.
- **Trading Restrictions:** Trading in IMPEXFERRO will be subject to a 100% applicable margin rate and a price band of 5% or lower.
- **Precautionary Advice:** NSE has advised members to take adequate precautions while trading in the security.

## Regulatory Changes

No new broad regulatory policies are introduced. This is a specific administrative surveillance action executed under the pre-existing Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework of the NSE, in continuation of circulars dating back to February 2017 (including NSE/SURV/34262, NSE/SURV/38389, and others).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Requirement:** Trading members must collect and maintain a 100% applicable margin rate for all buy/sell transactions in IMPEXFERRO.
- **Price Band Constraints:** System price parameters must be locked to a 5% or lower price band (as applicable) for IMPEXFERRO.
- **Member Diligence:** Members are required to exercise heightened due diligence and caution prior to facilitating trades in the affected security.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular:** August 05, 2026
- **Effective Date of Surveillance Action:** August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Security Impact:** The inclusion of Impex Ferro Tech Limited in GSM Stage I will significantly restrict price volatility. The 100% margin requirement will increase transaction costs (capital requirements) for traders, likely resulting in a decrease in the stock's overall trading volume and liquidity.
- **Market/Systemic Impact:** Extremely low. The surveillance action is highly localized and only applies to IMPEXFERRO, with no systemic risk or broader impact on market infrastructure or other listed securities.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Trading members and brokerages must update their risk management systems (RMS) to reflect the 100% margin and 5% price limit on the target security prior to market open on August 06, 2026.
