## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the provisional empanelment of M/s Futures Unchained LLP as an Algo Provider. The firm is authorized to provide Whitebox Algorithmic Trading Solutions to trading members of the Exchange. The empanelment is effective immediately.

## Key Points

- **Empaneled Vendor:** M/s Futures Unchained LLP.
- **Algorithm Type:** Whitebox Algorithmic Trading Solutions.
- **Effective Date:** Applicable with immediate effect (July 31, 2026).
- **Contact Details:** Located in Bangalore, Karnataka (Ph: +91 9902824662).
- **Reference List:** The updated list of empaneled Algo Providers is available on the official NSE website.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no changes to existing regulations. This circular serves as an administrative notification of a new provisionally empaneled vendor under existing algorithmic trading frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members planning to utilize the vendor's solutions must ensure that all algorithmic systems undergo necessary testing and obtain prior approval from the Exchange as per standard protocols.
- No immediate action is required for members not using this vendor's solutions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** July 31, 2026 (immediate applicability)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. Adds a new vendor option for trading members utilizing Whitebox algorithms, enhancing market choice.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Affects only those trading members who choose to onboard or migrate to the newly empaneled provider's solutions.
