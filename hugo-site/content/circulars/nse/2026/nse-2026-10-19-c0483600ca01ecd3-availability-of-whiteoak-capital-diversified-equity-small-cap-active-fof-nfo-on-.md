## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the availability of Whiteoak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF of Whiteoak Capital Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities starting October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Whiteoak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF (Regular-Growth, ISIN: INF03VN01AO0)
- Scheme Name: Whiteoak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF (Direct-Growth, ISIN: INF03VN01AN2)
- Facilities Enabled: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026.
- Segment: NMF TM Segment (Download Ref No: NSE/NMFTM/76997, Circular Ref. No: 1795/2026).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational circular for mutual fund scheme onboarding and platform availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the NSE MF Invest Platform should take note of the scheme availability and ISIN details for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low impact. Facilitates trading members and investors to transact in the newly available Whiteoak Capital Small Cap Active FOF schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
