## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and commencement of trading for further issues of equity shares of Medicamen Organics Limited on the SME EMERGE segment, effective from August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Medicamen Organics Limited
- Symbol: MEDIORG
- Series: SM
- ISIN: INE0PE401018 (Temporary ISIN applicable as per SEBI guidelines for additional issues)
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 35/-
- Number of Securities: 2,714,285 equity shares allotted on a preferential basis
- Distinctive Number Range: 12165911 to 14880195
- Market Lot: 2,000 shares
- Pari Passu: Yes

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) - SME EMERGE in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Lock-in details as per Annexure A:
- 2,000,000 shares (Distinctive numbers 12165911 to 14165910): Lock-in expiry date 28-Feb-2027
- 714,285 shares (Distinctive numbers 14165911 to 14880195): Lock-in expiry date 28-Feb-2028

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 09-Jun-2026
- Effective Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of capital base through preferential allotment for Medicamen Organics Limited on the SME platform.
