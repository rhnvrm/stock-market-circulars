## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Privately placed securities including Commercial Papers from multiple companies have been admitted to dealings.
- Effective date of listing is September 03, 2026.
- Issuances include securities from Reliance Retail Ventures Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, Godrej Agrovet Limited, Axis Securities Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Small Industries Development Bank of India, and Trust Investment Advisors Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to the designated security codes and trading parameters specified in the exchange annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates trading and settlement of newly issued commercial papers on the debt segment.
