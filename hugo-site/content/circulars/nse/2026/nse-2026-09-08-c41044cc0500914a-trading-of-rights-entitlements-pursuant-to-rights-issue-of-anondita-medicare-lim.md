## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Anondita Medicare Limited, effective from September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: ANOND-RE
- Company Name: Anondita Medicare Limited
- Security Description: Trading of Rights Entitlement pursuant to Rights Issue
- ISIN: INE0VTV20012
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 958,000
- Trading Period for Renunciation: From September 09, 2026, to September 11, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 15, 2026
- Market Lot: 10
- Segment: Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of rights entitlements to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) - SME EMERGE pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs, highlighting the specific ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares. Prominent disclaimers must be included in contract notes regarding the rights and conditions of REs, including application and renunciation deadlines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Commencement of Trading: September 09, 2026
- Trading Period End Date (Renunciation): September 11, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and investors to trade or renounce rights entitlements of Anondita Medicare Limited within the specified timeframe under the SME EMERGE segment.
