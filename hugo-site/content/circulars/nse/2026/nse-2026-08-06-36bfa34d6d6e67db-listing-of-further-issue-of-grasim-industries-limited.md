## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular notifying the admission and listing of 7,072 further equity shares of Grasim Industries Limited (GRASIM) with effect from August 07, 2026. These shares are converted from partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares and will trade under the permanent ISIN INE047A01021.

## Key Points

- **Issuer**: Grasim Industries Limited
- **Symbol / Series**: GRASIM / EQ
- **Quantity**: 7,072 equity shares
- **Distinctive Numbers**: 680470008 to 680477079 (Total 7,072 shares)
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value**: Rs. 2/- per share
- **Permanent ISIN**: INE047A01021
- **Temporary ISIN**: IN8047A01020 (shares originally credited under this temporary ISIN pending listing activation)
- **Status**: The further shares rank pari passu with the existing fully paid-up equity shares of the company.
- **Lock-in**: Not Applicable.
- **Market Lot**: 1

## Regulatory Changes

No macro regulatory changes are introduced by this circular. The listing and trading permissions are granted in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A. These conversions are processed in accordance with SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 dated August 02, 2012, and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012 dated September 11, 2012, regarding ISIN activation for additional share issuances.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Trading members must identify the security by its designated code (GRASIM) and trade in specified market lots of 1.
- **Depository Participants / Custodians**: Ensure credit transfer from the temporary ISIN (IN8047A01020) to the permanent ISIN (INE047A01021) as per standard procedural guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading**: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Issuer Impact**: Negligible capital structure shift. The conversion of partly paid-up shares to fully paid-up represents a routine corporate action with a very small volume (7,072 shares).
- **Market Impact**: Extremely low. The additional shares are highly liquid and will easily be absorbed under the existing ticker, with no noticeable price or volume pressure.
- **Operational Impact**: Systems must be updated to enable trading of these converted shares under the permanent ISIN from the effective date.
