## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for ICICI Prudential BSE Power ETF on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from October 26, 2026, to October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: ICICI Prudential BSE Power ETF (Direct - Growth & Regular - Growth)
- ISIN: INF109K1A724
- Category: Equity ETF
- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 03, 2026 till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 26, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: October 30, 2026 (till 11:30 pm)
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: November 03, 2026 (till 12:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine NFO offering providing investors trading access to power sector equity via ETF on the NSE platform.
