## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective August 26, 2026.
- Includes further issues of equity shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and other corporate actions.
- Companies involved include Arvind Fashions Limited, Centum Electronics Limited, Cyient Limited, HDFC Asset Management Company Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, The Karnataka Bank Limited, Lenskart Solutions Limited, Lodha Developers Limited, Manaksia Coated Metals & Industries Limited, Onesource Specialty Pharma Limited, and VIP Industries Limited.
- Trading will be conducted in designated security codes and respective market lots as specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Allotment Dates: Various (ranging from August 10, 2026 to August 20, 2026 depending on the company)

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital for the respective listed entities with no major market-wide operational impact.
