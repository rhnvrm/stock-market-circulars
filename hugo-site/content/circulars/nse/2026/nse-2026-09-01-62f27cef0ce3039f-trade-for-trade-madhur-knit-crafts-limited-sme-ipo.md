## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Madhur Knit Crafts Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM) effective September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security: Madhur Knit Crafts Limited (Symbol: MADHURKNIT)
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: ST)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: September 16, 2026
- Reference to previous circular: NSE/CML/76067 dated August 31, 2026, and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are shifted pursuant to SEBI guidelines on trade for trade segments for SME IPOs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should take note of the change in series and trading segment for the security.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for Madhur Knit Crafts Limited, improving liquidity and trading convenience for market participants.
