## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of Titanium Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund NFO under Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) of Tata Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Tata Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Titanium Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund
- NFO Open/Close Period: September 23, 2026 to October 7, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: October 12, 2026
- Available Plans: Regular and Direct Plans across IDCW Reinvestment, Growth, and IDCW Payout options.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 12, 2026 till 3:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: September 23, 2026
- NFO End Date: October 7, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: October 12, 2026 (up to 3:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to subscribe to the new fund offer via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
