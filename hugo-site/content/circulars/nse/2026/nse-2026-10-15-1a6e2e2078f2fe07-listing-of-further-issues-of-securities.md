## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from October 16, 2026, following allotments made under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- Companies involved include Axis Bank Limited, CARE Ratings Limited, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, Insolation Energy Limited, and Tracxn Technologies Limited.
- Securities are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from October 16, 2026.
- All newly allotted shares rank pari passu with the existing equity shares of the respective companies.
- Specific allotments include 92,708 shares for Axis Bank, 14,200 shares for CARE Ratings, 4,009 shares for ICICI Lombard, 1,552 shares for ICICI Prudential Life, 46,750 shares for Insolation Energy, and shares allotted for Tracxn Technologies.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine capital market listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies and trading members must ensure trading in these securities is conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital due to ESOP exercises, with negligible impact on overall market liquidity or stock prices.
