## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced that Ortel Communications Limited will be moved to Stage III of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework effective October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security moving to Stage III: ORTEL Communications Limited (ISIN: INE849L01019).
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026.
- Trading Frequency: Restricted to once a week (every Monday or the first trading day of the week).
- Settlement Basis: Trade-for-trade with no netting off allowed.
- Price Band: 5% or lower as applicable.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD): 100% of trade value to be deposited by buyers.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities placed under Stage III of the GSM framework face strict surveillance actions, including trade-for-trade settlement, weekly trading limitations, and full 100% additional surveillance deposits to curb excessive speculation and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while executing trades in the affected security, ensure compliance with trade-for-trade settlement rules, collect 100% ASD from buyers, and adhere to the once-a-week trading schedule.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity for Ortel Communications Limited will be severely restricted due to weekly trading limits, 100% ASD requirements, and trade-for-trade settlement, impacting market participants holding or trading the security.
