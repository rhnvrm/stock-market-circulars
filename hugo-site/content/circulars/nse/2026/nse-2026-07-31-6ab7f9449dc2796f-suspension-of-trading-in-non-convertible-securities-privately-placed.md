## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities issued by LIC Housing Finance Limited. This suspension is executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A, effective from August 3, 2026, due to the scheduled redemption of the securities.

## Key Points

- **Affected Entity:** LIC Housing Finance Limited
- **Security Type:** Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed NCDs)
- **ISIN:** INE115A07KE6
- **Suspension Date:** August 3, 2026
- **Reason for Suspension:** Redemption of the underlying security
- **Regulatory Reference:** Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes introduced. This is a routine execution of existing provisions under Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Must note the suspension of trading in the specified ISIN (INE115A07KE6) and ensure no trades are placed or executed on or after August 3, 2026.
- **Issuer (LIC Housing Finance Limited):** No further actions are required as the suspension is standard procedure following NCD redemption.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Suspension Effective Date:** August 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine operational event associated with NCD maturity and redemption. It has no negative implications for the issuer's credit standing or other active securities.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Systems will be updated to disable trading and settlement capabilities for ISIN INE115A07KE6 from the suspension date.
