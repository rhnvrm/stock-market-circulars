## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the availability of Nippon India Quarterly Interval Fund - Series II (Interval Fund) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Nippon India Mutual Fund will offer this scheme for a specified transaction period from August 07, 2026, to August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Transaction Period:** Subscription and redemption requests will be accepted from August 07, 2026, to August 10, 2026.
- **Business Hours & Cut-offs:** Transactions must be placed during normal business hours as per the scheme cut-off timings on the NSE MF Invest platform. The applicable Net Asset Value (NAV) will be determined based on the date and time of receipt of the transaction, subject to fund confirmation.
- **Supported Schemes and ISINs:**
  - Nippon India Quarterly Interval Fund - Series II - IDCW Plan - Payout (ISIN: `INF204K01DO3`)
  - Nippon India Quarterly Interval Fund - Series II - IDCW Plan - Reinvestment (ISIN: `INF204K01DP0`)
  - Nippon India Quarterly Interval Fund - Series II - Direct IDCW Plan - Payout (ISIN: `INF204K01F46`)
  - Nippon India Quarterly Interval Fund - Series II - Direct IDCW Plan - Reinvestment (ISIN: `INF204K01F53`)
  - Nippon India Quarterly Interval Fund - Series II – Direct Growth Plan (ISIN: `INF204K01F61`)
  - Nippon India Quarterly Interval Fund - Series II - Growth Plan (ISIN: `INF204K01DN5`)
- **Category:** All listed schemes belong to the Debt category.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced in this circular. It is a standard operational update facilitating transacting on the mutual fund platform under existing guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Members:** Mutual fund trading members must process subscription and redemption orders within the specified transaction period and adhere strictly to the platform's cut-off timings.
- **NAV Application:** Ensure transactions are submitted in a timely manner as NAV applicability is subject to prescribed receipt times and fund confirmation.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 06, 2026
- **Transaction Opening Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Transaction Closing Date:** August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low. This is a routine operational opening of an interval fund's transaction window. It does not introduce new regulatory burdens or broad market changes, but provides specific liquidity opportunities for investors in the mentioned schemes.
