## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the early pay-in of ETFs of DSP Asset Managers Private Limited on account of a face value split (sub-division) from Rs 10/- per share to Re 1/- per share for DSP Gold ETF and DSP Silver ETF.

## Key Points

- Securities affected: DSP Mutual Fund - DSP Gold ETF (Symbol: GOLDADD, Old ISIN: INF740KA1SW3) and DSP Mutual Fund - DSP Silver ETF (Symbol: SILVERADD, Old ISIN: INF740KA1RE3).
- Corporate Action: Face Value Split from Rs 10/- Per Share to Re 1/- Per Share.
- Record Date and Ex-Date: August 28, 2026.
- Members must execute early pay-in in the depository systems using the existing/old ISIN without adjusting for the conversion for the specified settlement dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory compliance with instructions for early pay-in using old ISIN during the record/ex-date window before transitioning to the new ISIN.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants/custodians must execute early pay-in instructions in the old ISIN for the ex-date/record date.
- In case of early pay-in through block mechanism, client-wise early pay-in allocation file should not be uploaded.
- Custodians providing early pay-in through pool accounts must upload client-wise allocation details based on actual traded quantities.
- Post-record date, transactions must use the New ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: 28-AUG-2026
- Record Date: 28-AUG-2026
- EPI Dates: 28-AUG-2026 and 31-AUG-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants and custodians must correctly handle ISIN references during the corporate action window to avoid settlement mismatches.
