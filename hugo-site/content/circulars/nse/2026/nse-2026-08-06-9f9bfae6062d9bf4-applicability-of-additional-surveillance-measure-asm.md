## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) framework. Following the criteria defined in previous circulars, certain securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under Stage I of the Long-Term ASM framework. The applicable surveillance actions, including a 100% margin requirement, will take effect from August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Four securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) Stage I framework starting August 07, 2026.
- The shortlisted securities are: Indo Thai Securities Limited (INDOTHAI), Paras Petrofils Limited (PARASPETRO), Polysil Irrigation Systems Limited (POLYSIL), and Shree Tirupati Balajee FIBC Limited (TIRUPATI).
- Indo Thai Securities Limited (INDOTHAI) has moved from the Short-Term ASM (STASM) to the Long-Term ASM (LTASM) framework.
- No securities were shortlisted for Long-Term ASM Stage IV or for transitions between stages (Stage I to II, Stage II to III, or Stage I to IV).
- An applicable margin rate of 100% will be implemented on all open positions as of August 10, 2026, and new positions created from August 11, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under Stage IV (Criteria VII) would be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) on a T+3 basis.
- The ASM framework is applied purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specified securities under the Long-Term ASM Stage I framework.
- Imposition of a 100% margin rate on all open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.
- Potential shifting of securities qualifying under Criteria VII (Stage IV) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) to restrict speculative trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must implement a 100% margin rate on all open positions as of August 10, 2026, and all new positions created from August 11, 2026, for the shortlisted securities (INDOTHAI, PARASPETRO, POLYSIL, TIRUPATI).
- Members must monitor and comply with any trading segment shifts (from Series EQ to BE) if any security qualifies under Stage IV (Criteria VII) in future revisions.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 06, 2026
- **Shortlisting Effective Date**: August 07, 2026 (for inclusion in LT-ASM Stage I)
- **Margin Rate 100% Implementation Date**: August 11, 2026 (on all open positions as of August 10, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading & Liquidity Impact**: The high margin requirement of 100% significantly increases the cost of trading for the affected securities, likely leading to a reduction in trading volumes and intraday liquidity for INDOTHAI, PARASPETRO, POLYSIL, and TIRUPATI.
- **Settlement & Operations**: Clearers and brokers must adjust risk management parameters and collect 100% margin for the designated stocks.
- **Market Sentiment**: Shortlisting is done as a preventive surveillance measure and does not indicate corporate governance issues or adverse actions, but retail investors may perceive it as a negative signal, potentially putting downward pressure on the stock prices in the short term.
