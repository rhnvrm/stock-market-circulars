## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and trading commencement of equity shares of Tonbo Imaging India Limited under its Initial Public Offering (IPO) effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total fully paid-up equity shares: 5,72,82,100
- Promoter & Promoter Group holding: 83,42,600 shares (14.56%)
- Public shareholding: 4,70,34,800 shares (82.11%)
- Employee Trust holding: 19,04,700 shares (3.33%)
- Listing Date: October 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and ICDR regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entity must adhere to ongoing disclosures under SEBI LODR Regulations including shareholding pattern submissions and corporate governance norms.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public market access and liquidity for the equity shares of Tonbo Imaging India Limited on the NSE.
