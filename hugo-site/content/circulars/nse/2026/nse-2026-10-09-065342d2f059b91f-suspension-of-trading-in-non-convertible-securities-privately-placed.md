## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of Sundaram Home Finance Limited pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SUNDARAM HOME FINANCE LIMITED
- ISIN: INE667F07IO8
- Suspension Date: 12-Oct-2026
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

None. Action taken pursuant to standard debt market regulations upon redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members take note of the suspension of trading in the specified non-convertible securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 09, 2026
- Suspension Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact as the suspension is due to redemption of the securities.
