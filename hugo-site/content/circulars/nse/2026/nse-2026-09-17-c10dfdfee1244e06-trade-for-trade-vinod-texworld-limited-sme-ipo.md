## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Vinod Texworld Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vinod Texworld Limited
- Symbol: VINOD
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026
- Shift from Trade for Trade segment (series: ST) to Rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Reference to previous Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76367 dated September 16, 2026, and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

Nil

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members need to note the change in segment and series for trading in the security.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the security will move to the normal rolling segment, enabling regular settlement cycles.
