## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further issue of equity shares of Lloyds Enterprises Limited effective from October 26, 2026. The issue comprises 121 equity shares resulting from the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Lloyds Enterprises Limited
- **Symbol:** LLOYDSENT
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE080I01025 (Temporary ISIN: IN8080I01024)
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Re. 1/-
- **Number of Securities:** 121 equity shares
- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Purpose:** Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular for trading.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact, standard corporate action regarding listing of additional converted shares.
