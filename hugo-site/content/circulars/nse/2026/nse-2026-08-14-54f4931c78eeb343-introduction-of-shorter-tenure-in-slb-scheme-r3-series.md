## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the introduction of shorter tenure contracts under the "R3" series in the Securities Lending & Borrowing (SLB) Scheme, effective from August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Shorter tenure contracts will be generated and made available on a daily basis under the series "R3".
- First leg of trades executed on Transaction Day (T Day) settles on T+1 day, and the corresponding reverse leg settlement is scheduled on T+3 day (excluding Settlement Holidays).
- Applicable only for stocks eligible for trading in the Equity Derivatives Segment.
- Shorter tenure contracts will not be foreclosed during AGM/EGM, and facilities of Repay/Recall/Rollover are not applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

- Addition of the "R3" series for short-tenure SLB transactions with T+3 reverse leg settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians should take note of the new series and ensure systems account for the daily availability and settlement timeline of "R3" series contracts.

## Important Dates

- EOD August 14, 2026: Security file shared by the Exchange includes the additional series "R3".
- August 17, 2026: Shorter tenure contracts ("R3" series) made available for trading from Transaction Day.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants with increased flexibility and shorter turnaround times for securities lending and borrowing in eligible equity derivative stocks.
