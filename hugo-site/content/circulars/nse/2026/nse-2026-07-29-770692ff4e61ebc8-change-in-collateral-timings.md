## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has announced revised cut-off timings for placing collateral release requests in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment. This is in partial modification to the previous circular ref. no. 0265/2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/EGR/75320 dated July 21, 2026). The revised timings are effective from July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Revised Cut-off for Cash and FDR Release (Intraday)**: Advanced from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
- **Revised Cut-off for Cash and FDR Release (End of Day)**: Remains unchanged at 8:00 PM.
- **Revised Cut-off for Securities EOD Release**: Advanced from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
- **Effective Date**: The new timings are effective starting July 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partially modifies circular ref. no. 0265/2026 / Download Ref No: NCL/EGR/75320 (dated July 21, 2026) regarding timings for collateral addition and release in the EGR segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Operational Shift**: Clearing Members, Custodians, and Professional Clearing Members (PCMs) must update their workflows to ensure that collateral release requests are submitted before the new 8:00 PM cut-off.
- **Deadline Adherence**: Requests submitted after 8:00 PM will not be processed under the previous intraday or EOD windows for that business day.

## Important Dates

- **July 29, 2026**: Circular issuance date.
- **July 30, 2026**: Effective date of revised timings.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: High-to-medium impact on operational teams as the deadline for requesting intraday cash/FDR and EOD securities release is advanced by one hour (from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM).
- **Risk Management**: Helps NCL in earlier risk-assessment and processing of collateral releases, aligning different collateral release types to a uniform 8:00 PM cut-off.
