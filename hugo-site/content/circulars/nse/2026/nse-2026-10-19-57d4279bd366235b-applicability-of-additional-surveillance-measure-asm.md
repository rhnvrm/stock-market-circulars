## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Certain securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Stage-I of the Long-Term ASM framework effective October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I: Comfort Intech Limited (COMFINTE - INE819A01049) and Shanti Gold International Limited (SHANTIGOLD - INE06ZD01017).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective October 23, 2026, on all open positions as on October 22, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company/entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term ASM Stage-I measures for specified scrips.
- Imposition of 100% margin requirement on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure 100% margin is maintained on open positions and new positions for the shortlisted securities as per the specified timeline.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ASM inclusion: October 21, 2026
- Open positions cutoff date for margin applicability: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin requirement: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants and investors dealing in the shortlisted securities (COMFINTE and SHANTIGOLD) will face higher margin requirements (100%), impacting liquidity and trading leverage for these scrips.
