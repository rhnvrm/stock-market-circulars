## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for several debt schemes offered by Mirae Asset Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 25, 2026. These modifications are made pursuant to SEBI circular requirements on the categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes.

## Key Points

- Mirae Asset Ultra Short Duration Fund is renamed to Mirae Asset Ultra Short-Term Fund.
- Mirae Asset Low Duration Fund is renamed to Mirae Asset Ultra Short to Short Term Fund.
- Mirae Asset Short Duration Fund is renamed to Mirae Asset Short Term Fund.
- Mirae Asset Long Duration Fund is renamed to Mirae Asset Long Term Fund.
- Mirae Asset Dynamic Bond Fund is renamed to Mirae Asset Dynamic Term Fund.
- Mirae Asset Banking and PSU Fund is renamed to Mirae Asset Banking and PSU Debt Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

Changes are implemented in accordance with the SEBI Circular dated February 26, 2026, on Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes, requiring mutual funds to align nomenclature with revised scheme categories.

## Compliance Requirements

Asset Management Companies (AMCs) must ensure that necessary and incidental changes are reflected in the Scheme Information Documents (SIDs), Key Information Memorandums (KIMs), and Statement of Additional Information (SAI).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The name changes are administrative and regulatory in nature. Existing unitholders and investors will continue with their respective investments under the updated nomenclature without any operational or financial impact.
