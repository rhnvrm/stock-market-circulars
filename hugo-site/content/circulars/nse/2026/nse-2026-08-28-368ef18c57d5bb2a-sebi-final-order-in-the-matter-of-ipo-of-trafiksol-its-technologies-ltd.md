## Summary

National Stock Exchange has communicated SEBI's final order dated August 28, 2026, regarding the Initial Public Offer (IPO) of Trafiksol ITS Technologies Ltd. SEBI has restrained Trafiksol ITS Technologies Limited and two related individuals from accessing the securities market and prohibited them from buying or selling securities for a period of one year.

## Key Points

- Entities debarred: Trafiksol ITS Technologies Limited, Mr. Jitendra Narayan Das, and Ms. Poonam Das.
- Period of restraint: 1 year from the date of the order (August 28, 2026).
- Open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts are permitted to be squared off or closed out within 3 months or upon expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Pre-order settlement obligations are allowed to be completed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement action taken under SEBI Act sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1) and 11B(2).

## Compliance Requirements

- NSE members are advised to take note and ensure compliance regarding debarred entities.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 28, 2026
- De-barment Period: August 28, 2026 to August 27, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Prevents the named entities from participating directly or indirectly in the securities market, ensuring market integrity following IPO irregularities.
