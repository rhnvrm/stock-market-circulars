## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for specific securities. Securities shortlisted under ESM shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 08, 2026, on all open positions as on October 07, 2026, and new positions created thereafter. Securities qualifying under ESM are also shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- The Peria Karamalai Tea & Produce Company Limited (PKTEA) is shortlisted under ESM Stage - I w.e.f. October 07, 2026.
- Affected securities attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 08, 2026 on open positions as on October 07, 2026 and new positions created from October 08, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 07, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shifting of qualified securities from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).
- Imposition of minimum 100% margin requirements on open and new positions.
- Periodic Call Auction with a 2% price band for Stage II securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities.
- Market participants must note the shift of securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- **October 06, 2026:** Circular issuance date.
- **October 07, 2026:** Effective date for ESM Stage - I shortlisting for PKTEA / Stage II shift.
- **October 08, 2026:** Effective date for 100% margin applicability and shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Enhanced surveillance measures impose stricter trading controls, higher margin obligations, and trade-for-trade settlement on listed securities, impacting liquidity and trading mechanisms for market participants.
