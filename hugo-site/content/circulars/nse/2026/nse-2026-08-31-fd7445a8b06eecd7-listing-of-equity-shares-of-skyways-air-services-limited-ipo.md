## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of equity shares of Skyways Air Services Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), with trading effective from September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Skyways Air Services Limited
- Symbol: SKYWAYS
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE0PX301025
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- Issue Price for Current Public Issue: Rs. 138/-
- Total No. of Securities: 145,343,544 equity shares
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per relevant SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 31, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Skyways Air Services Limited equity shares on the NSE Capital Market segment, providing liquidity to investors following the IPO.
