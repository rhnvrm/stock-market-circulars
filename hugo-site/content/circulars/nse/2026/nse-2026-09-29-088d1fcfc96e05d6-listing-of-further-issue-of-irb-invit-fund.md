## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issue of 317,460,316 units of IRB InvIT Fund on QIP basis, effective from September 30, 2026.

- Symbol: IRBINVIT
- ISIN: INE183W23014 (with temporary ISIN IN8********* used for demat crediting prior to activation)
- Issue Price: Rs. 63 per unit
- Date of Allotment: September 29, 2026
- Number of Units: 317,460,316
- Distinctive Number Range: 1335600001 to 1653060316
- Pari Passu: Yes
- Lock-in Expiry Date: Not Applicable

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; notification of security admission under NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

Trading in the securities shall be identified by designated codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

- Date of Allotment: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 30, 2026

Routine capital expansion via QIP for IRB InvIT Fund, adding 317,460,316 units to the trading segment.
