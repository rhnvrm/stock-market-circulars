## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the revocation of temporary suspension and the resumption of fresh/additional subscriptions (lumpsum/switch-in) under the ICICI Prudential Midcap Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Effective October 8, 2026, investments are permitted up to a maximum limit of ₹10 Lakh per PAN per day.

## Key Points

- Temporary suspension on fresh/additional subscriptions for ICICI Prudential Midcap Fund has been revoked.
- Lumpsum and switch-in transactions are allowed up to ₹10 Lakh per PAN per day.
- Effective date for the new limit is October 8, 2026.
- Any transaction resulting in a breach of the ₹10 Lakh limit will be completely rejected (partial amounts will not be accepted).

## Regulatory Changes

- Resumption of subscription facilities on NSE MF Invest Platform for ICICI Prudential Midcap Fund.
- Implementation of a strict daily transaction ceiling of ₹10 Lakh per PAN.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must adhere to the ₹10 Lakh per PAN per day limit for lumpsum and switch-in transactions.
- Transactions exceeding the limit will face complete rejection without partial acceptance.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 7, 2026
- Effective Date: October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors can resume investments in ICICI Prudential Midcap Fund through NSE MF Invest Platform up to the specified daily limit.
- Members need to note the transaction restrictions and rejection rules for breaching limits.
