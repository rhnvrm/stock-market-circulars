## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) announced a mock trading session in the Futures & Options (F&O) segment on Saturday, September 05, 2026, ahead of the live implementation of the modified pre-open call auction framework effective from September 07, 2026. Members are required to use the new NEAT+ version 7.9.0.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, September 05, 2026, across multiple sessions (Primary and DR sites).
- Introduction of new NEAT+ version 7.9.0 specifically for the revised pre-open call auction framework.
- Existing NEAT+ version 7.8.9 will be discontinued on October 10, 2026.
- Mandatory migration to new market data broadcast parameters starting September 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modifications in the Pre-Open Call Auction framework in the Equity Derivatives segment, effective in LIVE from September 07, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must download and use NEAT+ version 7.9.0 available from September 04, 2026, 18:00 hours.
- Members must load the latest master files before the mock trading session.
- Mandatory migration for members using direct connection via NNF to new market data broadcast parameters.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 05, 2026
- Live Implementation Date: September 07, 2026
- Discontinuation of Version 7.8.9: October 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Members need to upgrade their trading applications to NEAT+ 7.9.0 and participate in the mock trading sessions to ensure seamless operations under the revised pre-open call auction framework.
