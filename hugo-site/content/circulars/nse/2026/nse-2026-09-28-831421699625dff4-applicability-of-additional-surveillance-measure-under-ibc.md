## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework. Effective September 29, 2026, specific securities are excluded or maintained under the ASM IBC framework as per exchange guidelines.

## Key Points

- Securities excluded from ASM under IBC: Zicom Electronic Security Systems Limited (ZICOM).
- Consolidated ASM IBC list includes Aban Offshore Limited (ABAN), Ankit Metal & Power Limited (ANKITMETAL), and Impex Ferro Tech Limited (IMPEXFERRO).
- The framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under ASM IBC is purely for market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Updates to the list of securities under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing processes as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are requested to take note of the shortlisted and excluded securities and ensure compliance with applicable surveillance margins and trading restrictions effective September 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading in specified securities subject to ASM IBC surveillance measures, impacting member risk management and trading execution.
