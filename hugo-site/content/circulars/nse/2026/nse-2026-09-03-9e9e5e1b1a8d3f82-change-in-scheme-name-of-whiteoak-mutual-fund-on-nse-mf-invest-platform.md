## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme name for WhiteOak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform. Pursuant to SEBI Master Circular provisions on Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes, the scheme name "WhiteOak Capital - Ultra Short Duration" is revised to "WhiteOak Capital - Ultra Short Term Fund" with effect from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Scheme Name: WhiteOak Capital - Ultra Short Duration / WhiteOak Capital Ultra Short Duration Fund
- Revised Scheme Name: Whiteoak Capital - Ultra Short Term Fund / WhiteOak Capital Ultra Short Term Fund
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Applicable Platform: NSE MF Invest platform

## Regulatory Changes

- Changes implemented in accordance with Para 3.8.9 of the SEBI Master Circular on Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026, relating to Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- All other terms and conditions of the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) remain unchanged.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Circular Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Purely administrative update reflecting nomenclature changes aligned with SEBI's scheme categorization guidelines with no direct impact on investors' holdings or fund strategy.
