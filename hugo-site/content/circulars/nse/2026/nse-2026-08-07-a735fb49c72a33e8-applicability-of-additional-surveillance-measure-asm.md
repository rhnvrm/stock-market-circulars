## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular updating the list of securities under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Based on surveillance criteria, specific securities have met the requirements for inclusion in Stage-I of the Long-Term ASM framework. The surveillance actions, including 100% margin requirements, will become effective from August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Shortlisted Securities**: Three securities have been shortlisted under the Long-Term ASM Stage-I framework: Ashima Limited (ASHIMASYN), Kabra Extrusion Technik Limited (KABRAEXTRU), and V Marc India Limited (VMARCIND).
- **BSE Action**: Ashima Limited satisfies the ASM criteria at BSE as well.
- **Margin Requirements**: An applicable rate of margin of 100% will be enforced starting August 12, 2026, on all open positions as of August 11, 2026, and on any new positions created from August 12, 2026, onwards.
- **Trade-for-Trade Shift**: Securities qualifying under criteria VII (shifting to Stage IV) will be moved from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) starting August 12, 2026 (no additions to Stage IV were reported in this annexure).
- **Framework Context**: The ASM framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures. Moving out of the framework reinstates the price band applicable prior to shortlisting, unless overridden by another active surveillance measure.
- **Regulatory Stance**: Shortlisting under ASM is purely a market surveillance measure and should not be interpreted as an adverse action against the listed companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage-I framework for designated securities.
- Mandating a 100% margin requirement on all open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must update risk systems to collect and maintain a 100% margin rate on all positions (open and new) for ASHIMASYN, KABRAEXTRU, and VMARCIND from August 12, 2026.
- **Operations**: Ensure compliance with potential segment shifts (Rolling to Trade-for-Trade) for any securities that move to Stage IV of the ASM framework.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 07, 2026
- **ASM Selection Effective Date**: August 10, 2026
- **Position Freeze Date for Margin Calculation**: August 11, 2026
- **100% Margin Effective Date**: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Impact**: The shortlisted securities (ASHIMASYN, KABRAEXTRU, and VMARCIND) will require 100% margin, which eliminates leverage. This is expected to temporarily reduce trading volumes, volatility, and liquidity in these specific scripts.
- **Operational Impact**: Brokerages and trading members must verify margin calculations and enforce compliance limits for client accounts holding these securities starting August 12, 2026.
