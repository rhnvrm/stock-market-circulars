## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular announcing the availability and reopening of the Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities effective from September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund Direct Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1580, Category: Equity)
- Scheme Name: Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund Regular Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1572, Category: Equity)
- Facilities Available: Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, SWP
- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants should note the inclusion of the scheme for platform operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 02, 2026
- Circular Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to transact in the Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
