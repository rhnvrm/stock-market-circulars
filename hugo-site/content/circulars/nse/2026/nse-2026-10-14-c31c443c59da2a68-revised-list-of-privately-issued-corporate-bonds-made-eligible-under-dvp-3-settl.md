## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has released a revised list of privately issued corporate bonds and debentures that are made eligible under the DVP-3 settlement mechanism on the Institutional Platform of the Debt Segment.

## Key Points

- Details a comprehensive annexure of eligible ISINs, instrument names, symbols, maturity dates, and issuance details.
- Includes instruments from prominent issuers such as REC, IRFC, NABARD, EXIM Bank, HDFC Bank, SBI, HPCL, L&T Finance, Gujarat Fluorochemicals, and Axis Bank.
- Streamlines settlement processing for institutional debt transactions through Delivery versus Payment (DVP-3).

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the roster of approved securities eligible for trading and settlement on the debt segment institutional platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and institutional participants must update their trading and back-office systems with the revised ISIN eligibility list for settlement.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for trades executed and settled under the DVP-3 mechanism on the Institutional Debt Segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Improves operational efficiency, transparency, and risk management in institutional corporate bond settlements.
