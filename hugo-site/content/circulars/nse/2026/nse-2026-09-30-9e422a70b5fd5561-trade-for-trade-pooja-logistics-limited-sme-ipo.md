## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced that securities of Pooja Logistics Limited shall be transferred from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM) with effect from October 15, 2026, further to Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76620 dated September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pooja Logistics Limited
- Symbol: POOJALOGIS
- Transfer from: Trade for trade segment (Series: ST)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to SEBI guidelines (circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012) and previous exchange circulars, the security is moving from the trade-for-trade settlement segment to the regular rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members need to note the change in segment and series for trading in Pooja Logistics Limited.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This transition enables normal rolling settlement for Pooja Logistics Limited shares, improving liquidity and trading convenience for investors.
