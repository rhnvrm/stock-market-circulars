## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Behari Lal Engineering Limited (IPO) on the Capital Market segment with effect from August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: BLEL
- ISIN: INE1EEM01017
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 285/-
- Total No. of Securities: 42,302,482
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the security shall be in the specified lot size and identified by its designated code.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Behari Lal Engineering Limited shares on the NSE capital market segment.
