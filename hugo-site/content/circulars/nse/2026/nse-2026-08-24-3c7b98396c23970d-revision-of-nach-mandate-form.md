## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing members that the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) has revised the NACH Mandate Form. Members are required to implement the revised mandate format from August 31, 2026, and update their systems accordingly.

## Key Points

- NPCI has revised the NACH Mandate Form vide Notice No. NPCI/2026-27/NACH/001.
- Effective date for implementation is August 31, 2026.
- Previous NACH mandate formats remain valid up to September 30, 2026.
- Destination banks may accept or reject previous format mandates after September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Adoption of the revised standard NACH mandate format issued by NPCI for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must update their systems and processes with the revised NACH mandate requirements and inform all concerned teams within the stipulated timeline.

## Important Dates

- Effective Implementation Date: August 31, 2026
- Previous Format Validity End Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational updates required for members and intermediaries handling NACH mandates on the NSE MF platform to ensure seamless processing without rejection during or after the transition period.
