## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) on select securities pursuant to the surveillance framework.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: AUGMONT, EUROTEXIND, SPRL, THOMASCOTT, VINOD, XELPMOC.
- Securities moving from Stage I to Stage II: SKYTECH.
- Applicable margin rate for Stage I: 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. October 07, 2026 on all open positions as on October 06, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Applicable margin rate for Stage II: 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. October 07, 2026 on all open positions as on October 06, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Surveillance action is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of additional surveillance margins under the ST-ASM framework to safeguard market integrity and curb excessive volatility in specific securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% to 100%) for open and new positions in the specified securities starting October 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Surveillance Actions: October 07, 2026
- Open Positions Baseline Date: October 06, 2026
- Circular Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected stocks, requiring participants to manage collateral efficiently.
