## Summary

In pursuance of surveillance review to ensure market safety and safeguard investor interests, the National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the shifting of specified securities into the Trade for Trade segment with a price band of 5% or lower, effective August 20, 2026. Additionally, certain securities will continue in the Trade for Trade segment based on the current fortnightly review.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure 1 will be shifted from the rolling segment (series: EQ/SM) to the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/ST) with a 5% or lower price band.
- Securities listed in Annexure 2 will continue in the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/BZ or ST/SZ).
- Settlement for these securities will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Transfer is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in specified securities moved to series BE/ST with a 5% price band limit, removing them temporarily or conditionally from rolling settlement (EQ/SM).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate precaution while trading in these securities, keeping in mind the trade-to-trade settlement mechanism and the prohibition of netting off.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 20, 2026 (Thursday)

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity for the affected securities may be impacted due to the mandatory trade-to-trade settlement and strict 5% price band restrictions.
