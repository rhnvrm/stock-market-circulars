## Summary

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, the Exchange has notified the admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the Debt Market Segment with effect from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange.
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026.
- Includes commercial papers from multiple issuers such as Godrej Industries Limited, Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Sundaram Finance Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Export Import Bank of India, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, Godrej Consumer Products Limited, JM Financial Services Limited, and Motilal Oswal Finvest.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Issued under standard listing procedures pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and trading members must ensure compliance with applicable debt segment operational guidelines and settlement schedules.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date / Allotment Date:** September 16, 2026 / September 17, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Varies by security (ranging from October 2026 to September 2027)

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for the newly listed commercial papers on the debt segment.
