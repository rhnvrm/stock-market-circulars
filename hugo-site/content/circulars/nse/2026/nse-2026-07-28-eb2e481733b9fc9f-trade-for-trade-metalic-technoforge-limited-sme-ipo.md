## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the transfer of equity shares of Metalic Technoforge Limited from the trade for trade segment to the rolling segment.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Metalic Technoforge Limited
- **Symbol:** METALIC
- **Segment Shift:** From trade for trade segment (Series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM).
- **Effective Date:** August 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- The transfer is carried out pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to note the change in trading series/segment for Metalic Technoforge Limited starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **July 28, 2026:** Circular release date.
- **August 11, 2026:** Effective date of transition from trade-for-trade to rolling segment.

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity:** Moving to the rolling segment generally increases liquidity as intra-day net settlement is permitted, unlike the trade-for-trade segment where every trade must be delivery-settled.
