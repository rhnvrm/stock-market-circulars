## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of Magnum Equity Ex - Top 100 Long Short Fund NFO under Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) of SBI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform with effect from September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes are available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, and SWP facilities.
- Applicable to multiple plans including Regular and Direct plans for IDCW and Growth.
- Effective date: September 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants must ensure proper processing of orders through the NSE MF Invest Platform according to standard operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading and investment access for the specified SBI Mutual Fund scheme to market participants on the NSE MF platform.
