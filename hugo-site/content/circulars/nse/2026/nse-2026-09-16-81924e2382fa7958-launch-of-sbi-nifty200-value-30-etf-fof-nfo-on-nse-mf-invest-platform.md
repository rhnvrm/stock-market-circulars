## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of the SBI Nifty200 Value 30 ETF FOF New Fund Offer (NFO) from SBI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- **NFO Window:** Available for transactions from September 17, 2026, to September 30, 2026, till 11:59 PM.
- **Schemes Included:** Multiple variants including Regular and Direct options for IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment, and Growth.
- **Allotment Date:** Scheduled for October 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 05, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** September 17, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** September 30, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- **Fund Pay-in Deadline:** October 05, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates members and investors in subscribing to the SBI Nifty200 Value 30 ETF FOF NFO via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
