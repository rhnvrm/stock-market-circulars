## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a revised list of Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) eligible for cross-margin benefits, superseding circular no. 0305/2026 dated August 24, 2026. The revised list and updated minimum quantity requirements take effect from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Supersedes circular no. 0305/2026 (Download Reference No: NCL/CMPT/75921) dated August 24, 2026.
- Effective date for the revised list of eligible ETFs and minimum quantities is September 30, 2026.
- Covers various ETFs across major fund houses including Nippon India, SBI, ICICI Prudential, HDFC, Kotak Mahindra, UTI, and Mirae Asset.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic update of eligible securities and minimum quantity thresholds for the cross-margining facility in the capital market segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must ensure compliance with the updated minimum quantity requirements for cross-margin eligible ETFs when calculating margin offsets effective September 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update ensuring accurate cross-margin calculations based on current ETF lots and liquidity thresholds.
