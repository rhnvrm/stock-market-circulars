## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the close-out procedure for shortages in overnight ETFs and liquid ETFs, in partial modification of earlier consolidated circulars and in line with SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Shortages in normal market for overnight and liquid ETFs that cannot be bought in auction or where the auction seller fails to deliver will be closed out at the highest price prevailing across Exchanges from trade date till auction day, or 5% over the settlement price on the auction day, whichever is higher.
- Where auction pay-out cannot be done on or before Record Date/Book closure minus 1 business day, shortages will be directly closed out using the same pricing formula.
- Applicable to settlement numbers starting from 2026169.

## Regulatory Changes

- Specifies detailed valuation mechanism for closing out shortages in overnight and liquid ETFs when auction mechanisms fail or cannot be completed ahead of record dates/book closure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must adhere to the revised close-out pricing and settlement timelines for overnight and liquid ETFs.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: Trade date September 07, 2026 (T day).
- Settlement Number: 2026169 onwards.

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts clearing and settlement operations for participants dealing in overnight and liquid ETFs, ensuring certainty in handling delivery shortages.
