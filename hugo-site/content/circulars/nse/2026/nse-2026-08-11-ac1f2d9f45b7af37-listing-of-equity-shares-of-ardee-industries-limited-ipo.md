## Summary

This circular pertains to the listing of equity shares of Ardee Industries Limited (Symbol: ARDEE) on the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The shareholding pattern of the listed entity is filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, representing the post-issue shareholding structure. The total number of equity shares of the company stands at 31,52,00,950, with promoters holding 67.62% of the total share capital.

## Key Points

- **Listing Event**: Ardee Industries Limited is listing its equity shares on the exchange post its Initial Public Offering.
- **Total Shares**: The company has an outstanding equity capital of 31,52,00,950 fully paid-up equity shares.
- **Shareholding Pattern**:
  - **Promoters & Promoter Group**: Hold 21,31,50,700 shares, representing **67.62%** of the total equity.
  - **Public Shareholders**: Hold 10,20,50,250 shares, representing **32.38%** of the total equity.
  - **Locked-in Shares**: Out of the total shares, 25,89,54,584 shares (**82.16%**) are subject to lock-in requirements.
- **Differential Voting Rights / Significant Beneficial Owner (SBO)**: The company does not have differential voting rights. It has declared the presence of a Significant Beneficial Owner (SBO).

## Regulatory Changes

- Listing is carried out in accordance with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations and SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations.
- Filing of the shareholding pattern is executed under Regulation 31(1)(a) of the SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company and its promoters must comply with the locked-in share guidelines as prescribed under SEBI ICDR Regulations.
- Continuous disclosure of the shareholding pattern must be made as per the timelines prescribed by Regulation 31 of SEBI LODR Regulations in subsequent quarters.

## Important Dates

- **Shareholding Pattern Reference Date**: August 11, 2026 (Post-Issue representation).
- **Listing Date**: August 12, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity**: The listing introduces ARDEE to the secondary market, providing immediate liquidity and exit options to IPO investors and onboarding public shareholders.
- **Promoter Lock-in**: The high volume of locked-in shares (82.16%) ensures price stability and reduces near-term selling pressure from early investors and promoters.
