## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for several schemes of Canara Robeco Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **Fund House:** Canara Robeco Mutual Fund

### Scheme Name Changes:
- *Canara Robeco Savings Fund* renamed to *Canara Robeco Ultra Short to Short Term Fund*
- *Canara Robeco Short Duration Fund* renamed to *Canara Robeco Short Term Fund*
- *Canara Robeco Income Fund* renamed to *Canara Robeco Medium to Long Term Fund*
- *Canara Robeco Dynamic Bond Fund* renamed to *Canara Robeco Dynamic Term Fund*
- *Canara Robeco Equity Hybrid Fund* renamed to *Canara Robeco Aggressive Hybrid Fund*
- *Canara Robeco Infrastructure* renamed to *Canara Robeco Infrastructure Fund*
- *Canara Robeco ELSS Tax Saver* renamed to *Canara Robeco ELSS - Tax Saver Fund*

## Regulatory Changes

Changes are in accordance with SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026, pertaining to 'Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes'.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to note the updated scheme names for all future transactions and reporting on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 25, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a nomenclature and categorization update with no operational disruption or direct impact on existing unitholder values beyond name changes.
