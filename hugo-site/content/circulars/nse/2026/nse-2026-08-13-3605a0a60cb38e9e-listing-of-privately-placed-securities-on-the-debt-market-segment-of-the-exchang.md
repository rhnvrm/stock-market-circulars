## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from August 13, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 13, 2026
- Segment: Debt Market Segment
- Securities Listed: Commercial Papers (CP) from multiple issuers including Blue Star Limited, Tata Capital Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, NABARD, Godrej Properties Limited, Aseem Infrastructure Finance Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, and CEAT Limited.
- Allotment Date: August 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine administrative listing notification in compliance with Exchange regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and issuers must ensure adherence to the designated security codes and trading regulations specified for the debt segment.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 12-Aug-2026
- Listing/Effective Date: 13-Aug-2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from November 2026 across respective issuances.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for the newly listed commercial papers on the exchange's debt platform.
