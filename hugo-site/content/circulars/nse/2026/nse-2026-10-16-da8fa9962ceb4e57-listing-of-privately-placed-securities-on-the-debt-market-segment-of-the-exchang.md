## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the admission of privately placed commercial papers issued by several companies to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Privately placed commercial papers for various issuers have been approved for listing and trading on the debt market segment.
- Issuers include Axis Securities Limited, The Ramco Cements Limited, UltraTech Cement Limited, Blue Star Limited, Godrej Agrovet Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Tata Steel Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, and Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited.
- Allotment date for the securities is October 15, 2026, with varying maturity and redemption dates ranging from October 2026 to April 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard issuance and listing notification pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and trading members must ensure adherence to standard settlement, depository, and reporting norms applicable to debt securities and commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Listing:** October 16, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** October 15, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** October 28, 2026, to April 13, 2027 (depending on specific security tranche)

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for the listed commercial papers on the Exchange debt segment without direct impact on equity market operations.
