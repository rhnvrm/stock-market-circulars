## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced a revision to the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) as well as the position limits applicable for Participants, Institutional clients, and Non-Institutional clients for the symbol GOODLUCK under the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) Scheme. The revised limits take effect from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** GOODLUCK
- **Effective Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Market Wide Position Limit (Old vs New):** Increased from 1,528,965 shares to 4,586,896 shares.
- **Institutional Client Limit (Old vs New):** Increased from 152,896 shares to 458,689 shares.
- **Participant Limit (Old vs New):** Increased from 152,896 shares to 458,689 shares.
- **Non-Institutional Client Limit (Old vs New):** Increased from 15,289 shares to 45,868 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

Position limits for the security GOODLUCK in the SLB scheme have been updated following earlier circulars (NCL/CMPT/73999 and NCL/CMPT/75530).

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians must take note of the revised limits and ensure compliance while executing transactions in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Allows higher participation limits for trading members, institutional investors, and non-institutional clients in the SLB segment for GOODLUCK in line with updated market metrics.
