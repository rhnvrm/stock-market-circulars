## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the availability of the DSP Financial Services Sectoral Debt Fund NFO of DSP Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform for transactions.

## Key Points

- **Subscription Period:** August 19, 2026 to August 24, 2026 (till 11:45 PM).
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Schemes Available:** Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- **Allotment Date:** August 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 26, 2026 till 11:59 PM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** August 19, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** August 24, 2026 (11:45 PM)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** August 26, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard product launch enabling market participants and investors to subscribe to the new debt fund offering through NSE infrastructure.
