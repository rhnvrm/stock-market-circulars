## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Vishal Nirmiti Limited (IPO). The security will trade under the symbol VNL with ISIN INE1O5L01025.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vishal Nirmiti Limited
- Symbol: VNL
- ISIN: INE1O5L01025
- Department: Listing
- Download Ref No: NSE/CML/76762
- Circular Ref. No: 1804/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are informed of the forthcoming listing; the exact date of listing will be communicated via a separate circular.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 07, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular.
