## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has notified the suspension of trading in privately placed non-convertible securities for Creamline Dairy Products Limited, LIC Housing Finance Limited, and Canara Bank, effective October 08, 2026, due to redemption.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects non-convertible securities (privately placed) of three issuers.
- Creamline Dairy Products Limited (ISIN: INE412L08029) suspended effective October 08, 2026.
- LIC Housing Finance Limited (ISIN: INE115A07KS6) suspended effective October 08, 2026.
- Canara Bank (ISIN: INE476A08126) suspended effective October 08, 2026.
- Reason for suspension in all cases is redemption.
- Action executed pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational procedure reflecting debt security redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the removal of these securities from active trading on the debt segment.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 07, 2026
- Suspension/Redemption Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Negligible market impact as the suspension is due to maturity/redemption of the debt instruments.
