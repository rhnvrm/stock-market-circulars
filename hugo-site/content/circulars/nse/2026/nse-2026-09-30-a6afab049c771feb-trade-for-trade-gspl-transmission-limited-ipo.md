## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that securities of GSPL Transmission Limited will be transferred from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) effective October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities affected: GSPL Transmission Limited (Symbol: GSPLTRANS)
- Transfer from trade for trade segment (series: BE) to rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective date: October 15, 2026
- Reference to previous Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76590 dated September 28, 2026 and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI guidelines (circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012), IPO shares are initially traded in the trade-for-trade segment before moving to the rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants need to note the change in trading series for GSPLTRANS starting from October 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026
- Previous Circular Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Allows normal rolling settlement for GSPL Transmission Limited shares, improving liquidity and trading convenience.
