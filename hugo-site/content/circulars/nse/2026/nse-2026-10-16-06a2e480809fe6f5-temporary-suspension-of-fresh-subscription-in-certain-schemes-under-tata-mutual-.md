## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, lumpsum investments, switch-ins, top-ups, and new Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) or Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) registrations in Tata Gold ETF Fund of Fund under Tata Mutual Fund, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Affected Schemes:** Tata Gold ETF Fund of Fund (Tata Gold ETF FOF).
- **Restricted Transactions:** Lumpsum purchases, fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, top-ups, new SIP registrations, and new STP registrations.
- **Unaffected Transactions:** Redemptions, switch-outs, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), and existing SIP/STP instalments registered prior to the effective date.
- **Effective Date:** October 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary restriction implemented by Tata Mutual Fund on specific scheme subscriptions through the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note that fresh applications/registrations for the affected scheme will not be processed during the suspension period.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 16, 2026.
- **Addendum Date:** October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors looking to make fresh lump-sum investments or start new SIPs/STPs in Tata Gold ETF Fund of Fund will be temporarily restricted, while existing investors can continue existing SIP/STP/SWP and redemption operations without disruption.
