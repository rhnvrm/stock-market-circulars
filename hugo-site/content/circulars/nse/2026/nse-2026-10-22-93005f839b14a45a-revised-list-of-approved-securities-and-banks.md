## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued the revised list of approved securities, corporate bonds, and empaneled banks for collateral and liquid assets, effective for the month of November 2026. This supersedes the previous circular no. 017/2026 dated September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Period:** Valid for the month of November 2026.
- **Annexure 1:** List of Sovereign Gold Bonds, GOI Securities (G-Sec) / T-Bills forming part of the cash component of liquid assets with applicable haircuts.
- **Annexure 2:** List of Corporate Bonds (non-cash component of liquid assets) with market-wide limits and haircuts.
- **Annexure 3:** List of banks empaneled for issuing Bank Guarantees (BGs) and Fixed Deposit Receipts (FDRs).
- **G-Sec / T-Bills Shut Period:** Has a shut period of two days; members are requested to release the G-Sec/T-Bills at least two days before maturity.
- **SLR Restriction:** G-Sec provided as collaterals should not be reckoned for SLR purpose of the banks and not be used for trading.

## Regulatory Changes

Updates the monthly list of eligible collaterals, haircut rates, and empaneled banks for risk management in the debt segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members/Custodians/PCMs must adhere to the revised haircut rates and approved securities lists.
- Release G-Sec/T-Bills at least two days prior to maturity.
- Ensure member banks do not reckon collateral G-Secs for SLR or trading.

## Important Dates

- **Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Effective Month:** November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is routine for monthly collateral rebalancing and haircut adjustments across trading members and custodians.
