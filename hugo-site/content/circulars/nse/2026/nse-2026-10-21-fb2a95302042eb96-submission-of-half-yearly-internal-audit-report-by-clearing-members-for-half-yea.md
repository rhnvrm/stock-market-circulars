## Summary

NSE has issued a manual outlining the procedure for submission of the half-yearly internal audit report by Clearing Members / Professional Clearing Members (PCM) for the half year ended September 30, 2026. The circular details steps for sub-user creation, portal login, internal auditor registration, and conflict of interest submissions across CM, FO, CD, and COM segments.

## Key Points

- Clearing Members need to create two sub-user IDs on the NCL Member Portal: one for member's submission (NCL Inspection role) and one for auditor's submission (Inspection - Internal Audit role).
- Members must register their Internal Auditors for each audit cycle via the "Auditor Registration" dropdown in the NCL Inspection module.
- Auditors receive login credentials via email to submit Annexure I, II, and V for CM, FO, CD, and COM segments.
- All registration fields are mandatory except the 'Expiry Date of COP'.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes mandatory digital portal workflow for registering internal auditors and submitting half-yearly internal audit reports for the period April 2026 to September 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing Members must create required sub-users with appropriate roles (NCL Inspection and Inspection - Internal Audit).
- Register internal auditors on the NCL Member Portal (https://ims.connect2nsccl.com/NCLMemberPortal/) and ensure correct email addresses are provided.
- Ensure internal auditors complete and submit Annexure I, II, and V for all applicable segments (CM, FO, CD, COM) for the half-year ended September 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Reporting Period: April 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines compliance verification and internal audit reporting for clearing members through a structured online portal mechanism, ensuring adherence to regulatory standards across all trading segments.
