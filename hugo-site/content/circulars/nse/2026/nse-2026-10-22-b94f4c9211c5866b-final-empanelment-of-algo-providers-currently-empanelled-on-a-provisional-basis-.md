## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the final empanelment of M/s Futures Unchained LLP as an algorithmic trading solution provider, upgrading their previous provisional empanelment granted via circular NSE/INVG/75504 dated July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Vendor Name: M/s Futures Unchained LLP
- Type of Algo: Whitebox
- Address: No 43, 1st main, 3rd Cross, Siddeshwara Layout, Nagasandra Post, Bangalore North, Bangalore- 560073, Karnataka, India
- Contact: +91 9902824662, nnfreg_support@nse.co.in, invg@nse.co.in
- Applicability: Immediate effect

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; provisional empanelment status updated to final upon condition fulfillment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members utilizing algorithmic trading solutions from the empanelled vendor can proceed in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members with verified access to Whitebox algorithmic trading solutions from M/s Futures Unchained LLP.
