## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 17, 2026. There is no new software version release for this session.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** October 17, 2026.
- **Segment:** Commodity Derivatives (CD).
- **Trading Timings (DR Site):** Normal market open at 14:00 hrs, close at 14:10 hrs.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades during the mock session will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- **NEAT Version:** 1.5.5.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 19, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.
- Set appropriate branch and user limits from the Corporate Manager Terminal before placing orders.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Session Date:** October 17, 2026
- **Live Re-login Timings:** 15:00 hrs to 15:30 hrs
- **Live Environment resumption:** October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. The mock session ensures connectivity and system readiness for members without any financial implications.
