## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for various securities satisfying the inclusion criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage I shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100% w.e.f. September 10, 2026 on all open positions as on September 09, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Applicable margin rate for Stage II shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. September 10, 2026 on all open positions as on September 09, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance actions on specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements on open positions and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Surveillance Measures: September 09, 2026 / September 10, 2026.
- Circular Date: September 08, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations for trading in the shortlisted securities, potentially impacting liquidity and volatility for the affected scrips.
