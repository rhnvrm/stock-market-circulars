## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment with effect from September 22, 2026. The circular covers fresh issues of securities from Godrej Housing Finance Limited and Godrej Industries Limited.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** September 22, 2026
- **Godrej Housing Finance Limited:** 8.4% 2031 Sr D1 (Symbol: 84GHFL31, ISIN: INE02JD07074, Maturity: 17-Sep-2031, Market Lot: 1)
- **Godrej Industries Limited:** 8.50% 2032 (Symbol: 850GIL32, ISIN: INE233A08220, Maturity: 18-Mar-2032, Market Lot: 1)
- Trading shall be identified strictly by designated codes and respective lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, securities are admitted to dealings and respective lot sizes are established.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure trading is conducted using the designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 21, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 22, 2026
- **Maturity Dates:** 17-Sep-2031 (Godrej Housing Finance) and 18-Mar-2032 (Godrej Industries)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine addition of privately placed debt instruments to the capital market segment, enabling secondary market liquidity for the specified NCDs.
