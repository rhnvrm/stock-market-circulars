## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a change in the cut-off timing for NAV applicability for the WhiteOak Capital Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FoF on the NSE MF Invest Platform, pursuant to communication received from WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 19, 2026.
- **Applicable Transactions:** Switch-In, STP-In, Redemption, SWP, Switch-Out, and STP-Out executed/received in the scheme, as well as subscription transactions (Lump Sum purchase and SIP).
- **Revised Cut-off Time:** The cut-off time for determining applicable NAV for valid applications/redemptions has been revised from 3:00 p.m. to 1:30 p.m.
- **Subscription Funds Requirement:** For subscription transactions, funds must be credited to the AMC collection account on or before 1:30 p.m. to avail the applicable NAV of the business day.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of cut-off timing for redemption, repurchases, and switch-outs from 3:00 p.m. to 1:30 p.m. at the Official Points of Acceptance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must ensure that valid applications and fund credits for subscription transactions are completed within the revised cut-off time of 1:30 p.m. to secure the applicable NAV for the business day.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Circular Date:** October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Low. Investors and trading members need to align their transaction processing timelines to the earlier cut-off time of 1:30 p.m. for the specified WhiteOak Capital scheme.
