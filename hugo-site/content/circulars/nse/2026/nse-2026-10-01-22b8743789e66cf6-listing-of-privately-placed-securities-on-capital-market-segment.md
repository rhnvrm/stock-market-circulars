## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on its capital market segment, effective from October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective October 05, 2026.
- Issuers include Sundaram Home Finance Limited, Nabkisan Finance Limited, Western Capital Advisors Private Limited, and Reliance Industries Limited.
- Trading will take place under designated security codes and specified market lots.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in these securities must adhere strictly to the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 05, 2026
- Circular Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands investment and trading options in debt instruments on the capital market segment.
