## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Purple Style Labs Limited (IPO) on the Capital Market segment with effect from September 07, 2026. The security will be identified by the symbol PERNIASPOP.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Purple Style Labs Limited
- Symbol: PERNIASPOP
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE0I1B01016
- Issue Price: Rs. 575/-
- Face Value: Rs. 10/-
- Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- No. of Securities: 80,061,086
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the above security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per SEBI circular no. CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 04, 2026
- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Purple Style Labs Limited equity shares on the NSE Capital Market segment for investors and trading members.
