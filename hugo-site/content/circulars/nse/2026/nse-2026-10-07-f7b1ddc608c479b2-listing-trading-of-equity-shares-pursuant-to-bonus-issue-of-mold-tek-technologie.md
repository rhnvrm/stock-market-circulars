## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and trading of equity shares of Mold-Tek Technologies Limited pursuant to its bonus issue. The securities will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mold-Tek Technologies Limited
- Symbol: MOLDTECH
- ISIN: INE835B01035
- Number of Securities: 2,88,05,118 equity shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 28805119 to 57610236
- Record Date: October 09, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: October 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; the circular relates to standard capital change and security admission under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that the security is identified by its designated codes for trading on the system effective from the operational date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 09, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date for Listing & Trading: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increases the total listed equity base of Mold-Tek Technologies Limited on the exchange, enhancing liquidity and reflecting the corporate bonus action.
