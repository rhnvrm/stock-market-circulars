## Summary

M/s. Quantiply Technologies Private Limited, previously empanelled as an Algo Provider on a provisional basis for White Box Algorithmic Trading Solutions, is now empanelled for providing Black Box Algorithmic Trading Solutions as well.

## Key Points

- Vendor Name: M/s. Quantiply Technologies Private Limited
- Type of Algo: Whitebox And Blackbox
- Correspondence Address: Plot 344, Dharmashi Bhuvan, 4th Floor, Chandavarkar Cross Road, Matunga (E), Mumbai- 400019
- Contact: +919920266899 / nnfreg@nse.co.in, invg@nse.co.in
- Effective Date: Immediate effect

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework change; update to the list of empanelled algorithmic trading solution providers.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members intending to use Black Box Algorithmic Trading Solutions from the vendor may refer to the updated list on the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Expands the availability of empanelled Black Box algorithmic trading solutions for trading members through Quantiply Technologies Private Limited.
