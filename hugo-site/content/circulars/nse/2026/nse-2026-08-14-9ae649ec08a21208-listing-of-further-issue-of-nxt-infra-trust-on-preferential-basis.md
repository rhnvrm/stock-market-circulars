## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further units of Nxt-Infra Trust issued on a preferential basis, effective from August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: NXT-INFRA
- Series: IV
- ISIN: INE0SF023016
- Issue Price: Rs. 95
- Number of Units Allotted: 16,65,43,895
- Distinctive Number Range: 358700001 to 525243895
- Date of Allotment: 13-Aug-2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Units are subject to lock-in requirements as specified in Annexure A of the circular, with lock-in expiry dates of 14-Aug-2027 and 14-Aug-2029 for respective quantities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026
- Allotment Date: August 13, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry: August 14, 2027 and August 14, 2029

## Impact Assessment

Addition of preferential units to regular trading segment for Nxt-Infra Trust.
