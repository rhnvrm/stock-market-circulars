## Summary

NSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The shareholding pattern details post-issue indicate a total of 76,98,43,411 equity shares with promoter and promoter group holding 79.51% and public holding 20.49%.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited
- Total Equity Shares: 76,98,43,411
- Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 61,21,27,347 shares (79.51%)
- Public Holding: 15,77,16,064 shares (20.49%)
- Outstanding ESOPs reported as YES; locked-in shares applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal admission and commencement of trading for equity shares of Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited on the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and submission of specified shareholding patterns under Reg. 31(1)(a).

## Important Dates

- Listing / Effective Date: August 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited equity shares on the NSE platform.
