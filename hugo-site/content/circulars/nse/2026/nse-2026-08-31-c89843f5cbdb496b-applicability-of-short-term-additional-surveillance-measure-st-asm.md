## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for 15 securities based on specific surveillance criteria effective September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- 15 securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I.
- No securities were reported under ST-ASM Stage II.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100% w.e.f. September 02, 2026.
- Surveillance measures are in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework provisions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure that the applicable margin of 50% (or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%) is maintained on all open positions as on September 01, 2026, and new positions created from September 02, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026 / September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for trading in the shortlisted securities, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for market participants trading in these scrips.
