## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission for dealing of further issue of equity shares of Bharti Airtel Limited effective from August 19, 2026. A total of 4,60,340 equity shares resulting from the conversion of partly paid-up shares to fully paid-up shares are being listed.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bharti Airtel Limited
- Symbol: BHARTIARTL
- ISIN: INE397D01024 (Temporary ISIN: IN8397D01015)
- Number of Securities: 4,60,340 equity shares
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/-
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 19, 2026
- Circular Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing of converted shares with no immediate operational disruption.
