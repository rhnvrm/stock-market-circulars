## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the revision of the allotment date for the WhiteOak Capital Dividend Yield Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- The allotment date for WhiteOak Capital Dividend Yield Fund NFO has been revised from August 31, 2026, to August 28, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations authentication must be completed by clients before 11:59 PM on the NFO closure date.
- Clear funds through any payment mode must be received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 27, 2026, by 06:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds are received by the specified deadline and that client SIP/XSIP registrations are authenticated appropriately.

## Important Dates

- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** August 27, 2026, 06:00 PM
- **Revised Allotment Date:** August 28, 2026
- **Previous Allotment Date:** August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact restricted to investors and members participating in the WhiteOak Capital Dividend Yield Fund NFO, requiring adherence to the accelerated timelines.
