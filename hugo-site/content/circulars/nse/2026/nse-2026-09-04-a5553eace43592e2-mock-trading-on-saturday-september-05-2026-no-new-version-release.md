## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) on Saturday, September 05, 2026. The session aims to test system readiness, connectivity, and disaster recovery procedures.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, September 05, 2026.
- Trading Session-1 from Primary Site: Normal market open at 09:00 hrs, contingency start at 09:45 hrs, contingency close at 11:00 hrs, normal market close at 11:30 hrs.
- Trading Session-2 from DR Site: Normal market open at 12:30 hrs, normal market close at 13:00 hrs.
- Live Re-login timings: Start time 18:00 hrs, close time 18:30 hrs.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session; members using NNF software must clear orders manually.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine mock trading session for operational readiness.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, September 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Mock Date: September 05, 2026
- Live resumption date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No direct financial impact; helps ensure operational continuity and robust connectivity for trading members.
