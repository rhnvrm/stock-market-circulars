## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the movement of securities to Stage I of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) effective October 09, 2026. Ankit Metal & Power Limited has been moved to Stage I surveillance.

## Key Points

- Security moved: Ankit Metal & Power Limited (Symbol: ANKITMETAL, ISIN: INE106I01010).
- Surveillance stage: Stage I of Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM).
- Effective date: October 09, 2026.
- Applicable restrictions: 100% margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.

## Regulatory Changes

- Ankit Metal & Power Limited is subjected to Stage I GSM surveillance framework actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin rate and 5% or lower price band applicability while trading in ANKITMETAL.
- Adequate precautions should be taken by trading members.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 09, 2026.
- Circular Date: October 08, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading for ANKITMETAL will face strict price bands (5% or lower) and 100% margin requirements, mitigating excessive price volatility and safeguarding market integrity.
