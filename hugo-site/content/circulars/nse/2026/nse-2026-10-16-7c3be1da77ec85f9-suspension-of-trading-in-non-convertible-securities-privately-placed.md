## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the suspension of trading in privately placed non-convertible securities of Alpha Alternatives Financial Services Private Limited due to redemption.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Alpha Alternatives Financial Services Private Limited
- ISIN: INE0L6807096
- Suspension Date: October 19, 2026
- Reason: Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the suspension of trading in the specified non-convertible securities.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine suspension resulting from the redemption of debt securities with no broader market impact.
