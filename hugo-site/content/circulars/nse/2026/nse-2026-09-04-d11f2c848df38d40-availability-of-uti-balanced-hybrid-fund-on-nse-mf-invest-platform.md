## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund schemes from UTI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.

## Key Points

- Schemes will be reopened for ongoing transactions effective September 07, 2026.
- Covers Regular and Direct plans for Growth and Payout IDCW options.
- Facilities include SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can transact in the specified schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment access and liquidity options for investors in UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund through the NSE platform.
