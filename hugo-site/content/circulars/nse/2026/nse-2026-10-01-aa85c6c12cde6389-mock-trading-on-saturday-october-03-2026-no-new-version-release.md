## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a mock trading session in the Capital Market segment on Saturday, October 03, 2026. The session will operate across both Primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites with structured timings for pre-open, normal market, block deal windows, and closing sessions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for October 03, 2026.
- Trading conducted in two sessions from Primary and DR sites.
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 to be used for CAS, while existing version 7.8.9 is scheduled for discontinuation on October 10, 2026 (mock).
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- No financial obligations arise from mock trading.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Operational notification regarding market testing and connectivity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must review connectivity parameters, installation guides, and software versions as specified in previous circulars (NSE/MSD/73993 and NSE/MSD/74511).

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 03, 2026
- NEAT+ 7.8.9 Discontinuation Date: October 10, 2026 (mock)

## Impact Assessment

Low impact. Enables members to test systems and connectivity without real financial risk.
