## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities—including commercial papers and other debt securities—on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Privately placed securities specified in the Annexure admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment effective October 22, 2026.
- Fresh issues of Commercial Papers (CPs) listed for companies including Indian Oil Corporation Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, NTPC Limited, IIFL Capital Services Limited, and Hero FinCorp Limited.
- Fresh issues of Other Debt Securities listed for Akme Fintrade (India) Limited.
- Re-issued Commercial Papers listed for IIFL Finance Limited.
- Allotment date for the securities is October 21, 2026 (and October 19, 2026 for Akme Fintrade).

## Regulatory Changes

- Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and issuers must adhere to NSE debt market trading, clearing, and settlement norms for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Dates: October 19, 2026 and October 21, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 22, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Varying maturity dates ranging from November 2026 through April 2029.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances liquidity and transparency by enabling secondary market trading of privately placed corporate debt instruments and commercial papers.
