## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Prasol Chemicals Limited pursuant to its IPO, effective September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: PRASOLCHEM
- ISIN: INE455U01024
- Issue Price: Rs. 676/- per share
- Face Value/Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/-
- No. of Securities: 59,183,431 equity shares
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular Annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Permits trading of Prasol Chemicals Limited equity shares on the NSE Capital Market segment.
