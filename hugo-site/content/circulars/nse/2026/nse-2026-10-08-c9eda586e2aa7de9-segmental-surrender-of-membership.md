## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has approved the request of Finwizard Technology Private Limited for the segmental surrender of membership in the Currency Derivatives segment including IRF, effective from October 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Trading Member:** Finwizard Technology Private Limited
- **SEBI Registration Number:** INZ000209036
- **Surrendered Segment:** Currency Derivatives including IRF
- **Approval Date:** October 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative update regarding the surrender of a specific trading segment by a member.

## Compliance Requirements

All members are requested to take note of the segmental surrender by the aforementioned trading member.

## Important Dates

- **Surrender Approval Date:** October 07, 2026
- **Circular Date:** October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to the cessation of trading operations by the member in the surrendered Currency Derivatives segment.
