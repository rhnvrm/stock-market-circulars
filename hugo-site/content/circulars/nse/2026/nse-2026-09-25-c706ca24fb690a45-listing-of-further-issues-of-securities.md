## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies with effect from September 28, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Securities further admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective September 28, 2026.
- Includes shares allotted under ESOP and preferential issues for companies such as Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited, Antelopus Selan Energy Limited, Emkay Global Financial Services Limited, ESAF Small Finance Bank Limited, Hindustan Foods Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, Kopran Limited, Lloyds Engineering Works Limited, NIIT Learning Systems Limited, and Oracle Financial Services Software Limited.
- Trading will be conducted in specified lot sizes identified by designated security codes.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Member brokers and market participants must ensure adherence to designated security codes and lot sizes for trading the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 28, 2026
- Allotment dates vary by company (e.g., September 21, 2026 for Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC and Antelopus Selan Energy; July 13, 2026 for Emkay Global).

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the respective listed entities without creating any broad market or operational disruption.
