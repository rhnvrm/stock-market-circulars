## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Axiom Gas Engineering Limited pursuant to its SME IPO, along with the submission of its post-issue shareholding pattern as on September 23, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: Axiom Gas Engineering Limited
- Scrip Symbol: AXIOMGAS
- Class of Security: Equity Shares
- Shareholding Pattern Filing Basis: Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 as on 23/09/2026
- Locked-in shares are applicable as part of SME IPO requirements.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard compliance disclosure for newly listed SME securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to statutory disclosure norms under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, including lock-in provisions for specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / As on Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the formal commencement of trading for Axiom Gas Engineering Limited on the NSE SME exchange platform.
