## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for shortlisted securities. An applicable rate of margin of 100% shall be levied w.e.f. October 28, 2026, on all open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I w.e.f. October 26, 2026:
  - Moxsh Overseas Educon Limited (MOXSH - INE0N6D01014)
  - NINtec Systems Limited (NINSYS - INE395U01014)
  - Rudrabhishek Enterprises Limited (REPL - INE364Z01019)
- Applicable rate of margin: 100% w.e.f. October 28, 2026, on all open positions as on October 27, 2026 and new positions created from October 28, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- ASM shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Inclusion of specific scrips into the Long-Term ASM framework, enforcing stringent margin requirements and potential settlement segment migration.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that a 100% margin is maintained on all open and new positions for the shortlisted securities effective October 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 23, 2026
- Shortlisting Effective Date: October 26, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: October 27, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in the shortlisted securities will experience higher capital requirements due to the 100% margin rule, which may affect liquidity and trading volumes for these scrips.
