## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of Triveni Power Transmission Limited from the trade-for-trade segment (Series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) with effect from October 27, 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines and previous exchange circulars.

## Key Points

- Security Symbol: TRIVENIPT
- Company Name: Triveni Power Transmission Limited
- Transfer Segment: From trade-for-trade segment (Series: BE) to rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: October 27, 2026
- Reference Circulars: NSE/CML/76805 dated October 08, 2026, and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of the security from trade-for-trade to rolling settlement based on regulatory surveillance and compliance criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure trading systems are updated for the change in series (from BE to EQ) effective October 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for Triveni Power Transmission Limited shares, facilitating regular trading and improved liquidity for market participants.
