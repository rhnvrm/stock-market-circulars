## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) segment on Saturday, October 17, 2026. There is no new version release for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock Date: October 17, 2026.
- Normal Market open time: 12:45 hrs; Normal Market close time: 14:00 hrs (Trading session from DR Site).
- Live Re-login start time: 15:00 hrs; Live Re-login close time: 15:30 hrs.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session; NNF users must manually clear orders.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.
- NOTIS application will be accessible during the mock trading session.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine operational mock trading session conducted in compliance with standard market requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 19, 2026.
- Ensure telnet connectivity to Exchange host and verify Box ID setup.
- Set appropriate branch and user limits from the Corporate Manager Terminal.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 15, 2026
- Mock Trading Date: October 17, 2026
- Live Environment Resume Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. The session is designed to ensure seamless connectivity and system robustness for members ahead of regular trading days without any financial obligation.
