## Summary

National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and new SIP/STP registrations for specific schemes under Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Schemes affected: Kotak Gold Fund and Kotak Gold Silver Passive FOF.
- Effective date: October 16, 2026.
- Restrictions apply to fresh/additional lump sum investments, switch-in transactions, and new SIP/STP inflows (including Step up in existing SIP).
- No restrictions on Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), Switch-out, and STP out.
- Existing SIP/STP registrations prior to October 16, 2026, will continue and be processed at applicable NAV.
- Purchase or switch-in transactions timestamped on or before 3:00 PM on October 15, 2026, are accepted and processed.

## Regulatory Changes

Temporary operational restriction on subscription inflows for select Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund schemes as communicated by the AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the suspension of fresh subscriptions and new SIP/STP registrations for the specified Kotak Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- Cut-off for prior transactions: October 15, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Effective date of suspension: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors wishing to make fresh investments, new SIPs, or switch-ins into Kotak Gold Fund and Kotak Gold Silver Passive FOF will be temporarily restricted on the NSE MF Invest Platform until further notice, while existing systematic plans and redemptions remain unaffected.
