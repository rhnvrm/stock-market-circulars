## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives (F&O) segment on Saturday, September 12, 2026, across primary and disaster recovery (DR) sites.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, September 12, 2026, with two trading sessions from primary and DR sites.
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 must be used explicitly for the revised Pre-Open framework.
- Existing NEAT+ exe version 7.8.9 will be discontinued on October 10, 2026 (mock).
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Tuesday, September 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Mock Date: September 12, 2026
- Discontinuation of NEAT+ version 7.8.9: October 10, 2026 (mock)
- Live Re-login: September 12, 2026, 18:00 hrs to 18:30 hrs

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational check for members to ensure trading system readiness and connectivity.
