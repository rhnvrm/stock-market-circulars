## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing members about the availability and reopening of Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund's Apex Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund and Apex Equity Long-Short Fund under the Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) category on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes from Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund made available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: September 01, 2026.
- Included Schemes & ISINs:
  - Apex Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund - Regular Growth (INF209K30081)
  - Apex Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund - Direct Growth (INF209K30073)
  - Apex Equity Long-Short Fund - Regular Growth (INF209K30107)
  - Apex Equity Long-Short Fund - Direct Growth (INF209K30099)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the availability of these schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform accessibility for trading members and investors to transact in the specified mutual fund schemes.
