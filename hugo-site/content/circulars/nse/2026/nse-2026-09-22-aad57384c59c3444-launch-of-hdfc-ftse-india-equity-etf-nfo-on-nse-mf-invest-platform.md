## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for HDFC FTSE India Equity ETF schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Names:** HDFC FTSE India Equity ETF - Regular Growth & Direct Growth
- **ISIN:** INF179KC1LA2
- **Category:** Equity
- **NFO Window:** September 23, 2026 to October 7, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** October 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through valid payment modes are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 12, 2026, up to 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open:** September 23, 2026
- **NFO Close:** October 7, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** October 12, 2026 (03:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates trading and subscription of the HDFC FTSE India Equity ETF NFO for eligible members and investors on the NSE MF platform.
