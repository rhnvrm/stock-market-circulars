## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Moneyview Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), effective October 01, 2026. The shares will be traded on the Capital Market segment under the symbol MONEYVIEW.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Moneyview Limited
- Symbol: MONEYVIEW
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE0PTN01011
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Re. 1/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 34/-
- Total No. of Securities: 1,760,231,268 equity shares
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of securities to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the security shall be identified by its designated codes in specified lot sizes.
- The security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips as per SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.
- Adherence to lock-in details and distinctive number ranges as outlined in Annexure I of the circular.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2026
- Financial Year: April 2026 - March 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates public market trading and liquidity for equity shares of Moneyview Limited on NSE.
