## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The scheme will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities starting September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Index Fund - Direct Plan Growth & Regular Plan Growth
- ISINs: INF789F1AH67 (Direct Plan), INF789F1AH59 (Regular Plan)
- Category: Equity Index Fund
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, SWP

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants using the NSE MF Invest Platform can facilitate transactions for the aforementioned schemes as per standard operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date for Platform Availability: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with access to the UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Index Fund on the NSE MF platform with full operational facilities including SIP, STP, and SWP.
