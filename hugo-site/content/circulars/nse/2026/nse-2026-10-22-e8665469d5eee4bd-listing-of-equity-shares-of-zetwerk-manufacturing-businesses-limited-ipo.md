## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), with trading effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: ZETWERK
- Company Name: Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE09PO01019
- Face Value & Paid-up Value: Re. 1/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 151/- per share
- Total Number of Securities: 1,694,254,778 equity shares
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment of NSE in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Respective lot sizes specified under Regulation 2.5.5.
- Included in the pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must identify the security by its designated code (ZETWERK) and trade in the specified market lot of 1.
- Share lock-in details and distinctive number ranges apply as specified in Annexure I of the circular.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: October 23, 2026
- Lock-in expiry dates vary ranging from November 19, 2026 to April 22, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides public market liquidity and official exchange-traded status to Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited shares following its successful IPO.
