## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding updated trading parameters for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) w.e.f September 07, 2026, based on SEBI circulars dated June 15, 2026, and August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date**: September 07, 2026.
- **Segment**: Capital Market Segment, Normal (N) market type.
- **Base Price**: Determined using T-1 day closing price (last 30 minutes VWAP), LTP if no trading in the last 30 minutes, or latest available closing NAV if no trade on T-1 day.
- **Tick Size**: Rs 0.05 for new Gold ETFs; Rs 0.01 for other than Gold ETFs (existing Gold ETFs with Rs 0.01/0.05 continue).
- **Price Bands**: 
  - Equity & Debt ETFs (excl. Overnight/Liquid): Initial +10%, flexed up to +20% after cooling-off period.
  - Overnight & Liquid ETFs: Fixed +5%.
  - Commodity ETFs (Gold/Silver): Initial +6%, flexed by 3% after cooling-off period.
- **Market Sessions**: Pre-Open (Commodity ETFs only), Block Trading, Continuous, and Closing sessions.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised norms for Base Price, Price Bands, Call Auction in pre-open session, and Close-out procedures for ETFs as directed by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to load the updated `security.gz` and `NSE_CM_security_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz` files from the Extranet server (`cmftp/common/ntneat`) or website path into their trading applications prior to trading from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: September 01, 2026
- **Effective Date**: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members dealing in ETFs must update their trading systems and parameters to comply with the new base price determination, tick sizes, and dynamic/fixed price band structures.
