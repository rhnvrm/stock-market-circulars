## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, August 29, 2026. There is no new version release associated with this session.

## Key Points

- Mock Date: August 29, 2026.
- Trading Session-1 from Primary Site runs with pre-open starting at 12:45 IST and normal market closing at 15:15 IST.
- Live Re-login timings: Start time 18:30 IST, Close time 19:00 IST.
- NEAT+ version details: Version 1.0.0 (Download path: /egftp/egcommon/NEATPlus100).
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine mock trading operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to participate in the mock trading session to test connectivity and trading parameters. Default password for login is "Neat@EG1".

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact; operational readiness check for trading members in the EGR segment.
