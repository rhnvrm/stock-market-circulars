## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the revised list of approved securities and banks that form part of the non-cash component of liquid assets for the Currency Derivatives segment, specifying permitted quantities and haircut details.

## Key Points

- Contains the updated list of equity shares forming part of the non-cash component of liquid assets.
- Specifies overall permitted quantities across all segments and member-wise permitted quantities for the Currency Derivatives segment.
- Applicable haircut for the listed securities is based on VaR.
- Value-based prudential norms specify that exclusion of a pledged security should not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.

[Details of regulatory changes if any]

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must ensure adherence to the specified permitted quantities and monitor margin shortfalls within the 25% prudential norm threshold when excluding pledged securities.

## Important Dates

Effective immediately as per the circular issuance.

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading members and clearing members utilizing equity shares as non-cash collateral in the Currency Derivatives segment.
