## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of SS Retail Limited (IPO). Members are informed that the security details including symbol SSRETAIL and ISIN INE1EQW01028 have been assigned.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SS Retail Limited
- Symbol: SSRETAIL
- ISIN: INE1EQW01028
- Listing date to be announced via a separate circular.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the upcoming listing and ensure necessary operational readiness.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 22, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced separately

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading in the equity shares of SS Retail Limited upon official listing date notification.
