## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of the Motilal Oswal Nifty Midcap150 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window opens on October 12, 2026, and closes on October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Motilal Oswal Nifty Midcap150 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund (Direct Growth & Regular Growth)
- ISINs: INF247L01HQ6 (Direct Growth), INF247L01HR4 (Regular Growth)
- NFO Period: October 12, 2026 to October 26, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational circular regarding product availability on the exchange trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure clear funds through approved payment modes are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 30, 2026, till 02:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: October 12, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 26, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Clear Funds Cut-off: October 30, 2026 (02:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to the new hybrid index fund combining midcap equity and government securities on the NSE MF platform.
