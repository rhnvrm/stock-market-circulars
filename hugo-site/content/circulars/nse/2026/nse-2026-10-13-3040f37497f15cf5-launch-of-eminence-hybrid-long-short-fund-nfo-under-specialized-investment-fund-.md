## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the availability of the Eminence Hybrid Long-Short Fund NFO under the Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) category by AlphaGrep Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window runs from October 14, 2026, to October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available include Direct and Regular plans for Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout.
- Fund House: AlphaGrep Mutual Fund.
- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Subscription Period: October 14, 2026 to October 28, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- Allotment Date: November 4, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 29, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: October 14, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 28, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 29, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: November 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational availability for trading members to facilitate investor subscriptions in the new fund offer.
