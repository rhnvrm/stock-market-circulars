## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the movement of securities to Stage IV of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM). Effective September 17, 2026, Univa Foods Limited (Symbol: UNIVAFOODS, ISIN: INE275F01019) is being moved from its previous stage to Stage IV of the GSM framework due to ongoing surveillance reviews.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** September 17, 2026.
- **Security Affected:** Univa Foods Limited (Symbol: UNIVAFOODS, ISIN: INE275F01019).
- **Surveillance Measure:** Stage IV of Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM).
- **Previous Circular References:** NSE/SURV/34262, NSE/SURV/38389, NSE/SURV/42790, NSE/SURV/59425, NSE/SURV/64066, NSE/SURV/74254 and NSE/SURV/76204.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in UNIVAFOODS will be subject to strict surveillance actions under Stage IV guidelines:
- Trade-for-trade settlement basis with no netting off allowed.
- Price band of 5% or lower as applicable.
- Trading permitted only once a week (every Monday / 1st trading day of the week).
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 100% of the trade value to be deposited by buyers with no upward movement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in the affected security.
- Settlement must be executed strictly on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off.
- Buyers must ensure 100% ASD is deposited as mandated.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 16, 2026
- **Effective Date of Movement:** September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading frequency for Univa Foods Limited will be severely constrained due to weekly trading limits, 100% ASD requirements, and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions. Investors and traders holding or trading the security will experience strict limitations on position unwinding and intraday execution.
