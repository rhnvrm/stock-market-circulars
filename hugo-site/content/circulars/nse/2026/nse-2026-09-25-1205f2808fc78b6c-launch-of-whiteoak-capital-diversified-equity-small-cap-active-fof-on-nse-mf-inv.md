## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the launch and transaction window for Whiteoak Capital Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription period runs from September 28, 2026 to October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available: Whiteoak Capital Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF Regular Growth and Direct Growth.
- ISINs: INF1J2R01197 (Regular Growth), INF1J2R01189 (Direct Growth).
- Transaction window on NSE MF Invest Platform: September 28, 2026 to October 12, 2026.
- Allotment Date: October 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational circular regarding a new mutual fund scheme launch.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 16, 2026, up to 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Subscription Start Date: September 28, 2026
- Subscription End Date: October 12, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: October 16, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to the new Whiteoak Capital fund scheme on the NSE MF platform with clear operational timelines.
