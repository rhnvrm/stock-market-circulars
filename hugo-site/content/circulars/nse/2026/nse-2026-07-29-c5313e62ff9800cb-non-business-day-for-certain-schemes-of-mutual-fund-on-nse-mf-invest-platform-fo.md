## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued circular reference `NSE/NMFTM/75439` (Circular Ref. No: `1524/2026`) dated July 29, 2026, notifying market participants of non-business days for designated schemes of Axis Mutual Fund and DSP Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform for the month of August 2026. Consequently, transaction activities including Subscriptions, Redemptions, Switches, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs will not be permitted for the affected schemes on these specific dates.

## Key Points

- **Suspension of Platform Transactions:** Subscriptions, Redemptions, Switches, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs will not be allowed on the NSE MF Invest platform on the specified non-business days.
- **Affected Mutual Funds & Schemes:** Stated schemes from Axis Mutual Fund and DSP Mutual Fund are affected.
- **Staggered Non-Business Days:** Depending on the scheme, the non-business days fall on August 3, August 26, or August 31, 2026.
- **ISIN Reference:** Detailed scheme names and their respective ISINs are listed in Annexure I of the circular.

## Regulatory Changes

This is an operational announcement based on communication from the respective Asset Management Companies (AMCs) and does not represent a change in regulatory policies or frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members & Distributors:** Must ensure that their front-end and back-end order management systems do not accept or forward transactions (including systematic instructions like SIP, STP, SWP) for the affected schemes on their designated non-business days on the NSE platform.
- **System Configurations:** Automated trading and order entry platforms must be updated with the scheme-specific non-business day calendar to prevent order rejection and handle client expectations.

## Important Dates

Based on Annexure I of the circular, the effective non-business days for the mutual fund schemes are:
- **August 3, 2026 (03.08.2026):** Non-business day for DSP Value Fund schemes (including Direct & Regular Growth/IDCW plans).
- **August 26, 2026 (26.08.2026):** Non-business day for DSP Global Clean Energy Overseas Equity Omni Fund of Fund schemes.
- **August 31, 2026 (31.08.2026):** Non-business day for Axis NASDAQ 100 US Specific Equity Passive FoF and Axis US Specific Treasury Dynamic Debt Passive FoF schemes.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Medium. Standard transactions on the NSE MF Invest platform will proceed as usual, except for the specified international and dynamic schemes on their respective non-business days.
- **Liquidity & Valuation:** Investors will experience a brief pause in liquidity and trading capability for the affected schemes on the respective non-business days. Orders placed during these times will be executed on the subsequent business day, subject to the applicable Net Asset Value (NAV).
