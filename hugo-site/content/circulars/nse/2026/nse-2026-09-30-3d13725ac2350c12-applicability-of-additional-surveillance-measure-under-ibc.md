## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective October 01, 2026. The circular details shortlistings, stage upgrades, and exclusions for specific securities based on corporate disclosures and IBC criteria.

## Key Points

- **New Shortlisting (Corporate Announcement/Disclosure):** Auri Grow India Limited (AURIGROW) has satisfied criteria under ASM for companies as per IBC.
- **Stage Upgrade:** Impex Ferro Tech Limited (IMPEXFERRO) has moved from Stage I to Stage II under the ASM IBC framework.
- **Exclusion/Movement:** Several securities including Aban Offshore Limited (ABAN), Ankit Metal & Power Limited (ANKITMETAL), Reliance Infrastructure Limited (RELINFRA), Simbhaoli Sugars Limited (SIMBHALS), and Vivimed Labs Limited (VIVIMEDLAB) are moving to receipt of corporate announcement/disclosure from the company as per IBC.
- **Framework Integration:** The ASM IBC framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Surveillance measures are applied or updated for companies undergoing insolvency and bankruptcy proceedings in accordance with established exchange circulars and regulatory guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance status of the listed securities and ensure compliance with trading restrictions and margin requirements associated with ASM stages.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Circular Date:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to the specified ASM IBC surveillance framework, potentially impacting liquidity, margin requirements, and trading monitoring for market participants.
