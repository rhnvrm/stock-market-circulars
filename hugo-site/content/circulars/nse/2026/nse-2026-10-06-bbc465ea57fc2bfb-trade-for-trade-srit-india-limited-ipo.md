## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced that securities of SRIT India Limited (IPO) will be transferred from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) effective October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SRIT India Limited
- Symbol: SRIT
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to SEBI guidelines (circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012) and further to Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76706 dated October 05, 2026, the security is moved to the rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members should note the change in segment and ensure adherence to rolling segment settlement procedures for transactions in SRIT India Limited.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for SRIT India Limited shares, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility for investors.
