## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Securities listed in the annexure have satisfied the criteria for inclusion or stage movement under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM).

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 16, 2026, on all open positions as on September 15, 2026, and new positions created from September 16, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. September 16, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company/entity.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) framework, including enhanced margin requirements and segment shifts where applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities and adhere to trade-for-trade settlement rules where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions: September 16, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date for Margin Applicability: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High impact on liquidity and trading operations for the specified securities due to 100% margin requirements and potential segment shifts.
