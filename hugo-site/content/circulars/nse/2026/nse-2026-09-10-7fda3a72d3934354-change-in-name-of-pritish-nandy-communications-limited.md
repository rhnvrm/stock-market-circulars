## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change of name for Pritish Nandy Communications Limited. Effective September 17, 2026, the company will operate under the new name PNC Media and Entertainment Limited while retaining its existing symbol.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Pritish Nandy Communications Limited
- New Company Name: PNC Media and Entertainment Limited
- Trading Symbol: PNC
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; update pertains strictly to corporate identity modification on the exchange trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records and trading systems to reflect the new company name.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating records with the new corporate name for the listed entity.
