## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I to certain securities effective October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for ST-ASM Stage I include Bombay Super Hybrid Seeds Limited (BSHSL), Hero Motors Limited (HEROMOTORS), and Modi Naturals Limited (MODINATUR).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. October 21, 2026, on all open positions as on October 19, 2026, and new positions created from October 21, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I surveillance measures and enhanced margin requirements on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with applicable margin rates (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open positions as on October 19, 2026, and new positions from October 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date for ST-ASM Framework: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Provisions: October 21, 2026 (on open positions as of October 19, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted scrips (BSHSL, HEROMOTORS, MODINATUR) may affect liquidity and trading volume for these securities during the surveillance period.
