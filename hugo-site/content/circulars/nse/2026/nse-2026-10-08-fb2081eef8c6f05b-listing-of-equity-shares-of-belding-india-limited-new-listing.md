## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Belding India Limited (Symbol: BELDING) with effect from October 12, 2026. A total of 14,478,849 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each fully paid up are admitted to trading.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Belding India Limited
- Symbol: BELDING
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE363L01045
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Number of Securities: 14,478,849
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 14478849
- Market Lot: 1
- Trading Segment: Normal Market segment (Rolling Settlement) in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of new equity securities to dealings on the National Stock Exchange Capital Market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the security shall be in compulsory demat mode with a market lot of 1. Lock-in details apply as specified in Annexure I of the circular (870,000 shares free, 3,619,713 shares locked in up to 14-Jul-2027, 9,989,136 shares free).

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Belding India Limited equity shares on the NSE Capital Market segment, expanding investment opportunities and providing liquidity to shareholders.
