## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Runwal Enterprises Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO), including the submission of the shareholding pattern under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: Runwal Enterprises Limited
- Security Class: Equity Shares (Post Issue)
- As on Date: October 1, 2026
- Shareholding pattern filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing notification and shareholding disclosure compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to ongoing disclosure and compliance norms as mandated under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the official trading and public market participation of Runwal Enterprises Limited equity shares on the exchange platform.
