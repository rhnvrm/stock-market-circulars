## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing all members about the reopening of Monarch Overnight Fund of Monarch Mutual Fund for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** September 10, 2026
- **Fund House:** Monarch Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** Monarch Overnight Fund (Various plans including Direct and Regular options for Growth, Daily IDCW, and Monthly IDCW)
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform

### Scheme Details and ISINs:
- Monarch Overnight Fund- Direct Daily IDCW - Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2ZSC01044)
- Monarch Overnight Fund- Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF2ZSC01028)
- Monarch Overnight Fund- Direct Monthly IDCW - Payout (ISIN: INF2ZSC01085)
- Monarch Overnight Fund- Direct Monthly IDCW - Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2ZSC01069)
- Monarch Overnight Fund- Regular Daily IDCW - Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2ZSC01036)
- Monarch Overnight Fund-Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF2ZSC01010)
- Monarch Overnight Fund- Regular Monthly IDCW - Payout (ISIN: INF2ZSC01077)
- Monarch Overnight Fund- Regular Monthly IDCW - Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2ZSC01051)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational circular regarding scheme availability on the trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can facilitate client transactions in the aforementioned schemes starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 09, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to additional liquid fund options on the NSE MF Invest platform with full support for systematic investment and withdrawal facilities.
