## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing of further issues of equity shares allotted under ESOP (Employee Stock Option Plan) for several companies, effective from October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Further issues of equity shares admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment of NSE.
- Effective date for trading: October 26, 2026.
- Companies involved include Antelopus Selan Energy Limited, Axis Bank Limited, Bandhan Bank Limited, Beta Drugs Limited, and others.
- Securities are identified by their designated codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; the notification is issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes as specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the respective listed entities without direct operational or market volatility impact.
