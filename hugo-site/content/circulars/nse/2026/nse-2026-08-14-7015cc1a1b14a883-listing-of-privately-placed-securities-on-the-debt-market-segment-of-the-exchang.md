## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on its debt market segment, effective from August 14, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted: Commercial Papers (CPs) from multiple corporate issuers.
- Effective Date of Dealings: August 14, 2026.
- Issuing Companies include Axis Securities Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, L&T Finance Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, CEAT Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, 5Paisa Capital Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited, and Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing notification under existing debt market regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and issuers must ensure adherence to the designated security codes and trading regulations specified for debt market segments.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 13, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: August 14, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from September 2026 to August 2027 depending on the specific commercial paper tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice enabling secondary market trading of issued commercial papers on the debt segment.
