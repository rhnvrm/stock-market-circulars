## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the recommencement of trading in the equity shares of **Burnpur Cement Limited** (Symbol: **BURNPUR**) with effect from **August 11, 2026**. This follows the capital reduction of the company implemented under the Resolution Plan approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Kolkata Bench, vide its order dated October 30, 2024. The equity shares will resume trading in the 'BE' series (Trade for Trade) with a face value of Rs. 10/- each.

## Key Points

- **Company Name**: Burnpur Cement Limited
- **Symbol**: BURNPUR
- **Series**: BE (Trade for Trade) - will subsequently shift to Series 'EQ' as per SEBI regulations.
- **ISIN**: INE817H01022
- **Face Value**: Rs. 10/- per share
- **Paid-up Value**: Rs. 10/- per share
- **No. of Securities**: 1,72,24,873 equity shares
- **Distinctive Number Range**: 1 to 17224873
- **Market Lot**: 1 share
- **Pari Passu**: Yes
- **Effective Date**: August 11, 2026 (Part of pre-open session on this date)

## Regulatory Changes

- **Capital Reduction**: Under the approved NCLT Resolution Plan, the entire issued and paid-up equity capital of Burnpur Cement Limited was reduced by 80% on a proportionate basis to write off accumulated losses.
- **Share Value Adjustments**: The paid-up value of each equity share was reduced from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/- per share, followed by consolidation or adjustments, resulting in post-capital-reduction equity shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid-up (1,72,24,873 shares).
- **Temporary ISIN**: Equity shares allotted in dematerialized mode have been credited under temporary ISIN (IN8*********) as per SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012 pending final ISIN activation.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Series**: The stock will initially be available for trading only under the 'BE' series (Trade for Trade segment), meaning every trade must result in delivery and no intraday net-off is permitted.
- **Transition**: The security will eventually be shifted to the 'EQ' series in accordance with SEBI guidelines.
- **Pre-Open Session**: Trading members must note that the security will participate in the pre-open session on August 11, 2026, as per SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Important Dates

- **NCLT Order Date**: October 30, 2024 (Kolkata Bench)
- **Circular Release Date**: August 07, 2026
- **Trading Recommencement / Effective Date**: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Shareholders**: Existing shareholders see an 80% reduction in their shareholding on a proportionate basis as part of the capital restructuring to write off accumulated losses.
- **Market Liquidity**: Resuming under the Trade for Trade ('BE') series will limit speculative trading and ensure 100% delivery-based settlement initially, reducing immediate volatility.
- **Corporate Health**: The completion of the NCLT-approved Resolution Plan and the recommencement of trading marks a critical step in the company's financial turnaround and restructuring process.
