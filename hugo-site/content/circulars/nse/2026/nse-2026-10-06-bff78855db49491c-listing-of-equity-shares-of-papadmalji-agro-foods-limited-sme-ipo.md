## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Papadmalji Agro Foods Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform effective from October 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Papadmalji Agro Foods Limited
- **Symbol:** PAPADMALJI
- **ISIN:** INE1V7901010
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 72/- per share
- **Total Securities:** 9,392,388 Equity Shares
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 1 to 9392388
- **Market Lot:** Minimum 1600 Equity Shares
- **Trading Series:** Series 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment – Settlement Type 'W') initially, with subsequent shift to Series 'SM' (Normal Rolling Segment – Settlement Type 'N').
- **Effective Date:** October 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission to dealings on the NSE EMERGE SME Platform in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Lot sizes determined under Regulation 2.5.5.
- Mandatory trade-for-trade surveillance segment compliance as per SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market maker requirements and lock-in details must be adhered to as per Annexure I of the circular.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 06, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** October 07, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** Spanned across October 15, 2027, October 15, 2028, and October 15, 2029 for specified promoter/pre-issue shares.

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and trading liquidity for Papadmalji Agro Foods Limited shares on the SME platform, impacting market participants trading in SME scrips.
