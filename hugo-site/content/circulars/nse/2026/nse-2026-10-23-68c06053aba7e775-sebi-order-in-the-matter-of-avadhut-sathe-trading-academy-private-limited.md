## Summary

SEBI has issued a final order against Avadhut Sathe Trading Academy Private Limited (ASTAPL), Avadhut Dinkar Sathe, and Gouri Avadhut Sathe for operating as unregistered investment advisers and research analysts, and for engaging in fraudulent and manipulative practices by publishing misleading and exaggerated trading returns.

## Key Points

- Proceedings originate from an Interim Order cum Show Cause Notice dated December 04, 2025.
- Noticees engaged in unregistered investment advisory and research analyst activities under the guise of stock market education.
- Published selective profitable trades and claimed consistent investor gains while actual participants incurred substantial net losses.
- Previously issued an Administrative Warning on March 01, 2024, which was ignored, leading to continued misleading social media promotions and investor complaints.
- Violated Section 12(1) of the SEBI Act, IA Regulations 2013, RA Regulations 2014, and PFUTP Regulations 2003.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement actions under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B, and 11D of the SEBI Act, 1992 against unregistered advisory and fraudulent schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities and individuals offering stock market training must not provide explicit or implicit investment advice or stock recommendations without mandatory SEBI registration as Investment Advisers or Research Analysts.
- Prohibition on misrepresenting trading performance and publishing selective profitable trades.

## Important Dates

- Interim Order cum Show Cause Notice: December 04, 2025
- Prior Administrative Warning: March 01, 2024

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on finfluencers, stock market trainers, and educational academies providing implicit investment advisory services without registration.
