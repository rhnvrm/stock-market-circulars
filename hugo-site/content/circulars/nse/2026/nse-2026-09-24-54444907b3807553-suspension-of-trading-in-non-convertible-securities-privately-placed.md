## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has notified the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) issued by Alpha Alternatives Financial Services Private Limited. The suspension is effective from September 25, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A, following the redemption of the securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Alpha Alternatives Financial Services Private Limited
- ISIN: INE0L6807104
- Suspension Date: September 25, 2026
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption
- Circular Reference: NSE/CML/76519 (Circular Ref. No: 1689/2026)

## Regulatory Changes

Suspension of trading in debt market segment in accordance with National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A, Regulation 3.1.2 upon redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the suspension and ensure no further trades are executed in these securities on the exchange platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 24, 2026
- Suspension Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update reflecting the redemption and retirement of privately placed debt instruments for Alpha Alternatives Financial Services Private Limited.
