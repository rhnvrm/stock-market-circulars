## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued operational guidelines regarding master data replication, login credentials, trading rights, and password reset facilities in the Simulated Market Environment across segments.

## Key Points

- Master data (including security, contract, member, user, trading limits, trading rights, and password data) is replicated from the Live environment on a T-1 basis, where T is the trading day in the Simulated Market Environment.
- Members must use the login credentials and password active in the Live/Production environment on the previous trading day.
- User trading rights and conversions of NEAT user IDs to NNF user IDs are replicated according to the T-1 master data cycle.
- Password reset facilities for dealer and branch manager user IDs are supported via self-service options on NEAT Trading Terminals and Non-NEAT front ends.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clarification and formalization of operational procedures and master data replication cycles for simulated market testing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure correct trading rights and user configurations are enabled in the live environment to ensure seamless reflection in the simulated environment.

## Important Dates

- Effective for simulated trading sessions starting September 24, 2026 (Circular Ref. No: 65/2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines software testing and application registration for trading members by clarifying authentication and master data synchronization rules.
