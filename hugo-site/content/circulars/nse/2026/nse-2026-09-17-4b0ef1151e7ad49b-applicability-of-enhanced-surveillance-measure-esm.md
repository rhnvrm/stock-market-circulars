## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) to certain securities based on preset criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I are included under ESM Stage-I w.e.f. September 18, 2026.
- Affected securities will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 21, 2026, on all open positions as on September 18, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities will be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 21, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities satisfying ESM criteria are subjected to heightened margins and trade-for-trade settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin is collected on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities as specified.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ESM Stage-I / Shifting: September 18, 2026 / September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in shortlisted securities (DEEP, FONEBOX, KSR, MEDICAMEQ) will be restricted to trade-for-trade settlement with enhanced margin requirements.
