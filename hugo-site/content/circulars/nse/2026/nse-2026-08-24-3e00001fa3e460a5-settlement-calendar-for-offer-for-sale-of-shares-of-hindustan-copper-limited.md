## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar and timings for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares of Hindustan Copper Limited. The circular outlines order dates, custodial confirmation times, settlement dates, and fund/security pay-in and pay-out schedules for both retail and non-retail categories.

## Key Points

- Settlement calendar covers Offer for Sale of Hindustan Copper Limited shares.
- Applicable for non-retail (IS) and retail (RS) investor categories with specific order dates spanning August 25-26, 2026, and settlement dates on August 27, 2026.
- Funds pay-in is scheduled by T+1 day at 11:00 AM.
- Securities and funds pay-out is scheduled by T+1 day at 01:30 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Settlement follows existing rules for secondary market transactions as referenced in circular NCL/CMPT/73996.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members, custodians, and PCMs must ensure timely order entry, custodial confirmation, and funds pay-in/pay-out as per the specified calendar.
- Securities transaction tax (STT) and stamp duty will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Important Dates

- **Order Dates:** August 25, 2026 and August 26, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in:** T+1 day by 11:00 AM
- **Securities & Funds Pay-out:** T+1 day by 01:30 PM
- **Settlement Date:** August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for market participants taking part in the Hindustan Copper Limited OFS.
