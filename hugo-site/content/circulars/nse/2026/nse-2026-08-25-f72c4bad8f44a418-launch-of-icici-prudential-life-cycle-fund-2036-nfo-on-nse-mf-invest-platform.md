## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced that the ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 New Fund Offer (NFO) by ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund will be available for trading on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 26, 2026, to September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Names: ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 - Regular Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A575) and ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A583).
- Category: Equity.
- Allotment Date: September 15, 2026.
- NFO Window: August 26, 2026 (starting) to September 09, 2026 (till 11:30 PM).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 11, 2026, till 12:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 26, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 09, 2026
- Allotment Date: September 15, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 11, 2026 (12:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification for trading members facilitating subscriptions to the ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 NFO.
