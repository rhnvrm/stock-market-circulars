## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the launch of the Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window opens on October 20, 2026, and closes on November 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund (Regular & Direct Plans - Growth)
- Category: Equity
- Subscription Window: October 20, 2026 to November 03, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: November 05, 2026
- ISINs: INF2KCX01111 (Regular Plan - Growth), INF2KCX01103 (Direct Plan - Growth)

## Regulatory Changes

[N/A]

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 05, 2026 till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 20, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: November 03, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: November 05, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: November 05, 2026 (03:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to subscribe to Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund equity scheme via the NSE MF Invest platform.
