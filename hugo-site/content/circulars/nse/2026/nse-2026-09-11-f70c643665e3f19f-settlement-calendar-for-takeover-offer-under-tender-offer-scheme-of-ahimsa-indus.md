## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement calendar and guidelines for the takeover offer under the tender offer scheme of Ahimsa Industries Limited. The trade period runs from September 18, 2026, to October 01, 2026, with the settlement date scheduled for October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Type:** X
- **Settlement No.:** 2026113
- **Trade Start Date:** 18-SEP-2026
- **Trade End Date:** 01-OCT-2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 16-OCT-2026
- **Settlement Date:** 19-OCT-2026
- Demat shares must be tendered through the early pay-in mechanism for Market Type Buyback.
- Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The circular implements the operational settlement schedule for an existing takeover offer scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

- Early pay-in of shares must be executed well in advance on the trade end date for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m.
- Members should review the Tender Offer Acceptance Report for accepted, partially accepted, and rejected orders along with reason codes.

## Important Dates

- **Trade Start Date:** September 18, 2026
- **Trade End Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** October 16, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and custodians participating in the Ahimsa Industries Limited takeover offer must adhere to the specified early pay-in procedures and settlement timelines to ensure smooth execution.
