## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the transfer of securities of Manika Plastech Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Manika Plastech Limited
- Symbol: MANIKA
- Segment Transfer: From trade for trade segment (series: BE) to rolling segment (series: EQ)
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026
- Reference to prior exchange circular no. NSE/CML/76421 dated September 18, 2026 and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Execution pursuant to existing SEBI and Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members need to take note of the change in series for Manika Plastech Limited effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for the security, improving liquidity and trading convenience.
