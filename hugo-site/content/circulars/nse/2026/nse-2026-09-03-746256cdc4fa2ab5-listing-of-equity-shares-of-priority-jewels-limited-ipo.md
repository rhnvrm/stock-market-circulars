## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Priority Jewels Limited under an Initial Public Offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Priority Jewels Limited
- Symbol: PRIORITY
- ISIN: INE15EH01014
- The exact date of listing will be communicated via a separate circular.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the security details for trading operations upon commencement.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 03, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular.

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to prepare for trading in the equity shares of Priority Jewels Limited upon official listing.
