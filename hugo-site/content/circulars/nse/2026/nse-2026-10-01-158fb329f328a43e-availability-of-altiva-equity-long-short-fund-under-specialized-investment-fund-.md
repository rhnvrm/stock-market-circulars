## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of Altiva Equity Long-Short Fund under Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) of Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities effective October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available include Direct Plan and Regular Plan options (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- Effective date for availability is October 5, 2026.
- Applicable facilities include ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; procedural circular regarding mutual fund scheme availability on the platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants must update their systems with the new ISINs for the respective Edelweiss Mutual Fund schemes if applicable.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 1, 2026
- Effective Date: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment offerings on the NSE MF Invest Platform for investors accessing Specialized Investment Funds (SIF).
