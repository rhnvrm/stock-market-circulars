## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, lumpsum purchases, switch-ins, and fresh registrations of Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) in certain schemes of HSBC Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective August 26, 2026. This is in order to comply with SEBI's overseas investment limits.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes: HSBC Asia Pacific (Ex Japan) Dividend Yield Fund, HSBC Brazil Fund, and HSBC Global Emerging Markets Fund.
- Restrictions apply to lumpsum purchases, switch-ins, fresh SIP, fresh STP, and IDCW Transfer Plan where the designated scheme is a target scheme.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and processing of existing registered SIP/STP/SWP instalments remain unaffected.
- Suspension is temporary until overseas investment limits are enhanced by SEBI/RBI or headroom increases.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adherence to SEBI letter dated June 17, 2022, permitting investments in overseas securities up to available headroom without breaching limits as of February 1, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the suspension of fresh inflows into the designated overseas schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Circular Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors looking to make fresh investments or new SIP/STP registrations in the specified HSBC overseas funds will be restricted until further notice, while existing SIPs and redemptions continue smoothly.
