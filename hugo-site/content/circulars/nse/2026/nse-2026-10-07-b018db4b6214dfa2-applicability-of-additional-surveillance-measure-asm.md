## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for specific securities effective October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Stage I Shortlisting:** Namo eWaste Management Limited (NAMOEWASTE) and Optiemus Infracom Limited (OPTIEMUS) have been shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I w.e.f. October 08, 2026.
- **Stage Movement:** Indo Thai Securities Limited (INDOTHAI) has moved from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I to Stage II w.e.f. October 08, 2026.
- **Margin Requirements:** Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 12, 2026, on all open positions as on October 09, 2026, and new positions created from October 12, 2026 onwards.
- **Market Surveillance:** ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures imposed by the exchanges.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities identified under the Long-Term ASM framework are subjected to enhanced surveillance and strict margin obligations to safeguard market integrity and protect investors from excessive volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the 100% margin requirement applicable to open positions and new positions in the specified scrips starting October 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 07, 2026
- **Effective Date for ASM Inclusion/Movement:** October 08, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** October 09, 2026
- **Effective Date for 100% Margin:** October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and investors dealing in NAMOEWASTE, OPTIEMUS, and INDOTHAI will be subject to 100% margin requirements, which may impact liquidity and leverage for these securities.
