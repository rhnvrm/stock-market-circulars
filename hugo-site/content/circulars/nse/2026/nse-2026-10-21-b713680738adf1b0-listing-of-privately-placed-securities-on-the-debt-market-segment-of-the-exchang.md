## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the admission of privately placed securities, including commercial papers and other debt issues, to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listing of Commercial Papers and Debt Securities from issuers such as HSBC Investdirect Financial Services, Godrej Properties, ICICI Securities, Tata Projects, SIDBI, Nomura Capital, Alpha Alternatives, and NABARD.
- Securities admitted under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.
- Allotment dates primarily on October 19, 2026 (with specific issues dated October 15, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine administrative listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must adhere to standard debt segment trading and settlement guidelines for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 21, 2026
- Allotment Dates: October 15, 2026, and October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances liquidity and enables secondary market transactions for the listed privately placed debt instruments and commercial papers.
