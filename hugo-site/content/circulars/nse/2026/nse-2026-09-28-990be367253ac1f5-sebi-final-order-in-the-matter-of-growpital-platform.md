## Summary

SEBI has issued a final order under Sections 11(1), (4), (4A) and Sections 11B(1) and (2) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Rule 5 of the SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995 in the matter of Growpital Platform, involving numerous noticees including Rituraj Sharma, Krishnna Joshi, Yotta Agro Venture Pvt Ltd, and others.

## Key Points

- The order addresses allegations and findings regarding unauthorized collective investment activities and platform operations under Growpital.
- Proceedings qua Noticee Nos. 15 (Nutrikosh India Private Limited) and 16 (Alok Chaudhary) were settled vide settlement order dated September 28, 2026, under SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018.
- Addresses preliminary objections raised by noticees regarding jurisdiction and adjudication processes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement actions and directions issued against entities operating unauthorized investment and agro-tech schemes without regulatory approvals.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees must comply with the specific directions, restrictions, and penalties outlined in the final order.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight and enforcement against illegal fundraising platforms operating outside statutory frameworks.
