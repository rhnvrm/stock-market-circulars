## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in ISIN for the equity shares of Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited (KIRLPNU) on account of a sub-division of face value from Rs. 2/- to Re. 1/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited
- Symbol: KIRLPNU
- New ISIN: INE811A01038
- Reason: Sub-Division from Rs. 2/- to Re. 1/-
- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the new ISIN for all trades executed on and from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Ex-date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; ensures smooth settlement under the new ISIN corresponding to the sub-divided equity shares.
