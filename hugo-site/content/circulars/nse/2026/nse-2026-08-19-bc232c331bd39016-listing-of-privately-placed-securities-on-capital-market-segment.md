## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment with effect from August 20, 2026. The circular details the security codes, issue descriptions, coupon rates, maturity dates, and ISINs for the listed securities.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 20, 2026
- Securities admitted for National Bank For Financing Infrastructure And Development (Symbol: 754NBFID41, ISIN: INE0KUG08167)
- Securities admitted for Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited (Symbol: 823ABHF36, ISIN: INE831R08118)
- Trading will be conducted in the specified market lots through designated security codes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure transactions use the designated security codes and adhere to the prescribed market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 20, 2026
- Maturity Dates: August 19, 2041 (NABF) and August 18, 2036 (ABHFL)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing event providing liquidity and trading access for the specified privately placed debt securities.
