## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Certain securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion or shifting across various stages of the ASM framework, effective September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 07, 2026, on all open positions as on September 04, 2026, and new positions created from September 07, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of the Long-Term ASM framework provisions, requiring enhanced margins and potential segment shifts for shortlisted scrips under surveillance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure 100% margin requirements are maintained on open and new positions for the affected securities starting from the specified effective dates. Transactions in applicable scrips must comply with Trade-for-Trade settlement restrictions where mandated.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 02, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Segment Shifts: September 07, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for shortlisted securities (such as CYIENTDLM, IPRINGLTD, ONIXSOLAR, and TBZ) may experience impacts due to 100% margin requirements and transfer to the trade-for-trade segment.
