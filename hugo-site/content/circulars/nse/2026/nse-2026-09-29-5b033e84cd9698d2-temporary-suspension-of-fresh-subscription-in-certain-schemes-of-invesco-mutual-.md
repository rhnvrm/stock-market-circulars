## Summary

Invesco Asset Management (India) Pvt. Ltd. has announced a temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions through lumpsum purchases and switch-ins in certain overseas Fund of Fund schemes of Invesco Mutual Fund, effective September 29, 2026. This measure complies with SEBI's guidelines on overseas investment headroom limits.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes (Designated Schemes):
  1. Invesco India - Invesco Global Equity Income Fund of Fund
  2. Invesco India - Invesco Pan European Equity Fund of Fund
  3. Invesco India - Invesco Global Consumer Trends Fund of Fund
- Fresh registration of SIP, STP, and IDCW Transfer Plan into Designated Schemes is restricted to a maximum limit of Rs. 10 lakh per day per PAN (at first holder level).
- Switch-out, redemptions, switches between plans/options, and fresh/existing Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) processing remain unaffected.
- The suspension is temporary and will remain in effect until further notice of resumption.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adherence to SEBI letter dated June 17, 2022, permitting investments in overseas funds/securities up to available headroom without breaching February 1, 2022 limits.

## Compliance Requirements

- Mutual fund distributors, platforms (such as NSE MF Invest), and investors must ensure that lumpsum purchases and switch-ins for the Designated Schemes are not processed.
- SIP, STP, and IDCW Transfer Plan registrations must cap aggregate investments at Rs. 10 lakh per day per PAN.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors seeking new lumpsum allocations into Invesco's overseas Fund of Fund schemes will face temporary restrictions, while existing SIPs and systematic withdrawals continue under specified daily caps.
