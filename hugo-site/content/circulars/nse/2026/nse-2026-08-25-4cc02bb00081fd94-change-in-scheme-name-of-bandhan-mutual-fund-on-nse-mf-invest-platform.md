## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names of Bandhan Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026. This change is pursuant to SEBI circulars on the categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Applies to multiple schemes of Bandhan Mutual Fund including Gilt, Banking and PSU, Dynamic Bond, ELSS Tax Saver, Floater, and Duration funds.
- All references to existing scheme names will be replaced with revised nomenclature across all relevant platforms and documents.

## Regulatory Changes

- Changes implemented in accordance with SEBI Circular dated February 26, 2026, on 'Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes' read with the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMC and platform participants must ensure all references to the affected scheme names are updated in accordance with the revised nomenclature.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; strictly a change in scheme names and terminology without altering fundamental scheme features.
