## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued operational guidelines regarding early pay-in of securities for Shankara Buildpro Limited (BUILDPRO) on account of its face value split (sub-division) from Rs 10/- per share to Rs 2/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company: Shankara Buildpro Limited (Symbol: BUILDPRO)
- Corporate Action: Face Value Split from Rs 10/- to Rs 2/- per share
- Record Date & Ex-Date: October 08, 2026
- Old/Existing ISIN: INE24OJ01011
- Settlement Numbers: 2026190 (08-10-2026) and 2026190 & 2026191 (09-10-2026)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; procedural guidelines for handling early pay-in during corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Execute early pay-in instructions in the depository systems using the old ISIN without adjustment for conversion on the record/ex-date.
- For early pay-in via the block mechanism, client-wise early pay-in allocation files must not be uploaded.
- Custodians providing early pay-in through pool accounts must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details as per actual traded quantities.
- Execute pay-in/early pay-in instructions in the new ISIN after the record date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 08, 2026
- Ex-Date: October 08, 2026
- Early Pay-in Dates: October 08, 2026 and October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement and clearing operations for market participants and custodians dealing with securities undergoing stock split.
