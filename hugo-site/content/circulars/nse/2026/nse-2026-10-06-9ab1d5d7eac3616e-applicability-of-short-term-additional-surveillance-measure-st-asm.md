## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) to certain securities. Several securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I include ANI Integrated Services Limited (AISL), Lokesh Machines Limited (LOKESHMACH), Rox Hi Tech Limited (ROXHITECH), Sharp Chucks and Machines Limited (SCML), Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited (SHUKRAPHAR), SMC Global Securities Limited (SMCGLOBAL), Sarthak Metals Limited (SMLT), and Vijaypd Ceutical Limited (VIJAYPD).
- Surveillance actions apply in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I framework for specified securities, imposing enhanced margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. October 08, 2026 on all open positions as on October 07, 2026 and new positions created from October 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 06, 2026
- **Effective Date (Framework applicability):** October 07, 2026
- **Margin Applicability Date:** October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in the shortlisted securities will experience increased margin requirements (50% or existing, capped at 100%), which may affect trading volumes and liquidity in these scrips.
