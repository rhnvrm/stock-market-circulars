## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long-Term ASM framework. Specific securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Long Term ASM Stage I effective October 16, 2026, with surveillance actions taking effect from October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I: Jinkushal Industries Limited (JKIPL) and The Ruby Mills Limited (RUBYMILLS).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective October 21, 2026, on all open positions as on October 19, 2026, and new positions created from October 21, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specified securities into Long-Term ASM Stage I framework.
- Imposition of 100% margin requirement on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin is maintained for open positions as on October 19, 2026, and new positions created from October 21, 2026 onwards for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date for Inclusion: October 16, 2026
- Open Positions as on: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in JKIPL and RUBYMILLS will experience higher capital requirements due to the 100% margin mandate under the Long-Term ASM framework.
