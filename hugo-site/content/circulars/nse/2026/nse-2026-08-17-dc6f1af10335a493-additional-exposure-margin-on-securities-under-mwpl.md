## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the levy of additional exposure margins on securities under the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL). An additional exposure margin of 15% in the equity derivatives segment will be applicable to shortlisted securities where top 10 clients account for more than 20% of MWPL.

## Key Points

- Additional exposure margin @15% applies to the equity derivatives segment for identified securities.
- Criteria: Securities where top 10 clients account for >20% of MWPL based on 3 months rolling data.
- Higher of additional exposure margin or additional surveillance margin will be levied if both are applicable.
- Review frequency: Monthly review based on rolling data.
- Total of 16 securities shortlisted in Annexure 1.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of additional exposure margins under the MWPL framework as per NSE circular NSE/INVG/40472 dated March 18, 2019.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must account for the 15% additional exposure margin on the specified securities in the equity derivatives segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026
- Circular Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for trading in futures and options for the listed 16 securities, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for positions in these counters.
