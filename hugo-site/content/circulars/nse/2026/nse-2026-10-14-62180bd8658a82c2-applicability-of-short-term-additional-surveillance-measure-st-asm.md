## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities. Effective October 16, 2026, 10 securities are placed under ST-ASM Stage I, requiring increased margins on open and new positions.

## Key Points

- 10 securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I effective October 16, 2026 (on open positions as on October 15, 2026, and new positions created from October 16, 2026).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate capped at 100%.
- No securities were listed under ST-ASM Stage II or moving from Stage I to Stage II in this circular.
- Surveillance measures are applied purely for market surveillance and do not constitute adverse actions against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework provisions for shortlisted securities in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or existing margin, capped at 100%) for open positions as on October 15, 2026, and all new positions created from October 16, 2026 onwards for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance / Margins: October 16, 2026 (on open positions as on October 15, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members dealing in the shortlisted securities (including Abans Enterprises, Delaplex, Ganga Bath Fittings, Kridhan Infra, Phoenix International, Shringar House of Mangalsutra, Simplex Realty, Uttam Sugar Mills, Veekayem Fashion and Apparels, and W S Industries) will experience higher margin requirements, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in these scrips.
