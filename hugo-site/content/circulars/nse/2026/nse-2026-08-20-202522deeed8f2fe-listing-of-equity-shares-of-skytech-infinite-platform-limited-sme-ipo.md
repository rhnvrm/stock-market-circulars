## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of equity shares of Skytech Infinite Platform Limited under the SME IPO segment, detailing shareholding patterns and post-listing details.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Skytech Infinite Platform Limited
- Scrip Code/Name: SKYTECH/EQUITY
- Date of Allotment: 19-08-2026
- Date of Report: 20-08-2026
- Total Issued and Paid-up Capital: 9,820,600 equity shares
- Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 6,874,945 shares (70.01%)
- Public Shareholding: 2,945,655 shares (29.99%)

Compliance with SEBI (ICDR) Regulations and Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements (LODR) Regulations.
