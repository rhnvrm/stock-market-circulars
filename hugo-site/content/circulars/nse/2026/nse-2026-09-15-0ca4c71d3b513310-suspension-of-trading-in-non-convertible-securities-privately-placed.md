## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) for Aditya Birla Capital Limited with effect from September 16, 2026, on account of redemption.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aditya Birla Capital Limited
- ISIN: INE860H07HQ2
- Suspension Date: 16-Sep-2026
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption
- Regulation: Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors are advised to take note of the suspension of trading in the aforementioned securities.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard operational procedure following the redemption of privately placed non-convertible securities and has minimal impact on equity market operations.
