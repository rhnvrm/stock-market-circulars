## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the resumption of subscriptions in UTI Gold ETF FOF under UTI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Following communication from UTI Mutual Fund, lumpsum purchases and switch-ins are resumed without limit restrictions with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Subscription through lumpsum purchases and switch-in under UTI Gold ETF FOF resumed without limit restrictions.
- Effective date for the resumption of subscriptions in UTI Gold ETF and UTI Gold ETF Fund of Fund is August 25, 2026.
- Large investor subscription transactions directly with UTI Mutual Fund (minimum Rs. 25 crore) in UTI Gold ETF also resumed from August 25, 2026.
- This follows the temporary restriction placed earlier vide Addendum No. 18/2026-27 dated June 6, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational update regarding fund subscription limits.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors are requested to take note of the resumption of lumpsum subscriptions and switch-ins without restriction.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 25, 2026
- **Circular Date:** September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors can freely resume lumpsum investments and switch-ins into the UTI Gold ETF Fund of Fund through the NSE MF Invest Platform without prior restrictions.
