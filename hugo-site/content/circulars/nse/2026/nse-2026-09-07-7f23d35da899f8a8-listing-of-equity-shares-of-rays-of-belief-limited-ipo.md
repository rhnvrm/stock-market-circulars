## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Rays of Belief Limited (IPO) on the Capital Market segment effective from September 08, 2026. The shares will trade under the symbol MOMSBELIEF in Series 'BE' and will subsequently be shifted to Series 'EQ'.

## Key Points

- Symbol: MOMSBELIEF
- Company Name: Rays of Belief Limited
- ISIN: INE1FCO01014
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 239/-
- Total No. of Securities: 20,901,682
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: September 08, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Admission to dealings is in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the designated security codes and lot sizes. Securities will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips as per SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 08, 2026
- Lock-in details vary from October 2026 to September 2029 as per Annexure I.

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of equity shares for Rays of Belief Limited on the NSE Capital Market segment.
