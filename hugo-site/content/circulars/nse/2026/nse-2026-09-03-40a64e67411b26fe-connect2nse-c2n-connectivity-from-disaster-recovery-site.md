## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited will conduct its operations for the Debt Segment from its Disaster Recovery (DR) Site on September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026. Members accessing N-MASS and NCMS applications do not need to change configuration settings, but applications will experience scheduled downtime.

## Key Points

- Operations for the Debt Segment will run from the Disaster Recovery (DR) Site on September 3 and September 4, 2026.
- No configuration changes or connection parameter updates are required for members accessing N-MASS and NCMS applications through internet or leased lines.
- N-MASS and NCMS applications will be unavailable for live operations or updates between 01:00 hrs and 08:00 hrs on September 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; operational continuity measure via Disaster Recovery Site invocation.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must take note of the scheduled downtime for N-MASS and NCMS applications and plan their operations accordingly.

## Important Dates

- DR Site Operations: September 03, 2026, and September 04, 2026
- Application Downtime (N-MASS and NCMS): September 05, 2026 (01:00 hrs to 08:00 hrs)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact on members as connection parameters remain unchanged, with a brief planned outage during non-peak hours for maintenance/switchback.
