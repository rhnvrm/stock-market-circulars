## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of LIC Housing Finance Limited due to redemption, effective from August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: LIC Housing Finance Limited
- ISIN: INE115A07PI6
- Suspension Date: 18-Aug-2026
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption
- Download Reference No: NSE/CML/75786
- Circular Ref. No: 1372/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants should note the suspension of trading for the specified ISIN in accordance with Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified non-convertible securities of LIC Housing Finance Limited will cease on the effective date following their redemption.
