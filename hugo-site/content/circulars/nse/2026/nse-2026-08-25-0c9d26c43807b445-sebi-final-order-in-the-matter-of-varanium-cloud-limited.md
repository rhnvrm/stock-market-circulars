## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding SEBI's final order in the matter of Varanium Cloud Limited. SEBI has restrained Varanium Cloud Limited and several associated noticees from buying, selling, or dealing in securities, or associating with the securities market for varying debarment periods.

## Key Points

- Varanium Cloud Limited and Harshawardhan Hanmant Sabale debarred for 7 years.
- First Overseas Capital Limited and Athos Capital Advisors Private Limited debarred for 2 years.
- Other noticees including Vinayak Vasant Jadhav, Mukundan Raghavan, Raj Jagtani, and Fahim Iunus Shaikh debarred for 1 year.
- Hetal Somani and Kalpesh Anil Acharekar charges were disposed off.
- Reference made to earlier interim order dated October 21, 2024.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of SEBI debarment orders against specified noticees involved in the Varanium Cloud Limited matter.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the debarred entities and ensure that they do not facilitate any trading, buying, selling, or dealing in securities for the listed entities and individuals.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 25, 2026
- SEBI Order Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High impact on the named entities and members who must restrict dealing with the debarred persons and companies to ensure market integrity and compliance.
