## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the introduction of Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) facilities under the Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) of ICICI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. ICICI Prudential Trust Limited has approved these facilities across four SIF investment strategies effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 16, 2026.
- **Applicable Schemes:** 
  1. iSIF Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund
  2. iSIF Equity Long-Short Fund
  3. iSIF Hybrid Long-Short Fund
  4. iSIF Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund
- **Frequencies & Installment Minimums:** 
  - STP frequencies: Daily (available only in equity-oriented strategies: Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short and Equity Long-Short), Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, Quarterly. Minimum installment amount is ₹1,000 (and in multiples of ₹1/- thereafter) with 6 minimum installments (4 for Monthly, 2 for Quarterly).
  - SWP frequencies: Monthly, Quarterly, Half-yearly, Annually. Minimum installment amount is ₹1,000 (and in multiples of ₹1/- thereafter) with 2 minimum installments.
- **Conditions:** Non-business days default to the next business day. Each transfer/withdrawal is permitted only if the Minimum Investment Threshold is maintained.

## Regulatory Changes

- Integration and enablement of SWP and STP facilities for SIF investment strategies on the NSE MF platform in accordance with the notice-cum-addendum issued by ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors should note the updated features, minimum installment amounts, frequencies, and conditions for SIF investment strategies.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 15, 2026 (Circular Ref. No: 1757/2026)
- **Addendum Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors in ICICI Prudential SIF strategies gain greater flexibility in managing their capital inflows and outflows through systematic transfer and withdrawal plans on the NSE MF platform.
