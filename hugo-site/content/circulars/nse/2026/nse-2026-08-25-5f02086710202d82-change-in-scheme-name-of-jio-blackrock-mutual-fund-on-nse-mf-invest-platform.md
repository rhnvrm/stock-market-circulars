## Summary

Jio BlackRock Asset Management Private Limited has issued a notice-cum-addendum announcing changes to the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) of its mutual fund schemes, effective August 26, 2026. These changes are made to align with the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name Changes:**
  - JioBlackRock Short Duration Fund is renamed to JioBlackRock Short Term Fund.
  - JioBlackRock Low Duration Fund is renamed to JioBlackRock Ultra Short to Short Term Fund.
- **Regulatory Alignment:** Changes are carried out in compliance with Part IV – Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes of the SEBI Master Circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- Recategorization and renaming of debt schemes to match standardized nomenclature and Macaulay duration guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Asset Management Companies (AMCs) must update scheme documentation and inform investors of name and feature modifications in line with SEBI directives.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Existing and prospective investors need to note the updated scheme names and features for transaction and reporting purposes on platforms like NSE MF Invest.
