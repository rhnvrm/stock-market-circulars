## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in units of several HDFC Mutual Fund schemes due to redemption, effective from September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in 6 schemes of HDFC Mutual Fund (HDFC FMP 1269D Mar 2023 series).
- Reason for suspension: Redemption.
- Effective Suspension Date: September 03, 2026.
- Closing hours for trading: September 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the suspension and ensure no trades are executed in these units post the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Closing Hours of Trading: September 02, 2026
- Suspension Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational restriction limited to the specified HDFC Mutual Fund FMP schemes undergoing redemption.
