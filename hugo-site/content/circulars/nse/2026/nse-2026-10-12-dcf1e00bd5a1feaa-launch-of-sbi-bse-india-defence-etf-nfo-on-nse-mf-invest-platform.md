## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the launch of the SBI BSE India Defence ETF New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The NFO period runs from October 13, 2026, to October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Names: SBI BSE India Defence ETF - Direct Growth and SBI BSE India Defence ETF - Regular Growth.
- ISIN: INF200KB1CJ6 for both plans.
- Category: Equity ETF.
- NFO Window: October 13, 2026 to October 19, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- Allotment Date: October 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that clear funds through any approved payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 23, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 13, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: October 19, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 23, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates participation in the defence sector theme via ETF investment through the NSE MF Invest Platform for market investors.
