## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular notifying members about the removal of debarment on Dollar Advisory and Financial Services (Proprietor: Mr. Ravi Prakash Mohta, PAN: AKBPM7004R) following the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated October 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Previous SEBI debarment order and NSE circular (NSE/INVG/ 54662 dated November 30, 2022) restricted the entity from accessing the securities market.
- SEBI, via email dated October 16, 2026, directed the removal of the debarment based on the SAT order dated October 6, 2026 (Appeal No. 239 of 2023).
- SAT order set aside directions in paragraph 138 (A) to (H), modified paragraph 138(I) to a penalty of ₹1 Lakh under Section 15EB of the SEBI Act, 1992, and set aside penalties under Sections 15HA and 15HB.

## Regulatory Changes

The debarment and trading restrictions previously imposed on Dollar Advisory and Financial Services and its proprietor Ravi Prakash Mohta have been lifted pursuant to the SAT ruling.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the removal of debarment and verify entities against the updated consolidated list of debarred entities available on the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- **SAT Order Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Circular Date:** October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This circular ensures market participants are updated on the lifting of restrictions for the named entity, ensuring accurate compliance and verification checks.
