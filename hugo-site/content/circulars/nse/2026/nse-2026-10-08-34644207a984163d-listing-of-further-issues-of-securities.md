## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of equity shares for various companies, effective from October 09, 2026. The securities are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings with effect from October 09, 2026.
- Includes equity shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and schemes of arrangement.
- Affected companies include Axis Bank Limited, BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Limited, Coforge Limited, Exicom Tele-Systems Limited, and HEG Advanced Materials Limited, among others.
- Trading will be conducted in the specified lot sizes and identified by designated security codes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further issue of securities under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adherence to designated security codes, ISINs, and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 09, 2026
- Allotment dates vary by company (ranging from September to October 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the respective issuers with no direct regulatory or operational impact on overall market stability.
