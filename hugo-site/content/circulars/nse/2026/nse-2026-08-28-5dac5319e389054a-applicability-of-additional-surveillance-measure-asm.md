## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for certain securities based on specified market surveillance criteria, effective August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I include Greenchef Appliances Limited, Natural Capsules Limited, Nectar Lifesciences Limited, Nicco Parks & Resorts Limited, and PVP Ventures Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 02, 2026, on all open positions as on September 01, 2026, and new positions created from September 02, 2026, onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measures on identified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must maintain a 100% margin on open positions and new positions for the shortlisted securities as per the schedule.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 31, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: September 02, 2026 (on open positions as on September 01, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (100%) for trading in the specified securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
