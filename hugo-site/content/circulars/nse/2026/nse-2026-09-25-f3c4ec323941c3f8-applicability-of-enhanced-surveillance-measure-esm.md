## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for certain securities. Securities satisfying the criteria are subject to enhanced margins, shifting to trade-for-trade settlement, or moving between ESM stages effective September 28 and September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **ESM Stage I Shortlisting:** Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 29, 2026, on all open positions as on September 28, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- **Segment Shifting:** Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 29, 2026.
- **Stage Movement:** Securities shifting to Stage II (e.g., ACS Technologies Limited) are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 28, 2026.
- **Reinstatement:** Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework are reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures under NSE/SURV/56948 and subsequent circulars.
- Mandatory trade-for-trade settlement and 100% margin requirements for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted scrips.
- Members must note the shift of specified securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Stage II Price Band / Shifting:** September 28, 2026
- **Effective Date for 100% Margin & Trade-for-Trade Settlement (Stage I):** September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and trading mechanics for the affected scrips (CHAVDA, CUBEXTUB, KSHITIJPOL, PRESSTONIC, USHAFIN, UTKAL, ACSTECH) due to 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement, and periodic call auction price bands.
