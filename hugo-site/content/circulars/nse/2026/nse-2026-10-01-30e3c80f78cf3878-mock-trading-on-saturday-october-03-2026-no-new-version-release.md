## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, October 03, 2026. There is no new version release for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: October 03, 2026.
- Segment: Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR).
- NEAT+ version: 1.0.1 (/egftp/egcommon/NEATPlus101), default password 'Neat@EG1'.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Members participating in the EGR segment are advised to participate in the mock trading session and note the interactive parameters, login details, and multicast IP broadcast parameters.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Mock Trading Date: October 03, 2026
- Pre-open session: 10:45 to 10:53 Hrs
- Normal Market open: 11:00 Hrs, close: 12:30 Hrs
- Block Deal Window: 12:00 to 12:15 Hrs
- Auction Market: 11:30 to 12:15 Hrs
- Live Re-login: 16:30 to 17:00 Hrs

## Impact Assessment

Low impact. Designed for members to test systems and connectivity for the EGR segment with no financial obligations.
