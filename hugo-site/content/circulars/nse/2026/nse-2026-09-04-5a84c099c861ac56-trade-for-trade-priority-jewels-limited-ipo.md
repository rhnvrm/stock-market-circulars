## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Priority Jewels Limited from the trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) with effect from September 21, 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines and previous exchange circulars.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Priority Jewels Limited
- Symbol: PRIORITY
- Movement: Transferred from trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) to rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026
- Reference to previous circular: NSE/CML/76162 dated September 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities moved to the normal rolling settlement segment in accordance with SEBI guidelines (circular reference CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are required to note the change in trading series for Priority Jewels Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026
- Previous Circular Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the equity shares of Priority Jewels Limited will now take place in the regular rolling segment, facilitating normal settlement cycles.
