## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has released the Mutual Fund Service System (MFSS) Settlement Calendar for September 2026. The calendar provides specific schedules for normal subscriptions (operating on a T+1 settlement cycle) and liquid subscriptions (operating on a T day units settlement cycle). It lists the settlement numbers, subscription order dates, and corresponding pay-in/pay-out dates for both funds and units throughout the month.

## Key Points

- **Normal Subscription (Annexure 'A')**: Outlines a T+1 settlement cycle where subscription orders placed on day T have fund pay-in/pay-out on day T and unit pay-in/pay-out on day T+1. This section lists settlement numbers from 2026164 to 2026184.
- **Liquid Subscription (Annexure 'B')**: Outlines a T day units settlement cycle where subscription order date, fund pay-in/pay-out, and unit pay-in/pay-out all occur on the same day T. This section lists settlement numbers starting from 2026164.
- **Holidays/Non-Settlement Days**: Identified gaps in settlement days, such as the period between September 11, 2026, and September 15, 2026, indicate weekends and market holidays.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory policy changes introduced in this circular. It represents a routine, periodic release of the settlement schedule under existing operational guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Operational Alignment**: Market participants, including Mutual Fund Distributors, Asset Management Companies (AMCs), and clearing members, must configure their systems and operational workflows to match the settlement calendar for September 2026.
- **Funding and Units Provision**: Ensure that necessary funds and units are available for pay-in according to the respective T+0 (Liquid) or T+1 (Normal) timelines specified in the circular.

## Important Dates

- **Settlement Period**: September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- **September 1, 2026**: First subscription order date for Settlement No. 2026164.
- **September 30, 2026**: Last subscription order date for Settlement No. 2026184 (Normal Subscription).
- **September 14, 2026**: Non-settlement day (indicated by the gap in Normal Subscription from Sept 11 to Sept 15).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Low impact. The circular is standard and operational, helping market participants ensure seamless transaction settlement for mutual fund units through the MFSS platform without introducing structural or regulatory disruptions.
