## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the revision of the New Fund Offer (NFO) close date and allotment date for the HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF on the NSE MF Invest Platform, following communication from HDFC Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- The NFO closing date for HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF has been preponed from September 07, 2026, to August 27, 2026.
- Switch requests can be submitted in the NFO on or before 3:00 PM on August 27, 2026.
- The allotment date has been revised from September 10, 2026, to September 01, 2026.
- Clear funds through any payment mode must be received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 01, 2026, by 03:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an operational adjustment of scheme timelines as requested by the AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure clear funds are received by the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 01, 2026, 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Close Date (Revised):** August 27, 2026 (3:00 PM for switch requests)
- **Allotment Date (Revised):** September 01, 2026
- **Clear Funds Deadline:** September 01, 2026 (3:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and members participating in the HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF NFO must take note of the accelerated timeline for subscription closure and fund clearance.
