## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the movement of securities to Stage II of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM). Effective September 01, 2026, Blue Chip India Limited will be moved to Stage II of GSM.

## Key Points

- **Security Affected:** Blue Chip India Limited (Symbol: BLUECHIP, ISIN: INE657B01025).
- **Effective Date:** September 01, 2026.
- **Reason for Addition:** Adding as per BSE and Series Change from EQ to BE.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in the affected security shall be available in Trade for Trade with a price band of 5% or lower as applicable.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value is to be deposited by buyers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to take adequate precautions while trading in the above security.
- Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity for Blue Chip India Limited will be impacted due to the mandatory trade-for-trade settlement and the requirement for buyers to deposit a 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD).
