## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission to dealings and trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Ratnaveer Precision Engineering Limited. Trading will take place from September 02, 2026, to September 03, 2026, under the trade-to-trade segment in compulsory demat.

## Key Points

- Symbol: RATNA-RE
- Company Name: Ratnaveer Precision Engineering Limited
- ISIN: INE05CZ20011
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 12,474,130
- Trading Period for Renunciation: From September 02, 2026 to September 03, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 04, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

The Rights Entitlements are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and clarifying that they should not be mistaken for ordinary shares. Members must include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes/bills informing clients about the nature of REs, application requirements, and the consequence of lapsing if unexercised or unrenounced by the issue closing date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Trading Start Date: September 02, 2026
- Trading End Date / Renunciation Closure: September 03, 2026
- Suspension Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders of Ratnaveer Precision Engineering Limited receive rights entitlements allowing them to participate in the rights issue or renounce their entitlements within the specified trading window. Trading is restricted to the trade-to-trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors.
