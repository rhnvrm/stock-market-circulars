## Summary

National Stock Exchange has circulated SEBI Interim Order No. WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-SEC-7/32667/2026-27 dated August 19, 2026, in the matter of Dhenu Buildcon Infra Limited. SEBI has issued various directions including restraints on buying, selling, or dealing in securities, restrictions on corporate actions, and prohibition on disposing of shares for specified noticees.

## Key Points

- Noticee No. 1 (Dhenu Buildcon Infra Limited) is restricted from undertaking corporate actions such as name change, capital structure alteration, bonus issue, rights issue, stock split, and dividends until further orders.
- Noticee Nos. 2 to 7 are restrained from disposing of shares of DBIL held by them, directly or indirectly.
- Noticee Nos. 8 and 9 (Surendra Kumar Jain and Virendra Jain) are restrained from buying, selling, or dealing in securities or associating with the securities market.
- Noticees with open positions in exchange traded derivative contracts may close out/square off within 3 months or at contract expiry, whichever is earlier.

## Regulatory Changes

SEBI has exercised powers under Sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B of the SEBI Act to issue interim directions against the entities involved in the matter.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the order and ensure compliance with the restrictions imposed on the specified noticees. Consolidated list of debarred entities is available on the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 19, 2026
- Derivative position square-off window: Within 3 months from the date of the order or contract expiry, whichever is earlier.

## Impact Assessment

High impact for the named noticees and listed entities involved due to trading bans, asset freezes, and prohibitions on corporate actions.
