## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities, including commercial papers and debt securities, on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuers Involved**: Includes ICICI Securities Limited, Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited, CEAT Limited, Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI), Blue Star Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Piramal Finance Limited, and Hdfc Securities Limited.
- **Securities Issued**: Multiple commercial paper (CP) tranches and debt securities (e.g., Piramal Finance 8.62% 2029).
- **Effective Date**: October 09, 2026.
- **Exchange Segment**: Debt Market Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, securities specified in the Annexure are admitted to dealings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the security codes, ISINs, face values, and maturity dates for settlement and reporting purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Listing/Effective Date**: October 09, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**: October 08, 2026 and October 09, 2026
- **Redemption Dates**: Ranging from November 30, 2026 to November 22, 2029 across various tranches.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity and formal exchange trading venue for privately placed commercial papers and debt securities issued by various financial institutions and corporate entities.
