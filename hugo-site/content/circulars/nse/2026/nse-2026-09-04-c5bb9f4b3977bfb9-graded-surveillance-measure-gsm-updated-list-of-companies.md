## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued an updated list of companies under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework following the quarterly review. The circular outlines securities newly placed in the GSM framework, those retained, and those moving out.

## Key Points

- Securities identified for placing in the GSM framework (Annexure I) include Asian Tea & Exports Limited (ASIANTNE) and HMT Limited (HMT).
- Certain securities shall continue to remain in the GSM framework (Annexure II), including AGS Transact Technologies Limited, Ankit Metal & Power Limited, and others.
- Securities given in Annexure III shall move out of the GSM framework effective September 08, 2026.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) collected on securities moving out of GSM shall be released on September 15, 2026.
- Securities under GSM Stage VI shall continue to remain in Stage VI until further directions.

## Regulatory Changes

Quarterly identification of securities for placing in the GSM framework based on fundamentals and financial health criteria such as earnings, book value, net worth, and market capitalization.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to exercise additional due diligence while trading in these securities either on their own account or on behalf of their clients.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for moving out of GSM: September 08, 2026
- Release of Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD): September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct impact on liquidity and trading parameters for the specific securities placed in or removed from the Graded Surveillance Measure framework.
