## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Coreintegra Consulting Services Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** COREIN
- **Company Name:** Coreintegra Consulting Services Limited
- **ISIN:** INE11ES01010
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 78/- per share
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- **No. of Securities:** 10,487,005 equity shares (Distinctive numbers 1 to 10487005)
- **Market Lot:** Minimum 1,600 Equity Shares
- **Series:** Series 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment – Settlement Type 'W') initially, to be subsequently shifted to Series 'SM' (Normal Rolling Segment).
- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of securities to dealings on the NSE EMERGE SME Platform pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Initial trading in Series 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment - TFTS) in compliance with SEBI circular guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to note the security codes, pre-open session rules for IPO scrips, and continuous market trading availability.
- Market maker obligations apply as per exchange norms (Rikhav Securities Limited acting as Market Maker).

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Listing / Effective Date:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public trading access for the newly listed SME IPO shares of Coreintegra Consulting Services Limited under the trade-for-trade surveillance segment initially.
