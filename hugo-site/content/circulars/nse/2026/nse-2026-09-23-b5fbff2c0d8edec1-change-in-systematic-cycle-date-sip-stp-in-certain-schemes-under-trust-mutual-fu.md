## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding revisions to the Fixed Systematic Transfer Plan (FSTP) and Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) cycle dates for schemes under Trust Mutual Fund, effective September 23, 2026, on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- **Daily FSTP Revision:** Existing cycle dates allowing any date from 1st to 28th have been revised to 'All Business Days'. First execution date remains on or after 7 calendar days from submission date.
- **Fortnightly SIP Revision:** Existing cycle dates between 1st to 28th of the month have been revised to the 1st and 16th of every month.
- **Applicability:** The revised provisions apply to new registrations received and registered on or after the Effective Date (September 23, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of cycle dates for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Fixed Systematic Transfer Plans (FSTP) for Trust Mutual Fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Intermediaries and investors must note the updated cycle dates for new FSTP and SIP registrations under Trust Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minor operational adjustment for investors and distributors setting up new systematic investment or transfer plans for Trust Mutual Fund schemes on the NSE platform.
