## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Manas Polymers and Energies Limited under the SME EMERGE segment effective from September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: MPEL-RE
- ISIN: INE0U4H20011
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 15,552,000
- Trading Period for Renunciation: From September 08, 2026, to September 16, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 17, 2026
- Market Lot: 3,200
- Segment: Trade to Trade in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs, ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares, and include a mandatory disclaimer in contract notes regarding application or renunciation deadlines before REs lapse.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 08, 2026
- Trading Renunciation Period: September 08, 2026, to September 16, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and investors to trade or renounce rights entitlements for Manas Polymers and Energies Limited within the specified timeframe.
