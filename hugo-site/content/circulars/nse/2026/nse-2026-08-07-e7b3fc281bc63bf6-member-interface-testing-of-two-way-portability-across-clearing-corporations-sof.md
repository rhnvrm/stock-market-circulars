## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) is conducting a mock session of the Software as a Service (SaaS) setup on August 08, 2026. This initiative is in line with the SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/P/CIR/2023/192 dated December 20, 2023, regarding Business Continuity for Clearing Corporations through the SaaS Model, which mandates quarterly mock sessions. The objective is to familiarize and train members on the functionalities of two-way portability across Clearing Corporations and to test the associated interfaces.

## Key Points

- **Purpose**: Test the SaaS setup, member interfaces, and two-way portability functionalities across Clearing Corporations.
- **SaaS Invocation**: NCL will invoke its SaaS setup between 13:45 hrs – 14:15 hrs.
- **Operational Hours**: NCL will operate using the SaaS setup (NCL@ICCL) from 14:15 hrs to 15:40 hrs.
- **Participation**: All members are requested to actively participate and test the requisite functionalities during the mock session.
- **Access Credentials**: Members can use their existing login credentials for the mock session. New users must request credentials by emailing support@nsccl.co.in.

## Regulatory Changes

- There are no new regulatory amendments introduced in this circular; it implements the mandate from the SEBI circular on Business Continuity (SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/P/CIR/2023/192) dated December 20, 2023.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Functional Testing**: Members must connect and test all functionalities associated with the SaaS setup on the scheduled mock day.
- **Reference Guidelines**: Members should refer to NCL circular no. 056/2023 (dated April 13, 2023) and NCL circular no. 059/2024 (dated May 10, 2024) for standard operating procedures (SOPs).
- **Access Links**:
  - RTRMS Link: [https://isrm.connect2nsccl.com/stocks/jsp/rms/](https://isrm.connect2nsccl.com/stocks/jsp/rms/)
  - Collateral Link: [https://isrmcoll.connect2nsccl.com/application/applogin/login.aspx](https://isrmcoll.connect2nsccl.com/application/applogin/login.aspx)
  - Extranet Link: [https://isrmxnet.connect2nsccl.com/Extranet_Login.aspx](https://isrmxnet.connect2nsccl.com/Extranet_Login.aspx)

## Important Dates

- **Mock Session Date**: August 08, 2026.
- **SaaS Invocation Window**: August 08, 2026, 13:45 hrs – 14:15 hrs.
- **SaaS Operational Window (NCL@ICCL)**: August 08, 2026, 14:15 hrs to 15:40 hrs.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Clearing members must dedicate resources to participate in the mock testing session on August 08, 2026. Testing is critical to ensure that members can seamlessly transition to the SaaS setup during any contingency or business disruption.
- **Market Impact**: Minimal immediate market impact as the session is a scheduled mock run outside of normal market hours; however, it strengthens long-term market resilience and business continuity.
