## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective from August 19, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Privately placed securities including Commercial Papers (CPs) from various issuers have been approved for trading.
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026.
- Issuers include Godrej Properties Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Hdfc Securities Limited, Blue Star Limited, Tata Capital Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, TATA Projects Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, HDB Financial Services Ltd, and Tata Communications Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; notification is in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and issuers must ensure adherence to standard depository and settlement procedures for the listed commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 18, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 19, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from August 28, 2026 to February 17, 2027 depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing update with standard operational impact on the debt market segment.
