## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the settlement calendar for the reverse leg settlement and reverse leg auction for the newly introduced shorter tenure 'R3' series under the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for August and September 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers reverse leg settlement schedules (Settlement Type 'P') and reverse leg auction schedules (Settlement Type 'Q') for the 'R3' series expiring in August and September 2026.
- First leg settlements continue under Settlement Type 'L' as specified in previous calendars.
- No change in settlement pay-in timings for settlement types 'L', 'P', and 'Q'.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes specific trade dates, settlement numbers, and reverse leg settlement dates for the 'R3' series from August 17, 2026, to September 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians must align their SLBS operations and settlement planning with the provided reverse leg settlement and auction calendars.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for trade dates starting August 17, 2026, extending through early October 2026 for reverse leg settlements.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational workflow and clearing timelines for participants trading in the shorter tenure 'R3' series of SLBS.
