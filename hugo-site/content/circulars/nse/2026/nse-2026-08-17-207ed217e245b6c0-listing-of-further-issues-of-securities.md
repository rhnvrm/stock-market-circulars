## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for several companies effective from August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers companies such as Amagi Media Labs Limited, Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited, Five-Star Business Finance Limited, HDFC Bank Limited, Kalyan Jewellers India Limited, Dr. Lal Path Labs Ltd., Laxmi Organic Industries Limited, M & B Engineering Limited, Prime Focus Limited, The Phoenix Mills Limited, Polycab India Limited, RBL Bank Limited, Tirupati Forge Limited, Tracxn Technologies Limited, and V-Mart Retail Limited.
- Securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from August 18, 2026.
- Allotments include shares issued under ESOP.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Notification issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in these securities shall be in the specified lot sizes and identified by their designated codes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion and listing of shares for the listed entities with no broader market impact.
