## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Jindal Supreme (India) Limited (IPO) on the Capital Market segment effective from September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: JSIPL
- ISIN: INE1DLO01028
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 93/-
- No. of Securities: 51,023,769
- Market Lot: 1
- Series: BE (to be subsequently shifted to Series EQ as per SEBI guidelines)

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the security shall be in the specified lot size and codes as per the exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Jindal Supreme (India) Limited equity shares on the NSE Capital Market segment.
