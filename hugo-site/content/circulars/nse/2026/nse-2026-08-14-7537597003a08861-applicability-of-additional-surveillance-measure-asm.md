## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to specific securities satisfying criteria for inclusion under Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. August 19, 2026 on all open positions as on August 18, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities listed in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I include AQYLON, SKYGOLD, and SUMEETINDS.
- Shortlisting under ASM is on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) Stage - I.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure 100% margin requirements are met for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Framework / Surveillance Actions: August 17, 2026
- Margin applicability effective date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to a 100% margin requirement, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for market participants.
