## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited is conducting a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026, across primary and disaster recovery (DR) sites. There is no new version release and trades executed during the mock session will not result in any financial obligation.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** October 24, 2026.
- **Session 1 (Primary Site):** Normal market open at 11:00 hours and close at 12:30 hours.
- **Session 2 (DR Site):** Normal market open at 13:15 hours and close at 14:30 hours.
- **Cut-off & Modification:** Position Limit/Collateral value set up cut-off time and Trade Modification end time are at 14:40 hours.
- **Live Re-login Timings:** Start at 16:00 hours and close at 16:30 hours on October 24, 2026.
- **Order Purge:** All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session. Members using NNF software must manually clear orders.
- **NOTIS Availability:** The NOTIS application will not be accessible during the mock session.
- **Encryption Migration Reminder:** NNF members must migrate to new encryption mechanisms (ports 10869/10871) by December 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Routine operational mock session conducted in compliance with regulatory requirements as referenced in SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 26, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.
- Extranet and Connect2NSE facilities will not be available from 19:00 hours to 22:00 hours.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Session Date:** October 24, 2026
- **Live Re-login Window:** October 24, 2026 (16:00 - 16:30 hrs)
- **Live Trading Resume:** October 26, 2026
- **NNF Encryption Migration Deadline:** December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact; tests member system readiness and connectivity for the Commodity Derivatives segment with zero financial risk or settlement impact.
