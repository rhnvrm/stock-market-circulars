## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified all trading members regarding the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Limited under the scheme "ICICI Prudential BSE Insurance ETF" (Symbol: INSUREIETF). Trading of these units on the Capital Market segment of the exchange will commence on August 05, 2026. A total of 3,806,442 units, allotted on July 31, 2026, at an issue price of Rs. 15.69/- per unit with a face value of Rs. 10/- per unit, are admitted under the EQ series.

- **ISIN**: INF109K1A567

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory frameworks are established by this circular. The listing and admission process is completed under standard regulations, specifically in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must update their systems to incorporate the new ETF symbol INSUREIETF and ensure that trading can be initiated in lot sizes of 1 unit beginning August 05, 2026.
- **Market Makers**: The eleven approved market makers are required to fulfill their market-making obligations to facilitate liquidity in the security as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment of Units**: July 31, 2026
- **Circular Release Date**: August 04, 2026
- **Listing and Trading Commencement**: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: The addition of the ICICI Prudential BSE Insurance ETF expands the offering of sector-specific exchange-traded funds on the NSE, specifically focusing on the insurance sector, giving passive investors targeted exposure.
- **Operational Impact**: Negligible operational impact. Standard trading and settlement processes apply. Market makers will support orderly trading and spread management.
