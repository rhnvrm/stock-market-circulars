## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the admission of three Treasury Bills (T-bills) and six State Development Loans (SDLs) to dealings on the Capital Market segment, effective from August 07, 2026. This listing is conducted pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- **Admission to Dealings**: Multiple government debt securities (Treasury Bills and State Development Loans) will be traded on the Capital Market segment starting August 07, 2026.
- **Lot Size**: The lot size for trading all of these newly admitted securities is set to 100, as specified under Regulation 2.5.5.
- **Securities Listed**:
  - Three Government of India Treasury Bills: 91-Day (maturing Nov 05, 2026), 182-Day (maturing Feb 04, 2027), and 364-Day (maturing Aug 05, 2027).
  - Six State Development Loans from Odisha, Tamil Nadu, and Uttarakhand with varying maturities (ranging from 2030 to 2051) and coupon rates between 6.94% and 7.70%.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory frameworks are introduced. The listing is executed under the existing provisions of:
- **Regulation 3.1.1** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A (governing admission to dealings).
- **Regulation 2.5.5** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A (governing lot sizes).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, brokers, and trading members must update their systems with the new symbols, designated codes, lot sizes, and ISINs to facilitate trading in these securities from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 05, 2026
- **Effective/Trading Commencement Date**: August 07, 2026
- **Securities Maturity Dates**:
  - GOI TBILL 91D-05/11/26: November 05, 2026
  - GOI TBILL 182D-04/02/27: February 04, 2027
  - GOI TBILL 364D-05/08/27: August 05, 2027
  - SDL OR 6.94% 2030: August 05, 2030
  - SDL TN 7.49% 2036: August 05, 2036
  - SDL UK 7.64% 2038: August 05, 2038
  - SDL OR 7.64% 2040: August 05, 2040
  - SDL TN 7.62% 2041: August 05, 2041
  - SDL TN 7.7% 2051: August 05, 2051

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. This is a routine listing of government/state sovereign debt instruments. It provides additional liquidity channels and investment avenues for institutional and retail investors seeking low-risk fixed-income instruments.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Trading members need to ensure the new ISINs and symbols are mapped correctly in their front-end and back-office systems prior to the market open on August 07, 2026.
