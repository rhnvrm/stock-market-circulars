## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of New Fund Offers (NFOs) for Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market Index Fund and Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market ETF on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window opens on September 30, 2026, and closes on October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available: Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market Index Fund (Regular/Direct Growth) and Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market ETF Growth (Regular/Direct).
- NFO Period: September 30, 2026 to October 14, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- Allotment Date: October 22, 2026.
- Clear funds deadline: On or before October 21, 2026, till 03:00 pm in the designated Clearing Corporation account.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure clear funds through approved payment modes are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account by October 21, 2026, 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: September 30, 2026
- NFO End Date: October 14, 2026
- Funds Submission Deadline: October 21, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with access to new passive investment options tracking the BSE Total Market Index via the NSE MF platform.
