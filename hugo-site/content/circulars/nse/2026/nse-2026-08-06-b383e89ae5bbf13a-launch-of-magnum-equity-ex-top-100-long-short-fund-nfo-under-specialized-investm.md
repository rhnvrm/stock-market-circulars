## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that SBI Mutual Fund's **Magnum Equity Ex - Top 100 Long Short Fund NFO**, under the Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) category, will be available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 07, 2026, to August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** SBI Mutual Fund.
- **Scheme Name:** Magnum Equity Ex - Top 100 Long Short Fund (Specialized Investment Fund).
- **Subscription Timeline:** August 07, 2026, to August 20, 2026, until 11:59 PM.
- **Allotment Date:** August 27, 2026.
- **Available Scheme Plans & ISINs:**
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout (`INF200K30114`)
  - Direct Plan - Growth (`INF200K30106`)
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout (`INF200K30080`)
  - Direct Plan - IDCW (`INF200K30122`)
  - Regular Plan - Growth (`INF200K30072`)
  - Regular Plan - IDCW (`INF200K30098`)
- **Clearing Requirements:** Members must ensure clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 25, 2026, by 03:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes or updates are introduced by this circular. This is a routine operational enablement of an NFO on the exchange's mutual fund platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members participating on the NSE MF Invest platform must submit subscription orders during the designated transaction period.
- Members are required to secure and transfer clear funds to the Clearing Corporation account before the specified deadline of August 25, 2026, 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** August 07, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** August 20, 2026 (until 11:59 PM)
- **Funding Cut-off:** August 25, 2026 (until 03:00 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low. This is a standard enablement of a newly launched mutual fund scheme, facilitating broader investment opportunities for market participants and investors without disrupting existing operations.
