## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (debt instruments) on the capital market segment with effect from August 18, 2026. The circular details the security codes, ISINs, and lot sizes for the issued securities.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted for trading effective August 18, 2026.
- Includes issuance by Aditya Birla Capital Limited (7.98% 2031 Sr E1, ISIN: INE674K07168).
- Includes issuance by Sundaram Home Finance Limited (7.81% 2031 Sr 357, ISIN: INE667F07JH0).
- Trading lot size is set to 1 for both securities.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the specified securities shall be conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Issue Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date: August 18, 2026
- Maturity Dates: August 13, 2031 (Aditya Birla Capital) and August 14, 2031 (Sundaram Home Finance)

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and secondary market access for the newly listed privately placed debt securities.
