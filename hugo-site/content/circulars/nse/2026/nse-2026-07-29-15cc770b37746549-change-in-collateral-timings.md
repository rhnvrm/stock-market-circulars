## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has announced a revision in the cut-off timings for placing collateral release requests (cash, FDR, and securities) in the Currency Derivatives Segment. Under the updated schedule, the intraday cut-off for cash/FDR release and the EOD cut-off for securities release will be advanced from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM, starting July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Intraday Cash and FDR Release**: Cut-off time changed from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
- **End of Day Cash and FDR Release**: Cut-off time remains unchanged at 8:00 PM.
- **End of Day Securities Release**: Cut-off time changed from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
- **Effective Date**: The new timings are effective from July 30, 2026.
- **Reference Circular**: This is in partial modification to circular ref. no. 033/2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/CD/75317 dated July 21, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial modification of NCL currency derivatives collateral operational guidelines (Circular Ref No: 033/2026 / Download Ref No: NCL/CD/75317).

## Compliance Requirements

- All Clearing Members, Custodians, and Professional Clearing Members (PCMs) must ensure that collateral release requests are placed with NCL before the revised 8:00 PM cut-off time.
- Back-office and treasury systems should be configured to reflect the new cut-off deadlines to prevent failed or unprocessed requests.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Issue Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Effective Date of Changes**: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Operations**: Moving the daily cut-off time up by an hour reduces the time clearing members have to manage intraday margins and request collateral releases, necessitating tighter operational schedules.
- **Liquidity Management**: Clearing members must anticipate and coordinate their cash/FDR requirements earlier in the day.
