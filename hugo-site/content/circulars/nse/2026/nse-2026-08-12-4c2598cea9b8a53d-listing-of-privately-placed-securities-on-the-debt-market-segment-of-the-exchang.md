## Summary

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has admitted privately placed Commercial Papers (CPs) issued by ten corporate entities to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange. The listing is effective from August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Securities Listed:** Commercial Papers (CPs) representing short-term debt instruments of multiple corporate issuers.
- **Face Value:** ₹5,00,000 per unit for all listed issues.
- **Listing Details by Issuer:**
  - **Piramal Finance Limited:** 2,000 units (PFL CP 14/05/27, ISIN: INE202B14PT3)
  - **UltraTech Cement Limited:** 6,000 units (UCL CP 09/11/26, ISIN: INE481G14FW7)
  - **Aditya Birla Money Limited:** 2,000 units (ABML CP 09/11/26, ISIN: INE865C14QW7)
  - **Godrej Properties Limited:** 2,000 units (GPL CP 06/11/26 Sr 64, ISIN: INE484J14F48)
  - **Aditya Birla Capital Limited:** 15,000 units (ABCL CP 09/11/26 Sr 85, ISIN: INE674K14CV8)
  - **NTPC Limited:** 60,000 units (NTPC Limited CP 06/11/26, ISIN: INE733E14CH4)
  - **Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited:** 21,000 units (HPCL CP 11/09/26 Sr 54, ISIN: INE094A14KK6)
  - **Jio Credit Limited:** 19,000 units (JCL CP 09/11/26, ISIN: INE282H14154)
  - **Axis Securities Limited:** 10,000 units (ASL CP 11/11/26 Sr 41, ISIN: INE110O14JJ5)
  - **Kotak Securities Limited:** 17,000 units (KSL CP 11/11/26 Sr 143, ISIN: INE028E14XP8)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced in this circular. The action is a routine application of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no direct compliance requirements or actions required from general market participants or retail investors. The notification serves to inform trading members of the newly available instruments on the Debt Market Segment.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates:** 
  - August 11, 2026 (for Piramal Finance, UltraTech Cement, Aditya Birla Money, Godrej Properties, Aditya Birla Capital, NTPC, HPCL, and Jio Credit)
  - August 12, 2026 (for Axis Securities and Kotak Securities)
- **Effective Trading Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Redemption / Maturity Dates:**
  - Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited: September 11, 2026
  - Godrej Properties Limited: November 06, 2026
  - NTPC Limited: November 06, 2026
  - UltraTech Cement Limited: November 09, 2026
  - Aditya Birla Money Limited: November 09, 2026
  - Aditya Birla Capital Limited: November 09, 2026
  - Jio Credit Limited: November 09, 2026
  - Axis Securities Limited: November 11, 2026
  - Kotak Securities Limited: November 11, 2026
  - Piramal Finance Limited: May 14, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Improves liquidity and institutional access to short-term corporate debt instruments (Commercial Papers) on the exchange's Debt Market Segment.
- **Operational Impact:** Exchange trading members are authorized to deal in these securities using the designated security codes and ISINs specified in the Annexures with effect from August 12, 2026.
