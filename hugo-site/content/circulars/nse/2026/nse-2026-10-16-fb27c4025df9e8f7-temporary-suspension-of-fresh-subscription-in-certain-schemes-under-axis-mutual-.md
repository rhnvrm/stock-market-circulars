## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that Axis Mutual Fund has temporarily disabled certain schemes for lumpsum subscription, switch-in, and fresh registration under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026. This applies specifically to the Axis Gold and Silver Passive FoF.

## Key Points

- Temporary suspension affects Axis Gold and Silver Passive FoF.
- Impacted transactions: Lumpsum subscriptions/switches and fresh registrations of systematic transactions (SIP/STP) into the scheme with effect from 3:00 p.m. on October 16, 2026.
- Unaffected transactions: Existing systematic transactions, redemptions, switch-outs, SWP, and systematic transactions out of the scheme continue normally.
- Suspension is temporary until further notice by Axis AMC.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A (Operational update from AMC)

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors to note the temporary suspension of subscriptions and fresh systematic registrations in Axis Gold and Silver Passive FoF.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Cut-off Time: October 16, 2026, from 3:00 p.m. onwards.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding or planning fresh investments in Axis Gold and Silver Passive FoF will need to pause lumpsum/fresh SIP/STP allocations until the suspension is lifted by Axis AMC. Existing investments, redemptions, and systematic withdrawals remain fully operational.
