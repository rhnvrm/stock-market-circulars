## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) issued a circular notifying market participants that the face value and paid-up value of the equity shares of Tembo Global Industries Limited (TEMBO) will be changed from Rs. 10 to Re. 1 with effect from August 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name**: Tembo Global Industries Limited
- **Symbol**: TEMBO
- **Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value**: Rs. 10
- **New Face Value & Paid-up Value**: Re. 1
- **Effective Date**: August 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The face value of TEMBO's equity shares listed on the NSE will be updated from Rs. 10 to Re. 1 on the exchange's trading systems starting August 05, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market intermediaries must update their back-office systems, databases, and client portfolios to account for the stock split before the market opens on August 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity**: The 10-for-1 stock split will reduce the market price per share proportionally and increase the number of outstanding shares, which typically aims to improve liquidity and retail investor accessibility.
- **Operational Impact**: System adjustments are required to handle the corporate action on the ex-date.
