## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the Altiva Equity Long Short Fund NFO under the Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) category by Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Edelweiss Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Altiva Equity Long Short Fund (Direct and Regular plans with Growth and IDCW options)
- NFO Transaction Period: September 10, 2026 to September 24, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 01, 2026
- Available on the NSE MF Invest Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 29, 2026, till 3:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 10, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 24, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 29, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine availability of a new mutual fund scheme for trading and investment by market participants on the NSE platform.
