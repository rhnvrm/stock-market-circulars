## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the Abakkus Large & Mid Cap Fund NFO of Abakkus Mutual Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting from August 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Platform Reopening:** Enables ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Schemes Involved:**
  - Abakkus Large & Mid Cap Fund - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF2JJD01193, Category: Equity)
  - Abakkus Large & Mid Cap Fund - Regular Growth (ISIN: INF2JJD01201, Category: Equity)
- **Effective Date:** August 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

This circular is informational and operational, with no changes to external regulatory frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors utilizing the NSE MF Invest Platform should update their systems and client advisory records to support transactions in these newly reopened schemes starting August 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 05, 2026
- **Effective Date for Scheme Transactions:** August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low operational impact. This is a routine onboarding of mutual fund schemes to the digital investment platform, expanding investment options available to platform users.
