## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of the ICICI Prudential Contra Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window runs from September 28, 2026, to October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- NFO Transaction Window: September 28, 2026 to October 12, 2026 (till 11:30 pm).
- Available schemes include Growth, IDCW, IDCW Payout, and Direct Plan variants with corresponding ISINs.
- Allotment Date: October 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 14, 2026 till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: September 28, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: October 12, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 14, 2026
- Allotment Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates participation for trading members and investors in the ICICI Prudential Contra Fund NFO via the NSE MF Invest platform.
