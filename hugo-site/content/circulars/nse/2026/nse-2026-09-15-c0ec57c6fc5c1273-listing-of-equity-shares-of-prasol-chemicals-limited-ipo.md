## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Prasol Chemicals Limited under the Initial Public Offering (IPO) route.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Prasol Chemicals Limited
- Symbol: PRASOLCHEM
- ISIN: INE455U01024
- The exact date of listing of the security will be announced through a separate circular.

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members of the exchange are informed of the upcoming listing details for trading and settlement setup.

- Circular Date: September 15, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular

Enables trading members and market participants to prepare for the commencement of trading in equity shares of Prasol Chemicals Limited.
