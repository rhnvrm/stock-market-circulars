## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a scheduled complete downtime for the NSE MF Invest Platform due to maintenance activity. The downtime is scheduled on October 03, 2026, from 10:00 hrs to 12:00 hrs.

## Key Points

- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform
- Scheduled Downtime: 03 October 2026, 10:00 hrs to 12:00 hrs
- Scope: Complete downtime across all modes including APIs; no orders will be accepted during this period.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine platform maintenance notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to refrain from carrying out any activities and transactions in the system during the specified time window. Members connecting via API must inform their technical team and vendors accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Maintenance Window Start: 03 October 2026, 10:00 hrs
- Maintenance Window End: 03 October 2026, 12:00 hrs

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of mutual fund transactions and order placements on the NSE MF Invest Platform for a duration of 2 hours. Operations will resume normally post-maintenance.
