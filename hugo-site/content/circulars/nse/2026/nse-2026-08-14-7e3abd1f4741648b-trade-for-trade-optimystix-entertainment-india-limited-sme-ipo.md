## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Optimystix Entertainment India Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM), following previous exchange circulars and SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Security affected: Optimystix Entertainment India Limited (Symbol: OPTIMYSTIX).
- Transfer from trade for trade segment (series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM).
- Effective date: August 28, 2026.
- Reference to previous Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/75736 dated August 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members need to note the change in trading series and adjust their systems accordingly for settlement and trading of OPTIMYSTIX shares.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Allows normal rolling settlement for Optimystix Entertainment India Limited, which typically enhances liquidity and trading flexibility compared to the trade-for-trade segment.
