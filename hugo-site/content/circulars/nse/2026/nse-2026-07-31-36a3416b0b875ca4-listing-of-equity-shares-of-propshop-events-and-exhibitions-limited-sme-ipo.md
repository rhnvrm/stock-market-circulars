## Summary

This circular announces the listing of the equity shares of Propshop Events and Exhibitions Limited on the SME platform of the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE Emerge) following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). Additionally, it presents the Post-Issue Shareholding Pattern of the company as of July 30, 2026, which has been filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- **Listed Entity**: Propshop Events and Exhibitions Limited (Post-Issue).
- **Listing Platform**: National Stock Exchange (NSE) SME platform (NSE Emerge).
- **Asset Class/Security**: Equity shares.
- **Reporting Date**: July 30, 2026.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: The shareholding pattern is filed in compliance with Regulation 31(1)(a) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
- **Declaration Details**:
  - The company has locked-in shares (YES).
  - No partly paid-up shares, convertible securities, warrants, outstanding ESOPs, or depository receipts are issued.
  - No promoter-held shares are pledged, encumbered under Non-Disposal Undertaking (NDU), or otherwise encumbered.
  - The company does not have any shares with differential voting rights.
  - There is no Significant Beneficial Owner (SBO) declared.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no direct changes to exchange or SEBI regulations. This is a standard post-IPO listing procedure and a routine compliance disclosure under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For the Issuer (Propshop Events and Exhibitions Limited)**:
  - Must ensure timely disclosure of shareholding patterns under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations post-listing on a periodic basis (quarterly and upon any capital restructuring).
  - Adhere to the lock-in guidelines for promoters' shares and pre-IPO shares as prescribed by SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations.
- **For Market Participants/Investors**:
  - Review the post-issue shareholding structure and locked-in details to assess float availability and liquidity on the NSE SME platform.

## Important Dates

- **As-on Date for Shareholding Pattern**: July 30, 2026.
- **Listing Date (NSE SME Platform)**: August 3, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: This listing increases the visibility of Propshop Events and Exhibitions Limited and provides liquidity to its shareholders. Given that it is listed on the NSE Emerge SME platform, trading is subject to specific lot sizes (e.g., 2,000 shares) and is intended for informed/institutional investors. The disclosure of zero promoter encumbrances and outstanding convertible instruments reduces dilution and leverage risks, providing confidence to incoming public investors.
