## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Admission of commercial papers issued by multiple entities including NIIF Infrastructure Finance, Aditya Birla Money, Godrej Properties, Export Import Bank of India, CEAT, Godrej Industries, India Cements, Motilal Oswal Finvest, Hero FinCorp, and Nirma.
- Effective date of listing: September 24, 2026.
- Allotment date across issues: September 23, 2026.
- Maturity dates range from October 2026 to June 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must comply with standard debt market segment regulations and settlement procedures.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 23, 2026
- Listing Date: September 24, 2026
- Redemption Dates: October 23, 2026 to June 14, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine debt financing operation providing short-term liquidity for issuers with minimal impact on broader market operations.
