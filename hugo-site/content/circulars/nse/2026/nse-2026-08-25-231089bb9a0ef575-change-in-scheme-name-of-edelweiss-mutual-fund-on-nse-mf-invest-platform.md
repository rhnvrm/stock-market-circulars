## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026. This change is implemented in compliance with SEBI circular dated February 26, 2026, on Categorization and Rationalisation of Mutual Fund Schemes.

## Key Points

- Edelweiss Low Duration Fund has been renamed to Edelweiss Ultra Short to Short term Fund.
- Edelweiss Government Securities Fund has been renamed to Edelweiss Gilt Fund.
- The changes are effective from August 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI circular no. HO/24/13/15(2)2026-IMDRAC4/I/5764/2026 dated February 26, 2026, regarding Categorization and Rationalisation of Mutual Fund Schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary changes will be carried out in the relevant sections of the SID, KIM, and SAI of the designated schemes of Edelweiss Mutual Fund.
- All other provisions of the SID / KIM / SAI except as specifically modified will remain unchanged.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Notice Date: August 21, 2026
- Circular Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; investors and trading members need to take note of the updated scheme names for transactions on the NSE MF Invest platform.
