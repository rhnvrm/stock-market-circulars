## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on specific surveillance criteria, effective September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II are subject to enhanced margin requirements.
- Applicable rate of margin for ST-ASM Stage I is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100% w.e.f. September 22, 2026, on all open positions as on September 21, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Applicable rate of margin for ST-ASM Stage II is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher) w.e.f. September 22, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened margin criteria under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure framework for listed scrips satisfying volatility and surveillance criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin rates (50% to 100%) on open and new positions for the affected securities starting September 22, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Provisions: September 22, 2026 (on all open positions as on September 21, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Increased capital requirements for market participants trading in the shortlisted securities due to higher margin obligations, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in those scrips.
