## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a surveillance circular (Ref No: NSE/SURV/75669) regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) process. These updates specify the addition of new securities to ASM Stage I and the transition of other securities w.e.f. August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **New Shortlistings to ASM Stage I:** Impex Ferro Tech Limited (`IMPEXFERRO`) and Reliance Infrastructure Limited (`RELINFRA`) have been shortlisted under ASM IBC Stage I w.e.f. August 11, 2026.
- **Securities Transition:** Neueon Corporation Limited (`NEUEON`) is moved to "Receipt of corporate announcement / disclosure from the company" under the IBC framework w.e.f. August 11, 2026.
- **Consolidated List:** The consolidated list of securities under ASM IBC includes Aban Offshore Limited (`ABAN`) and Impex Ferro Tech Limited (`IMPEXFERRO`) in Stage I.
- **Clarification on Nature of Action:** Shortlisting under the ASM IBC framework is purely for market surveillance purposes and does not represent an adverse action against the listed entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the active list of securities subject to Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework as per criteria set in previous circulars (the most recent being circular NSE/SURV/75652 dated August 07, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Stockbrokers and trading members must implement the applicable margin rates, price bands, and other risk management controls associated with ASM Stage I for `IMPEXFERRO` and `RELINFRA` effective from August 11, 2026.
- **Investors:** Market participants trading in these securities should take note of the surveillance status and associated trading parameters.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Moderate for the specifically named stocks (`RELINFRA` and `IMPEXFERRO`), as placement under ASM Stage I typically involves higher margin requirements and could lead to temporary volume/liquidity reduction.
- **Operational Impact:** Stockbrokers and compliance teams need to adjust risk parameters and margin collections for the specified stocks in their trading systems prior to market opening on August 11, 2026.
