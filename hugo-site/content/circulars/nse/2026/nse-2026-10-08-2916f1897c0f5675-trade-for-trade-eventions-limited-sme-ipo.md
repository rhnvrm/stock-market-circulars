## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the transfer of Eventions Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (series: SM) effective October 23, 2026, following earlier circulars and SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Security: Eventions Limited (Symbol: EVENTIONS)
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: ST)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Reference: Follows Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76763 dated October 07, 2026 and SEBI guidelines CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Movement of securities from trade-for-trade settlement to normal rolling settlement segment upon completion of mandatory trade-for-trade period for SME IPOs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members need to update their systems regarding the change in trading series for Eventions Limited effective from October 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date of Transfer: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in Eventions Limited shares will move from trade-for-trade settlement (where compulsory delivery-based trading applies without netting) to regular rolling settlement, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility.
