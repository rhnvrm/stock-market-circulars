## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding multiple settlements scheduled on October 21, 2026, on account of a settlement holiday on October 20, 2026, in the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment. Revised timings for pay-in, pay-out, auction cycles, and unpaid MTF file uploads have been detailed.

## Key Points

- Multiple settlements scheduled on October 21, 2026, due to settlement holiday on October 20, 2026.
- Includes revised settlement schedules, pay-in, and pay-out timings for trade dates 19-Oct-26 and 20-Oct-26.
- Revised auction trading and settlement cycle under T+1 outlined.
- Auction of shortages of settlement 2026111 will be conducted in 2026112; no auction in 2026111.
- Specific timings for uploading UNPAID MTF files for EGR Normal provided for October 20 and October 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of settlement schedules, auction cycles, and operational timings for the Electronic Gold Receipt segment due to a holiday.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must take note of the revised settlement timings, pay-in/pay-out hours, auction schedules, and UNPAID MTF file upload cut-off timings at depositories.

## Important Dates

- Settlement Holiday: October 20, 2026
- Multiple Settlements Date: October 21, 2026
- Trade Dates: October 19, 2026 and October 20, 2026
- Auction and MTF Timings: October 20–23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Clearing members trading in Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) must align their fund/securities pay-in and pay-out operations, auction participation, and MTF file uploads with the revised holiday-adjusted schedule to avoid defaults.
