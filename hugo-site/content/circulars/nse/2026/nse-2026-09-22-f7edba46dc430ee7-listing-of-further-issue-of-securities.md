## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further issue of equity shares of Saakshi Medtech and Panels Limited on the SME EMERGE segment effective from September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Saakshi Medtech and Panels Limited
- Symbol: SAAKSHI
- Series: SM
- ISIN: INE0PSK01027 (Temporary ISIN IN8********* applicable for demat credit prior to listing)
- Issue Type: Preferential basis
- Number of Securities: 2,010,259 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each at an issue price of Rs. 207/-
- Distinctive Number Range: 17656001 to 19666259
- Market Lot: 600 shares
- Lock-in Expiry Date: 31-Mar-2027

## Regulatory Changes

- Admitted in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the securities shall be in the specified market lot and identified by designated codes.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 21-Aug-2026
- Effective Date: 23-Sep-2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: 31-Mar-2027

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity capital base of Saakshi Medtech and Panels Limited through preferential allotment.
