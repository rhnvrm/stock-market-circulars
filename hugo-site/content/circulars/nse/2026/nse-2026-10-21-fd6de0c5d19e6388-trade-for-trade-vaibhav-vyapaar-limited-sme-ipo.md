## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of Vaibhav Vyapaar Limited's securities from the trade-for-trade segment to the rolling segment.

## Key Points

- Symbol: LOANFRONT
- Company Name: Vaibhav Vyapaar Limited
- Transfer from trade-for-trade segment (series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM).
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; executed pursuant to SEBI guidelines (circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012) and previous exchange circular NSE/CML/77010 dated October 19, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the segment change for trading in the security.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables regular rolling settlement for the security, improving liquidity and trading convenience for market participants.
