## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Motilal Oswal Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund by Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund is available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from September 25, 2026, to October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund
- Schemes available: Motilal Oswal Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF247L01HO1) and Regular Growth (ISIN: INF247L01HP8)
- NFO Transaction Window: September 25, 2026 to October 09, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: October 15, 2026
- Clear funds submission deadline: October 15, 2026 (till 02:00 pm)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported in this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through approved payment modes are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 15, 2026, till 02:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: September 25, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: October 09, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: October 15, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 15, 2026 (02:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates investors and trading members in subscribing to the Motilal Oswal Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund NFO through the NSE MF Invest Platform with clear timelines for subscription and fund clearance.
