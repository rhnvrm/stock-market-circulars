## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by Zerodha CASE Asset Management Private Limited under the Zerodha Nifty Next 100 ETF scheme, effective from October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Zerodha Nifty Next 100 ETF
- Symbol: NXT100CASE
- ISIN: INF0R8F01257
- Date of Allotment: October 08, 2026
- Number of Units: 5,533,110
- Face Value and Paid Up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 9.99
- Market Lot: 1 unit
- Market Makers include East India Securities Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, and Dolat Algotech Limited among others.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard listing circular in pursuance of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with trading access to the newly listed Zerodha Nifty Next 100 ETF on the NSE capital market segment.
