## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced revisions to the contract parameters and expiry dates for Gold Mini Futures and Gold Mini Options on Futures. These changes are applicable from October 05, 2026, for contracts expiring in October 2026 and onwards.

## Key Points

- **Gold Mini Futures Expiry Date Revision**: Changed from the 5th day of the contract expiry month (or preceding working day if holiday) to the 3rd last business day of the month.
- **Gold Mini Options on Futures Expiry Date Revision**: Changed to five business days prior to the first business day of the Tender Period of the underlying futures contract.
- **Pre-maturity**: Existing Gold Mini Futures and Gold Mini Options on Futures contracts will be pre-matured on the end of day (EOD) of October 01, 2026.
- **Extranet Update**: Members must upload the latest `co_contract.gz` file from the NSE Extranet before trading commences on October 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of expiry dates and staggered delivery periods for Gold Mini Futures and Options contracts starting October 2026.
- Pre-maturity of existing contracts on October 01, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must download and upload the updated `co_contract.gz` file from the NSE Extranet (`/comtftp/comtcommon`) prior to trading on October 05, 2026.
- Ensure client positions and open contracts are managed in accordance with the pre-maturity on October 01, 2026, and revised expiry schedules.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: September 24, 2026
- **Pre-maturity Date for Existing Contracts**: October 01, 2026 (EOD)
- **Effective Date**: October 05, 2026
- **Revised Expiry Examples**:
  - Gold Mini Futures (Oct 2026): October 28, 2026
  - Gold Mini Futures (Nov 2026): November 25, 2026
  - Gold Mini Futures (Dec 2026): December 29, 2026
  - Gold Mini Options on Futures (Oct 2026): October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members dealing in commodity derivatives, specifically gold futures and options, must update their trading systems with the latest contract files and adjust their risk management strategies for pre-matured contracts.
