## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for multiple schemes of Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026, pursuant to SEBI circulars on categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes.

## Key Points

- Covers numerous schemes across debt, hybrid, and equity categories managed by Kotak Mutual Fund.
- Examples include renaming 'Kotak Savings Fund' to 'Kotak Ultra Short Term Fund' and 'Kotak Low Duration Fund' to 'Kotak Ultra Short to Short Term Fund'.
- Changes are implemented in line with SEBI circular guidelines on scheme categorization and characteristics without altering fundamental attributes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI Circular No. HO/24/13/15(2)2026-IMD-RAC4/I/5764/2026 dated February 26, 2026, and Master Circular dated March 20, 2026, on scheme categorization and rationalization.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMC and platform participants to update scheme nomenclature and reflect the revised names across trading and investment platforms.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Circular Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update affecting scheme display names and terminology on platforms; no direct impact on unit holders' existing investments or fundamental attributes.
