## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has informed all members that the Franklin India Short Term Fund NFO of Franklin India Mutual Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic transaction facilities (SIP, STP, and SWP) on the NSE MF Invest Platform with effect from August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Reopening Date:** Reopens for ongoing transactions on August 13, 2026.
- **Facilities Enabled:** Subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic facilities (SIP, STP, SWP).
- **Plans Covered:** Both Regular and Direct plans are enabled across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- **Asset Class:** All six enabled plans are categorized as Debt schemes.

## Scheme Details

| Sr. No | Scheme Name | ISIN | Category |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Franklin India Short Term Fund - Regular Growth | INF090I01YT5 | Debt |
| 2 | Franklin India Short Term Fund - Regular IDCW Payout | INF090I01YU3 | Debt |
| 3 | Franklin India Short Term Fund - Regular IDCW Reinvestment | INF090I01YV1 | Debt |
| 4 | Franklin India Short Term Fund - Direct Growth | INF090I01YW9 | Debt |
| 5 | Franklin India Short Term Fund - Direct IDCW Payout | INF090I01YX7 | Debt |
| 6 | Franklin India Short Term Fund - Direct IDCW Reinvestment | INF090I01YY5 | Debt |

## Regulatory Changes

There are no changes to existing regulations. This is a routine operational update regarding scheme availability on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Mutual Fund Members:** Trading members and distributors on the NSE MF Invest platform must update their system catalog and scheme lists to allow ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP requests for the listed ISINs starting August 13, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Reopening / Effective Date:** August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is standard operational maintenance allowing transactions of an existing fund house's schemes on the exchange platform.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Trading members and distributors on the NSE MF Invest Platform will have to enable these specific ISINs for their customers.
