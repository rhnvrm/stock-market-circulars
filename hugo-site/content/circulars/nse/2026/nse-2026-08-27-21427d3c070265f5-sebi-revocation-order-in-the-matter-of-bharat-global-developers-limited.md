## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding SEBI's revocation order in the matter of Bharat Global Developers Limited. SEBI has revoked the debarment and restraint on buying, selling, or dealing in securities for several noticees with immediate effect, modifying its earlier directions from December 2024.

## Key Points

- References previous NSE Circular No. NSE/INVG/65737 dated December 23, 2024, and SEBI Order dated December 23, 2024.
- SEBI vide its order no. WTM/KV/CFID/CFID/32590/2026-27 dated August 27, 2026, revoked the debarment for specified noticees.
- Affected noticees include Nirali Prabhatbhai Karetha, Ghanshyam Babubhai Mistry, Bhupinder Duchaina, Arun Mohanbhai Kordia, Kavit Bharat Sanghavi, Sheetal Mithilesh Jhala, Minal Ghanshyamsinh Gohil, and others.
- Revocation takes effect immediately.

## Regulatory Changes

- Lifting of trading and capital market access restrictions previously imposed under SEBI interim/ad-interim or final orders for the specified noticees.

## Compliance Requirements

- NSE members are advised to take note of the revocation and update their internal compliance records regarding debarred entities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Permits the specified noticees to resume normal securities market operations and dealings, restoring their access to capital markets.
