## Summary

National Stock Exchange has notified the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by Nippon Life India Asset Management Limited for Nippon India CRISIL-IBX FAR Gilt ETF effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Nippon India CRISIL-IBX FAR Gilt ETF
- Symbol: FARBEES
- ISIN: INF204KC1HS1
- Number of Units: 1,578,503
- Face Value & Paid Up Value: Rs. 10/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 132.14/-)
- Date of Allotment: October 14, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Market Makers: Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, East India Securities Limited, Kanjalochana Finserve Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard compliance with NSE trading and settlement regulations for ETF units.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 15, 2026
- Date of Allotment: October 14, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides secondary market liquidity and trading access for investors in the Nippon India CRISIL-IBX FAR Gilt ETF on the NSE capital market segment.
