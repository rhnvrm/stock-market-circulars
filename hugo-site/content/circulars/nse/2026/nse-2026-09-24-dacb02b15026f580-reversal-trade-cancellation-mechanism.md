## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular detailing the Reversal Trade Cancellation Mechanism (RTCM) applicable for the month of October 2026. The circular specifies the methodology used for shortlisting symbols across series EQ, BE, BZ, SM, ST, and SZ.

## Key Points

- RTCM is applicable to symbols listed in file `CM_RTCM_oct2026.csv` across series EQ, BE, BZ, SM, ST & SZ.
- Exclusions from RTCM include approved securities list published by NSE Clearing Ltd, constituents of Nifty 500 index, and ETFs.
- The symbol list is made available on the extranet path `/common/Surveillance/RTCM`.
- RTCM serves to help prevent the incidence of suspicious reversal trades between two PANs upon breach of specific thresholds.

## Regulatory Changes

- Finalized and published the list of symbols subject to RTCM for October 2026 in accordance with guidelines established under circular NSE/SURV/62493.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the applicability of RTCM for listed symbols and ensure strict adherence to circular NSE/INVG/46662 dated December 16, 2020. The RTCM facility does not absolve members of their core regulatory and compliance obligations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Month: October 2026
- Circular Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members dealing in the shortlisted securities must monitor trading patterns to prevent unintended or suspicious reversal trades and ensure robust surveillance compliance.
