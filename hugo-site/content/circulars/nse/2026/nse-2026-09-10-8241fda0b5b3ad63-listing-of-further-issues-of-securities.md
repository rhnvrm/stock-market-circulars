## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for multiple companies admitted to dealings with effect from September 11, 2026, pursuant to allotments made under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 11, 2026.
- Total Companies Included: 16 companies including Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Axis Bank Limited, Bajaj Healthcare Limited, Coromandel International Limited, The Federal Bank Limited, Godawari Power And Ispat limited, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, Max Healthcare Institute Limited, MBL Infrastructure Limited, MphasiS Limited, Niva Bupa Health Insurance Company Limited, Quess Corp Limited, Sai Life Sciences Limited, Tata Elxsi Limited, Wanbury Limited, and Zensar Technologies Limited.
- Reason for Issuance: Allotment of equity shares under ESOP.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure dealings are conducted using designated security codes and respective market lot sizes as specified in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the respective listed entities without direct market-wide systemic impact.
