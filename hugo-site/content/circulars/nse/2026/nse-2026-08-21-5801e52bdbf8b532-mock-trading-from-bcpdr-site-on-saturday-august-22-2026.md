## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for Currency Derivatives members from the Disaster Recovery (DR) / Business Continuity Plan (BCP) site on Saturday, August 22, 2026. The session includes a non-graceful shutdown from the primary site and is followed by a live re-login session from the primary site to ensure smooth operations on Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, August 22, 2026.
- Normal Market open time: 09:00 hrs; Close time: 17:00 hrs; Trade modification end time: 17:30 hrs at BCP site.
- Re-Log in session from Primary Site: Start time 18:30 hrs, Close time 19:00 hrs.
- Exchange operations will shift from the primary site to the DR site as a non-graceful shutdown.
- All outstanding orders will be purged before trading starts from the BCP site.
- Multicast TBT sequence numbers will start from '1' once trading begins from the BCP site.
- No financial obligations will arise from trades executed during the mock session.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The circular relates to operational readiness and mock testing as per standard regulatory guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members using NNF software must manually clear outstanding orders of session 1 before trading from the BCP site.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.
- Members should take necessary actions as per circular NSE/MSD/48662 to bring systems into a consistent state following the non-graceful shutdown.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 22, 2026
- Live Re-login: August 22, 2026 (18:30 hrs to 19:00 hrs)
- Normal Trading Resume: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness for members to ensure seamless connectivity and system recovery during unexpected site switchovers or disaster scenarios.
