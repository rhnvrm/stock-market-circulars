## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF NFO by Shriram Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window opens on September 11, 2026, and closes on September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Shriram Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF (Direct and Regular plans including Growth and IDCW options)
- Subscription Window: September 11, 2026 to September 24, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 28, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 11, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 24, 2026
- Allotment Date: September 28, 2026
- Fund Pay-in Deadline: September 28, 2026 (03:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors access to a new gold ETF fund of funds product on the NSE platform.
