## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular notifying members of SEBI Order no. QJA/MN/IVD-1/ID10/32730/2026-27 dated September 24, 2026. SEBI has restrained Omaxe Limited and several associated noticees from accessing the securities market and prohibited them from buying, selling, or otherwise dealing in securities for specified debarment periods due to non-compliance with minimum public shareholding norms.

## Key Points

- Noticees debarred: Omaxe Limited (3 months), Rohtas Goel (1 year), Sunil Goel (1 year), Jai Bhagwan Goel (1 year), Dream Home Developers Pvt Ltd (1 year), and Guild Builders Pvt Ltd (1 year) (along with Sushma Goel and Seema Goel).
- Reason: Non-Compliance of Minimum Public Shareholding in Omaxe Limited.
- Noticees with open exchange-traded derivative positions are permitted to square off/close out such positions within 3 months from the date of the order or at contract expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Settlement of pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions executed prior to the close of trading on the order date is permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of market debarment and trading restrictions under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1), 11B(2) and 11(2)(j) read with Section 12A(1)(a) of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

NSE members are advised to take note of the debarred entities, ensure compliance, and verify entities against the consolidated list available on the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 24, 2026
- Derivative Square-off Window: Within 3 months from the date of the order

## Impact Assessment

High impact on the company, promoters, and related corporate entities due to regulatory debarment and restrictions on securities market access.
