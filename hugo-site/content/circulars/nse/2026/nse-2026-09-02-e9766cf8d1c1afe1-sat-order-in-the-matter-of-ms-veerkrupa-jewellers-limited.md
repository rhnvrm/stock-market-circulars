## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to the Hon'ble SAT order in the matter of M/s Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited. Following the deposit of the full penalty amount by the appellant, Mr. Rushabh Shroff, the earlier restraint imposed by SEBI has been stayed.

## Key Points

- References earlier NSE Circular No. NSE/INVG/74494 dated June 01, 2026, and SEBI Order No. QJA/SS/IVD-2/ID13/32429/2026-27 dated May 29, 2026.
- Mr. Rushabh Shroff (PAN: ATZPS8741C) was previously restrained from accessing the securities market for 5 years.
- Hon'ble SAT vide order dated July 20, 2026, granted a stay on the impugned order subject to the deposit of the full penalty.
- SEBI confirmed via email on September 02, 2026, that the appellant has paid the penalty amount in favour of SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Stay of the debarment/restraint order against Mr. Rushabh Shroff subsequent to penalty compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the stay and ensure compliance with exchange regulations and debarred entities lists.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 02, 2026
- SAT Order Date: July 20, 2026
- SEBI Communication Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational status for Mr. Rushabh Shroff is updated in accordance with the SAT stay order upon penalty fulfillment.
