## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Based on the surveillance criteria, five securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I, effective from August 05, 2026, with margin changes taking effect from August 06, 2026. No securities were shortlisted for ST-ASM Stage II, and no other transitions or exclusions were specified in the active portion of the annexures.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusion**: Five securities have met the criteria for inclusion in ST-ASM Stage I: A2Z Infra Engineering Limited (A2ZINFRA), Amba Auto Sales and Services Limited (AMBAAUTO), Thangamayil Jewellery Limited (THANGAMAYL), Transpek Industry Limited (TRANSPEK), and Ujaas Energy Limited (UEL).
- **Margin Revisions**: The applicable rate of margin for Stage I securities is increased to 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100%.
- **Scope of Margin Applicability**: The revised margin rates apply to all open positions as of August 05, 2026, and all new positions created starting August 06, 2026.
- **Surveillance Nature**: The Exchange emphasizes that ASM shortlisting is purely a proactive market surveillance measure and should not be interpreted as adverse action against the companies involved.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of higher margin requirements (minimum 50%, capped at 100%) for the five specified securities under the ST-ASM Stage I framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must update risk parameters to collect a minimum 50% margin (or existing margin, whichever is higher) on all open and new positions in A2ZINFRA, AMBAAUTO, THANGAMAYL, TRANSPEK, and UEL.
- **Positions Handling**: Apply the revised margins starting August 06, 2026, on all open positions as of August 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **August 04, 2026**: Date of circular issuance.
- **August 05, 2026**: Effective date of shortlisting in ST-ASM Stage I; date for determining existing open positions.
- **August 06, 2026**: Effective date for the revised margin rates on open and new positions.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: The affected stocks (A2ZINFRA, AMBAAUTO, THANGAMAYL, TRANSPEK, UEL) may experience reduced leverage and potential temporary decreases in trading volume or liquidity.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading members must ensure their automated risk management and margin calculation systems are updated to enforce the ST-ASM Stage I margin rates by the specified effective date.
