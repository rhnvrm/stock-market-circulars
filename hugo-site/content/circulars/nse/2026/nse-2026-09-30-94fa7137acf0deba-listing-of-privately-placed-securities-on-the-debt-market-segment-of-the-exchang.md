## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 30, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Admissions include commercial papers from issuers such as Kotak Securities Limited, Godrej Agrovet Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, Sundaram Finance Limited, Tata Projects Limited, Minda Corporation Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, and Aditya Birla Capital Limited.
- Effective date of listing: September 30, 2026.
- Various maturity dates ranging from October 2026 to September 2027 with a face value of 500,000 per security.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine debt listing notification under NSE Debt Market Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers must comply with applicable regulations and listing agreements for debt securities on the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 29, 2026
- Listing / Effective Date: September 30, 2026
- Redemption Dates: October 2026 - September 2027 (varying by security)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing of commercial papers with no direct impact on equity market pricing or broad regulatory compliance.
