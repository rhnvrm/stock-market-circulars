## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) is conducting a mock trading session for its Debt Segment from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site on Saturday, August 22, 2026. Members are advised to participate actively to test connectivity across various platforms.

## Key Points

- **Date:** August 22, 2026
- **Platforms Covered:** Order Matching Platform, Negotiated Trade Reporting Platform, Tri-Party Repo Market Platform, Electronic Bidding Platform (EBP), CBRICS, and RFQ.
- **URLs:** Existing production URLs will be used; no configuration changes are required at the member end.
- **Settlement & Obligations:** Trades resulting from the mock session do not attract any fund or security pay-in/pay-out obligations.
- **UCC Handling:** Any new UCC uploaded during the mock session will be removed upon completion and must be re-uploaded after activity ends.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine mock trading and Disaster Recovery drill.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should participate actively in the mock session to verify connectivity. No data files should be transferred for this session, and trades reported on the Negotiated Trade Reporting Platform during the mock session will not be considered.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Session:** August 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational check only. There is no financial or structural impact on live market operations.
