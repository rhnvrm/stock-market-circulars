## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of equity shares for three companies under the SME EMERGE segment, effective from August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Magson Retail and Distribution Limited: 428,950 equity shares issued under preferential allotment (ISIN: INE0O1S01012, Symbol: MAGSON).
- Moxsh Overseas Educon Limited: 41,667 equity shares issued under preferential allotment (ISIN: INE0N6D01014, Symbol: MOXSH).
- Oneclick Logistics India Limited: 133,000 equity shares issued under preferential allotment (ISIN: INE0OPS01011, Symbol: OLIL).
- Trading in these securities will be permitted in designated market lots effective from August 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to trading regulations and designated lot sizes as specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Addition of further issued shares to the SME EMERGE segment trading platform for the respective companies.
