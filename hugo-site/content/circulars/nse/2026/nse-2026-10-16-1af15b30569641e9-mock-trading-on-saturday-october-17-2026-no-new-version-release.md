## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, October 17, 2026, with no new version release.

## Key Points

- Mock Date: October 17, 2026.
- Pre-open session: Open at 12:45 hrs, Close at 12:53 hrs (with random closure in the last one minute).
- Normal Market: Open at 13:00 hrs, Close at 14:00 hrs.
- Block Deal Window Session: Open at 13:30 hrs, Close at 13:45 hrs.
- Auction Market: Open at 13:15 hrs, Close at 13:50 hrs.
- Trade modification end time: 14:10 hrs.
- Live Re-login: Start time 15:00 hrs, Close time 15:30 hrs.
- NEAT+ Version Details: Version 1.0.2 is recommended; Version 1.0.1 discontinuation date is November 14, 2026 (mock).
- Default login password: Neat@EG1.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational mock trading circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of session timings, connectivity parameters, and ensure that NEATPlus EGR Version 1.0.2 is installed as per the guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 17, 2026
- NEAT+ Version 1.0.1 Discontinuation (Mock): November 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out. It provides members an opportunity to test systems and connectivity.
