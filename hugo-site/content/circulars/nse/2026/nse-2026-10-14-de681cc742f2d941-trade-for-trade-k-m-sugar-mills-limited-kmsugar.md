## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the transfer of K. M. Sugar Mills Limited (KMSUGAR) from the trade-for-trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ) pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company: K. M. Sugar Mills Limited
- Symbol: KMSUGAR
- Action: Transferred from trade-for-trade segment (series BE) to rolling segment (series EQ)
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Reference: NSE/CML/76903 (superseding/referencing earlier circular NSE/CML/76575 and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012)

## Regulatory Changes

- The security will no longer be restricted to the trade-for-trade settlement mechanism (where netting of trades is not allowed and delivery is mandatory on a gross basis).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that their trading and back-office systems reflect the correct series (EQ) for KMSUGAR starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors will be able to trade KMSUGAR shares under standard rolling settlement, improving liquidity and normal market operations.
