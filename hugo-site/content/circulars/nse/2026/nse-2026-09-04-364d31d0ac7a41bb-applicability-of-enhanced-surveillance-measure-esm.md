## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Securities satisfying the criteria have been shortlisted for inclusion under ESM Stage-I, moving from Stage-I to Stage-II, or moving from Stage-II to Stage-I, along with applicable margin requirements and segment shifts.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage-I attract a minimum 100% margin effective September 08, 2026, on all open positions as on September 07, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shift of securities from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 08, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 07, 2026.
- Affected securities include ARIES, RBZJEWEL, SRIKPRIND (Stage I), HOACFOODS, SABAR (Stage I to Stage II), and FAIRCHEMOR, NRL (Stage II to Stage I).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures in conjunction with prevailing surveillance frameworks to safeguard market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities and note the segment shifts to Trade-for-Trade.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II / Stage movements: September 07, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade shift: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement terms for the specified securities will be directly impacted due to movement into Trade-for-Trade segments, 100% margin requirements, and price band restrictions.
