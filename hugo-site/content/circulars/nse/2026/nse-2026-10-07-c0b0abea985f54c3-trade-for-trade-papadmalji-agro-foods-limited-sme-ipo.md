## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of Papadmalji Agro Foods Limited from the trade for trade segment (Series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM), effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Papadmalji Agro Foods Limited
- Symbol: PAPADMALJI
- Segment Change: Transferred from trade for trade segment (Series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026
- Reference: Pursuant to SEBI guidelines (CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012) and exchange circular NSE/CML/76746.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities are moved from the trade-for-trade segment to the normal rolling segment in accordance with established SEBI guidelines for SME IPOs once initial surveillance/holding requirements are met.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate segment mapping and operational readiness for trading PAPADMALJI in the rolling segment (Series: SM) starting October 22, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables regular rolling settlement for Papadmalji Agro Foods Limited shares, facilitating smoother trading and improved liquidity for market participants.
