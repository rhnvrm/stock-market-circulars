## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Several securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II, while some securities are moving between stages effective August 17, 2026 (based on positions as of August 14, 2026).

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Allcargo Logistics Limited, Finolex Cables Limited, PNGS Reva Diamond Jewellery Limited, Singer India Limited, TCPL Packaging Limited, and Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited.
- Securities moving from ST-ASM Stage II to Stage I: Picturepost Studios Limited (PPSL).
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage I is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage II is 100% or existing margin, capped at 100%.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ST-ASM framework as per ongoing exchange guidelines to monitor market volatility and safeguard investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that applicable margin rates of 50% or 100% (depending on the stage) are levied on open positions and new positions created from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions / Margin Applicability: August 17, 2026 (on open positions as on August 14, 2026 and new positions created thereafter)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for specified securities may impact short-term liquidity and trading volumes for the affected stocks.
