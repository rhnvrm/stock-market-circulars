## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the methodology and symbol list for the Reversal Trade Cancellation Mechanism (RTCM) applicable for the month of September 2026.

## Key Points

- RTCM is applicable for series EQ, BE, BZ, SM, ST & SZ pertaining to symbols provided in file `CM_RTCM_sep2026.csv`.
- Exclusions from RTCM applicability include approved securities published by NSE Clearing Ltd, constituents of Nifty 500 index, and ETFs.
- The symbol list file for September 2026 has been made available on the extranet path `/common/Surveillance/RTCM`.

## Regulatory Changes

Reiteration of the methodology for shortlisting symbols subject to the Reversal Trade Cancellation Mechanism (RTCM) to prevent potential reversal trades between PANs.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the applicable symbol list and adhere to relevant surveillance circulars and obligations under circular NSE/INVG/46662.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Period: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members dealing in the specified symbols need to incorporate the RTCM guidelines into their surveillance and trading compliance systems.
