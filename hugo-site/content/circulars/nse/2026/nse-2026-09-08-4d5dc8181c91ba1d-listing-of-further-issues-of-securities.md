## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of securities for several companies with effect from September 09, 2026. The listings include equity shares allotted under ESOPs and preferential warrant conversions.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 09, 2026.
- Companies involved include Aurum PropTech Limited, Emkay Global Financial Services Limited, ESAF Small Finance Bank Limited, HDFC Life Insurance Company Limited, and others.
- Securities admitted include equity shares issued via ESOP and preferential warrant conversions.
- Trading will be conducted under designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notification pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the specified security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 08, 2026
- Effective Date: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing update with negligible direct market impact.
