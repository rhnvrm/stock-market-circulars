## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a revision in the frequency of real-time index dissemination for the Nifty 50 index in the Capital Market (CM) and Futures & Options (FO) segments. Effective September 15, 2026, the dissemination frequency will be increased from 250 milliseconds (0.25 seconds) to 100 milliseconds (0.10 seconds).

## Key Points

- The current dissemination frequency of 250 milliseconds for the Nifty 50 index is being revised.
- The new proposed frequency is 100 milliseconds (0.10 second).
- The change applies to the Nifty 50 index disseminated on the trading system in CM and FO segments.
- A mock session is planned for September 12, 2026, to test the updated dissemination frequency.

## Regulatory Changes

- Real-time index dissemination frequency for Nifty 50 updated from 0.25 seconds to 0.10 seconds.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to take note of the changes and ensure their systems can handle the higher frequency real-time index dissemination.
- Participate in the mock session on September 12, 2026, to verify readiness.

## Important Dates

- Mock Session Date: September 12, 2026
- Effective Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and market participants will receive faster real-time updates for the Nifty 50 index, requiring necessary adjustments in data processing systems and trading algorithms.
