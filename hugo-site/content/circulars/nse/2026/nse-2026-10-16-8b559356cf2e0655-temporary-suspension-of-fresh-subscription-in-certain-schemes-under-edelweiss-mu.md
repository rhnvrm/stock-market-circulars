## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions in certain schemes under Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh subscriptions, including Lumpsum (with Switches) and fresh registration of Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), are disabled for Edelweiss Gold ETF Fund of Fund and Edelweiss Gold and Silver ETF Fund of Fund.
- Existing installments of SIP and STP registered prior to the effective date will continue to be processed normally.
- Top-ups in existing SIP/STP (as Target Scheme for STP) will not be processed.
- No restrictions are placed on Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), Switch-out, and STP-out.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of temporary restriction on fresh subscriptions and new SIP/STP registrations for designated Edelweiss Mutual Fund schemes as per AMC notice cum addendum.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must take note of the restrictions on fresh subscriptions and update their investment workflows accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: Opening of business hours of October 16, 2026.
- Notice Date: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors looking to invest fresh capital via lumpsum or new SIP/STP into Edelweiss Gold ETF Fund of Fund and Edelweiss Gold and Silver ETF Fund of Fund will be restricted, while existing systematic plans and redemption requests remain unaffected.
