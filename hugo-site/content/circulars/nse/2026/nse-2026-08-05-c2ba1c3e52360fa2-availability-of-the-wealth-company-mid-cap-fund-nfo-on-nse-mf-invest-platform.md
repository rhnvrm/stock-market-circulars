## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular announcing the reopening of The Wealth Company Mid Cap Fund of The Wealth Company Mutual Fund for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective August 06, 2026. The platform will support ongoing transactions as well as Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) facilities across six scheme options (both direct and regular, with growth and IDCW options).

## Key Points

- **Scheme Reopening**: The Wealth Company Mid Cap Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Supported Facilities**: In addition to standard subscriptions and redemptions, the schemes will support Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) facilities.
- **Effective Date**: The fund and its systematic facilities will be available starting August 06, 2026.
- **Schemes & ISINs Listed**:
  - The Wealth Company Mid Cap Fund Direct IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF2F0001669)
  - The Wealth Company Mid Cap Fund Direct IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2F0001677)
  - The Wealth Company Mid Cap Fund Direct Growth (ISIN: INF2F0001651)
  - The Wealth Company Mid Cap Fund Regular IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF2F0001636)
  - The Wealth Company Mid Cap Fund Regular IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2F0001644)
  - The Wealth Company Mid Cap Fund Regular Growth (ISIN: INF2F0001628)

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced by this circular. It is an operational notification detailing the availability of specific mutual fund schemes on an exchange-operated mutual fund platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members and Distributors**: Update back-office systems, transaction portals, and product master lists with the new schemes and their respective ISINs to facilitate seamless subscription, redemption, SIP, STP, and SWP orders starting August 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 05, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Low. This is a routine operational listing of a newly launched mutual fund scheme on the exchange's mutual fund transaction platform, enhancing the product suite available to members and retail investors.
- **Trading Impact**: Low. Trading members using the NSE MF Invest Platform can execute orders in these schemes from the effective date.
