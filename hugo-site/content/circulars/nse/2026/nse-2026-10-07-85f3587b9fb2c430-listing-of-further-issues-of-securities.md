## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from October 08, 2026, following allotments under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- Listing of further equity shares for companies including Aarti Industries, Aditya Birla Capital, ICICI Bank, ICICI Lombard, Ideaforge Technology, Kansai Nerolac Paints, L&T Finance, MIC Electronics, Skipper, and Sona BLW Precision Forgings.
- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective October 08, 2026.
- Allotments primarily executed under ESOP (Employee Stock Option Plans).
- Trading shall be identified by designated security codes and respective lot sizes specified in the annexure (market lot of 1).

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine admission of further issues under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must ensure adherence to trading regulations, ISIN parameters, and designated security codes.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Allotment dates vary by company (ranging from late September to early October 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Marginal increase in paid-up equity capital and floating shares for the respective companies due to ESOP exercises. No significant broader market impact.
