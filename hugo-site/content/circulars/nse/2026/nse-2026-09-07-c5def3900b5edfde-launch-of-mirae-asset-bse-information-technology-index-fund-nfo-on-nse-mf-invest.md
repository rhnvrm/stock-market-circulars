## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the launch of the Mirae Asset BSE Information Technology Index Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window runs from September 08, 2026, to September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Mirae Asset Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Mirae Asset BSE Information Technology Index Fund (Direct and Regular plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options)
- Category: Equity Index Fund
- NFO Open Date: September 08, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 22, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- Allotment Date: September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 24, 2026, till 3:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening: September 08, 2026
- NFO Closing: September 22, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 24, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to transact in the new information technology sector index fund through the NSE MF Invest Platform during the NFO period.
