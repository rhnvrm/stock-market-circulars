## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular announcing the availability of Nippon India Income Plus Arbitrage Omni Fund of Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes of Nippon India Income Plus Arbitrage Omni Fund of Fund are reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions.
- Facilities such as SIP, STP, and SWP are enabled on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Includes multiple plans: Direct Growth Plan, Growth Plan, Direct IDCW Payout, Direct IDCW Reinvestment, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can transact in the mentioned mutual fund schemes starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 04, 2026
- **Circular Date:** September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform access for investors to transact in the specified debt-category fund schemes of Nippon India Mutual Fund.
