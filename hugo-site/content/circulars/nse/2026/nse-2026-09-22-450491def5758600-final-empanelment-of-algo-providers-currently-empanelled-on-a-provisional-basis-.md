## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the final empanelment of five entities that were previously empanelled on a provisional basis for providing algorithmic trading solutions to trading members, upon successful fulfillment of all required conditions.

## Key Points

- Five entities granted final empanelment as Algo Providers.
- Includes vendors providing whitebox and/or blackbox algorithmic trading solutions.
- Entities affected include AlgoBulls Technologies Private Limited, Quantiply Technologies Private Limited, Simply Algo Fintech Private Limited, Stratzy Fintech Private Limited, and Modern Algos Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of provisional empanelment status to final empanelment for specified algorithmic trading providers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members may continue or initiate utilization of empanelled vendors in compliance with exchange guidelines for algorithmic trading.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal direct market impact; formalizes the status of approved algorithmic service vendors for trading members.
