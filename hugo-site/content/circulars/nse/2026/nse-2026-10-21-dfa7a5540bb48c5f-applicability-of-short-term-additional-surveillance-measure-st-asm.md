## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular announcing the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Specific securities have been shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I, while others are moving from Stage I to Stage II, attracting enhanced margin requirements effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusion:** Securities including Amal Limited, Pashupati Cotspin Limited, and Prismx Global Ventures Limited have been included under ST-ASM Stage I.
- **Movement to Stage II:** Utssav CZ Gold Jewels Limited has been shortlisted to move from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II.
- **Margin Requirements:** 
  - For Stage I securities: Applicable rate of margin is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. October 23, 2026, on open positions as on October 22, 2026, and new positions created from October 23, 2026.
  - For Stage II securities / movements: Applicable rate of margin is 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. October 23, 2026, on open positions as on October 22, 2026, and new positions created from October 23, 2026.
- **Clarification:** Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of enhanced surveillance and margin frameworks under the ST-ASM guidelines in conjunction with existing measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must levy the applicable increased margin rates (50% or 100%) on all open positions as of October 22, 2026, and on all new positions created from October 23, 2026, for the respective shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Surveillance Actions:** October 23, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhanced margin requirements may moderate trading liquidity and short-term volatility in the affected securities.
