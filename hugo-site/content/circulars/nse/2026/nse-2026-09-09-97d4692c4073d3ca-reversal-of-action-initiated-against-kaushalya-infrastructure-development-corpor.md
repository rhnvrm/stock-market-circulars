## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the reversal of action initiated against Kaushalya Infrastructure Development Corporation Limited (Symbol: KAUSHALYA) due to the payment of Annual Listing Fees (ALF) for the Financial Year ended 2025-26.

## Key Points

- The company paid the Annual Listing Fees prior to the effective date of the previous restrictive circular (NSE/CML/75688).
- No further action will be taken against the company.
- Trading in the security will continue in the 'BE' series.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reversal of previously notified restricted trading action.

## Compliance Requirements

- Company has complied with the Annual Listing Fee requirements for FY 2025-26.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 09, 2026
- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normal trading operations continue for Kaushalya Infrastructure Development Corporation Limited without disruption.
