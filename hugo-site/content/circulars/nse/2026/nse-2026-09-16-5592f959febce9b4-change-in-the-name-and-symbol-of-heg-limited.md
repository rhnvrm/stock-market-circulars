## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that the name and symbol of HEG Limited will be changed effective September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: HEG Limited
- New Name: HEG Advanced Materials Limited
- Existing Symbol: HEG
- New Symbol: HEGAM
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the change and update their systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact; trading members and market participants must update client and trading systems with the new symbol (HEGAM).
