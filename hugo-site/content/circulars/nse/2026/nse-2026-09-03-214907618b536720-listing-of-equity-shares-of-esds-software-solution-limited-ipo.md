## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the admission of equity shares of ESDS Software Solution Limited to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective from September 04, 2026, following its Initial Public Offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Symbol: ESDS
- ISIN: INE0DRI01029
- Face Value and Paid-up Value: Re. 1/- per share
- Issue Price: Rs. 429/- per share
- Total No. of Securities: 117,210,969
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: September 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Admission is carried out under standard trading regulations and SEBI circular guidelines for IPO pre-open sessions.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category of scrips as per relevant SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 04, 2026
- Lock-in details vary from October 2026 to September 2029 depending on share categories.

## Impact Assessment

Commencement of secondary market trading for ESDS Software Solution Limited equity shares on NSE's capital market segment.
