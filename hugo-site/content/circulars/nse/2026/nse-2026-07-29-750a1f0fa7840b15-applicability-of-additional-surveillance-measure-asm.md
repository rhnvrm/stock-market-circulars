## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has notified the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) framework for specific securities. Based on the surveillance criteria, Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DIACABS) has satisfied the criteria for inclusion in the Long-Term ASM Stage I framework, moving from the Short-Term ASM (STASM) framework.

## Key Points

- **Security Inclusion**: Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (Symbol: DIACABS, ISIN: INE989C01038) is shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Stage-I.
- **Margin Requirement**: An applicable margin rate of 100% will be applied starting August 03, 2026 on all open positions as of July 31, 2026, and on any new positions created from August 03, 2026 onwards.
- **Trade-for-Trade Segment Shift**: Securities qualifying under Criteria VII (shifted to Stage IV) will be moved from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) on a T+3 basis, effective August 03, 2026. (No securities are currently listed under this category in this circular).
- **Nature of Surveillance**: NSE reiterates that shortlisting under ASM is purely a surveillance measure and should not be considered an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shifting of DIACABS from the STASM framework to the LTASM Stage I framework.
- Implementation of surveillance actions in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures as outlined in previous Exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Implement a 100% margin rate on all open positions of DIACABS as of July 31, 2026, and on new positions from August 03, 2026.
- **Risk Management**: Update internal risk parameters and surveillance systems to reflect the changes for the shortlisted security.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 29, 2026
- **LTASM Stage-I Shortlisting Effective Date**: July 30, 2026
- **100% Margin Effective Date**: August 03, 2026 (based on open positions as of July 31, 2026)
- **Trade-for-Trade Shifting (if applicable under Criteria VII)**: August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market and Trading Impact**: DIACABS will see reduced trading leverage due to the 100% margin requirement, which may lower trading volumes and speculative activity in the scrip.
- **Operational Impact**: Members must ensure system updates are in place to collect 100% margins on the specified dates to avoid margin shortfalls or penalties.
