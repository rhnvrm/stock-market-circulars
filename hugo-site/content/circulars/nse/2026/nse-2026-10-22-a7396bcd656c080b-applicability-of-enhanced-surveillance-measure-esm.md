## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for certain securities. Securities qualifying under ESM Stage I shall attract a minimum 100% margin and be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026 (with margins applicable on open positions as on October 23, 2026, and new positions created from October 26, 2026).
- **Segment Shift:** Securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).
- **Margin Requirements:** Minimum 100% margin on all applicable positions.
- **Shortlisted Securities:** Includes Ashapuri Gold Ornament Limited (AGOL), Global Surfaces Limited (GSLSU), Onward Technologies Limited (ONWARDTEC), Prismx Global Ventures Limited (PRISMX), and Swarnsarita Jewels India Limited (SWARNSAR).

## Regulatory Changes

Securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion under ESM are placed under Stage I, requiring a 100% margin and transfer to the Trade-for-Trade segment. The framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open positions as on October 23, 2026, and new positions from October 26, 2026, alongside managing settlements under the Trade-for-Trade segment for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Effective Date (Margin & Trade-for-Trade):** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the shortlisted securities will be restricted to the Trade-for-Trade segment with 100% margins, impacting liquidity and settlement mechanics for market participants holding positions in these scrips.
