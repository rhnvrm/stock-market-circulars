## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular updating the list of securities under the Additional Surveillance Measure for Companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (ASM-IBC). Effective August 07, 2026, Shrenik Limited has been shortlisted under this framework. This surveillance measure operates in conjunction with other prevailing exchange surveillance actions to protect market integrity.

## Key Points

- **New Shortlisting**: Shrenik Limited (Symbol: SHRENIK, ISIN: INE632X01030) has been shortlisted under the 'Receipt of corporate announcement / disclosure from the company' category.
- **Exclusions**: No securities have been excluded or moved out of the ASM-IBC framework in this update.
- **Consolidated List**: Securities currently active in the ASM-IBC framework include Neueon Corporation Limited (Stage I), Aban Offshore Limited, and AGS Transact Technologies Limited.
- **Surveillance Context**: Shortlisting under ASM-IBC is purely a market surveillance measure and does not indicate adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no structural regulatory changes. This is a routine operational list update within the existing ASM-IBC framework governed by previous guidelines (including circulars NSE/SURV/37979, NSE/SURV/52368, NSE/SURV/55582, and NSE/SURV/70074).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Member Awareness**: Exchange members must apply corresponding margin or trading limitations to transactions involving Shrenik Limited and other listed companies.
- **Client Advice**: Members should advise clients on the surveillance status and associated risk profile when trading the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Affected Securities**: Margins and trading bands for Shrenik Limited may be adjusted to meet surveillance criteria, potentially reducing intraday liquidity and trading volume for the stock.
- **Market/Operational Impact**: Minimal broader market impact, as this is a company-specific surveillance update, but essential for compliance teams and active traders in these specific counters.
