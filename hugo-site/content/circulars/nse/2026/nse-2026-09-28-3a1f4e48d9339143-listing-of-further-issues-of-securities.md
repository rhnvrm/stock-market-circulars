## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of securities for several companies, including Ice Make Refrigeration Limited, Pennar Industries Limited, Viji Finance Limited, Virinchi Limited, Aditya Infotech Limited, Wheels India Limited, Capital Infra Trust, and IRB InvIT Fund, effective from September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment effective September 29, 2026.
- Includes preferential issue equity shares for Ice Make Refrigeration Limited, Pennar Industries Limited, and Viji Finance Limited with specified lock-in details and distinctive number ranges.
- Trading will be identified by designated security codes and specified market lots.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are executed using designated security codes and adhere to applicable lot sizes and lock-in restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2026
- Allotment Dates: Ice Make (10-Sep-2026), Pennar Industries (14-Jul-2026), Viji Finance (12-Aug-2026).

## Impact Assessment

Increases the listed equity and unit capital base for the respective issuers on the exchange.
