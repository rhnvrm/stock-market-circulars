## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 10, 2026. The session features no new version release and provides members an opportunity to test systems, disaster recovery switchover, and Order Snapshot Recovery via TCP/IP.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 10, 2026, in the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- No new version release; NEAT version remains 1.5.5.
- Testing of Order Snapshot Recovery via TCP/IP for MTBT broadcast in the Commodity Segment, scheduled to be effective in LIVE from October 12, 2026.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session; members using NNF software must manually clear orders.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Conducted to meet standard regulatory testing and operational readiness requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment between 18:30 and 19:00 hours on the mock day to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 12, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed. UCC validation is skipped during contingency time for order entry.
- Connect2NSE and Extranet facility unavailable from 12:30 hours to 21:00 hours; Extranet API unavailable from 12:30 to 14:00 hours.

## Important Dates

- Mock Date: October 10, 2026
- Live Effective Date for Order Snapshot Recovery via TCP/IP: October 12, 2026
- Live Re-login start time: 18:30 hours
- Live Re-login close time: 19:00 hours

## Impact Assessment

- Low operational impact, ensuring member systems, trading applications, and connectivity parameters are fully verified and operational for live trading.
