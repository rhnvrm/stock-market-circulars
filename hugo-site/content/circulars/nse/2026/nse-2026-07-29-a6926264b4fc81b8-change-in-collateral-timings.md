## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has announced a revised schedule for collateral timings, specifically the cut-off times for placing release requests for Cash, FDR, and Securities in the Futures and Options segment. This circular partially modifies the earlier circular ref. no. 090/2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/CMPT/75321 dated July 21, 2026).

## Key Points

- **Intraday Cash and FDR Release:** The cut-off time is moved from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
- **End of Day Cash and FDR Release:** The cut-off time remains unchanged at 8:00 PM.
- **Securities EOD Release:** The cut-off time is moved from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
- All revised timings will become effective from July 30, 2026.
- Applies to all Members, Custodians, and Professional Clearing Members (PCM) active in the Futures and Options Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial modification to circular ref. no. 090/2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/CMPT/75321) regarding timings for collateral addition and release.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members, Custodians, and PCMs must ensure that all intraday Cash/FDR release requests and Securities EOD release requests are submitted before the new cut-off time of 8:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Effective Date:** July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Moderate. Operations and treasury teams of clearing members and custodians must submit their collateral release requests one hour earlier than previously required.
- **Market Impact:** Low. This is an operational timing adjustment with no impact on risk margins or trading rules.
