## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding SEBI Ex-Parte Interim Order No. WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-SEC-6/32673/2026-27 dated August 19, 2026, concerning manipulative trades during the Call Auction Session (CAS) on SENSEX expiry at BSE on August 13, 2026. Noticees have complied with directions by depositing impounding amounts and creating a lien in favor of SEBI.

## Key Points

- SEBI issued an interim order regarding manipulative trades during CAS on SENSEX expiry at BSE on August 13, 2026.
- Noticees involved: Copthall Mauritius Investment Limited (PAN: AAACC4303M) and Mansi Share and Stock Broking Private Limited (PAN: AADCM6645D).
- Noticees have complied with the interim order by depositing the requisite impounding amount and creating a lien in favor of SEBI.
- Certain restrictions cease to apply upon crediting of the impounding amount in terms of the interim order.

## Regulatory Changes

SEBI directions restrict specific noticees from accessing securities markets and participating in the Call Auction Session (CAS) in the equity segment until further orders, with exemptions applying upon compliance and deposition of impounding amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the debarred entities list available on the NSE website and ensure compliance with SEBI directions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 19, 2026
- Circular Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and market integrity by restricting entities involved in manipulative trading practices during Call Auction Sessions.
