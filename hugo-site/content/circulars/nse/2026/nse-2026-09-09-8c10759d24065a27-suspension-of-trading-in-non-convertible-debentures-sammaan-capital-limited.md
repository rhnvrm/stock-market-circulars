## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in five Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) issued by Sammaan Capital Limited, effective from September 10, 2026, due to maturity.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sammaan Capital Limited
- Reason for Suspension: Maturity of securities
- Effective Date: September 10, 2026
- Affected ISINs: INE148I07TZ6, INE148I07UI0, INE148I07US9, INE148I07UH2, INE148I07UG4

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to note the suspension of trading in the specified NCDs of Sammaan Capital Limited.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the matured NCDs of Sammaan Capital Limited will be halted on the exchange platform.
