## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued guidelines pursuant to amendments in SEBI KRA Regulations, 2011. PANs uploaded to KRAs till August 31, 2026, whose primary KYC status is reported as "On-Hold", are restricted from trading and squaring off open positions effective September 26, 2026, until validation requirements are met.

## Key Points

- All regulated entities must block debit transactions and suspend trading account transactions for demised investors as per SEBI circular dated October 03, 2023.
- Trading members must ensure all client KYCs are uploaded to KRAs and permit trading only for clients with "KYC Registered" or "KYC Validated" status.
- PANs with Primary KYC status reported as "On-Hold" as of August 31, 2026, will be barred from trading and squaring off open positions w.e.f. September 26, 2026.
- Non-compliant PANs will be flagged by the Exchange on September 25, 2026.
- Clients becoming KRA compliant subsequently will be permitted to trade on T+1 based on KRA information received by the Exchange on T Day.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of stricter KYC validation norms and automatic trading restrictions for non-compliant PANs through KRA integration.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must review the list of non-validated clients available on the member portal (ENIT > Log > Downloads > <TM Code>_Non-Validated_Clients_by_KRA).
- Ensure clients complete KRA validation requirements to avoid trading restrictions and position squaring off.

## Important Dates

- **August 31, 2026:** Cut-off date for PANs uploaded to KRAs.
- **September 25, 2026:** Exchange flags non-compliant PANs.
- **September 26, 2026:** Trading and square-off restrictions take effect for non-compliant clients.

## Impact Assessment

High operational impact for trading members and clients whose KYC status is On-Hold, resulting in immediate suspension of trading rights and inability to square off open positions until validation is completed.
