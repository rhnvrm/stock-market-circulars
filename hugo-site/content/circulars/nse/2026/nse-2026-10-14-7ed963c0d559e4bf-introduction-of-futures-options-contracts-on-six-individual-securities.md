## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the introduction of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on six individual securities, effective October 28, 2026. This addition is based on the stock selection criteria prescribed by SEBI and subsequent approvals.

## Key Points

- F&O contracts will be available for trading w.e.f. October 28, 2026, subject to fulfillment of eligibility criteria of the Quarter sigma computation cycle of October 2026.
- Securities included:
  1. Coromandel International Limited (COROMANDEL)
  2. City Union Bank Limited (CUB)
  3. Data Patterns (India) Limited (DATAPATTNS)
  4. Karur Vysya Bank Limited (KARURVYSYA)
  5. Navin Fluorine International Limited (NAVINFLUOR)
  6. Syrma SGS Technology Limited (SYRMA)
- Market lot, scheme of strikes, and quantity freeze details will be published on October 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of new securities into the derivatives segment pursuant to SEBI master circular guidelines and stock selection criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated contract.gz and contract master CSV.gz files in their trading application before trading on the effective date.
- Files can be obtained from the faoftp/faocommon directory on the Extranet server or NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 14, 2026
- Market Lot & Scheme of Strikes Announcement: October 27, 2026
- Effective Trading Date for F&O Contracts: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the derivatives trading universe, enabling participants to trade futures and options on these six securities for hedging and speculative purposes.
- Increases liquidity and structured participation in underlying equities.
