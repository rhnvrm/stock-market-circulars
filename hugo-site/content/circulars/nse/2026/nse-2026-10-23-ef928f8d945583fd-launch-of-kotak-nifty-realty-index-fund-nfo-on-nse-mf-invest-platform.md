## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of the Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window runs from October 26, 2026 to November 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund Name: Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund (Regular and Direct plans, Growth and IDCW options).
- NFO Window: October 26, 2026 to November 09, 2026 till 11:59 pm.
- Allotment Date: November 13, 2026.
- Multiple scheme variants (Regular/Direct, Growth/IDCW Reinvestment/IDCW Payout) with assigned ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard product offering circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 12, 2026 till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: October 26, 2026
- NFO Close Date: November 09, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: November 12, 2026 (12:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates trading and investment in the Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund for market participants via the NSE MF platform.
