## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of units of Knowledge Realty Trust, outlining dates for non-retail and retail categories, order dates, custodial confirmation times, and settlement dates.

## Key Points

- Settlement calendar covers Offer for Sale of units of Knowledge Realty Trust.
- Settlement rules follow secondary market transaction guidelines as referenced in circular NCL/CMPT/73996.
- Activities include funds pay-in by T+1 day 11:00 am and securities & funds pay-out by T+1 day 01:30 pm.
- Applicable securities transaction tax (STT) and stamp duty will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Regulatory Changes

No structural regulatory changes; outlines specific execution timelines for the subject OFS.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must adhere to the specified settlement dates, order entry timelines, and custodial confirmation deadlines.

## Important Dates

- Order Date / Custodial Confirmation for Settlement No. 2026164 (Non-retail): August 31, 2026
- Settlement Date for 2026164: September 1, 2026
- Order Date for Settlement No. 2026165 (Retail & Non-retail): September 1, 2026
- Settlement Date for 2026165: September 2, 2026
- Funds Pay-in: T+1 day by 11:00 am
- Securities & Funds Pay-out: T+1 day by 01:30 pm

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement execution and clear operational guidelines for market participants engaging in the OFS of Knowledge Realty Trust units.
