## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from October 23, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Covers further issues of equity shares for companies including Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Capital Small Finance Bank Limited, Digicontent Limited, DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Limited, Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited, Kellton Tech Solutions Limited, Optiemus Infracom Limited, Sai Life Sciences Limited, Shivalik Rasayan Limited, Shriram Finance Limited, The Federal Bank Limited, Gandhar Oil Refinery (India) Limited, and Karur Vysya Bank Limited.
- Allotments include shares issued under ESOP and preferential allotments.
- Effective date for trading in these further issues is October 23, 2026.
- Market lots and security details (ISIN, face value, paid-up value, distinctive number ranges) are specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory policy changes; routine admission of further securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure trading in these securities is conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action enabling trading of newly allotted shares without broader market impact.
