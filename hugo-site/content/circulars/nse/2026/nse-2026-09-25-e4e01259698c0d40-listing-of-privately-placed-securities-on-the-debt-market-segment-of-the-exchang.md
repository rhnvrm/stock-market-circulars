## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 25, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Issuers Involved**: Godrej Agrovet Limited, Tata Capital Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Jubilant Ingrevia Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, Varroc Engineering Limited, Motilal Oswal Finvest, and Trust Investment Advisors Private Limited.
- **Security Type**: Commercial Papers (CP).
- **Effective Date**: September 25, 2026.
- **Allotment Date**: September 24, 2026 and September 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; notification is issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations for the admission of privately placed debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers must comply with all continuing disclosure and regulatory requirements stipulated by SEBI and the Exchange for debt securities listed on the debt market segment.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment**: September 24, 2026 / September 25, 2026
- **Listing / Effective Date**: September 25, 2026
- **Redemption Dates**: Ranging from November 25, 2026 to June 21, 2027 depending on the specific tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Provides formal exchange listing and liquidity on the debt market segment for the newly issued commercial papers of the respective companies.
