## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) and SEBI have announced an extension of the timeline for implementing provisions related to base price, price bands, call auction in pre-open sessions, and close-out procedures for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs). Originally set to take effect on September 1, 2026, the implementation date has been postponed to September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Refers to SEBI Circular No. HO/47/11/11(1)2026-MRD-POD3/I/19839/2026 dated August 28, 2026.
- Timeline for implementation extended based on feedback received from Stock Exchanges.
- All other provisions of the original SEBI Circular dated June 15, 2026, remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- The effective implementation date for norms on base price, price bands, call auction in pre-open sessions, and close-out procedures for ETFs is deferred from September 1, 2026, to September 7, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock Exchanges, Clearing Corporations, and Asset Management Companies must update their systems and operational readiness in line with the revised deadline.
- Relevant byelaws, rules, and regulations should be amended if required.
- Market participants and investors must be duly notified.

## Important Dates

- **August 28, 2026:** Circular issued.
- **September 7, 2026:** Revised effective date for implementation of the ETF norms.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market infrastructure institutions and participants additional time to prepare and test system changes for ETF trading norms, minimizing potential technical disruptions.
