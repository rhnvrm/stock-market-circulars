## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies following allotments made under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP). The listings will be effective from September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Companies involved include Anthem Biosciences Limited, Cipla Limited, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited, Max Estates Limited, One 97 Communications Limited, The Phoenix Mills Limited, and Polycab India Limited.
- Shares are allotted under ESOP and rank pari passu with existing equity shares.
- Effective date for trading: September 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading shall be conducted in the specified lot sizes using the designated security codes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate action increasing the paid-up equity capital of the respective issuers without immediate operational disruption.
