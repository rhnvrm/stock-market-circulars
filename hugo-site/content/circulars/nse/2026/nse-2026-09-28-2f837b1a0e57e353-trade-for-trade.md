## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the shifting of selected securities to the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/ST) with a price band of 5% or lower, pursuant to surveillance reviews and market safety measures.

## Key Points

- Securities shifted from Rolling segment (series: EQ) to Trade for Trade segment (series: BE): DSFCL, LCCINFOTEC, MONEYBOXX, RAJTV, CRESTO, EKI, RKEC, APOORVA.
- Securities shifted from Rolling segment (series: SM) to Trade for Trade segment (series: ST): PRESSTONIC, NAMAN.
- Price band restricted to 5% or lower for the affected securities.
- Settlement will be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Transfer is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in specified securities moved to Trade for Trade segment under Capital Market Segment Trading Regulations Part - A, 2.6.
- Securities will no longer be available in the rolling segment (series: EQ/SM).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must exercise adequate precaution while trading in these securities, as settlements require trade-to-trade clearing without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2026 (Thursday).

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading flexibility for the affected securities are restricted due to the 5% price band and trade-to-trade settlement requirements.
