## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of various plans under the Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF of Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes are available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Includes multiple regular and direct plans with growth and IDCW options.
- Effective date: August 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members can facilitate client investments in the listed schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors access to the Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF via the NSE platform.
