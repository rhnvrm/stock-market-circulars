## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Eventions Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform effective from October 08, 2026. The security will trade under the symbol EVENTIONS with a market lot of 1,200 equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Eventions Limited
- Symbol: EVENTIONS
- ISIN: INE20M201019
- Issue Price: Rs. 118/- per share
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- Total No. of Securities: 12,183,909 equity shares
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Series: ST (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment - Settlement Type 'W')
- Market Maker: Aftertrade Broking Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the equity shares of Eventions Limited is admitted on the EMERGE SME Platform in Series 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment – Settlement Type 'W') and will subsequently be shifted to Series 'SM' (Normal Rolling Segment – Settlement Type 'N') as per applicable SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with pre-open session guidelines for IPOs and continuous market trading norms as specified by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members can trade in the equity shares of Eventions Limited on the SME platform starting October 08, 2026, subject to the specified market lot and surveillance segment rules.
