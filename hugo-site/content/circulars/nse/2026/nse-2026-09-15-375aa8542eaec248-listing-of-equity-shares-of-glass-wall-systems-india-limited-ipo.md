## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Glass Wall Systems (India) Limited following its initial public offering (IPO), including shareholding pattern disclosure details under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Glass Wall Systems (India) Limited
- Scrip/Class of Security: Equity Shares
- Shareholding pattern filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) as of 12.09.2026 (Post Issue)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, specifically Regulation 31 regarding shareholding pattern disclosures.

## Important Dates

- As on Date: 12.09.2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Glass Wall Systems (India) Limited equity shares on the exchange platform and provides transparency into post-issue shareholding distribution.
