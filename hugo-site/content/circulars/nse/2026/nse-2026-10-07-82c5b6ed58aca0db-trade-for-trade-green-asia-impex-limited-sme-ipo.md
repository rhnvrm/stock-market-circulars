## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the transfer of Green Asia Impex Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (series: SM), effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Green Asia Impex Limited
- Symbol: GREENASIA
- Action: Transferred from trade for trade segment (Series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026
- Reference: Pursuant to exchange circular no. NSE/CML/76752 dated October 06, 2026, and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of SME IPO shares from the trade-for-trade segment to the normal rolling settlement segment as per standard regulatory guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should note the change in series from ST to SM for GREENASIA and ensure their trading systems and back-offices are updated accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Transitioning to the rolling segment improves liquidity and permits normal rolling settlement for shares of Green Asia Impex Limited.
