## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular (Ref. No: 1269/2026, Download Ref No: NSE/CML/75526) dated July 31, 2026, announcing the listing of further equity shares of P. E. Analytics Limited (Symbol: PROPEQUITY) on the SME EMERGE segment. A total of 3,97,800 equity shares of face value Rs. 10 each, issued at Rs. 201 per share under a preferential allotment, will be admitted to dealings effective August 03, 2026. These shares will rank pari passu with existing shares and are subject to lock-in requirements until February 09, 2027.

## Key Points

- **Admitted Securities**: 3,97,800 equity shares of P. E. Analytics Limited issued under preferential allotment.
- **Trading Platform**: SME EMERGE segment of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) with Series SM.
- **Security Codes**: Symbol is PROPEQUITY, and the ISIN is INE0KN801013.
- **Pricing & Valuation**: Face value of Rs. 10 per share, fully paid-up, and issued at a price of Rs. 201.
- **Distinctive Numbers**: 10482105 to 10879904.
- **Trading Parameters**: Market lot of 600 shares.
- **Temporary ISIN**: Allotted dematerialized shares have been initially credited under a temporary ISIN (IN8*********) in accordance with SEBI circulars on additional issue of shares before being merged into the permanent ISIN.
- **Lock-in Period**: The listed shares are subject to a lock-in period ending on February 09, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Regulation References**: Admitted in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 (for listing and dealing) and Regulation 2.5.5 (for determining respective lot sizes) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- **Additional Securities ISIN Activation**: Credited under a temporary ISIN sequence (IN8*********) pursuant to SEBI Circulars CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 dated August 02, 2012, and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012 dated September 11, 2012, designed to manage the activation of ISINs for additional issuances.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Exchange Members**: Trading members must identify the security by its designated symbol (PROPEQUITY) and Series (SM).
- **Trading Lot Sizes**: Members are required to execute trades only in the specified market lot of 600 shares on the SME EMERGE trading system.
- **Lock-in Compliance**: The 3,97,800 newly listed shares must remain locked-in till February 09, 2027, as per the allotment details.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment**: July 03, 2026
- **Circular Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date**: August 03, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date**: February 09, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. The listing of 3,97,800 further equity shares increases the total share capital of P. E. Analytics Limited on the SME EMERGE segment. Given the specific allotment (preferential issue) and lock-in restrictions until February 2027, there will be no immediate liquid float pressure from these newly listed shares in the market.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading members and back-office systems must ensure that the new shares are traded in market lots of 600 under the SM series. Demat accounts and depository systems will handle the temporary ISIN transition to permanent ISIN per SEBI guidelines.
