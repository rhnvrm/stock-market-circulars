## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the levy of an additional exposure margin @15% in the equity derivatives segment on securities where top 10 clients account for more than 20% of the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL). This framework is effective from October 30, 2026, and is based on 3 months rolling data reviewed monthly.

## Key Points

- Additional exposure margin of 15% levied on specific securities in the equity derivatives segment.
- Applies to securities where top 10 clients account for >20% of MWPL.
- If additional surveillance margin is applicable, the higher of the two margins will be levied.
- Effective date: October 30, 2026.
- Covers 21 shortlisted securities including Adani Energy Solutions, Ambuja Cements, Vodafone Idea, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of additional exposure margin rules based on client concentration under MWPL data.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must ensure adequate margin maintenance for positions in the shortlisted securities effective October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for trading in equity derivatives for the 21 listed securities, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for positions in these counters.
