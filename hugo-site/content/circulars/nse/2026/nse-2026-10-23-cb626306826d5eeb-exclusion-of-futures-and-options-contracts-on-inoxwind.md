## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on Inox Wind Limited (INOXWIND) in accordance with SEBI circular guidelines and exchange eligibility criteria.

## Key Points

- Contracts for new expiry months in INOXWIND will not be issued upon the expiry of existing contract months.
- Existing unexpired contracts for October 2026, November 2026, and December 2026 will continue to be available for trading until their respective expiries.
- New strikes will continue to be introduced in the existing contract months until final cessation.
- No F&O contracts shall be available for trading in INOXWIND with effect from December 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Exclusion of derivative contracts based on updated stock eligibility criteria prescribed under SEBI Circular Ref. No: SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024, and chapter 3.5 of Exchange circular No. NSE/FAOP/73928.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that no new expiry month contracts will be issued for INOXWIND, and positions must be managed accordingly through existing unexpired expiry cycles.

## Important Dates

- **October 23, 2026**: Circular issuance date.
- **December 30, 2026**: Effective date from which no contracts shall be available for trading in INOXWIND.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding positions in INOXWIND F&O contracts can trade existing unexpired contracts (October, November, December 2026) until their respective expiries, but must wind down or square off positions ahead of the complete exclusion effective December 30, 2026.
