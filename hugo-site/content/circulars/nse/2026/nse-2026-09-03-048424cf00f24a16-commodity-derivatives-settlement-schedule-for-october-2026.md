## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued the settlement schedule for the Commodity Derivatives segment for October 2026, outlining the corresponding settlement dates for each trade date during the month.

## Key Points

- Covers trade dates starting from October 01, 2026, to October 30, 2026.
- Specifies respective settlement dates for all trading days in the commodity derivatives segment.
- Settlement for the final trade date of October 30, 2026, is scheduled for November 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must take note of the trade and settlement dates to ensure timely fund and commodity pay-ins/pay-outs.

## Important Dates

- **October 01, 2026:** First trade date of the schedule.
- **October 30, 2026:** Last trade date of the schedule.
- **November 02, 2026:** Settlement date for trades executed on October 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for commodity derivatives participants during October 2026.
