## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in the equity shares of AvenuesAI Limited (Symbol: CCAVENUE) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A. The suspension is effective from October 13, 2026 (closing hours of trading on October 12, 2026) due to the consolidation of equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: AvenuesAI Limited
- Symbol: CCAVENUE
- Corporate Action: Consolidation of equity shares from Re. 1/- per share to Rs. 10/- per share.
- Effective Date: October 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Action taken pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the suspension of trading in CCAVENUE effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date / Effective Date: October 13, 2026 (closing hours of October 12, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the equity shares of AvenuesAI Limited is temporarily suspended on the exchange to facilitate the share consolidation process from Re. 1/- to Rs. 10/-.
