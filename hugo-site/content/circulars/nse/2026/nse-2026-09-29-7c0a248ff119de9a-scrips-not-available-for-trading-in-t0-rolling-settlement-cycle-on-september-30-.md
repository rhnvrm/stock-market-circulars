## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the list of securities that will not be available for trading in the T+0 rolling settlement cycle on September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 30, 2026.
- Reason for Exclusion: Securities moving in or out of indices on which derivatives contracts are available due to index rebalancing.
- Affected Scrips include Ambuja Cements, Ashok Leyland, Bank of Baroda, Biocon, Wipro, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of trading restrictions in the T+0 segment as outlined in chapter 1.6 of the Capital Market Consolidated Circular (NSE/CMTR/73927 dated April 28, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the specified scrips that are ineligible for T+0 settlement on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- September 29, 2026: Circular issued (Download Ref No: NSE/CMTR/76609, Circular Ref. No: 137/2026).
- September 30, 2026: Date of restriction for T+0 settlement.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational clarity for market participants regarding eligible securities in the T+0 rolling settlement cycle.
