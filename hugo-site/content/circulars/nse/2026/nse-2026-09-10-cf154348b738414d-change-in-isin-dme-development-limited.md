## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the change in ISIN (International Securities Identification Number) for the non-convertible securities of DME Development Limited, effective from September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: DME Development Limited
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Old ISIN INE0J7Q07215 changed to New ISIN INE0J7Q07272
- Old ISIN INE0J7Q07223 changed to New ISIN INE0J7Q07264

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative update reflecting a change in ISIN codes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and depositories are required to take note of the new ISIN codes for clearing and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to back-office and record-keeping updates by members and market participants handling the affected non-convertible securities.
