## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for securities, detailing shortlistings, stage movements, and exclusions effective from September 25, 2026, and September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage I and attract a minimum 100% margin effective September 28, 2026, on open positions as on September 25, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting from Stage I to Stage II (Radiowalla Network Limited, Tirupati Forge Limited) are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction effective September 25, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) effective September 28, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely for market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures including 100% margin requirements and migration to Trade-for-Trade segment for identified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and note the segment transfer from EQ/SM to BE/ST for impacted scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II movement & price band: September 25, 2026
- Open positions cut-off date for margin: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin & segment shift (EQ/SM to BE/ST): September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and trading mechanics for shortlisted securities due to 100% upfront margins and Trade-for-Trade settlement.
