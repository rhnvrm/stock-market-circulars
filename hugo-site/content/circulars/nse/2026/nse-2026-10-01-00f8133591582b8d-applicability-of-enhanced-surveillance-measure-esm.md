## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for selected securities effective October 05 and October 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I are shortlisted under ESM Stage I w.e.f. October 05, 2026, and attract a minimum 100% margin on open positions as on October 05, 2026, and new positions created from October 06, 2026.
- Shortlisted securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 06, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 05, 2026.
- Specified securities are eligible to move from ESM Stage II to Stage I w.e.f. October 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures to monitor volatility and ensure market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement terms for all affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for stage transitions and price band applicability: October 05, 2026
- Effective date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade shift: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for impacted scrips (BALPHARMA, DCMNVL, JHS, KATARIA, ABMINTLLTD, AMANTA, INNOMET, JALAN) will be affected due to strict margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
