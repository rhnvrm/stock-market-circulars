## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding SEBI Ex-Parte Interim Order No. WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-SEC-6/32673/2026-27 dated August 19, 2026, in the matter of manipulative trades during Call Auction Session (CAS) on SENSEX expiry at BSE on August 13, 2026. Directions have been issued against Copthall Mauritius Investment Limited and Mansi Share and Stock Broking Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Noticee No. 1 (Copthall Mauritius Investment Limited, PAN: AAACC4303M) and Noticee No. 2 (Mansi Share and Stock Broking Private Limited, PAN: AADCM6645D) are restrained from accessing securities markets.
- For Noticee No. 2, the restriction applies to its proprietary trading account only.
- Both entities are prohibited from participating in the Call Auction Session (CAS) in the equity segment (directly or indirectly, including placing, modifying, or cancelling orders) until further orders.
- Entities with open positions in exchange traded derivative contracts may close out/square off within 3 months from the date of the order or at expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Permitted to settle pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions before the close of trading hours on the date of the order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of SEBI ex-parte interim directions against entities involved in market manipulation during CAS.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the debarred entities and ensure compliance with SEBI directions.
- Consolidated list of debarred entities is available on the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 19, 2026
- Circular Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on the named entities regarding market access and CAS participation restrictions. Members must verify client status against debarred lists.
