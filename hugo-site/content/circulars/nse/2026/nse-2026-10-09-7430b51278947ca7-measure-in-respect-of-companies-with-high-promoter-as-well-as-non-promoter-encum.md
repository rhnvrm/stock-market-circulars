## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance in accordance with Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited has been added to the surveillance measure, attracting a minimum 75% margin.

## Key Points

- **Annexure I (New Inclusions):** Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited (`BRAHMINFRA`, ISIN: INE320I01017) is included under the surveillance measure effective October 14, 2026, on all open positions as on October 13, 2026, and new positions created from October 14, 2026.
- **Annexure II (Exclusions):** Nil securities are eligible to move out from the framework effective October 12, 2026.
- **Annexure III (Consolidated List):** Includes BPL Limited (`BPL`), Brahmaputra Infrastructure Limited (`BRAHMINFRA`), and Race Eco Chain Limited (`RACE`).
- **Margin Requirement:** Shortlisted securities attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of the surveillance framework established under Exchange circular no. NSE/SURV/51189 dated January 31, 2022, monitoring high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that a minimum 75% margin is maintained on open positions and new positions for securities shortlisted under Annexure I, effective from October 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Exclusions (Annexure II):** October 12, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margin Requirements (Annexure I):** October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants dealing in shortlisted securities must comply with enhanced margin requirements, which may impact liquidity and position management in the affected scrips.
