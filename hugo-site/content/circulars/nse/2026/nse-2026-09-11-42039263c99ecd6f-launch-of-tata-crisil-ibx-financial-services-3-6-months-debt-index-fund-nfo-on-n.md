## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of the Tata Crisil-IBX Financial Services 3-6 Months Debt Index Fund NFO of TATA Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window runs from September 15, 2026, to September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: TATA Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Tata Crisil-IBX Financial Services 3-6 Months Debt Index Fund (Regular and Direct plans across IDCW and Growth options)
- Subscription Period: September 15, 2026, to September 22, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 23, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 15, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 22, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 23, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to transact in the new debt index fund scheme via the NSE MF Invest Platform within the specified timeline.
