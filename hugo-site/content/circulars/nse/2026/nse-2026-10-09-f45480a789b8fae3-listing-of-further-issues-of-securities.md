## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for five companies effective from October 12, 2026, following allotments under ESOPs and preferential warrant conversions.

## Key Points

- **Ather Energy Limited**: 1,14,350 equity shares of Re. 1/- each allotted under ESOP (Distinctive Nos: 396603752 to 396718101, ISIN: INE0LEZ01016).
- **Emkay Global Financial Services Limited**: 1,45,449 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP (Distinctive Nos: 27380132 to 27525580, ISIN: INE296H01011).
- **PC Jeweller Limited**: 1,10,50,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each allotted pursuant to conversion of warrants issued on preferential basis at an issue price of Rs. 18/- (Distinctive Nos: 9741084856 to 9752134855, ISIN: INE785M01021, Lock-in up to 11-Apr-2028).
- **Quick Heal Technologies Limited**: 2,431 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP (Distinctive Nos: 72276495 to 72278925, ISIN: INE306L01010).
- **Safari Industries (India) Limited**: 1,130 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP (Distinctive Nos: 48997307 to 48998436, ISIN: INE429E01023).

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in these securities shall be identified by designated codes and lot sizes as specified in the Annexure. Shares allotted in dematerialized mode credited under temporary ISINs per SEBI circulars where applicable.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: October 12, 2026
- **Circular Date**: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing of further securities issued via ESOPs and preferential warrant conversion. Expands the paid-up equity capital base of the respective companies without broader systemic market impact.
