## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the Axis Nifty Energy ETF New Fund Offer (NFO) of Axis Mutual Fund will be available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 12, 2026, to August 21, 2026, ending at 11:59 pm.

## Key Points

- **Platform Availability:** The Axis Nifty Energy ETF NFO can be transacted on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Transaction Window:** Active from August 12, 2026, to August 21, 2026, until 11:59 pm.
- **Schemes Details:**
  - **Axis Nifty Energy ETF Direct Growth** (ISIN: `INF846KA1598`, Category: Equity, Allotment Date: August 27, 2026)
  - **Axis Nifty Energy ETF Regular Growth** (ISIN: `INF846KA1598`, Category: Equity, Allotment Date: August 27, 2026)
- **Funding Requirement:** Members must ensure clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 27, 2026, till 12:30 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced in this circular. This is a standard operational update facilitating the availability of a new mutual fund product under existing frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Funds Clearance:** Trading members and participants must verify that payment modes used for transactions ensure clear funds reach the Clearing Corporation's designated account by 12:30 pm on August 27, 2026.
- **Order Timelines:** Order collection and processing must strictly adhere to the operational deadline of August 21, 2026, at 11:59 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** August 12, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** August 21, 2026 (until 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Clearing Pay-In Deadline:** August 27, 2026 (by 12:30 pm)

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Members/Brokers:** Low operational impact. System setups should ensure availability of the scheme ISIN and compliance with the specified pay-in deadline.
- **Investors:** Provides access to the Axis Nifty Energy ETF NFO during the specified subscription window.
- **Market/System:** Neutral. Represents standard mutual fund platform operations.
