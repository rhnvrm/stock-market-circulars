## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. While the annexure reports nil securities shortlisted across the various stages for this cycle, the circular reiterates the applicable surveillance actions and operational provisions under the ASM framework.

## Key Points

- Announces provisions and applicability under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) framework.
- Annexure lists for Stage I, Stage IV, and stage transitions currently record no securities shortlisted (Nil).
- Applicable rate of margin is set to 100% on open and new positions for affected securities.
- Scrips qualifying under criteria VII are shifted from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term ASM surveillance provisions in conjunction with prevailing exchange measures.
- Shift of qualifying scrips under criteria VII from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) on a T+3 basis.

## Compliance Requirements

- NSE members must note that a 100% margin rate applies w.e.f. October 26, 2026, on all open positions as on October 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for any shortlisted securities.
- Market participants must ensure compliance with trade-for-trade settlement requirements where applicable.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Position Open Date for Margin:** October 23, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margin & Segment Shifts:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Establishes clear surveillance oversight and margin safeguards under the Long-Term ASM framework.
- Current reporting indicates zero impacted securities for this specific issuance, resulting in neutral immediate market impact while maintaining standard regulatory preparedness.
