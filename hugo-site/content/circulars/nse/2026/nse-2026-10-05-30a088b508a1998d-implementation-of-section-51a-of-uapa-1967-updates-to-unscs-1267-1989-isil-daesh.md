## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding updates to the United Nations Security Council's (UNSC) 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List pursuant to Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967 (UAPA).

## Key Points

- UNSC Committee enacted amendments to its ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List via Note SC/16465 dated September 28, 2026.
- Stock exchanges, depositories, KRAs, and registered intermediaries must ensure they do not maintain accounts for individuals or entities appearing on the updated sanctions lists.
- Delisting requests are to be forwarded electronically to the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) and marked to the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA).

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates to the UNSC 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List implemented under Section 51A of UAPA, 1967 and SEBI Master Circular guidelines on AML/CFT.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock exchanges, depositories, KRAs, and registered intermediaries must verify client databases against the updated UNSC lists and ensure asset freezes and prohibition of financial services where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- UNSC Note Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ongoing operational necessity for intermediaries to maintain robust screening mechanisms against updated UNSC sanctions lists to ensure compliance and prevent terrorist financing.
