## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions in HSBC Gold Fund of Fund under HSBC Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from close of business hours on October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Scheme: HSBC Gold Fund of Fund (FOF).
- Suspended Activities: Lumpsum purchases, switch-ins, fresh registration of Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal Option (IDCW) Transfer Plan (where HSBC Gold FOF is the target scheme).
- Unaffected Activities: Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-out, switches between plans/options of the scheme, and processing of existing registered SIP, STP, IDCW Transfer Plan, and SWP instalments.
- Effective Date: Close of business hours on October 15, 2026, until further notice.
- Fresh applications received after the cut-off time on the Effective Date are liable to be rejected with amounts refunded within 5 business days.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; operational suspension initiated by the AMC (HSBC Mutual Fund).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the suspension of fresh registrations and lumpsum subscriptions in HSBC Gold FOF on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026 (close of business hours).
- Circular Date: October 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors wishing to make fresh lumpsum investments or start new SIPs/STPs in HSBC Gold FOF will be restricted. Existing investors and ongoing systematic plans remain fully functional for processing and redemptions.
