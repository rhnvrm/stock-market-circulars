## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the extension of trading of Rights Entitlements (RE) and the issue closing date for Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited pursuant to its Rights Issue. The last date for on-market renunciation is extended to September 23, 2026, and the issue closing date is extended to September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited
- Symbol / RE Symbol: RCDL / RCDL-RE
- ISIN: INE0BZQ01011
- Revised Last Date for On-Market Renunciation: Wednesday, September 23, 2026
- Revised Issue Closing Date: Tuesday, September 29, 2026
- Suspension Date: Wednesday, September 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Timelines for the post-issue activities and market renunciation have been revised by the Rights Issue Committee to provide additional time for shareholders to exercise their rights.

## Compliance Requirements

Eligible equity shareholders and trading members must take note of the revised schedule and ensure applications along with the application amounts are submitted within the extended timelines.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: Monday, August 31, 2026
- Last Date for On-Market Renunciation: Wednesday, September 23, 2026
- Date of Suspension: Wednesday, September 24, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: Tuesday, September 29, 2026
- Finalisation of Basis of Allotment: Wednesday, September 30, 2026
- Date of Allotment / Credit of Shares / Listing: Tuesday, October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides extended operational flexibility and trading window for investors participating in the Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited rights issue.
