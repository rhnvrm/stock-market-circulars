## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular informing members that SEBI, vide confirmatory order no. WTM/KV/CFID/CFID-SEC6/32770/2026-27 dated October 14, 2026, has ordered that Mr. Rajesh Mehta shall continue to be restrained from buying, selling, or otherwise dealing in the securities of Rajesh Exports Limited, directly or indirectly, until further orders.

## Key Points

- References previous SEBI interim order dated June 03, 2026, and NSE circular no. NSE/INVG/74537.
- Restrains Mr. Rajesh Mehta (PAN: ADKPM0172C) from dealing in securities of Rajesh Exports Limited.
- Confirmatory order follows proceedings covering non-cooperation, non-disclosure, and financial misrepresentation findings.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of debarment and trading restrictions on specified entity in respect of Rajesh Exports Limited securities until further notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the order and ensure strict compliance, ensuring no prohibited transactions are undertaken for the restrained entity.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 14, 2026
- Date of SEBI Confirmatory Order: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High regulatory impact ensuring enforcement of debarment and maintaining market integrity regarding securities of Rajesh Exports Limited.
