## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the launch of AlphaGrep Liquid Omni FOF New Fund Offer (NFO) under AlphaGrep Mutual Fund. The NFO will be available for transacting on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 04, 2026, to August 06, 2026, until 11:59 pm.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Availability:** AlphaGrep Liquid Omni FOF NFO of AlphaGrep Mutual Fund will be active on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 4, 2026, to August 6, 2026.
- **Scheme Variations:** The NFO includes six schemes in total (Regular and Direct, each with Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options), all falling under the Debt category.
- **ISIN Codes:** Individual ISINs are assigned ranging from INF2VOA01160 to INF2VOA01152.
- **Funding Condition:** Trading members must ensure clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 6, 2026, till 11:59 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory updates or changes are associated with this operational announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members participating in the NFO must ensure that client funds, regardless of payment mode, are settled into the specified Clearing Corporation account before the cutoff time of August 06, 2026, 11:59 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 04, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** August 06, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low. The circular provides routine operational specifications for a new fund launch on the exchange's mutual fund platform. Only transacting members on the NSE MF Invest Platform need to ensure operational alignment and timely funds settlement.
