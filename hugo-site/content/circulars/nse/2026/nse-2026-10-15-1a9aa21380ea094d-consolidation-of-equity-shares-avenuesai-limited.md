## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the consolidation of equity shares of AvenuesAI Limited. Trading in the company's shares will resume effective October 19, 2026, following the share consolidation.

## Key Points

- Company Name: AvenuesAI Limited
- Symbol: CCAVENUE
- New Face Value: Rs. 10/- per share (consolidated from Re. 1/-)
- New ISIN: INE483S01038
- Effective Date of Resumption of Trading: October 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, trading is resumed following the corporate action of share consolidation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the new ISIN, face value, and trading resumption date for AvenuesAI Limited.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date / Trading Resumption Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding shares of AvenuesAI Limited will see their equity shares consolidated, altering the face value and ISIN, with trading resuming smoothly on the specified date.
