## Summary

NSE has announced the listing of equity shares of ArMee Infotech Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The company has also submitted its shareholding pattern pursuant to Regulation 31 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 as of September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: ArMee Infotech Limited
- Class of Security: Equity Shares
- Filing Regulation: Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015
- Effective Date / Post Issue of IPO: September 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; routine listing and statutory shareholding pattern disclosure under SEBI LODR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers must maintain ongoing compliance with SEBI LODR requirements regarding periodic shareholding pattern submissions and corporate disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public trading of ArMee Infotech Limited equity shares on the exchange, providing market liquidity and expanding investment opportunities.
