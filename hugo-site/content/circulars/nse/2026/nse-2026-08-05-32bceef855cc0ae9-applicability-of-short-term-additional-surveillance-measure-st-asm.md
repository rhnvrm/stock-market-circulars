## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Based on surveillance parameters, twelve (12) securities have met the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I. Enhanced margin requirements will take effect from August 07, 2026. No securities have been added to ST-ASM Stage II, and no securities are transitioning from Stage I to Stage II in this update.

## Key Points

- **Inclusion in ST-ASM Stage I**: 12 securities have been shortlisted and placed under ST-ASM Stage I.
- **Enhanced Margins for Stage I**: Shortlisted Stage I securities are subject to an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at a maximum of 100%.
- **No Stage II Additions**: No securities were shortlisted for ST-ASM Stage II or transitioned from Stage I to Stage II.
- **Surveillance Action Clarification**: Shortlisting under the ASM framework is based purely on market surveillance criteria and is not an adverse action against the listed companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- **ST-ASM Stage I Shortlisting**: The following 12 securities are shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I:
  1. Aarti Surfactants Limited (AARTISURF)
  2. Ambalal Sarabhai Enterprises Limited (AMBALALSA)
  3. Arihant Foundations & Housing Limited (ARIHANT)
  4. Best Agrolife Limited (BESTAGRO)
  5. Deep Industries Limited (DEEPINDS)
  6. Dhariwalcorp Limited (DHARIWAL)
  7. E2E Networks Limited (E2E)
  8. Indo Rama Synthetics (India) Limited (INDORAMA)
  9. Indo Thai Securities Limited (INDOTHAI)
  10. M.V.K. Agro Food Product Limited (MVKAGRO)
  11. Restaurant Brands Asia Limited (RBA)
  12. RMC Switchgears Limited (RMC)

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Collection**: Trading members must collect and maintain the enhanced margin of 50% (or existing margin, whichever is higher, up to 100%) on all open positions as of August 06, 2026, and on new positions created from August 07, 2026, onwards.
- **Risk Management**: Risk Management Systems (RMS) must be updated to enforce the new margin caps on the 12 shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- **August 05, 2026**: Date of circular issuance.
- **August 06, 2026**: Margin applicable on all open positions held at the end of this day.
- **August 07, 2026**: Effective date of the enhanced margin rates and new surveillance actions.

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading and Market Impact**: Shortlisted securities will likely see reduced speculative activity, higher borrowing/trading costs, and potential near-term pressure on liquidity or trading volumes due to the increased margin requirements.
- **Operational Impact**: Brokerages and trading members must verify margin compliance and ensure systems are aligned with the new margin rates starting August 07, 2026.
