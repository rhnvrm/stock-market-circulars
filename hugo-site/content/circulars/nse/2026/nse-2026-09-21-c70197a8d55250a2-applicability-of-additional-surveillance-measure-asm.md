## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for specific securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion under Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Stage I).

## Key Points

- Securities included in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I: Avalon Technologies Limited, Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited, and Shivam Autotech Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective September 24, 2026, on all open positions as on September 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long-Term ASM framework measures including 100% margin requirements on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must maintain 100% margins on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Segment Shifts: September 24, 2026
- Framework Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will face higher margin constraints (100%), impacting liquidity and leverage for market participants.
