## Summary

SEBI has issued an order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1) and 11B(2) read with Section 15HA and 15I of the SEBI Act, 1992 in the matter of front running of orders of a Big Client (certain family trusts) by certain entities including Hansraj Randhir Shah HUF, Dipesh Mehta HUF, and other noticees.

## Key Points

- Involves allegations and findings regarding front running of orders of a Big Client (family trusts).
- Noticees include numerous individuals and HUFs (such as Hansraj Randhir Shah HUF, Dipesh Mehta HUF, Shankar Tukaram Vadatkar, etc.).
- Covers procedural aspects including delay in issuance of SCN, Adjudication Rules, settlement proceedings (e.g. Angel One and Noticees 1 to 5 and 25), and cross-examination requests.
- Sets out comprehensive findings on merits, conclusions, and specific orders/directions.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and directions issued pursuant to SEBI Act, 1992 against entities involved in front running and unauthorized trading practices.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and intermediaries named in the order must comply with the specific directions, restraint orders, and monetary penalties or settlement terms as mandated by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Proceedings: 2025-26 (Reference settlement order dated December 19, 2024)

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight against market malpractices, front running, and unauthorized trading activities across market participants and intermediaries.
