## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for several companies, effective from August 13, 2026, pursuant to preferential issues and ESOP allotments.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on NSE capital market segment effective August 13, 2026.
- Includes shares issued via preferential warrants and ESOPs.
- Companies include B.A.G Films and Media Limited, CARE Ratings Limited, Cipla Limited, Cohance Lifesciences Limited, EPL Limited, The Federal Bank Limited, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure trading occurs using designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action with minor impact on equity capital expansion for the listed entities.
