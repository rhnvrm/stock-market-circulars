## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and commencement of trading for equity shares of Oneclick Logistics India Limited pursuant to a bonus issue, effective from October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Oneclick Logistics India Limited
- Symbol: OLIL
- ISIN: INE0OPS01011
- Number of Securities: 34,22,358
- Distinctive Number Range: 6844724 to 10267081
- Record Date: October 15, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify the security solely by its designated codes for all trading and settlement operations.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 15, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Oneclick Logistics India Limited through the addition of bonus shares, enhancing liquidity and trading volume for shareholders.
