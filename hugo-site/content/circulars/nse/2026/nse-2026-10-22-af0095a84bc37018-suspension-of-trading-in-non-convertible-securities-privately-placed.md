## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the suspension of trading in non-convertible securities (privately placed) for REC Limited, effective from October 23, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A due to redemption.

## Key Points

- Company Name: REC Limited
- ISIN: INE020B08AA3
- Suspension Date: October 23, 2026
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension executed pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the trading suspension in the specified non-convertible securities and ensure no further trades are executed post the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 22, 2026
- Suspension Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine market operation reflecting the redemption and delisting/suspension of privately placed non-convertible debentures; minimal impact on broader equity market operations.
