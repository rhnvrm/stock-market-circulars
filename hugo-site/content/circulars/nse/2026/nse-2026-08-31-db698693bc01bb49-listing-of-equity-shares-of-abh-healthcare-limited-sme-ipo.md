## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of ABH Healthcare Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform, effective from September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: ABH Healthcare Limited
- Symbol: ABH
- ISIN: INE1R2M01019
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- Issue Price: Rs. 102/- per share
- Total No. of Securities: 11,42,9600
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 11429600
- Market Lot: 1200 Equity Shares
- Series: ST (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment – Settlement Type ‘W’)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the designated security codes, lot sizes, and trading segments for order entry and execution.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of ABH Healthcare Limited equity shares on the NSE EMERGE SME platform under the trade-for-trade segment initially.
