## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares of Supreme Commercial Enterprises Limited. The circular outlines key dates, order times, custodial confirmation windows, and settlement dates for retail and non-retail investors.

## Key Points

- Settlement calendar applies to Offer for Sale of Supreme Commercial Enterprises Limited.
- Covers non-retail (IS) and retail (RS) categories for trade dates October 13, 2026 and October 14, 2026.
- Funds pay-in is scheduled for T+1 day by 11:00 am, and securities & funds pay-out by 01:30 pm.
- Applicable securities transaction tax (STT) and stamp duty will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes timeline and procedures for OFS settlement under secondary market transaction rules as referenced in circular NCL/CMPT/73996 dated April 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must adhere to order entry, custodial confirmation, and margin requirements within the specified timeframes.
- Funds pay-in and securities pay-out must be completed by T+1 deadlines.

## Important Dates

- **October 13, 2026:** Order date for non-retail/retail OFS tranche 1 (Settlement Date: October 14, 2026).
- **October 14, 2026:** Order date for non-retail/retail OFS tranche 2 (Settlement Date: October 15, 2026).
- **Funds Pay-In:** T+1 day by 11:00 am.
- **Securities & Funds Pay-Out:** T+1 day by 01:30 pm.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth clearing, settlement, and tax collection for market participants participating in the Offer for Sale.
