## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has scheduled a mock session on August 08, 2026, to test its Software as a Service (SaaS) setup and ensure two-way portability across Clearing Corporations. This exercise is in line with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/P/CIR/2023/192 dated December 20, 2023, which mandates quarterly mock sessions to train members and ensure business continuity. During this mock session, NCL will simulate operations using the SaaS setup (NCL@ICCL) during specified hours.

## Key Points

- **SaaS Mock Session**: NCL will conduct a mock session on August 08, 2026, to test the SaaS model of business continuity.
- **Portability Focus**: The test focuses on two-way portability across Clearing Corporations under the SaaS setup.
- **Session Timings**: NCL will invoke the SaaS setup from 13:45 hrs to 14:15 hrs, and NCL will be fully operational via the SaaS setup (NCL@ICCL) between 14:15 hrs and 15:30 hrs.
- **Access Credentials**: Members can use their existing login credentials for the scheduled mock session. New login credentials can be requested by emailing `support@nsccl.co.in`.
- **References**: Guidelines and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) are available in NCL circular 0138/2023 (dated April 13, 2023) and NCL circular 088/2024 (dated May 10, 2024).

## Regulatory Changes

- **SEBI Mandate**: The mock session is held pursuant to SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/P/CIR/2023/192 dated December 20, 2023.
- **Quarterly Requirement**: SEBI mandates mock testing of SaaS-based clearing corporation BCP (Business Continuity Planning) setups at least once in a quarter to maintain robust risk management and service availability.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Participation**: All clearing members are requested to actively participate and test requisite functionalities on the mock trading day.
- **Testing Interface**: Members must test the key interfaces, including RTRMS, Collateral, and Extranet links:
  - RTRMS Link: https://isrm.connect2nsccl.com/stocks/jsp/rms/
  - Collateral Link: https://isrmcoll.connect2nsccl.com/application/applogin/login.aspx
  - Extranet Link: https://isrmxnet.connect2nsccl.com/Extranet_Login.aspx
- **Credential Verification**: Members who have not yet obtained their login credentials must email `support@nsccl.co.in` to secure them prior to the session.

## Important Dates

- **August 07, 2026**: Date of circular issuance.
- **August 08, 2026**: Date of mock session testing.
  - **13:45 hrs – 14:15 hrs**: Invocation of SaaS setup.
  - **14:15 hrs – 15:30 hrs**: Active operation of NCL via SaaS setup (NCL@ICCL).

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: High during the specified test window. Members must configure and verify their interfaces to connect to the NCL@ICCL SaaS environment. This ensures that trading and clearing members are prepared to seamlessly migrate to the secondary site in the event of an actual disaster.
- **Market Integrity**: Promotes market-wide resiliency by validating the backup clearing infrastructure and business continuity procedures across clearing institutions.
