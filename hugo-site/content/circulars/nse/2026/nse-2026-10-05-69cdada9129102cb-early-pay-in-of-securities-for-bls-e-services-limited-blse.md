## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued guidelines regarding the early pay-in (EPI) of securities for BLS E-Services Limited (BLSE) on account of a face value split (sub-division) from Rs 10/- per share to Rs 5/- per share.

## Key Points

- **Corporate Action**: Face value split from Rs 10/- per share to Rs 5/- per share.
- **Security Details**: BLS E-Services Limited (Symbol: BLSE, Old ISIN: INE0NLT01010).
- **Record Date & Ex-Date**: October 6, 2026.
- **EPI Instructions**: Members executing early pay-in for specified settlement dates (October 6, 2026, and October 7, 2026) must make early pay-in in the depository systems using the existing/old ISIN without adjusting for conversion in the applicable settlement type.
- **Block Mechanism**: Members must adhere to the NCL circular on the Block Mechanism in clients' demat accounts; client-wise early pay-in allocation file should not be uploaded when using block mechanism.
- **Pool Account EPI**: Custodians providing early pay-in through the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details based on actual traded quantity to pass benefits to clients.
- **Post Record Date**: Participants/custodians must execute pay-in/early pay-in instructions in the new ISIN after the record date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Procedural adjustments for handling early pay-in of securities in old vs. new ISIN during corporate actions (face value split).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members/custodians must execute early pay-in in the old ISIN for the specified settlement numbers (2026188, 2026189).
- Adherence to block mechanism guidelines without uploading client-wise allocation files where applicable.
- Mandatory uploading of client-wise early pay-in allocation details for pool account transactions based on actual traded quantity.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date**: 06-10-2026
- **Ex Date**: 06-10-2026
- **EPI Dates / Settlements**: 
  - 06-10-2026 (Settlement No: 2026188)
  - 07-10-2026 (Settlement No: 2026188 & 2026189)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures seamless settlement processing and correct ISIN handling by market participants and custodians during the stock split of BLS E-Services Limited.
