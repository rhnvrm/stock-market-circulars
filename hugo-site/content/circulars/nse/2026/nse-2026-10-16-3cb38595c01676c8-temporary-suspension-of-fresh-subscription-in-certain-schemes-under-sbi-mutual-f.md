## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, including switch-ins and fresh registration under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), for certain schemes under SBI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and new registrations for SIP and STP are disabled for certain SBI Mutual Fund schemes.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and STP-outs remain unaffected.
- Existing SIPs and STPs will continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA end without any changes.
- Applicable on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

Temporary restriction on subscriptions and fresh systematic investment/transfer registrations for specific SBI Mutual Fund schemes based on communication received from SBI Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors must take note of the restrictions and ensure that clients are advised accordingly regarding affected SBI Mutual Fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: With immediate effect (October 16, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Investors are unable to make fresh investments, switch-ins, or initiate new SIPs/STPs in the restricted SBI Mutual Fund schemes through the NSE MF Invest platform. Liquidity via redemptions and processing of existing recurring instructions remain uninterrupted.
