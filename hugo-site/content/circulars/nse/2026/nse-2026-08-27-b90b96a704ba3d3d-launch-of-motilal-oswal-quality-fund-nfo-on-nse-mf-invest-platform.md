## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the Motilal Oswal Quality Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from August 28, 2026, to September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Motilal Oswal Quality Fund (Direct and Regular plans including Growth and IDCW options)
- NFO Open Date: August 28, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 11, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 18, 2026, till 3:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening: August 28, 2026
- NFO Closing: September 11, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 18, 2026 (3:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to transact in the new fund offerings smoothly via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
