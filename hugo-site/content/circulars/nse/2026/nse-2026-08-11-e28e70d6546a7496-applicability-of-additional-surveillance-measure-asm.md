## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued circular ref. no. 614/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/SURV/75692) dated August 11, 2026, regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Based on surveillance criteria, five securities have met the parameters for inclusion under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) Stage-I, effective from August 12, 2026. This surveillance measure carries a 100% margin requirement on all positions, effective from August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Long-Term ASM Stage-I Inclusion**: Five securities have met the criteria and are shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Stage-I:
  1. **Aimtron Electronics Limited** (AIMTRON | ISIN: INE0RUV01018)
  2. **Sri Havisha Hospitality and Infrastructure Limited** (HAVISHA | ISIN: INE293B01029) - *Satisfies criteria at BSE*
  3. **Tribhovandas Bhimji Zaveri Limited** (TBZ | ISIN: INE760L01018)
  4. **Tijaria Polypipes Limited** (TIJARIA | ISIN: INE440L01017)
  5. **Vishnu Prakash R Punglia Limited** (VPRPL | ISIN: INE0AE001013)
- **Surveillance Actions**: For the shortlisted securities, a 100% margin rate will be applicable on all open positions as of August 13, 2026, and all new positions created from August 14, 2026 onwards.
- **Stage IV Transition to Trade-for-Trade**: Securities qualifying under Criteria VII (shifted to Stage IV) will move from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. August 14, 2026. Currently, no new securities are shortlisted under Stage IV in this circular.
- **Nature of Action**: The exchange emphasizes that the shortlisting of securities under the ASM framework is purely a market surveillance measure and should not be interpreted as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

No broad regulatory policy changes are introduced in this circular. This is an operational update applying the existing Long-Term ASM framework (established under prior circulars including NSE/SURV/39265, NSE/SURV/45111, etc.) to a new set of shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members/Brokers**:
  - Must collect and maintain a 100% margin rate on all open positions as of August 13, 2026, and on all new positions created from August 14, 2026, for the five shortlisted securities (AIMTRON, HAVISHA, TBZ, TIJARIA, VPRPL).
  - Ensure systems and risk management processes are updated to reflect the 100% margin requirement.
  - Advise clients regarding the increased margin requirements and potential risk of trade-for-trade shifting for any securities moving to Stage IV under Criteria VII.

## Important Dates

- **August 11, 2026**: Date of circular issuance.
- **August 12, 2026**: Effective date for shortlisting in the Long-Term ASM Stage-I framework.
- **August 13, 2026**: All open positions at the end of this day will be subject to the 100% margin requirement.
- **August 14, 2026**: 100% margin rate takes effect on all open and new positions. Additionally, any qualifying Criteria VII (Stage IV) scrips are shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series BE) (no new scrips shortlisted for Stage IV under the current annexure).

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity Impact**: Shortlisted securities (AIMTRON, HAVISHA, TBZ, TIJARIA, VPRPL) will likely experience decreased trading volume and liquidity due to the stringent 100% margin requirement. Traders must fully fund their positions, which discourages speculative intraday leverage.
- **Capital Cost**: Market participants holding overnight or long positions in these stocks will face increased capital lock-in, as they can no longer leverage these positions.
- **No Corporate Prejudice**: As highlighted by the Exchange, this measure is a surveillance tool to curb extreme volatility and does not indicate corporate governance or operational issues in the shortlisted firms.
