## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability and reopening of HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities starting September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes Reopening: HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF of HDFC Mutual Fund.
- Effective Date: September 07, 2026.
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to note the ISIN details for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform:
- HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF Regular Growth: INF179KC1KN7 (Category: Debt)
- HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF Direct Growth: INF179KC1KO5 (Category: Debt)

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to transact in the specified HDFC Mutual Fund scheme via the NSE MF Invest Platform with full facility support.
