## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from September 04, 2026. These shares were allotted pursuant to ESOP exercises and preferential warrant conversions.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment effective September 04, 2026.
- Includes further issuances from Angel One Limited, Axis Bank Limited, Borosil Renewables Limited, DCB Bank Limited, and others.
- Allotments comprise shares issued under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and preferential warrant conversions.
- Trading will be conducted in the designated lot sizes and security codes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 03, 2026
- Effective Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing event for the respective companies with minimal direct market impact.
