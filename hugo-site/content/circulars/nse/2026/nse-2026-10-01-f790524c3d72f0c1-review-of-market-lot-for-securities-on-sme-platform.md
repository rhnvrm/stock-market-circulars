## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has conducted its half-yearly review of market lot sizes for active securities on the SME platform based on SEBI guidelines. A total of 60 symbols have their market lots revised downwards, effective November 02, 2026, while 514 symbols remain unchanged.

## Key Points

- Half-yearly review conducted for SME platform securities based on SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DSA/06/2012.
- 60 symbols have market lots revised downwards.
- 514 symbols remain unchanged.
- Effective date for the revised lot sizes is November 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standardization and periodic half-yearly review of trading lot sizes on the SME exchange platform to align with liquidity and price guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to load the updated `security.gz` / `nnf_security.gz` file from the `/common/NTNEAT` directory on the extranet server into their trading applications prior to trading on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date for Revised Lot Size: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading participants and investors dealing in the affected SME securities must update their systems and take note of the revised order quantities effective November 02, 2026.
