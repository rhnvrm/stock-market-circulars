## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has notified all members regarding the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities (NCDs) of three companies: Creamline Dairy Products Limited, NTPC Limited, and UltraTech Cement Limited. The suspension is scheduled to take effect from August 06, 2026. This action is taken in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A, following the scheduled redemption of these securities.

## Key Points

- **Suspension of NCDs**: Trading in specific privately placed Non-Convertible Securities of Creamline Dairy Products Limited, NTPC Limited, and UltraTech Cement Limited will be suspended.
- **Effective Date**: The suspension will take effect starting August 06, 2026.
- **Reason for Suspension**: The suspension of these securities is due to their redemption/maturity.
- **Regulatory Provision**: The action is implemented under Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no structural regulatory changes introduced by this circular. This is an operational enforcement of existing Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A regarding the suspension of matured/redeemed debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members/Market Participants**: Debt market trading members must note the suspension date (August 06, 2026) and ensure no further trades are executed or cleared for the affected ISINs post-suspension.
- **Issuers**: The issuing companies (Creamline Dairy Products Limited, NTPC Limited, and UltraTech Cement Limited) must complete the scheduled redemption processes and necessary reporting to the exchange and depositories.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 05, 2026
- **Suspension Date (Effective Date)**: August 06, 2026

**Affected Securities:**
1. **Creamline Dairy Products Limited**: ISIN `INE412L08011` (Suspension Date: August 06, 2026; Reason: Redemption)
2. **NTPC Limited**: ISIN `INE733E07KE8` (Suspension Date: August 06, 2026; Reason: Redemption)
3. **UltraTech Cement Limited**: ISIN `INE481G07190` (Suspension Date: August 06, 2026; Reason: Redemption)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Minimal. The suspension is a routine operational procedure corresponding to the scheduled redemption/maturity of the private placement NCDs. There is no disruption to the equity shares of NTPC Limited (NTPC) or UltraTech Cement Limited (ULTRACEMCO) or other active debt instruments.
- **Investor Impact**: Investors holding these specific ISINs will receive the redemption proceeds as per the terms of the issue, and trading liquidity for these instruments on the exchange platform will cease.
