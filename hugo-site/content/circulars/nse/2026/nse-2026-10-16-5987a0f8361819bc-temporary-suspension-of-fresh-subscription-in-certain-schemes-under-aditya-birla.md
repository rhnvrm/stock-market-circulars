## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and fresh SIP/STP registrations in Aditya Birla Sun Life Gold Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Acceptance of fresh subscriptions and switch-in applications for Aditya Birla Sun Life Gold Fund is discontinued effective October 16, 2026.
- Fresh registrations under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) are disabled with immediate effect.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and STP-outs remain fully unrestricted.
- Existing Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA end without changes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary operational restriction on fresh inflows into specific mutual fund schemes on the platform as per AMC communication.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must note the suspension and avoid submitting new lumpsum or fresh SIP/STP applications for the affected scheme on the platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Last date for fresh subscriptions/switch-ins: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact; affects investors attempting fresh inflows into Aditya Birla Sun Life Gold Fund via NSE MF Invest Platform, while existing systematic investments and redemptions remain unaffected.
