## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular updating the list of securities under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective July 29, 2026. Under these measures, three securities are shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I, and two securities are excluded from the ASM framework. The applicable margin rate changes for Stage I securities (50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%) will take effect on all open positions as on July 29, 2026, and new positions created from July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Shortlisting**: Three securities—Asahi Songwon Colors Limited (ASAHISONG), Optivalue Tek Consulting Limited (OPTIVALUE), and Picturepost Studios Limited (PPSL)—have satisfied the criteria for inclusion in Short-Term ASM Stage I.
- **ST-ASM Stage II Shortlisting**: No securities have been shortlisted for Stage II or transitioned between Stage I and Stage II.
- **Exclusions**: Rappid Valves (India) Limited (RAPPID) and SIL are excluded from the ASM framework.
- **Margin Requirements**: For Stage I securities, the margin rate is increased to 50% (or existing margin, whichever is higher, up to 100%). For Stage II, the margin rate is 100%.
- **Non-Adverse Action**: Shortlisting under the ASM framework is based purely on market surveillance parameters and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Application of ST-ASM Stage I surveillance action for the shortlisted securities under the pre-existing ASM frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members/Brokers**: Must implement the enhanced margin rate of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) for ASAHISONG, OPTIVALUE, and PPSL starting July 30, 2026.
- **Client Leverage**: Collection of higher margin requirements from clients for both outstanding open positions (as of July 29, 2026) and new positions.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 28, 2026
- **Effective Date for Inclusion/Exclusion**: July 29, 2026
- **Effective Date for Revised Margin Rates**: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: The shortlisted securities (ASAHISONG, OPTIVALUE, PPSL) will see higher margin requirements, likely reducing trading leverage, volume, and volatility in these names. Conversely, the exclusion of RAPPID and SIL from the ASM framework removes margin restrictions, which may improve liquidity and interest in these stocks.
- **Operational Impact**: Risk management systems of brokerages must be updated to reflect the new margin requirements for the shortlisted securities before market open on July 30, 2026.
