## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and trading of equity shares of Pearl Global Industries Limited pursuant to a bonus issue. The shares will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pearl Global Industries Limited
- Symbol: PGIL
- ISIN: INE940H01022
- Record Date: September 11, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Number of Bonus Securities: 4,61,90,542
- Distinctive Number Range: 46190543 to 92381084

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure trading is conducted using the designated security codes effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 11, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date for Listing & Trading: September 16, 2026

Expands the equity capital base and liquidity of Pearl Global Industries Limited on the NSE Capital Market segment.
