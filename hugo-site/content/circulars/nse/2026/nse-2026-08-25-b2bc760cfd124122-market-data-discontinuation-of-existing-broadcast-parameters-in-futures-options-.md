## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a reminder to all members regarding the discontinuation of existing market data broadcast parameters in the Futures & Options (F&O) segment. Members must complete their migration to the new parameters prior to September 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reminder for NNF users regarding discontinuation of existing market data broadcast parameters (5 depth 1 second refresh).
- Change involves data type for token-level cumulative volume (total traded volume) updated from "Unsigned Integer" (4 bytes) to "LONG" (8 bytes).
- Coexistence period was provided via earlier circulars to facilitate smooth migration.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition from old broadcast parameters to new broadcast parameters for F&O market data.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members using NNF must complete migration or ensure readiness of their migration plan before the discontinuation date.
- Review respective market data broadcast configurations to ensure uninterrupted receipt of F&O market data.

## Important Dates

- **Discontinuation Date:** September 05, 2026 (mock) onwards.

## Impact Assessment

- Members failing to migrate before the deadline may face disruption in receiving F&O market data broadcasts.
