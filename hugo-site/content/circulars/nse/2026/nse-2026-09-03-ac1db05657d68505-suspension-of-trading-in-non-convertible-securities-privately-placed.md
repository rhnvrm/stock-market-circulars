## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) for three companies—CEAT Limited, Sammaan Finserve Limited, and HDFC Bank Limited—effective from September 4, 2026, following their redemption.

## Key Points

- **CEAT Limited:** ISIN INE482A08025 suspended from trading effective 04-Sep-2026 due to Redemption.
- **Sammaan Finserve Limited:** ISIN INE244L07242 suspended from trading effective 04-Sep-2026 due to Redemption.
- **HDFC Bank Limited:** ISIN INE040A08369 suspended from trading effective 04-Sep-2026 due to Redemption.
- **Regulatory Basis:** Action taken in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to take note of the suspension of trading in the aforementioned securities.

## Important Dates

- **Suspension Date:** September 04, 2026
- **Circular Date:** September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified non-convertible securities of the listed entities is halted due to complete redemption, with no broader operational impact on equity markets.
