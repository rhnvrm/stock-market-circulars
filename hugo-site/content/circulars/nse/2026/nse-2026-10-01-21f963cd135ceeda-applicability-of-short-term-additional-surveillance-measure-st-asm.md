## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I to specific securities effective October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Flywings Simulator Training Centre Limited (FWSTC), Hero Motors Limited (HEROMOTORS), Quest Laboratories Limited (QUESTLAB), and Veritaas Advertising Limited (VERITAAS).
- Applicable rate of margin is set at 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100%.
- Enhanced margin provisions take effect from October 06, 2026, applicable on all open positions as on October 05, 2026, and new positions created from October 06, 2026.
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance controls and margin caps under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for identified volatile securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% minimum, capped at 100%) for open positions as of October 05, 2026, and new positions created from October 06, 2026, in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Inclusion Date: October 05, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements may lead to reduced leverage and lower trading liquidity for the affected scrips during the surveillance period.
