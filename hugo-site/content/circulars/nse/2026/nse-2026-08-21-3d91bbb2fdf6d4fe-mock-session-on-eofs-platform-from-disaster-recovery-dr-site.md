## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock bidding session for the eOFS platform from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site scheduled for August 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mock session scheduled for August 22, 2026.
- Conducted from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site.
- No changes required at members' end for connecting to Primary/DR site.
- Same URL (https://eofs.nseindia.com) to be used for login.
- Bids placed during the mock session will not attract any obligation in terms of funds and securities.
- Includes test securities: TESTOFS1 (Non-retail category) and TESTOFS2 (Retail and Employee categories).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to participate actively in the mock bidding session to check connectivity and system readiness.

## Important Dates

- Mock Session Date: August 22, 2026
- Normal Market Open: 09:15 hrs
- Normal Market Close: 15:30 hrs

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct operational impact as this is a voluntary/mock testing session with no financial or securities obligations.
