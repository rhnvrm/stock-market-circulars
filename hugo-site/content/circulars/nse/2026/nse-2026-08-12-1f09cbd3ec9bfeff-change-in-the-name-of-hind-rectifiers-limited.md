## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular notifying market participants that the name of Hind Rectifiers Limited will be changed to Hirect Limited. The corporate name change is scheduled to take effect from August 18, 2026. The existing trading symbol HIRECT remains unaffected.

## Key Points

- **Corporate Name Change:** Hind Rectifiers Limited is officially changing its name to Hirect Limited.
- **Ticker Symbol:** The current trading symbol 'HIRECT' remains unchanged.
- **Effective Date:** The changes will be implemented in the Exchange systems starting August 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes are introduced by this circular. It is an administrative notification of corporate name alteration.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market intermediaries should update their master data, back-office databases, and customer-facing interfaces to reflect the new company name 'Hirect Limited' for ticker symbol 'HIRECT' starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Issue Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date of Name Change:** August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. There is no disruption to trading or liquidity, as only the corporate name is changing while the ticker symbol and underlying securities remain active.
- **Operational Impact:** Minor. Intermediaries need to update company mapping and reference databases to reflect the new entity name.
