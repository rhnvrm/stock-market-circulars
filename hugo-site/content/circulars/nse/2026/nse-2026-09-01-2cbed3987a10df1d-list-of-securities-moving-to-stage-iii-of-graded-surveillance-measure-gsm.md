## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the movement of securities to Stage III of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM). Effective September 02, 2026, Blue Chip India Limited (Symbol: BLUECHIP, ISIN: INE657B01025) will be shifted to Stage III.

## Key Points

- **Security Affected:** Blue Chip India Limited (BLUECHIP)
- **ISIN:** INE657B01025
- **Effective Date:** September 02, 2026
- **Surveillance Measure:** Stage III of GSM

## Regulatory Changes

Securities moving to Stage III of GSM are subject to stringent surveillance actions to protect investors and maintain market integrity, building upon previous framework guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading shall be available in Trade for Trade mode with a price band of 5% or lower as applicable.
- Trading is permitted only once a week (every Monday or the first trading day of the week).
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 100% of the trade value must be deposited by buyers.
- Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 1, 2026
- **Effective Date of Action:** September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and frequency for Blue Chip India Limited will be severely restricted due to weekly trading windows, mandatory 100% ASD, and trade-for-trade settlement without netting off, requiring heightened caution from market participants.
