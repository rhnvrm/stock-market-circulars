## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular (Download Ref No: NSE/CML/75613) announcing the listing of units issued by DSP Asset Managers Private Limited for the DSP BSE MidSmall Private Banks ETF. The listing is effective from August 07, 2026. A total of 3,202,980 units of face value Rs. 10/- each, allotted on August 03, 2026, at an issue price of Rs. 17.37/- per unit, will be admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment).

## Key Points

- **Issuer**: DSP Asset Managers Private Limited (Trustee: DSP Trustee Private Limited; AMC: DSP Asset Managers Private Limited)
- **Scheme Name**: DSP BSE MidSmall Private Banks ETF (under DSP Mutual Fund)
- **Symbol**: MIDBANKADD
- **ISIN**: INF740KA1ZH9
- **No. of Units**: 3,202,980 units
- **Series**: EQ
- **Face Value**: Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Paid Up Value**: Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Issue Price**: Rs. 17.37/- per unit
- **Market Lot**: 1
- **Designated Market Makers**:
  - Kanjalochana Finserve Private Limited (TM code: 90246)

  - Kotak Securities Limited (TM code: 08081)
  - Choice Equity Broking Private Limited (TM code: 13773)

- **Registrar and Share Transfer Agent (RTA)**: Computer Age Management Services Limited (CAMS)

## Regulatory Changes

This is a routine operational listing and does not introduce new regulatory changes. It is executed in pursuance of:
- **Regulation 3.1.1** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, concerning admission of securities to dealings.
- **Regulation 2.5.5** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, regarding designated lot sizes for trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are notified that the units of the scheme will be traded under the symbol MIDBANKADD and identification codes specified in the Annexure.
- Trading must be carried out in the designated market lot of 1.
- Market makers appointed for the scheme must fulfill their quoting and market-making obligations under applicable NSE guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment**: August 03, 2026
- **Circular Date**: August 06, 2026
- **Listing & Effective Date**: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Extremely low. The listing of these ETF units provides liquidity and a secondary market trading venue for investors of the DSP BSE MidSmall Private Banks ETF.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading members and the listed market makers can commence trading and market-making activities in the EQ series of MIDBANKADD on the NSE Capital Market segment starting August 07, 2026.
