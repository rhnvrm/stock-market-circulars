## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to certain securities. The shortlisted securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under Long Term ASM or movement between stages effective September 24/28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 28, 2026, on all open positions as on September 25, 2026, and new positions created from September 28, 2026 onwards.
- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Stage I include Apoorva Leasing Finance and Investment Company Limited, Gretex Corporate Services Limited, and Toyam Sports Limited.
- Arvee Laboratories (India) Limited moved from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I to Stage II.
- Scrips shortlisted under criteria VII are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of 100% margin and potential shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment for securities satisfying Long-Term ASM criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collections (100%) on open and new positions for the affected securities starting from the specified effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions: September 28, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement for impacted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the specified scrips.
