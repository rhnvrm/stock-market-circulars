## Summary

Industrial Investment Trust Limited has announced a buyback offer under the Tender Offer scheme using the Exchange's web-based bidding platform. The issue period runs from August 21, 2026, to August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Industrial Investment Trust Limited
- Security Symbol: IITL
- ISIN: INE886A01014
- Issue Size: Up to 16,66,667 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 150/- per equity share
- Issue Period: 21-August-2026 to 28-August-2026 (Excluding 26-Aug-2026 due to Id-E-Milad)
- Bidding Time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. (Custodial confirmation on closure up to 4.00 p.m.)
- Merchant Banker: Systematix Corporate Services Limited
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can use their existing user IDs for the web-based IPO bidding platform to participate in the Tender Offer Facility. Members without access must use the admin terminal to create branches and users.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: 21-August-2026
- Issue Closing Date: 28-August-2026 (Excluding 26-Aug-2026)

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders of Industrial Investment Trust Limited to tender their shares through the NSE bidding platform during the specified issue period.
