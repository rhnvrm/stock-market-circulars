## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities—including Commercial Papers (CPs) and other debt securities—on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from October 19, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Fresh Issue of Commercial Papers: Godrej Industries Limited, IndoStar Capital Finance Limited, Axis Securities Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, and Barclays Investments & Loans (India) Private Limited.
- Fresh Issue of Other Debt Securities: Emerald Haven Life Spaces 3 Private Limited and Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI).
- Re-Issue of Commercial Papers: L&T Finance Limited.
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Issued in accordance with Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations governing debt segment admissions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers must maintain compliance with all applicable listing conditions, disclosure standards, and regulatory filings required by the Exchange and SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 16, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 19, 2026
- Date of Redemption: Varies by security, ranging from November 2026 (e.g., Axis Securities CP) up to October 2046 (Emerald Haven Life Spaces).

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates institutional liquidity and debt capital raising operations for participating companies on the exchange debt market segment.
