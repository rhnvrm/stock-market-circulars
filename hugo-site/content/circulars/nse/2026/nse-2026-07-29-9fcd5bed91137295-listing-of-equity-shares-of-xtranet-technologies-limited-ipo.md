## Summary

This circular outlines the official listing of equity shares of Xtranet Technologies Limited (XTTL) on the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) post-IPO. It includes the shareholding pattern filed under Regulation 31(1)(b) for the period ending July 28, 2026, representing a total post-issue paid-up capital of 52,285,700 equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Name of Listed Entity**: Xtranet Technologies Limited (Post-Issue)
- **Trading Symbol / Scrip Name**: XTTL
- **Shareholding Pattern Summary**:
  - **Promoter & Promoter Group**: Holds 32,743,570 shares (62.62% of total shares). None of these shares are pledged or otherwise encumbered.
  - **Public**: Holds 19,542,130 shares (37.38% of total shares).
  - **Total Shareholders**: 43,240 (4 promoters and 43,236 public shareholders).
- **Lock-in Details**:
  - A total of 43,091,900 shares (82.42% of total capital) are under lock-in.
  - 100% of promoter shares (32,743,570 shares) are locked in.
  - 52.95% of public shares (10,348,330 shares) are locked in.

## Regulatory Changes

This circular does not introduce new regulatory changes but complies with Regulation 31 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

The company is required to maintain disclosures under SEBI LODR, ensuring timely reporting of quarterly shareholding patterns and compliance with lock-in provisions.

## Important Dates

- **Reporting Date / Quarter Ending**: July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: The listing of XTTL shares provides public liquidity. The significant lock-in of 82.42% of the shares restricts the initial floating stock, which may impact trading volumes and volatility.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading commences in XTTL equity shares on the NSE platform.
