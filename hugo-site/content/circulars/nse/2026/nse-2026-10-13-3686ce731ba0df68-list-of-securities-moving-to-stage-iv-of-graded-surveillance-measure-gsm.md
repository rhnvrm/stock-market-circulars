## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the movement of securities to Stage IV of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. Effective October 14, 2026, CLC Industries Limited (Symbol: CLCIND, ISIN: INE376C01038) will be transitioned to Stage IV.

## Key Points

- Security Affected: CLC Industries Limited (CLCIND, ISIN: INE376C01038).
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026.
- Surveillance Measure: Stage IV of Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM).
- Trading Frequency: Permitted once a week (every Monday / 1st trading day of the week).

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities moved to Stage IV are subject to trade-for-trade settlement with a price band of 5% or lower as applicable.
- Trading is restricted to once a week (every Monday or the first trading day of the week).
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 100% of the trade value is to be deposited by the buyers with no upward movement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are required to take adequate precaution while trading in the security.
- Settlement must be executed on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Buyers must deposit 100% ASD.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity for CLCIND will be severely restricted due to weekly trading frequency and 100% ASD requirements.
- Market participants must ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules and avoid netting off.
