## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for various securities effective September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II based on surveillance criteria.
- Applicable margin rate for ST-ASM Stage I is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100% w.e.f. September 07, 2026 on all open positions as on September 04, 2026 and new positions.
- Applicable margin rate for ST-ASM Stage II is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100% w.e.f. September 07, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I, Stage II, and stage migrations for identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure applicable margin requirements (50% or 100%) are maintained for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: September 07, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date for Open Positions: September 04, 2026
- Circular Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for trading in shortlisted securities like AKI India Limited, Atal Realtech Limited, Balaji Phosphates Limited, DCG Cables & Wires Limited, Modison Limited, Ujaas Energy Limited, UFLEX Limited, and Burnpur Cement Limited.
