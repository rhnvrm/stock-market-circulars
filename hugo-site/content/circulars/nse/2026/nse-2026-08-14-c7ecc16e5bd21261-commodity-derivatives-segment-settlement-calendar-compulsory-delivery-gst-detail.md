## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued the settlement calendar for compulsory delivery and the GST details reporting calendar for commodity derivatives segment contracts expiring in September 2026. Market participants dealing in various bullion and base metal commodity derivatives must adhere to the specified timelines for delivery marking, payout of commodities, and GST details uploading.

## Key Points

- Applicable to Commodity Derivatives Segment for September 2026 expiry contracts including gold, silver, aluminum, copper, lead, nickel, and zinc variants.
- Outlines specific timelines for Seller Member and Buyer Member GST details uploading windows.
- Specifies delivery marking, payout of commodities, delivery intent, and funds pay-in/pay-out schedules.
- Mandates proper GST registration, statutory returns filing, and timely delivery of GST invoices between trading members and clients.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct structural regulatory changes introduced; this circular provides routine operational calendars and schedules as per standard exchange procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members accepting or giving delivery of commodities must possess necessary GST registrations under applicable jurisdiction.
- Sellers must raise invoices with accurate GST registration numbers, HSN codes, and file statutory GST returns within stipulated dates.
- Sellers must ensure timely delivery of GST invoices to buyer members/clients as per confirmed details.
- Invoicing processes should be completed at the earliest, respecting the maximum timelines prescribed.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 14, 2026
- Applicability Period: September 2026 expiry contracts
- GST Uploading and Settlement Windows: Commencing from late August 2026 through September 2026 as detailed in Annexure 1 and Annexure 2.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement workflows, tax compliance, and transparent delivery operations for commodity derivatives trading members during the September 2026 settlement cycle.
