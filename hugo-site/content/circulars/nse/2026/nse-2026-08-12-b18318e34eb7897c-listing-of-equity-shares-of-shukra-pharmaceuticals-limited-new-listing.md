## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has approved the listing and admission of 439,614,440 equity shares of Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited (Symbol: SHUKRAPHAR) of face value Re. 1/- each to dealings on the exchange. The circular is effective from August 14, 2026, and the shares will be traded in the Normal Market segment (Rolling Settlement) under compulsory dematerialized form for all investors.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited
- **Symbol:** SHUKRAPHAR
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE551C01044
- **Security Description:** Equity shares of Re. 1/- each fully paid up
- **Total Admitted Securities:** 439,614,440 shares
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 1 to 439,614,440
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Lock-in Details (Annexure I):**
  - 86,600,000 shares are locked in until November 11, 2026 (Distinctive Nos. 1 to 86,600,000)
  - 131,135,600 shares are locked in until November 12, 2026 (Distinctive Nos. 86,600,001 to 217,735,600)
  - 1,735,000 shares are locked in until February 13, 2028 (Distinctive Nos. 217,735,601 to 219,470,600)
  - 220,143,840 shares are free from lock-in (Distinctive Nos. 219,470,601 to 439,614,440)

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory framework changes introduced by this circular. The action is taken under the existing regulatory provisions of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A:
- **Regulation 3.1.1:** Relating to further admission of securities to dealings.
- **Regulation 2.5.5:** Relating to the specification of respective lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Members are advised to trade the security only under its designated codes (Symbol: SHUKRAPHAR, ISIN: INE551C01044) and in the specified market lot of 1.
- **Listing Regulation Compliances:** Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited has filed its Shareholding Pattern under Regulation 31(1)(b) for the pre-listing date of August 10, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Pre-Listing Shareholding Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Circular Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective/Trading Commencement Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry (86.6M shares):** November 11, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry (131.1M shares):** November 12, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry (1.7M shares):** February 13, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Provides secondary market liquidity for the equity shares of Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited on a premier national platform. It will increase visibility and trading access for the company's equity capital.
- **Operational Impact:** Trades in SHUKRAPHAR will settle on a standard rolling settlement basis in demat form. Trading systems will support trading in a lot size of 1.
