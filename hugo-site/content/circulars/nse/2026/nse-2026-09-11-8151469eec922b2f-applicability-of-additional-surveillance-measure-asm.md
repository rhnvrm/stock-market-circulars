## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to certain securities satisfying criteria for inclusion under Long-Term ASM.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 17, 2026, on all open positions as on September 16, 2026, and new positions created from September 17, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. September 17, 2026.
- Shortlisted securities include Akg Exim Limited (AKG), Mangalam Drugs And Organics Limited (MANGALAM), Medi Caps Limited (MEDICAPQ) under Stage I, Kabra Extrusion Technik Limited (KABRAEXTRU) and TAKE Limited (TAKE) moving from Stage I to Stage II, and Polysil Irrigation Systems Limited (POLYSIL) moving from Stage II to Stage III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) provisions including margin requirements and segment shifting for specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note the surveillance actions and ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and segment shifts where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions / Margin / Segment Shift: September 17, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 16, 2026
- Framework Effective From Date (Annexure): September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to strict surveillance measures, 100% margins, and potential segment shifts, impacting liquidity and trading behavior for the concerned scrips.
