## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has issued a circular detailing multiple settlements scheduled for the Capital Market segment on August 27, 2026. This schedule is necessitated by a settlement holiday on August 26, 2026. The circular details specific timelines for custodial confirmations, pay-in/pay-out, revised auction cycles, and unpaid Margin Trading Facility (MTF) file uploads to ensure smooth clearing and settlement operations.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Holiday**: Due to a settlement holiday on August 26, 2026, multiple settlements have been scheduled on August 27, 2026.
- **Settlement Details**: Covers settlements for trade dates August 25, 2026 (Settlements A 2026159 and M/Z/B 2026160) and August 26, 2026 (Settlement M/Z/B 2026161).
- **Revised Auction Cycle**: Auction trading and settlement under the revised cycle are specified. Crucially, there will be no separate auction for settlement 2026161; instead, its shortages will be clubbed and conducted with settlement 2026162.
- **Unpaid MTF Files**: Specific timelines for uploading UNPAID MTF files for T+1 settlements are provided across August 25, 26, and 27, 2026.
- **Early Pay-In Cut-off**: The cut-off for early pay-in of securities on August 27, 2026, is set at 9:00 PM for margin reporting benefits.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Operational Schedule Revisions**: Temporary modification of standard T+1 settlement cycles, establishing compressed intraday timelines on August 27, 2026, to accommodate three settlements sequentially.
- **Auction Consolidation**: Structural revision of the T+1 auction cycle where shortages of settlement 2026161 are carried forward and conducted with the shortages of 2026162 in auction A 2026162.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Pay-in and Pay-out Timings**: Members and custodians must strictly adhere to the designated timing blocks on August 27, 2026:
  - **Settlement A 2026159**: Custodial Confirmation/Pay-in by 08:00 hrs, Pay-out by 10:00 hrs.
  - **Settlement M/Z/B 2026160**: Custodial Confirmation/Pay-in by 10:30 hrs, Pay-out by 15:30 hrs.
  - **Settlement M/Z/B 2026161**: Custodial Confirmation/Pay-in by 16:30 hrs, Pay-out by 21:00 hrs.
- **UNPAID MTF File Upload**:
  - For **M, Z 2026160**: Upload window on August 25 and August 26 (15:30 to 22:00 hrs) and August 27 (06:30 to 10:00 hrs).
  - For **M, Z 2026161**: Upload window on August 27 (06:30 to 13:00 hrs and 15:30 to 22:00 hrs).
- **Early Pay-In (EPI)**: Members must complete early pay-in of securities in the depository system before 9:00 PM on August 27, 2026, to ensure they are considered for margin reporting as per exchange circular NSE/INSP/57112.

## Important Dates

- **August 11, 2026**: Circular publication date.
- **August 25, 2026**: Trade date for settlements A 2026159 and M/Z/B 2026160.
- **August 26, 2026**: Settlement holiday and trade date for settlement M/Z/B 2026161.
- **August 27, 2026**: Date of multiple settlements (A 2026159, M/Z/B 2026160, and M/Z/B 2026161), auction trade date for A 2026160, and the early pay-in cut-off.
- **August 28, 2026**: Settlement date for Auction A 2026160.
- **August 31, 2026**: Settlement date for Auction A 2026162.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Load**: High impact on back-office, compliance, and treasury desks of members and custodians due to three distinct settlement cycles executing on a single day.
- **System Congestion**: Potential for high transaction density during the narrow windows of pay-in and pay-out on August 27, 2026.
- **Margin Risks**: Failure to execute early pay-in before the 9:00 PM cut-off may lead to margin shortfalls or penalties for the affected trade days.
