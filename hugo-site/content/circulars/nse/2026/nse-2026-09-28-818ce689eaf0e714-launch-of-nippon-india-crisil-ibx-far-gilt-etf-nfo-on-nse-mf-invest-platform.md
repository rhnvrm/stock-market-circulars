## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of the Nippon India Crisil-IBX Far Gilt ETF New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Members can transact in the schemes from September 29, 2026, to October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Names: Nippon India Crisil-IBX Far Gilt ETF Growth Plan and Direct Plan
- ISIN: INF204KC1HS1
- Category: Debt
- NFO Open Date: September 29, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 12, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine product launch notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 13, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening: September 29, 2026
- NFO Closing: October 12, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 13, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to subscribe to the gilt ETF via the NSE MF platform under standard operational procedures.
