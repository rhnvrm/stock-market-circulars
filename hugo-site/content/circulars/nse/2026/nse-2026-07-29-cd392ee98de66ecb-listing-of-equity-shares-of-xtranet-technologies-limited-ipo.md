## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of the equity shares of Xtranet Technologies Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The equity shares will be listed under the symbol XTRANET with ISIN INE0NG701011. The exact date of listing will be communicated via a separate circular.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Xtranet Technologies Limited
- **Symbol:** XTRANET
- **ISIN:** INE0NG701011
- **Listing Type:** Initial Public Offering (IPO)
- The official date of listing will be announced through a subsequent circular.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no direct regulatory changes introduced in this circular; it is a standard listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no direct action items or compliance requirements for existing trading members other than updating systems with the new symbol and ISIN once the trading date is announced.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Listing Date:** To be announced in a separate circular.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a standard corporate action notifying the forthcoming listing of a new equity security on the Exchange.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Trading members will need to register the symbol `XTRANET` and ISIN `INE0NG701011` in their systems prior to the official listing date once announced.
