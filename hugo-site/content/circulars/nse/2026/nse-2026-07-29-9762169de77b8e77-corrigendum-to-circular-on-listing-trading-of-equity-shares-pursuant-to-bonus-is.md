## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a corrigendum (Circular Ref. No: 1247/2026) regarding the listing and trading of equity shares issued pursuant to the bonus issue of Hardwyn India Limited. This circular references the earlier Exchange Circular No. NSE/CML/75372 dated July 23, 2026, and updates the total number of securities and the distinctive number range for the listing.

## Key Points

- **Updated Securities Details**: The number of bonus equity shares and their corresponding distinctive number range have been revised for Hardwyn India Limited.
- **Reference Circular**: This circular modifies the details previously notified under Exchange Circular No. NSE/CML/75372 dated July 23, 2026.
- **Revised Details**:
  - **Symbol**: HARDWYN
  - **Company Name**: Hardwyn India Limited
  - **ISIN**: INE626Z01029
  - **Record Date**: July 28, 2026
  - **Deemed Date of Allotment**: July 29, 2026
  - **Revised No. of Securities**: 19,53,61,440
  - **Revised Distinctive Number Range**: 488434055 to 683795494

## Regulatory Changes

There are no broader regulatory changes introduced by this circular. It is a specific administrative corrigendum to correct listing details of a corporate action (bonus issue) for a single listed entity.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members & Market Participants**: Must update their systems and back-office records to reflect the revised number of securities and distinctive number range for HARDWYN to ensure seamless trading, settlement, and reconciliation.
- **Depositories & Registrars**: Ensure that the credited bonus shares align with the revised distinctive number range (488434055 to 683795494) and total security count (19,53,61,440).

## Important Dates

- **Reference Circular Date**: July 23, 2026 (Circular No: NSE/CML/75372)
- **Record Date for Bonus Issue**: July 28, 2026
- **Deemed Date of Allotment**: July 29, 2026
- **Corrigendum Circular Date**: July 29, 2026 (Effective immediately for listing/trading reference)

## Impact Assessment

- **Company Impact**: Affects Hardwyn India Limited (HARDWYN) by ensuring the accurate recording and listing of its bonus equity shares.
- **Market/Operational Impact**: Extremely low impact on the broader market. The correction is administrative and ensures correct distinctive numbers are mapped to the ISIN INE626Z01029 for trading and settlement systems.
