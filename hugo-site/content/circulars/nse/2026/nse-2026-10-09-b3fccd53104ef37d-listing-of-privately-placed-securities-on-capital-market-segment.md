## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities issued by Piramal Finance Limited on the capital market segment, effective from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Piramal Finance Limited
- **Symbol:** 862PFL29
- **Series:** N0
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Issue Description:** PFL 8.62% 2029
- **Coupon Rate:** 8.62%
- **Date of Maturity:** 22-Nov-2029
- **ISIN Code:** INE202B07KA8

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be identified strictly by their designated security codes and conducted in the specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 09, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** November 22, 2029

## Impact Assessment

Standard listing notification providing market participants with trading identifiers and lot size specifications for the newly admitted debt securities.
