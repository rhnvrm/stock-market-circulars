## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the introduction of futures and options (F&O) contracts on three individual securities: Ather Energy Limited (ATHERENERG), Bank of Maharashtra (MAHABANK), and Sagility Limited (SAGILITY), effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026.
- **Securities and Lot Sizes:**
  - Ather Energy Limited (Symbol: ATHERENERG) - Lot Size: 375
  - Bank of Maharashtra (Symbol: MAHABANK) - Lot Size: 6500
  - Sagility Limited (Symbol: SAGILITY) - Lot Size: 12000
- **Strike Schemes:** Detailed in Annexure A, specifying step values and strike schemes for near, mid, and far months under narrow and wide ranges.
- **Tick Size:** Futures contracts shall have the same tick size as applicable in the CM Segment. Options tick size follows circular NSE/FAOP/70382 dated September 23, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- Expansion of the equity derivatives segment by adding F&O contracts on Ather Energy Limited, Bank of Maharashtra, and Sagility Limited in continuation of prior circular NSE/FAOP/75371.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must load the updated `contract.gz` and `NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz` files from the Extranet server (`faoftp/faocommon`) or website before trading on the effective date.
- Ensure appropriate quantity freeze settings and strike schemes are applied as per the contract files.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 25, 2026
- **Effective/Trading Date:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands trading opportunities and hedging instruments for market participants in the newly added securities.
- Requires back-office and trading terminal updates by members prior to market hours on August 26, 2026.
