## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the revised list of approved securities, ETFs, Sovereign Gold Bonds, GOI Securities/T-Bills, open-ended mutual funds, and empaneled banks for the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) and non-cash component of liquid assets.

## Key Points

- Updates lists across 6 annexures covering equity shares, ETFs, cash and non-cash components, Sovereign Gold Bonds, G-Secs/T-Bills, open-ended mutual funds, and empaneled banks.
- G-Sec/T Bills have a shut period of two days; members must release them at least two days before maturity.
- Bank members must ensure G-Sec provided as collaterals are not reckoned for SLR purpose or used for trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revised haircut rates, market-wide limits, and member-specific limits are made applicable for the approved securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and custodians must take note of the updated limits and haircut structures.
- Release G-Sec/T Bills at least two days before maturity considering the two-day shut period.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 2026
- Circular Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects collateral valuation, margining limits, and permissible securities for participants in the SLBS segment and liquid asset components.
