## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long-Term ASM framework. Specific securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion and will attract applicable surveillance actions starting September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 04, 2026, on all open positions as on September 03, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisted securities include Creative Newtech Limited (CNL) and SecMark Consultancy Limited (SECMARK) under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I.
- Shortlisting of securities is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities included in the Long-Term ASM framework are subject to heightened margin requirements (100%) and potential shifts to the Trade-for-Trade segment depending on the stage criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (100%) on open and new positions for the shortlisted scrips from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 01, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions: September 04, 2026
- Open Positions Evaluation Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities under the ASM framework may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
