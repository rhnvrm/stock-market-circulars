## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular (Ref. No: 606/2026, Download Ref No: NSE/SURV/75645) on August 07, 2026, regarding the periodic review and relaxation of surveillance actions under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. In coordination with other exchanges, a review of securities under GSM has been carried out, resulting in the relaxation of surveillance stages for five eligible securities. These changes are scheduled to become effective on August 10, 2026. The circular refers to previous guidelines and updates on GSM, including GSM for the SME segment, PSU companies, and exemptions.

## Key Points

- **Surveillance Relaxation**: Five securities have been identified for downward revision of their Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages.
- **Full Removal from GSM**: Two securities (GLOBALE TESSILE LIMITED and MT EDUCARE LIMITED) are moving from GSM Stage I to Stage 0, effectively removing them from GSM restrictions.
- **Series Upgrade**: BLUE CHIP INDIA LIMITED is moving from GSM Stage II to Stage I, with a series change from BE (Book Entry/Trade-for-Trade) to EQ (normal equity segment), which will significantly improve trading liquidity.
- **Incremental Relaxation**: GRAND FOUNDRY LIMITED and UNIVA FOODS LIMITED are moving from GSM Stage IV to Stage III.
- **Reference Framework**: The actions are based on the periodic review of GSM provisions established under various previous circulars from 2017 through 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory frameworks are introduced. This action represents the operational execution of existing Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) provisions, specifically the periodic review mechanism to assess the downward movement (relaxation) of securities based on surveillance criteria.

The specific changes for the eligible securities are:

1. **GLOBALE (GLOBALE TESSILE LIMITED)**: GSM Stage I -> Stage 0 (Series BE)
2. **MTEDUCARE (MT EDUCARE LIMITED)**: GSM Stage I -> Stage 0 (Series BE)
3. **BLUECHIP (BLUE CHIP INDIA LIMITED)**: GSM Stage II -> Stage I (Series changes from BE to EQ)
4. **GFSTEELS (GRAND FOUNDRY LIMITED)**: GSM Stage IV -> Stage III (Series BE)
5. **UNIVAFOODS (UNIVA FOODS LIMITED)**: GSM Stage IV -> Stage III (Series BE)

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members/Brokers**: Must update their trading and risk management systems to reflect the revised GSM stages and series changes for the specified securities before the market opens on the effective date.
- **Margin & Settlement Processing**: Ensure that appropriate margin requirements and trading limits are applied in accordance with the relaxed GSM stages. In particular, trading systems must handle the transition of BLUECHIP from BE series (trade-for-trade, 100% margin/delivery) to EQ series (normal trading segment).
- **Client Advisories**: Members may inform clients holding these securities about the relaxation of trading restrictions and improved liquidity conditions.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 07, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 10, 2026 (for the relaxation of surveillance actions and series changes)

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity Enhancement**: The movement of securities to lower GSM stages reduces trading restrictions, transaction costs (such as Additional Surveillance Deposit requirements), and price bands, which generally enhances trading volumes and liquidity.
- **Major Beneficiary**: BLUE CHIP INDIA LIMITED (BLUECHIP) is the primary beneficiary of this review. The transition from GSM Stage II to Stage I and the corresponding series change from BE to EQ allows the stock to be traded in the normal rolling segment rather than on a compulsory trade-for-trade basis. This will significantly increase its market liquidity and ease of execution.
- **Full Freedom**: GLOBALE and MTEDUCARE are completely out of active GSM stages (Stage I to 0), allowing them to operate under standard market surveillance conditions.
- **Risk Mitigation**: The relaxation demonstrates that the joint-exchange surveillance mechanism dynamically adjusts restrictions based on the evolving risk and volatility profile of listed securities, protecting market integrity while avoiding prolonged, unnecessary constraints on trading.
