## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Annu Projects Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) pursuant to SEBI guidelines and previous exchange circulars.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Annu Projects Limited
- Symbol: ANNU
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026
- Reference to previous circular: NSE/CML/76115 dated September 01, 2026, and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Movement of scrip from compulsory trade-for-trade settlement to normal rolling settlement as per standard IPO listing guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure systems are updated to reflect the change in series for ANNU from BE to EQ effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the equity shares of Annu Projects Limited will move to the regular rolling segment, facilitating standard intraday and delivery-based trading without the previous trade-for-trade restrictions.
