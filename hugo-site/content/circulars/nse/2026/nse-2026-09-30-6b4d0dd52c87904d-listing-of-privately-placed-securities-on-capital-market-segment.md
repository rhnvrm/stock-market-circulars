## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and commencement of trading for privately placed securities on the capital market segment, effective October 01, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 01, 2026.
- **Issuing Companies:** NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited and Bajaj Auto Credit Limited.
- **Trading Identification:** Securities will be identified solely by their designated codes and traded in specified market lot sizes (lot size of 1 for all listed securities).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; notification of admission to dealings pursuant to existing trading regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure trading in the specified securities uses the designated security codes and correct lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Maturity Dates:** 
  - NIIF 8.18% 2029: November 22, 2029
  - BACL 8.25% 2032: September 29, 2032
  - NIIF 8.19% 2032 Sr 5: February 19, 2032

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update providing secondary market liquidity and trading availability for the newly listed privately placed debt instruments.
