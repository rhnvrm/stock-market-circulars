## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers and debt securities) on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers fresh issues of commercial papers and other debt securities from various issuing companies.
- Issuers include Tata Capital Limited, Barclays Investments & Loans (India) Private Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, NABARD, HDB Financial Services Ltd, The India Cements Limited, Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited, and Godrej Housing Finance Limited.
- Effective date for trading and dealings: September 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and trading members must ensure adherence to standard settlement and depository guidelines for the listed commercial papers and debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment dates: September 25, 2026.
- Effective Listing Date: September 28, 2026.
- Redemption dates range across various maturities from October 2026 to September 2027.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational listing providing secondary market liquidity for privately placed debt instruments.
