## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the deferral of the go-live date for pre-trade risk controls concerning pre-emptive order cancellation on account of NNF ID-Algo ID validations in the Currency Derivatives Segment.

## Key Points

- The go-live date has been revised from October 12, 2026, to October 19, 2026.
- This circular follows previous circulars NSE/CD/76749 and NSE/CD/76824.
- All other provisions mentioned in the aforesaid circulars remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of the implementation timeline for pre-emptive order cancellation mechanisms based on NNF ID and Algo ID validations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should take note of the revised timeline and ensure system readiness and alignment with the new go-live date of October 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Revised Go-Live Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides trading members with an additional week to test and implement required risk control and validation systems without immediate disruption.
