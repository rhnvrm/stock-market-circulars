## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities for three issuing entities effective October 26, 2026, pursuant to their redemption.

## Key Points

- Companies involved: Housing & Urban Development Corporation Limited, Indian Railway Finance Corporation Limited, and The Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited.
- Effective Suspension Date: October 26, 2026.
- Reason: Redemption of securities (ISINs: INE031A09FD3, INE053F09HU6, INE017A08268).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure no further trades are executed in the suspended ISINs as of the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard administrative suspension following maturity and redemption of debt securities with no broader market impact.
