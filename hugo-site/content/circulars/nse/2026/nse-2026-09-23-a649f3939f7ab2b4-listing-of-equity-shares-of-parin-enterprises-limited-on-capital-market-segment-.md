## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the migration of equity shares of Parin Enterprises Limited (formerly known as Parin Furniture Limited) from the SME Emerge platform to the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) effective from September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Parin Enterprises Limited (Formerly known as Parin Furniture Limited)
- Symbol: PARIN
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE00U801010
- Face Value and Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Number of Securities: 11,118,000
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 11118000
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date of Migration: September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in equity shares of the company is moved from SME Platform (EMERGE) to the Capital Market Segment (Main Board).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are informed of the security codes, ISIN, and market lot details for dealings on the Main Board.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates mainstream trading and enhanced visibility for Parin Enterprises Limited on the NSE Capital Market Segment.
