## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Ducon Infratechnologies Limited. The REs will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment starting September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: DUCON-RE1
- Name of the Company: Ducon Infratechnologies Limited
- ISIN: INE741L20034
- Total Number of Rights Entitlements: 249,904,968
- Trading Period for Renunciation: From September 03, 2026 to September 07, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 08, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Segment: Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading of Rights Entitlements pursuant to the Rights Issue admitted under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs, mentioning the ISIN and clarifying that they should not be mistaken for ordinary shares. Members must include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes/bills stating that purchase of REs only gives the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before the closing date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 03, 2026
- Trading Period for Renunciation: September 03, 2026 to September 07, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding or trading REs must apply with requisite application money or renounce them on or before the Issue Closing Date, failing which the REs will lapse and be extinguished.
