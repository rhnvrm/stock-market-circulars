## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedule for the Commodity Derivatives segment for the month of November 2026 via Circular Ref. No: 0357/2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/COM/76737). The schedule outlines the corresponding settlement dates for each trade date during November 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers all trading days in November 2026 for the commodity derivatives segment.
- Specifies exact trade dates matching their respective settlement dates.
- First trade date of the month is 02-Nov-2026 (settling on 03-Nov-2026).
- Last trade date of the month is 30-Nov-2026 (settling on 01-Dec-2026).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational circular detailing the monthly settlement calendar.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and clearing members must align their clearing and settlement operations in accordance with the specified schedule for November 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 06, 2026
- **Schedule Period:** November 02, 2026 to November 30, 2026
- **First Settlement Date:** November 03, 2026
- **Final Settlement Date in Schedule:** December 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low impact. Ensures smooth operational planning and clearing activities for trading members in the commodity derivatives segment.
