## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of Coreintegra Consulting Services Limited (SME IPO) from the trade-for-trade segment to the rolling segment.

## Key Points

- Company: Coreintegra Consulting Services Limited
- Symbol: COREIN
- Transfer from trade for trade segment (Series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM)

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012 and exchange circular NSE/CML/76623.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants to update trading parameters for COREIN as per rolling segment rules.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement and enhances liquidity for the security.
