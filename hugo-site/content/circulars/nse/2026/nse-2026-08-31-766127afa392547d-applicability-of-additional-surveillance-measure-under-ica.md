## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies as per the Inter Creditor Agreement (ICA). Effective September 01, 2026, certain securities have been excluded from the ASM ICA framework based on the specified exclusion criteria, while no new securities were added.

## Key Points

- Framework refers to ASM for Companies as per Inter Creditor Agreement (ICA).
- Effective Date: September 01, 2026.
- No securities were shortlisted or shifted between stages under ASM ICA.
- Sadbhav Engineering Limited (Symbol: SADBHAV, ISIN: INE226H01026) has satisfied the exclusion criteria and is removed from the framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities satisfying exclusion criteria under the ASM ICA framework are removed from surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the exclusion of securities from the ASM ICA list effective September 01, 2026.
- ASM ICA measures operate in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The exclusion of Sadbhav Engineering Limited from ASM ICA removes specific surveillance restrictions associated with the framework for this security, though other prevailing measures may still apply.
