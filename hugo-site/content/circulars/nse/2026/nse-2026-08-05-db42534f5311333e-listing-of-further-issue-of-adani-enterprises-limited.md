## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the listing of further equity shares of Adani Enterprises Limited. A total of 15,100 equity shares, which have been converted from partly paid-up to fully paid-up shares, will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment of the Exchange with effect from August 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Adani Enterprises Limited (Symbol: ADANIENT)
- **Quantity:** 15,100 equity shares
- **ISIN:** INE423A01024 (Temporary ISIN: IN8423A01015)
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Re. 1/- each
- **Security Description:** Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares
- **Market Lot:** 1 share
- **Pari Passu:** Yes, these shares rank pari passu with existing shares.
- **Lock-in Details:** Not Applicable
- **Distinctive Numbers:**
  - From 1212447619 to 1212447635 (17 shares)
  - From 1347245380 to 1347260379 (15,000 shares)
  - From 1408675761 to 1408675843 (83 shares)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes are introduced. This listing is processed under existing regulations, specifically Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- No action is required from general market participants or members.
- Equity shares allotted in dematerialized mode are initially credited under the temporary ISIN `IN8423A01015` in accordance with SEBI circulars regarding the activation of ISINs for additional issues of shares.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 05, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. The addition of 15,100 shares represents an extremely minor increase in the company's total outstanding equity capital and is expected to have no noticeable impact on market dynamics or share price.
- **Operational Impact:** Listing and trading operations for these shares will proceed under the designated codes and ISIN on the exchange starting August 06, 2026.
