## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Fusion CX Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO). Trading in the securities will commence on October 22, 2026, under the symbol FUSIONCX.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Fusion CX Limited
- Symbol: FUSIONCX
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE0N2201019
- Issue Price: Rs. 289/- per share
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Re. 1/-
- Total No. of Securities: 144,360,333 equity shares
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admission to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category of scrips as per SEBI circular no. CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012. Lock-in details as specified in Annexure I of the circular apply.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective / Listing Date: October 22, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: Various dates including November 17, 2026, January 16, 2027, April 21, 2027, and April 21, 2028 as per Annexure I.

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Fusion CX Limited equity shares on the NSE capital market segment, providing liquidity to shareholders and expanding market participation.
