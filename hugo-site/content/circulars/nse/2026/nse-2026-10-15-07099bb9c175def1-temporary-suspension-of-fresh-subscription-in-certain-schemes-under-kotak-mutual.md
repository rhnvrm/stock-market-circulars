## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and fresh registrations for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) in specific schemes under Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes: Kotak Medium Term Fund and Kotak Credit Risk Fund.
- Restricted Transactions: Fresh/additional lumpsum, switch-ins, and fresh SIP/STP registrations.
- Unaffected Transactions: Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, STP-outs, and existing SIP/STP instalments.
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational restriction implemented as per communication received from Kotak Mahindra Asset Management Company Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors must update their systems and cease processing fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and new SIP/STP registrations for the specified Kotak Mutual Fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding existing SIPs/STPs can continue without interruption, but new investors or fresh capital allocations into Kotak Medium Term Fund and Kotak Credit Risk Fund via the NSE MF platform are temporarily halted.
