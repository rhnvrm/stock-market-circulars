## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission to dealings of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Viceroy Hotels Limited (Symbol: VHLTD-RE1, ISIN: INE048C20025). Trading will commence on September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Viceroy Hotels Limited
- **Symbol:** VHLTD-RE1
- **Display Name:** Viceroy Hotels Ltd-RE
- **ISIN:** INE048C20025
- **Series:** BE
- **Number of Rights Entitlements:** 9,203,008
- **Trading Period for Renunciation:** From September 03, 2026 to September 07, 2026
- **Date of Suspension:** September 08, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Segment:** Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Notification issued pursuant to standard National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Trading members must include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes/bills informing clients that purchasing REs only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before the issue closes.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs on or before the Issue Closing Date shall lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date / Commencement of Trading:** September 03, 2026
- **Trading Period End / Renunciation Closure:** September 07, 2026
- **Date of Suspension:** September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and investors to trade or renounce Rights Entitlements of Viceroy Hotels Limited within the specified trading window under the trade-to-trade segment.
