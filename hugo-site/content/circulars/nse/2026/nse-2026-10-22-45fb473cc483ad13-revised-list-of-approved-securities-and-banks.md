## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the revised list of approved securities, GOI securities, open-ended mutual funds, corporate bonds, and empaneled banks effective for the month of November 2026. This circular supersedes the previous circular dated September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers updated lists of eligible equity shares, ETFs, Sovereign Gold Bonds, G-Secs, T-Bills, mutual funds, and corporate bonds for collateral purposes.
- Specifies market-wide limits, member-specific limits, and haircut rates across all annexures.
- G-Sec and T-Bills carry a shut period of two days; members must release them at least two days before maturity.
- Bank members must ensure G-Secs provided as collateral are not reckoned for SLR purposes or used for trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated eligible collateral securities and corresponding haircut rates applicable for the Futures and Options segment effective November 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and custodians must review the updated lists across Annexures 1 through 7.
- Ensure G-Sec/T-Bills are released at least two days before maturity due to the shut period.
- Bank members must refrain from counting collateral G-Secs towards SLR or trading.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** November 2026
- **Circular Date:** October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Clearing members and custodians must adjust their collateral management systems and margin calculations in accordance with the revised approved securities, limits, and haircut percentages.
