## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the launch of the Zerodha Arbitrage Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) of Zerodha Mutual Fund on its NSE MF Invest Platform. The scheme will be available for transaction from August 12, 2026, to August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name**: Zerodha Arbitrage Fund Direct Growth
- **ISIN**: INF0R8F01232
- **Category**: Equity
- **Platform Availability**: Available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 12, 2026, to August 14, 2026.
- **Allotment Date**: August 18, 2026
- **Payment Cut-off**: Clear funds must be received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 18, 2026, till 11:59 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced by this circular. It is a standard operational announcement regarding platform product availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Members participating in the transaction of this NFO must:
- Ensure transaction requests are made within the designated window (August 12, 2026, to August 14, 2026).
- Ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 18, 2026, by 11:59 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date**: August 12, 2026
- **NFO End Date**: August 14, 2026
- **Allotment Date**: August 18, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline**: August 18, 2026, by 11:59 pm

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. This is a routine product enablement on the exchange's mutual fund platform.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Trading members transacting on the NSE MF platform must ensure timely fund transfers to meet the clear funds deadline on the allotment date.
