## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced revised final cut-off timings for Early Pay-in (EPI) of funds and securities, as well as the download time for margin reports, applicable for October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- EPI of Funds final cut-off time revised from 07:45 PM to 07:00 PM.
- EPI of Securities final cut-off time revised from 09:00 PM to 07:00 PM.
- EPI of Securities for clients clearing directly with the UPI block facility revised from 08:00 PM to 06:00 PM.
- Download of margin report revised from 10:30 PM to 09:00 PM.
- NSE Clearing shall endeavour to release Cash/FDR/BG within one hour of request on October 16, 2026, subject to collateral availability post margin checks.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of operational settlement timelines and early pay-in cut-off hours in reference to SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DOP/P/CIR/2022/10 dated July 27, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- All Members, Custodians, and PCMs must align their operational workflows to meet the earlier cut-off timings for funds and securities pay-in on October 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 09, 2026
- Applicable Date / Revised Timelines: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Clearing and settlement operations require tightened coordination to ensure pay-ins are completed ahead of the advanced cut-off deadlines.
