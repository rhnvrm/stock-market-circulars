## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited is conducting a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives Segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026. There is no new version release associated with this session.

## Key Points

- Trading Session-1 from Primary Site: Normal Market open at 11:00 hrs and close at 12:30 hrs.
- Trading Session-2 from DR Site: Normal Market open at 13:15 hrs and close at 14:30 hrs; Trade Modification end time at 14:40 hrs.
- Live Re-login Timings: Start at 16:00 hrs and close at 16:30 hrs.
- Reminder for NNF members regarding the discontinuation of the existing encryption mechanism with the old interactive port (10885) effective December 07, 2026.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session. Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; reiterates compliance with prior circulars regarding encryption mechanism migration and UCC/PAN validation.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must manually clear orders in NNF software, ensure valid UCC/PAN compliance, and migrate to recommended new encryption mechanisms ahead of the December 07, 2026 deadline.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 24, 2026
- Encryption Mechanism Migration Deadline: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low impact; routine operational mock session designed to test system connectivity and recovery switchover.
