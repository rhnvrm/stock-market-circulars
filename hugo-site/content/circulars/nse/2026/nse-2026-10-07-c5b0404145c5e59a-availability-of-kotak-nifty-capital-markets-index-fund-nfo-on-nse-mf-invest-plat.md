## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the Kotak Nifty Capital Markets Index Fund of Kotak Mutual Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** Kotak Mutual Fund
- **Scheme:** Kotak Nifty Capital Markets Index Fund
- **Effective Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **Facilities Available:** Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, SWP
- **Eligible ISINs & Plans:**
  - Regular Plan - Growth: INF174KA1G31
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF174KA1G56
  - Direct Plan - Growth: INF174KA1G07
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF174KA1G23
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout: INF174KA1G49
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout: INF174KA1G15

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational notification for mutual fund scheme availability on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure systems are updated to support transactions in the newly available Kotak Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 7, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment choices for market participants using the NSE MF Invest Platform by enabling seamless access to the Kotak Nifty Capital Markets Index Fund across multiple plan variants.
