## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the merger of Motilal Oswal Asset Allocation Passive Fund of Fund - Conservative (Merging Scheme) into Motilal Oswal Multi Asset Passive Fund of Funds (Surviving Scheme) under Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund, effective after business hours on November 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Merging Scheme: Motilal Oswal Asset Allocation Passive Fund of Fund - Conservative.
- Surviving Scheme: Motilal Oswal Multi Asset Passive Fund of Funds.
- Effective Date of Merger: After close of business hours on November 13, 2026.
- Exit Load-free Redemption/Switch Window: Investors not in favour of the merger can redeem or switch to other schemes without exit load from October 09, 2026 up to 3:00 PM on November 09, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP/SWP handling: Existing SIP/XSIP/SWP will continue during the exit load period and will shift to surviving schemes after business hours on November 13, 2026.
- Inflows & Outflows Discontinuation: Lump sum mode (including switches) and fresh registrations of SIP, STP, and SWP in the merging scheme will be discontinued effective November 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Merger executed in accordance with Regulation 22(9)(c) of SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, para 3.2 of SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026, and noted by SEBI on September 24, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must take note of the merger timeline, exit load-free window, and discontinuation of fresh inflows/outflows in the merging scheme.

## Important Dates

- Exit Load Period Start Date: October 09, 2026
- Exit Load Period End Date & Time: November 09, 2026 up to 3:00 PM
- Discontinuation of Inflows/Outflows (Lump sum, fresh SIP/STP/SWP): November 10, 2026
- Effective Date of Merger: Close of business hours on November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors in the merging scheme have an exit load-free window to exit or switch. Existing systematic instructions will automatically transition to the surviving scheme post-merger.
