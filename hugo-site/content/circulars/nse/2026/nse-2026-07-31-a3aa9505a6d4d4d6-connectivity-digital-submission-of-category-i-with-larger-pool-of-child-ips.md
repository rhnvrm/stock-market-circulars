## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the introduction of a new Lease Line Connectivity category, Category "I", which offers a larger pool of child IPs. This option is designed for members who want to access only the Commodity Derivatives Segment via a dedicated leased line. This offering will be available starting August 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Segment Restriction:** Category "I" connectivity is limited strictly to accessing the Commodity Derivatives Segment.
- **Widespread Availability:** Available to members across all Points of Presence (POPs) in both Managed Service and Self-Service models.
- **IP Allocation:** Members receive 1 Parent IP initially and can obtain up to 119 additional Child IPs at no extra cost.
- **Message Capacity:** Members are allotted 120 messages per second per IP subscribed on the Category "I" link.
- **Annual Charges:** Recovery charges of Rs 2,000 + applicable taxes apply at the scenario level for each parent IP.
- **Conversion Limit:** Leased lines under Category "I" cannot be converted to other categories, and other categories cannot be converted to Category "I".

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of Lease Line Category "I" for the Commodity Derivatives Segment with a larger pool of child IPs, updating the connectivity options defined in circular NSE/MSD/73957.

## Compliance Requirements

- **No Mandatory Action:** There is no mandatory compliance requirement for general members.
- **Optional Registration:** Members wanting to opt for Category "I" or request additional child IPs can submit requests via the ENIT portal:
  - **Category "I" Application:** ENIT > Membership > TCP IP scenario > Activation
  - **Child IP Application:** ENIT > Membership > TCP IP scenario > Activation (Type – Child)

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 3, 2026, Beginning of Day (BOD)

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Allows interested participants to consolidate existing connectivity and network architecture, saving troubleshooting time and administrative effort.
- **Financial Impact:** Lowers connectivity-related charges since additional child IPs are provided without additional cost.
