## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Adroit Industries (India) Limited following its IPO, along with the submission of its shareholding pattern under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Adroit Industries India Limited
- Security: Equity shares post-issue dated 29.09.2026
- Shareholding pattern filed under Regulation 31(1)(a)
- Company reports locked-in shares and significant beneficial owners

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with listing and disclosure norms under SEBI ICDR and LODR regulations for post-IPO equity share listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to ensure ongoing compliance with shareholding pattern disclosures and lock-in requirements as applicable.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Post Issue Date: 29.09.2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates trading of equity shares on the exchange post-IPO and ensures transparency in shareholding structure.
