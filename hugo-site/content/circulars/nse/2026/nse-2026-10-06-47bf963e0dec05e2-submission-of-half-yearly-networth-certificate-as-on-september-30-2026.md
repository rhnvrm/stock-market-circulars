## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the submission of half-yearly networth certificates as on September 30, 2026, for clearing members of NCL. Members are mandated to maintain net worth in accordance with SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 2026, and applicable NCL guidelines.

## Key Points

- Clearing members of NCL must maintain continuing membership net worth norms at all times.
- Networth requirement is defined as either Base Net worth or Variable Net worth, whichever is higher.
- Base Net worth thresholds apply differently across Self-Clearing Members (SCM), Clearing Members (CM), and Professional Clearing Members (PCM), including specific limits for currency derivative segments.
- Networth certificates must be prepared using Format C-1 and derived from audited or unaudited standalone financial statements.
- Separate Business Units operating in GIFT-IFSC must segregate their networth as per SEBI circular dated May 02, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adoption of updated computation methodologies under Chapter X of SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 2026.
- Enhanced certification requirements requiring declarations on conflict of interest, partner PAN disclosures, and UDIN.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members, Clearing Members, and Professional Clearing Members must submit the verified networth certificate as on September 30, 2026.
- Certification must be performed by independent Chartered Accountants or Company Secretaries with valid firm registration and membership numbers.

## Important Dates

- Financial reference date for networth computation: September 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Strengthens risk management and capital adequacy oversight for clearing corporations and stock exchanges by ensuring transparent financial health reporting.
