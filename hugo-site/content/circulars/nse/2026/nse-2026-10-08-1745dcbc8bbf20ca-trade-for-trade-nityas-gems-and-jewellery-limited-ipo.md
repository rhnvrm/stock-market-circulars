## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the transfer of securities of Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited from the trade-for-trade segment (Series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) with effect from October 23, 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited
- Symbol: NITYAS
- Transfer from: Trade for trade segment (Series: BE)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012 and further to Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76782 dated October 07, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to note the segment change for Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited from Series BE to Series EQ effective October 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for equity shares of Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited, facilitating regular trading and improved market liquidity.
