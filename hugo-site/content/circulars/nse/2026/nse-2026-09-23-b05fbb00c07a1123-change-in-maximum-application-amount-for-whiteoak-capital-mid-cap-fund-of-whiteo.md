## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the revision in the maximum application/subscription amount for Whiteoak Capital Mid Cap Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- The limit for acceptance of lumpsum (fresh/additional) subscriptions and switch-ins has been revised from Rs. 1 lakh to Rs. 2 lakh per day per PAN.
- The limit applies cumulatively across all modes of purchase/switch-in per primary holder PAN.
- Applications in the name of a minor and guardian under the same PAN are considered together under the Rs. 2 lakh limit.
- SIP and STP facilities continue without any changes or restrictions.
- Redemptions, switch-outs, and SWP continue as per normal terms.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of maximum lumpsum investment limit for Whiteoak Capital Mid Cap Fund to Rs. 2,00,000 per PAN per day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the revised subscription limits while placing orders on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2026
- Circular Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors wishing to make lumpsum investments in Whiteoak Capital Mid Cap Fund are now capped at Rs. 2 lakh per day per PAN, while SIP/STP investments remain unaffected.
