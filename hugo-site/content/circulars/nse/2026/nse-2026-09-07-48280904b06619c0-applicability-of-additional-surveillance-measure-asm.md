## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Several securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) Framework Stage-I.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 10, 2026, on all open positions as on September 09, 2026, and new positions created from September 10, 2026 onwards.
- Securities shortlisted include Artemis Electricals and Projects Limited (AEPL), Jindal Worldwide Limited (JINDWORLD), Modison Limited (MODISONLTD), Shivalik Bimetal Controls Limited (SBCL), and S V Global Mill Limited (SVGLOBAL).
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) Stage-I for specified securities with stringent margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure 100% margin requirements are maintained for open and new positions in the affected securities as per the specified effective dates.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 07, 2026
- Framework Effective Date: September 08, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: September 10, 2026 (on open positions as on September 09, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements to 100% on affected securities may impact liquidity and leverage for market participants trading in these scrips.
