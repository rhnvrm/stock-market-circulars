## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of the Aditya Birla Sun Life Fixed Maturity Plan-Series VB (136 days) NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window opens on October 22, 2026, and closes on October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Subscription Period: October 22, 2026 to October 26, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- Schemes Offered: Regular and Direct options covering IDCW, Growth, and IDCW Reinvestment variants.
- Allotment Date: October 27, 2026.
- ISINs included: INF209KC1886, INF209KC1860, INF209KC1878, INF209KC1852, INF209KC1837, and INF209KC1845.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are required to ensure that clear funds through authorized payment modes are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 27, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening: October 22, 2026
- NFO Closing: October 26, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: October 27, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables seamless investor subscription to debt fixed maturity plans via the NSE MF Invest platform with standard operational timelines.
