## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Ola Electric Mobility Limited. The REs will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective from October 22, 2026, under the symbol OLAELEC-RE.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ola Electric Mobility Limited
- Symbol: OLAELEC-RE
- ISIN: INE0LXG20016
- Total Number of Rights Entitlements: 369,689,663
- Trading Period for Renunciation: From October 22, 2026 to October 26, 2026
- Date of Suspension: October 27, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Segment: Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Regulatory Changes

Rights Entitlements are admitted to dealings under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, with lot sizes specified under Regulation 2.5.5.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs, highlighting their distinct ISIN (INE0LXG20016) so they are not mistaken for ordinary shares. Members must include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes stating that purchase of REs grants the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce them before the closing date. Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the Issue Closing Date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Trading Commencement: October 22, 2026
- Trading Period for Renunciation Ends: October 26, 2026
- Date of Suspension: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and investors to trade Rights Entitlements of Ola Electric Mobility Limited or renounce them within the specified window, operating under Trade to Trade settlement rules.
