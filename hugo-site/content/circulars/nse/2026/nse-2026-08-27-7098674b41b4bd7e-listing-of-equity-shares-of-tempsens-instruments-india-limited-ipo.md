## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Tempsens Instruments (India) Limited under its Initial Public Offering (IPO). Trading in the securities will commence effective August 28, 2026, under the symbol TEMPSENS.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Tempsens Instruments (India) Limited
- **Symbol:** TEMPSENS
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE1KZI01025
- **Face Value:** Rs. 4/-
- **Paid-up Value:** Rs. 4/-
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 300/-
- **Number of Securities:** 83,832,691
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Effective Date:** August 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Admitted to dealings in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per relevant SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- **Listing/Effective Date:** August 28, 2026
- **Lock-in Details:** Various tranches ranging from September 23, 2026 to February 25, 2028 (with certain shares free).

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of equity shares of Tempsens Instruments (India) Limited on the NSE Capital Market segment.
