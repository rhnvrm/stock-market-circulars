## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued the delivery settlement schedule and GST details confirmation schedule for commodity derivatives contracts expiring in November 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines T+1 delivery settlement schedules for various commodity contracts including Gold (GOLDM, GOLD1G, GOLD), Silver (SILVERM, SILVER100, GOLDGUINEA, GOLD10G), and base metals (Aluminium, Copper, Lead, Nickel, Zinc).
- Specifies timelines for delivery marking, commodity and fund pay-ins/pay-outs, and supplementary settlements.
- Details the schedules for seller and buyer member GST details uploading windows.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine operational schedule release with no underlying regulatory rule changes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must upload GST details and execute pay-ins/pay-outs strictly within the designated operational windows.

## Important Dates

- Expiry and delivery schedules effective across October and November 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures timely and seamless settlement and tax reporting for commodity derivatives market participants.
