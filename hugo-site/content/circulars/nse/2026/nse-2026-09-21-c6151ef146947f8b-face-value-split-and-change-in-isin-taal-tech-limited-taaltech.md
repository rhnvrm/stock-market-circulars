## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the face value split and change in ISIN for Taal Tech Limited (TAALTECH) effective from September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Taal Tech Limited
- Symbol: TAALTECH
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/- per share
- New ISIN: INE524T01029

## Regulatory Changes

- The face value and paid-up value of equity shares are split from Rs. 10 to Rs. 2.
- The ISIN of the company's equity shares is updated to INE524T01029.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are required to note the change in ISIN and face value for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading and clearing systems will reflect the updated ISIN and face value for Taal Tech Limited starting from the effective date.
