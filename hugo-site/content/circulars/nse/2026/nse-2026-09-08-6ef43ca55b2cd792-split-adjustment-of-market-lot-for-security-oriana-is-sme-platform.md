## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the adjustment of the market lot for Oriana Power Limited on account of a corporate stock split (5:1 ratio) on the SME Platform. The adjusted market lot is set to 375, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Oriana Power Limited
- **Symbol:** ORIANA
- **Corporate Action Type:** Split
- **Split Ratio:** 5:1
- **Adjustment Factor:** 5
- **Adjusted Market Lot:** 375

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of market lot following corporate action in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated security.gz / nnf_security.gz / MII - Security File.gz file in the trading application before trading on the effective date.
- Files can be obtained from the directory common/NTNEAT on the Extranet server or via the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- **Ex-date / Effective Date:** September 18, 2026
- **Record Date:** September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading participants must update their trading terminals with the new security files to reflect the revised market lot size of 375 for ORIANA prior to the effective date.
