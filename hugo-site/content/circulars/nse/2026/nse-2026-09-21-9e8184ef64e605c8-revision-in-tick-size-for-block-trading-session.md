## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the revision in tick size for the Block Trading session (BL and TL series) in the Capital Market segment, effective from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised tick size changes are applicable for the Block Trading session (BL and TL series).
- Effective date of the change is October 12, 2026.
- Members are required to update security files in their trading applications before trading on the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

The revised applicable tick sizes for the Block Trading session based on underlying security price ranges are as follows:
- Below 250: 0.01
- ≥ 250 to 1,000: 0.05
- > 1,000 to 5,000: 0.10
- > 5,000 to 10,000: 0.50
- > 10,000 to 20,000: 1.00
- > 20,000: 5.00

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must load the updated security.gz / nnf_security.gz / NSE_CM_security_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz file in their trading application prior to trading on the effective date. These files can be obtained from the directory common/NTNEAT on the Extranet server or via the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants operating in the block trading session need to align their trading systems and applications with the revised tick size structure.
