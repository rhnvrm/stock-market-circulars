## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued circular Ref. No: 565/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/SURV/75431) dated July 28, 2026, regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). The circular specifies securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion in Stage I of the ESM framework, transition of securities to Stage II, exclusions, and provides a consolidated list of securities currently under the ESM framework.

## Key Points

- **Stage I Inclusions**: Three securities (FRESHARA, ORIENTLTD, and PRECISION) have qualified for ESM Stage I. They will attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions starting July 30, 2026.
- **Stage I to Stage II Movement**: One security (FAIRCHEMOR) is shortlisted to move from ESM Stage I to Stage II.
- **Exclusions**: No securities are excluded from the ESM framework in this current update.
- **Trading Segment Shift**: Securities shortlisted under ESM will shift from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).
- **Stage II Trading Measures**: Securities shifting to Stage II will trade in the Trade-for-Trade segment with a restrictive price band of 2% under the Periodic Call Auction framework.
- **Surveillance Scope**: Shortlisting of securities under ESM is a preventive market surveillance measure and should not be construed as adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shortlisted Stage I securities (FRESHARA, ORIENTLTD, PRECISION) will shift from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. July 30, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II (FAIRCHEMOR) will trade under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. July 29, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Collection**: Trading members must implement a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as of July 29, 2026, and new positions created from July 30, 2026, for the shortlisted Stage I securities.
- **Settlement Rules**: Members must ensure transactions in the shortlisted securities adhere to the Trade-for-Trade settlement segment rules and Periodic Call Auction conditions, as applicable.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 28, 2026
- **Stage II Movement & Price Band Restrictions Effective Date**: July 29, 2026
- **100% Margin Requirement and Trade-for-Trade Segment Shift Effective Date**: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading and Liquidity**: Shifting securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) and placing Stage II securities under the Periodic Call Auction (with a 2% price band) will drastically reduce intraday liquidity and trading volumes for these counters.
- **Risk Management**: The 100% margin mandate significantly increases capital requirements for market participants holding or initiating trades in the affected securities, dampening speculative activity.
