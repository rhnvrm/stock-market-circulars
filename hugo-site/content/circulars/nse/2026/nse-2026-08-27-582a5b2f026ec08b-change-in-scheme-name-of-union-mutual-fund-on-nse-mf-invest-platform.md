## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names and categorization updates for several schemes of Union Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, pursuant to SEBI's Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme name changes implemented by Union Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform.
- Changes follow SEBI Master Circular guidelines regarding categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes.
- Existing schemes updated to reflect revised terminology and types.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates in compliance with SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds Chapter 3, Part IV (Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and investors take note of the revised scheme names and scheme types on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Circular Date: August 27, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Informational update for investors and platform participants holding or trading affected Union Mutual Fund schemes.
