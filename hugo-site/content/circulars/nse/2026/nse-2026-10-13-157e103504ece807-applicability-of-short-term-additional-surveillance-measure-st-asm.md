## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and transitions from Stage I to Stage II for specified securities effective October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: Armour Security (India) Limited (ARMOUR), AVRO INDIA LIMITED (AVROIND), BMW Ventures Limited (BMWVENTLTD), BN Agrochem Limited (BNAGROCHEM), Indiabulls Limited (IBULLSLTD), Kabra Jewels Limited (KKJEWELS), Mega Flex Plastics Limited (MEGAFLEX), One Point One Solutions Limited (ONEPOINT), Slone Infosystems Limited (SLONE), Terai Tea Company Limited (TERAI).
- Securities moving from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II: Vinod Texworld Limited (VINOD).
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage I is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher (capped at 100%) w.e.f. October 15, 2026 on open positions as on October 14, 2026 and new positions created from October 15, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage II is 100% or existing margin whichever is higher w.e.f. October 15, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the entities.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for identified volatile securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100% as applicable) on all open positions and new positions created from October 15, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Action / Framework: October 14, 2026 / October 15, 2026 for margins

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the shortlisted securities.
