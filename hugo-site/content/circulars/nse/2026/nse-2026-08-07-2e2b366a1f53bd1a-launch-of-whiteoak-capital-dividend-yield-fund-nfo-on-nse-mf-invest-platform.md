## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the WhiteOak Capital Dividend Yield Fund New Fund Offer (NFO), offered by WhiteOak Mutual Fund, will be open for transaction on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription period for the NFO starts on August 10, 2026, and closes on August 24, 2026, at 11:59 pm. Members must ensure that clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 30, 2026, by 06:00 pm.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** WhiteOak Mutual Fund.
- **Scheme Name:** WhiteOak Capital Dividend Yield Fund.
- **Platform Availability:** NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Transaction Window:** August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026, till 11:59 pm.
- **Fund Settlement Deadline:** Clear funds must be received in the Clearing Corporation account by August 30, 2026, till 06:00 pm.
- **Scheme Details & ISINs:** Six plan options (Regular/Direct, Growth/IDCW/IDCW Payout) are introduced with their respective ISINs and an allotment date of August 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory framework or change is introduced by this circular. It is a standard operational announcement under existing NSE MF Invest guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Members/Distributors:** Ensure that investor orders are placed within the NFO window (August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026).
- **Funding & Settlement:** Ensure that clear funds for the transactions are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 30, 2026, by 06:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 10, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** August 24, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Fund Settlement Deadline:** August 30, 2026 (till 06:00 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. It increases the offering of equity-oriented mutual fund schemes on the exchange's mutual fund platform, providing investors with more diversification choices.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. The process relies on existing transaction and settlement mechanisms on the NSE MF Invest Platform. No additional infrastructure or integration is required for members already active on the platform.
