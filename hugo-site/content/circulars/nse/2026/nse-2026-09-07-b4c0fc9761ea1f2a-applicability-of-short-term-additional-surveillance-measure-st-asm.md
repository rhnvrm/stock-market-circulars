## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Certain securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I effective September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I are subject to enhanced surveillance measures.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. September 09, 2026 on all open positions as on September 08, 2026 and new positions created from September 09, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I framework provisions for the identified list of securities in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (50% or existing, whichever is higher, capped at 100%) for open positions and new positions created in the shortlisted securities starting September 09, 2026 based on positions as of September 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Shortlisting: September 08, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Provisions: September 09, 2026 (on open positions as on September 08, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations for market participants trading in the shortlisted securities (ABSMARINE, BIRLACABLE, BLUEPEBBLE, E2ERAIL, HAPPY, MAL, MUKKA, RAMBHAJO, SKYTECH, VISAMAN, WELENT), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in these scrips.
