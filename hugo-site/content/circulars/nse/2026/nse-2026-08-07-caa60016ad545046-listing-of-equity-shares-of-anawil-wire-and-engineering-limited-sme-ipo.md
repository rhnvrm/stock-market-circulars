## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Anawil Wire and Engineering Limited on its SME Emerge platform. This follows the successful completion of the company's SME Initial Public Offering (IPO). The circular provides details regarding the shareholding pattern of the listed entity as of the quarter ending August 7, 2026, as required under Regulation 31(1)(b) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- **Listing of Shares:** Equity shares of Anawil Wire and Engineering Limited (Scrip Name/Ticker: ANAWI) are listed on the NSE SME platform.
- **Shareholding Pattern:** The shareholding pattern of the specified securities has been filed under Regulation 31(1)(b), with the reporting period ending August 7, 2026.
- **Promoter Shareholding:** The company has confirmed that none of the promoter holdings are encumbered under "Pledged" or "Non-Disposal Undertaking".
- **Locked-in Shares:** The company has declared that certain shares held by promoters/public are under locked-in periods (Yes to "Whether the Listed Entity has any shares in locked-in?").
- **Other Securities:** The company has declared that there are no outstanding partly paid-up shares, convertible securities, warrants, ESOPs, or depository receipts.

## Regulatory Changes

No broad regulatory changes are introduced by this circular. It is an administrative listing circular in compliance with existing SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Reg. 31(1)(b) Compliance:** The listed entity must submit the statement showing holding of specified securities on a quarterly basis within 21 days from the end of each quarter.
- **Locked-in Shares Compliance:** Shares identified under lock-in must comply with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations regarding the lock-in period.

## Important Dates

- **Quarter Ending / Shareholding Reporting Date:** August 7, 2026 (07-08-2026)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a company-specific listing of an SME IPO and does not impact the broader market or standard trading rules.
- **Company Impact:** High. Enables public trading of Anawil Wire and Engineering Limited's equity shares on a major national stock exchange, providing liquidity and capital access.
