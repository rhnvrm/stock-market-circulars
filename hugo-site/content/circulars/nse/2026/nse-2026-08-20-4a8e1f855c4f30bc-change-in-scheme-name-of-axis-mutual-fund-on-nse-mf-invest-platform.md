## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has informed members that Axis Mutual Fund has changed the name of its scheme 'Axis Multi-Asset Active FOF' to 'Axis Multi-Asset Omni FOF' on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Scheme Name: Axis Multi-Asset Active FOF
- Revised Scheme Name: Axis Multi-Asset Omni FOF
- Effective Date: August 13, 2026
- Platform: NSE MF Invest

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of features in the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) of the scheme.
- Expansion of investment universe in type of scheme to include index/ETFs alongside equity-oriented and debt-oriented mutual fund schemes and commodity-based ETFs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and investors must note the revised scheme name and features for all future transactions and reporting.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Change: August 13, 2026
- Circular Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact on market operations; primarily an administrative update for investors and trading members dealing with Axis Mutual Fund schemes.
