## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the proposed Offer for Sale (OFS) of units of Sustainable Energy Infra Trust on September 24, 2026. The issue size is up to 4,86,00,000 units of InvITs with a floor price of Rs. 120 per unit.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sustainable Energy Infra Trust
- Symbol: SEITINVIT
- ISIN: INE0R8O23017
- Issue Size: Upto 4,86,00,000 units of InvITs of face value of Rs. 100/- each (Base Offer Size)
- Date of Offer: September 24, 2026
- Floor Price: Rs. 120
- Market Lot: 25000 units
- Tick Size: Re. 0.01
- Non-Retail Category Bidding Time: September 24, 2026, from 09:15 Hrs. to 15:30 Hrs.

## Regulatory Changes

- The Offer for Sale shall be in accordance with the implementation of Interoperability among Clearing Corporations along with the Exchange Scheme circular NSE/CMTR/72975 dated February 24, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible trading members can participate using the eOFS platform (https://eofs.nseindia.com).
- Members yet to receive Admin user IDs must request them via the ENIT portal under ENIT-NEW-TRADE→Membership→Enablement→eOFS.
- Compliant UCC of self (PRO) and clients must be updated in the Unique Client Identification (UCI) system a day prior to the OFS bidding session.

## Important Dates

- Bidding Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates the secondary market offer for sale of Sustainable Energy Infra Trust units for eligible institutional and non-retail investors through the NSE eOFS platform.
