## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular (Ref No: 1284/2026, Download Ref No: NSE/CML/75562) dated August 04, 2026, announcing the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, including Balrampur Chini Mills Limited, BLACKBUCK LIMITED, Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited, Cybertech Systems And Software Limited, Shadowfax Technologies Limited, Shoppers Stop Limited, and Tube Investments of India Limited. These shares have been admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) with effect from August 05, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- **Further Issue Listing**: Equity shares of multiple companies have been admitted to trading on the NSE (Capital Market segment) effective from August 05, 2026.
- **Allotment under ESOP**: The securities for the listed companies (Balrampur Chini Mills, BLACKBUCK, Clean Max Enviro Energy) are equity shares of face value Re. 1/- each allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).
- **Listing Details (Partial list from text)**:
  - **Balrampur Chini Mills Limited**:
    - **Symbol**: BALRAMCHIN
    - **ISIN**: INE119A01028
    - **No. of Securities**: 199,907 equity shares of Re. 1/- each.
    - **Date of Allotment**: July 29, 2026
    - **Distinctive Number Range**: 269966425 to 270166331
  - **BLACKBUCK LIMITED**:
    - **Symbol**: BLACKBUCK
    - **ISIN**: INE0UIZ01018
    - **No. of Securities**: Two tranches of allotment: 345 equity shares (allotted on July 24, 2026, distinctive range 182138946 to 182139290) and 11,131 equity shares (allotted on July 24, 2026, distinctive range 182139291 to 182150421).
  - **Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited**:
    - **Symbol**: CLEANMAX
    - **ISIN**: INE647U01026
    - **No. of Securities**: 35,800 equity shares of Re. 1/- each.
    - **Date of Allotment**: July 30, 2026
- **Trading Identifier**: For trading, securities are identified by their designated symbols, series, and ISINs, and will trade in specified lot sizes of 1.
- **Pari Passu Status**: All new equity shares rank pari passu with the existing shares of the respective companies. No lock-in is applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

This is an operational announcement pursuant to existing regulations:
- **Regulation 3.1.1** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A (notifying admission to dealings).
- **Regulation 2.5.5** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A (notifying designated security codes and respective market lot sizes).

## Compliance Requirements

There are no major compliance actions required from market participants or other listed entities. Broker-dealers and trading members should:
- Update their security master files to reflect the new listing of shares and their updated distinctive number ranges for clearing and settlement purposes.
- Ensure systems recognize the updated paid-up share capital for these listed companies.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular**: August 04, 2026
- **Effective Date (Listing Date)**: August 05, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**:
  - Balrampur Chini Mills Limited: July 29, 2026
  - BLACKBUCK LIMITED: July 24, 2026
  - Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal/Low. These are minor allotments under employee option schemes (ESOPs), resulting in a negligible dilution of existing equity capital.
- **Operational Impact**: Positive for trading members and investors as additional floating stock becomes available for trading from August 05, 2026. No changes to trading hours, settlement cycles, or margins.
