## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the launch of the Quant Nifty Midcap 150 Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The NFO transaction window opens on October 12, 2026, and closes on October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** Quant Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** Quant Nifty Midcap 150 Index Fund (Direct and Regular plans including Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options)
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **ISINs:**
  - Direct Plan - Growth: INF966L01EQ8 (Sr. 1)
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout: INF966L01ER6 (Sr. 2)
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF966L01EP0 (Sr. 3)
  - Regular Plan - Growth: INF966L01ET2 (Sr. 4)
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout: INF966L01EU0 (Sr. 5)
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF966L01ES4 (Sr. 6)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this circular is a standard operational notification for a mutual fund product launch.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any accepted payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 28, 2026, by 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** October 12, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** October 26, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- **Fund Cleared Payment Deadline:** October 28, 2026 (till 12:00 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors with operational details for participating in the Quant Nifty Midcap 150 Index Fund NFO through NSE infrastructure with minimal systemic impact.
