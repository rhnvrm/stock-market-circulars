## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has informed all members that the International Securities Identification Number (ISIN) for the Non-Convertible Securities of Globe Fincap Limited will change with effect from August 11, 2026. This change is due to the alteration in the security charge from Unsecured to Secured.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** Globe Fincap Limited.
- **Instrument Type:** Non-Convertible Securities (NCDs).
- **Effective Date:** August 11, 2026.
- **Reason for Change:** Transition of security charge from Unsecured to Secured.
- **ISIN Changes:**
  1. Old ISIN: INE013U08018 is replaced by New ISIN: INE013U07028.
  2. Old ISIN: INE013U08026 is replaced by New ISIN: INE013U07036.

## Regulatory Changes

No major regulatory policy changes are introduced. This is an administrative update following a change in security charge of the debt instruments under listing and disclosure regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading & Settlement:** Members, clearing corporations, and depository participants must update their systems and records to reflect the new ISINs for trading and settlement of these securities from August 11, 2026, onwards.
- **Holders & Investors:** Investors should note the change of the ISIN codes associated with their holdings of Globe Fincap Limited's non-convertible securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Effective Date of ISIN Change:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Very low. This is issuer-specific and affects only the debt segment holders of these specific non-convertible securities.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Requires update in back-office, trading, and depository systems with the new ISIN codes.
