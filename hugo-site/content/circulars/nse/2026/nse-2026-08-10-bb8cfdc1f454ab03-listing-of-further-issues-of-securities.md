## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued circular NSE/CML/75664 dated August 10, 2026, announcing the listing and admission of further securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment, effective from August 11, 2026. These additional securities represent allotments made under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and Qualified Institutional Placements (QIP) by several listed companies.

## Key Points

- **Admitted Securities**: Further issues of shares from Aptus Value Housing Finance India Limited, AYM Syntex Limited, Globus Spirits Limited, PG Electroplast Limited, Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited, and Vaibhav Global Limited are admitted to dealings starting August 11, 2026.
- **Identification & Trading**: Securities will be identified by their designated symbols, ISINs, and series EQ. Trading will be in designated market lots as detailed in the Annexure (market lot size of 1 for the listed securities).
- **Allotment Details**:
  - **Aptus Value Housing Finance India Limited**: 25,000 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP on July 31, 2026 (ISIN: INE852O01025).
  - **AYM Syntex Limited**: 53,792 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP on July 30, 2026 (ISIN: INE193B01039).
  - **Globus Spirits Limited**: 2,380,952 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under QIP at an issue price of Rs. 840/- on August 6, 2026 (ISIN: INE615I01010).
  - **PG Electroplast Limited**: 850,700 equity shares of Re. 1/- each allotted under ESOP on July 2, 2026 (ISIN: INE457L01029).
  - **Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited & Vaibhav Global Limited**: Included in the further issue admission list (details partially truncated in source).
- **Pari Passu Status**: All new shares rank pari passu (equally) with the existing equity shares of the respective companies. No lock-in details are applicable for the ESOP and QIP allotments listed.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no major regulatory changes introduced. The admission has been approved in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 (listing admission) and Regulation 2.5.5 (market lot specifications) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Trading members must use the specified security symbols, series, and ISINs for identifying and clearing transactions in these securities.
- **Depository/Registrar**: Registrars and Transfer Agents (RTAs) and depositories must ensure that the distinctive number ranges are credited and integrated into the existing ISINs.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular**: August 10, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing/Trading**: August 11, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**:
  - PG Electroplast: July 2, 2026
  - AYM Syntex: July 30, 2026
  - Aptus Value Housing: July 31, 2026
  - Globus Spirits: August 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. The admission of further issues under ESOPs and QIP is a routine operational process. It increases the outstanding float of the respective companies but represents minor dilution, except for the QIP of Globus Spirits which adds 2,380,952 shares (representing capital infusion).
- **Operational Impact**: Low. The newly admitted securities will seamlessly merge with existing equity shares (pari passu) under the EQ series and are immediately tradeable on the NSE Capital Market segment.
