## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for multiple companies, including Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited, Ideaforge Technology Limited, Onesource Specialty Pharma Limited, and Tata Consumer Products Limited, effective from October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Further listing of equity shares allotted under ESOPs.
- Effective date for trading: October 22, 2026.
- Companies involved include Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited, Ideaforge Technology Limited, Onesource Specialty Pharma Limited, Tata Consumer Products Limited, Tech Mahindra Limited, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing notification pursuant to National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026
- Allotment dates vary by company (e.g., October 13, 2026 for Anand Rathi; October 14, 2026 for Ideaforge; September 25, 2026 for Onesource Specialty Pharma; October 12, 2026 for Tata Consumer Products).

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital through ESOP allotments with minimal market impact.
