## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced updated quantity freeze limits for index derivatives, effective from October 05, 2026. The previous computation methodology specified in circular NSE/FAOP/73928 dated April 28, 2026, will no longer apply to indices.

## Key Points

- Previous quantity freeze computation methodology for indices is discontinued.
- New explicit quantity freeze limits are established for major indices: NIFTY (3,510), BANKNIFTY (1,440), FINNIFTY (3,240), MIDCPNIFTY (5,760), NIFTYNXT50 (1,125), and NIFTYFPI (53,900).
- Trading members must load updated contract files before trading on the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

Discontinuation of the earlier computation methodology for index quantity freeze limits and implementation of fixed freeze limits per index symbol.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to download and load the updated contract.gz and NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz files from the Extranet server (faoftp/faocommon) or NSE website into their trading applications prior to trading on October 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants must update their trading systems with the new quantity freeze limits and contract files to avoid order rejections in index F&O contracts.
