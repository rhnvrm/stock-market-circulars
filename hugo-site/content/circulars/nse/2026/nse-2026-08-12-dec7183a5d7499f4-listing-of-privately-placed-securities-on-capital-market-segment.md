## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular (Ref. No: 1335/2026, Download Ref No: NSE/CML/75710) dated August 12, 2026, notifying the listing and admission of privately placed debt securities on its Capital Market segment. This admission is effective from August 13, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A. The circular includes details on the fresh issue of securities by REC Limited and Vedika Credit Capital Limited, specifying their series, market lots, coupon rates, maturity dates, and ISIN codes.

## Key Points

- **Regulatory Authority:** National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE), Listing Department.
- **Effective Date:** August 13, 2026.
- **Relevant Regulations:** Regulation 3.1.1 (admission to dealings) and Regulation 2.5.5 (lot sizes) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- **Listed Securities:** Three new debt security instruments have been admitted to trading:
  1. **REC Limited (7.49% 2041 Sr 257 B):**
     - Symbol: `749RECL41`
     - Series: `N0`
     - Market Lot: `1`
     - Coupon Rate: `7.49%`
     - Date of Maturity: `August 31, 2041`
     - ISIN Code: `INE020B08GH5`
  2. **REC Limited (7.28% 2029 Sr 257 A):**
     - Symbol: `728RECL29`
     - Series: `N0`
     - Market Lot: `1`
     - Coupon Rate: `7.28%`
     - Date of Maturity: `August 31, 2029`
     - ISIN Code: `INE020B08GI3`
  3. **Vedika Credit Capital Limited (VCCL31B 11.50% 2031):**
     - Symbol: `115VCCL31B`
     - Series: `N0`
     - Market Lot: `1`
     - Coupon Rate: `11.5%`
     - Date of Maturity: `August 11, 2031`
     - ISIN Code: `INE04HY07419`
- **Trading Identification:** For system trading, the securities shall be identified solely by their designated codes and traded in specified lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no structural or rule-based regulatory changes introduced in this circular. It is a standard administrative announcement implementing existing provisions under:
- **Regulation 3.1.1** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A (admission to dealings).
- **Regulation 2.5.5** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A (respective lot sizes).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Members must update their systems to incorporate the designated security symbols, series, and ISIN codes for trading in the Capital Market segment.
- **Operational Systems:** Back-office systems, trading platforms, and clearing/settlement networks of brokerage firms must be updated to facilitate correct lot sizes and ISINs.
- **Issuing Companies:** The issuers (REC Limited and Vedika Credit Capital Limited) must ensure continuous compliance with listing regulations and timely payment of coupon/maturity obligations.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Listing Effective Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Maturity Dates of Securities:**
  - REC 7.28% 2029 Sr 257 A (INE020B08GI3): August 31, 2029
  - VCCL31B 11.50% 2031 (INE04HY07419): August 11, 2031
  - REC 7.49% 2041 Sr 257 B (INE020B08GH5): August 31, 2041

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine listing announcement for privately placed debt securities, expanding options in fixed income without altering market-wide policies.
- **Liquidity & Trading:** Adds high-quality and mid-yield options for institutional and retail investors seeking specific maturities ranging from 2029 to 2041.
- **Systemic Operations:** Minimal operational impact. Trading is conducted within the established framework of the Capital Market segment.
