## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued guidelines detailing the operational timings, settlement calendars, collateral schedules, and client margin reporting requirements for the special Diwali Muhurat Trading session scheduled on Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Two risk parameter files based on previous close and end-of-day prices will be provided.
- Holdings reporting for FPI (Category I & II) and Mutual Funds for additional index product limits are set at 16:45 hrs and 17:15 hrs respectively.
- Pay-in/pay-out transactions for trade dates November 06, 2026, and November 08, 2026, will be settled on November 09, 2026, at 8:30 hrs.
- Specific client margin reporting schedules are established for trade dates ranging from November 02, 2026, to November 08, 2026.
- Custodian Participant (CP) code modification and Give up confirmation window is set between 14:20 hrs and 15:50 hrs on November 08, 2026.
- Specific start and end times are defined for cash additions, collateral allocations, securities release requests, and cash releases.
- BOD Contract Delta file (Contract_Delta_08112026_i01.csv) will be used for monitoring positions in stocks under ban period.

## Regulatory Changes

- Special operational timings and revised settlement/margin reporting schedules applicable specifically for the Diwali Muhurat Trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with revised margin reporting cut-off dates, collateral allocation timings, and CP code modification windows.

## Important Dates

- Muhurat Trading Date: November 08, 2026
- Settlement Date for Nov 06 & Nov 08 trades: November 09, 2026 at 8:30 hrs
- Client Margin Reporting Cut-Off Dates: November 08, 2026 to November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures orderly clearing, settlement, and risk management operations during the special Diwali trading session.
