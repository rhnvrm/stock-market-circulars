## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of surveillance measures on companies with high 'encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Certain securities have been added to the surveillance framework, while others have been excluded.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I will attract a minimum 35% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments w.e.f. September 07, 2026, on all open positions as on September 04, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities listed in Annexure II are eligible to move out from the framework effective from September 03, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margin requirements (minimum 35%) for companies exhibiting high promoter share encumbrance under SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (at least 35%) in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments for the shortlisted securities effective September 07, 2026.
- Representations against placement in the framework were required to be submitted by September 03, 2026 (5:00 PM).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for exclusion (Annexure II): September 03, 2026
- Cut-off for representations: September 03, 2026 (5:00 PM)
- Open positions reference date: September 04, 2026
- Effective Date for surveillance margin (Annexure I): September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for affected securities may impact trading liquidity and short-term volatility for the specific stocks listed in Annexure I.
