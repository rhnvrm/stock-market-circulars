## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that the Invesco India Nifty Chemical Index Fund of Invesco Mutual Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Effective date for availability: October 9, 2026.
- Covers Regular and Direct plans across Growth and IDCW (Payout & Reinvestment) options.
- Schemes include ISINs: INF205KA1CJ2, INF205KA1CK0, INF205KA1CL8, INF205KA1CM6, INF205KA1CN4, and INF205KA1CO2.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can transact in the schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform adhering to standard operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 8, 2026
- Effective Date: October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors access to trade the Invesco India Nifty Chemical Index Fund schemes on the NSE platform with standard mutual fund facilities.
