## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the adjustment of the market lot for security IDEALTECHO (Ideal Technoplast Industries Limited) on the SME platform due to a 1:1 bonus issue. The adjusted market lot is set to 1000, effective from September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ideal Technoplast Industries Limited
- Symbol: IDEALTECHO
- Type of Corporate Action: Bonus
- Bonus Ratio: 1:1
- Adjustment Factor: 2
- Adjusted Market Lot: 1000

## Regulatory Changes

Trading members must update their trading applications with the latest security files reflecting the adjusted market lot.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to load the updated security.gz / nnf_security.gz / MII - Security File.gz file in the trading application before trading on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-date / Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Record Date: September 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors dealing in IDEALTECHO on the SME platform need to account for the revised market lot size of 1000 shares post-bonus issue.
