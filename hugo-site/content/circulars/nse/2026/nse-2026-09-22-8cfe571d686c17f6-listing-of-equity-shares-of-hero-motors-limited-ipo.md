## Summary

Hero Motors Limited shares are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The shareholding pattern and disclosures under Regulation 31(1)(a) have been submitted.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: Hero Motors Limited
- Scrip Code/Symbol: Equity Shares
- Date of Filing: 22.09.2026
- Outstanding ESOPs and locked-in shares are reported as applicable per regulatory disclosure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entity must adhere to ongoing disclosures regarding shareholding patterns and corporate governance.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 22.09.2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Hero Motors Limited equity shares on the exchange platform.
