## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the listing and commencement of trading for multiple Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on its Capital Market Segment, effective July 31, 2026. This action is taken in compliance with Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, which govern the admission of securities to dealings and their respective lot sizes.

## Key Points

- **Admitted Securities**: A total of five government debt securities are being introduced, consisting of three Treasury Bills and two State Development Loans.
- **Trading Lot Size**: The designated lot size for trading each of these securities on the system is 100 units.
- **Identification**: Trading will be conducted exclusively using the designated symbols and ISIN codes specified in the circular.
- **Series Categories**:
  - Treasury Bills are categorized under the **TB** series.
  - State Development Loans are categorized under the **SG** series.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no changes to existing regulations. This circular represents the operational execution of existing listing and trading framework rules under:
- **Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A** (Admission to Dealings).
- **Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A** (Standard Trading Lot Sizes).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market intermediaries must configure their trading terminals, back-office, and risk management systems to support the new symbols, ISINs, and series (**TB** and **SG**).
- Operational personnel must ensure that order placement and trading rules conform to the standard lot size of 100 units for these instruments.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Security-Specific Dates**:
  - **91D291026** (GOI TBILL 91D-29/10/26, ISIN: IN002026X172): Maturity date October 29, 2026
  - **182D280127** (GOI TBILL 182D-28/01/27, ISIN: IN002026Y170): Maturity date January 28, 2027
  - **364D290727** (GOI TBILL 364D-29/07/27, ISIN: IN002026Z177): Maturity date July 29, 2027
  - **768AP51** (SDL AP 7.68% 2051, ISIN: IN1020260106): Maturity date July 29, 2051
  - **768RJ44** (SDL RJ 7.68% 2044, ISIN: IN2920260105): Maturity date July 29, 2044

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Low. The addition of standard government debt instruments provides broader trading access to secure fixed-income securities. Market operations remain unaffected as these are routine debt listings.
