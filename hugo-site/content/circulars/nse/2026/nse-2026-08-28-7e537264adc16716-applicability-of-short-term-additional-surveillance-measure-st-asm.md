## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II. Securities meeting the specified criteria have been shortlisted under the ST-ASM framework, attracting enhanced margin requirements effective from August 31, 2026 / September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: BIOPOL, CLASSICEIL, DAVANGERE, ENTERO, FMNL, IMAGICAA, OAL, SAROJA, and SUNTECH.
- No securities were reported under ST-ASM Stage II.
- ST-ASM framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely for market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- ST-ASM Stage I margin requirement: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. September 01, 2026 on all open positions as on August 31, 2026 and new positions created from September 01, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities in accordance with the ST-ASM guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 31, 2026 / September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the shortlisted securities will be subject to stricter margin rules, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for traders holding open positions in these scrips.
