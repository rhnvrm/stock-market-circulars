## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of various Government Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on the capital market segment, effective from September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted include 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day Treasury Bills, along with several State Development Loans (SDLs) from states like Jammu & Kashmir, Goa, Punjab, Meghalaya, and Rajasthan.
- Trading will take place in designated lot sizes of 100 as specified in the annexure.
- Securities will be identified exclusively by their designated codes and respective ISINs on the trading system.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, securities are admitted with specified lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure orders are placed using the correct security codes and designated lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Circular Date: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands investment and trading options in sovereign debt instruments on the capital market segment for market participants.
