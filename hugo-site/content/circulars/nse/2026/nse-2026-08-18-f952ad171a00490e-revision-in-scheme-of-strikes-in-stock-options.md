## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the revision in the scheme of strikes in stock options, specifying applicable step values and the number of strikes available for trading effective August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable step values and number of strikes for specific securities are revised effective August 20, 2026.
- Step values and strikes for all other securities remain unchanged.
- Members must load the updated contract file on their trading applications before trading on the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of strike schemes and step values in stock options as per exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated contract.gz / MII contract (`NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz`) file from the Extranet server (`faoftp/faocommon`) or NSE website on their trading application prior to trading on August 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for trading members dealing in derivative segments to ensure correct contract specifications are loaded.
