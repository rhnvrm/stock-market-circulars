## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of equity shares of Lumino Industries Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), with trading effective from September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: LUMINO
- ISIN: INE185Q01025
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 82/-
- Total No. of Securities: 304,553,705
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Securities will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 02, 2026
- Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Lumino Industries Limited equity shares on the NSE capital market segment.
