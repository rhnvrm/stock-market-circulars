## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, September 05, 2026. This session marks the introduction of the new NEAT+ version 1.0.1 and the discontinuation of the existing NEAT version 1.0.0.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, September 05, 2026.
- Discontinuation of NEAT version 1.0.0 and introduction of NEAT+ version 1.0.1 effective from the mock session.
- Includes multiple trading sessions from Primary Site and DR Site, along with block deal window and auction market timings.
- Default password for the mock session is "Neat@EG1".
- Trades executed during mock sessions do not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

- Discontinuation of NEAT version 1.0.0 in favor of the new NEAT+ version 1.0.1 (/egftp/egcommon/NEATPlus101).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to download and use the latest NEAT exe (version 1.0.1) from September 05, 2026 onwards.
- Refer to the updated NEATPlus installation procedure as per circular NSE/MSD/76159 dated September 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 05, 2026
- NEAT Version 1.0.0 Discontinuation Date: September 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness check for trading members in the EGR segment with no financial obligations arising from the mock trades.
