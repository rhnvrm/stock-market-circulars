## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the face value split of equity shares of Oriana Power Limited from Rs. 10 to Rs. 2 per share, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Oriana Power Limited
- Symbol: ORIANA
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10 per share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 2 per share
- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action communicated through listing department circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants need to note the change in face value and corporate action effective date for trading and settlement systems.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders of Oriana Power Limited will see their shareholdings adjusted according to the 5-for-1 stock split ratio, enhancing trading liquidity.
