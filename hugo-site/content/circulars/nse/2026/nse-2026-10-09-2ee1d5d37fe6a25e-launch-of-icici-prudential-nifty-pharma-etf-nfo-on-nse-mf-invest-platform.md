## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the launch of the ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from October 12, 2026, to October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF (Direct Growth & Regular Growth)
- ISIN: INF109K1A716
- Category: Equity
- Subscription Window: October 12, 2026, to October 19, 2026 (till 11:30 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

[N/A]

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 22, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: October 12, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 19, 2026 (11:30 PM)
- Fund Clear Funds Deadline: October 22, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors an opportunity to invest in the pharmaceutical sector via ETF on the NSE MF platform with standard operational workflows.
