## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the launch and availability of the Franklin India Short Term Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on its MF Invest Platform. The subscription window for the NFO is open from August 05, 2026, to August 11, 2026, ending at 11:59 pm. The scheme comprises regular and direct plans with growth, IDCW payout, and IDCW reinvestment options, all categorized as Debt schemes.

## Key Points

- **NFO Timeline:** Available for subscription and transaction on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 05, 2026, to August 11, 2026 (until 11:59 pm).
- **Scheme Options and ISIN details:**
  - Franklin India Short Term Fund - Regular Growth (ISIN: `INF090I01YT5`)
  - Franklin India Short Term Fund - Regular IDCW Payout (ISIN: `INF090I01YU3`)
  - Franklin India Short Term Fund - Regular IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: `INF090I01YV1`)
  - Franklin India Short Term Fund - Direct Growth (ISIN: `INF090I01YW9`)
  - Franklin India Short Term Fund - Direct IDCW Payout (ISIN: `INF090I01YX7`)
  - Franklin India Short Term Fund - Direct IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: `INF090I01YY5`)
- **Allotment Date:** The allotment date for the schemes is August 12, 2026.
- **Payment/Settlement Deadline:** Members must ensure clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 12, 2026, by 11:00 am.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced in this circular. It represents standard operational procedure for listing and enabling trading access to a mutual fund scheme on the exchange platform under current SEBI regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Transaction Window:** Trading members must restrict orders for the NFO to the specified dates and times (August 05, 2026, to August 11, 2026, till 11:59 pm).
- **Fund Transfer:** Trading members must ensure clear funds are successfully credited to the Clearing Corporation's designated bank account before the deadline of 11:00 am on August 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 05, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** August 11, 2026 (until 11:59 pm)
- **Clear Funds Cut-off:** August 12, 2026 (until 11:00 am)
- **Allotment Date:** August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a standard mutual fund NFO launch in the debt category, expanding options for debt mutual fund investors on the platform.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Trading members need to monitor settlement deadlines to ensure funding is completed within the short window before the allotment on August 12.
