## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Privately placed securities/commercial papers specified in the annexure are admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment.
- Effective date of listing: August 18, 2026.
- Issuing entities include Hdfc Securities Limited, Tata Steel Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Sundaram Finance Limited, Action Construction Equipment Limited, Kisetsu Saison Finance (India) Private Limited, and Nu Vista Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard compliance and operational requirements as applicable to privately placed debt securities and commercial papers on the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 18, 2026
- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update providing liquidity and trading access to newly issued commercial papers on the debt segment.
