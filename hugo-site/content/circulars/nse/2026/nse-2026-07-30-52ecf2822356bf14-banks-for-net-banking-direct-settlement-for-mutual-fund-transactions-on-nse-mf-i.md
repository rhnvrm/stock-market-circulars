## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has issued a circular updating the list of banks available for direct settlement of net banking payments for mutual fund transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Transactions using net banking from these designated banks are settled to the clearing corporation account on the same day.

## Key Points

- **Direct Same-Day Settlement**: Payments made via net banking from specified banks are settled directly to NCL's account on the same day.
- **Approved Banks for Direct Settlement**:
  1. Axis Bank
  2. HDFC Bank
  3. ICICI Bank Limited
  4. IDFC First Bank Ltd
  5. IndusInd Bank
  6. Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited
- **Best Effort Basis**: Direct settlement for State Bank of India (SBI) will be done on a best effort basis.
- **Reference**: This circular refers to the earlier circular NCL/MFSS/71454 dated November 25, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced. This is an operational update to the list of approved direct settlement banks under the existing Mutual Fund Service System (MFSS) structure.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants on the NSE MF Invest Platform are requested to:
- Note the list of direct settlement banks and update internal processes accordingly.
- Account for the fact that State Bank of India transactions are handled on a best effort basis for same-day settlement.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 30, 2026
- **Effective Date**: Immediate operational effect

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Streamlines payment clearing operations and transaction settlement processing for users transacting via the listed direct settlement banks.
- **Market Impact**: Increases processing transparency on the NSE MF platform and reduces potential payment clearing delays for mutual fund transactions.
