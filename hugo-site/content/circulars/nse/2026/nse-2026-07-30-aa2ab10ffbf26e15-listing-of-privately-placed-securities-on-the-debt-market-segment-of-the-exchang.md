## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has admitted several privately placed debt securities, including Commercial Papers (CPs) and Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs), to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from July 30, 2026. This action is taken in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations. The listing includes fresh issuances of Commercial Papers from Piramal Finance Limited, EPL Limited, HDB Financial Services Ltd, Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI), L&T Finance Limited, and TVS Credit Services Limited, as well as a debt security issue from Rupeek Capital Private Limited. Additionally, Commercial Paper re-issuances from IIFL Finance Limited and L&T Finance Limited have been admitted.

## Key Points

- **Admissions to Dealings:** Privately placed securities have been admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange under Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.
- **Effective Date:** Admitted with effect from July 30, 2026.
- **Diverse Issuers:** The circular lists securities from multiple major financial and corporate issuers.
- **Types of Securities:** Includes fresh issues of Commercial Papers (CP), fresh issues of other debt securities (DB), and re-issues of Commercial Papers (CP).
- **ISINs and Details:** Each security is identified by a unique ISIN and has specified allotment dates, redemption dates, face values, and issue prices detailed in the Annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes introduced. The admissions are executed under the established Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Issuers:** Ensure adherence to ongoing disclosure norms and listing compliance guidelines as specified under the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations and NSE Debt Market regulations.
- **For Trading Members:** Update back-office systems, trading terminals, and security master records with the designated security codes, descriptors, and ISINs specified in the Annexure to facilitate trading from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **July 28, 2026:** Allotment date for Rupeek Capital Private Limited's debt securities.
- **July 29, 2026:** Allotment date for all Commercial Paper fresh issues and re-issuances listed in the Annexure.
- **July 30, 2026:** Effective date of admission to dealings/trading on the Debt Market Segment.
- **Redemption Dates:**
  - **August 05, 2026:** HDB Financial Services Ltd CP (HFSL CP 05/08/26 Sr 348)
  - **September 21, 2026:** L&T Finance Limited CP re-issue (LTFL CP 21/09/26)
  - **October 26, 2026:** IIFL Finance Limited CP re-issue (IIFL CP 26/10/26)
  - **October 27, 2026:** EPL Limited CP (EPL CP 27/10/26)
  - **October 27, 2026:** TVS Credit Services Limited CP (TCSL CP 27/10/26)
  - **October 28, 2026:** Small Industries Development Bank of India CP (SIDBI CP 28/10/26 Sr 9)
  - **October 28, 2026:** L&T Finance Limited CP (LTFL CP 28/10/26)
  - **February 18, 2027:** Piramal Finance Limited CP (PFL CP 18/02/27)
  - **January 28, 2028:** Rupeek Capital Private Limited Debt Securities (RCPL 11% 2028 Sr I)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine listing of privately placed short-term and medium-term debt instruments. It provides secondary market liquidity for the institutional subscribers of these papers.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Trading members must integrate the new security descriptors, symbols, and ISINs into their trading platforms and systems to enable seamless transaction processing.
