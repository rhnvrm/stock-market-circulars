## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for two companies under the SME Emerge segment, effective October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Cellecor Gadgets Limited (CELLECOR):** 14,811,207 equity shares of Re. 1/- each allotted under FCCB on September 01, 2026. Distinctive Number Range: 222326939 to 237138145. Market Lot: 3,000.
- **GP Eco Solutions India Limited (GPECO):** 600 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP on September 01, 2026. Distinctive Number Range: 12329801 to 12330400. Market Lot: 200.
- **Effective Date:** October 08, 2026.
- **Temporary ISIN Note:** Equity shares allotted in dematerialized mode have been credited under temporary ISINs in accordance with SEBI circulars.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Admission to dealings is in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the securities shall be conducted using designated security codes and respective market lots.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 01, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action allowing trading of newly issued shares resulting from FCCB conversion and ESOP exercise for the respective companies.
