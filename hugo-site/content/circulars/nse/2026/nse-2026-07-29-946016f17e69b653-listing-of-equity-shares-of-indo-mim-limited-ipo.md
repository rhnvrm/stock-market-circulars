## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the listing of equity shares of **INDO-MIM Limited** on the Capital Market segment, effective from **July 30, 2026**. This listing follows the company's recent Initial Public Offering (IPO). The shares will be traded in the compulsory dematerialized form with the designated symbol **INDOMIM** and ISIN **INE084101034**.

## Key Points

- **Listing Date**: July 30, 2026
- **Symbol**: INDOMIM
- **Series**: EQ
- **ISIN**: INE084101034
- **No. of Securities**: 494,480,907 Equity Shares
- **Face Value**: Re. 1/-
- **Issue Price**: Rs. 485/-
- **Market Lot**: 1 share
- **Pre-open Session**: The security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other categories of scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

- This listing is executed in pursuance of **Regulation 3.1.1** (dealings admittance) and **Regulation 2.5.5** (market lot sizes) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- The pre-open session will be conducted as per SEBI circulars **CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012** and **CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012** dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Guidelines**: Members of the Exchange are notified that trading must be carried out only under the designated code and specified lot sizes in dematerialized form.
- **Lock-in Details**: A portion of the shares is subject to lock-in periods as detailed in Annexure I (with lock-in expiry dates ranging from August 26, 2026, to February 5, 2028).

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Effective Listing & Trading Date**: July 30, 2026
- **Pre-open Session Date**: July 30, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Milestones**:
  - August 26, 2026
  - October 25, 2026
  - February 5, 2027
  - February 5, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: The listing provides a new investment avenue in the manufacturing/engineering sector under the symbol INDOMIM.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading members must update their front-office and back-office systems with the new symbol, ISIN, and security parameters to facilitate client trading in INDOMIM from the pre-open session on July 30, 2026.
