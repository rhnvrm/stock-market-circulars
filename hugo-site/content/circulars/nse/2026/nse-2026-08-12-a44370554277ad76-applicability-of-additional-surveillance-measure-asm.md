## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Based on the surveillance criteria, five securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under Long-Term ASM Stage-I, effective from August 13, 2026. A 100% margin requirement will apply to these securities starting August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Five securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Long-Term ASM Stage-I.
- The applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. August 17, 2026 on all open positions as on August 14, 2026 and new positions created from August 17, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under Criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. August 17, 2026.
- Shortlisting under the ASM framework is purely due to market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of selected securities into the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage-I framework.
- Application of 100% margin requirements for the shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **NSE Members:** Apply a 100% margin rate starting August 17, 2026 on all open positions as on August 14, 2026 and on new positions created from August 17, 2026 onwards for the affected securities.
- **Market Participants:** Monitor the list of ASM-shortlisted securities to manage leverage, risk, and potential settlement transitions.

## Important Dates

- **August 13, 2026:** Effective date for securities shortlisted in the Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I.
- **August 14, 2026:** Date for determining open positions subject to the revised 100% margin.
- **August 17, 2026:** Effective date of 100% margin requirement on both open and new positions.

## Impact Assessment

- **Affected Stocks:**
  - ABM International Limited (Symbol: ABMINTLLTD | ISIN: INE251C01025)
  - Beta Drugs Limited (Symbol: BETA | ISIN: INE351Y01019)
  - Ind-Swift Laboratories Limited (Symbol: INDSWFTLAB | ISIN: INE915B01019)
  - Sakar Healthcare Limited (Symbol: SAKAR | ISIN: INE732S01012)
  - Zenith Exports Limited (Symbol: ZENITHEXPO | ISIN: INE058B01018)
- **Market/Trading Impact:** Shortlisted securities will require a 100% margin, which increases trading costs and eliminates leverage. This usually results in a temporary decrease in trading volume and liquidity for the affected counters.
