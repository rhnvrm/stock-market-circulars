## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a reminder to all members regarding the discontinuation of existing broadcast parameters in the Futures & Options (F&O) segment. This follows previous circulars introducing new broadcast parameters to change the data type for token-level cumulative volume from Unsigned Integer (4 bytes) to LONG (8 bytes).

## Key Points

- Reminder to complete migration to new market data broadcast parameters or ensure migration plan readiness.
- Affects NNF users receiving 5 depth 1 second refresh market data in the F&O segment.
- Coexistence period is concluding, leading up to the discontinuation of legacy parameters.

## Regulatory Changes

- Change in data type for token-level cumulative volume (total traded volume) from “Unsigned Integer” (4 bytes) to “LONG” (8 bytes).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members using NNF must review their market data broadcast configurations and complete required changes within the stipulated timeline to ensure uninterrupted receipt of F&O market data broadcast.

## Important Dates

- Discontinuation Date: September 05, 2026 (mock) onwards.

## Impact Assessment

- Members failing to migrate before the deadline risk disruption in receiving F&O market data broadcasts.
