## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to certain securities satisfying criteria for inclusion under Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 22, 2026, on all open positions as on September 21, 2026 and new positions created from September 22, 2026 onwards.
- SRG Housing Finance Limited (Symbol: SRGHFL, ISIN: INE559N01010) moved from STASM to LTASM framework under Stage - I.
- Shortlisting of securities under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) provisions on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure 100% margin requirements are met for the applicable securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026 (for inclusion list)
- Margin Applicability Date: September 22, 2026 (on open positions as on September 21, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements (100%) for impacted scrips like SRGHFL, affecting liquidity and position management for market participants trading these securities.
