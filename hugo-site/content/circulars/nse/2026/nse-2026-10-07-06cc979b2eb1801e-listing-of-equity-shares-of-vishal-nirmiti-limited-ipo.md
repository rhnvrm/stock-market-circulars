## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Vishal Nirmiti Limited following its initial public offering (IPO), along with the shareholding pattern disclosures under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Vishal Nirmiti Limited
- Class of Security: Equity Shares
- Post-Issue Shareholding Pattern Date: 06.10.2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- Submission of specified securities holding patterns and ongoing statutory disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates public trading of Vishal Nirmiti Limited equity shares on the exchange platform.
