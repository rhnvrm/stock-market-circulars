## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has notified the revised settlement calendar (compulsory delivery) and GST details reporting calendar for the Commodity Derivatives Segment for October 2026, pursuant to partial modification of earlier circulars and contract parameter changes for Gold Mini Futures and Options.

## Key Points

- Revised settlement and delivery schedules are issued for commodity futures contracts expiring in October 2026.
- Covers precious metals (Gold Mini, Silver, Silver 100, Gold Guinea, Gold 10G) and base metals (Aluminium, Alumini, Copper, Lead, Lead Mini, Nickel, Zinc, Zinc Mini).
- Includes supplementary settlement schedules for weight, location, quality differentials, making charges, and GST.
- Annexure 2 outlines the GST details confirmation schedule, specifying windows for seller and buyer members to upload GST details for October 2026 expiry contracts.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial modification of circular NCL/COM/76325 following circular NSE/COM/76535 dated September 24, 2026, regarding modifications in contract parameters of Gold Mini Futures and Gold Mini Options on Futures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members participating in commodity derivatives must adhere to the revised delivery settlement schedules, delivery intention timings, delivery marking, and pay-in/pay-out timelines.
- Seller and buyer members must upload and confirm GST details within the designated windows specified in Annexure 2.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 29, 2026.
- Delivery settlement and GST reporting windows apply across October and November 2026 for respective contract expiries.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures operational alignment and accurate settlement/GST processing for commodity derivatives clearing members following contract parameter revisions.
