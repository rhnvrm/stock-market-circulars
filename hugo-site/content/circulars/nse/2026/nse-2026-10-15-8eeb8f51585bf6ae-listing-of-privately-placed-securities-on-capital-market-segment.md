## Summary

National Stock Exchange has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities of Hero FinCorp Limited on the capital market segment with effect from October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Issuer: Hero FinCorp Limited
- Security Symbol: 925HFL37
- Issue Description: HFL 9.25% 2037 Sr 82
- ISIN: INE957N08227
- Coupon Rate: 9.25%
- Maturity Date: 07-Jul-2037
- Market Lot: 1
- Series: N0

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, securities are admitted to dealings with designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the specified securities shall be conducted using designated security codes and lot sizes as per exchange regulations.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Maturity Date: July 07, 2037

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading liquidity and formal listing for the privately placed debt securities on the exchange capital market segment.
