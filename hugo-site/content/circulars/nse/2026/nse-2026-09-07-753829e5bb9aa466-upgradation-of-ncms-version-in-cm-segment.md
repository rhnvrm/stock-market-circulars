## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the release of a new EXE version (4.2.5) of the National Clearing Management System (NCMS) for the Capital Market segment, following up on circular no. NCL/CMPT/75482 dated July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- New NCMS EXE version: 4.2.5.
- No change in user experience for NCMS-CM.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational software upgrade.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to download and install the new NCMS version for their operations.

## Important Dates

- **Download Availability:** September 12, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 15, 2026 (Trade Date)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Members will need to update their software to version 4.2.5, though user experience remains unchanged.
