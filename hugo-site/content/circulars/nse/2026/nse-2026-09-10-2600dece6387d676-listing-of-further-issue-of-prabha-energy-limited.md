## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further equity shares of Prabha Energy Limited effective from September 11, 2026. This follows the conversion of 95,13,903 partly paid-up equity shares into fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Prabha Energy Limited
- Symbol: PRABHA
- ISIN: INE0I0M01023
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Re. 1/-
- Number of Securities: 95,13,903 equity shares
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Pari Passu: Yes
- Lock-in Details: Not Applicable

## Regulatory Changes

None. The circular is issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the designated security codes and trading lot sizes specified for the further issue.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The additional equity shares will be available for trading alongside existing shares under the same ISIN, increasing the listed capital of Prabha Energy Limited.
