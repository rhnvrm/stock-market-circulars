## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a revised list of Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) eligible for cross margining in the Futures & Options segment, superseding circular no. 0101/2026 dated August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 30, 2026.
- Covers multiple ETFs across fund houses such as Nippon India, SBI, ICICI Prudential, Kotak Mahindra, HDFC, UTI, and Mirae Asset.
- Specifies minimum quantity requirements and multiples thereon for each eligible ETF.

## Regulatory Changes

- Update to the approved list of securities eligible for cross margining benefits in the F&O segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must take note of the revised eligible ETFs and corresponding minimum quantity requirements while computing cross margins.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 28, 2026.
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts trading members and clearing members utilizing cross-margining facilities between cash/ETF holdings and derivative positions.
