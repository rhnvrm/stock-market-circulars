## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the commencement of trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Shanti Gold International Limited. Trading of these REs will be admitted on the capital market segment under the Trade to Trade (T2T) category (Series BE) with effect from August 14, 2026. The circular provides specific details regarding security identifiers, trading periods, suspension dates, and market lot requirements to ensure smooth and informed operations for trading members and clients.

## Key Points

- **Trading Commencement:** Trading of Shanti Gold International Limited Rights Entitlements (Symbol: `SHANTI-RE`, ISIN: `INE06ZD20017`) will begin on August 14, 2026.
- **Segment and Series:** The REs will be traded under Series BE (Trade to Trade segment) in compulsory dematerialized form for all categories of investors.
- **Market Lot:** The minimum market lot for trading of these Rights Entitlements is set to `1`.
- **Trading Quantity:** A total of 4,625,879 Rights Entitlements have been admitted to dealings on the exchange.
- **Client Disclaimers:** Trading members must prominently feature a disclaimer in client contract notes/bills clarifying that purchasing REs only grants the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce them, and does not constitute buying ordinary shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Regulation References:** The notifications are issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 (further securities admission) and Regulation 2.5.5 (market lot sizes specification) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- **T2T Mandate:** Compulsory trading of the Rights Entitlements under the Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat mode for risk containment and orderly trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Client Information:** Trading members must inform clients about the start of trading of the REs, specifically mentioning the RE's ISIN to prevent any confusion with the ordinary shares of Shanti Gold International Limited.
- **Prominent Disclaimers:** Contract notes or bills for the purchase of REs must prominently include a disclaimer informing clients that:
  - Purchasing REs only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before closing.
  - REs that are neither subscribed (by paying application money) nor renounced on or before the closing date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date (Trading Commencement):** August 14, 2026
- **Trading Period for Renunciation of REs:** From August 14, 2026, to August 18, 2026 (both days inclusive)
- **Date of Suspension:** August 19, 2026 (trading of REs suspended post-renunciation period)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Provides liquidity to shareholders of Shanti Gold International Limited by enabling them to trade or liquidate their Rights Entitlements during the specified renunciation window.
- **Operational Impact:** Trading members need to update their contract note templates to prominently include the mandatory RE disclaimer and configure trading terminals to support `SHANTI-RE` in the T2T segment (compulsory demat, lot size 1).
- **Investor Protection:** Mandatory disclaimers protect retail investors from mistakenly conflating Rights Entitlements with ordinary shares, thereby reducing operational risks and potential disputes.
