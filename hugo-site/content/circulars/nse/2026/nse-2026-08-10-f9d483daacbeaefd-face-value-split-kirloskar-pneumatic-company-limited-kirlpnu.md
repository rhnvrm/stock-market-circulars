## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular informing members about the face value split of Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited (Symbol: KIRLPNU). The face value and paid-up value of the company's equity shares will be reduced from Rs. 2 to Re. 1, effective from August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited
- **Symbol:** KIRLPNU
- **Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 2 per share
- **New Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Re. 1 per share
- **Effective Date:** August 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

There are no broader regulatory changes. This circular announces a stock split corporate action specific to the equity shares of Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants should adjust their trading systems and databases to reflect the new face value and paid-up value of Re. 1 for KIRLPNU with effect from August 18, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Effective Date of Split:** August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Standard adjustment of share price and liquidity for KIRLPNU in proportion to the 2:1 stock split.
- **Operational Impact:** Listing parameters and trading systems will update the face value of the security to Re. 1. No impact on the broader market or other securities.
