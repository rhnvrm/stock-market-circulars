## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued operational guidelines and schedules for the Muhurat Trading session on account of Diwali, scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026. The circular outlines modified timings for trade modifications, collateral management, margin reporting, and settlements.

## Key Points

- **Session Date**: Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- **CP Code Modification**: Allowed between 14:20 hrs and 16:15 hrs on November 08, 2026.
- **VaR Files**: Two VaR files based on previous close prices and end-of-day prices will be provided.
- **Collateral Timings**: Cash addition and release (for same-day cash) between 12:15 and 16:15; allocation of collaterals between 11:45 and 19:00.
- **Settlement Schedule**: Special settlement timelines established for trades executed on November 08, 2026, and multiple settlements scheduled for November 09, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of client margin reporting schedule for trade dates spanning November 02, 2026 to November 08, 2026 to accommodate the special trading session.
- Special settlement schedule and pay-in/pay-out timings for trades executed during the Muhurat trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must adhere to revised cut-off timings for obligation transfer requests, early pay-in (EPI) of funds and securities, and margin reporting.
- Custodians must complete trade confirmations and CP code modifications within the designated window.

## Important Dates

- **Muhurat Trading Session**: November 08, 2026
- **Settlement Pay-in for Muhurat Trades**: November 09, 2026 at 16:30 hrs
- **Settlement Pay-out for Muhurat Trades**: November 09, 2026 at 21:00 hrs
- **Client Margin Cut-off Dates**: Adjusted across November 02 to November 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants, custodians, and trading members must update their internal systems and operational workflows to align with the revised clearing, collateral allocation, and settlement timelines for the Diwali Muhurat trading session.
