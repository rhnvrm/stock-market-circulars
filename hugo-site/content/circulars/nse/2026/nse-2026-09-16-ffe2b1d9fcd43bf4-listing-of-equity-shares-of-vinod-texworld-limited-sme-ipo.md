## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Vinod Texworld Limited on the SME platform. The circular includes details of the shareholding pattern post-issue, highlighting promoter and public holding percentages.

- Security: Equity Shares (SME IPO)

- Promoter Holding: 10,801,200 shares (66.84%)
