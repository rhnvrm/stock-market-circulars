## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, lumpsum investments (including switch-ins), fresh SIPs, and STP-in registrations for Shriram Gold ETF Passive Fund of Fund under Shriram Mutual Fund, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Scheme: Shriram Gold ETF Passive Fund of Fund.
- Restricted Transactions: Lumpsum subscriptions (including switch-in), fresh SIP registrations, modifications with top-up, STP-in, and IDCW TP registrations.
- Unaffected Transactions: Redemptions, SWPs, Switch-outs, STP-outs, and existing SIPs/STPs registered on or before October 15, 2026, will continue without change.
- Cut-off Timings: Applications received by October 15, 2026, up to 3:00 p.m. will be processed; subsequent applications will be refunded.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary implementation of subscription restrictions by Shriram Mutual Fund as per Notice-cum-Addendum No. 24/2026-27.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and platform participants must ensure no fresh subscription or SIP/STP-in orders are processed for the affected scheme starting October 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026.
- Cut-off Date for Prior Applications: October 15, 2026 (3:00 p.m.).

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding existing SIPs or wishing to redeem units are unaffected, while new investors or existing investors wishing to add fresh lumpsum/SIP allocations to Shriram Gold ETF Passive Fund of Fund must pause or seek alternative schemes.
