## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 15, 2026 on all open positions as on October 14, 2026 and new positions created from October 15, 2026. Additionally, certain securities are shifting between stages or moving out of the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I (Stage I) will attract a minimum 100% margin and shift from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 15, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 14, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company/entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Application of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework rules.
- Shifting of specified securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Imposition of 100% margin requirements on open and new positions.
- Movement of securities between ESM stages (e.g., Stage I to Stage II).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements and trading segment shifts for affected securities.
- Monitor open and new positions according to the effective dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Margin & Segment Shift (Stage I): October 15, 2026 (on open positions as on October 14, 2026).
- Effective Date for Stage II price band / movement: October 14, 2026.
- Circular Date: October 13, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for the shortlisted securities (including AMEYA, ARENTERP, CPCAP, PCCL, TARAPUR, UDAYJEW, VERANDA, APSISAERO, PANACHE) will be significantly impacted due to the 100% margin requirement and Trade-for-Trade settlement transfer.
