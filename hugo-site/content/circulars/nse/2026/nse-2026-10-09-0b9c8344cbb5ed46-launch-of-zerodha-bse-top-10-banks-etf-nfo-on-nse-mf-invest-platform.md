## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of the Zerodha BSE Top 10 Banks ETF New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The NFO period runs from October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026, until 11:59 PM.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** Zerodha Mutual Fund
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **NFO Window:** October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- **Schemes Included:**
  - Zerodha BSE Top 10 Banks ETF - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF0R8F01265)
  - Zerodha BSE Top 10 Banks ETF - Regular Growth (ISIN: INF0R8F01265)
- **Category:** Equity ETF

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational circular regarding a new fund offer launch on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any accepted payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 29, 2026, up to 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** October 12, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** October 26, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- **Fund Allotment Date:** October 29, 2026
- **Clear Funds Deadline:** October 29, 2026 (03:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to the new Zerodha BSE Top 10 Banks ETF scheme on the NSE MF platform with minimal operational impact.
