## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The circular provides updates on securities shortlisted for ESM Stage-I and Stage-II, securities transitioning between stages, and those excluded from the framework. It also includes a consolidated list of securities under ESM.

## Key Points

- **Stage-II Shifting**: Eurotex Industries and Exports Limited (EUROTEXIND) has been shortlisted to move from ESM Stage-I to Stage-II.
- **Rolling to Trade-for-Trade Segment**: Securities qualifying under ESM will shift from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).
- **Price Band and Call Auction**: Stage-II securities will trade under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction.
- **Margin Requirements**: Shortlisted ESM securities will attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions and new positions.
- **Exclusions & Stage-I additions**: No securities were added to Stage-I or excluded from the ESM framework in this circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Trading Segment Reclassification**: Shifting of securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade (Series: BE/ST) segment.
- **Periodic Call Auction Implementation**: Stage-II securities are subjected to a restrictive 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction.
- **Surveillance Framework Application**: The measures are implemented in conjunction with existing ESM guidelines set forth in previous circulars from 2023 to 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Collection**: Members must collect a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on August 04, 2026, and new positions created on or after August 05, 2026, for the qualifying securities.
- **Operational Adjustments**: Clearing and settlement systems must accommodate the segment transition from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade starting August 05, 2026.
- **Risk Management Systems**: Systems must update the price bands to 2% and move affected security (EUROTEXIND) transactions to the Periodic Call Auction segment starting August 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **August 03, 2026**: Circular date.
- **August 04, 2026**: Effective date for Eurotex Industries and Exports Limited (EUROTEXIND) to move to ESM Stage-II and trade in Periodic Call Auction with a 2% price band.
- **August 05, 2026**: Effective date for ESM securities to shift to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) and for 100% margin applicability.

## Impact Assessment

- **Affected Securities**: Direct impact on Eurotex Industries and Exports Limited (EUROTEXIND) as it moves to Stage-II, as well as ongoing restrictions for consolidated ESM list securities like Accord Synergy Limited (ACCORD), Amanta Healthcare Limited (AMANTA), Apsis Aerocom Limited (APSISAERO), and Ascom Leasing & Investments Limited (ASCOM).
- **Liquidity Impact**: The move to a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction is highly likely to reduce the trading volume and liquidity of the affected securities significantly.
- **No Punitive Action**: The Exchange clarifies that ESM shortlisting is purely a market surveillance measure and should not be considered an adverse action against the companies.
