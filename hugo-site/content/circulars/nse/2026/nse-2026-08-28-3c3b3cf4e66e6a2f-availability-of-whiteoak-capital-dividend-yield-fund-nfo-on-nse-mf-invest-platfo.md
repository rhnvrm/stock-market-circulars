## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of WhiteOak Capital Dividend Yield Fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from August 31, 2026. The circular provides ISIN details and facility types for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: WhiteOak Capital Dividend Yield Fund
- Effective Date: August 31, 2026
- Facilities Available: Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- Platforms: NSE MF Invest Platform
- Covered Plans: Regular and Direct variants for Growth and IDCW (Reinvestment & Payout)

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants should update their systems with the new ISINs for trading and reporting on the NSE MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to transact in the new fund schemes via the NSE MF Invest Platform starting August 31, 2026.
