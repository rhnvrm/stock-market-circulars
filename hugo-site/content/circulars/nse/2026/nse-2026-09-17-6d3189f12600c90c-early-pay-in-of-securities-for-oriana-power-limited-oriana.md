## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued operational guidelines regarding the early pay-in (EPI) of securities for Oriana Power Limited (ORIANA) on account of its face value split (sub-division) from Rs 10/- per share to Rs 2/- per share. Members are required to execute early pay-in in the old/existing ISIN (INE0OUT01019) for specific settlement numbers without adjusting for the conversion.

## Key Points

- Company: Oriana Power Limited (Symbol: ORIANA, Old ISIN: INE0OUT01019)
- Corporate Action: Face Value Split (Sub-Division) from Rs 10/- to Rs 2/- per share.
- Record Date & Ex-Date: 18-SEP-2026.
- Early pay-in for settlement numbers 2026177 and 2026178 on 18-SEP-2026 and 21-SEP-2026 must be executed using the old/existing ISIN.
- Custodians using the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details based on actual traded quantity to pass benefits to clients.
- Block mechanism early pay-in should not include uploaded client-wise early pay-in allocation files.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and custodians must execute early pay-in instructions in the Old ISIN for the record/ex-date.
- Post-record date, pay-in/early pay-in instructions must be executed in the New ISIN.
- Custodians providing EPI through pool accounts must upload client-wise allocation details per actual traded quantities.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 18-SEP-2026
- Ex-Date: 18-SEP-2026
- EPI Dates: 18-SEP-2026 and 21-SEP-2026

## Impact Assessment

Clearing members and custodians must follow specific ISIN instructions for early pay-in during the corporate action window to avoid settlement discrepancies.
