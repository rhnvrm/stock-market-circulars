## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed non-convertible securities of Star Health And Allied Insurance Company Limited, effective October 14, 2026, due to redemption by exercise of a call option.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Star Health And Allied Insurance Company Limited
- **ISIN:** INE575P08040
- **Suspension Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Reason for Suspension:** Redemption by exercise of call option
- **Regulation:** Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate debt action pursuant to exchange regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note that trading in the specified non-convertible securities stands suspended from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Suspension Date:** October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impact is limited to holders of the specific non-convertible securities undergoing redemption and call option exercise.
