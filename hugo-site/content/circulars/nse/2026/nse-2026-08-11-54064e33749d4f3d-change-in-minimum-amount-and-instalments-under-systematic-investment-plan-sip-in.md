## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified all trading members and distributors of immediate updates to the Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) parameters for schemes of JM Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform. Following a notice-cum-addendum issued by JM Financial Asset Management Limited, the minimum instalment amount and minimum number of instalments have been revised across daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly, and quarterly frequencies. Additionally, a daily SIP frequency has been introduced for the JM Large & Mid Cap Fund.

## Key Points

- **Introduction of Daily SIP:** Daily SIP facility has been introduced specifically for the JM Large & Mid Cap Fund.
- **Standardized Minimum Instalments:** The minimum number of instalments for all SIP frequencies (Daily, Weekly, Fortnightly, Monthly, and Quarterly) has been consolidated and standardized to 6 instalments.
- **Revised Minimum Amounts:** 
  - **Daily, Weekly, and Fortnightly:** Revised to Rs. 500 per instalment.
  - **Monthly and Quarterly:** Revised to Rs. 1,000 per instalment.
- **ELSS Exception:** For the JM ELSS Tax Saver Fund, the minimum SIP amount remains Rs. 500 and in multiples of Rs. 500 thereafter.

## Regulatory Changes

JM Financial Asset Management Limited has modified the 'Minimum Number of installments & Frequency' provisions under the Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility across all its schemes. This modification amends the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) of the respective schemes, as well as the Statement of Additional Information (SAI) of JM Financial Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- **System and Platform Validation:** Mutual fund distributors and members operating on the NSE MF Invest platform must update their transaction routing engines to enforce the revised minimum investment amounts and instalment requirements.
- **Client Disclosure:** Intermediaries must disclose these revised parameters to investors initiating new SIP applications in JM Mutual Fund schemes to prevent transaction failures or rejections.
- **Grandfathering:** Existing active SIP mandates registered before the effective date remain unaffected.

## Important Dates

- **AMC Addendum Effective Date:** August 07, 2026
- **NSE Circular Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Implementation Date on NSE MF Invest Platform:** Immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Low. Requires update to the validation logic on front-end transaction portals and NSE MF Invest platform integration interfaces to comply with new subscription boundaries.
- **Investor Impact:** Low. While the minimum investment amount has increased (from Rs. 100/250 to Rs. 500/1,000 depending on the frequency), the minimum required tenure/instalments have been significantly reduced to 6, which may lower the commitment period for newer investors.
