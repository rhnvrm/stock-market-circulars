## Summary

SEBI has passed a Final Order in the matter of front-running the trades of Unifi Capital Pvt. Ltd. (a Portfolio Management Service provider, referred to as the "Big Client") by Ashok Maheshwari (a dealer at Kotak Securities Limited) and other associated noticees. The investigation, covering the period from April 1, 2021 to May 31, 2022, revealed that Ashok Maheshwari leveraged his position as a dealer to front-run the Big Client's trades, leading to significant unlawful gains for several connected entities.

## Key Points

- **The Scheme:** Ashok Maheshwari, as a dealer at Kotak Securities placing orders for Unifi Capital, shared order information or traded ahead of Unifi Capital's orders using accounts of other noticees.
- **Noticees Involved:** The noticees include Ashok Maheshwari, Darshan Bakul Shah, Khusboo Darshan Shah, Darshan Bakul Shah (HUF), Benzer Department Stores Pvt. Ltd., Mihir Dhirajalal Savla, CHL Stock Concepts Pvt. Ltd., and Chirag Mahendra Shah.
- **Trading Pattern:** Significant intraday profits and trading instances of the noticees occurred on common scrip days matching Unifi Capital's trades, with 80.52% of the noticees' intraday profits made on those common days.
- **Interim Action:** An Interim Order-cum-Show Cause Notice was issued on April 26, 2024, which has culminated in these final directions under the SEBI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

No new systemic regulatory changes are introduced by this order, as it is a specific enforcement action. However, it reaffirms SEBI's strict stance against front-running and fraudulent/unfair trade practices under the PFUTP Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Market Intermediaries:** Brokers and portfolio managers must maintain strict confidentiality of client orders and implement robust surveillance systems to detect potential front-running or information leakage by dealers.
- **Dealers:** Absolute adherence to code of conduct rules, prohibiting the sharing of sensitive client order information or personal/associated trading ahead of client orders.

## Important Dates

- **Investigation Period (IP):** April 1, 2021, to May 31, 2022.
- **Interim Order Date:** April 26, 2024.
- **Final Order Date:** August 13, 2026 (pursuant to SAT order directions).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Reinforces market integrity by penalizing unfair trading practices, boosting investor confidence in institutional portfolio management services.
- **Operational Impact:** Broking firms and PMS providers may face increased scrutiny and are expected to strengthen internal controls, order-handling protocols, and audio/communication recording logs of dealers.
