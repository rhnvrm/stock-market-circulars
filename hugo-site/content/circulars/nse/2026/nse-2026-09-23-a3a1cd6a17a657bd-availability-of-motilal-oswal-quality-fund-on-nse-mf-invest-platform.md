## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of various schemes under Motilal Oswal Quality Fund for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP on the NSE MF Invest Platform with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Motilal Oswal Quality Fund schemes are reopened for ongoing transactions.
- Facilities available include subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Covers both Direct and Regular plans including Growth and IDCW options (ISINs: INF247L01HI3, INF247L01HJ1, INF247L01HK9, INF247L01HL7, INF247L01HM5, INF247L01HN3).
