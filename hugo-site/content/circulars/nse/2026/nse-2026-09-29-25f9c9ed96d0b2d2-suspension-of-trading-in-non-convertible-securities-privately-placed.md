## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities (NCDs) for five entities, effective from September 30, 2026, on account of redemption.

## Key Points

- Suspension takes effect on September 30, 2026.
- Reason for suspension: Redemption of securities.
- Affected entities include Punjab Infrastructure Development Board, GMR Pochanpalli Expressways Limited, National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD), and Indian Railway Finance Corporation Limited (IRFC).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; executed pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the suspension of trading in the specified ISINs and avoid executing trades in these securities post the suspension date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 29, 2026
- Suspension Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on market operations as the suspension is a standard procedure following the redemption of debt securities.
