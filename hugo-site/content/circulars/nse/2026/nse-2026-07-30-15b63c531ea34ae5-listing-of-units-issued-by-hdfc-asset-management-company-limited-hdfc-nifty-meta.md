## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified the listing and admission of units of the **HDFC Nifty Metal ETF** to dealings on the Capital Market segment of the Exchange. The listing is issued by **HDFC Asset Management Company Limited** and will be effective from **July 31, 2026**.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** HDFC Mutual Fund - HDFC Nifty Metal ETF
- **Trading Symbol:** HDFCNIMEG
- **ISIN:** INF179KC1KT4
- **Total Units Admitted:** 1,087,926 units
- **Series:** EQ
- **Date of Allotment:** July 27, 2026
- **Face Value per Unit:** Rs. 124.88/-
- **Paid-Up Value per Unit:** Rs. 124.88/-
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 124.88/-
- **Market Lot:** 1 unit
- **Market Makers:** Multiple designated market makers including East India Securities, Kotak Securities, HDFC Securities Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- The units are admitted further to dealings in pursuance of **Regulation 3.1.1** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- The respective lot sizes are specified in pursuance of **Regulation 2.5.5** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Identification:** For trading on the system, the security shall be identified only by its designated code/symbol (`HDFCNIMEG`) and trading must be in designated lot sizes (Market Lot of 1 unit).
- **Key Entities Contact Information:**
  - **Trustee:** HDFC Trustee Company Limited, Mumbai
  - **Asset Management Company (AMC):** HDFC Asset Management Company Limited, Mumbai
  - **Registrar and Share Transfer Agent (RTA):** Computer Age Management Services Private Limited (CAMS), Chennai

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** July 27, 2026
- **Circular Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Effective/Trading Commencement Date:** July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Provides investors with access to a new sector-specific ETF (Nifty Metal index) on the NSE, enhancing liquidity and trading options in the metal sector.
- **Operational Impact:** Allows trading members to execute orders in the new ETF units under the symbol `HDFCNIMEG` starting July 31, 2026. Multiple market makers have been appointed to ensure liquidity.
