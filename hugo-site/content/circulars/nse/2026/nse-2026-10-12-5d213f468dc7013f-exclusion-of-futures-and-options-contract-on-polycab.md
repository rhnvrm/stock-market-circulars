## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on Polycab India Limited (POLYCAB) from its Equity Derivatives segment, following exchange circular no. NSE/SURV/76858 dated October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Polycab India Limited (Symbol: POLYCAB)
- **New Expiry Months:** No contracts for new expiry months will be issued upon the expiry of existing contract months.
- **Existing Contracts:** Unexpired contracts for October 2026, November 2026, and December 2026 remain available for trading until their respective expiry dates, and new strikes will continue to be introduced in these existing contract months.
- **Full Cessation:** No contracts shall be available for trading in the security effective from December 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of Polycab India Limited from the list of eligible securities in the Equity Derivatives segment in continuation of surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure that no new derivative contracts are initiated for expiry months beyond the existing unexpired contracts and manage open positions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **October 12, 2026:** Issuance of circular regarding F&O exclusion.
- **December 30, 2026:** Effective date when no contracts will be available for trading in POLYCAB.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding positions in POLYCAB derivatives must square off or settle their positions ahead of the final expiry and cessation date of December 30, 2026.
