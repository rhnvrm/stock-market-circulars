## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has approved the admission and listing of privately placed debt securities on its Capital Market segment. Effective from August 06, 2026, these securities will be admitted to dealings and traded under their designated security codes and series. This notice covers two new debt issuances: one by SMFG India Credit Company Limited and another by Ambium Finserve Limited, outlining their respective ISINs, coupon rates, market lots, and maturity dates.

## Key Points

- **Listing and Admission**: Approval of listings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) with trading starting on August 06, 2026.
- **SMFG India Credit Company Limited Security Details**:
  - **Symbol**: 773SICC29
  - **Series**: N0
  - **Market Lot**: 1
  - **Description**: SICC 7.73% 2029 Sr 119
  - **Coupon Rate**: 7.73%
  - **Maturity Date**: August 03, 2029
  - **ISIN**: INE535H07CV1
- **Ambium Finserve Limited Security Details**:
  - **Symbol**: 1116AFL28A
  - **Series**: N0
  - **Market Lot**: 1
  - **Description**: AFL 11.16% 2028
  - **Coupon Rate**: 11.16%
  - **Maturity Date**: August 04, 2028
  - **ISIN**: INE0RU307270
- **Identification and Trading**: The securities will be identified solely by their designated symbols, and trading must strictly conform to the specified market lot of 1.

## Regulatory Changes

- **National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A**:
  - **Regulation 3.1.1**: Invoked for admitting the list of securities further to dealings on the Capital Market segment.
  - **Regulation 2.5.5**: Invoked to establish and notify the respective market lot sizes for the admitted securities.
- This action constitutes a standard regulatory execution of existing exchange trading framework guidelines, rather than any new structural policy change.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members and Brokerages**:
  - Must configure their front-office and back-office systems to map the new symbols (`773SICC29`, `1116AFL28A`) and respective ISIN codes prior to market open on August 06, 2026.
  - Ensure that trading systems enforce a minimum and multiplier lot size of 1 unit for order entry in these specific debt instruments.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 05, 2026
- **Listing / Effective Date**: August 06, 2026
- **SMFG India Credit Maturity Date**: August 03, 2029
- **Ambium Finserve Maturity Date**: August 04, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Extremely low. The listing of these privately placed debt securities primarily facilitates trading access and liquidity for specific institutional or individual holders of these papers, with no impact on the broader equity or derivatives markets.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading operations, clearing, and settlement systems will need to support trading in these newly listed series starting from the effective date.
