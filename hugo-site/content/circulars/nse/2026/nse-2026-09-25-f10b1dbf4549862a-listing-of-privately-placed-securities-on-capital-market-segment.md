## Summary

National Stock Exchange has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities of Achintya Securities Limited on the capital market segment with effect from September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Achintya Securities Limited
- Symbol / Security Code: 10ASL27
- Series: N0
- Market Lot: 1
- Issue Description: ASL 10% 2027
- Coupon Rate: 10%
- Date of Maturity: 26-Dec-2027
- ISIN Code: INE0RH707013
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the securities are admitted to dealings under designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in these securities shall be conducted solely using the designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 28, 2026
- Maturity Date: 26-Dec-2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing update for market participants trading in privately placed debt securities on the NSE capital market segment.
