## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP), effective from September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Further issue of equity shares admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective September 08, 2026.
- Companies involved include Borosil Scientific Limited, Carborundum Universal Limited, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited, Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited, Fractal Analytics Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, Shadowfax Technologies Limited, Smartworks Coworking Spaces Limited, and Wakefit Innovations Limited.
- Securities will be identified by their designated codes and traded in specified lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adherence to designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital increase through ESOP allotments with negligible market impact.
