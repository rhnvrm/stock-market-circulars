## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of privately placed securities on the capital market segment with effect from October 15, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Listing of L&T Finance Limited (Symbol: 857LTFL36, ISIN: INE498L08077, Maturity: 10-Oct-2036, Coupon: 8.57%).
- Listing of Alpha Alternatives Financial Services Private Limited (Symbol: AAFS32C, ISIN: INE0L6807377, Maturity: 24-Aug-2032, Coupon: Reset).
- Market lot for both securities is set to 1.
- Effective date of listing and trading: October 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of privately placed securities to dealings on the Capital Market segment pursuant to NSE Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the specified securities shall be identified solely by their designated codes and executed in the specified market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026
- Maturity Dates: August 24, 2032 (AAFS32C) and October 10, 2036 (857LTFL36).

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational listing enabling secondary market trading for the newly issued privately placed debt securities.
