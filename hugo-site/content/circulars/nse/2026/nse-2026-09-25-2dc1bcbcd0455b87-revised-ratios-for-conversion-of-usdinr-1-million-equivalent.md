## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the revised ratios for the conversion of USD 1 million equivalent in the Currency Derivatives segment. These updates supersede previous conversion factors starting October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised conversion ratios for USD 1 million equivalent:
  - EUR: 0.87 million
  - GBP: 0.74 million
  - JPY: 159.53 million
- Total position across exchanges must remain within the prescribed limit of USD 100 million equivalent.
- Excess positions require disclosure of underlying exposure to Trading Members.

## Regulatory Changes

The circular updates the conversion factors for foreign currencies against USD 1 million equivalent in the Currency Derivatives segment, referencing earlier circulars NSE/SURV/29575 and NSE/SURV/37410.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must ensure their positions across exchanges stay within the USD 100 million equivalent limit. If positions exceed this threshold, clients are required to provide disclosures of their underlying exposure to Trading Members in accordance with existing practices.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 25, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 29, 2026 onwards
- **Previous Factor Expiry:** October 28, 2026 (conversion factor under NSE/SURV/74902 dated June 25, 2026 continues till then)

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants dealing in currency derivatives must update their systems and monitoring parameters to reflect the new conversion ratios and ensure compliance with position limits.
