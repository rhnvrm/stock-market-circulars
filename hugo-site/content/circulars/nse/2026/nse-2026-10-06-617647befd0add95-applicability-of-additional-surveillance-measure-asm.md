## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the application of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage-I for specific securities effective October 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I: Nimbus Projects Limited (NIMBSPROJ) and Surani Steel Tubes Limited (SURANI).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 09, 2026 on all open positions as on October 08, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specified scrips under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage-I.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure 100% margin maintenance on all open positions as of October 08, 2026, and new positions created from October 09, 2026 onwards for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective date of ASM shortlisting: October 07, 2026.
- Effective date for 100% margin requirement: October 09, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (100%) will reduce leverage and potentially impact trading liquidity for the shortlisted securities.
