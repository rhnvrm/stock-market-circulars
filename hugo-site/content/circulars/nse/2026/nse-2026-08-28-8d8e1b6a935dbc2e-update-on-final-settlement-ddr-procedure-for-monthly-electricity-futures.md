## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued an update regarding the Final Settlement (DDR) procedure for monthly electricity futures. Building upon circular NCL/COM/69390, the methodology involving provisional settlement (considering provisional DDR) followed by final settlement (considering final DDR) is now extended to specific additional scenarios involving Sundays, Mondays, and trading holidays.

## Key Points

- Updates the Final Settlement (DDR) computation methodology for monthly electricity futures.
- Extends the provisional and final settlement procedure to three specific calendar and holiday conditions.
- Applies to contracts cleared through NSE Clearing Limited's Commodity Derivatives Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- The provisional settlement (using Provisional DDR) and subsequent final settlement (using Final DDR) mechanism is now mandated for the following cases:
  1. Last calendar day of the month falls on a Sunday.
  2. Last calendar day of the month falls on a Monday.
  3. Second last calendar day of the month falls on a Trading Holiday.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading participants must align their back-office systems and settlement workflows with the extended DDR computation procedure for monthly electricity futures.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective: Immediate (referencing prior circular NCL/COM/69390)

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures operational consistency and transparent risk management for monthly electricity futures settlement when expiry dates coincide with weekends or trading holidays.
