## Summary

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified the listing and admission of several privately placed debt securities on its Debt Market Segment. These admissions—comprising fresh issues of commercial papers and other debt instruments, as well as re-issuances—are effective from August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Admitted Securities**: Multiple fresh and re-issued commercial papers (CPs) and other debt instruments (debentures/NCDs) have been admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment under designated security codes and ISINs.
- **Effective Date**: Deals on these securities commence on August 03, 2026.
- **Issuers Involved**:
  - **Fresh CP Issues**: Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Sundaram Finance Limited, and Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited.
  - **Fresh Other Issues (Debentures)**: Hero FinCorp Limited and Muthoot Finance Limited.
  - **Re-issued CPs**: HDB Financial Services Ltd, Hero FinCorp Limited, and L&T Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no changes to existing regulatory frameworks. The action is taken in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations to admit newly allotted privately placed securities for trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members**: Members must ensure their trading and settlement systems are updated with the newly notified security codes and ISINs to facilitate smooth transaction execution.
- **For Issuers**: Issuers are required to maintain compliance with ongoing disclosure, interest payments, and redemption schedules associated with these listings.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates**: July 30, 2026 and July 31, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date**: August 03, 2026
- **Redemption Dates**:
  - **August 18, 2026**: HDB Financial Services Ltd CP (INE756I14GL9)
  - **September 04, 2026**: HDB Financial Services Ltd CP (INE756I14GI5)
  - **October 30, 2026**: Sundaram Finance Limited CP (INE660A14ZD7) & Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited CP (INE549H14040)
  - **February 15, 2027**: Hero FinCorp Limited CP (INE957N14KT8)
  - **July 30, 2027**: Aditya Birla Capital Limited CP (INE674K14CT2)
  - **July 31, 2028**: Hero FinCorp Limited 8.40% Debentures (INE957N07898)
  - **September 04, 2029**: Muthoot Finance Limited T-Bill Linked Debentures (INE414G07KC4)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Facilitates market liquidity by expanding the pool of active, high-credit-quality fixed-income assets and short-term commercial paper available to institutional and retail debt market participants.
- **Operational Impact**: Standardized listing information, including ISIN mappings and exact option dates, reduces settlement risks and ensures accurate record-keeping on the exchange.
