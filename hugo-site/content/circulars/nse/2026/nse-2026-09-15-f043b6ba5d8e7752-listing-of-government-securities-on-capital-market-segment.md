## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed Government Securities on the Capital Market Segment, effective from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** GOI LOAN 7.63% 2056
- **ISIN Code:** IN0020260082
- **Security Code / Symbol:** 763GS2056 / GS
- **Coupon Rate:** 7.63%
- **Date of Maturity:** 15-Sep-2056
- **Effective Date:** September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be conducted using the designated security codes and respective lot sizes specified in the circular.

## Important Dates

- **Issuance / Circular Date:** September 15, 2026
- **Effective Date / Trading Commencement:** September 17, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** September 15, 2056

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants with trading access to the newly issued Government Security on the Capital Market Segment.
