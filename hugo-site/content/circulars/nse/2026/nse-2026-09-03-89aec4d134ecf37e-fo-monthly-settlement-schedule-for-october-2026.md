## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the trade dates and corresponding settlement dates for the Futures & Options (F&O) segment for the month of October 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines daily trade dates matching respective settlement dates for October 2026.
- Covers regular clearing operations for F&O segment members.
- Final trade date listed is October 30, 2026, with settlement on November 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must align their clearing and settlement operations according to the specified schedule.

## Important Dates

- Schedule Effective: October 01, 2026
- Final Trade Date in Schedule: October 30, 2026
- Final Settlement Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update ensuring clear timelines for F&O market participants during October 2026.
