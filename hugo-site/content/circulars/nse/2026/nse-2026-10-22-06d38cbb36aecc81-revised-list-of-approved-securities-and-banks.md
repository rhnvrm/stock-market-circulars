## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the revised list of approved securities and banks via circular reference NCL/CMPT/77079 (Circular No. 0373/2026) dated October 22, 2026. This supersedes the previous circular no. 0342/2026 and is effective for the month of November 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers updated market-wide limits, member-specific limits, and applicable haircut rates across various asset classes.
- Annexure 1: Equity shares (non-cash liquid assets) with applicable VAR-based haircuts and market-wide limits.
- Annexure 2 & 3: Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) for non-cash and cash components of liquid assets.
- Annexure 4: Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs), GOI Securities (G-Sec), and T-Bills with applicable haircut rates and a 2-day shut period.
- Annexure 5: Open-ended mutual funds acceptable as collaterals with market-wide limits and haircuts.
- Annexure 6: Corporate bonds (non-cash component) with market-wide limits and haircuts.
- Annexure 7: List of banks empaneled for issuing Bank Guarantees (BGs) and Fixed Deposit Receipts (FDRs).

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated approved lists and applicable haircut rates for collateral securities effective November 2026.
- Strict adherence to prudential norms: exclusion of a pledged security must not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must take note of revised limits, market-wide limits, and member-specific limits.
- G-Sec / T-Bills have a shut period of two days; members must release them at least two days before maturity.
- Member-banks must ensure that G-Sec provided as collaterals are not reckoned for SLR purposes and are not used for trading.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Clearing members and custodians must update their collateral management systems with the new approved securities lists, limits, and haircut percentages effective November 2026.
