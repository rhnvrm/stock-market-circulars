## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular informing members about the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Lohia Corp Limited under the IPO category.

## Key Points

- Forthcoming listing of equity shares of Lohia Corp Limited on the Exchange.
- **Symbol:** LCL
- **ISIN:** INE0QJW01029
- The official date of listing will be communicated via a separate circular.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes have been introduced. This is a standard operational announcement for the listing of a new security.

## Compliance Requirements

No immediate compliance actions are required from trading members. Members should take note of the upcoming listing and security identifiers (Symbol: LCL, ISIN: INE0QJW01029) for future trading operations.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Listing Date:** To be announced through a separate circular

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** The introduction of Lohia Corp Limited (LCL) will expand the range of investable equity assets in the NSE IPO/listing segment. Members should ensure their trading systems are prepared to support trading in symbol LCL once the official listing date is announced.
