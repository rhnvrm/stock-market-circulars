## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Skyways Air Services Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Name of the company: Skyways Air Services Limited
- Symbol: SKYWAYS
- ISIN: INE0PX301025
- The exact date of listing will be communicated via a separate circular.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the security details for their systems.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 31, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced separately

## Impact Assessment

Standard addition of a new security to the exchange listing segment for trading.
