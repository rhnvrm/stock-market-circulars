## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long-Term ASM framework. Specific securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under Long-Term ASM Stage-I effective September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I w.e.f. September 10, 2026: Alkali Metals Limited (ALKALI), KSR Footwear Limited (KSR), Vilin Bio Med Limited (VILINBIO), and Wonder Electricals Limited (WEL) (moved from STASM to LTASM).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 15, 2026 on all open positions as on September 11, 2026 and new positions created from September 15, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Long-Term ASM framework including 100% margin requirements for short-listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure 100% margin requirements are maintained on open positions and new positions as per the effective dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Inclusion: September 10, 2026
- Open Positions Date for Margin: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date of Margin Applicability: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will attract a 100% margin requirement from September 15, 2026, impacting liquidity and position sizing for market participants holding or trading these scrips.
