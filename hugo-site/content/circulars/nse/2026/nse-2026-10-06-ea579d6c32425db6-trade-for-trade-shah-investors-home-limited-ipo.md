## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Shah Investor's Home Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) effective October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shah Investor's Home Limited
- Symbol: SHAHINVEST
- Segment Transfer: From trade for trade segment (series: BE) to rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026
- Reference Circulars: NSE/CML/76721 dated October 05, 2026, and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities transition from trade-for-trade settlement to normal rolling settlement pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the shift in trading series for SHAHINVEST effective October 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Resumption of regular rolling settlement for SHAHINVEST shares, enabling standard trading operations.
