## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing of units issued by Axis Asset Management Company Limited under the scheme Axis Nifty Energy ETF, effective from September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** ENERGYAXIS
- **ISIN:** INF846KA1598
- **Number of Units:** 3,581,880
- **Face Value & Paid Up Value:** Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 19.06/- per unit
- **Date of Allotment:** August 27, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Series:** EQ

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing in pursuance of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and designated market makers must adhere to the trading regulations and lot sizes specified for the security code.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of units of Axis Nifty Energy ETF on the National Stock Exchange.
