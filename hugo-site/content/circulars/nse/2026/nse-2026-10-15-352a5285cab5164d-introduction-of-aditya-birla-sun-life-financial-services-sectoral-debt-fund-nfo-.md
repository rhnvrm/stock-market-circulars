## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that the Aditya Birla Sun Life Financial Services Sectoral Debt Fund will be available for subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Aditya Birla Sun Life Financial Services Sectoral Debt Fund
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Available Facilities: Transactions, subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- ISINs include various Regular and Direct options (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure proper integration and adherence to NSE MF Invest Platform guidelines for processing investor orders.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members immediate access to transact in the new debt fund scheme via the NSE MF platform.
