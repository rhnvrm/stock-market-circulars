## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment with effect from September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, various privately placed securities are admitted to dealings.
- Effective date of listing: September 21, 2026.
- Securities will be identified by their designated codes and traded in specified lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of privately placed non-convertible debentures and debt securities to the trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026
- Maturity Dates across issuers range from September 12, 2028, to September 17, 2031.

## Impact Assessment

- Expands trading availability for the newly listed debt instruments on the capital market segment.
