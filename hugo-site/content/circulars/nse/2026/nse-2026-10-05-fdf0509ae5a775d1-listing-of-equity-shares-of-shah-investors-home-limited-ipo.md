## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Shah Investor's Home Limited (IPO) on the Capital Market segment effective from October 06, 2026. The security will trade under the symbol SHAHINVEST in Series 'BE' and will subsequently be shifted to Series 'EQ'.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shah Investor's Home Limited
- Symbol: SHAHINVEST
- Series: BE* (shifting to EQ as per SEBI circular)
- ISIN: INE029N01014
- Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- Issue Price: Rs. 167/- per share
- Total No. of Securities: 21,153,200 equity shares
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the securities will be subject to pre-open session guidelines for IPO and other categories of scrips as per SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and market participants must note the designated trading codes, lot sizes, and lock-in details specified in the circular and annexures. Securities are initially available in Series 'BE'.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: October 06, 2026
- Lock-in Upto Dates: Various tranches up to April 04, 2027 (as per Annexure I)

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Shah Investor's Home Limited equity shares on NSE, providing liquidity to shareholders and expanding investment opportunities in the capital market.
