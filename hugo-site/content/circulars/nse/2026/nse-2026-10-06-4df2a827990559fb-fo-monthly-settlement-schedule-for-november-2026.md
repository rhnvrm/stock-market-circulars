## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the trade date and settlement date schedule for the Futures & Options (F&O) segment for the month of November 2026. Market participants and trading members are required to note the corresponding settlement dates for each trade date during the month.

## Key Points

- Covers all trading and settlement dates for the F&O segment in November 2026.
- First trade date of the month: November 02, 2026 (settlement on November 03, 2026).
- Final trade date of the month: November 30, 2026 (settlement on December 01, 2026).
- Issued by NSE Clearing Limited (Download Ref No: NCL/CMPT/76736).

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure adequate funds and securities are available for settlement on the respective settlement dates as per the schedule.

## Important Dates

- November 02, 2026: First trade date for November 2026 F&O settlement.
- November 30, 2026: Last trade date for November 2026 F&O settlement.
- December 01, 2026: Settlement date for trades executed on November 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational schedule ensuring smooth clearing and settlement operations in the F&O segment for November 2026.
