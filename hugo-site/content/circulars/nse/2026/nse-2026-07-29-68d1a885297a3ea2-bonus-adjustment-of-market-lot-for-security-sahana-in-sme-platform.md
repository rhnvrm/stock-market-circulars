## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the adjustment of the market lot for SAHANA System Limited (Symbol: SAHANA) on the SME Platform. This adjustment is necessitated by a corporate action involving a 1:5 bonus issue with an adjustment factor of 1.2. The ex-date/effective date and the record date for this corporate action are both July 31, 2026. The adjusted market lot for the security has been set to 150.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** SAHANA SYSTEM LIMITED (Symbol: SAHANA)
- **Corporate Action Type:** BONUS
- **Bonus Ratio:** 1:5
- **Adjustment Factor:** 1.2
- **Adjusted Market Lot:** 150
- **Trading Files:** Members are advised to load updated security files (`security.gz`, `nnf_security.gz`, or `MII - Security File.gz`) in their trading applications before the market opens on the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework changes are introduced. This is a routine operational adjustment of trading parameters (market lot) in accordance with the NSE's standard operating procedures for corporate actions on the SME platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must:
- Download the updated `security.gz`, `nnf_security.gz`, or `MII - Security File.gz` files from the Extranet server under the directory `common/NTNEAT` or from the NSE website.
- Ensure these updated files are successfully loaded into their trading applications before trading begins on the effective date (July 31, 2026) to avoid any trading disruptions or order entry errors.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Ex-Date / Effective Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Record Date:** July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This adjustment affects only the specific security, SAHANA System Limited, trading on the SME platform.
- **Operational Impact:** Medium for trading members who must ensure their trading software and market data systems are updated with the revised market lot of 150 before trading starts on July 31, 2026, to prevent any order submission/execution issues.
