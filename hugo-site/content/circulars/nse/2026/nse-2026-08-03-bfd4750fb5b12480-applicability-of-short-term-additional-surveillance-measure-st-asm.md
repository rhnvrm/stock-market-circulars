## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a surveillance circular announcing the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Based on periodic market surveillance criteria, six securities have met the requirements for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I, effective from August 05, 2026. No securities were added to or transitioned within Stage II in this review.

## Key Points

- **Inclusion in ST-ASM Stage I**: Six securities (63MOONS, GATECH, GSMFOILS, MEDICO, RSDFIN, and TSC) are shortlisted in the ST-ASM Stage I framework.
- **Margin Action (Stage I)**: Applicable margin rate shall be 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), subject to a maximum margin cap of 100%. This is applicable to all open positions as of August 04, 2026, and new positions created w.e.f. August 05, 2026.
- **No Stage II Additions**: No securities have been shortlisted for Stage II or transitioned between Stage I and Stage II.
- **Surveillance Nature**: Shortlisting of securities under ASM is done purely on account of market surveillance to ensure market integrity and is not an adverse action against the shortlisted companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I surveillance action for the specified securities in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must apply the revised margin rate of 50% (or existing margin, whichever is higher, max 100%) on all open positions as of August 04, 2026, and new positions created from August 05, 2026, for the six shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- **August 04, 2026**: Shortlist effective date and cut-off date for determining open positions subject to surveillance margin.
- **August 05, 2026**: Effective date for revised margin rates on all open and new positions.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Leveraged trading in the six shortlisted securities will be restricted due to the minimum 50% margin requirement, potentially leading to a temporary reduction in trading volume and liquidity for these counters.
- **Corporate Impact**: No operational or regulatory impact on the corporate status of the listed entities, as the measure is purely surveillance-driven.
