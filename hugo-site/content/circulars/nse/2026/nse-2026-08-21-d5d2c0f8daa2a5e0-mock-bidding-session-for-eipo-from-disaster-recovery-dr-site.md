## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock bidding session for eIPO from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site scheduled for August 22, 2026. Members are advised to participate actively to test connectivity.

## Key Points

- Mock bidding session scheduled for August 22, 2026.
- Conducted from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site.
- No changes required at member end for connecting to the DR site (URL: https://eipo.nseindia.com/).
- Bids placed during the mock session will not attract any fund or security obligations.
- NCB security parameters include Mock Govt Security (MCKGS) with normal market open at 10:00 hrs and close at 17:00 hrs.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine mock drill for disaster recovery preparedness.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are encouraged to actively participate to check connectivity and ensure seamless operations from the DR site.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 21, 2026
- Mock Session Date: August 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Designed exclusively for testing and connectivity validation without any financial or settlement implications.
