## Summary

National Stock Exchange has empaneled M/s. Oraph Private Limited for providing Black Box Algorithmic Trading Solutions, complementing their existing White Box empanelment.

## Key Points

- Vendor Name: M/s. Oraph Private Limited
- Type of Algo: Whitebox And Blackbox
- Effective Date: Immediate effect (October 08, 2026)
- Reference Circular: NSE/INVG/71820 dated December 16, 2025

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative update regarding authorized algorithmic trading providers.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members utilizing algorithmic trading solutions from M/s. Oraph Private Limited must ensure compliance with exchange norms.

## Important Dates

- Applicable with immediate effect: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members with an expanded list of approved vendors for black box algorithmic trading solutions.
