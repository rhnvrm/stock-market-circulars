## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of Sammaan Capital Limited with effect from September 09, 2026, pursuant to maturity.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sammaan Capital Limited
- Affected Securities: NCDs with symbols 925SCL26, 889SCL26, and 975SCL28
- ISINs: INE148I07KM3, INE148I07KN1, INE148I07KP6
- Reason: Maturity

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension of trading under Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the suspension of trading in the specified NCDs effective from the given date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified debt securities of Sammaan Capital Limited will cease on the exchange platform due to their maturity.
