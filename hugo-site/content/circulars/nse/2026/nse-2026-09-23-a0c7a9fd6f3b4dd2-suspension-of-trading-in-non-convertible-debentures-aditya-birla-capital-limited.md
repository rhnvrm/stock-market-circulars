## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of Aditya Birla Capital Limited due to their maturity, effective from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aditya Birla Capital Limited
- Affected Symbols: ABCL26, 8ABCL26
- ISINs: INE860H07IR8, INE860H07IQ0
- Reason: Maturity of securities
- Effective Date: September 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Action taken pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are advised to take note of the suspension and avoid trading in the specified NCDs effective from the given date.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Effective Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and routine impact limited to the expiration and delisting/suspension of the specific mature debt instruments.
