## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the restriction in trading of securities of Supreme Engineering Limited (SUPREMEENG) in the Trade for Trade category pursuant to non-compliance with Regulation 14 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for the financial year ended 2025-26, following the withdrawal of its Corporate Insolvency Resolution Process (CIRP).

## Key Points

- Listed entities are required under Regulation 14 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 to pay Annual Listing Fees (ALF).
- Supreme Engineering Limited was initially exempt from trading restrictions due to admission into CIRP by NCLT Mumbai.
- CIRP proceedings were subsequently withdrawn as per an NCLT order dated August 06, 2026.
- The company has been given a deadline until October 19, 2026, to pay the outstanding ALF to avoid trading restrictions.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of trading restrictions under Exchange Circular Ref No. NSE/CML/2021/22 for non-payment of Annual Listing Fees as CIRP proceedings are no longer active.

## Compliance Requirements

The company must clear its pending Annual Listing Fees on or before October 19, 2026, to avoid trading limitations.

## Important Dates

- Deadline for ALF payment: October 19, 2026
- Discontinuation of daily trading in BZ series: October 23, 2026
- Trading allowed only on the first trading day of every week in trade for trade category (Series: BZ): October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading frequency for Supreme Engineering Limited (SUPREMEENG) will be severely restricted if outstanding listing fees are not paid by the stipulated deadline.
