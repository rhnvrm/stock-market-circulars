## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the launch of the DSP NIFTY 10 YR BENCHMARK G-SEC ETF New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The NFO will be open for transactions from August 10, 2026, to August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** DSP NIFTY 10 YR BENCHMARK G-SEC ETF (Direct Growth and Regular Growth options).
- **ISIN:** INF740KA1ZI7 for both options.
- **Category:** Equity (as designated in the circular details, though it is a G-Sec ETF).
- **Allotment Date:** August 14, 2026.
- **Availability:** Open from August 10, 2026, to August 12, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- **Payment Deadline:** Clear funds must be received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 13, 2026, till 11:59 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; this is a standard operational announcement for a new product launch on the exchange's mutual fund platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Members (Trading Members):** Ensure that clear funds for subscription transactions are received in the Clearing Corporation's designated account by August 13, 2026, before 11:59 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 10, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** August 12, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** August 13, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine listing of a new exchange-traded fund (ETF) focusing on the 10-year G-Sec benchmark.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Members need to facilitate transaction routing and ensure timely settlement/funds pay-in for client applications within the specified deadlines.
