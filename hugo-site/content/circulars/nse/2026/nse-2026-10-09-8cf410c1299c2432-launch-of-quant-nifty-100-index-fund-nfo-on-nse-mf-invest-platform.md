## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that the Quant Nifty 100 Index Fund NFO by Quant Mutual Fund will be available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Quant Nifty 100 Index Fund NFO is available on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Subscription window: October 12, 2026 to October 26, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- Includes multiple plans: Direct and Regular plans for IDCW (Payout/Reinvestment) and Growth options.
- Allotment date across schemes is October 28, 2026.
- Clear funds must be received in the designated Clearing Corporation account by October 28, 2026, till 12:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard operational circular regarding a new fund offer (NFO).

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through permitted payment modes are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 28, 2026, till 12:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: October 12, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 26, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)
- Allotment Date: October 28, 2026
- Clear Funds Cut-off: October 28, 2026 (till 12:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors with investment access to the Quant Nifty 100 Index Fund through the NSE MF Invest platform during the specified NFO window.
