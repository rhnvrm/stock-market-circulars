## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular announcing the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and new SIP/STP registrations in the Kotak CRISIL-IBX AAA Bond Financial Services Index – Dec 2026 Fund under Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Scheme: Kotak CRISIL-IBX AAA Bond Financial Services Index – Dec 2026 Fund.
- Restrictions: Lumpsum subscription including switch-in and fresh registration under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) are disabled.
- Unaffected Transactions: Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-out, STP out, and existing SIP/STP instalments registered prior to October 26, 2026, will continue without any change.
- Cut-off Time: All purchase or switch-in transactions timestamped on or before 3:00 PM on October 23, 2026, are accepted and processed at applicable NAV.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary restriction on new inflows into the specified debt index fund scheme of Kotak Mutual Fund as per AMC communication.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must note the suspension of fresh investments and new SIP/STP registrations in the scheme on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Cut-off for existing applications: October 23, 2026, up to 3:00 PM.
- Effective Date of Suspension: October 26, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors wishing to make fresh investments or start new SIPs/STPs in the Kotak CRISIL-IBX AAA Bond Financial Services Index – Dec 2026 Fund will be unable to do so via the NSE MF Invest Platform until further notice. Existing investments and ongoing SIP/STP instalments remain unaffected.
