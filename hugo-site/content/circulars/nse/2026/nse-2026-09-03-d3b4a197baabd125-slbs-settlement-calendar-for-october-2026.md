## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) settlement calendar for the month of October 2026 via circular NCL/CMPT/76153. The circular outlines the schedule for First Leg Settlement (Settlement Type ‘L’), Reverse Leg Settlement (Settlement Type ‘P’), and Reverse Leg Auction (Settlement Type ‘Q’).

## Key Points

- Covers trade dates, custodial confirmation dates, and settlement dates for First Leg (Settlement Type 'L') throughout October 2026.
- Provides reverse leg settlement dates (Settlement Type 'P') for various series starting from October 2026 through October 2027.
- Notes that the 10/XO series expiring on 05-Oct-27 will be available from 07-Oct-26 following the expiry of the current 10/XO series on 06-Oct-26.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine operational schedule issuance pursuant to circular no. NCL/CMPT/73999 dated April 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians must adhere to the specified trade dates, custodial confirmation dates, and settlement dates for SLBS transactions during October 2026.

## Important Dates

- **First Leg Settlement Dates:** Range from October 5, 2026, to November 2, 2026, for trades conducted throughout October 2026.
- **Reverse Leg Settlement Dates:** Specific monthly series settlement dates starting from October 6, 2026 (Series 10/XO).

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational execution and clearing for market participants engaging in Securities Lending & Borrowing transactions.
