## Summary

National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding the delisting offer under the Tender Offer scheme for Hitech Corporation Limited by the acquirer Geetanjali Trading and Investments Private Limited. The bidding session is scheduled from October 07, 2026, to October 13, 2026, at an offer price of Rs. 252 per equity share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Hitech Corporation Limited
- Acquirer Name: Geetanjali Trading and Investments Private Limited
- Security Symbol: HITECHCORP
- ISIN: INE120D01012
- Issue Size: Up to 43,91,220 Equity Shares
- Offer Type: Delisting (Reverse Book Building)
- Offer Price: Rs. 252
- Issue Period: 07-Oct-2026 to 13-Oct-2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Conduct of delisting offer under the Tender Offer Scheme in accordance with Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible trading members of the Capital Market segment can participate using existing user IDs on the web-based IPO/Tender Offer bidding platform (https://eipo.nseindia.com).
- Members without system access can log in to the admin terminal to create branches and users (User ID: Adminxxxxx where xxxxx is the Member Code).
- Bidding time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m., with custodial confirmation on issue closure day up to 4.00 p.m.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: 07-Oct-2026
- Issue Closing Date: 13-Oct-2026
- Custodial Confirmation on Issue Closure Day: Up to 4.00 p.m. on 13-Oct-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public shareholders of Hitech Corporation Limited to tender their shares through the web-based bidding platform as part of the reverse book building process for delisting.
