## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the submission of the Action Taken Report (ATR) pertaining to the System Audit and Cyber Security & Cyber Resilience Audit for the period ending March 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reminder for submitting Action Taken Reports (ATRs) for System Audit and Cyber Security & Cyber Resilience Audits.
- Applies to non-compliances reported by auditors for the period ending March 31, 2026.
- Auditors must review corrective actions and submit the compliance status (Compliant or Non-Compliant) on ENIT.
- Disciplinary actions for delayed or non-submission apply as per Exchange circular NSE/INSP/73792.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a compliance reminder referencing prior circulars NSE/INSP/73849, NSE/INSP/73850, and NSE/INSP/73792.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take corrective action for each non-compliance reported in the System Audit Report and Cyber Audit Report, and submit the ATR through ENIT by the specified due date.

## Important Dates

- **Due Date for ATR Submission:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure timely submission of ATRs on ENIT to avoid disciplinary actions and penalties outlined in Exchange guidelines.
