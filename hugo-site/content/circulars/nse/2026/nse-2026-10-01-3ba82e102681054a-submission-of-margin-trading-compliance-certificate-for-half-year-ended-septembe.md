## Summary

National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding the submission of the Margin Trading Compliance Certificate for the half-year ended September 30, 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Trading members who have obtained approval and commenced Margin Trading Facility (MTF) must submit an auditor’s certificate certifying compliance.
- Trading members who have obtained approval for MTF but have not commenced it must submit a Self-Certified certificate signed by the Compliance Officer or Designated Director.
- Submissions must be executed via the ENIT module at path: ENIT-New-Compliance > Compliance > MTR > MTR Compliance Certificate submission.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandated under Chapter 1 at Para 4.10.3 of SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024, requiring semiannual compliance verification and reporting.

## Compliance Requirements

- Submit the respective compliance certificate (Annexure I or Annexure II) through the ENIT portal on or before October 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Half-year ended: September 30, 2026
- Submission deadline: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures regulatory adherence and proper accounting/auditing controls for members offering Margin Trading Facility.
