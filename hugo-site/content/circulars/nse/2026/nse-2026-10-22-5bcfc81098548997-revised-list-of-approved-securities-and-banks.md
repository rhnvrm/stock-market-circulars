## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the revised list of approved securities, Government Securities (G-Sec), Treasury Bills (T-Bills), Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs), Sovereign Gold Bonds, open-ended mutual funds, and empaneled banks for the Currency Derivatives segment, effective for the month of November 2026.

## Key Points

- Details updated lists across five annexures covering equity shares (non-cash liquid assets), ETFs, Sovereign Gold Bonds & G-Secs/T-Bills, open-ended mutual funds, and empaneled banks for Bank Guarantees (BGs) and Fixed Deposit Receipts (FDRs).
- G-Sec and T-Bills carry a shut period of two days; members must release them at least two days before maturity.
- Member-banks are reminded that G-Secs provided as collateral must not be reckoned for Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR) purposes or used for trading.
- Value-based prudential norms specify that the exclusion of a pledged security should not result in a margin shortfall exceeding 25% at the Clearing Member level.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic revision of market-wide limits, member-specific limits, and applicable haircut rates for eligible securities and collaterals in the Currency Derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and custodians must adhere to the updated haircut rates, limits, and approved lists for collateral maintenance.
- Ensure timely release of G-Sec/T-Bills prior to their maturity shut period.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date: November 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Directly impacts trading members and custodians regarding collateral planning, margin calculations, and acceptable security composition for the Currency Derivatives segment.
