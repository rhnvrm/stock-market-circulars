## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further equity shares of Krishival Foods Limited with effect from August 18, 2026. The issue comprises 27,72,095 equity shares resulting from the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Krishival Foods Limited
- Symbol: KRISHIVAL
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE0GGO01015 (Temporary ISIN: IN80GGO01014)
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Number of Securities: 27,72,095 equity shares
- Effective Date: August 18, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Pari Passu: Yes

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the security shall be identified by its designated codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure. Equity shares allotted in dematerialized mode were credited under temporary ISIN in accordance with relevant SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion and listing update for existing shareholders and market participants trading in Krishival Foods Limited shares.
