## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names of various schemes of LIC Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **Fund House:** LIC Mutual Fund
- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026
- **Scheme Name Changes:**
  - *LIC MF ELSS Tax Saver* changed to *LIC MF ELSS Tax Saver Fund*
  - *LIC MF Ultra Short Duration Fund* changed to *LIC MF Ultra Short Term Fund*
  - *LIC MF Low Duration Fund* changed to *LIC MF Ultra Short to Short Term Fund*
  - *LIC MF Medium to Long Duration Fund* changed to *LIC MF Medium to Long Term Fund*
  - *LIC MF Short Duration Fund* changed to *LIC MF Short Term Fund*
  - *LIC MF Banking & PSU Fund* changed to *LIC MF Banking & PSU Debt Fund*

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to communication from LIC Mutual Fund regarding scheme name updates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors are requested to note the revised scheme names for transactions and reporting on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; ensures system records reflect the updated nomenclature provided by the Asset Management Company (AMC).
