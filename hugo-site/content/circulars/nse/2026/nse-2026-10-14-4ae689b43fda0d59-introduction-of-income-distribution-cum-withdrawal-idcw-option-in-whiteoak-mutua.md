## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the introduction of the Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) option for hybrid schemes of WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- IDCW option (including IDCW Payout and IDCW Reinvestment sub-options) has been introduced for WhiteOak Capital hybrid schemes.
- Default sub-option is IDCW Reinvestment. If no option is selected, investments default to the Growth Option.
- Applicable schemes include WhiteOak Capital Balanced Advantage Fund, Balanced Hybrid Fund, Multi Asset Allocation Fund, Equity Savings Fund, and Aggressive Hybrid Fund.
- Effective date: October 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Addition of IDCW option details, policies, procedures, and taxation parameters into the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) of WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors trading via the NSE MF Invest Platform must take note of the newly available IDCW options and ISIN details for order placement.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides investors on the NSE MF platform with greater flexibility in choosing income distribution preferences for WhiteOak Capital hybrid mutual fund schemes.
