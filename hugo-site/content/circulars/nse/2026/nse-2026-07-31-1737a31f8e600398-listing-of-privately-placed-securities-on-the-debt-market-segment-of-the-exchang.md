## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the admission of privately placed commercial papers to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from July 31, 2026. This is in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations. A total of 10 commercial papers issued by prominent financial and securities firms, including HDFC Securities Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, HDB Financial Services Ltd, L&T Finance Limited, Hero FinCorp Limited, Axis Securities Limited, and HSBC Investdirect Financial Services (I) Limited, have been approved for listing.

## Key Points

- **Admission**: Admission of privately placed commercial papers on the Debt Market Segment of NSE, effective from July 31, 2026.
- **Scope**: The approvals cover 10 distinct commercial paper series with allotment dates of July 30, 2026, and July 31, 2026.
- **Issuers**: Key issuers include HSBC Investdirect Financial Services, HDFC Securities, Aditya Birla Money, HDB Financial Services, L&T Finance, Hero FinCorp, and Axis Securities.
- **Security Parameters**: Details for each security (including ISIN, Quantity, Face Value, Issue Price, Allotment Date, and Redemption Date) are specified in the Annexure.
- **Face Value**: The face value for all listed commercial papers is INR 500,000 per security.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory framework is introduced. This listing is executed under the existing provisions of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Access**: Trading members of the Exchange are notified of the admission and the designated security codes (ISINs) for dealings in these commercial papers on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange.
- **Maturity & Redemption**: Issuers must ensure compliance with redemption timelines as designated in the terms of the issue (redemption dates range from August 7, 2026, to July 29, 2027).

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates**: July 30, 2026, and July 31, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Redemption/Maturity Dates**:

  - HSBC Investdirect Financial Services (HIFSIL CP 29/10/26 Sr 28 - INE790I14IC3): October 29, 2026
  - HDFC Securities (HSL CP 29/10/2026 - INE700G14VC9): October 29, 2026
  - Aditya Birla Money (ABML CP 29/10/26 - INE865C14QU1): October 29, 2026
  - Axis Securities (ASL CP 30/10/26 Sr 36 - INE110O14JF3): October 30, 2026
  - HDFC Securities (HSL CP 02/11/2026 - INE700G14VE5): November 2, 2026

  - L&T Finance (LTFL CP 29/07/27 - INE498L14GE7): July 29, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Provides institutional investors with regulated trading and exit options, enhancing the secondary market liquidity of these short-term debt instruments.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading members can commence dealings in these commercial papers on the Debt Market Segment starting July 31, 2026, under their designated codes and ISINs.
