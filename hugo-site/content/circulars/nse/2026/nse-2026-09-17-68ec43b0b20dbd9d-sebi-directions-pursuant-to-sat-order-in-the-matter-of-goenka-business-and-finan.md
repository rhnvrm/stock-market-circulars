## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to a Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order in the matter of Goenka Business and Finance Limited. Hon'ble SAT stayed the impugned SEBI order subject to the deposit of the full penalty, which has now been paid by the appellant.

## Key Points

- Reference to SEBI Order No. WTM/AS/IVD-2/ID16/32460/2026-27 dated June 30, 2026, which restrained Goenka Business and Finance Limited.
- Hon'ble SAT vide order dated September 04, 2026, stayed the impugned order subject to the deposit of full penalty.
- SEBI confirmed via email on September 17, 2026, that the appellant has paid the penalty amount.

## Regulatory Changes

- Stay of restraint/debarment against Goenka Business and Finance Limited following penalty compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the compliance status and check the consolidated list of debarred entities on the Exchange website.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 17, 2026
- SAT Order Date: September 04, 2026
- Previous SEBI Order Date: June 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Clarifies the regulatory standing of Goenka Business and Finance Limited following penalty payment and SAT stay order.
