## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that a special live trading session (Muhurat Trading) on account of Diwali will be held on Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Special live trading session on account of Muhurat trading on Diwali.
- Date of session: Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- Includes pre-open, normal market, block deal, special pre-open, call auction, closing, and post-close sessions with detailed timings.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine scheduling circular for the annual Diwali Muhurat trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members need to take note of the special trading schedule and timings for November 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 19, 2026
- Muhurat Trading Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants to partake in the symbolic and auspicious Diwali Muhurat trading session with defined special timings across various market segments.
