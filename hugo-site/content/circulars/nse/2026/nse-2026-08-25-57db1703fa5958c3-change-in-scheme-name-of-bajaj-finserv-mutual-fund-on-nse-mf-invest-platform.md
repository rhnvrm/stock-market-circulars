## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for Bajaj Finserv Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Bajaj Finserv ELSS Tax Saver Fund renamed to Bajaj Finserv ELSS - Tax Saver Fund
- Bajaj Finserv Low Duration Fund renamed to Bajaj Finserv Ultra Short to Short Term Fund
- Bajaj Finserv Large and Mid Cap Fund renamed to Bajaj Finserv Large & Mid Cap Fund
- Bajaj Finserv Banking and PSU Fund renamed to Bajaj Finserv Banking and PSU Debt Fund

## Regulatory Changes

Changes are made pursuant to Part IV of Chapter 3 relating to Categorization and Rationalization of mutual fund schemes of the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

AMCs and relevant platforms need to update the scheme names across records. The change is not considered a fundamental attribute change.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Notice Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; informational update for investors and trading members.
