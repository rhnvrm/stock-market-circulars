## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the takeover offer under the Tender Offer scheme for Ahimsa Industries Limited. The acquirer, Mr. Guruprasad Seetharam Kudva, has approached the Exchange to use the web-based bidding platform.

## Key Points

- Security Symbol: AHIMSA
- Series: TO
- ISIN: INE136T01014
- Issue Period: 18-Sep-2026 to 01-Oct-2026
- Issue Size: Up to 14,23,238 Equity shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 25/- per share
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Lot Size: 3000 Equity shares
- Merchant Banker: Rarever Financial Advisors Private Limited
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. The offer is in accordance with the Tender Offer Scheme Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can participate via the web-based IPO bidding facility using existing user IDs or admin terminals. Order modification (quantity) and cancellation are not permitted for takeover offers.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: 18-Sep-2026
- Issue Closure Date: 01-Oct-2026
- Bidding Time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.
- Custodial Confirmation Time (Issue closure day): 9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders of Ahimsa Industries Limited to tender their shares through trading members on the NSE web-based bidding platform during the specified issue period.
