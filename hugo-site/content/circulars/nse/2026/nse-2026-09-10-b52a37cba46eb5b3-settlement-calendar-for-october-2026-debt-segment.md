## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar for the Debt Segment and Debt Auction Segment (Settlement Type 'I' and '2') for the month of October 2026 in pursuance of Regulation 6.2 of NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997).

## Key Points

- Outlines daily settlement dates and trade dates for October 2026 under rolling T+1 settlement for the Debt Segment.
- Covers Annexure 'A' for Debt Settlement (Settlement Type 'I') and Annexure 'B' for Debt Auction Settlement (Settlement Type '2').
- Provides operational schedule for members, custodians, and PCMs participating in the debt segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine operational calendar notification.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must adhere to the specified trade dates and daily settlement dates for October 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective for trade dates spanning October 2026, starting from 01-Oct-2026 through 30-Oct-2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for market participants dealing in the debt segment during October 2026.
