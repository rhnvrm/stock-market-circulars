## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for multiple companies, effective from August 24, 2026. The securities include equity shares allotted under ESOP for companies such as Brigade Enterprises Limited, ITC Limited, and others.

## Key Points

- Further issues of equity shares admitted to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment.
- Effective date for trading: August 24, 2026.
- Companies involved include Brigade Enterprises Limited, ITC Limited, Ivalue Infosolutions Limited, Kfin Technologies Limited, Orbit Exports Limited, Rishabh Instruments Limited, Keystone Realtors Limited, Sai Life Sciences Limited, Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited, and Sona BLW Precision Forgings Limited.
- Shares allotted primarily under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing notification pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing/Trading: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing of shares issued through ESOP exercises, with negligible direct market impact.
