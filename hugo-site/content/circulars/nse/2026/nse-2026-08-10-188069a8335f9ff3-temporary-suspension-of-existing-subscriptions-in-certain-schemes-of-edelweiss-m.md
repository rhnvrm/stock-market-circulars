## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of existing systematic subscriptions, including Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP), under certain designated schemes of Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. This suspension is effective from the post-cutoff of August 11, 2026, following communication from Edelweiss Mutual Fund that their available headroom for overseas investments is approaching the permissible threshold limit set as of February 1, 2022.

## Key Points

- **Temporary Suspension of Systematic Registrations:** All existing Systematic Investment Plans (SIP/XSIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) under designated schemes are paused and will not be triggered or processed.
- **Permitted Transactions:** Redemption, Switch-out, and STP-out transactions continue to be permitted without any amount restrictions.
- **Designated Schemes Affected:**
  1. Edelweiss ASEAN Equity Off-shore Fund
  2. Edelweiss Greater China Equity Off-shore Fund
  3. Edelweiss US Technology Equity Fund of Fund
  4. Edelweiss Emerging Markets Opportunities Equity Offshore Fund
  5. Edelweiss Europe Dynamic Equity Offshore Fund
  6. Edelweiss US Value Equity Off-shore Fund
  7. Edelweiss MSCI India Domestic & World Healthcare 45 Index Fund

## Regulatory Changes

- The suspension is in continuation of the notice-cum-addendum dated July 09, 2026, and is pursuant to SEBI letter no. `SEBI/HO/OW/IMD-II/DOF3/25095/2022` dated June 17, 2022. This letter clarified that mutual funds can resume subscriptions and make investments in overseas funds/securities up to the headroom available without breaching the overseas investment limits as of February 1, 2022, at the mutual fund level. Edelweiss AMC is temporarily suspending subscriptions because this headroom is now approaching the permissible threshold.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members/MF Partners:** Members are advised to pause existing systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP/STP) on the NSE MF Invest platform for the designated schemes. No triggers should be processed post the cutoff of August 11, 2026.
- **Systems/Operations:** Systems must be configured to block new and existing systematic inflows into the affected schemes while allowing uninterrupted redemptions and switch-outs.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Effective Date of Suspension:** August 11, 2026 (post-cutoff / close of business hours)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** This halts systematic domestic investment inflows into seven key international and healthcare funds managed by Edelweiss Mutual Fund. It will restrict investors from increasing exposure to these specific schemes until additional overseas headroom becomes available.
- **Operational Impact:** Low to moderate. Automated systematic investment execution on the NSE MF Invest Platform must be temporarily deactivated for these schemes.
