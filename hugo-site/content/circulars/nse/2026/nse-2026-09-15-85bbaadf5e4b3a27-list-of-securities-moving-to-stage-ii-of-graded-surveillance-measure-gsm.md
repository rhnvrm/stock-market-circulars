## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that DIGJAM LIMITED (Symbol: DIGJAMLMTD, ISIN: INE731U01028) will be moved to Stage II of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) effective from September 16, 2026. The security will also undergo a series change from EQ to BE.

## Key Points

- Security moving to Stage II of GSM: DIGJAM LIMITED (DIGJAMLMTD).
- Effective Date: September 16, 2026.
- Series change from EQ to BE.
- Trading will be available in Trade for Trade with a price band of 5% or lower.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value to be deposited by buyers.

## Regulatory Changes

The security is subjected to GSM Stage II surveillance actions pursuant to the exchange's ongoing surveillance framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must take adequate precautions while trading in the security. Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed. Buyers are required to deposit an Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of the trade value.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in DIGJAM LIMITED will face stricter liquidity and settlement constraints due to trade-to-trade delivery requirements, 5% price bands, and the 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit for buyers.
