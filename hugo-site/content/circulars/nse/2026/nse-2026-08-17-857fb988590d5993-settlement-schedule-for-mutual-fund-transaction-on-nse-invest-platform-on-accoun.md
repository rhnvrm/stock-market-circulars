## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the revised settlement schedule for mutual fund transactions on the NSE Invest Platform on account of the clearing holiday (Id-E-Milad) on August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Liquid and debt scheme subscriptions received on August 25, 2026 (S 2026159), will settle on August 25, 2026, with units credited on August 27, 2026.
- There will be no liquid subscription orders and settlements on August 26, 2026.
- Normal subscriptions placed on August 25 and August 26, 2026, will have their unit pay-in/pay-out on August 27, 2026.
- Redemption units for orders placed on August 26, 2026, will transfer on the same day, while redemption fund payouts will follow scheme category guidelines (T+1 to T+8).

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of standard settlement cycles for mutual fund transactions on NSE Invest Platform due to clearing holiday on August 26, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must take note of the revised timelines for funds pay-in, subscription order dates, and unit transfer dates.

## Important Dates

- August 25, 2026: Normal and liquid subscription order date for S 2026159 and K 2026159.
- August 26, 2026: Clearing holiday (Id-E-Milad), normal subscription order date S 2026160, and redemption order date U 2026160.
- August 27, 2026: Unit pay-in and pay-out date for normal subscriptions; funds settled to AMC for liquid/debt schemes.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth processing and transparency of mutual fund orders, subscriptions, and redemptions around the clearing holiday.
