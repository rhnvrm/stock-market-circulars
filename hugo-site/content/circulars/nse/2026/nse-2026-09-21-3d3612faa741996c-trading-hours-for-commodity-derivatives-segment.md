## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the trading hours for the Commodity Derivatives segment, specifically for Internationally Reference-able Non-Agri Commodities, effective from November 02, 2026 to March 12, 2027.

## Key Points

- Applicable period: November 02, 2026 to March 12, 2027 (both days inclusive).
- Commodity Category: Internationally Reference-able Non-Agri Commodities.
- Trade Start Time: 9:00 AM.
- Trade End Time: 11:55 PM.
- Trade modification end time: 11:59 PM.
- Position limit/Collateral value Set up Cut off end time: 11:59 PM.
- Timings are subject to change based on Trading Holidays and Muhurat Trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- None; continuation of seasonal trading hours referencing SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CDMRD/DMP/CIR/P/2018/146.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised timings for the specified period.

## Important Dates

- Effective Start Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective End Date: March 12, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; standard seasonal adjustment for daylight saving or international alignment of commodity trading hours.
