## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that ICICI Prudential Contra Fund schemes from ICICI Mutual Fund will be available for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes covered include Growth, IDCW, IDCW Payout, Direct Plan - Growth, Direct Plan - IDCW, and Direct Plan - IDCW Payout.
- Applicable ISINs: INF109K1A658, INF109K1A666, INF109K1A674, INF109K1A682, INF109K1A690, and INF109K1A708.
- Category: Equity.
- Effective date for trading and transactions: October 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants should update their systems and records regarding the availability of these schemes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with expanded access to ICICI Prudential Contra Fund options through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
