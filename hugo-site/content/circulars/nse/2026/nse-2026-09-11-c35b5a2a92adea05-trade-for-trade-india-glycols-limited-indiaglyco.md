## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that India Glycols Limited (INDIAGLYCO) will be transferred from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ) effective September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: India Glycols Limited
- Symbol: INDIAGLYCO
- Transfer from: Trade for trade segment (series: BE)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (series: EQ)
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026
- Reference: Previous circular NSE/CML/75969 dated August 25, 2026, and SEBI guidelines CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of trading series based on regulatory guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants need to update their trading terminals and systems to reflect the change in series for INDIAGLYCO from BE to EQ.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The transfer to the rolling segment (EQ) allows normal rolling settlement for transactions in India Glycols Limited, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility for market participants.
