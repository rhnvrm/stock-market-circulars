## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the suspension of trading in units of SBI Mutual Fund (FMP) - Series 50 on account of redemption.

## Key Points

- Securities will be suspended from trading pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Affected schemes include SBI Mutual Fund - (FMP) - Series 50 across various options (RP-G, RP-IDCW Payout, DP-G, DP-).
- ISIN Codes affected: INF200KA1Z49, INF200KA1Z56, INF200KA1Z64, INF200KA1Z72.

## Regulatory Changes

- Execution in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must note the closure of trading in the specified scheme units.

## Important Dates

- Closing hours of trading: September 22, 2026
- Suspension Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine closure of mature fixed maturity plans; no broader market impact.
