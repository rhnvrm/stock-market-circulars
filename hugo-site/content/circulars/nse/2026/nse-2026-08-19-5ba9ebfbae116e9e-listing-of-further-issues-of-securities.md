## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of securities for several companies effective from August 20, 2026, following allotments under employee stock option plans (ESOP) and other corporate actions.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 20, 2026.
- Covers multiple companies including Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, Allied Digital Services Limited, Coforge Limited, Prataap Snacks Limited, and others.
- Securities admitted are primarily equity shares allotted under ESOP.
- Trading will take place under designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular Annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes as per the Annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 19, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate capital expansion via ESOP allotments with negligible immediate market disruption or volatility.
