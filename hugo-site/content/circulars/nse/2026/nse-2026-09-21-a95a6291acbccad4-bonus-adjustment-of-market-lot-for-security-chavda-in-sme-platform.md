## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the adjustment of the market lot for CHAVDA INFRA LIMITED on the SME Platform on account of a 1:1 bonus issue.

## Key Points

- Security Symbol: CHAVDA
- Company Name: CHAVDA INFRA LIMITED
- Type of Corporate Action: BONUS (Ratio 1:1)
- Adjustment Factor: 2
- Ex-date / Effective Date: September 24, 2026
- Record Date: September 24, 2026
- Adjusted Market Lot: 2000

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to load the updated security.gz / nnf_security.gz / MII - Security File.gz file in the trading application before trading on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-date / Effective Date: September 24, 2026
- Record Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must update their security files to ensure smooth execution of trades with the revised market lot of 2000 for CHAVDA on the SME platform.
