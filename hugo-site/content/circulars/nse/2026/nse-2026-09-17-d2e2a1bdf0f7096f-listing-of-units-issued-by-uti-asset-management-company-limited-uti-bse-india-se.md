## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by UTI Asset Management Company Limited under the UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Exchange Traded Fund, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Exchange Traded Fund
- Symbol: SECLEDBETA
- ISIN: INF789F1AH42
- Number of Units: 4,348,792
- Issue Price: Rs. 14.15 per unit
- Face Value / Paid Up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of the newly allotted ETF units on the National Stock Exchange capital market segment.
