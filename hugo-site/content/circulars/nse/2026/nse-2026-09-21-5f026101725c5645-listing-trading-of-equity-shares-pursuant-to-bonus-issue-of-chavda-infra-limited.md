## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and trading of equity shares of Chavda Infra Limited pursuant to its bonus issue. A total of 3,26,56,000 equity shares are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective from September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Chavda Infra Limited
- Symbol: CHAVDA
- ISIN: INE0PT101017
- Record Date: September 24, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: September 25, 2026
- Number of Bonus Securities: 3,26,56,000
- Distinctive Number Range: 32656001 to 65312000

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members shall ensure that trading is conducted using the designated security codes and verify distinctive number ranges where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 24, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The admission of bonus shares expands the trading float and liquidity of Chavda Infra Limited on the National Stock Exchange.
