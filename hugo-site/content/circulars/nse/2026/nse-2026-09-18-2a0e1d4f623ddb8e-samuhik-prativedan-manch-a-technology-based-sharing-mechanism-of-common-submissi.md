## Summary

NSE has issued a circular extending the 'Samuhik Prativedan Manch'—a technology-based sharing mechanism for common compliance submissions—to Self-Clearing Members (SCMs) and Clearing Members (CMs) of Clearing Corporations who are also stock brokers of NSE. Effective in a phased manner starting September 30, 2026, the mechanism eliminates the requirement to submit specific periodic and event-based reports separately to each Clearing Corporation.

## Key Points

- The Samuhik Prativedan Manch was originally introduced by SEBI and Stock Exchanges on July 18, 2025, for stock brokers.
- SEBI (vide PR No. 58/2026 dated September 17, 2026) has now extended this sharing mechanism to Clearing Members and Self-Clearing Members.
- Implementation begins in a phased manner with effect from September 30, 2026.
- In Phase 1, NSE stock brokers who are also SCMs or CMs are exempt from submitting 14 specific compliance reports/submissions separately to Clearing Corporations where they are registered; NSE will share these submissions directly.

## Regulatory Changes

- Unified compliance sharing via Samuhik Prativedan Manch is expanded to cover Clearing Corporations alongside stock exchanges.
- Reduction of redundant filings for dual-registered entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Periodic submissions no longer required to be submitted to CCs from immediate effect include:
  1. Submission of Half-yearly Net Worth certificate (Within 60 days from half-year ending September 30th and March 31st or extended timelines).
  2. Submission of Annual Returns (Annual by October 31st or extended timelines).
- Ongoing membership compliances (such as PAN updates of KMPs/Directors, change in non-designated/designated directors, change in shareholding, change in control, constitution changes, appointment/change of Compliance Officer, change in addresses, member name change, NOC for WOS/JV in GIFT IFSC/Abroad, and reporting of bank accounts) will not be required to be submitted to CC after standardisation of formats, with a separate circular to announce the implementation date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 18, 2026
- Implementation Date (Phase 1): September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Significantly reduces operational overhead and compliance friction for Clearing Members and Self-Clearing Members by centralizing reporting through a single technology portal.
