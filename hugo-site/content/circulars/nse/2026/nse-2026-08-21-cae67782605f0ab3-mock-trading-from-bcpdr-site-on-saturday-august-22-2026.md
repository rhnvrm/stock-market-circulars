## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) members from the BCP/DR site on Saturday, August 22, 2026. The mock session will be followed by a Re-Log in session from the primary site to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mock trading schedule (Saturday, August 22, 2026): Normal Market from BCP Site (09:15 hrs to 17:00 hrs).
- Re-Log in session from Primary site: 18:30 hrs to 19:00 hrs.
- Exchange will perform a non-graceful shutdown from the primary site during the mock session to simulate disaster recovery switchover.
- All outstanding orders will be purged before the start of trading from the BCP site.
- No financial obligations or fund pay-ins/pay-outs will result from trades executed during mock sessions.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine operational mock trading circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are required to participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, August 24, 2026.
- Members using NNF software must manually clear outstanding orders of session 1 in their systems before trading from the BCP site.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 22, 2026
- Live Operations Resume: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures member systems are prepared for disaster recovery switchovers and avoids login/connectivity issues on subsequent trading days.
