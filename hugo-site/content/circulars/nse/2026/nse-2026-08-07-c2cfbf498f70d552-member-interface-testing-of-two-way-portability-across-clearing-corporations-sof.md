## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) is conducting a mock session on August 08, 2026, to test the Software as a Service (SaaS) model setup. This is in accordance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/P/CIR/2023/192 dated December 20, 2023, regarding Business Continuity for Clearing Corporations through the SaaS Model, which mandates quarterly mock sessions. The testing aims to familiarize and train members on the system interfaces and functionalities for two-way portability across Clearing Corporations. NCL will operate using the SaaS setup (NCL@ICCL) during the mock session. Members are urged to participate actively and test all relevant functionalities.

## Key Points

- **Mock Session Objective:** To test the Software as a Service (SaaS) setup and familiarize clearing members with the interfaces and associated functionalities for two-way portability.
- **SEBI Mandate:** The session complies with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/P/CIR/2023/192 dated December 20, 2023, requiring quarterly mock SaaS sessions.
- **SaaS Invocation & Operation:** NCL will invoke the SaaS setup between 13:45 hrs and 14:15 hrs, and will be fully operational via the SaaS setup (NCL@ICCL) from 14:15 hrs to 15:30 hrs on August 08, 2026.
- **Reference Guidelines:** Guidelines and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for accessing the interface are detailed in NCL circular no. 025/2023 (Download No: NCL/CD/56357) dated April 13, 2023.
- **Login Credentials:** Members who have already obtained login credentials under the April 2023 circular can use them. Other members must email support@nsccl.co.in to request credentials.
- **Key Access Portals:**
  - RTRMS Link: https://isrm.connect2nsccl.com/stocks/jsp/rms/
  - Collateral Link: https://isrmcoll.connect2nsccl.com/application/applogin/login.aspx
  - Extranet Link: https://isrmxnet.connect2nsccl.com/Extranet_Login.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

- **SaaS Business Continuity Mandate:** Implements the SEBI directive (SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/P/CIR/2023/192) for ensuring business continuity in clearing corporations using a SaaS-based model (NCL@ICCL) and requiring at least one mock training session every quarter.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Mandatory Participation:** All clearing members are requested to actively participate on the mock day and test all functionalities associated with the SaaS setup.
- **Credential Verification:** Members must verify that they have login credentials. If not, they must immediately request them by emailing support@nsccl.co.in.
- **SOP Adherence:** Members must follow standard operating procedures issued in NCL circular no. 025/2023 dated April 13, 2023.

## Important Dates

- **August 08, 2026:** Date of the mock session:
  - **13:45 hrs – 14:15 hrs:** NCL invokes SaaS setup.
  - **14:15 hrs – 15:30 hrs:** NCL operational using SaaS setup (NCL@ICCL).

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Low to Medium. It is a scheduled mock session outside normal trading/settlement hours. However, it requires active preparation and resource allocation by members to perform systems testing.
- **System Stability & Resilience:** High long-term positive impact. Conducting these mock drills ensures that the two-way portability and disaster recovery/business continuity mechanisms function seamlessly during actual contingencies.
