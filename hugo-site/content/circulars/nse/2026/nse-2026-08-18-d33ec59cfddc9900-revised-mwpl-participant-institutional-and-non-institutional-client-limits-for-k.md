## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the revision of Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) and position limits for Participants, Institutional clients, and Non-Institutional clients for symbol KIRLPNU under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme.

## Key Points

- Symbol: KIRLPNU
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026
- Market Wide Position Limit increased from 3,975,106 to 7,950,213 shares.
- Institutional Client Limit increased from 397,510 to 795,021 shares.
- Participant Limit increased from 397,510 to 795,021 shares.
- Non-Institutional Client Limit increased from 39,751 to 79,502 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

Position limits for the symbol KIRLPNU in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme have been doubled following previous circular references.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians must take note of the revised limits and ensure compliance while executing transactions in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme for KIRLPNU.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 18, 2026
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Allows higher participation and position capacity for market participants in the SLB segment for KIRLPNU.
