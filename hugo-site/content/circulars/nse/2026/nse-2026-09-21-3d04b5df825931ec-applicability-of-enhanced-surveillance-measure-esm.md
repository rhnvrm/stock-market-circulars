## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the criteria are subject to additional margins, shifting to the trade-for-trade segment, or moving between stages.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 23, 2026, on open positions as on September 22, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 22, 2026.
- Bilcare Limited (Symbol: BI) has moved from ESM Stage I to Stage II.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) stages, margin requirements, and movement of eligible securities into trade-for-trade settlement and periodic call auction mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities, and note the shift to trade-for-trade settlement.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage Shifts / Price Bands / Margins: September 22, 2026 / September 23, 2026
- Circular Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in securities under the ESM framework face increased margin obligations and trading restrictions (trade-for-trade and periodic call auction) designed to address excessive volatility and enhance market integrity.
