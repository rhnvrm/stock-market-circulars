## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities for two companies, Housing & Urban Development Corporation Limited and Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- **Housing & Urban Development Corporation Limited**: ISIN INE031A09FB7, suspension effective from October 05, 2026, due to redemption.
- **Power Grid Corporation of India Limited**: ISIN INE752E07MA2, suspension effective from October 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Action taken in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members take note of the suspension of trading in the specified non-convertible securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: October 01, 2026
- **Suspension Date**: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for securities undergoing redemption with minimal impact on regular equity or active debt market trading.
