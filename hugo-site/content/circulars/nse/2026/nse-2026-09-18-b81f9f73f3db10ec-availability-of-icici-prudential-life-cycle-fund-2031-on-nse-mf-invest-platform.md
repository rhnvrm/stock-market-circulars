## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the reopening of ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031 for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) facility on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031 (Growth and Direct Plan - Growth options).
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026.
- Facilities Available: Subscriptions, redemptions, and STP facility.
- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to ensure correct ISINs are used for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless access for members and investors to trade in the specified ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund scheme through the NSE platform.
