## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) to inform members about a revised settlement schedule. Due to a settlement holiday on August 26, 2026, multiple settlements have been consolidated and rescheduled to take place on August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- A settlement holiday is observed on August 26, 2026.
- Three specific settlements (L 2026160, P 2026850, L 2026161) originally affected by the holiday will all be processed on August 27, 2026.
- Specific pay-in and pay-out timings have been designated for each settlement to ensure smooth operations.
- Participants are advised to note revised pay-in cut-off timings at the depositories' end.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no structural regulatory changes; this is purely an operational adjustment to the settlement calendar.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians must adjust their back-office systems and ensure securities and funds are available according to the revised pay-in cut-off timings on August 27, 2026, to prevent settlement failures.

## Important Dates

- **August 25-26, 2026:** Transaction dates for the affected settlements.
- **August 26, 2026:** Settlement Holiday.
- **August 27, 2026:** Revised Settlement Date for multiple settlements.
  - L 2026160: Pay-in at 08:00 hrs, Pay-out at 09:30 hrs
  - P 2026850: Pay-in at 08:30 hrs, Pay-out at 09:45 hrs
  - L 2026161: Pay-in at 10:15 hrs, Pay-out at 12:00 hrs

## Impact Assessment

The impact is strictly operational. Custodians and clearing members handling SLBS trades need to coordinate closely with depositories to meet the tight, staggered pay-in deadlines on the morning of August 27, 2026.
