## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued circular Download Ref No: NSE/CML/75470 dated July 30, 2026, announcing the transfer of equity shares of Xtranet Technologies Limited from the trade-for-trade segment to the rolling segment. Pursuant to SEBI guidelines, the shares will move from Series BE to Series EQ with effect from August 13, 2026. This transition follows the initial public offering (IPO) listing of the company.

## Key Points

- **Company Name**: Xtranet Technologies Limited
- **Symbol (Ticker)**: XTRANET
- **Segment Transfer**: Shifting from trade-for-trade segment (Series: BE) to rolling segment (Series: EQ).
- **Preceding Circular**: Reference is made to the Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/75450 dated July 29, 2026.
- **Regulatory Basis**: Pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, which governs the trading segments for newly listed IPO shares.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulations are introduced. The transfer is a standard operational procedure executed under existing SEBI guidelines (circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012) which mandate that newly listed shares are placed in the trade-for-trade segment for a certain duration before transitioning to the normal rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members**: Exchange members must update their back-office systems and trading terminals to reflect the change in series from BE to EQ for the symbol XTRANET starting from the effective date.
- **For Investors**: Transactions in XTRANET shares will no longer be restricted to gross settlement (trade-for-trade), allowing netting of buy and sell transactions within the same settlement cycle under the rolling segment.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 30, 2026
- **Preceding Circular Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Effective Date of Transfer**: August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Liquidity**: Transitioning to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) is expected to significantly enhance the trading liquidity of Xtranet Technologies Limited's shares, as intraday trading and netting of trades will now be permitted.
- **Margin and Settlement**: Transactions will be settled on a T+1 rolling basis with standard margin requirements, replacing the 100% upfront margin and delivery-only requirements of the trade-for-trade (BE) segment.
- **Market Impact**: Minimal broader market impact, but highly positive for shareholders and traders of XTRANET.
