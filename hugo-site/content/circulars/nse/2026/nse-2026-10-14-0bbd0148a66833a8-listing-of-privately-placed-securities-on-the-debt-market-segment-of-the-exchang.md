## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the admission of privately placed securities, including commercial papers and other debt securities, to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh issuance and listing of commercial papers and debt securities by multiple corporate and financial entities.
- Effective listing date: October 14, 2026.
- Participating issuers include Afcons Infrastructure Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, CEAT Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, NABARD, Astec LifeSciences Limited, Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited, L&T Finance Limited, and Alpha Alternatives Financial Services Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine listing notification issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and issuers must comply with standard operational, clearing, and settlement guidelines applicable to the debt market segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Listing Date: October 14, 2026
- Allotment Dates: October 2026 (various dates)
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from November 2026 through October 2036 across respective tranches.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances liquidity and trading transparency for privately placed debt instruments and commercial papers in the secondary market.
