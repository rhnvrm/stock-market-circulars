## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed Government Securities (6.57% GS 2033 and 6.20% GS 2029) on the Capital Market Segment with effect from August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted: GOI LOAN 6.57% 2033 (ISIN: IN0020260066) and GOI LOAN 6.20% 2029 (ISIN: IN0020260058).
- Effective date of trading: August 19, 2026.
- Designated security codes and respective lot sizes as specified in the annexure apply.

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the specified lot sizes and security codes for trading these government securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

 Expands the availability and liquidity of government securities on the exchange segments for market participants.
