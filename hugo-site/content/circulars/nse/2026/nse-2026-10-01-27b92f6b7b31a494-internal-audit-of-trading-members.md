## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has outlined circular guidelines and certificate formats for the internal audit of trading members. The circular covers audit requirements across segments including Capital Market, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Debt Segment, Securities Lending & Borrowing, and Commodity Derivatives, along with sampling directives for material non-compliances.

## Key Points

- Trading members must undergo half-yearly internal audits conducted by eligible Chartered Accountants, Company Secretaries, or Cost and Management Accountants.
- Audit scope verifies adherence to the SEBI Act 1992, Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act 1956, SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations 1992, and NSE bye-laws.
- Audit firms must satisfy eligibility criteria prescribed under Exchange Circular no. NSE/INSP/58709 dated September 29, 2023.
- Specific sampling guidelines apply for repeat violations and material non-compliances observed during inspections.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standardizes internal audit certification frameworks and reporting structures for trading members across all exchange segments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must facilitate thorough audits covering all applicable operational segments and submit valid audit certificates complete with UDIN.
- Auditors must declare independence and absence of conflict of interest with the trading member.

## Important Dates

- Applicable for ongoing half-yearly compliance and audit inspection periods (such as October 2025 to March 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances regulatory oversight, improves internal governance, and ensures consistent compliance across all trading members on the exchange.
