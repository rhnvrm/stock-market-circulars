## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of the Jio BlackRock Balanced Advantage Fund NFO from Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Fund Name: Jio BlackRock Balanced Advantage Fund
- NFO Period: September 11, 2026 to September 25, 2026 (till 06:00 pm)
- Available Plans: Direct Growth (ISIN: INF22M001366) and Regular Growth (ISIN: INF22M001374)
- Category: Equity
- Allotment Date: October 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 30, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 11, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 25, 2026
- Clear Funds Submission Deadline: September 30, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables members and investors to transact in the new fund offer of Jio BlackRock Balanced Advantage Fund via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
