## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued operational guidelines regarding trading timings, collateral allocations, margin reporting, and settlement schedules for the Diwali Muhurat Trading session scheduled on Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Muhurat Trading Date:** Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- **CP Code Modification:** Timings for custodian participant (CP) code modification in NCMS for trades done on November 08, 2026, are from 14:20 to 15:40.
- **Risk Parameters:** Two risk parameter files based on securities prices at previous close and end of day will be provided.
- **Trade Confirmations & EPI:** EPI of Funds and Securities cut-off is 17:45 hrs; Custodian trade confirmation end time is 10:00 hrs on T+1 day (November 09, 2026).
- **Collateral Timings:** Specific time windows established for cash addition (12:15 - 16:15), collateral allocation (11:45 - 19:00), securities release requests (cut-off 16:15), and cash release (12:15 - 16:15).
- **Settlement Schedule:** Pay-in/pay-out transactions for trade dates November 06, 2026, and November 08, 2026, will be settled on November 09, 2026 across multiple settlement batches.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revised settlement calendars and multi-batch settlement schedules on November 09, 2026, to accommodate trades executed during the Muhurat Trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must adhere to revised cut-off times for CP code modification, EPI of funds/securities, collateral allocation/release, and depository pay-in on November 08 and November 09, 2026.
- Adhere to revised client margin reporting cut-off schedules for trade dates between November 02, 2026, and November 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Trade Date (Muhurat Session):** November 08, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 09, 2026
- **Client Margin Reporting Cut-off Dates:** November 08, 09, 11, 12, 13, and 16, 2026 depending on the respective trade dates.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational workflows, timely settlement, and adequate collateral management for market participants during the festive holiday trading session.
