## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Macpower CNC Machines Limited has satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) Stage - I.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. August 31, 2026 on all open positions as on August 28, 2026 and new positions created from August 31, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. August 31, 2026.
- Macpower CNC Machines Limited (INE155Z01011) moved from STASM to LTASM framework.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) criteria involving 100% margins and movement across stages as per exchange surveillance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open positions and new positions created from August 31, 2026 onwards for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions / Margins: August 31, 2026 (on open positions as on August 28, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for affected securities may impact trading liquidity and positions for market participants.
