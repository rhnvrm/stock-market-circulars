## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular detailing trading restrictions for companies that have failed to pay the outstanding Annual Listing Fees (ALF) for the financial year ended 2025-26, pursuant to Regulation 14 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. Listed entities that do not pay the outstanding fees on or before September 7, 2026, will face restrictions in normal segment trading and will be shifted to the Trade for Trade category (Series: BE/BZ), allowing trading only on the first trading day of every week starting mid-September 2026.

## Key Points

- **Requirement**: Regulation 14 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 requires listed entities to pay Annual Listing Fees (ALF) to recognized Stock Exchanges.
- **Deadline**: Non-compliant companies must make the ALF payment on or before September 7, 2026.
- **First Restriction**: Trading in the affected securities (except those temporarily suspended for capital reduction) in the normal segment (EQ Series) will be restricted starting September 10, 2026.
- **Weekly Trading Restriction**: Affected securities will be allowed to trade only on the first trading day of every week on a Trade for Trade basis (Series: BE/BZ) starting September 15, 2026, until compliance and payment are completed.
- **Exceptions**: Entities already suspended under SEBI SOP circulars, Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016, or on account of SDD non-compliance are listed in Annexure III and will not face additional actions under this circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of trading restrictions under **Regulation 14 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015**.
- Imposition of Trade for Trade restrictions for fee non-compliance under joint exchange protocols (Exchange Circular Ref. No. NSE/CML/2021/22 dated June 25, 2021).

## Compliance Requirements

- Non-compliant listed entities must clear all outstanding Annual Listing Fees (ALF) to the recognized Stock Exchange on or before September 7, 2026.
- Members and trading brokers must adjust order entry systems to comply with the restricted trading schedule (once-weekly, BE/BZ series) for the listed non-compliant entities.

## Important Dates

- **Payment Deadline for ALF**: September 7, 2026
- **Effective Date of EQ Series Restriction**: September 10, 2026
- **Effective Date of Trade for Trade (BE/BZ Series) Weekly Restriction**: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Nine listed companies face trading restrictions. Five of these companies (ABHISHEK, ASIL, KSERASERA, LEEL, OMKARCHEM) are already temporarily suspended due to capital reduction and will remain suspended. The active companies (CEREBRAINT, KAUSHALYA, LASA, SELMC) will experience reduced liquidity as trading is restricted to once a week on the first trading day.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading brokers must ensure system preparedness to restrict trading in the affected securities to Trade for Trade (BE/BZ series) solely on the first trading day of every week starting September 15, 2026.
