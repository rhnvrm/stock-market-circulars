## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of SBI Nifty200 Value 30 ETF FOF schemes of SBI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, effective from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** SBI Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** SBI Nifty200 Value 30 ETF FOF (Multiple Plans: Regular/Direct Growth & IDCW Payout/Reinvestment)
- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Available Facilities:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can transact in the specified schemes through the NSE MF Invest Platform in accordance with existing operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 09, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to trade SBI Nifty200 Value 30 ETF FOF schemes through the NSE MF Invest platform with various investment facilities.
