## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified all members that August 11, 2026, will be a non-business day for specific mutual fund schemes from ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund and Nippon India Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Consequently, transaction activities such as Subscriptions, Redemptions, Switches, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs will not be permitted for these specific schemes on this date.

## Key Points

- **Non-Business Day:** August 11, 2026, is designated as a non-business day for the affected mutual fund schemes.
- **Affected Mutual Funds & Schemes:**
  - **ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund:** ICICI Prudential Global Advantage Fund
  - **Nippon India Mutual Fund:** Nippon India Japan Equity Fund
- **Transaction Restrictions:** Subscriptions, Redemptions, Switches, Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), Systematic Transfer Plans (STPs), and Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWPs) are suspended for these schemes on the NSE MF Invest platform on the specified date.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes are introduced. This is an operational announcement communicating scheme-specific non-business day suspensions based on instructions from the respective Asset Management Companies (AMCs).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members & Intermediaries:** Must ensure that no subscriptions, redemptions, switches, SIPs, STPs, or SWPs are submitted or processed for the specified schemes on August 11, 2026, via the NSE MF Invest platform.
- **System Configurations:** Front-end applications and investor portals should be configured to block or reject transactional requests for the affected schemes on the designated non-business day.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Effective Date (Non-Business Day):** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Low overall, but high for the specific transactions related to the affected schemes (ICICI Prudential Global Advantage Fund and Nippon India Japan Equity Fund) since all transactions are suspended for the day.
- **Market Impact:** Minimal/Low, as the suspension is highly localized to specific funds on a single day, with other schemes and general platform operations continuing unaffected.
