## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the final settlement price (Due Date Rate) for the Monthly Electricity Futures contract (ELECMBL) that expired on July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Contract Code:** ELECMBL
- **Due Date Rate (DDR):** Fixed at 3814.00
- **Base Unit/Quantity:** 1 MWh
- **Reference Circulars:** NCL/COM/69054 dated July 10, 2025, and NCL/COM/69390 dated July 30, 2025 (Final Settlement Procedure).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes or amendments are introduced in this circular. It represents standard operational procedure.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must use the declared DDR price of 3814.00 for final settlement and position adjustment purposes.

## Important Dates

- **July 30, 2026:** Expiry date of the ELECMBL future contract.
- **July 31, 2026:** Issuance of the DDR circular.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. Routine settlement of existing derivative contracts.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Standard ledger entries and position close-outs based on the announced rate.
