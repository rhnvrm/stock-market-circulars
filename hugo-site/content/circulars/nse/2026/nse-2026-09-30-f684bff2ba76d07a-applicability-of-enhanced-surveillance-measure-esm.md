## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for various securities. Securities satisfying the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin and shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective October 05, 2026, while certain securities move between stages effective October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (Hindcon Chemicals Limited, JHS Svendgaard Retail Ventures Limited, RTS Power Corporation Limited, Tamilnadu Telecommunication Limited) attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 05, 2026, and shift from Rolling Settlement (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade (BE/ST) segment.
- Grand Foundry Limited shifts from ESM Stage I to Stage II w.e.f. October 01, 2026, under Trade for Trade with a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of specified securities to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) and imposition of 100% margin requirements.
- Securities shifting to Stage II placed under Trade for Trade with a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 01, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure 100% margin on all open positions as on October 01, 2026, and new positions created from October 05, 2026 for applicable securities.
- Trading members to take note of segment transfers and price band restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Stage II price band / periodic call auction effective date: October 01, 2026.
- Margin and Trade-for-Trade shift effective date: October 05, 2026 (on open positions as on October 01, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened margin requirements and trading restrictions affect liquidity and trading mechanics for shortlisted securities.
