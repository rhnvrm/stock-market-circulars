## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the criteria have been shortlisted for inclusion under ESM Stage I, shifting from the Rolling Settlement segment (EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST), and attracting a 100% margin. Additionally, certain securities are moving from Stage I to Stage II under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (DAICHI, ESCONET, MANAKSTEEL, RNBDENIMS) are shortlisted under ESM Stage-I.
- Securities shifting to ESM Stage-I will attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions and new positions.
- Uravi Defence and Technology Limited (URAVIDEF) moves from ESM Stage-I to Stage-II.
- Affected securities shift from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).
- Securities shifting to Stage II will have a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework criteria on shortlisted securities.
- Shifting of securities between Rolling Settlement and Trade-for-Trade segments along with applicable margin requirements and price bands.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for applicable securities.
- Adhere to Trade-for-Trade settlement and 2% price bands under Periodic Call Auction for securities in Stage II.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ESM Stage I inclusion, margin applicability, and segment shift: September 07, 2026 (on open positions as on September 04, 2026).
- Effective Date for Stage II movement and price band applicability: September 04, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement mechanics for the shortlisted securities (including DAICHI, ESCONET, MANAKSTEEL, RNBDENIMS, and URAVIDEF) will be restricted due to Trade-for-Trade settlement, mandatory 100% margins, and price band constraints.
