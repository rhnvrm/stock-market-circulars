## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular announcing the availability of Zerodha Life Cycle Fund 2031 on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Zerodha Life Cycle Fund 2031 - Direct Growth
- ISIN: INF0R8F01240
- Category: Equity
- Facilities Available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: September 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the NSE MF Invest Platform can facilitate transactions for the aforementioned scheme in accordance with existing platform guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to trade units of Zerodha Life Cycle Fund 2031 seamlessly through the NSE MF Invest Platform with access to systematic investment and withdrawal facilities.
