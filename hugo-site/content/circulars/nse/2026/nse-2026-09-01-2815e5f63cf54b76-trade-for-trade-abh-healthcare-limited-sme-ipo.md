## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of ABH Healthcare Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM).

## Key Points

- Security: ABH Healthcare Limited (Symbol: ABH)
- Transfer from trade for trade segment (series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: September 16, 2026
- Reference to previous Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76075 dated August 31, 2026, and SEBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Pursuant to standard SEBI guidelines and previous exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note the transfer of segment for the specified security.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the security will move from the trade-for-trade segment to the normal rolling segment, enabling regular settlement cycles.
