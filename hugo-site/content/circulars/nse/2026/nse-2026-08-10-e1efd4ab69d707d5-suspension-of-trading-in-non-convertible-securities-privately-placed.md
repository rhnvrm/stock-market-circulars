## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified market participants about the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities issued by TVS Credit Services Limited and Power Finance Corporation Limited. The suspension is effective from August 11, 2026, following the redemption of these securities.

## Key Points

- **Securities Suspended**: Privately placed Non-Convertible Securities of TVS Credit Services Limited and Power Finance Corporation Limited.
- **Effective Date**: The suspension is effective starting August 11, 2026.
- **Reason for Suspension**: Both securities are suspended due to redemption.
- **Affected ISINs**:
  - TVS Credit Services Limited: `INE729N08048`
  - Power Finance Corporation Limited: `INE134E08LK2`

## Regulatory Changes

- This suspension is executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the suspension and ensure no further trading activities are executed under the specified ISINs on or after August 11, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 10, 2026
- **Suspension Effective Date**: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Low. Standard operational procedure when debt securities mature and are redeemed.
- **Issuer Impact**: None. This is a routine event for the issuers, TVS Credit Services Limited and Power Finance Corporation Limited, indicating successful completion of redemption processes for these specific series.
