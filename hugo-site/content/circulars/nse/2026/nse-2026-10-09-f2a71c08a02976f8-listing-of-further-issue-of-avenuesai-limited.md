## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of a further issue of equity shares for AvenuesAI Limited (Symbol: CCAVENUE). A total of 10,845 equity shares, resulting from the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares, will be admitted to dealings effective from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** AvenuesAI Limited
- **Symbol:** CCAVENUE
- **Security Description:** Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares
- **Number of Securities:** 10,845 equity shares
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Re. 1/- per share
- **ISIN:** INE483S01020 (Temporary ISIN: IN8483S01029)
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 3471667118 to 3471677962
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Pari Passu:** Yes
- **Lock-in Details:** Not Applicable

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; processed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members need to note the designated security codes and trading lot sizes for dealings in the further issue of shares.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 09, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion for the company with negligible immediate market impact, ensuring proper trading activation of converted fully paid-up shares.
