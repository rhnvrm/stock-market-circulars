## Summary

NSE has announced the resumption of subscriptions in the Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua Fund of Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Following a temporary suspension on July 22, 2026, to avoid breaching overseas investment limits, Baroda BNP Paribas Asset Management India Private Limited (AMC) decided to resume accepting fresh and additional subscriptions, switch-ins, and fresh registrations for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) starting August 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Resumption of Activity**: Fresh and additional subscriptions, switch-ins, and new registrations for SIP and STP have resumed on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Effective Date**: The resumption took effect from August 3, 2026.
- **Reason for Suspension**: The previous temporary suspension (notified on July 22, 2026) was implemented to prevent breaching overseas investment limits.
- **Discretionary Clause**: The AMC reserves the right to suspend subscriptions again if the headroom limits are approached, with a separate notice to be issued if that occurs.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no broader regulatory changes introduced by this circular. It updates the operational status of a specific mutual fund scheme in compliance with existing SEBI guidelines regarding overseas investment limits.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Mutual Fund Intermediaries & Members**: Trading members and distributors on the NSE MF Invest Platform must update their systems to allow transactions (fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, SIPs, and STPs) for the Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua Fund of Fund.
- **AMC Action**: Baroda BNP Paribas Asset Management India Private Limited will monitor headroom limits closely and reserve the right to suspend subscriptions as needed.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Effective Date of Resumption**: August 3, 2026
- **Previous Suspension Date**: July 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Enables investors to allocate capital into overseas equities via the Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua Fund of Fund once again, which is positive for asset management volume and retail participation.
- **Operational Workload**: Minimal operational workload for members, as they simply need to reactivate subscription and transaction flows for the specific Scheme on the platform.
