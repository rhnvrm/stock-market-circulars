## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the list of securities further admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from August 17, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026.
- Segment: Capital Market Segment.
- Includes numerous companies such as ARCL, Addi Industries, Advik Laboratories, Alfred Herbert India, Alufluoride, Amal, Amarjothi Spinning Mills, Andrew Yule & Company, and others with designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the Exchange under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the specified securities shall be identified only by their designated codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026.
- Circular Date: August 14, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the list of tradeable securities on the NSE Capital Market segment, allowing market participants to trade in the newly admitted companies' shares.
