## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the ICICI Prudential Dynamic Asset Allocation Passive FOF New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- **NFO Window:** August 26, 2026, to September 09, 2026 (till 11:30 PM).
- **Schemes Available:** 
  - ICICI Prudential Dynamic Asset Allocation Passive FOF - Regular - Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A633)
  - ICICI Prudential Dynamic Asset Allocation Passive FOF - Direct - Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A641)
- **Category:** Equity
- **Allotment Date:** September 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 11, 2026, till 12:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 26, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** September 09, 2026
- **Fund Pay-in Deadline:** September 11, 2026 (12:00 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification for trading members and investors participating in the ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund NFO on the NSE platform.
