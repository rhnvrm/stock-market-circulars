## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued procedures for the adjustment of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts in the security **INDUS TOWERS LIMITED (INDUSTOWER)**. This adjustment is pursuant to NSE Circular No. 112/2026 (Download no. NSE/FAOP/75563) dated August 04, 2026, and is necessitated by a dividend of Rs. 14/- per share.

## Key Points

- **Futures Adjustment Process**: Open positions in INDUSTOWER futures contracts will be marked-to-market on the last cum-dividend date (August 07, 2026) based on the daily settlement price. Subsequently, open positions will be carried forward at the daily settlement price reduced by the dividend amount of Rs. 14/-.
- **Ex-Dividend Trading**: From August 10, 2026 (the ex-dividend date), daily mark-to-market settlement of INDUSTOWER futures contracts will proceed under normal procedures.
- **Margin Treatment**: Begin-of-day margins on August 10, 2026, will be computed based on the adjusted carry-forward value. Intra-day margins will continue to be computed based on active traded prices.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no changes to regulatory policies; this is a standard operational adjustment for corporate action under existing exchange regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading and Clearing Members**: Must monitor client positions in INDUSTOWER futures and ensure that end-of-day processes on August 07, 2026, correctly reflect the adjusted carrying price (daily settlement price minus Rs. 14/-).
- **Risk Management Systems**: Ensure that margin calculation systems utilize the adjusted carry-forward value for begin-of-day margin calculations on August 10, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **August 07, 2026**: Last cum-dividend date and execution date for futures position price adjustments.
- **August 10, 2026**: Ex-dividend date. Adjusted positions commence trading with normal mark-to-market procedures and margins calculated from the adjusted value.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: The price of INDUSTOWER futures contracts will drop by Rs. 14 on the ex-dividend date to reflect the dividend payout. The adjustment ensures that contract holders are not unfairly penalized or rewarded by the ex-dividend price drop.
- **Operational Impact**: Clearing and trading members must verify that their back-office and risk systems correctly ingest and display the adjusted prices and updated begin-of-day margin requirements.
