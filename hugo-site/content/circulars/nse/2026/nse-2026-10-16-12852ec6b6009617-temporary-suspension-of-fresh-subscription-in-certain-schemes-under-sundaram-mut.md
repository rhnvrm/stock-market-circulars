## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and fresh registrations under Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) for certain schemes under Sundaram Mutual Fund (such as Sundaram Medium Term Fund) on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Immediate suspension of fresh lumpsum subscriptions, switch-ins, and new SIP/STP registrations for affected Sundaram Mutual Fund schemes on NSE MF Invest Platform.
- No restrictions on redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and outgoing STPs.
- Existing SIPs and STPs will continue to be processed normally at the AMC/RTA end without any changes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational restriction implemented on the NSE MF Invest Platform per communication received from Sundaram Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must note the suspension of fresh subscriptions and ensure no new SIP/STP registrations or lumpsum investments are submitted for the affected schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact on overall market operations; affects only investors seeking new subscriptions or fresh SIP/STP registrations in specific Sundaram Mutual Fund schemes.
