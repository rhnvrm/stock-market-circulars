## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs) of Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited effective from September 23, 2026, due to conversion.

## Key Points

- Security affected: Compulsorily Convertible Debentures of Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited
- Symbol: CHOLAFIN
- ISIN: INE121A08PJ0
- Reason for suspension: Conversion
- Effective date: September 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the specified security has been suspended from trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the suspension and ensure no trades are executed in the specified security from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified CCDs of Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited will be halted on the exchange platform pursuant to their conversion.
