## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from October 07, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Companies involved include Ather Energy Limited, Bajel Projects Limited, BOROSIL RENEWABLES LIMITED, Dev Information Technology Limited, Lloyds Metals And Energy Limited, Madhya Bharat Agro Products Limited, Northern Arc Capital Limited, Spectrum Electrical Industries Limited, TD Power Systems Limited, Virtuoso Optoelectronics Limited, and Yes Bank Limited.
- Shares are allotted under ESOP and Preferential Issue.
- Effective date for trading: October 07, 2026.
- Market lot for the listed securities is 1.

## Regulatory Changes

Listing of further issues of securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in such securities shall be identified only by their designated codes and lot sizes as specified in the Annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date: October 07, 2026
- Allotment Dates: Various (e.g., Sep 01, 2026 for Ather Energy; Sep 29, 2026 for Bajel Projects; Jul 30, 2026 for Borosil Renewables; Sep 11, 2026 for Dev Information Technology)

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing of further equity shares for listed companies with no significant market disruption.
