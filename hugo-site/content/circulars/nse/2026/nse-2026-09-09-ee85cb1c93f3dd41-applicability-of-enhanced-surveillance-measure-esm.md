## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities listed in Annexure I have satisfied criteria for inclusion and stage shifts under the measure, attracting increased margins, shifting to the trade-for-trade segment, or moving under specific price bands.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage - I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 11, 2026, on all open positions as on September 10, 2026, and new positions created from September 11, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 10, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The shortlisting of securities under ESM is implemented in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures and is purely on account of market surveillance, not construed as adverse action.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements and segment shifts for open and new positions in the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II price band and shifting: September 10, 2026 / September 11, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading mechanics for affected securities will be restricted due to trade-for-trade settlement and periodic call auction price bands.
