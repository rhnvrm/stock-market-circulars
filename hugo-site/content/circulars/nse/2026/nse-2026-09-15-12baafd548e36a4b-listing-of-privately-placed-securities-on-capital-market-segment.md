## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment, effective from September 16, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Effective date of trading: September 16, 2026.
- Includes fresh issues of non-convertible debentures and debt securities from various issuers including Akme Fintrade (India) Limited, Sammaan Capital Limited, Sundaram Home Finance Limited, and Dar Credit & Capital Limited.
- Trading will be identified by designated security codes and specific market lots as specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the specified securities must adhere to the designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2026
- Circular Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing of debt instruments on the exchange with no broad market impact.
