## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the provisional empanelment of M/s QICAP Management Private Limited as an algo provider for supplying algorithmic trading solutions (Whitebox and Blackbox) to trading members of the Exchange, effective from October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Vendor Name: M/s QICAP Management Private Limited
- Type of Algo: Whitebox and Blackbox
- Address: Unit No. 503-504, X-Change Plaza, Block 53, Road 5E, Zone-5, Gift City Gandhinagar Gujarat- 382050
- Contact Number: +91 9880252699
- Applicability: With immediate effect

## Regulatory Changes

- Provisional empanelment of a new algorithmic trading provider by the Exchange's Investigation Department.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members engaging with the empaneled algo provider must ensure compliance with all applicable exchange regulations, guidelines, and risk management norms governing algorithmic trading.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the list of approved algorithmic trading solution providers available to trading members, facilitating enhanced technological access for trading operations.
