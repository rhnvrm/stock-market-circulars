## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has informed members about a change in ISIN codes for two Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) managed by DSP Asset Managers Private Limited, effective August 28, 2026, following a sub-division of units from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes: DSP Gold ETF and DSP Silver ETF.
- Nature of Corporate Action: Sub-Division of units from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-.
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine ISIN update reflecting a scheme sub-division.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the new ISINs for all trades executed on and from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date / Effective Date: August 28, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for trading members and clearing houses to update security master files with new ISIN codes.
