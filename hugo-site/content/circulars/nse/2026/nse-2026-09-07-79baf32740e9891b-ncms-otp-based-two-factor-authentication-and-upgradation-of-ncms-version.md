## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the implementation of OTP-based Two Factor Authentication (2FA) and an upgraded version (4.2.5) for user logins in NCMS-EGR (covering both admin and sub-users) to enhance system security.

## Key Points

- New EXE version 4.2.5 will be available from September 12, 2026, on the member extranet/portal (`cmftp/common/NCMSSetup_425.`).
- OTP-based Two Factor Authentication (2FA) will be implemented starting September 15, 2026.
- First-time login registration requires registering mobile number and email ID.
- Entering 4 invalid OTPs will disable the user ID, requiring assistance from NSE Clearing Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory integration of OTP-based 2FA for all NCMS-EGR logins.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must download and upgrade to NCMS version 4.2.5.
- Users must register their mobile number and email ID during the initial login under the new 2FA setup.

## Important Dates

- **Version Availability Date:** September 12, 2026
- **Implementation Date for 2FA:** September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational workflows for accessing NCMS-EGR will require additional authentication steps via OTP sent to registered mobile numbers and email addresses.
