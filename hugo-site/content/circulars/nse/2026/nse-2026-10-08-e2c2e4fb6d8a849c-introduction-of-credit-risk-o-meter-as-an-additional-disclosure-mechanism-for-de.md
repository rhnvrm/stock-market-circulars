## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the introduction of the 'Credit Risk-o-Meter' as an additional mandatory disclosure mechanism for debt securities, pursuant to SEBI Circular No. HO/17/11/22(1)2026-DDHS-POD1 dated October 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Introduction of a colour-coded 'Credit Risk-o-Meter' to assist investors in assessing credit risk prior to investing in debt securities.
- Mandatory component for Offer Documents, Abridged Prospectuses, Private Placement Memorandums (PPMs), all advertisements by issuers/OBPPs, and web/mobile platforms of Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs).
- Amendments made to Chapter II-C of the Master Circular for issue and listing of Non-Convertible Securities, Securitised Debt Instruments, Security Receipts, Municipal Debt Securities and Commercial Paper (NCS Master Circular).

## Regulatory Changes

- Insertion of Chapter II-C titled “Disclosure of Credit Risk-o-Meter for debt securities” in the NCS Master Circular dated October 15, 2025.
- Insertion of Clause 14 in Annexure-XXIB under Chapter XXI of the NCS Master Circular specifying minimum disclosure requirements for each security offered on Online Bond Platforms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers of debt securities, OBPPs, Stock Exchanges, and Depositories must ensure necessary systems and processes are in place.
- Issuers and OBPPs must incorporate the Credit Risk-o-Meter in all applicable offer documents, advertisements, and trading platforms.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 08, 2026 (SEBI Circular dated October 07, 2026).
- Effective Date: The provisions of the circular shall come into force after 45 days from the date of issuance.

## Impact Assessment

Enhances transparency for retail and institutional investors in debt securities and online bond platforms by providing standardized, colour-coded credit risk visualization.
