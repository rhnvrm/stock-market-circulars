## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a corrigendum regarding the face value split of equity shares of Waterways Leisure Tourism Limited (Symbol: CORDELIA). With reference to the earlier circular NSE/CML/75804 dated August 18, 2026, the effective date for the change in face value and paid-up value has been revised to August 25, 2026, instead of August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Waterways Leisure Tourism Limited
- Symbol: CORDELIA
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Re. 1/- per share
- Revised Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The effective implementation date for the face value split of CORDELIA has been advanced by one day from August 26, 2026 to August 25, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are requested to take note of the revised effective date for settlement and trading adjustments related to CORDELIA.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: August 24, 2026
- Revised Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact on overall market operations; affects only trading participants dealing with the equity shares of Waterways Leisure Tourism Limited with respect to the revised effective date.
