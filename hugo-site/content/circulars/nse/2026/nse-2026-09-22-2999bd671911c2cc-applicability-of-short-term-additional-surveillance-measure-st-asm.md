## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II, or moved between stages, with specific margin requirements applicable w.e.f. September 23/24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: CSM, Fedders Holding Limited, Kshitij Polyline Limited, Manugraph India Limited.
- Securities moving from Stage I to Stage II: Moneyboxx Finance Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage I is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. September 24, 2026 on open positions as on September 23, 2026 and new positions.
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage II is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. September 24, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of stricter margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for listed securities meeting surveillance criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin rates (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for open positions and new positions created in the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance / Framework: September 23, 2026 / September 24, 2026 for margins

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin obligations for market participants trading in the shortlisted securities, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
