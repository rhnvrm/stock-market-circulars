## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular notifying that Invesco Mutual Fund has disabled certain schemes for lumpsum subscription, switch-ins, and fresh registrations under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective with the close of business hours on October 15, 2026. Specifically, this applies to the Invesco India Gold ETF Fund of Fund.

## Key Points

- Temporary suspension applies to lumpsum purchases, switch-ins, and fresh registrations for SIP, STP, and IDCW Transfer Plan for select schemes (e.g., Invesco India Gold ETF Fund of Fund).
- No restrictions on redemptions, switch-outs, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), or processing of already registered SIPs and STPs.
- For existing SIPs with a top-up facility, no top-up enhancements will be effected during the suspension period.
- Restriction on STP and IDCW Transfer Plan registration applies only where the scheme is a target scheme.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an operational update and temporary restriction initiated directly by the Asset Management Company (AMC).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the suspension of fresh subscriptions and new SIP/STP/IDCW transfer plan registrations in the affected Invesco Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: Close of business hours on October 15, 2026.
- Circular Date: October 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors wishing to make fresh lumpsum investments or start new SIPs/STPs in the affected Invesco schemes (such as the Gold ETF Fund of Fund) will be temporarily unable to do so on the platform until further notice. Existing investments and redemptions remain unaffected.
