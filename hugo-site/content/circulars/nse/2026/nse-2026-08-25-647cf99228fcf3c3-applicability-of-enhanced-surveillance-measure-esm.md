## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities listed in Annexure I have satisfied criteria for inclusion and will attract a minimum 100% margin along with shifting from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment effective August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (KOTARISUG, MPDL, SIMCA, SUPTANERY) are included under ESM Stage I and attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 27, 2026, on open positions as on August 26, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to Stage II (AMANTA, PANACHE, PRIZOR) shall be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 26, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 27, 2026.
- Stage II shifting securities attract a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 26, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the margin requirements and settlement segment changes for the shortlisted securities and ensure compliance with surveillance measures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II shifting / price band: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin applicability and Trade-for-Trade transfer: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the impacted securities must maintain 100% margins and accommodate trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
