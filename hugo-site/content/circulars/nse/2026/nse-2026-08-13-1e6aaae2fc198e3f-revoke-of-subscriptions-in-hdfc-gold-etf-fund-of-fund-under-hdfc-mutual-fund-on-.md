## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the resumption of subscriptions in the HDFC Gold ETF Fund of Fund under HDFC Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Following previous restrictions on lump-sum subscriptions, HDFC Mutual Fund has announced that subscriptions via lump-sum purchases and switch-ins will resume without any restriction effective August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Resumption of lump-sum purchases and switch-ins in HDFC Gold ETF Fund of Fund.
- Effective date of resumption is August 14, 2026.
- All other terms and conditions of the Scheme Information Document (SID) / Key Information Memorandum (KIM) remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an operational update regarding the lifting of temporary investment restrictions on the specific fund scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors can resume unrestricted lump-sum subscriptions and switch-ins for the scheme on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting August 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Addendum Date: August 10, 2026
- Circular Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date of Resumption: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors looking to invest in HDFC Gold ETF Fund of Fund via lump-sum or switch-ins can do so without previous limitations, restoring normal operational capabilities on the platform.
