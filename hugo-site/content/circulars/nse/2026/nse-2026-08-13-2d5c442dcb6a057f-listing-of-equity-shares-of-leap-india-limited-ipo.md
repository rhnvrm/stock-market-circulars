## Summary

LEAP India Limited's equity shares have been approved for listing and trading on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) following its Initial Public Offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Entity Name: LEAP India Limited
- Security Type: Equity Shares
- Listing Date: 13.08.2026
- Shareholding pattern filed under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of new equity shares to dealing on the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 13.08.2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of LEAP India Limited shares on NSE.
