## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular informing members about updates to the UN Security Council's 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) & Al-Qaida Sanctions List and the 1988 (2011) Taliban Sanctions List pursuant to Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967.

## Key Points

- Amendment of 2 entries in the UNSC's 1267/1989 ISIL & Al-Qaida Sanctions List.
- Amendment of 1 entry in the UNSC's 1988 (2011) Taliban Sanctions List.
- Members are required to scan existing and future accounts against the updated sanction lists.
- Procedures for de-listing requests and freezing of assets must be strictly followed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of updates from the UNSC Sanctions Committee as communicated by SEBI under Section 51A of UAPA, 1967.

## Compliance Requirements

- Take note of the UNSC press release and amended entries.
- Follow the procedure outlined in the Central Government Order dated Feb 02, 2021.
- Scan all existing and future accounts to ensure no association with listed entities or individuals.
- Forward any de-listing requests electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA, and Joint Secretary (UNP), MEA.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High importance for trading members and intermediaries to ensure robust AML/CFT screening mechanisms are updated with the latest UNSC lists to avoid regulatory non-compliance.
