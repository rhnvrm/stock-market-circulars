## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Skytech Infinite Platform Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Skytech Infinite Platform Limited
- Symbol: SKYTECH
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (series: ST) to Rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: September 04, 2026
- Reference Circulars: NSE/CML/75849 dated August 20, 2026, and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012

## Regulatory Changes

None. The transfer is executed pursuant to established SEBI guidelines and previous exchange circulars regarding SME IPOs.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members need to take note of the change in series for Skytech Infinite Platform Limited effective from September 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for the security, improving liquidity and trading efficiency for market participants holding Skytech Infinite Platform Limited shares.
