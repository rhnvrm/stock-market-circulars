## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Privately placed securities/Commercial Papers admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment.
- Effective date of listing: August 17, 2026.
- Issuing entities include Aditya Birla Money Limited, NABARD, HDFC Securities Limited, CEAT Limited, Tata Projects Limited, Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, IIFL Capital Services Limited, and JM Financial Services Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Notification issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Standard depository and exchange compliance applicable to commercial paper listings.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 14, 2026
- Listing/Effective Date: August 17, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Various (spanning August 2026 to December 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine market operations for debt segment participants with no broader market impact.
