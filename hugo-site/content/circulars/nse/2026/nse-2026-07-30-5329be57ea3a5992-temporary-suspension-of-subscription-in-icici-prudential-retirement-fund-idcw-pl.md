## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions and new registrations for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) under the Income Distribution Cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) plan options of the ICICI Prudential Retirement Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective July 31, 2026. This action aligns with the notice-cum-addendum issued by ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Limited.

## Key Points

- **Suspension of Subscriptions:** Discontinuation of fresh subscriptions, including lump sum mode and switches, in the IDCW option.
- **Suspension of New Registrations:** Fresh registrations for SIP and/or STP (as target schemes) and other special products (e.g., Freedom SIP, SIP Top-up, Booster SIP, Flex STP, Booster STP, Capital Appreciation STP, and Trigger Facility) are discontinued.
- **Affected Schemes:**
  1. ICICI Prudential Retirement Fund – Pure Equity Plan
  2. ICICI Prudential Retirement Fund – Hybrid Aggressive Plan
  3. ICICI Prudential Retirement Fund – Hybrid Conservative Plan
  4. ICICI Prudential Retirement Fund – Pure Debt Plan
- **No Restrictions on Outflows:** Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), Switch-outs, and STP-outs remain fully functional and unrestricted.
- **Existing Commitments:** Active SIP, STP, and other systematic transactions registered before July 31, 2026, will continue to be processed as usual by the AMC/RTA.

## Regulatory Changes

This is an operational suspension initiated by the AMC (ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund) and implemented by the NSE MF Invest Platform rather than a broad regulatory policy change from SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members & Distributing Entities:** Must ensure that fresh lumpsum subscription orders and new SIP/STP registration requests for the IDCW options of the specified plans are disabled on their respective terminals and platforms with effect from July 31, 2026.
- **Existing Plans:** Existing systematic plans must continue to run without any disruption.

## Important Dates

- **Notice-cum-Addendum Date:** July 27, 2026
- **NSE Circular Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Effective Date:** July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Mutual fund distributors, advisors, and the NSE MF Invest Platform must adjust their systems to block incoming new investment requests for the specified scheme options.
- **Investor Impact:** Investors cannot open new investment lines under the IDCW options of the specified retirement plans, but existing investments, systematic plans, and redemption/liquidity options are entirely unaffected.
