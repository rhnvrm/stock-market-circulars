## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a modification in the contract parameters for products under the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment, partially modifying earlier circulars dated June 19, 2026, and May 04, 2026. The revision applies to symbols GOLD1KG99, GOLD1KG95, GOLD100G99, and GOLD100G95, changing their quotation base value and tick size.

## Key Points

- **Symbols Impacted:** GOLD1KG99, GOLD1KG95, GOLD100G99, and GOLD100G95.
- **GOLD1KG99 & GOLD1KG95:** Quotation/Base Value revised from ₹ per 1 Kg to ₹ per 10 grams; Tick Size revised from ₹ 10 to ₹ 1.
- **GOLD100G99 & GOLD100G95:** Quotation/Base Value revised from ₹ per 100 grams to ₹ per 10 grams; Tick Size revised from ₹ 10 to ₹ 1.
- **Effective Date:** September 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of trading parameters—specifically quotation base value and tick size—for specific gold products traded under the EGR segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading terminals and systems to reflect the revised quotation base values and tick sizes for the specified EGR products.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) need to align their systems and bidding strategies with the revised tick sizes and quotation values effective from September 07, 2026.
