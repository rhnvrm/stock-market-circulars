## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, lumpsum purchases, switch-ins, and fresh registrations for Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) in Quantum Gold ETF FOF under Quantum Mutual Fund with effect from the close of business hours of October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Scheme: Quantum Gold ETF FOF (Quantum Mutual Fund).
- Suspended Activities: Lumpsum purchases, switch-ins, and fresh registrations for SIP and STP.
- Effective Date: Close of business hours of October 15, 2026.
- Unaffected Activities: Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and processing of already registered SIPs and STPs.
- Reason: Broader economic and market conditions.

## Regulatory Changes

Modification of Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) of Quantum Gold ETF FOF regarding temporary suspension of subscriptions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, distributors, and investors must note the temporary suspension of fresh investments in the specified scheme until further notice.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: Close of business hours of October 15, 2026
- Circular Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding or planning to invest in Quantum Gold ETF FOF through lumpsum or fresh SIP/STP will be restricted from making new investments, while existing SIPs/STPs and redemption options remain fully operational.
