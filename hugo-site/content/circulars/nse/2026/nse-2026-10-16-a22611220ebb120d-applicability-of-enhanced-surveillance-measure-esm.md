## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for specified securities. Securities shortlisted under ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin and be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I are shortlisted under ESM Stage-I w.e.f. October 19, 2026, attracting a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on October 19, 2026, and new positions created from October 21, 2026.
- Securities in Annexure II (e.g., LYKALABS) move from ESM Stage-I to Stage-II w.e.f. October 19, 2026, and will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction.
- Securities shifting from Stage-II to Stage-I (e.g., CEREBRAINT, MINALIND, SYSTMTXC) move back to Stage-I w.e.f. October 19, 2026.
- Shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) takes effect from October 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The ESM framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures. Shortlisted securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement (EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and note the shifting of affected scrips to the Trade-for-Trade segment or Periodic Call Auction as applicable.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ESM Stage-I / Stage-II movement / Periodic Call Auction: October 19, 2026 (open positions as on October 19, 2026).
- Effective Date for Margin / Trade-for-Trade shifting: October 21, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in specified securities will experience heightened surveillance, 100% margin requirements, and trade-for-trade settlement or periodic call auction restrictions, impacting liquidity and positioning for market participants.
