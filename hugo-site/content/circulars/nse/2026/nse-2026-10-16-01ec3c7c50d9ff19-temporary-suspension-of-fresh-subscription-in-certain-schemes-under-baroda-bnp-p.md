## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, including lump-sum, switch-ins, and fresh registrations for Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STPs), in the Baroda BNP Paribas Gold ETF Fund of Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from the close of business hours of October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Scheme: Baroda BNP Paribas Gold ETF Fund of Fund.
- Suspended Transactions: Lump-sum subscriptions, additional purchases, switch-ins, fresh SIP registrations, and fresh STP registrations.
- Unaffected Transactions: Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWPs), switch-outs, STP-outs, and existing SIPs/STPs will continue to process normally.
- Effective Date: Close of business hours of October 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary restriction implemented based on communication from Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the suspension of fresh subscriptions and ensure no new subscription or fresh systematic registration orders are submitted for the affected scheme until further notice.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026 (close of business hours).

## Impact Assessment

- Investors wishing to invest fresh capital into Baroda BNP Paribas Gold ETF Fund of Fund via lumpsum or new SIP/STP will be unable to do so on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Existing investments and redemptions remain unaffected.
