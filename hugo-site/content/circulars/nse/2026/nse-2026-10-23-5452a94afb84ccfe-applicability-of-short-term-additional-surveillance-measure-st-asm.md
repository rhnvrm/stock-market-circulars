## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I, Stage II, and stage migrations for selected securities effective October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I: eMudhra Limited (EMUDHRA) and Kanani Industries Limited (KANANIIND).
- Securities moving from Stage I to Stage II: Phoenix International Limited (PHOENXINTL).
- Applicable margin rate for Stage I is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) on all open positions as on October 26, 2026, and new positions created from October 27, 2026.
- Applicable margin rate for Stage II is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) on all open positions as on October 26, 2026, and new positions created from October 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of enhanced surveillance measures and margin requirements under the ST-ASM framework to monitor market volatility and price movements in specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with applicable margin rates (50% or 100% as applicable) on open positions and new positions for the shortlisted securities w.e.f. October 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date / Applicability Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the short term.
