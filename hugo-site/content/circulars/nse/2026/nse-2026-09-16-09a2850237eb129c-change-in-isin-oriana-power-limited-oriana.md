## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in ISIN for the equity shares of Oriana Power Limited (Symbol: ORIANA) following a stock split/sub-division from face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Oriana Power Limited
- Symbol: ORIANA
- Old ISIN: Not specified
- New ISIN: INE0OUT01027
- Reason: Sub-Division from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-

## Regulatory Changes

- ISIN code update for trading and settlement in the depository system.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure systems are updated with the new ISIN prior to the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 16, 2026
- Ex-Date / Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The new ISIN will be effective for all trades executed on and from the ex-date, ensuring smooth clearing and settlement for the sub-divided shares.
