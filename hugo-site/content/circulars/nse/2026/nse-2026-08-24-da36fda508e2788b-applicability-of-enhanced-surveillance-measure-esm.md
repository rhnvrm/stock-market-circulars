## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin and will be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment with effect from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **ESM Stage - I Inclusions:** Securities including ACS Technologies Limited (ACSTECH), Cressanda Railway Solutions Limited (CRSL), Cambridge Technology Enterprises Limited (CTE), Kronox Lab Sciences Limited (KRONOX), Minal Industries Limited (MINALIND), and Swaraj Suiting Limited (SWARAJ) are shortlisted under ESM Stage - I and will attract 100% margin w.e.f. August 26, 2026, and shift from EQ/SM to BE/ST segment.
- **Stage Shifts:** Jalan Transolutions (India) Limited and Transworld Shipping Lines Limited are moving from ESM Stage - I to Stage - II with trade-for-trade and price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 25, 2026.
- **Nature of Action:** Shortlisting of securities under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).
- Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure applicable 100% margins are maintained on all open positions as on August 25, 2026, and new positions created from August 26, 2026, for securities listed under ESM Stage - I.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Stage II shift & Periodic Call Auction:** August 25, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margin and Segment Shift (EQ/SM to BE/ST):** August 26, 2026 (on open positions as on August 25, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement terms for affected securities will be strictly monitored under the enhanced surveillance framework, impacting intraday positions and requiring full margins.
