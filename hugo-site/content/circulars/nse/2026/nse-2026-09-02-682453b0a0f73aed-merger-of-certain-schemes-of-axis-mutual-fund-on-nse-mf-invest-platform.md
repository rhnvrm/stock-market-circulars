## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the merger of certain schemes of Axis Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform. Axis CRISIL-IBX AAA Bond Financial Services - Sep 2027 Index Fund and Axis CRISIL-IBX AAA Bond NBFC-HFC - Jun 2027 Index Fund (merging schemes) will merge into Axis CRISIL-IBX AAA Bond NBFC - Jun 2027 Index Fund (surviving scheme). The merger becomes effective after close of business hours on September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Merging Schemes: Axis CRISIL-IBX AAA Bond Financial Services - Sep 2027 Index Fund & Axis CRISIL-IBX AAA Bond NBFC-HFC - Jun 2027 Index Fund.
- Surviving Scheme: Axis CRISIL-IBX AAA Bond NBFC - Jun 2027 Index Fund.
- Effective Date: Merger is effective after business hours on September 11, 2026.
- Exit Load Window: Investors not in favour of the merger can redeem or switch to other schemes without exit load from 12.08.2026 to 10.09.2026 (up to 5:30 PM on September 10, 2026).
- SIP/XSIP/SWP Handling: Existing SIP/XSIP/SWP will continue to be processed in merging schemes during the exit load period, and will be shifted to surviving schemes after business hours on September 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Members and platform participants must note the scheme merger timelines, facilitate exit load-free redemptions or switches during the specified window, and ensure seamless migration of ongoing SIP/SWP mandates to the surviving scheme post-merger.

## Important Dates

- Exit Load Period Start: August 12, 2026
- Exit Load Period End: September 10, 2026 (up to 5:30 PM)
- Merger Effective Date: September 11, 2026 (after close of business hours)

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding units in the merging schemes are provided an exit window without load charges. Existing systematic investment and withdrawal instructions will automatically transition to the surviving scheme, ensuring continuity of investments without requiring fresh registrations.
