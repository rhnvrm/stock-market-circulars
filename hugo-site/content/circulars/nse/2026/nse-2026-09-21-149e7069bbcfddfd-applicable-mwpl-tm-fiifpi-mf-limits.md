## Summary

NSE has released the applicable Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL), Trading Member limits, FII/FPI, and Mutual Fund limits for various listed securities as per regulatory requirements.

## Key Points

- Details Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) in terms of number of shares for listed stocks.
- Specifies overall stock limits for clients and proprietary trading.
- Outlines limits for Trading Members (Client + Proprietary), FPI (Category I), and Mutual Funds.
- Provides category-wise limits for FPI Category II (individuals, family offices, corporates, and others).

## Regulatory Changes

- Regular periodic update of position limits for derivatives segment securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with the specified position limits to avoid position breaches and subsequent penalties.

## Important Dates

- Effective for trade dates as specified by the exchange circular.

## Impact Assessment

- Dictates the maximum allowable open interest and position sizes for trading members, institutional investors, and clients in the derivatives segment.
