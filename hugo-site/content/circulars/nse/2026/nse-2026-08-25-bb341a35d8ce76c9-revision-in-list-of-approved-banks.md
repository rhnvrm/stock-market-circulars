## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has updated its list of approved banks by adding YES Bank Limited for Electronic Bank Guarantee (E-BG) facilities, supplementing the existing circular issued on August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- YES Bank Limited has been added to the list of approved banks.
- Facility approved: E-BG (Electronic Bank Guarantee).
- Refers to previous circular dated August 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Expansion of the approved bank list for clearing and collateral operations at NSE Clearing Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members may utilize YES Bank Limited for E-BG collateral arrangements in accordance with existing operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: Aug 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides trading and clearing members with an additional banking option for submitting Electronic Bank Guarantees as collateral.
