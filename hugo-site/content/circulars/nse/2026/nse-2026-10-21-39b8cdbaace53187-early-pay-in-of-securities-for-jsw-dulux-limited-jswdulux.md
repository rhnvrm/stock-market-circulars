## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued guidelines for members and custodians regarding early pay-in (EPI) of securities for JSW Dulux Limited (JSWDULUX) on account of a face value split (sub-division) from Rs 10/- per share to Re 1/- per share.

## Key Points

- Corporate Action: Face value split from Rs 10/- to Re 1/- per share.
- Existing/Old ISIN: INE133A01011.
- Record Date and Ex-Date: October 22, 2026.
- Members executing early pay-in on October 22, 2026, and October 23, 2026, must make early pay-in in the depository systems using the existing/old ISIN without adjusting for the conversion.
- Custodians providing early pay-in through the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details based on actual traded quantities.

## Regulatory Changes

Mandates specific ISIN handling for early pay-in transactions during corporate actions (face value split) to ensure seamless clearing and settlement without premature adjustments in depository systems.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants/custodians must execute early pay-in instructions in the old ISIN for ex-date/record date settlements.
- If using the block mechanism in client demat accounts, client-wise early pay-in allocation files must not be uploaded.
- Custodians using the pool account must upload client-wise allocation files reflecting actual traded quantities to pass benefits.
- Post record date, pay-in and early pay-in instructions must be executed in the New ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 22, 2026
- Ex-Date: October 22, 2026
- EPI Dates and Settlement Numbers: October 22, 2026 (Settlement No. 2026199); October 23, 2026 (Settlement Nos. 2026199 & 2026200)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures accurate matching of trades and settlements during stock split corporate actions, avoiding mismatch errors in depository allocations for trading members and custodians.
