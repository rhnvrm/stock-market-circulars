## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the listing of equity shares of Fermenta Biotech Limited (symbol: FERMENTA) with effect from August 4, 2026. The company was previously listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and will now be dual-listed on both major Indian exchanges. Ahead of this listing, the company has submitted its pre-listing shareholding pattern as of July 24, 2026, in accordance with Regulation 31(1)(b) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Fermenta Biotech Limited (ISIN: INE225B01021, Scrip Code: 506414).
- **Total Listed Capital:** 2,94,30,987 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹5 each.
- **Listing Segment:** Equity Segment (Series: EQ, Rolling Settlement).
- **Shareholding Pattern:** As of July 24, 2026, the company has filed its shareholding pattern showing no partly paid-up shares, no outstanding convertible securities or warrants, no ESOPs outstanding, and no depository receipts.
- **Promoter Share Encumbrance:** No promoter shares are pledged, encumbered under Non-Disposal Undertakings (NDUs), or otherwise encumbered.
- **SME Status:** The company is classified as a non-SME public limited company.

## Regulatory Changes

No major regulatory changes are introduced by this circular. It is a standard listing operation carried out in compliance with NSE regulations and SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Shareholding Pattern:** The company has satisfied the pre-listing compliance requirements by filing its Shareholding Pattern under Regulation 31(1)(b) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
- **Continued Compliance:** Post-listing, the company must continue to comply with all continuous disclosure requirements, quarterly/annual reporting, and other compliance obligations under SEBI (LODR) Regulations.

## Important Dates

- **Pre-listing Shareholding Pattern Date:** July 24, 2026
- **Circular Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** August 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity:** The new listing on NSE provides increased liquidity, wider trading access, and enhanced visibility to the public and institutional investors for trading in Fermenta Biotech Limited shares.
- **Trading Operations:** Investors and members can trade the company's equity shares on both BSE (Scrip Code: 506414) and NSE (Symbol: FERMENTA) starting from August 4, 2026, in the rolling settlement (EQ) segment.
