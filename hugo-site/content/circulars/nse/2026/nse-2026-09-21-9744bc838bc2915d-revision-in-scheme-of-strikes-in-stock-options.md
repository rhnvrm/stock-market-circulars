## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the revision in the scheme of strikes in stock options, updating step values and strike schemes for various stocks effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Refers to chapter 1.11 of the strike scheme of stock options from circular NSE/FAOP/73928 dated April 28, 2026.
- Revised applicable step values and number of strikes are provided in the Annexure for multiple stock option contracts.
- Changes become effective for trading starting September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of step values and strike schemes (Narrow Range, Wide Range, and Reserve strike) across various stock option symbols.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to note the revised applicable step values and number of strikes.
- Members must load the updated contract.gz file in the trading application before trading, available from the `/faoftp/faocommon` directory on the extranet server.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 21, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members dealing in stock options must update their trading applications with the latest contract definitions to ensure accurate strike availability and order execution.
