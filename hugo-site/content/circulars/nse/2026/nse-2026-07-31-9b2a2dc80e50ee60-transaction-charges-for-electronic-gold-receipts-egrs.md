## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that no transaction charges will be levied on trades within the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGRs) Segment of the exchange. This fee waiver is designed to encourage active participation in the segment following its launch on May 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Segment Launch:** The EGRs Segment was launched by the Exchange on May 04, 2026.
- **Transaction Charges Waiver:** No transaction charges will be levied on trades in the EGRs Segment to promote market participation.
- **Waiver Period:** The transaction charge waiver is effective and will remain in place till November 03, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- There are no structural regulatory changes. The exchange has temporarily suspended the collection of transaction fees for trades executed under the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGRs) Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members do not need to perform any compliance or administrative updates. The zero-transaction-charge policy will be handled automatically by the Exchange billing system.

## Important Dates

- **EGR Segment Launch Date:** May 04, 2026
- **Circular Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Waiver Validity Period:** Till November 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low impact overall, but highly positive for EGR market participants. The zero transaction fee structure reduces the cost of carrying out EGR transactions, which is expected to improve liquidity and trading volumes in the newly introduced segment.
