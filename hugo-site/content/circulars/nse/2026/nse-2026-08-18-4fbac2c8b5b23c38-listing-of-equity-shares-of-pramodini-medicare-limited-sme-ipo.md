## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Pramodini Medicare Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform effective from August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pramodini Medicare Limited
- Symbol: PRAMODINI
- ISIN: INE2JG601017
- Series: ST (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment – Settlement Type ‘W’)
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 118/- per share
- Total No. of Securities: 22,045,595
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 22045595
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares

## Regulatory Changes

Security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other categories of scrips and available for trading in the continuous market as per established exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the security shall be in the specified lot sizes and identified by its designated codes under the Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment before shifting to the normal rolling segment as per SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 19, 2026
- Circular Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Pramodini Medicare Limited shares on the NSE EMERGE SME platform under surveillance segment rules.
