## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for multiple companies on the SME EMERGE segment, effective from October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026.
- **Companies Involved:** Fonebox Retail Limited, Lloyds Luxuries Limited, Richa Info Systems Limited, Sonu Infratech Limited, and Transteel Seating Technologies Limited.
- **Fonebox Retail Limited:** 3,000,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential issue (ISIN: INE0Q4701019, Distinctive Range: 10260001 to 13260000, Lot Size: 1000).
- **Lloyds Luxuries Limited:** 18,912 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE0DTQ01028, Distinctive Range: 24134874 to 24153785, Lot Size: 1000).

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, further issues are admitted to dealings and designated lot sizes are established.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in these securities shall be conducted using the designated security codes and respective lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 23, 2026.
- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026.
- **Fonebox Allotment Date:** September 8, 2026.
- **Lloyds Luxuries Allotment Date:** October 14, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading of newly allotted preferential and ESOP shares for the respective SME companies on the NSE SME EMERGE platform.
