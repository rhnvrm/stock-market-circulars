## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued an update regarding cautionary messages on trading terminals for International Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs). Due to observed price variances (premiums/discounts relative to NAV) in International ETFs, the Exchange in consultation with SEBI has mandated trading members to implement cautionary pop-up alert messages.

## Key Points

- International ETFs frequently trade at significant premiums or discounts relative to their underlying Net Asset Value (NAV).
- Trading members must integrate cautionary pop-up messages for International ETFs on non-NEAT front ends (IBT, STWT, CTCL, etc.).
- The pop-up message format requires user confirmation ('Yes' / 'No') before order execution, proceeding only upon selecting 'Yes'.
- Trading members must maintain logs of all such displays and user selections for periodic audit and compliance verification.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of mandatory cautionary message requirements for International ETFs in the REG1 file structure.
- Inclusion of verbatim pop-up warning: 'International ETFs may trade above their NAV?' (or applicable standard wording).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their trading front-ends (IBT, STWT, CTCL) to display the mandatory pop-up alert for International ETF orders.
- Ensure orders are routed to the Exchange only when 'Yes' is selected by the investor.
- Maintain robust logs of displayed alerts and client responses for management review and system auditor inspection.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances investor awareness and protection against unintended trading losses resulting from premium/discount trading in International ETFs.
- Requires technical implementation by trading members across non-NEAT trading front-ends and log maintenance systems.
