## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) "National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30". Trading members are advised to adopt measures such as simplifying digital interfaces, overcoming language barriers using tools like Bhashini, and deploying AI/ML-based public query systems.

## Key Points

- RBI released the National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30, dated December 01, 2025.
- Highlights the need to simplify digital interfaces and remove language barriers to promote financial inclusion.
- Advises the use of AI-powered language translation platforms such as Bhashini.
- Advises trading members to develop AI & ML-based public query systems on securities market-related aspects for client support via websites and social media extensions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Alignment with RBI's NSFI 2025-30 guidelines on digital accessibility, vernacular availability, and AI/ML customer query tools.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must review and simplify digital interfaces to facilitate ease of use.
- Address language barriers through vernacular languages.
- Utilize AI-powered language translation platforms like Bhashini.
- Facilitate the development of AI & ML-based public query systems on common securities market-related aspects on websites and social media extensions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 23, 2026.
- NSFI Strategy Period: 2025–2030.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances accessibility, investor awareness, and digital inclusion across diverse investor demographics through vernacular and AI-driven support mechanisms.
