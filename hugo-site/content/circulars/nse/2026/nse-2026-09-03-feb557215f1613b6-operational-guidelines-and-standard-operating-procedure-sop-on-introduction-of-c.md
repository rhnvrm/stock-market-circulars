## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued an update regarding the operational guidelines and Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for the introduction of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Cash Segment. The guidelines for reference price computation and applicable price bands (previously applicable to stock futures) are now extended to include index futures contracts effective September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Extension to Index Futures: Reference price computation and price band guidelines specified in clauses 2.3 and 3.2 shall apply to index futures contracts in addition to stock futures.
- Effective Date: The provisions become effective from September 7, 2026.
- Reference Price Calculation (Clause 2.3): Based on VWAP between 3:00 p.m. and 3:15 p.m., or LTP if no trades occurred, or theoretical price / base price if no trades occurred during the day.
- Futures Price Band (Clause 3.2): Price band during 3:15 p.m. to 3:40 p.m. shall be +/- 3% from the reference price for both stock and index futures. Dynamic flexing of price bands shall not apply during this window.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of circular NSE/CMTR/73362 (dated March 18, 2026) to explicitly incorporate index futures alongside stock futures under the CAS reference price and price band framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the inclusion of index futures in the CAS reference price calculation and 3% price band restrictions from 3:15 p.m. to 3:40 p.m.
- Orders outside revised price bands will be canceled sequentially during this period.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 7, 2026
- Circular Date: September 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Brings consistency in price discovery and risk management between stock futures and index futures during the closing auction window.
