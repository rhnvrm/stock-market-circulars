## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives Segment on Saturday, September 26, 2026, utilizing both primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, September 26, 2026.
- Covers Trading Session-1 from Primary Site and Trading Session-2 from DR Site.
- NEAT Version: NEAT 3.5.4.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session; members using NNF software must manually clear orders.
- No financial obligations or fund pay-in/pay-out will result from trades during mock sessions.
- UCC/PAN validation rules apply as per existing guidelines; validation is skipped during contingency time for order entry.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; routine mock trading conducted to meet regulatory requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, September 28, 2026.
- Ensure valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded before cutoff.
- Verify telnet connectivity and Box ID setup prior to the mock session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Date: September 26, 2026
- Live Re-login start time: September 26, 2026 at 16:00 Hrs
- Live Re-login close time: September 26, 2026 at 16:30 Hrs
- Live market resumption: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness for trading members in the Currency Derivatives segment with no financial impact.
