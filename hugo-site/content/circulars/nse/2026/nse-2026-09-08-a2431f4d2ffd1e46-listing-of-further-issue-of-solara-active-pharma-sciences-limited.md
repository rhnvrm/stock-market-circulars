## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further equity shares of Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited effective from September 09, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited
- Symbol: SOLARA
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE624Z01016 (Temporary ISIN: IN8624Z01015)
- Number of Securities: 2,75,705 equity shares
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- Effective Date: September 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 09, 2026
- Circular Date: September 08, 2026

Routine listing update for market participants holding or trading the security.
