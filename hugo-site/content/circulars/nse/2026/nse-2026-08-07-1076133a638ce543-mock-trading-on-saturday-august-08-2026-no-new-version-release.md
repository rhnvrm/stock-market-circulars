## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has scheduled a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) on Saturday, August 08, 2026. This session is designed to test disaster recovery switchover and connectivity, and does not involve any new software version release.

## Key Points

- **Session Schedule**: Trading Session-1 will run from the Primary Site (11:15 to 13:30), followed by Trading Session-2 from the DR Site (14:30 to 15:30).
- **Re-login Schedule**: A live re-login window will be open from 16:30 to 17:00 to verify connectivity for the next live trading day.
- **Software Version**: The mock trading session uses NEAT Version 1.3.3.
- **No Financial Obligation**: Mock trades do not result in any financial pay-in or pay-out obligations.
- **Unavailability of Extranet/Portal**: Connect2NSE and Extranet facility will be unavailable from 12:30 to 21:00, and the Extranet API from 12:30 to 14:00.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes or software version releases associated with this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Participation**: All members enabled for SLBM segment trading are encouraged to participate to ensure seamless operations.
- **NNF Systems**: Members using Non-Neat Front End (NNF) software must manually clear outstanding orders in their systems prior to each session.
- **Connectivity Verification**: Members must perform live re-login between 16:30 and 17:00 on August 08, 2026, to verify connection readiness for Monday, August 10, 2026.
- **UCC/PAN Validity**: Only valid and compliant UCC/PANs approved prior to the cut-off will be allowed. UCC validation is skipped during contingency time.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Session**: Saturday, August 08, 2026
- **Live Re-login Window**: Saturday, August 08, 2026, 16:30 to 17:00
- **Next Live Trading Day**: Monday, August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Low. While the mock session is necessary for disaster recovery validation, it is a non-disruptive procedure scheduled on a weekend.
- **System Impact**: Low. No new software installation or updates are required, maintaining existing system configurations.
