## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular notifying market participants that specific securities will be transitioned to Stage II of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. Effective from August 10, 2026, **Ortel Communications Limited** (Symbol: ORTEL) and **Impex Ferro Tech Limited** (Symbol: IMPEXFERRO) will be moved to Stage II. This action is a continuation of previous surveillance circulars and implements trade-for-trade restrictions, limited price bands, and an Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) requirement for buyers.

## Key Points

- **Affected Securities**:
  1. **Ortel Communications Limited** (Symbol: ORTEL, ISIN: INE849L01019)
  2. **Impex Ferro Tech Limited** (Symbol: IMPEXFERRO, ISIN: INE691G01015)
- **Surveillance Action**: Transition to Stage II of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.
- **Trading Restriction**: Trading in these securities will be restricted to the **Trade for Trade** segment.
- **Price Band**: Limited to a maximum price band of **5% or lower** as applicable.
- **Collateral/Margin Requirement**: Buyers must deposit an **Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50%** of the trade value.
- **Previous References**: Continued from circulars dated between February 23, 2017, and June 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory rules are created. However, the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework is enforced dynamically on specified securities to monitor and control unusual price movements and protect investor interests. Stage II actions restrict speculative trading of these instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

NSE Members are requested to:
- Take adequate precautions and carry out necessary diligence while trading in the designated securities.
- Ensure that buyers of ORTEL and IMPEXFERRO deposit the mandatory **50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD)** before execution of trades, starting August 10, 2026.
- Maintain appropriate margin collection policies aligning with Trade for Trade guidelines and the 5% price bands.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 7, 2026
- **Effective Date of Stage II GSM**: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Affected Companies**: Ortel Communications Limited and Impex Ferro Tech Limited will likely experience a significant drop in trading volume and liquidity due to the restrictive trade-for-trade mode and the 50% ASD requirement on buyers.
- **Market Impact**: Minimal for the broader market as the actions are stock-specific, but highly restrictive for market players and investors holding these specific counters.
- **Operational Impact**: Brokerages and trading members must update their risk management systems to enforce the 5% price bands, trade-for-trade routing, and collect the required 50% ASD from clients intending to purchase these securities.
