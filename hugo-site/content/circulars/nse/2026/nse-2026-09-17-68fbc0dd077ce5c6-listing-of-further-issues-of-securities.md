## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has notified the admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares of RNFI Services Limited and Vision Infra Equipment Solutions Limited under the SME Emerge segment, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **RNFI Services Limited**: 37,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment at an issue price of Rs. 270/-. Distinctive number range: 25356944 to 25393943. Market lot: 600. Lock-in details up to 30-Mar-2027.
- **Vision Infra Equipment Solutions Limited**: 200,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued under preferential allotment at an issue price of Rs. 250/-. Distinctive number range: 24642401 to 24842400. Market lot: 800. Lock-in details up to 30-Apr-2027.
- Trading will be permitted in designated security codes and specified lot sizes starting September 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing of further issues under applicable exchange regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to trading regulations, lot sizes, and lock-in requirements as specified in the circular and annexures.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: September 18, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**: 08-Aug-2026 (RNFI), 17-Jul-2026 (Vision Infra)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact; standard expansion of equity capital for the respective SME companies.
