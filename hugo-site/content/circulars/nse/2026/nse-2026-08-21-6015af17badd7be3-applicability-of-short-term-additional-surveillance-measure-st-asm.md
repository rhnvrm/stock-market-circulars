## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stages I and II to various securities. The surveillance actions include enhanced margin requirements effective August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. August 25, 2026, on all open positions as on August 24, 2026, and new positions created from August 25, 2026.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher capped at 100% w.e.f. August 25, 2026, on all open positions as on August 24, 2026, and new positions created from August 25, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of enhanced margin rates (50% to 100%) for securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance for open positions and new trades in the affected securities in accordance with the ST-ASM framework.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins: August 25, 2026 (on open positions as on August 24, 2026, and new positions thereafter)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin obligations for specified securities (including Bajaj Hindusthan Sugar, Dalmia Bharat Sugar, etc.) may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the short term.
