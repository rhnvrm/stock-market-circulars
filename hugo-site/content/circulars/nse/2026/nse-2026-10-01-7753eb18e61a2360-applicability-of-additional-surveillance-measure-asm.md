## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long-Term ASM framework. Specific securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion and will attract heightened surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I: Bhageria Industries Limited (BHAGERIA) and Birla Cable Limited (BIRLACABLE).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective October 07, 2026, on all open positions as on October 06, 2026, and new positions created from October 07, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for securities placed under Long-Term ASM Stage I.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that a 100% margin is levied on open positions as of October 06, 2026, and all new positions created from October 07, 2026, for the specified scrips.

## Important Dates

- Open Positions Cut-off Date: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date of Margin Applicability: October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The imposition of 100% margins on affected scrips may impact liquidity and trading volumes for Bhageria Industries Limited and Birla Cable Limited while reinforcing market integrity and risk management.
