## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme name of NJ Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform. Effective August 26, 2026, 'NJ ELSS Tax Saver Scheme' has been renamed to 'NJ ELSS Tax Saver Fund' pursuant to SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026, and AMFI communications.

## Key Points

- **Existing Scheme Name:** NJ ELSS Tax Saver Scheme
- **Revised Scheme Name:** NJ ELSS Tax Saver Fund
- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **Reason:** Categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes as per SEBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- Changes made pursuant to the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026, read with AMFI Communication 35 P / MEM – COR / 07 / 2026-27 and 35P/MEM–COR/07-A/2026-27.
- Scheme type and category updated in accordance with the Equity Linked Saving Scheme, 2005 notified by the Ministry of Finance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must take note of the revised scheme name for all future transactions and reporting on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026
- **Circular Date:** August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal, affecting only nomenclature and scheme identification on trading and investment platforms.
