## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission to dealings of units issued by DSP Asset Managers Private Limited (DSP Nifty 10 Yr Benchmark G-Sec ETF) on the Capital Market segment effective from August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: DSP Nifty 10 Yr Benchmark G-Sec ETF
- Symbol: GSEC10ADD
- ISIN: INF740KA1ZI7
- Number of Units: 3,786,682
- Face Value / Paid Up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 26.89/-
- Date of Allotment: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading: August 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Admission in pursuance of regular trading regulations of NSE Capital Market segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with applicable trading regulations and designated market makers setup as specified in the circular annexure.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 14, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the investment choices available to market participants in the ETF and G-Sec segment.
