## Summary

SEBI, in consultation with Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations (CCs), has issued guidelines regarding margin waiver on subsequent buy/sell transactions post Early Pay-In (EPI) in the cash segment. Where a client has a net sell obligation in a settlement and the EPI request for the sold securities has been successfully accepted by the CC, the CC shall, on a real-time basis, not collect margin from the Trading Member (TM)/Clearing Member (CM) on subsequent buy/sell positions up to the amount of such accepted EPI.

## Key Points

- Real-time margin waiver up to the accepted EPI amount for subsequent buy/sell positions across all segments.
- Available value of EPI is computed on the trade date as the lower of the value of EPI done or value of securities sold.
- If a client squares off the sale position for which EPI benefit was availed, the EPI value is removed, and any margin shortfall is blocked from the proprietary collateral of the TM/CM by the CC (excluding Trade for Trade securities).
- Trading Members must ensure risk management systems permit client position square-off or new positions only with available requisite margins.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clearing Corporations are mandated to not collect margin on a real-time basis from TMs/CMs on subsequent buy/sell positions up to accepted EPI value.
- CCs must amend relevant bye-laws, rules, regulations, and carry out necessary system changes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing Corporations to update bye-laws, implement system changes, and disseminate circular provisions on their websites.
- Trading Members to configure risk management systems to check client margins before allowing square-off or new positions.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date: Four months from the date of issuance of this circular.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances liquidity and capital efficiency for market participants by leveraging EPI value for subsequent trading positions.
- Streamlines margin requirements while upholding risk mitigation through proprietary collateral blocking upon position square-off.
