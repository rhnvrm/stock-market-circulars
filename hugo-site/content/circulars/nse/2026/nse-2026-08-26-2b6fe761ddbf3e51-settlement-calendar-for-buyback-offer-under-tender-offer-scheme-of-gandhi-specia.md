## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar and procedural instructions for the buyback offer under the tender offer scheme of Gandhi Special Tubes Limited. The trade period runs from August 27, 2026, to September 2, 2026, with settlement scheduled for September 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: X, Settlement No: 2026108.
- Trade Start Date: 27-AUG-2026; Trade End Date: 02-SEP-2026.
- Obligation Download Date: 08-SEP-2026; Settlement Date: 09-SEP-2026.
- Demat shares must be tendered via Early Pay-in of Shares through the depositories' early pay-in mechanism for Market Type Buyback.
- Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure early pay-in of shares is made well in advance for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m. on the trade end date.

- Trade End Date: September 2, 2026
