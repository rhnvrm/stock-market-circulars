## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to certain securities. The circular lists securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion under Long-Term ASM Stage-I and securities moving between stages.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. August 21, 2026, on all open positions as on August 20, 2026, and new positions created from August 21, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. August 21, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of 100% margin requirements on shortlisted securities under the Long-Term ASM framework.
- Reclassification of eligible scrips to the Trade-for-Trade segment as per surveillance criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.
- Members need to note the shifting of applicable scrips to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective August 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Margin/Stage Shifts: August 21, 2026 (based on positions as of August 20, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading operations for the shortlisted stocks (including Assam Entrade Limited, Morepen Laboratories Limited, Rubicon Research Limited, Global Surfaces Limited, and Teja Engineering Industries Limited) will be impacted due to 100% margin requirements and potential segment shifts.
