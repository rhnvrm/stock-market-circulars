## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares of Shri Ahimsa Naturals Limited on the SME Emerge segment with effect from September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Shri Ahimsa Naturals Limited
- **Symbol:** SHRIAHIMSA
- **ISIN:** INE0DM401012 (Temporary ISIN applicable as per SEBI guidelines where required)
- **Issue Type:** Preferential Issue
- **Number of Securities:** 377,600 equity shares
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 227/-
- **Market Lot:** 600
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 23430901 to 23808500

## Regulatory Changes

None. Admitted in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be in the specified lot size of 600 shares using designated security codes. Lock-in details apply as specified in Annexure A (224,000 shares locked in until 29-Mar-2028 and 153,600 shares locked in until 29-Mar-2027).

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 12-May-2026
- **Effective Date:** September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing procedure for SME equity shares admitted to dealings on the exchange.
