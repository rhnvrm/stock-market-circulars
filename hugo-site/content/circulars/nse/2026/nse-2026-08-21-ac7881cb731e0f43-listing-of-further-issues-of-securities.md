## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of further issues of securities for TAC Infosec Limited. Equity shares allotted under ESOP will be admitted to dealings on the SME EMERGE segment effective from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TAC Infosec Limited
- Symbol: TAC
- Series: SM
- ISIN: INE0SOY01013
- Security Description: Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP.
- Number of Securities: 1,040
- Distinctive Number Range: 21026241 to 21027280
- Market Lot: 200
- Date of Allotment: 24-Jul-2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be in the specified lot sizes and identified by their designated codes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 24, 2026
- Allotment Date: July 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up capital due to ESOP allotment with minimal market impact.
