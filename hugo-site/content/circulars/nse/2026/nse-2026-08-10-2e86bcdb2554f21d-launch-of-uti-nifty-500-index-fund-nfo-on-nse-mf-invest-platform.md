## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) by UTI Mutual Fund will be available for transacting on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is scheduled to run from August 10, 2026, through August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **NFO Subscription Window**: Available from August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026, till 11:59 PM.
- **Offered Schemes & ISINs**:
  - UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund-Direct Plan- Growth (ISIN: `INF789F1AG92`)
  - UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund-Regular Plan- Growth (ISIN: `INF789F1AG84`)
- **Category**: Equity
- **Allotment Date**: Scheduled for August 27, 2026.
- **Payment Guidelines**: Members must ensure clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account via any valid payment mode on or before August 24, 2026, till 11:59 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory policy changes introduced in this circular. It is a standard operational enablement of a mutual fund scheme under the existing platform guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Fund Transfer**: Trading members must guarantee that clear funds for subscriptions are credited to the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before the deadline of August 24, 2026, by 11:59 PM.
- **Order Entry**: Ensure all transactions are successfully processed within the specified operational window.

## Important Dates

- **August 10, 2026**: Circular issuance date and launch of the NFO transaction window.
- **August 24, 2026 (till 11:59 PM)**: Closing date for NFO transactions and deadline for clear funds receipt.
- **August 27, 2026**: Allotment date of the mutual fund units.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: This update has a low operational impact. It increases product availability on the NSE MF Invest Platform for UTI's new index fund. Transacting members must adhere strictly to the settlement deadline to avoid order rejections due to outstanding fund clearance.
