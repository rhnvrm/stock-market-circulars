## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Securities as per the attached annexure have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM).

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 09, 2026 on all open positions as on September 08, 2026 and new positions created from September 09, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. September 09, 2026.
- Prajay Engineers Syndicate Limited (Symbol: PRAENG, ISIN: INE505C01016) has moved from STASM to LTASM framework effective September 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The shortlisting of securities under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the 100% margin requirements applicable to open and new positions for the shortlisted securities as specified.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Shortlisting: September 07, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: September 09, 2026 (on open positions as on September 08, 2026)
- Circular Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted scrips will be subject to higher margin rates (100%), affecting liquidity and leverage for participants holding or creating positions in these securities.
