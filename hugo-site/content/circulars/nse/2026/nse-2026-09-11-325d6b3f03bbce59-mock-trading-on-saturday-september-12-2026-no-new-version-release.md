## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives Segment on Saturday, September 12, 2026. The session will test trading from both the Primary Site and Disaster Recovery (DR) Site to ensure system readiness and seamless connectivity.

## Key Points

- Mock Date: September 12, 2026.
- Trading Session-1 from Primary Site: Normal Market open at 11:00 hours, close at 11:30 hours.
- Trading Session-2 from DR Site: Normal Market open at 12:15 hours, close at 12:30 hours; Trade Modification end time at 12:40 hours.
- Live Re-login Timings: Start time at 17:30 hours, close time at 18:00 hours.
- NEAT Version: NEAT 3.5.4.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session. Members using NNF software must manually clear orders.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; conducted as a routine operational readiness test.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Tuesday, September 15, 2026. Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 12, 2026
- Live Environment Resumption: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact; ensures broker systems and connectivity are fully tested and functional prior to live market operations.
