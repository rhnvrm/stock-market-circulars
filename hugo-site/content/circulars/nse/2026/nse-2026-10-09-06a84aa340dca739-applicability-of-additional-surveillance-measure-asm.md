## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). The attached annexure indicates no securities meeting the criteria for inclusion or movement under the Long-Term ASM Framework for this cycle.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 14, 2026 on all open positions as on October 13, 2026 and new positions created from October 14, 2026 onwards for applicable securities.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. October 14, 2026.
- Review of annexure shows no securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I, Stage-IV, or moving between stages for the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of standard Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework provisions in conjunction with prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members are requested to note the surveillance actions and margin applicability for any applicable securities under the ASM framework.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date for Annexure List: October 12, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date / Trade-for-Trade Shift Effective Date: October 14, 2026 (on open positions as on October 13, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal immediate impact on the market as no specific securities are listed under the annexure tables for this circular edition.
