## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released a revised list of approved securities and banks applicable for the month of October 2026. This circular updates the list of Government of India (GOI) securities, Treasury Bills, Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs), and Corporate Bonds accepted as collateral, along with their respective haircuts and market-wide limits, as well as the list of empaneled banks for bank guarantees and fixed deposit receipts.

## Key Points

- Revised list includes Annexure 1 (GOI Securities/T-Bills/SGBs and cash component liquid assets with haircuts), Annexure 2 (Corporate Bonds and non-cash component liquid assets), and Annexure 3 (Empaneled banks for BGs and FDRs).
- G-Sec and T-Bills carry a shut period of two days; members must release them at least two days before maturity.
- Member banks must ensure G-Sec provided as collaterals are not reckoned for SLR purposes or used for trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- Regular monthly update of approved collaterals, limits, and haircuts for the debt segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must take note of the revised list and ensure timely release of G-Sec/T-Bills before their maturity shut period.
- Member-banks must comply with restrictions regarding SLR calculations and trading usage for pledged G-Sec collaterals.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 2026
- Circular Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact on clearing members and custodians managing collateral margins and liquidity in the NSE debt segment.
