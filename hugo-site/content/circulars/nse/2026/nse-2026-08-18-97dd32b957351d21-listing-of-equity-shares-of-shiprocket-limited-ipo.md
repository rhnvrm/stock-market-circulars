## Summary

NSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Shiprocket Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO), detailing the shareholding pattern and specified securities under Regulation 31(1)(a) as of August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total paid-up equity shares stand at 72,75,77,587.
- Public shareholding comprises 68,95,77,093 shares (94.78%).
- Shares held by Employees Trusts account for 3,80,00,494 shares (5.22%).
- Outstanding ESOPs and locked-in shares are reported, while promoter pledging or encumbrances are nil.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shareholding pattern filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entity is required to maintain compliance regarding disclosures of specified securities, locked-in shares, and outstanding ESOPs.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Formal admission and commencement of trading for Shiprocket Limited equity shares on the NSE platform.
