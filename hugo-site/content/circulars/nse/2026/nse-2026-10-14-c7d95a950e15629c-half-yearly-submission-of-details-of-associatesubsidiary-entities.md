## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the half-yearly submission of details pertaining to Associate/Subsidiary Entities under the Risk Based Supervision (RBS) framework. All trading members are required to submit these details for the half-year ended March 31, 2026, on or before November 15, 2026, via the ENIT portal.

## Key Points

- Mandatory half-yearly submission of Associate/Subsidiary Entity details for all Trading Members under the RBS framework.
- Submission deadline for the half-year ended March 31, 2026, is November 15, 2026.
- Online module developed on the ENIT Portal (Path: ENIT → ENIT-NEW-Compliance → Compliance → Associate and Subsidiary → Associate and Subsidiary Submission).
- Any changes in associate/subsidiary details (additions, deletions, modifications) must be notified to the Exchange within 30 calendar days.
- Trading members registered with multiple exchanges (NSE, BSE, MSE, MCX, NCDEX) need only submit to NSE, which will share the submission with respective exchanges as per technology-based sharing mechanisms.

## Regulatory Changes

Certain information under the Risk Based Supervision (RBS) framework is now sourced internally or via specific submissions, with associate/subsidiary details mandated as a separate half-yearly submission following representations from Brokers Industry Standard Forum (ISF) in consultation with SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

All trading members must log in to the ENIT portal, navigate to the Associate and Subsidiary Submission section, declare whether they have associate/subsidiary entities, fill in the required details, and submit on or before the due date. Any subsequent changes must be reported within 30 calendar days.

## Important Dates

- **Half-year Ended:** March 31, 2026
- **Submission Due Date:** November 15, 2026
- **Change Notification Window:** Within 30 calendar days of any modification, addition, or deletion

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and transparent monitoring of associate and subsidiary structures of trading members under the Risk Based Supervision framework, streamlining multi-exchange compliance submissions.
