## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers and other debt securities) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh Issue of Commercial Papers:
  - Bajaj Auto Credit Limited: 10,000 CPs (ISIN: INE18UV14091), maturity December 7, 2026.
  - HDFC Securities Limited: 200 CPs (ISIN: INE700G14WO2), maturity December 24, 2026.
  - Tata Communications Limited: 11,000 CPs (ISIN: INE151A14362), maturity December 18, 2026.
  - Godrej Industries Limited: 1,500 CPs (ISIN: INE233AD4199), maturity December 28, 2026.
  - HSBC Investdirect Financial Services (I) Limited: 500 CPs (ISIN: INE790I14IN0), maturity December 31, 2026.
- Fresh Issue of Other Debt Securities:
  - Power Grid Corporation of India Limited: 200,000 bonds (PGC 7.74% 2036 LXXXIV, ISIN: INE752E08817), maturity September 29, 2036.
  - Axis Max Life Insurance Limited: 68,600 debt securities (AMLIL 8.58% 2036, ISIN: INE511N08040), maturity September 28, 2036.
- Re-Issue of Commercial Papers:
  - ICICI Securities Limited: 1,600 CPs (ISEC CP 25/03/27, ISIN: INE763G14L37), maturity March 25, 2027.
  - HDB Financial Services Ltd: 4,000 CPs (HFSL CP 07/10/26 Sr 359, ISIN: INE756I14GO3), maturity October 7, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Complies with standard exchange listing procedures for privately placed debt instruments and commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing / Admission: September 29, 2026
- Allotment Dates: September 28-29, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from October 7, 2026 to September 29, 2036

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for privately placed corporate debt securities and commercial papers.
