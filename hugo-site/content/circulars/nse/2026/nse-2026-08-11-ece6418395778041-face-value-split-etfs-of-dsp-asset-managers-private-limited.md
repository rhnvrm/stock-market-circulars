## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular informing members that the face value and paid-up value of two Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) managed by DSP Asset Managers Private Limited will be changed from Rs. 10 to Re. 1 per unit, effective August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Face Value Split**: The face value and paid-up value of specified ETFs will change from Rs. 10 to Re. 1.
- **Impacted Schemes**:
  1. **DSP Mutual Fund - DSP Gold ETF** (Symbol: `GOLDADD`)
  2. **DSP Mutual Fund - DSP Silver ETF** (Symbol: `SILVERADD`)
- **Effective Date**: The corporate action is set to take effect on Friday, August 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory framework or rule changes are introduced; this is a standard corporate action executed under existing listing guidelines for ETFs.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Back-office databases, trading systems, and customer portfolios must be updated to account for the new face value of Re. 1 and corresponding units adjustment.
- **Asset Management Company (AMC)**: DSP Asset Managers Private Limited must manage operational modifications in alignment with standard procedural requirements for exchange-traded mutual fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 11, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market and Trading Impact**: The reduction in face value from Rs. 10 to Re. 1 is expected to lower the per-unit price, potentially increasing trading liquidity and accessibility for retail investors.
- **Systemic Impact**: Minimal. The corporate action is specific to two thematic commodity ETFs managed by DSP Asset Managers Private Limited, having no broader market-wide implications.
