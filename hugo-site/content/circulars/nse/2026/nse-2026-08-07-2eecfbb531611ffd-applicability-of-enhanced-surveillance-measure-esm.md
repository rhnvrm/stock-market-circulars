## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) to various securities. Based on predefined surveillance criteria, certain securities are shortlisted for inclusion under ESM Stage-I, some are transitioned from Stage-I to Stage-II, others are moved back from Stage-II to Stage-I, and eligible securities are moved out of the framework entirely. This measure is a routine market surveillance action and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Key Points

- **ESM Stage-I Inclusions**: Securities satisfying the criteria are placed in ESM Stage-I and will attract a minimum of 100% margin on all open positions. These securities will also be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).
- **Stage Transitions**: Securities are being transitioned between ESM Stage-I and Stage-II based on surveillance criteria. Stage-II securities are traded under the Trade-for-Trade segment with a 2% price band under the Periodic Call Auction framework.
- **Framework Exclusions**: Securities moving out of the ESM framework will have their price bands reinstated to their pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other active surveillance measures.
- **Participating Securities**: Key securities affected include MOLDTECH, RPPL, NEWJAISA, RAMASTEEL, ASCOM, AVATAR, DIVYADHAN, and NAMOEWASTE.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of newly shortlisted ESM Stage-I securities from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade (Series: BE/ST).
- Implementation of a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction for securities shifting to ESM Stage-II.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for securities moving out of the ESM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must collect a minimum 100% margin on all open positions (as of August 10, 2026) and new positions created from August 11, 2026, for the shortlisted ESM Stage-I securities.
- **Trading Terminals**: Systems must be updated to reflect the shifting of series (EQ/SM to BE/ST) and trading mechanism changes (Periodic Call Auction for Stage-II).

## Important Dates

- **August 10, 2026**:
  - Implementation of Stage-II Periodic Call Auction with 2% price band for transitioning securities.
  - Calculation of open positions for 100% margin requirement.
- **August 11, 2026**:
  - Effective date for minimum 100% margin on ESM Stage-I securities.
  - Shifting of Stage-I securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series BE/ST).

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity Impact**: Securities shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment or Periodic Call Auction (Stage-II) will experience a significant drop in trading volumes and liquidity due to the lack of intraday netting and the restricted 2% price band.
- **Margin & Capital Cost**: Trading members and clients will face 100% margin requirements on positions in affected securities, increasing capital requirements.
- **Corporate Reputation**: The circular clarifies that ESM shortlisting is purely a surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the listed entities.
