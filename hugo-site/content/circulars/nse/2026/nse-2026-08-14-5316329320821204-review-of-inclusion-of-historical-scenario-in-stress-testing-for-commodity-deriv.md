## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the review of the inclusion of historical scenarios in stress testing for the commodity derivatives segment, following SEBI circular dated August 12, 2026. The Z-score threshold used for replacing extreme price movements in peak historical returns has been modified from 10 to 5.

## Key Points

- SEBI reviewed extant provisions related to the Z-score for the commodity derivatives market based on stakeholder representations and Risk Management Review Committee (RMRC) recommendations.
- The Z-score threshold for replacing extreme price movements beyond that threshold in peak historical returns of all commodities has been revised from 10 to 5.
- Mean and sigma of returns over the applicable MPOR period across 15 years continue to be used for calculating the Z-score.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modified Part A of paragraph 22 ("Standardized Stress Testing for Commodity Derivatives") of Annexure O of the SEBI Master Circular for Commodity Derivatives Segment dated August 4, 2023.
- Replaced the Z-score value of 10 with 5 for historical scenario calculations involving peak historical returns.

## Compliance Requirements

- Recognised Clearing Corporations having a Commodity Derivatives Segment must adopt the revised Z-score threshold of 5 for standardized stress testing.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 14, 2026 (SEBI Circular dated August 12, 2026)
- Effective Date: With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances risk management practices and aligns stress testing parameters with revised regulatory standards for clearing corporations handling commodity derivatives.
