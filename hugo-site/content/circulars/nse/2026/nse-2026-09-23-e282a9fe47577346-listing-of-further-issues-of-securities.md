## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of securities for several companies with effect from September 24, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings effective from September 24, 2026.
- Includes equity shares allotted under ESOP for companies such as 360 ONE WAM Limited, Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, and others.
- Trading will be conducted in designated lot sizes as specified in the circular annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate action reflecting capital expansion through ESOP allotments for listed entities.
