## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the admission to dealings of further issues of securities for several companies, effective from August 14, 2026. These include equity shares issued under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and other corporate allocations for companies such as Aether Industries Limited, AU Small Finance Bank Limited, Axis Bank Limited, and others.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Segment:** Capital Market Segment (Equity)
- **Reason for Issuance:** Allotment of equity shares under ESOP / employee schemes.
- **Market Lot:** 1 for the listed securities.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; processed under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the exchange annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Effective Date of Dealing:** August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital expansion and listing of shares issued through ESOPs, leading to a corresponding increase in the paid-up equity base of the respective listed entities without direct operational disruption.
