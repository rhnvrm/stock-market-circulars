## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Axis Nifty500 Low Volatility 50 Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window runs from September 09, 2026, to September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Axis Nifty500 Low Volatility 50 Index Fund (Direct Growth & Regular Growth options available).
- ISINs: INF846KA1614 (Direct Growth), INF846KA1606 (Regular Growth).
- NFO Period: September 09, 2026, to September 22, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- Allotment Date: September 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 28, 2026, till 12:30 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 09, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 22, 2026
- Allotment Date: September 28, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 28, 2026 (12:30 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for trading members participating in mutual fund transactions on the NSE MF platform.
