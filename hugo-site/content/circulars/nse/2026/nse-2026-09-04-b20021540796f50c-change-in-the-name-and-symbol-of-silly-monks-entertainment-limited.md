## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in name and trading symbol of Silly Monks Entertainment Limited effective from September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Silly Monks Entertainment Limited
- New Name: CRESTO TECHNO LIMITED
- Existing Symbol: SILLYMONKS
- New Symbol: CRESTO
- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the change and update their records accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to trading records and client communications reflecting the new name and symbol.
