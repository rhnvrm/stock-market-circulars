## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of the Monarch Overnight Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from September 03, 2026, to September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Monarch Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Monarch Overnight Fund NFO
- Subscription Window: September 03, 2026, to September 07, 2026
- Allotment Date: September 09, 2026
- Category: Liquid Fund

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 09, 2026 till 3:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 03, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 07, 2026
- Allotment Date: September 09, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: September 09, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to transact in various direct and regular plans of the Monarch Overnight Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
