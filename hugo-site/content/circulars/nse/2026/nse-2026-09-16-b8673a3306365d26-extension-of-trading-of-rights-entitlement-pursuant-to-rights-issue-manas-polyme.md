## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced an extension of trading of Rights Entitlement (RE) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Manas Polymers and Energies Limited, modifying the last date for on-market renunciation and the date of suspension.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Manas Polymers and Energies Limited
- Symbol: MPEL-RE
- New Last Date for On-Market Renunciation: September 21, 2026
- New Date of Suspension: September 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Extension of timelines originally communicated via NSE circular reference no. NSE/CML/76206 dated September 04, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the revised dates for on-market renunciation and suspension of trading in the Rights Entitlements.

## Important Dates

- Last Date for On-Market Renunciation (New): September 21, 2026
- Date of Suspension (New): September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors additional time for on-market renunciation of rights entitlements for Manas Polymers and Energies Limited.
