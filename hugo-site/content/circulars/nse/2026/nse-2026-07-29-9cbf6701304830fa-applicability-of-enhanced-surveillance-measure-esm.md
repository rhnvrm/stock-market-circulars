## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has released an update regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Several securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion in ESM Stage I, triggering a shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment and imposing a minimum 100% margin requirement on trading.

## Key Points

- **ESM Stage I Shortlisting:** Three securities (Asahi Songwon Colors Limited, Ganesh Benzoplast Limited, and Optivalue Tek Consulting Limited) are shortlisted for ESM Stage I.
- **Segment Shift:** Securities under ESM Stage I will shift from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. July 31, 2026.
- **Margin Requirement:** A minimum 100% margin will apply w.e.f. July 31, 2026, on all open positions as of July 30, 2026, and on new positions created from July 31, 2026.
- **Stage II Actions:** Securities shifting to Stage II will trade under Periodic Call Auction in the Trade-for-Trade segment with a price band of 2% w.e.f. July 30, 2026.
- **Nature of Measure:** The ESM framework is purely a market surveillance initiative and does not represent adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of the shortlisted securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).
- Mandated 100% margin collection on open and new positions for the affected securities.
- Introduction of a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction for any securities shifting to Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure 100% margin is collected on all open positions as on July 30, 2026, and all new positions created starting July 31, 2026, for the shortlisted securities.
- Compliance with trading limits and segment restrictions (T2T) for the affected symbols must be implemented in order management systems.

## Important Dates

- **July 29, 2026:** Date of circular issuance.
- **July 30, 2026:** Margin calculations begin on existing open positions; Stage II periodic call auction measures take effect.
- **July 31, 2026:** Shift to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series BE/ST) takes effect; 100% margin begins for new positions.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity:** Shifting ASAHISONG, GANESHBE, and OPTIVALUE to the Trade-for-Trade segment prevents intraday trading and is expected to significantly reduce trading volume and liquidity in these counters.
- **Capital Sufficiency:** A 100% margin requirement locks up client capital, elevating transactional barriers for retail participants trading in these counters.
- **Volatility Mitigation:** Tightening price bands to 2% and utilizing call auctions for Stage II assets restricts speculative trading and assists in price discovery without extreme price swings.
