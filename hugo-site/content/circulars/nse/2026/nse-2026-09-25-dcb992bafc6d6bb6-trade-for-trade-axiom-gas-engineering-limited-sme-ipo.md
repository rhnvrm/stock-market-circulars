## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of Axiom Gas Engineering Limited (SME IPO) from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM) with effect from October 12, 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Axiom Gas Engineering Limited
- Symbol: AXIOMGAS
- Segment Change: Transferred from trade for trade segment (ST) to rolling segment (SM)
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026
- Reference: Previous Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76534 dated September 24, 2026, and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities moved from trade for trade segment to normal rolling segment as per standard SME IPO transition guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must update their systems regarding the series change for AXIOMGAS from ST to SM effective October 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates normal rolling settlement for Axiom Gas Engineering Limited shares, enhancing liquidity and trading efficiency for market participants.
