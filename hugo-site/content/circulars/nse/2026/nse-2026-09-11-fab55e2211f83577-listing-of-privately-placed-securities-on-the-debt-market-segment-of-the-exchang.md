## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has notified the admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 11, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted include commercial papers issued by Tata Capital Limited, Power Finance Corporation Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited, JM Financial Services Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, and CEAT Limited.
- Effective date of listing: September 11, 2026.
- Allotment dates range across September 2026 with various maturity profiles up to September 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Issued under standard listing procedures pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure proper settlement and tracking using the specified ISINs and security descriptions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Allotment Dates: September 10, 2026
- Redemption Dates: September 2026 through September 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing update with standard market impact for debt segment participants.
