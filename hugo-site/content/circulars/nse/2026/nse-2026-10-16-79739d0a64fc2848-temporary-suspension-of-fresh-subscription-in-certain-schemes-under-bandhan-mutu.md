## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions in certain schemes under Bandhan Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, specifically impacting Bandhan Gold ETF FOF pursuant to Notice-cum-Addendum No. 47 of 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh subscriptions including lumpsum, switches, fresh SIP registrations, STP registrations (as target scheme), and IDCW transfer-ins are disabled with immediate effect from October 16, 2026.
- Applications received on or before 3:00 p.m. on October 15, 2026, will be processed normally.
- Applications received after the cut-off time on October 15, 2026, are liable to rejection and refund.
- Existing SIP and STP transactions registered on or before October 15, 2026, will continue without change, though fresh top-up registrations are disallowed.
- Redemptions, SWP, switch-outs, and STP-outs remain unaffected and operational.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary operational suspension of fresh inflows into Bandhan Gold ETF FOF as mandated by Bandhan AMC Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and distributors must ensure no fresh lump sum, SIP, or STP orders are placed for the affected scheme on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Cut-off Time for Prior Applications: 3:00 p.m., October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Restricts new capital inflows into Bandhan Gold ETF FOF while protecting existing systematic investors and enabling seamless redemptions.
