## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of 1,20,00,000 equity shares of Jyoti Resins & Adhesives Limited on the Capital Market segment effective from October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Jyoti Resins & Adhesives Limited
- **Symbol:** JYOTIRES
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE577D01013
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- **Number of Securities:** 12,000,000 (Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 12000000)
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Pari Passu:** Yes
- **Lock-in Details:** NA
- **Trading Segment:** Normal Market segment (Rolling Settlement) in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Regulatory Changes

Admission to dealings on the National Stock Exchange Capital Market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and specification of lot sizes under Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

All trading in the equity shares must be conducted in compulsory demat mode using the designated security code and specified market lot size.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 06, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with liquidity and trading access for Jyoti Resins & Adhesives Limited equity shares on NSE's Capital Market segment.
