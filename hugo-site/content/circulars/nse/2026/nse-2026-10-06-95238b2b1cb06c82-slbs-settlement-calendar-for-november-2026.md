## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement calendar for the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for November 2026, pursuant to circular no. NCL/CMPT/73999 dated April 30, 2026. The circular outlines schedules for First Leg Settlement (Settlement Type 'L'), Reverse Leg Settlement (Settlement Type 'P'), and Reverse Leg Auction (Settlement Type 'Q').

## Key Points

- First Leg Settlement (Settlement Type 'L') covers trade dates throughout November 2026 with specific custodial confirmation and settlement dates.
- Reverse Leg Settlement (Settlement Type 'P') details series expiration and settlement dates ranging from November 2026 to November 2027.
- Reverse Leg Auction (Settlement Type 'Q') specifies timelines for series 11/XN with trade date November 3, 2026.
- Incorporates holiday adjustments including Diwali Laxmi Pujan.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework changes; this is a routine periodic settlement calendar issuance.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians must align their clearing and settlement operations with the specified trade dates, custodial confirmation dates, and settlement dates for November 2026.

## Important Dates

- First Leg Trade Dates: November 2, 2026, to November 30, 2026.
- 11/XN Series Reverse Leg Settlement Date: November 3, 2026.
- 12/XD Series Reverse Leg Settlement Date: December 1, 2026.
- New 11/XN Series Availability: Available from November 4, 2026, following the expiry of the current series on November 2, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparent, predictable operational workflows and settlement timelines for participants engaged in securities lending and borrowing activities.
