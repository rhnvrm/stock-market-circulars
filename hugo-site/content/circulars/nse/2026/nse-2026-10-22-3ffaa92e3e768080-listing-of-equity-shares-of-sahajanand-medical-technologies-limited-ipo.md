## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the admission to dealings of equity shares of Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO) on the Capital Market segment effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited
- **Symbol:** SMT
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE356Y01018
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Re. 1/- per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 344/- per share
- **Total Securities Admitted:** 101,646,778 equity shares
- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Registrar:** MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. The listing is conducted pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips as per SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides secondary market liquidity and trading access for equity shares of Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited on the NSE Capital Market segment.
