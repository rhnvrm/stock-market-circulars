## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for specific securities effective October 09, 2026. Avadh Sugar & Energy Limited and Quintegra Solutions Limited are shortlisted under Stage I, while Morepen Laboratories Limited is shifted from Stage I to Stage IV.

## Key Points

- **Avadh Sugar & Energy Limited** (INE349W01017) and **Quintegra Solutions Limited** (INE033B01011) shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I w.e.f. October 09, 2026.
- **Morepen Laboratories Limited** (INE083A01026) shortlisted to move from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I to Stage IV w.e.f. October 09, 2026.
- Quintegra Solutions Limited moved from STASM to LTASM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 13, 2026 on all open positions as on October 12, 2026 and new positions created from October 13, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV, including Morepen Laboratories Limited) shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. October 13, 2026 on a T+3 basis.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for the affected securities starting October 13, 2026.
- Trading in Morepen Laboratories Limited will transition to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 08, 2026
- **Effective Date for ASM Shortlisting:** October 09, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margin (100%) & Trade-for-Trade Settlement:** October 13, 2026 (open positions as on October 12, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in AVADHSUGAR, QUINTEGRA, and MOREPENLAB face heightened margin requirements (100%) and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions (for MOREPENLAB), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in these scrips.
