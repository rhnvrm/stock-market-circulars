## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Karamtara Engineering Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO). Trading will commence on September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Karamtara Engineering Limited
- Symbol: KARAMTARA
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE590T01012
- Issue Price: Rs. 254/- per share
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Total No. of Securities: 321,822,799
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing notification under NSE Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips as per SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 16, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of Karamtara Engineering Limited equity shares following its IPO.
