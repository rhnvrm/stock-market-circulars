## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the shifting of certain securities to the Trade for Trade segment (series: BE/ST) with a price band of 5% or lower, effective October 15, 2026, pursuant to surveillance review.

## Key Points

- Securities shifting from Rolling segment (series: EQ) to Trade for Trade segment (series: BE): EUROTEXIND, GTECJAINX, QUINTEGRA, TARAPUR.
- Securities shifting from Rolling segment (series: SM) to Trade for Trade segment (series: ST): INSPIRE, CEDAAR.
- Criteria for shifting: Price Earnings Multiple, Price Variation & Market Capitalization.
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Certain securities (such as ABAN, ANKITMETAL, CAPTRUST, DSKULKARNI, GFSTEELS, PARSVNATH) will continue in the Trade for Trade segment under the current fortnightly review.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in specified securities is moved to the Trade for Trade segment with a 5% or lower price band, ensuring market safety and investor protection.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions as settlement will be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026
- Circular Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity in the affected securities may be impacted due to the trade-to-trade settlement requirement and 5% price band restriction.
