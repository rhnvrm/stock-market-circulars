## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long-Term ASM framework. Specific securities satisfying the criteria have been shortlisted under Stage I effective October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Shortlisted Security: AAA Technologies Limited (Symbol: AAATECH, ISIN: INE0D0U01013) under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I.
- Applicable rate of margin is set at 100% w.e.f. October 22, 2026 on all open positions as on October 21, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is strictly based on market surveillance parameters and does not represent an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Qualifying securities are subjected to enhanced surveillance measures, including mandatory 100% margins to curb excessive volatility and speculative trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on all open and new positions for the affected scrips starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Shortlisting: October 19, 2026
- 100% Margin Applicability Date: October 22, 2026 (based on open positions as of October 21, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Participants trading in affected securities will face increased capital requirements due to the 100% margin rule, which may affect liquidity and trading volume in these scrips.
