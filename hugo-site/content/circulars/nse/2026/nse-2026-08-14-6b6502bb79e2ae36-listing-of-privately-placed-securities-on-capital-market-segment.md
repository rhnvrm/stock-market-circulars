## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (market-linked debentures) issued by SMFG India Credit Company Limited on the capital market segment, effective from August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** SMFG India Credit Company Limited
- **Symbol:** SICCL29
- **Series:** N0
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Issue Description:** SICCL Mkt Lkd 2029
- **Coupon Rate:** 7.3%
- **Date of Maturity:** 13-Aug-2029
- **ISIN Code:** INE535H07CW9

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the specified securities shall be conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes as per the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** August 13, 2029

## Impact Assessment

Standard listing procedure for privately placed debt securities with no broader market impact.
