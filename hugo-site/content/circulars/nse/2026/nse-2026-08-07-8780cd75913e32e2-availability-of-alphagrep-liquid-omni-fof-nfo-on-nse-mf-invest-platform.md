## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the AlphaGrep Liquid Omni FOF (Fund of Funds) of AlphaGrep Mutual Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic transaction facilities (SIP, STP, and SWP) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. This reopening is effective from August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Reopening**: The AlphaGrep Liquid Omni FOF NFO is reopening for ongoing transactions.
- **Available Facilities**: Subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) will be supported.
- **Affected Platform**: NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Schemes Covered**: Six specific schemes under the Debt category are covered:
  1. AlphaGrep Liquid Omni FOF - Regular - Growth (ISIN: INF2VOA01160)
  2. AlphaGrep Liquid Omni FOF - Regular - IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2VOA01178)
  3. AlphaGrep Liquid Omni FOF - Regular - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF2VOA01186)
  4. AlphaGrep Liquid Omni FOF - Direct - Growth (ISIN: INF2VOA01137)
  5. AlphaGrep Liquid Omni FOF - Direct - IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2VOA01145)
  6. AlphaGrep Liquid Omni FOF - Direct - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF2VOA01152)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced by this circular. It is an operational update regarding the availability of mutual fund schemes on the NSE platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- **NSE MF Members/Distributors**: Ensure that internal systems, transactional portals, and client-facing interfaces are configured to accept subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP orders for the newly available AlphaGrep Liquid Omni FOF ISINs starting August 10, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 07, 2026
- **Effective Date of Reopening**: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. The update is specific to the ongoing availability of a single Fund of Funds on the NSE MF platform.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Mutual fund distributors and transaction intermediaries utilizing the NSE MF Invest Platform need to ensure these schemes and ISINs are active and enabled for client placement.
