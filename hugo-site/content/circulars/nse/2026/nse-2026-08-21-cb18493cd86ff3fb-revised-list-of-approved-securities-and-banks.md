## Summary

NSE has issued a revised list of approved securities and banks, outlining the equity shares that form part of the non-cash component of liquid assets along with their overall permitted quantities and applicable haircuts.

## Key Points

- Contains the updated list of approved securities eligible for non-cash component of liquid assets.
- Specifies overall permitted quantities across all segments and memberwise permitted quantities for the F&O segment.
- Incorporates value-based prudential norms regarding the exclusion of pledged securities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the permissible list and quantities of securities accepted towards margins and liquid assets.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must ensure that the exclusion of pledged securities does not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level, following the prudential norms.

## Important Dates

- Effective as per the circular issuance date.

## Impact Assessment

- Directly affects clearing members and trading participants utilizing equity shares as collateral for margins and liquid assets.
