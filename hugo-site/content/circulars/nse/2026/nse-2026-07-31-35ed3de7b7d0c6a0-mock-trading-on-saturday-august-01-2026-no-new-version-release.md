## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives Segment on Saturday, August 01, 2026. This session will involve trading from both the Primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites, using the existing NEAT version 3.5.4 with no new software release. The primary objective is to test system performance and connectivity.

## Key Points

- **Mock Trading Date:** Saturday, August 01, 2026.
- **Multiple Sessions:** Session 1 will be conducted from the Primary Site (normal market open at 09:00, close at 10:00), and Session 2 will be conducted from the Disaster Recovery (DR) Site (normal market open at 11:45, close at 11:55).
- **Software Version:** No new software version release; members will continue to use NEAT version 3.5.4.
- **Downtime Details:** Connect2NSE and Extranet facilities will be unavailable from 14:30 hours until the end of the mock session. Extranet API will be unavailable from 14:30 to 15:30 hours.
- **Mandatory Re-login:** Members are required to re-login to the live environment between 14:00 and 15:00 hours on August 01, 2026, to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, August 03, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes are introduced by this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Participation:** All members enabled for trading in the Currency Derivatives segment must participate using all trading software.
- **Order Purge:** Since all outstanding orders will be cleared before each mock session, members using Non-NEAT Frontend (NNF) software must manually clear orders in their systems.
- **Client Verification:** Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploads approved before the cutoff will be allowed to trade. Note that UCC validation is skipped during the contingency time for order entry.
- **Post-Mock Check:** Members must perform live re-login verification between 14:00 and 15:00 hours on August 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Session:** Saturday, August 01, 2026
- **Live Re-login Period:** Saturday, August 01, 2026 (14:00 to 15:00 hours)
- **Next Live Trading Session:** Monday, August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** No financial obligation, as trades during mock sessions do not result in fund pay-in or pay-out.
- **Operational Impact:** Low impact. Standard testing of the disaster recovery environment. Brief offline windows for Extranet and Connect2NSE on Saturday afternoon.
