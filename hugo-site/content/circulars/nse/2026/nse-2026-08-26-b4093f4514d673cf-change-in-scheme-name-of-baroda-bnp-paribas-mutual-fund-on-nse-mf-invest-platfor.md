## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for several debt schemes of Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective with immediate effect / August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Baroda BNP Paribas Ultra Short Duration Fund is renamed to Baroda BNP Paribas Ultra Short Term Fund.
- Baroda BNP Paribas Low Duration Fund is renamed to Baroda BNP Paribas Ultra Short to Short Term Fund.
- Baroda BNP Paribas Short Duration Fund is renamed to Baroda BNP Paribas Short Term Fund.
- Baroda BNP Paribas Dynamic Bond Fund is renamed to Baroda BNP Paribas Dynamic Term Fund.
- Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Advantage Fund is renamed to Baroda BNP Paribas Dynamic Asset Allocation Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- Changes carried out in line with Chapter 3, Part IV of the SEBI Master Circular dated March 20, 2026, for alignment with the revised SEBI Categorization and Rationalization Framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors take note of the updated scheme names on the NSE MF Invest platform for future transactions and reporting.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026
- Circular Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact; operational update reflecting scheme name changes and alignment with SEBI categorization frameworks.
