## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the admission and commencement of trading for specific Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on its Capital Market segment, effective from August 14, 2026. This listing is executed under the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- **Listed Securities**: Six new securities are introduced for trading, comprising three State Development Loans (SDLs) and three Treasury Bills (T-bills).
- **Series and Lots**: SDLs are listed under the 'SG' series and T-bills under the 'TB' series, both with a market lot size of 100.
- **Specific Securities Listed**:
  - **SDL AP 7.64% 2056** (Symbol: `764AP56`, ISIN: `IN1020260122`)
  - **SDL AP 7.46% 2035** (Symbol: `746AP35`, ISIN: `IN1020260114`)
  - **SDL PN 7.65% 2043** (Symbol: `765PN43`, ISIN: `IN2820260080`)
  - **GOI TBILL 91D-12/11/26** (Symbol: `91D121126`, ISIN: `IN002026X198`)
  - **GOI TBILL 182D-11/02/27** (Symbol: `182D110227`, ISIN: `IN002026Y196`)
  - **GOI TBILL 364D-12/08/27** (Symbol: `364D120827`, ISIN: `IN002026Z193`)

## Regulatory Changes

- **NSE Trading Regulations**: These actions are pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 (further admission of securities to dealings) and Regulation 2.5.5 (lot sizes specification) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Market participants must update corporate systems and security master databases to include the newly listed securities, symbols, ISINs, and lot sizes ahead of trading commencement.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 14, 2026
- **Maturity Dates**:
  - `764AP56`: August 12, 2056
  - `746AP35`: August 12, 2035
  - `765PN43`: August 12, 2043
  - `91D121126`: November 12, 2026
  - `182D110227`: February 11, 2027
  - `364D120827`: August 12, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Low. Provides additional avenues for sovereign and semi-sovereign fixed-income investment on the exchange segment, standard operational procedures apply.
