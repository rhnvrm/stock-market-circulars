## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Shankesh Jewellers Limited pursuant to its initial public offering (IPO), effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: SHANKESH
- ISIN: INE1WFC01025
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 93/- per share
- Total No. of Securities: 14,70,31,420
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the designated security codes and lot sizes. The security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips as per SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026
- Lock-in details and dates vary as specified in Annexure I (ranging from September 2026 to February 2028, with a portion free).

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Shankesh Jewellers Limited equity shares on the NSE platform.
