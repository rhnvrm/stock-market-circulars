## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041 NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window opens on August 26, 2026, and closes on September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Names: ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041 - Regular Growth and ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041 - Direct Growth
- ISINs: INF109K1A617 (Regular Growth), INF109K1A625 (Direct Growth)
- Category: Equity
- NFO Period: August 26, 2026 to September 09, 2026 (till 11:30 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 11, 2026 till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 26, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 09, 2026
- Clear Funds Submission Deadline: September 11, 2026 (12:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables members and investors to transact in the newly offered ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041 schemes via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
