## Summary

On July 30, 2026, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) issued a final order (WTM/AS/CFD/CFD-SEC-4/32528/2026-27) in the matter of Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited. The order restrains the company and its key individuals from accessing the securities market and prohibits them from buying, selling, or dealing in securities, either directly or indirectly, or being associated with the securities market in any manner. Noticees No. 1 and 2 (Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited and Niranjan D Agarwal) are debarred for a period of 2 years, while Noticees No. 3 and 4 (Aditya N Agarwal and Sunitadevi Niranjan Agarwal) are debarred for a period of 1 year. This order follows the interim directions and order issued on February 10, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Debarred Entities & Individuals**:
  - **Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited** (Noticee No. 1, PAN: AAGCK1765P) is debarred for **2 years**.
  - **Niranjan D Agarwal** (Noticee No. 2, PAN: ADFPA1858G) is debarred for **2 years**.
  - **Aditya N Agarwal** (Noticee No. 3, PAN: AKTPA4024B) is debarred for **1 year**.
  - **Sunitadevi Niranjan Agarwal** (Noticee No. 4, PAN: ADRPS3295H) is debarred for **1 year**.
- **Scope of Restraint**: Prohibited from accessing the securities market and buying, selling, or otherwise dealing in securities, directly or indirectly, or being associated with the securities market in any manner whatsoever.
- **Derivative Positions**: Allowed to close out or square off open derivative positions within 3 months from the date of the order or at contract expiry, whichever is earlier.
- **Obligation Settlement**: Permitted to settle pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions executed before the close of trading on the date of the order (July 30, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- SEBI has issued a final order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1), and 11B(2) of the SEBI Act, read with Rule 4(1) of the SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995.
- The final order supersedes/extends the interim restraint directions issued on February 10, 2025, against the specified entities and individuals.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Exchange Members**: Trading members of the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) must take note of this order and ensure compliance by not facilitating any securities market transactions or associations for the debarred noticees during their debarment period.
- **Noticees**: Must immediately cease all securities market transactions, square off open derivative positions within 3 months, and may settle pending pay-in/pay-out obligations from trades executed before the order date.

## Important Dates

- **Date of SEBI Order & NSE Circular**: July 30, 2026 (Effective Date of debarment)
- **Debarment Periods**:
  - **Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited & Niranjan D Agarwal**: 2 Years (July 30, 2026 to July 29, 2028)
  - **Aditya N Agarwal & Sunitadevi Niranjan Agarwal**: 1 Year (July 30, 2026 to July 29, 2027)
- **Derivative Close-out Deadline**: October 30, 2026 (3 months from order date)

## Impact Assessment

- **Company Operations & Trading**: As Kalahridhaan Trendz Limited (KTL) is a listed entity, a 2-year debarment from accessing the securities market and dealing in securities severely restricts active trading of its equity shares and halts any public market capital activities.
- **Management Impact**: The restraint of key individuals (promoters/directors) prevents them from managing public market operations and engaging in any securities transactions on behalf of the company or themselves.
- **Market Discipline**: Reflects SEBI's continued enforcement and final action following up on interim orders to protect market integrity and investor interest.
