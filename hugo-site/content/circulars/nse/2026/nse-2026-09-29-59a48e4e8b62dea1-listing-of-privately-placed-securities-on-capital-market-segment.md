## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment for Power Grid Corporation of India Limited and Axis Max Life Insurance Limited, effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Power Grid Corporation of India Limited**: Issued PGC 7.74% 2036 LXXXIV (Symbol: 774PGCIL36, ISIN: INE752E08817, Coupon: 7.74%, Maturity: 29-Sep-2036, Market Lot: 1).
- **Axis Max Life Insurance Limited**: Issued AMLIL 8.58% 2036 (Symbol: 858AMLIL36, ISIN: INE511N08040, Coupon: 8.58%, Maturity: 28-Sep-2036, Market Lot: 1).
- Trading shall be conducted using the designated security codes and specified lot sizes pursuant to NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify securities solely by their designated security codes and execute trades in conformity with the specified market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: September 29, 2026
- **Effective Date**: September 30, 2026
- **Maturity Dates**: September 28, 2036 and September 29, 2036

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading and liquidity for the newly admitted privately placed debt instruments.
