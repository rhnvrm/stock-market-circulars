## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. This surveillance measure is applied to specific securities meeting predefined criteria and is aimed at safeguarding market integrity. In this update, four securities have met the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I, which mandates enhanced margin requirements effective from August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusion**: Four securities are shortlisted under Stage I: Dc Infotech And Communication Limited (DCI), Morepen Laboratories Limited (MOREPENLAB), Sumeet Industries Limited (SUMEETINDS), and Total Transport Systems Limited (TOTAL).
- **Stage I Margin Requirement**: The applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum margin cap of 100%. This is effective from August 10, 2026, on all open positions as of August 07, 2026, and new positions created from August 10, 2026.
- **ST-ASM Stage II Margin Requirement**: The applicable margin rate is 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum margin cap of 100%. No new securities were added to Stage II or shifted between stages in this circular.
- **Exclusion**: The list of securities to be excluded from the ASM framework is referenced in Annexure II.
- **Nature of Action**: The Exchange clarified that the shortlisting of securities under ASM is solely for market surveillance purposes and does not indicate any adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised margin rules for ST-ASM Stage I shortlisted securities.
- Enforced compliance with Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) guidelines across trading terminals for the listed entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Collection**: All NSE members and trading brokers must collect the revised higher margin of 50% (or existing higher margin up to 100%) for the specified securities on all open positions as of August 07, 2026, and all new positions created from August 10, 2026.
- **System Updates**: Back-office and risk management systems must be updated to reflect the increased margin requirements for DCI, MOREPENLAB, SUMEETINDS, and TOTAL.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 06, 2026
- **Position Freeze/Observation Date**: August 07, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margin Changes**: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Affected stocks (DCI, MOREPENLAB, SUMEETINDS, TOTAL) may experience temporary pressure on liquidity, reduced speculative trading, and lower trading volumes due to the increased leverage restriction (minimum 50% margin required).
- **Operational Impact**: Brokerages and trading members must ensure their automated risk assessment tools and margin calculation engines are correctly updated to prevent margin shortfalls and potential regulatory penalties.
