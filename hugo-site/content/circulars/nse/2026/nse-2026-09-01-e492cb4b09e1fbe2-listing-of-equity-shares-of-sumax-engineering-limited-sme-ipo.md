## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of equity shares of Sumax Engineering Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform effective from September 02, 2026. The shares will be traded in the Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment (TFTS) initially.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sumax Engineering Limited
- Symbol: SUMAX
- ISIN: INE11Z001019
- Issue Price: Rs. 101/- per share
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Total No. of Securities: 19,022,700
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 19022700
- Market Lot: Minimum 1200 Equity Shares
- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings on the EMERGE SME Platform.
- Trading will initially be in Series 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment - Settlement Type 'W') and subsequently shifted to Series 'SM' (Normal Rolling Segment).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to note the designated security codes, lot sizes, and pre-open session guidelines as per relevant SEBI and Exchange circulars.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Sumax Engineering Limited equity shares on the NSE SME platform.
