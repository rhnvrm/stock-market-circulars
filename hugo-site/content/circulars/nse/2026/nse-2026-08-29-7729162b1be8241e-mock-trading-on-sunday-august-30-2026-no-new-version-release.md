## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Futures & Options (F&O) segment on Sunday, August 30, 2026, operating from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site. There is no new version release associated with this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Sunday, August 30, 2026.
- Trading session conducted from the DR site with specified pre-open and normal market timings.
- Members advised to use NEAT versions 7.8.8 and 7.8.9.
- Discontinuation date for NEATPlus version 7.8.8 is September 05, 2026 (mock).
- Reference price determination for stock future contracts aligned with CAS in the CM segment based on VWAP between 12:00 and 12:15.

## Regulatory Changes

- Alignment of price band for stock future contracts applicable during CAS in the CM segment using VWAP trades executed between 12:00 and 12:15.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members using direct connection via NNF must plan to migrate to new market data broadcast parameters on priority if still using existing parameters.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 30, 2026
- NEATPlus Version 7.8.8 Discontinuation Date: September 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact on live market operations as this is a scheduled weekend mock trading session designed to test system connectivity and readiness.
