## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and commencement of trading of privately placed securities of Ambium Finserve Limited on the capital market segment with effect from October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ambium Finserve Limited
- Symbol / Security Code: 1130AFL28
- Series: N0
- Market Lot: 1
- Issue Description: AFL 11.30% 2028
- Coupon Rate: 11.3%
- Date of Maturity: 12-Oct-2028
- ISIN Code: INE0RU307288

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of privately placed securities to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify securities by their designated codes and adhere to specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date / Commencement of Trading: October 13, 2026
- Maturity Date: October 12, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing providing liquidity and trading access for privately placed debt securities of Ambium Finserve Limited on the NSE platform.
