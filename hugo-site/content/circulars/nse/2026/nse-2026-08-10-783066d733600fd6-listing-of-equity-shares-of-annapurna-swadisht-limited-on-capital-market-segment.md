## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the listing and commencement of trading of the equity shares of **Annapurna Swadisht Limited** (Trading Symbol: **ANNAPURNA**) on the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) of the Exchange. This listing is pursuant to the company's successful migration from the SME Emerge platform to the Main Board. In connection with this transition, the company has submitted its Shareholding Pattern (SHP) as of the quarter ending July 31, 2026, under Regulation 31(1)(b) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- **Migration to Main Board:** Annapurna Swadisht Limited has officially migrated from the NSE Emerge (SME) platform to the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) of the NSE.
- **Capital Structure:** The company's total paid-up equity share capital consists of 21,820,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹10 each.
- **Promoter Shareholding:**
  - There are **4** promoter shareholders holding **8,887,000** equity shares, representing **40.73%** of the total equity capital.
  - Out of these shares, **7,115,000** shares (**80.06%** of the promoter group holding and **32.61%** of the total equity of the company) are **pledged or otherwise encumbered**.
- **Public Shareholding:**
  - There are **4,206** public shareholders holding **12,933,000** equity shares, representing **59.27%** of the total equity capital.
- **Other Declarations:**
  - The company has no partly paid-up shares, outstanding convertible securities, warrants, ESOPs, or depository receipts.
  - None of the shares held by promoters are locked-in.
  - There are no shares held by promoters subject to a Non-Disposal Undertaking (NDU) or other encumbrances besides the pledge.
  - The company has not issued equity shares with differential voting rights and has no significant beneficial owners (SBO) disclosed.

## Regulatory Changes

- With the migration from the SME platform to the Capital Market Segment (Main Board), the company transitions to a more rigorous regulatory reporting environment, becoming subject to the more stringent compliance, disclosure, and continuous reporting requirements applicable to Main Board listed entities under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Ongoing Disclosures:** The company must now comply with Chapter IV of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, which dictates disclosure requirements for Main Board listed entities. This includes quarterly filing of shareholding patterns, financial results, and corporate governance reports within the stipulated timelines.
- **Promoter Encumbrances:** Given that **80.06%** of the promoter holding is pledged, any change in promoter encumbrance/pledge status must be promptly disclosed to the stock exchanges under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) and SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Important Dates

- **Shareholding Pattern Reference Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Circular Release / Effective Date:** August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity & Investor Reach:** Migration to the Main Board is expected to significantly enhance trading liquidity and open up investment opportunities to a broader institutional and retail investor base who may be restricted from trading on SME platforms.
- **Risk Assessment (Promoter Pledge):** A high level of promoter pledge (80.06% of their total holding) is a critical risk factor for investors to monitor. If the stock price experiences significant downward pressure, it could trigger margin calls and lead to involuntary sale of pledged shares by lenders, potentially impacting the stock price further.
