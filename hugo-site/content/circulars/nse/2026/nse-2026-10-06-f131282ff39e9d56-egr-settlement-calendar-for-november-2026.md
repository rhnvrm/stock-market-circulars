## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement calendar for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment for the month of November 2026 in pursuance of Regulation 6.2 of the NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997). The circular outlines the normal T+1 settlement schedules (Type 6) and auction settlement schedules (Type 7).

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment for November 2026.
- Details Normal T+1 settlement type '6' schedule in Annexure A.
- Details Auction settlement type '7' schedule in Annexure B.
- Specifies handling of shortages and auction conducted dates for specific settlement numbers.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine calendar notification pursuant to Regulation 6.2 of the NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must adhere to the specified trade start dates, trade end dates, and settlement dates for normal and auction settlements in the EGR segment during November 2026.

## Important Dates

- Settlement schedules apply throughout November 2026, commencing from November 02, 2026, through December 02, 2026 (for final auction settlement).

## Impact Assessment

Operational and settlement clarity for market participants trading in Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) on the NSE platform during November 2026.
