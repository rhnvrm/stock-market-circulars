## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). In reference to previous circulars, five securities have met the criteria to move to the stage of "Receipt of corporate announcement / disclosure from the company" under the ASM IBC framework, effective from August 03, 2026. No securities were shortlisted for Stage I, Stage II, or other staged movements in this update.

## Key Points

- **Applicability:** The updated ASM IBC list is effective from August 03, 2026.
- **Affected Securities:** Five securities are transitioning to the "Receipt of corporate announcement / disclosure from the company" stage under the ASM IBC framework:
  - AGS Transact Technologies Limited (AGSTRA)
  - CLC Industries Limited (CLCIND)
  - Gensol Engineering Limited (GENSOL)
  - Impex Ferro Tech Limited (IMPEXFERRO)
  - Reliance Infrastructure Limited (RELINFRA)
- **Status of Stage I & Stage II:** No new securities have been added to ASM IBC Stage I or migrated between Stage I and Stage II in this circular.
- **Nature of Action:** Shortlisting of securities under ASM IBC is purely a market surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the shortlisted companies.
- **Price Band Reinstatement:** When a security moves out of the framework, its price band will be reinstated to the one applicable before its shortlisting, provided it is not subject to other surveillance frameworks.

## Regulatory Changes

The ASM framework for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) operates under the rules established by previous Exchange circulars (NSE/SURV/37979, NSE/SURV/52368, NSE/SURV/55582, and NSE/SURV/70074). This circular updates the status of securities subject to surveillance under these guidelines but does not introduce new regulatory framework rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** NSE members are advised to note the changes in the ASM stages of the specified securities and ensure that relevant trading and margin parameters are applied.
- **Market Participants:** Participants must ensure compliance with all prevailing risk management and margin guidelines associated with securities under ASM IBC.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading & Volatility:** The move of the five specified securities to the corporate announcement disclosure stage under ASM IBC may impose higher margin requirements or trading limits, aimed at controlling speculative volatility.
- **Price Bands:** The price bands for these securities will be governed by the specific surveillance framework, potentially restricting their daily price movement.
- **Corporate Reputation:** As noted by NSE, this shortlisting is purely a surveillance mechanism and does not represent any adverse finding or punitive regulatory action against the companies.
