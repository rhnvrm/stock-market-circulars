## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Mirae Asset Life Cycle Fund 2056 on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from September 28, 2026, to October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Availability:** Mirae Asset Life Cycle Fund 2056 NFO is available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **NFO Window:** September 28, 2026, up to October 12, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- **Available Plans/Options:** Includes Direct and Regular plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- **ISINs Covered:** INF769K01RU2, INF769K01RW8, INF769K01RV0, INF769K01RR8, INF769K01RT4, INF769K01RS6.
- **Allotment Date:** Scheduled for October 19, 2026 across all listed scheme variants.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational circular for mutual fund scheme introduction.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that clear funds through authorized payment modes are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 16, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** September 28, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** October 12, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** October 16, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless access for members and investors to subscribe to the Mirae Asset Life Cycle Fund 2056 NFO through the NSE MF Invest infrastructure with defined timelines for fund clearing and allotment.
