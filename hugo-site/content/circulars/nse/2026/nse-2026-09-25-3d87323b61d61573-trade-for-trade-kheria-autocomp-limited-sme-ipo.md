## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Kheria Autocomp Limited from the trade-for-trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (series: SM), pursuant to SEBI guidelines and earlier exchange circulars.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kheria Autocomp Limited
- Symbol: KHERIAAUTO
- Segment Shift: Transferred from trade-for-trade segment (series: ST) to rolling segment (series: SM)
- Effective Date: October 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are moving out of the mandatory trade-for-trade settlement into the regular rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members need to note the change in series and settlement mechanics for Kheria Autocomp Limited effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date of Segment Transfer: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Allows normal rolling settlement for Kheria Autocomp Limited shares, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility for market participants.
