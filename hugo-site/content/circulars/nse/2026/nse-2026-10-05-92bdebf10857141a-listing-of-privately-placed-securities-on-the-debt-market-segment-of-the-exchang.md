## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities, including Commercial Papers (CPs) and other debt issues, on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 05, 2026.
- Fresh Issue of Commercial Papers by: HDFC Securities Limited, Godrej Agrovet Limited, IIFL Capital Services Limited, and Tata Steel Limited.
- Other Debt Issues / Bonds / Perpetual Securities by: Kaleidofin Capital Private Limited, Vikabh Securities Pvt Ltd, IIFL Finance Limited, Apollo Tyres Limited, and Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited.
- Allotment dates span September 30, 2026, and October 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must adhere to the trading and settlement regulations of the NSE debt market segment for the admitted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date / Effective Date: October 05, 2026
- Allotment Dates: September 30, 2026 / October 01, 2026
- Redemption / Maturity Dates: Ranging from December 2026 to October 2028 (with perpetual or 2036 call option dates for specific perpetual debt).

## Impact Assessment

Provides official trading and listing platforms for corporate debt issuances and commercial papers on NSE.
