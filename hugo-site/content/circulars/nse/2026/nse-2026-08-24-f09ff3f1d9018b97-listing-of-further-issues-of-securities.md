## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for several companies, including Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited, Brigade Enterprises Limited, Creditaccess Grameen Limited, Indegene Limited, and others, effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Further equity shares admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment).
- Effective date for trading of these securities is August 25, 2026.
- Shares are allotted under employee stock option plans (ESOP) or similar corporate mechanisms.
- Designated security codes and lot sizes apply as specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further issues of securities pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure trading is conducted using designated security codes and applicable market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026
- Allotment Dates vary by company (e.g., August 18, 2026 for Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC; August 12, 2026 for Brigade Enterprises; August 20, 2026 for Creditaccess Grameen).

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital increase and addition of shares to the trading segment with no significant direct market or operational disruption.
