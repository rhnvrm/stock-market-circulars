## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a revision of the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) and position limits applicable to Participants, Institutional clients, and Non-Institutional clients for NATCOPHARM under the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) Scheme for October 2026. The revised limits take effect from October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol affected: NATCOPHARM
- Scheme: Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLB)
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026
- Revised MWPL: 9,133,860 shares (up from 9,059,474 shares)
- Institutional Client Limit: 913,386 shares (up from 905,947 shares)
- Participant Limit: 913,386 shares (up from 905,947 shares)
- Non-Institutional Client Limit: 91,338 shares (up from 90,594 shares)

## Regulatory Changes

The position limits for NATCOPHARM in the SLB segment have been updated pursuant to circular NCL/CMPT/73999 (dated April 30, 2026) and partial modification of circular NCL/CMPT/76656 (dated September 30, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians must ensure that trading and position limits in the SLB segment for NATCOPHARM adhere to the newly revised thresholds effective October 5, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine revision affecting market participants trading in the SLB segment for NATCOPHARM, providing marginally higher position and participation headroom.
