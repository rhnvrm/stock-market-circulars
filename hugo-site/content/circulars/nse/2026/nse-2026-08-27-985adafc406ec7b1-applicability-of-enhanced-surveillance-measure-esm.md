## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability and stage changes of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, effective from August 31, 2026 (with certain changes effective August 28, 2026).

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying inclusion criteria attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 31, 2026, on open positions as on August 28, 2026, and new positions created from August 31, 2026.
- EEPL shortlisted under ESM Stage - I.
- ABM International Limited (ABMINTLLTD) and Uma Exports Limited (UMAEXPORTS) move from Stage - I to Stage - II.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 31, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures in accordance with prior Exchange circulars concerning the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and segment shifts for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II price band and movement: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date for margin requirements and segment shift (EQ/SM to BE/ST): August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities face increased margin requirements and transfer to the Trade-for-Trade segment, potentially impacting liquidity and trading mechanisms.
