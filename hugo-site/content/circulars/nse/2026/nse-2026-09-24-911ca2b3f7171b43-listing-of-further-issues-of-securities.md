## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of equity shares for multiple companies, effective from September 25, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Further issues of equity shares admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective September 25, 2026.
- Includes companies such as Alkyl Amines Chemicals Limited, Asian Energy Services Limited, Axis Bank Limited, Coforge Limited, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited, ITC Limited, Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Limited, Nippon Life India Asset Management Limited, Protean eGov Technologies Limited, SBI Life Insurance Company Limited, Sterlite Technologies Limited, and Wipro Limited.
- Shares issued primarily under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) with face value and paid-up value matching existing shares.
- Lot size for trading is 1 with Pari Passu ranking and no lock-in details applicable for the highlighted ESOP allotments.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard listing approval under NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies must ensure compliance with designated security codes, distinctive number ranges, and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on market liquidity and share capital structure, representing routine incremental capital expansion via ESOP allotments.
