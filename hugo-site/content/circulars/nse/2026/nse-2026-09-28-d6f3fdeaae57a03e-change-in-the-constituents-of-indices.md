## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a change in the constituents of the Nifty 50 index. Wipro Ltd. is being excluded from the index, and BSE Ltd. is being included.

## Key Points

- Index Affected: Nifty 50
- Excluded Company: Wipro Ltd. (Symbol: WIPRO)
- Included Company: BSE Ltd. (Symbol: BSE)
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026 (at the close of September 29, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their index tracking systems, derivative contract reference parameters, and algorithmic trading models accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Press Release Date: August 10, 2026
- Effective Date / Implementation: September 30, 2026 (Close of September 29, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Passive index funds, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), and futures and options contracts tracking the Nifty 50 will rebalance their portfolios to reflect the inclusion of BSE Ltd. and the exclusion of Wipro Ltd., leading to potential rebalancing trades and volume shifts.
