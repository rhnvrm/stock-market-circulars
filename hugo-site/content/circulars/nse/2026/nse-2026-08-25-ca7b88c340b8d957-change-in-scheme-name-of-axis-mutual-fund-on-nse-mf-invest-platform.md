## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for several schemes of Axis Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Axis Ultra Short Duration Fund is renamed to Axis Ultra Short Term fund.
- Axis Short Duration Fund is renamed to Axis Short Term Fund.
- Axis Long Duration Fund is renamed to Axis Long Term Fund.
- Axis Dynamic Bond Fund is renamed to Axis Dynamic Term Fund.
- Axis Floater Fund is renamed to Axis Floating Interest Rates Fund.
- Axis Strategic Bond Fund is renamed to Axis Medium Term Fund.
- Axis Treasury Advantage Fund is renamed to Axis Ultra Short to Short Term Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

Changes are executed pursuant to the SEBI Circular dated February 26, 2026, on Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

All references to the existing scheme names stand replaced with the revised names across all platform records and documents from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on investors and trading members, limited to administrative updating of scheme names across documentation and platforms.
