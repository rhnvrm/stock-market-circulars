## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Uday Jewellery Industries Limited (Symbol: UDAYJEW) effective from September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: UDAYJEW
- ISIN: INE551B01012
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- Total No. of Securities: 34,052,925 equity shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 37,215,825 (reflecting 31,62,900 forfeited shares)
- Effective Date of Trading: September 25, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Segment: Normal Market segment (Rolling Settlement) in compulsory demat

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of further securities to dealings on the National Stock Exchange Capital Market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be in compulsory demat mode for all investors under the designated symbol and security codes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public trading of Uday Jewellery Industries Limited equity shares on the NSE main board, increasing market liquidity and investor reach.
