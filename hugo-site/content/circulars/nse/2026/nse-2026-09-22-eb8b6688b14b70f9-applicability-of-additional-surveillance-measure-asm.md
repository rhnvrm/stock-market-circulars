## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Certain securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LTASM) or have been shifted between various stages effective September 23, 2026, with applicable margin rates effective September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I w.e.f. September 23, 2026: Aarey Drugs & Pharmaceuticals Limited, Indo Rama Synthetics (India) Limited, Paisalo Digital Limited, and Raymond Limited.
- Securities shortlisted to move from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I to Stage-II w.e.f. September 23, 2026: Alkali Metals Limited and Xtranet Technologies Limited.
- Securities shortlisted to move from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I to Stage-IV w.e.f. September 23, 2026: Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 25, 2026, on all open positions as on September 24, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. September 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for securities under the surveillance framework.
- Reclassification of eligible stage-IV securities from rolling settlement to trade-for-trade settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that 100% margins are collected for open positions and new positions created in the affected securities starting September 25, 2026.
- Market participants must account for the shift of specified scrips to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Surveillance Action / Stage Shifting: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin Rate and Trade-for-Trade Segment Shifting: September 25, 2026 (on open positions as on September 24, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin obligations (100%) will increase capital requirements for trading participants holding or taking positions in the impacted scrips.
- Shifting to trade-for-trade settlement for stage IV scrips eliminates intraday netting and may impact liquidity and trading volumes.
