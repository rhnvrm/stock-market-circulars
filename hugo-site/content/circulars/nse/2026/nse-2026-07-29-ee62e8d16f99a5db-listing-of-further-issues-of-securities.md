## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has approved the listing and trading of further equity shares of Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DIACABS) with effect from July 30, 2026. A total of 71,100,000 equity shares of face value Re. 1/- each, allotted under a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) on July 28, 2026, have been admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited
- **Symbol:** DIACABS
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE989C01038
- **No. of Securities:** 71,100,000 equity shares
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 227/- per share
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Re. 1/- each
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 526971061 to 598071060
- **Date of Allotment:** July 28, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Pari Passu:** Yes
- **Lock-in Details:** Not Applicable
- **Temporary ISIN:** Equity shares credited under temporary ISIN (IN8*********) in accordance with SEBI guidelines before the activation of the permanent ISIN.

## Regulatory Changes

This circular is issued in accordance with:
- **Regulation 3.1.1** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, concerning listing of securities.
- **Regulation 2.5.5** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, concerning lot size specifications.
- **SEBI Circulars CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012** (August 02, 2012) and **CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012** (September 11, 2012) regarding activation of ISINs in case of additional issue of shares / securities.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance action is required from market participants or trading members. Members should update their security databases to reflect the listing of additional equity shares for Diamond Power Infrastructure Limited (DIACABS) starting from July 30, 2026. 

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** July 28, 2026
- **Date of Circular:** July 29, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing and Trading:** July 30, 2026
- **SEBI Circulars Reference Dates:** August 02, 2012, and September 11, 2012

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. The addition of 71,100,000 shares will increase the outstanding share capital and potentially enhance trading liquidity for DIACABS.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. The shares will be traded in standard lots of 1 under the EQ series, with temporary depository credit under standard SEBI procedures.
