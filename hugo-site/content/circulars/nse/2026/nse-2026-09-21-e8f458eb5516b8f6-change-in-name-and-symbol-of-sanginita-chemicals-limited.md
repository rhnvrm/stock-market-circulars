## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in name and trading symbol of Sanginita Chemicals Limited effective from September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Sanginita Chemicals Limited
- New Company Name: Agastya Infrastructure Limited
- Existing Symbol: SANGINITA
- New Symbol: AGASTYA
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records and trading terminals with the new name and symbol for Sanginita Chemicals Limited from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Market participants need to ensure systems reflect the updated symbol and company name for order matching and reporting.
