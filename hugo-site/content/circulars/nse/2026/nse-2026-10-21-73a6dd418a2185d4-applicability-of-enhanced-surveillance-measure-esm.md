## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The circular specifies securities shortlisted for inclusion under ESM Stage - I, securities moving from Stage I to Stage II, and the corresponding trading and settlement restrictions.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ESM Stage - I attract a minimum 100% margin effective October 23, 2026, on all open positions as on October 22, 2026, and new positions created from October 23, 2026.
- Securities shifting from Stage I to Stage II (Cubex Tubings Limited, ICDS Limited, Valiant Laboratories Limited) are subject to Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction effective October 22, 2026.
- Shortlisted securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) effective October 23, 2026.
- The shortlisting is strictly on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance measures, including 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement, and periodic call auction price bands for identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin collection on open and new positions for affected securities and adhere to trade-for-trade settlement rules.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for Stage II shifts and price bands: October 22, 2026.
- Effective date for 100% margins and segment transfer (EQ/SM to BE/ST): October 23, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading mechanisms for shortlisted securities are impacted due to mandatory trade-for-trade settlement, upfront margin requirements, and periodic call auctions, serving as a risk mitigation measure.
