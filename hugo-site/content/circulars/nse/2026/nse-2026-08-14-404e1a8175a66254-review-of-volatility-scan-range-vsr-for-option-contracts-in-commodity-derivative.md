## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the review of the Volatility Scan Range (VSR) for option contracts in the commodity derivatives segment for the month of September 2026, in continuation of earlier circulars and SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Applicable VSR for September 2026 is specified for six key commodities including variants.
- Copper, Zinc, and Gold have a VSR of 5%.
- Silver and Natural Gas have a VSR of 6%.
- Crude Oil has a VSR of 30%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic review of Volatility Scan Range (VSR) for option contracts in compliance with SEBI guidelines (circular SEBI/HO/CDMRD/DRMP/CIR/P/2021/08 dated January 11, 2021).

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must take note of the revised VSR percentages while calculating margins for commodity option contracts effective from September 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Month: September 2026
- Circular Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects option margin calculations and risk management parameters for commodity derivatives traders and clearing members dealing in the specified commodities.
