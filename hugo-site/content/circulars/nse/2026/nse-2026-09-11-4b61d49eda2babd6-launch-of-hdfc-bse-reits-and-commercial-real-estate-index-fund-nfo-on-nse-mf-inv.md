## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the HDFC BSE REITS and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Fund House: HDFC Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: HDFC BSE REITS and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund (Direct and Regular options available in Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment variants)
- Subscription Window: September 15, 2026, to September 17, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 22, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 15, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 17, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 22, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 22, 2026 (till 03:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to transact in the new HDFC Mutual Fund index scheme via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
