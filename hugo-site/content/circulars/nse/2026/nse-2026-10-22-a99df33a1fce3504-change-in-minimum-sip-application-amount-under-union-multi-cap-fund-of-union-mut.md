## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in minimum Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) application amount under Union Multi Cap Fund of Union Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- The minimum SIP application amount for Union Multi Cap Fund has been reduced from Rs. 500 to Rs. 250 (in multiples of Re. 1 thereafter).
- This change applies to Weekly, Fortnightly, and Monthly frequencies.
- The revision is effective from October 22, 2026, as per the notice-cum-addendum issued by Union Mutual Fund dated October 16, 2026.
- Existing systemic transactions registered prior to the effective date remain unaffected.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of scheme specific terms in the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) regarding minimum SIP application and purchase amounts for Union Multi Cap Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors trading via NSE MF Invest Platform to note the revised minimum SIP amounts for Union Multi Cap Fund.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Lower entry barrier for retail investors participating in SIPs under Union Multi Cap Fund through reduced minimum investment thresholds.
