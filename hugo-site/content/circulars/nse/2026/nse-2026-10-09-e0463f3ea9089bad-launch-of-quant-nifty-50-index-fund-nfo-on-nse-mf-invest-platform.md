## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of the Quant Nifty 50 Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) by Quant Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The NFO subscription window is open from October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund Name:** Quant Nifty 50 Index Fund (Available in Direct and Regular plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- **NFO Window:** October 12, 2026 to October 26, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **ISINs Covered:** INF966L01EW6, INF966L01EX4, INF966L01EV8, INF966L01EZ9, INF966L01FA9, INF966L01EY2.

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are requested to ensure that clear funds through any accepted payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 28, 2026, till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** October 12, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** October 26, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** October 28, 2026 (12:00 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides seamless subscription access for investors and members to participate in the Quant Nifty 50 Index Fund NFO through NSE's mutual fund infrastructure.
