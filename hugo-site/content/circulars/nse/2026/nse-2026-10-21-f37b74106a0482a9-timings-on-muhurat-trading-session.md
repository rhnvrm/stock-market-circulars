## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the operational guidelines, settlement schedules, client margin reporting cut-offs, and collateral timings in view of the special Muhurat Trading session scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026, on account of Diwali.

## Key Points

- **Risk Parameters:** Two risk parameter files based on prices of securities at previous close and at the end of the day will be provided.
- **Settlement Schedule:** Pay-in/pay-out transactions for trade dates November 06, 2026, and November 08, 2026, will be settled on November 09, 2026, at 8:30 hrs.
- **Custodian Participant (CP) Code Modification:** Timings for CP code modification and give-up confirmation for trades done on November 08, 2026, are set between 14:20 hrs. and 15:40 hrs.
- **Collateral Timings:** Cash addition (12:15 to 16:15), allocation of collaterals (11:45 to 19:00), securities release request cut-off (up to 16:15), and cash release for same-day deposits (12:15 to 16:15).

## Regulatory Changes

No structural regulatory changes; outlines operational procedures and schedules specific to the Diwali Muhurat Trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must adhere to the revised client margin reporting schedule and collateral submission timelines specified for the Muhurat Trading session.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Muhurat Trading Date:** November 08, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational schedules and clearing timelines are adjusted to accommodate the holiday trading session with minimal disruption to normal clearing and settlement workflows.
