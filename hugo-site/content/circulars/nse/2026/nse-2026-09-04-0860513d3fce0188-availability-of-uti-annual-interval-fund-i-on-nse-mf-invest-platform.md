## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of UTI Annual Interval Fund - I on the NSE MF Invest Platform for a specified transaction period from September 07, 2026, to September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes will be available for subscription and redemption during the specified transaction period.
- Transaction requests will be accepted during normal business hours from September 07, 2026, to September 08, 2026 (noting a typographical reference in the source text).
- Applicable NAV will be as per the cut-off timing prescribed based on the date and time of receipt of transaction and subject to fund confirmation.
- Covers multiple plans including Direct and Regular plans for IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment, and Growth under Debt category.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are submitted in accordance with the scheme cut-off timings on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Transaction Period Start Date: September 07, 2026
- Transaction Period End Date: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to transact in the UTI Annual Interval Fund - I schemes through the NSE platform within the designated window.
