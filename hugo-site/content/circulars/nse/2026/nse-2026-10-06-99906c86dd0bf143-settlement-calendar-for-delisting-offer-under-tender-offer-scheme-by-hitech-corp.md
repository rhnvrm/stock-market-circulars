## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued the settlement calendar for the delisting offer under the tender offer scheme by Hitech Corporation Limited under Settlement Type X and Settlement Number 2026119. Trading is scheduled from October 7, 2026, to October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: X
- Settlement Number: 2026119
- Trade Start Date: 07-OCT-2026
- Trade End Date: 13-OCT-2026
- Obligation Download Date: 14-OCT-2026
- Settlement Date: 15-OCT-2026
- Demat shares must be tendered through the early pay-in mechanism for Market Type Buyback.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational schedule and timeline notification for the delisting offer settlement process.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure early pay-in of shares well in advance on the trade end date up to 03:30 p.m.
- Securities and funds pay-out will be processed post receipt of fund pay-in on the settlement day.
- Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.
