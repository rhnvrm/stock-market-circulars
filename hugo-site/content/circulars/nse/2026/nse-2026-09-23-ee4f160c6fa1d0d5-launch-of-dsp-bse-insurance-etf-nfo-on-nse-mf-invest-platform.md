## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the DSP BSE Insurance ETF NFO from DSP Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- NFO Period: September 24, 2026 to September 28, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- Schemes available: DSP BSE Insurance ETF - Regular Growth and DSP BSE Insurance ETF - Direct Growth.
- ISIN: INF740KA1ZR8 for both schemes.
- Category: Equity.
- Allotment Date: September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 29, 2026 till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open: September 24, 2026
- NFO Close: September 28, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 29, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables members and investors to transact in the new ETF scheme via the NSE MF Invest Platform within the specified subscription window.
