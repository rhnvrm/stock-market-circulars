## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the revision of the temporary subscription limit for the DSP Multi Asset Allocation Fund under DSP Mutual Fund. The maximum subscription amount has been revised from ₹10 lakh to ₹30 lakh per transaction/instalment per investor per day.

## Key Points

- The subscription limit for DSP Multi Asset Allocation Fund has been revised from ₹10 lakh to ₹30 lakh per transaction/instalment per investor per day.
- The change takes effect from October 12, 2026.
- Applies to all subscription transactions (including lump sum, switch-in, new SIP/STP-in registrations, IDCW transfer) received after the cut-off time (3:00 p.m.) on October 09, 2026.
- Systematic transactions registered prior to the effective date and IDCW reinvestment options will continue to allot units.
- Intra-scheme and intra-plan switches will continue to be accepted irrespective of amount.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amendment to the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) of DSP Multi Asset Allocation Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors on the NSE MF Invest Platform must note the revised temporary restriction limit of ₹30 lakh per day per investor for the scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 09, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Applicability Cut-off:** Subscription transactions received after 3:00 p.m. on October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors looking to invest larger lump sum amounts or systematic investments in DSP Multi Asset Allocation Fund now have an increased daily cap of ₹30 lakh instead of the previous ₹10 lakh limit.
