## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular following SEBI's directive extending the timeline for the implementation of provisions relating to base price, price bands, call auction in the pre-open session, and close-out procedures for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs). The new effective date is September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Implementation timeline for SEBI Circular dated June 15, 2026, regarding ETF norms has been extended.
- The provisions will now come into effect from September 7, 2026, instead of September 1, 2026.
- All other provisions of the original SEBI circular remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Timeline extension granted based on feedback received from Stock Exchanges to ensure smooth implementation.
- Effective date revised from September 1, 2026 to September 7, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs) must put in place necessary systems and make required byelaw/rule amendments.
- Market participants and investors must be notified of the revised timeline.

## Important Dates

- Original Effective Date: September 1, 2026
- Revised Effective Date: September 7, 2026
- Circular Date: August 31, 2026 (SEBI circular dated August 28, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants and AMCs additional time to configure systems and ensure compliance with new ETF trading norms and close-out procedures.
