## Summary

On August 03, 2026, the National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) issued circular Ref. No. 583/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/SURV/75543) detailing the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). These measures, implemented in conjunction with existing surveillance protocols, are part of the market surveillance framework and are not intended as adverse actions against the listed companies.

## Key Points

- **New Shortlisting (Annexure I):** TV Vision Limited (TVVISION, ISIN: INE871L01013) has satisfied the criteria and is shortlisted under the 'Receipt of corporate announcement / disclosure from the company' category, effective August 04, 2026.
- **No New Stage-Specific Exclusions (Annexure II):** No securities were excluded from the ASM IBC list or transitioned between Stage I and Stage II as of this update.
- **Consolidated ASM (IBC) Status:**
  - Neueon Corporation Limited (NEUEON, ISIN: INE333I01044) remains in Stage I.
  - Aban Offshore Limited (ABAN, ISIN: INE421A01028) remains in the 'Receipt of corporate announcement / disclosure from the company' category.
  - AGS Transact Technologies Limited (AGSTRA, ISIN: INE583L01014) is in the 'Receipt of corporate announcement' category.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the active monitoring roster of securities under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework tailored for companies undergoing insolvency resolution (IBC) in accordance with the exchange's ongoing surveillance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading and Surveillance Systems:** Trading members must update their internal risk management and surveillance systems to incorporate the latest changes to the ASM IBC security classifications, specifically for TV Vision Limited (TVVISION).
- **Margin and Pricing Bands:** Members must ensure that appropriate margins, trading limits, and pricing bands are applied to the active and newly shortlisted securities listed in the circular.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular:** August 03, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Affected securities (TVVISION, NEUEON, ABAN, AGSTRA) may witness altered liquidity, modified pricing bands, or enhanced margin requirements as a result of their continuing or new inclusion in the ASM IBC framework.
- **Operational Impact:** Low to medium operational impact. Risk and compliance departments of trading members must update their security lists to track the updated surveillance statuses.
