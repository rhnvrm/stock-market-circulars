## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued circular Ref. No: 595/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/SURV/75608) dated August 06, 2026, regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The circular notifies market participants about the revision of surveillance stages for specific securities and updates to the consolidated list of securities under the ESM framework. Specifically, three securities are shifting from Stage I to Stage II, which triggers stricter trading and margin restrictions.

## Key Points

- **Framework Inclusion and Stage Shifts**: Securities are shortlisted under ESM solely based on objective market surveillance parameters. This shortlisting is a preventive measure and is not an adverse regulatory action against the concerned companies.
- **Stage II Transition**: Three securities are transitioning from ESM Stage I to Stage II: ATC Energies System Limited (ATCENERGY), Lyka Labs Limited (LYKALABS), and OnMobile Global Limited (ONMOBILE).
- **Consolidated List**: A consolidated list of securities under the ESM framework is provided in Annexure III, which includes Accord Synergy Limited (ACCORD) and Amanta Healthcare Limited (AMANTA) under Stage II, and Apsis Aerocom Limited (APSISAERO).
- **No Direct Inclusions or Exclusions**: There are no new direct inclusions to Stage I (listed as Nil under Annexure I) and no exclusions from the ESM framework in this update (listed as Nil under Annexure II).

## Regulatory Changes

- **Settlement Segment Shift**: Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) starting August 10, 2026.
- **Trading Restrictions under Stage II**: Securities transitioning to Stage II are placed under the Trade-for-Trade series with a restricted price band of 2% and are tradable only under the Periodic Call Auction mechanism starting August 07, 2026.
- **Margin Requirements**: Shifting securities are subject to a minimum of 100% margin on all open positions as of August 07, 2026, and all new positions created from August 10, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Maintenance**: Trading members and market participants must ensure a minimum of 100% margin is collected and maintained for all open positions of the affected securities starting August 07, 2026, and for all new positions from August 10, 2026.
- **Segment Handling**: Members must update their front-end trading systems to reflect the shift of Series from EQ/SM to BE/ST for the affected securities on the effective date.
- **Trading Limits and Order Placement**: Member systems must accommodate the 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction order matching process for Stage II securities.

## Important Dates

- **August 06, 2026**: Circular issuance date.
- **August 07, 2026**: 
  - Shift of transitioning Stage II securities (ATCENERGY, LYKALABS, ONMOBILE) to Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction.
  - Requirement of 100% margin on all open positions as of this date.
- **August 10, 2026**: 
  - Transitioning ESM securities shift from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade (Series: BE/ST).
  - Minimum 100% margin applies to all new positions created on or after this date.

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity Impact**: The transition of ATCENERGY, LYKALABS, and ONMOBILE from Stage I to Stage II under the Periodic Call Auction mechanism with a 2% price band will significantly reduce their trading liquidity. Trade-for-Trade settlement prevents intraday squaring off, requiring 100% upfront delivery.
- **Margin and Capital Cost**: The 100% margin requirement on all open and new positions increases capital costs for traders and investors holding positions in these securities.
- **Price Volatility Control**: The 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction is designed to curb excessive price volatility in these shortlisted securities.
