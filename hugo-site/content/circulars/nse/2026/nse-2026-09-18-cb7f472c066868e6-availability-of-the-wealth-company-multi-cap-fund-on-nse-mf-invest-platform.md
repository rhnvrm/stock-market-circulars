## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular announcing that multiple schemes of The Wealth Company Multi Cap Fund will be available on the NSE MF Invest Platform for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities starting September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available include direct and regular growth, as well as IDCW payout and reinvestment options for The Wealth Company Multi Cap Fund.
- Effective date for availability is September 21, 2026.
- Covers ISINs ranging from INF2F0001685 to INF2F0001735.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants should note the availability of these schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 18, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to additional multi-cap equity fund options on the NSE platform.
