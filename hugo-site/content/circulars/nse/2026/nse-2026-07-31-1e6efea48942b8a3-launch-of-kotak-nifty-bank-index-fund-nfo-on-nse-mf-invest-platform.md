## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO), managed by Kotak Mutual Fund, will be available for subscription and transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is set to open on August 03, 2026, and close on August 17, 2026, at 11:59 pm.

## Key Points

- **Platform Availability:** The NFO will be hosted on the NSE MF Invest Platform for transaction.
- **Transacting Period:** August 03, 2026, to August 17, 2026 (until 11:59 pm).
- **Eligible Schemes & ISINs:**
  - Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund-Direct-Growth (ISIN: INF174KA1C68)
  - Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund-Direct-IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF174KA1C76)
  - Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund-Direct-IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF174KA1C84)
  - Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund-Regular-Growth (ISIN: INF174KA1C92)
  - Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund-Regular-IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF174KA1D00)
  - Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund-Regular-IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF174KA1D18)
- **Allotment Date:** Allotment for all options is scheduled for August 21, 2026.
- **Payment & Funding:** Members must ensure that clear funds are received in the Clearing Corporation account by August 20, 2026, before 12:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or compliance policy changes are introduced by this circular. It is an operational notification enabling the transaction of a newly launched mutual fund scheme under existing exchange platforms.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Fund Clearance Deadline:** Members must ensure that clear funds for the NFO subscription are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 20, 2026, by 12:00 pm.
- **Adherence to Timelines:** Transactions must be executed strictly within the active window from August 03, 2026, to August 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 03, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** August 17, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** August 20, 2026 (by 12:00 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Low. Operational parameters and timelines are established specifically for members transacting on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine offering of an index-based mutual fund scheme on a digital platform and does not impact broader market operations or listing status of existing equities.
