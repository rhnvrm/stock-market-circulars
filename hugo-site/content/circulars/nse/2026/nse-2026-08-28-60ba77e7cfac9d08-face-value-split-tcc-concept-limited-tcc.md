## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced a face value and paid-up value split for TCC Concept Limited (TCC). The existing face value and paid-up value of Rs. 10 per share will be divided into Rs. 2 per share, effective from September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TCC Concept Limited
- Symbol: TCC
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10 per share
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 2 per share
- Effective Date: September 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Corporate action regarding the subdivision of equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are requested to take note of the change in face value and paid-up value for trading in TCC Concept Limited shares.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and investors must update their systems and records to reflect the new face value of Rs. 2 for TCC Concept Limited starting from the effective date.
