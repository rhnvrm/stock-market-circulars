## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in ISIN Code for the equity shares of TCC Concept Limited due to a sub-division of shares from face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TCC Concept Limited
- Symbol: TCC
- New ISIN: INE887D01024
- Reason: Sub-Division from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-
- Effective Date: September 04, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- ISIN code updated in the depository system for equity shares following corporate action.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members of the Exchange are advised to take note of the new ISIN for all trades executed on and from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Ex-date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The change is procedural to reflect the stock sub-division in trading and settlement systems with no direct operational disruption.
