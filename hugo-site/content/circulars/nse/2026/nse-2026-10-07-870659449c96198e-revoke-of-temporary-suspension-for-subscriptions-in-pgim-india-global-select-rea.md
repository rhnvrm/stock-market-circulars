## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the revocation of temporary suspension for subscriptions in the PGIM India Global Select Real Estate Securities Fund of Fund scheme on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Temporary suspension on subscriptions for PGIM India Global Select Real Estate Securities Fund of Fund has been revoked.
- Effective date for reopening of subscription is October 08, 2026.
- Permitted transactions include Lumpsum purchase (fresh / additional), switch-in transactions, and existing Systematic Investment Plans (SIP).
- Fresh registrations of Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) / Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) and existing Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) remain temporarily suspended.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reopening of overseas investment-bound fund scheme subscription in accordance with SEBI guidelines permitting investments up to headroom available as of EOD February 1, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must take note of the eligible transaction types while processing orders on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Unit holders are requested to update their PAN, KYC, email address, mobile number, and nominee details with AMC.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date for Reopening: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors can resume lumpsum purchases, switch-ins, and continue existing SIPs in the PGIM India Global Select Real Estate Securities Fund of Fund through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
