## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the equity shares of Manipal Health Enterprises Limited will be admitted to dealings on the NSE's Capital Market segment with effect from August 05, 2026. The listing follows the company's recent Initial Public Offering (IPO). The designated symbol for the stock is MANIPALHOS, and it will trade under the EQ series.

## Key Points

- **Listing Date:** Trading of the equity shares of Manipal Health Enterprises Limited will commence on Wednesday, August 05, 2026.
- **Stock Information:**
  - **Symbol:** MANIPALHOS
  - **Series:** EQ
  - **ISIN:** INE459N01021
  - **Face Value:** Rs. 2/-
  - **Paid-up Value:** Rs. 2/-
  - **Issue Price:** Rs. 590/- per share
- **Quantity and Lot Size:** A total of 1,315,377,202 equity shares are being listed, with a market lot size of 1 share.
- **Pre-Open Session:** The security will participate in the pre-open session for IPOs and other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.
- **Registered Office:** The company is headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, with KFin Technologies Limited acting as the Registrar.
- **Lock-in Details:** Specific shares are subject to lock-in periods extending up to August 2027 and August 2029 as detailed in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the securities are admitted to dealings and lot sizes are defined.
- Pre-open session participation is governed by SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Members are required to note the designated security codes (Symbol: MANIPALHOS, ISIN: INE459N01021) and market lot size (1 share) for trading execution.
- **Pre-Open Session:** Ensure systems and trading desks are configured for the pre-open trading window for this newly listed IPO security on August 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 04, 2026
- **Effective Listing & Trading Date:** August 05, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:**
  - 344,677,193 shares locked up to February 03, 2027
  - 35,314,358 shares locked up to September 01, 2026
  - 35,314,410 shares locked up to October 31, 2026
  - 550,385,853 shares locked up to August 03, 2027
  - 263,080,441 shares locked up to August 03, 2029

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Provides secondary market liquidity for Manipal Health Enterprises Limited's equity shares, allowing public trading post-IPO.
- **Sector Representation:** Adds a major healthcare service provider to the listed healthcare sector on the NSE, potentially attracting institutional and retail investment.
- **Operational Impact:** Trading members and platforms must update corporate action and security master databases to include the symbol MANIPALHOS.
