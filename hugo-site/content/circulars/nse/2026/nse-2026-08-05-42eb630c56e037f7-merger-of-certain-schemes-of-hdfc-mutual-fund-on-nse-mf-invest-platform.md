## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the merger of HDFC FMP 1269D March 2023 - Series 47 (Merging Scheme) into HDFC Banking and PSU Debt Fund (Surviving Scheme) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The merger, which was approved by the Board of Directors of HDFC Asset Management Company Limited and the Trustees of HDFC Mutual Fund, is scheduled to become effective on September 09, 2026. The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) granted its no-objection communication on July 01, 2026. An exit option and positive consent window is available for affected investors from August 10, 2026, to September 08, 2026, enabling load-free redemptions or switches.

## Key Points

- **Merging Scheme**: HDFC FMP 1269D March 2023 - Series 47.
- **Surviving Scheme**: HDFC Banking and PSU Debt Fund.
- **Effective Date**: September 09, 2026 (or the next business day if September 09 is a non-business day).
- **Exit Option & Consent Window**: Open from August 10, 2026, to September 08, 2026 (until 3:00 PM on the NSE MF Invest platform, and 5:30 PM general deadline).
- **Rationale**: The merger provides a tax-efficient continuation route for investors, preventing capital gains taxation on maturity, and shifts assets to a high-quality portfolio focused on debt instruments from SCBs, PSUs, PFIs, and municipal corporations.
- **Regulatory Approvals**: Approved by the AMC and Trustees, with a no-objection communication from SEBI on July 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no broad market-wide regulatory changes introduced by this circular. The merger is executed under existing SEBI mutual fund guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members and Distributors**: Inform relevant clients of the merging scheme about the consent requirements and free exit windows.
- **For HDFC AMC / Mutual Fund**: Ensure appropriate setup on the NSE MF Invest platform to handle positive consent, load-free redemption, and switching options within the specified timeframe.
- **For Unitholders**: Submit positive consent to merge, or opt to redeem/switch out of the scheme before September 08, 2026, to avoid automatic actions or exit loads.

## Important Dates

- **July 01, 2026**: SEBI issued a no-objection communication for the merger.
- **August 05, 2026**: Circular issuance date.
- **August 10, 2026**: Commencement of the positive consent and exit option period.
- **September 08, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM)**: Last date for exit/switch transactions on the NSE MF Invest platform.
- **September 09, 2026**: Effective date of the merger.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: The merger will be executed on the NSE MF Invest platform on September 09, 2026. Plans and options will be systematically consolidated into the surviving scheme's options as per Annexure-I.
- **Investor Impact**: Highly beneficial for unitholders wishing to maintain their debt asset allocation tax-efficiently without realizing capital gains upon the FMP maturity. Non-consenting unitholders can exit without any exit load.
- **Market Impact**: Limited to unitholders of the specific HDFC FMP scheme, with zero broad market volatility or systemic risk.
