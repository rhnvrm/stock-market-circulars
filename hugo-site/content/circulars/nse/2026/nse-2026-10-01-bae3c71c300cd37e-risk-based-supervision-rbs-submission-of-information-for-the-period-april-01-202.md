## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the submission of information for Risk Based Supervision (RBS) for the half-year period from April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026. All active trading members must submit the required data electronically through the Inspection module in the Member portal by November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mandatory submission for all active trading members (members who executed at least one trade during April 01, 2026 – September 30, 2026).
- Data must be submitted electronically via the Inspection module in the NSE Member portal.
- Deadline for submission is November 30, 2026.
- Collected data is shared with SEBI; delayed or non-submission will attract disciplinary actions.
- Details of information sought are provided in Annexure A, procedure in Annexure B, and disciplinary actions in Annexure C.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforces standard periodic Risk Based Supervision (RBS) data reporting requirements for trading members as aligned with SEBI oversight.

## Compliance Requirements

- Active trading members must compile and submit the Risk Template for the assessment period April 01, 2026 – September 30, 2026.
- Submission must be completed via the Member portal inspection module no later than November 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Assessment Period Start: April 01, 2026
- Assessment Period End: September 30, 2026
- Submission Deadline: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure accurate and timely collation of branch counts, order placement facility details, and client risk categorization (such as Internet Trading and high-risk clients) to avoid regulatory penalties and disciplinary action.
