## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that securities of Shanti Inorganics Limited will be transferred from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM) with effect from September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Shanti Inorganics Limited (SME IPO)
- Symbol: SHANTIINOR
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: ST)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI guidelines (circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012) and further to Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76203 dated September 04, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are requested to note the segment change for trading in the security.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for Shanti Inorganics Limited, improving liquidity and trading convenience for investors.
