## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure for Companies as per Inter Creditor Agreement (ICA). Based on the surveillance criteria, Sadbhav Engineering Limited has satisfied the criteria and has been shortlisted under Stage I of the ASM ICA framework, effective from July 31, 2026. No securities have been excluded or transitioned between stages in this update.

## Key Points

- **Inclusion**: Sadbhav Engineering Limited (Symbol: SADBHAV, ISIN: INE226H01026) is shortlisted under ASM ICA Stage I w.e.f. July 31, 2026.
- **No Exclusions**: No securities are excluded from the ASM ICA list in this update.
- **No Stage Transitions**: There are no transitions between Stage I and Stage II of the framework.
- **Surveillance Nature**: This shortlisting is purely a surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no changes to existing regulations. This action is implemented under the provisions of previous circulars (NSE/SURV/37979 dated June 08, 2018, NSE/SURV/42218 dated September 24, 2019, and NSE/SURV/52368 dated May 20, 2022).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading & Risk Management**: Exchange members must ensure that compliance and margin systems are updated to apply Stage I ASM ICA rules and constraints to SADBHAV starting July 31, 2026.
- **Coexistence**: These measures operate in conjunction with all other existing surveillance measures applicable to the security.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 30, 2026
- **Effective Date**: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Sadbhav Engineering Limited**: The stock will be subject to surveillance controls (including potential margin increases and trading limitations) under ASM ICA Stage I, which may affect its short-term liquidity and trading patterns.
- **Market Operations**: No systemic impact expected as only a single security is affected and no other changes have been made to the surveillance lists.
