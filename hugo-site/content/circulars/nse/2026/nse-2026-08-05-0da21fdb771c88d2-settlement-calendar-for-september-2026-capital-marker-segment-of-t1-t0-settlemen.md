## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has notified all members, custodians, and professional clearing members (PCMs) regarding the settlement calendar for September 2026 in the Capital Market Segment. The circular, issued under Regulation 6.2 of the NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997), outlines schedules for Normal Rolling (T+1), Normal Auction (Rolling T+1), Trade for Trade (T+1), ITP for SME (T+1), and Normal Rolling (T+0) settlement types.

## Key Points

- The clearing corporation will observe specific settlement schedules for the month of September 2026 across five settlement types.
- The schedule for Normal Rolling (T+1) (Settlement Type M) begins with Settlement No. 2026165 (Trade Start Date: September 1, 2026) and ends with Settlement No. 2026185 (Trade End Date: September 30, 2026).
- Normal Auction (Rolling T+1) (Settlement Type A) schedule has been defined with Settlement No. 2026165 starting its trade/confirmation on September 2, 2026, and settling on September 3, 2026.
- Other included schedules are Trade for Trade (T+1) (Settlement Type Z), ITP for SME (T+1) (Settlement Type B), and Normal Rolling (T+0) (Settlement Type 5).

## Regulatory Changes

There are no major regulatory changes introduced in this circular. It is a routine operational issuance of settlement calendars in compliance with existing Regulation 6.2 of the NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, including members, custodians, and professional clearing members, must configure their settlement systems, funding arrangements, and custodial confirmation workflows to align with the specified dates for each settlement type to ensure timely settlement and avoid penalties.

## Important Dates

- **August 5, 2026**: Circular issuance date.
- **September 1, 2026**: Commencement of the September 2026 settlement cycle with Normal Rolling T+1 (Settlement No. 2026165).
- **September 30, 2026**: Trade end date for the final September settlement cycle (Settlement No. 2026185).
- **October 1, 2026**: Settlement date for the final September settlement cycle.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Direct operational relevance for clearing and back-office operations. Teams must synchronize settlement calendars and systems to match the specified settlement and custodial confirmation timelines.
- **Market Impact**: Neutral. This is a routine monthly calendar and does not affect security valuations, trading limits, or market structures.
