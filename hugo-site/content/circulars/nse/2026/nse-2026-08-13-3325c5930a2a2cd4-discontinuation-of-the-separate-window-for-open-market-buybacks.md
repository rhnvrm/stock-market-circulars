## Summary

This circular outlines the regulatory framework and discontinuation related to the separate window for open market buybacks under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018.

## Key Points

- References SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 along with subsequent amendments.
- Addresses the operational changes regarding open market buybacks through stock exchanges.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates and framework definitions under SEBI Buy-Back Regulations affecting stock exchange trading windows.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and listed companies must comply with the revised buyback procedures and channel transactions through appropriate mainstream mechanisms as mandated.

## Important Dates

- Effective as per the relevant notifications and regulatory timelines.

## Impact Assessment

- Changes how companies execute open market buybacks, eliminating the specialized separate window and requiring adherence to updated market operation guidelines.
