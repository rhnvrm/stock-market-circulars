## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Augmont Enterprises Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO). Trading in the securities will commence effectively from August 31, 2026, under the symbol AUGMONT.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** AUGMONT
- **Name of the Company:** Augmont Enterprises Limited
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE16W401027
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 5/- per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 788/- per share
- **No. of Securities:** 91,373,505 equity shares
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Effective Date:** August 31, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the above security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012. Lock-in details apply as specified in Annexure I of the circular.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 28, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 31, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** Various dates ranging from September 25, 2026 to March 03, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Augmont Enterprises Limited equity shares on the NSE capital market segment, expanding investment opportunities for market participants.
