## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Certain securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under the framework, attracting a 100% margin and shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ESM Stage - I: Sreeleathers Limited (SREEL), Universus Photo Imagings Limited (UNIVPHOTO), and Vertexplus Technologies Limited (VERTEXPLUS).
- Securities under ESM Stage - I shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 15, 2026, on all open positions as on September 11, 2026, and new positions created from September 15, 2026.
- Affected securities will be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 15, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) criteria for shortlisted securities, requiring additional margins and trade-for-trade settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement and understand the shift of specified securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II price band / applicability: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shift: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities will experience enhanced margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
