## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) to specific securities. Based on the surveillance criteria, ten securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I, and one security has been shortlisted to move from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II, effective August 12, 2026. No securities were newly shortlisted directly for Stage II. The applicable surveillance actions, primarily in the form of increased margin requirements, will take effect on August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusion:** Ten securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion in ST-ASM Stage I. These include ACS Technologies Limited (ACSTECH), ATLANTAA LIMITED (ATLANTAA), and Shilpa Medicare Limited (SHILPAMED), among others.
- **ST-ASM Stage II Movement:** GSM Foils Limited (GSMFOILS) has been shortlisted to move from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II.
- **No Direct Stage II Inclusions:** There are no new direct inclusions under ST-ASM Stage II.
- **Surveillance Actions:** For Stage I, the applicable rate of margin is 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%. For Stage II, the applicable rate of margin is 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- **Nature of Action:** The shortlisting of securities under ASM is purely due to market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no direct changes to the regulatory framework. Rather, this is an application of the pre-existing Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework (as detailed in previous Exchange Circulars dated between October 2018 and September 2024) to the specified list of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

NSE members and market participants must ensure:

1. **Margin Collection:** Collect and maintain increased margins for the specified securities as per the ST-ASM stage requirements.
2. **System Updates:** Update back-office and trading systems to reflect the revised margin rates (50% or 100%) for open positions as on August 12, 2026, and new positions from August 13, 2026.
3. **Client Information:** Inform clients trading in these securities about the applicable margin increases and potential impact on leverage.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Shortlisting Effective Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Surveillance Actions Effective Date:** August 13, 2026 (for all open positions as on August 12, 2026, and new positions created from August 13, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Members & Investors:** Higher margin requirements (50% or 100%) reduce the leverage available on these securities. This can lead to a decrease in trading volumes, lower liquidity, and potential short-term price volatility for the affected stocks.
- **Affected Stocks:**
  - **Stage I (10 stocks):** ACSTECH, ATLANTAA, COMSYN, MACPOWER, MANAKALUCO, MANYAVAR, SBCL, SHILPAMED, VCL, VR.
  - **Stage II (1 stock):** GSMFOILS (moving from Stage I).
- **Market Operations:** The ASM framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures, ensuring market integrity and reducing excessive speculation in the shortlisted stocks.
