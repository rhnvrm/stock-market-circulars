## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the takeover offer under the Tender Offer scheme of Shankara Building Products Limited. The Ballygunge Family Trust along with PACs have approached the Exchange for the usage of the web-based bidding platform. The issue period is scheduled from September 07, 2026, to September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: The Ballygunge Family Trust along with PACs (Mr. Sukumar Srinivas, Ms. Parwathi Srikanth Mirlay, Mr. Dhananjay Mirlay Srinivas, and Shankara Holdings Privates Limited).
- Issue Size: Up to 63,04,825 Equity Shares.
- Offer Price: Rs. 150/- per share.
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- each.
- ISIN: INE274V01019.
- Security Symbol: SHANKARA (Series: TO).
- Live URL for Members: https://eipo.nseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

The offer is conducted in accordance with the Tender Offer Scheme Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- All trading members of the Capital Market segment are eligible to participate in the Tender Offer Facility.
- Existing users of the web-based IPO bidding platform can use their existing user IDs; non-access members must log in to the admin terminal to create branches and users.
- In case of Takeover offers, order modification (quantity) or cancellation shall not be permitted.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: 07-Sep-2026
- Issue Closure Date: 21-Sep-2026
- Bidding Time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.
- Custodial confirmation on Issue closure day: 9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates shareholders of Shankara Building Products Limited to tender their shares through trading members using the NSE web-based bidding platform during the specified takeover offer window.
