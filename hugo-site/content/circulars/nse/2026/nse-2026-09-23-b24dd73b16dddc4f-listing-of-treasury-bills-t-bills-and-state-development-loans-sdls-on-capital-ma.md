## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on its capital market segment with effect from September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities include State Development Loans from Punjab (PN) and Gujarat (GA), along with 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day Government Treasury Bills.
- Trading will take place in specified lot sizes (100 units for the listed SDLs and T-bills).
- Identification of securities for trading will be strictly based on designated codes and ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, these securities are admitted with designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adherence to designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2026
- Circular Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands investment and trading options in sovereign and sub-sovereign debt instruments on the capital market segment.
