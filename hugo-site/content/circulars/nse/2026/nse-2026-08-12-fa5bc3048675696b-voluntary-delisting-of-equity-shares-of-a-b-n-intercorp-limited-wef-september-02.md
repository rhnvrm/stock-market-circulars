## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified the upcoming suspension of trading and voluntary delisting of the equity shares of A B N Intercorp Limited (Symbol: ABNINT). Pursuant to the company's application for voluntary delisting under SEBI regulations, trading in the company's equity shares will be suspended w.e.f. August 26, 2026. Furthermore, the admission to dealings in the equity shares of the company shall be withdrawn (delisted) w.e.f. September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name**: A B N Intercorp Limited
- **Symbol**: ABNINT
- **Action**: Suspension of trading followed by voluntary delisting of equity shares.
- **Suspension Effective Date**: Trading will be suspended from August 26, 2026.
- **Delisting Effective Date**: Dealings will be officially withdrawn (delisted) from September 02, 2026.
- **Regulatory Approval**: The company applied for Voluntary Delisting in terms of Regulation 290 under Chapter X of SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 and Regulation 106ZD(2)(a) of the SEBI (Listing of Specified Securities on Institutional Trading Platform) Regulations, 2013 read with SEBI circular no. CIR/MRD/DSA/33/2013 dated October 24, 2013.

## Regulatory Changes

- Invokes Regulation 290 under Chapter X of the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018, which prescribes the rules and process for voluntary delisting.
- Invokes Regulation 106ZD(2)(a) of the SEBI (Listing of Specified Securities on Institutional Trading Platform) Regulations, 2013 and SEBI circular no. CIR/MRD/DSA/33/2013.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Members are advised to take note of the suspension of trading in ABNINT w.e.f. August 26, 2026, and the formal delisting w.e.f. September 02, 2026, to prevent any further order routing or execution of trades for this security on or after these dates.
- **Investors/Shareholders**: Public shareholders holding equity shares of A B N Intercorp Limited should refer to the voluntary delisting offer details and take necessary action within the timelines provided in the company's individual disclosures.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular**: August 12, 2026
- **Suspension of Trading Effective Date**: August 26, 2026
- **Withdrawal of Dealings (Delisting) Effective Date**: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity & Trading**: After August 26, 2026, there will be no public trading of A B N Intercorp Limited shares on the National Stock Exchange of India. Post-delisting on September 02, 2026, the company's shares will become entirely illiquid on public platforms.
- **Operational Alignment**: Brokerage firms, exchange systems, and market intermediaries must adjust their backend systems to implement the suspension on August 26, 2026, and complete removal of the ticker symbol ABNINT on September 02, 2026.
