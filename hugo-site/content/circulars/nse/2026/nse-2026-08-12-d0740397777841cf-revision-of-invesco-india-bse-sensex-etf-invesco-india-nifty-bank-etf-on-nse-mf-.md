## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced a revision in the schedule for the Invesco India BSE Sensex ETF and the Invesco India Nifty Bank ETF on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Based on a communication from Invesco Mutual Fund, the allotment date and the New Fund Offer (NFO) reopen date have been advanced to earlier dates.

## Key Points

- The schedule revision applies to two specific exchange-traded funds (ETFs): Invesco India BSE Sensex ETF and Invesco India Nifty Bank ETF.
- The allotment date has been moved forward from August 17, 2026, to August 14, 2026.
- The NFO reopen date has been moved forward from August 24, 2026, to August 21, 2026.
- These updates are specific to transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes are introduced in this circular. It is a purely operational schedule adjustment based on a direct update from Invesco Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and mutual fund distributors utilizing the NSE MF Invest Platform should update their systems, calendars, and client communication channels to reflect the revised allotment and reopening timeline.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Previous Allotment Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Revised Allotment Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Previous NFO Reopen Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Revised NFO Reopen Date:** August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Minimal. The change only shifts the operational timelines for allotment and reopening of these specific ETF schemes by three days.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Members operating on the NSE MF Invest Platform need to ensure their records and transaction processing align with the advanced dates to prevent any processing mismatches.
