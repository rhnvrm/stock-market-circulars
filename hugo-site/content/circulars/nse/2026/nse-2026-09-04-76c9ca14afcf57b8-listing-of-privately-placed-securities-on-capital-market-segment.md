## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment for Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) and IIFL Finance Limited, effective from September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted for Small Industries Development Bank of India: SIDBI 7.7% 2029 Sr V (Symbol: 77SIDBI29, ISIN: INE556F08LH9, Maturity: 10-Oct-2029).
- Securities admitted for IIFL Finance Limited: IIFL 9.25% 2028 Sr D 37 (Symbol: 925IIFL28, ISIN: INE530B07674, Maturity: 01-Sep-2028).
- Both securities are designated under series N0 with a market lot size of 1.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Notification issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify securities strictly by their designated codes and adhere to the specified market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing with no broad market impact.
