## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further securities for several companies, including Anthem Biosciences Limited, Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited, DCB Bank Limited, and HDFC Bank Limited, with effect from August 04, 2026. These shares have been allotted under Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOPs) and are designated with specific ISINs and trading ranges.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** Trading of the newly listed securities is effective from August 04, 2026.
- **Anthem Biosciences Limited:** 23,250 equity shares (ISIN: INE0CZ201020) of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP. No lock-in is applicable.
- **Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited:** 40,100 equity shares (ISIN: INE549H01021) of Rs. 5/- each allotted under ESOP. These shares are subject to a lock-in period until July 14, 2027.
- **DCB Bank Limited:** 71,260 equity shares (ISIN: INE503A01015) of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP. No lock-in is applicable.
- **HDFC Bank Limited:** Listing of equity shares of Re. 1/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE040A01034).
- **Regulatory Framework:** This notification is issued under Regulations 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes are enacted. This is an administrative listing notification issued under existing NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

No general compliance action is required for market participants. The listed companies must ensure proper updating of corporate share registers and filing of necessary returns under the SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations and relevant listing obligations.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates:**
  - Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited: July 14, 2026
  - Anthem Biosciences Limited: July 21, 2026
  - DCB Bank Limited: July 30, 2026
- **Circular Date:** August 03, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** August 04, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry (Anand Rathi):** July 14, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Very low. The listings represent minor additions to the outstanding share capitals of the respective companies due to ESOP exercises, presenting minimal dilution and having no immediate impact on market operations or stock price behavior.
