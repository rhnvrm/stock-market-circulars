## Summary

NSE has issued a revised list of approved securities and banks forming part of the non-cash component of liquid assets and Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) scheme, specifying applicable VAR haircuts and value-based prudential norms.

## Key Points

- Lists approved equity shares forming part of the non-cash component of liquid assets.
- Specifies that applicable haircuts for listed securities are based on VAR.
- Enforces value-based prudential norms regarding the exclusion of pledged securities to limit margin shortfalls.
- Details overall permitted quantities and memberwise permitted quantities across segments.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the list of eligible securities for margins and collateral management with corresponding VAR-based haircut rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must ensure that the exclusion or inclusion of pledged securities does not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular release.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects collateral calculations, margin requirements, and risk management for trading and clearing members holding non-cash components.
