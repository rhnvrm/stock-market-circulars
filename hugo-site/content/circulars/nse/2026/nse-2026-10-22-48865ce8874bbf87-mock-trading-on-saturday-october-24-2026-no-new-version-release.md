## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026. The session covers trading from both the Primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites with no new software version release.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** October 24, 2026.
- **Trading Session-1 (Primary Site):** Normal Market open at 11:00 hours, close at 12:30 hours.
- **Trading Session-2 (DR Site):** Normal Market open at 13:15 hours, close at 14:30 hours.
- **Live Re-login Timings:** Start time at 16:00 hours, close time at 16:30 hours.
- **Software Version:** NEAT Version 1.3.3.
- **Order Purge:** All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session. Members using NNF software must manually clear orders in their systems.
- **System Availability:** Connect2NSE and Extranet facility will not be available from 19:00 hours to 22:00 hours.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; conducted as part of routine exchange mock trading and business continuity testing requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed to participate.
- Members must ensure successful telnet connectivity to the Exchange host and correct Box ID setup.
- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 26, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** October 24, 2026
- **Live Resume Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact. Trades executed during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out. It provides an opportunity for members to verify systems, disaster recovery switchover readiness, and connectivity ahead of live market operations.
