## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Currency Derivatives Segment on Saturday, September 19, 2026. No new version release is associated with this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: September 19, 2026.
- Normal Market open time: 11:15 hours; Normal Market close time: 12:30 hours.
- Trade Modification end time: 12:40 hours; Live Re-login start time: 14:00 hours, close time: 14:30 hours.
- NEAT Version: NEAT 3.5.4.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session. Members using NNF software must manually clear orders.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- NOTIS application accessible from 11:30 to 13:00 hours.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, September 21, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 19, 2026
- Live Environment Resumption: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Allows trading members to verify connectivity, box ID setups, and system readiness prior to live market operations.
