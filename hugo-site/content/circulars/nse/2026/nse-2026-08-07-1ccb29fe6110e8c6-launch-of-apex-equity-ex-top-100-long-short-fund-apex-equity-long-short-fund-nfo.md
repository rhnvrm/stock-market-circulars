## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified members about the launch of the Apex Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund and the Apex Equity Long-Short Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) under the Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) category of Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund. These schemes will be open for transaction on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **NFO Transaction Window:** Available from August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026, until 11:59 pm.
- **Investment Threshold for New Investors:** If total investment at the PAN and Holding Pattern level is less than Rs 10 Lakhs, the order will be rejected at the time of NFO allotment.
- **Schemes Available:**
  1. **Apex Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund- Regular Growth** (ISIN: `INF209K30081`, Category: Equity)
  2. **Apex Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund- Direct Growth** (ISIN: `INF209K30073`, Category: Equity)
  3. **Apex Equity Long-Short Fund- Regular Growth** (ISIN: `INF209K30107`, Category: Equity)
  4. **Apex Equity Long-Short Fund- Direct Growth** (ISIN: `INF209K30099`, Category: Equity)
- **Allotment Date:** All schemes are scheduled for allotment on August 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes are introduced by this circular. It represents standard platform enablement of new mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Funds Settlement:** Members must ensure that clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 31, 2026, by 10:00 am.
- **Minimum Subscription Enforcement:** Intermediaries must ensure new investors are aware of and meet the minimum investment criteria of Rs 10 Lakhs at the PAN and Holding Pattern level to avoid rejection of orders during allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 10, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** August 24, 2026 (until 11:59 pm)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** August 31, 2026 (by 10:00 am)
- **Allotment Date:** August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a standard mutual fund NFO launch expanding options for SIF category investors without altering wider trading guidelines.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Active members on the NSE MF Invest Platform must align their systems for the specified ISINs, enforce the Rs 10 Lakhs minimum for new investors, and comply with the clearing funds deadline.
