## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of the Zerodha Life Cycle Fund 2031 New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform for trading and transactions.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Zerodha Life Cycle Fund 2031
- ISIN: INF0R8F01240
- Category: Equity
- NFO Window: August 27, 2026, to September 10, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 16, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 27, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 10, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Allotment Date: September 16, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 16, 2026 (03:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to transact in the Zerodha Life Cycle Fund 2031 NFO directly through the NSE MF Invest Platform within the specified timeframe.
