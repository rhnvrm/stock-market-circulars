## Summary

SEBI conducted an investigation into suspected front running of trades of big client Anustup Trading Private Limited by Prijesh Kurani and others during the period from January 1, 2022, to February 28, 2023. The order outlines connections between the noticees, family members, authorized persons, and dealers involved in executing front running activities.

## Key Points

- Investigation Period: January 01, 2022 to February 28, 2023.
- Main Front Running Entity: Prijesh Kurani (Noticee 1).
- Associated Family Members: Navnit Gadoya (Noticee 2), Bharati Navnit Gadoya (Noticee 3), Rekha Arun Kurani (Noticee 4), and Dharini Kurani (Noticee 5).
- Information Carriers / Dealers: Vilpa Vora (Authorized Person of Marfatia Broking Pvt. Ltd., broker for Big Client Anustup Trading Private Limited) and Pranav Vora (operated trading terminal in her name).
- Big Client (BC): Anustup Trading Private Limited.
- Stock Broker: Marfatia Broking Pvt. Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement actions and directions issued by SEBI pursuant to SAT orders regarding fraudulent and unfair trading practices (PFUTP Regulations) related to front running.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, stock brokers, and authorized persons must maintain strict confidentiality of client order flows and implement robust surveillance and internal controls to prevent front running and misuse of confidential information.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: January 01, 2022 to February 28, 2023.
- Order Reference: 2026-27.

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights regulatory scrutiny on front running, collusion between authorized persons/dealers and external entities, and reinforces compliance standards for brokerages handling large institutional client orders.
