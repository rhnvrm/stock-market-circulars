## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective from October 19, 2026. These shares were allotted primarily under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- **AU Small Finance Bank Limited:** 403,913 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE949L01017).
- **Capital Small Finance Bank Limited:** 885 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE646H01017).
- **Delhivery Limited:** 228,116 equity shares of Re. 1/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE148O01028).
- **Five-Star Business Finance Limited:** 2,920 equity shares of Re. 1/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE128S01021).
- **ICICI Bank Limited:** 113,464 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE090A01021).
- **Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited:** 33,500 equity shares of Re. 1/- each allotted under ESOP (ISIN: INE811A01038).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; regular admission of further securities pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in these securities shall be identified by their designated codes and lot sizes as specified in the exchange annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 16, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion via ESOP allotments with negligible immediate market impact.
