## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) for two entities, effective September 18, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A, following their redemption.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading applies to Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed).
- Effective suspension date: September 18, 2026.
- Affected Issuers:
  1. THDC India Limited (ISIN: INE812V07013)
  2. Trust Investment Advisors Private Limited (ISIN: INE723X07125)
- Reason for suspension: Redemption.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine procedural suspension under existing debt market trading regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the suspension of trading for the specified ISINs and ensure no further trades are executed on the NSE debt segment post the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 17, 2026
- Suspension Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and localized impact limited to holders of the specific ISINs due to security redemption.
