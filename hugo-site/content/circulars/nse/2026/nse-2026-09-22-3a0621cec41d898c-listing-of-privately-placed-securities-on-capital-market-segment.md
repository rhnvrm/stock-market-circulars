## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment with effect from September 23, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 23, 2026
- Securities Admitted:
  - **Tvs Credit Services Limited**: TCSL 7.92% 2029 (ISIN: INE729N07115), Coupon: 7.92%, Maturity: 21-Sep-2029, Market Lot: 1, Symbol: 792TCSL29, Series: N0
  - **Piramal Finance Limited**: PFL 8.57% 2029 (ISIN: INE202B07JZ7), Coupon: 8.57%, Maturity: 05-Nov-2029, Market Lot: 1, Symbol: 857PFL29, Series: N0
  - **Care Health Insurance Limited**: CHI 10% 2036 Sr 2 (ISIN: INE119J08032), Coupon: 10%, Maturity: 18-Sep-2036, Market Lot: 1, Symbol: 10CHI36A, Series: N0

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine administrative listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify securities strictly by their designated codes and adhere to the specified market lots.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital market listing enabling secondary market trading for the specified privately placed debt securities.
