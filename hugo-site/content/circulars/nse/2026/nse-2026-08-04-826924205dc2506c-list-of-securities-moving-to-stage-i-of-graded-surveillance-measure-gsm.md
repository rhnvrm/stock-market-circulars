## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the movement of securities to Stage I of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM). Based on the surveillance review, CLC Industries Limited (CLCIND) is scheduled to be transitioned to Stage I of GSM, effective from August 05, 2026. This action is a continuation of previous Exchange surveillance circulars.

## Key Points

- **Security Affected**: CLC Industries Limited (Symbol: CLCIND, ISIN: INE376C01038) is moved to Stage I of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM).
- **Effective Date**: The surveillance measure is applicable from August 05, 2026.
- **Trading Restrictions**: Trading in CLCIND will be available with an applicable margin rate of 100% and a price band of 5% or lower.
- **Precautionary Advice**: Trading members are advised to exercise adequate caution when trading in the listed security under surveillance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of CLC Industries Limited (CLCIND) to GSM Stage I under the overall Graded Surveillance Measure framework designed to enhance market integrity and protect investor interests.
- Surveillance framework adjustments as per prior guidelines outlined in circulars dating back from February 23, 2017, through June 19, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Rules**: Trading members must enforce a 100% margin rate on trades in the specified security (CLCIND).
- **Price Band Limits**: Enforce and adhere to the 5% price band limit (or lower as applicable) on the stock's daily movements.
- **Due Diligence**: Members must implement precautionary checks and risk management protocols while handling client orders in GSM Stage I securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 04, 2026
- **Effective Date of GSM Stage I**: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **For Investors & Traders**: Liquidity for CLC Industries Limited (CLCIND) may decline due to the high margin requirement of 100% and limited price fluctuation range (5% band).
- **For Trading Members**: Higher margin collections and strict monitoring of price boundaries are required to maintain compliance with surveillance instructions.
