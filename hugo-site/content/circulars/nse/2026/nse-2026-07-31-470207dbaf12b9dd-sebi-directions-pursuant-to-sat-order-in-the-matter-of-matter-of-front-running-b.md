## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular in reference to SEBI directions and a Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order concerning the front-running case of Prijesh Kurani and Others. Previously, SEBI's order dated April 30, 2026, restrained Pranav Vora from accessing the securities market and prohibited him from buying, selling, or dealing in securities for three years. On July 3, 2026, the Hon'ble SAT stayed this debarment order, conditional upon the appellant depositing the full penalty amount within four weeks. SEBI confirmed on July 31, 2026, that the penalty has been paid in full, and advised the exchange to comply with the stay. Consequently, the NSE has advised its members to take note of the stay.

## Key Points

- **Stay of Debarment**: The 3-year market debarment on Pranav Vora (PAN: ACYPV7581R) has been stayed by the Hon'ble SAT.
- **Payment of Penalty**: The stay was conditional on the deposit of the full penalty amount. SEBI confirmed on July 31, 2026, that the appellant successfully paid the penalty.
- **Case Allegations**: The case involves allegations that Pranav Vora and his wife Vilpa Vora (an authorized person) shared confidential big client order information with Noticee No. 1 (Prijesh Kurani) to enable front-running.
- **Evidence Context**: The appellant challenged the SEBI order on the grounds that there was no substantial evidence of front-running misconduct besides WhatsApp chats prior to the investigation period.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no broad regulatory policy changes introduced by this circular, as it addresses a case-specific tribunal stay and enforcement compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must update their compliance databases to reflect that the market access and trading restrictions on Pranav Vora are stayed, in line with the SAT directions.
- **Consolidated List Verification**: Intermediaries should cross-reference client onboarding and active accounts against the updated SEBI debarred entities list available on the NSE website.

## Important Dates

- **April 30, 2026**: Date of the original SEBI restraining and debarment order.
- **July 3, 2026**: Date of the SAT order granting a conditional stay.
- **July 31, 2026**: Date of the NSE circular confirming compliance with the SAT stay following penalty payment.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Medium. Trading members must ensure their internal systems permit trading activities for the specified individual pending the final decision of the SAT appeal.
- **Regulatory Implications**: Highlights the legal recourse available to entities under SEBI orders and underscores the regulatory focus on front-running operations, especially those involving the sharing of non-public order information via chat applications.
