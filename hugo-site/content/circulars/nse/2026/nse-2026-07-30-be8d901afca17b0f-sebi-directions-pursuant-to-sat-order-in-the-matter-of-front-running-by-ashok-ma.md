## Summary

SEBI has issued a Final Order under the SEBI Act, 1992, in the matter of front-running by Ashok Maheshwari and others. The order details a front-running scheme where Ashok Maheshwari, a dealer at Kotak Securities Limited who placed orders for Unifi Capital Pvt. Ltd. (a Portfolio Management Service provider referred to as the 'Big Client'), utilized non-public information to front-run trades in collusion with other noticees. This scheme took place during the Investigation Period (IP) from April 1, 2021, to May 31, 2022, yielding substantial illicit profits.

## Key Points

- **Noticees Involved**: The order names eight noticees including Ashok Maheshwari (Noticee 1), Darshan Bakul Shah (Noticee 2), Khusboo Darshan Shah (Noticee 3), Darshan Bakul Shah (HUF) (Noticee 4), Benzer Department Stores Pvt. Ltd. (Noticee 5), Mihir Dhirajalal Savla (Noticee 6), CHL Stock Concepts Pvt. Ltd. (Noticee 7), and Chirag Mahendra Shah (Noticee 8).
- **Modus Operandi**: Noticee 1, acting as Unifi Capital's dealer at Kotak Securities, shared sensitive upcoming trade information. Other noticees then executed trades in the same scrips just before the Big Client's orders, making riskless profits.
- **Unusual Trading Activities**: During the Investigation Period, the monthly Gross Traded Value (GTV) of Noticees 2, 3, and 4 surged to 4.3 times their volume prior to the IP. 
- **Overlapping Trade Analysis**: Noticees 2, 3, and 4 shared 27.01% of their overall scrip days and 56.31% of their intraday trading scrip days with the Big Client. These overlapping days accounted for 80.52% of their total intraday profits.

## Regulatory Changes

- There are no direct legislative or policy amendments in this order. It represents an enforcement of existing regulations, specifically Section 11 and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992, and the SEBI (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices relating to Securities Market) Regulations, 2003 (PFUTP Regulations).

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Noticees**: Noticees must comply with the market bans and disgorgement instructions specified in the final order.
- **For Market Intermediaries**: Stockbrokers and Portfolio Management Services (PMS) must enforce rigorous information-barrier protocols, monitor employee communication/trading, and strictly control dealer activities to prevent unauthorized leakage of client order books.

## Important Dates

- **Investigation Period**: April 1, 2021, to May 31, 2022.
- **Interim Order / SCN**: April 26, 2024.
- **Final Order Date**: August 12, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Highly significant. Front-running by intermediaries degrades market fairness and investor trust. This order highlights SEBI's zero-tolerance policy towards information leakage and front-running inside brokerages.
- **Operational Impact**: Intermediaries must review and reinforce their internal surveillance and data security frameworks to detect and deter information theft and collusive trading.
