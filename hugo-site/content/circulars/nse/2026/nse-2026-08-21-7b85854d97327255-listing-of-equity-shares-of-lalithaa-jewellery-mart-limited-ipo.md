## Summary

Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern and associated declarations under Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, in connection with the listing of its equity shares following its Initial Public Offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited
- Scrip Class: Equity Shares
- Filing Date: 21.08.2026
- Shareholding pattern filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory policy changes introduced; routine IPO listing disclosure pursuant to SEBI LODR requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entity must maintain compliance with continuous disclosure standards under SEBI LODR regulations regarding shareholding patterns and capital structure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 21.08.2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading and public visibility of Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited equity shares on the exchange platform.
