## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has introduced the "User Id Product Vendor Report" on the ENIT-Member Portal, providing trading members with access to product, vendor, and version mapping details along with activation and deactivation dates for all NEAT converted to NNF and NON-NEAT user IDs.

## Key Points

- Introduction of "User Id Product Vendor Report" on ENIT-Member Portal.
- Covers mapping details for NEAT converted to NNF and NON-NEAT user IDs.
- Includes corresponding activation and deactivation dates.
- Accessible via path: ENIT-New Trade > Trade > User Id Request > User Id Product Vendor Report.
- Effective from start of business hours on October 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory compliance mandates; this is an operational enhancement providing administrative reporting capabilities to trading members.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can utilize the new report on ENIT to audit and verify user ID product-vendor-version mappings for their operations.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides better transparency and self-service capabilities to trading members regarding their user ID inventory and vendor mappings on the NSE platform.
