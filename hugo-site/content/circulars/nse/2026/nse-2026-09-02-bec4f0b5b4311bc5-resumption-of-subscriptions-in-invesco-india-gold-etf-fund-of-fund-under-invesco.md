## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the resumption of subscriptions in Invesco India Gold ETF Fund of Fund under Invesco India Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Temporary restrictions on lumpsum subscriptions and switch-ins for Invesco India Gold Exchange Traded Fund and Invesco India Gold ETF Fund of Fund have been revoked.
- Subscriptions resume without any limit restrictions effective from August 24, 2026.
- The update follows the notice-cum-addendum issued by Invesco Asset Management (India) Pvt. Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors can process lumpsum purchases and switch-ins into the specified schemes without prior limit restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Resumption: August 24, 2026
- Circular Issue Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Unrestricted lumpsum investments are now re-enabled for investors on the NSE MF Invest Platform for the mentioned gold ETF schemes.
