## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of GSPL Transmission Limited pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement. Trading in these securities will commence effective September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** GSPL Transmission Limited
- **Symbol:** GSPLTRANS
- **ISIN:** INE1TUG01013
- **Series:** BE (Trade for Trade, to be subsequently shifted to Series 'EQ')
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- each
- **Number of Securities:** 312,743,617 equity shares
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 1 to 312743617
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The listing follows the Ministry of Corporate Affairs order dated April 09, 2026, approving the Scheme of Arrangement between Gujarat State Petroleum Corporation Limited, Gujarat State Petronet Limited, GSPC Energy Limited, Gujarat Gas Limited, and GSPL Transmission Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note that the securities will be available for trading in Series 'BE' (Trade for Trade) initially as per SEBI circular guidelines before being shifted to Series 'EQ'.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 28, 2026
- **Effective Date / Admission to Dealings:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

 Shareholders receiving shares pursuant to the scheme of arrangement can trade the newly listed shares on the NSE capital market segment under the specified trade-for-trade category.
