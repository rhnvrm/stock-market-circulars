## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the review of the margin framework for the Commodity Derivatives Segment, modifying previous circulars in line with SEBI guidelines. The circular specifies the updated categorization, minimum Initial Margin (IM) %, Minimum MPOR, and Volatility Scan Range (VSR) % for various non-agri commodities.

## Key Points

- Updates the margin requirements and parameters for commodities including Gold, Lead, Copper, Aluminium, Silver, Natural Gas, Crude Oil, Brent Crude Oil, Nickel, Zinc, and ELECMBL.
- Specifies particular minimum IM %, MPOR, and VSR (%) values for each commodity category based on volatility.
- Highlights special minimum IM (%) requirements for Brent Crude Oil (30%), Crude Oil (30%), Silver (11.50%), and Natural Gas (13%) as per prior circulars.
- Minimum IM % specified shall not be scaled up by MPOR.
- For options on goods, MPOR shall be at least equal to three days or MPOR of corresponding futures contracts, whichever is higher.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification to circular no. 0274/2025 (Download Ref no NCL/COM/70068) dated September 05, 2025, and SEBI circulars on Review of Margin Framework and Volatility Scan Range for Option contracts in Commodity Derivatives Segment.
- All other margins including Extreme Loss Margin, ICMTM Margin, Tender Period Margin, Delivery Period Margin, Concentration Margin, Additional Margin, and Special Margin remain applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must take note of the revised margin framework parameters and ensure compliance and adequate risk management systems are updated before the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: Provisions are applicable from the beginning of the day on October 01, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in commodity derivatives will need to adjust their margin calculations and capital allocations in accordance with the updated minimum IM, MPOR, and VSR parameters.
