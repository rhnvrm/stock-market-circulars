## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities for Rupeek Capital Private Limited on the capital market segment, effective from August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Rupeek Capital Private Limited
- **Security Symbol:** 11RCPL28A
- **Series:** N0
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Issue Description:** RCPL 11% 2028 Sr II
- **Coupon Rate:** 11%
- **Date of Maturity:** 26-Feb-2028
- **ISIN Code:** INE0DG907388

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine admission pursuant to National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the specified securities shall be conducted using the designated security code and specified lot size.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 31, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** February 26, 2028

Enables secondary market trading for the newly listed privately placed debt securities of Rupeek Capital Private Limited.
