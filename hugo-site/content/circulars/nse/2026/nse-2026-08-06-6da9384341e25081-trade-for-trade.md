## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular under its Surveillance department announcing the periodic shifting of securities to and from the Trade for Trade segment. Effective from August 11, 2026 (Tuesday), 16 mainline securities will be shifted from the Rolling Segment (series EQ) to the Trade for Trade Segment (series BE), and 3 SME securities will be shifted from the Rolling Segment (series SM) to the Trade for Trade Segment (series ST). These shifts are accompanied by a price band of 5% or lower. Additionally, existing securities in the Trade for Trade segment (series BE/BZ/ST/SZ) will continue under surveillance with a 5% or lower price band.

## Key Points

- **Segment Shift**: 19 securities in total are being shifted from the Rolling Segment (series EQ/SM) to the Trade for Trade Segment (series BE/ST).
- **Effective Date**: The changes will take effect starting August 11, 2026.
- **Price Band Constraints**: Affected securities will be traded with a price band of 5% or lower.
- **Settlement Process**: Settlement will be on a trade-to-trade basis; no intraday netting off of trades is permitted, requiring 100% delivery-based settlement.
- **Surveillance Measure**: The relocation is purely a market surveillance measure to ensure market safety and protect investor interests, not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Rolling to Trade for Trade (Series EQ to BE)**: 16 companies, including CAPTRUST, E2E, LOTUSDEV, and COUNCODOS, are shifted to series BE.
- **Rolling to Trade for Trade (Series SM to ST)**: 3 SME companies (KCK, TAC, and KODYTECH) are shifted to series ST.
- **Price Band Adjustments**: A uniform price band limit of 5% (or lower as applicable) is implemented for the newly shifted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Safeguards**: Trading members are instructed to exercise extreme precaution while handling trades in these securities.
- **Settlement Execution**: Members must ensure all transactions are processed on a gross settlement basis with no netting allowed.
- **Risk Management Systems (RMS)**: System configurations must be updated to restrict intraday orders (MIS, CO, BO) and enforce 100% upfront margins and delivery-based settlement for the specified symbols starting August 11, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular**: August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date of Transition**: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity**: Shifted securities are expected to experience a temporary decrease in trading volumes and liquidity due to the elimination of speculative intraday trading.
- **Volatility Mitigation**: The 5% price band limit will successfully cap extreme daily fluctuations and safeguard retail investors.
- **Operational Adjustments**: Brokerage operations must align their order routing systems to handle the series change (from EQ to BE/SM to ST) and update margin profiles accordingly.
