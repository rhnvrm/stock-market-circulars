## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listing of Commercial Papers for multiple issuers including Aditya Birla Money Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Hdfc Securities Limited, Angel One Limited, Tata Projects Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, CESC Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, and IIFL Finance Limited.
- Effective date of admission: September 08, 2026.
- Issued under the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine listing notification under existing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers must comply with standard depository and exchange reporting requirements for debt instruments.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 08, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 08, 2026
- Allotment and Redemption dates vary by specific commercial paper issue (ranging from December 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Provides secondary market liquidity and official listing status for the newly issued commercial papers.
