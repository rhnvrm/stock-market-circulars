## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of the Nippon India Income Plus Arbitrage Omni Fund of Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from August 17, 2026, to August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Nippon India Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Nippon India Income Plus Arbitrage Omni Fund of Fund
- Subscription Period: August 17, 2026 to August 31, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Category: Debt
- Allotment Date: September 04, 2026
- Available Plans include Direct Growth, Growth, Direct IDCW Payout, Direct IDCW Reinvestment, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 01, 2026, till 11:59 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 17, 2026
- NFO Close Date: August 31, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 01, 2026
- Allotment Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to subscribe to the new debt fund scheme through the NSE MF Invest platform.
