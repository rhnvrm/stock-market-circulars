## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The shares will be traded under the symbol MILKYMIST.

- Company Name: Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited
- Symbol: MILKYMIST
