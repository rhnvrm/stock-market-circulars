## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding SEBI direction pursuant to the Hon'ble SAT order in the matter of Mr. Satish Vadilal Sheth. Hon'ble SAT granted a stay on the impugned SEBI order subject to the deposit of the full penalty, which has now been paid by the appellant.

## Key Points

- Reference to earlier NSE Circular No. NSE/INVG/74494 dated June 01, 2026, regarding SEBI Order No. QJA/SS/IVD-2/ID13/32429/2026-27 dated May 29, 2026.
- Noticee Mr. Satish Vadilal Sheth (PAN: AAQPS3939Q) was restrained from accessing the securities market for five years.
- Hon'ble SAT vide order dated July 20, 2026, ordered a stay of the impugned order subject to the deposit of the full penalty.
- SEBI confirmed via email dated August 18, 2026, that the appellant has paid the penalty amount of Rs. 20,00,000/- in favour of SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Stay of the debarment/restraint order following the deposit of the penalty amount as directed by SAT.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the above and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 18, 2026
- SAT Order Date: July 20, 2026
- SEBI Confirmation Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Compliance and operational update for trading members regarding the status of debarred/restrained entities.
