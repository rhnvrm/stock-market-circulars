## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability and movement of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities shortlisted under ESM are subject to additional trading restrictions, margin requirements, and shifting between settlement segments.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ESM and will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 04, 2026, on open positions as on September 03, 2026, and new positions created from September 04, 2026.
- Securities shifting to ESM Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 03, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 04, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework measures including 100% margin requirements and movement to Trade-for-Trade segment for specified securities.
- Movement of specified securities between ESM Stage I and Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements and trading segment changes for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II price band and movement: September 03, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and segment shift (EQ/SM to BE/ST): September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affected securities will experience increased margin requirements and liquidity restrictions due to Trade-for-Trade settlement and price band limitations, impacting trading participants dealing in these scrips.
