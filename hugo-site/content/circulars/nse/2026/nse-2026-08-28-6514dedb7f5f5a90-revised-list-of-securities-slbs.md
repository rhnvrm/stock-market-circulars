## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a revised list of securities eligible for transactions under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for the month of September 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines and previous consolidated circulars.

## Key Points

- Applicable for all participants and custodians engaging in SLBS transactions.
- Effective for the month of September 2026.
- Contains the complete updated list of approved scrips/symbols in Annexure-I.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic monthly update reflecting changes in eligibility for securities lending and borrowing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians must ensure that SLBS transactions are executed strictly in accordance with the updated list of eligible securities for September 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 28, 2026
- **Effective Period:** September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Dictates the eligible universe of stocks available for lending and borrowing activities during the month, directly impacting market participants utilizing SLBS facilities.
