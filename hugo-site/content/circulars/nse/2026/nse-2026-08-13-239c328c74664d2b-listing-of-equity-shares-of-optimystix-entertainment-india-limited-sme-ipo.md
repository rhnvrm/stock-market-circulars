## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Optimystix Entertainment India Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform, effective from August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Optimystix Entertainment India Limited
- Symbol: OPTIMYSTIX
- ISIN: INE1JZB01022
- Issue Price: Rs. 175/- per share
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Total Number of Securities: 23,268,900
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 23268900
- Market Lot: Minimum 800 Equity Shares
- Series: 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment – Settlement Type 'W')
- Effective Date: August 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings on the EMERGE SME Platform pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the security will be in Series 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment) and subsequently shifted to Series 'SM' (Normal Rolling Segment) as per applicable SEBI circulars.
- Market maker maximum permissible spread shall be within 10%.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Optimystix Entertainment India Limited shares on the NSE SME platform under trade-for-trade surveillance measures.
