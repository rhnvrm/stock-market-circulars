## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II, as well as securities moving from Stage I to Stage II, effective October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I attract an applicable rate of margin of 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. October 26, 2026, on all open positions as on October 23, 2026, and new positions created from October 26, 2026.
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. October 26, 2026.
- Stage I Securities: DCM Shriram Industries Limited, Generic Engineering Construction and Projects Limited, Lagnam Spintex Limited, Marco Cables & Conductors Limited, One Global Service Provider Limited, Spencer's Retail Limited, Transworld Shipping Lines Limited, and Yudiz Solutions Limited.
- Securities moving from Stage I to Stage II: Avro India Limited.
- ASM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measures (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to monitor price volatility and safeguard market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100%) for open positions as on October 23, 2026, and all new positions created from October 26, 2026, onwards.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions / Margins: October 26, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the short term, ensuring heightened caution and risk management among market participants.
