## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the admission of several privately placed commercial papers to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations. These listings are effective from August 07, 2026. A total of 10 commercial paper issuances from nine prominent corporate entities—including Blue Star, ICICI Securities, Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI), HPCL, Motilal Oswal Financial Services, Alembic Pharmaceuticals, JM Financial Services, PNB Housing Finance, and L&T Finance—have been listed.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** Admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from August 07, 2026.
- **Regulatory Framework:** Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.
- **Instrument Type:** All listed instruments are short-term, privately placed Commercial Papers (CP) with a face value of ₹5,00,000 per security.
- **Issuers & Security Quantities:**
  - **Blue Star Limited:** 2,000 securities (`BSL CP 21/09/26`, ISIN: `INE472A14PG1`).
  - **ICICI Securities Limited:** 24,300 securities (`ISEC CP 05/11/26`, ISIN: `INE763G14M51`).
  - **Small Industries Development Bank of India:** 34,000 securities (`SIDBI CP 09/06/27 Sr 10`, ISIN: `INE556F14MP8`).
  - **Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited:** 23,000 securities (`HPCL CP 10/09/26 Sr 53`, ISIN: `INE094A14KJ8`).
  - **Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited:** 5,500 securities (`MOFS CP 05/11/2026`, ISIN: `INE338I14NC1`).
  - **Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited:** 4,000 securities (`APL CP 28/09/26`, ISIN: `INE901L14CO8`).
  - **JM Financial Services Limited:** 5,000 securities (`JFSL CP 05/11/26 Sr 19`, ISIN: `INE012I14SW9`).
  - **PNB Housing Finance Limited:** 18,000 securities (`PHFL CP 05/11/26 Sr 535`, ISIN: `INE572E14KR8`).
  - **L&T Finance Limited:** Two issuances:
    - 32,000 securities (`LTFL CP 05/11/26`, ISIN: `INE498L14GG2`).
    - 23,500 securities (`LTFL CP 28/08/26`, ISIN: `INE498L14GI8`).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory requirements or policy changes are introduced by this circular. The listings are executed under the pre-existing Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no direct compliance or reporting requirements imposed on trading members or investors through this circular. The notice is purely for informational purposes regarding the listing of new debt securities.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** All listed commercial papers were allotted on **August 06, 2026**.
- **Effective Listing Date:** Dealt on the exchange segment from **August 07, 2026**.
- **Redemption / Maturity Dates:**
  - L&T Finance Limited (`LTFL CP 28/08/26`): **August 28, 2026**
  - Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (`HPCL CP 10/09/26`): **September 10, 2026**
  - Blue Star Limited (`BSL CP 21/09/26`): **September 21, 2026**
  - Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited (`APL CP 28/09/26`): **September 28, 2026**
  - ICICI Securities Limited (`ISEC CP 05/11/26`): **November 05, 2026**
  - Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited (`MOFS CP 05/11/2026`): **November 05, 2026**
  - JM Financial Services Limited (`JFSL CP 05/11/26`): **November 05, 2026**
  - PNB Housing Finance Limited (`PHFL CP 05/11/26`): **November 05, 2026**
  - L&T Finance Limited (`LTFL CP 05/11/26`): **November 05, 2026**
  - Small Industries Development Bank of India (`SIDBI CP 09/06/27`): **June 09, 2027**

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** This listing increases the liquidity and trading avenues for short-term corporate debt on the NSE Debt Market Segment.
- **Operational Impact:** Allows market participants, institutional investors, and trading members to clear and settle transactions for the newly admitted commercial papers using the designated security codes and ISINs.
- **Company Specifics:** Provides low-cost short-term capital matching the working capital requirements of the respective corporate issuers.
