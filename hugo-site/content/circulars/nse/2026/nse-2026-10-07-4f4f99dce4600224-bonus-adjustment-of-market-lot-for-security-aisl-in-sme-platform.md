## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the adjustment of the market lot for ANI Integrated Services Limited (AISL) on the SME platform on account of a 1:1 bonus issue. The adjusted market lot becomes 1200, effective from October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Symbol: AISL (ANI Integrated Services Limited)
- Corporate Action: Bonus issue (1:1 ratio)
- Adjustment Factor: 2
- Adjusted Market Lot: 1200
- Ex-date / Effective Date: October 09, 2026
- Record Date: October 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Market lot adjustment in accordance with corporate action guidelines for SME platform securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to load the updated security.gz / nnf_security.gz / MII - Security File.gz file in their trading application before trading on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Ex-date: October 09, 2026
- Record Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members dealing in AISL securities on the SME platform must update their trading terminals with the revised market lot of 1200 prior to the effective date.
