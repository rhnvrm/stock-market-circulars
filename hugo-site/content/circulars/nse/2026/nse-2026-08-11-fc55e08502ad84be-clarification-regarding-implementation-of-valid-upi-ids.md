## Summary

NSE has issued a clarification regarding the implementation of standardized, validated, and exclusive "@valid" UPI IDs for investor-facing bank accounts (upstreaming accounts) maintained by trading members. This follows SEBI's communication dated August 07, 2026, aimed at ensuring that all collection accounts receiving funds from individual/non-institutional investors can be verified through the "SEBI Check" facility. Trading members and other intermediaries must achieve compliance within their respective timelines.

## Key Points

- **Mandatory Linking:** All investor-facing bank accounts (upstreaming accounts) used to receive funds from individual or non-institutional investors (via UPI, NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, or bank transfer) must be linked with a "@valid" UPI ID.
- **SEBI Check Verification:** This initiative ensures all such bank accounts are visible and verifiable through the "SEBI Check" facility, mitigating risks of fraudulent fund transfers.
- **Multi-Exchange Registration:** Members registered with more than one stock exchange are advised to submit their request for a "@valid" UPI handle through only one exchange.
- **New Accounts:** Any new investor-facing bank account opened after the issuance of this circular must also be mandatorily linked to a "@valid" UPI ID.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clarification and standard FAQs issued by SEBI on August 07, 2026, regarding its earlier circular dated June 11, 2025, on standardizing and validating UPI handles for registered intermediaries.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Application Process:** Trading members must apply for a "@valid" UPI handle on the NSE portal (ENIT) via: `ENIT -> ENIT-NEW-COMPLIANCE -> COMPLIANCE -> UPI IDs Request Portal -> UPI IDs Request Submission`.
- **Bank Documentation:** After submitting the request on the portal, members must approach their respective banks to complete the documentation and processing required for obtaining the handle.
- **Timeline:** Compliance must be achieved within T+30 days (by September 06, 2026) for Stock Brokers and Depository Participants.

## Important Dates

- **T (Clarification Reference Date):** August 07, 2026
- **Circular Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Compliance Deadline (Mutual Funds, Stock Brokers, Depository Participants):** September 06, 2026 (T+30 Days)
- **Compliance Deadline (Investment Advisers, Research Analysts):** October 06, 2026 (T+60 Days)

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Members/Brokers:** High operational impact due to the tight 30-day timeline to map all existing upstreaming accounts, apply on ENIT, and coordinate with banks for verification.
- **Investors:** High positive impact, enabling investors to verify legitimacy of broker collection accounts using SEBI Check before initiating fund transfers, reducing fraud.
