## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the revised Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) and position limits applicable for Participants, Institutional Clients, and Non-Institutional Clients under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for the month of September 2026, pursuant to NCL Circular No. (NCL/CMPT/73999) dated April 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Specifies MWPL and position limits for Participants, Institutional Clients, and Non-Institutional Clients.
- Effective for the month of September 2026.
- Covers numerous eligible securities and ETFs traded on the platform.

## Regulatory Changes

- Monthly update of position limits in accordance with established SLBS guidelines and previous circular NCL/CMPT/73999.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians must ensure compliance with the revised position limits for securities and ETFs under SLBS during September 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 31, 2026
- Effective Period: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants engaging in Securities Lending and Borrowing must update their systems with the revised limits to prevent breaches during the settlement and lending cycle for September 2026.
