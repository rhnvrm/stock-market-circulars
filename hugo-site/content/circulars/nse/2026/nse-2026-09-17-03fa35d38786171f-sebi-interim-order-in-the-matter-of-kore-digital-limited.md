## Summary

National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding SEBI Interim Order No. WTM/KV/CFID/CFID-SEC1/32724/2026-27 dated September 17, 2026. SEBI has restrained specific noticees (Ravindra Doshi, Chaitanya Doshi, and Kashmira Doshi) from buying, selling, or dealing in securities of Kore Digital Limited, directly or indirectly, until further orders.

## Key Points

- SEBI issued an interim order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992.
- Noticees restrained: Ravindra Doshi, Chaitanya Doshi, and Kashmira Doshi.
- Prima facie findings include misrepresentation of financial statements, manipulation of accounts, inflation of revenue, suspicious accounting entries, and diversion of preferential issue proceeds.

## Regulatory Changes

- Direct restraint on trading and dealing in the securities of Kore Digital Limited for the specified noticees.

## Compliance Requirements

- NSE members are advised to take note of the order and ensure strict compliance by preventing the specified noticees from dealing in the securities of Kore Digital Limited.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on the named noticees and restricted trading activities concerning Kore Digital Limited securities to protect investor interests.
