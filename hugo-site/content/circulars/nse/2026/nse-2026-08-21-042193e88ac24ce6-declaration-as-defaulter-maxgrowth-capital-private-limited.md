## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular providing updated details of the directors of Maxgrowth Capital Private Limited, which was previously declared as a defaulter under NSE Circular Ref No. NSE/COMP/64689 dated October 23, 2024.

## Key Points

- Reference to earlier NSE Circular Ref No. NSE/COMP/64689 dated October 23, 2024, declaring Maxgrowth Capital Private Limited as a defaulter.
- Annexure A provides the updated list of directors and their MCA appointment dates/PAN/DIN details.
- Directors listed include Anita Sarna (PAN: ACAPS8427R, Appointed: 23/09/2021) and Ashansh Roshan (DIN: 10313360, Appointed: 12/09/2023).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this circular serves as an update to previously declared defaulter records.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to take note of the updated details regarding the defaulting member.

## Important Dates

- Original Declaration Date: October 23, 2024
- Circular Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Informational update for trading members with negligible direct operational impact on general market participants.
