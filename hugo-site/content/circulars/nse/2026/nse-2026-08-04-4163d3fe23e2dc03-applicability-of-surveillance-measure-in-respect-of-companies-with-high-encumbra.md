## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of surveillance measures for companies exhibiting high 'Encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 (SAST). This surveillance measure is intended to monitor and manage risks associated with high levels of promoter share pledging. The circular details the newly shortlisted securities subject to these measures, securities that have been excluded, and provides a consolidated list of all active securities under this framework.

## Key Points

- **Surveillance Framework:** Imposes additional risk controls on companies with high levels of promoter encumbrance (pledged shares) under Reg. 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.
- **Margin Requirements:** Shortlisted securities will attract a minimum margin of 35% in both the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- **Securities Newly Shortlisted (Annexure I):** Hexaware Technologies Limited (HEXT), Jayaswal Neco Industries Limited (JAYNECOIND), Magellanic Cloud Limited (MCLOUD), Medplus Health Services Limited (MEDPLUS), Share India Securities Limited (SHAREINDIA), Wanbury Limited (WANBURY), and Patanjali Foods Limited (PATANJALI).
- **Securities Excluded (Annexure II):** MSP Steel & Power Limited (MSPL), Artemis Medicare Services Limited (ARTEMISMED), GPT Infraprojects Limited (GPTINFRA), NRB Bearing Limited (NRBBEARING), and Themis Medicare Limited (THEMISMED).
- **Consolidated List (Annexure III):** Active securities include Indo Borax & Chemicals Limited (INDOBORAX), Nuvama Wealth Management Limited (NUVAMA), Vedanta Limited (VEDL), and Embassy Developments Limited (EMBDL).
- **Nature of Action:** The placement of securities under this framework is purely on account of market surveillance parameters and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Surveillance Status Update:** Updates the list of active securities monitored under the SEBI (SAST) Regulation 28(3) high-encumbrance framework.
- **Increased Margin Mandate:** Mandates a minimum 35% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments for shortlisted securities, replacing standard or lower default margins.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:**
  - Must implement and collect a minimum margin of 35% in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments on all open positions as of August 06, 2026, and all new positions created on or after August 07, 2026, for the newly shortlisted securities listed in Annexure I.
  - Ensure risk management systems are configured to apply these updated margin rates to the relevant tickers.
- **Listed Companies:**
  - Any company seeking to make a representation regarding its placement within this surveillance framework must submit it to the Exchange latest by 5:00 PM on August 05, 2026.
  - Enquiries and representations should be directed to the NSE Surveillance department via email at surveillance@nse.co.in.

## Important Dates

- **August 04, 2026:** Circular publication date.
- **August 05, 2026:** Effective date for exclusions (Annexure II securities move out of the framework).
- **August 05, 2026 (by 5:00 PM):** Deadline for listed companies to submit representations to the Exchange.
- **August 06, 2026:** Cut-off date for existing open positions subject to the updated margin requirement.
- **August 07, 2026:** Effective date for the 35% margin requirement on open and new positions in shortlisted securities (Annexure I).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Participants & Traders:** Trading members and investors holding open positions in the seven newly shortlisted securities (such as Patanjali Foods, Medplus, and Hexaware) will face higher funding costs and capital lockups due to the increased 35% margin requirement. This may lead to short-term volatility or a temporary reduction in trading volumes for these specific counters.
- **Market Integrity:** By raising margin requirements on companies with high promoter encumbrance, the Exchange mitigates systemic settlement and default risks arising from potential promoter share liquidations, bolstering overall market safety.
- **Excluded Companies:** The five excluded companies (such as Artemis Medicare and NRB Bearing) are freed from the 35% surveillance margin framework, which is expected to normalize trading capital requirements and potentially restore historical trading activity.
