## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in multiple Treasury Bills (T-Bills), State Government Bonds (SDL), Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB), and Government Securities (GS) on account of their maturity and redemption, effective across various dates starting from September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension affects specific T-Bills, State Government Bonds, Sovereign Gold Bonds, and Government Securities.
- Reason for suspension: Maturity and redemption.
- Effective dates for suspension range from September 29, 2026, through October 28, 2026.
- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine operational suspension upon security maturity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the suspension dates and avoid trading in these suspended securities from the respective effective dates.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 25, 2026
- Suspension Dates: Starting from September 29, 2026 up to October 28, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operation ensuring that matured securities are halted from trading.
