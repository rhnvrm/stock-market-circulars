## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the admission to dealings of privately placed securities on its capital market segment with effect from September 02, 2026. The circular provides the designated security codes, lot sizes, and details of the newly admitted securities for REC Limited and AVP Infracon Limited.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the capital market segment effective September 02, 2026.
- Covers privately placed debt/securities issued by REC Limited (Symbol: 757RECL36, ISIN: INE020B08GJ1) and AVP Infracon Limited (Symbol: 12AIL28, ISIN: INE0R9407016).
- Trading shall be conducted using designated security codes and specified market lot sizes (Market Lot: 1 for both).

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are identified by the designated codes and executed in the specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 02, 2026
- Maturity Dates: 31-Oct-2036 (REC Limited), 28-Aug-2028 (AVP Infracon Limited)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing of privately placed debt securities enabling secondary market liquidity for the specified instruments.
