## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued operational guidelines regarding early pay-in (EPI) of securities for Waterways Leisure Tourism Limited (CORDELIA) on account of its face value split from Rs. 10/- per share to Re. 1/- per share.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Waterways Leisure Tourism Limited (CORDELIA)
- Old/Existing ISIN: INE0LZF01013
- Corporate Action: Face Value Split (Sub-Division) from Rs. 10/- per share to Re. 1/- per share.
- Record Date: 26-AUG-2026
- Ex-Date: 25-AUG-2026
- Members doing EPI on specific dates and settlement numbers must use the old/existing ISIN or new ISIN accordingly without adjusting for conversion on trade dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of specific ISIN usage for early pay-in during the face value split transition period across settlement numbers 2026160 and 2026161.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants/custodians must execute early pay-in instructions in Old ISIN for ex-date/record date.
- In case of early pay-in through block mechanism, client-wise early pay-in allocation file should not be uploaded.
- Custodians providing early pay-in through the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details as per the actual traded quantity to pass benefits to clients.
- Execute pay-in/early pay-in instructions in New ISIN after the record date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: 25-AUG-2026
- Record Date: 26-AUG-2026
- EPI Date 25-AUG-2026 (Settlement 2026160): Old/Existing ISIN
- EPI Date 26-AUG-2026 (Settlement 2026160 & 2026161): Old/Existing ISIN
- EPI Date 27-AUG-2026 (Settlement 2026160 & 2026161): New ISIN

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and custodians must adhere to prescribed ISIN usage and allocation file procedures to ensure smooth settlement and prevent allocation errors during the stock split.
