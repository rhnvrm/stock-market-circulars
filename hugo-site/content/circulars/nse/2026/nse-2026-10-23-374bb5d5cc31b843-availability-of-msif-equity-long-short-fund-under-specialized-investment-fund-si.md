## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding the availability of MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund under the Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) category of Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026.
- **Fund House:** Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund.
- **Facilities Available:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- **Eligible Schemes:** Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options.

## Scheme Details

- MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund - Regular - Growth (ISIN: INF174V30019)
- MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund - Regular – IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF174V30035)
- MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund - Regular - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF174V30027)
- MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund - Direct - Growth (ISIN: INF174V30043)
- MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund - Direct - IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF174V30068)
- MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund - Direct - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF174V30050)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants must ensure correct scheme mapping and ISIN usage on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment options available to investors and trading members on the NSE MF Invest Platform for Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund's Specialized Investment Fund schemes.
