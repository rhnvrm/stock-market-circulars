## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a special live trading session (Muhurat Trading) on account of Diwali to be held on Sunday, November 08, 2026, in continuation of previous circular No. NSE/COM/71784 dated December 15, 2025.

## Key Points

- Special live trading session for Diwali scheduled on Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- Normal Market Open: 14:20 Hrs.
- Normal Market Close: 15:30 Hrs.
- Set up cut off time for Position Limit / Collateral value: 15:40 Hrs.
- Trade modification end time: 15:40 Hrs.
- All trades executed during the Diwali Muhurat trading session result in settlement obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; announcement of the annual special trading session schedule.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure operational preparedness and adhere to the specified timelines for position limits, collateral setup, and trade modifications during the session.

## Important Dates

- November 08, 2026: Diwali Muhurat trading session date.

## Impact Assessment

Provides a festive trading window for market participants with standard settlement obligations.
