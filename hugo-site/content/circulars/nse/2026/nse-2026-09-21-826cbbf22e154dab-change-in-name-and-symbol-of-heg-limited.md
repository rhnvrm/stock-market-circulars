## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the change in name and symbol of HEG Limited under the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme, effective September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Old Symbol: HEG
- New Symbol: HEGAM
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026
- Reference to previous circular: NSE/CML/76359 dated September 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading symbol for HEG Limited has been updated in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians are advised to take note of the updated symbol for all future trades and contracts in the security under the scheme.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; participants must update their trading systems to reflect the new symbol (HEGAM) for HEG Limited.
