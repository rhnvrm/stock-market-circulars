## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced that no transaction charges will be levied on trades executed in Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on the Nifty India FPI 150 Index (NIFTYFPI). This waiver is effective retrospectively from the product launch date of August 12, 2026, through February 28, 2027.

## Key Points

- Applies to Futures and Options contracts on the Nifty India FPI 150 Index (NIFTYFPI).
- Zero transaction charges levied on all trades during the specified incentive period.
- Aimed at encouraging active market participant engagement in the newly introduced index derivative segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary waiver of exchange transaction fees for NIFTYFPI F&O contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members can take advantage of the fee waiver automatically for eligible trades without requiring separate applications.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Start Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective End Date:** February 28, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Reduces trading costs for market participants engaging in Nifty India FPI 150 Index derivatives, potentially boosting liquidity and trading volumes during the introductory phase.
