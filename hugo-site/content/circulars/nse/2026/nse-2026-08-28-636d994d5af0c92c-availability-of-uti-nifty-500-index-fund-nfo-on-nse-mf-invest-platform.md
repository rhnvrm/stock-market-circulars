## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular announcing that the UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund of UTI Mutual Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes included: UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund-Direct Plan-Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AG92) and UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund-Regular Plan-Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AG84).
- Category: Equity Index Fund.
- Facilities available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating on the NSE MF Invest Platform can facilitate transactions in the mentioned schemes for their clients.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 31, 2026
- Circular Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform users and investors access to the UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund for systematic and lump-sum investments.
