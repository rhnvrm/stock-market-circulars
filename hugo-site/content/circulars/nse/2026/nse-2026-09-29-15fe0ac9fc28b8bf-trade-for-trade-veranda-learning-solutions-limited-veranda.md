## Summary

Pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement, the National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced that Veranda Learning Solutions Limited (VERANDA) will move to the Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE) effective October 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Veranda Learning Solutions Limited
- Stock Symbol: VERANDA
- New Trading Segment: Trade for Trade (Series: BE)
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026 (Tuesday)
- Pre-open Session: Will be part of call auction in pre-open session as per SEBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory delivery-based trading without netting off for VERANDA shares in the Trade for Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must update their systems to reflect the segment change for VERANDA effective from October 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading mechanics for VERANDA will change as trades must result in delivery, restricting intraday square-off positions.
