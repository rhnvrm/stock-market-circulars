## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Fourteen securities have met the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I, effective September 30, 2026. No securities were listed under Stage II.

## Key Points

- 14 securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I effective September 30, 2026.
- Applicable margin rate is set at 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Enhanced margin requirements apply to all open positions as on September 30, 2026, and new positions created from October 01, 2026.
- Surveillance shortlisting is a risk-management measure and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of ST-ASM Stage I surveillance actions and margin capping provisions as per exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must collect and maintain the mandated 50% margin on all open and new positions for the specified securities starting October 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Higher margin requirements for shortlisted securities may curb excessive speculation and impact short-term liquidity and trading volumes.
