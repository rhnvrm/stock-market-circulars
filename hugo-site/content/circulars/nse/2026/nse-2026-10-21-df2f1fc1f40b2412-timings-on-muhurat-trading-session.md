## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced operational timings, settlement schedules, margin reporting, and collateral timelines for the Commodity Derivatives Segment on account of the Diwali Muhurat Trading session scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Muhurat Trading Date**: Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- **Risk Parameters**: Two risk parameter files based on security prices at previous close and end of day will be provided.
- **Settlement Schedule**: Pay-in/pay-out transactions for trade dates November 06, 2026, and November 08, 2026, will be settled on November 09, 2026, at 8:30 hrs.
- **Client Margin Reporting**: Specific cut-off dates are assigned for trade dates ranging from November 02, 2026, to November 08, 2026.
- **CP Code Modification**: Timings for custodian participant (CP) code modification and give-up confirmation for trades on November 08, 2026, are set between 14:20 hrs and 15:40 hrs.
- **Collateral Timings**: Specific windows for cash addition (12:15 to 16:15), collateral allocation (11:45 to 19:00), securities release request (cut-off at 16:15), and cash release (12:15 to 16:15).

## Regulatory Changes

No structural regulatory changes; outlines temporary operational and settlement adjustments for the festive trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must adhere to revised client margin reporting cut-off dates, collateral allocation windows, and settlement timelines associated with the Muhurat Trading session.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: October 21, 2026
- **Muhurat Trading Date**: November 08, 2026
- **Settlement Date for Nov 06 & Nov 08 Trades**: November 09, 2026 (at 8:30 hrs)
- **Margin Reporting Cut-off Dates**: Nov 02 trade -> Nov 08 cut-off; Nov 03 trade -> Nov 09 cut-off; Nov 04 trade -> Nov 11 cut-off; Nov 05 trade -> Nov 12 cut-off; Nov 06 trade -> Nov 13 cut-off; Nov 08 trade -> Nov 16 cut-off.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing, settlement, and risk management operations during the special Diwali Muhurat Trading session without disrupting standard clearing cycles.
