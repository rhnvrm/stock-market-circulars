## Summary

Following the listing of Propshop Events and Exhibitions Limited, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the transfer of the company's equity shares from the trade-for-trade segment (Series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM). This shift is in accordance with SEBI guidelines regarding SME IPOs.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** PROPSHOP
- **Company Name:** Propshop Events and Exhibitions Limited
- **Segment Change:** Transfer from the trade-for-trade segment (ST series) to the rolling segment (SM series).
- **Authority:** Pursuant to SEBI guidelines under circular ref. no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.
- **Reference Circular:** Further to Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/75516 dated July 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory framework is introduced. This is a standard execution of existing SEBI guidelines for transitioning SME securities after their listing phase.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Update internal system configurations, risk management systems, and client-facing trading platforms to reflect the series transition of PROPSHOP from 'ST' to 'SM' effective from the market open on August 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 03, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** The transfer to the rolling segment (SM series) typically improves liquidity and trade flexibility for the stock as it enables normal trading rules (including intraday netting), transitioning away from the mandatory trade-for-trade settlement (which requires 100% upfront delivery with no netting).
