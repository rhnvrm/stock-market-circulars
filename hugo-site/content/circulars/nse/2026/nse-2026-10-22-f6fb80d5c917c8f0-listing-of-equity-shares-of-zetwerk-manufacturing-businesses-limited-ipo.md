## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited (IPO). The security will trade under the symbol ZETWERK with ISIN INE09PO01019.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited
- Symbol: ZETWERK
- ISIN: INE09PO01019
- Department: LISTING
- Download Ref No: NSE/CML/77062
- Circular Ref. No: 1906/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative notification regarding a forthcoming security listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the security details and ISIN for operational readiness.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the admission to dealings and subsequent trading of equity shares of Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited on the NSE main board.
