## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has admitted multiple privately placed commercial papers (CPs) to dealings on its Debt Market Segment, effective from August 11, 2026. This admission is conducted in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or statutory changes are introduced. This is an operational listing circular implementing established trading regulations under existing procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuing companies must maintain continuing disclosure compliance and ensure the timely fulfillment of redemption requirements as specified in their commercial paper terms. General market participants do not need to take immediate action except to note the newly available security codes and ISINs for secondary market operations.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment**: August 10, 2026 (for all issuances)
- **Effective Date for Dealings/Trading**: August 11, 2026
- **Redemption Dates**:
  - **October 30, 2026**: Godrej Agrovet Limited (Sr 10)
  - **November 5, 2026**: Godrej Properties Limited (Sr 63)
  - **November 6, 2026**: Tata Projects Limited (Sr 221), ICICI Securities Limited, Nuvoco Vistas Corporation Limited
  - **November 9, 2026**: Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Hdfc Securities Limited, Godrej Agrovet Limited (Sr 11), Toyota Financial Services India Limited (Sr 3), NABARD

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Neutral to positive. The listings provide institutional investors with organized secondary market trading avenues and increased liquidity options for high-quality corporate debt and short-term instruments.
- **Operational Impact**: Clearing and settlement configurations on the NSE Debt Market Segment are updated to support the listed ISINs starting August 11, 2026.
- **Issuer Impact**: Broadens the marketability and trading depth for the issuers' short-term money market securities.
