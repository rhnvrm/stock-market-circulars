## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of privately placed securities on its capital market segment, effective from September 11, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective September 11, 2026.
- Includes issuance from Alphagrep Securites Private Limited (10.50% 2027, ISIN: INE961U07010) and IIFL Finance Limited (9.35% 2033 Sr D 38, ISIN: INE530B08268).
- Trading shall be conducted in designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the capital market segment pursuant to regulatory provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure transactions are executed using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Maturity Dates: September 12, 2027 (Alphagrep) and September 8, 2033 (IIFL Finance)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine market operations supporting the listing and trading of newly issued privately placed securities.
