## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Long-Term ASM framework. Securities satisfying the specified criteria have been shortlisted and placed under Long-Term ASM Stage I effective from October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 15, 2026, on all open positions as on October 14, 2026, and new positions created from October 15, 2026 onwards.
- Securities shortlisted include Aastha Spintex Limited, Cedaar Textile Limited, Davangere Sugar Company Limited, and Tarapur Transformers Limited.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shortlisting of securities under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I.
- Implementation of 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for affected scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure 100% margin is maintained on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities starting October 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Framework / Inclusion: October 13, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin Requirement: October 15, 2026 (on open positions as of October 14, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the shortlisted securities will attract stricter margin norms (100%), impacting liquidity and leverage for market participants trading in these scrips.
