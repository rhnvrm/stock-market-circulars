## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has issued the settlement calendar and operational schedules for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares of Life Insurance Corporation of India (LICI). The settlement of securities in the OFS will follow the existing rules for secondary market transactions, referencing the earlier circular NCL/CMPT/73996 dated April 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Process**: Settlement of securities under the OFS will align with standard secondary market transaction rules.
- **Settlement Calendar**: Clear schedules have been established for both non-retail (IS series) and retail (RS series) categories across multiple order and settlement dates.
- **Financial Levies**: Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) and stamp duty will be collected on the respective settlement days.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory rules are introduced; this circular executes standard procedures under existing guidelines (Ref: NCL/CMPT/73996).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Market Participants**: Members, custodians, and Professional Clearing Members (PCMs) must adhere to the specified custodial confirmation and settlement deadlines for retail and non-retail orders.
- **STT and Stamp Duty**: Ensure sufficient funds are available for the collection of STT and stamp duty on the respective settlement days.
