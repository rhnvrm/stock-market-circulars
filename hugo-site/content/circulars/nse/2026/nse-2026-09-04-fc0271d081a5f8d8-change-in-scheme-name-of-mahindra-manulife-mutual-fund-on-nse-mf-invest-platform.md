## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names of Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective with immediate effect / August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mahindra Manulife Low Duration Fund is renamed to Mahindra Manulife Ultra Short to Short Term Fund.
- Mahindra Manulife Dynamic Bond Fund is renamed to Mahindra Manulife Dynamic Term Fund.
- Mahindra Manulife Ultra Short Duration Fund is renamed to Mahindra Manulife Ultra Short Term Fund.
- Mahindra Manulife Short Duration Fund is renamed to Mahindra Manulife Short Term Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

Changes are carried out pursuant to Part IV of Chapter 3 on “Categorization & Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes” of the SEBI Master Circular dated March 20, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are requested to note the revised scheme names and categories on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 04, 2026
- Effective Date of Changes: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating scheme nomenclature across trading and investment records.
