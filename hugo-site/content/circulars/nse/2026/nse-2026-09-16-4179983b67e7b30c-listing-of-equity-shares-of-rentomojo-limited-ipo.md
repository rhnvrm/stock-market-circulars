## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Rentomojo Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO), detailing the shareholding pattern and post-issue details.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Rentomojo Limited
- Date of Filing/Listing: 16.09.2026
- Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 20,555,509 shares (20.47%)
- Public Holding: 74,951,793 shares (74.65%)
- Total Paid-up Equity Shares: Disclosed as per the shareholding pattern.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard post-issue listing disclosure under Regulation 31(1)(a).

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with SEBI listing regulations and continuous disclosure norms.

- Effective Date: 16.09.2026
