## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of ZCZP (Zero Coupon Zero Principal) Instruments of National Skill Development Corporation on the Capital Market segment effective from October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Description: NSDC ZCZP 0%
- Symbol: NSDC-SE
- Series: N0
- Number of Securities: 4,000,000
- ISIN: INS08SO12012
- Maturity Date: 16-Oct-2029
- Note: The above-mentioned securities will not be traded on the Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard notification for the listing of ZCZP instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

Securities are identified only by their designated codes for listing purposes on the system.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026
- Maturity Date: October 16, 2029

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact as these securities will not be traded on the exchange.
