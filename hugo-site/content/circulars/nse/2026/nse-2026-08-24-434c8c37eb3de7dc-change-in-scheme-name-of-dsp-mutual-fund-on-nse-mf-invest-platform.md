## Summary

DSP Mutual Fund has announced a change in the scheme name and features of its debt scheme, DSP 10Y G-Sec Fund, pursuant to SEBI Master Circular requirements dated March 20, 2026. The changes become effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name Change: 'DSP 10Y G-Sec Fund' is renamed to 'DSP 10 year Constant Maturity Gilt Fund'.
- Portfolio Allocation: Minimum investment of 80% of total assets in government securities such that the Macaulay duration of the portfolio is equal to 10 years.
- TREPs/repo or any other alternatives as provided by RBI remain allocated between 0% to 20%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Changes are implemented pursuant to Part IV under Chapter 3 of the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026, and SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Unitholders are notified of the scheme name change and updated portfolio duration structure for ongoing compliance.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact for investors on trading platforms, as existing folios will reflect the updated scheme name and features automatically.
