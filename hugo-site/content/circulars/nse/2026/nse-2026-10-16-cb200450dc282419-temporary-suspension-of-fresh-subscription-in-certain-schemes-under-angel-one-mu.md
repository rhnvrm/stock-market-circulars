## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and new registrations for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) in the Angel One Gold ETF FOF under Angel One Mutual Fund, effective immediately on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Fresh subscriptions and lumpsum switch-ins in Angel One Gold ETF FOF are suspended with immediate effect.
- Fresh registrations of Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) are disabled.
- Existing SIPs and STPs will continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA end, but top-ups on existing SIPs are not permitted.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and STP-outs remain fully unrestricted.
- The suspension will remain in effect until further notice.

## Regulatory Changes

Operational restriction implemented by Angel One Asset Management Company Limited on the NSE MF Invest platform regarding scheme subscriptions.

## Compliance Requirements

Intermediaries, distributors, and investors must halt fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, and new SIP/STP registrations for the affected scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 15, 2026
- **Circular Date:** October 16, 2026
- **Effective Date:** With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Investors are restricted from deploying fresh capital or initiating new recurring investments (SIP/STP) into Angel One Gold ETF FOF. Existing unit holders retain full operational liquidity through redemptions and SWPs.
