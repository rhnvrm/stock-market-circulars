## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced a face value split for Bansal Wire Industries Limited (BANSALWIRE). The face value and paid-up value of equity shares will be reduced from Rs. 5 to Re. 1 per share, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bansal Wire Industries Limited
- Symbol: BANSALWIRE
- Existing Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/-
- New Face Value & Paid-up Value: Re. 1/-
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Stock split implementation modifying the nominal face value and paid-up value of equity shares from Rs. 5 to Re. 1.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the face value change and price adjustment in trading systems effective from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The face value split increases the number of shares proportionally and adjusts market prices accordingly, enhancing liquidity in retail trading segments.
