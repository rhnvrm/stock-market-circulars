## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for several companies, effective from August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities of companies including Aster DM Quality Care Limited, Bharat Wire Ropes Limited, Aditya Infotech Limited, Delhivery Limited, and others are admitted to dealings.
- Allotments include equity shares issued pursuant to schemes of arrangement and ESOPs.
- Trading will commence from August 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notification in pursuance of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to designated security codes and market lot sizes as specified in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026
- Circular Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for market participants regarding newly admitted equity shares.
