## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of LCC Projects Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The security will trade under the symbol LCCPROJECT.

## Key Points

- Company Name: LCC Projects Limited
- Symbol: LCCPROJECT
- ISIN: INE1FPN01026
- Department: LISTING

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed about the forthcoming listing details and ISIN for record and operational readiness.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 16, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular.

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and market participants to update systems and prepare for the commencement of trading in equity shares of LCC Projects Limited.
