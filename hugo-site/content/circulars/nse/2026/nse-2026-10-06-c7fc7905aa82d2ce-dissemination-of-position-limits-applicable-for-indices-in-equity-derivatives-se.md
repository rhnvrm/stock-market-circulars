## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the dissemination of position limits for all indices in the Equity Derivatives segment, effective from October 6, 2026, pursuant to prior circulars on monitoring position limits.

## Key Points

- Position limits for all index derivatives are disseminated starting October 6, 2026.
- Specifies client category-wise position limits for Index Futures across FPI-I, Mutual Funds, Trading Members, and FPI-II (broken down by entity type).
- Trading Member-wise position limits established for Index Futures (Rs. 7,500 crores or 15% of total open interest) and Index Options.
- Client-level limits in Index Options are monitored on a Future Equivalent (FutEq/Delta) basis.
- Report available on NSE website under Market Data => Derivatives => Daily Reports (Index_deriv_pos_limit_DDMMYYYY).

## Regulatory Changes

Formalizes the ongoing monitoring and dissemination schedule of position limits for equity index derivatives with specific caps categorized by participant types.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with applicable client-level and trading member-level position limits for index futures and options, referencing files made available on the Exchange website.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 6, 2026
- File Availability: w.e.f. October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and adherence to prescribed position limits for trading members, institutional investors, and FPIs operating in index derivatives segments.
