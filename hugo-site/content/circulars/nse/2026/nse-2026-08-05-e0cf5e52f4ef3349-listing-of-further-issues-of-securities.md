## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified the listing of further equity shares of three companies on its SME EMERGE platform with effect from August 06, 2026. The companies are Party Cruisers Limited (allotted under ESOP), Ramdevbaba Solvent Limited (allotted under Preferential allotment), and Z-Tech (India) Limited (allotted under Preferential Issue).

## Key Points

- **Party Cruisers Limited**: Listing of 4,800 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP.
- **Ramdevbaba Solvent Limited**: Listing of 1,137,600 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under Preferential allotment at Rs. 139 per share.
- **Z-Tech (India) Limited**: Listing of 205,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under Preferential Issue at Rs. 500 per share.
- **Trading Details**: All shares will be admitted to dealings on the SME EMERGE platform under their respective lot sizes and designated ISIN/security codes.

## Regulatory Changes

No structural regulatory changes have been introduced. This is a routine listing announcement issued under:
- **Regulation 3.1.1** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A (concerning admission of securities to dealings).
- **Regulation 2.5.5** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A (concerning market lot sizes).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Market Lot Sizes**: Trading must be done in the respective designated market lots:
  - **PARTYCRUS**: 1,000 shares
  - **RBS**: 800 shares
  - **ZTECH**: 300 shares
- **Lock-in Period**: Members and investors must note and comply with the lock-in requirements as specified in Annexure A for each issuer.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular**: August 05, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing**: August 06, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**:
  - Party Cruisers Limited: May 07, 2026
  - Ramdevbaba Solvent Limited: July 09, 2026
  - Z-Tech (India) Limited: July 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. These are small-to-medium-scale listings of further issues on the SME EMERGE platform, resulting in minor changes to the outstanding equity capital of the respective firms.
- **Trading Impact**: Immediate trading of the newly listed securities becomes active starting August 06, 2026.
