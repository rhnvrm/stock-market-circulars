## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The schemes will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.

## Key Points

- SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund of SBI Mutual Fund is reopened for ongoing transactions.
- Facilities enabled include ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective date for availability is September 04, 2026.
- Includes multiple plans such as Direct Plan - Growth, Direct Plan - IDCW Payout, Regular Plan - IDCW, Direct Plan - IDCW, Regular Plan - Growth, and Regular Plan - IDCW Payout.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants should note the scheme details and ISINs for transaction processing on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 03, 2026
- Effective Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to trade and manage investments in the specified SBI Mutual Fund schemes through the NSE platform.
