## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced that the ASK Liquid Fund NFO of Ask Mutual Fund will be available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The transaction window opens on August 12, 2026, and closes on August 25, 2026, with the allotment date set for September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mutual Fund Scheme**: ASK Liquid Fund NFO.
- **Transaction Period**: August 12, 2026, to August 25, 2026, till 11:59 pm.
- **Category**: Debt.
- **Allotment Date**: September 01, 2026.
- **Scheme Variations**: Twelve distinct Regular and Direct plans are covered (including Growth, Daily IDCW Reinvestment, Weekly/Monthly IDCW Reinvestment, and Weekly/Monthly IDCW Payout).
- **ISIN details**:
  - Regular Plan Growth: INF2XUU01021
  - Direct Plan Growth: INF2XUU01013
  - Regular Plan IDCW Daily Reinvestment: INF2XUU01047
  - Direct Plan IDCW Daily Reinvestment: INF2XUU01039
  - Regular IDCW Monthly Reinvestment: INF2XUU01104
  - Direct IDCW Monthly Reinvestment: INF2XUU01120
  - Regular IDCW Monthly Payout: INF2XUU01096
  - Direct IDCW Monthly Payout: INF2XUU01112
  - Regular IDCW Weekly Reinvestment: INF2XUU01062
  - Direct IDCW Weekly Reinvestment: INF2XUU01088
  - Regular IDCW Weekly Payout: INF2XUU01054
  - Direct IDCW Weekly Payout: INF2XUU01070

## Regulatory Changes

This circular is an operational announcement and does not change or update any existing regulatory policies.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Funds Clearance**: Members must ensure that clear funds for subscriptions are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 01, 2026, till 08:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date**: August 12, 2026
- **NFO End Date**: August 25, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date**: September 01, 2026
- **Funds Clearance Deadline**: September 01, 2026 (till 08:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Low. The implementation of the NFO represents standard operational support for the listing and distribution of mutual funds, enabling seamless execution of liquid fund transactions for stockbroker members and their investors.
