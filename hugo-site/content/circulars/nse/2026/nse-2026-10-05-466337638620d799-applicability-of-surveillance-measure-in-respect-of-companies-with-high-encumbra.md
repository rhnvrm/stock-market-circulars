## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of surveillance measures for companies with high 'Encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 35% margin, while securities in Annexure II are moved out of the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 35% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective October 08, 2026 (on all open positions as on October 07, 2026, and new positions created thereafter).
- Securities in Annexure II move out of the framework effective October 06, 2026.
- Shortlisting is based purely on market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011 regarding high encumbrance.
- Margin requirements set to a minimum of 35% for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure 35% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives for securities listed in Annexure I.
- Company representations against inclusion must be submitted by 5:00 PM on October 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Representation Deadline: October 06, 2026, by 5:00 PM
- Exclusion Effective Date (Annexure II): October 06, 2026
- Open Positions Review Date: October 07, 2026
- Surveillance Measure Effective Date (Annexure I): October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in the affected securities will experience modified margin requirements (35% minimum margin), impacting leverage and position management in both equity and derivative segments.
