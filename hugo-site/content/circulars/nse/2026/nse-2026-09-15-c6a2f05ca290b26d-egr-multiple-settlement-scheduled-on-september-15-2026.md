## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding multiple settlements scheduled for September 15, 2026, necessitated by a settlement holiday on September 14, 2026. The circular details revised settlement calendars, pay-in/pay-out timings, auction schedules, and UNPAID MTF file upload timings for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment.

## Key Points

- Multiple settlements (Settlement Numbers 2026084, 2026085, and 2026086) are scheduled for September 15, 2026.
- Revised cut-off timings for pay-in at depositories have been established for September 15, 2026.
- Auction trading and settlement under the T+1 revised auction cycle are detailed for settlements 2026085, 2026086, and 2026087.
- Auction of shortages of settlement 2026086 will be conducted in 2026087 with no auction in 2026086 directly.
- Timings for uploading UNPAID MTF files for EGR Normal are specified for September 14 and September 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of settlement cycles and operational timelines for the EGR segment due to a prior settlement holiday.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take note of the revised settlement timings, pay-in/pay-out windows, and cut-off timings at the depositories' end.
- Members must adhere to the specified timings for uploading UNPAID MTF files and participate in auctions according to the revised schedule.

## Important Dates

- September 14, 2026: Settlement holiday.
- September 15, 2026: Multiple settlements date, revised pay-in/pay-out timings, and UNPAID MTF file uploading.
- September 16-17, 2026: Subsequent auction settlement dates.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants dealing in Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) must align their operational workflows, funds/securities readiness, and upload schedules with the revised timelines to prevent defaults and penalties.
