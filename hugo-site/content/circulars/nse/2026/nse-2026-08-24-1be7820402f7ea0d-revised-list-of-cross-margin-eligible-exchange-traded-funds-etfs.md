## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released a revised list of Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) eligible for cross margining in the Futures & Options segment, superseding the previous circular dated July 27, 2026. The updated list and minimum required quantities come into effect from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Supersedes circular no. 0094/2026 (Download Reference No: NCL/CMPT/75424) dated July 27, 2026.
- Specifies the updated list of eligible ETFs across multiple fund houses (including Nippon India, SBI, ICICI Prudential, Mirae Asset, Kotak Mahindra, HDFC, UTI, and Aditya Birla Sun Life).
- Defines the minimum quantity required (and multiples thereof) for each eligible ETF to qualify for cross margining.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic revision of the cross margin eligible ETF list as part of clearing and settlement operations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must ensure compliance with the updated minimum quantity requirements and eligible ETF securities when calculating or applying cross margins from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts trading members and clearing members utilizing cross-margining facilities with eligible ETF collateral in the Futures & Options segment by updating the eligible basket and required lot/quantity thresholds.
