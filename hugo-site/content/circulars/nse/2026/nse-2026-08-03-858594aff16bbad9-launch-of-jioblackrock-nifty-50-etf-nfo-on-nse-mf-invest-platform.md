## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the launch of the JioBlackRock Nifty 50 ETF New Fund Offer (NFO) under JioBlackRock Mutual Fund. The scheme will be available for transaction on the NSE MF Invest Platform from August 4, 2026, to August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Names & Details**:
  - JioBlackRock Nifty 50 ETF - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF22M001184, Category: Equity)
  - JioBlackRock Nifty 50 ETF - Regular Growth (ISIN: INF22M001184, Category: Equity)
- **Subscription Period**: Available on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting August 4, 2026, until August 11, 2026, up to 6:00 PM.
- **Allotment Date**: Scheduled for August 14, 2026.
- **Clear Funds Submission**: Clear funds must be received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 13, 2026, by 11:59 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced by this circular. It is a standard operational announcement facilitating transaction access for a new fund offer under existing platform guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members participating on the NSE MF platform must ensure client subscription requests are submitted before the 6:00 PM cut-off on August 11, 2026.
- Members must ensure that 100% clear funds are transferred and received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before the August 13, 2026 deadline (11:59 PM).

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date**: August 4, 2026
- **NFO Close Date**: August 11, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline**: August 13, 2026, by 11:59 PM
- **Allotment Date**: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. This is a standard product listing expansion under JioBlackRock Mutual Fund offering conventional passive equity index tracking.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Trading members will need to facilitate timely order capture and ensure strict adherence to the funds settlement timeline on the NSE MF platform.
