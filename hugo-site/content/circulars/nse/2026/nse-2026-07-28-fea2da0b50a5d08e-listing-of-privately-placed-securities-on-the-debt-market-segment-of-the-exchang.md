## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the admission of privately placed Commercial Papers (CPs) issued by multiple major companies to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange. In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, these commercial papers have been admitted to trading with effect from **July 28, 2026**.

The participating issuers are prominent entities across the manufacturing and financial sectors: Aditya Birla Money Limited, Axis Securities Limited, EPL Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, PNB Housing Finance Limited, and HDFC Securities Limited. Both fresh issuances and re-issuances of commercial papers are covered by this circular.

## Key Points

- **Admittance to Debt Segment:** Privately placed Commercial Papers have been formally admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of NSE.
- **Wide Issuer Range:** Key participants include financial intermediaries (Aditya Birla Money, Axis Securities, HDFC Securities), housing finance (PNB Housing Finance), diversified holding companies (Aditya Birla Capital), and packaging manufacturing (EPL Limited).
- **High Volume Issuance:** The list includes large allotments, notably Aditya Birla Capital's fresh issue of 16,000 securities and re-issue of 9,000 securities, and HDFC Securities' fresh issue of 10,000 securities.
- **Standardized Face Value:** All admitted commercial papers have a uniform face value of ₹5,00,000 per security.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Regulation Invoked:** Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.
- **Market Segment Integration:** Issuers must comply with established guidelines for debt-segment dealings, with securities mapping directly to unique ISINs and designated security codes.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading & Settlement:** Debt Market segment trading members must ensure that all trades in these commercial papers are reported and settled in accordance with standard NSE Debt Market procedures and schedules.
- **ISIN Verification:** Trading terminals and operations departments must verify the exact ISINs for each issuer series before execution, particularly where there are multiple maturities or re-issues for the same issuer (e.g., Aditya Birla Capital and HDFC Securities).

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates:** July 27, 2026 and July 28, 2026.
- **Effective Date of Dealings:** July 28, 2026.
- **Redemption Calendars:**

  - **Aditya Birla Capital Limited (ABCL CP 26/10/26):** October 26, 2026 (Fresh).
  - **Aditya Birla Money Limited (ABML CP 26/10/26):** October 26, 2026.
  - **Hdfc Securities Limited (HSL CP 26/10/2026):** October 26, 2026 (Re-issue).
  - **Hdfc Securities Limited (HSL CP 27/10/2026):** October 27, 2026 (Fresh).
  - **Axis Securities Limited (ASL CP 27/10/26 Sr 33):** October 27, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- **Secondary Market Liquidity:** Admitting these short-term instruments to the exchange segment facilitates secondary market liquidity for institutional investors and mutual funds.
- **Refinancing and Capital Flow:** The successful placement and listing of these papers indicate consistent market demand and stable cost of short-term borrowing for the issuing financial and corporate conglomerates.
- **Operational Impact:** This is a routine operational notice with low structural impact on the equity market prices of the respective parent or group companies (such as Axis Bank, HDFC Bank, Aditya Birla Capital, or EPL), though it signals positive credit-market health.
