## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a change of Market Maker for SME company Prime Cable Industries Limited (Symbol: PRIMECAB), effective from October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Prime Cable Industries Limited
- Symbol: PRIMECAB
- Old Market Maker: Alacrity Securities Ltd. (Member Code: 09098)
- New Market Maker: NDA Securities Ltd. (Member Code: 06438)
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants should take note of the updated market maker details for settlement and trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational change with minimal impact on overall market liquidity or investors.
