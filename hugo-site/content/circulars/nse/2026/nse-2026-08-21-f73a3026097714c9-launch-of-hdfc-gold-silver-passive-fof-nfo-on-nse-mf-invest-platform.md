## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF New Fund Offer (NFO) by HDFC Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- Schemes available: HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF Regular Growth and HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF Direct Growth.
- ISINs: INF179KC1KN7 (Regular Growth) and INF179KC1KO5 (Direct Growth).
- NFO Transaction Window: August 24, 2026 to September 07, 2026 till 11:59 pm.
- Allotment Date: September 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 10, 2026 till 11:59 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 24, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 07, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 10, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 10, 2026 (11:59 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to subscribe to the HDFC Gold Silver Passive FOF NFO via the NSE MF Invest Platform within the specified dates.
