## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers and other debt securities) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fresh Issues (Commercial Papers):** 
  - IIFL Samasta Finance Limited (400 CPs, face value ₹5,00,000, maturing 30-Mar-2027, ISIN: INE413U14398)
  - Godrej Industries Limited (1,500 CPs, face value ₹5,00,000, maturing 06-Jan-2027, ISIN: INE233AD4215)
  - 5Paisa Capital Limited (300 CPs, face value ₹5,00,000, maturing 24-Mar-2027, ISIN: INE618L14359)
  - Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited (4,000 CPs, face value ₹5,00,000, maturing 04-Dec-2026, ISIN: INE901L14CT7)
  - JM Financial Services Limited (200 CPs, face value ₹5,00,000, maturing 07-Apr-2027, ISIN: INE012I14TK2)
  - Senco Gold Limited (400 CPs, face value ₹5,00,000, maturing 30-Mar-2027, ISIN: INE602W14012)
  - National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) (1,00,000 CPs, face value ₹5,00,000, maturing 04-Jan-2027, ISIN: INE261F14QI1)
- **Fresh Issues (Other Debt Securities):** 
  - Lakshminarayan Securitisation Trust (4,500 PTCs, 8.37% coupon rate, maturing 28-Sep-2031, ISIN: INE31CW15014)
- **Re-Issues (Commercial Papers):** 
  - Chambal Fertilizers & Chemicals Limited (6,000 CPs, face value ₹5,00,000, maturing 07-Dec-2026, ISIN: INE085A14IK8)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; notification issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard listing disclosures and debt market compliance apply to the respective issuers.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates:** October 05, 2026 and October 07-08, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** October 08, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Ranging from December 04, 2026 to September 28, 2031

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations reflecting capital raising activities by issuers through debt instruments and commercial papers on the exchange.
