## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the revised list of securities available for transactions under the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for the month of October 2026, pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/18/2013 dated May 30, 2013 and consolidated Circular no. NCL/CMPT/73999 dated April 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Updated list of approved securities for SLBS transactions effective for October 2026.
- Supersedes previous circulars regarding the SLBS security list.
- Applies to all participants and custodians.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic revision of eligible securities under the Securities Lending & Borrowing Scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians must ensure trading and lending/borrowing activities in SLBS adhere to the updated list of eligible securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective for transactions for the month of October 2026.
- Circular Date: September 28, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants must update their systems and risk parameters based on the revised list of eligible securities for SLBS.
