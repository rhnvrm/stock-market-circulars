## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has approved the listing of privately placed debt securities on its Capital Market segment. This admission to dealings is effective from July 31, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A. The security belongs to Rupeek Capital Private Limited.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date**: Trading and dealings in the security will commence on July 31, 2026.
- **Security Identification**: Rupeek Capital Private Limited's 11% 2028 Series I (Symbol: 11RCPL28, ISIN: INE0DG907370).
- **Market Lot**: The designated trading lot size is 1.
- **Regulation Alignment**: The listing and admission comply with NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, specifically Regulation 3.1.1 for admissions and Regulation 2.5.5 for lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory policy or guideline modifications introduced by this circular. This is an administrative listing approval executed under existing Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

No general compliance actions are required from members, except that trading in this security must utilize the designated code (Symbol: 11RCPL28) and respect the specified market lot of 1.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 30, 2026
- **Effective Date of Trading**: July 31, 2026
- **Maturity Date**: January 28, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. The listing is limited to a single private placement issue from a private corporation, affecting only its specific investors.
- **Operational Impact**: Members and trading systems need to configure and map the security details (Symbol: 11RCPL28, ISIN: INE0DG907370, Market Lot: 1) for trading and settlement starting July 31, 2026.
