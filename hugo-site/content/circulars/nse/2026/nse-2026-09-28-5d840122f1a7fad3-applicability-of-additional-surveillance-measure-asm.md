## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for various securities effective September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I: Docmode Health Technologies Limited (DHTL), Man Industries (India) Limited (MANINDS), Menon Bearings Limited (MENONBE), and STL Networks Limited (STLNETWORK).
- Securities moved from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I to Stage - II: Sumeet Industries Limited (SUMEETINDS).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective October 01, 2026, on all open positions as on September 30, 2026, and new positions created from October 01, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) effective October 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Shortlisting of securities under the ASM framework based on market surveillance parameters, enforcing strict margin rules and movement across surveillance stages.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure 100% margin collection on open positions and new positions for the affected securities starting October 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ASM shortlisting / stage shifts: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin requirement: October 01, 2026 (on open positions as of September 30, 2026 and new positions onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and trading margin requirements for the shortlisted scrips (DHTL, MANINDS, MENONBE, STLNETWORK, SUMEETINDS).
