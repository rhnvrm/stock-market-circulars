## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has notified the suspension of trading in five series of privately placed Non-Convertible Securities issued by the International Finance Corporation. The suspension is effective from August 10, 2026, and is being executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A due to a buyback of these securities.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** International Finance Corporation
- **Type of Securities:** Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed)
- **Number of Instruments:** 5 ISINs affected
- **Effective Date of Suspension:** August 10, 2026
- **Reason for Suspension:** Buyback of the securities
- **Regulatory Clause:** Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory framework changes are introduced. This is an operational action taken in accordance with existing Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Holders of the affected ISINs and trading members should note the suspension from trading starting August 10, 2026. No trading actions can be executed on these securities on the exchange post this date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Suspension Effective Date:** August 10, 2026

| 5 | INE375R08058 | 10-Aug-2026 | Buyback |

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Nil. The suspension is localized to the specific Non-Convertible Securities of the International Finance Corporation.
- **Operational Impact:** Trading in the specified ISINs will be disabled on the Debt Market segment of NSE starting August 10, 2026, to facilitate the buyback process.
