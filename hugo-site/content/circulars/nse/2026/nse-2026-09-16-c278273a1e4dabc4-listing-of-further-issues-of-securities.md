## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of equity shares for various companies pursuant to ESOP allotments, effective from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) effective September 17, 2026.
- Includes companies such as Amagi Media Labs Limited, AU Small Finance Bank Limited, Bajaj Electricals Limited, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited, Diamines & Chemicals Limited, Dixon Technologies (India) Limited, and others.
- Shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the circular annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 16, 2026
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the respective listed entities.
