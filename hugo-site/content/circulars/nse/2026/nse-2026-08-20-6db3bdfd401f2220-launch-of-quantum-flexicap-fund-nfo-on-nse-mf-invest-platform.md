## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of the Quantum Flexicap Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window opens on August 21, 2026, and closes on September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Quantum Flexi Cap - Direct Growth & Regular Growth
- ISINs: INF082J01507 (Direct Growth), INF082J01515 (Regular Growth)
- Category: Equity
- NFO Window: August 21, 2026 to September 04, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 09, 2026, till 08:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: August 21, 2026
- NFO End Date: September 04, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: September 09, 2026 (08:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the Quantum Flexicap Fund NFO via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
