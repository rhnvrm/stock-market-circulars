## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding a change in the International Securities Identification Number (ISIN) for the equity shares of Narmada Agrobase Limited (NARMADA). This change is necessitated by a sub-division of the company's equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Narmada Agrobase Limited
- **Symbol:** NARMADA
- **New ISIN:** INE117Z01029
- **Reason for Change:** Sub-division of equity shares from a face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/-
- **Effective Date:** July 31, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

There are no broad regulatory policy changes, but the identification code (ISIN) for trading NARMADA shares is updated to ensure proper clearing and settlement of trades post-sub-division.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange must update their systems with the new ISIN (INE117Z01029) for all trades executed on or after the effective date of July 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Ex-Date / Effective Date:** July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a standard corporate action affecting only the specific security (Narmada Agrobase Limited).
- **Operational Impact:** All trade execution, settlement, and clearing processes for NARMADA from July 31, 2026, onwards must utilize the new ISIN code to prevent settlement failures.
