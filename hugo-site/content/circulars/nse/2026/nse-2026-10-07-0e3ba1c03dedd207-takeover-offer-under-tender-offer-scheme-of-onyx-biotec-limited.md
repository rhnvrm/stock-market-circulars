## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the takeover offer under the tender offer scheme for Onyx Biotec Limited. The acquirers (Mr. Kunal Bhardwaj along with Mr. Gautam Bhardwaj, Mr. Kanhaiya Bhardwaj, and Mr. Kapil Bhardwaj) have approached the Exchange for the usage of the web-based bidding platform.

## Key Points

- **Security Symbol:** ONYX
- **ISIN:** INE0WVU01018
- **Issue Period:** October 13, 2026 to October 27, 2026
- **Issue Size:** Up to 47,14,372 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 36.50/- per share
- **Lot Size:** 2,000 Equity shares
- **Offer Type:** Takeover / Fixed price
- **Merchant Banker:** VC Corporate Advisors Private Limited
- **Registrar:** MAS Services Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is conducted in accordance with the Tender Offer Scheme Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.
- In case of takeover offers, order modification (quantity) / cancellation shall not be permitted.

## Compliance Requirements

- All trading members of the Capital Market segment are eligible to participate in the Tender Offer Facility.
- Existing web-based IPO bidding platform user IDs can be used. Members without access must log in to the admin terminal to create branches and users.
- Bidding time and custodial confirmation time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. (Custodial confirmation on issue closure day up to 4.00 p.m.).

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Issue Closing Date:** October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables eligible shareholders of Onyx Biotec Limited to tender their shares through trading members on the NSE web-based platform during the specified issue period.
