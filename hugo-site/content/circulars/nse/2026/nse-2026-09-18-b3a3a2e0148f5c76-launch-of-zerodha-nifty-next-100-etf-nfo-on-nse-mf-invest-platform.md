## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the availability of the Zerodha Nifty Next 100 ETF NFO from Zerodha Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from September 21, 2026, to October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Zerodha Nifty Next 100 ETF Direct Growth
- **ISIN:** INF0R8F01257
- **Category:** Equity
- **Allotment Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 8, 2026, by 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** September 21, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** October 5, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Fund Pay-in Deadline:** October 8, 2026 (till 03:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors an opportunity to transact in the new Zerodha Nifty Next 100 ETF scheme through the NSE mutual fund platform.
