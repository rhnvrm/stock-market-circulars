## Summary

On July 27, 2026, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) announced that the Axis Nifty50 Equal Weight Index Fund NFO of Axis Mutual Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic transaction facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from July 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Reopening of Schemes:** Axis Nifty50 Equal Weight Index Fund is made available for ongoing subscription, redemption, and systematic facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Scheme Details Covered:**
  - **Axis Nifty50 Equal Weight Index Fund Direct Growth**: ISIN `INF846KA1564` (Category: Equity)
  - **Axis Nifty50 Equal Weight Index Fund Regular Growth**: ISIN `INF846KA1556` (Category: Equity)
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced by this circular. This is a standard administrative enablement of a mutual fund scheme on the exchange's mutual fund platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members/Distributors:** Registered members of the NSE MF segment may facilitate transactions (subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic plans) for these schemes for their clients.
- **Action Required:** Ensure back-office systems and client-facing portals are updated to support the ISINs `INF846KA1564` and `INF846KA1556` for ongoing transactions from July 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 27, 2026
- **Effective Date:** July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a standard operational update adding an investment option for investors on the platform.
- **Operational Impact:** Enables trading members and investors to perform transactions, including SIP, STP, and SWP, in the newly available Axis Mutual Fund schemes.
