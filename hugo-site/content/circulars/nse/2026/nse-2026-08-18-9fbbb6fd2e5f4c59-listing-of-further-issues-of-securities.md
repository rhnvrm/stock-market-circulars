## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for several companies, effective from August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities further admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective August 19, 2026.
- Companies include Bandhan Bank Limited, Fischer Medical Ventures Limited, Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Limited, India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited, and others.
- Allotments include shares under ESOP and Preferential Issue.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing notification in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes as specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for secondary market trading of newly allotted equity shares.
