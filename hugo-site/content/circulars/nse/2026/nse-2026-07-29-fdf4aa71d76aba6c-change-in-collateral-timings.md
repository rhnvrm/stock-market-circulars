## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has announced a revision in the cut-off timings for placing collateral release requests. In partial modification of the earlier circular (NCL/CMPT/75318 dated July 21, 2026), the deadline for placing requests for Cash and FDR Intraday release, as well as Securities End of Day (EOD) release, has been advanced from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM. These revised timings will come into effect from July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Revision of Cut-off Timings**: The final timing for submitting collateral release requests to NCL has been revised and made uniform.
- **Cash and FDR Release (Intraday)**: Cut-off time moved earlier from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
- **Cash and FDR Release (End of Day)**: Cut-off time remains unchanged at 8:00 PM.
- **Securities EOD**: Cut-off time moved earlier from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
- **Effective Date**: The changes are applicable starting July 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The circular partially modifies the previous guidelines set out in circular ref. no. 0263/2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/CMPT/75318) dated July 21, 2026, which governed the timings for collateral addition and release.

| Release Type | Current Cut-off Time | New Cut-off Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cash and FDR Release (Intraday) | 9:00 PM | 8:00 PM |
| Cash and FDR Release (End of Day) | 8:00 PM | 8:00 PM |
| Securities EOD | 9:00 PM | 8:00 PM |

## Compliance Requirements

- **Operational Alignment**: Clearing Members, Custodians, and Professional Clearing Members (PCM) must align their internal processes and systems to ensure that all collateral release requests are submitted before the new 8:00 PM cut-off.
- **Client Communication**: Inform active clients and internal treasury operations about the advanced timeline to avoid delayed releases.

## Important Dates

- **July 29, 2026**: Circular issuance date.
- **July 30, 2026**: Effective date for the revised collateral timings.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Shorter operating window for managing intraday collateral releases. Members must ensure release requests are initiated at least one hour earlier than before.
- **Liquidity Management**: Improved standardization of clearing house closing timelines, but members need to plan their liquidity and capital requirements slightly earlier in the evening.
