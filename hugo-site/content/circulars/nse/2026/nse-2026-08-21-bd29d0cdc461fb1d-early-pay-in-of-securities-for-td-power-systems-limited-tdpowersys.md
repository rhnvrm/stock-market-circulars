## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited issued instructions regarding the early pay-in (EPI) of securities for TD Power Systems Limited (TDPOWERSYS) on account of a face value split (sub-division) from Rs 2/- per share to Re 1/- per share. Market participants and custodians are advised to execute early pay-in instructions using the old ISIN for the specified trade dates and settlements.

## Key Points

- Security: TD Power Systems Limited (TDPOWERSYS)
- Corporate Action: Face Value Split from Rs 2/- to Re 1/-
- Existing/Old ISIN: INE419M01027
- Record Date & Ex-Date: August 24, 2026
- EPI Dates and Settlements: August 24, 2026 (Settlement No. 2026159) and August 25, 2026 (Settlement Nos. 2026159 & 2026160).

## Regulatory Changes

- Participants must execute early pay-in in the depository systems using the old ISIN without adjusting for the conversion for the applicable settlement type on the trade date.
- Block mechanism guidelines apply as per SEBI circular; client-wise early pay-in allocation file should not be uploaded when using the block mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Custodians providing early pay-in through the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details as per the actual traded quantity to pass benefits to clients.
- Market participants and custodians must execute pay-in/early pay-in instructions in the New ISIN after the record date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: 24-AUG-2026
- Record Date: 24-AUG-2026
- EPI Date: 24-AUG-2026 and 25-AUG-2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement processes and correct ISIN handling during corporate actions like stock splits for market participants, custodians, and clients.
