## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular announcing the revocation of suspension of trading in units of UTI Annual Interval Fund - I. The revocation is effective from October 14, 2026, following confirmation from UTI Asset Management Company Limited regarding the continuation of the scheme post the closure of the Specified Transaction Period (STP).

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 14, 2026.
- **Fund House:** UTI Asset Management Company Limited.
- **Scheme Name:** UTI Annual Interval Fund - I.
- **Reason:** Confirmation of scheme continuation post closure of STP (Specified Transaction Period).
- **Affected Schemes / Symbols / ISINs:**
  - UTFIAIPRGI (INF789F01FG4)
  - UTFIAIPRRI (INF789F01FF6)
  - UTFDAIPIPI (INF789F01VU2)
  - UTFIAIPIGI (INF789F01FJ8)
  - UTFDAIPIGI (INF789F01VW8)
  - UTFIAIPRPI (INF789F01FE9)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational notification regarding the resumption of trading in interval fund units.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can resume trading in the specified units of UTI Annual Interval Fund - I from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Effective Date of Revocation:** October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and market participants to trade units of the specified UTI Annual Interval Fund - I schemes on the exchange platform following the successful conclusion of the interval period processing.
