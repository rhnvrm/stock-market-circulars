## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from October 13, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Fresh Issues:** Commercial papers admitted for listing include:
  - **NTPC Limited:** NTPC CP 23/11/26 (ISIN: INE733E14CM4), 60,000 securities, Face Value ₹5,00,000, Allotment Date: 12-Oct-2026, Redemption Date: 23-Nov-2026.
  - **ICICI Securities Limited:** ISEC CP 09/04/27 (ISIN: INE763G14P09), 1,000 securities, Face Value ₹5,00,000, Allotment Date: 12-Oct-2026, Redemption Date: 09-Apr-2027.
  - **CEAT Limited:** CL CP 08/04/27 (ISIN: INE482A14HM5), 1,000 securities, Face Value ₹5,00,000, Allotment Date: 12-Oct-2026, Redemption Date: 08-Apr-2027.
  - **Shriram Finance Limited:** SFIL CP 18/12/26 (ISIN: INE721A14DZ7), 40,000 securities, Face Value ₹5,00,000, Allotment Date: 12-Oct-2026, Redemption Date: 18-Dec-2026.
  - **Aditya Birla Capital Limited:** ABCL CP 22/12/26 (ISIN: INE674K14DF9), 10,800 securities, Face Value ₹5,00,000, Allotment Date: 12-Oct-2026, Redemption Date: 22-Dec-2026.
  - **IIFL Finance Limited:** IIFL CP 11/01/27 (ISIN: INE530B14JI3), 500 securities, Face Value ₹5,00,000, Allotment Date: 12-Oct-2026, Redemption Date: 11-Jan-2027.
  - **UltraTech Cement Limited:** UCL CP 10/12/26 (ISIN: INE481G14GA1), 10,000 securities, Face Value ₹5,00,000, Allotment Date: 13-Oct-2026, Redemption Date: 10-Dec-2026.
- **Re-issues:** Commercial papers re-issued and admitted include:
  - **Tata Capital Limited:** TCL CP 24/12/26 Sr 1 (ISIN: INE976I14RZ0), 8,000 securities, Face Value ₹5,00,000, Allotment Date: 12-Oct-2026, Redemption Date: 24-Dec-2026.
  - **ICICI Securities Limited:** ISEC CP 15/03/27 (ISIN: INE763G14L78) and ISEC CP 25/03/27 (ISIN: INE763G14L37), Allotment Date: 12-Oct-2026, Redemption Dates: 15-Mar-2027 and 25-Mar-2027.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; notification of security listings under existing debt market regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers must ensure compliance with National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations and continuous disclosure norms.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Listing Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Allotment Dates:** October 12, 2026 & October 13, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** November 23, 2026 to April 9, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine debt market listing providing liquidity and secondary market access for commercial papers issued by corporate entities.
