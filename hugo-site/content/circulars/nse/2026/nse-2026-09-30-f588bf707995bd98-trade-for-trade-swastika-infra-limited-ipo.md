## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of Swastika Infra Limited's equity shares from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ) effective October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Swastika Infra Limited
- Symbol: SWASTIKAIN
- Action: Transferred from Trade for Trade segment (series: BE) to Rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026
- Reference Circulars: NSE/CML/76628 dated September 29, 2026; SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are moved to the regular rolling settlement segment pursuant to SEBI guidelines on IPO trading segments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should note the change in segment and series for the security from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for Swastika Infra Limited shares, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility.
