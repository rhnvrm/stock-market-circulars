## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement schedule for the Futures & Options (F&O) segment for the month of September 2026. The calendar specifies the corresponding settlement date for each trade date during the month.

## Key Points

- **Routine Calendar**: Provides clear trading and settlement timelines for September 2026 to facilitate orderly market operations.
- **First Trade of Month**: Trades executed on September 01, 2026, will be settled on September 02, 2026.
- **Last Trade of Month**: Trades executed on September 30, 2026, will be settled on October 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes or policy amendments introduced in this circular. It is a standard operational announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

All trading and clearing members must align their settlement systems and ensure adequate funding/collateral availability in accordance with the specified schedule to avoid settlement defaults.
