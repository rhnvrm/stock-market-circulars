## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Qualiance International Limited (SME IPO) effective from September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Qualiance International Limited
- Security: Equity Shares
- Scrip Code/Name: QUALIANCE
- Date of Listing / As on: 09.09.2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 as applicable to post-issue shareholding patterns and disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 09.09.2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Qualiance International Limited shares on the NSE SME platform.
