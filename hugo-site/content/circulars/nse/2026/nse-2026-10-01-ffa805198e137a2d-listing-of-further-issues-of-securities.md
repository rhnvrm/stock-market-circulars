## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing of further equity shares of Transteel Seating Technologies Limited on the SME Emerge segment, effective from October 05, 2026, following a preferential issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Transteel Seating Technologies Limited
- Symbol: TRANSTEEL
- Security Description: Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued under Preferential Allotment.
- Number of Securities: 785,000 equity shares
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 80/-)
- Distinctive Number Range: 22882011 to 23667010
- ISIN: INE0NVI01020 (Temporary ISIN IN8********* used initially as per SEBI circular guidelines)
- Market Lot: 1,000 (Pari Passu)
- Lock-in Details: Up to April 05, 2027

## Regulatory Changes

None. Admitted under existing trading regulations (Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: July 23, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: October 05, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 05, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up equity capital base of Transteel Seating Technologies Limited on the NSE SME Emerge platform with standard lock-in restrictions.
