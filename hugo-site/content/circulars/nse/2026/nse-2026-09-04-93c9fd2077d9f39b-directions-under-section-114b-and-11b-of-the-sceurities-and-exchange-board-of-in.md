## Summary

SEBI issued interim/final directions under Sections 11(4)(b) and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992 against Akar Laminators Limited and its directors (Mr. Hitesh Ravindra Shah, Mr. Vinodbhai Tarachand Sheth, and Mr. Hasmukhbhai Tarachand Sheth) due to their persistent failure to resolve pending investor grievances and submit required status reports.

## Key Points

- Numerous investor complaints remained pending and unresolved against Akar Laminators Limited.
- The company and its directors failed to respond to multiple letters, reminders, and a public notice issued by SEBI advising them to redress investor grievances.
- SEBI initiated action under Sections 11(4)(b) and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992 to protect investors and maintain market integrity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Restraint on the company and its directors from accessing the securities market and prohibiting them from dealing in securities until pending investor grievances are resolved.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company and its directors must resolve all pending investor grievances immediately and report compliance to SEBI to have the restrictions lifted.

## Important Dates

- Date of order/notice actions span through 2008 to 2010.

## Impact Assessment

- Direct restriction on the company's access to capital markets and prohibition of trading activities for its directors until full compliance is achieved.
