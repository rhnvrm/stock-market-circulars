## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the discontinuation of the existing encrypted mechanism of interactive messages in the Cash Market (CM), Futures & Options (FO), Currency Derivatives (CD), and Commodity Derivatives (COM) segments. Effective December 07, 2026, the existing encryption mechanism through old ports will be permanently discontinued, and trading members must migrate to the new encryption mechanisms.

## Key Points

- Discontinuation Date: December 07, 2026, for the existing encryption mechanism across CM, FO, CD, and COM segments.
- Affected Segments: Cash Market (CM), Futures & Options (FO), Currency Derivatives (CD), and Commodity Derivatives (COM).
- Interim coexistence period concludes on December 07, 2026.
- No extensions will be granted beyond the discontinuation date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Phasing out of legacy encryption ports and mechanisms in favor of the new encryption mechanism with MD5 checksum removal and new immediate acknowledgment of order-related messages.
- Updating of gateway router IP addresses, subnet ranges, and new ports for NNF and NEAT / NEAT Adapter users.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members using NNF, NEAT, or NEAT Adapter must take note of new parameters and plan to mandatorily migrate to either the 'new encryption mechanism with MD5 checksum removal' or 'new encryption mechanism & immediate acknowledgement of order related messages' before the deadline.
- Complete migration activity on or before December 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Discontinuation Date: December 07, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must update their network and gateway configurations promptly to avoid disruption in order routing and interactive message processing across CM, FO, CD, and COM segments.
