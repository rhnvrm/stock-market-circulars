## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the revision of Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) and position limits for Participants, Institutional clients, and Non-Institutional clients for the symbol PGIL under the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) Scheme for September 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol affected: PGIL
- Effective date of revised limits: September 15, 2026
- Market Wide Position Limit increased from 1,795,929 to 3,591,859 shares.
- Institutional client limit increased from 179,592 to 359,185 shares.
- Participant limit increased from 179,592 to 359,185 shares.
- Non-Institutional client limit increased from 17,959 to 35,918 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

Position limits for the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme for PGIL have been revised upwards in pursuance of earlier circulars (NCL/CMPT/73999 and NCL/CMPT/76085).

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians are advised to take note of the revised limits while executing transactions under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The revision allows increased participation and borrowing/lending limits for PGIL in the SLB segment in line with updated market metrics.
