## Summary

National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding non-business days for certain mutual fund schemes for September 2026 based on communication received from various AMCs.

## Key Points

- Subscriptions, Redemptions, Switches, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs will not be allowed in specific schemes on their respective non-business days in September 2026.
- Affected schemes belong to AMCs including Axis, DSP, Franklin Templeton, Invesco, Kotak Mahindra, Nippon India, PGIM India, and Edelweiss.
- The restrictions apply to transactions processed through the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Operational notification regarding scheme-specific non-business days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that transactions for the specified schemes are not processed on the effective non-business dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Dates: Various dates across September 2026 (e.g., September 7, 2026, September 21, 2026, September 23, 2026, September 25, 2026, and September 28, 2026).
- Circular Date: September 04, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Temporary restriction of specific mutual fund transactions on the NSE MF Invest platform for the listed schemes on their respective non-business days.
