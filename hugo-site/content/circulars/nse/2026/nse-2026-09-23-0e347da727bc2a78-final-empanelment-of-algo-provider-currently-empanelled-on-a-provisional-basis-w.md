## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has granted final empanelment to Multiicon Ideotechnology Private Limited as an Algo Provider for trading members, following the successful fulfillment of conditions from its prior provisional empanelment.

## Key Points

- Multiicon Ideotechnology Private Limited has been granted final empanelment.
- Previously empanelled on a provisional basis via circular NSE/INVG/72238 dated January 09, 2026.
- Vendor provides Whitebox algorithmic trading solutions.
- Applicable with immediate effect.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative empanelment update.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members utilizing algorithmic trading solutions can engage with the newly finalized empanelled provider in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2026
- Previous Provisional Circular Date: January 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; provides trading members with an officially finalized vendor option for algorithmic trading solutions.
