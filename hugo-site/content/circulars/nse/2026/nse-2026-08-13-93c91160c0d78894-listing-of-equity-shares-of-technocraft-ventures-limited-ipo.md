## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Technocraft Ventures Limited pursuant to its IPO, effective from August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: TECHNOCRAF
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE1D0W01018
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 212/-
- Total No. of Securities: 39,606,200
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: August 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of further securities to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be in the specified lot sizes and identified by the designated security codes. The security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of Technocraft Ventures Limited equity shares on the NSE capital market segment.
