## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the rights issue of Natco Pharma Limited. Trading will commence on October 12, 2026, in the Trade to Trade segment.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Natco Pharma Limited
- **Symbol:** NATCO-RE
- **ISIN:** INE987B20018
- **Number of Rights Entitlements:** 17,058,082
- **Trading Period for Renunciation:** October 12, 2026 to October 16, 2026
- **Date of Suspension:** October 19, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of Rights Entitlements to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of NSE Trading Regulations Part A. The REs shall be traded in the Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares. Contract notes or bills containing purchases of REs must include a prominent disclaimer informing clients that purchase of REs only gives them the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue by making an application with requisite application money or renouncing the REs before the issue closes. Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the Issue Closing Date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Trading Period for Renunciation:** October 12, 2026 to October 16, 2026
- **Date of Suspension:** October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Eligible investors receive rights entitlements which can be traded during the designated window or used to apply for shares in the rights issue. Market participants must adhere to trading restrictions and disclosure requirements in contract notes.
