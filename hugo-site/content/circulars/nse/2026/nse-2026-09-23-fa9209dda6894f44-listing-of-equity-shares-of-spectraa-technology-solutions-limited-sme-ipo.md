## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of SpectraA Technology Solutions Limited following its SME IPO, along with the shareholding pattern under Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: SpectraA Technology Solutions Limited
- Scrip Code/Name: Equity shares issued via SME IPO
- Shareholding pattern filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) post issue of IPO on September 23, 2026.
- Declared that locked-in shares are applicable, while other items like partly paid shares, convertible securities, warrants, and depository receipts are not applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for shareholding pattern disclosure post-IPO.

## Compliance Requirements

- The listed entity must maintain compliance with SEBI LODR regulations regarding shareholding disclosures and locked-in shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of filing/post issue: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading of equity shares of SpectraA Technology Solutions Limited on the NSE SME platform.
