## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Commodity Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, August 08, 2026. This session will involve trading from both the Primary site and the Disaster Recovery (DR) site. There is no new software version release for this mock session, and its primary purpose is to test connectivity, disaster recovery switchover, and ensure smooth operations for the upcoming live trading session on Monday, August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mock Session Date**: Saturday, August 08, 2026.
- **No New Version Release**: The NEAT version remains at 1.5.5.
- **Two Trading Sessions**:
  - **Session 1 (Primary Site)**: Normal Market open at 11:00 Hrs and close at 13:30 Hrs.
  - **Session 2 (DR Site)**: Normal Market open at 14:15 Hrs and close at 15:30 Hrs.
- **Disaster Recovery (DR) Test**: A switchover to the DR site will be conducted during Session 2.
- **Order Purge**: All outstanding orders will be cleared before each mock session. NNF users must manually clear orders in their systems.
- **No Financial Obligations**: Trades during the mock session are for testing purposes only and will not result in any financial pay-in or pay-out.
- **UCC/PAN Validity**: Only valid and compliant UCC/PANs approved before the cutoff are allowed to participate, though validation is skipped during contingency order entry.
- **Service Disruption**: Connect2NSE, Extranet facility, and Extranet APIs will have temporary unavailability periods during the mock session afternoon.

## Regulatory Changes

- There are no direct regulatory policy updates in this circular. The mock session is held in compliance with existing guidelines such as SEBI circular `SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234` and Exchange circulars on interactive connectivity and UCC/PAN validation.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Participation**: Members are strongly advised to participate actively using all their trading software and complete the live re-login process on Saturday to prevent connectivity issues on Monday, August 10, 2026.
- **Manual Order Clearance**: Members using Non-NEAT Frontend (NNF) software must manually clear outstanding orders before each session.
- **UCC Compliance**: Ensure all UCC/PAN data is updated and approved prior to the cutoff.
- **Telnet Testing**: Verify telnet connectivity to the Exchange host from the intended IP address.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Session**: Saturday, August 08, 2026.
- **Live Re-login Window**: Saturday, August 08, 2026, between 16:30 Hrs and 17:00 Hrs.
- **Next Live Trading Day**: Monday, August 10, 2026.
- **Extranet Unavailability**: 12:30 Hrs to 21:00 Hrs on August 08, 2026 (Extranet API unavailable from 12:30 Hrs to 14:00 Hrs).

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: High during the test window on Saturday, August 08, 2026, as members must perform tests and re-login. No impact on live trading, and no financial risk since trades carry no financial obligations.
- **System Impact**: Verifies system resilience and backup failover mechanisms via the Disaster Recovery site. Ensures high availability for future live sessions.
