## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the revocation of the temporary suspension for subscriptions in the ICICI Prudential Aggressive Hybrid Fund Monthly IDCW option on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Resumption applies to lumpsum transactions (fresh/additional purchase), switch-in, and systematic transactions (SIP & STP).
- Effective date for the resumption of services is October 22, 2026.
- Based on the Notice-cum-Addendum issued by ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund dated October 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an operational update regarding the availability of fund subscription and systematic transaction modes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors can resume investments across all permitted transaction modes starting October 22, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members can resume normal subscription and investment activities in the specified scheme option on the NSE platform without prior restrictions.
