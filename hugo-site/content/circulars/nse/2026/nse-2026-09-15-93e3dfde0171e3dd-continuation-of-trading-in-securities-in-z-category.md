## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the continuation of trading in securities under the 'Z' Category ('BZ/SZ' Series) for companies identified as non-compliant with Regulation 31 (Submission of Shareholding Pattern) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (March 31, 2026, and June 30, 2026).

## Key Points

- 17 companies have been identified as non-compliant with Regulation 31 for the quarters ending March 31, 2026, and June 30, 2026.
- Securities will continue to trade in the 'Z' Category ('BZ/SZ' Series) effective September 28, 2026.
- Trades in these securities will continue to be settled on a Trade for Trade Basis.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated July 11, 2023, and last updated on January 30, 2026.
- Enforcement of trading restrictions due to non-submission of shareholding pattern.

## Compliance Requirements

- Affected companies must fulfill the pending compliance regarding the submission of shareholding patterns under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 28, 2026
- Non-compliant Quarters: March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors dealing in the securities of these 17 companies will experience continued restrictions as trades will settle on a Trade for Trade basis in the Z category.
