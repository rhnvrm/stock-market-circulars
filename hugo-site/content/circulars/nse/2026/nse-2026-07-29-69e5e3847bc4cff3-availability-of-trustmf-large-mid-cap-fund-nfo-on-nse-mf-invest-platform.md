## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the reopening of Trustmf Large & Mid Cap Fund NFO of Trust Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Starting July 30, 2026, the schemes will be available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic transaction facilities (SIP, STP, SWP).

## Key Points

- **Scheme Reopening**: Reopening of Trust Mutual Fund's Trustmf Large & Mid Cap Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Available Facilities**: Subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) are all supported.
- **Effective Date**: The changes are effective from July 30, 2026.
- **Scheme Details**:
  - **Trustmf Large & Mid Cap Fund - Direct Plan-Growth** (ISIN: INF0GCD01891, Category: Equity)
  - **Trustmf Large & Mid Cap Fund - Regular Plan-Growth** (ISIN: INF0GCD01909, Category: Equity)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational platform enablement and not a modification to regulatory policies or frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Platform Participants / Intermediaries**: Members and mutual fund distributors utilizing the NSE MF Invest Platform should update their backend systems to support transactions in the reopened schemes starting July 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Effective/Reopening Date**: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. This is a standard mutual fund platform onboarding update that affects investors and distributors transacting in Trust Mutual Fund schemes.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Offers operational convenience by allowing systematic and lump sum transactions in the newly reopened schemes directly on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
