## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has issued a circular (Ref. No: 0277/2026, Download Ref No: NCL/COM/75460) dated July 29, 2026, announcing a change in the cut-off timings for collateral operations in the Commodity Derivatives Segment. This circular partially modifies the previous circular (Ref No. 0264/2026 / Download Ref No: NCL/COM/75319 dated July 21, 2026) regarding timings for collateral addition and release. Specifically, the revised cut-off time for placing Cash/FDR (Intraday and End of Day) and Securities EOD release requests with NCL has been shortened to 8:00 PM.

## Key Points

- **Scope of Modification:** Partially modifies circular ref. no. 0264/2026 (Download Ref No: NCL/COM/75319 dated July 21, 2026).
- **Revised Cut-Off Times:** The deadline for submitting collateral release requests has been standardized and accelerated to 8:00 PM across all major types.
  - **Cash and FDR Release (Intraday):** Reduced from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
  - **Cash and FDR Release (End of Day):** Remains at 8:00 PM.
  - **Securities EOD:** Reduced from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
- **Affected Parties:** Applies to all Members, Custodians, and Professional Clearing Members (PCMs) participating in the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- **Effective Date:** The revised timings will take effect starting July 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial modification of the previously established collateral addition and release schedule outlined in circular Ref No. 0264/2026.
- Standardization of the daily cut-off time to 8:00 PM for Cash/FDR (Intraday/EOD) and Securities EOD collateral release requests under NCL's Commodity Derivatives framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Earlier Submission:** Members, custodians, and PCMs must ensure that all collateral release requests (for Cash, FDR, and Securities) are completed and submitted to NCL by the new 8:00 PM deadline.
- **Process Adjustments:** Internal operations and systems should be updated to account for the one-hour reduction (from 9:00 PM to 8:00 PM) for Intraday Cash/FDR and Securities EOD release requests to avoid processing delays or failed releases.

## Important Dates

- **July 21, 2026:** Date of the previous circular (Ref No. 0264/2026 / Download Ref No: NCL/COM/75319).
- **July 29, 2026:** Date of issuance of the current circular (Ref No. 0277/2026).
- **July 30, 2026:** Effective date of the new cut-off timings.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** High relevance for Treasury and Clearing operations of clearing members, professional clearing members (PCMs), and custodians. The shortened window means tighter timelines for managing intraday liquidity and collateral optimization on a daily basis.
- **Liquidity Management:** Intraday release of funds (Cash/FDR) must be managed more proactively since the deadline has been moved up by an hour (to 8:00 PM), potentially affecting overnight positioning or fund transfers.
- **System Automation:** Automated or semi-automated trading and clearing tools must be updated with the revised 8:00 PM threshold to avoid automated alerts or late submittals.
