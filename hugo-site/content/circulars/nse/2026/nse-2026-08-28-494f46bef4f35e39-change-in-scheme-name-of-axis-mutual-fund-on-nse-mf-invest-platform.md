## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names of Axis Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective immediately with communication from the fund house.

## Key Points

- **Existing Scheme Name**: Axis ELSS Tax Saver Fund
- **Revised Scheme Name**: Axis ELSS- Tax Saver Fund
- **Existing Scheme Name**: Axis Banking & PSU Debt Fund
- **Revised Scheme Name**: Axis Banking and PSU Debt Fund
- The changes are implemented following communication received from Axis Mutual Fund along with a notice cum addendum.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates to scheme nomenclature as per regulatory categorization guidelines and fund house filings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are advised to note the revised scheme names on the NSE MF Invest platform for ongoing transactions and reporting.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 28, 2026
- **Effective Date**: With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact limited to display name updates on trading platforms and investor folios.
