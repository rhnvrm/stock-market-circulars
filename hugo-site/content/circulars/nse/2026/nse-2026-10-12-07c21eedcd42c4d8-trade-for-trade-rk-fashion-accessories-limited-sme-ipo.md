## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of R.K. Fashion Accessories Limited from the trade for trade segment (Series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** R.K. Fashion Accessories Limited
- **Symbol:** RKFAL
- **Segment Transfer:** From trade for trade segment (series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM)
- **Effective Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Reference:** Further to Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76831 dated October 09, 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines (CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine transition under existing SEBI and Exchange guidelines following an SME IPO.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the change in segment and series for trading in the securities of R.K. Fashion Accessories Limited effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in RKFAL shares will move to the normal rolling settlement segment, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility for market participants.
