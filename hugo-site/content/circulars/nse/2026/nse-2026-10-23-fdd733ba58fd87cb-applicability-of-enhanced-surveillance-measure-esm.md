## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) for selected securities. Four securities (Bilcare Limited, Flexituff Ventures International Limited, Oil Country Tubular Limited, and United Heat Transfer Limited) are moving from ESM Stage II to Stage I effective October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Four securities are shifting from Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) Stage II to Stage I w.e.f. October 26, 2026.
- Affected securities: Bilcare Limited (BI), Flexituff Ventures International Limited (FLEXITUFF), Oil Country Tubular Limited (OILCOUNTUB), and United Heat Transfer Limited (UHTL).
- ESM framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under ESM is based on market surveillance criteria and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities shifting under ESM are subject to enhanced surveillance framework requirements including margin requirements and settlement mechanisms.
- Price band and settlement segment adjustments apply as specified for ESM stages.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the movement of securities between ESM stages and ensure compliance with applicable margin rates and settlement segments.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ESM Stage movement: October 26, 2026.
- Margin applicability date: w.e.f. October 27, 2026, on all open positions as on October 26, 2026, and new positions created from October 27, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in Bilcare Limited, Flexituff Ventures International Limited, Oil Country Tubular Limited, and United Heat Transfer Limited will experience changes in margin requirements and settlement types in accordance with ESM Stage I parameters.
