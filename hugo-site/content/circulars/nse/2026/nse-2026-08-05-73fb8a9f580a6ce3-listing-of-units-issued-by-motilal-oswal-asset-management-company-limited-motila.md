## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular announcing the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by Motilal Oswal Asset Management Company Limited on the Exchange's Capital Market segment. Specifically, this applies to the units of **Motilal Oswal Nifty Metal ETF** and **Motilal Oswal Nifty Oil & Gas ETF** with effect from August 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Schemes Listed**:
  1. **Motilal Oswal Nifty Metal ETF**:
     - **Symbol**: MOMETAL
     - **ISIN**: INF247L01HG7
     - **No. of Units**: 4,250,000
     - **Face Value**: Rs. 10/-
     - **Issue Price**: Rs. 12.88/-
  2. **Motilal Oswal Nifty Oil & Gas ETF**:
     - **Symbol**: MOOILGAS
     - **ISIN**: INF247L01HH5
     - **No. of Units**: 4,750,000
     - **Face Value**: Rs. 10/-
     - **Issue Price**: Rs. 11.30/-
- **Series**: EQ for both schemes.
- **Market Lot**: 1 unit.
- **Allotment Date**: August 03, 2026.
- **Admitted Market Makers**:
  - East India Securities Limited (TM Code: 08134)
  - Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited (TM Code: 10412)
  - Kanjalochana Finserve Private Limited (TM Code: 90246)
  - Irage Broking Services LLP (TM Code: 90068)
- **Key Entities**:
  - **Trustee**: Motilal Oswal Trustee Company Limited
  - **Asset Management Company**: Motilal Oswal Asset Management Company Limited
  - **Registrar**: KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No systemic regulatory changes are introduced. The admission of these securities is performed under standard regulatory provisions, specifically Regulation 3.1.1 (admission to dealings) and Regulation 2.5.5 (lot sizes) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Trading members should ensure that their front-end trading systems are updated with the new symbols, ISINs, and a market lot size of 1 unit.
- **Market Makers**: Designated market makers are required to provide continuous liquidity and bid-ask spreads for the schemes in accordance with relevant Exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date**: August 03, 2026
- **Circular Date**: August 05, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing & Trading**: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: The listings expand investment avenues by offering targeted, exchange-traded access to the metal and oil & gas sectors. This is expected to attract retail and institutional capital interested in sectoral asset allocation.
- **Operational Impact**: Standard listing processes apply. Operations teams, custodians, and brokers must ensure that the master files and security databases are populated with the symbols MOMETAL and MOOILGAS before trading commences on August 06, 2026.
