## Summary

National Stock Exchange has notified the admission of privately placed securities (commercial papers) to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listing of commercial papers from multiple issuers including Godrej Industries Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Toyota Financial Services India Limited, Minda Corporation Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, JM Financial Services Limited, NABARD, Tata Steel Limited, and Reliance Retail Ventures Limited.
- Effective date of trading: September 15, 2026.
- Issued under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard compliance as applicable to debt market issuances and commercial paper listings.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 11-Sep-2026
- Listing/Effective Date: 15-Sep-2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from September 2026 to December 2026 across various securities.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing of commercial papers on the debt segment with no broad market impact.
