## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted for Stage I, movement to Stage II, margin requirements, and trade-for-trade settlement.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin effective October 12, 2026, on open positions as on October 09, 2026, and new positions created from October 12, 2026.
- Shift of securities from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 12, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 09, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) stages for specified securities.
- Margin requirement set to a minimum of 100%.
- Segment shifting from EQ/SM to BE/ST (Trade-for-Trade).
- Stage II securities placed under Periodic Call Auction with a 2% price band.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must note the 100% margin applicability on open and new positions.
- Ensure correct settlement series (BE/ST) mapping for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II price band & auction / movement: October 09, 2026.
- Margin applicability effective date: October 12, 2026 (on open positions as on October 09, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience higher margin obligations and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes.
