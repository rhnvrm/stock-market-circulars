## Summary

NSE is conducting a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives (F&O) segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026, across both Primary and Disaster Recovery (DR) sites. No new software version is released for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for October 24, 2026, in the Equity Derivatives (F&O) segment.
- Two trading sessions: Session-1 from Primary Site (Pre-Open at 11:00 hrs, Normal Close at 13:15 hrs) and Session-2 from DR Site (Pre-Open at 16:00 hrs, Normal Close at 16:30 hrs).
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 remains applicable, available at `/faoftp/faocommon/NEATPlus790`.
- Reminder for NNF members to migrate away from the old interactive port (10837) and encryption mechanism ahead of the December 07, 2026 discontinuation date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adherence to SEBI and Exchange guidelines for conducting mock trading and validating disaster recovery switchovers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must manually clear outstanding orders in NNF systems before sessions.
- Valid and approved UCC/PAN checks apply as per existing guidelines.
- NNF members must migrate to new gateway routers and ports supporting updated encryption mechanisms.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 24, 2026
- NNF Old Encryption Discontinuation: December 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures operational readiness and DR switchover capability for F&O members. Trades executed during mock sessions carry no financial obligations.
