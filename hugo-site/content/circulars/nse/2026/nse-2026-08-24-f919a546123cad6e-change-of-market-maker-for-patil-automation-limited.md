## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change of Market Maker for the SME company Patil Automation Limited (Symbol: PATILAUTOM).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Patil Automation Limited
- Symbol: PATILAUTOM
- Old Market Maker: MANSI SHARE & STOCK BROKING PRIVATE LIMITED (Member Code- 12972)
- New Market Maker: ASNANI STOCK BROKER PRIVATE LIMITED (Member Code- 90087)
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational change of market maker status for an SME listed security.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the updated market maker details for their records and operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 25-Aug-2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact restricted to liquidity provision and market making operations for Patil Automation Limited on the SME platform.
