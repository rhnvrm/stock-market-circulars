## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding a corrigendum published by Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund. The corrigendum clarifies reference numbers for previous addendums and confirms that there is no change in the scheme name of Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Advantage Fund.

## Key Points

- Reference to previous addendum nos. 74/2026 and 75/2026 dated August 24, 2026.
- Addendum No. 74/2026 pertaining to Debt Schemes is to be read as Addendum No. 77/2026.
- Confirms there is no change in the name of the scheme "Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Advantage Fund".

## Regulatory Changes

- Correction of addendum reference numbers following revised SEBI Categorization and Rationalization Framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and investors are advised to take note of the corrected addendum reference and scheme name status.

## Important Dates

- Corrigendum Date: August 26, 2026
- Circular Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; purely informational clarification for investors and platform participants.
