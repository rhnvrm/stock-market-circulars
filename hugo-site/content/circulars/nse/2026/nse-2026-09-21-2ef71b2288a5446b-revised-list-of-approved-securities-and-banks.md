## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the revised list of approved securities, GOI securities, open-ended mutual funds, and empaneled banks to be used as collateral in the currency derivatives segment, effective for October 2026. This circular supersedes the previous circular no. 039/2026 dated August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective month: October 2026.
- Covers equity shares, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), sovereign gold bonds (SGBs), GOI securities (G-Sec)/T-Bills, open-ended mutual funds, and empaneled banks for BGs and FDRs.
- G-Sec/T Bills have a shut period of two days; members are requested to release them at least two days before maturity.
- Members who are also banks must ensure G-Sec provided as collaterals are not reckoned for SLR purposes or used for trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated market-wide and member-specific limits, along with applicable haircut rates for approved securities and liquid assets.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must note the updated limits and haircut rates for margin and collateral management.
- Release G-Sec/T Bills at least two days prior to maturity due to the two-day shut period.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 2026
- Circular Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts clearing members and custodians trading in the currency derivatives segment by updating acceptable collateral valuations and limits.
