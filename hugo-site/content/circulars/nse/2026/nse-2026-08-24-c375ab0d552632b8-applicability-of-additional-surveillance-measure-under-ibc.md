## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The changes take effect from August 25, 2026, and apply to specific securities based on corporate announcements and IBC criteria.

## Key Points

- Gangotri Textiles Limited has been shortlisted w.r.t. receipt of corporate announcement/disclosure.
- Vivimed Labs Limited has been shortlisted in ASM IBC Stage I.
- Supreme Engineering Limited has been excluded from ASM IBC.
- Consolidated list of securities under ASM IBC Stage I includes Aban Offshore Limited, Impex Ferro Tech Limited, and Reliance Infrastructure Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance measures under the ASM IBC framework in conjunction with other prevailing measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must note the surveillance status of the listed securities and ensure compliance with respective trading margins and requirements.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to surveillance measures and enhanced monitoring as per the ASM framework.
