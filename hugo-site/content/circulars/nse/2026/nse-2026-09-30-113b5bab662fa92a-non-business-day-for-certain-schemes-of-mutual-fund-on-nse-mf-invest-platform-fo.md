## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding non-business days for certain mutual fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest platform for the month of October 2026, as communicated by various Asset Management Companies (AMCs).

## Key Points

- Subscriptions, Redemptions, Switches, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs will not be allowed in the specified schemes on their respective non-business days in October 2026.
- Affected AMCs include Axis, Bandhan, DSP, Edelweiss, Invesco, Kotak, Nippon, PGIM, and Sundaram.
- Effective dates for non-business days span across October 2026 (e.g., October 1, 9, 12, 19, 26).

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; this is a routine operational circular regarding fund-specific non-business days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must take note of the non-business days when scheduling transactions (SIPs, subscriptions, redemptions, etc.) for the affected schemes on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Month: October 2026 (Specific dates include October 1, 9, 12, 19, and 26, 2026 across various schemes).

## Impact Assessment

- Temporary operational restriction on transactions for specific overseas and thematic mutual fund schemes on the NSE MF platform during the declared non-business days.
