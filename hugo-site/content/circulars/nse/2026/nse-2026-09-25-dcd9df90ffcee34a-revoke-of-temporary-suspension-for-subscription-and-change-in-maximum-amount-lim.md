## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the revocation of temporary suspension of subscription and implementation of a maximum amount limit under certain overseas fund of fund schemes of Invesco Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Subscription, switch-ins, and fresh registrations of SIP, STP, and IDCW Transfer Plan have resumed.
- Affected Schemes:
  1. Invesco India - Invesco Global Equity Income Fund of Fund
  2. Invesco India - Invesco Pan European Equity Fund of Fund
  3. Invesco India - Invesco Global Consumer Trends Fund of Fund
- Maximum investment limit: Rs. 10 Lakh per PAN, per day (inclusive of existing registered SIP/STP installments).
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revocation of the temporary suspension issued on May 11, 2026, allowing investments up to available headroom under overseas investment limits.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and platforms must ensure that aggregate investment transactions (lumpsum, switch-ins, and SIP/STP installments) do not exceed Rs. 10 lakh per day per PAN to avoid rejection.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 28, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors can resume participation in Invesco's designated overseas fund of fund schemes subject to the daily per-PAN ceiling and overall overseas investment limits.
