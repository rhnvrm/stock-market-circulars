## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Privately placed securities/commercial papers admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment effective September 22, 2026.
- Issuing entities include Hdfc Securities Limited, Hsbc Investdirect Financial Services (I) Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, NTPC Limited, Motilal Oswal Finvest, Reliance Retail Ventures Limited, Aseem Infrastructure Finance Limited, and Motilal Oswal Wealth Limited.
- Allotment dates are primarily September 21, 2026, with various redemption dates spanning from October 2026 to December 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Standard listing compliance for privately placed debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2026
- Allotment Dates: September 21, 2026
- Redemption Dates: October 2026 - December 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational listing with no broad market impact.
