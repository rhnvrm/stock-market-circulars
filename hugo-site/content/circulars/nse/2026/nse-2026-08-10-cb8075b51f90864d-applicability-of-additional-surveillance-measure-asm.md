## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) framework. The circular specifies the list of securities shortlisted for surveillance actions, as well as the transition of specific stocks within the ASM stages. This surveillance framework operates alongside other prevailing measures and is not an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Key Points

- **Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Applicability**: Securities listed in the Annexure have met the criteria for inclusion in the Long-Term ASM framework.
- **Margin Requirements**: A 100% margin rate will be applicable on all open positions as of August 12, 2026, and on new positions created starting August 13, 2026.
- **Segment Shift**: Securities qualifying under Criteria VII (Stage IV) will be moved from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. August 13, 2026.
- **Non-Adverse Action**: Shortlisting of securities under ASM is purely for market surveillance purposes and does not imply any negative assessment or adverse action against the issuer.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory frameworks are introduced; the action is taken under the existing ASM frameworks established by previous circulars (such as NSE/SURV/39265, NSE/SURV/64066, etc.).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading & Settlement**: Members must enforce the revised margin requirements of 100% for the shortlisted securities starting from August 13, 2026.
- **Series Transition**: Market operations must shift eligible securities under Criteria VII to the Trade-for-Trade (BE series) segment.

## Important Dates

- **August 11, 2026**: Shortlisting under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I takes effect for selected securities (e.g., A2Z Infra Engineering Limited).
- **August 12, 2026**: All open positions on this date will attract 100% margin starting the next day.
- **August 13, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and segment shifts (EQ to BE series).

## Impact Assessment

- **Affected Securities**: A2Z Infra Engineering Limited (`A2ZINFRA`) is moved from the Short-Term ASM (STASM) framework to Stage-I of the Long-Term ASM (LTASM) framework w.e.f. August 11, 2026. No other securities were added or transitioned in this specific update (Nil reported for other stages).
- **Market & Liquidity Impact**: Affected securities transitioned to Trade-for-Trade or subjected to 100% margins are likely to experience reduced trading volumes, decreased intraday leverage, and limited speculative activity.
