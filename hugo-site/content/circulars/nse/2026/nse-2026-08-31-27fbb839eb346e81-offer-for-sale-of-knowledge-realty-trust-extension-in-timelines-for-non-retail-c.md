## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced an extension of bidding timings for the Non-Retail category under the Offer for Sale (OFS) of Knowledge Realty Trust. The timelines have been extended until 7 PM on August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Extension applies to the Non-Retail category for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of Knowledge Realty Trust.
- Bidding timings extended till 7 PM on August 31, 2026.
- Continuation of previous exchange circular number NSE/CML/76042 dated August 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Timelines adjusted for bidding in the Non-Retail category of the specified OFS.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants participating in the OFS non-retail category must submit bids within the revised timeline (up to 7 PM on August 31, 2026).

## Important Dates

- August 31, 2026: Non-retail category bidding extended till 7 PM.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides additional time for non-retail investors to participate in the Knowledge Realty Trust Offer for Sale.
