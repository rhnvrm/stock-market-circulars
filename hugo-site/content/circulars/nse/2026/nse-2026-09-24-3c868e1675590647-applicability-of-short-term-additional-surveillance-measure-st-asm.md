## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for specified securities effective September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I include Ansal Buildwell Ltd, Brace Port Logistics Ltd, Jaykay Enterprises Ltd, Kore Digital Ltd, KPI Green Energy Ltd, Mos Utility Ltd, Optiemus Infracom Ltd, PC Jeweller Ltd, and Sat Kartar Life Ltd.
- Anlon Healthcare Ltd (AHCL) has been shortlisted to move from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II.
- Applicable margin rate for Stage I is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) effective September 28, 2026, on open positions as on September 25, 2026 and new positions.
- Applicable margin rate for Stage II is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) effective September 28, 2026, on open positions as on September 25, 2026 and new positions.
- Inclusion under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of enhanced surveillance margins (50% to 100%) under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure framework for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with applicable margin rates (50% or 100%) on open positions as of September 25, 2026, and all new positions created from September 28, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 24, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Action / Margins: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for affected securities may impact trading liquidity and volatility, requiring market participants to manage risk positions accordingly.
