## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of the equity shares of **Manipal Health Enterprises Limited** on the Exchange following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The exact date of commencement of trading will be notified via a separate circular.

## Key Points

- **Company Name**: Manipal Health Enterprises Limited
- **Symbol**: MANIPALHOS
- **ISIN**: INE459N01021
- **Status**: Forthcoming listing under the IPO category.
- **Next Steps**: The date of listing will be communicated to all members through a separate circular.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced by this notification. It is an administrative/listing update.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Ensure back-office systems and trading terminals are updated to support the symbol `MANIPALHOS` and ISIN `INE459N01021` prior to the official listing date.
- **Action Required**: No immediate action is required until the listing date is officially announced.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 04, 2026
- **Listing Date**: To be announced in a future circular.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Positive for market liquidity and the healthcare sector, allowing public trading of Manipal Health Enterprises Limited's equity shares once the listing date is finalized.
- **Operational Impact**: Systems must be configured to process transactions for symbol `MANIPALHOS` once active trading starts.
