## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued updates regarding the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment, revising the trade value computation formula and updating the 'Instrument Type' value in Udiff reports.

## Key Points

- Effective September 07, 2026, the trade value for EGR shall be computed as: Trade quantity * Trade price * (Trading unit / Quotation unit).
- Trade prices will continue to be reflected in the 'Quotation unit' across various member interfaces and reports provided by NCL.
- The 'Instrument Type' value (FinInstrmTp) in various Udiff reports will be modified from 'STK' to 'EGR'.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification in trade value computation parameters for Products under the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) Segment.
- Update to Udiff report instrument type codes for EGR products.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing and trading members must update their back-office systems and trading interfaces to align with the revised trade value formula and the new instrument type code ('EGR').

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 07, 2026
- Circular Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures accurate computation of trade values and standardizes instrument identification across Udiff reports for all market participants dealing in Electronic Gold Receipts.
