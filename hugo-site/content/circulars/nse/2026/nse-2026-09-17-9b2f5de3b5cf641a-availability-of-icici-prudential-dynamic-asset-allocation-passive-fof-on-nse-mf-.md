## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing members about the availability of ICICI Prudential Dynamic Asset Allocation Passive FOF on the NSE MF Invest Platform with effect from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes Reopened: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Effective Date: September 18, 2026.
- Eligible Schemes:
  - ICICI Prudential Dynamic Asset Allocation Passive FOF - Direct - Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A641, Category: Equity)
  - ICICI Prudential Dynamic Asset Allocation Passive FOF - Regular - Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A633, Category: Equity)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No specific compliance requirements mandated for trading members beyond standard platform operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors access to trade the mentioned ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund scheme through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
