## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities shortlisted under ESM shall attract a minimum 100% margin, shift to the trade-for-trade segment, or move between stages as specified in the annexures effective from August 31, 2026, and September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage I and attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 01, 2026 (on open positions as on August 31, 2026, and new positions created thereafter).
- Securities in Annexure II are eligible to move out from the framework.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 31, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures including 100% margins and migration to Trade-for-Trade settlement for specified scrips.
- Price band reinstatements for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (100%) for open and new positions in shortlisted securities.
- Trading members must take note of the shift in settlement segments and price band restrictions under Periodic Call Auction.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II price band under Periodic Call Auction: August 31, 2026.
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shift: September 01, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trading restrictions (Trade-for-Trade segment and Periodic Call Auction) for affected scrips, impacting liquidity and positioning for market participants.
