## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for Ambani Orgochem Limited and Dhansa Labs Limited on the SME EMERGE segment, effective August 26, 2026. These shares were allotted pursuant to the conversion of warrants issued on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- **Ambani Orgochem Limited**: 979,810 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 90) allotted on June 11, 2026. Symbol: AMBANIORGO, ISIN: INE00C501018.
- **Dhansa Labs Limited**: Multiple tranches of equity shares of Rs. 10/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 46) allotted pursuant to preferential warrant conversions. Symbol: DHANSA, ISIN: INE0M3I01029.
- Trading in these securities will commence with effect from August 26, 2026, under the SME EMERGE segment with specified market lots and lock-in expiry dates.

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes, market lots, and lock-in details specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: August 26, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**: June 11, 2026 (Ambani Orgochem); May 01, 2026 and May 12, 2026 (Dhansa Labs)

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action expanding the paid-up capital base for the respective SME listed entities through warrant conversions.
