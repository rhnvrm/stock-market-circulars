## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the benefit of early pay-in in the Commodity Derivatives Segment, referencing prior SEBI and NCL circulars.

## Key Points

- Members and clients providing early pay-in via the Inventory Management System (IMS) interface will be exempt from margins on open positions in relevant commodity derivative contracts.
- Effective date for the provisions is September 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Reaffirms and clarifies margin exemptions for early pay-in made through the Inventory Management System (IMS) interface in the Commodity Derivatives Segment, pursuant to SEBI circular ref. no. HO/47/16/13(4)2026-MRD-POD1/I/14266/2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must utilize the Inventory Management System (IMS) interface for early pay-in to avail margin exemptions on open positions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity and margin relief for market participants using early pay-in facilities in commodity derivatives.
