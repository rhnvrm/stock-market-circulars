## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions under the Income Distribution Cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) option in specific schemes of HSBC Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes: HSBC Nifty 50 Index Fund and HSBC Nifty Next 50 Index Fund (Direct and Regular Plans, including Reinvestment and Payout of IDCW).
- Restrictions: Suspension applies to lumpsum purchases, switch-ins, and fresh registrations of systematic transactions such as Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP)-in under the IDCW option.
- Exemptions: Existing SIPs and STPs registered prior to the effective date will continue to be processed normally. There are no restrictions on redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, or STP-outs.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must take note of the subscription restrictions for the IDCW option in the specified HSBC Mutual Fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026
- Notice Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors wishing to invest fresh capital in HSBC Nifty 50 Index Fund and HSBC Nifty Next 50 Index Fund using the IDCW option will be restricted, while existing systematic investments remain unaffected.
