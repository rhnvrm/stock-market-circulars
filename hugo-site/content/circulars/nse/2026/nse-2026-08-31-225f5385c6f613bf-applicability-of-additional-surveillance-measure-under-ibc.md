## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective from September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities have been shortlisted under ASM for Companies as per IBC based on criteria satisfaction effective September 01, 2026.
- Owais Metal And Mineral Processing Limited (Symbol: OWAIS, ISIN: INE0R8M01017) is shortlisted in Stage I under the ASM IBC framework.
- The ASM IBC framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under ASM IBC is purely for market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance measures under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code framework for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to note the list of securities under ASM IBC and ensure compliance with associated trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 31, 2026
- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in affected securities will be subject to additional surveillance measures and associated margins or trading checks as mandated by the exchange.
