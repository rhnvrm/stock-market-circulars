## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has notified the suspension of trading in privately placed Non-Convertible Securities (NCDs) issued by Power Finance Corporation Limited. The suspension will take effect on August 10, 2026, in view of the redemption of these securities.

## Key Points

- Trading of Power Finance Corporation Limited's NCDs under two specific ISINs will be suspended.
- The effective suspension date is August 10, 2026.
- The reason for suspension is redemption.
- The suspension is carried out pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced by this circular. This is an administrative suspension under the existing Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no specific compliance actions required for general members or investors, except noting the suspension of trading in these instruments.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Suspension Date:** August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a standard procedure for maturing privately placed debt securities, and does not affect the broader market or the trading of other securities of Power Finance Corporation Limited.
- **Affected Securities:**
  - Power Finance Corporation Limited (ISIN: INE134E08MT1)
  - Power Finance Corporation Limited (ISIN: INE134E08MS3)
