## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (BE/ST) w.e.f. August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (ALPHAGEO, UCAL, VCL) attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on August 19, 2026, and new positions created from August 20, 2026, and shift from EQ/SM to BE/ST series.
- Ushanti Colour Chem Limited (UCL) moves from ESM Stage - I to Stage - II w.e.f. August 19, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 19, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities and account for segment shifts (EQ/SM to BE/ST).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Margin and Segment Shift: August 20, 2026
- Stage II Price Band / Move Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for the affected securities will experience tightened controls in line with the ESM framework rules.
