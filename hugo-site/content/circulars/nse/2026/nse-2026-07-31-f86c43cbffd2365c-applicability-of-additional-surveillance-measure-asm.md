## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued circular ref no. 576/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/SURV/75517) regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. Based on the surveillance criteria, Sabar Flex India Limited (SABAR) has been shortlisted for inclusion under Long-Term ASM Stage-I. The circular details the surveillance actions, including a 100% margin requirement and trading segment shifts for qualifying securities.

## Key Points

- **Long-Term ASM Stage-I Shortlisting**: Sabar Flex India Limited (Symbol: SABAR, ISIN: INE0DZ101013) has satisfied the criteria for inclusion under Long-Term ASM Stage-I.
- **Margin Requirements**: An applicable margin rate of 100% will be applied to all open positions in shortlisted securities.
- **Trade-for-Trade Segment Shift**: Securities qualifying under criteria VII (shifting to Stage IV) will move from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE).
- **Surveillance Actions Nature**: Shortlisting under ASM is purely for market surveillance purposes and does not represent an adverse action against the listed entities.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no permanent changes to the regulatory framework. Rather, this is an application of the existing Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework established under previous exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Collection**: Trading members must collect and maintain a 100% margin on all open positions of SABAR starting August 05, 2026.
- **System Updates**: Members should update their risk management systems to reflect the increased margin requirements and ensure compliance with settlement changes.

## Important Dates

- **ASM Stage-I Effective Date (Inclusion)**: August 03, 2026 (for SABAR under Long-Term ASM Stage-I).
- **100% Margin Rate Effective Date**: August 05, 2026 (on all open positions as of August 04, 2026, and new positions created thereafter).
- **Trade-for-Trade Segment (Series BE) Shift**: August 05, 2026 (for any securities qualifying under Stage IV Criteria VII).

## Impact Assessment

- **Sabar Flex India Limited (SABAR)**: Traders of SABAR will face increased capital requirements due to the 100% margin mandate, which may temporarily reduce trading volumes and liquidity for the stock.
- **Market Participants**: Risk management systems of brokerages must be updated to avoid margin shortfall penalties on clients' open positions.
