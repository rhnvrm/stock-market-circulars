## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for various securities based on market surveillance criteria effective August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I have been announced.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. August 26, 2026.
- Margin applies to all open positions as on August 25, 2026, and new positions created from August 26, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I provisions on listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure applicable margin rates (50% up to 100% cap) are maintained for open and new positions in the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: August 25, 2026
- Circular Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for specified securities may affect liquidity and trading volumes for the listed scrips in ST-ASM Stage I.
