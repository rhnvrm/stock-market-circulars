## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for shortlisted securities effective from August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Stage-I include BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Limited, Kaya Limited, Maha Rashtra Apex Corporation Limited, and Vadivarhe Speciality Chemicals Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. August 18, 2026, on all open positions as on August 17, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Action is purely on account of market surveillance and does not indicate an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long-Term ASM framework provisions for specified scrips.
- 100% margin requirement on open and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure 100% margin is maintained for open positions and new positions created in the affected scrips starting August 18, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Action / Margins: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified securities will face stricter margin norms, potentially impacting liquidity and short-term volatility.
