## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment from the BCP/DR site on Saturday, August 22, 2026. The mock session will be followed by a re-login session from the primary site to ensure seamless operations for Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Date of Mock Trading:** Saturday, August 22, 2026.
- **BCP Site Timings:** Normal Market open at 09:00 hrs, close at 17:00 hrs; trade modification end time at 17:30 hrs.
- **Primary Site Re-Login Timings:** Live re-login start time at 18:30 hrs, close time at 19:00 hrs.
- **Connectivity:** No changes in NEAT Adapter settings are required; existing settings as of Friday, August 21, 2026, will be retained.
- **Order Management:** All outstanding orders will be purged before trading starts from the BCP site. Members using NNF software must manually clear outstanding orders.
- **Multicast TBT:** Sequence numbers will start from '1' once trading commences from the BCP site.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades executed during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational mock trading circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff are used.
- Members using NNF software must clear session 1 outstanding orders before trading from the BCP site.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** August 22, 2026
- **Live Re-Login Date:** August 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct impact on live trading. Helps members test Disaster Recovery (DR) and Business Continuity Plan (BCP) readiness without financial implications.
