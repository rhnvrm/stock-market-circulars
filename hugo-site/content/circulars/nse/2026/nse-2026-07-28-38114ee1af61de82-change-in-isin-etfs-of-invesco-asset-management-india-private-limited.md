## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular informing members about a change in the International Securities Identification Number (ISIN) for the units of Invesco India Nifty 50 Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) managed by Invesco Asset Management (India) Private Limited. The change is due to a sub-division (split) of the units' face value from Rs. 10 to Re. 1.

## Key Points

- **Affected ETF**: Invesco India Nifty 50 Exchange Traded Fund (Symbol: IVZINNIFTY).
- **Nature of Change**: Sub-division of unit face value from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-.
- **New ISIN**: The units will transition to the new ISIN INF205KA1CC7.
- **Effective Date**: The change will take effect for all trades executed on or after July 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No major regulatory changes; this is a standard stock split/sub-division and ISIN update processed in accordance with NSE listing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading and Clearing Members**: Ensure trading and back-office systems are updated with the new ISIN INF205KA1CC7 for symbol IVZINNIFTY prior to July 31, 2026.
- **Investors**: No direct action is required as the conversion of units under the new ISIN will be automatically handled by the depository.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 28, 2026
- **Ex-Date / Effective Date**: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. The sub-division is a standard corporate action that adjusts the unit price proportionally, potentially increasing trading liquidity.
- **Operational Impact**: Back-office and trading systems must transition to the new ISIN on the ex-date to avoid trading and settlement issues.
