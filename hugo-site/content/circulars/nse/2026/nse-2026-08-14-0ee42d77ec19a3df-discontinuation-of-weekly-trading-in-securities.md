## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the discontinuation of weekly trading in securities for Debock Industries Limited and Future Consumer Limited. The mandated six months of trading on the first trading day of every week on a 'Trade for Trade' basis (Series "BZ") will be completed on September 02, 2026, leading to the discontinuation effective September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous exchange circulars regarding suspension of trading for non-compliance with Regulation 31 (Submission of Shareholding pattern) of SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015.
- Mandated six months of weekly trading on 'Trade for Trade' basis concludes on September 02, 2026.
- Weekly trading in securities of Debock Industries Limited and Future Consumer Limited on 'Trade for Trade' basis (Series "BZ") is discontinued.

## Regulatory Changes

- Execution in accordance with SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated July 11, 2023, last updated January 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities of the specified companies will have weekly trading discontinued under the specified guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026 (closing hour of trading on August 31, 2026).
- Completion of 6 months: September 02, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Directly impacts trading operations and market accessibility for shares of Debock Industries Limited (DIL) and Future Consumer Limited (FCONSUMER).
