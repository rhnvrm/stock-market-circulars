## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding SEBI directions pursuant to an Hon'ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated September 7, 2026, in the matter of Vedprakash Devkinandan Chiripal and Savitridevi V. Chiripal. The SAT granted a stay on the impugned SEBI restraint order subject to the deposit of the full penalty amount, which has now been paid by the appellants.

## Key Points

- Reference to previous NSE Circular No. NSE/INVG/74958 dated June 30, 2026, regarding SEBI restraint order against Vedprakash Devkinandan Chiripal and Savitridevi V. Chiripal.
- Hon'ble SAT vide order dated September 7, 2026 (Appeal No. 281 of 2026 and Misc. Application No. 826 of 2026), stayed the impugned SEBI order subject to deposit of full penalty.
- SEBI confirmed via email dated September 30, 2026, that the appellants paid the penalty amount in favour of SEBI.
- Members are advised to comply with the SAT order and note the updated list of debarred entities on the NSE website.

## Regulatory Changes

- Stay of SEBI restraint order against Vedprakash Devkinandan Chiripal (PAN: AAHPC2102Q) and Savitridevi V. Chiripal (PAN: AAQPA0515Q) upon deposit of full penalty.

## Compliance Requirements

- NSE members are advised to take note of the SAT stay order and SEBI's confirmation of penalty deposit.
- Refer to the consolidated list of debarred entities available on the NSE website for operational updates.

## Important Dates

- June 30, 2026: Date of original SEBI restraint order and NSE Circular No. NSE/INVG/74958.
- September 7, 2026: Hon'ble SAT order granting stay of the impugned order subject to penalty deposit.
- September 30, 2026: SEBI confirmation of penalty payment and issuance of current circular.

## Impact Assessment

- Relieves the restrained entities from market access restrictions following compliance with the SAT penalty deposit condition, updating member compliance records.
