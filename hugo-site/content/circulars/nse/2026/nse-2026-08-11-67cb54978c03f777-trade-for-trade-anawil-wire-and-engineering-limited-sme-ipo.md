## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular announcing the transition of Anawil Wire and Engineering Limited (SME IPO) from the trade-for-trade segment to the rolling segment.

## Key Points

- **Company Name**: Anawil Wire and Engineering Limited
- **Symbol**: ANAWIL
- **Segment Transition**: From Trade for Trade (Series: ST) to Rolling Segment (Series: SM).
- **Effective Date**: August 24, 2026.
- **Regulatory Reference**: SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of securities from trade for trade segment (Series: ST) to rolling segment (Series: SM) pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to update their systems to reflect the change in series from ST to SM with effect from August 24, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **August 11, 2026**: Date of the NSE circular.
- **August 24, 2026**: Effective date for the transition to the rolling segment.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity**: Transitioning to the rolling segment (Series: SM) from the trade-for-trade segment (Series: ST) is expected to increase trading flexibility and liquidity for ANAWIL shares, as it allows intraday trading and netting.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading systems must be updated to support the series change from ST to SM on the effective date.
