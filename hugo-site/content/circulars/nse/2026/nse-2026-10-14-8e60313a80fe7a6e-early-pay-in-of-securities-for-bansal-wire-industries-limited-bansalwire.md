## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued guidelines for early pay-in of securities for Bansal Wire Industries Limited (BANSALWIRE) on account of a face value split (sub-division) from Rs 5/- per share to Re 1/- per share.

## Key Points

- **Security:** Bansal Wire Industries Limited
- **Symbol:** BANSALWIRE
- **Old ISIN:** INE0B9K01025
- **Corporate Action:** Face Value Split from Rs 5/- per share to Re 1/- per share
- **Record Date & Ex-Date:** October 16, 2026
- **Settlement Numbers:** 2026196 and 2026197 (EPI dates: 16-10-2026 and 19-10-2026)

## Regulatory Changes

- Early pay-in must be executed in the old/existing ISIN without adjusting for conversion for the specified settlement numbers and trade dates.
- Post record date, pay-in/early pay-in instructions must be executed in the new ISIN in depositories.

## Compliance Requirements

- For early pay-in through block mechanism, client-wise early pay-in allocation file should not be uploaded.
- Custodians providing early pay-in through pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details as per the actual traded quantity.

## Important Dates

- **Ex-Date / Record Date:** October 16, 2026
- **EPI Dates:** October 16, 2026 and October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides procedural clarity for market participants and custodians to ensure seamless clearing and settlement during the stock split corporate action.
