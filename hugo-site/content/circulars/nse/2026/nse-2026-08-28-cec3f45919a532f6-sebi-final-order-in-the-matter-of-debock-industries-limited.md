## Summary

SEBI issued a final order regarding Debock Industries Limited and associated noticees following an investigation that revealed serious violations including migration to the main board through fictitious preferential issues, inflation of sales and purchases, and submission of false bank statements.

## Key Points

- Investigation by SEBI uncovered fictitious preferential issues of warrants and subsequent conversion to equity shares used to meet migration eligibility criteria.
- Substantial inflation of sales (approx. 72% in FY22 and 77% in FY23) and purchases (approx. 94% in both years) through circuitous transactions.
- Submission of fabricated bank statements to SEBI during the preliminary examination.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of directions under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1) and 11B(2) of the SEBI Act, 1992 against the company and noticees.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees must comply with the directions and penalties specified in the WTM final order.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: 2026-27 (referencing SCN dated September 15, 2025 and Interim Order dated August 23, 2024).

## Impact Assessment

- High regulatory and enforcement impact on the company and involved noticees, ensuring market integrity and penalizing fraudulent capital raising and financial misstatement.
