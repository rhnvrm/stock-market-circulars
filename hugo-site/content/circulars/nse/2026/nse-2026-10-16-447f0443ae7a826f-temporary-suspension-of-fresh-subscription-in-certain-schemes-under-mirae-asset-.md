## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions in certain schemes under Mirae Asset Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from October 16, 2026, pursuant to SEBI advisory.

## Key Points

- Affected schemes include Mirae Asset Gold ETF Fund of Fund and Mirae Asset Gold Silver Passive FoF.
- Suspended transactions include lumpsum investments, switch-ins, and fresh systematic registrations under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP).
- The restriction applies to applications received post cut-off time of 15:00 hours on October 15, 2026.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and existing registered SIP/STP transactions remain unaffected and will continue to be processed normally.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI advisory, temporary suspension of subscriptions has been implemented for specific gold/silver passive and fund of fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the suspension of fresh subscriptions and ensure no new lumpsum or fresh systematic registrations are submitted for the affected Mirae Asset schemes until further notice.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026 (applicable for applications received post cut-off time of 15:00 hours on October 15, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Investors looking to make fresh investments or new SIP/STP registrations in Mirae Asset Gold ETF Fund of Fund and Mirae Asset Gold Silver Passive FoF will be temporarily restricted, while existing investments and redemptions remain operational.
