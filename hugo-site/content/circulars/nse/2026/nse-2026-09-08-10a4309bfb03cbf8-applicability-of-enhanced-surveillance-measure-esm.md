## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The circular provides lists of securities shortlisted under ESM Stage I, securities moving between stages, and the consolidated list of securities under the ESM framework effective from September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in ESM Stage - I w.e.f. September 09, 2026 include Jay Jalaram Technologies Limited (KORE), Menon Pistons Limited (MENNPIS), and Total Transport Systems Limited (TOTAL), with trading moving from EQ/SM to BE/ST w.e.f. September 10, 2026.
- Minal Industries Limited (MINALIND) is shortlisted to move from ESM Stage - I to Stage - II w.e.f. September 09, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under ESM Stage II is provided, comprising multiple companies subject to enhanced surveillance margins and trading checks.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework to monitor price volatility and market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance measures, applicable margin requirements, and trading restrictions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 09, 2026
- Trading shift to BE/ST effective date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trading restrictions apply to the affected securities, potentially impacting their liquidity and trading volume.
