## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for certain securities satisfying the inclusion criteria, effective October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I w.e.f. October 08, 2026: DPWIRES, IDENTICAL, JAYKAY, KRISHNADEF, MYMUDRA, QUINTEGRA, RNPL, and VIGOR.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100%.
- The surveillance actions are effective from October 09, 2026, on all open positions as on October 08, 2026, and new positions created from October 09, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I on listed securities meeting specific exchange surveillance criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with the increased margin requirements of 50% (capped at 100%) on open positions as of October 08, 2026, and all new positions created from October 09, 2026, for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 07, 2026
- ST-ASM Stage I Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Margin Applicability Date: October 09, 2026 (on open positions as on October 08, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in the shortlisted securities will experience higher capital/margin blockage due to the 50% minimum margin requirement, which may impact short-term liquidity and trading volumes in these counters.
