## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the revision of Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) and position limits applicable for Participants, Institutional clients, and Non-Institutional clients for the symbol MOLDTKPAC under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme, effective from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: MOLDTKPAC
- Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) revised from 2,219,272 to 4,438,544 shares.
- Institutional Client Limit revised from 221,927 to 443,854 shares.
- Participant Limit revised from 221,927 to 443,854 shares.
- Non-Institutional Client Limit revised from 22,192 to 44,385 shares.
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The position limits in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme for MOLDTKPAC have been revised in pursuance of earlier circulars (NCL/CMPT/73999 and NCL/CMPT/76656).

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians must take note of the revised position limits and ensure compliance while executing transactions in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme for MOLDTKPAC.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The revision adjusts participation limits in Securities Lending and Borrowing for MOLDTKPAC, impacting participants, institutional, and non-institutional clients trading in the scrip.
