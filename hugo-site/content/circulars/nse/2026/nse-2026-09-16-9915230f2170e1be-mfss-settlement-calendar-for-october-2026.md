## Summary

NSE has announced the MFSS settlement calendar for October 2026, detailing the schedule for normal subscription (T+1) and liquid subscription settlement dates, order dates, fund pay-in/payouts, and unit pay-in/payouts.

## Key Points

- Outlines settlement schedule for normal subscription (Settlement Type 'S') with T+1 cycle throughout October 2026.
- Outlines settlement schedule for liquid subscription (Settlement Type 'K') with same-day unit settlement throughout October 2026.
- Covers settlement numbers starting from 2026185 to 2026204.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational schedule circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, trading members, and mutual fund distributors must align their order placement, fund pay-in, and payout operations according to the specified dates for October 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective period: October 1, 2026, to November 2, 2026 (spanning October 2026 settlement cycles).

## Impact Assessment

Ensures seamless operational planning and clearing/settlement execution for mutual fund transactions on the NSE MFSS platform for October 2026.
