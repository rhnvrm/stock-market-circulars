## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the change in constituents of the Nifty FPI 150 index, based on a press release by NSE Indices Limited.

## Key Points

- Index affected: Nifty FPI 150.
- Exclusion: Swiggy Ltd. (Symbol: SWIGGY).
- Inclusion: Union Bank of India (Symbol: UNIONBANK).
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026 (based on close of September 10, 2026).
