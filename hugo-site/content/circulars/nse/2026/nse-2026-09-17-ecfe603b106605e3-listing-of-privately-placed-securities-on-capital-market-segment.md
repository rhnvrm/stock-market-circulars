## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on its capital market segment, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment with effect from September 18, 2026.
- Trading shall be identified by designated security codes and specific lot sizes as specified in the annexure.
- Covered issuers include LIC Housing Finance Limited, NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited, and REC Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure trading in the specified securities adheres to the designated codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026
- Circular Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational listing update with minimal direct impact on broader market liquidity.
