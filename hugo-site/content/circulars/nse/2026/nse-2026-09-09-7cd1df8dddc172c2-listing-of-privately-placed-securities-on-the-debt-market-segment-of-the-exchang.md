## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Privately placed commercial papers from various companies have been approved for listing and trading.
- Effective date of admission: September 09, 2026.
- Issuing companies include Aditya Birla Money Limited, CEAT Limited, Indian Oil Corporation Limited, UltraTech Cement Limited, Angel One Limited, Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, and IIFL Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and issuers must ensure adherence to the Debt Market Segment trading regulations and settlement schedules.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 08-Sep-2026
- Listing/Effective Date: 09-Sep-2026
- Redemption Dates ranging from October 2026 to January 2027.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational listing update with no direct broad market volatility impact.
