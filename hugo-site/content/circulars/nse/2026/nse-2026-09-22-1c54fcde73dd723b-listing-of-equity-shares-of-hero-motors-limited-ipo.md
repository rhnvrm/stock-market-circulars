## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Hero Motors Limited under an Initial Public Offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Hero Motors Limited
- Symbol: HEROMOTORS
- ISIN: INE012G01022
- The actual date of listing will be communicated through a separate circular.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Exchange members are informed of the security details for forthcoming trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 22, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants to prepare systems and records for the trading and settlement of Hero Motors Limited shares upon listing.
