## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has approved the request of Gyandeep Stocks Private Limited for the segmental surrender of its membership in the Currency Derivatives and IRF segments.

## Key Points

- **Trading Member Name:** Gyandeep Stocks Private Limited
- **SEBI Registration Number:** INZ000001230
- **Surrendered Segments:** Currency Derivatives & IRF
- **Surrender Approval Date:** 06-Oct-2026
- **Download Ref. No:** NSE/COMP/76800
- **Circular Ref. No:** 91/2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this represents a standard administrative processing of a member's request to surrender membership in specific segments.

## Compliance Requirements

All members are requested to take note of the discontinuation of trading privileges for the mentioned entity in the surrendered segments.

## Important Dates

- **Surrender Approval Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Circular Issuance Date:** October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Negligible market impact, as this is an isolated administrative update regarding a single trading member's segmental exit.
