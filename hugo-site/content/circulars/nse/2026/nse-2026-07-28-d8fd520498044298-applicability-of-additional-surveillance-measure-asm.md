## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Based on market surveillance criteria, certain securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) framework.

Three securities have been shortlisted for Stage-I of the framework, effective from July 29, 2026. The applicable surveillance actions include a 100% margin rate on all open positions starting July 31, 2026. The exchange clarified that ASM is a surveillance measure and does not imply any adverse action against the shortlisted companies.

## Key Points

- **Long-Term ASM Stage-I Shortlist:** Sheetal Cool Products Limited, Sikko Industries Limited, and Tatva Chintan Pharma Chem Limited (moved from STASM to LTASM) are shortlisted effective July 29, 2026.
- **100% Margin Requirement:** A 100% margin rate will apply to all open positions in the shortlisted securities as of July 30, 2026, and all new positions created from July 31, 2026 onwards.
- **Trade-for-Trade Segment Shifting:** Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Stage IV) will shift from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade (Series: BE) starting July 31, 2026. No securities were shortlisted for Stage IV in this update.
- **No Adverse Action:** Shortlisting under ASM is purely for market surveillance purposes.

## Regulatory Changes

No fundamental policy changes are introduced, but the surveillance framework is applied to newly shortlisted securities in accordance with existing Long-Term ASM guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Collection:** Trading members must collect and maintain a 100% margin rate on all open and new positions for SCPL, SIKKO, and TATVA starting July 31, 2026.
- **System Updates:** Trading terminals and risk management systems must be updated to implement the 100% margin restriction and any segment shifts (if applicable under Criteria VII).

## Important Dates

- **Inclusion Effective Date:** July 29, 2026
- **100% Margin Effective Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Rolling to Trade-for-Trade Shift Date (for Stage IV, if applicable):** July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Shortlisted stocks (SCPL, SIKKO, TATVA) may experience reduced volatility but also lower trading volume and liquidity due to the removal of leverage (100% margin requirement).
- **Operational Impact:** Brokerages must configure their automated risk systems to restrict leverage and enforce the 100% margin requirement on the affected stocks.
