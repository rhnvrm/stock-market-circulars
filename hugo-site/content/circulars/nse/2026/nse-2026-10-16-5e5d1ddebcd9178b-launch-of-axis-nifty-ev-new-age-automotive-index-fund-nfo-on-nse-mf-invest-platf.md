## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the Axis Nifty EV & New Age Automotive Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from October 16, 2026, to October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Names: Axis Nifty EV & New Age Automotive Index Fund - Direct Growth and Regular Growth.
- ISINs: INF846KA1630 (Direct Growth), INF846KA1622 (Regular Growth).
- Asset Category: Equity.
- NFO Period: October 16, 2026 to October 30, 2026 (till 11:59 PM).
- Allotment Date: November 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 05, 2026, till 10:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: October 16, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 30, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- Clear Funds Submission Deadline: November 05, 2026 (10:00 PM)
- Allotment Date: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification enabling market participants and investors to transact in the new mutual fund scheme via the NSE MF platform.
