## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for specified securities effective September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- Applicable rate of margin for ST-ASM Stage I is 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Applicable rate of margin for ST-ASM Stage II is 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Surveillance actions apply to all open positions as on September 24, 2026, and new positions created from September 25, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure framework in conjunction with existing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the revised margin requirements (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective September 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026
- Open positions cut-off: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for listed securities under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected stocks.
