## Summary

On July 29, 2026, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) issued a circular (Ref No: NSE/CML/75448) announcing the listing and commencement of trading of the equity shares of **Lohia Corp Limited** (Symbol: LCL) post its Initial Public Offering (IPO). A total of 10,56,50,000 equity shares of face value Re. 1/- each will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment of NSE, effective from **July 30, 2026**. Additionally, the company's post-issue Shareholding Pattern under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 has been submitted.

## Key Points

- **Security Details**: Equity shares of Lohia Corp Limited (LCL), EQ series, fully paid up with ISIN `INE0QJW01029`.
- **Trading Commencement**: Dealing in the shares will begin on Thursday, July 30, 2026.
- **Issue Price**: Rs. 425/- per equity share for the public issue.
- **Total Listed Capital**: 10,56,50,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each, totaling a paid-up capital of Rs. 10,56,50,000. Note that distinctive numbers 1 to 100,000 were cancelled pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement.
- **Pre-Open Session**: The security will participate in the pre-open session for IPO scrips on July 30, 2026, as per SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.
- **Lock-in Details**: Of the 10,56,50,000 shares:
  - 5,789,560 shares are locked in until August 26, 2026.
  - 5,789,573 shares are locked in until October 25, 2026.
  - 1,647,000 shares are locked in until January 21, 2027.
  - 56,939,480 shares are locked in until January 30, 2027.
  - 21,132,113 shares are locked in until January 30, 2028.
  - 14,352,274 shares are completely free from lock-in (Free).
- **Shareholding Pattern**: Submitted under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, depicting the post-issue shareholding configuration as of July 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework changes are introduced. This circular is an operational listing notification in pursuance of:
- **NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A (Regulation 3.1.1)** for admitting securities to dealings.
- **NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A (Regulation 2.5.5)** regarding designated market lot sizes.
- **SEBI Circulars CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012** dated January 20, 2012, governing pre-open sessions for IPO and other categories of scrips.
- **SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 (Regulation 31)** governing the disclosure of shareholding patterns.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members**: Ensure compliance with the pre-open trading session rules and lot-size specifications (market lot is 1 share) for Symbol LCL.
- **For the Issuer (Lohia Corp Limited)**: Continual compliance with NSE Listing Regulations, including timely corporate disclosures, and managing lock-in releases in coordination with the registrar and depositories.

## Important Dates

- **July 29, 2026**: Date of NSE Listing Circular and date of post-issue shareholding pattern report.
- **July 30, 2026**: Effective listing date and commencement of trading of LCL equity shares on NSE Capital Market segment.
- **August 26, 2026**: Release of lock-in for 5,789,560 shares (distinctive numbers 100001 to 5889560).
- **October 25, 2026**: Release of lock-in for 5,789,573 shares (distinctive numbers 5889561 to 11679133).
- **January 21, 2027**: Release of lock-in for 1,647,000 shares (distinctive numbers 82970888 to 84617887).
- **January 30, 2027**: Release of lock-in for 56,939,480 shares (distinctive numbers 26031408 to 82970887).
- **January 30, 2028**: Release of lock-in for 21,132,113 shares (distinctive numbers 84617888 to 105750000).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Medium. The listing of Lohia Corp Limited marks the entry of a prominent entity in the industrial packaging/machinery sector with an issue size of over Rs. 4,400 crores at Rs. 425 per share.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Trading members can trade Symbol LCL under Series EQ with normal market lot 1 from July 30, 2026. The stock will undergo the standard IPO pre-open session.
- **Liquidity Impact**: High after lock-in releases. Initially, only 14,352,274 shares are free (approx 13.58% of total shares). The remainder of the capital has phased lock-in releases spanning from August 2026 to January 2028, which will progressively increase the free float and market liquidity of the stock.
