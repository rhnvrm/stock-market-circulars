## Summary

NSE has issued a circular regarding the submission of the Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026. Trading Members (TM) and Clearing Members (CM) must comply with minimum networth requirements set by SEBI under the SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 2026.

## Key Points

- Base Networth requirement is ₹1,00,00,000/- for members in Capital Market, F&O, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivative, and Debt segments.
- Variable networth requirement is 10% of the average daily cash balance of clients retained across segments/exchanges in the previous 6 months, as per NSE circular NSE/COMP/72672.
- Certificates must adhere to Format C-1 for Corporates, Firms, Individuals, and Banks, complete with UDIN and CA/CS partner details.
- Networth of Separate Business Units in GIFT-IFSC must be segregated in accordance with SEBI Circular dated May 02, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance aligned with SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 2026.
- Mandatory declarations confirming no conflict of interest and non-related party status of certifying professionals.

## Compliance Requirements

- TMs and CMs must verify and submit their computed networth and variable networth figures as of September 30, 2026.
- Submission must include all required annexures, PAN details, and valid UDIN.

## Important Dates

- Networth evaluation date: September 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures financial resilience and transparency across trading and clearing member operations.
