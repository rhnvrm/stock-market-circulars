## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) segment on Saturday, August 29, 2026. No new software version release is scheduled for this session.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** August 29, 2026.
- **Trading Session-1 (Primary Site):** Normal Market open time at 13:15 hrs, Normal Market close time at 15:45 hrs.
- **Live Re-login Timings:** Start time at 18:30 hrs, close time at 19:00 hrs.
- **NEAT Version:** NEAT 1.3.3.
- **Order Purge:** All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session. Members using NNF software must manually clear orders in their systems.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- **UCC/PAN Validity:** Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Conducted as per existing regulatory requirements and standard exchange mock procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, August 31, 2026.
- Ensure successful telnet connectivity and verify Box ID setups and NNF application parameters.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Date:** August 29, 2026
- **Live Re-login:** August 29, 2026 (18:30 hrs to 19:00 hrs)
- **Live Trading Resume:** August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness check for members trading in the SLBM segment to ensure seamless connectivity and system stability.
