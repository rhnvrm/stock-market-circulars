## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100%.
- Effective date for the surveillance action is August 20, 2026, applicable on all open positions as on August 19, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Inclusion under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I framework provisions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure appropriate margin collection (50% or existing, capped at 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities starting August 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 18, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: August 20, 2026 (on open positions as on August 19, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for specified securities may impact short-term liquidity and trading volumes for the affected stocks.
