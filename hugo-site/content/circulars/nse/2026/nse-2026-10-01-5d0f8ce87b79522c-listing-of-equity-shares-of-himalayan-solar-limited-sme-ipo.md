## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Himalayan Solar Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform effective from October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: HIMALAYAN
- ISIN: INE1B7I01014
- Issue Price: Rs. 103/- per share
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Total Number of Securities: 22,109,699 equity shares
- Market Lot: Minimum 1200 Equity Shares
- Trading Segment: Series 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment – Settlement Type 'W') initially, shifting to Series 'SM' (Normal Rolling Segment) as per applicable SEBI circulars.
- Market Maker: Anant Securities

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the securities will be conducted in accordance with NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations and SEBI circulars regarding pre-open sessions for IPOs and continuous market trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must note the designated security codes, lot sizes, pre-open session guidelines, and lock-in details specified in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 05, 2026
- Lock-in periods vary across tranches up to October 02, 2029.

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and market makers to trade equity shares of Himalayan Solar Limited on the NSE EMERGE SME platform with specific surveillance and settlement parameters.
