## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of equity shares of Adroit Industries (India) Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ) pursuant to Exchange circular reference and SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Security Symbol: ADROITIND
- Company Name: Adroit Industries (India) Limited
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade (Series: BE) to Rolling Segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are shifted from trade-for-trade settlement (where compulsory delivery of shares is required on a trade-by-trade basis without netting off) to the normal rolling settlement segment following applicable SEBI and exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must update their trading and back-office systems to reflect the change in series from BE to EQ for ADROITIND effective October 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 30, 2026
- Reference Circular: NSE/CML/76616 dated September 29, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates normal liquidity and rolling settlement for Adroit Industries (India) Limited shares post-IPO trade-for-trade surveillance period.
