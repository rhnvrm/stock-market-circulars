## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited issued a revised list of approved securities, GOI securities, open-ended mutual funds, corporate bonds, and banks empaneled for issuing Bank Guarantees (BGs) and Fixed Deposit Receipts (FDRs), effective for October 2026.

## Key Points

- Supersedes previous circular no. 100/2026 dated August 21, 2026.
- Details multiple annexures covering equity shares, ETFs, Sovereign Gold Bonds, G-Secs/T-Bills, open-ended mutual funds, corporate bonds, and empaneled banks with applicable market-wide limits and haircut rates.
- G-Sec/T Bills have a shut period of two days; members must release them at least two days before maturity.
- G-Secs provided as collaterals by member-banks must not be reckoned for SLR purposes or used for trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated liquid asset components and haircut rates for the upcoming month of October 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and custodians must review the revised limits, haircut rates, and permitted quantities across all annexures.
- Members should ensure timely release of G-Sec/T Bills prior to their maturity (two-day shut period).

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 2026
- **Circular Date:** September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects risk management, collateral valuation, and margin calculations for clearing members and custodians trading in the Futures and Options segment.
