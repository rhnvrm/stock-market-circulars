## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing trading members about the Gazette Notification No. F. No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/323 dated October 08, 2026, issued by SEBI regarding the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026.

## Key Points

- SEBI issued the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026.
- Introduces Chapter III-D containing the Common Advertisement Code (सामान्य विज्ञापन संहिता).
- Provides formal definitions for advertisements across print, broadcast, digital, and outdoor media, as well as definitions for celebrities and influencers.

## Regulatory Changes

- Insertion of Chapter III-D regarding the Common Advertisement Code (सामान्य विज्ञापन संहिता) under SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.
- Detailed classification of advertisements and promotion standards for regulated entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and regulated entities must take note of the amendment regulations and ensure adherence to the new Common Advertisement Code provisions.

## Important Dates

- Notification Date: October 08, 2026 / October 09, 2026
- Circular Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective Date: As specified in the Gazette notification (180 days from publication in the Official Gazette).

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances regulatory oversight on marketing, advertising, and promotional campaigns by intermediaries and regulated entities.
