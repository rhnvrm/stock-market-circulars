## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Debt Market Segment
- **Effective Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Issuing Entities Include:** Tata Capital Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, SRF Limited, Hdfc Securities Limited, Axis Securities Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited, and Tata Projects Limited.
- **Security Type:** Commercial Papers (CP) with various tenures and ISINs.

None. Routine listing under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard compliance requirements for debt securities admitted to dealings on the Exchange apply.

## Important Dates

- **Listing/Effective Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Between October 30, 2026, and February 12, 2027
