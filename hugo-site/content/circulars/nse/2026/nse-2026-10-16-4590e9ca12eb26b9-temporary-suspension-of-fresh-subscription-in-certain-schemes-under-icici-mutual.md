## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, lumpsum investments (including switches), and fresh registrations of Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) in ICICI Prudential Gold ETF FOF on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh subscriptions, lumpsum investments, and switches in ICICI Prudential Gold ETF FOF are disabled with immediate effect.
- Fresh registrations of SIP and STP (as Target scheme) under special products/features are discontinued.
- Existing installments of SIP, STP, and Transfer-in of IDCW registered prior to the effective date will continue to be processed without change.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), Switch-out, and STP-out remain unrestricted.
- Top-ups in existing SIP/STP are not allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of subscription restrictions on ICICI Prudential Gold ETF FOF as per the notice-cum-addendum issued by ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the discontinuation of fresh inflows and new SIP/STP registrations in the specified scheme.
- Existing running SIPs and SWPs will continue as per prior registration terms.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Notice Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors wishing to make fresh investments in ICICI Prudential Gold ETF FOF via NSE MF Invest Platform cannot do so until further notice.
- Existing investors holding units or active SIPs/STPs are unaffected for their ongoing commitments, though top-ups are restricted.
