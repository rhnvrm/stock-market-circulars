## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Fascinate Textiles Limited (SME IPO) on the EMERGE SME Platform effective from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: FASCINATE
- Company Name: Fascinate Textiles Limited
- ISIN: INE1Q2D01019
- Issue Price: Rs. 151/- per share
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- No. of Securities: 13,760,893
- Market Lot: Minimum 800 Equity Shares
- Series: ST (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment – Settlement Type W), subsequently shifting to Series SM (Normal Rolling Segment – Settlement Type N)
- Effective Date: August 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admission to dealings on the NSE EMERGE SME Platform pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the security shall be in accordance with the specified lot sizes and pre-open session guidelines as per SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Fascinate Textiles Limited equity shares on the NSE SME platform under Series ST.
