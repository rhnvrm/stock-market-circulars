## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the monthly settlement schedule for Mark to Market (MTM) settlement in the Currency Derivatives Segment for September 2026. The circular provides trade date-to-settlement date mapping along with specific final settlement dates for currency derivatives, cash-settled interest rate contracts, 91-Day GOI T-Bill futures, and overnight call rate (MIBOR) contracts.

## Key Points

- Detailed trade-by-trade settlement calendar for September 2026.
- Outlines final settlement dates for September 2026 Cash Settled Interest Rate Contracts.
- Details final settlement dates for September 2026 Currency Futures and Options Contracts.
- Highlights final settlement dates for 91-Day GOI T-Bill Futures and Overnight Call Rate (MIBOR) Contracts.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced in this circular. It is a standard operational release conforming to established clearing house schedules.

## Compliance Requirements

- All clearing members of the Currency Derivatives Segment must ensure timely settlement according to the scheduled timelines.
- Members should configure their operational workflows to align with the specified settlement cycle, particularly on final settlement dates.

## Important Dates

- **Settlement Schedule Period:** Trade dates from September 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026, with settlements running from September 02, 2026, to October 01, 2026.
- **Final Settlement for Cash Settled Interest Rate Contracts (September 2026):** September 25, 2026.
- **Final Settlement for Currency Futures & Options Contracts (September 2026):** September 30, 2026.
- **Final Settlement for 91-Day GOI T-Bill Futures & Overnight Call Rate (MIBOR) Contracts (September 2026):** October 01, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low. This is a routine operational schedule. It enables participants to manage liquidity and back-office settlement processes smoothly without any expected disruptions.
