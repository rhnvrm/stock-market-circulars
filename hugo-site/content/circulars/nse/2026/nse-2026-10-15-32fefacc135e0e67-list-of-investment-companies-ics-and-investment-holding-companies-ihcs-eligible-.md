## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has released a circular detailing the list of Investment Companies (ICs) and Investment Holding Companies (IHCs) eligible for a special call-auction mechanism for price discovery, pursuant to SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD3/P/CIR/2024/86 dated June 20, 2024. The special call-auction is scheduled to be held on October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Special call-auction mechanism scheduled for October 30, 2026, for eligible ICs and IHCs.
- If the call auction for a security is not successful on day 1, it shall continue on the next day and subsequent days until price discovery is achieved.
- Covers eight listed entities including GFL Limited, Kalyani Investment Company Limited, Maharashtra Scooters Limited, Nalwa Sons Investments Limited, Pilani Investment and Industries Corporation Limited, SIL Investments Limited, TVS Holdings Limited, and Williamson Magor & Company Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of special call-auction price discovery mechanism for eligible Investment Companies and Investment Holding Companies as per SEBI framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the eligible securities and adhere to the special call-auction operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Special Call-Auction Date: October 30, 2026 (continuing on subsequent days if unsuccessful on day 1).

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates efficient price discovery for eligible holding and investment companies, impacting market operations and trading sessions for the listed securities.
