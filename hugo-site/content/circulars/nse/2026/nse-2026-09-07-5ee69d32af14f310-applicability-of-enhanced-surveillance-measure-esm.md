## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Several securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage-I and will attract a minimum 100% margin along with shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I (AKI, Birla Precision Technologies, Burnpur Cement, Cyber Media, ICDS, and Lancor Holdings) are shortlisted under ESM Stage-I.
- Minimum 100% margin applicable w.e.f. September 09, 2026, on all open positions as on September 08, 2026, and new positions created from September 09, 2026.
- Affected securities will be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 09, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specified securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Shift of securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure maintenance of 100% margins on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.
- Trading in these securities will settle under the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ESM Stage-I measures / margin requirements / segment shift: September 09, 2026 (with open positions evaluated as of September 08, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity for the affected securities may be impacted due to the Trade-for-Trade segment transfer and the 100% margin requirement on open and new positions.
