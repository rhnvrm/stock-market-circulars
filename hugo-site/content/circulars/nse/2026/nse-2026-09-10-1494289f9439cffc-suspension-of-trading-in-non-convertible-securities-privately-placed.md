## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed non-convertible securities for three companies, effective September 11, 2026, due to redemption.

## Key Points

- **Bank of Maharashtra**: ISIN INE457A08035 suspended due to redemption (Effective: 11-Sep-2026).
- **Aditya Birla Capital Limited**: ISIN INE860H08DV9 suspended due to redemption (Effective: 11-Sep-2026).
- **IIFL Home Finance Limited**: ISIN INE477L07AK5 suspended due to redemption (Effective: 11-Sep-2026).

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are advised to note the suspension of trading in the aforementioned non-convertible securities.

## Important Dates

- **Suspension Date**: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard procedure following the redemption of debt securities; negligible impact on secondary market operations.
