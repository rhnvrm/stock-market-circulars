## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the periodic review and relaxation of surveillance actions under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework in coordination with other exchanges. Eligible securities will move to lower stages of the GSM framework effective September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Periodic review of securities under GSM carried out to assess relaxation of surveillance actions.
- Effective Date: September 15, 2026.
- Securities moving to lower stages include Net Avenue Technologies Limited (CBAZAAR), Morarjee Textiles Limited (MORARJEE), and Univa Foods Limited (UNIVAFOODS).
- Series change noted for Net Avenue Technologies Limited from ST to SM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are moved from higher stages to lower stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework based on periodic review parameters.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are advised to take note of the revised GSM stages for the listed securities and ensure compliance with applicable trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date for GSM Relaxation: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impact is specific to the securities mentioned in the Annexure, providing relaxation in trading constraints as they transition to lower GSM stages.
