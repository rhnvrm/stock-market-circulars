## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework. Effective October 12, 2026, 5 securities have been shortlisted under ASM IBC Stage I.

## Key Points

- Five securities shortlisted under ASM for Companies as per Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) Stage I w.e.f. October 12, 2026.
- Affected stocks include Aban Offshore Limited (ABAN), Ankit Metal & Power Limited (ANKITMETAL), Reliance Infrastructure Limited (RELINFRA), Simbhaoli Sugars Limited (SIMBHALS), and Vivimed Labs Limited (VIVIMEDLAB).
- Shortlisting under ASM IBC is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned entity.
- The ASM IBC framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities meeting the criteria under the ASM framework for companies undergoing or subject to Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) provisions are placed under Stage I surveillance with applicable price bands and monitoring.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance measures and ensure compliance with trading restrictions and price bands applicable to the shortlisted scrips.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the shortlisted securities will be subject to additional surveillance controls, margin requirements, and price bands, impacting trading dynamics for investors holding or trading in ABAN, ANKITMETAL, RELINFRA, SIMBHALS, and VIVIMEDLAB.
