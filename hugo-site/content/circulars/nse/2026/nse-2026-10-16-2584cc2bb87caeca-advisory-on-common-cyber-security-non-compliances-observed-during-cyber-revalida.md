## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued an advisory detailing common cyber security non-compliances observed during the special purpose cyber revalidation audit and monitoring via the CERT-In Cyber Swachhta Kendra (CSK) platform. The observations indicate existing gaps in the understanding and implementation of the Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF).

## Key Points

- SEBI and Stock Exchanges conducted a special purpose cyber revalidation audit to assess cyber security comprehensiveness and CSK platform monitoring effectiveness.
- Common non-compliances were identified across governance, policies, and security systems of trading members.
- Key governance issues include lack of clear critical asset classification, incomplete asset inventories (missing end-of-life/support details), absence of detailed policy exceptions and deviations, incorrect positioning of the CISO role relative to CTO/CIO, and misaligned RTO/RPO timelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulations introduced; this is an advisory reinforcing compliance with existing requirements under the Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF).

## Compliance Requirements

- All trading members must review their cyber security posture and implement necessary systems, controls, and procedures.
- Ensure asset inventories are comprehensive and include critical assets along with end-of-life, end-of-support, and installation dates.
- Standardize RTO and RPO timelines in line with CSCRF specifications.
- Ensure proper positioning and independence of the CISO.

## Important Dates

- Issued on October 16, 2026.
- Immediate implementation and compliance review required for all trading members.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances market resilience by guiding trading members to address systemic cyber security gaps and strengthen compliance under SEBI and Exchange frameworks.
