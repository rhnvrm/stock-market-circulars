## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the revision of the Edelweiss Ultra Short Term Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The allotment date and purchase cut-off time have been revised as per communication from Edelweiss Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Edelweiss Ultra Short Term Fund NFO schedule has been revised.
- Allotment Date changed from August 31, 2026, to August 25, 2026.
- Purchase cut-off time is set at 2:59 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- SIP/XSIP registrations authentication must be completed by clients before 03:00 PM on the NFO closure date.
- Clear funds through any payment mode must be received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before August 24, 2026, 06:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Clear Funds Deadline: August 24, 2026, 06:00 PM
- Revised Allotment Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational changes affect participants and investors dealing with the Edelweiss Ultra Short Term Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
