## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has approved the request of trading member R.L.P. Securities Pvt. Ltd. for the segmental surrender of its membership in the Interest Rate Futures segment.

## Key Points

- **Trading Member**: R.L.P. Securities Pvt. Ltd. (SEBI Registration Number: INZ000166638).
- **Surrendered Segment**: Interest Rate Futures.
- **Approval Date**: August 6, 2026.
- **Circular Notification Date**: August 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no systemic regulatory changes introduced by this circular. It is an administrative approval of an individual trading member's request under existing Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

All members are requested to take note of the segmental surrender of membership by R.L.P. Securities Pvt. Ltd. in the Interest Rate Futures segment. No further compliance actions are required from other trading or clearing members.

## Important Dates

- **Surrender Approval Date**: August 6, 2026
- **Circular Notification Date**: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: None. This is a standard administrative notification affecting only one member and one derivative segment.
- **Operational Impact**: R.L.P. Securities Pvt. Ltd. will no longer be permitted to trade or operate in the Interest Rate Futures segment on the NSE. Other operations of the member and the segment itself remain unaffected.
