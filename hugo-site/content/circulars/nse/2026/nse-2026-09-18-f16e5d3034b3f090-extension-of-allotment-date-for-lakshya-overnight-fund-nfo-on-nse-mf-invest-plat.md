## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the extension of the allotment date for Lakshya Overnight Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform, pursuant to communication received from Lakshya Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- The allotment date for Lakshya Overnight Fund NFO has been revised from September 21, 2026, to September 24, 2026.
- The re-opening date for the fund has been revised from September 22, 2026, to September 25, 2026.
- Clear funds through any payment mode must be received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 23, 2026, by 03:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) timelines for Lakshya Overnight Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that investor funds are cleared and received in the designated Clearing Corporation account by the stipulated deadline.

## Important Dates

- Previous Allotment Date: September 21, 2026
- Revised Allotment Date: September 24, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: September 23, 2026 (03:00 PM)
- Re-opening Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact limited to investors and members participating in the Lakshya Overnight Fund NFO subscription timeline.
