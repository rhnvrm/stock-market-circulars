## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced that the Edelweiss Ultra Short Term Fund NFO of Edelweiss Mutual Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** Edelweiss Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** Edelweiss Ultra Short Term Fund
- **Effective Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform
- **Facilities Available:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with operational guidelines for mutual fund transactions on the platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with access to debt fund options on the NSE mutual fund platform.
