## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the update of quantity freeze limits for futures and options contracts on indices, effective from September 01, 2026, pursuant to consolidated circular NSE/FAOP/73928.

## Key Points

- Updated quantity freeze limits apply to index derivatives contracts w.e.f. September 01, 2026.
- Specific limits: BANKNIFTY (600), NIFTY (1800), FINNIFTY (1800), MIDCPNIFTY (2800), NIFTYNXT50 (600), NIFTYFPI (8500).
- Members must load the updated contract files before trading on the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of quantity freeze limits for index F&O contracts based on established computation methodologies.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to load the updated contract.gz and NSE_FO_contract_ddmmyyyy.csv.gz files in their trading applications from the faoftp/faocommon directory on the Extranet server prior to trading on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026
- Circular Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members need to update their systems with the new contract files and quantity freeze parameters to ensure seamless order execution for index derivatives.
