## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and trading of privately placed securities of IIFL Finance Limited and Jio Credit Limited on the capital market segment, effective from October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment effective October 08, 2026.
- Issuer 1: IIFL Finance Limited (Symbol: IIFL30, ISIN: INE530B07690, Market Linked Debenture maturing 08-Apr-2030, Lot size: 1).
- Issuer 2: Jio Credit Limited (Symbols: 0JCL30 & 859JCL31, ISINs: INE282H07117 & INE282H07125, maturing in 2030 and 2031, Lot size: 1).
- Trading shall be identified strictly by designated security codes in specified lot sizes pursuant to NSE Trading Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure trading and settlement are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Maturity Dates: 08-Apr-2030 (IIFL30), 04-Oct-2030 (0JCL30), 05-Oct-2031 (859JCL31)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital market listing providing liquidity and trading access for the newly issued privately placed debt/market-linked securities.
