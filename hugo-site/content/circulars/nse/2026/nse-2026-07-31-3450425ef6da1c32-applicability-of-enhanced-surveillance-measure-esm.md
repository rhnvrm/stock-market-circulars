## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability and stage transitions of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The surveillance actions include new stage inclusions subjecting securities to 100% margin requirements and shifting them to the Trade-for-Trade segment, as well as transitioning several securities from ESM Stage II down to Stage I.

## Key Points

- **New ESM Stage I Inclusion**: GATECHDVR (GACM Technologies Limited) is shortlisted under ESM Stage I. It attracts a minimum 100% margin from August 04, 2026, and shifts from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST).
- **ESM Stage II to Stage I Transition**: Seven securities (AAREYDRUGS, AKSHOPTFBR, DCMSIL, GRCL, GTECJAINX, PRIZOR, and SADHNANIQ) are transitioning from ESM Stage II to Stage I effective August 03, 2026.
- **Price Band Restoration**: Scrips moving out of the ESM framework will have their price bands reinstated to their pre-ESM levels, provided they are not under other surveillance measures.
- **Non-Adverse Action**: Shortlisting under the ESM framework is a routine market surveillance activity and does not constitute any adverse regulatory action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Segment Shift**: Securities qualifying under ESM Stage I are shifted from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 04, 2026.
- **Stage II Periodic Call Auction**: Securities shifting to ESM Stage II are subject to Trade-for-Trade segment with a restrictive 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction.

## Compliance Requirements

- **100% Margin Obligation**: Trading members must levy a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on August 03, 2026, and all new positions created from August 04, 2026, for GATECHDVR.
- **Systems and Back-office Updates**: Operational teams must configure systems to reflect the segment shifts and revised price bands for transitioning scrips on the respective effective dates.

## Important Dates

- **August 03, 2026**: Effective date for Stage II to Stage I transitions. Margin baseline date for open positions of newly shortlisted securities.
- **August 04, 2026**: Effective date for segment shifting and 100% margin implementation for GATECHDVR.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment eliminates intraday leverage/speculation as 100% upfront margin and physical delivery are required. This results in lowered trading liquidity for the affected scrips.
- **Investment/Trading Impact**: Traders holding open positions in GATECHDVR must provide 100% margin, which may prompt position liquidations. Conversely, stocks moving from Stage II to Stage I will benefit from relaxed price bands (typically 5% instead of 2% under Periodic Call Auction), increasing liquidity and trading flexibility.
