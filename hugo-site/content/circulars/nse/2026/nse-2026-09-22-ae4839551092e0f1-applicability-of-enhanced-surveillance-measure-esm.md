## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities shortlisted under ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin and will be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 24, 2026, on all open positions as on September 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. September 24, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 23, 2026.
- Bikewo Green Tech Limited (BIKEWO) is moving from Stage I to Stage II of the ESM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework in conjunction with existing exchange rules.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement for the specified list of securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II movement and price band: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date for margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade shift: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in shortlisted securities must maintain adequate margins and note the shift in settlement mechanism and price bands.
