## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for certain securities effective September 03, 2026, based on criteria satisfaction.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I include Alok Industries Limited, Cyient Limited, Mohite Industries Limited, MV Electrosystems Limited, Rhetan TMT Limited, and Trust Fintech Limited.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. September 03, 2026.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on September 02, 2026, and new positions created from September 03, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I framework provisions on specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure the applicable margin rates of 50% (capped at 100%) are maintained for open positions and new positions in the specified securities starting September 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: September 03, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date for Open Positions: September 02, 2026
- Circular Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will require higher margins (50% or existing, whichever is higher), potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes for these counters.
