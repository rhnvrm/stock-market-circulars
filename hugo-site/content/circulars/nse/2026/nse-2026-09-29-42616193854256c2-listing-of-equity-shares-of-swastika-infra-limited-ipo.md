## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Swastika Infra Limited under its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The security will trade under the symbol SWASTIKAIN.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Swastika Infra Limited
- Trading Symbol: SWASTIKAIN
- ISIN: INE1QJA01015
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular

None.

Trading members are advised to update their systems and master databases with the new symbol and ISIN details for Swastika Infra Limited prior to the commencement of trading.

- Circular Date: September 29, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced separately

Facilitates the official admission to dealings and subsequent trading of equity shares of Swastika Infra Limited on the NSE platform.
