## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the submission of the Accessibility Audit Report for Digital Platforms and Remediation of findings pursuant to SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131 dated September 25, 2025. Trading members are required to submit their reports by October 31, 2026, through the online module on the ENIT Portal.

## Key Points

- Mandatory submission of the Accessibility Audit Report for digital platforms and remediation of findings.
- Applicable to all Trading Members under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 and associated Rules.
- Submission is hosted on the ENIT Portal under the compliance module.
- Detailed step-by-step submission guide provided in Annexure A of the circular covering parameter selection (Member Code, Name, Financial Year 2025-2026, and Proprietary Trading status).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of digital accessibility standards as per SEBI guidelines under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 for all regulated entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must review displayed website URLs, confirm WCAG accessibility levels, provide compliance status, and submit IAAP auditor details.
- Submission must be completed via ENIT -> NEW-Compliance -> Compliance -> Compliance with RPwD Act, 2016 -> RPwD Submission.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 01, 2026
- Submission Deadline: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure their digital platforms (websites, mobile apps, and other portals) comply with accessibility guidelines and complete the ENIT portal filing prior to the deadline to avoid regulatory non-compliance.
