## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced that the Jio BlackRock Balanced Advantage Fund of Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund will be reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform starting October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes included: Jio BlackRock Balanced Advantage Fund - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF22M001366) and Jio BlackRock Balanced Advantage Fund - Regular Growth (ISIN: INF22M001374).
- Scheme Category: Equity.
- Facilities Enabled: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: October 8, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational notice regarding platform scheme availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with existing operating guidelines when processing transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 7, 2026
- Effective Date: October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with expanded access and ongoing investment facilities for Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund schemes on the NSE platform.
