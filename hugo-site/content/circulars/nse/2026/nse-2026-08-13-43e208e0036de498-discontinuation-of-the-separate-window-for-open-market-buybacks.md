## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the discontinuation of the separate window for open market buybacks, in line with SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 (amended July 6, 2026) and previous Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/73927.

## Key Points

- The separate open market buyback window is discontinued with effect from August 01, 2026.
- Members undertaking buybacks must use normal Unique Client Codes (UCC) for order entry.
- UCCs beginning with “BUYBACK***” or “BUYBACK” must not be used for buyback order entry.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to amended SEBI regulations, a separate open market buyback window is no longer applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must route open market buyback orders through normal UCCs rather than dedicated buyback prefixes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 01, 2026
- Circular Date: August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational changes for trading members handling open market buybacks, requiring updates to order entry protocols and UCC usage.
