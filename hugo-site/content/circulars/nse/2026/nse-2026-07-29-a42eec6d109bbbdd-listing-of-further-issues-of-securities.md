## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities on its SME EMERGE platform (Capital Market Segment). Effective from **July 30, 2026**, additional equity shares of **Aimtron Electronics Limited**, **Ducol Organics and Colours Limited**, and **Transteel Seating Technologies Limited** are admitted to dealings. These shares were allotted under preferential issues.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** The circular is effective from July 30, 2026.
- **Trading Platform:** National Stock Exchange Capital Market segment - SME EMERGE.
- **Regulatory Reference:** Notification issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 (further admissions to dealings) and Regulation 2.5.5 (respective lot sizes) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- **Pari Passu Status:** Newly admitted securities rank pari passu with existing equity shares.

### Details of Admitted Securities:

1. **Aimtron Electronics Limited (AIMTRON):**
   - **ISIN:** INE0RUV01018
   - **Quantity:** 232,180 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each.
   - **Allotment Date:** April 27, 2026.
   - **Issue Price:** Rs. 680.64
   - **Distinctive Number Range:** 20609178 to 20841357.
   - **Market Lot:** 200
   - **Lock-in Details:**
     - 2,400 shares (distinctive numbers 20609178 to 20611577) locked-in until **January 31, 2028**.
     - 229,780 shares (distinctive numbers 20611578 to 20841357) locked-in until **January 31, 2027**.

2. **Ducol Organics and Colours Limited (DUCOL):**
   - **ISIN:** INE0LZO01015
   - **Quantity:** 1,764,697 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each.
   - **Allotment Date:** June 26, 2026.
   - **Issue Price:** Rs. 170
   - **Distinctive Number Range:** 16293959 to 18058655.
   - **Market Lot:** 800
   - **Lock-in Details:**
     - 1,553,609 shares (distinctive numbers 16293959 to 17847567) locked-in until **February 10, 2027**.
     - 211,088 shares (distinctive numbers 17847568 to 18058655) locked-in until **February 10, 2027**.

3. **Transteel Seating Technologies Limited (TRANSTEEL):**
   - **ISIN:** INE0NVI01020
   - **Face Value:** Rs. 10/-
   - **Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/-
   - **Issue Price:** Rs. 80
   - **Series:** SM
   - **Description:** Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under Preferential Issue.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework amendments are introduced. The listings are executed under standard and existing Regulations 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Stockbrokers and Members:** Trading in these securities must adhere strictly to designated codes/symbols and the specified market lot sizes (200 for AIMTRON and 800 for DUCOL).
- **For Investors & Custodians:** Note the lock-in periods and respective expiry dates for AIMTRON and DUCOL before transfer operations can be planned.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Allotment Dates:**
  - Aimtron Electronics: April 27, 2026
  - Ducol Organics: June 26, 2026
- **Listing Effective Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates (AIMTRON):**
  - January 31, 2027 (for 229,780 shares)
  - January 31, 2028 (for 2,400 shares)
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates (DUCOL):**
  - February 10, 2027 (for 1,553,609 shares and 211,088 shares)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity:** The listing of further issues increases the total outstanding share capital and trading float for the respective companies, potentially boosting trading liquidity over time.
- **Lock-in Period:** A significant portion of the newly listed shares (almost the entirety for AIMTRON and all for DUCOL) are under lock-in until early 2027/2028, meaning they cannot be immediately sold on the exchange floor, which prevents immediate downward price pressure from these allotments.
- **SME EMERGE Platform Operations:** Reflects ongoing fundraising and capital-raising activities on the NSE's SME platform.
