## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar and guidelines for the Buyback Offer under the Tender Offer scheme of Industrial Investment Trust Limited. Members must adhere to the specified trade dates, obligation dates, and settlement procedures.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: X
- Settlement Number: 2026107
- Trade Start Date: 21-AUG-2026
- Trade End Date: 28-AUG-2026
- Obligation Download Date: 02-SEP-2026
- Settlement Date: 03-SEP-2026

## Regulatory Changes

- None specific to regulations; operational schedule issued pursuant to the buyback scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be tendered exclusively by placing Early Pay-in of Shares through the early pay-in mechanism for Market Type Buyback.
- On the trade end date, early pay-in of shares must be executed well in advance for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m.
- Securities and funds pay-out will be processed post-receipt of funds pay-in on the settlement day.
- Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Important Dates

- Trade Start: 21-AUG-2026
- Trade End: 28-AUG-2026
- Obligation Download: 02-SEP-2026
- Settlement Date: 03-SEP-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants and custodians dealing with Industrial Investment Trust Limited's buyback must complete share tendering and fund pay-ins in accordance with the specified schedule to avoid rejection or penalties.
