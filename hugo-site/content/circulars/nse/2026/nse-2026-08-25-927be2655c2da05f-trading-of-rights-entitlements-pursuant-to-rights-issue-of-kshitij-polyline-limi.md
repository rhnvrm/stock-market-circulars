## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Kshitij Polyline Limited. Trading will commence on August 27, 2026, under the Trade to Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Symbol: KSHITIJ-RE
- Company Name: Kshitij Polyline Limited
- ISIN: INE013820027
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 61,697,950
- Trading Period for Renunciation: From August 27, 2026 to September 01, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 02, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment in the Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Trading members must include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes/bills regarding RE purchases and the necessity to apply or renounce before the issue closing date, or else they will lapse.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Commencement of Trading: August 27, 2026
- Trading Period End Date: September 01, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables shareholders and investors to trade Rights Entitlements or renounce them within the specified window before they lapse and are extinguished after the issue closing date.
