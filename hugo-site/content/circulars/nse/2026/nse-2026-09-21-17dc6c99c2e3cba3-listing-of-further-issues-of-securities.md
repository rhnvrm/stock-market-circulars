## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for several companies, including Antelopus Selan Energy Limited, Arman Financial Services Limited, Axis Bank Limited, Bandhan Bank Limited, DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Limited, and numerous others.

## Key Points

- Securities further admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective from September 22, 2026.
- Includes shares allotted primarily under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).
- Designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexures of the circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in these securities shall be identified only by designated codes and in specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital increase and equity distribution for the listed companies without broad market impact.
