## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a circular regarding the early pay-in (EPI) of securities for Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited (KIRLPNU) on account of its face value split from Rs 2/- per share to Re 1/- per share. Market participants and custodians are instructed on executing early pay-in in the old and new ISINs across specified settlement dates.

## Key Points

- **Corporate Action:** Face value split (sub-division) of Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited from Rs 2/- per share to Re 1/- per share.
- **ISIN Details:** Existing/Old ISIN is INE811A01020.
- **Record Date & Ex-Date:** August 18, 2026.
- **Settlement Details:** For trade dates affecting settlement numbers 2026155 and 2026156 on August 18 and August 19, 2026, early pay-in should be made in the old/existing ISIN without adjustment for conversion.
- **Block Mechanism:** In case of early pay-in through block mechanism, client-wise early pay-in allocation files should not be uploaded.
- **Pool Account Pay-in:** Custodians providing early pay-in through pool accounts must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details according to the actual traded quantity to pass benefits to clients.

## Regulatory Changes

- Instructions adhere to SEBI circular on Block Mechanism in demat accounts of clients undertaking sale transactions (NCL circular NCL/CMPT/53386 dated August 22, 2022).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and custodians must execute early pay-in instructions in the old ISIN for the record/ex-date and switch to the new ISIN thereafter.
- Custodians using pool accounts must upload proper client-wise allocation files matching actual traded quantities.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Ex-Date:** August 18, 2026
- **EPI Dates:** August 18, 2026 and August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures seamless clearing and settlement operations for market participants executing trades prior to and during the stock split of Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Limited.
