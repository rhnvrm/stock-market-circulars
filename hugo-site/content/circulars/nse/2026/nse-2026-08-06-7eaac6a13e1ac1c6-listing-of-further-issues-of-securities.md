## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued circular Ref No: 1303/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/CML/75614) dated August 06, 2026, announcing the listing and admission of further equity shares of several listed entities to dealings on the Capital Market segment, with effect from August 07, 2026. These additional shares have been allotted under respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and will rank pari passu with the existing equity shares of the companies.

## Key Points

- **Further Issue Admission:** Admission of further securities of multiple companies for trading on the NSE Capital Market segment with effect from August 07, 2026.
- **Pari Passu Status:** Newly allotted shares are pari passu, meaning they carry the same rights, privileges, and dividend entitlements as the existing outstanding equity shares.
- **Participating Companies:**
  - **Angel One Limited:** 168,488 equity shares allotted under ESOP.
  - **Axis Bank Limited:** 285,127 equity shares allotted under ESOP.
  - **BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Limited:** 342,766 equity shares allotted under ESOP.
  - **Capillary Technologies India Limited:** 116,748 equity shares allotted under ESOP.
  - **Other Companies:** Including ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, Sona BLW Precision Forgings Limited, Tata Consumer Products Limited, and Uniparts India Limited.
- **Trading Lot Size:** The market lot size for trading these securities is 1.

## Regulatory Changes

No specific statutory or regulatory rule changes are introduced. The listings are executed under standard provisions of **Regulation 3.1.1** (dealing permissions) and **Regulation 2.5.5** (lot sizes) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members:** No active compliance action is required for brokers and market participants. Members can deal in these securities using the designated trading symbols and codes under normal settlement practices.
- **For Listed Companies:** Companies must complete the necessary listing and depository processes to ensure the newly allotted shares are credited to respective demat accounts and available for trading on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotments:**
  - Angel One: July 31, 2026
  - Axis Bank: August 04, 2026
  - BlueStone Jewellery: July 31, 2026
  - Capillary Technologies: July 23, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** August 07, 2026 (effective date for trading dealings on the exchange).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. The listing of further shares allotted under ESOP represents routine corporate administrative action. The dilution is negligible and the corporate action is non-eventful for overall market liquidity or price discovery.
- **Trading Operations:** Standard market lot size (1) and existing ISINs apply, ensuring seamless integration into the automated trading and settlement systems of the exchange.
