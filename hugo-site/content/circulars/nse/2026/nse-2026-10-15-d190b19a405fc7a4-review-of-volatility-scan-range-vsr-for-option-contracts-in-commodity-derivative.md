## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a review of the Volatility Scan Range (VSR) for option contracts in the commodity derivatives segment for the month of November 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Applicable VSR percentages are announced for key commodities including variants for November 2026.
- Copper: 5%
- Gold: 5%
- Silver: 6%
- Zinc: 5%
- Crude Oil: 30%
- Natural Gas: 6%

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the applicable Volatility Scan Range (VSR) for commodity option contracts in accordance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/CDMRD/DRMP/CIR/P/2021/08 dated January 11, 2021.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and market participants must take note of the revised VSR percentages for margin calculations and risk management in commodity derivatives options for November 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective for the month of November 2026.
- Circular Date: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Directly impacts margin requirements and risk parameters for trading option contracts in the specified commodity derivatives.
