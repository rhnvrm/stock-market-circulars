## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the availability of the Bank of India Value Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window opens on August 28, 2026, and closes on September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Bank of India Value Fund (Direct and Regular plans for Growth and IDCW options)
- **NFO Window:** August 28, 2026, to September 11, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** September 21, 2026
- **Asset Category:** Equity

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 20, 2026, till 3:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** August 28, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** September 11, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** September 20, 2026 (3:00 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to subscribe to the new equity value fund scheme on the NSE MF Invest platform.
