## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective August 10, 2026, Aban Offshore Limited has been shortlisted under Stage I of the ASM IBC framework. No securities have been excluded or moved between stages in this regulatory update.

## Key Points

- **Surveillance Shortlisting**: Aban Offshore Limited (Symbol: ABAN, ISIN: INE421A01028) has been shortlisted under Stage I of the ASM IBC framework with effect from August 10, 2026.
- **No Exclusions**: There are no securities meeting the exclusion criteria or moving from Stage I to Stage II / Stage II to Stage I in this circular.
- **Framework Operation**: The ASM IBC framework works in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- **Nature of Action**: Shortlisting under ASM IBC is purely for market surveillance purposes and is not an adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Placement of Aban Offshore Limited in ASM IBC Stage I.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their prior levels, subject to not being part of any other surveillance measure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to note the addition of Aban Offshore Limited to the Stage I ASM IBC list.
- Ensure compliance with applicable margins, trading restrictions, and price bands for the shortlisted security under Stage I ASM IBC.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 07, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Impact**: Aban Offshore Limited (ABAN) will see adjustments to its trading parameters, including price band limitations and margin requirements appropriate for Stage I of the ASM IBC framework.
- **Operational Impact**: Compliance and surveillance systems of trading members must be updated to reflect the new ASM status of ABAN starting August 10, 2026.
