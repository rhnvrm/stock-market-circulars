## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Based on criteria defined in prior circulars, specific securities have been identified for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II, effective from August 04, 2026. The margin requirements for these securities will be adjusted accordingly, with open positions as of August 03, 2026, and new positions from August 04, 2026, subject to the revised margin rates.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusion:** Four securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion in Short-Term ASM Stage I: Matrix Geo Solutions Limited (MGSL), Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited (RCDL), Sigma Solve Limited (SIGMA), and SML Mahindra Limited (SMLMAH).
- **Stage I Margin Requirement:** Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate capped at 100% w.e.f. August 04, 2026.
- **ST-ASM Stage II Inclusion:** No securities are shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II in this circular (listed as Nil).
- **Stage II Margin Requirement:** Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. August 04, 2026.
- **Nature of ASM:** The shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.
- **Price Band Reinstatement:** The price band of a scrip moving out of the framework will be reinstated to its previous level, subject to not being part of any other surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance actions under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Framework.
- Revision of margins for Stage I shortlisted securities to 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% effective August 04, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Collection:** Members must collect the revised applicable margins from clients trading in the shortlisted securities (MGSL, RCDL, SIGMA, SMLMAH) starting August 04, 2026, based on open positions as of August 03, 2026, and new positions from August 04, 2026.
- **Risk Management System Update:** Trading and clearing members must update their risk parameters and system margins to reflect the revised ST-ASM Stage I rules for the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Position Cut-off Date for Margin Applicability:** August 03, 2026
- **Effective Date of Revised Margin Requirements:** August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Trading Impact:** Shortlisted securities (MGSL, RCDL, SIGMA, SMLMAH) will face higher margin requirements, which increases transaction costs and reduces leverage. This typically results in a temporary reduction in liquidity and trading volumes in these counters.
- **Operational Impact:** Compliance and risk departments of brokerage firms must ensure proper system configuration to collect the required margins and avoid margin shortfall penalties.
