## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified members regarding the Gazette Notification issued by SEBI (No. F.No. SEBI/LAD-DOP/2026/319 dated October 7, 2026) titled "Securities and Exchange Board of India (Certification of Associated Persons in the Securities Markets) (Amendment) Regulations, 2026". The amendment updates terminology by replacing references to "associated person" with "designated person" and provides a revised definition.

## Key Points

- Refers to SEBI Gazette Notification F.No. SEBI/LAD-DOP/2026/319 dated October 7, 2026.
- Amends the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Certification of Associated Persons in the Securities Markets) Regulations, 2007.
- Trading members are requested to take note of the changes and ensure compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Across the 2007 regulations, terms such as "associated person", "associated persons", etc., are replaced with "designated person" ("निर्धारित व्यक्ति") and its plural forms.
- In Regulation 2(1), clause (c) is removed, and a new clause (द) is inserted defining "designated person" to mean a principal or employee of an intermediary, or an agent or distributor or any other person engaged in securities market business, including employees of foreign portfolio investors or foreign venture capital investors operating in India.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and intermediaries must take note of the terminology changes and ensure alignment with the amended regulations across their internal compliance and certification frameworks.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 8, 2026 (date of publication in the Official Gazette).
- Circular Date: October 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative and terminology update across compliance documentation and regulatory records concerning certified individuals and designated persons in the securities market.
