## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the commencement of trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Sunrest Lifescience Limited. Trading will take place on the Capital Market segment (SME Emerge) effective from August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sunrest Lifescience Limited
- Symbol: SUN-RE
- ISIN: INE0PLZ20020
- Total Number of Rights Entitlements: 4,291,200
- Trading Period for Renunciation: August 18, 2026, to August 28, 2026
- Date of Suspension: August 31, 2026
- Market Lot: 1600
- Segment: Trade to Trade in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of Rights Entitlements to dealings on the NSE SME Emerge segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of RE trading and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes must include a prominent disclaimer explaining that purchase of REs grants the right to participate in the Rights Issue by making an application with requisite money or renouncing them before closure.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the Issue Closing Date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Commencement of Trading: August 18, 2026
- Trading Period Closes (Renunciation): August 28, 2026
- Date of Suspension: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates active trading and renunciation of rights entitlements for eligible shareholders and investors of Sunrest Lifescience Limited within a defined timeframe.
