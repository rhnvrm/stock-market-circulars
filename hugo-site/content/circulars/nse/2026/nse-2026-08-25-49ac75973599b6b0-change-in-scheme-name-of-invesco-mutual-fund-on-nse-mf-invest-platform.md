## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for multiple schemes of Invesco Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Invesco India Ultra Short Duration Fund** renamed to **Invesco India Ultra Short Term Fund**
- **Invesco India Low Duration Fund** renamed to **Invesco India Ultra Short to Short Term Fund**
- **Invesco India Short Duration Fund** renamed to **Invesco India Short Term Fund**
- **Invesco India Medium Duration Fund** renamed to **Invesco India Medium Term Fund**
- **Invesco India Banking and PSU Fund** renamed to **Invesco India Banking and PSU Debt Fund**

## Regulatory Changes

- Changes implemented pursuant to Part IV under Chapter 3 of the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026, regarding Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors take note of the updated scheme names on the NSE MF Invest platform for future transactions and reporting.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Purely operational update concerning nomenclature with no direct financial impact on existing unitholders.
