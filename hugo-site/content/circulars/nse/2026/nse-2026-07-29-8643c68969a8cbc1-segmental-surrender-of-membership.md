## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has approved the request of Derivium Tradition Securities (India) Private Limited for the surrender of its membership in the Currency Derivatives segment. The surrender was approved on July 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Member Name:** Derivium Tradition Securities (India) Private Limited
- **SEBI Registration Number:** INZ000287035
- **Surrendered Segment:** Currency Derivatives
- **Approval Date:** July 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced in this circular. It is an administrative approval of a membership surrender under existing Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

All members are requested to take note of the surrender. No specific action is required from other trading members.

## Important Dates

- **Surrender Approval Date:** July 24, 2026
- **Circular Date:** July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. The surrender of membership in the Currency Derivatives segment by a single private entity does not affect market liquidity or trading operations.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Other members should update their records to reflect that Derivium Tradition Securities (India) Private Limited is no longer a member in the Currency Derivatives segment.
