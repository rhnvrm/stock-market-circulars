## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under the framework, attracting a minimum 100% margin and shifting from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) effective August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- U. Y. Fincorp Limited (UYFINCORP) has been shortlisted under ESM Stage - I w.e.f. August 18, 2026 (effective for margins and trading changes).
- Securities qualifying under ESM shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 19, 2026, on all open positions as on August 18, 2026, and new positions created from August 19, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 19, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 18, 2026.
- A consolidated list includes companies such as Apsis Aerocom Limited, ATC Energies System Limited, Bafna Pharmaceuticals Limited, and DCM Shriram Fine Chemicals Limited under Stage II.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specific securities under Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) stages.
- Imposition of 100% margin requirements on open and new positions.
- Shifting of affected securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that applicable 100% margins are levied on open positions as on August 18, 2026, and new positions created from August 19, 2026.
- Trading participants must note the segment shift to Trade-for-Trade for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for margins and segment shift: August 19, 2026 (with certain provisions effective August 18, 2026).
- Circular Date: August 17, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced surveillance measures increase trading restrictions and margin requirements for the shortlisted securities, potentially impacting liquidity and price discovery for the affected scrips.
