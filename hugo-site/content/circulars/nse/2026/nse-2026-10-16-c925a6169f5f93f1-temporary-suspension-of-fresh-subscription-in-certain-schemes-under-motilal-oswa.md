## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular announcing that, as per communication received from Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund, certain schemes have been disabled for lumpsum subscriptions, switch-ins, and fresh registrations under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) on the NSE MF Invest Platform with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Affected Scheme: Motilal Oswal Gold and Silver Passive Fund of Funds.
- Disabled Features: Fresh and additional purchases through lump sum mode, switch-in transactions from other schemes, and fresh registrations under SIP and STP.
- Unaffected Features: Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, STP-outs, and existing SIPs/STPs will continue to be processed normally at the AMC/RTA end.
- Cut-off Time: Effective from the cut-off time of October 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension of fresh inflows and registrations into the designated Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund scheme in compliance with AMC instructions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors must note the restriction on fresh subscriptions and new SIP/STP registrations for the designated scheme on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Subscription amounts received after the applicable cut-off time will be refunded without interest.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Cut-off Time: October 15, 2026.
- Circular Date: October 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors wishing to make fresh investments or start new SIPs/STPs in the Motilal Oswal Gold and Silver Passive Fund of Funds will be unable to do so via the NSE MF Invest Platform. Existing investments, redemptions, and ongoing SIPs/STPs remain unaffected.
