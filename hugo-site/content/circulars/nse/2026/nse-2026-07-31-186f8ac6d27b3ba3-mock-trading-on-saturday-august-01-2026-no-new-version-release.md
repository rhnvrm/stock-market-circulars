## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) on Saturday, August 01, 2026. This session will test systems and network connectivity from both the Primary site and the Disaster Recovery (DR) site. No new software version is being released for this session; the active NEAT version remains 1.3.3. Members are required to participate and subsequently perform a live re-login check to ensure smooth connectivity for trading on Monday, August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mock Trading Schedule**:
  - **Trading Session-1 (Primary Site)**: Normal Market open at 08:00 hrs, Contingency start at 08:45 hrs, Contingency end at 09:55 hrs, Normal Market close at 10:00 hrs.
  - **Trading Session-2 (DR Site)**: Normal Market open at 10:45 hrs, Normal Market close at 11:45 hrs.
  - **Live Re-login Window**: Saturday, August 01, 2026, from 18:30 hrs to 19:30 hrs.
- **Order Purge**: All outstanding orders will be automatically cleared before each session. Members utilizing NNF software must manually clear outstanding orders within their systems.
- **UCC/PAN Validation**: Only valid and compliant UCC/PANs approved before the cutoff will be permitted. UCC validation will be bypassed during contingency order entry.
- **No Financial Obligation**: Trades executed during the mock sessions are strictly for testing and will not result in any financial pay-in or pay-out obligations.
- **Facility Downtime**: Connect2NSE and Extranet facilities will be unavailable from 14:30 hrs until the end of the mock session. The Extranet API will be unavailable from 14:30 to 15:30 hrs.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced. This is a routine mock trading session conducted in compliance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 and Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- All SLBM market-enabled members must participate in the mock trading session using existing User IDs, IPs, and Box IDs.
- Members must verify successful telnet connectivity to the Exchange host from their designated IP addresses.
- NNF application users must ensure their User IDs are of dealer type, converted for NNF, and mapped to the appropriate IP.
- Members must perform the mandatory live re-login check between 18:30 hrs and 19:30 hrs on Saturday, August 01, 2026, to verify connectivity for live trading on Monday.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Session**: Saturday, August 01, 2026
- **Live Re-login Window**: Saturday, August 01, 2026 (18:30 - 19:30 hrs)
- **Next Live Trading Day**: Monday, August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Low. The session takes place on a Saturday and involves no financial obligations or software updates, posing zero risk to live market operations.
- **System Impact**: Crucial for validating the disaster recovery switchover readiness and ensuring members' network configurations are correctly aligned with the Exchange hosts.
