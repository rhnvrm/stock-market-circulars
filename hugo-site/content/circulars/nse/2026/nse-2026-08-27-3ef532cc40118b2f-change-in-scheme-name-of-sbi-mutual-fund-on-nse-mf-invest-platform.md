## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names of several SBI Mutual Fund schemes on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective immediately, as per communication received from SBI Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Notice affects multiple SBI Mutual Fund schemes including equity, hybrid, debt, and gilt funds.
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Equity Hybrid Fund -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Aggressive Hybrid Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Medium to Long Duration -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Medium to Long Term Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Medium Duration Fund -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Medium Term Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Dynamic Bond Fund -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Dynamic Term Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Savings Fund -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Money Market Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Ultra Short Duration Fund -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Ultra Short Term Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Constant Maturity 10 Year Gilt Fund -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI 10 year Constant Mat Gilt Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Short Term Debt Fund -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Short Term Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Floating Rate Debt Fund -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Floating Interest Rates Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Long Duration Fund -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Long Term Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Arbitrage Opportunities -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Arbitrage Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Low Duration Fund -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Ultra Short to Short Term Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: SBI Banking & PSU Fund -> Revised Scheme Name: SBI Banking & PSU Debt Fund

## Regulatory Changes

- Nomenclature changes implemented for specified mutual fund schemes in accordance with AMC notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors take note of the updated scheme nomenclature on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update with minimal market impact; purely procedural nomenclature alignment.
