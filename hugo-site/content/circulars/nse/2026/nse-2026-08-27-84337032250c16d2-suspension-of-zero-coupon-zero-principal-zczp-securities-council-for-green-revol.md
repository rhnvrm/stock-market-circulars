## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in Zero Coupon Zero Principal (ZCZP) securities issued by Council for Green Revolution, effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Council for Green Revolution
- Symbol: CGRZCZP-SE
- ISIN: INS1MX512011
- Effective Date of Suspension: August 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members are advised to take note of the suspension of trading in the aforementioned security.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified ZCZP security will be halted on the Exchange starting from the effective date.
