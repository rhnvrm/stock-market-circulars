## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the buyback offer of equity shares by Global Pet Industries Limited under the Tender Offer scheme, scheduled from October 1, 2026 to October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Global Pet Industries Limited
- Security Symbol: GLOBALPET
- Series: BB
- Issue Size: Up to 8,47,500 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 160/- per equity share
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- per equity share
- Lot Size: 750 Equity Shares
- ISIN: INE0PS501019
- Merchant Banker: Mark Corporate Advisors Private Limited
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. The offer is conducted in accordance with the Tender Offer Scheme as per Exchange circular NSE/CMTR/52776 dated June 29, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

Eligible trading members can participate in the Tender Offer Facility using their existing user IDs on the web-based IPO bidding platform or via the admin terminal.

## Important Dates

- Issue Period: 01-October-2026 to 08-October-2026
- Bidding Time: 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.
- Custodial Confirmation on Closure: Up to 4.00 p.m.

## Impact Assessment

Provides a structured liquidity window and exit opportunity for shareholders at the fixed buyback price.
