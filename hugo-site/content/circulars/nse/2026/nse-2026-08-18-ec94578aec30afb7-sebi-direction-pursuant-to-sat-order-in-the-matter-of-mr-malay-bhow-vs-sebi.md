## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding SEBI's direction pursuant to the Hon'ble SAT order in the matter of Mr. Malay Bhow. The Hon'ble SAT stayed the impugned 5-year debarment order subject to the deposit of 50% of the penalty amount. SEBI confirmed that the appellant has paid the penalty amount of Rs. 50,00,000.00/-, and advised compliance with the SAT order.

## Key Points

- Reference to earlier NSE circular NSE/INVG/74958 dated June 30, 2026, regarding SEBI Order No. WTM/AS/IVD-2/ID16/32460/2026-27.
- Hon'ble SAT vide order dated August 03, 2026, granted a stay of the debarment order subject to depositing 50% of the penalty.
- Appellant Mr. Malay Bhow paid the penalty amount of Rs. 50,00,000.00/- in favour of SEBI.
- SEBI vide email dated August 18, 2026, advised compliance with the SAT order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Stay of SEBI's debarment order against Mr. Malay Bhow (PAN: AEJPB9509C) following compliance with the penalty condition.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the debarment status update and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 18, 2026
- SAT Order Date: August 03, 2026
- SEBI Communication Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulatory clarity regarding the operational status and debarment stay of the concerned individual in the securities market.
