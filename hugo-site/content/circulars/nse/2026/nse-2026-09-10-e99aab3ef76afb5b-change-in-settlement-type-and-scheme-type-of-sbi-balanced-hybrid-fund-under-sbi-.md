## Summary

National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding the change in settlement type and scheme type of SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund under SBI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective immediately.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund
- Previous Settlement & Scheme Type: T2, Equity
- Revised Settlement & Scheme Type: T1, Debt
- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Update to the settlement type and scheme classification for SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund on the NSE MF platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and participants must take note of the revised settlement and scheme type for transactions involving SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational alignment for members transacting in SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
