## Summary

Cube Highways Trust has successfully completed its conversion from a privately listed Infrastructure Investment Trust (InvIT) to a publicly listed InvIT. In connection with this transition, the trust released its post-issue Unit Holding Pattern report as of July 29, 2026. This conversion is aimed at widening the investor base and facilitating public trading of the trust's units on stock exchanges.

## Key Points

- **Transition to Public Listing**: Cube Highways Trust is now a publicly listed InvIT, shifting from its previous privately listed status to enable active trading and public ownership.
- **Sponsor & Sponsor Group Shareholding**: Post-issue, the Sponsor and Sponsor Group hold 25,05,11,454 units, representing **18.64%** of the total outstanding units. All units in this category are held by foreign corporate bodies.
- **Public Institutional Holdings**: Public institutions account for **40.66%** of the total units (54,65,52,003 units). Key public institutional holdings include:
  - **Foreign Bodies Corporate**: 18.02% (24,21,42,068 units)
  - **Insurance Companies**: 7.62% (10,23,52,693 units)
  - **Mutual Funds**: 7.07% (9,50,27,955 units)
  - **Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs)**: 2.35% (3,16,07,624 units)
  - **Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs)**: 2.04% (2,74,37,129 units)
  - **Provident/Pension Funds**: 1.86% (2,49,58,951 units)
  - **Financial Institutions/Banks**: 1.71% (2,30,25,583 units)

## Regulatory Changes

This conversion has been executed in accordance with SEBI guidelines regulating Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs), transitioning the entity under public disclosure regimes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Cube Highways Trust is now subject to the rigorous continuous disclosure requirements applicable to publicly listed InvITs, including regular publication of financial results, unit holding patterns, and corporate governance updates.
- Reporting must align with SEBI's regulatory guidelines for public investment trusts.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Post-Issue Report Date**: July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: The transition enhances secondary market liquidity for the trust's units and establishes transparent market-driven valuation.
- **Operational Impact**: Requires the Investment Manager to implement more stringent compliance, investor relation frameworks, and reporting systems to meet the expectations of public markets and regulatory standards.
