## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion shall attract a minimum 100% margin and be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Minimum 100% margin applicable on all open positions as of August 20, 2026, and new positions created from August 21, 2026, for securities shortlisted under ESM Stage - I (Aarnav Fashions Limited, Ambalal Sarabhai Enterprises Limited, Bilcare Limited).
- Affected securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 21, 2026.
- Consolidated list includes securities such as Apsis Aerocom Limited under Stage II.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ESM surveillance criteria resulting in margin enhancements and segment migrations for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure appropriate margin collection (100%) and monitor trading segments for the impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Margin & Segment Shift: August 21, 2026 (open positions as of August 20, 2026)
- Circular Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions for listed securities under the ESM framework to deter excessive volatility.
