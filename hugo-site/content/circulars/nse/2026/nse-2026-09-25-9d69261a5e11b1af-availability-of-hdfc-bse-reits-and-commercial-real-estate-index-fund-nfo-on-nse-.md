## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of HDFC BSE REITS and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund of HDFC Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes made available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Effective date: September 28, 2026.
- Covers multiple options including Direct and Regular plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment).
- ISINs affected: INF179KC1KX6, INF179KC1KY4, INF179KC1KZ1, INF179KC1KU2, INF179KC1KV0, INF179KC1KW8.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine platform availability notification for a mutual fund scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading and clearing members participating in the NSE MF Invest Platform should take note of the availability of these schemes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to transact in the HDFC BSE REITS and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund through the NSE platform.
