## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has approved the request of CIL Securities Ltd. for the segmental surrender of membership in the Currency Derivatives including IRF segment, effective August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Trading Member:** CIL Securities Ltd.
- **SEBI Registration Number:** INZ000169535
- **Surrendered Segment:** Currency Derivatives including IRF
- **Surrender Approval Date:** August 11, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine membership status update.

## Compliance Requirements

All members are requested to take note of the segmental surrender by CIL Securities Ltd.

## Important Dates

- **Approval Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Circular Date:** August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to the specific trading member discontinuing operations in the specified segment.
