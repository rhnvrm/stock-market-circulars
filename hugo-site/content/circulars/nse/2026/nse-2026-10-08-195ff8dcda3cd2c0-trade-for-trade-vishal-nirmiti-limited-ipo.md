## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the transfer of Vishal Nirmiti Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (Series: EQ), effective October 23, 2026, following previous exchange circulars and SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Security: Vishal Nirmiti Limited (Symbol: VNL)
- Action: Transferred from trade for trade segment (series: BE) to rolling segment (Series: EQ).
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026.
- Reference: Building upon Exchange Circular no. NSE/CML/76773 dated October 07, 2026, and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are moved from the trade for trade segment to the normal rolling settlement segment in accordance with SEBI guidelines for IPOs and listing norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members need to note the change in trading series for Vishal Nirmiti Limited starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for Vishal Nirmiti Limited shares, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility for investors.
