## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a surveillance circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Effective August 12, 2026, nine securities have been shortlisted for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I. There are no securities shortlisted for Stage II, nor any movements between stages in this circular.

The ST-ASM framework is a proactive surveillance mechanism implemented by the Exchange in coordination with SEBI to ensure market integrity. The Exchange emphasizes that inclusion under the ASM framework is purely based on market surveillance parameters and should not be construed as any adverse action against the shortlisted companies.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Inclusion**: Nine securities have been shortlisted for Stage I under the ST-ASM framework, effective August 11, 2026 (with margin actions effective August 12, 2026).
- **Surveillance Margin Requirements**: An applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) will apply on all open positions as of August 11, 2026, and new positions created from August 12, 2026.
- **Stage II / Movements**: No securities have been added to Stage II or moved between Stage I and Stage II in this circular.
- **Nature of Action**: The ASM classification is purely for market surveillance purposes and is not a disciplinary or adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory structures are introduced; this circular applies the existing provisions of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework established under prior circulars (NSE/SURV/39265, NSE/SURV/46557, NSE/SURV/52144, NSE/SURV/58558, and NSE/SURV/64066).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must collect and upload the enhanced surveillance margins (50% or existing, capped at 100%) from clients trading in the shortlisted Stage I securities starting from August 12, 2026.
- **Margin Maintenance**: The surveillance margin applies to all open positions as of August 11, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 10, 2026
- **Shortlisting Effective Date**: August 11, 2026
- **Margin Actions Effective Date**: August 12, 2026
- **Position Cut-off Date**: August 11, 2026 (for open positions)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Medium. The shortlisted securities will face higher margin requirements, which could lead to reduced liquidity, lower trading volumes, and potential short-term price volatility as leverage is restricted.
- **Operational Impact**: Medium. Trading members must update their risk management systems (RMS) to enforce the 50% minimum margin requirement on the nine listed securities for both existing open positions and new trades.
