## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch and availability of the Quant Silver ETF New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from August 17, 2026, to August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** Quant Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** Quant Silver ETF Regular Plan Growth & Quant Silver ETF Direct Plan Growth
- **ISIN:** INF966L01EI5
- **Category:** Equity (as specified in the circular table)
- **NFO Window:** August 17, 2026 to August 19, 2026 till 11:59 pm
- **Allotment Date:** September 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 20, 2026, till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** August 17, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** August 19, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** September 20, 2026
- **Fund Pay-in Deadline:** September 20, 2026 (12:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to transact in the new Quant Silver ETF NFO via the NSE MF Invest platform within the specified timelines.
