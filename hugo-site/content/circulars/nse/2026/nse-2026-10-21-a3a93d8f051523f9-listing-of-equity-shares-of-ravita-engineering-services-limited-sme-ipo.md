## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Ravita Engineering Services Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform under symbol RAVITA, effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ravita Engineering Services Limited
- Symbol: RAVITA
- ISIN: INE1UEL01023
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 5/- per share
- Issue Price: Rs. 112/- per share
- Total No. of Securities: 37,680,682
- Market Lot: Minimum 1200 Equity Shares
- Series: 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment - Settlement Type 'W') initially, shifting to 'SM' (Normal Rolling Segment - Settlement Type 'N') subsequently.
- Market Maker: Pace Stock Broking Services Private Limited
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; admission to dealings in accordance with NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations and SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with pre-open session guidelines and continuous market trading parameters as per exchange circulars.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations and trading commencement for the SME IPO securities.
