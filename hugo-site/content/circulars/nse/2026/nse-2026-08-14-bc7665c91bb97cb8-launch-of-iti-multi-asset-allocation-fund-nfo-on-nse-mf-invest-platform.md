## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch and availability of the ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window runs from August 17, 2026, to August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund Name: ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund
- Available on: NSE MF Invest Platform
- Subscription Period: August 17, 2026 to August 31, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Includes multiple plans such as Direct Plan, Regular Plan, Growth, and Payout/Reinvestment options across various ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 03, 2026 till 11:00 am.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 17, 2026
- NFO Close Date: August 31, 2026
- Allotment Date: September 07, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 03, 2026 (11:00 am)

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to subscribe to the new ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund scheme through the NSE platform.
