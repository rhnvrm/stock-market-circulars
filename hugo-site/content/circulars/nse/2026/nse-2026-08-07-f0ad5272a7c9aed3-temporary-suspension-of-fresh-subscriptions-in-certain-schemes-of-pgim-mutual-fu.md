## Summary

PGIM India Mutual Fund and the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) have announced the temporary suspension of all existing Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) instalments into certain designated schemes. The suspension is effective after the cutoff timing on August 07, 2026. This measure has been taken to comply with SEBI's regulatory limits on overseas investments at the mutual fund industry level.

## Key Points

- **Suspension of Subscriptions:** All existing systematic investments (SIPs and STPs) into Designated Schemes are temporarily suspended post-cutoff of August 07, 2026.
- **Designated Schemes Affected:**
  - PGIM India Global Equity Opportunities Fund of Fund
  - PGIM India Emerging Markets Equity Fund of Fund
  - PGIM India Global Select Real Estate Securities Fund of Fund
- **Allowed Transactions:** The suspension does not apply to redemptions, switch-out facilities, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWPs), STPs originating from these Designated Schemes, intra-scheme switches (Regular to Direct), and intra-plan switches (Growth to IDCW).
- **Regulatory Background:** The limits are governed by SEBI's letter dated June 17, 2022, permitting mutual funds to invest overseas only within the headroom available without exceeding the EOD February 1, 2022 limit.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Overseas Investment Cap:** Compliance with SEBI Letter No. SEBI/HO/OW/IMD-II/DoF3/P/25095/2022, which restricts mutual funds from accepting subscriptions for overseas investments beyond specified overall industry/mutual fund-level caps set in February 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members and Platforms:** NSE MF Invest platform participants and mutual fund distributors must halt the processing and registration of any new/existing SIP/STP transactions for the designated PGIM schemes after the cutoff on August 07, 2026.
- **Unit Holder Records:** Investors are advised by the AMC to update their PAN, KYC, email address, mobile number, nominee details, and ensure their PAN is linked with Aadhaar.

## Important Dates

- **PGIM AMC Notice Date:** August 04, 2026
- **NSE Circular Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Effective Date of Suspension:** August 07, 2026 (post cutoff timing)

## Impact Assessment

- **Investor Impact:** Investors with ongoing SIPs or STPs targeting the specified international schemes will experience a pause in automated investment execution. No new funds can be routed to these schemes through systematic routes. However, liquidity remains intact as redemptions and transfers out of these funds are unaffected.
- **Market/Operations Impact:** Intermediaries and the NSE NMF TM segment must update their backend systems to prevent order placements for the suspended transactions.
