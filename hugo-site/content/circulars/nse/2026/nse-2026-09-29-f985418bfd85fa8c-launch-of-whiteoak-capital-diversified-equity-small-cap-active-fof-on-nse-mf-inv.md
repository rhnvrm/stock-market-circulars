## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in ISIN for the Whiteoak Capital Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform with immediate effect, based on communication from Whiteoak Capital Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Applies to Whiteoak Capital Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF NFO.
- Immediate implementation on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Previous ISINs: INF1J2R01189, INF1J2R01197.
- Revised ISINs: INF03VN01AN2, INF03VN01AO0.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an administrative update of security identification numbers (ISIN).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and participants must update their systems with the revised ISIN codes for transactions related to the scheme.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal, requiring only internal database and system updates for the new ISINs.
