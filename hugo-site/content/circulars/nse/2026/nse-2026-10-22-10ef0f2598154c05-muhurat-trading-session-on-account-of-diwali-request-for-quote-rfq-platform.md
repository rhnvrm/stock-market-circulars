## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a special live trading session in the Request for Quote (RFQ) platform on Sunday, November 08, 2026, on account of Diwali Muhurat trading for Corporate Bonds (T+1 settlement).

## Key Points

- Special live trading session in RFQ platform on Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- Applicable only to Corporate Bonds as defined under SEBI (Issue and Listing of Non-Convertible Securities Regulations).
- Market timings:
  - 14:05 hrs to 15:00 hrs: Trading, Confirmation, Modification, Cancellation
  - 15:00 hrs to 15:30 hrs: Confirmation, Modification, Cancellation
- All trades executed during the session shall result in settlement obligations (T+1 settlement).
- T+1 deals can be cancelled/modified till 5 PM on the settlement day.
- CBRICS Platform shall not be available during the Diwali Muhurat trading session.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a special trading session announcement in continuation of Exchange circular No. NSE/DS/65902 dated December 23, 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must adhere to the trading timings and settlement guidelines specified for the Diwali Muhurat trading session.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 22, 2026
- Special Trading Session Date: November 08, 2026 (Sunday)
- Settlement modification/cancellation deadline: Up to 5 PM on settlement day

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates special trading opportunities for market participants in corporate bonds during the Diwali festival season with defined market hours and settlement rules.
