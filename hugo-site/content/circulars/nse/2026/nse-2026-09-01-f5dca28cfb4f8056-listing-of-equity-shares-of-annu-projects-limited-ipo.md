## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Annu Projects Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The securities will be admitted to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment with effect from September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: ANNU
- Company Name: Annu Projects Limited
- Series: BE (subsequently to be shifted to Series EQ as per SEBI guidelines)
- ISIN: INE103001017
- Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 99/-
- Total No. of Securities: 65,492,670 equity shares
- Market Lot: 1
- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will trade in Series 'BE' initially and shift to 'EQ' as per SEBI circular nos. CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012 & CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.
- Subject to pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are required to note the designated security codes and trading lot sizes specified in the exchange annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 01, 2026
- Effective Date/Listing Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Annu Projects Limited equity shares on the NSE capital market segment.
