## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the appointment of an additional market maker for Studio LSD Limited (STUDIOLSD) in the Capital Market Segment.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Studio LSD Limited
- **Symbol:** STUDIOLSD
- **Current Market Maker:** Rikhav Securities Limited (Member Code - 12804)
- **Additional Market Maker:** Aftertrade Broking Private Limited (Member Code - 90113)
- **Effective Date:** October 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational circular regarding market maker appointments for SME securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the concerned market makers should take note of the additional market maker appointment.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances liquidity and market depth for Studio LSD Limited shares on the SME platform.
