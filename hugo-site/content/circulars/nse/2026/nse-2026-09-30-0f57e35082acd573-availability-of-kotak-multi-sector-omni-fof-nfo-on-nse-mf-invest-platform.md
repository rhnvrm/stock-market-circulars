## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced that the Kotak Multi Sector Omni FOF of Kotak Mutual Fund will be available for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, along with SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective October 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: Kotak Multi Sector Omni FOF (covering various Direct and Regular Plans across Growth and IDCW options).
- Effective Date: October 1, 2026.
- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Supported Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure correct scheme codes and ISINs are used when executing transactions on behalf of clients.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless investment operations and systematic investment planning for investors in the Kotak Multi Sector Omni FOF through NSE's platform.
