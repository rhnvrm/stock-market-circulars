## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular (Ref No: NSE/CML/75454, Circular Ref No: 1242/2026) dated July 29, 2026, announcing the listing and admission to dealings of further privately placed debt securities on its Capital Market segment, effective from July 30, 2026. This notification specifies the designated symbols, trading series, lot sizes, and ISIN codes of the newly listed instruments.

## Key Points

- **Admissions to Dealings**: NSE has approved the listing of privately placed debt securities for trading on the Capital Market segment starting July 30, 2026.
- **Designated Lot Sizes**: Pursuant to trading regulations, the market lot size for trading on the system for all these debt securities is set at 1.
- **Security Identification**: For system trading, each security will be identified solely by its designated symbol and traded in specified lot sizes.
- **Detailed Instrument Listing**:
  1. **Summit Digitel Infrastructure Limited**:
     - **Symbol**: 7505SDIL31 (Series N0, Market Lot: 1)
     - **Description**: SDIL 7.505% 2031
     - **Coupon Rate**: 7.51% (7.505% in description)
     - **Maturity Date**: June 30, 2031
     - **ISIN**: INE507T07211
  2. **Sundaram Finance Limited**:
     - **Symbol**: 774SFL28 (Series N0, Market Lot: 1)
     - **Description**: SFL 7.74% 2028 Sr AA2
     - **Coupon Rate**: 7.74%
     - **Maturity Date**: July 27, 2028
     - **ISIN**: INE660A07SF1
  3. **Western Capital Advisors Private Limited**:
     - **Symbol**: 1075WCAP29 (Series N0, Market Lot: 1)
     - **Description**: WCAPL10.75% 2029
     - **Coupon Rate**: 10.75%
     - **Maturity Date**: January 28, 2029
     - **ISIN**: INE0A3007081

## Regulatory Changes

- **Applicable Regulations**: The admission is conducted under **Regulation 3.1.1** (admission to dealings) and the market lot size is mandated under **Regulation 2.5.5** of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A. There are no new regulatory amendments; this is a standard application of the existing framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Identification**: Trading members must configure their front-end and back-office systems to recognize these instruments using only their designated symbols (`7505SDIL31`, `774SFL28`, and `1075WCAP29`).
- **Lot Size Restrictions**: All orders entered into the system must comply with the minimum/standard market lot size of 1.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Effective/Listing Date**: July 30, 2026
- **Maturity Dates**:
  - Summit Digitel Infrastructure Limited Bond: June 30, 2031
  - Sundaram Finance Limited Bond: July 27, 2028
  - Western Capital Advisors Private Limited Bond: January 28, 2029

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. This represents a routine administrative addition of privately placed debt instruments to the capital market segment, enhancing liquidity options for debt investors but with no systemic market impact.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Standard operational procedure for brokerages and clearing corporations to update security master directories with the new symbols, ISINs, and lot sizes for seamless execution and settlement.
