## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Triveni Power Transmission Limited pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement with Triveni Engineering & Industries Limited. A total of 10,47,54,338 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each will be traded starting October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Triveni Power Transmission Limited
- Symbol: TRIVENIPT
- ISIN: INE2WKE01011
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/- each
- Number of Securities: 10,47,54,338
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 104754338
- Market Lot: 1
- Pari Passu: Yes
- Series: BE (Trade for Trade), to be subsequently shifted to Series 'EQ' as per SEBI circular guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission to dealings under Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- Lot sizes determined under Regulation 2.5.5.
- Securities to be part of the pre-open session as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.
- Trading in Series 'BE' with subsequent shifting to Series 'EQ' pursuant to SEBI circular dated September 2, 2010.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the 'BE' series trade-for-trade restriction and ensure compliance with trading regulations and pre-open session norms.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date (Admission to Dealings): October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading and market liquidity for shareholders holding equity shares of Triveni Power Transmission Limited resulting from the NCLT-approved Scheme of Arrangement.
