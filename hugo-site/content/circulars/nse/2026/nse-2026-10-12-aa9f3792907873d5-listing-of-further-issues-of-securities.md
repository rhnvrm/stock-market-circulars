## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers further issuance of equity shares for companies including 3i Infotech Limited, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, Indo Thai Securities Limited, Kirloskar Oil Engines Limited, Kirloskar Industries Limited, Mahindra Holidays & Resorts India Limited, Polycab India Limited, PTC Industries Limited, Religare Enterprises Limited, and VA Tech Wabag Limited.
- Shares are allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and preferential issues.
- Trading effective date: October 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities shall be identified by designated codes and traded in specified market lots.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate action reflecting capital expansion through stock options and preferential issues for the listed entities.
