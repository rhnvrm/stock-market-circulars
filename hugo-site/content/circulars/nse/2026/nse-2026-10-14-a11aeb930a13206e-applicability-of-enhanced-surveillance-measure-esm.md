## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Mawana Sugars Limited has been shortlisted under ESM Stage I, attracting a minimum 100% margin and shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- **Mawana Sugars Limited (MAWANASUG)** is shortlisted under ESM Stage I w.e.f. October 16, 2026.
- Affected securities attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on October 15, 2026, and new positions created from October 16, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 16, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 15, 2026.
- Consolidated list includes securities such as ACS Technologies Limited (ACSTECH), Apsis Aerocom Limited (APSISAERO), and Bafna Pharmaceuticals Limited (BAFNAPH) under Stage II.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shortlisting of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Imposition of 100% margin requirements on open and new positions.
- Transfer of securities from rolling settlement to trade-for-trade settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure 100% margin collection for impacted securities.
- Market participants must account for the shift in settlement series from EQ/SM to BE/ST.

## Important Dates

- **October 15, 2026:** Reference date for open positions / Stage II Periodic Call Auction effective date.
- **October 16, 2026:** Effective date for 100% margin applicability and shifting from EQ/SM to BE/ST.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading liquidity and operational settlement for shortlisted securities are impacted due to strict margin norms and trade-for-trade restrictions.
