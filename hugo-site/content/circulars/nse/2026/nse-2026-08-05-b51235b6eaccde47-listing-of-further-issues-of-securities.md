## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has approved the listing and trading of further issues of securities on the Capital Market segment, effective from August 06, 2026. The circular covers equity shares issued under Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOP) by 15 companies, including Affle 3i Limited, Ajanta Pharma Limited, Arvind Fashions Limited, CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited, and The Federal Bank Limited.

## Key Points

- **Admissions to Dealings**: Multiple listings of further equity shares have been admitted to trading on the NSE Capital Market segment with effect from August 06, 2026.
- **Regulations**: Issued under Regulation 3.1.1 (for admission of securities to dealings) and Regulation 2.5.5 (for respective lot sizes) of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
- **ESOP Allotments detailed in Annexure**:
  - **Affle 3i Limited (AFFLE)**: 60,000 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted on July 25, 2026 (No lock-in).
  - **Ajanta Pharma Limited (AJANTPHARM)**: 23,400 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted on July 30, 2026 (Lock-in until July 30, 2027).
  - **Arvind Fashions Limited (ARVINDFASN)**: 12,000 equity shares of Rs. 4/- each allotted on July 23, 2026 (No lock-in).
  - **CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited (CGPOWER)**: 218,150 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted on July 30, 2026 (No lock-in).
  - **The Federal Bank Limited (FEDERALBNK)**: 719,066 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each allotted on August 03, 2026 (No lock-in).
  - **HDB Financial Services Limited (HDBFS)**: Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under ESOP (details truncated in circular text).

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes. This circular represents routine compliance and administrative notifications under existing NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

No specific action is required from general trading members or retail investors. The listed companies must ensure all procedural conditions for listing further shares under ESOP have been met.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 05, 2026
- **Effective Date of Trading**: August 06, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**:
  - Arvind Fashions Limited: July 23, 2026
  - Affle 3i Limited: July 25, 2026
  - Ajanta Pharma Limited: July 30, 2026
  - CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited: July 30, 2026
  - The Federal Bank Limited: August 03, 2026
- **Lock-in Period (Ajanta Pharma)**: July 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Extremely low. These are routine additions to the share capital of the respective listed companies.
- **Liquidity Impact**: Very minor increase in the floating stock of the respective shares, which is unlikely to significantly affect trading prices.
