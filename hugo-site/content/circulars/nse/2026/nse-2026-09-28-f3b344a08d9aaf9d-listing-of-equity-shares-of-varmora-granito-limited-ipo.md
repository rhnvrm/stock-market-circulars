## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Varmora Granito Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO). Trading will commence effective September 29, 2026, under symbol VARMORA.

## Key Points

- Symbol: VARMORA
- Company Name: Varmora Granito Limited
- ISIN: INE0M5301040
- Issue Price: Rs. 148/- per share
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 2/- each
- Total Securities Admitted: 226,011,824 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 29, 2026
- Lock-in expiration dates vary (ranging from October 24, 2026 to September 28, 2029, with certain shares free).

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Varmora Granito Limited shares on NSE, expanding investment opportunities and providing market liquidity for shareholders.
