## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar and guidelines for the Takeover Offer under the Tender Offer scheme by Onyx Biotec Limited. Trading for the takeover offer is scheduled from October 13, 2026, to October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Offer Details**: Takeover Offer under Tender Offer scheme for Onyx Biotec Limited (Reference circulars NCL/CMPT/73996 and NSE/CML/76764).
- **Settlement Type**: X
- **Settlement Number**: 2026120
- **Trade Start Date**: 13-OCT-2026
- **Trade End Date**: 27-OCT-2026
- **Obligation Download Date**: 09-NOV-2026
- **Settlement Date**: 11-NOV-2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishment of official settlement schedule and timelines for the tender offer mechanism regarding Onyx Biotec Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be tendered exclusively by placing Early Pay-in of Shares via the early pay-in mechanism of depositories for Market Type Buyback.
- On the trade end date, early pay-in of shares must be completed well in advance for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m.
- Securities and funds pay-out will be processed post receipt of funds pay-in on the Settlement Day.
- Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.
- Members should review the Tender Offer Acceptance Report for order status (accepted, partially accepted, rejected) along with rejection reason codes.

## Important Dates

- **Trade Start Date**: October 13, 2026
- **Trade End Date**: October 27, 2026
- **Obligation Download Date**: November 9, 2026
- **Settlement Date**: November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Clearing members, custodians, and trading participants involved in the Onyx Biotec Limited takeover offer must adhere to the specified settlement calendar, early pay-in deadlines, and STT collection guidelines to ensure smooth execution and avoid settlement delays.
