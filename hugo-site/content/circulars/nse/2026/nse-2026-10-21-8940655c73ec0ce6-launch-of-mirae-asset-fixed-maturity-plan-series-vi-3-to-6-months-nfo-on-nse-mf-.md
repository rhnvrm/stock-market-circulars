## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the launch and availability of the Mirae Asset Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VI - 3 to 6 Months NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from October 22, 2026, to October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Schemes Included:** Four variants including Direct and Regular plans for Growth and IDCW Payout options (ISINs: INF769K01RZ1, INF769K01SA2, INF769K01RX6, INF769K01RY4).
- **Transaction Window:** Available for subscription from October 22, 2026 to October 27, 2026.
- **Allotment Date:** Scheduled for October 28, 2026.
- **Fund Category:** Debt.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 27, 2026, till 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** October 22, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** October 27, 2026 (Funds clearance by 03:00 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to new debt mutual fund fixed maturity plans on the NSE platform with standard operational timelines.
