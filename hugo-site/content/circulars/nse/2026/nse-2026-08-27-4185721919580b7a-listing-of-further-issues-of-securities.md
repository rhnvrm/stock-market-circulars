## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of securities for several companies effective from August 28, 2026. These listings primarily comprise equity shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP).

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026.
- Covers multiple companies including 360 ONE WAM Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Axis Bank Limited, and others.
- Securities admitted are part of the Capital Market segment under respective designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up capital and listed equity shares for the respective companies with minimal broader market impact.
