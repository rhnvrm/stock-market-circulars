## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the resumption of subscriptions in Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua Fund of Fund under Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective September 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Subscriptions, lumpsum purchases, additional purchases, and switch-ins have resumed for Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua Fund of Fund.
- Fresh registrations of Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) are now accepted.
- This follows the earlier temporary suspension (Notice no - NSE/NMFTM/75820 / Notice-cum-Addendum no. 73/2026) enacted to avoid breaching overseas investment limits.
- The AMC reserves the right to suspend subscriptions again if headroom limits are approached.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a scheme-specific operational update.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must adhere to the standard terms, conditions, and minimum application amounts specified in the Scheme Information Document (SID).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 1, 2026
- Notice Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors can resume regular investment activities including SIPs and lumpsum purchases in the Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua Fund of Fund through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
