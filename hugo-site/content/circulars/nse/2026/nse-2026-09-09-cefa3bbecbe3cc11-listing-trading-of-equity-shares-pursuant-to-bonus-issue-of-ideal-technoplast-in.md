## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and commencement of trading for equity shares of Ideal Technoplast Industries Limited pursuant to a bonus issue. A total of 50,00,000 equity shares will be admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective from September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ideal Technoplast Industries Limited
- Symbol: IDEALTECHO
- ISIN: INE0T9I01011
- Number of Bonus Securities: 50,00,000
- Distinctive Number Range: 5000001 to 10000000
- Record Date: 12-Sep-2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: 15-Sep-2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the National Stock Exchange pursuant to a corporate action (bonus issue).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure that trading is conducted using the designated security codes and ISIN provided.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 12, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date for Listing and Trading: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the total listed and traded equity shares of Ideal Technoplast Industries Limited, improving liquidity and distributing bonus shares to eligible shareholders.
