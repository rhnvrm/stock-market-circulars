## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the migration and listing of equity shares of Sudarshan Pharma Industries Limited from the SME Platform of BSE to the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) of NSE. Trading will be effective from October 27, 2026 under the symbol SUDARSHAN with a total of 254,470,600 equity shares admitted to dealings.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Sudarshan Pharma Industries Limited
- **Symbol:** SUDARSHAN
- **ISIN:** INE00TV01023
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Re. 1/- per share fully paid up
- **Total No. of Securities:** 254,470,600 equity shares
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 1 to 254470600
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Pari Passu:** Yes
- **Effective Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Segment:** Normal Market segment (Rolling Settlement) in compulsory demat
- **Registrar:** Kfin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in equity shares of Sudarshan Pharma Industries Limited is migrated from the SME Platform of BSE to the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) of NSE pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities shall be traded in the Normal Market segment (Rolling Settlement) in compulsory demat for all investors.
- Lock-in details as per Annexure I:
  - 128,226,490 shares (Distinctive Nos. 1 to 128226490) locked in up to 31-Dec-2027.
  - 9,000,000 shares (Distinctive Nos. 128226491 to 137226490) locked in up to 20-Feb-2028.
  - 117,244,110 shares (Distinctive Nos. 137226491 to 254470600) are free.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Effective / Listing Date:** October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables broader investor participation and enhanced liquidity for Sudarshan Pharma Industries Limited as it transitions from the SME platform to the Main Board of NSE.
