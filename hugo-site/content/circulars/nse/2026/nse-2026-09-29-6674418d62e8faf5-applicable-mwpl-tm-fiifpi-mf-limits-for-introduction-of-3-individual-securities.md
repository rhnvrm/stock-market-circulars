## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the applicable market wide position limits (MWPL), trading member-wise position limits, FII/FPI (Category I & II), mutual fund position limits, trading member proprietary limits, and client level limits for the introduction of 3 individual securities. These limits are effective from trade date September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities covered: ANANDRATHI, ENRIN, and UJJIVANSFB.
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026.
- Issued pursuant to previous circulars nos. 050/2026 (NCL/CMPT/73997) and 108/2026 (NCL/CMPT/76459).
- Details specified include Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL), Trading Member Stock Limits, Client Limits, FII/FPI Category I & MF limits, and Category II FPI limits.

## Regulatory Changes

Establishes specific position limits and regulatory thresholds for trading derivative contracts on the newly introduced individual securities in accordance with NSE Clearing guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with the prescribed market-wide, trading member, client, FII/FPI, and mutual fund position limits for the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly trading and risk management for the introduction of new derivative contracts across the three securities without exceeding regulatory position thresholds.
