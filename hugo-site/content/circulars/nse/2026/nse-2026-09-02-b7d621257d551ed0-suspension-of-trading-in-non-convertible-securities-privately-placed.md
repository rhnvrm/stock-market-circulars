## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) for three entities effective September 3, 2026, on account of their redemption.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects privately placed non-convertible securities of three issuers.
- Effective date of suspension: September 3, 2026.
- Reason for suspension: Redemption of securities.

### Suspended Securities Details

1. **Bank Of Baroda** | ISIN: INE077A08098 | Suspension Date: 03-September-2026 | Reason: Redemption
2. **Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited** | ISIN: INE033L07IC6 | Suspension Date: 03-September-2026 | Reason: Redemption
3. **IDFC First Bank Limited** | ISIN: INE092T08DM3 | Suspension Date: 03-September-2026 | Reason: Redemption

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the suspension of trading for the specified ISINs and ensure no further trades are executed post the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 02, 2026
- **Suspension Date:** September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational procedure following the redemption of privately placed debt securities. No broader market impact.
