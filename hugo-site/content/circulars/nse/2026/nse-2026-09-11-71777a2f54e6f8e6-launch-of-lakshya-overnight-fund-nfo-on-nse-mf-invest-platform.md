## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the Lakshya Overnight Fund NFO on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window is open from September 15, 2026, to September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund Name: Lakshya Mutual Fund - Lakshya Overnight Fund
- Available Plans: Direct Plan - Growth and Regular Plan - Growth
- ISINs:
  - Direct Plan Growth: INF2ZPR01014
  - Regular Plan Growth: INF2ZPR01022
- NFO Window: September 15, 2026 to September 18, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 21, 2026, till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: September 15, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: September 18, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: September 21, 2026
- Clear Funds Submission Deadline: September 21, 2026 (12:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the subscription of the new overnight fund scheme through the NSE platform.
