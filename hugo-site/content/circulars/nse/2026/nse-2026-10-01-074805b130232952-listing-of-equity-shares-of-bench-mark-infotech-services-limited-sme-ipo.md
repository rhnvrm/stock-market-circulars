## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Bench Mark Infotech Services Limited on the EMERGE SME Platform. Trading will commence on October 05, 2026, under the symbol BMISL.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bench Mark Infotech Services Limited
- Symbol: BMISL
- ISIN: INE2OKU01014
- Issue Price: Rs. 110/- per share
- Face Value / Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/-
- Total No. of Securities: 14,272,650
- Market Lot: Minimum 1200 Equity Shares
- Trading Segment: Series 'ST' (Trade for Trade Surveillance Segment - Settlement Type 'W') initially, shifting to Series 'SM' (Normal Rolling Segment).

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of further securities to dealings on the NSE Capital Market segment EMERGE SME Platform pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are required to note the designated security codes, market lot sizes, and trading in pre-open session for IPO scrips as per relevant SEBI and Exchange circulars.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members can trade in the equity shares of Bench Mark Infotech Services Limited on the NSE Emerge platform under the specified trading series and lot size parameters.
