## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of further issue of equity shares of TIL Limited effective from August 17, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TIL Limited
- Symbol: TIL
- ISIN: INE806C01018
- Number of Securities: 21,55,557 equity shares
- Reason for Issue: Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares
- Effective Date: August 17, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Securities allotted in dematerialized mode were previously credited under temporary ISIN (IN8806C01017) and are now being transitioned in accordance with SEBI guidelines for additional issues.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026
- Circular Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital structure update for TIL Limited involving the listing of converted fully paid-up shares with no broader market impact.
