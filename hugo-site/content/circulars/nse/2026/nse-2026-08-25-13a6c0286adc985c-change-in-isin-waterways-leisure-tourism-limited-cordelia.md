## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in ISIN code for the equity shares of Waterways Leisure Tourism Limited (CORDELIA) effective from August 25, 2026, on account of a sub-division of shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Waterways Leisure Tourism Limited
- Symbol: CORDELIA
- Action: Change in ISIN due to Sub-Division from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-
- New ISIN: INE0LZF01039

## Regulatory Changes

- ISIN code updated to reflect the sub-division of equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are requested to take note of the new ISIN for all trades in the scrip.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Ex-date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact limited to updating security master files and trading systems with the new ISIN code for subsequent settlements.
