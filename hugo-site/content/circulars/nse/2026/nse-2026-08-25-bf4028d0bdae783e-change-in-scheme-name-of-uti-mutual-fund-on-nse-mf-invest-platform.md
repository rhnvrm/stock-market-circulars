## Summary

National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme names for various schemes of UTI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from August 26, 2026, pursuant to SEBI norms on categorization and rationalization.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Platform: NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Fund House: UTI Mutual Fund.
- Multiple debt, equity, and hybrid scheme names have been updated to align with revised SEBI categorization norms.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revised norms for categorization and rationalization of mutual fund schemes as per SEBI master circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors to take note of the updated scheme names for trading and reporting purposes on the platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; standard scheme nomenclature update on trading platforms.
