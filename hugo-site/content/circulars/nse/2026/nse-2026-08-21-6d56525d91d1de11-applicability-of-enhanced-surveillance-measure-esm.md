## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Several securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion, shifting stages, or moving out of the framework, attracting a minimum 100% margin and trade-for-trade settlement.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I shortlisted under ESM Stage I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 25, 2026, on open positions as on August 24, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 25, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 24, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The ESM framework is applied in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures. Securities shortlisted in Annexure I include BULKCORP, KAYA, METALIC, MYSORPETRO, and SIGIND for Stage I, UHTL moving to Stage II, and BAFNAPH moving from Stage II to Stage I.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement specifications for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage I/II shifts and margin requirements: August 24, 2026 / August 25, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement for the impacted securities will be restricted due to the 100% margin requirement and shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment.
