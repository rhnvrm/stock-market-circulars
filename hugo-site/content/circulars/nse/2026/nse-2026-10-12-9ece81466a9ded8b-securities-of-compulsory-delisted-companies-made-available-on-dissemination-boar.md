## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced that Atcom Technologies Limited will be moved to the Dissemination Board (DB) of the Exchange with effect from October 14, 2026, following its compulsory delisting.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Atcom Technologies Limited
- Symbol: ATCOM
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026
- Board: Dissemination Board (DB) of NSE

## Regulatory Changes

- Shares are made available for buying and selling on the Dissemination Board in terms of Regulation 32(1) of SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021, and related regulatory provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants take note of the transfer of the security to the Dissemination Board.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Restricted impact affecting shareholders of Atcom Technologies Limited, providing a mechanism for buying and selling on the Dissemination Board.
