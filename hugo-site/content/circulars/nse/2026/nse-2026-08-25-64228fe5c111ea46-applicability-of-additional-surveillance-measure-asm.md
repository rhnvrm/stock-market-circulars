## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Securities listed in the Annexure have satisfied criteria for inclusion under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM).

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. August 28, 2026, on all open positions as on August 27, 2026, and new positions created from August 28, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. August 28, 2026.
- Shortlisted securities include DIGJAMLMTD, EBIX, ONIXSOLAR, SHILPAMED, and VISHWARAJ under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements and potential shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment for identified securities under the ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities effective from August 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for 100% Margin: August 28, 2026 (on open positions as on August 27, 2026 and new positions thereafter)
- Circular Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the shortlisted securities will be subject to stricter margin constraints, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for these scrips.
