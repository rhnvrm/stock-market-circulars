## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding SEBI direction pursuant to the Hon'ble SAT order dated August 10, 2026, in the matter of Madhav Stock Vision Pvt. Ltd. and Others. The appellants have paid the penalty and disgorgement amount in favor of SEBI, leading to a stay on debarment subject to compliance with the SAT order.

## Key Points

- References previous NSE Circular No. NSE/INVG/75403 dated July 24, 2026, regarding SEBI Order No. WTM/AS/ISD/ISD-SEC-4/32507/2026-27.
- Noticees involved include Madhav Stock Vision Pvt. Ltd., Ajay Sampatraj Jain, and Rajkumar Prabhu Damani.
- Hon’ble SAT vide order dated August 10, 2026, noted the payment of penalty and disgorgement amount by the appellants.
- SEBI vide email dated August 18, 2026, directed a stay on debarment subject to deposit of the entire penalty and disgorgement amount.

## Regulatory Changes

- Stay on the market debarment previously imposed on Madhav Stock Vision Pvt. Ltd. and associated noticees, following deposit of penalties and disgorgements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the stay on debarment and ensure compliance as per the attached SAT order and consolidated list of debarred/stayed entities on the Exchange website.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: August 10, 2026
- SEBI Direction Date: August 18, 2026
- Circular Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Clarifies the current regulatory and trading status of the named entities following deposit of penalties and SAT relief.
