## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Futures & Options (F&O) segment on Saturday, October 10, 2026. No new software version release is scheduled for this mock session, and NEATPlus version 7.9.0 remains mandatory while version 7.8.9 is discontinued.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 10, 2026, across primary and DR sites.
- Trading Session-1 from Primary Site runs from 11:00 hours to 14:00 hours.
- Trading Session-2 from Disaster Recovery (DR) Site runs from 14:45 hours to 16:20 hours.
- Live re-login timings are from 18:30 hours to 19:00 hours.
- NEATPlus version 7.9.0 is mandatory; version 7.8.9 is discontinued effective October 10, 2026.
- Pre-Trade risk controls regarding pre-emptive order cancellation on account of NNF ID-Algo ID validations will be available for testing and effective live from October 12, 2026.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of pre-trade risk controls (pre-emptive order cancellation on account of NNF ID-Algo ID validations) effective October 12, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are required to participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 12, 2026.
- Ensure UCC/PAN validity and compliance before cutoff.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 10, 2026
- NEATPlus Version 7.8.9 Discontinuation Date: October 10, 2026
- Pre-Trade Risk Controls Effective Date (LIVE): October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness check for members to ensure seamless connectivity and system stability for live trading on October 12, 2026.
