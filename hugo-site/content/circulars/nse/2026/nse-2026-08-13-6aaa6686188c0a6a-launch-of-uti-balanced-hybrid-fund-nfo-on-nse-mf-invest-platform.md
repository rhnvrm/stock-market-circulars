## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of the UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund NFO of UTI Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform for investor transactions.

## Key Points

- **Subscription Period:** Available from August 14, 2026, to August 28, 2026 (till 11:59 pm).
- **Schemes Included:** Regular Growth, Direct Growth, Regular Payout IDCW, and Direct Payout IDCW.
- **Allotment Date:** September 02, 2026.
- **Fund Details:** 
  - UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund - Regular Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AH00)
  - UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund - Direct Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AH18)
  - UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund - Regular Payout IDCW (ISIN: INF789F1AH26)
  - UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund - Direct Payout IDCW (ISIN: INF789F1AH34)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 02, 2026, till 06:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** August 14, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** August 28, 2026 (11:59 pm)
- **Allotment Date:** September 02, 2026
- **Funded Payout / Clear Funds Deadline:** September 02, 2026 (06:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to transact in the new fund offer seamlessly through the NSE MF Invest Platform.
