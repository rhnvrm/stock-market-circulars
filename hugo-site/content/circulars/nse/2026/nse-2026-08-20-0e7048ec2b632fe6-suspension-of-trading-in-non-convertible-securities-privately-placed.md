## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the suspension of trading in privately placed non-convertible securities for TVS Motor Company Limited and Toyota Financial Services India Limited, effective August 21, 2026, following redemption.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.
- Affected Entities:
  1. TVS Motor Company Limited (ISIN: INE494B04019)
  2. Toyota Financial Services India Limited (ISIN: INE692Q07449)
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must take note of the suspension of trading for the specified ISINs.

## Important Dates

- Suspension Date: 21-August-2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified non-convertible securities of the listed companies will be halted on the NSE debt market segment as they have reached redemption.
