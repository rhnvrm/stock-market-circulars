## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular reminding active trading members using algorithmic software to submit reporting for Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) applications and systems. The submission is mandatory for all active trading members who executed even a single trade using algorithm software during the half-year period from April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026, regardless of whether AI-ML systems are utilized or not.

## Key Points

- Mandatory submission for all active Trading Members using Algorithm software between April 01, 2026, and September 30, 2026.
- Members not using AI-ML systems are required to submit a NIL report.
- Reporting must be completed through the ENIT portal under ENIT-New-Compliance > COMPLIANCE > ML and AI > ML and AI Details.
- Submission window opens on October 01, 2026, and closes on October 15, 2026.
- Non-compliance or failure to report within the deadline will attract disciplinary action as per Exchange circular NSE/INSP/73792 dated April 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforces reporting obligations in line with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DOS2/CIR/P/2019/10 and Exchange circular NSE/COMP/59700.
- Standardized electronic reporting workflow introduced via the ENIT interface.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members utilizing algorithmic trading must access the ENIT portal and submit detailed application/system names or file a NIL return.
- Submissions must be successfully completed on or before October 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Period of assessment: April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Submission start date: October 01, 2026.
- Submission deadline: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Algorithmic trading members must maintain accurate records of any AI/ML tools integrated into their trading systems and ensure timely compliance filings via ENIT to avoid regulatory penalties.
