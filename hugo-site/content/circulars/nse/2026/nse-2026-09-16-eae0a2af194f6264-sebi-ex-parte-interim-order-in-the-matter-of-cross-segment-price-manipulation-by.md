## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding SEBI's Ex-Parte Interim Order (Order No. WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-TPD2/32723/2026-27 dated September 16, 2026) in the matter of cross segment price manipulation by Prrsaar Sampada Private Limited, Chaubara Eats Private Limited, and associated individuals. SEBI has restrained the noticees from accessing the securities market until further orders.

## Key Points

- Noticees restrained from accessing the securities market.
- Entities involved: Prrsaar Sampada Private Limited, Ved Prakash Gupta, Priti Gupta, Chaubara Eats Private Limited, Saroj Gupta, and Gaurav Tomar.
- Permission granted to close out/square off open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts within 3 months or upon expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Entities permitted to settle pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions executed before the close of trading on the date of the order.

## Regulatory Changes

SEBI interim restraint under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11B(1), and 11D of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the debarred entities and ensure compliance by restricting access and monitoring positions as per the order.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 16, 2026
- Square-off Window: Within 3 months from the date of order or at the contract expiry, whichever is earlier.

## Impact Assessment

High regulatory impact on the specified entities and increased vigilance by exchanges regarding cross-segment price manipulation.
