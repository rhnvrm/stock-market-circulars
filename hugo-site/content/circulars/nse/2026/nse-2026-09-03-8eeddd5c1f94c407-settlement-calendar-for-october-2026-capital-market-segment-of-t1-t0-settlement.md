## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedules for the Capital Market Segment for October 2026. The schedule covers multiple settlement types including Normal Rolling (T+1) type M, Normal Auction type A, Trade for Trade (T+1) type Z, ITP for SME (T+1) type B, and Normal Rolling (T+0) type 5 in pursuance of Regulation 6.2 of the NSE Clearing Limited (Capital Market Regulations, 1997).

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules for October 2026 for the Capital Market Segment.
- Includes Normal Rolling (T+1) settlement type M (Annexure A).
- Includes Normal Auction settlement type A (Annexure B).
- Includes Trade for Trade (T+1) settlement type Z (Annexure C).
- Includes ITP for SME (T+1) settlement type B (Annexure D).
- Includes Normal Rolling (T+0) settlement type 5 (Annexure E).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine monthly schedule issuance.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members, custodians, and PCMs must adhere to the specified trade dates, custodial confirmation dates, and settlement dates outlined in the annexures for October 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 03, 2026
- Settlement Period: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures seamless operational planning and settlement execution for market participants for trading activities conducted during October 2026.
