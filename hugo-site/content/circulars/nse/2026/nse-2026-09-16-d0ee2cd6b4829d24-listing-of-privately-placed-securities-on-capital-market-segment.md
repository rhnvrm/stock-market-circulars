## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment with effect from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities from Nomura Capital (India) Private Limited, Hero FinCorp Limited, and Reliance Industries Limited are admitted to dealings.
- Effective date for trading: September 17, 2026.
- Trading will take place using designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Notification issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure trades are executed using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2026
- Maturity Dates: October 29, 2027 (Nomura Capital), September 15, 2028 (Hero FinCorp), September 16, 2031 (Reliance Industries)

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading for the newly listed privately placed securities.
