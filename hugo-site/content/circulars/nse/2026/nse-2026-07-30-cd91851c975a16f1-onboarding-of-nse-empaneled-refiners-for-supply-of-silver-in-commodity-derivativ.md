## Summary

In continuation of NSE Circular No. NSE/COM/73215 dated March 10, 2026, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has approved the onboarding of NSE-empaneled gold refiners (conforming to BIS standards) to supply silver for the settlement of NSE Silver derivative contracts. This initiative is designed to enhance participation in the physical silver settlement ecosystem and strengthen the overall commodity derivatives segment.

## Key Points

- **Eligibility**: Refiners already empaneled under the NSE Refiner Standards (NRS) for Gold Bars and Coins conforming to BIS Standards are now permitted to supply silver for settlement of NSE Silver derivative contracts.
- **Standards**: Onboarded refiners must ensure silver bars and coins conform to all applicable NSE product specifications (including purity, weight, dimensions, markings, packaging, traceability, and operational standards).
- **Exchange Rights**: NSE reserves the authority to approve, reject, suspend, or withdraw any refiner's onboarding based on compliance, risk, or regulatory considerations.
- **Making Charges**: Making charges (exclusive of taxes/levies) for silver supplied through these refiners are specified as:
  - **Silver Futures (30kgs)**: Nil
  - **Silver Mini Futures (5kgs)**: Rs. 400/- per bar of 1kg
  - **Silver 100 grams Futures**: Rs. 400/- per bar of 100 grams

## Regulatory Changes

- This decision extends the scope of the NSE Refiner Standards (NRS), allowing gold-empaneled refiners to participate in the silver delivery mechanism.
- Integrates the silver supply framework with the existing BIS-conforming gold refiner standard framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Onboarded refiners must ensure compliance with responsible sourcing, quality assurance, BIS standards, and audit requirements.
- They must provide any additional information or documentation requested by the Exchange and permit physical inspections if deemed necessary.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: Immediate effect (July 30, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Enhances liquidity and simplifies physical delivery for Silver futures, mini futures, and micro (100 grams) futures.
- **Operational Impact**: Expands the pool of eligible refiners for the silver delivery ecosystem, lowering operational bottlenecks for physical delivery.
