## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, lumpsum investments, switch-ins, and new registrations for Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) in Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua Fund of Fund under Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** August 19, 2026.
- **Suspended Transactions:** Lumpsum subscriptions, switch-ins, and fresh registrations of SIPs and STPs into the Scheme are not accepted.
- **Unaffected Transactions:** Existing SIP/STP instalments registered on or before August 18, 2026, redemptions, switch-outs, SWPs, and intra-scheme/intra-plan switches remain unaffected.
- **Reason:** To avoid potential breach of overseas investment limits set by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adherence to SEBI directives regarding overseas investment limits and headroom availability at the Fund level.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note that transactions received after the cut-off time on August 18, 2026, for lumpsum and fresh SIP/STP registrations will not be processed.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 19, 2026
- **Cut-off for existing/fresh instructions:** August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors wishing to make fresh investments or start new SIPs/STPs in Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua Fund of Fund will be restricted, while existing investors can continue their ongoing systematic plans and redemptions without interruption.
