## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified members regarding the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions in certain schemes under The Wealth Company Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh subscriptions, lumpsum investments (including switches), and fresh registrations of Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) are disabled for The Wealth Company Gold ETF FOF.
- Existing SIPs, STPs, and other systematic transactions registered prior to the effective date will continue to be processed normally at the AMC/RTA end.
- Redemptions, Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP), switch-outs, and STP-outs remain unrestricted.
- Top-ups in existing SIPs/STPs are not allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational restriction on scheme subscription modes as communicated by The Wealth Company Mutual Fund via Notice-cum-Addendum.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors must take note of the subscription restrictions and avoid accepting fresh lumpsum, switch-in, or new SIP/STP applications for the affected scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding existing SIPs/STPs in The Wealth Company Gold ETF FOF are unaffected for ongoing instalments, but new investors or additional lumpsum investments cannot be made until further notice.
