## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed debt securities issued by Care Health Insurance Limited on the Capital Market segment. Effective from August 07, 2026, these securities will be available for trading under the designated codes and market lot sizes as specified by the exchange.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** Care Health Insurance Limited
- **Symbol:** 10CHI36
- **Series:** N0
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Issue Description:** CHI 10% 2036 Sr 1
- **Coupon Rate:** 10%
- **Maturity Date:** August 05, 2036
- **ISIN Code:** INE119J08024

## Regulatory Changes

- No direct regulatory changes. The listing is carried out under the provisions of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure their systems are updated with the new security codes, symbol, and lot sizes before the commencement of trading on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** August 07, 2026
- **Date of Maturity:** August 05, 2036

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine listing of a privately placed debt security on the capital market segment, which will provide secondary market liquidity for the holders of this ISIN.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Trading systems must register the new ISIN (INE119J08024) and trading parameter configurations (Symbol: 10CHI36, Series: N0, Market Lot: 1).
