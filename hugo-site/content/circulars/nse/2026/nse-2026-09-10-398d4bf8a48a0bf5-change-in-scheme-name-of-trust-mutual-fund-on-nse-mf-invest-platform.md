## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in scheme name for Trust Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective September 10, 2026. The change is pursuant to guidelines on Categorization and Rationalization of Mutual Fund Schemes.

## Key Points

- Existing Scheme Name: TRUSTMF Short Duration Fund
- Revised Scheme Name: TRUSTMF Short Term Fund
- The change is immediate and applies across all scheme-related documents including SID, KIM, and SAI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Alignment of nomenclature under the 'Category of Fund' section pursuant to SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- All market participants and platform users must update references to the scheme name in relevant documentation and systems.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact; administrative name change for better alignment with regulatory categories without altering the underlying scheme characteristics.
