## Summary

Swaraj Suiting Limited is migrating its equity shares from the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) SME EMERGE platform to the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) of NSE. The trading of the company's equity shares under symbol `SWARAJ` will commence on the Main Board with effect from August 13, 2026. The company secretary is Mr. Rahul Kumar Verma, and the registrar is Bigshare Services Private Limited.

## Key Points

- **Migration to Main Board:** Swaraj Suiting Limited is migrating from the SME Emerge platform to the Main Board (Capital Market Segment) of the National Stock Exchange of India.
- **Trading Details:** Trading will be admitted under Series `EQ`, ISIN `INE0GMR01016`, face value of Rs. 10/-, and a market lot size of 1.
- **Trading Effective Date:** The migration and admission to dealings on the Main Board will be effective from August 13, 2026.
- **Lock-in and Shareholding:** The total paid-up share capital consists of 26,418,260 equity shares, with specific lock-in details specified under Annexure-I. Shareholding pattern is filed under Regulation 31(1)(b) as of August 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no direct changes to exchange-wide regulations; however, this represents an individual company compliance with the requirements for migration from SME Emerge to the Main Board under the NSE Listing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Members/Brokers:** Market participants and members must adjust trading terminals and front-office systems to reflect the change in trading segment from SME platform to the Main Board for Symbol `SWARAJ` with effect from August 13, 2026.
- **Company Requirements:** Swaraj Suiting Limited has filed its shareholding pattern under Regulation 31(1)(b) as of August 07, 2026, and must continue to fulfill all Main Board listing and disclosure requirements (LODR).

## Important Dates

- **August 07, 2026:** Date of the shareholding pattern filed under Regulation 31(1)(b).
- **August 11, 2026:** Date of the circular publication.
- **August 13, 2026:** Effective date of migration and commencement of trading on the Capital Market Segment (Main Board).
- **Lock-in Release Dates:** Specific portions of locked-in shares will become free on dates ranging from August 16, 2026, to December 10, 2027, as per Annexure I.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Liquidity:** Migration from the SME Emerge platform to the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) typically increases liquidity and makes the stock accessible to a wider investor base, including institutional investors who may have restrictions on trading SME stocks.
- **Lot Size Change:** The market lot size is reduced to 1 (from typical SME lot sizes), which lowers the minimum investment barrier for retail investors.
- **Operational Impact:** High-frequency and standard retail trading systems will now route orders through the Capital Market (Main Board) segment instead of the SME segment.
