## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh issuance of Commercial Papers admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment.
- Issuing companies include CEAT Limited, Apollo Tyres Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, Axis Securities Limited, Bajaj Auto Credit Limited, HSBC Investdirect Financial Services (I) Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Tata Capital Limited, and Godrej Industries Limited.
- Allotment date for the securities is August 24, 2026, with varying maturity and redemption dates ranging from November 2026 to February 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the security codes and ISINs for trading and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 25, 2026
- Redemption Dates: November 20, 2026 to February 22, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing event expanding debt market instruments available for trading.
