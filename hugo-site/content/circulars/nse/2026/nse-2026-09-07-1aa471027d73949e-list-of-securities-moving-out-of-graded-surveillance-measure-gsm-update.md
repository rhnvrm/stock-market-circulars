## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the list of securities moving out of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework w.e.f. September 08, 2026, in furtherance of the earlier circular dated September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities moving out of GSM framework effective September 08, 2026.
- Applicable series and price bands have been revised for the affected securities.
- Affected stocks include Ansal Properties & Infrastructure Limited, Grand Foundry Limited, IL&FS Engineering and Construction Company Limited, Impex Ferro Tech Limited, SKIL Infrastructure Limited, Sab Events & Governance Now Media Limited, and Winsome Yarns Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are removed from the Graded Surveillance Measure framework with updated price bands and series.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and members must note the revised series and price bands applicable to these securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 08, 2026
- Circular Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading parameters such as price bands and series change for the listed securities as they exit the surveillance framework.
