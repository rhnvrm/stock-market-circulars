## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by quant Money Managers Limited under the quant Silver ETF scheme, effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** SILVER001
- **Description:** Units of Rs. 10/- each of quant Mutual Fund - Quant Silver ETF
- **ISIN:** INF966L01EI5
- **Number of Units:** 27,851,994
- **Series:** EQ
- **Date of Allotment:** August 20, 2026
- **Face Value / Paid Up Value:** Rs. 10/- per unit
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 23.84/-
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Market Makers:** Emkay Global Financial Services Limited and Sushil Financial Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Admission of securities is in pursuance of relevant NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 20, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of quant Silver ETF units for exchange participants and investors.
