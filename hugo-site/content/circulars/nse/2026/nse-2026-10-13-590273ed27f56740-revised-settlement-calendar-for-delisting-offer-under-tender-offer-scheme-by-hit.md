## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a revised settlement calendar for the delisting offer under the tender offer scheme by Hitech Corporation Limited, superseding or updating previous schedules in reference to circulars NCL/CMPT/76727 and NSE/CML/76677.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Hitech Corporation Limited
- **Offer Type:** Delisting Offer under Tender Offer scheme
- **Settlement Type:** X
- **Settlement Number:** 2026119
- **Trade Start Date:** 07-OCT-2026
- **Trade End Date:** 13-OCT-2026
- **Obligation Download:** 22-OCT-2026
- **Settlement Date:** 23-OCT-2026

## Regulatory Changes

Update and revision of the settlement schedule for the delisting tender offer process.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must take note of the revised dates for obligation download (22-OCT-2026) and settlement (23-OCT-2026). Clearing Corporation may notify further revisions if there are delays in receipt of acceptance details from the RTA or funds pay-in by buying trading members.

## Important Dates

- **07-OCT-2026:** Trade Start Date
- **13-OCT-2026:** Trade End Date
- **22-OCT-2026:** Obligation Download Date
- **23-OCT-2026:** Settlement Date

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless clearing and settlement operations for market participants and custodians participating in the Hitech Corporation Limited delisting tender offer.
