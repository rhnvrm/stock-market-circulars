## Summary

This circular issues the operational guidelines for Trading Members on the handling of client unpaid securities, framed in consultation with SEBI, Depositories, and the Brokers' Industry Standards Forum (Brokers' ISF). For trades not covered under the Margin Trading Facility (MTF), unpaid securities must be paid out directly to the client's demat account, followed by an automatic pledge creation request (with the reason 'unpaid') in favor of a separate demat account titled 'Client Unpaid Securities Pledgee Account' (CUSPA). This process replaces the practice of holding client unpaid securities in broker pool accounts, thereby increasing security and alignment with client-asset segregation rules.

## Key Points

- **Direct Payout**: Unpaid securities for non-MTF trades must be paid out directly to the client's demat account.
- **Mandatory Auto-Pledge**: Following the direct payout, an auto-pledge creation request is automatically triggered with the reason 'unpaid' in favor of the CUSPA, requiring no separate client instruction.
- **Broad Consultation**: Formulated through joint efforts between Stock Exchanges, SEBI, Depositories, and the Brokers' ISF.
- **Phased Timeline**: Operational Guidelines 1 to 9 take effect from October 31, 2026. Guideline 10 takes effect from January 03, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- **SEBI Compliance**: Implements SEBI Circular No. HO/38/11/(9)2026-MIRSD-POD/I/15382/2026 dated July 03, 2026.
- **Abolishment of Pool Retention**: Disallows trading members from holding unpaid client securities in their own accounts, establishing a secure CUSPA-based auto-pledge instead.
- **Exceptional Extension Rules**: Standardizes conditions under which a pledge may be extended under exceptional circumstances.

## Compliance Requirements

- **CUSPA Setup**: Trading members must open and configure a 'Client Unpaid Securities Pledgee Account' (CUSPA).
- **Infrastructure Alignment**: Integrate back-office and trading platforms with depositories and clearing corporations to automate the payout and auto-pledge flow.
- **Adherence to Timelines**: Ensure operational readiness for Guidelines 1 to 9 by October 31, 2026, and Guideline 10 by January 03, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **July 03, 2026**: Publication date of the reference SEBI Circular.
- **July 31, 2026**: Publication date of this operational circular.
- **October 31, 2026**: Effective date for Operational Guidelines 1 to 9 (three months from issuance).
- **January 03, 2027**: Effective date for Operational Guideline 10 (Extension of pledge in exceptional circumstances).

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: High. Trading members must overhaul their risk management, settlement, and technical integrations with depositories to manage automated pledges and CUSPA accounts.
- **Client Impact**: Highly positive. Improves investor protection by ensuring securities are held in the client's own demat account rather than being pooled at the broker level.
- **Market Integrity**: Strengthens systemic risk control by standardizing client asset handling and reducing potential defaults or misuse of client securities.
