## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of the Motilal Oswal Multi Asset - Omni Fund of Funds New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The subscription window runs from October 21, 2026, to November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Motilal Oswal Multi Asset - Omni Fund of Funds (Direct & Regular options across Growth and IDCW Payout/Reinvestment).
- **NFO Period:** October 21, 2026 (open) to November 04, 2026 till 11:59 PM (close).
- **Allotment Date:** November 11, 2026.
- **Platform:** NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; this is a standard NFO launch notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any supported payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before November 11, 2026, till 12:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** October 21, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** November 04, 2026 (11:59 PM)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** November 11, 2026 (12:00 PM)
- **Allotment Date:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors with investment access to the Motilal Oswal Multi Asset - Omni Fund of Funds through the NSE platform.
