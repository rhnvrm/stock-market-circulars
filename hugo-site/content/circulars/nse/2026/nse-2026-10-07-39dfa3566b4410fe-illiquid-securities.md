## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding illiquid securities pursuant to SEBI guidelines on the rationalization of periodic call auctions. Periodic call auction sessions will be conducted for identified illiquid securities starting October 12, 2026, based on trading activity from April 2026 to September 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 12, 2026 (Monday).
- Securities in Periodic Call Auction: DCM FINANCIAL SERVICES LIMITED (Symbol: DCMFINSERV, ISIN: INE891B01012).
- Securities moving out of Periodic Call Auction: None.
- Assessment Period: Trading activity during April 2026 – September 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic call auction mechanism is enforced in accordance with SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/38/2013 dated December 19, 2013, and exchange circular NSE/CMTR/25320 dated December 20, 2013, to manage price volatility and ensure fair price discovery for thinly traded scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to exercise additional due diligence while trading in these securities either on their own account or on behalf of clients.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date for Periodic Call Auction: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in DCMFINSERV will be subject to periodic call auction sessions, affecting liquidity and execution frequency. Market participants dealing in this security must adapt their trading strategies accordingly.
