## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the listing of privately placed Government Securities on its Capital Market Segment and Debt Market Segment, effective from July 29, 2026. Specifically, the Government of India (GOI) Loan 7.06% 2041 has been admitted to dealings.

## Key Points

- **Security Admitted:** GOI LOAN 7.06% 2041 has been approved for trading on the Capital Market Segment.
- **Symbol:** 706GS2041 (Series: GS).
- **ISIN:** IN0020260041.
- **Coupon Rate:** 7.06% per annum.
- **Maturity Date:** July 27, 2041.
- **Effective Date:** Trading starts on July 29, 2026.
- **Lot Size:** The market lot size for trading is 1.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations, the privately placed securities specified in the Annexure are admitted to dealings on the Debt Market Segment.
- In accordance with Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the respective lot sizes for trading have been defined.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Market Members:** Trading members must update their trading systems and master files with the new security codes (Symbol: `706GS2041`, Series: `GS`, ISIN: `IN0020260041`) and the specified lot size of 1 to enable trading.
- **Identification:** For system trading, the security must be identified exclusively by its designated symbol and codes.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 27, 2026
- **Effective Date of Trading:** July 29, 2026
- **Date of Maturity:** July 27, 2041

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** This listing provides increased liquidity and trading avenues for government debt instruments, specifically the 7.06% GOI 2041 bond, on the NSE Capital Market segment.
- **Operational Impact:** Low operational impact. Trading members need to configure their back-office and front-office systems with the new symbol and ISIN code prior to the market open on July 29, 2026.
