## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issue of equity shares of Adani Enterprises Limited effective from September 08, 2026. The listing follows the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Adani Enterprises Limited
- Symbol: ADANIENT
- Series: EQ
- ISIN: INE423A01024
- Purpose: Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares (575 shares total)
- Effective Date: September 08, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and lot sizes specified.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing update with negligible direct market impact.
