## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the early redemption facility for Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs) held in physical mode for the period October 2026 to December 2026, pursuant to RBI guidelines and GOI notifications allowing premature redemption after completion of the fifth year from the date of issue.

## Key Points

- Premature redemption facility is available for SGB units completed 5 years from issue date.
- Covers specific tranches maturing/eligible during October 2026 - December 2026.
- Partial redemption, modification, and cancellation are permitted during the specified redemption window.
- Early redemption price is based on the simple average closing gold price of 999 purity published by IBJA of the week preceding the redemption date or as declared by RBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of RBI Press Release Ref. No. 2026-2027/938 dated August 21, 2026, providing the premature redemption calendar.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure investors desirous of participating provide consent and adhere to applicable legal frameworks.
- Members must input Investor ID and Investment Serial Number correctly after selecting tranche details.
- Members must check and update KYC of SGB investors before placing requests on the eIPO portal and verify displayed bank and investor details to avoid returned payments.

## Important Dates

- **SGB202101R (2020-21, Series I):** Start: 28-Sep-2026 | End: 19-Oct-2026 | Redemption Date: 28-Oct-2026
- **SGB212201R (2021-22, Series I):** Start: 23-Oct-2026 | End: 16-Nov-2026 | Redemption Date: 25-Nov-2026
- **SGB212202R (2021-22 Series II):** Start: 02-Nov-2026 | End: 20-Nov-2026 | Redemption Date: 01-Dec-2026
- **SGB212203R (2021-22, Series III):** Start: 09-Nov-2026 | End: 30-Nov-2026 | Redemption Date: 08-Dec-2026
- **SGB202103R (2020-21, Series III):** Start: 13-Nov-2026 | End: 07-Dec-2026 | Redemption Date: 16-Dec-2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables physical SGB holders of eligible tranches to exit and realize proceeds prior to final maturity through the NSE eIPO platform.
