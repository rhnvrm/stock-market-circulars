## Summary

This circular pertains to an appeal filed before the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) in Mumbai by Trifid Research and its partners, Vivek Tyagi and Lidya Thomas, against an order passed by the Whole Time Member (WTM) of SEBI on December 29, 2022. The SEBI order had debarred the appellants from accessing the securities market for five years due to various violations of the SEBI (Investment Advisory) Regulations, 2013, identified during an inspection carried out in October 2019.

The debarment, which commenced on March 30, 2021 (the date of the interim order), effectively ended on March 29, 2026. However, SEBI's order directed that the debarment would continue until the appellants complied with directions to resolve client complaints and refund unreasonable fees. The SAT heard submissions regarding the status of compliance and the resolution of these complaints.

## Key Points

- **Appellants Involved:** Trifid Research (a SEBI-registered investment advisory firm) and its partners, Vivek Tyagi and Lidya Thomas.
- **Key Violations Identified by SEBI:**
  - Failure to carry out proper risk profiling of clients.
  - Failure to provide investment advice based on suitability.
  - Charging unreasonable and unfair fees from clients.
- **Regulatory Action:** SEBI had imposed a 5-year debarment starting from the interim order dated March 30, 2021.
- **Current Dispute:** Although the primary 5-year debarment period ended on March 29, 2026, the debarment was ordered to continue under paragraph 50(c) of SEBI's order until compliance with refund/complaint resolution directions under paragraph 50(b) was achieved.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new legislative or regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement of the existing **SEBI (Investment Advisory) Regulations, 2013**, specifically focusing on risk profiling, suitability of advice, and fee reasonability.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Investment Advisers:**
  - Must strictly implement and document risk profiling for all clients.
  - Must ensure suitability of advice provided to clients based on their risk profiles.
  - Must maintain transparent and reasonable fee structures in compliance with SEBI guidelines.
  - Must proactively resolve investor grievances to avoid prolonged regulatory debarment and enforcement actions.

## Important Dates

- **Inspection Period:** April 1, 2018, to October 17, 2019
- **SEBI Interim Order (Debarment Commenced):** March 30, 2021
- **SEBI WTM Order (Impugned Order):** December 29, 2022
- **Primary Debarment End Date:** March 29, 2026
- **SAT Order Date:** July 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Industry Impact:** This serves as a strong regulatory signal to registered Investment Advisers (IAs) regarding the serious consequences of failing to adhere to risk profiling, suitability, and fee charging regulations. Non-compliance can lead to severe multi-year debarments and business disruption.
- **Investor Protection:** Reaffirms SEBI's commitment to protecting retail investors from unfair practices and unreasonable charges by registered intermediaries.
