## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Confirmatory Order (WTM/KV/CFID/CFID-SEC2/32656/2026-27) on August 10, 2026, confirming the ad-interim directions issued via the Interim Order dated October 14, 2025, against Nirman Agri Genetics Limited (NAGL) and its promoter and Managing Director, Mr. Pranav Kailas Bagal. The investigation revealed a systematic diversion and siphoning of approximately 93% of the IPO proceeds raised by the company, routing them through conflicting vendor profiles, non-existent entities, and shell layers to ultimately benefit the promoter group and connected persons. Despite multiple extensions and a personal hearing via video conferencing, the noticees failed to file written submissions with supporting documents to substantiate their claims that some funds had been repatriated. Consequently, SEBI confirmed the interim restrictions, including restraining them from accessing the securities market and halting planned corporate actions.

## Key Points

- **IPO Proceeds Diversion:** SEBI's examination revealed that Nirman Agri Genetics Limited siphoned off approximately 93% of its IPO proceeds.
- **Routing Mechanism:** Funds were routed using conflicting vendor profiles, non-existent entities, and shell layers to benefit the promoter group and connected persons.
- **Ad-Interim Directions Confirmed:** 
  - Nirman Agri Genetics Limited remains restrained from accessing the securities market in any manner.
  - NAGL must halt corporate actions relating to its bonus issue, stock split, and proposed company name change.
  - Mr. Pranav Kailas Bagal (Promoter and MD) is restrained from buying, selling, or dealing in the scrip of NAGL, directly or indirectly.
- **Failure to Cooperate:** The noticees failed to file written submissions or provide concrete evidence supporting their claim of fund repatriation despite several extensions.

## Regulatory Changes

No broad regulatory changes to market-wide frameworks are introduced. However, the order enforces specific enforcement directions and restrictions under Sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992, against the noticees to protect market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Nirman Agri Genetics Limited (NAGL):** Must immediately comply with the ban on accessing the securities market. The company must completely halt any ongoing or proposed corporate actions, specifically its bonus issue, stock split, and change of company name.
- **Pranav Kailas Bagal (Promoter and MD):** Must refrain from buying, selling, or dealing in the shares/scrip of NAGL, directly or indirectly.
- **Intermediaries and Exchanges:** Brokerages, depositories, and exchanges must ensure that the restrictions on market access and trading in NAGL scrip by the specified promoter are enforced, and that no corporate actions for NAGL are processed.

## Important Dates

- **October 14, 2025:** Date of the initial Interim Order.
- **May 13, 2026:** Original date scheduled for a personal hearing.
- **July 16, 2026:** Video-conference personal hearing conducted.
- **August 10, 2026:** Date of the SEBI Confirmatory Order (directions remain in force until further orders).

## Impact Assessment

- **For NAGL and Its Shareholders:** High negative impact. All planned corporate actions (bonus issue, stock split, name change) are frozen. The ongoing market ban restricts the company's ability to raise capital or utilize public markets.
- **For the Promoter:** Direct and severe restriction on dealing in the company's scrip, severely limiting liquidity and control over personal holding actions.
- **For the Market:** Reinforces SEBI's strict stance against the misuse of IPO proceeds, demonstrating robust surveillance and post-issue monitoring of SME listings, thereby boosting investor confidence in market integrity.
