## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective from August 17, 2026, based on corporate announcements and disclosures.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under ASM for Companies as per Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) are listed in Annexure I.
- Teerth Gopicon Limited (Symbol: TGL, ISIN: INE0K6601012) has been shortlisted w.r.t. 'Receipt of corporate announcement / disclosure from the company' effective August 17, 2026.
- Securities satisfying the exclusion criteria are listed in Annexure II.

## Regulatory Changes

- ASM IBC framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework are reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the shortlisting of securities under the ASM IBC framework for trading and surveillance purposes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026
- Circular Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified securities will be subject to surveillance measures as per the ASM IBC framework.
