## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced a change in the settlement type for the HDFC Mutual Fund scheme on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: HDFC BSE REITS and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund
- Current Settlement Type: T1
- Revised Settlement Type: T2
- Effective Date: Immediate effect (September 30, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

- The settlement type for HDFC BSE REITS and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund is revised from T1 to T2 on the NSE MF Invest Platform as per communication received from HDFC Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must update their systems and operational workflows to accommodate the revised T2 settlement type for transactions in the HDFC BSE REITS and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minor operational adjustment for members dealing with the specified HDFC Mutual Fund scheme regarding settlement timelines.
