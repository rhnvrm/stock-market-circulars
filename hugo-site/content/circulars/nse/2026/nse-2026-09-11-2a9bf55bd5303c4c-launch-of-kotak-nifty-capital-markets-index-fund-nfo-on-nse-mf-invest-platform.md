## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the Kotak Nifty Capital Markets Index Fund under Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Key Points

- **NFO Window:** Available for transactions from September 15, 2026 (12:00 am) to September 29, 2026 (11:59 pm).
- **Schemes Available:** Includes various plans (Regular and Direct) covering Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options.
- **Allotment Date:** Scheduled for October 05, 2026 across all listed ISINs.
- **Fund Category:** Equity Index Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds through valid payment modes are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 02, 2026, till 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** September 15, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** October 02, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors with an additional investment option in capital markets themed index funds via the NSE platform.
