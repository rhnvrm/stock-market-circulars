## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the change in ISIN for Azad India Mobility Limited (AZADIND) effective October 23, 2026, following a sub-division of equity shares from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Azad India Mobility Limited
- Symbol: AZADIND
- New ISIN: INE566M01025
- Reason: Sub-Division from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine update for corporate action.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that all trades on and from the ex-date use the new ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Ex-date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update with minimal market impact.
