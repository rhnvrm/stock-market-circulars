## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced a revision in the auction market timings for the ARCL Triparty Repo-Market (TRM) platform in partial modification of earlier circular NSE/DS/57672.

## Key Points

- Applies to the Debt Segment of the Exchange.
- Modifies the repo auction market operating hours.
- Effective date: September 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Repo Auction Market trading hours revised from 03.00 PM - 03.30 PM to 03.30 PM - 04.00 PM.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must update their internal systems and workflows to align with the revised auction timings.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal, requiring a 30-minute shift in execution schedules for participants engaging in the ARCL Triparty Repo-Market auction.
