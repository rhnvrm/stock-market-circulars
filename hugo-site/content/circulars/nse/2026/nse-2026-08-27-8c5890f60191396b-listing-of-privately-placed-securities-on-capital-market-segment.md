## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment, effective from August 28, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Securities will be available for trading starting August 28, 2026.
- Trading will be identified by designated security codes and specific market lot sizes.
- Covered entities include Kisetsu Saison Finance (India) Private Limited and Power Finance Corporation Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further securities to dealings on the capital market segment under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must use designated security codes and prescribed market lots for trading the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Circular Issue Date: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026
- Maturity Dates: 24-Feb-2028 (Kisetsu Saison Finance), 26-Aug-2041 (Power Finance Corporation)

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for the newly listed privately placed debt/securities of the respective issuers.
