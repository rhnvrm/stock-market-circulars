## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has notified trading members regarding the listing and commencement of trading of Rights Entitlements (REs) of Genesys International Corporation Limited (Symbol: GENESYS-RE) pursuant to its Rights Issue. Trading will commence on August 14, 2026, and the trading window for the renunciation of REs will close on August 18, 2026. Trading will be conducted in the Trade to Trade (BE series) segment in compulsory demat format, with a market lot of 1.

## Key Points

- **Admittance to Dealings:** Securities representing Rights Entitlements (REs) of Genesys International Corporation Limited are further admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment of NSE.
- **Trading Period:** Trading of Rights Entitlements on the system will begin on August 14, 2026, and close on August 18, 2026.
- **Suspension of Trading:** Trading in REs will be suspended with effect from August 19, 2026.
- **Quantity:** A total of 25,065,332 Rights Entitlements will be available for trading.
- **Trading Segment & Series:** The REs will trade in the "BE" series (Trade to Trade segment) and are subject to compulsory demat for all investors.
- **ISIN:** The designated ISIN for the Rights Entitlement is INE727B20018.

## Regulatory Changes

- There are no major macro-regulatory changes introduced by this circular. It is an operational notification issued under Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Client Notification:** Trading members are required to inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs, explicitly mentioning the ISIN (INE727B20018) and cautioning that REs must not be mistaken for ordinary shares of the company.
- **Disclaimer in Contract Notes:** Trading members must prominently include a disclaimer in all contract notes or bills involving the purchase of REs. This disclaimer must inform clients that:
  - The purchase of REs only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue by making an application with the requisite application money or to renounce the REs before the issue closes.
  - REs which are neither subscribed (by submitting the application and money) nor renounced on or before the Issue Closing Date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date / Trading Commencement:** August 14, 2026
- **Renunciation Trading Period:** From August 14, 2026, to August 18, 2026 (both days inclusive)
- **Date of Suspension:** August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Company Impact:** Facilitates the price discovery and trading of rights entitlements for shareholders of Genesys International Corporation Limited who wish to monetize their rights or for new investors wanting to participate in the Rights Issue.
- **Trading Member Impact:** Brokers must update their front-office messaging and contract note templates to include the mandatory disclaimers for clients purchasing these REs.
- **Market Impact:** Low overall market impact. This is a standard corporate action notification specific to GENESYS-RE trading on NSE's Capital Market segment.
