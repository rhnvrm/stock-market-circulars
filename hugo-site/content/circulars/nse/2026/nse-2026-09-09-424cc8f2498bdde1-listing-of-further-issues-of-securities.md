## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the listing of further issues of securities for several companies, including BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Limited, Computer Age Management Services Limited, CARE Ratings Limited, Clean Science and Technology Limited, and others, effective from September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Further issues of equity shares admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective September 10, 2026.
- Shares allotted primarily under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) across various companies.
- Trading will take place in designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with designated security codes and market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 10, 2026
- Allotment Dates: Varying dates in August and September 2026 depending on the company.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up capital of the respective listed entities with no broader market disruption.
