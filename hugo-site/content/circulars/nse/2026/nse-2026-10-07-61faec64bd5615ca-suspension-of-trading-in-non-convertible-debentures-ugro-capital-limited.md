## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in three series of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) issued by Ugro Capital Limited, effective from October 09, 2026, pursuant to their maturity.

## Key Points

- Suspension applies to three Non-Convertible Debentures of Ugro Capital Limited: symbols 10UCL26 (ISIN: INE583D07562), 1039UCL26 (ISIN: INE583D07570), and 1025UCL26A (ISIN: INE583D07521).
- Reason for suspension: Maturity of the securities.
- Effective date of suspension: October 09, 2026.
- Executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.
