## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced a change in the ISIN code for Bansal Wire Industries Limited (BANSALWIRE) effective October 16, 2026, following a sub-division of equity shares from a face value of Rs. 5/- to Re. 1/-.

## Key Points

- Company: Bansal Wire Industries Limited
- Symbol: BANSALWIRE
- New ISIN: INE0B9K01033
- Reason: Sub-Division from Rs. 5/- to Re. 1/-
- Effective Date (Ex-date): October 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of ISIN code in trading and depository systems corresponding to the corporate action share sub-division.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure their trading and back-office systems are updated with the new ISIN for BANSALWIRE effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective / Ex-date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action update ensuring accurate security identification and seamless settlement post-split.
