## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) for various securities. Effective from September 29, 2026, specified securities are placed under ST-ASM Stage I, and certain securities are moved from Stage I to Stage II.

## Key Points

- **Stage I Inclusion:** 9 securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I w.e.f. September 29, 2026.
- **Stage II Inclusion:** Nil securities newly added to Stage II directly.
- **Stage I to Stage II Migration:** 1 security (DUCON) is moving from ST-ASM Stage I to Stage II w.e.f. September 29, 2026.
- **Shortlisted Securities (Stage I):** Bohra Industries Limited (BOHRAIND), Bharat Parenterals Limited (BPLPHARMA), Naman Industries Proxima Limited (NAMAN), Raj Television Network Limited (RAJTV), Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited (RCDL), RKEC Projects Limited (RKEC), Shanti Gold International Limited (SHANTIGOLD), Turtlemint Fintech Solutions Limited (TURTLEMINT), and Whirlpool of India Limited (WHIRLPOOL).
- **Shortlisted Securities (Stage I to II):** Ducon Infratechnologies Limited (DUCON).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance actions under the ST-ASM framework as per previous exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Stage I Margin:** Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. September 30, 2026, on all open positions as on September 29, 2026, and new positions created from September 30, 2026.
- **Stage II Margin:** Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100% w.e.f. September 30, 2026, on all open positions as on September 29, 2026, and new positions created from September 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 29, 2026 (for shortlisting/movement).
- **Margin Applicability Date:** September 30, 2026 (on open positions as of September 29, 2026, and new positions).

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in the affected securities must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the shortlisted stocks.
