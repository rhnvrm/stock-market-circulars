## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM). Several securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I effective August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities added to ST-ASM Stage I: BALUFORGE, BIRLAPREC, CORDELIA, LAMBODHARA, MAKERSL, NAHARINDUS, PELATRO, QUADFUTURE, ROCKINGDCE, SHANTIGEAR, and VIRAT.
- Applicable rate of margin under ST-ASM Stage I is set to 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100%.
- The surveillance action applies to all open positions as on August 26, 2026, and new positions created from August 27, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely for market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of heightened surveillance measures under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for listed securities exhibiting high volatility or specific market criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure that appropriate margins (50% or higher, capped at 100%) are maintained for open and new positions in the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: August 27, 2026 (on open positions as of August 26, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Trading participants holding positions in the listed securities will experience higher margin requirements, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for those specific scrips.
