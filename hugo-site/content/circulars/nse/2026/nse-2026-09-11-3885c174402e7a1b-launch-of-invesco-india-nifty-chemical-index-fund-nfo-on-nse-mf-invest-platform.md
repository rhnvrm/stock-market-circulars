## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the launch of the Invesco India Nifty Chemical Index Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The NFO will be available for transactions from September 15, 2026, to September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund Name: Invesco India Nifty Chemical Index Fund (Regular and Direct Plans across Growth and IDCW options)
- Subscription Window: September 15, 2026 to September 29, 2026 (till 11:59 pm)
- Allotment Date: October 06, 2026
- Payment Deadline: October 06, 2026, till 03:00 pm

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 06, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 15, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 29, 2026
- Allotment Date: October 06, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: October 06, 2026 (03:00 pm)

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to subscribe to the new chemical sector index fund scheme through the NSE MF Invest platform.
