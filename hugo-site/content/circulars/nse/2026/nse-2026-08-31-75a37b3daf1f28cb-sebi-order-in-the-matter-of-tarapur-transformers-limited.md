## Summary

SEBI issued a comprehensive order regarding Tarapur Transformers Limited and associated noticees, addressing severe violations including diversion of funds, manipulation of trade receivables, corporate governance failures, and non-compliance with regulatory summons under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Key Points

- Investigation uncovered significant diversion of funds involving the company and promoter group entities.
- Irregularities and falsification regarding trade receivables were highlighted across multiple financial years.
- Serious corporate governance lapses and failure to comply with statutory summons issued by SEBI.
- Multiple noticees including corporate entities and individuals were found liable for violations of securities laws.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and directions issued pursuant to Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1), and 11B(2) read with Section 15A(a), 15HA, and 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Noticees and the company are required to comply with the directions, penalties, and remedial measures specified in the final order of SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: 2026-27 (as per SEBI reference)

## Impact Assessment

Strict regulatory scrutiny on listed entities engaging in fund diversion, accounting irregularities, and non-cooperation with regulatory investigations.
