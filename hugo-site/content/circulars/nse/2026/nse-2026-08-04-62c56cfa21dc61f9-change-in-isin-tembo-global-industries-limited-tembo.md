## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced a change in the International Securities Identification Number (ISIN) for the equity shares of Tembo Global Industries Limited (TEMBO) due to a sub-division (stock split) of its shares.

## Key Points

- **Symbol**: TEMBO
- **Company Name**: Tembo Global Industries Limited
- **New ISIN**: INE869Y01028
- **Reason for Change**: Sub-division (stock split) from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-
- **Effective Date**: August 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes are introduced. This is an operational update to facilitate a corporate action (stock split) under the exchange's listing rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems, algorithms, and databases with the new ISIN **INE869Y01028** for TEMBO.
- All trades executed on and after the Ex-date of August 05, 2026, must be cleared and settled using the new ISIN.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 04, 2026
- **Effective / Ex-Date**: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading and Settlement**: Clear operational adjustment for brokers and clearing houses to shift trading activity to the new ISIN.
- **Shareholders**: Shareholders' existing shares of Rs. 10 face value will be split into ten shares of Re. 1 face value each. Share prices will adjust proportionally on the Ex-date.
