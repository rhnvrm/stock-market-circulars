## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specified securities effective September 18, 2026, pursuant to the surveillance framework.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100% w.e.f. September 21, 2026, on all open positions as on September 18, 2026, and new positions created from September 21, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company/entity.
- Affected stocks include ABH Healthcare Limited, Birla Cable Limited, eMudhra Limited, Ideal Technoplast Industries Limited, Madhur Knit Crafts Limited, Maxposure Limited, Quint Digital Limited, and Utssav CZ Gold Jewels Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

The framework introduces enhanced margin requirements for designated securities under surveillance measures to safeguard market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the revised margin requirements (50% or existing margin, up to 100% cap) for open and new positions in the listed short-term ASM securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of ST-ASM: September 18, 2026
- Margin applicability date: w.e.f. September 21, 2026 (on open positions as on September 18, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Enhanced margin requirements may impact trading volumes, liquidity, and leverage for the securities shortlisted under the ST-ASM Stage I framework.
