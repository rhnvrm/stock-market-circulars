## Summary

NSE Clearing Ltd. has issued the settlement calendar for the CBRICS platform covering the month of September 2026. Participants and custodians must transfer securities using the 'On-market' settlement mode based on the specified schedule.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Debt Segment
- **Market Type:** Corporate Bond
- **Clearing Member Name:** NSE Clearing Ltd.
- **CM-BP ID:** IN568177
- **DP Details Note:** Previously used DP details (IN001002-10000617) are not considered for settlement on the CBRICS platform effective September 3, 2019.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational schedule release.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and custodians are requested to transfer securities for CBRICS settlement in accordance with the specified dates and 'On-market' mode using the provided CM-BP ID.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** 24 August, 2026
- **Settlement Dates:** Spanning from 01-Sep-2026 to 30-Sep-2026 for NSDL/CDSL transfers.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational compliance for market participants trading corporate bonds on the CBRICS platform, ensuring seamless monthly settlement cycles.
