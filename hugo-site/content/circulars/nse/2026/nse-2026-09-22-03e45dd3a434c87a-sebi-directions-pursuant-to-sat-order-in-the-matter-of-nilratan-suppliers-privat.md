## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding SEBI's final order in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips, pursuant to the SAT Order. Entities have been restrained from accessing the securities market and monetary penalties have been levied.

## Key Points

- SEBI vide order no. WTM/AS/IVD-2/ID16/32460/2026-27 dated June 30, 2026, issued the final order in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips.
- Restraint periods range from 4 years to 7 years for various noticees from the date of the order, with set-offs against previously undergone periods.
- Monetary penalties ranging from ₹5 lakhs to ₹10 crores have been levied on noticees.
- Noticees with open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts as of the order date are permitted to close out/square off such open positions within 3 months.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of debarment and monetary penalties by SEBI against specified entities involved in the matter.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the debarred entities list available on the Exchange website and ensure compliance.
- Entities with open derivative positions are allowed a 3-month window to square off.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: June 30, 2026
- Square-off window for derivatives: Within 3 months from the date of the order

## Impact Assessment

- Strict compliance required by trading members to ensure debarred entities do not access the securities market.
