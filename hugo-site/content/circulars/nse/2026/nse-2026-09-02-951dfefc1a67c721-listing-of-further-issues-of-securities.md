## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of further issues of securities for several companies with effect from September 03, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 03, 2026.
- Companies involved include Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Aye Finance Limited, Birlasoft Limited, Cello World Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, Wipro Limited, Yes Bank Limited, and others.
- Securities admitted consist largely of equity shares allotted under Employee Stock Options (ESOP).
- Trading will be conducted in designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notification under Capital Market Trading Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure compliance with applicable trading regulations and distinctive number ranges specified for the further issue of shares.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 02, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact; standard expansion of paid-up capital and listing of shares issued through ESOPs and other corporate actions.
