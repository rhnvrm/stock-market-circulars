## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for six companies, effective from October 21, 2026. The issuances include shares allotted under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCB).

## Key Points

- **Aavas Financiers Limited**: 1,540 equity shares allotted under ESOP at face value/paid-up value of Rs. 10/- each (ISIN: INE216P01012).
- **Carborundum Universal Limited**: 4,750 equity shares allotted under ESOP at face value/paid-up value of Re. 1/- each (ISIN: INE120A01034).
- **Davangere Sugar Company Limited**: 265,905,000 equity shares allotted under FCCB at face value/paid-up value of Re. 1/- each and issue price of Rs. 3.6 (ISIN: INE179G01029).
- **Vedant Fashions Limited**: 303 equity shares allotted under ESOP at face value/paid-up value of Re. 1/- each (ISIN: INE825V01034).
- **Mastek Limited**: 4,454 equity shares allotted under ESOP at face value/paid-up value of Rs. 5/- each (ISIN: INE759A01021).
- **One Mobikwik Systems Limited**: 314,767 equity shares allotted under ESOP at face value/paid-up value of Rs. 2/- each (ISIN: INE0HLU01028).

## Regulatory Changes

None. The notification is issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that trading in these securities is conducted using the designated security codes and specified market lots (market lot of 1 for all listed securities, pari passu rank).

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: October 19, 2026
- **Effective Date**: October 21, 2026
- **Allotment Dates**: Ranging from August 25, 2026, to October 12, 2026, depending on the company.

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital base of the respective companies. No direct operational impact on market participants beyond standard trading availability.
