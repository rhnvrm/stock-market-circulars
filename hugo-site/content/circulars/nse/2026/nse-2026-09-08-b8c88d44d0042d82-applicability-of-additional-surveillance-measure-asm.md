## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM). Several securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion or stage migration under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) framework.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 11, 2026, on all open positions as on September 10, 2026, and new positions created from September 11, 2026 onwards for shortlisted securities.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. September 11, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of securities in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I (e.g., IVC, PKTEA, XTRANET).
- Movement of securities from Long-Term ASM Stage - I to Stage - II (e.g., SAKAR).
- Movement of securities from Long-Term ASM Stage - I to Stage - IV (e.g., MODISONLTD).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement and note the segment shifts for affected scrips starting September 11, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Surveillance Actions / Margin Applicability: September 11, 2026
- Position Evaluation Date for Margins: September 10, 2026
- Framework Effective Date: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected scrips will experience increased margin requirements (100%), and scrips shifted to Stage IV will be traded under the Trade-for-Trade segment, impacting liquidity and settlement terms.
