## Summary

Mirae Asset Mutual Fund has announced the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, switch-in applications, systematic transactions, and lumpsum investments under the Income Distribution Cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) Option for 29 passive schemes, effective Monday, October 12, 2026. This is in pursuance of the SEBI letter to AMFI dated May 13, 2026, regarding the non-declaration of dividends/IDCW payouts by mutual funds under IDCW plans.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026 (cut-off timing after Friday, October 09, 2026).
- **Scope:** Discontinuation of fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, systematic transactions, and lumpsum investments under IDCW options across 29 passive schemes.
- **Exemptions:** Existing systematic transactions (SIPs/STPs) registered prior to the effective date will continue to be processed.
- **Affected Schemes:** Includes 29 passive schemes such as Mirae Asset Nifty 50 Index Fund, Mirae Asset Gold ETF Fund of Fund, Mirae Asset NYSE FANG+ ETF Fund of Fund, and various other FOFs and index funds.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implemented pursuant to SEBI letter to AMFI dated May 13, 2026, on "Non-declaration of Dividends/Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) Pay-outs by Mutual Funds under IDCW Plans".

## Compliance Requirements

- Scheme Information Documents (SIDs) and Key Information Memorandums (KIMs) of the affected schemes stand amended suitably to reflect these changes.

## Important Dates

- **Cut-off Date/Time:** Friday, October 09, 2026 (post cut-off applications not processed).
- **Effective Date:** Monday, October 12, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors wishing to invest in the affected passive schemes via IDCW option can no longer make fresh subscriptions or lumpsum/systematic inflows, though existing SIPs/STPs remain unaffected. Growth options or other schemes remain available as per regular fund offerings.
