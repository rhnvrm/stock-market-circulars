## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and commencement of trading for units of Invesco India Nifty Bank ETF and Invesco India BSE Sensex ETF issued by Invesco Asset Management (India) Private Limited, effective from August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Invesco India Nifty Bank ETF**: Symbol `IVZBANKNF`, ISIN `INF205KA1CB9`, 1,511,525 units allotted on August 14, 2026, with an issue price of Rs. 57.49/-.
- **Invesco India BSE Sensex ETF**: Symbol `IVZSENSEX`, ISIN `INF205KA1CA1`, 1,015,290 units allotted on August 14, 2026, with an issue price of Rs. 78.00/-.
- Market lot for both ETFs is set at 1.
- Trading will take place on the Capital Market segment under the EQ series.
