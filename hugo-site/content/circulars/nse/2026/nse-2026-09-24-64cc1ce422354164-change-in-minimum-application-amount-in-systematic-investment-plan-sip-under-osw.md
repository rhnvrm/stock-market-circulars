## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the change in the minimum application amount under the Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) for Motilal Oswal Special Opportunities Fund under daily frequency on the NSE MF Invest platform, effective from September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme:** Motilal Oswal Special Opportunities Fund
- **SIP Frequency:** Daily
- **Existing Minimum Instalment Amount:** Rs. 100/- and multiple of Re. 1/- thereafter
- **Revised Minimum Instalment Amount:** Rs. 21/- and multiple of Re. 1/- thereafter
- **Effective Date:** September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of the minimum daily SIP instalment amount as communicated by Motilal Oswal Asset Management Company Limited pursuant to notice cum addendum to SID and KIM.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must take note of the revised minimum instalment limits for daily SIP transactions in the specified scheme on the NSE MF Invest platform.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 24, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides greater flexibility and accessibility for retail investors participating in daily SIPs for the Motilal Oswal Special Opportunities Fund with a lower entry barrier.
