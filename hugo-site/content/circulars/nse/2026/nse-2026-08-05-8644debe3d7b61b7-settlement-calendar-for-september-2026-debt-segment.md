## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued the settlement and auction calendars for the Debt Segment for the month of September 2026. The calendar covers both standard rolling settlement and auction settlement on a Rolling T+1 basis.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Type 'I' (Debt Settlement):** Covers trades from September 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026, with settlements scheduled between September 02, 2026, and October 01, 2026 (detailed in Annexure A).
- **Settlement Type '2' (Debt Auction Settlement):** Covers trades from September 02, 2026, to September 30, 2026, with settlements scheduled between September 03, 2026, and October 01, 2026 (detailed in Annexure B).
- **Settlement Cycle:** Both standard and auction settlements operate on a Rolling T+1 basis.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced in this circular. It is an operational announcement issued under Regulation 6.2 of the NSE Clearing Limited Capital Market Regulations, 1997.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members, custodians, and Professional Clearing Members (PCMs) must ensure that their operations, fund management, and securities delivery are aligned with the specified daily settlement dates for September 2026.

## Important Dates

- **September 01, 2026:** First trading day under the September 2026 Debt Segment calendar (Settlement No. 2026164).
- **September 30, 2026:** Last trading day under the September 2026 Debt Segment calendar (Settlement No. 2026184).
- **October 01, 2026:** Final settlement day for trades executed on September 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low. This is a standard operational scheduling update, ensuring market participants can plan their settlements for the upcoming month. No disruptions or system modifications are expected.
