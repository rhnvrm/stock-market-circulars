## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has issued a revised list of approved securities and banks for the Commodity Derivatives Segment, superseding previous circular no. 0343/2026 dated September 21, 2026. This circular details the eligible collaterals, market-wide limits, member-specific limits, and applicable haircut rates effective for the month of November 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers equity shares, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs), GOI Securities/T-Bills, open-ended mutual funds, and corporate bonds accepted as non-cash/cash components of liquid assets.
- Includes a list of empaneled banks for issuing Bank Guarantees (BGs) and Fixed Deposit Receipts (FDRs).
- G-Sec/T-Bills carry a shut period of two days; members must release them at least two days before maturity.
- G-Sec provided as collaterals by member-banks must not be reckoned for SLR purposes or used for trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated market-wide limits, member-specific limits, and VAR-based haircut rates for approved securities.
- Value-based prudential norm applies: Exclusion of a pledged security should not result in a margin shortfall of more than 25% at a Clearing Member level.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and custodians must ensure compliance with the revised limits, haircut rates, and prudential norms when pledging securities as collateral.
- Member-banks must ensure G-Secs pledged as collateral are excluded from SLR calculations and trading activities.
- Members must monitor maturity dates of G-Sec/T-Bills to release them at least two days prior to maturity due to the two-day shut period.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Effective Date:** November 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Directly impacts clearing members and custodians operating in the commodity derivatives segment by defining acceptable collaterals, valuation haircuts, and operational constraints for November 2026.
