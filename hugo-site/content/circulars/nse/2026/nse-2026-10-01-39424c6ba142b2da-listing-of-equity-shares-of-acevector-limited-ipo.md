## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the forthcoming listing of equity shares of AceVector Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The equity shares will be traded under the symbol ACEVECTOR with ISIN INE580P01029.

## Key Points

- Company Name: AceVector Limited
- Symbol: ACEVECTOR
- ISIN: INE580P01029
- Listing Type: IPO (Initial Public Offering)

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; standard listing notification under exchange bylaws.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the security details and ensure necessary system updates for trading and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular.

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading access for investors and market participants in the equity shares of AceVector Limited upon commencement of trading.
