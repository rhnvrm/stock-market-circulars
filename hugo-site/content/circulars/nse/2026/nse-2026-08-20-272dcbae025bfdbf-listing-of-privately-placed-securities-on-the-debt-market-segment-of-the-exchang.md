## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from August 20, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted: Commercial Papers (CP) from various issuers.
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026.
- Issuing Companies include Toyota Financial Services India Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, Godrej Agrovet Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, CESC Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Minda Corporation Limited, and ICICI Securities Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Notification of securities admission under existing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to NSE Debt Market Trading Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 19-Aug-2026
- Listing/Effective Date: 20-Aug-2026
- Redemption Dates ranging from August 2026 to May 2027 depending on the specific security issue.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine market operation facilitating debt trading and liquidity for commercial papers.
