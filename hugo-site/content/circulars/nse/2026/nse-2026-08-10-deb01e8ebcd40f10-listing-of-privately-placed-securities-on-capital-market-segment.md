## Summary

Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has admitted privately placed debt securities of Lloyds Metals And Energy Limited to dealings on the Capital Market segment of the Exchange, effective from August 11, 2026. The circular provides designated security codes, series details, and market lot sizes to facilitate trading.

## Key Points

- **Admitted Security**: Non-convertible debentures (NCDs) issued by Lloyds Metals And Energy Limited.
- **Symbol & Series**: The security is identified by symbol `902LMAEL36` and series `N0`.
- **Maturity & Coupon**: The instrument description is LMAEL 9.02% 2036, with a coupon rate of 9.02% per annum and a maturity date of August 06, 2036.
- **Market Lot**: The lot size for trading on the system is set to 1.
- **ISIN**: The security is registered under ISIN Code `INE281B07021`.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Regulation 3.1.1**: Directs the admission of these privately placed securities further to dealings on the National Stock Exchange's Capital Market segment.
- **Regulation 2.5.5**: Establishes the specified lot sizes for the trading of the newly admitted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Identification**: Members and trading participants must identify the security solely by the designated symbol (`902LMAEL36`) and trade in the lot size of 1.
- **Trading Guidelines**: Trading in these securities must comply with the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 10, 2026
- **Effective / Listing Date**: August 11, 2026
- **Maturity Date**: August 06, 2036

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Low. This is a routine listing of privately placed debt securities. It has minimal impact on broad market operations but provides secondary market liquidity for the holders of these specific debentures.
- **Issuer Impact**: Enables secondary market trading of the privately placed debentures for Lloyds Metals And Energy Limited, increasing pricing transparency and liquidity.
