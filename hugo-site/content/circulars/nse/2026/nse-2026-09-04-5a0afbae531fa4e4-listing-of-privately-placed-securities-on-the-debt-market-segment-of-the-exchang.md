## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange with effect from September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Privately placed securities including commercial papers from multiple companies have been admitted to dealings.
- Effective date of admission: September 04, 2026.
- Issuing entities include NIIF Infrastructure Finance Limited, Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited, Alembic Pharmaceuticals Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Hdfc Securities Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, and CEAT Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to the trading and settlement regulations of the debt market segment.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 03-Sep-2026
- Listing/Effective Date: 04-Sep-2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from November 2026 to March 2027 depending on the specific commercial paper series.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine market operations reflecting primary issuance activity in debt and commercial papers on the exchange.
