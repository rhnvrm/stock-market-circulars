## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM). Securities satisfying the specified criteria are placed under ESM Stage-I, attracting a minimum 100% margin and shifting from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (Adroit Infotech Limited and Nureca Limited) are shortlisted under ESM Stage-I w.e.f. September 30, 2026.
- Attracts a minimum 100% margin on all open positions as on September 30, 2026, and new positions created from October 01, 2026.
- Shifting from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 01, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shifting of shortlisted securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment with applicable margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure 100% margin is maintained on open positions as on September 30, 2026, and new positions created from October 01, 2026, for the affected securities.
- Accommodate the shift of securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for ESM Stage-I / Margin applicability / Shifting to Trade-for-Trade: October 01, 2026 (open positions as on September 30, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience increased margin requirements and trading restrictions due to the shift to the trade-for-trade segment, impacting liquidity and positioning for market participants.
