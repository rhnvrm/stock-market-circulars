## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for Futures & Options members from the Business Continuity Plan (BCP) / Disaster Recovery (DR) site on Saturday, August 22, 2026. The session will be followed by a live re-login session from the primary site to prevent login issues on Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mock Trading Date:** Saturday, August 22, 2026.
- **BCP Site Schedule:** Pre-Open starts at 09:00 hrs, Normal Market opens at 09:15 hrs, closes at 15:15 hrs, and trade modification ends at 16:15 hrs.
- **Primary Site Re-Login Schedule:** Start time at 18:30 hrs, close time at 19:00 hrs.
- **Non-Graceful Shutdown:** The switch to the DR site will be a non-graceful shutdown; members must take necessary actions as per circular NSE/MSD/48662.
- **Order Purge:** All outstanding orders will be purged before trading starts from the BCP site.
- **Multicast TBT:** Sequence numbers will start from '1' once trading begins from the BCP site.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members using NNF software should clear outstanding orders of session 1 manually before trading from the BCP site.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading & Re-Login Date:** August 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members need to participate in the mock trading session to verify their connectivity parameters, order management, and system readiness for disaster recovery switchovers without any financial obligation.
