## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Molbio Diagnostics Limited (IPO) on the Capital Market segment, effective from August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** MOLBIO
- **Name of the Company:** Molbio Diagnostics Limited
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE869T01028
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Re. 1/- per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 807/-
- **No. of Securities:** 115,239,996 equity shares
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Effective Date:** August 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing procedure pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and other category of scrips as per relevant SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Effective Date / Trading Commencement:** August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Molbio Diagnostics Limited equity shares on the National Stock Exchange capital market segment following its initial public offering.
