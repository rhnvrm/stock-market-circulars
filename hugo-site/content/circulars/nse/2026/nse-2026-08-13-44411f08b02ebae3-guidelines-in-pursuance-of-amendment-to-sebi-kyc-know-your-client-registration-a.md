## Summary

NSE has issued guidelines in pursuance of amendments to SEBI KRA Regulations, 2011. Clients whose KYCs are not validated by KRAs (i.e., status is "On Hold" for Aadhaar or Non-Aadhaar based OVDs uploaded between July 01, 2026, and July 31, 2026) shall not be permitted to trade or square off open positions effective August 29, 2026, until validation requirements are met.

## Key Points

- Trading members must ensure all client KYCs are uploaded to KRAs and permit trading only for clients with "KYC Registered" or "KYC Validated" status.
- Clients with "On Hold" KYC status uploaded from July 01, 2026, to July 31, 2026, cannot trade or square off open positions from August 29, 2026.
- Open positions for non-compliant clients will naturally expire on their respective contract expiry dates.
- The Exchange will flag non-compliant PANs provided by KRAs as Not Permitted to Trade w.e.f. August 29, 2026.
- PANs becoming KRA compliant subsequently will be permitted to trade on T+1 based on KRA information received by the Exchange on T Day.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of stricter KYC validation norms pursuant to SEBI KRA Regulations, 2011 and related circulars, restricting trading access for non-validated/On Hold client accounts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must check the list of non-validated clients available on the ENIT portal (ENIT > Log > Downloads > `<TM Code>_Non-Validated_Clients_by_KRA`).
- Ensure clients comply with KRA validation requirements to resume trading access.

## Important Dates

- **August 13, 2026:** Circular issuance date.
- **July 01, 2026 - July 31, 2026:** Period of KYC uploads evaluated for non-validation.
- **August 29, 2026:** Effective date from which non-compliant clients are barred from trading and squaring off positions.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and their clients with unvalidated or "On Hold" KRA statuses face immediate suspension of trading rights and inability to execute square-off transactions on open positions starting August 29, 2026, until compliance is restored.
