## Summary

SEBI has reviewed and modified certain aspects of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the equity cash segment, market timings, and the methodology for determining settlement prices of stock and index derivative contracts, following its circular dated January 16, 2026, and subsequent consultation paper.

## Key Points

- Implements CAS as a 10-minute session from 3:30 p.m. to 3:40 p.m. on all trading days with structured order entry and random closure between 3:38 p.m. and 3:40 p.m.
- Index derivatives trading continues until 3:45 p.m. on all trading days, except on expiry days when expiring index derivatives contracts cease trading at 3:30 p.m.
- Stock derivatives trading continues until 3:45 p.m. on all trading days, including expiry days.
- Post-close session in the cash segment operates from 3:50 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. at the closing price.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modifies provisions of SEBI circular dated January 16, 2026, and Chapter 5 of the Master Circular for Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations dated December 30, 2024.
- Establishes precise session timings, order entry conditions for limit and market orders, and transition rules for unexecuted iceberg orders.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations must align their trading systems, operational workflows, and dissemination mechanisms with the revised CAS and settlement frameworks.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Improves transparency, efficiency in price discovery, and orderly market operations across cash and derivatives segments.
