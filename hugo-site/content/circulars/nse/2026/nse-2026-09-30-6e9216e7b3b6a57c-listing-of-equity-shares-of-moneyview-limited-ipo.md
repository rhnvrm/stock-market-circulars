## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the forthcoming listing of equity shares of Moneyview Limited under the IPO process. The security will trade under the symbol MONEYVIEW with ISIN INE0PTN01011.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Moneyview Limited
- Symbol: MONEYVIEW
- ISIN: INE0PTN01011
- Department: Listing (Download Ref No: NSE/CML/76638, Circular Ref. No: 1747/2026)

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard notification of forthcoming security listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members take note of the security details and ISIN for trading operations upon official listing.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 30, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate circular.

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants to prepare systems for the trading of Moneyview Limited equity shares upon commencement.
