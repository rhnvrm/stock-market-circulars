## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective September 30, 2026. AGS Transact Technologies Limited (AGSTRA) has been shortlisted under Stage I of the ASM IBC framework.

## Key Points

- AGS Transact Technologies Limited (ISIN: INE583L01014) is shortlisted in ASM for Companies as per IBC Stage I w.e.f. September 30, 2026.
- Consolidated list includes securities such as Aban Offshore Limited (ABAN), AGS Transact Technologies Limited (AGSTRA), and Ankit Metal & Power Limited under the ASM IBC framework.
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.
- ASM IBC framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Additional Surveillance Measure under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework for specified companies undergoing corporate insolvency or disclosure requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must note the surveillance status of the listed securities and ensure compliance with applicable margin and trading restrictions under the ASM IBC framework.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities will be subject to additional surveillance measures, potentially impacting liquidity and requiring higher margins or specific trade monitoring for members.
