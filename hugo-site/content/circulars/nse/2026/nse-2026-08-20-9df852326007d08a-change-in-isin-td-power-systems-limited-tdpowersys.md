## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced a change in the ISIN code for the equity shares of TD Power Systems Limited (TDPOWERSYS) due to a stock sub-division from Rs. 2/- to Re. 1/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TD Power Systems Limited
- Symbol: TDPOWERSYS
- New ISIN: INE419M01035
- Reason: Sub-Division of face value from Rs. 2/- to Re. 1/-
- Effective Date: August 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members are advised to take note of the new ISIN for trading and settlement purposes effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date / Effective Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for clearing, settlement, and trading systems following the corporate stock split action.
