## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the admission to dealings of Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Century Extrusions Limited, effective from September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbol: CENTEXT-RE
- Company Name: Century Extrusions Limited
- ISIN: INE281A20018
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 29,978,165
- Trading Period for Renunciation: From September 23, 2026, to October 08, 2026
- Date of Suspension: October 09, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Segment: Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Notification issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares. Contract notes must include a disclaimer stating that purchase of REs only gives the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce them before the issue closes.

## Important Dates

- Commencement of Trading: September 23, 2026
- Renunciation End Date: October 08, 2026
- Date of Suspension: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and investors to trade or renounce the Rights Entitlements of Century Extrusions Limited within the specified trading window.
