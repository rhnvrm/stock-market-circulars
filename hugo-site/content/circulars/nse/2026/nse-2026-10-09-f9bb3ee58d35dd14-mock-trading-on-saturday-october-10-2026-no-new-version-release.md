## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) on Saturday, October 10, 2026. There is no new software version release for this session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, October 10, 2026 in the SLBM segment.
- Trading Session-1 from Primary Site: Normal Market open at 11:00 hours, Contingency Start at 11:45 hours, Contingency Close at 13:15 hours, Normal Market close at 14:00 hours.
- Trading Session-2 from DR Site: Normal Market open at 14:45 hours, Normal Market close at 15:30 hours.
- Live Re-login start time at 18:30 hours, close at 19:00 hours.
- NEAT Version: 1.3.3.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; conducted as per standard mock trading and testing requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff.
- Verify telnet connectivity and Box ID setup.
- Non-NEAT Frontend (NNF) users must ensure user ID is of dealer type, converted for NNF, and mapped with the IP.
- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 10, 2026
- Live Resume / Monday Connectivity: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational mock session ensuring system readiness and connectivity for members with zero financial obligation.
