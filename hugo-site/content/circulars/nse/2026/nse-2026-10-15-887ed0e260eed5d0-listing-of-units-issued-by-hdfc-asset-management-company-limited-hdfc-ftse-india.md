## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing and commencement of trading for units of HDFC FTSE India Equity ETF issued by HDFC Asset Management Company Limited, effective from October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: HDFC FTSE India Equity ETF
- Symbol: HFTSEINDIA
- ISIN: INF179KC1LA2
- Number of Securities: 2,253,502 units
- Face Value / Issue Price: Rs. 30.34/- each
- Date of Allotment: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing circular issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and market lots specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with liquidity and trading access to the HDFC FTSE India Equity ETF on the NSE capital market segment.
