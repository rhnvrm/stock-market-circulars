## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to certain securities. The measures include 100% margin requirements and shifting of eligible scrips to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 08, 2026, on all open positions as on September 07, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) shall be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE) w.e.f. September 08, 2026.
- Shortlisted securities include Bodal Chemicals Limited, ICDS Limited, and Polysil Irrigation Systems Limited across various stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measures as part of ongoing market surveillance frameworks to deter excessive volatility or abnormal price movements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (100%) and adhere to segment restrictions for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 08, 2026
- Open positions cutoff: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and margin blocking for investors holding or trading in the shortlisted securities under the Long-Term ASM framework.
