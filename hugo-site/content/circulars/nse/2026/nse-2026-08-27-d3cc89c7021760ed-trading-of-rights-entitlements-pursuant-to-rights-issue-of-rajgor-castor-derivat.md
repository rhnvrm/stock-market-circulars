## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the commencement of trading for Rights Entitlements (REs) pursuant to the Rights Issue of Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited. Trading will take place on the SME Emerge segment from August 31, 2026, to September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited
- Symbol: RCDL-RE
- ISIN: INE0BZQ20011
- Number of Rights Entitlements: 23,895,000
- Trading Period for Renunciation: August 31, 2026 to September 04, 2026
- Market Lot: 3,000 shares
- Segment: Trade to Trade segment in compulsory demat for all investors

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) - SME EMERGE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Trading members must include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes regarding the terms of REs, including application procedures and the lapse of unapplied/unrenounced REs.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 31, 2026
- Trading Period Start: August 31, 2026
- Trading Period End: September 04, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables existing shareholders and investors to trade Rights Entitlements or apply for shares in the ongoing rights issue of Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited under Trade to Trade settlement.
