## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the extension of the New Fund Offer (NFO) period and allotment date for the Navi Nifty REITs and Realty Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, based on communication from Navi Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- NFO Close Date has been extended from September 10, 2026 to September 11, 2026.
- Allotment Date has been revised from September 15, 2026 to September 16, 2026.
- Clear funds through any payment mode must be received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before September 12, 2026, 03:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational schedule revision requested by the fund house.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must ensure that clear funds are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account by the revised timeline.

## Important Dates

- Revised NFO Close Date: September 11, 2026
- Clear Funds Submission Deadline: September 12, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- Revised Allotment Date: September 16, 2026
- Revised Scheme Reopen Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to participants subscribing to the Navi Nifty REITs and Realty Index Fund NFO.
