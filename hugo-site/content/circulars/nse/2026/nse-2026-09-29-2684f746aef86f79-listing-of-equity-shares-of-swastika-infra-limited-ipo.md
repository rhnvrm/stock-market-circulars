## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of equity shares of Swastika Infra Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** SWASTIKAIN
- **Name of the Company:** Swastika Infra Limited
- **Series:** BE (subsequently to be shifted to Series EQ as per SEBI guidelines)
- **ISIN:** INE1QJA01015
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 185/- per share
- **Total No. of Securities:** 34,120,187 equity shares
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the securities shall be conducted in accordance with NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations and SEBI circulars relating to pre-open sessions for IPO and other scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the 'BE' series designation and ensure adherence to pre-open session norms and lock-in details specified in Annexure I of the circular.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Effective Date / Listing Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Milestones:** Various tranches ending October 27, 2026, December 26, 2026, March 29, 2027, and March 29, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Swastika Infra Limited shares on the NSE capital market segment, expanding investment opportunities and providing market liquidity for shareholders.
