## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued an update on the applicability of the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) framework. Based on the specified surveillance criteria, Mahamaya Steel Industries Limited (MAHASTEEL) has been shortlisted to move from Long-Term ASM Framework Stage I to Stage II. No other new securities have been added to the framework or transitioned to other stages in this update.

## Key Points

- **Surveillance Framework**: Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM).
- **Securities Affected**: Mahamaya Steel Industries Limited (Symbol: MAHASTEEL, ISIN: INE451L01014) is transitioned from Stage I to Stage II.
- **Other ASM Stages**: All other lists (Stage I additions, Stage IV additions, Stage II to III, Stage I to IV, Stage II to IV) reported nil securities.
- **Action Nature**: Shortlisting under the ASM framework is a regular market surveillance measure and should not be considered an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes introduced. This is an operational update in accordance with prevailing surveillance guidelines under pre-existing circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must implement the applicable surveillance actions, including the 100% margin requirement on all open positions of the affected security from the designated effective date.
- **Margin Collection**: 100% margin rate applies starting August 06, 2026, on all open positions as of August 05, 2026, and on any new positions created from August 06, 2026, onwards.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular**: August 03, 2026
- **Effective Date for Stage Transition**: August 04, 2026
- **Applicable Date for 100% Margin**: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Affected Securities**: Only Mahamaya Steel Industries Limited (MAHASTEEL) is impacted.
- **Market/Trading Impact**: The 100% margin requirement on MAHASTEEL from August 06, 2026, may temporarily reduce trading leverage and liquidity for this specific security. No broader market-wide impact is anticipated.
