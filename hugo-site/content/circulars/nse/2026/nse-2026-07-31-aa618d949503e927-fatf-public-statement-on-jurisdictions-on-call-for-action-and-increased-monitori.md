## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) Public Statement on jurisdictions under call for action and increased monitoring from the June 2026 Plenary. Trading members are required to submit their actions taken or a declaration of non-applicability in the FATF module on the ENIT New Compliance portal by August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mandatory Submission:** Trading members must submit actions taken or declare non-applicability for jurisdictions listed in the FATF June 2026 statement.
- **Portal Path:** Submissions must be made via the ENIT New Compliance portal under the path: Compliance >> FATF >> Member Submission.
- **Proprietary-only Members:** Members doing only proprietary trading can submit a "One-Time Declaration."
- **Other Trading Members:** Must use the "Data Submission" tab to enter client details in the exact Excel template or select "not applicable" for jurisdictions with no client presence.
- **Consequences:** Failure to comply within the timelines will attract penal charges or disciplinary actions as prescribed under earlier NSE circulars.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates requirements for tracking, screening, and reporting clients associated with FATF high-risk jurisdictions on call for action or under increased monitoring following the June 2026 Plenary.

## Compliance Requirements

- Access the NSE Member Portal and navigate to ENIT New Compliance -> Compliance -> FATF -> Member Submission.
- Proprietary trading members submit the "One-Time Declaration".
- Other trading members select the applicable statement date under "Data Submission" and complete/upload client data in the specified Excel format without modifying its file name. If no clients exist for a jurisdiction, mark it as "not applicable."
- Ensure final submission is completed before the deadline.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Submission Deadline:** Friday, August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Members:** Medium operational impact. Compliance teams must screen client bases, gather necessary data, and file declarations or Excel-based updates.
- **Market Impact:** Low systemic impact, but necessary to maintain anti-money laundering (AML) regulatory standards across exchange participants.
