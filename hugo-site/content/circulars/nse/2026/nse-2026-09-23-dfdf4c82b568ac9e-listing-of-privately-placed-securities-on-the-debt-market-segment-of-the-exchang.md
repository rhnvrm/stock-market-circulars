## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of several fresh commercial paper issuances admitted to dealings effective September 23, 2026.
- Issuing entities include Godrej Industries Limited, HDB Financial Services Ltd, ICICI Securities Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, Hdfc Securities Limited, and JM Financial Services Limited.
- All securities have a face value of INR 500,000 with varying issue prices, quantities, and redemption dates ranging from October 2026 to March 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- Notification issued pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are notified of the security codes and ISINs for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 23, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Various dates between October 1, 2026 and March 3, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing update for debt market participants with no direct impact on equity segments.
