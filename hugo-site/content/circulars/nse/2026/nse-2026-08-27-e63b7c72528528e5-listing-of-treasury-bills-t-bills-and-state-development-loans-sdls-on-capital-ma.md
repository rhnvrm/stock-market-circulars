## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of various Government Treasury Bills (T-bills) and State Development Loans (SDLs) on its capital market segment effective from August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted: 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day Treasury Bills, along with multiple State Development Loans (SDLs).
- Effective Date: August 31, 2026.
- Trading Lot Size: Standard lot size of 100 for all specified securities.
- Identification: Securities will be identified by their respective designated codes and ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure orders are placed using the designated security codes and correct lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 31, 2026
- Maturity Dates ranging from November 2026 to August 2049 across respective securities.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity and secondary market trading access for investors in government securities and state development loans.
