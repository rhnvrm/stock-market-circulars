## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced a proposed Offer for Sale (OFS) of equity shares of Life Insurance Corporation of India (LICI). The seller intends to offer up to 31,62,49,885 equity shares (Base Offer Size) with an oversubscription option of up to 50,59,99,816 shares, and an additional 50,00,000 shares for eligible employees. The OFS bidding window will open on August 4, 2026, and August 5, 2026. The circular outlines platform details, admin ID setup processes, compliance deadlines for Unique Client Codes (UCC), and margin requirements.

## Key Points

- **Issuer details:** Life Insurance Corporation of India (LICI), ISIN: INE0J1Y01017.
- **OFS Dates:** August 4, 2026 (non-retail category) and August 5, 2026 (retail and employee categories).
- **Issue Size:**
  - Base Offer: Up to 31,62,49,885 Equity shares.
  - Oversubscription Option: Up to 50,59,99,816 Equity shares.
  - Employee Option: Up to 50,00,000 Equity shares.
- **Bidding Platform:** eOFS platform (https://eofs.nseindia.com).
- **Access and Admin IDs:** Eligible members must request Admin IDs via the ENIT portal (ENIT-NEW-TRADE -> Membership -> Enablement -> eOFS) if they have not yet received them.
- **UCC Requirements:** Members must update compliant Unique Client Codes (UCC) in the UCI system at least a day prior to the bidding session. Late updates on the day of the bidding session will result in bidding exclusion.
- **Collateral Management:** Sufficiency of collaterals must be ensured prior to carry-forward of orders on T+1 day. Rejection due to insufficient margins is final.

## Regulatory Changes

- Bidding is governed by the Interoperability among Clearing Corporations guidelines and the Exchange Scheme circular NSE/CMTR/72975 dated February 24, 2026.
- Admin user registration request processing intervals are set at 11:00 a.m., 2:00 p.m., and 3:00 p.m. Requests post 3:00 p.m. will be processed the next day by 8:45 a.m.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Admin Creation:** Members should raise only one request for Admin User creation via the ENIT portal; once raised, requests cannot be modified.
- **UCI Database Update:** Ensure compliant UCC for self (PRO) and client accounts are updated in the UCI database by 22:00 hrs on the previous day to be permitted for bidding.
- **Margin Maintenance:** Pre-verify margin availability on T+1 day carry-forward orders to avoid irreversible order rejection.
- **API Reference:** Technical integration must follow the latest eOFS WEB API documentation available on the NSE platform.

## Important Dates

- **August 3, 2026:** Circular release and deadline (by 22:00 hrs) for updating compliant UCCs in the UCI database for Day 1 bidding.
- **August 4, 2026:** Day 1 of the Offer for Sale (primarily for non-retail bidding session). Intimation of intention to exercise oversubscription must be sent by the seller on or before 5:00 p.m.
- **August 5, 2026:** Day 2 of the Offer for Sale (for retail and employee categories bidding sessions).

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Members:** Demands immediate verification of client lists and compliance statuses in the UCI system. Operational readiness on the eOFS platform and proper setup of Admin accounts are critical to facilitate seamless client bids.
- **Investors:** Offers of this scale in a flagship PSU (LICI) will drive high volume. The clear segregation of categories (IS, RS, ES) ensures streamlined access.
- **Markets:** Large-scale secondary market liquidity event that might temporarily absorb liquidity, although structured across two days to minimize market impact.
