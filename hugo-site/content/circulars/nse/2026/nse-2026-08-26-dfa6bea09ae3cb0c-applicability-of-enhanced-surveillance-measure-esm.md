## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the criteria have been added to ESM Stage-I or moved between stages, attracting 100% margins and a shift to the trade-for-trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I (SABAR, TAURIAN, VALIANTLAB, WOMANCART) are shortlisted for ESM Stage-I and will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 28, 2026, on all open positions as on August 27, 2026, and new positions created from August 28, 2026.
- WELINV is moving from ESM Stage-I to Stage-II.
- Securities shifting to ESM shall be moved from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 28, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 27, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) Stage-I and Stage-II criteria.
- Mandatory 100% margin requirements and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (100%) on open and new positions for securities listed under ESM.
- Trades in affected securities will settle in the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II price band and movement: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shift: August 28, 2026 (on open positions as on August 27, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement for the impacted securities (SABAR, TAURIAN, VALIANTLAB, WOMANCART, WELINV) will be restricted due to the 100% margin requirement, shift to the trade-for-trade segment, and periodic call auction price bands.
