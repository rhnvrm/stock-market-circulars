## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the listing of 23,913,000 equity shares of Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited allotted under a rights issue, effective from October 06, 2026. The shares are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment - SME EMERGE under the symbol RCDL.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited
- **Symbol:** RCDL
- **Series:** SM
- **ISIN:** INE0BZQ01011
- **Security Description:** Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted under Rights Issue
- **Date of Allotment:** 30-September-2026
- **Number of Securities:** 23,913,000
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 23915853 to 47828852
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 10/- each
- **Market Lot:** 3,000 shares
- **Effective Date:** October 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of further issue of equity shares to dealings on the SME Emerge segment pursuant to Regulations 3.1.1 and 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Dematerialized shares credited under temporary ISIN in accordance with SEBI circulars. Trading shall be in designated codes and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 30, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity capital base of Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited through rights issue allotment, enhancing liquidity and trading activity for existing shareholders in the SME Emerge segment.
