## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued an update regarding the proposed revision of the Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA) and Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) structure on the NSE MF Invest Platform. Following feedback from Registrar and Transfer Agents (RTAs) who are still evaluating the structure and expecting additional changes, the implementation of the revised structure has been deferred. The existing FATCA/UBO structure will continue until further notice.

## Key Points

- **Postponement of Revision:** The planned revision to the FATCA/UBO structure, originally referenced in circular ref no. NSE/NMFTM/74386 dated May 26, 2026, has been deferred.
- **RTA Evaluation:** Registrar and Transfer Agents (RTAs) are still evaluating the proposed structure and anticipate additional modifications.
- **Status Quo Maintained:** The existing FATCA/UBO structure on the NSE MF Invest Platform will continue to remain in effect until further notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Circular Reference:** Refer to previous circular NSE/NMFTM/74386 dated May 26, 2026.
- There are no active regulatory or structural changes being implemented at this time, as the planned changes are deferred.

## Compliance Requirements

- **No Immediate Action:** Trading Members and participants on the NSE MF Invest Platform do not need to update or modify their current FATCA/UBO integration or reporting structures.
- **Continue Existing Processes:** Members must continue using the existing FATCA/UBO structure for submissions until further notice.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Effective Date:** Existing structure remains active indefinitely (until further notice).

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Low. Since the existing system remains operational, there is no immediate need for system refactoring, testing, or migration to a new FATCA/UBO structure by the members.
- **System Stability:** High. Maintaining the status quo avoids potential disruptions or integration failures while RTAs refine the requirements.
