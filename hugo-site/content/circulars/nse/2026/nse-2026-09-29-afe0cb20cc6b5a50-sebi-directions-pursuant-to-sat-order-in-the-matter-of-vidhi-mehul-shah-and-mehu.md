## Summary

SEBI has issued a final order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1), and 11B(2) of the SEBI Act, 1992, pursuant to the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order in the matter of Vidhi Mehul Shah and Mehul Hasmukh Shah, involving multiple entities and scrips such as Robert Resources Ltd., Econo Trade India Ltd., Mauria Udyog Ltd., and others.

## Key Points

- Final order issued by SEBI under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1), and 11B(2) of the SEBI Act, 1992.
- Proceedings involve numerous entities and companies including Robert Resources Ltd., Econo Trade India Ltd., Econo Broking Pvt. Ltd., Sai Metaltech LLP, Mauria Udyog Ltd., Vee Em Infocenter Ltd., and individuals.
- Directions issued pursuant to SAT order in the matter of Vidhi Mehul Shah and Mehul Hasmukh Shah.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement directions, restrictions, and regulatory measures imposed on non-compliant entities and connected persons as per SEBI's final order.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities named in the order must adhere strictly to the directions, prohibitions, and remedial measures mandated by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified in the SEBI order.

## Impact Assessment

High impact on the named entities and associated scrips regarding trading restrictions, regulatory standing, and compliance enforcement.
