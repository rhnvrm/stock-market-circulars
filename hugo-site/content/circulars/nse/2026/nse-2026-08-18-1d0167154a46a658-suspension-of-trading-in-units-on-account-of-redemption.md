## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in units of four schemes under SBI Mutual Fund - SBI FMP - Series 49, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A, on account of redemption. The suspension will take effect from August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Four schemes of SBI Mutual Fund - SBI FMP - Series 49 (1823 Days) are suspended due to redemption.
- Suspension Date: August 20, 2026.
- Closing hours of trading: August 19, 2026.
- Affected Symbols: SFMP49GR, SFMP49DR, SFMP49GD, SFMP49DD.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note that trading in the suspended schemes will not be available after the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Last Date of Trading: August 19, 2026
- Suspension Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine cessation of trading for matured fixed maturity plan units; negligible impact on broader market operations.
