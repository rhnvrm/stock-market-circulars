## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I for specific securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I are listed in Annexure I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum rate of margin capped at 100%.
- The surveillance action is effective from August 19, 2026, on all open positions as on August 18, 2026, and new positions created from August 19, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is a surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I surveillance framework on specified scrips.
- Margin requirements revised to 50% minimum (capped at 100%) for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance for open and new positions in the affected securities in accordance with the ST-ASM guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on August 18, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations for traders holding or taking positions in securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in the affected scrips.
