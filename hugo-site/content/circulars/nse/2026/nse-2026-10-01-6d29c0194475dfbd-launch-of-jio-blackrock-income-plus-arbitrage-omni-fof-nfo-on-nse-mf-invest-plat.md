## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced that the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Jio BlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF (Direct Growth and Regular Growth variants) will be available for transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform from October 05, 2026, to October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Jio BlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF - Direct Growth & Regular Growth
- Category: Debt
- ISINs: INF22M001382 (Direct Growth), INF22M001390 (Regular Growth)
- NFO Window: October 05, 2026 (starting) to October 19, 2026 (till 06:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; this is a standard NFO launch notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that clear funds through any payment mode are received in the designated Clearing Corporation account on or before October 22, 2026, till 03:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: October 05, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: October 19, 2026 (06:00 pm)
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 22, 2026 (03:00 pm)
- Allotment Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors access to subscribe to the new debt-category fund of Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund via the NSE MF Invest platform.
