## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding measures for companies with high Promoter as well as non-Promoter 'Encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Certain securities are moving out of the framework, while a consolidated list is maintained.

## Key Points

- Securities eligible to move out from the framework effective from September 01, 2026.
- Elpro International Limited, Steel Exchange India Limited, and Thyrocare Technologies Limited are moving out of the framework.
- Indo Tech Transformers Limited remains in the consolidated list of securities under the framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of surveillance measures under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the securities included, excluded, or continuing under the framework.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for exclusions: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants dealing with the specified securities need to adjust their monitoring and compliance as per the revised list.
