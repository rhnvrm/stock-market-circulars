## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the shifting of trading in securities of specified companies to the 'Z' Category ('BZ' Series) effective from October 05, 2026, due to non-compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Rajvir Industries Limited has been identified as non-compliant under Regulation 27(2) (Submission of Corporate Governance Report) for two consecutive quarters (March 31, 2026, and June 30, 2026). Its trades will continue to be settled on a Trade for Trade basis in the Z category.
- Cressanda Railway Solutions Limited (CRSL), traded under the 'Permitted for Trade' category at NSE, is non-compliant under Regulation 18(1) (Constitution of Audit Committee) for the same two consecutive quarters at BSE Limited.
- Trading in securities of Cressanda Railway Solutions Limited will be transferred to the 'Z' Category ('BZ' Series) effective October 05, 2026, unless compliance is achieved on or before September 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 regarding actions against non-compliant listed entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Affected companies must fulfill the pending compliance requirements under the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 within the stipulated timelines to avoid trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Compliance Deadline for Cressanda Railway Solutions Limited: September 29, 2026.
- Effective Date for Transfer to Z Category: October 05, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Securities transferred to the 'Z' category face increased trading restrictions and are settled on a Trade for Trade basis, impacting liquidity and market operations for the affected scrips.
