## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has notified all members, custodians, and professional clearing members (PCMs) regarding the settlement calendar and operational guidelines for the Takeover Offer under the Tender Offer scheme of Niraj Cement Structurals Limited (NIRAJ). The circular details the trade and settlement dates, early pay-in instructions, and guidelines for order acceptance, rejection, and tax collections.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Schedule**: Establishes the timeline for Settlement No. 2026094.
- **Tendering Mechanism**: Demat shares must be tendered specifically by placing an Early Pay-in of Shares (EPI) via depositories under the "Market Type Buyback" mechanism.
- **Cut-off Deadline**: Demat shares must be tendered with EPI well in advance for order acceptance up to 03:30 p.m. on the trade end date.
- **Acceptance/Rejection Reports**: Clearing members will receive a Tender Offer Acceptance Report highlighting accepted, partially accepted, and rejected bids, with relevant rejection codes (detailed in Annexure I).
- **Taxation**: Applicable Securities Transaction Tax (STT) will be collected on the respective settlement day.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory framework is introduced. This circular applies the existing SEBI/NSE tender offer and clearing guidelines to this specific corporate takeover action.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members/Custodians**: Must ensure that client shares are tendered strictly using the early pay-in mechanism for the "Buyback" market type.
- **Deadline Adherence**: Ensure all EPI processes are finalized prior to the 03:30 p.m. daily cut-off on the trade end date (August 13, 2026).
- **Funds Pay-in**: Buying trading members must complete the funds pay-in on time on the designated settlement day to prevent overall settlement delays.

## Important Dates

- **Settlement Type**: X
- **Settlement Number**: 2026094
- **Trade Start Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Trade End Date**: August 13, 2026
- **Obligation Download**: August 18, 2026
- **Settlement Date**: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. The circular is limited to the shareholders and market participants of Niraj Cement Structurals Limited.
- **Operational Impact**: Medium. Trading members and custodians processing tenders for this scheme must align their operations with the specific timelines, depository codes, and funds pay-in requirements.
