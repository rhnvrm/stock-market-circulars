## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the availability of various plans under the Edelweiss Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes are available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Includes both Direct and Regular plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- Applicable ISINs cover multiple options under Edelweiss Mutual Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Members may note the ISIN details and scheme availability for investor transactions on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading and investment operations for the specified Edelweiss Mutual Fund schemes on the NSE platform.
