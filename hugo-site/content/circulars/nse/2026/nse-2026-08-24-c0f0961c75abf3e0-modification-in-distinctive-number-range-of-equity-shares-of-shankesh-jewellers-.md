## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the modification in the distinctive number range and lock-in of equity shares of Shankesh Jewellers Limited pursuant to its IPO, based on an amendment and rectification requested by the company.

## Key Points

- Modifies the distinctive number ranges and lock-in details previously issued via circular ref. no. NSE/CML/75930 dated August 24, 2026.
- Applies to Shankesh Jewellers Limited equity shares listed pursuant to IPO.
- Specifies revised lock-in periods ranging from September 2026 to February 2028 for various tranches of shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- Rectification of distinctive number ranges and lock-in schedules as requested by the issuer.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and members are requested to take note of the revised distinctive number ranges and lock-in details for Shankesh Jewellers Limited equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 24, 2026
- Various lock-in expiry dates ranging from September 19, 2026 to February 25, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

- Informational update for trading members to align records with the corrected distinctive number ranges and lock-in periods.
