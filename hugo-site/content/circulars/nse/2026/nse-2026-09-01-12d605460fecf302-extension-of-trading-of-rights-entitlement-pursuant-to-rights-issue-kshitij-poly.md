## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the extension of trading of Rights Entitlements (RE) and the revision of the rights issue closure period for Kshitij Polyline Limited. The last date for on-market renunciation is extended to September 04, 2026, and the date of suspension is revised to September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kshitij Polyline Limited
- Symbol: KSHITIJPOL, KSHITIJ-RE
- Last Date for On-market Renunciation (New): September 04, 2026
- Date of Suspension (New): September 07, 2026
- Issue closing date extended from Friday, September 04, 2026 to Wednesday, September 09, 2026 (an extension of 5 days).

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of timeline for the Rights Issue of 6,16,97,950 fully paid-up Equity Shares aggregating up to ₹1,955.83 lakh.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the revised dates for on-market renunciation and trading suspension of rights entitlements.

## Important Dates

- Last Date for On-Market Renunciation: September 04, 2026
- Date of Suspension: September 07, 2026
- Revised Rights Issue Closing Date: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding rights entitlements have extended time to renounce their entitlements on the market up to September 04, 2026.
