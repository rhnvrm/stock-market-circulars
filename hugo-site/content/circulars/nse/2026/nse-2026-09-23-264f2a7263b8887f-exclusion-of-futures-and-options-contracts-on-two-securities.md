## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on two securities: Indian Energy Exchange Limited (IEX) and Indian Renewable Energy Development Agency Limited (IREDA), pursuant to SEBI circular guidelines and exchange eligibility criteria.

## Key Points

- New expiry month contracts for IEX and IREDA will not be issued upon the expiry of existing contract months.
- Existing unexpired contracts for September 2026, October 2026, and November 2026 will continue to trade till their respective expiry dates, and new strikes will be introduced in these existing contract months.
- No F&O contracts will be available for trading in IEX and IREDA with effect from November 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Exclusion executed in reference to SEBI Circular Ref. No: SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024, and chapter 3.5 of Exchange circular No. NSE/FAOP/73928 dated April 28, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the non-issuance of new expiry contracts and manage existing open positions in IEX and IREDA up to November 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date for Cessation of All Trading: November 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding positions in IEX and IREDA F&O contracts must square off or settle their positions before the final exclusion date of November 25, 2026.
