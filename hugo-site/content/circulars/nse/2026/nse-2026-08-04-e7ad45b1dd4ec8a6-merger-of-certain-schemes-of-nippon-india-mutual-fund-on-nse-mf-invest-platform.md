## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the merger of certain mutual fund schemes of Nippon India Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. The Board of Directors of Nippon Life India Trustee Limited (NLITL) and Nippon Life India Asset Management Limited (NAM India) have approved the merger of 'Nippon India Nifty AAA PSU Bond Plus SDL - Sep 2026 Maturity 50 50 Index Fund' (Merging Scheme) into 'Nippon India Corporate Bond Fund' (Surviving Scheme). SEBI has communicated its no-objection to this merger, which constitutes a change in the fundamental attributes of the scheme under SEBI Mutual Fund regulations.

## Key Points

- **Merging Scheme**: Nippon India Nifty AAA PSU Bond Plus SDL - Sep 2026 Maturity 50 50 Index Fund.
- **Surviving Scheme**: Nippon India Corporate Bond Fund.
- **Regulatory No-Objection**: Received from SEBI via email dated July 14, 2026.
- **Fundamental Attribute Change**: The proposed merger will be carried out by implementing a change in the fundamental attributes of the schemes under Regulation 22(9)(c) of SEBI (MF) Regulations 2026.
- **Allotment of Units**: Unit holders of the Merging Scheme who agree with the merger will be allotted units in the Surviving Scheme based on the Net Asset Value (NAV) on the Effective Date of the merger.
- **Lien Restrictions**: Only free (unencumbered) units in the merging scheme will be eligible for merger into the surviving scheme.

## Regulatory Changes

- The merger of schemes complies with **Regulation 22(9)(c) of the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026** and **Clause 1.9.2 of the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated March 20, 2026**.
- This action results in a change to the fundamental attributes of the schemes, which incorporated comments from SEBI prior to implementation.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Asset Management Company (AMC)**: Nippon Life India Asset Management Limited must execute the merger, ensure proper valuation, and transition assets from the merging scheme to the surviving scheme.
- **Platform Operators**: The NSE MF Invest Platform and registered intermediaries must update scheme codes, suspend subscriptions to the merging scheme, and facilitate the transition of unit holdings.
- **Unit Holders**: Unit holders of the Merging Scheme must decide whether to accept the merger and have their holdings transitioned, or exit the scheme prior to the merger's effective date.

## Important Dates

- **SEBI No-Objection Date**: July 14, 2026
- **Index Maturity and Termination Date**: September 02, 2026 (The target maturity index of the Merging Scheme matures and terminates on this date).
- **Effective Date of Merger**: To be announced by the AMC, on which units will be converted based on the prevailing NAV.

## Impact Assessment

- **Investor Impact**: Investors in the merging index fund are provided an opportunity to remain invested in fixed-income assets. However, the Surviving Scheme (Nippon India Corporate Bond Fund) has a slightly higher risk profile, carrying a Moderate Credit Risk (Scheme Risk-o-meter: Moderate) compared to the Low Credit Risk (Scheme Risk-o-meter: Low) of the Merging Scheme.
- **Operational Impact**: Simplifies product offerings for the AMC by merging a terminating target maturity index fund. Trading and transaction systems on the NSE MF Invest Platform will require configuration to process the unit swap.
