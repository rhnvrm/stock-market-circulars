## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing Market (SLBM) on Saturday, September 19, 2026. No new software version release is scheduled.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: September 19, 2026.
- Normal Market open time (DR Site): 11:15 hours; Normal Market close time: 12:30 hours.
- Live Re-login start time: 14:00 hours; Live Re-login close time: 14:30 hours.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members should participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, September 21, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.
- Ensure telnet connectivity to the Exchange host from the intended IP address.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 19, 2026
- Live Environment resumption: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational readiness check for trading members participating in the SLBM segment to ensure system connectivity and smooth operations for the upcoming week.
