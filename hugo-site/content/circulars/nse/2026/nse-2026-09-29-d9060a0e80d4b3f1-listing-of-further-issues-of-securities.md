## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for five companies on the SME Emerge segment. The listing becomes effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Companies involved: De Neers Tools Limited, Lead Reclaim and Rubber Products Limited, Magson Retail and Distribution Limited, Sheetal Universal Limited, and Manas Polymers and Energies Limited.
- Segment: SME Emerge (Capital Market Segment).
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026.
- Securities admitted under preferential basis with specific lot sizes, face values, and lock-in details as specified in the circular annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard administrative listing notification pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE Capital Market Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and mandatory market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026
- Allotment Dates vary by company (e.g., De Neers: 23-Jul-2026, Lead Reclaim: 10-Jul-2026, Magson Retail: 04-Aug-2026).

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing update for the listed SME companies with minimal direct impact on general market operations.
