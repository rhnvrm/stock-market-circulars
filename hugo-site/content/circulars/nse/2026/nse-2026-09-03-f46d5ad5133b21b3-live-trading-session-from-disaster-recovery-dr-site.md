## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the scheduled live trading sessions from its Disaster Recovery (DR) site on September 03, September 04, and September 07, 2026, across multiple segments including Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, SLB, and EGR.

## Key Points

- Live trading sessions from DR site are scheduled for September 3 to September 4, and September 7, 2026.
- No changes in NEAT Adapter settings are required for connecting to the Primary/DR site.
- Applicable segments include Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, SLB, and EGR.
- Web-based platforms (eIPO, eOFS, CBRICS, RFQ, EBP, NDM, Negotiated Trade Reporting, and Tri-Party Repo Market Platform) will use existing URLs for live bidding from Primary/DR site.
- Colocation facility and Multicast Tick by Tick (MTBT) feed will be available on production IPs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational transition to Disaster Recovery site in accordance with established business continuity planning (BCP) frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure proper connectivity to the extranet and use designated IP addresses and APIs.
- MTBT users are advised to subscribe to both multicast channels (Source 1 and Source 2) in an active-active manner to minimize potential data loss during broadcast.

## Important Dates

- September 03, 2026: Live Trading Session from DR Site
- September 04, 2026: Live Trading Session from DR Site
- September 07, 2026: Live Trading Session from Primary Site

## Impact Assessment

- Latencies experienced by colocation participants may differ during the DR/BCP trading day compared to normal trading days.
