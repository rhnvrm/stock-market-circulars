## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) issued a circular regarding measures for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities meeting the criteria attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.

## Key Points

- Minimum 75% margin applied on Equity and Equity Derivatives open and new positions for shortlisted securities.
- Security AQYLON is excluded from the encumbrance framework and moved to LTASM effective August 17, 2026.
- Consolidated list includes INDOTECH, STEELXIND, THYROCARE, and ELPROINTL.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of surveillance measures as per SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011, requiring stringent margin rates on companies exhibiting high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance levels.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the inclusion, exclusion, and consolidated lists and ensure appropriate margin collections on all open and new positions as per the effective dates.

## Important Dates

- **Exclusion Effective Date:** August 17, 2026 (for Annexure II securities)
- **Position Assessment Date:** August 18, 2026 (open positions)
- **Measure Effective Date:** August 19, 2026 (margin requirements apply)

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in affected securities face increased margin requirements (minimum 75%), impacting liquidity and trading strategies in the equity and derivative segments.
