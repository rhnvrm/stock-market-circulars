## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued an update in continuation of previous circulars regarding the streamlining of End-of-Day (EOD) information dissemination for Bhavcopy. Effective October 12, 2026, the Exchange will additionally disseminate the CM segment bhavcopy report in .DAT format on the Extranet.

## Key Points

- Continuation of circulars NSE/MSD/74764 (June 18, 2026) and NSE/MSD/75001 (July 02, 2026).
- Additional dissemination of the bhavcopy report (.DAT format) on the Extranet for the Capital Market (CM) segment.
- File Name: `FCM_INTRM_BCDDMMYYYY.DAT`
- Extranet Path: `/cmftp/common/bhavcopy`
- Existing website download links remain available at https://www.nseindia.com/all-reports.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this circular specifies technological and operational enhancements for data dissemination channels.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the additional Extranet path for automated fetching of EOD bhavcopy reports starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 12, 2026
- Circular Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides members with an alternative secure Extranet channel to access EOD bhavcopy data, improving reliability and workflow automation for back-office systems.
