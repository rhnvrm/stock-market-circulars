## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives (F&O) segment on Saturday, September 26, 2026. The session aims to ensure smooth connectivity and system readiness for members.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, September 26, 2026, in the F&O segment.
- NEAT+ version 7.9.0 must be used explicitly for the revised Pre-Open framework.
- Existing NEAT+ version 7.8.9 is scheduled for discontinuation on October 10, 2026 (mock).
- Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; procedural guidelines for mock trading and software version compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must participate using trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, September 28, 2026.
- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before cutoff will be allowed.
- NEAT+ 7.9.0 required for the revised Pre-Open framework (/faoftp/faocommon/NEATPlus790).

## Important Dates

- Mock Date: September 26, 2026
- NEAT+ 7.8.9 Discontinuation Date (mock): October 10, 2026
- Live Re-login Start Time (Mock Day): 18:00 Hrs
- Live Re-login Close Time (Mock Day): 18:30 Hrs

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness for F&O segment members with no financial impact or live risk.
