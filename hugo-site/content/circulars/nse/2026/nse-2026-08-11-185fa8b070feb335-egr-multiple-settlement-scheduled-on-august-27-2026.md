## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited (NCL) has rescheduled the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment settlements due to a scheduled settlement holiday on August 26, 2026. To accommodate this, three distinct settlements (Nos. 2026071, 2026072, and 2026073) will be processed on August 27, 2026. This circular outlines the revised schedule, revised auction cycle timelines, and specific upload windows for UNPAID MTF files.

## Key Points

- **Rescheduled Settlements on August 27, 2026**:
  - **Settlement 2026071**: Trade Date August 25, 2026; Pay-in at 09:30 hrs, Pay-out at 11:30 hrs.
  - **Settlement 2026072**: Trade Date August 25, 2026; Pay-in at 14:00 hrs, Pay-out at 16:30 hrs.
  - **Settlement 2026073**: Trade Date August 26, 2026; Pay-in at 17:30 hrs, Pay-out at 20:00 hrs.
- **Revised Auction Cycle for T+1**:
  - **Auction Settlement 2026072**: Conducted on Trade Date August 27, 2026; Settlement Date August 28, 2026.
  - **Auction Settlement 2026074**: Conducted on Trade Date August 28, 2026; Settlement Date August 31, 2026.
  - **No Auction for 2026073**: Shortages for Settlement 2026073 will be conducted with shortages of 2026074. No separate auction will be held for 2026073.
- **UNPAID MTF Upload Timings**:
  - **Settlement 2026072** (Trade Date August 25): Upload on August 26 from 07:30 to 23:00, and on August 27 from 07:30 to 13:00.
  - **Settlement 2026073** (Trade Date August 26): Upload on August 27 from 07:30 to 16:30 (No upload facility on August 26).

## Regulatory Changes

- No structural or regulatory policy changes are introduced by this circular. It is a procedural settlement-holiday modification pursuant to existing clearing and settlement guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Depository End Cut-offs**: Members must monitor and adhere to the respective depositories' revised cut-off timings for pay-in instructions to align with the revised settlement hours.
- **UNPAID MTF Upload Windows**: Trading members uploading UNPAID MTF files for EGR Normal must adhere strictly to the modified deadlines (concluding at 13:00 hrs for 2026072 and 16:30 hrs for 2026073 on August 27, 2026).

## Important Dates

- **August 11, 2026**: Date of NCL circular issuance.
- **August 25, 2026**: Trade date for Settlements 2026071 and 2026072.
- **August 26, 2026**: Settlement holiday. UNPAID MTF upload window opens for Settlement 2026072 from 07:30 to 23:00 hrs.
- **August 27, 2026**: Execution of multiple settlements (2026071, 2026072, 2026073). Auction trade date for 2026072.
- **August 28, 2026**: Settlement date for Auction 2026072. Trade date for Auction 2026074.
- **August 31, 2026**: Settlement date for Auction 2026074 (encompassing merged shortages from Settlement 2026073).

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational & Cleardown Impact**: High concentration of operational steps on August 27, 2026. Back-offices must coordinate three distinct pay-in and pay-out processes in rapid succession.
- **Shortage Mitigation**: The deferment and consolidation of shortages from Settlement 2026073 into Settlement 2026074 means physical deliveries of gold receipts for shortages occurring on August 26 trades will experience a delay, settling on August 31 instead.
