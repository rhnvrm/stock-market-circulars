## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the introduction of a weekly frequency under the Income Distribution cum withdrawal (IDCW) option for the Bajaj Finserv Money Market Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Details:** Bajaj Finserv Money Market Fund Direct Plan Weekly IDCW (ISIN: INF0QA701CK6) and Regular Plan Weekly IDCW (ISIN: INF0QA701CJ8).
- **Effective Date:** August 14, 2026.
- **Payout & Reinvestment:** The IDCW option offers payout facilities on a weekly basis. IDCW amounts equal to or less than Rs. 100/- will be compulsorily reinvested in the same plan and option.
- **Record Date:** Every Monday or the immediately succeeding business day if Monday is a non-business day.

## Regulatory Changes

- Addendum to the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) of Bajaj Finserv Money Market Fund reflecting the new weekly IDCW frequency.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and investors are advised to take note of the newly added scheme options and ISINs on the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Notice Date:** July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides enhanced payout flexibility for investors holding units under the Bajaj Finserv Money Market Fund IDCW option with weekly distribution preferences.
