## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced that the Invesco India Pharma and Healthcare Fund is reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes made available include Direct and Regular plans for Growth and IDCW (Payout and Reinvestment).
- Effective Date: September 10, 2026.
- Applicable ISINs cover multiple options under the Invesco India Pharma and Healthcare Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants must note the ISIN details and scheme availability on the platform for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides expanded investment options for market participants using the NSE MF Invest platform for mutual fund transactions.
