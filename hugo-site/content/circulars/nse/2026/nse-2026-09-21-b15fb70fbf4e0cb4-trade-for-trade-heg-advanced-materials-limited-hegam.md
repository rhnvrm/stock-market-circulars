## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the transfer of HEG Advanced Materials Limited (formerly known as HEG Limited) from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ).

## Key Points

- Company Name: HEG Advanced Materials Limited (formerly known as HEG Limited)
- Symbol/Identifier: HEGAM
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026
- Reference to previous circular: NSE/CML/76106 dated September 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI guidelines vide circular reference no CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, the security is moved to the rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants need to update their systems to reflect the change in series for HEG Advanced Materials Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in equity shares of HEG Advanced Materials Limited will now take place in the rolling segment (series: EQ), improving liquidity and standard settlement cycles compared to the trade for trade segment.
