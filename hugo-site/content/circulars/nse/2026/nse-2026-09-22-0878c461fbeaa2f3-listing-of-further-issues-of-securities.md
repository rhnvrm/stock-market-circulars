## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has notified the admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for several companies with effect from September 23, 2026. The issuances are primarily a result of ESOP exercises and schemes of arrangement.

## Key Points

- Companies involved include Avantel Limited, Gland Pharma Limited, Larsen & Toubro Limited, Orchid Pharma Limited, Shadowfax Technologies Limited, TATA CONSUMER PRODUCTS LIMITED, Tejas Networks Limited, UTI Asset Management Company Limited, Vimta Labs Limited, and Viyash Scientific Limited.
- Shares admitted under ESOP and schemes of arrangement will rank pari passu with existing equity shares.
- Effective date for trading of these further issues is September 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Notification issued in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure trading is conducted using designated security codes and correct lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action impacting share capital of the respective listed entities without systemic market impact.
