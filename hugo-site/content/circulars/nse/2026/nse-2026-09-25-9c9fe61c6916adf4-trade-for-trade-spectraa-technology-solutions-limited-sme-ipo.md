## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of SpectraA Technology Solutions Limited from the trade for trade segment (Series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM) pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SpectraA Technology Solutions Limited
- Symbol: SPECTRAA
- Transfer from: Trade for Trade segment (Series: ST)
- Transfer to: Rolling segment (Series: SM)
- Effective Date: October 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are moved from trade-for-trade settlement to regular rolling settlement in accordance with exchange circular NSE/CML/76503 dated September 23, 2026 and SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the change in series and segment for trading in the security.

## Important Dates

- Previous Circular Reference: September 23, 2026 (NSE/CML/76503)
- Effective Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for investors in SpectraA Technology Solutions Limited, enhancing trading liquidity.
