## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing and admission to dealings of units issued by Jio BlackRock Asset Management Private Limited under the JioBlackRock Nifty 50 ETF scheme, effective from August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: JioBlackRock Nifty 50 ETF
- Symbol: NIFTYJBLK
- ISIN: INF22M001184
- Number of Units: 6,2,902,315
- Face Value and Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- per unit
- Issue Price: Rs. 12.50/- per unit
- Date of Allotment: August 14, 2026
- Market Lot: 1 unit
- Market Makers: East India Securities Limited, Zanskar Securities Private Limited, IRAGE Broking Services LLP, and Choice Equity Broking Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with trading regulations regarding designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of JioBlackRock Nifty 50 ETF units on the NSE Capital Market segment.
