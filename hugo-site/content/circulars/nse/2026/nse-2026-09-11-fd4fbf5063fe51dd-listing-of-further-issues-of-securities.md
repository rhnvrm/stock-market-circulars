## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular announcing the listing and admission to dealings of further issues of equity shares for several companies, including preferential issues and ESOP allotments, effective from September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Further issues of securities admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective September 15, 2026.
- Includes shares allotted under preferential issues and ESOPs across multiple companies such as Bhagyanagar India Limited, CreditAccess Grameen Limited, Digitide Solutions Limited, India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited, and others.
- Trading will be identified by designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Companies and trading members must ensure compliance with trading regulations and designated lot sizes for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 11, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update facilitating the trading of newly allotted shares and ESOPs for the listed entities.
