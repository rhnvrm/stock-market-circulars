## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities shortlisted under ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective August 24, 2026, based on criteria satisfaction.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I are included under ESM Stage I, attracting a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 24, 2026, on open positions as on August 21, 2026, and new positions created from August 24, 2026.
- Affected securities will be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 24, 2026.
- Investment & Precision Castings Limited moves from ESM Stage I to Stage II w.e.f. August 21, 2026, placing it under Trade for Trade with a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures including 100% margins and movement to Trade-for-Trade segment for specified securities.
- Shifting of securities to Stage II invokes Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (minimum 100%) for open and new positions in shortlisted securities.
- Trades in applicable securities will settle under the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II movement and price band applicability: August 21, 2026.
- Effective Date for margin requirements and segment shifting (EQ/SM to BE/ST): August 24, 2026 (on open positions as of August 21, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities will experience increased margin requirements and restricted settlement mechanisms (Trade-for-Trade), which may impact liquidity and volatility for the affected scrips.
