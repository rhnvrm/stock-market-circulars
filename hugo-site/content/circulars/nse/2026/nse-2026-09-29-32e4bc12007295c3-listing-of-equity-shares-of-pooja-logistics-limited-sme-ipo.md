## Summary

The National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares and shareholding pattern of Pooja Logistics Limited following its SME IPO, filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: Pooja Logistics Limited
- Event: Post-Issue of SME IPO Shareholding Pattern and Listing
- Filing Regulation: Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015
- Date of Filing: 29-SEP-2026
- Class of Security: Equity Shares

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; standard compliance filing and listing notification under SEBI LODR regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure timely submission of shareholding patterns and compliance with post-IPO listing guidelines as mandated by the stock exchange and SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Filing/Listing Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of equity shares of Pooja Logistics Limited on the NSE SME platform, providing liquidity to investors and fulfilling statutory disclosure requirements.
