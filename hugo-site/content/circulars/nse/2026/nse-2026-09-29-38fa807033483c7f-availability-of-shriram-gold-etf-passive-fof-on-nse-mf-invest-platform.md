## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the availability of Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF schemes of Shriram Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective date: September 30, 2026.
- Includes multiple plans such as Direct Plan-Growth, Regular Plan-Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options across various ISINs (INF680P01653, INF680P01661, INF680P01695, INF680P01703, INF680P01679, INF680P01687).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants must note the availability of these schemes on the NSE MF Invest Platform for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment options for market participants and investors accessing gold ETF fund-of-funds via the NSE MF platform.
