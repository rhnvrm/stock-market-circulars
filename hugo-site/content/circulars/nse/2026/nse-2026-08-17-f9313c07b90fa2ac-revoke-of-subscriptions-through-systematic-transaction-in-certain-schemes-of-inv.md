## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the resumption of subscriptions through systematic transactions in certain overseas schemes of Invesco Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Temporary suspension of subscription is revoked for already registered Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and IDCW Transfer Plan.
- Applicable schemes:
  1. Invesco India - Invesco Global Equity Income Fund of Fund
  2. Invesco India - Invesco Pan European Equity Fund of Fund
  3. Invesco India - Invesco Global Consumer Trends Fund of Fund
- Subscriptions through these already registered installments will be allowed up to available headroom without breaching overseas investment limits as of February 1, 2022.
- Temporary suspension on lumpsum purchases, switch-ins, and fresh registrations of SIP/STP/IDCW Transfer Plan remains in force.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial relaxation of investment restrictions on designated overseas Fund of Fund schemes of Invesco Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must note the resumption parameters and ensure compliance with available overseas investment limits.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding already registered systematic plans in the designated overseas schemes can resume their installment processing subject to fund-level headroom limits.
