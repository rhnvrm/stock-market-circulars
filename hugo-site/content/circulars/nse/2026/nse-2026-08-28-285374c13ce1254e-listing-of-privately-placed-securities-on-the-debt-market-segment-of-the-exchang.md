## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Admission of commercial papers issued by multiple entities including Godrej Properties Limited, LIC Housing Finance Limited, Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited, Aditya Birla Money Limited, HDFC Securities Limited, Angel One Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, HDB Financial Services Ltd, Tata Steel Limited, and Aditya Birla Capital Limited.
- Effective date of dealing: August 28, 2026.
- All securities are fresh issues of commercial papers with varying maturity dates ranging from October 2026 to November 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notice pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must adhere to standard depository and exchange reporting requirements for commercial paper listings.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** August 28, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** October-November 2026 (varying by issuer)

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational listing update with no direct market-wide regulatory impact.
