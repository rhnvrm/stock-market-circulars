## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing all members about the availability and reopening of Quantum Flexi cap Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform with effect from September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Quantum Flexi Cap - Direct Growth and Quantum Flexi Cap - Regular Growth.
- Facilities available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members may note the ISIN details and scheme availability for investor transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to transact in the Quantum Flexi Cap Fund schemes via the NSE MF Invest Platform.
