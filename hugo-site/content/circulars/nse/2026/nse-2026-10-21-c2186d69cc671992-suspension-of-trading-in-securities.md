## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has announced the suspension of trading in securities of Wardwizard Innovations & Mobility Limited (Symbol: WARDINMOBI) effective November 23, 2026. The action stems from the company's failure to comply with Regulation 76 (Submission of Reconciliation of Share Capital Audit Report) of SEBI (Depository and Participants) Regulations, 2018 for two consecutive quarters ending March 31, 2026, and June 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Wardwizard Innovations & Mobility Limited (WARDINMOBI).
- Non-compliance: Failure to comply with Regulation 76 for two consecutive quarters (March 31, 2026 and June 30, 2026) at BSE Limited.
- Effective Suspension Date: November 23, 2026, unless compliance is achieved on or before November 18, 2026.
- Post-Suspension Trading: After 15 days of suspension, trading will be permitted on a Trade for Trade basis (Z category) on the first trading day of every week for six months.

## Regulatory Changes

- Action taken pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 issued on July 11, 2023, and last updated on January 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company must rectify the non-compliance and satisfy the provisions of the Master Circular at BSE on or before November 18, 2026, to prevent suspension.

## Important Dates

- Compliance Deadline: November 18, 2026.
- Suspension Effective Date: November 23, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders and investors face liquidity constraints as trading in WARDINMOBI securities will be suspended and subsequently restricted to weekly Trade for Trade sessions if compliance is not restored.
