## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released guidelines regarding early pay-in (EPI) of securities for TCC Concept Limited (TCC) on account of a face value split (sub-division) from Rs 10/- per share to Rs 2/- per share. Market participants and custodians are advised to execute early pay-in in the old ISIN for the specified settlement dates.

## Key Points

- Company: TCC Concept Limited (Symbol: TCC, Old ISIN: INE887D01016)
- Corporate Action: Face Value Split from Rs 10/- to Rs 2/- per share.
- Record Date & Ex-Date: September 04, 2026.
- Members must execute early pay-in of securities in the depository systems using the existing/old ISIN without adjusting for the conversion on the specified trade/EPI dates (04-SEP-2026 and 07-SEP-2026).
- Execution in New ISIN should be done after the record date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adherence to SEBI circular on Block Mechanism in demat account of clients undertaking sale transactions (NCL circular NCL/CMPT/53386).

## Compliance Requirements

- For early pay-in through block mechanism, client-wise early pay-in allocation file should not be uploaded.
- Custodians providing early pay-in through the pool account must upload client-wise early pay-in allocation details as per the actual traded quantity to pass benefits to clients.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 04-SEP-2026
- Ex-Date: 04-SEP-2026
- EPI Dates: 04-SEP-2026 and 07-SEP-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth settlement processing during corporate actions by defining clear ISIN usage for early pay-in instructions.
