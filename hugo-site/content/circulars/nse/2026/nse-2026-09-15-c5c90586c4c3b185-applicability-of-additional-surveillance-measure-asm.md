## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) to certain securities. Specifically, securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) Stage - I effective September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long-Term ASM Framework Stage - I: Atal Realtech Limited (ATALREAL), Quadpro Ites Limited (QUADPRO), and Vivid Electromech Limited (VIVIDEL).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. September 18, 2026, on all open positions as on September 17, 2026, and new positions created from September 18, 2026 onwards.
- Securities qualifying under criteria VII (Scrips shifted to Stage IV) would be shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE), though no new securities are listed under Stage IV in this circular.
- The ASM framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of 100% margin requirements and surveillance actions under the Long-Term Additional Surveillance Measure framework for specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure proper margin collection (100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities starting September 18, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Actions: September 18, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities (ATALREAL, QUADPRO, VIVIDEL) will be subject to heightened margin requirements, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for these scrips.
