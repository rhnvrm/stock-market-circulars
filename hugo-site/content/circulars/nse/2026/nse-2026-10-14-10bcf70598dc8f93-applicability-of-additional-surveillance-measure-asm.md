## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (Long Term - ASM) for selected securities. Effective October 19, 2026, applicable margin rates will be 100% on all open positions and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under Long Term ASM Framework Stage - I effective October 15, 2026.
- Shortlisted stocks: Alpine Texworld Limited (ALPINETEX), AMBANI ORGOCHEM LIMITED (AMBANIORGO), and Hi-Green Carbon Limited (HIGREEN).
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% w.e.f. October 19, 2026 on all open positions as on October 16, 2026 and new positions created from October 19, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long Term ASM Stage - I measures for specific scrips.
- Application of 100% margin requirements to curb excessive volatility and protect market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure 100% margin is maintained for open and new positions in the specified securities starting October 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date for ASM Framework: October 15, 2026
- Open Positions Snapshot Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in ALPINETEX, AMBANIORGO, and HIGREEN will experience stricter margin requirements (100%), impacting liquidity and leverage for these securities.
