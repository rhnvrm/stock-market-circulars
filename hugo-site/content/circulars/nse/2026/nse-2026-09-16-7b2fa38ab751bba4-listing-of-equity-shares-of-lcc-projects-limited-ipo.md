## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of equity shares of LCC Projects Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO), with details on shareholding patterns and specified securities.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: LCC Projects Limited
- Date of Share Holding Pattern: 16.09.2026
- Total Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 260,415,000 equity shares (89.90%)
- Total Public Holding: 29,256,232 equity shares (10.10%)
- Total Issued Equity Shares: 289,671,232

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 regarding shareholding pattern disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: 16.09.2026

## Impact Assessment

Commencement of trading for the equity shares of LCC Projects Limited on the exchange platform.
