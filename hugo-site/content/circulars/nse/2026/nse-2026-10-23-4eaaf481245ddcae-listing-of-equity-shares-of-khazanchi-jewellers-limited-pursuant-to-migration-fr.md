## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing of equity shares of Khazanchi Jewellers Limited on the Capital Market Segment (Main Board) following its migration from the SME Platform of BSE. The shareholding pattern was submitted pursuant to Regulation 31(1)(b) / 31(1)(c) for the quarter ending September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: Khazanchi Jewellers Limited
- Scrip Code / Security: 543953 / EQUITY
- Migration from SME Platform of BSE to Capital Market Segment (Main Board) of NSE.
- Shareholding pattern filed for the quarter ending September 30, 2026.
- Declaration indicates no partly paid-up shares, convertible securities, warrants, ESOPs, depository receipts, locked-in shares, or promoter encumbrances.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of the company's securities from the SME segment to the Main Board trading platform.
- Compliance with regular listing obligations and disclosure requirements under SEBI regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Continued adherence to Main Board listing regulations, ongoing disclosures, and corporate governance norms.

## Important Dates

- Quarter/Allotment Ending Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced liquidity and visibility for shareholders as the company transitions to the Main Board segment of NSE.
