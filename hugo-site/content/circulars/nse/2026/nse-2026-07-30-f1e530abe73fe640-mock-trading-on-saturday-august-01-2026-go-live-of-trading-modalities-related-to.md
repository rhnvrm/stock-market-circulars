## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) will conduct a mock trading session in the Futures & Options segment on Saturday, August 01, 2026. This session is designed to test changes in trading modalities resulting from the introduction of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Cash segment. These new modalities, alongside the deployment of the new NEAT+ trading terminal version 7.8.9, will go live on Monday, August 03, 2026. Members are required to participate in the mock sessions to ensure smooth transition and operational readiness.

## Key Points

- **Go-Live Date**: Changes in the trading modalities of the Equity Derivatives segment due to the introduction of CAS in the equity segment will be effective from Monday, August 03, 2026.
- **NEAT+ Terminal Upgrade**: A new version of NEAT+ (7.8.9) is introduced, while the older version (7.8.8) will be discontinued following the mock trading session on September 05, 2026.
- **Multi-session Mock Structure**: The mock trading on August 01, 2026, comprises three sessions: Session 1 from the Primary Site, Session 2 from the Disaster Recovery (DR) Site, and Session 3 from the Primary Site.
- **Price Band Updates**: Simulation of stock future price bands alignment with CAS in the Equity Cash Market will be updated at 11:30 Hrs (Session 2) and 15:15 Hrs (Session 3).
- **Relogin Verification**: A live re-login session is scheduled on August 01, 2026, from 20:00 Hrs to 21:00 Hrs to confirm system alignment.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Closing Auction Session (CAS)**: Under the guidelines in previous circulars (NSE/CMTR/73362 dated March 18, 2026, and NSE/FAOP/74467 dated May 29, 2026), the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Cash segment goes live, altering related trading modalities in the Equity Derivatives segment to align settlement and price close procedures.
- **Price Band Alignment**: Adjustments in the price bands for stock future contracts will be synchronized during the CAS segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- **NEAT+ Software Download**: All members must download and install the new version of NEAT+ (7.8.9) from the NSE Extranet path `/faoftp/faocommon/NEATPlus789`.
- **Mandatory Transition**: Members must completely migrate to NEAT+ 7.8.9 before NEAT+ 7.8.8 is discontinued on September 05, 2026.
- **Mock Trading Participation**: Members must actively participate in all three mock trading sessions on August 01, 2026, and execute the live re-login verification between 20:00 Hrs and 21:00 Hrs.
- **Information Review**: Members should review the FAQs provided at `https://www.nseindia.com/static/trade/continuous-markets-FAQs` under "Closing Auction Session (CAS) in Equity segment(CM) and changes in Equity Derivatives (F&O) Segment".

## Important Dates

- **July 30, 2026**: Date of circular issuance.
- **August 01, 2026**: Mock trading sessions (07:45 to 15:50 Hrs) and Live Re-login verification (20:00 to 21:00 Hrs).
- **August 03, 2026**: Go-live of modified trading modalities and NEAT+ version 7.8.9.
- **September 05, 2026**: Discontinuation date of NEAT+ version 7.8.8.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: High. Trading members are required to perform critical upgrades to their terminal configurations (NEAT+ version 7.8.9) and execute mock scenarios involving primary and DR site switch-overs.
- **Market Impact**: Medium. Introducing the Closing Auction Session in the Cash segment ensures more robust closing price discovery and aligns pricing bands for related stock futures, minimizing arbitrage risk and operational friction between segments.
