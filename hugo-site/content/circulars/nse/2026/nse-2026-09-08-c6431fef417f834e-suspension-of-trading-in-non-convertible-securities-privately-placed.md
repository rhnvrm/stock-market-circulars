## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of Power Finance Corporation Limited due to redemption, effective from September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Power Finance Corporation Limited
- **ISIN:** INE134E08IL6
- **Suspension Date:** 09-Sep-2026
- **Reason for Suspension:** Redemption
- **Regulation:** Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the suspension of trading for the specified ISIN.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 08, 2026
- **Suspension Date:** September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified non-convertible securities of Power Finance Corporation Limited will be halted on the NSE debt segment following their redemption.
