## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Securities (Privately Placed) of Summit Digitel Infrastructure Limited, effective October 16, 2026, pursuant to redemption under Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Summit Digitel Infrastructure Limited
- ISIN: INE507T07112
- Suspension Date: October 16, 2026
- Reason for Suspension: Redemption
- Circular Reference: Download Ref No: NSE/CML/76908, Circular Ref. No: 1862/2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension executed pursuant to Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must note the suspension of trading in the specified non-convertible securities effective from the suspension date.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 15, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the privately placed non-convertible securities of Summit Digitel Infrastructure Limited will cease on the NSE debt market segment due to full redemption.
