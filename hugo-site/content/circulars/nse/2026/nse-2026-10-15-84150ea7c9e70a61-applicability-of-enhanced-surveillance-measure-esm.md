## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the applicability and stage migration of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ESM Stage I or movement between stages effective October 16, 2026.
- Affected securities shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 19, 2026, on all open positions as on October 16, 2026, and new positions created from October 19, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. October 19, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Enhanced Surveillance Measures including margin requirements, price bands, and trade-for-trade settlement for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and segment shifts for shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Margin & Settlement Shift Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in shortlisted securities is restricted with enhanced margins, price bands, and trade-for-trade settlement to address market surveillance observations.
