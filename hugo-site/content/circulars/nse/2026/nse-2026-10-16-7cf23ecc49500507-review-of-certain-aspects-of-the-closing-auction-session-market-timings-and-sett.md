## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the review of certain aspects of the Closing Auction Session (CAS), market timings, and settlement methodology for derivatives contracts, pursuant to SEBI circular dated October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Updates the CAS framework introduced on January 16, 2026, for the equity cash segment.
- Specifies revised timings and operational procedures for CAS from 3:30 p.m. to 3:40 p.m.
- Details transition rules for unexecuted iceberg orders from Continuous Trading Session (CTS) to CAS.
- Modifies methodology for determining settlement prices of stock and index derivatives contracts.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modifies provisions of SEBI circular dated January 16, 2026, and Chapter 5 of the Master Circular for Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations dated December 30, 2024.
- Establishes a 10-minute CAS from 3:30 p.m. to 3:40 p.m. across 3 distinct sub-sessions (Reference price calculation / transition, order entry for limit and market orders, and order entry exclusively for limit orders with random closure between 3:38 p.m. and 3:40 p.m.).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the revised CAS timings, order entry rules, and derivatives settlement price methodology and update their trading systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 16, 2026
- Reference SEBI Circular Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances price discovery and orderly functioning of both cash and derivatives segments by refining closing auction timings and derivative settlement calculations.
