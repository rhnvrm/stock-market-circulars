## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing and trading of equity shares of Arihant Academy Limited pursuant to a bonus issue. A total of 60,55,200 equity shares are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment effective from October 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Arihant Academy Limited
- Symbol: ARIHANTACA
- ISIN: INE0NCC01015
- Number of Securities: 60,55,200
- Distinctive Number Range: 6055201 to 12110400
- Record Date: October 01, 2026
- Deemed Date of Allotment: October 05, 2026
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine execution under National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must identify the security using its designated code and verify operational parameters ahead of trading commencement.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 01, 2026

Expands the paid-up equity capital base of Arihant Academy Limited through the issuance of bonus shares, increasing market liquidity for the scrip.
