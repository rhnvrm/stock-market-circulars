## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, effective from October 15, 2026. These securities are admitted to dealings on the Capital Market segment.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026.
- Companies include Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Asian Energy Services Limited, Computer Age Management Services Limited, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited, Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited, and others.
- Securities issued under ESOP allotments.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must use designated security codes and lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action with standard dilution effects.
