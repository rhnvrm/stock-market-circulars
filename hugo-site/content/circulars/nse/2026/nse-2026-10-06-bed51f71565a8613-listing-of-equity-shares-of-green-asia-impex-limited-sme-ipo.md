## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing of equity shares of Green Asia Impex Limited under its SME IPO segment, detailing shareholding patterns and specified securities information.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Green Asia Impex Limited
- Segment: SME IPO / Specified Securities Listing
- Quarter Ending: 06-10-2026
- Declarations regarding partly paid shares, convertible securities, warrants, and encumbrances submitted as per regulatory guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with Regulation 31(1)(a)/31(1)(b)/31(1)(c) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entity must ensure accurate submission of holding of specified securities and adhere to ongoing disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Report / Listing Date: 06-10-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates public trading of Green Asia Impex Limited equity shares on the NSE SME platform, enhancing liquidity and investor participation.
