## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the listing and commencement of trading of equity shares of Manipal Payment and Identity Solutions Limited (IPO) on the Capital Market segment effective from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** MPIMANIPAL
- **Series:** EQ
- **ISIN:** INE241U01028
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 2/- each fully paid-up
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 339/- per share
- **No. of Securities:** 231,804,528 equity shares
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Effective Date:** September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note that the security will be part of the pre-open session for IPO and Other category of scrips as per SEBI circulars dated January 20, 2012.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 16, 2026
- **Effective Date / Listing Date:** September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Manipal Payment and Identity Solutions Limited equity shares on the NSE capital market segment, providing liquidity to shareholders and investors.
