## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions and registrations in certain schemes under Zerodha Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh/additional lumpsum subscriptions and switch-ins into Zerodha Gold ETF FoF are disabled.
- Fresh registrations of Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP) where the scheme is the target are suspended.
- The step-up facility in existing SIPs is unavailable.
- Existing SIPs (without step-up) and STPs registered before the effective date continue to be processed normally.
- No restrictions on redemptions, switch-outs, or Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP).

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary operational restriction on new inflows into Zerodha Gold ETF FoF as per communication from Zerodha Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that any applications for fresh subscriptions, switch-ins, or new SIP/STP registrations received on or after October 16, 2026, are rejected and refunded to investors in accordance with regulations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026 until further notice.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors cannot initiate new investments or fresh SIP/STP registrations in the affected scheme, though existing regular investments and redemptions remain fully operational.
