## Summary

National Stock Exchange (NSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities issued by Akme Fintrade (India) Limited on the capital market segment, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Akme Fintrade (India) Limited
- Security 1: Symbol 114AFIL28A, 11.40% 2028 Sr A1, ISIN: INE916Y07123, Maturity: 19-Oct-2028, Market Lot: 1
- Security 2: Symbol 116AFIL29A, 11.60% 2029 Sr A2, ISIN: INE916Y07131, Maturity: 19-Apr-2029, Market Lot: 1
- Trading Regulations: Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine admission of securities to trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are identified by designated security codes and executed in specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Maturity Dates: 19-Oct-2028 and 19-Apr-2029

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading and liquidity for the privately placed debt securities issued by Akme Fintrade (India) Limited.
