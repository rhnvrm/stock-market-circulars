## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the applicability of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for securities based on market surveillance criteria. Dhunseri Tea & Industries Limited (DTIL) has been shortlisted under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I effective August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security shortlisted: Dhunseri Tea & Industries Limited (Symbol: DTIL, ISIN: INE341R01014) under Long-Term ASM Framework Stage-I.
- Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% effective September 01, 2026, on all open positions as on August 31, 2026, and new positions created from September 01, 2026, onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities satisfying specified criteria are subjected to heightened surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (100%) for open and new positions in the affected security starting September 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of ASM Inclusion: August 28, 2026
- 100% Margin Applicability Date: September 01, 2026 (on open positions as on August 31, 2026, and new positions thereafter)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for DTIL may impact trading liquidity and open interest as participants adjust to 100% margin coverage.
