## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the extension of the trading of Rights Entitlement (RE) and the issue closing date for Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited pursuant to its Rights Issue.

## Key Points

- Symbol: RCDL-RE (Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited)
- Last date for on-market renunciation (New): September 18, 2026
- Date of Suspension (New): September 21, 2026
- Issue Closing Date (New): Friday, September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Timelines for the Rights Issue of Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited have been extended to provide eligible shareholders additional time to exercise their rights.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and shareholders must take note of the revised schedule for on-market renunciation and issue closure.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: August 31, 2026 (Unchanged)
- Last Date for On-Market Renunciation: September 18, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: September 25, 2026
- Basis of Allotment: September 28, 2026
- Date of Credit of Rights Equity Shares: October 01, 2026
- Date of Listing: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides extended flexibility for eligible shareholders to apply and trade rights entitlements for Rajgor Castor Derivatives Limited.
