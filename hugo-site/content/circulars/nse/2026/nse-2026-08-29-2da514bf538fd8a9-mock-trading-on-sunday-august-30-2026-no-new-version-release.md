## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session in the Capital Market Segment on Sunday, August 30, 2026, operating from the Disaster Recovery (DR) site. No new software version release is associated with this mock session.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** August 30, 2026.
- **Software Versions:** Members are advised to use NEAT versions 7.8.8 and 7.8.9.
- **NEATPlus Discontinuation Date:** Version 7.8.8 discontinuation is scheduled for September 05, 2026 (mock).
- **Master Files:** Members must load master files (security.gz, participant.gz, nnf_security.gz, nnf_participant.gz, NSE_CM_security_28082026.csv.gz, cm_contract_stream_info.csv) before the mock session.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades during the mock session will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Conducted as per standard exchange mock operations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.
- All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** August 30, 2026
- **NEATPlus 7.8.8 Discontinuation:** September 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness check for trading members with zero financial impact.
