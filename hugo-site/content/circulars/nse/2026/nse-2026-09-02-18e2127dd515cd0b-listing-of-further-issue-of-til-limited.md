## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the listing of further issue of equity shares for TIL Limited, effective from September 03, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TIL Limited
- Symbol: TIL
- ISIN: INE806C01018 (Temporary ISIN: IN8806C01017)
- Number of Securities: 17,500 equity shares
- Description: Conversion of partly paid-up equity shares to fully paid-up equity shares
- Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in the securities shall be identified by the designated codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 03, 2026
- Circular Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase and conversion of partly paid shares for TIL Limited with negligible broader market impact.
