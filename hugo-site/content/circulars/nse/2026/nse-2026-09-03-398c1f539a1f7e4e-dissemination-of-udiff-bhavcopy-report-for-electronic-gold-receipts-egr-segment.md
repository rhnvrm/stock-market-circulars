## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced the proposed commencement of the dissemination of the Unified Distilled File Format (UDiFF) Bhavcopy for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment. Details regarding the file structure, naming conventions, and extranet/website paths have been provided in the annexure.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR).
- **Report Type:** UDiFF Bhavcopy.
- **File Nomenclature:** `BhavCopy_<entity>_<segment>_0_0_0_<YYYYMMDD>_<flag>_<hhmm>.csv.zip` (for EGR, segment code is `EG`).
- **Extranet Path:** `/egftp/egcommon/Bhavcopy`
- **Website Path:** `https://www.nseindia.com/all-reports-egr`
- **Go-Live Date:** To be communicated through a separate circular.

## Regulatory Changes

Introduction of the UDiFF file format standard for the EGR segment to streamline market data distribution.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the proposed file structure and prepare their internal systems for the upcoming UDiFF Bhavcopy integration for the EGR segment.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular:** September 03, 2026
- **Go-Live Date:** To be announced separately.

## Impact Assessment

Operational enhancement for market participants trading in Electronic Gold Receipts, facilitating standardized data consumption through the UDiFF framework.
