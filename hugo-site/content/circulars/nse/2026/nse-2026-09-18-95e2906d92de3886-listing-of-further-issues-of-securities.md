## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has notified the listing of further issues of equity shares for several companies, including Advait Energy Transitions Limited, Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Limited, Emcure Pharmaceuticals Limited, EPACK Durable Limited, EPack Prefab Technologies Limited, Eureka Forbes Limited, The Federal Bank Limited, Brainbees Solutions Limited, Fractal Analytics Limited, Happy Forgings Limited, HDFC Bank Limited, Latent View Analytics Limited, Neogen Chemicals Limited, Prime Securities Limited, and RBL Bank Limited.

## Key Points

- Securities are admitted to dealings on the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market segment) with effect from September 21, 2026.
- Shares have been allotted primarily under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) or related schemes.
- Trading will take place in designated security codes and lot sizes specified in the annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure trading is conducted using the correct security codes and designated lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate action resulting in minor equity capital expansions for the respective issuers with negligible direct market impact.
