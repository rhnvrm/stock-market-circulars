## Summary

SEBI issued a final order regarding Modex International Securities Ltd., its whole-time executive directors Mr. Dharmendra Kumar Arora and Mr. Pavan Kumar Sachdeva, following forensic audits and investigations revealing massive diversion and misappropriation of client securities and funds, along with severe misrepresentation of books and accounts.

## Key Points

- Forensic audit revealed falsification of the Register of Securities (RoS) through fake transfer entries.
- Substantial shortfall and misappropriation found in client securities and funds.
- Misrepresentation of financial ledgers, payments, and receipts of clients.
- Periodic payments made to certain clients masquerading as assured returns on investments.

## Compliance Requirements

Stock brokers and intermediaries must maintain strict segregation of client funds and securities, ensure accurate bookkeeping in the Register of Securities, and avoid any unauthorized diversion or assured return schemes.

- Order Date: As per SEBI proceedings (2026)

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight on broker compliance, custodial safety of client assets, and strict enforcement actions against fraudulent bookkeeping and misappropriation.
