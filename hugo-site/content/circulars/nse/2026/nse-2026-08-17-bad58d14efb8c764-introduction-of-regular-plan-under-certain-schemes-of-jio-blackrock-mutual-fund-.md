## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular regarding the introduction of an additional plan namely "Regular Plan" for investments routed through distributors under certain schemes of Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Regular plans for multiple Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund schemes have been added to the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Applies to investments routed through distributors empanelled with the Fund with a valid AMFI Registration Number (ARN).
- Schemes added include liquid funds, overnight funds, money market funds, index funds, flexi cap, low/short duration funds, arbitrage, sector rotation, and large cap funds.

## Regulatory Changes

- Addition of Regular Plans for distributor-routed investments in Jio BlackRock schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and distributors can facilitate investments in the newly added Regular Plans through the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands investment options for distributors and investors utilizing the NSE MF Invest Platform for Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund schemes.
