## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives (CD) segment on Saturday, October 10, 2026. No new software version release is involved in this session.

## Key Points

- **Mock Date:** October 10, 2026.
- **Trading Sessions:** Includes Trading Session-1 from the Primary Site and Trading Session-2 from the Disaster Recovery (DR) Site.
- **Pre-Trade Risk Controls:** Testing of pre-emptive order cancellation on account of NNF ID-Algo ID validations (effective live from October 12, 2026).
- **NEAT Version:** NEAT 3.5.4.
- **Order Purge:** All outstanding orders will be cleared before each session.
- **Financial Obligation:** Trades during mock sessions will not result in any fund pay-in or pay-out.
- **UCC/PAN Validity:** Only valid and compliant UCC/PAN uploaded and approved before the cutoff will be allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory policy changes; routine operational mock trading and testing of pre-trade risk controls.

## Compliance Requirements

Members enabled for trading in the Currency Derivatives segment are requested to participate using all trading software and re-login into the live environment to ensure smooth connectivity for Monday, October 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** October 10, 2026
- **Live Effective Date for Risk Controls:** October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational drill for members to verify connectivity, system behavior, and pre-trade risk controls with zero financial impact.
