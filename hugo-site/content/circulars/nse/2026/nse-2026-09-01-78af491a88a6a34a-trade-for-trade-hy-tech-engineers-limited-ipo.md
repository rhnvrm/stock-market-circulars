## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Hy-Tech Engineers Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: BE) to the rolling segment (series: EQ) effective from September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Hy-Tech Engineers Limited
- Symbol: HTEL
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade (Series: BE) to Rolling Segment (Series: EQ)
- Effective Date: September 16, 2026
- Reference to previous exchange circular no. NSE/CML/76064 dated August 31, 2026 and SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The transfer is executed pursuant to existing SEBI guidelines regarding IPO securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members need to note the change in series for trading in the security from September 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Normal trading in the rolling segment will resume for Hy-Tech Engineers Limited, enabling standard settlement cycles.
