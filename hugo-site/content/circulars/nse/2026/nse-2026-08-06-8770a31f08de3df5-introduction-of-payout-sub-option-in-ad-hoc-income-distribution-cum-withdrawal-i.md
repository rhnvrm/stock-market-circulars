## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has issued circular ref. no. 1542/2026 (Download Ref No: NSE/NMFTM/75601) announcing the introduction of the Ad-Hoc payout sub-option in the Income Distribution cum withdrawal (IDCW) option for various mutual fund schemes of Kotak Mutual Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform. This implementation is based on the notice-cum-addendum issued by Kotak Mahindra Trustee Company Limited, effective from August 07, 2026. The introduction of this additional option allows the fund house to declare Ad-Hoc IDCWs, subject to the availability of distributable surplus.

## Key Points

- **Platform Availability**: The Ad-Hoc payout/reinvestment option is introduced on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Affected Schemes**: The change applies to both Regular and Direct plans of Kotak Savings Fund, Kotak Corporate Bond Fund, Kotak Money Market Fund, and Kotak Liquid Fund.
- **Option Customization**: For Kotak Money Market Fund, Kotak Savings Fund, and Kotak Corporate Bond Fund, the IDCW frequencies are expanded to include monthly, weekly, and Ad-Hoc frequencies (at the discretion of the Trustee).
- **Approved by Trustee**: The Ad-Hoc declaration of IDCW has been approved by Kotak Mahindra Trustee Company Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory change from SEBI is introduced by this circular. It is a product-level addition/update by the AMC (Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund) and its Trustee, which is being incorporated operationally into the NSE MF Invest Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Mutual Fund Trading Members/Distributors**: Members using the NSE MF Invest platform should update their systems, user interfaces, and advising materials to reflect the newly introduced Ad-Hoc payout options for the specified Kotak Mutual Fund schemes.
- **Investor Communication**: Distributors should inform relevant investors about the availability of the new Ad-Hoc payout option in their portfolios.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **For Investors**: Provides greater flexibility in receiving payouts and managing cash flows through the Ad-Hoc option, which is declared based on the availability and adequacy of distributable surplus.
- **For Market Operations**: Minimal operational impact. The NSE MF Invest Platform has updated its scheme master to support the new ISINs and option combinations.
