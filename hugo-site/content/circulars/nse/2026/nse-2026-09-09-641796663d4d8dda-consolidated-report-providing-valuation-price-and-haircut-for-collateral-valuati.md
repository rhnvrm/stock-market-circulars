## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has introduced a new consolidated report containing valuation prices and haircuts for all collateral securities, including equities, mutual funds, corporate bonds, government securities, and T-bills, to enhance the ease of doing business for members.

## Key Points

- A single consolidated report incorporates valuation prices and haircuts across multiple asset classes.
- Report nomenclature: `APPSEC_ALL_DDMMYYYY.csv` (where date is the applicable date).
- Report fields include Sr. No., Symbol, ISIN, Valuation Price, and Applicable Haircut.

## Regulatory Changes

- Streamlining of collateral reporting through a unified daily file format.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians can access the new report via the NSE/NSE Clearing website and extranet for operational and collateral management.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Improves operational efficiency for members by eliminating the need to pull multiple reports for different asset classes' valuations and haircuts.
