## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India (NSE) has announced the reopening of **NJ Momentum Fund** and **NJ Value Fund** (Direct & Regular options with Growth and IDCW Payout facilities) for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) on the **NSE MF Invest Platform**. This operational update is effective from **August 03, 2026**.

## Key Points

- **Schemes Available**: Reopening of both NJ Momentum Fund and NJ Value Fund across growth and IDCW (Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal) payout options.
- **Investment Routes**: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities will be fully supported.
- **Platform**: The services are offered on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- **Scheme Categories**: All listed schemes are categorized as **Equity** funds.
- **ISIN Scheme Details**:
  - **NJ Momentum Fund - Direct Growth**: INF0J8L01172
  - **NJ Momentum Fund - Direct IDCW Payout**: INF0J8L01180
  - **NJ Momentum Fund - Regular Growth**: INF0J8L01198
  - **NJ Momentum Fund - Regular IDCW Payout**: INF0J8L01206
  - **NJ Value Fund - Direct Growth**: INF0J8L01214
  - **NJ Value Fund - Direct IDCW Payout**: INF0J8L01222
  - **NJ Value Fund - Regular Growth**: INF0J8L01230
  - **NJ Value Fund - Regular IDCW Payout**: INF0J8L01248

## Regulatory Changes

There are no direct regulatory changes introduced in this circular. It is a standard operational announcement to enable transacting in newly launched schemes post their New Fund Offer (NFO) period on the exchange's mutual fund platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members / Mutual Fund Distributors**: Ensure systems are updated and clients are notified that these schemes are available for regular transactions (subscription, redemption, SIP, STP, SWP) on the NSE MF Invest platform starting August 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: July 31, 2026
- **Effective Date (Reopening)**: August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. This is a routine platform update adding newly reopened mutual fund schemes for regular trading/transaction access.
- **Operational Impact**: Positive for mutual fund distributors and investors using the NSE MF Invest Platform, as they gain seamless operational access to NJ Mutual Fund's momentum and value offerings.
