## Summary

NSE has issued a revised list of approved securities including Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs), Government Securities (G-Secs), and Treasury Bills (T-Bills) accepted as collateral that will form part of the cash component of liquid assets.

## Key Points

- Lists approved Sovereign Gold Bonds along with their ISINs, security descriptions, symbols, and applicable haircuts (0.1).
- All SGBs, G-Secs, and T-Bills issued by RBI during the month shall be accepted from their date of issue.
- Includes numerous tranches of Sovereign Gold Bonds ranging across multiple financial years (2018-19 to 2023-24).

## Regulatory Changes

- Update to the eligible list of securities accepted towards cash component of liquid assets collateral.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and clearing participants must take note of the revised list and applicable haircuts when pledging securities as collateral.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular release.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects collateral valuation and liquidity management for trading members utilizing G-Secs and SGBs.
