## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the admission of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) to dealings on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from September 18, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Listing of Commercial Papers for multiple companies including Toyota Financial Services India Limited, Kotak Securities Limited, CEAT Limited, Angel One Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, Godrej Industries Limited, Astec LifeSciences Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited, and Aseem Infrastructure Finance Limited.
- Effective date of admission: September 18, 2026.
- Securities issued under various tenures and ISINs as specified in the exchange annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; standard listing notification under debt market regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and trading members must comply with standard operational and reporting guidelines for debt market segment transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine market operations with no direct impact on secondary equity trading or overall market stability.
