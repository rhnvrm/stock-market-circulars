## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India has issued a circular informing members about the reopening and availability of various schemes of ASK Liquid Fund on the NSE MF Invest Platform effective September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- ASK Liquid Fund of ASK Mutual Fund is reopened for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions.
- Facilities including SIP, STP, and SWP will be available on the NSE MF Invest Platform.
- Effective date for availability is September 03, 2026.
- Multiple plans including Regular and Direct Growth, as well as IDCW Daily, Monthly, and Weekly options, are included with corresponding ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants should note the inclusion of these ISINs for client transactions on the platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 02, 2026
- Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment and liquidity management options for investors and members utilizing the NSE MF Invest Platform.
