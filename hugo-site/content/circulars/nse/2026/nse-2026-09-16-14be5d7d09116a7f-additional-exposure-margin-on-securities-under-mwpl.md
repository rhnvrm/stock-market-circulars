## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has announced the levy of an additional exposure margin of 15% in the equity derivatives segment on securities where the top 10 clients account for more than 20% of the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL). This framework is based on 3 months of rolling data and reviewed monthly.

## Key Points

- Additional exposure margin of 15% applicable in the equity derivatives segment.
- Triggered when top 10 clients account for >20% of MWPL.
- For securities with an additional surveillance margin, the higher of the two margins will apply.
- Identified based on 3 months rolling data with monthly reviews.
- Lists 17 securities shortlisted under Annexure 1.

## Regulatory Changes

- Levy of @15% additional exposure margin on specific securities meeting the MWPL client concentration criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must maintain adequate margins for the shortlisted securities in the F&O segment as per the new framework.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2026
- Circular Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for trading in the derivatives segment for the 17 listed securities, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume for those counters.
