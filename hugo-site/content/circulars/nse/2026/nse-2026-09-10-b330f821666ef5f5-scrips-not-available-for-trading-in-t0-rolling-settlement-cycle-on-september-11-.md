## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding scrips not available for trading in the T+0 rolling settlement cycle on September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Specific T+0 securities are excluded from trading on September 11, 2026, pursuant to chapter 1.6 of the Capital Market Consolidated Circular.
- Affected securities: Swiggy Limited (SWIGGY) and Union Bank of India (UNIONBANK).
- Reason for exclusion: Security is either moving in or out of the index on which derivatives contracts are available for trading due to index rebalancing.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are required to note the exclusion of the specified securities from the T+0 rolling settlement cycle for the trade date September 11, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in T+0 rolling settlement for SWIGGY and UNIONBANK will be temporarily unavailable on the specified date due to index rebalancing parameters.
