## Summary

Before the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) in Mumbai, Appeal No. 289 of 2026 was heard between Avance Ventures Private Limited (Appellant) and SEBI (Respondent). The appellant challenged a SEBI order involving debarment for two years and monetary penalties related to warrant subscription and alleged round-tripping of funds involving Debock Industries Limited, Impex Agrotech Ltd., and Avance Technologies Limited.

## Key Points

- Appellant company is a subsidiary of Avance Technologies Limited and subscribed to warrants of Debock Industries Limited.
- Allegations involve round-tripping of funds between Debock Industries Ltd., Impex Agrotech Ltd., and the parent company.
- Appellant initially claimed non-service of show cause notice due to emails landing in the 'spam' folder, which was subsequently withdrawn by the appellant.
- SAT directed the appellant to deposit the full penalty within four weeks as a condition to stay the impugned SEBI debarment order.
- Penalty amount shall be kept in an interest-bearing account by SEBI, with the next hearing scheduled for December 9, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Appellant company must deposit the full penalty within four weeks from the date of the order (09.09.2026) to maintain the stay on the debarment order.
- SEBI to keep the deposited penalty in an interest-bearing account.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 9, 2026
- Penalty Deposit Deadline: Within four weeks from September 9, 2026
- Next Hearing Date: December 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The interim relief stays the debarment of Avance Ventures Private Limited subject to the condition of depositing the full penalty amount, allowing operations to continue pending the final appeal outcome.
