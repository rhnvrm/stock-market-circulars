## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India has announced the transfer of securities of Fascinate Textiles Limited from the trade for trade segment (series: ST) to the rolling segment (Series: SM) pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Fascinate Textiles Limited
- Symbol: FASCINATE
- Segment Transfer: From trade for trade segment (ST) to rolling segment (SM)
- Effective Date: September 07, 2026
- Reference to previous exchange circular no. NSE/CML/75891 dated August 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of the security's trading segment in accordance with SEBI circular reference no. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the segment change and ensure compliance with respective settlement procedures for the rolling segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normalization of trading for Fascinate Textiles Limited as it moves to the rolling segment, enabling regular rolling settlement for market participants.
