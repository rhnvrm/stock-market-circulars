## Summary

Pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement, Bhagyanagar India Limited (BHAGYANGR) is shifting to the Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE) on the National Stock Exchange, effective October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Bhagyanagar India Limited
- Symbol: BHAGYANGR
- Segment/Series: Trade for Trade segment (Series: BE)
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Pre-open Session: Subject to call auction in pre-open session as per SEBI Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the security is moved to the Trade for Trade segment following the scheme of arrangement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with Trade for Trade settlement rules where netting off is not allowed and mandatory delivery applies.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Intraday trading will not be available for the security in the Trade for Trade segment, which generally results in lower speculative volume and enforces delivery-based transactions.
