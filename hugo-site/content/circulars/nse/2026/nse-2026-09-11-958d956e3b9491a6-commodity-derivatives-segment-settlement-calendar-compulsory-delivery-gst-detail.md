## Summary

NSE Clearing Limited has released the delivery settlement schedule (compulsory delivery) and GST details confirmation schedule for October 2026 expiry contracts in the commodity derivatives segment.

## Key Points

- Outlines delivery settlement schedules for precious metals (GOLD, SILVER series) and base metals (Aluminium, Copper, Lead, Nickel, Zinc series).
- Specifies timelines for delivery intention, delivery marking, delivery pay-in/pay-out of commodities, and fund pay-in/pay-out for October 2026 expiries.
- Details the GST details uploading windows for both seller and buyer members.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard operational schedule release for commodity derivatives settlement and GST reporting for October 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members participating in the delivery of commodities must ensure compliance with GST registration requirements and adhere strictly to the GST details uploading windows specified for sellers and buyers.

## Important Dates

- Expiry and delivery schedules span late September 2026 through October 2026, depending on the specific commodity contract.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth clearing, settlement, and statutory GST reporting for commodity derivatives contracts expiring in October 2026.
