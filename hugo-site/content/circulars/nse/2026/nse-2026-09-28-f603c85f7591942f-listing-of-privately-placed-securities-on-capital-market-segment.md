## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment with effect from September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted for trading include issuances from Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited and Godrej Housing Finance Limited.
- Trading will take place using designated security codes and specific market lots as outlined in the annexure.
- Effective date for commencement of trading is September 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the designated security codes and specified lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date: September 29, 2026
- Maturity Dates: 25-Sep-2029 (Tata Capital Housing Finance Limited) and 24-Sep-2036 (Godrej Housing Finance Limited)

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update enabling secondary market trading for the newly listed privately placed debt securities.
