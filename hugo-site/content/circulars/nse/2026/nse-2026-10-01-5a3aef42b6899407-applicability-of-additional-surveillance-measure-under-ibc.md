## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has issued a circular regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective October 05, 2026, Owais Metal And Mineral Processing Limited (ISIN: INE0R8M01017) is excluded from the ASM IBC framework.

## Key Points

- No new securities have been shortlisted under the ASM IBC framework (Annexure I is nil).
- Owais Metal And Mineral Processing Limited is removed from the ASM IBC framework effective October 05, 2026.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels, subject to no other active surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Exclusion of specified companies from the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) Additional Surveillance Measure framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the exclusion of Owais Metal And Mineral Processing Limited from the ASM IBC framework.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 05, 2026
- Circular Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Specific surveillance measures and price band restrictions under the ASM IBC framework are lifted for Owais Metal And Mineral Processing Limited, restoring standard trading parameters unless subject to other surveillance mechanisms.
