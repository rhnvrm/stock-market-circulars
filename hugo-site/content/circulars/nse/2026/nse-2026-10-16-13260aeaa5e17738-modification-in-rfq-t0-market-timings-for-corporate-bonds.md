## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular announcing the modification of market timings for T+0 settlement of all types of Corporate Bonds (Non-Convertible Securities, Securitised Debt Instruments, Security Receipts, and Municipal Debt Securities) executed on the Request for Quote (RFQ) Platform. The revised timings become effective from October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised market hours for T+0 settlement on the RFQ Platform: 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM (excluding G-sec, SDL, and T-bills).
- Market hours for T+1 settlement remain unchanged at 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM for all eligible securities.
- Applies across Non-Convertible Securities, Securitised Debt Instruments, Security Receipts, and Municipal Debt Securities.
- No changes in market timings for other instruments.

## Regulatory Changes

Modification of trading window hours for T+0 settlement of corporate bonds on the RFQ platform.

## Compliance Requirements

All participants must update their internal systems, operational schedules, and trading setups to align with the revised market timings.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in corporate bonds via the RFQ platform benefit from an expanded T+0 settlement window from 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM, offering enhanced liquidity management and execution flexibility.
