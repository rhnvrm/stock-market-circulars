## Summary

The National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework to shortlisted securities. Under this framework, specific securities that meet the established surveillance criteria are either added to ESM Stage I, transitioned between Stage I and Stage II, or shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment to ensure market integrity and investor protection.

## Key Points

- **ESM Stage I Additions**: Four securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion in ESM Stage I: GACM Technologies Limited (GATECH), RSD Finance Limited (RSDFIN), Shalimar Paints Limited (SHALPAINTS), and Shreedhar Spinners Limited (SHREEDHAR).
- **Margin Requirements**: Shortlisted Stage I securities will attract a minimum of 100% margin on all open positions as of August 05, 2026, and on new positions created from August 06, 2026.
- **Segment Shifting (Stage I)**: Securities qualifying under ESM Stage I will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment (Series: EQ/SM) to the Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) effective August 06, 2026.
- **ESM Stage II Transition**: Kesoram Industries Limited (KESORAMIND) is eligible and shortlisted to move from ESM Stage I to Stage II effective August 05, 2026.
- **Stage II Restrictions**: Securities transitioning to Stage II will be placed under the Trade-for-Trade segment with a tight price band of 2% under the Periodic Call Auction mechanism effective August 05, 2026.
- **Surveillance Intent**: The NSE emphasizes that shortlisting under the ESM framework is purely a market surveillance measure and must not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shifting of shortlisted ESM Stage I securities from Rolling Settlement (Series: EQ/SM) to Trade-for-Trade segment (Series: BE/ST) w.e.f. August 06, 2026.
- Placement of ESM Stage II transition securities (e.g., Kesoram Industries Limited) under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band under Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 05, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members and Market Participants**:
  - Must implement a minimum 100% margin on all open positions of shortlisted Stage I securities (GATECH, RSDFIN, SHALPAINTS, SHREEDHAR) starting August 06, 2026 (based on open positions as of August 05, 2026).
  - Must update trading and settlement systems to reflect the segment shift of Stage I securities to the Trade-for-Trade (BE/ST) segment w.e.f. August 06, 2026.
  - Must enforce a 2% price band and trade execution via Periodic Call Auction for Kesoram Industries Limited (KESORAMIND) starting August 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **August 04, 2026**: Date of issue of the NSE circular.
- **August 05, 2026**: Effective date for Kesoram Industries Limited's movement to ESM Stage II (2% price band under Periodic Call Auction) and determination date for open positions attracting 100% margin.
- **August 06, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and shifting of ESM Stage I securities to the Trade-for-Trade (BE/ST) segment.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market and Liquidity Impact**: Transitioning securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment severely curtails intraday trading leverage since every transaction must result in delivery. Moving Kesoram Industries to ESM Stage II under Periodic Call Auction with a 2% price band further reduces liquidity, transaction frequency, and trading volumes for these specific counters.
- **Operational and Margin Impact**: Market participants must allocate higher capital (100% upfront margin) to trade shortlisted ESM Stage I securities, increasing transactional costs and eliminating leverage opportunities.
- **Corporate Governance Clarification**: Because this classification is based solely on quantitative surveillance parameters, it does not reflect any deterioration in corporate governance, operational stability, or financial health of the listed companies.
