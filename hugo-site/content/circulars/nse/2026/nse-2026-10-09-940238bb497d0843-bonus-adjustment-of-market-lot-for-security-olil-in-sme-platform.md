## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced an adjustment of market lot for ONECLICK LOGISTICS INDIA LIMITED (OLIL) on the SME platform on account of a bonus issue in the ratio of 1:2.

## Key Points

- Company Name: ONECLICK LOGISTICS INDIA LIMITED
- Symbol: OLIL
- Corporate Action: Bonus issue
- Bonus Ratio: 1:2
- Adjustment Factor: 1.5
- Ex-date / Effective Date: October 15, 2026
- Record Date: October 15, 2026
- Adjusted Market Lot: 600

## Regulatory Changes

Market lot adjustment for security OLIL in the SME platform reflecting the corporate action.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to load the updated security.gz/ nnf_security.gz/ MII - Security File.gz file in the trading application before trading on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Ex-date: October 15, 2026
- Record Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must update security files to reflect the new market lot of 600 for OLIL prior to trading on the effective date.
