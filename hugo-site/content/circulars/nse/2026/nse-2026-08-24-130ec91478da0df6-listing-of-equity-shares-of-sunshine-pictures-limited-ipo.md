## Summary

Sunshine Pictures Limited has announced the listing of its equity shares on the exchange following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The shareholding pattern and declarations regarding specified securities have been submitted.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: Sunshine Pictures Limited
- Report for Quarter ending: 22-08-2026
- No partly paid-up shares, convertible securities, or warrants issued.
- Locked-in shares are present as per regulatory requirements.
- Significant beneficial owners identified.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 regarding shareholding pattern submission.

## Important Dates

- Quarter/Allotment Ending Date: 22-08-2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Sunshine Pictures Limited equity shares on the exchange platform.
