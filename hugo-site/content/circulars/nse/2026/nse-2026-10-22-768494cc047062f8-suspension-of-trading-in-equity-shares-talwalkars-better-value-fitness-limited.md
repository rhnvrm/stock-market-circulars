## Summary

National Stock Exchange has announced the suspension of trading in equity shares of Talwalkars Better Value Fitness Limited (Symbol: TALWALKARS) on account of capital reduction pursuant to NCLT and NCLAT orders.

## Key Points

- Company: Talwalkars Better Value Fitness Limited
- Symbol: TALWALKARS
- Record Date: October 26, 2026
- Reason: Capital reduction pursuant to NCLT and NCLAT orders dated February 26, 2026, and September 03, 2026, respectively.

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension executed in pursuance of Regulation 3.1.2 of the National Stock Exchange (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the suspension and ensure compliance with trading restrictions in the affected security.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 26, 2026
- NCLT Order Date: February 26, 2026
- NCLAT Order Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in equity shares of Talwalkars Better Value Fitness Limited is suspended, restricting market operations in the security.
