## Summary

The National Stock Exchange (NSE) has notified the listing and admission to dealings of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the Debt Market Segment of the Exchange with effect from October 12, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Key Points

- Admission of commercial papers issued by multiple companies to dealings on the Debt Market Segment effective October 12, 2026.
- Issuing entities include Tata Capital Limited, Axis Securities Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Tata Steel Limited, CEAT Limited, NTPC Limited, Motilal Oswal Finvest, and Hero FinCorp Limited.
- Allotment date for all listed commercial papers is October 9, 2026, with maturity dates ranging from October 2026 to April 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Issued under existing provisions of Regulation 3.1.1 of the National Stock Exchange Debt Market (Trading) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuing entities must maintain compliance with all ongoing reporting and debt listing obligations mandated by SEBI and NSE.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** October 9, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Varies by security (October 29, 2026 to April 6, 2027)

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances secondary market liquidity and trading transparency for the newly listed commercial papers.
