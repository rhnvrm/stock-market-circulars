## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has announced the adjustment of the market lot for Abhishek Integrations Limited (AILIMITED) on the SME platform on account of a 1:1 bonus issue, effective from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Abhishek Integrations Limited
- Symbol: AILIMITED
- Type of Corporate Action: BONUS (1:1 ratio)
- Adjustment Factor: 2
- Adjusted Market Lot: 3000
- Ex-date / Effective Date: August 24, 2026
- Record Date: August 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action processing.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to load the updated security.gz/ nnf_security.gz/ MII - Security File.gz file in the trading application before trading on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Ex-date: August 24, 2026
- Record Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members dealing in AILIMITED must update their trading files to reflect the new market lot of 3000 prior to market opening on August 24, 2026.
