## Summary

National Stock Exchange of India Limited has notified the admission to dealings of privately placed securities on the capital market segment pursuant to Regulation 3.1.1 and Regulation 2.5.5 of the NSE (Capital Market) Trading Regulations Part A, effective from October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Nomura Capital (India) Private Limited**: 
  - Symbol: 885NOM29 (NOM 8.85% 2029 TR 1), ISIN: INE357L07531, Coupon: 8.85%, Maturity: 19-Oct-2029, Market Lot: 1
  - Symbol: 890NOM29 (NOM 8.90% 2029 TR 2), ISIN: INE357L07549, Coupon: 8.90%, Maturity: 19-Oct-2029, Market Lot: 1
- **Alpha Alternatives Financial Services Private Limited**: 
  - Symbol: AAFS32D (AAFS MKT LKD 2032 Sr O), ISIN: INE0L6807385, Coupon: Reset, Maturity: 26-Oct-2032, Market Lot: 1
- **National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD)**: 
  - Symbol: 813NBRD30 (NBRD 8.13% 2030 Sr 27B), ISIN: INE261F08EW0, Coupon: 8.13%, Maturity: 11-Jan-2030, Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of further privately placed securities to dealings on the capital market segment under NSE Trading Regulations Part A.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading in these securities shall be identified strictly by their designated security codes and conducted in the specified market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: October 21, 2026
- **Effective Date**: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard listing update expanding trading opportunities in privately placed debt securities on the exchange.
