## Summary

SEBI has reviewed and modified the position limit norms and penalty provisions for violations or breaches of position limits in the commodity derivatives segment, based on stakeholder representations, Working Group recommendations, and public comments to facilitate Ease of Doing Business.

## Key Points

- Revised monetary penalties on trading members for client-level open interest violations are linked to the quantum/value of violation and charged per day of violation.
- Violations exceeding 2% of the prescribed limit incur a penalty based on limit exceeded x closing price x number of days x 2% or Rs. 2,00,000/-, whichever is lower.
- Violations up to 2% of the prescribed limit incur a penalty based on limit exceeded x closing price x number of days x 2% or Rs. 10,000/-, whichever is lower.
- Members must reduce positions to within prescribed limits by the next trading day; otherwise, the Exchange will square-off excess positions without further notice.
- Additional square-off and monetary penalty provisions apply if violations occur more than 3 times in a calendar month.

## Regulatory Changes

- Annexure J under sub-para (ii) of Para 3.4.3 of the SEBI Master Circular dated August 04, 2023, stands revised to update monitoring and penal provisions for position limit violations in commodity derivatives.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must monitor client-level open interest positions closely to avoid position limit breaches.
- Ensure prompt reduction of excess positions by the next trading day following any violation.

## Important Dates

- Circular issued on September 09, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances clarity and reasonableness of penalties for position limit breaches in commodity derivatives, promoting compliance and ease of doing business for market participants.
