## Summary

National Stock Exchange has issued a circular regarding the alignment of SEBI's Cyber Incident Reporting Portal with the FIRE Format, referencing SEBI circular dated August 24, 2026, and previous frameworks on Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience for SEBI Regulated Entities (REs).

## Key Points

- References SEBI Circular no. HO/(449)2026-ITD-5_DIV1/I/19448/2026 dated August 24, 2026.
- Focuses on the alignment of SEBI's Cyber Incident Reporting Portal with the FIRE Format.
- Applicable to all Trading Members and various SEBI regulated entities (AIFs, Clearing Corporations, Depositories, etc.).

## Regulatory Changes

- SEBI's Cyber Incident Reporting Portal has been updated to align with the FIRE format for standardized reporting of cyber security incidents.

## Compliance Requirements

- All Trading Members are advised to take note of the contents and comply with the reporting standard guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 25, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines cyber incident reporting for regulated entities through standardized digital formats.
