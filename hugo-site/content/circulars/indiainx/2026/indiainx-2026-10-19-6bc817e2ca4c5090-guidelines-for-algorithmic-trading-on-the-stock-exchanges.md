## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited has issued a circular drawing the attention of members and participants to the IFSCA circular regarding Guidelines for Algorithmic Trading on the Stock Exchanges.

## Key Points

- Members and participants are requested to take note of IFSCA circular no. IFSCA/CMD-DMIIT/ALGO/2026-27/01 dated October 16, 2026.
- The guidelines pertain to algorithmic trading operations on stock exchanges.
- A detailed copy of the guidelines is enclosed as Annexure-1.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adoption of IFSCA regulatory framework and guidelines for algorithmic trading across exchange segments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and participants must review and ensure compliance with the algorithmic trading standards issued by IFSCA.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 19, 2026
- IFSCA Circular Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standardizes algorithmic trading procedures and strengthens compliance requirements for market participants.
