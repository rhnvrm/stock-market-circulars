## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited (India INX) has issued a circular reminding all members to submit their Annual Returns, including Audited Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss Account, Cash Flow Statement, Net Worth Certificate, and Auditor’s Report for the financial year ended March 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Applicability:** All members of the Exchange.
- **Required Documents:** Audited Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss Account, Cash Flow Statement, Net Worth Certificate, and Auditor’s Report (Formats Annexure 1 to 8).
- **Submission Deadline:** October 31, 2026.
- **Submission Mode:** Via email to `inxmembership.ops@indiainx.com`.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic annual regulatory filing required by exchange members to demonstrate financial health and compliance with exchange bye-laws.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must prepare and submit the prescribed annual financial documents and net worth certificates in the required formats on or before October 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Submission Deadline:** October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Timely submission ensures regulatory compliance and avoids potential disciplinary actions or penalties from the exchange membership department.
