## Summary

India International Exchange (IndiaINX) has notified all registered broker-dealers regarding the submission of quarterly reports for the quarter ending September 2026, in accordance with IFSCA circular and master circular guidelines.

## Key Points

- Applicable to all registered broker-dealers of the Exchange, regardless of active or inactive membership status.
- Members registered with multiple Exchanges in IFSC are required to submit their respective data to the concerned Exchange only.
- Submissions must include the quarterly report (Annexure-1) and a signed undertaking by the Principal Officer and Compliance Officer (Annexure-2).

## Regulatory Changes

- Reinforces compliance with IFSCA Circular No. F.No.1/IFSCA/CMI Supervision/2023-24 dated April 08, 2026, and IFSCA Master Circular No. IFSCA/CMD/MIIT/MCBDCM/2026 dated May 12, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Submit the quarterly report and undertaking via email to inxmembership.ops@indiainx.com on or before the specified deadline.
- Ensure adherence to timelines by all registered broker-dealers.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 06, 2026
- Submission Deadline: October 21, 2026 (for quarter ending September 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Routine regulatory compliance obligation requiring broker-dealers to maintain accurate reporting schedules and timely submissions to regulatory operations.
