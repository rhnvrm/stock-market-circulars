## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited (India INX) has announced the listing of U.S.$10,000,000 8.85 per cent Senior Notes due 2027 issued by Midland Microfin Limited on its Global Securities Market, effective from October 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Midland Microfin Limited
- **Issue Size:** USD 10,000,000 (USD Ten Million)
- **Coupon Rate:** 8.85 per cent
- **Maturity Date:** 26 April 2027
- **Listing Date:** 06 October 2026
- **ISIN:** INIFD1305058
- **Scrip Code:** 1100199
- **Security Symbol:** 8850MMFL0427
- **Credit Rating:** CareEdge: B/Stable

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants and trading members must take note of the listing parameters and contract details for trading on the India INX platform.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Date:** 18 September 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** 06 October 2026
- **Maturity Date:** 26 April 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands debt instrument availability on the Global Securities Market, providing trading opportunities for eligible participants.
