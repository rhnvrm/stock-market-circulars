## Summary

India International Exchange (IndiaINX) has announced the revision of contract lot sizes for Indian Single Stock Futures & Options contracts, building upon circular No. 20260408-3 dated April 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Upward revision in contract size for 9 contracts (Annexure 1).
- Downward revision in contract size for 17 contracts (Annexure 2).
- No change in contract size for 82 contracts (Annexure 3).
- Effective date for the revised lot sizes is January 29, 2027.
- Applies to existing February 2027, March 2027 monthly contracts and new contracts introduced for April 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

The contract lot sizes for Indian Single Stock Futures & Options are revised across upward, downward, and unchanged categories as detailed in the annexures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to update their trading terminals, risk management systems, and client interfaces with the revised contract lot sizes ahead of the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** January 29, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in single stock derivatives must recalibrate position limits, margin computations, and order placement sizes in line with the new lot sizes.
