## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Limited (IndiaINX) has announced a relaxation in documentation requirements for the submission of Annual Returns for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. Based on stakeholder feedback and representations, the Exchange has decided to waive the requirement of submitting the Auditor’s Report (Annexure-1).

## Key Points

- Waiver of Auditor's Report (Annexure-1) for annual returns submission for the financial year ended March 31, 2026.
- Decision taken in furtherance of ease of doing business following representations from market participants.
- Deadline for submission is October 31, 2026.
- Submissions must be sent via email to inxmembership.ops@indiainx.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- The mandatory submission of the Auditor’s Report (Annexure-1) as part of the annual returns package has been officially waived off.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must submit their Annual Returns (Annexure 2 to 8, excluding Annexure 1) to the Exchange on or before October 31, 2026.
- Submissions should be emailed to inxmembership.ops@indiainx.com.
- All other document submission requirements specified in circular no. 20261012-1 remain unchanged.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Submission Deadline:** October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reduces compliance documentation overhead for exchange members by eliminating the requirement to procure and submit an Auditor's Report for annual filings.
