## Summary

IndiaINX has issued a circular requesting all clearing and trading members to submit their Half-Yearly Net Worth Certificate and computation as on September 30, 2026, latest by November 30, 2026, in accordance with IFSCA (Capital Market Intermediaries) Regulations, 2025.

## Key Points

- Mandatory submission of net worth certificate (Annexure 1) and computation of net worth (Annexure 2) as on September 30, 2026.
- Minimum net worth requirements specified under IFSCA (Capital Market Intermediaries) Regulations, 2025 apply (BD-SCM: USD 675,000; BD-CM and PCM: USD 1,350,000).
- Liquid net worth computations must account for base minimum capital, interest-free deposits, and margins maintained with exchanges/clearing corporations, while excluding liabilities as per CMI Regulations.
- For branch entities, minimum net worth can be maintained at the parent level in the home jurisdiction provided it is ring-fenced for the IFSC branch.

## Regulatory Changes

Reiteration of compliance with IFSCA (Capital Market Intermediaries) Regulations, 2025 (as amended up to September 05, 2026), Master Circular for Broker Dealers and Clearing Members dated May 12, 2026, and clarification circular dated December 30, 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must submit the Net Worth Certificate as per Annexure 1.
- Members must submit the Computation of Net Worth as per Annexure 2.
- Submissions must adhere strictly to the prescribed formats and definitions of liquid net worth.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 8, 2026
- Net Worth As On Date: September 30, 2026
- Submission Deadline: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and monitors capital adequacy and liquid asset requirements across all clearing and broker-dealer members operating on the exchange.
