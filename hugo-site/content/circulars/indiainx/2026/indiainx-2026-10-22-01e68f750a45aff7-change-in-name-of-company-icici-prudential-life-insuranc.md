## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. has issued a circular regarding the change in name and asset symbol of ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Ltd in the derivatives segment, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Asset Name / Symbol: ICICI PRUDENTIAL LIFE INSURANC (ICICIPRULI)
- New Asset Name / Symbol: ICICI Life Insurance Ltd (ICICILIFE)
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- All other parameters of the contract remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Exchange records and contract specifications updated to reflect the new company name and symbol.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and clearing participants must update their trading terminals, risk management systems, and back-office records with the new symbol (ICICILIFE).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; standard administrative update for contract identification.
