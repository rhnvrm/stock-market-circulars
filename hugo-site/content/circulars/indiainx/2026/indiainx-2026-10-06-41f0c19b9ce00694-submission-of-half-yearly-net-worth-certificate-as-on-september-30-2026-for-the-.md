## Summary

India INX has issued a circular reminding all Exchange members to submit their Half-Yearly Net Worth Certificate and computation as on September 30, 2026, for the financial year 2026-27. The submission deadline is November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Submission Deadline: November 30, 2026.
- Applicable to: All members of the Exchange (Broker Dealers, Clearing Members, etc.).
- Minimum net worth requirements specified under IFSCA (Capital Market Intermediaries) Regulations, 2025 range from USD 135,000 to USD 1,350,000 based on membership type.
- Required documents include Net Worth Certificate (Annexure 1) and Computation of Net Worth (Annexure 2).

## Regulatory Changes

- Highlights IFSCA (Capital Market Intermediaries) Regulations, 2025, specifically regarding the definition of liquid assets and net worth (excluding liabilities).
- Clarifies inclusion of base minimum capital, interest-free deposits, and margins in liquid net worth.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must submit the prescribed Annexures (Annexure 1 & Annexure 2) latest by November 30, 2026.
- Failure to maintain the required minimum net worth at any time results in restrictions from undertaking existing or new business activities in IFSC until restored, along with potential disciplinary action by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: October 06, 2026.
- Net Worth As on Date: September 30, 2026.
- Submission Deadline: November 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance, financial stability, and adherence to IFSCA capital adequacy standards for all intermediaries operating within the IFSC segment.
