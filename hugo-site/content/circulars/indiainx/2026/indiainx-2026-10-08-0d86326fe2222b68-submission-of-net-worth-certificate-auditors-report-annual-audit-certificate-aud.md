## Summary

India International Clearing Corporation (IFSC) Limited has issued a circular requesting Clearing Members (CMs) to submit their Net worth certificate, Auditor’s Report, Annual Audit Certificate, and Audited Annual Accounts for the financial year ended March 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable to all Clearing Members.
- Covers submission of Net worth certificate, Auditor's Report, Annual Audit Certificate, and Audited Annual Accounts for FY ended March 31, 2026.
- Prescribed formats are enclosed as Annexures 1 to 8.
- Submissions must be sent via email to membership@indiaicc.com.

## Regulatory Changes

Routine annual compliance submission requirement for clearing members.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing Members must submit the required audited accounts and certificates in the prescribed formats (Annexures 1 to 8) to membership@indiaicc.com.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 8, 2026
- Submission Deadline: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and ongoing financial health monitoring of clearing members by the clearing corporation.
