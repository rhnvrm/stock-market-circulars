## Summary

India International Clearing Corporation (IFSC) Limited (India ICC) has issued a reminder regarding the quarterly reporting requirements for Capital Market Intermediaries for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, pursuant to IFSCA circulars and master circulars.

## Key Points

- Applicable to all registered clearing members of India ICC regardless of active or inactive status.
- Members must submit data pertaining strictly to their respective clearing corporation.
- Submission requires the quarterly report (Annexure-1) and a duly signed undertaking by the Principal Officer and Compliance Officer (Annexure-2).

## Regulatory Changes

Reiterates adherence to IFSCA Circular No. F.No.1/IFSCA/CMI Supervision/2023-24 dated April 08, 2026, and IFSCA Master Circular No. IFSCA/CMD/MIIT/MCBDCM/2026 dated May 12, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

All registered clearing members must submit the completed quarterly report and undertaking via email to membership@indiaicc.com.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Submission Deadline:** October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures continuous regulatory oversight and compliance monitoring of capital market intermediaries operating within the IFSC.
