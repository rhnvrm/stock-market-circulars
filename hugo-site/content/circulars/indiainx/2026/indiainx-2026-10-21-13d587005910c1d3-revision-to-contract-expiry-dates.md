## Summary

India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. (IndiaINX) has announced a revision to the contract expiry date for BRENTCRUDE futures contracts, moving the expiry from 30-December-2026 to 29-December-2026 as per underlying market updates.

## Key Points

- **Product:** BRENTCRUDE Futures
- **Current Expiry Date:** 30-Dec-26
- **Revised Expiry Date:** 29-Dec-26
- **Implementation:** Incorporated in Contract Master files dated 22 October 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; operational schedule update aligned with the underlying market.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their systems and trading terminals with the revised contract master files reflecting the new expiry date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** 21 October 2026
- **Contract Master Update Date:** 22 October 2026
- **Revised Expiry Date:** 29-December-2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact affecting only participants trading BRENTCRUDE futures contracts.
