## Summary

The India International Exchange (IFSC) Ltd. (India INX) has announced a mock trading session on Saturday, October 10, 2026. The session is intended for trading members to test their trading applications, algorithmic trading setups, and ETI APIs under simulated market conditions.

## Key Points

- **Session Date:** Saturday, October 10, 2026.
- **Session Timings:** 
  - 1st Session: 14:00:00 Hrs. to 15:00:00 Hrs.
  - 2nd Session: 15:00:01 Hrs. to 16:00:00 Hrs.
- **Access Methods:** Trading members can access the environment via BoltPlus on Web and in-house or vendor-developed software.
- **Algorithmic Trading:** All user-IDs approved for algorithmic trading are required to participate in the mock trading session.
- **No Financial Implications:** Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or create real rights and liabilities.
- **Feedback:** Members are requested to provide feedback to the Business Operations team by 16:15 Hrs. on the mock trading day.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational mock trading session notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- All user-IDs approved for algorithmic trading must participate in the mock trading session, regardless of whether algorithms have changed.
- Members utilizing third-party empanelled vendor platforms or in-house developed systems via ETI APIs should use this opportunity to test their applications.
- Feedback regarding the mock trading session must be submitted to the Business Operations team by 16:15 Hrs.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** October 10, 2026
- **Feedback Submission Deadline:** October 10, 2026, by 16:15 Hrs.

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact. The session is purely for testing and familiarization purposes, ensuring system readiness and robust connectivity for trading members.
