## Summary

Effective from August 07, 2026, the equity shares of Prima Innovation Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the XT group of securities, following a scheme of arrangement/demerger.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544855
- ISIN: INE13LA01027
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: PRIMAINNO
- Trade-for-Trade Segment: The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.
- Face Value: Rs. 5/- each fully paid.
- Total Securities: 1,10,00,470 equity shares allotted pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement.

## Regulatory Changes

- Approval received from Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench vide order dated March 20, 2026, for the demerger of the 'Rotational Moulding Business Undertaking' of Prima Plastics Limited into Prima Innovation Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the special pre-open session (SPOS) applicability for IPOs and relisted scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 05, 2026
- Effective Date: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of Prima Plastics Limited receive shares in Prima Innovation Limited as per the demerger ratio, impacting trading dynamics in the short term.
