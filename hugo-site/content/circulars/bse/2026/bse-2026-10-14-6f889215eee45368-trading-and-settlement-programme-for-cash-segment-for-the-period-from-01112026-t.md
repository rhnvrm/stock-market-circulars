## Summary

BSE has released the trading and settlement schedule for the cash segment covering the period from November 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026, encompassing both T+1 and T+0 settlement cycles.

## Key Points

- Details settlement numbers, trading dates, depository data entry timelines, confirmation deadlines, and pay-in/pay-out schedules for the T+1 settlement cycle (Settlement Nos. DR-747/26-27 to DR-766/26-27).
- Outlines schedules for the T+0 settlement cycle (Settlement Nos. DR-444/26-27 onwards).
- Specifies auction offer submission and settlement dates.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard periodical settlement calendar release.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must ensure timely entry of 6A/7A data, confirmations, and fulfillment of pay-in and pay-out obligations as per the specified calendar.

## Important Dates

- **Period Start:** November 1, 2026
- **Period End:** November 30, 2026
- **Notice Date:** October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless operational planning and settlement management for market participants trading in the cash segment.
