## Summary\n\nBSE has informed trading members that further new securities issued under ESOP / ESOS by 14 companies have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Friday, September 25, 2026.\n\n## Key Points\n\n- Total Companies: 14 companies including Alkyl Amines Chemicals, AXIS Bank, Coforge, ITC, Wipro, and others.\n- Effective Date: September 25, 2026.\n- Securities: Further equity shares issued pursuant to Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS).\n\n## Regulatory Changes\n\nNone. Routine corporate listing action.\n\n## Compliance Requirements\n\nTrading members and market participants are requested to take note of the listed securities and corresponding lock-in details where applicable (e.g., ITC Limited).\n\n## Important Dates\n\n- Notice Date: September 24, 2026\n- Trading Effective Date: September 25, 2026\n\n## Impact Assessment\n\nRoutine capital expansion for the respective companies with minimal overall market impact.
