## Summary

BSE issued a notice to verify various news articles appearing in media and websites regarding listed companies. Clarification was sought and received from Jana Small Finance Bank Ltd, while a reply remains awaited from Vodafone Idea Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260825-21
- Clarification received from Jana Small Finance Bank Ltd (Scrip Code: 544118) regarding news on https://www.fortuneindia.com.
- Reply awaited from Vodafone Idea Ltd (Scrip Code: 532822) regarding news on https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to clarify news items as sought by the exchange to ensure accuracy and transparency for investors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 25 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct operational or market impact; informational notice regarding media verification.
