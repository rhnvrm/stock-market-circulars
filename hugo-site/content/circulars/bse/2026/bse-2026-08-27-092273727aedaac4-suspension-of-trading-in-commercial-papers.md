## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in commercial papers for several companies following the fixation of record dates for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the mentioned companies starting from the respective record/ex-dates.
- Affected entities include Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited, Mirae Asset Capital Markets (India) Private Limited, Indian Oil Corporation Ltd., The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd., Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, Poonawalla Fincorp Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, Sharekhan Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Godrej Finance Limited, Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, KEC International Ltd., TATA STEEL LIMITED, and Nuvama Wealth Management Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified commercial papers of the listed entities from the effective record dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 27 August 2026
- Record / Ex-Dates: 31 August 2026 to 08 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading operations for the specified commercial papers are suspended on the exchange platform in alignment with their redemption schedules.
