## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members that pursuant to the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated September 7, 2026, the appellants Vedprakash Devkinandan Chiripal and Savitridevi V. Chiripal have paid the full penalty amount in favor of SEBI in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips.

## Key Points

- Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260701-4 dated July 01, 2026.
- Hon’ble SAT order dated September 07, 2026 stayed the impugned order subject to the deposit of the full penalty within four weeks.
- SEBI confirmed via email dated September 30, 2026, that the appellants have successfully paid the penalty amount.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework change; confirms compliance with SAT conditional stay order via penalty deposit.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the penalty payment and ensure compliance with exchange instructions.

## Important Dates

- July 01, 2026: Previous Exchange Notice issued
- September 07, 2026: SAT Order directing penalty deposit
- September 30, 2026: SEBI confirmation of penalty payment

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for market participants regarding compliance status of the noticees; minimal direct impact on general trading operations.
