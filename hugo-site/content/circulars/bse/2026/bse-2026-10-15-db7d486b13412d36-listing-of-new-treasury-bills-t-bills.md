## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of three new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under the G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listing of three new Treasury Bills: 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day T-Bills.
- Effective trading date: October 16, 2026.
- Securities will not be available for trading two working days prior to their respective maturity/redemption dates.
- Details of the listed T-Bills:
  - Scrip 805280 | ISIN: IN002026X289 | Symbol: 91TB140127 | Maturity: 14/01/2027
  - Scrip 805281 | ISIN: IN002026Y287 | Symbol: 182TB15427 | Maturity: 15/04/2027
  - Scrip 805282 | ISIN: IN002026Z284 | Symbol: 364TB14127 | Maturity: 14/10/2027

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing of sovereign debt instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the trading restriction rule: securities are unavailable for trading two working days prior to the maturity/redemption date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 16, 2026
- Maturity Dates: January 14, 2027 (91TB140127), April 15, 2027 (182TB15427), October 14, 2027 (364TB14127)

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of debt trading instruments available to market participants on the exchange.
