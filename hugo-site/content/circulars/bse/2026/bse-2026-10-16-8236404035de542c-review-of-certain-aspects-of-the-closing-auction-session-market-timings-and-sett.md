## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about SEBI circular SEBI/HO/47/11/11(3)2025-MRD-POD2/I/23843/2026 dated October 15, 2026. The circular covers the review of certain aspects of the Closing Auction Session, market timings, and the settlement methodology for derivatives contracts.

## Key Points

- Refers to SEBI circular dated October 15, 2026.
- Focuses on Closing Auction Session, market timings, and derivatives settlement methodology.
- Copy of the SEBI circular is enclosed as an annexure for trading members' compliance and review.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SEBI guidelines regarding market timings, closing auction sessions, and derivatives contract settlement procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the guidelines and ensure necessary operational alignment with the revised framework.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational transparency and aligns market closing and derivatives settlement mechanisms with updated regulatory standards.
