## Summary

Mirae Asset Mutual Fund has announced that the Mirae Asset Life Cycle Fund 2056 will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and facilities like SIP, STP, and SWP on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting October 21, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20260925-53.

## Key Points

- Schemes available across Regular and Direct plans covering Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- Effective date for ongoing transactions: October 21, 2026.
- End date for SIP, STP, and SWP: On or before October 19, 2056.
- Available ISINs include INF769K01RR8, INF769K01RT4, INF769K01RS6, INF769K01RU2, INF769K01RW8, and INF769K01RV0.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors must ensure correct scheme codes and ISINs are used while processing transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 20, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026
- SIP/STP/SWP End Date: October 19, 2056

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless ongoing investment and systematic facility options for investors in the Mirae Asset Life Cycle Fund 2056 via the BSE StAR MF platform.
