## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are advised to test their connectivity to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site using the specified technical parameters.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Equity Derivatives segment.
- Connection to the DR site of BOLT Plus trading system can be established via BOLT TWS, 3rd party applications using ETI APIs, or exchange web-based systems.
- Primary DR IP and Port for ETI: 10.255.241.16; Port: 15910.
- Secondary DR IP and Port for ETI: 10.255.241.17; Port: 15910.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should verify their disaster recovery connection parameters and configurations ahead of the mock session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: Saturday, March 14, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Helps trading members ensure business continuity readiness by testing disaster recovery failover mechanisms for equity derivatives trading.
