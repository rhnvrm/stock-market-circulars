## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the new ISIN number for Samor Reality Ltd (Scrip Code: 543376) following the sub-division of its equity shares from a face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Samor Reality Ltd
- Scrip Code: 543376
- New ISIN: INE0GD201022
- Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-
- Ex-Date: October 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Allocation of new ISIN number corresponding to the capital restructuring / stock split.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure trades on and from the ex-date are processed using the new ISIN.
