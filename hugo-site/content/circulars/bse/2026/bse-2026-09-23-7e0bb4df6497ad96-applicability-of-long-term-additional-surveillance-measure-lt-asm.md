## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and transfer of specific securities to designated groups.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable effective September 28, 2026, on all open positions as on September 25, 2026, and new positions created from September 28, 2026 onwards for securities under Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework and subject to Lower Price Bands effective September 24, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Securities continuing in the LT-ASM framework transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group effective September 28, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- Securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective September 24, 2026, are listed in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of stringent surveillance measures including 100% margin rates and lower price bands for shortlisted securities based on XBRL submissions.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure adequate margin maintenance for open and new positions as per the specified effective dates.
- Monitor securities listed in the respective annexures for group transfers and trading band revisions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date for Higher Stages / Price Bands / Moving Out: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins / Group Transfers: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations for market participants trading in securities placed under higher stages of the LT-ASM framework.
- Potential liquidity impact on scrips subjected to lower price bands and transfer to specific trading groups.
