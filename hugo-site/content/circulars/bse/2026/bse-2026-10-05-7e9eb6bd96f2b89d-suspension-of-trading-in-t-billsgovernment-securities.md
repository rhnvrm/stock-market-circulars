## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities due to their maturity, with effect from October 06, 2026. Trading members are advised not to deal in these specified securities.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in three T-bills/Government Securities (Scrip Codes: 805200, 805129, 805238).
- Effective date of suspension: October 06, 2026 (DR- 729/2026-2027).
- Affected securities include 182TB81026, 364TB81026, and 91TB081026 with respective ISINs IN002026Y014, IN002025Z286, and IN002026X149.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine market operation action corresponding to the maturity and redemption of sovereign debt instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid T-bills/Government Securities with effect from October 06, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension / Redemption Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard procedure for maturing debt instruments with no broader market or equity impact.
