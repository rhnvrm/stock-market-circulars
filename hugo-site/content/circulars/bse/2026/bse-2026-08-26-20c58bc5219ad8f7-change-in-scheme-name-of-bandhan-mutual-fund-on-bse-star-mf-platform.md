## Summary

Bandhan Mutual Fund has updated the scheme names for several of its schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective from August 26, 2026, following communication received by BSE from the fund house.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Affected Entity: Bandhan Mutual Fund / Bandhan AMC.
- Scope: Scheme name updates across multiple debt, hybrid, and equity funds (e.g., Bandhan Gilt Fund with 10 Year Constant Maturity changed to Bandhan 10 Year Constant Maturity Gilt Fund, Bandhan Banking and PSU Fund changed to Bandhan Banking and PSU Debt Fund).

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors should note the updated scheme names for future transactions, order routing, and reporting on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal impact; primarily requires updating internal system nomenclature and reference data to reflect the revised scheme names.
