## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the application and revision of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on specific securities pursuant to the revised framework. The measures include 100% margins, movement to higher stages, transfer to specific trading groups, and price band modifications.

## Key Points

- 100% margins become applicable effective August 22, 2026, on all open positions as of August 21, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages or continuing in the framework may face lower price bands or transfers to T / XT / MT / TS groups effective August 20 or August 22, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework, with their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is a market surveillance action, not an adverse corporate action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins on select securities under LT-ASM.
- Revision of price bands and transfer of securities to specific segments/groups (T / XT / MT / TS).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance and adhere to revised price bands and trading restrictions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Bands / Stage Movements / Moving out: August 20, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: August 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities may be impacted due to 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions.
