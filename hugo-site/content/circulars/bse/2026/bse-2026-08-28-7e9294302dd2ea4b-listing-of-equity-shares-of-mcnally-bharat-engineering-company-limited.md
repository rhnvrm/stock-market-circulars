## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, September 01, 2026, the equity shares of McNally Bharat Engineering Company Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the T Group (Trade-to-Trade segment). This follows the implementation of a Resolution Plan approved under the provisions of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016, by the NCLT.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Details:** Scrip Code: 532629, ISIN: INE748A01024, Scrip ID: MBECL.
- **Share Capital Restructuring:** Post-resolution plan, the total paid-up equity share capital is changed to Rs. 33,33,33,340/- divided into 3,33,33,334 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each.
- **Allotment Breakdown:** Includes 16,66,667 shares post capital reduction, 16,66,667 shares allotted on preferential basis to public shareholders, and 3,00,00,000 shares allotted on preferential basis to the Successful Resolution Applicant (SRA).
- **Trading Group:** Available for trading in the T group (Trade-to-Trade segment).

## Regulatory Changes

- Extinguishment of entire shareholding held by erstwhile promoters.
- Reduction of balance shareholding of public shareholders to constitute 5% post-issuance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the special pre-open session for IPO and other categories as per applicable SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 01, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Resumption of trading for McNally Bharat Engineering Company Limited under revised capital structure and Trade-to-Trade surveillance category.
