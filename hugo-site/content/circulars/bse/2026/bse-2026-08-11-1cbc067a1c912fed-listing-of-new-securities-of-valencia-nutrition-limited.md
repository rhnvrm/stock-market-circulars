## Summary

Trading members are informed that 15,20,000 new equity shares of Valencia Nutrition Limited (Scrip Code: 542910) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Wednesday, August 12, 2026. These shares are issued on a preferential basis to promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 15,20,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 30/-.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 40/- per share.
- **ISIN:** INE08RT01016
- **Effective Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Lock-in Details:** 15,20,000 shares under lock-in up to 18/02/2028 (Dist. Nos. 18320342 to 19840341).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Complies with standard preferential allotment and warrant conversion listing requirements.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 09/06/2026
- **Listing Date:** 12/08/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 18/02/2028

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase via warrant conversion for promoters; minimal direct market impact.
