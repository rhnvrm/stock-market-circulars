## Summary

Access Computech Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with BSE under the SME segment, containing disclosures as specified under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations. The document is hosted on the BSE SME website for public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Access Computech Limited
- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Department: DCS-Listing (SME IPO)
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from the date of notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com
- Hosting Website: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

- Adherence to Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations regarding public disclosure and 21-day public comment period for SME IPO draft offer documents.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stakeholders and the public may submit comments on the Draft Offer Document within 21 days of the notice via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Comments Closing Date: October 15, 2026 (21 days post-notice)

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates market transparency and regulatory review by enabling public feedback on Access Computech Limited's proposed SME public issue.
