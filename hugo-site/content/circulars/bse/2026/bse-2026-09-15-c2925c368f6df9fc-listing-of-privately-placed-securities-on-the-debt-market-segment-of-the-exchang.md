## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities issued by Sammaan Capital Limited on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sammaan Capital Limited
- Securities Listed: Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) under Scrip Codes 978142 and 978143.
- ISINs: INE148I07YM4 and INE148I07YL6.
- Coupon Rates: 9.05% p.a. (maturing 11/09/2029) and 9.10% p.a. (maturing 11/09/2031).
- Form of Trading: Dematerialised form only.
- Tick Size: 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialised form adhering to the specified ISINs and tick size.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 11/09/2026
- Effective Listing Date: 15/09/2026
- Interest Payment: Annually from 11/09/2027
- Redemption Dates: 11/09/2029 and 11/09/2031

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and secondary market trading access for the newly issued debt instruments of Sammaan Capital Limited.
