## Summary

THE WEALTH COMPANY MID CAP FUND is reopening for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform with effect from August 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopens for ongoing transactions starting August 06, 2026.
- Includes Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- Facilities available include subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants must ensure transactions are processed in accordance with the specified ISINs and scheme details.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational availability of the mutual fund scheme for investors and trading members on the BSE StAR MF platform.
