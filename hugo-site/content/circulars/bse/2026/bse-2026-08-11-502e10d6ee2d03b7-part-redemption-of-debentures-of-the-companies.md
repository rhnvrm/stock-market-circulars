## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260811-49
- Department: Listing Operations
- Covers multiple companies undergoing part redemption of debt securities/debentures.
- Trading will resume with reduced face values as per the schedule.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of reduced face values for specified NCDs following part redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to note the reduced face values and respective record/ex-dates for trading debentures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 11 August 2026
- Record Dates: 15 August 2026, 16 August 2026, 28 August 2026, and 29 August 2026 (varying by security).
- Ex-Dates: 14 August 2026 and 28 August 2026 (varying by security).

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding the affected NCDs will receive part redemption payments and interest according to the fixed record dates with corresponding adjustments to the face value of the securities.
