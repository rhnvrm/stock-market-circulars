## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in the debt securities/debentures of IL&FS Financial Services Limited due to their maturity on September 12, 2026. Trading members are advised to cease dealing in these debentures effective September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: IL&FS Financial Services Limited
- Scrip Code: 954850
- Particulars: ILFS-8.51%-12-9-26-PVT
- ISIN: INE121H07BQ2
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debentures with effect from September 10, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 09, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: September 10, 2026
- Maturity Date: September 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified debt securities of IL&FS Financial Services Limited is halted ahead of maturity, restricting further secondary market transactions.
