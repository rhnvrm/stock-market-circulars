## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the rights issue of equity shares of Esaar (India) Ltd. (Scrip code: 531502). The company has fixed the record date for the rights issue, and transactions will be on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Esaar (India) Ltd. (Scrip Code: 531502)
- Record Date: 25/08/2026
- Ex-Rights Basis From Date & Sett. No.: 25/08/2026 (DR- 701/2026-2027)
- Issue Details: Issue of 44 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each for cash at par on rights basis for every 15 Equity Shares held.
- Issue Price: Rs. 10/- per share payable on application.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are required to take note of the record date and ex-rights date for processing transactions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 25/08/2026
- Ex-Rights Date: 25/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares as of the record date are eligible for the rights issue entitlement. Share prices will adjust on the ex-rights date to reflect the corporate action.
