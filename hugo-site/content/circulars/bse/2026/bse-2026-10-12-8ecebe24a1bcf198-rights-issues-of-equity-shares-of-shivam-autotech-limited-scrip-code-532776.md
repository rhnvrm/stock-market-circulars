## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Shivam Autotech Limited has fixed October 15, 2026, as the Record Date for its rights issue of equity shares. Transactions in the equity shares of the company will take place on an ex-rights basis starting from the same date.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Shivam Autotech Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 532776
- **Record Date:** October 15, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Basis Date:** October 15, 2026
- **Issue Ratio:** 19 (Nineteen) equity shares of Rs. 2/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 10/- per share for every 25 (twenty-five) equity shares held.
- **Payment Terms:** The full issue price of Rs. 12/- per equity share is payable on application.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to note the record date and ex-rights trading commencement date for proper settlement and execution of transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Record Date:** October 15, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Date:** October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders holding shares as of the record date are eligible for rights entitlements. Stock prices will adjust on the ex-rights date to reflect the corporate action.
