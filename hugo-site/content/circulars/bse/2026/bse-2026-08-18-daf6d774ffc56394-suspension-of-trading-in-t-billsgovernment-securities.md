## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities due to their maturity on the redemption date of August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 805182
- Particulars: 182TB21826
- ISIN: IN002025Y461
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid securities effective from August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no further transactions are executed in the specified T-bill starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Redemption Date: August 18, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for maturing government securities with no broader market impact.
