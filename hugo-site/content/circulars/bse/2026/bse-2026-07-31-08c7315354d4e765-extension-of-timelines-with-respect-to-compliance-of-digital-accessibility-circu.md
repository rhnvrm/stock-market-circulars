## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the extension of timelines for complying with Digital Accessibility Circulars, following SEBI's Circular No. HO/(411)2026-ITD-5_DIV2/I/17922/2026 dated July 31, 2026. The deadline for conducting accessibility audits for digital platforms and remediating audit findings has been extended to October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Extension granted for conducting digital accessibility audits and remediating findings.
- New compliance deadline is October 31, 2026.
- Applicable to all regulated entities under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of previously established timelines by SEBI concerning digital platform accessibility compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated entities must complete their digital platform accessibility audits and remediation of findings no later than October 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Extended Deadline:** October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulated entities additional time to ensure their digital platforms fully comply with accessibility standards mandated for persons with disabilities.
