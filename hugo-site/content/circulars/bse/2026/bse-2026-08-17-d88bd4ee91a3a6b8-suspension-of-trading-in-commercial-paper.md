## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CP) for Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited and Can Fin Homes Ltd. due to the fixation of record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified CPs from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited (Scrip Code: 732286):** CP ID `KMIL-19-8-26-CP`, ISIN `INE975F14D24`, Record Date & Ex-Date: `18/08/2026`, Settlement No: `696/2026-2027`.
- **Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited (Scrip Code: 732275):** CP ID `KMIL-20-08-26-CP`, ISIN `INE975F14D32`, Record Date & Ex-Date: `19/08/2026`, Settlement No: `697/2026-2027`.
- **Can Fin Homes Ltd. (Scrip Code: 731730):** CP ID `CFHL-21-8-26-CP`, ISIN `INE477A14EH2`, Record Date & Ex-Date: `20/08/2026`, Settlement No: `698/2026-2027`.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of the mentioned companies on and from the specified record/ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **18/08/2026:** Record Date and Ex-Date for Kotak Mahindra Investments Ltd. (INE975F14D24)
- **19/08/2026:** Record Date and Ex-Date for Kotak Mahindra Investments Ltd. (INE975F14D32)
- **20/08/2026:** Record Date and Ex-Date for Can Fin Homes Ltd. (INE477A14EH2)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth processing of commercial paper redemptions by halting secondary market transactions ahead of the record dates.
