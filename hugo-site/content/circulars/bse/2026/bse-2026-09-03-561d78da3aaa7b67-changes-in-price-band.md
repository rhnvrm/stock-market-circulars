## Summary

Stock Exchange, BSE, has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 11 scrips with effect from September 04, 2026, as part of its surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 11 scrips.
- Effective date: September 04, 2026.
- Includes scrips such as AKI India Ltd, ENS Enterprises Ltd, Integrated Proteins Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands for the mentioned scrips have been modified from their existing levels to new percentages (ranging from 2% to 20%).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while placing orders in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity for the specified scrips will be constrained within the newly defined percentage limits, mitigating excessive volatility.
