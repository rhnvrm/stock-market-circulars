## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Xtranet Technologies Limited (Scrip Code: 544838) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment (B Group) with effect from Thursday, August 13, 2026, following up on the exchange's previous notice dated July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Xtranet Technologies Limited
- Scrip Code: 544838
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade Segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling Segment (B Group) / Normal Rolling Settlement
- Effective Date: August 13, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Shift of securities from the restrictive Trade for Trade segment back to the regular Rolling segment, permitting normal trading and settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment change for executing transactions and managing settlements accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- Effective Date: August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Normalizes trading liquidity and settlement procedures for the equity shares of Xtranet Technologies Limited by moving them out of the Trade for Trade restriction.
