## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the inclusion of securities satisfying criteria for the measure, movement of securities to lower stages, and securities moving out of the framework. Shortlisted securities will attract a minimum 100% margin and be shifted to the trade-for-trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM and attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 10, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II shall be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 09, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 10, 2026.
- Periodic reviews determine securities moving to lower stages or moving out of the ESM framework entirely.
- Shortlisting is strictly a market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margins (100%) on open and new positions for shortlisted securities.
- Reclassification of eligible securities to the Trade-for-Trade settlement segment.
- Application of price bands (2%) and Periodic Call Auction for securities moving to Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on open positions as of September 09, 2026, and new positions created from September 10, 2026.
- Trading members should note the segment shifts and price band changes applicable to affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II adjustments and moving out of framework: September 09, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shift: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity for securities placed under the ESM framework may experience constraints due to 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and 2% price bands.
