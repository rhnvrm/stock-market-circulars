## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of subscriptions, including fresh and additional lumpsum purchases as well as Switch-In transactions, for the Invesco India Gold ETF Fund of Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Refers to previous Notice No. 20260615-34 dated June 15, 2026, which had restricted subscriptions.
- Lumpsum investments and switch-in transactions are now fully enabled without restrictions.
- Changes implemented following communication and request from Invesco Mutual Fund / AMC.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of trading/subscription restrictions on the specified mutual fund scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and trading participants are advised to note the changes for executing transactions under the scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors can resume normal lumpsum and switch-in investments in the Invesco India Gold ETF Fund of Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform without prior restrictions.
