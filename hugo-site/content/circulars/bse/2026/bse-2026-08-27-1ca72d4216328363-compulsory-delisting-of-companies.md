## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the compulsory delisting of two companies—Atcom Technologies Ltd and Ridings Consulting Engineers India Ltd—pursuant to orders of the Delisting Committee under SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2009/2021. The companies have remained suspended for more than 6 months and will be delisted from the Exchange platform effective August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date of Delisting: August 31, 2026.
- Companies to be delisted:
  1. Atcom Technologies Ltd (Scrip Code: 527007) - Delisted under SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2009.
  2. Ridings Consulting Engineers India Ltd (Scrip Code: 541151) - Delisted under SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021.
- Securities will cease to be listed and will no longer be available for trading on the Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to Regulation 34(1) of SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021 (and corresponding 2009 regulations), the delisted company, its whole-time directors, promoters, and associated companies are barred from directly or indirectly accessing the securities market or seeking listing for 10 years.
- Promoters are required to purchase shares from public shareholders at a fair value determined by an independent valuer appointed by the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Promoters must provide an exit option to public shareholders in compliance with regulatory provisions.
- For companies with a positive fair value, promoter share transfers, pledges, and corporate benefits (dividend, rights, bonus, split, etc.) are frozen until the exit option is successfully provided.
- Promoters, whole-time directors, and compliance-responsible persons are ineligible to become directors of any listed company until the exit option is provided.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 27, 2026
- Delisting Effective Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Public shareholders of the affected companies will receive an exit option based on fair valuation by an independent valuer. Trading access for these securities on the exchange is permanently terminated, and strict regulatory penalties are imposed on the management and promoters.
