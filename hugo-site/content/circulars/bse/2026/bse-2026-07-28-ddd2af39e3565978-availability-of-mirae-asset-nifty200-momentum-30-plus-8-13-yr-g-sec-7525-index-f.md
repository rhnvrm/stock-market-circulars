## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that pursuant to Notice No. 20260709-50 dated July 09, 2026, the Mirae Asset Nifty200 Momentum 30 Plus 8-13 Yr G-Sec 75:25 Index Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective from July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reopening of ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Effective Date: July 29, 2026.
- Covers multiple plans including regular and direct growth/IDCW options.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 28, 2026
- **Effective Date:** July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless access for investors and distributors to execute ongoing transactions and systematic investment plans for the specified Mirae Asset index fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.
