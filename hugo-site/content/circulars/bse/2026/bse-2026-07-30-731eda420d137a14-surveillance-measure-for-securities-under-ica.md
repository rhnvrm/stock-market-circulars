## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies under the Inter Creditor Agreement (ICA), with effect from July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the ASM criteria under the Inter Creditor Agreement (ICA) are listed in the attached Annexure I.
- The framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under this framework is a market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the company.
- Securities placed under this framework will be reviewed periodically.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ASM for companies under ICA starting July 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and members must note the list of securities under the framework and ensure adherence to the surveillance measures.
- Clarifications can be sought by writing to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in affected securities may be subject to additional surveillance controls and monitoring as per exchange norms.
