## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the draft TRAI Direction on Minimum Information Requirements for OTP SMS Content Template Format.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260925-2 dated September 25, 2026.
- Issued under the General segment / Settlement/RMS by BSECL.
- Refers to Annexure A containing draft guidelines by TRAI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Draft direction issued by TRAI concerning minimum information standards for OTP SMS content templates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities and market participants should review Annexure A for specific draft template requirements and proposed norms.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 25 September 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- The circular serves as notice of a draft regulatory direction affecting communication templates and messaging standards.
