## Summary

Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund is launching its New Fund Offer (NFO) for Motilal Oswal Multi Asset - Omni Fund of Funds along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from October 21, 2026, to November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes include Regular and Direct plans for Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options under Motilal Oswal Multi Asset - Omni Fund of Funds.
- Tentative allotment date is scheduled for November 11, 2026.
- Lumpsum purchases on BSE StAR MF Platform extended till midnight on November 04, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on November 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational circular for mutual fund NFO participation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 11, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** October 21, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** November 04, 2026
- **SIP/XSIP Authentication Deadline:** November 04, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** November 11, 2026
- **Clear Funds Deadline:** November 11, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for trading members and investors participating in the Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
