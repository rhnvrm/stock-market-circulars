## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of INDRAYANI BIOTECH LIMITED are permitted on the Exchange with effect from Tuesday, September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: INDRAYANI BIOTECH LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751142
- Scrip ID: INDRA-RE
- ISIN No.: INE007C20013
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: September 15, 2026 to October 08, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: September 15, 2026 to October 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must settle Rights Entitlements (REs) on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only. Members must inform clients about the RE commencement, ensure REs are not mistaken for ordinary shares, and include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes explaining that purchase of REs only gives the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce them before the issue closes.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Start Date: September 15, 2026
- RE Trading End Date: October 08, 2026
- Rights Issue Opening Date: September 15, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding or trading in the REs of Indrayani Biotech Limited must execute transactions within the specified window and note that unapplied or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the Issue Closing Date.
