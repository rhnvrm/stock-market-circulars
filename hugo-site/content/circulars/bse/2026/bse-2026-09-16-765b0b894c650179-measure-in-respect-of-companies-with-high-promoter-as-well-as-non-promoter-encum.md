## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding measures for companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter 'encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I have satisfied criteria for inclusion and will attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Securities listed in Annexure II are eligible to move out from the framework.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of a minimum 75% margin requirement on short-listed securities exhibiting high encumbrances pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the margin requirements and ensure compliance across all applicable open and new positions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Inclusion/Margin:** September 21, 2026 (on all open positions as on September 18, 2026, and new positions created thereafter).
- **Effective Date for Exclusion:** September 17, 2026.
- **Notice Date:** September 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding positions in securities with high encumbrances will face higher margin obligations (minimum 75%), potentially impacting liquidity and trading strategies for those specific stocks.
