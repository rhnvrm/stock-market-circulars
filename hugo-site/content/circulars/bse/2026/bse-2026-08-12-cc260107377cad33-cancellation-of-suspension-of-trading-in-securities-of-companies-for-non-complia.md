## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has decided not to proceed with the suspension of trading in the securities of Padmalaya Telefilms Ltd (Scrip code: 532350) and Shiva Granito Export Ltd (Scrip code: 540072), effective August 14, 2026. This is in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260714-12 dated July 14, 2026, which had proposed the suspension of trading due to non-compliance with Regulation 76 of SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018 for the consecutive quarters of December 2025 and March 2026.

## Key Points

- **Suspension Revoked:** BSE will not proceed with the suspension of trading for the specified companies.
- **Companies Affected:** Padmalaya Telefilms Ltd and Shiva Granito Export Ltd.
- **Effective Date:** Trading continuity is maintained with effect from August 14, 2026.
- **Unchanged Provisions:** All other provisions from the previous notice dated July 14, 2026, remain unchanged.
- **Clarifications:** Any inquiries regarding this notice can be sent to bse.soplodr@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- **SEBI (Depositories & Participants) Regulations, 2018:** Under Regulation 76, companies must submit reconciliation of share capital audit reports. Non-compliance for consecutive quarters triggers automatic suspension procedures, which have now been cancelled for these entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Listed Companies:** Continuous adherence to reconciliation of share capital audit requirements is mandatory to avert suspension actions.
- **For Market Participants:** No compliance actions required; the shares of these companies continue to trade as normal.

## Important Dates

- **July 14, 2026:** Issue date of the original suspension warning notice (Notice No. 20260714-12).
- **August 12, 2026:** Issue date of this cancellation of suspension notice.
- **August 14, 2026:** Official effective date from which the suspension is averted.

## Impact Assessment

- **For Listed Companies:** Highly positive outcome for Padmalaya Telefilms Ltd and Shiva Granito Export Ltd as they retain their active trading status on BSE.
- **For Investors:** Prevents loss of liquidity and allows existing shareholders to continue trading their holdings without interruption.
- **Market Impact:** Neutral for the broader market but showcases active enforcement of Listing Compliance procedures by the exchange.
