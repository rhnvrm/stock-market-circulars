## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the application of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stages I and II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Effective September 11, 2026, enhanced margin requirements will be enforced on all open positions as of September 10, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying specific criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin is 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable rate of margin is 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Measures apply in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely for market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision and enforcement of ST-ASM framework parameters for shortlisted securities as per the attached annexure.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated unless bound by other surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities starting September 11, 2026.
- Members can direct queries to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 09 Sep 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance/Margins: September 11, 2026 (on open positions as on September 10, 2026, and new positions onwards)

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in the shortlisted securities will experience increased capital requirements due to higher margin obligations (up to 100%), which may affect liquidity and trading volumes in those specific scrips.
