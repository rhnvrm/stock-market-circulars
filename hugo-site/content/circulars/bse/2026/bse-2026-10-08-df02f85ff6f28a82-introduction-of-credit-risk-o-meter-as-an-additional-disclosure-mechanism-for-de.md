## Summary

BSE has introduced the Credit Risk-o-Meter as an additional disclosure mechanism for debt securities, enhancing transparency regarding credit risk profiles for investors.

## Key Points

- Introduction of Credit Risk-o-Meter for debt securities.
- Serves as an additional disclosure mechanism to reflect credit risk changes.
- Applicable to listed companies with debt securities.

## Regulatory Changes

Issuers of debt securities are now required to incorporate the Credit Risk-o-Meter in their periodic disclosures and relevant reporting mechanisms as mandated.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities issuing debt securities must ensure timely and accurate disclosure of the Credit Risk-o-Meter along with their financial results or periodic filings.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 08 October 2026
- Effective as specified in the circular and regulatory guidelines.

## Impact Assessment

Improves investor awareness and risk assessment capabilities for debt instruments by providing clear, standardized risk indicators.
