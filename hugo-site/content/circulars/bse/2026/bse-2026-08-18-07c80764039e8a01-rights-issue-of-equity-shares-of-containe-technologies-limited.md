## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the rights issue of equity shares by Containé Technologies Limited. The company has fixed the record date and ex-rights date for the corporate action.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Containé Technologies Limited (Scrip Code: 543606)
- Record Date: 21/08/2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: 21/08/2026
- Rights Issue Ratio: 02 (Two) Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 5/- per share for every 01 (One) Equity Share held.
- Issue Price: Entire issue price of Rs. 15/- per share is payable at the time of making the application.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the ex-rights date and ensure transactions are conducted accordingly under the dematerialised securities rolling settlement segment.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Rights Date: 21/08/2026
- Record Date: 21/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares as of the record date are eligible for the rights issue entitlement. Price adjustment for the stock will take effect from the ex-rights date.
