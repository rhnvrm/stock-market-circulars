## Summary

BARODA BNP Paribas Mutual Fund has announced the merger of specific schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective after business hours on August 21, 2026. Investors are provided an exit load waiver period for redemptions or switches from July 22, 2026, to August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Merger effective date: After the close of business hours on August 21, 2026.
- Transferor schemes include BARODA BNP Paribas Credit Risk Fund, Baroda BNP Paribas Short Duration Fund, and Baroda BNP Paribas Nifty SDL December 2026 Index Fund.
- Exit load is waived for redemptions or switches out of transferor schemes between July 22, 2026, and August 20, 2026.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP) will be automatically migrated to corresponding transferee schemes after business hours on August 20, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None specific; operational procedure governed by mutual fund scheme merger guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must take note of the transaction cut-off dates for demat and non-demat modes.
- Transferor schemes cease to be available for transaction processing after business hours on August 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Exit Load Waiver Period: July 22, 2026, to August 20, 2026.
- Subscription Acceptance (Demat): August 16, 2026.
- Subscription Acceptance (Non-Demat): August 20, 2026.
- Merger Effective Date: August 21, 2026 (after close of business hours).

## Impact Assessment

- Operational transition for investors holding units in the transferor schemes with automated migration of systematic investments and seamless exit load waivers.
