## Summary

Gleaming Apparels Limited has submitted its Draft Offer Document to the BSE SME platform on September 29, 2026, in compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations. The document is hosted for public review and comments for at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Issuer Company: Gleaming Apparels Limited
- Filing Date: September 29, 2026
- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice
- Designated Email for Comments: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine public notification under ICDR Regulations for SME IPO draft offer documents.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public may review the Draft Offer Document on the BSE SME website and submit any comments or feedback within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 29, 2026
- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Comment Deadline: 21 days from September 30, 2026
