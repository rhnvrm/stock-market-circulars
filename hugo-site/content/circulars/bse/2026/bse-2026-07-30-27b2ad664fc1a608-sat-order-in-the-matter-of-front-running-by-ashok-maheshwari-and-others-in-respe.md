## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the SAT order in the matter of front running by Ashok Maheshwari and others in respect of Mihir Dhirajlal Savla, noting that the penalty amount of Rs. 31,00,000.00 has been paid by the appellant in favor of SEBI.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous exchange notices dated April 27, 2026, and July 29, 2026.
- SAT order dated July 03, 2026, granted a stay of the impugned order subject to the deposit of a 100% penalty amount within four weeks.
- SEBI confirmed via email on July 29 and July 30, 2026, that the appellants (Benzer Department Stores Private Limited & Anr / Mihir Dhirajlal Savla) paid the penalty of Rs. 31,00,000.00 in favor of SEBI.
- Noticee Name: Mihir Dhiralal Savla (PAN: AQRPS0359K).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SAT stay conditions and confirmation of penalty receipt by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the payment and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- SAT Order Date: July 03, 2026
