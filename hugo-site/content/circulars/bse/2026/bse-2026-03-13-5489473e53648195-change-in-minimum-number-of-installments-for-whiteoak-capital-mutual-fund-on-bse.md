## Summary

WhiteOak Capital Asset Management Limited has issued an addendum to the Statement of Additional Information (SAI) announcing a reduction in the minimum number of instalments for Fixed Systematic Transfer Plan (FSTP) and Flex Systematic Transfer Plan (Flex STP) under the WhiteOak Capital Mid Cap Fund, effective March 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Facility Affected:** Fixed Systematic Transfer Plan (FSTP) and Flex Systematic Transfer Plan (Flex STP).
- **Scheme:** WhiteOak Capital Mid Cap Fund.
- **Change:** The minimum number of instalments for the Daily frequency is reduced from 60 to 15.
- **Effective Date:** March 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational adjustment by the Asset Management Company (AMC).

## Compliance Requirements

This addendum forms an integral part of the SAI and Scheme Information Document (SID) of WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund. All other terms and conditions remain unchanged.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Notice:** March 13, 2026
- **Effective Date:** March 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides greater flexibility to investors opting for daily Systematic Transfer Plan facilities by lowering the commitment threshold for instalment counts.
