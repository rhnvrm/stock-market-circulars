## Summary

BSE has announced a change in the settlement type for SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, moving from T+2 to T+1 settlement with immediate effect following communication from SBI Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund
- Current Settlement Type: T+2
- Revised Settlement Type: T+1
- Effective Date: With immediate effect (Notice Date: September 10, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; operational update regarding fund settlement cycles on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members need to take note of the revised settlement type for transactions in the scheme on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines trade settlement for investors and distributors in SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund by accelerating the settlement cycle to T+1.
