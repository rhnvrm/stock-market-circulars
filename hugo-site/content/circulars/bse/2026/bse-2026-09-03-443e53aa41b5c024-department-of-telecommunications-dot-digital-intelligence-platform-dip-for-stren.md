## Summary

The Exchange has issued a notice regarding communications from the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) concerning enhancements to the Digital Intelligence Platform (DIP), including a new Stakeholder Feedback facility and updated guidelines for utilizing the Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL) and Reactivation List by Investment Advisers and regulated entities.

## Key Points

- Introduction of a Stakeholder Feedback facility under the SancharSaathi Utilities section of the DIP for direct feedback and suggestions.
- Investment Advisers are advised to utilize the Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL) and Reactivation List updated daily on the DIP portal.
- Mandates proper verification of disconnected mobile numbers, adherence to the 90-day window, and execution of Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) before taking any action.
- Investment Advisers must complete onboarding on the DoT Digital Intelligence Platform to avail services.

## Regulatory Changes

- Integration of the Stakeholder Feedback facility under SancharSaathi Utilities on the DIP portal.
- Updated Quick Reference Guide for Banks, Financial Institutions, NBFCs, and SEBI-regulated entities regarding MNRL and Financial Fraud Risk Indicator (FRI) related actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must download and use the latest versions of MNRL and Reactivation List datasets on a daily basis.
- Verify mobile numbers against the latest Reactivation List before taking action; ensure date of reactivation >= date of disconnection.
- Restrict actions to numbers remaining in the effective MNRL pertaining to the last 90 days following appropriate EDD.
- Complete the onboarding process on the DoT Digital Intelligence Platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 03 Sep 2026
- Continuing from Exchange Notice No. 20260612-25 dated June 12, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational efficiency and data transparency for Investment Advisers in cyber fraud prevention and mobile number verification processes.
