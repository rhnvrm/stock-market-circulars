Trading members of the exchange are informed that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Manipal Health Enterprises Limited, 7,06,28,768 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 590/- per equity share as of July 28, 2026.

- Company Name: Manipal Health Enterprises Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investors Portion)
- Shares Subscribed: 7,06,28,768 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 590/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: July 28, 2026
