## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 9 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) have been approved for listing and admission to trade on the exchange starting from Monday, September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** September 07, 2026
- **Total Companies Involved:** 9 listed entities
- **Securities Category:** Equity shares issued under ESOP/ESOS
- **Lock-in Status:** Shares are listed with no lock-in (NA) across all mentioned issuances.

## Companies and Issuance Details

1. **Anthem Biosciences Limited:** 1,20,035 shares (ISIN: INE0CZ201020, Scrip Code: 544449)
2. **Cipla Limited:** 52,811 shares (ISIN: INE059A01026, Scrip Code: 500087)
3. **ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited:** 4,212 shares (ISIN: INE765G01017, Scrip Code: 540716)
4. **Kirloskar Ferrous Industries Limited:** 35,630 shares (ISIN: INE884B01025, Scrip Code: 500245)
5. **Max Estates Limited:** 51,525 shares (ISIN: INE03EI01018, Scrip Code: 544008)
6. **One 97 Communications Limited:** 4,74,714 shares (ISIN: INE982J01020, Scrip Code: 543396)
7. **Polycab India Limited:** 14,970 shares (ISIN: INE455K01017, Scrip Code: 542652)
8. **The Phoenix Mills Limited:** 14,139 shares (ISIN: INE211B01039, Scrip Code: 503100)
9. **WSFX Global Pay Limited:** 13,250 shares (ISIN: INE549D01012, Scrip Code: 511147)

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the new securities admitted for trading.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 04, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion resulting from employee stock option exercises. Marginal increase in total paid-up equity capital for the respective companies without any immediate trading restrictions or lock-in obligations.
