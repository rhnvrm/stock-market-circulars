## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the partial redemption and reduced face value of debentures issued by Credit Wise Capital Pvt Ltd, effective from September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Credit Wise Capital Pvt Ltd
- Security Details: CWCPL-11.25%-29-09-27-PVT (ISIN: INE0LN107030, Scrip Code: 977170)
- Corporate Action: Part Redemption of Debentures
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 5,000/- per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the reduced face value and ensure trades from the effective date reflect the updated terms.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026
- Notice Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors holding the affected debentures must account for the reduced face value in trading and valuation operations.
