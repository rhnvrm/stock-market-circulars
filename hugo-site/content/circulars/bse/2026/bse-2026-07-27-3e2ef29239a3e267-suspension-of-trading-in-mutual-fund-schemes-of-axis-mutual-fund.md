## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific mutual fund schemes of Axis Mutual Fund due to the record date fixed for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in Axis Fixed Term Plan - Series 113 plans effective from the specified record/ex-date.
- Affected schemes include various plans (Growth, Quarterly IDCW, Half Yearly IDCW) under Axis Fixed Term Plan - Series 113.
- Record Date and Ex-Date for the redemptions is 29/07/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no further trading is conducted in the specified ISINs after the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 27 Jul 2026
- Record Date: 29/07/2026
- Ex-Date: 29/07/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding units in Axis Fixed Term Plan - Series 113 schemes will have their trading suspended on the exchange in alignment with the redemption process.
