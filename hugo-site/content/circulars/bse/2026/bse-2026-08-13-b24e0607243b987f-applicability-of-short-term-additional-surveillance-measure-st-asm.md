## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities. The surveillance actions include applicable margin rates of 50% or 100% effective August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100% w.e.f. August 17, 2026, on all open positions as on August 14, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to maximum capped at 100% w.e.f. August 17, 2026, on all open positions as on August 14, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and enforcement of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework provisions for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements applicable to open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins: August 17, 2026 (on open positions as on August 14, 2026, and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities placed under ST-ASM Stage I and II may affect liquidity and trading volume for the affected scrips.
