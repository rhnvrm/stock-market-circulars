## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of new securities of Veranda Learning Solutions Limited (Scrip Code - 543514) effective October 1, 2026. A total of 1,55,763 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 311/- (total issue price of Rs. 321/-) to non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Veranda Learning Solutions Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 543514
- **ISIN:** INE0IQ001011
- **Issue Type:** Preferential issue upon warrant conversion
- **Number of Shares:** 1,55,763 equity shares
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10/- each at a premium of Rs. 311/- (Issue Price: Rs. 321/-)
- **Allottees:** Non-promoter
- **Distribution Nos.:** 96350848 to 96506610
- **Date of Allotment:** September 4, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Lock-in Details:** 1,55,763 shares locked-in up to April 14, 2027

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported in this notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that the newly listed shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares and are subject to lock-in restrictions until April 14, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** September 4, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** April 14, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion via warrant conversion for Veranda Learning Solutions Limited with negligible market-wide impact.
