## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) for multiple companies as they have fixed record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of specified companies.
- Covers multiple companies including Can Fin Homes Ltd., Hinduja Housing Finance Limited, Vertis Infrastructure Trust, and GIC Housing Finance Ltd.
- Record dates and ex-dates span across August 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable. Routine corporate action notice regarding commercial paper redemption and trading suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no trading activity occurs in the specified commercial papers on or after the respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Can Fin Homes Ltd. (ISIN: INE477A14EI0): Record Date - 22/08/2026, Ex Date - 21/08/2026
- Hinduja Housing Finance Limited (ISIN: INE401Y14064): Record Date - 25/08/2026, Ex Date - 25/08/2026
- Vertis Infrastructure Trust CP (ISIN: INE0KXY14063): Record Date - 26/08/2026, Ex Date - 25/08/2026
- Vertis Infrastructure Trust CP (ISIN: INE0KXY14071): Record Date - 27/08/2026, Ex Date - 27/08/2026
- GIC Housing Finance Ltd. (ISIN: INE289B14KB9): Record Date - 28/08/2026, Ex Date - 28/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Prevents further secondary market transactions in commercial papers approaching maturity and redemption, ensuring orderly settlement processes.
