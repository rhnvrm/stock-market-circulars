## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 22 scrips in the Equity segment, effective from August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 22 scrips affected by price band changes.
- Revised price bands range from 2% to 20% depending on the specific security.
- Effective date of implementation is August 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Price bands for specified scrips have been revised from their existing levels by the Surveillance department.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands while placing orders in the affected scrips to avoid order rejections or compliance issues.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 26 August 2026
- Effective Date: 27 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members dealing in the affected scrips will experience modified daily price fluctuation limits starting from the effective date.
