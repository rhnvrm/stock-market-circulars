## Summary

Effective from Thursday, October 8, 2026, the equity shares of Omara Ventures India Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE SME platform under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Omara Ventures India Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544976
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** OMARA
- **ISIN No.:** INE1IA801011
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 311/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-, Premium: Rs. 301/-)
- **Total Securities Listed:** 4,360,000 Equity Shares
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 1 to 4360000
- **Market Lot:** 400 Equity Shares
- **Date of Allotment:** October 6, 2026
- **Market Maker:** Rikhav Securities Limited
- **Registrar:** Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard SME listing procedures and standard trade-for-trade monitoring apply.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that trading will take place exclusively in dematerialized form in minimum market lots of 400 equity shares.
- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, October 8, 2026.
- Necessary corporate actions have been executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository’s records.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Listing and Trading Effective Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Trade-for-Trade Period:** October 8, 2026 onwards (for 10 trading days)

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors can commence trading in the equity shares of Omara Ventures India Limited starting October 8, 2026, noting the initial 10-day trade-for-trade settlement restriction.
