## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of VANS ELECTROENGINEERINGS LIMITED on the SME platform ('MT' Group) effective Wednesday, October 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544971
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: VANS
- ISIN: INE2SO401011
- Issue Price: Rs. 118/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-, Premium: Rs. 108/-)
- Total Securities: 10,880,000 Equity Shares fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 10880000)
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Segment: Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form pursuant to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in the specified minimum market lot of 1,200 shares.
- Necessary corporate actions for locking in shares have been executed in the depository's records as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 5, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading and liquidity for equity shares of VANS ELECTROENGINEERINGS LIMITED on the BSE SME exchange platform, subject to initial trade-for-trade surveillance restrictions.
