## Summary

BSE has released the settlement schedule and guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Purple Finance Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from July 1, 2026, to July 14, 2026, under settlement number 2026078.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Purple Finance Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement Number:** 2026078
- **Bid Start Date:** 01/07/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 14/07/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 20/07/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 21/07/2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Conduct of acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange as per revised guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL & NSDL).
- Members and custodians must use Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement number '2026078' in the depository system.
- Early pay-in securities will be earmarked/blocked in client accounts; members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should check the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Opening:** July 1, 2026
- **Bid Closing:** July 14, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** July 20, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** July 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Directly impacts shareholders, members, and custodians participating in the takeover acquisition window for Purple Finance Limited by setting clear operational deadlines and depository procedures.
