## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for September 14, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding facility is available through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.
- Bid collection start date: September 13, 2026, at 10:00 AM.
- Bid collection end date for direct investors: September 13, 2026, until 5:00 PM.
- Bid collection end date for members: September 14, 2026, until 8:00 AM.
- Auction date: September 14, 2026.
- Settlement date: September 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must submit their bids within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module of the iBBS platform.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start:** September 13, 2026 (10:00 AM)
- **Direct Investor Bid End:** September 13, 2026 (5:00 PM)
- **Member Bid End:** September 14, 2026 (8:00 AM)
- **Auction Date:** September 14, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates participation of members and direct investors in the State Government Securities (SDL) auction process through electronic bidding platforms.
