## Summary

BSE has issued the trading and settlement schedule for the cash segment covering both T+1 and T+0 settlement cycles for the period from October 1, 2026, to October 31, 2026. The circular outlines key dates for trading, depository data entry, confirmation by custodians, pay-in/pay-out, and auction schedules.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules for the entire month of October 2026 (01.10.2026 to 31.10.2026).
- Details timelines for both T+1 and T+0 settlement cycles in the cash segment.
- Includes specific schedules for depository 6A/7A data entry, member confirmations, custodian confirmations, and pay-in/pay-out dates.
- Outlines auction schedules and deadlines for submission of auction offers.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine operational schedule release with no new regulatory changes introduced.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must adhere to the specified timelines for data entry, confirmation, and pay-in/pay-out obligations for each settlement number.

## Important Dates

- **Period Start Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Period End Date:** October 31, 2026
- **First Settlement (T+1):** DR-727/26-27 (Trading Date: 01-10-2026, Pay-in/Pay-out: 05-10-2026)
- **First Settlement (T+0):** DR-424/26-27 (Trading Date: 01-10-2026, Pay-in/Pay-out: 01-10-2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational planning and settlement processing for market participants operating in the BSE cash segment during October 2026.
