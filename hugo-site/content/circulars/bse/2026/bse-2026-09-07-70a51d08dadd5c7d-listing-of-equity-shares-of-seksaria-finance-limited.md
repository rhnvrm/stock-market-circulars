## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, September 09, 2026, the equity shares of Seksaria Finance Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE in the XT Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Seksaria Finance Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544907
- **ISIN:** INE0IBX01013
- **Group:** XT
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 10/- each fully paid
- **Total Securities:** 1,00,80,000 equity shares of face value of Rs. 10/- each allotted pursuant to Scheme of Arrangement.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 1 - 10080000
- **Scrip ID on BOLT System:** FINANCE
- **Abbreviated Name on BOLT:** FINANCE

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission to dealings on the Exchange under the XT Group of Securities.
- Scrip placed in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 and SEBI/HO/MRD-TPD1/CIR/P/2023/55.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note that the security is part of the special pre-open session (SPOS) for IPO and other categories of scrips.
- Prior paid-up equity capital of 50,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each has been cancelled.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 07, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** September 09, 2026
- **Appointed Date:** April 01, 2021
- **Effective Date (Scheme):** April 01, 2024
- **Date of Allotment:** September 19, 2024

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of Ishwarshakti Holdings and Traders Limited (IHTL) receive equity shares in Seksaria Finance Limited in the ratio of 7:1 pursuant to the demerger of the sugar business.
- Trading will be restricted to the Trade-for-Trade segment for the initial 10 trading days.
