## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) issued a notice regarding the debt securities of Aditya Birla Capital Limited, updating the ISIN and paid-up value effective from August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 975871
- Old ISIN: INE674K08034 -> New ISIN: INE674K08091
- Old Paid-Up Value: Rs. 8,60,000/- Per Debenture -> New Paid-Up Value: Rs. 10,00,000/- Per Debenture
- Effective Date: Tuesday, August 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational update regarding security identifiers and terms.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records with the new ISIN and paid-up value details.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to back-office and trading system updates for the specific debt securities.
