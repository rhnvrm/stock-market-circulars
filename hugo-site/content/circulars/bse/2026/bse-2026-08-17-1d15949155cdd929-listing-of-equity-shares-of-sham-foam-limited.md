## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Sham Foam Limited effective from Tuesday, August 18, 2026. The scrip will be in the 'MT' Group of Securities and placed in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sham Foam Limited
- Scrip Code: 544869
- ISIN: INE0Z9N01013
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SHAMFOAM
- Market Lot: 1000 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 130/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 120/-)
- Total Securities: 11,490,750 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: August 14, 2026
- Market Maker: JSK Securities and Services Private Limited
- Registrar: Alankit Assignments Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip placed in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the minimum market lot of 1000 shares and ensure trades comply with demat requirements.
- Necessary corporate action for lock-in marking has been executed in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 14, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 18, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Sham Foam Limited equity shares on the BSE SME platform with standard trade-for-trade safeguards for initial trading sessions.
