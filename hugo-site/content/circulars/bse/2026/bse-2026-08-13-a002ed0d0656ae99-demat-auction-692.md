## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction regarding Rolling Settlement No. DR-692/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on August 13, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on August 13, 2026.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m. on August 13, 2026.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. on August 13, 2026.
- Pay-in of the auction will be held on Friday, August 14, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians are requested to check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Trading members should take utmost care while punching security codes and rates, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 13, 2026
- Pay-in Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members participating in the settlement should adhere to the specified auction timings and review their obligations to ensure timely settlement.
