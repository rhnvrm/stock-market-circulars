## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units of Edelweiss BSE Top 10 Bank ETF under Edelweiss Mutual Fund, effective from Wednesday, August 5, 2026. The scheme replicates or tracks the BSE Top 10 Bank Total Return Index.

## Key Points

- Name of Mutual Fund: Edelweiss Mutual Fund
- Name of Investment Manager: Edelweiss Asset Management Limited
- Scrip Code: 544849
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: EBANK10
- ISIN: INF754K01XH9
- Issue Price: Rs. 16.87 per unit
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00 per unit
- Date of Allotment: 31/07/2026
- Number of Units: 12,322,326 Units
- Group: B
- Market Lot: 1 Unit

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

As per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, units of the mutual fund will only be traded in dematerialized form. Trades will take place in unit market lots (i.e., one unit).

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: July 31, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with a new exchange-traded fund option tracking the BSE Top 10 Bank Total Return Index on the B Group list.
