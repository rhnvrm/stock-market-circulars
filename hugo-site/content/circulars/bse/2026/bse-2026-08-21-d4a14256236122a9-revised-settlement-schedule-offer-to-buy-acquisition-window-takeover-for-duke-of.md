## Summary

BSE has issued a revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) pertaining to Duke Offshore Limited, following previous notices.

## Key Points

- Takeover bidding window was conducted from 06/08/2026 to 19/08/2026 under Settlement No. 2026099.
- Revised Obligation Download date is set for 24/08/2026.
- Revised Settlement Date is scheduled for 25/08/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories.
- Members and custodians must use Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026099 in the depository system.
- Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: 06/08/2026
- Bid End Date: 19/08/2026
- Revised Obligation Download: 24/08/2026
- Revised Settlement Date: 25/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for market participants involved in the Duke Offshore Limited takeover offer.
