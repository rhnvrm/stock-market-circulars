## Summary

UAE Exchange & Finance Ltd. (Member No. 6674) has voluntarily chosen to close its business, resulting in the cessation of its operations in the Cash segment of the Exchange effective from July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: UAE Exchange & Finance Ltd.
- Member Code: 6674
- Segment affected: Cash Segment
- Effective Date: July 31, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members or clients requiring clarifications regarding this notice are advised to contact the Membership Operations department via email at membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- Effective Date of Cessation: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct impact on broader market liquidity, as this is a voluntary closure of a single trading member's operations in the cash segment.
