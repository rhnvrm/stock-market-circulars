## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that the equity shares of Kanohar Electricals Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in due course. The scrip code is 544911, symbol is KANOHAR, and ISIN is INE877D01025.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kanohar Electricals Limited
- Scrip Code: 544911
- Symbol: KANOHAR
- ISIN: INE877D01025
- Department: DCS-Listing

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the upcoming admission to dealings; specific trading dates will be notified separately.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 15, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced through a separate notice

Enables trading of Kanohar Electricals Limited equity shares on the BSE platform once the listing date is announced.
