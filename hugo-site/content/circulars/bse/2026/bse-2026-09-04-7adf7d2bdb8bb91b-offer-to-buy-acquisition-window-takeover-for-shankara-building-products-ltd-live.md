## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) of shares of Shankara Building Products Ltd. The offer period is scheduled from Monday, September 7, 2026, to Monday, September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Shankara Building Products Ltd
- Security Symbol: SHANKARA, Scrip Code: 540425, ISIN: INE274V01019
- Offer Size: Up to 63,04,825 Equity Shares at an Offer Price of Rs. 150.00 per share (Face Value Rs. 10.00)
- Opening Date: September 7, 2026; Closing Date: September 21, 2026
- Trading Members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form with updated UCC and PAN.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database. Members are required to use the iBBS and RTRMS platforms for bidding and modifications.

## Important Dates

- Opening Date: 07-Sep-2026
- Closing Date: 21-Sep-2026
- Give-up / Take-up Period: 07-Sep-2026 to 18-Sep-2026
- Last Day Give-up: 21-Sep-2026
- Last Day Take-up: 21-Sep-2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly participation and settlement of the takeover offer for shareholders of Shankara Building Products Ltd through designated exchange mechanisms.
