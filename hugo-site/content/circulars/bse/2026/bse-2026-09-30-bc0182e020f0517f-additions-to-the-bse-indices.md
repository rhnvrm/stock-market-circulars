## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of seven newly listed companies to its BSE IPO and BSE SME IPO indices following their listing on the exchange effective September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **BSE IPO Index Additions (Effective October 5, 2026):** Elevate Campuses Limited (544945), Swastika Infra Limited (544949), Armee Infotech Limited (544951), and Adroit Industries Limited (544950).
- **BSE SME IPO Index Additions (Effective October 1, 2026):** Unitec Fibres Limited (544948), SK Offset Limited (544947), and Liqvd Digital India Limited (544946).
- These corporate actions are included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None for market participants; informational notice regarding index constituents.

## Important Dates

- **October 1, 2026:** Effective date for BSE SME IPO index additions.
- **October 5, 2026:** Effective date for BSE IPO index additions.

## Impact Assessment

Routine index adjustment with minimal operational impact on general market participants.
