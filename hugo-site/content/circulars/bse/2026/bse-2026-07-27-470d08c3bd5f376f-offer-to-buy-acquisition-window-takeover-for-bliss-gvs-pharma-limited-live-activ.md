## Summary

The BSE has released the live activities schedule and operational instructions for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) of shares of Bliss GVS Pharma Limited, scheduled from July 28, 2026, to August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: BLISS GVS PHARMA LIMITED
- Security Symbol: BLISSGVS
- ISIN: INE416D01022
- Scrip Code: 506197
- Offer Size: Upto 2,77,26,848 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 299.00 per share
- Opening Date: Tuesday, 28th July 2026
- Closing Date: Monday, 10th August 2026

- Access platforms include iBBS, RTRMS, and Extranet as per standard exchange guidelines.
