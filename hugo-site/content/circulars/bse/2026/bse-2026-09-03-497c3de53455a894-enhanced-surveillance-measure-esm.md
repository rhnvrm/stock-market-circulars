## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the list of securities satisfying criteria for inclusion, moving to lower stages, or being excluded from the framework effective September 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 07, 2026.
- Certain securities will move to lower Stage I of the ESM framework w.e.f. September 04, 2026.
- Qualifying securities will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 04, 2026.
- The price band of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework, including margin requirements, segment shifts, and price band modifications.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the margin requirements, segment shifts, and price band restrictions for open and new positions in the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II / Shifting / Move-outs: September 04, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Requirements & Trade-for-Trade Shift: September 07, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions may impact liquidity and trading activity for the securities specified in the annexures.
