## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about SEBI Circular no. HO/47/11/11(3)2025-MRD-POD2/I/23843/2026 dated October 15, 2026, concerning the review of certain aspects of the Closing Auction Session, market timings, and settlement methodology for derivatives contracts.

## Key Points

- References SEBI circular dated October 15, 2026.
- Focuses on the closing auction session, market timings, and derivatives contract settlement.
- Annexure 1 encloses the detailed SEBI circular for member reference.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SEBI's reviewed framework for closing auction sessions, market timings, and settlement methodology for derivatives.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the guidelines set forth in the SEBI circular and ensure operational compliance.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 16 October 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: 15 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts market participants and trading members involved in derivatives trading and closing auction operations.
