## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the new ISIN number assigned to Spice Islands Industries Ltd following the sub-division of its equity shares from a face value of Rs. 10/- each to Rs. 2/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Spice Islands Industries Ltd
- Scrip Code: 526827
- New ISIN Number: INE882D01025
- Sub-Division Details: Equity shares split from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-
- Ex-Date: August 28, 2026 (DR- 704/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard corporate action update regarding identification numbers.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the new ISIN for settlement and trading purposes effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 24, 2026
- Ex-Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating trading and clearing systems with the new ISIN for the sub-divided shares effective from the ex-date.
