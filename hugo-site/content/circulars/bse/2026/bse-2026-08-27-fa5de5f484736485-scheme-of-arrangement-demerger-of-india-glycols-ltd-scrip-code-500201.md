## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Scheme of Arrangement (Demerger) of India Glycols Ltd (Scrip Code: 500201) pursuant to the NCLT Allahabad Bench order. The demerger involves the transfer of the Biopharma Undertaking into Ennature Bio Pharma Limited (EBL) and the Spirit and Biofuel Undertaking into IGL Spirits Limited. The record date has been set for September 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- Demerged Company: India Glycols Limited (Scrip Code: 500201)
- Resulting Company 1: Ennature Bio Pharma Limited (EBL)
- Resulting Company 2: IGL Spirits Limited (IGSL)
- Record Date: September 2, 2026
- Entitlement Ratio for Demerger 1 (Biopharma Undertaking): Ennature Bio Pharma Limited shall issue 1 (One) Equity Share of face value INR 5 each to shareholders of India Glycols Ltd holding 3 (Three) Equity Shares of face value INR 5 each.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the record date and the ex-entitlement date for the demerger of India Glycols Ltd to ensure correct processing of trades and corporate action entitlements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 27, 2026
- Record Date: September 2, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: September 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders of India Glycols Ltd as of the record date will be eligible for shares in the resulting companies according to the specified share swap ratios. Trading and settlement mechanisms will adjust accordingly on the ex-date.
