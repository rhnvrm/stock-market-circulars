## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Effective September 10, 2026, applicable margin rates are revised to 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) for Stage I, and 100% for Stage II, applicable to all open positions as of September 09, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ST-ASM Stage I will attract a margin rate of 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher (capped at 100%).
- Securities qualifying for ST-ASM Stage II will attract a margin rate of 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher (capped at 100%).
- Measures apply to all open positions as on September 09, 2026, and new positions created from September 10, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely a market surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and enforcement of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance frameworks on specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the revised margin requirements (50% / 100%) effective from September 10, 2026, across all affected open and new positions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 10, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off:** September 09, 2026
- **Notice Date:** September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips. Market participants must monitor positions to maintain adequate margins.
