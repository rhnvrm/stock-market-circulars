## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies, in accordance with the revised framework.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. October 12, 2026 on all open positions as on October 09, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Lower Price Band and movement to higher stages of LT-ASM framework effective October 08, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 12, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions, not an adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of LT-ASM stages as per Annexure attachments.
- Application of 100% margins and revised price bands.
- Transfer of specified securities to T / XT / MT / TS groups.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions for shortlisted securities.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 07, 2026
- Movement to higher stages / Lower Price Band / Moving out effective: October 08, 2026
- Open positions cutoff date: October 09, 2026
- 100% Margins / Group transfer effective: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in shortlisted securities will experience stricter margin requirements (100% margins) and price band restrictions, impacting liquidity and risk parameters for market participants holding positions in these scrips.
