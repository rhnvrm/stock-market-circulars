## Summary

The President of India, acting through the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India, is offering to sell up to 31,62,49,885 Equity Shares of Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) of face value Rs. 10 each (representing up to 2.50% of the total paid up equity share capital) with an option to additionally sell up to 50,59,99,816 Equity Shares (up to 4.00%), making a total of up to 82,22,49,701 equity shares (6.50%) under the Offer for Sale (OFS) mechanism. The floor price is set at Rs. 382/- per Equity Share.

## Key Points

- **Base Offer Size:** 31,62,49,885 Equity Shares (2.50% of paid-up capital).
- **Oversubscription Option:** Additional 50,59,99,816 Equity Shares (4.00% of paid-up capital).
- **Total Offer Shares:** Up to 82,22,49,701 Equity Shares (6.50% of paid-up capital).
- **Floor Price:** Rs. 382/- per Equity Share.
- **Employee Offer:** Up to 50,00,000 equity shares (0.04% of Offer Shares) offered to eligible employees of the Corporation.
- **Discount:** Retail and eligible employees are eligible for a discount of Rs. 10 per equity share on the cut-off price on T Day (August 04, 2026).
- **Designated Stock Exchange:** BSE Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI guidelines for Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares through Stock Exchange Mechanism and revised exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must adhere to the bidding guidelines for the OFS segment specified by BSE and NSE.
- Non-retail investors bid on T Day (August 04, 2026) and can carry forward un-allotted bids to T+1 Day.
- Retail investors and employees bid on T+1 Day (August 05, 2026).

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 03, 2026
- **T Day (Non-Retail Investors):** August 04, 2026
- **T+1 Day (Retail Investors, Employees, and carry forward Non-Retail):** August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High market significance due to the massive size of the divestment by the Government of India in the country's largest life insurance corporation.
