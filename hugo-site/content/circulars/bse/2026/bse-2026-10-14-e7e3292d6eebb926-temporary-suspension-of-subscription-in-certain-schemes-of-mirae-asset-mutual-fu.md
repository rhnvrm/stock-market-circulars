## Summary

BSE has announced the temporary suspension of fresh lump-sum investments, switch-in transactions, Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP-ins) under certain schemes of Mirae Asset Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, with immediate effect, following up on Notice No. 20261013-29.

## Key Points

- Temporary suspension applies to fresh and additional lump-sum purchases, switch-ins, SIPs, and STP-ins in specific Mirae Asset Mutual Fund schemes.
- Implemented with immediate effect on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- References previous Notice No. 20261013-29 dated October 13, 2026.
- Annexure I and Notice-cum-Addendum issued by Mirae Asset Mutual Fund provide detailed scheme lists.

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension of transaction processing for specific mutual fund schemes as communicated by Mirae Asset Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors must ensure no fresh subscriptions, SIP registrations, or switch-ins are processed for the affected Mirae Asset Mutual Fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026.
- Effective Date: Immediate (referencing October 13, 2026 context).

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members cannot execute fresh subscriptions, SIPs, or switch-ins in the affected Mirae Asset Mutual Fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF Platform until further notice.
