## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of several companies. The suspension is due to the fixation of record dates for the purpose of payment of interest or redemption of these securities.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures and bonds of companies as per the announced record and ex-dates.
- Affected companies include Bank of Maharashtra, SJVN Limited, REC Limited, Nido Home Finance Limited, Hinduja Leyland Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Ltd, and ECL Finance Limited.
- Trading is restricted based on the respective record dates ranging from September 11, 2026, to September 24, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure they do not deal in the specified debentures/bonds of the companies during the restricted period.

## Important Dates

- **Record Dates:** September 11, 2026, to September 24, 2026 (varying by security)
- **Ex-Dates:** September 11, 2026, to September 24, 2026 (varying by security)

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of secondary market transactions in the listed NCDs and bonds for the affected issuers to facilitate interest payment and redemption processing.
