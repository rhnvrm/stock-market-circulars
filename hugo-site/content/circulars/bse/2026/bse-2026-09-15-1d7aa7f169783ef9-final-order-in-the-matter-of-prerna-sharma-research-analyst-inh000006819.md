## Summary

SEBI, vide its final order dated September 10, 2026, has cancelled the certificate of registration of Ms. Prerna Sharma as a Research Analyst (SEBI Registration No. INH000006819) effective from September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260915-23
- Entity Affected: Ms. Prerna Sharma (Research Analyst)
- SEBI Registration Number: INH000006819
- Order Reference: QJA/BS/MIRSD/MIRSD-SEC-4/32714/2026-27
- Effective Date of Cancellation: September 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Cancellation of the research analyst certificate of registration by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- All research analysts and investors are requested to take note that Ms. Prerna Sharma is no longer a registered research analyst.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 10, 2026
- Circular Date: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The concerned individual is prohibited from undertaking research analyst activities. Market participants and investors must cease any professional engagement with the entity under this registration.
