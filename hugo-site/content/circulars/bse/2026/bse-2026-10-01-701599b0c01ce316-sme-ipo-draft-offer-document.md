## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the Draft Offer Document for the SME IPO of Technomed Devices India Limited, filed on September 30, 2026. The document is hosted on the BSE SME website for public review and comments for a period of at least 21 days pursuant to Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Issuer Company: Technomed Devices India Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Hosting Platform: BSE SME Website
- Public Comments Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None. The filing adheres to the disclosures specified under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments and feedback on the draft offer document must be submitted within 21 days from the date of the notice via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from notice date (approx. October 22, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates stakeholder review and transparency during the preliminary public offering phase for Technomed Devices India Limited on the SME platform.
