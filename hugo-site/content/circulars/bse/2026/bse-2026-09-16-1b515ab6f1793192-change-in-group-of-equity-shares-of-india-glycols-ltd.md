## Summary

Trading in the equity shares of India Glycols Ltd (Scrip Code: 500201) will be permitted under the 'A' Group and transferred from the 'T' Group with effect from September 17, 2026, following continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260827-46 dated August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: India Glycols Ltd
- Scrip Code: 500201
- Previous Group: 'T' Group
- New Group: 'A' Group
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The equity shares are being shifted from the 'T' Group (trade-for-trade segment) to the 'A' Group, indicating a change in trading segments and surveillance categorization.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to take note of the change and ensure compliance with the applicable trading norms for the 'A' Group.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 16, 2026
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shifting from the 'T' Group to the 'A' Group generally allows for normal rolling settlement and improved liquidity/trading participation for market participants in the scrip.
