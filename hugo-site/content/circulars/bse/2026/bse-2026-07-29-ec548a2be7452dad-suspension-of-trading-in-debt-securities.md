## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities of IL&FS Financial Services Limited due to the maturity of the securities on August 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: IL&FS Financial Services Limited
- Scrip Code: 954576
- ISIN: INE121H07BM1
- Particulars: ILFSL-8.90%-01-08-26- PVT
- Effective Date of Trading Restriction: July 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debentures with effect from July 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Maturity Date: August 1, 2026
- Trading Restriction Effective Date: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified debt securities of IL&FS Financial Services Limited is halted ahead of maturity to ensure smooth settlement.
