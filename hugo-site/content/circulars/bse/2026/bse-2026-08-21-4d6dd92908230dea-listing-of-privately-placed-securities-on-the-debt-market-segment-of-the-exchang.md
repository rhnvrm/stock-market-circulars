## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by multiple companies on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are issued on a private placement basis and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective August 21, 2026.
- Traded exclusively in dematerialized form in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuers include Bajaj Finance Limited, Dolat Algotech Limited, Avenue Supermarts Limited, Sharekhan Limited, and Can Fin Homes Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades comply with standard dematerialized trading norms and standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 21, 2026
- Allotment Date: August 20, 2026
- Various redemption dates ranging from November 2026 to February 2027.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational listing update with negligible direct impact on general market liquidity.
