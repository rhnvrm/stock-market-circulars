## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities moving into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework effective from August 04, 2026. These measures include trading restrictions, 100% margin requirements, trade-to-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) depending on the assigned GSM stage.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 04, 2026.
- Notice Reference: 20260803-27.
- Applies to securities listed in the attached Annexure moving into higher GSM stages (Stages I, II, III, and IV).
- Settlement for affected securities will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Stage I:** Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- **Stage II:** Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value to be deposited by buyers.
- **Stage III:** Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and ASD of 100% of trade value.
- **Stage IV:** Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week with no upward movement, and ASD of 100% of trade value.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in these securities, noting that settlements are strictly trade-to-trade without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 03, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity in the affected securities is expected to be substantially restricted due to mandatory trade-to-trade settlement, higher margin requirements, and Additional Surveillance Deposits.
