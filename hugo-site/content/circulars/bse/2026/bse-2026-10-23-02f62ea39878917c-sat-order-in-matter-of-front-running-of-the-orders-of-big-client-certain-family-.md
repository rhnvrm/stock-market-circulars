## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated August 10, 2026, in the matter of front running of orders of Big Client (certain family trusts) by certain entities (Appeal No. 007 of 2026). SEBI has confirmed that the specified appellants have paid the reduced penalty of ₹5 Lakhs each.

## Key Points

- SAT modified the impugned order, reducing the penalty to ₹5 Lakhs on each appellant and imposing a one-year debarment.
- SEBI confirmed via email dated October 23, 2026, that all listed appellants have paid the penalty amounts.
- Listed Appellants include Hansraj Randhir Shah HUF, Randhir Virji Shah HUF, Pinakin Hansraj Shah HUF, Punaiben Hansraj Shah, Raahul Hansraj Shah HUF, Dipesh Mehta HUF, and Piyush Mehta HUF.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SAT order modifying penalties and debarment terms for the named entities involved in front running.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the SAT order and SEBI confirmation regarding the payment of penalties by the appellants.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: August 10, 2026
- SEBI Confirmation Date: October 23, 2026
- Notice Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal as this is a compliance update regarding individual enforcement actions and penalty payments.
