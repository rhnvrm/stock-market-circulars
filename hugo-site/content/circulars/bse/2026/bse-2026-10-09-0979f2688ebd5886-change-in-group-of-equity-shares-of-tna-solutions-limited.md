## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of TNA Solutions Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group). This change becomes effective from Monday, October 26, 2026, following the previous notice dated October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** TNA Solutions Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544983
- **Segment Change:** Transferred from Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Shifting of scrip from MT Group (Trade for Trade) to M Group (Rolling segment), allowing normal rolling settlement for transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the segment change and ensure compliance with respective trading and settlement parameters.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 9, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members will experience normal rolling settlement mechanics for TNA Solutions Limited equity shares starting from the effective date, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility.
