## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability and revision of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities. The measures include 100% margins, lower price bands, and transfers to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS) based on the revised framework.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. August 26, 2026, on all open positions as on August 25, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Lower price bands and movement to higher stages of LT-ASM effective from August 24, 2026, for securities listed in Annexure I (Part B).
- Transfer of certain securities within the LT-ASM framework to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective from August 26, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- Movement of certain securities out of the LT-ASM framework effective from August 24, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the revised LT-ASM framework, including margin requirements, price band restrictions, and group migrations for short-listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure adherence to the enhanced margin requirements and price band restrictions applicable to the specified securities. Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is a market surveillance measure, not an adverse regulatory action.

## Important Dates

- August 24, 2026: Lower Price Band applicability, movement to higher stages of LT-ASM, and removal of certain securities from LT-ASM.
- August 25, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to new margin requirements.
- August 26, 2026: 100% margin applicability on open and new positions; transfer of specified securities to T / XT / MT / TS Groups.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and leverage for the affected securities will be restricted due to the imposition of 100% margins, lower price bands, and transfer to specialized trading groups.
