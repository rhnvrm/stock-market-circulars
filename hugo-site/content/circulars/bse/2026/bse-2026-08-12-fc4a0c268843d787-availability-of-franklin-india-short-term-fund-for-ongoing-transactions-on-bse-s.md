## Summary

BSE has informed market participants that Franklin India Short Term Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting August 13, 2026. This follows the previous BSE Notice No. 20260804-33 dated August 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Reopening**: Franklin India Short Term Fund will be available for subscriptions, redemptions, and SIP transactions.
- **Platform**: The transactions are supported on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **Schemes Included**:
  1. Franklin India Short Term Fund - Regular Plan - Debt Growth (ISIN: INF090I01YT5)
  2. Franklin India Short Term Fund - Regular Plan - Debt IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF090I01YV1)
  3. Franklin India Short Term Fund - Regular Plan - Debt IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF090I01YU3)
  4. Franklin India Short Term Fund - Direct Plan - Debt Growth (ISIN: INF090I01YW9)
  5. Franklin India Short Term Fund - Direct Plan - Debt IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF090I01YY5)
  6. Franklin India Short Term Fund - Direct Plan - Debt IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF090I01YX7)

## Regulatory Changes

There are no direct regulatory changes; this is an operational reopening of existing mutual fund schemes for trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Mutual fund distributors and trading members on the BSE StAR MF platform should update their systems to allow client orders/SIPs in the specified schemes from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **August 4, 2026**: Prior notice issued (Notice No. 20260804-33).
- **August 12, 2026**: Circular publication date.
- **August 13, 2026**: Effective date for reopening of schemes for transactions.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. Provides liquidity and transaction capability for investors in the specific Franklin India Mutual Fund schemes.
- **Operational Impact**: Medium for mutual fund distributors and platform users who need to process transactions for these schemes starting August 13, 2026.
