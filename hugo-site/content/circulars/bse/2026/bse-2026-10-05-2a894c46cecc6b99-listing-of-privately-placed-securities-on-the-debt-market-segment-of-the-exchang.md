## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis on the debt market segment effective October 05, 2026. The securities will trade in dematerialized form with standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs and a tick size of 1 paise.

## Key Points

- **Muthoot Capital Services Ltd.:** 400 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732701, ISIN: INE296G14636, Rate: 8.82% p.a., Maturity: 28/06/2027).
- **Bajaj Housing Finance Limited:** 5,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732702, ISIN: INE377Y14CK2, Rate: 6.47% p.a., Maturity: 14/12/2026).
- **TATA STEEL LIMITED:** 15,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732705, ISIN: INE081A14HP8, Rate: 6.37% p.a., Maturity: 30/12/2026).
- **SG FINSERVE LIMITED:** 600 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732706, ISIN: INE618R14059, Rate: 7.55% p.a., Maturity: 27/11/2026).
- **Navi Finserv Limited:** 1,040 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732703, ISIN: INE342T14HX8, Rate: 9.70% p.a.).

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of privately placed debt securities (Commercial Papers) to trading on the BSE Debt Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in dematerialized form in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** 01/10/2026
- **Effective Date:** 05/10/2026
- **Maturity Dates:** Ranging from November 2026 to June 2027 across various issuers.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates secondary market liquidity for debt instruments and commercial papers issued by leading financial institutions and corporations.
