## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the New Fund Offer (NFO) with SIP facility of Nippon India Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, notifying members of a revised allotment date for the NIPPON INDIA INCOME PLUS ARBITRAGE OMNI FUND OF FUND.

## Key Points

- The tentative allotment date for NIPPON INDIA INCOME PLUS ARBITRAGE OMNI FUND OF FUND - NFO has been revised from September 04, 2026, to September 03, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 02, 2026.
- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure clear funds are available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by the stipulated deadlines and adhere to the cancellation criteria for uninitiated transactions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 02, 2026
- Funds Availability Deadline: September 02, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to participants and investors transacting in the specified Nippon India Mutual Fund NFO scheme on the BSE StAR MF platform.
