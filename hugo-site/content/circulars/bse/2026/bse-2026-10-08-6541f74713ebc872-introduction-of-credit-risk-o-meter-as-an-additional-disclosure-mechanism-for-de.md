## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the introduction of the Credit Risk-o-Meter as an additional disclosure mechanism for debt securities, referencing SEBI Circular No. HO/17/11/22(1)2026-DDHS-POD1 dated October 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Introduction of Credit Risk-o-Meter for debt securities.
- Based on SEBI Circular No. HO/17/11/22(1)2026-DDHS-POD1 dated October 07, 2026.
- Trading members and relevant entities are requested to take note and ensure compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Credit Risk-o-Meter disclosures as mandated by SEBI for debt instruments to enhance risk visibility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and issuers must adhere to the new disclosure requirements regarding the Credit Risk-o-Meter as stipulated by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 08 Oct 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: 07 Oct 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances transparency and risk disclosure standards for investors holding debt securities by providing a standardized risk indicator.
