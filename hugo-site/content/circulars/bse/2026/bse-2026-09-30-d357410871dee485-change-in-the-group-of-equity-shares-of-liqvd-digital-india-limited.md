## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of LIQVD DIGITAL INDIA LIMITED (Scrip Code: 544946) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Thursday, October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: LIQVD DIGITAL INDIA LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544946
- Segment Shift: Transferred from Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026
- Reference: In continuation of Exchange’s Notice No. 20260929-58 dated September 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of securities from MT Group (Trade for Trade) to M Group (Rolling segment), enabling regular rolling settlement for dealings in the company's equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the segment change and ensure compliance with trading and settlement procedures applicable to the Rolling segment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances trading liquidity and operational efficiency for investors by moving the stock from Trade for Trade settlement to normal rolling settlement.
