## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Jaikisan Agrotech India Limited for its SME IPO. The document was filed on August 31, 2026, pursuant to Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations and has been made available on the stock exchange website to seek public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Jaikisan Agrotech India Limited
- Filing Date: August 31, 2026
- Notice Date: September 01, 2026
- Segment: SME
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice
- Submission Email for Comments: equity.ipo@bseindia.com
- Hosted URL: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; the filing is in accordance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Interested stakeholders and the public can review the draft offer document and submit comments within 21 days of the notice to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: August 31, 2026
- Notice Date: September 01, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: Within 21 days from September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites public scrutiny for the upcoming SME Initial Public Offering of Jaikisan Agrotech India Limited.
