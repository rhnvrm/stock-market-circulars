## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 along with SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 26, 2026, to September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 (Regular Plan - Growth Equity, ISIN: INF109K1A575; Direct Plan - Growth Equity, ISIN: INF109K1A583).
- NFO Period: August 26, 2026, to September 09, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 15, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 11, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (September 09, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 26, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 09, 2026
- Clear Funds Cut-off: September 11, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 NFO through the BSE StAR MF platform.
