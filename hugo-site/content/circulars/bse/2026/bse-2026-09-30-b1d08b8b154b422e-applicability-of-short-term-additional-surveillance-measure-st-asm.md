## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Applicable margin rates are revised effective October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II as per attached annexures.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable margin rate shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%.
- Effective date for surveillance actions: October 05, 2026, applicable on all open positions as on October 01, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% for Stage I, 100% for Stage II) on all applicable open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Open positions reference date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Higher margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact trading volumes and liquidity for the affected scrips.
