## Summary

BSE has released notice details regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled on October 23, 2026, pursuant to the RBI press release.

## Key Points

- **Securities Offered:** 
  - 6.53% GS 2031 (Re-issue, Issue Size: Rs. 19,000 Cr, Min Sub Units: 100, Min Amt: Rs. 10,000, Max Amt: Rs. 2 Cr)
  - 7.71% GS 2066 (Re-issue, Issue Size: Rs. 14,000 Cr, Min Sub Units: 100, Min Amt: Rs. 10,000, Max Amt: Rs. 2 Cr)
- **Bidding Platform:** Exchange's iBBS web-based system via the NCB-GSec module.
- **Bid Collection Start Date:** October 21, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards.
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor):** October 22, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** October 23, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- **Settlement Date:** October 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Conducted in continuation of Exchange circular no. 20180423-42 dated April 23, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module of the iBBS system and ensure timely pay-in through ICCL.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start:** October 21, 2026
- **Direct Investor Bid End:** October 22, 2026
- **Member Bid End / Auction Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity and participation schedules for market participants interested in Government of India dated securities auctions.
