## Summary

BSE has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts of Polycab India Ltd from the Equity Derivatives Segment in accordance with SEBI circular guidelines and eligibility criteria reviews.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Polycab India Ltd (Equity Scrip Code: 542652, Derivatives Asset Code: POLY).
- Discontinuation of new contracts effective January 01, 2027.
- Existing unexpired contracts will continue to trade until their respective expiry dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- In reference to SEBI Circular Ref. No: SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2024/116 dated August 30, 2024, and BSE Notice no 20240902-3 dated September 02, 2024 regarding eligibility criteria for entry/exit of stocks in the derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that no new expiry month contracts will be generated for Polycab India Ltd from January 01, 2027.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 13, 2026
- October 2026 Contract Expiry: October 29, 2026
- November 2026 Contract Expiry: November 26, 2026
- December 2026 Contract Expiry: December 31, 2026
- Effective Exclusion Date: January 01, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in F&O contracts for Polycab India Ltd will cease completely after the expiration of December 2026 contracts, impacting derivative traders holding positions in this security.
