## Summary

BSE has issued the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window - Takeover) of Ramgopal Polytex Limited, scheduled to take place from September 25, 2026, to October 9, 2026 under Settlement No. 2026116.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Ramgopal Polytex Limited
- **Settlement Type & Number:** Takeover, Settlement No. 2026116
- **Bidding Window:** September 25, 2026 to October 9, 2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Early Pay-in:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in depositories (CDSL/NSDL), where securities will be earmarked/blocked.
- **EPN File:** Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework amendments; operational procedure aligns with the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange (Notice No. 20200528-32).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must ensure early pay-in of securities is completed and reflected in BSE/BSECL systems before the OTB platform bidding and confirmation timelines close.
- Must use Market Type "(Takeover)" and settlement number 2026116 in the depository system.
- Client-wise stamp duty charges are to be verified from the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** September 25, 2026
- **Bid End Date:** October 9, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** October 22, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless execution and settlement of the open offer takeover for shareholders and market participants in Ramgopal Polytex Limited while ensuring timely blocking and transfer of tendered securities.
