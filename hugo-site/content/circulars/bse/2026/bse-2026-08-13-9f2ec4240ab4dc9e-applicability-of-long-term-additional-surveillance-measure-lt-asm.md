## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework on various securities. The measures include 100% margins on open and new positions, movement of securities to higher stages with lower price bands, transfers to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS), and removal of certain securities from the framework effective August 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable effective August 18, 2026, on open positions as of August 17, 2026, and new positions onwards.
- Certain securities moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework with lower price bands effective August 14, 2026.
- Selected securities to continue in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective August 18, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the framework provided in Annexure II, with price bands reinstated subject to other applicable measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework guidelines.
- Surveillance actions taken based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and market surveillance parameters.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure 100% margin maintenance for impacted securities.
- Trading members to take note of group transfers and lower price band applicability for client trades.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Lower Price Bands / Movement to Higher Stages / Moving out: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and price bands will restrict speculative trading and enhance risk containment for shortlisted securities. Liquidity may be temporarily impacted for securities subjected to higher surveillance stages or group transfers.
