## Summary

BSE has issued the trading and settlement schedule for the New Debt Segment (NDS) covering the period from October 01, 2026, to October 31, 2026, specifying settlement numbers, trading dates, entry dates, pay-in/pay-out dates, and auction schedules.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement numbers from NDS-124/26-27 to NDS-143/26-27.
- Details specific timelines for NDS 6A/7A entry, pay-in, pay-out, and auction processes.
- Pay-in is scheduled at 11:00 a.m. with member instructions to Depositories/banks latest by 10:50 a.m.
- Auction pay-in is scheduled at 9:30 a.m. with member instructions latest by 9:20 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational schedule issuance.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must adhere to the prescribed timings for submitting pay-in instructions to Depositories and banks for NDS settlements.

## Important Dates

- Programme Period: 01.10.2026 to 31.10.2026
- First Settlement (NDS-124/26-27): Trading on 01-10-2026, Pay-in/Pay-out on 05-10-2026
- Last Settlement (NDS-143/26-27): Trading on 30-10-2026, Pay-in/Pay-out on 02-11-2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement operations and clarity for market participants trading in the New Debt Segment during October 2026.
