## Summary

Effective from Monday, September 28, 2026, the equity shares of FX Multitech Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE SME segment under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544941
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: FXML
- ISIN: INE1D0M01019
- Issue Price: Rs. 116/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and Premium of Rs. 106/-)
- Total Securities: 1,43,59,600 Equity Shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 14,359,600
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 24, 2026
- Market Maker: Basan Equity Broking Limited
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form.
- Scrip placed in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to the minimum market lot size of 1,200 shares.
- Corporate actions for locking in shares have been executed in the depository records as confirmed by the company.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 24, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): September 28, 2026
- Commencement of Trading: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and liquidity for FX Multitech Limited on the BSE SME platform while maintaining standard trade-for-trade surveillance controls during initial trading sessions.
