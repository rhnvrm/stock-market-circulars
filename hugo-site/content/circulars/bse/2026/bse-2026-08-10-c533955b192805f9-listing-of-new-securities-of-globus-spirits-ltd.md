## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 23,80,952 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) pursuant to a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) by Globus Spirits Ltd are listed and admitted for trading effective Tuesday, August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Globus Spirits Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 533104
- **ISIN No.:** INE615I01010
- **Security Details:** 23,80,952 Equity shares of Rs.10/- each allotted to QIBs pursuant to Qualified Institutional Placement (ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares).
- **Date of Allotment:** August 06, 2026
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 840/-
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 29080342 to 31461293
- **Effective Date of Trading:** Tuesday, August 11, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with standard listing and depository guidelines for the newly issued securities.

- **Date of Allotment:** August 06, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

Expansion of the equity capital base of Globus Spirits Ltd through QIP allotment.
