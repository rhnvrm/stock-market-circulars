## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, July 29, 2026, the equity shares of Shree Balaji (Mala) Textiles Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544835
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: MALA
- ISIN No.: INE1N3Y01016
- Number of Securities: 9,905,000 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 70/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 60/-)
- Market Lot: 2,000 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: July 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on July 29, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the scrip will be subject to a Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.
- Lock-in period marked in depository records as per company notification.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 28, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Shree Balaji (Mala) Textiles Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with initial trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
