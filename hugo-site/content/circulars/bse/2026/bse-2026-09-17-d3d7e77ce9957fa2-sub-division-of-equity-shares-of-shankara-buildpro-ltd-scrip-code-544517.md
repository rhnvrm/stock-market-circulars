## Summary

Shankara Buildpro Ltd (Scrip Code: 544517) has announced the record date for the sub-division of its equity shares. The existing equity shares with a face value of Rs. 10/- each will be sub-divided into five equity shares with a face value of Rs. 2/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shankara Buildpro Ltd
- Scrip Code: 544517
- Old Face Value: Rs. 10/- each
- New Face Value: Rs. 2/- each (One equity share into Five equity shares)
- Record Date: 08/10/2026
- ISIN No. INE24OJ01011 (Rs. 10/- paid up) will cease to be valid for exchange transactions on or after 08/10/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note that transactions executed on or after the record date will not be valid under the old ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 17 September 2026
- Record Date: 08 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders holding shares as of the record date will receive five shares of Rs. 2/- for every single share of Rs. 10/- held, adjusting liquidity and market price accordingly.
