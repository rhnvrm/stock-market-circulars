## Summary

Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund is launching the Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund NFO along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from October 26, 2026, to November 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered include regular and direct plans with growth and IDCW options (reinvestment and payout).
- ISINs range from INF174KA1H55 to INF174KA1H30.
- Tentative allotment date is November 13, 2026.
- Switch transactions are accepted during the NFO period, subject to receipt of funds prior to the allotment date.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on November 09, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 12, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by 3:00 PM on November 09, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** October 26, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** November 09, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in/Clear Funds Deadline:** November 12, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** November 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscription and systematic investment plan (SIP) setup for market participants and investors in the Kotak Nifty Realty Index Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
