## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities of IEDCL due to their maturity as of August 28, 2026. Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures starting August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: IEDCL
- Scrip Code: 957969
- ISIN: INE938L08098
- Particulars: IEDCL-9.53%-28-8-26-PVT
- Effective Date of Trading Cessation: August 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the aforesaid debentures with effect from August 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Maturity Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational halt of trading for the matured debt instrument.
