## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Spice Islands Industries Ltd has fixed a record date for the sub-division of its equity shares. The face value of the shares will be sub-divided from Rs. 10/- each into Rs. 2/- each effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Spice Islands Industries Ltd
- Scrip Code: 526827
- Record Date: 28/08/2026
- Sub-division: Existing 1 Equity Share of Rs. 10/- into 5 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each.
- Effective Date: 28/08/2026

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- The old ISIN No. INE882D01017 (Rs. 10/- paid up) will not be valid for transactions done on the Exchange on or after 28/08/2026.
- The new ISIN Number for the Rs. 2/- paid-up shares will be notified separately.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders will receive 5 shares of Rs. 2/- for every 1 share of Rs. 10/- held as of the record date. Trading members must take note of the ISIN change for subsequent transactions.
