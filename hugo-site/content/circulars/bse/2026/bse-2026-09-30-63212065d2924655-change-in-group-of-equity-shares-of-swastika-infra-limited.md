## Summary

BSE has announced that the equity shares of Swastika Infra Limited will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment (B Group) with effect from October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Swastika Infra Limited
- Scrip Code: 544949
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026
- In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260929-59 dated September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Shift of securities trading mechanism from compulsory trade-for-trade settlement (where netting is not allowed and 100% margin/delivery is required) to normal rolling settlement under B Group.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment change and ensure client trades from October 15, 2026 onwards follow rolling settlement rules under B Group.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement and enhances liquidity and trading flexibility for the equity shares of Swastika Infra Limited.
