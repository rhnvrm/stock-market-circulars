## Summary

Parshav Vatika LLP, along with PACs (K8 Products LLP and Tidagela Ventures Private Limited), has issued a Letter of Offer to acquire up to 50,37,541 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹10/- each, representing 26% of the total voting share capital of Lykis Limited on a fully diluted basis, at a cash price of ₹34.50/- per share.

## Key Points

- **Acquirer:** Parshav Vatika LLP along with PACs (K8 Products LLP, Tidagela Ventures Private Limited).
- **Target Company:** Lykis Limited.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 50,37,541 Equity Shares (26% of total voting share capital on a fully diluted basis).
- **Offer Price:** ₹34.50/- per fully paid-up equity share in cash.
- **Regulatory Basis:** Pursuant to Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.
- **Conditions:** Not conditional upon any minimum level of acceptance; no differential pricing.

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is being made in compliance with SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public shareholders wishing to tender their shares should consult their stockbrokers or the Manager/Registrar to the Offer and follow the prescribed acquisition window mechanism.

## Important Dates

- **Last date for upward revision of Offer Price/Size:** Up to 1 working day before the commencement of the Tendering Period (i.e., Monday, February 09, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit opportunity for public shareholders of Lykis Limited through the open offer acquisition window.
