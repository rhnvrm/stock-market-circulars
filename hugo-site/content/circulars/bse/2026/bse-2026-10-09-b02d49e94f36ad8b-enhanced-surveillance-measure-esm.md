## Summary

BSE has announced the application and review of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for certain securities effective from October 12 and October 13, 2026. Securities under the ESM framework will attract a minimum 100% margin, shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment, and/or experience price band restrictions and periodic call auctions depending on their stage.

## Key Points

- Minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 13, 2026, on all open positions as on October 12, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for qualifying securities.
- Certain securities will move to lower stages (e.g., Stage I) or move out of the ESM framework w.e.f. October 12, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 13, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 12, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely for market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) directing specific securities into higher/lower stages, mandatory 100% margins, and Trade-for-Trade settlement segments.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement handling, and updated price bands for affected scrips effective October 12/13, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 09 Oct 2026
- Effective Date for Stage Changes / Price Bands / Moving Out: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin & Trade-for-Trade Settlement: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity for securities placed under or remaining within the ESM framework will be impacted due to 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
