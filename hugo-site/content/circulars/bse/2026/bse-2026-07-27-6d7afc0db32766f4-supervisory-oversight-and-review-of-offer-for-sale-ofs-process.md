## Summary

BSE has issued a notice advising trading members to review and strengthen their internal procedures and control frameworks governing Offer for Sale (OFS) transactions. The directive follows observations of shortcomings in the process relating to the placement of OFS orders, specifically for large-value and institutional orders.

## Key Points

- Shortcomings were noticed in the placement of OFS orders, particularly for large-value and institutional orders during market oversight reviews.
- Trading members are required to implement robust location and access controls, ensure authorized personnel handle orders, and maintain strict supervisory oversight.
- Comprehensive periodic reviews of OFS processes and controls are mandated to ensure risk management and operational resilience.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishment of strict oversight protocols for Offer for Sale (OFS) order placements.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Location and Access Controls:** Terminals used for OFS bidding must be subject to the same access controls, monitoring mechanisms, and risk management controls as standard capital market segment terminals.
- **Authorized Personnel:** OFS bids and orders on behalf of clients must be placed only by personnel authorized to operate capital market terminals whose details are registered with the Exchange.
- **Supervisory Oversight:** All institutional orders without upfront margin must be reviewed during the OFS bidding window/trading hours, with complete audit trails and records maintained for all OFS activities.
- **Periodic Review:** Trading members must periodically conduct comprehensive reviews of their OFS processes and controls.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 27 Jul 2026
- **Notice No.:** 20260727-11

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must immediately audit their existing terminal access controls, verify staff authorizations for OFS bidding, and institute real-time review mechanisms for institutional orders placed without upfront margins to ensure full regulatory compliance.
