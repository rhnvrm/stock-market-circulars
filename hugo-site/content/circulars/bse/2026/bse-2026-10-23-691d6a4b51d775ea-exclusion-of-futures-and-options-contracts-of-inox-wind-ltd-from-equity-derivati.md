## Summary

BSE has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts of Inox Wind Ltd from the Equity Derivatives segment, effective January 01, 2027, pursuant to SEBI circular guidelines on stock eligibility criteria.

## Key Points

- Futures and Options contracts for Inox Wind Ltd (Derivatives Asset Code: INOX, Equity Scrip Code: 539083) are discontinued from trading in the Equity Derivatives segment starting January 01, 2027.
- No new expiry month contracts will be generated for the security.
- Existing unexpired contracts for October 2026, November 2026, and December 2026 will continue to trade and will expire on their respective expiry dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI Circular Ref. No: SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2024/116 dated August 30, 2024, regarding the eligibility criteria of stocks in the derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that no new F&O contracts for Inox Wind Ltd will be available for trading with effect from January 01, 2027.

## Important Dates

- October 23, 2026: Notice issuance date.
- October 29, 2026: Expiry date for October 2026 contracts.
- November 26, 2026: Expiry date for November 2026 contracts.
- December 31, 2026: Expiry date for December 2026 contracts.
- January 01, 2027: Effective date of discontinuation and exclusion from trading in the Equity Derivatives segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding existing unexpired F&O contracts in Inox Wind Ltd can trade them until their respective expiry dates, after which no further derivative positions can be initiated or rolled over in this security.
