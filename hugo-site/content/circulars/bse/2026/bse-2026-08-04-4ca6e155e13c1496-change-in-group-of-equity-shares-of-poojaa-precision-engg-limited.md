## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Poojaa Precision Engg. Limited (Scrip Code: 544844) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Tuesday, August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Poojaa Precision Engg. Limited
- Scrip Code: 544844
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: August 18, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange’s Notice No. 20260803-22 dated August 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares are moving from the restrictive Trade for Trade segment to the regular Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the segment change for execution and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 4, 2026
- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Improves trading liquidity and operational flexibility for investors trading in the equity shares of Poojaa Precision Engg. Limited by shifting them to the Rolling segment.
