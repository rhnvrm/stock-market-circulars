## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the application of Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the criteria under the IBC surveillance framework are placed under the measure effective October 5, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities moving out of the IBC framework are listed in Annexure II, with price bands reinstated to their pre-shortlisted levels (barring other applicable surveillance measures).
- A consolidated list of all securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post-recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% are listed in Annexure IV.
- Shortlisting is purely for market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation and periodic review of additional surveillance measures for companies undergoing insolvency resolution under the IBC framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the updated lists of securities placed under or moving out of the IBC framework and ensure compliance with respective price bands and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 01 Oct 2026
- Effective Date: 05 Oct 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts trading operations, price bands, and surveillance monitoring for specific securities undergoing corporate insolvency resolution processes or emerging from them.
