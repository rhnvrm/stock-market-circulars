## Summary

Noble Polymers Ltd has announced the consolidation of its equity shares pursuant to notice no. 20261008-53 dated October 8, 2026. The company has fixed October 16, 2026, as the record date for consolidating its existing two equity shares of face value Rs. 5/- each into one equity share of face value Rs. 10/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Noble Polymers Ltd
- Scrip Code: 539200
- Record Date: 16/10/2026
- Corporate Action Details: Consolidation of existing two equity shares of Rs. 5/- each into one equity share of Rs. 10/- each.
- No Dealings From Date: 16/10/2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action processed under BSE listing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised not to deal in the equity shares of the company as per the scheduled details and to take note of the share consolidation.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 8, 2026
- Record Date: October 16, 2026
- No Dealings From: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Share consolidation alters the face value and total number of shares held by shareholders without changing their proportionate ownership stake in Noble Polymers Ltd.
