## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from August 18, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and ensure adequate precautions as settlement will be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure move to higher GSM stages effective August 18, 2026.
- Stage I: 100% applicable margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value by buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and 100% ASD to be deposited by buyers (Stage IV with no upward movement).
- Settlement will be executed on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened surveillance measures on specified securities pursuant to the provisions of the GSM Framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities and ensure compliance with margin requirements, ASD deposits, and trade-to-trade settlement rules.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience constrained liquidity and restricted trading frequency depending on their assigned GSM stage, alongside higher capital requirements for buyers.
