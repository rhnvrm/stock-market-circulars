## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, September 02, 2026, equity shares of Annu Projects Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'T' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Annu Projects Limited
- Scrip Code: 544893
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: ANNU
- ISIN No.: INE103001017
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- per share; Issue Price: Rs. 99/- per share (including premium of Rs. 89/-)
- No. of Securities: 6,54,92,670 Equity Shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 65492670
- Date of Allotment: August 31, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Registrar: KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing day.

## Compliance Requirements

- The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.
- Corporate action executed for lock-in period marking in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 31, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Annu Projects Limited equity shares on the exchange, subject to trade-for-trade monitoring rules for the initial 10 trading days.
