## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the schedule for client margin reporting and applicable penalty dates for the Equity Derivatives segment covering trading dates in August 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines trade dates, last dates of margin reporting, and penalty dates for August 2026.
- Applies specifically to members operating in the Equity Derivatives segment.
- Details daily reporting requirements throughout the month.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational schedule notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the specified margin reporting timelines to avoid penalties.

## Important Dates

- Schedule Effective: August 3, 2026 to August 31, 2026 (Trade Dates)
- Penalty dates run sequentially starting from August 11, 2026 through September 8, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; routine administrative schedule for clearing and settlement procedures.
