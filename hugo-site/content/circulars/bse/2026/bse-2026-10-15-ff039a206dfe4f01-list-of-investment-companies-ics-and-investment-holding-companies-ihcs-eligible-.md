## Summary

BSE has released the list of Investment Companies (ICs) and Investment Holding Companies (IHCs) eligible for Special Call-Auctions, pursuant to SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-3/P/CIR/2024/86 dated June 20, 2024. The special call auction is scheduled to be held on Friday, October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice issued by BSE Listing Compliance-Monitoring department.
- Lists eligible Investment Companies (ICs) and Investment Holding Companies (IHCs) for Special Call-Auctions provided in Annexure 1.
- Special call auction scheduled for October 30, 2026.
- If the call auction for a security is not successful on Day 1, it will continue on subsequent days until price discovery is achieved.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-3/P/CIR/2024/86 dated June 20, 2024 regarding special call auctions for ICs and IHCs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and listed entities must note the eligible securities and participate/facilitate trading in the special call auction scheduled on October 30, 2026.
- Monitor continuation days if price discovery is not successful on the first day.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 15, 2026
- **Special Call Auction Date:** October 30, 2026 (continuing on subsequent days if price is not discovered on Day 1)

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates price discovery for eligible Investment Companies and Investment Holding Companies through special call auctions, impacting liquidity and trading mechanisms for these scrips.
