## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the extension of the New Fund Offer (NFO) period for the NAVI Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform up to September 11, 2026. The circular outlines updated instructions for transactions, funds pay-out, tentative allotment dates, and reopen dates.

## Key Points

- The NFO period for NAVI NIFTY REITS & REALTY INDEX FUND has been extended from September 10, 2026, to September 11, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF platform.
- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Tentative allotment date is set for September 15, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 15, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on September 11, 2026.
- The revised reopen date is September 18, 2026.

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure client funds are initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day for timely processing.
- Clear funds must reach the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 15, 2026, for allotment eligibility.
- SIP/XSIP registrations require client authentication by 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Extension Closure Date:** September 11, 2026 (up to 6:00 PM for Lumpsum)
- **SIP/XSIP Authentication Deadline:** September 11, 2026, by 3:00 PM
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 15, 2026
- **Funds Pay-out / Clear Funds Deadline:** September 15, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- **Revised Reopen Date:** September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides extended investment window and clear operational instructions for members and investors participating in the NAVI Mutual Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
