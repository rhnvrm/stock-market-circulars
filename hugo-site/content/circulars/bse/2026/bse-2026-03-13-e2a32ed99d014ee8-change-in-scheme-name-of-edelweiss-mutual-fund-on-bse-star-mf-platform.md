## Summary

Edelweiss Asset Management Limited has issued a notice cum addendum regarding changes to the scheme name and fundamental attributes of the Edelweiss Income Plus Arbitrage Active Fund of Funds, effective March 16, 2026. These updates comply with Regulation 18(15A) of the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996.

## Key Points

- Notice issued by Edelweiss Asset Management Limited and Edelweiss Trusteeship Company Limited.
- Affects unitholders of Edelweiss Income Plus Arbitrage Active Fund of Funds.
- Changes involve fundamental attributes and scheme features as per SEBI regulations.
- Effective date for the proposed changes is March 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Changes classified as Fundamental Attribute Change ('FAC') under Regulation 18(15A) of the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996.
- Requires implementation of the formal process for changing scheme fundamental attributes.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMC and Trustee company must follow prescribed SEBI procedures for fundamental attribute changes, including communicating options to unitholders.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: March 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Unitholders are provided with details on proposed feature modifications and options available pursuant to fundamental attribute changes.
