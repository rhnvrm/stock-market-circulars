## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for new securities of Capital Infra Trust (Scrip Code - 544338) effective from September 29, 2026. A total of 1,66,11,200 units have been allotted to the sponsor for cash consideration on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Capital Infra Trust
- **Scrip Code:** 544338
- **ISIN:** INE0Z8Z23013
- **Issue Size:** 1,66,11,200 Units
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 75.25/-
- **Date of Allotment:** 24/09/2026
- **Effective Date:** 29/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the listing and lock-in details of the newly issued units.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 24, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Lock-in Period Up to (Tranche 1):** October 7, 2029 (24,91,680 units, Dist. Nos. 491551734 - 494043413)
- **Lock-in Period Up to (Tranche 2):** October 7, 2027 (1,41,19,520 units, Dist. Nos. 494043414 - 508162933)

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing of preferential units for the trust, expanding the paid-up unit capital with standard lock-in stipulations.
