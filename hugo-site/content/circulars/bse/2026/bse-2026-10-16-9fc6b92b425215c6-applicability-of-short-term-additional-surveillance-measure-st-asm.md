## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities in ST-ASM Stage I attract a 50% margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Securities in ST-ASM Stage II attract a 100% margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Measures apply to all open positions as on October 19, 2026 and new positions created from October 21, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM framework Stage I and Stage II surveillance actions in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I, 100% for Stage II) on open positions as of October 19, 2026, and new positions created from October 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips starting October 21, 2026.
