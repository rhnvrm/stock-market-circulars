## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the temporary suspension of lumpsum investments, switch-in transactions, fresh Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP-In) registrations under certain schemes of HSBC Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes include HSBC Asia Pacific (Ex Japan) Dividend Yield Fund, HSBC Brazil Fund, and HSBC Global Emerging Markets Fund.
- Lumpsum investments (fresh and additional), switch-ins, and new SIP/STP-In registrations are disabled on the BSE StAR MF Platform from August 26, 2026.
- Applications received after the cut-off time on August 25, 2026, will not be accepted or processed.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIPs and STPs) prior to the effective date will continue to be processed normally.
- Redemptions, Switch-Outs, STP-Outs, and SWP transactions remain fully permitted and operational.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the suspension of fresh subscriptions and ensure no new orders for lumpsum or fresh systematic registrations in the affected HSBC schemes are entered on the BSE StAR MF platform after the cut-off.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Cut-off for prior acceptance: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding or seeking fresh exposure to the affected HSBC schemes will need to halt lumpsum and new systematic investments via the BSE StAR MF platform, while existing systematic plans and exit options remain unaffected.
