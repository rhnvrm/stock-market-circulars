## Summary

BSE has informed members that pursuant to Notice No. 20260714-21 dated July 14, 2026, the Abakkus Large & Mid Cap Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform with effect from August 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Abakkus Large & Mid Cap Fund
- **Effective Date:** August 06, 2026
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform
- **Available Facilities:** Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP

### Scheme Details

1. **ABAKKUS LARGE & MID CAP FUND - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH**
   - **Category:** Equity
   - **ISIN:** INF2JJD01201

2. **ABAKKUS LARGE & MID CAP FUND - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH**
   - **Category:** Equity
   - **ISIN:** INF2JJD01193

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can facilitate transactions in the aforementioned schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume regular transactions, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs in the regular and direct growth plans of Abakkus Large & Mid Cap Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
