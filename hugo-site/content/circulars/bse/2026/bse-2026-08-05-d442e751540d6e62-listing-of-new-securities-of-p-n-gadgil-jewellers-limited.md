## Summary

Trading members are informed that 1,14,94,252 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted to QIBs pursuant to a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) by P N Gadgil Jewellers Limited are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from August 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: P N Gadgil Jewellers Limited
- Scrip Code: 544256
- ISIN No.: INE953R01016
- Security Details: 1,14,94,252 Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted to QIBs pursuant to QIP.
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- Date of Allotment: August 4, 2026
- Issue Price: Rs. 609/-
- Distinctive Numbers: 135708334 To 147202585

None
