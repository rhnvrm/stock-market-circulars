## Summary

Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund is offering the Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund NFO, along with an SIP facility, on the BSE StAR MF Platform from August 03, 2026, to August 17, 2026. The tentative allotment date is August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- NFO Period: August 03, 2026, to August 17, 2026.
- Schemes offered include Regular and Direct plans for Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment.
- Tentative allotment date: August 21, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on August 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on August 20, 2026, for allotment eligibility.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: August 03, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: August 17, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: August 20, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to subscribe to the new index fund scheme and set up SIPs through the BSE StAR MF platform.
