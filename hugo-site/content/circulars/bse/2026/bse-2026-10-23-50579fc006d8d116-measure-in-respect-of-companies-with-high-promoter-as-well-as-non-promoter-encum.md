## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding measures for companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segment.
- Securities in Annexure II are eligible to move out from the framework effective October 26, 2026.
- Annexure III provides a consolidated list of securities under the framework.
- Measures apply in conjunction with other prevailing exchange measures and are subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of a minimum 75% margin on specified securities with high encumbrance levels under SEBI (SAST) Regulation 28(3).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure minimum 75% margin is maintained on all open positions and new positions created in affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for margin applicability: October 28, 2026 (on open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions).
- Exit Date for eligible securities moving out: October 26, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for specified encumbered securities may affect liquidity and trading positioning in the equity and derivatives segments.
