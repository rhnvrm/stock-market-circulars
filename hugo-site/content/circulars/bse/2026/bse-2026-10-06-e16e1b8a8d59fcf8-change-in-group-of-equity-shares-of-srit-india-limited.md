## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of SRIT India Limited will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment (B Group).

## Key Points

- Company Name: SRIT India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544965
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: Wednesday, October 21, 2026
- Reference Notice: Notice No. 20261006-4 (in continuation of Notice No. 20261005-42 dated October 05, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

The equity shares are moving out of the restrictive Trade for Trade segment into the standard Rolling segment, allowing normal rolling settlement for dealings.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems and take note of the change in segment and group from October 21, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This shift enhances trading flexibility and liquidity for shareholders of SRIT India Limited as dealings move to the B Group rolling segment.
