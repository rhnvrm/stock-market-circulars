## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of an offer to buy for the voluntary delisting of equity shares of Elpro International Limited. The acquirers (I G E (India) Private Limited and Zenox Technology Services Private Limited) along with PACs are offering to acquire 4,23,70,160 equity shares (25.00% of the paid-up equity share capital) held by public shareholders at a fixed delisting price of Rs. 181.80 per share.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Elpro International Limited
- **Acquirers:** I G E (India) Private Limited and Zenox Technology Services Private Limited
- **Persons Acting in Concert (PACs):** Mr. Surbhit Dabriwala and Mrs. Yamini Dabriwala
- **Offer Size:** 4,23,70,160 Equity Shares representing 25.00% of the paid-up equity share capital
- **Fixed Delisting Price:** Rs. 181.80/- per equity share aggregating up to Rs. 770.28 Crs
- **Tender Period:** From Tuesday, August 4, 2026, to Monday, August 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021, and SEBI circulars on mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to Tender-Offers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must participate in accordance with the prescribed acquisition window guidelines and schedule during the tender period.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** August 4, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Results in the complete delisting and exit opportunity for public shareholders of Elpro International Limited at the fixed price via the stock exchange acquisition window.
