BSE sought clarification from SpiceJet Ltd (Scrip Code: 500285) regarding various news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels (referencing NDTV Profit) to ensure accuracy and inform investors. The reply received from the company is attached to the notice.

- Company reply is made available via attachment to the notice.
