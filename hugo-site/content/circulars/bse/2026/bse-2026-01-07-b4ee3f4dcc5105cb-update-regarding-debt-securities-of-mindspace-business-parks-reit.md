## Summary

Mindspace Business Parks REIT issued a clarification notice to the stock exchange regarding inadvertent mathematical and typographical errors present in the disclosures forming part of its Postal Ballot Notice dated November 28, 2025.

## Key Points

- Clarification pertains to figures disclosed in the Postal Ballot Notice regarding the preferential issue of units.
- Correction of the number of new units to be issued under the preferential allotment from 3,91,78,713 to 3,08,64,750.
- Correction of post-preferential unitholding totals from 64,83,62,617 to 64,83,62,347 units.
- Confirmed that these rectifications are purely technical and do not impact the resolutions already approved by unitholders.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes; purely an issuer disclosure correction.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers must ensure accurate disclosures in postal ballot notices and timely clarifications if discrepancies are identified.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: January 7, 2026
- Original Postal Ballot Notice Date: November 28, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Neutral market impact as the updates are minor arithmetic corrections and do not alter the underlying business acquisitions or unitholder approvals.
