## Summary

Parswam Fashion Limited has submitted its Draft Offer Document for an SME IPO on September 29, 2026. The document is hosted on the BSE SME website for public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Parswam Fashion Limited
- Filing Date: September 29, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comments Window: 21 days from the date of the notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations for SME IPO draft offer document disclosure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stakeholders and public can review the draft offer document and submit comments within 21 days via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from notice date

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates transparency and public feedback for proposed SME initial public offerings on the BSE SME platform.
