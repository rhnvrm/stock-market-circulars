## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities (PTCs) of specific companies due to the attainment of their maturity dates.

## Key Points

- Trading members are informed that debt securities of Agri Xchng (Scrip Code: 977062, ISIN: INE2G1D15012) and Sapphire (Scrip Code: 977082, ISIN: INE2GPK15010) have matured.
- Trading in these debt securities (PTCs) is suspended effective from August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debt securities (PTCs) of the companies from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Effective Date of Suspension:** August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact restricted to holders and traders of the specified matured debt securities.
