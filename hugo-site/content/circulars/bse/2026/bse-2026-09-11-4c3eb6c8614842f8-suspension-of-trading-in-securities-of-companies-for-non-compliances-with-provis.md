## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in the securities of Polo Hotels Ltd effective October 12, 2026, due to persistent non-compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and SEBI (Depositories & Participants) Regulations, 2018. Additionally, the entire shareholding of the promoters in the non-compliant entity and other securities held in their demat accounts will remain frozen during the suspension period.

## Key Points

- Company Affected: Polo Hotels Ltd (Scrip Code: 526687).
- Effective Date of Suspension: October 12, 2026.
- Reasons for Non-Compliance: Failure to submit shareholding patterns (Regulation 31) and financial results (Regulation 33) for two consecutive quarters (June 2025 and September 2025), failure to submit reconciliation of share capital audit reports (Regulation 76), and failure to submit annual reports (Regulation 34) for financial years ended March 2024 and March 2025.
- Promoter Demat Freeze: The entire shareholding of the promoter in the company, along with all other securities held in their demat accounts, shall remain frozen during the suspension period.

## Regulatory Changes

Actions taken pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 and standard operating procedures for suspension and revocation of trading of specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must adhere to periodic filings including shareholding patterns, financial results, annual reports, and reconciliation of share capital audit reports within prescribed timelines to avoid penal actions and trading suspensions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 11, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the securities of Polo Hotels Ltd will be halted on the exchange, severely impacting liquidity and investor ability to trade shares. Promoters face a complete freeze on their demat holdings.
