## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 12,50,000 new equity shares of face value Re. 1/- each issued to promoters on a preferential basis by TD Power Systems Ltd. (Scrip Code: 533553, ISIN: INE419M01035) with effect from October 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: TD Power Systems Ltd. (Scrip Code: 533553, ISIN: INE419M01035)
- Securities: 12,50,000 equity shares of face value Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 599/- (Issue Price: Rs. 600.00/- per share).
- Allottees: Promoters (preferential basis).
- Effective Date of Trading: October 7, 2026.
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Distribution Nos.: 312456861 to 313706860.
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to April 9, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure adherence to the specified lock-in period and trading permissions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 20, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing/Trading: October 7, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 9, 2028

## Impact Assessment

The preferential allotment increases the paid-up capital of TD Power Systems Ltd. by 12,50,000 shares issued to promoters, enhancing promoter holding with statutory lock-in restrictions until April 2028.
