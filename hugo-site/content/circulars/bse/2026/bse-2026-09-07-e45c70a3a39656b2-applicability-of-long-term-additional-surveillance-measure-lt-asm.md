## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and transfers to specific groups (T / XT / MT / TS) effective September 8 and September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. September 10, 2026, on all open positions as of September 09, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands applicable w.e.f. September 08, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Securities transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. September 10, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- List of securities moving out of LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. September 08, 2026 is provided in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margin requirements on short-listed securities under the LT-ASM framework.
- Revision of price bands and transfer of securities to special trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure adequate margin maintenance (100%) for open and new positions in affected securities.
- Compliance with revised price bands and group categorization.

## Important Dates

- **September 08, 2026**: Effective date for higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price bands, and removal of securities from LT-ASM.
- **September 10, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and transfer of securities to T / XT / MT / TS Groups.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased capital requirements for traders and investors holding or creating positions in securities placed under LT-ASM stages due to 100% margin mandates.
- Potential reduction in liquidity and increased volatility management for short-listed scrips.
