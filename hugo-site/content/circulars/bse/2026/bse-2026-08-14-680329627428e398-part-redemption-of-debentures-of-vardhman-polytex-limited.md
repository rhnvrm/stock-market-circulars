## Summary

In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260804-16 dated August 4, 2026, regarding the Record Date for Part Redemption of Debentures for Vardhman Polytex Limited (Scrip Code: 977714, ISIN: INE835A07018), the company has informed the Exchange that the part redemption will not take place. Consequently, the face value of the NCDs is restated to Rs. 1,00,000/- per debenture.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vardhman Polytex Limited
- Scrip Code: 977714
- ISIN: INE835A07018
- Instrument: VPL-18%-31-3-31-PVT (NCD)
- Revised Face Value: Rs. 1,00,000/- per debenture (cancellation of part redemption)
- Effective Date: August 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a corporate action update regarding debenture redemption cancellation.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note that the debentures of the company will be traded with the revised face value of Rs. 1,00,000/- with effect from August 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date for Revised Face Value: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors holding the NCDs of Vardhman Polytex Limited need to update their records to reflect the restated face value of Rs. 1,00,000/- per debenture as the planned part redemption has been cancelled.
