## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of newly listed companies, Manipal Health Enterprises Limited and H. R. Hygiene Products Limited, to the BSE Indices effective August 6 and August 7, 2026, respectively.

## Key Points

- **Manipal Health Enterprises Limited** (Ticker: 544847) added to the **BSE IPO** index effective at the open of Friday, August 07, 2026.
- **H. R. Hygiene Products Limited** (Ticker: 544848) added to the **BSE SME IPO** index effective at the open of Thursday, August 06, 2026.
- Actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for market participants; informational notice regarding index constituents.

## Important Dates

- August 06, 2026: H. R. Hygiene Products Limited added to BSE SME IPO index.
- August 07, 2026: Manipal Health Enterprises Limited added to BSE IPO index.

Minor informational update for index trackers and benchmark portfolios holding the newly added securities.
