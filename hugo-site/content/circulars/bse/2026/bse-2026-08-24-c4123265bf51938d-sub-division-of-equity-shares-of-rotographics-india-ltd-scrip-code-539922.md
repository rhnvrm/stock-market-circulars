## Summary

Rotographics (India) Ltd has fixed August 28, 2026, as the Record Date for the sub-division of its equity shares from the face value of Rs. 10/- each into five equity shares of Rs. 2/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Rotographics (India) Ltd
- Scrip Code: 539922
- Record Date: 28/08/2026
- Sub-division Details: Existing equity shares of Rs. 10/- each subdivided into 5 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each.
- Old ISIN (INE364S01014) will be invalid for transactions on or after 28/08/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the change in face value and the invalidation of the old ISIN from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for trading members and shareholders reflecting the change in face value and share count.
