## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of three new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS effective from Friday, September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code 805262: 91-day T-Bill maturing on 03/12/2026 (ISIN: IN002026X222)
- Scrip Code 805263: 182-day T-Bill maturing on 04/03/2027 (ISIN: IN002026Y220)
- Scrip Code 805264: 364-day T-Bill maturing on 02/09/2027 (ISIN: IN002026Z227)
- Market lot for all listed T-Bills is 1.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that the securities shall not be available for trading two (2) working days prior to the maturity/redemption date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 4, 2026
- Maturity Dates: December 3, 2026 (91-day), March 4, 2027 (182-day), and September 2, 2027 (364-day)

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt market listing providing additional short-term government securities investment instruments for trading members and investors.
