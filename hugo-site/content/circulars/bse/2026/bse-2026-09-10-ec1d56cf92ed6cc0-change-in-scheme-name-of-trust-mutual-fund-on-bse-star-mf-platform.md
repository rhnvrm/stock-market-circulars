## Summary

TRUST Mutual Fund has updated the scheme name for one of its offerings on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect. The existing scheme name 'TRUSTMF SHORT DURATION FUND' has been revised to 'TRUSTMF SHORT TERM FUND'.

## Key Points

- Fund House: TRUST Mutual Fund
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform
- Existing Scheme Name: TRUSTMF SHORT DURATION FUND
- Revised Scheme Name: TRUSTMF SHORT Term Fund
- Effective Date: Immediate

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme name for future transactions and references on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate (10 Sep 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating the scheme nomenclature in trading systems and records.
