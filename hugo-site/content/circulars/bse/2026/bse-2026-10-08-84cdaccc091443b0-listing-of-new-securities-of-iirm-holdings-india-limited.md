## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of IIRM Holdings India Limited (Scrip Code: 526530) effective from October 9, 2026, following a preferential allotment to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- Total new securities listed: 15,59,883 equity shares of face value Rs. 5/- each.
- Issue price: Rs. 143.28/- per share (including a premium of Rs. 138.28/-).
- Allottees: Non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- Date of Allotment: September 11, 2026.
- Effective date for trading: October 9, 2026.
- ISIN: INE670C01026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice for preferential shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and depository participants must note the specified lock-in periods for the newly allotted shares:
- 62,814 shares (Distinctive Nos. 68144251 to 68207064) locked-in up to April 15, 2027.
- 14,97,069 shares (Distinctive Nos. 68207065 to 69704133) locked-in up to April 14, 2027.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 11, 2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: October 9, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: April 15, 2027 and April 14, 2027

## Impact Assessment

The listing expands the paid-up equity capital of IIRM Holdings India Limited. The newly issued shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares, subject to applicable lock-in restrictions.
