## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, October 6, 2026, the equity shares of Shivchem Agro Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will operate in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Shivchem Agro Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544967
- **Scrip ID:** SHIVCHEM
- **ISIN:** INE18CU01023
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 62/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/-, Premium Rs. 57/-)
- **Number of Securities:** 7,532,873 Equity Shares
- **Market Lot:** 2000 equity shares
- **Date of Allotment:** October 1, 2026
- **Market Maker:** Nikunj Stock Brokers Limited
- **Registrar:** Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing admission in accordance with SEBI guidelines and exchange regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded strictly in dematerialised form.
- Minimum market lot requirement of 2,000 equity shares.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on listing day.
- Lock-in period marked in depository records as per regulatory requirements.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 1, 2026
- **Listing and Trading Commencement Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Trade-for-Trade Segment Duration:** 10 trading days from listing

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and public market access to shareholders of Shivchem Agro Limited while subjecting trading to initial trade-for-trade surveillance safeguards for 10 trading days.
