## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers on the debt market segment of the Exchange effective August 25, 2026. The securities belong to various issuers including Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited, Adani Enterprises Ltd., Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited, and Navi Finserv Limited.

## Key Points

- Securities are issued on a private placement basis and traded in dematerialised form.
- Standard trading denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Covers multiple issuances with varying maturities, interest rates ranging from 6.83% to 10.80% p.a., and top-tier credit ratings (e.g., CRISIL A1+, CARE A1+, ICRA A1+).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard demat form and market lot specifications for debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Allotment / Effective Dates: August 24–25, 2026
- Redemption Dates: August 31, 2026 to August 19, 2027 depending on the specific commercial paper tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Standard routine listing activity with no broad market or operational impact.
