## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) of several companies due to fixed record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies on or after the respective ex-dates and record dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261001-72 (Date: 01 Oct 2026)
- Segment: Debt Segment | Department: Listing Operations
- Covers commercial paper redemptions scheduled between October 7, 2026, and October 20, 2026.
- Affected entities include Titan Company Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, NLC India Limited, Poonawalla Fincorp Limited, Bajaj Housing Finance Limited, Capri Global Capital Limited, Time Technoplast Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory suspension of trading in specified commercial papers ahead of their redemption record dates to ensure orderly settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the listed companies from the respective ex-dates / record dates onwards.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: Range from October 7, 2026 to October 20, 2026 depending on the specific commercial paper.

## Impact Assessment

- Prevents secondary market trading of commercial papers approaching maturity and redemption, ensuring clarity for investors and clearing corporations.
