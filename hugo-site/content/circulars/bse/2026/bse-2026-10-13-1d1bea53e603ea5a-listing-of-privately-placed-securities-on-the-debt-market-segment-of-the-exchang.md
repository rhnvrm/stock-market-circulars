## Summary

Trading members are informed that new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by various companies have been listed and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Bajaj Financial Securities Limited**: Scrip Code 732736, ISIN INE01C314HZ7, Issue Size 5,500 CPs of Rs. 5,00,000/- each, Rate of Interest 7.15% p.a., Redemption Date 11/01/2027.
- **NLC India Limited**: Scrip Code 732737, ISIN INE589A14488, Issue Size 20,000 CPs of Rs. 5,00,000/- each, Rate of Interest 6.47% p.a., Redemption Date 29/12/2026.
- **Fedbank Financial Services Limited**: Scrip Code 732738, ISIN INE007N14FS4, Issue Size 1,000 CPs of Rs. 5,00,000/- each, Rate of Interest 7.10% p.a., Redemption Date 08/12/2026.
- **Fedbank Financial Services Limited**: Scrip Code 732739, ISIN INE007N14FR6, Issue Size 4,000 CPs of Rs. 5,00,000/- each, Rate of Interest 7.10% p.a., Redemption Date 10/12/2026.
- **DSP Finance Private Limited**: Scrip Code 732740, ISIN INE422H14743, Issue Size 4,000 CPs of Rs. 5,00,000/- each, Redemption Date 11/02/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of privately placed debt securities (Commercial Papers) to trading on the BSE Debt Segment under standard exchange norms.

## Compliance Requirements

Securities are to be traded only in dematerialised form under the specified ISINs with standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and tick size of 1 paise.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date**: October 12, 2026
- **Listing / Effective Date**: October 13, 2026
- **Redemption / Maturity Dates**: Ranging from December 2026 to February 2027.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates liquidity and secondary market trading for privately placed commercial papers issued by corporate entities.
