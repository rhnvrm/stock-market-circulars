## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants and members that the Baroda BNP Paribas Services Fund has reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and associated facilities like SIP, STP, and SWP on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective August 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopening date: August 5, 2026.
- Covers multiple plans including Regular and Direct plans with Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- Facilities available include subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors using the BSE StAR MF Platform can facilitate transactions in the specified scheme plans in accordance with existing exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 4, 2026
- Effective Date: August 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to ongoing transactions and systematic investment facilities for Baroda BNP Paribas Services Fund on the platform.
