## Summary

Mirae Asset Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the Mirae Asset BSE Information Technology Index Fund along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription period runs from September 08, 2026, to September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Mirae Asset BSE Information Technology Index Fund (Regular and Direct Plans for Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **ISINs:** 
  - Regular Plan - Growth: INF769K01RL1
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout: INF769K01RN7
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF769K01RM9
  - Direct Plan - Growth: INF769K01RO5
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout: INF769K01RQ0
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF769K01RP2
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with an 'N' flag will be reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 24, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** September 08, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** September 22, 2026
- **Clear Funds Cut-off Time:** September 24, 2026 by 6:00 PM
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates investors and trading members to subscribe to the new index fund offering seamlessly through the BSE StAR MF infrastructure.
