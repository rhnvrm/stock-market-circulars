## Summary

In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260901-37 dated September 01, 2026, the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced that trading in the equity shares of Annu Projects Limited shall be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment under Group B.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Annu Projects Limited
- Scrip Code: 544893
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: Thursday, September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The securities are moving out of the restrictive Trade for Trade segment into the normal rolling settlement segment, enabling normal trading and settlement cycles for the shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the change in segment and group classification for order placement and risk management purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 02, 2026
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shift to the rolling segment facilitates greater liquidity and regular settlement for investors holding equity shares of Annu Projects Limited.
