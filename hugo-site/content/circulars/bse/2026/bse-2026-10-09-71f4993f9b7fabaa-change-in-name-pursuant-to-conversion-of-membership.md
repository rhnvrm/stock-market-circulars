## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that SEBI has approved the conversion of LLP membership of Anisha Fincap Consultants LLP into a corporate entity, Anisha Fincap Consultants Limited (Member No. 6658), effective October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name Change: Anisha Fincap Consultants LLP has been renamed to Anisha Fincap Consultants Limited.
- Member Number: 6658.
- Regulatory approval received from SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and relevant departments to note the change in name and update internal records accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 9, 2026
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Administrative update with no material impact on general market operations.
