## Summary

Effective from Monday, August 3, 2026, the Equity Shares of ADVANCE TECHNOFORGE LIMITED shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the 'MT' Group of Securities in the SME segment. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544843
- ISIN No: INE13ZJ01010
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: ADVANCETL
- Market Lot: 1200 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 95/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 85/-)
- Date of Allotment: July 30, 2026
- Total Securities: 9,030,800 Equity Shares

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on listing day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action executed for lock-in marking in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: July 30, 2026
- Listing Date: August 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Advance Technoforge Limited shares on the SME exchange platform.
