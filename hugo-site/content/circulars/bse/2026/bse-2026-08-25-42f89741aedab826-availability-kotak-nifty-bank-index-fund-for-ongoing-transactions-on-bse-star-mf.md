## Summary

BSE has announced the reopening of Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective August 26, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20260731-38.

## Key Points

- Schemes include various regular and direct plans (Growth and IDCW options) of Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund.
- Effective date for availability of transactions: August 26, 2026.
- Applies to BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the listed ISINs starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors ongoing transaction access (subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, SWP) to the Kotak Nifty Bank Index Fund variants on the BSE StAR MF platform.
