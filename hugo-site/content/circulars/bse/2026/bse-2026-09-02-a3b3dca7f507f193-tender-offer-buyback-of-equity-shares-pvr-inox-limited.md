## Summary

PVR Inox Limited has announced through BSE that September 4, 2026 has been fixed as the Record Date for the purpose of determining the entitlement and names of equity shareholders eligible to participate in the upcoming buyback tender offer.

## Key Points

- Company Name: PVR Inox Limited
- Scrip Code / ISIN: 532689 / 132689
- Record Date: 04/09/2026
- Ex-Entitlement Basis / Settlement No.: 04/09/2026 (DR-709/2026-2027)
- Purpose: To determine shareholder entitlement and eligibility for participation in the buyback offer.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible shareholders must take note of the record date and ex-entitlement date schedule for dematerialised securities under the rolling settlement segment.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 4, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders holding shares as of the record date will be eligible to participate in the buyback offer according to their calculated entitlements.
