## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Black Opal Consultants Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Thursday, October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Black Opal Consultants Limited
- Scrip Code: 544972
- Segment: SME IPO
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The trading segment for equity shares of Black Opal Consultants Limited is shifted from Trade for Trade (where trades settle on a gross basis with mandatory delivery) to the Rolling segment under M Group, allowing normal rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems and take note of the segment change for executing transactions in Black Opal Consultants Limited starting from October 22, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date of Segment Transfer: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This transfer enhances liquidity and normalizes trading mechanisms for equity shares of Black Opal Consultants Limited on the BSE SME platform, enabling standard rolling settlement.
