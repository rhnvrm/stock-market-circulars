## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 16,26,016 new equity shares of Ather Energy Limited (Scrip Code: 544397) issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters are listed and permitted to trade effective October 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** Ather Energy Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544397
- **ISIN:** INE0LEZ01016
- **Number of Shares:** 16,26,016 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1229/- (Issue Price: Rs. 1230/-)
- **Allotment Date:** 25/08/2026
- **Distribution Numbers:** 394493185 to 396119200
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to 02/04/2027
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing approval for preferential issue of shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants must take note of the lock-in period up to April 2, 2027, and the effective trading date of October 1, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 25, 2026
- **Listing and Trading Effective Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** April 2, 2027

## Impact Assessment

The listing increases the paid-up equity share capital of Ather Energy Limited, providing liquidity to the newly issued preferential shares subject to applicable lock-in restrictions.
