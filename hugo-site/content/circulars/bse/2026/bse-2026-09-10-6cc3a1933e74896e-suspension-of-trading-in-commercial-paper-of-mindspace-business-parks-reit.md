## Summary

Mindspace Business Parks REIT has fixed a Record Date for the purpose of the redemption of its Commercial Paper. Consequently, trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial paper of the company starting from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mindspace Business Parks REIT
- Scrip Code: 731581
- Scrip ID: MBPR-15-9-26-714/2026-2027
- ISIN: INE0CCU14138
- Record Date: 14/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 11/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are advised not to deal in the Commercial Paper of Mindspace Business Parks REIT from the ex-date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: 11/09/2026
- Record Date: 14/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified Commercial Paper of Mindspace Business Parks REIT will be suspended in preparation for its redemption.
