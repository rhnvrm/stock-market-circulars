## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) to specific securities. Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria have been placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II, triggering specific margin requirements effective from August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the defined criteria are shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I applies a 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II applies a 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Surveillance actions apply to all open positions as on August 07, 2026, and new positions created from August 10, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is a market surveillance measure and does not constitute an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised frameworks for Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) as per prior exchange circulars, enforcing enhanced margins on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for open and new positions starting August 10, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 06 Aug 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions: August 10, 2026
- Applicable Open Positions Cut-off Date: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the shortlisted securities will experience increased margin blocking, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips during the surveillance period.
