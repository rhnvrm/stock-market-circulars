## Summary

BSE has announced the revocation of suspension of trading in the equity shares of RCC Cements Ltd (Scrip Code: 531825) in accordance with compliance requirements under Regulation 14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. Trading will resume in the Normal Segment (Group X) effective Wednesday, October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: RCC Cements Ltd
- Scrip Code: 531825
- Previous Group: XT
- Proposed Group: X (Normal Segment)
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Suspension of trading in equity shares has been revoked following the company's compliance with outstanding Annual Listing Fees (ALF) under Regulation 14 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to note that trading in equity shares of RCC Cements Ltd is restored and shifted from Group XT to Group X (Normal Segment) w.e.f. October 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 9, 2026
- Revocation and Resumption Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members can resume normal trading in the equity shares of RCC Cements Ltd as the company moves back to the Normal Segment, restoring liquidity and normal market operations for the scrip.
