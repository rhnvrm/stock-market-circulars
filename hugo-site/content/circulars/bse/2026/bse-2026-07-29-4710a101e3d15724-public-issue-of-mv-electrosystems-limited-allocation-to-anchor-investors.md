## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 30,70,587 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors portion in the public issue of MV Electrosystems Limited at an issue price of Rs. 425/- per equity share on July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: MV Electrosystems Limited
- Anchor Investors Portion Subscribed: 30,70,587 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 425/- per equity share
- Notice Date: July 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants are to take note of the anchor investor allocation details for settlement and record purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date / Announcement Date: July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding institutional participation and demand ahead of the public issue opening.
