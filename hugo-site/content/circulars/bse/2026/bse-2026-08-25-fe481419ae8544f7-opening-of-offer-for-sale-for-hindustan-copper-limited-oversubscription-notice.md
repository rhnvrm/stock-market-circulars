## Summary

The President of India, acting through the Ministry of Mines, proposed an Offer for Sale (OFS) of Hindustan Copper Limited shares. The seller has chosen to exercise the oversubscription option of 2,90,10,721 Equity Shares in addition to the base offer size of 2,90,10,721 Equity Shares, bringing the total offer size up to 5,80,21,442 Equity Shares (6.00% of total paid-up equity share capital).

## Key Points

- Base Offer Size: 2,90,10,721 Equity Shares (3.00% of paid-up equity capital).
- Oversubscription Option: Additional 2,90,10,721 Equity Shares (3.00% of paid-up equity capital).
- Total Offer Size: Up to 5,80,21,442 Equity Shares (6.00% of paid-up equity capital).
- Retail Category: 10% of the total offer (58,02,146 Equity Shares) available on T+1 day (August 26, 2026).
- Employee Offer: 25,000 Equity Shares (0.0026% of paid-up equity capital) offered to eligible employees.
