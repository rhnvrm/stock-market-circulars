BSE Limited has informed trading members that the equity shares of Technocrats Plasma Systems Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in due course under the SME segment (Scrip Code: 544877, Symbol: TECHNOCRAT, ISIN: INE19QK01022).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Technocrats Plasma Systems Limited
- Scrip Code: 544877
- Symbol: TECHNOCRAT
- ISIN: INE19QK01022
- Segment: SME

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the scrip code and details for trading operations once the listing date is announced.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 20, 2026
- Listing Date: To be announced via a separate notice.

Enables trading in the equity shares of Technocrats Plasma Systems Limited on the BSE SME platform.
