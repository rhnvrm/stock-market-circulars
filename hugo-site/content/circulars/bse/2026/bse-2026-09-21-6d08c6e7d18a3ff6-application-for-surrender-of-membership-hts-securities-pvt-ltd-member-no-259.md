## Summary

HTS Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member no. 259) has applied to surrender its membership of the BSE Exchange in accordance with the Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations of the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Member Name: HTS Securities Pvt. Ltd.
- Member Number: 259
- Department: Membership Department

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members wishing to raise any objection to the surrender of membership must submit their objections in writing to the Membership Department on or before October 5, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 21, 2026
- Objection Deadline: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No significant market impact; standard administrative process for a member surrender.
