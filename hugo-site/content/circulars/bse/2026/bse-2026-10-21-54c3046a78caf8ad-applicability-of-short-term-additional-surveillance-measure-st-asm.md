## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to specific securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I mandates a margin rate of 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II mandates a margin rate of 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Applicable on all open positions as on October 22, 2026, and new positions created from October 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened margin surveillance requirements under the ST-ASM framework to curb excessive volatility and speculative trading in shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) for all open positions as of October 22, 2026, and all new positions created from October 23, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Base Date for Open Positions:** October 22, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I and II will experience increased capital and margin commitments, potentially impacting short-term liquidity and trading volumes in those specific scrips.
