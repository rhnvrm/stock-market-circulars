## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about debt restructuring and revised trading terms for the debt securities of Iinspira Springdale Private Limited, effective from September 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- In continuation of previous notice No. 20251016-46 dated October 16, 2025.
- Scrip Code: 977214, ISIN: INE2C4407019.
- Available for trading with revised terms on account of debt restructuring.
- Effective Date: Tuesday, September 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised trading terms following debt restructuring of the issuer.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to note the revised terms and scrip details for transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 08, 2026
- Notice Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update affecting specific debt security holders and trading members dealing with the mentioned ISIN.
