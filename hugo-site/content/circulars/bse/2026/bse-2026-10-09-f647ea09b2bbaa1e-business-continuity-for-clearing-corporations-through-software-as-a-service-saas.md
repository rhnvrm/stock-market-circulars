BSE Clearing Corporation Limited (BSECL) has issued a notice announcing a mock session for its members concerning Business Continuity for Clearing Corporations through Software as a Service (SaaS) Mode.

- Focuses on Business Continuity through Software as a Service (SaaS) mode.
