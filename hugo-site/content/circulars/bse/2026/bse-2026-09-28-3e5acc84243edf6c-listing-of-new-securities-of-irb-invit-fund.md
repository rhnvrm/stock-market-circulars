## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading of 5,40,00,000 new units of IRB InvIT Fund (Scrip Code: 540526) issued on a preferential basis to the sponsor for cash consideration, effective from September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** IRB InvIT Fund
- **Scrip Code:** 540526
- **ISIN:** INE183W23014
- **Security Type:** Units issued on a preferential basis for cash consideration
- **Number of Units:** 5,40,00,000 units
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 65/- per unit
- **Date of Allotment:** 25/09/2026
- **Effective Date:** Tuesday, September 29, 2026
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with old equity shares / units of the fund

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice for preferential allotment of units by an InvIT.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the lock-in details and distribution numbers for the newly listed units:
- 8,100,000 units (Dist. Nos. 1281600001 - 1289700000) under lock-in up to 30/09/2029.
- 45,900,000 units (Dist. Nos. 1289700001 - 1335600000) under lock-in up to 30/09/2027.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** September 25, 2026
- **Listing & Trading Effective Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** September 30, 2027 and September 30, 2029

## Impact Assessment

The listing of 54 million preferential units increases the capitalization of IRB InvIT Fund. The specified lock-in periods ensure sponsor commitment while expanding unit availability and liquidity post lock-in.
