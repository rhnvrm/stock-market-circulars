## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the record dates and reduced face values for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of several companies, effective from September 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading members are informed about fixed record dates for interest payment and part redemption of NCDs/bonds.
- Trading in the debentures of the respective companies will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.
- Affected entities include U.P. Power Corporation Limited, Magnum Ventures Limited, Infinity Fincorp Solutions Private Limited, and Kanakadurga Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- No direct regulatory policy changes; standard corporate action processing for debt segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for the listed debentures and bonds.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / Ex-Date: September 15, 2026, and September 17, 2026, depending on the specific scrip.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified NCDs and bonds will proceed with reduced face values as per the schedule, impacting debt segment settlements for the listed ISINs.
