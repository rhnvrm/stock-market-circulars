## Summary

Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund is launching the Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund NFO along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window is open from October 05, 2026, to October 19, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth and IDCW options).
- NFO Period: October 05, 2026, to October 19, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 19 for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 26, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on October 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 23, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be client-authenticated by 3:00 PM on October 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: October 05, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 19, 2026 (midnight for lumpsum)
- SIP/XSIP Authentication Deadline: October 19, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Clear Funds Deadline (BSE Clearing): October 23, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless investment in the new Kotak Nifty 500 Index Fund NFO for members and investors through the BSE StAR MF platform.
