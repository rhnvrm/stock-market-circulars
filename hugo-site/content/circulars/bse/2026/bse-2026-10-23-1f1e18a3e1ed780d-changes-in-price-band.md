## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 30 scrips in the Equity segment, effective from October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 30 scrips have undergone price band revisions.
- Revised price bands range from 2% to 20% depending on the specific scrip.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is October 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Price bands for specified equity scrips are revised to curb excessive volatility and protect investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure their trading systems are updated accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected scrips will be subject to the new price limit percentages starting from the effective date.
