## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The applicable margin rate is set at 50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities identified under ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Securities identified under ST-ASM Stage II will attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Effective date for the surveillance actions is October 16, 2026, applicable on all open positions as on October 15, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure maintenance of required margin levels (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) for open positions and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026 (on open positions as of October 15, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the shortlisted securities will experience higher margin blocking, affecting liquidity and position sizing in those specific scrips.
