## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the extension of timelines for compliance with Digital Accessibility Circulars, following SEBI's directive. The deadline for conducting accessibility audits for digital platforms and remediating findings has been extended to October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Extension granted for conducting accessibility audits of digital platforms and remediation of findings.
- New compliance deadline is October 31, 2026.
- Applicable to Investment Advisers and other regulated entities under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
- References previous SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131 and recent SEBI Circular No. HO/(411)2026-ITD-5_DIV2/I/17922/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

SEBI has extended the timeline for mandatory compliance regarding digital platform accessibility audits and remediation.

## Compliance Requirements

Investment Advisers and regulated entities must take note of the revised timeline and complete the digital accessibility audits and necessary remediation by October 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Extension Deadline:** October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides regulated entities additional time to ensure their digital platforms comply with accessibility standards mandated under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.
