## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that new privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) issued by various companies have been listed and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Birla Group Holdings Private Limited**: 4,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732654, Scrip ID: BGHPL24926, ISIN: INE09OL14JY2), maturing on 24/11/2026 at 6.97% p.a. interest.
- **ICICI Securities Primary Dealership Limited**: 4,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732655, Scrip ID: ISPD240926, ISIN: INE849D14IC8), maturing on 24/12/2026 at 6.95% p.a. interest.
- **Dolat Algotech Limited**: 200 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732656, Scrip ID: DAL230926, ISIN: INE966A14157), maturing on 22/09/2027 at 9.50% p.a. interest.
- **Navi Finserv Limited**: 400 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732657, Scrip ID: NFL230926, ISIN: INE342T14HT6), maturing on 24/03/2027 at 9.90% p.a. interest.
- **Redington Limited**: 4,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732658, Scrip ID: RL230926, ISIN: INE891D14D68), maturing on 22/12/2026 at 6.67% p.a. interest.
- Trading takes place in dematerialised form with a standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard issuance and listing notice on the debt market segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard dematerialised trading norms and denomination requirements for debt instruments.

## Important Dates

- Allotment / Effective Date: September 23 - 24, 2026
- Redemptions / Maturity Dates: Ranging from November 24, 2026 to September 22, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Provides debt market liquidity and investment opportunities through commercial paper instruments for institutional and eligible investors.
