## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment scheduled for Saturday, August 22, 2026. Trading members using third-party platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their applications under various conditions such as risk-reduction mode and trading halt.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, August 22, 2026, for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment.
- No new BOLT TWS Release will accompany this session.
- Timings: Log-in from 08:15 am to 09:00 am; Order Entry from 09:00 am to 09:07 am; Matching period from 09:07 am to 09:15 am; Continuous Trading from 09:15 am to 12:00 pm; Block Deal Window from 11:45 am to 12:00 pm; Trade Modification from 12:00 pm to 12:10 pm.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 12:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively to test their trading systems and provide feedback to the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 22, 2026
- Feedback Submission Deadline: August 22, 2026, by 12:00 pm

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct market impact, as the session is purely for testing and system familiarization without financial obligations.
