## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, August 22, 2026. Trading members using third-party platforms of empaneled vendors or in-house developed systems via ETI APIs can test their trading applications under exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, August 22, 2026, in the Equity Derivatives segment.
- Designed for testing third-party or in-house trading platforms via ETI APIs.
- No new BOLT TWS Release.
- Trades resulting from the session will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 12:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively in the mock trading session and submit feedback to the BSE helpdesk or tech support team by 12:00 pm.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 22, 2026
- Login Time: 08:15 am
- Pre-Open: 09:00 am
- Order Entry Period: 09:00 am to 09:52 am
- Continuous Trading: 10:00 am to 12:10 pm

## Impact Assessment

Zero financial or operational impact on live markets, as the session is strictly for testing and familiarization purposes.
