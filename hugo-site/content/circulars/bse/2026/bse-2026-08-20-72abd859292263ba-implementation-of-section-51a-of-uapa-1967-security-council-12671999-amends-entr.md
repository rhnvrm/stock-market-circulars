## Summary

The Exchange has issued a notice communicating SEBI's update regarding the implementation of Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967 (UAPA), reflecting amendments to the United Nations Security Council's (UNSC) 1267(1999) Sanctions List concerning ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida.

## Key Points

- Notice No. 20260820-10 issued by BSE DOSS department on August 20, 2026.
- Incorporates updates to the UNSC 1267(1999) Sanctions List including the amendment of 29 entries.
- Highlights SEBI communications and URLs for checking updated lists of individuals and entities subject to asset freezes and financial service denials.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of updates to the UNSC 1267(1999) Sanctions List under Section 51A of UAPA, 1967.
- Adherence to Central Government Order dated February 02, 2021.

## Compliance Requirements

- Scan all existing and future accounts to ensure no accounts are held by or linked to entities or individuals included in the sanctions list.
- Follow clause 54 of SEBI Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Combating Financing of Terrorism (CFT) guidelines dated June 06, 2024.
- Forward any de-listing requests electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA and a copy to MEA, Joint Secretary (UNP).
- Inform concerned parties regarding the independent Ombudsperson procedure for removal requests.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 20, 2026
- UNSC Sanctions List updates dated: August 19, 2026 and August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market intermediaries and regulated entities must immediately update their screening systems and scan databases against the revised UNSC Sanctions List to ensure full compliance with AML/CFT obligations.
