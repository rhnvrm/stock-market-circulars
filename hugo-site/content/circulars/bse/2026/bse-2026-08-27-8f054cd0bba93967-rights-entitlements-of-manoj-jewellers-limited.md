## Summary

The Exchange has informed trading members that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of MANOJ JEWELLERS LIMITED will be permitted starting August 31, 2026. The Rights Issue itself will remain open from August 31, 2026, to September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: MANOJ JEWELLERS LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751132
- Scrip ID: MANOJ-RE
- ISIN No.: INE0MV020018
- Market Lot: 2000
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: August 31, 2026, to September 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard settlement procedures for Rights Entitlements apply.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- A disclaimer must be prominently included in contract notes and bills for purchases of REs, informing clients about participation rights, renunciation, and the risk of lapsing.
- REs must be tradable in dematerialized form only and will be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis under a trade-for-trade mechanism.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 27, 2026
- Trading Period for REs: August 31, 2026 – September 24, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: August 31, 2026 – September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding eligible shares or trading in REs can participate in the rights issue or renounce their entitlements within the specified window. Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and extinguish after the issue closing date.
