## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of two newly listed companies, Symbiotec Pharmalab Limited and Skyways Air Services Limited, to the BSE IPO Index effective from the open of Thursday, September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Symbiotec Pharmalab Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544889) and Skyways Air Services Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544890) are being added to the BSE IPO Index.
- The inclusion is effective at the open of trading on September 03, 2026.
- Both stocks were listed on BSE effective September 01, 2026.
- Actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No specific compliance actions required for market participants regarding this index addition.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine index adjustment with minimal direct market impact, affecting passive funds tracking the BSE IPO Index.
