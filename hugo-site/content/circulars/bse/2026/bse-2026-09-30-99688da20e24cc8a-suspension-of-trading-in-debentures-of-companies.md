## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest or redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures of these companies as per the announced record and ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260930-62, dated September 30, 2026.
- Segment: Debt Segment, Listing Operations Department.
- Covers multiple companies including Power Grid Corporation of India, Berar Finance, KLM Axiva Finvest, Chemmanur Credits and Investments, Electronica Finance, REC Limited, Poonawalla Fincorp, Piramal Finance, Vivriti Capital, and Krazybee Services.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures during the record/ex-date periods.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational circular regarding debt securities suspension around record dates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance by refraining from trading in the specified NCDs and bonds on and from the respective ex-dates/record dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates range from October 5, 2026 to October 22, 2026 across various securities.

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to debt market participants holding or trading the specific non-convertible debentures mentioned during their respective record and ex-dates.
