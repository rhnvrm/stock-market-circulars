## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Prabhatam Infra Venture Limited (Scrip Code - 531647) effective from September 21, 2026, following a preferential issue and share swap.

## Key Points

- Total new securities listed: 20,25,10,350 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each.
- Issue types include preferential basis pursuant to a share swap and issuance to non-promoters.
- Effective trading date: Monday, September 21, 2026.
- ISIN: INE265C01025.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and distribution numbers as specified.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 21/07/2026
- Listing/Trading Effective Date: 21/09/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 25/03/2027 and 25/03/2028

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Prabhatam Infra Venture Limited with specific lock-in restrictions applied to promoter and non-promoter holdings.
