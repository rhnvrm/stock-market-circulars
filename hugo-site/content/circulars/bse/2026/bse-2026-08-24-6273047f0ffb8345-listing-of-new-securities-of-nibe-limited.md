## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of NIBE LIMITED (Scrip Code - 535136) effective from August 25, 2026, following the conversion of warrants into equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 3,20,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1248/- (Issue Price: Rs. 1258/- per share).
- **Allottees:** Issued to Non-Promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- **Ranking:** The new equity shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 14941274 to 15261273
- **ISIN:** INE149O01018
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in upto March 30, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are to note the addition of the new securities to the trading segment.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 08/06/2026
- **Listing / Effective Date:** 25/08/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 30/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Involves expansion of paid-up capital due to warrant conversion for Nibe Limited with standard lock-in restrictions applied to the newly allotted shares.
