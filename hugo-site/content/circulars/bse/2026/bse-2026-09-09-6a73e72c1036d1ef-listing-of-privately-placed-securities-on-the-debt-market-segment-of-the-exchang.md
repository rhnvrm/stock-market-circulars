## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by various companies, effective September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are traded only in dematerialised form under specific ISINs.
- Standard denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuances include companies such as Lodha Developers Limited, Can Fin Homes Ltd., Avenue Supermarts Limited, SBICAP Securities Limited, and Grasim Industries Ltd.
- Allotment date for the listed commercial papers is 08/09/2026 with redemption dates ranging between December 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard commercial paper trading norms and dematerialised form requirements.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 08/09/2026
- Effective Listing Date: 09/09/2026
- Redemption Dates: 07/12/2026 to 08/12/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update reflecting debt segment issuances and liquidity instruments availability.
