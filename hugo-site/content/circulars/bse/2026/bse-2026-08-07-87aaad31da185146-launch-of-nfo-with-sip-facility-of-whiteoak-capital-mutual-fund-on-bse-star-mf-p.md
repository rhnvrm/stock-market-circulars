## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for WhiteOak Capital Dividend Yield Fund along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** WhiteOak Capital Dividend Yield Fund (Regular and Direct plans, covering Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options).
- **NFO Period:** August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 31, 2026.
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 10, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** August 28, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the new mutual fund scheme offering through the BSE StAR MF platform.
