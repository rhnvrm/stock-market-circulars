## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the mock trading schedule following the switchover of trading systems from the Primary (PR) location to the Disaster Recovery (DR) location on October 10, 2026. Trading operations commence from 2:45 PM onwards across equity, equity derivatives, currency derivatives, commodity derivatives, and Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segments.

## Key Points

- Switchover of trading systems from PR location to DR location initiated on October 10, 2026.
- Pre-open session at DR location starts from 2:45 PM for Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments.
- Detailed timings provided for equity pre-open, continuous trading, special pre-open for IPO/re-listed scrips, periodic call auction, block deal window, and closing sessions.
- Equity derivatives segment switchover to DR occurred between 2:00 PM and 2:45 PM followed by pre-open and continuous trading (DR) till 4:55 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational transition and switchover from Primary (PR) to Disaster Recovery (DR) trading environment for mock session testing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are required to note the revised mock trading session timings and conduct operations accordingly during the DR test.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Notice Date: October 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Zero direct impact on live market settlements or regular production trading as this is part of scheduled Disaster Recovery (DR) mock exercises.
