## Summary

Effective from Thursday, October 1, 2026, equity shares of A-ONE Steels India Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: A-ONE Steels India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544952
- ISIN No: INE0OTC01025
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: AONESTEELS
- Total Securities: 77,234,693 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 405/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 395/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 29, 2026
- Market Lot: 1 share

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing notification under SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities must be traded only in dematerialised form pursuant to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 1, 2026.
- Necessary corporate actions executed for lock-in marking in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 29, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market liquidity and enables shareholders and investors to trade equity shares of A-ONE Steels India Limited on the BSE platform.
