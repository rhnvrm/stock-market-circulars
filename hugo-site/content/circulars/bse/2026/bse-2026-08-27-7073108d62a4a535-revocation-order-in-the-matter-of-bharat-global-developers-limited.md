## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revocation of directions issued against Bharat Global Developers Limited, following a SEBI order dated August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous Exchange notices dated December 23, 2024, and March 26, 2025.
- SEBI order no. WTM/KV/CFID/CFID/32590/2026-27 dated August 27, 2026.
- Revocation with immediate effect of all directions qua Identified Noticees issued via Interim Order and confirmed via Confirmatory Order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Previous restrictions and directions on identified noticees under the interim and confirmatory orders have been revoked with immediate effect.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the revocation and ensure compliance accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Relieves the identified noticees from prior regulatory restrictions imposed by SEBI's earlier interim and confirmatory orders.
