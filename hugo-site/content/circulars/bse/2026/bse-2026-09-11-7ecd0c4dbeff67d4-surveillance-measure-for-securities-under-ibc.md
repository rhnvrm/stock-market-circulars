## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Securities satisfying the criteria will be placed under or moved out of the framework effective September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are subjected to additional surveillance measures effective September 15, 2026.
- Annexure I lists securities newly satisfying the criteria.
- Annexure II contains the list of securities moving out of the IBC Framework.
- Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities under the framework.
- Annexure IV lists securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities placed under the IBC framework will be reviewed on a periodical basis.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their pre-shortlisting levels, subject to not being part of any other surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note that the framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely for market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the company.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in affected securities will be governed by the surveillance framework conditions, impacting price bands and monitoring mechanisms for companies undergoing insolvency proceedings.
