## Summary

BSE has issued Notice No. 20261022-37 on October 22, 2026, announcing the revision of price bands for 24 scrips, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 24 listed scrips.
- Effective date: October 23, 2026.
- Revised bands range across 2%, 5%, 10%, and 20% limits depending on the scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of daily price operating range for specified equities to manage market volatility and protect investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while placing orders in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Directly impacts trading operations and order placement limits for market participants trading in the affected scrips.
