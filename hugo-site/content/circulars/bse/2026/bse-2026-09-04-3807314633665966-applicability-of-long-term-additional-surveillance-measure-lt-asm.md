## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on the revised framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and transfers to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS) for shortlisted securities.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. September 09, 2026, on open positions and new positions for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Lower price bands applicable w.e.f. September 07, 2026, for securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM (Annexure I, Part B).
- Transfer of certain securities to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. September 09, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- List of securities moving out of LT-ASM Framework effective September 07, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the revised Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework to monitor market volatility and safeguard investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and adjust trading strategies for securities placed under various stages of the LT-ASM framework.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date (Price Bands / Moving Out):** September 07, 2026
- **Effective Date (Margins / Group Transfers):** September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin obligations and trading restrictions for shortlisted securities may impact short-term liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
