## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Non-Competitive Bidding for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for August 18, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated August 14, 2026. Trading members can participate via the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding platform available via BSE iBBS web-based system (NCB-GSec module).
- Bid collection start date: August 17, 2026, from 10:00 AM onwards (24 hours availability).
- Bid collection end date for direct investors: August 17, 2026, till 5:00 PM.
- Bid collection end date for members: August 18, 2026, till 8:00 AM.
- Auction Date: August 18, 2026; Settlement Date: August 19, 2026.
- Multiple SDL securities from various states (such as Assam, Bihar, Delhi, Jammu & Kashmir, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Mizoram, Odisha, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and West Bengal) are available for re-issue and new issuance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Facilitation of non-competitive bidding in State Government Securities auctions in line with RBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure bids are submitted within the prescribed timelines through the NCB-GSec module and coordinate with ICCL for pay-in and settlement obligations.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start: August 17, 2026, 10:00 AM
- Direct Investor Bid End: August 17, 2026, 5:00 PM
- Member Bid End: August 18, 2026, 8:00 AM
- Auction Date: August 18, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables market participants and retail/direct investors to access and bid for State Government Securities smoothly through the exchange infrastructure.
