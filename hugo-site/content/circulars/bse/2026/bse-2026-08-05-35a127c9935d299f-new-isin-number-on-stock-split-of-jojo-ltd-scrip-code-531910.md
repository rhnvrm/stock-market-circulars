## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the allocation of a new ISIN number for JOJO LTD following the sub-division of its equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: JOJO LTD
- Scrip Code: 531910
- New ISIN: INE312M01024
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares from Rs.10/- to Rs.5/-
- Ex-Date: 07-08-2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the new ISIN for trades executed on and from the Ex-Date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05 August 2026
- Ex-Date: 07-08-2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement and identification of equity shares following the stock split and face value change.
