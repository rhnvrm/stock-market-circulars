## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of specified securities into respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective October 01, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and take adequate precautions.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure move to higher GSM stages starting October 01, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, including 100% margin rates, 5% price bands, trade-for-trade settlement with Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD), and weekly trading restrictions.
- Settlement shall be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance measures under the GSM framework as established on February 23, 2017, and subsequent revisions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities and adhere to trade-to-trade settlement rules without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities experience increased liquidity constraints and strict margin/trading frequency requirements, impacting market participants trading in these scrips.
