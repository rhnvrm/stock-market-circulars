## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from August 05, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and take adequate precautions.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure move to higher stages of GSM effective August 05, 2026.
- Stage I triggers: 100% margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II triggers: Trade-for-trade with 5% price band and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value by buyers.
- Stage III & IV triggers: Trade-for-trade with 5% price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday), and 100% ASD (with no upward price movement for Stage IV).
- Settlement shall be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the revised Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework originally published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent updates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate precautions while trading in these securities, ensuring compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules and deposit requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 04 Aug 2026
- Effective Date: 05 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Significant impact on market liquidity, price discovery, and settlement obligations for the affected securities due to trade restrictions and mandatory deposit requirements.
