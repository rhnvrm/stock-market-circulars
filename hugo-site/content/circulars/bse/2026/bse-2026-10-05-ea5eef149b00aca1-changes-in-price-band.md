## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of 16 scrips under Notice No. 20261005-34 dated October 05, 2026. These changes are effective from October 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total scrips affected: 16
- Effective date: October 06, 2026
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance)
- Segment: Equity
- Scrips and revised price bands:
  - Bal Pharma Ltd (524824): 5%
  - Benchmark Computer Solutions Ltd (544052): 5%
  - Betala Global Securities Ltd (531530): 5%
  - Century Business Media Ltd (544928): 20%
  - Cospower Engineering Ltd (543172): 2%
  - D.P. Abhushan Ltd (544161): 10%
  - Injecto Polymers Ltd (544930): 20%
  - Jaipan Industries Ltd (505840): 2%
  - JHS Svendgaard Laboratories Ltd (532771): 5%
  - Karnimata Cold Storage Ltd (537784): 5%
  - Manika Plastech Ltd (544929): 20%
  - Naturite Agro Products Ltd (538926): 5%
  - Palm Jewels Ltd (541444): 10%
  - SVC INDUSTRIES Ltd (524488): 5%
  - Tuni Textile Mills Ltd (751144): 10%
  - Turtlemint Fintech Solutions Ltd (544799): 5%

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of price limits to curb excessive volatility and ensure orderly trading in respective scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure their trading systems and clients adhere to the updated limits.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and traders dealing in these 16 scrips will experience modified upper/lower circuit limits starting October 06, 2026.
