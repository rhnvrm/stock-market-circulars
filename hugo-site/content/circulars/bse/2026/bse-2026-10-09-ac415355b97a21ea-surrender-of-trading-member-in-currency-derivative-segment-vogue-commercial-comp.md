## Summary

BSE Ltd has approved the surrender of trading membership in the currency derivative segment by Vogue Commercial Company Ltd. (Member No. 6147) effective October 8, 2026.

- Member No.: 6147
- Segment: Currency Derivative Segment
- Effective Date: October 8, 2026
- Issuing Authority: BSE Ltd. (Membership - Registrations Operation & Compliance Department)

No new regulatory changes; this represents a standard procedural surrender of membership.
