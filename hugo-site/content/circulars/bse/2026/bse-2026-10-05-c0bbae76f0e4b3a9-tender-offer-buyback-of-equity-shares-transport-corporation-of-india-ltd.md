## Summary

Transport Corporation of India Ltd has announced the fixation of the Record Date for its upcoming Tender Offer (Buyback) of Equity Shares. Trading members and market participants are informed that the record date is set for October 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: TRANSPORT CORPORATION OF INDIA LTD (BSE: 532349)
- Record Date: October 9, 2026
- Purpose: To determine the entitlement and names of equity shareholders eligible to participate in the buyback offer.
- Ex-Entitlement Date: October 9, 2026 (Notice Ref: DR-732/2026-2027)
- Segment: Dematerialised Securities – Rolling Settlement Segment

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to take note of the record date and ex-entitlement date for processing transactions and determining eligible shareholders for the buyback offer.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 9, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders holding shares as of the record date will be eligible to participate in the buyback tender offer. Trading members should ensure proper alignment with the ex-entitlement date schedule.
