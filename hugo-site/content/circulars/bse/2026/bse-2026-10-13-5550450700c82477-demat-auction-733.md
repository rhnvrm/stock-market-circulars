## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. 733 for Rolling Settlement No. DR-733/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on October 13, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on October 13, 2026.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m. on October 13, 2026.
- Money statement and final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m. on October 13, 2026.
- Pay-in of the auction will take place on Wednesday, October 14, 2026 at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is removed pursuant to the uniform membership structure implementation in Equity Cash Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 13, 2026
- Pay-in Date: October 14, 2026 (08:00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Routine settlement operation affecting trading members participating in short-delivery auctions.
