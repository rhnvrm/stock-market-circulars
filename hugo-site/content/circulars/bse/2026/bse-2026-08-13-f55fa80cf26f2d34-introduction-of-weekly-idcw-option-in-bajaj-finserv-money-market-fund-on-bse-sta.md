## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of the Weekly Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) option under the Bajaj Finserv Money Market Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Weekly IDCW option with Payout Facility introduced for Bajaj Finserv Money Market Fund.
- Effective date of implementation: August 14, 2026.
- Applicable platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Notice issued based on communication received from Bajaj Finserv Mutual Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can access the newly introduced scheme option on the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an additional payout frequency option (Weekly IDCW) for liquidity and regular cash flow management within the Bajaj Finserv Money Market Fund.
