## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debentures and bonds for multiple entities due to fixed record dates for payment of interest and redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs).

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures/bonds of specified companies starting from their respective ex-dates.
- Affected entities include IDBI Bank Ltd, John Deere Financial India Private Limited, Kerala Financial Corporation, Vastu Housing Finance Corporation Limited, ICICI Home Finance Company Limited, Muthoot Mcredit Limited, and Midland Microfin Limited.
- Record dates and ex-dates fall primarily between August 14, 2026, and August 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the advisory and refrain from dealing in the specified debentures and bonds on and from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- **Record Dates:** August 14, 2026, to August 30, 2026
- **Ex-Dates:** August 14, 2026, to August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of secondary market trading in the specified debt instruments for corporate actions such as interest payment and redemption.
