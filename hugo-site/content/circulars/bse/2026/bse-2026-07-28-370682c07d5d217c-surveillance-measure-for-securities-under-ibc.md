## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Surveillance Measure for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective from July 29, 2026, specific securities have satisfied the criteria under the Additional Surveillance Measure for Companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP).

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the Additional Surveillance Measure for IRP as per IBC are subjected to surveillance actions effective July 29, 2026.
- Review of securities placed under the framework is conducted on a periodical basis.
- List of securities moving out of the IBC Framework, as well as the consolidated list of securities under the framework, are provided in the annexures of the notice.
- The framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is strictly a market surveillance measure and should not be construed as adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures for securities undergoing insolvency proceedings under the IBC framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the list of securities included, reviewed, or moving out of the IBC surveillance framework and adjust trading and risk parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Notice Date:** July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their original bands, subject to not being part of any other surveillance measures.
- Provides transparency on securities under corporate insolvency resolution processes impacting trading and surveillance.
