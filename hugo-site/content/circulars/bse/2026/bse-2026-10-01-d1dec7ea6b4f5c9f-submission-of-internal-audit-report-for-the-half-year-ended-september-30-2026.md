## Summary

BSE has issued Notice No. 20261001-37 dated October 01, 2026, through its Department of Surveillance and Supervision (DOSS), detailing the submission requirements for the Internal Audit Report for the half year ended September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20261001-37
- Notice Date: 01 Oct 2026
- Category: Compliance
- Segment: General
- Department: DOSS
- Multiple attachments and reporting formats provided via Annexure I, II, III, IIIA, IV, V, and VI.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulations enacted; this notice serves as the recurring procedural directive for half-yearly compliance filings.

## Compliance Requirements

Regulated entities must submit their Internal Audit Report for the half-year ended September 30, 2026, adhering strictly to the guidelines and formats specified in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 01 October 2026
- Period Ended: 30 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates ongoing regulatory oversight and ensures operational transparency through systematic internal audit submissions.
