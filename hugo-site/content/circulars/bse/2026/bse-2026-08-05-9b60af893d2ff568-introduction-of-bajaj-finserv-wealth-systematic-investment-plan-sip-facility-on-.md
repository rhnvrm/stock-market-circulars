## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of the Bajaj Finserv Wealth Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) Facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. This facility allows members to register SIP and XSIP for clients with a predefined tenure and select a Target Scheme for withdrawal upon maturity.

## Key Points

- Wealth SIP facility supports both SIP and XSIP registrations in Non-Demat Mode only.
- Fixed tenure options available: 8, 10, 12, 15, 20, 25, or 30 years.
- Applicable only for specified eligible schemes of Bajaj Finserv Mutual Fund.
- Available for all tax status categories except Minor tax status.
- Investors can nominate a Target Scheme at registration for withdrawal upon maturity.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; this is a product and platform feature integration.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with guidelines prescribed by Bajaj Finserv AMC and RTA while submitting registrations.
- Careful review of Annexures (Process Notes, Eligible Scheme List, Product Overview, and API Specifications) is advised to avoid rejections.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances product offerings on the BSE StAR MF platform, giving distributors and investors structured long-term systematic investment and withdrawal options.
