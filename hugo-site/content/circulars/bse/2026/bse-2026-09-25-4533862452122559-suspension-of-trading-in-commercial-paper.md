## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) for multiple companies following the fixation of record dates for their redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the listed companies from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects commercial papers of companies including J.K. Cement, Tata Steel, Bajaj Finance, Capri Global Capital, and Poonawalla Fincorp.
- Record dates and ex-dates for redemption fall between October 5, 2026, and October 12, 2026.
- Trading members are strictly advised not to deal in the commercial papers of these entities as specified.
- Issued by the Listing Operations and Compliance department of BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; standard operational procedure for commercial paper redemption and suspension of trading on record dates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure zero trading activity in the specified commercial papers of the listed companies on and after the respective record/ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Record / Ex-Dates: October 5, 2026, to October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly settlement and redemption of commercial papers without secondary market interference post-record date.
