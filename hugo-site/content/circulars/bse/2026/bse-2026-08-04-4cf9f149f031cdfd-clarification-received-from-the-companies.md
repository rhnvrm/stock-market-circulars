## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price or volume movements in specific scrips and requested clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from: KIOCL Ltd and Shalimar Paints Ltd (regarding volume variation).
- Replies awaited from: Medico Remedies Ltd, RSD Finance Ltd, Amit Securities Ltd, Aryan Share and Stock Brokers Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Crazy Snacks Ltd, Gconnect Logitech and Supply Chain Ltd, Sheetal Cool Products Ltd, and Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies identified by surveillance must submit required price/volume clarifications promptly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 04 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Informational notice regarding ongoing exchange surveillance measures to maintain market transparency.
