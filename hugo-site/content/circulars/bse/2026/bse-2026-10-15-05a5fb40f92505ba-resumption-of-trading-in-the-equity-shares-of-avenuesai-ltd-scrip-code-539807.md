## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of trading in the consolidated equity shares of AvenuesAI Ltd (Scrip Code: 539807) with effect from October 19, 2026. This follows the consolidation of equity shares from a face value of Re. 1/- each to Rs. 10/- each.

## Key Points

- Trading in consolidated equity shares of AvenuesAI Ltd resumes on October 19, 2026.
- New ISIN allocated: INE483S01038.
- Face value changed from Re. 1/- fully paid up to Rs. 10/- fully paid up.
- Market lot: 1 (One).

## Regulatory Changes

- Share consolidation executed under corporate action (DR-738/2026-2027), updating the company's capital structure and ISIN.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take abundant precautions while mentioning the rates for the equity shares of the company following the face value consolidation to Rs. 10/- each.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date for Resumption of Trading / New ISIN validity: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders and trading members must account for the consolidated face value (Rs. 10/-) and new ISIN (INE483S01038) for all transactions effective October 19, 2026.
