## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis effective from August 07, 2026. The securities include issues from companies such as The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd., SBICAP Securities Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Capri Global Capital Limited, and Vivriti Capital Limited.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Debt Segment
- **Trading Form:** Dematerialised form under specific ISIN numbers.
- **Market Lot & Denomination:** Standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof with a market lot of 1.
- **Tick Size:** 1 paisa.

## Listed Securities Details

   - Scrip Code: 732229 | Scrip ID: TBBTCL6826 | ISIN: INE050A14937
   - Description: Commercial Paper (500 quantity, Issue Price: Rs. 496,409.50, Rate: 8.00% p.a., Maturity: 08/09/2026)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialised form in accordance with the specified ISINs and standard market denominations.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Ranges from September 2026 to February 2027 depending on the specific commercial paper.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update providing liquidity and investment options in commercial papers on the BSE debt segment.
