## Summary

Stock exchanges and trading members are hereby informed that the trading member Malik And Jain Securities Pvt. Ltd. has changed its name to Alphamatrix Securities Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Old Name: Malik And Jain Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 6879)
- New Name: Alphamatrix Securities Private Limited
- Effective Date: 21st September 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may contact membership operations for any clarifications.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 21 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to record updates for the trading member's name change.
