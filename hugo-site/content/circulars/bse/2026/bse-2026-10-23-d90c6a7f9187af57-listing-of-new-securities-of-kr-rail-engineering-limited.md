## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission for trading of 36,98,168 new equity shares of Rs.10/- each of K&R Rail Engineering Limited with effect from October 26, 2026. These shares were issued pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement between K&R Rail Engineering Limited and KVR Rail Infra Projects Private Limited.

## Key Points

- **Company:** K&R Rail Engineering Limited (Scrip Code: 514360)
- **Security Details:** 36,98,168 equity shares of Rs.10/- each ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **ISIN Number:** INE078T01026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 38109645 - 41807812
- **Date of Allotment:** 30/10/2025
- **Effective Date:** Monday, October 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing notice following a corporate restructuring / scheme of arrangement.

## Compliance Requirements

Lock-in requirements apply in accordance with the exchange observation letter dated December 19, 2013, requiring 75% of the total promoter shareholding post-scheme to be locked in for 1 year from the date of listing (lock-in valid up to October 30, 2027).

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 30, 2025
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** October 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of K&R Rail Engineering Limited following the scheme of arrangement, with standard promoter lock-in enforcement.
