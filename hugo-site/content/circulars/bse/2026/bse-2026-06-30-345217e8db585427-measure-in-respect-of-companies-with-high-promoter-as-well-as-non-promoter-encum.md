## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding measures for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrances as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Specific securities listed in Annexures will attract a minimum 75% margin.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Securities in Annexure II move out of the framework effective July 01, 2026.
- A consolidated list is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of 75% margin on open and new positions for shortlisted securities under the encumbrance framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised margin requirements and ensure appropriate risk management for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Exiting Framework: July 01, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins: July 03, 2026 (on open positions as of July 02, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for listed securities with high encumbrances will impact liquidity and position holding costs for market participants.
