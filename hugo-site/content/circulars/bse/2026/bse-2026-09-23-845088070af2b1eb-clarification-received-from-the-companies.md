## Summary

BSE issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in various scrips. Replies containing clarifications have been received from several companies, while responses from others remain awaited.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from 9 listed companies following observed price/volume movements.
- Replies are awaited from 10 listed companies.
- Aimed at ensuring investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must timely respond to exchange queries regarding significant price and volume fluctuations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 23 Sep 2026

Routine surveillance update with minimal direct market impact beyond company-specific disclosures.
