## Summary

Trading in the equity shares of Injecto Polymers Limited (Scrip Code: 544930) is being transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Injecto Polymers Limited
- Scrip Code: 544930
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the shift in the trading segment for the company's equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Normalizes trading for the stock by moving it from the Trade for Trade segment to the regular rolling segment.
