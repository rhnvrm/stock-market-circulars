## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has notified listed entities about SEBI Circular No. HO/17/11/17(5)2026-DDHS-POD2/I/18791/2026 dated August 14, 2026. The circular establishes a standardized framework for the calculation of Net Distributable Cash Flows for Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs).

## Key Points

- SEBI has introduced a comprehensive framework for calculating Net Distributable Cash Flows for InvITs.
- The circular comes into force with immediate effect.
- Listed entities and InvITs are required to take note of the guidelines and ensure compliance accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of SEBI's specified framework regarding Net Distributable Cash Flows computation for InvITs.

## Compliance Requirements

- InvITs and listed entities must comply with the calculation framework for Net Distributable Cash Flows as prescribed in the SEBI circular.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 14, 2026
- Notice Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date: With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

- Brings greater transparency, uniformity, and clarity in cash flow distributions by InvITs to their unitholders.
