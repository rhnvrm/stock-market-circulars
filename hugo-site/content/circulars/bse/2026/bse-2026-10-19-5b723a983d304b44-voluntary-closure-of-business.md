## Summary

BSE has announced that trading member Kamlesh R. Shah (operating under the trade name Ramanlal D. Shah, Member No. 601) will voluntarily cease doing business in the cash segment effective Wednesday, October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: Kamlesh R. Shah
- Trade Name: Ramanlal D. Shah
- Member Number: 601
- Segment: Cash segment
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational member closure notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members or clients requiring clarifications can contact membership operations at membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date of Closure: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Localized impact affecting only clients and counterparties associated with Member No. 601 in the cash segment.
