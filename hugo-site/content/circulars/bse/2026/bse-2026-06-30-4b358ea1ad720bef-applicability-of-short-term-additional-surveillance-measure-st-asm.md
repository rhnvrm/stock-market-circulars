## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to specific securities satisfying inclusion criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher.
- Measures are effective from July 02, 2026, on all open positions as on July 01, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance actions including enhanced margin obligations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure applicable margins (50% or 100%) are maintained for shortlisted securities under ST-ASM.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 02, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: July 01, 2026
- Notice Date: June 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for listed securities under ST-ASM stages may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
