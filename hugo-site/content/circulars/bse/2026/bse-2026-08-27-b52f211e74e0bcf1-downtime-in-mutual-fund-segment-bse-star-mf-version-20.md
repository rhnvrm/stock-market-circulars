## Summary

BSE has announced a scheduled downtime for the BSE StAR MF Version 2.0 platform due to system maintenance activity.

## Key Points

- Platform: BSE StAR MF Version 2.0
- Date: Saturday, 29th August 2026
- Time: 13:00 hours to 19:00 hours
- Reason: System maintenance activity
- Availability: Platform will not be available during this period

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take note of the downtime and plan their activities accordingly.
- Members connecting via API should inform their technical team or vendor and provide full cooperation.

## Important Dates

- Maintenance Date: 29-Aug-2026 (13:00 to 19:00 hours)

## Impact Assessment

Temporary disruption in mutual fund order placement and related operations via the BSE StAR MF Version 2.0 platform during the maintenance window.
