## Summary

SEBI has approved the conversion of LLP membership of Trimurti Capital Broking LLP into a corporate entity named Trimurti Capital Broking Private Limited (Member No. 6849). Consequently, the trading member's name has been updated on the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name Change: Trimurti Capital Broking LLP is now Trimurti Capital Broking Private Limited.
- Member Number: 6849.
- Regulatory Approval: Approved by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an administrative update reflecting the corporate structure conversion of the trading member.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and clients dealing with the entity should note the updated corporate name for records and correspondence.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 19, 2026.
- Notice Date: October 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Low impact, restricted to administrative and record-keeping updates for the trading member.
