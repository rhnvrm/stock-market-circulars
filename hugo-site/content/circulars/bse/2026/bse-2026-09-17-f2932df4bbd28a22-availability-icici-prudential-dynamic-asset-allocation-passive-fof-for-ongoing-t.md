## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that the ICICI Prudential Dynamic Asset Allocation Passive FoF will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reopening of ICICI Prudential Dynamic Asset Allocation Passive FoF for ongoing transactions.
- Facilities available include subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective date of availability: September 18, 2026.
- Affected schemes include Regular Plan Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A633) and Direct Plan Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A641).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can process transactions for the specified schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with existing operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 17, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and distributors to resume regular transactions and systematic investment/withdrawal plans for the mentioned ICICI Prudential fund scheme on the BSE platform.
