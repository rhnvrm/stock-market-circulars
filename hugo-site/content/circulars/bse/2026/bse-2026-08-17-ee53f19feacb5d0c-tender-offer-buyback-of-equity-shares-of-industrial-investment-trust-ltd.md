## Summary

Industrial Investment Trust Ltd. has fixed a record date for determining the entitlement and names of equity shareholders eligible to participate in the upcoming buyback tender offer.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Industrial Investment Trust Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 501295
- Notice Number: 20260817-18
- Record Date: 18/08/2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: 18/08/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are requested to take note of the record date and ex-entitlement date for processing transactions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 18, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Eligible shareholders holding shares as of the record date can participate in the buyback tender offer. Trading members need to account for the ex-entitlement status starting from the record date.
