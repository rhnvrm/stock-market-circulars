## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by eight companies under ESOP / ESOS have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Tuesday, October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 8 companies have issued further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective date for trading of these securities is October 13, 2026.
- Companies involved include 3i Infotech, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company, ICICI Bank, Kirloskar Industries, Kirloskar Oil Engines, Mahindra Holidays & Resorts India, Polycab India, and VA Tech Wabag.
- All securities are under lock-in status 'NA' (Not Applicable).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure compliance with BSE listing operations and depository guidelines for the newly listed ESOP shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase due to ESOP exercises with minimal immediate market impact.
