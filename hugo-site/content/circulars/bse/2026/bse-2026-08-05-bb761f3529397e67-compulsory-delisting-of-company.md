## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the compulsory delisting of VKJ Infradevelopers Ltd pursuant to an order by the Delisting Committee under SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021. The company, which remained suspended for over 6 months, will be delisted effective August 7, 2026, and moved to the Dissemination Board.

## Key Points

- Company Name: VKJ Infradevelopers Ltd (Scrip Code: 536128).
- Effective Date of Delisting: August 7, 2026.
- Reason: Remaining suspended for more than 6 months.
- Movement: The company will be moved to the Dissemination Board of the Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities cease to be listed and are no longer available for trading on the Exchange platform.
- Promoters and whole-time directors are barred from accessing the securities market or seeking listing for 10 years.

## Compliance Requirements

- Promoters must purchase shares from public shareholders at a fair value determined by an independent valuer.
- Freezing of promoter equity shares, corporate benefits (dividends, rights, bonus, etc.), and transfer restrictions until an exit option is provided.
- Promoters and key managerial personnel are ineligible to become directors of any listed company until the exit option is fulfilled.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 5, 2026
- Effective Date: August 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Public shareholders are provided an exit option through a fair-value purchase by promoters, while promoters and directors face multi-year restrictions from the securities market and directorships of listed entities.
