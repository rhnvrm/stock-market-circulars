## Summary

BSE has issued a revision regarding the group change for exclusively listed companies, notifying that trading in the equity shares of Lotus Chocolate Company Ltd (Scrip Code: 523475) will continue under the 'B' group effective September 25, 2026, in continuation of the earlier Exchange Notice No. 20260918-27 dated September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lotus Chocolate Company Ltd
- Scrip Code: 523475
- Group: 'B' group continuation
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026
- Reference: Revision to Exchange Notice No. 20260918-27 dated September 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is an operational revision concerning the trading group classification of the specified company.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the continued categorization under the 'B' group.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact as it maintains the existing trading group continuation status for the listed company.
