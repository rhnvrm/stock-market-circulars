## Summary

BSE has issued notice no. 20260925-41 dated September 25, 2026, informing trading members that the price bands for 35 scrips have been revised from their existing levels with effect from September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260925-41
- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026
- Total scrips with revised price bands: 35
- Category: Trading (Equity Segment, DOSS Department)

## Regulatory Changes

Price bands for specified scrips (including ACS Technologies Ltd, AI Champdany Industries Ltd, Almondz Global Securities Ltd, and others) are modified to new percentage limits ranging from 1% to 20% to manage market volatility and protect investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure their trading terminals and risk management systems reflect the updated limits effective from September 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date of Price Band Revision: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricts excessive intraday price movements in the affected scrips, enhancing market integrity and risk management for market participants.
