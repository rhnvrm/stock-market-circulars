## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Treasury Bills (T-Bills) scheduled for September 23, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated September 18, 2026. Trading members and direct investors can participate via the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Three Treasury Bills are offered: 91 Day T-Bill (091T231226) for Rs. 9,000 Cr, 182 Day T-Bill (182T240327) for Rs. 8,000 Cr, and 364 Day T-Bill (364T230927) for Rs. 7,000 Cr.
- Bid collection start date: September 21, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards.
- Bid collection end date for direct investors: September 22, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- Bid collection end date for members: September 23, 2026, till 8:00 am.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit bids through the NCB-GSec module on the iBBS web-based system adhering to specified lot sizes and minimum amounts.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** September 21, 2026
- **Direct Investors End Date:** September 22, 2026
- **Members End Date:** September 23, 2026
- **Auction Date:** September 23, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless participation of retail and institutional investors in Government of India Treasury Bill auctions through exchange mechanisms.
