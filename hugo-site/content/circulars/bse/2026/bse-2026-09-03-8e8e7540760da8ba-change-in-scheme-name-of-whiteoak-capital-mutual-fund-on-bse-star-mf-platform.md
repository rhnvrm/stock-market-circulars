## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the change in scheme name for WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect, as communicated by the AMC.

## Key Points

- Existing Scheme Name: WHITEOAK CAPITAL ULTRA SHORT DURATION FUND
- Revised Scheme Name: WHITEOAK CAPITAL ULTRA SHORT TERM FUND
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform
- Effective Date: Immediate (03 Sep 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are advised to take note of the revised scheme name for transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 03 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal impact; purely a nomenclature change for the specified mutual fund scheme.
