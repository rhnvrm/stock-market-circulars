## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants that trading in the equity shares of Triveni Power Transmission Ltd will be permitted under the 'B' group starting from Tuesday, October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Triveni Power Transmission Ltd
- BSE Scrip Code: 544824
- New Trading Group: 'B' group
- Effective Date: October 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational categorization update.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are required to note the change in trading group for positioning and execution.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on general market operations; affects categorization and trading parameters for the specified scrip on the BSE platform.
