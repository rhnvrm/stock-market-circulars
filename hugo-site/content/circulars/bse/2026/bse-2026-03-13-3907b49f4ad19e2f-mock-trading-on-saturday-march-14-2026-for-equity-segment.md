## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Equity segment on Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are advised to connect to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site of the BOLT Plus trading system and web-based applications using the specified technical parameters and connection links.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Equity segment.
- Connection parameters provided for BOLT TWS users via scrip profile icon or Shift+F12 shortcut.
- Primary and Secondary DR IP and Port details provided for 3rd party trading applications through ETI APIs.
- Details for web-based systems including RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, and Extranet access provided.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should verify their disaster recovery connectivity and configuration settings prior to the mock trading session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures business continuity and tests member readiness for disaster recovery failover scenarios.
