## Summary

BSE has updated the list of securities under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The framework applies to securities whose prices are not commensurate with financial health and fundamentals such as earnings, book value, net worth, and market capitalization.

## Key Points

- Securities identified for placing in the GSM Framework along with monitoring dates are provided in Annexure I.
- Securities listed in Annexure II will continue to remain in the GSM framework with their applicable stages.
- Securities listed in Annexure III will move out of the GSM Framework effective September 08, 2026.
- Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) collected on securities in Annexure III will be released effective September 15, 2026.
- Securities under GSM Stage VI based on prior SEBI directions will continue to remain in Stage VI until further directions.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic review for identification of securities for the GSM framework has been carried out based on XBRL submissions by listed companies in conjunction with SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to exercise additional due diligence while trading in these securities either on their own account or on behalf of their clients.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for exclusion from GSM: September 08, 2026
- Release Date for ASD for exiting securities: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in securities newly added or continuing in the GSM framework will be subject to heightened surveillance and collateral/deposit requirements, while exiting securities will see normal trading conditions restored and deposits released.
