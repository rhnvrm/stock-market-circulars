## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Sigma Advanced Systems Limited, issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sigma Advanced Systems Limited (Scrip Code: 532408)
- Security Details: 1,32,56,470 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 337/- (Issue Price: Rs. 347/- per share).
- Allotment Date: 18/07/2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 176239706 to 189496175
- ISIN: INE933B01012
- Lock-in Details: 1,32,56,470 shares are locked-in up to 19/03/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the listing and lock-in details of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Trading: September 10, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: March 19, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of the company with shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
