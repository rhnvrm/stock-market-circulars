## Summary

Effective from October 8, 2026, the equity shares of Dharti Proteins Limited are listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE under the XT Group in the Trade-to-Trade segment. The listing follows an approved Resolution Plan under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), involving capital reduction and preferential allotments to the Successful Resolution Applicant (SRA) and secured financial creditors.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 8, 2026.
- Scrip Code: 531171.
- ISIN: INE248C01021.
- Group: XT (Trade-to-Trade segment).
- Total Securities: 5,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each (including 25,000 shares on capital reduction, 425,000 shares on preferential allotment to SRA/Promoter Group, and 50,000 shares to secured financial creditor).
- Special pre-open session applicable for IPO and other categories of scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of the approved resolution plan under IBC for Dharti Proteins Limited, regularizing and restructuring its equity capital structure.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the trading restrictions under the Trade-to-Trade segment (XT Group) and adherence to SEBI circulars regarding special pre-open sessions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 6, 2026.
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: October 8, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Restoration of trading in equity shares of Dharti Proteins Limited under strict surveillance (Trade-to-Trade segment) following corporate insolvency resolution, providing liquidity to stakeholders while maintaining regulatory safeguards.
