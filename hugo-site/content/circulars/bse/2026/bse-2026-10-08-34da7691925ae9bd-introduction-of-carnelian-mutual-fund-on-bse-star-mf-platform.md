## Summary

BSE has announced the activation of Carnelian Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform effective October 8, 2026. The platform now supports transactions for NRI and minor clients under Carnelian Mutual Fund schemes, with the Carnelian Liquid Fund NFO scheduled for order routing on October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Carnelian Mutual Fund activated on BSE StAR MF Platform from October 8, 2026.
- Transactions for NRI and minor clients are permitted under Carnelian Mutual Fund schemes.
- NRI applications from Canada, United States of America, North Korea, Iran, Myanmar, and Pakistan are not accepted.
- Carnelian Liquid Fund - NFO scheme enabled for order routing from October 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; operational enablement of a new mutual fund on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the restriction on NRI applications from specified countries (Canada, US, North Korea, Iran, Myanmar, Pakistan).

## Important Dates

- October 8, 2026: Activation date of Carnelian Mutual Fund on BSE StAR MF Platform.
- October 12, 2026: Effective date for order routing of Carnelian Liquid Fund - NFO scheme.

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment opportunities on the BSE StAR MF platform by onboarding Carnelian Mutual Fund, enabling broader participation for NRI and minor investors.
