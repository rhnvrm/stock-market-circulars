## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026. Trading members utilizing third-party trading platforms, empanelled vendor systems, or in-house systems via ETI APIs are invited to test their applications and functionalities under exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 24, 2026, for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment.
- Applicable for trading members using third-party platforms, empanelled vendors, or in-house systems via ETI APIs.
- Testing covers functionalities including risk-reduction mode and trading halts.
- No new BOLT TWS Release is deployed for this session.
- Trades executed during the mock session attract no margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or create rights and liabilities.
- Members are encouraged to provide feedback on the session by 03:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this circular pertains exclusively to operational testing and market readiness.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to actively participate in the mock trading session and submit feedback via the provided contact numbers or email addresses by 03:00 pm on the day of the mock session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 24, 2026
- Login: 08:15 am – 09:00 am
- Pre-Open: 09:00 am – 09:08 am
- Order Entry Period: 09:08 am – 09:15 am
- Matching Period: 09:15 am – 02:00 pm
- Continuous Trading: 11:00 am – 11:15 am
- Block Deal Window: 02:00 pm – 02:10 pm
- Feedback Submission Deadline: October 24, 2026, by 03:00 pm

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal and positive, providing members an opportunity to validate trading application robustness and fault tolerance without financial risk.
