## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 700 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-700/2026-27, detailing the schedule for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and pay-in obligations.

## Key Points

- Invites offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction for Rolling Settlement No. DR-700/2026-27.
- Auction offer entry is scheduled on August 25, 2026, from 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m.
- Auction matching will take place from 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction is scheduled for Thursday, August 27, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; reiterates standard operational procedures for auctions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- **Auction Date:** August 25, 2026
- **Auction Offer Entry:** 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. (Aug 25, 2026)
- **Auction Matching:** 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. (Aug 25, 2026)
- **Delivery Statement:** 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m. (Aug 25, 2026)
- **Money Statement:** 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m. (Aug 25, 2026)
- **Pay-in Date:** August 27, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational notice affecting clearing and settlement workflows for members participating in the demat auction.
