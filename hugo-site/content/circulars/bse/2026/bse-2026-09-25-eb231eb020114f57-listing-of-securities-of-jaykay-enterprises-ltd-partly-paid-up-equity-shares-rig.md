## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading of 2,05,71,642 partly paid-up equity shares of Jaykay Enterprises Ltd. issued on a rights basis, effective from Tuesday, September 29, 2026. The shares will be traded in the 'B' Group of Securities under Scrip Code 890240 and ISIN IN9903A01015.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Jaykay Enterprises Ltd.
- Securities: 2,05,71,642 Equity Shares of Re. 1/- each partly paid-up of Re. 0.50/- each issued on Rights Basis.
- Scrip Code: 890240
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: JAYKAYPP
- ISIN: IN9903A01015
- Group: B
- Face Value: Re. 1/-
- Paid-up Value: Re. 0.50/- per share (Re. 0.50/- paid on application, remainder payable on subsequent calls)
- Issue Price: Re. 1/-
- Date of Allotment: 23/09/2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 130287067 - 150858708

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary ISIN No. IN8903A01024 is suspended in terms of SEBI Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012 regarding activation of ISIN for additional issue of shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to note the suspension of the temporary ISIN and the activation of the new ISIN for trading effective September 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 23, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading of partly paid-up rights shares on the BSE platform for investors and trading members, expanding the company's listed capital structure.
