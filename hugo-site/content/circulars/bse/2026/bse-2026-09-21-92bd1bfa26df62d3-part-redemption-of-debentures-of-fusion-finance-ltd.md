## Summary

Fusion Finance Ltd has informed the Exchange regarding the partial redemption of its non-convertible debentures (NCDs), resulting in a reduced face value of Rs. 75,000/- per debenture. Trading members are advised that the debentures will trade with the reduced face value starting September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: FUSION FINANCE Ltd
- Scrip Code / ISIN: 977381 / INE139R07449 (FFL-10.95%-16-12-28-PVT)
- Purpose: Part Redemption of Debentures
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 75,000/- per debenture
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to take note of the reduced face value and ensure trading and settlement systems reflect the updated parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to trading members and investors holding the specified debenture series, ensuring transactions occur at the updated reduced face value.
