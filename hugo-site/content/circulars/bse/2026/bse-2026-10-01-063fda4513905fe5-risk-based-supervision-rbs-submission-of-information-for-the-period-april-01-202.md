## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice reminding all active trading members to submit their Risk Based Supervision (RBS) data for the half-year period from April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026. The deadline for submission is November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mandatory for all active trading members (those who executed at least one trade during the assessment period).
- Submissions must be made exclusively online via BEFS under the 'Risk Based Supervision' menu.
- Physical copies are not required or accepted.
- Data collated is shared with SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Strict penalty and disciplinary framework enforced for delays or non-submission as per Notice No. 20260417-45.
- Monetary penalties: ₹1,500/- per day for Non-QSB and ₹3,000/- per day for QSB for the first 7 calendar days.
- Escalation to ₹2,500/- per day (Non-QSB) and ₹5,000/- per day (QSB) from the 8th to 21st calendar day.
- Further non-submission leads to prohibition of new client registrations, issuance of disablement notices, and eventual trading facility disablement across all segments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Active trading members must upload required details via BEFS by November 30, 2026.
- Refer to Annexure A for details to be provided and Annexure B for the user manual.

## Important Dates

- **Assessment Period:** April 01, 2026 – September 30, 2026
- **Due Date:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members failing to meet the deadline face escalating financial penalties, restriction on new client onboarding, and potential trading suspension, making timely compliance critical.
