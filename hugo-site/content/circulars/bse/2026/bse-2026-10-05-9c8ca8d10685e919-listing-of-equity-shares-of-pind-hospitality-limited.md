## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that equity shares of Pind Hospitality Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in due course under the SME segment.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pind Hospitality Limited
- Scrip Code: 544966
- Symbol: PIND
- ISIN No: INE0RNF01016
- Segment: SME
- Department: DCS-Listing

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard listing notification for a new SME public issue.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the upcoming listing and admission to dealings. Specific dates for listing and commencement of trading will be announced through a separate notice.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 5, 2026
- Listing & Trading Date: To be announced via a separate notice

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public trading and liquidity for equity shares of Pind Hospitality Limited on the BSE SME platform.
