## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd has announced a revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) pertaining to Reliable Ventures India Limited. The bidding period took place from August 14, 2026, to August 28, 2026, under settlement number 2026101.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement No.:** 2026101
- **Bid Period:** 14/08/2026 to 28/08/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download Date:** 03/09/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 04/09/2026
- **Delivery Mechanism:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement No. 2026101.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this notice provides a revised settlement calendar pursuant to previous guidelines for acquisitions through the stock exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must use the specified Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement number 2026101 in the depository system. Stamp duty charges for clients can be verified via the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 14/08/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 28/08/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download:** 03/09/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 04/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants involved in the takeover offer for Reliable Ventures India Limited must update their operational schedules according to the revised obligation download and settlement dates.
