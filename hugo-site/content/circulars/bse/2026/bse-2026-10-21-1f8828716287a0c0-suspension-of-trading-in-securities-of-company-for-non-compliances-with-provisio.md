## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in securities of Mercury Trade Links Ltd (Scrip Code: 512415) effective November 23, 2026, due to non-compliance with Regulation 17(1) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (March 2026 and June 2026).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mercury Trade Links Ltd (Scrip Code: 512415)
- Non-Compliance: Regulation 17(1) of SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015 for March 2026 and June 2026 quarters.
- Suspension Date: Scheduled for November 23, 2026, unless compliance is achieved by November 18, 2026.
- Promoter Shareholding: Entire shareholding of promoters and all other securities in their demat accounts will remain frozen during suspension.
- Trade for Trade: 15 days after suspension, trading will be allowed on a Trade for Trade basis in Z group only on the first trading day of every week for six months upon continued non-compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

Action taken pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated July 11, 2023 (updated January 30, 2026) regarding penal actions and SOP for suspension and revocation of trading.

## Compliance Requirements

The company must comply with all provisions of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 to the satisfaction of the Exchange on or before November 18, 2026, to avoid trading suspension.

## Important Dates

- Compliance Deadline: November 18, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in securities of Mercury Trade Links Ltd will be halted and promoter holdings frozen unless compliance is regularized before the deadline, significantly impacting liquidity and investor access.
