## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of subscriptions for the ICICI Prudential Aggressive Hybrid Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective October 22, 2026, following communication from ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Resumption covers lump sum investments (fresh/additional purchases), switch-ins, and fresh registrations under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) in the IDCW MONTHLY option.
- Applicable scheme: ICICI Prudential Aggressive Hybrid Fund.
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026.
- Systemic changes have been successfully implemented on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors are advised to take note of the resumption and execute transactions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors can resume investments, systematic plans, and switch-ins in the ICICI Prudential Aggressive Hybrid Fund through the BSE StAR MF Platform.
