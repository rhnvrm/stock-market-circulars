## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-717/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: September 18, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction matching timings: September 18, 2026, from 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted offers: September 18, 2026, from 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: September 18, 2026, from 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Auction pay-in is scheduled for Monday, September 21, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should exercise care while punching securities codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- **18 Sep 2026**: Auction offer entry, matching, and report generation.
- **21 Sep 2026**: Auction pay-in at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification affecting trading members participating in settlement DR-717/2026-27 with short-delivered securities.
