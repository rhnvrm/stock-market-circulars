## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II on certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II are shortlisted as per the attached Annexure I.
- Applicable rate of margin for ST-ASM Stage I is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) effective September 18, 2026.
- Applicable rate of margin for ST-ASM Stage II is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) effective September 18, 2026.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on September 17, 2026, and new positions created from September 18, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and implementation of ST-ASM framework rules and margin caps in conjunction with existing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the securities listed in Annexure I starting September 18, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 16 Sep 2026
- **Effective Date:** 18 Sep 2026 (on open positions as on 17 Sep 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted scrips may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities.
