## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Mac Hotels Limited issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mac Hotels Limited
- Scrip Code: 541973
- ISIN: INE004Z01011
- Number of Shares: 7,25,133 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 35/- (Issue Price: Rs. 45/-)
- Allotment Date: 14/05/2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 5631289 to 6356421
- Lock-in Upto: 29/04/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and trading parameters.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Trading: September 10, 2026
- Date of Allotment: May 14, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 29, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Minor equity expansion for Mac Hotels Limited due to preferential allotment.
