## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are instructed to verify and test their Disaster Recovery (DR) site connection parameters for BOLT Plus, ETI APIs, and web-based systems such as RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, and Extranet.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Equity Derivatives segment.
- Members can connect to the DR site of the BOLT Plus trading system using BOLT TWS, 3rd party vendor applications via ETI APIs, or in-house systems.
- Primary DR IP: 10.255.241.16, Port: 15910; Secondary DR IP: 10.255.241.17, Port: 15910.
- Guidelines provided for connecting to exchange web-based applications (RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, Extranet) using designated leased line and internet URLs.
- Instructions included for updating setting.ini files for online trade file applications and Extranet Plus.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A (Operational mock trading exercise)

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members participating in the Equity Derivatives segment must verify their DR connection settings and participate in the mock trading session to ensure business continuity readiness.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures members' trading applications, ETI API connections, and risk management systems function seamlessly during a Disaster Recovery failover scenario.
