## Summary

BSE has announced the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Niraj Cement Structurals Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from July 31, 2026, to August 13, 2026, under settlement number 2026094.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Niraj Cement Structurals Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement Number:** 2026094
- **Bid Start Date:** 31/07/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 13/08/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 18/08/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 19/08/2026

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in both depositories.
- Members and custodians must use the market type '(Takeover)' and settlement number '2026094'.
- Participants must ensure that early pay-in effects are reflected in BSE/ICCL systems prior to the prescribed timelines.
- Members are requested to refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bidding Start Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Bidding End Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members, custodians, and investors participating in the takeover offer must adhere to the specified timelines and early pay-in procedures to ensure smooth settlement of securities.
