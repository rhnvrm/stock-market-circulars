## Summary

BSE has circulated a notice informing members that SEBI has shared a copy of the Gazette Notification regarding the declaration of 1 organization as a 'Terrorist' under Section 35(1)(a) of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UA(P) Act), 1967, and its inclusion in the First Schedule.

## Key Points

- SEBI shared the official Gazette Notification concerning the UA(P) Act, 1967 updates.
- 1 organization has been declared as a 'Terrorist' and added to the First Schedule under Section 35(1)(a).
- The complete notification is provided as Annexure-1 for ready reference.
- Members are instructed to review and comply with the updated statutory list.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of a designated entity in the First Schedule of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the notification and ensure necessary compliance actions in line with anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CFT) frameworks.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 09 Oct 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High importance for compliance departments to cross-verify client databases and ensure no association or transactions involving the newly listed entity.
