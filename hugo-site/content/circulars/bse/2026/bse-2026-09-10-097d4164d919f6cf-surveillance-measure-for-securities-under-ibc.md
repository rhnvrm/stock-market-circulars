## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are placed under Additional Surveillance Measure effective September 11, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities moving out of the IBC framework are listed in Annexure II.
- Consolidated lists and securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% are provided in Annexures III and IV respectively.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under the IBC framework for identified securities.
- Periodic review of securities under the framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the list of securities under surveillance and ensure compliance with applicable price bands and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in affected securities will be subject to surveillance measures, potentially impacting liquidity and price bands for the specified companies.
