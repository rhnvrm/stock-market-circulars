## Summary

Ashutosh Paper Mills Ltd. has fixed a record date for the reduction and consolidation of its equity share capital, pursuant to an order passed by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), New Delhi Bench.

## Key Points

- Record Date for capital reduction: 18/08/2026.
- Suspension/No Dealings effective date: 18/08/2026.
- Paid-up value of each equity share is reduced from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/- per share (50% reduction on a proportionate basis).
- Simultaneously, every two equity shares of Rs. 5/- each are consolidated into one equity share of Rs. 10/- each fully paid-up.
- Total issued and paid-up capital is reduced from Rs. 6,52,54,000 (65,25,400 shares of Rs. 10 each) to Rs. 3,26,27,000 (32,62,700 shares of Rs. 10 each).

## Regulatory Changes

- Capital reduction executed under the scheme approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, New Delhi Bench and in terms of Section 61 of the Companies Act, 2013.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the capital reduction and share consolidation scheme.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 18/08/2026
- Suspension/No Dealings Effective Date: 18/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders will hold fewer shares of the same face value (Rs. 10/-) due to the 50% proportionate reduction followed by a 2:1 consolidation.
