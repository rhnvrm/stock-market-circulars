## Summary

Parshav Vatika LLP, along with Persons Acting in Concert (PACs) K8 Products LLP and Tidagela Ventures Private Limited, has issued a Letter of Offer to acquire up to 50,37,541 equity shares representing 26% of the total voting share capital of Lykis Limited at an offer price of ₹34.50 per fully paid-up equity share pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Lykis Limited
- **Acquirer:** Parshav Vatika LLP
- **PACs:** K8 Products LLP and Tidagela Ventures Private Limited
- **Offer Size:** Up to 50,37,541 Equity Shares (26% of fully diluted voting share capital)
- **Offer Price:** ₹34.50 per equity share for cash
- **Regulatory Framework:** Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is made in compliance with SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.
- The offer is unconditional regarding minimum level of acceptance and has no differential pricing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders wishing to tender their shares must follow the procedures detailed in the Letter of Offer through their respective stockbrokers or the Registrar to the Offer.

## Important Dates

- **Last date for upward revision of Offer Price/Size:** Monday, February 09, 2026
- **Tendering Period:** As specified in the public announcement and schedule of the acquisition window.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit opportunity for public shareholders of Lykis Limited and results in a change in substantial shareholding and control as per the takeover regulations.
