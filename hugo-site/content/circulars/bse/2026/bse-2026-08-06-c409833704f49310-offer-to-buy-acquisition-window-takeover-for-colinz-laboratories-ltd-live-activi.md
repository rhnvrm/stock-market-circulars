## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the live activities schedule for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window - Takeover) of shares of Colinz Laboratories Ltd. The offer period runs from Friday, 7th August 2026 to Thursday, 20th August 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Colinz Laboratories Ltd
- **Security Symbol:** COLINZ
- **ISIN:** INE923C01011
- **Scrip Code:** 531210
- **Offer Size:** Upto 6,55,966 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 54.00 per share
- **Opening Date:** Friday, 7th August 2026
- **Closing Date:** Thursday, 20th August 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00

## Session Timings

- **Bid Offering Period / Modifications / Cancellation:** 7th August 2026 to 20th August 2026, 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.
- **Give-up / Take-up:** 7th August 2026 to 19th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.); last day (20th August 2026) Give-up until 9.15 a.m. - 3.30 p.m., Take-up until 4.00 p.m.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members to ensure UCC of the Investor is registered and/or updated with correct PAN Number and Category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading Members can bid for Investors having shares in demat form.
- Access platforms include iBBS, RTRMS, and Extranet for Files as detailed in the circular.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** 7th August 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** 20th August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity and schedules for trading members and custodians participating in the takeover offer of Colinz Laboratories Ltd.
