## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding all trading members to submit their half-yearly Networth Certificate and Computation of Networth as of September 30, 2026. The general deadline for submission is November 30, 2026, while members availing margin trading facilities must submit by November 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- All trading members must maintain minimum networth at all times and submit half-yearly networth certificates.
- Deadline for general submission is November 30, 2026; deadline for members with margin trading facility is November 15, 2026.
- Dual-registered members (BSE and NSE) are required to submit their networth certificate to NSE on ENIT, while exclusive BSE members continue submissions via the BEFS portal.
- Variable networth requirement continues at 10% of the average daily cash balance of clients retained across segments/exchanges in the previous 6 months.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of existing half-yearly networth submission norms and continuation of variable networth calculations as per previous circulars.
- Mandated specific submission portals based on exchange registration (NSE ENIT vs BSE BEFS).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must submit the Networth Certificate and Computation of Networth as on September 30, 2026, adhering to the prescribed formats.
- Dual members must use NSE ENIT; exclusive BSE members must use the BEFS portal.
- Non-submission or shortfall will attract disciplinary actions and penalties as stipulated in prior circulars.

## Important Dates

- Networth Date: September 30, 2026
- Submission Deadline (Margin Trading Facility Availers): November 15, 2026
- Submission Deadline (General): November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure timely computation and submission of networth certificates through the designated portals to avoid regulatory penalties, shortfall notices, or disciplinary actions by the Exchange.
