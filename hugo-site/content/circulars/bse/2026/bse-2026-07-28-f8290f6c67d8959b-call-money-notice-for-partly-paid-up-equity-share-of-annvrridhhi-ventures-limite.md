## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the activation of the Call Money Module of the IBBS platform for Annvrridhhi Ventures Limited for its second and final call on partly paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- Issuer Name: Annvrridhhi Ventures Limited
- Type of Call: Second and Final Call
- Security Symbol: ANVRDHIFCM
- Issue Opening Date: July 31, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: August 14, 2026
- Call Money Amount: Rs. 4 per share
- Registrar: Purva Sharegistry (India) Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Activation of the Call Money Module of the IBBS platform in accordance with Exchange notice dated January 4, 2021, and SEBI circular dated December 8, 2020.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members, Self-Certified Syndicate Banks (SCSBs), and intermediaries are required to note the bidding period and submit call money details collected during the issue period.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: July 31, 2026 (10:00 AM)
- Issue Closing Date: August 14, 2026 (5:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders holding partly paid-up shares of Annvrridhhi Ventures Limited must pay the second and final call money of Rs. 4 per share within the specified window to avoid default or forfeiture.
