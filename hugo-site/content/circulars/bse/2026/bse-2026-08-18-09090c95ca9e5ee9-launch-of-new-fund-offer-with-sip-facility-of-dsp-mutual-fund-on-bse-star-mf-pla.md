## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) with an SIP facility for DSP Mutual Fund's 'DSP FINANCIAL SERVICES SECTORAL DEBT FUND' on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from August 19, 2026, to August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: DSP Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: DSP Financial Services Sectoral Debt Fund (Regular and Direct Plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options)
- NFO Window: August 19, 2026, to August 24, 2026 (extended till midnight on August 24, 2026)
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 27, 2026
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 26, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: August 19, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: August 24, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: August 26, 2026 (by 6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification for trading members and distributors participating in the BSE StAR MF platform for mutual fund transactions.
