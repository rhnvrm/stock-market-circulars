## Summary

Veranda Learning Solutions Limited is demerging J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited with effect from the start of trading hours on Tuesday, October 06, 2026. Shareholders of Veranda will receive 1 equity share of J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited for every 1 equity share held. A Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS) will be conducted by BSE on October 06, 2026, for price discovery of Veranda Learning Solutions post-demerger.

## Key Points

- Demerger Entity: J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited from Veranda Learning Solutions Limited (Exchange Ticker: 543514, VERANDA).
- Share Entitlement Ratio: 1 equity share of J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited for every 1 equity share held in Veranda Learning Solutions Ltd.
- Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS): Held on Tuesday, October 06, 2026, for price discovery of Veranda post-demerger.
- Index Additions: J.K.Shah Commerce Education Limited will be added to BSE SMALLCAP, BSE ALLCAP, BSE CONSUMER DISCRETIONARY, and BSE MIDSMALLCAP indices at zero price.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of scheme of arrangement resulting in demerger and index restructuring.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and investors must note the demerger effective date, share entitlement, and index inclusion parameters.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date & SPOS Date: Tuesday, October 06, 2026.
- Close of Pre-demerger Basis: Monday, October 05, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects shareholders of Veranda Learning Solutions Limited with the allotment of shares in the demerged entity and requires index tracking adjustments for BSE Smallcap, Allcap, Consumer Discretionary, and Midsmallcap indices.
