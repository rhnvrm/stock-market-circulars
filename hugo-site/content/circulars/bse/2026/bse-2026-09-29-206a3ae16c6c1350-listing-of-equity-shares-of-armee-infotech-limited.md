## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Wednesday, September 30, 2026, the equity shares of ARMEE INFOTECH LIMITED shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: ARMEE INFOTECH LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544951
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: ARMEE
- ISIN No.: INE0TWE01016
- Number of Securities: 31,731,386 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 31,731,386
- Issue Price: Rs. 375/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 365/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 28, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing procedure and guidelines apply.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Wednesday, September 30, 2026.
- Lock-in details and shareholding pattern as per Annexure I and Annexure II of the prospectus.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 28, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of ARMEE INFOTECH LIMITED shares on the BSE main board, providing liquidity to investors.
