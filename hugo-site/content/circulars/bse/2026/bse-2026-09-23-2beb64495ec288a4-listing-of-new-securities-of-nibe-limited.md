## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of NIBE LIMITED (Scrip Code: 535136) effective from September 24, 2026, following the conversion of warrants into equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 2,40,500 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1248/- on a preferential basis.
- **Effective Date:** Trading permitted with effect from Thursday, September 24, 2026.
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 15261274 to 15501773
- **ISIN:** INE149O01018
- **Lock-in Details:** 2,40,500 shares are locked-in up to 30/05/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the listing and lock-in details of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 14/07/2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** 24/09/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 30/05/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action expanding the equity capital base via preferential warrant conversion with no immediate operational disruption.
