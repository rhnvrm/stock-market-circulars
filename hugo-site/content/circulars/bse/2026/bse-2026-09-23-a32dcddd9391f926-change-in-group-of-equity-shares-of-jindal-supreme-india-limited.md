## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced that the equity shares of JINDAL SUPREME (INDIA) LIMITED will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment (B Group), effective Thursday, October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: JINDAL SUPREME (INDIA) LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544935
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Reference to previous notice: Exchange's Notice No. 20260922-24 dated September 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Shift of securities from the compulsory trade-for-trade settlement category to the normal rolling segment, enabling normal rolling settlement for transactions in the company's shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment change and ensure compliance with the rolling settlement guidelines for dealings in the equity shares of JINDAL SUPREME (INDIA) LIMITED starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date of Transfer: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Permits normal trading and settlement in the equity shares under the B Group rolling segment, which can improve liquidity and trading participation compared to the restrictive Trade for Trade segment.
