## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that pursuant to Notice No. 20260825-37 dated August 25, 2026, the ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041 will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and facilities such as SIP, STP, and SWP on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopening for ongoing transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Facilities available include subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Applicable schemes include ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041 - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A617) and ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041 - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF109K1A625).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate transactions for investors starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026
- Reference Notice Date: August 25, 2026 (Notice No. 20260825-37)
- Notice Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to resume transactions, systematic investment plans (SIP), systematic transfer plans (STP), and systematic withdrawal plans (SWP) for the specified ICICI Prudential mutual fund scheme through the BSE StAR MF platform.
