## Summary

The BSE has issued operational guidelines regarding algorithmic market orders following the introduction of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Segment. Trading members are informed that no penalties will be levied on algorithmic market orders placed during the Closing Auction Session (CAS).

## Key Points

- References SEBI circular dated January 16, 2026, and Exchange notice dated January 19, 2026, regarding the introduction of the Closing Auction Session (CAS).
- Penalties for algorithmic market orders during normal market sessions continue to be governed by Exchange Notice No. 20251010-20.
- **Exemption:** No penalty shall be levied on algorithmic market orders specifically during the Closing Auction Session (CAS).

## Regulatory Changes

- Explicitly exempts algorithmic market orders executed during the CAS from surveillance penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members can utilize algorithmic orders during the CAS without incurring penalties related to such orders during this specific session.
- Clarification queries can be sent to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 Jul 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulatory clarity and relief for algorithmic trading desks operating during the newly introduced closing auction window.
