## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of three Treasury Bills (T-Bills) scheduled on August 05, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated July 31, 2026. Trading members and direct investors can participate using the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Securities offered: 91 Day T-Bill (091T041126), 182 Day T-Bill (182T030227), and 364 Day T-Bill (364T050827).
- Bid collection start date: August 03, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability).
- Direct investors bid collection end date: August 04, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- Members bid collection end date: August 05, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- Auction date: August 05, 2026.
- Settlement date: August 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational notification following RBI's announcement for Government of India Treasury Bill auctions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure bids are submitted within the prescribed timelines through the NCB-GSec module on the iBBS platform.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start: August 03, 2026
- Direct Investor Bid End: August 04, 2026 (5:00 PM)
- Member Bid End: August 05, 2026 (8:00 AM)
- Auction Date: August 05, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates smooth participation of non-competitive bidders in the Government of India Treasury Bill auction through the BSE platform.
