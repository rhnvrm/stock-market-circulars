## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the change in scheme names for Axis Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective immediately, as per communication received from the fund house.

## Key Points

- AXIS INCOME PLUS ARBITRAGE ACTIVE FOF has been renamed to AXIS INCOME PLUS ARBITRAGE OMNI FOF.
- AXIS MULTI-ASSET ACTIVE FOF has been renamed to AXIS MULTI-ASSET OMNI FOF.
- Changes are applicable on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for future transactions on the platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 20 Aug 2026
- Effective Date: With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating scheme descriptions and names on trading systems and order entry interfaces.
