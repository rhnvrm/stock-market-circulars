## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing of 18,39,573 new equity shares of Eco Hotels And Resorts Ltd. (Scrip Code: 514402) pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares on a rights basis, effective from Monday, October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Eco Hotels And Resorts Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 514402
- **ISIN:** INE638N01012
- **Securities:** 18,39,573 Equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Re.10/- each issued on rights basis.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 61156791 - 62996363
- **Effective Date:** October 5, 2026
- **Status:** Pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational listing of securities following corporate action.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities with effect from October 5, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increases the paid-up capital base of Eco Hotels And Resorts Ltd. through the successful conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up equity shares.
