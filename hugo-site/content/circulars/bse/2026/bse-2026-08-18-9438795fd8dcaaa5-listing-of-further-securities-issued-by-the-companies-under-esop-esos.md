## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued by 13 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Wednesday, August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 13.
- Effective date for trading of new securities: August 19, 2026.
- Includes companies such as Bandhan Bank Limited, Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Limited, India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited, Interarch Building Solutions Limited, Jana Small Finance Bank Limited, Laurus Labs Limited, Lodha Developers Limited, Nippon Life India Asset Management Limited, Orient Bell Limited, PDS Limited, SBFC Finance Limited, Shadowfax Technologies Limited, and WEP Solutions Limited.
- Securities are issued under ESOP / ESOS with face values ranging across companies (e.g., Re. 1, Rs. 2, Rs. 5, and Rs. 10).

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine listing of shares issued under employee stock option schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the listing and admission of further securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 18, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Incremental equity capital expansion for the respective listed companies due to ESOP/ESOS exercises. Minimal direct market impact.
