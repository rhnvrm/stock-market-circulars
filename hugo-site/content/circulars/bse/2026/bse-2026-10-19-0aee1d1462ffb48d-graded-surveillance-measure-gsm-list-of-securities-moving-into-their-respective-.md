## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of specified securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective October 21, 2026. Trading members are required to comply with heightened surveillance actions depending on the assigned GSM stage.

## Key Points

- Securities moving to GSM Stage I attract a 100% margin rate and a price band of 5% or lower.
- Securities moving to GSM Stage II attract trade-for-trade settlement with a 5% or lower price band and a 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value.
- Securities moving to GSM Stage III attract trade-for-trade settlement, trading permitted only once a week (every Monday or the first trading day of the week), and 100% ASD.
- Securities moving to GSM Stage IV attract trade-for-trade settlement, trading permitted once a week with no upward price movement, and 100% ASD.
- Settlement must be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with strictly no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of enhanced surveillance actions and restrictions under the GSM framework for securities listed in the attached Annexure pursuant to established exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in the affected securities, ensure adherence to trade-to-trade settlement rules without netting off, and collect the mandated margins and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) from buyers.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will experience reduced liquidity, restricted trading windows for higher stages, and higher capital lock-in due to mandatory 100% margins and ASD requirements.
