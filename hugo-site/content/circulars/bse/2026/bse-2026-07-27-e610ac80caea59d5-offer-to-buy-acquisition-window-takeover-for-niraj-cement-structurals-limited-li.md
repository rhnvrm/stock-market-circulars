## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the live activities schedule and operational instructions for the Offer to Buy through the Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Niraj Cement Structurals Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from Friday, 31st July 2026, to Thursday, 13th August 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Niraj Cement Structurals Limited
- **Security Symbol:** NIRAJ
- **ISIN:** INE368I01016
- **Scrip Code:** 532986
- **Offer Size:** Up to 1,55,20,529 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 29.00 per share
- **Opening Date:** Friday, 31st July 2026
- **Closing Date:** Thursday, 13th August 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational notice for a corporate takeover offer.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bids can be placed for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Access urls for iBBS and RTRMS platforms are provided for member participation.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Offering Period:** 31st July 2026 to 13th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- **Give-up / Take-up:** 31st July 2026 to 12th August 2026
- **Take-up (last day of the offer):** 13th August 2026 (up to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless execution of the open offer and acquisition window for market participants and investors dealing in shares of Niraj Cement Structurals Limited.
