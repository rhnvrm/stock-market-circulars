## Summary

In continuation of notice number 20260710-34 dated July 10, 2026, the BSE has announced that debt securities of Reilly Homes Realty Pvt Limited will be available for trading with revised terms and a new ISIN effective from Thursday, August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 976021
- Previous ISIN: INE0YTX08017 (Existing Terms: Unsecured NCDs)
- Revised ISIN: INE0YTX07019 (Revised Terms: Secured NCDs)
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Conversion of security terms from unsecured non-convertible debentures (NCDs) to secured non-convertible debentures (NCDs).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the updated ISIN and security classification for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for holders and trading members dealing with the specific scrip code of Reilly Homes Realty Pvt Limited.
