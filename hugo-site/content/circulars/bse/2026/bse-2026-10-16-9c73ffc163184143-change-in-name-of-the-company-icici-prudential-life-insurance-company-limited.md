## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited has changed its name to ICICI Life Insurance Company Limited. Consequently, the Scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited
- New Company Name: ICICI Life Insurance Company Limited
- Scrip Code: 540133
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): ICICILIFE
- New Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus): ICICILIFE

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of trading system identifiers (Scrip ID and Abbreviated Name) to reflect the new company name.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their internal records and trading terminals with the new Scrip ID and abbreviated name.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Purely operational nomenclature update on the trading platform with no impact on underlying shareholding or fundamentals.
