## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter 'Encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities meeting the criteria will attract a minimum 75% margin.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective September 29, 2026, on all open positions as on September 28, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities in Annexure II are moving out of the framework effective September 25, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- This measure acts in conjunction with other prevailing exchange measures and is subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of minimum 75% margin requirements for companies flagged under high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance framework pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure the applicable minimum 75% margin is maintained for securities included in Annexure I across equity and equity derivatives segments effective September 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Exit effective date (Annexure II): September 25, 2026
- Position date for open positions: September 28, 2026
- Effective date for minimum 75% margin (Annexure I): September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for listed securities with high encumbrance levels, affecting liquidity and position-holding costs for market participants.
