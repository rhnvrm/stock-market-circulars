## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific Treasury Bills (T-bills) due to their maturity, effective October 19, 2026. Trading members are instructed not to deal in the specified securities.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading applies to maturing Treasury Bills as per the redemption schedule.
- Affected securities include Scrip Code 805206 (182T221026, ISIN: IN002026Y030) and Scrip Code 805244 (91TB221026, ISIN: IN002026X164).
- Trading restriction takes effect from October 19, 2026 (DR- 738/2026-2027).

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine operational suspension of matured government securities and treasury bills from the exchange trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no further transactions are executed in the specified T-bills from October 19, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal and routine market impact limited to the settlement and cessation of trading in matured government debt instruments.
