## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-726/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timing: Thursday, Oct 01, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction Matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction will be held on Monday, Oct 5, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- Post-implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment, settlement is executed through the Clearing Member of a Trading Member.
- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement where they have short delivered such securities has been removed.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and Custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should exercise utmost care while entering security codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members should refer to the cutoff rate in the “Auction offer Entry Screen” and check notices regarding Corporate Actions (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split etc.).

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 1, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: October 5, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice regarding demat auction settlement for trading members.
