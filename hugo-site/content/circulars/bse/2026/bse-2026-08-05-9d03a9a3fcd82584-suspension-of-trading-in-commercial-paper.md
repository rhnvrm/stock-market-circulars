## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that various companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of their commercial papers. Members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260805-2
- Notice Date: 05 Aug 2026
- Segment: Debt
- Department: Listing Operations
- Affected issuers include Standard Chartered Securities (India) Limited, Infina Finance Private Limited, Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd, Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, Tata Housing Development Company Limited, and Tata Steel Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational notice regarding corporate actions (redemption record dates).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the listed companies from their respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates and Ex-Dates range from August 6, 2026, to August 14, 2026, depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified commercial papers is suspended temporarily around their record/ex-dates to facilitate smooth redemption processing.
