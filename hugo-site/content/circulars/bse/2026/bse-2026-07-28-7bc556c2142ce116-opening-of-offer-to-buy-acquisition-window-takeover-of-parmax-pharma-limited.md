## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of an open offer to buy through the acquisition window for Parmax Pharma Limited. The acquirers and PACs are offering to acquire up to 23,46,250 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 26.00% of the expanded voting capital) at a price of Rs. 42.80 per equity share payable in cash, pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Parmax Pharma Limited
- **Acquirers & PACs:** Dhiren Chandulal Shah, Sunil Chinubhai Shah, and others acting as PACs.
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 42.80 per Equity Share (payable in cash).
- **Offer Size:** Up to 23,46,250 equity shares (26.00% of expanded voting capital).
- **Offer Period:** Thursday, July 30, 2026, to Wednesday, August 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, and SEBI circulars concerning the acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to Tender-Offers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must participate according to the revised guidelines for tender offers under takeovers, buybacks, and delistings through the acquisition window on the stock exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 28, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public shareholders of Parmax Pharma Limited to tender their shares during the specified acquisition window within the open offer timeline.
