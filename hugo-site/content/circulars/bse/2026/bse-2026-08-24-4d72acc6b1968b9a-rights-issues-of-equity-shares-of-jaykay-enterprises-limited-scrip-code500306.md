## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Jaykay Enterprises Limited has fixed a record date for a rights issue of equity shares. Transactions in the equity shares of the company will take place on an ex-rights basis starting from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Jaykay Enterprises Limited (Scrip Code: 500306)
- Record Date: August 28, 2026
- Ex-Right Basis Date: August 28, 2026
- Rights Issue Details: 3 Equity Shares of Re. 1/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 74/- per share (Issue price of Rs. 75/- per share) for every 19 Equity Shares held.
- Payment Structure: Rs. 37.50 per share payable on application and Rs. 37.50 on one or more subsequent calls.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to note the record date and ex-rights date for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 28, 2026
- Ex-Rights Basis From: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are eligible for the rights issue entitlement, affecting share capital structure and trading valuation on an ex-rights basis.
