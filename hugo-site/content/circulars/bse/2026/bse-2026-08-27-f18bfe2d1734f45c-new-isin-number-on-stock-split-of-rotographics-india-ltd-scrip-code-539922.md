## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the new ISIN number assigned for the sub-divided equity shares of Rotographics (India) Ltd following a stock split.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Rotographics (India) Ltd
- Scrip Code: 539922
- New ISIN: INE364S01022
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity shares from face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-
- Ex-Date: 28-08-2026

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are informed to take note of the new ISIN for trades done on and from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 27, 2026
- Ex-Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth trading operations and settlement under the new ISIN following the share sub-division.
