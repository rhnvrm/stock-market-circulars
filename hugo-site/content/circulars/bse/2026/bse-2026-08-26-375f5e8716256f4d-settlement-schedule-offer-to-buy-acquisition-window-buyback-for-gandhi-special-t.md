## Summary

BSE has issued a settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Buyback) of GANDHI SPECIAL TUBES LIMITED. The bidding window is scheduled to run from August 27, 2026, to September 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Type:** Buyback
- **Settlement No.:** 2026108
- **Bid Start Date:** 27/08/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 02/09/2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 08/09/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 09/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL & NSDL).
- Members and custodians must use Market Type (Buyback) and Settlement No. 2026108 in the depository system.
- Early pay-in securities will be earmarked or blocked in clients' accounts; members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should refer to the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Opening:** August 27, 2026
- **Bid Closing:** September 2, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** September 8, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures structured settlement and smooth execution of the share buyback process for Gandhi Special Tubes Limited through the BSE OTB platform.
