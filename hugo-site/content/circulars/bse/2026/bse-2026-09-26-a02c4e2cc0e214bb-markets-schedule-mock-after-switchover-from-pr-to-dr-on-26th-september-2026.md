## Summary

BSE has initiated the switchover of its trading systems from the Primary (PR) location to the Disaster Recovery (DR) location on September 26, 2026. Trading for Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segments commences in the pre-open session at the DR location from 2:45 PM onwards.

## Key Points

- Switchover of trading systems from PR location to DR location on 26th September 2026.
- Pre-open session at DR location starts from 2:45 PM onwards across multiple segments (Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR).
- Detailed timings for pre-open, continuous trading, special pre-open, periodic call auction, and closing sessions for equity and derivative segments are specified.

## Regulatory Changes

Operational mock session schedule update following infrastructure switchover.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants should take note of the mock session timings and schedule post switchover to the DR site.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Mock Session Date: 26th September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational mock session designed to test disaster recovery preparedness with no direct impact on live trading funds or positions.
