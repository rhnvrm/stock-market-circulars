## Summary

Trading members are informed that specific companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of their commercial papers. Consequently, trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of these companies starting from the specified ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **BOBCARD LIMITED**: Scrip Code 731530, ISIN INE027214944, Record Date 12/08/2026, Ex-Date 12/08/2026.
- **Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited**: Scrip Code 731659, ISIN INE523L14BJ2, Record Date 12/08/2026, Ex-Date 12/08/2026.
- **Nuvama Wealth Management Limited**: Scrip Code 731725, ISIN INE531F14FU5, Record Date 19/08/2026, Ex-Date 19/08/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the commercial papers of the mentioned companies on and from the respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **12/08/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for BOBCARD LIMITED and Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited.
- **19/08/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Nuvama Wealth Management Limited.

## Impact Assessment

Temporary halt in secondary market trading for the specified commercial papers to facilitate smooth redemption processing.
