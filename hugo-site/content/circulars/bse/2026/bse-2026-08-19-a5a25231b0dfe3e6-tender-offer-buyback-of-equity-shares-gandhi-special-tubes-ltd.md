## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members that Gandhi Special Tubes Ltd. has fixed a record date for a tender offer (buyback) of its equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Company Name & Code:** Gandhi Special Tubes Ltd. (BSE Code: 513108, Notice Ref: DR-699/2026-2027)
- **Record Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Ex-Entitlement Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Purpose:** To determine the entitlement and names of equity shareholders eligible to participate in the buyback offer.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Standard corporate action notification).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the record date and ex-entitlement date for proper settlement and execution.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Ex-Entitlement Date:** August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders holding shares as of the record date will be eligible to participate in the buyback tender offer. Trading members need to adjust positions accordingly keeping the ex-date in view.
