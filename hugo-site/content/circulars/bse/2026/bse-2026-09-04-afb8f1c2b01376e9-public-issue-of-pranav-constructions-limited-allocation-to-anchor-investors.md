Trading members of the Exchange are informed that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the Public Issue of Pranav Constructions Limited, 67,94,034 Equity Shares have been subscribed at Rs. 124/- per equity share.

- Company Name: Pranav Constructions Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investors Portion)
- Number of Shares Subscribed: 67,94,034 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 124/- per equity share
- Date of Notice: September 04, 2026
