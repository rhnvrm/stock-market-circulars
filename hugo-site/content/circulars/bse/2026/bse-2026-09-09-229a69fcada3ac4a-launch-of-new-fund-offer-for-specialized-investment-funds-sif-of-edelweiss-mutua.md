## Summary

Edelweiss Mutual Fund is launching the Altiva Equity Long-Short Fund (SIF) NFO along with an SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from September 10, 2026, to September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Altiva Equity Long-Short Fund (SIF)
- NFO Period: September 10, 2026, to September 24, 2026 (extended till midnight for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 01, 2026.
- Includes multiple plans (Regular and Direct) covering Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options across ISINs INF754K30219, INF754K30227, INF754K30235, INF754K30177, INF754K30185, and INF754K30193.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 29, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on September 24, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 10, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: September 24, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: September 29, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates trading and subscription of Edelweiss Mutual Fund's new Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) offering for market participants and investors on the BSE StAR MF platform.
