## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading in these debentures/bonds will be conducted with reduced face values effective from the specified ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Covers part redemption and interest payment for NCDs/bonds of 7 companies: Ambit Finvest Private Limited, Suruchi Properties Private Limited, Keertana Finserv Limited, Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited, Greaves Finance Limited, Light Microfinance Private Limited, and Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited.
- Trading will take place with reduced face values starting from the respective ex-dates (October 9, 2026 or October 12/13, 2026).
- Record dates are set between October 10 and October 13, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for settlement and trading in the respective non-convertible debentures and bonds.
