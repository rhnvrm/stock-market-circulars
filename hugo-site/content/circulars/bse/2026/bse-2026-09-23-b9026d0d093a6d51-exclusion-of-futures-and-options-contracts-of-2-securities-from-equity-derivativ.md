## Summary

BSE has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts for two securities—Indian Energy Exchange Ltd (IEEX) and Indian Renewable Energy Development Agency Ltd (IREDA)—from the Equity Derivatives Segment, pursuant to SEBI eligibility criteria guidelines.

## Key Points

- Futures and Options contracts for Indian Energy Exchange Ltd and Indian Renewable Energy Development Agency Ltd are discontinued from the Equity Derivatives segment starting November 27, 2026.
- No new expiry month contracts will be generated for these securities.
- Existing unexpired contracts for September 2026, October 2026, and November 2026 will expire on their respective normal expiry dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Discontinuation executed in reference to SEBI Circular Ref. No: SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-2/P/CIR/2024/116 dated August 30, 2024, regarding stock eligibility criteria in the derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the phase-out schedule and ensure no new positions are taken for new expiry months beyond the permitted timeline.

## Important Dates

- **September 24, 2026:** Expiry of September 2026 contracts.
- **October 29, 2026:** Expiry of October 2026 contracts.
- **November 26, 2026:** Expiry of November 2026 contracts (final unexpired contracts).
- **November 27, 2026:** Complete cessation of F&O contracts availability for trading.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding derivative positions in IEX and IREDA must square off or hold contracts until their respective final expiry dates. Liquidity in these contracts will taper off leading up to November 26, 2026.
