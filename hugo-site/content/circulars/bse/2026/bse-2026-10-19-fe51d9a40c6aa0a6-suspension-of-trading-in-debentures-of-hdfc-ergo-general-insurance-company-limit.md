## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debentures (NCDs) of HDFC ERGO General Insurance Company Limited. The company has fixed the record date for full redemption through the exercise of a call option along with final interest payment.

## Key Points

- Company Name: HDFC ERGO General Insurance Company Limited
- Security Details: HDFCEGICL-7.10%-9-11-31-PVT, ISIN: INE225R08014, Scrip Code: 973567, Notice Ref: DR- 741/2026-2027
- Record Date: October 24, 2026
- No Dealings / Suspension From: October 23, 2026
- Purpose: Exercise of Call Option for Redemption of NCDs in full along with final interest due on November 9, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate debt redemption action.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of the company from the specified date onwards.

## Important Dates

- No Dealings From: October 23, 2026
- Record Date: October 24, 2026
- Final Interest / Redemption Date: November 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified NCDs of HDFC ERGO General Insurance Company Limited will cease as the securities are being redeemed in full.
