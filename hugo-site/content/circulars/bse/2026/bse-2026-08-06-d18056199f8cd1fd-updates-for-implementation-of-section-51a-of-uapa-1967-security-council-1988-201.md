## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about SEBI's press release concerning the amendment of 05 entries in the UNSC 1988 Taliban Sanctions List under Section 35 of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260806-25
- Notice Date: 06 Aug 2026
- Category: Compliance (General / DOSS)
- Subject: Updates for Implementation of Section 51A of UAPA, 1967 regarding Security Council 1988 (2011) Taliban Sanctions List - Amendment in 05 entries.
- SEBI shared a copy of the press release regarding the amendments under Section 35 of the UAPA.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amendment of 05 entries in the UNSC 1988 Taliban Sanctions List.
- Implementation requirements pursuant to Section 51A of the UAPA, 1967 and Section 35 of the UAPA.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the updates and ensure necessary actions and compliances.
- Verification of client databases against the updated sanctions list.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 06 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures adherence to statutory anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing (AML/CFT) frameworks by market participants.
