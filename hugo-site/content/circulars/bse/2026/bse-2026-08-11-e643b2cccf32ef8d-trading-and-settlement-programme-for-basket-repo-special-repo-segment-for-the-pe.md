## Summary

BSE has issued the trading and settlement programme for the Basket Repo and Special Repo segment covering the period from September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026, under Notice No. 20260811-11.

## Key Points

- Covers trading and settlement schedules for the basket repo and special repo segments.
- Applicable period spans from September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Issued by the BSECL department.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are required to adhere to the notified settlement calendar and operational timelines for repo transactions during the specified period.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Start Date:** September 1, 2026
- **Effective End Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables smooth operational planning and settlement cycle execution for participants trading in the basket repo and special repo segments.
