## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued Notice No. 20260812-46 announcing a group transfer for Inox Green Energy Services Ltd (Scrip Code: 543667). Effective from August 14, 2026, the equity shares of the company will be permitted for trading under the "A" Group, transferring from the restricted "T" Group (Trade-for-Trade). This movement follows the exchange's prior Notice No. 20260728-46 dated July 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Inox Green Energy Services Ltd
- **BSE Scrip Code:** 543667
- **NSE Ticker:** INOXGREEN
- **Group Transition:** Movement from "T" Group (Trade-for-Trade) to "A" Group (Active / highly liquid segment).
- **Effective Date:** August 14, 2026.
- **Preceding Reference:** Continuation of BSE Notice No. 20260728-46 dated July 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No macro-level regulatory changes are introduced by this circular. This is an operational reclassification of a specific equity security per the exchange's established group movement and surveillance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members:** Brokerages and trading members must update their internal systems to map Scrip Code 543667 (INOXGREEN) to "A" Group from the start of trading on August 14, 2026.
- **Settlement & Margining:** Intraday netting, leverage, and standard settlement rules for "A" Group securities will now apply, replacing the 100% upfront margin and delivery-only requirements of the "T" Group.

## Important Dates

- **July 28, 2026:** Preceding notice date (Notice No. 20260728-46).
- **August 12, 2026:** Notice issuance date.
- **August 14, 2026:** Effective date of the group change.

## Impact Assessment

- **Liquidity & Volume:** Highly positive for the target stock. Removing Trade-for-Trade restriction allows speculative/intraday trading, significantly increasing liquidity, trading volumes, and easing transaction executions.
- **Systemic Impact:** Extremely low. This is a routine operational migration for a single stock and has no broader market structure implications.
