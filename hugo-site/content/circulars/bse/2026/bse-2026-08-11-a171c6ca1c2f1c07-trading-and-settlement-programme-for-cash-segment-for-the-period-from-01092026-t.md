## Summary

BSE has issued the trading and settlement schedule for the cash segment covering the period from September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026, encompassing both T+1 and T+0 settlement cycles.

## Key Points

- Covers trading and settlement schedules for September 2026.
- Applicable to the cash segment under T+1 and T+0 settlement cycles.
- Refers to attachments detailing specific settlement dates and cycles (CASH0926).

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must align their fund and security pay-in/pay-out operations with the published schedule for September 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Period: 01.09.2026 to 30.09.2026
- Notice Date: 11.08.2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for market participants during September 2026.
