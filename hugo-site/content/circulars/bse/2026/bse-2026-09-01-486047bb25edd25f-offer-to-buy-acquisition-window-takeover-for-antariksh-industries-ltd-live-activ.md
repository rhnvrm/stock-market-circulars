## Summary

 BSE has issued a circular outlining the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy through the Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Antarkish Industries Ltd. The bidding period is scheduled from Wednesday, September 2, 2026, to Wednesday, September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Antarkish Industries Ltd
- **Security Symbol:** ANTARIKSH
- **ISIN:** INE825M01017
- **Scrip Code:** 501270
- **Offer Size:** Up to 6,31,785 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 86.00 per share
- **Opening Date:** Wednesday, 2nd September 2026
- **Closing Date:** Wednesday, 16th September 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- None specific to regulations; standard operational schedule for takeover acquisition window.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Offering Period:** 2nd September 2026 to 16th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- **Give-up / Take-up:** 2nd September 2026 to 15th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- **Give-up (last day of offer):** 16th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- **Take-up (last day of offer):** 16th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

- Direct impact on shareholders and trading members participating in the takeover offer for Antarkish Industries Ltd. Ensures smooth execution of the acquisition window through the iBBS and RTRMS platforms.
