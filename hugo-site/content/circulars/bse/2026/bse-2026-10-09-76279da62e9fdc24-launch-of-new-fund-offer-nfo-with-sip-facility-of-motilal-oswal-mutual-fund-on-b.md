## Summary

Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund is launching its New Fund Offer (NFO) for the **Motilal Oswal Nifty Midcap150 Plus 8-13 Yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund** (Regular and Direct Plans - Growth) along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Motilal Oswal Nifty Midcap150 Plus 8-13 Yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund (Regular Plan - Growth: INF247L01HR4; Direct Plan - Growth: INF247L01HQ6).
- **NFO Window:** October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026. The NFO remains extended till midnight on October 26, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases.
- **SIP/XSIP Registrations:** Must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on October 26, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 30, 2026, as confirmed by Motilal Oswal AMC.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational circular for mutual fund NFO launch.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Payment initiation remains pending in the system until 3:00 PM on the last day of funds pay-out or T+5 day, whichever is earlier.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 30, 2026.
- Cheque, OTM, and switch transactions are accepted during the NFO subject to timely fund receipt before allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** October 12, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** October 26, 2026 (Midnight for Lumpsum; 3:00 PM for SIP/XSIP authentication)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Clear Funds Deadline (BSE Clearing Limited):** 6:00 PM on October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members an opportunity to participate in the Motilal Oswal Nifty Midcap150 Plus 8-13 Yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund NFO through the BSE StAR MF Platform with clearly defined cut-off and settlement instructions.
