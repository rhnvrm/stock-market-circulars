## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Deepa Jewellers Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange effective from Tuesday, September 8, 2026, under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Deepa Jewellers Limited
- Scrip Code: 544903
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: DEEPA
- ISIN No.: INE1M2N01020
- Face Value: Rs. 2/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 177/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 2/- and premium of Rs. 175/-)
- Total No. of Securities: 96,124,293 Equity Shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 96124293
- Market Lot: 1
- Date of Allotment: September 4, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be a part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action has been executed to have the lock-in period marked in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 4, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Deepa Jewellers Limited shares on the BSE exchange.
