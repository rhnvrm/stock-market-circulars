## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants that the equity shares of Burnpur Cement Limited (BSE Scrip Code: 532931) will be shifted and permitted to trade under the 'B' group.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Burnpur Cement Limited
- BSE Scrip Code: 532931
- Action: Trading permitted under 'B' group
- Effective Date: Tuesday, August 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of the company's equity shares to the 'B' trading group category.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members should take note of the group change for order entry and risk management.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact; standard administrative re-grouping of securities.
