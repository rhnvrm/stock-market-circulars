## Summary

BSE has announced the inclusion of securities under Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II, effective August 26, 2026, based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Applicable rate of margin for ST-ASM Stage I securities shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective August 26, 2026.
- Applicable margin rate for ST-ASM Stage II securities shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective August 26, 2026.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on August 25, 2026, and new positions created from August 26, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance margins under the ST-ASM framework for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100% as applicable) for open and new positions in the short-listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for specified securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the short term.
