## Summary

SEBI vide its Final Order No. QJA/BS/MIRSD/MIRSD-SEC-6/32674/2026-27 dated August 21, 2026, has passed an order against M/s Trade Nexa Research Investment Advisor (Prop - Minakshi Asavani), SEBI Registration No. INA000009083. The entity is prohibited from taking new clients for a period of one month from the date of the SEBI order.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: M/s Trade Nexa Research Investment Advisor (Prop - Minakshi Asavani)
- SEBI Registration No.: INA000009083
- Restriction: Prohibited from taking new clients for a period of one month.
- Order Date: August 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action taken by SEBI restricting business operations by prohibiting the onboarding of new clients.

## Compliance Requirements

All Investment Advisers are requested to take note of the order and ensure compliance with regulatory directions.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 21, 2026
- Notice Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The named investment adviser is barred from expanding its client base for the specified duration.
