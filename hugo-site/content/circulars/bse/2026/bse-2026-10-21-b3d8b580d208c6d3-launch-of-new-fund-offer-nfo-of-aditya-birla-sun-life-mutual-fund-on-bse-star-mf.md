## Summary

Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Aditya Birla Sun Life Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VB (136 Days) on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from October 22, 2026, to October 26, 2026, with tentative allotment scheduled for October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: Aditya Birla Sun Life Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VB (136 Days) (Regular and Direct plans available in Growth and IDCW options).
- NFO Window: October 22, 2026, to October 26, 2026 (extended till 6:00 PM on October 26, 2026 for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 27, 2026.
- ISINs include INF209KC1860, INF209KC1886, INF209KC1878, INF209KC1837, INF209KC1852, and INF209KC1845.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 26, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments are subject to clear funds receipt by 6:00 PM on October 26, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: October 22, 2026
- NFO End Date / Clear Funds Deadline: October 26, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the new debt fund series via the BSE StAR MF platform with clear operational timelines.
