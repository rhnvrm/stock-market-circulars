## Summary

IBL Finance Limited has officially withdrawn its ongoing public issue of Secured, Rated, Listed, Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). The withdrawal is effective from October 22, 2026, and is attributed to certain difficulties preventing the company from achieving the mandatory minimum subscription requirement.

## Key Points

- IBL Finance Limited withdrew its ongoing public issue of Secured, Rated, Listed, Redeemable NCDs.
- The withdrawal is due to the company's inability to achieve mandatory minimum subscription requirements.
- Effective date of withdrawal is October 22, 2026.
- Merchant Banker to the issue submitted the requisite undertaking regarding the withdrawal.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; procedural withdrawal of a debt public issue pursuant to regulatory guidelines on minimum subscription requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

The company and merchant banker must complete formal cancellation/withdrawal procedures, inform applicants/investors as per applicable regulations, and release any blocked funds or proceed with necessary compliance reporting.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date of Withdrawal: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts investors and market participants who subscribed or intended to subscribe to the NCD issue; reflects prevailing market demand conditions for SME/corporate debt issuances.
