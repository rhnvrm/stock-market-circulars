## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, August 26, 2026, the equity shares of Gaja Alternative Asset Management Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: Gaja Alternative Asset Management Limited
- Scrip Code: 544885
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: GAJA
- ISIN No.: INE18UN01038
- Number of Securities: 141,010,230 Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 160/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 5/- and premium of Rs. 155/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 24, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities of the company will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Wednesday, August 26, 2026.
- Corporate action executed for lock-in period marking in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 24, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members can commence trading in the equity shares of Gaja Alternative Asset Management Limited starting August 26, 2026, under the 'B' Group of Securities.
