## Summary

Tuni Textile Mills Ltd. has fixed the record date for its upcoming rights issue of equity shares. Trading members are informed that transactions in the equity shares of the company will be on an ex-rights basis starting from 16/09/2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Tuni Textile Mills Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 531411
- Settlement No.: DR- 716/2026-2027
- Record Date: 16/09/2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: 16/09/2026
- Rights Details: Issue of 15 Equity Shares of Re.1/- each for cash at par on rights basis for every 04 Equity Shares held.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are requested to take note of the record date and ex-rights trading schedule for Tuni Textile Mills Ltd.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 16/09/2026
- Ex-Rights Date: 16/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are eligible for the rights issue entitlement of 15 equity shares for every 4 shares held at par value (Re. 1/- each). Market participants need to account for the ex-rights price adjustment.
