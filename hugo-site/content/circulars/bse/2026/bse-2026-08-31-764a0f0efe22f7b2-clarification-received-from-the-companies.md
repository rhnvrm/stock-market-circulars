## Summary

The BSE has issued a surveillance notice concerning significant price and volume movements observed in several scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the concerned companies. Replies have been received from 10 companies, while replies from another 11 companies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- **Clarifications Received:** Replies were received from companies including Amkay Products Ltd, Exicom Tele-Systems Ltd, IKIO Technologies Ltd, Imagicaaworld Entertainment Ltd, INOX India Ltd, Moschip Technologies Ltd, Network People Services Technologies Ltd, Newgen Software Technologies Ltd, Precision Wires India Ltd, and Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Ltd.
- **Clarifications Awaited:** Replies are still awaited from 11 companies including Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Garg Furnace Ltd, Infobeans Technologies Ltd, iStreet Network Ltd, Mac Hotels Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Mayur Leather Products Ltd, PVP Ventures Ltd, and Triumph International Finance India Ltd.
- **Purpose:** To maintain market integrity, ensure transparency, and provide investors with relevant information regarding unusual market activity.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities flagged for significant price/volume movements are required to submit clarifications to the Exchange in a timely manner.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 31 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and protects investors by highlighting scrips experiencing unusual trading activity and tracking company responses.
