## Summary

Trading members are informed that equity shares of Technocrats Plasma Systems Limited (SME IPO, Scrip Code: 544877) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Friday, September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Technocrats Plasma Systems Limited
- Segment: SME IPO
- Scrip Code: 544877
- Transfer From: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- Transfer To: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: September 4, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The equity shares are being shifted from the Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment following standard exchange procedures post-listing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to take note of the segment change for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date: September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normal trading in the rolling segment will commence for the scrip, improving liquidity and trading convenience for market participants.
