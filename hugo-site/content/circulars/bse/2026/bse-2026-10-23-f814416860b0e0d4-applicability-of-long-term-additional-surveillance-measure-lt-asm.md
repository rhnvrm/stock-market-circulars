## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework on various securities pursuant to its revised guidelines. The measures include 100% margins, movement to higher stages, group transfers, and price band restrictions.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. October 28, 2026, on all open positions as on October 27, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM framework and Lower Price Band applicable w.e.f. October 26, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Securities continuing in LT-ASM framework transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 28, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- Securities moving out of LT-ASM framework effective October 26, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework attached as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the revised LT-ASM framework, combining 100% margin requirements, price band restrictions, and group migrations for shortlisted securities based on XBRL submissions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure adherence to 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for applicable securities and account for price band and group transfer changes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Band / Movement / Removal: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins / Group Transfers: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in specified securities will be subject to stricter surveillance controls, higher margin requirements, and modified price bands, impacting liquidity and trading operations for those scrips.
