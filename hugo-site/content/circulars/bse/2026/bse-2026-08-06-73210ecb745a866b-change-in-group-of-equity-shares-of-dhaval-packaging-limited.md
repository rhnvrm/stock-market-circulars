## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of DHAVAL PACKAGING LIMITED from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective from Thursday, August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: DHAVAL PACKAGING LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544854
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of trading segment from MT Group to M Group following previous exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members need to take note of the segment shift for dealings in the equity shares of the company.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minor operational transition for trading members dealing in the equity shares of DHAVAL PACKAGING LIMITED.
