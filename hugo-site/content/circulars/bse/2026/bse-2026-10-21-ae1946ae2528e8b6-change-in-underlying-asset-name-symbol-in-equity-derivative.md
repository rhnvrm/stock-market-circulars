## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing a change in the underlying asset name and symbol for ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Ltd within the equity derivatives segment, taking effect from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code (Equity Segment): 540133
- Old Underlying Scrip Name: ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Ltd
- New Underlying Scrip Name: ICICI Life Insurance Ltd
- Existing Underlying Asset Name (Column 5 in Contract Master Files): ICICIPRULI
- Revised Underlying Asset Name (Column 5 in Contract Master Files): ICICILIFE

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of contract master files in the equity derivatives segment to reflect the updated underlying asset name and symbol.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that their systems and contract master files are updated accordingly. There are no changes to any other parameters in the said files.

## Important Dates

- End of Day Contract Master Files Update: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; trading members and automated trading systems must reference the updated derivative contract symbol from ICICIPRULI to ICICILIFE.
