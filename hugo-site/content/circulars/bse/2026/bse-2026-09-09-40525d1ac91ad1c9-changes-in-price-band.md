## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the price bands for 18 scrips have been revised from their existing levels, effective September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 18 scrips affected by price band revisions.
- Effective date for the new price bands is September 10, 2026.
- Price band percentages vary between 2%, 5%, and 10% depending on the scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of price bands for specified equities as part of ongoing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while placing orders in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the affected scrips will be subject to the newly established price limits, mitigating extreme volatility.
