## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units of Zerodha Nifty Next 100 ETF, an open-ended scheme replicating/tracking the Nifty Next 100 Total Return Index (TRI), effective Wednesday, October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name**: Zerodha Nifty Next 100 ETF
- **Mutual Fund**: Zerodha Mutual Fund
- **Investment Manager**: Zerodha CASE Asset Management Private Limited
- **Scrip Code**: 544986
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name**: NXT100CASE
- **ISIN**: INF0R8F01257
- **Group**: B
- **Market Lot**: 1 unit (traded in dematerialized form only)
- **Face Value**: Rs. 10.00 per unit
- **Issue Price**: Rs. 9.99 per unit
- **Date of Allotment**: 08/10/2026
- **Number of Units**: 5,533,110 units
- **Market Makers**: Kanjalochana Finserve Private Limited, East India Securities Limited, iRage Broking Services LLP, Fyres Securities Private Limited, Dolat Algotech Limited, Parwati Capital Private Limited, Motilal Oswal Financial Services Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notice under SEBI guidelines for mutual fund units.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure units are traded in dematerialized form in unit market lots as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment**: October 8, 2026
- **Listing/Commencement of Trading Date**: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to buy and sell units of Zerodha Nifty Next 100 ETF on the BSE trading platform.
