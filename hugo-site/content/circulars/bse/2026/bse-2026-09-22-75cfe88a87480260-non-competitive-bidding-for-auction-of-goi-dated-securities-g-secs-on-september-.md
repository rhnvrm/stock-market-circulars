## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled on September 25, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security offered: 6.94% GS 2036 (Symbol: 694GS36S26, Issue Size: Rs. 34,000 Cr).
- Bidding Platform: NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system (https://ibbs.bseindia.com).
- Bid Collection Start Date: September 22, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability).
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): September 24, 2026, till 5.00 pm.
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): September 25, 2026, till 8.00 am.
- Auction Date: September 25, 2026.
- Settlement Date: September 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Conducted under established non-competitive bidding framework for Government Securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit bids through the NCB-GSec module within the specified timelines and ensure compliance with minimum subscription units and amounts.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start: September 22, 2026
- Direct Investor Bid End: September 24, 2026
- Member Bid End: September 25, 2026
- Auction Date: September 25, 2026
- Settlement Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates non-competitive participation in Government Securities auctions for eligible investors through trading members.
