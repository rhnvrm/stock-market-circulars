## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision in terms and ISIN for debt securities of JSW JFE Electrical Steel Nashik Private Ltd, effective from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 976360
- Existing ISIN: INE1EY607018 (Secured NCDs)
- Revised ISIN: INE1EY608024 (Unsecured NCDs)
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Mode of Security changed from Secured NCDs to Unsecured NCDs.
- ISIN updated to INE1EY608024.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to note the revised terms and ISIN for trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading members holding or trading the specified debt securities.
