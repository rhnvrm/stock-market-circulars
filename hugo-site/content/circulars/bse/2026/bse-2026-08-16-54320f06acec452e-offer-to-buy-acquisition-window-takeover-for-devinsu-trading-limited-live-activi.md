## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the live activities schedule and operational parameters for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for shares of Devinsu Trading Limited.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** DEVINSU TRADING LIMITED
- **Security Symbol:** DEVITRD
- **ISIN:** INE07LH01016
- **Scrip Code:** 512445
- **Offer Size:** Up to 1,52,880 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 355.00 per share
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00
- **Opening Date:** Monday, 18th August 2026
- **Closing Date:** Monday, 31st August 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure that the UCC of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bids can be placed for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Access URLs for iBBS, RTRMS, and Extranet are provided for member participation and file retrieval.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Offering Period:** 17th August 2026 to 31st August 2026
- **Bid Modifications/Cancellation:** 17th August 2026 to 31st August 2026
- **Give-up/Take-up (Last Day):** 31st August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the smooth execution and settlement of the takeover offer for Devinsu Trading Limited through the exchange acquisition window.
