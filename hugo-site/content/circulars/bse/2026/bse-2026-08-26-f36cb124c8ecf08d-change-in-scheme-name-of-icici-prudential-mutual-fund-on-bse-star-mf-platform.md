## Summary

ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund has communicated a change in scheme names for 18 of its schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Total Schemes Affected: 18 schemes, including equity, debt, hybrid, and specialized funds.
- Examples include renaming 'ICICI PRUDENTIAL DIVIDEND YIELD EQUITY FUND' to 'ICICI PRUDENTIAL DIVIDEND YIELD FUND' and 'ICICI PRUDENTIAL MIDCAP FUND' to 'ICICI PRUDENTIAL MID CAP FUND'.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for future transactions and reporting on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; purely administrative nomenclature update for mutual fund schemes.
