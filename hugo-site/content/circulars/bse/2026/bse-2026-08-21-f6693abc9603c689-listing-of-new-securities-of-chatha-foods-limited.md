## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 1,03,000 new equity shares of Chatha Foods Limited (Scrip Code: 544151) effective from August 24, 2026, following the conversion of warrants issued on a preferential basis to promoters.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Chatha Foods Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544151
- **ISIN:** INE0AH901011
- **Securities Issued:** 1,03,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 117/- (Issue Price: Rs. 127/- per share) pursuant to warrant conversion.
- **Allottees:** Promoters (preferential basis).
- **Effective Date:** August 24, 2026.
- **Ranking:** New equity shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 24081880 to 24184879
- **Lock-in Details:** 1,03,000 shares are under lock-in up to March 6, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the new securities being admitted to dealings and the associated lock-in periods.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** June 22, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** March 6, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital due to preferential warrant conversion by promoters, with shares subject to standard lock-in stipulations.
