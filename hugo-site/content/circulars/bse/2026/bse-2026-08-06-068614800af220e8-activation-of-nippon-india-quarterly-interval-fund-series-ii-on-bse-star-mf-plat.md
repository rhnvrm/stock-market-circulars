## Summary

Nippon India Mutual Fund is offering the Nippon India Quarterly Interval Fund - Series II during the Specified Transaction Period (STP) from August 07, 2026, to August 10, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Specified Transaction Period (STP): August 07, 2026, to August 10, 2026.
- Accepted transactions: Subscription and Redemption during normal business hours.
- Payment restrictions: MICR Cheque, Nodal, and OTM modes of payment are not accepted during the STP.
- Cut-off time & NAV applicability: As per scheme cut-off timings on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Clear funds must be received by the Exchange on the last date of the Specified Transaction Period before 2:30 PM, failing which transactions are liable for rejection.

## Important Dates

- STP Start Date: August 07, 2026
- STP End Date: August 10, 2026 (Clear funds deadline by 2:30 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and distributors to execute subscriptions and redemptions for the specified debt schemes of Nippon India Mutual Fund within the designated transaction window.
