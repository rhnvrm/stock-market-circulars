## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the rights issue of equity shares by Shanti Gold International Limited. The company has fixed the record date as August 6, 2026, and transactions on an ex-rights basis will commence from the same date under the rolling settlement segment.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shanti Gold International Limited (Scrip Code: 544459)
- Record Date: August 06, 2026
- Ex-Right Basis From: August 06, 2026
- Purpose: Issue of 19 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each at a premium of Rs. 205/- per share on a rights basis for every 295 equity shares held.
- Issue Price: Full amount of Rs. 215/- per share payable on application.
