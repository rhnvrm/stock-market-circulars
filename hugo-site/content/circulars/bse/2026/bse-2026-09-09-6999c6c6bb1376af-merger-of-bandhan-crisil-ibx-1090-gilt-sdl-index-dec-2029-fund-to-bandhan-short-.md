## Summary

Bandhan Mutual Fund has announced the merger of the BANDHAN CRISIL-IBX 10:90 GILT + SDL INDEX – DEC 2029 FUND (Transferor Scheme) into the BANDHAN SHORT DURATION FUND (Transferee Scheme). The merger takes effect after the close of business hours on September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Transferor Scheme: BANDHAN CRISIL-IBX 10:90 GILT + SDL INDEX – DEC 2029 FUND
- Transferee Scheme: BANDHAN SHORT DURATION FUND
- Effective Date: After the close of business hours on September 11, 2026
- Exit load-free redemption or switch window for dissenting investors: August 13, 2026, to September 11, 2026.
- Systematic investment facilities (SIP/STP/SWP) with positive consent will automatically transition to the Transferee scheme.

## Regulatory Changes

- Scheme-of-arrangement merger executed by Bandhan AMC under regulatory guidelines for mutual funds.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must take note of transaction timelines for demat and non-demat modes.
- Investors not wishing to continue can redeem or switch without exit load until September 11, 2026.
- Cancellation requests for systematic facilities should be submitted within AMC timelines if discontinuation is desired.

## Important Dates

- Exit Load Waiver Period: August 13, 2026, to September 11, 2026
- Demat Subscription Cut-off: September 04, 2026
- Non-Demat Subscription Cut-off: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date of Merger: September 11, 2026 (Post business hours)

## Impact Assessment

- Investors in the transferor scheme are transitioned to the short duration fund unless they opt to redeem or switch out. Trading availability of the transferor scheme ceases on the BSE StAR MF Platform post September 11, 2026.
