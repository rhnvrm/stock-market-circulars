## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CPs) of select companies that have fixed record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **Chennai Petroleum Corporation Ltd.**: Scrip Code 732638, ISIN INE178A14HU2, Record Date: 19/10/2026, Ex-Date: 19/10/2026.
- **Fedbank Financial Services Limited**: Scrip Code 732347, ISIN INE007N14FP0, Record Date: 22/10/2026, Ex-Date: 22/10/2026.
- **TATA STEEL LIMITED**: Scrip Code 732729, ISIN INE081A14HQ6, Record Date: 28/10/2026, Ex-Date: 28/10/2026.
- Trading members must refrain from dealing in these commercial papers on and after the specified ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; routine operational suspension of trading ahead of commercial paper redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no transactions are executed in the specified commercial papers of the listed companies past their ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: 15 October 2026
- **Chennai Petroleum CP Record/Ex-Date**: 19 October 2026
- **Fedbank Financial Services CP Record/Ex-Date**: 22 October 2026
- **Tata Steel CP Record/Ex-Date**: 28 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly settlement and redemption of commercial papers by preventing trading activity post record date.
