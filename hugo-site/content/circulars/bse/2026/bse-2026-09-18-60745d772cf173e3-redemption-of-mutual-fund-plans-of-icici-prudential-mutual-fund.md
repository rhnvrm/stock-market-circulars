## Summary

ICICI Prudential Asset Management Company Limited has announced the record date for the redemption of specific Mutual Fund Fixed Maturity Plans. Trading members are advised not to deal in these plans as of the specified record date.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260918-49
- Segment: Mutual Fund
- Fund House: ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund
- Affected Plans: ICICI Prudential Fixed Maturity Plan – Series 88 – 1303 Days Plan S (Growth, IDCW Payout, Direct Plan - Growth, and Direct Plan - IDCW Payout).
- Scrip Codes: 543815, 543816, 543817, 543818
- ISINs: INF109KC12R6, INF109KC13R4, INF109KC14R2, INF109KC15R9

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified Mutual Fund Plans starting from the record date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 18 Sep 2026
- Record Date: 30/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 30/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors holding units in the specified fixed maturity plans must note the redemption schedule and trading restrictions.
