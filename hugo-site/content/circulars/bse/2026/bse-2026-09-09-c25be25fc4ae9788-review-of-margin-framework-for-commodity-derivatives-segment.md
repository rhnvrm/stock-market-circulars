## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has reviewed the categorization of commodities and the applicable minimum margin requirements for the commodity derivatives segment, in modification of earlier circulars and following SEBI guidelines. The revised framework specifies updated Initial Margins, Short Option Minimum Margins (SOMM), Margin Period of Risk (MPOR), and Volatility Scan Ranges (VSR) for commodities including Crude Oil, WTI Crude, Gold, and Silver.

## Key Points

- Revised categorization of commodities based on volatility and SEBI guidelines.
- Minimum Initial Margin (IM), Short Option Minimum Margin (SOMM), MPOR, and Volatility Scan Range (VSR) updated for key commodities.
- Specific lead exchange risk management framework overrides applied to WTI Crude Oil, Crude Oil, and Silver contracts.
- Provisions become effective from the beginning of the day on October 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Crude Oil / WTI Crude**: Categorized as Non-Agri, High volatility. Minimum IM: 10%, SOMM: 10%, MPOR: 3, VSR: 6%. (Note: Lead exchange's revised framework specifies 30% IM/SOMM and 30% VSR).
- **Gold**: Categorized as Non-Agri, Medium volatility. Minimum IM: 8%, SOMM: 8%, MPOR: 2, VSR: 5%.
- **Silver**: Categorized as Non-Agri, High volatility. Minimum IM: 10%, SOMM: 10%, MPOR: 3, VSR: 6%. (Note: Lead exchange's framework specifies 11.50% IM/SOMM and 6% VSR).
- Minimum IM % shall not be scaled up by MPOR.
- Option contracts MPOR shall be at least equal to two days or MPOR of corresponding futures contracts, whichever is higher.
- Margins for option sellers shall be the higher of SOMM or VaR scaled up by MPOR.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and participants must ensure systems and risk management setups reflect the updated margin parameters for commodity derivatives before the effective date.
- Other applicable margins such as Extreme Loss Margin, Crystallized Loss Margin, Tender Period Margin, Delivery Period Margin, Concentration Margin, Additional Margin, and Special Margin continue as before.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: October 01, 2026 (beginning of day).

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in commodity derivatives, particularly crude oil and precious metals, will need to adjust capital allocations and collateral management in line with the revised margin requirements.
