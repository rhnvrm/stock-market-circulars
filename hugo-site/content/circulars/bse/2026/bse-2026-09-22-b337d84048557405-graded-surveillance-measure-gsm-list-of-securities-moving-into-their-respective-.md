## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework effective from September 23, 2026. These measures include strict margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) depending on the assigned stage.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 23, 2026.
- Applicable to securities moving into higher GSM Stages (I, II, III, IV).
- Stage I: 100% margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade for trade with 5% price band and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade for trade, trading permitted once a week (every Monday), and 100% ASD with no upward price movement for Stage IV.
- Settlement on trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities identified under the surveillance framework are subjected to enhanced margins, trading frequency restrictions, and surveillance deposits as per the GSM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in these securities, keeping in mind the trade-to-trade settlement and prohibition of netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities may be restricted due to increased margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and limitations on trading frequency.
