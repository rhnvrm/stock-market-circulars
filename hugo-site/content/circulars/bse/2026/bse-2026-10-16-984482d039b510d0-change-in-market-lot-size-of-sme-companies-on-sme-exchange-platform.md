## Summary

BSE has announced a revision in the market lot size for 62 SME companies listed on its SME Exchange/Platform, pursuant to SEBI guidelines dated February 21, 2012 (CIR/MRD/DSA/06/2012). The revised market lots will come into effect from November 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Market lot sizes have been revised for 62 SME companies based on periodic review.
- Changes are aligned with SEBI's standardized guidelines for secondary market trading on SME platforms.
- Effective date for the revised lot sizes is November 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Standardized lot size adjustments implemented in accordance with SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DSA/06/2012 to ensure proper liquidity and retail participation in SME scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems and terminals with the revised market lot sizes for the listed SME companies prior to the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: November 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The revision in market lot sizes alters the minimum investment amount required for trading in the affected SME stocks, potentially improving liquidity and participation.
