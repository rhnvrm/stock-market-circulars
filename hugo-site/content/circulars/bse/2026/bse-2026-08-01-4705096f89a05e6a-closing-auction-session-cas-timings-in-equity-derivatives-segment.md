## Summary

In partial modification of Exchange Notice No. 20260430-19 dated April 30, 2026, BSE has announced revised trading timings in the Equity Derivatives segment following the introduction of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) effective August 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Introduction of CAS:** Effective August 3, 2026, the Closing Auction Session is introduced in the Equity Derivatives segment.
- **Pre-Open Timings:** Order Entry period runs from 09:00 AM to 09:08 AM (with random stoppage in the last 1 minute), and Order Matching & Buffer period runs from 09:08 AM to 09:15 AM.
- **Continuous Trading:** Scheduled from 09:15 AM to 03:40 PM.
- **Stock Future Price Bands:** Update of stock future price bands for alignment with CAS in the Equity Segment is scheduled for 03:15 PM* (revised price bands applied sequentially starting from 03:15 PM).
- **Trading Modification:** Allowed up to 04:15 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

Partial modification of trading hours and session schedules in the Equity Derivatives segment pursuant to Exchange Notice No. 20260430-19.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must align their trading systems and operations with the revised session timings and price band update schedules effective August 3, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 3, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustments for trading members and participants dealing in equity derivatives regarding order entry, matching, and price band alignment.
