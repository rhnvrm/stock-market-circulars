## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Carnelian Liquid Fund by Carnelian Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from October 12, 2026, to October 14, 2026, with the NFO extended till midnight on October 14, 2026, for lumpsum purchases.

## Key Points

- **Fund Name:** Carnelian Mutual Fund - Carnelian Liquid Fund
- **NFO Period:** October 12, 2026, to October 14, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 14, 2026, for lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 16, 2026.
- **Schemes Included:** 
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF30CN01061)
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF30CN01038)
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund - Regular Plan - Daily IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF30CN01079)
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund - Direct Plan - Daily IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF30CN01046)
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund - Regular Plan - Weekly IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF30CN01087)
  - Carnelian Liquid Fund - Direct Plan - Weekly IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF30CN01053)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported in this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 15, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted during the NFO period subject to timely clear funds receipt.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** October 12, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** October 14, 2026 (extended till midnight for lumpsum)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** October 15, 2026 by 6:00 PM
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification providing trading members and investors with structural timelines and scheme details for participating in the Carnelian Liquid Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
