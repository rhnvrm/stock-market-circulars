## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the resumption of subscriptions in Axis Gold Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, following communication from Axis Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Lumpsum investments (fresh and additional purchases) and Switch-In transactions under Axis Gold Fund are re-enabled.
- Restrictions previously imposed via Notice No. 20260608-39 dated June 08, 2026, have been lifted.
- Changes take effect on August 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are advised to take note of the resumption for executing transactions under the scheme.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to resume regular lumpsum and switch-in investments in Axis Gold Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform without previous restrictions.
