## Summary

The President of India, acting through the Ministry of Finance, is offering up to 31,62,49,885 Equity Shares (2.50% equity) with an oversubscription option of up to 50,59,99,816 Equity Shares (4.00% equity), totaling 82,22,49,701 Equity Shares (6.50% of total paid-up equity share capital) of Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) through an Offer for Sale (OFS).

## Key Points

- Base Offer Size: 31,62,49,885 Equity Shares (2.50% of paid-up capital).
- Oversubscription Option: Up to 50,59,99,816 Equity Shares (4.00% of paid-up capital).
- Total Offer Size: 82,22,49,701 Equity Shares (6.50% of paid-up capital) upon exercising the oversubscription option.
- Allocation for Retail Investors: 10% of the total offer size (8,22,24,971 Equity Shares) reserved on T+1 day (August 05, 2026).
- Employee Reservation: Up to 50,00,000 Equity Shares offered to eligible employees.
- Discount: Rs. 10/- per equity share for Retail Investors and Employees bidding at cut-off price.

## Regulatory Changes

- Execution of large public sector divestment via OFS mechanism on designated windows of BSE and NSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Bids must be placed in accordance with the OFS guidelines of the Stock Exchanges for non-retail and retail phases.

## Important Dates

- T Day (August 04, 2026): Non-Retail Investors only.
- T+1 Day (August 05, 2026): Retail Investors, Employees, and Non-Retail Investors carrying forward un-allotted bids.

## Impact Assessment

- Significant liquidity event and secondary market offering involving India's largest life insurer, impacting market trading volumes and retail/institutional participation.
