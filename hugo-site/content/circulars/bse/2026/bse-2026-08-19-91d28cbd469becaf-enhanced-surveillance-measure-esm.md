## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities meeting specific criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, move to lower or higher stages, or shift between rolling settlement and trade-for-trade segments effective August 20 and August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 21, 2026, on all open positions as on August 20, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities will move to lower Stage I under the ESM framework w.e.f. August 20, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. August 21, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 20, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the ESM framework is provided in Annexure II, and a consolidated list is given in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened surveillance measures including 100% margins, price bands, and trade-for-trade settlement for shortlisted securities under the ESM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements on open and new positions and note the shift of applicable securities to the trade-for-trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II adjustments / movement to lower Stage I / moving out of ESM: August 20, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shifting: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in securities listed under the ESM framework will experience increased margin obligations and trading restrictions (such as trade-for-trade settlement and price bands).
