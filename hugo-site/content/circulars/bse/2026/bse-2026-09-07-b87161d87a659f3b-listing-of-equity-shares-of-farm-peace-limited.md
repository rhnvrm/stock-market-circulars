## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, September 08, 2026, the equity shares of Farm Peace Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will operate in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Farm Peace Limited
- Scrip Code: 544905
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: FARMPEACE
- ISIN No.: INE0W2E01010
- Issue Price: Rs. 59/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 49/-)
- Market Lot: 2000 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 04, 2026
- Market Maker: Shreni Shares Limited
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

 Securities will trade in dematerialised form only, with a mandatory minimum market lot of 2000 shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades comply with the Trade-for-Trade segment restrictions for the initial 10 trading days and participate in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) as applicable.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 04, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Farm Peace Limited shares on the SME platform with standard regulatory lock-in and surveillance measures.
