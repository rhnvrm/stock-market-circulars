## Summary

Sundaram Mutual Fund has activated its Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) on the BSE StAR MF platform effective October 19, 2026, following SEBI's regulatory framework. Additionally, the Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund NFO will be activated for order routing effective October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Sundaram Mutual Fund SIF activated on BSE StAR MF platform from October 19, 2026.
- Transactions for NRI and Minor clients are accepted, subject to specific country restrictions.
- Non-acceptance of NRI applications applies to investors from Korea, Democratic People’s Republic Of and Iran, Islamic Republic Of.
- Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund NFO opens for order routing from October 21, 2026.
- Detailed transaction processes and regulatory frameworks are outlined in Annexures I, II, and III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation in accordance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/IMD/IMD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/26 dated February 27, 2025, concerning the regulatory framework for Specialized Investment Funds (SIF).

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with minimum investment thresholds and country-specific restrictions for NRI investors (excluding Korea, Democratic People’s Republic Of, and Iran, Islamic Republic Of).

## Important Dates

- October 19, 2026: Activation of Sundaram Mutual Fund SIF on BSE StAR MF Platform.
- October 21, 2026: Activation of Select Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund NFO for order routing.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates trading and subscription of Specialized Investment Funds and NFOs for Sundaram Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, expanding investment product offerings for institutional, retail, NRI, and minor investors while adhering to regulatory constraints.
