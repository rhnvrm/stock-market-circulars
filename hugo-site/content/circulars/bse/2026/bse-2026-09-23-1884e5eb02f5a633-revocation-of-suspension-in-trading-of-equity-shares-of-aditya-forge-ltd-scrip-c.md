## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that the suspension in trading of equity shares of Aditya Forge Ltd (Scrip Code: 522150) will be revoked effective October 01, 2026. Trading will resume in the "XT" group pursuant to SEBI Master Circular guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aditya Forge Ltd
- Scrip Code: 522150
- Effective Date of Revocation: October 01, 2026
- Trading Group: Resumed in "XT" group
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): Scrip will be part of the SPOS for IPOs and relisted scrips on October 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading is resumed pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the revocation and the inclusion of the scrip in the Special Pre-open Session.
- The Information Memorandum of the company is made available on the Exchange’s website under Corporates → Corporate Filings → Information Memorandum → Revocation.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date / Relist Session: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding shares of Aditya Forge Ltd will regain liquidity as trading resumes on the exchange following the revocation of suspension.
