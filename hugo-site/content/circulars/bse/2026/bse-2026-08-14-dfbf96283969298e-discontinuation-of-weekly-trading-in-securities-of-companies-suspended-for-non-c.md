## Summary

BSE has announced the discontinuation of weekly trading on a 'Trade for Trade' basis in the Z group for securities of specific companies that were previously suspended for non-compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. This change comes into effect on September 1, 2026, upon completion of the mandated six-month trading period.

## Key Points

- Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260112-12 dated January 12, 2026.
- Suspension was triggered by non-compliance with Regulation 31 (Submission of Shareholding Pattern) for two consecutive quarters (June 2025 and September 2025).
- Mandated six-month period of trading on the first trading day of every week completes on August 31, 2026.
- Weekly trading on 'Trade for Trade' basis in Z group is discontinued effective September 1, 2026.
- Affected companies include Future Consumer Ltd, Master Chemicals Ltd, and Vardhman Concrete Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 issued on July 11, 2023, and updated on January 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Companies must ensure compliance with SEBI LODR Regulations, particularly regarding the submission of shareholding patterns and other periodic filings to regularize their status.

## Important Dates

- **January 12, 2026:** Initial notice regarding suspension of trading.
- **August 31, 2026:** Completion of the mandated six-month weekly trading period.
- **September 01, 2026:** Discontinuation of weekly trading in securities of the specified companies.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding securities in the affected companies will see changes in trading availability as the weekly trading window concludes. Clarifications can be sought via bse.soplodr@bseindia.com.
