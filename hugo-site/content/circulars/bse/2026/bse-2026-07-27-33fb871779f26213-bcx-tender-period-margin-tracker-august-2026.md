## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd has issued a notice regarding the Tender Period / Pre-Expiry Margin Tracker applicable to futures contracts in the Commodity Derivatives segment expiring in August 2026.

## Key Points

- Applies to futures contracts in the Commodity Derivatives segment expiring in August 2026.
- Outlines data pertaining to the Tender Period and applicable Tender Period Margin Percent for each Tender Period Day.
- Detailed schedule and percentages are provided in the attached Annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard risk management procedure for expiring commodity derivatives contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants trading in commodity derivatives expiring in August 2026 must take note of the tender period margin requirements and ensure adequate margin maintenance.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 July 2026
- Expiry Month: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and preparedness for members regarding margin requirements during the pre-expiry and tender period phases of commodity derivatives contracts.
