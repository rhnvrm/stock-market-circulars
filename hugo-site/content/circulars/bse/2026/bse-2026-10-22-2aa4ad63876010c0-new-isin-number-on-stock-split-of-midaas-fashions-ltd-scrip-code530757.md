## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the allocation of a new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of Midaas Fashions Ltd (Scrip Code: 530757). The stock split involves the sub-division of equity shares from the face value of Rs. 10/- each to Rs. 5/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Midaas Fashions Ltd
- Scrip Code: 530757
- Corporate Action: Stock Split (Sub-Division of Equity Shares)
- Face Value Change: From Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/-
- New ISIN Number: INE0OUS01029
- Ex-Date: October 26, 2026 (DR-742/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

Allocation of a new ISIN number to reflect the capital change resulting from the stock split in depository and exchange systems.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading and back-office systems with the new ISIN (INE0OUS01029) for Midaas Fashions Ltd effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 22, 2026
- Ex-Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures seamless clearing and settlement of trades for the sub-divided equity shares under the correct ISIN starting from the ex-date.
