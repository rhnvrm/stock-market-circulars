## Summary

BSE has announced the inclusion of securities under Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II based on market surveillance criteria. The applicable surveillance actions, including revised margin rates, take effect from September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II have been shortlisted.
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage I is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. September 23, 2026, on open positions as of September 22, 2026, and new positions.
- Applicable rate of margin for Stage II is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. September 23, 2026, on open positions as of September 22, 2026, and new positions.
- Inclusion under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of margin rates and surveillance requirements under the ST-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the revised margin requirements for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the short term.
