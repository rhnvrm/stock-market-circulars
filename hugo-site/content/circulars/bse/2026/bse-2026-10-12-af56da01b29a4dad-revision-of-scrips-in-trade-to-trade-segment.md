## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of securities in the Trade-to-Trade (T / XT / MT / TS) segment as part of surveillance measures and based on the extant SEBI framework, effective October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria I, II, and III will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 15, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities mentioned in Annexure II will continue to remain in T / XT / MT / TS / P / Z / ZP Group with a 5% price band.
- Shortlisting of securities is based on XBRL submissions by respective listed companies.
- Transfer is purely a market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer of specified securities for settlement on a Trade-to-Trade basis in consultation with SEBI under surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions as settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off positions allowed, along with a 100% VAR margin levy.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and settlement processes for short-listed scrips due to mandatory trade-to-trade settlement and 100% VAR margin requirements.
