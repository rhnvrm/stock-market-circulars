## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Augmont Enterprises Limited on the Exchange effective from Monday, August 31, 2026. The shares will be traded in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: Augmont Enterprises Limited
- Scrip Code: 544888
- ISIN No.: INE16W401027
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: AUGMONT
- Total Securities: 91,373,505 Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 788/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/- and Premium of Rs. 783/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 27, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): Applicable on Monday, August 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Corporate action executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository’s records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 27, 2026
- Commencement of Trading / Listing Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors who were allotted shares in the IPO can trade them on the Exchange starting August 31, 2026, following the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS).
