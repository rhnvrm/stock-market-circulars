## Summary

P. Suryakant Share & Stock Brokers Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 792) has announced the voluntary closure of its business in the Cash and Currency derivative segments of the BSE, effective September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: P. Suryakant Share & Stock Brokers Pvt. Ltd.
- Member Number: 792
- Segments affected: Cash and Currency derivative segments
- Effective Date: Monday, September 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a voluntary cessation of business operations by the trading member.

## Compliance Requirements

For any clarifications regarding this notice, members can contact membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Cessation: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impact is limited to clients and counterparties dealing with P. Suryakant Share & Stock Brokers Pvt. Ltd. in the affected segments.
