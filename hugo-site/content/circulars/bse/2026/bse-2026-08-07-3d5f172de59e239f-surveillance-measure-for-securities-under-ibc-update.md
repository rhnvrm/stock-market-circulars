## Summary

BSE announced a revision to the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) following a Joint Surveillance Meeting of Exchanges and SEBI held on August 7, 2026. The revised framework takes effect on August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- The IBC framework applies in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under IBC is based on market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.
- Sets clear criteria for moving companies out of the IBC framework upon attaining non-promoter holding (NPH) >= 25%.
- Specifies protocols for handling NCLAT/Court stay orders.

## Regulatory Changes

- Stage 0: Corporate announcement/disclosure triggers gross settlement, ±5% price band, and 100% margin from T+2 day.
- Stage I: Triggered by close-to-close price variation greater than 25% in 5 trading days or 50% in 15 trading days, invoking stricter settlement terms and price bands.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to the revised surveillance actions and margin requirements for listed securities under IBC.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increases trading safeguards and margin requirements for companies undergoing insolvency proceedings to curb excessive volatility.
