## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The applicable surveillance actions include enhanced margin rates effective July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable margin rate is 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective July 30, 2026, on open positions as on July 29, 2026, and new positions.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate is 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective July 30, 2026, on open positions as on July 29, 2026, and new positions.
- Inclusion under ASM is purely for market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margin requirements under the ST-ASM framework for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100% as applicable) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 30, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for securities under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II may affect liquidity and trading volumes in the shortlisted scrips.
