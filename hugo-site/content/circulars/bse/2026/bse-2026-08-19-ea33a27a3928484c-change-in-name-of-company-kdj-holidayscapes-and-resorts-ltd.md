## Summary

Trading members are informed that KDJ Holidayscapes and Resorts Ltd has changed its name to KDJ Avenique Limited. Consequently, the Scrip ID and Abbreviated Name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: KDJ Holidayscapes and Resorts Ltd
- New Company Name: KDJ Avenique Limited
- Scrip Code: 530701
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): AVENIQUE
- New Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus): AVENIQUE
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to note the updated scrip ID and abbreviated name for all trading and operational purposes in the BOLT Plus system.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to the update of trading system nomenclature for the company's equity shares.
