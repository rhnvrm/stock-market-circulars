## Summary

Effective from Thursday, September 17, 2026, the equity shares of Amtech Esters Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Amtech Esters Limited
- Scrip Code: 544920
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: AMTECH
- ISIN: INE0RMA01019
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- Issue Price: Rs. 75/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 65/-)
- Total No. of Securities: 88,29,168 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1,600 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Market Maker: Nikunj Stock Brokers Limited
- Registrar to the Issue: Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Operates under standard listing guidelines and SEBI framework for IPOs and relisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that securities will be traded strictly in dematerialised form in the specified minimum market lot of 1,600 shares.
- Part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.
- Lock-in period has been duly marked in the depository's records for applicable shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Amtech Esters Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with initial trade-for-trade surveillance controls.
