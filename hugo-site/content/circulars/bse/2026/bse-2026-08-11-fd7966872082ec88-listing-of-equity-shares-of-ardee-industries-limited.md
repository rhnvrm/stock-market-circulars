## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, August 12, 2026, the equity shares of Ardee Industries Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ardee Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 544860
- Scrip ID: ARDEE
- ISIN No: INE0XNF01022
- Face Value: Rs. 2/- per share
- Issue Price: Rs. 53/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 2/- and premium of Rs. 51/-)
- Total Securities Listed: 315,200,950 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: August 10, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Registrar: KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Wednesday, August 12, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action has been executed for lock-in period marking in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 10, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Ardee Industries Limited equity shares on the exchange platform for investors and trading members.
