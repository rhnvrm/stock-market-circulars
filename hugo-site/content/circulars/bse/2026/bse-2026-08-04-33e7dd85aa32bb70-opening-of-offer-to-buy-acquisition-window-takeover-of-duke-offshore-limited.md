## Summary

Aspect Global Ventures Private Limited has made an open offer to public shareholders of Duke Offshore Limited to acquire up to 25,62,872 fully paid equity shares (representing 26% of the voting share capital) at a price of Rs. 30 per share payable in cash, pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: Aspect Global Ventures Private Limited.
- Target Company: Duke Offshore Limited.
- Offer Price: Rs. 30.00 per equity share.
- Offer Size: Up to 25,62,872 fully paid equity shares (26% of Voting Share Capital).
- Offer Period: August 06, 2026 to August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The offer is conducted in compliance with SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 and associated SEBI/Exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must adhere to the mechanism for acquisition of shares through stock exchange pursuant to tender offers under takeover regulations as per updated guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 04, 2026
- Open Offer Start Date: August 06, 2026
- Open Offer End Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the substantial acquisition of shares and change in control for Duke Offshore Limited through the stock exchange acquisition window mechanism.
