## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Gazette Notification F. No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/321 dated October 10, 2026, issued by SEBI, concerning the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Research Analysts) (Amendment) Regulations, 2026.

## Key Points

- Refers to SEBI Gazette Notification dated October 10, 2026.
- Pertains to the Research Analysts (Amendment) Regulations, 2026.
- A copy of the notification is attached as Annexure 1 for reference.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amendments to the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Research Analysts) Regulations as specified in the SEBI Gazette Notification F. No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/321.

## Compliance Requirements

- All Research Analysts are requested to take note of the Gazette Notification and ensure compliance with the amended regulations.
- Clarifications can be sought by writing to Iara.Compliance@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notification Date: October 10, 2026 (SEBI Gazette)
- Notice Date: October 22, 2026 (BSE)

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative and compliance update for registered research analysts operating in the market.
