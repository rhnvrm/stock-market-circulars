## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) along with a SIP facility for ICICI Prudential Dynamic Asset Allocation Passive FOF on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026, to September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Names: ICICI Prudential Dynamic Asset Allocation Passive FOF - Regular Plan (ISIN: INF109K1A633) and Direct Plan (ISIN: INF109K1A641).
- Subscription Window: August 26, 2026, to September 09, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 15, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on September 09, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 11, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: August 26, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: September 09, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 11, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless trading, subscription, and SIP registration for investors participating in the ICICI Prudential NFO via the BSE StAR MF platform.
