## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding all enlisted Investment Advisers (IAs) to submit their Half-Yearly Periodic Report for the period ended September 30, 2026. Submissions must be carried out through the designated portal between October 1, 2026, and October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applies to all enlisted Investment Advisers (IAs).
- Reporting period covers the half-year ended September 30, 2026.
- Submissions must be completed via the designated portal using existing login credentials.
- The submission window remains open from October 01, 2026, up to October 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory framework introduced; this is a periodic compliance filing directive.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must submit the Half-Yearly Periodic Report through the BSE/SEBI portal at https://iaraportal.sebi.gov.in/.
- IAs are required to use their existing login credentials created during registration; creation of new credentials is not permitted.
- Queries can be directed to iara.compliance@bseindia.com or via telephone at 022-22725680 / 5854.

## Important Dates

- **Reporting Period Ended**: September 30, 2026
- **Portal Opening Date**: October 01, 2026
- **Portal Closing / Deadline Date**: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures regulatory oversight and monitoring of Investment Advisers. Timely compliance is mandatory to avoid regulatory penalties or adverse actions.
