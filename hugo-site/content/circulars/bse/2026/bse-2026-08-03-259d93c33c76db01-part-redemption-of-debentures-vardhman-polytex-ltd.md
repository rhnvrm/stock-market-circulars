## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the company Vardhman Polytex Ltd has cancelled the previously announced record date for part redemption of its debentures (Scrip Code: 977714). Consequently, the face value of the debentures is reverted and trading will take place with the revised face value effective from August 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vardhman Polytex Ltd
- Security: NCD (VPL-18%-31-3-31-PVT, ISIN: INE835A07018, Scrip Code: 977714)
- Action: Cancellation of part redemption record date and reversion of face value.
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 100,000/- per Debenture.
- Effective Date: August 04, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the revised face value of the debentures for all trading and operational purposes effective from August 04, 2026.
