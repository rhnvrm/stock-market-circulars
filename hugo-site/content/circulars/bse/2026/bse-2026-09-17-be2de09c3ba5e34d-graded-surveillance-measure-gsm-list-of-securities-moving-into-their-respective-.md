## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the list of securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from September 18, 2026. The measures include increased margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and additional surveillance deposits (ASD).

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure shall move to higher stages of GSM effective September 18, 2026.
- Applicable surveillance actions vary by stage, ranging from 100% margins and lower price bands to trade-for-trade settlement and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement for these securities shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened surveillance actions under the GSM framework as established by previous exchange notices (dating from February 2017 to November 2023).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, ensuring compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules, applicable margin rates, and ASD deposits.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 17 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: 18 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity for the affected securities may experience contractions due to strict margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement without netting, and mandatory Additional Surveillance Deposits.
