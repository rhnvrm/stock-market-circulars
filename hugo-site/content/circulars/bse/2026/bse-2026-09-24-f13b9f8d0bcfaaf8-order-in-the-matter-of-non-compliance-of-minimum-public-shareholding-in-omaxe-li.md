## Summary

SEBI vide order dated September 24, 2026, issued directions regarding the non-compliance of Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) norms by Omaxe Limited. Omaxe Limited and several associated promoters and entities have been debarred from accessing the securities market for specified periods ranging from 3 months to 1 year.

## Key Points

- SEBI Order No.: QJA/MN/IVD-1/ID10/32730/2026-27 dated September 24, 2026.
- Omaxe Limited (PAN: AAACO0171H) restrained from accessing the securities market for 3 months.
- Promoters and related entities (Rohtas Goel, Sunil Goel, Jai Bhagwan Goel, Dream Home Developers Pvt. Ltd., Guild Builders Pvt. Ltd.) debarred for 1 year.
- Open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts can be squared off within 3 months or at contract expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Settlement of pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions prior to the order date is permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

[Enforcement action enforcing Minimum Public Shareholding compliance norms]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the debarment directions and ensure that the noticees are restricted from buying, selling, or dealing in securities directly or indirectly during their respective debarment periods.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date: September 24, 2026
- Derivative Square-off Window: Within 3 months or upon contract expiry

## Impact Assessment

High operational and market impact on Omaxe Limited and its promoter entities due to regulatory debarment and trading restrictions.
