## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has released the trading and settlement schedule for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment covering the period from October 1, 2026, to October 30, 2026. The circular outlines settlement numbers, trading dates, pay-in/pay-out dates, and corresponding auction schedules.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement periods from DR-133/2026-2027 to DR-153/2026-2027.
- Defines timeline for trading submission, depository pay-in/pay-out, and auction settlement.
- Pay-in of funds and securities must be completed by 03:00 p.m. on respective pay-in dates.
- Pay-out of funds and securities is effected by 04:00 p.m.
- Auction pay-in is scheduled at 9:00 a.m. with member instructions latest by 8:50 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure adherence to the specified timings for pay-in instructions to depositories and banks by 03:00 p.m. for regular settlements and 08:50 a.m. for auction pay-ins.

## Important Dates

- **Period Start Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Period End Date:** October 30, 2026
- **First Settlement (DR-133):** Trading on October 1, 2026; Pay-in/Pay-out on October 5, 2026
- **Last Settlement (DR-153):** Trading on October 30, 2026; Pay-in/Pay-out on November 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for market participants trading in the EGR segment during October 2026.
