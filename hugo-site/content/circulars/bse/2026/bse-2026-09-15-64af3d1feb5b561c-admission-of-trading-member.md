## Summary

BSE Ltd. has issued a notice regarding the admission of new applicants as Trading Members and the addition of new trading segments for existing members, pursuant to the Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations of the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Several entities have been admitted as Trading Members with trading rights on BSE Ltd. across various segments including Cash, Equity Derivatives, Debt, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR.
- Total of 11 entities are listed with details regarding their member numbers, approved segments, and membership types (e.g., New Membership, Additional Segment Membership).
- Contact information is provided for trading members requiring clarifications regarding the notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Administrative admission of trading members.

## Compliance Requirements

- Admitted trading members must comply with the Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations of the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands trading membership and market participation capacity on the BSE platform.
