## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) on various securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and revised frameworks. The measures include 100% margins, movement to higher stages, group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS Groups), and changes in price bands.

## Key Points

- 100% Margins applicable on all open positions and new positions for securities in Annexure I (part A) w.e.f. September 16, 2026.
- Movement of securities to higher stages of LT-ASM Framework and applicability of Lower Price Band w.e.f. September 11, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Transfer of certain securities to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. September 16, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- Movings out of securities from LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. September 11, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions, not an adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework rules including margin requirements and price band restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and adhere to revised price bands and group trading structures for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Band and Movement (Annexure I Part B & Annexure II): September 11, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers (Annexure I Part A & Part D): September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and leverage for the shortlisted securities will be restricted due to 100% margin requirements and price band caps.
