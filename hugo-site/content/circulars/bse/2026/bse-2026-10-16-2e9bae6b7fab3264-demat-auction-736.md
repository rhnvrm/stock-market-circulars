## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the schedule for Demat Auction No. 736 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-736/2026-27, detailing auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and pay-in timings for trading members.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: Friday, Oct 16, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Auction Pay-in date: Monday, Oct 19, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; standard operational auction notice under rolling settlement rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Members should exercise utmost care when punching security codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Members must check cutoff rates on the Auction Offer Entry Screen and stay updated on corporate actions (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 16, 2026
- Pay-in Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process ensuring settlement completion for short-delivered securities in rolling settlement DR-736/2026-27.
