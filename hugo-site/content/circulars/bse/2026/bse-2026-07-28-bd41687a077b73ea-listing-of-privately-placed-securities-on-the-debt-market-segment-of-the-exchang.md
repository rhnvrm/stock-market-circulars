## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis have been listed and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective July 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are traded only in dematerialised form.
- Standard market denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Covers fresh listings of commercial papers from Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, and Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard demat trading norms for commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 28, 2026
- Allotment / Effective Dates: July 27, 2026 and July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations providing liquidity and trading access to newly issued commercial papers.
