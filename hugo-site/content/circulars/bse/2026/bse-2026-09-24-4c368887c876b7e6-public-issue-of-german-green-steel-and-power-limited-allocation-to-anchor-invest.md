## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that German Green Steel and Power Limited has successfully allocated 65,58,991 equity shares to anchor investors at Rs. 139 per share as part of its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company: German Green Steel and Power Limited
- Anchor Investor Portion: 65,58,991 equity shares subscribed
- Issue Price: Rs. 139/- per equity share
- Notice Reference: 20260924-31 dated September 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational and informational notice regarding anchor investor allocation.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the anchor investor allocation details.
