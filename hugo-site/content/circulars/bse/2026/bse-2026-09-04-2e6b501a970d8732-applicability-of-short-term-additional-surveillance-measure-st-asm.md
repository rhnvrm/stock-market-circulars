## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Securities qualifying for ST-ASM Stage II will attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher).
- Surveillance measures apply to all open positions as on September 07, 2026, and new positions created from September 08, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is strictly a market surveillance measure and does not imply an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) for all applicable open and new positions starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 04 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: 08 Sep 2026
- Applicable on open positions as of: 07 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin obligations for participants trading in securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stages I and II, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for these scrips.
