## Summary

Sceptre Medical (India) Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with the BSE on September 30, 2026, for its upcoming SME IPO. In compliance with ICDR Regulations (Schedule VI), the document has been made available on the stock exchange website to invite public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sceptre Medical (India) Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comment Window: 21 days from the date of the notice
- Contact for Comments: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; complies with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stakeholders and the public wishing to submit feedback or comments on the draft offer document must do so via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Comments Deadline: 21 days from October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Allows transparent market participation and stakeholder feedback prior to the finalization of the SME IPO offering.
