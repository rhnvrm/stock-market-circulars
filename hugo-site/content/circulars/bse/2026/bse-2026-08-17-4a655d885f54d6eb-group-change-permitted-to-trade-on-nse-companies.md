## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that multiple listed companies are undergoing a group change from Group X to Group B with effect from August 18, 2026.

- Notice Number: 20260817-26
- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Department: Listing Operations
- Effective Date of Group Change: August 18, 2026
- Change: Migration from Group X to Group B for listed companies.

Reclassification of listed entities' trading groups on the exchange platform.

Trading members should take note of the group reclassification for order execution and risk management.

- Effective Date: August 18, 2026
