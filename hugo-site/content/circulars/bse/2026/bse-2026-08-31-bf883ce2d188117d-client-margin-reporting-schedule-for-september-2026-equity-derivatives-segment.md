## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has issued the schedule for client margin reporting and applicable penalty dates for the Equity Derivatives segment covering trade dates in September 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines trade dates, corresponding last dates of margin reporting, and penalty dates for September 2026.
- Applies specifically to members operating in the Equity Derivatives Segment.
- Ensures timely reporting and compliance with margin norms to avoid penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine operational schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must ensure that client margin reporting is completed within the specified timelines for each trade date to avoid penalties.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Period:** Trade dates ranging from 01-09-2026 to 30-09-2026.
- **Reporting & Penalties:** Margin reporting deadlines and penalty dates are scheduled consecutively throughout September and early October 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational clarity for brokers and market participants regarding margin reporting deadlines in the Equity Derivatives segment for September 2026.
