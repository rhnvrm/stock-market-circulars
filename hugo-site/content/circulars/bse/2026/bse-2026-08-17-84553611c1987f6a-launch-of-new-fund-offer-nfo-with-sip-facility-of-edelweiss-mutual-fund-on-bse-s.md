## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the Launch of New Fund Offer (NFO) with SIP Facility of EDELWEISS Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, specifically revising the allotment date for EDELWEISS ULTRA SHORT TERM FUND from August 31, 2026, to August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised allotment date for EDELWEISS ULTRA SHORT TERM FUND is August 25, 2026.
- NFO scheme is extended up to 6:00 PM on August 25, 2026, exclusively for Lumpsum purchases.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on August 25, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 24, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 24, 2026, for cheque, OTM, and switch payments.

## Important Dates

- NFO Closure / Extension Date: August 25, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 25, 2026
- Funds Availability Deadline: August 24, 2026, by 6:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors participating in the Edelweiss Ultra Short Term Fund NFO must adhere to the accelerated timelines and fund availability deadlines to ensure successful allotment.
