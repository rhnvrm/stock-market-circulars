## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited (Scrip Code - 543273) effective from August 14, 2026. The issuance comprises 1,55,892 equity shares issued to promoters upon preferential warrant conversion.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited
- Scrip Code: 543273
- ISIN: INE0CJD01029
- Number of Shares: 1,55,892 equity shares of Rs. 5/- each
- Issue Price / Premium: Issued at Rs. 950/- per share (including a premium of Rs. 945/-)
- Date of Allotment: 26/06/2026
- Effective Date: August 14, 2026
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 15-03-2028 (Distribution Nos. 24444109 to 24600000)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure adherence to lock-in requirements and trading permissions for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: June 26, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: August 14, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: March 15, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up equity capital through preferential warrant conversion for promoters, with standard lock-in constraints applicable to the newly allotted shares.
