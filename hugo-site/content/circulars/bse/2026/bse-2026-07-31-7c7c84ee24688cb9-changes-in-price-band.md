## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the price bands for 44 scrips have been revised from their existing levels. The changes will come into effect from August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260731-32, dated July 31, 2026.
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance).
- Total number of scrips affected: 44.
- Revised price bands range from 1% to 20% across various securities.

## Regulatory Changes

The price limits for specified equity scrips have been updated to curb excessive volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure their trading systems reflect these changes before market opening on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 Jul 2026
- Effective Date: 03 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected scrips will be restricted to the newly defined price boundaries, directly impacting intraday volatility and order placement for these securities.
