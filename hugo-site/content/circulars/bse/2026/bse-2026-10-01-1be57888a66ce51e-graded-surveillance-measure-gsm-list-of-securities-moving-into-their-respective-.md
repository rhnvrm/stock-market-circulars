## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of specified securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective October 05, 2026, pursuant to various historical exchange notices.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure will move to higher stages of GSM.
- Effective date of implementation is October 05, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary across Stages I, II, III, and IV, including 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement, additional surveillance deposits (ASD), and restricted trading frequencies.
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are subjected to the provisions of the GSM Framework published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent amendments.
- Depending on the stage, applicable margin rates become 100% (Stage I), trade-for-trade with 5% price bands and 50% ASD (Stage II), or trade-for-trade with weekly trading and 100% ASD (Stages III & IV).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities.
- Buyers must deposit the required Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) and maintain applicable margin rates.
- No netting off is allowed as settlement occurs on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date of GSM Movement: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and investors holding or trading securities moving into GSM stages face increased liquidity constraints, mandatory deposits, and restrictions on netting off, impacting execution and capital requirements.
