## Summary

This circular compiles responses from multiple listed companies providing clarifications to stock exchange surveillance queries concerning recent movements in their share prices or trading volumes. The companies confirmed compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, stating that all material information has been duly disclosed and that market movements are driven by prevailing market conditions.

## Key Points

- **Modern Threads (India) Limited:** Clarified that price movement is market-driven and that the company has complied with all disclosure requirements under Regulation 30.
- **Afcons Infrastructure Ltd:** Responded to volume movement queries, confirming no undisclosed price-sensitive information exists.
- **Confidence Petroleum India Ltd:** Acknowledged surveillance communication regarding volume movements.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine stock exchange surveillance clarification process.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must promptly respond to exchange inquiries regarding unusual market activity and reaffirm compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- **Clarification Dates:** March 11–13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No direct financial or operational impact on the market or investors; confirms transparency and adherence to continuous disclosure norms.
