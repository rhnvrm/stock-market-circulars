## Summary

BSE has issued a notice communicating SEBI's directive regarding updates to the United Nations Security Council's (UNSC) 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List pursuant to Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967.

## Key Points

- Notification covers the amendment of 01 entry in the UNSC 1267/1989 ISIL & Al-Qaida Sanctions List.
- Members must ensure compliance with Central Government Orders and SEBI AML/CFT guidelines.
- Regular monitoring of the UNSC consolidated list and SEBI website updates is required.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of amendments made to the UNSC Sanctions List under Section 51A of UAPA, 1967.

## Compliance Requirements

- Scan all existing and future accounts to ensure no accounts are held by or linked to entities/individuals on the sanctions list.
- Follow procedures detailed in the Central Government Order dated Feb 02, 2021.
- Forward de-listing requests electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA and MEA Joint Secretary (UNP).
- Inform individuals/entities seeking removal about the UN Ombudsperson application process.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 01 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High compliance importance for market intermediaries to prevent terrorist financing and adhere to AML/CFT standards.
