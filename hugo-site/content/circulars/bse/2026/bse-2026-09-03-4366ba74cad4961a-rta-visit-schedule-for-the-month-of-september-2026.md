## Summary

BSE has announced the schedule of representatives from various Registrar and Transfer Agents (RTAs) visiting the Department of Investor Services (DIS) during September 2026 to address member and investor grievances.

## Key Points

- RTAs will be available at the Department of Investors Services, 1st Floor, Cama Building, Dalal Street, Mumbai–400001.
- Scheduled dates span from September 7, 2026, to September 11, 2026.
- Participating RTAs include KFin Technologies Ltd., Bigshare Services Pvt. Ltd., MCS Share Transfer Agent Ltd., MUFG Intime India Private Ltd., Universal Capital Securities P. Ltd., Sharex Dynamics India P. Ltd., TSR Darashaw Ltd., Integrated Ent. (India) Ltd., Adroit Corporate Services Pvt. Ltd., Purva Sharegistry (India) Pvt. Ltd., Datamatics Financial Services Pvt. Ltd., and Cameo Corporate Services Pvt. Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors having complaints against client companies of the listed RTAs can approach their representatives during the designated timings.

## Important Dates

- **RTA Visits:** September 7, 2026 to September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates direct interaction and grievance resolution between investors and RTAs, enhancing operational support for market participants.
