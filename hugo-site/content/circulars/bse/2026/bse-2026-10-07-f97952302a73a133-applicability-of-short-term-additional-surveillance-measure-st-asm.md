## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Enhanced margin requirements become effective from October 09, 2026, on open positions as of October 08, 2026, and newly created positions.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I are shortlisted for ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- Stage I securities attract a 50% margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Stage II securities attract a 100% margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely a market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the companies.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their original levels unless subject to other surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised ST-ASM framework rules as detailed in previous exchange circulars, enforcing stricter margin controls on specified volatile or shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I, 100% for Stage II) on all applicable open and new positions starting October 09, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 07, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 09, 2026
- **Applicable Open Positions Date:** As on October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in the shortlisted securities will experience increased capital requirements due to higher margin rates, helping mitigate excessive speculation and volatility.
