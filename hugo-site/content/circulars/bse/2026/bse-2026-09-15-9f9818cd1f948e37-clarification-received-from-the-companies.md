## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in certain scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from several companies, while replies from others remain awaited.

## Key Points

- Clarifications have been received from 13 companies, including 3B Films Ltd, Apollo Finvest India Ltd, Authum Investment & Infrastructure Ltd, Camex Ltd, Dhanwel Hybrid Seeds Ltd, Kirloskar Industries Ltd, Ladderup Finance Ltd, Modern Diagnostic & Research Centre Ltd, Moneyboxx Finance Ltd, RACL Geartech Ltd, Shashwat Furnishing Solutions Ltd, STL Networks Ltd, and Workmates Core2Cloud Solution Ltd.
- Replies are currently awaited from 8 companies, including CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Jhandewalas Foods Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Mopshop Distribution Ltd, Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Ltd, Nirmitee Robotics India Ltd, and Sera Investments & Finance India Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine surveillance monitoring communication.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies identified with significant price/volume fluctuations must submit requested clarifications to the Exchange in a timely manner.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 15 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Informational notice intended to maintain market transparency regarding unusual stock price or volume movements.
