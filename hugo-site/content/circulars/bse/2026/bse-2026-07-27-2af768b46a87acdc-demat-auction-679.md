## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-679/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timing: July 27, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction will be held on Tuesday, July 28, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate; no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: July 27, 2026
- Pay-in Date: July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for clearing short deliveries in rolling settlements.
