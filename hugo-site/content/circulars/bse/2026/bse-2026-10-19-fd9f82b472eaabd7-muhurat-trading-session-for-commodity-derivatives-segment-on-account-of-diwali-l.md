## Summary

BSE has announced the schedule and timings for the special Muhurat Trading Session for the Commodity Derivatives Segment on account of Diwali Laxmi Pujan, to be held on Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Muhurat Trading Session scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- Log-in timings: 01:15 pm to 02:20 pm.
- Continuous Trading timings: 02:20 pm to 03:30 pm.
- Trade Modification timings: 02:20 pm to 04:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the special trading timings and coordinate with the BSE Helpdesk or Relationship Manager for any queries.

## Important Dates

- Trading Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational schedule update for festival trading without broader regulatory or market structure impact.
