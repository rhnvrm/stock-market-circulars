## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that Bansisons Tea Industries Ltd has changed its name to Novyra Pharmachem Limited. Consequent to this change, the scrip ID and abbreviated name for the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Bansisons Tea Industries Ltd
- New Company Name: Novyra Pharmachem Limited
- Scrip Code: 519353
- New Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System: NOVYRA
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: NOVYRA
- Effective Date: August 31, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems and records with the new name, scrip ID, and abbreviated name for Scrip Code 519353.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading terminals and databases to reflect the new company name and scrip identifiers.
