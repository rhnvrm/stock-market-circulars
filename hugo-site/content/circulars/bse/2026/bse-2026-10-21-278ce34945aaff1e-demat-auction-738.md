## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-738/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on October 21, 2026.
- Auction matching: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Auction Pay-in scheduled for Thursday, October 22, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine auction schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must exercise utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Members should refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the Auction Offer Entry Screen and corporate action notices.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 21, 2026
- Pay-in Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for clearing and settlement of short-delivered securities with no broad market impact.
