## Summary

The equity shares of Sollfege Smart Electronics Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE exchange in the 'MT' Group of Securities effective from Thursday, October 8, 2026. In accordance with SEBI guidelines, the scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Name:** Sollfege Smart Electronics Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544977
- **ISIN No:** INE17HG01015
- **Scrip ID on BOLT System:** SOLLFEGE
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 55/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 45/-)
- **No. of Securities:** 10,000,000 Equity Shares
- **Market Lot:** 2000 Equity Shares
- **Segment:** SME / Trade-for-Trade (for 10 trading days)
- **Market Maker:** MNM Stock Broking Private Limited
- **Registrar:** KFIN Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; the listing complies with standard SEBI and exchange guidelines for SME IPOs and Trade-for-Trade segments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialised form only.
- Minimum market lot size is 2000 equity shares.
- Securities will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, October 8, 2026.
- Necessary corporate actions have been executed in the depository's records for lock-in periods where applicable.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 6, 2026
- **Listing and Trading Effective Date:** October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Sollfege Smart Electronics Limited shares on the BSE SME platform, providing liquidity to investors while operating under standard initial trade-for-trade surveillance safeguards.
