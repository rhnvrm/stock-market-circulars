## Summary

BSE has announced updates regarding securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework pursuant to previous notices. Securities satisfying the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment, or move across different stages of the ESM framework effective October 16 and October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 19, 2026 on all open positions as on October 16, 2026 and new positions created from October 19, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in ESM but move to lower Stage I w.e.f. October 16, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 19, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 16, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the ESM framework have their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements, Trade-for-Trade settlement, and periodic call auctions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the margin requirements, settlement segment changes, and price band restrictions for securities listed in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- October 16, 2026: Effective date for movement of securities to lower Stage I/Stage II and shifting out of ESM.
- October 19, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

High operational and liquidity impact for market participants trading in securities included in the ESM framework due to strict margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
