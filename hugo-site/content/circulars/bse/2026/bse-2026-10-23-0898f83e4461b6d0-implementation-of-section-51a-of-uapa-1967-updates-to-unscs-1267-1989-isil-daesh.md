## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding amendments made to the UNSC's 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List pursuant to Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967, following a communication from SEBI.

## Key Points

- Amendment of 03 entries in the UNSC 1267/1989 ISIL and Al-Qaida Sanctions List.
- Obligation for intermediaries to scan all existing and future accounts against the updated sanction lists.
- Adherence to Central Government Order dated Feb 02, 2021, and SEBI AML/CFT guidelines.
- Instructions regarding handling and forwarding of de-listing requests to MHA and MEA.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates communicated via SEBI referencing UNSC sanctions committee list modifications.
- Enforcement of statutory obligations under Section 51A of UAPA, 1967 and SEBI guidelines on AML/CFT standards (Clause 54).

## Compliance Requirements

- Scan all existing client accounts to ensure no accounts are held by or linked to listed entities or individuals.
- Implement automated or periodic screening for future accounts.
- Forward any de-listing requests electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA (jsctcr-mha@gov.in) with a copy to MEA Joint Secretary (UNP) (jsunp@mea.gov.in).
- Inform delisting applicants about the United Nations Ombudsperson application process.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 23 Oct 2026
- Compliance Effective Date: Immediate

## Impact Assessment

Intermediaries must ensure robust database screening mechanisms are active to prevent asset holding, account operation, or financial services access to newly updated entities on the sanctions list.
