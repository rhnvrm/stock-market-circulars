## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 36,00,000 new equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued to promoters at a price of Rs. 25.20/- per share (including a premium of Rs. 24.20/-) pursuant to the conversion of warrants, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name**: Kellton Tech Solutions Ltd. (Scrip Code: 519602, ISIN: INE164B01030).
- **Securities Issued**: 36,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued on a preferential basis to promoters.
- **Issue Price**: Rs. 25.20/- per share (Face Value: Re. 1/-, Premium: Rs. 24.20/-).
- **Effective Date**: October 23, 2026.
- **Distribution Nos.**: 527804671 to 531404670.
- **Date of Allotment**: 31/03/2026.
- **Lock-in Details**: 36,00,000 shares locked-in up to 30/04/2028.
- **Ranking**: Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard corporate listing procedure for preferential allotment and warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to lock-in requirements up to April 30, 2028, for the newly allotted promoter shares.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: October 22, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date**: October 23, 2026
- **Allotment Date**: March 31, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date**: April 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- **Capital Expansion**: Increases the paid-up equity share capital of Kellton Tech Solutions Ltd. by 36,00,000 shares.
- **Promoter Shareholding**: Reflects successful conversion of warrants into equity by promoters, increasing their equity stake subject to a lock-in period until April 2028.
