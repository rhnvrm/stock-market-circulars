## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities (Sovereign Gold Bond) effective October 21, 2026, due to maturity.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading applies to 364T231026 (Scrip Code: 805135, ISIN: IN002025Z302) and Sovereign Gold Bond 2019 Series (Scrip Code: 800288, ISIN: IN0020180249).
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid securities with effect from October 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no further trading or dealing in the specified T-bills and Government Securities takes place from October 21, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading Suspension: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational procedure for matured debt instruments with minimal market impact.
