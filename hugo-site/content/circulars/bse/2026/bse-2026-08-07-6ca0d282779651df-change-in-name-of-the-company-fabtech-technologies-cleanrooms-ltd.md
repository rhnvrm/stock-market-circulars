## Summary

Trading members are informed that Fabtech Technologies Cleanrooms Limited has changed its name. Accordingly, the scrip ID and abbreviated name for the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective August 13, 2026.

- **Existing Name:** Fabtech Technologies Limited
- **New Name:** Fabtech Technologies Cleanrooms Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544332
- **Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System:** FABCLEAN (No Change)
- **Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System:** FABCLEAN (No Change)
- **Effective Date:** August 13, 2026

None. Administrative update reflecting corporate name change records.

Trading members should take note of the updated company name for all future trading and settlement references.
