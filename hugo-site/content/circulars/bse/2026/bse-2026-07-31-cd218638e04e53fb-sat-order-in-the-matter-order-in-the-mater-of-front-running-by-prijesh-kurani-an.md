## Summary

The Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) issued an order dated July 3, 2026, staying the impugned order pending disposal of the appeal, conditioned upon appellant Pranav Vora depositing the full penalty within four weeks. SEBI confirmed via email on July 31, 2026, that the appellant has paid the penalty amount of Rs. 10,00,000 in favor of SEBI.

## Key Points

- SAT stayed the impugned order pending appeal subject to full penalty payment.
- Appellant Pranav Vora deposited the full penalty amount of Rs. 10,00,000.
- SEBI confirmed receipt of the penalty payment via email dated July 31, 2026.
- Trading members are advised to take note and ensure compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Interim stay of the regulatory order granted by SAT upon fulfillment of penalty deposit condition.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and relevant entities to take note of the SAT order and SEBI confirmation regarding the penalty payment.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: July 03, 2026
- SEBI Confirmation Date: July 31, 2026
- Previous Reference Notice Date: April 30, 2026 (Exchange Notice No. 20260430-30)
