## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding Research Analysts (RAs) regarding the submission procedure and deadlines for the Annual Compliance Audit Report (ACR) for the financial year ending March 31, 2026. The ACR module is accessible on the BSE portal through filing by IA/RA or filing through an auditor.

## Key Points

- The Annual Compliance Audit module is available on the BSE portal via two flows: Filing by IA/RA and Filing through an auditor.
- Submissions made via email will not be considered valid.
- Annual certificates confirming compliance with client-level segregation requirements must form part of the compliance audit.
- Login issues on the BSE portal can be reported to Iara.Compliance@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

Reiteration of existing compliance timelines and mandatory portal filing procedures for Research Analysts under RAASB supervision.

## Compliance Requirements

- Complete the annual compliance audit within six months from the end of the financial year (March 31, 2026), i.e., latest by September 30, 2026.
- Submit the compliance audit report to RAASB within one month from the date of the audit report, but not later than October 31, 2026.
- Submit any adverse findings along with action taken reports (approved by the individual RA or management) to RAASB by October 31, 2026.
- Include an annual certificate from a member of ICAI, ICSI, ICMAI, or an auditor confirming client-level segregation compliance.

## Important Dates

- Audit Completion Deadline: September 30, 2026
- Audit Report Submission Deadline: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures strict regulatory adherence, transparency, and timely reporting among Research Analysts regarding client fund segregation and operational compliance.
