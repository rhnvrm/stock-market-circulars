## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice detailing the application, stage revision, group transfers, and removal of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, effective from late September and early October 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins become applicable w.e.f. October 01, 2026, on all open positions as on September 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Lower price bands take effect w.e.f. September 29, 2026, for securities moving to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework (Annexure I, Part B).
- Specified securities continuing in the LT-ASM framework will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. October 01, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- Securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective September 29, 2026, will have their price bands reinstated.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of stringent surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and segment/group transfers.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and ongoing market surveillance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (100% margin) and adhere to revised price bands and group trading restrictions for impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- September 29, 2026: Lower price bands and movement to higher stages or out of LT-ASM framework take effect.
- September 30, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margin requirements.
- October 01, 2026: 100% margins become applicable; group transfers to T / XT / MT / TS groups take effect.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased capital requirements and trading restrictions on securities under LT-ASM aim to curb excessive volatility and protect market integrity, impacting liquidity and positioning for affected scrips.
