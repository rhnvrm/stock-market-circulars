## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Lancer Container Lines Limited issued on a preferential basis to promoters, effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lancer Container Lines Limited
- Scrip Code: 539841
- ISIN: INE359U01028
- Number of Shares: 1,85,18,518 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 10.80/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/- + Premium Rs. 5.80/-)
- Allottees: Promoters (Preferential Basis)
- Effective Date of Trading: August 28, 2026
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 15/03/2028
- Distinctive Numbers: 353263719 to 371782236

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Shares are ranking pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company and are subject to lock-in requirements as specified.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 20/07/2026
- Effective Date for Trading: 28/08/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 15/03/2028

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up capital of Lancer Container Lines Limited due to preferential allotment to promoters.
