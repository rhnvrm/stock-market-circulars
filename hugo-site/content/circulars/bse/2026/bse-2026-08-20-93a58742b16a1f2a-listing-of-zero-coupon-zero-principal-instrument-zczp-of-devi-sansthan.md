## Summary

Effective from Friday, August 21, 2026, the Zero Coupon Zero Principal Instrument ("ZCZP") of DEVI Sansthan shall be listed on the BSE under the SSE segment.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: DEVI Sansthan
- Scrip Code: 798004
- Scrip ID: DEVIZCZP
- ISIN No: INS2SFY12018
- Number of Securities: 8,378,936 ZCZPs of Rs. 1/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 1/- per share
- Date of Allotment: August 10, 2026
- Maturity Date: August 11, 2027
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Pvt. Ltd.
- Note: The securities will not be traded on the Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the entity must adhere to the listing guidelines specified for the Social Stock Exchange (SSE) segment.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 10, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: August 21, 2026
- Maturity Date: August 11, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Enables the listing of ZCZP instruments on the SSE segment without trading rights on the exchange.
