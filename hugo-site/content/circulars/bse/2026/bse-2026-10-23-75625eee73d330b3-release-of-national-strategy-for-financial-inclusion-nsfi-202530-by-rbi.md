## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has issued the National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30, dated December 01, 2025. In accordance with SEBI's advice, trading members are required to adopt measures for simplifying digital interfaces, addressing language barriers using vernacular languages and AI platforms such as Bhashini, and developing AI/ML-based public query systems for securities market-related aspects.

## Key Points

- RBI released the National Strategy for Financial Inclusion (NSFI) 2025–30 emphasizing simplified digital interfaces and AI use.
- Trading members must simplify digital interfaces to improve user experience and informed customer choices.
- Address language barriers using vernacular languages and AI translation tools like Bhashini.
- Develop an AI & ML-based public query system on common securities market-related aspects via websites and social media extensions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of RBI's NSFI 2025–30 guidelines concerning digital interface simplification and AI integration within trading member operations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take measures to simplify digital interfaces, utilize vernacular languages with AI tools like Bhashini, and set up AI/ML-based public query systems for investors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 23 October 2026
- NSFI 2025–30 Date: 01 December 2025

## Impact Assessment

Enhances retail investor accessibility, transparency, and financial inclusion across securities markets through digital simplification and AI-driven multilingual support and query resolution.
