## Summary

Bank of India Mutual Fund has introduced the Choti SIP facility under the Growth Option of select schemes (Bank of India Balanced Advantage Fund, Bank of India Multi Asset Allocation Fund, and Bank of India Multicap Fund) offered under the Regular Plan on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Choti SIP facility introduced for Bank of India Balanced Advantage Fund, Bank of India Multi Asset Allocation Fund, and Bank of India Multicap Fund (Growth Option, Regular Plan).
- Monthly SIP/XSIP installment amount of ₹250 requires a minimum of 60 installments for Choti SIP eligibility.
- Monthly SIP amounts ranging from ₹250 to any amount with fewer than 60 installments will be processed as Normal SIP (minimum 6 installments).
- Registrations not complying with the prescribed minimum installment criteria and validations at the AMC/RTA level will not be eligible for the Choti SIP facility.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that SIP/XSIP registrations with a ₹250 monthly installment amount select the correct minimum number of installments (60 installments) to qualify for the Choti SIP facility.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 23 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

- Provides enhanced flexibility for retail investors on the BSE StAR MF Platform through lower systematic investment ticket sizes.
