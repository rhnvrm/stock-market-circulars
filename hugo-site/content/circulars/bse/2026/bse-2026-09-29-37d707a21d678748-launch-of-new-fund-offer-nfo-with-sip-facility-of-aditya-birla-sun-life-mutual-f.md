## Summary

Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund has announced the launch of the Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market Index Fund NFO (New Fund Offer) along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window is open from September 30, 2026, to October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Details**: Includes Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF209KC1761) and Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF209KC1753).
- **NFO Period**: September 30, 2026, to October 14, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 14, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- **SIP/XSIP Registrations**: Must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on October 14, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date**: October 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 22, 2026, for allotment eligibility.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date**: September 30, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date**: October 14, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date**: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an opportunity to invest in the Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market Index Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform with smooth operational guidelines for clearing and settlement.
