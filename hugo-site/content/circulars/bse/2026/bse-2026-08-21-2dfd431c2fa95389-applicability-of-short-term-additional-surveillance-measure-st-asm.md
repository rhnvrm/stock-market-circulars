## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for securities that have satisfied the relevant criteria. The applicable surveillance actions include enhanced margin requirements effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II are subject to enhanced surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting under ASM is based on market surveillance criteria and is not an adverse action against the companies.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM framework provisions in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must note the revised margin requirements for open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.
- Inquiries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026 (applicable on all open positions as on August 24, 2026, and new positions created thereafter).

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations (50% for Stage I, 100% for Stage II) for market participants trading in the shortlisted securities, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage.
