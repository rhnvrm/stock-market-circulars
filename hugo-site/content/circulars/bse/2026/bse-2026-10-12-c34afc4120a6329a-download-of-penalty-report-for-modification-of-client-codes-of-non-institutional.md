## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the penalty report for client code modifications of non-institutional trades across all segments for the month of September 2026. Trading members can access the report file `PM0926.Clg no.` in the Equity Transaction Folder in the Extranet.

## Key Points

- Penalty reports for non-institutional client code modifications done during September 2026 are now available.
- Files have been downloaded with the filename format `PM0926.Clg no.` in the Equity Transaction Folder via Extranet.
- Based on Exchange Notice Nos. 20110729-24 (dated July 29, 2011) and 20110826-4 (dated August 26, 2011).
- Members are requested to save the downloaded files for future reference and compliance audits.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory framework introduced; this is a routine monthly execution of existing penalty rules for client code modifications under Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must download, verify, and archive the penalty report (`PM0926.Clg no.`) from the Extranet Equity Transaction Folder.
- For any queries or clarifications, members may contact BSE Trading Operations at 022-2272 8435 / 022-2272 5785 or via email at bse.reports@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Period Covered: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact operational requirement for trading members to verify penalty charges associated with client code modifications during September 2026.
