## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the Non-Competitive Bidding Facility for the auction of Treasury Bills (T-Bills) scheduled for July 29, 2026, following an RBI press release announcing the sale of three Treasury Bills by the Government of India.

## Key Points

- **Securities Offered**: 91 Day T-Bill (091T281026), 182 Day T-Bill (182T270127), and 364 Day T-Bill (364T290727).
- **Bidding Platform**: NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system (`https://ibbs.bseindia.com`).
- **Auction Date**: July 29, 2026.
- **Settlement Date**: July 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operates under the framework established for the non-competitive bidding facility to participate in RBI Government Securities auctions (Exchange circular no. 20180423-42).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must adhere to the specified bid collection timelines and lot size/investment limits.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date**: July 27, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability).
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors)**: July 28, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members)**: July 29, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- **Auction Date**: July 29, 2026.
- **Settlement Date**: July 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates participation of retail and institutional investors in Government of India Treasury Bill auctions through the exchange mechanism.
