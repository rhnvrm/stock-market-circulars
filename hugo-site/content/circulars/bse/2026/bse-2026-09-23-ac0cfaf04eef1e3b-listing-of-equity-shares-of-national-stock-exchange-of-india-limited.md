## Summary

Effective from Thursday, September 24, 2026, the equity shares of National Stock Exchange of India Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the Exchange in the 'A' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: National Stock Exchange of India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544937
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: NSE
- ISIN No.: INE721I01024
- No. of Securities: 2,475,000,000 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 1785/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 1/- and premium of Rs. 1784/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 22, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, September 24, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action has been executed to have the lock-in period marked in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of National Stock Exchange of India Limited equity shares for all trading members.
