## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability and revision of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on company XBRL submissions. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, movement to higher surveillance stages, and transfers to T, XT, MT, or TS trading groups.

## Key Points

- 100% margins are applicable effective October 26, 2026, on all open positions as of October 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moving to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework or subject to lower price bands take effect on October 22, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Group effective October 26, 2026.
- Selected securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective October 22, 2026, with their price bands reinstated.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition and revision of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) in conjunction with prevailing exchange surveillance mechanisms.
- Realignment of price bands and trading group classifications for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure adherence to 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions for the affected securities.
- Members and market participants should note that shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions and market surveillance.

## Important Dates

- October 22, 2026: Effective date for lower price bands, higher surveillance stage movements, and securities exiting LT-ASM.
- October 23, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margin.
- October 26, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and transfers to T / XT / MT / TS groups.

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading participation in shortlisted securities may be constrained due to 100% margin rules and lower price bands.
- Reinforces market surveillance, transparency, and investor protection.
