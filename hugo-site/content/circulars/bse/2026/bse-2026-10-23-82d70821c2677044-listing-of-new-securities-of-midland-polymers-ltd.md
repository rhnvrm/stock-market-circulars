## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 1,53,51,868 new equity shares of Midland Polymers Ltd. issued on a preferential basis to promoter and non-promoter categories, effective from October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Midland Polymers Ltd. (Scrip Code: 531597, ISIN: INE046M01036).
- Issue Size: 1,53,51,868 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at par on a preferential basis.
- Distribution Nos.: 668761 to 16020628.
- Date of Allotment: July 29, 2026.
- Effective Trading Date: Monday, October 26, 2026.
- Ranking: New shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Lock-in Details: As per Annexure I.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must ensure compliance with lock-in restrictions specified in Annexure I and standard exchange disclosure and listing norms.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: July 29, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up equity capital for Midland Polymers Ltd. through preferential allotment with standard locking and ranking pari-passu to existing shares.
