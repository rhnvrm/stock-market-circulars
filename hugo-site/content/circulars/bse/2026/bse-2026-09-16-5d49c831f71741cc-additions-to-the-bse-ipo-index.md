## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of three newly listed companies to the BSE IPO Index. Kanohar Electricals Limited, Prasol Chemicals Limited, and Glass Wall Systems (India) Limited will be included in the index effective at the open of Friday, September 18, 2026, following their listing on BSE.

## Key Points

- Kanohar Electricals Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544911), Prasol Chemicals Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544912), and Glass Wall Systems (India) Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544913) are added to the BSE IPO Index.
- The inclusion becomes effective at the open of trading on September 18, 2026.
- Actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for market participants; informational notice for index tracking and constituents.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine index adjustment with minimal direct market impact, affecting only portfolios and benchmarks tracking the BSE IPO Index.
