## Summary

BSE has updated the list of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing inclusions, movements to lower stages, and exclusions effective October 15 and October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 16, 2026, on all open positions as on October 15, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 15, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 16, 2026.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of enhanced surveillance measures, higher margin obligations, and trade-for-trade settlement for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement changes, and price band restrictions for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date (Stage II / moving out / lower stage): October 15, 2026
- Effective Date (100% margin / Trade-for-Trade shift): October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity and liquidity for shortlisted securities will be restricted due to mandatory 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement, and price band limitations.
