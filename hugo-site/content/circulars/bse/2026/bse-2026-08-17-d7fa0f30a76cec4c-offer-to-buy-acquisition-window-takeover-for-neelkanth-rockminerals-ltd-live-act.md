## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Acquisition Window (Takeover) offer of Neelkanth Rockminerals Ltd. The offer period is scheduled from Tuesday, August 18, 2026, to Tuesday, September 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Neelkanth Rockminerals Ltd (Security Symbol: NEELKAN, Scrip Code: 531049, ISIN: INE741D01015).
- Offer Size: Up to 13,11,362 Equity Shares with a face value of Rs. 10.00 at an Offer Price of Rs. 19.40.
- Opening Date: August 18, 2026; Closing Date: September 1, 2026.
- Market Lot: 1 equity share.
- Trading Members can bid for investors having shares in demat form.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. The notice pertains to standard operational scheduling for a corporate takeover offer under existing regulatory frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure that the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Members and custodians should refer to Exchange Notices no. 20150930-1 and 20170203-29 for operational participation instructions.

## Important Dates

- Opening Date: August 18, 2026 (9:15 a.m.)
- Closing Date: September 1, 2026 (3:30 p.m. / Take-up up to 4:00 p.m.)
- Give-up / Take-up Period: August 18, 2026 to August 31, 2026 (General), and September 1, 2026 (Final day).

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates orderly bidding, modification, cancellation, and settlement processes for trading and custodian members participating in the takeover offer of Neelkanth Rockminerals Ltd.
