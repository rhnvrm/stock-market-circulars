## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 14 companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS), effective from Thursday, September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 14
- Effective date for trading: September 17, 2026
- Securities category: Equity
- Includes issuances from prominent entities such as AU Small Finance Bank, Bajaj Electricals, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company, Dixon Technologies, and INOX India.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the newly listed securities and their respective lock-in statuses.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 16, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of equity capital base for the listed entities through ESOP/ESOS exercises with negligible market impact.
