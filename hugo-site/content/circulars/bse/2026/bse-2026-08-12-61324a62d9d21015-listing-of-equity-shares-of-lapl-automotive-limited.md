## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that the equity shares of LAPL Automotive Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in due course under the SME segment. The company's scrip details, including Scrip Code (544863), Symbol (LAPL), and ISIN (INE0X9I01023), have been designated, with listing dates and security details to be communicated through a separate notice.

## Key Points

- **Listing Approval:** The equity shares of LAPL Automotive Limited have been approved for listing and dealings on the BSE SME platform.
- **Scrip Details:**
  - **Scrip Code:** 544863
  - **Symbol:** LAPL
  - **ISIN:** INE0X9I01023
- **Listing Date:** The specific date of listing and additional details regarding the securities will be announced in a future, separate notice.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes or amendments introduced in this notice. It is a standard listing notice issued under BSE's listing and compliance framework for the SME segment.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no direct compliance requirements or actions required from trading members or market participants at this stage. Trading members should monitor future notices for the official listing and trading commencement date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026 (Wednesday)
- **Listing & Trading Commencement Date:** To be announced via a separate notice.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine notice concerning the upcoming listing of a single SME company and does not affect the broader market structure or regulations.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Trading members and market intermediaries will need to configure their trading systems and master databases with the new Scrip Code (544863) and Symbol (LAPL) once the listing date is finalized and trading begins.
