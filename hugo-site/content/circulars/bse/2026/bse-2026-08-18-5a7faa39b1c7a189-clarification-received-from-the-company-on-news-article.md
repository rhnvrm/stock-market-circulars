## Summary

The Exchange sought clarification from Aegis Logistics Ltd regarding news articles circulating in various media outlets to ensure accuracy and transparency for investors. The company's reply has been received and attached by the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Company involved: Aegis Logistics Ltd (Scrip Code: 500003)
- Purpose: Verification of news articles appearing in media/websites/television channels.
- Source referenced in notice: Economic Times

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to clarify news articles as requested by the exchange to prevent market rumors and misinformation.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 18 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational disclosure with no immediate direct market-wide impact.
