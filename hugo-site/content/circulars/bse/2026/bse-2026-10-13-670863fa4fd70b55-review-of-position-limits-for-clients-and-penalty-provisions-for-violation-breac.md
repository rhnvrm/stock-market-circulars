## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing all market participants about SEBI circular No. HO/47/16/13(5)2026-MRD-POD1/ I/20735/2026 dated September 09, 2026, concerning the review of position limits for clients and penalty provisions for violations or breaches of position limits in the commodity derivatives segment.

## Key Points

- References SEBI circular dated September 09, 2026 (Ref. No. HO/47/16/13(5)2026-MRD-POD1/ I/20735/2026).
- Applicable to the commodity derivatives segment.
- Covers comprehensive review of client position limits.
- Details penalty provisions for any violation or breach of established position limits.
- Attached annexure provides the complete text of the SEBI circular for market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adoption and implementation of revised SEBI guidelines on client position limits and breach penalties in commodity derivatives.

## Compliance Requirements

- All market participants, trading members, and clearing members must review their trading operations and risk management systems to ensure compliance with the updated position limits and penalty frameworks.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 13, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Strengthens risk management, market integrity, and compliance discipline within the commodity derivatives segment by enforcing standardized position limits and penalties.
