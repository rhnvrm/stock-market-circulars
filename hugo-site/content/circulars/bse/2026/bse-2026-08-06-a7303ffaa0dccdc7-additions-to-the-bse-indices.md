## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of four newly listed companies to its respective indices following their public offerings and listings on the mainboard and SME platforms.

## Key Points

- MV Electrosystems Limited (Ticker: 544851) and Juniper Green Energy Limited (Ticker: 544853) will be added to the BSE IPO index effective at the open of Monday, August 10, 2026.
- Oneindig Technologies Limited (Ticker: 544852) and Dhaval Packaging Limited (Ticker: 544854) will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open of Friday, August 07, 2026.
- These changes are incorporated in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for market participants; informational notice regarding index constituents.

## Important Dates

- August 07, 2026: Effective date for SME index additions.
- August 10, 2026: Effective date for mainboard IPO index additions.

## Impact Assessment

Standard index adjustments with minimal direct market impact beyond passive funds tracking the affected BSE indices.
