## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures and bonds of several companies due to fixed record dates for interest payment and redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures/bonds of the listed companies around the record and ex-dates.
- Affected companies include Satin Creditcare Network Limited, Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, Birla Corporation Ltd., NHPC Limited, NTPC Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Ltd., and others.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the debentures and bonds of the mentioned companies as per the specified schedule.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates and Ex-Dates range from August 27, 2026, to September 3, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of secondary market trading for specific debt instruments of the listed entities to facilitate corporate actions such as interest payment and redemption.
