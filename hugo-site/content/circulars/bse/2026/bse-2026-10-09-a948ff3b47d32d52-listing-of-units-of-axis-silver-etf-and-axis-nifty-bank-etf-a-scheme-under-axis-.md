## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units of two exchange-traded funds (ETFs) under Axis Mutual Fund, effective from Monday, October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Axis Nifty Bank ETF**: 5,188,279 units, Scrip Code: 544985, Group: B, ISIN: INF846K01X63, Scrip ID: BNKETFAXIS.
- **Axis Silver ETF**: 93,026,836 units, Scrip Code: 544984, Group: E, ISIN: INF846K011K1, Scrip ID: SILVERAXIS.
- **Investment Manager**: Axis Asset Management Company Limited.
- **Registrar and Transfer Agent**: Computer Age Management Services Limited (CAMS).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard trading admission and listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing and market maker details for smooth trading operations.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: October 12, 2026.
- **Notice Date**: October 9, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading access for investors in Axis Nifty Bank ETF and Axis Silver ETF on the BSE platform.
