## Summary

The Exchange has scheduled a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, August 08, 2026. Trading members utilizing third-party trading platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test various functionalities, including risk-reduction mode and trading halts.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Commodity Derivatives
- **Date of Mock Trading:** August 08, 2026
- **Log-in Time:** 09:15 AM to 10:00 AM
- **Continuous Trading:** 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM
- **Trade Modification:** 12:00 PM to 12:10 PM
- No new BOLT TWS Release is scheduled.
- Trades resulting from mock trading do not attract any margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out, or create rights and liabilities.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to participate actively in the mock trading session and provide their feedback by 1:00 PM via the provided contact numbers or email.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** August 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Zero financial or operational impact on live markets, as the session is strictly for testing and system familiarization.
