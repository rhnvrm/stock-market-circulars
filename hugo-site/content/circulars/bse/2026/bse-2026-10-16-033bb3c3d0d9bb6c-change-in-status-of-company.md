## Summary

Pursuant to an order issued by the Hon'ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT), the delisting of Sai Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 530905) stands rescinded. Consequently, the company's status will be changed from "Delisted" to "Suspended" with effect from October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sai Industries Ltd
- Scrip Code: 530905
- Previous Status: Delisted
- New Status: Suspended
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026
- Basis: Order issued by the Hon'ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT)

## Regulatory Changes

The delisting of Sai Industries Ltd has been officially rescinded following directions from the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT).

## Compliance Requirements

Provisions of Regulation 34(1) and Regulation 34(2) of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021 are not applicable to the company.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date of Status Change: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The change in status from delisted to suspended restores trading/listing purview under suspension protocols for Sai Industries Ltd, impacting market participants holding or tracking the scrip.
