## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 15,48,500 new equity shares of Eforu Entertainment Limited issued on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters, effective from October 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Eforu Entertainment Limited (Scrip Code: 531190)
- **Security Details:** 15,48,500 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 81/- (Issue Price: Rs. 91/- per share) on a preferential basis.
- **Effective Date:** October 1, 2026.
- **ISIN:** INE355H01015
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 5991901 to 7540400
- **Date of Allotment:** August 5, 2026
- **Lock-in Details:**
  - 2,98,900 shares (Dist. Nos. 5991901 to 6290800) locked in up to 30/04/2028.
  - 12,49,600 shares (Dist. Nos. 6290801 to 7540400) locked in up to 30/04/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in periods and trading restrictions as specified for the preferential shares.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** August 5, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry:** April 30, 2027 and April 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up capital base for Eforu Entertainment Limited with specified lock-in constraints on promoter and non-promoter shareholdings.
