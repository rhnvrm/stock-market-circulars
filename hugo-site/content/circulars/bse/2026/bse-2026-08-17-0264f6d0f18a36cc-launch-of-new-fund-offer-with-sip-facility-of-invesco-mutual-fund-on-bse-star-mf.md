## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) along with the SIP facility for Invesco India Pharma and Healthcare Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 18, 2026, to September 01, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Invesco India Pharma and Healthcare Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth and IDCW options).
- **NFO Period:** August 18, 2026, to September 01, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 07, 2026.
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 07, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (September 01, 2026).

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** August 18, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** September 01, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides trading members and investors access to subscribe to the new Invesco Mutual Fund scheme via the BSE StAR MF platform with standard NFO guidelines.
