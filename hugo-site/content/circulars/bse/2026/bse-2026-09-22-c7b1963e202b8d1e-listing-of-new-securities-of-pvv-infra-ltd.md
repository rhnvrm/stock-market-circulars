## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that trading is permitted in the new securities of PVV Infra Ltd. effective from Wednesday, September 23, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: PVV Infra Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 536659
- ISIN: INE428B01021
- Securities: 1,17,61,207 Equity shares of Rs.5/- each issued on rights basis pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up.
- Distinctive Numbers: 137812130 to 149573336
- Effective Date: September 23, 2026
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the pool of fully paid-up equity shares available for trading for PVV Infra Ltd.
