## Summary

DSP Mutual Fund is offering the DSP BSE MIDSMALL PRIVATE BANKS ETF - NFO and its direct plan from July 28, 2026, to July 30, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: DSP BSE MIDSMALL PRIVATE BANKS ETF and DSP BSE MIDSMALL PRIVATE BANKS ETF - DIRECT PLAN
- NFO Window: July 28, 2026, to July 30, 2026 (extended till midnight for lumpsum purchases)
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 03, 2026
- Category: Equity

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag shall be reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on July 31, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments must adhere to the clear funds deadline to remain eligible for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: July 28, 2026
- NFO End Date: July 30, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: July 31, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice for market participants and distributors using the BSE StAR MF platform for mutual fund transactions.
