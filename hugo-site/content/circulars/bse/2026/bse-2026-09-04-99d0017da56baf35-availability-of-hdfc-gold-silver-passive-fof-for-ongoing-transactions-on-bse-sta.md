## Summary

Following previous notices, the HDFC Gold Silver Passive FoF is reopening for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and related facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes reopen for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions from September 7, 2026.
- Facilities such as SIP, STP, and SWP are available.
- Applicable to both regular and direct growth plans of the HDFC Gold Silver Passive FoF.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants must ensure transactions are processed according to standard BSE StAR MF operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and members can resume regular transactions, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs in the specified HDFC mutual fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform.
