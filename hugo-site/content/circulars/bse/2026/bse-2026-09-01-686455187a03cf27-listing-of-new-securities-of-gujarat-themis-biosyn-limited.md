Trading members are informed that 2,11,86,440 new equity shares of Gujarat Themis Biosyn Limited have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from September 02, 2026, following allotment to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) via a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP).
