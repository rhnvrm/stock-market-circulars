## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of two newly listed companies, Century Business Media Limited and Injecto Polymers Limited, to the BSE SME IPO index, effective from the open of Tuesday, September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Century Business Media Limited (Exchange ticker: 544928) and Injecto Polymers Limited (Exchange ticker: 544930) are being added to the BSE SME IPO index.
- The inclusion is effective at the open of Tuesday, September 22, 2026.
- These stocks were listed on BSE effective Monday, September 21, 2026.
- The actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).
