## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that AvenuesAI Ltd. has forfeited 69,43,139 partly paid-up equity shares due to the non-payment of allotment and call monies.

## Key Points

- Company Name: AvenuesAI Ltd. (Scrip Code: 539807)
- Forfeited Shares: 69,43,139 equity shares of Re. 1/- each.
- Distinctive Numbers: 3482537931 to 3489481069.
- Reason for Forfeiture: Non-payment of allotment/call monies on shares issued under a rights issue.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; standard operational notice regarding share forfeiture.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the share forfeiture and update their records accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts shareholders who failed to pay call monies, leading to the extinguishment of their partly paid-up shareholdings in AvenuesAI Ltd.
