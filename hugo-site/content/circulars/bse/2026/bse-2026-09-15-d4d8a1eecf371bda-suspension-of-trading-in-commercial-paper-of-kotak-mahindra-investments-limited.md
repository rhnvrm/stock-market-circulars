## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in the Commercial Paper of Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited on account of its redemption record date.

- Company Name: Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited
- Scrip Code: 732522
- Scrip ID: KMIL-17-09-26-CP
- ISIN: INE975F14D65
- Record Date: 16/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 16/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Paper of the company starting from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 16 September 2026
- Ex-Date: 16 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for commercial paper redemption with no broader market impact.
