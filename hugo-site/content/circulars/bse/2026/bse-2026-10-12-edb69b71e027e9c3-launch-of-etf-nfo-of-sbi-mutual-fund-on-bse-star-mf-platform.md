## Summary

SBI Mutual Fund is offering the SBI BSE India Defence ETF and SBI BSE India Defence ETF - Direct Plan NFO on the BSE StAR MF Platform from October 13, 2026, to October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme names: SBI BSE INDIA DEFENCE ETF and SBI BSE INDIA DEFENCE ETF - DIRECT PLAN.
- NFO period: October 13, 2026, to October 19, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 19, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative allotment date: October 26, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with an 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 23, 2026.
- Cheque, OTM, and switch transactions are subject to timely receipt of clear funds prior to allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: October 13, 2026
- NFO End Date: October 19, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 23, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides members and investors clear operational guidelines and timelines for participating in the SBI BSE India Defence ETF NFO.
