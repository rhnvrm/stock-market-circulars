## Summary

BSE has issued the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for JAI MATA GLASS LTD. The offer opens on Friday, August 21, 2026, and closes on Friday, September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: JAI MATA GLASS LTD (Symbol: JAIMATAG, ISIN: INE250C01027, Scrip Code: 523467)
- Offer Size: Up to 2,60,00,000 Equity Shares
- Face Value: Rs. 1.00 | Offer Price: Rs. 1.85
- Opening Date: Friday, 21st August 2026
- Closing Date: Friday, 4th September 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members to ensure UCC of the Investor is registered and/or updated stating the correct PAN Number and Category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading Member(s) can bid for Investors having shares in demat form.
- Members and Custodians to note that if no securities are accepted there will be no End of Issue files generated in Extranet.

## Important Dates

- Bid Offering Period: 21st August 2026 to 4th September 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Bid Modifications/Cancellation: 21st August 2026 to 4th September 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Give-up / Take-up: 21st August 2026 to 3rd September 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Give-up (last day of the offer): 4th September 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Take-up (last day of the offer): 4th September 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and custodians participating in the takeover offer must follow the specified session timings and platform accessibility links (iBBS and RTRMS) to ensure smooth bidding and settlement.
