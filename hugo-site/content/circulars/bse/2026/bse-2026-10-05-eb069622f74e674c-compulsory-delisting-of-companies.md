## Summary

BSE has announced the compulsory delisting of Poddar Housing and Development Ltd (Scrip Code: 523628) effective from October 07, 2026, pursuant to the order of the Exchange's Delisting Committee under SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021, following a suspension period exceeding 6 months.

## Key Points

- Affected Company: Poddar Housing and Development Ltd (Scrip Code: 523628).
- Effective Date: October 07, 2026.
- Reason for Delisting: Company remained suspended for more than 6 months.
- Post-Delisting Action: The company will be moved to the Dissemination Board of the Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities cease to be listed and are no longer available for trading on the Exchange platform.
- Promoters, whole-time directors, persons responsible for compliance, and related entities are prohibited from directly or indirectly accessing the securities market, seeking listing, or acting as intermediaries for 10 years.
- Promoters and directors are ineligible to become directors of any listed company until public exit requirements are fulfilled.

## Compliance Requirements

- Promoters must provide an exit option to public shareholders by purchasing shares at a fair value determined by an independent valuer appointed by the Exchange.
- All equity shares held by the promoter/promoter group are frozen regarding transfers (sale, pledge, etc.) and corporate benefits (dividends, rights, bonus, split, etc.) until the public exit option is successfully provided.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Delisting Date: October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Public shareholders are entitled to a fair-value exit option backed by an independent valuation, while promoters and key management face stringent 10-year market prohibitions, directorship bans, and asset/benefit freezes until compliance is achieved.
