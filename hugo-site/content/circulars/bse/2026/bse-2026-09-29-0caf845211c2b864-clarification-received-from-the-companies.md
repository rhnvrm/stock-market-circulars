## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movement in certain scrips in the recent past and sought clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information. Replies received from several companies are attached to the notice, while replies from other companies are awaited.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from 9 companies: Complete Sports and Management India Ltd (544900), Dynamic Portfolio Management & Services Ltd (530779), GEM Enviro Management Ltd (544199), Liotech Industries Ltd (544796), Raj Television Network Ltd (532826), Sham Foam Ltd (544869), Syschem India Ltd (531173), Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Ltd (532371), and Turtlemint Fintech Solutions Ltd (544799).
- Replies awaited from 16 companies: Billwin Industries Ltd (543209), Citichem India Ltd (544324), CMX Holdings Ltd (532217), Deep Polymers Ltd (541778), El Forge Ltd (531144), Farm Peace Ltd (544905), Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Ltd (544904), Jhandewalas Foods Ltd (540850), Kunststoffe Industries Ltd (523594), Mafia Trends Ltd (543613), Mopshop Distribution Ltd (544883), Rex Sealing and Packing Industries Ltd (543744), S. M. Gold Ltd (542034), Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd (543541), Vikram Aroma Ltd (544371), and Vellora Impact Ltd (531257).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine surveillance disclosure notice issued by BSE.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies experiencing significant price and volume movements are required to provide timely clarifications to the stock exchange as part of market surveillance and disclosure norms.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 29 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Promotes market transparency and investor protection by publicly identifying scrips with abnormal price/volume activity and sharing company responses.
