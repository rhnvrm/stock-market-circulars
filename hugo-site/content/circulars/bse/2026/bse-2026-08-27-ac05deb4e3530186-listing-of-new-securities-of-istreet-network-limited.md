## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of iStreet Network Limited effective from August 28, 2026, pursuant to the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: iStreet Network Limited
- Scrip Code: 524622
- ISIN: INE532B01020
- Securities Issued: 10,83,334 equity shares of Rs. 4/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 2/-
- Allotment Date: 06/05/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: August 28, 2026
- Lock-in details apply to specified distribution numbers through March 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the new securities listing and lock-in details.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 06/05/2026
- Effective Trading Date: 28/08/2026
- Lock-in Upto Dates: 04/03/2027 and 05/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase via warrant conversion for the listed entity.
