## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective September 30, 2026, specific securities have satisfied criteria to be placed under the framework, while others are moving out or classified due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25%.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I are placed under the IBC surveillance framework effective September 30, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the IBC framework are listed in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) < 25% are listed in Annexure IV.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic review framework continues in conjunction with prevailing exchange surveillance measures to monitor companies undergoing insolvency proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must note the revised list of securities under the IBC surveillance framework and adhere to applicable price bands and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants holding or trading affected securities will experience price band modifications and heightened surveillance monitoring in accordance with IBC guidelines.
