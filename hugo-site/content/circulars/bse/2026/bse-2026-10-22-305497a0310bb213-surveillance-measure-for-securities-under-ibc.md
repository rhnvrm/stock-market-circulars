## Summary

BSE has announced surveillance measures and updates for securities under the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the criteria are placed under Additional Surveillance Measures for IBC with effect from October 23, 2026 (Annexure I).
- List of securities moving out of the IBC Framework is provided in Annexure II, with price bands reinstated.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) below 25% are listed in Annexure IV.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures and price band revisions for companies undergoing corporate insolvency resolution process under IBC.
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the surveillance status of securities and adhere to applicable price bands and trading restrictions.
- Members can contact bse.surv@bseindia.com for clarifications.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Notice Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects trading and price bands of specified securities undergoing insolvency resolution or moving out of the IBC surveillance framework.
