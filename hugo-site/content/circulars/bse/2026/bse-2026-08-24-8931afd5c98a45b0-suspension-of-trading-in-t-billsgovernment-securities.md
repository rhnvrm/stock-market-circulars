## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities due to their maturity on the redemption date.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects Scrip Code 805111 (364TB28826, ISIN: IN002025Z229) and Scrip Code 805220 (91TB28826, ISIN: IN002026X081).
- Trading members are advised not to deal in these securities starting from August 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from trading in the specified T-bills and Government Securities effective from August 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to the cessation of secondary market trading in the matured government securities.
