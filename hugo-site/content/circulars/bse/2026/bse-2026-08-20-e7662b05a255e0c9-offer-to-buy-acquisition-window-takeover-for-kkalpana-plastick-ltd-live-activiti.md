## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the schedule of activities for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for shares of KKALPANA PLASTICK LTD. The offer is scheduled from Friday, 21st August 2026 to Friday, 4th September 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** KKALPANA PLASTICK LTD
- **Security Symbol:** KKPLASTICK
- **ISIN:** INE465K01016
- **Scrip Code:** 523652
- **Offer Size:** Upto 14,37,420 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 28.00 per share
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00
- **Market Lot:** 1

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational schedule notice for an ongoing corporate takeover offer.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of investors is registered/updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bids can be placed for investors having shares in demat form.
- Access URLs for iBBS, RTRMS, and Extranet platforms are provided for participation and reporting.

## Important Dates

- **Opening Date:** Friday, 21st August 2026
- **Closing Date:** Friday, 4th September 2026
- **Bid Offering / Modification Period:** 21st August 2026 to 4th September 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- **Give-up / Take-up (Last day of offer):** 4th September 2026 (Give-up up to 3.30 p.m., Take-up up to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the orderly execution and bidding process for shareholders and trading members participating in the takeover offer of KKALPANA PLASTICK LTD.
