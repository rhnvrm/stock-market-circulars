## Summary

BSE issued a notice regarding the opening of the Offer for Sale (OFS) for Knowledge Realty Trust (the "Trust"). The sellers (BREP Asia entities and Sponsor group members) have exercised the oversubscription option for an additional 37,00,00,000 Units, bringing the total Offer Units to 11,10,000,000 Units (representing 25.03% of the Unit capital of the Trust).

## Key Points

- **Sellers:** BREP Asia SG L&T Holding (NQ) Pte. Ltd., BREP Asia SG DRPL Holding (NQ) Pte. Ltd., BREP Asia II Indian Holding Co IV (NQ) Pte. Ltd., and BREP Asia II Indian Holding Co VII (NQ) Pte. Ltd., along with other Sponsor group members.
- **Base Offer Size:** Up to 74,00,00,000 Units (16.69% of unit capital).
- **Oversubscription Option:** Up to 37,00,00,000 Units (8.34% of unit capital).
- **Total Offer Units (upon exercising oversubscription):** 11,10,000,000 Units (25.03% of the unit capital).
- **Retail Reservation:** 10% in aggregate of the Base Offer Units and Oversubscription Option would be reserved for allocation to Retail Investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Bidding windows open on designated stock exchanges (BSE and NSE) for non-retail and retail investors according to the OFS guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **T Day (August 31, 2026):** Opening for Non-Retail Investors only.
- **T+1 Day (September 01, 2026):** Opening for Retail Investors and Non-Retail Investors carrying forward un-allotted bids.

## Impact Assessment

Significant trading activity expected in the units of Knowledge Realty Trust due to the sizeable Offer for Sale and exercised oversubscription option.
