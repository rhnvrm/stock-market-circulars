## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 8 companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS), effective from Thursday, August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 8
- Effective date of trading: August 27, 2026
- Companies include Amagi Media Labs Limited, Carborundum Universal Limited, Federal Bank Limited, Ideaforge Technology Limited, PNB Housing Finance Limited, Poonawalla Fincorp Limited, Rose Merc. Limited, and Samhi Hotels Limited.
- All issued securities are admitted with no lock-in (NA).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the listing of these securities for their records and trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 26, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to the updating of scrip details and share capital for the respective listed companies.
