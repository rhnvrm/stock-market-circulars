## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Raj Polypack Limited on September 30, 2026. The document is hosted on the SME website for public comments for a period of at least 21 days pursuant to the ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Raj Polypack Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice
- Hosting Website: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

None. Complies with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public may submit comments on the draft offer document via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Comment Deadline: 21 days from October 01, 2026 (approx. October 22, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites public scrutiny for the proposed SME IPO of Raj Polypack Limited.
