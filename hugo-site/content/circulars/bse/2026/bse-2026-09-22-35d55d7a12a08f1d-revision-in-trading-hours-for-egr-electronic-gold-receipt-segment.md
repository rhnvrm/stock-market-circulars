## Summary

BSE has announced a revision in the trading hours for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment, effective from November 02, 2026, to March 12, 2027 (both days inclusive), following changes in US daylight saving timings.

## Key Points

- Revised trading hours apply to the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment.
- Effective duration is from November 02, 2026, to March 12, 2027.
- Continuous Trading session timing is set from 09:00 AM to 11:55 PM.
- Trade Modification Timings are extended until 11:59 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Seasonal adjustment in market trading hours corresponding to the US daylight saving schedule changes referenced from prior exchange notice no. 20260619-10.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to take note of the revised timings and ensure operational alignment for the EGR segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Start Date: November 02, 2026
- Effective End Date: March 12, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact on general market operations; affects only trading participants active in the EGR segment during evening hours.
