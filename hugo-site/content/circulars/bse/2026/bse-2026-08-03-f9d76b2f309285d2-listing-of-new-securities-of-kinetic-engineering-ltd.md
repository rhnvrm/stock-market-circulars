## Summary

Trading members are informed that 31,00,000 new equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each of Kinetic Engineering Ltd. (Scrip Code - 500240) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Tuesday, August 4, 2026. These shares were issued at a premium of Rs. 161/- to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kinetic Engineering Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 500240
- ISIN: INE266B01017
- Number of Shares: 31,00,000 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 171/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/- + Premium: Rs. 161/-)
- Allotment Date: 11/03/2026
- Distribution Nos.: 113919124 to 117019123
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the lock-in details and distribution numbers for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Trading: August 4, 2026
- Date of Allotment: March 11, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: February 28, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase via preferential allotment of equity shares resulting from warrant conversion, with standard lock-in applicable to promoter shares.
