## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 16,000 new equity shares of Chatha Foods Limited (Scrip Code: 544151), issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants, effective from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Chatha Foods Limited
- Scrip Code: 544151
- ISIN: INE0AH901011
- Number of Shares: 16,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 117/- (Issue Price: Rs. 127/- per share).
- Distribution Nos.: 24184880 to 24200879
- Date of Allotment: 10/08/2026
- Lock-in Details: 16,000 shares locked in up to 14/05/2028.
- Effective Date: Trading permitted with effect from Monday, October 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard corporate listing action.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in periods and trading parameters for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 10, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: October 12, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 14, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine preferential allotment and warrant conversion with minimal immediate impact on market liquidity or capitalization structure.
