## Summary

Edelweiss Asset Management Limited has issued a notice regarding a change in the scheme name and fundamental attributes of the Edelweiss Income Plus Arbitrage Active Fund of Funds, effective March 16, 2026, pursuant to Regulation 18(15A) of SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996.

## Key Points

- Notice cum addendum issued for Edelweiss Income Plus Arbitrage Active Fund of Funds.
- Changes categorized as Fundamental Attribute Change (FAC).
- Effective date for the changes is March 16, 2026.
- Approval obtained from Edelweiss Trusteeship Company Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with Regulation 18(15A) of the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 1996, regarding changes in fundamental attributes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Implementation of the process for change in fundamental attributes as prescribed by SEBI.
- Notification to unit holders regarding the proposed changes and provision of exit options where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: March 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Existing unit holders are impacted by the fundamental attribute changes and will be provided appropriate options as per regulatory guidelines.
