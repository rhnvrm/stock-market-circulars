## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the activation of the Call Money Module of the IBBS platform (Internet Based Book Building Software) for Ace Software Exports Limited. The company is raising the first call money of Rs. 30.25 per share on its partly paid-up equity shares (Security Symbol: ASELCM).

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Ace Software Exports Limited
- **Type of Call:** First Call
- **Security Type:** Partly Paid Equity Shares (Symbol: ASELCM, Market Lot: 1)
- **Number of Partly Paid-up Equity Shares:** 54,71,101
- **Call Money Amount:** Rs. 30.25 per share payable
- **Registrar to the Issue:** MUFG Intime India Private Limited (formerly Link Intime India Private Limited)

## Regulatory Changes

- In accordance with SEBI circular dated December 8, 2020, the Call Money Module is made available to Self-Certified Syndicate Banks (SCSBs) and Trading Members (with 3-in-1 type accounts) to submit details of call money collected.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members, SCSBs, and intermediaries are required to note the activation of the IBBS platform module and facilitate the collection and submission of call money details during the designated issue period.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** Wednesday, September 02, 2026
- **Issue Closing Date:** Wednesday, September 16, 2026
- **Bidding Period:** 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily during the issue period

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders holding partly paid-up shares of Ace Software Exports Limited must pay the first call money of Rs. 30.25 per share within the stipulated bidding period through authorized SCSBs or trading members to avoid default or forfeiture actions.
