## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the purpose of payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in the debentures of these companies will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260903-12, dated September 3, 2026.
- Segment: Debt.
- Affected companies include J.K. Cement Ltd, Vivriti Capital Limited, Aavas Financiers Limited, Mizuho Capsave Finance Private Limited, MAS Financial Services Limited, and Sammaan Capital Limited.
- Trading will resume with reduced face values reflecting the part redemptions.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for executing trades in the respective NCDs accurately.

## Important Dates

- **J.K. Cement Ltd:** Record Date & Ex-Date: 04/09/2026 (Reduced Face Value: Rs. 37,500/- Per debenture)
- **Vivriti Capital Limited:** Record Date & Ex-Date: 09/09/2026 (Reduced Face Value: Rs. 74,296/- Per Debenture)
- **Aavas Financiers Limited:** Record Date & Ex-Date: 10/09/2026 (Reduced Face Value: Rs. 1,00,000/- Per Debenture)
- **Mizuho Capsave Finance Private Limited (INE0DBJ07226):** Record Date & Ex-Date: 10/09/2026 (Reduced Face Value: Rs. 66,664/- Per Debenture)
- **MAS Financial Services Limited:** Record Date & Ex-Date: 10/09/2026 (Reduced Face Value: Rs. 83,333.33/- Per Debenture)
- **Mizuho Capsave Finance Private Limited (INE0DBJ07234):** Record Date & Ex-Date: 10/09/2026 (Reduced Face Value: Rs. 66,666/- Per Debenture)
- **Sammaan Capital Limited (INE148I07PK6):** Record Date & Ex-Date: 11/09/2026 (Reduced Face Value: Rs. 400/- Per Debenture)
- **Sammaan Capital Limited (INE148I07PL4):** Record Date & Ex-Date: 11/09/2026 (Reduced Face Value: Rs. 400/- Per Debenture)

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the affected NCDs will receive part redemption payments, and subsequent secondary market trading will occur at the adjusted lower face values.
