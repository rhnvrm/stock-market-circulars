## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the strike price intervals applicable for stock options in the equity derivatives segment, effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable for stock options in equity derivatives.
- Effective date: September 30, 2026.
- Detailed intervals are provided in the attached Annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Specification of strike price intervals for underlying scrips in equity derivatives.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to note the updated strike price intervals for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational update for trading members participating in equity derivatives.
