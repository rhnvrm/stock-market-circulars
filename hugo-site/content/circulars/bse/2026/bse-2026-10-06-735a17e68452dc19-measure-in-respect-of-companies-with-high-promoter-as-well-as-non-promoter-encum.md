## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding measures for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities listed in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments, while securities in Annexure II are eligible to move out of the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Effective date for margin implementation is October 09, 2026, on all open positions as on October 08, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities in Annexure II move out of the framework effective October 07, 2026.
- A consolidated list is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of minimum 75% margin requirements for companies exhibiting high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the application of minimum 75% margins on open positions and new positions for securities specified in Annexure I effective from October 09, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **October 07, 2026**: Securities in Annexure II move out of the framework.
- **October 08, 2026**: Cut-off date for open positions subject to the margin requirement.
- **October 09, 2026**: Minimum 75% margin effective date for Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding open positions in securities identified with high encumbrance will face significantly higher margin requirements (minimum 75%), affecting capital allocation and liquidity for those specific counters.
