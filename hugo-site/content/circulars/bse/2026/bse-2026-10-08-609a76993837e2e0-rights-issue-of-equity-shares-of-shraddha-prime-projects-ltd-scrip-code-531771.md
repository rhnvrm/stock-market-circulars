## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Shraddha Prime Projects Ltd has fixed October 15, 2026 as the Record Date for its upcoming rights issue of equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Issue Ratio**: 03 (Three) Equity Shares for every 20 (Twenty) Equity Shares held as on the Record Date.
- **Issue Price**: Rs. 160/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- each at a premium of Rs. 150/- per share).
- **Payment Terms**: Rs. 144/- per share payable on application and Rs. 16/- per share on subsequent call.
- **Record Date**: October 15, 2026.
- **Ex-Right Basis Date**: October 15, 2026 (Settlement No.: DR- 736/2026-2027).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure transactions in the company's equity shares are conducted on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: October 8, 2026
- **Record Date**: October 15, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Date**: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are eligible for the rights entitlement. Stock price may adjust on the ex-rights date reflecting the rights issue terms.
