## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, August 22, 2026. Trading members using third-party platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their applications under exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, August 22, 2026.
- No new BOLT TWS Release.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin or pay-in/pay-out obligations.
- Active participation and feedback requested from members by 12:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to actively participate in the mock trading session to test their systems and provide feedback.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 22, 2026
- Log-in Time: 08:15 AM to 09:00 AM
- Continuous Trading: 09:00 AM to 12:00 PM
- Trade Modification: 12:00 PM to 12:10 PM
- Feedback Submission Deadline: August 22, 2026, by 12:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Operational drill for testing trading applications with no financial or settlement impact.
