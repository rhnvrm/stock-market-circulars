## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice announcing the revision of transaction charges in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment. To encourage active market participation and enhance liquidity in the EGR segment, BSE has decided to completely waive transaction charges. No transaction fees will be levied in this segment with immediate effect until further notice.

## Key Points

- **Zero Transaction Charges**: BSE will not levy any transaction charges on trades executed in the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment.
- **Encouraged Participation**: This decision aims to encourage active trading and enhance participant engagement in EGRs.
- **Immediate Effect**: The waiver is effective starting from the notice date, August 12, 2026.
- **Support and Clarifications**: Trading members may contact their respective Relationship Managers for further queries.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Suspension of Transaction Levies**: Complete waiver/removal of transaction fees in the EGR segment until further notice. This suspends any active transaction fee schedule previously set for EGR trades.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members' Actions**: Members must configure their trading, billing, and back-office systems to ensure that transaction charges are set to zero for all EGR trades beginning August 12, 2026.
- **Client Dissemination**: Trading members should pass on the benefit of this transaction charge waiver to their clients and inform them accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 12, 2026 (Immediate effect)
- **Waiver Period**: Valid from August 12, 2026, until further notice

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: This is a positive development for market participants. The waiver lowers transactional friction and trading costs in Electronic Gold Receipts, likely prompting higher volumes and better liquidity. The operational implementation is straightforward, requiring only a simple billing/system update by the trading members.
