## Summary

The President of India, acting through the Ministry of Mines, Government of India, is offering up to 2,90,10,721 Equity Shares (representing 3.00% of the total issued and paid-up equity share capital) of Hindustan Copper Limited as the Base Offer Size, with an additional oversubscription option of up to 2,90,10,721 Equity Shares (3.00%). The total offer size represents 6.00% of the total paid-up equity share capital of the company. The floor price for the Offer is set at Rs. 514/- per Equity Share.

## Key Points

- **Promoter & Seller:** The President of India, represented by the Ministry of Mines, Government of India.
- **Company:** Hindustan Copper Limited.
- **Base Offer Size:** 2,90,10,721 Equity Shares (3.00% of total equity).
- **Oversubscription Option:** Up to 2,90,10,721 Equity Shares (3.00% of total equity).
- **Total Offer Size:** Up to 5,80,21,442 Equity Shares (6.00% of total equity).
- **Floor Price:** Rs. 514/- per Equity Share.
- **Employee Offer:** Up to 25,000 equity shares (0.0026% of Offer Shares) offered to eligible employees (who may apply for shares up to Rs. 5,00,000/-).

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI guidelines on Offer for Sale (OFS) through Stock Exchange Mechanism including circulars SEBI/HO/MRD/DOPI/CIR/P/2018/159, SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-3/P/CIR/2023/10, and SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-3/P/CIR/2024/82.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must adhere to the revised guidelines for bidding in the OFS segment issued via prior exchange notices and SEBI frameworks.
- Non-retail investors and retail investors must place bids on their respective designated dates.

## Important Dates

- **T Day (August 25, 2026):** Opening of Offer for Sale for Non-Retail Investors only.
- **T+1 Day (August 26, 2026):** Opening for Retail Investors, Employees, and Non-Retail Investors from T Day carrying forward un-allotted bids.

## Impact Assessment

- Significant liquidity event for Hindustan Copper Limited with government disinvestment of up to 6.0% equity.
- Direct trading impact on BSE and NSE through a separate designated OFS window.
