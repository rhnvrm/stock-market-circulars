## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities included in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective September 16, 2026. Securities in Annexure II are moving out of the framework, while a consolidated list is provided in Annexure III.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Margin applies to all open positions as on September 15, 2026, and new positions created from September 16, 2026.
- Securities in Annexure II move out of the framework effective September 11, 2026.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Measures are in conjunction with other prevailing exchange measures and subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margin requirements (minimum 75%) for securities identified under the high encumbrance framework as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the inclusion and exclusion of securities and ensure compliance with the 75% margin requirement for affected open and new positions from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Exclusion Date (Annexure II): September 11, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date for Open Positions: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Requirements: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations for market participants holding positions in securities listed under Annexure I, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage in those scrips.
