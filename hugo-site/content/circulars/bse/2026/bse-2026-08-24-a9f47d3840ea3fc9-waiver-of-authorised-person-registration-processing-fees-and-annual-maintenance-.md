## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the waiver of registration processing fees and Annual Maintenance Charges (AMC) for Authorised Persons (APs) operating in the Commodity Derivatives and Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segments, effective from August 24, 2026, to September 30, 2028.

## Key Points

- Processing fees for new and existing AP registrations in Commodity Derivatives and EGR segments are waived.
- Waiver period is from August 24, 2026, to September 30, 2028.
- AMC is waived for existing and new APs registered exclusively in the Commodity Derivatives and EGR segments.
- APs registered in other segments alongside Commodity Derivatives or EGR will continue to pay applicable AMC as per standard structure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary fee exemption on AP registrations and maintenance for specific segments to boost market intermediary participation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible intermediaries can onboard or register in the commodity derivatives and EGR segments without incurring processing fees during the specified window.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Start Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Effective End Date:** September 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- Reduces cost barriers for intermediaries expanding into or operating within the commodity derivatives and electronic gold receipt segments on the BSE platform.
