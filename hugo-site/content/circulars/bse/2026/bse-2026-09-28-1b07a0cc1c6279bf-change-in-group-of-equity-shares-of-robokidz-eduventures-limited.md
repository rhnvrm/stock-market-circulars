## Summary

BSE has announced that equity shares of Robokidz Eduventures Limited (SME IPO, Scrip Code: 544940) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Tuesday, October 13, 2026, following Notice No. 20260925-42.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Robokidz Eduventures Limited
- Scrip Code: 544940
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 13, 2026
- Reference Notice: 20260925-42 dated September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in equity shares of the company is moved out of the Trade for Trade settlement restriction into normal rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading systems and take note of the change in segment and group for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for the scrip, potentially improving liquidity and trading convenience for investors.
