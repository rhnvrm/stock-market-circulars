## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-701/2026-27. The auction offers will be accepted on trading terminals on August 27, 2026, followed by pay-in obligations on August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement Number: DR-701/2026-27.
- Auction Offer Entry Timing: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on August 27, 2026.
- Auction Matching Timing: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on August 27, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Friday, August 28, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- Following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment, settlement is executed through the Clearing Member of a Trading Member.
- The restriction preventing trading members from offering securities in an auction for the settlement in which they short-delivered those securities has been removed.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must exercise utmost care while punching security codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members are advised to refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the "Auction offer Entry Screen" and monitor notices regarding corporate actions (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split, etc.).

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 27, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process ensuring smooth clearing and settlement of short deliveries in rolling settlements without broader systemic impact.
