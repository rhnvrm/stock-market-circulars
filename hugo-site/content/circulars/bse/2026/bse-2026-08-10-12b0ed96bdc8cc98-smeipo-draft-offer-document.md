## Summary

Logix Built Solutions Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with BSE for its SME Initial Public Offering (IPO). The document is hosted on the BSE SME website and is available for public comments for a period of at least 21 days in compliance with ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Logix Built Solutions Limited
- **Filing Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Department:** DCS-Listing (SME Segment)
- **Public Comments Deadline:** Within 21 days from the date of the notice
- **Feedback Email:** equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None. The disclosure is made pursuant to Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments or objections on the draft offer document must be submitted to BSE via email within 21 days of the notice date.

## Important Dates

- **Draft Filing Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Public Comments Window:** August 10, 2026, to August 31, 2026 (approx. 21 days)

## Impact Assessment

Initiates the public review phase for Logix Built Solutions Limited's proposed SME IPO offering.
