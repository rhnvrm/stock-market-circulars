## Summary

Shardul Securities Limited has announced the opening of its buyback offer (acquisition window) through the stock exchange mechanism under SEBI tender-offer guidelines. The company proposes to buy back up to 1,92,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each, representing 21.94% of the total issued and paid-up equity share capital, at a price of Rs. 60/- per equity share for an aggregate consideration of up to Rs. 1,15,20,00,000/-.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Shardul Securities Limited
- **Buyback Size:** Up to 1,92,00,000 equity shares (21.94% of total paid-up equity).
- **Buyback Price:** Rs. 60/- per equity share payable in cash.
- **Aggregate Consideration:** Up to Rs. 1,15,20,00,000/- (Rupees One Hundred Fifteen Crore Twenty Lakh Only).
- **Record Date:** Thursday, October 8, 2026.
- **Offer Period:** Wednesday, October 14, 2026, to Wednesday, October 21, 2026 (Excluding SEBI Holiday on Tuesday, October 20, 2026).
- **Categories:** Divided into (i) reserved category for small shareholders and (ii) general category for all other shareholders.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted pursuant to SEBI circulars (CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131) and relevant BSE exchange notices regarding the acquisition of shares through stock exchange under tender offers for buyback.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must adhere to the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through the stock exchange mechanism during the tender offer window.
- Eligible shareholders holding equity shares as of the record date can participate via the tender offer process.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** October 21, 2026 (excluding SEBI holiday on October 20, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity to eligible shareholders of Shardul Securities Limited through a proportionate buyback at Rs. 60/- per share.
- Impacts company capital structure by extinguishing up to 21.94% of equity shares, representing 24.92% and 14.16% of paid-up capital and free reserves as of March 31, 2026.
