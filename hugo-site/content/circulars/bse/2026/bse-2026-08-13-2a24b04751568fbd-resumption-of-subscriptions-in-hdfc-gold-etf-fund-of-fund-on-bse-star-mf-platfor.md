## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about the resumption of lumpsum subscriptions and switch-in transactions in the HDFC Gold ETF Fund of Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, following a previous temporary suspension.

## Key Points

- Subscriptions for HDFC Gold ETF Fund of Fund were previously temporarily suspended vide Notice No. 20260605-43 dated June 05, 2026.
- Lumpsum investments (fresh and additional purchases) and Switch-In transactions are re-enabled without any restriction.
- Changes have been implemented on the BSE StAR MF Platform following communication from HDFC Mutual Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an operational update regarding a specific mutual fund scheme's subscription status.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and distributors are advised to take note of the resumption for executing transactions under the scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors can resume lumpsum investments and switch-ins into the HDFC Gold ETF Fund of Fund seamlessly through the BSE StAR MF platform.
