## Summary

This notice provides the live activities schedule and operational parameters for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window / Takeover) for Dukes Offshore Limited (Security Symbol: DUKEOFS, Scrip Code: 531471, ISIN: INE397G01019). The offer runs from Thursday, August 6, 2026, to Friday, August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Dukes Offshore Limited
- **Security Symbol:** DUKEOFS
- **ISIN:** INE397G01019
- **Scrip Code:** 531471
- **Offer Size:** Up to 25,62,872 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 30.00 per share
- **Opening Date:** Thursday, 6th August 2026
- **Closing Date:** Friday, 19th August 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational guidelines governed by previous Exchange Notices no. 20150930-1 and 20170203-29.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bids can be placed for investors holding shares in demat form through the iBBS platform.

## Important Dates

- **Opening Date:** 6th August 2026
- **Closing Date:** 19th August 2026
- **Bid Offering & Modification Period:** 6th August 2026 to 19th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- **Give-up / Take-up Window:** 6th August 2026 to 19th August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates the smooth execution and settlement of the takeover offer for shareholders of Dukes Offshore Limited through the dedicated acquisition window.
