## Summary

BSE has announced the allocation of a new ISIN number for Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Ltd following the sub-division of its equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: KIRLOSKAR PNEUMATIC COMPANY LTD
- Scrip Code: 505283
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares from Rs. 2/- to Re. 1/-
- New ISIN: INE811A01038

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the new ISIN for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: 18-08-2026
- Notice Date: 17-08-2026

## Impact Assessment

Trades will take place under the new ISIN starting from the ex-date.
