## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding active trading members using algorithmic software to submit their Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) applications and systems reporting for the half-yearly period from April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mandatory submission for all active trading members using algorithmic software, regardless of whether they use AI/ML systems (NIL submission required if not using).
- Members registered with NSE must submit AI/ML details to NSE only, which will share it with BSE. Members not registered with NSE must submit directly to BSE via the BEFS portal.
- Submission deadline is October 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reinforces SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/DOS2/CIR/P/2019/10 and joint exchange reporting mechanisms for AI/ML systems.

## Compliance Requirements

- Active trading members using algo software must log in to the BEFS (BSE Electronic Filing System) module or NSE portal as applicable.
- Navigate to BEFS-Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Machine Learning > Reporting Form (A I M L).
- Ensure submission on or before October 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Reporting Period: April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Submission Deadline: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures regulatory oversight of AI/ML applications used in algorithmic trading and streamlines common regulatory submissions across exchanges for dual-registered members.
