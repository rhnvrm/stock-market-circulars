## Summary

Intergen Energy Limited has submitted its Draft Offer Document to the BSE for its proposed SME IPO on September 29, 2026. The document is available for public review and comments for a period of at least 21 days in compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Intergen Energy Limited
- Filing Date: September 29, 2026
- Purpose: SME IPO Draft Offer Document made available for public comments
- Review Period: At least 21 days from the date of the notice
- Submission Email for Comments: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None. The filing is in accordance with standard ICDR Regulations for SME initial public offerings.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public may review the draft offer document hosted on the SME website and submit any comments or feedback within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Public Comment Deadline: 21 days from September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Initiates the public consultation phase for Intergen Energy Limited's upcoming SME public issue, allowing market participants to review disclosures and provide inputs.
