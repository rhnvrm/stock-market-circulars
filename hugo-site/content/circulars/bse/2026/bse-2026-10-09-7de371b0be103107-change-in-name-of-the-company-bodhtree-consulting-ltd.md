## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Bodhtree Consulting Ltd has changed its name to Bodhtree Datakosa Limited. Consequently, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Bodhtree Consulting Ltd
- New Name: Bodhtree Datakosa Limited
- Scrip Code: 539122
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): DATAKOSA
- Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus): DATAKOSA

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative update reflecting the official name change of the company.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals, databases, and client records to reflect the new scrip ID and name.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact, restricted to updating records and trading systems with the new scrip ID and company name.
