## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers and privately placed debt securities on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective August 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are issued on a private placement basis and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment.
- Standard denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Includes commercial paper issues from Tata Steel Limited, Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, ICICI Home Finance Company Limited, and Muthoot Mcred Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard dematerialised form requirements under the respective ISIN numbers.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 04, 2026
- Allotment and Effective Listing Dates: August 03, 2026, and August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital market operations for debt instruments with no broader systemic impact.
