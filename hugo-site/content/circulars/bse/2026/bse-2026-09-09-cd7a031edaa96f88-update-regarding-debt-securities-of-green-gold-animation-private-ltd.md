## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in the debt securities of Green Gold Animation Private Ltd on account of debt restructuring, effective from September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Green Gold Animation Private Ltd
- Scrip Code: 974917
- ISIN: INE05RZ07024
- Effective Date of Suspension: Tuesday, September 15, 2026
- Reason: Debt restructuring

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the specified debt securities has been restricted in continuation of earlier Exchange Notice 20260710-17 dated July 10, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are advised to take note of the suspension and avoid executing trades in the affected scrip code and ISIN from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 09, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity for holders of the debt securities of Green Gold Animation Private Ltd will be temporarily or indefinitely halted due to the suspension of trading on the exchange.
