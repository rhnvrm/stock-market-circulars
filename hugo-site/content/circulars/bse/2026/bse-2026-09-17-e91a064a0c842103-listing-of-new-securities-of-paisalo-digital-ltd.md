## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of 46,27,240 new equity shares of Paisalo Digital Ltd. with effect from September 18, 2026, following the conversion of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Paisalo Digital Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 532900
- ISIN Number: INE420C01059
- Number of Shares: 46,27,240 Equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at par.
- Reason for Issuance: Conversion of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs).
- Effective Date of Trading: September 18, 2026.
- Date of Allotment: 12/08/2026.
- Distinctive Numbers: 909521875 to 914149114.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 12, 2026
- Listing / Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase due to FCCB conversion with no adverse impact on market operations.
