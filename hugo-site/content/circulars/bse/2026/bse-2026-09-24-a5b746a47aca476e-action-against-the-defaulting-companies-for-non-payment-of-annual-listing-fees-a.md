## Summary

BSE has announced the revocation of trading suspension in the equity shares of Quintegra Solutions Limited (Scrip Code: 532866) following compliance with the payment of outstanding Annual Listing Fees (ALF) under Regulation 14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. Trading in the company's equity shares will resume in the Normal Segment (Group B) effective Monday, September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Quintegra Solutions Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 532866
- **Segment/Group Shift:** Moved from Group T to Group B (Normal Segment)
- **Effective Date:** September 28, 2026
- **Reason:** Compliance and payment of outstanding Annual Listing Fees (ALF) pursuant to Regulation 14 of SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revocation of trading suspension previously imposed due to non-payment of Annual Listing Fees.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants are advised to note the resumption of trading in Quintegra Solutions Limited under Group B.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 24, 2026
- **Effective Date of Resumption:** September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members can resume normal trading in the equity shares of Quintegra Solutions Limited as the security moves from the surveillance/suspension category (Group T) to the normal trading segment (Group B).
