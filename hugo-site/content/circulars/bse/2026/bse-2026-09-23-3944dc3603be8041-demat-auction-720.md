## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-720/2026-27. The notice outlines the schedule for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and money statements, along with operational guidelines for trading members and custodians.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement Number: DR-720/2026-27.
- Auction offer entry timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on September 23, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on September 23, 2026.
- Delivery and money statement timings scheduled between 03:00 p.m. and 05:30 p.m. on September 23, 2026.
- Auction pay-in is scheduled for Thursday, September 24, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.
- Restriction on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they had short delivered has been removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; reaffirms operational guidelines for auctions under the Uniform Membership structure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Extreme care must be taken while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members are advised to check the cutoff rate on the Auction Offer Entry Screen and refer to corporate action notices (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split) before submitting offers.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 23, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: September 24, 2026 at 08:00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational notice ensuring smooth settlement and clearance of auction obligations for trading members with no broader market-wide impact.
