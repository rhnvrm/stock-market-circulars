## Summary

BSE Clearing Corporation Limited (BSECL) has issued notice number 20260812-26 regarding a comprehensive review of the inclusion of historical scenarios in the stress testing framework of the Commodity Derivatives Segment.

## Key Points

- **Notice Details:** Issued under Settlement/RMS category by the BSECL department, dated August 12, 2026.
- **Focus Area:** Enhancing and reviewing the stress testing frameworks for commodity derivatives.
- **Historical Scenarios:** The review aims to refine the historical data and market shock scenarios incorporated in the stress testing models.
- **Annexure:** Specific details and methodology changes are provided in Annexure_1.

## Regulatory Changes

- Historical stress testing scenarios for the Commodity Derivatives Segment are being evaluated and updated to reflect contemporary market extreme conditions and ensure robust risk mitigation measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and relevant market participants must examine the updated historical scenarios in Annexure_1.
- Back-testing, stress-testing, and risk-management systems of members must be updated to align with the revised guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date:** As detailed in Annexure_1 or subject to further BSECL directives.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Moderate. Requires updates to internal risk modeling software and database inputs by commodity clearing participants.
- **Risk Management Impact:** High. Ensures a more comprehensive representation of extreme market stress situations, strengthening overall systemic safety in the commodity derivatives segment.
