## Summary

BSE has informed market participants that 4,19,22,416 equity shares of Rs.5/- each of Torrent Pharmaceuticals Limited are listed and permitted for trading on the Exchange effective from July 28, 2026. These shares were issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation of J.B. Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Limited with Torrent Pharmaceuticals Limited.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Torrent Pharmaceuticals Limited (Scrip Code: 500420)
- Security Details: 4,19,22,416 Equity Shares of Rs.5/- each
- Purpose: Issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation of J.B. Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Limited with Torrent Pharmaceuticals Limited
- ISIN Number: INE685A01028
- Distribution Nos.: 338445441 – 380367856
- Date of Allotment: July 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notice for shares issued under a scheme of amalgamation.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and depository participants must note the ISIN and distribution numbers for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: July 20, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital of Torrent Pharmaceuticals Limited following its amalgamation with J.B. Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Limited.
