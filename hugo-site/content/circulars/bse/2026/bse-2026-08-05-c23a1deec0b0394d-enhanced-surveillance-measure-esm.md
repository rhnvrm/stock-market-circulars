## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing the application of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) to certain securities. The framework includes inclusion of new securities, shifting of existing securities to lower stages, and moving securities out of the ESM framework effective August 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin effective August 07, 2026, on all open positions as on August 06, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities continue under the ESM framework but are moved to a lower stage (Stage I) effective August 06, 2026.
- Qualifying securities will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective August 06, 2026.
- Price bands for securities moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other prevailing measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margins (100%) and movement across different stages of the ESM framework.
- Shift of impacted securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (100%) for affected open and new positions.
- Trading members need to account for Trade-for-Trade settlement restrictions and periodic call auctions where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II / Lower Stage movements: August 06, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin and Trade-for-Trade shifting: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement mechanics for the shortlisted securities will be significantly restricted due to 100% margin requirements and movement into the Trade-for-Trade segment.
