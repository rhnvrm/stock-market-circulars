## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that the debt scrip codes of specified companies which were previously suspended will be officially delisted from the exchange effective September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date of Delisting: September 25, 2026.
- Segment: Debt Segment.
- Affected companies include Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, Birla Corporation Limited, Jamnagar Utilities & Power Pvt Ltd, and Mahindra Rural Housing Finance Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal delisting of suspended debt securities in accordance with exchange listing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the delisting dates and cease any related trading operations for the specified debt scrips as of the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2026
- Delisting Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and investors holding the affected debt scrips will be impacted by the complete delisting from the exchange platform.
