## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the assignment of new ISIN numbers for units of DSP Gold ETF and DSP Silver ETF following a split and change in face value from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/- per unit.

## Key Points

- DSP Gold ETF (Scrip Code: 543903) new ISIN: INF740KA1ZP2.
- DSP Silver ETF (Scrip Code: 543572) new ISIN: INF740KA1ZQ0.
- Face value changes from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/- per unit.
- Effective for trades done on and from the Ex-Date: August 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Administrative update of ISIN and face value for ETF schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the new ISIN numbers for trading effective from the specified Ex-Date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 20, 2026
- Ex-Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating trading systems and records with the new ISINs for the respective ETF units.
