## Summary

BSE has announced the release of BOLT Pro TWS version 13.70, scheduled to go live on October 19, 2026. The upgrade is compulsory for trading members operating in the Equity Derivatives segment and optional for Equity, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments.

## Key Points

- Release of BOLT Pro TWS Version 13.70 featuring technical enhancements.
- Compulsory upgrade for the Equity Derivatives segment.
- Optional upgrade for Equity, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments.
- Available for download from October 16, 2026, 7:00 PM onwards.
- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 17, 2026, for testing.

## Regulatory Changes

- Technical enhancements and system updates introduced in BOLT Pro TWS ver. 13.70.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members using leased line connections must upgrade to version 13.70 by selecting it in the TWS Version tab of the Auto-Download Interface or downloading via the exchange website.
- Upgrade is mandatory for members trading in the Equity Derivatives segment.

## Important Dates

- **Download Start Date:** October 16, 2026 (from 7:00 PM)
- **Mock Trading / Testing:** October 17, 2026
- **Effective / Go-Live Date:** October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members in Equity Derivatives must complete the upgrade before the live date to ensure seamless trading operations. Members in other segments may upgrade optionally.
