## Summary

SEBI has issued orders cancelling the Certificates of Registration of certain Investment Advisers due to the non-payment of prescribed renewal fees under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013. Consequently, the enlistment granted to these Investment Advisers by BSE Limited also stands cancelled.

## Key Points

- BSE disseminated a list of Investment Advisers whose SEBI registrations and BSE enlistments have been cancelled.
- Cancellations are primarily due to non-payment of prescribed renewal fees.
- Affects 17 listed Investment Advisers with SEBI registration numbers such as INA200014858, INA000002785, etc., along with respective SEBI order numbers and dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 regarding mandatory fee renewals and subsequent registration cancellations.

## Compliance Requirements

- The listed Investment Advisers are no longer authorized to provide investment advisory services under their cancelled registrations and BSE enlistment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 14 August 2026
- SEBI Order Dates: 07-08-2026 and 21-07-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacted entities lose their BSE enlistment and regulatory standing to operate as investment advisers. Investors are advised to verify the registration status of investment advisers before engaging with them.
