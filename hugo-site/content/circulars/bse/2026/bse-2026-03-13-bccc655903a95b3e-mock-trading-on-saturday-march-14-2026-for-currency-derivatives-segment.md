## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are advised to participate and test their connectivity using the specified Disaster Recovery (DR) parameters for BOLT Plus, ETI APIs, and web-based applications.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Currency Derivatives segment.
- Members can connect to the DR site using BOLT TWS or 3rd party trading applications via ETI APIs.
- Detailed primary and secondary DR IP addresses and port numbers provided for ETI and web-based applications (RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, Extranet, etc.).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should verify their disaster recovery setup, login credentials, and connection parameters prior to the mock trading session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness test for trading members with no direct impact on live market operations.
