## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in certain scrips in the recent past. Replies providing clarification have been received from three companies, while responses from several other companies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- The Exchange sought relevant information from select companies following notable price/volume movements to ensure transparency for investors.
- Replies were received from Euphoria Infotech (India) Ltd, Taylormade Renewables Ltd, and Game Changers Texfab Ltd.
- Replies are currently awaited from 14 other listed entities, including Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to furnish necessary clarifications and disclosures to the exchange promptly when requested regarding unusual market movements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 11 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Informational notice providing transparency on exchange surveillance inquiries into stock price and volume volatility.
