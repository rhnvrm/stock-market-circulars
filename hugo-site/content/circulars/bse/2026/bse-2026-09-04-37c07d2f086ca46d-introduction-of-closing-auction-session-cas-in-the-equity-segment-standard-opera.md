## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the Introduction of Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Segment. The guidelines for reference price computation and applicable price bands—previously applicable to stock futures—are extended to include index futures contracts effective September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scope Extension:** Reference price computation and applicable price band guidelines now apply to index futures contracts in addition to stock futures contracts.
- **Effective Date:** The provisions become effective from September 7, 2026.
- **Reference Price Methodology:** Based on VWAP of trades between 3:00 p.m. and 3:15 p.m., falling back to LTP, theoretical price, or base/previous close price if necessary.
- **Price Band:** A price band of +/- 3% from the reference price applies to futures contracts during the period from 3:15 p.m. to 3:40 p.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- Sub-clauses 2.3 and 3.2 of Exchange Notice No. 20260319-21 dated March 19, 2026, are updated to explicitly include index futures.
- Dynamic flexing of price bands for futures as specified in SEBI Master Circular dated December 30, 2024, shall not apply during 3:15 p.m. to 3:40 p.m.
- Last half an hour of CTS is considered from 02:45 pm to 03:15 pm.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must align their systems and order management procedures with the updated reference price calculation and price band framework for both stock and index futures during the closing session.
- All outstanding orders outside the revised price band will be cancelled sequentially starting at 3:15 p.m.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 4, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Derivatives market participants trading index and stock futures will experience standardized closing session price bands and reference pricing mechanisms aligned with the cash segment's Closing Auction Session (CAS).
