## Summary

Exchange notice announces that trading in equity shares of Shellz India Limited (SME IPO, Scrip Code: 544998) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shellz India Limited
- Segment: SME
- Scrip Code: 544998
- Previous Group/Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Group/Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Friday, November 6, 2026
- Reference Notice: Notice No. 20261022-45 (dated October 22, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

The equity shares are moving from trade-for-trade settlement restrictions into standard rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the change in segment and group for settlement and execution purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date: November 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables regular rolling settlement for equity shares of Shellz India Limited, improving trading liquidity and operational flexibility for market participants.
