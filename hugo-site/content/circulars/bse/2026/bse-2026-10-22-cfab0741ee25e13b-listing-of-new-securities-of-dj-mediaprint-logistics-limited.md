## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade for new equity shares of DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Limited (Scrip Code: 543193) with effect from Friday, October 23, 2026. The issuance comprises 2,61,503 equity shares issued upon the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 543193
- **ISIN:** INE0B1K01014
- **Securities Issued:** 2,61,503 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 104/-
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 114/- per share
- **Allottees:** Non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 34669853 to 34931355
- **Date of Allotment:** 01/07/2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Lock-in requirements apply to the newly issued shares: 2,61,503 shares (Dist. Nos. 34669853 to 34931355) are locked in up to 30/04/2027.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** 01/07/2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** 23/10/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 30/04/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the company following warrant conversion.
