## Summary

BSE has announced the settlement schedule and acquisition window for the delisting offer of Hitech Corporation Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from October 7, 2026, to October 13, 2026, under settlement number 2026119.

## Key Points

- Delisting offer for Hitech Corporation Limited via Acquisition Window (Offer to Buy).
- Bidding start date: October 7, 2026; Bidding end date: October 13, 2026.
- Settlement Type: Delisting, Settlement No.: 2026119.
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using market type "(Delisting)" and settlement no. 2026119.
- CDSL early pay-in requires direct transfer by clients to the early pay-in account of the member/custodian, while NSDL early pay-in involves earmarking/blocking in the client account followed by execution instructions to the member's NSDL pool account.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file. Client-wise Stamp Duty charges can be verified via the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revised guidelines and mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange (refer Notice No. 20200528-32 dated May 28, 2020) apply to this delisting settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must ensure early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/ICCL systems before prescribed timelines for accepting/confirming bids on the BSE OTB platform.
- Use market type "(Delisting)" and settlement number 2026119 in depository systems.
- Contact BSECL Help Desk (022-69158590 / 45720490 / 45720690 or iccl.helpdesk@bseclearing.com) for clarifications.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: 07/10/2026
- Bid End Date: 13/10/2026
- Obligation Download: 14/10/2026
- Settlement Date: 15/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders participating in the delisting offer must complete early pay-in of securities in advance.
- Trading members and custodians must adhere strictly to early pay-in mechanisms and settlement timelines to avoid failed settlements or rejection of bids.
