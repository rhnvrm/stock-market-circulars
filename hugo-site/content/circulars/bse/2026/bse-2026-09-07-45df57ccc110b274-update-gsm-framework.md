## Summary

BSE has issued a notice stating that securities part of Annexure III of exchange notice no. 20260306-35 will be moved out of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework effective September 08, 2026. The applicable group and daily price bands for these securities will be updated accordingly.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure III of the earlier March 06, 2026 notice are removed from GSM.
- Effective date for removal and revised price bands is September 08, 2026.
- Members can contact surveillance via email for any clarifications.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are exited from the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, lifting specific surveillance restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update systems for the revised price bands and applicable groups for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normalization of trading conditions for the affected securities as they move out of surveillance stages, resulting in revised price bands.
