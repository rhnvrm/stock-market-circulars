## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the schedule for Demat Auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-724/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of specified securities.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry session is scheduled on September 29, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction matching takes place from 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statements and money statements are generated between 03.00 p.m. and 05.30 p.m. on September 29, 2026.
- Auction pay-in is scheduled for Wednesday, September 30, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they short-delivered has been removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

Reflects ongoing operational rules under the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment, including the removal of restrictions on trading members offering short-delivered securities in auctions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Members must exercise care while punching security codes and rates as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 29, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: September 30, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational workflow for clearing and settlement participants; minimal broader market impact.
