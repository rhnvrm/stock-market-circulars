## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of several companies due to fixed record dates for the payment of interest and redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures and bonds of the specified companies starting from the respective ex-dates / record dates.
- Companies involved include Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, UNO Minda Limited, IFCI Ltd., REC Limited, SK Finance Limited, Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited, Krazybee Services Ltd, InCred Financial Services Limited, Indian Railway Finance Corporation, JM Financial Home Loans Limited, Chemmanur Credits and Investments Limited, and OXYZO Financial Services Limited.
- Record dates and settlement details span across October 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary suspension of trading in specified debt securities on account of record dates for interest payment and redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no trading activity takes place in the specified scrips during the suspension period associated with the record/ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 06/10/2026
- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: Between 09/10/2026 and 27/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members should note the temporary trading suspension to align with interest payment and redemption schedules for the respective debt instruments.
