## Summary

BSE has announced the release of BOLT Pro TWS Version 13.35, which will be compulsory for trading members across Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments effective from Monday, August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- New version: BOLT Pro TWS Version 13.35.
- Mandatory for Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments.
- Includes technical enhancements.
- Available for download and mock trading prior to the live release.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compulsory software version upgrade for all trading members using BOLT Pro TWS.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must upgrade their TWS systems to version 13.35 before the effective date to ensure uninterrupted trading access.

## Important Dates

- Download Availability: July 28, 2026, from 7:00 PM onwards.
- Mock Trading Session: August 29, 2026.
- Effective Date (Live): August 31, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational requirement for IT and trading desks to update their trading terminals to avoid compliance and connectivity issues.
