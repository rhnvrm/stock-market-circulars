## Summary

BSE StAR MF has issued a notice regarding the temporary suspension of fresh lump-sum investments (fresh and additional purchases), switch-in transactions, Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP-In) in Gold ETF Fund of Fund schemes of specified AMCs on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective from the close of business hours on October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Suspension of Subscriptions:** Fresh lump-sum investments, switch-ins, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-In) under specified Gold ETF Fund of Fund schemes are disabled effective October 15, 2026.
- **Existing SIP/STP Continuity:** Existing SIP and STP-In registrations made prior to the effective date will continue to be processed by respective AMCs/RTAs without change.
- **Permitted Transactions:** Redemption, switch-out, STP-Out, and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) transactions continue to be allowed.
- **Triggered Orders:** Subscription orders received prior to the suspension date but triggered to RTAs on or after the close of business hours on October 15, 2026, will be rejected and refunded.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary halting of incoming fresh capital flows into Gold ETF Fund of Fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform as per communication from respective Mutual Funds / AMCs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take note of the disabled subscription categories and advise investors accordingly while executing transactions in the affected Gold ETF Fund of Fund schemes.
- Ensure subscription orders submitted past the deadline are managed correctly regarding rejection and refunds.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Suspension:** Close of business hours on October 15, 2026.
- **Notice Date:** October 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding existing SIPs in Gold ETF Fund of Funds remain unaffected for ongoing installments, but new systematic registrations and lump-sum purchases are temporarily halted, impacting near-term inflows into these specific schemes on the BSE platform.
