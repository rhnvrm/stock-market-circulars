## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by multiple companies on the debt market segment effective August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are issued on a private placement basis and traded in dematerialised form.
- Standard denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuers include Vivriti Capital Limited, Titan Company Limited, Tata Power Co. Ltd, and Network 18 Media & Investments Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard dematerialised trading norms and denominations for debt market segments.

## Important Dates

- Allotment and Effective Listing Date: August 31, 2026
- Redemptions range from November 2026 through March 2027 depending on the specific commercial paper tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing with standard liquidity and trading provisions for institutional debt participants.
