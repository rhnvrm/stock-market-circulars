## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Ascend Telecom Infrastructure Private Limited (Scrip Code: 974895, ISIN: INE889K08038): Record date and Ex-date on 14/08/2026; reduced face value of Rs. 72,500/- per debenture.
- Achiievers Finance India Limited (Scrip Code: 977310, ISIN: INE065507BM2): Record date and Ex-date on 17/08/2026; reduced face value of Rs. 25,000/- per debenture.
- Achiievers Finance India Limited (Scrip Code: 977613, ISIN: INE065507BS9): Record date and Ex-date on 17/08/2026; reduced face value of Rs. 58,333/- per debenture.
- Achiievers Finance India Limited (Scrip Code: 977891, ISIN: INE065507BX9): Record date and Ex-date on 17/08/2026; reduced face value of Rs. 83,333/- per debenture.
- Unigold Finance Limited (Scrip Code: 977098, ISIN: INE0O7U07012): Record date and Ex-date on 27/08/2026; reduced face value of Rs. 3,333.33 per debenture.
- Finnable Credit Private Limited (Scrip Code: 977363, ISIN: INE14H407074): Record date and Ex-date on 27/08/2026; reduced face value of Rs. 70,000/- per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and record dates for trading and settlement purposes in the debt segment.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: August 14, 2026, August 17, 2026, and August 27, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to holders of the respective non-convertible debentures undergoing part redemption.
