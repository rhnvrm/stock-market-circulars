## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, September 1, 2026, the equity shares of Skyways Air Services Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE under the 'B' Group of Securities following its initial public offering.

## Key Points

- Scrip Name: SKYWAYS AIR SERVICES LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544890
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: SKYWAYS
- ISIN No.: INE0PX301025
- Total Securities Listed: 145,343,544 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 138/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 128/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 28, 2026
- Market Lot: 1 Share
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed for lock-in marking in depository records as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 28, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: September 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Skyways Air Services Limited equity shares on the exchange platform.
