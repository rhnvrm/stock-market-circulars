## Summary

KOTAK MAHINDRA Mutual Fund is launching the KOTAK MULTI SECTOR OMNI FOF NFO along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform from September 08, 2026, to September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: KOTAK MULTI SECTOR OMNI FOF (Regular and Direct Plans - Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, IDCW Payout).
- Subscription Period: September 08, 2026, to September 22, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 28, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 08, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 22, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Cut-off: September 25, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for trading members participating in the Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
