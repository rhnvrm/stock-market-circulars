## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The changes take effect from September 09, 2026, and entail specific surveillance actions such as applicable margin rates, price bands, trade-to-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the annexure are moved to higher stages of GSM effective September 09, 2026.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value by buyers.
- Stage III: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week), and ASD of 100% of trade value by buyers.
- Stage IV: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week with no upward movement, and ASD of 100% of trade value by buyers.
- Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities are subjected to enhanced surveillance measures according to the provisions of the GSM framework published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent amendments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities, keeping in mind the trade-to-trade settlement restrictions, lack of netting off, and applicable ASD and margin requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 08 Sep 2026
- Effective Date of GSM Stage Movement: 09 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and market participation for the affected securities are likely to be constrained due to stringent margin requirements, trade-to-trade settlement limitations, and mandatory surveillance deposits.
