## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of 26,59,05,000 new equity shares of Davangere Sugar Company Limited (Scrip Code: 543267) with effect from October 21, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the conversion of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs).

## Key Points

- Company: Davangere Sugar Company Limited
- Scrip Code: 543267
- ISIN Number: INE179G01029
- Security Details: 26,59,05,000 Equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 2.60/- each upon FCCB conversion.
- Date of Allotment: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 21, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 1429990799 to 1695895798

## Regulatory Changes

Listing and trading approval for newly allotted equity shares following corporate action (FCCB conversion).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants to take note of the admission of new shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 25, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increases the total paid-up equity capital of Davangere Sugar Company Limited through conversion of foreign currency bonds.
