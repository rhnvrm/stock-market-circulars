## Summary

In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260902-11 dated September 02, 2026, the BSE has issued a correction regarding the ex-dates for the suspension of trading in debentures and bonds of specific companies. The ex-date was inadvertently mentioned as the record date instead of September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Correction in Ex-dates for trading suspension of debentures and bonds.
- The correct Ex-date for the listed scrips is 11/09/2026.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures/bonds as on the ex-date.

## Regulatory Changes

- Correction of previously communicated ex-dates for debt instruments suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the corrected Ex-date (11/09/2026) and avoid dealing in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10/09/2026
- Corrected Ex Date: 11/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures accuracy in trading operations and settlement schedules for debt securities following inadvertent reporting errors in earlier notices.
