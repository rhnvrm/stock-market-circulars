## Summary

BSE has announced the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Buyback) for **Shardul Securities Limited**. The bidding period is scheduled from October 14, 2026, to October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Shardul Securities Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Buyback
- **Settlement Number:** 2026123
- **Bid Start Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Bid End Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** October 27, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** October 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; procedural settlement schedule notification under existing buyback guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL and NSDL).
- Members and custodians must use Market Type "(Buyback)" and settlement number 2026123.
- Early pay-in securities will be earmarked/blocked in clients' accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should refer to the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Bid End Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** October 27, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless execution and settlement of the buyback process for shareholders of Shardul Securities Limited within the prescribed timelines.
