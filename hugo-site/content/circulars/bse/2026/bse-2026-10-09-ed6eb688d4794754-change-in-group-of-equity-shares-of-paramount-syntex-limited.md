## Summary

BSE has announced that the equity shares of Paramount Syntex Limited (SME IPO, Scrip Code: 544982) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment under the M Group, effective Monday, October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Paramount Syntex Limited
- Scrip Code: 544982
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Monday, October 26, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20261007-35 dated October 8, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the equity shares of the company is shifting from compulsory trade-for-trade settlement to regular rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the segment change and ensure compliance with the applicable settlement procedures under the M Group starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 9, 2026
- Effective Date of Transfer: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and investors can trade the company's equity shares under regular rolling settlement, improving operational efficiency and trading flexibility.
