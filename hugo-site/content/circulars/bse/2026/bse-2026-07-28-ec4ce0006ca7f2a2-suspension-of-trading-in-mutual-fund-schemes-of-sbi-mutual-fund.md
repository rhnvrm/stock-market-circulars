## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in four fixed maturity plan (FMP) schemes of SBI Mutual Fund on account of their maturity as of the redemption date.

## Key Points

- Four schemes of SBI Mutual Fund are maturing and their trading is suspended.
- Affected schemes include SBI Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) - Series 46 (1850 Days) across Regular and Direct plans (Growth and IDCW Payout options).
- Trading members are advised not to deal in these schemes effective from July 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified SBI Mutual Fund schemes effective from July 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 28, 2026
- **Effective Date:** July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational procedure for maturing closed-ended mutual fund schemes with no broader market impact.
