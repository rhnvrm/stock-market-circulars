## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing financial disincentives for trading members who operated unregistered or incorrectly registered TWS/ETI/BOLTPLUS terminal locations during July 2026. A penalty of ₹1,000 per instance applies to orders punched from such locations.

## Key Points

- File filename LCS0726.xxxx indicating the financial disincentive for July 2026 is available in the Equity Transaction Folder on Extranet.
- Disincentives will be debited to the General Charges Account 60 days from the notice date.
- Members seeking waivers or reviews must follow the procedure outlined in Exchange Notice No. 20251008-67 and email Waiver-Review@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of terminal location registration compliance under existing exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure strict compliance by uploading terminal location details before punching orders.
- Waiver/review applications must be emailed to Waiver-Review@bseindia.com with the specified subject format.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10 Aug 2026
- Debit Date: 60 days from notice date

## Impact Assessment

- Direct financial impact on trading members who failed to register terminal locations properly during July 2026.
