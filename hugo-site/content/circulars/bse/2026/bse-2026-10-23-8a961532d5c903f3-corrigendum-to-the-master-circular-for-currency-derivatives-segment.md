## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has issued a corrigendum and updated Master Circular for the Currency Derivatives Segment, superseding the previous master circular published vide Notice 20260429-39 on April 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Amendments have been made to the Master Circular for the Currency Derivatives Segment, attached as an annexure.
- All notices issued prior to this master circular and the existing master circular relevant to the segment stand rescinded.
- Previous actions, rights, obligations, liabilities, and penalties under the rescinded guidelines remain fully protected and unaffected.
- Effective immediately from the date of its issue (October 23, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal replacement and updating of the currency derivatives master circular.
- Inclusion of standard savings clauses ensuring past actions and ongoing proceedings under rescinded guidelines remain valid.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take note of the updated master circular available under the 'Home>Downloads>Master Circulars' section on BSECL's website.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative update ensuring regulatory clarity and consolidation of guidelines for the currency derivatives segment.
