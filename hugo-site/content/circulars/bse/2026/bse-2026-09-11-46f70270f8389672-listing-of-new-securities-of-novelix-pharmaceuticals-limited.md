## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of Novelix Pharmaceuticals Limited (Scrip Code: 536565) effective from September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Type:** Equity shares issued on a preferential basis to promoters upon conversion of warrants.
- **Quantity:** 33,75,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 10/- (Issue Price: Rs. 20.00/-).
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 23895001 to 27270000
- **ISIN:** INE314I01036
- **Date of Allotment:** 09/07/2026
- **Lock-in Details:** 33,75,000 shares are locked-in up to 14/03/2028.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with the lock-in periods and trading permissions effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** September 15, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** July 9, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** March 14, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital due to preferential warrant conversion with standard lock-in stipulations.
