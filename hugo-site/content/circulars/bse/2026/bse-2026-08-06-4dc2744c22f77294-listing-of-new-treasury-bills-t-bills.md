## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and admission to dealings of new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS effective from August 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three new Treasury Bills are being listed: 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day T-Bills.
- Effective listing date: August 07, 2026.
- Group: G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS.
- Trading restriction: Securities will not be available for trading two working days prior to the maturity/redemption date.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the trading restrictions leading up to the maturity dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 06, 2026
- Effective Date: August 07, 2026
- Maturity Dates: 05/11/2026 (91TB051126), 04/02/2027 (182T040227), 05/08/2027 (364TB50827)

## Impact Assessment

Routine issuance and listing of sovereign debt instruments providing short-term investment and liquidity management options.
