## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) for multiple companies due to upcoming record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified commercial papers.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261006-27
- Segment: Debt Segment, Listing Operations
- Companies involved include Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd, Adani Enterprises Ltd., Nuvama Custodial Services Limited, SBICAP Securities Limited, Axis Finance Limited, InCred Financial Services Limited, Time Technoplast Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, Ugro Capital Limited, Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, and Manappuram Finance Limited.
- Record dates span from October 12, 2026 to October 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational notice regarding commercial paper redemption and suspension of trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the listed companies mentioned in the notice during the suspension period surrounding the record date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 06 October 2026
- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: 12 October 2026 to 22 October 2026 (varying by company/CP).

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational impact for debt market participants holding or trading the specified commercial papers prior to redemption.
