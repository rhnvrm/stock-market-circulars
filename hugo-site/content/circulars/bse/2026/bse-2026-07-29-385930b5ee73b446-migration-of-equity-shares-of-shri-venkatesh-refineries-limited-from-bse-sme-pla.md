## Summary

Trading members are informed that effective Thursday, July 30, 2026, the equity shares of Shri Venkatesh Refineries Limited, currently listed on the BSE SME Platform, will be migrated and admitted to dealings on the Mainboard Platform under the 'T' Group list.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shri Venkatesh Refineries Limited
- Effective Date: July 30, 2026
- Scrip Code: 543373
- Scrip ID / BOLT System Name: SVRL
- ISIN: INE0HLJ01013
- Market Lot: 1 share
- Group: 'T'

## Regulatory Changes

Trading of securities will be in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, with unit market lot.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the transition from SME to Mainboard platform and trade under the specified 'T' Group.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 29, 2026
- Effective Date: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market visibility and liquidity for Shri Venkatesh Refineries Limited following its graduation from the SME platform to the Mainboard.
