## Summary

Pentad Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 6560) has notified the Exchange of its voluntary closure of business in the currency derivative segment, effective Monday, September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: Pentad Securities Pvt. Ltd.
- Member Code: 6560
- Segment Affected: Currency Derivative Segment
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members requiring clarification may contact the Membership Operations and Compliance department at membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Closure: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to the specific member's operations in the currency derivative segment.
