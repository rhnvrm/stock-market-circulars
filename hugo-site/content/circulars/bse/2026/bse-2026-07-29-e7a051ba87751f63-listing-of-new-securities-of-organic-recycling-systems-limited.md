## Summary

Trading members are informed that 17,40,000 new equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued by Organic Recycling Systems Limited at a premium of Rs. 263/- on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants are listed and permitted to trade with effect from Thursday, July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Organic Recycling Systems Limited
- Scrip Code: 543997
- ISIN: INE0MIO01019
- Security Details: 17,40,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 273/- (issue price) to Non-Promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- Effective Date of Trading: July 30, 2026

## Allotment Details

- 10,00,000 shares allotted on 14/05/2026 (Distinctive Nos: 8659276 - 9659275)
- 7,40,000 shares allotted on 21/05/2026 (Distinctive Nos: 9659276 - 10399275)

## Compliance Requirements

- Shares rank pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.
- Lock-in applicable on 17,40,000 shares (Distinctive Nos: 8659276 to 10399275) up to 09/02/2027.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Dates: 14/05/2026 and 21/05/2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: 30/07/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: 09/02/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate listing action increasing the paid-up equity capital of the company via warrant conversion.
