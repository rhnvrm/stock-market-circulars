## Summary

BSE has issued a surveillance notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in various scrips in the recent past. Clarifications have been received from 7 companies, while replies remain awaited from 19 companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from 7 companies including Himalaya Nutravedics India Ltd, Infrax Renewable Ltd, Kaarya Facilities and Services Ltd, Modi Naturals Ltd, Molbio Diagnostics Ltd, Stanley Lifestyles Ltd, and TVS Electronics Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 19 companies including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, and others.
- Issued by the Surveillance Department of BSE Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to clarify significant price and volume movements promptly upon exchange inquiry to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 19 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to investors regarding abnormal price and volume movements across specified scrips and monitors company responsiveness.
