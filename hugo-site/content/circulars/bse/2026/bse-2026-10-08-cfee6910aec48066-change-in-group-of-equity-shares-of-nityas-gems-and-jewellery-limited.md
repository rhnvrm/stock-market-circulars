## Summary

In continuation of Notice No. 20261007-26 dated October 07, 2026, BSE has informed trading members that the equity shares of Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment under B Group, effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited
- Scrip Code: 544974
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment under B Group
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Contact Person: Mr. Anurag Jain (Tel: 022-2272 8822)

## Regulatory Changes

 Equity shares are transitioned from restrictive settlement (Trade for Trade) to standard rolling settlement under the B Group.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment and group change for executing transactions and managing client positions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables regular rolling settlement and improves trading convenience and liquidity for investors in Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited.
