## Summary

BSE has notified members regarding the temporary suspension of fresh investments, switch-ins, and new systematic registrations (SIP/STP-in) in KOTAK CRISIL - IBX AAA BOND FINANCIAL SERVICES INDEX - DEC 2026 FUND on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective from the close of business on October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh lump-sum investments (fresh and additional purchases), switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in) have been disabled.
- Applications received after the applicable cut-off time on October 23, 2026, will not be accepted or processed.
- Existing SIP and STP-in registrations made before the effective date will continue to be processed by the AMC/RTA without any change.
- Redemption, switch-out, STP-out, and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) transactions shall continue to be permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

Operational changes implemented on the BSE StAR MF platform pursuant to communication received from Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund regarding scheme subscription restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and distributors must take note of these operational changes when executing transactions and ensure no new lump-sum, switch-in, or systematic registrations are submitted after the cut-off time.

## Important Dates

- Cut-off time for applications: October 23, 2026
- Effective date of suspension: Close of business on October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and distributors are restricted from making fresh allocations into this target maturity debt fund via BSE StAR MF, while existing systematic plans and exit routes (redemptions and switch-outs) remain fully operational.
