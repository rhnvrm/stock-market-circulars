## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of Paramount Syntex Limited effective from Friday, October 9, 2026. A total of 18,399,382 equity shares are listed under the 'MT' Group of Securities and will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Paramount Syntex Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544982
- **Scrip ID:** PARASYNTEX
- **ISIN:** INE0TBC01014
- **Number of Securities:** 18,399,382 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up (Issue Price: Rs. 127/- per share)
- **Market Lot:** 1000 equity shares
- **Allotment Date:** October 7, 2026
- **Market Maker:** MNM Stock Broking Private Limited
- **Registrar:** Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Standard listing notification and admission to dealings in accordance with SEBI guidelines and BSE procedures for IPOs and relisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Trading members must note the scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 9, 2026.
- Corporate action executed for locking-in shares in depository records.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** October 7, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public market trading and liquidity for equity shares of Paramount Syntex Limited on the BSE SME platform.
