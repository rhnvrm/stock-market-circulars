## Summary

Sab Events & Governance Now Media Limited has fixed a record date for the reduction of its equity share capital pursuant to a Resolution Plan approved by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: Sab Events & Governance Now Media Limited (Scrip Code: 540081)
- Record Date: 05/08/2026
- Cancellation of Promoter Shareholding: Entire existing promoter shareholding shall be cancelled and extinguished in its entirety to NIL.
- Reduction of Public Shareholding: Existing equity shares held by public shareholders shall be reduced in the ratio of 100:5 (5 shares retained for every 100 shares held).
- Shareholders holding lower than 20 equity shares shall stand reduced to NIL, fractional entitlements shall be cancelled, and no consideration is payable for cancelled shares or fractions. Face value remains Rs. 10/-.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of capital reduction scheme under an NCLT-approved Resolution Plan.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants are required to take note of the capital reduction, cancellation of promoter shares, and adjustment of public shareholdings.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 03 Aug 2026
- Record Date / Suspension w.e.f. / Effective Date: 05/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Significant restructuring of share capital and equity holdings for Sab Events & Governance Now Media Limited under the NCLT resolution framework.
