## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding measures for companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter 'encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Certain securities will attract a minimum 75% margin, while others are eligible to move out of the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Securities listed in Annexure II are eligible to move out from the framework.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Measures are in conjunction with all other prevailing exchange measures and are subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of a minimum 75% margin on open and new positions for securities meeting the high encumbrance criteria under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the margin applicability and ensure compliance across equity and equity derivatives segments for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Margin Applicability: August 11, 2026 (on all open positions as on August 10, 2026, and new positions thereafter)
- Effective Date for Removal of Securities: August 07, 2026
- Notice Date: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities with high promoter/non-promoter encumbrances will experience increased margin obligations (minimum 75%), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in those specific counters.
