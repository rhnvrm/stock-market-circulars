## Summary

BSE has announced that ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund has revised the limit for acceptance of lumpsum subscriptions in ICICI Prudential Mid Cap Fund, effective October 08, 2026. Fresh and additional purchases through lumpsum and switch-in are now restricted to ₹10,00,000 per primary holder PAN per day on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Fresh/Additional Purchases through Lump sum & Switch-in under ICICI Prudential Mid Cap Fund are restricted up to ₹10,00,000 per primary holder PAN per day.
- SIP and STP purchases/subscriptions continue without any restrictions or limits.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIPs, XSIPs, and AMC STPs) will continue to be processed normally.
- Redemption, Switch-out, and STP-out transactions are permitted without any amount restrictions.
- Subscription orders above ₹10,00,000 received and triggered to RTA on or after the closure of business hours on October 07, 2026, will be rejected and refunded.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of lumpsum subscription cap of ₹10,00,000 per primary holder PAN per day for ICICI Prudential Mid Cap Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised lumpsum subscription limit while executing transactions in the scheme and ensure orders adhere to the ₹10,00,000 daily cap per primary holder PAN.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Cut-off / Reference Date: Closure of business hours on October 07, 2026 for pending/exceeding orders.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors planning large lumpsum investments or switches into ICICI Prudential Mid Cap Fund will be capped at ₹10,00,000 per day per PAN. Systematic investments (SIP/STP) remain unaffected.
