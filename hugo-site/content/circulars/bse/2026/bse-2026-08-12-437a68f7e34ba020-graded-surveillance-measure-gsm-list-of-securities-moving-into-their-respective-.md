## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced that several securities will be moved to higher stages under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. These transitions take effect from August 13, 2026, and subject the affected securities to specific surveillance actions, including high margin rates, additional surveillance deposits (ASD), trade-for-trade settlement, and trading frequency limitations.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** The transition to higher GSM stages is effective from August 13, 2026.
- **GSM Stage Framework & Surveillance Actions:**
  - **Stage I:** Mandates an applicable margin rate of 100% and a price band of 5% or lower.
  - **Stage II:** Trade-for-trade settlement with a price band of 5% or lower, plus a 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) to be paid by the buyer.
  - **Stage III:** Trade-for-trade settlement with a price band of 5% or lower, trading limited to once a week (every Monday or first trading day), and a 100% ASD to be paid by the buyer.
  - **Stage IV:** Trade-for-trade settlement with a price band of 5% or lower, trading limited to once a week (every Monday or first trading day), a 100% ASD paid by the buyer, and no upward price movement allowed.
- **Settlement Restraints:** No netting off of transactions is allowed, as settlement must be done strictly on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Regulatory Changes

No systemic regulatory changes have been made to the GSM framework itself. This notification represents the routine operational assignment of specific securities into higher surveillance tiers under existing exchange directives dated back to February 23, 2017.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Must take immediate precautions when executing trades in these securities and ensure their systems enforce the 100% margin/ASD requirements and restricted trading schedules.
- **Risk Management & Operations:** Back-offices must block netting off for the affected securities, as they are restricted to trade-for-trade settlement.
- **Buyers:** Must deposit the requisite ASD (50% for Stage II, 100% for Stage III & IV) when purchasing affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date of Movement:** August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Securities entering GSM Stages II, III, and IV will experience severe drops in trading volume and liquidity because of high deposit requirements, restrictive price bands, and the weekly trading schedules (for Stage III and IV).
- **Operational Impact:** Trading members must update their risk management parameters and systems to correctly identify the newly restricted securities, prevent netting off, and collect the required ASD from buyers.
