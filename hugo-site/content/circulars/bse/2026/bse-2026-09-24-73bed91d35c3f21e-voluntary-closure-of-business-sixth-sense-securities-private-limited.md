## Summary

Sixth Sense Securities Private Limited (Member No. 6834) has voluntarily ceased to conduct business in the Cash and Equity Derivative segments of the Exchange with effect from September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member: Sixth Sense Securities Private Limited
- Member Number: 6834
- Affected Segments: Cash and Equity Derivative segments
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Queries or clarifications regarding this notice should be directed to membership operations at membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impact is limited to clients and counterparties associated with Sixth Sense Securities Private Limited.
