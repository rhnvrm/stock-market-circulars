## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-723/2026-27. The auction sessions, matching, reporting, and pay-in timelines have been specified for September 28 and September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Auction Schedule**: Offer entry from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m., matching from 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m., delivery statements from 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m., and money statements from 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. on September 28, 2026.
- **Pay-in**: Securities and funds pay-in scheduled for Tuesday, September 29, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- **Membership & Short Delivery**: Settlement is handled through Clearing Members under the uniform membership structure. Restrictions on trading members offering securities in auctions where they short delivered have been removed.
- **Precautions**: Trading members must exercise utmost care while entering security codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted after punching.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; reiterates standard auction procedures and operational guidelines following the uniform membership structure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Members must review cutoff rates on the Auction Offer Entry Screen before submitting offers.

## Important Dates

- **Auction Offer Entry**: September 28, 2026 (02:00 PM - 02:45 PM)
- **Auction Matching**: September 28, 2026 (02:45 PM - 03:00 PM)
- **Auction Pay-in**: September 29, 2026 (08:00 AM)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for clearing short deliveries in rolling settlement DR-723/2026-27 with minimal market-wide impact.
