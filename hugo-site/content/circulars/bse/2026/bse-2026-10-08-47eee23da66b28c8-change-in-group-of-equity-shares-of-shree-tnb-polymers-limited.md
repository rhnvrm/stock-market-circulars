## Summary

Trading in the equity shares of SHREE TNB POLYMERS LIMITED (Scrip Code: 544978) is being transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Friday, October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SHREE TNB POLYMERS LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544978
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Friday, October 23, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange’s Notice No 20261007-35 dated October 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Equity shares are shifted from the Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment under the M Group, regularizing standard settlement procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the segment change and ensure compliance with rolling settlement norms for transactions in the company's equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables standard rolling settlement for the equity shares of SHREE TNB POLYMERS LIMITED, improving liquidity and trading efficiency for market participants.
