## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities issued by Shriram Finance Limited on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Issuer: Shriram Finance Limited
- Security Type: Privately Placed Debt Instrument (NCD)
- Scrip Code: 977963
- Scrip ID: 780SFL29
- ISIN Number: INE721A07SS4
- Issue Size / Quantity: 222,000 units
- Face Value: Rs. 100,000.00 per unit
- Issue Price: Rs. 99,892.97 per unit
- Interest Rate: 7.80% p.a., payable annually
- Date of Allotment: 09/09/2026
- Date of Redemption: 07/09/2029

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialised form under the specified ISIN with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 9, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 11, 2026
- Interest Payment: Annually from 07/09/2027 to 07/09/2029
- Redemption Date: September 7, 2029

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official trading venue for the newly listed debt instruments on the BSE debt segment.
