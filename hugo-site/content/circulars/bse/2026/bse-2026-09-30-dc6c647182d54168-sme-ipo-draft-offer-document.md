BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Solanki Mechanic Works Limited on September 30, 2026. The document contains disclosures specified under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations and is hosted on the BSE SME website for public comments.

- Company Name: Solanki Mechanic Works Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO
- Public Comment Period: At least 21 days from the date of the notice
- Submission Email for Comments: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

No regulatory changes; procedural filing under ICDR Regulations.

Public and stakeholders may submit comments on the Draft Offer Document within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

- Comment Deadline: Within 21 days from September 30, 2026

Standard SME IPO process initiation allowing market participants and the public to review and provide feedback on the draft disclosures.
