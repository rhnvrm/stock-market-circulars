## Summary

BSE has announced the imposition and revision of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities pursuant to revised framework guidelines. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, transfers to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS), and removal of certain securities from the framework.

## Key Points

- **100% Margins**: Applicable effective August 12, 2026, on all open positions as on August 11, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for specified securities (Annexure I, Part A).
- **Higher Stages & Price Bands**: List of securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and attracting lower price bands effective August 10, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- **Group Transfers**: Securities continuing in the LT-ASM framework but transferring to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective August 12, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- **Exits**: Securities moving out of the LT-ASM Framework effective August 10, 2026, with price bands reinstated to previous levels subject to other applicable surveillance measures (Annexure II).
- **Consolidated List**: Available as Annexure III for market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures under the revised LT-ASM framework based on XBRL submissions by listed companies.
- Measures operate in conjunction with all other prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure adequate 100% margins are maintained for open and new positions in securities specified under Annexure I (Part A) starting August 12, 2026.
- Members and trading participants must take note of revised price bands and group migrations (T/XT/MT/TS groups).
- For clarifications, members can reach out to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **August 10, 2026**: Effective date for movement to higher stages, lower price bands, and moving out of LT-ASM.
- **August 12, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Groups.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trading restrictions (such as lower price bands and trade-for-trade group transfers) will impact liquidity and trading activity for the affected securities. Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against companies.
