## Summary

BSE has notified the listing and trading permission for 55,00,000 new equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 29/- per share (issue price Rs. 39/-) to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants for Purple Finance Limited (Scrip Code - 544191), effective from October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Purple Finance Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544191
- **ISIN:** INE0CYK01015
- **Security Details:** 55,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 29/-
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 39/-
- **Allotment Date:** September 10, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Lock-in Details:** 55,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 58937963 to 64437962) locked in up to April 20, 2028

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice for preferential allotment and warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the issuance, ISIN, and lock-in period applicable to the newly listed equity shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 10, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** April 20, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of the company's paid-up equity capital through preferential warrant conversion with standard statutory lock-in restrictions.
