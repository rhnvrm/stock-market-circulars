## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of five newly listed companies to its respective indices following their public offerings and listings on the exchange. Bonfiglioli Transmissions Limited, Tonbo Imaging India Limited, Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited, and Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited will be added to the BSE IPO index effective October 27, 2026. Additionally, Shellz India Limited will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Bonfiglioli Transmissions Limited (Ticker: 544996) added to BSE IPO index effective October 27, 2026.
- Tonbo Imaging India Limited (Ticker: 544999) added to BSE IPO index effective October 27, 2026.
- Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited (Ticker: 545000) added to BSE IPO index effective October 27, 2026.
- Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited (Ticker: 545001) added to BSE IPO index effective October 27, 2026.
- Shellz India Limited (Ticker: 544998) added to BSE SME IPO index effective October 26, 2026.
- Corporate actions included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE) for October 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No compliance requirements for market participants; informational notice regarding index composition updates.

## Important Dates

- October 23, 2026: Notice issuance and inclusion in end-of-day corporate action files.
- October 26, 2026: Effective date at market open for Shellz India Limited on BSE SME IPO index.
- October 27, 2026: Effective date at market open for Bonfiglioli Transmissions, Tonbo Imaging India, Zetwerk Manufacturing, and Sahajanand Medical Technologies on BSE IPO index.

## Impact Assessment

Routine index adjustment affecting index funds and portfolios tracking the BSE IPO and BSE SME IPO indices.
