## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of privately placed securities (commercial papers) issued by various companies on the debt market segment effective August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective August 18, 2026.
- Issuances include commercial papers from Time Technoplast Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, TATA Steel Limited, and Godrej Housing Finance Limited.
- Traded strictly in dematerialised form under specific ISIN numbers with standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard demat form and standard commercial paper denominations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 18, 2026
- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt market listing activity with no broader operational or market impact.
