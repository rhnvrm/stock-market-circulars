## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Madhuraam Construction (India) Limited for its SME IPO on August 14, 2026. The document is made available on the stock exchange websites to invite public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Madhuraam Construction (India) Limited
- Filing Date: August 14, 2026
- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice

## Regulatory Changes

- Disclosures made as specified under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public comments on the draft offer document must be submitted within 21 days of the notice on email id equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: August 14, 2026
- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Comment Deadline: Within 21 days from August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides transparency and allows market participants and the public to review and submit comments on the proposed SME IPO.
