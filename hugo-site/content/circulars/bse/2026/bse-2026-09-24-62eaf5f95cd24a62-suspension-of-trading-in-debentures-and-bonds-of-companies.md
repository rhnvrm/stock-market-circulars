## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of several companies. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures/bonds as the companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and redemption.

## Key Points

- **Purpose:** Suspension of trading due to record dates for interest payment and redemption of NCDs/Bonds.
- **Affected Issuers:** Kosamattam Finance Limited, L&T Finance Limited, Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, State Bank of India, Mankind Pharma Limited, U.P. Power Corporation Limited, Krazybee Services Ltd, Edelweiss Financial Services Ltd., and A.K. Capital Finance Limited.
- **Trading Restriction:** Trading members are advised not to deal in the listed debentures/bonds starting from the respective ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational notice regarding corporate debt actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with trading restrictions on the specified debentures and bonds during the record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 24 September 2026
- **Record Dates:** October 1, 2, 3, 5, and 7, 2026 across various securities.
- **Ex-Dates:** Primarily October 1, 5, and 7, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and routine market impact affecting only secondary market trading of the specified debt instruments during their record and ex-dates.
