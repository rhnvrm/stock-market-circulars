## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) granted a stay on the debarment of Mr. Malay Bhow in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips, subject to the deposit of 50% of the penalty. SEBI confirmed via email that the appellant has successfully paid the penalty amount of Rs. 50,00,000.00/- in favour of SEBI.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous Exchange Notice No. 20260701-4 dated July 01, 2026.
- SAT order dated August 03, 2026, stayed the impugned debarment order pending final hearing, subject to penalty deposit.
- SEBI confirmed on August 18, 2026, that Mr. Malay Bhow paid the penalty of Rs. 50,00,000/-.
- Trading members advised to take note and ensure compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the SAT stay order and SEBI's confirmation of penalty payment and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- **August 03, 2026:** SAT order granting stay on debarment
- **August 18, 2026:** SEBI confirmation email regarding penalty payment of Rs. 50,00,000/-

## Impact Assessment

The notice provides regulatory clarity on the status of the debarment and penalty compliance for the concerned individual regarding the specified scrips.
