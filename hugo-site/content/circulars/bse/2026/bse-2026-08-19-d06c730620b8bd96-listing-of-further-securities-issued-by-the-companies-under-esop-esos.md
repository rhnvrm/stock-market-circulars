## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 14 companies under ESOP/ESOS are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Thursday, August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 14
- Effective date for trading: August 20, 2026
- Companies include Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, Coforge Limited, ICICI Bank Ltd., Shriram Finance Limited, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants are requested to take note of the listings for trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 19, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard issuance and listing of shares under employee stock option schemes, resulting in minor equity capital increases for the respective companies.
