## Summary

The Exchange has issued a notice communicating updates from the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) regarding the Digital Intelligence Platform (DIP). Key updates include the introduction of a Stakeholder Feedback facility under SancharSaathi Utilities and revised guidelines on the utilization of the Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL) and Reactivation List for cyber fraud prevention by banks, financial institutions, and trading members.

## Key Points

- **Stakeholder Feedback Facility:** DoT has launched a new feedback mechanism under SancharSaathi Utilities on the DIP portal to collect suggestions and requirements from stakeholders regarding dashboards, data views, and platform functionalities.
- **Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL):** Trading members and financial institutions are advised to daily download and utilize the latest effective MNRL and Reactivation List (updated daily).
- **90-Day Rule:** Disconnected numbers in MNRL may be reallocated after 90 days. Actions should consider numbers remaining in the effective MNRL (MNRL minus Reactivation List) pertaining to the last 90 days.
- **Verification Requirement:** Mobile numbers must be verified against the latest Reactivation List before taking any action (reactivation date must be >= disconnection date).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of the updated Quick Reference Guide for Banks, FIs, NBFCs, and SEBI-regulated entities using the DIP portal for MNRL and Financial Fraud Risk Indicator (FRI) related actions.
- Requirement for onboarding onto the DoT Digital Intelligence Platform and sharing required details with DoT.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members, Banks, and FIs must download and use updated MNRL and Reactivation datasets daily.
- Appropriate Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) must be carried out in accordance with internal policies and regulatory guidelines before taking any action based on MNRL.
- Review and revocation of action on numbers appearing in MNRL Reactivation or after the 90-day period as deemed fit.
- Complete onboarding on the DoT Digital Intelligence Platform by sharing required organization details with DoT.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 02 Sep 2026
- **Continuation of Notice No.:** 20260612-6 dated June 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances operational efficiency and data accuracy for trading members and financial institutions in preventing financial cyber frauds using real-time telecommunications datasets and feedback loops.
