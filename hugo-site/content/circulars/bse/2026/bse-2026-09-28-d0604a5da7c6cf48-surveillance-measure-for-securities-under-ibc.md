## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the IBC framework criteria are placed under surveillance effective September 29, 2026.
- Annexure I lists newly included securities, Annexure II lists securities moving out, Annexure III provides the consolidated list, and Annexure IV lists securities classified post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) below 25%.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated unless subject to other surveillance measures.
- Review of securities under the framework is conducted on a periodical basis.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Additional Surveillance Measures for companies under the Insolvency Resolution Process (IBC).
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips exiting the IBC framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the inclusion, exclusion, and consolidated list of securities under the IBC surveillance framework.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2026
- Notice Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading restrictions and surveillance measures apply to affected securities under IBC. Price bands are adjusted accordingly upon entry or exit from the framework.
