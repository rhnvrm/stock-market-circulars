## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission of 22,76,400 new equity shares of Rs. 5/- each issued at par on a preferential basis to non-promoters by Noble Polymers Limited (Scrip Code: 539200), effective from Tuesday, October 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: NOBLE POLYMERS LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 539200
- ISIN: INE203Q01026
- Number of Shares: 22,76,400 equity shares of Rs. 5/- each issued at par on a preferential basis to non-promoters.
- Effective Date of Trading: October 6, 2026.
- Allotment Date: August 7, 2026.
- Ranking: Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard corporate listing notice for preferential share issuance.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and distribution numbers as specified:
- 17,51,080 shares (Dist. Nos. 6479001 - 8230080) locked in up to 20/04/2028.
- 5,25,320 shares (Dist. Nos. 8230081 - 8755400) locked in up to 20/04/2027.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 7, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading: October 6, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: April 20, 2027 (5,25,320 shares) and April 20, 2028 (17,51,080 shares)

## Impact Assessment

The listing expands the equity capital base of Noble Polymers Limited by 22,76,400 shares issued on a preferential basis. The impact is limited to the company's equity structure and shareholders holding the newly listed securities, adhering to specified lock-in periods.
