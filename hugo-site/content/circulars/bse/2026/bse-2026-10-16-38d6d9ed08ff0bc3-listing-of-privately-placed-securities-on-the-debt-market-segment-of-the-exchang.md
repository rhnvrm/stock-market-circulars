## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by various companies on the debt market segment effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Issuers include Indian Oil Corporation Ltd., Tata Steel Limited, IKF Finance Limited, and A.K. Capital Services Ltd.
- Securities are traded in dematerialised form with standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- Tick size for the securities is 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with dematerialised trading standards and ISIN specifications.

## Important Dates

- Allotment / Effective Date: October 15, 2026 / October 16, 2026
- Maturity / Redemption Dates ranging from October 2026 to March 2027 depending on the specific commercial paper issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine debt segment listing providing liquidity for commercial papers with minimal direct impact on equity markets.
