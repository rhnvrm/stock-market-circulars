## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity segment on Saturday, August 22, 2026. Trading members can test their trading applications, including third-party platforms and in-house systems via ETI APIs, for modifications related to the Pre-open Call Auction Session and other market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, August 22, 2026, for the Equity segment.
- Focuses on testing modifications in the Pre-open Call Auction Session as per circular no. 20260803-35.
- Allows testing of exceptional market conditions, risk-reduction mode, trading halts, and special pre-open for IPO & re-listed scrips.
- Trades resulting from the mock session will not attract margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation and testing of modifications in the Pre-open Call Auction Session for equity trading members.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members using third-party or in-house trading applications are requested to actively participate and test their systems.
- Members are encouraged to provide feedback on the mock trading session to help improve system features.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 22, 2026
- Login Time: 08:15 am to 08:45 am
- Block Deal Session: 08:45 am to 09:00 am
- Continuous Trading End: 12:00 pm
- Trade Modification End: 12:20 pm

## Impact Assessment

- Zero financial or operational impact as the session is purely for testing and familiarization purposes with no margin or settlement obligations.
