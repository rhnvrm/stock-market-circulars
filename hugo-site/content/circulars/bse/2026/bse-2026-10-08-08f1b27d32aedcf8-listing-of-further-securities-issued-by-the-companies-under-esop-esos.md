## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 18 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Friday, October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing securities under ESOP/ESOS: 18.
- Effective Date for trading: October 09, 2026.
- Includes prominent companies such as Axis Bank Limited, Coforge Limited, Wipro Limited, RBL Bank Limited, and others.
- All listed further securities are free from lock-in (marked as NA under lock-in column).
