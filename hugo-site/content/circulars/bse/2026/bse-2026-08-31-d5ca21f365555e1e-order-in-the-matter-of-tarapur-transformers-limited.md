## Summary

SEBI vide its order no. QJA/SS/CFID/CFID-SEC6/32688/2026-27 dated August 31, 2026, has restrained Tarapur Transformers Limited and several other entities from accessing the securities market and prohibited them from dealing in securities in any manner.

## Key Points

- Tarapur Transformers Limited and 7 other corporate entities are restrained for a period of 3 years.
- Noticee No. 10 (Rajendra Kumar Choudhary) is restrained for a period of 5 years.
- Noticees are permitted to square off open derivative positions within 3 months or at expiry, whichever is earlier, and settle existing pay-in and pay-out obligations.
- The order is effective immediately from August 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the debarment and ensure that the restrained entities do not execute any prohibited transactions in the securities market.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 31, 2026
- Effective Date: August 31, 2026
- Square off deadline for open derivative positions: Within 3 months from the date of the order or at contract expiry, whichever is earlier.

## Impact Assessment

High impact on the listed entity and associated noticees, restricting their participation and dealing in the capital markets.
