## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CPs) for multiple companies that have fixed record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies as per the schedule.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260904-7, dated September 04, 2026.
- Segment: Debt (Listing Operations).
- Covers multiple companies including SBICAP Securities Limited, JSW Energy Limited, Titan Company Limited, and Manappuram Finance Limited.
- Trading members are advised to refrain from dealing in the specified Commercial Papers.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance by avoiding any trading or dealing in the specified Commercial Papers of the listed companies from their respective record/ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates and Ex-Dates range from 07/09/2026 to 12/09/2026 depending on the specific company's Commercial Paper redemption schedule.

## Impact Assessment

Restricts secondary market trading in specific Commercial Papers approaching their redemption dates to facilitate smooth settlement and redemption processes.
