## Summary

Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Ltd has announced the sub-division of its equity shares. The existing face value of Rs. 2/- per share will be split into two equity shares of Re. 1/- each, with the record date set for August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kirloskar Pneumatic Company Ltd (Scrip Code: 505283)
- Sub-Division: One equity share of Rs. 2/- each into Two equity shares of Re. 1/- each.
- Record Date: August 18, 2026.
- Existing ISIN (INE811A01020 of Rs. 02/- paid up) will become invalid for transactions on or after August 18, 2026.
- New ISIN for the Re. 1/- paid-up shares will be notified separately by the exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the invalidation of the old ISIN and await the notification of the new ISIN for transactions post-record date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 18, 2026
- Effective Date for Sub-Division: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Existing shareholders will see their holdings doubled with a corresponding halving of the share price due to the stock split, with no change in total equity value.
