## Summary

Tips Music Limited has announced the buyback of its fully paid-up equity shares of face value Rs. 1/- each from the open market through stock exchanges, in accordance with SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018. The maximum buyback price is set at Rs. 750/- per equity share for an aggregate amount not exceeding INR 44,55,00,000/-.

## Key Points

- Buyback Type: Open market through Stock Exchange.
- Maximum Buyback Price: Rs. 750/- per equity share.
- Maximum Buyback Size: INR 44,55,00,000/-.
- Company's Broker: ITI Securities Broking Limited (Member Clearing No. 176).
- Registrar to the Buyback: MUFG Intime India Private Limited.
- Manager to the Buyback: Inga Ventures Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with the SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 updated as of July 6, 2026, and Exchange Notice No. 20260731-9 dated July 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- All payment obligations relating to the buyback must be completed before the last date of the buyback.

## Important Dates

- Date of Opening of the Buyback: September 09, 2026.
- Last Date for the Buyback: Earlier of December 11, 2026 (66 working days), completion of the buyback by deploying the maximum size, or earlier closure determined by the board.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit opportunity or value realization for existing shareholders through open market purchases up to the specified aggregate limit.
