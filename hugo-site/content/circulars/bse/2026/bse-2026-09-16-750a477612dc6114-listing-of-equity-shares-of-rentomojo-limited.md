## Summary

Effective from Thursday, September 17, 2026, the equity shares of Rentomojo Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities. The public issue comprised 104,114,824 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up at an issue price of Rs. 404/- per share.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Rentomojo Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544915
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** RENTOMOJO
- **ISIN No.:** INE08T701025
- **Total Securities:** 104,114,824 Equity Shares (Face Value Rs. 1/-)
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 404/- per share (including premium of Rs. 403/-)
- **Date of Allotment:** September 15, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Registrar:** Kfin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- The scrip will be a part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, September 17, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed for lock-in period marking in depository records.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 15, 2026
- **Listing and Trading Effective Date:** September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Rentomojo Limited shares on the BSE platform under the 'B' Group with mandatory demat settlement.
