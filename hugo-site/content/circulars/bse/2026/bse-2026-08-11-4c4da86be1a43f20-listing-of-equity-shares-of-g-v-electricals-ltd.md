## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, August 12, 2026, the Equity Shares of G V ELECTRICALS LTD shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE SME segment under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544859
- ISIN No: INE1ZGR01024
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: GVELECTRIC
- Total Securities: 11,279,190 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 11279190)
- Issue Price: Rs. 130/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-, Premium: Rs. 120/-)
- Market Lot: 1000 equity shares
- Date of Allotment: August 10, 2026
- Market Maker: Mansi Share and Stock Broking Private Limited
- Registrar: Mudra RTA Ventures Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Wednesday, August 12, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in the specified minimum market lot of 1000 shares.
- Necessary corporate actions have been executed by the company to mark the lock-in period in depository records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 11, 2026
- Date of Allotment: August 10, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides initial trading access for G V ELECTRICALS LTD equity shares on the BSE SME platform, with temporary trade-for-trade settlement restrictions for the first 10 trading days.
