## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. 704 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-704/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: Monday, August 31, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction will be held on Tuesday, September 01, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 31, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine settlement operation affecting trading members participating in auction for settlement DR-704/2026-27.
