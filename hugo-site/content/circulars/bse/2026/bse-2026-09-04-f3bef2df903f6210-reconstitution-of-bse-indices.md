## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the reconstitution results for various BSE indices, including BSE AllCap, LargeCap, MidCap, SmallCap, and others. The changes will be effective at the open of Monday, September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reconstitution results announced for multiple BSE indices.
- Effective date: Monday, September 21, 2026.
- Changes include additions and deletions across broad-market, sectoral, and thematic indices.
- Actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Clients and market participants are advised to note the changes in index constituents for portfolio management and benchmarking purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 04, 2026
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Constituent changes will necessitate rebalancing by index funds and ETFs tracking the respective BSE indices.
