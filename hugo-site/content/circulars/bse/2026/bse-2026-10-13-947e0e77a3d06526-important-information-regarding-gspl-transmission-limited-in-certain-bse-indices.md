## Summary

BSE Index Services Pvt. Ltd. has announced that GSPL Transmission Limited (Scrip code: 544801) will be excluded from multiple BSE indices effective prior to the open of trading on Thursday, October 15, 2026, because the company did not hit the lower circuit on October 12 and October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- GSPL Transmission Limited (Scrip code: 544801) is excluded from BSE 500, BSE PSU, BSE MIDCAP, BSE ALLCAP, BSE ENERGY, and several other indices.
- Exclusion follows the process announced in Notice No: 20250403-46 regarding the removal of spun-off scrips from BSE Indices.
- Effective date of exclusion is prior to market open on October 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of index governance rules regarding spun-off scrips and circuit filter conditions in BSE indices.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and index-tracking funds must note the index constituents change reflected in tonight's end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Important Dates

- October 12-13, 2026: Observation days for circuit filter status.
- October 15, 2026: Effective date of exclusion prior to trading open.

## Impact Assessment

Funds tracking the affected BSE indices (such as BSE 500, BSE PSU, BSE MIDCAP, etc.) will rebalance their portfolios to account for the exclusion of GSPL Transmission Limited.
