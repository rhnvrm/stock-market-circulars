## Summary

BSE has issued a notice highlighting significant price and volume movements observed in various scrips in the recent past. The exchange sought clarifications from multiple companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information. Clarifications have been received from 14 companies, while responses from 22 other companies are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- **Clarifications Received From:** Aureate Tradde Ltd, JHS Svendgaard Retail Ventures Ltd, Kunststoffe Industries Ltd, Mach Travel Solutions Ltd, Magellanic Cloud Ltd, Medico Remedies Ltd, Mukand Ltd, Remsons Industries Ltd, Riyaasat Lifestyle Ltd, Shri Krishna Devcon Ltd, Starteck Finance Ltd, Sunflag Iron & Steel Company Ltd, Tribhovandas Bhimji Zaveri Ltd, and Vikram Aroma Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited From:** Billwin Industries Ltd, Citichem India Ltd, Cityman Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, El Forge Ltd, Farm Peace Ltd, Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Ltd, Gabriel Pet Straps Ltd, Jhandewalas Foods Ltd, Kanishk Aluminium India Ltd, Landsmill Green Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Mopshop Distribution Ltd, Nettlinx Ltd, Rex Sealing and Packing Industries Ltd, S. M. Gold Ltd, Safecure Services Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, UHM Vacation Ltd, and Vellora Impact Ltd.
- **Objective:** To maintain market transparency and ensure timely disclosure of material information to market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; routine surveillance communication under exchange bylaws.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities experiencing significant price or volume volatility are required to promptly clarify price-sensitive information to the exchange upon request.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors dealing in the mentioned scrips should exercise caution and monitor upcoming corporate disclosures or exchange clarifications.
