## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the penalty report for the modification of client codes of non-institutional trades across all segments for the month of July 2026. The report has been downloaded to the EQUITY TRANSACTION FOLDER in the Extranet.

## Key Points

- **Report File Name:** PM0726.Clg no.
- **Period Covered:** July 2026
- **Applicability:** All trading members with non-institutional client code modifications.
- **Reference Exchange Notices:** 20110729-24 (dated July 29, 2011) and 20110826-4 (dated August 26, 2011).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is an operational notice regarding monthly penalty report distribution under existing exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to download and save the penalty report files from the Extranet for their future reference and compliance records.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Data Period:** July 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification for trading members to verify and archive their monthly client code modification penalty reports.
