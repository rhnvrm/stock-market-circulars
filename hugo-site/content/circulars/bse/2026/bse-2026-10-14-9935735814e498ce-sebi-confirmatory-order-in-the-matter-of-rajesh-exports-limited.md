## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding SEBI's confirmatory order dated October 14, 2026, in the matter of Rajesh Exports Limited. The order continues the restraint on Noticee no. 2, Mr. Rajesh Mehta, from buying, selling, or dealing in the securities of Rajesh Exports Limited until further orders.

## Key Points

- In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260603-30 dated June 03, 2026.
- SEBI Order No.: WTM/KV/CFID/CFID-SEC6/32770/2026-27 dated October 14, 2026.
- Mr. Rajesh Mehta (Noticee no. 2) continues to be restrained from buying, selling, or otherwise dealing in the securities of Rajesh Exports Limited in any manner whatsoever.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of enforcement directions and restrictions on securities dealings by specified noticees under SEBI regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the order and ensure strict compliance regarding the restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Order Date / Effective Date: October 14, 2026
- Notice Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Maintains regulatory restrictions on trading and dealings in securities of Rajesh Exports Limited by the restrained individual.
