## Summary

BSE has updated the list of securities subjected to the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). This update, effective from August 13, 2026, details securities added to the ASM framework, securities moving out of the IBC framework, and securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) falling below 25%.

## Key Points

- **Inclusions**: Securities in Annexure I satisfy the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) criteria for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) under IBC, effective from August 13, 2026.
- **Exclusions**: Securities in Annexure II are moving out of the IBC Framework, effective from August 13, 2026.
- **Consolidated List**: Annexure III provides a ready-reference consolidated list of securities currently under the IBC Framework.
- **Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) Classification**: Annexure IV lists securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding being less than 25%.
- **Price Band Reinstatement**: The price band for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their pre-shortlisted level, provided they are not under any other surveillance measures.
- **Nature of Surveillance**: The selection under this framework is purely based on market surveillance parameters and does not represent any adverse action against the concerned entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- This circular updates the surveillance lists defined under previous Exchange notices: no. 20180608-34, no. 20220520-52, no. 20230210-55, no. 20250905-47, and no. 20260807-39 in respect of Additional Surveillance Measures for IRP/IBC companies.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Price Band Application**: Market participants and trading members must apply the reinstated price bands for securities moving out of the framework, subject to other active surveillance measures.
- **Operational Monitoring**: Trading desks and risk management systems must adjust margin/leverage parameters for the newly added and deleted securities in accordance with the updated Annexures.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Tightened surveillance (such as price band restrictions or additional margins) will apply to securities listed under Annexure I, potentially reducing their short-term trading liquidity.
- **Exiting Securities Impact**: Securities exiting the framework (Annexure II) will see price bands restored to pre-shortlist limits, likely restoring normalized trading activity and liquidity, unless bound by other active surveillance measures.
