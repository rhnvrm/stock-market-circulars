## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the Lakshya Overnight Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window is open from September 15, 2026, to September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Lakshya Overnight Fund
- **NFO Period:** September 15, 2026, to September 18, 2026 (extended till 6:00 PM on September 18, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- **Plans & ISINs:**
  - Regular Plan - Growth: `INF2ZPR01022` (Tentative Allotment Date: September 21, 2026)
  - Direct Plan - Growth: `INF2ZPR01014` (Tentative Allotment Date: September 21, 2026)
- **Transaction Rules:** 
  - Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
  - Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 18, 2026.
  - Cheque and OTM payments are accepted subject to timely receipt of clear funds.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that client funds are initiated and cleared within the stipulated timelines for valid allotment under the NFO scheme.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** September 15, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** September 18, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** September 18, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to subscribe to the new overnight mutual fund scheme via the BSE StAR MF platform with clear operational timelines.
