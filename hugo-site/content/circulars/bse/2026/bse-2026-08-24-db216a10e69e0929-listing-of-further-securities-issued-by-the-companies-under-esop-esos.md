## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by nine companies under their Employee Stock Ownership Plan / Employee Stock Options Scheme (ESOP / ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Tuesday, August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 9
- Effective date for trading: August 25, 2026
- Securities category: Equity shares issued under ESOP/ESOS
- Lock-in status: Not Applicable (NA) for all listed shares in this batch.
