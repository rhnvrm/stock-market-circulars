## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the updated SEBI circular concerning norms for base price, price bands, call auction in pre-open sessions, and closeout procedures for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs), in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260616-23 dated June 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous Exchange Notice No. 20260616-23 dated June 16, 2026.
- Market participants are required to note the updated SEBI circular regarding ETF trading norms.
- Covers base price determination, price bands, call auction sessions, and closeout procedures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of updated SEBI guidelines for ETF trading operations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of and comply with the updated SEBI circular provisions for ETFs.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects trading operations, order matching, and settlement procedures specifically for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs).
