## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of their non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- **Niyogin Fintech Limited**: ISIN INE480D07025, Record Date: 04/09/2026, Ex Date: 04/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 100/- per Debenture.
- **Earlysalary Services Private Limited**: ISIN INE01YL07300, Record Date: 12/09/2026, Ex Date: 11/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 11111/- per Debenture.
- **Varthana Finance Private Limited**: ISIN INE125T07337, Record Date: 12/09/2026, Ex Date: 11/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 40000/- per Debenture.
- **Navi Finserv Limited**: ISIN INE342T07585, Record Date: 12/09/2026, Ex Date: 11/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 3333/- per Debenture.
- **Satin Finserv Limited**: ISIN INE03K307074, Record Date: 14/09/2026, Ex Date: 11/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 50000/- per Debenture.
- **Manba Finance Limited**: ISIN INE939X07242, Record Date: 12/09/2026, Ex Date: 11/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 60000/- per Debenture.
- **Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited**: ISIN INE08XP07381, Record Date: 13/09/2026, Ex Date: 11/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 769.24/- per Debenture.
- **Greaves Finance Limited**: ISIN INE1QWF07022, Record Date: 14/09/2026, Ex Date: 11/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 29166.61/- per Debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for debt segment trading.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: 03/09/2026
- **Record Dates**: Between 04/09/2026 and 14/09/2026
- **Ex Dates**: 04/09/2026 and 11/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the affected NCDs will receive part redemption payments, and subsequent trading in these securities will occur at the updated reduced face values.
