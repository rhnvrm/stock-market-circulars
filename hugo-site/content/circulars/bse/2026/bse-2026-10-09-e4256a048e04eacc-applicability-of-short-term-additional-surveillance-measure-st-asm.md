## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for specified securities, effective October 13, 2026, based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I attract a margin rate of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II attract a margin rate of 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Measures apply to all open positions as on October 12, 2026, and new positions created from October 13, 2026 onwards.
- Action is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II framework rules in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure applicable margin rates (50% for Stage I, 100% for Stage II) are maintained on all open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 13, 2026 (applicable on open positions as of October 12, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for those specific scrips.
