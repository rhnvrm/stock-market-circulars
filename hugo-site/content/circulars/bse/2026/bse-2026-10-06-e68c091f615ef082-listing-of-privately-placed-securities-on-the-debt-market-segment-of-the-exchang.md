## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by several companies effective October 6, 2026. Trading takes place in dematerialised form in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Key Points

- Chennai Petroleum Corporation Ltd.: 20,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732708, ISIN: INE178A14HV0) maturing on 03/11/2026 with 5.95% interest.
- Navi Finserv Limited: 1,600 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732709, ISIN: INE342T14HY6) maturing on 04/01/2027 with 9.25% interest.
- Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited: 8,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732710, ISIN: INE916D147K0) maturing on 06/10/2027 with 7.90% interest.
- IKF Finance Limited: 1,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732711, ISIN: INE859C14200) maturing on 21/01/2027 with 8.75% interest.
- Bajaj Finance Limited: 8,000 Commercial Papers (Scrip Code: 732712, ISIN: INE296A14K71) maturing on 23/11/2026 with 6.75% interest.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard debt segment listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must trade the securities in dematerialised form under the specified ISIN numbers with standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and 1 paise tick size.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Dates: October 5, 2026 and October 6, 2026
- Redemption Dates: November 3, 2026, November 23, 2026, January 4, 2027, January 21, 2027, and October 6, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates short-term debt financing and secondary market trading for the listed commercial papers.
