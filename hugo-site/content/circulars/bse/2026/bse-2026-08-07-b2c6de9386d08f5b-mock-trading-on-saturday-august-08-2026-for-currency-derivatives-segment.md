## Summary

The BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, August 08, 2026. Trading members using third-party empaneled vendor platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their trading applications under various market conditions.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Currency Derivatives & Cross Currency Derivatives
- **Date:** Saturday, August 08, 2026
- **Purpose:** Testing and familiarization; trades will not attract margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.
- **No New Release:** There shall be no new BOLT TWS Release.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to actively participate in the mock trading sessions and provide feedback by 1:00 PM on the day of the session.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** August 08, 2026
- **Log-in Time:** 09:15 AM to 10:00 AM
- **Continuous Trading (Currency & Cross Currency):** 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM
- **Trade Modification (Currency & Cross Currency):** Until 12:10 PM

## Impact Assessment

Operational preparedness and system validation for trading members with zero financial impact or clearing obligation.
