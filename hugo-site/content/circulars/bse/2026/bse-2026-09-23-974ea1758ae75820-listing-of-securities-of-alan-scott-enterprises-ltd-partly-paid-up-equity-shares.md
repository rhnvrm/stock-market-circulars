## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Friday, September 25, 2026, the new securities of Alan Scott Enterprises Ltd. shall be listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange in the 'XT' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Security Description: 9,52,931 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each partly paid-up of Rs. 7.50/- each issued on Rights Basis.
- Scrip Code: 890239
- Group: XT
- Market Lot: 1
- Scrip ID / BOLT System: ASCOTTPP
- ISIN No.: IN9273F01038
- Face Value: Rs. 10/-
- Paid-up Value: Rs. 7.50/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 10/- (Rs. 7.50/- paid on application and Rs. 2.50/- to be paid on one or more subsequent calls as determined by the Board)
- Distinctive Numbers: 1 - 952931
- Date of Allotment: 17/09/2026
- Dividend Entitlement: Proportionate to the amount paid-up

## Regulatory Changes

Temporary ISIN No. IN8273F01054 is suspended in terms of SEBI Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/21/2012 and CIR/MRD/DP/24/2012 regarding activation of ISIN in case of additional issue of shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to note the suspension of the temporary ISIN and the activation of the new ISIN for trading effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 17/09/2026
- Effective Date for Listing and Trading: 25/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of the newly allotted partly paid-up rights equity shares of Alan Scott Enterprises Ltd. on the BSE platform under the 'XT' group.
