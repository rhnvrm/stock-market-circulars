## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding Gazette Notification no. F.No. SEBI/LAD-DOP/2026/319 dated October 7, 2026, issued by SEBI, concerning amendments to the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Certification of Associated Persons in the Securities Markets) Regulations, 2007.

## Key Points

- Refers to SEBI Gazette Notification F.No. SEBI/LAD-DOP/2026/319 dated October 7, 2026.
- Concerns amendments to the Certification of Associated Persons in the Securities Markets Regulations, 2007.
- Trading members are advised to take note and ensure compliance.
- Clarifications can be sought via membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

Amendments to the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Certification of Associated Persons in the Securities Markets) Regulations, 2007, as detailed in the SEBI Gazette Notification dated October 7, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must review the notified amendments and ensure full compliance across associated persons and operations.

## Important Dates

- Notification Date: October 7, 2026 (SEBI Gazette Notification)
- Notice Date: October 16, 2026 (BSE Notice)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures that certified associated persons in the securities markets adhere to updated regulatory standards, maintaining professional competence and compliance.
