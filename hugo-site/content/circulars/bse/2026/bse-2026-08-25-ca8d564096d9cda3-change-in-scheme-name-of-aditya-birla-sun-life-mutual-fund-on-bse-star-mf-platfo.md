## Summary

Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund has issued a notice regarding the change in scheme names for ten of its mutual fund schemes available on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260825-38
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Segment: Mutual Fund
- Department: Trading Operations
- Total Schemes Affected: 10

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable. These are operational scheme name updates initiated by the Asset Management Company (AMC).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for order placement and reporting on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Existing unitholders and investors will see the updated scheme names on the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.
