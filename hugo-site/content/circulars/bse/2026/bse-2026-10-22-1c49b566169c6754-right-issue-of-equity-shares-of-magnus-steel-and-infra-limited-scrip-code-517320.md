## Summary

Magnus Steel and Infra Limited has announced a Rights Issue of Equity Shares for its eligible shareholders. The company has fixed October 27, 2026, as the record date to determine shareholder eligibility. Trading in the company's equity shares will commence on an ex-rights basis starting from the same date under the rolling settlement segment.

## Key Points

- **Company Name & Scrip Code:** Magnus Steel and Infra Limited (Scrip Code: 517320)
- **Issue Ratio:** 59 (Fifty-Nine) Equity Shares for every 05 (Five) Equity Shares held on the record date.
- **Issue Price:** Rs.10/- each for cash at par, payable entirely on application.
- **Record Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Basis Date:** October 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory framework changes; processed in accordance with standard BSE listing operations and corporate action guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with ex-rights settlement schedules and verify client holdings as of the record date.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Date:** October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Existing shareholders receive rights entitlements in the specified ratio, impacting shareholding dilution and portfolio valuation. Market prices adjust on the ex-rights date reflecting the rights issue terms.
