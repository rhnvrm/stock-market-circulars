## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window for Buyback) of equity shares of Transport Corporation of India (TCI). The buyback offer is scheduled from Thursday, October 15, 2026, to Thursday, October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** TRANSPORT CORPORATION OF INDIA
- **Security Symbol:** TCI
- **ISIN:** INE688A01022
- **Scrip Code:** 532349
- **Offer Size:** Up to 15,62,500 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 960.00 per share
- **Face Value:** Rs. 2.00
- **Opening Date:** Thursday, 15th October 2026
- **Closing Date:** Thursday, 22nd October 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Operational notification for an ongoing corporate buyback action.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure UCC of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading Members can bid for investors having shares in demat form.
- Members and Custodians must adhere to operational instructions outlined in Exchange Notices no. 20150930-1 and 20170203-29.

## Important Dates

- **Opening Date:** October 15, 2026 (9:15 a.m.)
- **Closing Date:** October 22, 2026 (3:30 p.m.)
- **Give-up / Take-up Period:** October 15, 2026 to October 21, 2026 (Give-up on last day: October 22, 2026; Take-up on last day: October 22, 2026 up to 4:00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless execution and settlement of the buyback offer for TCI shareholders within the acquisition window framework.
