## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for several scrips under the Equity segment, effective from October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised for 35 scrips.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is October 19, 2026.
- Revised price bands range from 1%, 2%, 5%, 10%, to 20% across various scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading members are informed of the updated price limits for specific equity scrips to maintain market integrity and manage volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading systems and risk management parameters to reflect the new price bands for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and traders dealing in the listed scrips must take note of the revised operating price limits starting from the effective date.
