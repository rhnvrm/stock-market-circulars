## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Shah Investor's Home Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment under B Group, effective October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shah Investor's Home Limited
- Scrip Code: 544963
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange's Notice No. 20261005-41 dated October 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The trading category and settlement segment for the company's equity shares are updated from Trade for Trade to regular Rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the segment shift and ensure alignment with Rolling segment settlement procedures for transactions executed from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shifting from the Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment allows normal rolling settlement for the scrip, which typically improves liquidity and trading flexibility for investors.
