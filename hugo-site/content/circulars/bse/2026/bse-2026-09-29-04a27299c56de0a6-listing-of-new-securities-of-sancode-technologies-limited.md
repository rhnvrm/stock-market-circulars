## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 11,11,111 new equity shares of Sancode Technologies Limited, issued on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants, effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sancode Technologies Limited (Scrip Code - 543897)
- Security Details: 11,11,111 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 44/- (Issue Price: Rs. 54/-).
- Allotment Date: 16/05/2026
- ISIN: INE0P7001013
- Distribution Nos.: 4068868 to 5179978
- Lock-in Details: 4,44,444 shares (Dist. 4068868 to 4513311) locked-in up to 15/04/2028; 6,66,667 shares (Dist. 4513312 to 5179978) locked-in up to 15/04/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with lock-in requirements as mandated for preferential issues.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 16/05/2026
- Effective Trading Date: 30/09/2026
- Lock-in Expiry: 15/04/2027 and 15/04/2028

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of equity capital base through warrant conversion with standard lock-in stipulations.
