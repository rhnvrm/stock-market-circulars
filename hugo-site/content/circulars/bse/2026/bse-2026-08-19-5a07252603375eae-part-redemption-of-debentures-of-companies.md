## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that specific companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of their non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in the debentures of these companies will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **Ratnaafin Capital Private Limited**: Scrip Code 977619, ISIN INE0MJR07033. Record Date: 22/08/2026, Ex-Date: 21/08/2026. Reduced face value: Rs. 91,667/- per debenture.
- **Choice Finserv Private Limited**: Scrip Code 977625, ISIN INE04SE07152. Record Date: 28/08/2026, Ex-Date: 28/08/2026. Reduced face value: Rs. 7,500/- per debenture.
- Trading in the respective debentures will operate with the reduced face value starting from the ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for proper settlement and execution of trades in the respective NCDs.

## Important Dates

- **Ratnaafin Capital Ex-Date**: 21/08/2026
- **Ratnaafin Capital Record Date**: 22/08/2026
- **Choice Finserv Ex-Date & Record Date**: 28/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for trading members regarding reduced face value and part redemption payouts for the specified NCDs.
