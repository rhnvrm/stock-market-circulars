## Summary

India Advantage Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 3036) is voluntarily closing its business in the currency derivative segment, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member: India Advantage Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 3036)
- Segment: Currency derivative segment
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Reason: Voluntary closure of business

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Queries regarding the closure should be directed to membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Closure: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Limited to the cessation of currency derivative trading services by India Advantage Securities Pvt. Ltd. on BSE.
