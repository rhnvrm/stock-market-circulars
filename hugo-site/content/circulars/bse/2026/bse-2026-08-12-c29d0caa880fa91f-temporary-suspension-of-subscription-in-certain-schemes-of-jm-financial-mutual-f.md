## Summary

The BSE has notified members and mutual fund distributors regarding the temporary suspension of fresh subscriptions, including fresh lumpsum investments, additional purchases, switch-ins, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in), for specific schemes of JM Financial Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The suspension is effective starting August 12, 2026, following a direct request and communication from JM Financial Asset Management Company (AMC).

## Key Points

- **Suspended Transactions:** Fresh lumpsum investments, additional purchases, switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in) are temporarily disabled for the specified schemes.
- **Effective Date:** The suspension is active on the platform from August 12, 2026. Applications received after the applicable cut-off time on August 11, 2026, will not be accepted or processed.
- **Existing Systematic Plans:** All existing Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP-IN) registered prior to the effective date on the BSE StAR MF Platform will continue to be processed normally without any change.
- **Permitted Transactions:** Redemption, switch-out, STP-out, and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) transactions remain fully operational and permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory framework changes are introduced. The operational suspension is implemented based on a direct notice-cum-addendum and request from JM Financial AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

- **BSE StAR MF Members & Distributors:** Must update their internal systems and advisory processes to ensure no fresh subscriptions or new systematic registrations are submitted for the affected schemes starting August 12, 2026.
- **Client Communications:** Advise existing and prospective investors of the suspension, clarifying that redemption and existing systematic transactions are unaffected.

## Important Dates

- **Cut-off for Processing:** August 11, 2026 (applicable cut-off time).
- **Effective Date of Suspension:** Close of business hours on August 11, 2026 (effective for transactions starting August 12, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Medium for mutual fund distributors and prospective investors on the BSE StAR MF platform, who will not be able to register new investments in the specified schemes.
- **Investor Liquidity:** Low/No impact on current holders, as redemptions and systematic withdrawals remain active and unaffected.
- **Market Impact:** Low systemic impact, localized to specific schemes under JM Financial Mutual Fund on the BSE mutual fund platform.
