## Summary

Bizotic Commercial Limited has fixed a record date for a bonus issue of equity shares in the ratio of 5:1. Concurrently, the exchange has announced a revision in the trading market lot for the company's equity shares effective from August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Bonus Issue Ratio: 05 (Five) Bonus Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each for every 01 (One) existing Equity Share of Rs. 10/- each.
- Record Date: August 17, 2026.
- Ex-Bonus Date: August 17, 2026.
- Revised Market Lot: Changed from 200 to 1200.
- Total Allotment: 48,210,000 Equity Shares expected to be allotted as on August 18, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the bonus issue, ex-date, and the revised market lot for Bizotic Commercial Limited (Scrip Code: 543926).

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 17, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date for Market Lot Change: August 17, 2026
- Allotment Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors need to update their systems for the revised market lot of 1200 shares and account for the price adjustment due to the 5:1 bonus issue.
