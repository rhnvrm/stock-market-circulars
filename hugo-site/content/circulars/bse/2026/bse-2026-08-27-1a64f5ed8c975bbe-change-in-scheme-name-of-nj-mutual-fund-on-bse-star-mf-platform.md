## Summary

NJ Mutual Fund has communicated a change in the scheme name for its ELSS scheme on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect, changing 'NJ ELSS TAX SAVER SCHEME' to 'NJ ELSS TAX SAVER FUND'.

## Key Points

- Affected Fund: NJ Mutual Fund
- Existing Scheme Name: NJ ELSS TAX SAVER SCHEME
- Revised Scheme Name: NJ ELSS TAX SAVER FUND
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform
- Effective Date: Immediate (Notice Date: August 27, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational scheme nomenclature update by the Asset Management Company (AMC).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors should take note of the revised scheme name for transactions and reporting on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date: With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating scheme descriptions and display names on trading terminals and portals.
