## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that specified debt securities, which were previously suspended due to maturity or record dates fixed for redemption, will be formally delisted effective from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260821-16
- Effective Date of Delisting: August 24, 2026
- Scope: Debt securities suspended due to maturity and/or redemption record dates.
- Attached File: Annexure 21082026 detailing the specific securities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal removal of matured/redeemed debt securities from the exchange listing records.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to take note of the delisted securities to prevent further trading in the affected instruments.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 21, 2026
- Effective Delisting Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact as these securities were already suspended due to prior maturity or redemption.
