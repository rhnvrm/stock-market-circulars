## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Esaar (India) Ltd. issued on a rights basis, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Esaar (India) Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 531502
- ISIN: INE404L01039
- Security Details: 5,99,64,667 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at par on a rights basis.
- Distribution Numbers: 20442501 to 80407167
- Date of Allotment: 15/09/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: September 18, 2026
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure adherence to standard listing and settlement norms.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 15/09/2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: 18/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of the company's paid-up equity share capital base through the listing of rights shares.
