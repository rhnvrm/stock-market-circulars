## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Non-Competitive Bidding for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for September 22, 2026, conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). Trading members can participate via the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Participation is available through the NCB-GSec module of the iBBS web-based platform (https://ibbs.bseindia.com).
- Covers multiple State Government Securities (SDL) re-issues and new issues from states including AP, GA, GJ, JK, MH, PB, and RJ.
- Bid collection start date: September 21, 2026, from 10:00 AM onwards (24-hour availability).
- Direct Investor bid collection end date: September 21, 2026, till 5:00 PM.
- Trading Members bid collection end date: September 22, 2026, till 8:00 AM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Facilitates non-competitive bidding in State Government Securities auctions as per RBI press release and exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure bids are submitted within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module.
- Pay-in and settlement obligations must be met through ICCL operations.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: September 21, 2026, 10:00 AM
- Direct Investor Bid Collection End Date: September 21, 2026, 5:00 PM
- Members Bid Collection End Date: September 22, 2026, 8:00 AM
- Auction Date: September 22, 2026
- Settlement Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables eligible investors and trading members to participate in the primary auction of State Government Securities efficiently through the electronic bidding platform.
