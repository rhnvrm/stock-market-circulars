## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction with respect to Rolling Settlement No. DR-730/2026-27, scheduled for October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 8, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on October 8, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Friday, Oct 9, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.
- Settlement is executed through the Clearing Member of a Trading Member following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians are requested to check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Trading members must exercise utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 8, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: October 9, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational notice ensuring smooth clearing and settlement of short-delivered securities through the demat auction mechanism.
