## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has issued the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for ECS Biztech Limited, scheduled to be conducted from September 17, 2026, to September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Takeover
- Settlement No.: 2026114
- Bid Start Date: 17/09/2026
- Bid End Date: 30/09/2026
- Obligation Download Date: 08/10/2026
- Settlement Date: 09/10/2026
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants must ensure that the effect of early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/BSECL systems prior to the prescribed timelines for bidding and confirmation. Market type "(Takeover)" and settlement number 2026114 must be used in the depository system.

## Important Dates

- Bid Period: 17/09/2026 to 30/09/2026
- Obligation Download: 08/10/2026
- Settlement Date: 09/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates smooth execution and settlement of shares tendered under the takeover acquisition window for ECS Biztech Limited.
