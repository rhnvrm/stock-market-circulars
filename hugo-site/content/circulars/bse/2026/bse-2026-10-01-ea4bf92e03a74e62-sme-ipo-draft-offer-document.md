## Summary

Ace Furnaces Limited has submitted its Draft Offer Document for an SME Initial Public Offering (IPO) to BSE on September 30, 2026. The document has been made available on the SME website for public review and comments for a period of at least 21 days as per ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ace Furnaces Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Hosting Platform: BSE SME website (bsesme.com)
- Public Comment Window: 21 days from the date of the notice

## Regulatory Changes

None. The filing complies with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public can submit comments on the Draft Offer Document within 21 days via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Draft Document Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from notice date

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites public participation and feedback on the proposed SME IPO of Ace Furnaces Limited before final issuance.
