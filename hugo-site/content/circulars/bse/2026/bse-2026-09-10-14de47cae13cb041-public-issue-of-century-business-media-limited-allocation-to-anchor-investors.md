## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that 6,54,400 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors portion in the public issue of Century Business Media Limited at an issue price of Rs. 74/- per equity share on September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Century Business Media Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue / IPO
- Portion: Anchor Investors (AIs)
- Number of Shares Subscribed: 6,54,400 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 74/- per equity share
- Date of Announcement: September 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are to note the anchor investor allocation details for trading and settlement reference.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 10, 2026
- Announcement Date: September 10, 2026

Provides transparency regarding institutional participation and anchor investor allotment in the public issue of Century Business Media Limited.
