## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units of JioBlackRock Nifty 50 ETF under Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund, effective from Thursday, August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Mutual Fund: Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund
- Name of the Scheme: JioBlackRock Nifty 50 ETF (Scrip Code: 544873, Scrip ID: NIFTYJBLK, ISIN: INF22M001184)
- Group: B, Market Lot: 1
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00 per unit, Issue Price: Rs. 12.50 per unit
- Date of Allotment: 14/08/2026
- Total No. of Units: 62,902,315 Units
- Registrar and Transfer Agent: Computer Age Management Services Limited (CAMS)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that units of the mutual fund will only be traded in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with a new exchange-traded fund option tracking the Nifty 50 Index on the BSE platform.
