## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that the Edelweiss Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and related facilities (SIP, STP, and SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Reopening: Edelweiss Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund reopens for ongoing transactions effective August 28, 2026.
- Available Facilities: Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP (Systematic Investment Plan), STP (Systematic Transfer Plan), and SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan).
- Affected Plans: Regular and Direct plans including Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- ISIN Details:
  - Regular Plan - Growth: INF754K01XN7
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout: INF754K01XO5
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF754K01XP2
  - Direct Plan - Growth: INF754K01XJ5
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout: INF754K01XK3
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF754K01XL1

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the scheme on the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with existing exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to resume transactions and systematic investment facilities in the Edelweiss Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
