## Summary

In continuation to notice no. 20260921-45, BSE has informed trading members that the ongoing Rights Issue of Century Extrusions Limited has been extended up to Wednesday, October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Ongoing rights issue of Century Extrusions Limited extended.
- New closing date: Wednesday, October 21, 2026 (previously October 14, 2026).
- Issued in continuation of notice no. 20260921-45.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the revised timeline for participation and application submissions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 09, 2026
- Extended Rights Issue Closing Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides eligible shareholders additional time to subscribe to the rights issue of Century Extrusions Limited.
