## Summary

BSE Ltd has announced the introduction of futures and options (F&O) contracts on three individual securities in the Equity Derivatives segment, effective from October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Segment:** Equity Derivatives (Trading Operations)
- **Underlying Securities:**
  1. Anand Rathi Wealth Ltd (Scrip Code: 543415)
  2. Siemens Energy India Ltd (Scrip Code: 544390)
  3. Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Ltd (Scrip Code: 542904)
- **Contract Details:** Available in the derivatives contract master file generated at the end of day on September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Inclusion of the specified securities into the Equity Derivatives segment for trading in futures and options contracts.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to note the addition of these securities and ensure systems are updated with the derivatives contract master file available through the extranet and BSEIndia website.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Contract Master Availability:** September 30, 2026 (End of Day)
- **Effective Trading Date:** October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the derivatives product offerings on BSE, providing market participants with hedging and trading opportunities on Anand Rathi Wealth Ltd, Siemens Energy India Ltd, and Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Ltd.
