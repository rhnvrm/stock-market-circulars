## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that a record date has been set for Quint Digital Limited's partly paid-up warrants to determine eligible holders for the First and Final Call Money payment of Rs. 5/- per warrant.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Quint Digital Limited
- Scrip Code: 961921
- Record Date / No Dealings From Date: 15/10/2026
- Purpose: To ascertain holders of partly paid warrants eligible to pay the First and Final Call of Rs. 5/- per warrant.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary restriction on dealings in partly paid warrants of the company on the specified record date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members of the Exchange are required to take note and refrain from dealing in the partly paid warrants of the company from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date and No Dealings From Date: 15/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures orderly identification of warrant holders for call money collection and subsequent conversion processes.
