## Summary

BSE has issued the trading and settlement schedule for the Institutional Trading Platform (ITP) under the SME segment for the period starting from September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026, outlined under Notice No. 20260811-13.

## Key Points

- Covers trading and settlement cycles for the SME segment Institutional Trading Platform (ITP).
- Applicable period: September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Issued by BSECL (BSE Clearing Corporation Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must adhere to the specified trading and settlement calendar for transactions executed on the ITP SME segment during September 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Start Date: September 1, 2026
- Effective End Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth operational planning and settlement cycle adherence for members trading in the ITP SME segment.
