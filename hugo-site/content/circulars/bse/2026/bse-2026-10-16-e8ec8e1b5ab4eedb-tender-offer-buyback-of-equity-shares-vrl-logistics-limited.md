## Summary

VRL Logistics Limited has informed the Exchange that it has fixed October 22, 2026, as the Record Date for determining the entitlement and names of equity shareholders eligible to participate in the upcoming Buyback Offer.

## Key Points

- Company Name: VRL Logistics Limited (BSE Scrip Code: 539118)
- Purpose: Determination of shareholder entitlement and eligibility for participation in the Buyback Offer
- Record Date: October 22, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: October 22, 2026
- Segment: Equity / Dematerialised Securities – Rolling Settlement Segment

## Regulatory Changes

Corporate action announced pursuant to SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations and listing obligations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible shareholders must take note of the record date and ex-entitlement date for executing transactions on the rolling settlement segment.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 22, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts shareholders holding equity shares of VRL Logistics Limited by establishing the exact cutoff date for buyback participation entitlement.
