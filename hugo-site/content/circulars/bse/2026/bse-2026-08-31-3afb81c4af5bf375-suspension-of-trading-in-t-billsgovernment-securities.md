## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific Treasury Bills (T-Bills) and Government Securities due to their maturity effective from September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three securities are maturing, including 182TB30926, 364TB30926, and 91TB30926.
- Trading members are instructed not to deal in these securities starting from September 01, 2026.
- Notice reference: DR- 706/2026-2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified T-Bills and Government Securities with effect from September 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 31, 2026
- **Effective Date of Suspension:** September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification ensuring market participants do not trade in matured government instruments.
