## Summary

SEBI has issued its final order no. WTM/AS/CFD/CFD-SEC-4/32528/2026-27 dated July 30, 2026, regarding Kalahridhaan Trendz Ltd., restraining specific noticees from the securities market for varying durations.

## Key Points

- Noticees Nos. 1 and 2 are restrained and prohibited from accessing the securities market, buying, selling, or dealing in securities directly or indirectly for 2 years.
- Noticees Nos. 3 and 4 are restrained and prohibited from accessing the securities market, buying, selling, or dealing in securities directly or indirectly for 1 year.
- Open derivative positions may be closed out/squared off within 3 months or upon expiry, whichever is earlier. Pre-existing transactions can settle pay-in/pay-out obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement directions issued by SEBI under its statutory powers against specified noticees.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the order and ensure that the restrained noticees do not trade or transact in violation of the directions.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: July 30, 2026
- Effective Date: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and enforces market integrity by penalizing non-compliant entities associated with the company.
