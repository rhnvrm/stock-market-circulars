## Summary

Trading members are informed that units of HDFC Nifty Metal ETF are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange effective July 31, 2026, under the 'B' Group.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: HDFC Nifty Metal ETF
- Mutual Fund: HDFC Mutual Fund
- Scrip Code: 544842
- ISIN: INF179KC1KT4
- Date of Allotment: 27/07/2026
- Face Value / Issue Price: Rs. 124.88 per unit
- Market Lot: 1 unit (traded in dematerialized form only)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialized form in unit market lots.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 27/07/2026
- Listing / Effective Date: 31/07/2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to trade units of the HDFC Nifty Metal ETF on the exchange platform.
