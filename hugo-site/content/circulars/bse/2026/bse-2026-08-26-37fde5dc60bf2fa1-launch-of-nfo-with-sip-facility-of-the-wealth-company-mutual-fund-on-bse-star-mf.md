## Summary

The Wealth Company Mutual Fund is launching The Wealth Company Multi Cap Fund NFO along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform from August 27, 2026, to September 10, 2026. The tentative allotment date is September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund Name: The Wealth Company Multi Cap Fund (Regular and Direct plans for Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- NFO Window: August 27, 2026, to September 10, 2026 (extended till midnight on September 10, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 17, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on September 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag shall be reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on September 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 27, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 10, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform participants and investors access to a new multi-cap mutual fund scheme with standard operational timelines.
