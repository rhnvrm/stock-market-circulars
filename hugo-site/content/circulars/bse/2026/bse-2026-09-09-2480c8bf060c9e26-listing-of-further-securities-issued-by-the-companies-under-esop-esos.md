## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued by 14 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange, effective from Thursday, September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 14 listed entities.
- Nature of issuance: Further securities issued under ESOP / ESOS.
- Effective trading date: September 10, 2026.
- Companies include prominent firms such as ICICI Bank Limited, Mankind Pharma Limited, Computer Age Management Services Limited (CAMS), and Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are requested to take note of the listing of these securities for their records and operational updates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 9, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard operational listing notice reflecting the regular expansion of equity capital due to stock option exercises by employees across various listed companies.
