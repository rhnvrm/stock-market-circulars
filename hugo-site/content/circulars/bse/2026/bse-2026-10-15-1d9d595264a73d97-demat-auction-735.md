## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 735 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-735/2026-27, detailing auction offer entry, matching, delivery statement, and money statement timings for October 15, 2026, with pay-in scheduled for October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement Number: DR-735/2026-27.
- Auction Offer Entry Timing: October 15, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction Matching Timing: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and Report: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Auction Pay-in: Friday, October 16, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine operational auction notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians are requested to check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Trading members must exercise utmost care while punching securities code and rate as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date / Offer Entry: October 15, 2026
- Auction Pay-in: October 16, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and localized impact limited to trading members participating in Rolling Settlement No. DR-735/2026-27.
