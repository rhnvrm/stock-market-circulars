## Summary

CUBE Highway Trust ("CUBE InvIT") has been converted from a Privately Placed Infrastructure Investment Trust (Private InvIT) to a Public Infrastructure Investment Trust (Public InvIT) effective July 31, 2026. Consequently, it ceases to be governed by the regulatory framework applicable to privately placed InvITs and will comply with all provisions applicable to publicly offered and listed InvITs.

## Key Points

- **Name of InvIT:** Cube Highways Trust
- **Symbol:** CUBEINVIT
- **Scrip Code:** 543899
- **ISIN:** INE0NR623014
- **Number of Units Admitted:** 1,344,069,762
- **Trading Lot:** 1 (one) unit
- **Effective Date of Conversion:** July 31, 2026
- **Pre-open Session:** Part of Call Auction in Pre-open Session on July 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The trust is now subject to the SEBI (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014, the SEBI Master Circular for Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs), and framework guidelines regarding the conversion of private listed InvITs into public InvITs.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the trust must adhere to the regulatory requirements specified for publicly listed InvITs, including dematerialised form trading and lock-in/unitholding pattern guidelines as per Annexures I and II.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Effective Date of Conversion:** July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Transitioning to a Public InvIT broadens compliance scope, enhances market visibility, and shifts regulatory oversight to standard public infrastructure investment trust norms.
