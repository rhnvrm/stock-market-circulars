## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding compliance with Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) directions and guidelines communicated via SEBI. Trading Members are required to adhere to specific operational directives regarding mobile number revocation lists, service call numbering series, SMS template pre-tagging, and consumer awareness.

## Key Points

- Integration with the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL) for periodic reconciliation and risk workflows.
- Strict use of the 1600 numbering series exclusively for service and transactional calls, not promotional calls.
- Compliance with TRAI's direction on pre-tagging variable fields in SMS content templates before blocker mode activation.
- Execution of consumer awareness campaigns regarding the 1600 and 140 numbering series.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of mandatory pre-tagging of variable fields in SMS content templates with strict validation.
- Blocker mode activation where SMS messages failing variable-field validation will be blocked from delivery.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must integrate with DoT's MNRL and build checks into risk, authentication, and communication workflows.
- Review all SMS content templates used in the preceding 90 days to identify, pre-tag variable fields, and rectify logger-mode scrubbing deficiencies.
- Undertake consumer awareness campaigns for 1600 and 140 numbering series.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 16 September 2026
- Reference TRAI Direction Date: 18 November 2025

## Impact Assessment

Operational workflows involving customer communications, SMS notifications, and risk checks must be updated to incorporate MNRL validations and SMS template compliance to prevent delivery failures.
