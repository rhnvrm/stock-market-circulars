## Summary

HDFC Mutual Fund is offering the HDFC Gold Silver Passive FoF NFO from August 24, 2026, to September 07, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The tentative allotment date is September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: HDFC Gold Silver Passive FoF (Regular Plan - Growth & Direct Plan - Growth).
- NFO Window: August 24, 2026, to September 07, 2026 (extended till midnight for Lumpsum purchases).
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on September 07, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 10, 2026.
- ISINs: INF179KC1KN7 (Regular), INF179KC1KO5 (Direct).

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag shall be reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 10, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: August 24, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: September 07, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables members and investors to subscribe to the new fund offer of HDFC Mutual Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform with standard NFO operational guidelines.
