## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding companies with high Promoter as well as non-Promoter 'Encumbrance' pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities included in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin, while securities in Annexure II are moving out of the framework effective September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Securities listed in Annexure II are eligible to move out of the framework.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Measures apply in conjunction with other prevailing exchange measures and are subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of minimum 75% margin on companies identified with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance under SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the margin requirements on open positions and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for inclusion/margins: September 03, 2026 (on all open positions as on September 02, 2026, and new positions created thereafter)
- Effective Date for moving out: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (minimum 75%) on specified securities will impact trading liquidity and leverage for market participants holding positions in these stocks.
