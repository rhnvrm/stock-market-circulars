## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the introduction of shorter tenure Securities Lending & Borrowing (SLB) contracts effective from Transaction Day, August 17, 2026. These contracts will be introduced under the “D” series prefix (e.g., SBIN20AUG26D-MCF) and generated daily.

## Key Points

- Shorter tenure SLB contracts will be available starting August 17, 2026.
- Contracts will be issued under the "D" series prefix and generated daily.
- First leg settles on T+1; corresponding reverse leg settles on T+3 (excluding settlement holidays).
- Contracts are exempt from foreclosure during AGMs/EGMs.
- Available exclusively for stocks eligible for trading in the Equity Derivatives Segment.
- Repay, Recall, and Rollover facilities are not applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of daily-generated shorter tenure SLB contracts with T+1 and T+3 settlement configuration under series prefix 'D'.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants and custodians must make necessary operational arrangements to support the new 'D' series shorter tenure SLB contracts.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances flexibility and liquidity options for market participants engaging in securities lending and borrowing for derivatives-eligible stocks.
