## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margins, lower price bands, and transfer to specific trading groups (T, XT, MT, TS) effective from October 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. October 09, 2026, on all open positions as on October 08, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities under Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM and subject to Lower Price Band w.e.f. October 07, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Transfer of certain securities in LT-ASM to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. October 09, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- Securities moving out of LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. October 07, 2026, with price bands reinstated to previous levels (Annexure II).
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions by listed companies, and is not an adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates to the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework as per previous circulars dated March 21, 2018, October 27, 2018, December 04, 2020, August 09, 2024, and September 20, 2024.
- Imposition of stringent margin requirements and price band restrictions on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements for open and new positions on affected securities.
- Trading members should review the detailed lists in Annexures I, II, and III for specific scrip-wise applicability.

## Important Dates

- **October 07, 2026**: Effective date for movement to higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price bands, and moving out of LT-ASM framework.
- **October 08, 2026**: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margin.
- **October 09, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Groups.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and lower price bands for shortlisted scrips will restrict speculative trading and enhance market integrity.
- Investors holding securities in the affected lists may experience reduced liquidity and increased volatility.
