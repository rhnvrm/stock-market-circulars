## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the implementation and adjustments under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Following previous notices, specific securities meeting the established criteria will be placed under the ESM framework, shifted to lower stages (Stage I), or moved out of the framework entirely. These changes entail higher margin requirements, transition to the Trade-for-Trade segment, or periodic call auction trading based on the respective stage, with changes taking effect on August 13 and August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **ESM Inclusion:** Securities satisfying the ESM criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin with effect from August 14, 2026, on all open positions as of August 13, 2026, and new positions created from August 14, 2026.
- **Stage Downward Revision:** Securities continuing in the ESM framework but moving to the lower Stage I with effect from August 13, 2026, are detailed in Annexure I.
- **Segment Shifting:** Securities qualifying under ESM will shift from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment starting August 14, 2026.
- **Stage II Action:** Securities shifting to Stage II will be placed under Trade-for-Trade settlement with a price band of 2% and subject to Periodic Call Auctions with effect from August 13, 2026.
- **Framework Exclusions:** Securities moving out of the ESM framework starting August 13, 2026, are detailed in Annexure II. Their price bands will be reinstated to pre-ESM levels, provided they are not under other surveillance measures.
- **Consolidated Tracking:** Annexure III provides a consolidated list of all securities currently under the ESM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Re-classification of specific securities under different stages of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Capping of price bands to 2% and implementation of Periodic Call Auctions for Stage II ESM securities.
- Shift of newly qualified ESM securities from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Margin Operations:** Members must collect a minimum 100% margin on all open positions from August 13, 2026, and new positions from August 14, 2026, for newly included ESM securities.
- **Trading Terminal Adjustments:** Trading systems and risk management desks must be updated to restrict affected securities to Trade-for-Trade settlement and accommodate Periodic Call Auctions for Stage II scrips.
- **Price Limit Monitoring:** Risk departments must adjust price bands to 2% for Stage II securities and reinstate original price bands for securities moving out of ESM.

## Important Dates

- **August 13, 2026:**
  - Movement of securities to lower Stage (Stage I) under ESM.
  - Movement of securities to Stage II (Trade-for-Trade, 2% price band, Periodic Call Auction).
  - Exclusion of securities from the ESM framework and reinstatement of their pre-ESM price bands.
- **August 14, 2026:**
  - Shift of newly qualified ESM securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
  - Application of minimum 100% margin on open/new positions.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Shortlisted securities will likely face reduced liquidity and trading volumes due to the Trade-for-Trade segment restrictions, the 100% margin mandate, and the narrow 2% price band for Stage II scrips.
- **Operational Impact:** Brokers and trading participants must seamlessly update their risk parameters, margin modules, and trading systems to reflect the changes to avoid compliance violations or settlement failures.
