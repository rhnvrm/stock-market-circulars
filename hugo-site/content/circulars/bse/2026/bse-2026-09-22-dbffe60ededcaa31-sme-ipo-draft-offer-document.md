## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Laxmi Security (Gujarat) Limited for its SME IPO. The document has been made available on the stock exchange website for public comments for at least 21 days as per ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Laxmi Security (Gujarat) Limited
- Filing Date: September 18, 2026
- Regulation: Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations
- Public Comment Period: At least 21 days from the date of the notice

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments on the draft offer document can be submitted via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Draft Document Filing Date: September 18, 2026
- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Comment Deadline: Within 21 days from the notice date

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites public feedback on the proposed SME IPO of Laxmi Security (Gujarat) Limited.
