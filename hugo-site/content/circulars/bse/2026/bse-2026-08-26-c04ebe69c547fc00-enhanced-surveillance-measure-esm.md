## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice detailing updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin effective August 28, 2026, and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment. Additionally, certain securities are moving to lower stages or exiting the framework entirely.

## Key Points

- Minimum 100% margin levied on all open positions as of August 27, 2026, and new positions created from August 28, 2026, for securities satisfying ESM criteria (Annexure I).
- Securities shifting to Stage II face Trade-for-Trade settlement with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective August 27, 2026.
- Certain securities will move to lower Stage I under the ESM framework (Annexure I).
- List of securities moving out of the ESM framework provided in Annexure II, with price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shortlisted securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment effective August 28, 2026.
- Stage II shifting securities are subject to Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective August 27, 2026.
- ESM framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the inclusion, staging changes, and margin requirements for affected securities.
- Ensure appropriate margin maintenance (100%) for open and new positions on applicable scrips.
- For clarifications, members may contact bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Stage II Changes / Moving Out / Lower Stage:** August 27, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margin / Trade-for-Trade Shifting:** August 28, 2026
- **Open Position Cut-off Date for Margins:** August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement mechanisms for scrips listed under the ESM framework will be restricted due to 100% margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade settlement, reducing speculative activity and increasing holding costs for market participants.
