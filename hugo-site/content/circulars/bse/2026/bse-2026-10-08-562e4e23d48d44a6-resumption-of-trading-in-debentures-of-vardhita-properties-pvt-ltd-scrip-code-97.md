## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of trading in the debentures of Vardhita Properties Pvt Ltd (Scrip Code 976433) with effect from October 09, 2026. The company clarified that the earlier proposed redemption was contingent upon sufficient distributable surplus, which was not confirmed; hence, no redemption was undertaken and the principal amount continues to remain outstanding.

## Key Points

- Trading in debentures of Vardhita Properties Pvt Ltd (Scrip Code 976433) resumes from October 09, 2026.
- The company clarified that no redemption of debentures has been undertaken or effected.
- The principal amount of the debentures continues to remain outstanding.
- This notice is in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260911-6 dated September 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational notice regarding the resumption of trading following company clarification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to note the resumption of trading in the debentures.

## Important Dates

- **September 11, 2026**: Initial notice regarding record date for redemption (Notice No. 20260911-6).
- **October 08, 2026**: Notice issued announcing resumption of trading.
- **October 09, 2026**: Effective date for resumption of trading in debentures (DR-732/2026-2027).

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact limited to debenture holders of Vardhita Properties Pvt Ltd, restoring liquidity as debentures continue to remain outstanding.
