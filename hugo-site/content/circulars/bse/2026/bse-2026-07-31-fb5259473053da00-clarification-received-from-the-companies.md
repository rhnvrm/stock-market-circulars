## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movements in certain scrips and sought clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to relevant information.

## Key Points

- The Exchange requested clarifications from multiple companies due to significant price/volume movements.
- Replies have been received and attached for several companies, including AIK Pipes and Polymers Ltd, Atul Auto Ltd, Continental Controls Ltd, GSP Crop Science Ltd, and Kinetic Trust Ltd.
- Replies are currently awaited from other companies such as Amit Securities Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to respond to Exchange inquiries regarding significant price and volume movements in a timely manner.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 July 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures market transparency by providing investors with clarification regarding unusual stock price or volume behavior.
