## Summary

Members are informed that Zerodha Mutual Fund will offer the Zerodha Arbitrage Fund - Direct Plan - Growth NFO along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform from August 12, 2026, to August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Zerodha Arbitrage Fund - Direct Plan - Growth
- Scheme Category: Equity
- ISIN: INF0R8F01232
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 18, 2026
- NFO Period: August 12, 2026, to August 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (August 14, 2026).
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 18, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 12, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: August 14, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to participate in the new fund offer and SIP registration for Zerodha Arbitrage Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
