## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of their Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260910-12, Dated: 10 Sep 2026.
- Segment: Debt Segment, Department: Listing Operations.
- Affected companies include Sammaan Capital Limited, IOT Utkal Energy Services Limited, Piramal Finance Limited, Fedbank Financial Services Limited, and JM Financial Home Loans Limited.
- Record Date and Ex-Date for all listed ISINs is set for 11/09/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in the specified debentures will continue with a reduced face value reflecting the part redemption amount.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to note the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for proper settlement and execution of trades in the debt segment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10/09/2026
- Record Date: 11/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 11/09/2026
- Settlement DR: 714/2026-2027

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding the affected NCDs will receive part redemption payments and interest according to the revised reduced face values starting from the record date.
