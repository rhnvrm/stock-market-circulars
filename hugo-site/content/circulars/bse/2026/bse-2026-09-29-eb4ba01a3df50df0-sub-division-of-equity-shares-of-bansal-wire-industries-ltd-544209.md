## Summary

Bansal Wire Industries Ltd has announced the sub-division of its equity shares, changing the face value from Rs. 5/- per share into five equity shares of Re. 1/- each. The company has established October 16, 2026, as the record date for this corporate action.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Bansal Wire Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 544209)
- **Corporate Action:** Sub-division of existing equity shares from Rs. 5/- each into five equity shares of Re. 1/- each.
- **Record Date:** October 16, 2026.
- **Old ISIN:** INE0B9K01025 (invalid for transactions on or after October 16, 2026).
- **New ISIN:** To be announced separately by the exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

- Face value adjustment of equity shares from Rs. 5/- to Re. 1/- resulting in a 5-for-1 stock split.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are informed that old ISIN INE0B9K01025 will not be valid for transactions executed on or after October 16, 2026.
- A subsequent exchange notice will provide the new ISIN for the Re. 1/- paid-up shares.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** October 16, 2026
- **Effective Date / W.E.F.:** October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Existing shareholders will hold five shares of Re. 1/- face value for every single share of Rs. 5/- face value held as of the record date.
- Market participants must update systems for the new ISIN once notified.
