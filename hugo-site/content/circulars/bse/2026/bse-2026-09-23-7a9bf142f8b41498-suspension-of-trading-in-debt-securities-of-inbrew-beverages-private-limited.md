## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities of Inbrew Beverages Private Limited due to their upcoming maturity.

## Key Points

- Trading in specified debt securities of Inbrew Beverages Private Limited is suspended due to maturity.
- Affected Scrip Codes: 974276 and 976716.
- Effective date of suspension: September 24, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debt securities with effect from September 24, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: September 24, 2026
- Maturity Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational restriction on trading of specific matured debt instruments of the issuer.
