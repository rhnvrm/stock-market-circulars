## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, stage revision, group transfer, and removal of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, effective September 18, 2026, and September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins become applicable effective September 22, 2026, on open positions as of September 21, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM or having lower price bands applied effective September 18, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS groups effective September 22, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- Securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective September 18, 2026, are listed in Annexure II.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is a market surveillance measure, not an adverse regulatory action.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions including 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and group transfers under the revised LT-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance and adherence to trading restrictions for securities specified in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Bands, Higher Stages, and Removal: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and required capital for open positions in the affected scrips will be impacted due to 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions.
