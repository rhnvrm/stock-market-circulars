## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited (Scrip Code - 543273) effective from September 9, 2026. A total of 7,64,317 equity shares were issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Limited
- Scrip Code: 543273
- ISIN: INE0CJD01029
- Number of Shares: 7,64,317 equity shares of Rs. 5/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 1962.53/- per share (including premium of Rs. 1957.53/-)
- Effective Date of Trading: September 9, 2026
- Lock-in Details: 7,64,317 shares locked-in up to 20/03/2027
