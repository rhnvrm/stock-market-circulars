## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-716/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on September 17, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and accepted offers report: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction is scheduled for Friday, Sep 18, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities has been removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching security codes and rates as no corrections will be permitted.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 17, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: September 18, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine settlement process impacting clearing and trading members participating in Rolling Settlement No. DR-716/2026-27.
