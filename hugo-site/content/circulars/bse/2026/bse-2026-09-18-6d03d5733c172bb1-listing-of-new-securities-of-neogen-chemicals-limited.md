## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and admission for trading of 26,60,753 equity shares of Neogen Chemicals Limited with effect from Monday, September 21, 2026. These shares were allotted to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) through a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Neogen Chemicals Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 542665
- **ISIN:** INE136S01016
- **Security Details:** 26,60,753 Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted to QIBs pursuant to QIP.
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Date of Allotment:** September 16, 2026
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 2255/- per share
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 27381675 To 30042427

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the listing of new securities for trading.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 16, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Neogen Chemicals Limited following successful QIP allotment to QIBs, with trading commencement scheduled for September 21, 2026.
