## Summary

BSE, in coordination with other exchanges, has reviewed the securities under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework and announced the lower revision of stages for eligible securities effective October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Review of GSM stages carried out in coordination with other exchanges.
- Eligible securities will move to lower stages of the GSM framework.
- Effective date for the revision is October 12, 2026.
- Reference made to earlier framework notices dated February 23, 2017, and November 29, 2019.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic review and recalibration of surveillance stages under the GSM framework to ease trading restrictions on eligible securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must verify the list of securities moving to lower GSM stages as provided in the annexure and ensure compliance with applicable trading parameters.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 9, 2026
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Securities transitioning to lower surveillance stages experience relaxed trading restrictions, which may improve market liquidity and normal trading activity.
