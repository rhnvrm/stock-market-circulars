## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of two newly listed companies to its indices. Purple Style Labs Limited will be added to the BSE IPO index effective September 09, 2026, and Phychem Technologies Limited will be added to the BSE SME index effective September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Purple Style Labs Limited (Ticker: 544901):** Listed on BSE effective September 07, 2026; added to the **BSE IPO** index effective open of **September 09, 2026**.
- **Phychem Technologies Limited (Ticker: 544902):** Listed on the SME platform of BSE effective September 07, 2026; added to the **BSE SME** index effective open of **September 08, 2026**.
- Actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine index composition update.

## Compliance Requirements

No specific compliance actions required for market participants other than incorporating the index additions into systems tracking the respective indices.

## Important Dates

- **September 07, 2026:** Notice issuance date and listing date for both companies.
- **September 08, 2026:** Effective date for Phychem Technologies Limited addition to BSE SME index.
- **September 09, 2026:** Effective date for Purple Style Labs Limited addition to BSE IPO index.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact, affecting only index trackers and benchmark portfolios holding these newly listed securities.
