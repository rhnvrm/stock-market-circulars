## Summary

An open offer is made by the acquirers (Mr. Manoj Agrawal and Ms. Komal Amit Kumar Saraogi) along with PACs to acquire up to 3,77,44,200 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 26% of the total issued voting share capital) of Cubical Financial Services Limited at an offer price of Rs. 2.50 per share along with applicable interest of up to Rs. 0.021 per equity share.

## Key Points

- Target Company: Cubical Financial Services Limited (CUBFIN).
- Acquirers: Mr. Manoj Agrawal and Komal Amit Kumar Saraogi, along with PACs (Mrs. Shikha Agrawal, M/s Manoj Agrawal HUF, Mrs. Kanchan Saraogi).
- Offer Price: Rs. 2.50 per fully paid-up equity share plus applicable interest of up to Rs. 0.021 per equity share.
- Offer Size: Up to 3,77,44,200 equity shares (26% of total issued equity and voting share capital).
- Offer Period: From September 17, 2026 (Thursday) to September 30, 2026 (Wednesday).
- Registrar to the Offer: Beetal Financial & Computer Services Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is conducted pursuant to Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, and SEBI circulars governing the mechanism for acquisition of shares through the stock exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the tender offer guidelines and participate through the acquisition window on the stock exchange during the specified dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 16, 2026
- Open Offer Opening Date: September 17, 2026
- Open Offer Closing Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Public shareholders of Cubical Financial Services Limited can tender their shares through the acquisition window during the offer period to exit or partially divest their holdings at the specified offer price.
