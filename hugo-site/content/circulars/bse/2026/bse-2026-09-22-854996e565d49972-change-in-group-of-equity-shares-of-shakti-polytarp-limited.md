## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Shakti Polytarp Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective from Wednesday, October 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shakti Polytarp Limited
- Scrip Code: 544933
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Wednesday, October 7, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The trading segment for the company's equity shares is being shifted in continuation of the previous exchange notice dated September 21, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the segment shift and conduct transactions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the company's shares will move from the Trade for Trade restriction to the regular Rolling segment, allowing normal rolling settlement.
