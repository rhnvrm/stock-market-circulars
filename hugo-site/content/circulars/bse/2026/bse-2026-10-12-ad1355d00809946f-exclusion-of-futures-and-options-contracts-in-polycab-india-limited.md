## Summary

BSE has announced the exclusion of futures and options (F&O) contracts in Polycab India Limited (Scrip Code: 542652) following its shortlisting under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework. New expiry month contracts will not be issued, existing contracts will phase out by December 2026, and a 100% margin is levied on all derivative positions.

## Key Points

- Polycab India Limited has been shortlisted under the IBC framework.
- No new F&O contracts for new expiry months will be issued upon the expiration of October 2026 contracts.
- Existing contracts with October, November, and December 2026 expiries will trade until their respective expiry dates.
- No F&O contracts will be available for trading from January 01, 2027 onwards.
- A 100% margin is levied on all existing derivative contracts on a T+2-day basis.

## Regulatory Changes

Futures and options trading in Polycab India Limited is being systematically withdrawn pursuant to the company's inclusion under the IBC framework. Further surveillance actions such as price bands and gross settlement will apply from January 01, 2027.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note that no new F&O contracts will be introduced for Polycab India Limited.
- A 100% margin applies to all open positions as on October 13, 2026, and new positions created from October 14, 2026, onwards.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- October 12, 2026: Notice issuance date.
- October 13, 2026: Open positions baseline for 100% margin.
- October 14, 2026: New positions subject to 100% margin.
- October 2026 Expiry: Expiration of October contracts; no new expiry month contracts issued thereafter.
- January 01, 2027: Complete cessation of F&O trading in Polycab India Limited and initiation of further IBC surveillance actions.

## Impact Assessment

Derivatives traders and market participants holding positions in Polycab India Limited face liquidity wind-down and stringent 100% margin requirements leading up to the complete cessation of F&O contracts by January 2027.
