## Summary

Stock exchange BSE has announced that the SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and associated facilities such as SIP, STP, and SWP on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 04, 2026. The circular lists various regular and direct plans along with their respective ISINs and SIP investment parameters.

## Key Points

- SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund reopens for ongoing transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Applies to multiple plans including Regular and Direct options for growth and IDCW.
- Effective date for reopening is September 04, 2026.
- Specific SIP investment amount ranges and minimum installment requirements are established by SBI AMC.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors must take note of the scheme availability and updated SIP installment criteria on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 03, 2026
- Effective Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and market participants to resume transactions, systematic investment plans, and redemptions for the specified SBI mutual fund schemes on the BSE platform.
