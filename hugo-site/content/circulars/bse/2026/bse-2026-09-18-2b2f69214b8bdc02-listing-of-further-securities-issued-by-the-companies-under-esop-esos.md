## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 14 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Monday, September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 14
- Effective date for trading: September 21, 2026
- Securities category: Equity shares issued under ESOP/ESOS
- Notable companies include HDFC Bank Limited, Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Limited, Federal Bank Limited, and RBL Bank Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are requested to take note of the listing of further securities and ensure proper updating of records.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action with minimal direct market impact, representing normal equity capital expansion via employee stock options.
