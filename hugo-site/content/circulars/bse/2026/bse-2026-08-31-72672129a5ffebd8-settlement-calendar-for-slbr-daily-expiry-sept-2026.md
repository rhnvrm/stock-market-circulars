## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released the settlement calendar for the Securities Lending & Borrowing (SLB) Reverse Leg Segment for the period of September 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026, following the introduction of shorter tenure contracts.

## Key Points

- Covers trade dates from September 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Specifies Depository Settlement Numbers and corresponding SLBR Pay-in / Pay-out dates.
- Pay-in shall be conducted at 09:00 a.m. with member instructions required by 08:50 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulations; schedule follows existing SLB contract provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and participants must submit pay-in instructions to Depositories or banks latest by 08:50 a.m. on the respective pay-in dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Period: September 01, 2026 to September 30, 2026.
- First Trade Date: September 01, 2026 (Settlement No. 2627810, Pay-in/Pay-out: September 04, 2026).
- Last Trade Date: September 30, 2026 (Settlement No. 2627007, Pay-in/Pay-out: October 06, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational clearance and settlement timelines for market participants trading shorter tenure SLB contracts.
