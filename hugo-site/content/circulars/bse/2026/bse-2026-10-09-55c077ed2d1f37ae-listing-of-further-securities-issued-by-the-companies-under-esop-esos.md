## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that further new securities issued by five companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Monday, October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Ather Energy Limited**: 114,350 shares (ISIN: INE0LEZ01016, Scrip Code: 544397)
- **Emkay Global Financial Services Limited**: 145,449 shares (ISIN: INE296H01011, Scrip Code: 532737)
- **Kirloskar Ferrous Industries Limited**: 123,240 shares (ISIN: INE884B01025, Scrip Code: 500245)
- **Quick Heal Technologies Limited**: 2,431 shares (ISIN: INE306L01010, Scrip Code: 539678)
- **Safari Industries (India) Limited**: 1,130 shares (ISIN: INE429E01023, Scrip Code: 523025)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure compliance with standard depository and exchange guidelines regarding the listing and trading of ESOP shares.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: October 9, 2026
- **Effective Date**: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of equity capital due to employee stock option exercises with minimal immediate market impact.
