## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 691 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-691/2026-27, scheduled for Wednesday, August 12, 2026. The circular outlines the session timings for auction entry and matching, the pay-in schedule, and key procedural changes resulting from the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Key Points

- **Demat Auction 691**: Offers are invited from trading members for Rolling Settlement No. DR-691/2026-27.
- **Session Timings**:
  - Auction offer entry: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m.
  - Auction Matching: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.
  - Delivery Statement & reports: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
  - Money Statement & final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- **Auction Pay-In**: Thursday, August 13, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.
- **Rule Simplification**: Trading members are no longer restricted from offering securities in an auction for a settlement in which they have short delivered those securities, thanks to the Uniform Membership structure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Under the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment, settlement is executed through the Clearing Member of a Trading Member. 
- Consequently, the previous restriction that prohibited trading members from offering securities in an auction in the settlement in which they had short delivered such securities has been removed.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Obligation Verification**: Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and ensure execution of securities pay-in in depositories within the specified cut-off times.
- **Accuracy in Entry**: Members must exercise extreme caution when inputting security codes and rates, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- **Reference Screen**: Members are advised to consult the cutoff rate displayed on the "Auction offer Entry Screen" and keep abreast of relevant corporate actions (such as Ex-Rights, Ex-Bonus, and Ex-Split notices).

## Important Dates

- **August 12, 2026**: Auction offer entry and matching sessions.
- **August 13, 2026 (08:00 a.m.)**: Pay-in of the auction.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Low. This is a routine operational notice for settlement processes. However, the removal of the short-delivery restriction for auctions under the Uniform Membership structure streamlines operations and provides additional flexibility for clearing/trading members managing short deliveries.
