## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 9 scrips with effect from July 28, 2026. The price bands have been adjusted from their existing levels as part of surveillance and risk management measures.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260727-27
- Notice Date: 27 Jul 2026
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance)
- Effective Date: July 28, 2026
- Affected Scrips: Antariksh Industries Ltd, Dhyaani Tradeventtures Ltd, Kcp Sugar & Industries Corporation Ltd, Orient Press Ltd, Pearl Green Clubs And Resorts Ltd, Pioneer Investcorp Ltd, Ramco Systems Ltd, Spenta International Ltd, and Triveni Glass Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Price bands for the specified scrips have been revised to new percentage limits ranging from 2% to 10%:
- Antariksh Industries Ltd (501270): 2%
- Dhyaani Tradeventtures Ltd (543516): 2%
- Kcp Sugar & Industries Corporation Ltd (533192): 10%
- Orient Press Ltd (526325): 10%
- Pearl Green Clubs And Resorts Ltd (543540): 5%
- Pioneer Investcorp Ltd (507864): 5%
- Ramco Systems Ltd (532370): 5%
- Spenta International Ltd (526161): 10%
- Triveni Glass Ltd (502281): 10%

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure their trading systems reflect the updated limits to prevent order execution errors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 27, 2026
- Effective Date: July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members dealing in the affected scrips must adjust their risk parameters and trading strategies to accommodate the new price movement restrictions.
