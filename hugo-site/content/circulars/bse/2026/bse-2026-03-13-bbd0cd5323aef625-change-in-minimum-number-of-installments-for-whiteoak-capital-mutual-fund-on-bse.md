## Summary

WhiteOak Capital Asset Management Limited has issued an addendum to the Statement of Additional Information (SAI) announcing a revision to the minimum number of instalments under the Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) facility for WhiteOak Capital Mid Cap Fund, effective March 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applies to Fixed Systematic Transfer Plan (FSTP) and Flex Systematic Transfer Plan (Flex STP) under WhiteOak Capital Mid Cap Fund.
- The minimum number of instalments for daily frequency has been reduced from 60 to 15.
- Effective date: March 16, 2026.
- All other terms and conditions of the Scheme Information Document (SID) and SAI remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Mutual fund investors and distributors need to take note of the reduced minimum instalment requirements when setting up daily STP transactions for the specified scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** March 13, 2026
- **Effective Date:** March 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides greater flexibility to investors by lowering the minimum instalment commitment required to initiate a daily Systematic Transfer Plan.
