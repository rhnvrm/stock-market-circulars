## Summary

Samor Reality Ltd has announced a sub-division of its equity shares, changing the face value from Rs. 10/- each to Rs. 2/- each (yielding 5 equity shares for every 1 equity share held). The record date for this corporate action is October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Samor Reality Ltd (Scrip Code: 543376)
- Action: Sub-division of equity shares
- Ratio: 1 equity share of Rs. 10/- into 5 equity shares of Rs. 2/-
- Record Date: October 15, 2026
- Old ISIN: INE0GD201014 (invalid for transactions on/after October 15, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

- Stock split implementation under exchange listing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that the old ISIN (INE0GD201014) of Rs. 10/- paid up will not be valid for transactions done on the Exchange on or after October 15, 2026.
- New ISIN number for Rs. 2/- paid up will be communicated via a separate notice.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 15/10/2026
- Effective Date: 15/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders will receive 5 shares of Rs. 2/- for every 1 share of Rs. 10/- held as of the record date. Liquidity and price per share will adjust accordingly.
