## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that SEBI has confirmed the lifting of debarment with respect to Ms. Vilpa Vora in connection with the SAT order in the matter of front running by Prijesh Kurani and Others (Appeal No. 187 of 2026).

## Key Points

- In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260430-30 dated April 30, 2026.
- The Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) had stayed the impugned order pending disposal of the appeal via its order dated July 03, 2026.
- SEBI confirmed via email dated October 23, 2026, to lift the debarment against Ms. Vilpa Vora (PAN: AGJPV0381Q).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational notice communicating SEBI's confirmation regarding an individual debarment status.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the lifting of debarment for Ms. Vilpa Vora and ensure compliance accordingly.

## Important Dates

- October 23, 2026: SEBI confirmation and BSE notice date.
- July 03, 2026: SAT order date staying the impugned order.
- April 30, 2026: Date of previous exchange notice.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact, limited to the compliance status of the specific individual named in the notice.
