## Summary

SEBI has issued a final order (WTM/AS/CFID/CFID-SEC4/32684/2026-27 dated August 28, 2026) regarding Debock Industries Limited, imposing debarment and trading restrictions on several noticees for varying periods.

## Key Points

- Noticees no. 1 (Debock Industries Limited) and 2 (Mr. Mukesh Manveer Singh) are debarred from the securities market for 7 years from the date of the Interim Order (August 23, 2024).
- Noticee no. 3 (Mr. Sunil Kalot) is debarred for 5 years.
- Noticees no. 4 (Ms. Priyanka Sharma) and 27 (Mr. Gaurav Jain) are debarred for 3 years.
- Noticees no. 10 (Ms. Nishu Goyal), 11 (Ms. Vandana Patidar), 25 (Avance Ventures Private Limited), 26 (Impex Agrotch Limited), 28 (Naturo IndiaBull Limited), and 29 (Ms. Jyoti Choudhary) are debarred for 2 years.
- Proceedings against Noticees no. 5–9 and 12–24 were disposed of without directions or monetary penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of market access restrictions and trading prohibitions pursuant to SEBI's final order.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note and ensure compliance with the debarment and restriction directions.
- Entities with open derivative positions are permitted to square off/close out positions within 3 months or upon contract expiry.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026 (with debarment for certain noticees reckoned from August 23, 2024)

## Impact Assessment

- Direct restriction on market access for specified entities and listed company Debock Industries Limited, ensuring regulatory compliance and investor protection.
