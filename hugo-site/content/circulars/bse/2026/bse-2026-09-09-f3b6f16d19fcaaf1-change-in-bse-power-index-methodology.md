## Summary

BSE Index Services Pvt. Ltd. announced methodology changes to the BSE Power Index. The upcoming scheduled reconstitution of the index will be based on the revised methodology.

## Key Points

- Basic industry eligibility criteria updated to include Power Trading and Power Distribution while maintaining core sectors like Heavy Electrical Equipment, Power Generation, Integrated Power Utilities, and Power Transmission.
- Liquidity requirement mandates companies to have a minimum trading frequency of 90% in the preceding six months.
- Constituent weighting scheme adopts a float-adjusted market capitalization weighting with an individual stock weight cap of 33% and a basic industry weight cap of 30%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revisions to the rules governing the selection and weighting of constituents in the BSE Power Index.

## Compliance Requirements

- Index constituents and market participants tracking the BSE Power Index must take note of the updated methodology for upcoming reconstitutions.

## Important Dates

- Effective for the upcoming scheduled reconstitution of the BSE Power Index.

## Impact Assessment

- The methodology changes refine the sector representation, liquidity filters, and stock/industry weight caps for the BSE Power Index, influencing index composition and rebalancing.
