## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new securities (Commercial Papers) issued on a private placement basis on the debt market segment effective September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are traded only in dematerialised form under specific ISIN numbers.
- Standard trading denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Companies issuing commercial papers include Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, Redington Limited, SBICAP Securities Limited, InCred Financial Services Limited, and Nuvama Wealth Management Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to the specified standard denomination and dematerialised format guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 03/09/2026
- Effective Listing Date: 04/09/2026
- Redemption Dates: Between 03/12/2026 and 03/03/2027 depending on the specific issuance.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update for debt segment participants.
