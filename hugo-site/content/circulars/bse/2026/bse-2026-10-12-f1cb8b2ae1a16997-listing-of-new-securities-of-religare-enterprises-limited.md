## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 83,43,263 new equity shares of Religare Enterprises Limited (Scrip Code: 532915) with effect from October 13, 2026. These shares are issued at a price of Rs. 235/- per share (face value Rs. 10/- at a premium of Rs. 225/-) to promoters and non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Religare Enterprises Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 532915
- **ISIN:** INE621H01010
- **Securities Issued:** 83,43,263 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 225/- (Issue Price: Rs. 235.00/-)
- **Allotment Date:** 04-06-2026
- **Effective Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Distribution Nos.:** 332890480 to 341233742
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing of securities issued via preferential allotment upon warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the lock-in periods for specific tranches of shares as detailed in the allotment schedule.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** June 4, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** April 12, 2027 and April 12, 2028 (depending on the tranche)

## Impact Assessment

Addition of 83,43,263 shares increases the paid-up equity capital of Religare Enterprises Limited. Certain tranches are subject to lock-in restrictions until April 2027 and April 2028.
