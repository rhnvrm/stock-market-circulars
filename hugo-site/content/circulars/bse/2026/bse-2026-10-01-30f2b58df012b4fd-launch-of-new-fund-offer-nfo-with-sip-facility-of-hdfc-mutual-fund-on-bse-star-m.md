## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of New Fund Offer (NFO) for HDFC Nifty Largemidcap250 Plus 8-13 Yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund by HDFC Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window is open from October 05, 2026, to October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: HDFC Nifty Largemidcap250 Plus 8-13 Yr G-Sec 70:30 Index Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth and IDCW options).
- NFO Period: October 05, 2026, to October 19, 2026, with SIP facility available.
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 23, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational circular for mutual fund NFO launch.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 23, 2026, for valid allotment.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date shall not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: October 05, 2026
- NFO End Date: October 19, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 23, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: October 23, 2026, by 6:00 PM

Provides trading members and investors with operational timelines and instructions for subscribing to the new HDFC Mutual Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
