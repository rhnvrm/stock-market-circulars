## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the timelines for Trading Members (TMs) to take disciplinary action and submit Action Taken Reports (ATRs) for non-compliances observed during inspections of Authorised Persons (APs).

## Key Points

- TMs must take disciplinary action against APs within 2 months from the end of the quarter for general non-compliances observed during inspections.
- Action Taken Reports (ATRs) along with the status of closure must be submitted to the Exchange within 3 months from the end of the quarter.
- For non-compliances requiring termination as prescribed under Exchange guidelines, TMs must take disciplinary action within seven days from the date of receipt of the AP's inspection report or identification of the non-compliance.
- The guidelines are applicable for AP inspection reports for the July–September 2026 quarter and onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

Establishes strict timelines for Trading Members to enforce disciplinary actions and report compliance status regarding Authorised Persons following inspections.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading Members must adhere to the specified timelines for taking disciplinary action and submitting ATRs to the Exchange, particularly ensuring a 7-day turnaround for termination-level non-compliances starting from the July-September 2026 quarter.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July–September 2026 quarter and onwards.
- General Disciplinary Action Timeline: Within 2 months from the end of the quarter.
- ATR Submission Timeline: Within 3 months from the end of the quarter.
- Termination Action Timeline: Within 7 days of receiving the inspection report/identification for serious violations.

## Impact Assessment

Streamlines the enforcement and reporting mechanism for Trading Members concerning Authorised Persons, ensuring faster resolution and closure of inspection observations.
