## Summary

Trading in the equity shares of Maharaja & Speedex India Limited shall be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment with effect from Monday, October 05, 2026. The dealings will be shifted under the M Group.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Maharaja & Speedex India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544925
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer of equity shares from Trade for Trade segment to Rolling segment, enabling normal rolling settlement for the scrip.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the segment change for executing trades in the scrip from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances liquidity and trading flexibility for investors by moving the scrip from the restrictive trade-for-trade segment to the standard rolling segment.
