## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of trading in the debentures of Prachay Capital Ltd (Scrip Code 976847) effective August 17, 2026. Trading was previously restricted due to a record date set for a put option and interest payment.

## Key Points

- Trading in debentures of Prachay Capital Ltd (Scrip Code 976847) resumes from August 17, 2026.
- The resumption follows the expiration of the put option period where the put option was not exercised by debenture holders.
- Previous reference made to Exchange Notice No. 20260708-5 dated July 08, 2026, regarding the record date of July 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the resumption date and handle trades accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **August 17, 2026:** Effective date for resumption of trading.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact; normal trading operations for the specified debentures resume for market participants.
