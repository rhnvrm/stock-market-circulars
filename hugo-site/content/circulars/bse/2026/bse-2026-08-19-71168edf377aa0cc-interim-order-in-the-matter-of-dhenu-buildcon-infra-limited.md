## Summary

SEBI vide its order no. WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-SEC-7/32667/2026-27 dated August 19, 2026, issued directions regarding Dhenu Buildcon Infra Limited (DBIL), restraining specific noticees from dealing in securities or disposing of shares.

## Key Points

- Noticee nos. 2 to 7 are restrained from disposing of shares of DBIL held by them, directly or indirectly, until further orders.
- Noticee nos. 8 and 9 are restrained from buying, selling, or dealing in securities or associating with the securities market.
- Noticees with open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts can square off or close out positions within 3 months or upon expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Noticees are permitted to settle pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions executed before the close of trading on the date of the order.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of trading and holding restrictions on specific noticees under SEBI interim directions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the restrictions and ensure compliance with SEBI's interim directions regarding the specified noticees and shares of Dhenu Buildcon Infra Limited.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 19, 2026
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and halts potential unauthorized market practices or share disposal by restricted entities associated with Dhenu Buildcon Infra Limited.
