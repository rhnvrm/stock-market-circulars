Moneyboxx Finance Limited has fixed a record date for interest payment and part redemption of its Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs), resulting in a reduced face value effective from the specified date.

- Company Name: Moneyboxx Finance Limited
- Scrip Code: 976440
- Scrip ID: MFL-MONEYBOXX 12.50%-07-12-26-PVT
- ISIN: INE296Q07084
- Record Date: 22/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 22/09/2026
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 20000/- per Debenture

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note that trading in the debentures of the company will be with reduced face value effective from the record/ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 22/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 22/09/2026

Trading members and investors holding the NCDs will be subject to the reduced face value and part redemption payout as per the schedule.
