## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-722/2026-27 held on September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on September 25, 2026.
- Auction Matching timings: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction is scheduled for Monday, September 28, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Members must exercise utmost care while entering security codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 25, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: September 28, 2026 (08:00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational notice for trading members participating in the settlement auction process with minimal market-wide impact.
