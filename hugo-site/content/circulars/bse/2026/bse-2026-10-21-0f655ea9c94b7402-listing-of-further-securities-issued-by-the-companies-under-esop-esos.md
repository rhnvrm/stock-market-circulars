## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that further securities issued by eight companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 8
- Effective date for trading: October 22, 2026
- Participating companies include Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited, Ideaforge Technology Limited, Onesource Specialty Pharma Limited, Tata Consumer Products Limited, Tech Mahindra Limited, Vardhman Special Steels Limited, Viyash Scientific Limited, and Wipro Limited.
- Lock-in details specified where applicable (e.g., Anand Rathi Share and Stock Brokers Limited has lock-in upto 12/10/2027 for 44,350 shares).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing action.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure compliance with applicable SEBI ICDR and LODR regulations regarding the listing and trading of ESOP shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion resulting from employee stock option exercises. Market impact is negligible and routine.
