## Summary

In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260522-45 dated May 22, 2026, the BSE has notified that SEBI vide email dated July 31, 2026, has directed the removal of the freeze or ban on trading accounts for specific noticees involved in the matter of trading in certain scrips through social media recommendations.

## Key Points

- Relates to SEBI's investigation into stock recommendations given on social media platforms.
- SEBI has instructed the removal of trading account freezes for seven specific noticees.
- Affected noticees include Hemant Kumar Gupta, Sharon Gupta, Rohan Gupta, Leana Hemant Gupta, Rajani Hemant Gupta, Aniket Hemant Gupta, and Purvangi Hemant Gupta.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial relief/modification of earlier interim directions regarding account freezes for specified individuals.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the removal of restrictions and ensure compliance for the listed accounts.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 31, 2026
- Reference to earlier notice dated May 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Limited impact restricted to the specific trading accounts and noticees named in the circular, restoring their trading access.
