## Summary

Sundaram Mutual Fund has communicated a change in the scheme names for several of its mutual fund schemes available on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme names have been updated across multiple categories.
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026.
- Affected Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

### Scheme Name Changes:
1. SUNDARAM BANKING AND PSU FUND changed to SUNDARAM BANKING AND PSU DEBT FUND
2. SUNDARAM LOW DURATION FUND changed to SUNDARAM ULTRA SHORT TO SHORT TERM FUND
3. SUNDARAM ULTRA SHORT DURATION FUND changed to SUNDARAM ULTRA SHORT TERM FUND
4. SUNDARAM SHORT DURATION FUND changed to SUNDARAM SHORT TERM FUND
5. SUNDARAM MEDIUM DURATION FUND changed to SUNDARAM MEDIUM TERM FUND
6. SUNDARAM BALANCED ADVANTAGE FUND changed to SUNDARAM DYNAMIC ASSET ALLOCATION FUND

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to note the updated scheme names while placing orders on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal impact; purely a nomenclature change for existing and prospective investments in the specified schemes.
