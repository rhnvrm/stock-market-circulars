## Summary

BSE has sought clarification from companies regarding news articles appearing in various media outlets to verify accuracy and inform investors. A reply was received from Whirlpool of India Ltd, while a reply from SpiceJet Ltd is awaited.

## Key Points

- Exchange sought clarification from listed companies regarding news articles.
- Whirlpool of India Ltd (Scrip Code: 500238) provided its reply.
- SpiceJet Ltd (Scrip Code: 500285) reply is currently awaited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies must provide timely clarifications on news articles as required by exchange regulations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 24 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine disclosure update with no direct financial impact.
