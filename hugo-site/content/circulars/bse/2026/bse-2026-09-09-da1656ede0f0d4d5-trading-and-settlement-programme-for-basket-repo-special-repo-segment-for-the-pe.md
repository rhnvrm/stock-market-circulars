## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing the trading and settlement schedule for the Basket Repo and Special Repo segment for the period from October 1, 2026, to October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules for Ready Leg T+0 and Ready Leg T+1.
- Specifies transaction take-up dates, securities/funds pay-in dates, and pay-out dates for October 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must adhere to the specified pay-in and pay-out dates for repo transactions as per the calendar.

## Important Dates

- Effective Period: 01.10.2026 to 31.10.2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational calendar ensuring smooth settlement cycles for repo market participants.
