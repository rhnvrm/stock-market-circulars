## Summary

WAISL LIMITED has announced the record date and effective date for the part redemption of its Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). Trading members are informed that trading will take place with a reduced face value.

## Key Points

- Company Name: WAISL LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 975113
- ISIN: INE07XR08015
- Scrip ID: WL-9.45%-27-9-29-PVT
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 70,000/- per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face value and record/ex-date for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 10/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 10/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for holders of the specified non-convertible debentures due to part redemption and reduced face value.
