## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of City Pulse Multiventures Limited (Scrip Code - 542727) effective from October 16, 2026. A total of 42,74,800 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each were issued on a preferential basis at an issue price of Rs. 358/- (including a premium of Rs. 348/-).

## Key Points

- Company Name: City Pulse Multiventures Limited
- Scrip Code: 542727
- ISIN: INE056001014
- Total New Securities Listed: 42,74,800 equity shares
- Issue Type: Preferential basis to Promoter and Non-Promoter
- Issue Price: Rs. 358/- per share (Face value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 348/-)
- Date of Allotment: January 7, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 16, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 10664034 to 14938833

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; standard listing of securities issued on a preferential basis.

## Compliance Requirements

Lock-in requirements apply to the newly allotted shares:
- 20,30,900 shares (Distinctive Nos. 10664034 - 12694933) locked-in up to August 31, 2027.
- 22,43,900 shares (Distinctive Nos. 12694934 - 14938833) locked-in up to May 31, 2028.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: January 7, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: August 31, 2027 and May 31, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of the company's equity capital base through preferential allotment, with shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
