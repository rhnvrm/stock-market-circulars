## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding trading members with approval for the Margin Trading Facility (MTF) to submit their compliance certificate for the half year ended September 30, 2026. Submissions must be completed electronically via the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS) by October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable pursuant to Chapter 1, Para 4.10.3 of SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024.
- Active MTF trading members must submit a compliance certificate (Annexure 1) audited and signed by a Chartered Accountant.
- Members who have approval for MTF but have not commenced operations must submit a self-certified certificate (Annexure 2) signed by the Compliance Officer or Designated Director.
- Submissions must be made exclusively through BEFS (https://befs.bseindia.com -> Margin Trading).
- Physical or email submissions will be treated as non-submission and will not be accepted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiteration and enforcement of existing compliance reporting norms for Margin Trading Facility as mandated by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Conduct audit of books of accounts pertaining to MTF through a qualified Chartered Accountant (for active members).
- Execute self-certification for uncommenced MTF approvals.
- Upload scanned copy via BEFS and ensure receipt of an acknowledgment email before the due date.

## Important Dates

- **Half Year Ended:** September 30, 2026
- **Submission Deadline:** October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures continuous regulatory oversight and financial compliance among trading members offering margin trading facilities, safeguarding market stability.
