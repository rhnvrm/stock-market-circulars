## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 8,96,873 new equity shares of Arihant Foundations & Housing Ltd. with effect from September 11, 2026, following the conversion of warrants issued to promoters and non-promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Arihant Foundations & Housing Ltd. (Scrip Code: 531381)
- Security Details: 8,96,873 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 470/- (Issue Price: Rs. 480/-) pursuant to warrant conversion.
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026.
- ISIN: INE413D01011
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard corporate listing procedure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the new securities and lock-in details specified for the allotted shares.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Dates: May 5, 2026 and May 15, 2026.
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: September 11, 2026.
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: March 16, 2027 and March 16, 2028 for respective tranches.

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the paid-up equity capital base of Arihant Foundations & Housing Ltd. through preferential allotment to promoters and non-promoters.
