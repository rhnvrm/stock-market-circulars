## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of specified companies due to fixed record dates for interest payment and/or redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of the mentioned companies as per the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260911-6
- Segment: Debt
- Department: Listing Operations
- Affected Companies: Vardhita Properties Private Limited, Muthoot Finance Limited, and THDC India Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are advised not to deal in the debentures of the listed companies during the suspension period associated with the record dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 11/09/2026
- Record Dates: 15/09/2026, 18/09/2026, and 19/09/2026 (varying by security)
- Ex-Dates: 15/09/2026 and 18/09/2026 (varying by security)

## Impact Assessment

Temporary trading suspension in specific debt securities of the mentioned issuers to facilitate interest payment and redemption processing.
