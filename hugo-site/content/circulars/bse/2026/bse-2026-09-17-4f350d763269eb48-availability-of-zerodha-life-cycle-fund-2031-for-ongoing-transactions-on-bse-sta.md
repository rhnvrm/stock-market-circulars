## Summary

BSE has announced that the Zerodha Life Cycle Fund 2031 (Direct Plan - Growth) will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Zerodha Life Cycle Fund 2031 - Direct Plan - Growth
- ISIN: INF0R8F01240
- Scheme Category: Equity
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors must take note of the availability of the scheme for ongoing transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026
- Notice Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute ongoing transactions and systematic investment plans for the specified Zerodha mutual fund scheme on the BSE platform.
