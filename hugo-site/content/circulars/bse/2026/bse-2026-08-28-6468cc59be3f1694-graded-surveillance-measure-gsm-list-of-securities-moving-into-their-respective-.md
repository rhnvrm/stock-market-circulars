## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the list of securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure will transition to higher GSM stages starting August 31, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, ranging from 100% margin rates and 5% price bands to trade-for-trade settlement and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement for affected securities will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures as per the provisions of the GSM Framework.
- Application of stage-specific restrictions including price bands, trade-for-trade settlement, weekly trading restrictions, and ASD requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Buyers are required to deposit the applicable Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) and adhere to trade-for-trade settlement rules.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 Aug 2026
- Effective Date: 31 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading frequency for securities moving into higher GSM stages (such as Stages III and IV) will be restricted to once a week.
- Increased capital requirements for buyers due to mandatory ASD and 100% margin rates.
