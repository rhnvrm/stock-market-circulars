## Summary

BSE has announced that the Carnelian Liquid Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting October 19, 2026, following Notice No. 20261009-43 dated October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: Carnelian Liquid Fund (Regular and Direct Plans - Growth, Daily IDCW Reinvestment, Weekly IDCW Reinvestment).
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026.
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational notification regarding fund availability on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate transactions in the mentioned ISINs on the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date for Transactions: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute transactions across multiple plans of Carnelian Liquid Fund seamlessly via the BSE StAR MF platform.
