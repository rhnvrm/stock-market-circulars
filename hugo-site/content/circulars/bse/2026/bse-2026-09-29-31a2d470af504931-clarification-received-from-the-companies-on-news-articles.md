## Summary

BSE sought clarification from various companies regarding news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels to verify their accuracy for investors. Panorama Studios International Ltd submitted its reply, while responses from SpiceJet Ltd, Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Ltd, Tata Chemicals Ltd, and Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- BSE sought clarification from companies regarding news articles in media to ensure market accuracy.
- Panorama Studios International Ltd (Scrip Code: 539469) submitted its reply.
- Reply is awaited from SpiceJet Ltd (Scrip Code: 500285).
- Reply is awaited from Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Ltd (Scrip Code: 532371).
- Reply is awaited from Tata Chemicals Ltd (Scrip Code: 500770).
- Reply is awaited from Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd (Scrip Code: 500570).

## Regulatory Changes

Exchange surveillance mechanism for seeking clarifications on news reports affecting listed entities.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must provide timely clarifications regarding news articles as requested by the exchange surveillance department.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard surveillance procedure ensuring transparency and verification of media reports concerning listed companies.
