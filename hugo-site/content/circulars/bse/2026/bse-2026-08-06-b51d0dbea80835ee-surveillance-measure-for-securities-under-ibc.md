## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application of Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective August 7, 2026, specific securities meeting the criteria are placed under the IBC framework, while others are moving out. A consolidated list is provided via exchange annexures.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC Additional Surveillance Measure framework are placed under surveillance effective August 7, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the IBC framework effective August 7, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.
- The shortlisting is purely a market surveillance measure and does not constitute adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures as per the established IBC framework under BSE notice guidelines.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their original levels, subject to not being part of any other active surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the shortlisted securities and ensure compliance with the applicable surveillance margins and price bands.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 7, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affected securities may experience trading restrictions or price band adjustments in accordance with the IBC surveillance framework. Investors and members should verify the specific annexures for impacted scrips.
