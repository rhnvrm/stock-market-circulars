## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd. issued a notice regarding the stock-wise Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) and position limits applicable to Clearing Members (CM), Institutional Investors, and Clients under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for the month of September 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable for the month of September 2026 under the SLB segment.
- Based on the broad framework of the Securities Lending Scheme, 1997 and SEBI circular MRD/DoP/SE/Dep/Cir-14/2007.
- Detailed stock-wise limits are provided in Annexure I of the notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; routine monthly specification of position limits under existing frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, clearing members, institutional investors, and clients must adhere to the specified position limits under the SLBS for September 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 1, 2026
- Notice Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures compliance and risk management parameters are aligned with allowable position limits for shorting and lending activities in the SLB segment for September 2026.
