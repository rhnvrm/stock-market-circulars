## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revised reopening date for the New Fund Offer (NFO) of INVESCO INDIA PHARMA AND HEALTHCARE FUND on the BSE StAR MF platform, following a communication from Invesco Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Reference to previous Notice No. 20260817-40 dated August 17, 2026.
- Revised reopening date for INVESCO INDIA PHARMA AND HEALTHCARE FUND - NFO changed from September 10, 2026, to September 11, 2026.
- Applies to the BSE StAR MF platform with SIP facility.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to inform their clients regarding the revised reopen date.

## Important Dates

- **Revised Reopening Date:** September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; update ensures investors and members are aligned with the correct NFO reopening timeline.
