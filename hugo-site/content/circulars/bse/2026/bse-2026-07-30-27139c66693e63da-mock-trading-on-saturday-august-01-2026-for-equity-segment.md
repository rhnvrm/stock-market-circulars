## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session in the Equity segment on Saturday, August 01, 2026. This session is part of preparations for the introduction of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) and associated Standard Operating Procedures applicable from Monday, August 03, 2026, as outlined in previous circulars.

## Key Points

- **Date of Mock Trading:** Saturday, August 01, 2026.
- **Segment:** Equity segment.
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Trading members can use this session or the UAT environment to fulfill regulatory requirements under SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.
- **Platform Testing:** Third-party trading platform users can test functionalities including call auction sessions, risk-reduction modes, trading halts, and block deals.
- **Scrip Master:** No separate scrip master will be provided on the mock trading day; the end-of-day file from Friday, July 31, 2026, must be used for both mock trading and live trading starting August 03, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Introduction of Closing Auction Session (CAS) and updated Standard Operating Procedures effective August 03, 2026, following prior circulars (Nos. 20260319-21, 20260610-41, and 20260728-42).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should participate in the mock trading session to test systems, ensure compatibility with the upcoming Closing Auction Session framework, and fulfill mandatory software testing requirements.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** August 01, 2026
- **Effective Date for New Procedures & CAS:** August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth market transition to the new Closing Auction Session (CAS) and verifies that trading member systems and third-party platforms function correctly under updated operational timelines.
