## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Viceroy Hotels Ltd. issued on a rights basis, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Viceroy Hotels Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 523796
- ISIN: INE048C01025
- Security Details: 92,03,008 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 105/- on a rights basis.
- Distinctive Numbers: 67578949 to 76781956
- Date of Allotment: 15/09/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: September 18, 2026
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with exchange norms regarding the trading of the newly listed rights shares.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 15/09/2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: 18/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing of rights issue shares with no direct systemic market impact.
