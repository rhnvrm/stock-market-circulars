## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Government of India Treasury Bills (T-Bills) comprising 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day T-Bills scheduled for August 27, 2026, through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding facility available for three Treasury Bills: 91 Day T-Bill (091T261126), 182 Day T-Bill (182T250227), and 364 Day T-Bill (364T270827).
- Bid collection start date: August 24, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards.
- Bid collection end date for direct investors: August 26, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- Bid collection end date for members: August 27, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- Auction Date: August 27, 2026.
- Settlement Date: August 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The notice is in reference to the RBI press release dated August 21, 2026, and Exchange circular no. 20180423-42 dated April 26, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible investors must submit bids through the NCB-GSec module of the iBBS web-based platform within the stipulated timelines.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start: August 24, 2026, 10:00 am
- Bid Collection End (Direct Investors): August 26, 2026, 5:00 pm
- Bid Collection End (Members): August 27, 2026, 8:00 am
- Auction Date: August 27, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless participation of trading members and direct investors in government securities auctions via the BSE iBBS platform.
