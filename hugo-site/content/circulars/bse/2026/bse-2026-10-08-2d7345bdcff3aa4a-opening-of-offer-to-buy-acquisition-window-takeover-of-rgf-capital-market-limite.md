## Summary

An open offer has been made by Mr. Nishad Jitendra Shah and co-acquirers to acquire up to 3,90,06,240 fully paid-up equity shares representing 26.00% of the voting equity share capital of RGF Capital Markets Limited at an offer price of Rs. 1.10 per share. The acquisition window for the tender offer runs from October 13, 2026, to October 27, 2026, in compliance with SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Acquirers: Mr. Nishad Jitendra Shah, M/s. Nishad Jitendra Shah HUF, Ms. Rajshree Nishad Shah, Mr. Parshva Nishad Shah, Ms. Payal Parshva Shah, M/s. Trupti Management Services Private Limited, M/s. Rocksolid Investments, M/s. Rocksolid Enterprise, and M/s. Rockthree Framework LLP.
- Target Company: RGF Capital Markets Limited.
- Offer Price: Rs. 1.10/- per fully paid-up equity share payable in cash.
- Offer Size: Up to 3,90,06,240 equity shares representing 26.00% of the voting equity share capital.
- Offer Period: October 13, 2026 to October 27, 2026 (excluding SEBI holiday on October 20, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

None. The transaction adheres to existing SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 and revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to tender offers under takeovers, buybacks, and delisting.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians are required to note the acquisition window mechanism for tender offers and facilitate eligible public shareholders of RGF Capital Markets Limited in tendering their shares through the stock exchange platform in accordance with SEBI and Exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Offer Opening Date: October 13, 2026
- SEBI Holiday (No trading): October 20, 2026
- Offer Closing Date: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public shareholders of RGF Capital Markets Limited an exit opportunity at the specified offer price of Rs. 1.10 per share while facilitating substantial acquisition of shares and control by the acquirers under SEBI Takeover regulations.
