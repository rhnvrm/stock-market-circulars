## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Talwalkars Better Value Fitness Limited has fixed the record date for the reduction of its equity share capital as per the order passed by the Hon’ble National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT), Mumbai Bench (Order No. DR-742/2026-2027). The entire existing paid-up equity share capital held by existing promoters and public shareholders will be extinguished and cancelled without any payment or consideration. Concurrently, a minimum of 5% of the post-reduction paid-up capital will be issued to public shareholders and/or strategic investors.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Scrip Code: Talwalkars Better Value Fitness Limited (533200)
- Record Date: 26/10/2026
- Ex-Date / Suspension w.e.f. Date: 26/10/2026
- Capital Reduction Mechanism: Entire existing paid-up equity share capital extinguished/cancelled without payment or consideration to existing shareholders.
- New Issuance: Minimum 5% of post-reduction paid-up capital issued to public shareholding and/or strategic investors.
- Trading Status: Trading in the equity shares of the company is currently under suspension.

## Regulatory Changes

- Execution of capital reduction scheme mandated by the Hon'ble NCLAT Mumbai Bench order (DR-742/2026-2027).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are required to take note of the capital reduction, extinguishment of existing equity shares, and the record/ex-date of October 26, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 26/10/2026
- Ex-Date / Suspension w.e.f. Date: 26/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Total extinguishment of existing equity holdings without payout represents a complete write-down for current shareholders, with new issuance restricted to a minimum of 5% for public/strategic investors.
