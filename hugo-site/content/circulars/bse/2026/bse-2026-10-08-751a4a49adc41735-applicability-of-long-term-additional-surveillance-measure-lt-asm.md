## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, stage revisions, and removal of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, segment transfers, and removals based on periodic reviews.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable effective October 13, 2026, on all open positions as on October 12, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands become effective from October 09, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Certain securities will continue in LT-ASM but transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 13, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- Securities moving out of LT-ASM framework effective October 09, 2026, with price bands reinstated (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) including 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions.
- Group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS) for specified securities under surveillance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure 100% margins are maintained for applicable securities from October 13, 2026.
- Monitor price band restrictions and group transfers effective October 09 and October 13, 2026.

## Important Dates

- October 09, 2026: Lower price bands effective, stage revisions effective, and removal of securities effective.
- October 12, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margin.
- October 13, 2026: 100% margins applicable; segment transfers (T / XT / MT / TS Group) effective.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in securities under the LT-ASM framework will experience increased margin requirements (100% margin) and trading restrictions such as lower price bands and segment transfers, impacting liquidity and position management for affected scrips.
