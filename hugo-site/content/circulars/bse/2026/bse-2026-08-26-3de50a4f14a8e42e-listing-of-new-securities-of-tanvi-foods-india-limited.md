## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 34,000 new equity shares of Tanvi Foods (India) Limited with effect from August 27, 2026, following the conversion of warrants issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Tanvi Foods (India) Limited (Scrip Code: 540332)
- Security Details: 34,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 80/- (Issue Price: Rs. 90/-) upon warrant conversion.
- Effective Date of Trading: August 27, 2026.
- ISIN: INE978V01015
- Distinctive Numbers: 13019001 to 13053000
- Lock-in Details: 34,000 shares are under lock-in until March 31, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Ensure compliance with lock-in requirements up to March 31, 2027, for the newly allotted shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 30/06/2026
- Commencement of Trading: 27/08/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 31/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Tanvi Foods (India) Limited through preferential allotment of shares upon warrant conversion.
