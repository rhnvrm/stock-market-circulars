## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the merger of UTI Childrens Hybrid Fund (Transferor Scheme) into UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund (Transferee Scheme) of UTI Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective after business hours on October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- UTI Childrens Hybrid Fund is merging into UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund.
- Effective date of merger is after the close of business hours on October 23, 2026.
- Fresh subscriptions, lumpsum investments, and SIP/XSIP registrations in the Transferor scheme were suspended effective September 15, 2026.
- Redemption and switch-out transactions in the Transferor scheme remain permitted up to AMC timelines.
- Investors not wishing to participate in the merger can redeem or switch to other UTI schemes without exit load between September 21, 2026, and October 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory migration of existing SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP registrations to corresponding options of the Transferee scheme after October 23, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take note of the merger and advise investors wishing to opt-out to initiate redemption/switch requests or systematic facility cancellations within specified timelines.

## Important Dates

- September 15, 2026: Suspension of fresh subscriptions and SIP registrations in Transferor scheme.
- September 21, 2026 to October 23, 2026: Window for exit-load-free redemptions or switches.
- October 23, 2026: Effective date of merger after business hours and cessation of Transferor scheme trading availability.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding units in the Transferor scheme will have their investments and systematic registrations automatically migrated or must exercise exit/switch options prior to the deadline.
