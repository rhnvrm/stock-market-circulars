## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission for 21,44,239 new equity shares of Scan Steels Limited (Scrip Code - 511672) with effect from September 04, 2026. These shares are issued on a preferential basis following the conversion of Optionally Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares (OCRPS).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Scan Steels Limited
- Scrip Code: 511672
- ISIN: INE099G01011
- Number of Shares: 21,44,239 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 48/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 58/-
- Effective Date of Trading: September 04, 2026
- Date of Allotment: 27/04/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants take note of the new securities being admitted to dealings.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 27/04/2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: 04/09/2026
- Lock-in details:
  - 11,62,884 shares (Dist. Nos. 58602296 - 59765179): Locked-in upto 06/03/2028
  - 9,81,355 shares (Dist. Nos. 59765180 - 60746534): Locked-in upto 04/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing of preferential shares for Scan Steels Limited.
