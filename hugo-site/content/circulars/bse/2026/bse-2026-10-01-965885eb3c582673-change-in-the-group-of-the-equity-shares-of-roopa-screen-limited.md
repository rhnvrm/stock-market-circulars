## Summary

Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced that the equity shares of Roopa Screen Limited (Scrip Code: 544954) will be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Roopa Screen Limited
- Scrip Code: 544954
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Friday, October 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer of scrip from Trade for Trade settlement to normal rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the change in segment and ensure compliance with trading norms for the Rolling segment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Allows normal rolling settlement for equity shares of Roopa Screen Limited, improving liquidity and trading convenience for market participants.
