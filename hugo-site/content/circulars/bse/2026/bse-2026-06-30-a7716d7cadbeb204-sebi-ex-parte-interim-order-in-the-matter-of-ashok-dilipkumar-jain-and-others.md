## Summary

SEBI vide its order no. WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-SEC-2/32457/2026-27 dated June 30, 2026, has issued interim directions restraining ten noticees from buying, selling, or dealing in securities of Darjeeling Industries Limited, either directly or indirectly, until further orders.

## Key Points

- SEBI issued an ex-parte interim order on June 30, 2026.
- Ten individuals (Noticees) are restrained from dealing in the securities of Darjeeling Industries Limited.
- Effective immediately from the date of the order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Restraint on trading and dealing in securities of Darjeeling Industries Limited for the specified noticees.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the order and ensure compliance by preventing the specified noticees from executing trades in the company's securities.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: June 30, 2026
- Effective Date: June 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Direct regulatory restriction on specific individuals prohibiting transactions in Darjeeling Industries Limited shares.
