## Summary

Viceroy Hotels Ltd. has announced a Rights Issue of Equity Shares. The company has fixed the record date for determining the eligible shareholders entitled to participate in the rights issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Viceroy Hotels Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 523796
- Issue Details: 06 (SIX) Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 105/- per share on Rights Basis for every 07 (SEVEN) Equity Shares held.
- Record Date: 20/08/2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Effective Date: 20/08/2026
- Settlement Number: DR- 698/2026-2027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that transactions in the equity shares of the company are conducted on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified effective date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Rights Date: 20/08/2026
- Record Date: 20/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date will receive rights entitlements to subscribe to the new equity shares according to the specified ratio.
