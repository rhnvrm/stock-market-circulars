## Summary

BSE has issued a circular updating the formats for publishing unaudited and audited quarterly financial results and filing financial results in XBRL mode for all life, general, health insurers, and reinsurers, following IRDAI and SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Formats for publishing quarterly financial results by listed insurers have been revised.
- Issuers can download the Excel utility for 'Integrated Filling Financials-Insurance IND AS' from the BSE Listing Centre.
- Instructions are provided for downloading the XBRL excel utility and submitting XML files via the BSE Listing Centre.
- Insurers granted forbearance for FY 2026-27 for Ind AS implementation shall continue publishing results under Schedule II of the 2024 IRDAI Regulations using specified legacy circulars.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revised reporting formats in alignment with IRDAI Circular dated July 08, 2026, and SEBI Master Circular dated January 30, 2026.
- Mandates XBRL mode filing for integrated financials under Ind AS for applicable insurance and reinsurance companies.

## Compliance Requirements

- Life, general, health insurers, and reinsurers must use the updated Excel utility from the BSE Listing Centre for XBRL filing.
- Submissions must be uploaded via the designated path: Listing Centre Menu > Listing compliance > XBRL > E-filing > Integrated Financial Result > Integrated Financial Result IND AS.
- Exemptions apply to insurers with Ind AS forbearance for FY 2026-27, who must continue adhering to legacy circulars.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Insurers and reinsurers must update their internal reporting systems and compliance workflows to adopt the new Ind AS XBRL filing formats on the BSE Listing Centre.
