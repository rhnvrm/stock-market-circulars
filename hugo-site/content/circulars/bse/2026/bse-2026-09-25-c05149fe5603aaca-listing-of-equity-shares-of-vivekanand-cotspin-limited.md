## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Vivekanand Cotspin Limited on the SME platform effective from Monday, September 28, 2026, under the 'MT' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544939
- ISIN: INE1JJP01018
- Market Lot: 3,000 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 37/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 27/-)
- Total Securities: 2,22,50,000 equity shares fully paid up
- Trade-for-Trade Segment: Applicable for 10 trading days as per SEBI guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing procedure for SME IPOs under existing SEBI and Exchange frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Mandatory participation in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.
- Lock-in period marked in depository records as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 24, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors dealing in Vivekanand Cotspin Limited shares must note the trade-for-trade segment restrictions for the initial 10 trading sessions and ensure adherence to the minimum market lot of 3,000 shares.
