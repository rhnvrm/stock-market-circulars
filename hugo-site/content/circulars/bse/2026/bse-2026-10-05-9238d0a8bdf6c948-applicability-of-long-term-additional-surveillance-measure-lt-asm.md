## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) on various securities as per the revised framework. The measures include 100% margins, movement of securities to higher stages, transfer to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS), and removal of securities from the framework.

## Key Points

- 100% margins shall be applicable effective October 08, 2026, on all open positions as on October 07, 2026, and new positions created from October 08, 2026 onwards.
- Securities moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework and securities subject to lower price bands are effective from October 06, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 08, 2026.
- Review and shortlisting of securities are based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and market surveillance.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) parameters including margin requirements and price band restrictions for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and note the price band changes and group transfers for applicable scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Bands and Higher Stages / Moving Out: October 06, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity and liquidity in the shortlisted securities may be impacted due to 100% margin requirements, price band restrictions, and group reclassifications.
