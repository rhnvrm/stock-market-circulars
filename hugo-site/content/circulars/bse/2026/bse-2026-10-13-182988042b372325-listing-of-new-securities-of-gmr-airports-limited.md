## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 10,20,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each of GMR Airports Limited (Scrip Code: 532754), effective from October 14, 2026. These shares were issued pursuant to the conversion of Optionally Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares (OCRPS) under the composite scheme of amalgamation and arrangement.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** GMR Airports Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 532754
- **ISIN Number:** INE776C01039
- **Security Details:** 10,20,00,000 Equity Shares of Re. 1/- each issued pursuant to conversion of OCRPS.
- **Date of Allotment:** September 29, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Distribution Nos.:** 10558980453 to 10660980452
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
