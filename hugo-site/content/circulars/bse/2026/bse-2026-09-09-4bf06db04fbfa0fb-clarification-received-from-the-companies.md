## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in certain scrips. To ensure investors and trading members have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the concerned companies. Replies received from several companies are attached to the notice, while responses from other listed entities remain awaited.

## Key Points

- **Scrips with Clarifications Received:**
  1. Allcargo Global Ltd (Scrip Code: 544602)
  2. Astec Lifesciences Ltd (Scrip Code: 533138)
  3. Bodhtree Consulting Ltd (Scrip Code: 539122)
  4. Dynamic Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 524818)
  5. Indo Farm Equipment Ltd (Scrip Code: 544328)
  6. Manoj Vaibhav Gems N Jewellers Ltd (Scrip Code: 543995)
  7. NOCIL Ltd (Scrip Code: 500730)
  8. Radhika Jeweltech Ltd (Scrip Code: 540125)

- **Scrips with Replies Awaited:**
  1. Billwin Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 543209)
  2. CMX Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 532217)
  3. Deep Polymers Ltd (Scrip Code: 541778)
  4. Mahesh Developers Ltd (Scrip Code: 542677)
  5. Mukta Agriculture Ltd (Scrip Code: 535204)
  6. Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Ltd (Scrip Code: 544510)
  7. Nirmitee Robotics India Ltd (Scrip Code: 543194)
  8. Sera Investments & Finance India Ltd (Scrip Code: 512399)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes were introduced; this is a routine surveillance and market disclosure measure.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities experiencing significant market activity are required to promptly clarify material information to the Exchange under surveillance guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 09 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and aids investors in evaluating the reasons behind abnormal price and volume fluctuations in specified scrips.
