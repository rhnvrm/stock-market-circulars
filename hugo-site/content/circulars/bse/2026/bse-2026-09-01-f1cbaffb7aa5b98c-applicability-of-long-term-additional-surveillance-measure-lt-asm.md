## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, stage revisions, and removal of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Certain securities are subjected to 100% margins, lower price bands, or group transfers effective September 2, 2026, and September 4, 2026, based on periodic reviews and XBRL submissions.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. September 04, 2026, on open positions as of September 03, 2026, and new positions for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands effective September 02, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Securities continuing in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS groups effective September 04, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- List of securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective September 02, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided in Annexure III.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of strict surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions under the revised LT-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements and price band restrictions for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Bands / Higher Stages / Removal: September 02, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date for Margins: September 03, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins / Group Transfers: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and leverage for the shortlisted securities will be restricted due to 100% margin requirements and lower price bands, while securities exiting the framework will have their price bands reinstated.
