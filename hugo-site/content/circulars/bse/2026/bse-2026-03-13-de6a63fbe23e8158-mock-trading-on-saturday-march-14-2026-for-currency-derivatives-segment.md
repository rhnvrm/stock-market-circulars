## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment on Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are advised to participate and test their disaster recovery (DR) site connection parameters for BOLT Plus, ETI APIs, and web-based exchange systems.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Currency Derivatives segment.
- Details provided for connecting to the DR site of the BOLT Plus trading system.
- Instructions included for 3rd party trading applications utilizing ETI APIs and exchange web-based systems (RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, Extranet).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should verify their disaster recovery connection parameters and ensure readiness for the mock trading session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; routine technical drill to ensure business continuity and disaster recovery preparedness.
