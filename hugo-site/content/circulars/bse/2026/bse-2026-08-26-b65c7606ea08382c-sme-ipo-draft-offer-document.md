## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Vishwas Refoils & Consumer Limited for its upcoming SME IPO. The document has been filed in accordance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations and is available for public comments for at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vishwas Refoils & Consumer Limited
- Filing Date: August 22, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO
- Public Comment Period: At least 21 days from the date of the notice
- Hosting Platform: BSE SME website

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public can review the Draft Offer Document on the BSE SME website and submit any comments within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 26, 2026
- Draft Document Filing Date: August 22, 2026
- Comment Submission Deadline: Within 21 days of the notice

## Impact Assessment

Standard procedural notice for an SME initial public offering, allowing market participants to review disclosures and provide feedback before final issuance.
