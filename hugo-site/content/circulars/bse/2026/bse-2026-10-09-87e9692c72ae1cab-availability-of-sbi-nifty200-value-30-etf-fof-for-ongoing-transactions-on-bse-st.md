## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that the SBI NIFTY200 VALUE 30 ETF FOF will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and facilities like SIP, STP, and SWP on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026.
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **Schemes Involved:** Regular and Direct plans of SBI NIFTY200 VALUE 30 ETF FOF (Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options).
- **Investment Range & Installments:** 
  - Monthly frequency for Rs. 500 to Rs. 999 requires a minimum of 12 installments.
  - Monthly frequency for Rs. 1,000 and above requires a minimum of 6 installments.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational circular regarding mutual fund scheme transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors must take note of the reopening of the schemes and investment ranges for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 9, 2026
- **Effective Date for Transactions:** October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates ongoing investor participation and systematic investment plans (SIP/STP/SWP) in the SBI NIFTY200 VALUE 30 ETF FOF through the BSE StAR MF platform.
