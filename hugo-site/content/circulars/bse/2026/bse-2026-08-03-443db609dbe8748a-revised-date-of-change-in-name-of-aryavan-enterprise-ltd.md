## Summary

In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260729-12, the BSE has informed trading members that the effective date for the change in scrip ID and abbreviated name for Aryavan Enterprise Ltd on the BOLT Plus system has been revised to August 05, 2026, instead of the previously notified August 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name Change:** Aryavan Enterprise Ltd to Ecofinity Limited (Scrip Code: 539455).
- **New Scrip ID:** ECOFINITY
- **New Abbreviated Name:** ECOFINITY
- **Revised Effective Date:** August 05, 2026 (previously August 04, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a schedule revision for an existing corporate action notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the revised effective date for trading and system updates.

## Important Dates

- **Revised Effective Date:** August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact restricted to trading and settlement system displays reflecting the updated company name and scrip ID.
