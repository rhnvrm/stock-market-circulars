## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of three newly listed companies to its indices. ESDS Software Solution Limited will be added to the BSE IPO index effective September 08, 2026. Additionally, Paluck Technologies Limited and Complete Sports and Management India Limited will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- ESDS Software Solution Limited (Ticker: 544898) listed on BSE effective September 04, 2026, and added to the BSE IPO index effective September 08, 2026.
- Paluck Technologies Limited (Ticker: 544897) listed on the BSE SME platform effective September 04, 2026.
- COMPLETE SPORTS AND MANAGEMENT INDIA LIMITED (Ticker: 544900) listed on the BSE SME platform effective September 04, 2026.
- Both Paluck Technologies and Complete Sports will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective September 07, 2026.
- Actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE) for September 04, 2026.

None.

## Important Dates

- **Listing Dates:** September 04, 2026
- **BSE SME IPO Index Inclusion Date:** September 07, 2026
- **BSE IPO Index Inclusion Date:** September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard index inclusion for newly listed equities on the mainboard and SME platforms of BSE.
