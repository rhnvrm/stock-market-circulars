## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission for trading of 1,50,000 new equity shares of Stellant Securities (India) Limited issued to promoters on a preferential basis upon conversion of warrants, effective from September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Stellant Securities (India) Limited
- Scrip Code: 526071
- Security Details: 1,50,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 330/- (Issue Price: Rs. 340/-).
- Effective Date of Trading: Wednesday, September 16, 2026.
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Distinctive Numbers: 5535996 to 5685995
- ISIN: INE395F01023

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares are subject to lock-in up to March 31, 2028, as per the specified distinctive numbers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 08/07/2026
- Commencement of Trading: 16/09/2026
- Lock-in Expiry: 31/03/2028

## Impact Assessment

Involves expansion of the company's paid-up equity capital base through preferential allotment to promoters with standard lock-in compliance.
