## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the merger of schemes of Axis Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The merger becomes effective after the close of business hours on September 11, 2026. Transferor schemes include AXIS CRISIL-IBX AAA BOND NBFC-HFC – JUN 2027 INDEX FUND and AXIS CRISIL-IBX AAA BOND FINANCIAL SERVICES – SEP 2027 INDEX FUND, which are merging into AXIS CRISIL-IBX AAA BOND NBFC – JUN 2027 INDEX FUND.

## Key Points

- Effective date of merger: After close of business hours on September 11, 2026.
- Transferor schemes: AXIS CRISIL-IBX AAA BOND NBFC-HFC – JUN 2027 INDEX FUND and AXIS CRISIL-IBX AAA BOND FINANCIAL SERVICES – SEP 2027 INDEX FUND.
- Transferee scheme: AXIS CRISIL-IBX AAA BOND NBFC – JUN 2027 INDEX FUND.
- Exit load waiver period provided for investors wishing to redeem or switch without exit load from August 12, 2026, to September 10, 2026.
- Existing SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP registrations will be automatically migrated to the transferee schemes after business hours on September 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must take note of transaction timelines and deadlines for demat and non-demat modes.
- Investors wishing to cancel systematic registrations in transferor schemes must submit requests within applicable timelines.

## Important Dates

- Exit Load Waiver Period: August 12, 2026, to September 10, 2026
- Demat Subscription Cut-off: September 09, 2026
- Non-Demat Subscription Cut-off: September 10, 2026
- Transferor Schemes Cessation: After business hours on September 10, 2026
- Merger Effective Date: After business hours on September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Unitholders of the transferor schemes are impacted and provided with options to redeem/switch without exit load or migrate their investments and systematic facilities to the transferee scheme.
