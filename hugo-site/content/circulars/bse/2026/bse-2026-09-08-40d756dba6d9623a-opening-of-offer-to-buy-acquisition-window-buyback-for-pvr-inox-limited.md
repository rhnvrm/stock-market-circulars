## Summary

PVR Inox Limited has announced a buyback of up to 20,68,965 fully paid-up equity shares of face value INR 10 each, representing 2.11% of the total paid-up equity share capital as of March 31, 2026. The buyback will be executed through the 'Tender Offer' process in the acquisition window at a price of Rs. 1,450 per equity share, aggregating to a maximum amount of Rs. 300 crores.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** PVR Inox Limited
- **Buyback Size:** Up to 20,68,965 equity shares (2.11% of total paid-up equity share capital)
- **Buyback Price:** Rs. 1,450/- per equity share payable in cash
- **Maximum Amount:** Rs. 300,00,00,000/- (excluding transaction costs)
- **Record Date:** Friday, September 4, 2026
- **Offer Period:** Thursday, September 10, 2026, to Thursday, September 17, 2026 (Excluding SEBI Holiday on Monday, September 14, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

- Executed pursuant to SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 along with relevant Exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must participate according to the Revised Guidelines of SEBI and BSE exchange notices for tender-offer acquisitions.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** September 4, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** September 10, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** September 17, 2026 (Excluding September 14, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Provides eligible shareholders an opportunity to tender their shares at a premium through the stock exchange acquisition window.
