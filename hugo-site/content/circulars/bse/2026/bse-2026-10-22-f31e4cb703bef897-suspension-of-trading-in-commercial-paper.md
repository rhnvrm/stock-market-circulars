## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers of several companies due to fixed record dates for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of the specified companies as record dates for redemption have been fixed.
- Affected companies include Standard Chartered Securities (India) Limited, Muthoot Housing Finance Company Limited, ICICI Home Finance Company Limited, UGRO Capital Limited, Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited, Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, Mizuho Capsave Finance Private Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Mirae Asset Financial Services (India) Private Limited, Grasim Industries Ltd, and Godrej Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the Commercial Papers of the listed companies from the respective ex-dates/record dates onwards.

## Important Dates

- Record dates and Ex-dates range from October 27, 2026, to November 4, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified Commercial Papers is suspended temporarily or permanently depending on redemption schedules, impacting secondary market liquidity for these instruments.
