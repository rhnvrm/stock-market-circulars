## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment, or move to lower stages/out of the framework effective September 21/22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying under ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 22, 2026, on all open positions as on September 21, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Affected securities will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 22, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 21, 2026.
- Review is conducted on a periodical basis, and shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance (not an adverse action against companies).

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of specific securities under the ESM framework with a 100% margin requirement and movement to Trade-for-Trade settlement.
- Movement of certain securities to lower Stage I or out of the ESM framework entirely.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Stage II / Movement:** September 21, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade:** September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for securities listed under the ESM framework may be impacted due to higher margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
