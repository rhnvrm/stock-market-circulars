## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction relating to Rolling Settlement No. DR-737/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on Monday, Oct 19, 2026.
- Auction Matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction is scheduled for Wednesday, Oct 21, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine auction schedule notice under established settlement mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and Custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate; no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Members should refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the "Auction offer Entry Screen" and corporate action notifications (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 19, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: October 21, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational process governing settlement defaults and auction purchases for BSE rolling settlements.
