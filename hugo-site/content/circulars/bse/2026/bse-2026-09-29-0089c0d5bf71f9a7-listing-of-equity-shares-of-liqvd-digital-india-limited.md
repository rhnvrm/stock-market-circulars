## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Liqvd Digital India Limited on the SME segment effective from September 30, 2026. The scrip will be in the 'MT' Group of Securities and subject to trade-for-trade segment restrictions for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Liqvd Digital India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544946
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: LIQVD
- ISIN: INE0CJT01025
- Total Securities: 2,22,63,177 Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 54/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/-, Premium Rs. 49/-)
- Market Lot: 2,000 Equity Shares
- Segment: SME ('MT' Group)
- Date of Allotment: September 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.
- Part of Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in the minimum market lot of 2,000 shares in demat form.
- Corporate actions for locking in shares have been executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 28, 2026
- Listing and Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members can trade shares of Liqvd Digital India Limited in the SME segment under the scrip code 544946 with standard SME liquidity and trade-for-trade safeguards for initial trading days.
