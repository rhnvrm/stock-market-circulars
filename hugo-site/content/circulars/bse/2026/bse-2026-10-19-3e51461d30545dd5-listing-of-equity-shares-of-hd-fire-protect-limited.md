## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, October 21, 2026, the equity shares of HD Fire Protect Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: HD Fire Protect Limited
- Scrip Code: 544994
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: HDFIRE
- ISIN No: INE18AK01028
- Total Securities: 175,230,000 Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 271/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/-, Premium Rs. 266/-)
- Market Lot: 1
- Date of Allotment: October 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory policy changes introduced; routine listing notice under SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Wednesday, October 21, 2026.
- Lock-in period has been marked in the depository's records for demat shares as per Annexures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Date of Allotment: October 16, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 21, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of HD Fire Protect Limited shares on the BSE exchange, expanding investment opportunities and liquidity for shareholders.
