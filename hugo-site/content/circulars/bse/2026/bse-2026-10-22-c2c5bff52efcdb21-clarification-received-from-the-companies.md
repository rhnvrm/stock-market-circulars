## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing companies that have submitted clarifications regarding recent significant price and volume movements in their scrips, as well as listing companies whose replies are currently awaited. This surveillance measure ensures investors and trading members have access to relevant information.

## Key Points

- **Clarifications Received:** Replies were received from 7 companies including Ashapuri Gold Ornament Ltd, Comfort Intech Ltd, Dudani Retail Ltd, Fine Line Circuits Ltd, Fractal Analytics Ltd, Pashupati Cotspin Ltd, and Rapicut Carbides Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited:** Responses are pending from 22 listed entities including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, El Forge Ltd, and others.
- **Surveillance Objective:** Aimed at maintaining market integrity and ensuring timely disclosure of material information to investors following abnormal price or volume volatility.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory rules or framework changes introduced; part of ongoing surveillance operations under BSE Surveillance Department.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed companies experiencing significant price/volume movements must submit timely clarifications to the Exchange as requested by Surveillance.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 22 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances market transparency and investor protection by publicly tracking company responses to unusual market activity.
