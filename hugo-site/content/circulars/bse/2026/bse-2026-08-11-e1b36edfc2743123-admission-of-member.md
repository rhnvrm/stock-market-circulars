## Summary

BSE Ltd. has informed trading members that specific applicants have been admitted as Trading Members or granted additional segment memberships on the exchange, subject to its Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Soaring Peaks Capital Services Private Limited** (Member No. 6994) admitted for New Membership in Cash/Equity and Derivatives segments.
- **Promore Broking Private Limited** (Member No. 6995) admitted for New Membership in Cash/Equity and Derivatives segments.
- **NK Securities Research Pvt. Ltd.** (Member No. 6638) granted Additional Segment Membership in Commodity Derivatives.
- **Marwadi Shares & Finance Ltd** (Member No. 910) granted Additional Segment Membership in the Debt Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members with queries regarding the admissions may contact the Membership Department via email at membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 11 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update reflecting changes in exchange membership roster with no direct impact on general market participants or stock prices.
