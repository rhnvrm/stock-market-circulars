## Summary

Motilal Oswal AMC has announced a revision in the minimum Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) investment amount for the Motilal Oswal Special Opportunities Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective with immediate effect on September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: Motilal Oswal Special Opportunities Fund
- Frequency: Daily
- Revised Minimum Amount: Rs. 21 and in multiples of Re.1/-
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational update regarding scheme-specific investment parameters initiated by the AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and distributors are advised to take note of the revised SIP criteria and ensure transaction execution aligns with the scheme-wise details provided in the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate effect (28 Sep 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to investors and distributors transacting in the daily SIP facility for Motilal Oswal Special Opportunities Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform.
