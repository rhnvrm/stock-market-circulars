## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities of Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Ltd. In continuation of earlier notice dated November 04, 2025, the specified scrip code and ISIN will be suspended for trading effective Monday, August 17, 2026, on account of debt restructuring.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 977267
- **ISIN:** INE647U08039
- **Reason for Suspension:** Debt restructuring
- **Effective Date:** August 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an operational notice enforcing trading suspension pursuant to corporate debt restructuring.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the suspension and ensure no fresh trades are executed in the affected scrip code and ISIN from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Effective Date of Suspension:** August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity in the specific debt securities of Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Ltd will be temporarily halted on the exchange platform, impacting investors holding positions in Scrip Code 977267.
