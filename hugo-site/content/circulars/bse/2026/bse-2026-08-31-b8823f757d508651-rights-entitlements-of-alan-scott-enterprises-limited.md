## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the commencement of trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of ALAN SCOTT ENTERPRISES LIMITED effective from September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of Company: ALAN SCOTT ENTERPRISES LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 751133
- Scrip ID: ALAN-RE2
- ISIN No.: INE273F20030
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: September 01, 2026 to September 09, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: September 01, 2026 to September 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares. A disclaimer must be included in contract notes/bills regarding the nature of REs and the requirement to apply or renounce before the issue closes.

## Important Dates

- Trading Period for Renunciation Starts: September 01, 2026
- Trading Period for Renunciation Closes: September 09, 2026
- Rights Issue Opens: September 01, 2026
- Rights Issue Closes: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and market participants to trade or renounce rights entitlements for Alan Scott Enterprises Limited on a T+1 rolling settlement basis under trade-for-trade category.
