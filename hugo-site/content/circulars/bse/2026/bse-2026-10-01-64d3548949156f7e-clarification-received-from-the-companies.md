## Summary

The BSE has observed significant price and volume movements in several scrips in the recent past. To ensure trading members and investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the concerned companies. This notice lists the companies that have submitted their replies as well as those whose responses are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- **Replies Received:** Clarifications have been received from 19 companies, including Alicon Castalloy Ltd, Astec Lifesciences Ltd, Firstsource Solutions Ltd, and JSW Holdings Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited:** Responses are still pending from 23 companies, including Deep Polymers Ltd, Nettlinx Ltd, and Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd.
- **Objective:** Maintain market transparency and ensure investors are informed of any material developments affecting scrip prices and volumes.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine surveillance communication issued under exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies flagged for significant price/volume movements must furnish necessary clarifications and disclosures to the Exchange promptly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 01 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market integrity and investor protection by enforcing timely clarifications regarding abnormal stock price and volume behavior.
