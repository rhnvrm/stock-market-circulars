## Summary

Trading members are informed that 94,05,655 equity shares of Eco Hotels And Resorts Ltd. (Scrip Code: 514402) issued pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up on a rights basis are permitted for trading with effect from July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Eco Hotels And Resorts Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 514402
- ISIN: INE638N01012
- Details of Securities: 94,05,655 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued on rights basis (Distinctive Numbers: 51751136 – 61156790).
- Effective Date: Friday, July 31, 2026.
- Ranking: These new shares are pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the inclusion of these newly converted fully paid-up shares for regular trading.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital structure adjustment reflecting the conversion of partly paid-up shares into fully paid-up shares.
