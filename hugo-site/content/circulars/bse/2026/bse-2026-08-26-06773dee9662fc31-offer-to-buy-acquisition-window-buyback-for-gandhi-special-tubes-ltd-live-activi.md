## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the live activities schedule and operational parameters for the Offer to Buy (Buyback) of equity shares of Gandhi Special Tubes Ltd. The buyback acquisition window is scheduled from Thursday, August 27, 2026, to Wednesday, September 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** GANDHI SPECIAL TUBES LTD
- **Security Symbol:** GANDHITUBE
- **ISIN:** INE524B01027
- **Scrip Code:** 513108
- **Offer Size:** Upto 8,68,100 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 900.00 per share
- **Opening Date:** Thursday, 27th August 2026
- **Closing Date:** Wednesday, 2nd September 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Face Value:** Rs. 5.00

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action trading schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bidding is permitted for investors holding shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- **Opening Date:** 27th August 2026
- **Closing Date:** 2nd September 2026
- **Bid Offering Period:** 27th August 2026 to 2nd September 2026 (9:15 AM to 3:30 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates market participants and trading members in executing buyback bids within the designated acquisition window on the BSE iBBS platform.
