## Summary

S.I. Capital and Financial Services Limited has announced the record date for its upcoming rights issue of equity shares. Trading members are informed that transactions in the equity shares of the company will be on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: S.I. Capital and Financial Services Limited (Scrip Code: 530907)
- Record Date: 31/07/2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: 31/07/2026 (Settlement No. DR- 684/2026-2027)
- Rights Ratio: 03 (Three) Equity Shares of Rs.10/- each for cash at par for every 02 (Two) Equity Shares held.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the record date and ex-rights trading schedule for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 31/07/2026
- Ex-Rights Date: 31/07/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification for trading members regarding the rights issue adjustments for S.I. Capital and Financial Services Limited.
