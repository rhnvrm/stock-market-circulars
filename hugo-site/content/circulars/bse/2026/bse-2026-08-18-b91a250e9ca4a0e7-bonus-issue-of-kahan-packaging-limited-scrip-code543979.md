## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the corporate action of Kahan Packaging Limited (Scrip Code: 543979), announcing a bonus issue of equity shares in the ratio of 3:1 along with a corresponding revision in the trading market lot effective from August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Bonus Ratio:** 03 (Three) Bonus Equity Shares of Rs.10/- each for every 01 (One) existing Equity Share of Rs.10/- each held.
- **Record Date:** August 20, 2026.
- **Ex-Bonus Date:** August 20, 2026.
- **Market Lot Change:** Revised from 1600 to 6400 effective August 20, 2026.
- **Total Allotment:** 8,160,000 Equity Shares to be allotted as on August 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the change in the market lot and ensure proper execution of transactions on an ex-bonus basis starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Record Date / Ex-Bonus Date:** August 20, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares of Kahan Packaging Limited as of the record date will receive bonus shares. The market lot change ensures appropriate liquidity and lot sizing following the price adjustment due to the bonus issue.
