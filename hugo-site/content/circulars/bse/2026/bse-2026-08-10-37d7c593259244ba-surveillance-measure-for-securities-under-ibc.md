## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are listed in the attached annexures with effect from August 11, 2026.
- Framework includes securities moving into the framework (Annexure I), moving out of the framework (Annexure II), consolidated list (Annexure III), and those classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% (Annexure IV).
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the company.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic review of securities placed under the IBC framework.
- Reinstatement of price bands for securities moving out of the framework, unless subject to other surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and members must note the surveillance status and price band applicability for affected securities.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 11, 2026
- Notice Date: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading participants holding or trading securities undergoing IRP must account for surveillance measures and potential price band adjustments.
