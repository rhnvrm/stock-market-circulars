## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd. has announced the introduction of a new 24L series in the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) segment as the existing 3L series approaches exhaustion. With this introduction, the token length will change from 6 digits to 7 digits.

## Key Points

- Existing token series under the 3L series in the SLB segment is nearing exhaustion.
- A new 24L series is introduced.
- Token length changes from 6 digits to 7 digits.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of the 24L series and modification of token length for SLB segment instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and participants must update their back-office systems and processes to accommodate the new 7-digit token length.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 01-09-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants need to modify their internal trading and back-office systems to handle the extended token length without disruption.
