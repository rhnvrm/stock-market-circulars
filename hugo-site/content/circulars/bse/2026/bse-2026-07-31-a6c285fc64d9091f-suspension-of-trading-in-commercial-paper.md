## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) for multiple companies as they have fixed record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260731-44, dated July 31, 2026, issued by the Listing Operations Department.
- Covers multiple companies including Adani Enterprises Ltd., The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd., L&T Metro Rail (Hyderabad) Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, 360 ONE Prime Limited, Grasim Industries Ltd., Tata Power Co. Ltd., and others.
- Trading members are instructed not to deal in the commercial papers of the mentioned companies around their record and ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension of trading in specified commercial paper instruments due to impending redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no transactions occur in the listed commercial paper scrips past their respective record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates and Ex-Dates range from August 4, 2026, to August 17, 2026, depending on the specific company issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Prevents secondary market trading in commercial papers that are due for redemption, protecting investors and ensuring orderly settlement processes.
