## Summary

ASK Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for ASK Liquid Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 12, 2026, to August 25, 2026. The tentative allotment date is September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ASK LIQUID FUND
- Scheme Category: LIQUID
- NFO Period: August 12, 2026, to August 25, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 01, 2026
- Lumpsum purchases extended till midnight on August 25, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 28, 2026, for the tentative allotment date of September 01, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments accepted subject to clear funds receipt by 6:00 PM on September 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: August 12, 2026
- NFO End Date: August 25, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: August 28, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to subscribe to the ASK Liquid Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF Platform with specified operational guidelines and timelines.
