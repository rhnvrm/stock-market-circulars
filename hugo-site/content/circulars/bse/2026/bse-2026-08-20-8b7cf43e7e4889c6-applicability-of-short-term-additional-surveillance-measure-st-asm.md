## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for specified securities effective August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%, effective August 24, 2026, on all open positions as on August 21, 2026, and new positions.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable rate of margin is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%, effective August 24, 2026, on all open positions as on August 21, 2026, and new positions.
- Shortlisting is purely for market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and enforcement of surveillance measures under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework in conjunction with existing exchange rules.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must maintain the mandated higher margin rates (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 20 Aug 2026
- Effective Date for Open/New Positions: August 24, 2026 (based on open positions as on August 21, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities under ST-ASM Stage I and II will affect leverage and liquidity for traders holding positions in the affected scrips.
