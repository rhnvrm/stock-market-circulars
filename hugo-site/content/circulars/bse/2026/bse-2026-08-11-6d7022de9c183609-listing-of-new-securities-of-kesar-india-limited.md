## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 80 new equity shares of Kesar India Limited under the 'Kesar India Employee Stock Purchase Scheme – 2025' effective from August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Kesar India Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 543542
- **Security Details:** 80 Equity shares of Rs.10/- each fully paid up.
- **Effective Date:** August 12, 2026
- **ISIN:** INE0L1C01019
- **Lock-in Details:** The new equity shares issued under the ESPS are locked in for a minimum period of one year from the date of allotment (up to July 16, 2027).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with the lock-in requirements stipulated for the ESPS shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 17/07/2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** 12/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Negligible market impact due to the extremely small quantum of shares (80 shares) being listed.
