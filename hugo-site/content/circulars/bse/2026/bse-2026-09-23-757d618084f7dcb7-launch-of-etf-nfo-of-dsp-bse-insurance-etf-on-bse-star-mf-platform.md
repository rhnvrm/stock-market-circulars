## Summary

DSP Mutual Fund is offering the New Fund Offer (NFO) for DSP BSE INSURANCE ETF and DSP BSE INSURANCE ETF - DIRECT PLAN on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from September 24, 2026, to September 28, 2026. The tentative allotment date is September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Names: DSP BSE INSURANCE ETF and DSP BSE INSURANCE ETF - DIRECT PLAN.
- Scheme Category: Equity.
- NFO Window: September 24, 2026, to September 28, 2026 (extended till midnight on September 28, 2026, for lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 30, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 29, 2026, for eligibility.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted subject to timely realization of clear funds.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 24, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 28, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: September 29, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an opportunity to invest in the DSP BSE INSURANCE ETF via the BSE StAR MF Platform with standard operational timelines.
