## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 12,00,000 new equity shares of Novelix Pharmaceuticals Limited issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from October 9, 2026.

- Company: Novelix Pharmaceuticals Limited (Scrip Code: 536565)
- Security Details: 12,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 47/- (Issue Price: Rs. 57/-)
- ISIN: INE314I01036
- Distinctive Numbers: 27270001 to 28470000
- Date of Allotment: 09/07/2026
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 20/04/2027
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares

None
