## Summary

Rajeswari Infrastructure Limited has fixed a record date for the reduction of its equity share capital pursuant to a resolution plan approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Division Bench, Chennai. Trading members are advised not to deal in the equity shares of the company effective from October 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Record Date & Suspension Date:** October 1, 2026.
- **Promoter Share Extinguishment:** All shares held by the promoter/promoters' group shall stand fully extinguished without any payment under the Resolution Plan.
- **Public Share Capital Reconstitution & Consolidation:** Public share capital face value is reduced from Rs. 10/- to Re. 0.20 per share, followed immediately by consolidation into equity shares having a face value of Rs. 10/- each (1 new equity share for every 50 old shares).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of the NCLT-approved resolution plan involving capital restructuring for Rajeswari Infrastructure Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are instructed not to deal in the equity shares of Rajeswari Infrastructure Limited (Scrip Code: 526823) effective from the record date.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date / Suspension Effective Date:** October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Complete extinguishment of promoter shareholding and restructuring of public share capital significantly alter the equity structure and trading status of the company.
