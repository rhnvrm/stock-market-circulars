## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying criteria for inclusion will attract a minimum 100% margin and shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective September 03, 2026. Specific securities are moved to lower or higher stages with applicable price bands and periodic call auctions.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 03, 2026 on open positions as of September 02, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 02, 2026.
- Selected securities are moved to lower Stage I or move out of the ESM framework entirely.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and is not an adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shifting of qualifying securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Price band restrictions and periodic call auctions for specific stages.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure appropriate 100% margin maintenance for affected securities.
- Trading members need to account for shift to Trade-for-Trade settlement and revised price bands.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 01, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage Changes / Moving Out: September 02, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade Settlement: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for securities listed under the ESM framework may be impacted due to stricter margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and price band constraints.
