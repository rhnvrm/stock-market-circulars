## Summary

BSE Limited has announced that the surrender of trading membership by Zerodha Broking Ltd. (Member No. 6498) in the currency derivative segment has been approved by the Exchange with effect from September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member: ZERODHA BROKING LTD. (Member No. 6498)
- Segment: Currency Derivative Segment
- Effective Date: September 01, 2026
- Exchange: BSE Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members having any clarifications may contact the Membership Department via email at membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Surrender: September 01, 2026
- Notice Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to the cessation of trading membership for Zerodha Broking Ltd. within the BSE currency derivative segment.
