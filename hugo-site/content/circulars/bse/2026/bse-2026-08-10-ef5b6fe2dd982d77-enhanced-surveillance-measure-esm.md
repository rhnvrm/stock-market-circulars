## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the inclusion of securities, movement of securities to lower stages (Stage I), and securities moving out of the framework. Securities qualifying under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin and are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM and attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 12, 2026 on all open positions as on August 11, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but are moved to a lower stage (Stage I) w.e.f. August 11, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 11, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the ESM framework is given in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shifting of qualifying securities from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II adjustments and moving out of framework: August 11, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin requirement and shift to Trade-for-Trade: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (100%) and trade-for-trade settlement impact liquidity and trading flexibility for securities listed under the ESM framework.
