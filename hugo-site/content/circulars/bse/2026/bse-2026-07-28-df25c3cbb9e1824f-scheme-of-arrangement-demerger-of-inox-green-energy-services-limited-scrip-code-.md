## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Scheme of Arrangement (Demerger) between Inox Green Energy Services Limited and Inox Renewable Solutions Ltd, following approval from the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, Ahmedabad Bench. The power evacuation business of Inox Green Energy Services Limited is being demerged into Inox Renewable Solutions Ltd.

## Key Points

- **Demerged Company:** Inox Green Energy Services Limited (Scrip Code: 543667)
- **Resulting Company:** Inox Renewable Solutions Ltd
- **Record Date:** August 1, 2026
- **Ex-Date / Entitlement Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Share Entitlement Ratio:** 122 Equity Shares (Face Value Rs. 10/- each) of Inox Renewable Solutions Ltd for every 1000 Equity Shares (Face Value Rs. 10/- each) held in Inox Green Energy Services Limited.
- **Segment Transfer:** Trading in equity shares of Inox Green Energy Services Limited will be transferred from 'A' Group to 'T' Group w.e.f. July 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer of trading group from 'A' to 'T' pursuant to SEBI circular guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the record date, ex-date, and share entitlement ratio for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 28, 2026
- **Ex-Date / Group Transfer Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Record Date:** August 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders of Inox Green Energy Services Limited as of the record date are entitled to receive shares of the resulting company based on the specified swap ratio. Trading of the scrip moves to the trade-for-trade segment.
