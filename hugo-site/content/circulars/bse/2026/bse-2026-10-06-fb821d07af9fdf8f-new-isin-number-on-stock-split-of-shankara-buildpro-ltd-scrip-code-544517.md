## Summary

BSE has announced the new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of Shankara Buildpro Ltd (Scrip Code: 544517) following a stock split from face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company: Shankara Buildpro Ltd
- Scrip Code: 544517
- Action: Stock sub-division from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-
- New ISIN Number: INE24OJ01029
- Reference to previous exchange notice: No. 20260917-4 dated September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Assignment of new ISIN for the sub-divided equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are informed of the new ISIN for trades in the company's equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: October 08, 2026 (DR- 731/2026-2027)
- Notice Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures seamless settlement and identification of equity shares post stock split under the new ISIN.
