## Summary

BSE has announced the non-competitive bidding process for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for August 11, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated August 07, 2026. Trading members can participate through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Bid Collection Start Date: August 10, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability).
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): August 10, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): August 11, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- Auction Date: August 11, 2026.
- Settlement Date: August 12, 2026.
- Includes various new and re-issued SDL securities from states such as Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Meghalaya, Punjab, and Rajasthan.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the SDL auction must submit bids within the stipulated timelines through the NCB-GSec module of the iBBS platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 10, 2026
- Bid Start Date: August 10, 2026
- Auction Date: August 11, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors an opportunity to bid for State Government Securities through the non-competitive bidding facility.
