## Summary

SBI Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the SBI Nifty200 Value 30 ETF FOF along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from September 17, 2026, to September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: SBI Nifty200 Value 30 ETF FOF (Regular and Direct Plans for Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options).
- NFO Window: September 17, 2026, to September 30, 2026 (extended till midnight on September 30, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 07, 2026.
- SIP Minimum Installment Criteria: Rs. 500 to Rs. 999 requires 12 installments (Monthly); Rs. 1,000 and above requires 6 installments (Monthly).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 07, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on September 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: September 17, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: September 30, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors access to subscribe to the new SBI mutual fund scheme and associated SIP facilities through the BSE StAR MF platform.
