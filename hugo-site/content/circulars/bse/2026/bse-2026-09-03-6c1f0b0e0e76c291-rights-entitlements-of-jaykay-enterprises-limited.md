## Summary

Trading members of the exchange are informed that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of JAYKAY ENTERPRISES LIMITED are permitted on the exchange with effect from Monday, September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: JAYKAY ENTERPRISES LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Trading period for Renunciation of REs: September 07, 2026 to September 15, 2026
- Scrip Code: 751140
- Scrip ID: JAYKAY-RE1
- ISIN No.: INE903A20025
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Rights Issue Period: September 07, 2026 to September 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs, ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares, and include a mandatory disclaimer in contract notes informing clients about the rights, renunciation, and lapse conditions.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Start Date: September 07, 2026
- RE Trading End Date: September 15, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: September 07, 2026
- Rights Issue Close Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables shareholders and eligible investors to trade or renounce rights entitlements for JAYKAY ENTERPRISES LIMITED under T+1 rolling settlement on a trade-for-trade basis.
