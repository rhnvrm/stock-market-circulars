## Summary

SEBI vide its order no. WTM/KV/CFID/CFID-SEC2/32656/2026-27 dated August 10, 2026, has confirmed the ad-interim directions issued vide the Interim Order dated October 14, 2025, against the noticees in the matter of Nirman Agri Genetics Limited.

## Key Points

- Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20251014-61 dated October 14, 2025.
- SEBI confirmed ad-interim directions via order dated August 10, 2026.
- Effective immediately from the date of the order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Confirmation of existing ad-interim restrictions and directions on the noticees involved.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and concerned entities are advised to take note of the order and ensure strict compliance.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 10, 2026
- Effective Date: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures ongoing adherence to regulatory restrictions placed on the entities involved in the matter.
