## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding SEBI's order cancelling the Certificates of Registration of certain Research Analysts due to non-payment of prescribed renewal fees under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014. Consequently, the BSE enlistment granted to these Research Analysts also stands cancelled.

## Key Points

- SEBI cancelled certificates of registration for specific Research Analysts for non-payment of renewal fees.
- BSE enlistment for these analysts stands cancelled in view of SEBI's order.
- Affected entities include Mitesh Thacker, Rahul Lala (Greentix Research), and Sunil Kumar Dhavala.

## Regulatory Changes

Cancellation of registration under SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014.

## Compliance Requirements

Cancelled research analysts are prohibited from acting as research analysts and must cease regulated activities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 25 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact as this is an administrative cancellation for non-payment of fees affecting specific individual analysts.
