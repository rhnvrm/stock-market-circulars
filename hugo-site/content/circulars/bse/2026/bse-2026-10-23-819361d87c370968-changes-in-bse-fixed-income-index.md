## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced constituent changes for the BSE 4-8 Year G-Sec Index, effective October 30, 2026. Other fixed income indices such as the BSE 8-13 Year G-Sec Index, BSE India 5 Year Sovereign Bond Index, and BSE India 10 Year Sovereign Bond Index remain unchanged.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 30, 2026.
- Index Affected: BSE 4-8 Year G-Sec Index.
- Additions: 7.18% GS 2033 (ISIN: IN0020230085) and 6.57% GS 2033 (ISIN: IN0020260066).
- Drops: 6.54% GS 2032 (ISIN: IN0020210244) and 6.68% GS 2033 (ISIN: IN0020250133).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine index rebalancing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and fund managers tracking the BSE 4-8 Year G-Sec Index should incorporate these constituent updates into their tracking portfolios and benchmarks.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Routine index rebalancing with minimal direct impact on equity markets; relevant for fixed income portfolios, gilt funds, and debt index trackers.
