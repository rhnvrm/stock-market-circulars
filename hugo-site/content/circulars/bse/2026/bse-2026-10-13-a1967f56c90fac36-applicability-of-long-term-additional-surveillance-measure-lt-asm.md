## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision of stages, group transfers, and removal of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The framework is designed to curb excessive volatility and market manipulation in specific scrips.

## Key Points

- 100% margins become applicable effective October 16, 2026, on all open positions as on October 15, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moving to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework and lower price bands are effective from October 14, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective October 16, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective October 14, 2026, will have their price bands reinstated.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions and serves as a surveillance measure rather than an adverse regulatory action.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and segment/group transfers for short-listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and brokers must ensure adherence to 100% margin requirements, monitor price band restrictions, and manage trading positions in accordance with group transfers for securities specified in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- October 14, 2026: Effective date for higher stages, lower price bands, and exit from LT-ASM for respective scrips.
- October 15, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- October 16, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS Groups).

## Impact Assessment

High impact on liquidity and trading operations for securities placed under surveillance stages due to 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions.
