## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are issued on a private placement basis and traded in dematerialised form.
- Standard denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Companies listed include Aditya Birla Lifestyle Brands Limited, Bharat Petroleum Corpn. Ltd., Time Technoplast Limited, Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, and Mizuho Capsave Finance Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard debt segment guidelines and dematerialised settlement rules.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 14, 2026
- Allotment and Redemption dates vary across instruments (ranging from August 2026 to November 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice with minimal direct impact on general equity market participants.
