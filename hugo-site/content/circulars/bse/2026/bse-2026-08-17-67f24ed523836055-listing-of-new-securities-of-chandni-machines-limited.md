## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that new securities of Chandni Machines Limited (Scrip Code - 542627) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Tuesday, August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Chandni Machines Limited
- Scrip Code: 542627
- ISIN: INE01GZ01011
- Security Details: 38,10,900 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 42.50/- to Non-Promoters on a preferential basis.
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.
- Distinctive Numbers: 3227434 to 7038333
- Date of Allotment: 30/01/2026
- Issue Price: Rs. 52.50/-

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the lock-in details and distribution numbers for the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Trading: 18/08/2026
- Date of Allotment: 30/01/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 20/02/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing of preferential shares for Chandni Machines Limited.
