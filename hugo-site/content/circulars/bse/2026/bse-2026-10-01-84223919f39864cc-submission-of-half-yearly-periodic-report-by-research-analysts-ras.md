## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding all enlisted Research Analysts (RAs) to submit their Half-Yearly Periodic Report for the period ended September 30, 2026. The submission must be completed through the designated portal between October 1, 2026, and October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Reporting Period:** Half-yearly period ended September 30, 2026.
- **Portal:** Accessible at https://iaraportal.sebi.gov.in/.
- **Credentials:** RAs must use existing login credentials created during registration; new credential creation is not permitted.
- **Submission Window:** October 01, 2026 to October 30, 2026.
- **Support & Queries:** Contact iara.compliance@bseindia.com or call 022-22725680 / 5854.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic reporting mandate compliance update for Research Analysts.

## Compliance Requirements

All enlisted Research Analysts must carefully follow the user manual and submit the periodic report through the BSE/SEBI portal on or before October 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Opening Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Deadline Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Period Ended:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and continuous oversight of registered Research Analysts.
