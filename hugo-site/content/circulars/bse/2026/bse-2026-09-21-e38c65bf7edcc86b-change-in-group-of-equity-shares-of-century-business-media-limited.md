## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Century Business Media Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective from Tuesday, October 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Century Business Media Limited
- Scrip Code: 544928
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares are moved out of the Trade for Trade segment into the normal rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to take note of the segment shift for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normalizes trading for the scrip under the M Group rolling segment.
