## Summary

Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund is introducing its Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) offering, the MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund, on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The New Fund Offer (NFO) runs from September 30, 2026, to October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth and IDCW options).
- **NFO Period:** September 30, 2026, to October 14, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 14, 2026 for Lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 22, 2026.
- **ISINs:** Regular Growth (INF174V30019), Regular IDCW Reinvestment (INF174V30035), Regular IDCW Payout (INF174V30027), Direct Growth (INF174V30043), Direct IDCW Reinvestment (INF174V30068), Direct IDCW Payout (INF174V30050).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational circular regarding NFO launch on the platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 22, 2026, for allotment eligibility.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted during the NFO period subject to timely fund realization.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** September 30, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Clear Funds Deadline:** October 22, 2026 (by 6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscription and trading of Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund's new Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) scheme for market participants and investors on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
