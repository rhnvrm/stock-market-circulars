## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd. is providing a facility for custodians to disseminate information regarding Custodial Participant (CP) codes for which clearing and settlement activities will not be carried out. The list of inactive CP codes from all custodians (both BSECL and NCL) will be made available to members via an Inactive CP file starting October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Custodians can upload information regarding inactive CP codes using the newly provided upload facility and user manual.
- The Inactive CP file will be available to members by 08:00 PM on the (T-1) day.
- File Path: Home > Common > Month > Current Date.
- File nomenclature, format, and sample files are detailed in Annexure A.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of a formal communication channel for custodians to report inactive CP codes to clearing corporations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Custodians are required to update inactive CP code information using the designated portal/user manual.
- Trading members should access and review the Inactive CP file by 08:00 PM on (T-1) day to ensure accurate clearing and settlement execution.

## Important Dates

- Facility and Member File Availability: 05-Oct-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational transparency and reduces settlement risks by ensuring trading members are promptly informed of inactive CP codes before trade execution and clearing cycles.
