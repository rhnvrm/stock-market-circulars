## Summary

SEBI has cancelled the Certificates of Registration of certain Research Analysts due to the non-payment of prescribed renewal fees under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014. Consequently, the enlistment granted to these entities by BSE Limited also stands cancelled.

## Key Points

- SEBI orders issued between July 2026 cancelling registrations for non-payment of renewal fees.
- BSE enlistment for the affected research analysts is terminated accordingly.
- A total of 16 research analysts and entities are listed in Annexure A.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of cancellation of registration under SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 for default in renewal fee payments.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed research analysts are barred from acting as research analysts following the cancellation of their SEBI registration and BSE enlistment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 14 August 2026
- SEBI Order Dates: Ranging from 09-07-2026 to 28-07-2026

## Impact Assessment

The named entities lose their authorization to operate as research analysts on the exchange platform.
