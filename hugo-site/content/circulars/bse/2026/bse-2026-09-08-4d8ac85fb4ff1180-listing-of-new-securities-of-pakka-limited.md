## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Pakka Limited (Scrip Code: 516030) effective from September 9, 2026. A total of 27,20,000 equity shares were issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Pakka Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 516030
- **ISIN:** INE551D01018
- **Security Details:** 27,20,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 100/- (Issue Price: Rs. 110.00/- per share) on a preferential basis to non-promoters.
- **Ranking:** The new shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 44948101 to 47668100
- **Date of Allotment:** 09/06/2026
- **Lock-in Details:** 27,20,000 shares are under lock-in up to 31/03/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard exchange compliance for listing and trading of newly allotted preferential shares.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Trading:** September 9, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** June 9, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** March 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Involves the expansion of the equity capital base of Pakka Limited through a preferential allotment with standard lock-in provisions.
