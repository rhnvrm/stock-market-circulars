## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has notified the listing and trading approval of additional equity shares issued under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) and Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) by 14 listed companies. These securities will be admitted for trading with effect from Thursday, August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Admissions to Trading:** A total of 14 companies have listed further new shares issued under their employee option plans.
- **Trading Effective Date:** Thursday, August 13, 2026.
- **Lock-in Period:** Metropolis Healthcare Limited has lock-in requirements as specified in Annexure 1. For all other companies, there are no lock-in restrictions (Lock-in: NA).
- **Key Listed Securities:**
  1. **CARE Ratings Limited:** 6,766 shares (ISIN: INE752H01013, Scrip: 534804, Face Value: ₹10)
  2. **Cipla Limited:** 7,850 shares (ISIN: INE059A01026, Scrip: 500087, Face Value: ₹2)
  3. **Cohance Lifesciences Limited:** 30,190 shares (ISIN: INE03QK01018, Scrip: 543064, Face Value: ₹1)
  4. **EPL Limited:** 16,500 shares (ISIN: INE255A01020, Scrip: 500135, Face Value: ₹2)
  5. **Federal Bank Limited:** 1,502,976 shares (ISIN: INE171A01029, Scrip: 500469, Face Value: ₹2)
  6. **Hero MotoCorp Limited:** 48,269 shares (ISIN: INE158A01026, Scrip: 500182, Face Value: ₹2)
  7. **ICICI Bank Limited:** 300,684 shares (ISIN: INE090A01021, Scrip: 532174, Face Value: ₹2)
  8. **Kirloskar Oil Engines Limited:** 13,556 shares (ISIN: INE146L01010, Scrip: 533293, Face Value: ₹2)
  9. **Larsen & Tourbo Limited:** 136,908 shares (ISIN: INE018A01030, Scrip: 500510, Face Value: ₹2)
  10. **Marico Limited:** 1,451 shares (ISIN: INE196A01026, Scrip: 531642, Face Value: ₹1)
  11. **Meesho Limited:** 1,186,589 shares (ISIN: INE0VDM01015, Scrip: 544632, Face Value: ₹1)
  12. **Metropolis Healthcare Limited:** 36,900 shares (ISIN: INE112L01020, Scrip: 542650, Face Value: ₹2, Lock-in: As per Annexure 1)
  13. **Suzlon Energy Limited:** 1,823,720 shares (ISIN: INE040H01021, Scrip: 532667, Face Value: ₹2)
  14. **Waaree Renewable Technologies Limited:** 33,425 shares (ISIN: INE299N01021, Scrip: 534618, Face Value: ₹2)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced in this circular. The approvals are granted under existing SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations and exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Exchanges and Depositories:** Must credit and update trading limits for the newly listed securities.
- **Metropolis Healthcare Limited:** Must adhere to the lock-in schedules detailed in Annexure 1.
- **Trading Members:** Take notice of the newly listed equity shares available for active trading.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date of Trading:** August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low. This is a routine capital addition representing minimal dilution to overall equity across the mentioned firms, resulting in negligible market or price impact.
