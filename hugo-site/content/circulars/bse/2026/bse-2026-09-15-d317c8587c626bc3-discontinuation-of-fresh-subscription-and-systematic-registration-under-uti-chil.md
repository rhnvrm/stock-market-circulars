## Summary

BSE has announced the discontinuation of fresh subscriptions, lumpsum investments, switch-ins, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP) under the UTI Childrens Hybrid Fund (Growth option) on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from the close of business hours on September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh subscriptions, additional purchases, switch-in transactions, and new SIP/XSIP registrations for UTI Childrens Hybrid Fund (Growth option) are disabled.
- Applications received after the cut-off time on September 15, 2026, will not be accepted or processed.
- Existing SIP/XSIP registrations made prior to the effective date will continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA level.
- Redemptions, switch-outs, and Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP) remain allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the suspension and ensure no new fresh subscriptions or systematic registrations are entered for the scheme after the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2026 (Effective from closer of business September 15, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Investors will no longer be able to make fresh investments or start new SIPs in the UTI Childrens Hybrid Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform, though existing SIPs and redemption facilities remain unaffected.
