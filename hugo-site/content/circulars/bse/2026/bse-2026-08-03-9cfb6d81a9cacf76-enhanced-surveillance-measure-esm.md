## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying inclusion criteria, securities moving to lower stages (Stage I), and securities moving out of the framework entirely. Effective August 5, 2026, applicable securities will attract a minimum 100% margin and shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 5, 2026, on open positions as of August 4, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II face Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 4, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Review of securities under the framework is conducted on a periodical basis.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of securities meeting ESM criteria under stringent surveillance measures.
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure appropriate 100% margins are maintained for impacted securities.
- Trading members need to account for the shift of qualifying securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II / Shifting / Moving Out: August 4, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin / Trade-for-Trade Shifting: August 5, 2026
- Notice Date: August 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade restrictions will significantly affect liquidity and trading mechanics for the shortlisted securities.
