## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debentures and bonds due to the fixation of record dates for payment of interest and redemption by various companies.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261014-18, dated October 14, 2026.
- Segment: Debt Segment / Listing Operations.
- Affected companies include Sammaan Capital Limited, Muthoot Homefin (India) Limited, L&T Finance Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, Indel Money Limited, Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, JM Financial Products Limited, Paisalo Digital Limited, Hinduja Leyland Finance Limited, and MAS Financial Services Limited.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures/bonds on or after the respective record/ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine administrative notice regarding corporate debt actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with trading restrictions in the specified NCDs/bonds as per the announced record dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026
- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: Span across late October and early November 2026 (e.g., October 23, 2026, to November 1, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational impact for debt market participants holding or trading the specified non-convertible debentures and bonds.
