## Summary

In continuation of Exchange notice no. 20260806-42 dated August 06, 2026, BSE has announced that several securities are eligible to move out of the trade-for-trade category.

## Key Points

- Multiple securities across various groups (T, XT) are moving to regular settlement groups (B, X).
- The revision facilitates normal trading for the specified scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of trade-for-trade restrictions for the listed securities, enabling regular rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to note the category changes and adjust their trading operations accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced liquidity and normal market mechanics for the listed scrips as they move out of the restrictive trade-for-trade segment.
