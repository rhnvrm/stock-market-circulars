## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by various companies effective July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are listed on the BSE Debt Segment and traded in dematerialised form.
- Standard denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuers include Nuvama Clearing Services Limited, Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Capri Global Capital Limited, and Adani Enterprises Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted in dematerialised form under the specified ISIN numbers and standard denominations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- Allotment Dates: July 29, 2026 and July 30, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from October 2026 to March 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update facilitating trading of newly issued commercial papers.
