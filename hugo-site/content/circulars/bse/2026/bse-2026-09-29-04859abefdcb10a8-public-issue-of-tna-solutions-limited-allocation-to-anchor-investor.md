## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 15,36,000 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of TNA Solutions Limited at a price of Rs 70/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TNA Solutions Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue / IPO
- Anchor Portion Subscribed: 15,36,000 Equity Shares
- Issue Price / Allocation Price: Rs 70/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: September 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the anchor investor allocation details.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding institutional participation and anchor investor allotment ahead of the public issue opening.
