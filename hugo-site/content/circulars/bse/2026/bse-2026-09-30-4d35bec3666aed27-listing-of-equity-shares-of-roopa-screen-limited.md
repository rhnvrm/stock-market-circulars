## Summary

Effective from Thursday, October 1, 2026, the equity shares of Roopa Screen Limited are listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE SME segment under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Roopa Screen Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544954
- **ISIN:** INE2BTZ01011
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** ROOPA
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 64/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-, Premium: Rs. 54/-)
- **Number of Securities:** 11,067,500 Equity Shares
- **Market Lot:** 2,000 equity shares
- **Date of Allotment:** September 29, 2026
- **Market Maker:** B.N. Rathi Securities Limited
- **Registrar:** Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Securities of the company will be traded strictly in dematerialised form in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) scheduled for October 1, 2026. Corporate actions for marking lock-in periods in depository records have been executed.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 29, 2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading for Roopa Screen Limited on the BSE SME platform with standard trade-for-trade risk containment measures for the initial 10 trading sessions.
