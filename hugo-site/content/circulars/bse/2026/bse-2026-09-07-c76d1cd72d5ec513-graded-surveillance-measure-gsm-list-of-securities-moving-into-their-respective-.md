## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, effective September 08, 2026. Trading members and market participants are required to take adequate precautions as these securities will attract stricter surveillance actions and margin requirements as per the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure move to higher GSM stages effective September 08, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, ranging from 100% margin rates and 5% price bands to trade-for-trade settlement and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement for affected securities will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Stage I:** Applicable margin rate of 100% and a price band of 5% or lower.
- **Stage II:** Trade-for-trade with a price band of 5% or lower, plus an Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value to be deposited by buyers.
- **Stage III:** Trade-for-trade with a price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week), and ASD of 100% of trade value.
- **Stage IV:** Trade-for-trade with a price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week with no upward movement, and ASD of 100% of trade value.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must exercise adequate caution while executing trades in the specified securities.
- Ensure compliance with applicable margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement terms, and timely deposition of Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 07 Sep 2026
- **Effective Date:** 08 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market liquidity and trading flexibility for the affected securities will be significantly restricted due to trade-for-trade settlement, weekly trading windows in higher stages, and steep margin or deposit requirements.
