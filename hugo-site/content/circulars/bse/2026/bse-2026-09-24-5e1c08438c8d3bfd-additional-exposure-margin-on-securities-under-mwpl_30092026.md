## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has announced the levy of an additional exposure margin of 15% in the equity derivatives segment for securities where the top 10 clients account for more than 20% of the Market-Wide Position Limit (MWPL).

## Key Points

- Additional exposure margin of 15% levied in equity derivatives segment.
- Applicable to securities where top 10 clients hold more than 20% of MWPL.
- If additional surveillance margin applies, the higher of the two margins will be levied.
- Identification based on 3 months rolling data with monthly reviews.
- Securities specified in the Annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of risk-based additional exposure margins for high concentration securities in the equity derivatives segment based on MWPL utilization.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure adequate margin maintenance for securities specified in the Annexure starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026 (post September 2026 expiry)
- Applicable Until: October 2026 expiry

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the specified derivatives securities will experience higher margin requirements, impacting position sizing and capital allocation.
