## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction regarding Rolling Settlement No. DR-683/2026-27. The auction sessions, matching, and pay-in schedules are detailed for July 31, 2026, and August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on July 31, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction will be held on Monday, August 03, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short-delivered such securities is removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off times.
- Utmost care must be taken while entering security codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: July 31, 2026
- Pay-in Date: August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational procedure for resolving short deliveries via the auction mechanism with no direct material impact on general market liquidity or stock prices.
