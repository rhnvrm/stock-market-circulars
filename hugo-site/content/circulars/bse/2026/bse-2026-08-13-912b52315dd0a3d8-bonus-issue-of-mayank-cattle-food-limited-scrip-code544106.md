## Summary

Mayank Cattle Food Limited has fixed a Record Date for a bonus issue of equity shares. Additionally, the market lot for the company's scrip is revised effective from August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Bonus Issue Ratio: 1 (One) Bonus Equity Share of Rs. 10/- each for every 1 (One) existing Equity Share of Rs. 10/- each held.
- Record Date: August 24, 2026.
- Ex-Bonus Basis Date: August 24, 2026.
- Market Lot Change: Current market lot of 600 is revised to 1200 effective August 24, 2026.
- Allotment: 5,400,000 Equity Shares to be allotted as on August 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the change in market lot and ensure transactions are processed on an ex-bonus basis from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 24, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: August 24, 2026
- Allotment Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders will receive bonus shares in the ratio of 1:1, and the trading lot size on the exchange is doubled from 600 to 1200.
