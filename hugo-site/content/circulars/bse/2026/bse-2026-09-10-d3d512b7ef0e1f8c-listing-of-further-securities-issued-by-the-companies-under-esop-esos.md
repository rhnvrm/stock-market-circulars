## Summary

BSE Limited has notified trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further securities issued by 15 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS). These securities become effective for trading from September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 15
- Effective date for trading: September 11, 2026
- Companies include major entities such as Aditya Birla Capital Limited, AXIS Bank Limited, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, Max Healthcare Institute Limited, and Mphasis Limited.
- All issued shares rank pari passu with existing equity shares of the respective companies.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to take note of the listing of new securities for the mentioned companies.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital for the listed companies resulting from ESOP/ESOS exercises. No direct market-wide impact.
