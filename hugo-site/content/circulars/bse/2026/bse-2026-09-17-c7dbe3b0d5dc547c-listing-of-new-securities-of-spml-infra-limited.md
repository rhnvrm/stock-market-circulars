## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of new securities of SPML Infra Limited (Scrip Code - 500402) effective from September 18, 2026, following a preferential issue to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- 6,93,999 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 184/-.
- Total issue price: Rs. 186/- per share.
- Issued to Non-Promoters on a preferential basis.
- Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- ISIN: INE937A01023

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to lock-in requirements as specified in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 18/07/2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: 18/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the company's equity base through preferential allotment.
