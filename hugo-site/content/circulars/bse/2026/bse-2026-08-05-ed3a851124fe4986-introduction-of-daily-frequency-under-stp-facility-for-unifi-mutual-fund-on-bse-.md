## Summary

BSE StAR MF has introduced the "Daily" frequency under the existing Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) facility for UNIFI Mutual Fund, effective August 05, 2026. This updates applies to specific schemes including UNIFI Liquid Fund, UNIFI Flexi Cap Fund, and UNIFI Dynamic Asset Allocation Fund.

## Key Points

- Introduction of Daily frequency under the STP facility on BSE StAR MF platform.
- Effective date: August 05, 2026.
- Applicable schemes include UNIFI Liquid Fund, UNIFI Flexi Cap Fund, and UNIFI Dynamic Asset Allocation Fund.
- Minimum STP amount is Rs. 1,000/- and in multiple of Re. 1 with a minimum of 6 instalments.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can utilize the newly added daily frequency for STP transactions in the specified schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with enhanced flexibility for systematic transfers into UNIFI Mutual Fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.
