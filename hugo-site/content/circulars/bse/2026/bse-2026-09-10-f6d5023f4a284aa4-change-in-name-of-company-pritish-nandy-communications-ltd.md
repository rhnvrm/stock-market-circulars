## Summary

Pritish Nandy Communications Ltd has informed the Exchange about a change in the name of the Company to PNC Media and Entertainment Limited. The change will be effective from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Pritish Nandy Communications Ltd
- New Name: PNC Media and Entertainment Limited
- Scrip Code: 532387
- Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System: PNC (No Change)
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: PNC

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to note the change in the company name for trading and settlement systems.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Only the display name of the company is updated across exchange trading systems with the scrip code and ID remaining unchanged.
