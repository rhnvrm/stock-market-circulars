## Summary

In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20251028-5 dated October 28, 2025, the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued an update regarding the debt securities of Century Joint Developments Pvt Ltd. Specific scrip codes and ISINs will be available for trading with revised terms due to debt restructuring.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026.
- Affected Entity: Century Joint Developments Pvt Ltd.
- Affected Scrip Codes & ISINs:
  - Scrip Code 976196 (ISIN: INE050R07091)
  - Scrip Code 976527 (ISIN: INE050R07109)
- Reason: Debt restructuring resulting in revised terms for trading members.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading terms for the specified debt securities have been revised following a debt restructuring scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to note the revised terms for the scrip codes and ISINs mentioned and reach out to exchange officials for further clarifications if needed.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date for Revised Trading Terms: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding or trading the debt securities of Century Joint Developments Pvt Ltd under the specified ISINs will be subject to the revised terms following the debt restructuring.
