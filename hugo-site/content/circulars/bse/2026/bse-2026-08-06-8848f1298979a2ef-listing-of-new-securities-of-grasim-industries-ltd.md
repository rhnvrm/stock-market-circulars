## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that 7,072 new equity shares of Grasim Industries Ltd. (Scrip Code - 500300) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Friday, August 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Grasim Industries Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 500300
- Security Details: 7,072 Equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Rs.2/- each issued on rights basis.
- ISIN: INE047A01021
- Distinctive Numbers: 680470008 to 680477079
- Effective Date: August 7, 2026

None.

Trading members must ensure compliance with the listed security details.
