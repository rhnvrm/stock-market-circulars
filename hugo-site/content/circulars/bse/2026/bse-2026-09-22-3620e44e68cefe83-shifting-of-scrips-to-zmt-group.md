## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the shifting of scrips of specific listed entities to the ‘Z’ / ‘MT’ group effective October 05, 2026. This action is taken due to non-compliance with Regulation 17(1) or 18(1) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (March 2026 & June 2026).

## Key Points

- Scrips affected: Clara Industries Ltd, Mercury Trade Links Ltd, and Cressanda Railway Solutions Ltd.
- Reason: Non-compliance with Regulation 17(1)/18(1) for March 2026 and June 2026 quarters.
- Grace period: Companies have until September 29, 2026, to rectify the non-compliance.
- Settlement type: Trades executed in the ‘Z’ / ‘MT’ group will be settled on a Trade for Trade basis.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of penal actions under SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 and Standard Operating Procedure for suspension and revocation of trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Affected companies must fulfill compliance requirements on or before September 29, 2026, to avoid transfer to the ‘Z’ / ‘MT’ group.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Compliance Deadline: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date of Shifting: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the affected scrips will move to a Trade for Trade settlement basis, reducing liquidity and increasing settlement friction for investors holding these securities.
