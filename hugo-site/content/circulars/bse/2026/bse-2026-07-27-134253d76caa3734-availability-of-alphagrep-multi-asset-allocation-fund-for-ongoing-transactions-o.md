## Summary

Alphagrep Multi Asset Allocation Fund has reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from July 27, 2026, pursuant to notice no. 20260703-1 dated July 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund Name: Alphagrep Multi Asset Allocation Fund
- Effective Date: July 27, 2026
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, and SWP
- Covered Schemes:
  - Alphagrep Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF2VOA01046)
  - Alphagrep Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2VOA01053)
  - Alphagrep Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF2VOA01061)
  - Alphagrep Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF2VOA01012)
  - Alphagrep Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF2VOA01020)
  - Alphagrep Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF2VOA01038)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants using the BSE StAR MF Platform can execute transactions for the mentioned schemes in accordance with standard platform operating procedures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates ongoing investments and redemptions for investors and members through the BSE StAR MF platform.
