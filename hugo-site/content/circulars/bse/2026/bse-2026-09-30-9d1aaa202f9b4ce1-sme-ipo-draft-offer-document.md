## Summary

Steam Equipments Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with BSE for its proposed SME Initial Public Offering (IPO). In compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations, the document is hosted on the stock exchange websites to invite public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Steam Equipments Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Review Period: At least 21 days from the date of the notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com
- SME IPO Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

- Adherence to Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations regarding public dissemination and comment invitation for SME IPO draft offer documents.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stakeholders and the public may review the draft offer document and submit any comments within 21 days via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: October 21, 2026 (21 days from notice date)

## Impact Assessment

- Enables regulatory transparency and public feedback collection for the upcoming SME public issue of Steam Equipments Limited.
