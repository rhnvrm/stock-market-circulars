## Summary

Equity shares of SRIT India Limited are listed and admitted to dealing on BSE effective Tuesday, October 6, 2026, under the 'T' Group of Securities (Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days).

## Key Points

- Total Securities: 6,42,71,757 Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 64271757).
- Scrip Code: 544965, Scrip ID: SRIT, ISIN: INE307K01028.
- Issue Price: Rs. 130/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/-, Premium Rs. 125/-).
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) applicable on listing day (October 6, 2026).
- Registrar: KFin Technologies Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing notice issued in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines.
- Corporate action executed to mark lock-in periods in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 1, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and liquidity for SRIT India Limited shareholders, with standard trade-for-trade surveillance safeguards for initial trading sessions.
