## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the official change of its name from "Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL)". All existing arrangements, services, and ongoing business activities will continue without interruption. Stakeholders and investors are advised to update their records and mention the new name in all future mandates, transactions, and correspondence.

## Key Points

- Company name changed from Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL) to BSE Clearing Limited.
- All existing arrangements, services, and ongoing business activities continue without interruption.
- Stakeholders must update internal records, mandates, bank details, and related documents.
- Investors must mention "BSE Clearing Limited" in place of "Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL)" when submitting mandates or instructions under the Mutual Fund platform.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders, market participants, and investors must update their internal systems, bank details, and records to reflect the revised entity name for all future transactions and correspondence.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 27 August 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal as services continue without interruption; however, participants must ensure proper documentation and mandate updates to reflect the new entity name.
