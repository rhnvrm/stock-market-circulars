## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are hereby informed that the undermentioned new equity shares of ASTAL LABORATORIES LIMITED (Scrip Code: 512600) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, August 13, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to Non-Promoters pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Securities Listed:** 14,70,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 40/- each (issued at a premium of Rs. 30/-).
- **Share Class & Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Allotment Details:** Issued on a preferential basis to Non-Promoters pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- **ISIN:** INE307N01014.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 42241933 to 43711932.
- **Lock-in Details:** 14,70,000 shares are locked-in until February 22, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no broader regulatory changes associated with this notice. This is a standard listing approval issued under existing Exchange regulations and SEBI guidelines for preferential issues and listing of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members:** Update back-office and trading systems to enable trading of the newly listed 14,70,000 equity shares from August 13, 2026.
- **For Depositories/Issuer:** Ensure that the lock-in restriction on Distinctive Nos. 42241933 to 43711932 is maintained until the lock-in expiry date of February 22, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** May 12, 2026
- **Date of BSE Notice:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** August 13, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** February 22, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine listing of a small volume of shares (14.70 lakh shares) for a single micro/small-cap company, which is unlikely to have a significant effect on the stock's price, volatility, or overall market dynamics.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Systems should be configured to accept the increased share count and handle the new shares under ISIN INE307N01014.
