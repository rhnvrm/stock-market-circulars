## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) with a SIP facility for the JIOBLACKROCK Balanced Advantage Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from September 11, 2026, to September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Names: JIOBLACKROCK Balanced Advantage Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF22M001374) and JIOBLACKROCK Balanced Advantage Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF22M001366).
- Scheme Category: Equity.
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 01, 2026.
- NFO Window: September 11, 2026, to September 25, 2026.
- Lumpsum purchases extended till midnight on September 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, with the 'N' flag reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 01, 2026, for allotment eligibility.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on September 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: September 11, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: September 25, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 01, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 01, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscription and systematic investment plan (SIP) transactions for investors participating in the JIOBLACKROCK Balanced Advantage Fund NFO via the BSE StAR MF platform.
