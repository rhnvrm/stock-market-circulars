## Summary

BSE StAR MF has announced a change in the cut-off time for NAV applicability in respect of WhiteOak Capital Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective immediately, following communication from WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- The cut-off time for valid transactions (Purchase, SIP, Switch-In, STP-In, Redemption, SWP, Switch-Out, and STP-Out) has been revised from 3:00 p.m. to 1:30 p.m.
- For valid applications received up to 1:30 p.m., the closing NAV of the same business day is applicable; after 1:30 p.m., the next business day's NAV applies.
- For subscription transactions (Lump Sum purchase and SIP), funds must be credited to the AMC collection account on or before 1:30 p.m.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 12:30 p.m. on the transaction/business day.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of NAV applicability cut-off time from 3:00 p.m. to 1:30 p.m. for WhiteOak Capital Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that transactions in the aforesaid scheme are executed in accordance with the revised 1:30 p.m. cut-off time and timely fund clearances.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: With immediate effect (Notice Date: 21 October 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Operational adjustments required for members and investors dealing in WhiteOak Capital Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF on BSE StAR MF to meet earlier cut-off and fund realization timings.
