## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of subscriptions (lumpsum fresh and additional purchases) and switch-in transactions in the Aditya Birla Sun Life Gold Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Resumption applies to lumpsum investments (fresh and additional purchases) and switch-in transactions.
- Effective date of resumption is August 13, 2026.
- Restrictions previously imposed via Notice No. 20260608-42 dated June 08, 2026, have been lifted.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the resumption and execute transactions under the scheme accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to resume investments in the Aditya Birla Sun Life Gold Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform without previous restrictions.
