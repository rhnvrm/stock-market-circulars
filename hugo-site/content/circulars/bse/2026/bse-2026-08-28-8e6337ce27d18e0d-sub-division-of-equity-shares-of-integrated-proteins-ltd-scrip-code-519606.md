## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the sub-division of equity shares of Integrated Proteins Ltd from a face value of Rs. 10/- each into ten equity shares of Re. 1/- each, effective from September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Integrated Proteins Ltd
- Scrip Code: 519606
- Old Face Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- New Face Value: Re. 1/- per share (10 shares for every 1 share held)
- Record Date: September 4, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The existing ISIN INE177M01013 (Rs. 10/- paid up) will not be valid for transactions done on the Exchange on or after September 4, 2026.
- A new ISIN number for the Re. 1/- paid-up shares will be communicated via a separate notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the new ISIN status and ensure compliance regarding transactions post the record date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 4, 2026
- Effective Date for Sub-Division / New ISIN Validity: September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders of Integrated Proteins Ltd will receive 10 shares of Re. 1/- each for every 1 share of Rs. 10/- held as of the record date. Liquidity and share price will adjust accordingly.
