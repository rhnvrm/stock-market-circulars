## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the continuation of trading of specified securities in the 'Z' group effective September 28, 2026, due to non-compliance with Regulation 31 (Submission of Shareholding Pattern) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (March 2026 & June 2026).

## Key Points

- 28 scrips are listed for transfer/continuation in the 'Z' group.
- Trades executed in the 'Z' group will continue to be settled on a Trade for Trade basis.
- Action taken pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of penal actions for non-compliance of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, specifically Regulation 31 concerning the submission of shareholding patterns.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure timely submission of shareholding patterns and compliance with SEBI LODR regulations to avoid penal actions and transfer to restrictive trading groups.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected scrips will continue under the 'Z' group on a Trade for Trade basis, impacting liquidity and settlement for investors holding these securities.
