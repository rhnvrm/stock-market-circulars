## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Anil Fashions Limited on September 30, 2026. The document contains disclosures specified under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations and is hosted on the BSE SME website for public comments.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Anil Fashions Limited
- **Filing Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Segment:** SME
- **Hosting Period:** At least 21 days from the date of the notice
- **Hosting Link:** https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

None. The filing complies with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments on the Draft Offer Document may be submitted within 21 days of the notice to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Public Comment Deadline:** 21 days from October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites stakeholder feedback on the proposed SME IPO of Anil Fashions Limited prior to final issuance.
