## Summary

BSE StAR MF has announced the temporary suspension of subscriptions, fresh SIP/XSIP and STP-In registrations, and switch-in transactions in select schemes of Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund, effective October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes: Kotak Medium Term Fund and Kotak Credit Risk Fund.
- Lumpsum investments (fresh and additional), switch-ins, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-In) are disabled with immediate effect.
- Existing SIP/XSIP and STP-In registrations prior to the effective date continue to be processed by the AMC/RTA without changes.
- Redemption, Switch-Out, STP-Out, and SWP transactions remain fully permitted without restriction.
- Subscription orders received prior to the effective date but triggered to the RTA on or after October 14, 2026, will be rejected and refunded.

## Regulatory Changes

Operational suspension of subscriptions and new systematic registrations implemented by Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that no new subscription orders, fresh SIP/STP registrations, or switch-in transactions are entered into the affected Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026
- RTA Trigger Cut-off Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and distributors utilizing the BSE StAR MF platform will be unable to execute fresh investments or new systematic plans in Kotak Medium Term Fund and Kotak Credit Risk Fund, whereas exit options such as redemptions and switch-outs remain unaffected.
