## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, effective September 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins are applicable w.e.f. September 29, 2026 on open positions as of September 28, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands are effective from September 25, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue in LT-ASM but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. September 29, 2026.
- Specific securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective September 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and group reclassifications as per revised LT-ASM guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on shortlisted securities and note the group transfers and price band restrictions.

## Important Dates

- September 25, 2026: Effective date for higher stages, price band changes, and removal of securities from LT-ASM.
- September 28, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- September 29, 2026: Effective date for 100% margins and group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS Group).

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin blocking for shortlisted securities will be directly impacted due to 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions.
