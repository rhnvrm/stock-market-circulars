## Summary

Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund has announced the merger of its transferor scheme, MOTILAL OSWAL ASSET ALLOCATION PASSIVE FUND OF FUND - CONSERVATIVE, into the transferee scheme, MOTILAL OSWAL MULTI ASSET PASSIVE FUND OF FUND, effective after business hours on November 13, 2026. Investors can exit or switch without exit load until November 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Transferor Scheme: MOTILAL OSWAL ASSET ALLOCATION PASSIVE FUND OF FUND - CONSERVATIVE.
- Transferee Scheme: MOTILAL OSWAL MULTI ASSET PASSIVE FUND OF FUND.
- Effective Date of Merger: After close of business hours on November 13, 2026.
- Exit Load Window: Investors not wishing to continue can redeem or switch without exit load via BSE StAR MF from October 09, 2026, to November 09, 2026.
- Fresh subscriptions (Lump Sum, Additional, Switch-in, STP-in, SIP/XSIP) cease after cut-off time on November 09, 2026.
- Systematic investment facilities (SIP/STP/SWP) will continue in the transferee scheme for investors providing positive consent.

## Regulatory Changes

- Scheme merger processed in accordance with regulatory guidelines and communications received from Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors must inform investors about the merger timeline, exit load-free redemption window, and subscription cut-off dates.
- Cancellation requests for ongoing systematic facilities must be initiated within AMC timelines if investors do not wish to migrate.

## Important Dates

- Redemption/Switch-out without exit load window: October 09, 2026 to November 09, 2026.
- Fresh subscription cut-off date: November 09, 2026.
- Demat transaction acceptance date: November 09, 2026.
- Non-demat transaction acceptance date: November 13, 2026.
- Effective Date / Cessation of Transferor Scheme: November 13, 2026 (after close of business hours).

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding units in the transferor scheme will have their investments migrated or refunded based on their active consent and redemption preferences, ensuring smooth operational transition on the BSE StAR MF platform.
