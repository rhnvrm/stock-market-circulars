## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 54,47,222 new equity shares of SG Finserve Limited (Scrip Code - 539199) with effect from Thursday, August 20, 2026. These shares are issued to promoters and non-promoters on a preferential basis upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SG Finserve Limited
- Scrip Code: 539199
- ISIN: INE618R01015
- Number of Shares: 54,47,222 equity shares
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- each (issued at a premium of Rs. 440/- per share)
- Issue Price: Rs. 450/- per share
- Date of Allotment: 27/03/2026
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must adhere to the lock-in requirements specified for the preferential shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 27/03/2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: 20/08/2026
- Lock-in Upto Dates: 19/02/2027 (for 26,22,222 shares) and 19/02/2028 (for 28,25,000 shares)

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion via preferential allotment of equity shares resulting from warrant conversion. No direct impact on market operations.
