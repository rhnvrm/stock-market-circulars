## Summary

BSE has announced that the scheduled maintenance activity for the BSE StAR MF Version 1.0, previously planned for Saturday, October 17, 2026, has been deferred until further notice. The revised date and timing will be communicated separately.

## Key Points

- Maintenance activity for BSE StAR MF Version 1.0 scheduled for October 17, 2026, stands deferred.
- Reference is made to Notice No. 20261014-14 dated October 14, 2026.
- Revised schedule will be announced in due course.
- Trading and member teams should inform respective technical teams and vendors.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to communicate the deferment to their technical teams and vendors.

## Important Dates

- October 17, 2026: Originally scheduled maintenance date (deferred).
- Future Date: Revised maintenance schedule to be announced.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact as the maintenance is postponed, ensuring uninterrupted availability of the platform for the scheduled date.
