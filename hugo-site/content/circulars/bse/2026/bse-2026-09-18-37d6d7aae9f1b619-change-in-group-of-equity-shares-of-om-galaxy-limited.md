## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Om Galaxy Limited (Scrip Code: 544922) are being transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group).

## Key Points

- Company Name: OM GALAXY LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544922
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Monday, October 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The trading segment for the company's equity shares is changed from Trade for Trade to Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the segment change and ensure compliance with trading norms for the Rolling segment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the equity shares of Om Galaxy Limited will shift to the regular Rolling segment, affecting settlement and trading dynamics for market participants.
