## Summary

BSE has released the revised trading and settlement programme for the cash segment covering both T+1 and T+0 settlement cycles for the period between September 1, 2026, and September 30, 2026. The circular details settlement numbers, trading dates, depository data entry, confirmation timelines, pay-in/pay-out dates, and auction schedules.

## Key Points

- Covers trading and settlement schedules for September 2026 for the cash segment.
- Includes schedules for both T+1 and T+0 settlement cycles.
- Details specific timelines for settlement numbers ranging from DR-706/26-27 to DR-726/26-27 for T+1, and corresponding T+0 schedules.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; this is a routine calendar notification.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must adhere to the specified timelines for data entry, confirmation, pay-in, and pay-out.

## Important Dates

- Programme Period: September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Notice Date: September 9, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational workflows and clear planning for clearing and settlement activities for market participants during September 2026.
