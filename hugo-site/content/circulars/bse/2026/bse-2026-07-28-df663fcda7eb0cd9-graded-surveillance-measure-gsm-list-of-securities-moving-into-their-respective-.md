## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from July 29, 2026. The affected securities will attract specific surveillance actions such as 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure will move to higher GSM stages effective July 29, 2026.
- Stage I entails a 100% margin rate and a price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II entails trade-for-trade settlement with a 5% price band and a 50% ASD on trade value.
- Stages III and IV entail trade-for-trade settlement, trading permitted only once a week (every Monday), and 100% ASD.
- Settlement will be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the GSM framework as published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent updates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities due to the trade-to-trade settlement and prohibition of netting off.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 28 Jul 2026
- **Effective Date:** 29 Jul 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity for the securities moving into higher GSM stages will be significantly restricted due to mandatory deposits, trade-for-trade settlement, and weekly trading limitations.
