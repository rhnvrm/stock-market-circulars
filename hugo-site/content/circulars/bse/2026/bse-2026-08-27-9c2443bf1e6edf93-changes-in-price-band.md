## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 15 scrips, effective from August 28, 2026, as part of its routine surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 15 scrips across various categories.
- Changes take effect from August 28, 2026.
- Affected scrips include Ankit Metal & Power Ltd, Dalmia Bharat Ltd, Nectar Lifesciences Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands for the specified securities have been modified from their existing levels to new limits ranging from 2% to 20%.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while executing orders in the mentioned scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026
- Notice Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and traders dealing in the affected scrips will be subject to the new intraday price movement limits, impacting volatility and order placement.
