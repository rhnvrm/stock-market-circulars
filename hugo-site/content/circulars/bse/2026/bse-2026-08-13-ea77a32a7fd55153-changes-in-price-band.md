## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 12 scrips, effective from August 14, 2026, as part of its surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Total scrips affected: 12
- Effective date: August 14, 2026
- Revised price bands range from 2% to 20% across different scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands for specific scrips have been modified from their existing levels to new limits (2%, 5%, 10%, and 20%) to manage volatility and protect investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while placing orders in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members trading in these 12 scrips will be subject to the new price movement limits, restricting maximum daily price fluctuations accordingly.
