## Summary

Axis Mutual Fund has communicated the merger of AXIS NIFTY SDL SEPTEMBER 2026 DEBT INDEX FUND (Transferor Scheme) into AXIS BANKING AND PSU DEBT FUND (Transferee Scheme). The merger takes effect after business hours on September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Transferor Scheme: AXIS NIFTY SDL SEPTEMBER 2026 DEBT INDEX FUND
- Transferee Scheme: AXIS BANKING AND PSU DEBT FUND
- Effective Date: After close of business hours on September 30, 2026.
- Exit Load Waiver Period: August 31, 2026, to September 29, 2026 (both days inclusive) for investors wishing to redeem or switch without exit load.
- Fresh Subscriptions: Lump Sum, Additional Purchase, Switch-in, AMC STP-in, and SIP/XSIP registrations continue during the exit load waiver period.
- Systematic Registrations: Existing SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP registrations migrate automatically to the corresponding plan/option of the Transferee scheme after business hours on September 29, 2026.
- Platform Availability: Transferor schemes cease transaction processing on BSE StAR MF after business hours on September 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors wishing to cancel SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP facilities or exercise exit load-free redemptions/switches must do so within the specified timelines.

## Important Dates

- Exit Load Waiver Period / Redemption Window: August 31, 2026, to September 29, 2026
- Migration of Systematic Registrations: After close of business hours on September 29, 2026
- Cessation of Transferor Scheme Transactions: After close of business hours on September 29, 2026
- Merger Effective Date: After close of business hours on September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding units in the Transferor scheme are impacted by the scheme merger and must review migration options, exit load waivers, and transaction timelines on the BSE StAR MF platform.
