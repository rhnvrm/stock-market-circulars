## Summary

BSE has announced that the Whiteoak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform with effect from October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Whiteoak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF
- **Eligible Plans & ISINs:** 
  - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF03VN01AO0)
  - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF03VN01AN2)
- **Facilities:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **Effective Date:** October 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors can execute transactions in the specified scheme through the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless trading and investment operations for market participants and investors dealing in Whiteoak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF on the BSE StAR MF platform.
