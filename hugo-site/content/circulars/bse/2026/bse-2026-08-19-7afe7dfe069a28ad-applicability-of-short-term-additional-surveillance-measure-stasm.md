## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (STASM) Stage I and Stage II for specified securities. Applicable margin rates are revised to 50% or 100% depending on the stage, effective from August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to maximum rate of margin capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher subject to maximum rate of margin capped at 100%.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on August 20, 2026, and new positions created from August 21, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and enforcement of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance frameworks and margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100%) for applicable securities effective August 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 21, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the short-listed scrips.
