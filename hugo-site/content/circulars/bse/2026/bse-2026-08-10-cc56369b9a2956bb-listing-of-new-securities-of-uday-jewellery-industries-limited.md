## Summary

BSE has informed market participants about the listing and permission for trading of 97,81,025 equity shares of Uday Jewellery Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 539518) with effect from August 11, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation of Narbada Gems and Jewellery Limited with Uday Jewellery Industries Limited.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Uday Jewellery Industries Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 539518
- **Security Details:** 97,81,025 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation of Narbada Gems and Jewellery Limited (Transferor Company) with Uday Jewellery Industries Limited (Transferee Company).
- **ISIN Number:** INE551B01012
- **Distribution Nos.:** 26984801 – 36765825
- **Date of Allotment:** March 05, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and depository participants need to note the distribution numbers and ISIN for trading and holding reconciliations.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** March 05, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Commencement Date:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action regarding listing of shares issued via amalgamation scheme; normal operational impact for shareholders and market participants.
