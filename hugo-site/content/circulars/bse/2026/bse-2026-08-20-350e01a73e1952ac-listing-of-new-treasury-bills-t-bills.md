## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under the G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS segment, effective from Friday, August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three new Treasury Bills with different tenures (91-day, 182-day, and 364-day) are admitted to trading.
- Scrip codes, ISINs, market lots, symbols, and maturity dates have been detailed for the listed instruments.
- Securities will not be available for trading two (2) working days prior to their respective maturity or redemption dates.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the trading restrictions two working days prior to maturity/redemption dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Listing Date: August 21, 2026
- Maturity Dates: November 19, 2026 (91TB191126), February 18, 2027 (182TB18227), August 19, 2027 (364TB19827)

## Impact Assessment

Standard expansion of debt market instruments available for trading on the exchange.
