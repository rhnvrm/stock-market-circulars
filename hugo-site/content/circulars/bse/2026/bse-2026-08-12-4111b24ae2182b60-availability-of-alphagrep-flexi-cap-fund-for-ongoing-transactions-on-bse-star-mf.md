## Summary

The BSE StAR MF Platform has announced the reopening of Alphagrep Flexi Cap Fund for ongoing transactions, including subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic facilities (SIP, STP, and SWP), with effect from August 13, 2026. This reopening is pursuant to the prior BSE Notice No. 20260720-35 dated July 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Reopening of Fund**: The Alphagrep Flexi Cap Fund will be available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic transactions.
- **Supported Facilities**: Systematic Investment Plans (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plans (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plans (SWP) are fully supported.
- **Effective Date**: The schemes will be active on the platform starting August 13, 2026.
- **Schemes and ISINs available**:
  1. ALPHAGREP FLEXI CAP FUND - REGULAR PLAN - EQUITY GROWTH (ISIN: INF2VOA01103)
  2. ALPHAGREP FLEXI CAP FUND - REGULAR PLAN - EQUITY IDCW REINVESTMENT (ISIN: INF2VOA01111)
  3. ALPHAGREP FLEXI CAP FUND - REGULAR PLAN - EQUITY IDCW PAYOUT (ISIN: INF2VOA01129)
  4. ALPHAGREP FLEXI CAP FUND - DIRECT PLAN - EQUITY GROWTH (ISIN: INF2VOA01079)
  5. ALPHAGREP FLEXI CAP FUND - DIRECT PLAN - EQUITY IDCW REINVESTMENT (ISIN: INF2VOA01087)
  6. ALPHAGREP FLEXI CAP FUND - DIRECT PLAN - EQUITY IDCW PAYOUT (ISIN: INF2VOA01095)

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced by this notice. It is a standard operational reopening of mutual fund schemes on the trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no new compliance requirements for investors or intermediaries. Trading members and mutual fund distributors can resume facilitating transactions for these specific schemes starting August 13, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **July 20, 2026**: Issuance date of the prior notice (Notice No. 20260720-35).
- **August 12, 2026**: Date of the current circular announcement.
- **August 13, 2026**: Effective date for reopening the fund for transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Low. This update restores standard transaction access to Alphagrep Flexi Cap Fund schemes for distributors and retail investors using the BSE StAR MF platform, enhancing liquidity and investment options for this specific fund.
- **Systemic Impact**: None.
