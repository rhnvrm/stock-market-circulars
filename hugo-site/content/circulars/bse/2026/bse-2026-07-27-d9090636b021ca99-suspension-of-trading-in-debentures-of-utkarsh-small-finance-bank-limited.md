## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) of Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Limited due to the early redemption and exercise of a call option.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Limited
- Security Details: NCD (USFB-12.5%-26-6-27-PVT, ISIN: INE735W08038, Scrip Code: 959644)
- Record Date: 29/07/2026
- No Dealings From: 29/07/2026
- Reason: Early Redemption on Exercise of Call Option

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures of the company effective from the record date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 29/07/2026
- No Dealings From Date: 29/07/2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specific NCD series of Utkarsh Small Finance Bank Limited is halted following the early redemption process.
