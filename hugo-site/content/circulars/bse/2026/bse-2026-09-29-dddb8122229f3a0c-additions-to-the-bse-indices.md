## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of three newly listed companies—Varmora Granito Limited, Himalaya Nutravedics India Limited, and Anand Seamless Limited—to its respective indices effective between September 30 and October 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Varmora Granito Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544942) will be added to the BSE IPO index effective October 1, 2026.
- Himalaya Nutravedics India Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544943) and Anand Seamless Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544944) will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective September 30, 2026.
- All three companies were listed on BSE effective September 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None. Standard index inclusion notice for information and tracking purposes.

## Important Dates

- September 29, 2026: Listing date for Varmora Granito, Himalaya Nutravedics, and Anand Seamless.
- September 30, 2026: Index addition effective date for BSE SME IPO index stocks (544943, 544944).
- October 1, 2026: Index addition effective date for BSE IPO index stock (544942).

## Impact Assessment

Routine index inclusion with minimal direct operational impact on market participants, affecting index tracking portfolios.
