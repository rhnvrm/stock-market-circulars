## Summary

BSE has issued Notice No. 20261021-28 on October 21, 2026, announcing the revision of price bands for 27 scrips under the Equity segment.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised for 27 scrips ranging from 2% to 20%.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is October 22, 2026.
- Members can reach out to bse.surv@bseindia.com for clarifications.

## Regulatory Changes

The operating price limits (price bands) for the listed scrips have been modified as per surveillance review.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure systems are updated with the new price bands effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Limits price movement volatility for the affected scrips, impacting intraday trading strategies and order placements.
