## Summary

Gandhi Special Tubes Limited has announced a buyback of up to 8,68,100 fully paid-up equity shares of face value INR 5/- each, representing 7.14% of the total issued, subscribed, and paid-up equity share capital. The buyback price is set at INR 900/- per equity share through the tender offer route, aggregating to a maximum amount of INR 78,12,90,000/-. The offer period runs from Thursday, August 27, 2026, to Wednesday, September 2, 2026, with BSE acting as the designated stock exchange.

## Key Points

- Company: Gandhi Special Tubes Limited
- Buyback Size: Up to 8,68,100 Equity Shares (7.14% of total equity share capital)
- Buyback Price: INR 900/- per Equity Share
- Aggregate Amount: INR 78,12,90,000/-
- Record Date: Friday, August 21, 2026
- Route: Tender Offer Route via Acquisition Window
- Designated Stock Exchange: BSE

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted pursuant to SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131, along with relevant Exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the tender offer timeline and adhere to the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through the stock exchange.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 21, 2026
- Issue Opening Date: August 27, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: September 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit opportunity for eligible shareholders/beneficial owners as of the record date through a proportional tender offer buyback mechanism.
