## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 4,24,647 new equity shares of face value Rs. 5/- each issued by TANFAC Industries Ltd. on a preferential basis, effective from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TANFAC Industries Ltd. (Scrip Code: 506854)
- Number of Securities: 4,24,647 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 2341/- per share (including premium of Rs. 2336/-)
- Allottees: Promoter and Non-Promoters on a preferential basis
- ISIN: INE639B01023
- Lock-in Details: 2,60,065 shares locked-in up to 12/04/2028; 1,64,582 shares locked-in up to 12/04/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to statutory lock-in periods and trading permissions as specified by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 09/09/2026
- Listing/Effective Date: 12/10/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: 12/04/2027 and 12/04/2028

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up equity capital of TANFAC Industries Ltd. with standard lock-in constraints applicable to preferential allotments.
