## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the addition of two newly listed companies, Molbio Diagnostics Limited and Dhoot Transmission Limited, to the BSE IPO Index. The inclusion becomes effective at the open of Wednesday, August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Molbio Diagnostics Limited** (Exchange Ticker: 544866) and **Dhoot Transmission Limited** (Exchange Ticker: 544867) are being listed on BSE effective August 17, 2026.
- Both stocks will be officially added to the BSE IPO Index effective at the market open on August 19, 2026.
- These corporate actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and index trackers are required to note the index additions for portfolio and benchmarking adjustments.

## Important Dates

- **Listing Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Index Effective Date:** August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for index tracking funds and investors monitoring the BSE IPO Index.
