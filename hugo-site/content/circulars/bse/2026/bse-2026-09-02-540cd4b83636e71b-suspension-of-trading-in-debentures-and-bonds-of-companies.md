## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and Bonds of various companies due to the fixation of record dates for payment of interest and redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures/bonds of the specified companies as per the fixed record dates.
- Affected companies include Jindal Stainless Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, L&T Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Ltd, and others.
- Record dates and ex-dates fall typically between September 3, 2026, and September 14, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance by refraining from dealing in the specified debentures and bonds during the suspension period associated with the record dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: 03/09/2026 to 14/09/2026
- Settlement DR: 2027

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of secondary market trading in specific debt instruments for the purpose of interest payment and redemption processing.
