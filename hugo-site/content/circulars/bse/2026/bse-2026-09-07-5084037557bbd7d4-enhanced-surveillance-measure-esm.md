## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the inclusion of securities meeting surveillance criteria, movement of securities between stages, and the removal of certain securities from the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying ESM criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin effective September 09, 2026, on open positions as of September 08, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction effective September 08, 2026.
- Qualifying securities under ESM will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective September 09, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III, while Annexures I and II contain specific inclusion and exclusion lists.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the ESM framework in conjunction with prevailing exchange measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage Movements and Price Band changes: September 08, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade shift: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity for the shortlisted securities may be impacted due to higher margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
