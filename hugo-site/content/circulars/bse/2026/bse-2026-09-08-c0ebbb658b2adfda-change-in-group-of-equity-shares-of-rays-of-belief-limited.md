## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Rays of Belief Limited (Scrip Code: 544906) will be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment (B Group) with effect from Wednesday, September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Rays of Belief Limited
- Scrip Code: 544906
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: September 23, 2026
- Reference to previous Exchange Notice No. 20260907-27 dated September 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The trading category and segment for the company's equity shares have been modified from Trade for Trade to normal rolling settlement under B Group.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment change for executing transactions in the company's equity shares from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 08, 2026
- Effective Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for the equity shares of Rays of Belief Limited, improving liquidity and trading convenience compared to the Trade for Trade segment restrictions.
