## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Effective September 22, 2026, enhanced margin requirements will be enforced on open positions and new positions created for the shortlisted securities.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at a maximum of 100%.
- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage II will attract a margin rate of 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher.
- The surveillance measures are implemented in conjunction with existing exchange rules and do not constitute an adverse action against the companies.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their original levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and enforcement of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for qualified scrips to manage market volatility and risk.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100% as applicable) w.e.f. September 22, 2026, on all open positions as on September 21, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 18 Sep 2026
- **Effective Date:** 22 Sep 2026 (applicable on open positions as of 21 Sep 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin obligations for market participants trading in securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stages I and II, potentially affecting liquidity and short-term trading volumes for the impacted scrips.
