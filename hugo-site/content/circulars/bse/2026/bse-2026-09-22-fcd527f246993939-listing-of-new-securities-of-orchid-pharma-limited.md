## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 4,45,86,052 new equity shares of Orchid Pharma Limited with effect from September 23, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation and Arrangement of Dhanuka Laboratories Limited with Orchid Pharma Limited.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Orchid Pharma Limited (Scrip Code: 524372)
- Security Details: 4,45,86,052 Equity Shares of Rs.10/- each issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation.
- Effective Date of Trading: September 23, 2026.
- ISIN Number: INE191A01027
- Distribution Nos.: 50719106 to 95305157
- Date of Allotment: August 01, 2026.
- Cross Holding Cancellation: 3,54,19,957 shares held by Dhanuka Laboratories Limited in Orchid Pharma Limited shall be cancelled.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable (Corporate restructuring and share listing notice).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and shareholders should note the updated share capital, distribution numbers, and ISIN details for trading and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 01, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up equity capital for Orchid Pharma Limited following the amalgamation with Dhanuka Laboratories Limited, along with the cancellation of cross-held shares.
