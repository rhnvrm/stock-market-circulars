## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities that are maturing. Trading members are advised not to deal in these securities effective from July 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three specific T-bills/Government Securities have reached maturity.
- Affected Scrip Codes and ISINs:
  - Scrip Code 805173 (182TB30726) - ISIN: IN002025Y438
  - Scrip Code 805099 (364TB30726) - ISIN: IN002025Z187
  - Scrip Code 805208 (91TB300726) - ISIN: IN002026X040
- Trading in these securities is suspended with effect from July 28, 2026 (DR- 681/2026-2027).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard operational notice for maturing government securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified T-bills/Government Securities starting from July 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 27, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to the orderly settlement and cessation of trading for the matured sovereign debt instruments.
