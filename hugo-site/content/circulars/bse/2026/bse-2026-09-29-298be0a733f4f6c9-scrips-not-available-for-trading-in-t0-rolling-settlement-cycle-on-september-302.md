## Summary

BSE has announced that 20 specific scrips, including Bank of Baroda, Ashok Leyland, and Wipro, will not be available for trading in the T+0 rolling settlement cycle on Wednesday, September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260929-24 dated September 29, 2026.
- Applies to the T+0 rolling settlement cycle on September 30, 2026.
- Affects 20 prominent scrips across various sectors (Banking, Auto, Cement, Pharma, IT, etc.).
- Normal trading in the T+1 settlement cycle for these scrips remains unaffected.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary restriction on T+0 rolling settlement eligibility for the listed scrips on the specified date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the non-availability of these scrips in the T+0 segment and inform clients accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants accustomed to trading certain scrips in the T+0 segment will need to use the T+1 settlement cycle for transactions in these securities on September 30, 2026.
