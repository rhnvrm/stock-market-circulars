## Summary

BSE has scheduled maintenance activity for BSE StAR MF Version 1.0 on Saturday, October 17, 2026, from 06:00 AM to 15:00 PM. During this period, access to the portal and API interfaces will be unavailable.

## Key Points

- Affected System: BSE StAR MF Version 1.0
- Maintenance Window: Saturday, October 17, 2026, from 06:00 AM to 15:00 PM
- Affected Interfaces: Web portal (https://bsestarmf.in/Index.aspx) and API interfaces

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to note the outage schedule and inform their respective technical teams and vendors to plan their activities accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Maintenance Date: October 17, 2026 (06:00 AM to 15:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Temporary interruption of access to BSE StAR MF web portal and APIs during the scheduled maintenance window.
