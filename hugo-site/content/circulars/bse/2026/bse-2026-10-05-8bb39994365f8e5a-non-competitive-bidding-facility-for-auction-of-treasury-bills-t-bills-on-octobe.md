## Summary

BSE has announced the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of three Treasury Bills (T-Bills)—91 Day, 182 Day, and 364 Day—scheduled for October 07, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Securities Offered**: 91 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 091T060127, Issue Size: ₹8,000 Cr), 182 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 182T070427, Issue Size: ₹8,000 Cr), and 364 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 364T071027, Issue Size: ₹7,000 Cr).
- **Bidding Platform**: NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system (https://ibbs.bseindia.com).
- **Settlement Date**: October 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Facilitates non-competitive bidding participation in RBI government securities auctions through exchange infrastructure in accordance with RBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must submit bids through the NCB-GSec module within the stipulated timelines.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date (Direct Investors & Members)**: October 05, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability).
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors)**: October 06, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members)**: October 07, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- **Auction Date**: October 07, 2026.
- **Settlement Date**: October 08, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Provides eligible investors and trading members a streamlined mechanism to participate in the Government of India Treasury Bill auctions.
