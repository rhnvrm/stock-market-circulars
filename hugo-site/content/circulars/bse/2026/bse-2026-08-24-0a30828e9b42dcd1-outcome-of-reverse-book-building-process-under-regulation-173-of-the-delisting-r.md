## Summary

Exchange notice regarding the outcome of the Reverse Book Building Process (RBBP) under Regulation 17(3) of the SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021, for Avadh Rail Infra Limited.

## Key Points

- The delisting offer was made by Abhishek Saraff (Acquirer, member of Promoter Group) to public shareholders of Avadh Rail Infra Limited for up to 4,765 equity shares (0.89% of equity share capital).
- The bidding process took place from Tuesday, August 18, 2026, to Monday, August 24, 2026.
- Purva Sharegistry (India) Pvt. Ltd. (Registrar) and Intelligent Money Managers Private Limited (Manager) analyzed the bid data.
- The outcome is disclosed and announced in terms of Regulation 17(3) of the SEBI Delisting Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with Regulation 17(3) of the SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021.

## Compliance Requirements

- Dissemination of the outcome of the reverse book building process to stakeholders and compliance with subsequent delisting procedures as applicable.

## Important Dates

- Offer Period: August 18, 2026, to August 24, 2026.
- Outcome Date: August 24, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects the trading status and liquidity of Avadh Rail Infra Limited equity shares on the exchange pursuant to the delisting process.
