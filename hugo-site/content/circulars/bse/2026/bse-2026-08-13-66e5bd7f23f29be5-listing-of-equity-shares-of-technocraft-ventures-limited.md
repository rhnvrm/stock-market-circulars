## Summary

Effective from Friday, August 14, 2026, the equity shares of Technocraft Ventures Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Technocraft Ventures Limited
- Scrip Code: 544864
- ISIN No: INE1D0W01018
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name on BOLT: TECHNOCRAF
- Number of Securities: 3,96,06,200 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 212/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium of Rs. 202/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 12, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Corporate actions executed to mark lock-in periods in depository records.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the scrip is part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 12, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors who participated in the public issue can trade their shares starting August 14, 2026. Normal market operations and liquidity commence for Technocraft Ventures Limited.
