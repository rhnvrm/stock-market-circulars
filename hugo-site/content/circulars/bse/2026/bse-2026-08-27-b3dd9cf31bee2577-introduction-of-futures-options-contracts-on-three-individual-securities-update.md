## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of Futures & Options (F&O) contracts on three individual securities—Ather Energy Ltd, Bank of Maharashtra, and Sagility Ltd—in the Equity Derivatives segment with effect from August 28, 2026. The contract details and parameters, including market lots and strike price intervals, have been specified in the notice.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** August 28, 2026.
- **Securities Included:**
  - Ather Energy Ltd (Scrip Code: 544397, Asset Code: ATEL, Market Lot: 375, Strike Price Interval: 20)
  - Bank of Maharashtra (Scrip Code: 532525, Asset Code: BOMA, Market Lot: 6500, Strike Price Interval: 1)
  - Sagility Ltd (Scrip Code: 544282, Asset Code: SAGL, Market Lot: 12000, Strike Price Interval: 1)
- **Strike Structure:** Minimum 7-1-7 (ITM-ATM-OTM) strike price structure for all three securities.
- **Contract Master File:** Available in the derivatives contract master file generated at the end of the day on August 27, 2026, via extranet and the BseIndia website.

## Regulatory Changes

- Expansion of the equity derivatives segment to include new derivative contracts for eligible individual securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to refer to the operational parameters and update their trading and risk management systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Contract Master File Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants with additional hedging and trading instruments in the derivatives segment for Ather Energy Ltd, Bank of Maharashtra, and Sagility Ltd.
