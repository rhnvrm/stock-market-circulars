## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing changes in the Future Equivalent (FutEq)/(Delta) Intraday Position Limits Monitoring framework for Equity Index Derivatives and Order to Trade Ratio (OTR) calculations due to the Closing Auction Session (CAS), in continuation of previous exchange and SEBI circulars.

## Key Points

- No intraday snapshot will be taken by the Exchange from the start of the CAS period until the equilibrium price of the index is determined.
- An additional period is added for a fifth snapshot post-determination of the equilibrium index price up to 15:40 hrs.
- The cure period of 15 minutes will not apply to snapshots taken post 14:45 hrs.
- The close price of the underlying index shall be used in the delta computation formula and for position valuation for snapshots post equilibrium price determination.
- Contracts expiring on the same day (Expiry date = Trade date) are included in intraday monitoring snapshots up to 15:40 hrs.
- Time to expiry for delta calculation via Black & Scholes formula shall use the revised derivatives market close time of 15:40 hrs on expiry day.
- Orders placed during CAS shall not be considered for OTR computation in the cash segment for stocks available in CAS.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modifications to the framework for Intraday Position Limits Monitoring for Equity Index Derivatives and OTR on account of CAS following SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/CIR/P/2025/122 and SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD1/P/CIR/2025/79.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised snapshot schedules, delta computation rules, and OTR exclusions during CAS and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 Jul 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in equity index derivatives and cash segment stocks with CAS must adjust their risk management systems, algorithmic execution, and OTR monitoring to accommodate the revised snapshot timing and CAS order exclusions.
