## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that the TATA CRISIL-IBX Financial Services 3-6 Months Debt Index Fund has reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes reopen for ongoing transactions including subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Covers Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- Specific STP investment amount ranges and minimum installment numbers apply to avoid rejections.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational notification regarding mutual fund scheme availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure correct scheme ISINs and adhere to prescribed minimum installment numbers for STP registrations as specified by TATA AMC.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute transactions and systematic investment plans for the specified Tata index fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.
