## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the commencement of trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for the renunciation of S.I. Capital & Financial Services Limited, effective from August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: S.I. Capital & Financial Services Limited
- Scrip Code: 751128
- Scrip ID: SICAPI-RE
- ISIN No.: INE417F20017
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: August 10, 2026 to August 28, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: August 10, 2026 to September 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs, ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares. Contract notes must include a prominent disclaimer explaining that purchase of REs grants the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce them before the issue closes. Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the Issue Closing Date.

## Important Dates

- Trading in REs Commences: August 10, 2026
- Trading in REs Closes: August 28, 2026
- Rights Issue Opening Date: August 10, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to trading members and investors participating in the rights issue of S.I. Capital & Financial Services Limited, requiring proper disclaimers and timely settlement on a T+1 rolling trade-for-trade basis.
