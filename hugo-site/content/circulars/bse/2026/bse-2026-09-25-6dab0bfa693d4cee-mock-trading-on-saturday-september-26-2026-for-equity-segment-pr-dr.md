## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity segment on Saturday, September 26, 2026, utilizing both the Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR). Trading members using third-party trading platforms can test their applications and functionalities under various market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, September 26, 2026.
- Sites involved: Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR).
- Applicable segment: Equity segment.
- Testing scope includes call auction sessions, risk-reduction mode, trading halts, block deals, and various order entry/matching schedules.
- There shall be no new BOLT TWS Release.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational mock trading session notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may participate in the mock trading session to verify their respective trading applications and operational readiness.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 26, 2026
- Notice Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low impact. Designed for testing and validation purposes with no effect on live market data or funds.
