## Summary

BSE has announced updates regarding securities under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the IRP criteria under IBC are placed under surveillance with effect from October 01, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities moving out of the IBC framework are listed in Annexure II, and their price bands will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% are detailed in Annexure IV.
- The shortlisting is purely a market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures and price band restrictions for companies undergoing insolvency proceedings under the IBC framework.
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, provided they are not subject to other surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the revised list of securities under the IBC surveillance framework and adhere to applicable trading controls and price bands.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in shortlisted securities will be subject to specific surveillance controls and price band restrictions, while scrips moving out will have their normal price bands reinstated.
