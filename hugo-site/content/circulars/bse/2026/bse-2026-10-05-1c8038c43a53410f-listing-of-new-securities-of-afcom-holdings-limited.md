## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 2,15,000 new equity shares of Afcom Holdings Limited (Scrip Code - 544224) effective from October 6, 2026. These shares are issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Afcom Holdings Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544224
- **ISIN:** INE0OXY01013
- **Security Details:** 2,15,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 853.17/-
- **Effective Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Distribution Nos.:** 28698617 to 28913616
- **Lock-in Details:** 15,000 shares (dist. 28698617 - 28713616) locked-in up to 15/04/2027; 2,00,000 shares (dist. 28713617 - 28913616) locked-in up to 15/04/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a corporate action listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the lock-in periods and distribution numbers for the newly listed equity shares.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 5, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** April 15, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Minor expansion of equity capital base for Afcom Holdings Limited due to warrant conversion, with shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
