## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Viji Finance Limited (Scrip Code - 537820) effective from October 16, 2026, following the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Viji Finance Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 537820
- **ISIN:** INE159N01027
- **Security Details:** 1,00,00,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1.80 (Issue Price: Rs. 2.80/-) to Non Promoters on a preferential basis.
- **Effective Date:** October 16, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** August 26, 2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 221000001 to 231000000
- **Lock-in Details:** 1,00,00,000 shares locked in up to April 18, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action notice regarding the listing of securities issued on a preferential basis.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the listing and lock-in details of the securities as specified in the notice.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 26, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** October 16, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** April 18, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up equity capital of Viji Finance Limited via warrant conversion, with the new shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
