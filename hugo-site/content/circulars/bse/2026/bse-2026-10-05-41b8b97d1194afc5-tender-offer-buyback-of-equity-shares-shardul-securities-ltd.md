## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Shardul Securities Ltd. has fixed October 8, 2026 as the record date for the tender offer (buyback) of its equity shares. Eligible shareholders as of the record date will be entitled to participate in the buyback offer.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shardul Securities Ltd. (Scrip Code: 512393)
- Record Date: October 8, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: October 8, 2026
- Settlement Number: DR-731/2026-2027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the record date and ex-entitlement details to ensure accurate settlement and processing of transactions for eligible shareholders.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 5, 2026
- Record Date: October 8, 2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders holding shares in the dematerialised rolling settlement segment as of the record date are eligible to participate in the buyback offer. Trading members should factor in the ex-entitlement date for trading and settlement.
