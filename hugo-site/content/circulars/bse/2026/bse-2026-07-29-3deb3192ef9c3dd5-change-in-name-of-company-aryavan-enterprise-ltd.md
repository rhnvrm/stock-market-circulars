## Summary

Trading members are informed that Aryavan Enterprise Ltd has changed its name to Ecofinity Limited. Consequently, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective August 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Existing Name:** Aryavan Enterprise Ltd
- **New Name:** Ecofinity Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 539455
- **Existing Scrip ID:** ARYAVAN
- **New Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System:** ECOFINITY
- **Existing Abbreviated Name:** ARYAVAN ENTERPRISE LTD
- **New Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System:** ECOFINITY LIMITED

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading systems and records with the new name, scrip ID, and abbreviated name for all future transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 4, 2026
- **Notice Date:** July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating internal trading terminals and records with the new identifiers for Scrip Code 539455.
