## Summary

Astal Laboratories Limited has announced the listing and permission to trade 1,13,300 new equity shares on the BSE, effective from August 17, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 512600
- **ISIN:** INE307N01014
- **Number of Shares:** 1,13,300 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 30/-
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 40/-
- **Effective Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares
- **Lock-in Details:** 1,13,300 shares locked-in up to February 22, 2027 (Distinctive Nos. 43711933 to 43825232)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the lock-in period and trading permissions associated with the newly issued shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** May 25, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** February 22, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action expanding the paid-up capital base of Astal Laboratories Limited through warrant conversion.
