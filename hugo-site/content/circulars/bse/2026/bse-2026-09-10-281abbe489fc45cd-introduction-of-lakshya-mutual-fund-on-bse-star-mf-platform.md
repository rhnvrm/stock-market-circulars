## Summary

Lakshya Mutual Fund has been activated on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 10, 2026. Transactions for NRI and Minor clients can be processed through the platform, subject to country-specific restrictions.

## Key Points

- Lakshya Mutual Fund activated on BSE StAR MF Platform from September 10, 2026.
- Transactions for NRI and Minor clients are enabled under Lakshya Mutual Fund schemes.
- NRI applications from Canada and the United States of America are not accepted.
- Lakshya Overnight Fund - NFO scheme order routing enabled effective September 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that NRI applications from Canada and the United States of America are not accepted for Lakshya Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- Activation Date: September 10, 2026
- Lakshya Overnight Fund - NFO Scheme Enablement Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Extends trading and investment access for Lakshya Mutual Fund schemes to BSE StAR MF platform participants, expanding options for NRI (excluding US and Canada) and Minor clients.
