## Summary

BSE has announced that NJ Mutual Fund schemes will reopen for ongoing subscription, redemption, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting August 03, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20260709-47.

## Key Points

- Affected Fund: NJ Mutual Fund
- Schemes available: NJ Value Fund and NJ Momentum Fund
- Facilities enabled: Subscription, redemption, SIP, STP, and SWP

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate transactions in the specified schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 03, 2026
- Notice Date: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume transactions, SIPs, and redemptions for NJ Value Fund and NJ Momentum Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
