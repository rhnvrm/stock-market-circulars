## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities that satisfy inclusion criteria, move to lower stages, or exit the framework. Effective dates for these surveillance actions are August 28, 2026, and August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin effective August 31, 2026, on open positions as of August 28, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective August 28, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective August 31, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III, and securities moving out of ESM are listed in Annexure II.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) including mandatory 100% margins and migration to Trade-for-Trade settlement for shortlisted scrips.
- Price band restrictions and periodic call auctions applied to securities moving to Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the margin requirements and settlement changes for the securities listed in the respective annexures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage II / Shifting out / Lower Stage: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shift: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin blocking for affected scrips will experience tightening due to 100% margin requirements and movement into the Trade-for-Trade segment.
