## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that NCL RESEARCH AND FINANCIAL SERVICES has fixed a record date for a rights issue of equity shares. Transactions in the equity shares will take place on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: NCL RESEARCH AND FINANCIAL SERVICES
- Scrip Code: 530557
- Record Date: 27/08/2026
- Ex-Right Basis From Date & Sett. No.: 27/08/2026, DR- 703/2026-2027
- Issue Details: 07 (SEVEN) Equity Shares of Re.1/- each for CASH AT PAR on Rights Basis for every 15 (FIFTEEN) Equity Shares held.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the record date and ensure transactions are executed on an ex-rights basis from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 27 August 2026
- Ex-Rights Basis From: 27 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders of NCL RESEARCH AND FINANCIAL SERVICES as of the record date are eligible for the rights issue entitlement. Market participants need to adjust trading strategies according to the ex-rights pricing.
