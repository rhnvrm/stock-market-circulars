## Summary

Choice Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund along with an SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from October 20, 2026, to November 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes Offered:
  - Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF2KCX01111)
  - Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF2KCX01103)
- NFO Window: October 20, 2026 to November 3, 2026 (extended till midnight on November 3, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- SIP/XSIP Registrations: Must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on November 3, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 5, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 5, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 20, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: November 3, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 5, 2026
- Funds Pay-in/Clear Funds Deadline: 6:00 PM on November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides structured operational guidelines and timelines for trading members and investors participating in the Choice Large & Mid Cap Fund NFO via the BSE StAR MF platform.
