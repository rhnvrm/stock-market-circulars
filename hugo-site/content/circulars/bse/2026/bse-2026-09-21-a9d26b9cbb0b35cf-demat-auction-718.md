## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-718/2026-27. The auction offer entry and matching will take place on September 21, 2026, followed by pay-in on September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction applies to Rolling Settlement No. DR-718/2026-27.
- Auction offer entry timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on September 21, 2026.
- Auction matching: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on September 21, 2026.
- Pay-in of the auction will be held on Tuesday, September 22, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.
- Restriction on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they short delivered is removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Members must take utmost care while punching security codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- **Auction Offer Entry:** September 21, 2026 (02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m.)
- **Auction Matching:** September 21, 2026 (02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.)
- **Delivery Statement & Report:** September 21, 2026 (03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.)
- **Money Statement & Final Reports:** September 21, 2026 (03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.)
- **Auction Pay-in:** September 22, 2026 (08:00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational impact for trading members involved in rolling settlement short deliveries and auction processes.
