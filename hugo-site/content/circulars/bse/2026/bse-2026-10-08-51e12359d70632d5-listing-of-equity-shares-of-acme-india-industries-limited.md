## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of Acme India Industries Limited on the SME segment effective from Friday, October 9, 2026, under Scrip Code 544981 and Scrip ID ACMERAIL. The scrip will operate in the 'MT' Group and remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Acme India Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 544981
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: ACMERAIL
- ISIN: INE162701010
- Total Securities Listed: 23,472,200 Equity Shares of face value Rs. 10/- each fully paid up.
- Issue Price: Rs. 196/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 186/-).
- Date of Allotment: October 7, 2026.
- Market Lot: 600 Equity Shares.
- Group / Segment: SME Segment ('MT' Group, Trade-for-Trade for 10 trading days).
- Market Maker: Hem Finlease Private Limited
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form in accordance with SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will participate in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date (October 9, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate actions have been executed by the company to mark lock-in periods in the depository's records.
- Trading members must adhere to the minimum market lot of 600 shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 8, 2026
- Date of Allotment: October 7, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and liquidity for Acme India Industries Limited shares on the BSE SME platform while enforcing standard trade-for-trade surveillance during initial trading sessions.
