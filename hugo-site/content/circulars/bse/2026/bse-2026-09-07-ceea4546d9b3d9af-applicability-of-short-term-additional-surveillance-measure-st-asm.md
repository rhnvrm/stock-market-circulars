## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The applicable surveillance actions take effect from September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- For Stage I, the applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%.
- For Stage II, the applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on September 08, 2026, and new positions created from September 09, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM framework in conjunction with prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 09, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin obligations for shortlisted securities may impact trading liquidity and position sizing for market participants.
