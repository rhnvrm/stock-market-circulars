## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the settlement calendar and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) of Bliss Gvs Pharma Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from July 28, 2026, to August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Bliss Gvs Pharma Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement Number:** 2026092
- **Bidding Dates:** 28/07/2026 to 10/08/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 21/08/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 24/08/2026
- **Delivery Mechanism:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in CDSL and NSDL.

## Regulatory Changes

- None specific other than adherence to revised guidelines for the acquisition of shares through stock exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must ensure early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/ICCL systems prior to the closure of the bidding/confirmation windows.
- Market Type "(Takeover)" and settlement number 2026092 must be used in the depository system.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 28/07/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 10/08/2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 21/08/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 24/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants and custodians dealing with the takeover offer must adhere strictly to the early pay-in timelines to avoid failed obligations and ensure proper processing of client bids.
