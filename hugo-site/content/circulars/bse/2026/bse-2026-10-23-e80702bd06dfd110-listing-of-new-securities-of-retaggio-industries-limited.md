## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission for 3,30,000 new equity shares of Retaggio Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 544391) with effect from October 26, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to promoters upon the conversion of warrants at an issue price of Rs. 26/- each (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 16/-).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Retaggio Industries Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544391
- **ISIN:** INE0KWJ01014
- **Security Details:** 3,30,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 16/-.
- **Issue Type:** Preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- **Allottees:** Promoters.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 18900161 to 19230160.
- **Date of Allotment:** 29/04/2026.
- **Lock-in Details:** 3,30,000 shares locked-in up to 15/05/2028.
- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice for securities issued via warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the lock-in period up to May 15, 2028, for the respective distinctive numbers.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** April 29, 2026
- **Listing & Trading Effective Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 15, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Low impact. Represents standard equity capital expansion through preferential warrant conversion by promoters, ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
