## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 69,47,154 equity shares of Annvrridhhi Ventures Ltd. (Scrip Code: 538539) resulting from the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares on a rights basis are permitted for trading with effect from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Annvrridhhi Ventures Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 538539
- ISIN: INE075K01013
- Details: 69,47,154 equity shares of Rs.10/- each issued on a rights basis, converted from partly paid to fully paid-up.
- Distinctive Numbers: 16200001 to 23147154
- Ranking: These new shares are pari-passu with the existing old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may note the availability of the new securities for trading.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 23 September 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: 24 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital update and operational listing for the company's equity shares with no broader market impact.
