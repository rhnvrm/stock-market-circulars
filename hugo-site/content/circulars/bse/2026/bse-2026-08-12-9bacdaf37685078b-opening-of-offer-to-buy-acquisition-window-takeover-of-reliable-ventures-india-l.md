## Summary

BSE has announced the opening of an "Offer to Buy" (acquisition window) under the Takeover Regulations for the Public Shareholders of Reliable Ventures India Limited (Target Company). The open offer is made by Mr. Chennupati Sarath Kumar and Mr. Vasireddy Sivanag (collectively the "Acquirers") to acquire up to 28,63,354 fully paid-up equity shares of face value of Rs. 10.00 each, representing 26.00% of the voting share capital of the Target Company. The offer price is set at Rs. 21.00 per share, payable in cash. The acquisition window will be open from Friday, August 14, 2026, to Friday, August 28, 2026 (excluding the SEBI holiday on Wednesday, August 26, 2026).

## Key Points

- **Acquirers**: Mr. Chennupati Sarath Kumar (Acquirer 1) and Mr. Vasireddy Sivanag (Acquirer 2).
- **Target Company**: Reliable Ventures India Limited (BSE: 532124, NSE: RELIABVEN).
- **Offer Price**: Rs. 21.00 per share, payable in cash.
- **Offer Size**: Up to 28,63,354 fully paid equity shares of Rs. 10.00 face value each, constituting 26.00% of the voting share capital.
- **Offer Period**: Friday, August 14, 2026, to Friday, August 28, 2026 (excluding August 26, 2026, which is a SEBI holiday).
- **Regulatory Framework**: The offer is made in compliance with Regulations 3(1) and 4 of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, and subsequent amendments.

## Regulatory Changes

- The transaction is conducted through the stock exchange mechanism pursuant to Tender-Offers under Takeover, Buy Back, and Delisting as per SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015, and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 dated December 09, 2016, along with subsequent BSE guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members and Custodians**: Must take note of the operational guidelines, revised mechanisms, and settlement procedures for tender offers/open offers through the stock exchange's acquisition window.
- **For Shareholders**: Eligible public shareholders who wish to participate in the open offer must submit their bids/tender their shares through their respective registered trading members/brokers within the specified tendering period.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: August 12, 2026
- **Tendering Period Open Date**: Friday, August 14, 2026
- **Tendering Period Close Date**: Friday, August 28, 2026
- **SEBI Holiday**: Wednesday, August 26, 2026 (No tendering/trading in acquisition window)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: The open offer establishes a floor price reference of Rs. 21.00 per share for RELIABVEN, which may lead to increased trading volume and support the stock price during the offer period. Public shareholders are provided an exit window.
- **Operational Impact**: Stock exchange systems will host the acquisition window specifically for this corporate action, requiring coordination among stock brokers, custodians, and clearing corporations for share allocation and payout.
