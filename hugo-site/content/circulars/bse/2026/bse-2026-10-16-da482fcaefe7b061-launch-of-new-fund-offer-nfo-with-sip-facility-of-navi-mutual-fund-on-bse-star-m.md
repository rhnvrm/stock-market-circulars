## Summary

NAVI Mutual Fund is launching the NFO of NAVI Multi Asset Omni FOF (Regular Plan - Growth and Direct Plan - Growth) along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription period runs from October 19, 2026, to November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: NAVI MULTI ASSET OMNI FOF - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF959L01HV6) and NAVI MULTI ASSET OMNI FOF - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF959L01HW4).
- NFO Window: October 19, 2026, to November 02, 2026 (extended till midnight on November 02, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 04, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be client-authenticated by 3:00 PM on November 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without funds initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 04, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after allotment date are not eligible for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: October 19, 2026
- NFO Close Date: November 02, 2026 (midnight for lumpsum; SIP authentication by 3:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 04, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: November 04, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform participants and investors with access to the new mutual fund scheme offering and outlines clear operational timelines and fund clearance requirements.
