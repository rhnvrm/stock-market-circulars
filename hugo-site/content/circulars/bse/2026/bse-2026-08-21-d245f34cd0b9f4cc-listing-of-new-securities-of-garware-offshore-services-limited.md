## Summary

Stock exchanges have announced the listing and trading permission for new equity shares of Garware Offshore Services Limited following the conversion of warrants issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Garware Offshore Services Limited (Scrip Code: 501848)
- Securities: 27,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 82/-
- Trading Effective Date: Monday, August 24, 2026
- ISIN: INE446C01013
- Distinctive Numbers: 30743444 to 30770443
- Lock-in Details: 27,000 shares are under lock-in up to 27/02/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure adherence to the specified lock-in periods and trading commencement dates.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 25/06/2026
- Effective Date for Trading: 24/08/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: 27/02/2027

Standard corporate action expanding the equity base of the company through warrant conversion.
