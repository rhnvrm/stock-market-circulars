## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing all Research Analysts (RAs) that the Annual Compliance Audit for FY 2025-26 must be conducted on or before September 30, 2026, in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260504-19.

## Key Points

- The Annual Compliance Audit for FY 2025-26 is mandatory for all Research Analysts.
- Deadline for completion is September 30, 2026.
- The portal for submission is expected to be available by the end of September 2026, with a separate intimation to be issued.
- Two options will be provided for submission: appointment of an auditor through the system or direct submission by the RA.
- Only submissions made via the official Exchange portal will be considered valid; email or other modes will not be accepted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Re-emphasizes mandatory annual compliance verification framework for Research Analysts under Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research Analysts must ensure the compliance audit for FY 2025-26 is completed and submitted through the designated Exchange portal on or before the due date using either system-appointed auditors or direct submission.

## Important Dates

- **Audit Deadline:** September 30, 2026
- **Portal Availability:** Expected by the end of September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Research Analysts must prepare documentation and coordinate with auditors or system options to ensure timely audit completion and platform submission to avoid compliance breaches.
