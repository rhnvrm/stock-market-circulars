## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that the equity shares of Maharaja & Speedex India Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange effective from September 18, 2026, in the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Maharaja & Speedex India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544925
- ISIN: INE0SVD01012
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SPEEDEX
- Number of Securities: 16,345,140 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 186/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 176/-)
- Market Lot: 600 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 16, 2026
- Market Maker: Choice Equity Broking Private Limited
- Registrar: Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialised form and respect the minimum market lot of 600 shares.
- Lock-in details and shareholding patterns are as per Annexure I and Annexure II of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: September 18, 2026
- Date of Allotment: September 16, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Maharaja & Speedex India Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with initial trade-for-trade restrictions to monitor settlement integrity.
