## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 9,04,800 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Shellz India Limited at Rs 179 per equity share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shellz India Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue / IPO
- Anchor Portion Subscribed: 9,04,800 Equity Shares
- Issue Price / Allocation Price: Rs 179/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: October 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

Trading members take note of the anchor investor allocation details for information and reference.
