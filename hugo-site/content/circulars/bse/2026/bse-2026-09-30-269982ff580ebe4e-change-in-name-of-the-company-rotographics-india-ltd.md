## Summary

BSE has announced the change of name for Rotographics (India) Ltd to Novalum Materials Limited. The new scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective October 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Rotographics (India) Ltd
- New Name: Novalum Materials Limited
- Scrip Code: 539922
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus System): NOVAMAT
- New Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus System): NOVAMAT

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of company name and trading symbols across exchange records and trading systems.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to take note of the change and update their back-office and trading systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact on trading operations, restricted to cosmetic updates of company name and scrip identifiers.
