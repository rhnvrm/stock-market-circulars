## Summary

BSE has announced the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Marco Secure Solutions Limited on September 11, 2026. The document is made available on stock exchange websites for public comments for a period of at least 21 days as specified under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Marco Secure Solutions Limited
- Filing Date: September 11, 2026
- Segment: SME
- Public Comments Deadline: Within 21 days from the date of the notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments on the Draft Offer Document must be submitted via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days from the notice date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 11, 2026
- Public Comments Closing Date: October 2, 2026 (approx. 21 days from notice)

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites stakeholder feedback regarding the upcoming SME public issue of Marco Secure Solutions Limited.
