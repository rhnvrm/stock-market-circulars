## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 16 scrips, effective from September 16, 2026, as part of its surveillance framework.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 16 scrips.
- Effective date: September 16, 2026.
- Scrips affected include Arnold Holdings Ltd, Davin Sons Retail Ltd, Dindigul Farm Product Ltd, and others with revised limits ranging from 2% to 20%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of price band limits for listed equities based on surveillance review.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure orders are placed within the new permissible limits.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Limits extreme price volatility for the affected scrips during trading sessions.
