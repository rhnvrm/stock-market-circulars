## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, periodical review, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective dates for various surveillance actions range from September 10, 2026, to September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable on all open positions as on September 11, 2026, and new positions created from September 15, 2026 onwards for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework and subject to Lower Price Bands effective September 10, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Securities continuing in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective September 15, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- Securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective September 10, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Shortlisting is based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions under the revised LT-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on specified open and new positions and note the change in price bands and group transfers for impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Lower Price Bands / Movement to higher stages / Moving out: September 10, 2026
- Base date for open positions subject to 100% margin: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and price band restrictions for short-listed securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
