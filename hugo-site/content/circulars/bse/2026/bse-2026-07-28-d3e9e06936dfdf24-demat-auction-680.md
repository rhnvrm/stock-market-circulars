## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-680/2026-27. The auction offers will be accepted on trading terminals on Tuesday, July 28, 2026, with the pay-in scheduled for Wednesday, July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Auction Offer Entry:** July 28, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- **Auction Matching:** July 28, 2026, from 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- **Delivery Statement and Reports:** July 28, 2026, from 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- **Money Statement & Final Reports:** July 28, 2026, from 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- **Pay-in Date:** Wednesday, July 29, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- **Uniform Membership:** Following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment, settlement is conducted through a Clearing Member of a Trading Member. Restrictions preventing trading members from offering securities in auction for settlements where they short-delivered have been removed.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must exercise care while entering securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Members should verify cut-off rates on the Auction Offer Entry Screen and check corporate action notices (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- **Auction Date:** July 28, 2026
- **Pay-in Date:** July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations notice ensuring smooth clearing and settlement of short-delivered securities through the auction window.
