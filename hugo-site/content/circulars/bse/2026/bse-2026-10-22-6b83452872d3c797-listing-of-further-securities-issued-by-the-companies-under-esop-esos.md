## Summary

BSE Limited has notified the listing and admission for trading of further equity shares issued by eight companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS), effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026.
- **Total Companies:** 8 companies have issued further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- **Participating Companies:** 
  1. Aditya Birla Capital Limited (21,081 shares, ISIN: INE674K01013, Scrip Code: 540691)
  2. Capital Small Finance Bank Limited (25,000 shares, ISIN: INE646H01017, Scrip Code: 544120)
  3. Digicontent Limited (61,350 shares, ISIN: INE03JI01017, Scrip Code: 542685)
  4. Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited (4,574 shares, ISIN: INE063P01018, Scrip Code: 543243)
  5. Federal Bank Limited (99,954 shares, ISIN: INE171A01029, Scrip Code: 500469)
  6. Gandhar Oil Refinery (India) Limited (9,290 shares, ISIN: INE717W01049, Scrip Code: 544029)
  7. Sai Life Sciences Limited (54,500 shares, ISIN: INE570L01029, Scrip Code: 544306)
  8. Shriram Finance Limited (41,758 shares, ISIN: INE721A01047, Scrip Code: 511218)
- **Lock-in Status:** Shares are listed without additional lock-in restrictions.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine corporate action notice regarding the listing of ESOP/ESOS shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure accurate updating of share capital and ISIN records in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion through employee stock options with negligible market impact on share prices or liquidity.
