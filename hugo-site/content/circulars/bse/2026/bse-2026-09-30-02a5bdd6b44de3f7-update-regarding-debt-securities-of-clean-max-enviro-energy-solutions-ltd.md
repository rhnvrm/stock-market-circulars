## Summary

In continuation to Exchange Notice No. 20260814-14, BSE has issued an update regarding the debt securities of Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Ltd. Effective October 01, 2026, the company's Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) are transitioning from unsecured to secured status along with a corresponding ISIN change.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 977267
- Existing ISIN: INE647U08039 (Unsecured NCDs)
- Revised ISIN: INE647U07049 (Secured NCDs)
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The mode of security for the NCDs is revised from unsecured NCDs to secured NCDs.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to note the revised terms and ISIN for trading effective from October 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides enhanced security for debenture holders as debt securities transition from unsecured to secured NCDs with updated ISIN.
