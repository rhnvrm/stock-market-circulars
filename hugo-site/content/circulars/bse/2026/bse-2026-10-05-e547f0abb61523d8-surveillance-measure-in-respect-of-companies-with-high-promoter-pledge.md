## Summary

BSE has announced surveillance measures regarding companies with high promoter pledge in continuation of earlier notices. Securities meeting the criteria (Annexure I) will attract a minimum 35% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective October 8, 2026. Securities in Annexure II are moving out of the framework effective October 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Minimum 35% margin applicable on securities in Annexure I for Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Effective date for margin requirement: October 8, 2026 (applicable on all open positions as of October 7, 2026, and new positions created from October 8, 2026).
- Securities listed in Annexure II will move out of the framework effective October 6, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Representations regarding placement in the framework must be submitted by 5:00 PM on October 6, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of BSE surveillance framework for companies with high promoter pledge, originally initiated via notices dated October 24, 2019, and April 17, 2020. Imposes stricter margin requirements (minimum 35%) to manage market risk and surveillance.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure the collection of minimum 35% margin on applicable securities in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments. Companies wishing to make representations against their inclusion must do so by 5:00 PM on October 6, 2026, via bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Representation Deadline: October 06, 2026 (by 5:00 PM)
- Exit Effective Date (Annexure II): October 06, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date for Open Positions: October 07, 2026
- Margin Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and market participants dealing in securities with high promoter pledge will experience increased margin requirements (35%), which may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the affected scrips.
