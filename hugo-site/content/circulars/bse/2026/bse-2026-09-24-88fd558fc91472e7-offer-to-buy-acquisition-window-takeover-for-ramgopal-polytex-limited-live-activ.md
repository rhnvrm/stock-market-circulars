## Summary

BSE has announced the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy through the Acquisition Window (Takeover) for RAMGOPAL POLYTEX LIMITED. The offer period runs from September 25, 2026, to October 9, 2026, at an offer price of Rs. 17.10 per equity share for up to 37,70,000 equity shares.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** RAMGOPAL POLYTEX LIMITED
- **Security Symbol:** RAMGOPOLY
- **ISIN:** INE410D01017
- **Scrip Code:** 514223
- **Offer Size:** Upto 37,70,000 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 17.10
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00
- **Opening Date:** 25th September 2026
- **Closing Date:** 9th October 2026
- **Session Timings:** 9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. (Bid offering and modifications/cancellation; take-up on last day up to 4.00 p.m.)

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this notice pertains specifically to the operational schedule of an ongoing takeover offer under existing acquisition window frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians should note that if no securities are accepted, no end-of-issue files will be generated on the Extranet.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** 25th September 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** 9th October 2026
- **Give-up / Take-up Period:** 25th September 2026 to 8th October 2026
- **Give-up (Last Day):** 9th October 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- **Take-up (Last Day):** 9th October 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly participation of eligible shareholders and trading members in the takeover acquisition window for Ramgopal Polytex Limited, ensuring proper settlement and platform accessibility via iBBS and RTRMS.
