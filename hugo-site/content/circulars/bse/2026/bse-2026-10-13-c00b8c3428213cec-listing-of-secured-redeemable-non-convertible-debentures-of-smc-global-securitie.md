## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of SMC Global Securities Limited on the exchange effective from Wednesday, October 14, 2026, under the "F GROUP".

## Key Points

- Securities: Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of SMC Global Securities Limited.
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026.
- Trading Group: F GROUP.
- Dematerialization: As per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, bonds will trade strictly in dematerialized form in unit market lots (one bond).
- Registrar and Share Transfer Agent: MUFG Intime India Private Limited (formerly Link Intime India Private Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing notice adhering to SEBI guidelines for dematerialized debt trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with dematerialization requirements and unit market lot specifications.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing with minimal direct market impact on equity shareholders, providing debt instrument liquidity on the exchange.
