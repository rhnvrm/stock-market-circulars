## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in the Commercial Paper of Solar Industries India Limited due to its maturity on the redemption date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Solar Industries India Limited
- Scrip Code: 731937
- Particulars: SIIL-17-9-26-CP
- ISIN: INE343H14220
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid Commercial Paper of the company with effect from September 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 15, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard procedure for maturity and suspension of trading in Commercial Paper with no broader market impact.
