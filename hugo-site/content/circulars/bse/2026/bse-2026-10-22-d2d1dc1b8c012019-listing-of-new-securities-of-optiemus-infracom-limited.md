## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 7,38,081 new equity shares of Optiemus Infracom Limited with effect from October 23, 2026. These shares are issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Optiemus Infracom Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 530135
- **ISIN:** INE350C01017
- **Securities Issued:** 7,38,081 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each at an issue price of Rs. 672.25/- (including premium of Rs. 662.25/-).
- **Allottees:** Non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.
- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 89261120 to 89999200.
- **Lock-in Details:** 7,38,081 shares locked-in up to 10/05/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the new securities and their lock-in status.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 4, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** May 10, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up capital of Optiemus Infracom Limited due to preferential conversion of warrants with minimal market impact.
