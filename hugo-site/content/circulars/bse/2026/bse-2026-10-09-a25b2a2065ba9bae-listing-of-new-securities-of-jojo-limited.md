## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission of 1,80,00,000 equity shares of Jojo Limited (Scrip Code - 531910) with effect from October 12, 2026. These shares were issued at a premium of Rs. 20/- per share (face value Rs. 5/- each, issue price Rs. 25/-) to promoters and non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Jojo Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 531910
- **ISIN:** INE312M01024
- **Securities Issued:** 1,80,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 5/- each at an issue price of Rs. 25/- (including premium of Rs. 20/-).
- **Allottees:** Promoters and Non-Promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026.
- **Date of Allotment:** May 7, 2026.
- **Lock-in Details:** 
  - 80,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 50961401 to 58961400) locked-in upto May 12, 2028.
  - 10,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 58961401 to 59961400) locked-in upto May 11, 2028.
  - 10,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 59961401 to 60961400) locked-in upto May 12, 2027.
  - 80,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 60961401 to 68961400) locked-in upto May 11, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants should take note of the new securities listing and associated lock-in periods.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** May 7, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** May 11, 2027, May 12, 2027, May 11, 2028, and May 12, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity capital base of Jojo Limited through preferential warrant conversion. No systemic market impact.
