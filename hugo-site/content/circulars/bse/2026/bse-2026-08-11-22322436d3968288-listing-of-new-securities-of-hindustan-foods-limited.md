## Summary

BSE has informed market participants about the listing and permission for trading of 4,81,39,085 equity shares of Hindustan Foods Limited (Scrip Code: 519126) with effect from Wednesday, August 12, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement between Avalon Cosmetics Private Limited and Vanity Case India Private Limited and Hindustan Foods Limited.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Hindustan Foods Limited (Scrip Code: 519126)
- Security Details: 4,81,39,085 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each issued pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement.
- ISIN Number: INE254N01026
- Distinctive Numbers: 119481763 to 167620847
- Date of Allotment: May 21, 2026
- Note: 4,64,58,145 shares of Hindustan Foods Limited shall be cancelled on account of cross holding held by Vanity Case India Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and depository participants need to note the ISIN and distinctive numbers for trading and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: August 12, 2026
- Date of Allotment: May 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of the equity base of Hindustan Foods Limited following the corporate restructuring and scheme of arrangement.
