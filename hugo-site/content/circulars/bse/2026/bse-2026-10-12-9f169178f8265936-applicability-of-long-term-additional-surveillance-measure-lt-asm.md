## Summary

BSE has announced the application and revision of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) frameworks for various securities, in accordance with prior notice guidelines. The measures include 100% margin requirements, price band revisions, and shifts to specific trading groups.

## Key Points

- 100% margins are applicable effective October 15, 2026, on all open positions as on October 14, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities are moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework along with lower price bands effective October 13, 2026.
- Specific securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective October 15, 2026.
- Selected securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework with price bands reinstated to previous levels.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is a market surveillance measure, not an adverse regulatory action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of 100% margin requirements on securities shortlisted under LT-ASM Annexure I (Part A).
- Lower price band applicability and movement to higher stages for applicable securities.
- Transfer of specific securities to T, XT, MT, or TS trading groups.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions on applicable scrips.
- Members can direct clarifications to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date for Price Bands & Higher Stages / Exits: October 13, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities listed under the LT-ASM framework will experience increased margin funding requirements (100% margins) and trading restrictions such as lower price bands and group transfers, impacting liquidity and position management for the affected scrips.
