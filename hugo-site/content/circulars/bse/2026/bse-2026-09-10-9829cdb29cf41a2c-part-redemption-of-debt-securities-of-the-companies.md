## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the part redemption and payment of interest on non-convertible debentures (NCDs) for several companies. Trading members and market participants are informed of the revised face values and relevant record and ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260910-13, dated September 10, 2026.
- Covers part redemption and interest payment for companies including IKF Finance Limited, Vistaar Financial Services Pvt Ltd, Laxmi India Finance Limited, ReNew Solar Energy (Jharkhand Five) Private Limited, Oxyzo Financial Services Limited, Rajgarh Transmission Limited, and Aavas Financiers Limited.
- Trading in debentures will take place with reduced face values effective from the specified dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable (corporate action notice).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the reduced face values and settlement details for the respective NCDs.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates range between September 11, 2026, and September 14, 2026.
- Ex-Dates: September 11, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects holders of the specified NCDs regarding cash flows from part redemption and interest payouts, adjusting trading lots/values to the reduced face value.
