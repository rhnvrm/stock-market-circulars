## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of Peshwa Wheat Limited on the SME segment effective October 1, 2026. A total of 19,028,196 equity shares of face value Rs. 10 each are listed at an issue price of Rs. 101 per share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Peshwa Wheat Limited
- Scrip Code: 544955
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: PESHWA
- ISIN: INE0SR101016
- Total Securities: 19,028,196 Equity Shares (Face Value Rs. 10/- each, Issue Price Rs. 101/- per share)
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 19028196
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Segment: SME ('MT' Group of Securities)
- Trade-for-Trade Segment: Applicable for 10 trading days as per SEBI guidelines.
- Market Maker: Bhansali Value Creations Private Limited
- Registrar: Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; compliance with SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, placing the scrip in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trading is conducted in dematerialized form and adheres to the minimum market lot of 1,200 equity shares. Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 1, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 29, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Peshwa Wheat Limited shares on the BSE SME platform, providing liquidity to investors while enforcing initial trade-for-trade surveillance controls.
