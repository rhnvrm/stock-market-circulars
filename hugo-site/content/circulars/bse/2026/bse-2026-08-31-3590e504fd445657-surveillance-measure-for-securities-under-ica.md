## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies under the Inter Creditor Agreement (ICA) effective from September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure have satisfied the criteria under ASM for Companies as per Inter Creditor Agreement (ICA).
- The framework becomes effective on September 01, 2026.
- Securities placed under this framework are reviewed on a periodical basis.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under the Inter Creditor Agreement (ICA) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and members are requested to note the securities satisfying the ASM criteria and comply with the associated trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the shortlisted securities will be subject to surveillance measures in conjunction with other prevailing rules.
