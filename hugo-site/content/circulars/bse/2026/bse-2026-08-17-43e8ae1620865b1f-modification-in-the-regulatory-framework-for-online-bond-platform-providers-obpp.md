## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding modifications in the regulatory framework for Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs), incorporating measures to promote ease of doing business, following SEBI Circular No. HO/17/11/(2)2026-DDHS-POD1/I/18769/2026 dated August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- References SEBI Circular dated August 14, 2026.
- Focuses on modifying the regulatory framework for Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs).
- Includes measures aimed at promoting ease of doing business in the debt segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates to the operational and regulatory guidelines for Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs) as specified in the attached SEBI circular (Annexure A).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and relevant entities are required to take note of the revised guidelines and ensure strict compliance.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective based on SEBI Circular dated August 14, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines operations for Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs) and enhances ease of doing business within the debt market segment.
