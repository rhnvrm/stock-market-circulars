## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework effective from September 24, 2026. Trading members are required to comply with the applicable surveillance actions and margin requirements.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the Annexure will move to higher GSM stages starting September 24, 2026.
- Applicable surveillance actions depend on the assigned GSM Stage (Stages I, II, III, and IV).
- Settlements for affected securities will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance measures under the existing GSM framework for identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Strict adherence to applicable margin rates, price bands, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) as specified per GSM stage.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity for the securities moved into higher GSM stages will be restricted due to trade-to-trade settlement, higher margin requirements, and mandatory Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
