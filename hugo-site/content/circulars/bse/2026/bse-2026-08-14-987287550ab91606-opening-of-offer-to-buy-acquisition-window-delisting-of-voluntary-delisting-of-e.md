## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the offer to buy for the voluntary delisting of equity shares of Avadh Rail Infra Limited through the acquisition window and reverse book-building process.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Avadh Rail Infra Limited.
- **Acquirer:** Abhishek Saraff.
- **Offer Size:** 4,765 Equity Shares representing 0.89% of the Equity Share Capital held by Public Shareholders.
- **Floor Price:** Rs. 4,660 per equity share.
- **Bidding Period:** Tuesday, August 18, 2026, to Monday, August 24, 2026.
- **Exchange Listing:** Listed on The Calcutta Stock Exchange Limited (CSE).

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021, and SEBI circulars on the mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to Tender-Offers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the reverse book-building process and timeline for tendering shares.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Public shareholders of Avadh Rail Infra Limited can tender their shares through the acquisition window at the floor price of Rs. 4,660 per share during the specified dates.
