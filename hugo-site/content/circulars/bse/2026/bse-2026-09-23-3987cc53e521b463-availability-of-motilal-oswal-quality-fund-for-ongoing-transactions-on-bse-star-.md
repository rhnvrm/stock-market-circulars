## Summary

Motilal Oswal Quality Fund has reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and related facilities including SIP, STP, and SWP on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 23, 2026, following notice no. 20260827-32.

## Key Points

- Reopening of ongoing transactions for Motilal Oswal Quality Fund on BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Facilities available include subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Covers Regular and Direct plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants must note the ISIN details for order placement on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume trading and operational activities for the specified Motilal Oswal Quality Fund schemes on the BSE platform.
