## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of subscriptions through lump sum investments, switch-ins, and fresh registrations under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) for the Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua Fund of Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Resumption applies to Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua Fund of Fund.
- Includes lump sum investments (fresh/additional purchases), switch-ins, and fresh registrations under SIP and STP.
- Effective date of implementation: August 03, 2026.
- Follows previous temporary suspension notice (Notice No. 20260723-44).

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the resumption while executing transactions in the aforesaid scheme on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume normal transactional activities including SIPs and lump sum investments in the Baroda BNP Paribas Aqua Fund of Fund on the platform.
