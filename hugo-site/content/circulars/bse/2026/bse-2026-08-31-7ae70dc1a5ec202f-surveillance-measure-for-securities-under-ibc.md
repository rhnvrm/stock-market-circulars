## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective September 01, 2026. Securities satisfying the criteria are placed under the framework, while others move out or are classified based on non-promoter holding criteria.

## Key Points

- New securities meeting the criteria are subjected to the IBC surveillance framework effective September 01, 2026 (Annexure I).
- List of securities moving out of the IBC framework effective September 01, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided in Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% listed in Annexure IV.
- Action is purely a market surveillance measure and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic review of securities under the IBC surveillance framework.
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the securities entering, exiting, or remaining under the IBC surveillance framework and adjust trading and risk parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in affected securities will be governed by specific surveillance measures and price band restrictions associated with the IBC framework, impacting liquidity and volatility management for these scrips.
