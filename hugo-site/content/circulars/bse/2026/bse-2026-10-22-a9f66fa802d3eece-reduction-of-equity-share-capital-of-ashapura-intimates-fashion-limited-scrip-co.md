## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the reduction of equity share capital of Ashapura Intimates Fashion Limited (Scrip code: 535467) pursuant to the Relief Order passed by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Mumbai Bench, under the provisions of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) 2016. The record date and ex-date is set for October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Ashapura Intimates Fashion Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 535467
- **Record Date / Ex-Date:** October 26, 2026
- **NCLT Reference:** DR-742/2026-2027
- **Capital Reduction Details:** The entire existing shareholding of promoters and promoter group shall be cancelled and extinguished without any further payment. Public shareholding shall be reduced to 5% from the existing shareholding.
- **Trading Status:** Trading in equity shares of the company is currently under suspension.

## Regulatory Changes

Capital reduction executed under the Relief Order of the NCLT Mumbai Bench pursuant to the provisions of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the capital reduction, extinguishment of promoter shares, and reduction of public shareholding to 5% with effect from the record/ex-date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Record Date / Ex-Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Substantial restructuring of equity capital involving total wipeout of promoter equity and significant reduction in public shareholding for a company currently under trading suspension.
