## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in several scrips in the recent past. The Exchange sought clarifications from multiple companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information. The notice details companies that have submitted their replies alongside those whose clarifications remain awaited.

## Key Points

- Observed significant price/volume movement across multiple listed scrips.
- Clarifications received and attached from 9 companies, including Allied Digital Services Ltd, AVT Natural Products Ltd, JSW Holdings Ltd, and others.
- Replies are awaited from 16 companies, including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, and others.
- Aimed at maintaining market transparency and investor protection through timely disclosures.

## Regulatory Changes

- No direct regulatory changes introduced; enforces ongoing surveillance and disclosure norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed companies observing significant price or volume movements must promptly respond to Exchange surveillance queries and provide necessary clarifications.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 12 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances market transparency regarding abnormal price/volume activity and alerts investors to pending clarifications from specific companies.
