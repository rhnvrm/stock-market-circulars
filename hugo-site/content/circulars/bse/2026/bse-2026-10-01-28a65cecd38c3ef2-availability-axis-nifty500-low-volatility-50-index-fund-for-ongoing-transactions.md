## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the Axis Nifty500 Low Volatility 50 Index Fund for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform, pursuant to previous Notice No. 20260908-22.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Axis Nifty500 Low Volatility 50 Index Fund
- Eligible Plans & ISINs:
  - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1606)
  - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1614)
- Available Facilities: Subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate transactions for investors in the specified schemes starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 1, 2026
- Effective Date: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and market participants to execute ongoing transactions and systematic investment plans in the Axis Nifty500 Low Volatility 50 Index Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
