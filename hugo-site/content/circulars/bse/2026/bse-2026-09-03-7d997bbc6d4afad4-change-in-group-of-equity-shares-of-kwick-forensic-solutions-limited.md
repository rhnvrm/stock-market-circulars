## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of KWICK FORENSIC SOLUTIONS LIMITED (Scrip Code: 544895) from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: KWICK FORENSIC SOLUTIONS LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544895
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: September 18, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange’s Notice No. 20260902-50 dated September 02, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading framework for the company's shares is being updated to allow normal rolling settlement instead of trade for trade settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the segment change and update their systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 03, 2026
- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This shift allows standard rolling settlement for transactions in the company's equity shares, improving liquidity and trading convenience for market participants.
