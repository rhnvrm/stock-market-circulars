## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of multiple scrips effective from August 24, 2026, under Notice No. 20260821-36.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for various scrips across different percentages (e.g., 1%, 2%, 5%, 10%).
- Effective date of revision is August 24, 2026.
- Applicable to the Equity segment under Department DOSS.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of price limits for specified scrips to manage market volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure systems are updated to reflect the revised price bands for the mentioned scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Limits intraday price movements for the affected scrips according to the newly assigned percentage bands.
