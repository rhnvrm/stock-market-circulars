## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing the new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of BLS E-Services Ltd consequent to its stock split from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/- per share. The new ISIN (INE0NLT01028) becomes effective for trades starting from the Ex-Date of October 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: BLS E-SERVICES LTD
- Scrip Code: 544107
- New ISIN Number: INE0NLT01028
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/-
- Ex-Date: October 6, 2026 (DR- 729/2026-2027)
- Reference to prior Exchange Notice No. 20260922-39 dated September 22, 2026.
