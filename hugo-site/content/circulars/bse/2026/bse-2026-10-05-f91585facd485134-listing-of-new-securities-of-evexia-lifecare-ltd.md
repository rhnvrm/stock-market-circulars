## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission for trading of 34,07,14,286 equity shares of Evexia Lifecare Ltd. with effect from October 6, 2026. These shares are issued at par pursuant to the conversion of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs) of the company.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 524444
- ISIN Number: INE313M01030
- Number of Shares: 34,07,14,286 Equity shares of Re. 1/- each
- Purpose: Conversion of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs)
- Date of Allotment: 11/09/2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 1877328996 to 2218043281
- Effective Date of Trading: October 6, 2026
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

Standard listing and trading approval for equity shares issued upon conversion of FCCBs pursuant to SEBI regulations and BSE operational guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records and systems regarding the increased equity share capital and ISIN INE313M01030.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing and Trading: October 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing of 34.07 crore equity shares via FCCB conversion expands the paid-up capital of Evexia Lifecare Ltd., impacting market liquidity and share float.
