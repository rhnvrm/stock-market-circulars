## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading in these debentures/bonds will take place with reduced face values starting from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **Issuers Involved:** Includes SRG Housing Finance Limited, Lodha Developers Limited, Casablanca Industries Private Limited, Keertana Finserv Limited, Greaves Finance Limited, India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, Kanakadurga Finance Limited, Purva Oak Private Limited, and Varthana Finance Private Limited.
- **Action Type:** Part redemption and interest payment on NCDs/Bonds along with face value reduction.
- **Trading Update:** Trading will occur with reduced face values effective from the specified ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard corporate action processing for debt segment securities involving capital reduction via part redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the reduced face values, record dates, and ISIN details for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Record / Ex-Dates:** 
  - October 14, 2026: SRG Housing Finance Limited, Lodha Developers Limited
  - October 15, 2026: Casablanca Industries Private Limited, Keertana Finserv Limited, Greaves Finance Limited, India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited
  - October 16, 2026: Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, Kanakadurga Finance Limited, Purva Oak Private Limited (multiple tranches), Varthana Finance Private Limited

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding the affected NCDs will receive part redemption payouts and interest as per the specified record dates, with subsequent trading continuing on the reduced face value basis.
