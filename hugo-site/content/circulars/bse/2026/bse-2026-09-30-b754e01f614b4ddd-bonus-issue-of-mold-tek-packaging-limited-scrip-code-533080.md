## Summary

Mold-Tek Packaging Limited has announced a record date of October 9, 2026, for its bonus issue of equity shares in the ratio of 1:1 (one bonus equity share of Rs. 5/- each for every one existing equity share of Rs. 5/- each).

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: Mold-Tek Packaging Limited (Scrip Code: 533080)
- Record Date: October 9, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: October 9, 2026
- Bonus Ratio: 1:1 (One bonus equity share for every one existing equity share held)
- Expected Allotment Date: On or around October 12, 2026 (33,228,914 equity shares to be allotted)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard corporate action notification under listing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the ex-bonus date and record date for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Record Date: October 9, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: October 9, 2026
- Allotment Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders will receive additional shares in a 1:1 ratio, resulting in capital expansion and price adjustment on an ex-bonus basis starting October 9, 2026.
