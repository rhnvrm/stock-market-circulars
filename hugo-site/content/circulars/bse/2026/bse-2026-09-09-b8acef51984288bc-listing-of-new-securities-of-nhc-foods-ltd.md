## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of 18,18,79,020 new equity shares of NHC Foods Ltd. with effect from September 10, 2026. These shares were issued at par upon the conversion of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** NHC Foods Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 517554
- **ISIN Number:** INE141C01036
- **Security Details:** 18,18,79,020 Equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at par pursuant to conversion of FCCBs.
- **Date of Allotment:** 25/08/2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 761927041 to 943806060
- **Effective Date:** September 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing and admission for trading of the new securities ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** 25/08/2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** 10/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up equity capital due to the conversion of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds into equity shares.
