## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from September 04, 2026. These measures include applicable margin rates, price bands, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the Annexure will move to higher GSM stages starting September 04, 2026.
- Stage I: 100% margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade with a 5% price band and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value for buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade-for-trade with a 5% price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and 100% ASD to be deposited by buyers (with no upward movement for Stage IV).
- Settlement will be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance actions under the existing GSM framework provisions published on February 23, 2017.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities and note that no netting off is permitted for trade-to-trade settlements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 03 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: 04 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity for the affected securities may be heavily restricted due to stringent margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and weekly trading limitations in higher GSM stages.
