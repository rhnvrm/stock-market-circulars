## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of specific securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The changes take effect from September 15, 2026, and impose enhanced surveillance actions, margin requirements, and trading restrictions on the affected securities.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure will move to higher GSM stages.
- Effective date for the surveillance measures is September 15, 2026.
- Settlement for these securities will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Specific surveillance actions apply based on the assigned GSM stage (Stages I to IV).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance actions as per the provisions of the GSM Framework published on February 23, 2017:
- **Stage I:** Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- **Stage II:** Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value to be deposited by buyers.
- **Stage III:** Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week), and ASD of 100% of trade value.
- **Stage IV:** Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week with no upward movement, and ASD of 100% of trade value.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules and prohibition of netting off.
- Deposit applicable margins and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) as required by the assigned stage.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 11 Sep 2026
- **Effective Date:** 15 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities are likely to be restricted due to trade-to-trade settlement, weekly trading windows (for higher stages), and heavy margin/ASD requirements.
