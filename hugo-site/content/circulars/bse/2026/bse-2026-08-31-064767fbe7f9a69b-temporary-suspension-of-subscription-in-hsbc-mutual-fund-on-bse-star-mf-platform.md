## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the temporary suspension of lumpsum investments, switch-in transactions, and new Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP-In) registrations under certain schemes of HSBC Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Temporary suspension applies to HSBC Mutual Fund schemes including HSBC NIFTY NEXT 50 INDEX FUND and HSBC NIFTY 50 INDEX FUND.
- Lumpsum investments (fresh and additional purchases), switch-in transactions, and new SIP/STP-In registrations have been disabled on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Applications received after the cut-off time on August 31, 2026, are not accepted or processed.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIPs and STPs) prior to the effective date continue to be processed normally.
- Redemption, Switch-Out, STP-Out, and SWP transactions continue to be permitted and processed as usual.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational changes implemented on the BSE StAR MF platform restricting fresh subscriptions and inbound systematic registrations for specified HSBC AMC schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants are advised to note the suspension of fresh subscription channels and ensure no new applications or fresh SIP/STP-In registrations are submitted for the affected schemes after the cut-off time.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026
- Cut-off Time / Notice Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and distributors cannot initiate fresh investments, additional purchases, or new SIPs/STPs in the specified HSBC index funds through the BSE StAR MF platform, while existing systematic investments and exit options remain unaffected.
