## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of subscriptions for Baroda BNP Paribas Funds Aqua Fund of Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, following a previous temporary suspension.

## Key Points

- Resumption applies to Lump Sum investments (fresh and additional purchases), Switch-ins, and fresh registrations under Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP).
- Effective immediately with respect to communication received from Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund.
- Modifies the previous temporary suspension notified via Notice No. 20260819-26 dated August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the resumption while executing transactions in the aforesaid scheme on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 2, 2026
- Reference Notice Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors can resume investments, switch-ins, and setup new SIPs/STPs in Baroda BNP Paribas Funds Aqua Fund of Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform without restrictions.
