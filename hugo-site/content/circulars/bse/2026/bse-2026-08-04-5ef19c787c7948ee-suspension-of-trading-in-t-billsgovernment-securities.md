## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in certain T-bills and Government Securities due to their maturity, effective from August 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 810717
- Particulars: 76GJSDL26
- ISIN: IN1520160087
- Effective Date of Suspension: August 05, 2026 (DR- 687/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid T-bills/Government Securities with effect from August 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice for trading members regarding matured government securities.
