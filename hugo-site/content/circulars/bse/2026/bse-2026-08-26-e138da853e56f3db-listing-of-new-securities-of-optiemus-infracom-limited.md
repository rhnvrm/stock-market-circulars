## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission for trading of 5,72,336 new equity shares of Optiemus Infracom Limited (Scrip Code - 530135) with effect from August 27, 2026. These shares are issued to promoters and non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Optiemus Infracom Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 530135
- **ISIN:** INE350C01017
- **Number of Shares:** 5,72,336 equity shares
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10/- each
- **Issue/Premium Price:** Issued at a premium of Rs. 662.25/- (Issue Price: Rs. 672.25/-)
- **Effective Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must adhere to the specified lock-in periods and distribution details for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 21/07/2026
- **Listing Date:** 27/08/2026
- **Lock-in Upto (3,75,000 shares):** 05/03/2028
- **Lock-in Upto (1,97,336 shares):** 05/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up capital of the company through preferential warrant conversion with standard lock-in provisions.
