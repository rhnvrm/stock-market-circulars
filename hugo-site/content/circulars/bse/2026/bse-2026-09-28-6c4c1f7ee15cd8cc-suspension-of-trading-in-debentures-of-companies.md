## Summary

BSE has issued notice no. 20260928-32 dated September 28, 2026, informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and/or redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of the specified companies during the applicable ex-dates and record dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260928-32, dated September 28, 2026.
- Segment: Debt Segment, Listing Operations.
- Affected Issuers include Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, Embassy Office Parks REIT, Manba Finance Limited, Kosamattam Finance Limited, Sikka Ports & Terminals Ltd, Muthoot Capital Services Ltd., InCred Financial Services Limited, Muthoot Fincorp Limited, Indel Money Limited, Lendingkart Finance Limited, JM Financial Home Loans Limited, Vivriti Capital Limited, and Navi Finserv Limited.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures as per the schedule.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard exchange procedure for temporary suspension of trading in debt securities ahead of record dates for interest and redemption payments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no transactions are executed in the listed debentures during the restricted ex-date and record date windows.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates and Ex-Dates fall between October 1, 2026, and October 15, 2026, depending on the specific scrip.

## Impact Assessment

- Temporary trading restrictions on specific debt instruments to facilitate accurate processing of interest payments and redemptions by issuers and depositories.
