## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 24 scrips in the Equity segment, effective from September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands for 24 scrips have been revised from their existing levels.
- The changes become effective starting September 11, 2026.
- Affected scrips include Anmol India Ltd, Caprihans India Ltd, Novartis India Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

Modification of daily price limits for specified securities as part of exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the revised price bands while placing orders in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: 11 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity for the listed scrips will be subject to the newly established price fluctuation limits.
