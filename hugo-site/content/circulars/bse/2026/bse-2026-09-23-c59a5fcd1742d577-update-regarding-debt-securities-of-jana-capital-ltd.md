## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the debt securities (Non-Convertible Debentures) of Jana Capital Limited. Trading members are informed that specific scrip codes will be available for trading with revised terms, including updated ISINs, scrip long names, and maturity dates, effective from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised terms apply to NCDs of Jana Capital Limited effective Thursday, September 24, 2026.
- Scrip Code 974868 (Old ISIN: INE028U08032) is updated to New ISIN INE028U08065, with maturity extended from 30/06/2026 to 31/12/2026.
- Scrip Code 975230 (Old ISIN: INE028U08040) is updated to New ISIN INE028U08073, with maturity extended from 30/06/2026 to 31/12/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of ISIN and scrip long names reflecting revised maturity terms for listed debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the updated ISINs and scrip details for trading and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2026
- Revised Maturity Date: December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational alignment for trading members holding or trading the specified NCDs of Jana Capital Limited with the new ISINs and extended maturity terms.
