## Summary

SAMCO Mutual Fund is offering the SAMCO Equity Savings Fund NFO along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform from October 19, 2026, to November 02, 2026. The tentative allotment date is scheduled for November 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- **NFO Period:** October 19, 2026, to November 02, 2026.
- **Schemes Offered:** SAMCO Equity Savings Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF0K1H01347) and Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF0K1H01339).
- **SIP/XSIP Registrations:** Must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on November 02, 2026.
- **Lumpsum Extension:** NFO remains extended till midnight on November 02, 2026, for lumpsum purchases on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported in this notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client fund initiation by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 06, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** October 19, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** November 02, 2026
- **SIP Authentication Deadline:** November 02, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates member participation and investor subscriptions in the new SAMCO Equity Savings Fund scheme with standard operational timelines and payment settlement rules.
