## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) along with the SIP facility for Edelweiss Ultra Short-Term Fund by Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription period runs from August 17, 2026, to August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Edelweiss Mutual Fund
- Scheme Name: Edelweiss Ultra Short-Term Fund (Direct and Regular plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options)
- Subscription Window: August 17, 2026, to August 24, 2026 (extended till midnight on August 24, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases)
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 31, 2026
- ISINs: INF754K01XR8, INF754K01XS6, INF754K01XT4, INF754K01XV0, INF754K01XW8, INF754K01XX6

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 27, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on August 24, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 17, 2026
- NFO Close Date: August 24, 2026
- Clear Funds Pay-in Deadline: August 27, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the Edelweiss Ultra Short-Term Fund NFO and set up SIP facilities through the BSE StAR MF platform.
