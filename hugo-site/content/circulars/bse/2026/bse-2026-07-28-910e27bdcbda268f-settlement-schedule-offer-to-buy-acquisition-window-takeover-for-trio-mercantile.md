## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Trio Mercantile & Trading Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from July 29, 2026, to August 11, 2026, under settlement number 2026093.

## Key Points

- **Bidding Window:** Open from 29/07/2026 to 11/08/2026.
- **Settlement Type & No.:** Takeover, Settlement No. 2026093.
- **Obligation Download Date:** 24/08/2026.
- **Settlement Date:** 25/08/2026.
- **Early Pay-in:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in depositories, earmarked/blocked in the client's account under the requisite market type and settlement number. No EPN file upload is required.

## Regulatory Changes

- Settlement conducted as per Revised Guidelines of Mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange (refer Notice No. 20200528-32).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must ensure early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/ICCL systems before the prescribed timelines for bidding and confirmation.
- Members must refer to the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 29/07/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 11/08/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 24/08/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 25/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the operational settlement process for trading members and custodians participating in the takeover acquisition window for Trio Mercantile & Trading Limited.
