## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) issued a notice detailing the applicability, review, and revision of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework for various securities effective August 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins become applicable effective August 7, 2026, on all open positions as of August 6, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework and subject to Lower Price Bands effective August 5, 2026.
- Specific securities continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective August 7, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective August 5, 2026, with their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins on open and new positions for specified securities.
- Transfer of continuous LT-ASM securities to specialized trading groups (T, XT, MT, TS).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure adequate margin provisions (100% margin) for affected securities from the effective date.
- Monitor consolidated lists of securities under Annexures I through III for compliance and trading adjustments.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 04 Aug 2026
- Effective Date for Higher Stages / Price Band / Moving Out: 05 Aug 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers: 07 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the impacted securities will face stricter margin requirements and potential trading group reclassifications, affecting liquidity and execution parameters.
