## Summary

Best Capital Services Limited has announced a partial redemption of its debentures, resulting in a reduced face value per debenture effective from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: BEST CAPITAL SERVICES LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 978129
- ISIN: INE04UP07139
- Scrip ID / Description: BCSL-15.50%-27-3-27-UL-PVT
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 37,500 per debenture
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note that the debentures will be traded with the reduced face value starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 16, 2026
- Effective Date for Reduced Face Value: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors holding the affected debentures must account for the revised face value in trading and valuation.
