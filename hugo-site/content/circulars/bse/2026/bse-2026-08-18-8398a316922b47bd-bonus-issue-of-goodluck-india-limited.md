## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Goodluck India Limited has fixed a record date for the purpose of a bonus issue of equity shares. The stock will trade on an ex-bonus basis starting from August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Goodluck India Limited (BSE Scrip Code: 530655)
- Bonus Ratio: 2 (Two) Bonus Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each for every 1 (One) existing Equity Share of Rs. 2/- each.
- Record Date: 21/08/2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: 21/08/2026
- Expected Allotment: 66,477,018 equity shares to be allotted on or around August 24, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to note the ex-bonus date and record date for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 21, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: August 21, 2026
- Allotment Date (As informed): August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders will receive two additional shares for every share held as of the record date, leading to a corresponding price adjustment on the ex-date.
