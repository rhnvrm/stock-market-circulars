## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window - Takeover) in respect of J.A. Finance Limited. The bidding window is scheduled to run from October 19, 2026, to November 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Takeover
- Settlement Number: 2026125
- Bid Start Date: 19/10/2026
- Bid End Date: 02/11/2026
- Obligation Download: 16/11/2026
- Settlement Date: 17/11/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine settlement schedule notification for takeover acquisition window.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL & NSDL).
- Members and custodians must use Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement number 2026125.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Stamp duty charges can be verified via the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: October 19, 2026
- Bid End Date: November 2, 2026
- Obligation Download: November 16, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement and execution of shares acquisition via the stock exchange mechanism for J.A. Finance Limited.
