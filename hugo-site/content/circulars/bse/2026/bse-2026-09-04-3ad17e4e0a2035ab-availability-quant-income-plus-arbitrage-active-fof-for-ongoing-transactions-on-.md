## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of ongoing transactions for the Quant Income Plus Arbitrage Active FOF scheme on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from September 07, 2026, pursuant to notice no. 20260814-45.

## Key Points

- Reopening for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Applicable to multiple plans under Quant Income Plus Arbitrage Active FOF including Regular and Direct plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- Effective Date: September 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the specified ISINs through the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume transactions and systematic investment/withdrawal facilities for the specified Quant Mutual Fund scheme through the BSE platform.
