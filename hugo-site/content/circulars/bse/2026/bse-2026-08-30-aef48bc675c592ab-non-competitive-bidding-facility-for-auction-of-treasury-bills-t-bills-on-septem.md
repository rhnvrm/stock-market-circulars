## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of three Treasury Bills (91 Day, 182 Day, and 364 Day T-Bills) scheduled for September 02, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Government of India announced the auction of three Treasury Bills on September 02, 2026.
- Bidding takes place through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.
- Covers three tenors: 91 Day T-Bill (091T021226), 182 Day T-Bill (182T030327), and 364 Day T-Bill (364T020927).
- Minimum lot size is Rs. 10,000 and multiples thereof.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine auction participation notification under existing RBI and Exchange frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must submit bids via the NCB-GSec module within specified timelines.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: August 31, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors): September 01, 2026, till 5:00 pm
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): September 02, 2026, till 8:00 am
- Auction Date: September 02, 2026
- Settlement Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational clarity for investors and trading members participating in government debt auctions through the BSE iBBS platform.
