## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the alignment of SEBI's Cyber Incident Reporting Portal with the Format for Incident Reporting Exchange (FIRE) Framework, developed by the Financial Stability Board (FSB) to standardize cyber incident reporting.

## Key Points

- SEBI issued Circular No. HO/(449)2026-ITD-5_DIV1/I/19448/2026 dated August 24, 2026.
- The update aligns SEBI's reporting portal with the Financial Stability Board's (FSB) FIRE format for structured and standardized incident reporting.
- Regulated Entities (REs) must continue reporting cyber incidents to the Exchange as per existing guidelines (Exchange Circular No. 20250108-42) in addition to SEBI portal reporting.
- Annexure A (SEBI circular copy) and Annexure B (User Manual for the portal) are provided for reference.

## Regulatory Changes

Adoption of the FSB's Format for Incident Reporting Exchange (FIRE) framework within SEBI's Cyber Incident Reporting Portal for standardized disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and regulated entities must take note of the new FIRE format requirements for reporting cyber incidents on SEBI's portal while continuing exchange-level reporting mandates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances standardization and structure in cyber incident reporting for regulated entities, ensuring alignment with international FSB standards.
