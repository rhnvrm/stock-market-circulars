## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice listing companies from which clarifications were sought regarding significant price and volume movements in their scrips. Replies received from several companies are attached to the notice, while responses from several other companies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- The Exchange observed significant price/volume movement in specific scrips in the recent past.
- Clarifications were sought to ensure investors and trading members possess the latest relevant information.
- Replies have been received from 8 companies, including Bharat Global Developers Ltd, Cords Cable Industries Ltd, and others.
- Replies are awaited from 10 companies, including Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to respond to exchange inquiries regarding significant market movements in a timely manner to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 03 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and investor protection by making clarifications on unusual price and volume movements publicly available.
