## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for various securities, effective October 26, 2026. The framework is designed to enhance market integrity and safeguard investor interests against excessive volatility.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting specified criteria are shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I mandates an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%), effective October 26, 2026, on open positions as of October 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- ST-ASM Stage II mandates an applicable margin rate of 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%), effective October 26, 2026, on open positions as of October 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and does not reflect an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of stricter surveillance measures and higher margin requirements under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the collection of applicable margins (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) on all open positions as of October 23, 2026, and new positions created from October 26, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations for participants trading in securities placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume for the affected scrips.
