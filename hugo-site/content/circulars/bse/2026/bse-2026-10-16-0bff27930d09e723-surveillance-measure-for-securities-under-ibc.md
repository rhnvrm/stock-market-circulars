## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IBC) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code. Effective October 19, 2026, specific securities are placed under or removed from the IBC surveillance framework, with consolidated lists and non-promoter holding (NPH) classifications provided.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria under the Additional Surveillance Measure for companies relating to IRP under IBC are placed under the framework effective October 19, 2026 (Annexure I).
- List of securities moving out of the IBC framework effective October 19, 2026 is provided in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% are listed in Annexure IV.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other active surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation and periodic review of Additional Surveillance Measures for companies undergoing corporate insolvency resolution processes in accordance with previous exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the surveillance status of securities as per the annexures. Inquiries may be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in specified securities is subject to surveillance measures and price band adjustments. Shortlisting is a market surveillance mechanism and not an adverse action against the companies.
