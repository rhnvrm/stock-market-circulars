## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued by 9 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Tuesday, September 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 9
- Effective Date for Trading: September 8, 2026
- Segment: Equity
- Includes issuances from prominent entities such as ICICI Bank Limited, Carborundum Universal Limited, and Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Limited.

1. **Borosil Scientific Limited**: ISIN INE02L001032, Scrip Code: 544184, 4,475 shares

4. **Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited**: ISIN INE647U01026, Scrip Code: 544717, 46,978 shares
