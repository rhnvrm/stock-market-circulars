## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that units of Mirae Asset Nifty200 Momentum 30 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 50:50 ETF are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the 'B' Group effective Friday, July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Scheme: Mirae Asset Nifty200 Momentum 30 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 50:50 ETF
- Scrip Code: 544841
- ISIN No: INF769K01RJ5
- Group: B
- Market Lot: 1 Unit
- Face Value and Issue Price: Rs. 10.00 per unit
- Date of Allotment: 27/07/2026
- Number of Units: 13,338,273 Units

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Units of the Mutual Fund will only be traded in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: July 27, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an additional investment option in hybrid ETFs tracking the Nifty200 Momentum 30 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 50:50 Total Return Index.
