## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. As per the notice, 100% margins, stage movements, lower price bands, and group transfers are imposed or updated for specified securities effective September 2026 based on XBRL submissions.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. September 11, 2026, on all open positions as on September 10, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands effective September 09, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in LT-ASM but transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. September 11, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of LT-ASM framework effective September 09, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised LT-ASM framework measures including enhanced margins, price band restrictions, and segment/group transfers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Bands / Higher Stages / Moving Out: September 09, 2026
- Open Positions Date for Margin Applicability: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins / Group Transfers: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affected securities will experience increased margin requirements and trading restrictions, potentially impacting liquidity and short-term price volatility.
