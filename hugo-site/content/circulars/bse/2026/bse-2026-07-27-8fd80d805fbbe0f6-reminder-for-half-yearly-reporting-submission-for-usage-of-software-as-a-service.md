## Summary

BSE has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the half-yearly reporting submission for the usage of Software as a Service (SaaS) based solutions and applications for the period covering January 2026 to June 2026. Submissions must be completed electronically via the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS) portal by July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Applicable Period:** January 2026 to June 2026.
- **Submission Mode:** Electronic form exclusively through the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS) portal (https://befs.bseindia.com).
- **Dual Members Exception:** Trading members registered with both BSE and NSE shall submit their SaaS reporting exclusively to NSE, as per technology-based sharing mechanisms for common submissions.
- **Exclusive Members:** Exclusive BSE members must continue submitting their reports through the BEFS portal.

## Regulatory Changes

- Re-emphasizes compliance with SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD2/DOR/CIR/P/2020/221 dated November 03, 2020, on advisory for financial sector organizations regarding SaaS based solutions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must submit the SaaS utilization report for the half-year period ending June 30, 2026, adhering to the guidelines provided in Annexure 1.

## Important Dates

- **Due Date:** July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational requirement for trading members to ensure timely regulatory filings through the appropriate exchange portal to avoid non-compliance penalties.
