## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has informed trading members that 12,27,000 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the Public Issue of Maharaja & Speedex India Limited at a price of Rs. 186/- per equity share on September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Maharaja & Speedex India Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue / Anchor Investors (AIs) portion
- Shares Subscribed: 12,27,000 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 186/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: September 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants are notified of the anchor investor allocation details for records and further settlement processing.
