## Summary

BSE has announced that the KOTAK MULTI SECTOR OMNI FOF shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform with effect from October 01, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20260907-24.

## Key Points

- Reopening of KOTAK MULTI SECTOR OMNI FOF effective October 01, 2026.
- Facilities enabled include ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Covers multiple ISINs across Regular and Direct plans (Growth and IDCW Reinvestment/Payout options).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; operational notice for mutual fund platform availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can facilitate transactions in the specified scheme plans through the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures seamless transactional access for investors and members in the Kotak Multi Sector Omni FOF across regular and direct plans.
