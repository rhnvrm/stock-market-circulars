## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of SG Finserve Limited (Scrip Code - 539199) effective from August 20, 2026, following the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** SG Finserve Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 539199
- **ISIN:** INE618R01015
- **Security Details:** 39,25,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 440/- (Issue Price: Rs. 450/- per share) to promoter and non-promoter categories.
- **Effective Date:** August 20, 2026.
- **Ranking:** Shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the distribution numbers and lock-in details for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 19/03/2026
- **Listing Date:** 20/08/2026
- **Lock-in Details:** 
  - 20,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 55895001 to 57895000) locked-in up to 19/02/2027.
  - 19,25,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 57895001 to 59820000) locked-in up to 19/02/2028.

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate listing update reflecting an increase in paid-up equity capital due to warrant conversion.
