## Summary

Effective from Thursday, September 17, 2026, the equity shares of Karamtara Engineering Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip Name: KARAMTARA ENGINEERING LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544917
- ISIN No.: INE590T01012
- Issue Price: Rs. 254/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 244/-)
- Total Securities: 321,822,799 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, September 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Karamtara Engineering Limited shares on the BSE platform.
