## Summary

Effective from Monday, October 5, 2026, the equity shares of German Green Steel and Power Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: German Green Steel and Power Limited
- Scrip Code: 544957
- Scrip ID on BOLT: GERMAN
- ISIN: INE0D4501011
- Total Securities: 75,349,197 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 139/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 129/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2026
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 5, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action executed for lock-in marking in depository records.
- Trading members to note lock-in details and demat trading norms.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 30, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of German Green Steel and Power Limited shares on the BSE exchange, increasing liquidity and market participation.
