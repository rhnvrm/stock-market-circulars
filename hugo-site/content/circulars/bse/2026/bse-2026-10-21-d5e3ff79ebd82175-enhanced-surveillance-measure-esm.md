## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the inclusion, movement to lower stages, and exclusion of securities effective October 22 and October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy the criteria for inclusion under ESM and attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 23, 2026 on all open positions as on October 22, 2026 and new positions created from October 23, 2026.
- Securities continuing in ESM but moving to lower Stage I w.e.f. October 22, 2026 are included in Annexure I.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 23, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 22, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the ESM framework w.e.f. October 22, 2026 is given in Annexure II, with price bands reinstated subject to other prevailing measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of minimum 100% margin on securities under ESM.
- Shifting of securities from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Placement of Stage II securities under 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure collection of 100% margin on open positions as of October 22, 2026 and new positions w.e.f. October 23, 2026.
- Market participants must adhere to trade-for-trade settlement and price band restrictions for short-listed securities.

## Important Dates

- October 22, 2026: Movement to lower Stage I, application of Stage II restrictions (2% price band, periodic call auction), and exclusion of specified securities from ESM.
- October 23, 2026: Effective date for minimum 100% margin and shifting of securities to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Increases trading safeguards, margin requirements, and settlement friction for specified securities, potentially impacting liquidity and short-term volatility.
