## Summary

BSE has issued a reminder to all trading members regarding the submission of the Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) report for the FY 2025-26, referencing earlier notices dated May 14, 2026, and June 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Dual-registered members (BSE and NSE) must submit their VAPT audit report exclusively to NSE.
- Members registered only with BSE must continue submissions to BSE via the BEFS portal.
- User manuals for members and auditors are provided as Annexure A and Annexure B.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clarification on cross-exchange submission mechanism to avoid duplicate filings for members registered with both BSE and NSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure VAPT reports are submitted electronically through the BEFS portal on or before the due date to avoid penal or disciplinary action.
- Submission must follow approval from the respective IT Committee.

## Important Dates

- VAPT audit conducted by CERT-In empanelled Auditor due date: June 30, 2026.
- Report submission after IT Committee approval: July 31, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures compliance with IT security standards across trading members and prevents regulatory penalties.
