## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for various securities. Effective August 03, 2026, applicable margins will be revised to 50% or 100% depending on the surveillance stage.

## Key Points

- Securities have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, w.e.f. August 03, 2026.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, w.e.f. August 03, 2026.
- Applicable on all open positions as on July 31, 2026 and new positions created from August 03, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and enforcement of the ST-ASM framework for shortlisted securities under market surveillance.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements for affected securities under ST-ASM Stage I and II.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 Jul 2026
- Effective Date: 03 Aug 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: 31 Jul 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities under ST-ASM stages, potentially affecting liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
