## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the Offer to Buy through the Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Dolphin Medical Services Limited. The open offer is made by Mr. Amarandhar Reddy Kotha (Acquirer 1) and Mr. Mallour Rajesh Kumar (Acquirer 2) to acquire up to 39,25,988 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 26.00% of the voting share capital) at an offer price of Rs. 4.80/- per share in cash.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Dolphin Medical Services Limited
- **Acquirers:** Mr. Amarandhar Reddy Kotha and Mr. Mallour Rajesh Kumar
- **Offer Size:** Up to 39,25,988 equity shares (26.00% of voting share capital)
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 4.80/- per equity share payable in cash
- **Offer Period:** From Friday, July 31, 2026 to Thursday, August 13, 2026
- **Regulation:** Pursuant to Regulation 4 of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011

## Regulatory Changes

- None specific to regulations; follows established SEBI tender offer and acquisition window frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the schedule and guidelines for tendering shares through the stock exchange mechanism under SEBI and Exchange circulars.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Offer Start Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Offer End Date:** August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of Dolphin Medical Services Limited can tender their equity shares during the specified acquisition window through eligible trading members.
