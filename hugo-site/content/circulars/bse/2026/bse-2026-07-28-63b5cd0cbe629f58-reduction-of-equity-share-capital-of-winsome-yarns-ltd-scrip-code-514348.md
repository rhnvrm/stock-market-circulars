## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the reduction of equity share capital of Winsome Yarns Ltd. (Scrip code: 514348) pursuant to an order approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Chandigarh Bench.

## Key Points

- **Company Name & Code:** Winsome Yarns Ltd. (514348)
- **Record Date / Suspension Date:** 31/07/2026
- **Promoter Shareholding:** Entire existing equity shareholding of erstwhile promoters extinguished (reduced from 2,73,39,609 to Nil).
- **Public Shareholding:** Reduced on a pro-rata basis from 4,33,67,620 to 1,31,579 shares, resulting in public shareholders collectively holding 5% of the post-restructuring paid-up equity share capital.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of NCLT Chandigarh Bench approved resolution plan involving capital reduction.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants are required to take note of the capital reduction, extinction of promoter shareholding, and adjustment of public shareholding.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date / No Dealings / Suspension w.e.f.:** 31/07/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Substantial restructuring of equity capital affecting existing promoter and public shareholdings as per the tribunal order.
