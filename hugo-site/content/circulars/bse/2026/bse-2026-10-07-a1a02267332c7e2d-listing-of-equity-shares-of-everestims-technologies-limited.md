## Summary

Effective from Thursday, October 8, 2026, the equity shares of Everestims Technologies Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE SME segment under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will operate in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Everestims Technologies Limited
- Scrip Code: 544979
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: EIMS
- ISIN: INE0YUW01020
- Issue Price: Rs. 85/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 75/-)
- Total Securities: 21,638,178 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1,600 Equity Shares
- Market Maker: KG Stock Broking Private Limited
- Registrar: Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Trading members must note compliance with SEBI guidelines requiring securities to be traded in dematerialised form and subject to Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will trade strictly in dematerialised form in minimum market lots of 1,600 shares.
- Scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days.
- Lock-in details and shareholding patterns are executed in depository records as per Annexure I and Annexure II.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 6, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and public market access to Everestims Technologies Limited shares while applying standard surveillance and trade-for-trade monitoring for SME IPO listings.
