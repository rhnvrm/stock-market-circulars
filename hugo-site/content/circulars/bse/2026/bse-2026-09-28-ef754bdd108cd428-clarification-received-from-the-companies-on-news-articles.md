## Summary

BSE has issued a notice seeking clarification from select companies regarding various news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels to ensure accuracy and inform investors. Replies have been received from Federal Bank Ltd and Jana Small Finance Bank Ltd, while responses from SpiceJet Ltd and Panorama Studios International Ltd are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- Exchange surveillance seeks to verify news reports impacting market integrity and investor awareness.
- Clarification received from Federal Bank Ltd (Scrip Code: 500469).
- Clarification received from Jana Small Finance Bank Ltd (Scrip Code: 544118).
- Clarification awaited from SpiceJet Ltd (Scrip Code: 500285).
- Clarification awaited from Panorama Studios International Ltd (Scrip Code: 539469).

## Regulatory Changes

[No regulatory changes introduced in this circular; routine surveillance communication.]

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must respond promptly to exchange inquiries regarding news articles.
- Companies with pending replies must submit their clarifications to the exchange as required.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard administrative and surveillance notification with minimal market impact, intended to maintain transparency and curb misinformation.
