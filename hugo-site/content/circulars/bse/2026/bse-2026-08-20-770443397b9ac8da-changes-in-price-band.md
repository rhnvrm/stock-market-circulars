## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that the price bands for 21 scrips have been revised from their existing levels.

## Key Points

- Total of 21 scrips affected by the price band revision.
- Revisions include changes to 2%, 5%, 10%, and 20% price bands.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is August 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Price bands for specified scrips are adjusted by the surveillance department as part of risk management and price stabilization measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure their trading systems and clients are aware of the revised price bands to prevent order rejections or execution errors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 20, 2026
- Effective Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and traders dealing in the affected scrips will experience modified permissible daily price movement limits.
