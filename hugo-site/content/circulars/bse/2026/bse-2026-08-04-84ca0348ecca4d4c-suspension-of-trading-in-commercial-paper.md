## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of their Commercial Papers (CPs). Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260804-7
- Department: Listing Operations
- Segment: Debt Segment
- Subject: Suspension of Trading In Commercial Paper
- Affected entities include DSP Finance Private Limited, Nuvama Clearing Services Limited, Aditya Birla Real Estate Limited, GIC Housing Finance Ltd., Vivriti Capital Limited, Time Technoplast Limited, Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, and Bajaj Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational suspension of trading in commercial papers due to redemption record dates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial paper of the mentioned companies as of the respective record dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 04 August 2026
- Record / Ex-Dates: 05 August 2026, 06 August 2026, 10 August 2026, and 12-13 August 2026 depending on the specific commercial paper.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified commercial paper issues is suspended temporarily or permanently as per redemption schedules, preventing further secondary market transactions in those specific instruments.
