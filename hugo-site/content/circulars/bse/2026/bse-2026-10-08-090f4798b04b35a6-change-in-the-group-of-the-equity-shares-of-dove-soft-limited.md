## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Dove Soft Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group). This change takes effect from Friday, October 23, 2026, following the exchange's previous notice dated October 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: DOVE SOFT LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544975
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the equity shares will now take place under the Rolling segment (M Group) instead of the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the segment change for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Normalizes trading mechanics for Dove Soft Limited equity shares as they move to the regular rolling segment.
