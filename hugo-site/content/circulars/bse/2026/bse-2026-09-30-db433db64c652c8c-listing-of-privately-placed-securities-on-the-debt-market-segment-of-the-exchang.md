## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities issued by India Infradebt Limited on the debt market segment effective September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: India Infradebt Limited
- Scrip Code 978231 (ISIN: INE537P07919): 80,000 units of 7.8825% p.a. NCDs maturing on 29/01/2029.
- Scrip Code 978232 (ISIN: INE537P07935): 128,800 units of 8.1905% p.a. NCDs maturing on 26/12/2031.
- Scrip Code 978233 (ISIN: INE537P07927): 40,000 units linked to 3 Months MIBOR OIS maturing on 29/01/2029.
- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine debt listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with dematerialised trading requirements and refer to the Placement Memorandum for further details.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 28/09/2026
- Listing / Effective Date: 30/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides debt market investors with new listed debt instruments issued by India Infradebt Limited with negligible direct impact on broader equity markets.
