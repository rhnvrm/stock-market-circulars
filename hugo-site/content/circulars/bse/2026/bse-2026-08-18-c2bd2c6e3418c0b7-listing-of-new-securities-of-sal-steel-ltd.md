## Summary

Stock exchange notification regarding the listing and trading permission for 3,57,50,000 newly issued equity shares of S.A.L. Steel Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532604) issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.

## Key Points

- Company Name: S.A.L. Steel Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 532604
- ISIN: INE658G01014
- Number of Shares: 3,57,50,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 8/- (Issue Price: Rs. 18/-).
- Allottees: Promoters (via preferential allotment upon warrant conversion).
- Effective Date of Trading: August 19, 2026.
- Distribution Nos.: 109016701 to 144766700.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares are subject to lock-in requirements as specified:
- 49,03,340 shares (Dist. Nos. 109016701 - 113920040) locked-in up to 27/02/2028.
- 3,08,46,660 shares (Dist. Nos. 113920041 - 144766700) locked-in up to 27/02/2027.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 14/02/2026
- Trading Commencement Date: 19/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity base of the company through warrant conversion by promoters.
