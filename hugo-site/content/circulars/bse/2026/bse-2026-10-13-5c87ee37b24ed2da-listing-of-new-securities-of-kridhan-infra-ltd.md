## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 48,00,000 new equity shares of Kridhan Infra Limited (Scrip Code: 533482) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from October 14, 2026. These shares were issued pursuant to the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- Company: Kridhan Infra Limited
- Scrip Code: 533482
- ISIN: INE524L01026
- Number of Shares: 48,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each
- Issue / Allotment Price: Rs. 4.05/- per share (including premium of Rs. 2.05/-)
- Allotment Date: March 17, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: October 14, 2026
- Lock-in Details: 48,00,000 shares locked in up to May 31, 2027
- Distribution Nos.: 94779206 to 99579205

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action listing procedure for preferential allotment upon warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the listing, distribution numbers, and lock-in period restrictions for the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: March 17, 2026
- Notice Date: October 13, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: October 14, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Increase in paid-up equity share capital of Kridhan Infra Limited following warrant conversion. Minimal market impact as shares are pari-passu with existing equity shares, subject to applicable lock-in norms.
