## Summary

Salem Erode Investments Ltd has announced a rights issue of equity shares. The company has fixed September 3, 2026, as the record date to determine shareholders eligible for the rights issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Salem Erode Investments Ltd (Scrip Code: 540181)
- Rights Issue Details: 01 (ONE) Equity Share of Re. 1/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 19/- per share (total issue price Rs. 20/- per share) for every 01 (ONE) Equity Share held.
- Record Date: September 3, 2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: September 3, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the ex-rights date and ensure transactions in the company's equity shares are conducted on an ex-rights basis starting from September 3, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 03/09/2026
- Ex-Rights Date: 03/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are eligible to participate in the rights issue. Share prices will adjust on an ex-rights basis from the effective date.
