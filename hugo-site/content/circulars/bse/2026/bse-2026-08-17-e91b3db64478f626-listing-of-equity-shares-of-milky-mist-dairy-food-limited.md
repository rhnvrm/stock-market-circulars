## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, August 18, 2026, the equity shares of Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited
- Scrip Code: 544868
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: MILKYMIST
- ISIN No.: INE00IT01020
- Total Securities: 769,843,411 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 140/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 2/- and premium of Rs. 138/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 14, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action has been executed for marking the lock-in period in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 14, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited equity shares on the BSE exchange platform.
