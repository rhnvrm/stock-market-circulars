## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Kkalpana Plastick Limited under Notice No. 20260819-8. The bidding window will be operational from August 21, 2026, to September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Kkalpana Plastick Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement Number:** 2026106
- **Bid Start Date:** 21/08/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 04/09/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 18/09/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 21/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. The process follows established guidelines for the acquisition of shares through the stock exchange mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL & NSDL).
- Members and custodians must use Market Type "(Takeover)" and settlement number 2026106.
- Securities will be earmarked/blocked in the client's account; members/custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should check the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Opening Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Bid Closing Date:** September 4, 2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** September 18, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants, members, and custodians involved in the takeover acquisition window for Kkalpana Plastick Limited must adhere to the specified early pay-in timelines and settlement schedules to ensure smooth processing.
