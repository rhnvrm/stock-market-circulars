## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Dhanwel Hybrid Seeds Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective Wednesday, September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Dhanwel Hybrid Seeds Limited
- Scrip Code: 544884
- Segment Shift: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: September 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the equity shares will no longer be restricted to the Trade for Trade segment and will move to the normal rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their systems to reflect the change in segment for the scrip from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Improves liquidity and trading flexibility for investors in Dhanwel Hybrid Seeds Limited by moving out of the Trade for Trade restriction.
