## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, September 15, 2026, the equity shares of Pranav Constructions Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pranav Constructions Limited
- Scrip Code: 544909
- ISIN No: INE0H4201019
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: PRANAV
- Total Securities: 11,26,22,782 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 124/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 114/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 10, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Tuesday, September 15, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed for locking-in shares in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 10, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Pranav Constructions Limited shares on the BSE platform, providing liquidity to investors.
