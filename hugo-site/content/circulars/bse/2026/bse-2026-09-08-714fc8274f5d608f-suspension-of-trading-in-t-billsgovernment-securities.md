## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities effective September 9, 2026, due to their scheduled maturity.

## Key Points

- Trading members are informed that specific T-bills and Government Securities are maturing.
- Affected ISINs include INY020010081 and IN0020010081.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in these securities starting from September 09, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no further dealing or trading takes place in the specified T-bills and Government Securities with effect from September 09, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 08, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to the cessation of trading for the matured government securities and T-bills.
