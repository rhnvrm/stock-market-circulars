## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion and movement under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The framework entails stringent trading restrictions, including a minimum 100% margin and shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I qualify for inclusion under ESM and will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 29, 2026, on all open and new positions.
- Certain securities are moved to lower Stage I under ESM w.e.f. September 28, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 29, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 28, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III, while securities moving out of ESM are listed in Annexure II.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) framework in conjunction with existing market surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with minimum 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II / moving out / lower stage movement: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date for margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade shifting: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High operational impact for market participants trading in shortlisted securities due to increased margin requirements and restricted settlement mechanisms.
