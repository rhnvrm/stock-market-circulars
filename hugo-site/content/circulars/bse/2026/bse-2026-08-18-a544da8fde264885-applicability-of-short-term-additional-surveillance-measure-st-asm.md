## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities effective August 20, 2026, based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- Effective date for surveillance actions: August 20, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on August 19, 2026, and new positions created thereafter).
- Shortlisting under ASM is strictly for market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- **ST-ASM Stage I:** Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100%.
- **ST-ASM Stage II:** Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100%.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 18 August 2026
- **Effective Date:** 20 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities under ST-ASM Stage I and II will affect liquidity and leverage for traders holding positions in the affected scrips.
