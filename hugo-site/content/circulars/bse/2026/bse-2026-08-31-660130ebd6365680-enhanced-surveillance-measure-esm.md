## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin effective September 02, 2026, and will be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment. Certain securities are also moving to lower stages or exiting the framework.

## Key Points

- Minimum 100% margin applicable on all open positions as of September 01, 2026, and new positions created from September 02, 2026.
- Qualifying securities shifted from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 02, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II placed under Trade for Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 01, 2026.
- Shortlisting is strictly on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures under the ESM framework in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance mechanisms.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (100%) for securities listed under Annexure I.
- Compliance with Trade-for-Trade settlement rules for applicable short-listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Stage II / Move to Lower Stage / Exit:** September 01, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margin and Trade-for-Trade Shift:** September 02, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and collateral requirements for impacted scrips will be significantly affected due to the 100% margin requirement and mandatory Trade-for-Trade settlement.
