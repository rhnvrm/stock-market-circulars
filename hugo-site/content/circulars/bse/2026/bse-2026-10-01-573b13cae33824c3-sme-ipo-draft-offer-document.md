## Summary

Hyrefox Consultants Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with BSE SME on September 30, 2026, pursuant to Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations. The document is made available on the exchange websites to seek public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Issuer Name: Hyrefox Consultants Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; standard compliance under Schedule VI of ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public and stakeholders may review the Draft Offer Document on the BSE SME website and submit comments within 21 days via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Comment Submission Deadline: 21 days from notice date
