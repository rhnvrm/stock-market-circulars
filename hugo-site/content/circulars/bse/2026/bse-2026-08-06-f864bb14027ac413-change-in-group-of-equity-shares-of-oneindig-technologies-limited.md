## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Oneindig Technologies Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Oneindig Technologies Limited
- Scrip Code: 544852
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Thursday, August 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Equity shares are shifted from compulsory trade-for-trade settlement to normal rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the change in segment and group for executing trades in the scrip.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 6, 2026
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Improves liquidity and trading flexibility as shares move from trade-for-trade to the rolling settlement segment.
