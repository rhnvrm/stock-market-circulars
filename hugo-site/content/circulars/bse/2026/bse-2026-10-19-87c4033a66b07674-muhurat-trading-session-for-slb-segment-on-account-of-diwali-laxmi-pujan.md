## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the timings for the special Muhurat Trading Session in the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) segment on account of Diwali Laxmi Pujan, scheduled for Sunday, November 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Segment: Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB)
- Trading Date: Sunday, November 8, 2026
- Log-in Time: 01:15 pm to 02:20 pm
- Continuous Trading Time: 02:20 pm to 03:30 pm
- Contact details for queries: BSECL Helpdesk at 022-45720400/600 or 022-69158500, or via email at CM_settlements@bseclearing.com

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a scheduled operational timing notice for a special trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the SLB segment should take note of the special timings for log-in and continuous trading on November 8, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Muhurat Trading Date: November 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact, providing participants with scheduled timings for holiday trading.
