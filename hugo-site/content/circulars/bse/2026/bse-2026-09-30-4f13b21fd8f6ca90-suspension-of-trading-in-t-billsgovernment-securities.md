## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific State Development Loans (SDL) / Government Securities due to their maturity. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified securities with effect from October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security: 601RAJSDL26 (ISIN: IN2920210316)
- Effective Date of Suspension: October 01, 2026
- Segment: Debt Segment
- Department: Listing Operations

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine trading suspension following the redemption/maturity schedule of the government security / SDL.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified T-Bills / Government Securities / SDL from October 01, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal and routine, affecting only holders and traders of the matured SDL security (601RAJSDL26).
