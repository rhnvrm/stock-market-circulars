## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of Dove Soft Limited on its SME platform, effective from Thursday, October 8, 2026. A total of 2,43,86,755 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up will be listed under the 'MT' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Dove Soft Limited
- Scrip Code: 544975
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: DSL
- ISIN: INE0NXJ01016
- Issue Price: Rs. 110/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-, Premium: Rs. 100/-)
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Segment: SME (Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days)
- Date of Allotment: October 6, 2026
- Market Maker: Swastika Investmart Limited
- Registrar: Purva Sharegistry (India) Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealing in the 'MT' Group of Securities and placed in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.
- Trading restricted to dematerialised form only in accordance with SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are effected in minimum market lot of 1,200 equity shares.
- Necessary corporate action executed in demat form to mark lock-in periods in depository records as per Annexure I.
- Participation in Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on listing date (October 8, 2026).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 7, 2026
- Date of Allotment: October 6, 2026
- Listing / Effective Date: October 8, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): October 8, 2026
- Trade-for-Trade Segment duration: 10 trading days from listing date

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and public market access for shareholders of Dove Soft Limited while ensuring initial surveillance and monitoring through the Trade-for-Trade segment and SPOS on the BSE SME platform.
