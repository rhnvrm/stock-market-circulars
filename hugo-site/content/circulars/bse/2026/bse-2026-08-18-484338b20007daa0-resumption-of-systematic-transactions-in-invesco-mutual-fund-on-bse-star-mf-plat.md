## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of subscription through systematic transactions already registered in select schemes of Invesco Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Resumption applies to systematic transactions (SIP/XSIPs) already registered in specific Invesco Mutual Fund schemes.
- Effective date: August 18, 2026.
- Affected schemes include:
  - INVESCO INDIA – INVESCO Global Consumer Trends Fund of Fund
  - INVESCO INDIA – INVESCO Global Equity Income Fund of Fund
  - INVESCO INDIA – INVESCO Pan European Equity Fund of Fund
- Existing SIP/XSIP registrations will continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA end without any changes.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No specific compliance actions required for market participants; informational notice regarding system operations.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to resume systematic investments via the BSE StAR MF platform for the specified Invesco schemes following earlier temporary suspensions.
