## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the record dates and reduced face values for part redemption and interest payment of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) issued by several companies.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260814-19
- Department: Listing Operations
- Segment: Debt
- Trading in the debentures of the respective companies will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Important Dates

- **Regency Fincorp Limited (ISIN: INE964R07085)**: Record Date: 17/08/2026, Ex-Date: 17/08/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 84,211/- per Debenture
- **Keystone Realtors Limited (ISIN: INE263M07109)**: Record Date: 23/08/2026, Ex-Date: 21/08/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 59,701.49 per Debenture
- **Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited (ISIN: INE08XP07514)**: Record Date: 28/08/2026, Ex-Date: 28/08/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 95,455/- per Debenture
- **Aye Finance Limited (ISIN: INE501X07711)**: Record Date: 28/08/2026, Ex-Date: 28/08/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 60,000/- per Debenture
- **Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd (ISIN: INE094I07049)**: Record Date: 18/08/2026, Ex-Date: 18/08/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 45,185/- per Debenture
- **Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd (ISIN: INE094I07072)**: Record Date: 18/08/2026, Ex-Date: 18/08/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 38,036/- per Debenture
- **Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd (ISIN: INE094I07080)**: Record Date: 18/08/2026, Ex-Date: 18/08/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 90,381/- per Debenture
- **Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd (ISIN: INE094I07098)**: Record Date: 18/08/2026, Ex-Date: 18/08/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 89,357/- per Debenture

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are requested to note the reduced face values and respective record/ex-dates for settlement purposes.

## Impact Assessment

Impacts holders of the specified Non-Convertible Debentures regarding adjusted capital values and upcoming interest/principal payouts.
