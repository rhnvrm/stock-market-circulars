## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility for ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund (ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041) on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 26, 2026, to September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041 (Regular Plan - Growth Equity, ISIN: INF109K1A617; Direct Plan - Growth Equity, ISIN: INF109K1A625).
- NFO Period: August 26, 2026, to September 09, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 15, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 11, 2026.
- Lumpsum purchases via the platform remain extended till midnight on September 09, 2026, while SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by 3:00 PM on the same day.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with an 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be credited to the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 11, 2026, to be eligible for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 26, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: September 09, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 11, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for trading members participating in mutual fund transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.
