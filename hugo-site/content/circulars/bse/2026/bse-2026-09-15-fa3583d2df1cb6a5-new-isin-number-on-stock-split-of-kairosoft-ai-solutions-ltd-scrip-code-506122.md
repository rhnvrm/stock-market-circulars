## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the new ISIN number for KAIROSOFT AI SOLUTIONS LTD following the sub-division of its equity shares from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-, effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: KAIROSOFT AI SOLUTIONS LTD
- Scrip Code: 506122
- Old ISIN: Not specified (Refer to previous notice)
- New ISIN: INE820M01026
- Corporate Action: Sub-division of Equity Shares from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-

## Regulatory Changes

- Assignment of a new ISIN number subsequent to the stock split.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that trades executed on and from the ex-date use the new ISIN number.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 15, 2026
- Ex-Date: September 18, 2026 (DR- 718/2026-2027)

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures proper identification and clearing/settlement of equity shares following the stock split.
