## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 3,05,50,000 new equity shares of PC Jeweller Limited, issued to promoters on a preferential basis following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: PC JEWELLER LIMITED (Scrip Code: 534809)
- Securities: 3,05,50,000 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 17/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 18/-
- Effective Date of Trading: Tuesday, September 22, 2026
- ISIN: INE785M01021
- Lock-in Details: 3,05,50,000 shares locked-in upto 22/03/2028

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares and are subject to lock-in requirements as specified.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 30/07/2026
- Listing/Trading Date: 22/09/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 22/03/2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity base of PC Jeweller Limited through promoter preferential allotment upon warrant conversion.
