## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities effective September 15, 2026, due to their upcoming maturity dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260911-10
- Affected Securities:
  - Scrip Code 805120 | 364TB17926 | ISIN: IN002025Z252
  - Scrip Code 805229 | 91TB17926 | ISIN: IN002026X115
- Trading Members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid securities from the effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from trading in the specified maturing T-bills and Government Securities starting September 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date (Suspension): September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational measure for maturing government debt instruments with no broader market impact.
