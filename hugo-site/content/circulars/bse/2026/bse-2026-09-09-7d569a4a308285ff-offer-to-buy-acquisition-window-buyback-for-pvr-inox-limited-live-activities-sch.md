## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a live activities schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Buyback) of equity shares of PVR INOX LIMITED. The buyback offer is scheduled from Thursday, 10th September 2026 to Thursday, 17th September 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: PVR INOX LIMITED
- Security Symbol: PVRINOX
- ISIN: INE191H01014
- Scrip Code: 532689
- Offer Size: Up to 20,68,965 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00
- Offer Price: Rs. 1450.00 per share
- Opening Date: Thursday, 10th September 2026
- Closing Date: Thursday, 17th September 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational schedule for acquisition window buyback mechanism under exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading Members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians must adhere to specified session timings for bid offering, modifications, cancellations, give-ups, and take-ups.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: 10th September 2026
- Issue Closing Date: 17th September 2026
- Bid Offering Period / Modifications / Cancellation: 10th September 2026 to 17th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- Give-up / Take-up: 10th September 2026 to 16th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- Give-up (last day of offer): 17th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- Take-up (last day of offer): 17th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly execution of the PVR INOX Limited share buyback through the BSE iBBS acquisition window, enabling eligible shareholders to tender their shares efficiently.
