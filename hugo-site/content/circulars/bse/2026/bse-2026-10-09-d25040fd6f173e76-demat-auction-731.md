## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 731 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-731/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 9, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on October 9, 2026.
- Delivery statement and reports: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m. on October 9, 2026.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. on October 9, 2026.
- Auction pay-in scheduled for Monday, October 12, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Trading members should exercise utmost care when punching security codes and rates, as no corrections will be permitted.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 9, 2026
- Pay-in Date: October 12, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational circular affecting short-delivery settlement and demat auctions for participating trading members.
