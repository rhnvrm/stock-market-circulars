## Summary

BSE has announced the record date and ex-bonus details for Jonjua Overseas Limited. The company has fixed September 4, 2026, as the record date for its upcoming bonus issue of equity shares, along with a revision in the market lot.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Jonjua Overseas Limited (Scrip Code: 542446)
- Bonus Ratio: 7 (Seven) Bonus Equity Shares of Rs.10/- each for every 24 (Twenty-Four) existing Equity Shares held.
- Record Date: September 4, 2026.
- Ex-Bonus Date: September 4, 2026.
- Revised Market Lot: Changed from 4821 to 6227 effectively from September 04, 2026.
- Total Allotment: 7,955,966 Equity Shares are expected to be allotted on or around September 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the bonus issue and the revised market lot for Jonjua Overseas Limited starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 4, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: September 4, 2026
- Effective Date for Market Lot Change: September 4, 2026
- Expected Allotment Date: September 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares of Jonjua Overseas Limited as of the record date will receive bonus shares in the specified ratio, and trading members must update their systems for the revised market lot of 6227.
