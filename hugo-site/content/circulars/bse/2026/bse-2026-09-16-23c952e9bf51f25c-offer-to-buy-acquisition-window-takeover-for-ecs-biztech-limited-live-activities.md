## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the schedule of activities for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for ECS Biztech Limited. The offer period is scheduled from Thursday, September 17, 2026, to Wednesday, September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: ECS BIZTECH LIMITED
- Security Symbol: ECS
- ISIN: INE925Q01024
- Scrip Code: 540063
- Offer Size: Up to 53,44,313 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 10.50 per share
- Opening Date: 17th September 2026
- Closing Date: 30th September 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the UCC of the investor is registered/updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bids can be placed for investors having shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Bid Offering Period: 17th September 2026 to 30th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- Bid Modifications/Cancellation: 17th September 2026 to 30th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- Give-up / Take-up: 17th September 2026 to 29th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- Last day Give-up: 30th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.)
- Last day Take-up: 30th September 2026 (9:15 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants, trading members, and custodians to execute takeover orders within the specified acquisition window seamlessly.
