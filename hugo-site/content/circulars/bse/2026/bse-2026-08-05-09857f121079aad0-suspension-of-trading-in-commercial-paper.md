## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers due to upcoming record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of specified companies starting from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260805-42 dated August 5, 2026.
- Covers commercial paper redemption and suspension for various companies.
- Includes companies such as ECL Finance Limited, Tata Steel Limited, Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited, ICICI Home Finance Company Limited, and others.
- Record dates and ex-dates range primarily between August 7, 2026, and August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard trading restriction procedure for commercial paper redemptions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must refrain from dealing in the commercial papers of the listed entities on or after the specified ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05/08/2026
- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: 07/08/2026, 10/08/2026, 12/08/2026, and 18/08/2026 to 19/08/2026 depending on the specific commercial paper tranche.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures orderly settlement of commercial paper redemptions and prevents trading in securities nearing maturity and redemption.
