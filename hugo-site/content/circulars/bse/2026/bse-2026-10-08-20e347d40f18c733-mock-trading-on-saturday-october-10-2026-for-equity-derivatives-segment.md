## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 10, 2026, utilizing both the Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR). Members can use this opportunity to test their trading applications, algorithms, and third-party empanelled vendor platforms under simulated market conditions.

## Key Points

- **Date of Mock Session:** Saturday, October 10, 2026.
- **Sites Used:** Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR) with a scheduled switchover at 02:00 pm.
- **New Software Release:** New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.60 shall be released with details published separately.
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Fulfills testing requirements pursuant to SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations and create no rights or liabilities.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory policy changes introduced; reinforces compliance testing requirements for trading software as per SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members using third-party or in-house trading platforms through IML/ETI APIs are encouraged to participate to fulfill periodic testing and risk management requirements.
- Members are requested to provide feedback on the mock trading session via email or contact numbers by 5:30 pm.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** October 10, 2026
- **Feedback Submission Deadline:** October 10, 2026 by 5:30 pm

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal and positive, allowing brokers and trading members to validate disaster recovery switchover, order matching, risk-reduction modes, and trading halts in a risk-free environment.
