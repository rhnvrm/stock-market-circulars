## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 5,000 new equity shares of Yarn Syndicate Ltd (Scrip Code: 514378) resulting from the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares on a rights basis are permitted for trading effective September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading in 5,000 new equity shares of Yarn Syndicate Ltd (Scrip Code: 514378) commences on September 30, 2026.
- Shares are issued pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Rs. 10/- each issued on a rights basis.
- ISIN: INE564C01013
- Distinctive Numbers: 4203886 to 4208885
- These shares rank pari-passu with the existing old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised take note of the listing and commencement of trading in the new securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action listing having minimal impact on overall market operations, affecting only the specific scrip's paid-up capital structure.
