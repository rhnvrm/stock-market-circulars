## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the temporary suspension of fresh lump-sum investments, switch-in transactions, Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs), and Systematic Transfer Plans (STP-in) in LIC MF Medium to Long Term Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective October 16, 2026. Existing SIP/STP-in registrations and redemptions/switch-outs continue to be permitted.

## Key Points

- Temporary suspension applies to fresh and additional lump-sum purchases, switch-ins, new SIP/XSIP registrations, and STP-in transactions in LIC MF Medium to Long Term Fund.
- Effective date of suspension: October 16, 2026.
- Applications received after the applicable cut-off time on October 16, 2026, will not be accepted or processed.
- All existing SIP and STP-in registrations made before the effective date will continue to be processed normally.
- Redemption, switch-out, STP-out, and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) transactions remain fully permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of trading restrictions on specific mutual fund scheme subscriptions as requested by LIC Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must take note of the suspension while placing new transactions in LIC MF Medium to Long Term Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform.
- Ensure no new subscription or SIP/STP registrations are submitted after the cut-off time on October 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026.
- Cut-off Time: Applicable cut-off time on October 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors wishing to make fresh investments or start new SIPs/STPs in LIC MF Medium to Long Term Fund are restricted.
- Existing investors can continue holding, redeeming, or running pre-existing systematic plans without interruption.
