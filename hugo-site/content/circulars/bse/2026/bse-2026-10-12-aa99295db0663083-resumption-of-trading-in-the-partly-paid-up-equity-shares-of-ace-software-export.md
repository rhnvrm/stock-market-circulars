## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of trading in 50,17,556 partly paid-up equity shares of Ace Software Exports Limited (Scrip Code: 890230, ISIN: IN9849B01034) effective October 16, 2026. This follows the receipt of the first call money.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Ace Software Exports Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 890230
- **ISIN:** IN9849B01034
- **Number of Shares:** 50,17,556 Partly Paid Equity Shares
- **Face Value & Paid-up Status:** Face Value of Rs. 10/- each issued on rights basis, partly paid up to Rs. 7.25 per share.
- **Call Money Details:** First call money received of Rs. 30.25 per share (comprising Rs. 2.75 towards face value and Rs. 27.50 towards premium).
- **Market Lot:** 1

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; continuation of existing listing operations and corporate action procedures pursuant to the receipt of call money.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members of the exchange are required to take note of the resumption of trading and update their systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Resumption of Trading Date:** October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables shareholders who have paid the first call money to trade their partly paid-up shares on the exchange platform. Impact is limited to investors and trading members dealing in the specified scrip.
