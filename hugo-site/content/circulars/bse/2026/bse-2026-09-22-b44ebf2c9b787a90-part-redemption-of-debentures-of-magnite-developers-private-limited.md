## Summary

MAGNITE DEVELOPERS PRIVATE LIMITED has fixed a record date for the payment of interest and part redemption of its Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). Trading in the debentures will continue with a reduced face value effective from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: MAGNITE DEVELOPERS PRIVATE LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 974290
- Scrip ID: MDPL-10%-31-3-27-PVT
- ISIN: INE0M1Q07015
- Record Date: 23/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 23/09/2026
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 350214/- per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are requested to note the reduced face value and record/ex-dates for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 23/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 23/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the NCDs of the company will proceed with the revised reduced face value of Rs. 350214/- per debenture.
