## Summary

BSE has notified the suspension of trading in debt securities of specified companies due to their upcoming maturity. Trading members are advised not to deal in these debt securities effective from September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading covers debt securities maturing on September 30, 2026.
- Affected securities include Scrip Code 958304 (FEL-10.15%-30-09-26-PVT, ISIN: INE623B07933) and Scrip Code 955032 (ILFSL-8.50%-30-09-26-PVT, ISIN: INE121H07BS8).
- Trading is restricted with effect from September 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard operational notice regarding debt maturity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure they do not execute any trades in the specified debt securities from September 29, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 29, 2026
- Maturity Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational restriction with minimal market impact.
