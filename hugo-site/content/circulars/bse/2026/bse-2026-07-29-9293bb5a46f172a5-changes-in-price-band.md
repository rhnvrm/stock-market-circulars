## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 11 scrips in the Equity segment, effective from July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260729-29
- Notice Date: 29 Jul 2026
- Department: DOSS
- Total scrips affected: 11

## Affected Scrips and Price Bands

- Aci Infocom Ltd (Scrip Code: 517356) - Revised to 5%
- Aerpace Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 534733) - Revised to 5%
- Asian Granito India Ltd (Scrip Code: 532888) - Revised to 10%
- Devson Catalyst Ltd (Scrip Code: 544823) - Revised to 20%
- Ganon Products Ltd (Scrip Code: 512443) - Revised to 5%
- Orient Press Ltd (Scrip Code: 526325) - Revised to 5%
- Paras Petrofils Ltd (Scrip Code: 521246) - Revised to 5%
- Ritesh International Ltd (Scrip Code: 519097) - Revised to 5%
- Sadbhav Engineering Ltd (Scrip Code: 532710) - Revised to 5%
- Shivansh Finserve Ltd (Scrip Code: 539593) - Revised to 5%
- Spenta International Ltd (Scrip Code: 526161) - Revised to 5%

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must take note of the revised price limits to ensure compliance during order entry and execution.
