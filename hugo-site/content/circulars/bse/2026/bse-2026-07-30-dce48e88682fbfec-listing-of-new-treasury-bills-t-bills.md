## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under the G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS list, effective from July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three new Treasury Bills are being listed: 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day T-Bills.
- Scrip codes: 805247, 805248, and 805249.
- Market lot for all listed T-Bills is set to 1.
- Securities will not be available for trading two working days prior to their respective maturity/redemption dates.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the trading restrictions prior to the maturity or redemption dates.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Maturity Dates:**
  - 91TB291026: October 29, 2026
  - 182T280127: January 28, 2027
  - 364TB29727: July 29, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt listing providing investment and liquidity options in short-term government securities.
