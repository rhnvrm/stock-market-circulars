## Summary

SIS Limited has announced the buyback of its fully paid-up equity shares of face value Rs. 5/- each from the open market through the stock exchange. The buyback is in accordance with the SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 and BSE Exchange Notice No. 20260731-9. The maximum buyback size is INR 1,06,00,00,000 (Rupees One Hundred and Six Crores only), with a maximum buyback price not exceeding Rs. 478.50 per share.

## Key Points

- **Buyback Method:** Open market purchase through the Stock Exchange.
- **Maximum Buyback Size:** Up to INR 1,06,00,00,000 (Rupees One Hundred and Six Crores only).
- **Maximum Buyback Price:** Not exceeding Rs. 478.50 per equity share.
- **Broker to the Buyback:** Elara Securities (India) Private Limited (Clearing No. 3241).
- **Merchant Banker:** Elara Capital (India) Private Limited.
- **Registrar:** MUFG Intime India Private Limited (formerly Link Intime India Private Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

The buyback is executed in compliance with the SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 dated July 6, 2026, and BSE Exchange Notice No. 20260731-9 dated July 31, 2026. No new regulatory changes are introduced in this specific operational notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members/Broker:** Elara Securities (India) Private Limited is appointed as the company's broker for executing the buyback on the Stock Exchange.
- **For SIS Limited:** Ensure compliance with the maximum and minimum buyback size limits, deployment timelines, and disclosure rules under SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018.
- **For Market Participants:** Trading members and investors can contact the designated merchant banker, registrar, or company compliance officer for queries regarding the scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Opening of Buyback:** Thursday, August 13, 2026
- **Maximum Closing Date:** November 20, 2026 (66 working days from opening), or earlier if the Maximum Buyback Size is deployed or determined by the Board after deploying the Minimum Buyback Size.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Positive to neutral. The buyback of shares totaling up to INR 106 Crores represents a significant equity consolidation for SIS Limited, which may support the share price and enhance earnings per share (EPS).
- **Operational Impact:** Low. The buyback will be executed via standard open market exchange mechanisms using a single broker (Elara Securities), resulting in normal trading volume operations without any market disruption.
