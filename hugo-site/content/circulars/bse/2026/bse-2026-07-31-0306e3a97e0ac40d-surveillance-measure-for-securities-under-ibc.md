## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective from August 03, 2026. Securities satisfying the criteria are listed in Annexure I, while securities moving out of the framework are detailed in Annexure II.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the criteria under the IBC framework are subjected to additional surveillance measures starting August 03, 2026.
- A list of securities moving out of the IBC framework is provided in Annexure II, and a consolidated list is available in Annexure III.
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned companies.
- The price band of a scrip moving out of the framework will be reinstated to its original applicable band, subject to not being part of any other surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures in conjunction with existing exchange frameworks for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must take note of the inclusion and exclusion of securities under the IBC surveillance framework and adhere to applicable price bands and trading conditions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 03, 2026
- Notice Date: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects trading and price bands of specified securities undergoing insolvency proceedings, ensuring market integrity and transparency.
