## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the action against defaulting companies for non-payment of Annual Listing Fees (ALF) for the financial year 2025-26 under the Equity Segment. Based on compliance with Regulation 14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, the exchange confirmed that no further action or no action will be taken against specified compliant companies.

## Key Points

- Covers companies that failed to pay outstanding Annual Listing Fees for F.Y. 2025-26.
- No further action will be taken against 10 listed companies that have fulfilled their compliance requirements.
- No action will be taken against Rajeswari Infrastructure Limited pursuant to Exchange Notice No. 20210625-43.
- Trading members and stakeholders are instructed to note the updated compliance status of these entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of Regulation 14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed companies must ensure timely payment of Annual Listing Fees to avoid punitive exchange actions and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 9, 2026
- Financial Year: 2025-26

## Impact Assessment

- Removes potential trading or regulatory restrictions for the listed companies that cleared their dues, ensuring uninterrupted market operations for their securities.
