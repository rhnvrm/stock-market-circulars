## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in debentures of VR Dakshin Private Limited with effect from October 21, 2026, following the full redemption of the NCDs by the company.

## Key Points

- Issuer: VR Dakshin Private Limited
- Reason: Full redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs)
- Effective Date of Suspension: October 21, 2026
- Affected ISINs / Scrip Codes:
  - VRDPL-TBILL+SPREAD-31-3-35-PVT (INE084S07049, Scrip Code: 974772)
  - VDPL-10.70%-31-3-35-PVT (INE084S07031, Scrip Code: 974773)
  - VDPL-MIBOR+SPREAD-31-3-35-PVT (INE084S07056, Scrip Code: 974774)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid NCDs of the company with effect from October 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational closure of trading in the fully redeemed debt instruments with no adverse financial impact on investors as dues have been fully paid.
