## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of New Fund Offer (NFO) schemes of QUANT Mutual Fund along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window is open from October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: QUANT NIFTY 50 Index Fund, QUANT NIFTY 100 Index Fund, and QUANT NIFTY Midcap 150 Index Fund.
- Category: Equity Index Funds.
- Subscription Period: October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 28, 2026.
- The NFO is extended till midnight on October 26, 2026, specifically for Lumpsum purchases on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, with CC reporting 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on October 26, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 28, 2026, for allotment.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: October 12, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 26, 2026 (midnight for lumpsum)
- SIP Authentication Deadline: October 26, 2026, by 3:00 PM
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 28, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 28, 2026, by 6:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to subscribe to new index fund schemes of QUANT Mutual Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform with standard operational guidelines and timelines.
