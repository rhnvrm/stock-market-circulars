## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding action against defaulting companies for non-payment of Annual Listing Fees (ALF) under Regulation 14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. Trading suspension is revoked for two companies, moving them to the normal trading segment, while trading remains suspended for another company.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in equity shares of Aadi Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 530027) and Innocorp Limited (Scrip Code: 531929) is revoked, moving them from Group XT to Group X.
- Trading for Dharti Proteins Ltd (Scrip Code: 531171) continues to remain under suspension.
- Actions are taken pursuant to non-payment of Annual Listing Fees under Regulation 14 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of Regulation 14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 regarding Annual Listing Fees.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure timely payment of Annual Listing Fees to avoid suspension or ensure revocation upon clearance of dues.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date for Revocation: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares in Aadi Industries Ltd and Innocorp Limited will be able to trade their shares in the normal segment effective September 25, 2026. Shares of Dharti Proteins Ltd remain suspended from trading.
