## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movements in certain scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies. Replies have been received from several companies, while responses from others are still awaited.

## Key Points

- **Replies Received From:**
  1. Asian Granito India Ltd (Scrip Code: 532888)
  2. GHV Infra Projects Ltd (Scrip Code: 505504)
  3. Jupiter Wagons Ltd (Scrip Code: 533272)
  4. Justo Realfintech Ltd (Scrip Code: 544542)
  5. Purple Wave Infocom Ltd (Scrip Code: 544627)
  6. Spenta International Ltd (Scrip Code: 526161)
  7. Sula Vineyards Ltd (Scrip Code: 543711)

- **Replies Awaited From:**
  1. AIK Pipes and Polymers Ltd (Scrip Code: 544072)
  2. CMX Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 532217)
  3. Continental Controls Ltd (Scrip Code: 531460)
  4. Crazy Snacks Ltd (Scrip Code: 544807)
  5. Gconnect Logitech and Supply Chain Ltd (Scrip Code: 544156)
  6. Kinetic Trust Ltd (Scrip Code: 531274)
  7. Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 539117)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely clarify significant price and volume movements as requested by the Exchange to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 July 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and dissemination of relevant information to investors regarding abnormal price and volume activity.
