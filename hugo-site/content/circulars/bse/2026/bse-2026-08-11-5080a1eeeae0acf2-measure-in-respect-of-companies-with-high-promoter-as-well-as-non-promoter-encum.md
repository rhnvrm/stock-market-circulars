## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities satisfying the criteria in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective August 14, 2026. Securities in Annexure II are moving out of the framework effective August 12, 2026, and a consolidated list is provided in Annexure III.

## Key Points

- Minimum 75% margin applied to securities listed in Annexure I for Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Effective date for margin implementation: August 14, 2026, applicable to all open positions as of August 13, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities (Annexure II) are eligible to move out of the framework effective August 12, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.
- Measures are in conjunction with other prevailing exchange measures and subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 concerning high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the maintenance of the minimum 75% margin on applicable open and new positions for securities included in Annexure I.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Exiting Framework: August 12, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date for Margin: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Requirements: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding open positions in securities listed under Annexure I will experience increased margin requirements starting August 14, 2026, potentially impacting leverage and liquidity for those specific scrips.
