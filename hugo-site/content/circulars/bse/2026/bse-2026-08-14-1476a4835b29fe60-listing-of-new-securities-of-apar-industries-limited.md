## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of 16,88,618 new equity shares of Apar Industries Limited resulting from a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP), effective from Monday, August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Apar Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 532259, ISIN: INE372A01015)
- **Security Details:** 16,88,618 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) via QIP.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 14,805/- per share.
- **Effective Date of Trading:** August 17, 2026.
- **Date of Allotment:** August 13, 2026.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 40411870 to 42100487.
- **Ranking:** The new shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the new securities admission for market operations.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Apar Industries Limited through QIP capital raising without regulatory or operational disruptions to general market participants.
