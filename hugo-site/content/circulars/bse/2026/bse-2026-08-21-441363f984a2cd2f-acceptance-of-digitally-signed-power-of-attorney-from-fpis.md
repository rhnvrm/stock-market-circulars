## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the acceptance of digitally signed Power of Attorney (PoA) from Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs).

## Key Points

- Permits FPIs to submit digitally signed Power of Attorney.
- Aims to streamline operational processes and documentation compliance.
- Annexure_1 provides further details/formats.

## Regulatory Changes

- Recognition and acceptance of digital signatures on Power of Attorney documents executed by FPIs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and FPIs must ensure that digitally signed PoAs comply with applicable IT and regulatory standards.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as of notice date 21 Aug 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Reduces paperwork and turnaround time for FPI onboarding and operational execution.
