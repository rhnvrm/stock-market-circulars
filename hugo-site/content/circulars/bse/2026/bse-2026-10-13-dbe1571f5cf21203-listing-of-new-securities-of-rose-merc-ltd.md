## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 16,917 new equity shares of Rose Merc Ltd. (Scrip Code: 512115) are listed and permitted to trade with effect from October 14, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters upon conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company: Rose Merc Ltd. (Scrip Code: 512115, ISIN: INE649C01012).
- Securities: 16,917 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 80/- (Issue Price: Rs. 90/-).
- Allotment Date: 20/07/2026.
- Distribution Nos.: 6857131 to 6874047.
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- Lock-in Details: All 16,917 shares (Dist. Nos. 6857131 - 6874047) are under lock-in up to 25/09/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and trading permissions as specified.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 20/07/2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: 14/10/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 25/09/2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of Rose Merc Ltd. through preferential warrant conversion with standard lock-in stipulations.
