## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the revision of price bands for 11 scrips under the Equity segment, effective from July 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 11 scrips have their price bands revised from existing levels.
- Effective date of revision: July 01, 2026.
- Revised price bands include 2%, 5%, and 10% limits depending on the specific scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

- Price bands for specified securities adjusted by the Surveillance department.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure orders are placed within the newly specified price band limits.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading participants and investors trading in the affected scrips must adjust their order placement strategies to align with the new price limits.
