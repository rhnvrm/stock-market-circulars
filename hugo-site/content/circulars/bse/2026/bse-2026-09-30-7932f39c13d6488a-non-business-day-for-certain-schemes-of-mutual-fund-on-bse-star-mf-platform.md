## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about non-business days for certain schemes of mutual funds on the BSE StAR MF platform for the month of October 2026, based on communications received from respective Mutual Funds.

## Key Points

- Markets will be closed for certain mutual fund schemes on account of "Non-Business Day" during October 2026.
- Applies to the BSE StAR MF platform.
- Notice includes an annexure detailing specific schemes and NBD dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

- Subscription, redemption, switch, SIP, normal STP, SWP, and AMC-STP will not be allowed under the specified schemes on their respective Non-Business Day (NBD) dates.
- Trading members must refer to the attached annexure for scheme-wise non-business day schedules.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026.
- Applicable Period: October 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is limited to investors and trading members transacting in specific mutual fund schemes observing non-business days during October 2026.
