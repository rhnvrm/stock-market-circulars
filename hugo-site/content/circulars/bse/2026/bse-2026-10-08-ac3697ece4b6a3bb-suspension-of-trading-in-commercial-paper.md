## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that multiple companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of commercial papers. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of these entities as per the scheduled dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20261008-54
- Department: Listing Operations / Debt Segment
- Affected entities include Titan Company Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd, Grasim Industries Ltd, and others.
- Trading members are instructed to avoid trading in these commercial papers prior to redemption.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational notice regarding commercial paper redemptions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must comply with the restriction against dealing in the specified commercial papers starting from the respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 8, 2026
- Redemption record and ex-dates span from October 13, 2026 to October 26, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operation ensuring smooth settlement and redemption of commercial papers.
