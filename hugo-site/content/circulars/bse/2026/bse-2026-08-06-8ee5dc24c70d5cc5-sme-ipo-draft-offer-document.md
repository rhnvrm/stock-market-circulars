## Summary

BSE has published a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Faith Industries Limited on August 04, 2026. The document is hosted on the SME website for public comments for a period of at least 21 days as per Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Faith Industries Limited
- Filing Date: August 04, 2026
- Notice Date: August 06, 2026
- Public Comment Period: At least 21 days from the date of notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public can submit their comments on the draft offer document within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 06, 2026
- Comment Deadline: Within 21 days from August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites public participation and feedback on the proposed SME IPO of Faith Industries Limited.
