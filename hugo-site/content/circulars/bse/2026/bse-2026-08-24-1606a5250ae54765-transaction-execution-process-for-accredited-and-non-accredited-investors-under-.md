## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the transaction execution process for accredited and non-accredited investors under Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs) through the BSE StAR MF Platform, referencing SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/IMD/IMD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/26 dated February 27, 2025.

## Key Points

- AMCs have launched Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) schemes and enabled transactions via the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Non-accredited new investors can place multiple purchase orders across SIF schemes on the same day if the aggregate investment amount is ₹10 lakh or more.
- Members must refer to the attached process note detailing transaction execution for both accredited and non-accredited investors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of transaction execution standards for SIF schemes in alignment with SEBI's regulatory framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure strict adherence to the prescribed process while executing transactions in SIF schemes.
- Third-party vendor solutions and in-house trading applications must be updated and validated to ensure seamless processing at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational clarity and compliance for brokers and investors participating in Specialized Investment Funds through the BSE StAR MF Platform.
