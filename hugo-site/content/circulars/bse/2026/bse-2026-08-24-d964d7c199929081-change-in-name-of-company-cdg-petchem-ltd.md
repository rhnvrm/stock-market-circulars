## Summary

CDG Petchem Ltd has informed the Exchange about a change in the name of the Company to Jujhar Logistics Limited. Consequent to this change, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: CDG Petchem Ltd
- New Company Name: Jujhar Logistics Limited
- Scrip Code: 534796
- New Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System: JUJHARLOGI
- New Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: JUJHARLOGI

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the change and update their systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Name and Scrip ID Change: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to trading terminals and records reflecting the new company name and scrip ID for scrip code 534796.
