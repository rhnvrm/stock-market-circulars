## Summary

Adani Airport Holdings Limited has announced a part redemption of its debentures (Scrip Code: 976065), setting the actual reduced face value at Rs. 60,790 per debenture effective from August 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Adani Airport Holdings Limited
- Purpose: Part Redemption of Debentures
- Scrip Code: 976065
- ISIN: INE0GCN07047
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 60,790/- per Debenture
- Effective Date: August 7, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised reduced face value for trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 6, 2026
- Effective Date: August 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members will trade the debentures with the updated reduced face value starting August 7, 2026.
