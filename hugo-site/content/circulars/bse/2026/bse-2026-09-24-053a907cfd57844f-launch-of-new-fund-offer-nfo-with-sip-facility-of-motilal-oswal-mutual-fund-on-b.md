## Summary

Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the Motilal Oswal Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund, along with a SIP facility, on the BSE StAR MF platform. The NFO period runs from September 25, 2026, to October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Motilal Oswal Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund (Regular Plan - Growth: INF247L01HP8; Direct Plan - Growth: INF247L01HO1).
- **NFO Window:** September 25, 2026, to October 09, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 9, 2026 for lumpsum purchases).
- **SIP/XSIP Registration:** Must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on October 09, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 15, 2026.
- **Payment & Funding Rules:** Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 15, 2026. Transactions without funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure client funds are initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day and that SIP/XSIP registrations are authenticated by 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date. Clear funds for allotment must reach the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** September 25, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** October 09, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investment access to the Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund for investors via the BSE StAR MF platform with standard processing and settlement timelines.
