## Summary

 BSE has announced the new ISIN number for TD Power Systems Ltd following the sub-division of its equity shares from face value of Rs. 2/- each to Re. 1/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TD Power Systems Ltd
- Scrip Code: 533553
- Old Face Value: Rs. 2/-
- New Face Value: Re. 1/-
- New ISIN: INE419M01035
- Ex-Date: August 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the new ISIN for trades in the scrip.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 20, 2026
- Ex-Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The new ISIN becomes effective for trades done on and from the ex-date.
