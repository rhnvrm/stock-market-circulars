## Summary

Carnet Books Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document for an SME IPO on September 30, 2026, in compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations. The document has been made available on the stock exchange and SME websites to seek public comments for at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Carnet Books Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 1, 2026
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com
- Hosted URL: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

None. The filing adheres to Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Interested stakeholders and the public are invited to submit any comments on the Draft Offer Document within 21 days of the notice to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 1, 2026
- Public Comment Deadline: 21 days from October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public transparency and stakeholder feedback for the proposed SME IPO of Carnet Books Limited.
