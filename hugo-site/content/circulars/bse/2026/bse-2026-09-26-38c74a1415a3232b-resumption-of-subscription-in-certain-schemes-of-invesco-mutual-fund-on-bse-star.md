## Summary

BSE StAR MF platform has announced the resumption of fresh subscriptions, switch-in transactions, fresh SIPs, and STPs in three schemes of Invesco Mutual Fund, effective September 28, 2026. These subscriptions are subject to an overall limit of ₹10 Lakh per PAN per day at the first holder level across all subscription modes.

## Key Points

- Applicable to Invesco India – Invesco Global Consumer Trends Fund of Fund, Invesco Global Equity Income Fund of Fund, and Invesco Pan European Equity Fund of Fund.
- Subscriptions, additional purchases, switch-ins, fresh SIPs, and STP-in registrations are permitted up to ₹10 Lakh per PAN per day.
- Existing SIP and STP registrations continue to be processed normally according to existing mandates.
- Redemption, switch-out, and STP-out transactions remain unrestricted.

## Regulatory Changes

Removal of temporary suspension on subscriptions for specified Invesco Mutual Fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform, accompanied by a transaction ceiling of ₹10 Lakh per PAN per day.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and trading participants must ensure that all new subscription, switch-in, SIP, and STP-in orders in the specified Invesco schemes comply with the ₹10 Lakh per PAN per day limit.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 26, 2026
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and distributors regain the ability to invest in Invesco's global fund of fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform, albeit with a daily transactional cap per PAN.
