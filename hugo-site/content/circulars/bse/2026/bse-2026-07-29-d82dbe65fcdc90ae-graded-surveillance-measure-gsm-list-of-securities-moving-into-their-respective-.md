## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from July 30, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions, margin rates, price bands, and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) requirements for securities placed under various GSM stages.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the annexure move to higher GSM stages effective July 30, 2026.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade with a 5% or lower price band and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value by buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade-for-trade with a 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday), and 100% ASD to be deposited by buyers (with Stage IV having no upward movement).
- Settlement for these securities will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

The notice enforces enhanced surveillance mechanisms as outlined in previous framework guidelines dating back to February 2017, aimed at curbing excessive price volatility and protecting market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must exercise adequate caution while executing trades in the affected securities.
- Buyers must ensure necessary funds for Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) and 100% margins where applicable.
- No netting off is permitted for settlements on impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 30, 2026
- Notice Date: July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding or trading securities moved to higher GSM stages will face severe liquidity constraints, restricted trading frequencies (for Stages III and IV), and higher capital lock-up due to mandatory ASD and 100% margin requirements.
