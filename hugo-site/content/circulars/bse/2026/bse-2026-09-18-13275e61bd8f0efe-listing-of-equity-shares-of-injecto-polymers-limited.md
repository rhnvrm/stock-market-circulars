## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Injecto Polymers Limited under the 'MT' Group of Securities in the SME segment, effective from September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Injecto Polymers Limited
- Scrip Code: 544930
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: INJECTO
- ISIN: INE1JJF01019
- Issue Price: Rs. 100/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 90/-)
- Number of Securities: 20,78,9,600 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the mandatory demat form requirement and minimum market lot of 1,200 shares.
- Lock-in period has been marked in the depository's records as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 17, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 21, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides public market access and liquidity for Injecto Polymers Limited shares under the SME segment with standard trade-for-trade monitoring.
