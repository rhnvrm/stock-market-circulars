## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CPs) of several companies. The companies have fixed a record date for the redemption of their commercial papers, and trading members are advised not to deal in these CPs starting from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects commercial papers of multiple companies including Nuvama Wealth Management Limited, Capri Global Capital Limited, Grasim Industries Ltd, Bajaj Finance Limited, and TATA STEEL LIMITED.
- Record Date and Ex-Date for the redemptions are set for September 10, 2026.
- Trading members are strictly advised not to trade in the specified commercial papers of these entities to ensure proper redemption processing.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the commercial papers listed in the notice as of the ex-date to ensure compliance with exchange settlement procedures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 9, 2026
- Record Date: September 10, 2026
- Ex-Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Holders of the commercial papers for the listed entities will undergo redemption processes, and secondary market liquidity in these specific CPs will be temporarily halted or terminated as they approach maturity.
