## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has issued a notice detailing the schedule of client margin reporting and applicable penalty dates for the Commodity Derivatives Segment for the month of August 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers trade dates starting from August 3, 2026, through August 31, 2026.
- Specifies corresponding last dates for margin reporting and penalty dates for each trade date.
- Applies to all members trading in the Commodity Derivatives Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; this is a routine calendar notification.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure client margins are reported within the stipulated timelines for each trade date to avoid penalties.

## Important Dates

- Effective Period: August 3, 2026, to August 31, 2026 (Trade Dates).
- Penalty Dates: Ranging from August 11, 2026, to September 8, 2026, depending on the respective trade date.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational compliance reminder for clearing members and brokers active in the commodity derivatives segment to align their back-office margin reporting systems.
