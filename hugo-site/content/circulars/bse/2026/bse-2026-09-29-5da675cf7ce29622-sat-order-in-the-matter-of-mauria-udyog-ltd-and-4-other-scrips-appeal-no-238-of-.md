## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that SEBI confirmed via email dated September 29, 2026, that appellants Vidhi Mehul Shah and Mehul Hasmukh Shah have paid the required penalty amount in favor of SEBI, pursuant to the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated September 04, 2026, in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips (Appeal No. 238 of 2026 and Appeal No. 239 of 2026).

## Key Points

- SAT order dated September 04, 2026, granted a stay of the impugned order subject to the deposit of the full disgorgement amount and penalty by Mehul Hasmukh Shah and the full penalty by Vidhi Mehul Shah within four weeks in an interest-bearing account.
- SEBI confirmed via email on September 29, 2026, that the appellants have successfully paid the penalty amount in favor of SEBI.
- This notice is a continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260701-4 dated July 01, 2026.
- Trading members are advised to take note of the compliance update.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SAT order conditions regarding penalty deposits in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and associated scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the penalty payment compliance by the noticees.

## Important Dates

- July 01, 2026: Exchange Notice No. 20260701-4 issued in the captioned matter.
- September 04, 2026: SAT order passed granting stay subject to penalty deposit within four weeks.
- September 29, 2026: SEBI confirmation email regarding penalty payment and BSE notice issued.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational and regulatory update regarding compliance with tribunal orders for listed entities and associated noticees; minimal direct impact on general trading operations.
