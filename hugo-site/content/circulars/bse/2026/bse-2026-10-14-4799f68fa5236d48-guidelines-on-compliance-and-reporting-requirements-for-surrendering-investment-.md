## Summary

BSE has issued guidelines pursuant to SEBI directives regarding the compliance and reporting obligations of Investment Advisers (IAs) submitting surrender applications. The guidelines clarify that submitting a surrender application does not automatically absolve intermediaries from statutory duties, and regulatory obligations continue until formal acceptance by SEBI.

## Key Points

- Continuity of regulatory obligations until formal surrender acceptance by SEBI.
- Date of cessation of compliance and code of conduct obligations is the date of formal acceptance by SEBI, not application submission date.
- Mandatory maintenance of active compliance mechanisms and submission of regulatory returns during the review pendency.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clarification under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 regarding the interim period between surrender application and formal acceptance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must continue to fulfil all compliance, grievance redressal, and periodic reporting requirements.
- Maintain active compliance mechanisms to avoid enforcement actions for non-compliance during the surrender review process.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures uninterrupted investor protection and regulatory oversight during the transition or winding down of investment advisory practices.
