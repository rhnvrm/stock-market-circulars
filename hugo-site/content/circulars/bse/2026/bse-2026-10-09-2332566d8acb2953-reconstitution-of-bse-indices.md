## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the monthly reconstitution results for various BSE indices, including BSE SME IPO segments. The changes will be effective starting from the open of trading on Monday, October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Monthly reconstitution results announced by BSE Index Services Private Limited.
- Includes additions and changes across equity and BSE SME IPO indices.
- Effective date for all reconstitution adjustments is October 19, 2026.
- Numerous companies and scrips across sectors are impacted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic index maintenance and constituent updates in accordance with standard BSE Index methodologies.

## Compliance Requirements

- Index funds, institutional investors, and trading participants must update their benchmarks, portfolios, and trading systems to align with the reconstituted index constituents prior to the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 9, 2026
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts portfolio rebalancing for index-linked funds and ETFs tracking BSE indices.
- Influences trading volumes and liquidity for the affected scrips.
