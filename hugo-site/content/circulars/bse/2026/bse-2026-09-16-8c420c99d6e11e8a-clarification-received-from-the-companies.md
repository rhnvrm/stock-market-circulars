## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing companies that have provided or are yet to provide clarifications regarding recent significant price and volume movements in their scrips. Replies from seven companies have been received and attached, while replies from eleven other companies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- The Exchange observed significant price/volume movements in specific scrips and sought clarifications to ensure investors have relevant information.
- Replies received from 7 companies: Brainbees Solutions Ltd, Firstsource Solutions Ltd, Jindal Poly Films Ltd, JSW Holdings Ltd, Quick Heal Technologies Ltd, Redtape Ltd, and Accedere Ltd.
- Replies awaited from 11 companies: Ashoka Metcast Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Jhandewalas Foods Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Miven Machine Tools Ltd, Mopshop Distribution Ltd, Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Ltd, Nirmitee Robotics India Ltd, Sera Investments & Finance India Ltd, and Varvee Global Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely clarify significant price and volume movements as requested by the Exchange to maintain market integrity and investor awareness.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 16 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected scrips may experience continued volatility or scrutiny pending receipt and review of outstanding company clarifications.
