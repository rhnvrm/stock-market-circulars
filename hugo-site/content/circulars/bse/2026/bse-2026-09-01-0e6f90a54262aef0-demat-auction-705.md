## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. 705 (Rolling Settlement No. DR-705/2026-27), inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction on September 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on September 1, 2026.
- Auction matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Wednesday, Sep 02, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.
- Restriction on trading members on offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities has been removed following the implementation of Uniform Membership structure in Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 1, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: September 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine settlement process with standard operational guidelines for clearing and trading members.
