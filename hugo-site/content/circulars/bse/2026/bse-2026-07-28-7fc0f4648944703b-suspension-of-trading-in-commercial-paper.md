## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) for various companies that have fixed record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified commercial papers starting from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in commercial papers due to redemption record dates.
- Affected entities include ECL Finance Limited, Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, Can Fin Homes Ltd., Capri Global Capital Limited, Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, ICICI Home Finance Company Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Aarti Industries Ltd, 360 ONE WAM Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Muthoot Microfin Limited, Time Technoplast Limited, and Sikka Ports & Terminals Ltd.
- Record dates span from August 3, 2026, to August 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure zero trading activity in the specified commercial paper scrips from their respective ex-dates/record dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 Jul 2026
- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: 03/08/2026, 06/08/2026, 11/08/2026, and 12/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricts secondary market trading in specific commercial papers around their redemption windows to facilitate orderly processing by depositories and exchanges.
