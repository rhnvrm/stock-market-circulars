## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 6 companies under ESOP / ESOS are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Friday, October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective date for trading: October 16, 2026.
- Total companies involved: 6 (AXIS Bank Limited, CARE Ratings Limited, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, Insolation Energy Limited, and Tracxn Technologies Limited).
- Securities issued under ESOP / ESOS with no lock-in applicable across all listed tranches.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine corporate action listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard compliance requirements for listing of shares issued under employee stock option plans.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact as these represent routine ESOP issuances across listed entities.
