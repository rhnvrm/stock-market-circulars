## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application of Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC surveillance framework are placed under ASM effective August 28, 2026.
- Periodic reviews are conducted for securities under this framework.
- Annexures detail securities moving into the framework, moving out of the framework, consolidated lists, and those classified post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25%.
- Measures operate in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures for specified securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework.
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are required to note the list of securities under the IBC surveillance framework and adjust trading and risk parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026
- Notice Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in affected securities is subject to specific surveillance monitoring and potential price band restrictions, ensuring market integrity for companies undergoing corporate insolvency resolution.
