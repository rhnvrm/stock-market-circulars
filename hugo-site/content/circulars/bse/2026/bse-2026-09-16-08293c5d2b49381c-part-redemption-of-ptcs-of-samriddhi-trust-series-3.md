## Summary

Catalyst Trusteeship Limited has informed the Exchange regarding the partial redemption and reduced face value of Pass-Through Certificates (PTCs) issued under Samriddhi Trust Series 3. Trading members are advised that the PTCs will be traded with the reduced face value effective from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Samriddhi Trust Series 3
- Scrip Code / ISIN: 978119 / INE303715010 (STS3-12.50%-15-8-31-PTC)
- Purpose: Part-Redemption of PTCs
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 12,998,330.52 per PTC
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are required to take note of the reduced face value for trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 16, 2026
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading members to reflect the revised face value of the specified debt instrument in their systems.
