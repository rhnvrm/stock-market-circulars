## Summary

The Exchange has observed significant price and volume movements in various scrips and requested clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarifications have been received from 10 companies, including DCM Shriram Industries Ltd, Mahanagar Telephone Nigam Ltd (MTNL), and Spencers Retail Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 21 companies, including Deep Polymers Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, and others.
- Issued by the Surveillance Department of BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must provide prompt clarification to the Exchange upon observing significant price and volume movements when requested.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 23 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Promotes market transparency and investor protection by publicly disclosing companies under surveillance for price/volume volatility.
