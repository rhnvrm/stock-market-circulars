## Summary

SEBI has notified amendments to the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, dated July 06, 2026. Consequently, the separate window for buy-back offers shall cease to be available, and trading shall be undertaken through the same window as applicable for the secondary market.

## Key Points

- Separate window for buy-back offers will cease to be available.
- Trading for open market buybacks will be executed through the regular secondary market window.
- Applicable to all Open Market Buyback offers where the Board of Directors approves the resolution on or after August 01, 2026.
- Operational requirements from the March 08, 2023 guidance continue to apply.

## Regulatory Changes

Amendments to the SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018, mandates routing open market buybacks through the secondary market trading window.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market intermediaries must conduct open market buybacks via the secondary market window for applicable resolutions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 July 2026
- Effective Date for Board Resolutions: August 01, 2026 onwards

## Impact Assessment

Changes the operational execution mechanism for open market buybacks, aligning them directly with standard secondary market trading windows.
