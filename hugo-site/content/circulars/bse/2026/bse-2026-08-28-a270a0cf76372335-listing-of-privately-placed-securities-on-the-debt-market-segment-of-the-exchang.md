## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new securities issued on a private placement basis (commercial papers) on the debt market segment effective August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are traded only in dematerialised form under specified ISIN numbers.
- Standard trading denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuances include commercial papers from Bajaj Housing Finance Limited, SBICAP Securities Limited, Tata Steel Limited, and Sharekhan Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard demat form and denomination requirements for debt segment instruments.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026
- Allotment and redemption dates vary by specific commercial paper issue (ranging from August 2026 to February 2027).

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update facilitating trading of newly listed commercial papers on the BSE debt segment.
