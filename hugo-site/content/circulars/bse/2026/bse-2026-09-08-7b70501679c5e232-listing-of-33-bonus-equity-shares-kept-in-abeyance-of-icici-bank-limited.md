## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 33 bonus equity shares of ICICI Bank Limited (Scrip Code: 532174) that were previously kept in abeyance, effective from Wednesday, September 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: ICICI Bank Limited
- Scrip Code: 532174
- ISIN: INE090A01021
- Securities: 33 Bonus Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each kept in abeyance.
- Distinctive Numbers range:
  - 8 shares: 6412509687 to 6412509694
  - 25 shares: 6412594331 to 6412594355

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing of the new securities for trading.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 8, 2026
- Effective Date: September 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Negligible market impact given the minuscule number of shares (33 shares) being released from abeyance.
