## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity segment on Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are advised to participate and test their connectivity to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site using the specified parameters.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Equity segment.
- Connection parameters provided for BOLT TWS users and 3rd party trading applications through ETI APIs.
- Details for connecting to web-based systems (RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, Extranet, etc.) at the DR site are outlined.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine technical mock trading exercise.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should verify their disaster recovery connectivity parameters and participate in the mock trading session to ensure business continuity readiness.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: Saturday, March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on regular market operations as the session is conducted on a non-business day for testing disaster recovery setups.
