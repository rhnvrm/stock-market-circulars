## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) for multiple companies due to the fixation of record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the record date.

## Key Points

- Trading members are instructed not to deal in commercial papers of listed companies with upcoming redemption record dates.
- Affected companies include 360 ONE WAM Limited, Muthoot Fincorp Limited, Sharekhan Limited, ICICI Home Finance Company Limited, Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Fedbank Financial Services Limited, and Tata Steel Limited.
- Record Date and Ex-Date for all listed commercial papers is set for 01/09/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the commercial papers of the mentioned companies effective from the specified record/ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 01/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 01/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly redemption and settlement of commercial papers by preventing trading activity past the record date.
