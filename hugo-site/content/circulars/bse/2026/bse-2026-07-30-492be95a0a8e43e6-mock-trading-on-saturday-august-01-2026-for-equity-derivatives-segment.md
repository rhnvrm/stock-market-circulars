## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, August 01, 2026. This session is intended to help members test their trading applications, including compliance with the introduction of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) and Standard Operating Procedures applicable from Monday, August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, August 01, 2026, in the Equity Derivatives segment.
- Focuses on testing CAS functionality, risk-reduction modes, and trading halts.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.30 release announced separately.
- Trades resulting from the mock session will not attract any margin obligations, pay-ins, or pay-outs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Closing Auction Session (CAS) & Standard Operating Procedures applicable from August 03, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members can participate in the mock trading session or UAT environment to fulfill regulatory testing requirements in accordance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 04:30 pm on the day of the mock session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 01, 2026
- Effective Date for CAS & SOPs: August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness for trading members using third-party or in-house platforms ahead of new market timing and auction procedures.
