## Summary

BSE has announced the live activities schedule for the Offer to Buy (OTB) – Acquisition Window (Takeover) of AAR Shyam India Investment Company Limited (Scrip Code: 542377, ISIN: INE512R01010). The bidding window is scheduled from Monday, October 19, 2026, to Monday, November 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** AAR Shyam India Investment Company Limited
- **Security Symbol:** AARSHYAM
- **Scrip Code:** 542377
- **ISIN:** INE512R01010
- **Offer Size:** Up to 58,43,327 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 15.00 per share
- **Opening Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Closing Date:** November 2, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational participation governed by previous Exchange Notices no. 20150930-1 and 20170203-29.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure UCC of the investor is registered/updated with correct PAN and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bidding is allowed only for investors holding shares in demat form.
- If no securities are accepted, no End of Issue files will be generated on Extranet.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Offering Period / Modifications / Cancellation:** October 19, 2026 to November 2, 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- **Give-up / Take-up:** October 19, 2026 to October 30, 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- **Give-up (last day of offer):** November 2, 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- **Take-up (last day of offer):** November 2, 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and custodians must utilize the specified iBBS, RTRMS, and Extranet platforms adhering to scheduled timings for successful participation in the takeover acquisition window.
