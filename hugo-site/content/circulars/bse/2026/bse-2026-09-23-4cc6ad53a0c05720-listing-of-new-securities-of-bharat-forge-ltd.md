## Summary

Bharat Forge Ltd. has announced the listing and admission for trading of 1,05,82,010 newly allotted equity shares issued to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) via a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP), effective from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 500493
- ISIN No.: INE465A01025
- Number of Securities: 1,05,82,010 Equity shares of Rs. 2/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 1890/-
- Date of Allotment: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading: September 24, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 478088633 to 488670642
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the admission of new securities for trading.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 22, 2026
- Trading Commencement Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Inclusion of new equity shares from QIP increases the paid-up capital of Bharat Forge Ltd., ranking pari-passu with existing shares.
