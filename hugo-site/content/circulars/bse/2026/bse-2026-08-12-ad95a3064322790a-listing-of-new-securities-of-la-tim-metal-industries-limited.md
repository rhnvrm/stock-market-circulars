## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has notified trading members that trading is permitted in the newly listed equity shares of La Tim Metal & Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 505693). This follows the conversion of 1,18,064 partly paid-up equity shares of Re. 1/- each to fully paid-up equity shares, which were initially issued on a rights basis. The shares will be available for trading starting Thursday, August 13, 2026, and will rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** La Tim Metal & Industries Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 505693
- **ISIN:** INE501N01020
- **Total Shares Listed:** 1,18,064 equity shares
- **Share Details:** Equity shares of Re. 1/- each, fully paid-up, converted from partly paid shares issued on a rights basis.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 130630627 to 130748690
- **Fungibility:** These newly listed shares are pari-passu (on equal footing) with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

No specific regulatory changes are introduced in this notice. It is a standard operational listing of newly converted equity shares under BSE's listing rules and procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance actions are required for trading members or external entities, other than updating internal systems/portfolios to reflect the listing of these additional fully paid-up shares.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date of Trading:** August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine operational listing involving a small quantity of shares (1,18,064 shares).
- **Operational Impact:** Low. No changes to trading hours, settlement cycles, or margins are required. Trading members should ensure that their database is updated with the expanded share count and the listing of these shares under the existing ISIN (INE501N01020).
