## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Hy-Tech Engineers Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment (B Group), effective Wednesday, September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Hy-Tech Engineers Limited
- Scrip Code: 544891
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: September 16, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Shift of trading segment from T Group to B Group based on continuation of Exchange's Notice No. 20260831-24.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members to take note of the segment change for execution and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 1, 2026
- Effective Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors can trade in the company's shares under the Rolling segment (Group B) starting from the effective date.
