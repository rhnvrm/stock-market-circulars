## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Dolphin Medical Services Ltd. The offer period runs from Friday, 31st July 2026 to Thursday, 13th August 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: DOLPHIN MEDICAL SERVICES LTD
- Security Symbol: DOLPHMED
- ISIN: INE796B01013
- Scrip Code: 526504
- Offer Size: Up to 39,25,988 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 4.80 per share
- Opening Date: Friday, 31st July 2026
- Closing Date: Thursday, 13th August 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00

## Regulatory Changes

- None specific other than the standard OTB framework instructions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Bid Offering Period: 31st July 2026 to 13th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Bid Modifications/Cancellation: 31st July 2026 to 13th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Give-up / Take-up: 31st July 2026 to 12th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Give-up (last day): 13th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Take-up (last day): 13th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly bidding, modification, and settlement for market participants and custodians participating in the takeover acquisition window for Dolphin Medical Services Ltd.
