## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-740/2026-27. The auction sessions and related activities are scheduled for October 23, 2026, with pay-in scheduled for October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Auction Offer Entry:** 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 23, 2026.
- **Auction Matching:** 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on October 23, 2026.
- **Delivery Statement and Report:** 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m. on October 23, 2026.
- **Money Statement & Final Reports:** 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. on October 23, 2026.
- **Auction Pay-in:** Monday, October 26, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- Restrictions on trading members offering securities in auctions where they had short-delivered securities are removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine operational circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off times.
- Trading members must exercise utmost care while punching security codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Members should refer to the cut-off rate on the Auction Offer Entry Screen and review corporate action notices (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split) issued by respective departments.

## Important Dates

- **Auction Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Pay-in Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for rolling settlement short deliveries without broader market implications.
