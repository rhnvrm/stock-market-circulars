## Summary

Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd has fixed a record date for the part redemption of its non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading members are informed that trading in these debentures will be conducted with a reduced face value effective from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd
- Purpose: Part Redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures
- Record Date: 29/08/2026
- Ex-Date: 28/08/2026
- Affected Scrip Codes: 974771, 976030, 977231, 977351

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and ensure transactions are settled accordingly with effect from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 27/08/2026
- Ex-Date: 28/08/2026
- Record Date: 29/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified debentures of Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd will proceed with updated reduced face values per debenture.
