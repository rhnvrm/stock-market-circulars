## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) for multiple companies due to the fixation of record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice covers multiple companies including Muthoot Fincorp Limited, Tata Power Co. Ltd, Redington Limited, and others.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of these companies as per the specified schedule.
- Record and Ex-dates are primarily set for September 09, 2026, and September 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the commercial papers of the listed companies from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / Ex-Date: 09/09/2026 and 10/09/2026
- Settlement Numbers: 712/2026-2027 and 713/2026-2027

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of trading in the specified commercial papers of the listed entities to facilitate redemption processing.
