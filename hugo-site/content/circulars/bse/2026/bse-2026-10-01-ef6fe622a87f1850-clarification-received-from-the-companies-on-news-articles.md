## Summary

BSE has sought and received clarifications from listed companies regarding various news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels to ensure investor awareness and accuracy of information.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd, Schneider Electric Infrastructure Ltd, and Adani Enterprises Ltd.
- Reply is awaited from SpiceJet Ltd.
- Covers media references across business-standard.com, economictimes.indiatimes.com, and ndtvprofit.com.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; part of standard ongoing stock exchange surveillance and compliance monitoring.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to clarify news articles as mandated by exchange surveillance mechanisms when requested.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 01 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct market impact; provides transparency regarding circulating news items concerning listed entities.
