## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the schedule of activities and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window - Takeover) of shares of PASUPATI FINCAP LTD (Security Symbol: PASUFIN, Scrip Code: 511734, ISIN: INE527C01010). The offer runs from October 1, 2026, to October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: PASUPATI FINCAP LTD (PASUFIN)
- ISIN: INE527C01010, Scrip Code: 511734
- Offer Size: Up to 12,22,000 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 12.00 per share
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00, Market Lot: 1
- Opening Date: Thursday, 1st October 2026
- Closing Date: Thursday, 15th October 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None specific; standard operating procedures for acquisition window and takeover operations apply as per prior Exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of investors is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and investor category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians must note that if no securities are accepted, no End of Issue files will be generated on the Extranet.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: 1st October 2026 (9.15 a.m.)
- Issue Closing Date: 15th October 2026 (3.30 p.m. / Take-up up to 4.00 p.m.)
- Bid Offering & Modification Period: 1st October 2026 to 15th October 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Give-up / Take-up Period: 1st October 2026 to 14th October 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.); Give-up on 15th October 2026; Take-up on 15th October 2026 up to 4.00 p.m.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly execution and settlement of the takeover acquisition window for Pasupati Fincap Ltd shareholders and participating trading/custodian members.
