## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips concerning Noticee Nilratan Suppliers Private Limited. SEBI confirmed that the appellant has paid the full penalty amount in favor of SEBI as directed by the SAT order.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous Exchange Notice No. 20260701-4 dated July 01, 2026.
- Hon’ble SAT vide order dated September 07, 2026, stayed the impugned order subject to the deposit of the full penalty within four weeks in an interest-bearing account.
- SEBI confirmed via email on September 22, 2026, that Nilratan Suppliers Private Limited has paid the required penalty amount.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative compliance update.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the payment of the penalty and ensure compliance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: September 07, 2026
- SEBI Confirmation Date: September 22, 2026
- Notice Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational and compliance update for trading members with no direct broad market impact.
