## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd has released the Tender Period / Pre-Expiry Margin Tracker applicable to futures contracts in the Commodity Derivatives segment expiring in October 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines data pertaining to the Tender Period for October 2026 commodity futures contracts.
- Specifies the applicable Tender Period Margin Percent for each Tender Period Day.
- Attached as Annexure_October for member and participant reference.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants are requested to take note of the tender period margin tracker and applicable percentages.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 16 Sep 2026
- Expiry Period: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational compliance for market participants trading commodity derivatives futures contracts expiring in October 2026.
