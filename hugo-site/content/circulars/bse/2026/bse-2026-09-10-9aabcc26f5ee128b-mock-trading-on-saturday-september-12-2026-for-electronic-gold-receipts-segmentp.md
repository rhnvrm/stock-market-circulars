## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, September 12, 2026, utilizing both the primary site (PR) and disaster recovery site (DR). Trading members using third-party or in-house applications via ETI APIs can test various functionalities including risk-reduction mode and trading halts.

## Key Points

- **Date:** Saturday, September 12, 2026.
- **Segment:** Electronic Gold Receipts (PR & DR).
- **Purpose:** Testing applications, trading functionalities, risk-reduction mode, and trading halts under simulated conditions.
- **No BOLT TWS Release:** There shall be no new BOLT TWS Release for this session.
- **No Financial Obligation:** Trades do not attract margin obligations, pay-in, pay-out, or create actual rights and liabilities.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants are requested to participate actively in the mock trading sessions and provide feedback via email or contact numbers provided by 4:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** September 12, 2026
- **Log-in:** 09:15 AM - 10:00 AM
- **Pre-Open (PR):** 10:00 AM - 10:15 AM
- **Continuous Trading (PR):** 10:15 AM - 12:00 PM
- **Switchover to DR & Pre-Open:** 12:00 PM - 01:00 PM
- **Continuous Trading (DR):** 01:00 PM - 02:45 PM
- **Closing:** 02:45 PM

## Impact Assessment

Operational readiness test for members to verify trading system compatibility with BSE infrastructure during regular and disaster recovery site switchovers.
