## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has informed trading members about companies that have fixed record dates for interest payment and part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) / Bonds. Trading in these debentures will be conducted with a reduced face value effective from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **Shalibhadra Finance Ltd:** Scrip Code: 977720, ISIN: INE861D07018, Record Date: 27/09/2026, Ex-Date: 25/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 7,500/- per debenture.
- **SI Creva Capital Services Private Limited:** Scrip Code: 978157, ISIN: INE996U07545, Record Date: 27/09/2026, Ex-Date: 25/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 58,333.35 per debenture.
- **Pahal Financial Services Private Limited:** Scrip Code: 976133, ISIN: INE514Q07379, Record Date: 29/09/2026, Ex-Date: 29/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 23,263.64 per debenture.
- **Signatureglobal (India) Limited:** Scrip Code: 977218, ISIN: INE903U07087, Record Date: 30/09/2026, Ex-Date: 30/09/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 75,001/- per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard debt corporate action processing.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the reduced face values and ensure that trading in the respective debentures is executed accordingly with effect from the specified dates.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 24 September 2026
- **Ex-Dates:** 25 September 2026 to 30 September 2026
- **Record Dates:** 27 September 2026 to 30 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for debt segment participants reflecting capital reduction through part redemption of debentures.
