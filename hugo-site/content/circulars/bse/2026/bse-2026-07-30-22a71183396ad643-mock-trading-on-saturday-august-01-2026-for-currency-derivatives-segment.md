## Summary

The Exchange has scheduled a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment on Saturday, August 01, 2026. Trading members can use this session to test software and fulfill regulatory requirements.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, August 01, 2026, for the Currency Derivatives segment.
- Fulfills regulatory requirements in accordance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.30 shall be released.
- Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Conducted in accordance with existing SEBI circular guidelines for software testing and risk management.

## Compliance Requirements

Members may choose to participate either in the Mock Trading Session or UAT environment to fulfill their regulatory testing requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- Mock Trading Date: August 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No operational or financial impact as trades are purely for testing and familiarization purposes.
