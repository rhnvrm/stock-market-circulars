## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of select securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM), effective August 10, 2026. These securities will attract various surveillance actions such as 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) depending on their assigned GSM stage.

## Key Points

- Effective date for movement to higher GSM stages: August 10, 2026.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower and 50% ASD to be deposited by buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday), and 100% ASD.
- Settlement on these securities will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities listed in the Annexure are moved to higher GSM stages in accordance with the established GSM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities due to trade-to-trade settlement and prohibition of netting off.
- Market participants must ensure required margins and ASD are duly deposited.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 07 Aug 2026
- Effective Date: 10 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading volumes for securities placed under higher GSM stages (especially Stages II, III, and IV) will be severely restricted due to trade-for-trade mechanisms, weekly trading windows, and heavy ASD requirements.
