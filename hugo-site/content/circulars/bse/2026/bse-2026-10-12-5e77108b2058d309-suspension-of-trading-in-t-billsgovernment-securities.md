## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific Treasury Bills (T-bills) and Government Securities effective October 13, 2026, due to their upcoming maturity.

## Key Points

- Suspension applies to four specific T-bills and Government Securities maturing on October 15, 2026.
- Affected scrip codes include 805203 (182T151026), 805132 (364T151026), 805241 (91TB151026), and 800650 (GS15OCT26C).
- Trading members are advised not to deal in these securities from October 13, 2026 onwards.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes; this is a standard operational notification regarding security maturity and cessation of trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that no transactions are executed in the specified T-bills and Government Securities starting from October 13, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading Suspension: October 13, 2026
- Redemption Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact, representing routine operational closure of maturing sovereign debt instruments.
