## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of 1,31,53,77,202 equity shares of Manipal Health Enterprises Limited in the 'B' Group of Securities, effective Wednesday, August 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544847
- ISIN No: INE459N01021
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: MANIPALHOS
- Issue Price: Rs. 590/- per share (Face Value Rs. 2/-, Premium Rs. 588/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 3, 2026
- Market Lot: 1 share

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members note that the scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on August 5, 2026.
- Corporate action for lock-in marking has been executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 3, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Manipal Health Enterprises Limited shares on the BSE exchange platform, expanding investment options in the healthcare sector.
