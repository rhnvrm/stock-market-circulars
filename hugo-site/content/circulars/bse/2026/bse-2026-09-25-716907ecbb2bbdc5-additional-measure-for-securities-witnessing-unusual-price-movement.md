## Summary

BSE has carried out a review for the identification of securities for placing in and moving out of the Additional Measure framework for securities witnessing unusual price movement, pursuant to exchange circular no. 20251107-56 dated November 07, 2025.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the specified criteria are included under the additional measure w.e.f. September 28, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities moving out of the framework w.e.f. September 28, 2026, are specified in Annexure II.
- A consolidated list of all securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Market participants can direct clarifications to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic update and enforcement of surveillance measures for securities exhibiting unusual price movements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the inclusion and exclusion of securities under the additional surveillance measure and ensure compliance accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading norms and surveillance margins/restrictions for the specific securities included or removed from the framework.
