## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Indo Thai Securities Limited (Scrip Code - 533676) effective from August 20, 2026, following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Indo Thai Securities Limited
- Scrip Code: 533676
- ISIN: INE337M01021
- Securities Issued: 10,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 49.00 to Non-Promoters on a preferential basis.
- Effective Date of Trading: August 20, 2026.
- Lock-in Details: 10,00,000 shares under lock-in up to August 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in periods and trading permissions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 08/06/2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: 20/08/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 27/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase via warrant conversion with minimal immediate market disruption.
