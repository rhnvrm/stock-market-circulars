## Summary

SI Creva Capital Services Private Limited has announced part redemption of its debentures, resulting in a reduced face value effective from September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip code 978136 (INE996U07404): Reduced face value of Rs. 33,333.32 per debenture.
- Scrip code 978137 (INE996U07529): Reduced face value of Rs. 66,666.66 per debenture.
- Effective date for trading with reduced face value is September 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to trade the debentures with the aforementioned reduced face value.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2026
- Notice Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors need to update their systems with the new reduced face values for the respective debentures.
