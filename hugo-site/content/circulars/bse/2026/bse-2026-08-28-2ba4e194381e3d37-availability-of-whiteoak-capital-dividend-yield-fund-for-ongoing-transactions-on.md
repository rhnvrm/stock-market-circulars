## Summary

BSE has announced that the Whiteoak Capital Dividend Yield Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic facilities (SIP, STP, and SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting August 31, 2026, following Notice No. 20260807-41.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 31, 2026.
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Covered ISINs include various regular and direct plans (Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout) of Whiteoak Capital Dividend Yield Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate transactions in the specified schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and market participants to transact in the Whiteoak Capital Dividend Yield Fund schemes seamlessly through the BSE StAR MF infrastructure.
