## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 23 companies under ESOP / ESOS have been listed and admitted for trading on the exchange with effect from Tuesday, September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 23.
- Purpose: Listing of further securities issued under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS).
- Effective Date for Trading: September 22, 2026.
- Included companies span banking, financial services, technology, healthcare, and manufacturing sectors, including Axis Bank, ICICI Bank, IDFC First Bank, and PB Fintech.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the listed securities and corresponding lock-in details where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity capital base of the respective listed entities.
