## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that the group code for several Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) has been modified, with the changes effective from Wednesday, August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Group code changes affect multiple ETFs across various fund houses including Motilal Oswal, Kotak, SBI, Nippon India, UTI, LIC, HDFC, Aditya Birla Sun Life, Bandhan, ICICI Prudential, and Mirae Asset.
- The changes are implemented by the DCS-Listing department under Notice No. 20260811-20.
- Effective date for the group code changes is August 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Administrative reclassification of ETF group codes on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the group code modifications for their operational and trading records.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact on trading operations; serves as an administrative notice regarding exchange group code assignments for specific ETFs.
