## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to dealings of new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under the G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS list effective from Friday, August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three new Treasury Bills are being listed: 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day T-Bills.
- Effective date of trading: August 28, 2026.
- Securities will not be available for trading two working days prior to their respective maturity dates.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the trading restrictions two working days prior to maturity/redemption dates.

## Important Dates

- **Listing Date:** August 28, 2026
- **Maturity Dates:** 
  - 91TB271126: November 27, 2026
  - 182TB26227: February 26, 2027
  - 364TB27827: August 27, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt market issuance providing participants additional short-term sovereign investment instruments.
