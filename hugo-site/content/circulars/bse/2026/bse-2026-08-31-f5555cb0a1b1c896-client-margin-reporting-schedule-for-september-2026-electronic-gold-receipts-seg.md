## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the trade dates, last dates of margin reporting, and corresponding penalty dates for the Electronic Gold Receipts segment for the month of September 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines the complete schedule of margin reporting obligations for September 2026.
- Specifies trade dates ranging from September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Provides clear timelines for last date of margin reporting and penalty dates to ensure compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational schedule notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members and trading members must adhere to the specified margin reporting deadlines for the Electronic Gold Receipts segment to avoid applicable penalties.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Start Date:** September 1, 2026
- **Effective End Date:** October 9, 2026 (final penalty date for trade date September 30, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Operational guidance for members trading in Electronic Gold Receipts to manage collateral and margin reporting schedules efficiently.
