## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 8,04,10,000 new equity shares of Mehai Technology Limited (Scrip Code - 540730) issued on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants are listed and permitted to trade effective from September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Mehai Technology Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 540730
- **ISIN:** INE062Y01020
- **Securities Issued:** 8,04,10,000 equity shares of face value Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 2.50/- (Issue Price: Rs. 3.50/-)
- **Allottees:** Promoter and Non-Promoter on a preferential basis
- **Effective Trading Date:** September 28, 2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 748620001 to 829030000
- **Lock-in Details:**
  - 7,00,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 748620001 - 818620000) locked-in up to 30/03/2028
  - 96,90,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 818620001 - 828310000) locked-in up to 30/03/2027
  - 7,20,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 828310001 - 829030000) locked-in up to 31/03/2027

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing procedure for preferential issue of shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to lock-in periods and regulatory guidelines concerning preferential shares and warrant conversions.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** April 27, 2026
- **Listing & Trading Effective Date:** September 28, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** March 30, 2027; March 31, 2027; March 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up equity capital of the company with specific lock-in restrictions on promoter and non-promoter allotments.
