## Summary

BSE has issued a notice outlining updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities meeting inclusion criteria, moving to lower stages, or exiting the framework effective September 30 and October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 01, 2026, on all open positions as on September 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 30, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 01, 2026.
- Consolidated lists of securities under the framework are provided in Annexure III, with exiting securities detailed in Annexure II.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of surveillance controls including mandatory 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement migration, and restricted price bands for shortlisted scrips under the ESM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure adherence to 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities and update trading systems for trade-for-trade settlement changes.

## Important Dates

- September 30, 2026: Effective date for Stage II restrictions, movement to lower stages, and exiting securities.
- October 01, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin requirement and shift to Trade-for-Trade settlement segment.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity, margin obligations, and trading mechanics for affected securities will be tightened in accordance with market surveillance requirements.
