## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the movement of specified securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the Annexure move to higher stages of GSM (Stages I, II, III, or IV).
- Applicable surveillance measures include 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement, Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) up to 100% of trade value, and restricted trading frequency.
- Settlement for these securities will be executed on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance actions in accordance with the provisions of the GSM Framework published on February 23, 2017.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in the affected securities.
- Compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules and deposit requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin and deposit requirements along with trade-for-trade restrictions impact liquidity and trading operations for the specified securities.
