## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by various companies effective July 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are listed and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective July 27, 2026.
- Trading takes place in dematerialised form in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Companies listed include Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited, A.K. Capital Finance Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, and Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard dematerialised trading rules and denomination requirements for debt instruments.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 27, 2026
- Effective Date: July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update facilitating secondary market trading for newly issued commercial papers.
