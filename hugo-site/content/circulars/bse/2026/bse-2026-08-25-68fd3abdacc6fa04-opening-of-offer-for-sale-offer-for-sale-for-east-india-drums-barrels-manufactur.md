## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the Offer for Sale (OFS) of East India Drums & Barrels Manufacturing Limited by its promoter and seller, Madhav Jayesh Valia. The seller has accepted 22,55,833 equity shares from bids received by Non-Retail Investors on August 24, 2026 (T Day). In compliance with SEBI guidelines, the unsubscribed portion of the non-retail segment is carried forward to T+1 day (August 26, 2026) for bidding in the retail segment.

## Key Points

- Promoter/Seller: Madhav Jayesh Valia
- Company: East India Drums & Barrels Manufacturing Limited
- Total shares offered: Up to 29,07,200 equity shares (19.68% of paid-up equity share capital)
- Non-Retail Investor bids accepted: 22,55,833 equity shares on August 24, 2026 (T Day)
- Unsubscribed portion carry forward: Allowed to be carried forward to T+1 day for the retail segment

## Regulatory Changes

None. Conducted in compliance with clause 7.5 of the SEBI OFS Circular dated January 10, 2023.

## Compliance Requirements

Non-retail and retail investors participating in the OFS must adhere to the designated bidding windows and settlement terms specified by the BSE.

## Important Dates

- T Day (Non-Retail Investors): August 25, 2026
- T+1 Day (Retail Investors and carry-forward Non-Retail Investors): August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the continuation of the Offer for Sale process for the company's shares, ensuring proper handling of undersubscribed non-retail portions through retail segment participation.
