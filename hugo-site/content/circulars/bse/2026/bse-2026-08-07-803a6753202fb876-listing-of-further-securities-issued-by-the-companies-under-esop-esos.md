## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 14 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS). These securities become effective for trading from Monday, August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** August 10, 2026.
- **Total Companies Involved:** 14 companies, including major entities like ICICI Bank, Infosys, IDFC First Bank, and One 97 Communications (Paytm).
- **Nature of Issue:** Further issuance of equity shares under ESOP / ESOS.
