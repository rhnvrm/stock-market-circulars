## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-682/2026-27. The auction offers will be accepted on trading terminals on July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Auction Offer Entry:** 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on July 30, 2026.
- **Auction Matching:** 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on July 30, 2026.
- **Delivery Statement & Report:** 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m. on July 30, 2026.
- **Money Statement & Final Reports:** 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. on July 30, 2026.
- **Pay-in Schedule:** The Pay-in of the Auction will be held on Friday, July 31, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Extreme care must be taken while punching securities code and rate; no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members should refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the "Auction offer Entry Screen" and monitor corporate action notices (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- **Auction Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Pay-in Date:** July 31, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth clearing and settlement of short-delivered securities through the Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd (ICCL) auction mechanism.
