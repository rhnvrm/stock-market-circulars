## Summary

BSE has issued a notice clarifying the implementation of "@valid" UPI IDs for investor-facing bank accounts maintained by Research Analysts, following SEBI communication dated August 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Research Analysts must ensure all investor-facing bank accounts receiving funds from individual/non-institutional investors are linked with a "@valid" UPI ID.
- UPI IDs must be verifiable through SEBI Check.
- Applications for a "@valid" UPI handle must be submitted via the BSE portal (BSE portal -> UPI Request Handle).
- Upon generating the UTN, Research Analysts must approach their respective banks for documentation and process completion.
- New investor-facing bank accounts opened after the issuance of the circular must also be mandatorily linked to a "@valid" UPI ID.

## Regulatory Changes

Adoption of Standardised, Validated, and Exclusive UPI IDs for payment collection by SEBI Registered Intermediaries from investors, extended to Research Analysts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Apply for a "@valid" UPI handle on the BSE portal.
- Complete necessary bank documentation and obtain the UPI handle.
- Comply with the requirements within T+60 days where T is August 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **T (Reference Date):** August 07, 2026
- **Compliance Deadline:** October 06, 2026 (T+60 days)

## Impact Assessment

Research Analysts must update their banking operations and payment collection mechanisms to ensure full compliance and avoid regulatory penalties.
