## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in commercial papers for several companies following the fixation of record dates for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies ahead of their redemption record dates.
- Affected entities include The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd., Muthoot Fincorp Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, IGH Holdings Private Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, and Bajaj Finance Limited.
- Record dates and ex-dates span across August 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from executing trades in the specified commercial papers on or after the respective ex-dates/record dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates: Between 18/08/2026 and 31/08/2026 depending on the specific commercial paper.
- Ex-Dates: Matching the respective record dates in August 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for debt segment commercial paper redemption, with no broader market impact.
