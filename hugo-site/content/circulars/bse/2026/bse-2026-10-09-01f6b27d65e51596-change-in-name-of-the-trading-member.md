## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the name of trading member Way2wealth Brokers Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 3117) has been changed to Shriram Financial Services Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Old Trading Member Name: Way2wealth Brokers Pvt. Ltd.
- New Trading Member Name: Shriram Financial Services Private Limited
- Member Code: 3117
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative update of a trading member's corporate name.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and clients should take note of the name change for future correspondence and records. For clarifications, contact membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 9, 2026
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating records and documentation reflecting the new name of the trading member.
