## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the temporary suspension of all existing investments through Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) in certain schemes of Edelweiss Mutual Fund, effective from the close of business hours on August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** Close of business hours from August 11, 2026.
- **Impacted Schemes:** 
  - EDELWEISS ASEAN EQUITY OFF-SHORE FUND
  - EDELWEISS EUROPE DYNAMIC EQUITY OFF-SHORE FUND
  - EDELWEISS EMERGING MARKETS OPPORTUNITIES EQUITY OFF-SHORE FUND
  - EDELWEISS GREATER CHINA EQUITY OFF-SHORE FUND
  - EDELWEISS US VALUE EQUITY OFFSHORE FUND
  - EDELWEISS US TECHNOLOGY EQUITY FUND OF FUND
  - EDELWEISS MSCI INDIA DOMESTIC & WORLD HEALTHCARE 45 INDEX FUND
- **Permitted Transactions:** Redemption, Switch-out, and STP-out transactions shall continue to be permitted without any amount restrictions.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of temporary suspension on fresh subscriptions and systematic registrations on the BSE StAR MF Platform pursuant to the Notice-cum-Addendum issued by Edelweiss Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and distributors must note that existing Systematic Registrations (SIP/XSIP/STPAMC) will be paused and will not be processed or triggered until further notice.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 11, 2026 (Close of business hours)

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding systematic investment plans in the specified Edelweiss schemes will experience pauses in automated contributions, while exit routes via redemptions and switches remain fully functional.
