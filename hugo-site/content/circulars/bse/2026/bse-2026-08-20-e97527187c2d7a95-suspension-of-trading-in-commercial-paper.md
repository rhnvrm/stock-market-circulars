## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CPs) for multiple companies following the fixation of record dates for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of specified companies as per the fixed record dates.
- Affected companies include Adani Enterprises Ltd., Pilani Investment and Industries Corporation Limited, Fedbank Financial Services Limited, and SG Finserve Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure they do not execute trades in the specified Commercial Papers beyond the respective record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: 24/08/2026, 26/08/2026, and 28/08/2026 depending on the specific ISIN and company.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding these commercial papers will undergo redemption processing based on the set record dates.
