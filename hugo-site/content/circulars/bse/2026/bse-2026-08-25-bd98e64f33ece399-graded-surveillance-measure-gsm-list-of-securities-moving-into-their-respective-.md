## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the list of securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are moved to higher stages of GSM based on predefined surveillance criteria.
- Effective date of implementation: August 26, 2026.
- Applicable surveillance actions vary by stage, ranging from 100% margin rates and 5% price bands to trade-for-trade settlement and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities falling under Stage I attract a 100% margin rate and price bands of 5% or lower.
- Securities under Stage II move to trade-for-trade with a 5% or lower price band and a 50% ASD on trade value.
- Securities under Stage III and IV face trade-for-trade settlement, trading restricted to once a week (every Monday), and 100% ASD.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities.
- Settlement must be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 25 August 2026
- Effective Date: 26 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities may be significantly impacted due to stringent margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
