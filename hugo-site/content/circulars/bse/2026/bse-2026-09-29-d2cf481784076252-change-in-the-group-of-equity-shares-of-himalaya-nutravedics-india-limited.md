## Summary

BSE has announced that the equity shares of Himalaya Nutravedics India Limited (Scrip Code: 544943) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective Wednesday, October 14, 2026. This is in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260928-40 dated September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Himalaya Nutravedics India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544943
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Wednesday, October 14, 2026
- Contact Person: Mr. Anurag Jain (Tel No. 022-2272 8822)

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer of equity shares from Trade for Trade settlement to normal rolling settlement under M Group.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their systems and take note of the segment change for equity shares of Himalaya Nutravedics India Limited.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates normal rolling settlement for investors in Himalaya Nutravedics India Limited, improving trading flexibility and liquidity post-transfer.
