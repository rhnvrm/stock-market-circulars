## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying criteria for inclusion, movement to lower stages, and consolidation lists effective from August/September 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 01, 2026, on open positions as on August 31, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II face Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 31, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 01, 2026.
- Certain securities move to lower Stage I w.e.f. August 31, 2026, while others move out of the ESM framework entirely.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ESM surveillance actions including mandatory 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement shifts, and price band restrictions for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the margin requirements and settlement segment shifts for affected securities and adjust open positions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II and moving out/lower stage: August 31, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade shift: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhanced surveillance actions increase margin requirements and restrict liquidity/trading mechanisms for specified scrips, impacting short-term trading and settlement operations.
