## Summary

BSE has announced the inclusion of four newly listed companies into its benchmark indices effective in October 2026 following their respective IPO listings.

## Key Points

- A-One Steels India Ltd (Ticker: 544952) and Moneyview Ltd (Ticker: 544953) will be added to the BSE IPO index effective October 6, 2026.
- Roopa Screen Ltd (Ticker: 544954) and Peshwa Wheat Ltd (Ticker: 544955) will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective October 5, 2026.
- These corporate actions are processed based on exchange listing notices and included in end-of-day corporate action files (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine index inclusion notice for newly listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and index-tracking funds should update their portfolio weightings and benchmark tracking accordingly.

## Important Dates

- October 1, 2026: Notice issuance and effective listing date for the companies.
- October 5, 2026: Effective date for addition of Roopa Screen Ltd and Peshwa Wheat Ltd to the BSE SME IPO index.
- October 6, 2026: Effective date for addition of A-One Steels India Ltd and Moneyview Ltd to the BSE IPO index.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct operational impact on issuers, but index funds and quantitative portfolios tracking BSE IPO indices will adjust holdings to reflect the new constituents.
