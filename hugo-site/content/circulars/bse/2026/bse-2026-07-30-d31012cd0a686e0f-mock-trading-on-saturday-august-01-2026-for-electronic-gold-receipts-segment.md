## Summary

The Exchange has scheduled a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, August 01, 2026. Trading members can use this session to test their trading applications, risk-management systems, and third-party or in-house software.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR)
- **Date:** Saturday, August 01, 2026
- **Purpose:** Testing of software, risk-reduction mode, trading halt mechanisms, and fulfilling regulatory requirements under SEBI circular dated November 24, 2020.
- **New Version:** New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.30 shall be released.

## Regulatory Changes

- Facilitates compliance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 regarding software testing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members may participate either in the Mock Trading Session or UAT environment to meet regulatory testing obligations.
- Members are requested to provide feedback on the mock session by 4:30 PM on the day of trading.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 30 Jul 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** 01 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trades resulting from the mock session are for testing purposes only and will not attract any margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.
