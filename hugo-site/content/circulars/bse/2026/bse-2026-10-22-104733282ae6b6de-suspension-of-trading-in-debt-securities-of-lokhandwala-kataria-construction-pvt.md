## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities of Lokhandwala Kataria Construction Pvt. Ltd. due to their upcoming maturity on October 26, 2026. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures with effect from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261022-5 (Reference: DR-741/2025-2026)
- Affected Entity: Lokhandwala Kataria Construction Pvt. Ltd.
- Suspension Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Maturity Date: October 26, 2026
- Affected Scrip Codes and ISINs:
  - Scrip 960212 (ISIN: INE999H07033) - LKCPL-7.00%-26-10-26-PVT
  - Scrip 960281 (ISIN: INE999H07058) - LKCPL-7%-26-10-26-PVT
  - Scrip 973921 (ISIN: INE999H07074) - LKCP-7.0%-26-10-26-PVT
  - Scrip 974380 (ISIN: INE999H07082) - LKCPL-7%-26-10-26-PVT

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational procedure for debt security maturity and trading suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debentures of Lokhandwala Kataria Construction Pvt. Ltd. effective from October 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 22, 2026
- Trading Suspension Date: October 23, 2026
- Maturity Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Limited to holders and trading members dealing in the specific debt securities of Lokhandwala Kataria Construction Pvt. Ltd. as the securities reach maturity.
