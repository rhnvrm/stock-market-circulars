## Summary

Trading members are informed that the units of Invesco India BSE Sensex ETF, an open-ended scheme replicating/tracking the BSE Sensex TRI, will be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the B Group effective August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of Mutual Fund: Invesco Mutual Fund
- Scrip Code: 544878
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: IVZSENSEX
- ISIN: INF205KA1CA1
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00 per unit
- Issue Price: Rs. 78.00 per unit
- Date of Allotment: 14/08/2026
- Number of Units: 1,015,290 Units
- Market Lot: 1 unit

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Units of the Mutual Fund will only be traded in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard addition of a new exchange-traded fund to the B group listing, providing investors with an additional investment option tracking the BSE Sensex TRI.
