## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities of InCred Financial Services Limited due to the maturity of the securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: InCred Financial Services Limited
- Scrip Code: 975938
- ISIN: INE321N07426
- Particulars: IFSL-9.5%-18-8-26-PVT
- Effective Date of Suspension: August 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debentures with effect from August 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Maturity/Effective Suspension Date: August 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified debt securities is halted due to maturity, with no broader market impact.
