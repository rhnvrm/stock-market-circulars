## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that JOJO Ltd has fixed August 7, 2026, as the Record Date for the subdivision of its equity shares. The existing equity shares with a face value of Rs. 10/- each are being subdivided into two equity shares with a face value of Rs. 5/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: JOJO Ltd (Scrip Code: 531910)
- Purpose: Subdivision of existing equity shares from Rs. 10/- each into two equity shares of Rs. 5/- each.
- Record Date: 07/08/2026
- Effective Date: 07/08/2026
- Old ISIN INE312M01016 (Rs. 10/- paid-up) will cease to be valid for transactions on or after 07/08/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition to new ISIN structure for the subdivided Rs. 5/- paid-up shares to be notified separately.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the new ISIN validity and update their systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 7, 2026
- Effective Date: August 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of JOJO Ltd will receive two shares of Rs. 5/- face value for every single share of Rs. 10/- face value held as of the record date.
