## Summary

BSE has notified the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for specified securities. Effective September 30, 2026, enhanced margin requirements apply to all open positions as of September 29, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Key Points

- Securities placed under ST-ASM Stage I attract a margin rate of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Securities placed under ST-ASM Stage II attract a margin rate of 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Surveillance action takes effect from September 30, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is a market surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must maintain the applicable increased margins (50% or 100%) on open and new positions for securities shortlisted in Annexure I starting September 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 Sep 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: 29 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: 30 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Higher margin obligations for shortlisted securities may affect trading liquidity and short-term price volatility.
