## Summary

Trading in the equity shares of GSPL Transmission Limited (BSE Scrip Code: 544801) shall be permitted under the 'B' group effective Thursday, October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: GSPL Transmission Limited
- BSE Scrip Code: 544801
- New Trading Group: 'B' group
- Notice Reference: 20260930-16

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of the trading group for equity shares on the BSE platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must update their trading systems and records regarding the group change for GSPL Transmission Limited.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact on overall market operations; operational alignment required for participants trading in the scrip.
