## Summary

Notice regarding the reopening of Whiteoak Capital - Aggressive Hybrid Fund for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective July 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes reopening for ongoing transactions: Whiteoak Capital - Aggressive Hybrid Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF03VN01AG6) and Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF03VN01AF8).
- Reopening date: July 01, 2026.
- Specifies STP investment amount ranges and minimum installment requirements for daily/weekly frequencies to avoid rejections from AMC/RTA.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure correct minimum installment numbers are selected for STP registrations on the BSE StAR MF platform within specified investment amount ranges to prevent rejections.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: June 30, 2026
- Effective Date: July 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity for trading members regarding transactions and STP registrations for Whiteoak Capital - Aggressive Hybrid Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform.
