## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in four SBI Mutual Fund Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) Series 52 schemes due to their maturity. Trading members are advised not to deal in these schemes effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading applies to four SBI Mutual Fund Series 52 FMP schemes (Regular and Direct plans for Growth and IDCW payout).
- Affected Scrip Codes: 543368, 543369, 543370, 543371.
- Affected ISINs: INF200KA12A3, INF200KA13A1, INF200KA14A9, INF200KA15A6.
- Effective date of trading suspension: October 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[N/A - Standard corporate action/operational notice regarding scheme maturity]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified SBI Mutual Fund schemes with effect from October 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Secondary market trading in these specific FMP schemes is halted upon maturity, with final redemption handled directly through the mutual fund redemption process.
