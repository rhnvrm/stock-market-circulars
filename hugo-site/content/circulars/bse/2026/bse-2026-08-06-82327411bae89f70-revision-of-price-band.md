## Summary

BSE Ltd has announced a revision of price bands for various scrips as part of its periodic surveillance action review. The updated price bands will become effective from August 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised for multiple scrips following a surveillance review.
- Scrips currently in Trade for Trade segments will continue to attract a 5% price band.
- Detailed lists of affected scrips and their respective revised price bands are provided in the attached Annexure.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of operational price limits on specific listed securities to manage market volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance in order execution from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and traders transacting in the affected scrips must adjust order placements to align with the new price limits, mitigating potential order rejections or execution limits.
