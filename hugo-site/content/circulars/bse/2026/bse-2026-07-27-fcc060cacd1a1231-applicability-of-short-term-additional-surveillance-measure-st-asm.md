## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) to specific securities under Stage I and Stage II, effective July 29, 2026. This framework imposes higher margin requirements on affected securities to safeguard market integrity.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective July 29, 2026, on all open positions as on July 28, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective July 29, 2026, on all open positions as on July 28, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of heightened surveillance measures via ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% to 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities starting July 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 27 Jul 2026
- Effective Date: 29 Jul 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: 28 Jul 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities under ST-ASM Stage I and II will impact liquidity and capital allocation for traders holding positions in the affected scrips.
