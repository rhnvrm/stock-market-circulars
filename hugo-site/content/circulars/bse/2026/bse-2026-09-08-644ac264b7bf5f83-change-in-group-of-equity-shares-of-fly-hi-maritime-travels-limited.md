## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Limited shall be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment effective Wednesday, September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Limited
- Scrip Code: 544904
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Wednesday, September 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Shift of trading segment category for the specified company's equity shares following previous exchange communications.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are to note the segment change for execution and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 08, 2026
- Effective Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the company's shares will transition from the Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment, modifying settlement mechanics for market participants.
