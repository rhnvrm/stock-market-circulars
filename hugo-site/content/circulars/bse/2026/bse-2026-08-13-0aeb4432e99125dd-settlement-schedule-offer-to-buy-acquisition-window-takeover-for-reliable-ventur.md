## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Reliable Ventures India Limited. The bidding is scheduled from August 14, 2026, to August 28, 2026, under Settlement No. 2026101.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Reliable Ventures India Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement No.:** 2026101
- **Bid Start Date:** 14/08/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 28/08/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 10/09/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 11/09/2026
- **Demat Delivery:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026101.
- **Stamp Duty:** Members should refer to the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable; routine settlement schedule issuance under existing takeover guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must ensure early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/ICCL systems prior to bidding and confirmation window closures.
- CDSL & NSDL early pay-in securities will be earmarked/blocked in clients' accounts for the specified market type and settlement number.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Period:** August 14, 2026 – August 28, 2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** September 10, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly execution and settlement of the takeover acquisition window for Reliable Ventures India Limited, ensuring market participants adhere strictly to early pay-in and depository protocols.
