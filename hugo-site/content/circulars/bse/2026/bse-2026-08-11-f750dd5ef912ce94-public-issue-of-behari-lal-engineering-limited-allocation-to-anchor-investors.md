## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the allocation of 31,74,946 Equity Shares under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Behari Lal Engineering Limited at Rs. 285/- per equity share, as per the company's announcement dated August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Behari Lal Engineering Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue / Anchor Investor Allocation
- Number of Shares Subscribed: 31,74,946 Equity Shares
- Issue Price / Allocation Price: Rs. 285/- per equity share
- Notice Date: August 11, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are to take note of the anchor investor allocation details for the public issue.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 11, 2026
- Announcement Date: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and official confirmation of anchor investor participation and pricing ahead of the public issue rollout.
