## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, August 25, 2026, the equity shares of Sunshine Pictures Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip ID on BOLT System: SUNSHINE
- Scrip Code: 544881
- ISIN No.: INE1B3O01011
- Number of Securities: 31,148,784 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 360/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 350/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Tuesday, August 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 21, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Sunshine Pictures Limited equity shares on the BSE exchange.
