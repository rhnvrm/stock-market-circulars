## Summary

Effective from Thursday, July 30, 2026, the equity shares of Lohia Corp Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lohia Corp Limited
- Scrip Code: 544839
- ISIN No: INE0QJW01029
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: LCL
- Number of Securities: 105,650,000 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 425/- per share (Face Value Rs. 1/- and Premium Rs. 424/-)
- Date of Allotment: July 28, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- The scrip will be a part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, July 30, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed for locking-in shares in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: July 30, 2026
- Date of Allotment: July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides public trading liquidity for the equity shares of Lohia Corp Limited on the BSE platform.
