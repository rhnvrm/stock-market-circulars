## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of scrips in the Trade-to-Trade segment as part of surveillance measures based on the extant SEBI framework. Specified securities will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS groups effective August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria I, II, and III will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. August 20, 2026.
- Securities in Annexure II will continue in T / XT / MT / TS / P / Z / ZP Group with a 5% price band.
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off positions allowed.
- A VAR Margin of 100% will be levied on these scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer of various securities for settlement on a Trade-to-Trade basis in consultation with SEBI as a preventive surveillance measure.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities, keeping in mind the trade-to-trade settlement and 100% VAR margin requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in affected securities will require upfront delivery/pay-in without position netting, impacting liquidity and trading behavior for the listed scrips.
