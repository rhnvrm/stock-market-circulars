## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities that have satisfied the specified criteria. The applicable surveillance actions, including revised margin rates, take effect from September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on September 18, 2026 and new positions created from September 21, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) frameworks as per exchange guidelines for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the revised margin requirements (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for open and new positions starting September 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 17 Sep 2026
- Effective Date for Open Positions (as of 18 Sep 2026) and New Positions: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact trading liquidity and open position management for market participants.
