## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 17, 2026. Trading members utilizing third-party trading platforms or in-house developed systems through ETI APIs can test their applications and functionalities under exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 17, 2026, for the Equity Derivatives segment.
- No new BOLT TWS Release.
- Trades resulting from the session will not attract margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out obligations, and will not create rights and liabilities.
- Members are requested to provide feedback to BSE via contact numbers or email by 02:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to actively participate in the mock trading session to test trading applications, risk-reduction modes, and trading halts.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 17, 2026
- Log-in: 09:15 AM to 10:00 AM
- Pre-Open Session Order Entry: 10:00 AM to 10:10 AM
- Continuous Trading: 10:15 AM to 01:10 PM
- Trade Modification: up to 01:20 PM

## Impact Assessment

Purely operational and optional testing activity with zero financial impact or regulatory change.
