## Summary

Trading members are informed that effective from Tuesday, August 11, 2026, the equity shares of Burnpur Cement Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the T Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 11, 2026
- Company Name: Burnpur Cement Limited
- Scrip Code: 532931
- ISIN No.: INE817H01022
- Segment: T Group (Trade-for-Trade for 10 trading days)
- Capital Reduction: Approved by NCLT Kolkata on October 30, 2024, reducing paid-up value and consolidating shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of capital reduction scheme reducing issued and paid-up share capital from Rs. 86,12,43,630/- (8,61,24,363 equity shares of Rs. 10/-) to Rs. 17,22,48,730/- (1,72,24,873 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should take due care and abundant caution while dealing, receiving, or delivering these shares as temporary ISIN procedures apply.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 07, 2026
- Effective Date: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members must observe trade-for-trade restrictions for the initial 10 trading sessions following listing.
