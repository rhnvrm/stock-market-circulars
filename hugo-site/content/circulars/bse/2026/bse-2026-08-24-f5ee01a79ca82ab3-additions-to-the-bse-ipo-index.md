## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited announced the addition of two newly listed companies, Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited and Horizon Industrial Parks Limited, to the BSE IPO Index, effective at the open of Wednesday, August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544879) and Horizon Industrial Parks Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544880) are being added to the BSE IPO Index.
- The inclusion is effective from the market open on August 26, 2026.
- These actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Index Addition: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine index adjustment with minimal direct market impact, affecting passive funds tracking the BSE IPO Index.
