## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, August 29, 2026. The session is structured to facilitate testing of changes related to modifications in the pre-open call auction session as well as standard trading functionalities in preparation for live trading starting Monday, August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mock Trading Session 1:** Facilitates testing of changes related to Modifications in Pre-open Call Auction Session.
- **Mock Trading Session 2:** Tests trading functionalities without pre-open modifications to facilitate live trading from Monday, August 31, 2026.
- **BOLT Pro TWS Versions:** Version 13.50 released for testing during Session 1 (via lease line DLOAD only); Version 13.35 required for Session 2 and subsequent live trading.
- **Market Data:** NFCAST stream format version 6.2 available during Session 1, while existing format version 5.0 applies to Session 2 and live trading.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of modifications in the Pre-open Call Auction Session for the Equity Derivatives segment.
- Sequential updating of stock future price bands for alignment with CAS in the Equity Segment during mock sessions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that BOLT Pro TWS version 13.35 is utilized for live trading effective August 31, 2026.
- Members are required to participate and test system connectivity and order entry workflows according to the designated schedule.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** August 29, 2026
- **Live Trading Effective Date:** August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must update their trading workstations (BOLT Pro TWS) and verify data stream configurations to ensure seamless operational continuity and readiness for the upcoming live trading changes.
