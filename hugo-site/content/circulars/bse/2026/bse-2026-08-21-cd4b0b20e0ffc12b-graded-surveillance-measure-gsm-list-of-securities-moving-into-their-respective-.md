## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities that are moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from August 24, 2026. The framework introduces stringent surveillance actions across different stages to safeguard market integrity.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 24, 2026.
- Framework applies to securities listed in the Annexure of the notice.
- Surveillance actions vary across stages I to IV, including 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement, Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD), and restricted trading frequencies.
- No netting off is allowed as settlements are conducted on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities moving into GSM stages become subject to heightened monitoring and specific restrictions under the GSM framework established by the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the specified securities, ensuring compliance with the trade-to-trade settlement and deposit mandates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Actions: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions may reduce liquidity and increase trading friction for securities entering higher GSM stages.
