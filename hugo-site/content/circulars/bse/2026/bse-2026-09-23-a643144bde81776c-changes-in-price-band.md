## Summary

Stock exchanges periodically revise price bands for scrips as part of surveillance measures to curb excessive volatility. BSE has announced changes to the price bands for 20 listed scrips, effective from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands for 20 scrips have been revised from their existing levels.
- The revision becomes effective on September 24, 2026.
- Revised price limits range between 2%, 5%, 10%, and 20% depending on the scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands for specific equities have been updated under surveillance guidelines to manage market volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure orders placed in the mentioned scrips comply with the new limits to avoid order rejections or compliance issues.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 23, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and traders dealing in the affected scrips must adjust their trading strategies and risk parameters to accommodate the new price band limits.
