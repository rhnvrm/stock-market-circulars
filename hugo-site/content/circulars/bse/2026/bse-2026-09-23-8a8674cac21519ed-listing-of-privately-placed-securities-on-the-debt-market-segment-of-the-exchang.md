## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of privately placed securities (commercial papers) on the debt market segment effective September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are issued on a private placement basis and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective September 23, 2026.
- Includes issuances from entities such as Tata Power Co. Ltd, Mirae Asset Financial Services (India) Private Limited, Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, and Bajaj Financial Services Limited.
- Trading takes place in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs in dematerialised form with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades comply with standard dematerialised settlement and denomination requirements.

## Important Dates

- Allotment / Effective Date: September 23, 2026 (and September 22, 2026 for specific tranches)
- Redemption / Maturity Dates: Ranging from December 8, 2026 to December 22, 2026 across different issuances.

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official market admission for the listed commercial papers on the debt segment.
