## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Shanti Gold International Limited issued on a rights basis, effective from August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544459
- Company Name: SHANTI GOLD INTERNATIONAL LIMITED
- Security Details: 46,43,471 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 205/- on a rights basis.
- ISIN: INE06ZD01017
- Distinctive Numbers: 72096001 to 76739471
- Date of Allotment: 24/08/2026
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the security details and ISIN for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 27, 2026

Expands the equity capital base of Shanti Gold International Limited by listing rights shares.
