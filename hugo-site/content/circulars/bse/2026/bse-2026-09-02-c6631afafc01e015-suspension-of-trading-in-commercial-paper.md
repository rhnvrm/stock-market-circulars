## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers for various companies that have fixed record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies as per the provided schedule.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260902-7, dated September 2, 2026.
- Segment: Debt (Listing Operations).
- Subject: Suspension Of Trading in Commercial Paper.
- Affected Issuers include Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, Northern Arc Capital Ltd., The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd., Nuvama Wealth Management Limited, Capri Global Capital Limited, Grasim Industries Ltd, Bajaj Finance Limited, Godrej Finance Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the mentioned companies starting from the respective ex-dates / record dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates and Ex-Dates range from September 3, 2026, to September 10, 2026, depending on the specific commercial paper.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified commercial papers is restricted around the record dates to ensure smooth redemption processing.
