## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding all active Research Analysts (RAs) to submit their half-yearly Software as a Service (SaaS) Compliance Report for the audit period ended September 30, 2026. The submission must be completed through the SEBI IA/RA Portal on or before October 31, 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines on SaaS solutions.

## Key Points

- Applies to all active Research Analysts (RAs).
- Covers the audit period ended September 30, 2026.
- Submissions must be made via the BSE IA/RA Portal.
- Deadline for submission is October 31, 2026.
- Negative/Nil declarations required if no SaaS solutions are utilized.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiterates compliance standards outlined in SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD2/DOR/CIR/P/2020/221 dated November 03, 2020, regarding Financial Sector Organizations using SaaS-based solutions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Active Research Analysts must disclose complete and accurate details of all SaaS-based solutions and hosted services utilized.
- If no SaaS solutions are used, an appropriate declaration must still be submitted through the portal.
- Submissions must be made through https://iaraportal.sebi.gov.in under RAIA Inspection → SaaS Submission Form.

## Important Dates

- Audit Period Ended: September 30, 2026
- Submission Deadline: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures regulatory oversight and data security compliance across SaaS solutions utilized by Research Analysts. Timely filing prevents non-compliance penalties or regulatory scrutiny from BSE/SEBI.
