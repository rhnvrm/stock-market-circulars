## Summary

Zerodha Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for ZERODHA NIFTY NEXT 100 ETF on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from September 21, 2026, to October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ZERODHA NIFTY NEXT 100 ETF
- Scheme Category: EQUITY
- NFO Period: September 21, 2026, to October 05, 2026 (extended till midnight for lumpsum purchases)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 08, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 08, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted during the NFO period subject to timely receipt of clear funds.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 21, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 05, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for trading members participating in mutual fund NFO transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.
