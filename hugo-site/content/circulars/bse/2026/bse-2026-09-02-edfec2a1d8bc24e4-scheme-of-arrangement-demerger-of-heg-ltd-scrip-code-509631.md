## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the record date fixed by HEG Ltd. pursuant to the Composite Scheme of Arrangement (for Demerger) approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, Indore Bench. The scheme entails the demerger of the entire graphite business of HEG Ltd. into HEG Graphite Limited and the issuance of shares to eligible shareholders.

## Key Points

- **Demerged Company:** HEG Ltd. (Scrip Code: 509631)
- **Resulting Company:** HEG Graphite Limited
- **Transferor Company:** Bhilwara Energy Limited
- **Record Date:** September 7, 2026
- **Share Entitlement:** 1 (One) fully paid-up equity share of Rs. 2/- each of HEG Graphite Limited for every 1 (One) equity share of Rs. 2/- each held in HEG Ltd.
- **Trading Group Transfer:** Trading in equity shares of HEG Ltd. will be transferred from 'A' Group to 'T' Group w.e.f. September 7, 2026 pursuant to SEBI Circular No. SEBI/Cir/ISD/1/2010.
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** The scrip shall be a part of Call Auction in Special Pre-open Session on September 7, 2026 pursuant to SEBI Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/01/2012.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in shares of HEG Ltd. shifted to 'T' Group (Trade-for-Trade segment) and subject to Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) call auction on the record/effective date.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the record date, share entitlement ratio, and the transfer of the scrip to the 'T' Group along with its participation in the Special Pre-open Session.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 2, 2026
- **Record Date:** September 7, 2026
- **Ex-Entitlement Date:** September 7, 2026 (DR- 710/2026-2027)

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders of HEG Ltd. as of the record date are entitled to shares in HEG Graphite Limited in the specified swap ratio. Trading restrictions and surveillance measures ('T' Group movement and SPOS) apply to HEG Ltd. starting on the record date.
