## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Anand Seamless Limited on the SME segment effective from Tuesday, September 29, 2026. A total of 11,969,458 equity shares are listed under the 'MT' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544944
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: ANAND
- ISIN: INE0XDX01018
- Market Lot: 1600 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 72/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 62/-)
- Segment: Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): Applicable on September 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Shares will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in the minimum market lot of 1,600 shares.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed for lock-in marking in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 25, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and liquidity to shareholders of Anand Seamless Limited via the BSE SME platform, subject to initial trade-for-trade restrictions.
