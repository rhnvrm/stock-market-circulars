## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) on specified securities. The measures include 100% margins, movement to higher stages, lower price bands, and transfers to T/XT/MT/TS groups, effective September 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. September 07, 2026 on all open positions as on September 04, 2026 and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM or subject to Lower Price Band effective September 03, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue in LT-ASM but get transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. September 07, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of LT-ASM Framework effective September 03, 2026, is provided in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of surveillance measures under the revised LT-ASM framework based on XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and note the price band and group transfer changes for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date (Price Bands / Moving out / Higher Stages):** September 03, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** September 04, 2026
- **Effective Date (100% Margins / Group Transfers):** September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trading restrictions (such as lower price bands and group transfers) for shortlisted securities, potentially impacting liquidity and trading behavior.
