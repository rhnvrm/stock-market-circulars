## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Takeover) acquisition window for Pasupati Fincap Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from October 1, 2026, to October 15, 2026, with the final settlement date set for October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Pasupati Fincap Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement Number:** 2026118
- **Bid Start Date:** 01/10/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 15/10/2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 29/10/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 30/10/2026

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; procedural notification pursuant to revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in CDSL and NSDL depositories using Market Type '(Takeover)' and Settlement No. 2026118.
- Participants must ensure early pay-in securities are earmarked/blocked in clients' accounts prior to BSE OTB bidding and confirmation window closures.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN files.
- Members should refer to the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Opening:** 01/10/2026
- **Bid Closing:** 15/10/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 29/10/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 30/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement and clearing operations for market participants and custodians engaging in the open offer / takeover acquisition window for Pasupati Fincap Limited.
