## Summary

Manoj Jewellers Limited has fixed the record date for the Rights Issue of Equity Shares. Trading members are informed that transactions in the equity shares of the company should be done on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified date.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: Manoj Jewellers Limited (544400)
- Notice Number: 20260818-46
- Record Date: 21/08/2026
- Ex-Right Basis Date: 21/08/2026
- Issue Details: 01 (ONE) Equity Share of Rs. 10/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 10/- per share on Rights Basis for every 01 (ONE) Equity Share held.
- Issue Price Terms: The entire issue price of Rs. 20/- per share is payable at the time of making the application.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the ex-rights date and ensure transactions are executed accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 18/08/2026
- Record Date: 21/08/2026
- Ex-Rights Date: 21/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are eligible to participate in the rights issue. Share prices will adjust on an ex-rights basis from the effective date.
