## Summary

BSE has issued a settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy through the Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Parmax Pharma Limited. The bidding is scheduled to be conducted from July 30, 2026, to August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Parmax Pharma Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement No.:** 2026095
- **Bid Start Date:** 30/07/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 12/08/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 24/08/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 25/08/2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Settlement follows the Revised Guidelines of Mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange as per Notice No. 20200528-32.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Securities mechanism available in both depositories.
- Members and custodians must use Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement number 2026095.
- Early pay-in effects must reflect in BSE/ICCL systems prior to prescribed bid confirmation timelines. No EPN file upload is required.

## Important Dates

- **Bidding Period:** July 30, 2026 – August 12, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** August 24, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures structured execution and settlement of shares tendered during the takeover acquisition window for Parmax Pharma Limited.
