## Summary

Effective from Friday, September 18, 2026, the equity shares of OM GALAXY LIMITED shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Company Name: OM GALAXY LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544922
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: OMG
- ISIN No.: INE2D0Q01027
- Number of Securities: 3,38,78,024 Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 90/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/- and Premium Rs. 85/-)
- Market Lot: 1,600 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 16, 2026
- Market Maker: Aikyam Capital Private Limited
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action has been executed for lock-in marking in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 16, 2026
- Listing and Trading Commencement Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members can trade in the scrip starting September 18, 2026, with a minimum market lot of 1,600 shares, subject to Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) guidelines.
