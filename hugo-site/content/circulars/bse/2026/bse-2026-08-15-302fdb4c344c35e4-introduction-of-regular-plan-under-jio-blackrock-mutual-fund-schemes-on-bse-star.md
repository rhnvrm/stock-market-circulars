## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of Regular Plans for investments routed through distributors under open-ended schemes of Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fourteen (14) open-ended schemes of Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund are activated for fresh subscriptions under the Regular Plan.
- Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) registration facilities are enabled for the specified schemes.
- The IDCW Option (Payout and Reinvestment) is made available exclusively under the Jio BlackRock Liquid Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; operational enablement of mutual fund schemes on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and distributors must ensure compliance with platform guidelines while processing subscriptions, SIPs, and STPs for the newly added schemes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands investment options available to market participants and distributors on the BSE StAR MF platform for Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund schemes.
