## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of three newly listed companies to the BSE SME IPO Index effective at the open of Monday, October 12, 2026. The companies being added are Acme India Industries Limited, Paramount Syntex Limited, and TNA Solutions Limited.

## Key Points

- ACME INDIA INDUSTRIES LIMITED (Ticker: 544981) added to BSE SME IPO Index.
- PARAMOUNT SYNTEX LIMITED (Ticker: 544982) added to BSE SME IPO Index.
- TNA SOLUTIONS LIMITED (Ticker: 544983) added to BSE SME IPO Index.
- Effective Date for index inclusion: October 12, 2026.
- Actions included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE) for October 09, 2026.
