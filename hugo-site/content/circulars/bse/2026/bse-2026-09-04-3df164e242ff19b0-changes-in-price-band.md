## Summary

Stock exchanges periodically review and revise price bands for various scrips to manage volatility and protect investor interests. BSE has announced changes to the price bands for multiple scrips effective September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for multiple scrips across different percentages (e.g., 5%, 10%, 20%).
- Effective date for the revised price bands is September 07, 2026.
- Trading members are required to take note of the revised limits.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of daily operating price bands for specified equity scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their trading systems and ensure compliance with the revised price limits for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 04 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: 07 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Directly affects trading parameters and permissible price movement ranges for the listed equities on the exchange.
