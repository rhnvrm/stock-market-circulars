## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Key Points

- 100% margins shall be applicable w.e.f. September 25, 2026, on all open positions as on September 24, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities are moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework and/or lower price bands w.e.f. September 23, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. September 25, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework with their price bands reinstated.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised LT-ASM framework guidelines including margin requirements, price band restrictions, and group migrations for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and adjust trading parameters according to the assigned surveillance stages and groups.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Bands and Higher Stages / Moving Out: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: September 25, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and collateral requirements for the affected securities will face tightening due to the imposition of 100% margins and lower price bands.
