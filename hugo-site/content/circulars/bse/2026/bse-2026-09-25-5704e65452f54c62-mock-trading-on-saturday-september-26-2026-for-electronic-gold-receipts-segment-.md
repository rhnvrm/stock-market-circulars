## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, September 26, 2026, utilizing both primary (PR) and disaster recovery (DR) sites. Trading members can test third-party or in-house trading applications and functionalities such as risk-reduction mode and trading halts.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, September 26, 2026, for the EGR segment.
- Covers log-in, pre-open, continuous trading, switchover to DR site, and closing sessions.
- No new BOLT TWS Release; tests via third-party empaneled vendors, in-house systems, or ETI APIs.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin, pay-in, pay-out obligations, rights, or liabilities.
- Members requested to provide feedback by 5:30 PM on the day of mock trading.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational mock trading notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members using third-party trading platforms or in-house systems are encouraged to participate actively and submit feedback to the BSE Tech Support team or Helpdesk by 5:30 PM.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 25, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** September 26, 2026
- **Feedback Submission Deadline:** September 26, 2026, by 5:30 PM

## Impact Assessment

Zero financial or market impact, as this is solely a simulated testing environment for trading infrastructure and disaster recovery switchover.
