## Summary

Gujarat Natural Resources Ltd has informed the Exchange that the company has fixed November 5, 2026, as the Record Date for the sub-division of existing equity shares. The face value of equity shares will be sub-divided from Rs. 10/- each into ten equity shares of Re. 1/- each.

## Key Points

- Company: Gujarat Natural Resources Ltd (Scrip Code: 513536)
- Corporate Action: Sub-division (stock split) of equity shares
- Old Face Value: Rs. 10/- each (ISIN: INE207H01018)
- New Face Value: Re. 1/- each (New ISIN to be communicated separately)
- Record Date: November 5, 2026
- Effective Date / W.E.F.: November 5, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable; routine corporate action processing under exchange listing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed that the old ISIN (INE207H01018) of Rs. 10/- paid up will not be valid for transactions done on the Exchange on or after November 5, 2026. The new ISIN number for Re. 1/- paid up will be informed via a separate notice.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: November 5, 2026
- Effective Date / W.E.F.: November 5, 2026
- Old ISIN Validity Expiry: On or after November 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders will receive 10 equity shares of Re. 1/- each for every 1 equity share of Rs. 10/- held as of the record date. Liquidity and share count will adjust accordingly while total equity value remains unaffected.
