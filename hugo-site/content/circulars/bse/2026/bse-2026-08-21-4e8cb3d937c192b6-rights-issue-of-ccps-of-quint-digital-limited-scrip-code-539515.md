## Summary

Quint Digital Limited has announced a record date for the rights issue of Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS) along with detachable warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: Quint Digital Limited (539515)
- Record Date: 25/08/2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: 25/08/2026
- Issue Details: 7 (Seven) 10% Non-Cumulative Non-Participating Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares ("CCPS") along with 7 (Seven) detachable Warrant(s) for every 40 fully paid-up Equity Shares held as on the Record Date.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible equity shareholders are required to take note of the record date and ex-rights date for transactions.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 25/08/2026
- Ex-Rights Date: 25/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts existing shareholders of Quint Digital Limited eligible for the rights entitlement of CCPS and warrants.
