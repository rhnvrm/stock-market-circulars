## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of specified securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective August 03, 2026. Trading members are required to exercise caution and comply with strict surveillance actions, including trade-to-trade settlement and additional deposits where applicable.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 03, 2026.
- Affected securities will be moved to higher stages (Stage I, II, III, or IV) of the GSM framework.
- Surveillance actions include enhanced margin requirements, price band restrictions, trade-for-trade settlement with no netting off, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities moved to Stage I attract a 100% margin rate and a price band of 5% or lower.
- Securities moved to Stage II are placed on trade-for-trade with a 5% or lower price band and an ASD of 50% of trade value.
- Securities moved to Stage III and IV are placed on trade-for-trade with trading permitted only once a week (every Monday) and an ASD of 100% of trade value (with no upward movement for Stage IV).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions when trading in the listed securities.
- Settlement must be executed on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 Jul 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Actions: 03 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will experience restricted liquidity and enhanced operational scrutiny due to trade-for-trade settlement and mandatory deposits for buyers.
