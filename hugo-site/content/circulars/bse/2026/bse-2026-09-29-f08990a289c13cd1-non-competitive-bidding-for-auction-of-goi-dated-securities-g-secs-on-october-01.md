## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of four Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled on October 01, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding is available through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.
- Securities offered:
  - 6.20% GS 2029 (Re-issue, Rs. 9,000 crs)
  - 6.57% GS 2033 (Re-issue, Rs. 12,000 crs)
  - 7.63% GS 2056 (Re-issue, Rs. 9,000 crs)
  - 7.50% GOI SGrB 2056 (Re-issue, Rs. 3,000 crs)
- Bid Collection Start Date: September 29, 2026 (10:00 am onwards)
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): September 30, 2026 (till 5:00 pm)
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): October 01, 2026 (till 8:00 am)
- Auction Date: October 01, 2026
- Settlement Date: October 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a scheduled auction notification under existing RBI and Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module on the iBBS platform and ensure timely pay-in of funds.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Direct Investor Bid End Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Member Bid End Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Auction Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates participation of retail and non-competitive bidders in Government securities auctions through organized exchange infrastructure.
