## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of 77,41,935 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) via Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) by TD Power Systems Ltd., effective from October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TD Power Systems Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 533553
- ISIN: INE419M01035
- Security Details: 77,41,935 Equity shares of Re. 1/- each allotted pursuant to QIP.
- Issue Price: Rs. 775/-
- Distinctive Numbers: 313706861 to 321448795
- Effective Trading Date: October 14, 2026
- Date of Allotment: October 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notice for securities allotted under a QIP.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with position limits and verify distinctive numbers (313706861 to 321448795) for trading settlement.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 12, 2026
- Notice Date: October 13, 2026
- Trading Commencement Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Listing of QIP shares increases the total paid-up equity capital of TD Power Systems Ltd. by 77,41,935 shares, ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares, which may result in equity dilution and increased trading liquidity.
