## Summary

BSE has announced actions against defaulting companies failing to pay Annual Listing Fees (ALF) for the financial year 2025-26 under the Equity Segment pursuant to Regulation 14 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. Securities of non-compliant companies listed in Annexure I face suspension of trading effective September 10, 2026, if dues are not cleared by September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applies to listed companies failing to pay Annual Listing Fees (ALF) for F.Y. 2025-26.
- Trading in securities of defaulting companies (Annexure I) will be suspended effective September 10, 2026.
- During suspension, trading will be allowed on a Trade for Trade basis only on the first trading day of every week.
- Companies clearing their dues on or before September 7, 2026, will avoid suspension.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of Regulation 14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 regarding the payment of Annual Listing Fees.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulting companies must clear their outstanding ALF dues to avoid trading suspension or to seek revocation of suspension.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 11, 2026
- Deadline to pay dues to avoid suspension: September 7, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct trading restrictions and suspension for non-compliant listed entities failing to pay annual listing fees, affecting market liquidity for those specific securities.
