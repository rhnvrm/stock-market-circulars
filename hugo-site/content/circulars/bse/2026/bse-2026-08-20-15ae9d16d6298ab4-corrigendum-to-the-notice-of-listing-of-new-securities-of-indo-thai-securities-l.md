## Summary

BSE has issued a corrigendum regarding Notice No. 20260819-20 dated August 19, 2026, concerning the listing of 10,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 49/- to non-promoters of Indo Thai Securities Limited (Scrip Code: 533676) on a preferential basis. Trading members are informed that the aforesaid notice stands withdrawn.

## Key Points

- Notice No. 20260819-20 dated August 19, 2026, is officially withdrawn.
- The notice pertained to the listing of 10,00,000 preferential equity shares of Indo Thai Securities Limited.
- Scrip Code affected: 533676.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note that the previous listing notice stands cancelled and is no longer valid.

## Important Dates

- **Corrigendum Date:** August 20, 2026
- **Original Notice Date:** August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational clarification for trading members regarding the withdrawal of the preferential shares listing for Indo Thai Securities Limited.
