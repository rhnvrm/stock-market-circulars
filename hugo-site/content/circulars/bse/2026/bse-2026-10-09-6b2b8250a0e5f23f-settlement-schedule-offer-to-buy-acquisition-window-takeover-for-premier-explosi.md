## Summary

BSE has announced the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Takeover) acquisition window of Premier Explosives Limited (Notice No. 20261009-28). The bidding is scheduled from October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Premier Explosives Limited.
- **Market Type & Settlement No.:** Takeover / Settlement No. 2026122.
- **Bid Period:** October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026.
- **Obligation Download:** November 3, 2026.
- **Settlement Date:** November 4, 2026.
- **Early Pay-in:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in CDSL and NSDL.
- **Stamp Duty:** Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; the notice implements standard settlement procedures under the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange (Notice No. 20200528-32).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must use Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026122 in the depository system.
- Early pay-in of securities must be completed and reflected in BSE/BSECL systems before the closure of the bidding and confirmation windows.
- Securities will be earmarked/blocked in client accounts; members/custodians are not required to upload an EPN file.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 12/10/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 26/10/2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 03/11/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 04/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates smooth execution and settlement of the takeover acquisition window for Premier Explosives Limited with clear instructions for market participants and custodians regarding early pay-in and stamp duty compliance.
