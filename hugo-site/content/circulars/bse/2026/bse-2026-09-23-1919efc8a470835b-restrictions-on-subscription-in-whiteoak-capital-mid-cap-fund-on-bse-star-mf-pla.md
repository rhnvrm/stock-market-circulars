## Summary

BSE issued a notice regarding the revision in limits for accepting lumpsum subscriptions in WhiteOak Capital Mid Cap Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh and Additional Purchases through Lumpsum and Switch-in are restricted to a maximum of ₹2,00,000 per primary holder PAN per day.
- SIP and STP purchases/subscriptions continue without any restrictions or limits.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIPs/XSIPs and AMC STPs) remain unaffected and will be processed normally.
- Redemption, Switch-out, and STP-out transactions are permitted without amount restrictions.
- Orders above ₹2,00,000 received and triggered to RTA on or after the closure of business hours on September 23, 2026, will be rejected and refunded.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of a daily lumpsum subscription ceiling of ₹2,00,000 per PAN for WhiteOak Capital Mid Cap Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that lumpsum and switch-in orders for the scheme do not exceed ₹2,00,000 per primary holder PAN per day.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2026
- Cut-off for existing orders: Closure of business hours on September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors wishing to invest larger lumpsum amounts in WhiteOak Capital Mid Cap Fund will need to stagger their investments or utilize systematic investment facilities (SIPs/STPs).
