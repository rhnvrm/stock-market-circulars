## Summary

BSE has announced the acceptance of Electronic Bank Guarantees (e-BG) and Electronic Fixed Deposit Receipts (e-FDR) to facilitate efficient collateral management for settlement and risk management purposes.

## Key Points

- Introduction of Electronic Bank Guarantees (e-BG) for collateral.
- Acceptance of Electronic Fixed Deposit Receipts (e-FDR).
- Streamlines operational workflows and reduces physical paperwork for trading members.

## Regulatory Changes

Permits the submission and acceptance of digital/electronic collateral instruments (e-BG and e-FDR) in place of physical instruments for exchange settlement and risk management.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members opting for electronic collateral submission must follow the prescribed digital procedures and guidelines issued by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 21 October 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate / As applicable per notice guidelines

## Impact Assessment

Improves turnaround times for collateral processing, lowers operational friction, and enhances security and transparency in margin management.
