## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of new privately placed securities (Commercial Papers and Debt Securities) on the debt market segment effective October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- **The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd.**: Commercial Paper of Rs.5,00,000/- each (Scrip Code: 732714, ISIN: INE050A14986, Rate: 8.05% p.a., Maturity: 06/01/2027).
- **Capri Global Capital Limited**: Commercial Paper of Rs.5,00,000/- each (Scrip Code: 732715, ISIN: INE180C14AC7, Rate: 8.30% p.a., Maturity: 23/03/2027).
- **Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited**: Commercial Paper of Rs.5,00,000/- each (Scrip Code: 732716, ISIN: INE824H14VG6, Rate: 7.05% p.a., Maturity: 15/12/2026).
- **SI Creva Capital Services Private Limited**: Privately placed debt instrument (Scrip Code: 978285, ISIN: INE996U07552, Rate: 12.03% p.a., Maturity: 22/05/2028).

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in dematerialised form under the specified ISINs with standard denominations/market lots and tick sizes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date / Allotment Dates**: October 7-8, 2026 (various)
- **Redemption / Maturity Dates**: Ranging from December 2026 to May 2028.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations for debt and commercial paper issuances with no broader market impact.
