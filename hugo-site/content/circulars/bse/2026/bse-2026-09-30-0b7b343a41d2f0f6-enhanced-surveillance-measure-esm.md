## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying criteria for inclusion, movement to lower stages, and exiting the framework effective October 1 and October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 5, 2026, on open positions as of October 1, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities continuing in ESM but moving to lower Stage I effective October 1, 2026, are included in Annexure I.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 1, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 5, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the ESM framework effective October 1, 2026, are listed in Annexure II, with price bands reinstated subject to other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of surveillance framework measures including 100% margin requirements, shift to Trade-for-Trade settlement segment, 2% price bands, and periodic call auctions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised margin obligations, settlement segment changes, and price band restrictions for affected securities across Annexures I, II, and III.

## Important Dates

- **October 1, 2026**: Effective date for stage movements, price band adjustments, periodic call auctions, and removal of securities from ESM.
- **October 5, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment for newly included securities.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in securities under the ESM framework will experience increased margin obligations, stricter settlement terms, and price band constraints.
