## Summary

Trading members are informed that equity shares of Lumino Industries Limited are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange effective from Thursday, September 3, 2026, under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip Name: Lumino Industries Limited
- Scrip ID: LUMINO
- Scrip Code: 544894
- ISIN No.: INE185Q01025
- Total Securities: 304,553,705 Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 82/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/- and premium Rs. 77/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 1, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on listing day.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 1, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: September 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Lumino Industries Limited shares on the BSE platform.
