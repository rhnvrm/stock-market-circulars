## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II on specific securities based on market surveillance criteria. Effective October 22, 2026, enhanced margin requirements apply to all open positions and newly created positions.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I:** Applicable rate of margin is set at 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- **ST-ASM Stage II:** Applicable rate of margin is set at 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- **Applicability:** Effective from October 22, 2026, on open positions as of October 21, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- **Nature of Action:** Shortlisting is strictly a market surveillance measure and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.
- **Price Band Restoration:** Scrips moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated to pre-shortlisted levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) under the ST-ASM framework for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for all open positions and new positions created in the shortlisted securities effective October 22, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Open Positions Baseline:** October 21, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding positions in securities designated under ST-ASM Stage I and II will experience increased margin obligations, enhancing risk management and curbing excessive price volatility.
