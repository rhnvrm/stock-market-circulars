## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants, particularly Investment Advisers, about the Gazette Notification (F. No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/323) dated October 08, 2026, issued by SEBI regarding the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026.

## Key Points

- Refers to SEBI Gazette Notification no. F. No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/323 dated October 08, 2026.
- Concerns the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026.
- Directed towards all Investment Advisers.
- Clarifications can be sought via email at Iara.Advertisement@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

Adoption and notification of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026 as issued by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

All Investment Advisers are required to take note of the amended regulations and comply with the updated norms set forth by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Notification Date: October 08, 2026
- Notice Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory alignment and compliance for Investment Advisers under the updated SEBI Intermediaries framework.
