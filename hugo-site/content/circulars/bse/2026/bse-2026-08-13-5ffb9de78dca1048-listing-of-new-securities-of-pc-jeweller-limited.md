## Summary

Stock exchanges have informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of PC Jeweller Limited (Scrip Code: 534809) with effect from August 14, 2026. A total of 1,06,19,31,680 equity shares of face value Rs. 1/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 4.62/- (total issue price Rs. 5.62/-) to promoter and non-promoter categories on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** PC Jeweller Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 534809
- **ISIN:** INE785M01021
- **Security Details:** 1,06,19,31,680 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 4.62/-.
- **Issue Type:** Preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- **Effective Date:** August 14, 2026.
- **Ranking:** Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 8648603176 to 9710534855
- **Date of Allotment:** 10/04/2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing of securities issued via preferential warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the new securities added to trading and ensure adherence to applicable lock-in restrictions.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** April 10, 2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto Dates:** 
  - 21,60,01,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 8,64,86,03,176 - 8,86,46,04,175) locked-in up to 15/02/2028
  - 84,59,30,680 shares (Dist. Nos. 8,86,46,04,176 - 9,71,05,34,855) locked-in up to 15/02/2027

## Impact Assessment

Increases the total paid-up equity capital of PC Jeweller Limited through the conversion of warrants into equity shares, bringing in capital and creating lock-in constraints for the allotted shares over the specified periods.
