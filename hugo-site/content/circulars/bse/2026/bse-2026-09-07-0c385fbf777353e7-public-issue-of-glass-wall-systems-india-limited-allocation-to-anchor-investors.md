## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the allocation of equity shares to Anchor Investors under the public issue of GLASS WALL SYSTEMS (INDIA) LIMITED.

## Key Points

- Total of 70,53,127 equity shares subscribed under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion.
- Issue price is fixed at Rs. 182/- per equity share.
- Announcement date: September 07, 2026.
