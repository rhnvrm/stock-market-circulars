## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities (6.94% GS 2036) scheduled for October 16, 2026, following the RBI press release.

## Key Points

- Security: 6.94% GS 2036 (Symbol: 694GS36O26, Re-issue, Issue Size: Rs. 34,000 Cr).
- Bidding Platform: NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system (https://ibbs.bseindia.com).
- Bid Collection Start Date: October 13, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability).
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): October 15, 2026, till 5.00 pm.
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): October 16, 2026, till 8.00 am.
- Auction Date: October 16, 2026.
- Settlement Date: October 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; conducted in accordance with RBI press release and Exchange circular no. 20180423-42 dated April 23, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit bids through the NCB-GSec module adhering to the specified timelines and minimum/maximum unit multiples (Min units & multiples: 100, Min Amt: Rs. 10,000).

## Important Dates

- Bid Start: October 13, 2026, 10:00 am
- Direct Investor Bid End: October 15, 2026, 5:00 pm
- Member Bid End: October 16, 2026, 8:00 am
- Auction Date: October 16, 2026
- Settlement Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides retail and institutional investors an avenue to participate in Government securities auctions through non-competitive bidding with minimal market disruption.
