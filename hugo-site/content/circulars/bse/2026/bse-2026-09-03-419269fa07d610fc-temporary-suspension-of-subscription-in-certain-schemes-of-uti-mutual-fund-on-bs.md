## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the temporary suspension of fresh lumpsum investments, switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in) under certain schemes of UTI Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from the close of business hours on September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh lumpsum investments, switch-ins, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in) for specified UTI Mutual Fund schemes have been disabled on the BSE StAR MF platform.
- Applications received after the applicable cut-off time on September 03, 2026, will not be accepted or processed.
- Existing SIP/STP-IN registrations made prior to the effective date will continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA end.
- Redemptions, switch-outs, STP-outs, and SWP transactions remain unaffected and are allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary operational restriction on fresh subscriptions for specific UTI Mutual Fund schemes as requested by UTI AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to note the suspension for transaction execution and ensure that applications received after the cut-off time on September 03, 2026, are not processed.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 04, 2026 (from closer of business September 03, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and members will be unable to make fresh subscriptions or initiate new SIPs/STPs in the affected UTI Mutual Fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform until further notice, though existing systematic plans and redemptions will function normally.
