## Summary

Bank of India Value Fund schemes have reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 22, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20260827-31.

## Key Points

- Schemes reopen for ongoing transactions including SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective date: September 22, 2026.
- Covers multiple plans including Regular and Direct options with Growth and IDCW payouts/reinvestments.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors should take note of the availability of these schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and mutual fund distributors to execute regular transactions, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs for Bank of India Value Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
