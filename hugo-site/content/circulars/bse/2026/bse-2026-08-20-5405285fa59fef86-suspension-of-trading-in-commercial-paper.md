## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) for multiple companies following the fixation of record dates for their redemption. Trading members are advised to refrain from dealing in the specified commercial papers.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects commercial papers of several entities including Vivriti Capital Limited, Godrej Housing Finance Limited, 360 ONE Prime Limited, SBICAP Securities Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited, Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, Tata Power Co. Ltd, and J.K. Cement Ltd.
- Record dates for redemption fall on August 21, 2026, and August 26, 2026.
- Trading members are instructed not to deal in these commercial papers from the respective ex-dates/record dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard exchange operational procedure regarding debt segment commercial paper redemption and trading suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no further trading or dealing occurs in the specified commercial papers from the effective ex-date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates: August 21, 2026 and August 26, 2026
- Ex-Dates: August 21, 2026 and August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Halts secondary market trading in the specified commercial papers prior to their redemption to facilitate smooth processing by the depositories and issuers.
