## Summary

 BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission to dealings of units of UTI Nifty 500 Exchange Traded Fund under UTI Mutual Fund, effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: UTI Nifty 500 Exchange Traded Fund
- Mutual Fund: UTI Mutual Fund
- Scrip Code: 544887
- ISIN: INF789F1AG76
- Issue Price: Rs. 37.85 per unit
- Date of Allotment: 25/08/2026
- Market Lot: 1 Unit (traded in dematerialized form only)

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Units of the Mutual Fund will only be traded in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an additional investment option tracking the Nifty 500 TRI index on the exchange platform.
