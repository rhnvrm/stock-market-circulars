## Summary

BSE has issued an updated list of securities identified for placement under the Add-on Price Band Framework, following a periodic review conducted in terms of exchange circular no. 20210811-6 dated August 11, 2021.

## Key Points

- Periodic review carried out for the identification of securities under the Add-on Price Band Framework.
- Updated list of securities provided in Annexure I of the circular.
- Market participants can direct clarifications to the BSE Surveillance department at bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- Application of Add-on Price Bands to specific securities as per surveillance review results.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the updated price band restrictions applicable to the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 03 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the identified securities will be subject to the specified add-on price band limits, impacting volatility management and execution parameters.
