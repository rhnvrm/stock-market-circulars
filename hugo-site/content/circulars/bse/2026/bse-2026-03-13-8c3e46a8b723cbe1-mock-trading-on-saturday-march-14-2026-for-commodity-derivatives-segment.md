## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are required to test their connectivity and systems using the Disaster Recovery (DR) site parameters provided by the Exchange.

## Key Points

- **Event Date:** Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026.
- **Segment:** Commodity Derivatives segment.
- **Connectivity:** Details provided for connecting to the DR site via BOLT TWS, 3rd party trading applications through ETI APIs, and web-based applications (RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, Extranet, etc.).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational mock trading session notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should verify their technical parameters, ETI API settings, and DR configuration (such as IP addresses and port numbers for primary and secondary DR sites) to ensure seamless connectivity during the mock trading session.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on live market operations. Members can validate their disaster recovery protocols and system readiness ahead of regular trading days.
