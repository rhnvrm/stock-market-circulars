## Summary

BSE has announced that the equity shares of SJP Ultrasonics Limited (Scrip Code: 544980) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective from Friday, October 23, 2026, in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20261007-37.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SJP Ultrasonics Limited
- Scrip Code: 544980
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group) / M Group
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The trading segment classification for SJP Ultrasonics Limited is updated from Trade for Trade to Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the segment change for execution and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables regular rolling settlement for the equity shares of SJP Ultrasonics Limited, enhancing trading efficiency and liquidity.
