## Summary

BSE has issued a circular highlighting suggested best practices for listed companies to ensure full compliance with the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 (PIT Regulations), specifically regarding the maintenance of a Structured Digital Database (SDD) and handling of Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI).

## Key Points

- Listed companies must maintain an SDD containing details of the nature of UPSI, names, and PANs (or authorized identifiers) of persons sharing and receiving the information.
- Intermediaries and fiduciaries handling UPSI must also record the names and identifiers of individuals involved.
- Companies are advised to periodically review internal policies to prevent UPSI leakage.
- Regular training and awareness programs must be conducted for employees and fiduciaries.

## Regulatory Changes

- Re-emphasis on strict adherence to the provisions under the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015, and related FAQs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Maintain a structured digital database for all UPSI sharing events.
- Implement and periodically review internal leakage prevention policies.
- Operationalize the company's Code of Conduct regarding PIT Regulations.
- Conduct periodic training sessions for staff and fiduciaries.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances regulatory oversight and strengthens corporate governance regarding the handling of sensitive financial information.
