## Summary

BSE has announced the reopening of the Invesco India Pharma and Healthcare Fund for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 11, 2026, pursuant to notice no. 20260817-40.

## Key Points

- Fund Name: Invesco India Pharma and Healthcare Fund
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- Covered Plans: Regular and Direct plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors must ensure correct ISINs are used for transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume transactions and systematic investment plans in the specified Invesco mutual fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform.
