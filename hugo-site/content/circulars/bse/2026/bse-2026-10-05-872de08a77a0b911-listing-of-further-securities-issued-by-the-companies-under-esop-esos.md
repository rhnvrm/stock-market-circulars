## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the listing and admission for trading of further securities issued by eight companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS), effective from Tuesday, October 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading members are informed that new securities issued under ESOP/ESOS by 8 companies are admitted for trading.
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026.
- Companies involved include Angel One Limited, DCB Bank Limited, Global Health Limited, IndusInd Bank Limited, Jaro Institute of Technology Management and Research Limited, Meesho Limited, Metropolis Healthcare Limited, and Suryoday Small Finance Bank Limited.
- Lock-in status and distinct number ranges are specified for each company.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard corporate listing action under existing capital issuance and stock option frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies and trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements where applicable (e.g., Metropolis Healthcare Limited as per Annexure I).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity share capital for the respective listed entities due to ESOP exercises, with negligible overall market impact.
