Provident Cedar Private Limited has announced a part redemption of its debentures, resulting in a reduced face value per debenture of Rs. 58,235.17. Trading members are advised that the debentures will trade at this reduced face value effective September 30, 2026.

- Company Name: Provident Cedar Private Limited
