## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. As per the revised framework, specific securities are subjected to surveillance actions such as 100% margins, movement to higher stages, transfer to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS), or moving out of the framework entirely based on periodical reviews.

## Key Points

- 100% margins become applicable effective August 28, 2026, on all open positions as of August 27, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework or subjected to a Lower Price Band are effective from August 26, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective August 28, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework is effective from August 26, 2026.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is purely for market surveillance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins on applicable securities under LT-ASM.
- Application of Lower Price Bands and transfer of specific scrips to T / XT / MT / TS trading groups.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the revised margin requirements and trading group placements for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Lower Price Band & Movement / Exit: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities listed under the LT-ASM framework annexures will experience stricter margin requirements and potential trading restrictions, affecting liquidity and position management for the designated scrips.
