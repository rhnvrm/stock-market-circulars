## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the compulsory delisting of Seven Hill Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 511760) pursuant to the order of the Delisting Committee of the Exchange under SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021. The company, which remained suspended for more than 6 months, will be delisted from the exchange platform effective August 6, 2026, and moved to the Dissemination Board.

## Key Points

- **Company Delisted:** Seven Hill Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 511760).
- **Effective Date:** August 6, 2026.
- **Reason:** Remained suspended from trading for more than 6 months.
- **Post-Delisting Location:** Moved to the Dissemination Board of the Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

- Application of SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021.
- Ten-year debarment from accessing the securities market or seeking listing for the delisted company, whole-time directors, responsible persons, promoters, and related entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Promoters must purchase shares from public shareholders at a fair value determined by an independent valuer appointed by the Exchange.
- Freezing of equity shares held by promoters/promoter group and corporate benefits (dividends, rights, bonus, split, etc.) until an exit option is provided.
- Promoters, whole-time directors, and responsible compliance officers become ineligible to become directors of any listed company until the exit option is successfully provided.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 4, 2026
- **Effective Date of Delisting:** August 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Equity shares of Seven Hill Industries Ltd will cease to be available for trading on the exchange platform, and public shareholders will need to rely on the promoter exit offer determined by the independent valuer.
