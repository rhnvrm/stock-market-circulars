## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the revised reopen date for specific schemes of ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, moving the date from September 18, 2026, to September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260917-48, dated September 17, 2026.
- Affected Schemes: ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 and ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2041.
- Revised Reopen Date: September 21, 2026.
- Registrations due during the change period are liable for rejection by the RTA/AMC.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should note the revised reopen date and ensure that SIP or other registrations falling within the transition period are managed accordingly to prevent rejections.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 17, 2026
- Revised Reopen Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to investors and distributors participating in the affected ICICI Prudential NFO schemes during the transition window.
