## Summary

BSE has notified that Anvi Publishing Limited filed its Draft Offer Document on September 30, 2026, pursuant to Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations. The document is hosted on the SME website to seek public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Issuer: Anvi Publishing Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO
- Public Comment Window: At least 21 days from the notice date
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com
- Hosting Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

None. The filing adheres to Schedule VI requirements under ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Interested stakeholders may review the Draft Offer Document and submit any comments within 21 days of the notice to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 05, 2026
- Draft Offer Document Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Comments Deadline: Within 21 days from October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard disclosure procedure ensuring regulatory transparency and stakeholder participation for SME public issues.
