## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movements in specific scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, BSE sought clarifications from the respective companies. Replies received from several companies are attached, while responses from others are still awaited.

## Key Points

- Companies with replies received include Anant Raj Ltd, Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd, ICDS Ltd, Lead Financial Services Ltd, Modison Ltd, Phaarmasia Ltd, Q&T Foods Ltd, Shree Precoated Steels Ltd, Ujaas Energy Ltd, and Triumph International Finance India Ltd.
- Companies with replies awaited include Atal Realtech Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Infobeans Technologies Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, PVP Ventures Ltd, Sera Investments & Finance India Ltd, and Shah Alloys Ltd.
- Issued by the Surveillance department of BSE Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to clarify significant price/volume movements promptly upon exchange inquiry to ensure market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 04 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and timely information dissemination to investors regarding abnormal price or volume fluctuations.
