## Summary

BSE Ltd. has announced the approval of the surrender of trading membership in the equity derivative segment for Anvil Share & Stock Broking Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 32) with effect from October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Member Name: Anvil Share & Stock Broking Pvt. Ltd.
- Member No.: 32
- Segment: Equity Derivative Segment
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine administrative surrender of membership.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members requiring clarification on the notice may contact membership operations via email at membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Notice Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on the broader market as this represents a routine surrender of trading membership.
