## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has issued Notice No. 20260930-47 detailing the client margin reporting schedule and penalty dates for the Equity Segment for October 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines trade dates from October 1, 2026, to October 30, 2026.
- Specifies last dates of margin reporting and corresponding penalty dates.
- Mandatory compliance for all trading members operating in the equity segment.

## Regulatory Changes

Routine operational schedule notice with no underlying regulatory framework changes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must adhere to the margin reporting timelines to prevent levy of penalties on specified penalty dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Schedule Period: Trade dates from 01-10-2026 to 30-10-2026
- Reporting & Penalty Window: October 9, 2026, to November 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational transparency and compliance deadlines for clearing and trading members managing client margins.
