## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about revised terms for debt securities (NCDs) of Jana Holdings Limited, effective from September 24, 2026. The updates include a change in ISIN, extension of maturity date, and corresponding updates to the scrip long name.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 974867
- Old ISIN: INE682V08158 -> New ISIN: INE682V08174
- Old Maturity Date: 30/06/2026 -> New Maturity Date: 31/12/2026
- Old Scrip Long Name: JHL-3%-30-6-26-PVT -> New Scrip Long Name: JHL-3%-31-12-26-PVT
- Effective Date: September 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to note the revised terms and ISIN for trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2026
- New Maturity Date: December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading members holding or transacting in the specified debt securities of Jana Holdings Limited.
