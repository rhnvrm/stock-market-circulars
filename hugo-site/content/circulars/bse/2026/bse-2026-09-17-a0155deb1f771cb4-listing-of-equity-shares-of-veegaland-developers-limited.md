## Summary

Effective from Friday, September 18, 2026, the equity shares of Veegaland Developers Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'T' Group of Securities in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Veegaland Developers Limited
- Scrip Code: 544923
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: VEEGALAND
- ISIN No.: INE1JTV01015
- Total Securities: 4,87,50,000 Equity Shares of Rs.10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 140/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 130/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 16, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that the scrip remains in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.
- Depository records have been updated to mark the necessary lock-in period for demat shares.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 16, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Veegaland Developers Limited shares on BSE with standard trade-for-trade monitoring for the initial 10 trading sessions.
