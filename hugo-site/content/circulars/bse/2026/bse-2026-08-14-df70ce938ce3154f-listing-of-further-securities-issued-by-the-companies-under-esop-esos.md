## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing market participants about the listing and admission for trading of further equity shares issued by 10 companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS), effective from Monday, August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Total Companies Involved:** 10
- **Participating Companies & Details:**
  1. **Aditya Infotech Limited:** 449,950 shares (ISIN: INE819V01029, Scrip Code: 544466)
  2. **Bharat Wire Ropes Limited:** 198,337 shares (ISIN: INE316L01019, Scrip Code: 539799)
  3. **Delhivery Limited:** 112,304 shares (ISIN: INE148O01028, Scrip Code: 543529)
  4. **FSN E-Commerce Ventures Limited:** 226,200 shares (ISIN: INE388Y01029, Scrip Code: 543384)
  5. **Go Digit General Insurance Limited:** 134,679 shares (ISIN: INE03JT01014, Scrip Code: 544179)
  6. **ICICI Bank Limited:** 130,909 shares (ISIN: INE090A01021, Scrip Code: 532174)
  7. **ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited:** 13,890 shares (ISIN: INE765G01017, Scrip Code: 540716)
  8. **United Foodbrands Limited:** 31,274 shares (ISIN: INE382M01027, Scrip Code: 543283)
  9. **UTI Asset Management Company Limited:** 4,263 shares (ISIN: INE094J01016, Scrip Code: 543238)
  10. **Vishal Mega Mart Limited:** 500,000 shares (ISIN: INE01EA01019, Scrip Code: 544307)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the listing of new securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion resulting from employee stock option exercises across the listed entities with negligible market impact.
