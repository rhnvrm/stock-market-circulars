## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing Research Analysts about the extension of the timeline for enrolment with the Past Risk and Return Verification Agency (PaRRVA) until September 03, 2026, following SEBI Circular No. HO/38/14/(11)2026-MIRSD-POD1/I/18038/2026 dated August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- References previous SEBI Circular No. HO/38/14/(4)2026-MIRSD-POD/I/10557/2026 dated April 29, 2026, and Exchange notice no. 20260430-45 dated April 30, 2026.
- Extension granted for enrolment with PaRRVA.
- Research Analysts must ensure timely compliance before the revised deadline.

## Regulatory Changes

- The deadline for mandatory enrolment with PaRRVA has been extended to September 03, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- All registered Research Analysts are required to complete their enrolment with PaRRVA on or before September 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 04, 2026
- **Revised Enrolment Deadline:** September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides additional time for Research Analysts to complete operational and administrative requirements for PaRRVA enrolment without facing regulatory penalties for missing the prior deadline.
