## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund is offering the UTI Nifty India New Age Consumption Index Fund NFO along with SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform from October 22, 2026, to November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: UTI Nifty India New Age Consumption Index Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AH91) and Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AI09).
- Scheme Category: Equity (Index Fund).
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 11, 2026.
- NFO Period: October 22, 2026, to November 05, 2026.
- NFO extended till midnight on November 05, 2026, for lumpsum purchases.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated on or before 3:00 PM on November 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without funds initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Transactions with initiated payment remain pending until 3:00 PM on the last day of funds pay-out or T+5 day, whichever is earlier.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 11, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 22, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: November 05, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 11, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: November 11, 2026, by 6:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Standard NFO launch facilitating investments in the new age consumption theme via the BSE StAR MF platform.
