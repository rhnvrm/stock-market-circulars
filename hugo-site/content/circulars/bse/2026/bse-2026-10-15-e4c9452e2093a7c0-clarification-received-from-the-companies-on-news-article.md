## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) sought clarification from three listed banking companies—CSB Bank Ltd, Yes Bank Ltd, and IDBI Bank Ltd—regarding various news articles published across newspapers, websites, and television channels to ensure accuracy and inform investors.

## Key Points

- BSE sought clarification from CSB Bank Ltd (Scrip Code: 542867) regarding news on Livemint.
- Clarification was sought from Yes Bank Ltd (Scrip Code: 532648) regarding news on CNBC TV18.
- Clarification was sought from IDBI Bank Ltd (Scrip Code: 500116) regarding news on NDTV Profit.
- Replies received from the respective companies are attached with the notice.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard surveillance clarification process.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to clarify news articles as requested by the exchange to verify accuracy and prevent market rumors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 15 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Neutral operational impact; intended to maintain market transparency and verify media reports concerning the banks.
