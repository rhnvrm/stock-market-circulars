## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading admission of 14,81,72,756 units of Capital Infra Trust (Scrip Code: 544338, ISIN: INE0Z8Z23013) effective from Thursday, October 22, 2026, following an institutional placement to QIBs.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** Capital Infra Trust
- **Scrip Code:** 544338
- **ISIN:** INE0Z8Z23013
- **Security Details:** 14,81,72,756 Units allotted to QIBs pursuant to Institutional Placement
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company
- **Date of Allotment:** October 16, 2026
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 75.25/-
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 508162934 to 656335689

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing admission notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing and admission for trading of the new securities. No special compliance requirements mandated beyond standard trading operations.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 16, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing of 14.81 crore units issued via institutional placement expands the capital base and liquidity of Capital Infra Trust on the exchange.
