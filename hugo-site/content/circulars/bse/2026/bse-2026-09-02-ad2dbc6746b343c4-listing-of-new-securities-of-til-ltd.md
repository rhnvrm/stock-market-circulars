## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade in new securities of TIL Ltd. (Scrip Code: 505196) effective from September 3, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TIL Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 505196
- ISIN: INE806C01018
- Securities: 17,500 Equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Rs. 10/- each issued on rights basis.
- Distinctive Numbers: 17,500 shares (80243185 to 80260684)
- Effective Date: Thursday, September 03, 2026
- Ranking: Pari-passu with the old Equity Shares of the Company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 02 Sep 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: 03 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for trading members regarding the listing of converted equity shares of TIL Ltd.
