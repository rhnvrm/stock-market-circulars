## Summary

SBI Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the Magnum Equity Ex-Top 100 Long Short Fund (SIF) along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from August 7, 2026, to August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: MAGNUM EQUITY EX-TOP 100 LONG SHORT FUND (SIF)
- NFO Period: August 07, 2026 to August 20, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 27, 2026
- Available Plans: Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- ISINs include INF200K30098, INF200K30080, INF200K30072, INF200K30122, INF200K30106, and INF200K30114.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 27, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on August 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: August 07, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: August 20, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to subscribe to the new mutual fund scheme through the BSE StAR MF platform with standard processing and payment timelines.
