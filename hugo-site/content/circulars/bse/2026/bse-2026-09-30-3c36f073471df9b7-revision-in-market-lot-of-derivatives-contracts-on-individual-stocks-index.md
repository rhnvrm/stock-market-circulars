## Summary

BSE has announced the periodic revision of market lots for derivatives contracts on individual stocks and indices in pursuance of SEBI guidelines (Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/14/2015 and SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD-1/P/CIR/2024/132). Out of the reviewed contracts, 165 stocks and 3 indices see no change, 7 stocks have downward revisions (with revised lot not in multiple of old lot), 9 stocks have downward revisions, and 27 stocks and 1 index have upward revisions. Securities BAJAJHLDNG, IEX, and IREDA were excluded from the review due to their exclusion from the Futures and Options segment.

## Key Points

- Periodic revision of market lots for derivatives contracts on stocks and indices based on SEBI guidelines.
- 165 stocks and 3 indices remain unchanged.
- 7 stocks have downward revisions (non-multiple of old lot), 9 stocks have downward revisions, and 27 stocks and 1 index have upward revisions.
- BAJAJHLDNG, IEX, and IREDA are excluded from the review due to F&O exclusion.
- Effective dates vary: October 30, 2026 onwards for November 2026 and subsequent expiries (Annexure II), or January 2027 and subsequent expiries for Annexure III and IV.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of derivatives market lot sizes in accordance with SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/14/2015 and SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD-1/P/CIR/2024/132.
- Phased implementation schedule based on contract expiration and annexure classification.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems, risk management modules, and order placement workflows to reflect the revised market lot sizes for applicable futures and options contracts.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date (Annexure II): October 30, 2026 (for November 2026 and subsequent expiries)
- Effective Date (Annexures III & IV): October 30, 2026 (for January 2027 and subsequent expiries; November and December 2026 expiry contracts continue with existing market lots)

## Impact Assessment

- Adjusts trading lot sizes for various derivative contracts, altering required capital margins, lot structuring, and hedging strategies for market participants trading in the F&O segment.
