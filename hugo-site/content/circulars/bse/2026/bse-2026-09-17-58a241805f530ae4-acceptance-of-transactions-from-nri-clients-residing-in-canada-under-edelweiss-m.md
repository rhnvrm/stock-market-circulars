## Summary

Edelweiss Mutual Fund has announced that transactions from Non-Resident Indian (NRI) investors residing in Canada will be accepted under its schemes and Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective September 18, 2026. Members must obtain a prescribed declaration form and follow specific submission guidelines to the RTA and AMC.

## Key Points

- Effective date for accepting transactions from Canada-residing NRIs is September 18, 2026.
- Applies to schemes of Edelweiss Mutual Fund and Edelweiss Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) on BSE StAR MF.
- Requires submission of an original signed declaration form along with transaction numbers to KFin Technologies Limited.
- Scanned copies must be emailed to designated Edelweiss and KFin personnel on the same day.

## Regulatory Changes

- Relaxation or formalization of transaction acceptance for Canadian NRI investors under specified Edelweiss schemes via the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Obtain the prescribed declaration form duly signed by the investor(s).
- Send the original declaration form along with a sheet of transaction numbers to the RTA (KFin Technologies Limited, Hyderabad).
- Clearly label the envelope as "Declaration Form for the Canada Investors - Edelweiss Mutual Fund".
- Email scanned copies on the transaction day to Vaibhav.Chogale@edelweissmf.com, Prajakta.Gharale@edelweissmf.com, and emfprocessing@kfintech.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 17, 2026
- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates seamless mutual fund transactions for Canadian NRI clients on the BSE StAR MF platform while enforcing strict documentation and verification workflows.
