## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are advised to participate to test their disaster recovery (DR) site connection parameters, trading applications, and web-based systems.

## Key Points

- **Event Date:** Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026.
- **Segment:** Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) and Commodity Derivatives segment.
- **Connectivity Details:** Provides DR site IP addresses and port numbers for BOLT TWS, ETI APIs, web-based systems (RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, Extranet), and online trade files.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A - Routine operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should verify connectivity to the Exchange Disaster Recovery (DR) site using the specified technical parameters.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures member systems are robust and capable of seamless failover to disaster recovery sites during unforeseen contingencies.
