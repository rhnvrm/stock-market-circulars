## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the revised settlement schedule and obligation download dates for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Delisting) of Hitech Corporation Limited (Settlement No. 2026119), following the bidding period from October 7, 2026, to October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scrip:** Hitech Corporation Limited (Settlement No: 2026119, Settlement Type: Delisting).
- **Bidding Period:** Conducted from 07/10/2026 to 13/10/2026.
- **Revised Obligation Download Date:** October 22, 2026.
- **Revised Settlement Date:** October 23, 2026.
- **Early Pay-in:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type '(Delisting)' and Settlement No. 2026119.
- **Stamp Duty:** Members should refer to the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of settlement timelines for the delisting acquisition window as per established stock exchange acquisition guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must use the specified Market Type '(Delisting)' and Settlement No. 2026119, ensure demat shares are delivered via Early Pay-in, and review client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bidding Window:** 07/10/2026 to 13/10/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download:** 22/10/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 23/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement processing and timely completion of share pay-ins for the delisting offer of Hitech Corporation Limited.
