## Summary

The Exchange has received communication from The Wealth Company AMC stating that The Wealth Company Mid Cap Fund (NFO Scheme) shall remain open until midnight on July 29, 2026, for Lumpsum Purchase transactions and SIP/XSIP registrations on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Reference to Exchange Notice No. 20260714-23 dated July 14, 2026.
- Scheme: The Wealth Company Mid Cap Fund.
- Extended availability until midnight on July 29, 2026, for Lumpsum Purchase and SIP/XSIP registrations.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to inform clients that all SIP/XSIP registrations must be duly authenticated on or before midnight on July 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Closure Date / Time:** July 29, 2026, up to midnight.

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members additional time to complete lumpsum purchases and SIP/XSIP registrations for the NFO scheme.
