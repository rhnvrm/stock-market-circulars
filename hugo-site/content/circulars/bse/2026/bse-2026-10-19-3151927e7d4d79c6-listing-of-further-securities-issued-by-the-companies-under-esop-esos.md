## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of further securities issued by five companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS), effective from Wednesday, October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Total Companies:** 5
- **Companies Involved:** AAVAS Financiers Limited, Carborundum Universal Limited, Mastek Limited, One Mobikwik Systems Limited, and Vedant Fashions Limited.

## Details of Securities

1. **AAVAS Financiers Limited** (ISIN: INE216P01012, Scrip Code: 541988): 1,540 shares (Face Value: 10)
2. **Carborundum Universal Limited** (ISIN: INE120A01034, Scrip Code: 513375): 4,750 shares (Face Value: 1)
3. **Mastek Limited** (ISIN: INE759A01021, Scrip Code: 523704): 4,454 shares (Face Value: 5)
4. **One Mobikwik Systems Limited** (ISIN: INE0HLU01028, Scrip Code: 544305): 314,767 shares (Face Value: 2)
5. **Vedant Fashions Limited** (ISIN: INE825V01034, Scrip Code: 543463): 303 shares (Face Value: 1)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must take note of the newly listed securities admitted for trading.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase resulting from ESOP exercises, with negligible dilution impact on existing shareholders.
