## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the part redemption of non-convertible debentures and reduction of face value for Purple Finance Limited and Mahaveer Finance India Limited, with record dates set for August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Purple Finance Limited**: Scrip Code 977748, ISIN INE0CYK07020, featuring part redemption with a reduced face value of Rs. 8333/- per debenture. Record date and ex-date are 11/08/2026.
- **Mahaveer Finance India Limited**: Scrip Code 977818, ISIN INE911L07154, featuring part redemption with a reduced face value of Rs. 9285.71 per debenture. Record date and ex-date are 11/08/2026.
- Trading in the debentures of these companies will take place with the reduced face value effective from the record date.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard corporate action notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and depositories must note the reduced face values and updated redemption schedules for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date**: 11/08/2026
- **Ex-Date**: 11/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for holders of the specified non-convertible debentures reflecting partial capital repayment and adjusted face values.
