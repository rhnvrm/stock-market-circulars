## Summary

Trading members are informed that equity shares of Hero Motors Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange effective from Wednesday, September 23, 2026, under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Hero Motors Limited
- Scrip Code: 544936
- Scrip ID: HEROMOTORS
- ISIN No.: INE012G01022
- Number of Securities: 454,215,576 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 84/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 74/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Wednesday, September 23, 2026.
- Lock-in period details and shareholding patterns as per Annexure I and Annexure II respectively.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 23, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Hero Motors Limited equity shares on the BSE platform.
