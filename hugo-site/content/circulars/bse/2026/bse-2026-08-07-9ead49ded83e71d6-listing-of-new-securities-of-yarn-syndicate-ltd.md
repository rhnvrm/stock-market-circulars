## Summary

Trading members of the exchange are informed that 13,97,576 equity shares of Yarn Syndicate Ltd (Scrip Code: 514378) pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Rs. 10/- each issued on a rights basis are permitted for trading with effect from Monday, August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Yarn Syndicate Ltd
- Scrip Code: 514378
- ISIN: INE564C01013
- Number of Securities: 13,97,576 Equity shares (Distinctive numbers: 4,53,885 [3750001 to 4203885] and 9,43,691 [4208886 to 5152576])
- Ranking: These shares are pari-passu with the old Equity Shares of the Company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can trade in the newly listed securities as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 7, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in the paid-up equity base of the company through conversion of partly paid shares.
