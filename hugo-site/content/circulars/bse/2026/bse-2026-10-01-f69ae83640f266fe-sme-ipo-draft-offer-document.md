## Summary

Weldor CNC Machines Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with BSE for its proposed SME IPO on September 30, 2026. The document is available for public review and comments for a period of at least 21 days in compliance with ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Issuer Company: Weldor CNC Machines Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of notice
- Submission Email for Comments: equity.ipo@bseindia.com
- Hosted Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine draft offer document filing under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Public and stakeholders may submit comments on the draft offer document within 21 days of the notice to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites public scrutiny/feedback on the proposed SME public issue.
