## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective from September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised margin rules under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure framework for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100% as applicable) on all open positions as of September 16, 2026, and all new positions created from September 17, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for specified securities may impact trading liquidity and short-term volatility for the affected scrips.
