## Summary

Trading members are informed that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of Salem Erode Investments Limited will be permitted on the Exchange starting Tuesday, September 15, 2026. The rights issue is scheduled to open on September 15, 2026, and close on September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 751141
- Scrip ID: SALEM-RE
- ISIN No.: INE894E20010
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: September 15, 2026, to September 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs using the correct ISIN and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares. Contract notes must include a prominent disclaimer stating that the purchase of REs only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce the REs before the issue closes. Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the issue closing date.

## Important Dates

- Trading Period Start: September 15, 2026
- Trading Period End: September 21, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: September 15, 2026
- Rights Issue Close Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and market participants to trade or renounce rights entitlements on a T+1 rolling settlement basis under a trade-for-trade category.
