## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Non-Competitive Bidding facility for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled on September 01, 2026, based on the RBI press release.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding is available through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.
- Bid collection for direct investors starts on August 31, 2026, at 10:00 am and ends on August 31, 2026, at 5:00 pm.
- Bid collection for trading members closes on September 01, 2026, at 8:00 am.
- Multiple SDL securities across various states (such as Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Manipur, Odisha, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and West Bengal) are available for re-issue and new issuance.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine auction schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible direct investors must adhere to the bidding timelines and parameter specifications specified for the respective SDL securities.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: August 31, 2026
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): August 31, 2026 (5:00 pm)
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): September 01, 2026 (8:00 am)
- Auction Date: September 01, 2026
- Settlement Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates participation of retail and non-competitive bidders in State Government Securities auctions through the designated BSE platform.
