## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Wednesday, September 23, 2026, the equity shares of SS Retail Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SS Retail Limited
- Scrip Code: 544934
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SSRETAIL
- ISIN No.: INE1EQW01028
- Number of Securities: 74,367,575 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 424/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 414/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 21, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Registrar: Kfin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000. Necessary corporate action has been executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing and Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of SS Retail Limited shares on the BSE platform, starting with the Special Pre-open Session on September 23, 2026.
