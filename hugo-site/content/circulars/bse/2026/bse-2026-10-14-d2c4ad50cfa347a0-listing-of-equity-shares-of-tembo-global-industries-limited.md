## Summary

Effective from Thursday, October 15, 2026, equity shares of Tembo Global Industries Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Total Securities: 19,35,51,980 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up.
- Scrip Code: 544987
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: TEMBO
- ISIN No.: INE869Y01028
- Market Lot: 1 (One) Share (traded in dematerialised form only).
- Distinctive Nos.: 1 to 193551980.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Lock-in period marked in depository records for demat shares; physical share certificates enfaced with non-transferability.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with demat trading mandates and unit market lot rules.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: October 15, 2026.
- Notice Date: October 14, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public market trading and liquidity for Tembo Global Industries Limited equity shares on the BSE platform.
