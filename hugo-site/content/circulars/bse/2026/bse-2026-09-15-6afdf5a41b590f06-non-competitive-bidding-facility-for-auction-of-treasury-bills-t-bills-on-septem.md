## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility to participate in the auction of three Treasury Bills (T-Bills) announced by the Government of India, scheduled for September 16, 2026. Trading members and eligible investors can utilize the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- **Securities Offered:** 91 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 091T161226, Issue Size: Rs. 9,000 Cr), 182 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 182T170327, Issue Size: Rs. 8,000 Cr), and 364 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 364T160927, Issue Size: Rs. 7,000 Cr).
- **Bidding Platform:** NCB-GSec module via https://ibbs.bseindia.com.
- **Auction Date:** September 16, 2026.
- **Settlement Date:** September 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is in continuation of established exchange circular guidelines regarding non-competitive bidding for Government Securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure bids are submitted within the specified timelines and adhere to the minimum lot sizes and investment limits.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** September 14, 2026, from 10:00 AM onwards
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors):** September 15, 2026, till 5:00 PM
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** September 16, 2026, till 8:00 AM
- **Auction Date:** September 16, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides standard operational access for trading members and direct investors to participate in the RBI-conducted Treasury Bill auction.
