## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the offer to buy through the acquisition window for the takeover of Kkalpana Plastick Limited. The open offer is made by Mr. Ashish Begwani to acquire up to 14,37,420 fully paid equity shares representing 26% of the voting share capital at an offer price of Rs. 28 per share.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: Mr. Ashish Begwani (Acquirer 1).
- Target Company: Kkalpana Plastick Limited ("KPL").
- Offer Price: Rs. 28/- per equity share payable in cash.
- Offer Size: Up to 14,37,420 fully paid equity shares (26% of total issued equity and voting share capital).
- Offer Period: August 21, 2026 to September 04, 2026 (Excluding SEBI Holiday on August 26, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted pursuant to Regulations 3(1) & 4 of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 and subsequent amendments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians are required to follow the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through stock exchange pursuant to tender offers under takeover regulations.

## Important Dates

- Offer Opening Date: August 21, 2026
- Offer Closing Date: September 04, 2026
- SEBI Holiday (Exclusion): August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates public shareholders of Kkalpana Plastick Limited to tender their shares in the open offer via the acquisition window mechanism on the stock exchange.
