## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated October 06, 2026, in the matter of Dollar Advisory and Financial Services (Proprietor Ravi Prakash Mohta, Appeal No. 240 of 2023). SAT has set aside the directions contained in paragraph 138 (A) to (H) of the original matter, and SEBI has confirmed that the debarment imposed on the entity may be removed.

## Key Points

- Reference to previous Exchange Notice No. 20221201-1 dated December 01, 2022.
- SAT vide its order dated October 06, 2026, set aside directions contained in paragraph 138 (A) to (H).
- SEBI confirmed via email on October 16, 2026, that the debarment imposed on the entity may be removed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Directions and restrictions previously imposed under paragraph 138 (A) to (H) against Dollar Advisory and Financial Services have been set aside by SAT.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the removal of debarment and ensure compliance with the updated regulatory status.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: October 06, 2026
- SEBI Confirmation Date: October 16, 2026
- Notice Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Limited operational impact, serving as a compliance update regarding the lifting of restrictions on the named entity.
