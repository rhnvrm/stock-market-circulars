## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) of several companies due to the fixation of record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the respective ex-dates/record dates.

## Key Points

- Trading in commercial papers of multiple companies is suspended due to upcoming redemption record dates.
- Companies involved include Infina Finance Private Limited, KEC International Ltd., Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, Avenue Supermarts Limited, Fedbank Financial Services Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, Birla Group Holdings Private Limited, Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, Nuvama Wealth Management Limited, and IGH Holdings Private Limited.
- Record dates span across early September 2026 (specifically 04/09/2026 and 07/09/2026).

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must strictly avoid dealing in the specified commercial papers of the listed companies as per the schedule provided.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 02 Sep 2026
- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: 04/09/2026 and 07/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding these commercial papers must note the suspension of trading in preparation for redemption settlement.
