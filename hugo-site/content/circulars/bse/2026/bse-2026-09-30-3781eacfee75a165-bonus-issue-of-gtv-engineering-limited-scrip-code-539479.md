## Summary

GTV Engineering Limited has announced a bonus issue of equity shares in the ratio of 2:1 (2 bonus equity shares of Rs. 2/- each for every 1 existing equity share of Rs. 2/- each). The company has fixed October 7, 2026, as the record date for determining shareholders eligible for the bonus issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: GTV Engineering Limited
- Scrip Code: 539479
- Record Date: October 7, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: October 7, 2026
- Bonus Ratio: 2:1 (2 bonus shares for every 1 share held)
- Face Value: Rs. 2/- per share
- Total Projected Allotment: 101,600,732 equity shares as of October 8, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard corporate action notice in accordance with listing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the ex-bonus date and record date for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Bonus Date: October 7, 2026
- Allotment Date (as informed by company): October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The bonus issue increases the liquidity and equity base of GTV Engineering Limited. Trading in equity shares will take place on an ex-bonus basis starting from October 7, 2026.
