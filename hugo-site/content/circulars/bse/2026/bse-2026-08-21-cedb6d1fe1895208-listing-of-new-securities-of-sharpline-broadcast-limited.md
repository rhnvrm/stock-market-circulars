## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of Sharpline Broadcast Limited (Scrip Code - 543341) effective from August 24, 2026. A total of 1,18,57,140 equity shares were issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis at an issue price of Rs. 14/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 4/-).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sharpline Broadcast Limited
- Scrip Code: 543341
- ISIN: INE647W01014
- Total New Shares: 1,18,57,140 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 14/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-, Premium: Rs. 4/-)
- Allottees: Non-Promoters on a preferential basis
- Effective Date of Trading: August 24, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 16777777 to 28634916
- Lock-in Details:
  - 35,71,428 shares (Dist. Nos. 16777777 to 20349204) locked-in upto 15/03/2027
  - 28,57,142 shares (Dist. Nos. 20349205 to 23206346) locked-in upto 14/03/2027
  - 25,71,428 shares (Dist. Nos. 23206347 to 25777774) locked-in upto 14/03/2027
  - 28,57,142 shares (Dist. Nos. 25777775 to 28634916) locked-in upto 15/03/2027
