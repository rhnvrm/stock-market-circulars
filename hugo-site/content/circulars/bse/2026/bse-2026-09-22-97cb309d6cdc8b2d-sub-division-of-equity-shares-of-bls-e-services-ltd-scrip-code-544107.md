## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the sub-division of equity shares of BLS E-Services Ltd (Scrip Code: 544107) from one equity share of Rs. 10/- each into two equity shares of Rs. 5/- each, effective from the specified record date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: BLS E-Services Ltd
- Scrip Code: 544107
- Purpose: Sub-Division of existing Equity Shares from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/-
- Record Date: 06/10/2026
- Sub-divided Paid-up Value W.E.F.: 06/10/2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Existing ISIN No. INE0NLT01010 of Rs. 10/- paid up will not be valid for transactions done on the Exchange on or after 06/10/2026.
- A separate notice will be issued by the exchange to inform the new ISIN number for the Rs. 5/- paid-up shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the record date and the invalidation of the old ISIN for transactions from 06/10/2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 6, 2026
- Effective Date for Sub-Divided Paid-Up Value: October 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of BLS E-Services Ltd will receive two shares of Rs. 5/- face value for every single share of Rs. 10/- face value held as of the record date. Trading members must ensure compliance with the ISIN change.
