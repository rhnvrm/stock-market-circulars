## Summary

BSE has issued a settlement schedule pertaining to the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Dolphin Medical Services Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from July 31, 2026, to August 13, 2026, under Settlement No. 2026096.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Takeover
- Settlement No.: 2026096
- Bid Start Date: 31/07/2026
- Bid End Date: 13/08/2026
- Obligation Download Date: 27/08/2026
- Settlement Date: 28/08/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories.
- Members and custodians must use Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026096.
- Participants must ensure early pay-in effects are reflected in BSE/ICCL systems prior to bidding and confirmation window timelines.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Important Dates

- Bid Period: 31/07/2026 to 13/08/2026
- Obligation Download: 27/08/2026
- Settlement Date: 28/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational requirements for clearing members and custodians participating in the takeover offer of Dolphin Medical Services Limited, requiring adherence to early pay-in procedures.
