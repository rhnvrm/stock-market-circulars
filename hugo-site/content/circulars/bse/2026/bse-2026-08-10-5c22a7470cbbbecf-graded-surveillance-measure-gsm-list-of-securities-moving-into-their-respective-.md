## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of select securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The changes become effective from August 11, 2026, and entail specific surveillance actions as mandated by the GSM framework established on February 23, 2017.

## Key Points

- Affected securities are moved to higher GSM stages starting August 11, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, including 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement, Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD), and restricted trading frequencies.
- Settlement for these securities will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures across GSM Stages I, II, III, and IV as applicable to the listed securities in the attached annexure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the specified securities.
- Compliance with mandatory margin deposits (up to 100% ASD) and trade-for-trade settlement terms.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities are likely to be restricted due to trade-for-trade settlement and higher deposit requirements.
