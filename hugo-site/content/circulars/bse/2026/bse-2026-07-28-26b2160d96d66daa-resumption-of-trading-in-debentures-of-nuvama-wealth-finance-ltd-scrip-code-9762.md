## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of trading in the debentures of Nuvama Wealth Finance Ltd (Scrip Code: 976235) effective from July 29, 2026. This follows a clarification from the company that the earlier record date (May 29, 2026) was exclusively for the payment of interest rather than redemption.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 976235
- **Company Name:** Nuvama Wealth Finance Ltd
- **Effective Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Clarification:** The record date fixed on May 29, 2026, per Exchange Notice No. 20260522-49, was for the payment of interest and not redemption.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in the debentures is resumed after temporary suspension/restriction related to the record date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the resumption and facilitate transactions accordingly from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Previous Record Date:** May 29, 2026
- **Resumption Date:** July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normal trading operations resume for investors holding the debentures under Scrip Code 976235.
