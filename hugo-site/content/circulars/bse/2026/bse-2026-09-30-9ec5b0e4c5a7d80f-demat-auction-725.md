## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-725/2026-27. The auction sessions, matching, and statement timings are scheduled for September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement No.: DR-725/2026-27
- Auction Offer Entry Timing: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on September 30, 2026
- Auction Matching Timing: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on September 30, 2026
- Delivery Statement and Pay-in: Pay-in of the auction is scheduled for October 1, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine auction schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 30, 2026
- Pay-in Date: October 1, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for clearing short deliveries in rolling settlements with no broad market impact.
