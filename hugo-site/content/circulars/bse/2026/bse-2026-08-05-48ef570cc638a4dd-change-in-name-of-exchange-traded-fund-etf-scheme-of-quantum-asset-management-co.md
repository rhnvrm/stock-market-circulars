## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the name change of an Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) scheme managed by Quantum Asset Management Company Private Limited, effective August 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Existing Scheme Name:** Quantum Gold Fund - Exchange Traded Fund
- **New Scheme Name:** Quantum Gold ETF
- **ISIN:** INF082J01408
- **Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants should update their systems and records with the new scheme name.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating records and trading terminal nomenclature to reflect the new scheme name.
