## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, August 26, 2026, the equity shares of Mopshop Distribution Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange in the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Company Name: MOPSHOP DISTRIBUTION LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544883
- ISIN: INE2F5H01015
- Issue Price: Rs. 138/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 128/-)
- Total Securities: 7,200,000 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1000 shares
- Date of Allotment: August 24, 2026
- Market Maker: Prabhat Financials Services Limited
- Registrar: Cameo Corporate Services Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing procedures and trade-for-trade segment placement for SME IPOs applied per existing SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in dematerialised form.
- Minimum market lot of 1000 equity shares is applicable.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 24, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Mopshop Distribution Limited shares are now live for public trading on the BSE SME platform under the 'MT' Group.
