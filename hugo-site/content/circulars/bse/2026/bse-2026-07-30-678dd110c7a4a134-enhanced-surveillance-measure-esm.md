## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion, movement to lower stages, and exclusion from the framework. Securities qualifying under ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin and be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion or shifting to Stage I under the ESM framework w.e.f. July 31, 2026 / August 03, 2026.
- Attracts minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 03, 2026, on all open positions as on July 31, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shifting from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. August 03, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. July 31, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the ESM framework is given in Annexure II.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures in conjunction with existing exchange rules to monitor market volatility and safeguard investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements and monitor open positions in the specified securities under the ESM framework.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 Jul 2026
- Effective Date for ESM measures: August 03, 2026
- Stage II / Lower stage movement effective: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity for affected securities may be impacted due to the Trade-for-Trade settlement and 100% margin requirements.
