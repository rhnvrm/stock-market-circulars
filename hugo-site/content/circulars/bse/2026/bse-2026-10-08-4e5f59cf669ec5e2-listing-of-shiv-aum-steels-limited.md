## Summary

Effective from Friday, October 9, 2026, the equity shares of Shiv Aum Steels Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE under the 'B' Group of Securities. The company's scrip code is 544969 with ISIN INE719F01016.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: Shiv Aum Steels Limited
- Scrip Code: 544969
- ISIN No.: INE719F01016
- Security Details: 13,600,400 Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Market Lot: 1 (One) Share
- BOLT System Name / Scrip ID: SHIVAUM
- Effective Date: October 9, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing admission procedures apply.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Physical share certificates have been enfaced with non-transferability, and demat shares have lock-in markings in depository records where applicable.
- All shares rank pari-passu including dividend entitlement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 8, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and liquidity for the equity shares of Shiv Aum Steels Limited on the BSE platform.
