## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, September 30, 2026, the equity shares of Elevate Campuses Limited will be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities. The public issue price was Rs. 362/- per share (Face Value Rs. 1/- with a premium of Rs. 361/-).

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544945
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: ELEVATE
- ISIN No: INE147X01013
- Total Securities: 168,531,037 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up
- Date of Allotment: September 28, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Registrar: KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on September 30, 2026.
- Necessary corporate actions have been executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 28, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading / SPOS: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Elevate Campuses Limited equity shares on the BSE main board, providing liquidity to shareholders and expanding the exchange's equity segment.
