## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that multiple companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of their Commercial Papers (CPs). Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of the specified companies starting from the respective record/ex-dates in October 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in Commercial Papers due to redemption record dates.
- Affected issuers include Infina Finance, 360 ONE Prime, Muthoot Fincorp, InCred Financial Services, Bajaj Financial Securities, Bajaj Finance, Ashoka Buildcon, Aditya Birla Housing Finance, Navi Finserv, Kotak Mahindra Prime, Muthoot Mcred, Sikka Ports & Terminals, Poonawalla Fincorp, and Axis Finance.
- Record and Ex-Dates fall in October 2026 (primarily October 6, October 14, and October 15, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

Standard exchange operational procedure for debt instruments and commercial paper redemption and trading suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure they do not deal in the commercial papers of the mentioned companies as of the specified record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Record / Ex-Dates: October 6, 2026; October 14, 2026; and October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational restriction affecting secondary market trading of specific commercial papers prior to their redemption.
