## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has informed trading members that the name of the trading member HSBC Investdirect Securities (India) Limited (Member No. 125) is changing to "HSBC Investdirect Securities (India) Private Limited".

## Key Points

- **Old Name:** HSBC Investdirect Securities (India) Limited
- **New Name:** HSBC Investdirect Securities (India) Private Limited
- **Member Number:** 125
- **Effective Date:** 21st September 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative corporate name change of a trading member.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members or clients requiring clarification can contact the Membership Operations department via email at membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 18 September 2026
- **Effective Date:** 21 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact, limited to updating records with the new corporate entity name for the specified trading member.
