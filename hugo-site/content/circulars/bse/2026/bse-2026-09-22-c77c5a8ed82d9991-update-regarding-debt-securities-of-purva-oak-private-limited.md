## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the update of debt securities of Purva Oak Private Limited. Trading members are informed that specific scrip codes will be available for trading with revised terms and new ISINs effective from September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Purva Oak Private Limited
- Effective Date: September 23, 2026
- Security Type: Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs)
- Mode of Security changed from Unsecured to Secured NCDs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of terms for existing NCDs including security status and ISIN mapping.
- Scrip Codes 977543, 977542, and 977540 affected with corresponding ISIN updates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems with the new ISINs and revised terms for Purva Oak Private Limited debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date for Revised Terms: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures accurate tracking and trading of debt securities with updated security status and ISINs for market participants.
