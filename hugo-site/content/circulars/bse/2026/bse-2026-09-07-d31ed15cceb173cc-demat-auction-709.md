## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-709/2026-27. The auction sessions, timings, and subsequent pay-in schedules have been formally announced.

## Key Points

- Notice details rolling settlement auction DR-709/2026-27.
- Auction offer entry, matching, delivery, and money statements are scheduled for September 7, 2026.
- Pay-in of the auction is scheduled for September 8, 2026.
- Trading members are reminded to check auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Restrictions on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they faced short delivery have been removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (operational notice under existing framework).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and complete securities pay-in via depositories within the stipulated cut-off timings.
- Trading members must exercise caution while entering securities codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- **Auction Offer Entry:** September 07, 2026 (02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m.)
- **Auction Matching:** September 07, 2026 (02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.)
- **Delivery Statement & Report:** September 07, 2026 (03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.)
- **Money Statement & Final Reports:** September 07, 2026 (03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.)
- **Auction Pay-in:** September 08, 2026 (08:00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational process for handling short deliveries in rolling settlements with no broad market impact.
