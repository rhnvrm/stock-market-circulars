## Summary

BSE has issued a settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for DEVINSU TRADING LIMITED, scheduled to be conducted from August 17, 2026, to August 31, 2026 under settlement number 2026102.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement Number:** 2026102
- **Bid Start Date:** 17/08/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 31/08/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 07/09/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 08/09/2026
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories using Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement No. 2026102.
- CDSL & NSDL early pay-in securities will be earmarked/blocked in clients' accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; follows revised guidelines for mechanisms of acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange as per prior Notice No. 20200528-32.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants must ensure that the effect of early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/ICCL systems before prescribed timelines for accepting/confirming bids. Members are requested to refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Window:** August 17, 2026 to August 31, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** September 7, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth execution, clearing, and settlement for the takeover offer of Devinsu Trading Limited with clear timelines and mandatory early pay-in mechanics for participating members and custodians.
