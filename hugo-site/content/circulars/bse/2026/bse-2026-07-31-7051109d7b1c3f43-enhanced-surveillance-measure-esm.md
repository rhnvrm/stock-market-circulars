## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities meeting the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment, or move to lower/other stages effective early August 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy the inclusion criteria and attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 04, 2026, on open positions as on August 03, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade for Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 03, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. August 04, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving to lower Stage I or moving out of the ESM framework completely w.e.f. August 03, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework rules in conjunction with prevailing surveillance measures.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II adjustments and movement to lower Stage I / moving out: August 03, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shift: August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions apply to short-listed securities, affecting liquidity and trading mechanisms for market participants.
