## Summary

The BSE has issued a surveillance notice highlighting scrips that experienced significant price or volume movements in the recent past. The exchange sought clarifications from these companies to ensure that trading members and investors have access to the latest relevant information. Clarifications have been received from 15 companies, while responses from 14 other companies are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- **Clarifications Received:** Aashka Hospitals Ltd, Amkay Products Ltd, Balurghat Technologies Ltd, Citichem India Ltd, Gabriel Pet Straps Ltd, Hero Motors Ltd, IB Infotech Enterprises Ltd, Jhandewalas Foods Ltd, Lokesh Machines Ltd, Nettlinx Ltd, Pace Digitek Ltd, Rose Merc Ltd, S. M. Gold Ltd, Safecure Services Ltd, and UHM Vacation Ltd.
- **Clarifications Awaited:** Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, El Forge Ltd, Farm Peace Ltd, Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Ltd, M P K Steels (I) Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Mopshop Distribution Ltd, Rex Sealing and Packing Industries Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, and Vellora Impact Ltd.
- **Objective:** To maintain market integrity, transparency, and ensure timely dissemination of price-sensitive or explanatory information to investors.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes introduced; conducted under standard ongoing market surveillance operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies exhibiting abnormal price/volume behavior are required to provide prompt clarifications to the stock exchange as part of continuing disclosure obligations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with clarity on recent price/volume fluctuations for responding companies, while pending companies remain subject to exchange surveillance follow-ups.
