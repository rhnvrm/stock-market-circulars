## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that pursuant to Notice No. 20260818-16 dated August 18, 2026, the DSP Financial Services Sectoral Debt Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and facilities like SIP, STP, and SWP on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: DSP Financial Services Sectoral Debt Fund
- Effective Date: August 31, 2026
- Available Facilities: Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Applicable ISINs include multiple regular and direct plans for growth and IDCW options.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants may take note of the availability of the schemes for ongoing transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and trading members to execute ongoing transactions and systematic facilities for the specified debt fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform.
