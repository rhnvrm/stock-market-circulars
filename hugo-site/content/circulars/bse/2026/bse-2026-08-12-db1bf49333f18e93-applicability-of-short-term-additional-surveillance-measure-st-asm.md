## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for shortlisted securities, pursuant to the existing ASM framework revisions.

## Key Points

- **ST-ASM Stage I Surveillance Actions:** Applicable margin rate is increased to 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at a maximum rate of 100%. This applies to all open positions as of August 13, 2026, and new positions created from August 14, 2026.
- **ST-ASM Stage II Surveillance Actions:** Applicable margin rate is increased to 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at a maximum rate of 100%. This applies to all open positions as of August 13, 2026, and new positions created from August 14, 2026.
- **Nature of ASM:** Shortlisting of securities under ASM is purely for market surveillance purposes and should not be interpreted as an adverse action against the concerned companies.
- **Price Band Restoration:** When a scrip moves out of the framework, its price band will be reinstated to the one applicable before its shortlisting, unless it is subject to other ongoing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulation is introduced; rather, surveillance actions under the existing Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework are being applied to specific securities as per the criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Market Intermediaries and Trading Members:** Must ensure compliance with the revised margin requirements (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for the shortlisted securities.
- **Margin Maintenance:** Apply the updated margin rates on all open positions as of August 13, 2026, and new positions from August 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **August 13, 2026:** Determination of open positions subject to the updated ST-ASM margins.
- **August 14, 2026:** Effective date for the applicable rate of margins under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Shortlisted securities will face higher margin requirements, potentially reducing short-term trading liquidity and leverage in those specific scrips.
- **Operational Impact:** Trading members must update their risk management systems to reflect the increased margin requirements of 50% or 100% for the affected securities from the effective date.
