## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that 71,428 new equity shares of Kesar India Limited (Scrip Code: 543542) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from August 7, 2026, following the conversion of warrants issued on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kesar India Limited
- Scrip Code: 543542
- ISIN: INE0L1C01019
- Security Details: 71,428 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 340/- (Issue Price: Rs. 350/-).
- Allotment Date: 04/06/2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 30099893 to 30171320
- Lock-in Details: 71,428 shares locked-in up to 10/02/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company and are subject to specified lock-in requirements.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Trading: August 7, 2026
- Date of Allotment: June 4, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: February 10, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity capital base of Kesar India Limited.
