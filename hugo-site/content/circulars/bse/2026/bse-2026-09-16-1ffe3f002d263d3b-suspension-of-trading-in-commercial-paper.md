## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CPs) for multiple companies which have fixed record dates for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of specified companies as their record dates for redemption have been fixed.
- Affected entities include Manappuram Finance Limited, Axis Finance Limited, 360 ONE WAM Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, L&T Metro Rail (Hyderabad) Limited, REC Limited, KEC International Ltd., and others.
- The suspension ensures smooth processing of the commercial paper redemptions according to the exchange schedule.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard operational procedure for suspending trading in commercial papers ahead of redemption record dates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must refrain from executing trades in the specified Commercial Papers on or after the respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 16 September 2026
- Record Dates and Ex-Dates: 18 September 2026 and 19 September 2026 (varying by specific commercial paper).

## Impact Assessment

- Restricts secondary market trading in the affected commercial papers to facilitate redemption settlement for investors.
