## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding surveillance measures in respect of companies with high promoter pledge, detailing securities entering or exiting the framework and specifying revised margin requirements.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 35% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Securities in Annexure II are eligible to move out of the framework effective September 03, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of minimum 35% margin on open positions and new positions for securities meeting high promoter pledge surveillance criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised margin requirements and ensure compliance for all open and new positions in the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for margins: September 07, 2026 (on open positions as of September 04, 2026)
- Removal Date for Annexure II securities: September 03, 2026
- Company representation deadline: September 03, 2026, by 5:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for affected securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for companies with high promoter pledge.
