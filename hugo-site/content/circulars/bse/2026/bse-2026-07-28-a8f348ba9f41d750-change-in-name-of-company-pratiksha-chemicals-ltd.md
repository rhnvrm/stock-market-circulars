## Summary

Trading members are informed that Pratiksha Chemicals Ltd has changed its name to Vellora Impact Limited. Consequently, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: Pratiksha Chemicals Ltd
- New Name: Vellora Impact Limited
- Scrip Code: 531257
- Existing Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: VELLORA
- New Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: VELLORA
- Effective Date: August 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems with the new company name for scrip code 531257.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 03, 2026
- Notice Date: July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact, limited to the update of display names on trading terminals.
