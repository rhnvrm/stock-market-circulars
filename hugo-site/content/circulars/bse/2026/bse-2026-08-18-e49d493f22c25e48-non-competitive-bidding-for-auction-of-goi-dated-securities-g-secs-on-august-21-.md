## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled for August 21, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Four dated securities are being auctioned by the Government of India through RBI.
- Securities available: 7.06% GS 2041 (Size: 17,000 Cr) and 7.43% GS 2076 (Size: 11,000 Cr).
- Bidding available through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.
- Bid collection start date: August 18, 2026 (10:00 am onwards).
- Bid collection end date for members: August 21, 2026 (till 8:00 am).
- Bid collection end date for direct investors: August 20, 2026 (till 5:00 pm).

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine auction schedule notice under established RBI and Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the non-competitive bidding process must submit bids within the specified timelines via the iBBS NCB-GSec module.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start: August 18, 2026
- Direct Investor Bid End: August 20, 2026 (5:00 PM)
- Member Bid End: August 21, 2026 (8:00 AM)
- Auction Date: August 21, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity and timeline details for trading members and investors participating in government securities auctions.
