## Summary

BSE has observed significant price and volume movements in various scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors and trading members have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies.

## Key Points

- **Clarifications Received:** Replies have been received and attached from 12 companies, including Godrej Industries Ltd, RITES Ltd, and BLS International Services Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited:** Clarifications are pending from 19 companies, including Spright Agro Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, and Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd.
- **Surveillance Measure:** Routine administrative notice under Exchange surveillance mechanism to maintain market transparency.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard surveillance disclosure notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Awaited companies are required to submit their clarifications regarding price/volume movements to the Exchange promptly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 16 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal systemic market impact; provides transparency regarding company-specific stock price and volume movements.
