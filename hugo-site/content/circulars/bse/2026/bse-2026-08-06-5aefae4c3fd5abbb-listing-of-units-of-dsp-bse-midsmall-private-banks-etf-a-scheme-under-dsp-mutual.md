## Summary

Trading members are informed that units of DSP BSE MidSmall Private Banks ETF, an open-ended scheme replicating or tracking the BSE MidSmall Private Banks Index, are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange effective Friday, August 7, 2026, under Group B.

## Key Points

- Name of Mutual Fund: DSP Mutual Fund
- Investment Manager: DSP Asset Managers Private Limited
- Scrip Code: 544857
- Scrip ID: MIDBANKADD
- ISIN No: INF740KA1ZH9
- Number of Units: 3,202,980 Units
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00 per unit
- Issue Price: Rs. 17.37 per unit
- Date of Allotment: 03/08/2026
- Market Lot: 1 unit (traded exclusively in dematerialized form)
- Registrar and Transfer Agent: CAMS (Computer Age Management Services Limited)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Units of the Mutual Fund will only be traded in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 3, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with additional investment opportunities in private banking sector via an ETF structure on the BSE platform.
