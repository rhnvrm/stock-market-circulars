## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the Non-Competitive Bidding for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for September 29, 2026. Bids are to be submitted via the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Auction Date: September 29, 2026.
- Bid Collection Start Date: September 28, 2026, from 10:00 AM onwards (24 hours availability).
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors): September 28, 2026, till 5:00 PM.
- Bid Collection End Date (Trading Members): September 29, 2026, till 8:00 AM.
- Settlement Date: September 30, 2026.
- Includes re-issues and new issues of SDLs from various states including Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Haryana, Jharkhand, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Sikkim, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and West Bengal.

## Regulatory Changes

Facilitates participation in RBI-conducted State Government Securities auctions through the Exchange's non-competitive bidding facility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure bids are collected and submitted within the specified deadlines through the NCB-GSec module (https://ibbs.bseindia.com).

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start: September 28, 2026, 10:00 AM
- Direct Investor Bid End: September 28, 2026, 5:00 PM
- Member Bid End: September 29, 2026, 8:00 AM
- Auction Date: September 29, 2026
- Settlement Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless access for investors and members to participate in the non-competitive allocation of State Government Securities.
