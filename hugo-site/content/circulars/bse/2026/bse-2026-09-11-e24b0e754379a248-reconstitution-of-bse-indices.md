## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the reconstitution results for various BSE indices. The changes will be effective at the open of Monday, September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Periodic reconstitution involves additions and deletions across numerous BSE sectoral, thematic, and strategy indices.
- Affected indices include BSE IPO, BSE India Infrastructure Index, BSE FMCG, BSE Healthcare, BSE IT, BSE Commodities, BSE Consumer Discretionary, BSE Financial Services, and various strategy and smart beta indices.
- Changes become effective from the start of trading on September 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic index maintenance and rebalancing as per standard index governance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Index funds, ETFs, and market participants tracking these BSE indices must align their portfolios with the updated index constituents before the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Rebalancing trades may lead to increased volume and volatility in the added and dropped stocks leading up to the effective date.
