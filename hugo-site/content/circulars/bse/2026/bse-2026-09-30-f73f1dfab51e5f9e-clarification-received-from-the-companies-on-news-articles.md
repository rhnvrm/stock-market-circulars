## Summary

The BSE sought clarification from various listed companies regarding news articles appearing across newspapers, websites, and television channels to ensure investor awareness and accuracy. Replies have been received from seven companies, while responses from two companies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- Clarifications received from Rentomojo Ltd, Sun TV Network Ltd, Tata Chemicals Ltd, Tata Investment Corporation Ltd, Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd, Tata Technologies Ltd, and Tata Teleservices (Maharashtra) Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from Adani Enterprises Ltd and SpiceJet Ltd.
- Issued by the Surveillance department of BSE Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to clarify news reports as mandated by exchange surveillance mechanisms to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine disclosure update with minimal direct operational or market impact.
