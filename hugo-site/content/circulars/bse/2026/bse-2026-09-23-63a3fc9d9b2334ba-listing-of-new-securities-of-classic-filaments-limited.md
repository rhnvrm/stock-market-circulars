## Summary

Classic Filaments Limited has received listing and trading permission from BSE for 26,94,316 new equity shares issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Classic Filaments Limited (Scrip Code: 540310)
- Security Details: 26,94,316 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 41.50/- to Non-Promoters on a preferential basis.
- Issue Price: Rs. 51.50/-
- ISIN: INE181U01018
- Effective Date of Trading: September 24, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 6113351 to 8807666
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 06/04/2027 for the entire quantity of 26,94,316 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and verify distinct numbers as specified.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 18/08/2026
- Listing/Trading Effective Date: 24/09/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 06/04/2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Classic Filaments Limited with standard lock-in restrictions for preferential shares.
