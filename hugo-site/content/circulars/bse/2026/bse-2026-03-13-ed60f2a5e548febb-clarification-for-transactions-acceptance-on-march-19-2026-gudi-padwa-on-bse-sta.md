## Summary

BSE has issued a clarification on the acceptance and processing of mutual fund transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform for March 19, 2026, on account of the Gudi Padwa holiday. The circular details the modified timelines for subscription NAV applicability, unit allotment, and redemption payout dates across Equity, Debt, Liquid (L0), and Liquid (T1) categories.

## Key Points

- Clarifies transaction processing schedules for March 18 and March 19, 2026.
- Outlines specific rules for Equity, Debt, Liquid (L0), and Liquid (T1) mutual fund schemes.
- Details changes to NAV applicability, unit allotment dates, and redemption payout timelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Operational schedule update only).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, mutual fund distributors, and investors must take note of the revised settlement and NAV applicability timelines for transactions executed on March 18 and March 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **March 18, 2026**: Pre-holiday transaction date with regular/adjusted timelines.
- **March 19, 2026**: Gudi Padwa holiday; specific modified settlement and NAV applicability timelines apply.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth processing and transparency for mutual fund transactions during the holiday period, minimizing settlement confusion for investors and intermediaries.
