## Summary

BSE has released the settlement programme and calendar for the Futures and Options (F&O) segment covering the period from October 1, 2026, to October 31, 2026, under Notice No. 20260909-44.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules for the F&O segment for October 2026.
- Issued by the BSECL Department.
- Refers to the attached SettCalendarFnO document for detailed daily settlement timelines.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must adhere to the specified settlement dates and timelines for derivatives transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Period Start Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Period End Date:** October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for participants trading in the derivatives segment during October 2026.
