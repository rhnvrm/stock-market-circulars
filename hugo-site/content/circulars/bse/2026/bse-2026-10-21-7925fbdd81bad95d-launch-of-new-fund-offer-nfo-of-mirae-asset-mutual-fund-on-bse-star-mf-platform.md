## Summary

Mirae Asset Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Mirae Asset Fixed Maturity Plan - Series VI (3 to 6 Months) on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO runs from October 22, 2026, to October 27, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Offerings: Regular and Direct plans (Growth and IDCW Payout options) under Series VI - 3 to 6 Months.
- NFO Window: October 22, 2026, to October 27, 2026 (extended till 6:00 PM on October 27, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 28, 2026.
- ISINs: INF769K01RX6, INF769K01RY4, INF769K01RZ1, INF769K01SA2.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported to RTA/AMC with an 'N' flag.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 27, 2026, to be eligible for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 22, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: October 27, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Funds Pay-in / Clear Funds Deadline: October 27, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for trading members and investors participating in the debt fund NFO via the BSE StAR MF platform.
