## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Emkay Global Financial Services Ltd. (Scrip Code - 532737) effective from September 9, 2026, following the conversion of warrants issued to promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Emkay Global Financial Services Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 532737
- ISIN: INE296H01011
- Securities Issued: 1,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 229.50/- (Issue Price: Rs. 239.50/- per share)
- Allotment Basis: Preferential basis to promoters upon conversion of warrants
- Effective Date of Trading: September 9, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 27230132 to 27330131
- Lock-in Details: 1,00,000 shares locked-in up to 10/03/2028

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and depository guidelines for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 25/06/2026
- Effective Date of Listing/Trading: 09/09/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 10/03/2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine equity expansion for Emkay Global Financial Services Ltd. via preferential warrant conversion with standard lock-in stipulations.
