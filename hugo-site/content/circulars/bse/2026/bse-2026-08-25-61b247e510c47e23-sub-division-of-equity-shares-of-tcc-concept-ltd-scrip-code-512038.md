## Summary

TCC Concept Ltd (Scrip Code: 512038) has fixed September 4, 2026, as the Record Date for the sub-division of its equity shares. Existing equity shares with a face value of Rs. 10/- each will be sub-divided into five equity shares with a face value of Rs. 2/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TCC Concept Ltd
- Scrip Code: 512038
- Record Date: 04/09/2026
- Sub-Division Ratio: One equity share of Rs. 10/- each into five equity shares of Rs. 2/- each
- Effective Date: 04/09/2026
- Notice Reference: DR-709/2026-2027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are informed that the old ISIN No. INE887D01016 (Rs. 10/- paid-up) will not be valid for transactions done on the Exchange on or after 04/09/2026.
- The new ISIN Number for the Rs. 2/- paid-up shares will be communicated via a separate notice.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 4, 2026
- Effective Date: September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders will receive five shares of Rs. 2/- face value for every one share of Rs. 10/- face value held as of the record date, with a corresponding adjustment in market price.
