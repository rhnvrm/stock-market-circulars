## Summary

HEG Limited is demerging its graphite electrodes business into HEG Graphite Limited, effective from the start of trading hours on Monday, September 07, 2026. Shareholders of HEG Limited will receive 1 equity share of HEG Graphite Limited for every 1 equity share held. Additionally, HEG Graphite Limited will be added to several BSE indices at zero price on the effective date.

## Key Points

- Demerger of HEG Limited's graphite electrodes business into HEG Graphite Limited.
- Share exchange ratio: 1 equity share of HEG Graphite Limited for every 1 equity share held in HEG Limited.
- Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS) scheduled on Monday, September 07, 2026, for price discovery of HEG Limited post-demerger.
- HEG Graphite Limited to be added to multiple BSE indices at zero price without divisor adjustment effective September 07, 2026.

[None specified other than index composition updates and special trading sessions]

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors need to account for the demerger ratio and index inclusion adjustments effective from September 07, 2026.

- Notice Date: September 02, 2026
- Close of Trading (for index addition reference): September 04, 2026
- Effective Date / SPOS Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Significant impact on index composition across multiple BSE indices (including BSE 500, BSE SMALLCAP, BSE ALLCAP, etc.) and stock price adjustment for HEG Limited following the demerger and Special Pre-Open Session.
