## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund is launching the UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Index Fund NFO (Regular and Direct Plans - Growth) with an SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 31, 2026, to September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Details**: UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Index Fund (Regular Plan - Growth: ISIN INF789F1AH59; Direct Plan - Growth: ISIN INF789F1AH67).
- **NFO Window**: August 31, 2026, to September 11, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date**: September 17, 2026.
- **Extended Window**: Lumpsum purchases accepted until midnight on September 11, 2026. SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by 3:00 PM on September 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to the RTA/AMC.
- Payment initiation remains pending until 3:00 PM on the last day of funds pay-out or T+5 days.
- Clear funds must reach the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 17, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date**: August 31, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date**: September 11, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date**: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors access to the new UTI index fund scheme on the BSE StAR MF platform with standard operational guidelines for NFO processing.
