## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Waterways Leisure Tourism Ltd has fixed a record date for the sub-division of its equity shares. The face value of the equity shares is being sub-divided from Rs. 10/- each to Re. 1/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Waterways Leisure Tourism Ltd
- Scrip Code: 544802
- Existing Face Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- Sub-Divided Face Value: Re. 1/- per share
- Record Date: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The existing ISIN (INE0LZF01013) of Rs. 10/- paid-up will cease to be valid for transactions on the Exchange on or after August 26, 2026.
- A new ISIN number for the Re. 1/- paid-up shares will be notified separately.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the change in face value and the invalidation of the old ISIN for transactions executed on or after the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Record Date: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date / W.E.F.: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Stock split/sub-division modifies the liquidity and price per share proportionately, requiring traders and investors to ensure correct ISIN usage for settlements.
