## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of trading in debentures of Prachay Capital Limited (Scrip Code 976848) with effect from September 21, 2026, following confirmation that debenture holders did not exercise the put option for redemption.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260918-18
- Scrip Code: 976848 (Prachay Capital Limited)
- Effective Date for Resumption: September 21, 2026
- Previous reference: Exchange Notice No. 20260902-53 dated September 02, 2026 (Record Date: September 04, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note that trading in the debentures will resume as per the specified date.

## Important Dates

- September 04, 2026: Record Date for Redemption and Payment of Interest
- September 18, 2026: Notice issuance date
- September 21, 2026: Resumption of Trading

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification ensuring market participants are informed about the availability of the debentures for trading.
