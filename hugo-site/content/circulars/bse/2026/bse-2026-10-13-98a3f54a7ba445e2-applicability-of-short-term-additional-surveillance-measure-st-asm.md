## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II on specified securities based on market surveillance criteria. The surveillance actions become effective from October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in ST-ASM Stage I attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Securities in ST-ASM Stage II attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on October 14, 2026, and new positions created from October 15, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely for market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II framework provisions for shortlisted securities in conjunction with existing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100% as applicable) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements will raise capital requirements for traders holding or creating positions in shortlisted securities, potentially subduing volatility and liquidity in those scrips.
