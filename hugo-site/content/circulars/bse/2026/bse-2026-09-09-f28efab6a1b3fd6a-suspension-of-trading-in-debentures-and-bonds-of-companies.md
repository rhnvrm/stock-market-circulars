## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and/or redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading members are advised not to deal in these debentures/bonds as per the specified record and ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260909-18
- Segment: Debt Segment
- Purpose: Payment of Interest / Redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures/Bonds
- Action Required: Trading members are advised not to deal in the listed debentures/bonds from the respective ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the advisory not to trade in the specified debentures/bonds of the companies starting from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 09/09/2026
- Record Dates and Ex-Dates range from 10/09/2026 to 23/09/2026 depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of trading in specific debt instruments for the affected companies to facilitate interest payment or redemption processing.
