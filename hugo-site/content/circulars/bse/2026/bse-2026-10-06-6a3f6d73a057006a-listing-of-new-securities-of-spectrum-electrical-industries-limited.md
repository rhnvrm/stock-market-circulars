## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for new equity shares issued by Spectrum Electrical Industries Limited on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from October 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- 13,73,625 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1,992/- (Issue Price: Rs. 2,002/- per share).
- Issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.
- Distinctive Numbers: 15713841 to 17087465.
- Date of Allotment: September 11, 2026.
- ISIN: INE01EO01010.
- Scrip Code: 544386.
- Lock-in Period: Locked-in up to April 10, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the listing and trading permission effective from October 7, 2026, and adhere to the lock-in restrictions specified.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 11, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: October 7, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry: April 10, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of paid-up capital via preferential allotment of equity shares.
