## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the revised allotment date and operational instructions for the New Fund Offer (NFO) of Whiteoak Capital Dividend Yield Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, referencing a prior notice from August 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- The tentative allotment date for Whiteoak Capital Dividend Yield Fund NFO is revised to August 28, 2026 (from August 31, 2026).
- Clear funds must be available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on August 27, 2026.
- Transactions with uninitiated client funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with an 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Switch transactions are accepted subject to receipt of funds under the NFO scheme prior to the allotment date.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure clear funds are available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on August 27, 2026, and that SIP/XSIP registrations are authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date.

## Important Dates

- NFO Closure / Extension Date: August 24, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: August 27, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational timelines for members and investors participating in the Whiteoak Capital Dividend Yield Fund NFO are adjusted to reflect the revised allotment schedule.
