## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of three Treasury Bills (T-Bills)—91 Day, 182 Day, and 364 Day—scheduled for September 09, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Government of India announced the auction of three Treasury Bills on September 09, 2026.
- Bidding is available through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.
- Securities offered: 91 Day T-Bill (9,000 Cr), 182 Day T-Bill (8,000 Cr), and 364 Day T-Bill (7,000 Cr).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; issued in continuation of Exchange circular no. 20180423-42 regarding non-competitive bidding for Government Securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and direct investors must adhere to the prescribed timelines for bid collection and settlement through the NCB-GSec module.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** September 07, 2026 (10:00 AM onwards)
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors):** September 08, 2026 (till 5:00 PM)
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** September 09, 2026 (till 8:00 AM)
- **Auction Date:** September 09, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates smooth participation of non-competitive bidders and direct investors in government treasury bill auctions via the exchange infrastructure.
