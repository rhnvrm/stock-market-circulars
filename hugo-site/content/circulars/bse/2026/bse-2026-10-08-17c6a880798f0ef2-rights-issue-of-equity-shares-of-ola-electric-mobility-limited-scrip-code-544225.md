## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the rights issue of equity shares by Ola Electric Mobility Limited (Scrip Code: 544225). The company has fixed October 13, 2026, as the record date for determining eligible shareholders entitled to participate in the rights issue.

## Key Points

- **Issue Ratio:** 2 (Two) Equity Shares for every 25 (Twenty-Five) Equity Shares held as on the record date.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 27/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- + Premium Rs. 17/-).
- **Payment Structure:** Rs. 16.20 per share payable on application, and Rs. 10.80 per share by way of First and Final Call (expected to be completed on or prior to October 31, 2027).
- **Record Date:** October 13, 2026.
- **Ex-Rights Date:** October 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action notice regarding capital raising through a rights issue.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure that transactions in the equity shares of Ola Electric Mobility Limited are conducted on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified ex-right date.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Date:** October 13, 2026
- **First and Final Call Completion (Expected):** On or prior to October 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders will receive rights entitlements in proportion to their holding, allowing them to subscribe to additional shares at the specified issue price. The stock price will undergo standard price adjustment on the ex-rights date.
