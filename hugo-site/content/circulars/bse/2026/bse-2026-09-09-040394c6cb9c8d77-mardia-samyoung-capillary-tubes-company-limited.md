## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that 6,07,85,000 new equity shares of Mardia Samyoung Capillary Tubes Company Limited (Scrip Code: 513544, ISIN: INE277E01026) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, September 10, 2026. These shares are issued at a premium of Rs. 3.50/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 13.50/- per share) to promoters and non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Mardia Samyoung Capillary Tubes Company Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 513544
- **ISIN:** INE277E01026
- **Security Details:** 6,07,85,000 Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 3.50/- each.
- **Effective Date:** September 10, 2026
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.
