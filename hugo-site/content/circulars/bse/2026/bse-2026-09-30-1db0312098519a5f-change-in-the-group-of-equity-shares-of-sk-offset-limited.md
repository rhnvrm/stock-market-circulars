## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of S.K. Offset Limited (Scrip Code: 544947) will be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective Thursday, October 15, 2026, in continuation of Notice No. 20260929-54 dated September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: S.K. Offset Limited
- Scrip Code: 544947
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine segment transfer by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems to reflect the change in segment and settlement category for S.K. Offset Limited effective from October 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shifting from the Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment allows normal settlement and liquidity for equity shares of S.K. Offset Limited, facilitating regular trading and settlement without compulsory trade-for-trade restrictions.
