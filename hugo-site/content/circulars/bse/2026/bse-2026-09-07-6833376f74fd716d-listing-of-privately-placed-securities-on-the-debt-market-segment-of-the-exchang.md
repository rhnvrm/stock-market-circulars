## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers for multiple entities on the debt market segment effective September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are issued on a private placement basis and traded in dematerialised form.
- Standard denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Listed issuers include Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited, Bajaj Housing Finance Limited, DSP Finance Private Limited, Jio Credit Limited, and Birla Group Holdings Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard debt segment guidelines and dematerialised settlement.

## Important Dates

- Allotment dates range around September 04, 2026, to September 07, 2026.
- Redemptions scheduled between December 02, 2026, and December 11, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update for debt segment participants.
