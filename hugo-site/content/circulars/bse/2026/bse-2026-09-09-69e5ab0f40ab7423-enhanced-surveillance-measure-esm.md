## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice detailing updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying inclusion criteria or moving between stages will attract specific margin requirements, trading segment shifts, and price band modifications effective September 10 and 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion and will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 11, 2026, on open positions as of September 10, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Selected securities are moved to lower Stage I of the ESM framework w.e.f. September 10, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 10, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 11, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance framework adjustments including margin increases, movement across ESM stages, and segment reclassifications.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (100%), trade-for-trade settlement restrictions, and price band limits for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date (Stage Changes / Price Bands):** September 10, 2026
- **Effective Date (Margins / Settlement Shift):** September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement terms for specified scrips will be constrained due to increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade segment transfers.
