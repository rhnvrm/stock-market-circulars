## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the listing and admission for trading of 99,00,990 equity shares of Arvind Ltd. resulting from a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP), effective from August 10, 2026.

- **Company Name:** Arvind Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 500101
- **ISIN:** INE034A01011
- **Security Details:** 99,00,990 Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each allotted to QIBs pursuant to QIP.
- **Effective Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** August 5, 2026
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 505/-
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 262140741 to 272041730
