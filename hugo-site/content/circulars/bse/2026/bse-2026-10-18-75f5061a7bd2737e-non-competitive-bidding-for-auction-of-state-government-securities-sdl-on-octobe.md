## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for October 19, 2026, following the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) press release dated October 15, 2026. Trading members can participate through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Bidding is facilitated through the NCB-GSec module of the iBBS web-based platform.
- Covers multiple State Government Securities (SDLs) across various states including AP, GA, GJ, JK, MP, MH, ML, PB, RJ, TS, and TR.
- Bid collection for direct investors ended on October 18, 2026, while members can bid until October 19, 2026, 8:00 am.
- Auction Date: October 19, 2026.
- Settlement Date: October 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine issuance and auction facilitation under RBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members participating on behalf of clients or directly must ensure bids are submitted within the prescribed timelines through the NCB-GSec module.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: October 16, 2026 (10:00 am onwards)
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): October 18, 2026 (till 5:00 pm)
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): October 19, 2026 (till 8:00 am)
- Auction Date: October 19, 2026
- Settlement Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants and investors an opportunity to subscribe to State Government Securities through non-competitive bidding without direct impact on equity market segments.
