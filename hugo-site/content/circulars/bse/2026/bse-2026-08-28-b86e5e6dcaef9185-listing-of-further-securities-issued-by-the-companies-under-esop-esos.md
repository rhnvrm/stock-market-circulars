## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further securities issued by 8 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS), effective from Monday, August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 8
- Effective date for trading: August 31, 2026
- Participating companies include ICICI Bank Limited, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, IIFL Capital Services Limited, Infosys Limited, Mahindra Logistics Limited, PG Electroplast Limited, R Systems International Limited, and The Hi-Tech Gears Limited.
- All securities are issued under ESOP / ESOS schemes.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate securities listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the new securities admitted for trading.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase and stock listing resulting from employee stock option exercises across the listed entities.
