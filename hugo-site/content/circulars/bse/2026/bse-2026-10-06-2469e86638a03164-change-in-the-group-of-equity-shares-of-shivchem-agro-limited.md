## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Shivchem Agro Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shivchem Agro Limited
- Scrip Code: 544967
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Wednesday, October 21, 2026
- Continuing from Exchange Notice No. 20261005-27 dated October 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The trading segment for the company's equity shares has been modified from MT Group to M Group.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the change in segment and trading group for settlement and execution purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables normal rolling settlement for the equity shares of Shivchem Agro Limited, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility.
