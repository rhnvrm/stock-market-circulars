## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 40,00,000 equity shares of Samyak International Limited issued on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters, effective from October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Samyak International Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 530025
- **ISIN:** INE607G01011
- **Securities Issued:** 40,00,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each at an issue price of Rs. 17/- (including premium of Rs. 7/-) on a preferential basis.
- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Lock-in Details:** Specific lock-in periods apply to portions of the shares ranging up to May 1, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure adherence to lock-in periods and distribution numbers specified for the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 17, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto Dates:** April 30, 2027; May 1, 2027; May 1, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity capital base of Samyak International Limited through preferential allotment.
