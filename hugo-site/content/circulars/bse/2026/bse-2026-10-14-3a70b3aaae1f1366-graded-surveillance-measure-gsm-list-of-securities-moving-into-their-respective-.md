## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of specific securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective October 15, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and trade-to-trade settlement restrictions.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure move to higher stages of GSM effective October 15, 2026.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade for trade with 5% price band and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value.
- Stage III & IV: Trade for trade with 5% price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week), and ASD of 100% of trade value.
- Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened surveillance measures under the established GSM framework to protect investors and maintain market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, ensure trade-to-trade settlement without netting off, and collect necessary margins and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 14 Oct 2026
- Effective Date: 15 Oct 2026

## Impact Assessment

Heightened margin requirements, trading frequency limitations, and trade-to-trade settlement rules will impact liquidity and price stability for the affected securities.
