## Summary

BSE has issued a circular detailing changes to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, effective March 16, 2026. The circular outlines securities newly shortlisted for ESM, those moving to higher or lower stages, and those moving out of the framework entirely.

## Key Points

- **Shortlisted in ESM:** 7 securities are newly shortlisted under the ESM framework.
- **Movement to Higher Stage:** 2 securities will continue in the ESM framework but move to respective higher stages.
- **Movement to Lower Stage:** 14 securities will continue in the framework but move to respective lower stages.
- **Moving out of ESM:** 17 securities are moving out of the ESM framework, including some transitioning to SMP or IBC frameworks.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance actions under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for specified scrips.
- Adjustments to trading restrictions based on the periodic review of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the security movements into, across, and out of the ESM framework.
- Appropriate margin and trading safeguards apply as per the respective ESM stages.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** March 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the affected securities will be subject to the applicable surveillance mechanisms, margin requirements, and trading frequency restrictions corresponding to their assigned ESM stage.
