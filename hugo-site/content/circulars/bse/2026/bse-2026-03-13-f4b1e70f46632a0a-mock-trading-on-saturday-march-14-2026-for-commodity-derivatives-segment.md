## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are advised to test their connectivity to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site using the specified technical parameters.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Members can connect to the DR site using BOLT TWS or 3rd party trading applications via ETI APIs.
- Details provided for web-based systems including RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, and Extranet.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are encouraged to participate in the mock trading session to ensure seamless connectivity and readiness of their disaster recovery setups.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness check for trading members with no direct impact on live market operations.
