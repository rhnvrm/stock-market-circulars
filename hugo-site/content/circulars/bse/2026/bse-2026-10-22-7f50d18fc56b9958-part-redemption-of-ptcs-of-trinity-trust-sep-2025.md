## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that MITCON Credential Trusteeship Services Ltd has announced a partial redemption and reduced face value for the Pass-through Certificates (PTCs) of Trinity Trust Sep 2025, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TRINITY TRUST SEP 2025
- Scrip Code: 977166
- ISIN: INE2JU715017
- Scrip ID / Symbol: TTS25-11.75%-21-9-27-PTC
- Purpose: Part Redemption of PTCs
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 775.77 per PTC

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a corporate action notice regarding debt securities face value reduction.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised that the PTCs will be traded with the aforementioned reduced face value effective from October 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date for Reduced Face Value Trading: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to holders and traders of the specified Trinity Trust Sep 2025 PTCs, reflecting updated face value and valuation parameters.
