## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities included, moved to lower/higher stages, or exiting the framework. Affected securities will attract a minimum 100% margin, move to the trade-for-trade segment, or be subject to price bands and periodic call auctions as applicable.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 24, 2026, on all open positions as on September 23, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 23, 2026.
- Securities moving to Stage I or shifting out of the framework are detailed in the respective annexures.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework in conjunction with prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the increased margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement shifts, and price band restrictions for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II / Lower Stage moves: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin / Trade-for-Trade shifts: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin obligations and trading restrictions for short-listed scrips, enhancing market integrity and cautioning participants regarding volatility.
