## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the offer to buy (acquisition window) for the takeover of Devinsu Trading Limited. The open offer is made by the acquirers to public shareholders to acquire up to 1,52,880 fully paid equity shares (26% of voting share capital) at Rs 355.00 per share.

## Key Points

- Target Company: Devinsu Trading Limited
- Acquirers: Mr. Jaison Vijay Shah, Mr. Mukesh Kumar Bothra, and Yora Gems & Jewellery Private Limited
- Offer Price: Rs 355.00/- per equity share payable in cash
- Offer Size: Up to 1,52,880 fully paid equity shares (26% of voting share capital)
- Regulatory Basis: SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 (Regulations 3(1) and 4)

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted pursuant to SEBI circulars on the mechanism for acquisition of shares through stock exchange under tender offers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the operational schedule and guidelines for tendering shares through the acquisition window.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 13, 2026
- Offer Period: Monday, August 17, 2026, to Monday, August 31, 2026 (Excluding SEBI Holiday on Wednesday, August 26, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates public shareholders of Devinsu Trading Limited to tender their equity shares in the open offer via the stock exchange acquisition window.
