## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities moving into their respective higher Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages effective from September 07, 2026. Trading members are required to take adequate precautions as these securities will attract enhanced surveillance actions and trade-to-trade settlement with no netting off.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 07, 2026.
- Measures apply to securities moving into higher GSM stages (Stages I, II, III, IV).
- Settlement shall be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities moving into higher GSM stages attract various surveillance actions based on their stage:
- **Stage I:** Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- **Stage II:** Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value by buyers.
- **Stage III:** Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and ASD of 100% of trade value by buyers.
- **Stage IV:** Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week, and ASD of 100% of trade value with no upward movement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the listed securities and ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 04 Sep 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Actions: 07 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity for the affected securities will be significantly restricted due to trade-to-trade settlement, higher margin requirements, and weekly trading frequency limits for higher stages.
