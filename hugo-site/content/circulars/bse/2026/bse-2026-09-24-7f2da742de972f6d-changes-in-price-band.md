## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands for 21 scrips with effect from September 25, 2026. These changes are part of the exchange's surveillance mechanism to curb excessive price volatility in specific equities.

## Key Points

- Total Scrips Affected: 21
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance)
- Revised Price Bands range from 2% to 20% across various scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands for the following scrips have been revised:
- Advance Multitech Ltd (526331): Revised to 20%
- Alexander Stamps And Coin Ltd (511463): Revised to 10%
- Alkali Metals Ltd (533029): Revised to 5%
- Arvaya Healthcare Ltd (524723): Revised to 2%
- Candour Techtex Ltd (522292): Revised to 2%
- Cords Cable Industries Ltd (532941): Revised to 5%
- Cranes Software International Ltd (512093): Revised to 2%
- EKI Energy Services Ltd (543284): Revised to 10%
- Farm Peace Ltd (544905): Revised to 10%
- Hemant Surgical Industries Ltd (543916): Revised to 5%
- IEC Education Ltd (531840): Revised to 10%
- Jainex Aamcol Ltd (505212): Revised to 5%
- Kabra Extrusiontechnik Ltd (524109): Revised to 5%
- Rajasthan Petro Synthetics Ltd (506975): Revised to 2%
- Shining Tools Ltd (544607): Revised to 5%
- SW Investments Ltd (503659): Revised to 2%
- Tahmar Enterprises Ltd (516032): Revised to 5%
- Telogica Ltd (532975): Revised to 2%
- U. H. Zaveri Ltd (541338): Revised to 5%
- Umiya Mobile Ltd (544464): Revised to 10%
- Zelio E Mobility Ltd (544563): Revised to 2%

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure that their trading terminals and risk management systems reflect these changes prior to market open on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members dealing in the affected scrips must adjust their order placement strategies in accordance with the new percentage limits to avoid order rejections or unintended execution limits.
