## Summary

The Exchange has communicated updates from the Department of Telecommunications (DoT) regarding enhancements to the Digital Intelligence Platform (DIP), including a new Stakeholder Feedback facility under SancharSaathi Utilities and updated guidelines for utilizing the Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL) and Reactivation List.

## Key Points

- Introduction of a Stakeholder Feedback facility on the DIP portal to allow regulated entities to share feedback, suggestions, and functional requirements.
- Mandatory daily download and utilization of the Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL) and Reactivation List by stakeholders.
- Clear procedures for verifying mobile numbers against the Reactivation List and performing Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) prior to taking any action.
- Requirement for research analysts and regulated entities to complete onboarding on the DIP portal.

## Regulatory Changes

- Integration of a Stakeholder Feedback facility under the SancharSaathi Utilities section on the DIP portal for continuous platform improvement.
- Revision of the Quick Reference Guide for Banks, Financial Institutions, NBFCs, and SEBI-regulated entities regarding MNRL and Financial Fraud Risk Indicator (FRI) actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated entities must download and use the latest versions of MNRL and Reactivation Lists on a daily basis.
- Verify disconnected mobile numbers against the Reactivation List before taking action; ensure reactivation date is >= disconnection date.
- Take action only on numbers remaining in the effective MNRL (MNRL minus Reactivation List) pertaining to the last 90 days.
- Perform appropriate Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) in line with internal policies and regulatory guidelines before acting on any MNRL data.
- Complete the onboarding process on the DoT Digital Intelligence Platform.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: 03 Sep 2026
- Effective timeframe: Daily updates for MNRL/Reactivation datasets with a 90-day validity window.

## Impact Assessment

Enhances operational protocols for fraud risk management and strengthens cybersecurity compliance for regulated entities interacting with telecom indicators.
