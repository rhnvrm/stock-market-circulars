## Summary

BSE has issued a notice stating that SEBI confirmed compliance by the Noticees with interim order WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-SEC-6/32673/2026-27 dated August 19, 2026, by depositing the requisite impounding amount and creating a lien. Consequently, trading members are instructed to immediately de-freeze the Demat and Trading accounts of the Noticees, subject to continued restrictions regarding Closing Auction Session (CAS).

## Key Points

- Noticees have successfully complied with SEBI Interim Order dated August 19, 2026, by depositing impounding amounts and creating a lien in favor of SEBI.
- Trading members are instructed to immediately de-freeze the Demat and Trading accounts of the Noticees.
- De-freezing is subject to the restriction under Paragraph 98(iii), keeping entities restrained from participating in CAS until further orders.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial relief granted under Paragraph 98(x) of the SEBI Interim Order, lifting specific restrictions (clauses ii, iv, v, vi, vii, and viii) upon crediting of the impounding amount.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must immediately de-freeze the Demat and Trading accounts of the specified Noticees.
- Strict compliance with the ongoing restriction against participation in CAS (Closing Auction Session) must be maintained.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 20, 2026
- Reference Interim Order Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Restores standard trading and Demat account access for the Noticees, excluding participation in the Closing Auction Session (CAS) for SENSEX/derivatives.
