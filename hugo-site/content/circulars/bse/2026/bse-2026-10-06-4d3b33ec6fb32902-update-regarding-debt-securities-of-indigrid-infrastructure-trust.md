## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding updated terms for debt securities of IndiGrid Infrastructure Trust, revising the covenant threshold for non-transmission asset classes.

## Key Points

- Affected Scrip Codes: 975044 (ISIN: INE219X07413) and 974631 (ISIN: INE219X07355).
- Effective Date: October 07, 2026.
- Covenant Update: Asset class limit (besides transmission) increased from 25% to 35% of total InvIT assets.

## Regulatory Changes

- The threshold percentage of InvIT assets for non-transmission asset classes requiring debenture holder approval or prepayment has been raised from 25% to 35%.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuer must seek approval from each Debenture Holder if non-transmission asset class value exceeds 35% of total InvIT assets; otherwise, prepay without prepayment premium with 30 days' notice for dissenting holders.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 07, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Grants increased operational flexibility to IndiGrid Infrastructure Trust up to the new 35% threshold for non-transmission asset investments.
