## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the list of securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from September 11, 2026. Trading members are required to follow specific surveillance actions and margin requirements for these securities.

## Key Points

- Securities are moved to higher stages of GSM effective September 11, 2026.
- Actions include 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement, Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD), and trading restricted to once a week depending on the stage.
- No netting off is allowed as settlement is done on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of the GSM framework provisions as outlined in previous exchange notices, applying stricter surveillance, margin requirements, and trading frequency limitations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the listed securities, ensure compliance with applicable margin and ASD deposits, and note that settlement occurs on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: 11 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding or trading securities moving into GSM stages will experience increased margin requirements, restricted trading frequencies, and trade-for-trade settlement conditions, which may impact liquidity and price volatility.
