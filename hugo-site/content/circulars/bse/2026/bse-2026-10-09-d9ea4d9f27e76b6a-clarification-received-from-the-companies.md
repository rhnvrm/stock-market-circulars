## Summary

BSE has observed significant price and volume movements in certain scrips and sought clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have the latest relevant information. This notice outlines the status of replies received and awaited from various listed entities.

## Key Points

- Clarifications have been received from 11 companies including D.P. Wires Ltd, India Glycols Ltd, and Supreme Infrastructure India Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 15 companies including Deep Polymers Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, and Spright Agro Ltd.
- The exercise is part of routine surveillance measures by BSE to maintain market integrity and investor awareness.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine surveillance notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies notified by the exchange are required to submit clarifications regarding significant price/volume movements in their scrips promptly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 09 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding exchange queries on stock price and volume volatility across multiple listed scrips.
