## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that AvenuesAI Ltd has fixed a record date for the consolidation of its equity shares. Existing ten equity shares of face value Re. 1/- each will be consolidated into one equity share of face value Rs. 10/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: AvenuesAI Ltd
- Scrip Code: 539807
- Record Date: 13/10/2026
- No Dealings From: 13/10/2026
- Action: Consolidation of existing ten equity shares of Re. 1/- each into one equity share of Rs. 10/- each.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the equity shares of the company as per the specified schedule and take note of the share consolidation.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 08/10/2026
- Record Date / No Dealings From: 13/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders of AvenuesAI Ltd will experience a change in share denomination and total share count due to the 10-to-1 share consolidation, effective from the record date.
