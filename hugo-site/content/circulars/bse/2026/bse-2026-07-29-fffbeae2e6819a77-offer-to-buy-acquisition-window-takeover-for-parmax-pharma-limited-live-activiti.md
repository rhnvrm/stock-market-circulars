## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the live activities schedule and operational parameters for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window - Takeover) of shares of Parmax Pharma Limited. The offer is scheduled from Thursday, July 30, 2026, to Wednesday, August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Parmax Pharma Limited
- Security Symbol: PARMAX
- ISIN: INE240T01014
- Scrip Code: 540359
- Offer Size: Up to 23,46,250 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 42.80 per share
- Opening Date: Thursday, 30th July 2026
- Closing Date: Wednesday, 12th August 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Offer Opening Date: 30th July 2026
- Offer Closing Date: 12th August 2026
- Bid Offering Period: 30th July 2026 to 12th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Take-up (Last Day): 12th August 2026 (up to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates shareholders and trading members to participate in the open offer / takeover window for Parmax Pharma Limited smoothly via the iBBS platform.
