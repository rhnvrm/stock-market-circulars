## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund is offering the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Exchange Traded Fund and its Direct Plan - Growth from August 31, 2026, to September 11, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Exchange Traded Fund and UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Exchange Traded Fund - Direct Plan - Growth.
- NFO Period: August 31, 2026, to September 11, 2026 (extended till 6:00 PM on September 11, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to the RTA/AMC.
- Transactions with initiated payments remain pending until 3:00 PM on the last day of funds pay-out or T+5 day, whichever is earlier.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 15, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted during the NFO period subject to clear funds receipt by 6:00 PM on September 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: August 31, 2026
- NFO End Date: September 11, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors with investment access to the UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Exchange Traded Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
