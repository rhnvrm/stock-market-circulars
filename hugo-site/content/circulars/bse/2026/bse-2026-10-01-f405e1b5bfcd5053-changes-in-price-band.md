## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of price bands for 52 equity scrips with effect from October 05, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20261001-62 dated October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 52 scrips across various categories (ranging from 2%, 5%, 10%, to 20%).
- Effective date: October 05, 2026.
- Applies to Trading Members of the Exchange in the Equity segment.

## Regulatory Changes

Adjustment of daily price limits / price bands for specified scrips to curb excessive volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading systems and terminals with the revised price bands for the affected scrips before market hours on October 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the listed scrips will be subject to the new price band limits, affecting maximum permissible intraday price movements.
