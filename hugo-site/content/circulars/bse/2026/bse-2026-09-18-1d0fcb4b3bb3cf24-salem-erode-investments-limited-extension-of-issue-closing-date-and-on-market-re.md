## Summary

Salem Erode Investments Limited has announced an extension of its ongoing Rights Issue timelines, including the issue closing date and the last date for on-market renunciation.

## Key Points

- Rights Issue closing date extended from Friday, September 25, 2026 to Thursday, October 01, 2026.
- Last date for On-Market Renunciation for Rights Entitlements extended from Monday, September 21, 2026 to Monday, September 28, 2026.
- Issue Opening Date remains Tuesday, September 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must take note of the revised dates for executing on-market renunciations and participating in the rights issue.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: Tuesday, September 15, 2026
- Last Date for On-Market Renunciation: Monday, September 28, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: Thursday, October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with additional time to trade their rights entitlements and subscribe to the rights issue of Salem Erode Investments Limited.
