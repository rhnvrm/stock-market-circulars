## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of specified securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective September 28, 2026, aimed at enhancing market integrity and protecting investors from excessive volatility.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure shall move to higher stages of GSM.
- Effective date of implementation: September 28, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, including 100% margin rates, price bands of 5% or lower, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement for affected securities shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of enhanced surveillance measures across Stages I, II, III, and IV as per the provisions of the GSM Framework established on February 23, 2017.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to exercise adequate caution while executing trades in the listed securities, comply with trade-to-trade settlement restrictions, and deposit required margins and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) as applicable.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 25 September 2026
- Effective Date: 28 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities are likely to be constrained due to trade-for-trade settlement rules, mandatory ASD requirements, and restricted trading frequencies in higher stages.
