## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade in new securities of Sun Pharma Advanced Research Company Limited (Scrip Code: 532872) effective from September 30, 2026. The listing covers 16 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued pursuant to a scheme that were previously kept in abeyance.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Sun Pharma Advanced Research Company Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 532872
- **ISIN:** INE232I01014
- **Securities:** 16 Equity Shares of Re. 1/- each (pursuant to Scheme) kept in abeyance
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 119203 to 119218
- **Effective Date:** Wednesday, September 30, 2026
- **Form of Issuance:** Physical form
- **Ranking:** Pari passu with existing equity shares of the company

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is an operational listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the listing and trading permission of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing of 16 physical equity shares kept in abeyance has negligible impact on market liquidity or trading operations.
