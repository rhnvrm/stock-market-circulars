## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade in 5,999 new equity shares of Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited resulting from the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares on a rights basis, effective from August 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 541540
- **ISIN:** INE624Z01016
- **Effective Date:** August 3, 2026
- **Securities Details:** 5,999 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up on rights basis.
- **Status:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
