## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction with respect to Rolling Settlement No. DR-699/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offers will be accepted on trading terminals by the Exchange on August 24, 2026.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians should check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the "Auction offer Entry Screen".

## Important Dates

- **Auction Offer Entry:** August 24, 2026, 02:00 PM to 02:45 PM
- **Auction Matching:** August 24, 2026, 02:45 PM to 03:00 PM
- **Delivery Statement & Report:** August 24, 2026, 03:00 PM to 03:30 PM
- **Money Statement & Final Reports:** August 24, 2026, 03:35 PM to 05:30 PM
- **Pay-in of Auction:** August 25, 2026, at 08:00 AM

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for clearing short deliveries in rolling settlements.
