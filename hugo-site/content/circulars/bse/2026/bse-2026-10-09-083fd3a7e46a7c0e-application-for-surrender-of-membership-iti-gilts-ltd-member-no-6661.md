## Summary

ITI Gilts Ltd. (Member no. 6661) has formally applied to surrender its trading membership of the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) in accordance with the exchange's rules, bye-laws, and regulations.

## Key Points

- Member Name: ITI Gilts Ltd.
- Member Number: 6661
- Department: Membership Department
- Objection Deadline: October 23, 2026 (within 15 days of the notice date)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative surrender of exchange membership.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members wishing to raise any objections to the surrender of membership must submit their written objections to the Membership Department on or before October 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 9, 2026
- Objection Deadline: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact, as this is a routine surrender of membership by an individual trading member.
