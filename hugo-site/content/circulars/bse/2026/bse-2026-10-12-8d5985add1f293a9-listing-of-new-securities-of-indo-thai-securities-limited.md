## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Indo Thai Securities Limited (Scrip Code: 533676) with effect from October 13, 2026, following a preferential issue upon warrant conversion.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Indo Thai Securities Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 533676
- **ISIN:** INE337M01021
- **Security Details:** 17,20,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 49/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 50/-) to non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 131118191 to 132838190
- **Date of Allotment:** 13/07/2026
- **Effective Date:** October 13, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants take note of the new securities listing and lock-in details.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Trading:** October 13, 2026
- **Lock-in Period:** Locked-in up to 30/04/2027 for 17,20,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 131118191 - 132838190).

## Impact Assessment

Standard preferential allotment and warrant conversion listing on the exchange with no adverse market impact.
