## Summary

The BSE has issued guidelines pursuant to amendments to SEBI KYC Registration Agency (KRA) Regulations, 2011, regarding clients whose KYC records are not validated or marked as 'On Hold'. Effective October 31, 2026, clients with non-validated KYC statuses uploaded between September 1, 2026, and September 30, 2026, will be restricted from trading or squaring off open positions until compliance is achieved.

## Key Points

- Trading members must ensure all client KYCs are uploaded to KRAs, permitting only 'KYC Registered' or 'KYC Validated' clients to trade.
- Clients with KYCs marked 'On Hold' (both Aadhaar and Non-Aadhaar based OVD) uploaded between September 1, 2026, and September 30, 2026, are barred from trading and squaring off open positions w.e.f. October 31, 2026.
- Non-compliant PANs will be flagged as 'Not Permitted to Trade' by the Exchange starting October 31, 2026.
- Compliant PANs will regain trading permissions on T+1 day based on KRA updates received on T day.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of stricter validation requirements mandated by SEBI circulars concerning KRA KYC processes, risk management frameworks, and centralized mechanisms for reporting investor demise.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must review downloaded records of PANs marked 'On Hold' and 'Not Available' via the UCC path (`UCC → Download UCCs Having Invalid PAN (by KRA)`).
- Ensure clients complete KYC validation requirements to avoid trading suspensions.
- Access non-compliant client lists at `EQ\TRANSACTION\Oct-2026\14-10-2026\Non_Validated_Clients_by_KRAs_Clgno_xxxx.TXT`.

## Important Dates

- **October 14, 2026:** Notice issuance date.
- **October 31, 2026:** Effective date from which non-validated clients are barred from trading and squaring off open positions.

## Impact Assessment

Clients and trading members with pending or unvalidated KYC statuses face immediate trading suspensions and position square-off restrictions, necessitating urgent remediation and document resubmission.
