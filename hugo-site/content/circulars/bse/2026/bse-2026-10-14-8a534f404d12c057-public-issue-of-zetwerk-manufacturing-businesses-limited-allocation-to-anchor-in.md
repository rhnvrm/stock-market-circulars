BSE has informed trading members that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited, 14,90,88,220 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 151/- per equity share on October 14, 2026.

- Company Name: Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited
- Anchor Investors Portion Subscription: 14,90,88,220 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 151/- per equity share
- Notice Date: October 14, 2026
