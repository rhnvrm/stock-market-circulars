## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the list of securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective September 10, 2026. These securities will attract specific surveillance actions including enhanced margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 10, 2026.
- Affected securities will transition to higher stages of the GSM framework (Stages I, II, III, and IV).
- Surveillance actions include applicable margin rates of 100%, trade-for-trade settlement with lower price bands, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) ranging from 50% to 100% of the trade value.
- No netting off shall be allowed as settlement is conducted on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are subjected to stricter surveillance measures pursuant to the GSM framework published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent updates.
- Stage-specific restrictions are enforced ranging from 100% margin rates and 5% price bands to weekly trading frequency limits (every Monday) and 100% ASD without upward price movement for Stage IV.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the listed securities.
- Buyers are required to deposit the mandated Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) where applicable.
- Settlement must strictly follow trade-to-trade mechanisms with no netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 09, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Measures: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities are likely to be constrained due to stringent margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and restrictions on trading frequency for higher stages.
