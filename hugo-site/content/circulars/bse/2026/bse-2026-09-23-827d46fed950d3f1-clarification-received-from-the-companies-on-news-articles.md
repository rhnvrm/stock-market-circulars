## Summary

BSE has sought clarification from SpiceJet Ltd and Whirlpool of India Ltd regarding various news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels to ensure accuracy and protect investors.

## Key Points

- Exchange sought clarification to verify news articles.
- Companies involved: SpiceJet Ltd (Scrip Code: 500285) and Whirlpool of India Ltd (Scrip Code: 500238).
- Reply is currently awaited from the mentioned companies.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to clarify news reports upon exchange request to maintain transparency and market integrity.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 23 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct operational impact; awaiting company clarification responses for further market direction.
