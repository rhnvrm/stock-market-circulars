## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movements in several scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies. Replies received from several companies are attached to the notice, while replies from a few others are still awaited.

## Key Points

- **Companies with Replies Received:**
  1. Billwin Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 543209)
  2. Corona Remedies Ltd (Scrip Code: 544644)
  3. ESDS Software Solution Ltd (Scrip Code: 544898)
  4. India Nippon Electricals Ltd (Scrip Code: 532240)
  5. Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd (Scrip Code: 500109)
  6. Mukta Agriculture Ltd (Scrip Code: 535204)
  7. NPR Finance Ltd (Scrip Code: 530127)
  8. NR Agarwal Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 516082)

- **Companies with Replies Awaited:**
  1. CMX Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 532217)
  2. Deep Polymers Ltd (Scrip Code: 541778)
  3. Mahesh Developers Ltd (Scrip Code: 542677)
  4. Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Ltd (Scrip Code: 544510)
  5. Nirmitee Robotics India Ltd (Scrip Code: 543194)
  6. Sera Investments & Finance India Ltd (Scrip Code: 512399)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine surveillance disclosure notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely clarify significant price and volume movements upon inquiry by the Exchange to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 10 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market integrity and investor awareness by disclosing corporate clarifications regarding unusual market activity.
