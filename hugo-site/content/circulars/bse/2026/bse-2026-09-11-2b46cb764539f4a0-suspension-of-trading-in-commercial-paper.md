## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of commercial papers. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in commercial papers due to redemption record dates.
- Record Date and Ex-Date for multiple companies set for September 16, 2026 (and September 15, 2026 for Standard Chartered Securities).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the listed companies from the specified ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / Ex-Date: September 15, 2026 and September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification for debt segment trading members ensuring smooth redemption and settlement of commercial papers.
