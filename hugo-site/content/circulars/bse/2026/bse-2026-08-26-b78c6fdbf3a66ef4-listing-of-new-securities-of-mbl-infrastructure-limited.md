## Summary

BSE has announced that 19,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares of MBL Infrastructure Limited issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to an NCLT resolution plan will be listed and permitted for trading effective August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** MBL Infrastructure Limited (Scrip Code: 533152)
- **Security Details:** 19,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued on a preferential basis to promoters.
- **Basis:** Pursuant to the Resolution Plan approved by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Kolkata Bench.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **ISIN:** INE912H01013

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares are subject to lock-in requirements as specified.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** April 11, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** August 31, 2027 (for 19,00,000 shares, Dist. Nos. 152529257 to 154429256)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing of shares resulting from a corporate resolution plan with standard lock-in provisions.
