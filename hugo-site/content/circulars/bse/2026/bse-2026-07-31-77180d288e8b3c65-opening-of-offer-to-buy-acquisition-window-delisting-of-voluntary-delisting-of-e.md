## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the offer to buy for the voluntary delisting of equity shares of Nitin Castings Limited. The acquirers (Mr. Nirmal B Kedia, Mr. Nitin S Kedia, and M/s. Citrus Castings Private Limited) are offering to acquire 14,70,894 equity shares (representing 28.61% of the equity share capital) held by public shareholders at a floor price of Rs. 273.36 per share through the Reverse Book-Building Process.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Nitin Castings Limited
- **Acquirers:** Mr. Nirmal B Kedia, Mr. Nitin S Kedia, and M/s. Citrus Castings Private Limited
- **Offer Size:** 14,70,894 Equity Shares (28.61% of equity share capital)
- **Offer/Floor Price:** Rs. 273.36/- per share aggregating up to Rs. 40.20 Crores
- **Mechanism:** Reverse Book-Building Process (RBBP) via Acquisition Window
- **Bidding Period:** Wednesday, August 05, 2026, to Tuesday, August 11, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021, and related SEBI circulars on mechanisms for acquisition of shares through stock exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must adhere to the tender-offer guidelines and acquisition window timelines for delisting.
- Public shareholders wishing to participate must tender their shares through the designated acquisition window during the bidding period.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** August 05, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Results in the potential delisting of Nitin Castings Limited from the exchange upon successful completion of the reverse book-building process and compliance with delisting regulations.
