## Summary

BSE has informed market participants about the listing and trading permission granted to 13,68,21,382 equity shares of HEG Advanced Materials Limited (Scrip Code: 509631) effective from October 9, 2026. The shares are issued pursuant to the Composite Scheme of Arrangement involving HEG Advanced Materials Limited, HEG Graphite Limited, and Bhilwara Energy Limited.

## Key Points

- Company Name: HEG Advanced Materials Limited (formerly HEG Limited)
- Scrip Code: 509631
- ISIN Number: INE545A01024
- Number of Securities: 13,68,21,382 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each
- Distinctive Numbers: 192977531 to 329798912
- Date of Allotment: September 8, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: October 9, 2026
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory policy changes; this is a routine corporate action listing notice under the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and SEBI regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure systems are updated with the new ISIN and security details for trading and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 8, 2026
- Notice Date: October 8, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the total listed equity base of HEG Advanced Materials Limited by 13,68,21,382 shares.
- Enables liquidity and trading for shareholders receiving shares under the composite scheme of arrangement.
