## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 8,04,000 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Vans Electroengineerings Limited at a price of Rs 118 per equity share on September 28, 2026.

- Company Name: Vans Electroengineerings Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue / IPO
- Investor Category: Anchor Investors (AIs)
- Number of Shares Allocated: 8,04,000 Equity Shares
- Issue/Allocation Price: Rs 118/- per equity share
- Date of Notice: September 28, 2026
