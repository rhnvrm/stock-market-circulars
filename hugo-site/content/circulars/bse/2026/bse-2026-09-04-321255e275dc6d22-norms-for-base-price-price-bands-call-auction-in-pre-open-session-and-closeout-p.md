## Summary

BSE has issued revised norms regarding base price calculation, price bands, pre-open session call auctions, and close-out procedures for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs). These changes take effect from Monday, September 7, 2026, with mock trading scheduled for September 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Base Price for ETFs:** Determined based on the T-1 day closing price (last 30 minutes VWAP). Fallbacks include LTP or closing NAV if no trades occurred.
- **Equity and Debt ETFs:** Initial price bands of +/- 10% which can be flexed up to +/- 20% after a cooling period with no upper/lower caps on flexing frequency.
- **Overnight and Liquid ETFs:** Fixed price band of +/- 5%.
- **Commodity ETFs (Gold/Silver):** Dynamic price bands with an initial +/- 6% band, flexed by 3% after a cooling-off period. Introduction of call auction in the pre-open session.
- **Close-Out Procedure:** Applies to Overnight and Liquid ETFs based on the higher of the highest price recorded or 5% above the latest available closing price.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated parameters for price bands and base price calculations for different categories of ETFs.
- Introduction of pre-open session call auctions for Gold and Silver Commodity ETFs.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must update their trading systems and parameters to accommodate the revised ETF price bands, base price rules, and pre-open session procedures ahead of the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** September 5, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances price discovery and risk management for Exchange Traded Funds by refining volatility bands and standardizing close-out procedures across equity, debt, commodity, and liquid ETFs.
