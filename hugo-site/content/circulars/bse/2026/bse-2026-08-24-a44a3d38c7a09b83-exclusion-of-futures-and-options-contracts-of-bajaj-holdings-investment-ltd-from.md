## Summary

BSE has announced the exclusion of Futures and Options (F&O) contracts of Bajaj Holdings & Investment Ltd from its Equity Derivatives Segment in accordance with SEBI guidelines regarding the eligibility criteria of stocks.

## Key Points

- Futures and Options contracts for Bajaj Holdings & Investment Ltd (Scrip Code: 500490, Asset Code: BHIL) are being discontinued from the Equity Derivatives segment.
- No new expiry month contracts will be generated for the security effective October 30, 2026.
- Existing unexpired contracts will continue to trade until their respective expiry dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Discontinuation of derivative contracts based on SEBI eligibility criteria review.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the phase-out schedule and ensure no new contracts are initiated beyond the allowed expiry months.

## Important Dates

- August 27, 2026: Expiry of August 2026 contract
- September 24, 2026: Expiry of September 2026 contract
- October 29, 2026: Expiry of October 2026 contract
- October 30, 2026: Discontinuation date with no contracts available for trading

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding open positions in Bajaj Holdings & Investment Ltd F&O must square off or hold until the respective expiry dates through October 2026.
