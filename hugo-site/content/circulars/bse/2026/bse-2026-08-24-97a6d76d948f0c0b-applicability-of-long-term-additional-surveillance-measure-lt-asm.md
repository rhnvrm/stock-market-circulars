## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework.

## Key Points

- 100% margins are applicable effective August 27, 2026, on all open positions as of August 26, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for specific securities.
- Securities moving to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework or being transferred to T / XT / MT / TS groups are subject to lower price bands and group transfers effective August 25 and August 27, 2026.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and serves as a market surveillance measure rather than an adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins on open and new positions for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Re-routing of certain securities into T / XT / MT / TS Groups and movement of others out of the LT-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure adequate margin maintenance (100%) for open and new positions in securities specified under Annexure I, and comply with applicable price band restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Higher Stages / Price Bands / Moving Out: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trading restrictions for securities under LT-ASM may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
