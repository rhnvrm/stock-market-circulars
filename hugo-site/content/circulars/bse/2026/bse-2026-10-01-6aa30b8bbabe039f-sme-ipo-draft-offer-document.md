## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the Draft Offer Document filed by Best Engineering Aids & Consultancies Limited for its SME IPO. The document is hosted on the exchange websites for at least 21 days to invite public comments.

- Company Name: Best Engineering Aids & Consultancies Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Regulatory Framework: Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations
- Hosting Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard draft offer document filing procedure under ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public are invited to review the draft offer document and submit any comments within 21 days of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Comment Deadline: 21 days from October 01, 2026

Initiates the public review phase for the company's SME public issue, enabling market participants to examine proposed disclosures.
