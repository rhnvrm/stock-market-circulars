## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on criteria satisfaction. The applicable surveillance actions take effect from August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable margin rate shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%, effective August 12, 2026.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%, effective August 12, 2026.
- Applies to all open positions as on August 11, 2026, and new positions created from August 12, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised ASM framework guidelines and enhanced margin requirements on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100% as applicable) for open and new positions in the specified securities under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10 Aug 2026
- Effective Date: August 12, 2026 (on all open positions as on August 11, 2026 and new positions created thereafter)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities under ST-ASM Stage I and II may impact liquidity and trading volumes in the short term for the affected scrips.
