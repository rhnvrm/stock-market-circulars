## Summary

BSE has informed market participants that trading in the equity shares of Prima Innovation Limited (BSE Scrip code - 544855) will shift to the 'X' group effective Friday, August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Prima Innovation Limited
- **BSE Scrip Code:** 544855
- **New Trading Group:** 'X' group
- **Effective Date:** August 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of the company's equity shares into the 'X' trading group on the BSE platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the group change for order placement and risk management systems.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 7, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational alignment for brokers and market participants trading in the scrip under the new group category.
