## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the compulsory delisting of two companies, Niwas Spinning Mills Ltd and White Organic Retail Ltd, which have remained suspended for more than six months. The delisting takes effect from September 9, 2026, pursuant to orders of the Delisting Committee of the Exchange under SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Companies to be Delisted:** Niwas Spinning Mills Ltd (Scrip Code: 521009) and White Organic Retail Ltd (Scrip Code: 542667).
- **Effective Date:** September 09, 2026.
- **Reason:** Remained suspended from trading for more than six months.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will cease to be listed and will not be available for trading on the Exchange platform.
- Promoters, whole-time directors, and responsible persons are restricted from accessing the securities market or seeking listing for 10 years.

## Compliance Requirements

- Promoters are required to purchase shares from public shareholders at a fair value determined by an independent valuer appointed by the Exchange.
- Promoter share transfers, corporate benefits (dividends, rights, bonus, split), and voting rights/freezing provisions apply as per regulations until an exit option is provided.
- Promoters and whole-time directors are barred from becoming directors of any listed company until the exit option is successfully provided.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 07, 2026
- **Effective Date of Delisting:** September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Public shareholders of the affected companies will receive an exit opportunity through the promoters based on independent valuation. Promoters and directors face significant statutory debarment and restrictions on holding directorships in other listed entities until compliance is achieved.
