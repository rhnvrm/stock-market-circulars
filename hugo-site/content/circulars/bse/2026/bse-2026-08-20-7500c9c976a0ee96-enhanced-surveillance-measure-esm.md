## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, and certain securities will be shifted to different stages or moved out of the framework entirely, impacting settlement and price bands.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion and will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 24, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue under ESM but move to lower Stage I w.e.f. August 21, 2026.
- Qualifying securities will shift from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. August 24, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 21, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance criteria under the ESM framework in conjunction with existing exchange measures.
- Application of 100% margin requirements on open positions and new positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must note the list of securities under Annexures I, II, and III and ensure compliance with the mandated margins, settlement segments, and price bands.

## Important Dates

- **August 21, 2026:** Movement of securities to lower Stage I / Stage II and shifting out of framework.
- **August 24, 2026:** Effective date for minimum 100% margin and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in the affected securities will experience increased margin obligations, restricted trading segments (Trade-for-Trade), and potential price band restrictions, affecting liquidity and execution.
