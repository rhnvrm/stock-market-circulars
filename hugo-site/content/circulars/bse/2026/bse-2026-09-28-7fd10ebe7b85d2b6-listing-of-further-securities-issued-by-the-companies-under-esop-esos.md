## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 28 companies under ESOP / ESOS have been approved for listing and admission to trading on the Exchange, effective from Tuesday, September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 28.
- Effective Trading Date: September 29, 2026.
- Includes major companies such as HDFC Bank Limited, Infosys Limited, Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited, and Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited.
- Lock-in details specified where applicable (e.g., Bliss GVS Pharma Limited locked in up to March 3, 2027).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard operational circular for the listing of securities issued pursuant to employee stock option schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure proper settlement and handling of the newly admitted securities in accordance with exchange and depository guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 29, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry (Bliss GVS Pharma): March 3, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital resulting from the exercise of employee stock options, with negligible immediate market impact.
