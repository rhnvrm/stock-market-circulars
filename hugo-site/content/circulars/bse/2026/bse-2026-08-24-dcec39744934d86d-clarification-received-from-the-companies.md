## Summary

BSE has sought clarifications from several listed companies following significant price and volume movements in their scrips. Replies received from 13 companies have been attached to the notice, while responses from an additional 9 companies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- The Exchange monitors unusual price and volume movements to ensure transparency and availability of relevant information to investors.
- Clarifications were sought and received from 13 companies, including Advance Petrochemicals Ltd, Agi Greenpac Ltd, Dwarikesh Sugar Industries Ltd, JBM Auto Ltd, and V-Mart Retail Ltd.
- Replies are currently awaited from 9 companies, including Apt Packaging Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, and PVP Ventures Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine surveillance communication.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely respond to exchange queries regarding unusual market behavior to keep investors informed.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 24 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding recent stock price and volume volatility across the listed scrips mentioned.
