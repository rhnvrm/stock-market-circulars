## Summary

BSE issued a notice regarding the revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) pertaining to Shantai Industries Limited (Notice No. 20260615-20). The bidding was previously conducted from 26/05/2026 to 09/06/2026 under Settlement No. 2026056, with revised obligation download and settlement dates scheduled for June 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Shantai Industries Limited
- **Settlement Type & Number:** Takeover, Settlement No. 2026056
- **Original Bidding Period:** 26/05/2026 to 09/06/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download Date:** 16/06/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 17/06/2026
- **Delivery Mechanism:** Demat shares must be delivered strictly through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of the settlement calendar for the ongoing takeover offer under standard stock exchange acquisition window guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must use Market Type '(Takeover)' and Settlement No. 2026056 in the depository system.
- Demat shares must be delivered via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories.
- Members must refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 26/05/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 09/06/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download:** 16/06/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 17/06/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures accurate and timely execution of share transfers and fund settlements for members participating in the Shantai Industries Limited takeover acquisition window.
