## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (ICCL) has announced that an additional exposure margin of 15% will be levied in the equity derivatives segment on securities where the top 10 clients account for more than 20% of the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL). This framework is based on 3 months rolling data and reviewed monthly.

## Key Points

- Additional exposure margin rate: 15% in the equity derivatives segment.
- Applicability criteria: Securities where top 10 clients account for more than 20% of MWPL.
- If additional surveillance margin is applicable, the higher of the two margins shall be levied.
- Identification is based on 3 months rolling data and reviewed monthly.

## Regulatory Changes

Continuation of the framework specified in the BSE Notice dated March 18, 2019, regarding Additional Exposure Margin on Securities under MWPL.

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members must ensure adequate margins are maintained for securities specified in the Annexure as per the revised requirements effective from the specified dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026 (immediately following the expiry of August 2026 contracts, remaining applicable until September 2026 expiry).

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and participants trading in the affected derivative scrips will experience higher margin requirements, impacting leverage and capital allocation for those specific securities.
