## Summary

HEG Limited has informed the Exchange about a change in the name of the company. Consequently, the company's scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: HEG Limited
- New Company Name: HEG Advanced Materials Limited
- Scrip Code: 509631
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): HEGAM
- New Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus): HEGAM

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records and trading systems with the new scrip ID and abbreviated name.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 16, 2026
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to operational updates of trading symbols and identifiers in broker and exchange terminals.
