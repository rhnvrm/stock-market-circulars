## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by eight companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan / Scheme (ESOP / ESOS), effective from Thursday, October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 08, 2026.
- Total Companies Issuing Securities: 8 companies.
- Participating Companies:
  1. Aarti Industries Limited (14,669 shares, ISIN: INE769A01020, Scrip Code: 524208)
  2. Aditya Birla Capital Limited (68,888 shares, ISIN: INE674K01013, Scrip Code: 540691)
  3. ICICI Bank Limited (202,836 shares, ISIN: INE090A01021, Scrip Code: 532174)
  4. ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited (32,526 shares, ISIN: INE765G01017, Scrip Code: 540716)
  5. Ideaforge Technology Limited (8,826 shares, ISIN: INE349Y01013, Scrip Code: 543932)
  6. Kansai Nerolac Paints Limited (75,801 shares, ISIN: INE531A01024, Scrip Code: 500165)
  7. L&T Finance Limited (49,250 shares, ISIN: INE498L01015, Scrip Code: 533519)
  8. Sona BLW Precision Forgings Limited (41,850 shares, ISIN: INE073K01018, Scrip Code: 543300)
- All listed securities are ranked pari passu with existing equity shares of the respective companies.
- Lock-in status for all mentioned issuances is marked as NA.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine corporate action processing.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update records regarding the expanded equity base and further issuance of securities under ESOP/ESOS.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 07, 2026
- Effective Trading Date for New Securities: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity share capital for the participating companies resulting from employee stock option exercises.
