## Summary

BSE has issued the trading and settlement schedule for the Corporate Debt Segment (ICDM T+0 and T+1) covering the period from October 1, 2026, to October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines settlement schedules for ICDM T+0 and ICDM T+1 transactions.
- Specifies transaction dates, securities/funds pay-in dates, and securities/funds pay-out dates for October 2026.
- Settlement numbers range from 2627124 to 2627143.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants dealing in the corporate debt segment must adhere to the specified pay-in and pay-out timelines.

## Important Dates

- Programme Period Start: 01-10-2026
- Programme Period End: 31-10-2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement operations and clearing cycles for corporate debt market participants throughout October 2026.
