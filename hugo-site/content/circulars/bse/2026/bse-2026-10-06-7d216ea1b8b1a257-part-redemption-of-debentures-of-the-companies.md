## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the fix of record dates for interest payment and part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and Bonds for several companies. Trading members are informed that trading in these debentures/bonds will take place with reduced face values effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- **Si Creva Capital Services Private Limited**: Scrip Code 978242 (ISIN: INE996U07479), Record/Ex-Date: 08/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 49,999.99 per debenture.
- **Oberoi Realty Limited**: Scrip Code 976127 (ISIN: INE093I07074), Record/Ex-Date: 09/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 30,000/- per debenture.
- **SRG Housing Finance Limited**: Scrip Code 977242 (ISIN: INE559N07066), Record/Ex-Date: 09/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 81,818.18 per debenture.
- **Achiiievers Finance India Limited**: Multiple scrips (977310, 977613, 977891; ISINs: INE065507BM2, INE065507BS9, INE065507BX9), Record/Ex-Date: 09/10/2026, with reduced face values of Rs. 8,333/-, Rs. 41,667/-, and Rs. 66,667/- per debenture respectively.
- **IOT Utkal Energy Services Limited**: Multiple scrips (950199, 950213, 952676; ISINs: INE310L07985, INE310L07894, INE310L07AE1), Record/Ex-Date: 12/10/2026, with reduced face values of Rs. 48,809.44, Rs. 25,000/-, and Rs. 14,979/- per debenture.
- **Oxyzo Financial Services Limited**: Scrip Code 976577 (ISIN: INE04VS07388), Record/Ex-Date: 12/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 23,810/- per debenture.
- **Navi Finserv Limited**: Scrip Code 977755 (ISIN: INE342T07692), Record/Ex-Date: 12/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 80,000/- per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of reduced face value trading for debt securities undergoing part redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the reduced face values and record dates for proper settlement and execution of trades in the debt segment.

## Important Dates

- **08/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Si Creva Capital Services Private Limited.
- **09/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Oberoi Realty Limited, SRG Housing Finance Limited, and Achiiievers Finance India Limited.
- **12/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for IOT Utkal Energy Services Limited, Oxyzo Financial Services Limited, and Navi Finserv Limited.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures market transparency and correct valuation pricing for debenture holders undergoing capital reduction/part redemption.
