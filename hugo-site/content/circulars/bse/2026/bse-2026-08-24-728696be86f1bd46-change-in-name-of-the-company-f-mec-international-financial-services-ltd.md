## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that F Mec International Financial Services Ltd has changed its name to Dhvija Finance Limited. Consequently, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: F Mec International Financial Services Ltd
- New Name: Dhvija Finance Limited
- Scrip Code: 539552
- Existing Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: FMECL
- New Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: DHVIJAFIN
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to update their internal systems with the new name and scrip ID for the company.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading members and database systems to reflect the new company name and scrip identifier.
