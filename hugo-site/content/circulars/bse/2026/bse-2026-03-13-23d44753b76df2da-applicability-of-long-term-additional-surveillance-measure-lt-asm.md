## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the list of securities shortlisted, moved to higher/lower stages, or moving out of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective March 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Part A:** Securities shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework (e.g., Usha Martin Education & Solutions Ltd, Venus Remedies Ltd).
- **Part B:** Securities continuing in the framework but moving to higher ASM stages (Stages II).
- **Part C:** Securities moving to lower ASM stages (Stages I, II, III).
- **Part D:** Securities shortlisted in direct stage IV of the Long Term ASM Framework.
- **Annexure II:** List of securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework due to various reasons including inclusion in other surveillance frameworks (GSM, ESM, Trade for Trade, IBC).

## Regulatory Changes

Updates to the surveillance categorization of listed securities to safeguard market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** March 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, margins, and trading restrictions as mandated by the exchange's ASM framework.
