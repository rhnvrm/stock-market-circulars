## Summary

Carver Roboweld Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document for an SME IPO on August 25, 2026. The document is available on the stock exchange websites for at least 21 days to invite public comments.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Carver Roboweld Limited
- Filing Date: August 25, 2026
- Notice Date: August 26, 2026
- Segment: SME
- Purpose: Seeking public comments on the Draft Offer Document under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public can submit comments on the draft offer document within 21 days from the date of the notice to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Draft Offer Document Filing Date: August 25, 2026
- Notice Date: August 26, 2026
- Comment Deadline: Within 21 days from the date of notice

Provides transparency and invites public scrutiny for the upcoming SME IPO of Carver Roboweld Limited.
