## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window for Buyback) of equity shares of Shardul Securities Limited.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Shardul Securities Limited
- Security Symbol: SHARDUL (Scrip Code: 512393, ISIN: INE037B01020)
- Offer Size: Up to 1,92,00,000 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 60.00 per share
- Face Value: Rs. 2.00
- Opening Date: Wednesday, 14th October 2026
- Closing Date: Wednesday, 21st October 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that the Unique Client Code (UCC) of investors is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bids can be placed through the iBBS and RTRMS platforms during the scheduled window.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: 14th October 2026
- Issue Closing Date: 21st October 2026
- Give-up / Take-up Period: 14th October 2026 to 19th October 2026
- Last day Give-up / Take-up: 21st October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates eligible shareholders of Shardul Securities Limited to participate in the buyback offer through recognized trading members via the exchange acquisition window.
