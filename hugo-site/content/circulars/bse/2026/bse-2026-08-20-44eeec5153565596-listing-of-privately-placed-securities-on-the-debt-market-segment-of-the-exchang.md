## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed securities (Commercial Papers) on the debt market segment of the Exchange effective August 20, 2026. The notice covers multiple issuances with specified scrip codes, ISINs, and credit ratings.

## Key Points

- Securities are admitted to trading in dematerialised form only.
- Standard trading denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Covers Commercial Papers from issuers including Godrej Housing Finance Limited, 360 ONE Prime Limited, Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, Godrej Finance Limited, and DSP Finance Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard debt segment trading and dematerialisation norms.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 19, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: August 20, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from November 2026 to June 2027 depending on the specific issuance.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice with no broader market impact.
