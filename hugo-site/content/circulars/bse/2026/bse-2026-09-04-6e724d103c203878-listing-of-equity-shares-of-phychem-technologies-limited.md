## Summary

Effective from Monday, September 7, 2026, the equity shares of Phychem Technologies Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the SME segment under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Phychem Technologies Limited
- Scrip Code: 544902
- ISIN: INE24YP01017
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: PHYCHEM
- Market Lot: 2000 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 54/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 44/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 3, 2026
- Total Securities Listed: 10,240,000 Equity Shares

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in the minimum market lot of 2000 shares.
- Lock-in details and shareholding patterns are as per Annexure I and Annexure II of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 3, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Phychem Technologies Limited equity shares on the BSE SME platform with standard trade-for-trade surveillance for the first 10 trading sessions.
