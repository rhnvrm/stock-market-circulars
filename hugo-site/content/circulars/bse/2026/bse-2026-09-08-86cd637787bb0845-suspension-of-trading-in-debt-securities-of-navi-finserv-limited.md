## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities of Navi Finserv Limited due to the maturity of the securities on September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Navi Finserv Limited
- Scrip Code: 976489
- ISIN: INE342T07577
- Particulars: NFL-10.60%-10-9-26-PVT
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debentures with effect from September 09, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 08, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: September 09, 2026
- Maturity Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No broader market impact; standard procedure for debt instruments reaching maturity.
