Auto Recycling Systems Limited has submitted its Draft Offer Document for an SME IPO to BSE on September 28, 2026. The document is hosted on the BSE SME website for public review and comments for at least 21 days.

- Company Name: Auto Recycling Systems Limited
- Filing Date: September 28, 2026
- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO (Department of DCS-Listing)
- Public Comment Window: At least 21 days from the date of notice
