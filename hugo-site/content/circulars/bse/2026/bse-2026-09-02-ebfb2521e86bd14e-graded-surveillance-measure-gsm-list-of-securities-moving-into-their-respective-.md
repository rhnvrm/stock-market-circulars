## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of specific securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework effective September 03, 2026. These measures include strict trading and margin restrictions to alert investors and control excessive price volatility.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the Annexure will move to higher GSM stages (Stage I, II, III, or IV).
- Effective date for the transition is September 03, 2026.
- Trading shall be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off permitted for applicable stages.
- Surveillance actions range from 100% margin rates and 5% price bands to trading restricted to once a week and high Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance actions as per the provisions of the GSM Framework.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band and ASD of 50% of trade value by buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday), and ASD of 100% of trade value.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Settlement must be executed on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities are likely to be restricted due to stringent margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and potential weekly trading limitations.
