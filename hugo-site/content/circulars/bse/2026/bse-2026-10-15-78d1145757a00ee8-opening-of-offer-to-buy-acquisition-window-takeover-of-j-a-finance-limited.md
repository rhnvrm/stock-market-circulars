## Summary

An open offer has been announced by the acquirers (Mr. Sachin Ishwarchand Goyal, Mr. Numan Sachin Goyal, and Mr. Salil Vijay Sathe) to acquire up to 27,68,671 fully paid-up equity shares, representing 26.00% of the voting share capital of J. A. Finance Limited ("JAFL"), at an offer price of Rs. 44.82 per share (inclusive of interest of Rs. 4.82). The acquisition window runs from October 19, 2026, to November 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** J. A. Finance Limited (JAFL).
- **Acquirers:** Mr. Sachin Ishwarchand Goyal, Mr. Numan Sachin Goyal, and Mr. Salil Vijay Sathe.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 27,68,671 equity shares (26.00% of total paid-up equity and voting share capital).
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 44.82 per Equity Share (including interest of Rs. 4.82), payable in cash.
- **Offer Period:** Monday, October 19, 2026, to Monday, November 02, 2026 (excluding SEBI Holiday on Tuesday, October 20, 2026).
- **Regulatory Framework:** Compliance with Regulations 3(1), 4, 18(11), and 18(11A) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted pursuant to SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015, and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 dated December 09, 2016, along with subsequent exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must participate in accordance with the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to tender offers under Takeovers, Buy Back, and Delisting.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** October 19, 2026
- **SEBI Holiday (Exclusion):** October 20, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides public shareholders of J. A. Finance Limited an exit/tender opportunity pursuant to the takeover regulations during the designated acquisition window.
