## Summary

BSE has issued a notice (Notice No. 20260812-49) dated August 12, 2026, regarding a bonus issue of equity shares by Organic Recycling Systems Limited (Scrip Code: 543997). The company has fixed August 20, 2026, as the Record Date for determining the eligibility of shareholders for the bonus shares. Consequently, transactions in the company's equity shares will be traded on an ex-bonus basis starting from August 20, 2026. Additionally, the trading market lot for the company's shares on the BSE SME platform will be revised from 600 to 900 shares, effective from August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Bonus Issue Ratio**: 1 (one) bonus equity share of Rs. 10/- each for every 2 (two) existing equity shares of Rs. 10/- each held.
- **Record Date**: August 20, 2026, is set as the record date for the bonus issue.
- **Ex-Bonus Date**: August 20, 2026, is the effective date from which the shares will trade ex-bonus.
- **Market Lot Revision**: The market lot for Organic Recycling Systems Limited (Scrip Code: 543997) will be revised from 600 to 900 shares.
- **Share Allotment**: The company will allot 5,199,637 equity shares on August 21, 2026, as part of the bonus issue.
- **Signatory**: Marian Dsouza, Assistant Vice President, Listing Compliance and Operations.

## Regulatory Changes

No major regulatory changes are introduced. This notice pertains to standard corporate actions and market lot adjustments pursuant to existing exchange guidelines for SME-listed companies.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must update their systems to reflect the new market lot size of 900 shares for Organic Recycling Systems Limited (Scrip Code: 543997) effective August 20, 2026.
- **Company Operations**: The company must ensure timely allotment of the 5,199,637 bonus shares on August 21, 2026, in compliance with standard regulatory procedures and listing agreement requirements.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date**: August 12, 2026
- **Ex-Bonus Date**: August 20, 2026
- **Record Date**: August 20, 2026
- **Market Lot Revision Effective Date**: August 20, 2026
- **Share Allotment Date**: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Equity Valuation**: The outstanding share capital will increase with the allotment of 5,199,637 new shares. The share price will adjust proportionally downward on the ex-date (August 20, 2026) to reflect the 1:2 bonus ratio.
- **Market Liquidity**: The addition of shares may enhance market liquidity and trading volume over time.
- **Trading Lot Size**: The increase in market lot size from 600 to 900 shares keeps the aggregate transaction value of a single lot in line with SME segment requirements post the price adjustment.
