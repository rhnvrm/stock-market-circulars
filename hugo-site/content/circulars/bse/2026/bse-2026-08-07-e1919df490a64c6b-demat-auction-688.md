## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. 688 (Rolling Settlement No. DR-688/2026-27). Trading members can submit offers for the purchase of securities in auction on Friday, August 07, 2026, according to the specified session timings.

## Key Points

- Auction Offer Entry: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on August 07, 2026.
- Auction Matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on August 07, 2026.
- Delivery Statement & Report: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m. on August 07, 2026.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. on August 07, 2026.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Monday, August 10, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- Restrictions on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they short delivered are removed following the uniform membership structure implementation in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must check auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate as no corrections will be permitted.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 07, 2026
- Pay-in Date: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational impact for trading members participating in rolling settlement auctions.
