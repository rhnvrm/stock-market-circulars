## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for multiple scrips, effective from July 31, 2026. Trading members are informed that price bands have been adjusted from their existing levels for nine specific securities.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260730-46, dated July 30, 2026.
- Effective Date: July 31, 2026.
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance).
- Total scrips affected: 9 companies, with price bands revised to percentages such as 2%, 5%, 10%, and 20%.

## Affected Scrips and Price Bands

1. Amarnath Securities Ltd (Scrip Code: 538465) - Revised to 5%
2. Asahi Songwon Colors Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532853) - Revised to 5%
3. Ashiana Agro Industries Ltd. (Scrip Code: 519174) - Revised to 2%
4. Comrade Appliances Limited (Scrip Code: 543921) - Revised to 2%
5. Exide Industries Ltd. (Scrip Code: 500086) - Revised to 20%
6. Ganesh Benzoplast Ltd. (Scrip Code: 500153) - Revised to 5%
7. Nuvama Wealth Management Limited (Scrip Code: 543988) - Revised to 20%
8. Tips Films Limited (Scrip Code: 543614) - Revised to 10%
9. Triveni Glass Ltd. (Scrip Code: 502281) - Revised to 5%

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while executing orders in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- Effective Date: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Modifications to price bands restrict or expand the maximum allowable daily price movement for the mentioned equities, curbing excessive volatility or allowing normal market price discovery.
