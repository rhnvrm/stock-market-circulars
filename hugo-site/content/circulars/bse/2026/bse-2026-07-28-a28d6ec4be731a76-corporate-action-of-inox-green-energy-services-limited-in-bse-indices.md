## Summary

Inox Green Energy Services Limited (Exchange Ticker – 543667, "INOXGREEN") is demerging its Power Evacuation Business into Inox Renewable Solutions Limited ("INOXRENEW") effective from the start of trading hours on Friday, July 31, 2026. Shareholders will receive 122 equity shares of Inox Renewable Solutions Limited for every 1,000 equity shares held in Inox Green Energy Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Demerger Effective Date: July 31, 2026.
- Share Entitlement: 122 equity shares of Inox Renewable Solutions Limited for every 1,000 equity shares held in Inox Green Energy Services Limited.
- Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS): Conducted by BSE Limited on July 31, 2026, for price discovery of INOXGREEN post-demerger.
- Index Additions: Inox Renewable Solutions Limited will be added to multiple BSE indices (including BSE Smallcap, Allcap, Services, 1000, Next 500, 250 Microcap, etc.) at zero price effective July 31, 2026.
