## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of surveillance measures for companies with high promoter 'Encumbrance' under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities meeting the criteria will attract a minimum 35% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective August 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 35% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments w.e.f. August 07, 2026 on all open positions as on August 06, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities listed in Annexure II are eligible to move out from the framework effective August 05, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Representations regarding placement in the framework must be submitted by August 05, 2026, 5:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011 relating to high promoter encumbrances, enforcing stricter margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collections (minimum 35%) for applicable securities in equity and derivatives segments.
- Companies wishing to make representations against their inclusion must submit them to the exchange by 5:00 PM on August 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for removal of securities (Annexure II): August 05, 2026
- Representation deadline: August 05, 2026, 5:00 PM
- Open positions cut-off date for margin applicability: August 06, 2026
- Effective date for margin requirements (Annexure I): August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (35%) on affected securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the short-listed companies in the equity and derivatives segments.
