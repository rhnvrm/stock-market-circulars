## Summary

The BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for its Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, September 05, 2026. Trading members can use this session to test trading applications, risk management software, and new BOLT Pro TWS versions in compliance with SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, September 05, 2026, for the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Helps trading members fulfill regulatory software testing requirements under SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.50 is set to be released with details in a separate circular.
- Trades resulting from the mock session will not attract any margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Facilitates compliance with existing software testing mandates for trading and risk management systems.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively in the mock trading session or UAT environment to fulfill regulatory testing requirements. Feedback on the mock session should be submitted by 4:30 PM on the day of the mock session.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** September 05, 2026
- **Login Time:** 08:15 AM to 09:00 AM
- **Continuous Trading:** 09:00 AM to 03:30 PM
- **Feedback Deadline:** 04:30 PM on September 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal and restricted to voluntary testing and familiarization by trading members and software vendors.
