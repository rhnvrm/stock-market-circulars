## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of 8,54,700 equity shares of PTC Industries Ltd. (Scrip Code: 539006, ISIN: INE596F01018) with effect from Tuesday, October 13, 2026, following allotment to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) pursuant to a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** PTC Industries Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 539006
- **ISIN:** INE596F01018
- **Security Details:** 8,54,700 Equity shares of Rs. 10 each allotted to QIBs pursuant to QIP.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 21,060/- per share.
- **Date of Allotment:** October 9, 2026.
- **Trading Effective Date:** October 13, 2026.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 14992550 to 15847249.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the listing and trading commencement of the newly allotted shares under the specified scrip code and distinctive number range.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 9, 2026
- **Listing & Trading Effective Date:** October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of the company's equity capital base through institutional share allotment.
