## Summary

BSE has announced the temporary suspension of lumpsum investments (fresh and additional purchases) including switch-ins in certain schemes of Invesco Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from the close of business hours on September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected schemes include:
  - INVESCO INDIA – INVESCO Global Consumer Trends Fund of Fund
  - INVESCO INDIA – INVESCO Global Equity Income Fund of Fund
  - INVESCO INDIA – INVESCO Pan European Equity Fund of Fund
- Lumpsum investments, fresh/additional purchases, and switch-in transactions are disabled on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIPs, STPs) registered prior to the effective date continue to be processed normally.
- Redemption, Switch-Out, STP-Out, and SWP transactions remain permitted and processed as usual.

## Regulatory Changes

Operational suspension of subscriptions and switch-in transactions for specific fund of fund schemes as requested by Invesco AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that subscription orders received and orders triggered to RTA after close of business hours on September 29, 2026, will be rejected and refunded to investors after receipt of funds from AMC/RTA.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** Close of business hours on September 29, 2026
- **Cut-off for Prior Applications:** Applications received after applicable cut-off time on September 28, 2026, shall not be accepted or processed.
- **Notice Date:** September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding or planning fresh allocations in the affected Invesco global fund of fund schemes will not be able to make lumpsum purchases or switch-ins through the BSE StAR MF platform, while existing SIPs/STPs and exit routes (redemptions, switch-outs) remain unaffected.
