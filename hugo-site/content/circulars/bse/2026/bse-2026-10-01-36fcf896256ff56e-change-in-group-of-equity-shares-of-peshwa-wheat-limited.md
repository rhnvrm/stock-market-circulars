## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Peshwa Wheat Limited (SME IPO, Scrip Code: 544955) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Peshwa Wheat Limited
- Scrip Code: 544955
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Friday, October 16, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260930-36 dated September 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard transition of trading segments for an SME IPO security.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment shift for dealings in the equity shares of the company.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for the equity shares of Peshwa Wheat Limited starting from October 16, 2026.
