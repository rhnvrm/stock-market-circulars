## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Lloyds Engineering Works Limited (Scrip Code: 539992, ISIN: INE093R01011) effective from September 28, 2026. A total of 7,07,42,458 equity shares of face value Re. 1/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 70.25/- (issue price Rs. 71.25/-) to non-promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Lloyds Engineering Works Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 539992
- **ISIN:** INE093R01011
- **Number of Shares:** 7,07,42,458 equity shares
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 71.25/- per share (Face Value Re. 1/- + Premium Rs. 70.25/-)
- **Allotment Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** September 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing of securities issued pursuant to a preferential allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must adhere to the lock-in details specified for various tranches of shares ranging up to March 30, 2027, and March 31, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 17, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** September 28, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** March 30, 2027 and March 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

The listing increases the paid-up equity capital of Lloyds Engineering Works Limited by 7,07,42,458 shares issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis, with mandatory lock-in restrictions ensuring regulatory compliance.
