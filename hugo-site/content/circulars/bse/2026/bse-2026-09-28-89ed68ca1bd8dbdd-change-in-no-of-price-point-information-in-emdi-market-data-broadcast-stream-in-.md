## Summary

BSE has announced a reduction in the number of price points disseminated in the EMDI market data broadcast stream for the Equity segment, shifting from the top 20 price points to the top 10 price points effective October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- EMDI stream currently disseminates market data for an instrument based on activity in the top 20 price points.
- Effective October 12, 2026, dissemination will be restricted to activity in the top 10 price points.
- No changes will occur in any other type of market data broadcast stream.
- The EMDI multicast IP and port for the Equity segment remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Optimization of equity market data broadcast streams by reducing price point depth from 20 to 10.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and empanelled vendors must ensure their trading and data systems can handle the reduced price point broadcast change.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Vendors and trading members processing BSE equity market data streams must update their ingestion logic to accommodate top 10 price points instead of top 20.
