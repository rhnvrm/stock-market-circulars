## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund is offering the UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund (Regular and Direct Plans - Growth) along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AG84) and UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AG92).
- Tentative allotment date is August 27, 2026.
- NFO extended till midnight on August 24, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases only.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on August 24, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without funds initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 10, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: August 24, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the new index fund offering smoothly via the BSE StAR MF platform with clear operational timelines.
