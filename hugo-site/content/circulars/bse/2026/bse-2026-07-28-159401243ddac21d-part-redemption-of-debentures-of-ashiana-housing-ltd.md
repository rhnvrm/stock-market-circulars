## Summary

Ashiana Housing Ltd has announced a partial redemption of its debentures resulting in a reduced face value per debenture, effective from July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ashiana Housing Ltd
- Scrip Code: 973220
- ISIN: INE365D08026
- Purpose: Partial Redemption of Rs. 632,914.76 per debenture
- Effective Date: July 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised that the debentures will be traded with the reduced face value starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 28, 2026
- Effective Date: July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for trading members and investors holding the specified debt securities due to the change in face value following partial redemption.
