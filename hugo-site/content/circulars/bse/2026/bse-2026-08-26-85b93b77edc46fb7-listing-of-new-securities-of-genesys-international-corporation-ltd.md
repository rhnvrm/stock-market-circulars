## Summary

Trading members of the BSE are informed that new equity shares of Genesys International Corporation Ltd. (Scrip Code - 506109) resulting from a rights issue are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange effective from Thursday, August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Genesys International Corporation Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 506109
- ISIN: INE727B01026
- Security Details: 2,50,74,226 equity shares of Rs. 5/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 45/- on a rights basis.
- Distinctive Numbers: 41790378 to 66864603
- Date of Allotment: 24/08/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: August 27, 2026
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with standard settlement procedures for the newly listed rights shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 24, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase via rights issue for Genesys International Corporation Ltd. with no broader market impact.
