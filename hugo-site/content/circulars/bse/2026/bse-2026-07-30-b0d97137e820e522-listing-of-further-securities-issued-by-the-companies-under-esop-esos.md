## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 13 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS), effective from Friday, July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 13.
- Effective Date for trading: July 31, 2026.
- Included companies span various sectors, such as Affle (India) Limited, Axis Bank Limited, Federal Bank Limited, and Yes Bank Limited.
- Most issues have 'NA' under lock-in, with specific lock-in release dates noted where applicable (e.g., Bliss GVS Pharma Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the listing of new securities for the specified companies.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- Effective Date: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase via ESOP exercises with negligible immediate market impact.
