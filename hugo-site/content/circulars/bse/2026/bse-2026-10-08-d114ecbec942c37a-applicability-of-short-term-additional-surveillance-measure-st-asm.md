## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has notified the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Enhanced margin requirements are applicable effective October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Effective date for surveillance actions: October 12, 2026, on all open positions as on October 09, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margin obligations (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) for shortlisted securities under the ST-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure adequate margins are maintained for open positions and new trades in the affected securities as per the specified rates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements may impact liquidity, trading volumes, and leverage for securities placed under ST-ASM Stages I and II.
