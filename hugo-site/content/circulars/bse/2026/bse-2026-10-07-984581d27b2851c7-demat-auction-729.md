## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-729/2026-27 on Wednesday, October 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 7, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction will be held on Thursday, Oct 8, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.
- Settlement will be done through the Clearing Member of a Trading Member following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.
- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is now removed.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine auction scheduling notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and Custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members are requested to refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the 'Auction offer Entry Screen'.

## Important Dates

- Auction Offer Entry / Matching Date: October 7, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: October 8, 2026 at 08:00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations affecting participants involved in settlement DR-729/2026-27 short deliveries.
