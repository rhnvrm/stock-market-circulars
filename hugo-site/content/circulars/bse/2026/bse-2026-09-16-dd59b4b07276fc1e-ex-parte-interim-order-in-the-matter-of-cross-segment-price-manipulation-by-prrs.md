## Summary

SEBI has issued an ex-parte interim order dated September 16, 2026, in the matter of cross-segment price manipulation by Prrsaar Sampada Private Limited, Chaubara Eats Private Limited, and other noticees, imposing market access restrictions.

## Key Points

- Noticee Nos. 1 to 6 are restrained from accessing the securities markets (for Noticee No. 1, this applies to its proprietary trading account only).
- Noticees with open positions in exchange traded derivative contracts may close out/square off such positions within 3 months from the date of the order or at contract expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Noticees are permitted to settle pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions completed before trading hours closed on the date of the order.
- The order is effective immediately from September 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and market access restrictions imposed via SEBI interim order against entities involved in cross-segment price manipulation.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the order and ensure strict compliance with the restrictions placed on the specified noticees.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** September 16, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 16, 2026
- **Derivative Position Square-off Deadline:** Within 3 months from the date of the order or contract expiry, whichever is earlier.

## Impact Assessment

High regulatory impact on the specified noticees restricting their securities market access, ensuring market integrity and penalizing cross-segment price manipulation practices.
