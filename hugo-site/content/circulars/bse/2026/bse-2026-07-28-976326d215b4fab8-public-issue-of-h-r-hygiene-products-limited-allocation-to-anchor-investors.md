## Summary

Trading members of the exchange are informed that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of H R Hygiene Products Limited, 16,86,400 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 88/- per equity share as of July 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: H R Hygiene Products Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investors Portion)
- Shares Allocated: 16,86,400 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 88/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: July 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants are to note the anchor investor allocation details for the public issue.

- Notice Date: July 28, 2026

Provides transparency regarding institutional participation and anchor investor subscription levels prior to the wider public offering.
