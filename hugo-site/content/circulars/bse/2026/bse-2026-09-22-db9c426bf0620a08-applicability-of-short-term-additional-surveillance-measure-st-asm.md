## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities that have met the inclusion criteria. Effective September 24, 2026, revised margin requirements apply to both open positions as of September 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I attract a 50% margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II attract a 100% margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their original levels, subject to no other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance actions with enhanced margin criteria under the existing Exchange ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (50% or 100% depending on the stage) on all open positions as of September 23, 2026, and new positions created from September 24, 2026 onwards for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 22, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 24, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on September 23, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities will raise capital obligations for market participants trading in these scrips, curbing speculative activity and managing volatility.
