## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of specified securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are moved to higher GSM stages (Stage I, II, III, IV) based on surveillance parameters.
- Applicable surveillance actions include margin rate increases, price bands, trade-for-trade settlement, Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD), and trading frequency restrictions.
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed for affected securities.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance measures under the GSM framework as published on February 23, 2017.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities and ensure compliance with respective stage restrictions (such as ASD deposits and price bands).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement for the affected securities will be restricted due to trade-for-trade mechanisms, increased margin requirements, and Additional Surveillance Deposits.
