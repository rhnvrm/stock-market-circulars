## Summary

 BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 5,00,000 equity shares of Lokesh Machines Limited (Scrip Code - 532740) effective from September 10, 2026. These shares are issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lokesh Machines Limited
- Scrip Code: 532740
- ISIN: INE397H01017
- Number of Shares: 5,00,000 equity shares
- Issue Price/Details: Rs. 181.71/- per share (Face value Rs. 10/- at a premium of Rs. 171.71/-)
- Allotment Date: 11/07/2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 21296771 to 21796770
- Lock-in Details: 5,00,000 shares are under lock-in up to 31/03/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the listing and lock-in details of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 11/07/2026
- Effective Date for Trading: 10/09/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 31/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion via warrant conversion with standard lock-in conditions for preferential allottees.
