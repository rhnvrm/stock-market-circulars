## Summary

SEBI has issued a final order dated September 28, 2026, in the matter of Growpital Platform, continuing from earlier exchange notices. SEBI has restrained multiple noticees from buying, selling, or dealing in securities or accessing capital markets for specified periods.

## Key Points

- Noticee Nos. 1 to 8 are restrained from buying, selling, or dealing in securities or accessing capital markets for 5 years from the date of the order or till the completion of refunds, whichever is later.
- Noticee Nos. 9 to 14, 17, 18, 19, and 20 to 30 are restrained from buying, selling, or dealing in securities or accessing capital markets for 3 years.
- The order is effective immediately from September 28, 2026.
- Trading members are advised to take note and ensure strict compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and securities market bans imposed by SEBI under its regulatory powers against entities associated with the Growpital Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure that the restrained noticees (Noticee Nos. 1 to 30 as specified in the SEBI order) are restricted from accessing capital markets or trading in securities through their platforms.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The order ensures investor protection by barring entities connected with unauthorized or non-compliant schemes on the Growpital Platform from participating in securities markets, reinforcing regulatory oversight.
