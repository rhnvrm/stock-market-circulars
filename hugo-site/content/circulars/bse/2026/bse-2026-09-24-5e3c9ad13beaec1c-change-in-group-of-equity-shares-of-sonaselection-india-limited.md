## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of SonaSelection India Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment (B Group), effective Friday, October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SonaSelection India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544938
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group) / B Group
- Effective Date: October 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares are shifted from the Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment (B Group).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the segment transfer pursuant to Exchange Notice No. 20260923-35.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in equity shares of SonaSelection India Limited shifts to B Group under the Rolling segment.
