## Summary

Exchange notice informing trading members about the listing and admission to dealings of units of ICICI Prudential BSE Insurance ETF, an open-ended Index Exchange Traded Fund tracking the BSE Insurance Index, starting August 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of Mutual Fund: ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund
- Scrip Code: 544845
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: INSUREIETF
- ISIN: INF109K1A567
- Issue Price: Rs. 15.69 per unit
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00 per unit
- Date of Allotment: 31/07/2026
- Number of Units: 3,806,442 Units

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Units of the Mutual Fund will only be traded in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000. Trades will be executed in unit market lot (one unit).

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: July 31, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an additional exchange-traded fund product tracking the insurance sector on the BSE platform.
