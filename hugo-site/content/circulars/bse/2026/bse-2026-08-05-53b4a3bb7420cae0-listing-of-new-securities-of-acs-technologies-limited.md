## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of ACS Technologies Limited (Scrip Code - 530745) effective from August 6, 2026, following the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: ACS Technologies Limited
- Scrip Code: 530745
- ISIN: INE366C01021
- Securities Issued: 86,95,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 21.25/-
- Allottees: Non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants
- Effective Date of Trading: August 6, 2026
- Lock-in Details: 86,95,000 shares locked-in up to February 15, 2027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

- Allotment Date: July 3, 2026
- Listing/Trading Date: August 6, 2026
