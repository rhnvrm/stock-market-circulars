## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 728 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-728/2026-27, detailing offer entry, matching, delivery, and money statement timelines.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 6, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Wednesday, October 7, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians are requested to check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 6, 2026
- Pay-in Date: October 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational notice governing settlement and demat auction procedures for trading members.
