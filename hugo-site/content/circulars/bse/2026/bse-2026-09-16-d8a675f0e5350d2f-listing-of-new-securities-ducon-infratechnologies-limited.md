## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of Ducon Infratechnologies Limited under Scrip Code 534674, effective from September 17, 2026.

- Security Details: 24,99,42,759 Equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at par on a rights basis.
- ISIN: INE741L01018
- Distribution Nos.: 324925588 - 574868346
- Date of Allotment: 11/09/2026
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.

None.

Trading members may trade in the newly listed equity shares effective from the specified date.

- Date of Allotment: 11/09/2026
- Effective Date for Trading: 17/09/2026

Expands the paid-up equity capital base of Ducon Infratechnologies Limited with no operational disruption.
