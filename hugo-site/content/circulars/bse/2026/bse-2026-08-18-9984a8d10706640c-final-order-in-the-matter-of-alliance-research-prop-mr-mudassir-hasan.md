## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the final order passed by SEBI in the matter of Alliance Research (Proprietor: Mr. Mudassir Hasan). SEBI has vacated the directions and restrains imposed against the Noticee vide the earlier Interim Order.

## Key Points

- In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20220729-23 dated July 29, 2022.
- SEBI vide order no. WTM/AS/WRO/WRO-DIV-5/32666/2026-27 dated August 18, 2026, vacated all directions issued against the Noticee.
- Restraints imposed pursuant to the Interim Order cease to operate forthwith.

## Regulatory Changes

- Withdrawal of interim restrictions on Alliance Research (Prop: Mr. Mudassir Hasan).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the order and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 18, 2026
- Notice Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No direct market or operational impact as this is an entity-specific final order.
