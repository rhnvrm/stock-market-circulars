## Summary

BSE has issued a circular detailing the close-out procedure for shortages in overnight Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) and liquid ETFs, following previous SEBI guidelines. The procedures apply when securities are unavailable for procurement in the auction market or when auction payouts cannot be completed prior to the Record Date/Book Closure Date.

## Key Points

- Applies to shortages arising in the Normal Market segment for Overnight ETFs and Liquid ETFs.
- Covers scenarios where auction sellers fail to deliver securities or auction payouts cannot be completed before the Record/Book Closure Date.
- Mandates compulsory close-out at the higher of the highest price prevailing across recognized stock exchanges from trade execution up to the auction day, or 5% above the auction day settlement price.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of specific close-out rules for overnight and liquid ETFs aligned with SEBI norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must adhere to the compulsory close-out pricing mechanism for shortages in overnight and liquid ETFs.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: Trade date September 07, 2026 (T Day), applicable to shortages of settlement number 2627710.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances certainty and risk management in the settlement of overnight and liquid ETFs by defining clear financial close-out terms for delivery shortages.
