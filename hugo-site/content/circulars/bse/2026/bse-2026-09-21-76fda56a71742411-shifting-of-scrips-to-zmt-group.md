## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the shifting of various scrips to the ‘Z’/‘MT’ group pursuant to SEBI Master Circular provisions on penal actions for non-compliance with the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and SEBI (Depositories & Participants) Regulations, 2018.

## Key Points

- Scrips of companies failing to comply with Regulation 76 (Submission of Reconciliation of share capital audit report) for two consecutive quarters (March 2026 & June 2026) will be transferred to the ‘Z’ group.
- Compliance deadline is September 29, 2026, failing which the transfer takes effect on October 05, 2026.
- Trades in these scrips executed in the ‘Z’/‘MT’ group will be settled on a Trade for Trade basis.
- Several other companies are already listed under the ‘Z’/‘MT’ group for continuous non-compliance of Regulation 76.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of penal actions under SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 for non-compliance of Regulation 76 of SEBI (Depositories & Participants) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

Affected listed entities must submit the Reconciliation of Share Capital Audit Report on or before September 29, 2026, to avoid being transferred to the ‘Z’ group.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 21, 2026
- **Compliance Deadline:** September 29, 2026
- **Effective Date of Shifting:** October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Transfer to the ‘Z’ group restricts trading to a Trade for Trade basis, significantly impacting liquidity and market participation for the non-compliant scrips.
