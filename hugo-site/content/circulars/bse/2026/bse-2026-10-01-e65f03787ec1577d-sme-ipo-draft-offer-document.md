## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document for the SME IPO of LGC Engineering Limited on October 01, 2026. The document is hosted on the SME website for public review and comments for at least 21 days in accordance with ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Company Name: LGC Engineering Limited
- Filing Date: October 01, 2026
- Regulation: Schedule VI of ICDR Regulations
- Public Comment Period: At least 21 days from the notice date
- Email for Comments: equity.ipo@bseindia.com
- Hosted Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes announced; routine draft offer document filing under ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public can review the draft offer document and submit comments within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date / Filing Date: October 01, 2026
- Comment Deadline: 21 days from October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Initiates the public comment phase for LGC Engineering Limited's proposed SME IPO, allowing market participants to review disclosures and provide feedback.
