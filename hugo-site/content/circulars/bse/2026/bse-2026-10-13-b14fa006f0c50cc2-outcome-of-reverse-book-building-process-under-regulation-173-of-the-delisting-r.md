## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the outcome of the Reverse Book Building Process (RBBP) under Regulation 17(3) of the SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021, for the voluntary delisting of Hitech Corporation Limited.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Hitech Corporation Limited
- **Acquirer:** Geetanjali Trading and Investments Private Limited
- **Offer Details:** Acquisition of 43,91,220 equity shares held by public shareholders at a floor price of Rs. 252/- per share aggregating up to Rs. 110.65 crores.
- **Bidding Period:** October 07, 2026, to October 13, 2026.
- **Registrar:** MUFG Intime India Private Limited (formerly Link Intime India Private Limited)
- **Manager to the Offer:** Kreo Capital Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with Regulation 17(3) of the SEBI Delisting Regulations, requiring formal disclosure and announcement of the reverse book building outcome by the registrar and manager in consultation with the acquirer.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public shareholders, the acquirer, and intermediaries must comply with the disclosures, bidding outcomes, and subsequent settlement timelines established by the stock exchanges.

## Important Dates

- **Bidding Window Start Date:** October 07, 2026
- **Bidding Window End Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Notice Date:** October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Marks a critical milestone in the voluntary delisting process of Hitech Corporation Limited from BSE and NSE, determining share acquisition pricing and subsequent delisting status for investors.
