## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission for trading of 16,16,088 equity shares of Jhaveri Credits & Capital Limited (Scrip Code: 531550) with effect from September 25, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation amongst U R Energy (India) Private Limited and Jhaveri Credits & Capital Limited under Sections 230 to 232 of the Companies Act, 2013.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Jhaveri Credits & Capital Limited
- Scrip Code: 531550
- Security Details: 16,16,088 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each
- Purpose: Issued pursuant to Scheme of Amalgamation with U R Energy (India) Private Limited
- ISIN Number: INE865D01012
- Distinctive Numbers: 9485937 to 11102024
- Date of Allotment: June 29, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This notice represents the operational listing execution of a corporate amalgamation scheme approved under the Companies Act, 2013.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants must ensure that the newly listed shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares and are reflected properly in trading and depository systems.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Date of Allotment: June 29, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increases the paid-up equity capital base of Jhaveri Credits & Capital Limited following its amalgamation with U R Energy (India) Private Limited, allowing shareholders to trade the newly allotted shares on the exchange.
