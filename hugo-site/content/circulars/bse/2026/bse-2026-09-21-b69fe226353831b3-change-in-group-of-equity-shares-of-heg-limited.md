## Summary

Trading in the Equity Shares of HEG Limited (Scrip Code: 509631) will be permitted under the ‘A’ Group and transferred from the “T” Group with effect from September 22, 2026, in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260902-12 dated September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: HEG Limited
- Scrip Code: 509631
- Previous Group: T Group
- New Group: A Group
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of trading segment group on BSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the change in group for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normalization of trading group status for HEG Limited shares on the exchange platform.
