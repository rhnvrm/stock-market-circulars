## Summary

Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund is offering two Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) - Apex Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund and Apex Equity Long-Short Fund (SIF) - along with SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform from August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: Apex Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund and Apex Equity Long-Short Fund (SIF).
- NFO Period: August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 31, 2026.
- Minimum Investment Criterion: For New Investors, if the total amount invested at the PAN and Holding Pattern Level is not at least Rs 10 Lac, the order will be rejected at the time of NFO allotment.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 28, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on August 24, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 10, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: August 24, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline (BSE Clearing Account): August 28, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and distributors must ensure client subscriptions meet the minimum Rs 10 Lac threshold and clear funds are made available within the stipulated timelines to prevent order cancellation.
