## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Shree TNB Polymers Limited, 17,06,000 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 53/- per equity share on September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shree TNB Polymers Limited
- Shares Subscribed: 17,06,000 equity shares
- Allocation Price: Rs. 53/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are notified of the anchor investor allotment in compliance with public issue disclosure norms.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The anchor investor subscription indicates institutional confidence and establishes price reference points ahead of the public issue opening.
