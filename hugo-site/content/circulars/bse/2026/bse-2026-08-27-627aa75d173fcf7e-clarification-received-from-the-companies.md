## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a surveillance notice concerning significant price and volume movements observed in various scrips. Replies containing clarifications have been received from 10 companies, while responses from another 7 companies are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- **Action Trigger:** Significant price/volume movement observed in recent trading sessions.
- **Companies with Replies Received:** 
  1. Burnpur Cement Ltd (532931)
  2. Chandrima Mercantiles Ltd (540829)
  3. DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Ltd (543193)
  4. Ind-Swift Laboratories Ltd (532305)
  5. Inter State Oil Carrier Ltd (530259)
  6. Jain Irrigation Systems Ltd (500219)
  7. Oriental Aromatics Ltd (500078)
  8. Quess Corp Ltd (539978)
  9. Super Tannery Ltd (523842)
  10. TVS Srichakra Ltd (509243)
- **Companies with Replies Awaited:** 
  1. Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd (542176)
  2. CMX Holdings Ltd (532217)
  3. Deep Polymers Ltd (541778)
  4. Mahesh Developers Ltd (542677)
  5. Network People Services Technologies Ltd (544396)
  6. PVP Ventures Ltd (517556)
  7. Triumph International Finance India Ltd (532131)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine surveillance disclosure notice issued under exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities flagged by the exchange for unusual market activity are required to submit clarifications to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and aids investors in evaluating scrips experiencing high volatility and unusual trading volumes.
