## Summary

Trading members are informed that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Swastika Infra Limited, 26,08,782 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 185/- per equity share as of September 22, 2026.

- Company Name: Swastika Infra Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investors Portion)
- Shares Subscribed: 26,08,782 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 185/- per equity share
- Date of Announcement: September 22, 2026

None

Trading members and market participants are to note the anchor investor subscription details for the public issue.

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Announcement Date: September 22, 2026
