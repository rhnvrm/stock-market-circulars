## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the commencement of trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of VICEROY HOTELS LIMITED effective from September 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: VICEROY HOTELS LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Trading Period for Renunciation: September 03, 2026 to September 07, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: September 03, 2026 to September 11, 2026
- Scrip Code: 751138
- Scrip ID: VHLTD-RE1
- ISIN No.: INE048C20025
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R

## Regulatory Changes

- Dealings in Rights Entitlements permitted on the Exchange starting September 03, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Settlement shall be on T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis.
- REs are tradable in dematerialized form only.
- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs and include a disclaimer in contract notes regarding the terms of participation, renunciation, and lapse of unexercised REs.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Start Date: September 03, 2026
- RE Trading End Date: September 07, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: September 03, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of Viceroy Hotels Limited can trade or renounce their rights entitlements within the specified window and participate in the upcoming rights issue.
