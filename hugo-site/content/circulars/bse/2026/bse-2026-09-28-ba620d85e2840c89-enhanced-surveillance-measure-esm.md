## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, specifying securities meeting inclusion criteria, moving to lower stages, or exiting the framework. Affected securities will attract a minimum 100% margin and shift to the trade-for-trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 30, 2026, on open positions as of September 29, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities moving to lower Stage I of the ESM framework w.e.f. September 29, 2026, are included in Annexure I.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 30, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 29, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the ESM framework w.e.f. September 29, 2026, is given in Annexure II.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework updates, margin requirements, price band modifications, and settlement segment shifts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement changes effective September 30, 2026, for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage movements and exits: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade shift: September 30, 2026
- Open positions reference date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened surveillance measures increase margin obligations and trading restrictions for short-listed securities to safeguard market integrity and manage volatility.
