## Summary

Effective from Friday, August 7, 2026, the equity shares of Fusion Klassroom Edutech Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE in the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Fusion Klassroom Edutech Limited
- Scrip Code: 544856
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: KLASSROOM
- ISIN: INE1LMA01010
- Issue Price: Rs. 159/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and premium Rs. 149/-)
- Market Lot: 800 Equity Shares
- Segment: SME (Trade-for-Trade for 10 trading days)
- Date of Allotment: August 5, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are effected in dematerialised form and adhere to the minimum market lot of 800 equity shares. The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing day.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 6, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: August 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Fusion Klassroom Edutech Limited's equity shares on the BSE SME platform.
