## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the outcome of the Fixed Price Delisting Process for Elpro International Limited under Regulation 17(3) of the SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021. The offer was made by the Acquirers (I G E (India) Private Limited and Zenox Technology Services Private Limited) to acquire up to 4,23,70,160 equity shares at a fixed price of Rs. 181.80 per share from August 4, 2026, to August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Acquirers: I G E (India) Private Limited and Zenox Technology Services Private Limited.
- Target Company: Elpro International Limited.
- Delisting Offer Size: Up to 4,23,70,160 Equity Shares.
- Fixed Offer Price: Rs. 181.80 per Equity Share.
- Tendering Period: Tuesday, August 4, 2026, to Monday, August 10, 2026.
- Registrar to the Offer: MUFG Intime India Private Limited (formerly Link Intime India Private Limited).
- Manager to the Offer: Motilal Oswal Investment Advisors Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Process conducted in accordance with Regulation 17(3) of the SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021.

## Compliance Requirements

- Acquirers must make payments to all successful shareholders who tendered their shares in accordance with Regulation 24 and the settlement calendar of the Indian Clearing Corporation Limited.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 10, 2026
- Tendering Period: August 4, 2026 to August 10, 2026
- Outcome Date: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Successful completion of the fixed price delisting process enables the delisting of Elpro International Limited's equity shares from the stock exchange in accordance with regulatory provisions.
