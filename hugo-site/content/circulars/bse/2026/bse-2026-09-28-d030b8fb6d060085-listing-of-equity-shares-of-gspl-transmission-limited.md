## Summary

Effective from September 30, 2026, equity shares of GSPL Transmission Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE in the T Group of Securities. The listing follows a Scheme of Arrangement involving Gujarat Gas Limited, Gujarat State Petroleum Corporation Limited (GSPCL), and Gujarat State Petronet Limited (GSPL).

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544801
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: GSPLTRANS
- ISIN No.: INE1TUG01013
- Total Securities Listed: 31,27,43,617 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid pursuant to Scheme of Arrangement.
- Distinctive Numbers: 1 - 312743617
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: T (Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days)

## Regulatory Changes

- Shares admitted under T Group of Securities and subject to Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI Circulars CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 and SEBI/HO/MRD-TPD1/CIR/P/2023/55.
- Included in special pre-open session (SPOS) for IPO and other category of scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the trade-for-trade restrictions and special pre-open session guidelines for trading in GSPL Transmission Limited.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 30, 2026
- Appointed Date (Scheme): April 1, 2025
- Effective Date (Scheme): May 1, 2026
- Date of Allotment: July 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and investors can trade in GSPL Transmission Limited shares starting September 30, 2026, under the trade-for-trade segment norms for the initial 10 trading sessions.
