## Summary

Trading members are informed that several companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of their commercial papers. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of these companies starting from the respective ex-dates/record dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects commercial papers of multiple companies including ECL Finance, Nuvama Wealth, Can Fin Homes, Capri Global Capital, Bajaj Finance, and others.
- Record dates and ex-dates span from August 3, 2026, to August 12, 2026.
- Members must refrain from trading in the specified commercial paper scrips to ensure smooth settlement of redemption.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no transactions are executed in the specified commercial paper scrips on or after the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates: August 3, 2026, August 6, 2026, August 11, 2026, and August 12, 2026.
- Ex-Dates: August 3, 2026, August 6, 2026, August 11, 2026, and August 12, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Temporary cessation of secondary market trading for the listed commercial papers preceding their redemption.
