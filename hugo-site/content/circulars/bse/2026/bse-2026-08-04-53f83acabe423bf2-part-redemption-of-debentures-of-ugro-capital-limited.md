## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that UGRO Capital Limited has fixed a record date for the purpose of part redemption and payment of interest on its non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in the debentures will continue with a reduced face value effective from the record date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: UGRO Capital Limited
- Scrip Code: 976429
- Scrip ID: UCL-20-2-29-CP
- ISIN: INE583D07554
- Record Date: 05/08/2026
- Ex-Date: 05/08/2026
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 75,000/- per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in the NCDs of the company will be conducted with a reduced face value.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the reduced face value and settlement schedule for the debentures.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 05/08/2026
- Ex-Date: 05/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding the NCDs will receive part redemption proceeds and interest payments as per the schedule, with subsequent trading occurring on the reduced face value basis.
