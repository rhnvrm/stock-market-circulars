## Summary

BSE has published the demat auction list for Settlement No. 838 dated March 13, 2026. The circular details securities that are subject to auction due to delivery shortfalls or settlement failures in the demat segment.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement obligations and delivery shortages for trading members.
- Specifies scrip codes, names, and auction quantities for affected securities.
- Part of routine exchange clearing and settlement operations.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard exchange settlement mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must participate in the demat auction process and ensure required securities or funds are made available as per BSE settlement schedules.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date / Settlement No. 838: March 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low impact on the broader market as this represents standard operational settlement procedures for clearing delivery shortages.
