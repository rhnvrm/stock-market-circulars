## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in certain debt securities (debentures) effective August 17, 2026, following redemption or applications for delisting made by the respective companies.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading applies to specific debt securities detailed in the notice.
- Affected Scrip Codes/ISINs include: Scrip 975798 (INE0YNM15018 - PLUM25-3 SERIES A1), Scrip 975609 (INE0VIC15015 - 2024 PL DAMIAN), and Scrip 976415 (INE1M7715013 - CARTERA MFI 2025).
- Reason for suspension: Redemption / Application for Delisting.
- Effective date of suspension: August 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debt securities of the companies with effect from August 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors holding the specified debt securities will no longer be able to trade them on the BSE platform starting from the effective date.
