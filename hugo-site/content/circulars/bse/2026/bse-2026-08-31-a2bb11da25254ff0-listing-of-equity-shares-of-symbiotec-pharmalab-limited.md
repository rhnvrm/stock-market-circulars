## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, September 1, 2026, the equity shares of Symbiotec Pharmalab Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities. The public issue price was Rs. 988/- per share with a face value of Rs. 2/-.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544889
- ISIN No: INE899I01028
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: SYMBIOTEC
- Number of Securities: 64,256,112 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each fully paid up
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 64256112
- Market Lot: 1
- Date of Allotment: August 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Tuesday, September 1, 2026.
- Necessary corporate actions have been executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 28, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors can trade the equity shares of Symbiotec Pharmalab Limited on the BSE exchange starting September 1, 2026, under the 'B' group category.
