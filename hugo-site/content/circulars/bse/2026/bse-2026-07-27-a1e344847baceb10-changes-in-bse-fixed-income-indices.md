## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced constituent changes for the BSE 4–8-year G-Sec Index and BSE 8–13-year G-Sec Index, effective July 31, 2026. The BSE India 10 Year Sovereign Bond Index and BSE India 5 Year Sovereign Bond Index remain unchanged.

## Key Points

- **BSE 4-8 Year G-Sec Index:** Adds 7.02% GS 2031 (ISIN: IN0020240076) and drops 6.01% GS 2030 (ISIN: IN0020250067).
- **BSE 8-13 Year G-Sec Index:** Adds 7.54% GS 2036 (ISIN: IN0020220029) and drops 6.33% GS 2035 (ISIN: IN0020250026).
- **Unchanged Indices:** BSE India 10 Year Sovereign Bond Index and BSE India 5 Year Sovereign Bond Index.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine index constituent update by BSE Index Services Private Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- None specified for market participants; informational notice regarding index maintenance.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Notice Date:** July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact on general market operations, affecting only index-tracking funds and benchmark users.
