## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has issued the client margin reporting schedule for the Equity Segment for the month of August 2026, outlining the trade dates, last dates of margin reporting, and corresponding penalty dates.

## Key Points

- Specifies reporting and penalty timelines for every trade date in August 2026.
- Issued by BSE Clearing Limited.
- Applies to all trading members in the Equity Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine operational schedule update for margin reporting.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure client margins are reported within the stipulated deadlines to avoid financial penalties.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Month:** August 2026 (Trade dates ranging from 03-08-2026 to 31-08-2026).
- **Notice Date:** 30 July 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures operational transparency and compliance for member brokers and clients regarding margin collections and penalty enforcement.
