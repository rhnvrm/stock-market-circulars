## Summary

Trading members are informed that effective from Friday, September 18, 2026, the equity shares of Raksan Transformers Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE SME segment in the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Raksan Transformers Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544924
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** RAKSAN
- **ISIN:** INE1S7M01017
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10/- per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 273/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 263/-)
- **No. of Securities:** 20,893,400 Equity Shares
- **Market Lot:** 400 Equity Shares
- **Date of Allotment:** September 16, 2026
- **Market Maker:** Hem Finlease Private Limited
- **Registrar:** Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only in accordance with SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are effected in the minimum market lot of 400 equity shares.
- Corporate action executed for lock-in period marking in depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 17, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** September 16, 2026
- **Listing / Effective Date:** September 18, 2026
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Raksan Transformers Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with standard trade-for-trade monitoring for the initial 10 trading sessions.
