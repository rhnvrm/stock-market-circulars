## Summary

Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited has announced a buyback of its fully paid-up equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each from the open market through the Stock Exchanges. The maximum buyback price will not exceed Rs. 1530/- per equity share for an aggregate amount not exceeding Rs. 900,00,00,000/-.

## Key Points

- Buyback Type: Open Market through Stock Exchanges.
- Maximum Buyback Price: Up to Rs. 1530/- per equity share.
- Maximum Buyback Size: Not exceeding Rs. 900 crores.
- Opening Date: September 04, 2026.
- Company's Broker: Kotak Securities Limited (Clearing No. 0673).

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 updated as of July 6, 2026, and Exchange Notice No. 20260731-9.

## Compliance Requirements

- Payment obligations relating to the buyback must be completed before the last date of the buyback.

## Important Dates

- Date of Opening: September 04, 2026.
- Last Date for Buyback: Earlier of December 11, 2026 (66 working days), completion by deploying maximum amount, or earlier board-determined closure date.

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts liquidity and share capital structure of Great Eastern Shipping Company Limited through open market share repurchase.
