## Summary

Trading members are informed that new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by several companies have been listed and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are traded in dematerialised form under specified ISINs.
- Standard market lot and denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs apply with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Companies with newly listed commercial papers include DSP Finance Private Limited, SBICAP Securities Limited, L&T Metro Rail (Hyderabad) Limited, Poonawalla Fincorp Limited, and Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard debt segment guidelines and dematerialised settlement rules.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 11, 2026
- Allotment / Effective Date: August 10, 2026 / August 11, 2026
- Redemption Dates: November 2026 (varying by issuer)

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing providing liquidity and trading access for newly issued commercial papers on the debt market segment.
