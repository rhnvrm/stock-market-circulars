## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of newly listed companies, HERO MOTORS LIMITED (Exchange ticker: 544936) and SS Retail Ltd (Exchange ticker: 544934), to the BSE indices effective at the open of Friday, September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- HERO MOTORS LIMITED (Ticker: 544936) and SS Retail Ltd (Ticker: 544934) are listed on BSE effective September 23, 2026.
- Both stocks will be added to the BSE IPO index effective at the open of September 25, 2026.
- Actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None.

## Important Dates

- Listing Date: September 23, 2026
- Index Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine index inclusion with minimal direct market impact beyond standard index fund rebalancing.
