## Summary

Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund is launching the KOTAK NIFTY CAPITAL MARKETS INDEX FUND - NFO along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from September 15, 2026, to September 29, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund Offering: KOTAK NIFTY CAPITAL MARKETS INDEX FUND (Regular and Direct plans including Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- NFO Window: September 15, 2026, to September 29, 2026 (extended till midnight for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 05, 2026.
- ISINs Issued:
  - Regular Growth: INF174KA1G31
  - Regular IDCW Payout: INF174KA1G49
  - Regular IDCW Reinvestment: INF174KA1G56
  - Direct Growth: INF174KA1G07
  - Direct IDCW Payout: INF174KA1G15
  - Direct IDCW Reinvestment: INF174KA1G23

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 01, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on September 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: September 15, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: September 29, 2026
- Clear Funds Cut-off: October 01, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to subscription and SIP registrations for the new Kotak Nifty Capital Markets Index Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform.
