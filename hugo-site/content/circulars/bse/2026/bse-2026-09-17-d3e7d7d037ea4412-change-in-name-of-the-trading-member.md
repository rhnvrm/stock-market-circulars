## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that the name of trading member FINSOL SECURITIES PRIVATE LIMITED (Member No. 6947) has been changed to “ATHENA CAPITAL PRIVATE LIMITED” w.e.f. Friday, 18th September 2026.

## Key Points

- Former Trading Member Name: FINSOL SECURITIES PRIVATE LIMITED
- New Trading Member Name: ATHENA CAPITAL PRIVATE LIMITED
- Member Code: 6947
- Effective Date: 18th September 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members or clients requiring clarification can contact the Membership Operations department via email at membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 18th September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal impact restricted to administrative record updates for the concerned trading member.
