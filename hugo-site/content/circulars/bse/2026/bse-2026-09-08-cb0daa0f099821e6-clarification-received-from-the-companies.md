## Summary

The Exchange has sought clarifications from various companies regarding recent significant price and volume movements in their scrips to ensure investors have access to relevant information. Replies have been received from several companies, while responses from others remain awaited.

## Key Points

- **Companies that submitted replies:** Birla Cable Ltd, Pudumjee Paper Products Ltd, Sanghvi Brands Ltd, Welspun Enterprises Ltd, Western Overseas Study Abroad Ltd, and Zydus Wellness Ltd.
- **Companies with replies awaited:** Billwin Industries Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Mukta Agriculture Ltd, Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Ltd, Nirmitee Robotics India Ltd, and Sera Investments & Finance India Ltd.
- **Objective:** To maintain market transparency and ensure investors are informed of any material developments influencing price or volume changes.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard surveillance monitoring procedure under exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies observed with significant price or volume movements are required to clarify and disclose any price-sensitive information to the exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 08 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct operational impact; serves as a routine disclosure mechanism for market surveillance.
