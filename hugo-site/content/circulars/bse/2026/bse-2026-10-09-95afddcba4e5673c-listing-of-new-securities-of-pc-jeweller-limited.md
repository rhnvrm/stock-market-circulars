## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of PC Jeweller Limited (Scrip Code: 534809) with effect from Monday, October 12, 2026. A total of 1,10,50,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 17/- per share (issue price Rs. 18/-) to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: PC Jeweller Limited
- Scrip Code: 534809
- ISIN: INE785M01021
- Number of Shares: 1,10,50,000 equity shares
- Face Value: Re. 1/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 18/- per share (including premium of Rs. 17/-)
- Allottees: Promoters (preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion)
- Effective Date of Listing: October 12, 2026
- Date of Allotment: August 3, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 9741084856 to 9752134855
- Lock-in Upto: April 11, 2028

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing and trading permission of the new equity shares. The shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 3, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: October 12, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 11, 2028

## Impact Assessment

The issuance and listing of 1.105 crore equity shares pursuant to warrant conversion expands the company's equity capital base and increases promoter holding, reflecting capital restructuring and warrant exercise.
