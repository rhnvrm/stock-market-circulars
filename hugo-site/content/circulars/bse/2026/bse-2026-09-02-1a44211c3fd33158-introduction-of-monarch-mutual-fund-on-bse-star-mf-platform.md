## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the activation of Monarch Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 02, 2026. Transactions for NRI and Minor clients are supported, subject to a list of restricted countries for NRI investors. Additionally, the Monarch Overnight Fund NFO scheme will be enabled for order routing starting September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Monarch Mutual Fund is activated on the BSE StAR MF Platform from September 02, 2026.
- Transactions for NRI and Minor clients can be accepted under Monarch Mutual Fund schemes.
- Applications from NRI investors belonging to North Korea, Iran, Myanmar, Pakistan, Canada, and the United States of America are not allowed.
- Monarch Overnight Fund NFO scheme will be enabled for order routing starting September 03, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that NRI applications from restricted countries (North Korea, Iran, Myanmar, Pakistan, Canada, and USA) are not accepted for Monarch Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- Activation Date: September 02, 2026
- NFO Scheme Order Routing Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment choices on the BSE StAR MF platform by adding Monarch Mutual Fund and its schemes for members and investors.
