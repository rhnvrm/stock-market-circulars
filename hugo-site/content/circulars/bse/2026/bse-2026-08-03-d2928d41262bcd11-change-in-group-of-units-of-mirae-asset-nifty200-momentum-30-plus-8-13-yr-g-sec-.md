## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the Group Code for the units of Mirae Asset Nifty200 Momentum 30 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 50:50 ETF (Scrip Code: 544841) is changing from 'B' to 'F' effective August 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Mirae Asset Nifty200 Momentum 30 Plus 8-13 yr G-Sec 50:50 ETF
- **Scrip Code:** 544841
- **Previous Group:** 'B'
- **New Group:** 'F'
- **Effective Date:** August 4, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or compliance changes; this is an operational group classification update by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the group change for order entry and risk management systems.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 3, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on market participants, limited to administrative group code updating in trading terminals.
