## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of further equity shares issued by seven companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS), effective from Wednesday, August 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 7
- Effective date for trading: August 5, 2026
- Securities issued under: ESOP / ESOS
- Affected companies include Balrampur Chini Mills Limited, BlackBuck Limited, Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited, Cybertech Systems and Software Limited, Shadowfax Technologies Limited, Shoppers Stop Limited, and Tube Investments of India Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are to take note of the newly listed securities and their respective lock-in statuses (if applicable).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 4, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: August 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing of shares resulting in minor equity base expansions for the respective issuing companies.
