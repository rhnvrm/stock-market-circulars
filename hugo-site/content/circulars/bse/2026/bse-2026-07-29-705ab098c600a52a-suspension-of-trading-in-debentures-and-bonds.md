## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the fixation of record dates for payment of interest and redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds for various companies. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures and bonds as per the schedule.

## Key Points

- Notice covers multiple companies including Birla Corporation Ltd., Sakthi Finance Ltd., Power Finance Corporation Ltd., REC Limited, and others.
- Specifies scrip codes, scrip IDs, ISIN numbers, record dates, ex-dates, and distribution/redemption details.
- Trading members are cautioned against dealing in the listed debentures/bonds during the restricted period.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the advisory and refrain from trading in the specified NCDs and bonds on their respective record/ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 29 Jul 2026
- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: August 2026 (ranging from 03/08/2026 to 17/08/2026 depending on the security)

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of trading in specific debt securities for record date processing to facilitate interest payment and redemption.
