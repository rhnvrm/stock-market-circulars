## Summary

Nippon India Mutual Fund has announced the merger of Nippon India Nifty AAA PSU Bond Plus SDL - Sep 2026 Maturity 50:50 Index Fund (Transferor Scheme) into Nippon India Corporate Bond Fund (Transferee Scheme). The merger takes effect after business hours on September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Transferor Scheme: Nippon India Nifty AAA PSU Bond Plus SDL - Sep 2026 Maturity 50:50 Index Fund
- Transferee Scheme: Nippon India Corporate Bond Fund
- Effective Date of Merger: After close of business hours on September 02, 2026
- Exit Load Waiver Period: August 04, 2026, to September 02, 2026, for investors opting to redeem or switch out without exit load.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Members can process redemptions, switches, and fresh lump sum/additional purchases in the Transferor Scheme through BSE StAR MF during the exit load waiver period.
- Fresh SIP and STP-In registrations ceased after the cut-off time on August 03, 2026.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP) will automatically migrate to the Transferee Scheme after business hours on September 02, 2026, unless cancelled by investors.

## Important Dates

- August 03, 2026: Cut-off for fresh SIP/STP-In registrations
- August 04, 2026 to September 02, 2026: Exit load waiver period for redemption/switch-out/switch-in
- September 02, 2026: Effective date of merger after business hours; transferor scheme ceases to be available for transaction processing

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding units in the Transferor Scheme are provided an option to exit without penalty or migrate automatically to the Transferee Scheme following the merger timelines.
