## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that the Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market Index Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and facilities such as SIP, STP, and SWP on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market Index Fund
- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Facilities Available:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- **Eligible Plans & ISINs:**
  - Regular Plan - Growth: `INF209KC1761`
  - Direct Plan - Growth: `INF209KC1753`

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational circular regarding mutual fund platform availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can facilitate transactions in the specified schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with existing regulatory guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Reference Notice Date:** September 29, 2026 (Notice No. 20260929-44)
- **Effective Date for Transactions:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute ongoing transactions and systematic investment/withdrawal plans for the Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market Index Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform with minimal operational impact.
