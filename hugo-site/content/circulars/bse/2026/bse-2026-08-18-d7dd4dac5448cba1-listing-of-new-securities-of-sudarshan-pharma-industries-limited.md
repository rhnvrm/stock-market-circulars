## Summary

Trading members are informed that 90,00,000 new equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 15.98/- to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants by Sudarshan Pharma Industries Limited are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Wednesday, August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 543828
- **Company Name:** Sudarshan Pharma Industries Limited
- **Security Details:** 90,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 15.98/-
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 16.98/-
- **ISIN:** INE00TV01023
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 240658871 to 249658870
- **Date of Allotment:** 12/06/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Lock-in Details:** 90,00,000 shares (Distinctive Nos. 240658871 to 249658870) are under lock-in up to 20/02/2028.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 12/06/2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** 19/08/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 20/02/2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing of preferential shares resulting from warrant conversion. Increases the paid-up equity capital of the company with specific shares locked-in until February 2028.
