## Summary

BSE has issued a surveillance notice noting significant price and volume movements in various scrips. The Exchange sought clarifications from multiple listed companies to ensure trading members and investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarifications have been received from 15 companies including Advance Agrolife Ltd, Aeroflex Enterprises Ltd, Andhra Paper Ltd, and others.
- Replies are awaited from 18 companies including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, and CMX Holdings Ltd.
- The notice aims to maintain market transparency and protect investor interests amidst abnormal price/volume behavior.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies observed with significant price/volume movements are required to submit timely clarifications to the Exchange as requested by Surveillance.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 13 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding scrips experiencing unusual market activity and highlights companies pending regulatory compliance responses.
