## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities (Non-Convertible Debentures) issued by Bajaj Housing Finance Limited on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective October 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Bajaj Housing Finance Limited
- **Security Type:** Privately Placed Debt Instrument (NCD)
- **Scrip Code:** 978036
- **Scrip ID:** 787BHFL36
- **Detail Name:** BHFL-7.87%-04-08-36-PVT
- **ISIN:** INE377Y07680
- **Quantity:** 250,000 units
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Rs. 100,000.00 each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 96,191.72
- **Date of Allotment:** October 7, 2026
- **Rate of Interest:** 7.87% p.a. (Payable annually from 04/08/2027 to 04/08/2036)
- **Date of Redemption:** August 4, 2036
- **Credit Rating:** CRISIL AAA/Stable, IND AAA/Stable
- **Tick Size:** 1 paise

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine admission and listing of privately placed debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialised form under the specified ISIN and adhere to a tick size of 1 paise.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 7, 2026
- **Listing / Effective Date:** October 9, 2026
- **Interest Payment Dates:** Annually from August 4, 2027 to August 4, 2036
- **Redemption Date:** August 4, 2036

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official trading access on the BSE debt segment for investors holding the privately placed NCDs of Bajaj Housing Finance Limited.
