## Summary

BSE has announced the allocation of a new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of Jattashankar Industries Ltd following its stock split.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Jattashankar Industries Ltd
- Scrip Code: 514318
- Corporate Action: Sub-division of equity shares from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-
- New ISIN: INE722N01014
- Ex-Date: October 19, 2026 (DR-738/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are tagged with the new ISIN effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026
- Ex-Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for trading members and market participants regarding the updated ISIN for equity shares post stock split.
