## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL), formerly known as Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL), has announced the availability of an optional facility in the Real Time Risk Management System (RTRMS) for monitoring intraday uncrystallised Mark-to-Market (MTM) losses for futures contracts. This facility allows Clearing Members to track cumulative MTM loss limits of their constituent Trading Members at the Clearing Member level. The system computes MTM profit and loss in real-time across both client and proprietary positions, aggregating only net losses for monitoring. The facility is entirely non-mandatory and does not affect existing risk management, margin monitoring, or settlement processes.

## Key Points

- **Optional Facility:** Clearing Members can optionally request the activation of this facility in the Real Time Risk Management System (RTRMS).
- **Intraday MTM Monitoring:** The facility allows monitoring of intraday uncrystallised MTM losses arising from open futures positions at the Clearing Member level.
- **Constituent Tracking:** Once activated, Clearing Members can view the cumulative MTM loss limits of their constituent Trading Members.
- **Real-time Computation:** MTM profit and loss are computed in real-time based on open futures positions and the latest available futures prices. This is calculated across both client and proprietary positions, with only net losses aggregated at the Clearing Member level.
- **Independence from Margin Monitoring:** Monitoring of intraday MTM losses and collateral-based margin requirements are carried out independently and remain mutually exclusive.
- **No Process Changes:** The tool is purely informational and does not result in any changes to the existing risk management or margin monitoring processes of BSECL.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Reference Circular:** This notice is in continuation of BSE Circular No. 20260617-36 dated June 17, 2026, which initially introduced the facility for monitoring intraday uncrystallised MTM losses for futures contracts.
- **Activation Basis:** No regulatory policy shift is mandated; the facility is provided as an optional value-added system update for Risk Management.

## Compliance Requirements

- **No Mandatory Compliance:** There are no mandatory actions or compliance filings required for Clearing Members or Trading Members.
- **Requesting Activation:** Clearing Members who wish to avail of this optional monitoring facility must submit a formal request via email to the BSECL Risk Department at risk.monitoring@bseclearing.com or risk.iccl@bseclearing.com.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date:** Immediate (available on request as of August 12, 2026)
- **Previous Reference Date:** June 17, 2026 (Circular No. 20260617-36)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Nil. Since the facility is non-mandatory and does not alter trading or margin constraints, there is no direct impact on market liquidity or trading behavior.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Clearing Members who opt-in will gain improved real-time risk visibility over their constituent Trading Members' intraday positions. Those who do not opt-in face no operational changes.
- **Risk Management Impact:** Positive. For participating members, it consolidates net futures losses at the Clearing Member level in real-time, helping prevent unexpected exposure spikes without interfering with standard margin checks.
