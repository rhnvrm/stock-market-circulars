## Summary

BSE has announced the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window for Takeover) of shares of J.A. FINANCE LIMITED (Scrip Code: 543860, ISIN: INE825B01010). The offer size is up to 27,68,671 equity shares at an offer price of Rs. 44.82 per share, scheduled from October 19, 2026, to November 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** J.A. FINANCE LIMITED
- **Symbol / Scrip Code:** JAFINANCE / 543860
- **ISIN:** INE825B01010
- **Offer Size:** Up to 27,68,671 Equity Shares
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 44.82
- **Opening Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Closing Date:** November 2, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1

## Regulatory Changes

Operational guidelines and schedule adjustments for participation in the Acquisition Window (Takeover) mechanism as per extant Exchange norms and SEBI takeover regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of investors is registered and/or updated with correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bids can be placed for investors having shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians should note platform URLs (iBBS, RTRMS, Extranet) and contact details for technical support or password resets.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** October 19, 2026 (9:15 a.m.)
- **Issue Closing Date:** November 2, 2026 (3:30 p.m.)
- **Give-up / Take-up Period:** October 19, 2026 to October 30, 2026 (Give-up on last day: November 2, 2026 up to 3:30 p.m.; Take-up on last day: November 2, 2026 up to 4:00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly execution and settlement of the takeover offer for J.A. FINANCE LIMITED, providing clear timelines and platform accessibility for trading members, custodians, and eligible shareholders.
