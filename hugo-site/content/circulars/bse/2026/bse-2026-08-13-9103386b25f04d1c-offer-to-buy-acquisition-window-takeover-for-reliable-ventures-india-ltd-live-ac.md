## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice detailing the live activities schedule and operational parameters for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window for Takeover) of shares of Reliable Ventures India Ltd. The bidding window is scheduled from Friday, August 14, 2026, to Friday, August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: RELIABLE VENTURES INDIA LTD
- Security Symbol: RELIABVEN
- ISIN: INE419H01019
- Scrip Code: 532124
- Offer Size: Up to 28,63,354 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00
- Offer Price: Rs. 21.00
- Opening Date: Friday, August 14, 2026
- Closing Date: Friday, August 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Operational schedule issuance under existing takeover acquisition window frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians must follow operational instructions provided in prior Exchange notices (No. 20150930-1 and 20170203-29).

## Important Dates

- Opening Date: August 14, 2026
- Closing Date: August 28, 2026
- Give-up / Take-up Period: August 14, 2026 to August 27, 2026
- Last Day Give-up: August 28, 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Last Day Take-up: August 28, 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates market participation and share tendering for the takeover offer of Reliable Ventures India Ltd within the specified acquisition window timelines.
