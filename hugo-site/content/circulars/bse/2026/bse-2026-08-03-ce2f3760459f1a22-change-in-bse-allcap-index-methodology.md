## Summary

BSE Index Services Pvt Ltd has announced methodology changes to the BSE AllCap Index, effective September 21, 2026. The upcoming scheduled reconstitution of the index will be based on these revised guidelines, updating eligibility criteria, liquidity qualifications, and interim universe screening.

## Key Points

- Effective date for the revised methodology is September 21, 2026.
- Updates eligibility criteria including trading frequency, impact cost, listing history, and exclusion of specific groups (Y, Z, P, InvITs, SME platform).
- Introduces a Liquidity-Qualified Universe and Interim Universe screening steps.
- Aims for a minimum company count of 1200 at each reconstitution.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revised eligibility rules exclude companies belonging to groups Y, Z, P, and InvITs, alongside companies with less than six months of listing history (one month for IPOs/spin-offs).
- Stricter liquidity screens requiring trading frequency >= 80% and impact cost <= 1% over the prior six-month period.
- Exclusion of companies ranking in the bottom 10 percentile based on annualized traded value and 6-month average daily float-adjusted market capitalization from the Interim Universe.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed companies must maintain required trading frequencies and liquidity parameters to qualify for index inclusion under the revised framework.

## Important Dates

- Announcement Date: August 03, 2026
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reconstitution of the BSE AllCap Index will follow the new methodology, potentially altering index composition and constituent weights.
