## Summary

Effective from Thursday, August 6, 2026, the equity shares of Aarvi Encon Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of Company: Aarvi Encon Limited
- Scrip Code: 544850
- Scrip ID: AARVI
- ISIN No: INE754X01016
- Total Securities: 1,48,44,100 Equity Shares of Rs. 10 each fully paid up
- Market Lot: 1 (One) Share
- Effective Date: August 6, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities shall be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Physical shares have been enfaced with non-transferability.
- Demat shares have the lock-in period marked in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 4, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: August 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables liquidity and trading for shareholders of Aarvi Encon Limited on the BSE exchange platform.
