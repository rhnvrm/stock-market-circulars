## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding Trading Members to submit the Corrective Action Report (CAR) for any non-compliances identified during the Cyber Security & Cyber Resilience Audit for the period ended March 31, 2026. The deadline for submission is September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading members must take corrective action for each non-compliance reported in the Cyber Security & Cyber Resilience Audit for the period ending March 31, 2026.
- Auditors will review the CAR and submit the final compliance status (Compliant or Non-Compliant) through the BEFS Portal.
- Members registered with both BSE and NSE shall submit their Cyber Security & Cyber Resilience Audit report exclusively to NSE as part of technology-based sharing mechanisms.
- Submissions must adhere to the rationalization and standardization of penalties framework for Trading Members.

## Regulatory Changes

- Streamlining of common submissions between BSE and NSE for dual-registered trading members.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must submit the Corrective Action Report electronically through the BEFS portal under the specified path: `membersso.bseindia.com > BEFS > Cyber security & Cyber Resilience Audit > Cyber Resilience Audit Repot (Member CSCSRF) > CAR`.

## Important Dates

- **Due Date for CAR Submission:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure timely closure and reporting of any audit observations to avoid regulatory penalties under the standardized penalty structure.
