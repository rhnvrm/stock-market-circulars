## Summary

Trading members are informed that Sharp India Ltd has changed its name to SMAART TECH SERVICES LIMITED. Consequently, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Sharp India Ltd
- New Company Name: SMAART TECH SERVICES LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 523449
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): SMAART
- New Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus): SMAART
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Administrative update of company identifiers on the exchange trading platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records with the new name, scrip ID, and abbreviated name for future transactions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating trading terminals and internal systems with the new name and scrip identifiers.
