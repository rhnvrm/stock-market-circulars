## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of FX Multitech Limited (Scrip Code: 544941) are being transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective Tuesday, October 13, 2026. This follows the Exchange’s earlier notice dated September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** FX Multitech Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544941
- **Segment Change:** Transferred from Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group).
- **Effective Date:** Tuesday, October 13, 2026.
- **Previous Notice Reference:** Notice No. 20260925-22 dated September 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The shift moves the company's equity shares from the restricted Trade for Trade settlement framework into the standard Rolling segment, enabling normal settlement and trading dynamics under the M Group.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems and records regarding the segment change for FX Multitech Limited ahead of the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This transition provides regular trading and liquidity for investors holding equity shares of FX Multitech Limited, removing trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
