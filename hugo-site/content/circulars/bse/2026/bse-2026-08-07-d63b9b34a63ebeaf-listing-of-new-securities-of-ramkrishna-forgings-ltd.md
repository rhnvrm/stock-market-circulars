## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Ramkrishna Forgings Ltd. resulting from the conversion of warrants issued to promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ramkrishna Forgings Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532527, ISIN: INE399G01023)
- Securities: 3,35,000 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 2098/-
- Purpose: Conversion of warrants issued to promoters on a preferential basis
- Effective Date: Monday, August 10, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 181835018 to 182170017
- Lock-in Details: 3,35,000 shares locked-in up to 29/02/2028
