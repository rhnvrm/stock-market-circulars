## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026. Trading members using third-party platforms, empaneled vendors, or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their applications and exceptional market condition features.

## Key Points

- **Date:** Saturday, October 24, 2026.
- **Segment:** Equity Derivatives.
- **Purpose:** Testing trading applications, risk-reduction mode, trading halt, and other functionalities.
- **No Margin Impact:** Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out, or rights/liabilities.
- **Feedback:** Members are requested to provide feedback by 03:00 PM via email or contact numbers provided.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants are encouraged to actively participate in the mock trading session to ensure system readiness.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** October 24, 2026
- **Feedback Submission Deadline:** October 24, 2026, by 03:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Operational readiness testing with no direct regulatory or live financial impact on market participants.
