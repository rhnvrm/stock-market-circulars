## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability and revision of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities pursuant to the revised framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, transfers to specific trading groups, and movement of securities into or out of the LT-ASM framework.

## Key Points

- 100% margins are applicable effective October 21, 2026, on all open positions as of October 19, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Lower price bands and movement to higher stages of LT-ASM take effect from October 16, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective October 21, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the revised LT-ASM framework, including margin requirements, price band restrictions, and group transfers.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions for the affected securities. Members may direct clarifications to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Bands / Movement / Exits: October 16, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins / Group Transfers: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity in the shortlisted securities may be impacted due to stringent margin requirements (100% margin) and price band restrictions.
