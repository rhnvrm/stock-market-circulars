## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the Draft Offer Document filed by K M C Aluminum Limited for its SME IPO. The document has been made available on the stock exchange websites for public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: K M C Aluminum Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comments Window: 21 days from the date of notice
- Hosting Platform: BSE SME website (https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx)
- Contact Email for Comments: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations for SME IPO draft disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public and stakeholders are invited to review the draft offer document and submit any comments or feedback within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides transparency and stakeholder participation in the SME initial public offering process for K M C Aluminum Limited.
