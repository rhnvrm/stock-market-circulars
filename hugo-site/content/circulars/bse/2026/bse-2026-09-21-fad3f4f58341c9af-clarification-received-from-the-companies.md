## Summary

The BSE has observed significant price and volume movements in certain scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors and trading members have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies. This notice lists the companies that have submitted their replies as well as those whose replies are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- **Replies Received From:** Adani Total Gas Ltd, NRB Bearings Ltd, Omnitech Engineering Ltd, Patels Airtemp India Ltd, Promact Plastics Ltd, Senores Pharmaceuticals Ltd, Sera Investments & Finance India Ltd, and Shining Tools Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited From:** Alacrity Securities Ltd, Annvrridhhi Ventures Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Jhandewalas Foods Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Mopshop Distribution Ltd, and Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd.
- **Purpose:** Ensure market transparency and protect investors by demanding timely disclosures regarding unusual stock movements.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies observing significant price/volume movements must promptly respond to exchange queries and provide requisite clarifications to keep investors informed.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 21 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances information symmetry in the market, enabling investors to make informed decisions regarding scrips exhibiting abnormal price and volume fluctuations.
