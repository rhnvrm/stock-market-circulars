## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 9,57,600 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors portion in the public issue of Injecto Polymers Limited at Rs. 100/- per equity share, as per the announcement dated September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Injecto Polymers Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investors Portion)
- Number of Shares Allocated: 9,57,600 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 100/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: September 10, 2026
