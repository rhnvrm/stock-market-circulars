## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued by 17 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Wednesday, September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers 17 companies issuing further shares under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective date for trading of these securities: September 02, 2026.
- Includes major companies such as HDFC Bank Limited, Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited, Federal Bank Limited, and Lupin Limited.
- Quantities, face values, and distinctive numbers vary by company as detailed in the official exchange filing.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine operational listing notice for capital changes resulting from ESOP/ESOS exercises.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are requested to take note of the listed securities and ensure proper accounting and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 01, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact. Reflects normal equity expansion due to employee stock option exercises across multiple listed entities.
