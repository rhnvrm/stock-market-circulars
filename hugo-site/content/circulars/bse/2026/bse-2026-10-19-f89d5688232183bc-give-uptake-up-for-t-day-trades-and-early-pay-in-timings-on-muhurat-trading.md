## Summary

BSE has issued operational guidelines regarding give-up and take-up procedures for T-day trades and early pay-in timings during the Muhurat Trading session.

## Key Points

- Outlines procedural requirements for give-up/take-up of T-day trades on Muhurat Trading.
- Specifies timelines and early pay-in timings for securities settlement.
- Ensures seamless clearing and settlement operations for members during the special session.

## Regulatory Changes

- Specific operational timeline adjustments for trade allocation, give-up/take-up, and early pay-in on Muhurat Trading day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must complete give-up/take-up activities and adhere to early pay-in timings within the announced operational windows.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 19 October 2026
- Effective during the Muhurat Trading session.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates smooth operational handling and timely settlement of trades executed during the festive trading session without disruptions.
