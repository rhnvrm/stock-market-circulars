## Summary

SEBI has approved the revocation of debarment and restraint in the matter of Akar Laminators Limited and its directors, following the confirmation that all complaints lodged against the entity on SCORES have been successfully disposed of.

## Key Points

- Revocation of debarment/restraint approved by the competent authority.
- All investor complaints on SCORES against the entity have been disposed.
- Affects individuals including Vinodbhai Tarachand Sheth, Hasmukhbhai Tarachand Sheth, and Hitesh Ravindra Shah.

## Regulatory Changes

- Lifting of previous restrictions and debarment imposed via earlier exchange notices (such as Notice No. 20101208-21).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the revocation and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact; operational clearance for the named individuals and entity.
