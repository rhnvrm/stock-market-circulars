## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026. Trading members utilizing third-party empaneled vendor platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs are invited to test their applications and exceptional market conditions such as risk-reduction mode and trading halts.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, October 24, 2026.
- Session Timings: Log-in from 08:15 AM to 09:00 AM; Continuous Trading from 09:00 AM to 02:00 PM.
- No new BOLT TWS Release associated with this session.
- Mock trades attract no margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations and create no rights or liabilities.
- Members are requested to provide feedback on the session by 03:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively in testing their systems and submit feedback via the designated contact numbers or email (bse.tech@bseindia.com / bsehelp@bseindia.com) by 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 22 October 2026
- Mock Trading Date: 24 October 2026
- Feedback Deadline: 24 October 2026 (03:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact; ensures system readiness and connectivity for members trading in the Commodity Derivatives segment without any financial exposure.
