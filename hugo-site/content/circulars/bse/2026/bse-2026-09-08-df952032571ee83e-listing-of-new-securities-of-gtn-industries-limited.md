## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for new equity shares of GTN Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 500170) effective from September 9, 2026, following a preferential allotment.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** GTN Industries Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 500170
- **ISIN:** INE537A01013
- **Security Details:** 24,59,622 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 14/- (Issue Price: Rs. 24/-) to non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- **Effective Date:** September 9, 2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 20058779 to 22518400
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to 31/03/2027 for all 24,59,622 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the listing of new securities and their lock-in status.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 31/07/2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** 09/09/2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** 31/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion via preferential allotment of shares to non-promoters with standard lock-in requirements.
