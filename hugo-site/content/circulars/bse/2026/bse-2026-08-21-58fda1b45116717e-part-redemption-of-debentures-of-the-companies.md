## Summary

Trading members are informed that several companies have fixed record dates for the purpose of payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260821-43
- Segment: Debt
- Department: Listing Operations
- Covers multiple companies undergoing part redemption of debentures and interest payments.
- Trading will resume with reduced face values as per the schedule.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for debenture transactions.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates and Ex-Dates range from August 31, 2026, to September 8, 2026, depending on the specific company/scrip.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures market participants are updated on the altered face value and capital structure of the specified non-convertible debentures.
