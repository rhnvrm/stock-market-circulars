## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 2,82,000 new equity shares of Shivalik Rasayan Ltd. (Scrip Code - 539148) issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Shivalik Rasayan Ltd. (Scrip Code: 539148, ISIN: INE788J01021)
- Security Details: 2,82,000 equity shares of Rs. 5/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 245/- (Issue Price: Rs. 250/- per share) on a preferential basis to non-promoters.
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.
- Distribution Nos.: 15750366 to 16032365
- Date of Allotment: 14/09/2026
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 30/04/2027.
- Effective Date: Trading permitted with effect from Friday, October 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the new securities listed and permitted for trading.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 14, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: October 23, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of the equity capital base of Shivalik Rasayan Ltd. through preferential allotment to non-promoters, increasing paid-up capital subject to lock-in restrictions.
