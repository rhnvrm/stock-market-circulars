## Summary

Stock exchanges have announced a change in the name of Sarda Proteins Ltd to Fresita Proteins Limited. The change in scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be effective from September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Sarda Proteins Ltd
- New Company Name: Fresita Proteins Limited
- Scrip Code: 519242
- New Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System: FRESITA
- New Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: FRESITA
