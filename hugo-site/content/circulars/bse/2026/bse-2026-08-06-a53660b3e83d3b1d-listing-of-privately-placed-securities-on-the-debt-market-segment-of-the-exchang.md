## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis on the BSE Debt Segment effective August 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are traded in dematerialised form under specific ISIN numbers.
- Standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuers include Dolat Algotech Limited, Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, IGH Holdings Private Limited, and Grasim Industries Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with standard dematerialised trading rules for commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- Allotment / Effective Date: August 5 / August 6, 2026
- Redemption Dates: November 2026 to February 2027

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official market admission for the listed commercial papers on the exchange's debt segment.
