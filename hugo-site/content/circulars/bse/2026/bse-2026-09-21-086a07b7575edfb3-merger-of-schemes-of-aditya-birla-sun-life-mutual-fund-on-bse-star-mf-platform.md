## Summary

Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund has announced the merger of two of its schemes into the Aditya Birla Sun Life Corporate Bond Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The merger becomes effective after business hours on September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Transferor Schemes:
  - Aditya Birla Sun Life Nifty SDL Plus PSU Bond Sep 2026 60:40 Index Fund
  - ABSL CRISIL-IBX AAA NBFC-HFC Index Sep 2026 Fund
- Transferee Scheme: Aditya Birla Sun Life Corporate Bond Fund
- Effective Date: After close of business hours on September 30, 2026.
- Exit load waiver provided for investors wishing to redeem or switch without charges between September 01, 2026, and September 30, 2026.
- Systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP) will be automatically migrated to the corresponding plan/option of the transferee scheme.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members and trading/clearing participants are requested to take note of transaction acceptance timelines and inform investors accordingly. Investors wishing to cancel systematic facilities or exit without load must do so within the specified timelines.

## Important Dates

- Exit Load Waiver Period: September 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026
- Demat Transaction Acceptance Deadline: September 25, 2026
- Non-Demat Transaction Acceptance Deadline: September 30, 2026
- Merger Effective Date: September 30, 2026 (after close of business hours)

## Impact Assessment

Existing unitholders of the transferor schemes will have their units and systematic investment plans migrated to the transferee scheme unless they opt for redemption or switching during the exit-load-free window.
