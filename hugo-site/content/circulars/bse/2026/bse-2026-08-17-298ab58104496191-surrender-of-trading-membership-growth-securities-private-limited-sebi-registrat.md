## Summary

Growth Securities Private Limited (SEBI Registration no. INZ000299236) has demerged its stock broking business and depository participant activity into Algoquant Fintech Limited (Member No. 6758). Consequently, the exchange has approved the surrender of its trading membership.

## Key Points

- Growth Securities Private Limited demerged its stock broking and depository participant activities into Algoquant Fintech Limited.
- The exchange approved the surrender of trading membership for Growth Securities Private Limited.
- Trading members are requested to take note of the change.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the surrender and update their records accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 17 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to the administrative update of trading membership records.
