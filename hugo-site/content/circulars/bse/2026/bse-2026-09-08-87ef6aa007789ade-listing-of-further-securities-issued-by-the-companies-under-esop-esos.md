## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by eight companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan / Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOP / ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective Wednesday, September 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** September 09, 2026
- **Total Companies Involved:** 8
- **Companies and Securities Details:**
  1. **Aurum Proptech Limited:** 29,000 shares (ISIN: INE898S01029, Scrip Code: 539289)
  2. **ESAF Small Finance Bank Limited:** 90,418 shares (ISIN: INE818W01011, Scrip Code: 544020)
  3. **HDFC Life Insurance Company Limited:** 1,14,000 shares (ISIN: INE795G01014, Scrip Code: 540777)
  4. **Karnataka Bank Limited:** 40,389 shares (ISIN: INE614B01018, Scrip Code: 532652)
  5. **Quick Heal Technologies Limited:** 9,746 shares (ISIN: INE306L01010, Scrip Code: 539678)
  6. **Sona BLW Precision Forgings Limited:** 2,37,774 shares (ISIN: INE073K01018, Scrip Code: 543300)
  7. **Travel Food Services Limited:** 30,669 shares (ISIN: INE103V01028, Scrip Code: 544443)
  8. **Unimech Aerospace and Manufacturing Limited:** 45,114 shares (ISIN: INE0U3I01011, Scrip Code: 544322)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the listing of further securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 08, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action involving the admission of ESOP shares for trading, having negligible impact on overall market operations.
