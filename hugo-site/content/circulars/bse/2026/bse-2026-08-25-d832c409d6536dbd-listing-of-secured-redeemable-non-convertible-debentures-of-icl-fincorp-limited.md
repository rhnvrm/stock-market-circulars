## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of ICL Fincorp Limited in the "F GROUP" effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities: Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of ICL Fincorp Limited.
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Trading Group: F GROUP.
- Trading Form: Dematerialized form in unit market lot (i.e., One Bond).
- Financial Year Ends: March 31st.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in dematerialized form in unit market lots as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- Effective Trading Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of the listed non-convertible debentures for investors.
