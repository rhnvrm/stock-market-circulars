Shannon Finserve Private Limited (Member No. 6926) has voluntarily ceased its business operations in the Cash and Equity Derivative segments on the BSE exchange.

- Member Code: 6926
- Affected Segments: Cash and Equity Derivative segments
- Effective Date: August 3, 2026

None.

Minimal impact limited to clients and counterparties previously dealing through Shannon Finserve Private Limited in the cash and equity derivative segments.
