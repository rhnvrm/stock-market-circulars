## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 15 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Friday, August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 15
- Effective date for trading: August 21, 2026
- Companies include Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Anupam Rasayan India Limited, Arvind SmartSpaces Limited, AXIS Bank Limited, Bluspring Enterprises Limited, Dabur India Limited, Godrej Properties Limited, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, IndoStar Capital Finance Limited, Paradeep Phosphates Limited, Sasken Technologies Limited, Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited, Viyash Scientific Limited, Walchandnagar Industries Limited, and Wipro Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing of shares pursuant to ESOP/ESOS exercises.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the listing and admission for trading of the aforementioned securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 20, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action with minimal direct market impact, representing minor equity dilution due to employee stock option exercises.
