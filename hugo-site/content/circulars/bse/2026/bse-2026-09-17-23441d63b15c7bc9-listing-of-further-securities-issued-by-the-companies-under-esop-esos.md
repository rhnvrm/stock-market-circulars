## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 12 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS), effective from Friday, September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 12
- Effective date for trading: September 18, 2026
- Included companies: Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Avalon Technologies Limited, Clean Max Enviro Energy Solutions Limited, Delhivery Limited, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited, Laxmi Dental Limited, Mphasis Limited, Nippon Life India Asset Management Limited, Rose Merc. Limited, Tata Communications Limited, Tracxn Technologies Limited, and Varun Beverages Limited.
- Lock-in status: Not applicable (NA) for all listed quantities in this notice.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to take note of the listing of new securities for the mentioned companies.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 17, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital and listed shares for the respective companies through ESOP exercise.
