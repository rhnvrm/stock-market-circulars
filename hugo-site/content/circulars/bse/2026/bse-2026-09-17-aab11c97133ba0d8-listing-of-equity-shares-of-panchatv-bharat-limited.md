## Summary

Effective from Friday, September 18, 2026, the equity shares of Panchatv Bharat Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Panchatv Bharat Limited
- Scrip Code: 544926
- ISIN No.: INE0VXN01011
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- Issue Price: Rs. 140/- per share (including premium of Rs. 130/-)
- Total Securities: 5,851,000 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1000 shares
- Segment: SME / Trade-for-Trade for 10 trading days

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action executed for lock-in period marking in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 16, 2026
- Commencement of Trading: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Panchatv Bharat Limited shares on the BSE SME platform.
