## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing of 13,00,000 new equity shares of Continental Securities Limited (Scrip Code: 538868) issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants, effective from October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Continental Securities Limited
- Scrip Code: 538868
- ISIN: INE183Q01020
- Security Details: 13,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 19/- (Issue price: Rs. 21/- per share) to promoters on a preferential basis upon conversion of warrants.
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Distribution Nos.: 30453001 to 31753000.
- Allotment Dates: 
  - 08-07-2026: 5,00,000 shares
  - 16-07-2026: 4,00,000 shares
  - 21-07-2026: 4,00,000 shares

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: October 16, 2026
- Allotment Dates: July 8, 2026; July 16, 2026; July 21, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: April 30, 2028 (for all 13,00,000 shares)

## Impact Assessment

The listing of preferential equity shares increases the paid-up capital of Continental Securities Limited following the successful conversion of warrants by promoters. Shares are subject to lock-in requirements until April 30, 2028.
