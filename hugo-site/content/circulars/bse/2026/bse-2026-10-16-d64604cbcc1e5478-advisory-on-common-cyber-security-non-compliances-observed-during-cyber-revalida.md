## Summary

SEBI, in coordination with Stock Exchanges, conducted a special purpose cyber revalidation audit to evaluate stock brokers' cyber security and resilience, alongside monitoring via the CERT-In Cyber Swachhta Kendra (CSK) platform. The audit revealed common non-compliances highlighting gaps in implementation and interpretation of applicable requirements.

## Key Points

- Special purpose cyber revalidation audit conducted by SEBI and Stock Exchanges.
- Monitoring undertaken via the CERT-In Cyber Swachhta Kendra (CSK) platform.
- Identifies recurring cyber security non-compliances across trading members' systems.
- Aims to enhance awareness and bridge gaps between regulatory requirements and technical interpretation.

## Regulatory Changes

- Re-emphasizes strict adherence to existing guidelines under the Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF) without introducing new regulatory mandates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must review the observations enclosed in Annexure A of the advisory.
- Establish necessary systems, controls, and procedures to fortify cyber security posture.
- Ensure complete compliance with all applicable SEBI and Exchange circulars related to CSCRF.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 16 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Improves operational preparedness and cyber resilience across trading members by proactively addressing identified audit and monitoring gaps.
