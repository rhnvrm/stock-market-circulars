## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing all Research Analysts about the Gazette Notification (F. No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/323) dated October 08, 2026, issued by SEBI regarding the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reference to SEBI Gazette Notification No. F. No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/323 dated October 08, 2026.
- Concerns the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026.
- Annexure 1 contains the copy of the Gazette Notification regarding the common advertisement code.
- Applicable to all Research Analysts registered with BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amendments under the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026, issued by SEBI on October 08, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research analysts are requested to take note of the SEBI Gazette Notification and adhere to the updated intermediary regulations.
- For clarifications, queries can be sent to Iara.Advertisement@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notification Date: October 08, 2026
- Notice Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational and compliance awareness update for research analysts regarding SEBI intermediary regulations and advertisement codes.
