## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market ETF NFO on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from September 30, 2026, to October 14, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market ETF (Regular and Direct Plans)
- **NFO Period:** September 30, 2026, to October 14, 2026 (extended till midnight for Lumpsum purchases on October 14, 2026)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag shall be reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 22, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted during the NFO period subject to timely receipt of clear funds.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** September 30, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Clear Funds Deadline:** October 22, 2026 by 6:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification providing trading members with clear schedules, payment timelines, and allotment details for participating in the Aditya Birla Sun Life BSE Total Market ETF NFO.
