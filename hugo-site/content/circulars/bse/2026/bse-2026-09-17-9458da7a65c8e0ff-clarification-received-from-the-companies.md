## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movements in several scrips in the recent past and sought clarification from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information. Clarifications received from twelve companies have been attached, while replies from nine other companies are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- **Clarifications Received:** Replies have been received and attached from 12 companies including Anlon Healthcare Ltd, Ashoka Metcast Ltd, Dindigul Farm Product Ltd, Miven Machine Tools Ltd, Nirmitee Robotics India Ltd, Pecos Hotels and Pubs Ltd, Quest Flow Controls Ltd, Sarda Proteins Ltd, Tahmar Enterprises Ltd, Varvee Global Ltd, Skyline Millars Ltd, and York Exports Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited:** Responses are still pending from 9 companies including CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Jhandewalas Foods Ltd, Mahan Industries Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Mopshop Distribution Ltd, Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, and Sera Investments & Finance India Ltd.
- **Purpose:** Aimed at maintaining market transparency and ensuring trading members and investors are informed of relevant developments affecting scrip prices and volumes.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely respond to exchange inquiries regarding unusual market activity to ensure transparent information dissemination to investors.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 17 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to market participants regarding unusual price and volume movements across specified scrips.
