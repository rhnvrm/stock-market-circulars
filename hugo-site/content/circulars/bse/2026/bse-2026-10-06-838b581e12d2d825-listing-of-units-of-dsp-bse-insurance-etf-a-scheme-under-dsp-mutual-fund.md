## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units of DSP BSE Insurance ETF under DSP Mutual Fund, effective Wednesday, October 7, 2026. The open-ended scheme replicates and tracks the BSE Insurance Index.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** DSP BSE Insurance ETF
- **Mutual Fund:** DSP Mutual Fund
- **Investment Manager:** DSP Asset Managers Private Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544970
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** INSURADD
- **ISIN:** INF740KA1ZR8
- **Trading Group:** B (Market Lot: 1 unit)
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00 per unit
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 14.47 per unit
- **Number of Units:** 7,066,999 units
- **Date of Allotment:** September 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine listing approval in accordance with SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Units will be traded exclusively in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines.
- Settlement will be in unit market lot (one unit).

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 30, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** October 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an additional exchange-traded fund option tracking the BSE Insurance Index, enhancing investment opportunities in the insurance sector.
