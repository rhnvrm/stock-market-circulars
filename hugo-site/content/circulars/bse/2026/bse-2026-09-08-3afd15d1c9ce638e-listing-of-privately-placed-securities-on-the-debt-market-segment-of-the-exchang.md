## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities issued by Power Finance Corporation Ltd on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective from September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Power Finance Corporation Ltd
- **Scrip Codes & IDs:** 
  - Scrip Code: 978125, Scrip ID: 761PFCL36 (ISIN: INE134E08OE9), Coupon: 7.61% p.a., Maturity: 08/09/2036, Quantity: 216,850
  - Scrip Code: 978126, Scrip ID: 760PFCL31 (ISIN: INE134E08OF6), Coupon: 7.60% p.a., Maturity: 08/09/2031, Quantity: 197,500
- **Face Value / Issue Price:** Rs. 1,00,000 per security.
- **Credit Rating:** CARE AAA/Stable, CRISIL AAA/STABLE, ICRA AAA/STABLE.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted in dematerialised form under the specified ISIN numbers with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 07/09/2026
- **Listing / Effective Date:** 08/09/2026
- **Redemption Dates:** 08/09/2031 and 08/09/2036

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt listing expanding trading availability for Power Finance Corporation's privately placed instruments on the BSE Debt Segment.
