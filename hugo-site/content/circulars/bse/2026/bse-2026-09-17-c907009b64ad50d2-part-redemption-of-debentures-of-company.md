## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that Earlysalary Services Private Limited has fixed a record date for the payment of interest and part redemption of its Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). Trading in the debentures will take place with a reduced face value starting from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Earlysalary Services Private Limited
- Scrip Code: 976654
- Scrip ID: ESPL-11.20%-05-04-27-PVT
- ISIN: INE01YL07359
- Purpose: Interest Payment and Part Redemption
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 75,000/- per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and depository participants are required to take note of the reduced face value and record/ex-dates for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 20/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 18/09/2026
- Effective Date for Reduced Face Value: As per exchange schedule

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to holders and trading members dealing with the specific NCDs of Earlysalary Services Private Limited, ensuring accurate settlement at the reduced face value.
