## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that four companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) / bonds, with trading continuing at reduced face values.

## Key Points

- **Dishman Carbogen Amcis Limited**: Scrip Code 977467 (ISIN: INE385W07059), Record/Ex-Date: 05/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 75,000.01 per debenture.
- **Annapurna Finance Private Limited**: Scrip Code 976927 (ISIN: INE515Q07640), Record/Ex-Date: 07/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 37,499.95 per debenture.
- **A.K. Capital Finance Limited**: Scrip Code 975607 (ISIN: INE197P07326), Record/Ex-Date: 08/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 33,336 per debenture.
- **Krazybee Services Ltd**: Scrip Code 977909 (ISIN: INE07HK07874), Record/Ex-Date: 08/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 93,333.33 per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational notification for debt corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the record dates, ex-dates, and reduced face values for trading in the respective debentures.

## Important Dates

- **05/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Dishman Carbogen Amcis Limited.
- **07/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Annapurna Finance Private Limited.
- **08/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for A.K. Capital Finance Limited and Krazybee Services Ltd.

## Impact Assessment

Operational updating of trading systems for reduced face values of debentures and processing of part redemption payments.
