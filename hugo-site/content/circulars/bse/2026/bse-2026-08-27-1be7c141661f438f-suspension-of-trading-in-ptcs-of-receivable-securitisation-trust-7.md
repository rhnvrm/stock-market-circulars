## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the suspension of trading in Principal Protected Trust Certificates (PTCs) issued by Receivable Securitisation Trust 7, as the securities are maturing on August 31, 2026. Trading members are advised not to deal in these PTCs effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip codes affected: 976888 (RST-11.10%-31-8-26-PTC, ISIN: INE25OL15014) and 976889 (RST-12.75%-31-8-26-PTC, ISIN: INE25OL15022).
- Maturity date of the PTCs is August 31, 2026.
- Trading suspension takes effect from August 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine operational notice regarding security maturity and trading cessation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are instructed to refrain from dealing in the specified PTCs from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: August 28, 2026
- Maturity Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact limited to the holders and traders of the specified securitisation trust PTCs reaching maturity.
