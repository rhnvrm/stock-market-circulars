## Summary

Trading members of the exchange are informed that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for the renunciation of CENTURY EXTRUSIONS LIMITED are permitted on the exchange effective from September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: CENTURY EXTRUSIONS LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751143
- Scrip ID: CENTEXT-RE
- ISIN No: INE281A20018
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: September 23, 2026 to October 08, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: September 23, 2026 to October 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- REs shall be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis.
- Trading shall be conducted in dematerialized form only.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes/bills must include a prominent disclaimer informing clients that purchase of REs only grants the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce the REs before closure.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs as of the Issue Closing Date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Start Date: September 23, 2026
- RE Trading End Date: October 08, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: September 23, 2026
- Rights Issue Close Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares as of the record date receive REs which can be traded or applied for during the specified windows. Failure to act results in the lapsing of entitlements.
