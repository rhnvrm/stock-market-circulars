## Summary

BSE has notified members that SEBI vide its order no. WTM/KV/CFID/CFID-SEC6/32762/2026-27 dated October 09, 2026, has directed the revocation of all directions issued vide the interim order and confirmed vide order dated May 30, 2025, concerning LS Industries Limited and noticees, with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20250211-1 dated February 11, 2025.
- SEBI order dated October 09, 2026.
- Revocation of all directions issued under interim order and confirmed on May 30, 2025.
- Effective immediately from the date of the order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Withdrawal and revocation of prior regulatory restrictions and interim directions imposed on LS Industries Limited and noticees.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the revocation order and ensure compliance accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 09, 2026
- Order Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulatory relief to LS Industries Limited by lifting prior interim restrictions and directions.
