## Summary

In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260828-40 dated August 28, 2026, the Exchange has notified that the appellant, Avance Ventures Private Limited, has paid the full penalty amount in favor of SEBI as directed by the Hon’ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) vide its order dated September 09, 2026, in the matter of Debock Industries Limited (Appeal No. 289 of 2026).

## Key Points

- Reference made to SAT order dated September 09, 2026, requiring the full penalty deposit to stay the impugned order.
- SEBI confirmed via email dated September 17, 2026, that Avance Ventures Private Limited has paid the penalty amount.
- Trading members are advised to take note and ensure compliance.
