BSE has informed trading members about the allocation of 6,76,800 equity shares to anchor investors under the public issue of Amtech Esters Limited at a price of Rs. 75/- per equity share, effective September 08, 2026.

- Anchor Investors Portion: 6,76,800 equity shares subscribed

- Date of Announcement: September 08, 2026
