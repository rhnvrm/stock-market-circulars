## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of new equity shares issued by L. T. Elevator Limited (Scrip Code - 544518) with effect from Thursday, September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 21,00,078 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 178/- to non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing old equity shares of the company.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 19163056 to 21263133
- **Date of Allotment:** 27/07/2026
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 188/-
- **ISIN:** INE0TJ801010
- **Lock-in Details:** 21,00,078 shares with distinctive numbers from 19163056 to 21263133 are under lock-in up to 10/03/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard compliance regarding the lock-in period and trading permissions as per exchange regulations.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Trading:** 10/09/2026
- **Date of Allotment:** 27/07/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 10/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of L. T. Elevator Limited through preferential allotment of shares to non-promoters.
