## Summary

BSE has approved the transfer of business from Wellworth Share & Stock Broking Ltd. (Member No. 106) to Roha Wellworth Securities Pvt. Ltd. across Cash, Equity Derivative, and Debt Segments. Consequently, the name of the trading member stands changed effective Monday, October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Transferor: Wellworth Share & Stock Broking Ltd. (Member No. 106)
- Transferee / New Name: Roha Wellworth Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 106)
- Segments: Cash, Equity Derivative, and Debt Segments
- Effective Date: October 5, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an administrative approval of transfer of business and membership name change.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records with the new name for Member No. 106.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 1, 2026
- Effective Date: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on general market participants, restricted to updating records for Member No. 106.
