## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of three Treasury Bills (T-Bills) scheduled by the Government of India on August 12, 2026. Trading members can participate through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- **Auction Date:** August 12, 2026.
- **Securities Offered:** 91 Day T-Bill (091T111126), 182 Day T-Bill (182T100227), and 364 Day T-Bill (364T120827).
- **Bidding Platform:** iBBS web-based system (NCB-GSec module).
- **Settlement Date:** August 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; conducted in accordance with RBI press release dated August 07, 2026, and Exchange circular no. 20180423-42.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit bids within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module and ensure pay-in obligations are met with ICCL.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** August 10, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors):** August 11, 2026, till 5:00 pm
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** August 12, 2026, till 8:00 am
- **Auction Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides institutional and retail investors via trading members an organized mechanism to participate in government debt auctions.
