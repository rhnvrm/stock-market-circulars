## Summary

BSE has announced the partial redemption and reduced face value of Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) issued by Magnetron Aug 2025, effective from October 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company / Instrument**: Magnetron Aug 2025 (MA2025-11.50%-28-07-28-PTC)
- **ISINs & Scrip Codes**: 
  - INE2GPJ15012 (Scrip Code: 977078) - Reduced Face Value: Rs. 2414.33 per PTC
  - INE2GPJ15020 (Scrip Code: 977079) - Reduced Face Value: Rs. 5673.23 per PTC
- **Trustee**: Mitcon Credential Trusteeship Services Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

[N/A]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised that the PTCs will be traded with the aforementioned reduced face value.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: September 30, 2026
- **Effective Date**: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading and settlement for the concerned PTCs will reflect the revised face values starting from the effective date.
