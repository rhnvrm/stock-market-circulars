## Summary

BSE issued a notice detailing modifications in the pre-open call auction session for the Equity Derivatives Segment, following SEBI circular dated January 16, 2026. The changes introduce revised session timings, specific order types, order execution priorities, and information dissemination protocols.

## Key Points

- Pre-open session duration is set for 15 minutes from 9:00 am to 9:15 am with structured order entry, matching, and buffer periods.
- Specific restrictions on market order modifications/cancellations during the 9:05 am to 9:10 am window.
- Market orders take priority over limit orders during order matching.
- Enhanced information dissemination regarding indicative equilibrium prices, matchable quantities, and imbalance statistics.

## Regulatory Changes

- Alignment of the existing pre-open session framework with the Closing Auction Session (CAS) framework.
- Prohibition of revealed quantity orders and stop-loss orders in the pre-open session.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their trading systems and algorithms to comply with the revised pre-open session timings and order restriction rules.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and algorithmic traders must adapt to the new order entry restrictions and matching sequence in the derivatives segment pre-open window.
