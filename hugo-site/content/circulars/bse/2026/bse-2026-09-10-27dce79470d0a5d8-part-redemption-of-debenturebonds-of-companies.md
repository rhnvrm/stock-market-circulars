## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that certain companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will be with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Mufin Green Finance Limited (ISIN: INE08KJ07159): Record date 11/09/2026, Reduced face value Rs. 25000/- per debenture.
- Earlysalary Services Private Limited (ISIN: INE01YL07425): Record date 13/09/2026 (Ex-date 11/09/2026), Reduced face value Rs. 66666.67 per debenture.
- A.K. Capital Finance Limited (ISIN: INE197P07458): Record date 14/09/2026 (Ex-date 11/09/2026), Reduced face value Rs. 83333/- per debenture.
- Aadharshila Infratech Private Limited (ISIN: INE0REQ08025): Record date 11/09/2026, Reduced face value Rs. 99500/- per debenture.
- Greaves Finance Limited (ISIN: INE1QWF07071): Record date 13/09/2026 (Ex-date 11/09/2026), Reduced face value Rs. 87500/- per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for proper settlement of trades in the respective NCDs.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10 Sep 2026
- Record Dates: 11/09/2026, 13/09/2026, and 14/09/2026
- Ex-Dates: 11/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding these debentures will receive part redemption payments and subsequent trading will occur on the reduced face value basis.
