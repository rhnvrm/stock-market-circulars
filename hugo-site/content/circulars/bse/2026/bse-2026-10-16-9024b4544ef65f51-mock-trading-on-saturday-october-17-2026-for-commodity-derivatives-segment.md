## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 17, 2026. Trading members using third-party trading platforms of empaneled vendors or in-house developed systems through ETI APIs can test their respective trading applications and exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 17, 2026, for the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Log-in timing: 09:15 AM to 10:00 AM.
- Continuous Trading timing: 10:00 AM to 01:00 PM.
- No new BOLT TWS Release is associated with this session.
- Trades resulting from mock trading do not attract any margin obligations or pay-in and pay-out obligations.
- Members are requested to provide feedback on the mock trading session by 02:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are encouraged to actively participate in the mock trading session for system testing and familiarization.
- Members must submit feedback on the session to BSE Tech Support or Help Desk by 02:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Mock Trading Date: October 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational readiness and system verification for trading members with zero financial, margin, or settlement impact.
