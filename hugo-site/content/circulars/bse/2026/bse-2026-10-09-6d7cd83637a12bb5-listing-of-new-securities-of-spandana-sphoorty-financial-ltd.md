## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Spandana Sphoorty Financial Ltd (Scrip Code: 542759) effective from October 12, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Spandana Sphoorty Financial Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 542759
- **ISIN:** INE572J01011
- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Security Details:** 1,67,60,459 Equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Rs. 10/- each issued on rights basis.
- **Date of Allotment:** 29/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action notice regarding the listing of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing and permission to trade the newly converted fully paid-up equity shares, which rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational listing of converted shares with pari-passu status; no direct regulatory or market-wide impact.
