## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued notice number 20260812-43 dated August 12, 2026, regarding revised daily price bands for 11 equity scrips. The revised price limits will become effective from the start of trading on Thursday, August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** Revisions go into effect on August 13, 2026.
- **Scope:** 11 specific equity scrips listed on the BSE are affected.
- **Price Limit Ranges:** Revisions place securities into 2%, 5%, 10%, or 20% limit bands.
- **Issuing Authority:** BSE Surveillance Department (DOSS).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Systems Configuration:** Trading members and brokerage firms must update their risk management systems (RMS) and front-end trading terminals with the new price limits prior to the trading session on August 13, 2026.
- **Order Review:** Active or pending orders for the affected securities should be verified to ensure compliance with the newly established limits.
- **Support Channel:** Trading members can send clarification requests to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Trading Volume & Liquidity:** Securities moved to narrower price bands (such as 2% for Dhanlaxmi Cotex, Reliable Ventures, and Vipul) may experience restricted trading ranges and higher probability of hitting circuit limits, resulting in a temporary reduction in intraday liquidity. Conversely, securities with wider bands like Xtranet Technologies (20%) will benefit from greater pricing flexibility.
- **Risk Management:** Broker compliance officers must monitor these securities for any potential margin calls or trade rejections stemming from order executions beyond the revised daily boundaries.
