## Summary

BSE issued a notice on October 19, 2026, seeking clarification from Jana Small Finance Bank Ltd and Federal Bank Ltd regarding various news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels (specifically citing etnownews.com). The respective replies received from the companies have been attached by the exchange for investor information.

## Key Points

- Exchange sought clarification from Jana Small Finance Bank Ltd (Scrip Code: 544118) and Federal Bank Ltd (Scrip Code: 500469) to verify news reports.
- Clarifications aim to ensure market transparency and verify accuracy of reports circulating in media/websites.
- Replies from both companies are attached with the BSE notice.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard surveillance and disclosure enforcement action under exchange norms.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to timely clarify and verify market rumors or news articles when sought by the stock exchange to prevent misinformation.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct operational or financial impact on the companies, serving primarily as a compliance disclosure exercise to address media reports.
