## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Grand Foundry Limited has changed its name to Tikona Communication Limited. Correspondingly, the Scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated to TIKONA effective October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Grand Foundry Limited
- New Company Name: Tikona Communication Limited
- Scrip Code: 513343
- New Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: TIKONA
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of exchange database, trading systems (BOLT Plus), and security identifiers to reflect the new company name.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their internal systems, client portfolios, and records with the new name and Scrip ID.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 9, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Purely operational change affecting display names and trading identifiers; no impact on existing shareholdings, ISIN, or financial contracts.
