## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the draft offer document for Indexel Engineering Limited's SME IPO on its website, seeking public comments for at least 21 days as per ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Indexel Engineering Limited
- Filing Date: July 30, 2026
- Notice Date: July 31, 2026
- Public comment period: 21 days from the date of the notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations regarding public comments on draft offer documents.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public can submit comments on the draft offer document within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: July 30, 2026
- Notice Date: July 31, 2026
- Comment Deadline: Within 21 days from July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites public participation and scrutiny for the upcoming SME IPO offering.
