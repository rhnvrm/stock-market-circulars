## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, review, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective dates for various surveillance actions range from August 31, 2026, to September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. September 02, 2026, on all open positions as on September 01, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- List of securities moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework, along with lower price bands, effective from August 31, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. September 02, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective from August 31, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions by listed companies and is not an adverse action against the entity.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and implementation of surveillance measures under the LT-ASM framework, including margin requirements, price band revisions, and group transfers.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and adjust trading strategies according to the revised price bands and group migrations for shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- August 31, 2026: Lower price bands and movement to higher stages or out of LT-ASM framework take effect.
- September 01, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- September 02, 2026: 100% margins become applicable; transfer to T / XT / MT / TS Groups takes effect.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in affected securities will experience increased margin blockages and potential liquidity constraints due to surveillance measures, price band restrictions, and group transfers.
