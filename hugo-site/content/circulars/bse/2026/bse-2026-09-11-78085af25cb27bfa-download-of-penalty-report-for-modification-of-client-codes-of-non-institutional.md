## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the penalty report for client code modifications of non-institutional trades executed during the month of August 2026. The report has been made available in the respective trading members' Equity Transaction Folder in the Extranet.

## Key Points

- Covers client code modifications for non-institutional trades during August 2026.
- Report file-name: PM0826.Clg no.
- Download location: Equity Transaction Folder in Extranet.
- Based on Exchange Notice Nos. 20110729-24 (dated July 29, 2011) and 20110826-4 (dated August 26, 2011).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a recurring monthly operational report download pursuant to existing guidelines on client code modifications.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to download and save the penalty report files from the Extranet for their future reference and reconciliation.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 11 Sep 2026
- Period of Report: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low impact. Routine compliance check for trading members to verify any penalties associated with non-institutional client code modifications.
