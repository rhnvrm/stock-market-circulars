## Summary

Pursuant to Notice No. 20261022-22 issued by BSE Index Services Pvt. Ltd., BSE has announced changes to several indices on account of the Fast-Tracked IPO review. These changes will become effective at the open of trading on Friday, October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- National Stock Exchange of India Ltd. (Ticker: 544937) is added to multiple BSE indices including BSE 1000, BSE 500, BSE 200, BSE 100 Largecap, BSE Total Market, BSE Sensex Next 50, BSE 500 Equal Weight, and BSE 200 Equal Weight.
- Several companies are dropped from specific indices to accommodate the additions.
- Effective date for all index modifications is October 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic reconstitution and review of BSE indices based on Fast-Tracked IPO guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, index funds, and algorithmic trading systems must update their index tracking portfolios and benchmark components in accordance with the revised index constituents prior to the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 30, 2026 (at market open)

## Impact Assessment

- Moderate impact on passive funds and ETFs tracking the affected BSE indices (such as BSE 100, BSE 500, BSE 200, and Sensex Next 50) requiring rebalancing trades.
