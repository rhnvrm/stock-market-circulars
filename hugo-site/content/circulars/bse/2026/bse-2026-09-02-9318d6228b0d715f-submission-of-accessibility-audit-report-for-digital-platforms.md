## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding all Trading Members to submit the Accessibility Audit Report for their digital platforms and the remediation of findings, in accordance with SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131 and previous Exchange notices concerning the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

## Key Points

- Mandatory submission required from all Trading Members.
- Covers accessibility audit reports for digital platforms and remediation of findings.
- Online module has been made available on the BEFS Portal for submissions.
- Deadline for submission is October 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of compliance mandates under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 for regulated entities' digital platforms as per SEBI and Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must submit the "Accessibility Audit Report for the digital platforms and Remediation of findings from the audit" via the BEFS portal.
- Path: BEFS --> Rights of Persons with Disabilities → Final Audit Report for Digital Platform.
- Follow the guidelines/procedure provided in Annexure A of the notice.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 2, 2026
- **Submission Deadline:** October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Regulated entities and Trading Members must ensure their digital platforms comply with accessibility standards and timely submit the audit reports to avoid regulatory non-compliance penalties.
