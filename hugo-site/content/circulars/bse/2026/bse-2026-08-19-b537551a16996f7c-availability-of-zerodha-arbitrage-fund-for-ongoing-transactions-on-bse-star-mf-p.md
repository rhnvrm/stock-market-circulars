## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of Zerodha Arbitrage Fund for ongoing transactions, including subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ZERODHA ARBITRAGE FUND - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH
- ISIN: INF0R8F01232
- Facilities Available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants must ensure transactions are processed according to the specified ISIN and scheme details on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 19, 2026
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to conduct ongoing transactions and systematic investment plans for the Zerodha Arbitrage Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
