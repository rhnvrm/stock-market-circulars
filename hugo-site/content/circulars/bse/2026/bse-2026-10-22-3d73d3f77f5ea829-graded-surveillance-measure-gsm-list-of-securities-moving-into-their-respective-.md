## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective October 23, 2026. Trading members are required to take adequate precautions as these securities will attract strict surveillance actions.

## Key Points

- Securities will move to higher GSM stages starting October 23, 2026.
- Stage I incurs a 100% margin rate and a price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II entails trade-for-trade settlement with a 5% price band and a 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value.
- Stage III and IV involve trade-for-trade settlement, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week), and 100% ASD.
- No netting off is allowed as settlements are conducted on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework provisions as updated by the exchange, subjecting listed securities to enhanced surveillance and margin requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, ensure compliance with applicable margin and deposit requirements, and note that settlements are on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading frequency for securities moving into Stages II, III, and IV will be restricted, with increased capital requirements (ASD and 100% margins) for buyers and trading limited to once a week for higher stages.
