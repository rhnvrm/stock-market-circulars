## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) issued a notice regarding clarifications sought from listed companies concerning news articles appearing in various media platforms to ensure accuracy and transparency for investors. Replies received from the respective companies are attached to the notice.

## Key Points

- The Exchange sought clarification from companies to verify news articles in newspapers, websites, and television channels.
- Companies involved: Yatharth Hospital & Trauma Care Services Ltd (Scrip Code: 543950) and Happiest Minds Technologies Ltd (Scrip Code: 543227).
- Clarification replies received from the companies are made available through the Exchange attachments.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to clarify media reports and news articles when requested by the Exchange to prevent market rumors and misinformation.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct impact; ensures transparency and verifies media reports concerning the specified companies.
