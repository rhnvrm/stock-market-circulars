## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the fixation of Record Dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) by several companies. Trading in these debentures will take place with reduced face values effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260909-7, dated September 9, 2026.
- Segment: Debt.
- Companies involved include Western Transmission (Gujarat) Limited, Alipurduar Transmission Limited, Dvara Kshetriya Gramin Financial Services Private Limited, Lodha Developers Limited, Lakadia Banaskantha Transco Limited, WRSS XXI (A) Transco Limited, Adani Transmission Step-Two Limited, and Kharghar Vikhroli Transmission Limited.
- Trading in the debentures will continue with reduced face values starting from the respective record and ex-dates (September 11 and 12, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and record dates for the respective debentures to ensure accurate settlement and trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / Ex-Date: September 11, 2026, and September 12, 2026, depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors holding the affected NCDs need to account for the reduced face value and upcoming part redemption payouts in their valuations and settlement calculations.
