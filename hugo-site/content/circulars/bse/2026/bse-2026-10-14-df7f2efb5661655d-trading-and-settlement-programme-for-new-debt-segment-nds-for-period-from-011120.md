## Summary

BSE has released the trading and settlement schedule for the New Debt Segment (NDS) for the period from November 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026, detailing specific timelines for trade entry, confirmation, pay-in/pay-out, and auction schedules.

## Key Points

- Covers 19 settlement cycles for NDS during November 2026 (Settlement Nos. NDS-144/26-27 through NDS-162/26-27).
- Specifies matching dates for NDS Trading, entry/confirmation, pay-in/pay-out, and auction schedules.
- Pay-in is scheduled at 11:00 a.m. with member instructions required by 10:50 a.m.
- Auction pay-in is scheduled at 9:30 a.m. with member instructions required by 9:20 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine periodic settlement calendar notice issued by the Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must adhere strictly to the stipulated timing for submitting pay-in and auction instructions to Depositories and banks (latest by 10:50 a.m. for regular pay-in and 9:20 a.m. for auction pay-in).

## Important Dates

- Schedule Period: 01.11.2026 to 30.11.2026
- First Settlement Date: 02-11-2026 (Settlement No. NDS-144/26-27)
- Final Settlement Date in period: 30-11-2026 (Settlement No. NDS-162/26-27, with final auction pay-out on 02-12-2026)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and operational. Ensures smooth debt market clearing and settlement operations for market participants trading on the NDS segment during November 2026.
