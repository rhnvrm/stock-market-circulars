## Summary

BSE StAR MF has announced that transactions from Non-Resident Indian (NRI) investors residing in the United States of America (USA) and Canada will now be accepted under the schemes of Quant Mutual Fund and Quant Specialized Investment Fund (SIF) on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Applicable to NRI investors residing in USA and Canada investing in Quant Mutual Fund and Quant Specialized Investment Fund (SIF).
- Members must obtain prescribed declaration forms (MF_DECLARATION.pdf for Mutual Fund and QSIF_DECLARATION.pdf for SIF).
- Original declaration forms along with a separate sheet of transaction numbers must be submitted to KFin Technologies Limited in Hyderabad.
- Envelopes must be labeled: "Declaration Form for the USA and CANADA Investors – Quant Mutual Fund".
- Scanned copies of signed declaration forms must be emailed to help.investor@quant.in and quantqueries@kfintech.com on the same day of the transaction.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; operational enablement of US and Canadian NRI client transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform for Quant Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure prescribed declaration forms are duly executed by the investor(s), original forms and transaction details are submitted to the RTA (KFin Technologies Limited), and scanned copies are emailed to the designated email addresses on the transaction day.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 13 October 2026 (Immediate effect)

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity and compliance pathways for members and RTA to onboard and process transactions for NRI clients residing in the USA and Canada for Quant Mutual Fund schemes.
