## Summary

Hilton Metal Forging Limited has announced an extension of the closing date for its ongoing rights issue of 1,67,70,000 equity shares. The closing date has been extended from March 13, 2026, to April 02, 2026, while the opening date remains March 06, 2026. Trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) remains suspended as per the original schedule.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Hilton Metal Forging Limited
- **Rights Issue Size:** 1,67,70,000 Equity Shares
- **Opening Date:** March 06, 2026 (Unchanged)
- **Revised Closing Date:** April 02, 2026 (Extended from March 13, 2026)
- **Rights Entitlements (REs):** Trading in REs remains suspended with no extension provided for trading period.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; intimation made pursuant to SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders may take note of the extended timeline to apply for the rights issue up to April 02, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** March 06, 2026
- **Revised Issue Closing Date:** April 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides extended participation window for existing eligible shareholders to subscribe to the rights issue, while ensuring no changes to the trading period of Rights Entitlements.
