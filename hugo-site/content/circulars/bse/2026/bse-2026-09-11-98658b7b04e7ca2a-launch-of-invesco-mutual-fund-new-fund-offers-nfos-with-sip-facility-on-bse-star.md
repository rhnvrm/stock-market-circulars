## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about the launch of New Fund Offers (NFOs) from INVESCO Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from September 15, 2026, to September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available: INVESCO INDIA NIFTY INDIA DEFENCE INDEX FUND and INVESCO INDIA NIFTY CHEMICAL INDEX FUND.
- Subscription period: September 15, 2026, to September 29, 2026.
- SIP facility is available for both NFO schemes.
- Tentative allotment date is October 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without funds initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 06, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (September 29, 2026).

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: September 15, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: September 29, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform users and investors with access to new thematic index funds from INVESCO Mutual Fund with systematic investment options.
