## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) effective from September 01, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and margin requirements.

## Key Points

- Securities identified in the attached Annexure will move to higher GSM stages.
- Effective date of implementation: September 01, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, ranging from 100% margin rates and lower price bands to trade-for-trade settlement and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- No netting off allowed for trades subject to trade-to-trade settlement.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance framework provisions as outlined in previous BSE notices (dating from February 2017 to November 2023) under the GSM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Settlement will be executed on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off permitted.
- Buyers must deposit applicable Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) and margin rates as specified per stage.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 31, 2026
- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements, trade-for-trade restrictions, and liquidity constraints for securities moving into higher GSM stages, impacting trading participants and investors holding the affected stocks.
