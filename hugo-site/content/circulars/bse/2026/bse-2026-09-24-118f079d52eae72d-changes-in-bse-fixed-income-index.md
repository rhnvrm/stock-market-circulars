## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced constituent changes for the BSE 8-13 Year G-Sec Index and the BSE India 5 Year Sovereign Bond Index, effective September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **BSE 8-13 Year G-Sec Index:** Addition of 7.18% GS 2037 (ISIN: IN0020230077) and deletion of 7.54% GS 2036 (ISIN: IN0020220029).
- **BSE India 5 Year Sovereign Bond Index:** Addition of 6.53% GS 2031 (ISIN: IN0020260074) and deletion of 6.36% GS 2031 (ISIN: IN0020250141).
- Other indices such as the BSE 4–8 year G-Sec Index and BSE India 10 Year Sovereign Bond Index remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a periodic index rebalancing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for market participants, except for index trackers and benchmark funds adjusting their portfolios accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 24, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low impact on general trading; relevant primarily for passive funds, index funds, and portfolios tracking BSE fixed income indices.
