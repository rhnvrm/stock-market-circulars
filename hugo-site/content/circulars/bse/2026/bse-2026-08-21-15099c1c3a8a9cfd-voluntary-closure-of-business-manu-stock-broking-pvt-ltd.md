## Summary

Manu Stock Broking Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 3294) has voluntarily decided to cease its business operations in the currency derivative segment on the BSE, effective from Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: Manu Stock Broking Pvt. Ltd.
- Member Number: 3294
- Segment Affected: Currency Derivative Segment
- Effective Date: August 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members seeking clarifications can contact membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Cessation: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to the closure of business operations of the specific trading member in the currency derivative segment.
