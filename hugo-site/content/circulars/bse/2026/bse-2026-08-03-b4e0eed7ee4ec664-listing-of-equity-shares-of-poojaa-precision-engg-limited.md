## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Poojaa Precision Engg. Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange effective from Tuesday, August 4, 2026, under the 'MT' Group of Securities in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: POOJAA PRECISION ENGG. LIMITED
- Scrip ID: PPEL
- Scrip Code: 544844
- ISIN No.: INE288301026
- Market Lot: 400 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 301/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 291/-)
- Date of Allotment: July 31, 2026
- Total Securities: 19,94,4960 Equity Shares

## Regulatory Changes

- Scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines.
- Lock-in period marked in depository records as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: August 4, 2026
- Date of Allotment: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading of Poojaa Precision Engg. Limited shares on the BSE SME platform.
