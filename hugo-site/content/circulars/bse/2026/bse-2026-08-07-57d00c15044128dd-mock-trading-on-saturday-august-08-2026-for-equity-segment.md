## Summary

The Exchange has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity segment on August 08, 2026. Trading members using third-party trading platforms or in-house developed systems through ETI APIs can test their applications under exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, August 08, 2026, for the Equity segment.
- Members can test risk-reduction modes, trading halts, and other exceptional market conditions.
- No new BOLT TWS Release.
- Feedback requested by 2:00 PM on the day of the mock session.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate and provide feedback on the mock trading session via the provided contact numbers or email IDs by 2:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 08, 2026
- Log-in Time: 9:15 AM - 9:45 AM
- Continuous Trading T+1: 10:15 AM - 12:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

No direct financial impact; operational readiness exercise for trading members.
