## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the movement of specified securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. These changes take effect from August 27, 2026, and attract various trading restrictions and surveillance actions.

## Key Points

- Securities are moved to higher GSM stages (Stages I, II, III, and IV) based on the surveillance framework.
- Applicable measures include 100% margin rates, price bands of 5% or lower, trade-for-trade settlement with no netting off, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Trading frequency for higher stages (III and IV) is restricted to once a week (every Monday/first trading day of the week).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance actions under the GSM framework originally published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent updates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities since settlement is strictly on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Buyers are required to deposit applicable Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) as mandated by the respective GSM stage.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 26 August 2026
- Effective Date: 27 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities are expected to be significantly restricted due to trade-for-trade settlement, mandatory ASD requirements, and reduced trading frequency for higher stages.
