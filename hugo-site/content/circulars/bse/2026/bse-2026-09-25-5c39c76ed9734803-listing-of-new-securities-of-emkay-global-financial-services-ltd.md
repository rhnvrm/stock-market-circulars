## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 50,000 new equity shares of Emkay Global Financial Services Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532737) effective from September 28, 2026. These shares were issued to promoters on a preferential basis upon conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company: Emkay Global Financial Services Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532737, ISIN: INE296H01011).
- Securities: 50,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 229.50/- (Issue Price: Rs. 239.50/-).
- Allotment Date: July 13, 2026.
- Distribution Numbers: 27330132 to 27380131.
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Lock-in Details: 50,000 shares locked-in up to March 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action notice regarding the listing of securities issued via preferential warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants take note of the listing and trading permissions of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: July 13, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 28, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: March 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact, representing a routine preferential allotment and warrant conversion for promoters of Emkay Global Financial Services Ltd.
