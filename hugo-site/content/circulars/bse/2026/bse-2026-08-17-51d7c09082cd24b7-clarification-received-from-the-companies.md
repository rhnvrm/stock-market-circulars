## Summary

BSE has issued a surveillance notice detailing scrips that have experienced significant price and volume movements in the recent past. The exchange sought clarifications from these companies to ensure investors have access to relevant information. Replies from several entities have been received and attached, while responses from several others remain awaited.

- **Clarifications Received From:**
  1. Enbee Trade & Finance Ltd (Scrip Code: 512441)
  2. Medico Remedies Ltd (Scrip Code: 540937)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine surveillance disclosure notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely respond to exchange inquiries regarding significant market movements to maintain market integrity and transparency.
