## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the Draft Offer Document filed by Robokidz Eduventures Limited for its SME IPO. The document is hosted on the stock exchange website to invite public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Robokidz Eduventures Limited
- Filing Date: July 24, 2026
- Compliance: Disclosures specified under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public can submit their comments on the draft offer document via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days from the notice date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 27, 2026
- Submission Deadline: Within 21 days from July 27, 2026
