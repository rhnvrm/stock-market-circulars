## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Dollex Agrotech Limited effective from September 11, 2026, under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Dollex Agrotech Limited
- Scrip Code: 544908
- ISIN: INE0JHH01011
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: DOLLEX
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Market Lot: 1 (One) Share
- Total Issued Shares: 39,948,800 Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Lock-in period marked in depository records for demat shares and non-transferability enfaced on physical share certificates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with demat trading mandates and unit market lot requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 10, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Dollex Agrotech Limited equity shares on the BSE exchange platform in the 'B' group segment.
