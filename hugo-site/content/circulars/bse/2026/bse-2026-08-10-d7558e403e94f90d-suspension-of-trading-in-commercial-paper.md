## Summary

Trading members are informed that several companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of commercial papers. Members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects commercial papers of multiple companies including ECL Finance Limited, Time Technoplast Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, and others.
- Record dates and ex-dates range from August 11, 2026, to August 27, 2026.
- Trading members are instructed not to execute trades in these specific commercial papers as of the ex-date.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no transactions occur in the specified commercial papers of the listed entities on or after their respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates: August 11, 2026, to August 27, 2026
- Ex-Dates: August 11, 2026, to August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly settlement and redemption processes for commercial papers by halting trading prior to record dates.
