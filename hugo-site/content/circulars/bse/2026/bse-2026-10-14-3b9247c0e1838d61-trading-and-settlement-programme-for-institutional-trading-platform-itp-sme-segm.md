## Summary

BSE has announced the trading and settlement programme for the Institutional Trading Platform (ITP) under the SME segment for the period from November 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026. The schedule outlines settlement numbers, depository settlement numbers, ITP trading dates, and corresponding pay-in/pay-out dates.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement periods from ITP-147/26-27 to ITP-166/26-27.
- Details specific timings for pay-in and pay-out of funds and securities, including special timings for specific settlement dates (e.g., November 9, 2026).
- Members must submit pay-in instructions to Depositories/banks latest by 09:50 a.m. for standard cycles.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine operational schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Members participating in the SME segment Institutional Trading Platform must adhere to the specified trading dates, pay-in/pay-out timelines, and depository instruction deadlines.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Period:** 01.11.2026 to 30.11.2026
- **First Trading Date:** 02-11-2026 (Settlement No: ITP-147/26-27)
- **Last Trading Date:** 30-11-2026 (Settlement No: ITP-166/26-27)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth operational planning and settlement execution for trading members and institutional investors operating on the SME segment ITP platform.
