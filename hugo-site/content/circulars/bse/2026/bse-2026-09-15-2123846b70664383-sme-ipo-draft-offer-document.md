## Summary

Maharaja Polyfab Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with BSE for its proposed SME IPO on September 12, 2026. The document is available for public review and comments for a period of at least 21 days in compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Maharaja Polyfab Limited
- Filing Date: September 12, 2026
- Notice Date: September 15, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO
- Public Comment Window: 21 days from the date of the notice

## Regulatory Changes

None. The filing complies with existing Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments on the draft offer document can be submitted via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days from the date of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Draft Offer Document Filing Date: September 12, 2026
- Notice Date: September 15, 2026
- Comment Deadline: Within 21 days of the notice date

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and the public an opportunity to review and submit comments on the proposed SME IPO of Maharaja Polyfab Limited.
