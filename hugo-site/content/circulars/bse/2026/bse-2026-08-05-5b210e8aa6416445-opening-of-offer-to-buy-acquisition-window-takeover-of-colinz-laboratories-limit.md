## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the offer to buy (acquisition window) for an open offer to acquire up to 6,54,966 fully paid-up equity shares of Colinz Laboratories Limited, representing 26% of the voting share capital, at a price of Rs. 54/- per share.

## Key Points

- Acquirers: Annjana Dugar, Likhitta Dugar, and Antariksh Dugar, together with PAC Padam Dugar.
- Target Company: Colinz Laboratories Limited.
- Offer Price: Rs. 54/- per equity share payable in cash.
- Offer Size: Up to 6,54,966 equity shares (26% of Voting Share Capital).
- Purpose: Substantial acquisition of shares and voting rights accompanied with change in control pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 and relevant SEBI/Exchange guidelines for acquisition through stock exchange mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must follow the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through the stock exchange pursuant to tender offers under takeovers, buybacks, and delisting.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 05, 2026
- Open Offer Period: Friday, August 07, 2026 to Thursday, August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides public shareholders of Colinz Laboratories Limited an opportunity to tender their shares through the acquisition window on the stock exchange.
