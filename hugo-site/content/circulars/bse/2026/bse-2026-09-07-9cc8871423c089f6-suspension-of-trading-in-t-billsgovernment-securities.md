## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in three T-bills/Government Securities due to their upcoming maturity. Trading members are advised not to deal in these securities with effect from September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading applies to three specific T-bills maturing on September 10, 2026.
- Affected Scrip Codes / ISINs:
  1. Scrip Code: 805191 | 182TB10926 | ISIN: IN002025Y495
  2. Scrip Code: 805117 | 364TB10926 | ISIN: IN002025Z245
  3. Scrip Code: 805226 | 91TB10926 | ISIN: IN002026X107
- Effective date of trading restriction: September 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational notice regarding security maturity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that they do not deal in the specified T-bills/Government Securities effective from September 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: September 08, 2026
- Redemption/Maturity Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and localized impact, affecting only participants holding positions in these specific maturing T-bills.
