## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants that the HDFC NIFTY METAL ETF FOF of HDFC Mutual Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and SIP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective August 12, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20260718-2 dated July 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** HDFC NIFTY METAL ETF FOF
- **Fund House:** HDFC Mutual Fund
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform
- **Effective Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Eligible Schemes & ISINs:**
  - HDFC NIFTY METAL ETF FOF - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF179KC1KR8)
  - HDFC NIFTY METAL ETF FOF - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF179KC1KS6)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can facilitate transactions in the specified mutual fund schemes for investors through the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Effective Date / Reopening Date:** August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members continued access to transactions, redemptions, and systematic investment plans (SIP) for the HDFC Nifty Metal ETF Fund of Funds.
