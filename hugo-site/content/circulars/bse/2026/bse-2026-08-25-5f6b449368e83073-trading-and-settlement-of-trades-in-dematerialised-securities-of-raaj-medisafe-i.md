## Summary

Raaj Medisafe India Limited (scrip code: 524502) has signed an agreement with both depositories. Consequently, trading and settlement modalities for the company's scrip are revised effective August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: Wednesday, August 26, 2026 (Sett. No. DR-702/2026-2027).
- Scrip Group changed from P to X.
- Market Lot revised from 100 to 1 equity share.
- Trades will be in the Normal Rolling Segment and settled in compulsory demat form.
- Netting is allowed, and shortages will be auctioned.
- Existing ISIN: INE548H01015.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of the scrip to the Normal Rolling Segment with compulsory dematerialised settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised market lot, group change, and settlement modalities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Notice Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced liquidity and retail accessibility due to the reduction of market lot size to 1 equity share and trading in compulsory demat form.
