## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities of IL&FS Financial Services Limited due to their upcoming maturity.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 955027
- ISIN: INE121H08099
- Particulars: ILFSL-8.69%-29-09-26-PVT
- Maturity Date: September 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine debt maturity and trading suspension procedure.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debt securities of the company with effect from September 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Suspension Date: September 28, 2026
- Maturity Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must refrain from trading in these specific debt securities to ensure compliance with exchange operations.
