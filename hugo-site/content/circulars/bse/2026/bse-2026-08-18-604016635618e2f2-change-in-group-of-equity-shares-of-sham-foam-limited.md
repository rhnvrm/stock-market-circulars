## Summary

Trading in the equity shares of Sham Foam Limited (Scrip Code: 544869) is being transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), in continuation of the Exchange's earlier notice dated August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sham Foam Limited
- Scrip Code: 544869
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The trading segment for the equity shares of Sham Foam Limited has been modified from Trade for Trade to the Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment change and ensure compliance with the trading norms applicable to the Rolling segment starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 18, 2026
- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for the equity shares of Sham Foam Limited, improving liquidity and trading convenience for market participants.
