## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by various entities on the debt market segment, effective October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities admitted to trading include commercial papers from Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited, Dolat Algotech Limited, BOBCARD Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, and Navi Finserv Limited.
- Trading takes place in dematerialised form under designated ISIN numbers.
- Standard denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to dematerialised form requirements, standard denominations, and designated ISIN numbers.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 13, 2026
- Listing/Admission Date: October 14, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Ranging from December 30, 2026 to April 13, 2027.

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and secondary market trading access for the newly listed privately placed commercial papers.
