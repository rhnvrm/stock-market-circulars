## Summary

Bank of India Mutual Fund is launching the Bank of India Value Fund New Fund Offer (NFO) along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from August 28, 2026, to September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Bank of India Value Fund (Regular and Direct plans available in Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- NFO Window: August 28, 2026, to September 11, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 21, 2026.
- Lumpsum purchases remain extended till midnight on September 11, 2026, while SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by 3:00 PM on the closure date.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 18, 2026.
- Switch-out funds must be received prior to the allotment date to be considered valid.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: August 28, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: September 11, 2026
- Clear Funds Cut-off Date: September 18, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates investors and mutual fund distributors in subscribing to the Bank of India Value Fund NFO securely through the BSE StAR MF infrastructure.
