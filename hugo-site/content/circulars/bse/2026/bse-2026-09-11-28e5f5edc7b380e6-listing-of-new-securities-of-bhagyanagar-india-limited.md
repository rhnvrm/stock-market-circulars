## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Bhagyanagar India Limited (Scrip Code - 512296) effective from Tuesday, September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Bhagyanagar India Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 512296
- **ISIN:** INE458B01036
- **Number of Shares:** 15,01,434 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 348/- per share (including premium of Rs. 346/-)
- **Allottees:** Non-Promoters on a preferential basis
- **Effective Date:** September 15, 2026
- **Distribution Nos.:** 31995001 to 33496434
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to March 15, 2027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares. Lock-in requirements apply as specified.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 13, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** September 15, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** March 15, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of equity capital base due to preferential allotment of shares to non-promoter investors.
