## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the upcoming bonus issue of equity shares by Aastha Spintex Limited. The company has fixed September 28, 2026, as the record date for the 1:1 bonus issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: AASTHA SPINTEX LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544808
- Ratio: 1 (One) Bonus Equity Share of Rs. 10/- each for every 1 (One) existing Equity Share of Rs. 10/- each held.
- Record Date: 28/09/2026
- Ex-Bonus Basis From: 28/09/2026
- Total Equity Shares to be allotted: 44,142,190 shares as of September 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the ex-bonus date and record date for settlement and trading purposes in the scrip.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 28, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders will receive one additional equity share for every share held, leading to an expansion of the company's equity base to over 4.41 crore shares.
