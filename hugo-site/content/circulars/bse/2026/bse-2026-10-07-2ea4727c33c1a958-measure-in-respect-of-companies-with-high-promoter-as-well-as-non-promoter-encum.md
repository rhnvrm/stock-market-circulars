## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding measures for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011, detailing securities entering and exiting the framework along with applicable margin requirements.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Securities listed in Annexure II are eligible to move out of the framework.
- Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities currently under the framework.
- Measures apply in conjunction with other prevailing exchange measures and are subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of minimum 75% margin requirements on securities satisfying high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance criteria under SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of securities entering and exiting the framework and ensure requisite 75% margins are maintained on applicable open and new positions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 7, 2026
- Effective date for removal from framework (Annexure II): October 8, 2026
- Open positions snapshot date for margin application: October 9, 2026
- Effective date for minimum 75% margin (Annexure I) on open and new positions: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements (75%) on specified securities will impact liquidity and capital allocation for traders holding open positions in affected stocks starting October 12, 2026.
