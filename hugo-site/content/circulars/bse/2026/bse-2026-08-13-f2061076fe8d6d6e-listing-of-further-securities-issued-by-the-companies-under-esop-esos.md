## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued by 14 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the exchange with effect from Friday, August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 14
- Effective date for trading: August 14, 2026
- Securities category: Equity
- Issuing companies include Aether Industries Limited, AU Small Finance Bank Limited, AXIS Bank Limited, CarTrade Tech Limited, CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited, Equitas Small Finance Bank Limited, Eris Lifesciences Limited, Fedbank Financial Services Limited, Gland Pharma Limited, ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited, Prime Fresh Limited, Sai Life Sciences Limited, Sterling And Wilson Renewable Energy Limited, and Wonderla Holidays Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing action.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the listing and admission of new securities for trading.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard expansion of paid-up equity capital resulting from the exercise of employee stock options across the listed entities.
