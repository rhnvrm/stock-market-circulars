## Summary

HDFC Mutual Fund is offering the HDFC FTSE INDIA EQUITY ETF NFO on the BSE StAR MF Platform from September 23, 2026, to October 07, 2026. The tentative allotment date is October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Names: HDFC FTSE INDIA EQUITY ETF and HDFC FTSE INDIA EQUITY ETF - DIRECT PLAN.
- NFO Period: September 23, 2026, to October 07, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 12, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 12, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments accepted subject to receipt of clear funds by the deadline.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 23, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 07, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 12, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: 6:00 PM on October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading participants and investors an opportunity to invest in the new HDFC FTSE INDIA EQUITY ETF NFO via the BSE StAR MF platform with standard operational guidelines.
