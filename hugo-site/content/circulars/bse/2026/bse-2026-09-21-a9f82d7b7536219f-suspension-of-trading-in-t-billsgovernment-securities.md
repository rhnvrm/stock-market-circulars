## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specified T-bills and Government Securities due to their maturity on the redemption date.

## Key Points

- Trading members are informed that specific T-bills/Government Securities are maturing on the redemption date.
- Affected ISINs include IN002025Z260 (364TB24926) and IN002026X123 (91TB24926).
- Trading members are advised not to deal in these securities with effect from September 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified T-bills and Government Securities from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and routine impact limited to the expiration and delisting of specific maturing sovereign debt instruments from active trading segments.
