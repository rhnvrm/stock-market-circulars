## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by eight companies under ESOP/ESOS have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective Wednesday, July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 8 companies have issued further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective date for trading: July 29, 2026.
- Companies include Allied Digital Services Limited, Bandhan Bank Limited, Lodha Developers Limited, Onesource Specialty Pharma Limited, Steel Strips & Wheels Limited, STL Networks Limited, The Phoenix Mills Limited, and Veranda Learning Solutions Limited.
- Lock-in status for all listed tranches is marked as 'NA'.

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants are to take note of the new security listings.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 28 Jul 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** 29 Jul 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital increase resulting from employee stock option exercises with minimal overall equity dilution.
