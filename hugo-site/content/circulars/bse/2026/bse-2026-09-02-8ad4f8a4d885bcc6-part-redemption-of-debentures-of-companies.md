## Summary

Trading members are informed that the specified companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the respective record dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260902-54
- Department: Listing Operations
- Covers part redemption and interest payment for NCDs of Grihum Housing Finance Limited, Vivriti Capital Limited, and Sammaan Capital Limited.
- Trading to proceed with reduced face values from the specified record/ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and record dates for accurate settlement and trading in the debentures of the listed companies.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / Ex-Date for Grihum Housing Finance & Vivriti Capital: 04/09/2026
- Record Date / Ex-Date for Sammaan Capital: 11/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the specified NCDs will experience part redemption of principal along with interest, and subsequent trading will reflect the reduced face value per debenture.
