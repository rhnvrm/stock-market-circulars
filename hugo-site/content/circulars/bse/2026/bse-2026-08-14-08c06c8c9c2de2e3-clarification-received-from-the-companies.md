## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in certain scrips in the recent past. The Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarifications have been received from:
  1. Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 539117)
  2. Manoj Jewellers Ltd (Scrip Code: 544400)
- Replies are awaited from 13 other listed entities, including Apt Packaging Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, and PVP Ventures Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine surveillance communication.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies flagged for significant price/volume movements are required to submit clarifications to the Exchange in a timely manner.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 14 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures market transparency by informing investors about pending or received disclosures regarding unusual stock price or volume movements.
