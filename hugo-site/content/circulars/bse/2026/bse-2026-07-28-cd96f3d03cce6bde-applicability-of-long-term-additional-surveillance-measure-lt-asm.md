## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) on certain securities pursuant to the revised LT-ASM framework. Measures include 100% margins on open and new positions, movement of securities to higher stages or specific groups (T/XT/MT/TS), and revisions to price bands effective late July 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins become applicable effective July 31, 2026, on all open positions as on July 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities are moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework along with lower price band applicability effective July 29, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective July 31, 2026.
- Review and movement of securities are based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and market surveillance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins and transfer of securities to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS) under the LT-ASM framework.
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure adequate 100% margin maintenance for open and new positions in applicable securities as per Annexure I.
- Members and listed entities should take note of surveillance staging and communication channels.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Higher Stages / Price Bands / Movement Out: July 29, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements and surveillance grouping may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
