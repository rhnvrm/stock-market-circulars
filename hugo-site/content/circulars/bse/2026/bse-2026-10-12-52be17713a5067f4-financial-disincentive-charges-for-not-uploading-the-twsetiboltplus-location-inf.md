## Summary

BSE has announced financial disincentive charges for trading members who failed to upload or incorrectly registered TWS/ETI/BOLTPLUS terminal location information for the month of September 2026. Location codes attracting financial disincentives will be charged at ₹1,000 per instance.

## Key Points

- Financial disincentive file (LCS0926.xxxx) for September 2026 is available in today’s Equity Transaction Folder on the Extranet.
- Charges of ₹1,000 per instance apply to orders placed from unregistered or incorrectly registered terminal locations.
- Charges will be debited to the General Charges Account.
- Waiver or review requests can be submitted with necessary documentary evidence to Waiver-Review@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of terminal location reporting compliance as per Exchange Notice No. 20260417-45 and related prior notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure strict compliance and upload accurate TWS/ETI/BOLTPLUS terminal location details before punching orders from those locations.
- Waiver/review applications must be submitted per Exchange Notice No. 20251008-67 with the designated subject line.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 12 October 2026
- Period Covered: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is limited to trading members with unregistered or incorrectly registered terminal locations, resulting in standard financial disincentive debits.
