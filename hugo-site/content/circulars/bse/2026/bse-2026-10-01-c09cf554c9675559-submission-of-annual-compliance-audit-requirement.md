## Summary

BSE has issued a reminder regarding the submission of the Annual Compliance Audit Report (ACR) for Investment Advisers (IAs) for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. The ACR module is accessible on the BSE portal via filings by IAs/RAs or through auditors, with a final submission deadline of October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- ACR module is available on the BSE portal for both direct filing by IA/RA and filing through auditors.
- Submissions made via email will not be considered valid.
- Annual certificate confirming compliance with client-level segregation requirements from a qualified ICAI/ICSI/ICMAI member or auditor must be included in the audit.
- Adverse findings, if any, must be submitted along with action-taken reports approved by the IA or management.

## Regulatory Changes

Reiterates existing regulatory compliance mandates for Investment Advisers regarding annual audits and reporting timelines to IAASB.

## Compliance Requirements

- Complete annual compliance audit for FY ended March 31, 2026.
- Submit the compliance audit report and any adverse findings (with action taken reports) to IAASB within one month of the audit report date, and not later than October 31, 2026.
- Include annual certificate confirming client-level segregation compliance.

## Important Dates

- **October 31, 2026**: Absolute deadline for submitting the Annual Compliance Audit Report and adverse findings to IAASB.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory oversight and compliance among Investment Advisers regarding client segregation and operational standards, requiring timely audit submissions via the BSE portal.
