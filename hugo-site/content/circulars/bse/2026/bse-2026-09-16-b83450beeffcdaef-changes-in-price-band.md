## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members about the revision of price bands for 25 scrips. These changes come into effect from September 17, 2026, as part of the exchange's surveillance and risk management measures.

## Key Points

- Total of 25 scrips affected by price band revisions.
- Revised price bands range from 2%, 5%, 10%, to 20%.
- Effective date for the new price bands is September 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands of specific equities have been adjusted from their existing levels to curb excessive market volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that their trading terminals and risk management systems incorporate the updated price bands for the listed scrips prior to market open on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 16, 2026
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Traders and investors holding or trading in the affected scrips must note the new permissible daily price movement limits to manage their orders and risk exposures effectively.
