## Summary

Listing and trading permission for 6,00,000 new equity shares of Fruition Venture Limited (Scrip Code - 538568) issued on a preferential basis upon conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Fruition Venture Limited
- Scrip Code: 538568
- ISIN: INE836C01015
- Number of Shares: 6,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 10/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 20/-)
- Date of Allotment: 25/06/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: August 19, 2026
- Lock-in Details: 2,50,000 shares locked up to 31/03/2028; 3,50,000 shares locked up to 31/03/2027.

None.

Trading members must take note of the new securities and lock-in periods.

- Date of Allotment: 25/06/2026
- Trading Commencement Date: 19/08/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: 31/03/2027 and 31/03/2028

Standard capital expansion via warrant conversion with standard lock-in stipulations.
