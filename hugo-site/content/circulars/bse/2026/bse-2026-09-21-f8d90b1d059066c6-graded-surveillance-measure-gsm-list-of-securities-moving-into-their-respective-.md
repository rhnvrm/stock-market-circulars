## Summary

 BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from September 22, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and take adequate precautions.

## Key Points

- Effective date for movement into higher GSM stages: September 22, 2026.
- Applies to securities listed in the Annexure attached to the notice.
- Settlement for the affected securities shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Surveillance actions vary across stages I to IV, including 100% margin rates, price bands of 5% or lower, Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD), and restrictions on trading frequency.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures as per the provisions of the GSM Framework published on February 23, 2017.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower and ASD of 50% of trade value by buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and ASD of 100% of trade value by buyers (with no upward movement in Stage IV).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the specified securities.
- Ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement and prohibition of netting off.
- Deposit applicable Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) and maintain required margin rates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 21 Sep 2026
- Effective Date of GSM Movement: 22 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened surveillance and trade-to-trade settlement will significantly reduce liquidity and speculative trading in the affected securities.
- Increased capital requirements for buyers due to 100% margins and ASD deposits.
