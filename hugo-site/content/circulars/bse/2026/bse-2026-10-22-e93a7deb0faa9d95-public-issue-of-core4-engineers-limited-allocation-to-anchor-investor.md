## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Core4 Engineers Limited, 15,44,400 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs 93/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Core4 Engineers Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investor portion)
- Number of Shares Allocated: 15,44,400 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs 93/- per equity share
- Date of Announcement: October 22, 2026

None.
