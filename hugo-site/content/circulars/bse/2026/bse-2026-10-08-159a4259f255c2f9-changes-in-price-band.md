## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced revisions to the price bands of 11 scrips under the Equity segment, effective from October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total scrips affected: 11
- Effective date: October 09, 2026
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance)
- Revised price bands range from 2% to 20% across different scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands for specific scrips have been modified from their existing levels as part of ongoing market surveillance and risk management measures.

### Scrip Details:
- Capital Trust Ltd (511505): Revised to 2%
- Gold Coin Health Foods Ltd (538542): Revised to 5%
- Growington Ventures India Ltd (539222): Revised to 10%
- Jattashankar Industries Ltd (514318): Revised to 5%
- Pajson Agro India Ltd (544657): Revised to 10%
- Shentracon Chemicals Ltd (530757): Revised to 2%
- Sonaselection India Ltd (544938): Revised to 20%
- Steel Strips Infrastructures Ltd (513173): Revised to 5%
- Tuni Textile Mills Ltd (751144): Revised to 5%
- Vama Wovenfab Ltd (544931): Revised to 10%
- Veljan Denison Ltd (505232): Revised to 2%

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while executing trades in the affected scrips. Clarifications can be sought via email at bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members dealing in the affected scrips must adjust their trading strategies and order placements in accordance with the newly established price limits to prevent order rejections or unintended execution limits.
