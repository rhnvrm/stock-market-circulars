## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice detailing the schedule of representatives from various Registrar and Transfer Agents (RTAs) visiting the Department of Investor Services (DIS) during August 2026.

## Key Points

- RTAs will visit the DIS at BSE Ltd., 1st Floor, Cama Building, Dalal Street, Mumbai – 400001.
- Schedule covers multiple RTAs including KFin Technologies Ltd., Bigshare Services Pvt. Ltd., Purva Sharegistry (India) Pvt. Ltd., and others.
- Investors and trading members with complaints against client companies of these RTAs can approach their representatives during the specified timings.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Investors or members having grievances against client companies of the listed RTAs can meet representatives in person at the specified venue and schedule.

## Important Dates

- Visit dates span from August 3, 2026, to August 7, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates direct resolution of investor grievances related to specific client companies of the participating RTAs.
