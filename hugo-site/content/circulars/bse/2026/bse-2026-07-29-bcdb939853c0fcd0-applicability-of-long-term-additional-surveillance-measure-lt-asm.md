## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework for various securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies. Effective from July 30, 2026, and August 03, 2026, several securities will experience surveillance measures, including 100% margins, price band restrictions, or transfers to specific trading groups.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable effective August 03, 2026, on all open positions as on July 31, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Movement of securities to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework and application of lower price bands effective July 30, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue under the LT-ASM framework but will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective August 03, 2026.
- Provisions for securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework, restoring their original price bands subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions, and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of the LT-ASM framework as per previous notices.
- Imposition of strict margin requirements (100%) and price band restrictions on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure adequate margin compliance (100%) for open and new positions on securities listed under Annexure I (Part A) starting August 03, 2026.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Higher Stages / Movement Out / Price Band: July 30, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins / Group Transfers: August 03, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date for Margins: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity and liquidity in the affected securities may be restricted due to the imposition of 100% margins, lower price bands, and transfer to trade-for-trade groups.
