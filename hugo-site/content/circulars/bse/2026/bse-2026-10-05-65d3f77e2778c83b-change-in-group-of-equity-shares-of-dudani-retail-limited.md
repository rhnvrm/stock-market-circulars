## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of trading in equity shares of Dudani Retail Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective from Monday, October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Dudani Retail Limited
- Scrip Code: 544961
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Shifting of equity shares from Trade for Trade to Rolling segment enables normal rolling settlement for transactions in the company's shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to take note of the change in segment and group effective from October 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors can trade in the company's shares under the rolling segment, improving liquidity and normal settlement cycles.
