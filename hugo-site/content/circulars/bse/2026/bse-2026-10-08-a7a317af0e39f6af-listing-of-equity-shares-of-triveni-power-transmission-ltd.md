## Summary

Effective from Monday, October 12, 2026, the equity shares of Triveni Power Transmission Ltd. shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE in the T Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circulars.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Triveni Power Transmission Limited (TPTL)
- **Scrip Code:** 544824
- **ISIN No.:** INE2WKE01011
- **Scrip ID on BOLT:** TRIVENIPT
- **Face Value & Paid-up Value:** Rs. 2/- each fully paid
- **Total Securities:** 10,47,54,338 Equity Shares allotted pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement.
- **Group / Segment:** T Group (Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days).
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** The security will be part of SPOS for IPO and other category of scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission to dealings on the Exchange pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement approved by NCLT.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the trade-for-trade segment restriction for the first 10 trading days and adhere to Special Pre-open Session guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 08, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** October 12, 2026
- **Appointed Date:** April 01, 2026
- **Scheme Effective Date:** May 19, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of equity shares of Triveni Power Transmission Ltd. on the BSE platform following its demerger and restructuring from Triveni Engineering & Industries Limited.
