## Summary

BSE has announced the release of a new version of the BOLT Pro Trading Workstation (TWS), version 13.60, which will be compulsory across Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments effective October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Release & Availability:** Available for download from October 9, 2026 (7:00 PM onwards).
- **Compulsory Upgrade:** Mandatory version release for all users using Leased Line Connections across Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments.
- **Mock Trading:** Scheduled for Saturday, October 10, 2026.
- **Live Date:** Effective from Monday, October 12, 2026.
- **Technical Enhancements:** Handling of changes related to Trading System API enhancements in Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory software upgrade of BOLT Pro TWS to version 13.60 for trading members.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must download and upgrade their trading workstations to version 13.60 prior to the live date.
- Members should participate in the mock trading session on October 10, 2026, to verify functionality.

## Important Dates

- **Download Start Date:** October 09, 2026
- **Mock Trading Session:** October 10, 2026
- **Live Effective Date:** October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures compliance with updated exchange trading system APIs and prevents trading interruptions across equity, derivatives, and EGR segments.
