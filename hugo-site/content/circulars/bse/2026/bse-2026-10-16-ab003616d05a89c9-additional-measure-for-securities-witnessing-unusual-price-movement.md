## Summary

BSE has carried out a review for the identification of securities placed under the 'Introduction of Additional Measure for securities witnessing unusual price movement' framework, pursuant to exchange circular no. 20251107-56 dated November 7, 2025.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion are listed in Annexure I.
- Securities moving out of the framework are detailed in Annexure II.
- A consolidated list of all securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- The changes become effective from October 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Periodic review and application of additional surveillance measures for securities experiencing unusual price movements.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must take note of the inclusion and exclusion of securities under the framework and ensure compliance with respective trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 19, 2026
- Notice Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading norms and margins for specific securities identified under the unusual price movement surveillance framework.
