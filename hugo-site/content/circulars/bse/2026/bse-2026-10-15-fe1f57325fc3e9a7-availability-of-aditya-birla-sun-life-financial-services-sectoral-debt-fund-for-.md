## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that the Aditya Birla Sun Life Financial Services Sectoral Debt Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes available include Regular and Direct Plans across IDCW Payout, Growth, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Asset Category: Debt (ISINs range from INF209KC1811, INF209KC1803, INF209KC1829, INF209KC1787, INF209KC1779 to INF209KC1795).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors may facilitate subscriptions, redemptions, and SIP transactions for clients using the specified ISINs through the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 15 October 2026
- Effective Date: 16 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational readiness for ongoing transactions in the newly available debt fund schemes for market participants and investors.
