## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the Monarch Overnight Fund for ongoing transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform, following Notice No. 20260902-45 dated September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Monarch Overnight Fund reopens for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions.
- Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) facilities are included.
- Effective date of availability is September 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the scheme on the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to ongoing transactions and systematic facilities in the Monarch Overnight Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
