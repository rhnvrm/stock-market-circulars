## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has released the trading and settlement schedule for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment for the period spanning from September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026. The notice provides settlement numbers, trading dates, pay-in and pay-out dates, as well as auction settlement details for market participants.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement periods from DR-111/2026-2027 to DR-132/2026-2027.
- Details specific submission dates for trading, depository settlements, pay-in/pay-out dates, and corresponding auction schedules.
- Members are required to submit pay-in instructions to Depositories/banks latest by 03:00 p.m. on pay-in dates.
- Fund and security pay-outs are executed by 04:00 p.m.
- Auction pay-in is scheduled for 9:00 a.m., with member instructions required by 8:50 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must adhere to the stipulated timings for pay-in and pay-out instructions regarding funds and securities for the EGR segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Period Start: September 1, 2026
- Effective Period End: September 30, 2026
- First Settlement (DR-111): Trading on September 1, 2026; Pay-in/Pay-out on September 2, 2026
- Final September Settlement (DR-132): Trading on September 30, 2026; Pay-in/Pay-out on October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing, settlement, and operational predictability for participants trading in Electronic Gold Receipts on the BSE platform during September 2026.
