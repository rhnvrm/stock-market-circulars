## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin and be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective October 27, 2026. Specific provisions apply for securities moving to lower stages, remaining in the framework, or exiting it.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 27, 2026 on all open positions as on October 26, 2026 and new positions created from October 27, 2026.
- Affected securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 27, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 26, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework (Annexure II) have their price bands reinstated.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely due to market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework, including 100% margin requirements and shifting to Trade-for-Trade settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements on open positions and new positions, and account for the Trade-for-Trade segment restrictions for listed securities in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage II shifting / price band / moving out / lower stage: October 26, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade shift: October 27, 2026 (referencing open positions as on October 26, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions significantly impact liquidity and trading operations for the specified securities.
