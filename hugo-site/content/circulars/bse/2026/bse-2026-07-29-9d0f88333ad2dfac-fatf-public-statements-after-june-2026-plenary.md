## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Financial Action Task Force (FATF) Public Statements released after the June 2026 Plenary concerning high-risk jurisdictions subject to a call for action and jurisdictions under increased monitoring. All exchange members are required to submit their compliance declarations through the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS).

## Key Points

- References FATF Public Statements on jurisdictions under increased monitoring and jurisdictions subject to a call for action from the June 2026 Plenary.
- Members must update actions taken via the BEFS portal under `FATF Declaration >> Declaration Form`.
- Submissions must be furnished on the organization's letterhead and signed physically or digitally by the Authorized Signatory/Compliance Officer.
- Non-submission or delayed submission will attract disciplinary action as per Exchange Notice No. 20260417-45.

## Regulatory Changes

Mandatory reporting and declaration of actions taken regarding AML/CFT deficiencies in high-risk or monitored jurisdictions as identified by FATF.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance and submit the FATF declaration in electronic form through BEFS.
- Submissions must be uploaded on the organization's letterhead and duly signed by the Authorized Signatory or Compliance Officer.

## Important Dates

- **Submission Start Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Submission Deadline:** August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

All trading and clearing members must complete the mandatory filing within the stipulated timeline through BEFS to avoid regulatory penalties and disciplinary proceedings.
