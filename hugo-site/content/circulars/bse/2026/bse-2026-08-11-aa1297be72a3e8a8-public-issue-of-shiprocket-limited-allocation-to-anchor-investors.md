## Summary

Bse has informed trading members that 7,49,91,568 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the Public Issue of Shiprocket Limited at Rs. 97/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shiprocket Limited
- Anchor Investors Portion Subscription: 7,49,91,568 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 97/- per equity share
- Date of Announcement: August 11, 2026

None
