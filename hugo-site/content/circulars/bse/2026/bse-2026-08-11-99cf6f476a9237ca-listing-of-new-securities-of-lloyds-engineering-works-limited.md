## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade in new securities of Lloyds Engineering Works Limited (Scrip Code: 539992) effective from August 12, 2026. A total of 1,47,14,074 equity shares of Re. 1/- each were issued as fully paid up pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares issued on a rights basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lloyds Engineering Works Limited
- Scrip Code: 539992
- ISIN: INE093R01011
- Effective Date: August 12, 2026
- Securities: 1,47,14,074 equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up (Re. 1/- each) issued on a rights basis.
- Distinctive Numbers: 91,44,423 (from 1456289760 to 1465434182) and 55,69,651 (from 1465434183 to 1471003833).
- Ranking: These shares are pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may trade in the newly listed securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing update for existing shareholders and market participants.
