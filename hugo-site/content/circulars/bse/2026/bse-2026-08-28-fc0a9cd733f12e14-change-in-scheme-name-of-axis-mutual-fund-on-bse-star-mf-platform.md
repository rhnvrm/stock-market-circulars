## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants and members about a change in the scheme name for Axis Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective immediately.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260828-35, dated August 28, 2026.
- Category: Trading / Segment: Mutual Fund.
- Scheme Name Change:
  - Existing Name: AXIS ELSS TAX SAVER FUND
  - Revised Name: AXIS ELSS- TAX SAVER FUND

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are advised to take note of the revised scheme name while placing orders or conducting transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date: With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating records and order-matching nomenclature for the specified Axis Mutual Fund scheme on the platform.
