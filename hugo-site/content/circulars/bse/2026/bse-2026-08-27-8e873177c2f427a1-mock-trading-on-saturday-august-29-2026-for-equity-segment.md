## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity segment on Saturday, August 29, 2026. The session is designed to test modifications in the Pre-open Call Auction session and ensure readiness for live trading starting Monday, August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mock trading split into two sessions to test specific functionalities.
- Session 1 focuses on testing changes related to modifications in the Pre-open Call Auction Session.
- Session 2 tests trading functionalities without the modifications to facilitate live trading from August 31, 2026.
- Includes distinct timelines for block deals, pre-open, continuous trading (T+1 and T+0), special pre-open for IPOs, and closing auction sessions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Testing of recent modifications in the Pre-open Call Auction Session as outlined in circular no. 20260803-35.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to participate actively in the mock trading sessions using their trading terminals to verify connectivity and order matching functionality.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 27, 2026
- Mock Trading Date: August 29, 2026
- Live Implementation Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Zero direct impact on live market positions or financials; purely an operational and technical testing exercise for trading members.
