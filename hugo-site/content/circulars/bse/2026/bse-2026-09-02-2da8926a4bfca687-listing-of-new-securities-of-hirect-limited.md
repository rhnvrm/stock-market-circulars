## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of Hirect Limited (Scrip Code - 504036) effective from September 3, 2026, following a preferential issue of equity shares to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Hirect Limited
- Scrip Code: 504036
- Security Details: 10,86,366 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 918.50/- to non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- Issue Price: Rs. 920.50/-
- Date of Allotment: 21/07/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: September 3, 2026
- ISIN: INE835D01023
- Distribution Nos.: 34372240 to 35458605
- Lock-in Details: 10,86,366 shares under lock-in up to 14/03/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and distribution details as specified.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 21/07/2026
- Listing/Trading Commencement Date: 03/09/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 14/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity capital base of the company through preferential allotment.
