## Summary

Effective from Friday, September 4, 2026, the equity shares of ESDS Software Solution Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE under the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: ESDS Software Solution Limited
- Scrip Code: 544898
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: ESDS
- ISIN: INE0DRI01029
- Issue Price: Rs. 429/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 1/- and premium of Rs. 428/-)
- Total Securities Listed: 117,210,969 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 2, 2026
- Market Lot: 1 share

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on September 4, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 2, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing enables public trading of ESDS Software Solution Limited shares on the BSE exchange platform.
