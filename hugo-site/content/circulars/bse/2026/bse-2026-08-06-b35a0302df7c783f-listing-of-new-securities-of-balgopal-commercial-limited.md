## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Balgopal Commercial Limited (Scrip Code - 539834) with effect from August 7, 2026. The issuance consists of 23,38,000 equity shares issued to promoters on a preferential basis following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Balgopal Commercial Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 539834
- **ISIN:** INE119R01014
- **Security Details:** 23,38,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 50/- (Issue Price: Rs. 60/-) on a preferential basis to promoters.
- **Effective Date of Trading:** August 7, 2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 20910001 to 23248000
- **Lock-in Details:** 23,38,000 shares locked-in up to 31/03/2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the new securities added to trading and their respective lock-in periods.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 06/06/2026
- **Notice Date:** 06/08/2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** 07/08/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 31/03/2028

Expands the paid-up equity capital base of Balgopal Commercial Limited through preferential warrant conversion with standard lock-in stipulations.
