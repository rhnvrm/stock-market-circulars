## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of 10,41,77,040 equity shares of NHC Foods Ltd (Scrip Code: 517554) with effect from Friday, August 07, 2026. These shares were issued at par pursuant to the conversion of Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** NHC Foods Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 517554
- **ISIN Number:** INE141C01036
- **Security Details:** 10,41,77,040 Equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at par pursuant to conversion of FCCBs.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Date of Allotment:** 24/06/2026
- **Effective Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 657750001 to 761927040

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the newly listed equity shares available for trading starting from August 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** June 24, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate listing update reflecting an increase in paid-up equity capital due to FCCB conversion, with minimal direct market impact.
