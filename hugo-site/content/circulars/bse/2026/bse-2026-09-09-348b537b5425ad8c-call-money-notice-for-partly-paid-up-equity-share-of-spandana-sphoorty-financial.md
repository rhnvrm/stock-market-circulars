## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the activation of the Call Money Module on the IBBS platform for the first and final call on partly paid-up equity shares of Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited
- Security Symbol: SPANDANACM
- Type of Call: First and Final Call
- Call Money Amount: Rs. 115 per share
- Issue Opening Date: September 09, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: September 23, 2026
- Bidding Period: 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
- Number of Partly Paid-up Equity Shares: 1,73,34,362
- Registrar to the Issue: KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Operates under existing SEBI circular guidelines dated December 8, 2020.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members, Self-Certified Syndicate Banks (SCSBs), and intermediaries must submit details of call money notices collected through the IBBS platform during the specified bidding period.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: 09 Sep 2026
- Issue Closing Date: 23 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts shareholders holding partly paid-up shares of Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited who are required to pay the first and final call money of Rs. 115 per share within the stipulated timeline.
