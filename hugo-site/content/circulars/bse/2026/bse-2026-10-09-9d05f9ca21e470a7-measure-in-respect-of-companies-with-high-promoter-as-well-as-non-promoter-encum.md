## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Specific securities listed in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin, while securities in Annexure II are moving out of the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Margin is applicable w.e.f. October 14, 2026, on all open positions as on October 13, 2026, and new positions created from October 14, 2026.
- Securities in Annexure II are eligible to move out from the framework effective October 12, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter margin requirements (minimum 75%) for securities identified with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure the application of the minimum 75% margin on open and new positions for securities listed under Annexure I starting October 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- October 12, 2026: Securities in Annexure II move out of the framework.
- October 13, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to the new margin.
- October 14, 2026: Effective date for minimum 75% margin applicability on open and new positions.

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities with high encumbrance levels face increased margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and position sizing in affected scrips.
