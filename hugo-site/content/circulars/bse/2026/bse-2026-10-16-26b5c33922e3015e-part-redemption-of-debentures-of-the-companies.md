## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that specific companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the respective record and ex-dates in October 2026.

## Key Points

- **Adani Airport Holdings Limited**: Scrip Code 976065 (ISIN: INE0GCN07047), Record/Ex-Date: 26/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 51,709/- per debenture.
- **Belstar Microfinance Limited**: Scrip Code 977020 (ISIN: INE443L07190), Record/Ex-Date: 28/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 37,500/- per debenture.
- **Keertana Finserv Limited**: Scrip Code 977029 (ISIN: INE0NES07253), Record/Ex-Date: 28/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 66,666.67 per debenture.
- **IKF Finance Limited**: Scrip Code 977108 (ISIN: INE859C07246), Record/Ex-Date: 28/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 50,000/- per debenture.
- **Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited**: Scrip Code 977865 (ISIN: INE08XP07514), Record/Ex-Date: 28/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 86,365/- per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action processing for debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and ensure orders are placed accordingly with effect from the respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **26/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Adani Airport Holdings Limited.
- **28/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Belstar Microfinance Limited, Keertana Finserv Limited, IKF Finance Limited, and Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected NCDs will proceed with updated reduced face values. Investors holding these debentures will receive part redemption payouts as per the scheduled terms.
