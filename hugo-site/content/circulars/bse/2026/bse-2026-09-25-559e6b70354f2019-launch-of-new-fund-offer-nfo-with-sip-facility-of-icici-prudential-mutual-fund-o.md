## Summary

ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund is launching the ICICI Prudential Contra Fund NFO along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from September 28, 2026, to October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme NFO Period: September 28, 2026, to October 12, 2026.
- Available plans include Regular and Direct growth, IDCW reinvestment, and IDCW payout options.
- Tentative allotment date is October 15, 2026.
- NFO extended till midnight on October 12, 2026, exclusively for lumpsum purchases.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on October 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 14, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: September 28, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: October 12, 2026
- SIP/XSIP Authentication Deadline: October 12, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Clear Funds Deadline for BSE Clearing Limited: October 14, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates smooth subscription and allotment processes for trading members and investors participating in the ICICI Prudential Contra Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
