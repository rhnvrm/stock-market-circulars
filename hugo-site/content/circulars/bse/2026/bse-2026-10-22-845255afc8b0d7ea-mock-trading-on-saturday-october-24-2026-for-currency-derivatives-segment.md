## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026. Trading members using third-party trading platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their applications and functionalities under simulated market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 24, 2026.
- Covers Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives segments.
- Session Timings: Log-in from 08:15 AM to 09:00 AM; Continuous Trading from 09:00 AM to 02:00 PM.
- No new BOLT TWS Release.
- Mock trades do not attract margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or create rights and liabilities.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational testing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively and submit their feedback by 03:00 PM on the day of the mock session via provided contact details or email.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 24, 2026
- Feedback Deadline: October 24, 2026 (03:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Low impact. Enables members to verify system readiness and API connectivity without any financial risk.
