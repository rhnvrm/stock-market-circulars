## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities issued by Manba Finance Limited on the debt market segment of the Exchange effective September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Manba Finance Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 978202
- **Scrip ID:** 1065MFL28A
- **ISIN Number:** INE939X07275
- **Instrument:** MFL-10.65%-24-11-28-PVT (10.65% Privately Placed Debt Instrument)
- **Issue Size / Quantity:** 50,000 units at Rs. 10,000 face value
- **Credit Rating:** CARE BBB+
- **Date of Allotment:** 24/09/2026
- **Date of Redemption:** 24/11/2028
- **Interest Payment:** Monthly from 24/10/2026 to 24/11/2028 at 10.65% p.a.
- **Market Lot & Tick Size:** Market lot of 1, tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard admission of privately placed debt securities under existing exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in dematerialized form under the specified ISIN.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** September 24, 2026
- **Listing / Effective Date:** September 25, 2026
- **First Interest Payment Date:** October 24, 2026
- **Redemption Date:** November 24, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of Manba Finance Limited's newly listed debt securities on the BSE debt segment.
