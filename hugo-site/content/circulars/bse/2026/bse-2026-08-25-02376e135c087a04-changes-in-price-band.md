## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the price bands for 24 scrips have been revised from their existing levels. These changes come into effect from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 24 scrips affected by the price band modifications.
- Revised price bands range from 2%, 5%, 10%, to 20% depending on the specific scrip.
- Effective date for the new price bands is August 26, 2026.
- Members can reach out to bse.surv@bseindia.com for clarifications.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of price limits for specified equity scrips under exchange surveillance mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their trading terminals and risk management systems to reflect the revised price bands for the mentioned scrips effective from the start of trading on August 26, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Directly impacts intraday volatility limits and order placement thresholds for the 24 listed companies on the BSE platform.
