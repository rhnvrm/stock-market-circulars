BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Rysa Infratech Limited on October 01, 2026. The document is hosted on the SME website for public comments over a period of at least 21 days in accordance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

- Company Name: Rysa Infratech Limited
- Filing Date: October 01, 2026
- Notice Date: October 05, 2026
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice
- Feedback Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com
