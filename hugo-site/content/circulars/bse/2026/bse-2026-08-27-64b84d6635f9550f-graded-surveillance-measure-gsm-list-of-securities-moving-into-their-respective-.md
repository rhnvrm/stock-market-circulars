## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of specified securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from August 28, 2026. The measures include strict margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and trading frequency limitations depending on the assigned GSM stage.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure move to higher GSM stages effective August 28, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, ranging from 100% margin rates and lower price bands to trade-for-trade settlement and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement for affected securities will be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the GSM framework:
- **Stage I:** 100% applicable margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- **Stage II:** Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value by buyers.
- **Stage III:** Trade for trade, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week), and 100% ASD by buyers.
- **Stage IV:** Trade for trade, trading permitted once a week with no upward price movement, and 100% ASD by buyers.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities, ensuring compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules and required margin/ASD deposits.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for securities moving into higher GSM stages are likely to be restricted due to increased margin requirements, ASD deposits, and frequency limitations on trading.
