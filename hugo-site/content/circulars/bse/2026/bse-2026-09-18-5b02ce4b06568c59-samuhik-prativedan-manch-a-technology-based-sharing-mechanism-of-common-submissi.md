## Summary

SEBI, in consultation with Clearing Corporations, has extended the Samuhik Prativedan Manch—a technology-based sharing mechanism for compliance reports—to Self Clearing Members (SCMs) and Clearing Members (CMs) of Clearing Corporations who are also stock brokers of NSE.

## Key Points

- Samuhik Prativedan Manch allows members registered across multiple exchanges/clearing corporations to submit compliance reports to a single platform.
- Phase 1 is implemented with effect from September 30, 2026.
- NSE stock brokers who are SCMs or CMs of Clearing Corporations are exempt from submitting 14 specific compliance reports (listed in Annexure A) to individual Clearing Corporations separately; submitting to NSE will suffice.
- Phase 2 (scheduled for December 31, 2026) will explore remaining reports and the feasibility of extending the mechanism to Professional Clearing Members (PCMs).
- SCMs and CMs not registered with NSE will continue submitting reports separately to the respective Clearing Corporations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of single-window submission for specific clearing corporation compliance requirements via NSE for dual-registered members.
- Clearing Corporations will continue processing submissions, monitoring, and supervising compliance independently.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible SCMs and CMs must submit their Annexure A compliance reports/requests to NSE only starting September 30, 2026.
- Data submitted to NSE will be treated as official submission to the respective Clearing Corporations.

## Important Dates

- **September 17, 2026:** SEBI Press Release No. 58/2026 issued.
- **September 18, 2026:** Notice issued by BSE.
- **September 30, 2026:** Implementation date for Phase 1.
- **December 31, 2026:** Proposed implementation date for Phase 2 (remaining reports and PCMs).

## Impact Assessment

- Reduces compliance burden and paperwork for stock brokers who are also clearing members by eliminating duplicate filings across multiple exchanges and clearing corporations.
