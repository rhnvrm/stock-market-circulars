## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the trading and settlement schedule for the Basket Repo and Special Repo segment covering the period from November 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026. The schedule outlines transaction/takeup dates along with securities/fund pay-in and pay-out dates for both Ready Leg T+0 and Ready Leg T+1 settlements.

## Key Points

- Covers trading and settlement schedules for November 2026.
- Includes settlement timetables for Ready Leg T+0 and Ready Leg T+1 transactions.
- Settlement numbers range from 2627144 to 2627162.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine periodic schedule release for repo market operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and clearing members must adhere to the specified transaction, pay-in, and pay-out dates for the basket and special repo segments.

## Important Dates

- **Period Start Date:** 01-11-2026
- **Period End Date:** 30-11-2026
- **First Settlement (2627144):** Takeup Date 02-11-2026
- **Last Settlement (2627162):** Takeup Date 30-11-2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth operational planning and fund/securities clearing for repo participants throughout November 2026.
