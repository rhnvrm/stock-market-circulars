## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 10,00,000 new equity shares of Indo Thai Securities Limited (Scrip Code: 533676) issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Indo Thai Securities Limited
- Scrip Code: 533676
- ISIN: INE337M01021
- Securities: 10,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 49/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 50.00/-
- Date of Allotment: 08/06/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: Thursday, August 27, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 128618191 to 129618190
- Lock-in Details: 10,00,000 shares locked-in upto 27/02/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares. Lock-in requirements apply as specified up to February 27, 2027.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 08/06/2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: 27/08/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: 27/02/2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up capital of the company due to warrant conversion with standard lock-in constraints.
