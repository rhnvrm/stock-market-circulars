## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the outcome of the Reverse Book Building Process (RBBP) under Regulation 17(3) of the SEBI Delisting Regulations for the delisting of Equity Shares of Nitin Castings Limited. The offer was made by the acquirers to acquire 14,70,894 equity shares from public shareholders at a floor price of Rs. 273.36 per share.

## Key Points

- Target Company: Nitin Castings Limited.
- Acquirers: Mr. Nirmal B Kedia, Mr. Nitin S Kedia, and M/s. Citrus Castings Private Limited.
- Offer Period: Wednesday, August 05, 2026, to Tuesday, August 11, 2026.
- Floor Price: Rs. 273.36/- per equity share.
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited.
- Manager: Navigant Corporate Advisors Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Process conducted in accordance with SEBI Delisting Regulations, specifically Regulation 17(3).

## Compliance Requirements

- Disclosure of the RBBP outcome to the exchange and public shareholders as mandated by SEBI Delisting Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 11, 2026.
- Delisting Offer Period: August 05, 2026, to August 11, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects shareholders of Nitin Castings Limited regarding the delisting of their equity shares through the reverse book building mechanism.
