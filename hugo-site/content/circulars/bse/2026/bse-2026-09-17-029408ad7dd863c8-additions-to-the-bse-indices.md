## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of several newly listed companies to its indices, following their listing on the main and SME platforms of BSE.

## Key Points

- Main board listings added to the index effective at the open of Monday, September 21, 2026: Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Limited, Karmtara Engineering Limited, LCC Projects Limited, Manipal Payment And Identity Solutions Limited, Rentomojo Limited, and Steamhouse India Limited.
- SME platform listings added to the index effective at the open of Friday, September 18, 2026: Infrax Renewable Limited and Amtech Esters Limited.
- These actions are included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None.

## Important Dates

- September 18, 2026: Effective date for BSE SME index additions.
- September 21, 2026: Effective date for main board BSE index additions.

## Impact Assessment

Routine index inclusion for newly listed securities with minimal direct market impact.
