## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the submission of half-yearly Cyber Audit Reports for the audit period ending September 30, 2026, pursuant to SEBI's Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF).

## Key Points

- Cyber audit must cover 100% of critical systems and 25% of non-critical systems on a sample basis.
- Trading members and regulated entities must categorize themselves correctly and obtain approval from their Board of Directors, Designated Director, or equivalent authority.
- Auditors must verify the categorization and provide compliance status for each Terms of Reference (TOR) item.
- Submission is complete only after submission to the Exchange with management comments.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of timelines and audit norms under SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_CSC_EXT/P/CIR/2024/113 dated August 20, 2024, and subsequent clarification circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members (Qualified REs and Mid/Small-size REs providing IBT or Algo Trading) must conduct cyber audits through CERT-In empanelled auditors, obtain IT Committee approval, and submit reports along with subsequent Action Taken Reports (ATR).

## Important Dates

- **Audit Period Ending:** September 30, 2026
- **Conduct & Submission of Cyber Audit Report:** December 31, 2026
- **Submission of ATR/Revalidation Report:** March 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Ensures strict adherence to cybersecurity standards across trading members, reducing systemic IT risks and ensuring regulatory compliance with SEBI CSCRF guidelines.
