## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has published a surveillance notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in various scrips in the recent past. Clarifications have been received from 15 companies, while replies are awaited from 18 other companies.

## Key Points

- **Replies Received:** Clarifications were submitted by 15 companies including Abans Enterprises Ltd, Jindal Poly Films Ltd, Quick Heal Technologies Ltd, and Uttam Sugar Mills Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited:** Clarifications are pending from 18 companies including Deep Polymers Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, and Terai Tea Company Ltd.
- **Purpose:** To ensure investors and trading members have access to the latest relevant information regarding unusual market movements.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard surveillance monitoring and disclosure update.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities that observed significant price/volume movements are required to clarify and furnish relevant disclosures to the Exchange as mandated under surveillance guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 15 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard market transparency measure with negligible direct operational or financial impact on overall market indices.
