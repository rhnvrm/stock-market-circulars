## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the monthly reconstitution results for various BSE Indices, which will be effective at the open of Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Monthly reconstitution results announced by BSE Index Services.
- Changes affect various companies including Anthem Biosciences, Indiqube Spaces, NSDL, and others.
- Effective date for the reconstitution is August 24, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and index-tracking funds must align their portfolios with the reconstituted index constituents by the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 7, 2026
- Effective Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Moderate impact on trading volumes and re-balancing activities for funds tracking BSE indices.
