## Summary

Trading members are informed that 17,00,00,000 new equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 20.93/- on a preferential basis by Blue Cloud Softech Solutions Limited (Scrip Code: 539607) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange effective from Monday, August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Blue Cloud Softech Solutions Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 539607
- **ISIN:** INE373T01047
- **Security Details:** 17,00,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 20.93/- (Issue Price: Rs. 21.93/-) to non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 753081601 to 923081600
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to 28/02/2027.
- **Effective Date:** August 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the lock-in period and distinctive numbers for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 17/06/2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** 10/08/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 28/02/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action expanding the paid-up capital base of the company through preferential allotment.
