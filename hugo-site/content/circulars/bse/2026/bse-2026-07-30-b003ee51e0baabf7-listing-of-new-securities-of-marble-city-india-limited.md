## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 1,30,000 new equity shares of Marble City India Limited (Scrip Code - 531281) with effect from Friday, July 31, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Marble City India Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 531281
- **ISIN:** INE807H01023
- **Security Details:** 1,30,000 equity shares of Rs. 5/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 95.50/- (Issue Price: Rs. 100.50/-).
- **Effective Date:** July 31, 2026.
- **Lock-in Details:** 
  - 30,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 25515109 - 25545108) locked-in upto 15/02/2027.
  - 1,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 25545109 - 25645108) locked-in upto 14/02/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and trading restrictions for the specified distribution numbers.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 12/05/2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** 31/07/2026
- **Lock-in Expiry:** 15/02/2027 and 14/02/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase via preferential warrant conversion with no broad market impact.
