## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the change in scheme names for multiple schemes of Nippon India Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Fund House: Nippon India Mutual Fund
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform
- Changes involve multiple duration and category schemes being updated with revised terminology (e.g., changing 'DURATION' to 'TERM').

## Regulatory Changes

- NIPPON INDIA ULTRA SHORT DURATION FUND is renamed to NIPPON INDIA ULTRA SHORT TERM FUND.
- NIPPON INDIA LOW DURATION FUND is renamed to NIPPON INDIA ULTRA SHORT TO SHORT TERM FUND.
- NIPPON INDIA SHORT DURATION FUND is renamed to NIPPON INDIA SHORT TERM FUND.
- NIPPON INDIA FLOATER FUND is renamed to NIPPON INDIA FLOATING INTEREST RATES FUND.
- NIPPON INDIA BANKING AND PSU FUND is renamed to NIPPON INDIA BANKING AND PSU DEBT FUND.
- NIPPON INDIA MEDIUM TO LONG DURATION FUND is renamed to NIPPON INDIA MEDIUM TO LONG TERM FUND.
- NIPPON INDIA MEDIUM DURATION FUND is renamed to NIPPON INDIA MEDIUM TERM FUND.
- NIPPON INDIA DYNAMIC BOND FUND is renamed to NIPPON INDIA DYNAMIC TERM FUND.
- NIPPON INDIA NIVESH LAKSHYA LONG DURATION FUND is renamed to NIPPON INDIA NIVESH LAKSHYA LONG TERM FUND.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the revised scheme names for order placement and reporting on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for platform users and investors ensuring correct scheme identification during transactions.
