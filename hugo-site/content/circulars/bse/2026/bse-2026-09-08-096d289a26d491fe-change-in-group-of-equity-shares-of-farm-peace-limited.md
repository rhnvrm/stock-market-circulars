## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Farm Peace Limited (Scrip Code: 544905) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Wednesday, September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Farm Peace Limited
- Scrip Code: 544905
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: September 23, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260907-21 dated September 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The equity shares are being shifted from the restrictive Trade for Trade settlement category to the normal Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must take note of the segment change for execution and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 08, 2026
- Effective Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Improved trading fluidity and liquidity for investors as the shares move from Trade for Trade to the regular Rolling segment.
