## Summary

Purity Flexpack Ltd (scrip code: 523315) has signed agreements with both depositories. Effective Monday, August 03, 2026, trading and settlement modalities for the company's scrip will shift to normal rolling segment with compulsory demat form and a market lot of 1 equity share.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 523315
- Scrip Name: Purity Flexpack Ltd
- Effective Date: August 03, 2026 (Sett. No. DR-685/2026-2027)
- Revised Market Lot: 1 equity share
- Existing ISIN: INE898O01010

## Regulatory Changes

The scrip will be traded in the Normal Rolling Segment and settled in compulsory demat form with netting allowed.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised market lot and settlement modalities for Purity Flexpack Ltd.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 Jul 2026
- Effective Date: 03 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors dealing in Purity Flexpack Ltd must update their systems to reflect the market lot of 1 share and compulsory demat settlement.
