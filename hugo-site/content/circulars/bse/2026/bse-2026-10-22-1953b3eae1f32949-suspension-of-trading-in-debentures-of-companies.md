## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and/or redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) and Bonds. Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of the specified companies during the suspension period surrounding the record and ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Trading in debentures/bonds of multiple issuers is suspended temporarily in view of upcoming record dates for interest payment or redemption.
- Affected entities include municipal corporations, housing finance companies, banks, microfinance institutions, and corporate issuers.
- Trading members are cautioned against executing trades in these specific scrips during the restricted window.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; this is a standard stock exchange operational notice regarding corporate debt actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance by refraining from trading in the listed debentures during the designated ex-dates and record dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 22/10/2026
- Record Dates and Ex-Dates: Ranging from 31/10/2026 to 08/11/2026 depending on the specific debenture issue.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and routine market impact restricted to temporary trading suspension in specific debt instruments for interest/redemption processing.
