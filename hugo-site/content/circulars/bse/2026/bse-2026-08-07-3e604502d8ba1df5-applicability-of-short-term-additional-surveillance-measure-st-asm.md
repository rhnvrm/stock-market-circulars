## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities, effective from August 11, 2026, based on criteria satisfaction.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on August 10, 2026, and new positions created from August 11, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II framework rules as per previous exchange notices regarding surveillance revisions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must maintain the prescribed margin rates (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities effective August 11, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 07 Aug 2026
- Effective Date: August 11, 2026 (on open positions as on August 10, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for specified securities under ST-ASM stages may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
