## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released the schedule for client margin reporting for the Electronic Gold Receipts segment for August 2026. The circular specifies trade dates along with their corresponding last dates of margin reporting and penalty dates.

## Key Points

- Outlines the schedule for client margin reporting for August 2026 in the Electronic Gold Receipts segment.
- Details specific trade dates, last dates for margin reporting, and associated penalty dates to ensure compliance.
- Issued by BSE Clearing Limited (formerly Indian Clearing Corporation Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A (Routine operational circular)

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must adhere to the prescribed client margin reporting deadlines for the Electronic Gold Receipts segment to avoid penalties.

## Important Dates

- Effective for trade dates spanning from August 3, 2026, to August 31, 2026.
- Margin reporting and penalty dates follow sequentially throughout August and early September 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures market transparency and strict compliance with margin obligations for participants trading in Electronic Gold Receipts.
