## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of QUINT DIGITAL LIMITED are permitted on the Exchange effective from Wednesday, September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: QUINT DIGITAL LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Trading period for Renunciation of Rights Entitlements (REs): September 02, 2026 to September 07, 2026
- Scrip Code: 751134
- Scrip ID: QUINT-RE1
- ISIN No.: INE641R20025
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Rights Issue Period: September 02, 2026 to September 10, 2026
- 1 (one) partly paid-up Detachable Warrant shall be issued along with every 1 (one) Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS).

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members shall inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares. A prominent disclaimer must be included in contract notes/bills.

## Important Dates

- Trading Period Start Date: September 02, 2026
- Trading Period End Date: September 07, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: September 02, 2026
- Rights Issue Close Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables shareholders and investors to trade or renounce Rights Entitlements of Quint Digital Limited on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis.
