## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II. Securities satisfying the criteria will attract enhanced margin requirements effective September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable rate of margin of 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Securities qualifying for ST-ASM Stage II will attract an applicable rate of margin of 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher.
- The surveillance measures apply to all open positions as on August 31, 2026, and new positions created from September 01, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II framework actions as per existing guidelines and previous exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements for affected securities on open and new positions starting September 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 01, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off:** August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements may impact liquidity and trading volumes for securities listed under the ST-ASM stages.
