## Summary

Effective from Thursday, October 8, 2026, the equity shares of Shree TNB Polymers Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI circular dated January 20, 2012.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Shree TNB Polymers Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544978
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** SHREETNB
- **ISIN:** INE935K01018
- **Market Lot:** 2,000 Equity Shares
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 53/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 43/-)
- **Date of Allotment:** October 6, 2026
- **Total Securities:** 21,344,997 Equity Shares fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 21344997)
- **Market Maker:** Asnani Stock Broker Private Limited
- **Registrar:** MUFG Intime India Private Limited (formerly Link Intime India Private Limited)

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form pursuant to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in the minimum market lot of 2,000 equity shares.
- Corporate actions for locking in shares in demat form have been executed in depository records.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, October 8, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 6, 2026
- **Listing and Trading Effective Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and public market access for investors of Shree TNB Polymers Limited while maintaining standard SME surveillance and trade-for-trade monitoring during initial trading sessions.
