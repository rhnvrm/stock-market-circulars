## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Monday, September 21, 2026, the equity shares of MANIKA PLASTECH LIMITED shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the 'T' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: MANIKA PLASTECH LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544929
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: MANIKA
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT System: MANIKA
- ISIN No.: INE0KWF01020
- Face Value: Rs. 2/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 43/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 2/- and premium of Rs. 41/-)
- No. of Securities: 116511627 Equity Shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 To 116511627
- Market Lot: 1
- Date of Allotment: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities of the company will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Monday, September 21, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed to have the lock-in period marked in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 17, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Manika Plastech Limited equity shares on the BSE platform under the Trade-for-Trade segment for the initial 10 trading days.
