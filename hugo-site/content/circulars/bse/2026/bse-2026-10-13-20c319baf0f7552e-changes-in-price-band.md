## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that the price bands for 22 scrips have been revised from their existing levels with effect from October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total Scrips Affected: 22
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026
- Notice Number: 20261013-30 (Dated 13 Oct 2026)
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance)

- Abans Enterprises Limited (512165): 5%
- Alufluoride Limited (524634): 5%
- Anand Seamless Limited (544944): 20%
- Aptus Pharma Limited (544529): 2%
- Avro India Limited (543512): 10%

- Vama Wovenfab Limited (544931): 5%

## Regulatory Changes

Price band adjustments implemented as part of exchange surveillance measures to curb excessive volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure orders placed in the trading system comply with the new limits effective from October 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Directly impacts intraday trading, order placement, and volatility management for the specified 22 equities.
