## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of newly listed companies to the BSE IPO and BSE SME IPO indices. The inclusions follow the listing of these securities on the mainboard and SME platforms of BSE.

## Key Points

- **Vishal Nirmiti Limited (544973)** and **Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited (544974)** will be added to the BSE IPO index effective at the open of Monday, October 12, 2026.
- **Dove Soft Limited (544975)**, **Omara Ventures India Limited (544976)**, **Sollfege Smart Electronics Limited (544977)**, **Shree TNB Polymers Limited (544978)**, **Everestims Technologies Limited (544979)**, and **SJP Ultrasonics Limited (544980)** will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open of Friday, October 09, 2026.
- All corporate actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard index maintenance activity.

## Compliance Requirements

None. For informational purposes for market participants and index trackers.

## Important Dates

- **October 08, 2026:** Notice issuance date and listing date for the mentioned securities.
- **October 09, 2026:** Effective date for BSE SME IPO index additions.
- **October 12, 2026:** Effective date for BSE IPO index additions.

## Impact Assessment

Standard index rebalancing and inclusion update with minimal operational impact on market participants.
