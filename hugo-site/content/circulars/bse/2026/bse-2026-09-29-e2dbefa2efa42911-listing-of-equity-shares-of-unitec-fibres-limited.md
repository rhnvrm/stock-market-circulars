## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, September 30, 2026, the Equity Shares of UNITEC FIBRES LIMITED are listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE SME segment under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Total Securities: 14,418,578 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 14418578).
- Issue Price: Rs. 88/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 78/-).
- Scrip Code: 544948 | Scrip ID: UNITEC | ISIN: INE0WOK01014.
- Market Lot: 1,600 equity shares.
- Date of Allotment: September 28, 2026.
- Market Maker: Shreni Shares Limited.
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only and subject to a Trade-for-Trade segment restriction for the first 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with dematerialised trading norms and lock-in requirements as specified in the prospectus and exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 28, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public trading and liquidity for UNITEC FIBRES LIMITED on the BSE SME exchange platform.
