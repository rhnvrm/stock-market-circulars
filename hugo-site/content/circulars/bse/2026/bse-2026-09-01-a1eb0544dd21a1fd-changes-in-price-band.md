## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 14 scrips in the Equity segment, effective from September 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 14 scrips traded on the exchange.
- Effective date: September 2, 2026.
- Affected scrips include Aerpace Industries Ltd, Alok Industries Ltd, Bafna Pharmaceuticals Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of price bands from existing levels to revised percentages (2%, 5%, or 10%) as specified for each scrip.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while executing orders.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Limits extreme price volatility in the specified scrips by enforcing updated daily price operating ranges.
