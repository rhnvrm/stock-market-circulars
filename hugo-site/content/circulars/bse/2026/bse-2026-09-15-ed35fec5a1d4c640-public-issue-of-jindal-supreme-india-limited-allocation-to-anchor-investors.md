## Summary

Trading members are informed that 40,28,400 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors portion in the Public Issue of JINDAL SUPREME INDIA LIMITED at Rs. 93/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: JINDAL SUPREME INDIA LIMITED
- Anchor Investor Shares Allocated: 40,28,400 equity shares
- Issue Price / Share Price: Rs. 93/- per equity share
- Date of Announcement: September 15, 2026

None

Trading members and market participants must take note of the anchor investor allocation details for compliance and settlement reference.

- Notice Date: September 15, 2026

Provides transparency regarding institutional and anchor investor participation prior to the broader public issue opening.
