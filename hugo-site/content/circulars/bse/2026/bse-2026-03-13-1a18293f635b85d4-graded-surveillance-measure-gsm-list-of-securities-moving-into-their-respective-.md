## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a circular detailing the list of securities moving into their respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, effective as part of market surveillance mechanisms to safeguard investors.

## Key Points

- Jayatma Industries Ltd (Security Code: 531323, ISIN: INE250D01017) has moved to GSM Stage I.
- VK Global Industries Ltd (Security Code: 530177, ISIN: INE758B01013), Golechha Global Finance Ltd (Security Code: 531360, ISIN: INE427D01011), and ANS Industries Ltd (Security Code: 531406, ISIN: INE234J01018) have moved to GSM Stage II.
- Oswal Yarns Ltd (Security Code: 514460, ISIN: INE670H01017) and Iykot Hitech Toolroom Ltd (Security Code: 522245, ISIN: INE079L01013) have moved to GSM Stage III.
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower under the GSM framework due to inclusion in the ESM framework, while those marked with ($) move lower due to inclusion in the IBC framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance measures, including restrictions on trading, additional margin requirements, and impounding of deposits where applicable depending on the specific GSM stage.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and brokers must ensure adherence to trading restrictions and margin parameters applicable to securities placed under various GSM stages.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by exchange surveillance notifications corresponding to circular ID 20260313-35.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will face enhanced scrutiny, potentially resulting in reduced liquidity and increased volatility for the shorted scrips.
