## Summary

BSE has issued guidelines pursuant to SEBI directives regarding the continuity of compliance and reporting requirements for research analysts (RAs) who have submitted surrender applications.

## Key Points

- Submission of a surrender application to BSE/RAASB does not automatically absolve an intermediary from statutory duties.
- Research analysts remain legally registered with SEBI and are bound to fulfill all compliance, grievance redressal, and periodic reporting requirements until formal acceptance of surrender by SEBI.
- The date of cessation of regulatory obligations and code of conduct is the date of formal acceptance of surrender by SEBI, not the application submission date.

## Regulatory Changes

Clarification of regulatory status and duties during the transition/surrender review period for research analysts under SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014.

## Compliance Requirements

Intermediaries must maintain all active compliance mechanisms and submit necessary regulatory returns during the pendency of the surrender review process to avoid enforcement actions for non-compliance.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 14 October 2026
- Effective: Immediate

## Impact Assessment

Ensures continuous oversight, investor grievance redressal, and regulatory compliance of research analysts right up to the final approval of their surrender by SEBI.
