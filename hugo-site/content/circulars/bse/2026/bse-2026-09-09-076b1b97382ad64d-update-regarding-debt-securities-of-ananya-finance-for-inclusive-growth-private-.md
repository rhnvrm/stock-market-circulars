## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities of Ananya Finance For Inclusive Growth Private Limited due to debt restructuring, effective September 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ananya Finance For Inclusive Growth Private Limited
- Scrip Code: 975412
- ISIN: INE774L07081
- Reason for Action: Debt restructuring
- Effective Date: Monday, September 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in the specified debt securities is suspended in continuation of previous Exchange Notice No. 20240223-38 dated Feb 23, 2024.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the suspension and refrain from trading in the affected scrip code and ISIN from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 9, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity for holders of the specified debt securities (ISIN: INE774L07081) will be temporarily halted on the exchange platform due to restructuring proceedings.
