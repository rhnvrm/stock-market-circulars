## Summary

Effective Monday, August 03, 2026, the equity shares of D.S. Kulkarni Developers Limited are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the T Group of securities in the Trade-to-Trade segment, following the NCLT-approved resolution plan under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), 2016.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 523890
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: DSKULKARNI
- ISIN No: INE891A01022
- Group: T (Trade-to-Trade segment)
- Issue details include 95,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares issued on a preferential basis to the Successful Resolution Applicant and affiliates, and 5,00,000 equity shares issued to public shareholders via Offer for Sale (OFS).

## Regulatory Changes

- Listing implemented pursuant to the NCLT order dated June 23, 2023.

## Compliance Requirements

- Security will be part of the special pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips.
- Lock-in of 9,50,000 shares up to August 31, 2027.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- Effective Date: August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Re-admission of company shares to public trading under strict segment controls (T Group).
