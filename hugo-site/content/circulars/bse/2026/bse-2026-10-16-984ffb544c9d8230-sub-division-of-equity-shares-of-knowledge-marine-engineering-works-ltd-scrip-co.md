## Summary

Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Ltd has announced the sub-division of its equity shares. The company has fixed November 6, 2026, as the record date for sub-dividing its existing equity shares from one equity share of Rs. 5/- each into two equity shares of Re. 1/- each.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Knowledge Marine & Engineering Works Ltd (Scrip Code: 543273, ISIN: INE0CJD01029)
- **Corporate Action:** Sub-division (stock split) of equity shares.
- **Ratio:** 1 equity share of Rs. 5/- each into 2 equity shares of Re. 1/- each.
- **Record Date:** November 6, 2026.
- **Effective Date:** November 6, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action processing under rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are informed that ISIN INE0CJD01029 (Rs. 5/- paid up) will not be valid for transactions done on or after November 6, 2026.
- The new ISIN for Re. 1/- paid up shares will be notified separately by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date / Effective Date:** November 6, 2026
- **Notice Date:** October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders will receive two shares of Re. 1/- for every one share of Rs. 5/- held as of the record date. Liquidity and share price will adjust accordingly based on the split ratio.
