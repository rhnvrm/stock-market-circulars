## Summary

Effective from Monday, September 7, 2026, the equity shares of Purple Style Labs Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities following its initial public offering.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Purple Style Labs Limited
- Scrip Code: 544901
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: PERNIASPOP
- ISIN: INE0I1B01016
- Total Securities Listed: 80,061,086 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 575/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 565/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 3, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.
- Corporate action executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository's records as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 3, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members can trade in the equity shares of Purple Style Labs Limited starting September 7, 2026, with the scrip participating in the Special Pre-open Session.
