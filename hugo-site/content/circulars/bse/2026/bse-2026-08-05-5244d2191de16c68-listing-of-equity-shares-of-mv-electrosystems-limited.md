## Summary

Effective from Thursday, August 6, 2026, equity shares of MV Electrosystems Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544851
- Scrip ID: MVELECTRO
- ISIN No: INE0OWZ01020
- Total Securities: 27,282,728 Equity Shares of Rs.5/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 425/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/-, Premium Rs. 420/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 4, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on August 6, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action executed for demat lock-in period marking.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 4, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: August 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official exchange trading access for investors of MV Electrosystems Limited.
