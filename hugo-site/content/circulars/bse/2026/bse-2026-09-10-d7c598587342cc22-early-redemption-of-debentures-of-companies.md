## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the early redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) for GMR Airports Limited and Share India Securities Limited. Trading members are advised of the fixed record dates and settlement dates, and are instructed not to deal in the specified debentures as per the schedule.

## Key Points

- **GMR Airports Limited:** 
  - Scrip Code 976449 (ISIN: INE776C08059, Scrip ID: GMRAL-5%-25-2-28-PVT): Record Date 11/09/2026, Ex-Date 11/09/2026.
  - Scrip Code 976601 (ISIN: INE776C08067, Scrip ID: GMRALD-5%-3-4-28-PVT): Record Date 13/09/2026, Ex-Date 11/09/2026.
- **Share India Securities Limited:** 
  - Scrip Code 976824 (ISIN: INE932X07023, Scrip ID: SISL-10.70%-10-06-27-PVT): Record Date 15/09/2026, Ex-Date 15/09/2026.
  - Scrip Code 976825 (ISIN: INE932X07015, Scrip ID: SISL-10.75%-10-06-27-PVT): Record Date 15/09/2026, Ex-Date 15/09/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the mentioned debentures of the companies as per the specified dates.

## Important Dates

- **11/09/2026:** Record and Ex-Date for GMR Airports Limited (Scrip Code 976449); Ex-Date for GMR Airports Limited (Scrip Code 976601).
- **13/09/2026:** Record Date for GMR Airports Limited (Scrip Code 976601).
- **15/09/2026:** Record and Ex-Date for Share India Securities Limited (Scrip Codes 976824 and 976825).

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly settlement and halts trading in the specified NCDs ahead of their early redemption process.
