## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled for August 07, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated August 03, 2026. Two securities (6.36% GS 2031 and 7.71% GS 2066) will be available for bidding through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Securities offered: 6.36% GS 2031 (Issue size: ₹21,000 Cr) and 7.71% GS 2066 (Issue size: ₹11,000 Cr).
- Bidding Platform: https://ibbs.bseindia.com >> NCB-GSec module.
- Direct Investor Bid Collection End Date: August 06, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- Trading Members Bid Collection End Date: August 07, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- Auction Date: August 07, 2026.
- Settlement Date: August 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module of the iBBS system and ensure timely pay-in through ICCL operations.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: August 04, 2026 (10:00 am onwards)
- Direct Investor Bid Collection End Date: August 06, 2026 (till 5:00 pm)
- Members Bid Collection End Date: August 07, 2026 (till 8:00 am)
- Auction Date: August 07, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates non-competitive participation of trading members and direct investors in government dated securities auctions.
