## Summary

HSBC Mutual Fund has communicated a change in scheme names for six of its schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **HSBC Medium to Long Duration Fund** renamed to **HSBC Medium to Long Term Fund**
- **HSBC Short Duration Fund** renamed to **HSBC Short Term Fund**
- **HSBC Low Duration Fund** renamed to **HSBC Ultra Short to Short Term Fund Fund**
- **HSBC Ultra Short Duration Fund** renamed to **HSBC Ultra Short Term Fund**
- **HSBC Medium Duration Fund** renamed to **HSBC Medium Term Fund**
- **HSBC Dynamic Bond Fund** renamed to **HSBC Dynamic Term Fund**

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for order placement and transaction processing on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal impact; purely administrative change involving nomenclature of specific fund schemes.
