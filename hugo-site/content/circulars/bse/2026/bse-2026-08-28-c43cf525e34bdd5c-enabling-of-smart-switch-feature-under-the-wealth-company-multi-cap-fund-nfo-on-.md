## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about the enablement of the SMART Switch feature under THE WEALTH COMPANY MULTI CAP FUND NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform. The feature is available from August 28, 2026, until September 08, 2026, for eligible lumpsum non-Demat subscriptions.

## Key Points

- Feature availability: August 28, 2026, to September 08, 2026 (up to 1:00 PM).
- Applicable modes: User interface, Bulk Upload, and APIs.
- Restriction: Available only for non-Demat mode lump sum subscriptions in specified schemes.
- Switch Out processing: Processed on a FIFO basis for newly purchased units under the 'NFO SMART Switch' plan.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Operational guideline for BSE StAR MF platform transaction processing).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members using Direct APIs, API-based platforms, or Bulk Upload options must use appropriate scheme credentials strictly in accordance with investor requirements.

## Important Dates

- Feature Start Date: August 28, 2026
- Feature End Date / Deadline: September 08, 2026 (up to 1:00 PM)
- RTA Reporting Date for Switch Transactions: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates seamless switching of investments into THE WEALTH COMPANY MULTI CAP FUND NFO for investors through multiple platform modes.
