## Summary

In partial modification of Exchange Notice No. 20260430-18 dated April 30, 2026, BSE has announced the introduction of the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the equity segment w.e.f. August 03, 2026, along with revised timings for various trading sessions.

## Key Points

- Introduction of Closing Auction Session (CAS) effective August 03, 2026.
- Detailed schedule provided for continuous trading, pre-open sessions, block deals, and closing auction periods.
- Transition from Continuous Trading Session (CTS) to CAS starts at 03:15 pm and ends at 03:20 pm.
- Order entry for limit and market orders during CAS takes place between 03:20 pm and 03:25 pm, followed by limit orders only from 03:25 pm to 03:30 pm.
- Order matching/confirmation period runs from 03:30 pm to 03:35 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of trading hours and session timings to accommodate the new Closing Auction Session in the equity segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their trading systems and operational workflows to align with the new CAS timings and order entry windows.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact on all market participants and trading members executing trades in the equity segment during market close.
