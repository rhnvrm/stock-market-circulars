## Summary

Effective from Thursday, September 3, 2026, the equity shares of Kwick Forensic Solutions Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities in the SME segment.

## Key Points

- Scrip Name: KWICK FORENSIC SOLUTIONS LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544895
- ISIN: INE1SFA01019
- Market Lot: 1,600 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 90/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 80/-)
- Total Securities: 21,436,440 Equity Shares
- Segment: Trade-for-Trade for 10 trading days

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on listing day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in the minimum market lot of 1,600 shares.
- Lock-in and shareholding details apply as per the prospectus annexures.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 1, 2026
- Listing and Trading Date: September 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading for Kwick Forensic Solutions Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with standard trade-for-trade monitoring for the initial 10 trading sessions.
