## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of Zero Coupon Zero Principal instruments (ZCZP) of Light of Life Trust on the Social Stock Exchange (SSE) segment effective from September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Entity Name:** Light of Life Trust
- **Effective Date:** September 4, 2026
- **Scrip Code:** 798005
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** LOLTZCZP
- **ISIN No:** INS2QJV12010
- **Number of Securities:** 1,22,30,365 ZCZP instruments of Rs. 1/- each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 1/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 1)
- **Date of Allotment:** August 25, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** August 24, 2028
- **Note:** The above-mentioned securities will not be traded on the Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are informed of the listing details for record and reference purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 25, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** September 4, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** August 24, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Involves the formal listing of ZCZP instruments on the Social Stock Exchange (SSE) segment without trading permissions.
