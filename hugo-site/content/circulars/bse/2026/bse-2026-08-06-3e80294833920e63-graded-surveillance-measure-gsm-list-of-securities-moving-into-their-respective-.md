## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing the movement of certain securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from August 07, 2026. These measures include strict margin requirements, price band caps, and trade-for-trade settlement without netting off.

## Key Points

- Effective date of implementation: August 07, 2026.
- Affected securities move into higher stages (Stage I, II, III, or IV) of the GSM framework.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, ranging from 100% margin requirements and 5% price bands to trade-for-trade settlement and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement for these securities shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities identified in the annexure are subjected to enhanced surveillance measures according to the provisions of the GSM framework established on February 23, 2017.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the listed securities.
- Buyers must deposit applicable Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) and maintain 100% margin rates depending on the assigned GSM stage.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 06 Aug 2026
- Effective Date: 07 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for securities moving into higher GSM stages are expected to be constrained due to trade-for-trade settlement rules, prohibition of netting off, and mandatory deposits required from buyers.
