## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by multiple companies on the Debt Market Segment effective September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment in dematerialised form.
- Standard trading denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuers include Godrej Finance Limited, OXYZO Financial Services Limited, Redington Limited, Dalmia Cement (Bharat) Limited, and Bajaj Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the prescribed ISINs, standard denominations, and dematerialised trading requirements.

## Important Dates

- Effective Listing Date: September 17, 2026
- Allotment and Redemption dates vary by issuer (ranging from December 2026 to February 2027).

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice with no broader market impact.
