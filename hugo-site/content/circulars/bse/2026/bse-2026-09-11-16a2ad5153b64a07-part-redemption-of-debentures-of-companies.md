## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the fixed record date for payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) / bonds for several companies. Trading in these debentures will be with a reduced face value effective from the record date.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260911-11
- Category: Corporate Actions (Debt Segment)
- Record Date / Ex-Date: 15/09/2026
- Affected Companies: Total Environment Constructions Private Limited, Inspira Springdale Private Limited, Best Capital Services Limited, Renew Solar Energy (Jharkhand Three) Private Limited, and Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that trading in the debentures of the respective companies will proceed with reduced face values starting from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 11 Sep 2026
- Record Date: 15/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 15/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for trading members and debenture holders reflecting reduced face values and part redemption payouts.
