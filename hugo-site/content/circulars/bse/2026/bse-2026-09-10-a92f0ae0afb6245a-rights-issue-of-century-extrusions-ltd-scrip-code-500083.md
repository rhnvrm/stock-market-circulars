## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that Century Extrusions Ltd. has fixed a record date for a rights issue of equity shares. Transactions in the equity shares of the company will take place on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified date.

## Key Points

- **Company Name & Code:** CENTURY EXTRUSIONS LTD. (500083)
- **Record Date:** 15/09/2026
- **Ex-Right Basis Date:** 15/09/2026 (Sett. No. DR- 715/2026-2027)
- **Rights Ratio:** 03 (Three) Equity Shares for every 08 (Eight) Equity Shares held.
- **Issue Price:** Re. 1/- each for Cash at a Premium of Rs. 14/- per share (Total issue price of Rs. 15/- per share).
- **Payment Structure:** Issue price of Rs. 14/- per share (plus face value) is payable as Rs. 7.5 per share on application and Rs. 7.5 on one or more subsequent calls.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the record date and ensure proper execution and settlement on an ex-rights basis starting from 15/09/2026.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** 15/09/2026
- **Ex-Rights Date:** 15/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders as of the record date are eligible for the rights issue entitlement. Investors and traders should account for the price adjustment and payment terms associated with the partly-paid rights shares.
