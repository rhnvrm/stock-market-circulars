## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing the trading and settlement programme for the Mutual Fund segment covering the period from September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026. The circular outlines settlement numbers, transaction dates, fund pay-in, and payout dates for both liquid and non-liquid mutual fund subscriptions and redemptions.

## Key Points

- Covers mutual fund subscription and redemption schedules for September 2026 (Settlement Nos. 2627105 to 2627125).
- Non-liquid transactions funds pay-out to AMCs occurs before 3:00 PM on T day.
- NFO (MF) schemes funds pay-out happens on T day based on funds confirmation received by BSECL till 6:00 PM on T day.
- Liquid mutual fund subscriptions follow a same-day pay-out schedule.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and AMCs must adhere to the specified settlement dates and timings for pay-in and pay-out operations.
