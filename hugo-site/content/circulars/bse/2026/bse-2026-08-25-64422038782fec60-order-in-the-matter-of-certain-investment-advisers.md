## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the cancellation of the Certificate of Registration of Investment Adviser Sharanjit Singh (SEBI Registration No. INA100015036, BSE Enlistment No. 1616) pursuant to SEBI Circular No. CGM/SM/MIRSD/MIRSD-RAC-2/107/2026 dated August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- SEBI has cancelled the registration of Investment Adviser Sharanjit Singh.
- The cancellation is on account of non-payment of the prescribed renewal fees under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.
- Consequently, the enlistment granted to the Investment Adviser by BSE Limited also stands cancelled.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of registration cancellation under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 for failure to pay renewal fees.

## Compliance Requirements

- The concerned entity is no longer authorized to act as an Investment Adviser upon cancellation of registration and enlistment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Reference SEBI Circular Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational restriction on the deregistered investment adviser from providing investment advisory services.
