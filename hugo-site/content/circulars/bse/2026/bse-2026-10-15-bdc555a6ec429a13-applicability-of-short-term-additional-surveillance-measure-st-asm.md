## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II on certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable rate of margin of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II will attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Measures apply to all open positions as on October 16, 2026, and new positions created from October 19, 2026.
- The shortlisting is purely a market surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) under the ST-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must maintain applicable margin rates of 50% or 100% respectively on open positions as of October 16, 2026, and new positions created from October 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Benchmark Date for Open Positions: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements will reduce leverage and impact trading liquidity for market participants holding positions in securities designated under ST-ASM Stage I and II.
