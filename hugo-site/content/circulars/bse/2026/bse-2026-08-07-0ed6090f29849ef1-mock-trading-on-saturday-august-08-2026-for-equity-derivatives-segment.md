## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, August 08, 2026. Trading members utilizing third-party trading platforms can test their applications and various market functionalities during this session.

## Key Points

- **Date:** Saturday, August 08, 2026.
- **Segment:** Equity Derivatives.
- **Purpose:** Testing of third-party trading platforms, exceptional market conditions, call auction sessions, risk-reduction modes, trading halts, and block deals.
- **BOLT TWS:** No new BOLT TWS Release.
- **Feedback:** Members are requested to provide feedback by 1:00 PM via the provided contact numbers or email IDs.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A - Operational testing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to actively participate in the mock trading session and submit feedback to ensure system robustness.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** August 08, 2026
- **Log-in:** 9:15 AM - 10:00 AM
- **Order Entry Period:** 10:00 AM - 10:08 AM
- **Order Matching & Buffer Period:** 10:08 AM - 10:15 AM
- **Continuous Trading:** 10:15 AM - 12:10 PM
- **Trading Modification:** 12:30 PM
- **Feedback Deadline:** 1:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Helps trading members verify application readiness, risk management features, and system stability under simulated conditions.
