## Summary

BSE has released the trading and settlement schedule for the Institutional Trading Platform (ITP) under the SME segment for the period starting from October 1, 2026, to October 31, 2026, issued by the Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd (ICCL).

## Key Points

- Covers settlement numbers from ITP-127/26-27 to ITP-146/26-27.
- Specifies corresponding depository settlement numbers, ITP trading dates, and pay-in/pay-out dates.
- Pay-in is scheduled at 10:00 a.m. on the respective pay-in dates.
- Members are required to submit pay-in instructions to depositories and banks latest by 09:50 a.m.
- Pay-out of funds and securities will be completed by 11:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit pay-in instructions to depositories and banks by 09:50 a.m. on settlement dates.

## Important Dates

- **Period Start:** 01-10-2026
- **Period End:** 31-10-2026
- **First Settlement Date:** Trading on 01-10-2026, Pay-in/Pay-out on 05-10-2026
- **Last Settlement Date:** Trading on 30-10-2026, Pay-in/Pay-out on 02-11-2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement cycles and operational transparency for trading participants operating within the SME Institutional Trading Platform segment for October 2026.
