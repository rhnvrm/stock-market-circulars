## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the application, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, effective October 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable effective October 06, 2026, on open positions as on October 05, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM or out of the framework effective October 01, 2026.
- Lower price bands applicable effective October 01, 2026, for specified securities.
- Certain securities continued in LT-ASM framework but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins and lower price bands under revised LT-ASM guidelines.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and lower price band restrictions.
- Members and trading participants should review consolidated lists in Annexure attachments for affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- October 01, 2026: Effective date for higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price bands, and securities moving out of framework.
- October 05, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- October 06, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and transfer to T / XT / MT / TS groups.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for affected securities will experience tightened controls and restrictions in accordance with exchange surveillance protocols.
