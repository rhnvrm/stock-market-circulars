## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Borosil Renewables Limited (Scrip Code - 502219) with effect from October 7, 2026. A total of 12,52,629 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 529/- to non-promoters on a preferential basis following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Borosil Renewables Limited
- Scrip Code: 502219
- ISIN: INE666D01022
- Number of Securities: 12,52,629 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 530/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 1/-, Premium: Rs. 529/-)
- Allottees: Non-Promoters (preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants)
- Date of Allotment: 30/07/2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 145554521 to 146807149
- Lock-in Details: 12,52,629 shares locked-in up to 07/04/2027

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a corporate action listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are informed that the shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares and are admitted for trading starting October 7, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: July 30, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: October 7, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 7, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- The listing increases the paid-up equity capital of Borosil Renewables Limited due to warrant conversion, with standard lock-in compliance applicable to the newly allotted shares.
