## Summary

BSE has issued guidelines pursuant to the amendment of SEBI KRA Regulations, 2011, advising trading members that clients whose KYCs are not validated by KRAs (i.e., status is "On Hold" for both Aadhaar and Non-Aadhaar based OVD) uploaded between August 01, 2026, and August 31, 2026, will not be permitted to trade or square off open positions effective September 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- All regulated entities must block debit transactions and suspend trading account transactions for demised investors as per SEBI circular dated October 03, 2023.
- Trading members must ensure all client KYCs are uploaded to KRAs and permit trading only for clients with "KYC Registered" or "KYC Validated" status.
- Clients with KYCs marked as "On Hold" uploaded between August 01, 2026, and August 31, 2026, are barred from trading and squaring off open positions starting September 26, 2026.
- Non-compliant PANs will be flagged by the Exchange as "Not Permitted to Trade" w.e.f. September 26, 2026.
- List of non-compliant clients is made available via member back-office paths.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter validation checks via KRAs for client onboarding and continuous compliance under SEBI KYC regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must verify KRA validation status, restrict non-compliant clients from trading or squaring off open positions effective September 26, 2026, and regularize KYC records to resume trading on T+1 after KRA validation.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 16, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading Restrictions: September 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Clients with unvalidated or "On Hold" KYC statuses will face immediate trading suspensions and inability to square off positions until full compliance is achieved with KRAs.
