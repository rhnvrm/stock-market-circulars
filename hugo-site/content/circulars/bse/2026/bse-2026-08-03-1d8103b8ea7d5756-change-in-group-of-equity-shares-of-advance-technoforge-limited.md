## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Advance Technoforge Limited will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective from Monday, August 17, 2026, following the Exchange's earlier notice dated July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Advance Technoforge Limited
- Scrip Code: 544843
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: August 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The trading category for the company's equity shares moves out of the Trade for Trade restriction to standard rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems to reflect the change in segment and trading group.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 3, 2026
- Effective Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normalizes trading mechanics for Advance Technoforge Limited equity shares, enabling standard rolling settlement and improved liquidity.
