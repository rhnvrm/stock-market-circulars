## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Godavari Drugs Ltd with effect from October 22, 2026. A total of 7,86,690 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 79/- to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Godavari Drugs Ltd (Scrip Code - 530317, ISIN - INE362C01012)
- Number of Shares: 7,86,690 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 89/- per share (including premium of Rs. 79/-)
- Allotment Date: 14/08/2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 22, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 10127436 to 10914125
- Lock-in Period: Locked-in up to 29/04/2028

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate action involving the listing of new securities issued on a preferential basis.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and trading permissions as specified by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 14, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: October 22, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 29, 2028

## Impact Assessment

The listing increases the paid-up equity capital of Godavari Drugs Ltd through warrant conversion by promoters, with standard lock-in restrictions applicable.
