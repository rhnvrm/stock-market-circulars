## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability and revision of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Key Points

- 100% margins shall be applicable effective September 01, 2026, on all open positions as on August 31, 2026, and new positions created from September 01, 2026 onwards for securities under Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM Framework and securities with Lower Price Band applicability take effect from August 28, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective September 01, 2026.
- Selected securities are moving out of the LT-ASM Framework effective August 28, 2026, with price bands reinstated to their original levels.
- Shortlisting is based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins and lower price bands under the revised LT-ASM framework.
- Transfer of specific securities to trade-for-trade segments (T / XT / MT / TS Groups).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities.
- Members can reach out to bse.surv@bseindia.com for clarifications.

## Important Dates

- August 28, 2026: Movement to higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price band applicability, and removal of securities from LT-ASM.
- August 31, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to new margin rules.
- September 01, 2026: 100% margin applicability date and transfer of securities to T / XT / MT / TS Groups.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and risk management requirements for affected securities will tighten due to 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions.
