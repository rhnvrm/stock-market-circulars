## Summary

Pind Hospitality Limited (Scrip Code: 544966) equity shares are being transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective Wednesday, October 21, 2026, following Exchange Notice No. 20261005-39.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pind Hospitality Limited
- Scrip Code: 544966
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of trading segment from Trade for Trade (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should take note of the segment change and ensure compliance with respective trading norms.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Improved liquidity and normal trading settlement for equity shares of Pind Hospitality Limited upon shifting to the Rolling segment.
