## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities listed in the attached annexures have satisfied criteria for inclusion, movement to lower stages (Stage I), or removal from the ESM framework effective October 21 and October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 22, 2026, on open positions as on October 21, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 21, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 22, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced margins (100%), trade-for-trade settlement, and periodic call auctions for shortlisted securities under the ESM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure adequate margins (100%) are maintained for open and new positions in securities under ESM and adhere to trade-for-trade settlement norms.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II / Shifting / Movouts / Framework changes: October 21, 2026 and October 22, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading volumes for affected securities may experience moderation due to 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
