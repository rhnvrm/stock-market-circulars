## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Government of India Treasury Bills (T-Bills) scheduled for October 14, 2026. Trading members and eligible investors can participate through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- **Securities Offered:**
  - 91 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 091T130127, Issue Size: ₹8,000 Cr, Minimum Lot: ₹10,000, Max Amount: ₹400 Cr)
  - 182 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 182T140427, Issue Size: ₹8,000 Cr, Minimum Lot: ₹10,000, Max Amount: ₹400 Cr)
  - 364 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 364T141027, Issue Size: ₹7,000 Cr, Minimum Lot: ₹10,000, Max Amount: ₹350 Cr)
- **Platform:** Bidding is conducted via the NCB-GSec module on https://ibbs.bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine issuance notice based on the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) auction press release and Exchange circular no. 20180423-42.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must ensure bid submissions comply with the specified timelines and lot sizes.
- Fund pay-ins and settlement obligations must be fulfilled through ICCL operations.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** October 12, 2026, 10:00 AM onwards
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors):** October 13, 2026, till 5:00 PM
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** October 14, 2026, till 8:00 AM
- **Auction Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates seamless retail and institutional participation in Government securities auctions through organized exchange infrastructure.
