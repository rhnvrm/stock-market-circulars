## Summary

BSE Ltd has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 19 scrips, effective from August 18, 2026. These changes modify the allowable daily price movement limits for the specified securities.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 19 scrips across various categories/percentage limits.
- Effective date of revision: August 18, 2026.
- Issued by the Surveillance department of BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of price bands from existing levels to new specified percentages (2%, 5%, 10%, or 20%) for the listed scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure their trading systems and clients are updated accordingly.
- Queries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Directly impacts the intraday price volatility and circuit limits for the 19 scrips listed in the circular, restricting or expanding allowable price movements during trading sessions.
