## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, October 10, 2026, operating from both the Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR).

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 10, 2026.
- Includes testing across Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR) with scheduled switchover times.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.60 release details to be published separately.
- Trades executed during the mock session do not create any rights, liabilities, margin obligations, or pay-in/pay-out obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enables members to fulfill regulatory testing requirements pursuant to SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 dated November 24, 2020.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members utilizing third-party trading platforms or in-house systems via IML/ETI APIs should test trading functionalities and risk-management controls.
- Members are requested to share feedback on the mock session via contact numbers or email by 5:30 pm.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 10, 2026
- Log-in: 10:15 am to 11:00 am
- Continuous Trading (PR): 11:15 am to 02:00 pm
- Continuous Trading (DR): 03:00 pm to 04:45 pm

## Impact Assessment

- Purely operational and technical exercise for system verification and vendor testing with zero financial impact.
