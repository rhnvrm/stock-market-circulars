## Summary

Antala Industries Limited has submitted its Draft Offer Document to the BSE under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations for its upcoming SME IPO. The document is hosted on the BSE SME website for a minimum period of 21 days to invite public comments.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Antala Industries Limited
- **Filing Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Segment:** SME
- **Purpose:** Seeking public comments on the Draft Offer Document for at least 21 days
- **Feedback Email:** equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public may review the draft document and submit comments within 21 days of the notice.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Comment Submission Deadline:** Within 21 days from the notice date

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency into the upcoming SME public issue and allows stakeholders an opportunity to provide feedback.
