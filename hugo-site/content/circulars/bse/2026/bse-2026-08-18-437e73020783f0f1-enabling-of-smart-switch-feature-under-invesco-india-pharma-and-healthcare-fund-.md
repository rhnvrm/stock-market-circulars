## Summary

BSE has announced the enablement of the SMART Switch feature for the Invesco India Pharma and Healthcare Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform. The feature is available from August 18, 2026, until August 28, 2026, supporting lump sum subscriptions in non-Demat mode.

## Key Points

- SMART Switch feature available from August 18, 2026, to August 28, 2026 (up to 1:00 PM).
- Available through User Interface, Bulk Upload, and APIs.
- Applicable only for lump sum subscriptions in non-Demat mode for eligible Invesco India Mutual Fund schemes.
- Switch out processed on a FIFO basis for newly purchased units under the NFO SMART Switch plan.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must use appropriate scheme credentials strictly in accordance with investor requirements and ensure transactions are submitted before the stipulated cut-off time.

## Important Dates

- Feature Start Date: August 18, 2026
- Feature End / Cut-off Date: August 28, 2026 (up to 1:00 PM)
- RTA Reporting Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless switches for investors participating in the Invesco India Pharma and Healthcare Fund NFO through eligible existing holdings.
