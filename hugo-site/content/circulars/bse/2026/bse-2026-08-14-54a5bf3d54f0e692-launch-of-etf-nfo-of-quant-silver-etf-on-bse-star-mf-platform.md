## Summary

Quant Mutual Fund is launching the NFO for QUANT SILVER ETF and QUANT SILVER ETF - DIRECT PLAN on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 17, 2026, to August 19, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: QUANT SILVER ETF and QUANT SILVER ETF - DIRECT PLAN under the Equity category.
- NFO Period: August 17, 2026, to August 19, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 20, 2026.
- Lumpsum purchases for the NFO remain extended till 6:00 PM on August 19, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on the T+1 day will be cancelled, and the CC with 'N' flag will be reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 19, 2026, for allotment eligibility.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: August 17, 2026
- NFO End Date: August 19, 2026 (extended till 6:00 PM for lumpsum purchases)
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: August 19, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors an opportunity to subscribe to a new silver ETF scheme via the BSE StAR MF platform with clear operational guidelines for NFO transaction processing.
