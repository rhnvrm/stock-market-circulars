## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 21,50,000 equity shares of Virinchi Limited (Scrip Code - 532372) effective from September 29, 2026. These shares were issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Virinchi Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 532372
- **ISIN:** INE539B01017
- **Security Details:** 21,50,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 15/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 25/-).
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to March 31, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Ensure adherence to lock-in requirements up to March 31, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **Listing Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry:** March 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Increases the total paid-up equity capital of Virinchi Limited through preferential allotment upon warrant conversion.
