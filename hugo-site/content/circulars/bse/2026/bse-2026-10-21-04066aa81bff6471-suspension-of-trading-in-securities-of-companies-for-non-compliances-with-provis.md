## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in securities of four listed companies effective November 23, 2026, due to continued non-compliance with Regulation 76 of the SEBI (Depositories & Participants) Regulations, 2018 (submission of Reconciliation of Share Capital Audit Report) for two consecutive quarters (March 2026 and June 2026).

## Key Points

- **Effective Date of Suspension:** November 23, 2026.
- **Compliance Deadline:** Companies can avoid suspension if they comply to the satisfaction of the Exchange on or before November 18, 2026.
- **Promoter Shareholding:** Entire shareholding of promoters and all other securities held in promoters' demat accounts will remain frozen during the suspension period.
- **Post-Suspension Trading:** 15 days after suspension, trading will be allowed on a Trade for Trade basis in the Z group only on the first trading day of every week for six months.
- **Affected Companies:** CMX Holdings Ltd (532217), Mangalam Industrial Finance Ltd (537800), Vikalp Securities Ltd (531334), and Wardwizard Innovations & Mobility Ltd (538970).

## Regulatory Changes

Pursuant to SEBI Master Circular dated July 11, 2023 (last updated January 30, 2026), standard operating procedures for penal actions, suspension, and revocation of trading are enforced against listed entities failing to comply with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 and related depository regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Non-compliant listed entities must submit the Reconciliation of Share Capital Audit Report under Regulation 76 of SEBI (Depositories & Participants) Regulations, 2018 for the specified quarters.
- Compliance must be achieved and fines paid to the satisfaction of the Exchange on or before November 18, 2026, to prevent trading suspension.
- Revocation of suspension is subject to full compliance with procedures and extant norms.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Compliance Deadline:** November 18, 2026
- **Suspension Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares in the affected companies will face liquidity constraints and trading restrictions. Promoters will experience a complete freeze on their shareholdings and demat securities during the suspension period. Companies failing to regularize compliance risk entering the Z group trading segment.
