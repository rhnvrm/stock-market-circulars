## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, September 12, 2026. The session will be conducted across both the Primary Site (PR) and the Disaster Recovery Site (DR).

## Key Points

- **Date:** Saturday, September 12, 2026.
- **Segment:** Equity Derivatives.
- **Sites:** Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR).
- **Purpose:** Allows trading members using third-party trading platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs to test functionalities such as risk-reduction mode, trading halts, and switchover procedures.
- **No BOLT TWS Release:** There shall be no new BOLT TWS Release for this session.
- **Mock Nature:** Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or create any rights and liabilities.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to actively participate in the mock trading sessions and provide feedback by 4:00 PM on the day of the mock trading session via the provided contact numbers or email.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** September 12, 2026
- **Log-in Time:** 09:15 am - 10:00 am (PR)
- **Continuous Trading (PR):** 10:15 am - 12:00 pm
- **Switchover to (DR):** 12:00 pm - 12:45 pm
- **Continuous Trading (DR):** 01:00 pm - 02:55 pm

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal and restricted to testing and familiarization for brokers and trading members.
