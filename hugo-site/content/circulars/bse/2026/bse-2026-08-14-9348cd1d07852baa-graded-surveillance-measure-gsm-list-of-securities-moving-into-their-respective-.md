## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from August 17, 2026. Trading members are required to take adequate precautions as these securities will attract specific surveillance actions.

## Key Points

- Effective date of implementation: August 17, 2026.
- Securities listed in the attached Annexure shall move to higher stages of GSM.
- Settlement for these securities shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Applicable surveillance actions based on GSM stages:
- **Stage I**: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% and price band of 5% or lower as applicable.
- **Stage II**: Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower as applicable and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value to be deposited by the buyers.
- **Stage III**: Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower as applicable, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week), and ASD of 100% of trade value to be deposited by the buyers.
- **Stage IV**: Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower as applicable, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week), and ASD of 100% of trade value to be deposited by the buyers with no upward movement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, ensuring adherence to the mandatory margin requirements, ASD deposits, and trade-to-trade settlement rules.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: August 14, 2026
- **Effective Date**: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding or trading securities moving into higher GSM stages will experience increased liquidity constraints, mandatory higher margin/deposit commitments, and restricted trading frequencies (for Stages III and IV).
