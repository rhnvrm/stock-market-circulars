## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in four mutual fund schemes of SBI Mutual Fund due to their maturity, effective October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes: SBI Fixed Maturity Plan (FMP) - Series 51 (1846 Days) - Regular and Direct Plans (Growth & IDCW Payout).
- Scrip Codes: 543359, 543360, 543361, and 543362.
- ISINs: INF200KA1Z80, INF200KA1Z98, INF200KA10A7, and INF200KA11A5.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in these units with effect from October 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no further trading or dealings take place in the specified SBI Mutual Fund schemes starting October 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure following the maturity of closed-ended mutual fund schemes.
