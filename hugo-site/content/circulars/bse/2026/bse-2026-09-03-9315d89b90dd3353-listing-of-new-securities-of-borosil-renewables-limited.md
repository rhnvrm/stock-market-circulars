## Summary

Borosil Renewables Limited has announced the listing of 3,09,057 new equity shares issued at a premium upon the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis to non-promoters. These shares are permitted to trade on the Exchange effective from September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 502219
- Company Name: Borosil Renewables Limited
- Number of Shares: 3,09,057 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 530/- per share (including a premium of Rs. 529/-)
- Allotment Date: 15/07/2026
- ISIN: INE666D01022
- Distribution Nos.: 145245464 to 145554520
- Lock-in Upto: 08/03/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company and are subject to lock-in requirements as specified.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Trading: September 4, 2026
- Allotment Date: July 15, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: March 8, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion via warrant conversion with minimal immediate market impact.
