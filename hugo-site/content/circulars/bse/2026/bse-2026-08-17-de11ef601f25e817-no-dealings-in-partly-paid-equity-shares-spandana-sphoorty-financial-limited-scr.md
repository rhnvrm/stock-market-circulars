## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members that Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited has fixed a record date for determining holders of partly paid-up equity shares to whom the First and Final Call notice will be sent. Consequently, trading members are advised not to deal in the partly paid-up equity shares of the company starting from the specified record date.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 890221
- **Settlement No.:** DR- 697/2026-2027
- **Record Date / No Dealings From Date:** 19/08/2026
- **Purpose:** To ascertain shareholders of partly paid shares eligible to pay the First and Final call of Rs. 115/- per share (comprising Rs. 5.00 towards face value and Rs. 110/- towards premium).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action procedure for partly paid shares call money.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are required to take note and refrain from dealing in the partly paid-up equity shares of the company from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date / No Dealings From:** 19/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the partly paid-up equity shares (Scrip Code: 890221) of Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited is restricted from the record date to facilitate the First and Final Call process.
