## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. As per the revised guidelines, 100% margins, lower price bands, and group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS Group) are imposed on specified securities, while certain securities are moved to higher stages or removed from the framework entirely.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable effective September 18, 2026, on open positions as of September 17, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- List of securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM framework and lower price band applicable effective September 16, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group effective September 18, 2026.
- Review of securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective September 16, 2026, with price bands reinstated to previous levels subject to other surveillance measures.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided as Annexures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of stringent surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements and lower price bands under the LT-ASM framework.
- Transfer of specific securities to trade-for-trade segments (T / XT / MT / TS Group).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on applicable open and new positions.
- Members and listed entities should verify the respective Annexures for security-specific status updates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Band / Stage Revisions / Removal: September 16, 2016
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened margin requirements and potential transfer to trade-for-trade groups may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
