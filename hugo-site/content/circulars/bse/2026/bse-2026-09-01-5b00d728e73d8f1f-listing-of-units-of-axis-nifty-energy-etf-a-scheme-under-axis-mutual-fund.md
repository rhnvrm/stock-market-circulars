## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and admission to dealings of units of Axis Nifty Energy ETF under Axis Mutual Fund, effective from Wednesday, September 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Axis Nifty Energy ETF
- **Mutual Fund:** Axis Mutual Fund
- **Investment Manager:** Axis Asset Management Company Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544892
- **ISIN:** INF846KA1598
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** ENERGYAXIS
- **Group:** B
- **No. of Units:** 3,581,880 Units
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00 per unit
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 19.06 per unit
- **Date of Allotment:** 27/08/2026
- **Registrar and Transfer Agent:** KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, units of the mutual fund will only be traded in dematerialized form in unit market lots (one unit).

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 27, 2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** September 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with liquidity and trading access to the newly launched Axis Nifty Energy ETF on the BSE exchange platform.
