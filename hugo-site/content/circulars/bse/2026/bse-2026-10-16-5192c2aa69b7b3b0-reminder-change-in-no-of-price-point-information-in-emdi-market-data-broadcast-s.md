## Summary

BSE has issued a reminder and revised schedule regarding the change in the number of price points disseminated through the EMDI market data broadcast stream in the Equity segment. Originally communicated via Notice No. 20260928-39, the implementation was delayed due to technical reasons and is now rescheduled to go live on October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Go-live date for the EMDI price point change is rescheduled to 19 October 2026.
- Mock trading is scheduled for 17 October 2026 for system validation.
- The EMDI multicast IP and port for the Equity segment remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational update regarding market data broadcast formats.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and empanelled vendors must participate in mock trading on 17 October 2026 and ensure their systems are suitably prepared for the updated broadcast stream format by 19 October 2026.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: 17 October 2026
- Go-Live Date: 19 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and vendors utilizing the EMDI market data stream must update their systems to handle the revised price point information structure to avoid technical disruptions.
