## Summary

Chandni Machines Ltd has announced a sub-division of its existing equity shares, changing the face value from Rs. 10/- each to Re. 1/- each, with October 29, 2026 fixed as the record date.

## Key Points

- Company: Chandni Machines Ltd (Scrip Code: 542627)
- Sub-Division Ratio: 1 equity share of Rs. 10/- into 10 equity shares of Re. 1/-
- Record Date: 29/10/2026
- Old ISIN: INE01GZ01011 (invalid for transactions on or after 29/10/2026)

## Regulatory Changes

- New ISIN number for Re. 1/- paid up shares will be communicated via a separate notice by the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update records and ensure compliance with the new ISIN and share structure from the record date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / Effective Date: 29/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Existing shareholders will receive 10 shares of Re. 1/- for every 1 share of Rs. 10/- held as of the record date, enhancing liquidity while keeping total capital value constant.
