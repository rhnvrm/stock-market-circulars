## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities issued by Keertana Finserv Limited on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Keertana Finserv Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 978167
- **Scrip ID:** 1170KFL28
- **ISIN Number:** INE0NES07352
- **Issue Details:** 5,000,000 privately placed debt instruments with a face value and paid-up value of Rs. 10,000 each.
- **Coupon / Rate of Interest:** 11.70% p.a., payable monthly from 16/10/2026 to 16/12/2028.
- **Credit Rating:** ACUITE A-
- **Date of Allotment:** 16/09/2026
- **Date of Redemption:** 16/12/2028

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialized form under the specified ISIN number. Tick size for the securities is 1 paise.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 16/09/2026
- **Listing / Effective Date:** 18/09/2026
- **First Interest Payment Date:** 16/10/2026
- **Date of Redemption:** 16/12/2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice regarding debt market segment listings with minimal impact on broader market liquidity.
