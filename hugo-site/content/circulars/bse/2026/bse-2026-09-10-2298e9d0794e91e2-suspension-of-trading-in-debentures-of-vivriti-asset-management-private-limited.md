## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific non-convertible debentures (NCDs) of Vivriti Asset Management Private Limited following the fixation of a record date for early redemption on the exercise of a call option.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vivriti Asset Management Private Limited
- Security Details: VAMPL-11%-15-1-27-PVT, ISIN: INE0BXI07010, Scrip Code: 975094 (DR- 714/2026-2027)
- Purpose: Early Redemption of NCD on Exercise of Call Option
- Record Date: 14/09/2026
- No Dealings From: 11/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures of the company starting from the effective date of suspension.

## Important Dates

- No Dealings From: 11/09/2026
- Record Date: 14/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors holding the affected NCDs must take note of the suspension and cease trading in the specified securities from the effective date.
