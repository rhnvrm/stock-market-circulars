## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Shree Balaji (Mala) Textiles Limited (Scrip Code: 544835) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective from August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shree Balaji (Mala) Textiles Limited
- Scrip Code: 544835
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Wednesday, August 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The trading category of the company's equity shares has been shifted following continuation of Exchange's earlier notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the segment shift for executing trades in the company's equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 29, 2026
- Effective Date: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Allows regular rolling settlement for the equity shares of Shree Balaji (Mala) Textiles Limited, improving trading liquidity.
