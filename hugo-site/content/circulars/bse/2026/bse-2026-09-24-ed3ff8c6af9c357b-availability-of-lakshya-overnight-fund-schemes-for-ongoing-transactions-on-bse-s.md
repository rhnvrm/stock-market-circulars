## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that pursuant to prior notices, the LAKSHYA OVERNIGHT FUND will reopen for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes affected: LAKSHYA OVERNIGHT FUND - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH and LAKSHYA OVERNIGHT FUND - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational availability notice for mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can process ongoing subscriptions and redemptions for the specified ISINs through the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date for Transactions: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to resume regular subscription and redemption transactions in the specified overnight fund schemes on the BSE platform without restriction.
