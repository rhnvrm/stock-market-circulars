## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund has announced the reopening of the UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Index Fund for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and associated facilities (SIP, STP, and SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund Name: UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Index Fund
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- Eligible Plans: Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AH59) and Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AH67)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure correct ISIN mapping while placing transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 21, 2026
- Reopening Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume seamless transactions and systematic investment plans for the specified index fund units on the BSE platform.
