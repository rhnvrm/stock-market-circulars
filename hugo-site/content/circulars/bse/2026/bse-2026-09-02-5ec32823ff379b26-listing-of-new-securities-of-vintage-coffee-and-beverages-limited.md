## Summary

Vintage Coffee and Beverages Limited has announced the listing of 8,00,000 new equity shares issued on a preferential basis to promoters following the conversion of warrants, effective from September 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 538920
- Number of Shares: 8,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 114/- (Issue Price: Rs. 124.00/-)
- Allottee: Promoter
- Purpose: Conversion of warrants
- ISIN: INE498Q01014
- Lock-in Details: 8,00,000 shares locked-in upto 15/03/2028 (Distinctive Nos. 145688410 to 146488409)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed about the permitted trade of the new securities ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 07/07/2026
- Effective Date of Listing: 03/09/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 15/03/2028

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion via warrant conversion with standard promoter lock-in requirements.
