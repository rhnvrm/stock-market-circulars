## Summary

BSE has announced the schedule for the special Muhurat Trading Session for the Equity Segment on account of Diwali Laxmi Pujan, scheduled to be held on Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Special Muhurat Trading Session scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026.
- Log-in session timings: 01:15 pm to 01:50 pm.
- Block Deal Window: 01:50 pm to 02:05 pm.
- Continuous Trading session: 02:20 pm to 03:30 pm.
- Trade Modification allowed between 02:20 pm and 04:15 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

No structural regulatory changes; official scheduling of festival trading hours.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to note the specific session timings for order entry, matching, closing, and post-closing activities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Muhurat Trading Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides clear operational guidelines for market participants during the festive Muhurat trading session.
