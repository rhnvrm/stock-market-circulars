## Summary

BSE has issued a settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Neelkanth Rock-Minerals Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from August 18, 2026, to September 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Takeover
- Settlement No.: 2026103
- Bid Start Date: 18/08/2026
- Bid End Date: 01/09/2026
- Obligation Download Date: 09/09/2026
- Settlement Date: 10/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories.
- Members and custodians must use Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement number 2026103.
- Early pay-in securities will be earmarked/blocked in the client's account; members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members must refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: August 18, 2026
- Bid End Date: September 1, 2026
- Obligation Download Date: September 9, 2026
- Settlement Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement and operational processing for market participants and custodians engaging in the takeover acquisition window for Neelkanth Rock-Minerals Limited.
