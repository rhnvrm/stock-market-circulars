## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released the client margin reporting schedule and associated penalty dates for the Currency Derivatives segment for the month of October 2026.

## Key Points

- Details trade dates, last dates of margin reporting, and penalty dates for October 2026.
- Applicable to trading members and clearing members operating in the Currency Derivatives segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine operational notice setting out monthly margin reporting timelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must complete client margin reporting by the prescribed last dates for each corresponding trade date in October 2026 to avoid penalties.

## Important Dates

- Trade dates span from 01-10-2026 through 30-10-2026, with margin reporting and penalty dates extending into November 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures operational clarity and compliance discipline for margin reporting in the currency derivatives market.
