## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 1,20,00,000 equity shares of Kridhan Infra Limited resulting from the conversion of warrants issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis, effective from October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 1,20,00,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each issued at a price of Rs. 4.05/- (including premium of Rs. 2.05/-).
- **Allotment & Issuance:** Issued to Non-Promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.
- **Effective Date:** Trading permitted with effect from October 14, 2026.
- **Identification:** Scrip Code: 533482, ISIN: INE524L01026.
- **Lock-in Details:** All 1,20,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 99579206 to 111579205) are locked in up to May 31, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational listing notice under standard stock exchange procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with trading permissions and lock-in restrictions specified for the scrip.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** May 14, 2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Kridhan Infra Limited. The shares are locked in until May 2027, mitigating immediate circulating supply impact.
