## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has published a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in various scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the concerned companies. Replies received from several companies are attached to the notice, while responses from a few others remain awaited.

## Key Points

- **Objective:** Ensure transparency and timely availability of relevant information to trading members and investors following significant price/volume movements.
- **Companies with Replies Received:** 
  1. AKI India Ltd (542020)
  2. Atal Realtech Ltd (543911)
  3. AYM Syntex Ltd (508933)
  4. Cyber Media India Ltd (532640)
  5. Infobeans Technologies Ltd (543644)
  6. Jain Irrigation Systems Ltd (500219)
  7. Manali Petrochemicals Ltd (500268)
  8. Puravankara Ltd (532891)
  9. PVP Ventures Ltd (517556)
  10. Rane (Madras Ltd) (532661)
  11. Shah Alloys Ltd (513436)
  12. Tamilnadu Petroproducts Ltd (500777)
- **Companies with Replies Awaited:** 
  1. Billwin Industries Ltd (543209)
  2. CMX Holdings Ltd (532217)
  3. Deep Polymers Ltd (541778)
  4. Mahesh Developers Ltd (542677)
  5. Mukta Agriculture Ltd (535204)
  6. Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Ltd (544510)
  7. Sera Investments & Finance India Ltd (512399)

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework changes; standard surveillance procedure under stock exchange guidelines for price/volume clarification.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to promptly clarify any material information, price sensitive announcements, or reasons for unusual market movements upon receiving queries from the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 07 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and aids investors in making informed decisions by making company-submitted clarifications publicly available.
