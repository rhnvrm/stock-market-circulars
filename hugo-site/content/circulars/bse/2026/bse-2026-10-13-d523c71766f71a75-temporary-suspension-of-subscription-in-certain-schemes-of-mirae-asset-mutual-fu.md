## Summary

BSE has announced the temporary suspension of fresh lump-sum investments, switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in) under certain schemes of Mirae Asset Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from the close of business on October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh lump-sum investments, additional purchases, switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP, STP-in) are disabled for specified Mirae Asset Mutual Fund schemes.
- Applications received after the applicable cut-off time on October 09, 2026, are not accepted or processed.
- Existing SIP and STP-in registrations made prior to the effective date continue to be processed normally by the AMC and RTA.
- Redemption, switch-out, STP-out, and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) transactions remain fully permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of trading and subscription restrictions on specific mutual fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with communication received from Mirae Asset Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of these operational changes while executing transactions and refrain from submitting fresh purchase or new systematic registration requests for the affected schemes.

## Important Dates

- Cut-off for applications: October 09, 2026
- Suspension effective date: Close of business on October 12, 2026
- Notice date: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and members are unable to initiate fresh purchases or new SIP/STP registrations in the impacted Mirae Asset schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform. However, existing systematic investments and exit options such as redemptions and switch-outs remain unaffected.
