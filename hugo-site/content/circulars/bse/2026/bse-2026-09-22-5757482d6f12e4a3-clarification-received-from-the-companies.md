## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movements in certain scrips in the recent past and sought clarification from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information. Replies have been received from several companies, while responses from others remain awaited.

## Key Points

- **Source:** BSE Surveillance Department
- **Action:** Clarifications sought and received regarding recent price/volume volatility in specific scrips.
- **Companies with Replies Received:** Alacrity Securities Ltd, Annvrridhhi Ventures Ltd, Asian Star Company Ltd, Betala Global Securities Ltd, Bhageria Industries Ltd, Glass Wall Systems (India) Ltd, Kopran Ltd, KPI Green Energy Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Studds Accessories Ltd, Tarsons Products Ltd.
- **Companies with Replies Awaited:** CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Jhandewalas Foods Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Mopshop Distribution Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to submit clarifications to the Exchange regarding significant price and volume movements upon request to ensure market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 22 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard surveillance procedure intended to maintain market integrity and investor awareness without immediate direct financial impact.
