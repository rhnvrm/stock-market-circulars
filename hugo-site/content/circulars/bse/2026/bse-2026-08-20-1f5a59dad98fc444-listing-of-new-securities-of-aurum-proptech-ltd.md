## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that 39,775 new equity shares of Aurum PropTech Ltd (Scrip Code: 539289) are permitted to trade with effect from Friday, August 21, 2026, pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aurum PropTech Ltd
- Scrip Code: 539289
- ISIN: INE898S01029
- Securities: 39,775 Equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Rs. 5/- each issued on a rights basis.
- Effective Date: August 21, 2026
- These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can trade in the newly listed securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Trading: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact as this is a routine listing of shares following the conversion from partly paid to fully paid-up status.
