## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 21,55,557 new equity shares of TIL Ltd. (Scrip Code: 505196) issued on a rights basis are permitted to trade with effect from Monday, August 17, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TIL Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 505196
- ISIN: INE806C01018
- Securities: 21,55,557 Equity shares of Rs.10/- each pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up.
- Distinctive Numbers: 1,51,782 (80091403 to 80243184) and 20,03,775 (80260685 to 82264459).
- Effective Date: Monday, August 17, 2026.
- Ranking: These shares are pari-passu with the old Equity Shares of the Company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital structure adjustment for TIL Ltd. with no broader market impact.
