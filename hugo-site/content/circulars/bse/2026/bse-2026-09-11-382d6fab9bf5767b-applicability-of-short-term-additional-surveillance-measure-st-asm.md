## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for various securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective from September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II are subjected to enhanced margin requirements.
- Applicable margin for Stage I is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Applicable margin for Stage II is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Measures apply to all open positions as on September 15, 2026, and new positions created from September 16, 2026.
- Shortlisting is strictly a market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance actions under the revised ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% to 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 16, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations for market participants trading in securities placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II, which may impact liquidity and volatility for the affected scrips.
