## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the allocation of equity shares under the anchor investors portion in the public issue of Runwal Enterprises Limited. A total of 48,83,605 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 305/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Runwal Enterprises Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue / IPO (Anchor Investor Allocation)
- Shares Subscribed: 48,83,605 equity shares
- Allocation Price: Rs. 305/- per equity share
- Date of Announcement: September 24, 2026
