## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 20 companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS), effective from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading members are informed that new equity shares issued under ESOP/ESOS by 20 companies are admitted for trading effective September 24, 2026.
- Companies include 360 ONE WAM Limited, Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Limited, Federal Bank Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, HDFC Asset Management Company Limited, and others.
- All listed securities are available for regular trading with lock-in status specified as 'NA' for the listed tranches.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the new securities admitted for trading.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 23 September 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: 24 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase resulting from ESOP exercises across multiple listed entities with minimal immediate market impact.
