## Summary

BSE has announced the inclusion of two newly listed companies, Technocraft Ventures Limited and LEAP India Limited, into the BSE IPO Index effective from the open of trading on Tuesday, August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Technocraft Ventures Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544864) will be added to the BSE IPO Index.
- LEAP India Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544865) will be added to the BSE IPO Index.
- The additions are effective starting August 18, 2026.
- The corporate actions are included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None specific for market participants; informational notice regarding index composition.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Inclusion in the BSE IPO Index may increase visibility and passive tracking interest for the newly listed stocks among index funds and institutional investors.
