## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Wednesday, September 16, 2026, the equity shares of Kanohar Electricals Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: Kanohar Electricals Limited
- Scrip Code: 544911
- Scrip ID: KANOHAR
- ISIN: INE877D01025
- Number of Securities: 79,186,835 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 632/- per share (Face Value Rs. 2/- and Premium Rs. 630/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 11, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

a) Securities of the company will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
b) The scrip will be included in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Wednesday, September 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 11, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Kanohar Electricals Limited equity shares on the BSE platform.
