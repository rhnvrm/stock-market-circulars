## Summary

Choice Mutual Fund is launching the Choice Overnight Fund NFO along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from July 01, 2026, to July 03, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of July 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Choice Mutual Fund
- Scheme Offered: Choice Overnight Fund (Regular and Direct plans, Growth and Daily IDCW Reinvestment options)
- NFO Period: July 01, 2026, to July 03, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: July 04, 2026
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on July 03, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on July 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: 01-Jul-2026
- NFO Closure Date: 03-Jul-2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: 03-Jul-2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: 04-Jul-2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to subscribe to the new overnight fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform with standard NFO processing rules.
