## Summary

BSE has announced financial disincentives for trading members who placed orders from unregistered or incorrectly registered TWS/ETI/BOLTPLUS terminal locations during the month of August 2026. A penalty of ₹1,000 per instance applies to such locations.

## Key Points

- Financial disincentive file for August 2026 (filename LCS0826.xxxx) has been downloaded to the Equity Transaction Folder in Extranet.
- Penalty amount is ₹1,000/- per instance for orders placed from unregistered or incorrectly registered terminal locations.
- Debits to the General Charges Account will occur upon completion of 60 days from the date of the notice.
- Trading members seeking a waiver or review must follow the guidelines specified in prior Exchange Notice No. 20251008-67.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of terminal location registration requirements and application of financial disincentives for non-compliance as per previous notices (including Notice No. 20260417-45).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure strict compliance by uploading TWS/ETI/BOLTPLUS terminal location details before punching orders.
- Waiver or review requests must be emailed to Waiver-Review@bseindia.com with the subject line "Waiver / review of Financial Disincentive_<TM Name>_<Clg No.>_LOCATION MISMATCH- AUGUST -2026" within the stipulated time frame and including required processing fees.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10 September 2026
- Period Covered: August 2026
- Debit Date: Upon completion of 60 days from the date of the notice

## Impact Assessment

Trading members with unregistered or incorrectly registered terminal locations will face financial disincentives debited to their General Charges Account unless waivers are successfully processed.
