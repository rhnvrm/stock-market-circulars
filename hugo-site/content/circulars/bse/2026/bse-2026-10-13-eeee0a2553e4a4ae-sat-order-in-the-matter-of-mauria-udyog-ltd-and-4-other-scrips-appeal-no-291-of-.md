## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that Kanungo Financiers Limited has paid the full penalty amount in favour of SEBI, pursuant to the directions of the Hon'ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated September 07, 2026, in Appeal No. 291 of 2026 regarding Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips.

## Key Points

- In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260701-4 dated July 01, 2026.
- SAT vide order dated September 07, 2026, granted a stay of the impugned order subject to the deposit of the full penalty within four weeks in an interest-bearing account.
- SEBI confirmed via email on October 13, 2026, that Kanungo Financiers Limited has successfully paid the penalty amount in favour of SEBI.
- Trading members are advised to take note of the development and ensure necessary compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework changes; this notice serves as an update on appellate proceedings and compliance status regarding penalty deposits.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the compliance update regarding the penalty deposit and stay order.

## Important Dates

- **July 01, 2026**: Previous exchange notice issued.
- **September 07, 2026**: SAT order granting stay of the impugned order subject to penalty deposit.
- **October 13, 2026**: SEBI confirmation of penalty payment and issuance of BSE notice.

## Impact Assessment

The market and operational impact is low, being restricted to the specific noticee and scrips involved in the appellate proceedings.
