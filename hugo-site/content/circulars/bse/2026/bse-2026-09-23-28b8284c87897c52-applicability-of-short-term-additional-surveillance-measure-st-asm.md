## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The applicable surveillance actions, including enhanced margin rates, take effect from September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying specific criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- For ST-ASM Stage I, the applicable rate of margin is 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- For ST-ASM Stage II, the applicable rate of margin is 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on September 24, 2026, and new positions created from September 25, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance actions with enhanced margin requirements in conjunction with existing exchange measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities as per the Annexure.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026
- Applicable on Open Positions as of: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for affected securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the shortlisted scrips.
