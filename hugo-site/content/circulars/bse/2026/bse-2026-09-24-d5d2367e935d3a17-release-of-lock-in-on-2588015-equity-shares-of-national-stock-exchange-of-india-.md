## Summary

BSE has issued a notice (Notice No. 20260924-40) dated September 24, 2026, announcing the release of lock-in on 25,88,015 equity shares of National Stock Exchange of India Limited (Scrip Code: 544937) held by Faering Capital India Evolving Fund, effective from the date of the notice.

## Key Points

- **Holder:** Faering Capital India Evolving Fund
- **Company:** National Stock Exchange of India Limited (Scrip Code: 544937)
- **Shares Released:** 25,88,015 Equity Shares
- **Reason for Lock-in Release:** The lock-in was inadvertently triggered because the holder's demat account was incorrectly classified as a "Corporate" account instead of an Alternative Investment Fund (Category I) account.
- **Regulatory Exemption:** Under applicable SEBI Regulations, Category I Alternative Investment Funds are exempt from such lock-in requirements.

## Regulatory Changes

- Correction of classification error under SEBI Regulations exempting Category I AIFs from specific lock-in mandates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants may take note of the cessation of lock-in on the specified equity shares.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date / Effective Date:** September 24, 2026
- **In Continuation Of:** Notice No. 20260923-39 dated September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal direct market impact; administrative correction restoring proper Category I AIF exemption status for the specified shareholding.
