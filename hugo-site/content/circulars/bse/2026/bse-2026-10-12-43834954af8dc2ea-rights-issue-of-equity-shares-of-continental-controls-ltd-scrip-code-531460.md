## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Continental Controls Ltd. has fixed October 14, 2026, as the record date for its upcoming rights issue. Eligible shareholders will receive 16 equity shares for every 5 equity shares held at par value of Rs. 10/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Continental Controls Ltd. (Scrip Code: 531460)
- Corporate Action: Rights Issue of Equity Shares
- Issue Ratio: 16 equity shares for every 5 equity shares held
- Issue Price: Rs. 10/- each (payable fully on application at par)
- Record Date: October 14, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: October 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions in equity shares are conducted on an ex-rights basis starting from October 14, 2026 (Settlement No. DR- 735/2026-2027).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Record Date: October 14, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders on the record date receive rights entitlements, altering equity capital structure and requiring trading members to adjust settlement operations accordingly.
