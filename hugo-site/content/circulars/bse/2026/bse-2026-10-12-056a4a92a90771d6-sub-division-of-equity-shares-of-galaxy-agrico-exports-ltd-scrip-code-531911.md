## Summary

Galaxy Agrico Exports Ltd has informed the Exchange regarding the sub-division of its equity shares. The face value of the equity shares is being subdivided from Rs. 10/- each into ten equity shares of Re. 1/- each, with the record date fixed for October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Galaxy Agrico Exports Ltd (Scrip Code: 531911)
- Corporate Action: Sub-division / stock split of equity shares
- Ratio: 1 existing equity share of Rs. 10/- into 10 equity shares of Re. 1/-
- Record Date: 30/10/2026
- Old ISIN (INE803L01016) of Rs. 10/- paid up will not be valid for transactions on or after 30/10/2026.
- New ISIN for Re. 1/- paid up will be communicated via a separate notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the change in ISIN and face value effective from the record date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 30/10/2026
- Effective Date: 30/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Stock split increases the number of shares and improves retail accessibility and liquidity.
