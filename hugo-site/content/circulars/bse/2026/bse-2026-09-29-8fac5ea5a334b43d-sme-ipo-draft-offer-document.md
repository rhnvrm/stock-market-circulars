## Summary

Smart Roof Solar Solutions Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with the BSE under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations for its proposed SME Initial Public Offering (IPO). The document is hosted on the SME website for public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Smart Roof Solar Solutions Limited
- Filing Date: September 25, 2026
- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Public Comments Window: 21 days from the date of notice
- Submission Email for Comments: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

- No direct regulatory changes; compliance with ICDR Regulations Schedule VI requirements for SME IPO draft offer documents.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public and stakeholders are invited to review the Draft Offer Document and submit any comments within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from the notice date

## Impact Assessment

- Provides transparency and public participation opportunity for the upcoming SME IPO of Smart Roof Solar Solutions Limited.
