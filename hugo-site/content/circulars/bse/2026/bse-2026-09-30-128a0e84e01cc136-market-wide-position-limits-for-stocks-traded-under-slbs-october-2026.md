## Summary

BSE has announced the stock-wise Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) and position limits applicable to Clearing Members, Institutional Investors, and Clients under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for the month of October 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Specifies stock-wise Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) for October 2026 under SLBS.
- Details position limits for Clearing Members (CM), Institutional Investors, and Clients.
- Issued pursuant to SEBI Circular No. MRD/DoP/SE/Dep/Cir-14/2007 dated December 20, 2007.
- Detailed list of limits provided in Annexure I.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a routine periodic notice specifying monthly position limits under the existing framework ofeliz the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS).

## Compliance Requirements

Clearing members, institutional investors, and trading participants must adhere to the specified MWPL and position limits when executing transactions in the SLB segment for October 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 September 2026
- Effective Period: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly trading, risk management, and compliance with position limits in the Securities Lending and Borrowing segment during October 2026.
