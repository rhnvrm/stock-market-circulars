## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, September 12, 2026, utilizing both primary (PR) and disaster recovery (DR) sites. Trading members can test their applications, including exceptional market conditions and APIs.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, September 12, 2026.
- Covers Commodity Derivatives segment (PR & DR sites).
- Log-in time: 09:15 am to 10:00 am.
- Continuous Trading (PR): 10:00 am to 12:00 pm.
- Switchover to (DR): 12:00 pm to 12:45 pm.
- Continuous Trading (DR): 12:45 pm to 02:45 pm.
- Trades resulting from mock trading do not create any actual margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out, or rights and liabilities.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are requested to actively participate and provide feedback by 4:00 pm on the mock trading session via email or contact numbers provided.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables brokers and algorithmic traders to verify system readiness and connectivity via ETI APIs without financial risk.
