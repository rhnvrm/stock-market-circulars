## Summary

The Exchange has observed significant price and volume movement in certain scrips in the recent past. To ensure that trading members and investors have access to the latest relevant information, BSE sought clarifications from the concerned companies.

## Key Points

- Replies received and attached from:
  1. Caplin Point Laboratories Ltd (Scrip Code: 524742)
  2. Sattrix Information Security Ltd (Scrip Code: 544189)
  3. Umiya Mobile Ltd (Scrip Code: 544464)
- Replies are awaited from 12 companies including Billwin Industries Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Jhandewalas Foods Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Manugraph India Ltd, Mopshop Distribution Ltd, S. M. Gold Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, Vikram Aroma Ltd, MRC Agrotech Ltd, Dc Infotech and Communication Ltd, and Vellora Impact Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine market surveillance and disclosure enforcement action.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed scrips exhibiting unusual price or volume volatility are required to provide timely clarifications to the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 25 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Promotes investor protection and market transparency through timely disclosure of clarifications regarding unusual stock price and volume movements.
