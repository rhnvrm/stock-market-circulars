## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 30,00,000 new equity shares of Akme Fintrade (India) Limited (Scrip Code: 544200) with effect from Tuesday, October 6, 2026. These shares were issued at a premium of Rs. 10.10/- (issue price Rs. 11.10/- per share of face value Re. 1/-) to non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Akme Fintrade (India) Limited
- Scrip Code: 544200
- ISIN: INE916Y01027
- Security Details: 30,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 10.10/-
- Allotment Date: 29/06/2026
- Distribution Nos.: 426749961 to 429749960
- Effective Date of Trading: October 6, 2026
- Lock-in Details: 30,00,000 shares under lock-in up to 15/04/2027

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the lock-in period and distribution numbers for the newly listed equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: June 29, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: October 6, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 15, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up equity capital base of Akme Fintrade (India) Limited following warrant conversion, with shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
