## Summary

BSE Ltd has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 15 scrips in the equity segment, effective from September 23, 2026, as part of its surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands for 15 scrips have been revised to new percentages ranging from 2% to 20%.
- The changes become effective starting trading hours on September 23, 2026.
- Affected scrips include Alkali Metals Ltd, Farm Peace Ltd, Precision Electronics Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of price band limits for specified scrips under BSE surveillance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance during order entry and execution.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and traders dealing in the mentioned scrips must adjust their trading strategies to account for the new price band limits.
