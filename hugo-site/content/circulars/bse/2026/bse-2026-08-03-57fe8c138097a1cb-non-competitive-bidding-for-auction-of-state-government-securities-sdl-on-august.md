## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for August 04, 2026, following the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) announcement.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding is conducted through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.
- Bid collection starts on August 03, 2026, at 10:00 AM.
- Bid collection end date for direct investors is August 03, 2026, till 5:00 PM, and for members, it is August 04, 2026, till 8:00 AM.
- The auction date is August 04, 2026, and the settlement date is August 05, 2026.
- Multiple re-issue and new SDL securities across various states are available for bidding.

## Regulatory Changes

- Facilitation of non-competitive bidding in State Government Securities as per RBI guidelines and Exchange circular no. 20191122-28.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure bids are submitted within the specified timelines through the iBBS NCB-GSec module.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: August 03, 2026 (10:00 AM)
- Direct Investor Bid End Date: August 03, 2026 (5:00 PM)
- Members Bid End Date: August 04, 2026 (8:00 AM)
- Auction Date: August 04, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides retail and eligible investors an opportunity to participate in State Government Securities auctions through non-competitive bidding.
