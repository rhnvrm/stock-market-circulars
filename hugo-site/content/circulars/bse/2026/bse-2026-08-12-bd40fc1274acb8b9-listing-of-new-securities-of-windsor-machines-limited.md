## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members that 1,04,24,811 new equity shares of Windsor Machines Limited (Scrip Code: 522029, Ticker: WINDMACHIN) have been listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange, effective from Thursday, August 13, 2026. These shares, with a face value of Rs. 2/- each, were issued at a premium of Rs. 189.85/- per share (total issue price of Rs. 191.85/- per share) on a preferential basis to non-promoter entities upon the conversion of warrants. The allotment of these shares was conducted on June 18, 2026. The new securities will rank pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company, and are subject to a lock-in period until February 28, 2027.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** Windsor Machines Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 522029 (BSE)
- **ISIN:** INE052A01021
- **Quantity:** 1,04,24,811 equity shares
- **Face Value:** Rs. 2/- per share
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 191.85/- per share (including a premium of Rs. 189.85/-)
- **Allotment Basis:** Preferential allotment to non-promoters pursuant to conversion of warrants
- **Allotment Date:** June 18, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 91302202 to 101727012
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked in until February 28, 2027 (Distinctive Nos: 91302202 to 101727012)
- **Ranking:** Pari-passu with the existing old equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes are introduced by this notice. The listing process follows established SEBI Guidelines on preferential allotments and warrant conversions.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Trading members are advised to take note of the listing and commencement of trading of these new shares starting Thursday, August 13, 2026.
- **Depository/RTA:** Ensure proper book-entry credits and maintain the lock-in status of the specified 1,04,24,811 shares (Distinctive Numbers 91302202 to 101727012) until the lock-in expiry date on February 28, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** June 18, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** February 28, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Windsor Machines Limited:** The conversion of warrants into equity shares raises approximately Rs. 200 crores (1,04,24,811 shares at Rs. 191.85 each), strengthening the company's balance sheet, supporting its funding needs, but also diluting the existing shareholding percent of other shareholders.
- **Market/Trading Impact:** There is no immediate impact on market operations or tradeable float, as the entire batch of 1,04,24,811 shares is subject to a strict lock-in until February 28, 2027. Once the lock-in period ends, the free float of WINDMACHIN in the equity segment will expand correspondingly.
