## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of fresh subscriptions and switch-in transactions, including fresh SIPs and STPs, in select schemes of Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective September 24, 2026. Subscriptions are subject to an overall limit of ₹1 Crore per PAN per day.

## Key Points

- Resumption applies to Aditya Birla Sun Life International Equity Fund, Aditya Birla Sun Life Global Emerging Opportunities Fund, and Aditya Birla Sun Life Global Excellence Equity Fund of Fund.
- Effective date: September 24, 2026.
- Overall transaction limit: ₹1 Crore per PAN per day across all modes of subscription (fresh purchases, additional purchases, SIPs, and STPs).
- Existing SIP and STP registrations continue to be processed in accordance with existing mandates.
- Redemption, switch-out, and STP-out transactions continue without any restrictions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of temporary suspension on fresh inflows for the specified international and global fund of fund schemes, replacing it with a daily per-PAN limit.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors must ensure that fresh purchases, additional purchases, fresh SIPs, and STP-in registrations do not exceed ₹1 Crore per PAN per day in the specified schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and members can resume investments in the specified Aditya Birla Sun Life schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform, subject to the stipulated daily cap per PAN.
