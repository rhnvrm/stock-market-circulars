## Summary

Ducon Infratechnologies Limited has fixed the record date for its upcoming rights issue of equity shares. Trading members are informed that transactions in the equity shares of the company will take place on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ducon Infratechnologies Limited
- Scrip Code: 534674
- Record Date: 25/08/2026
- Ex-Right Basis Date: 25/08/2026
- Rights Issue Details: 10 (ten) equity shares of Re. 1/- each for cash at par on rights basis for every 13 (thirteen) equity shares held.
- Application Payment: Full amount of Re. 1/- per equity share is payable on application.
- Settlement Number: DR- 701/2026-2027

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are requested to take note of the record date and ensure transactions are handled on an ex-rights basis effective from 25/08/2026.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 25/08/2026
- Ex-Rights Date: 25/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders as of the record date are eligible for the rights entitlement as specified, impacting the stock price adjustment on the ex-rights date.
