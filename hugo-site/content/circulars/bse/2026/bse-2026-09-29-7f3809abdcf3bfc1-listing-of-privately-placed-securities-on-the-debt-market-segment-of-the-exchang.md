## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities (Non-Convertible Debentures) issued by GIC Housing Finance Ltd. and Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, effective September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- GIC Housing Finance Ltd. has listed 10,000 NCDs (Scrip Code: 978219, ISIN: INE289B07156) carrying an interest rate of 8.15% p.a., maturing on 19/12/2029.
- Power Grid Corporation of India Limited has listed 200,000 NCDs (Scrip Code: 978220, ISIN: INE752E08817) carrying an interest rate of 7.74% p.a., maturing on 29/09/2036.
- Both issues are admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment in dematerialised form with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing notice for privately placed debt instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to dematerialised trading norms and ISIN specifications.

## Important Dates

- GIC Housing Finance Allotment Date: 28/09/2026; Interest Payment: Annually 19/12/2026 to 19/12/2029; Redemption: 19/12/2029.
- Power Grid Corporation Allotment Date: 29/09/2026; Interest Payment: Annually 29/09/2027 to 29/09/2036; Redemption: 29/09/2036.

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital market operations with no broad market impact, providing liquidity and trading access for the listed debt instruments.
