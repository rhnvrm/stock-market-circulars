## Summary

BSE has issued a notice communicating the change in scheme names for 13 schemes of SBI Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective immediately, based on communication received from SBI Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Scheme name changes are applicable on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect.
- Affected funds include various duration, debt, hybrid, and arbitrage funds of SBI Mutual Fund.
- Notable changes include 'SBI LOW DURATION FUND' renamed to 'SBI ULTRA SHORT TO SHORT TERM FUND', and 'SBI EQUITY HYBRID FUND' renamed to 'SBI AGGRESSIVE HYBRID FUND'.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Administrative scheme name update by AMC).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for order placement and reporting.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 03 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact on operations; primarily a nomenclature update across trading platforms and portfolios.
