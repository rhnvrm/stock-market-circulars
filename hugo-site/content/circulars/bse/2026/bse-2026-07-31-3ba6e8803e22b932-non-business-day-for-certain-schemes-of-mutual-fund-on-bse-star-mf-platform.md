## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that certain mutual fund schemes will observe non-business days on the BSE StAR MF Platform during August 2026, as communicated by the respective mutual funds.

## Key Points

- Markets will be closed for specific mutual fund schemes on designated Non-Business Day (NBD) dates in August 2026.
- Affected transactions include Subscription, Redemption, Switch, SIP, Normal STP, SWP, and AMC-STP.
- Details are available in the attached annexure (NON_BUSINESS_AUG_2026_List).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must note the non-business days and ensure clients do not submit disallowed transactions for the affected schemes on those dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 July 2026
- Effective Period: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary restriction on transactions for specific mutual fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform on designated non-business days.
