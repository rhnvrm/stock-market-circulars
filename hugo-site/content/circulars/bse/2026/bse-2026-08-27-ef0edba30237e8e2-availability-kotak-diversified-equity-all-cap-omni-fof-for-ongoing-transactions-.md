## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and associated facilities such as SIP, STP, and SWP on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: KOTAK DIVERSIFIED EQUITY ALL CAP OMNI FOF
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Affected Plans and ISINs:
  - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH (INF174KA1D59)
  - REGULAR PLAN - IDCW REINVESTMENT (INF174KA1D75)
  - REGULAR PLAN - IDCW PAYOUT (INF174KA1D67)
  - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH (INF174KA1D26)
  - DIRECT PLAN - IDCW REINVESTMENT (INF174KA1D42)
  - DIRECT PLAN - IDCW PAYOUT (INF174KA1D34)

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can execute transactions on behalf of investors on the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with standard operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to regular and direct plans of Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF for ongoing transactions and systematic investment/withdrawal facilities.
