## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade for 4,62,000 new equity shares of Retaggio Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 544391) with effect from October 26, 2026. These shares are issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Retaggio Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 544391
- ISIN: INE0KWJ01014
- Number of Shares: 4,62,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 26/- (including a premium of Rs. 16/-)
- Allotment Date: April 21, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 18438161 to 18900160
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to May 14, 2028

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable. This is a corporate action listing notice rather than a regulatory change.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares and are subject to lock-in requirements up to May 14, 2028.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: October 26, 2026
- Allotment Date: April 21, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 14, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital expansion and promoter shareholding adjustment through preferential warrant conversion with standard lock-in compliance.
