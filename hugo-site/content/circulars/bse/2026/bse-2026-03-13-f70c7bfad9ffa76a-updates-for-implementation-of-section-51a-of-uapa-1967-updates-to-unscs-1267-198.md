## Summary

The circular notifies market participants about the updates to the United Nations Security Council's (UNSC) 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List, specifically detailing the amendment of 22 entries pursuant to Security Council Committee decisions under Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967.

## Key Points

- The UN Security Council Committee amended 22 entries on its sanctions list.
- Updates include changes to titles, aliases, passport details, and other identifying information for listed individuals.
- Financial institutions and intermediaries must screen their databases against the updated sanctions list.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of amendments to the UNSC Sanctions List under Section 51A of the UAPA, 1967.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities must verify client databases and transactions against the updated list of designated individuals and entities.
- Immediate freezing of assets and blocking of funds or economic resources if any match is found, followed by reporting to relevant authorities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 10 March 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational requirement for compliance and risk management teams to update screening filters and perform database checks.
