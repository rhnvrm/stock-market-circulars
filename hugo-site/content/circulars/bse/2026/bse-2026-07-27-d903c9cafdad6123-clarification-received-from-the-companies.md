## Summary

BSE has sought clarifications from various companies following significant price and volume movements in their scrips in the recent past. The notice highlights companies that have submitted their replies alongside a list of companies whose responses remain awaited.

## Key Points

- The Exchange observed notable price and volume movements across multiple scrips.
- Clarifications received have been attached by the exchange from companies including Dhanashree Electronics Ltd, Sonalis Consumer Products Ltd, and TechNVision Ventures Ltd.
- Responses are currently awaited from 8 other listed entities.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely furnish clarifications requested by the exchange regarding unusual market activity to ensure transparent information flow to investors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 27 July 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market transparency regarding unusual price and volume movements without introducing new compliance mandates.
