## Summary

The BSE issued a notice under its surveillance department to verify news articles appearing across various media regarding select listed companies. Clarifications were sought and received from Prestige Estates Projects Ltd, PVR Inox Ltd, and Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260907-17
- Department: Surveillance (DOSS)
- Companies queried for clarification:
  1. Prestige Estates Projects Ltd (Scrip Code: 533274)
  2. PVR Inox Ltd (Scrip Code: 532689)
  3. Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd (Scrip Code: 505537)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to respond to exchange queries regarding media reports to ensure accuracy and transparency for investors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 07 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to investors regarding circulating market news and company stances.
