## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment on Saturday, August 22, 2026. Trading members using third-party trading platforms or in-house developed systems via ETI APIs can test their applications under simulated market conditions.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives.
- **Date:** Saturday, August 22, 2026.
- **Purpose:** Testing and familiarization for trading members, vendors, and developers.
- **Financial Impact:** None; trades will not attract margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out, or create real rights and liabilities.
- **Feedback:** Members are requested to provide feedback by 12:00 PM on the day of the mock session.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational testing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively in the mock trading session to test their systems. Feedback must be submitted by 12:00 PM on August 22, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** August 22, 2026
- **Log-in Time:** 08:15 AM to 09:00 AM
- **Continuous Trading (Currency & Cross Currency):** 09:00 AM to 12:00 PM
- **Trade Modification:** Up to 12:10 PM

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Allows members to ensure their trading systems and ETI APIs function correctly under exceptional market conditions without any risk of real financial settlement.
