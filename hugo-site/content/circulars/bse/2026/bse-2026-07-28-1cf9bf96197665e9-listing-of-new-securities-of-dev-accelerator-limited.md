## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Dev Accelerator Limited (Scrip Code - 544513) with effect from July 29, 2026. A total of 44,44,440 equity shares were issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Dev Accelerator Limited
- Scrip Code: 544513
- ISIN: INE0VOV01021
- Securities: 44,44,440 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 43/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 45/-
- Allotment Date: 16/06/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: July 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares. Lock-in details apply to specified distribution numbers up to 31/01/2027.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 16/06/2026
- Listing/Trading Effective Date: 29/07/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 31/01/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion for the company via preferential allotment to non-promoters.
