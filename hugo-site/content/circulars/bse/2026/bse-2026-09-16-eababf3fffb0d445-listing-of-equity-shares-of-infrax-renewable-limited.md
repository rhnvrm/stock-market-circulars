## Summary

Effective from Thursday, September 17, 2026, the equity shares of Infrax Renewable Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities in the SME segment. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Infrax Renewable Limited
- Scrip Code: 544919
- ISIN: INE1YC401018
- Issue Price: Rs. 104/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 94/-)
- Number of Securities: 1,42,35,911 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Market Maker: Shreni Shares Limited
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action executed for locking-in shares in depository records as per prospectus and Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 17, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Infrax Renewable Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with standard initial trade-for-trade surveillance.
