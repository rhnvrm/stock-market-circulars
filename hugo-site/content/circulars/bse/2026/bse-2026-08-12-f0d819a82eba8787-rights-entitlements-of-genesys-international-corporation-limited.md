## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that trading in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of Genesys International Corporation Limited will be permitted on the Exchange with effect from Friday, August 14, 2026. The Rights Issue is scheduled to be open from August 14, 2026, to August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Genesys International Corporation Limited
- **Security Type:** Rights Entitlements (REs)
- **Scrip Code:** 751130
- **Scrip ID:** GENESYS-RE
- **ISIN No.:** INE727B20018
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Group:** R
- **Settlement Basis:** Settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement and trade-for-trade basis.
- **Form of Trading:** Tradable in dematerialized form only.

## Regulatory Changes

No major statutory or regulatory changes are introduced in this circular. The listing and trading of these Rights Entitlements are governed by BSE Notice No. 20200305-21 dated March 05, 2020, and Notice No. 20200518-34 dated May 18, 2020.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Member Obligations:**
  - Trading members must inform all clients about the trading commencement of REs, clearly mentioning the ISIN `INE727B20018` to ensure it is not mistaken for ordinary shares.
  - A prominent disclaimer must be included in client contract notes/bills for RE purchases, emphasizing that buying REs only grants the right to apply for the Rights Issue or renounce them prior to the issue closing.
- **Investor Action:** REs that are neither subscribed to by submitting the application and requisite money nor renounced on or before the closing date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- **Rights Issue Opening Date:** Friday, August 14, 2026
- **RE Trading Commencement:** Friday, August 14, 2026
- **RE Trading/Renunciation Closing Date:** Tuesday, August 18, 2026
- **Rights Issue Closing Date:** Friday, August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Trading in the GENESYS-RE security will occur on a trade-for-trade basis under Group R, settling on a T+1 rolling basis. This represents a brief liquidity and trading window for the stock's rights entitlements.
- **Stakeholder Impact:** Eligible shareholders can either exercise their rights, trade/renounce them to monetize the entitlement, or take no action (which will lead to the forfeiture and extinguishment of the REs). Investors purchasing REs from the market must execute them by applying for rights shares before August 21, 2026, to prevent loss of capital.
