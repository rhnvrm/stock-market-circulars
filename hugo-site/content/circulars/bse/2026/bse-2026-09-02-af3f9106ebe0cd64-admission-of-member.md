## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the admission of new trading members on September 02, 2026, granting them trading rights in the cash segment as per the exchange rules and regulations.

## Key Points

- Kifs Broking Private Limited (Member No. 5164) admitted for Cash Segment (Additional Segment Membership).
- Kifs Stock Brokers Private Limited (Member No. 5166) admitted for Cash Segment (Additional Segment Membership).
- Admission effective from September 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may direct clarifications to membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Admission Date: 02 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update with no direct market impact.
