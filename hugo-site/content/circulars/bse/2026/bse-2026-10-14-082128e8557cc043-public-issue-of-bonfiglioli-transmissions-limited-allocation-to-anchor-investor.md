## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the allocation of equity shares under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Bonfiglioli Transmissions Limited. A total of 1,29,56,712 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 586/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- Issuer: Bonfiglioli Transmissions Limited
- Portion: Anchor Investors (AIs)
- Shares Allocated: 1,29,56,712 equity shares
- Price: Rs. 586/- per equity share
- Notice Date: October 14, 2026

None.

Trading members and market participants take note of the anchor investor allocation in the public issue.
