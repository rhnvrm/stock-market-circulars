## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited announced methodology changes to the BSE REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index, taking effect on July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: July 29, 2026.
- Constituent weightings remain float-adjusted market capitalization weighted with quarterly capping rules.
- REITs at the basic industry level must maintain a minimum aggregate weight of 65%.
- Individual security weights are capped at 20%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clarified and retained capping and weighting methodology for REITs and Commercial Real Estate index constituents.

## Compliance Requirements

- Index participants and tracking funds should note the updated index methodology.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 29, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal direct operational impact; relevant for index funds and portfolio managers tracking the BSE REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index.
