## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of Minolta Finance Ltd. with effect from Tuesday, September 1, 2026, following a rights issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Minolta Finance Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 532164
- Security Details: 40,00,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Re. 0.20/- on Rights Basis.
- ISIN: INE514C01026
- Distinctive Numbers: 100000001 to 500000000
- Date of Allotment: 24/08/2026
- Effective Date of Listing: 01/09/2026
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members note the listing and trading permissions for the new securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 24/08/2026
- Effective Date of Listing: 01/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Minolta Finance Ltd. through the addition of rights issue shares.
