## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of 22 scrips, effective from September 09, 2026, as part of its surveillance measures to curb excessive volatility.

## Key Points

- Total scrips affected: 22
- Effective date: September 09, 2026
- Revised price bands range from 2% to 20% depending on the specific scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands for specified scrips have been revised from their existing levels to new limits (e.g., 2%, 5%, 10%, 20%).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems to reflect the revised price bands for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 08 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: 09 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and traders dealing in the affected scrips will be subject to the new daily price movement limits, mitigating extreme price volatility.
