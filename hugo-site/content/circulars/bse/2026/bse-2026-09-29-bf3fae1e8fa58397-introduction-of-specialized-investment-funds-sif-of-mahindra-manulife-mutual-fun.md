## Summary

Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) have been activated on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 29, 2026, pursuant to SEBI's regulatory framework. Additionally, the MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund NFO scheme becomes available for order routing starting September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund SIF activated on BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 29, 2026.
- Transactions of NRI and Minor clients are permitted under SIF schemes subject to specific country restrictions.
- Non-acceptance of NRI applications applies to investors from the United States of America, Korea (Democratic People's Republic of), Iran (Islamic Republic of), and Canada.
- MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund NFO scheme activated for order routing effective September 30, 2026.
- Detailed transaction acceptance process is outlined in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of SEBI circular SEBI/HO/IMD/IMD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/26 dated February 27, 2025, establishing the regulatory framework for Specialized Investment Funds (SIF).

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with NRI investment restrictions and verify that applications from restricted countries (USA, North Korea, Iran, Canada) are not accepted for SIF schemes.

## Important Dates

- September 29, 2026: Activation of Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund SIF on BSE StAR MF Platform.
- September 30, 2026: Activation of MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund NFO scheme for order routing.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless trading and order routing of Specialized Investment Funds and NFOs for Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund on BSE StAR MF, while enforcing compliance regarding NRI client jurisdictions.
