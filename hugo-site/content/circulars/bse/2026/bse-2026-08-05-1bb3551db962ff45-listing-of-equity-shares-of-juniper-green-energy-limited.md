## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Thursday, August 6, 2026, the equity shares of Juniper Green Energy Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Juniper Green Energy Limited
- Scrip Code: 544853
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: JNPR
- ISIN No.: INE05C901015
- Number of Securities: 568,998,442 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 225/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 215/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 4, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Registrar: KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, August 6, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed to have the lock-in period marked in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 4, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: August 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Juniper Green Energy Limited shares on the BSE exchange, providing liquidity to investors and expanding the exchange's equity segment offerings.
