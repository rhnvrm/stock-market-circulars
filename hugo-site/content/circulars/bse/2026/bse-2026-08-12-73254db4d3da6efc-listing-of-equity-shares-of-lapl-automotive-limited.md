## Summary

BSE has issued a notice (Notice No. 20260812-34) announcing the listing and admission of Equity Shares of **LAPL AUTOMOTIVE LIMITED** to dealings on the Exchange. The shares will be listed under the **'MT' Group of Securities** (SME Segment) effective from **Thursday, August 13, 2026**. Additionally, pursuant to SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, the scrip will trade under the **Trade-for-Trade (TFT)** segment for the first 10 trading days to prevent excessive volatility.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** LAPL AUTOMOTIVE LIMITED
- **Registered Office:** Plot No. 90, Sector No. 05, Auric City, Shendra Industrial Area, Chikalthana Industrial Area, Aurangabad, Maharashtra, India, 431006
- **Scrip ID on BOLT System:** LAPL
- **Abbreviated Name on BOLT System:** LAPL
- **Scrip Code:** 544863
- **ISIN No.:** INE0X9I01023
- **Total Securities:** 12,536,218 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 1 to 12536218
- **Market Lot:** 1,200 equity shares (minimum trading size)
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 94/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 84/-)
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** The scrip will be part of SPOS on Thursday, August 13, 2026, as per Exchange notice no. 20120216-29.
- **Dematerialised Form:** In line with SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, trading in the company's shares will only be permitted in dematerialised form.
- **Market Makers:**
  - *Giriraj Stock Broking Private Limited* (SEBI Registration No: INZ000212638)
  - *Mansi Share and Stock Broking Private Limited* (SEBI Registration No: INZ000247433)
- **Registrar to the Issue:** Maashitla Securities Private Limited (SEBI Registration Number: INR000004370)

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory policies are introduced by this circular.
- Trading is conducted in compliance with SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012 (Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days) and SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000 (compulsory dematerialised form).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:**
  - Must execute all trades in the minimum market lot size of 1,200 equity shares.
  - Must adhere to Trade-for-Trade (TFT) settlement rules, requiring 100% gross upfront margin and physical settlement (no intraday squaring off) for the first 10 trading days.
- **Depository/Company:**
  - The company has marked the necessary lock-in periods on depository records in respect of shares in dematerialised form.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment in the Public Issue:** August 11, 2026
- **Date of BSE Circular:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Listing & Commencement of Trading:** Thursday, August 13, 2026
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** Thursday, August 13, 2026
- **Trade-for-Trade Period:** From August 13, 2026, to approximately August 27, 2026 (10 trading days)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Adds LAPL Automotive Limited as a newly listed SME to the BSE SME platform. System administrators and brokers must configure their trading platforms and risk systems to support the new security symbols, ensure a minimum market lot of 1,200 shares, and enforce Trade-for-Trade rules for the specified initial period.
