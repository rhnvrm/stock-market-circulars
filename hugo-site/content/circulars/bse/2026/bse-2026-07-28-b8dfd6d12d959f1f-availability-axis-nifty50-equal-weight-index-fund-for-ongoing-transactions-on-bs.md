## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that the Axis Nifty50 Equal Weight Index Fund has reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and related facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective from July 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Axis Nifty50 Equal Weight Index Fund (Regular Plan - Growth, ISIN: INF846KA1556; Direct Plan - Growth, ISIN: INF846KA1564).
- Facilities available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: July 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate transactions in the mentioned schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 28 July 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute ongoing transactions and systematic investment plans for the Axis Nifty50 Equal Weight Index Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
