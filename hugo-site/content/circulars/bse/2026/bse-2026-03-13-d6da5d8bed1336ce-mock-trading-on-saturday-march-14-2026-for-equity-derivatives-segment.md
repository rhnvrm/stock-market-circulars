## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are advised to test connectivity to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site using the specified parameters for BOLT TWS, ETI APIs, and exchange web-based systems.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Equity Derivatives segment.
- DR connection parameters provided for BOLT Plus Trading system, including primary and secondary DR IP and port details.
- Details provided for web-based systems such as RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, and Extranet access via DR links.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should participate in the mock trading session to verify their DR connection parameters and system readiness.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational check for members to ensure business continuity and disaster recovery preparedness without affecting live market operations.
