## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-721/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on September 24, 2026.
- Auction matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Friday, Sep 25, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and Custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections will be permitted.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 24, 2026
- Pay-in Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational process for clearing and settling short deliveries in rolling settlements.
