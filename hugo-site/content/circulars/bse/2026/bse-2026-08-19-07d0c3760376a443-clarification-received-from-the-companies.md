## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a surveillance notice highlighting significant price and volume movements in various scrips. To ensure investors and trading members have access to relevant and timely information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies. Replies have been received from five companies, while responses from nine other companies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- **Companies with Replies Received:**
  1. Dolphin Offshore Enterprises (India) Ltd (Scrip Code: 522261)
  2. India Glycols Ltd (Scrip Code: 500201)
  3. Prabha Energy Ltd (Scrip Code: 544379)
  4. Windsor Machines Ltd (Scrip Code: 522029)
  5. Gallard Steel Ltd (Scrip Code: 544616)

- **Companies with Replies Awaited:**
  1. Apt Packaging Ltd (Scrip Code: 506979)
  2. Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd (Scrip Code: 542176)
  3. CMX Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 532217)
  4. Deep Polymers Ltd (Scrip Code: 541778)
  5. Gconnect Logitech and Supply Chain Ltd (Scrip Code: 544156)
  6. Mahesh Developers Ltd (Scrip Code: 542677)
  7. PVP Ventures Ltd (Scrip Code: 517556)
  8. Shree Balaji (Mala) Textiles Ltd (Scrip Code: 544835)
  9. Triumph International Finance India Ltd (Scrip Code: 532131)

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework changes introduced; this is a routine surveillance disclosure notice issued under market oversight procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities experiencing abnormal price/volume movements are required to promptly clarify and disclose relevant information to the Exchange for investor protection and transparency.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 19 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency by notifying investors of abnormal trading activity and making company clarifications publicly available, while flagging entities that have yet to respond.
