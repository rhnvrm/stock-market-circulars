## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing that IndiGrid Infrastructure Trust has received the final call money from debenture holders for its listed debt securities. Effective Thursday, September 10, 2026, the ISINs and paid-up values of these securities are updated.

## Key Points

- Final call money received from debenture holders of IndiGrid Infrastructure Trust.
- Effective Date: September 10, 2026.
- Scrip Code 978072: ISIN updated from INE219X07595 to INE219X07629; paid-up value increased from Rs. 20,000/- to Rs. 1,00,000/- per Debenture.
- Scrip Code 978073: ISIN updated from INE219X07603 to INE219X07611; paid-up value increased from Rs. 20,000/- to Rs. 1,00,000/- per Debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their systems with the new ISINs and paid-up values for the respective debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading members and depositories to reflect the full paid-up status and new ISIN identifiers for the debentures.
