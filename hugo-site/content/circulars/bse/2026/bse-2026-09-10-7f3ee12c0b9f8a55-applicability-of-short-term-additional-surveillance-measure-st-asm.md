## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for various securities based on market surveillance criteria, with effect from September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM framework operates in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and does not reflect adverse actions against companies.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their original bands unless subjected to other surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum cap of 100%.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure appropriate margins (50% or 100%) are maintained on all open positions as on September 11, 2026, and new positions created from September 15, 2026.
- For queries or clarifications, members can reach out to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10 Sep 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Requirements: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in securities shortlisted under ST-ASM will experience increased margin obligations (up to 100%), impacting liquidity and leverage for the affected scrips.
