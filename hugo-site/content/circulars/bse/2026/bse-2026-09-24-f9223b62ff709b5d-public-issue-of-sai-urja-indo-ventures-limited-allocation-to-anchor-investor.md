BSE has announced that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Sai Urja Indo Ventures Limited, 5,86,800 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs 113/- per equity share as of September 23, 2026.

- Number of Shares Subscribed: 5,86,800 Equity Shares
