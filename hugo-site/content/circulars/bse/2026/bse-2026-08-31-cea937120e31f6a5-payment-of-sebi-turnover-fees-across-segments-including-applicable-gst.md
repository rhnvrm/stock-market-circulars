## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the SEBI turnover fee for various segments (including Cash, Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, Interest Rate Derivatives, New Debt Segment, Repo Segment, and EGR segment) along with applicable GST at 18% will be debited from their settlement (valan) or exchange dues accounts on September 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- SEBI turnover fees are subject to 18% GST effective from July 18, 2022.
- Fees and applicable GST will be debited on the 3rd working day of the next month, specifically September 03, 2026.
- Turnover details and amounts will be available in EXTRANET (current date > EQ > Transaction) on or before September 02, 2026.
- Invoices for August 2026 will be available for download in EXTRANET (Periodic files > 2026 > ZIP with file name SEBIAUG2026.Member_Code) on or before September 02, 2026.
- Trading members must ensure sufficient funds are available in their settlement or exchange dues accounts.

## Regulatory Changes

- SEBI turnover fee across all segments remains subject to 18% GST as per previous norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adequate balance in their settlement (valan) accounts or exchange dues accounts for the smooth debit of SEBI turnover fees and GST by September 3, 2026.
- Members should verify their turnover details and invoices from the EXTRANET portal before the debit date.

## Important Dates

- **August 31, 2026:** Notice issuance date.
- **September 02, 2026:** Deadline for availability of turnover details in EXTRANET and invoice downloads.
- **September 03, 2026:** Date of debiting turnover fees and applicable GST from settlement/exchange dues accounts.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact on trading members to maintain sufficient liquidity in their settlement accounts for statutory and regulatory fee deductions.
