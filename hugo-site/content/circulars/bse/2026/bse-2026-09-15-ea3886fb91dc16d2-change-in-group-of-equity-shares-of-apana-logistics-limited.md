## Summary

Trading members are informed that equity shares of Apana Logistics Limited – SME IPO (Scrip Code: 544910) are being transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective from Tuesday, September 29, 2026, following Exchange Notice No. 20260911-46.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Apana Logistics Limited
- Scrip Code: 544910
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Tuesday, September 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may note the segment change for dealings in the equity shares of the company.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Normal trading operations for the company's equity shares under the Rolling segment.
