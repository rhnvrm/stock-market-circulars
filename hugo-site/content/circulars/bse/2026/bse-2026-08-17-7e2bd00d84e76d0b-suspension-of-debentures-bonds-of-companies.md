## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of several companies. The suspension is enacted because these companies have fixed record dates for the purpose of payment of interest and/or redemption of their respective NCDs.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified NCDs of the listed companies starting from the respective ex-dates/record dates.
- Affected issuers include Manappuram Home Finance Limited, JM Financial Products Limited, KLM Axiva Finvest Limited, Muthoot Fincorp Limited, and HDB Financial Services Limited.
- Record dates and ex-dates for the listed debentures are set for August 21, 2026, and August 27, 2026, depending on the specific security.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that no transactions are executed in the suspended NCDs of the mentioned companies during the restriction period.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Record / Ex-Dates:** August 21, 2026, and August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary restriction on secondary market liquidity for the specified debentures during the record date and interest payment/redemption processing phase.
