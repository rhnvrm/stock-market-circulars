## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by eight companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS), effective from Monday, September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- A total of 8 companies have listed further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective trading date: September 28, 2026.
- Companies involved include Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited, Antelopus Selan Energy Limited, ESAF Small Finance Bank Ltd, Hindustan Foods Ltd., ICICI Bank Ltd., Kopran Ltd., NIIT Learning Systems Limited, and Oracle Financial Services Software Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard operational listing notice for shares issued under employee stock option schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure compliance with applicable BSE listing regulations and depository procedures for newly admitted securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minor equity expansion for the respective companies due to ESOP exercise; no significant market impact.
