## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for new securities of SI Capital & Financial Services Limited resulting from a rights issue, effective from September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 530907
- Company Name: SI Capital & Financial Services Ltd
- Security Details: 75,75,000 Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at par on a rights basis
- ISIN: INE417F01017
- Distinctive Numbers: 5050001 - 12625000
- Date of Allotment: 09/09/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: September 15, 2026
- Ranking: Pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing and permission to trade the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 9, 2026
- Effective Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the total paid-up equity base of SI Capital & Financial Services Limited through the addition of rights issue shares.
