## Summary

BSE has issued a notice listing scrips that will not be available for trading in the T+0 rolling settlement cycle on Friday, September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260910-52 dated September 10, 2026.
- Affected scrips: Union Bank of India (Scrip Code: 132477, Symbol: UNIONBANK#) and Swiggy Limited (Scrip Code: 144285, Symbol: SWIGGY#).
- These scrips are excluded from the T+0 rolling settlement cycle for the specified date.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note that the specified scrips will not be available for trading under the T+0 rolling settlement cycle on the given date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to market participants trading in the specified scrips under the T+0 settlement mechanism on the effective date.
