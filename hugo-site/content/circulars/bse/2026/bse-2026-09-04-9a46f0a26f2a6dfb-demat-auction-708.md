## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction with respect to Rolling Settlement No. DR-708/2026-27. The notice outlines the schedule for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, money statements, and the subsequent pay-in date.

## Key Points

- Settlement Number: DR-708/2026-27
- Auction Offer Entry Timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on September 04, 2026
- Auction Matching Timings: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on September 04, 2026
- Delivery Statement Timings: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m. on September 04, 2026
- Money Statement Timings: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m. on September 04, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: Monday, September 07, 2026 at 08:00 a.m.
- Trading members are reminded that restrictions on offering securities in auction for settlements where they short delivered have been removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should exercise care while punching security codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Members should verify cut-off rates in the 'Auction offer Entry Screen' and consider corporate actions (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split) before submitting offers.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 04, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice affecting trading members participating in the demat auction process for rolling settlement DR-708/2026-27.
