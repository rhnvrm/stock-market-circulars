## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2026, based on SEBI notification No. SEBI/LAD-DOP/2026/329 dated October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- SEBI has notified the Third Amendment Regulations, 2026 under the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
- The notification reference is SEBI/LAD-DOP/2026/329 dated October 12, 2026.
- The regulations apply to all listed entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduces further amendments to the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- All listed entities are required to take note of the amendments and ensure ongoing compliance.

## Important Dates

- Notification Date: October 12, 2026
- Notice Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date: Date of publication in the Official Gazette

## Impact Assessment

- Listed companies must examine the notification details to ensure their governance, reporting, and disclosure practices align with the updated LODR provisions.
