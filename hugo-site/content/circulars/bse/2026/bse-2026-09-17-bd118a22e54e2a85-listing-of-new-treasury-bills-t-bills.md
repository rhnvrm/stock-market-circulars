## Summary

Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission to dealings of new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS, effective from Friday, September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three new Treasury Bills are being listed with different maturities:
  - 91TB171226 (Scrip code: 805268, ISIN: IN002026X248, Maturity Date: 17/12/2026)
  - 182TB18327 (Scrip code: 805269, ISIN: IN002026Y246, Maturity Date: 18/03/2027)
  - 364TB16927 (Scrip code: 805270, ISIN: IN002026Z243, Maturity Date: 16/09/2027)
- Market Lot for all listed T-Bills is set to 1.
- Securities will not be available for trading two (2) working days prior to their respective Maturity/Redemption Date.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine administrative listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the trading restrictions two working days prior to maturity.

## Important Dates

- Effective Listing Date: September 18, 2026
- Maturity Dates: December 17, 2026; March 18, 2027; September 16, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expands available short-term government debt instruments for trading members on the exchange.
