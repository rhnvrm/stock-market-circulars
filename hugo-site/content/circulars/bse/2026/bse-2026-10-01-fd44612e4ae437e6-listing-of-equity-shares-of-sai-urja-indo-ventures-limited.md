## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of 7,638,800 equity shares of Sai Urja Indo Ventures Limited on the SME segment effective from Monday, October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Name / ID: SAIURJA
- Scrip Code: 544958
- ISIN No.: INE1LLG01019
- Issue Price: Rs. 113/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 103/-)
- Market Lot: 1,200 equity shares
- Segment: MT Group (Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days)
- Market Maker: Prabhat Financial Services Limited
- Registrar: Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. The listing follows standard regulatory guidelines for SME IPOs under SEBI norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form.
- Trading must adhere to the minimum market lot of 1,200 equity shares.
- Lock-in arrangements are executed and marked in the depository's records as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): October 5, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and trading liquidity for Sai Urja Indo Ventures Limited shares on the BSE SME platform, with initial trade-for-trade surveillance measures in place.
