## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures of the companies as per the announced schedule.

## Key Points

- Notice covers multiple NCD and bond issuances across companies including Muthoot Fincorp Limited, Satin Creditcare Network Limited, Edelweiss Financial Services Ltd., Kosamattam Finance Limited, UGRO Capital Limited, Arohan Financial Services Ltd., and Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited.
- Record dates for the upcoming interest payments and redemptions fall between October 7, 2026, and October 10, 2026.
- Trading members are advised to take note of the restrictions and record dates while dealing in the affected debt securities.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational and corporate action notice regarding debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with trading restrictions in the specified debenture scrips around the record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: October 7, 2026 to October 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact limited to holders of the specific NCDs and bonds undergoing interest payment or redemption.
