## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding all Trading Members to submit their Corrective Action Report (CAR) pertaining to the System Audit Report for the period ended March 31, 2026. The submission must be completed electronically through the BEFS portal on or before September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applies to all Trading Members required to submit System Audit Reports for the period ended March 31, 2026.
- Trading Members must take corrective action for each non-compliance reported by the auditor.
- Submission deadline: September 30, 2026.
- Mode of submission: Electronic form only via the BEFS portal (BSE Electronic Filing System).
- Reference to Annexure 1 of Exchange notice no. 20260417-45 regarding rationalization and standardization of penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a procedural compliance notice enforcing previously established reporting requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must review non-compliances highlighted in the System Audit Report for the period ended March 31, 2026.
- Implement appropriate corrective actions.
- Submit the Corrective Action Report (TOR II CAR or TOR III CAR) via the BEFS portal (https://membersso.bseindia.com > BEFS > System Audit Report > Corrective Action Report) by the due date.

## Important Dates

- **Due Date for Submission:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members failing to submit the Corrective Action Report by the deadline may attract penalties as per the standardized penalty structure for trading members.
