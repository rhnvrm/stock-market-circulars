## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission for trading of 13,00,000 equity shares of Ecoplast Limited (Scrip Code: 526703) with effect from August 07, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the scheme of amalgamation of Kunal Plastics Private Limited with Ecoplast Limited.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ecoplast Limited
- Scrip Code: 526703
- ISIN Number: INE423D01010
- Security Details: 13,00,000 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each issued pursuant to amalgamation.
- Distribution Nos.: 3454526 – 4754525
- Date of Allotment: June 15, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading: August 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and depository participants are advised to note the distribution numbers and ISIN details for trading and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: June 15, 2026
- Notice Date: August 06, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

An increase in the paid-up equity capital of Ecoplast Limited due to the issuance of shares under the scheme of amalgamation with Kunal Plastics Private Limited.
