## Summary

BSE has published a notice regarding the draft TRAI direction on registration, allocation, monitoring, traceability, and regulatory enforcement relating to SIP/PRI telecom resources, DID numbers, and bulk mobile resources.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260925-1
- Notice Date: 25 Sep 2026
- Category: Settlement/RMS
- Segment: General
- Department: BSECL
- Attachments include Annexure A detailing the draft TRAI direction.

## Regulatory Changes

Introduction of draft regulatory measures by TRAI to enhance traceability, monitoring, and compliance for enterprise telecom resources such as SIP/PRI trunks, DID numbers, and bulk mobile connections.

## Compliance Requirements

Regulated entities and market participants utilizing bulk telecom resources should review the draft guidelines provided in Annexure A and adhere to applicable TRAI norms.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 25 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

General administrative and compliance update with minimal direct impact on exchange trading or stock valuations.
