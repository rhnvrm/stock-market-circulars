## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by eight companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS). These securities become effective for trading from Friday, August 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 8 companies have issued further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective date for trading: August 07, 2026.
- Included companies span various sectors such as financial services, banking, jewellery, technology, precision engineering, consumer products, and industrial components.

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable (Routine listing notice).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants are requested to take note of the new security listings and corresponding lock-in details (marked as NA for all listed entries in this notice).

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine capital increase resulting from employee stock option exercises. Minimal immediate market impact.
