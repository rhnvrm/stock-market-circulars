## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from September 02, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and apply adequate precautions.

## Key Points

- Securities will be moved to higher stages of GSM effective September 02, 2026.
- Applicable surveillance actions depend on the specific GSM stage (Stages I, II, III, and IV).
- Settlements for these securities shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Trading members are requested to refer to the FAQs and exercise caution while trading.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the GSM Framework published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent amending notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin rates and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) are collected as applicable per the GSM stage.
- No netting off is permitted as settlements are on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 01 Sep 2026
- **Effective Date:** 02 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in securities moving to higher GSM stages will be subject to stricter surveillance, margin requirements, price bands, and trading frequency limitations, impacting liquidity and settlement procedures.
