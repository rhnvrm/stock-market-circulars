## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of two newly listed companies, ENS Enterprises Limited and Technocrats Plasma Systems Limited, to the BSE SME IPO Index effective from the open of trading on Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- ENS Enterprises Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544876) and Technocrats Plasma Systems Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544877) are being listed on BSE effective August 21, 2026.
- Both stocks will be included in the BSE SME IPO Index starting August 24, 2026.
- Corporate actions have been updated in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Index Addition: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine inclusion reflecting the addition of newly listed SME companies into the index methodology.
