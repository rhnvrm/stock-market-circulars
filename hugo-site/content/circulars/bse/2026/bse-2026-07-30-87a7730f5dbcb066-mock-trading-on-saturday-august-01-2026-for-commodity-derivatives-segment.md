## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, August 01, 2026. Trading members can use this session to test their trading applications, risk-reduction modes, and trading halts in compliance with regulatory testing requirements.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, August 01, 2026, for the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Fulfills regulatory requirements under SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.30 shall be released separately.
- Timings: Log-in from 08:15 AM to 09:00 AM; Continuous Trading from 09:00 AM to 03:30 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Conducted as per existing regulatory testing frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may participate in the mock trading session or UAT environment to fulfill testing requirements pursuant to SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** August 01, 2026
- **Log-in Time:** 08:15 AM - 09:00 AM
- **Continuous Trading:** 09:00 AM - 03:30 PM
- **Feedback Deadline:** August 01, 2026, by 04:30 PM

## Impact Assessment

Trades resulting from mock trading do not attract margin obligations, pay-in, or pay-out obligations, ensuring zero financial impact.
