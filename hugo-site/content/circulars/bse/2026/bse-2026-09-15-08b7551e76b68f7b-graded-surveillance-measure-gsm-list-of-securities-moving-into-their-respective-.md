## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice detailing the list of securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. These measures take effect from September 16, 2026, and apply specific surveillance actions to safeguard market integrity and investors.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure move to higher stages of the GSM framework.
- Effective date for the surveillance actions is September 16, 2026.
- Settlement for these securities will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Specific surveillance actions include margin rate revisions, trade-for-trade restrictions, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Regulatory Changes

The securities are subjected to surveillance measures defined under the GSM Framework, depending on their assigned stage:
- **Stage I:** 100% applicable margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- **Stage II:** Trade-for-trade with a 5% or lower price band and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value by buyers.
- **Stage III:** Trade-for-trade with a 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and 100% ASD to be deposited by buyers.
- **Stage IV:** Trade-for-trade with a 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week, and 100% ASD with no upward movement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities. Settlements must strictly adhere to trade-to-trade guidelines with no netting off permitted. Members can direct clarifications or queries to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 15, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for securities moving into higher GSM stages are likely to be impacted due to strict trading restrictions, mandatory margin requirements, and trade-to-trade settlement rules.
