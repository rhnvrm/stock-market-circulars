## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 6,75,00,000 new equity shares of Sadhana Nitrochem Ltd. issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sadhana Nitrochem Ltd. (Scrip Code: 506642, ISIN: INE888C01040)
- Number of Shares: 6,75,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Re. 1.06/- (Issue Price: Rs. 2.06/-)
- Allotment Date: August 7, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 2964694386 to 3032194385
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to March 25, 2027

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported in this circular.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the distribution numbers, issue price, and lock-in restrictions applicable to the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 07, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: September 25, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: March 25, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing of preferential shares resulting in capital expansion for the company with standard lock-in compliance measures.
