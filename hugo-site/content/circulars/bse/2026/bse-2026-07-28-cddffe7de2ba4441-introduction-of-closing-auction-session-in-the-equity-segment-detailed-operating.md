## Summary

BSE has released updated operating guidelines and an FAQ document regarding the introduction of the Closing Auction Session in the Equity Segment, referencing earlier SEBI and Exchange circulars.

## Key Points

- Updated FAQ document enclosed as Annexure (Annexurev1-FAQs).
- FAQs available on the Exchange website starting July 31, 2026, under the 'Markets' section >> Equity >> Market Information >> Closing Auction Session.
- Contact details provided for technical and functional queries.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates operating guidelines and FAQs for the Closing Auction Session in the Equity Segment pursuant to SEBI circular dated January 16, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take note of the updated FAQ document and operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective/Availability Date: July 31, 2026
- Circular Date: July 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures market participants have clarity on operational procedures and frequently asked questions for the closing auction session in equity.
