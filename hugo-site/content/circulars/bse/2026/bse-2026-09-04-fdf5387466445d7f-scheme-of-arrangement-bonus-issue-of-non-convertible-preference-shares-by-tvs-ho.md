## Summary

TVS Holdings Ltd. has announced the record date pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement approved by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Chennai Bench. The company is issuing bonus non-convertible preference shares to its existing equity shareholders.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** TVS Holdings Ltd. (Scrip Code: 520056)
- **Record Date:** September 8, 2026
- **Ex-Entitlement Date:** September 8, 2026 (Sett. No. DR-711/2026-2027)
- **Bonus Ratio:** 46 (Forty Six) 6% Cumulative Non-Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares of Rs. 10/- each for every 1 (One) fully paid-up equity share of Rs. 5/- held.
- **Funding Source:** Utilisation of the company's retained earnings.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implemented pursuant to the NCLT, Chennai Bench sanctioned Scheme of Arrangement.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company will make necessary applications to the Exchange for listing and trading of the Non-Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares (NCRPS) subject to compliance with requisite formalities.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** September 8, 2026
- **Ex-Entitlement Date:** September 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Existing shareholders registered as of the record date will receive preference shares as bonus allotment, altering the capital structure through the utilization of retained earnings.
