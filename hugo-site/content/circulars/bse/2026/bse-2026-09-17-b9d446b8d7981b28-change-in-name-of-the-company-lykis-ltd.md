## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the change in name of Lykis Ltd to Krowniq Limited. The scrip ID and abbreviated name for the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Lykis Ltd
- New Company Name: KROWNIQ LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 530689
- New Scrip ID on BOLT Plus System: KROWNIQ
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: KROWNIQ

Trading members are advised to take note of the change and update their records accordingly.
