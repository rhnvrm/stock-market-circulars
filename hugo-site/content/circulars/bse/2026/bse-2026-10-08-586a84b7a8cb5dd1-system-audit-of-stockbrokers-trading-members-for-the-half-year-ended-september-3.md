## Summary

BSE issued a notice regarding the mandatory system audit of stockbrokers and trading members for the half-year ended September 30, 2026. The circular details submission timelines, selection criteria for BSE-empanelled system auditors, and technology-based supervision requirements.

## Key Points

- Stockbrokers utilizing or offering algorithmic trading facilities must undergo half-yearly system audits.
- Exchanges will exclusively accept System Audit Reports certified by BSE-empanelled system auditors effective from the half-year ending September 30, 2026.
- Audit plans must capture details of the auditor, audited systems, and office locations to be visited.
- Auditors must log into Cymmetri during physical premises visits to capture geo-location details.
- Trading members and auditors must preserve audit records, working papers, and visit logs for a minimum period of three years.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of mandatory certification strictly via BSE-empanelled system auditors.
- Integration of technology-based measures (geo-location via Cymmetri) during physical audit visits.

## Compliance Requirements

- Select only BSE-empanelled system auditors for conducting the audit.
- Submit complete audit plan details via the BSE BEFS portal by November 05, 2026.
- Ensure auditors capture geo-location via Cymmetri and enter visit-related details during physical inspections.
- Submit audit reports and Action Taken Reports (ATRs) within designated deadlines.

## Important Dates

- **Audit Period:** April 2026 – September 30, 2026
- **Submission of Audit Plan:** November 05, 2026
- **Submission of Audit Report:** November 30, 2026
- **Action Taken Report (ATR) Submission:** February 28, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must align compliance workflows with empanelled auditors, adhere strictly to portal submission deadlines, and comply with mandatory geo-location and record-keeping mandates.
