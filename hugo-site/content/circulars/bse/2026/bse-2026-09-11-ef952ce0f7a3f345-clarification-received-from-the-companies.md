## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in several scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from multiple companies. Replies received from several companies are attached to the notice, while responses from a few others are still awaited.

## Key Points

- **Surveillance Action:** The Exchange monitored significant price/volume volatility across multiple scrips.
- **Companies with Replies Received:** 
  1. Anmol India Ltd (Scrip Code: 542437)
  2. Astec Lifesciences Ltd (Scrip Code: 533138)
  3. Bal Pharma Ltd (Scrip Code: 524824)
  4. Chembond Material Technologies Ltd (Scrip Code: 530871)
  5. Indo Rama Synthetics (India) Ltd (Scrip Code: 500207)
  6. Mangalam Worldwide Ltd (Scrip Code: 544764)
  7. Tata Investment Corporation Ltd (Scrip Code: 501301)
  8. Texmo Pipes and Products Ltd (Scrip Code: 533164)
  9. Wendt (India) Ltd (Scrip Code: 505412)
- **Companies with Replies Awaited:** 
  1. CMX Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 532217)
  2. Deep Polymers Ltd (Scrip Code: 541778)
  3. Dhanwel Hybrid Seeds Ltd (Scrip Code: 544884)
  4. Mahesh Developers Ltd (Scrip Code: 542677)
  5. Mopshop Distribution Ltd (Scrip Code: 544883)
  6. Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Ltd (Scrip Code: 544510)
  7. Nirmitee Robotics India Ltd (Scrip Code: 543194)
  8. Sera Investments & Finance India Ltd (Scrip Code: 512399)
  9. Shashwat Furnishing Solutions Ltd (Scrip Code: 543519)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard surveillance clarification process.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies witnessing unusual market movements are required to clarify and provide relevant information to the Exchange as per regulatory guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 11 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures market integrity and protects investors by ensuring timely disclosure of material information by listed entities experiencing unusual trading activity.
