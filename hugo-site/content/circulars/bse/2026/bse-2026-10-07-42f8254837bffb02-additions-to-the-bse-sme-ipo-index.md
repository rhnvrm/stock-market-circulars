## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of two newly listed companies, Vans Electroengineerings Limited and Black Opal Consultants Limited, to the BSE SME IPO Index effective at the open of Thursday, October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Vans Electroengineerings Limited** (Exchange Ticker: 544971) added to the BSE SME IPO Index.
- **Black Opal Consultants Limited** (Exchange Ticker: 544972) added to the BSE SME IPO Index.
- Both companies are listed on BSE effective October 07, 2026.
- Index inclusion takes effect at the market open on October 08, 2026.
- Corporate action updates have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for market participants; informational notice for index tracking and operations.

## Important Dates

- **Listing Date:** October 07, 2026
- **Index Effective Date:** October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine index adjustment with minimal impact on overall market operations, affecting portfolio constituents tracking the BSE SME IPO Index.
