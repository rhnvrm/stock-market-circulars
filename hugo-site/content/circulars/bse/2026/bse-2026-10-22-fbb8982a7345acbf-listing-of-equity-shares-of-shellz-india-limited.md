## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that the equity shares of Shellz India Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in due course under the SME segment.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Shellz India Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544998
- **Symbol:** SHELLZ
- **ISIN:** INE0Z7301016
- **Segment:** SME

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing notification for a company entering the SME platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants should take note of the security details for settlement and trading once the listing date is announced.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Listing Date:** To be announced via a separate notice

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Shellz India Limited equity shares on the BSE SME platform upon announcement of the listing date.
