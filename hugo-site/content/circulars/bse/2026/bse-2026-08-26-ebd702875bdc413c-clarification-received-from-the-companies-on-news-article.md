## Summary

BSE has sought clarification from selected companies regarding news articles appearing in various media platforms to verify accuracy and inform market participants. Replies received from Vodafone Idea Ltd and DCB Bank Ltd have been attached by the exchange.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260826-22
- Date: August 26, 2026
- Companies involved: Vodafone Idea Ltd (Scrip Code: 532822) and DCB Bank Ltd (Scrip Code: 532772)
- Purpose: Verification of news articles appearing in media/newspapers/websites/television channels

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to clarify news reports as sought by the Exchange under listing regulations to ensure accurate dissemination of information to investors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 26 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct operational or market impact; serves transparency and investor awareness purposes.
