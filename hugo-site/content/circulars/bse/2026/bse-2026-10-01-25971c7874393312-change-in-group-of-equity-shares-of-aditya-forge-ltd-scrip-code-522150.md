## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the inclusion of equity shares of Aditya Forge Ltd (Scrip Code: 522150) under the 'X' group effective from October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aditya Forge Ltd
- Scrip Code: 522150
- Action: Inclusion under 'X' group
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares of Aditya Forge Ltd are reassigned to the 'X' trading group on the BSE platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the group change. Clarifications can be sought via email at bse.revocation@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative reclassification with minimal direct market impact for general investors and trading members.
