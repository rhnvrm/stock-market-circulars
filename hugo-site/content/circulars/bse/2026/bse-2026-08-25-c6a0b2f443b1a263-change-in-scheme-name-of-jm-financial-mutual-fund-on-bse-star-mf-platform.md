## Summary

JM Financial Mutual Fund has updated the scheme names for several of its mutual fund offerings on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026, pursuant to a communication received by the exchange.

## Key Points

- Effective date for the name change: August 26, 2026.
- Affected schemes include JM Midcap Fund, JM Flexicap Fund, JM ELSS Tax Saver Fund, and various duration/debt funds.
- All changes are updated on the BSE StAR MF Platform as per the Notice-cum-Addendum issued by JM Financial AMC.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to note the revised scheme names for order placement and reporting on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal impact on trading mechanics; ensures proper scheme identification under new nomenclature.
