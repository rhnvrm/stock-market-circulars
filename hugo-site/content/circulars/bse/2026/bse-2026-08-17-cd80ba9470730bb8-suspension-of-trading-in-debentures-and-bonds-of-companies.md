## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and/or redemption of their non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading members are advised not to deal in these debentures/bonds as of the specified record and ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260817-41
- Segment: Debt
- Department: Listing Operations
- Purpose: Record date fixation for interest payment and redemption of NCDs/Bonds.
- Affected Issuers include Nido Home Finance Limited, Muthoot Fincorp Limited, Power Finance Corporation Ltd, IndiGrid Infrastructure Trust, Mahindra & Mahindra Financial Services Limited, ICICI Home Finance Company Limited, India Infradebt Limited, and Lendingkart Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures/bonds of the listed companies effective from the ex-date/record date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 17 August 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Date: 31 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification ensuring market participants are aware of trading suspensions due to upcoming interest payments or redemptions for specific debt instruments.
