## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 1,90,225 new equity shares of Tanvi Foods (India) Limited with effect from Tuesday, August 18, 2026, following the conversion of warrants issued on a preferential basis to promoters.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Tanvi Foods (India) Limited (Scrip Code: 540332)
- Security Details: 1,90,225 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 80/- (Issue Price: Rs. 90/-).
- Allotment Date: 12/05/2026
- ISIN: INE978V01015
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.
- Lock-in Details: 1,90,225 shares locked-in up to 28/02/2028 (Dist. Nos. 12828776 to 13019000).

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and trading restrictions for the newly listed preferential shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 12/05/2026
- Effective Date for Trading: 18/08/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 28/02/2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the company via warrant conversion.
