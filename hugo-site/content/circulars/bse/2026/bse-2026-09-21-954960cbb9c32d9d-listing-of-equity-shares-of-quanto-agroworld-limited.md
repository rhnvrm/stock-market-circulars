## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, September 22, 2026, the equity shares of Quanto Agroworld Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: QUANTO AGROWORLD LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544932
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: QAL
- ISIN No.: INE0OAB01019
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 67/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 57/-)
- Total Securities: 17,487,259 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 2,000 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- In terms of SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, the scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that securities shall be traded only in dematerialised form.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed to have the lock-in period marked in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 22, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Quanto Agroworld Limited shares on the BSE SME segment with standard market lot and settlement parameters.
