## Summary

Trading members are informed that new securities of Midwest Energy Limited (Scrip Code - 526570) consisting of 9,24,250 equity shares issued on a preferential basis are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Tuesday, September 15, 2026.

- Company Name: Midwest Energy Limited
- Scrip Code: 526570
- ISIN: INE519N01014
- Number of Shares: 9,24,250 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1990/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 2000/-
- Effective Date: September 15, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 12047947 to 12972196
