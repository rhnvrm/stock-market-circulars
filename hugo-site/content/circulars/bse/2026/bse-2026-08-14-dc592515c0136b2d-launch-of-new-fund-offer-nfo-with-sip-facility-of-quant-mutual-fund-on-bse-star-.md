## Summary

Quant Mutual Fund is launching the QUANT INCOME PLUS ARBITRAGE ACTIVE FOF NFO, along with an SIP facility, on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period is from August 17, 2026, to August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: QUANT INCOME PLUS ARBITRAGE ACTIVE FOF (Regular and Direct plans including Growth and IDCW options).
- Subscription Window: August 17, 2026, to August 31, 2026 (extended till midnight on August 31, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 04, 2026.
- ISINs: INF966L01EM7, INF966L01EN5, INF966L01EO3, INF966L01EJ3, INF966L01EK1, INF966L01EL9.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 04, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on August 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 17, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: August 31, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the new debt fund scheme via the BSE StAR MF platform.
