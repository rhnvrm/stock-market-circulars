## Summary

Effective from Thursday, August 6, 2026, the equity shares of Dhaval Packaging Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Dhaval Packaging Limited
- Scrip Code: 544854
- ISIN: INE1HX301016
- Issue Price: Rs. 97/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 87/-)
- Total Securities: 13,736,800 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Segment: SME ('MT' Group)

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Scrip placed in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in the minimum market lot of 1,200 shares.
- Necessary corporate action executed by the company to mark lock-in periods in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 5, 2026
- Allotment Date: August 4, 2026
- Listing / Effective Date: August 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Dhaval Packaging Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with standard regulatory lock-ins and trade-for-trade monitoring.
