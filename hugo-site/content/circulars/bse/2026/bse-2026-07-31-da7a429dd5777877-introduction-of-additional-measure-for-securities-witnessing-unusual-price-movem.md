## Summary

BSE has carried out a review for the identification of securities witnessing unusual price movement to be placed under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework. The changes come into effect from August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Based on the review, specific securities have satisfied the criteria for inclusion under the additional surveillance measure.
- A list of securities moving out of the measure has also been identified.
- A consolidated list of all securities under the framework is provided in the annexures.
- Effective date for the inclusion and exclusion of securities is August 03, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of additional surveillance measures for securities experiencing unusual price volatility, pursuant to earlier exchange circular guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are advised to note the inclusion and exclusion lists of securities under ASM and ensure compliance with respective margin requirements and trading conditions.
- Clarifications can be sought by writing to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 July 2026
- Effective Date: 03 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in affected securities may be subject to enhanced surveillance margins or trading controls as stipulated under the ASM framework, potentially impacting short-term liquidity and volatility for the listed securities.
