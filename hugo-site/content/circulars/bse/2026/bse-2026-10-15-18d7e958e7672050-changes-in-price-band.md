## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 13 scrips with effect from October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261015-20 dated October 15, 2026.
- Price bands revised for 13 scrips across various percentages (2%, 5%, 10%, 20%).
- Effective date of revision: October 16, 2026.
- Affected scrips include Aditya Forge Limited, Billwin Industries Limited, BMW Ventures Limited, K M Sugar Mills Limited, Mawana Sugars Limited, Padmanabh Alloys & Polymers Limited, Peshwa Wheat Limited, Rajasthan Tube Manufacturing Company Limited, Riyaasat Lifestyle Limited, Roopa Screen Limited, Sejal Glass Limited, Viji Finance Limited, and VTM Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of daily operating price limits/bands for specified scrips as part of market surveillance and risk management.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while placing orders in the affected scrips from October 16, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the listed scrips will be restricted to the newly assigned price bands, curbing excessive volatility and managing market risk.
