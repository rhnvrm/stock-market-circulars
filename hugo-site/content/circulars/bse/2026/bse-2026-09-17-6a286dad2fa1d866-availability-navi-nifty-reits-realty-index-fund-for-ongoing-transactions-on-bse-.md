## Summary

Navi Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund (Regular and Direct Plans - Growth) shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes reopening: NAVI NIFTY REITS & REALTY INDEX FUND - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF959L01HU8) and NAVI NIFTY REITS & REALTY INDEX FUND - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF959L01HT0).
- Facilities available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Effective Date: September 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants must take note of the availability of these schemes for ongoing transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute transactions and systematic investment plans for the specified index fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.
