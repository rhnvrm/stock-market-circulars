## Summary

Effective from Friday, October 23, 2026, equity shares of Tonbo Imaging India Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities under Scrip Code 544999.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Tonbo Imaging India Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544999
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** TONBO
- **ISIN:** INE0LDG01026
- **Number of Securities:** 57,282,100 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each fully paid up
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 700/- per share (Face Value Rs. 2/-, Premium Rs. 698/-)
- **Date of Allotment:** October 21, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Registrar:** KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing and admission to dealings under SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 23, 2026.
- Corporate action executed for lock-in period marking in depository's records.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 21, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Tonbo Imaging India Limited shares on the BSE main board, providing liquidity to shareholders and market participants.
