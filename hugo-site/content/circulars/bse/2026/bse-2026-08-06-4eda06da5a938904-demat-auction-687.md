## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-687/2026-27. The notice outlines the precise timings for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and final reports scheduled for August 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement No.: DR-687/2026-27.
- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on August 6, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on August 6, 2026.
- Pay-in of the auction scheduled for August 7, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- Removal of restriction on trading members offering securities in auctions where they had short delivered.

## Regulatory Changes

- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is now removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- Members should refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the "Auction offer Entry Screen" and monitor corporate action notices.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 6, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: August 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational circular ensuring smooth settlement operations and providing clarity on auction timelines and clearing procedures for market participants.
