## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the transfer of debt securities from Aerogrid Advanced Hosting Solutions Private Limited (formerly known as Alotronix Warehousing Fourteen Private Limited) to South City Projects (Kolkata) Private Limited, effective July 29, 2026, pursuant to a Scheme of Arrangement.

## Key Points

- Scrips of Aerogrid Advanced Hosting Solutions Private Limited are transferred to South City Projects (Kolkata) Private Limited.
- Effective date of transfer: Wednesday, July 29, 2026.
- Pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement under Sections 230 to 232 read with Section 66 of the Companies Act, 2013.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the transfer of scrips under the Scheme of Arrangement.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 27, 2026
- **Effective Date:** July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading members holding or dealing with the specified debt securities.
