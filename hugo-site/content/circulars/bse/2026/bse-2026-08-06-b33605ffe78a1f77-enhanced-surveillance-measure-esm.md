## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying criteria for inclusion, movement to lower stages (such as Stage I), shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment, and revised margin requirements effective August 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 10, 2026, on open positions as on August 07, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Selected securities are moved to lower Stage I under the ESM framework w.e.f. August 07, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction where applicable.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely for market surveillance and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework provisions in conjunction with prevailing measures.
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement terms for short-listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for margins and trade-for-trade segment: August 10, 2026
- Review/Movement Date: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations and trade-for-trade restrictions affect liquidity and trading mechanics for the specified securities.
