## Summary

The Exchange has issued a notice regarding the implementation of Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act, 1967, following a communication from SEBI. The notice details updates to the UNSC's 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) & Al-Qaida Sanctions List (amendment of 2 entries) and the UNSC’s 1988 (2011) Taliban Sanctions List (amendment of 1 entry).

## Key Points

- Updates to UNSC's 1267/1989 ISIL & Al-Qaida Sanctions List (2 entries amended).
- Updates to UNSC’s 1988 (2011) Taliban Sanctions List (1 entry amended).
- Members are required to scan existing and future accounts to ensure compliance with asset freezing and financial service denial measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Section 51A of UAPA, 1967 based on SEBI communication and UNSC Committee notifications.
- Adherence to Central Government Order dated Feb 02, 2021.

## Compliance Requirements

- Scan all existing accounts and future accounts to ensure no linkage to listed entities or individuals as per clause 54 of SEBI AML/CFT Guidelines (June 06, 2024).
- Forward any de-listing requests electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA and Joint Secretary (UNP), MEA.
- Inform individuals/entities seeking removal about the UN Ombudsperson application process.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 11 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Intermediaries must immediately screen client databases against the updated UNSC sanctions lists to prevent any prohibited transactions or relationships.
