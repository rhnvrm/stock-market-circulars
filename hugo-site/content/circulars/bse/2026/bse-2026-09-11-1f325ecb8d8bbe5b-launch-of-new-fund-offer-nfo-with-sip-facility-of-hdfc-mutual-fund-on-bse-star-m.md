## Summary

HDFC Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for HDFC BSE REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund along with an SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from September 15, 2026, to September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes include various regular and direct plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment) under ISINs INF179KC1KU2, INF179KC1KV0, INF179KC1KW8, INF179KC1KX6, INF179KC1KY4, and INF179KC1KZ1.
- Tentative allotment date is set for September 22, 2026.
- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 22, 2026.
- Cheque, OTM, and switch payments are subject to standard NFO guidelines and timely fund receipt.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 15, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 17, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates trading and subscription of HDFC Mutual Fund's new REIT and commercial real estate index fund scheme for market participants and investors on the BSE StAR MF platform.
