## Summary

Effective Wednesday, September 16, 2026, the equity shares of CFF Fluid Controls Limited, currently listed on the BSE SME Platform, shall be migrated and admitted to dealings on the BSE Mainboard Platform in the 'B' Group.

## Key Points

- Company Name: CFF Fluid Controls Limited
- Scrip Code: 543920
- ISIN: INE0NJ001013
- Market Lot: 1 share
- Group: 'B'
- Total Securities: 2,09,74,100 Equity Shares of Re. 1/- each fully paid-up

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded on the BSE Mainboard Platform following migration from the SME platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- As per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, securities will be traded only in dematerialized form.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Migration: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced visibility and liquidity for shareholders as the company moves from the SME platform to the Mainboard.
