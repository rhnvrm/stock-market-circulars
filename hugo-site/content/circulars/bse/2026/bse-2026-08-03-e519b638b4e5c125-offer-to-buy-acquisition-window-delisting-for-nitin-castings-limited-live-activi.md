## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the schedule of activities for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Delisting) of shares of Nitin Castings Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from Wednesday, August 5, 2026, to Tuesday, August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: NITIN CASTINGS LIMITED
- Security Symbol: NITINCAST
- ISIN: INE861H01020
- Scrip Code: 508875
- Offer Size: Up to 14,70,894 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 273.36
- Opening Date: Wednesday, 5th August 2026
- Closing Date: Tuesday, 11th August 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the UCC of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors having shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Bidding Opening Date: 5th August 2026
- Bidding Closing Date: 11th August 2026
- Upper Modification and Cancellation: 5th August 2026 to 7th August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates shareholders and trading members to participate in the delisting offer-to-buy mechanism for Nitin Castings Limited within the specified schedule.
