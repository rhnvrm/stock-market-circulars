## Summary

BSE has announced the Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) available to Clients, Stock Brokers/Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) - Category I & II, and Mutual Funds for stock derivatives contracts of newly introduced scrips in the Equity Derivatives Segment, effective August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Applicable w.e.f. August 26, 2026, pursuant to prior notices.
- Covers newly introduced scrips: Ather Energy Ltd, Bank of Maharashtra, and Sagility Ltd.
- Specifies distinct limits for market-wide shares, scheme of mutual funds, TM proprietary, and TM (Proprietary + Client) / FPI (Cat I) / MF.

## Regulatory Changes

Modifies position limit allocations for newly introduced equity derivatives contracts as per ongoing market surveillance and risk management frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, brokers, and institutional investors must adhere to the prescribed position limits for the listed scrips to avoid penalty or regulatory breach.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts risk management and position sizing for participants trading derivatives in Ather Energy Ltd, Bank of Maharashtra, and Sagility Ltd.
