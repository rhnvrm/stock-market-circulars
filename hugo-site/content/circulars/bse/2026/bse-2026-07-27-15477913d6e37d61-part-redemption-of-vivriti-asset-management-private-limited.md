## Summary

Vivriti Asset Management Private Limited has announced a partial redemption of its Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs), resulting in a reduced face value. Trading members are informed that the debentures will be traded with the revised face value starting July 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Vivriti Asset Management Pvt Ltd
- **Purpose:** Part Redemption of debentures
- **Scrip Code / ISIN:** 975094 / INE0BXI07010 (VAMPL-11%-15-1-27-PVT)
- **Reduced Face Value:** Rs. 35,483.87 per debenture
- **Effective Date:** July 28, 2026 (DR-681/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the reduced face value and ensure trading occurs accordingly from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 27 Jul 2026
- **Effective Date:** 28 Jul 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for market participants holding or trading the specified NCDs of Vivriti Asset Management Private Limited.
