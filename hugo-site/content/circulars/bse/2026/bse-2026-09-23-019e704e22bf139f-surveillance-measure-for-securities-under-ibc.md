## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The framework becomes effective from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are listed in Annexure I.
- List of securities moving out of the IBC Framework is provided in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- List of securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% is given in Annexure IV.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities meeting the criteria are placed under the Additional Surveillance Measure framework for companies relating to IRP under IBC.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their original bands, subject to not being part of any other surveillance measure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are advised to take note of the securities placed under or moving out of the IBC framework.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2026
- Notice Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors dealing in securities under the IBC surveillance framework will experience applicable trading controls, monitoring, and price band restrictions.
