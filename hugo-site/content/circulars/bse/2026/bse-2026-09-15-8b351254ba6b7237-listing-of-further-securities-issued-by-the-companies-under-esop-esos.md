## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued by five companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the exchange with effect from Wednesday, September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** September 16, 2026
- **Companies Involved:**
  1. Aptus Value Housing Finance India Limited (ISIN: INE852O01025, Scrip Code: 543335, Shares: 12,500)
  2. ICICI Bank Ltd. (ISIN: INE090A01021, Scrip Code: 532174, Shares: 336,265)
  3. Meesho Limited (ISIN: INEVDM01015, Scrip Code: 544632, Shares: 1,352,253)
  4. S.J.S. Enterprises Limited (ISIN: INE284S01014, Scrip Code: 543387, Shares: 222,875)
  5. Wipro Ltd. (ISIN: INE075A01022, Scrip Code: 507685, Shares: 264,143)
- **Lock-in Status:** All listed shares are marked as 'NA' under lock-in for these specific ranges.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine corporate action notice regarding the listing of shares issued under ESOP/ESOS.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the listing of these securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 15, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing of ESOP shares with minimal direct market impact.
