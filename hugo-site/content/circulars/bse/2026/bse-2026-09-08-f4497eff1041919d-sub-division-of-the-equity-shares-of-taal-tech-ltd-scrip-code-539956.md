## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that TAAL Tech Ltd has fixed a record date for the sub-division of its equity shares. Each existing equity share with a face value of Rs. 10/- will be sub-divided into five equity shares with a face value of Rs. 2/- each, effective from September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TAAL Tech Ltd
- Scrip Code: 539956
- Old Face Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- New Face Value: Rs. 2/- per share
- Ratio: 1 equity share of Rs. 10/- into 5 equity shares of Rs. 2/-
- Record Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Existing ISIN (INE524T01011) of Rs. 10/- paid up will cease to be valid for transactions on the Exchange on or after September 22, 2026.
- A separate notice will be issued to inform the market regarding the new ISIN number for the Rs. 2/- paid-up shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the change in face value, ISIN invalidation, and update their systems accordingly to handle transactions post-record date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date (Sub-Division w.e.f.): September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders will receive five shares of Rs. 2/- face value for every single share held of Rs. 10/- face value as of the record date. Trading volumes and liquidity may see adjustments typical of stock split events.
