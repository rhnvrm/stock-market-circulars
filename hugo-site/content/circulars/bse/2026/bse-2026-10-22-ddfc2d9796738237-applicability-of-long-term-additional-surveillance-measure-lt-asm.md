## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies. The measures include 100% margin requirements, movement to higher surveillance stages, lower price band restrictions, and transfers to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS).

## Key Points

- **100% Margins:** Applicable w.e.f. October 27, 2026 on all open positions as on October 26, 2026 and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- **Higher Stages & Price Bands:** Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands effective October 23, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- **Group Transfers:** Securities continuing in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 27, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- **Exits:** List of securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework w.e.f. October 23, 2026 (Annexure II).
- **Price Band Reinstatement:** Scrips moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated unless subject to other surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised LT-ASM framework rules involving mandatory 100% margin deposits, price band restrictions, and segment/group transfers for flagged securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on applicable open and new positions, and take note of lower price bands and group transfers for impacted scrips.

## Important Dates

- **October 23, 2026:** Effective date for movement to higher stages, lower price bands, and exiting the LT-ASM framework.
- **October 26, 2026:** Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- **October 27, 2026:** Effective date for 100% margins and transfer to T / XT / MT / TS groups.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and leverage for securities listed under Annexure I will be restricted due to 100% margin requirements and lower price bands, while securities exiting the framework will resume normal trading parameters.
