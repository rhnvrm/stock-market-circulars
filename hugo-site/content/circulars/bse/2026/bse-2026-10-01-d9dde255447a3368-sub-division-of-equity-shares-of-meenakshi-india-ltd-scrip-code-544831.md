## Summary

Meenakshi India Ltd (Scrip Code: 544831) has informed the Exchange that it has fixed October 30, 2026, as the Record Date for the sub-division of existing equity shares. The existing equity share of face value of Rs. 10/- each will be sub-divided into two equity shares of face value of Rs. 5/- each.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Meenakshi India Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 544831
- **Old ISIN:** INE208H01016 (valid up to October 29, 2026)
- **New ISIN:** To be informed separately by the Exchange
- **Record Date:** October 30, 2026
- **Sub-Division Ratio:** One equity share of Rs. 10/- each into Two equity shares of Rs. 5/- each

## Regulatory Changes

- Capital restructuring through the sub-division of equity shares.
- Invalidation of old ISIN INE208H01016 for settlement and trading on the Exchange on or after the record date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised that transactions on the Exchange on or after October 30, 2026, cannot be conducted using the old ISIN (INE208H01016).
- The new ISIN number for the Rs. 5/- paid-up shares will be notified separately.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date / Effective Date:** October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders as of the record date will see their holding adjusted with two Rs. 5/- shares for every single Rs. 10/- share held.
- Market operations and dematerialised rolling settlement segments will update scrip master details accordingly.
