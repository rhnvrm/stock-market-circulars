## Summary

Quantum Flexi Cap Fund is reopening for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and related facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 11, 2026, pursuant to a previous notice dated August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopening covers regular and direct growth plans.
- Facilities available include subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Applicable schemes include QUANTUM FLEXI CAP FUND - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF082J01515) and QUANTUM FLEXI CAP FUND - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF082J01507).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the scheme on the BSE StAR MF Platform in accordance with existing operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Reference Notice Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume regular investment and redemption transactions in the Quantum Flexi Cap Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
