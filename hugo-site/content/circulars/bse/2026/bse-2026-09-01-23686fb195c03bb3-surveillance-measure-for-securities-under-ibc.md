## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application and review of Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are placed under Additional Surveillance Measure effective September 02, 2026.
- A list of securities moving out of the IBC framework is provided in Annexure II.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is attached as Annexure III.
- The price band of scrips moving out of the framework shall be reinstated subject to other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities under the IRP framework are subjected to periodic review under market surveillance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the inclusion and exclusion of securities under the IBC surveillance framework in conjunction with other prevailing measures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 02, 2026
- Notice Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in securities identified under the IBC framework is subject to specific surveillance controls and price bands.
