## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited effective from Monday, August 24, 2026. The shares will be traded in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited
- Scrip Code: 544879
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: LALITHAA
- ISIN No.: INE0K9O01026
- Total Securities: 55,97,09,811 Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 201/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 5/- and premium of Rs. 196/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 20, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action has been executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository's records for shares in demat form.
- Trading members should note the scrip will be a part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on August 24, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 20, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members can trade in the equity shares of Lalithaa Jewellery Mart Limited starting from August 24, 2026, under the 'B' group segment.
