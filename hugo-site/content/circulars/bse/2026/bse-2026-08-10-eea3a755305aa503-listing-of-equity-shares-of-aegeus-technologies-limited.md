## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, August 11, 2026, the equity shares of Aegeus Technologies Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE SME segment in the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544858
- Scrip ID: AEGEUS
- ISIN: INE0WR901013
- Number of Securities: 8,374,593 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 105/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 95/-)
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: August 7, 2026
- Market Maker: Prabhat Financial Services Limited
- Registrar: Skyline Financial Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the trade-for-trade segment restriction and market lot requirements.
- Necessary corporate actions for lock-in marking have been executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: August 11, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members can trade in the newly listed SME shares subject to the initial Trade-for-Trade segment rules and market lot size of 1,200 shares.
