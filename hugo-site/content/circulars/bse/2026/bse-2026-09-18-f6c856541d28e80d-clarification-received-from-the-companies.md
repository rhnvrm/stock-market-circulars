## Summary

The Exchange has noted significant price and volume movements in specific scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, BSE sought clarification from several companies. Replies have been received from a set of companies, while responses from others are still awaited.

## Key Points

- **Surveillance Action:** BSE Surveillance department tracked significant price/volume movements.
- **Replies Received From:** Astec Lifesciences Ltd, Karbonsteel Engineering Ltd, Magnus Steel and Infra Ltd, Mahan Industries Ltd, Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Ltd, Tega Industries Ltd, and Yuranus Infrastructure Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited From:** Alacrity Securities Ltd, Annvrridhhi Ventures Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Jhandewalas Foods Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Mopshop Distribution Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, and Sera Investments & Finance India Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely clarify significant market movements in price and volume upon exchange inquiry.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 18 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors should monitor disclosures from the listed companies whose replies are awaited or review the clarifications provided by companies that have responded.
