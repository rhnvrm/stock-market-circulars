## Summary

Minolta Finance Limited has extended the schedule for its ongoing rights issue and the associated on-market renunciation period, following notice no. 20260729-4.

## Key Points

- The ongoing Rights Issue closing date has been extended from Monday, August 17, 2026, to Thursday, August 20, 2026.
- The last date for On-Market Renunciation for Rights Entitlements has been extended from Tuesday, August 11, 2026, to Friday, August 14, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must take note of the revised timelines for participation and renunciation.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: Thursday, July 30, 2026
- Last Date for On-Market Renunciation: Friday, August 14, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: Thursday, August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members additional time to execute rights entitlements and subscribe to the issue.
