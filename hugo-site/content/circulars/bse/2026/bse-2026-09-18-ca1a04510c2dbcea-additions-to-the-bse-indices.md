## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of Veegaland Developers Limited and several SME platform companies to its indices following their listings on the exchange in September 2026.

## Key Points

- Veegaland Developers Limited (Ticker: 544923) is added to the BSE IPO index effective September 22, 2026.
- Four SME companies—Om Galaxy Limited (544922), Raksan Transformers Limited (544924), Maharaja & Speedex India Limited (544925), and Panchatv Bharat Limited (544926)—are added to the BSE SME IPO index effective September 21, 2026.
- These changes are reflected in the end-of-day corporate action files.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for market participants; informational notice regarding index composition changes.

## Important Dates

- September 18, 2026: Notice issuance date.
- September 21, 2026: Effective date for BSE SME IPO index additions.
- September 22, 2026: Effective date for BSE IPO index addition.

## Impact Assessment

Index funds and tracking portfolios tracking the BSE IPO and BSE SME IPO indices will adjust their holdings to include the newly added stocks.
