## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 10,77,105 new equity shares of Kesar India Limited (Scrip Code: 543542) effective from October 12, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Number of shares: 10,77,105 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 340/- (Issue Price: Rs. 350/-).
- Allottees: Promoter and Non-Promoter categories pursuant to warrant conversion.
- Effective Trading Date: October 12, 2026.
- ISIN: INE0L1C01019.
- Lock-in Details:
  - 8,00,000 shares (Distinctive Nos. 30171401 - 30971400) locked in up to April 15, 2028.
  - 2,77,105 shares (Distinctive Nos. 30971401 - 31248505) locked in up to April 15, 2027.
