## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from September 29, 2026. Trading members and market participants are required to take note of the applicable surveillance actions and margin requirements.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure will move to higher stages of GSM effective September 29, 2026.
- Actions across stages range from 100% margin rates and price bands of 5% or lower (Stage I) to trade-for-trade settlement, additional surveillance deposits (ASD), and weekly trading restrictions (Stages II, III, and IV).
- Settlement for affected securities shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of stricter surveillance frameworks (GSM Stages I to IV) as per provisions established on February 23, 2017, and subsequent updates.
- Imposition of Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) ranging from 50% to 100% of trade value for buyers in higher stages.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in securities listed in the annexure.
- Ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules and timely deposit of required margins and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Refer to BSE FAQs on GSM for operational clarifications.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 September 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Actions: 29 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affected securities will experience restricted liquidity and tighter price bands.
- Market participants holding or trading these securities face increased capital requirements due to mandatory 100% margins or ASD and trade-for-trade settlement.
