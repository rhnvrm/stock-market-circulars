## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from August 06, 2026. Trading members are required to note the specific surveillance actions applicable to each stage.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 06, 2026.
- Notice covers securities moving to higher stages of the GSM framework based on earlier established guidelines.
- Surveillance actions vary across stages (Stage I to IV), including 100% margin rates, price bands of 5% or lower, Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD), and trade-for-trade settlement with restricted trading frequency.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures as per the provisions of the GSM Framework published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent updates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in the affected securities.
- Settlement for these securities shall be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05 Aug 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions: 06 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trading limitations for securities placed under higher GSM stages, impacting liquidity and trading volume for the affected scrips.
