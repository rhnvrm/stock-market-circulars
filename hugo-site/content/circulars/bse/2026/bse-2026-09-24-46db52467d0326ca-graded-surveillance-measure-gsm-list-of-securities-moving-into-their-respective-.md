## Summary

BSE has announced that specified securities will be moved to higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) effective September 25, 2026. These securities will attract enhanced surveillance actions as per the GSM framework.

## Key Points

- Securities affected are listed in the attached Annexure_GSM of the notice.
- Effective date for movement to higher GSM stages is September 25, 2026.
- Trading will attract strict surveillance measures depending on the assigned GSM stage (Stages I to IV).
- Settlement for affected securities will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of higher GSM stages leading to increased surveillance actions.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value by buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and ASD of 100% of trade value with no upward movement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities.
- Ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules where no netting off is permitted.
- Ensure required margins and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) are deposited by buyers.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities may be significantly restricted due to trade-for-trade settlement and higher margin/deposit requirements.
- Market participants holding or trading these securities face heightened operational and financial requirements.
