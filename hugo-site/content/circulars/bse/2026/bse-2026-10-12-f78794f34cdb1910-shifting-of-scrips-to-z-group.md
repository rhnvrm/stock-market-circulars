## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the shifting of specified scrips to the 'Z' group with effect from October 23, 2026. This action is taken pursuant to SEBI Master Circular provisions and standard operating procedures for penal actions due to non-compliance with Regulation 33 (non-submission of financial results) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (March 2026 and June 2026).

## Key Points

- Scrips failing to comply on or before October 20, 2026, will be transferred to the 'Z' group effective October 23, 2026.
- Affected companies include Hampton Sky Realty Ltd, Harshil Agrotech Ltd, Kallam Textiles Ltd, Spicejet Ltd, Parsvnath Developers Ltd, and others.
- Certain other listed entities are also non-compliant for Regulation 33 for the same quarters but are already placed in the 'Z' group.
- Non-compliance pertains to the non-submission of financial results for March 2026 and June 2026 quarters.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of penal actions under SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 and SEBI LODR Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies must submit their financial results for the required quarters (March 2026 and June 2026) under Regulation 33 of SEBI LODR Regulations on or before October 20, 2026, to avoid transfer to the 'Z' group.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Compliance Deadline:** October 20, 2026
- **Effective Date for 'Z' Group Transfer:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Transfer to the 'Z' group imposes severe trading restrictions, heightened investor caution, and regulatory scrutiny on the affected listed entities, impacting liquidity and market operations.
