## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for several scrips effective from July 29, 2026, as part of surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260728-34
- Effective Date: July 29, 2026
- Total Scrips Affected: 9
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance)

## Regulatory Changes

Price bands for the mentioned scrips have been revised from their existing levels:
- Aastha Spintex Ltd (544808): 10%
- Amalgamated Electricity Company Ltd (501622): 2%
- Capricorn Systems Global Solutions Ltd (512169): 2%
- Cargotrans Maritime Ltd (543618): 5%
- Cubical Financial Services Ltd (511710): 2%
- Fairchem Organics Ltd (543252): 2%
- Inland Printers Ltd (530787): 2%
- Raw Edge Industrial Solutions Ltd (541634): 5%
- Usg Tech Solutions Ltd (532402): 2%

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands while placing orders in the respective scrips to avoid order rejections or compliance issues.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 Jul 2026
- Effective Date: 29 Jul 2026

## Impact Assessment

Limits price volatility for the affected scrips in accordance with exchange surveillance frameworks.
