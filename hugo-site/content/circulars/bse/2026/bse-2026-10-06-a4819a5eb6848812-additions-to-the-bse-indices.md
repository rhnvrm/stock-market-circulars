## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of SRIT India Limited and three SME IPO companies—Acme Universal Safezone 9 Limited, Pind Hospitality Limited, and Shivchem Agro Limited—to respective BSE indices following their listing on the exchange.

## Key Points

- SRIT India Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544965) is added to the BSE IPO index effective at the open of Thursday, October 08, 2026.
- Acme Universal Safezone 9 Limited (544964), Pind Hospitality Limited (544966), and Shivchem Agro Limited (544967) are added to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open of Wednesday, October 07, 2026.
- All actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine index constituent update based on new listings.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, index funds, and investors tracking BSE indices are required to update their benchmark portfolios accordingly.

## Important Dates

- October 06, 2026: Notice issuance date.
- October 07, 2026: Effective date for SME IPO index additions.
- October 08, 2026: Effective date for SRIT India Limited index addition.

Ensures accurate representation of newly listed mainboard and SME equities in BSE index portfolios.
