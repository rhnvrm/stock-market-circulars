## Summary

BSE has issued a notice urging all remaining members operating on the legacy BSE StAR MF platform to initiate and complete their migration to BSE StAR MF Version 2.0 at the earliest. The next-generation platform offers improved performance, enhanced functionality, greater scalability, and a future-ready architecture.

## Key Points

- BSE StAR MF Version 2.0 is designed to handle continuous growth in transaction volumes, increasing digital adoption, and evolving business requirements.
- Several existing members have already completed their migration, and new members are actively transacting on the new platform.
- Members currently using the older platform infrastructure are requested to transition promptly to leverage enhanced platform capabilities.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Members operating on BSE StAR MF must initiate the migration process to BSE StAR MF Version 2.0. For assistance, members can contact the Mutual Fund Helpdesk or raise queries through the CRS Portal (https://bsecrs.bseindia.com).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless operations, improved transaction processing speeds, and enhanced user experience for mutual fund members upon migrating to the Version 2.0 infrastructure.
