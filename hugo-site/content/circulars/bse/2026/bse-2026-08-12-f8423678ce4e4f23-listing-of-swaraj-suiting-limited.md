## Summary

BSE has informed Trading Members that effective from Thursday, August 13, 2026, the equity shares of Swaraj Suiting Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the list of 'B' Group of Securities. A total of 2,64,18,260 equity shares of Rs. 10 each fully paid up will be traded in dematerialized form with a unit market lot of 1 (One) share.

## Key Points

- **Company Name**: Swaraj Suiting Limited
- **Scrip Code**: 544861
- **Scrip ID (BOLT System)**: SWARAJ
- **ISIN No.**: INE0GMR01016
- **Total Shares**: 2,64,18,260 (Distinctive Nos. 1 to 26418260)
- **Market Lot**: 1 (One) share
- **Group**: 'B' Group of Securities
- **Registrar to the Issue**: Bigshare Services Private Limited
- **Registered Office**: F 483 to 487, RIICO, Growth Centre, Hamirgarh, Bhilwara – 311025 Rajasthan
- **Pari-Passu**: All shares rank pari-passu, including dividend entitlement.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced. The listing aligns with existing SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, mandating that the securities of the company can only be traded in dematerialized form.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Dematerialized Trading Only**: Trading members must trade Swaraj Suiting Limited shares solely in dematerialized form.
- **Lock-in Period Status**: For physical shares, share certificates have been enfaced with non-transferability. For dematerialized shares, corporate actions have been executed to mark lock-in periods within the depositories' records.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: Wednesday, August 12, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date**: Thursday, August 13, 2026
- **Financial Quarter Ended June 30, 2026**: Audited financial results are enclosed as Annexure-I in the notice.
- **Annual Report Year 2024-25**: Enclosed as Annexure-II.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. The circular impacts the trading availability of Swaraj Suiting Limited shares. It does not impose general regulatory burdens or system changes on other market participants.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Trading terminals and broker master databases need to be updated to support the new Scrip Code 544861 and Scrip ID SWARAJ in Group B.
