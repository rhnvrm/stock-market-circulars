## Summary

 BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of specified companies due to the fixation of record dates for payment of interest and redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures and bonds of Manipal Hospitals Private Limited and Sammaan Capital Limited as specified.
- Record dates and ex-dates are set for early September 2026.
- Affected securities include various NCDs and bonds.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from trading in the specified debentures and bonds on and from the respective ex-dates/record dates as applicable.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / Ex-Date for Manipal Hospitals Private Limited: 01/09/2026
- Record Date / Ex-Date for Sammaan Capital Limited: 11/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of trading in the listed debentures and bonds of the mentioned entities to facilitate interest payment and redemption processing.
