## Summary

Shriram Mutual Fund is launching the Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF NFO along with an SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from September 11, 2026, to September 24, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF (Regular and Direct Plans for Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- NFO Window: September 11, 2026, to September 24, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 28, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 28, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on September 24, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: September 11, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: September 24, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors access to subscription and SIP registrations for the new Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF scheme through the BSE StAR MF platform.
