## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 1,17,15,000 new equity shares of Mardia Samyoung Capillary Tubes Company Limited (Scrip Code: 513544) effective from September 29, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Mardia Samyoung Capillary Tubes Company Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 513544
- **ISIN:** INE277E01026
- **Securities Issued:** 1,17,15,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 3.50/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 13.50/-).
- **Effective Date:** Trading permitted from September 29, 2026.
- **Lock-in Details:** 
  - 78,15,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 67746411 to 75561410) locked-in up to 31/03/2027.
  - 39,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 75561411 to 79461410) locked-in up to 31/03/2027.
