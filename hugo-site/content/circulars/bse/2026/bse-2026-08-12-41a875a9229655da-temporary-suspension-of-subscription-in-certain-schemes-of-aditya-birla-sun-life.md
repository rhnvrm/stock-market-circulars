## Summary

Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund has temporarily suspended fresh lumpsum investments (including fresh and additional purchases), switch-ins, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in) under certain mutual fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform. This suspension is effective from the close of business on August 11, 2026 (active on the platform starting August 12, 2026).

## Key Points

- **Suspension of New Purchases:** All fresh lumpsum investments, additional purchases, switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in) are disabled for the affected schemes as listed in the Annexure.
- **Cut-off and Processing:** Applications received after the applicable cut-off time on August 11, 2026, will not be accepted or processed.
- **Existing Systematic Registrations:** All existing SIP and STP-in registrations registered prior to the effective date on the BSE StAR MF platform will continue to be processed without any change at the AMC/RTA end.
- **Redemptions and Outflows:** Redemptions, switch-outs, STP-outs, and systematic withdrawal plans (SWP) remain fully allowed and unaffected.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes from SEBI are introduced, but the action is taken following a request and notice-cum-addendum from Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Mutual Fund Distributors/Members:** Members of the BSE StAR MF platform must note the suspended schemes and advise investors accordingly. No new lumpsum, switch-in, or systematic registrations should be executed for the specified schemes on or after August 12, 2026.
- **Systems Update:** BSE has disabled the respective transaction options on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Suspension:** Close of business on August 11, 2026 (effective for transactions on August 12, 2026).
- **Cut-off Time for Last Accepted Applications:** Applicable cut-off time on August 11, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** High for distributors and investors utilizing the BSE StAR MF platform who intend to make new investments or register new systematic plans in the affected Aditya Birla Sun Life MF schemes.
- **Financial/Market Impact:** Limited to the specific mutual fund schemes under suspension, with existing systematic investments and all redemption channels remaining operational.
