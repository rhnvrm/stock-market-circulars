## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I attract a 50% margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage II attract a 100% margin rate or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%).
- Surveillance actions apply to all open positions as on September 28, 2026, and new positions created from September 29, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II margin restrictions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the specified securities.
- Queries regarding surveillance can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 28, 2026
- Notice Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I and II will experience increased capital requirements due to higher margin obligations (up to 100%), impacting liquidity and trading strategies in the affected scrips.
