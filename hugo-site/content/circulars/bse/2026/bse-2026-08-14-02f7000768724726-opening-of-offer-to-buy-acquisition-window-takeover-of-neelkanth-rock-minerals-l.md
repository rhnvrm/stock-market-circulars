## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the offer to buy (acquisition window) for the takeover of Neelkanth Rock-Minerals Limited. An open offer is made by Mr. Sesha Sai Nikhil Chintalapati to acquire up to 13,11,362 fully paid equity shares (26.00% of voting share capital) at Rs. 19.40 per share.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: Mr. Sesha Sai Nikhil Chintalapati
- Target Company: Neelkanth Rock-Minerals Limited
- Offer Price: Rs. 19.40/- per equity share payable in cash
- Offer Size: Up to 13,11,362 fully paid equity shares (26.00% of voting share capital)
- Offer Period: Tuesday, August 18, 2026, to Tuesday, September 01, 2026 (excluding SEBI Holiday on Wednesday, August 26, 2026)
- Regulations: Pursuant to Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011

## Regulatory Changes

None. The offer follows standard guidelines under SEBI circulars for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to tender offers.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must follow the revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through stock exchange pursuant to tender offers under takeover, buyback, and delisting.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 14, 2026
- Offer Opening Date: August 18, 2026
- SEBI Holiday (Exclusion): August 26, 2026
- Offer Closing Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public shareholders of Neelkanth Rock-Minerals Limited an exit opportunity via the acquisition window on the stock exchange during the specified tender offer period.
