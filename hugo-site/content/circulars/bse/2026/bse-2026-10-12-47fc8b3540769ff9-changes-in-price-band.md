## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 18 scrips with effect from October 13, 2026, as part of surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 18 scrips including BN Agrochem Limited, Morepen Laboratories Limited, and others.
- Changes take effect from October 13, 2026.
- Revised price limits range across 2%, 5%, 10%, and 20% categories.

## Regulatory Changes

- Price band limits updated for specified scrips based on exchange surveillance mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trading terminals and risk management systems reflect the updated price band percentages.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Restricts excessive intraday price volatility in the affected scrips, safeguarding investors.
