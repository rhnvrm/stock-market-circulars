## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the schedule of visits by representatives of various Registrar and Transfer Agents (RTAs) to the Department of Investor Services (DIS) for the month of October 2026. Members and investors having complaints against client companies of these RTAs can approach them directly during the scheduled timings.

## Key Points

- Representatives from multiple RTAs will visit the Department of Investor Services at BSE Ltd., 1st Floor, Cama Building, Dalal Street, Mumbai–400001.
- Schedule covers dates between October 5, 2026, and October 9, 2026.
- Participating RTAs include KFin Technologies Ltd., Bigshare Services Pvt. Ltd., MCS Share Transfer Agent Ltd., MUFG Intime India Private Ltd., Universal Capital Securities P. Ltd., Sharex Dynamics India P. Ltd., TSR Darashaw Ltd, Integrated Ent. (India) Ltd., Adroit Corporate Services Pvt. Ltd., Purva Sharegistry (India) Pvt. Ltd., Datamatics Financial Services Pvt. Ltd., and Cameo Corporate Services Pvt. Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors having grievances or complaints against client companies of the listed RTAs may approach their representatives during the designated timings at the BSE investor services department.

## Important Dates

- 05-10-2026: KFin Technologies Ltd. (11:30 AM - 12:30 PM), Bigshare Services Pvt. Ltd. (02:30 PM - 03:30 PM)
- 06-10-2026: MCS Share Transfer Agent Ltd. (03:30 PM - 4:30 PM)
- 07-10-2026: MUFG Intime India Private Ltd., Universal Capital Securities P. Ltd., Sharex Dynamics India P. Ltd., TSR Darashaw Ltd (02:30 PM - 03:30 PM)
- 08-10-2026: Integrated Ent. (India) Ltd. (11:30 AM - 12:30 PM), Adroit Corporate Services Pvt. Ltd. (02:30 PM - 03:30 PM), Purva Sharegistry (India) Pvt. Ltd. (03:30 PM - 4:30 PM)
- 09-10-2026: Datamatics Financial Services Pvt. Ltd. (02:30 PM - 03:30 PM), Cameo Corporate Services Pvt. Ltd. (03:30 PM - 4:30 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates direct resolution of investor grievances by connecting investors with the concerned Registrar and Transfer Agents at the exchange premises.
