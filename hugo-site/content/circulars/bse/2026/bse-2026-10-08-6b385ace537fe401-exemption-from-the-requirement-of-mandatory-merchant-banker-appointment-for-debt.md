## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the exemption from the requirement of mandatory merchant banker appointment for debt issued through private placement by certain listed issuers.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261008-22
- Date: 08 October 2026
- Category: Circulars / Listed Companies
- Segment: Debt
- Department: Listing / Compliance-Monitoring
- Subject: Exemption from the requirement of mandatory merchant banker appointment for debt issued through private placement by certain listed issuers.

## Regulatory Changes

Certain listed issuers are granted exemptions from appointing a mandatory merchant banker when issuing debt securities via private placement, streamlining the issuance process and reducing compliance costs.

## Compliance Requirements

Eligible listed issuers must verify their qualification for the exemption before proceeding with private placement of debt securities without a merchant banker.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 08 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reduces regulatory burden and issuance costs for eligible listed entities issuing debt through private placement.
