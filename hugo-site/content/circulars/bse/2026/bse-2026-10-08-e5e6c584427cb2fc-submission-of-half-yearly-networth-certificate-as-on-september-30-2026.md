## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has issued a notice reminding all clearing members and self-clearing members to submit their Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026, on or before November 30, 2026. Members registered with both BSECL and NSE are required to submit their certificates to NSE only, which will share the data with BSECL under the "Samuhik Prativedan Manch" mechanism. Other members must submit directly through the BSECL IEFS portal.

## Key Points

- Deadline for submitting the Half Yearly Networth Certificate as on September 30, 2026, is November 30, 2026.
- Clearing/Self-Clearing Members registered with both BSECL and NSE must submit their certificates to NSE only; NSE will share the data with BSECL.
- BSECL-only members and Professional Clearing Members must upload directly via the BSECL IEFS portal (https://iefs.bseindia.in).
- Non-submission or shortfall by the due date will attract penal consequences as specified in Annexure I.
- Issues regarding uploading should be reported to iccl.membership@bseclearing.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- Net worth requirements must follow Chapter X of the SEBI (Stockbrokers) Regulations, 2026 (Base Net worth or Variable Net worth, whichever is higher) and SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2022/73.
- Utilization of the "Samuhik Prativedan Manch" technology-based sharing mechanism for common members registered across BSECL and NSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing and self-clearing members must ensure timely computation and submission of the net worth certificate as per prescribed formats by November 30, 2026.
- Common members with NSE must submit through NSE; others must use the BSECL IEFS portal.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 8, 2026
- Net Worth As On Date: September 30, 2026
- Submission Due Date: November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and monitoring of financial strength and minimum net worth standards for all clearing and self-clearing members of BSECL.
