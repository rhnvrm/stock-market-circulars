## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) along with the SIP facility for JioBlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from October 5, 2026, to October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** JioBlackRock Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** JioBlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF (Regular Plan - Growth: INF22M001390, Direct Plan - Growth: INF22M001382)
- **NFO Window:** October 05, 2026, to October 19, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 19, 2026 for lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 23, 2026.
- **SIP Registration Deadline:** SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on October 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 23, 2026.
- Switch-out funds must be received prior to the allotment date.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** October 5, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members with operational timelines and instructions for participating in the JioBlackRock Income Plus Arbitrage Omni FOF NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
