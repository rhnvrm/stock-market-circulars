## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price/volume movement in specific scrips in the recent past and requested clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Key Points

- **Companies that submitted replies:** Ambalal Sarabhai Enterprises Ltd, Autoriders International Ltd, Choksi Asia Ltd, Kati Patang Lifestyle Ltd, Sheetals Cool Products Ltd, and The New India Assurance Company Ltd.
- **Companies with replies awaited:** Amit Securities Ltd, Aryan Share and Stock Brokers Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Crazy Snacks Ltd, Deco Mica Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Gconnect Logitech and Supply Chain Ltd, Medico Remedies Ltd, RSD Finance Ltd, Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd, and Walchand Peoplefirst Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to clarify significant price and volume movements upon inquiry by the Exchange to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 06 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and ensures investors are informed about unusual price or volume activities.
