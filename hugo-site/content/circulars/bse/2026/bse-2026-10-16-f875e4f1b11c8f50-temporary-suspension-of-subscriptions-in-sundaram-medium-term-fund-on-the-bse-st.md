## Summary

BSE StAR MF has announced the temporary suspension of fresh lump-sum investments, switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in) in Sundaram Medium Term Fund, effective from the close of business on October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh lump-sum investments (fresh and additional purchases), switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in) are disabled.
- Applications received after the applicable cut-off time on October 14, 2026, will not be accepted or processed.
- Existing SIP and STP-in registrations made before the effective date will continue to be processed normally.
- Redemption, switch-out, STP-out, and SWP transactions continue to be permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Platform-level transaction restrictions implemented pursuant to the AMC's communication regarding subscription suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must take note of the suspension when executing transactions in Sundaram Medium Term Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- Cut-off time for prior applications: October 14, 2026
- Effective date of suspension: October 15, 2026
- Notice date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors cannot make fresh investments or start new SIPs/STPs in the scheme, while existing systematic plans and exit options (redemptions, switches out) remain unaffected.
