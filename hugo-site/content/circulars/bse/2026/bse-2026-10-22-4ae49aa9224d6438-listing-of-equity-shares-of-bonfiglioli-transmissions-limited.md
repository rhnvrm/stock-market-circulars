## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Friday, October 23, 2026, the equity shares of Bonfiglioli Transmissions Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 544996
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** BONFIG
- **ISIN No.:** INE28SO01013
- **Number of Securities:** 187,995,834 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 586/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 576/-)
- **Date of Allotment:** October 21, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Group:** 'B'

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed for lock-in period marking in depository records for shares in demat form.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the inclusion of the scrip in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Friday, October 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 21, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Bonfiglioli Transmissions Limited shares on BSE, providing liquidity to investors and market participants.
