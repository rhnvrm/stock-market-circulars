## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding measures for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities listed in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective October 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Margin applies to all open positions as on October 5, 2026, and new positions created from October 6, 2026.
- Securities in Annexure II move out of the framework effective October 1, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of a minimum 75% margin on securities with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with the 75% margin requirement for affected securities across open and new positions starting October 6, 2026.

## Important Dates

- October 1, 2026: Securities in Annexure II move out of the framework.
- October 5, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to the margin requirement.
- October 6, 2026: Effective date for the 75% margin requirement on open and new positions.

## Impact Assessment

- Increases capital requirements and margin obligations for market participants trading in securities identified with high encumbrance levels, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in those scrips.
