## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the alignment of SEBI's Cyber Incident Reporting Portal with the Format for Incident Reporting Exchange (FIRE) Framework developed by the Financial Stability Board (FSB) to facilitate standardized and structured cyber incident reporting.

## Key Points

- Reference to SEBI Circular No. HO/(449)2026-ITD-5_DIV1/I/19448/2026 dated August 24, 2026.
- Adoption of the FSB's FIRE Framework for standardized cyber incident reporting.
- Regulated entities (REs) must continue reporting cyber incidents to the Exchange in accordance with existing guidelines (Exchange Circular No. 20250108-42 dated January 08, 2025) in addition to SEBI's portal.
- Annexure A includes a copy of the SEBI circular, and Annexure B contains the User Manual for the portal.

## Regulatory Changes

- Integration of the FIRE Framework into SEBI's Cyber Incident Reporting Portal for structured and standardized reporting.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated Entities (REs) are advised to take note of the alignment and ensure compliance with reporting requirements across both SEBI's portal and Exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 25, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines and standardizes cyber incident reporting for regulated entities using international frameworks, enhancing regulatory oversight.
