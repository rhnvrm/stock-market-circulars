## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the settlement programme for the Futures and Options (F&O) segment for the period starting from September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers the complete monthly settlement schedule for derivative transactions.
- Applicable to trading members participating in the BSE F&O segment.
- Reference attachment FNO092026 provides detailed timeline breakdowns.

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable (routine schedule issuance).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must align their back-office and clearing operations with the notified F&O settlement calendar for September 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective From:** September 1, 2026
- **Effective To:** September 30, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth operational planning and fund/securities pay-in and pay-out timelines for market participants trading derivatives on BSE during September 2026.
