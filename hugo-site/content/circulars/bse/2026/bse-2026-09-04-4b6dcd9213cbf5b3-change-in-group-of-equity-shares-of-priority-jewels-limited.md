## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Priority Jewels Limited will be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment under B Group, following previous notices.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Priority Jewels Limited
- Scrip Code: 544899
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: Monday, September 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares are moving out of the Trade for Trade segment into the normal rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants should note the change in segment and trading mechanism for the scrip.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading flexibility for Priority Jewels Limited equity shares are expected to improve with the shift to the rolling segment.
