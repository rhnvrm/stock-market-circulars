## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable margin rate shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%.
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026, applicable on all open positions as on September 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under the ASM framework is solely for market surveillance and is not an adverse regulatory action against the company.
- Price bands for scrips exiting the framework are reinstated unless subject to other surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of enhanced margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must maintain the prescribed margin rates (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) on all open and new positions for the affected securities starting October 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Open Positions Reference Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Higher margin requirements may lead to reduced leverage and potential liquidity impacts for traders holding positions in the shortlisted securities.
