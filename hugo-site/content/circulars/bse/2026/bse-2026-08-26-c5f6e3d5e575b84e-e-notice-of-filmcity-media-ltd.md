## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that new securities of Filmcity Media Ltd. (Scrip Code - 531486) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange effective from Thursday, August 27, 2026, following a preferential issue of 1,90,00,000 equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Filmcity Media Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 531486
- ISIN: INE600B01033
- Number of Shares: 1,90,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at par to Promoter and Non-Promoters on a preferential basis.
- Effective Date of Trading: August 27, 2026.
- Distribution Nos.: 30570970 to 49570969.
- Date of Allotment: 22/07/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to lock-in requirements as specified for the preferential shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 22/07/2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: 27/08/2026
- Lock-in upto 28/02/2027 for 1,20,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 37570970 to 49570969)
- Lock-in upto 29/02/2028 for 70,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 30570970 to 37570969)

## Impact Assessment

Permits trading of newly allotted preferential shares on the exchange while enforcing scheduled lock-in periods.
