## Summary

BSE has announced the operational details for the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Treasury Bills (T-Bills) scheduled for September 30, 2026, based on the RBI press release.

## Key Points

- Three Treasury Bills available for bidding: 91 Day T-Bill (091T301226), 182 Day T-Bill (182T310327), and 364 Day T-Bill (364T300927).
- Bidding takes place through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.
- Issue sizes are Rs. 9,000 Cr, Rs. 8,000 Cr, and Rs. 7,000 Cr respectively.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Facilitation of government securities auction as per RBI calendar.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the iBBS platform.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: September 28, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors): September 29, 2026, till 5:00 pm
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): September 30, 2026, till 8:00 am
- Auction Date: September 30, 2026
- Settlement Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables participation of retail and eligible investors in government T-Bill auctions through non-competitive bidding with minimal market impact.
