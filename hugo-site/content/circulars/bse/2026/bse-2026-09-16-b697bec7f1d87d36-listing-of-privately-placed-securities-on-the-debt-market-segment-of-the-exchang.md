## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis on the debt segment of the Exchange effective September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are admitted to trading in dematerialised form under specific ISINs.
- Standard denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Companies issuing commercial papers include Titan Company Limited, KEC International Ltd., Godrej Housing Finance Limited, Can Fin Homes Ltd., and Manappuram Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with standard demat trading norms and denomination requirements for debt instruments.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 16, 2026
- Allotment and redemption dates vary by issuer (ranging from September 2026 to December 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice with no direct market-wide impact.
