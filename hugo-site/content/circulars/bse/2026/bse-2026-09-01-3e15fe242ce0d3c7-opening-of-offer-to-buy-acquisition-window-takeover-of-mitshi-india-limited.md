## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the Offer to Buy / Acquisition Window for the takeover of Mitshi India Limited. Mr. Karronn Naresh Bajaj (Acquirer) has made an open offer to the public shareholders of Mitshi India Limited to acquire up to 22,88,000 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 26.00% of the total voting share capital on a fully diluted basis) at a price of Rs. 15/- per share in cash.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Mitshi India Limited
- **Acquirer:** Mr. Karronn Naresh Bajaj
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 15.00 per share payable in cash
- **Offer Size:** Up to 22,88,000 equity shares (26.00% of total voting share capital on a fully diluted basis)
- **Regulatory Basis:** Regulation 4 of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011
- **Offer Period:** Thursday, September 03, 2026, to Thursday, September 17, 2026 (Excluding SEBI Holiday on Monday, September 14, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015, and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 dated December 9, 2016, along with relevant Exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the acquisition window mechanism guidelines for tender offers under takeovers.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** September 03, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** September 17, 2026
- **Exclusion Date:** September 14, 2026 (SEBI Holiday)

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of Mitshi India Limited can tender their shares through the stock exchange acquisition window during the specified open offer period.
