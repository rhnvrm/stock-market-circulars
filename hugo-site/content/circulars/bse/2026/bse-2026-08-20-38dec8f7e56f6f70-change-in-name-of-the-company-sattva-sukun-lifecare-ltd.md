## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Sattva Sukun Lifecare Ltd has changed its name to Tavexia Lifecare Limited. Consequently, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Sattva Sukun Lifecare Ltd
- New Company Name: Tavexia Lifecare Limited
- Scrip Code: 539519
- New Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System: TAVEXIA
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: TAVEXIA
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to note the updated name and scrip identifiers for all future trading and operational purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 20, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact; purely an administrative update to reflect the new corporate identity of the company on the exchange trading systems.
