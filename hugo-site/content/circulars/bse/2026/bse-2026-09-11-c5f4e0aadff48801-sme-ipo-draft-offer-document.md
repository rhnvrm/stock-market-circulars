## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Bhagya Machine Tools Limited under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations. The document is available for public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bhagya Machine Tools Limited
- Filing Date: September 10, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments on the Draft Offer Document can be submitted via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days from the notice date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 11, 2026
- Filing Date: September 10, 2026
- Public Comment Deadline: Within 21 days of the notice

## Impact Assessment

Initiates the public comment phase for the proposed SME IPO of Bhagya Machine Tools Limited on the BSE SME platform.
