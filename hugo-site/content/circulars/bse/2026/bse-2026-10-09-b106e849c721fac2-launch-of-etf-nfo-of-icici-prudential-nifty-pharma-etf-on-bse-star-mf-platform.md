## Summary

ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund is offering the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF on the BSE StAR MF Platform from October 12, 2026, to October 19, 2026. The tentative allotment date for the scheme is October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Names: ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF and ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF - Direct Plan.
- Category: Equity ETF.
- NFO Period: October 12, 2026, to October 19, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 19, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 23, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 21, 2026.
- Transactions without client fund initiation by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure that client transactions adhere to fund initiation deadlines (9:00 AM on T+1 day) and that clear funds are available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 21, 2026, for eligibility.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 12, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: October 19, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: October 21, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to subscribe to the ICICI Prudential Nifty Pharma ETF securely via the BSE StAR MF Platform with standard operational and settlement guidelines.
