## Summary

BSE has announced that the SHRIRAM GOLD ETF PASSIVE FOF schemes will reopen for ongoing subscriptions and redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 30, 2026, pursuant to notice no. 20260910-36.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Facilities Available:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform
- **Schemes Included:**
  - Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF680P01661)
  - Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF680P01687)
  - Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF680P01703)
  - Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF680P01653)
  - Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF - Direct Plan - IDCW FOF Payout (ISIN: INF680P01679)
  - Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF - Direct Plan - IDCW FOF Reinvestment (ISIN: INF680P01695)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational notification concerning mutual fund scheme transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors must take note of the availability of these schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- **Reopening Date:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute transactions, systematic investment plans (SIP), systematic transfer plans (STP), and systematic withdrawal plans (SWP) in Shriram Gold ETF Passive FOF schemes via the BSE StAR MF platform.
