## Summary

Ashiana Housing Ltd has announced a partial redemption of its debentures, resulting in a reduced face value. Trading members are informed that the debentures will trade with the revised face value effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ashiana Housing Ltd
- Purpose: Part-Redemption of Debentures
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 626,882.14 per Debenture
- Scrip Code: 973220
- ISIN: INE365D08026
- Scrip ID / Symbol: AHL-8%-31-5-41-PVT

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are required to take note of the reduced face value for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for trading members and investors holding the specified debentures due to the change in face value resulting from partial redemption.
