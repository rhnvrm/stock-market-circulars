## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for securities meeting specific market surveillance criteria. Effective August 18, 2026, enhanced margin requirements apply to all open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin is 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable rate of margin is 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%.
- Measures are in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance frameworks and do not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM framework rules for shortlisted securities.
- Margin rates adjusted to 50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the revised margin requirements (50% or 100%) for all open positions as of August 17, 2026, and new positions created from August 18, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 18, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date: August 17, 2026
- Notice Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased capital requirements for trading in securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I and II, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume for the affected scrips.
