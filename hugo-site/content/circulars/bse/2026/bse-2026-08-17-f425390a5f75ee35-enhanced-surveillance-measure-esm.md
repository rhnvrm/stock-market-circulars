## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities that satisfy criteria for inclusion, movement to lower stages, or removal from the framework. Securities qualifying under ESM are subjected to a minimum 100% margin and shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the ESM criteria attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 19, 2026, on all open positions as on August 18, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but are moved to a lower Stage (Stage I) w.e.f. August 18, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. August 19, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 18, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely for market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) in conjunction with other prevailing exchange surveillance measures.
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement and trade-for-trade settlement terms for impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II measures and moves: August 18, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margins and Trade-for-Trade shift: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions will reduce liquidity and increase holding costs for securities placed under the ESM framework.
