## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the partial redemption of Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) issued by three trusts: Radhakrishna Securitisation Trust, Shivshakti Securitisation Trust, and Siddhivinayak Securitisation Trust. Trading members are informed that the PTCs will be traded with reduced face values effective October 6, 2026.

## Key Points

- Radhakrishna Securitisation Trust (Scrip Code: 977124, ISIN: INE2I7H15018, Scheme: RKST-7.66%-28-9-28-PTC): Reduced Face Value is Rs. 9,596,271.30 per PTC.
- Shivshakti Securitisation Trust (Scrip Code: 977121, ISIN: INE2I7G15010, Scheme: SST-7.73%-28-9-29-PTC): Reduced Face Value is Rs. 9,634,939.64 per PTC.
- Siddhivinayak Securitisation Trust (Scrip Code: 977125, ISIN: INE2I7F15012, Scheme: SST-7.80%-28-9-30-PTC): Reduced Face Value is Rs. 9,668,325.83 per PTC.
- Effective Date for trading with reduced face value: October 06, 2026 (DR-729/2026-2027).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard corporate action notice regarding debt segment partial redemption of PTCs.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are required to take note of the reduced face values and ensure trading operations reflect the updated values.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 5, 2026
- Effective Date: October 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to trading members and investors holding the respective PTCs, ensuring accurate valuation and settlement based on the revised face values.
