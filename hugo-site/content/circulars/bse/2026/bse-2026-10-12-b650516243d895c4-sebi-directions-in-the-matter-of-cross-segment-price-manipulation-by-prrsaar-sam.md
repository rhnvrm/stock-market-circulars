## Summary

In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260916-51 dated September 16, 2026, SEBI has communicated that noticees have complied with Interim Order WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-TPD2/32723/2026-27 dated September 16, 2026, by depositing the requisite impounding amount and creating a lien in favor of SEBI. Consequently, de-freezing is permitted subject to specific trading restrictions.

## Key Points

- Noticees have complied with SEBI's interim order by depositing impounding amounts and creating a lien.
- Entities are permitted to trade in the cash segment.
- Trading in equity derivatives (both options and futures) remains restricted.
- For Noticee No. 1, the derivatives restriction applies specifically to its proprietary trading account.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial modification of SEBI interim directions upon fulfillment of impounding and lien-creation requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and concerned entities must adhere to the ongoing restriction preventing equity derivatives trading while operating in the cash segment.

## Important Dates

- Order Date / Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Previous Order Date: September 16, 2026
- SEBI Email Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is limited to the named private entities (Prrsaar Sampada Private Limited and Chaubara Eats Private Limited) with no broader market-wide impact on listed securities.
