## Summary

Effective from Thursday, July 30, 2026, the equity shares of INDO-MIM Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Scrip Name: INDO-MIM Limited
- Scrip Code: 544837
- ISIN No.: INE084101034
- Total Securities Listed: 494,480,907 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 485/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 1/- and premium of Rs. 484/-)
- Date of Allotment: July 28, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) applicable on listing day (July 30, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

None. Securities will be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the lock-in details as per Annexure I and shareholding patterns as per Annexure II. Corporate actions for lock-in marking have been executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: July 28, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing and Trading: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of INDO-MIM Limited shares on the BSE platform, providing liquidity to public shareholders and investors.
