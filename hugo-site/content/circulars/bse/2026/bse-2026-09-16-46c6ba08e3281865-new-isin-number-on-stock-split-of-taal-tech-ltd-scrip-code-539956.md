## Summary

Stock exchanges have announced the allocation of a new ISIN number for the equity shares of TAAL TECH LTD following its sub-division from face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TAAL TECH LTD
- Scrip Code: 539956
- New ISIN: INE524T01029
- Corporate Action: Sub-division of equity shares from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-
- Ex-Date: 22-09-2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their systems with the new ISIN for trades effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 16, 2026
- Ex-Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for trading members to ensure seamless settlement under the new ISIN.
