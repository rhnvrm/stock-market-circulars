## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective from August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying specific criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable margin rate is 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate is 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on August 26, 2026, and new positions created from August 27, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is for market surveillance purposes and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and enforcement of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measures (ST-ASM) with revised margin requirements for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) for all applicable open and new positions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 27, 2026
- Position Cut-off Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities under ST-ASM Stage I and II may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
