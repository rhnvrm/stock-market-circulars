## Summary

Mirae Asset Mutual Fund is launching the NFO for 'MIRAE ASSET BSE LARGEMID (60:40) STABLE DIVIDEND 50 ETF' and its direct plan on the BSE StAR MF Platform from August 10, 2026, to August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **NFO Period:** August 10, 2026, to August 12, 2026 (extended till midnight for lumpsum purchases).
- **Schemes Offered:** 
  1. MIRAE ASSET BSE LARGEMID (60:40) STABLE EQUITY DIVIDEND 50 ETF
  2. MIRAE ASSET BSE LARGEMID (60:40) STABLE EQUITY DIVIDEND 50 ETF - DIRECT PLAN
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 14, 2026.
- **Fund House:** Mirae Asset Mutual Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 13, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open:** August 10, 2026
- **NFO Close:** August 12, 2026 (Midnight)
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** August 13, 2026, 6:00 PM
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members with operational instructions and timelines for participating in the new ETF NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
