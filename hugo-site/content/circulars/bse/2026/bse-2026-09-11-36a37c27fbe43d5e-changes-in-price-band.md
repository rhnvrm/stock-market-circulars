## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the revision of price bands for multiple scrips from their existing levels.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for several equity scrips.
- Changes take effect from September 15, 2026.
- Affected scrips include various companies across different price band percentages (e.g., 5%, 10%, 20%).

## Regulatory Changes

Modification of permissible daily price movement limits for specified securities to manage market volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure systems are updated with the revised price bands effective from the implementation date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 11, 2026
- Effective Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct impact on the intraday trading limits and price discovery mechanism for the listed scrips.
