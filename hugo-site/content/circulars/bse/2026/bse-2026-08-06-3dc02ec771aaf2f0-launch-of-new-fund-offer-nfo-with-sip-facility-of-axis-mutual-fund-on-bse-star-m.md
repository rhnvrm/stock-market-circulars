## Summary

AXIS Mutual Fund is launching the AXIS Nifty Energy Index Fund NFO, including a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility, on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 07, 2026, to August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: AXIS Nifty Energy Index Fund (Regular and Direct Plans - Growth)
- ISINs: INF846KA1572 (Regular Growth), INF846KA1580 (Direct Growth)
- NFO Period: August 07, 2026, to August 21, 2026 (Extended till midnight for Lumpsum purchases on August 21, 2026).
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without fund initiation by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on August 21, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 07, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: August 21, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an opportunity to invest in the Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform with standard operational guidelines.
