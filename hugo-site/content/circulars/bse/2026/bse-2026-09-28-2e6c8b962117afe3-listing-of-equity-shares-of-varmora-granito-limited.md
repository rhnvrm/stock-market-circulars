## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Varmora Granito Limited effective from Tuesday, September 29, 2026, under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Varmora Granito Limited
- Scrip Code: 544942
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: VARMORA
- ISIN: INE0M5301040
- Total Securities Listed: 22,60,11,824 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 226011824)
- Issue Price: Rs. 148/- per share (Face Value Rs. 2/-, Premium Rs. 146/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 25, 2026
- Market Lot: 1 share

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard IPO listing notification under SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on September 29, 2026.
- Necessary corporate actions executed for lock-in marking in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 25, 2026
- Listing & Trading Effective Date: September 29, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading and liquidity for Varmora Granito Limited shares on the exchange platform.
