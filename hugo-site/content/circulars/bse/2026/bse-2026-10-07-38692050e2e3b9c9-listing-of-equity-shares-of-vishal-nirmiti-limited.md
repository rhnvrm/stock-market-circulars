## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of Vishal Nirmiti Limited effective from October 8, 2026. The scrip will be traded in the 'T' Group of Securities under the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular dated January 20, 2012.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Vishal Nirmiti Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544973
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** VNL
- **ISIN:** INE1O5L01025
- **No. of Securities:** 2,639,0909 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 26390909)
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 220/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 210/-)
- **Date of Allotment:** October 6, 2026
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** Scheduled for Thursday, October 8, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be in Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure demat form trading and refer to SPOS guidelines.
- Lock-in period marked in depository records as per corporate actions.

## Important Dates

- **Listing Date / SPOS:** October 8, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** October 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Vishal Nirmiti Limited equity shares on the BSE platform, providing liquidity to investors while adhering to mandatory trade-for-trade surveillance for initial trading sessions.
