## Summary

The Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL) / Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) of Trio Mercantile & Trading Limited. This revision refers to the earlier Notice No. 20260728-10 regarding the settlement calendar. The bidding period for this takeover was conducted from July 29, 2026, to August 11, 2026, under Settlement No. 2026093. The obligation download and settlement dates have been updated to August 18, 2026, and August 19, 2026, respectively.

## Key Points

- **Revised Timeline**: Shifted obligation download to August 18, 2026, and settlement to August 19, 2026.
- **Delivery Mechanism**: Demat shares must be delivered exclusively using the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism, available across both depositories.
- **Depository Details**: Members and Custodians must utilize the Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026093 in the depository system.
- **Stamp Duty**: Client-wise stamp duty charges can be referenced in the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory guidelines introduced in this circular. It updates the operational schedule under existing guidelines, specifically referencing Notice No. 20200528-32 dated May 28, 2020, regarding the revised mechanism for the acquisition of shares through stock exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Early Pay-In**: Ensure demat shares are delivered only via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism.
- **Depository Parameters**: Members and Custodians must correctly input Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026093 in their depository instructions.
- **Stamp Duty Assessment**: Check the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file to determine applicable client-specific stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date**: July 29, 2026
- **Bid End Date**: August 11, 2026
- **Revised Obligation Download Date**: August 18, 2026
- **Revised Settlement Date**: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. The timeline update affects only a single company's takeover offer settlement, presenting no broader risk to market structures.
- **Operational Impact**: Medium. Participating broker-dealers, members, and custodians must adjust their systems and clearing routines to meet the new obligation download and settlement deadlines.
