## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued Circular No. HO/17/11/24(8)2026-DDHS-POD1/I/23125/2026 dated October 07, 2026, regarding the review of provisions related to International Securities Identification Number (ISIN) for debt securities issued on a private placement basis.

## Key Points

- SEBI Circular Reference: HO/17/11/24(8)2026-DDHS-POD1/I/23125/2026 dated October 07, 2026.
- Focuses on the review and update of provisions governing ISIN for privately placed debt securities.
- Comes into force with immediate effect.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates regulatory guidelines concerning the allocation, management, and reporting of ISINs for debt securities issued via private placement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities issuing debt securities on a private placement basis must take note of the revised framework and ensure full compliance.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 07, 2026
- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances regulatory oversight, transparency, and tracking of debt securities issued on a private placement basis through standardized ISIN provisions.
