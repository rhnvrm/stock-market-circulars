## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) by 16 companies have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listing of further equity shares issued under ESOP / ESOS by 16 companies including ICICI Bank, Federal Bank, SBI Life Insurance, and Aditya Birla Capital.
- Effective date for trading: October 15, 2026.
- No lock-in applicable for the listed shares.

## Regulatory Changes

Standard listing and admission of further securities pursuant to corporate ESOP allotments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants to note the admission of new shares for trading.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity base of the respective companies with minimal immediate market impact.
