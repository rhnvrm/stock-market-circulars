## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of three Government of India Treasury Bills (T-Bills) scheduled for October 21, 2026, pursuant to the RBI press release.

## Key Points

- Three Treasury Bills offered: 91 Day T-Bill (₹8,000 Cr), 182 Day T-Bill (₹8,000 Cr), and 364 Day T-Bill (₹7,000 Cr).
- Bidding takes place through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.
- Bid collection starts on October 19, 2026, at 10:00 am.
- Bid collection end date for direct investors is October 20, 2026, till 5:00 pm, and for members is October 21, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- Settlement date is scheduled for October 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a recurring auction notice under the established non-competitive bidding framework for Government Securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and eligible direct investors must ensure bid submissions adhere to the specified timelines via the NCB-GSec platform.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** October 19, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors):** October 20, 2026, till 5:00 pm
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** October 21, 2026, till 8:00 am
- **Auction Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants and retail/institutional investors access to participate in Government of India debt issuances smoothly through exchange infrastructure.
