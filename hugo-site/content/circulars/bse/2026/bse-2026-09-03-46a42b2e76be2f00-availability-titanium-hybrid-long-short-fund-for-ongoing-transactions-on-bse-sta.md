## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding operational changes for transactions in the Titanium Hybrid Long-Short Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective September 2, 2026, as communicated by Tata Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Redemption and switch-out transactions for the scheme are restricted to once a week (every Wednesday).
- If Wednesday is a non-business day, transactions will be processed on the next business day.
- Valid applications received up to 3:00 p.m. on Wednesday will be processed using that Wednesday's NAV.
- Applications received after 3:00 p.m. on Wednesday will be processed using the next Wednesday's NAV.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must take note of the revised frequency and timing for redemption and switch-out requests.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 2, 2026
- **Cut-off for previous terms:** September 01, 2026 (3:00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

- Limited to investors and distributors transacting in the Titanium Hybrid Long-Short Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
