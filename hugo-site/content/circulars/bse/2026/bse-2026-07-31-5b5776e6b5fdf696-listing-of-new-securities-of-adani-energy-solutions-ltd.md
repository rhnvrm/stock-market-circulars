## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of 2,16,71,826 equity shares of Adani Energy Solutions Ltd. issued pursuant to a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP). The shares are effective for trading from August 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 2,16,71,826 Equity shares of Rs.10/- each allotted to QIBs pursuant to Qualified Institutional Placement.
- **Scrip Code:** 539254
- **ISIN No.:** INE931S01010
- **Effective Date:** Monday, August 03, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** July 30, 2026
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 1615/-
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 1201282643 to 1222954468
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the addition of the new securities to the trading segment.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** July 30, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increases the paid-up equity capital of Adani Energy Solutions Ltd. by over 2.16 crore shares issued to Qualified Institutional Buyers at Rs. 1615/- per share.
