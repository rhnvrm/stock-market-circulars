## Summary

BSE has announced the shifting of certain company scrips to the 'Z' group effective August 10, 2026, due to non-compliance with Regulation 33 (Non-submission of financial results) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for two consecutive quarters (December 2025 and March 2026).

## Key Points

- Scrips to be transferred: Astron Paper & Board Mill Ltd (540824) and Supha Pharmachem Ltd (539561).
- Effective date for transfer: August 10, 2026, if companies fail to comply by August 05, 2026.
- Several other companies are noted as already being in the 'Z'/'MT' group for similar non-compliances.

## Regulatory Changes

- Actions taken pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 and SOP for suspension and revocation of trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Affected companies must fulfill their financial result reporting obligations under Regulation 33 on or before August 05, 2026, to avoid being shifted to the 'Z' group.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 28, 2026
- Compliance Deadline: August 05, 2026
- Effective Date of Shifting: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in securities of non-compliant companies moved to the 'Z' group will face severe restrictions, impacting investor liquidity and market operations.
