## Summary

The Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd. has released the settlement schedule for the Futures & Options (F&O) segment covering trade dates from November 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines day-to-day trade dates and corresponding daily settlement dates for November 2026.
- Applicable to all trading members participating in the F&O segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must align their back-office operations and fund/securities pay-in/pay-out schedules according to the notified calendar.

## Important Dates

- Period Start Date: November 1, 2026
- Period End Date: November 30, 2026
- First Trade Date in schedule: 02-11-2026 (Settlement Date: 03-11-2026)
- Last Trade Date in schedule: 30-11-2026 (Settlement Date: 01-12-2026)

## Impact Assessment

Operational routine for clearing and settlement participants in the derivatives segment.
