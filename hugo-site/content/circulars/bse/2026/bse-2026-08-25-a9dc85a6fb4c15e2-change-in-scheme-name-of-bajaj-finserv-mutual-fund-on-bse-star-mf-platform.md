## Summary

Bajaj Finserv Mutual Fund has announced a change in the scheme names for several of its funds on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- BAJAJ FINSERV ELSS TAX SAVER FUND is renamed to BAJAJ FINSERV ELSS - TAX SAVER FUND.
- BAJAJ FINSERV LOW DURATION FUND is renamed to BAJAJ FINSERV ULTRA SHORT TO SHORT TERM FUND.
- BAJAJ FINSERV LARGE AND MID CAP FUND is renamed to BAJAJ FINSERV LARGE & MID CAP FUND.
- BAJAJ FINSERV BANKING AND PSU FUND is renamed to BAJAJ FINSERV BANKING AND PSU DEBT FUND.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal impact; purely a nomenclature change for existing mutual fund schemes.
