## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment, or move to lower stages/out of the framework effective September 18 and September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 21, 2026, on open positions as of September 18, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 18, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 21, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ESM framework measures including enhanced margins, price band restrictions, and migration to the Trade-for-Trade segment for identified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements and note the shifting of specified securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment or lower stages as applicable.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II / Shifting out: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade Segment: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for affected securities will be strictly impacted due to movement into the ESM framework and Trade-for-Trade settlement.
