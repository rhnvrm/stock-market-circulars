## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of several companies as they have fixed record dates for the payment of interest or redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures/bonds of the specified companies as per the fixed record and ex-dates.
- Affected companies include Embassy Office Parks REIT, IKF Home Finance Limited, Adani Enterprises Ltd., and NTPC Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the listed debentures during the restricted period associated with the record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 20 Aug 2026
- **Record Dates:** Various dates ranging from 23/08/2026 to 31/08/2026
- **Ex-Dates:** Various dates ranging from 21/08/2026 to 31/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of secondary market trading in the specified debt securities to facilitate interest payment and redemption processing.
