## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of three newly listed companies to its indices. Gaja Alternative Asset Management Limited will be added to the BSE IPO index effective August 28, 2026. Additionally, Mopshop Distribution Limited and Dhanwel Hybrid Seeds Limited will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Gaja Alternative Asset Management Limited (Ticker: 544885) added to BSE IPO index, effective August 28, 2026.
- Mopshop Distribution Limited (Ticker: 544883) added to BSE SME IPO index, effective August 27, 2026.
- Dhanwel Hybrid Seeds Limited (Ticker: 544884) added to BSE SME IPO index, effective August 27, 2026.
- Actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

No specific compliance actions required by market participants other than incorporating index updates.

## Important Dates

- August 26, 2026: Notice issuance date and company listing date.
- August 27, 2026: Effective date for BSE SME IPO index additions.
- August 28, 2026: Effective date for BSE IPO index addition.

## Impact Assessment

Routine index reconstitution with minimal market impact, affecting only benchmark tracking for the newly added securities.
