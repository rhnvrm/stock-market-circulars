## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd. (ICCL) has announced the stock-wise Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) and position limits applicable to Clearing Members, Institutional Investors, and Clients under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for the month of August 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Applicable for the month of August 2026.
- Covers stock-wise Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL).
- Specifies position limits for Clearing Members (CM), Institutional Investors, and Clients under the SLB segment.
- Issued pursuant to SEBI Circular No. MRD/DoP/SE/Dep/Cir-14/2007 dated December 20, 2007.

## Regulatory Changes

- Periodic specification of position limits for the SLBS segment in accordance with established regulatory frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and participants must adhere to the specified position limits under the SLB segment during August 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 2026
- Notice Date: 31 July 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures orderly trading, risk management, and compliance within the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme segment.
