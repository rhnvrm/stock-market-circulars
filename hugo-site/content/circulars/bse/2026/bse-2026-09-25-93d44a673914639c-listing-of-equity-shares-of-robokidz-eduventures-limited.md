## Summary

Effective from Monday, September 28, 2026, the equity shares of Robokidz Eduventures Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the SME segment in the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 544940
- **ISIN:** INE1W8R01018
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** ROBOKIDZ
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 106/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 96/-)
- **Total Securities:** 10,859,453 Equity Shares fully paid up
- **Market Lot:** 1,200 Equity Shares
- **Date of Allotment:** September 24, 2026
- **Market Maker:** B.N. Rathi Securities Limited
- **Registrar:** Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form pursuant to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip is placed in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in the minimum market lot of 1,200 equity shares.
- Necessary corporate actions for lock-in marking have been executed in the depository records by the company.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 24, 2026
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) & Listing Date:** September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading and liquidity for Robokidz Eduventures Limited on the BSE SME platform, with standard trade-for-trade surveillance measures active during the initial trading window.
