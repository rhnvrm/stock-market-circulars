## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Deccan Health Care Limited (Scrip Code - 542248) effective from September 16, 2026, following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Deccan Health Care Limited
- Scrip Code: 542248
- ISIN: INE452W01019
- Securities: 7,15,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 20/- to Non-Promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- Effective Date of Trading: September 16, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 23425514 to 24140513
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants take note of the new securities listing.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 01/04/2025
- Effective Date: 16/09/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 31/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity capital base via warrant conversion with standard lock-in requirements.
