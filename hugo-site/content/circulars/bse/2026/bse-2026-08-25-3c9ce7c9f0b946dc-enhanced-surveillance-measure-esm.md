## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities meeting the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, move to lower stages, or transition into the Trade-for-Trade segment effective August 26 and August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Qualifying securities attract a minimum 100% margin effective August 27, 2026, on open positions as of August 26, 2026, and new positions.
- Certain securities will move to lower Stage I under the ESM framework.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade for Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance framework actions, including margin enhancements and shifts between ESM stages.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and monitor positions across impacted securities as per Annexures I, II, and III.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage Changes / Price Bands: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for affected securities will be strictly regulated under the ESM framework.
