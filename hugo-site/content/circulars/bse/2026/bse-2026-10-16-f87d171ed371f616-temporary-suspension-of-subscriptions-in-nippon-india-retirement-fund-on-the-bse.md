## Summary

BSE StAR MF has issued a notice regarding the temporary suspension of fresh lump-sum investments, switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-in) in the Nippon India Retirement Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective from the close of business on October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh lump-sum investments, additional purchases, switch-in transactions, and new SIP/XSIP and STP-in registrations are disabled.
- Applications received after the applicable cut-off time on October 15, 2026, will not be accepted or processed.
- Existing SIP and STP-in registrations made before the effective date will continue to be processed by the AMC/RTA without change.
- Redemption, switch-out, STP-out, and SWP transactions remain fully permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

Platform-level restrictions implemented by BSE StAR MF at the request of Nippon India Mutual Fund regarding scheme subscription halts.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must take note of the suspension rules when executing transactions in Nippon India Retirement Fund and ensure new subscriptions or SIP registrations are not submitted after the cut-off.

## Important Dates

- Cut-off time for prior applications: October 15, 2026
- Effective date of suspension: October 16, 2026 (close of business)

## Impact Assessment

Investors cannot make new investments or start new SIPs in Nippon India Retirement Fund via BSE StAR MF, though liquidity through redemptions and continuity of existing SIPs remain unaffected.
