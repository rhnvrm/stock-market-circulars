## Summary

The BSE has observed significant price and volume movements in various scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors and trading members have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the concerned companies. This notice lists the companies that have submitted their replies alongside those whose responses are still awaited.

## Key Points

- **Clarifications Received:** Replies have been received from BMW Ventures Ltd, BN Agrochem Ltd, DRA Consultants Ltd, Indiabulls Ltd, Linde India Ltd, and My Money Securities Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited:** Clarifications are pending from 19 companies, including Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, DAPS Advertising Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Deepa Jewellers Ltd, El Forge Ltd, Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Ltd, Geetanjali Credit and Capital Ltd, Kaarya Facilities and Services Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Mehul Telecom Ltd, Rex Sealing and Packing Industries Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, Spright Agro Ltd, Symbiox Investment & Trading Company Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, Terai Tea Company Ltd, and Vama Wovenfab Ltd.
- **Regulatory Objective:** Ensures transparency and protects market participants by making timely corporate disclosures available.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative surveillance disclosure.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities experiencing unusual market activity are required to promptly clarify price/volume movements to the Exchange upon request.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 14 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low overall market impact. Individual stock volatility may occur based on the clarifications provided or pending disclosures from specific scrips.
