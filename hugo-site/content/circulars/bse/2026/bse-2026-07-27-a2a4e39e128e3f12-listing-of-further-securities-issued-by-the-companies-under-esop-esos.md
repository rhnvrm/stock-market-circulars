## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 13 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Tuesday, July 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total Companies Involved: 13 listed entities.
- Effective Date: July 28, 2026.
- Purpose: Listing of further securities issued under ESOP / ESOS.
- List of Companies:
  1. 360 ONE WAM Limited (ISIN: INE466L01038)
  2. AU Small Finance Bank Limited (ISIN: INE949L01017)
  3. Campus Activewear Limited (ISIN: INE278Y01022)
  4. CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited (ISIN: INE067A01029)
  5. HDFC Asset Management Company Limited (ISIN: INE127D01025)
  6. Himadri Speciality Chemical Limited (ISIN: INE019C01026)
  7. ICICI Bank Limited (ISIN: INE090A01021)
  8. ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited (ISIN: INE765G01017)
  9. IIFL Capital Services Limited (ISIN: INE489L01022)
  10. Indigo Paints Limited (ISIN: INE09VQ01012)
  11. Intellect Design Arena Limited (ISIN: INE306R01017)
  12. L&T Technology Services Limited (ISIN: INE010V01017)
  13. TTK Prestige Limited (ISIN: INE690A01028)

None.
