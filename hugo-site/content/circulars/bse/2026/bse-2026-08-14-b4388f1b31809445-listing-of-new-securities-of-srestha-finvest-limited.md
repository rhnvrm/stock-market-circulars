## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of new equity shares of Srestha Finvest Limited (Scrip Code: 539217) effective from August 17, 2026, following the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- 11,75,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 0.05/- each.
- Issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.
- Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.
- Distinctive Numbers: 1640000001 to 1757500000.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Lock-in details apply to the newly allotted shares up to 28/02/2027.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 13/04/2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: 17/08/2026
- Lock-in up to: 28/02/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion via warrant conversion with no immediate adverse market impact.
