## Summary

JM Financial Services Limited (Member No. 325) has voluntarily chosen to cease its business operations in the Currency Derivative segment of the BSE, effective from Wednesday, August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: JM Financial Services Limited
- Member Number: 325
- Segment Affected: Currency Derivative segment
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and concerned clients should take note of the cessation and route any queries to membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 19 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to clients and counterparties dealing with JM Financial Services Limited in the BSE Currency Derivative segment.
