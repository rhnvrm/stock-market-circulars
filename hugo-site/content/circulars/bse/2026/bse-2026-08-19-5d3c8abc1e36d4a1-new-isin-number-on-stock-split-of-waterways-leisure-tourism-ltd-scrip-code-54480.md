## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of Waterways Leisure Tourism Ltd (Scrip Code: 544802) following a stock split from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Waterways Leisure Tourism Ltd
- Scrip Code: 544802
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-
- Old ISIN: Not explicitly stated in excerpt, but reference made to previous notice
- New ISIN: INE0LZF01039
- Ex-Date: 26-08-2026 (DR- 702/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

- Assignment of a new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of the company.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members of the exchange must take note of the new ISIN for trades in the company's equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 19, 2026
- Ex-Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth settlement and trading under the new ISIN for the sub-divided equity shares effective from the ex-date.
