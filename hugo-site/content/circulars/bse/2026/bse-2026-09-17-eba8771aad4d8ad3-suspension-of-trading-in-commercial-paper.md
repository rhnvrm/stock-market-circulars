## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CPs) for multiple companies due to the fixation of record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified Commercial Papers of the listed companies starting from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading applies to Commercial Papers of several entities including Muthoot Mcredit Limited, Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited, Mindspace Business Parks REIT, Redington Limited, KEC International Ltd., Tata Power Co. Ltd., Balrampur Chini Mills Ltd., J.K. Cement Ltd., Grasim Industries Ltd., Nexus Select Trust, and others.
- Record dates and ex-dates for redemption are set for September 22, 2026, and September 23, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable (operational notice regarding corporate action/redemption).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of the mentioned companies on or after the specified ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 17 Sep 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Date: 22/09/2026 and 23/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding the specified Commercial Papers must note the trading suspension for redemption processing.
