## Summary

BSE has announced a special trading session for BSE Indices on Sunday, November 08, 2026, on account of Diwali Laxmi Pujan. Trading will be conducted from 02:20 PM to 03:30 PM (IST).

## Key Points

- Special trading session scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026, for Diwali Laxmi Pujan.
- Trading hours: 02:20 PM (IST) to 03:30 PM (IST).
- End-of-day corporate action files (*.SDE) will be ready by 6:00 PM (IST) on the same day.
- Queries can be directed to bseindex@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the special trading hours and index calculation schedule for the Diwali Laxmi Pujan session.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 21, 2026
- Special Trading Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates uninterrupted market operations and index calculation during the special festival trading session.
