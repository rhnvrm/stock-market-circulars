## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of SWASTIKA INFRA LIMITED effective from Wednesday, September 30, 2026. A total of 3,41,20,187 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up are listed at an issue price of Rs. 185/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 175/-).

## Key Points

- Scrip ID: SWASTIKAIN
- Scrip Code: 544949
- ISIN: INE1QJA01015
- Total Securities: 34,120,187 Equity Shares
- Segment: 'T' Group of Securities (Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days)
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): Scheduled for Wednesday, September 30, 2026
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Securities admitted to dealing in the Trade-for-Trade segment pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000. Lock-in periods are marked in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: September 29, 2026
- Date of Allotment: September 28, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Swastika Infra Limited shares on BSE with initial surveillance via the Trade-for-Trade segment.
