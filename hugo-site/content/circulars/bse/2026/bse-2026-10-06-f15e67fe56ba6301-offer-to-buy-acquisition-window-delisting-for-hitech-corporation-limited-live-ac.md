## Summary

BSE has announced the live activities schedule for the Offer to Buy (OTB) acquisition window regarding the delisting of equity shares of Hitech Corporation Limited. The offer period runs from October 7, 2026, to October 13, 2026, at an offer price of Rs. 252.00 per share.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Hitech Corporation Limited
- Security Symbol: HITECHCORP | Scrip Code: 526217 | ISIN: INE120D01012
- Offer Size: Up to 43,91,220 Equity Shares
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00
- Offer Price: Rs. 252.00
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

Establishment of live session timings, modification/cancellation windows, and give-up/take-up schedules for trading and custodian members on the iBBS platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bids can be placed for investors holding shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Opening Date: 7th October 2026
- Closing Date: 13th October 2026
- Upper Modification and Cancellation: 7th October 2026 to 12th October 2026
- Take-up (last day of the offer): 13th October 2026 (up to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless participation for eligible shareholders and trading members through the iBBS and RTRMS platforms during the delisting acquisition window.
