## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of 13 scrips with effect from August 04, 2026, as part of its ongoing market surveillance and risk management measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been modified for 13 specific scrips across various categories.
- Revised price limits range from 2% to 20% depending on the scrip.
- The changes become effective for trading sessions starting August 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of existing price band limits for listed equities to curb excessive volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their trading terminals and risk management systems to reflect the revised price bands for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 03 August 2026
- Effective Date: 04 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and traders dealing in the affected scrips must adjust their order placement strategies to comply with the new percentage price limits.
