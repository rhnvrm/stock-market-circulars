## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by nine companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS). The listing of these equity shares becomes effective from Thursday, October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Total Companies Involved:** 9 listed companies
- **Purpose:** Allotment and listing of shares issued under ESOP / ESOS
- **Issuing Companies:** EPL Limited, Federal Bank Limited, Go Digit General Insurance Limited, Jana Small Finance Bank Limited, Kaynes Technology India Limited, Renaissance Global Limited, Sai Life Sciences Limited, STL Networks Limited, and Tejas Networks Limited.
- **Lock-in Status:** 'NA' (Not Applicable) for all listed tranches in this notice.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine corporate action processing for employee stock options.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with standard BSE listing operations and depository procedures for ESOP share admissions.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Marginal increase in paid-up equity capital for the respective companies due to ESOP exercises, with negligible dilution impact on existing shareholders.
