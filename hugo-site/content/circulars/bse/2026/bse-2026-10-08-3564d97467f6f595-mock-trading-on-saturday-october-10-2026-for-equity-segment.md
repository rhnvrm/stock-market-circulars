## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity segment on Saturday, October 10, 2026, conducting operations from both the Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR). Trading members can participate in mock trading or use the UAT environment to fulfill regulatory testing requirements in accordance with SEBI circular dated November 24, 2020.

## Key Points

- Scheduled Date: Saturday, October 10, 2026.
- Segments: Equity segment, including continuous trading, block deals, pre-open, special pre-open for IPO & re-listed scrips, and periodic call auctions.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.60 release announced with details to follow via separate circular.
- Switch over to Disaster Recovery (DR) site scheduled during the afternoon session.

## Regulatory Changes

- Fulfills regulatory testing requirements as per SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 dated November 24, 2020 and Exchange Notice No. 20201201-22.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members can participate in the mock trading session or UAT environment to test trading software and risk management systems.
- Third-party trading platforms can be tested for various market conditions and functionalities.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 10, 2026.
- Login Timings: 10:15 AM to 10:45 AM.
- Primary Site sessions running through morning and afternoon, with switch over to Disaster Recovery (DR) site at 02:00 PM.

## Impact Assessment

- Zero direct impact on live market operations as this is a scheduled weekend mock trading session designed for system testing and compliance verification.
