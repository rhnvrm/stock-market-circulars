## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the part redemption of Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) of FRIEZA 06 2025 (Scrip Code: 976876, ISIN: INE26SJ15017) and the resumption of trading with a reduced face value.

## Key Points

- Part redemption and payment of interest were processed based on the record date of September 1, 2026.
- Reduced face value: Rs. 0.10 per PTC.
- Trading with the reduced face value becomes effective from September 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the reduced face value for ongoing transactions in the specified scrip.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 1, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading with Reduced Face Value: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for trading members dealing in the debt segment for FRIEZA 06 2025 PTCs.
