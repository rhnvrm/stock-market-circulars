## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the updated terms of debt securities issued by Kanakadurga Finance Limited. Effective October 21, 2026, specified scrip codes will trade under revised security and asset composition terms.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 21, 2026.
- Scrip Code 977333 (ISIN: INE104W07229): Debentures secured by first-ranking exclusive and continuing charge over eligible Vehicle Loan receivables in substitution of existing Gold Loan receivables.
- Scrip Code 977086 (ISIN: INE104W07211): Hypothecated assets updated to comprise 100% Vehicle Loan receivables.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of terms of instrument regarding secured asset composition and substitution of hypothecated assets.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and debenture holders to take note of revised terms and asset composition for trading effective October 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures compliance and clarity for investors regarding collateral substitution for the company's listed debt securities.
