## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants that Nippon India Mutual Fund has declared August 11, 2026, as a Non-Business Day for the Nippon India Japan Equity Fund due to market closures.

## Key Points

- Scheme affected: Nippon India Japan Equity Fund.
- Reason: Market closures on August 11, 2026.
- Impacted transactions: Subscription, Redemption, Switch, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors must note that transactions through the BSE StAR MF Platform for the specified scheme will not be permitted on the declared date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 10, 2026
- Non-Business Day: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary restriction on transactions for the specific mutual fund scheme for one business day.
