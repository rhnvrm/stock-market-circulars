## Summary

BSE has enabled the SMART Switch feature under Invesco Mutual Fund for the NFOs of Invesco India Nifty Chemical Index Fund and Invesco India Nifty India Defence Index Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform. The feature is available from September 17, 2026, until September 25, 2026 (1:00 PM).

## Key Points

- **Feature Availability**: Available via User Interface, Bulk Upload, and APIs for non-Demat lump sum subscriptions only.
- **Eligible Schemes**: Invesco India Nifty Chemical Index Fund & Invesco India Nifty India Defence Index Fund NFOs.
- **Timeline**: Available from September 17, 2026, up to 1:00 PM on September 25, 2026.
- **Switch Processing**: Switch-out is processed on a FIFO basis from existing eligible units (e.g., Invesco India Overnight Fund) into the respective Growth/Direct options of the NFO schemes.
- **Reporting**: Purchase orders successfully allotted will be considered as 'Switch In' and reported to the RTA on September 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure correct scheme credentials and parameters are used via APIs, bulk uploads, or UI interfaces.
- Transactions must be executed in non-Demat mode strictly for lump sum subscriptions.

## Important Dates

- **Start Date**: September 17, 2026
- **Deadline / Cut-off**: September 25, 2026, up to 1:00 PM
- **RTA Reporting Date**: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless switching for investors participating in Invesco Mutual Fund NFOs through the BSE StAR MF platform with automated FIFO-based switch processing.
