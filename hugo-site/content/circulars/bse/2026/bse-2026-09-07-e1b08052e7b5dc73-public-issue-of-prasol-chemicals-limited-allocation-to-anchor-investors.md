## Summary

Trading members are informed that 22,18,930 Equity Shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the Public Issue of Prasol Chemicals Limited at Rs. 676/- per equity share on September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Prasol Chemicals Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue / Anchor Investors (AIs) portion
- Number of Shares Allocated: 22,18,930 Equity Shares
- Issue Price / Allocation Price: Rs. 676/- per equity share
- Date of Allocation: September 07, 2026

None

Trading members and market participants must take note of the anchor investor allocation details as per the enclosed announcement.
