## Summary

Union Asset Management Company (AMC) has announced a revision in the minimum Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) investment amount for Union Multicap Fund. Effective October 22, 2026, the updated SIP parameters have been implemented on the BSE StAR MF Platform for monthly frequency.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Affected:** Union Multicap Fund.
- **Frequency:** Monthly.
- **Revised Minimum Amount:** Rs. 250 and in multiples of Re. 1/-.
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **Effective Date:** October 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational update regarding scheme-specific investment thresholds communicated by the AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors are advised to take note of the revised SIP criteria and ensure that transactions executed on the BSE StAR MF Platform comply with the new scheme-wise details provided in the Annexure.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impact is restricted to investors and distributors participating in the SIP facility of Union Multicap Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, requiring transaction adjustments to meet the new Rs. 250 minimum threshold.
