## Summary

Star Wire (India) Limited, the promoter and seller of Supreme Commercial Enterprises Limited, has announced an Offer for Sale (OFS) of up to 30,221 equity shares representing 5.64% of the total issued and paid-up equity share capital. The bidding takes place through the designated window of BSE Limited, although the company is exclusively listed on the Metropolitan Stock Exchange of India Limited (MSEI).

## Key Points

- **Seller / Promoter:** Star Wire (India) Limited
- **Company:** Supreme Commercial Enterprises Limited
- **Offer Size:** Up to 30,221 equity shares (5.64% of total issued and paid-up equity share capital)
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10 per equity share
- **Floor Price:** Rs. 11 per equity share
- **Objective:** Achieving minimum public shareholding in the Company
- **Exchange Mechanism:** BSE Limited designated window (Company is exclusively listed on MSEI)

## Regulatory Changes

None. The offer adheres to existing SEBI and BSE guidelines for Offer for Sale (OFS) through the stock exchange mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must comply with SEBI and BSE revised guidelines for bidding in the OFS segment, including separate windows for non-retail and retail investors.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 12, 2026
- **T Day (Non-Retail Investors):** October 13, 2026
- **T+1 Day (Retail Investors & Non-Retail carrying forward bids):** October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates compliance with minimum public shareholding norms for Supreme Commercial Enterprises Limited via secondary market share sale on BSE.
