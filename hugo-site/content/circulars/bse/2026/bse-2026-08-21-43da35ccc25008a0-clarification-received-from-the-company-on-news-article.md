## Summary

The Exchange sought clarification from JBM Auto Ltd regarding certain news articles appearing in media channels to verify their accuracy and inform investors. The company's reply has been received and attached by the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Company involved: JBM Auto Ltd (Scrip Code: 532605).
- Purpose: Verification of news articles appearing in various newspapers, websites, or television channels.
- Action: Clarification sought by BSE Surveillance and reply received from the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with disclosure norms regarding news verification as mandated by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Informational notice intended to maintain market transparency regarding news reports concerning the listed entity.
