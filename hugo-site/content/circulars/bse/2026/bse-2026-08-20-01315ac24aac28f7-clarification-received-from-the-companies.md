## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a surveillance notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in several scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies.

## Key Points

- **Replies Received:** Clarifications have been received and attached from 9 companies, including Nilkamal Ltd, Adani Total Gas Ltd, and Kiri Industries Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited:** Responses are still pending from 9 companies, including PVP Ventures Ltd and Deep Polymers Ltd.
- **Objective:** Safeguard investors by ensuring transparency and timely disclosure regarding unusual market activity.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities experiencing significant price or volume movements must submit requested clarifications to the Exchange promptly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 20 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and alerts investors to ongoing surveillance queries for specific scrips.
