## Summary

BSE has announced the application and removal of Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria under ASM for IRP as per IBC are placed under the framework effective October 22, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities moving out from the IBC framework effective October 22, 2026 are listed in Annexure II.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) below 25% are listed in Annexure IV.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their pre-shortlisting bands (subject to no other active surveillance measures).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the IBC framework for identified companies in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must note the inclusion and exclusion of securities under the IBC surveillance framework and adhere to applicable price bands and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 22, 2026
- Notice Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading limits, price bands, and surveillance monitoring for specific securities undergoing insolvency resolution or moving out of the IBC framework.
