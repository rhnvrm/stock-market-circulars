## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the revised oversubscription notice for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of equity shares of Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) by the President of India, acting through the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance, Government of India. The promoter exercised the oversubscription option of up to 50,59,99,816 Equity Shares (4.00%), bringing the total offer size to 82,22,49,701 Equity Shares (6.50% of the total paid-up equity share capital).

## Key Points

- Total Offer Size: 82,22,49,701 Equity Shares (6.50% of paid-up equity share capital) including the base offer and oversubscription option.
- Allocation for Retail Investors: 10% of the total offer (8,22,24,971 Equity Shares) reserved on T+1 day (August 05, 2026).
- Employee Reservation: Up to 50,00,000 Equity Shares offered to eligible employees of the Corporation.
- Pricing: Retail investors and eligible employees bid at Rs. 373.10 per Equity share (after a Rs. 10 discount). The cut-off price for non-retail investors is Rs. 383.10 per Equity share.
- Dates: T Day for non-retail investors was August 04, 2026; T+1 Day for retail investors, employees, and carrying-forward non-retail investors was August 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Exercise of the Oversubscription Option under OFS Guidelines by the Promoter for additional 4.00% equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to the OFS Guidelines of Stock Exchanges by non-retail investors, retail investors, and eligible employees during bidding windows.

## Important Dates

- T Day (Non-Retail Investors): August 04, 2026
- T+1 Day (Retail Investors & Employees): August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Significant secondary market share supply for LIC, providing liquidity and expanding public shareholding in compliance with minimum public shareholding norms.
