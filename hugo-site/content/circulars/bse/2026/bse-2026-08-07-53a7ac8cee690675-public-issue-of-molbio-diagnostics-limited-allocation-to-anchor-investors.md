## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors under the public issue of Molbio Diagnostics Limited. A total of 34,87,717 equity shares were subscribed at Rs. 807/- per equity share on August 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Molbio Diagnostics Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investors Portion)
- Shares Subscribed: 34,87,717 equity shares
- Issue Price / Subscribed Price: Rs. 807/- per equity share
- Date of Announcement: August 7, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the anchor investor allocation details.
