## Summary

Adani Airport Holdings Limited has fixed a record date of September 25, 2026, for the payment of interest and part redemption of its Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). Trading in the debentures will continue with a reduced face value of Rs. 54,253 per debenture effective from the record date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Adani Airport Holdings Limited
- Scrip Code: 976065
- Scrip ID: AAHL-9.35%-8-9-28-PVT
- ISIN: INE0GCN07047
- Record Date: 25/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 25/09/2026
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 54,253/- per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action notice regarding debt securities redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and depositories are requested to note the reduced face value and settlement details for debentures of Adani Airport Holdings Limited effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Record Date: September 25, 2026
- Ex-Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Debenture holders will receive part redemption proceeds and interest payments as scheduled. Trading will reflect the reduced face value per debenture.
