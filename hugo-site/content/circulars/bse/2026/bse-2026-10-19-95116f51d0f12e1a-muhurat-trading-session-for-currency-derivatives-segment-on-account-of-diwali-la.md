## Summary

BSE has announced the schedule and timings for the Muhurat Trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment on account of Diwali Laxmi Pujan, scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Log-in time: 01:15 PM to 02:20 PM
- Continuous Trading (Currency Derivatives, Cross Currency Derivatives, and Interest Rate Derivatives): 02:20 PM to 03:30 PM
- Trade Modification: 02:20 PM to 04:00 PM
- Trade Annulment Request: Up to 04:05 PM

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the special session timings for operational readiness.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Muhurat Trading Date: November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; routine special trading session schedule.
