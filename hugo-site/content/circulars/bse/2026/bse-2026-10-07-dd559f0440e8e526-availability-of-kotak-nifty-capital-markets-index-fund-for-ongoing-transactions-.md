## Summary

BSE has announced that the KOTAK NIFTY CAPITAL MARKETS INDEX FUND shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective October 08, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20260911-33.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: KOTAK NIFTY CAPITAL MARKETS INDEX FUND
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Available Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Covered ISINs:
  - Regular Plan - Growth: INF174KA1G31
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout: INF174KA1G49
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF174KA1G56
  - Direct Plan - Growth: INF174KA1G07
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout: INF174KA1G15
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF174KA1G23

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational notice facilitating trading and transactions in mutual fund units on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors should ensure proper updating of systems for executing transactions across the specified ISINs.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates continuous investment and redemption operations for investors in the Kotak Nifty Capital Markets Index Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
