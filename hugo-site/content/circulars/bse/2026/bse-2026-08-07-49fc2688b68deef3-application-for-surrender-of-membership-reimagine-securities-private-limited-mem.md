## Summary

Reimagine Securities Private Limited (Member no. 6941) has applied to surrender its trading membership of the BSE in accordance with the Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations of the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Member Name: Reimagine Securities Private Limited
- Member Number: 6941
- Notice Date: August 07, 2026
- Objection Deadline: August 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members wishing to raise any objections to the surrender of membership must submit their objections in writing to the Membership Department within 15 days from the date of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 07 Aug 2026
- Objection Deadline: 21 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to the cessation of trading membership operations for the respective entity upon completion of the surrender process.
