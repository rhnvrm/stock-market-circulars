## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission for trading of further securities issued by 12 companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS). These new shares will be available for trading effective Wednesday, August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 12
- Effective date for trading: August 26, 2026
- Companies include Arvind Fashions Limited, Centum Electronics Limited, Cyient Limited, Deccan Gold Mines Limited, HDFC Asset Management Company Limited, ICICI Bank Limited, Karnataka Bank Limited, Lenskart Solutions Limited, Lodha Developers Limited, Onesource Specialty Pharma Limited, Sky Industries Limited, and V.I.P. Industries Limited.
- All listed securities are ordinary equity shares issued via ESOP/ESOS with 'NA' lock-in status indicated in the distribution range details.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are requested to take note of the securities admitted for trading.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity capital of the respective companies with minimal immediate impact on broader market operations.
