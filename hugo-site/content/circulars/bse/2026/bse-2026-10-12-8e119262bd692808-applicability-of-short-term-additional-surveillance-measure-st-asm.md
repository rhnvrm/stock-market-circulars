## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities, effective October 14, 2026, based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities in ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable rate of margin of 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. October 14, 2026, on open positions as on October 13, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities in ST-ASM Stage II will attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher, capped at 100%) w.e.f. October 14, 2026, on open positions as on October 13, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under the ASM framework is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the concerned entity.
- Price bands of scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance measures in conjunction with existing exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure the collection and maintenance of enhanced margins (50% for Stage I and 100% for Stage II) on all open positions as of October 13, 2026, and new positions created from October 14, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Open Positions Reference Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities will impact liquidity and capital allocation for market participants trading in these scrips.
