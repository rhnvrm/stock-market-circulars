## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has issued a notice reminding all Clearing Members and Self-Clearing Members to submit their Annual Returns for the financial year ended March 31, 2026, on or before October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Deadline:** Annual Returns for FY 2025-2026 must be submitted on or before October 31, 2026.
- **Financial Statements:** Members must submit financial statements in the format prescribed under the Companies Act, 2013, irrespective of the Act's applicability to the member.
- **Net Worth Requirements:** Members must adhere to the applicable Net worth (higher of Base Net worth or Variable Net worth) per SEBI guidelines.
- **Shared Submissions (Samuhik Prativedan Manch):** Clearing/Self-Clearing Members registered with NSE are required to submit their Half Yearly Net Worth Certificate to NSE only, which will share the data with BSECL.
- **Direct Submissions:** Members not registered with NSE and Professional Clearing Members registered with BSECL must submit directly via the online Electronic Filing System (IEFS).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of 'Samuhik Prativedan Manch' technology-based sharing mechanism for members registered with both NSE and BSECL to streamline regulatory submissions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Submit Annual Returns for FY 2025-2026 via IEFS or NSE (depending on registration) by October 31, 2026.
- Ensure compliance with net worth requirements and attach required documents specified in Annexure I.
- Report any uploading issues to iccl.membership@bseclearing.com with appropriate subject line details.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Submission Due Date:** October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Clearing and self-clearing members must ensure timely submission of their annual returns and net worth certificates to avoid penal actions by BSECL for non-submission or net worth shortfall.
