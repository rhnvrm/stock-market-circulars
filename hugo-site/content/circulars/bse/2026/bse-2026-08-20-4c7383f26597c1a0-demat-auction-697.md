## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-697/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on August 20, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on August 20, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m. on August 20, 2026.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m. on August 20, 2026.
- Pay-in of the Auction: August 21, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.
- Restrictions on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they short-delivered have been removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 20, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for clearing and settlement of short-delivered securities with standard market implications.
