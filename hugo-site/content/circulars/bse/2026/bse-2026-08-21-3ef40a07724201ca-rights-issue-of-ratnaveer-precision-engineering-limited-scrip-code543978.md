## Summary

Ratnaveer Precision Engineering Limited has fixed the record date for its upcoming Rights Issue of Equity Shares. Trading members are informed that transactions in the equity shares of the company will take place on an ex-rights basis starting from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ratnaveer Precision Engineering Limited (Scrip Code: 543978)
- Purpose: Rights Issue of 07 (Seven) Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 254/- per share on a Rights Basis for every 40 (Forty) Equity Shares held.
- Issue Price: Full amount of Rs. 264/- per Equity Share is payable on application by eligible equity shareholders.
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: August 25, 2026
- Record Date: August 26, 2026

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible equity shareholders are required to take note of the record date, ex-rights date, and payment terms for application.

- Ex-Rights Date: August 25, 2026
- Record Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts existing shareholders of Ratnaveer Precision Engineering Limited regarding their entitlement and trading of shares on an ex-rights basis.
