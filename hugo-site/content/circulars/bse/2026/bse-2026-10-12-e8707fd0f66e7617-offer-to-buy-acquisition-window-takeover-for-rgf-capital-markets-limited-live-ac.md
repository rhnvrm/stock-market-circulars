## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the schedule of activities for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) of shares of RGF Capital Markets Limited. The acquisition window is scheduled from Tuesday, 13th October 2026 to Tuesday, 27th October 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** RGF Capital Markets Limited
- **Security Symbol:** RGF
- **Scrip Code:** 539669
- **ISIN:** INE684D01025
- **Offer Size:** Up to 3,90,06,240 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 1.10 per share
- **Opening Date:** Tuesday, 13th October 2026
- **Closing Date:** Tuesday, 27th October 2026
- **Session Timings:** Bid offering, modifications, and cancellations are permitted between 9:15 a.m. and 3:30 p.m. during the issue period.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Operational schedule notification under existing takeover acquisition window norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading Members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians must note that if no securities are accepted, no End of Issue files will be generated on the Extranet.

## Important Dates

- **Opening Date:** 13th October 2026
- **Closing Date / Give-up Last Day:** 27th October 2026
- **Take-up Last Day:** 27th October 2026 (up to 4:00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly bidding, modifications, and settlement processes for market participants participating in the takeover offer of RGF Capital Markets Limited.
