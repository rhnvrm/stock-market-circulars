## Summary

Infinity Fincorp Solutions Private Limited has announced a partial redemption of its debentures, resulting in a reduced face value. Trading members are informed that the debentures will trade with the revised face value effective from September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Infinity Fincorp Solutions Private Limited
- **Debenture 1:** Scrip Code: 976074, ISIN: INE00VD07016, Symbol: IFSPL-11%-30-9-27-PVT, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 62,500/- per Debenture.
- **Debenture 2:** Scrip Code: 976594, ISIN: INE00VD07032, Symbol: IFSPL-10.50%-02-05-28-PVT, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 77,778/- per Debenture.
- **Effective Date:** September 09, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Nil

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to take note of the reduced face value and ensure trading occurs accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 08, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding the specified debentures will see a reduction in face value corresponding to the partial redemption payout.
