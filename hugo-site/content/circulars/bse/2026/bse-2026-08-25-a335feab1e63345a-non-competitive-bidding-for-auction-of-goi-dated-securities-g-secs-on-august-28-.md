## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled for August 28, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Four dated securities are being auctioned by the Government of India.
- Bidding will take place through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.
- Security involved: 6.94% GS 2036 (Symbol: 694GS36A26, Issue Size: Rs. 34,000 Cr).

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the iBBS NCB-GSec platform.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: August 25, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): August 27, 2026, till 5:00 pm
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): August 28, 2026, till 8:00 am
- Auction Date: August 28, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates non-competitive participation in government securities auctions for eligible members and investors.
