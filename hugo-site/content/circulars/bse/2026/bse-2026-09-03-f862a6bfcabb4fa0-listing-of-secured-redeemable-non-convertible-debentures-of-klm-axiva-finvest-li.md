## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that secured redeemable non-convertible debentures (NCDs) of KLM Axiva Finvest Limited are admitted to dealings on the Exchange with effect from Friday, September 4, 2026, under the 'F GROUP'.

## Key Points

- Securities: Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of KLM Axiva Finvest Limited.
- Effective Date: September 4, 2026.
- Trading Form: Dematerialized form only, in unit market lot (i.e. One Bond).
- Company Financial Year Ends: March 31.
- Registrar and Share Transfer Agent: KFIN Technologies Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 3, 2026
- Effective Date of Dealing: September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt listing enabling secondary market trading for investors holding the NCDs of KLM Axiva Finvest Limited.
