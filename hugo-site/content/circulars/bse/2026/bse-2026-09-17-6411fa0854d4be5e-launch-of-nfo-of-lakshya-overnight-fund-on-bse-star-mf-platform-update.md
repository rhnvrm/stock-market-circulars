## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the revised allotment date and NFO extension for the LAKSHYA OVERNIGHT FUND on the BSE StAR MF Platform, following communication from LAKSHYA Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Revised allotment date for LAKSHYA OVERNIGHT FUND - NFO changed from September 21, 2026, to September 24, 2026.
- NFO scheme extended till midnight on September 18, 2026, exclusively for Lumpsum purchases on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Revised reopen date is set for September 25, 2026.
- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 23, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments accepted subject to receipt of clear funds by 6:00 PM on September 23, 2026.
- Transactions without funds initiated by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.

## Important Dates

- NFO Extension (Lumpsum): September 18, 2026 (till midnight)
- Clear Funds Deadline: September 23, 2026 (by 6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 24, 2026
- Revised Reopen Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational timelines and funds pay-out schedules for the NFO of LAKSHYA OVERNIGHT FUND are updated for members and investors using the BSE StAR MF platform.
