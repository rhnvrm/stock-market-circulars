## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities issued by Bajaj Finance Limited on the debt market segment of the Exchange effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bajaj Finance Limited
- Security Type: Privately Placed Debt Instrument (Zero Coupon)
- Quantity: 212,000 units
- Scrip Code: 978334
- Scrip ID: 0BFL31
- Detail Name: BFL-ZC-21-10-31-PVT
- ISIN Number: INE296A07UG8
- Credit Rating: CRISIL AAA Stable
- Face Value / Issue Price: Rs. 100,000.00
- Date of Allotment: 21/10/2026
- Date of Redemption: 21/10/2031

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate debt listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted in dematerialized form under the specified ISIN with a tick size of 1 paise as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 21, 2026
- Listing / Admission Date: October 23, 2026
- Redemption Date: October 21, 2031

## Impact Assessment

Operational listing update facilitating secondary market trading for the specified privately placed debt securities of Bajaj Finance Limited.
