## Summary

Trading Members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Friday, October 23, 2026, the equity shares of Shree Vasu Logistics Limited are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: Shree Vasu Logistics Limited
- Scrip Code: 544997
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: SVLL
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT System: SVLL
- ISIN No.: INE00CE01017
- Securities: 11,493,700 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up.
- Distinctive Nos.: 1 to 11493700
- Market Lot: 1 (One) Share

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed in depository records for shares in demat form to mark the lock-in period.
- Physical share certificates have been enfaced with non-transferability.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must conduct trades in unit market lot (1 share) in dematerialised form.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date / Listing Date: October 23, 2026
- Financial Year Ending: March 31

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public market trading and liquidity for Shree Vasu Logistics Limited shares on the BSE platform.
