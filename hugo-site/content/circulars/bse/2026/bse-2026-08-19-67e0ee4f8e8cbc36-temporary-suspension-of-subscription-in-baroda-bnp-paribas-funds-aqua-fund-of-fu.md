## Summary

BSE has announced the temporary suspension of lumpsum investments, switch-in transactions, and fresh Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP-In) registrations under the Baroda BNP Paribas Funds Aqua Fund of Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Lumpsum investments (fresh and additional purchases), switch-ins, and new SIP/STP-In registrations are disabled on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Applications received after the cut-off time of 3:00 PM on August 18, 2026, are not accepted or processed.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIPs and STPs) prior to the effective date continue to be processed normally.
- Redemption, Switch-Out, STP-Out, and SWP transactions continue to be permitted and processed as usual.

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and distributors must take note of the disabled transaction types for the specified scheme and ensure no new purchase or SIP/STP-In applications are submitted after the cut-off time.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 19, 2026
- Cut-off Time for Acceptance: 3:00 PM on August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors wishing to make fresh investments, additional purchases, or start new SIPs in Baroda BNP Paribas Funds Aqua Fund of Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform will be restricted, while existing investors can continue redemptions and ongoing systematic plans.
