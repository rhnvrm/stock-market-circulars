## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for new equity shares of ACS Technologies Limited (Scrip Code - 530745) effective from October 26, 2026, following a preferential issue to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** ACS Technologies Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 530745
- **ISIN:** INE366C01021
- **Security Details:** 4,950,495 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 30.40/- (Issue Price: Rs. 40.40/-) on a preferential basis to non-promoters.
- **Effective Date:** Trading permitted with effect from Monday, October 26, 2026.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 69436949 to 74387443
- **Date of Allotment:** 01/10/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice for newly allotted preferential shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and trading parameters.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 1, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** May 4, 2027 (for 4,950,495 shares, Dist. Nos. 69436949 to 74387443)

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of ACS Technologies Limited through preferential allotment to non-promoter investors with standard lock-in restrictions.
