## Summary

Following Notice No. 20260826-36 dated August 26, 2026, THE WEALTH COMPANY MULTI CAP FUND is reopening for ongoing transactions including subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reopening of ongoing transactions for THE WEALTH COMPANY MULTI CAP FUND.
- Effective date for availability on BSE StAR MF Platform is September 21, 2026.
- Covers multiple ISINs across Regular and Direct plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the specified scheme variants through the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026
- Notice Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute transactions and systematic investment/withdrawal plans for the mentioned mutual fund schemes seamlessly on the BSE platform.
