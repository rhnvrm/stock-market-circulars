## Summary

Trading members of the exchange are informed that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of RATNAVEER PRECISION ENGINEERING LIMITED are permitted on the Exchange with effect from Wednesday, September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: RATNAVEER PRECISION ENGINEERING LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 751135
- Scrip ID: RATNA-RE
- ISIN No.: INE05CZ20011
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: September 02, 2026 to September 03, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: September 02, 2026 to September 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares. Members must include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes/bills informing clients that purchase of REs only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before the issue closes.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Start Date: September 02, 2026
- RE Trading End Date: September 03, 2026
- Rights Issue Opening Date: September 02, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Rights Entitlements will be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis and trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only. Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the Issue Closing Date.
