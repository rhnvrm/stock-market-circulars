## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies. The measures include 100% margins, movement to higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price bands, transfers to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS), and removal of securities from the framework as applicable.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. August 10, 2026, on all open positions as on August 07, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Movement of securities to higher stages of LT-ASM Framework and applicability of Lower Price Band w.e.f. August 06, 2026.
- Transfer of certain securities continuing in LT-ASM to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. August 10, 2026.
- Moving out of securities from LT-ASM Framework w.e.f. August 06, 2026, with price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL disclosures, not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised LT-ASM framework measures including enhanced margins, price band restrictions, and group migrations for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and adjusted price bands for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Higher Stages / Price Bands / Moving Out: August 06, 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: August 07, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in shortlisted securities may experience reduced liquidity and stricter margin constraints due to surveillance measures.
