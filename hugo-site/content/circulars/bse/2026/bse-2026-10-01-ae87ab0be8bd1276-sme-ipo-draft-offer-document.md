## Summary

P.P. Books Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with the stock exchange on September 29, 2026, containing disclosures specified under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations for its SME IPO. The document is hosted on the BSE SME website for public review and comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: P.P. Books Limited
- Filing Date: September 29, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Department: DCS-Listing (SME Segment)
- Hosting Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx
- Feedback Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations regarding disclosures and public consultation for SME Initial Public Offerings.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public are invited to submit comments or observations on the Draft Offer Document within 21 days from the date of the notice to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 29, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Comments Deadline: 21 days from the notice date

## Impact Assessment

Provides market transparency and enables public participation in the evaluation of prospective SME listings on the exchange.
