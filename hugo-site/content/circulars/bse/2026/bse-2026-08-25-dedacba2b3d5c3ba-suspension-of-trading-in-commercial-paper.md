## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers for various companies effective from their respective record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260825-55
- Department: Listing Operations
- Segment: Debt Segment
- Action: Suspension of trading in commercial papers due to redemption record dates.
- Affected entities include IGH Holdings, Birla Group Holdings, Chennai Petroleum Corporation Ltd., Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, KEC International Ltd., Titan Company Limited, Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited, Capri Global Capital Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Aarti Industries Ltd, and Tata Housing Development Company Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the mentioned companies on or after the specified record/ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 25 August 2026
- Record / Ex-Dates: 31/08/2026, 02/09/2026, 03/09/2026, and 04/09/2026 depending on the specific commercial paper.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified commercial papers is temporarily or permanently restricted post record date to facilitate smooth redemption processes.
