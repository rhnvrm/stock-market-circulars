## Summary

BSE has informed market participants about the listing and trading permission of 86,46,000 equity shares of Citizen Solar Limited, issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation of Citizen Solar Private Limited with Citizen Solar Limited, effective from August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Citizen Solar Limited (Scrip Code: 538786)
- Security Details: 86,46,000 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation.
- Effective Date of Trading: Wednesday, August 12, 2026.
- ISIN Number: INE473L01018
- Distribution Nos.: 5397301 – 14043300
- Date of Allotment: April 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard compliance regarding the trading and settlement of newly listed equity shares pursuant to corporate restructuring.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: April 03, 2026
- Notice Date: August 11, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of Citizen Solar Limited following amalgamation without direct wider market impact.
