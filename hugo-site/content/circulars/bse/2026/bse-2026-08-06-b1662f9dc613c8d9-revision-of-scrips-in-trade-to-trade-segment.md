## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the revision of scrips in the Trade-to-Trade (T / XT / MT / TS) segment as a preventive surveillance measure. Effective from August 11, 2026, certain scrips will be transferred into the trade-to-trade segment, some will continue based on existing categories, and others will be shifted back to their original groups.

## Key Points

- Scrips in Annexure I Part A will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group and Part B will continue to remain in T / XT / MT / TS Group, effective August 11, 2026.
- Trading and settlement for these scrips will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off positions allowed.
- A VaR Margin of 100% will be levied on these scrips, and they will continue to attract a price band of 5% or lower.
- Scrips in Annexure II are eligible for transfer but will remain in their respective P, Y, Z, ZP & ZY groups as they are already on a trade-to-trade basis.
- Scrips in Annexure III currently in the T / XT / MT / TS Group will be shifted back to their original groups effective August 11, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of preventive surveillance measures by transferring specified scrips to and from the trade-to-trade settlement category based on XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected scrips since settlements will be executed on a trade-to-trade basis without netting off positions, subject to a 100% VaR margin.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 06, 2026
- Effective Date: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity for the affected scrips will be subject to strict settlement and margin requirements (100% VaR and trade-to-trade delivery), which may impact short-term liquidity and volatility for the specific securities.
