## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) due to record dates fixed for interest payment and redemption by Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited and Moneyboxx Finance Limited.

## Key Points

- **Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited**: Scrip Code 976164 (Scrip ID: NWFL-9.5%-9-11-26-PVT, ISIN: INE918K07PP7). Record Date: 25/10/2026, Ex-Date: 23/10/2026.
- **Moneyboxx Finance Limited**: Scrip Code 976174 (Scrip ID: MFL-12%-11-11-26-PVT, ISIN: INE296Q07068). Record Date: 27/10/2026, Ex-Date: 27/10/2026.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures of these companies as per the announced schedule.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard operational notice for debt segment corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the restriction on trading debentures on and from the respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **Nuvama Ex-Date**: 23/10/2026
- **Nuvama Record Date**: 25/10/2026
- **Moneyboxx Ex-Date & Record Date**: 27/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational impact limited to holders of the respective non-convertible debentures.
