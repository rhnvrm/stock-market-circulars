## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Securities satisfying specific criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II, effective September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%, effective September 02, 2026, on all open positions as on September 01, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, subject to a maximum of 100%, effective September 02, 2026, on all open positions as on September 01, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the ST-ASM framework for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities as per the assigned ST-ASM stage.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 31, 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
