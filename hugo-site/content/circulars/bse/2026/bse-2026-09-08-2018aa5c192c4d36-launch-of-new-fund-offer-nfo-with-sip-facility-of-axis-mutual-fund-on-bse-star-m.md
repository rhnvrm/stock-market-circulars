## Summary

AXIS Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the AXIS Nifty500 Low Volatility 50 Index Fund along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from September 9, 2026, to September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: AXIS Nifty500 Low Volatility 50 Index Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1606) and Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1614).
- NFO Period: September 09, 2026, to September 22, 2026 (extended till midnight for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 28, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (September 22, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 28, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: September 9, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: September 22, 2026
- SIP Authentication Deadline: September 22, 2026 (up to 3:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the new low volatility index fund scheme through the BSE StAR MF platform with specific operational guidelines for funds pay-in and allotment.
