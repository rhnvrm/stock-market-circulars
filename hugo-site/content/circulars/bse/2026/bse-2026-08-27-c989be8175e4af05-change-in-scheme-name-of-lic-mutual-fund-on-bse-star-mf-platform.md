## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants and members about the change in scheme names for several schemes of LIC Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective immediately, as per communication received from LIC Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260827-36
- Effective Date: With immediate effect (27 August 2026)
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform
- Affected Fund House: LIC Mutual Fund

## Regulatory Changes

- LIC ULTRA SHORT DURATION FUND is renamed to LIC MF ULTRA SHORT TERM FUND
- LIC MF LOW DURATION FUND is renamed to LIC MF ULTRA SHORT TO SHORT TERM FUND
- LIC MF MEDIUM TO LONG DURATION FUND is renamed to LIC MF MEDIUM TO LONG TERM FUND
- LIC SHORT DURATION FUND is renamed to LIC MF SHORT TERM FUND
- LIC MF BANKING AND PSU FUND is renamed to LIC MF BANKING AND PSU DEBT FUND
- LIC MF ELSS TAX SAVER is renamed to LIC MF ELSS TAX SAVER FUND

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names while placing orders or executing transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 27 August 2026
- Implementation Date: Immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating scheme descriptions and names in trading terminals and portfolios.
