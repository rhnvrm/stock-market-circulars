## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-706/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Offers will be accepted on trading terminals on Wednesday, September 02, 2026.
- Auction Pay-in is scheduled for Thursday, September 03, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- Restrictions on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they faced short delivery have been removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.

## Important Dates

- **Auction Offer Entry:** 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. (02 Sep 2026)
- **Auction Matching:** 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. (02 Sep 2026)
- **Delivery Statement & Report:** 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m. (02 Sep 2026)
- **Money Statement & Final Reports:** 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. (02 Sep 2026)
- **Pay-in of Auction:** 03 Sep 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for managing short deliveries in rolling settlements with no broad market impact.
