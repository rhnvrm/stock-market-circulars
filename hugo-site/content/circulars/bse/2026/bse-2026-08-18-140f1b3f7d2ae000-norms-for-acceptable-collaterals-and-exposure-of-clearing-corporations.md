## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has issued a revised list of eligible banks effective August 18, 2026, in accordance with SEBI circular dated May 29, 2024, concerning the norms for acceptable collaterals and exposure of Clearing Corporations.

## Key Points

- Updated list contains 36 eligible banks, including public sector, private sector, and foreign banks.
- Replaces the previous list circulated via notice dated July 17, 2026.
- Selection follows Section 3.2 Criteria for selection of Banks of the referenced SEBI circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- Maintenance of the updated eligible bank criteria for member/custodian/PCM/bank collaterals and exposure management.

## Compliance Requirements

- All Members, Custodians, PCMs, and Banks must take note of the revised list for accepting and maintaining collaterals and exposure limits.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational alignment for clearing members and custodians regarding acceptable banking partners and collateral provisioning.
