## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, October 17, 2026. Trading members using third-party trading platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their trading applications under normal and exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 17, 2026, for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment.
- Members can test trading functionalities including risk-reduction mode, trading halt, and order entry.
- Session timings: Log-in at 09:15 am, Pre-Open from 10:00 am, Continuous Trading from 10:15 am to 01:00 pm, and Closing at 01:10 pm.
- Trades resulting from mock trading do not attract any margin obligations or pay-in/pay-out obligations.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 02:00 pm via designated contact numbers or email.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational mock trading notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to actively participate in the mock trading session for system verification and familiarization.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 17, 2026
- Feedback Submission Deadline: October 17, 2026 by 02:00 pm

## Impact Assessment

Zero financial or operational impact on live markets as trades are strictly for testing and familiarization.
