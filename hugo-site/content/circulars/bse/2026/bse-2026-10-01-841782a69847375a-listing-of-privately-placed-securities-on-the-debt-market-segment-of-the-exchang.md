## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers and debt securities on its debt market segment effective October 1, 2026. The notice covers fresh listings and further listings of commercial papers from issuers such as Avenue Supermarts Limited, Axis Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited, Capri Global Capital Limited, and debt securities from Nabkisan Finance.

## Key Points

- **Avenue Supermarts Limited**: Issued Commercial Paper (Scrip Code: 732698, ISIN: INE192R14394, Amount: Rs. 5 Lakhs face value, Issue Price: Rs. 492,567.00, Interest Rate: 6.12% p.a., Maturity Date: 29/12/2026).
- **Axis Finance Limited**: Issued Commercial Paper (Scrip Code: 732699, ISIN: INE891K14QJ3, Face Value: Rs. 5 Lakhs, Issue Price: Rs. 469,609.00, Interest Rate: 7.90% p.a., Maturity Date: 26/07/2027).
- **Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited**: Issued Commercial Paper (Scrip Code: 732700, ISIN: INE831R14GP2, Face Value: Rs. 5 Lakhs, Issue Price: Rs. 493,313.00, Interest Rate: 6.51% p.a., Maturity Date: 15/12/2026).
- **Capri Global Capital Limited**: Further listing of Commercial Paper (Scrip Code: 732188, ISIN: INE180C14938, Face Value: Rs. 5 Lakhs, Issue Price: Rs. 481,308.00, Interest Rate: 8.15% p.a., Maturity Date: 25/03/2027).
- **Trading Specifications**: Traded in dematerialised form with standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof; tick size is 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate debt listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard dematerialised trading norms and ISIN specifications.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date**: 30/09/2026 (for fresh issues), 01/10/2026 (for further issue)
- **Effective Listing Date**: 01/10/2026
- **Redemption Dates**: Ranging from 15/12/2026 to 26/07/2027 depending on the instrument.

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official exchange admission for privately placed commercial papers and debt instruments, facilitating secondary market trading for institutional investors.
