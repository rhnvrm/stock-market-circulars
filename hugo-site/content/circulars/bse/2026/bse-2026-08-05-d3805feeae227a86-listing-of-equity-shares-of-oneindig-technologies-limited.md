## Summary

Effective from Thursday, August 6, 2026, the equity shares of Oneindig Technologies Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE SME segment under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544852
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: ONEINDIG
- ISIN No.: INE0UR501013
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 96/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 86/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 4, 2026
- Total Securities Listed: 10,924,160 Equity Shares
- Market Maker: Share India Securities Limited
- Registrar: Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Subject to Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing day (August 6, 2026).
- Trade-for-Trade segment applicability for the first 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in the prescribed minimum market lot of 1,200 shares.
- Lock-in period has been duly marked in the depository's records for applicable shares as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 5, 2026
- Date of Allotment: August 4, 2026
- Listing and Trading Commencement Date: August 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity to investors of Oneindig Technologies Limited through the BSE SME platform while applying standard surveillance and settlement controls (Trade-for-Trade) for the initial trading period.
