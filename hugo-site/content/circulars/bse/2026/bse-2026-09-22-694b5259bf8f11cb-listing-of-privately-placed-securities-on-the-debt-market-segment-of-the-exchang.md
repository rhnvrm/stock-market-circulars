## Summary

BSE has notified the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities issued by Avaada Ventures Private Limited and Piramal Finance Limited on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Avaada Ventures Private Limited has listed two tranches of NCDs (Scrip Codes: 978177 and 978178) carrying an interest rate of 11.75% p.a. maturing in September 2029.
- Piramal Finance Limited has listed non-convertible debentures (Scrip Code: 979179) carrying an interest rate of 8.57% p.a. maturing in November 2029.
- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to the specified ISIN numbers and dematerialised settlement norms.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 22, 2026
- **Allotment Dates:** September 18, 2026 (Avaada Ventures), September 21, 2026 (Piramal Finance)
- **Redemption Dates:** September 18, 2026 / September 17, 2029 (Avaada Ventures), November 5, 2029 (Piramal Finance)

## Impact Assessment

Provides secondary market liquidity for the newly listed privately placed debt instruments on the BSE debt segment.
