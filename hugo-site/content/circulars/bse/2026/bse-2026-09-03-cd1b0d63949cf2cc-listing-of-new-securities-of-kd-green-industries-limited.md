## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of KD Green Industries Limited (Scrip Code - 512595) effective from September 4, 2026. A total of 4,35,00,000 equity shares were issued to promoters and non-promoters on a preferential basis upon warrant conversion.

## Key Points

- Company Name: KD Green Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 512595
- ISIN: INE348N01042
- Securities Issued: 4,35,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 5.50/- (Issue Price: Rs. 6.50/-)
- Allotment Date: 30/05/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: 04/09/2026
- Distribution Nos.: 58010501 to 101510500

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Shares are subject to lock-in requirements as specified:
- 1,55,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 58010501 - 73510500) locked-in upto 31/03/2028.
- 2,80,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 73510501 - 101510500) locked-in upto 31/03/2027.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 30/05/2026
- Listing/Trading Commencement Date: 04/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion via warrant conversion with associated lock-in periods for promoters and non-promoters.
