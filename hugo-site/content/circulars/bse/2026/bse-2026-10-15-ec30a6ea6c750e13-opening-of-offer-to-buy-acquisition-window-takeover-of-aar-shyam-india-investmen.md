## Summary

An open offer has been made by the acquirers (Mr. Radha Krishna Avudari, Mrs. Sudha Rani Avudari, and Mr. Nagabhyru Srikanth) to acquire up to 58,43,327 fully paid-up equity shares, representing 26% of the emerging paid-up equity share capital of AAR Shyam India Investment Company Limited, at an offer price of Rs. 15.00 per share.

## Key Points

- Target Company: AAR Shyam India Investment Company Limited.
- Acquirers: Mr. Radha Krishna Avudari, Mrs. Sudha Rani Avudari, and Mr. Nagabhyru Srikanth.
- Offer Price: Rs. 15.00/- per fully paid-up equity share payable in cash.
- Offer Size: Up to 58,43,327 fully paid equity shares (26% of emerging paid-up equity share capital).
- Regulatory Framework: SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 (Regulation 3(1) and Regulation 4).
- Offer Period: Monday, October 19, 2026 to Monday, November 02, 2026 (Excluding SEBI Holiday on Tuesday, October 20, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted pursuant to and in compliance with SEBI circular no. CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015, and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 dated December 9, 2016, along with revised guidelines and exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians are required to follow the acquisition window mechanism for tender offers under takeover regulations.
- Eligible equity shareholders may tender their equity shares through the stock exchange platform during the specified offer period.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Offer Opening Date: October 19, 2026
- SEBI Holiday (No Operations): October 20, 2026
- Offer Closing Date: November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates the takeover open offer process for eligible shareholders of AAR Shyam India Investment Company Limited to tender shares during the acquisition window.
