## Summary

BSE StAR MF has announced the discontinuation of fresh subscriptions, switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-In) in select schemes of Aditya Birla Sun Life Mutual Fund, effective from the close of business hours on September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Schemes: Aditya Birla Sun Life Global Excellence Equity Fund of Fund, Aditya Birla Sun Life Global Emerging Opportunities Fund, and Aditya Birla Sun Life International Equity Fund.
- Lumpsum investments, switch-ins, and new SIP/STP-In registrations have been disabled on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Existing SIP/XSIP and STP-In registrations made prior to the effective date will continue to be processed normally.
- Redemption, Switch-Out, STP-Out, and SWP transactions remain fully permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of investment restrictions on specific international and global fund of fund schemes as per AMC communication.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and distributors must ensure that fresh subscription orders and new systematic registrations are not initiated for the specified schemes. Orders received prior to the effective date but triggered to RTA on or after September 25, 2026, will be rejected and refunded.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date/Time: Close of business hours on September 24, 2026.
- Rejection / Refund Trigger Date: Orders triggered to RTA on or after September 25, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding existing systematic plans in these schemes are unaffected, but new investors and fresh capital allocations into these specific Aditya Birla Sun Life international schemes are halted.
