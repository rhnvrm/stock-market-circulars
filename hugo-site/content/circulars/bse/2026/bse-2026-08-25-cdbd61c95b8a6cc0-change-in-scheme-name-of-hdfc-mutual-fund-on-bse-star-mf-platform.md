## Summary

HDFC Mutual Fund has communicated changes in scheme names for several of its mutual fund schemes available on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260825-58
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Department: Trading Operations
- Mutual Fund: HDFC Mutual Fund

### Scheme Name Changes:
1. HDFC Dynamic Debt Fund -> HDFC Dynamic Term Fund
2. HDFC ELSS Tax Saver -> HDFC ELSS - Tax Saver Fund
3. HDFC Credit Risk Debt Fund -> HDFC Credit Risk Fund
4. HDFC Medium Term Debt Fund -> HDFC Medium Term Fund
5. HDFC Low Duration Fund -> HDFC Ultra Short To Short Term Fund
6. HDFC Large and Mid Cap Fund -> HDFC Large & Mid Cap Fund
7. HDFC Long Duration Debt Fund -> HDFC Long Term Fund
8. HDFC Retirement Savings Fund -> HDFC Retirement Fund
9. HDFC Income Fund -> HDFC Medium To Long Term Fund
10. HDFC Floating Rate Debt Fund -> HDFC Floating Interest Rates Fund
11. HDFC Short Term Debt Fund -> HDFC Short Term Fund
12. HDFC Hybrid Debt Fund -> HDFC Conservative Hybrid Fund
13. HDFC Hybrid Equity Fund -> HDFC Aggressive Hybrid Fund
14. HDFC Multi-Asset Allocation Fund -> HDFC Multi Asset Allocation Fund

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; administrative scheme name updates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names while placing orders on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating nomenclature across systems and platforms for the affected HDFC Mutual Fund schemes.
