## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and/or redemption of their non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures and bonds of these companies effective from the respective record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice covers multiple NCDs and bonds across various issuers including Essel Lucknow Raebareli Toll Roads Limited, Sheela Foam Limited, Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, Svatantra Microfin Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, L&T Finance Limited, IIFL Finance Limited, Kosamattam Finance Limited, Power Finance Corporation Ltd, and Indian Railway Finance Corporation.
- Record dates and ex-dates span from September 21, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Trading members are advised not to trade in these debentures during the restricted period.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified debentures and bonds of the listed companies as per the announced record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: September 21, 2026, to September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for debt segment corporate actions, ensuring smooth interest payment and redemption processing without market interference.
