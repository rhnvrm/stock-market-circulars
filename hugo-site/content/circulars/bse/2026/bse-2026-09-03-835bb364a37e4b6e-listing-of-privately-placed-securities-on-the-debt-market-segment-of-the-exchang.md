## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis effective September 3, 2026. The securities include issuances by DSP Finance Private Limited, Interise Trust, Mirae Asset Capital Markets (India) Private Limited, and Sharekhan Limited.

## Key Points

- Securities are listed and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective September 03, 2026.
- Traded strictly in dematerialised form under specific ISIN numbers.
- Standard trading denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuances include commercial papers from DSP Finance Private Limited, Interise Trust, Mirae Asset Capital Markets, and Sharekhan Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard commercial paper trading norms, dematerialised form requirements, and standard denominations.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** 02/09/2026
- **Listing Date:** 03/09/2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Ranging from 01/10/2026 to 02/12/2026 depending on the specific tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update facilitating secondary market trading for newly issued commercial papers.
