## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for specified securities. The surveillance actions come into effect on July 31, 2026, impacting open positions and new positions created from that date.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective July 31, 2026, on all open positions as on July 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective July 31, 2026, on all open positions as on July 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II framework guidelines as per previous exchange notices.
- Reinstatement of price bands upon moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100%) for securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II respectively.
- Members may contact bse.surv@bseindia.com for clarifications.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 31, 2026
- Open Positions Baseline Date: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may restrict leverage and impact liquidity and trading volumes for affected scrips.
