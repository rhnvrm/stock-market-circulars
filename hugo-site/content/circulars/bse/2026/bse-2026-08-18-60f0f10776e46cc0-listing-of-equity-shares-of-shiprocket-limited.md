## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, August 19, 2026, the equity shares of Shiprocket Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: Shiprocket Limited
- Scrip Code: 544871
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SHIPROCKET
- ISIN No: INE0FOO01011
- Total No. of Securities: 72,75,77,587 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 97/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 87/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 17, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action has been executed to have the lock-in period marked in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members can trade in the equity shares of Shiprocket Limited starting August 19, 2026, under the 'B' Group category.
