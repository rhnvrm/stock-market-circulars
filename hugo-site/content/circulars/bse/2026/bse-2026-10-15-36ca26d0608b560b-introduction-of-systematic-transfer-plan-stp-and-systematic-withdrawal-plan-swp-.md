## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) facilities under ISIF Investment Strategies of ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Facilities introduced: Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP).
- Fund House: ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund (SIF).
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026.
- Applicable Schemes:
  - ISIF ACTIVE ASSET ALLOCATOR LONG-SHORT FUND
  - ISIF EQUITY EX-TOP 100 LONG - SHORT FUND
  - ISIF EQUITY LONG-SHORT FUND
  - ISIF HYBRID LONG-SHORT FUND

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational update regarding platform feature extensions for mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can utilize the STP and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF platform for the specified schemes starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides enhanced flexibility for investors and members executing systematic transactions (STP/SWP) in ICICI Prudential ISIF schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform.
