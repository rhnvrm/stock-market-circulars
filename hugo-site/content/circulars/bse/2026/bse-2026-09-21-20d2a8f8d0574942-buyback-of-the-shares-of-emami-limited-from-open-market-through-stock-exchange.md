## Summary

EMAMI LIMITED has announced the buyback of its fully paid-up equity shares of face value of Rs. 1/- each from the open market through the Stock Exchanges, in accordance with SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018. The maximum buyback price is set at Rs. 475/- per equity share for an aggregate amount not exceeding Rs. 28,200/- Lakhs.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Emami Limited
- Maximum Buyback Price: Rs. 475/- per equity share
- Maximum Buyback Size: Rs. 28,200/- Lakhs
- Date of Opening of the Buyback: Tuesday, September 22, 2026
- Last Date of Buyback: Earlier of 66 working days (December 29, 2026), completion of deployment of maximum buyback size, or earlier closure date determined by the Board.
- Company's Broker: IIFL Capital Services Limited
- Registrar to the Buyback: Maheshwari Datamatics Pvt Ltd

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with the SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 updated as of July 6, 2026, and Exchange Notice No. 20260731-9 dated July 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- All payment obligations relating to the buyback shall be completed before the last date for the buyback.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 21, 2026
- Opening Date: Tuesday, September 22, 2026
- Last Date: Tuesday, December 29, 2026 (or earlier upon completion/notice)

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of Emami Limited can offer their equity shares for buyback through the open market mechanism on the stock exchanges starting from September 22, 2026.
