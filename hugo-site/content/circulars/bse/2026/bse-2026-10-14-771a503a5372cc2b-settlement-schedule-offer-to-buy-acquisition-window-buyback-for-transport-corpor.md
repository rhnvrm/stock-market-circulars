## Summary

BSE has announced the settlement schedule and acquisition window for the buyback of equity shares of Transport Corporation of India Limited (Notice No. 20261014-39). The bidding window will be open from October 15, 2026, to October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Transport Corporation of India Limited
- **Market Type & Settlement No.:** Buyback, Settlement No. 2026124
- **Bid Start Date:** 15/10/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 22/10/2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 27/10/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 28/10/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine settlement schedule notification for corporate buyback via stock exchange acquisition window.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL & NSDL).
- Members and custodians must use Market Type (Buyback) and Settlement No. 2026124.
- CDSL & NSDL early pay-in securities will be earmarked / blocked in clients' accounts; members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.
