## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of ESAAR (INDIA) LIMITED are permitted on the Exchange with effect from Wednesday, September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: ESAAR (INDIA) LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Trading Period for Renunciation: September 02, 2026 to September 08, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: September 02, 2026 to September 11, 2026
- Scrip Code: 751136
- Scrip ID: ESARIND-RE
- ISIN No.: INE404L20013
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members shall inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and clarifying that they should not be mistaken for ordinary shares. A prominent disclaimer must be included in contract notes/bills informing clients that purchase of REs only gives them the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before the issue closes.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Start Date: September 02, 2026
- RE Trading End Date: September 08, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: September 02, 2026
- Rights Issue Close Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The Rights Entitlements shall be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.
