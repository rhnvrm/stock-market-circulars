## Summary

360 ONE Mutual Fund has notified the exchange regarding the change in scheme name for one of its schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect, changing '360 ONE DYNAMIC BOND FUND' to '360 ONE Dynamic Term Fund'.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: With immediate effect from September 3, 2026.
- Existing Name: 360 ONE DYNAMIC BOND FUND
- Revised Name: 360 ONE DYNAMIC TERM FUND
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme name for future transactions on the platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 3, 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating nomenclature in trading and reporting systems.
