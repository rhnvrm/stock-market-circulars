## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities qualifying under ESM are subject to specific surveillance actions, including minimum 100% margins, movement to the Trade-for-Trade segment, and price band restrictions.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for ESM inclusion, attracting a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 15, 2026, on open positions as of October 14, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities moving to lower Stage I under ESM framework effective October 14, 2026, are included in Annexure I.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is given in Annexure III.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 14, 2026.
- Securities moving out from the ESM framework effective October 14, 2026, are listed in Annexure II.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) including 100% margin requirements and shifting from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Price band restrictions (2%) and Periodic Call Auction for securities moving to Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the margin requirements and settlement segment changes for the affected securities.
- Clarifications can be sought via email at bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **October 14, 2026**: Movement of securities to lower Stage I / Stage II / moving out of ESM.
- **October 15, 2026**: Minimum 100% margin effective on open and new positions; shifting from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and investors holding affected securities face increased margin requirements (100%) and liquidity/trading restrictions due to the Trade-for-Trade segment and price bands.
