## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the opening of the acquisition window for the open offer to acquire up to 48,75,000 fully paid-up equity shares, representing 26.00% of the voting share capital of The South India Paper Mills Limited. The offer price is Rs. 120/- per share payable in cash, and the open offer period runs from Thursday, October 22, 2026, to Wednesday, November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Target Company: The South India Paper Mills Limited.
- Acquirers & PACs: Nandini Modi, Kirit Modi, Sachin Kirit Modi, Swapnil Kirit Modi, Riddhi Sachin Modi, Bhuvi Swapnil Modi, Rihaan Sachin Modi, Rigid Containers Private Limited, and Fortune Packaging LLP.
- Offer Size: Up to 48,75,000 equity shares (26.00% of voting share capital) of face value Rs. 10.00/- each.
- Offer Price: Rs. 120/- per share payable in cash.
- Open Offer Period: From Thursday, October 22, 2026, to Wednesday, November 04, 2026.
- Purpose: Substantial acquisition of shares and voting rights accompanied by change in control pursuant to Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in compliance with SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, and SEBI circulars on the mechanism for acquisition of shares through stock exchange pursuant to tender offers (CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the operational guidelines and schedule for tender-offers under the takeover acquisition window.
- Eligible public shareholders can tender their shares through stock exchange trading members during the designated acquisition window.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026.
- Offer Opening Date: October 22, 2026.
- Offer Closing Date: November 04, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public shareholders of The South India Paper Mills Limited to participate in the open offer and tender their shares through the stock exchange mechanism within the specified timeline.
