## Summary

SBI Mutual Fund has announced the merger of SBI CPSE Bond Plus SDL Sep 2026 50:50 Index Fund (Transferor Scheme) into SBI Banking and PSU Debt Fund (Transferee Scheme). The merger becomes effective after business hours on October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Exit load is waived for investors wishing to redeem or switch investments during the period from September 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Fresh subscription transactions under the Transferor scheme will continue to be accepted up to September 25, 2026, for Demat mode and September 30, 2026, for Non-Demat mode on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Existing systematic investment facilities (SIP/STP/SWP) will automatically transition to the Transferee scheme upon positive consent from investors or as per AMC lists after September 30, 2026.
- The Transferor scheme will cease to be available for transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform after business hours on September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors must take note of transaction timelines and submit cancellation requests if they do not wish to continue systematic investment facilities post-merger.

## Important Dates

- Exit Load Waiver Period: September 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026
- Demat Subscription Cut-off: September 25, 2026
- Non-Demat Subscription Cut-off: September 30, 2026
- Cessation of Transferor Scheme Transactions: September 30, 2026 (close of business hours)
- Effective Date of Merger: October 01, 2026 (after close of business hours)

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding units in the Transferor scheme are provided seamless transition options or exit opportunities without penalty, minimizing disruption on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
