## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the review and identification of securities placed under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for securities witnessing unusual price movements, continuing from previous circulars.

## Key Points

- Review conducted to identify securities meeting criteria for the Additional Measure framework.
- Annexure I lists securities newly included under the measure.
- Annexure II lists securities moving out of the measure.
- Annexure III provides a consolidated list of all securities currently under the framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions and trading restrictions on identified securities in accordance with established Exchange frameworks for unusual price movements.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the included and excluded securities and adjust their trading and risk parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects liquidity and margin requirements for the specific securities entering or exiting the Additional Surveillance Measure framework.
