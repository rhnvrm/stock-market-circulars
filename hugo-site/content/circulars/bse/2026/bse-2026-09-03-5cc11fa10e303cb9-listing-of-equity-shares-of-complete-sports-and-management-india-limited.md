## Summary

Effective from Friday, September 4, 2026, the equity shares of Complete Sports and Management India Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Complete Sports and Management India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544900
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: CSML
- ISIN: INE2K6801014
- Total Securities: 2,05,60,000 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 135/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 125/-)
- Market Lot: 1,000 Equity Shares
- Segment: SME (Trade-for-Trade for 10 trading days)
- Date of Allotment: September 2, 2026
- Market Maker: Shreni Shares Limited
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action has been executed for lock-in marking in depository records for applicable shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 2, 2026
- Listing and Trading Commencement Date: September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members can trade in the scrip starting September 4, 2026, with compulsory demat settlement and a minimum market lot of 1,000 shares under the Trade-for-Trade segment.
