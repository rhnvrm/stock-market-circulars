## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT), vide its order dated July 07, 2026, held the period of debarment already undergone as sufficient and set aside the findings/observations of the Whole Time Member (WTM) with regard to PFUTP Regulations in the matter of Trifid Research. SEBI directed compliance with this SAT order via email dated August 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- SAT order dated July 07, 2026, concluded that the period of debarment already undergone by Trifid Research is sufficient.
- Findings and observations of the WTM regarding PFUTP Regulations have been set aside.
- SEBI directed compliance with the SAT order via email on August 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of previous penalties/debarment status as directed by SAT.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the SAT order and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: July 07, 2026
- SEBI Email / Notice Date: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal, serving primarily as a compliance update regarding regulatory and appellate tribunal directions.
