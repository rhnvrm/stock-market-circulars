## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of Paisalo Digital Limited effective from Thursday, August 20, 2026, under the 'F GROUP'.

## Key Points

- Securities: Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of Paisalo Digital Limited.
- Trading Effective Date: August 20, 2026.
- Market Lot: Unit market lot (i.e., One Bond).
- Form of Trading: Dematerialized form only, as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Company Financial Year ends on March 31.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in dematerialized form and unit market lots.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 19, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice with minimal direct market-wide impact, relevant primarily to market participants trading in Paisalo Digital Limited NCDs.
