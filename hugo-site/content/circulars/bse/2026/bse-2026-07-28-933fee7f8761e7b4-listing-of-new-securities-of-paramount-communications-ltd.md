## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Paramount Communications Ltd. (Scrip Code - 530555) effective from Wednesday, July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security:** 2,19,97,664 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 40/- to non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 42/-
- **ISIN:** INE074B01023
- **Effective Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to 02/02/2027 for the specified distribution numbers.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the listing and lock-in details of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 01/07/2026
- **Listing Date:** 29/07/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 02/02/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard preferential allotment and listing of equity shares with standard lock-in requirements.
