## Summary

As part of surveillance measures and based on the extant SEBI framework, BSE has announced the transfer of various securities to the Trade-to-Trade (T / XT / MT / TS Group) segment effective September 18, 2026. Securities mentioned in Annexure II will continue to remain in restrictive groups with a 5% price band.

## Key Points

- Transfer of securities satisfying surveillance criteria to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. September 18, 2026.
- Continuation of certain securities in T / XT / MT / TS / P / Z / ZP Group attracting a 5% price band.
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off positions allowed.
- A 100% VAR margin is levied on these scrips as per Exchange Notice No. 20050805-12.
- Action is purely a preventive surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance framework criteria for moving specific securities into trade-to-trade settlement based on XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, factoring in the trade-to-trade settlement rules and 100% VAR margin requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date of Transfer: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity for the affected scrips may be impacted as intraday netting off is prohibited and full margin requirements apply, reducing speculative trading and safeguarding investor interests.
