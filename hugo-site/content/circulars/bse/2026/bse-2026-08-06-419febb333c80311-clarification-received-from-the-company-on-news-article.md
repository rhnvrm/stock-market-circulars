BSE sought clarification from Vedanta Aluminium Metal Ltd and Cello World Ltd regarding various news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels to verify accuracy and inform investors.

## Key Points

- Clarification sought from Vedanta Aluminium Metal Ltd (Scrip Code: 544780).
- Clarification sought from Cello World Ltd (Scrip Code: 544012).
- Replies received from the respective companies are attached by the exchange.
