## Summary

BSE has announced the revised conversion factors for converting from USD to GBP, EURO, and JPY, effective from October 29, 2026. These factors are utilized by trading members to monitor the aggregate position limit of USD 100 million equivalent across currency derivative contracts including GBP-INR, EUR-INR, and JPY-INR.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 29, 2026.
- Revised Conversion Factors:
  - USD to EURO: 0.87
  - USD to GBP: 0.74
  - USD to JPY: 159.53
- Previous conversion factors from Exchange notice no. 20260625-24 remain applicable through October 28, 2026.
- Aggregate position limit across currencies remains restricted to USD 100 million equivalent.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the conversion rates for currency derivatives position limit monitoring in continuation of previous notices (No. 20150430-20 and 20180403-39).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure aggregate positions across exchanges stay within the prescribed limit of USD 100 million equivalent.
- If positions exceed USD 100 million equivalent, clients are required to provide a disclosure of underlying exposure to the Trading Member.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Previous Conversion Factors Valid Till: October 28, 2026
- New Conversion Factors Effective Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts trading members and market participants dealing in currency derivatives by updating exchange rates used for calculating equivalent USD positions.
