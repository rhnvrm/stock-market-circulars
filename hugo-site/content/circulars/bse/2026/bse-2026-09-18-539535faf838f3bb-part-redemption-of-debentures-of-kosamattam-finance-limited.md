## Summary

Kosamattam Finance Limited has announced a record date for the payment of interest and part redemption of its Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) / Bonds. Trading in the debentures will continue with a reduced face value effective from the specified record and ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kosamattam Finance Limited
- Scrip Code: 976897
- Scrip ID: KFL-10.62%-09-07-27-PVT
- ISIN: INE403Q07FZ7
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 75,000/- per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are requested to note the reduced face value and record date for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 22/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 22/09/2026
- Effective Date for Reduced Face Value: 22/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the debentures will receive part redemption payments and interest as per the corporate action, with subsequent trading reflecting the reduced face value of Rs. 75,000/- per debenture.
