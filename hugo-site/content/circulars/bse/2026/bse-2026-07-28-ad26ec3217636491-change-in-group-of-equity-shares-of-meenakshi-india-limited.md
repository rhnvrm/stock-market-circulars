## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Meenakshi India Limited (Scrip Code: 544831) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment with effect from Tuesday, August 11, 2026, and will be placed under the “B” Group.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Meenakshi India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544831
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to Rolling segment ("B" Group)
- Effective Date: Tuesday, August 11, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in equity shares shifts from compulsory settlement in the Trade for Trade segment to normal rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to take note of the segment change for executing trades accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced liquidity and normal trading settlement for shareholders of Meenakshi India Limited following the exit from the Trade for Trade segment.
