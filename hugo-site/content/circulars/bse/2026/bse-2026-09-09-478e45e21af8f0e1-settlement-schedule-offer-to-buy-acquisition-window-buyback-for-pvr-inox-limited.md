## Summary

BSECL has issued a settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Buyback) for PVR INOX LIMITED, scheduled to be conducted from September 10, 2026, to September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Buyback
- Settlement No.: 2026112
- Bid Start Date: 10/09/2026
- Bid End Date: 17/09/2026
- Obligation Download: 23/09/2026
- Settlement Date: 24/09/2026
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Participants must ensure that early pay-in of securities reflects in BSE/BSECL systems before prescribed timelines. CDSL and NSDL early pay-in securities will be earmarked or blocked in clients' accounts using the correct market type and settlement number.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: September 10, 2026
- Bid End Date: September 17, 2026
- Obligation Download: September 23, 2026
- Settlement Date: September 24, 2026
