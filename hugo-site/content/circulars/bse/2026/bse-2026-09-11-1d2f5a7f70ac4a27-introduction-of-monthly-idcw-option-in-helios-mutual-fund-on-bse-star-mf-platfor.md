## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about the introduction of the Monthly Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) option under Helios Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Scheme involved: Helios Balanced Advantage Fund.
- Facility type: Monthly IDCW Option with Reinvestment & Payout Facility.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Effective date: Immediate effect (11 September 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational product availability update on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can access the newly introduced scheme option for client transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 11 September 2026
- Effective Date: 11 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to transact in the monthly IDCW option of Helios Balanced Advantage Fund through the BSE StAR MF infrastructure.
