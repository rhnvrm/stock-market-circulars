## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants and members about the change in scheme names for multiple schemes of Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from September 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260902-47, dated September 2, 2026.
- Applies to 16 schemes of Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund.
- Changes are effective immediately on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Key renames include KOTAK BOND FUND to KOTAK MEDIUM TO LONG TERM FUND, KOTAK FLEXICAP FUND to KOTAK FLEXI CAP FUND, and several overseas and hybrid funds.

## Regulatory Changes

- Administrative updates to scheme nomenclature as communicated by Kotak Mahindra AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and intermediaries must update their systems and records with the revised scheme names to avoid discrepancies during order placement.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is minimal, requiring updates to internal databases and client-facing interfaces for accurate scheme identification.
