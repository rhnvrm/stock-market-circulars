## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the submission of the Internal Audit Report for the half year ended September 30, 2026, through the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS). Trading members are required to submit their reports along with the specified annexures within the stipulated timelines.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261016-18 dated October 16, 2026.
- Applicable to trading members for the half year ended September 30, 2026.
- Submissions must be made electronically via the BEFS portal.
- Includes multiple annexures (Annexure I through Annexure VIII) required for comprehensive reporting.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory framework introduced; this is a periodic compliance reminder and procedural circular for half-yearly internal audit report submissions under existing exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must complete their internal audits and upload the Internal Audit Report along with all requisite annexures (Annexure_I, Annexure_II, Annexure_III, Annexure_IIIA, Annexure_IV, Annexure_V, Annexure_VI, Annexure_VII, and Annexure_VIII) through the BEFS (BSE Electronic Filing System) within the prescribed deadline.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Audit Period Ended: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures adherence to regulatory standards and timely submission of compliance audits by trading members, maintaining market integrity and transparency.
