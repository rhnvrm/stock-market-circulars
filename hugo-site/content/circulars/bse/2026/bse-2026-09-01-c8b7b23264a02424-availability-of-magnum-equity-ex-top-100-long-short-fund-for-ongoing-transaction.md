## Summary

BSE has announced that the Magnum Equity Ex-Top 100 Long Short Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting September 2, 2026. Redemption and switch-out transactions for this SIF scheme will be restricted to twice a week (every Monday and Wednesday).

## Key Points

- Reopening date: September 02, 2026.
- Facilities available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, and SWP.
- Applicable schemes include multiple Regular and Direct plans (Growth and IDCW options).
- Redemption and switch-out transactions are restricted to Mondays and Wednesdays only.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the specified transaction days (Monday and Wednesday) for redemptions and switch-outs under the aforesaid SIF scheme.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 01, 2026
- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and members dealing in the Magnum Equity Ex-Top 100 Long Short Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform can resume transactions starting September 02, 2026, keeping in mind the restriction of redemption and switch-out processing to Mondays and Wednesdays.
