## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Everestims Technologies Limited, 16,12,800 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 85/- per equity share on September 28, 2026.

- Company Name: Everestims Technologies Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue / IPO Anchor Investor Allocation
- Number of Equity Shares Allocated: 16,12,800
- Issue Price: Rs. 85/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: September 28, 2026
