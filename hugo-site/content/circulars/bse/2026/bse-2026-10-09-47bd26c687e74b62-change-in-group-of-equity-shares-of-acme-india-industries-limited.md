## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Acme India Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 544981) from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective Monday, October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Acme India Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 544981
- Segment Transition: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 26, 2026
- Reference Notice: Notice No. 20261008-42 dated October 8, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the equity shares is shifted from the restrictive Trade for Trade segment to the standard Rolling segment, allowing normal rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment change and ensure compliance with M Group trading parameters.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 9, 2026
- Effective Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This shift enhances trading efficiency and liquidity for investors in Acme India Industries Limited by removing trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
