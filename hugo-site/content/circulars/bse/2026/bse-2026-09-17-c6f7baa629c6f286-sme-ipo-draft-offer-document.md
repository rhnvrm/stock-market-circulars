## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by R.D. Engineers (India) Limited for its upcoming SME IPO. The document is made available on stock exchange websites to seek public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: R.D. Engineers (India) Limited
- Filing Date: September 13, 2026
- Notice Date: September 17, 2026
- Purpose: Seeking public comments on the Draft Offer Document under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations
- Hosting Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations for SME IPO draft filings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stakeholders and the public are invited to submit comments within 21 days from the date of the notice.
- Comments should be sent via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 17, 2026
- Draft Document Filing Date: September 13, 2026
- Public Comment Deadline: Within 21 days from the notice date

## Impact Assessment

- Provides transparency and stakeholder participation during the SME initial public offering process for R.D. Engineers (India) Limited.
