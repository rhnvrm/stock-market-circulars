## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Omara Ventures India Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective Friday, October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Omara Ventures India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544976
- Segment Transition: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Reference: In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20261007-23 dated October 7, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The trading category and settlement structure for the company's equity shares are updated from restricted Trade for Trade settlement to standard rolling settlement under the M Group.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading systems and take note of the segment change for execution and settlement purposes starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 8, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shifting to the rolling segment facilitates regular liquidity, enabling standard intraday and delivery-based trading for market participants in the company's shares.
