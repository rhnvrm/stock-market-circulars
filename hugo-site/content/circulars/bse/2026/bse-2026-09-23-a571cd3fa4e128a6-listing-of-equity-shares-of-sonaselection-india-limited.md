## Summary

Effective from Thursday, September 24, 2026, equity shares of SonaSelection India Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the 'T' Group of Securities in the trade-for-trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SonaSelection India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544938
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: SONA
- ISIN No: INE0LLZ01011
- Total Securities: 56,82,8681 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 99/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 89/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 22, 2026
- Registrar: KFIN Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on September 24, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.
- Corporate action executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository's records for demat shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 22, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors can trade in the equity shares of SonaSelection India Limited starting September 24, 2026, subject to trade-for-trade segment restrictions for the initial 10 trading days.
