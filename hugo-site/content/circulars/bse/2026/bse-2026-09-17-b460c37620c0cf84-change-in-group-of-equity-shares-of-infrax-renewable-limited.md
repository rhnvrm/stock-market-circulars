## Summary

 BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Infrax Renewable Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment effective October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: INFRAX RENEWABLE LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544919
- Current Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of trading segment from MT Group (Trade for Trade) to Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to take note of the segment change for execution and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 17, 2026
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in equity shares will shift to the regular rolling segment, allowing normal rolling settlement instead of trade-for-trade restrictions.
