## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from July 01, 2026. The notice outlines specific surveillance actions applicable across GSM Stages I through IV.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure will transition to higher GSM stages starting July 01, 2026.
- Framework established based on previous notices dated February 23, 2017, March 03, 2017, July 20, 2018, November 29, 2019, and November 17, 2023.
- Settlement will be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed for affected securities.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Stage I:** Applicable margin rate of 100% with a price band of 5% or lower.
- **Stage II:** Trade-for-trade with a 5% or lower price band and an Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value to be deposited by buyers.
- **Stage III:** Trade-for-trade with a 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday / 1st trading day of the week), and 100% ASD to be deposited by buyers.
- **Stage IV:** Trade-for-trade with a 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week with no upward movement, and 100% ASD to be deposited by buyers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in the affected securities.
- Compliance with applicable margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement terms, and ASD deposit requirements is mandatory.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** June 30, 2026
- **Effective Date:** July 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Significant reduction in liquidity and restriction of intraday trading for securities moved to higher GSM stages due to 100% margins, trade-for-trade execution, and mandatory cash deposits (ASD).
