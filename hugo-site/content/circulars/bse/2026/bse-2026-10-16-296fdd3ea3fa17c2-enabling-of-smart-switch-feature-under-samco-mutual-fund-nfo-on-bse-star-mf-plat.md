## Summary

BSE has announced the enablement of the AUTO Switch feature under the SAMCO EQUITY SAVINGS FUND NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective from October 19, 2026, until October 29, 2026. Members can facilitate subscription transactions through user interfaces, bulk uploads, and APIs.

## Key Points

- AUTO Switch feature is available for SAMCO Mutual Fund schemes, plans, and options specified in Annexure I.
- Applicable only for lump sum subscriptions in non-Demat mode.
- Switch processing logic applies FIFO on newly purchased units when investors make additional purchases under the NFO AUTO Switch plan.
- Transactions will be accepted up to 1:00 PM on October 29, 2026.
- Orders successfully processed will be automatically considered as 'Switch In' to SAMCO EQUITY SAVINGS FUND.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational guidelines and scheme credentials updated in Scheme Master for seamless NFO switch execution.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members using direct APIs, API-based platforms, or bulk upload options must use appropriate scheme credentials strictly in accordance with investor requirements.
- Ensure all switch-out transactions follow the specified FIFO logic on newly purchased units.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Cut-off Date & Time:** Up to 1:00 PM on October 29, 2026
- **RTA Reporting Date (Switch Transactions):** November 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines mutual fund switch operations for participating members and investors during the Samco Equity Savings Fund NFO period.
