## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages effective from August 19, 2026. The notice outlines the surveillance actions applicable across different GSM stages (Stage I to Stage IV).

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure move to higher stages of the GSM framework.
- Applicable effective date: August 19, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, including increased margin rates, price band restrictions, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- No netting off is allowed as settlement takes place on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance framework actions as per BSE notice provisions and the GSM Framework published on February 23, 2017.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities. Settlement is conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 18 Aug 2026
- Effective Date: 19 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement for securities moving into GSM stages are impacted due to stricter surveillance measures, mandatory ASD requirements, and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
