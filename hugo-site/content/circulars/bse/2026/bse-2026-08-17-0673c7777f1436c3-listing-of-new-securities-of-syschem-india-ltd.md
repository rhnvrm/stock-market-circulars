## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Syschem (India) Ltd. with effect from August 18, 2026, following the conversion of warrants issued to promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Syschem (India) Ltd. (Scrip Code: 531173, ISIN: INE121D01036)
- **Securities Issued:** 43,52,500 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 39/-.
- **Effective Date:** August 18, 2026.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 49263001 to 53615500.
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to March 31, 2028.
- **Date of Allotment:** July 7, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard preferential allotment and listing compliance met by the company.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** July 7, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** March 31, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up equity capital of the company due to the conversion of warrants by promoters.
