## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 13 scrips trading on the exchange, effective from August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised from their existing levels for 13 scrips.
- Changes take effect starting from August 19, 2026.
- Affected scrips include companies across various price band limits ranging from 2% to 20%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Price band revisions implemented by the Surveillance department of BSE:
  - Dipna Pharmachem Ltd (543594): Revised to 10%
  - Dolphin Offshore Enterprises (India) Ltd (522261): Revised to 10%
  - East Buildtech Ltd (507917): Revised to 2%
  - Eureka Industries Ltd (521137): Revised to 2%
  - H. R. Hygiene Products Ltd (544848): Revised to 20%
  - ISL Consulting Ltd (511609): Revised to 5%
  - KVS Castings Ltd (544554): Revised to 10%
  - MPF Systems Ltd (532470): Revised to 5%
  - Praxis Home Retail Ltd (540901): Revised to 5%
  - Prima Agro Ltd (519262): Revised to 5%
  - Shetron Ltd (526137): Revised to 10%
  - Swati Projects Ltd (543914): Revised to 5%
  - Trade Wings Ltd (509953): Revised to 2%

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure trading systems are updated accordingly to prevent order rejections or compliance breaches.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 18, 2026
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members dealing in the affected scrips must adjust their risk parameters and trading strategies to accommodate the new price movement limits.
