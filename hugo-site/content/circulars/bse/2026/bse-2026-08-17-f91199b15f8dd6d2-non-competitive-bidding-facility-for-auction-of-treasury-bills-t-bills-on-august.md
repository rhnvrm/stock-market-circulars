## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Government of India Treasury Bills (T-Bills) scheduled for August 19, 2026. Trading members and direct investors can participate through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- **Auction Date:** August 19, 2026.
- **Settlement Date:** August 20, 2026.
- **Securities Offered:** 
  - 91 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 091T181126, Issue Size: Rs. 9,000 Cr)
  - 182 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 182T170227, Issue Size: Rs. 8,000 Cr)
  - 364 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 364T190827, Issue Size: Rs. 7,000 Cr)
- **Bidding Platform:** iBBS web-based system (NCB-GSec module).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is in continuation to Exchange circular no. 20180423-42 dated April 26, 2018, and RBI press release dated August 14, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure bids are submitted within the prescribed timelines and adhere to the specified minimum lot sizes and maximum investment limits.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date (Direct Investors & Members):** August 17, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability)
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors):** August 18, 2026, till 5:00 pm
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** August 19, 2026, till 8:00 am
- **Auction Date:** August 19, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates smooth participation for retail and institutional investors in Government of India Treasury Bill auctions through the Exchange platform with no direct corporate equity impact.
