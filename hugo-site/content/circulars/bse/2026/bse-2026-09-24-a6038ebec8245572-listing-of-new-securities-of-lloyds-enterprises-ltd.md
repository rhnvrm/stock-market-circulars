## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 21,08,867 new equity shares of Lloyds Enterprises Ltd. (Scrip Code: 512463) are permitted to trade with effect from September 25, 2026, pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Lloyds Enterprises Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 512463
- **ISIN:** INE080I01025
- **Effective Date:** September 25, 2026
- **Securities:** 21,08,867 Equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Re.1/- each issued on rights basis.
- **Ranking:** Pari-passu with the old Equity Shares of the Company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may execute trades in the newly listed securities effective from the specified trading date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 24, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing of converted equity shares. Expands the fully paid-up equity capital base of Lloyds Enterprises Ltd.
