## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Manoj Jewellers Limited with effect from October 6, 2026, following a rights issue.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Manoj Jewellers Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544400
- **ISIN:** INE0MV001018
- **Security Details:** 89,84,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 10/- on a rights basis.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 8985629 to 17969628
- **Date of Allotment:** 30/09/2026
- **Effective Date of Trading:** October 6, 2026
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed about the listing and permission to trade the new securities.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** October 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital base of Manoj Jewellers Limited through a rights issue.
