## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that the TRUSTMF Large & Mid Cap Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme reopening:** Ongoing transactions resume effective July 30, 2026.
- **Schemes included:** 
  - TRUSTMF LARGE & MID CAP FUND - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF0GCD01909)
  - TRUSTMF LARGE & MID CAP FUND - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF0GCD01891)
- **Facilities available:** Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can facilitate investor transactions in the aforementioned schemes via the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Effective Date:** July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and trading members to transact in the specified schemes of TRUSTMF Large & Mid Cap Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
