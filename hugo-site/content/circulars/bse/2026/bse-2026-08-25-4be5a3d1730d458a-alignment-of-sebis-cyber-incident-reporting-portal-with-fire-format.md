## Summary

SEBI has issued Circular No. HO/(449)2026-ITD-5_DIV1/I/19448/2026 dated August 24, 2026, aligning SEBI's Cyber Incident Reporting Portal with the Format for Incident Reporting Exchange (FIRE) Framework developed by the Financial Stability Board (FSB).

## Key Points

- Alignment of SEBI's Cyber Incident Reporting Portal with the FSB's FIRE Framework for standardized reporting.
- Regulated Entities (REs) must continue reporting cyber incidents to the Exchange as per existing guidelines (Exchange Circular No. 20250108-42) in addition to SEBI's portal.
- Annexure A includes a copy of the SEBI circular; Annexure B provides the User Manual for the Cyber Incident Reporting Portal.

## Regulatory Changes

Adoption of the FSB's FIRE format for structured and standardized cyber incident reporting through SEBI's portal.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and regulated entities must take note of the portal alignment and ensure compliance with cyber incident reporting requirements under both SEBI and Exchange frameworks.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- SEBI Circular Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Streamlines and standardizes cyber incident reporting for regulated entities in line with international financial stability standards.
