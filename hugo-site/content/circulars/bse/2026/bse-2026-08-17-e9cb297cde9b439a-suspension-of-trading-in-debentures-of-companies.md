## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of specified companies. The suspension is instituted because these companies have fixed record dates for the purpose of payment of interest and/or redemption of debentures.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures of the listed companies around the record/ex-dates.
- Affected companies include Manappuram Home Finance Limited, JM Financial Products Limited, KLM Axiva Finvest Limited, Muthoot Fincorp Limited, and HDB Financial Services Limited.
- Record dates and ex-dates fall between August 21, 2026, and August 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational market notice regarding corporate actions on debt instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that no trades are executed in the suspended NCD scrips contrary to the exchange's trading restrictions associated with the record dates.

## Important Dates

- **Record / Ex-Dates:** August 21, 2026, and August 27, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to debt market participants holding or trading the specified non-convertible debentures during their record date period.
