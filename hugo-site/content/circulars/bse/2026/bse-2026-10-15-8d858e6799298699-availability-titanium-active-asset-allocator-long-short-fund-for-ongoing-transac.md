## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that the Titanium Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopens for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities starting October 16, 2026.
- Covers 6 ISINs across Regular and Direct plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment).
- As communicated by TATA AMC, redemptions are processed once a week on Mondays (or the next business day if Monday is a non-business day).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational notice regarding mutual fund scheme availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must use the specified ISINs for transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform in compliance with TATA AMC guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to ongoing transactions and systematic facilities for the Titanium Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
