## Summary

BSE has issued a revised settlement schedule pertaining to the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Dolphin Medical Services Limited (Notice No. 20260730-9). The bidding was conducted from 31/07/2026 to 13/08/2026.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement No.:** 2026096
- **Bid Period:** 31/07/2026 to 13/08/2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 20/08/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 21/08/2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a schedule revision notice for a specific corporate acquisition.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism across both depositories.
- Members and custodians must use Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement number 2026096.
- Refer to Notice No. 20200528-32 dated May 28, 2020, for guidelines on share acquisition through the stock exchange.
- Client-wise Stamp Duty charges are detailed in the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 31/07/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 13/08/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 20/08/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 21/08/2026
