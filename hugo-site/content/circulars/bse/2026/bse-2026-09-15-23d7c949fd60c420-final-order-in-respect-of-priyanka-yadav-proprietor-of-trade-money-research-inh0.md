## Summary

SEBI, vide its final order dated September 10, 2026, has cancelled the certificate of registration of Priyanka Yadav, proprietor of Trade Money Research, operating as a Research Analyst under SEBI Registration No. INH000010991, effective from September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: Priyanka Yadav - Proprietor of Trade Money Research
- SEBI Registration Number: INH000010991
- Order Date: September 10, 2026
- Action Taken: Cancellation of certificate of registration

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

All research analysts and investors are advised to take note of the cancellation and avoid dealing with the aforementioned entity for research analyst services.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Cancellation: September 10, 2026
- Notice Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The entity is no longer authorized to provide research analyst services. Clients and market participants should ensure compliance by terminating any ongoing business associations with the entity.
