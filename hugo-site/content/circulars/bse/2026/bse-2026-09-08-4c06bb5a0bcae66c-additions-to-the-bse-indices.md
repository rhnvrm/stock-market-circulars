## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of three newly listed companies to its indices. Deepa Jewellers Limited will be added to the BSE IPO index, while Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Limited and Farm Peace Limited will be added to the BSE SME IPO index.

## Key Points

- Deepa Jewellers Limited (Ticker: 544903) lists on BSE effective September 08, 2026, and will be added to the BSE IPO index effective September 10, 2026.
- Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Limited (Ticker: 544904) and Farm Peace Limited (Ticker: 544905) list on the BSE SME platform effective September 08, 2026, and will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective September 09, 2026.
- These changes are reflected in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None specific to market participants; informational notice regarding index composition.

## Important Dates

- September 08, 2026: Listing date for Deepa Jewellers, Fly-Hi Maritime Travels, and Farm Peace.
- September 09, 2026: Effective date for SME IPO index addition.
- September 10, 2026: Effective date for BSE IPO index addition.

## Impact Assessment

Routine index inclusion for passive tracking and informational purposes for market participants.
