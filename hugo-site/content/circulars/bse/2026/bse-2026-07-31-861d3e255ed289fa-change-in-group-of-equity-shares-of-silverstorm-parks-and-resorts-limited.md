## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of SILVERSTORM PARKS AND RESORTS LIMITED from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SILVERSTORM PARKS AND RESORTS LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544840
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: August 14, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The trading segment for the company's equity shares has been changed in continuation of the Exchange's previous notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the shift in trading segment and update their systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 31, 2026
- Effective Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shifting from the Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment allows regular trading of the company's shares, improving liquidity and market participation.
