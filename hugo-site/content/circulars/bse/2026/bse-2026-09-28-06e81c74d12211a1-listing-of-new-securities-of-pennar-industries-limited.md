## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of 5,50,000 new equity shares of Pennar Industries Limited (Scrip Code: 513228) effective from September 29, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Pennar Industries Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 513228
- **ISIN:** INE932A01024
- **Number of Securities:** 5,50,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 5/- each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 168/- per share (including a premium of Rs. 163/- per share)
- **Allotment Date:** July 14, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Distribution Nos.:** 158485197 to 159035196
- **Lock-in Details:** 5,50,000 shares are locked-in up to March 31, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notification regarding preferential allotment and warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to note the listing and trading permission granted for the aforementioned securities.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** July 14, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** March 31, 2028

## Impact Assessment

The listing expands the paid-up equity capital of Pennar Industries Limited following warrant conversion. The shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.
