## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by six companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS), effective from Wednesday, October 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Six companies have issued further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Trading effective date: October 7, 2026.
- Participating companies: Ather Energy Limited, Bajel Projects Limited, Dev Information Technology Limited, Lloyds Metals and Energy Limited, Northern Arc Capital Limited, and Yes Bank Limited.
- Face values vary between Re. 1 and Rs. 10 with 'NA' lock-in status.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing and admission for trading of the new securities.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine issuance of shares under ESOP/ESOS leading to minor expansion of equity base for the respective companies with negligible market impact.
