## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS effective from Friday, October 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code 805277 (ISIN: IN002026X271): 91TB070127 maturing on 07/01/2027 with a market lot of 1.
- Scrip Code 805278 (ISIN: IN002026Y279): 182TB08427 maturing on 08/04/2027 with a market lot of 1.
- Scrip Code 805279 (ISIN: IN002026Z276): 364TB71027 maturing on 07/10/2027 with a market lot of 1.
- Securities shall not be available for trading two (2) working days prior to the maturity/redemption date.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing of government securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to note the non-availability of trading two working days prior to the respective maturity dates.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 9, 2026
- Maturity Date (91TB070127): January 7, 2027
- Maturity Date (182TB08427): April 8, 2027
- Maturity Date (364TB71027): October 7, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations expanding debt instrument availability on the exchange.
