## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying inclusion criteria, moving to lower stages, or moving out of the framework entirely. Affected securities will attract a minimum 100% margin, shift to the trade-for-trade segment, and face specific price band and periodic call auction restrictions.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 16, 2026, on all open positions as on September 15, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Eligible securities will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 16, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 15, 2026.
- The shortlisting of securities under ESM is strictly on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework restrictions in conjunction with other prevailing surveillance measures.
- Reinstatement of price bands to pre-shortlisted levels for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the inclusion, stage movement, and exclusion of securities as per Annexures I, II, and III.
- Ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for specified scrips.

## Important Dates

- **September 15, 2026:** Effective date for movement of securities to lower Stage I, application of Stage II restrictions (2% price band and periodic call auction), and exit of securities from ESM.
- **September 16, 2026:** Effective date for minimum 100% margin requirement and shifting of securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading operations for securities included or moving within the ESM framework will be significantly impacted due to mandatory trade-for-trade settlement, 100% margins, and stricter price bands.
