## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the Draft Offer Document filed by Bulls Eye Knowledge System Limited for its upcoming SME IPO. The document is hosted on the BSE SME website for public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bulls Eye Knowledge System Limited
- Filing Date: September 25, 2026
- Regulation: Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine draft offer document filing under ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Public and stakeholders are invited to review the draft offer document and submit any comments within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Comment Deadline: Within 21 days from September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates market transparency and regulatory compliance by inviting public feedback on proposed SME public issues prior to final prospectus issuance.
