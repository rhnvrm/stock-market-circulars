## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (ICCL) has announced the levy of an additional exposure margin @15% in the equity derivatives segment on securities where the top 10 clients account for more than 20% of the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL). For securities with an existing additional surveillance margin, the higher of the two margins will be applicable.

## Key Points

- ICCL levies an additional exposure margin of 15% in the equity derivatives segment.
- Applicable to securities where top 10 clients account for more than 20% of MWPL.
- If additional surveillance margin is applicable, the higher of the two margins is levied.
- Scrips are identified using 3 months rolling data and reviewed monthly.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of additional exposure margin framework based on client concentration in MWPL within equity derivatives.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must account for the 15% additional exposure margin while assessing margin requirements for identified scrips in the equity derivatives segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 31, 2026 (immediately following the expiry of July 2026 contracts).
- Applicability: Remains applicable until the August 2026 expiry.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for trading participants dealing in specific high-concentration derivatives securities, potentially impacting liquidity and leverage for those scrips.
