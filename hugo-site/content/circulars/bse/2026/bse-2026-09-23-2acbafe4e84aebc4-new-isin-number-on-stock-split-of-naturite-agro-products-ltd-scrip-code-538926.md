## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the new ISIN number assigned to the sub-divided equity shares of Naturite Agro Products Ltd following a stock split.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Naturite Agro Products Ltd
- Scrip Code: 538926
- Old Face Value: Rs. 10/-
- New Face Value: Rs. 5/-
- New ISIN: INE223R01022
- Ex-Date: September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Allocation of a new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that trades in the new ISIN are updated and processed correctly from the effective ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2026
- Ex-Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for trading members and depositories to reflect the new ISIN for equity shares of Rs. 5/- each.
