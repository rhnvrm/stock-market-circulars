## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by several companies on the debt market segment effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh listing of commercial papers for issuers including 360 ONE WAM Limited, Edelweiss Financial Services Ltd., Capri Global Capital Limited, Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited, and Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited.
- Securities are traded in dematerialised form under designated ISINs with a standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- Tick size for the securities is set at 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that transactions comply with standard debt market segment guidelines and proper ISIN mapping.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 22, 2026
- Allotment and redemption dates vary by specific commercial paper issuance (ranging from October 2026 to January 2027).

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing providing trading access and liquidity for newly issued short-term debt instruments.
