## Summary

BSE has announced the opening of the acquisition window for the takeover open offer made by Uday Narang ("Acquirer") to the public shareholders of Pasupati Fincap Limited ("Target Company"). The offer is to acquire up to 12,22,000 fully paid-up equity shares, representing 26% of the voting share capital, at a cash price of Rs. 12 per share.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: Uday Narang
- Target Company: Pasupati Fincap Limited
- Offer Price: Rs. 12/- per fully paid-up equity share payable in cash
- Offer Size: 12,22,000 equity shares (26% of Voting Share Capital)
- Regulatory Basis: Regulation 4 of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011
- Offer Period: October 01, 2026 to October 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulations introduced; the offer follows established guidelines for acquisition of shares through stock exchange pursuant to tender-offers under takeover regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians are required to adhere to SEBI circulars CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 along with relevant Exchange notices while participating in the acquisition window for tender-offers.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Offer Opening Date: October 01, 2026
- Offer Closing Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public shareholders of Pasupati Fincap Limited an exit opportunity via the stock exchange acquisition window under SEBI takeover norms.
