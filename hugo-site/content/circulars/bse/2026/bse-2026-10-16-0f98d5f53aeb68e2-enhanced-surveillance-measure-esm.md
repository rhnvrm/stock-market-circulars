## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying inclusion criteria, moving to lower stages, or moving out of the framework entirely.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 21, 2026, on open positions as of October 19, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II face a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 19, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 21, 2026.
- Review of securities under the ESM framework is conducted on a periodical basis.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ESM surveillance criteria in conjunction with prevailing exchange measures. Shortlisting is purely for market surveillance and not an adverse action against companies.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements, Trade-for-Trade segment settlements, and price band restrictions for impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage II / Movement out / Movement to Stage I: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin and Trade-for-Trade segment: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin blocked for affected securities will increase significantly due to 100% margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade settlement constraints.
