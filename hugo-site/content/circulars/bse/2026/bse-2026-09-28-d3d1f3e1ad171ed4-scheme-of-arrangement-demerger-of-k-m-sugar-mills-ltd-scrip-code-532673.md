## Summary

BSE has announced details regarding the Scheme of Arrangement (Demerger) of K. M. Sugar Mills Ltd. (Demerged Company) into KM Spirits and Allied Industries Limited (Resulting Company). The demerger involves the Distillery Division of K. M. Sugar Mills Ltd., with a specified record date of October 2, 2026, and ex-date of October 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Demerged Company:** K. M. Sugar Mills Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532673)
- **Resulting Company:** KM Spirits and Allied Industries Limited
- **Demerged Undertaking:** Distillery Division
- **Share Entitlement Ratio:** 1 (One) Equity Share of face value Rs. 10/- each of KM Spirits and Allied Industries Limited for every 5 (Five) Equity Shares of face value Rs. 2/- each held in K. M. Sugar Mills Ltd.
- **Trading Group Transfer:** Trading in equity shares of K. M. Sugar Mills Ltd. will be transferred from 'B' Group to 'T' Group w.e.f. October 1, 2026.
- **Pre-open Session:** Scrip will be part of Call Auction in Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 1, 2026 pursuant to SEBI circulars.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of scrip from 'B' Group to 'T' Group effective October 1, 2026.
- Subject to compliance, equity shares of the Resulting Company (KM Spirits and Allied Industries Limited) will be listed on BSE Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the demerger entitlement ratio, ex-date, record date, and the transfer of trading to 'T' group with Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 1, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Ex-Date:** October 1, 2026
- **Record Date:** October 2, 2026
- **Transfer to 'T' Group & SPOS:** October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders holding shares of K. M. Sugar Mills Ltd. as of the record date will receive shares of the resulting company according to the specified share swap ratio. Trading restrictions and special pre-open auction rules apply starting October 1, 2026.
