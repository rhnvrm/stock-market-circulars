## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has observed significant price and volume movements in the shares of several companies in the recent past. To ensure that trading members and investors have access to the latest relevant information, BSE issued queries to these companies seeking clarifications. This notice lists the companies that have submitted their replies, along with those whose replies are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- **Replies Received:** BSE has received and attached replies from the following 8 companies:
  1. Atlantaa Ltd (Scrip Code: 532759)
  2. Manaksia Aluminium Company Ltd (Scrip Code: 539045)
  3. Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Ltd (Scrip Code: 500109)
  4. Niyogin Fintech Ltd (Scrip Code: 538772)
  5. Travels & Rentals Ltd (Scrip Code: 544242)
  6. Vedant Fashions Ltd (Scrip Code: 543463)
  7. Crazy Snacks Ltd (Scrip Code: 544807)
  8. Bhudevi Infra Projects Ltd (Scrip Code: 526488)

- **Replies Awaited:** Clarification responses are still awaited from the following 13 companies:
  1. Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd (Scrip Code: 542176)
  2. Chatterbox Technologies Ltd (Scrip Code: 544546)
  3. CMX Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 532217)
  4. Deep Polymers Ltd (Scrip Code: 541778)
  5. Gconnect Logitech and Supply Chain Ltd (Scrip Code: 544156)
  6. Mahesh Developers Ltd (Scrip Code: 542677)
  7. Medico Remedies Ltd (Scrip Code: 540937)
  8. Nexxus Petro Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 544265)
  9. PVP Ventures Ltd (Scrip Code: 517556)
  10. RSD Finance Ltd (Scrip Code: 539875)

  12. Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 539117)
  13. Triumph International Finance India Ltd (Scrip Code: 532131)

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory frameworks or rules are introduced by this circular. It is an implementation of existing surveillance and disclosure mechanisms under the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Listed Companies (whose replies are awaited):** The listed entities whose clarifications are pending must submit their responses to the exchange promptly to ensure transparency.
- **For Listed Companies (whose replies are received):** No further immediate action is required, as their responses are already published and attached to the circular.
- **For Trading Members and Investors:** Monitor the disclosures and clarification letters of these companies to make informed investment decisions.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026.
- **Compliance Deadline:** Companies with pending clarifications must respond as per the exchange's specific timelines (usually immediate or within 24 hours of receipt).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. While there may be short-term volatility or price movement in the specific listed scrips, this is a routine surveillance query to check for any undisclosed price-sensitive information.
- **Corporate Impact:** Medium for the affected companies, as they must provide accurate and timely clarification to avoid potential regulatory action or trading suspension if non-compliance persists.
