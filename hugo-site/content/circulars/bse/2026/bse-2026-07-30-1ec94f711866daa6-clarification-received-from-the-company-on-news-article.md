## Summary

The Exchange sought clarification from Steel Authority of India Ltd regarding various news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, and television channels to ensure accuracy and inform investors. The company's reply is attached to the notice.

- Company: Steel Authority of India Ltd (Scrip Code: 500113)
- Purpose: Clarification on media reports/news articles.
- Source reference: Economic Times website.

None.
