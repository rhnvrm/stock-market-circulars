## Summary

SEBI, vide its order dated October 15, 2026, has restrained Capital Stroke Investment Services Pvt. Ltd. from accessing the securities market and prohibited them from buying, selling, or dealing in securities for a period of 5 years.

## Key Points

- SEBI Order No.: QJA/BS/WRO/WRO/32771/2026-27 dated October 15, 2026.
- 5-year debarment from accessing the securities market, direct or indirect trading, or association with the securities market.
- Period of restraint already undergone under Interim and Confirmatory Orders will be set off.
- Effective immediately from the date of the order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of regulatory debarment on Capital Stroke Investment Services Pvt. Ltd. under SEBI inspection proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the order and ensure strict compliance with the debarment directions.

## Important Dates

- Order Date / Effective Date: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures market integrity by penalizing non-compliant entities and restricting their participation in securities markets.
