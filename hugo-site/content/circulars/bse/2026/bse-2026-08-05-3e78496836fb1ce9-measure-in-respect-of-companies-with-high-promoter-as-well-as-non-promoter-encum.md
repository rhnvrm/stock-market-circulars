## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding measures for companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance, continuing from previous exchange notices. Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 75% margin in equity and equity derivatives segments.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
- Securities in Annexure II are eligible to move out of the framework.
- A consolidated list of securities is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of a minimum 75% margin on all open positions as of August 07, 2026, and new positions created from August 10, 2026, for securities meeting the high encumbrance criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the included and excluded securities and apply the mandated 75% margin requirements accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Exclusion Effective Date:** August 06, 2026 (for securities in Annexure II)
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margins:** August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for affected securities may impact liquidity and trading positions for market participants holding open positions in these scrips.
