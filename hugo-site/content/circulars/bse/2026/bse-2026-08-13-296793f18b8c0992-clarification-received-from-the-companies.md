## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movements in specific scrips in the recent past and sought clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarifications have been received and attached from:
  1. Billwin Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 543209)
  2. Nexxus Petro Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 544265)
  3. Rekvina Laboratories Ltd (Scrip Code: 526075)
- Replies are awaited from 13 other listed companies, including Apt Packaging Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, and PVP Ventures Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard surveillance monitoring procedure under BSE oversight.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely clarify price/volume movements upon exchange inquiry to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 13 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency for investors regarding unusual market activities in specific scrips.
