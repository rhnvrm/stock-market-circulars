## Summary

SEBI issued its final order (No. WTM/AS/CFID/CFID-SEC1/32687/2026-27) dated August 28, 2026, regarding the IPO of Trafiksol ITS Technologies Ltd., restraining specific noticees from the securities market for a period of one year.

## Key Points

- Noticees Nos. 1, 2, and 3 are restrained from accessing the securities market and prohibited from dealing in securities directly or indirectly for 1 year.
- Open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts can be closed out or squared off within 3 months or upon expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Trading members are advised to take note and ensure compliance with the directions.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and market debarment issued under SEBI WTM order in the matter of Trafiksol ITS Technologies Ltd. IPO.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that the restrained noticees do not trade or access the securities market during the debarment period.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 28, 2026
- Square off deadline for open positions: Within 3 months from the date of the order or contract expiry.

## Impact Assessment

Direct regulatory and operational restriction on the specified noticees involved in the Trafiksol ITS Technologies Ltd. IPO.
