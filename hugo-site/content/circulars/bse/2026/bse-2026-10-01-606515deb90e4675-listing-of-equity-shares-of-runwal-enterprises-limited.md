## Summary

Effective from Monday, October 5, 2026, the equity shares of Runwal Enterprises Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Scrip Name: Runwal Enterprises Limited
- Scrip Code: 544959
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: RUNWALENTR
- ISIN: INE804W01029
- Number of Securities: 147,788,829 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each fully paid up
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 147788829
- Market Lot: 1
- Issue Price: Rs. 305/- per share (Face Value Rs. 2/-, Premium Rs. 303/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2026
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; standard listing notification for an IPO.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 5, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action executed for demat lock-in period marking.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors can trade the equity shares of Runwal Enterprises Limited starting October 5, 2026, with demat settlement and SPOS participation.
