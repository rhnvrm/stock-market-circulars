## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of Muthoot Fincorp Limited on the exchange under the 'F GROUP' effective from September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities: Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of Muthoot Fincorp Limited.
- Effective Date: September 25, 2026.
- Trading Group: 'F GROUP' in unit market lot (i.e., One Bond) in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Company Financial Year: Ends on 31st March.
- Registered Office: Muthoot Centre, TC No 27/3022 Punnen Road, Trivandrum 695001, Kerala.
- Registrar and Transfer Agent: Integrated Registry Management Services Private Limited, Chennai.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine debt listing procedure adhering to SEBI guidelines for dematerialized trading of bonds.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure bonds are traded in dematerialized form in unit market lots (One Bond).
- Company Compliance Officer: Mr. Sachu Sivas.
- Exchange Contact: Mr. Hardik Bhuta (Assistant Vice President, DCS-Listing).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing providing trading access for Muthoot Fincorp Limited's NCDs on the BSE debt segment with minimal market impact.
