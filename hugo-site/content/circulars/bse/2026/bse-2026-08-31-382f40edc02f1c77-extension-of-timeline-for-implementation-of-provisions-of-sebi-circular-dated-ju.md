## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the extension of the timeline for the implementation of provisions outlined in the SEBI Circular dated June 15, 2026. The circular pertains to norms for base price, price bands, call auctions in pre-open sessions, and close-out procedures for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs).

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260831-21
- Category: Settlement/RMS
- Segment: General
- Department: BSECL
- Subject: Extension of timeline for implementation of provisions of SEBI Circular dated June 15, 2026 on norms for base price, price bands, call auction in pre-open session and close-out procedure for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs)
- Attachments include Annexure_1 providing further operational details.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension granted for implementing specific trading and settlement norms for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) as originally specified in the SEBI circular dated June 15, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members should review Annexure_1 for the revised timelines and align their system implementations accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 31, 2026
- Revised implementation dates are detailed in Annexure_1.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants with additional preparation time to update trading systems, algorithms, and risk management frameworks for ETF pre-open sessions, price bands, and close-out procedures.
