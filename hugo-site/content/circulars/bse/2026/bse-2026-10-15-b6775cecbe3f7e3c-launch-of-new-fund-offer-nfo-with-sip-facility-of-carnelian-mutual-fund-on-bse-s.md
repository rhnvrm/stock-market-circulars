## Summary

Carnelian Mutual Fund is launching the Carnelian Small Cap Fund NFO along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from October 16, 2026, to October 30, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of November 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Offerings**: Carnelian Small Cap Fund offers both Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.
- **Subscription Timeline**: NFO opens on October 16, 2026, and closes on October 30, 2026.
- **Platform**: Available for trading and investment via the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **Tentative Allotment**: Scheduled for November 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 05, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (October 30, 2026).
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date**: October 16, 2026
- **NFO Close Date**: October 30, 2026 (extended till midnight for Lumpsum; SIP/XSIP authentication by 3:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date**: November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading members and investors streamlined access to subscribe to Carnelian Small Cap Fund through the BSE StAR MF infrastructure with defined funding and allotment timelines.
