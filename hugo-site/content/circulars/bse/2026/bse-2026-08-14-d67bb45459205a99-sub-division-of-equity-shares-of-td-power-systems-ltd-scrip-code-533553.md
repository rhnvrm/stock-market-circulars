## Summary

TD Power Systems Ltd has fixed August 24, 2026, as the Record Date for the sub-division of its equity shares. The existing equity shares with a face value of Rs. 2/- each are being sub-divided into two equity shares with a face value of Re. 1/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TD Power Systems Ltd
- Scrip Code: 533553
- Old Face Value: Rs. 2/- per share
- New Face Value: Re. 1/- per share (Two shares of Re. 1/- each for every one share of Rs. 2/-)
- Record Date: August 24, 2026
- Old ISIN (INE419M01027 of Rs. 2/- paid up) will cease to be valid for transactions done on or after August 24, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Stock split and face value reduction executed as per corporate announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that transactions on or after the record date will not be valid under the old ISIN. A separate notice will be issued regarding the new ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date for Sub-Divided Paid-Up Value: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders of TD Power Systems Ltd as of the record date will receive two shares of Re. 1/- each for every existing share held of Rs. 2/- face value. Market participants must update systems for the new ISIN upon notification.
