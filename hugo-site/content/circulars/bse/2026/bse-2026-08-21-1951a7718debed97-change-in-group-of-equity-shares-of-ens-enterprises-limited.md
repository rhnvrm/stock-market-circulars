## Summary

Trading members are informed that equity shares of ENS Enterprises Limited (SME IPO, Scrip Code: 544876) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective Friday, September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: ENS Enterprises Limited
- Scrip Code: 544876
- Segment Transition: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: September 4, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard operational segment shift following the exchange's review of SME IPO trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading systems and ensure smooth settlement under the rolling segment for the scrip starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date: September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Transactions in the equity shares of ENS Enterprises Limited will shift from trade-for-trade settlement to normal rolling settlement, enhancing liquidity and trading flexibility for market participants.
