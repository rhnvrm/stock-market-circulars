## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific T-Bills and Government Securities due to their maturity effective September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading applies to 91TB011026 (Scrip Code: 805235, ISIN: IN002026X131) and 364TB21026 (Scrip Code: 805126, ISIN: IN002025Z278).
- Effective date for cessation of trading is September 29, 2026 (DR- 727/2026-2027).
- Issued by Listing Operations & Compliance department.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; standard operational procedure for maturing government securities and T-Bills.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid T-Bills/Government Securities effective September 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and routine market impact limited to the maturity and settlement of the specified maturing government debt instruments.
