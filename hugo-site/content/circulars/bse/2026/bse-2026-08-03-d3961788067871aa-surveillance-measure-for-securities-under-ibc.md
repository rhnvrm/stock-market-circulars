## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application, review, and removal of securities under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The changes take effect from August 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are placed under Additional Surveillance Measures effective August 4, 2026.
- A list of securities moving out of the IBC framework is also provided in Annexure II.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.
- The shortlisting is strictly a market surveillance measure and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process under the IBC.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the inclusion and exclusion of securities under the IBC surveillance framework and adjust trading parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 3, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in affected securities will experience surveillance-related controls, such as price band restrictions or reinstatements, ensuring transparent risk management.
