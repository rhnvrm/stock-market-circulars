## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the surveillance measures for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective August 26, 2026. Securities satisfying the criteria under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) have been updated, including inclusions, exclusions, consolidated lists, and classifications due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25%.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are subjected to ASM effective August 26, 2026.
- Periodic reviews are conducted for securities under this framework.
- Lists of securities moving into, out of, or remaining under the IBC framework are detailed in respective annexures (Annexure I to IV).
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures in conjunction with prevailing exchange rules for companies undergoing resolution under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the updated lists of securities under the IBC surveillance framework and adjust trading parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in specified securities will be subject to surveillance restrictions or price band reinstatements as applicable under the IBC framework.
