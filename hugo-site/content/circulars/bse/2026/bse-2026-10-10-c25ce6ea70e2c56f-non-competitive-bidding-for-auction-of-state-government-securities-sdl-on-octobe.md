## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled on October 13, 2026, based on the RBI press release.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding is available through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system (https://ibbs.bseindia.com).
- Direct investor bid collection start date: October 12, 2026, from 10:00 AM onwards (24 hours availability).
- Bid collection end date for direct investors: October 12, 2026, till 5:00 PM.
- Bid collection end date for members: October 13, 2026, till 8:00 AM.
- Auction Date: October 13, 2026.
- Settlement Date: October 14, 2026.
- Includes various State Development Loans (SDLs) across multiple states such as Assam, Bihar, Gujarat, Haryana, Karnataka, Kerala, Mizoram, Odisha, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, and Uttarakhand.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; conducted pursuant to RBI press release and existing exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and eligible investors must adhere to the specified bidding timelines and module parameters on the iBBS platform.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start: October 12, 2026, 10:00 AM
- Direct Investor Bid End: October 12, 2026, 5:00 PM
- Member Bid End: October 13, 2026, 8:00 AM
- Auction Date: October 13, 2026
- Settlement Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless participation of retail and non-competitive bidders in State Government Securities auctions through electronic platforms.
