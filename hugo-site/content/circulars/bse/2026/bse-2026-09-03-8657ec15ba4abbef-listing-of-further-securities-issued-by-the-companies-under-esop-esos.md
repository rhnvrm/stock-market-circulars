## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued by 10 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Friday, September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 10.
- Effective date for trading of new securities: September 04, 2026.
- Includes prominent entities such as Axis Bank Limited, Dr. Reddy's Laboratories Limited, and Angel One Limited.
- Shares are issued pursuant to exercise of options under respective company ESOP/ESOS schemes.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the listing and admission of these securities for trading.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 03, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the respective issuing companies without direct market-wide systemic impact.
