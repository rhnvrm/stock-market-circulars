## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Revised margin requirements come into effect on September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I attract a margin rate of 50% or existing margin, whichever is higher (capped at 100%).
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II attract a margin rate of 100% or existing margin, whichever is higher.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on September 25, 2026, and new positions created from September 28, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance frameworks with enhanced margin stipulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins: September 28, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on September 25, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Increased capital requirements for trading in securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I and II, potentially affecting liquidity and short-term volatility.
