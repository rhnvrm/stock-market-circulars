## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-690/2026-27 (Demat Auction 690). The notice outlines the schedule for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and final reports.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on Tuesday, Aug 11, 2026.
- Auction matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on Aug 11, 2026.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction is scheduled for Wednesday, Aug 12, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities codes and rates; no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members are requested to refer to the cutoff rate in the "Auction offer Entry Screen" and check for corporate actions.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 11, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for clearing short deliveries in rolling settlements with no broad market impact.
