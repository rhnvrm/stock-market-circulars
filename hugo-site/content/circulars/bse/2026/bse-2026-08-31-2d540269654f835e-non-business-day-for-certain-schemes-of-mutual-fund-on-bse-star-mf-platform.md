## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that certain mutual fund schemes will observe a 'Non-Business Day' on the BSE StAR MF platform for September 2026 as per communication received from respective Mutual Funds.

## Key Points

- Notice applies to specific schemes of Mutual Funds on the BSE StAR MF platform for September 2026.
- Operations such as Subscription, Redemption, Switch, SIP, Normal STP, SWP, and AMC-STP will not be allowed for the affected schemes on their respective Non-Business Days.
- Details of the schemes and dates are provided in the attached Annexure of the notice.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the non-business days for the specified schemes to avoid failed transactions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 Aug 2026
- Effective Period: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational restriction for specific mutual fund schemes during the month of September 2026.
