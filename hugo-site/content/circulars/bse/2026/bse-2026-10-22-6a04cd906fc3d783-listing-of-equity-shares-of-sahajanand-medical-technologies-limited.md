## Summary

Effective from Friday, October 23, 2026, equity shares of Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE under 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 545001
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SMT
- ISIN No.: INE356Y01018
- Issue Price: Rs. 344/- per share (Face Value Rs. 1/- & premium of Rs. 343/-)
- Number of Securities: 101,646,778 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up
- Date of Allotment: October 21, 2026
- Market Lot: 1 share

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on listing day (October 23, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate actions have been executed in demat form to have the lock-in period marked in the depository's records as per Annexure I.
- Registrar to the issue: MUFG Intime India Private Limited.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 21, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Sahajanand Medical Technologies Limited shares on BSE, providing market liquidity and expanding the exchange's equity segment listings.
