## Summary

BSE has announced the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for The South India Paper Mills Limited. The bidding is scheduled to be conducted from October 22, 2026, to November 4, 2026, under Settlement No. 2026127.

## Key Points

- Target Company: The South India Paper Mills Limited.
- Settlement Type: Takeover.
- Settlement Number: 2026127.
- Bid Start Date: 22/10/2026.
- Bid End Date: 04/11/2026.
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL & NSDL).
- Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement No. 2026127 must be used by Members/Custodians.
- Members and Custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Stamp duty charges are detailed in the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adherence to revised guidelines for the mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange as per Notice No. 20200528-32 dated May 28, 2020.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must ensure early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/BSECL systems before bidding and confirmation timelines.
- Securities in CDSL & NSDL will be earmarked or blocked in clients' accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: 22/10/2026
- Bid End Date: 04/11/2026
- Obligation Download: 18/11/2026
- Settlement Date: 19/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures structured and timely settlement for the takeover open offer of The South India Paper Mills Limited while simplifying delivery through depository early pay-in.
