## Summary

JK Salt Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with BSE SME on September 30, 2026. The document is hosted on the BSE SME website for public comments over a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: JK Salt Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Hosting Platform: BSE SME Website
- Public Comments Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

Complies with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations governing SME IPO draft offer document disclosures and public comment procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the general public can review the draft offer document and submit comments within 21 days of the notice date to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comment Deadline: 21 days from the notice date (approx. October 22, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites feedback from market participants on the proposed SME IPO before finalization of the offer document.
