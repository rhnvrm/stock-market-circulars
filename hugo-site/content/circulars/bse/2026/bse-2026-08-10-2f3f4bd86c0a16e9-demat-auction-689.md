## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice regarding Demat Auction 689 for Rolling Settlement No. DR-689/2026-27, detailing the session timings for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and pay-in schedules.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement No: DR-689/2026-27
- Auction Offer Entry Timing: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on August 10, 2026
- Auction Matching Timing: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on August 10, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: Tuesday, August 11, 2026 at 08:00 a.m.
- Restrictions on trading members offering securities in auctions for settlements where they short delivered are removed under the uniform membership structure.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; reaffirmation of existing operational procedures under the uniform membership structure in the equity cash segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Extreme care must be taken while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 10, 2026
- Pay-in Date: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational notice ensuring smooth clearing and settlement for short-delivered securities through the Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd.
