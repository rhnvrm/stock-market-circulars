## Summary

BSE has announced a revision in the price bands for several scrips with effect from October 07, 2026, as part of its surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 11 scrips.
- Effective date: October 07, 2026.
- Revised price bands range from 2% to 20% depending on the scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of daily operational price limits for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems with the revised price bands.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date: October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Restricts excessive volatility in the affected scrips by enforcing new price movement boundaries.
