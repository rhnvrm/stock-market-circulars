## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the suspension of trading in the Commercial Paper of Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited on account of its scheduled maturity.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited
- Scrip Code: 731795
- Particulars: JBCI-2-9-26-CP
- ISIN: INE824H14UL8
- Effective Date of Suspension: September 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid Commercial Paper of the company with effect from September 01, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 31, 2026
- Suspension Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification ensuring market participants cease trading in the matured debt instrument.
