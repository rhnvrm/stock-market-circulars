## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Dhanwel Hybrid Seeds Limited on its SME platform, effective from Wednesday, August 26, 2026. The scrip will be traded in the 'MT' Group of Securities and will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Dhanwel Hybrid Seeds Limited
- Scrip Code: 544884
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: DHANWEL
- ISIN: INE0VWO01011
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- Issue Price: Rs. 99/- per share (including premium of Rs. 89/-)
- Total Securities Listed: 9,103,320 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Segment: Trade-for-Trade for 10 trading days (MT Group)

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only in accordance with SEBI guidelines.
- Trading will be subject to a Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in the prescribed minimum market lot of 1,200 shares.
- Necessary corporate actions regarding lock-in periods have been executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 24, 2026
- Effective Listing / Trading Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Dhanwel Hybrid Seeds Limited shares on the BSE SME platform, providing liquidity to investors and capitalization for the company.
