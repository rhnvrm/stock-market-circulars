Trading members of the Exchange are informed that 53,25,909 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the Public Issue of SYMBIOTEC PHARMALAB LIMITED at Rs. 988/- per equity share on August 21, 2026.

- Company Name: SYMBIOTEC PHARMALAB LIMITED
- Number of Equity Shares Allocated: 53,25,909
- Price per Equity Share: Rs. 988/-
- Date of Allocation: August 21, 2026
