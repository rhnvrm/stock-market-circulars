## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released the client margin reporting schedule and corresponding penalty dates for the Commodity Derivatives Segment for the month of September 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers trade dates starting from September 1, 2026, through September 30, 2026.
- Specifies the exact last date of margin reporting and penalty dates for each trade date.
- Applies to all members trading in the Commodity Derivatives Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine schedule announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure client margins are reported within the stipulated deadlines to avoid financial penalties.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Month:** September 2026
- **First Trade Date:** 01-09-2026 (Margin Reporting: 08-09-2026, Penalty Date: 09-09-2026)
- **Last Trade Date:** 30-09-2026 (Margin Reporting: 08-10-2026, Penalty Date: 09-10-2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Operational compliance reminder for clearing members and brokers active in commodity derivatives to align their back-office reporting schedules.
