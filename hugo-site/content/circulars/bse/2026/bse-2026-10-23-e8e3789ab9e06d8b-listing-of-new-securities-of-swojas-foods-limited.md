## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Swojas Foods Limited (Scrip Code: 530217) effective from October 26, 2026, following the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Swojas Foods Limited
- Scrip Code: 530217
- ISIN: INE295B01016
- Securities Issued: 24,25,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 6.50/- (Issue Price: Rs. 16.50/-)
- Allottees: Non-promoters on a preferential basis
- Distinctive Numbers: 45863313 to 48288312
- Date of Allotment: 24/09/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: October 26, 2026
- Lock-in Details: 24,25,000 shares locked-in upto 30/04/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a corporate action listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard exchange compliance regarding preferential allotment and lock-in requirements met by the company.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 24, 2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: October 26, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of paid-up equity capital through preferential allotment of shares upon warrant conversion, with no broader market impact.
