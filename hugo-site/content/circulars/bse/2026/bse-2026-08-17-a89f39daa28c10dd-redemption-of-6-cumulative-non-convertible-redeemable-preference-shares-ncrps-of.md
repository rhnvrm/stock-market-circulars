## Summary

TVS Motor Company Ltd has fixed a record date for determining eligible holders of its 6% Cumulative Non-Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares (NCRPS) for the full and final redemption of Rs. 10 per preference share along with the applicable coupon payment at 6% per annum.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TVS Motor Company Ltd
- Security Details: 6% Cumulative Non-Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares (NCRPS), ISIN: INE494B04019, Scrip Code: 717506, Notice Ref: DR-699/2026-2027.
- Record Date: 21/08/2026.
- Purpose: Redemption of preference shares and coupon payment.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes reported; standard corporate action execution notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the NCRPS of the company starting from the record date (21/08/2026).

## Important Dates

- Record Date / No Dealings From Date: 21/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Holders of the preference shares will receive the full and final redemption amount of Rs. 10 per share plus the 6% coupon payment as per the company's corporate action schedule.
