## Summary

BSE has announced that the equity shares of Triveni Engineering & Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 532356) will be transferred from the "T" Group to the "A" Group, effective from August 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Triveni Engineering & Industries Ltd
- Scrip Code: 532356
- Previous Group: "T" Group
- New Group: "A" Group
- Effective Date: August 05, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260717-35 dated July 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Permitted trading group transition for the specified equity shares from surveillance/trade-for-trade category ("T" Group) to regular trading category ("A" Group).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are requested to take note of the group change and ensure compliance with the applicable trading segments.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 04, 2026
- Effective Date: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates normal trading under the "A" Group for investors and trading members, removing previous trade-for-trade restrictions associated with the "T" Group.
