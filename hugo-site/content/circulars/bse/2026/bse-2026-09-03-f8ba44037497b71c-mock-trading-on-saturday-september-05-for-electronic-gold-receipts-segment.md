## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on Saturday, September 05, 2026. Trading members can use this session to test trading applications, third-party platforms, or in-house systems via IML/ETI APIs, including testing for exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Scheduled Date: Saturday, September 05, 2026.
- Segment: Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR).
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.50 will be released with details published separately.
- Trades resulting from mock trading do not attract margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, and create no rights or liabilities.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 4:30 PM on the day of the mock trading session.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This session helps members fulfill regulatory requirements in accordance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 dated November 24, 2020.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members can participate in the mock trading session or UAT environment to fulfill testing requirements for software used in trading and risk management.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 05, 2026
- Feedback Submission Deadline: September 05, 2026, by 4:30 PM

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; the session is optional and intended solely for testing, software validation, and system familiarization.
