## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units of DSP Nifty 10 Yr Benchmark G-Sec ETF under DSP Mutual Fund, effective from Thursday, August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** DSP Nifty 10 Yr Benchmark G-Sec ETF
- **Mutual Fund:** DSP Mutual Fund
- **Asset Management Company:** DSP Asset Managers Private Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544875
- **Group:** F
- **Market Lot:** 1 unit
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00 per unit
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 26.89 per unit
- **Date of Allotment:** 14/08/2026
- **ISIN No.:** INF740KA1ZI7

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that units will be traded only in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 14, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to trade units of the DSP Nifty 10 Yr Benchmark G-Sec ETF on the BSE platform.
