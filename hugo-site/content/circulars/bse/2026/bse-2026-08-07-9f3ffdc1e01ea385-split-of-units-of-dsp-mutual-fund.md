## Summary

DSP Mutual Fund has announced a change in the face value (split) of its units for DSP Gold ETF and DSP Silver ETF. The face value of each unit is being split from Rs. 10/- to Re. 01/- effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Schemes Affected:** DSP Gold ETF and DSP Silver ETF.
- **Scrip Codes:** 543903 (DSP Gold ETF), 543572 (DSP Silver ETF).
- **ISIN:** INF740KA1SW3 (DSP Gold ETF), INF740KA1RE3 (DSP Silver ETF).
- **Face Value Change:** From Rs. 10/- per unit to Re. 01/- per unit.
- **Record Date / Effective Date:** August 28, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Corporate action involving unit face value split for exchange-traded funds.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the face value change and update their systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Effective Date / Record Date:** August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Unit holders will receive a proportionate increase in the number of units corresponding to the face value reduction, keeping the total investment value unchanged.
