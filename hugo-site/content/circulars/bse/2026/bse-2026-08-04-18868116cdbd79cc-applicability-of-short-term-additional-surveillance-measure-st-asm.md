## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for various securities based on market surveillance criteria. Effective August 06, 2026, specific margin rates apply to open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for ST-ASM Stage I face an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Securities satisfying criteria for ST-ASM Stage II face an applicable margin rate of 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Surveillance measures apply to all open positions as on August 05, 2026, and new positions created from August 06, 2026.
- Shortlisting is strictly a market surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of revised ST-ASM framework rules as referenced in previous exchange notices.
- Price band for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels, provided no other surveillance measures apply.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% to 100%) for open and new positions in securities specified in Annexure I.
- Clearances and inquiries can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 04 Aug 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance/Margins: August 06, 2026 (on positions as of August 05, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for shortlisted stocks may restrict leverage and short-term trading volumes, affecting market liquidity for specific scrips.
