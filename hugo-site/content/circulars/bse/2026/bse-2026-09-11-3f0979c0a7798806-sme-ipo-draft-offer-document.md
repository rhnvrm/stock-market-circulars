## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by ISSPL Limited on September 10, 2026. The document contains disclosures specified under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations and is hosted on the stock exchange websites to invite public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: ISSPL Limited
- Filing Date: September 10, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments on the Draft Offer Document can be submitted via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 11, 2026
- Submission Deadline: Within 21 days from the notice date

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites stakeholder feedback regarding the upcoming SME public issue of ISSPL Limited.
