## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, specifying securities that satisfy inclusion criteria, move to lower stages (such as Stage I), or shift from rolling settlement to the trade-for-trade segment effective July 28 and July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin on all open positions and new positions.
- Certain securities will continue under the ESM framework but move to a lower Stage I w.e.f. July 28, 2026.
- Qualifying securities will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. July 29, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. July 28, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework rules including margin increases, price band restrictions, and segment migrations for identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the margin obligations and segment changes for securities listed in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II and lower Stage I moves / price bands: July 28, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shifts: July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading liquidity and margin requirements for affected securities will be significantly restricted due to trade-for-trade settlement and 100% margin rules.
