## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Nippon India Income Plus Arbitrage Omni Fund of Fund by Nippon India Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 17, 2026, to August 31, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Offered:** Nippon India Income Plus Arbitrage Omni Fund of Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- **NFO Period:** August 17, 2026, to August 31, 2026 (extended till midnight on August 31, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- **SIP Authentication:** SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on August 31, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 04, 2026.
- **ISINs:** Covers multiple ISINs including INF204KC1HM4, INF204KC1HN2, INF204KC1HR3, INF204KC1HO0, INF204KC1HP7, and INF204KC1HQ5.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 04, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date are ineligible for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 17, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** August 31, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscription and SIP registration for investors participating in the new Nippon India debt fund scheme via the BSE StAR MF platform.
