## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-702/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: Friday, Aug 28, 2026, from 10.30 a.m. to 11.15 a.m.
- Auction matching timings: Friday, Aug 28, 2026, from 11.15 a.m. to 11.30 a.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted offers: 11.30 a.m. to 12.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.55 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Monday, Aug 31, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is now removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 28, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates orderly settlement of short-delivered securities for trading members through standard auction procedures.
