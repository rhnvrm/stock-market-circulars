## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Cubical Financial Services Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from September 17, 2026, to September 30, 2026 under Settlement No. 2026115.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Cubical Financial Services Limited
- **Market Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement Number:** 2026115
- **Bidding Dates:** 17/09/2026 to 30/09/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 09/10/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 12/10/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None specific to regulations; follows established guidelines for the mechanism of acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL & NSDL), using the specified Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement number 2026115.
- Securities will be earmarked/blocked in clients' accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 17/09/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 30/09/2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 09/10/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 12/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement and operational clarity for members and custodians participating in the takeover acquisition window for Cubical Financial Services Limited.
