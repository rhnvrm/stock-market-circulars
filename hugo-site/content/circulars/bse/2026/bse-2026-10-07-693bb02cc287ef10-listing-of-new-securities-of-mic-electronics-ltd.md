## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission for trading of new equity shares of MIC Electronics Ltd (Scrip Code: 532850) with effect from October 8, 2026. A total of 5,68,73,418 equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each have been issued at a premium of Rs. 39.38/- (issue price Rs. 41.38/- per share) to non-promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** MIC Electronics Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 532850
- **ISIN:** INE287C01037
- **Security Details:** 5,68,73,418 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 39.38/- (Total issue price: Rs. 41.38/- per share).
- **Allottees:** Non-Promoters on a preferential basis.
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 241011561 to 297884978.
- **Lock-in Details:** 5,68,73,418 shares are locked-in up to April 14, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory policy changes introduced; this is a standard corporate listing notice for securities issued on a preferential basis.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and trading permissions. Allotted shares are subject to lock-in until April 14, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 4, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** October 8, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** April 14, 2027

## Impact Assessment

The listing of preferential shares expands the equity capital base of MIC Electronics Ltd by 5,68,73,418 shares, resulting in equity dilution for existing shareholders. Trading members are permitted to trade the newly listed securities effective October 8, 2026, subject to applicable lock-in restrictions.
