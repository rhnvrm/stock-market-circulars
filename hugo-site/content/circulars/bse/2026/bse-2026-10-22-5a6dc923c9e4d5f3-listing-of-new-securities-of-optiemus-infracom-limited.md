## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for new equity shares of Optiemus Infracom Limited with effect from October 23, 2026, following a preferential allotment upon warrant conversion.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Optiemus Infracom Limited (Scrip Code: 530135, ISIN: INE350C01017)
- Securities Issued: 1,67,250 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 662.25/-.
- Issue Price: Rs. 672.25/- per share.
- Allottees: Non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.
- Effective Date: Trading permitted from October 23, 2026.
- Distinctive Numbers: 89999201 to 90166450.
- Lock-in Details: 1,67,250 shares are locked-in up to May 10, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine listing notice for securities issued under preferential allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities and trading members must note the distinctive numbers and lock-in period for the newly issued equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 11, 2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: October 23, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 10, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational or market impact, representing standard capital expansion via warrant conversion.
