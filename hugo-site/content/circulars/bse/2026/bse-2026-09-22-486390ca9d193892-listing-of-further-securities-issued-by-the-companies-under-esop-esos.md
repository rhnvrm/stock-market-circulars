## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued by 10 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Wednesday, September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 10.
- Effective date for trading of new securities: September 23, 2026.
- Includes issuances from companies such as Gland Pharma Limited, Larsen & Toubro Limited, and Tata Consumer Products Limited.
- All securities are issued under ESOP / ESOS with 'NA' lock-in status as specified in the distribution details.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the listing of further securities and ensure necessary back-office updates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase due to employee stock option exercises across listed entities, having minimal direct market impact.
