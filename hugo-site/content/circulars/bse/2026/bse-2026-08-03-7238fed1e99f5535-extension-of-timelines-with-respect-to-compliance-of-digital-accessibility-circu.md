## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing all members about the extension of timelines for complying with SEBI's Digital Accessibility Circulars, following reference to SEBI circular no. HO/(411)2026-ITD-5_DIV2/I/17922/2026 dated July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice refers to SEBI circular dated July 31, 2026 regarding Digital Accessibility Circulars.
- Timelines for compliance have been extended for members.
- Members are advised to take note of the revised timelines and ensure adherence.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of existing compliance deadlines for digital accessibility standards as mandated by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members of BSE Clearing Ltd. must review the enclosed Annexure 1 for specific timeline changes and ensure their digital platforms align with the updated requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 3, 2026
- Reference SEBI Circular Date: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulated entities additional time to implement necessary digital accessibility features and systems to comply with SEBI norms without immediate penalty risks.
