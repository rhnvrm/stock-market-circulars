## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of EverestIMS Technologies Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: EverestIMS Technologies Limited (Scrip Code: 544979).
- Segment Transfer: Moving from Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group).
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026.
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20261007-36 dated October 7, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares shifted from MT Group (Trade for Trade) to Rolling segment (M Group), enabling normal rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the change in segment and settlement category for EverestIMS Technologies Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 23, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances liquidity and normalizes trading mechanisms for shareholders of EverestIMS Technologies Limited by removing trade-for-trade restrictions.
