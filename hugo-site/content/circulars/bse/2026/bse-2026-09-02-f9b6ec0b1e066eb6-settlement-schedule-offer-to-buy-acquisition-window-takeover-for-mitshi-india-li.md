## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Mitshi India Limited. The bidding window is scheduled from September 3, 2026, to September 17, 2026 under settlement number 2026110.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Mitshi India Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement Number:** 2026110
- **Bid Start Date:** 03/09/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 17/09/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 30/09/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 01/10/2026
- **Early Pay-in:** Demat shares must be delivered through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement No. 2026110.
- **CDSL & NSDL:** Securities will be earmarked/blocked in clients' accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.

## Regulatory Changes

- Follows revised guidelines for the mechanism of acquisition of shares through stock exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must ensure early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/BSECL systems prior to the prescribed timelines for bidding/confirmation.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bidding Period:** September 3, 2026 – September 17, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** September 30, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates orderly execution and settlement of the takeover offer for Mitshi India Limited shares through the BSE acquisition window.
