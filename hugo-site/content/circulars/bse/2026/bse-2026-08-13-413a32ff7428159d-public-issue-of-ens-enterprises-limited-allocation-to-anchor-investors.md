BSE has informed trading members that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of ENS Enterprises Limited, 10,26,000 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 92/- per equity share as of August 13, 2026.

- Company Name: ENS Enterprises Limited
- Segment: Equity (Listing Department)
- Number of Shares Allocated: 10,26,000 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 92/- per equity share
- Date of Announcement: August 13, 2026
