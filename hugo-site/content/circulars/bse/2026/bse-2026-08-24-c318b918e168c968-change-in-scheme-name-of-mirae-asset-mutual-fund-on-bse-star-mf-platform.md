## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about the communication received from Mirae Asset Mutual Fund regarding the change in scheme names for six of its schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Mirae Asset Mutual Fund is updating scheme names for several mutual fund products.
- The changes become effective on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting August 25, 2026.
- Affected schemes include various duration and debt funds.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational scheme renaming update by the asset management company.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for order placement and reporting on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Existing transactions and systematic investment plans will map to the revised scheme names accordingly.
