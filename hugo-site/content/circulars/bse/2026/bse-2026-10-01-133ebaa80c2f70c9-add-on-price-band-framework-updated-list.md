## Summary

BSE has released an updated list of securities placed under the Add-on Price Band Framework pursuant to periodic surveillance reviews, building upon previous circular guidelines from August 11, 2021.

## Key Points

- Periodic review carried out for identifying securities for the Add-on Price Band Framework.
- Updated list provided in Annexure I of the circular.
- Trading members can reach out to BSE Surveillance for clarifications.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation and enforcement of the Add-on Price Band Framework as per Exchange circular no. 20210811-6.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must verify the updated list of securities in Annexure I and ensure compliance with applicable price bands during trading.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Securities listed in the annexure will be subject to the specified add-on price bands, helping curb excessive price volatility.
