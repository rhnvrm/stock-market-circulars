## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specified State Development Loans (SDLs) / government securities due to their approaching redemption and maturity dates, effective from October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 810896
- Particulars / Security: 741GUJSDL26
- ISIN: IN1520220121
- Effective Date of Suspension: October 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are advised not to deal in the aforesaid T-bills/Government Securities/SDLs with effect from October 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine maturity and cessation of trading for the specified security, resulting in no broader market impact.
