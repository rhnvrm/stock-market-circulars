## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 3,00,000 new equity shares of Caprihans India Ltd. issued on a preferential basis to promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Caprihans India Ltd. (Scrip Code: 509486)
- **Security Details:** 3,00,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 190/- (Issue Price: Rs. 200/-).
- **Effective Listing Date:** Monday, October 19, 2026.
- **ISIN:** INE479A01018
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 14623972 to 14923971
- **Lock-in Details:** 3,00,000 shares locked in up to 29/04/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notice for securities issued via preferential allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the listing and commencement of trading for the new equity shares effective from October 19, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 16, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** March 20, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** April 29, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase for Caprihans India Ltd. through promoter preferential warrant conversion with standard lock-in compliance.
