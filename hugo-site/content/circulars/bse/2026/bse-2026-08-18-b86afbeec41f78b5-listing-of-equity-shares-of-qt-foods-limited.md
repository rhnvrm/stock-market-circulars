## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, August 19, 2026, the equity shares of Q&T Foods Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Q&T Foods Limited
- Scrip ID: QTFOODS
- Scrip Code: 544872
- ISIN: INE13G401012
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 115/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 105/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 17, 2026
- Segment: SME (MT Group)
- Market Maker: Prabhat Financial Services Limited
- Registrar: Skyline Financial Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that trades in this scrip are conducted in dematerialized form and adhere to the minimum market lot of 1,200 equity shares. Securities will be subject to Trade-for-Trade segment restrictions for 10 trading days.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 17, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Q&T Foods Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with standard initial trade-for-trade monitoring safeguards.
