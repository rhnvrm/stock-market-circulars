## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of specified securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective October 09, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions, margin requirements, and trading restrictions for these securities.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure move to higher GSM stages effective October 09, 2026.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value by buyers.
- Stage III: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and ASD of 100% of trade value by buyers.
- Stage IV: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week, ASD of 100% of trade value by buyers with no upward movement.
- Settlement is conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures under the GSM framework originally published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent updates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Ensure compliance with applicable margin rates, ASD deposits, and trade-for-trade settlement rules.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened surveillance and trading restrictions on impacted securities, affecting liquidity, settlement mechanics, and trading frequency depending on the assigned GSM stage.
