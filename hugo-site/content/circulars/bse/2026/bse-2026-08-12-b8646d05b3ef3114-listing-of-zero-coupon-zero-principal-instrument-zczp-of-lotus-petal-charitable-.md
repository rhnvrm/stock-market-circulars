## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the listing of Zero Coupon Zero Principal (ZCZP) instruments of Lotus Petal Charitable Foundation on its Social Stock Exchange (SSE) segment. A total of 5,550 ZCZP instruments with a face value and issue price of Rs. 1,000 each will be listed effective Thursday, August 13, 2026. These instruments will not be traded on the Exchange.

## Key Points

- **Entity Name**: Lotus Petal Charitable Foundation
- **Instrument Type**: Zero Coupon Zero Principal (ZCZP)
- **Listing Date**: Effective from Thursday, August 13, 2026
- **Quantity and Value**: 5,550 ZCZP instruments of Rs. 1,000 each (total issue size Rs. 55.50 Lakhs)
- **Trading Restriction**: The securities will not be traded on the Exchange
- **Identifiers**: Scrip ID: LPCF, Scrip Code: 798003, ISIN: INS2OV712016
- **Allotment Date**: August 4, 2026
- **Maturity Date**: April 12, 2027
- **Registrar to the Issue**: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No major regulatory policy changes are introduced by this notice. It represents an operational implementation under the existing Social Stock Exchange (SSE) framework governed by SEBI regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Listing Entity**: The Lotus Petal Charitable Foundation must adhere to continuous disclosure and compliance requirements mandated for entities listed on the Social Stock Exchange (SSE).
- **Trading Members**: Trading members should note that these ZCZP securities are listed for informational/regulatory purposes and will not be available for trading on the Exchange floor.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment**: August 4, 2026
- **Notice Date**: August 12, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date**: August 13, 2026
- **Maturity Date**: April 12, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. The ZCZP instruments will not be traded on the Exchange, meaning there will be no secondary market trading or price discovery activities.
- **Operational Impact**: Low. Exchange systems will register the new security identifiers for listing record-keeping purposes on the Social Stock Exchange (SSE) segment.
