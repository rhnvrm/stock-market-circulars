## Summary

Effective from Thursday, September 17, 2026, the equity shares of LCC Projects Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities. The public issue comprised 28,96,71,232 equity shares at an issue price of Rs. 146/- per share.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: LCC Projects Limited
- Scrip Code: 544918
- Scrip ID: LCCPROJECT
- ISIN No.: INE1FPN01026
- Face Value: Rs. 5/- per share
- Issue Price: Rs. 146/- per share (including premium of Rs. 141/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Total Securities: 289,671,232 Equity Shares
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 289671232
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: 'B'

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities of the company will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, September 17, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed for lock-in period marking in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of LCC Projects Limited equity shares on the BSE platform, providing liquidity to investors and expanding market participation.
