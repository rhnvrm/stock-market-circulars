## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding communications received from JM Financial AMC about the introduction of Daily SIP Frequency for the JM Large & Mid Cap Fund and revisions to minimum SIP investment amounts and minimum instalments for certain schemes under JM Financial Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Introduction of Daily SIP Frequency for JM Large & Mid Cap Fund.
- Revision in minimum SIP investment amounts and minimum number of instalments.
- Changes implemented on the BSE StAR MF Platform for Daily, Monthly, and Quarterly frequencies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised SIP criteria for Daily, Monthly, and Quarterly frequencies under scheme-wise details provided in the annexure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the revised SIP criteria and ensure transaction execution in accordance with the annexure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 10 August 2026 (with immediate effect)

## Impact Assessment

- Operational alignment for mutual fund distributors and investors transacting on the BSE StAR MF platform regarding JM Financial Mutual Fund schemes.
