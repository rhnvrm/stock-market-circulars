## Summary

PEARL GLOBAL INDUSTRIES LIMITED has announced a bonus issue of equity shares in the ratio of 1:1. The company has fixed September 11, 2026, as the record date and the date from which transactions will be on an ex-bonus basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: PEARL GLOBAL INDUSTRIES LIMITED (Scrip Code: 532808)
- Bonus Ratio: 1 (One) Bonus Equity Share of Rs.5/- each for every 1 (One) existing Equity Share of Rs.5/- each held.
- Record Date: September 11, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Basis From: September 11, 2026
- Allotment Details: 4,61,90,542 Equity Shares are expected to be allotted on or around September 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the record date and ex-bonus trading date for settlement and transaction purposes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 11, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: September 11, 2026
- Expected Allotment Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders as of the record date will receive bonus shares, resulting in an expansion of the company's equity base and an appropriate price adjustment in the stock on the ex-bonus date.
