## Summary

Effective from Friday, July 31, 2026, the equity shares of Silverstorm Parks and Resorts Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Silverstorm Parks and Resorts Limited
- Scrip Code: 544840
- ISIN No: INE1EJ401023
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SSPRL
- Total Securities: 22,676,736 Equity Shares of Rs.10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 133/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 123/-)
- Market Lot: 1000 shares
- Date of Allotment: July 29, 2026
- Market Maker: Rikhav Securities Limited
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing admission per SEBI and Exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000. Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: July 29, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Silverstorm Parks and Resorts Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with initial trade-for-trade monitoring.
