## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the inclusion of securities under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II. The framework introduces enhanced margin requirements effective August 07, 2026, for open positions and new positions created from that date.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I have satisfied criteria for inclusion under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable margin rate is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%, effective August 07, 2026.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%, effective August 07, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of margins and surveillance application under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework in continuation of previous exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements for all open positions as of August 06, 2026, and any new positions created from August 07, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05 Aug 2026
- Effective Date: August 07, 2026 (applicable on open positions as on August 06, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in the shortlisted securities will experience higher margin blocking, which may temporarily affect liquidity and trading volumes in those specific scrips.
