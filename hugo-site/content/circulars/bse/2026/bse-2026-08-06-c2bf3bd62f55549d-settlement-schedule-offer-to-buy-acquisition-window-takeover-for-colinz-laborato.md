## Summary

BSE has issued a settlement schedule and guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Colinz Laboratories Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from August 7, 2026, to August 20, 2026, under settlement number 2026100.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Colinz Laboratories Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Takeover
- **Settlement No.:** 2026100
- **Bid Start Date:** 07/08/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 20/08/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 03/09/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 04/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both CDSL and NSDL depositories.
- Market Type "(Takeover)" and settlement number 2026100 must be used by Members and Custodians.
- Members and Custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members must refer to the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Opening:** August 7, 2026
- **Bid Closing:** August 20, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** September 3, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates orderly execution and settlement of the takeover acquisition window for shareholders and market participants trading in Colinz Laboratories Limited shares.
