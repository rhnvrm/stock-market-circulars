## Summary

Equity shares of Himalaya Nutravedics India Limited are listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE SME platform ('MT' Group) effective Tuesday, September 29, 2026. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI circular guidelines.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Himalaya Nutravedics India Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544943
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** HNIL
- **ISIN:** INE1OTR01013
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 106/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 96/-)
- **Total Securities Listed:** 88,06,320 Equity Shares (Distinctive Range: 1 to 8806320)
- **Market Lot:** 1,200 Equity Shares
- **Trading Group:** 'MT' Group
- **Market Maker:** Allwin Securities Limited
- **Registrar:** Kfin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Securities are admitted to dealings in dematerialised form only and participated in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date. Necessary corporate actions for lock-in periods have been executed in depository records.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must execute trades in dematerialised form subject to the minimum market lot of 1,200 equity shares. Securities will be locked-in as per regulatory guidelines and depository records.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 25, 2026
- **Listing and Trading Effective Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Trade-for-Trade Period:** September 29, 2026 onwards (for 10 trading days)

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and trading liquidity for Himalaya Nutravedics India Limited on the BSE SME segment, with standard trade-for-trade surveillance measures during initial trading days.
