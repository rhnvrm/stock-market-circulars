## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by various companies on the debt market segment effective October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are traded in dematerialised form under specified ISIN numbers.
- Standard denomination for trading is Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuing companies include Infina Finance Private Limited, Adani Enterprises Ltd., Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, BOBCARD LIMITED, and The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to the dematerialised format and standard trading denomination rules.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 15, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Dec 14, 2026 – Jan 13, 2027 (varying by security)

## Impact Assessment

Routine debt market activity providing liquidity and listing for commercial paper issuances.
