## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the discontinuation of weekly trading in securities of companies suspended for non-compliance with certain regulations of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. Specifically, trading in securities on a 'Trade for Trade' basis on the first trading day of every week is being discontinued for Amit International Ltd upon completion of the mandated six-month period.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous Exchange Notice No. 20260128-34 dated January 28, 2026.
- Suspension was due to non-compliance with Regulation 18(1) (non-compliance with the constitution of audit committee) for two consecutive quarters (June 2025 and September 2025).
- Mandated six months of trading on the first trading day of every week completes on September 21, 2026.
- Weekly trading on a 'Trade for Trade' basis in Z group will be discontinued effective September 22, 2026, for Amit International Ltd (Scrip Code: 531300).

## Regulatory Changes

- Governed by SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 issued on July 11, 2023, and last updated on January 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Affected companies and market participants must note the discontinuation of weekly trading slots as per the timeline specified.

## Important Dates

- **Completion Date:** September 21, 2026
- **Effective Date of Discontinuation:** September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects trading availability and procedures for the specified scrip code (531300 - Amit International Ltd) on the stock exchange.
