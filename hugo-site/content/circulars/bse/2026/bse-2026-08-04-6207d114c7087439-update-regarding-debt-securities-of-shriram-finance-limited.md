## Summary

In continuation of Exchange Notice 20250922-41 dated September 22, 2025, the BSE has announced revised terms for debt securities of Shriram Finance Limited, effective from Tuesday, August 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 975871
- **Old ISIN:** INE721A07SN5
- **New ISIN:** INE721A07ST2
- **Old Paid-Up Value:** Rs. 60,000/- Per Debenture
- **New Paid-Up Value:** Rs. 80,000/- Per Debenture
- **Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the updated ISIN and paid-up value details for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for market participants holding or trading the specified debt securities of Shriram Finance Limited.
