## Summary

Quantum Mutual Fund is launching the Quantum Flexi Cap Fund NFO (Regular Plan and Direct Plan - Growth) on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 21, 2026, to September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Details:** Quantum Flexi Cap Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF082J01515) and Quantum Flexi Cap Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF082J01507).
- **NFO Period:** August 21, 2026, to September 04, 2026 (extended till midnight for Lumpsum purchases on the BSE StAR MF Platform).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 10, 2026.
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 09, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (September 04, 2026).
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date shall not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** August 21, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** September 04, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** September 09, 2026 (by 6:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the new fund offer of Quantum Flexi Cap Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform with clear operational timelines and payment rules.
