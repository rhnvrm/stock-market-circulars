## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movement in certain scrips in the recent past and sought clarification from the respective companies to ensure investors have relevant information.

## Key Points

- Clarification was received from Shivansh Finserve Ltd (Scrip Code: 539593).
- Replies are awaited from 8 other companies including Amit Securities Ltd, Aryan Share and Stock Brokers Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Crazy Snacks Ltd, Gconnect Logitech and Supply Chain Ltd, KIOCL Ltd, Sheetal Cool Products Ltd, and Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to clarify significant price and volume movements upon the Exchange's request.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 03 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Informs market participants about ongoing surveillance inquiries into unusual stock price and volume movements.
