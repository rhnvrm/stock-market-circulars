## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, September 26, 2026, utilizing both the primary site (PR) and disaster recovery site (DR). Trading members using third-party trading platforms or in-house developed systems via ETI APIs can test their applications and functionalities under simulated market conditions.

## Key Points

- Scheduled for Saturday, September 26, 2026.
- Covers both Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR).
- Session schedule: Log-in (09:15 am - 10:00 am), Continuous Trading PR (10:00 am - 02:00 pm), Switchover to DR (02:00 pm - 02:45 pm), Continuous Trading DR (02:45 pm - 04:45 pm).
- No new BOLT TWS Release.
- Trades resulting from mock trading attract no margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This circular pertains strictly to operational testing and mock trading activities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to actively participate in the mock trading session and submit their feedback via email or contact numbers provided by 5:30 PM on the day of the session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 26, 2026
- Notice Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact. The session is strictly for testing and familiarization with no real financial obligations or rights created.
