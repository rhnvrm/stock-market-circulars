## Summary

BSE sought clarification from Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd regarding news articles appearing in various media outlets to verify accuracy and inform investors. The company's reply has been received and attached.

## Key Points

- Exchange sought clarification from Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd (Scrip Code: 505537).
- Clarification relates to news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, or television channels.
- Reply received from the company is made available to investors.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to clarify news items as and when requested by the exchange to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 14 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding media reports concerning the company.
