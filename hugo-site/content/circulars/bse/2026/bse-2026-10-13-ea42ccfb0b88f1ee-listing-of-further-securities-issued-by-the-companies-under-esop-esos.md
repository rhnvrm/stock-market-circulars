## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new equity shares issued by five companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS). The listing becomes effective from Wednesday, October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **AJAX Engineering Limited:** 4,516 equity shares issued under ESOP (ISIN: INE274Y01021, Scrip Code: 544356, Dist Nos: 114406801 - 114411316).
- **Avenue Supermarts Limited:** 63,546 equity shares issued under ESOP (ISIN: INE192R01011, Scrip Code: 540376, Dist Nos: 652245978 - 652309523).
- **CreditAccess Grameen Limited:** 19,360 equity shares issued under ESOP (ISIN: INE741K01010, Scrip Code: 541770, Dist Nos: 160518180 - 160537539).
- **IDFC First Bank Limited:** 583,022 equity shares issued under ESOP (ISIN: INE092T01019, Scrip Code: 539437, Dist Nos: 11269263470 - 11269846491).
- **Lodha Developers Limited:** 27,616 equity shares issued under ESOP (ISIN: INE670K01029, Scrip Code: 543287, Dist Nos: 999385817 - 999413432).
- All securities have 'NA' under lock-in status and are admitted for regular trading.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; routine corporate listing action pursuant to ESOP/ESOS allotments.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies and trading members must ensure proper accounting and demat credit of the listed equity shares in accordance with exchange bye-laws and depository regulations.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Effective Date for Trading:** October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minor equity capital expansion for the respective companies due to employee stock option exercises. Market impact is negligible and routine.
