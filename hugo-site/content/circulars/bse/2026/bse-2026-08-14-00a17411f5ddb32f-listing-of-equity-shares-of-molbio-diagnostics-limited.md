## Summary

Trading members are informed that equity shares of Molbio Diagnostics Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange effective from Monday, August 17, 2026, under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: Molbio Diagnostics Limited
- Scrip Code: 544866
- ISIN No.: INE869T01028
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name on BOLT System: MOLBIO
- Number of Securities: 115,239,996 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 807/- per share (Face Value Rs. 1/- and premium of Rs. 806/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 13, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be a part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on August 17, 2026.
- Lock-in period marked in depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 13, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Molbio Diagnostics Limited equity shares on the BSE platform.
