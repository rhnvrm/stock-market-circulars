## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in the Commercial Paper of Birla Group Holdings Private Limited on account of the fixing of the record date for redemption.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Birla Group Holdings Private Limited
- Scrip Code: 731728
- Scrip ID: BGHPL-21-08-26-CP
- ISIN: INE09OL14JF1
- Record Date: 20/08/2026
- Ex-Date: 20/08/2026
- Trading Members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Paper of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified commercial paper effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 20 August 2026
- Ex-Date: 20 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specific commercial paper of Birla Group Holdings Private Limited is halted as it approaches redemption.
