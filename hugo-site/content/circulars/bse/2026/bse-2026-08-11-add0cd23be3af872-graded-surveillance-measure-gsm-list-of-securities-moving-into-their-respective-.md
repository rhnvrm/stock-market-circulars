## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of specified securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective August 12, 2026, pursuant to previous framework guidelines.

## Key Points

- Affected securities will transition to higher GSM stages starting August 12, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, ranging from 100% margin rates and price band restrictions to trade-for-trade settlement and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement for impacted securities shall be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of higher GSM stages as per the framework published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities due to trade-to-trade settlement and lack of netting off.
- Market participants subject to GSM stages must adhere to the prescribed margin and ASD requirements (e.g., 50% or 100% ASD).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 12, 2026
- Notice Date: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading frequency for securities moving into Stages III and IV will be restricted to once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week).
- Buyers must deposit additional funds for ASD, increasing the capital requirement for trading these securities.
