## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited (Scrip Code: 524632) effective from Tuesday, August 4, 2026. A total of 17,35,000 equity shares were issued to promoters on a preferential basis following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shukra Pharmaceuticals Limited
- Scrip Code: 524632
- ISIN: INE551C01044
- Securities Issued: 17,35,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 34.56/- (Issue Price: Rs. 35.56/-)
- Allottees: Promoters (preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants)
- Distribution Nos.: 437879441 to 439614440
- Date of Allotment: 07/05/2026
- Lock-in Details: 17,35,000 shares locked-in upto 13/02/2028

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to note the listing and trading availability of the new securities under the specified scrip code and ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 07/05/2026
- Effective Date for Trading: 04/08/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: 13/02/2028

Standard corporate listing update regarding preferential allotment and warrant conversion with standard lock-in compliance for promoters.
