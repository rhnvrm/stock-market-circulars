## Summary

BSE has issued a notice extending the compliance timelines for digital accessibility circulars based on SEBI's directive. The deadline for conducting accessibility audits for digital platforms and remediating audit findings has been extended to October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reference to SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131 and subsequent SEBI Circular No. HO/(411)2026-ITD-5_DIV2/I/17922/2026.
- Extension granted for conducting accessibility audits and remediation of findings.
- Applies to all regulated entities under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

## Regulatory Changes

- Timeline extension for digital platform accessibility audits and remediation of findings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated entities must complete accessibility audits and remediate any findings by the extended deadline.

## Important Dates

- Extended Deadline: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulated entities additional time to ensure full digital accessibility compliance without immediate penal action.
