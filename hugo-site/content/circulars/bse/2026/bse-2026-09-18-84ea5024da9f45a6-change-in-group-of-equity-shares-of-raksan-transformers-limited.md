## Summary

The BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Raksan Transformers Limited (SME IPO, Scrip Code: 544924) from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective Monday, October 5, 2026. This follows the exchange's previous notice dated September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Raksan Transformers Limited
- Scrip Code: 544924
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 5, 2026
- Contact Person: Mr. Anurag Jain (Tel: 022 – 2272 8822)

## Regulatory Changes

- The trading category for the company's equity shares is being shifted from restricted trade-for-trade settlement to the standard rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the segment change and ensure appropriate processing of transactions in the company's equity shares as per the rolling segment rules starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shifting to the rolling segment removes trade-for-trade restrictions, enabling normal rolling settlement which typically enhances liquidity and trading flexibility for market participants.
