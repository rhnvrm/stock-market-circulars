## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by multiple companies on the BSE Debt Segment effective July 31, 2026. The list includes issuances from Bajaj Finance Limited, DSP Finance Private Limited, InCred Financial Services Limited, Jio Credit Limited, and Axis Finance Limited.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Debt Market Segment
- **Department:** DCS-Listing
- **Securities Listed:** Commercial Papers (CPs) issued on a private placement basis.
- **Trading Terms:** Dematerialised form under specific ISINs, standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs, and tick size of 1 paise.
- **Issuers Involved:**
  - Bajaj Finance Limited (Scrip Code: 732191, ISIN: INE296A14I75)
  - DSP Finance Private Limited (Scrip Code: 732192, ISIN: INE422H14545)
  - InCred Financial Services Limited (Scrip Code: 732193, ISIN: INE321N14513)
  - Jio Credit Limited (Scrip Code: 732194, ISIN: INE282H14147)
  - Axis Finance Limited (Scrip Code: 732195, ISIN: INE891K14QD6)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing announcement under the BSE debt market segment framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard dematerialised trading norms and denomination rules for commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Redemption Dates:** Ranging from October 2026 to July 2027 depending on the specific issuance.

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official exchange admission for the newly issued commercial papers, enabling secondary market trading for investors.
