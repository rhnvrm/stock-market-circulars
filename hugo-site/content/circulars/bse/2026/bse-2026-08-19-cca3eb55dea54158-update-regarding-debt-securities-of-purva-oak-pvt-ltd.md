## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in debt securities (Non-Convertible Debentures) of Purva Oak Pvt Ltd due to restructuring, effective from Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading in specific ISINs of Purva Oak Pvt Ltd will be suspended w.e.f. August 24, 2026.
- Affected ISINs include INE0SRE08062 (Scrip Code: 977540, 24,000 NCDs), INE0SRE08047 (Scrip Code: 977542, 15,500 NCDs), and INE0SRE08054 (Scrip Code: 977543, 4,500 NCDs).
- The suspension is part of a restructuring process following previous notice number 20260212-35.

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension of trading for specified debt securities pursuant to corporate restructuring.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and investors must note the suspension of trading for the listed ISINs and avoid executing trades in these securities from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: August 24, 2026
- Notice Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity for the specified NCDs of Purva Oak Pvt Ltd will be temporarily halted on the exchange platform due to the restructuring.
