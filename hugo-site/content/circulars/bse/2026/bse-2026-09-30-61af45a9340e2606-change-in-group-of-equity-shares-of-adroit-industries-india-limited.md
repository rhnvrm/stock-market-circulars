## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of ADROIT INDUSTRIES (INDIA) LIMITED (Scrip Code: 544950) from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment under B Group, effective October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: ADROIT INDUSTRIES (INDIA) LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544950
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: Thursday, October 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of trading segment from T Group to B Group for normal rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the change in segment and group classification for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances trading liquidity and operational flexibility for investors by moving the scrip from the Trade for Trade segment to the normal Rolling segment.
