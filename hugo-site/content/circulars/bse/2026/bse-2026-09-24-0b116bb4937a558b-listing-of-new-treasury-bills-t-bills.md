## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of three new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS effective September 25, 2026. The listing includes 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day T-Bills.

## Key Points

- Listing Effective Date: September 25, 2026.
- Segment: G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS.
- Scrip 805271 (ISIN: IN002026X255, Symbol: 91TB241226, Maturity: 24/12/2026).
- Scrip 805272 (ISIN: IN002026Y253, Symbol: 182TB25327, Maturity: 25/03/2027).
- Scrip 805273 (ISIN: IN002026Z250, Symbol: 364TB23927, Maturity: 23/09/2027).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that the listed securities shall not be available for trading two (2) working days prior to their respective Maturity/Redemption Date.

## Important Dates

- Listing Date: September 25, 2026
- Maturity Dates: December 24, 2026 (91-day), March 25, 2027 (182-day), September 23, 2027 (364-day)

## Impact Assessment

Routine sovereign debt issuance providing short-term money market investment instruments with minimal direct operational impact on equity markets.
