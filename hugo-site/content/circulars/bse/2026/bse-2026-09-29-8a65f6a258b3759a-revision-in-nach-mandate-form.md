## Summary

The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) has revised the NACH Mandate Form. BSE StAR MF will make the revised form available on its platform starting October 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- NPCI has revised the NACH Mandate Form.
- BSE StAR MF platform will provide the revised NACH Mandate Form effective October 1, 2026.
- Existing NACH Mandate Forms remain valid up to September 30, 2026.
- Post September 30, 2026, destination banks may accept or reject mandates in previous formats based on compliance with revised NPCI standards.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adoption of revised NACH Mandate Form standards prescribed by NPCI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that new NACH mandates are submitted in the revised format from October 1, 2026 onwards.
- Be aware that destination banks may reject older format mandates submitted after September 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **2026-09-30**: Last date for validity of existing NACH Mandate Form.
- **2026-10-01**: Effective date for availability and use of the revised NACH Mandate Form on BSE StAR MF.

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is low, requiring members and investors to transition to the revised NACH Mandate Form for mutual fund transactions on BSE StAR MF starting October 1, 2026.
