## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 3,00,00,000 new equity shares of Zee Media Corporation Limited (Scrip Code - 532794) with effect from July 31, 2026. These shares are issued to non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Zee Media Corporation Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 532794
- **ISIN:** INE966H01019
- **Securities:** 3,00,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 7.50/- (Issue Price: Rs. 8.50/-)
- **Effective Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Distribution Nos.:** 625428681 to 655428680
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to 31/01/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and distribution numbers as specified.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 30/06/2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** 31/07/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 31/01/2027

## Impact Assessment

Increase in paid-up equity capital of the company due to warrant conversion.
