## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that SEBI turnover fees for various segments along with applicable GST at 18% for the month of July 2026 will be debited from their settlement or exchange dues accounts on August 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- SEBI turnover fee for Cash, Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, Interest Rate Derivatives, New Debt Segment, Repo Segment, and EGR segment is subject to 18% GST.
- The turnover fees including GST will be debited on the 3rd working day of the next month (August 5, 2026).
- Extranet details and calculation files (TF2A through TF2J series) will be made available on or before August 4, 2026.
- Invoices for July 2026 will be available for download in the Extranet under Periodic files.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of 18% GST on SEBI turnover fees across eligible market segments effective from July 18, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure sufficient funds are available in their settlement (valan) account or exchange dues account on the debit date.
- Members should verify their turnover figures and fee computations from the EXTRANET files (column 26 of TF2A file, etc.) provided on or before August 4, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Extranet Files & Invoices Availability:** August 04, 2026
- **Account Debit Date:** August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Direct cash flow impact on trading members as monthly SEBI turnover fees and associated 18% GST are automatically debited from settlement accounts.
