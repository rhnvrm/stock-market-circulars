## Summary

BSE has issued operational guidelines following SEBI Circular No. HO/38/11/(9)2026-MIRSD-POD/I/15382/2026 dated July 03, 2026, regarding the handling of clients' unpaid securities by trading members.

## Key Points

- Operational guidelines developed in consultation with SEBI, Depositories, and Broker's Industry Standards Forum (ISF).
- Enclosed as Annexure-1 to the notice for member compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of provisions specified in SEBI Circular No. HO/38/11/(9)2026-MIRSD-POD/I/15382/2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- All trading members are advised to take note and comply with the operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 July 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects operational workflows of trading members concerning client securities management and compliance.
