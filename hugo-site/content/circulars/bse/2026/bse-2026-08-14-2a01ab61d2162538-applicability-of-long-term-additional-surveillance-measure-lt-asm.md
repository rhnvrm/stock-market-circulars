## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability, periodic review, and revision of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The changes include the imposition of 100% margins, movement to higher stages with lower price bands, transfers to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS), and removal of certain securities from the framework.

## Key Points

- **100% Margins:** Applicable effective August 19, 2026, on all open positions as of August 18, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- **Higher Stages & Price Bands:** Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM effective August 17, 2026, will have Lower Price Bands imposed.
- **Group Transfers:** Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS groups effective August 19, 2026.
- **Exits:** List of securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective August 17, 2026, is provided in Annexure II.
- **Nature of Action:** Shortlisting is strictly based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions by listed companies and is not an adverse action against the entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised LT-ASM frameworks in accordance with previous exchange guidelines.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements for applicable securities from the effective date.
- Monitor group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS) and price band restrictions for impacted scrips.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Higher Stages / Price Bands / Exits:** August 17, 2026
- **Effective Date for 100% Margins / Group Transfers:** August 19, 2026
- **Open Position Cut-off Date:** August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for securities listed under the LT-ASM framework may experience constraints due to higher margin obligations, lower price bands, and movement into restrictive settlement groups.
