## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the display of investor awareness messages by Trading Members on their trading apps and websites under Project Jagrook, following SEBI circular No. HO/38/24/(15)2026-MIRSD-PODMMC/I/22872/2026 dated October 01, 2026. The initiative aligns with SEBI's nationwide investor awareness initiative and World Investor Week 2026.

## Key Points

- Mandates display of specific Project Jagrook and Risk Disclosure messages for investor benefit, safety, and education.
- Specifies phased implementation timelines starting October 05, 2026, through November 01, 2026, onwards.
- Requires bilingual format (Hindi-English) for messages displayed from November 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading members must display specific investor awareness messages: ‘SEBI presents Samajh Se Investing Simple: Understand Before You Invest, Choose Regulated Investment Products, Start Early, Stay Invested, Beware of Investment Frauds’.
- From November 1, 2026, mandatory display on website landing pages and alternate day display on trading apps alongside risk disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

- **October 5 - October 31, 2026:** Mandatory website display of Project Jagrook messages; voluntary display on trading apps (where optional risk disclosures apply if displayed).
- **November 1, 2026 onwards:** Mandatory landing page display on websites; mandatory alternate day display on trading apps following a structured weekly schedule (Monday/Wednesday/Friday/Sunday for Jagrook messages, Tuesday/Thursday/Saturday for Risk Disclosure).
- Messages must be in bilingual format (Hindi-English) from November 1, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **October 01, 2026:** SEBI circular issuance date.
- **October 05, 2026:** Notice issuance date and start of phase 1 voluntary/mandatory period.
- **October 31, 2026:** End of phase 1 period.
- **November 01, 2026:** Commencement of mandatory bilingual and alternate-day display requirements.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must update their web portals and trading application landing pages to support scheduled bilingual display of Project Jagrook investor awareness messages and risk disclosures.
