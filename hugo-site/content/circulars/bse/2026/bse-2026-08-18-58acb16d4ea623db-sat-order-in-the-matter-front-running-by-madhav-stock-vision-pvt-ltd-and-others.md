## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order and SEBI's confirmation that the appellants in the matter of front running—Madhav Stock Vision Pvt. Ltd. and Others—have paid the requisite penalty and disgorgement amount in favor of SEBI.

## Key Points

- Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260724-26 dated July 24, 2026.
- SAT stated via its order dated August 10, 2026, that the stay on debarment is subject to the deposit of the entire penalty and disgorgement amount.
- SEBI confirmed via email on August 18, 2026, that Madhav Stock Vision Pvt. Ltd. and Ors. have successfully paid the penalty and disgorgement amount.
- Appellants include Madhav Stock Vision Pvt. Ltd., Ajay Sampatraj Jain, and Rajkumar Prabhu Damani.

## Regulatory Changes

None directly introduced by this notice; it serves as a status update on compliance with a previous SAT order and SEBI requirements regarding penalty payments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the compliance and deposit fulfillment by the mentioned entities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 18, 2026
- SAT Order Date: August 10, 2026
- SEBI Email Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Procedural update notifying market participants of compliance status fulfillment by the penalized entities.
