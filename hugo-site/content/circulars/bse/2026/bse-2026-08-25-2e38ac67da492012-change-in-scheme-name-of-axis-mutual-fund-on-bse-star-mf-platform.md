## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the change in scheme names for several schemes of Axis Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026, based on communication received from Axis AMC.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Axis Ultra Short Duration Fund is renamed to Axis Ultra Short Term Fund.
- Axis Short Duration Fund is renamed to Axis Short Term Fund.
- Axis Long Duration Fund is renamed to Axis Long Term Fund.
- Axis Dynamic Bond Fund is renamed to Axis Dynamic Term Fund.
- Axis Floater Fund is renamed to Axis Floating Interest Rates Fund.
- Axis Strategic Bond Fund is renamed to Axis Medium Term Fund.
- Axis Treasury Advantage Fund is renamed to Axis Ultra Short To Short Term Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; administrative nomenclature update by the Asset Management Company.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and distributors must update their internal systems and order-matching platforms to reflect the revised scheme names.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Investors and trading members should use the revised names for future transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.
