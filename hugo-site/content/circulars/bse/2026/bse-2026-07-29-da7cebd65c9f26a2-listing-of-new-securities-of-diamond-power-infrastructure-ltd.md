## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of 7,11,00,000 equity shares of Diamond Power Infrastructure Ltd (Scrip Code: 522163, ISIN: INE989C01038) with effect from Thursday, July 30, 2026. These shares were allotted to QIBs pursuant to a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) at an issue price of Rs. 227/- per share.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Diamond Power Infrastructure Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 522163
- **ISIN:** INE989C01038
- **Security Details:** 7,11,00,000 Equity shares of Rs.1/- each allotted to QIBs pursuant to QIP.
- **Effective Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** July 28, 2026
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 227/-
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 526971061 to 598071060
