## Summary

Titan Bio-Tech Ltd. has informed the exchange that it has fixed October 23, 2026, as the record date for the issuance of bonus equity shares in the ratio of 1:4 (one bonus equity share of Rs. 2/- each for every four existing equity shares of Rs. 2/- each held).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Titan Bio-Tech Ltd. (Scrip Code: 524717)
- **Bonus Ratio:** 1:4 (1 bonus share for every 4 shares held)
- **Face Value:** Rs. 2/- each
- **Record Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Ex-Bonus Basis From:** October 23, 2026 (Settlement No. DR-741/2026-2027)
- **Allotment Details:** 10,329,625 equity shares scheduled for allotment as on October 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange must take note of the record date and trade on an ex-bonus basis effective from October 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Record Date / Ex-Bonus Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The bonus issue increases the total equity share capital of the company, resulting in a proportional price adjustment on the ex-bonus date and improving liquidity.
