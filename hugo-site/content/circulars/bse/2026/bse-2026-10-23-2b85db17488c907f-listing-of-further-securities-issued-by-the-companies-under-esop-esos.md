## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 13 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Monday, October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 13 listed entities.
- Effective date for trading of new securities: October 26, 2026.
- Securities involved include equity shares issued pursuant to ESOP/ESOS exercises.
- Companies include major financial institutions like Axis Bank, HDFC Bank, Bandhan Bank, and SBFC Finance, alongside other corporate entities.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine corporate listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and listed entities must ensure proper accounting and demat crediting of the listed securities in accordance with existing depository and exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine issuance and listing of ESOP shares resulting in minor equity dilution and expansion of paid-up capital for the respective companies, with negligible overall market impact.
