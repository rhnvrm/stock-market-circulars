## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are required to test their connectivity and system configurations using the provided Disaster Recovery (DR) parameters.

## Key Points

- Mock trading is scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Details regarding DR connection parameters for BOLT Plus Trading system (BOLT TWS and ETI APIs) are provided.
- Web-based system links and respective IP/Port settings for RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, and Extranet are outlined.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should verify their system connectivity to the DR site and ensure all configuration settings (IPs and ports) are updated as per the guidelines.
