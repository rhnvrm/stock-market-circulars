## Summary

BSE has announced that specified securities will be moved to their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework effective October 12, 2026. Trading members are required to take adequate precautions as settlement will be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Key Points

- Securities move to higher GSM stages (I, II, III, or IV) as per the provisions of the GSM framework.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value by buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday), and 100% ASD (with no upward movement for Stage IV).
- Settlement on trade-to-trade basis with no netting off permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of higher surveillance stages for listed securities under the BSE Graded Surveillance Measure framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adherence to enhanced margin rates, ASD deposits, and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
- Exercise adequate due diligence and caution when trading in affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trading restrictions for impacted securities, potentially affecting liquidity and trading frequency.
