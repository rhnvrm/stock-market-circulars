## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that new securities of NCL Research And Financial Services Ltd (Scrip Code - 530557) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Details: 49,94,86,400 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each issued at par on rights basis.
- ISIN: INE132F01038
- Distribution Nos.: 1070328001 to 1569814400
- Date of Allotment: 21/09/2026
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 21/09/2026
- Commencement of Trading: 24/09/2026

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital of the company due to the listing of rights issue shares.
