## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the change of name for Rishab Special Yarns Ltd to Impera Worldwide Limited. The scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Rishab Special Yarns Ltd
- New Company Name: Impera Worldwide Limited
- Scrip Code: 514177
- New Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System: IMPERA
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: IMPERA
- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to take note of the new name and scrip identifiers for all future trading and settlement operations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update for trading systems to reflect the updated company name and scrip identifiers with no direct financial impact.
