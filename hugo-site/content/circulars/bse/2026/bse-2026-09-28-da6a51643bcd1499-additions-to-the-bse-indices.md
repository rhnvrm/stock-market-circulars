## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of three newly listed companies—FX Multitech Limited, Vivekanand Cotspin Limited, and ROBOKIDZ EDUVENTURES LIMITED—to the BSE SME IPO index, effective from the market open of Tuesday, September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- FX Multitech Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544941), Vivekanand Cotspin Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544939), and ROBOKIDZ EDUVENTURES LIMITED (Exchange Ticker: 544940) listed on BSE effective Monday, September 28, 2026.
- The stocks will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open of Tuesday, September 29, 2026.
- These corporate actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and index trackers should update their index constituents accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Index Addition: September 29, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Routine index adjustment with minimal direct market impact.
