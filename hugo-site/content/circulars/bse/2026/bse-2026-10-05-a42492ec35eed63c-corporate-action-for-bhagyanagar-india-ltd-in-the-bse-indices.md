## Summary

Bhagyanagar India Ltd is demerging Tieramet Limited, with the effective date set for October 8, 2026. Shareholders will receive 1 equity share of Tieramet Limited for every 1 equity share held in Bhagyanagar India Ltd. Additionally, Tieramet Limited will be added to several BSE indices starting October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Bhagyanagar India Ltd (Ticker: 512296, BHAGYANGR) is undertaking a demerger of Tieramet Limited.
- Share entitlement: 1 equity share of Tieramet Limited for every 1 equity share held in Bhagyanagar India Ltd.
- Effective date for demerger and index inclusion: Thursday, October 8, 2026.
- A Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS) will be held on October 8, 2026, for price discovery of Bhagyanagar India Ltd post-demerger.
- Tieramet Limited will be added to BSE SMALLCAP, BSE ALLCAP, BSE COMMODITIES, and BSE MIDSMALLCAP indices at zero price without divisor adjustment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of index additions and corporate action adjustments by BSE Index Services Private Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and shareholders must note the share entitlement ratio and participate in the Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS) on October 8, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 5, 2026
- Effective Date / SPOS / Index Addition: October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders receive demerged shares of Tieramet Limited. Market trading is facilitated via SPOS for price discovery, and index funds tracking BSE indices will account for the new constituent.
