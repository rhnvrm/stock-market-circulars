## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the suspension of trading in four mutual fund schemes of SBI Mutual Fund due to their maturity. Trading members are advised not to deal in these schemes effective July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Four fixed maturity plan (FMP) schemes of SBI Mutual Fund have reached their maturity date.
- Scrip codes affected: 543564, 543565, 543566, and 543567.
- Trading in these schemes is suspended with effect from July 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified mutual fund schemes from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- Effective Date: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is restricted to halting trading in matured fixed maturity plan units of the specified schemes.
