## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment scheduled for Saturday, August 08, 2026. Trading members using third-party platforms, empaneled vendors, or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their applications under various market conditions.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR)
- **Date:** Saturday, August 08, 2026
- **Purpose:** Testing and familiarization for trading applications and functionalities (including risk-reduction mode and trading halt).
- **No BOLT TWS Release:** There will be no new BOLT TWS release for this session.
- **Obligations:** Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract any margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations and will not create rights and liabilities.
- **Feedback:** Members are requested to provide feedback by 1:00 pm via the provided contact numbers or email.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are encouraged to participate actively in the mock trading session to ensure robustness of their systems and provide necessary feedback to the exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** August 08, 2026
- **Log-in Time:** 09:15 am to 10:00 am
- **Pre-Open Order Entry Period:** 10:00 am to 10:08 am
- **Pre-Open Matching Period:** 10:08 am to 10:15 am
- **Continuous Trading:** 10:15 am to 12:00 pm
- **Block Deal Window:** 11:15 am to 11:30 am
- **Closing Session:** 12:00 pm to 12:10 pm
- **Post-Closing:** 12:10 pm to 12:20 pm
- **Feedback Submission Deadline:** 08 August 2026 by 1:00 pm

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal and restricted to testing environments. No financial or regulatory risks are associated with this mock session.
