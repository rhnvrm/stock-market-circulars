## Summary

Effective from Monday, August 31, 2026, the equity shares of Trustwave Securities Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE in the XT Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI guidelines following a scheme of capital reduction approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench-I.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 31, 2026
- Company Name: Trustwave Securities Limited
- Scrip Code: 508963
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: TRUSTWAVE
- ISIN: INE668Y01016
- Group: XT
- Market Lot: 1
- Face Value & Paid-up Value: Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Total Securities: 3,26,880 fully paid-up equity shares
- Distinctive Numbers: 1 to 326880

## Regulatory Changes

- The Scheme of Reduction of Capital was approved by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench-I on March 19, 2026.
- Capital reduction involved decreasing the paid-up share capital from Rs. 6,53,76,000/- (65,32,600 shares of Rs. 10/- each) to Rs. 32,68,800/- (3,26,880 shares of Rs. 10/- each) against accumulated losses without payment of consideration.

## Compliance Requirements

- The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circulars CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 and SEBI/HO/MRD-TPD1/CIR/P/2023/55.
- The security will be part of the special pre-open session for IPO and other category scrips.

## Important Dates

- NCLT Approval Date: March 19, 2026
- Notice Date: August 27, 2026
- Listing & Trading Effective Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and investors are informed about the restructured capital base and the temporary placement of the scrip in the Trade-for-Trade segment.
