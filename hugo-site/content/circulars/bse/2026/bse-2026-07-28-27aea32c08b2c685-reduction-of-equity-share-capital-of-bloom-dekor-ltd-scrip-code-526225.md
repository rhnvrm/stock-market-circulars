## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that Bloom Dekor Ltd. has fixed a record date for the reduction of its equity share capital pursuant to a resolution plan approved by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Ahmedabad Bench.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bloom Dekor Ltd. (Scrip Code: 526225)
- Record Date / Suspension Date: 31/07/2026
- Capital Reduction Ratio: Against every 250 existing fully paid-up equity shares of Rs. 10 each, shareholders shall be allotted 1 new fully paid-up equity share of Rs. 10 each, and existing shares stand cancelled.
- Minimum Allocation: Shareholders holding less than 250 equity shares shall also receive at least 1 new fully paid-up equity share of Rs. 10 each, irrespective of the number of shares held.
- Settlement / Order Reference: DR- 684/2026-2027

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of NCLT approved reduction of equity share capital.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to note the record date and the adjustment in share capital.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 31/07/2026
- No Dealings / Suspension w.e.f.: 31/07/2026

## Impact Assessment

Substantial restructuring of equity shares and consolidation of holdings for existing shareholders of Bloom Dekor Ltd.
