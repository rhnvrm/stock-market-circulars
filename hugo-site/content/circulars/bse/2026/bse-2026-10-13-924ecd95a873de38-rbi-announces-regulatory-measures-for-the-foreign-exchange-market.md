## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants about the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) notification on "Regulatory Measures for the Foreign Exchange Market" dated October 10, 2026. All participants in the currency derivatives segment are advised to take note of the guidelines.

## Key Points

- BSE notified participants regarding RBI's foreign exchange market regulatory measures.
- The RBI notification is dated October 10, 2026.
- A copy of the RBI notification has been attached as an annexure to the notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adoption and implementation of RBI regulatory measures for the foreign exchange market as detailed in the attached notification.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must review and adhere to the updated regulatory requirements set forth by the RBI for foreign exchange and currency derivatives.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 13, 2026
- RBI Notification Date: October 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts entities trading in currency derivatives and operating within the foreign exchange market by ensuring compliance with the latest RBI circular.
