## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited announced the addition of three newly listed companies—Vama Wovenfab Ltd, Quanto Agroworld Ltd, and Shakti Polytarp Ltd—to the BSE SME IPO index, effective at the open of Wednesday, September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Vama Wovenfab Ltd (Exchange ticker: 544931), Quanto Agroworld Ltd (Exchange ticker: 544932), and Shakti Polytarp Ltd (Exchange ticker: 544933) are being listed on BSE effective September 22, 2026.
- Stocks will be included in the BSE SME IPO index effective from the market open on September 23, 2026.
- Actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine index inclusion for newly listed SME securities with minimal market-wide impact.
