## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of Sunshine Pictures Limited and Shankesh Jewellers Limited to the BSE IPO Index, effective at the open of Thursday, August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Sunshine Pictures Limited (Exchange ticker: 544881) and Shankesh Jewellers Limited (Exchange ticker: 544882) are being listed on BSE effective August 25, 2026.
- Both stocks will be included in the BSE IPO Index starting August 27, 2026.
- These actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE) for August 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No specific compliance actions required for market participants; for queries, contact bseindex@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Index Addition: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard index inclusion for newly listed IPO shares with minimal direct market impact beyond passive index tracking adjustments.
