## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-695/2026-27. The notice outlines the auction offer entry, matching, reporting timings, and pay-in schedule for August 2026.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement Number: DR-695/2026-27
- Auction offer entry timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on August 18, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on August 18, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted offers: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m. on August 18, 2026.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m. on August 18, 2026.
- Pay-in of Auction: Wednesday, August 19, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is now removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must exercise utmost care while punching security codes and rates, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members should refer to the cutoff rate in the "Auction offer Entry Screen" and monitor corporate actions (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 18, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational circular ensuring smooth settlement and clearing processes for short deliveries under rolling settlement protocols.
