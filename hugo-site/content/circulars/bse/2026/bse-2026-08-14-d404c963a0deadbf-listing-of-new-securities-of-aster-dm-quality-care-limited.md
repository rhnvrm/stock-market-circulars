## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading of new equity shares of Aster DM Quality Care Limited with effect from August 17, 2026, pursuant to a scheme of amalgamation.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aster DM Quality Care Limited (Scrip Code: 540975)
- Security Details: 35,35,51,410 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation amongst Quality Care India Limited (Transferor Company) and Aster DM Quality Care Limited (Transferee Company).
- ISIN Number: INE914M01019
- Distinctive Numbers: 523835315 to 877386724
- Date of Allotment: July 13, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are informed that the shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: July 13, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up capital base for Aster DM Quality Care Limited following the amalgamation with Quality Care India Limited.
