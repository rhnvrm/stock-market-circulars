## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities listed in the annexures have satisfied criteria for inclusion, movement to lower stages, or exit from the ESM framework.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin effective August 20, 2026, on all open positions as of August 19, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but move to lower Stage I effective August 19, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective August 20, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective August 19, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of ESM framework provisions including 100% margins and Trade-for-Trade segment transfers.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collections (minimum 100%) on applicable open and new positions.
- Market participants must account for settlement shifts to Trade-for-Trade for impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- **August 19, 2026:** Movement of securities to lower Stage I / Stage II (with 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction), and removal of specified securities from ESM framework.
- **August 20, 2026:** Effective date for minimum 100% margin requirement and shifting of qualifying securities to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading activity for securities placed under ESM or moving to Stage II may be impacted due to 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement, and periodic call auctions.
