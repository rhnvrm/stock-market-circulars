## Summary

Effective from Friday, September 4, 2026, the equity shares of Paluck Technologies Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities in the SME segment. In accordance with SEBI guidelines, the scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Paluck Technologies Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544897
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** PALUCK
- **ISIN No:** INE0LHB01010
- **Total Securities:** 20,822,282 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 1 to 20822282
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 48/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 38/-)
- **Market Lot:** 3,000 Equity Shares
- **Date of Allotment:** September 2, 2026
- **Market Maker:** Giriraj Stock Broking Private Limited
- **Registrar:** Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in the minimum market lot of 3,000 shares.
- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 2, 2026
- **Listing/Commencement of Trading Date:** September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Paluck Technologies Limited shares on the BSE SME platform, providing liquidity to investors subject to initial trade-for-trade surveillance constraints.
