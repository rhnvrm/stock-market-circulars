## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, outlining securities satisfying inclusion criteria, moving to lower stages, or continuing under surveillance. Affected securities will attract a minimum 100% margin, shift to the trade-for-trade segment, and be subject to specific price bands and periodic call auctions.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy inclusion criteria and attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 28, 2026, on all open positions as of September 25, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but move to lower Stage I w.e.f. September 25, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 25, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 28, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of Enhanced Surveillance Measures including mandatory 100% margins and trade-for-trade settlement for shortlisted securities.
- Imposition of stage-specific controls such as 2% price bands and periodic call auctions for securities shifting to Stage II.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with minimum 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities effective September 28, 2026.
- Members must account for settlement shifts from rolling settlement to trade-for-trade.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage II shift, lower stage movement, and review: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margins and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced trading restrictions, liquidity constraints, and margin obligations for market participants holding securities under the ESM framework.
- Improved price discovery and risk mitigation through periodic call auctions and narrowed price bands.
