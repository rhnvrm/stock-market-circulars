## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the New Fund Offer (NFO) of ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, specifically revising the reopen date for ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031.

## Key Points

- Reference to previous Notice No. 20260825-40 dated August 25, 2026.
- Communication received from ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund regarding the schedule change.
- Revised reopen date for ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031 changed from September 18, 2026, to September 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the revised reopen date for order placement and processing on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Revised Reopen Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to scheduling adjustments for investors and members participating in the ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund NFO.
