## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in certain scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies and published the replies received alongside a list of companies whose replies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- **Clarifications Received From:** Apt Packaging Ltd, Chaman Lal Setia Exports Ltd, IDBI Bank Ltd, Mawana Sugars Ltd, Minal Industries Ltd, Suraj Industries Ltd, UR Sugar Industries Ltd, and Victoria Mills Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited From:** Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, PVP Ventures Ltd, and Triumph International Finance India Ltd.
- **Objective:** To maintain market transparency and ensure timely dissemination of price-sensitive or material information to investors.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard market surveillance disclosure action.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities observing significant price/volume movements must respond promptly to exchange inquiries and provide clarifications as mandated under SEBI Listing Regulations.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 25 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market integrity and investor protection by highlighting companies experiencing unusual market activity and tracking their compliance in providing clarifications.
