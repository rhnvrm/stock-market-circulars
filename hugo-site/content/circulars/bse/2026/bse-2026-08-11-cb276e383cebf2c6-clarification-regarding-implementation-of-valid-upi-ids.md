## Summary

BSE has issued a clarification regarding the implementation of "@valid" UPI IDs for investor-facing bank accounts (upstream accounts) maintained by Trading Members, following SEBI guidelines and previous communications.

## Key Points

- All Trading Members must ensure that all investor-facing bank accounts (upstream accounts) receiving funds from individual or non-institutional investors are linked with a "@valid" UPI ID.
- Verification of these accounts is enabled through SEBI Check.
- Trading Members must apply for a "@valid" UPI handle via the BSE Portal under OMRS → Membership → UPI Request Handle.
- Members registered with multiple stock exchanges must submit their request through only one Exchange.
- Post application submission, members must coordinate with their respective banks to complete documentation.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/DEPA-II/DEPA-II_SPG/P/CIR/2025/86 dated June 11, 2025, and Exchange Notice No. 20250912-90 dated September 12, 2025.
- Mandatory linking of new investor-facing bank accounts opened after the issuance of this circular with a "@valid" UPI ID.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must apply for a "@valid" UPI handle on the BSE portal and approach their banks for completion.
- Full compliance must be achieved within T+30 days where T is August 07, 2026 (i.e., by September 06, 2026).

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: August 11, 2026 (Reference Date T: August 07, 2026)
- Compliance Deadline: September 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading Members must promptly audit their upstream accounts and complete the necessary portal submissions and bank formalities within the stipulated timeframe to avoid regulatory non-compliance.
