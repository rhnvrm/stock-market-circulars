## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. 714 for Rolling Settlement No. DR-714/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on September 15, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on September 15, 2026.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction scheduled for Wednesday, Sep 16, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is now removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Members should refer to the cut-off rate appearing in the 'Auction offer Entry Screen' and consider corporate actions.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 15, 2026
- Pay-in Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement of short-delivered securities through organized auction sessions, affecting clearing and trading members involved in Rolling Settlement DR-714/2026-27.
