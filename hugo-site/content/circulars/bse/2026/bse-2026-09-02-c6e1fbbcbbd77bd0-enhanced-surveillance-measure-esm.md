## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin effective September 04, 2026. Additionally, certain securities will move to lower stages or move out of the ESM framework, while qualifying securities will shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 04, 2026, on open positions as of September 03, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities moving to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 03, 2026.
- Qualifying securities under ESM will shift from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 04, 2026.
- Details of securities moving out of ESM and consolidated lists are provided in Annexures II and III respectively.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements and movement across ESM stages.
- Price band and settlement segment modifications for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the revised margin requirements and settlement mechanisms for affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 02, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage II / Shifting / Movouts: September 03, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade segment shift: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions for listed securities under the ESM framework, impacting liquidity and trading operations for specific scrips.
