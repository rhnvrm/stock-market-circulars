BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the equity shares of Advance Multitech Ltd (Scrip Code: 526331) will be included under the 'X' group effective September 25, 2026.

- Company Name: Advance Multitech Ltd
- Scrip Code: 526331
- New Group: 'X' group

- Designated Contact: bse.revocation@bseindia.com

Reclassification of equity shares into the 'X' group as per BSE listing compliance and monitoring guidelines.
