## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the revised schedule for client margin reporting and associated penalty dates for the Currency Derivatives segment for the month of August 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers trade dates starting from August 3, 2026, through August 31, 2026.
- Specifies corresponding last dates of margin reporting and penalty dates for each trade date.
- Issued by BSE Clearing Limited (formerly Indian Clearing Corporation Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of standard margin reporting timelines and penalty applicability schedules for the currency derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must adhere to the revised margin reporting deadlines to avoid penalties on respective penalty dates.

## Important Dates

- Trade Dates: 03-08-2026 to 31-08-2026
- Margin Reporting Dates: 10-08-2026 to 07-09-2026
- Penalty Dates: 11-08-2026 to 08-09-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures operational clarity and compliance for clearing members handling currency derivative trades during August 2026.
