## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 684 (Rolling Settlement No. DR-684/2026-27) inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of specified securities in auction. The auction sessions, matching, and reporting timings are scheduled for August 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement Number: DR-684/2026-27.
- Auction Offer Entry Timing: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on August 3, 2026.
- Auction Matching Timing: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on August 3, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and Report Timing: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m. on August 3, 2026.
- Money Statement & Final Reports Timing: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m. on August 3, 2026.
- Auction Pay-in Date: Tuesday, August 4, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching security codes and rates as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 3, 2026
- Pay-in Date: August 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market settlement process affecting participants involved in rolling settlement short deliveries.
