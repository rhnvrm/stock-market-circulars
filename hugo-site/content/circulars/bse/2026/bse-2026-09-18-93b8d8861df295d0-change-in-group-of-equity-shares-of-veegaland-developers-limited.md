## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Veegaland Developers Limited will be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment under B Group, effective October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Veegaland Developers Limited
- Scrip Code: 544923
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: Monday, October 05, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer of equity shares from the Trade for Trade segment to the normal rolling segment allowing standard settlement cycles.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the segment shift and ensure appropriate trading and settlement procedures are followed starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates normal liquidity and trading operations for the company's equity shares under the B Group rolling segment.
