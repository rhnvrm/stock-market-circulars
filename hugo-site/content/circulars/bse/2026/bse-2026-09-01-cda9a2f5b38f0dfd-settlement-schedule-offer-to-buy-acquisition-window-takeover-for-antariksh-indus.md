## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Antariksh Industries Limited. The bidding window is scheduled to be conducted from September 2, 2026, to September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Takeover
- Settlement No.: 2026109
- Bid Start Date: 02/09/2026
- Bid End Date: 16/09/2026
- Obligation Download: 23/09/2026
- Settlement Date: 24/09/2026
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in CDSL or NSDL.
- Members and custodians must use the Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement number 2026109.
- Members/Custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The notice reiterates adherence to previously established guidelines for the acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange mechanisms (Notice No. 20200528-32).

## Compliance Requirements

Participants must ensure that early pay-in securities are earmarked/blocked in clients' accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number, reflecting in BSE/BSECL systems before the prescribed timelines for accepting/confirming bids.

## Important Dates

- Bid Period: 02/09/2026 to 16/09/2026
- Obligation Download Date: 23/09/2026
- Settlement Date: 24/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact for trading members, custodians, and investors participating in the takeover offer for Antariksh Industries Limited, requiring adherence to the specified settlement calendar and early pay-in requirements.
