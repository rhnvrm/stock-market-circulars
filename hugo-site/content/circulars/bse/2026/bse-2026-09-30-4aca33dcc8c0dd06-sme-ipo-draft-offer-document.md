## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the Draft Offer Document filed by Bird Machines Limited on September 29, 2026, in accordance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations. The document has been hosted on the SME website to seek public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Issuer Company: Bird Machines Limited
- Filing Date: September 29, 2026
- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Hosting Period: At least 21 days
- SME Website Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard regulatory disclosure and public comment invitation under ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public are invited to submit any comments on the draft offer document within 21 days from the date of the notice via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 29, 2026
- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Public Comment Deadline: 21 days from September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Informational notice regarding SME IPO process initiation; no direct impact on existing market operations.
