## Summary

SEBI has issued an ex-parte interim order (Order No. WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-SEC-6/32673/2026-27 dated August 19, 2026) regarding manipulative trades during the Call Auction Session (CAS) on SENSEX expiry at BSE on August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Noticee Nos. 1 and 2 are restrained from accessing the securities markets (for Noticee No. 2, the restriction applies to its proprietary trading account only).
- Noticees with open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts are permitted to square off/close out such positions within 3 months from the date of the order or at expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Noticees are allowed to settle pay-in and pay-out obligations for transactions conducted before the close of trading hours on the date of the order.
- Banks are permitted to debit accounts to comply with these directions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of interim restraint directions by SEBI against entities involved in manipulative trades during SENSEX expiry CAS.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the order and ensure strict compliance with the restrictions imposed on the specified noticees.

## Important Dates

- Order Date / Effective Date: August 19, 2026
- Square-off Deadline for Open Positions: Within 3 months from the date of order or contract expiry, whichever is earlier.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures market integrity by penalizing alleged manipulative trading practices during index expiry call auctions.
