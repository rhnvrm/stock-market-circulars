## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 10, 2026. The session will operate from both Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR) to allow trading members to test their trading applications, software, and risk management systems.

## Key Points

- **Date:** Saturday, October 10, 2026.
- **Segment:** Currency Derivatives & Cross Currency Derivatives.
- **Sites:** Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR).
- **New Release:** New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.60 shall be released separately.
- **Regulatory Fulfillment:** Participation helps members fulfill regulatory requirements pursuant to SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 dated November 24, 2020.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine mock trading and software testing session conducted in accordance with existing SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may choose to participate either in the Mock Trading Session or UAT environment to fulfill their regulatory testing requirements. Members using third-party empaneled vendor platforms or in-house developed systems via IML/ETI APIs should test their applications under various market conditions.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** October 10, 2026
- **Log-in Time:** 10:15 am - 11:00 am
- **Continuous Trading (PR):** 11:00 am - 02:00 pm
- **Switchover to DR:** 02:00 pm - 02:45 pm
- **Continuous Trading (DR):** 02:45 pm - 04:45 pm
- **Trade Modification:** Up to 05:00 pm
- **Feedback Deadline:** October 10, 2026, by 5:30 pm

## Impact Assessment

Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or create rights and liabilities. Operational impact is minimal and restricted to testing hours.
