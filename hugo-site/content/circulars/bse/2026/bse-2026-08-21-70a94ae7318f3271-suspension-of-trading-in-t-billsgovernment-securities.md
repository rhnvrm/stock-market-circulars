## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific Treasury Bills (T-Bills) and Government Securities due to their maturity on the redemption date.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 805185
- Particulars: 182TB27826
- ISIN: IN002025Y479
- Trading members are instructed not to deal in the aforesaid T-bills/Government Securities effective from August 24, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational suspension following the maturity of government securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that no transactions are executed in the specified T-bills/Government Securities from the effective date onward.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date / Suspension Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and routine market impact restricted to the maturity and cessation of secondary market trading for the specified 182-day T-Bill (ISIN: IN002025Y479).
