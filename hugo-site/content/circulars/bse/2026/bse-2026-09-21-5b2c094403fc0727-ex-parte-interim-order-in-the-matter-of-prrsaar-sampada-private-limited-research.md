## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding SEBI's ex-parte interim order against Research Analyst Prrsaar Sampada Private Limited.

## Key Points

- SEBI passed an Ex-Parte Interim Order no. WTM/KV/ISD/ISD-TPD2/32723/2026-27 dated September 16, 2026.
- Target entity: Prrsaar Sampada Private Limited - Research Analyst (SEBI Registration No. INH000020323).
- All Research Analysts and investors are requested to take note of the order.

## Regulatory Changes

None specified other than the enforcement of the interim order.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and investors must take note of SEBI's interim order regarding the research analyst.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 16, 2026
- Notice Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct impact is restricted to the named entity, serving as a reminder of compliance standards for research analysts.
