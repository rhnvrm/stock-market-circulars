## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session in the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, September 05, 2026. This session allows trading members using third-party or in-house systems to test applications under exceptional market conditions, software changes related to modifications in the Pre-open Call Auction Session, and new data formats.

## Key Points

- Mock trading scheduled for Saturday, September 05, 2026, in the Equity Derivatives segment.
- Testing supports compliance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 and Exchange Notice No. 20201201-22.
- New version of BOLT Pro TWS 13.50 will be released for mock and live trading starting September 07, 2026.
- Market data via NFCAST stream available in the new format (version 6.2) and MDI / EMDI API version 1.4.5.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modifications in Pre-open Call Auction Session in the Equity Derivatives Segment applicable for live trading from Monday, September 07, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members may participate in the mock trading session or UAT environment to fulfill regulatory testing requirements.
- Members should test trading applications for changes related to Pre-open Call Auction Session modifications.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: 03 Sep 2026
- BOLT Pro TWS 13.50 Download: 04 Sep 2026 from 7:00 PM onwards
- Mock Trading Session: 05 Sep 2026
- Live Implementation Date: 07 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trades resulting from the mock trading session will not attract any margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out obligations, or create rights and liabilities. It serves purely for testing, familiarization, and validation of trading infrastructure.
