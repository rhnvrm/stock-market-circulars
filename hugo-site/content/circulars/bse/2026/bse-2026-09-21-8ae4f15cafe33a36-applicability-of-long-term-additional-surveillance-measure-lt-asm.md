## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability, review, and revision of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework for various securities based on XBRL submissions.

## Key Points

- 100% margins will be applicable effective September 24, 2026, on all open positions as on September 23, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework or being transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups will face lower price bands and group changes effective September 22 and 24, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework, and their price bands will be reinstated to pre-shortlisting levels unless subject to other surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is solely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions by listed companies and does not constitute an adverse action against the entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins on select securities under LT-ASM.
- Transfer of certain securities to T, XT, MT, or TS Groups.
- Revision and reinstatement of price bands for securities entering or exiting the framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (100%) for impacted securities.
- Members should refer to the attachments (Annexures I through III) for the specific lists of affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Band Changes & Move out / Higher Stages: September 22, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the impacted securities will experience increased margin blocking (100%) and potential liquidity constraints due to lower price bands and transfer to special trading groups.
