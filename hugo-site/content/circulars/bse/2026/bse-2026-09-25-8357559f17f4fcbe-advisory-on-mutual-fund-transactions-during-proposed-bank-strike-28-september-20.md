## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has issued an advisory regarding the proposed nationwide bank strike by the United Forum of Bank Unions (UFBU) from 28 September 2026 to 30 September 2026. Banking operations and clearances may be disrupted during this period, impacting mutual fund transaction processing.

## Key Points

- Nationwide bank strike scheduled from 28 September 2026 to 30 September 2026 called by UFBU.
- Clearance and realization of investor cheques may experience delays.
- Transaction processing dates will be determined based on actual fund realization in BSE Clearing Limited's designated bank account.
- Nodal or NACH-based collection mechanisms may also be impacted.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational advisory notice regarding banking strike impacts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must suitably inform investors that mutual fund transaction processing is subject to successful fund realization and banking service availability during the strike period.
- Take necessary precautions and communicate the advisory to investors.

## Important Dates

- 28 September 2026 to 30 September 2026: Proposed nationwide bank strike period.

## Impact Assessment

Mutual fund investors and members may face temporary delays in fund realizations, cheque clearances, and NACH collections, affecting the effective transaction processing dates on the BSE StAR MF platform.
