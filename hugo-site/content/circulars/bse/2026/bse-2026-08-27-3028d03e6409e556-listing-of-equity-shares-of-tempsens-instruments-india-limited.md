## Summary

Effective from Friday, August 28, 2026, the equity shares of Tempsens Instruments (India) Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip Name: TEMPSENS INSTRUMENTS (INDIA) LIMITED
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: TEMPSENS
- Scrip Code: 544886
- ISIN No.: INE1KZI01025
- Total Securities: 83,832,691 Equity Shares of Rs. 4/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 300/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 4/- and premium of Rs. 296/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) for IPOs & Relisted Scrips on August 28, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action executed to mark lock-in periods in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 25, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members can trade in the equity shares of Tempsens Instruments (India) Limited starting August 28, 2026, under the 'B' Group category with a market lot of 1.
