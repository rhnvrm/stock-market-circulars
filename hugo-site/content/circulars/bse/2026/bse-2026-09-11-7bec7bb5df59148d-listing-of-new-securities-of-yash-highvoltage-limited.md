## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade for new equity shares issued by Yash Highvoltage Limited on a preferential basis, effective from September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Yash Highvoltage Limited (Scrip Code: 544310)
- Securities Issued: 12,62,131 equity shares of face value Rs. 5/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 716/- (Issue Price: Rs. 721/-) to non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- Effective Date of Trading: Tuesday, September 15, 2026.
- ISIN: INE00GK01023
- Distinctive Numbers: 28625550 to 29887680
- Lock-in Details: As per Annexure I.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in details and distribution numbers as specified in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 06/08/2026
- Trading Commencement Date: 15/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Yash Highvoltage Limited with new shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
