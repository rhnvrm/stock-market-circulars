## Summary

BSE has announced that the ALTIVA EQUITY LONG-SHORT FUND schemes will be available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and SIP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting October 05, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20260909-28.

## Key Points

- Schemes covered include regular and direct plans with growth, IDCW payout, and IDCW reinvestment options.
- Effective date for ongoing transactions: October 05, 2026.
- ISINs: INF754K30219, INF754K30227, INF754K30235, INF754K30177, INF754K30185, INF754K30193.
- Non-business day provisions: If Monday or Wednesday is a non-business day, redemption and switch-out requests are processed on the next business day.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational platform availability notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Members should note the scheme details, ISINs, and processing rules for redemptions and switch-outs under the SIF schemes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date for Transactions: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to transact in the various plans of ALTIVA EQUITY LONG-SHORT FUND smoothly via the BSE StAR MF platform.
