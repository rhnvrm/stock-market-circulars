## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities under the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) pursuant to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), placing specified securities under Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) effective July 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are subjected to Additional Surveillance Measures effective July 01, 2026.
- Annexure I lists securities newly satisfying the criteria, Annexure II lists securities moving out of the framework, and Annexure III provides the consolidated list.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their original levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is strictly a market surveillance measure and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of additional surveillance measures for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process under the IBC.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the list of securities under the IBC surveillance framework and adhere to the applicable price bands and trading controls.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities under the IBC framework will experience surveillance controls and price band restrictions as outlined in the annexures.
