## Summary

ALPHAGREP MUTUAL FUND SIF has been activated on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective October 12, 2026, pursuant to SEBI's regulatory framework for Specialized Investment Funds (SIF). Additionally, the Eminence Hybrid Long - Short Fund NFO scheme is scheduled for order routing activation on October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Alphagrep Mutual Fund SIF activated on BSE StAR MF Platform effective October 12, 2026.
- Transactions for NRI and Minor clients are accepted under SIF schemes, with investment restrictions for residents of North Korea, Iran, Myanmar, Canada, and the United States of America.
- Eminence Hybrid Long - Short Fund NFO scheme activates for order routing starting October 14, 2026.
- Detailed transaction acceptance processes and regulatory guidelines are provided in Annexures I, II, and III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adoption of SEBI circular SEBI/HO/IMD/IMD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/26 dated February 27, 2025, establishing the regulatory framework for Specialized Investment Funds (SIF).

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure compliance with country exclusions for NRI investors (North Korea, Iran, Myanmar, Canada, and USA).
- Adherence to minimum investment thresholds and transaction execution guidelines for SIF on BSE StAR MF.

## Important Dates

- October 12, 2026: Activation of Alphagrep Mutual Fund SIF on BSE StAR MF Platform.
- October 14, 2026: Activation of Eminence Hybrid Long - Short Fund NFO for order routing.

## Impact Assessment

- Expands investment product offerings on the BSE StAR MF platform by integrating Specialized Investment Funds and facilitating automated order routing for eligible participants.
