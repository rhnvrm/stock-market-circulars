## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in three specific T-bills and Government Securities due to their maturity effective from August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified T-bills and Government Securities with effect from August 11, 2026.
- Affected securities include 182-day, 364-day, and 91-day T-bills maturing on the redemption date.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no further trading occurs in the specified maturing T-bills/Government Securities starting August 11, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational measure reflecting the expiry and redemption of specific sovereign debt instruments.
