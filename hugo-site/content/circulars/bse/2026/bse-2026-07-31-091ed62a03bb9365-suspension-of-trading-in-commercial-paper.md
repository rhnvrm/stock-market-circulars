## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of their commercial papers. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of these companies starting from the respective ex-dates and record dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260731-43, dated July 31, 2026.
- Department: Listing Operations, Debt Segment.
- Covers multiple companies including Tata Steel Limited, Krazybee Services Ltd, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, and others.
- Record dates and ex-dates span across mid-August 2026 (e.g., August 12 to August 20, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in specific commercial papers is suspended in alignment with redemption record dates to prevent unauthorized transactions post-record date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must refrain from dealing in the commercial papers of the listed companies as per the specified schedule.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 Jul 2026
- Record/Ex-Dates: 12-08-2026 to 20-08-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Direct operational restriction for trading members concerning the specified commercial paper ISINs during the redemption window.
