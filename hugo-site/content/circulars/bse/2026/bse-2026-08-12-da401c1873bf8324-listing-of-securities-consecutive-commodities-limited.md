## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has notified trading members that 48,04,50,000 new equity shares of Re. 1/- each of **Consecutive Commodities Limited** (Scrip Code: 539091) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from **Thursday, August 13, 2026**. These shares were issued on a rights basis and will rank *pari-passu* with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** Consecutive Commodities Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 539091
- **ISIN:** INE187R01029
- **Total Shares:** 48,04,50,000 equity shares of face value Re. 1/- each
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 160150001 to 640600000
- **Issue Type:** Rights Basis
- **Date of Allotment:** August 5, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Ranking:** Pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced by this circular. It is a standard operational listing notice under existing listing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

No general actions are required from trading members or investors, except to note the listing of these securities. Consecutive Commodities Limited and its Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA) must ensure compliance with all necessary listing and depository guidelines for the credit and trading of these shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** Wednesday, August 5, 2026
- **Notice Date:** Wednesday, August 12, 2026
- **Trading Commencement Date:** Thursday, August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine listing of new equity shares arising from a rights issue, impacting only Consecutive Commodities Limited and its stakeholders.
- **Liquidity Impact:** Positive for Consecutive Commodities Limited as the outstanding tradable shares increase by 48,04,50,000 shares.
- **Company Impact:** The rights shares rank pari-passu with existing shares, resulting in a dilutive effect on earnings per share but a strengthened equity capital base.
