## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 26 scrips across various categories, effective from September 08, 2026. Trading members must take note of these changes to comply with exchange surveillance guidelines.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 26 scrips.
- Effective date: September 08, 2026.
- Revised limits range from 2% to 20% across different companies.
- Applicable to the Equity segment under the Department of Surveillance (DOSS).

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands for specific securities have been modified from their existing levels to manage market volatility and prevent excessive price movements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals and risk management systems to reflect the revised price bands for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 07, 2026
- Effective Date: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and traders dealing in the listed scrips will be subject to the new upper and lower circuit limits, which may impact liquidity and intraday trading strategies for those specific stocks.
