## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, September 16, 2026, the equity shares of Glass Wall Systems (India) Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip ID: GLASSWALL
- Scrip Code: 544913
- ISIN No.: INE644Q01039
- Number of Securities: 8,79,35,253 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 182/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 2/- and premium of Rs. 180/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 11, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities of the company will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- The scrip will be a part of Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Wednesday, September 16, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed for lock-in period marking in the depository's records for demat shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 11, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Glass Wall Systems (India) Limited shares on the BSE platform, expanding investment opportunities in the 'B' Group segment.
