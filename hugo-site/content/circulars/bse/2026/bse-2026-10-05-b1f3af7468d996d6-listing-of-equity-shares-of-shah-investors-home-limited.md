## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of Shah Investor's Home Limited effective from Tuesday, October 6, 2026. The shares will be traded in the 'T' Group of Securities under the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Shah Investor's Home Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544963
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** SHAHINVEST
- **ISIN:** INE029N01014
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 167/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 157/-)
- **No. of Securities:** 21,153,200 Equity Shares
- **Date of Allotment:** October 1, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

In terms of SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, the scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on listing day (October 6, 2026).
- Physical share certificates have been enfaced with non-transferability, and demat lock-in has been executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 1, 2026
- **Listing and Trading Effective Date:** October 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members can trade in the shares of Shah Investor's Home Limited starting October 6, 2026, subject to trade-for-trade settlement restrictions for the initial 10 trading days.
