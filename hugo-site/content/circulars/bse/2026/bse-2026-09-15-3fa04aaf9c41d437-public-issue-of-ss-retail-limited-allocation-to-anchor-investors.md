## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of SS Retail Limited, 34,52,829 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 424/- per equity share as of September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SS Retail Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investors Portion)
- Shares Subscribed: 34,52,829 equity shares
- Issue Price / Allocation Price: Rs. 424/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: September 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

 Trading members are to take note of the anchor investor allocation details for their records and reference.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 15, 2026
- Announcement Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Signals investor demand and sets the stage for the progression of SS Retail Limited's public issue on the exchange.
