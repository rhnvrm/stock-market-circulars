## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-715/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: Wednesday, Sep 16, 2026, from 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
- Auction Matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement and report generation: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Auction Pay-in: Thursday, Sep 17, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and Custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Offer Entry Date: Sep 16, 2026
- Pay-in Date: Sep 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification impacting trading members participating in settlement DR-715/2026-27.
