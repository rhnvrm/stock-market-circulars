## Summary

BSE Ltd. has announced the admission of new trading members and additional segment memberships effective August 14, 2026, as per the Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations of the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Truenine Shares And Stock Broking Private Limited has been admitted for New Membership in the Cash segment (Member No. 6996).
- R.L.P Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 3024) has been granted an Additional Segment Membership for Equity Derivatives.
- Integrated Master Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 780) has been granted an Additional Segment Membership for Commodity Derivatives.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members requiring clarifications can contact membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Admission Date: 14 August 2026
- Notice Date: 17 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a routine operational circular with no direct impact on general market participants or listed securities.
