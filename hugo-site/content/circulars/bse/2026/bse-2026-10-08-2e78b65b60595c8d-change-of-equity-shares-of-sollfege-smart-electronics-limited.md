## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Sollfege Smart Electronics Limited (Scrip Code: 544977) from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sollfege Smart Electronics Limited
- Scrip Code: 544977
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20261007-27 dated October 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Shift of trading segment for the company's equity shares from Trade for Trade to Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the change in segment and trading arrangements for the scrip.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for the equity shares of Sollfege Smart Electronics Limited, improving liquidity and trading convenience.
