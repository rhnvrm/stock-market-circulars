## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled on July 31, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated July 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security under auction: 6.94% GS 2036 (Symbol: 694GS36J26, Issue Size: Rs. 34,000 Cr).
- Bidding platform: NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.
- Bid collection start date: July 28, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards.
- Bid collection end date for members: July 31, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- Bid collection end date for direct investors: July 30, 2026, till 5:00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with RBI press release and Exchange circular no. 20180423-42 dated April 23, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: July 31, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates participation of eligible investors in government securities through the non-competitive bidding facility.
