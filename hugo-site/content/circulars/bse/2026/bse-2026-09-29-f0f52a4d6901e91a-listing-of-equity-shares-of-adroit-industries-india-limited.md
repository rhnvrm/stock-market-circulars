## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that the equity shares of Adroit Industries (India) Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in due course. The specific date of listing and trading details will be announced through a separate notice.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Adroit Industries (India) Limited
- Scrip Code: 544950
- Symbol: ADROITIND
- ISIN No: INE505M01015
- Notice Number: 20260929-26

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory policy changes; standard admission to exchange trading under DCS-Listing department.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure systems are updated with the new scrip code, symbol, and ISIN ahead of the trading commencement date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Listing & Trading Date: To be announced via a separate notice

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading, price discovery, and liquidity for the equity shares of Adroit Industries (India) Limited upon official commencement of dealings.
