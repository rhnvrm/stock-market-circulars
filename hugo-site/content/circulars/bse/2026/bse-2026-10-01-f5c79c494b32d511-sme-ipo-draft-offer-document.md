## Summary

Grandmaa International Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with BSE for its upcoming SME Initial Public Offering (IPO). In compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations, the document has been made available on the stock exchange website to seek public comments for a minimum period of 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Grandmaa International Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Segment: SME
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the notice date
- Draft Offer Document Hosting Link: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard ICDR Regulation disclosures and review process for SME IPO draft offer documents.

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments or feedback on the draft offer document can be submitted via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days from the date of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from notice date (approx. October 22, 2026)

Provides market participants and investors an opportunity to review proposed SME public issues and submit observations or comments to regulatory bodies.
