## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Non-Competitive Bidding for the Auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for September 08, 2026, following the RBI press release dated September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Bidding takes place through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.
- Bid collection start date for direct investors and members began on September 07, 2026.
- Member bid collection end date is September 08, 2026, till 8:00 AM.
- Settlement date is scheduled for September 09, 2026.
- Includes multiple SDL securities such as AP, GA, GJ, JK, MH, ML, and PB SDLs.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Conducted under existing non-competitive bidding framework for State Government Securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members participating in the non-competitive bidding must ensure bids are submitted within the prescribed timelines through the iBBS web-based system.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: September 07, 2026, 10:00 AM
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): September 07, 2026, 5:00 PM
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): September 08, 2026, 8:00 AM
- Auction Date: September 08, 2026
- Settlement Date: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine primary market operations for State Government Securities with no direct impact on equity market segments.
