## Summary

Adani Airport Holdings Limited has announced the part redemption of its debentures, resulting in a reduced face value of Rs. 58,724/- per debenture effective from August 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Adani Airport Holdings Limited
- Scrip Code: 976065
- ISIN: INE0GCN07047
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 58,724/- per debenture
- Effective Date: August 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the reduced face value for trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 07, 2026
- Notice Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted using the updated face value of the debentures.
