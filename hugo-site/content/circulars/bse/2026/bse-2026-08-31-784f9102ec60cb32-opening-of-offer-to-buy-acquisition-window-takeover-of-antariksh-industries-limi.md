## Summary

Mr. Alpitkumar Pravinchandra Gor together with Riddhi Infocom Solutions LLP (PAC) has made an open offer to public shareholders of Antariksh Industries Limited to acquire up to 6,31,785 fully paid equity shares (representing 26.00% of the emerging voting share capital) at a price of Rs. 86/- per share payable in cash.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: Mr. Alpitkumar Pravinchandra Gor
- Person Acting in Concert (PAC): Riddhi Infocom Solutions LLP
- Target Company: Antariksh Industries Limited
- Offer Price: Rs. 86/- per share
- Offer Size: Up to 6,31,785 equity shares (26.00% of Emerging Voting Share Capital)
- Offer Period: Wednesday, September 02, 2026 to Wednesday, September 16, 2026 (Excluding SEBI Holiday on Monday, September 14, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

None. The offer is conducted pursuant to SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 (Regulations 3(1) and 4).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians are required to participate in accordance with the revised guidelines of SEBI circular no. CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 dated April 13, 2015, and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131 dated December 09, 2016, through the acquisition window.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 31, 2026
- Offer Opening Date: September 02, 2026
- Offer Closing Date: September 16, 2026 (Excluding SEBI Holiday on September 14, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public shareholders of Antariksh Industries Limited to tender their shares through the stock exchange acquisition window during the specified open offer period.
