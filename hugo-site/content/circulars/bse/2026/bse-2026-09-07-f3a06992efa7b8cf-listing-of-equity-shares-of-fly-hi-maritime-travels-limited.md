## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, September 8, 2026, the equity shares of Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities in the SME segment.

## Key Points

- Scrip Name: FLY-HI MARITIME TRAVELS LIMITED
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: FLYHI
- Scrip Code: 544904
- ISIN No: INE2J7801015
- Market Lot: 1200 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 102/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/- and premium of Rs. 97/-)
- Total Securities: 14,180,400 Equity Shares

## Regulatory Changes

- The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form.
- Corporate action executed to have the lock-in period marked in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 4, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with initial trade-for-trade monitoring.
