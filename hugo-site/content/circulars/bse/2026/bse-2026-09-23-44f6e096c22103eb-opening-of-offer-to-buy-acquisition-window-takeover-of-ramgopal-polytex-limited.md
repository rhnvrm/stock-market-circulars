## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the offer to buy (acquisition window) for an open offer made to the public shareholders of Ramgopal Polytex Limited by the acquirers, Mr. Pravin Kumar Shishodiya and Mr. Punit Shishodiya.

## Key Points

- **Acquirers:** Mr. Pravin Kumar Shishodiya (Acquirer 1) and Mr. Punit Shishodiya (Acquirer 2).
- **Target Company:** Ramgopal Polytex Limited (RPL).
- **Offer Details:** Acquisition of up to 37,70,000 equity shares representing 26% of the total equity share capital of the target company.
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 17.10 per equity share payable in cash.
- **Offer Period:** From September 25, 2026 to October 09, 2026 (excluding SEBI Holiday on Friday, October 02, 2026).
- **Regulatory Framework:** Pursuant to Regulation 3(1) and Regulation 4 of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Conducted under existing SEBI guidelines for acquisition of shares through stock exchange pursuant to tender offers.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must participate according to the revised guidelines of SEBI circulars and exchange notices governing the acquisition window mechanism for tender offers under takeovers.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 23, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** September 25, 2026
- **SEBI Holiday:** October 02, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** October 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public shareholders of Ramgopal Polytex Limited an exit or participation opportunity via the stock exchange acquisition window mechanism under the SEBI Takeover Regulations.
