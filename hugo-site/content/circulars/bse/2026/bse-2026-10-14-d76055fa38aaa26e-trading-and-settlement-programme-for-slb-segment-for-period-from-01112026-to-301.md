## Summary

BSE has announced the trading and settlement schedule for the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) segment covering the period from November 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026. The circular details settlement numbers, trading dates, entry, confirmation, and pay-in/pay-out timelines for market participants.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement numbers from SL-147/26-27 to SL-166/26-27 for November 2026.
- Specifies corresponding SLB trading dates, entry dates, and confirmation dates.
- Details pay-in and pay-out dates and specific instruction submission deadlines.
- Highlights special pay-in/pay-out timings for settlements SL-151/26-27 and SL-152/26-27 on November 9, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine periodic operational calendar issued by Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and clearing participants must submit pay-in instructions to depositories and banks latest by 07:50 a.m. (or specific times such as 10:05 a.m. for respective batches) on settlement pay-in days. Pay-in takes place at 08:00 a.m. or scheduled timings.

## Important Dates

- Period Start: 01-11-2026
- Period End: 30-11-2026
- First Settlement Pay-in/Pay-out: 03-11-2026 (SL-147/26-27)
- Final Settlement Pay-in/Pay-out: 01-12-2026 (SL-166/26-27)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures seamless operational execution, clearing, and settlement workflows for participants engaged in securities lending and borrowing activities on the BSE platform during November 2026.
