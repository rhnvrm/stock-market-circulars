## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of securities listed in the attached Annexure into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from October 13, 2026. These measures are designed to enhance market integrity and safeguard investor interests.

## Key Points

- Securities move to higher stages of GSM (Stages I, II, III, and IV) based on surveillance parameters.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, including 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement for affected securities will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Trading frequency for higher stages (III and IV) is restricted to once a week (every Monday or first trading day of the week).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance actions under the GSM framework as per historical exchange notices dated February 23, 2017, March 03, 2017, July 20, 2018, November 29, 2019, and November 17, 2023.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules without netting off, and collect applicable margins and ASD (50% or 100% as applicable).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity in the affected securities may be significantly restricted due to trade-for-trade settlement, mandatory ASD requirements, and restricted trading frequencies in higher stages.
