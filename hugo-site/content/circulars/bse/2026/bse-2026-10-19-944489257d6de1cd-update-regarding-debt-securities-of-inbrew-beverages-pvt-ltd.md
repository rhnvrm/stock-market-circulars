## Summary

In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20251222-21 dated December 22, 2025, BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in the debt securities of Inbrew Beverages Pvt. Ltd. on account of debt restructuring.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date**: Suspension of trading takes effect from Friday, October 23, 2026.
- **Affected Securities**:
  - Scrip Code: 974276 | ISIN: INE696R07059
  - Scrip Code: 976716 | ISIN: INE696R07042
- **Reason**: Debt restructuring.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the suspension and refrain from trading in the specified scrip codes and ISINs effective from the stated date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: October 19, 2026
- **Suspension Effective Date**: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the debt securities of Inbrew Beverages Pvt. Ltd. will be halted, impacting liquidity and market operations for holders of these specific instruments during the restructuring period.
