## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Composite Scheme of Arrangement (Demerger) approved by the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT), Division Bench, Chennai. The scheme involves the demerger of the Commerce Education Business of Veranda Learning Solutions Limited (Demerged Company) into J.K. Shah Commerce Education Ltd (Resulting Company).

## Key Points

- Demerged Company: Veranda Learning Solutions Limited (Scrip Code: 543514).
- Resulting Company: J.K. Shah Commerce Education Ltd.
- Share Entitlement: 1 (One) Equity Share of Face Value of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up in J.K. Shah Commerce Education Ltd for every 1 (One) Equity Share of Face Value of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up held in Veranda Learning Solutions Limited.
- Trading in equity shares of Veranda Learning Solutions Limited transferred from 'B' Group to 'T' Group (Trade-for-Trade segment) effective October 6, 2026.
- Subject to compliance, equity shares of the Resulting Company will be listed on BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

Execution of NCLT-approved composite scheme of arrangement for demerger and subsequent share allotment by the resulting company.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the record date, share entitlement ratio, transfer to 'T' Group (trade-for-trade settlement), and participation in Call Auction in Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 6, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Record Date & Ex-Entitlement Date: October 6, 2026
- Transfer to 'T' Group & SPOS: October 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts shareholders of Veranda Learning Solutions Limited through demerger entitlement of shares in J.K. Shah Commerce Education Ltd and imposes trade-for-trade surveillance restrictions effective from the record date.
