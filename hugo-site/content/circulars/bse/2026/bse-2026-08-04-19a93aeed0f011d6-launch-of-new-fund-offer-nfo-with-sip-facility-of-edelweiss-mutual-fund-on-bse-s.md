## Summary

Edelweiss Mutual Fund is launching the Edelweiss Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund NFO along with SIP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 05, 2026, to August 19, 2026, with tentative allotment scheduled for August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Offering multiple regular and direct plans under Edelweiss Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).
- Subscription period: August 05, 2026, to August 19, 2026.
- Tentative allotment date: August 26, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 24, 2026.
- Lumpsum purchases extended till midnight on August 19, 2026; SIP/XSIP must be authenticated by 3:00 PM on August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Clear funds for cheque, OTM, and switch transactions must meet specified deadlines to qualify for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 05, 2026
- NFO Close Date: August 19, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline / Clear Funds Availability: August 24, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscriptions and SIP registrations for investors participating in the Edelweiss Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund NFO through the BSE StAR MF platform.
