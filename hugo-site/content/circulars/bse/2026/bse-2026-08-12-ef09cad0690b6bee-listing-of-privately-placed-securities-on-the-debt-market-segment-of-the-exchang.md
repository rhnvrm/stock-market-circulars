## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed Commercial Papers (CPs) issued by five companies on the BSE Debt Segment, effective August 12, 2026. The issuing companies include Infina Finance Private Limited, Jio Credit Limited, 360 ONE WAM LIMITED, Godrej Finance Limited, and Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited.

## Key Points

- **Trading Commencement:** The new commercial papers are admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment starting August 12, 2026.
- **Issuing Entities & Securities:**
  - **Infina Finance Private Limited:** 500 units of Commercial Paper (Detail Name: IFPL-18-06-27-CP, ISIN: INE879F14NP2, Maturity: June 18, 2027).
  - **Jio Credit Limited:** 19,000 units of Commercial Paper (Detail Name: JCL-9-11-26-CP, ISIN: INE282H14154, Maturity: November 9, 2026).
  - **360 ONE WAM LIMITED:** 5,000 units of Commercial Paper (Detail Name: OWL-10-11-26-CP, ISIN: INE466L14GF1, Maturity: November 10, 2026).
  - **Godrej Finance Limited:** 500 units of Commercial Paper (Detail Name: GFL-6-11-26-CP, ISIN: INE02KN14788, Maturity: November 6, 2026).
  - **Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited:** 8,000 units of Commercial Paper (Detail Name: KMIL-18-8-26-CP, ISIN: INE975F14D16, Maturity: August 18, 2026).
- **Trading Unit & Format:** Trading will take place strictly in dematerialized form with a standard denomination/lot size of Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof.
- **Tick Size:** The tick size for trading these debt securities is 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes are introduced. This is a routine listing notice for debt market segment securities under existing BSE debt listing frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Dematerialization:** Issuers and trading members must ensure that the securities are traded only in dematerialized form under the specified ISINs.
- **Trading Lot Standards:** Transactions must adhere to the standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof, with a tick size of 1 paise.
- **Contact Info:** Trading members requiring clarifications can contact the BSE Debt Department at 22728352 / 8597 / 8995 / 5753 / 8915.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** August 11, 2026 (for all five issuances).
- **Trading Effective Date:** August 12, 2026.
- **Redemption Dates (Maturities):**
  - Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited: August 18, 2026
  - Godrej Finance Limited: November 6, 2026
  - Jio Credit Limited: November 9, 2026
  - 360 ONE WAM LIMITED: November 10, 2026
  - Infina Finance Private Limited: June 18, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine listing of privately placed commercial papers, which expands the available options in the secondary debt market but does not alter the regulatory landscape.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Trading members can trade these securities on the BSE Debt Segment in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs, ensuring standard settlement practices for commercial papers.
