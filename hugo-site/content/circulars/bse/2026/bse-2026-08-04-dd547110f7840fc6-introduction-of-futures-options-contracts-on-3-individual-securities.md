## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of futures and options (F&O) contracts on three individual securities—Ather Energy Ltd, Bank of Maharashtra, and Sagility Ltd—effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Futures and options contracts will be available for trading in the Equity Derivatives segment starting August 28, 2026.
- Covered securities include Ather Energy Ltd (Scrip Code: 544397), Bank of Maharashtra (Scrip Code: 532525), and Sagility Ltd (Scrip Code: 544282).
- Contract details will be published in the derivatives contract master file generated at the end of the trading day on August 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of new securities under the equity derivatives segment as per exchange norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must verify contract details from the derivatives contract master file available on the extranet and BSE website prior to trading.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 28, 2026
- **Contract Master File Availability:** August 27, 2026 (End of Day)

## Impact Assessment

- Expands the derivative product suite on BSE, allowing market participants to trade F&O on Ather Energy, Bank of Maharashtra, and Sagility.
