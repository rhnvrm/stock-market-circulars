## Summary

BSE issued a notice informing members that DSP Mutual Fund will offer the DSP Nifty 10 Yr Benchmark G-Sec ETF NFO from August 10, 2026, to August 12, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: DSP NIFTY 10 YR BENCHMARK G-SEC ETF and DSP NIFTY 10 YR BENCHMARK G-SEC ETF - DIRECT PLAN.
- NFO Period: August 10, 2026, to August 12, 2026 (extended till midnight on August 12, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 14, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 13, 2026, to be eligible for allotment.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted during the NFO period subject to timely fund receipt.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: August 10, 2026
- NFO End Date: August 12, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: August 13, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors access to a new G-Sec ETF scheme through the BSE StAR MF platform with standard operational guidelines.
