## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework to certain securities, detailing inclusions, staging changes, and margin requirements effective from July 29 and July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy the criteria for inclusion and will attract a minimum 100% margin effective July 30, 2026, on open positions as of July 29, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but are moved to lower Stage I effective July 29, 2026.
- Qualifying securities under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective July 30, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective July 29, 2026.
- The shortlisting is strictly on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) stages and trading restrictions for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must maintain the required 100% margins and adhere to the Trade-for-Trade segment rules for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage Changes / Price Bands: July 29, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade Settlement: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement for shortlisted securities may impact short-term liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
