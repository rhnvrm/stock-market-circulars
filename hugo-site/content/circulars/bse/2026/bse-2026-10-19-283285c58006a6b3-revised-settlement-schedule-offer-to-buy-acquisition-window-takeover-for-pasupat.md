## Summary

BSE has issued a revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window - Takeover) in respect of Pasupati Fincap Limited (Settlement No. 2026118), originally conducted from October 1, 2026, to October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised settlement schedule for the takeover offer of Pasupati Fincap Limited (Settlement No. 2026118).
- Bidding period was conducted from 01/10/2026 to 15/10/2026.
- Revised Obligation Download date: October 21, 2026.
- Revised Settlement Date: October 22, 2026.
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type '(Takeover)' and Settlement No. 2026118.
- Client-wise Stamp Duty charges are detailed in the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension and revision of settlement timelines for the open offer / takeover acquisition window under stock exchange mechanism guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must use Market Type '(Takeover)' and Settlement No. 2026118 in the depository system.
- Demat shares must be delivered through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in both depositories.
- Members and custodians are required to adhere to Notice No. 20200528-32 dated May 28, 2020, regarding revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange.
- Verify client-wise Stamp Duty charges via MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: 01/10/2026
- Bid End Date: 15/10/2026
- Revised Obligation Download: 21/10/2026
- Revised Settlement Date: 22/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth and orderly completion of share settlement and fund pay-outs for the takeover acquisition window of Pasupati Fincap Limited, providing clear operational timelines for members and custodians.
