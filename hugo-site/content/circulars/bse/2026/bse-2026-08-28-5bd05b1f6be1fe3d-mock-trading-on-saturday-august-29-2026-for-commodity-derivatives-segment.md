## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for its Commodity Derivatives segment scheduled for Saturday, August 29, 2026. Trading members using third-party platforms, in-house systems, or ETI APIs are encouraged to test their applications under normal and exceptional market conditions. Additionally, the new BOLT Pro TWS version 13.35 will be released.

## Key Points

- Scheduled date: Saturday, August 29, 2026.
- Segment: Commodity Derivatives.
- Focus areas: Testing third-party platforms, in-house systems, ETI APIs, risk-reduction mode, and trading halts.
- Release of New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.35.
- Session 1 timings: Log-in (08:15 AM - 09:00 AM), Continuous Trading (09:00 AM - 12:00 PM), Trade Modification (12:00 PM - 12:10 PM).
- Session 2 timings: Log-in (12:45 PM), Continuous Trading (01:15 PM - 02:15 PM), Trade Modification (02:15 PM - 02:25 PM).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to actively participate in the mock trading session and submit their feedback by 12:00 PM on the day of the session.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 29, 2026
- Feedback Submission Deadline: August 29, 2026, by 12:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Trades resulting from the mock session are for testing purposes only, do not attract margin or pay-in/pay-out obligations, and do not create actual rights or liabilities.
