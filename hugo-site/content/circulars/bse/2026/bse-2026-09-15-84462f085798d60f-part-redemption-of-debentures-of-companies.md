## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that certain companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value starting from the specified effective dates.

## Key Points

- **Kerala Infrastructure Investment Fund Board (KIIFB):** Scrip Code 974953, ISIN INE658F08037, Record Date 17/09/2026, Ex-Date 17/09/2026, Reduced Face Value Rs. 75,000/- per debenture.
- **Hampi Expressways Private Limited:** Scrip Code 975019, ISIN INE03ST08010, Record Date 18/09/2026, Ex-Date 18/09/2026, Reduced Face Value Rs. 97,750/- per debenture.
- **Namdev Finvest Limited:** Scrip Code 975808, ISIN INE0IX207171, Record Date 20/09/2026, Ex-Date 18/09/2026, Reduced Face Value Rs. 25,000.03 per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for proper settlement of trades in the mentioned debentures.

## Important Dates

- **Record Dates:** 17/09/2026, 18/09/2026, and 20/09/2026
- **Ex-Dates:** 17/09/2026 and 18/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to trading members and investors dealing in the specified NCDs of KIIFB, Hampi Expressways, and Namdev Finvest, ensuring transactions reflect the reduced face values and applicable redemptions.
