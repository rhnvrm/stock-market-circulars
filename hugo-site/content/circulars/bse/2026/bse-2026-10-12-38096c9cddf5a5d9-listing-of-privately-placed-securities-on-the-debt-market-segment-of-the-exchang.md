## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by companies including J.K. Cement Ltd, Tata Steel Limited, and Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, effective October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listing of Commercial Papers for J.K. Cement Ltd (Scrip Code: 732725, ISIN: INE823G14BO6, Quantity: 2,000, Issue Price: Rs. 493,610.00, Interest Rate: 6.30% p.a.).
- Listing of Commercial Papers for Tata Steel Limited (Scrip Codes: 732728, 732729, ISINs: INE081A14HR4, INE081A14HQ6, Quantities: 26,000 and 24,000, Interest Rates: 6.40% and 6.20% p.a.).
- Listing of Commercial Papers for Bajaj Financial Securities Limited (Scrip Codes: 732730, 732731, ISINs: INE01C314HU8, INE01C314HY0, Quantities: 5,000 and 4,500, Interest Rate: 7.08% p.a.).
- All securities are traded in dematerialised form in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine listing under the debt market segment following private placement issuance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must adhere to dematerialised trading formats and standard market denominations.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 09/10/2026
- Effective Date / Notice Date: 12/10/2026
- Redemption / Maturity Dates: Ranging from October 2026 to December 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational listing providing liquidity and secondary market trading for commercial paper issues.
