## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled on October 09, 2026, following the RBI press release dated October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities available for bidding: 7.06% GS 2041 (Symbol: 706GS41O26) and 7.43% GS 2076 (Symbol: 743GS76O26).
- Bidding conducted via the NCB-GSec module on the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.
- Minimum subscription and multiples of Rs. 10,000 apply.
- Auction Date: October 09, 2026; Settlement Date: October 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine auction participation notice under established guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and direct investors must submit bids within the specified operational windows and adhere to minimum subscription criteria.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: October 06, 2026, at 10:00 am
- Direct Investor Bid Collection End Date: October 08, 2026, at 5:00 pm
- Members Bid Collection End Date: October 09, 2026, at 8:00 am
- Auction Date: October 09, 2026
- Settlement Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables orderly non-competitive participation in G-sec auctions for eligible market participants.
