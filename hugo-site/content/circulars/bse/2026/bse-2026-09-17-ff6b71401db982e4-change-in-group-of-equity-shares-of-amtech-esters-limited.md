## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Amtech Esters Limited are being shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Amtech Esters Limited
- Scrip Code: 544920
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of equity shares trading category following exchange continuity notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to take note of the segment change for client trades and settlements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 17, 2026
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Improved liquidity and normal rolling settlement procedures for investors trading in the equity shares of Amtech Esters Limited starting October 01, 2026.
