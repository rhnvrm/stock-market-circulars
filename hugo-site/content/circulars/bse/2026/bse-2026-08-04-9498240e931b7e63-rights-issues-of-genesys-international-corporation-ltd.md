## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that Genesys International Corporation Ltd. has fixed a record date for the purpose of a rights issue of equity shares. Transactions in the equity shares of the company shall take place on an ex-rights basis starting from the specified date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Genesys International Corporation Ltd. (Scrip Code: 506109)
- Record Date: 06/08/2026
- Purpose: Issue of 3 (three) Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 45/- per share on a rights basis for every 5 (five) equity shares held.
- Ex-Rights Basis From Date & Settlement No.: 06/08/2026 (DR-688/2026-2027)

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the record date and ex-rights trading schedule for Genesys International Corporation Ltd.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 06/08/2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: 06/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders as of the record date are eligible for the rights issue entitlement under the specified ratio and pricing.
