## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading in these debentures/bonds will be conducted with reduced face value effective from the specified record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **Kodad Khammam Road Limited**: Record/Ex-date 16/10/2026 for scrips 977601 (reduced face value Rs. 94,424) and 977602 (reduced face value Rs. 71,558).
- **Keystone Realtors Limited**: Record/Ex-date 21/10/2026 for scrip 977174 (reduced face value Rs. 55,223.88).
- **Varthana Finance Private Limited**: Record/Ex-date 22/10/2026 for scrip 976219 (reduced face value Rs. 33,333).
- **Sammaan Capital Limited**: Record/Ex-date 23/10/2026 for multiple NCD scrips (938984, 938986, 938988, 938990, 938992, 938994, 938996) with corresponding face value reductions.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework change; routine corporate action processing for debt segment securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and depository participants must note the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- **16/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Kodad Khammam Road Limited.
- **21/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Keystone Realtors Limited.
- **22/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Varthana Finance Private Limited.
- **23/10/2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for Sammaan Capital Limited.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures accurate market pricing and settlement for debentures undergoing capital reduction via part redemption.
