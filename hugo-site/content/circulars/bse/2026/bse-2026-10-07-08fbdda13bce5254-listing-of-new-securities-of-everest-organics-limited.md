## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 2,63,157 new equity shares of Everest Organics Limited (Scrip Code: 524790) issued to promoters on a preferential basis upon warrant conversion.

## Key Points

- **Company:** Everest Organics Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 524790
- **ISIN:** INE334C01029
- **Security Details:** 2,63,157 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 142/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 152/-).
- **Allotment Date:** July 11, 2026
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 9710527 to 9973683
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- **Lock-in Details:** Locked-in up to April 15, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with lock-in restrictions and trading permissions as specified by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** July 11, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry:** April 15, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and preferential allotment processing with minimal market impact.
