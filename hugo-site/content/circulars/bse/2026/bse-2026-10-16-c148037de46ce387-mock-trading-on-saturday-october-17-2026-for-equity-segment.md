## Summary

The BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Equity segment on Saturday, October 17, 2026. Trading members utilizing third-party or proprietary trading platforms are provided an opportunity to test their applications and various system functionalities, including exceptional market conditions, call auction sessions, risk-reduction modes, trading halts, and block deals.

## Key Points

- **Date of Mock Session:** Saturday, October 17, 2026.
- **Applicable Segment:** Equity segment.
- **Tested Functionalities:** Call auction sessions, risk-reduction mode, trading halts, block deals, and specialized pre-open/closing sessions.
- **BOLT TWS Release:** No new BOLT TWS release is associated with this session.
- **Feedback Collection:** Members are requested to submit feedback by 02:00 PM on the day of the mock session via provided contact channels.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational testing notice issued by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Participation is voluntary but encouraged. Members are requested to test their systems and provide feedback to the BSE helpdesk or tech support team by 02:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 16, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date:** October 17, 2026
- **Feedback Deadline:** October 17, 2026 (02:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Enables brokers and trading members to validate their trading infrastructure, risk management systems, and third-party applications against simulated market conditions with zero direct operational risk to live trading environments.
