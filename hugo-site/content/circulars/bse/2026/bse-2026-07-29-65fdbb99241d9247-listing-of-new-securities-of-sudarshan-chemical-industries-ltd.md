## Summary

Trading members are informed that 9,80,000 new equity shares of Sudarshan Chemical Industries Ltd. (Scrip Code - 506655) issued to promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion are listed and permitted to trade effective from July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sudarshan Chemical Industries Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 506655
- Security Details: 9,80,000 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1017.75/- (Issue Price: Rs. 1019.75/-).
- Purpose: Conversion of warrants issued to promoters on a preferential basis.
- Effective Date: July 30, 2026.
- ISIN: INE659A01023
- Lock-in Details: 9,80,000 shares locked-in up to January 30, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and distribution numbers as specified.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 10-06-2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: 30-07-2026
- Lock-in Upto: 30-01-2028

## Impact Assessment

Inclusion of newly issued shares expands the paid-up equity capital base of the company with standard lock-in restrictions.
