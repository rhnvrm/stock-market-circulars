## Summary

BSE has announced the reopening of the NIPPON INDIA INCOME PLUS ARBITRAGE OMNI FUND OF FUND for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and related facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 4, 2026, following previous notices.

## Key Points

- Schemes reopen for ongoing transactions starting September 4, 2026.
- Facilities available include subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Covers multiple ISINs across Regular and Direct plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants should note the ISIN details for order placement on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume transactions, systematic investments, and withdrawals for the specified Nippon India debt fund of fund schemes on the BSE platform.
