## Summary

DSP Investment Managers Private Limited has announced the record date for the redemption and maturity of several plans under DSP Mutual Fund. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified mutual fund plans.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260918-51
- Fund House: DSP Mutual Fund
- Schemes maturing include DSP FMP - Series 264-60M-17D (Growth and IDCW options for both Regular and Direct plans).
- Record Date: 25/09/2026
- Maturity Date: 29/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified mutual fund plans of DSP Mutual Fund as of the record date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 25/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 25/09/2026
- Maturity Date: 29/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational closure of specified fixed maturity plans for investors and trading members.
