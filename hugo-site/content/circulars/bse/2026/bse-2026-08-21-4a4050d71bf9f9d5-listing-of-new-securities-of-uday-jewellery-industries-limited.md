## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has notified trading members about the listing and trading permission for new securities issued by Uday Jewellery Industries Limited (Scrip Code - 539518) effective from Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 4,50,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 144/- to promoters and non-promoters on a preferential basis due to the conversion of warrants.
- **Ranking:** These new shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 36765826 to 37215825
- **ISIN:** INE551B01012
- **Lock-in Details:**
  - 1,50,000 shares with distinctive numbers 36765826 - 36915825 are locked-in up to 31/03/2028.
  - 3,00,000 shares with distinctive numbers 36915826 - 37215825 are locked-in up to 30/03/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the new securities and lock-in periods as specified.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 01/05/2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** 24/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital base via warrant conversion, with applicable lock-in restrictions on allotted shares.
