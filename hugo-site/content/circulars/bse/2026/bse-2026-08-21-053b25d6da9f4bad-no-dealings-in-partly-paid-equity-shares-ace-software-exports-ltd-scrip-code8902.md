## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the fixation of the Record Date by Ace Software Exports Ltd. for the payment of First Call on Rights Equity Shares. Consequently, trading members are advised not to deal in the partly paid equity shares of the company starting from the specified no-dealings date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ace Software Exports Ltd. (Scrip Code: 890230)
- Purpose: To ascertain holders of partly paid shares eligible to pay the First Call Money.
- First Call Money: Rs. 30.25 per share (Rs. 2.75 towards face value and Rs. 27.50 towards securities premium).
- Record Date: 26/08/2026
- No Dealings From Date: 25/08/2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the Exchange are required to take note and ensure no dealings take place in the partly paid equity shares of the company from the no-dealings date onwards.

## Important Dates

- No Dealings From Date: 25/08/2026
- Record Date: 26/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary restriction on trading of partly paid equity shares of Ace Software Exports Ltd. to facilitate call money payment processing.
