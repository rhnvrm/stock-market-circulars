## Summary

Suvira Industries Limited has submitted its Draft Offer Document to BSE on September 28, 2026, under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations for its upcoming SME Initial Public Offering (IPO). The document has been made available on the stock exchange websites for public review and comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Suvira Industries Limited
- Filing Date: September 28, 2026
- Segment: SME
- Regulatory Framework: Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations
- Public Comment Period: At least 21 days from the date of the notice (September 29, 2026)
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; the notice is a standard disclosure regarding compliance with ICDR Regulations for SME IPO draft offer documents.

## Compliance Requirements

Public and stakeholders are invited to review the Draft Offer Document hosted on the SME website and submit any comments or feedback within 21 days from the notice date to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Draft Offer Document Filing Date: September 28, 2026
- Comment Deadline: Within 21 days from September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites stakeholder scrutiny during the preliminary stages of Suvira Industries Limited's SME IPO process, ensuring market integrity and regulatory compliance prior to final issuance.
