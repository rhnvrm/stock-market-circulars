## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of selected securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework effective from July 28, 2026. Trading members are required to comply with specific surveillance actions corresponding to each GSM stage.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure will move to higher GSM stages starting July 28, 2026.
- Stage I applies a 100% margin rate and a price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II implements trade-for-trade settlement with a 5% price band and a 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value for buyers.
- Stage III and IV enforce trade-for-trade settlement, trading restricted to once a week (every Monday), 100% ASD, and no upward price movement for Stage IV.
- Settlement will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities shifted to higher GSM stages based on the framework established by exchange notices dating from February 23, 2017, to November 17, 2023.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in securities subjected to GSM.
- Compliance with 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement restrictions, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) as applicable per stage.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 28, 2026
- Notice Date: July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading frequency for the affected securities will be heavily restricted, particularly for those moving into Stages II, III, and IV where trading is limited or subject to heavy deposits and trade-for-trade mechanisms.
