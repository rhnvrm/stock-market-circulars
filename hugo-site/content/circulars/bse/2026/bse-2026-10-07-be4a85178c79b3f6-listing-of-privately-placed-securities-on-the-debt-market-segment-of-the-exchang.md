## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new securities issued on a private placement basis on the Debt Segment effective October 07, 2026. The listing covers Commercial Papers and Privately Placed Debt Instruments for multiple companies.

## Key Points

- **Chennai Petroleum Corporation Ltd.**: Listed 3,000 Commercial Papers of face value Rs. 500,000 each (Scrip Code: 732638, ISIN: INE178A14HU2), maturing on 21/10/2026.
- **Credila Financial Services Limited**: Listed 26,000 Debt Securities carrying an interest rate of 9.25% p.a. maturing on 05/10/2029 (Scrip Code: 978276, ISIN: INE539K07361).
- **Jio Credit Limited**: Listed 176,650 Debt Securities at 8.59% p.a. maturing on 05/10/2031 (Scrip Code: 978277, ISIN: INE282H07125) and 85,000 zero-coupon/other debt securities maturing on 04/10/2030 (Scrip Code: 978278, ISIN: INE282H07117).
- **Lendingkart Finance Limited**: Listed 20,000 Debt Securities at 9.25% p.a. (Scrip Code: 978280, ISIN: INE090W07733).

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes reported; standard listing notifications under BSE DCS-Listing department guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure securities are traded only in dematerialised form under the specified ISIN numbers.
- Standard trading denomination for commercial papers is Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates**: Between 05/10/2026 and 07/10/2026.
- **Effective Listing Date**: 07/10/2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides secondary market liquidity for the newly listed privately placed debt securities and commercial papers.
