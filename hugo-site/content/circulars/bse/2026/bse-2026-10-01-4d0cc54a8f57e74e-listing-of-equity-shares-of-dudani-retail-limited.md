## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that the equity shares of Dudani Retail Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in due course under the SME segment. The company has been assigned scrip code 544961, symbol DUDANI, and ISIN INE1DLN01012.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Dudani Retail Limited
- Segment: SME
- Scrip Code: 544961
- Symbol: DUDANI
- ISIN: INE1DLN01012
- Department: DCS-Listing

None. This is a standard corporate admission and listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the upcoming listing details for client positioning and trade operations.
