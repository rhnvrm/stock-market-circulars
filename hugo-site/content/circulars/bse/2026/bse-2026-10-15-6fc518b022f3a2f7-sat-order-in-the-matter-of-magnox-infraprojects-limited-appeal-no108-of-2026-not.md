## Summary

The Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) has issued an order dated October 15, 2026, setting aside the SEBI Whole Time Member (WTM) order dated February 20, 2018, qua the appellant Jyotiranjan Panda in the matter of Magnox Infraprojects Limited.

## Key Points

- SAT Order Date: October 15, 2026
- Appellant / Noticee: Jyotiranjan Panda
- Company: Magnox Infraprojects Limited
- Previous Order Set Aside: SEBI WTM order dated February 20, 2018 (Exchange Notice No. 20180220-30)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this circular notifies members of a specific SAT tribunal order.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the SAT order and ensure compliance with the updated legal standing.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 15, 2026
- Previous Order Date: February 20, 2018

## Impact Assessment

Provides legal relief to the specific appellant as the earlier restrictive SEBI WTM order is set aside with respect to Jyotiranjan Panda.
