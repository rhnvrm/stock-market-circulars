## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by several companies on the debt market segment effective August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Commercial papers admitted to trading in dematerialized form under designated ISINs.
- Standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuers include Tata Housing Development Company Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Vivriti Capital Limited, Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited, and InCred Financial Services Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades comply with standard debt segment guidelines and dematerialized settlement procedures.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 25/08/2026
- Effective Listing Date: 27/08/2026
- Redemption Dates: Various (Nov 2026 to May 2027)

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt market listing with no broader market impact.
