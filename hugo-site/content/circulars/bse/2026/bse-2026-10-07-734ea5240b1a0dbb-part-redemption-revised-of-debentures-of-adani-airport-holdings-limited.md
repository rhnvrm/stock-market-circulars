## Summary

BSE has announced the actual reduced face value for part redemption of debentures issued by Adani Airport Holdings Limited, effective from October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Adani Airport Holdings Limited
- Scrip Code: 976065
- ISIN: INE0GCN07047
- Security Description: AAHL-9.35%-8-9-28-PVT
- Purpose: Part Redemption of Debentures (Revised)
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 56,759/- per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

- In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260924-5 dated September 24, 2026, trading members are informed of the actual reduced face value following part redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that the debentures are traded with the new reduced face value of Rs. 56,759/-.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 08, 2026 (DR-731/2026-2027)

## Impact Assessment

- Operational adjustment for trading and settlement of debt securities reflecting the updated face value.
