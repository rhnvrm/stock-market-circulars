## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that new securities of Suven Life Sciences Ltd. (Scrip Code - 530239) consisting of 1,85,70,133 equity shares issued pursuant to warrant conversion are listed and permitted to trade with effect from Tuesday, August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Suven Life Sciences Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 530239
- ISIN: INE495B01038
- Number of Shares: 1,85,70,133 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 133/- (Issue Price: Rs. 134/-) to non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- Date of Allotment: 08/07/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: August 25, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 263992554 to 282562686
- Lock-in Details: 18,570,133 shares locked-in up to 25/02/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company. Lock-in requirements apply as specified.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 08/07/2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: 25/08/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: 25/02/2027

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up equity capital due to conversion of warrants into equity shares for non-promoters.
