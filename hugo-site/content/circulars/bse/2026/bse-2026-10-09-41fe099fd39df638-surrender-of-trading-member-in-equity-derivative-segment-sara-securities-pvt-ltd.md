## Summary

BSE has announced that the surrender of trading membership of Sara Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 6348) in the Equity derivative segment has been approved by the Exchange with effect from October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: Sara Securities Pvt. Ltd.
- Member Number: 6348
- Segment: Equity derivative segment
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026
- Contact for Clarifications: membership.ops@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members seeking clarifications on the notice may contact the membership operations department via email at membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard administrative surrender of membership with negligible impact on overall market operations or liquidity.
