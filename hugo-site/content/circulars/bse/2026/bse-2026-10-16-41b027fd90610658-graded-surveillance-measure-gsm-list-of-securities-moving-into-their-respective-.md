## Summary

BSE has announced that specified securities will move into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework effective October 19, 2026. Trading members are required to comply with specific surveillance actions corresponding to each stage.

## Key Points

- Securities identified in the attached Annexure move to higher GSM stages effective October 19, 2026.
- Surveillance measures include 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) ranging from 50% to 100% of trade value.
- Settlement for applicable securities must be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Stage III and IV restrict trading to once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of stricter surveillance measures under the GSM framework as established in previous exchange notices (dating from February 2017 to November 2023).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions, ensure required margin and ASD amounts are deposited by buyers, and adhere to trade-to-trade settlement rules without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements, ASD obligations, and settlement restrictions will impact liquidity and trading frequency for the affected securities, increasing capital requirements for market participants.
