## Summary

BSE has announced the application and revision of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework for various securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies. Measures include 100% margin requirements, movement to higher stages, transfer to specific groups (T / XT / MT / TS), and price band revisions effective early July 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. July 03, 2026, on open positions as of July 02, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moving to higher stages of the LT-ASM Framework with lower price bands effective July 01, 2026.
- Certain securities transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective July 03, 2026.
- Review includes movement of securities out of the LT-ASM framework and consolidated lists provided in annexures.
- Action is purely for market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of LT-ASM framework implementation based on previous notices and company XBRL disclosures.
- Imposition of strict surveillance measures including compulsory 100% margins and price band constraints.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure adequate margin provisioning (100%) for securities under the LT-ASM framework as specified.
- Members and traders must account for lower price bands and group transfers (T/XT/MT/TS) for applicable scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Band and Higher Stages (Part B): July 01, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: July 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin obligations for market participants trading in securities placed under LT-ASM stages.
- Liquidity and trading activity in affected securities may be impacted due to 100% margin rules and lower price bands.
