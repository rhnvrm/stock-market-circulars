## Summary

HDFC Asset Management Co. Ltd has announced the record date for the redemption and rollover of multiple schemes under HDFC FMP 1269D March 2023. Trading members are advised not to deal in these specified schemes effective from the record date.

## Key Points

- Fund House: HDFC Mutual Fund (HDFC Asset Management Co. Ltd)
- Purpose: Redemption/Rollover of Mutual Fund Schemes
- Record Date: 03/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 03/09/2026
- Impacted Schemes include various growth and IDCW options of HDFC FMP 1269D March 2023 - Series 47.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading Members are advised not to deal in the specified HDFC Mutual Fund schemes as of the record date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 02 Sep 2026
- Record Date: 03/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 03/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding units in the specified HDFC Mutual Fund FMP schemes will be subject to redemption or rollover processing as per the fund house guidelines, and trading in these specific scrip codes is restricted.
