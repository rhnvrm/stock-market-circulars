## Summary

BSE has published a surveillance notice highlighting significant price and volume movements observed across various scrips. Clarifications have been received from 13 companies, while responses from 15 additional companies remain awaited to ensure investors have access to relevant information.

## Key Points

- Surveillance query initiated due to significant price/volume movement in specific scrips.
- Replies received and attached from 13 companies including Delta Corp Ltd, PB Fintech Ltd, and Shoppers Stop Ltd.
- Replies awaited from 15 companies including Deep Polymers Ltd and S. M. Gold Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard surveillance monitoring notice issued by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to furnish timely clarifications regarding abnormal trading movements to maintain market integrity and transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances investor awareness and market transparency by compiling company responses to abnormal trading activity.
