## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the declaration of a Non-Business Day for the Nippon India US Equity Opportunities Fund by Nippon India Mutual Fund due to market closures on September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: Nippon India Mutual Fund
- Scheme Affected: Nippon India US Equity Opportunities Fund
- Date of Non-Business Day: September 07, 2026
- Restricted Transactions: Subscription, Redemption, Switch, SIP, STP, and SWP

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Transactions through the BSE StAR MF Platform for the specified scheme are not permitted on the mentioned date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 04 Sep 2026
- Non-Business Day: 07 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and members will be unable to execute subscription, redemption, or systematic transactions in the specified scheme via the BSE StAR MF Platform on September 07, 2026.
