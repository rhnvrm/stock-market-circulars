## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the SEBI turnover fee across multiple segments (Cash, Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, Interest Rate Derivatives, New Debt Segment, Repo Segment, and EGR segment) is subject to 18% GST. The turnover fees along with GST for the month of September 2026 will be debited on October 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- SEBI turnover fee across Cash, Derivatives, Currency, Commodity, Interest Rate Derivatives, New Debt, Repo, and EGR segments attracts 18% GST.
- Fees and applicable GST will be debited to the trading member's respective settlement (valan) account or Exchange dues account on October 06, 2026.
- Details of turnover and amounts to be debited will be available in EXTRANET on or before October 04, 2026.
- Invoices for September 2026 will be available for download in EXTRANET under Periodic files > 2026 > ZIP with file name SEBISEP2026.Member_Code.
- Relevant file names include TF2Ammyy through TF2Jmmyy.

## Regulatory Changes

- SEBI turnover fees across various market segments continue to be subject to 18% GST as applicable since July 18, 2022.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure sufficient funds are available in their settlement account (valan account) / Exchange dues account prior to the debit date.
- Members should verify their turnover details and invoice files from the EXTRANET on or before October 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Turnover details and invoices available in EXTRANET: On or before October 04, 2026.
- Debit date from settlement/dues account: October 06, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Direct operational and cash flow impact on trading members for monthly statutory fee payments and GST accounting.
