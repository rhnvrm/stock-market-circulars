## Summary

BSE has issued the settlement schedule and calendar for shorter tenure contracts under the Securities Lending & Borrowing (SLB) Scheme in the SLB Reverse Leg Segment for the period from October 01, 2026, to October 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Details trade dates and corresponding SLR pay-in/pay-out dates throughout October 2026.
- Pay-in is conducted at 09:00 a.m., requiring members to submit pay-in instructions to depositories/banks latest by 08:50 a.m.
- All other provisions regarding market timings, clearing and settlement, risk management, and corporate actions remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants must submit pay-in instructions to Depositories and banks by 08:50 a.m. on the respective pay-in dates.

## Important Dates

- Schedule Period: October 01, 2026 to October 31, 2026
- First Trade Date: October 01, 2026 (Pay-in/Pay-out: October 07, 2026)
- Final Trade Dates in October: Up to October 30, 2026 (Pay-in/Pay-out extending into early November 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact, providing market participants with clear timeline visibility for SLBR daily expiries.
