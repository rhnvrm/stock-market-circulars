## Summary

BSE has issued a settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Shankara Building Products Limited. The bidding window is scheduled to run from September 7, 2026, to September 21, 2026, under settlement number 2026111.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Takeover
- Settlement No.: 2026111
- Bid Start Date: 07/09/2026
- Bid End Date: 21/09/2026
- Obligation Download: 01/10/2026
- Settlement Date: 05/10/2026
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories.
- Members and custodians must use the Market Type (Takeover) and settlement number 2026111.

## Regulatory Changes

[None specified beyond adherence to established takeover acquisition window settlement mechanisms and guidelines.]

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must ensure early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/BSECL systems before the prescribed timelines for bidding and confirmation.
- CDSL and NSDL early pay-in securities will be earmarked/blocked in the client's account.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: September 7, 2026
- Bid End Date: September 21, 2026
- Obligation Download Date: October 1, 2026
- Settlement Date: October 5, 2026
