## Summary

In continuation of the Exchange's Notice No. 20260811-35 dated August 11, 2026, trading members are informed that the equity shares of **G V ELECTRICALS LTD** (Scrip Code: **544859**) will transition from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from **Wednesday, August 26, 2026**.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** G V ELECTRICALS LTD
- **Scrip Code:** 544859
- **Previous Group:** MT Group (Trade for Trade segment)
- **New Group:** M Group (Rolling segment)
- **Effective Date:** Wednesday, August 26, 2026
- **Contact Person:** Mr. Anurag Jain (Tel. No. 022-2272 8822)

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of the company's shares from gross settlement / Trade for Trade rules to standard rolling settlement system for SME segment securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members:** Update brokerage and back-office systems to shift the security classification of Scrip Code 544859 from the MT Group to the M Group prior to market opening on August 26, 2026. Adjust margin collection practices to align with Rolling segment requirements.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date of Transition:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Liquidity for G V ELECTRICALS LTD is expected to improve as the Rolling segment allows for intraday netting and does not restrict transactions to 100% upfront delivery-based settlement.
- **Operational Impact:** Change in margin rules and transaction processing from gross settlement to rolling settlement.
