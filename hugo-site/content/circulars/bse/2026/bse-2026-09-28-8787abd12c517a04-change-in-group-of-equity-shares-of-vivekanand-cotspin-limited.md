## Summary

BSE has announced that the equity shares of Vivekanand Cotspin Limited (Scrip Code: 544939) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Tuesday, October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vivekanand Cotspin Limited
- Scrip Code: 544939
- Segment Shift: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Tuesday, October 13, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange’s Notice No. 20260925-46 dated September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the equity shares is moving from the restrictive Trade for Trade segment to the regular Rolling segment, permitting normal netting and settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should take note of the segment change and ensure appropriate operational handling for client trades in the scrip.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The transition enhances trading liquidity and flexibility for investors in Vivekanand Cotspin Limited as transactions will no longer be restricted to gross delivery without netting.
