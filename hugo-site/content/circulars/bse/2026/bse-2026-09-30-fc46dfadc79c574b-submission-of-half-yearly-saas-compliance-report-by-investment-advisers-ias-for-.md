## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding all Investment Advisers (IAs) to submit their half-yearly Software as a Service (SaaS) Compliance Report for the audit period ended September 30, 2026, pursuant to SEBI circular guidelines.

## Key Points

- Compliance reporting is required under SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD2/DOR/CIR/P/2020/221 dated November 03, 2020.
- Submission deadline is on or before October 31, 2026.
- Filings must be completed through the BSE IA/RA Portal under RAIA Inspection -> SaaS Submission Form.
- Complete and accurate disclosure of all SaaS-based solutions or hosted services utilized is mandatory, including nil declarations where applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulations introduced; this notice enforces existing reporting obligations under SEBI's SaaS advisory framework for financial sector organizations.

## Compliance Requirements

Investment Advisers must submit complete and accurate details of all SaaS-based solutions and hosted services used by the organization via the designated BSE portal on or before October 31, 2026. If no SaaS solutions are utilized, an appropriate declaration must be submitted.

## Important Dates

- Audit Period Ended: September 30, 2026
- Submission Deadline: October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory oversight and transparency regarding cloud and SaaS-based software solutions deployed by Investment Advisers, mitigating operational and cybersecurity risks.
