## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, and qualifying securities will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the inclusion criteria attract a minimum 100% margin effective September 17, 2026, on open positions as of September 16, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective September 16, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment effective September 17, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including 100% margins, price band restrictions, and movement to the Trade-for-Trade segment for short-listed securities under the ESM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the margin requirements, segment shifts, and price band changes applicable to the securities listed in the annexures of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II / Lower Stage movements / Price band changes: September 16, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shift: September 17, 2026
- Open positions reference date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in securities included in the ESM framework will experience increased margin requirements and trading restrictions such as Trade-for-Trade settlement and price bands.
