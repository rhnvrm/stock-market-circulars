## Summary

The BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment on Saturday, September 05, 2026. Trading members can use this session to test trading applications, software, and risk management systems.

## Key Points

- Scheduled Date: Saturday, September 05, 2026.
- Segments: Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives.
- New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.50 will be released with details to be published separately.
- Trades resulting from mock trading will not attract any margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Fulfills regulatory software testing requirements in accordance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234 dated November 24, 2020.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members using third-party empaneled vendors or in-house systems can test functionalities during the mock session.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 4:30 PM on the day of the mock session via provided contact numbers or email.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 05, 2026
- Log-in Time: 08:15 AM to 09:00 AM
- Continuous Trading (Currency & Cross Currency): 09:00 AM to 03:30 PM
- Trade Modification (Currency & Cross Currency): up to 03:40 PM

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness and software verification for trading members without financial risk or margin implications.
