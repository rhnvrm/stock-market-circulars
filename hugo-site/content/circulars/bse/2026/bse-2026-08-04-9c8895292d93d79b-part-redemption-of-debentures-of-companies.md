## Summary

Trading members are informed that several companies have fixed record dates for payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260804-16
- Segment: Debt
- Department: Listing Operations
- Involves companies such as Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, Vardhman Polytex Ltd., Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, Mufin Green Finance Limited, and India Shelter Finance Corporation Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in the debentures of the affected companies will resume with reduced face values as per the respective schedules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for debt instruments before executing transactions.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 04 Aug 2026
- Record Dates: 06/08/2026, 08/08/2026, 11/08/2026, and 12/08/2026 across different securities.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding the debentures will receive part redemption payments and interest based on the reduced face value and record dates.
