## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of Narmada Agrobase Ltd following a stock split from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Narmada Agrobase Ltd
- Scrip Code: 543643
- New ISIN: INE117Z01029
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares from Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/-

## Regulatory Changes

- Assignment of a new ISIN number consequent to the share split.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed using the new ISIN number effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: 31-07-2026
- Notice Date: 30-07-2026
