## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the change of name for KD Leisures Ltd to Vedic Ayurveda Limited. The scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: KD Leisures Ltd
- New Company Name: Vedic Ayurveda Limited
- Scrip Code: 540385
- Effective Date: August 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records and trading systems with the new name and scrip identifiers.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal market impact, ensuring accurate identification of the company under its new name on the exchange trading platform.
