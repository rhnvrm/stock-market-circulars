## Summary

GEOJIT INVESTMENTS LIMITED (Member No. 328) has voluntarily decided to cease doing business in the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment on BSE Limited, effective from Friday, September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Member Name:** GEOJIT INVESTMENTS LIMITED
- **Member Code:** 328
- **Segment:** Electronic Gold Receipt segment
- **Effective Date:** September 4, 2026
- **Reason:** Voluntary closure of business

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members requiring clarifications can contact membership operations at membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact limited to the cessation of operations by this specific trading member in the Electronic Gold Receipt segment.
