## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading in these debentures will take place with reduced face values starting from the respective effective dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260911-49, dated September 11, 2026.
- Covers part redemption and interest payment for NCDs/Bonds of multiple companies including Edelweiss Rural & Corporate Services Limited, Regency Fincorp Limited, A.K. Capital Finance Limited, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, and Unigold Finance Limited.
- Trading will be conducted with reduced face values effective from the specified record/ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational notice regarding corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and ISINs for the debentures and ensure compliance with settlement and trading parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / Ex-Date: September 16, 2026 and September 17, 2026 (depending on the specific security).

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is restricted to trading members dealing in the specified debt securities, ensuring transactions reflect the updated reduced face values.
