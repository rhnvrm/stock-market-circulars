## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the admission to dealings of Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of Chemmanur Credits and Investments Ltd with effect from August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities: Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of Chemmanur Credits and Investments Ltd.
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026, under the 'F GROUP'.
- Trading format: Dematerialized form in unit market lot (One Bond) as per SEBI guidelines.
- Financial year of the company ends on March 31.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in dematerialized form in unit market lots.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 20, 2026
- Notice Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides secondary market liquidity for the newly listed non-convertible debentures.
