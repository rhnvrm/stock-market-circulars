## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of 31,74,60,316 new units of IRB InvIT Fund, issued pursuant to a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP). These units are effective for trading from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: IRB InvIT Fund units
- Scrip Code: 540526
- ISIN: INE183W23014
- Number of Units: 31,74,60,316
- Issue Price: Rs. 63/- per unit
- Date of Allotment: September 29, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 1335600001 To 1653060316
- Ranking: Pari-passu with existing units of the trust

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard corporate listing notice under BSE Listing Operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading terminals and systems with the new units and distribution range.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 29, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing of QIP units increases the total outstanding units of IRB InvIT Fund, expanding its capital base while ranking pari-passu with existing units.
