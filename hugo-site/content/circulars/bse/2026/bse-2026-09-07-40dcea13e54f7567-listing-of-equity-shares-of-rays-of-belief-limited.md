## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, September 8, 2026, the equity shares of Rays of Belief Limited (For Profit Social Enterprise - FPSE) shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the 'T' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip ID: MOMSBELIEF
- Scrip Code: 544906
- ISIN No: INE1FCO01014
- Number of Securities: 2,09,01,682 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 239/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 229/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 4, 2026
- Registrar: Kfin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- The scrip shall be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI Circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed to have the lock-in period marked in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 4, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 8, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): Tuesday, September 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing enables public trading of Rays of Belief Limited equity shares on the BSE, providing liquidity to shareholders under standard trade-for-trade surveillance controls for the initial trading period.
