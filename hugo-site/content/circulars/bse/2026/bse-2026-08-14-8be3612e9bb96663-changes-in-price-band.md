## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of multiple scrips traded on the equity segment, effective from August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260814-38, dated August 14, 2026.
- Category: Trading, Segment: Equity, Department: DOSS.
- Price bands for various scrips have been revised to percentages such as 2%, 5%, 10%, and 20%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of price limits for listed securities to curb excessive volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their trading systems and ensure compliance with the revised price bands for the specified scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the listed scrips will be subject to the newly designated price band percentages, impacting maximum intraday price movements.
