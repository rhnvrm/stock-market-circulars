## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the sub-division of equity shares of Narmada Agrobase Ltd from a face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 5/-. The record date is set for July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Narmada Agrobase Ltd (Scrip Code: 543643)
- Sub-Division: Existing equity shares of Rs. 10/- each split into two equity shares of Rs. 5/- each.
- Record Date: 31/07/2026
- Effective Date: 31/07/2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Corporate action reflecting a change in the paid-up value and face value of equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- The old ISIN (INE117Z01011) of Rs. 10/- paid-up will not be valid for transactions done on the Exchange on or after 31/07/2026.
- The new ISIN number for Rs. 5/- paid-up will be communicated via a separate notice.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 31/07/2026
- Effective Date: 31/07/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders will receive sub-divided shares, doubling their holding quantity while halving the face value per share, maintaining total nominal investment value.
