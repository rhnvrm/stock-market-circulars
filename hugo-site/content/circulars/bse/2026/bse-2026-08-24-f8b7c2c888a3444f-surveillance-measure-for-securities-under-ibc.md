## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are placed under Additional Surveillance Measure effective August 25, 2026.
- Review of securities under the framework is conducted on a periodical basis.
- Annexure lists provide securities moving into, moving out of, and consolidated under the IBC framework, along with those classified due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% post-recommencement.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated to their original levels, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance framework measures for companies undergoing corporate insolvency resolution under the IBC.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and member brokers must note the list of securities under the IBC surveillance framework and adhere to applicable price bands and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading parameters and price bands of specific securities undergoing insolvency proceedings to ensure market integrity and investor protection.
