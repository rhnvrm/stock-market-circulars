## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds for several companies. Trading members and investors are informed that trading in these debentures/bonds will be conducted with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates in October 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260929-17, dated September 29, 2026.
- Covers part redemption and interest/principal payment for multiple companies including JSW Steel Limited, SI Creva Capital Services Private Limited, Krazybee Services Ltd, Vivriti Capital Limited, Mufin Green Finance Limited, and Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited.
- Trading in the respective debentures/bonds will occur with reduced face values starting from October 1, 2026, and October 8-9, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational update reflecting corporate debt redemption schedules and face value adjustments on the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the revised face values, record dates, and ex-dates for settlement and trading operations.

## Important Dates

- JSW Steel Limited (Scrip 959034): Record Date - 01/10/2026, Ex-Date - 01/10/2026, Reduced Face Value - Rs. 750,000/- per debenture.
- SI Creva Capital Services (Scrip 978141): Record Date - 02/10/2026, Ex-Date - 01/10/2026, Reduced Face Value - Rs. 8,333.37 per debenture.
- Krazybee Services Ltd (Scrip 976930): Record Date & Ex-Date - 08/10/2026, Reduced Face Value - Rs. 50,000/- per debenture.
- Vivriti Capital Limited (Scrip 976532): Record Date & Ex-Date - 09/10/2026, Reduced Face Value - Rs. 72,868/- per debenture.
- Mufin Green Finance Limited (Scrip 977318): Record Date - 11/10/2026, Ex-Date - 09/10/2026, Reduced Face Value - Rs. 16,666.67 per debenture.
- Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited (Scrip 977753): Record Date - 11/10/2026, Ex-Date - 09/10/2026, Reduced Face Value - Rs. 7,500/- per debenture.

## Impact Assessment

- Direct impact on debenture holders concerning partial capital repayment and adjusted face values for ongoing secondary market trading.
