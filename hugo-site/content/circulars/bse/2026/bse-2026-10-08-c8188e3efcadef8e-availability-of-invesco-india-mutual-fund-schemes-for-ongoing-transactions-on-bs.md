## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that pursuant to Exchange Notice No. 20260911-39 dated September 11, 2026, specific schemes of Invesco India Mutual Fund will be available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 9, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Schemes Included:
  1. Invesco India Nifty Chemical Index Fund (Equity)
  2. Invesco India Nifty India Defence Index Fund (Equity)
- Supported Transactions: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine platform availability notice for mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can facilitate transactions for the listed Invesco India Mutual Fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with existing exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 8, 2026
- Effective Date: October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members access to new thematic index fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform for systematic and lump-sum investments.
