## Summary

BSE has enabled the SMART Switch feature under the BANDHAN CONTRA FUND – NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform. The feature is available from August 10, 2026, until August 20, 2026, facilitating automated switch-ins to the NFO scheme from eligible source schemes.

## Key Points

- SMART Switch feature available via Manual Entry, Bulk Upload, and APIs.
- Applicable only for Bandhan Mutual Fund schemes specified in the annexures.
- Available exclusively for lump sum subscriptions in non-Demat mode.
- Transactions accepted up to 1:00 PM on August 20, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must use appropriate scheme credentials in accordance with investor requirements.
- Switch-out processing follows FIFO logic for newly purchased units.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 10, 2026
- Closing Date & Time: August 20, 2026, up to 1:00 PM
- RTA Reporting Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Streamlines the subscription and switch process for investors participating in the Bandhan Contra Fund NFO through the BSE StAR MF platform.
