## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 10 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Monday, October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- A total of 10 companies have issued further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective date for listing and trading: October 19, 2026.
- Participating companies include AU Small Finance Bank, Capital Small Finance Bank, Delhivery, Five-Star Business Finance, FSN E-Commerce Ventures, ICICI Bank, Kirloskar Pneumatic, Persistent Systems, Rose Merc, and Zensar Technologies.
- All listed securities are equity shares with lock-in status marked as NA.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing action for securities issued under employee stock option schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and issuing companies must ensure compliance with standard exchange and depository operational guidelines for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 16, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of paid-up equity capital due to the exercise of stock options by employees across various listed entities, resulting in minimal market dilution.
