## Summary

Trading members are informed that units of the Edelweiss BSE LargeMid (60:40) Stable Dividend 50 ETF under Edelweiss Mutual Fund are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the 'B' Group effective August 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Name of Scheme:** Edelweiss BSE LargeMid (60:40) Stable Dividend 50 ETF
- **Mutual Fund:** Edelweiss Mutual Fund
- **Investment Manager:** Edelweiss Asset Management Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544846
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** ELMDIV
- **ISIN:** INF754K01XI7
- **Date of Allotment:** 31/07/2026
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 36.60 per unit
- **No. of Units:** 1,706,710 Units
- **Market Lot:** 1 Unit

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Units of the Mutual Fund will be traded strictly in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** July 31, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** August 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations addition providing investors with an additional exchange-traded fund option tracking the BSE LargeMid (60:40) Stable Dividend 50 Total Return Index.
