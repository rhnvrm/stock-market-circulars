## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 17 scrips, effective from September 22, 2026. These changes are implemented as part of regular surveillance measures to manage market volatility in specific securities.

## Key Points

- Total of 17 scrips affected by price band revisions.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is September 22, 2026.
- Price bands have been revised to different levels including 2%, 5%, 10%, and 20% depending on the scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands for specified scrips have been modified from their existing levels to new percentages as determined by the exchange surveillance department.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems to reflect the revised price bands for the mentioned scrips to avoid order rejections or execution errors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and traders holding or trading in these 17 scrips will be subject to the new daily price movement limits, which may affect liquidity and intraday volatility.
