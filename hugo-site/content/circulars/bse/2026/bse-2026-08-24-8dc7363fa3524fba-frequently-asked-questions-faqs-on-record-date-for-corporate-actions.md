## Summary

BSE has issued a circular forwarding Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) on the intimation of Record Date for Corporate Actions for the reference and guidance of all listed entities.

## Key Points

- Annexed FAQs provide clarifications regarding the intimation of Record Dates for Corporate Actions.
- All listed entities are required to take note and comply accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clarifications and guidelines regarding corporate action record date intimations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must review the annexed FAQs and ensure compliance with record date intimation requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 24 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances clarity and standardizes compliance for listed companies executing corporate actions.
