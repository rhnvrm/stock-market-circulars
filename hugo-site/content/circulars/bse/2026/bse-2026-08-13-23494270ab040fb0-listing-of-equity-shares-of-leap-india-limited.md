## Summary

LEAP India Limited's equity shares are scheduled to be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange effective Friday, August 14, 2026, under the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Scrip Name / Code: LEAP India Limited / 544865
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name on BOLT: LEAPIND
- ISIN: INE00GO01025
- Total Securities: 440,536,458 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up (Issue Price: Rs. 159/- per share)
- Date of Allotment: August 12, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) applicable on listing day (August 14, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded exclusively in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Corporate actions regarding lock-in periods have been executed in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 12, 2026
- Listing and Trading Commencement Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of LEAP India Limited shares on the exchange platform, providing liquidity to investors and expanding market participation.
