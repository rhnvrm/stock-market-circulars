## Summary

BSE has issued a settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Delisting) for Avadh Rail Infra Limited (Settlement No. 2026104). The bidding window is scheduled to run from August 18, 2026, to August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Delisting
- Settlement No.: 2026104
- Bid Start Date: 18/08/2026
- Bid End Date: 24/08/2026
- Obligation Download: 02/09/2026
- Settlement Date: 03/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

- None specific other than adherence to the revised guidelines for mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories.
- Members and custodians must use Market Type '(Delisting)' and Settlement No. 2026104.
- CDSL early pay-in requires clients to transfer directly to the member/custodian's early pay-in account, while NSDL early pay-in involves earmarking/blocking securities in the client's account.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should check the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: August 18, 2026
- Bid End Date: August 24, 2026
- Obligation Download: September 2, 2026
- Settlement Date: September 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates the orderly execution, bidding, and settlement process for the delisting of Avadh Rail Infra Limited through the stock exchange acquisition window.
