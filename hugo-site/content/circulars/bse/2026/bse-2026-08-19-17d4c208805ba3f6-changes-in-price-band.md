## Summary

BSE has announced a revision of price bands for 20 scrips as part of its surveillance measures, effective from August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised for 20 scrips traded on the equity segment.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is August 20, 2026.
- Revised price bands include 2%, 5%, and 20% limits depending on the specific scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands for specified scrips have been modified from their existing levels to curb excessive volatility and ensure orderly market operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while executing trades in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: August 19, 2026
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members dealing in the mentioned 20 scrips must adjust their trading strategies and order placements to comply with the new price limits.
