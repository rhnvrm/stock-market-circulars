## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, August 19, 2026, the equity shares of Behari Lal Engineering Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Behari Lal Engineering Limited
- Scrip ID / Code: BLEL / 544870
- ISIN: INE1EEM01017
- Number of Securities: 42,302,482 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 285/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 275/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 17, 2026
- Trading starts in dematerialised form with Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines.
- Corporate action executed for lock-in marking in depository's records.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adherence to demat trading guidelines and special pre-open session parameters.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 17, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Behari Lal Engineering Limited shares on BSE.
