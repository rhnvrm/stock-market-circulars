## Summary

Mirae Asset Mutual Fund is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Mirae Asset Life Cycle Fund 2056 along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from September 28, 2026, to October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Mirae Asset Life Cycle Fund 2056 (Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- **NFO Period:** September 28, 2026, to October 12, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 19, 2026.
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 16, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be client-authenticated by 3:00 PM on October 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** September 28, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** October 16, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates investors and members in subscribing to the new Mirae Asset Mutual Fund scheme through the BSE StAR MF platform with established timelines and operational guidelines.
