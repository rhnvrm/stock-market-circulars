## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that trading is permitted in the new equity shares of Fusion Finance Ltd. (Scrip Code: 543652) effective from September 04, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Fusion Finance Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 543652
- ISIN: INE139R01012
- Securities: 2,27,856 Equity shares issued on a rights basis, converted from partly paid to fully paid-up (face value Rs. 10/- each).
- Effective Date: September 04, 2026.
- Ranking: These shares are pari-passu with the existing old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can trade in the newly listed securities as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Trading: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update allowing trading of newly converted fully paid equity shares.
