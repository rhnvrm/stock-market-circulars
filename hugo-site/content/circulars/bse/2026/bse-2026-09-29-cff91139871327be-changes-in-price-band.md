## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 23 scrips, effective from September 30, 2026, pursuant to surveillance measures and regulatory guidelines.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 23 scrips listed on the Exchange.
- Effective date of revision: September 30, 2026.
- Revised price bands range from 2% to 10% across various scrips.
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance).

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands of specified equity scrips have been modified from their existing levels to curb excessive volatility and ensure orderly trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems and risk management parameters to reflect the revised price bands for the affected scrips effective September 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected 23 scrips will be subject to the new price band limits, restricting maximum intraday price movements and impacting liquidity and volatility for these securities.
