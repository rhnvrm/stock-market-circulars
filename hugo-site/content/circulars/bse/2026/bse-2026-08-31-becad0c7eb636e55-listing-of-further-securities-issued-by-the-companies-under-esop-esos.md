## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new equity securities issued by nine companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange, effective from Tuesday, September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** September 01, 2026.
- **Total Companies Involved:** 9 companies.
- **Securities Type:** Equity shares issued pursuant to ESOP / ESOS exercises.
- **Lock-in Status:** Not Applicable (NA) across all listed allotments in this notice.

## Companies and Issue Details

1. **Aadhar Housing Finance Limited** (ISIN: INE883F01010, Scrip Code: 544176): 469,259 shares of face value ₹10.
2. **AU Small Finance Bank Limited** (ISIN: INE949L01017, Scrip Code: 540611): 647,261 shares of face value ₹10.
3. **Godrej Agrovet Limited** (ISIN: INE850D01014, Scrip Code: 540743): 51,423 shares of face value ₹10.
4. **HDB Financial Services Limited** (ISIN: INE756I01012, Scrip Code: 544429): 121,016 shares of face value ₹10.
5. **IndusInd Bank Limited** (ISIN: INE095A01012, Scrip Code: 532187): 7,800 shares of face value ₹10.
6. **Intellect Design Arena Limited** (ISIN: INE306R01017, Scrip Code: 538835): 89,870 shares of face value ₹5.
7. **Laxmi Dental Limited** (ISIN: INE0WO601020, Scrip Code: 544339): 59,360 shares of face value ₹2.
8. **RBL Bank Limited** (ISIN: INE976G01028, Scrip Code: 540065): 831,301 shares of face value ₹10.
9. **Tejas Networks Limited** (ISIN: INE010J01012, Scrip Code: 540595): 91,353 shares of face value ₹10.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are requested to take note of the new securities admitted for trading.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 31, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the respective issuers. Minimal impact on market liquidity and trading operations.
