## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Thursday, September 17, 2026, the equity shares of Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Limited
- Scrip Code: 544921
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: ARCIL
- ISIN No.: INE148G01016
- Number of Securities: 324,897,140 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 139/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 129/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, September 17, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository’s records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 17, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The company's equity shares become available for public trading on the BSE exchange under the 'B' group category.
