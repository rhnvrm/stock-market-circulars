## Summary

Franklin Templeton Mutual Fund has notified the change in scheme names for several mutual fund schemes available on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260824-23
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026
- Segment: Mutual Fund (BSE StAR MF Platform)
- Department: Trading Operations

### Scheme Name Changes

1. **Franklin India Medium to Long Term Fund** renamed to **Franklin India Medium to Long Duration Fund**
2. **Franklin India Corporate Debt Fund** renamed to **Franklin India Corporate Bond Fund**
3. **Franklin India Floating Rate Fund** renamed to **Franklin India Floating Interest Rates Fund**
4. **Franklin India Gilt Fund** renamed to **Franklin India Government Securities Fund**
5. **Franklin India Long Term Fund** renamed to **Franklin India Long Duration Fund**
6. **Franklin India Ultra Short Term Fund** renamed to **Franklin India Ultra Short Duration Fund**
7. **Franklin India Low Duration Fund** renamed to **Franklin India Ultra Short to Short Term Fund**

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational update regarding scheme nomenclature from the AMC.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors should update their systems and records with the revised scheme names to avoid confusion during transaction processing.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Existing unitholders and investors need to note the updated scheme names for future transactions on the platform.
