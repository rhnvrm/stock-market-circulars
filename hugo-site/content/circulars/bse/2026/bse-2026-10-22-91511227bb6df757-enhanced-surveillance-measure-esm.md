## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities qualifying under the framework will attract a minimum 100% margin, move across stages, or be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective October 23 and October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 26, 2026, on all open positions as on October 23, 2026, and new positions created from October 26, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but move to lower Stage I w.e.f. October 23, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 23, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 26, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the ESM Framework w.e.f. October 23, 2026, are listed in Annexure II, and the consolidated list is in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of enhanced surveillance measures, 100% margin requirements, price band restrictions, and trade-for-trade settlement for identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance, adherence to trade-for-trade settlement rules, and compliance with price band and periodic call auction mechanisms for impacted scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II movements and removals: October 23, 2026
- Open positions cutoff date for margin: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade settlement: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in affected securities must update collateral and margin arrangements and monitor settlement segment transitions.
