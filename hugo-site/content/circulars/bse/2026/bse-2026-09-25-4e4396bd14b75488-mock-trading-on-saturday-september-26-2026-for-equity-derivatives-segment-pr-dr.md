## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Equity Derivatives segment on Saturday, September 26, 2026, utilizing both the Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR). Trading members using third-party platforms can test applications and functionalities under exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading date: Saturday, September 26, 2026.
- Segments involved: Equity Derivatives segment (PR & DR).
- Applicability: Trading members using third-party trading platforms and proprietary systems.
- Features tested: Call auction sessions, risk-reduction mode, trading halt, block deals, etc.
- No new BOLT TWS Release.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational mock trading session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate in the mock trading session to test their trading applications, connectivity, and risk management systems.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational drill to ensure system readiness and resilience; no impact on live market operations or settlements.
