## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of two newly listed companies to its indices. Pranav Constructions Limited will be added to the BSE IPO index effective September 17, 2026, while Apana Logistics Limited will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Pranav Constructions Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544909) added to BSE IPO index effective September 17, 2026.
- Apana Logistics Limited (Exchange Ticker: 544910) added to BSE SME IPO index effective September 16, 2026.
- Actions included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Important Dates

- September 15, 2026: Notice issuance date.
- September 16, 2026: Apana Logistics Limited effective index addition date.
- September 17, 2026: Pranav Constructions Limited effective index addition date.
