## Summary

Trading members are informed that 31,25,633 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion for the public issue of Augmont Enterprises Limited at an issue price of Rs. 788/- per equity share, effective August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Augmont Enterprises Limited
- Portion: Anchor Investors (AIs)
- Number of Shares Subscribed: 31,25,633 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 788/- per equity share
- Date of Announcement: August 20, 2026

None
