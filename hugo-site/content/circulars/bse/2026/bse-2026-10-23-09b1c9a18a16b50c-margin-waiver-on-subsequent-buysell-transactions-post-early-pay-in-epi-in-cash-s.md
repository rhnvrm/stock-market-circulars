## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about the margin waiver on subsequent buy/sell transactions post Early Pay-In (EPI) in the cash segment, in reference to SEBI Circular no. HO/(320)2026-MRD-POD2/I/24141/2026 dated October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Refers to SEBI circular dated October 22, 2026 regarding margin waivers.
- Applies to subsequent buy/sell transactions in the cash segment following Early Pay-In (EPI).
- Issued by BSE Clearing Ltd. (formerly ICCL).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implements SEBI's guidelines on granting margin waivers for subsequent cash segment transactions after early pay-in of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are advised to take note of the SEBI circular and implement the margin waiver provisions accordingly in their trading and risk management systems.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 23, 2026.
- SEBI Circular Date: October 22, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational efficiency and liquidity for market participants by waiving margins on subsequent transactions after early pay-in of securities.
