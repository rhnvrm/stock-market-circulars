## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of Indo Thai Securities Limited (Scrip Code: 533676) effective from October 13, 2026. A total of 15,00,000 equity shares have been issued pursuant to the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Indo Thai Securities Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 533676
- **ISIN:** INE337M01021
- **Security Details:** 15,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 49/- (Issue Price: Rs. 50/- per share).
- **Basis:** Preferential issue pursuant to conversion of warrants to non-promoters.
- **Effective Date:** Trading permitted with effect from Tuesday, October 13, 2026.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 129618191 to 131118190.
- **Date of Allotment:** 03/07/2026.
- **Lock-in Details:** 15,00,000 shares locked-in up to 30/04/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing procedure for securities issued on a preferential basis following warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to note the listing of these equity shares and their lock-in status.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** July 3, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** April 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action expanding the paid-up capital base of Indo Thai Securities Limited with no direct immediate broader market impact.
