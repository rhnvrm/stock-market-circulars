## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in Demat Auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-739/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 22, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on October 22, 2026.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Auction Pay-in: Friday, Oct 23, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard auction procedure under the uniform membership structure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must exercise caution when punching security codes and rates as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 22, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice for clearing and settlement members participating in auction settlement DR-739/2026-27.
