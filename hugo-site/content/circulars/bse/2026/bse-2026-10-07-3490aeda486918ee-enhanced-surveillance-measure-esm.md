## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities moving to different stages, shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment, and attracting minimum 100% margin requirements.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for ESM inclusion attract a minimum 100% margin effective October 09, 2026, on open positions as of October 08, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities moving to lower Stage I under the ESM framework effective October 08, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will operate under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective October 08, 2026.
- Qualifying securities shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment effective October 09, 2026.
- List of securities moving out of the ESM framework effective October 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework, including stage revisions, price band restrictions, periodic call auctions, and shifts to trade-for-trade settlement for identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement rules, and price band restrictions for securities listed in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- October 08, 2026: Effective date for stage movements, price bands, periodic call auctions, and securities moving out of ESM.
- October 09, 2026: Effective date for minimum 100% margin and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

Heightened surveillance and trading restrictions on affected securities, impacting liquidity and requiring higher margin commitments from market participants.
