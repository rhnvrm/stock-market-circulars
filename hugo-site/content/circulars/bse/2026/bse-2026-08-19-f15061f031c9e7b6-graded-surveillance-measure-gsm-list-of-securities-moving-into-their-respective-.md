## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the movement of specific securities into higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure are moved to higher GSM stages.
- Effective date of implementation: August 20, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary across stages I to IV, including increased margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Regulatory Changes

- **Stage I:** Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- **Stage II:** Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value to be deposited by buyers.
- **Stage III:** Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday), and ASD of 100% of trade value.
- **Stage IV:** Trade-for-trade with price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday) with no upward movement, and ASD of 100% of trade value.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities, as settlement will be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 19, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and market participation in the affected securities will be restricted due to stringent margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and weekly trading limitations in higher GSM stages.
