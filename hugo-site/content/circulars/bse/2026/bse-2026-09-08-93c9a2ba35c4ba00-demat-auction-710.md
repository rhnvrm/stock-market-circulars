## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 710 for Rolling Settlement No. DR-710/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on Sep 08, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on Sep 08, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction scheduled for Wednesday, Sep 09, 2026 at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: Sep 08, 2026
- Pay-in Date: Sep 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine settlement activity affecting trading members participating in rolling settlement DR-710/2026-27.
