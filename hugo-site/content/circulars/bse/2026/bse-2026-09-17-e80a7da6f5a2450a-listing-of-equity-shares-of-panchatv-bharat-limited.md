## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that the equity shares of Panchatv Bharat Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in due course under the SME segment (Scrip Code: 544926, Symbol: PANCHATV, ISIN: INE0VXN01011).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Panchatv Bharat Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544926
- **Symbol:** PANCHATV
- **ISIN:** INE0VXN01011
- **Segment:** SME
- **Listing Status:** Approved for listing and admission to dealings in due course.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed of the upcoming admission to dealings; specific trading commencement dates will be announced separately.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 17 September 2026
- **Listing Date:** To be announced via a separate notice.

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to prepare for trading in the equity shares of Panchatv Bharat Limited once the official listing date is announced.
