## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of selected securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM), effective from July 31, 2026. These securities will attract specific surveillance actions as outlined in the GSM framework.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: July 31, 2026.
- Framework Reference: Based on previous exchange notices dating from February 23, 2017, to November 17, 2023.
- Surveillance Actions: Varying restrictions including 100% margin rates, price bands of 5% or lower, trade-for-trade settlement, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement on trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities moving into higher GSM stages become subject to enhanced surveillance measures to safeguard market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in the affected securities, taking note of the trade-to-trade settlement rules, prohibition of netting off, and applicable deposit requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- Effective Date of Surveillance Action: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for securities placed under higher GSM stages may be impacted due to strict margin requirements, price bands, and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
