## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that specific companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of their non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Consequently, trading in the debentures of these companies will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260907-7, dated September 07, 2026.
- Category: Corporate Actions (Debt Segment).
- Companies involved: Regency Fincorp Limited, Achievers Finance India Limited, and Kanakadurga Finance Limited.
- Trading will proceed with reduced face values for the respective NCDs starting from the ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in listed non-convertible debentures will reflect reduced face values following part redemptions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the revised face values, record dates, and ex-dates for the respective debentures to ensure accurate settlement and trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 08/09/2026 (Regency Fincorp) and 10/09/2026 (Achievers Finance India & Kanakadurga Finance).
- Ex-Date: 08/09/2026 and 10/09/2026 respectively.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding the debentures will receive part redemption payouts and interest as applicable, with subsequent trading occurring on the reduced face value basis.
