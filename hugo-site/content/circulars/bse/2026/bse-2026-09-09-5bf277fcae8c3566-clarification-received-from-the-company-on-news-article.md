## Summary

BSE sought clarifications from multiple listed companies to verify news articles appearing in various media outlets and to inform investors regarding their accuracy. Replies were received from CSB Bank Ltd, Siemens Energy India Ltd, and IIFL Finance Ltd, while a reply remains awaited from IDBI Bank Ltd.

## Key Points

- BSE requested clarification from companies to verify news articles and ensure market transparency.
- Companies that submitted replies: CSB Bank Ltd, Siemens Energy India Ltd, and IIFL Finance Ltd.
- Company with reply awaited: IDBI Bank Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to clarify news items as requested by the exchange to prevent market speculation.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 09 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard disclosure procedure with minimal direct market impact apart from company-specific clarifications.
