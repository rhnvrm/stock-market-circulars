## Summary

ASK Mutual Fund has been officially activated on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective August 11, 2026. Transactions for NRI and minor investors are permitted under its schemes, subject to specific country exclusions for NRIs. Additionally, the ASK Liquid Fund - NFO scheme is enabled for order routing starting August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- ASK Mutual Fund activated on BSE StAR MF Platform from August 11, 2026.
- Transactions for NRI and minor investors are permitted, with specific NRI country restrictions detailed in the annexure.
- ASK Liquid Fund - NFO scheme enabled for order routing effective August 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the activation and adhere to the specified guidelines regarding NRI and minor investor transactions for ASK Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 11, 2026 (Platform Activation)
- **NFO Routing Date:** August 12, 2026 (ASK Liquid Fund)

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment product offerings available to members and investors on the BSE StAR MF platform.
