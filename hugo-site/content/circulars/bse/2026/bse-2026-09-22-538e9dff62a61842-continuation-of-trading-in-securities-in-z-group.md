## Summary

Pursuant to SEBI Master Circular provisions regarding penal actions for non-compliance, the BSE has announced the transfer and continuation of trading for specified scrips in the 'Z' group effective October 05, 2026, due to non-compliance with Regulation 27(2) (Submission of Corporate Governance Report) for two consecutive quarters (March 2026 and June 2026).

## Key Points

- Scrips affected: Kobo Biotech Ltd (531541) and Rajvir Industries Ltd (532665).
- Companies are already in the 'Z' group, and trades executed will continue to be settled on a Trade for Trade basis.
- Action taken under SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of penal actions and standard operating procedures for suspension and revocation of trading for non-compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure timely submission of Corporate Governance Reports under Regulation 27(2) to avoid penal actions and transfer to restrictive trading groups.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected scrips remains restricted to the 'Z' group on a Trade for Trade settlement basis, limiting liquidity and increasing settlement scrutiny for investors.
