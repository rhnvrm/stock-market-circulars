## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the criteria for inclusion will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 15, 2026, and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment. Certain securities are moving to lower stages, shifting to Stage II with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction, while others are moving out of the framework entirely.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting inclusion criteria attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 15, 2026, on all open positions as on September 11, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 15, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 11, 2026.
- Review of securities under the framework is conducted on a periodical basis.
- Shortlisting is strictly on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the entity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion and movement within the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Adjustment of price bands and settlement segments for listed scrips based on surveillance reviews.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate 100% margin provisions and adhere to Trade-for-Trade settlement requirements for impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Stage II movement & exiting securities:** September 11, 2026
- **Effective Date for 100% margin & Trade-for-Trade shift:** September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity for the affected scrips may be impacted due to the mandatory 100% margin requirement, Trade-for-Trade settlement, and stricter price bands or periodic call auction mechanisms.
