## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on their revised framework. The measures include 100% margins on open and new positions, movement to higher stages, and transfers to specific trading groups.

## Key Points

- 100% margins shall be applicable effective August 06, 2026, on all open positions as of August 05, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities are moved to their respective higher stages of the LT-ASM Framework effective August 04, 2026, alongside lower price bands.
- Certain securities continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective August 06, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM Framework effective August 04, 2026, with price bands reinstated.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of the revised LT-ASM framework as per historical guidelines and notices.
- Imposition of 100% margins and mandatory transfers to specific surveillance trading groups.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure adequate margin maintenance (100%) for securities placed under the LT-ASM framework starting August 06, 2026.
- Listed entities and market participants should review the annexures for specific scrip-wise actions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Band and Higher Stage Movements: August 04, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and surveillance placement may affect liquidity and trading activity for the securities listed under the LT-ASM framework.
