## Summary

BSE has announced that the equity shares of Acme Universal Safezone 9 Limited (Scrip Code: 544964) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Wednesday, October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Acme Universal Safezone 9 Limited
- Scrip Code: 544964
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026
- Segment: SME

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of trading category from Trade for Trade to normal Rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should take note of the segment change for order placement and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates regular trading and improves liquidity for investors in Acme Universal Safezone 9 Limited under the M Group.
