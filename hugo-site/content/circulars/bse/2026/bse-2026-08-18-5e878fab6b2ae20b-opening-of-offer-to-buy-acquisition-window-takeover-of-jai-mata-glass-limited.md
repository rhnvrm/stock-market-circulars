## Summary

An open offer is made by the acquirers (Mr. Ashwani Gulati, Kiran Gulati, and M/s Veerasha Trust) to the public shareholders of Jai Mata Glass Limited to acquire up to 2,60,00,000 fully paid equity shares (representing 26% of the paid-up equity share and voting capital) at a price of Rs. 1.85 per share.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Jai Mata Glass Limited (JMGL)
- **Acquirers:** Mr. Ashwani Gulati, Kiran Gulati, and M/s Veerasha Trust
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 1.85 per equity share payable in cash
- **Offer Size:** Up to 2,60,00,000 equity shares (26% of paid-up equity and voting capital)
- **Offer Period:** August 21, 2026 to September 4, 2026 (excluding SEBI Holiday on August 26, 2026)
- **Regulatory Framework:** Pursuant to Regulation 3(1) and Regulation 4 of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI circulars on the mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to Tender-Offers under Takeovers, Buy Back and Delisting.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members and Custodians must follow the revised guidelines for tender offers through the stock exchange acquisition window platform.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** August 21, 2026
- **SEBI Holiday:** August 26, 2026 (Excluding trading)
- **Offer Closing Date:** September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public shareholders of Jai Mata Glass Limited to tender their shares through the acquisition window on the BSE platform during the specified open offer dates.
