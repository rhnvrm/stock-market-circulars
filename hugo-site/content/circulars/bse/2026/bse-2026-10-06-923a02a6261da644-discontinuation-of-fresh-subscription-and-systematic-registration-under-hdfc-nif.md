## Summary

BSE has announced the discontinuation of fresh subscriptions, switch-in transactions, and new systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and STP-In) under the HDFC NIFTY SDL OCT 2026 INDEX FUND on the BSE StAR MF platform, pursuant to communication from HDFC Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Lumpsum investments (fresh and additional purchases), switch-ins, and new systematic registrations are disabled with immediate effect.
- Existing SIP/XSIP and STP-In registrations will be cancelled by the Exchange after business hours on October 13, 2026.
- Redemption, Switch-Out, STP-Out, and SWP transactions continue to be permitted without restrictions.
- Subscriptions and switch-in transactions (including fresh SIPs/STPs) received and triggered to RTA on or after October 05, 2026, will be rejected.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of fund-level restrictions as per the Addendum issued by HDFC Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors must note the disabled transaction types and inform investors accordingly.
- Ensure no fresh orders or systematic registrations are processed for the scheme.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** Immediate effect (Notice Date: October 06, 2026)
- **Cut-off for RTA trigger rejections:** October 05, 2026 onwards
- **Cancellation of existing SIP/XSIP/STP-In:** After business hours on October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding existing SIPs or planning fresh investments in HDFC Nifty SDL Oct 2026 Index Fund must alter their investment strategies. Redemptions and exits remain fully operational.
