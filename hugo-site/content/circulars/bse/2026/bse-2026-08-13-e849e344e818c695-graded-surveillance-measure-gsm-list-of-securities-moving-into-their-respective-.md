## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure of the notice will move to higher GSM stages.
- Applicable surveillance actions include 100% margin rates, price bands of 5% or lower, trade-for-trade settlement, Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD), and restricted trading frequencies depending on the stage.
- No netting off is allowed as settlement takes place on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures as per the provisions of the GSM Framework published on February 23, 2017.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Buyers are required to deposit the specified Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) and adhere to trade-to-trade settlement rules.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased liquidity constraints and margin requirements for the securities moving into higher GSM stages, affecting trading activity and price discovery.
