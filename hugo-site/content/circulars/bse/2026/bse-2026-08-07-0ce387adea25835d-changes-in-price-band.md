## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the revision of price bands for several scrips under the equity segment. The revised price bands take effect from August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260807-32, dated August 07, 2026.
- Department: DOSS (Department of Surveillance).
- Segment: Equity.
- Price bands have been revised to various levels (e.g., 2%, 5%, 10%, 20%) for multiple scrips including Sicagen India Ltd, Bridge Securities Ltd, Restaurant Brands Asia Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of price band limits for listed scrips to manage volatility and prevent excessive price movements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure their trading systems and terminals reflect the updated price bands for the affected scrips starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 07, 2026
- Effective Date: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and traders dealing in the affected scrips must note the revised daily operating price limits which restrict maximum price fluctuations during the trading sessions.
