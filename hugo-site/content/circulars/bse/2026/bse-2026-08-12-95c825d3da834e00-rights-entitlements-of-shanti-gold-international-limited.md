## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of SHANTI GOLD INTERNATIONAL LIMITED will be permitted on the Exchange with effect from Friday, August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Rights Entitlements (REs) of SHANTI GOLD INTERNATIONAL LIMITED
- **Scrip Code:** 751129
- **Scrip ID:** SHANTI-RE
- **ISIN No.:** INE06ZD20017
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Group:** R
- **Settlement Basis:** Trades will be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement and trade-for-trade basis.
- **Trading Form:** REs shall be tradable in dematerialized form only.
- **Disclaimer Obligation:** Trading members must include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes/bills for purchase of REs informing clients that purchasing REs only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them prior to the issue closing.

## Regulatory Changes

No major regulatory changes. Trading members are directed to refer to notice no. 20200305-21 dated March 05, 2020 and notice no. 20200518-34 dated May 18, 2020 for further procedural guidelines on Rights Entitlements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs, explicitly mentioning the ISIN of the RE to ensure it is not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Trading members must incorporate a prominent disclaimer in contract notes / bills issued for purchase of REs.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs on or before the Issue Closing Date (August 21, 2026) shall lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- **Trading Commencement Date (REs):** August 14, 2026
- **Trading Period for Renunciation:** Friday, August 14, 2026 to Tuesday, August 18, 2026
- **Rights Issue Opening Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Rights Issue Closing Date:** August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Facilitates a structured, liquid trading window for eligible shareholders of Shanti Gold International Limited to transact or acquire Rights Entitlements.
- **Operational Impact:** Trading members must configure their systems to handle trade-for-trade T+1 rolling settlement for Scrip ID `SHANTI-RE` (Scrip Code `751129`) and comply with the mandatory disclosure and disclaimer guidelines.
