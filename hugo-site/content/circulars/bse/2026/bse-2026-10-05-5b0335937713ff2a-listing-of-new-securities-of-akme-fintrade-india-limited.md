## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of Akme Fintrade (India) Limited (Scrip Code - 544200) with effect from Tuesday, October 6, 2026. A total of 1,00,00,000 equity shares have been issued at a premium of Rs. 6/- (issue price Rs. 7/- per share) to non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Akme Fintrade (India) Limited
- Scrip Code: 544200
- ISIN: INE916Y01027
- Number of Securities: 1,00,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 7/- per share (Face Value Rs. 1/- + Premium Rs. 6/-)
- Allotment Date: 29/06/2026
- Effective Date for Trading: October 6, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 429749961 to 439749960
- Lock-in Details: 1,00,00,000 shares locked-in up to 15/04/2027

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is a routine corporate listing notice for preferential allotment upon warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the lock-in period up to April 15, 2027, and the effective trading date of October 6, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: June 29, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: October 6, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 15, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up capital of Akme Fintrade (India) Limited due to warrant conversion. Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.
