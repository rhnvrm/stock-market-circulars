## Summary

The Exchange sought clarification from Steel Authority of India Ltd and ITI Ltd to verify news articles appearing across various media sources and ensure accuracy for investors.

## Key Points

- Clarification sought from Steel Authority of India Ltd (Scrip Code: 500113) regarding news on Moneycontrol; reply attached.
- Clarification sought from ITI Ltd (Scrip Code: 523610) regarding news on CNBC-TV18; reply awaited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must clarify media reports impacting market accuracy when requested by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Informational notice regarding corporate transparency and verification of news reports.
