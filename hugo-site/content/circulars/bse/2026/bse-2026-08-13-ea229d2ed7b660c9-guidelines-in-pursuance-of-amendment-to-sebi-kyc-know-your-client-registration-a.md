## Summary

BSE has issued guidelines pursuant to SEBI KRA regulations and circulars mandating that trading members ensure all client KYCs are uploaded and validated. Clients whose KYCs are 'On Hold' at KRAs for uploads between July 1, 2026, and July 31, 2026, will be barred from trading or squaring off open positions effective August 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- All regulated entities must block debit transactions, suspend trading accounts, and close/inactivate UCCs for investors reported as deceased.
- Trading members must ensure client KYCs are uploaded and statuses are either 'KYC Registered' or 'KYC Validated'.
- Clients with KYC 'On Hold' (both Aadhaar and Non-Aadhaar based OVD) uploaded from July 1, 2026, to July 31, 2026, cannot trade or square off open positions from August 29, 2026.
- Non-compliant PANs will be flagged by the Exchange as 'Not Permitted to Trade' effective August 29, 2026.
- Subsequent compliance will restore trading permissions on T+1 based on T day KRA updates.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of strict KRA validation requirements where non-validated or 'On Hold' client accounts face immediate trading and square-off restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must verify client KRA statuses, clear 'On Hold' KYC issues, and download the non-compliant client list provided via the BSE member portal (`EQ\TRANSACTION\AUG-2026\13-08-2026 Non_Validated_Clients_by_KRAs_Clgno_xxxx.TXT`).

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Effective Date of Trading Restrictions:** August 29, 2026
- **KYC Upload Period Covered:** July 1, 2026, to July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Clients with pending or 'On Hold' KYC validations will experience disruptions as they will be unable to execute trades or square off open positions until full validation is achieved.
