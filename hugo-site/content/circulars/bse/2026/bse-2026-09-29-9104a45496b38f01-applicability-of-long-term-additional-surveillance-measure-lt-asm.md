## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, review, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective October 05, 2026, 100% margins will apply to open positions and new positions for securities in Annexure I (part A).

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. October 05, 2026 on all open positions as on October 01, 2026 and new positions.
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM framework w.e.f. September 30, 2026 (Annexure I Part B) with lower price bands.
- Certain securities transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 05, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- Securities moving out of LT-ASM framework w.e.f. September 30, 2026 (Annexure II) with price bands reinstated.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework attached as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of stricter surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and group shifts for short-listed securities based on XBRL submissions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and adjust trading strategies for securities listed in the respective annexures.

## Important Dates

- September 30, 2026: Movement to higher stages / moving out of LT-ASM / lower price bands effective.
- October 01, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to margins.
- October 05, 2026: 100% margins and group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS Group) effective.

## Impact Assessment

High impact on liquidity, margin requirements, and price volatility for securities listed under the LT-ASM framework.
