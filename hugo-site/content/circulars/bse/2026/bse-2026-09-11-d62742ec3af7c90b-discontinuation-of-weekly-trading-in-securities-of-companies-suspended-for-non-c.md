## Summary

BSE has announced the discontinuation of weekly trading on a 'Trade for Trade' basis in the Z group for Konark Synthetic Ltd, effective September 29, 2026, following the completion of the mandated six-month period. Additionally, the circular notes that trading in the securities of Sharanam Infraproject and Trading Ltd remains prohibited/suspended as per earlier notices.

## Key Points

- Weekly trading on the first trading day of every week for Konark Synthetic Ltd concludes its mandated six-month period on September 28, 2026.
- Trading discontinuation for Konark Synthetic Ltd takes effect on September 29, 2026.
- Sharanam Infraproject and Trading Ltd remains suspended as per notice dated May 29, 2026.
- Actions are taken pursuant to SEBI Master Circular requirements regarding non-compliance with Regulation 33 (submission of financial results).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 and SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies must ensure timely submission of financial results under Regulation 33 of SEBI LODR Regulations to avoid suspension and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- September 11, 2026: Notice issuance date
- September 28, 2026: Completion of mandated six months of weekly trading
- September 29, 2026: Discontinuation of weekly trading for Konark Synthetic Ltd

## Impact Assessment

Affects liquidity and trading access for investors holding securities in the specified non-compliant companies.
