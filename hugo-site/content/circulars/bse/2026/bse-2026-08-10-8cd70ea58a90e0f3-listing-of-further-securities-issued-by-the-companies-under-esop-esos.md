## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 5 companies under their Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS). These securities become effective for trading from Tuesday, August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Companies Involved:**
  1. Aptus Value Housing Finance India Limited (25,000 shares, ISIN: INE852O01025, Scrip Code: 543335)
  2. AYM Syntex Limited (53,792 shares, ISIN: INE193B01039, Scrip Code: 508933)
  3. PG Electroplast Limited (850,700 shares, ISIN: INE457L01029, Scrip Code: 533581)
  4. Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited (308,642 shares, ISIN: INE155A01022, Scrip Code: 500570)
  5. Vaibhav Global Limited (265,555 shares, ISIN: INE884A01027, Scrip Code: 532156)
- **Lock-in Status:** 'NA' for all listed issuances in this notice.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing operation for employee stock options.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the new securities listed for trading.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact, representing routine capitalization changes from employee stock option exercises.
