## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of Equity Shares of Acme Universal Safezone 9 Limited on the SME platform effective Tuesday, October 6, 2026. A total of 19,093,420 equity shares (Face Value Rs. 10/- each) are listed under Scrip Code 544964 and ISIN INE1KUL01020.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Scrip Code:** 544964
- **Scrip ID:** ACMEUNIV
- **ISIN:** INE1KUL01020
- **Group:** 'MT' Group (Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days)
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 71/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 61/-)
- **Total Securities:** 19,093,420 Equity Shares
- **Market Lot:** 1,600 shares
- **Market Maker:** JSK Securities and Services Private Limited
- **Registrar:** Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

In terms of SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, the scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form.
- Minimum market lot is 1,600 equity shares.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 6, 2026.
- Lock-in period has been marked in the depository’s records for demat shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 1, 2026
- **Listing Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Financial Year:** 31st March every year

## Impact Assessment

Provides trading access and liquidity to investors of Acme Universal Safezone 9 Limited on the BSE SME platform while ensuring regulatory compliance via trade-for-trade monitoring during initial trading days.
