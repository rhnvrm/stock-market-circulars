## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Quanto Agroworld Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective Wednesday, October 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Quanto Agroworld Limited
- Scrip Code: 544932
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The trading category for the company's equity shares has been shifted following previous exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment change for execution and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date: October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for the scrip, facilitating easier trading and enhanced liquidity for market participants.
