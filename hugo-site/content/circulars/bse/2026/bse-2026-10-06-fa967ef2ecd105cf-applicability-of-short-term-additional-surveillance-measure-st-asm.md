## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and II for securities that have satisfied the inclusion criteria. Applicable surveillance actions, including enhanced margin requirements, take effect from October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable rate of margin of 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Securities included under ST-ASM Stage II will attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Enhanced margins apply to all open positions as on October 07, 2026, and new positions created from October 08, 2026 onwards.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II surveillance frameworks in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective October 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 06 Oct 2026
- Open Positions Reference Date: 07 Oct 2026
- Effective Date for Surveillance Actions: 08 Oct 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I and II will experience higher capital requirements due to increased margin rates (50% and 100% respectively), potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume in those scrips.
