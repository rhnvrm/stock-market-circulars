## Summary

BSE has announced the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Delisting) of ELPRO INTERNATIONAL LIMITED. The bidding window is scheduled to run from August 4, 2026, to August 10, 2026, under settlement number 2026097.

## Key Points

- Delisting Offer-to-Buy for ELPRO INTERNATIONAL LIMITED.
- Bidding Period: 04/08/2026 to 10/08/2026.
- Settlement Type: Delisting, Settlement No: 2026097.
- Obligations Download: 11/08/2026; Settlement Date: 12/08/2026.
- Deliveries must be made exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; standard delisting acquisition window settlement procedures apply as per revised guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must use Market Type '(Delisting)' and settlement number 2026097 in the depository system.
- CDSL early pay-in must be done directly by clients to the member/custodian early pay-in account.
- NSDL early pay-in securities must be earmarked/blocked in the client's account and transferred to the member's NSDL pool account.
- No EPN file upload is required by members and custodians.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: 04/08/2026
- Bid End Date: 10/08/2026
- Obligation Download Date: 11/08/2026
- Settlement Date: 12/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates the smooth execution and settlement of the delisting offer for Elpro International Limited for market participants and shareholders.
