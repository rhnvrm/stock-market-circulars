## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Shakti Polytarp Limited on the exchange's SME segment effective from Tuesday, September 22, 2026. The scrip will be placed in the 'MT' Group of Securities and will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shakti Polytarp Limited
- Scrip Code: 544933
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SHAKTIPOLY
- ISIN: INE05UY01016
- Total Securities: 1,71,28,000 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 1,71,28,000)
- Issue Price: Rs. 59/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-, Premium: Rs. 49/-)
- Market Lot: 2,000 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 18, 2026
- Market Maker: Prabhat Financial Services Limited
- Registrar: Skyline Financial Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be subject to Trade-for-Trade segment restrictions for 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Lock-in details and shareholding patterns as per Annexure I and Annexure II respectively.
- Necessary corporate actions executed for lock-in marking in depository records for demat shares.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 18, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) & Listing Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Shakti Polytarp Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with standard trade-for-trade surveillance measures for initial trading sessions.
