## Summary

Trading members of the exchange are informed that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Kanohar Electricals Limited, 50,11,424 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs. 632/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kanohar Electricals Limited
- Segment: Equity
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investors Portion)
- Number of Shares Subscribed: 50,11,424 equity shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 632/- per equity share
- Date of Notice: September 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants are to note the completion of the anchor investor allocation as per the enclosed company announcement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 07, 2026
- Announcement Date: September 07, 2026

Marks the successful completion of the anchor investor bidding phase for Kanohar Electricals Limited's public issue, setting the stage for further phases of the IPO.
