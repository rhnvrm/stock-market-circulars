## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members of the actual reduced face value following the part redemption of debentures by Lodha Developers Ltd.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lodha Developers Ltd
- Scrip Code 976923 (INE670K07307, LDL-Reset Rate-21-01-28-PVT): Reduced face value of Rs. 74,846/- per debenture.
- Scrip Code 977293 (INE670K07349, LDL-TBILLS-13-11-28-PVT): Reduced face value of Rs. 49,204/- per debenture.
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026 (DR-728/2026-2027).

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that the debentures are traded with the updated reduced face value effective from October 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members are advised to update their records and trading systems with the new reduced face values for the respective debenture tranches.
