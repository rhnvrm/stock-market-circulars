## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that new securities issued on a private placement basis (commercial papers) have been listed and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective October 19, 2026. The listing covers issuances from multiple companies including Sikka Ports & Terminals Ltd, Fedbank Financial Services Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Redington Limited, and Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited.

## Key Points

- Securities: Commercial Papers issued on a private placement basis.
- Trading Form: Dematerialised form under specific ISIN numbers.
- Denomination & Tick Size: Standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuances include:
  - Sikka Ports & Terminals Ltd: 23,000 CPs, ISIN INE941D14790, maturing 15/01/2027 (Rate: 6.70% p.a.)
  - Fedbank Financial Services Limited: 3,000 CPs, ISIN INE007N14FT2, maturing 14/12/2026 (Rate: 7.25% p.a.)
  - 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited: 500 CPs, ISIN INE0TQI14673, maturing 15/10/2027 (Rate: 8.25% p.a.)
  - Redington Limited: 4,000 CPs, ISIN INE891D14D84, maturing 04/12/2026 (Rate: 6.49% p.a.)
  - Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited: 1,700 CPs, ISIN INE918K14DY1, maturing 14/01/2027

## Regulatory Changes

Admission of privately placed debt securities to trading on the BSE debt segment in compliance with exchange bylaws and regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard demat trading norms, Rs. 5 Lakhs minimum denomination, and 1 paise tick size.

## Important Dates

- Allotment / Listing Date: October 16 / October 19, 2026
- Redemptions: Between December 2026 and October 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations reflecting corporate short-term debt fundraising via commercial papers.
