## Summary

SEBI has issued a final order dated August 25, 2026, in the matter of Varanium Cloud Limited (VCL), debarring several entities and individuals from accessing the securities market for varying periods ranging from 1 to 7 years due to regulatory violations.

## Key Points

- Varanium Cloud Limited and Mr. Harshawardhan Sabale are debarred from the securities market for 7 years.
- Athos Capital Advisors Private Limited and Mr. Jinesh Mehta are debarred for 2 years.
- Mr. Raj Jagtani (Proprietor of BM Traders) is debarred for 4 years.
- Mr. Vinayak Vasant Jadhav, Mr. Mukundan Raghavan, and Mr. Fahim Iunus Shaikh are debarred for 1 year.
- First Overseas Capital Limited is debarred for 2 years, running consecutively after its previous October 23, 2025 debarment.
- Proceedings against Ms. Hetal Somani and Mr. Kalpesh Anil Acharekar were disposed of without directions or monetary penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the debarment directions and ensure that the debarred entities and individuals are restricted from accessing or dealing in the securities market directly or indirectly.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Strict enforcement action ensuring market integrity by restricting non-compliant companies and intermediaries from participating in trading activities.
