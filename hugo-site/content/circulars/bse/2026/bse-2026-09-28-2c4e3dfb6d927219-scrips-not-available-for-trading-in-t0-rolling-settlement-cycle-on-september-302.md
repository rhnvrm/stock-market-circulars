## Summary

BSE has announced that WIPRO LIMITED (Scrip Code: 107685, Symbol: WIPRO#) will not be available for trading in the T+0 rolling settlement cycle on Wednesday, September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 107685
- Symbol: WIPRO#
- Security Name: WIPRO LIMITED
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note that the specified scrip is restricted from trading in the T+0 rolling settlement cycle on the mentioned date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact, affecting only participants trading the specified scrip in the T+0 settlement cycle on September 30, 2026.
