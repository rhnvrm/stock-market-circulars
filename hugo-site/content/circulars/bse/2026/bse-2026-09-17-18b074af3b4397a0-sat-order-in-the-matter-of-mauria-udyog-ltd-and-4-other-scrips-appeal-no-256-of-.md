## Summary

The Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT), via its order dated September 04, 2026, allowed the interim prayer in Appeal No. 256 of 2026 (relating to Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips), staying the impugned order subject to the deposit of the full penalty within four weeks. SEBI confirmed via email on September 17, 2026, that the appellant, Goenka Business and Finance Limited, has paid the penalty amount in favor of SEBI to be kept in an interest-bearing account.

## Key Points

- Continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260701-4 dated July 01, 2026.
- SAT order dated September 04, 2026, granted an interim stay on the impugned order.
- Stay is conditional upon the deposit of the full penalty amount within four weeks.
- SEBI confirmed on September 17, 2026, that Goenka Business and Finance Limited has successfully paid the required penalty amount.
- Penalty funds will be maintained in an interest-bearing account by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct new regulatory changes; this circular communicates the compliance status regarding a specific SAT interim order and penalty deposit.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the penalty deposit compliance by Goenka Business and Finance Limited pursuant to the SAT order and ensure ongoing adherence.

## Important Dates

- July 01, 2026: Previous Exchange Notice No. 20260701-4 issued.
- September 04, 2026: SAT order issued granting stay subject to penalty deposit.
- September 17, 2026: SEBI confirmation of penalty payment and date of current circular.

## Impact Assessment

Operational and market impact is limited to the concerned entities involved in Appeal No. 256 of 2026, ensuring transparency regarding the stay of the impugned order following penalty fulfillment.
