## Summary

Vistaar Financial Services Pvt. Ltd. has fixed a record date for the payment of interest and part redemption of its Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). Trading in the debentures will continue with a reduced face value effective from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vistaar Financial Services Pvt. Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 975474
- ISIN: INE016P07195
- Purpose: Payment of Interest and Part Redemption
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 16,666.67 per debenture
- Record Date: 20/08/2026
- Ex-Date: 20/08/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are requested to note the reduced face value and settlement details for trading in the company's debentures.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: August 20, 2026
- Ex-Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the NCDs will receive interest along with part redemption of the principal, and subsequent trading will be conducted at the reduced face value of Rs. 16,666.67 per debenture.
