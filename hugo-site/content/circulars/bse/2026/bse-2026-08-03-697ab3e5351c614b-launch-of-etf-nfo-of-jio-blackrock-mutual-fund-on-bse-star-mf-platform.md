## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the JIOBLACKROCK NIFTY 50 ETF and JIOBLACKROCK NIFTY 50 ETF - DIRECT PLAN under JIO BLACKROCK Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from August 04, 2026, to August 11, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund House: JIO BLACKROCK Mutual Fund
- Schemes: JIOBLACKROCK NIFTY 50 ETF and JIOBLACKROCK NIFTY 50 ETF - DIRECT PLAN (Equity category)
- NFO Period: August 04, 2026, to August 11, 2026 (extended till 6:00 PM on August 11, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 14, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on August 14, 2026, for eligibility.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted during the NFO period subject to clear funds receipt by the specified deadline.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: August 04, 2026
- NFO End Date: August 11, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 14, 2026
- Clear Funds Deadline: August 14, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates investors and trading members in participating in the new ETF offering from Jio Blackrock Mutual Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
