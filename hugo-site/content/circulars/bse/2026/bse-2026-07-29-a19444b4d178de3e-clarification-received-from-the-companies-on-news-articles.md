## Summary

BSE sought clarification from Go Digit General Insurance Ltd regarding news articles appearing in media. A reply has been received from the company. Additionally, a reply is awaited from Steel Authority of India Ltd for similar queries.

## Key Points

- Exchange sought clarification to verify accuracy of news articles appearing in various media.
- Clarification received from Go Digit General Insurance Ltd.
- Reply awaited from Steel Authority of India Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to clarify news reports when requested by the exchange to ensure accurate dissemination of information to investors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 29 July 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact; standard compliance procedure for verifying media reports.
