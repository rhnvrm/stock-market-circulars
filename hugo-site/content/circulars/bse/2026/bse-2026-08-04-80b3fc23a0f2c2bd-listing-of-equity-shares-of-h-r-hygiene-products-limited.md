## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, August 5, 2026, the equity shares of H. R. Hygiene Products Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Scrip ID on BOLT System: HRHYGIENE
- Scrip Code: 544848
- ISIN No.: INE17DP01015
- Market Lot: 1600 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 88/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 78/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 3, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form in accordance with SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on August 5, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action has been executed for marking lock-in periods in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Effective Listing Date: August 5, 2026
- Date of Allotment: August 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of H. R. Hygiene Products Limited shares on the BSE SME platform.
