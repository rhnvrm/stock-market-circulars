## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives segments on Saturday, August 29, 2026. Trading members using third-party platforms of empaneled vendors or in-house systems via ETI APIs are encouraged to participate to test applications under exceptional market conditions. The session will also test the new BOLT Pro TWS version 13.35.

## Key Points

- Scheduled for Saturday, August 29, 2026, for Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives segments.
- Release of new BOLT Pro TWS version 13.35.
- Conducted in two distinct sessions with designated log-in, continuous trading, and trade modification times.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 12:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively in the mock trading sessions and provide feedback by 12:00 PM via the provided contact numbers or email IDs.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** August 29, 2026
- **Feedback Deadline:** August 29, 2026, by 12:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Operational drill to ensure system robustness, software readiness (BOLT Pro TWS v13.35), and API connectivity for trading members. No financial impact or market risk.
