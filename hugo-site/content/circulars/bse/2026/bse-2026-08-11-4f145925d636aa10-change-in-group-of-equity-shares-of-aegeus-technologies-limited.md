## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Aegeus Technologies Limited are being shifted from the Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aegeus Technologies Limited
- Scrip Code: 544858
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Tuesday, August 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The trading category and segment classification for the company's equity shares have been updated following exchange procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the segment change for executing transactions from the effective date onwards.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Improves liquidity and trading convenience for investors as the shares move from the Trade for Trade segment to the normal rolling settlement segment.
