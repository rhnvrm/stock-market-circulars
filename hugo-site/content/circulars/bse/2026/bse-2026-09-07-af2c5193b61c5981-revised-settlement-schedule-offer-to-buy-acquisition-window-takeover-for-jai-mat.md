## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Jai Mata Glass Limited (Settlement No. 2026105). The bidding was conducted from August 21, 2026, to September 4, 2026, and the revised obligation download and settlement dates have been updated.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Jai Mata Glass Limited
- **Settlement Type & No.:** Takeover, Settlement No. 2026105
- **Original Bidding Period:** 21/08/2026 to 04/09/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download Date:** 09/09/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 10/09/2026
- **Demat Delivery:** Must be delivered strictly through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement No. 2026105.
- **Stamp Duty:** Members are required to refer to the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a schedule revision notice referring to existing guidelines for the acquisition of shares through the stock exchange mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must use the correct Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement number (2026105) when executing Early Pay-in of shares in the depository system.
- Members should review client-wise stamp duty charges via the designated settlement files.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 21/08/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 04/09/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download:** 09/09/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 10/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment affecting clearing and settlement timelines for participants involved in the Jai Mata Glass Limited takeover offer. No broader market impact.
