## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that Alan Scott Enterprises Limited has fixed a record date to determine partly paid equity shareholders eligible to make payment for the first and final call of Rs. 35/- per partly paid-up equity share (comprising Rs. 2.50/- towards face value and Rs. 32.50/- towards securities premium). Consequently, trading members are advised not to deal in the partly paid equity shares of the company from the specified record date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Alan Scott Enterprises Limited
- Scrip Code: 890239
- Record Date: 09/10/2026
- No Dealings From Date: 09/10/2026
- Call Details: First and Final Call of Rs. 35/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 2.50/-, Securities Premium: Rs. 32.50/-)
- Settlement Number: DR- 732/2026-2027

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the partly paid equity shares of Alan Scott Enterprises Limited starting from the record date (09/10/2026). Eligible partly paid shareholders must make payment of the first and final call as specified.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / No Dealings From Date: 09/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Directly impacts trading in partly paid equity shares (Scrip Code: 890239) of Alan Scott Enterprises Limited during the call payment process and ascertainment of eligible shareholders.
