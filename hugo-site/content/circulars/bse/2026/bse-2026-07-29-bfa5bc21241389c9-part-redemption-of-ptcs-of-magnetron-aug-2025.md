## Summary

Mitcon Credential Trusteeship Services Ltd has informed the Exchange regarding the reduced face value on partial redemption of Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) for Magnetron Aug 2025. The changes are effective from July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: MAGNETRON AUG 2025 (Scrip Code: 977078 / ISIN: INE2GPJ15012) - Reduced Face Value: Rs. 3327.15 per PTC.
- Security Name: MAGNETRON AUG 2025 (Scrip Code: 977079 / ISIN: INE2GPJ15020) - Reduced Face Value: Rs. 6586.05 per PTC.
- Effective Date: July 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the reduced face value for trading purposes.

- Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Trading members must execute trades using the updated reduced face values.
