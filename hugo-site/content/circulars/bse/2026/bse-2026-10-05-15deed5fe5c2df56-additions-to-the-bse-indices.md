## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of several newly listed companies to its indices following their listing on the mainboard and SME platforms, with effective dates on October 6 and October 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Orient Cables (India) Limited (544960), German Green Steel and Power Limited (544957), Runwal Enterprises Limited (544959), and Acevector Limited (544962) will be added to the BSE IPO index effective at the open of Wednesday, October 07, 2026.
- Dudani Retail Limited (544961) and Sai Urja Indo Ventures Limited (544958), listed on the SME platform, will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open of Tuesday, October 06, 2026.
- These actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements; informational notice regarding index constituents.

## Important Dates

- October 05, 2026: Notice issuance date.
- October 06, 2026: Effective date for SME index additions.
- October 07, 2026: Effective date for mainboard index additions.

## Impact Assessment

Routine index adjustment reflecting recent primary market listings on BSE and BSE SME.
