## Summary

Total Environment Constructions Private Limited has partially redeemed its debentures (Scrip Code: 975621, ISIN: INE257Y07049). Consequently, trading in these debentures will resume with a reduced face value.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Total Environment Constructions Private Limited
- Security Details: TECPL-13.53%-31-3-29-PVT (Scrip Code: 975621, ISIN: INE257Y07049)
- Purpose: Part redemption of debentures
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 78,571/- per debenture
- Effective Date for Trading with Reduced Face Value: September 08, 2025

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the reduced face value and resumption of trading for the mentioned debentures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 08, 2025

Operational update for trading members holding or trading the specified debentures of Total Environment Constructions Private Limited.
