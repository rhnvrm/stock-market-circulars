## Summary

Naturite Agro Products Ltd has informed the Exchange regarding the fixation of the Record Date for the sub-division of existing equity shares.

## Key Points

- Existing Face Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- Sub-divided Face Value: Rs. 5/- per share
- Ratio: One equity share of Rs. 10/- each into Two equity shares of Rs. 5/- each
- Record Date: September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised that the old ISIN No. INE223R01014 (Rs. 10/- paid up) will not be valid for transactions done on the Exchange on or after September 25, 2026. The new ISIN for the Rs. 5/- paid-up shares will be communicated separately.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders holding shares as of the record date will receive sub-divided shares, increasing liquidity and adjusting the market price per share accordingly.
