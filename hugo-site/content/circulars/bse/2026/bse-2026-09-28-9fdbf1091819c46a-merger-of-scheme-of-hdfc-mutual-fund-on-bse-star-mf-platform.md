## Summary

HDFC Mutual Fund has announced the merger of **HDFC NIFTY SDL OCT 2026 INDEX FUND** (Transferor Scheme) into **HDFC BANKING AND PSU DEBT FUND** (Transferee Scheme), effective after business hours on October 14, 2026. The BSE StAR MF platform has issued operational guidelines regarding transactions, exits without exit load, and SIP/STP/SWP migrations.

## Key Points

- **Transferor Scheme**: HDFC NIFTY SDL OCT 2026 INDEX FUND
- **Transferee Scheme**: HDFC BANKING AND PSU DEBT FUND
- **Effective Date**: After the close of business hours on October 14, 2026.
- **Exit Load Waiver**: Investors can redeem or switch out without exit load between September 11, 2026, and October 13, 2026.
- **Transaction Cut-offs**: Demat subscriptions accepted up to October 09, 2026; Non-demat subscriptions accepted up to October 13, 2026.
- **Systematic Registrations**: SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP will be automatically migrated to the transferee scheme or cancelled after business hours on October 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory policy change; standard corporate action and mutual fund scheme merger processing guidelines apply.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors must inform investors about the merger and the exit load waiver window.
- Investors wishing to opt out of the merger must submit redemption or switch requests within the specified timelines.
- Systematic investment/withdrawal registrations must be reviewed, and cancellation requests must be filed if investors do not wish to continue post-merger.

## Important Dates

- **Exit Load Free Redemption/Switch Window**: September 11, 2026 to October 13, 2026
- **Subscription Deadline (Demat)**: October 09, 2026
- **Subscription Deadline (Non-Demat)**: October 13, 2026
- **Cease Transaction Processing**: After close of business hours on October 13, 2026
- **Effective Date of Merger**: After close of business hours on October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding units in the transferor scheme must review their investment objectives and decide whether to accept the merger into the debt fund or exit without load. Platform members must ensure adherence to cut-off dates for orders and manage systematic facility migrations smoothly.
