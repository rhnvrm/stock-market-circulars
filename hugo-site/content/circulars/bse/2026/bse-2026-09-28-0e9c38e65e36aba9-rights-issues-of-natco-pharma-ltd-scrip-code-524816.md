## Summary

Natco Pharma Ltd. has announced the record date of October 1, 2026, for its upcoming rights issue of equity shares. Trading in the company's equity shares will commence on an ex-rights basis from October 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Natco Pharma Ltd.
- Scrip Code (BSE): 524816
- Record Date: October 1, 2026
- Ex-Rights Basis Date: October 1, 2026
- Rights Ratio: 2 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each for cash at a premium of Rs. 748/- per share for every 21 equity shares held.

## Regulatory Changes

Standard corporate action notification in accordance with BSE listing operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the record date and ensure trades are executed on an ex-rights basis effective October 1, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Record Date: October 1, 2026
- Ex-Rights Date: October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts existing shareholders regarding rights entitlements and adjusts equity trading price on ex-rights basis.
