## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of 1,81,10,473 new equity shares of Inox Green Energy Services Ltd (Scrip Code: 543667, ISIN: INE510W01014) issued pursuant to a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP).

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 1,81,10,473 Equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each.
- **Allottees:** Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) pursuant to QIP.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 165.65/- per share.
- **Date of Allotment:** September 29, 2026.
- **Effective Date:** Monday, October 05, 2026.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 401492046 to 419602518.
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing approval for newly allotted securities under QIP.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the addition of the new equity shares to trading and ensure proper clearing and settlement procedures.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 29, 2026
- **Notice Date:** October 01, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing of QIP shares expands the equity base of Inox Green Energy Services Ltd, enhancing liquidity and trading volume while expanding institutional shareholding.
