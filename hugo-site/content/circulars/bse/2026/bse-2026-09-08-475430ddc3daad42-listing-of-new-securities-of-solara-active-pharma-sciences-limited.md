## Summary

 BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 2,75,705 new equity shares of Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited (Scrip Code: 541540) resulting from the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis, effective from September 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited
- Scrip Code: 541540
- ISIN: INE624Z01016
- Number of Shares: 2,75,705 equity shares
- Effective Date: Wednesday, September 9, 2026
- Date of Allotment: 19/08/2026
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the existing old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are to note the addition of the new securities to trading on the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 19, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: September 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update reflecting the capitalization of partly paid shares into fully paid-up equity shares for the company.
