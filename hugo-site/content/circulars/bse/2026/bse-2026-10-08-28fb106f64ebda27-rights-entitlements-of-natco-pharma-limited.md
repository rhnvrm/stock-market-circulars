## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of NATCO PHARMA LIMITED are permitted on the exchange with effect from Monday, October 12, 2026. The trading period for renunciation is scheduled from October 12, 2026, to October 16, 2026, while the underlying rights issue will remain open from October 12, 2026, to October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: NATCO PHARMA LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751145
- Scrip ID: NATCO-RE
- ISIN No.: INE987B20018
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: October 12, 2026 to October 16, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: October 12, 2026 to October 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs, highlighting the ISIN and clarifying that REs should not be mistaken for ordinary shares. Members must include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes or bills informing clients that purchasing REs only grants the right to participate in the rights issue by applying with the requisite money or renouncing before closure. Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the issue closing date.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Period Start: October 12, 2026
- RE Trading Period End: October 16, 2026
- Rights Issue Opening Date: October 12, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trades in Rights Entitlements will be settled on a T+1 rolling settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only. This provides existing shareholders of Natco Pharma Limited with the flexibility to either renounce their entitlements or subscribe to the rights issue.
