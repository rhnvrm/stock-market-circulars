## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding modifications in the Pre-open Call Auction Session in the Equity Segment, in partial modification to the Master Circular for the Equity segment, following SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Pre-open session duration is 15 minutes from 9:00 am to 9:15 am.
- Order entry period, order matching period, and buffer period timings have been specified.
- Market orders cannot be placed between 09:05 am and 09:10 am.
- Market orders are given priority over limit orders during matching.

## Regulatory Changes

- Alignment of the existing pre-open session framework with the Closing Auction Session (CAS) framework.
- Revision of session timings and introduction of specific restrictions on market orders during the order entry period.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised pre-open session timings, allowed order types, execution priorities, and dissemination requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 03 Aug 2026
- Reference SEBI Circular Date: January 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants and trading members need to update their trading systems and workflows to accommodate the revised pre-open session rules and timings.
