## Summary

BSE has announced necessary adjustments to all available Futures and Options (F&O) contracts on the underlying security Indus Towers Ltd (Derivative Asset Code – INFR, Equity Scrip Code - 534816) following the company's declaration of a dividend of Rs. 14.00 per equity share for the financial year 2026-27.

## Key Points

- **Record Date:** August 10, 2026, for the payment of dividend at Rs. 14.00/- per equity share.
- **Ex-Date:** August 10, 2026.
- **Effective Date for Adjustments:** August 07, 2026.
- **Options Contracts Adjustment:** The full dividend value of Rs. 14.00/- will be deducted from all strike prices generated at the end of August 07, 2026.
- **Futures Contracts Adjustment:** The adjusted futures price will be based on the daily mark-to-market settlement price minus the dividend amount (Rs. 14.00/-).

## Regulatory Changes

- In pursuance of Exchange notice number 20180710-26 and SEBI Master circular No SEBI/HO/MRD/DP/CIR/P/2016/135 dated December 16, 2016.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members of the Equity Derivatives Segment must take note of the strike price and base rate adjustments for F&O contracts on Indus Towers Ltd.

## Important Dates

- **Adjustment Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Ex-Date / Record Date:** August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Position holders in F&O contracts of Indus Towers Ltd will see corresponding price and strike adjustments to account for the dividend payout.
