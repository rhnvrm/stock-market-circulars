## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 20 scrips under the equity segment. These changes become effective from August 05, 2026, altering the maximum allowable daily price movement for the specified securities.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 20 companies with varying limits ranging from 2% to 20%.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is August 05, 2026.
- Affected scrips include Jumbo Bag Ltd, Best Agrolife Ltd, Kesoram Industries Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of price band percentages for specific scrips as part of routine surveillance and risk management.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure their trading systems and terminals incorporate the updated price bands effective August 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 04, 2026
- Effective Date: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in the affected scrips must adjust order placement parameters to comply with the new price limits.
