## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) pursuant to the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective from September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are subjected to additional surveillance measures effective September 16, 2026.
- Review of securities under the framework is conducted on a periodical basis.
- Lists of securities moving into, moving out of, and remaining under the IBC framework are provided in the respective annexures.
- Securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% are listed in Annexure IV.
- The price band of a scrip moving out of the framework shall be reinstated to its original level, subject to no other prevailing surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures in conjunction with existing exchange rules for companies undergoing insolvency proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the list of affected securities and ensure compliance with the applicable price bands and trading restrictions under the IBC surveillance framework.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in securities under the IBC framework is subject to specific surveillance controls and price band restrictions, affecting market operations for the listed scrips.
