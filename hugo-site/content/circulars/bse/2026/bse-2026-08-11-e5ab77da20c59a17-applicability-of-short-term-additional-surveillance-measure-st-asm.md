## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. Effective August 13, 2026, increased margin requirements apply to all open and new positions for the shortlisted securities.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- Stage I applicability: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Stage II applicability: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Effective date for surveillance measures: August 13, 2026, applicable on all open positions as on August 12, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and implementation of ST-ASM framework actions in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure proper margin maintenance (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for all open and new positions in the specified securities starting August 13, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 11 August 2026
- Effective Date: 13 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin obligations for traders holding or taking positions in securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I and II, potentially affecting liquidity and trading volumes for those scrips.
