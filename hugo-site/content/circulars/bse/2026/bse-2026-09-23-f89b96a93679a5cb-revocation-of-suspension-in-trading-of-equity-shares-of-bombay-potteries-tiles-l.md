## Summary

BSE has announced the revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares of Bombay Potteries & Tiles Ltd (Scrip Code: 502216) effective October 01, 2026. Pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026, trading will resume in the "XT" group.

## Key Points

- Trading in equity shares of Bombay Potteries & Tiles Ltd resumes effective October 01, 2026.
- Securities will be traded in the "XT" group.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) for IPOs and Relisted Scrips on October 01, 2026.
- Information Memorandum will be available on the Exchange’s website under Corporates → Corporate Filings → Information Memorandum → Revocation.
- Company profile is attached as Annexure I to the notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- Resumption governed pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to take note of the resumption of trading and participation in the Special Pre-open Session.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Revocation: October 01, 2026
- Notice Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members can resume trading in the equity shares of Bombay Potteries & Tiles Ltd starting October 01, 2026, under the XT group with pre-open session mechanisms.
