## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of newly listed companies, National Stock Exchange of India Limited (Ticker: 544937) and Sonaselection India Limited (Ticker: 544938), to the BSE IPO index effective from the open of Monday, September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- National Stock Exchange of India Limited (544937) and Sonaselection India Limited (544938) listed on BSE on September 24, 2026.
- Both stocks will be included in the BSE IPO index starting September 28, 2026.
- Corporate actions have been updated in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard index inclusion notice.

## Compliance Requirements

No specific compliance requirements for market participants other than noting the index additions.

## Important Dates

- **Listing Date:** September 24, 2026
- **Index Effective Date:** September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard index rebalancing and inclusion impact for benchmark tracking funds.
