## Summary

JSW Dulux Ltd has fixed October 22, 2026, as the Record Date for the sub-division of its equity shares from a face value of Rs. 10/- each into ten equity shares of Re. 1/- each.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: JSW Dulux Ltd (500710)
- Record Date: October 22, 2026
- Sub-Division Ratio: Existing 1 Equity Share of Rs. 10/- each into 10 Equity Shares of Re. 1/- each
- Old ISIN INE133A01011 will be invalid for transactions on or after October 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Corporate action execution for capital restructuring via share sub-division.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must ensure compliance with the record date and note that the old ISIN becomes invalid from October 22, 2026 onwards.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Record Date & Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders will hold 10 shares of Re. 1/- each for every 1 share of Rs. 10/- held previously, resulting in proportionate market price adjustments.
