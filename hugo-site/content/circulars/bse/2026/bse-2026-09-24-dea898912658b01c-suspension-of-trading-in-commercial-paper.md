## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CPs) of several companies due to upcoming record dates set for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of the specified companies from the respective ex-dates and record dates.
- Companies involved include Chennai Petroleum Corporation Ltd., 360 ONE Prime Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Capri Global Capital Limited, Time Technoplast Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Ashoka Buildcon Limited, Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited, The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd., ECL Finance Limited, Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd., and Muthoot Mcred Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure zero trading activity in the listed commercial papers as of the specified record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: September 29, 2026, to October 7, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for commercial paper redemptions ensuring orderly settlement.
