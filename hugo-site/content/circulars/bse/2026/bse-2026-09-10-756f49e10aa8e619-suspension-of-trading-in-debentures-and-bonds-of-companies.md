## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the purpose of payment of interest or redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading members are advised not to deal in these debentures/bonds of the respective companies as per the specified schedule.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260910-5
- Department: Listing Operations
- Segment: Debt
- Purpose: Record date for payment of interest / redemption of NCDs/Bonds.
- Affected Issuers include Muthoot Mercantile Limited, Vivriti Capital Limited, Varthana Finance Private Limited, Manba Finance Limited, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, Yes Bank Ltd., Tata Steel Limited, and Svatantra Microfin Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action notice regarding debt suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures and bonds of the listed companies during the effective restriction period around the record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10/09/2026
- Ex-Dates: 11/09/2026, 15/09/2026
- Record Dates: 12/09/2026, 13/09/2026, 15/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of trading in specified debt instruments to facilitate smooth processing of interest payments and redemptions for holders.
