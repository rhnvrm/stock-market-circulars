## Summary

BSE Ltd. has announced the admission of new applicants as Trading Members and approved additional segments for existing members in accordance with the Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations of the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Goenka Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 3313) admitted for Cash and Equity Derivatives.
- NDB Securities Private Limited (Member No. 3314) admitted for Cash and Equity Derivatives.
- Money Logix Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Member No. 3246) granted additional segment membership for Equity Derivatives.
- Butterfly Broking Private Limited (Member No. 6838) granted additional segment membership for Execution Only Platform (EOP).
- Alpha Matrix Securities Private Limited (Member No. 6879) granted additional segment membership for Commodity Derivatives.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine administrative admission of trading members.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members requiring clarification may contact membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 08 Oct 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of trading membership on the exchange with no direct impact on listed securities or market prices.
