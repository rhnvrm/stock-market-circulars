## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the client margin reporting schedule along with respective penalty dates for October 2026 for the Commodity Derivatives Segment.

## Key Points

- Details trade dates, last dates of margin reporting, and penalty dates for the entire month of October 2026.
- Applies specifically to members operating in the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Serves as a standard operational calendar to ensure timely margin compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine schedule notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading and clearing members must submit client margin reports by the specified last date for each respective trade date in October 2026 to avoid incurring financial penalties on the designated penalty dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 29-09-2026
- Schedule Period: Trade dates from 01-10-2026 through 30-10-2026, with margin reporting deadlines and penalty dates extending through November 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Negligible direct market impact; routine calendar notice for operational compliance in commodity derivatives.
