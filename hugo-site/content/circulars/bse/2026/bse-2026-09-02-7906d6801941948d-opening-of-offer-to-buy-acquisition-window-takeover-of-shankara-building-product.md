## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the opening of the offer to buy (acquisition window) for an open offer to acquire up to 63,04,825 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 26.00% of the paid-up equity share capital) of Shankara Building Products Limited at an offer price of INR 150/- per share in cash.

## Key Points

- **Acquirer & PACs:** The Ballygunge Family Trust (Acquirer) along with Mr. Sukumar Srinivas (PAC 1), Ms. Parwathi Srikanth Mirlay (PAC 2), Mr. Dhananjay Mirlay Srinivas (PAC 3), and Shankara Holdings Private Limited (PAC 4). 
- **Target Company:** Shankara Building Products Limited.
- **Offer Price:** INR 150/- per fully paid-up equity share payable in cash.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 63,04,825 equity shares (26.00% of paid-up equity share capital).
- **Offer Period:** From Monday, September 07, 2026, to Monday, September 21, 2026 (excluding SEBI holiday on Monday, September 14, 2026).
- **Designated Stock Exchange:** BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted pursuant to SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 and subsequent amendments, following the revised guidelines of SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the acquisition window mechanism and tender-offer timelines via the stock exchange platform in accordance with the Letter of Offer.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 02, 2026
- **Offer Opening Date:** September 07, 2026
- **SEBI Holiday (Exclusion):** September 14, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Public shareholders of Shankara Building Products Limited can tender their shares through the acquisition window on the designated stock exchange during the specified offer period.
