## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released the trading and settlement calendar for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment for the period from November 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026, detailing trading dates, pay-in/pay-out dates, and auction schedules.

## Key Points

- Outlines settlement numbers ranging from DR-154/2026-2027 to DR-175/2026-2027 for November 2026.
- Specifies depository pay-in/pay-out timelines, with members required to submit pay-in instructions to depositories/banks by 03:00 p.m. Funds and securities pay-out are effected by 04:00 p.m.
- Details auction settlement schedules (RA-154 to RA-175), including auction offer submissions and auction pay-in/pay-out timings (auction pay-in at 09:00 a.m., instructions by 08:50 a.m.).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must strictly adhere to the specified pay-in and pay-out deadlines for funds, securities, and auction obligations.

## Important Dates

- Schedule Period: 01.11.2026 to 30.11.2026
- First Settlement (DR-154): Trading Date 02-11-2026, Pay-in/Pay-out 03-11-2026
- Last Settlement (DR-175): Trading Date 30-11-2026, Pay-in/Pay-out 01-12-2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational calendar update ensuring smooth settlement cycles and transparency for market participants trading in the EGR segment.
