## Summary

BSE has issued a clarification regarding the implementation of "@valid" UPI IDs for investor-facing bank accounts maintained by Investment Advisers, in continuation of earlier SEBI and Exchange circulars. Investment Advisers must link all investor-facing bank accounts used for receiving funds with a "@valid" UPI ID to ensure verifiability through SEBI Check.

## Key Points

- Clarification provided by SEBI on August 07, 2026, regarding "@valid" UPI IDs for Investment Advisers.
- All investor-facing bank accounts for receiving funds from investors must be linked with a "@valid" UPI ID.
- Requests for a "@valid" UPI handle must be submitted through the BSE portal.
- Compliance must be achieved within T+60 days (where T is August 07, 2026, i.e., by October 06, 2026).
- Any new investor-facing bank accounts opened hereafter must also be mandatorily linked to a "@valid" UPI ID.

## Regulatory Changes

Adoption of Standardised, Validated, and Exclusive UPI IDs for payment collection by Investment Advisers from investors, verifiable via SEBI Check.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must apply for a "@valid" UPI handle via the BSE portal (https://iaraportal.sebi.gov.in/ → UPI Request Handle).
- Upon receiving the UTN, approach respective banks to complete documentation and obtain the handle.
- Ensure all existing and new investor-facing bank accounts are linked to the "@valid" UPI ID.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Start Date (T): August 07, 2026
- Compliance Deadline: October 06, 2026 (T+60 days)

## Impact Assessment

Investment Advisers need to update their banking and payment collection infrastructure to incorporate validated UPI IDs, ensuring regulatory compliance and seamless fund verification through SEBI Check.
