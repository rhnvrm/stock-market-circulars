## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction regarding Rolling Settlement No. DR-685/2026-27. The circular outlines the precise session timings for offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and final reports for August 04, 2026, along with pay-in schedules for August 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on Tuesday, Aug 04, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction will be held on Wednesday, Aug 05, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- Restriction on trading members on offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is now removed following the implementation of Uniform Membership structure in Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of trading member restrictions on offering securities in auction for settlements where short delivery occurred.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Extreme care must be taken while punching securities code and rate as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 04, 2026
- Pay-in Date: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth settlement operations and provides clear operational timelines for trading members participating in Demat Auction 685.
