## Summary

Hilton Metal Forging Limited has announced an extension of the closing date for its ongoing rights issue of 1,67,70,000 equity shares. The closing date has been extended from March 13, 2026, to April 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- The Board of Directors approved the extension during their meeting on March 13, 2026.
- The rights issue opening date remains unchanged (March 06, 2026).
- The revised closing date is April 02, 2026.
- Trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) has already been suspended and will not be reopened or extended.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

The intimation complies with the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.

## Important Dates

- Rights Issue Opening Date: March 06, 2026
- Original Closing Date: March 13, 2026
- Revised Closing Date: April 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Eligible shareholders have additional time to subscribe to the rights issue, though existing RE trading restrictions remain strictly in place.
