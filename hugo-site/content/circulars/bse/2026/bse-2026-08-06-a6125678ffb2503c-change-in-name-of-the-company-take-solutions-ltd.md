## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members that Take Solutions Ltd has changed its name to TAKE Limited. Consequently, the Scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Existing Name:** Take Solutions Ltd
- **New Name:** TAKE Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 532890
- **Effective Date:** August 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a corporate identity and system symbol update.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their internal systems to reflect the new company name, Scrip ID, and abbreviated name for scrip code 532890.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 06, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating trading terminals and reference records with the new name and abbreviated identifiers.
