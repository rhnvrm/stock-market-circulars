## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of 10,845 new equity shares of AvenuesAI Ltd. (Scrip Code: 539807) resulting from the conversion of partly paid-up shares into fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis. These shares become effective for trading from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** AvenuesAI Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 539807
- **ISIN:** INE483S01020
- **Securities Details:** 10,845 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up on a rights basis.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 3471667118 - 3471677962 (10,845 shares).
- **Ranking:** Pari-passu with the existing old equity shares of the company.
- **Effective Date:** Monday, October 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational listing notice for equity share conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities effective from the specified date.

- **Notice Date:** October 9, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026

Standard capital structure adjustment for AvenuesAI Ltd. with minimal direct market impact.
