## Summary

Namberdar Bio Fuels Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with the stock exchange under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations for its upcoming SME IPO. The document is hosted on the SME website to seek public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Namberdar Bio Fuels Limited
- Filing Date: August 03, 2026
- Notice Date: August 04, 2026
- Regulatory Framework: Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations
- Public Comment Window: 21 days from the date of the notice

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments on the Draft Offer Document should be submitted via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: August 03, 2026
- Notice Date: August 04, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites public feedback on the proposed SME IPO of Namberdar Bio Fuels Limited before final issuance.
