## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of scrips in the Trade-to-Trade segment as part of preventive surveillance measures based on the extant SEBI framework, effective October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria I, II, and III will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 01, 2026.
- Securities in Annexure II will continue in T / XT / MT / TS / P / Z / ZP Group with a 5% price band.
- Settlement will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off positions allowed.
- A VAR Margin of 100% will be levied on these scrips.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures transferring eligible securities to the Trade-to-Trade settlement segment in accordance with the SEBI framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate precaution while trading in these securities, as settlement occurs on a trade-to-trade basis without position netting off, and a 100% VAR margin applies.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for affected scrips may be constrained due to trade-to-trade settlement restrictions, absence of position netting, and 100% VAR margin requirements.
