## Summary

InCred Financial Services Limited has fixed a record date for the payment of interest and redemption of certain non-convertible debentures. Consequently, trading members are advised not to deal in these debentures starting from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: InCred Financial Services Limited
- Scrip Code: 975481
- Scrip ID: ICFSL-NIFTY50-12-10-26-PVT
- ISIN: INE321N07392
- Record Date: 27/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 25/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of the company as mentioned under the suspension notice.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: 25/09/2026
- Record Date: 27/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of trading in the specified non-convertible debentures of InCred Financial Services Limited for interest payment and redemption processing.
