## Summary

Transport Corporation of India Limited has announced a buyback of up to 1,562,500 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 2.03% of total paid-up equity shares as of March 31, 2026) at a price of Rs. 960 per equity share, aggregating to a maximum amount not exceeding Rs. 150 crore. The buyback will be executed through the 'Tender Offer' route on the stock exchange acquisition window.

## Key Points

- **Buyback Size:** Up to 1,562,500 equity shares (2.03% of total paid-up equity capital as of March 31, 2026).
- **Buyback Price:** Rs. 960/- per equity share payable in cash.
- **Total Maximum Amount:** Not exceeding Rs. 1,500,000,000 (Rs. 150 Crore).
- **Record Date:** Friday, October 9, 2026.
- **Buyback Period:** Thursday, October 15, 2026, to Thursday, October 22, 2026 (Excluding SEBI Holiday on Tuesday, October 20, 2026).
- **Regulatory Framework:** Conducted pursuant to SEBI Buyback Regulations and relevant stock exchange circulars.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; the transaction follows existing guidelines and framework for tender-offer buybacks through stock exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must follow established SEBI and stock exchange guidelines for participation in tender-offer buybacks.
- Eligible shareholders as of the record date may tender their shares through the designated acquisition window.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** October 9, 2026
- **Buyback Opening Date:** October 15, 2026
- **Buyback Closing Date:** October 22, 2026 (Excluding October 20, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Provides efficient capital return and liquidity to eligible shareholders of Transport Corporation of India Limited through the structured tender offer mechanism.
