## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Delisting) for Nitin Castings Limited. The bidding window is scheduled to run from August 5, 2026, to August 11, 2026, with settlement concluding on August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Nitin Castings Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Delisting
- **Settlement Number:** 2026098
- **Bid Start Date:** 05/08/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 11/08/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 19/08/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 20/08/2026

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered strictly through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL and NSDL).
- Members and custodians must use Market Type "(Delisting)" and Settlement No. 2026098 in the depository system.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Participants must ensure early pay-in is reflected in BSE/ICCL systems before the prescribed timelines for accepting/confirming bids on the BSE OTB platform.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Period:** August 5, 2026 – August 11, 2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** August 19, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Members, custodians, and shareholders participating in the delisting offer of Nitin Castings Limited must adhere to the specified early pay-in timelines and settlement schedules to ensure smooth execution of transactions.
