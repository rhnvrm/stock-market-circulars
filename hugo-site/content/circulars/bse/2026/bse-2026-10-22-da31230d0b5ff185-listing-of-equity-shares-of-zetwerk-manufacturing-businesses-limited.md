## Summary

Effective from Friday, October 23, 2026, the equity shares of Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited will be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities. The public issue price was Rs. 151/- per share (Face Value Rs. 1/- and premium Rs. 150/-).

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 545000
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: ZETWERK
- ISIN No.: INE09PO01019
- Total Securities Listed: 1,69,42,54,778 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 1694254778).
- Market Lot: 1
- Date of Allotment in Public Issue: October 21, 2026.
- Registrar to the Issue: KFin Technologies Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None specific to regulations; standard exchange listing notification complying with SEBI dematerialised trading guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will only be traded in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 23, 2026.
- Necessary corporate actions have been executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 21, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Zetwerk Manufacturing Businesses Limited shares on the BSE, providing liquidity to investors and expanding market participation in the 'B' Group segment.
