## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 10,00,000 new equity shares of Raj Oil Mills Limited (Scrip Code: 533093) issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from October 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 10,00,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 36/- per share (total issue price Rs. 46/- per share).
- **Allotment & ISIN:** Allotted on July 27, 2026 under ISIN INE294G01026.
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Lock-in Details:** Specific lock-in periods apply to various tranches of shares up to April 2027 (ranging between 24/04/2027 and 25/04/2027).

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants should take note of the listing and lock-in restrictions for the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** July 27, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** April 24, 2027 and April 25, 2027

## Impact Assessment

The listing of preferential shares increases the paid-up capital base of Raj Oil Mills Limited, with specific tranches subject to statutory lock-in periods.
