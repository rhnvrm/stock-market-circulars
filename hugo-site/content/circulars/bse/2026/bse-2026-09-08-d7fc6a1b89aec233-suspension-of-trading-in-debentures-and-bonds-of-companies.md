## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of several companies following the fixation of record dates for interest payment and redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures and bonds of the listed companies from the respective record/ex-dates.
- Affected companies include Power Finance Corporation Ltd, REC Limited, Shriram Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Capital Ltd, and others.
- The purpose of the record dates is payment of interest and/or redemption of the debentures/bonds.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard trading suspension pursuant to corporate actions (interest payment/redemption) for debt instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no dealings take place in the specified scrips during the suspension period.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates and Ex-Dates range from September 9, 2026, to September 20, 2026, depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

- Temporary restriction on secondary market trading for the specified debt securities to facilitate accurate beneficiary identification for interest payments and redemptions.
