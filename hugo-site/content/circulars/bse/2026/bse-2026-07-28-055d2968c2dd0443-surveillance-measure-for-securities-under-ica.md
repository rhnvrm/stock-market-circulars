## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding surveillance measures applicable to securities undergoing processes under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (ICA).

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260728-36
- Department: DOSS
- Segment: Equity
- Subject: Surveillance Measure for securities under ICA
- Attachments include ANNEXURE_ICA detailing the specific measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of targeted surveillance frameworks for securities linked to ICA proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and intermediaries must adhere to the trading and surveillance guidelines specified in the annexure for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 Jul 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts trading accessibility and monitoring levels for securities undergoing insolvency or related corporate restructuring.
