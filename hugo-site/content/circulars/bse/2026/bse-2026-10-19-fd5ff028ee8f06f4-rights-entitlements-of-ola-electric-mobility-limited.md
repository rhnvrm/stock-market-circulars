## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of OLA ELECTRIC MOBILITY LIMITED are permitted on the Exchange with effect from October 22, 2026. The RE trading period runs from October 22, 2026, to October 26, 2026, and the Rights Issue opens on October 22, 2026, closing on October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: OLA ELECTRIC MOBILITY LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751146
- Scrip ID: OLAELEC-RE
- ISIN No.: INE0LXG20016
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- RE Trading Period: October 22, 2026 to October 26, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: October 22, 2026 to October 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation is enabled on the Exchange under Group R with specific operational guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- REs shall be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.
- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of RE trading and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes must include a prominent disclaimer stating that RE purchase only grants the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce before the closing date.
- REs neither subscribed nor renounced on or before the Issue Closing Date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- RE Trading Commencement: October 22, 2026
- RE Trading Closure: October 26, 2026
- Rights Issue Opening: October 22, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding or trading REs of OLA ELECTRIC MOBILITY LIMITED must adhere to the strict demat-only and T+1 trade-for-trade settlement rules, ensuring timely action before the renunciation and issue closing dates.
