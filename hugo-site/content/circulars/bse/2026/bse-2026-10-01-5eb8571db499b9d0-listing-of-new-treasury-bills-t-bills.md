## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under the G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS with effect from Monday, October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three new Treasury Bills are admitted for trading on the Exchange.
- Scrip codes: 805274 (91TB311226, Maturity: 31/12/2026), 805275 (182TB01427, Maturity: 01/04/2027), and 805276 (364TB30927, Maturity: 30/09/2027).
- Market lot for all listed T-Bills is 1.
- Securities will not be available for trading two working days prior to the maturity/redemption date.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing of government debt instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the trading restrictions two working days prior to the maturity/redemption date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 05, 2026
- Maturity Dates: December 31, 2026; April 01, 2027; September 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations providing liquidity and investment options in short-term government debt instruments.
