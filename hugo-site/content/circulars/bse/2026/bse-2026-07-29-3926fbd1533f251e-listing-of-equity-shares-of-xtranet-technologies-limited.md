## Summary

Effective from Thursday, July 30, 2026, equity shares of Xtranet Technologies Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'T' Group of Securities in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544838
- ISIN: INE0NG701011
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: XTRANET
- Total Securities Listed: 52,28,5700 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 127/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 117/-)
- Date of Allotment: July 28, 2026
- Registrar: Kfin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on July 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the trade-for-trade segment placement for the first 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.
- Necessary corporate actions for lock-in periods have been marked in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 29, 2026
- Effective Listing Date / SPOS: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members can trade the newly listed equity shares of Xtranet Technologies Limited subject to initial trade-for-trade surveillance measures.
