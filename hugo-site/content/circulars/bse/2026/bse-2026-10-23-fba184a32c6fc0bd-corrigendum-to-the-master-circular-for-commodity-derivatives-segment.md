## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (formerly Indian Clearing Corporation Limited) has issued a corrigendum to the Master Circular for the Commodity Derivatives Segment, originally published vide Notice 20260429-40 on April 29, 2026. The updated master circular incorporates amendments and comes into effect immediately upon issue.

## Key Points

- Replaces and rescinds the previous Master Circular and prior notices relevant to the commodity derivatives segment.
- Actions, rights, obligations, liabilities, and investigations under previous guidelines remain unaffected as if the rescinded guidelines were never rescinded.
- Published under the 'Home>Downloads>Master Circulars' section on the BSECL website.

## Regulatory Changes

- Incorporation of specific amendments into the Master Circular for the Commodity Derivatives Segment.
- Full rescission of earlier notices and existing master circulars for the segment, subject to savings clauses protecting past actions, accrued rights, and pending proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and participants must take note of the amended master circular and ensure alignment with the updated guidelines.
- In case of any inconsistency between the master circular and individual applicable notices, the relevant notice should be referred to.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Notice Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is low, as the circular consolidates and updates existing regulatory guidelines for commodity derivatives clearing and settlement without altering fundamental market operations.
