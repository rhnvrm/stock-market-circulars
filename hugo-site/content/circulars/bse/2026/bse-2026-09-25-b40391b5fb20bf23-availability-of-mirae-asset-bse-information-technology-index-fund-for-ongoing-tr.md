## Summary

Mirae Asset Mutual Fund has announced that the Mirae Asset BSE Information Technology Index Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: MIRAE ASSET BSE INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY INDEX FUND
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform
- Available Facilities: Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- Plans covered: Regular and Direct plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment across ISINs INF769K01RL1, INF769K01RN7, INF769K01RM9, INF769K01RO5, INF769K01RQ0, INF769K01RP2).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process ongoing transactions for the specified ISINs through the BSE StAR MF Platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and distributors to transact in various regular and direct plans of the Mirae Asset BSE Information Technology Index Fund seamlessly on the BSE StAR MF platform.
