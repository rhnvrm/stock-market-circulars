## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the update of terms for debt securities (Scrip Code: 974028, ISIN: INE105U07030) of Haldia Petrochemicals Limited, effective from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 974028
- ISIN: INE105U07030
- Company Name: Haldia Petrochemicals Limited
- Effective Date: September 24, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Introduction of '16A. VOLUNTARY REDEMPTION' terms, allowing the company to prematurely redeem debentures subject to appropriate clearances and consents, by providing a written notice at least 21 days prior to the redemption date.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and debenture holders to take note of the revised terms for voluntary redemption.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact; affects only the specific debt security holders of Haldia Petrochemicals Limited with regards to early redemption provisions.
