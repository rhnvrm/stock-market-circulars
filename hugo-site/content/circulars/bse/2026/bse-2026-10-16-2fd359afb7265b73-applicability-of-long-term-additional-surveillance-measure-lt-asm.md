## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability, review, and revision of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The changes include 100% margin requirements, movement to higher stages with lower price bands, and transfer to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS) effective from October 19, 2026, and October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. October 22, 2026 on all open positions as on October 21, 2026 and new positions created thereafter (Annexure I, Part A).
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM framework with Lower Price Band applicable w.e.f. October 19, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Securities continuing in LT-ASM but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 22, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- Securities moving out of LT-ASM framework w.e.f. October 19, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework attached as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the revised LT-ASM framework, involving margin requirements, price band restrictions, and group transfers based on XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on applicable securities and adhere to revised price bands and segment transfers.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Lower Price Band & Movement out of LT-ASM: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers: October 22, 2026
- Open positions cutoff for margin applicability: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity in shortlisted securities will face enhanced margin requirements, price band restrictions, and potential liquidity constraints due to transfer to specialized trading groups.
