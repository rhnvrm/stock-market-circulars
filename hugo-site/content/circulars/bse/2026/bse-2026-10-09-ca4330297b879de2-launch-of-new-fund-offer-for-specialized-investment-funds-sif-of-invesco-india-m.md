## Summary

INVESCO INDIA MUTUAL FUND is launching the Summit Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund (SIF) NFO along with SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered include Regular and Direct Plans for Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment under Summit Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund (SIF).
- NFO Period: October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 26, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 30, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (October 26, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled with the 'N' flag reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 30, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date shall not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: October 12, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: October 26, 2026
- SIP Authentication Deadline: October 26, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to seamlessly participate in the Invesco India Mutual Fund SIF NFO through the BSE StAR MF platform while adhering to standardized payment and allotment timelines.
