## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and commencement of trading for Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) issued by Edelweiss Financial Services Limited, effective October 5, 2026, under the 'F GROUP' list.

## Key Points

- Securities: Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of Edelweiss Financial Services Limited.
- Effective Date: Trading commences on Monday, October 5, 2026.
- Trading Group: 'F GROUP' in dematerialized form only.
- Market Lot: Unit market lot of One Bond (demat form).
- Company Financial Year ends on March 31.
- Registrar and Transfer Agent: KFIN Technologies Limited, Hyderabad.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Adheres to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, requiring bonds to be traded exclusively in dematerialized form.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in unit market lots in dematerialized form as per SEBI and BSE guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 1, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing of debt instruments providing liquidity to debenture holders without broader market systemic impact.
