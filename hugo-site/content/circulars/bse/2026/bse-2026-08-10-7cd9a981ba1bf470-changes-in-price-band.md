## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for multiple scrips, effective from August 11, 2026. Trading members are required to note the updated price band percentages for the specified scrips.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised for various scrips across equity segments.
- Effective date of the revision is August 11, 2026.
- Revised price bands range from 2% to 20% depending on the specific scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

The exchange has adjusted the upper and lower limits within which scrips can trade on a given day to manage market volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure their trading systems and terminals reflect the updated price band limits for the affected scrips before market hours on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 10, 2026
- Effective Date: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and traders dealing in the affected scrips need to adjust their trading strategies and order placements to account for the new price band restrictions.
