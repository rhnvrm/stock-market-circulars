## Summary

Effective from Monday, October 5, 2026, the equity shares of Orient Cables (India) Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities following its recent public issue.

## Key Points

- Scrip ID: ORIENTCABL
- Scrip Code: 544960
- ISIN No: INE0J1N01028
- Total Securities Listed: 113,799,705 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 272/- per share (Face Value Rs. 1/- and premium of Rs. 271/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2026
- Market Lot: 1 share
- Registrar: KFin Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing and admission to dealing procedure for initial public offerings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date (October 5, 2026).
- Necessary corporate actions have been executed in demat form to mark lock-in periods in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 1, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors who participated in the public issue of Orient Cables (India) Limited will be able to trade their shares on the BSE starting October 5, 2026, enhancing liquidity and market participation.
