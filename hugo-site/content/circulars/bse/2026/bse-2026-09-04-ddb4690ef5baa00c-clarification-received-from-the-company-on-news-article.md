The BSE has sought clarification from Prestige Estates Projects Ltd regarding recent news articles appearing across various media channels to verify their accuracy and protect investor interests. The company's reply is currently awaited.

- Notice No: 20260904-16
- Notice Date: 04 Sep 2026
- Company Name: Prestige Estates Projects Ltd (Scrip Code: 533274)
- Purpose: Verification of news articles appearing in newspapers, websites, or television channels.
- Status: Reply is awaited from the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

The concerned company is required to submit a clarification to the exchange regarding the accuracy of the news articles.

- Notice Date: 04 Sep 2026
