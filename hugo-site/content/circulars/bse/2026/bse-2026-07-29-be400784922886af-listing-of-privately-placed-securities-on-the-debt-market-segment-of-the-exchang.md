## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by multiple companies on the debt market segment of the exchange, effective July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are issued on a private placement basis and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment.
- Trading takes place in dematerialised form with standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and a tick size of 1 paise.
- Companies listed include Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited, Fedbank Financial Services Limited, Nuvama Clearing Services Limited, A.K. Capital Finance Limited, and Poonawalla Fincorp Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to the specified ISINs, market lots, and standard denomination rules.

## Important Dates

- Allotment / Effective Date: July 28 / July 29, 2026
- Redemption Dates: Between September 2026 and March 2027 depending on the specific commercial paper.

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt market listing activity providing liquidity and trading access for newly issued commercial papers.
