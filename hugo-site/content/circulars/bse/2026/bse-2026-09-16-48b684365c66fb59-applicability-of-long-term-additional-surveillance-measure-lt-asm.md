## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision of stages, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. As per the framework, 100% margins, lower price bands, and transfers to specific trading groups (T/XT/MT/TS) are being implemented for shortlisted securities effective September 17 and September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. September 21, 2026, on all open positions as on September 18, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Revision of stages and movement of securities to higher stages of LT-ASM, along with Lower Price Band applicability w.e.f. September 17, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Transfer of certain securities continuing in LT-ASM to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. September 21, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- List of securities moving out from the LT-ASM framework w.e.f. September 17, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions by listed companies and is not an adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) including strict margin requirements and price band restrictions on designated scrips.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure adequate 100% margins are maintained for open and new positions in securities under the LT-ASM framework.
- Monitor specific annexures for company-wise categorization and group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Lower Price Band & Movement out of LT-ASM: September 17, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: September 21, 2026
- Base date for open positions evaluation: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin block for traders holding positions in securities placed under the LT-ASM framework.
- Enhanced price stability and reduced speculative volatility for shortlisted scrips due to lower price bands and trade-for-trade group transfers.
