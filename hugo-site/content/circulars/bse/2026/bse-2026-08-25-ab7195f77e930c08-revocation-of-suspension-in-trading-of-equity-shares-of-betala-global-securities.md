## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares of Betala Global Securities Ltd (Scrip Code: 531530) with effect from September 02, 2026. The revocation follows compliance with the requirements for the payment of annual listing fees pursuant to SEBI Master Circular guidelines.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 531530
- Company Name: Betala Global Securities Ltd
- Effective Date of Revocation: September 02, 2026
- Trading Session: The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) for IPOs and Relisted Scrips on September 02, 2026.
- Information Memorandum: Available on the Exchange's website under Corporates → Corporate Filings → Information Memorandum → Revocation.

## Regulatory Changes

Revocation is executed pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026, and earlier circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Company has completed compliance with the requirement of payment of annual listing fees.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading Revocation: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding shares in Betala Global Securities Ltd will be able to resume trading in the equity shares starting September 02, 2026, following participation in the Special Pre-open Session.
