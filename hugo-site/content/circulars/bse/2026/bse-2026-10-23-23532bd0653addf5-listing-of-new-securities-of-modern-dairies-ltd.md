## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 35,00,000 equity shares of Modern Dairies Ltd. effective from October 26, 2026, following preferential allotment to promoters upon conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Modern Dairies Ltd. (Scrip Code: 519287)
- **ISIN:** INE617B01011
- **Security Details:** 35,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 40/- (Issue Price: Rs. 50/-) on a preferential basis to promoters.
- **Allotment Breakdown:**
  - 16,00,000 shares allotted on 25/08/2026 (Dist. Nos. 28496762 to 30096761)
  - 19,00,000 shares allotted on 10/09/2026 (Dist. Nos. 30096762 to 31996761)
- **Lock-in Details:** 35,00,000 shares locked-in up to 30/05/2028 (Dist. Nos. 28496762 - 31996761).
- **Ranking:** New shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the listing, trading permissions, and applicable lock-in restrictions for the securities.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Dates:** August 25, 2026 and September 10, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** May 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and warrant conversion event for Modern Dairies Ltd. with normal operational and trading impact.
