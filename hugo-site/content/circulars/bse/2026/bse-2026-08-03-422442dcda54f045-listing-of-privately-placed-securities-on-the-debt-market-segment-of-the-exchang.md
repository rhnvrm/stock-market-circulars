## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by Axis Finance Limited, InCred Financial Services Limited, and Infina Finance Private Limited on the debt market segment effective August 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listing of Commercial Papers for Axis Finance Limited, InCred Financial Services Limited, and Infina Finance Private Limited.
- Trading will take place in dematerialised form under standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- Tick size for the securities is set at 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with standard dematerialised trading norms for debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 3, 2026
- Allotment Date: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt market listing with no broad systemic impact.
