## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities qualifying under the framework will attract a minimum 100% margin effective August 11, 2026, and will be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment. Certain securities will move to lower stages, while others move out entirely.

## Key Points

- Qualifying securities attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 11, 2026, on open positions as of August 10, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 10, 2026.
- Affected securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. August 11, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework updates, staging modifications, and margin adjustments.
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade settlement rules for securities included in the ESM framework.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 07, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin / Trade-for-Trade / Annexure I inclusion: August 11, 2026 (open positions as of August 10, 2026)
- Effective Date for Stage II and moving out provisions: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced margins and Trade-for-Trade restrictions may impact liquidity and trading volume for the shortlisted securities.
