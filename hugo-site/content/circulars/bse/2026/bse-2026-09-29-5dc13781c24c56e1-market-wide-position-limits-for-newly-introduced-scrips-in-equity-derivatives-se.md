## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) for newly introduced scrips in the Equity Derivatives segment under Notice No. 20260929-61 dated September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260929-61
- Notice Date: 29 Sep 2026
- Category: Settlement/RMS
- Segment: Derivatives
- Department: BSECL
- Attachments: No Attachment

## Regulatory Changes

Establishment of Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL) for newly introduced scrips in the equity derivatives segment to regulate open positions and manage market risk.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must monitor and comply with the prescribed Market Wide Position Limits for the newly introduced scrips in the equity derivatives segment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 29 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: As applicable for the newly introduced scrips in derivatives segment.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly trading, risk management, and compliance with position limit norms for newly added derivatives contracts.
