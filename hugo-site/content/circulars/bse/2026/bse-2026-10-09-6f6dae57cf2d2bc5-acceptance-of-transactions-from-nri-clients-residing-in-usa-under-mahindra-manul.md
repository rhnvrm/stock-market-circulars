## Summary

BSE StAR MF has announced the acceptance of transactions from Non-Resident Indian (NRI) investors residing in the United States of America (USA) under the schemes of Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund, including Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs), with immediate effect on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Key Points

- Applicable to NRI clients residing in the USA investing in Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund schemes and SIFs.
- Members must obtain prescribed, duly signed declaration forms from investors.
- Original declaration forms and separate transaction number sheets must be mailed to the CAMS RTA unit in Chennai.
- Scanned copies of the signed declaration form must be emailed on the transaction day to designated RTA and AMC email addresses.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory policy changes; operational procedure enablement for US-residing NRI mutual fund investments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Obtain completed and signed USA Investor Declaration Form from clients.
- Mail original forms and transaction number sheets to: CAMS Mahindra Manulife Unit, CAMS, New No.10, MGR Salai, Nungambakkam, Chennai- 600034, with the envelope marked "Declaration Form for the USA Investors - Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund".
- Email scanned copies on transaction day to Posting_mm@camsonline.com and MMIMPL_RTAOPS@mahindramanulife.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 9, 2026 (Immediate effect).

## Impact Assessment

Provides clear operational guidelines and compliance requirements for trading members facilitating mutual fund investments for US-residing NRIs in Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund schemes.
