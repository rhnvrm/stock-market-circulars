## Summary

NAVI Mutual Fund is offering the NAVI Nifty Reits & Realty Index Fund NFO along with an SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription period runs from September 01, 2026, to September 10, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: NAVI NIFTY REITS & REALTY INDEX FUND - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH EQUITY (ISIN: INF959L01HU8) and DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH EQUITY (ISIN: INF959L01HT0).
- NFO window: September 01, 2026, to September 10, 2026 (extended till midnight on September 10, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on September 10, 2026.
- Tentative allotment date: September 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with a 'N' flag to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 15, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 01, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: September 10, 2026
- SIP Authentication Deadline: September 10, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform participants and investors with access to a new real estate index fund scheme via the BSE StAR MF platform with standard operational guidelines.
