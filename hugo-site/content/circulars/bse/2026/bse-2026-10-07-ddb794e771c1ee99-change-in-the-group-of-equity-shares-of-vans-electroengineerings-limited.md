## Summary

BSE has announced that the equity shares of VANS ELECTROENGINEERINGS LIMITED (Scrip Code: 544971) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment with effect from Thursday, October 22, 2026. Consequently, dealings in the company's equity shares will be shifted under the M Group.

## Key Points

- Company Name: VANS ELECTROENGINEERINGS LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544971
- Segment Transition: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group).
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026.
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange's Notice No. 20261006-25 dated October 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Reclassification of trading segment from restrictive Trade for Trade mode to normal Rolling settlement mode on BSE SME platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems and take note of the segment shift for executing trades in VANS ELECTROENGINEERINGS LIMITED starting October 22, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Moving to the rolling segment removes mandatory delivery-level trading restrictions (Trade for Trade), facilitating normal intraday and delivery trading, thereby improving liquidity and price discovery for shareholders.
