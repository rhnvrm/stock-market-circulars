## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Lippi Systems Limited. The bidding period took place from 20/07/2026 to 31/07/2026, with the revised obligation download scheduled for 07/08/2026 and the final settlement date on 10/08/2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Lippi Systems Limited
- **Takeover Settlement No.:** 2026089
- **Bid Period:** 20/07/2026 to 31/07/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download Date:** 07/08/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 10/08/2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of settlement calendar as per revised guidelines for acquisition of shares through stock exchange mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism across depositories, using Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026089.
- Members and custodians should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 20/07/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 31/07/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 07/08/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 10/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures orderly completion and settlement of shares tendered during the takeover offer window for Lippi Systems Limited.
