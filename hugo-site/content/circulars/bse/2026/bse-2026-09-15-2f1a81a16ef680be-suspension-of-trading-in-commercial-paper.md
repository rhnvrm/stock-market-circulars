## Summary

Stock exchanges have informed trading members that multiple companies have fixed a record date for the redemption of their commercial papers. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of these companies starting from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260915-28 dated September 15, 2026.
- Segment: Debt Segment.
- Purpose: Record Date for Redemption of Commercial Paper.
- Trading Members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of listed companies starting from the record/ex-date.
- Affected entities include DSP Finance Private Limited, Axis Finance Limited, Grasim Industries Ltd, Manappuram Finance Limited, Redington Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Dalmia Cement (Bharat) Limited, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Routine operational notice regarding commercial paper redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no trading takes place in the specified commercial papers on or after the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 15/09/2026
- Record Date / Ex-Date: 17/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational restriction limited to trading members concerning the specific commercial paper scrips listed for redemption.
