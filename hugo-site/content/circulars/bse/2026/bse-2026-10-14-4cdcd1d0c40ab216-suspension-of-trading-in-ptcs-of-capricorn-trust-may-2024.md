## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the suspension of trading in Pass Through Certificates (PTCs) issued by Capricorn Trust May 2024, effective from October 15, 2026, due to maturity.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 975712
- Particulars: CTM24-11.50%-16-10-26-PTC
- ISIN: INE0XAN15015
- Date of Maturity: October 16, 2026
- Trading restriction effective date: October 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational procedure for maturing debt securities/PTCs.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforementioned PTCs with effect from October 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026
- Trading Suspension Effective Date: October 15, 2026
- Maturity Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impact is restricted to holders and trading members dealing in the specific Capricorn Trust May 2024 PTCs undergoing maturity.
