## Summary

SEBI issued an order dated July 31, 2026, regarding trading activities in the scrip of SecureKloud Technologies Limited, prohibiting specific noticees from accessing the securities market for a period of 2 years.

## Key Points

- SEBI order no. QJA/SS/IVD-2/ID9/32543/2026-27 dated July 31, 2026.
- Noticees No. 1 and 2 are prohibited and restrained from accessing the securities market and dealing in securities for 2 years.
- The period of restraint commences after the expiration of the restraint imposed vide the Final Order dated December 16, 2022.
- Show Cause Notice dated September 10, 2025, stands disposed against Noticee No. 3 without any direction.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and market access restrictions imposed on specific individuals/entities involved in the scrip.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the order and ensure compliance with the restrictions imposed on the specified noticees.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: July 31, 2026
- Effective Date: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct compliance impact for trading members to prevent restricted entities from executing trades or accessing the securities market.
