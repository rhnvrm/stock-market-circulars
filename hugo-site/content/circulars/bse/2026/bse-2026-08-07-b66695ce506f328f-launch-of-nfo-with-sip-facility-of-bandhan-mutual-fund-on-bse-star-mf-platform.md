## Summary

Bandhan Mutual Fund is launching the NFO for Bandhan Contra Fund with an SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026. The tentative allotment date is August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Offering:** Bandhan Contra Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- **NFO Period:** August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 28, 2026.
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 27, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (August 24, 2026).

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** August 10, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** August 27, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members operational guidelines and timelines for participating in the Bandhan Contra Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
