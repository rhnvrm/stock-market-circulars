## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the change in name of the debt-listed company 'JSW Kalinga Steel Ltd' to 'JSW JFE Kalinga Steel Limited'. The scrip IDs and abbreviated names for the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Name: JSW Kalinga Steel Ltd
- New Name: JSW JFE Kalinga Steel Limited
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026
- Affected Scrip Codes: 977660 and 977661

## Regulatory Changes

Name change update for debt listed securities in exchange trading systems.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to note the updated name and scrip identifiers in their systems.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating records with the new name and BOLT Plus identifiers for the specified debt securities.
