## Summary

BSE has announced a revision to the effective date for the change of company name of Fabtech Technologies Cleanrooms Ltd on the BOLT Plus system. In continuation to Notice No. 20260807-46 dated August 7, 2026, the name change will now be effective from August 14, 2026, instead of the earlier scheduled date of August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Date Rescheduled**: The effective date of the company name change on BOLT Plus has been deferred by one day to August 14, 2026.
- **Company Identity**:
  - Scrip Code: 544332
  - Existing Name: Fabtech Technologies Cleanrooms Ltd
  - New Name: Fabtech Cleanrooms Limited
  - Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus: FABCLEAN
- **No ID Change**: There is no change to the Scrip ID. All other details from the notice dated August 7, 2026, remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- The corporate name change of **Fabtech Technologies Cleanrooms Ltd** to **Fabtech Cleanrooms Limited** is registered and updated on the Exchange's trading systems.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Trading members are advised to update their internal back-office and trading systems to align with the new name "Fabtech Cleanrooms Limited" under Scrip ID "FABCLEAN" effective from August 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Original Notice Date**: August 7, 2026 (Notice No. 20260807-46)
- **Revised Notice Date**: August 12, 2026 (Notice No. 20260812-47)
- **New Effective Date**: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational/Market Impact**: Low. This is a standard name-change implementation transition that has been shifted by one business day. It does not affect the core trading operations or the security's ticker ID.
