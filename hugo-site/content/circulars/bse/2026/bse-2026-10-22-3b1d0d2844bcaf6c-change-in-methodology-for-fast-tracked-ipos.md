## Summary

BSE Index Services Pvt Ltd has announced a change in the methodology for fast-tracked IPOs across BSE indices, effective from October 23, 2026. The update revises the construction ranking criteria and minimum listing history requirements for the immediate inclusion of significantly sized IPOs and spin-offs.

## Key Points

- Effective date: October 23, 2026.
- Revises ranking criteria for fast-track inclusion in broad and market cap indices (excluding BSE AllCap Index).
- Specifies conditions for IPO stocks including DVR stocks.
- Establishes a minimum required listing history of one month where applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

- For broad/market cap indices (excluding BSE AllCap), the IPO must be among the top 30 companies based on the respective index's construction ranking criteria (previously top 10 companies).
- For indices allowing fast-track inclusion via IPOs or spin-offs, companies must be among the top 30 companies based on average daily metrics.
- DVR-inclusive IPO stocks must satisfy all other criteria under the "Eligibility and Index Construction" section of each index, requiring a minimum listing history of one month.

## Compliance Requirements

Index constituents and issuers planning fast-track IPO listings must align with the revised top 30 construction ranking criteria and eligibility rules.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Pursuing fast-track inclusion in BSE indices will require meeting the adjusted top 30 ranking threshold, potentially altering the speed and eligibility of newly listed entities joining major benchmark indices.
