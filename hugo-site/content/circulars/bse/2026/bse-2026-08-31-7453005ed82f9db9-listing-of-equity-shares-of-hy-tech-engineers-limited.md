## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, September 1, 2026, the equity shares of Hy-Tech Engineers Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'T' Group of Securities in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544891
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: HTEL
- ISIN No.: INE0LEG01024
- Total Securities Listed: 94,852,594 Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 94852594)
- Issue Price: Rs. 53/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 5/- and premium of Rs. 48/-)
- Date of Allotment in Public Issue: August 28, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only pursuant to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on September 1, 2026.
- Subject to Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Corporate action executed in demat form to mark the lock-in period in the depository’s records as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 28, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading Date: September 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public investors and trading members to trade in the equity shares of Hy-Tech Engineers Limited on the BSE exchange with standard trade-for-trade monitoring for the initial 10 trading sessions.
