## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund has notified the exchange regarding the change in scheme names for multiple schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Applies to several UTI Mutual Fund schemes, updating their names to align with revised nomenclature.

## Scheme Name Changes

1. UTI MEDIUM TO LONG DURATION FUND -> UTI MEDIUM TO LONG TERM FUND
2. UTI SHORT DURATION FUND -> UTI SHORT TERM FUND
3. UTI LOW DURATION FUND -> UTI ULTRA SHORT TO SHORT TERM FUND
4. UTI ULTRA SHORT DURATION FUND -> UTI ULTRA SHORT TERM FUND
5. UTI DYNAMIC BOND FUND -> UTI DYNAMIC TERM FUND
6. UTI BANKING & PSU FUND -> UTI BANKING & PSU DEBT FUND
7. UTI MEDIUM DURATION FUND -> UTI MEDIUM TERM FUND
8. UTI FLOATER FUND -> UTI FLOATING INTEREST RATES FUND
9. UTI FOCUSED FUND -> UTI FOCUSED FUND (30 STOCKS)
10. UTI GILT FUND WITH 10 YEAR CONSTANT DURATION -> UTI 10 YEAR CONSTANT MATURITY GILT FUND
11. UTI LONG DURATION FUND -> UTI LONG TERM FUND

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for order placement and reporting on the platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational updates for brokers and investors reflecting the new scheme names on the BSE StAR MF platform.
