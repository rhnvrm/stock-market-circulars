## Summary

Trading members are informed that effective from Friday, September 4, 2026, the equity shares of Priority Jewels Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the 'T' Group of Securities in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Priority Jewels Limited
- Scrip Code: 544899
- ISIN No: INE15EH01014
- Issue Price: Rs. 200/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 190/-)
- Number of Securities: 1,80,00,000 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 2, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on September 4, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action has been executed for lock-in period marking in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: September 4, 2026
- Date of Allotment: September 2, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Priority Jewels Limited shares on the BSE platform under the Trade-for-Trade segment.
