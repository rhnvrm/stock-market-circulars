## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies. The measures include 100% margins, movement to higher stages, transfer to specific settlement groups (T / XT / MT / TS), and price band revisions effective across late July and early August 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. August 04, 2026, on all open positions as on August 03, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework and lower price bands applicable w.e.f. July 31, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. August 04, 2026.
- Specific securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework w.e.f. July 31, 2026, with price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is strictly a market surveillance action and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for securities under Annexure I (Part A).
- Revisions to price bands and movement across LT-ASM stages for listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (100%) for affected securities starting August 04, 2026.
- Members and market participants should review Annexures I and II for the exact list of securities impacted.

## Important Dates

- **July 31, 2026:** Lower price bands and movement of securities to higher stages / moving out of LT-ASM framework effective.
- **August 03, 2026:** Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- **August 04, 2026:** 100% margins become effective; transfer of certain securities to T / XT / MT / TS groups effective.

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and surveillance placement will likely restrict speculative trading and impact liquidity for the short-listed securities.
