## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and trading permission of 1,02,00,000 new equity shares of B.A.G. Films & Media Ltd (Scrip Code: 532507, ISIN: INE116D01028). These shares have been issued at an issue price of Rs. 8.25 per share (including a premium of Rs. 6.25 per share) to the Promoter on a preferential basis, following the conversion of warrants. The shares will be listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** B.A.G. Films & Media Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 532507.
- **ISIN:** INE116D01028.
- **No. of Securities:** 1,02,00,000 equity shares.
- **Face Value & Issue Price:** Rs. 2.00 per share with a premium of Rs. 6.25 per share (Issue Price: Rs. 8.25 per share).
- **Allottee:** Promoter, on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.
- **Date of Allotment:** June 27, 2026.
- **Trading Commencement Date:** August 13, 2026.
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Distinctive Number Range:** 207718091 to 217918090.
- **Lock-in Details:** The shares are locked in until February 26, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no direct regulatory changes introduced by this notice. It is an operational announcement regarding listing approval.

## Compliance Requirements

### For the Company
- Ensure proper listing compliance and coordination with depositories (NSDL/CDSL) to credit/lock shares according to the specified lock-in period (locked in until February 26, 2028).

### For Trading Members
- Note the commencement of trading for these new shares with effect from Thursday, August 13, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** June 27, 2026
- **Date of Notice:** August 12, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing & Trading:** August 13, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date:** February 26, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine listing of shares following warrant conversion.
- **Capital Structure Impact:** The equity share capital of B.A.G. Films & Media Ltd increases by 1,02,00,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 2 each.
- **Liquidity:** The newly listed shares will be locked in until February 26, 2028, and will not immediately impact the freely tradable float of the company.
