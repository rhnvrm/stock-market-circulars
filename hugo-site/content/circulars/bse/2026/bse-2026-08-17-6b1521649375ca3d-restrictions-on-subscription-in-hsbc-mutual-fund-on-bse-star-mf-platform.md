## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding restrictions on subscription in certain schemes of HSBC Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 18, 2026, following communication from the AMC.

## Key Points

- Fresh and additional purchases through lump sum mode and switch-in are restricted up to ₹2,00,000 per PAN per month for specified schemes.
- Fresh systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP and AMC STP-In) are permitted under all frequencies up to ₹2,00,000 per PAN per month.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIPs/XSIPs and AMC STPs) will continue to be processed normally.
- Redemption, switch-out, and STP-out transactions are permitted without any amount restrictions.
- Affected schemes include HSBC Asia Pacific (Ex Japan) Dividend Yield Fund, HSBC Brazil Fund, and HSBC Global Emerging Markets Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of investment limits on specific schemes of HSBC Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure lump sum purchases and fresh systematic registrations adhere to the ₹2,00,000 per PAN per month ceiling for the specified HSBC Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 18, 2026
- Cut-off / Closure of business hours: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and members transacting in HSBC Asia Pacific (Ex Japan) Dividend Yield Fund, HSBC Brazil Fund, and HSBC Global Emerging Markets Fund will face monthly investment caps of ₹2,00,000 for fresh or additional lump sum and systematic subscriptions.
