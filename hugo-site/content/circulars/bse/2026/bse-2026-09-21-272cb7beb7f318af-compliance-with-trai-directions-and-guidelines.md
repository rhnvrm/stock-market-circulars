## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing Investment Advisers about necessary compliance requirements with TRAI directions and guidelines. Entities must integrate with the DoT's Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL), use the 1600 numbering series strictly for service and transactional calls, comply with pre-tagging requirements for SMS content templates, and conduct consumer awareness campaigns.

## Key Points

- Integration with DoT's Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL) for periodic reconciliation and risk workflows.
- Strict use of the 1600 numbering series for service and transactional calls only.
- Compliance with TRAI's direction on pre-tagging variable fields in SMS content templates.
- Conducting consumer awareness campaigns on the 1600 and 140 numbering series.

## Regulatory Changes

Adherence to TRAI directions regarding telecom resource usage, SMS scrubbing, and customer database reconciliation for Investment Advisers.

## Compliance Requirements

1. Integrate with DoT's MNRL and build checks into risk, authentication, and communication workflows.
2. Restrict the 1600 numbering series to service and promotional calls exclusively.
3. Review and pre-tag variable fields in SMS content templates used during the preceding 90 days before blocker mode activation.
4. Undertake consumer awareness campaigns to build trust and recognition.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 21 September 2026
- Reference TRAI Direction Date: 18 November 2025

## Impact Assessment

Investment Advisers must update their communication systems, SMS templates, and verification workflows to prevent service disruptions once blocker mode is activated.
