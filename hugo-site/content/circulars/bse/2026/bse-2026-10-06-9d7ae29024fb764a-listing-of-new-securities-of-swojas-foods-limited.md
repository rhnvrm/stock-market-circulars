## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade 16,00,000 new equity shares of Swojas Foods Limited (Scrip Code - 530217) effective from October 7, 2026, following the preferential conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Swojas Foods Limited
- Scrip Code: 530217
- ISIN: INE295B01016
- Number of Shares: 16,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 6.50/- each (Issue Price: Rs. 16.50/-)
- Effective Date: October 7, 2026
- Allotment Date: 24/08/2026
- Distribution Nos.: 44263313 to 45863312
- Lock-in Details: 16,00,000 shares locked in up to 20/04/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with lock-in restrictions up to April 20, 2027.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Listing Date: October 7, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: April 20, 2027

Reflects capital expansion through preferential warrant conversion for Swojas Foods Limited with standard lock-in constraints for non-promoter allottees.
