## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in certain scrips in the recent past. Responses requested from companies to ensure investors have relevant information include a received reply from Monarch Networth Capital Ltd and pending replies from several other entities.

## Key Points

- Exchange observed significant price/volume movement in specific scrips.
- Reply received and attached from Monarch Networth Capital Ltd (Scrip Code: 511551).
- Replies are awaited from 9 other listed companies.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to clarify significant price and volume movements upon the Exchange's request to ensure transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 July 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine disclosure notice with minimal direct market impact beyond company-specific information availability.
