## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of K.M. Sugar Mills Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532673) will be permitted under the 'B' group, having been transferred from the 'T' group effective October 16, 2026, in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260928-51 dated September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: K.M. Sugar Mills Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 532673
- Group Reclassification: Transferred from 'T' group to 'B' group.
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of trading group category on the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants are requested to take note of the group change for order placement and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact on market operations, reflecting standard administrative group reclassification for trading participants.
