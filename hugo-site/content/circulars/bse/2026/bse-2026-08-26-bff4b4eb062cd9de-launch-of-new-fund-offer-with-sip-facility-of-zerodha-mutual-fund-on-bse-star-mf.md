## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of Zerodha Mutual Fund's new scheme, ZERODHA LIFE CYCLE FUND 2031 - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH, along with an SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from August 27, 2026, to September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ZERODHA LIFE CYCLE FUND 2031 - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH
- Scheme Category: Equity
- ISIN: INF0R8F01240
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 16, 2026
- NFO Period: August 27, 2026, to September 10, 2026 (extended till midnight for lumpsum purchases).

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with an 'N' flag reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 16, 2026, for allotment.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (September 10, 2026).
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: August 27, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: September 10, 2026
- SIP Authentication Deadline: September 10, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors participating in the Zerodha Mutual Fund NFO must ensure timely fund initiation and SIP authentication to avoid transaction cancellations.
