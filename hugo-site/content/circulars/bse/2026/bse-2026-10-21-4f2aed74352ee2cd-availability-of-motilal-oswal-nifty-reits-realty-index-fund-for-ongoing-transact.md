## Summary

Motilal Oswal Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund has reopened for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: MOTILAL OSWAL NIFTY REITS & REALTY INDEX FUND - Regular Plan & Direct Plan (Growth)
- ISINs: INF247L01HP8 (Regular), INF247L01HO1 (Direct)
- Permitted Facilities: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can execute transactions for the aforementioned schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with the specified effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume normal trading, subscription, redemption, and systematic investment operations in the Motilal Oswal Nifty REITs & Realty Index Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
