## Summary

BSE has issued an advisory reminding all trading members to ensure that clients interested in trading exclusively listed securities on BSE are active and enabled for trading on the exchange. This is in continuation of SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/95 dated June 21, 2023.

## Key Points

- Trading members must ensure clients wishing to transact in exclusively listed securities on BSE are active and enabled.
- Necessary checks should be carried out in advance to prevent investor inconvenience.
- Trading members must ensure appropriate system readiness and client communication.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2023/95 dated June 21, 2023, regarding Trading Preferences by Clients.

## Compliance Requirements

- Verify client trading status and enablement for exclusively listed securities on BSE.
- Maintain system readiness and effective communication with clients.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines trading access and prevents last-minute transaction delays or failures for investors dealing in exclusively listed BSE securities.
