## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice announcing the new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of JSW Dulux Ltd following its stock split from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/- per share. The new ISIN becomes effective for trades starting from the ex-date, October 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** JSW Dulux Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 500710
- **New ISIN:** INE133A01029
- **Corporate Action:** Sub-Division of Equity Shares from Rs.10/- to Re. 1/-
- **Ex-Date:** October 22, 2026 (DR-740/2026-2027)
- **Previous Reference:** Exchange Notice No. 20260930-60 dated September 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Formalization and allocation of a new ISIN number reflecting the share capital restructuring resulting from the stock split.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading and depository records with the new ISIN (INE133A01029) for JSW Dulux Ltd effective from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Ex-Date / Effective Date:** October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational update for clearing, settlement, and trading systems concerning JSW Dulux Ltd equity shares.
