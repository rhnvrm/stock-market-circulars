## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window - Takeover) for MITSHI INDIA LIMITED, scheduled from September 3, 2026, to September 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: MITSHI INDIA LIMITED (Scrip Code: 523782, Symbol: MITSHI, ISIN: INE844D01017).
- Offer Size: Up to 22,88,000 Equity Shares at an Offer Price of Rs. 15.00 per share.
- Opening Date: September 3, 2026; Closing Date: September 17, 2026.
- Session Timings: Bid offering and modifications from 9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. on trading days.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and updated with the correct PAN and category.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Opening Date: 3rd September 2026
- Closing Date / Last Day of Offer: 17th September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates the takeover acquisition window for shareholders and trading members participating in Mitshi India Limited's open offer.
