## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that specific companies have fixed record dates for interest payment and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds, with trading to take place at reduced face values effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Steel Exchange India Ltd: Record date and ex-date set for 21/09/2026; reduced face value is Rs. 380037/- per debenture.
- Ananya Finance For Inclusive Growth Private Limited: Record date and ex-date set for 22/09/2026; reduced face value is Rs. 20000/- per debenture.
- Muthoot Capital Services Ltd.: Record date and ex-date set for 22/09/2026; reduced face value is Rs. 28571.45 per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and record dates for debentures/bonds trading.

## Important Dates

- Steel Exchange India Ltd Record/Ex-Date: 21/09/2026
- Ananya Finance Record/Ex-Date: 22/09/2026
- Muthoot Capital Services Record/Ex-Date: 22/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for trading members regarding the reduced face values of debentures for the specified companies.
