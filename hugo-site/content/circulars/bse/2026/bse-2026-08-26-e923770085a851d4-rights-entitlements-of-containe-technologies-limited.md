## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for renunciation of CONTAINE TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED are permitted on the Exchange effective from Monday, August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: CONTAINE TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751131
- Scrip ID: CONTAIN-RE
- ISIN No.: INE0M8920010
- Market Lot: 1000
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: August 31, 2026 to September 22, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: August 31, 2026 to September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Rights Entitlements for renunciation shall be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis.
- Trading is permitted in dematerialized form only.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and clarifying that REs should not be mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes and bills for the purchase of REs must include a prominent disclaimer informing clients that purchase of REs only gives the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before closure.
- REs neither subscribed by making an application with requisite money nor renounced on or before the issue closing date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- Trading Period for Renunciation Starts: August 31, 2026
- Rights Issue Opens: August 31, 2026
- Trading Period for Renunciation Closes: September 22, 2026
- Rights Issue Closes: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and market participants to trade or renounce the rights entitlements of Containe Technologies Limited within the specified window under T+1 settlement norms.
