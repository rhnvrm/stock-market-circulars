## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade in new securities of Bharti Airtel Ltd with effect from Wednesday, August 19, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bharti Airtel Ltd (Scrip Code: 532454, ISIN: INE397D01024)
- Securities: 4,60,340 Equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Rs. 5/- each issued on rights basis.
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026.
- Distinctive Numbers: 313842 (5883204263 to 5883518104) and 146498 (5883518105 to 5883664602).
- Ranking: These shares are pari-passu with the old Equity Shares of the Company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None specific for trading members other than standard trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Trading: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital change with negligible impact on overall market liquidity or operations.
