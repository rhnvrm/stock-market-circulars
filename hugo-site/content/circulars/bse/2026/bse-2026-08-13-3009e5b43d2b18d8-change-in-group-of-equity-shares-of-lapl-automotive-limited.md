## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of LAPL Automotive Limited (Scrip Code - 544863) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Thursday, August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: LAPL Automotive Limited
- Scrip Code: 544863
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: August 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of scrip across trading segments based on exchange norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to note the segment shift for dealings in the equity shares of the company.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normalization of trading for the scrip by moving it into the rolling settlement segment.
