## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, and certain securities will be moved to lower stages, shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment, or removed from the framework.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin effective August 17, 2026, on all open positions as of August 14, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Selected securities will move to lower Stage I under the ESM framework effective August 14, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective August 17, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction effective August 14, 2026.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework will be reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Updates to security classifications under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework based on periodical market surveillance reviews.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with increased margin requirements (minimum 100%), trade-for-trade settlement restrictions, and applicable price bands for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage Changes / Price Bands / Removals: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade Shifting: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for affected securities will be directly impacted due to enhanced surveillance restrictions, higher margin obligations, and trade-for-trade settlement.
