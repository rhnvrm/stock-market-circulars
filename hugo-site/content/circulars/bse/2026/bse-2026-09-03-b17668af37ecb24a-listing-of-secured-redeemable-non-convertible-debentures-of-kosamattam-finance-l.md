## Summary

Trading members are informed that Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of Kosamattam Finance Limited are admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'F GROUP' effective from September 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security: Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures.
- Effective Date: September 04, 2026.
- Trading Form: Dematerialized form in unit market lot (One Bond).
- Company Financial Year Ends: March 31st.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are conducted in dematerialized form in unit market lots as per SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 03, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing announcement enabling secondary market trading of Kosamattam Finance Limited's NCDs.
