## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the benefit of early pay-in in the Commodity Derivatives Segment, referencing prior SEBI and BSECL circulars. Members and clients providing early pay-in through the Collateral System will receive an exemption from margins on open positions in relevant underlying commodity derivative contracts.

## Key Points

- Reference to SEBI circular dated June 19, 2026, and BSECL circular dated June 23, 2026.
- Members and clients providing early pay-in via the Collateral System are exempt from margins on open positions in relevant derivative contracts.
- Effective date of the provisions is September 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formalizes and clarifies margin exemption rules for early pay-in in the Commodity Derivatives Segment using the Collateral System.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members/Participants should note the operational applicability of early pay-in benefits for commodity derivatives and adjust margin calculations accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Optimizes capital and margin utilization for market participants engaging in commodity derivatives by granting margin exemptions for early pay-in.
