## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. As per the revised framework, 100% margins, lower price bands, and group transfers (T/XT/MT/TS) are being imposed on specified securities effective October 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. October 14, 2026 on all open positions as on October 13, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Lower price bands applicable w.e.f. October 12, 2026 for certain securities moved to higher stages.
- Transfer of certain securities to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 14, 2026.
- Periodic review of securities placed under the framework.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is a market surveillance measure, not an adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) including 100% margins, price band restrictions, and group reclassifications.
- Reinstatement of price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure adequate margin maintenance (100%) for open and new positions in securities listed under Annexure I (Part A).
- Adhere to revised price bands and trading group assignments effective October 12 and October 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- October 12, 2026: Effective date for movement to higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price band applicability, and removal of securities from LT-ASM.
- October 14, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and transfer of securities to T / XT / MT / TS Group.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced risk management and surveillance for designated volatile securities through 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions.
- Potential liquidity and trading volume impact on securities subjected to stricter surveillance measures or transferred to trade-for-trade groups.
