## Summary

P H CAPITAL LIMITED (Scrip Code: 500143) has informed the Exchange that it has fixed October 7, 2026, as the record date for the bonus issue of equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: P H CAPITAL LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 500143
- Bonus Ratio: 10 Bonus Equity Shares of Rs.10/- each for every 01 Equity Share held
- Record Date: 07/10/2026
- Ex-Bonus Basis Date: 07/10/2026
- Estimated Allotment: 30,010,000 equity shares to be allotted as on October 8, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note that transactions in the equity shares of the company will be on an ex-bonus basis starting from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 5, 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Bonus Date: October 7, 2026
- Allotment Date: October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders receive 10 additional shares for every share held, resulting in an appropriate price adjustment and increased share liquidity on the exchange.
