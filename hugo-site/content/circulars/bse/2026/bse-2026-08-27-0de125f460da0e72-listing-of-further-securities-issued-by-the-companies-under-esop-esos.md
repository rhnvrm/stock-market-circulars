## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members that further new securities issued by 22 companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Friday, August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 22 listed entities.
- Event Type: Listing of further securities issued under ESOP / ESOS.
- Effective Date of Trading: August 28, 2026.
- Includes prominent companies such as Axis Bank, ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company, IDFC First Bank, Tech Mahindra, and SBI Life Insurance Company.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable - Routine Corporate Action Filing]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the newly listed securities and ensure proper adjustments in trading terminals and clearing systems where applicable.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice reflecting equity capital expansion due to employee stock option exercises. Impact is company-specific and negligible for overall market operations.
