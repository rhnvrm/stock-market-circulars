## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding Trading Members to submit their Annual Audited Networth Certificate, Computation of Networth, Auditor’s Report, Audited Annual Accounts, Director’s Report, and Financial Strength details for the financial year ended March 31, 2026. Submissions must be completed on or before October 31, 2026, through the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS).

## Key Points

- **Applicability:** All Trading Members of the Exchange (corporate and non-corporate).
- **Dual Registration Note:** Members registered with both BSE and NSE are required to submit their Annual Audited Networth Certificate to NSE only. Exclusive BSE members will continue submissions to BSE.
- **Format Standards:** Financial statements must follow the format prescribed under the Companies Act, 2013, in accordance with SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD2/CIR/P/2016/95.
- **Signatory Requirements:** Covering letters, Audited Networth Certificates, and audit report filings must be signed exclusively by the Proprietor, Designated Partners, Designated Directors, or Compliance Officer.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiteration of compliance with SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD2/CIR/P/2016/95 requiring stockbrokers to submit financial statements in Companies Act, 2013 format irrespective of entity type.

## Compliance Requirements

- Submission of complete annual reports, balance sheets, P&L accounts, auditor reports, computation statements, shareholding patterns, director/partner details, and PEP undertakings via BEFS.
- Strict adherence to prescribed annexures (Annexure 1 through Annexure 9).

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 9, 2026
- **Due Date for Submission:** October 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must ensure timely filing of networth certificates and financial accounts to avoid disciplinary actions, penalties for reporting shortfalls, or non-submission notices as stipulated in prior Exchange notices.
