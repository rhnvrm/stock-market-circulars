## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective March 16, 2026. The circular details securities placed under various stages of ASM, including Reliance Home Finance Ltd in Stage 1, along with a consolidated list of impacted companies.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: March 16, 2026.
- Reliance Home Finance Ltd (Scrip Code: 540709, ISIN: INE217K01011) is listed under IBC Stage 1.
- Includes a consolidated list of securities under corporate announcements / disclosure stages and Insolvency Resolution Process stages.

## Regulatory Changes

- Application of Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) specific to companies undergoing insolvency and bankruptcy proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and apply appropriate trading safeguards and margin requirements for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: March 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced monitoring and trading restrictions apply to the shortlisted securities to protect investor interests amidst corporate insolvency proceedings.
