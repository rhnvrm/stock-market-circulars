## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd and SI Creva Capital Services Private Limited have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of their Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will resume with a reduced face value effective from the respective record and ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20261015-2 dated 15 October 2026.
- Segment: Debt.
- Issuers: Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd and SI Creva Capital Services Private Limited.
- Trading commences with reduced face value on 16/10/2026 and 17/10/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational disclosure of corporate debt redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised face values and settlement details for trading in the specified NCDs.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 15/10/2026
- Record / Ex-Dates: 16/10/2026 and 17/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for market participants holding or trading the specified NCDs.
