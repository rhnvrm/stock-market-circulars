## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has issued a notice regarding the revised trading and settlement programme and settlement timings for the Electronic Gold Receipt (EGR) segment for the period from August 24, 2026, to August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- The trading and settlement schedule for settlement numbers DR-105/2026-2027 to DR-108/2026-2027 under the EGR segment has been revised.
- Revised settlement timings and pay-in/pay-out instructions deadlines have been specified for specific settlement dates.
- Auction pay-in is scheduled at 9:00 a.m., with members required to submit pay-in instructions to depositories/banks latest by 8:50 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of the settlement and auction dates for EGR transactions between August 24, 2026, and August 27, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adherence to the revised pay-in and pay-out deadlines and submit instructions to depositories and banks within the specified timeframes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Period: August 24, 2026, to August 27, 2026
- Specific settlement schedules updated for DR-105, DR-106, DR-107, and DR-108.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational adjustments required for clearing and settlement teams of trading members participating in the EGR segment to align with the revised timings.
