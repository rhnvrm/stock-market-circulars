## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and permission for trading of new equity shares of Cello World Limited with effect from September 03, 2026, pursuant to a Scheme of Amalgamation.

## Key Points

- 46,53,239 Equity Shares of Rs.5/- each of Cello World Limited (Scrip Code: 544012) are listed and permitted for trading.
- Shares issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation amongst Wim Plast Limited (WPL), Cello Consumer Products Private Limited (CCPPL), and Cello World Limited (CWL) and their respective shareholders and creditors.
- Effective date for trading of new securities: September 03, 2026.
- The new shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the Company.
- ISIN Number: INE0LMW01024
- Distribution Nos.: 220885035 to 225538273
- Date of Allotment: June 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None (Corporate action execution under Sections 230 to 232 of the Companies Act, 2013).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and depository participants need to note the successful listing and credit of the newly allotted shares under the specified ISIN and distribution numbers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: June 09, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increase in the total listed equity base of Cello World Limited due to issuance of shares under the amalgamation scheme.
