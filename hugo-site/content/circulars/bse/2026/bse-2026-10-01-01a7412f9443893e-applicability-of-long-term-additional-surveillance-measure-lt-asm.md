## Summary

BSE has announced the imposition and revision of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on specified securities pursuant to the revised framework guidelines. The measures include 100% margin requirements, movement to higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price bands, and transfers to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS).

## Key Points

- **100% Margins:** Applicable w.e.f. October 07, 2026, on all open positions as on October 06, 2026, and new positions created from October 07, 2026 onwards.
- **Higher Stages & Price Bands:** Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands effective October 05, 2026.
- **Group Transfers:** Securities continuing in LT-ASM transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. October 07, 2026.
- **Exits:** List of securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective October 05, 2026.
- **Consolidated List:** Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities under the framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the revised LT-ASM framework, enforcing stricter margin requirements and trading segment adjustments for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure systems are configured to collect 100% margins on affected securities and account for lower price bands and group transfers as of the specified effective dates.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Higher Stages / Exits / Lower Price Band:** October 05, 2026
- **Open Position Cut-off Date for Margins:** October 06, 2026
- **Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers:** October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities will experience increased margin blocking (100% margin), potential liquidity constraints due to group transfers (T / XT / MT / TS), and restricted price volatility via lower price bands.
