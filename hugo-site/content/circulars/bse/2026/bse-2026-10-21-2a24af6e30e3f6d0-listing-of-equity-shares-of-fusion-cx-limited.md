## Summary

Equity shares of Fusion CX Limited are listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE exchange effective Thursday, October 22, 2026, under the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544995
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: FUSIONCX
- ISIN: INE0N2201019
- Total Securities: 144,360,333 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up.
- Issue Price: Rs. 289/- per share (Face Value Rs. 1/-, Premium Rs. 288/-).
- Date of Allotment: October 19, 2026.
- Market Lot: 1 share.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should note that the scrip is part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on listing day.
- Necessary corporate action executed by the company to mark lock-in periods in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 21, 2026
- Date of Allotment: October 19, 2026
- Listing / Effective Date: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Fusion CX Limited shares on BSE, providing liquidity to shareholders and market participants.
