## Summary

Brooks Restaurant and Banquets Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document with the stock exchange on September 19, 2026, in compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations. The document is hosted for public review and comments for a minimum period of 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Brooks Restaurant and Banquets Limited
- Filing Date: September 19, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO
- Public Comments Window: 21 days from the date of the notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard SME IPO draft offer document disclosure procedure under ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments on the draft offer document must be submitted via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days from the date of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 19, 2026
- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Comments Deadline: Within 21 days of notice

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and the public an opportunity to review and provide feedback on the proposed SME IPO of Brooks Restaurant and Banquets Limited.
