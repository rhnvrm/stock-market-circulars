## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of SG Finserve Limited (Scrip Code - 539199) effective from August 20, 2026. The listing comprises 6,27,778 equity shares issued at a premium to promoters and non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SG Finserve Limited
- Scrip Code: 539199
- ISIN: INE618R01015
- Number of Shares: 6,27,778 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 440/- (Issue Price: Rs. 450/-)
- Allottees: Promoters and Non-Promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.
- Effective Date of Trading: August 20, 2026.
- Lock-in Details:
  - 3,27,778 shares (Dist. Nos. 65267223 to 65595000) locked-in up to 19/02/2028.
  - 3,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 65595001 to 65895000) locked-in up to 19/02/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Ensure adherence to lock-in periods and trading parameters specified for the newly listed shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 08/04/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: 20/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase via warrant conversion with standard lock-in conditions; no significant market impact.
