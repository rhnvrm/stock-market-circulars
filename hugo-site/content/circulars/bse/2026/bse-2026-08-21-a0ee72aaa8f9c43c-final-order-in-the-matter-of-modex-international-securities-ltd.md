## Summary

SEBI has issued a final order dated August 21, 2026, in the matter of Modex International Securities Ltd., restraining the noticees from accessing the securities market and prohibiting them from dealing in securities directly or indirectly until the settlement of claims of clients and investors of Noticee No. 1 is completed by NSE.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous Exchange Notice No. 20200430-44 dated April 30, 2020.
- SEBI order no. QJA/BS/MIRSD/MIRSD-SEC-5/32676/2026-27 dated August 21, 2026.
- Noticees are restrained from accessing the securities market and prohibited from buying, selling, or dealing in securities.
- Restrictions remain in place until the settlement of claims for clients/investors of Noticee No. 1 is completed by NSE.
- The order is effective immediately from the date of the order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of market access restrictions and trading prohibition on the specified noticees pursuant to SEBI directions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the order and ensure compliance by avoiding any dealings with the restrained entities.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures investor protection by barring non-compliant entities from market activities until client claims are fully resolved and settled by NSE.
