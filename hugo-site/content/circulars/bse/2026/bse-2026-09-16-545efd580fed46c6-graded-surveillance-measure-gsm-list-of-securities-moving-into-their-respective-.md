## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing the list of securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM), effective from September 17, 2026. Trading members are required to exercise adequate caution as specific surveillance actions and margin requirements apply.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the Annexure will move to higher GSM stages effective September 17, 2026.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value by buyers.
- Stage III: Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday), and ASD of 100% of trade value by buyers.
- Stage IV: Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday), and ASD of 100% of trade value by buyers with no upward movement.
- Settlement shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance frameworks under the provisions of the GSM Framework published on February 23, 2017.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules and deposit requirements without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: 16 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: 17 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities will be restricted due to stringent margin requirements, trade-to-trade settlement, and weekly trading frequency limits for higher stages.
