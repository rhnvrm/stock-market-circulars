## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) for multiple companies due to the fixation of record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260814-18, dated August 14, 2026.
- Applies to commercial papers of Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited, SBICAP Securities Limited, Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited, and GIC Housing Finance Ltd.
- Trading members are instructed to refrain from dealing in these specific CPs around the record and ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no transactions are conducted in the listed commercial papers post their respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates and Ex-Dates range from August 17, 2026, to August 28, 2026, depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational restriction for trading members concerning specific commercial paper instruments reaching redemption.
