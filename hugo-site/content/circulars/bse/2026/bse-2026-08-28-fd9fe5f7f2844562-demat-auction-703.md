## Summary

BSE Limited has announced the schedule and operational timelines for Demat Auction 703 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-703/2026-27. Trading members can submit offers for the purchase of securities on August 28, 2026, with pay-in scheduled for August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Number: DR-703/2026-27.
- Auction Offer Entry Timing: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on August 28, 2026.
- Auction Matching Timing: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on August 28, 2026.
- Pay-in of the Auction: August 31, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- Restriction on trading members offering securities in auction where they had short delivered is removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure.

## Regulatory Changes

- The restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities has been removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off times.
- Extreme care must be taken while punching security codes and rates as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- Members should refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the Auction offer Entry Screen and consider any corporate actions.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 28, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational process for clearing short deliveries in rolling settlements with no broad market impact.
