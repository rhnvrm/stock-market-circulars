## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of subscriptions (fresh and additional purchases) and switch-in transactions for the UTI GOLD ETF FUND OF FUND on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect, following communication from UTI Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Resumption applies to lumpsum investments (fresh and additional purchases).
- Switch-In transactions are also enabled.
- Changes are effective immediately on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Supersedes previous restrictions notified via Notice No. 20260608-38 dated June 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the resumption and execute transactions under the scheme accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 1, 2026
- Reference Notice Date: June 08, 2026 (Notice No. 20260608-38)

## Impact Assessment

Investors can now freely invest in the UTI GOLD ETF FUND OF FUND via lumpsum and switch-in options on the BSE StAR MF platform without previous restrictions.
