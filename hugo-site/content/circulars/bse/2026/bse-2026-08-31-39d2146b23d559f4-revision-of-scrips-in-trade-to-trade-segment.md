## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of scrips in the Trade-to-Trade segment as part of surveillance measures based on the extant SEBI framework. Securities satisfying specific surveillance criteria will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS groups effective September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria I, II, and III will be moved to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective September 03, 2026.
- Securities in Annexure II will remain in T / XT / MT / TS / P / Z / ZP Groups and attract a 5% price band.
- Settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off of positions allowed.
- A 100% VAR margin will be levied on these scrips as per Exchange Notice No. 20050805-12.
- The action is purely a preventive market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of trade-to-trade settlement for shortlisted securities based on XBRL submissions by listed companies.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in these securities as no netting off is permitted and a 100% VAR margin applies.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 31, 2026
- Effective Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reduced liquidity and restricted intraday trading due to mandatory delivery-based settlement (trade-to-trade) and 100% VAR margin for the affected scrips.
