## Summary

Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd. (ICCL) has issued the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Buyback) for Wipro Limited. The bidding is scheduled from June 11, 2026, to June 17, 2026, under settlement number 2026071.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Wipro Limited
- **Settlement Type & No.:** Buyback, Settlement No. 2026071
- **Bid Start Date:** 11/06/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 17/06/2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 23/06/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 24/06/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Conducted under established stock exchange acquisition window and revised guidelines for share acquisition.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered strictly through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL and NSDL).
- Members and custodians must use Market Type "(Buyback)" and Settlement No. 2026071.
- Securities will be earmarked/blocked in client accounts; members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Period:** June 11, 2026 – June 17, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** June 23, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** June 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates smooth execution and settlement of shares tendered in the Wipro Limited buyback through the BSE OTB platform, ensuring participants adhere to early pay-in and depository protocols.
