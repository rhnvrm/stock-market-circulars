## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, specifying securities entering the framework, moving to lower stages, or exiting. Affected securities are subject to 100% margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement, and price band restrictions.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting inclusion criteria attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 14, 2026, on open positions as on October 13, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moving to lower Stage I under the ESM framework effective October 13, 2026, are specified in Annexure I.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 14, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 13, 2026.
- Securities exiting the ESM framework effective October 13, 2026, are listed in Annexure II, with their price bands reinstated.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures including mandatory 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement, and periodic call auctions for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with heightened margin calls, price band limits, and settlement changes for securities listed in the annexures.

## Important Dates

- October 13, 2026: Effective date for stage movements, price band adjustments, periodic call auctions, and exit of specified securities.
- October 14, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading liquidity and operational workflows for affected securities are restricted due to enhanced surveillance, trade-for-trade settlement, and higher margin requirements.
