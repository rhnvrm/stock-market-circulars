## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of two newly listed companies, INDO-MIM LIMITED (Ticker: 544837) and LOHIA CORP LIMITED (Ticker: 544839), to the BSE IPO Index. The inclusion will be effective at the open of Monday, August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- INDO-MIM LIMITED (Exchange ticker: 544837) and LOHIA CORP LIMITED (Exchange ticker: 544839) are being added to the BSE IPO Index.
- These changes are effective from the market open on Monday, August 03, 2026.
- The actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).
