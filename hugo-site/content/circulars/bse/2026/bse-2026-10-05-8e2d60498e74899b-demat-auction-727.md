## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-727/2026-27.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 5, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Auction pay-in will be held on Tuesday, October 6, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

Following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment, settlement is conducted through Clearing Members, and restrictions on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they short-delivered remain removed.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should exercise utmost care when punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members must refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the Auction Offer Entry Screen and check notices regarding corporate actions (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 5, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: October 6, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice facilitating standard market settlement and clearing mechanisms for short deliveries.
