## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031 on the BSE StAR MF Platform, scheduled from August 26, 2026, to September 09, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031 (Regular and Direct Growth Equity plans).
- NFO Period: August 26, 2026, to September 09, 2026 (extended till midnight on the final day).
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 15, 2026.
- ISINs:
  - Regular Plan Growth Equity: INF109K1A591
  - Direct Plan Growth Equity: INF109K1A609

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported to RTA/AMC with an 'N' flag.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 11, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 26, 2026
- NFO Close Date: September 09, 2026
- Clear Funds Pay-in Deadline: September 11, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedures apply for mutual fund distributors and investors participating in the NFO through the BSE StAR MF platform.
