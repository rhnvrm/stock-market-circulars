## Summary

BSE StAR MF has announced a revision in the temporary subscription limit for fresh subscriptions and switch-in transactions, including fresh SIPs and STPs, under the DSP Multi Asset Allocation Fund with immediate effect. Subscriptions are now subject to an overall limit of ₹30 Lakh per PAN per day (at the first holder level).

## Key Points

- Overall subscription limit revised to ₹30 Lakh per PAN per day across all modes of subscription (fresh purchases, additional purchases, SIPs, and STPs).
- Fresh SIP and STP-In registrations are accepted up to ₹30 Lakh per investor per day.
- Existing SIP and STP registrations continue to be processed at the AMC/RTA end per existing mandates.
- Redemption, switch-out, and STP-out transactions remain unrestricted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of a ₹30 Lakh per investor per day cap on fresh investments and switch-ins for DSP Multi Asset Allocation Fund on BSE StAR MF platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must adhere to the daily subscription ceiling of ₹30 Lakh per PAN for fresh transactions and registrations in the scheme.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 13, 2026 (with immediate effect).

## Impact Assessment

- Investors planning large lump-sum or systematic investments exceeding ₹30 Lakh per day in DSP Multi Asset Allocation Fund will need to stagger their investments across multiple days.
