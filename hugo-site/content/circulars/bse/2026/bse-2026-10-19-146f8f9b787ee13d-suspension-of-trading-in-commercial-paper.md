## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers of Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited due to maturity and redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in these commercial papers effective from October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited
- Particulars: KMIL-22-10-26-CP
- Scrip Code: 732764
- ISIN: INE975F14D73
- Effective Date of Suspension: October 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid Commercial Papers of the company with effect from October 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine maturity of commercial paper with no broader market impact.
