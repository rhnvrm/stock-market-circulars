## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 15,100 new equity shares of Adani Enterprises Ltd. (Scrip Code - 512599) effective from Thursday, August 6, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up shares of Re. 1/- each issued on a rights basis.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Adani Enterprises Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 512599
- **ISIN:** INE423A01024
- **Effective Date:** August 6, 2026
- **Security Details:** 15,100 Equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Re. 1/- each issued on rights basis.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 
  - 1212447619 to 1212447635 (17 shares)
  - 1347245380 to 1347260379 (15,000 shares)
  - 1408675761 to 1408675843 (83 shares)
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

None.

- **Effective Date for Trading:** August 6, 2026

Minimal operational or market impact, representing a standard corporate action (conversion of partly paid shares).
