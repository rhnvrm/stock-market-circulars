## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 78,64,008 equity shares have been subscribed under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of HD Fire Protect Limited at Rs. 271/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: HD Fire Protect Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investor Portion)
- Number of Shares Subscribed: 78,64,008 Equity Shares
- Issue Price / Allocation Price: Rs. 271/- per equity share
- Notice Date: October 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to note the anchor investor allocation details as per the company announcement.

- Announcement / Notice Date: October 12, 2026
