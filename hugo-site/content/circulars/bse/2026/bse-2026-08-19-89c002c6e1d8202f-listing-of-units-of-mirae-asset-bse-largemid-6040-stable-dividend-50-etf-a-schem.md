## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that units of Mirae Asset BSE LargeMid (60:40) Stable Dividend 50 ETF are listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, August 20, 2026. The scheme is an open-ended scheme replicating/tracking the BSE LargeMid (60:40) Stable Dividend 50 Total Return Index.

## Key Points

- **Name of the Mutual Fund:** Mirae Asset Mutual Fund
- **Investment Manager:** Mirae Asset Investment Managers (India) Private Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544874
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** LARGEMID50
- **ISIN No.:** INF769K01RK3
- **Group:** B
- **Market Lot:** 1 Unit
- **Face Value:** Rs. 10.00 per unit
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 36.94 per unit
- **Date of Allotment:** 14/08/2026
- **No. of Units:** 1,800,669 Units

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

As per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, units of the Mutual Fund will only be traded in dematerialized form. Trades will be executed in unit market lot (i.e., one unit).

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables secondary market trading of the newly launched Mirae Asset BSE LargeMid (60:40) Stable Dividend 50 ETF for investors on the BSE platform.
