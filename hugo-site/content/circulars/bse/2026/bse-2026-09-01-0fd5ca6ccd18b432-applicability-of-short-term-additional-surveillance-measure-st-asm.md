## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on specified market surveillance criteria. Applicable margin rates are revised effective September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ST-ASM Stage I will attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Securities qualifying for ST-ASM Stage II will attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher.
- The surveillance actions are effective from September 03, 2026, on all open positions as on September 02, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- The shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and does not represent an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of margin requirements and surveillance frameworks under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the revised margin rates (50% or 100% as applicable) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective September 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 03, 2026
- **Base Date for Open Positions:** September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin obligations for market participants holding or creating positions in securities placed under ST-ASM Stages I and II, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
