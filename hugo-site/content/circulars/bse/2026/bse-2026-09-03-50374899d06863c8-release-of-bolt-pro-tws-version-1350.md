## Summary

BSE has announced the release of a new version of BOLT Pro TWS (Version 13.50), which will be made live and compulsory effective Monday, September 7, 2026. The release applies to Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments.

## Key Points

- **Version Release:** BOLT Pro TWS ver. 13.50 is a compulsory version release across Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments.
- **Functionality Changes:**
  - Modifications in Pre-Open Call Auction Session for Equity Derivatives.
  - Display of Imbalance Quantity at Equilibrium (IMBQ) and Imbalance Quantity at Equilibrium – Market Orders (IMBQ MKT) during the Pre-Open Session in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments.
  - Display of Closing Reference Price under the 'CLRP' field in Equity Derivatives simultaneously with CAS eligible securities in the Equity segment.
- **Download and Testing:** Available for download starting September 4, 2026, from 7:00 PM onwards, with a mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, September 5, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates to pre-open and closing auction session display parameters and reference price fields across segments to enhance price discovery transparency.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members using Leased Line Connections must upgrade to BOLT Pro TWS ver. 13.50 before the effective date by selecting the version in the TWS Version tab of the Auto-Download Interface or downloading via the exchange website.

## Important Dates

- **Download Availability:** September 04, 2026 (from 7:00 PM)
- **Mock Trading Session:** September 05, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must complete the software upgrade prior to the market opening on September 7, 2026, to ensure uninterrupted trading operations across the mandated segments.
