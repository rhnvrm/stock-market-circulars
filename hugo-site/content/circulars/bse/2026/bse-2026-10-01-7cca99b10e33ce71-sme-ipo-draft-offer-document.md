## Summary

BSE has announced the filing of the Draft Offer Document for the SME IPO of S.R.G. Narrow Fab Limited. The document is made available on the SME website for public review and comments for a period of at least 21 days in accordance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Issuer Company: S.R.G. Narrow Fab Limited
- Filing Date / Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Hosting Website: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx
- Public Comment Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None. Complies with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Interested stakeholders and the public may review the Draft Offer Document and submit feedback or comments within 21 days of the notice date to the designated email address.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Comment Deadline: 21 days from the notice date

## Impact Assessment

Enhances transparency by inviting public scrutiny and stakeholder feedback prior to the finalization and opening of the SME IPO.
