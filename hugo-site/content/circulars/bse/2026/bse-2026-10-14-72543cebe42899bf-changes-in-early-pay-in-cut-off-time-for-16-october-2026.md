## Summary

BSE has announced changes in the Early pay-in cut off time for the equity segment scheduled for 16-October-2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261014-42
- Notice Date: 14 Oct 2026
- Category: Settlement/RMS
- Segment: Equity
- Department: BSECL

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of the standard Early pay-in cut off time for the specified trade date to accommodate operational requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and clearing participants must ensure compliance with the revised early pay-in timings to avoid any settlement penalties or operational hurdles.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 14 Oct 2026
- Effective Date / Target Date: 16 Oct 2026

## Impact Assessment

Participants must align their back-office systems and depository instructions with the revised cut-off time for October 16, 2026.
