## Summary

BSE has announced the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window / Takeover) of The South India Paper Mills Limited. The offer is scheduled to run from Thursday, 22nd October 2026 to Wednesday, 4th November 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: The South India Paper Mills Limited (Scrip Code: 516108, ISIN: INE088G01014, Symbol: STHINPA).
- Offer Size: Up to 48,75,000 Equity Shares at an Offer Price of Rs. 120.00 per share with a Face Value of Rs. 10.00.
- Opening Date: 22nd October 2026; Closing Date: 4th November 2026.
- Session Timings: Bid offering and modifications/cancellations are available from 9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. during the issue period.
- Access Platforms: Available via iBBS, RTRMS, and Extranet platforms (both Internet and Leased Line options).

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory policy changes; operational schedule issued pursuant to standard Takeover and Offer to Buy framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure UCC of the investors is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and investor category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians should note contact details for new login IDs and password resets with BSE Trading Operations.

## Important Dates

- Offer Opening Date: 22nd October 2026
- Offer Closing Date: 4th November 2026
- Give-up / Take-up Period: 22nd October 2026 to 3rd November 2026 (Give-up on 4th November 2026, Take-up until 4.00 p.m. on 4th November 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates smooth execution and participation of institutional and retail investors in the takeover acquisition window for The South India Paper Mills Limited shares.
