## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework for securities based on XBRL submissions. 100% margins and lower price bands are introduced for applicable securities, along with transfers to specific trading groups.

## Key Points

- 100% margins become applicable effective August 11, 2026, on all open positions as of August 10, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities are moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework or transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective August 7, 2026 / August 11, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM Framework effective August 07, 2026, with price bands reinstated accordingly.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions, and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins on open and new positions under LT-ASM Annexure I (Part A).
- Movement of securities to higher stages and revised price bands.
- Transfer of certain continuing securities to T / XT / MT / TS Groups.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure appropriate 100% margin maintenance for securities listed under Annexure I (Part A).
- Members can reach out to bse.surv@bseindia.com for clarifications.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 06 Aug 2026
- Higher stages / moving out effective date: 07 Aug 2026
- 100% margins effective date: 11 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for traders holding positions in short-listed securities under the Long Term ASM framework.
- Enhanced monitoring and restricted price bands for securities identified under surveillance.
