## Summary

BSE has announced changes to several of its indices with respect to AvenuesAI Ltd. (Exchange Ticker: 539807) and Vedanta Ltd. (Exchange Ticker: 500295) on account of corporate actions including the consolidation of equity shares.

## Key Points

- Effective date for all index modifications is Tuesday, October 13, 2026.
- AvenuesAI Ltd. (539807) is being dropped from multiple indices including BSE Smallcap, BSE Allcap, BSE Financial Services, BSE Smallcap 500 Enhanced Value 50, BSE Midsmallcap, BSE 1000, BSE Next 500, BSE 250 Microcap, BSE 1000 Multicap Equal Size Weighted (25%), BSE Smallcap 500, and BSE Total Market.
- Vedanta Ltd. (500295) is being added to broad market and segment indices including BSE 1000, BSE Next 500, BSE 250 Microcap, BSE 1000 Multicap Equal Size Weighted (25%), BSE Smallcap 500, and BSE Total Market.

## Regulatory Changes

- Index maintenance and reconstitution based on corporate actions and regulatory compliance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and index-linked funds must adjust their portfolios and benchmarks in accordance with the revised index structures.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Moderate impact on passive funds and index-tracking portfolios holding or benchmarking against the affected BSE indices.
