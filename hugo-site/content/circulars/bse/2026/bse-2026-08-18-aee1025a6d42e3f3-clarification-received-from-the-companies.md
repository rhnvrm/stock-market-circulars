## Summary

The BSE has published a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in several scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies.

## Key Points

- Replies have been received and attached from 8 companies, including Chatterbox Technologies Ltd, Shreeshay Engineers Ltd, Kanungo Financiers Ltd, Rudra Global Infra Products Ltd, Premier Polyfilm Ltd, Continental Securities Ltd, Uniroyal Industries Ltd, and RSD Finance Ltd.
- Replies are currently awaited from 9 companies, including Apt Packaging Ltd, Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Gconnect Logitech and Supply Chain Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, PVP Ventures Ltd, Triumph International Finance India Ltd, and Shree Balaji (Mala) Textiles Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes introduced; part of routine surveillance measures to maintain market integrity and transparency.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely clarify significant price/volume movements upon exchange inquiry to ensure market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 18 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and aids investors in assessing the price/volume volatility of the specified scrips.
