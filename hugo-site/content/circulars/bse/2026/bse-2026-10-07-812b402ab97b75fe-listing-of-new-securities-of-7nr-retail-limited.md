## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that new securities of 7NR Retail Limited (Scrip Code - 540615) consisting of 9,00,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at par to non-promoters on a preferential basis are listed and permitted to trade with effect from October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: 7NR Retail Limited (Scrip Code: 540615, ISIN: INE413X01035).
- Security Details: 9,00,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at par on a preferential basis to non-promoters.
- Effective Date of Trading: October 8, 2026.
- Allotment Date: 10/09/2026.
- Issue Price: Rs. 10/-
- Distinctive Numbers: 28006801 to 118006800.
- Lock-in Details: 9,00,00,000 shares under lock-in up to 16/04/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard corporate listing of preferential shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members to note the listing and trading permissions along with lock-in restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 10, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: October 8, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: April 16, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of equity base through preferential allotment of shares to non-promoters, subject to lock-in requirements.
