## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of price bands for 26 scrips, effective from September 29, 2026, as part of its surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 26 scrips across various sectors.
- Effective date: September 29, 2026.
- Revised price band percentages range from 2% to 10%.

## Regulatory Changes

Price bands for specified scrips have been modified from their existing levels to new limits (2%, 5%, or 10%).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while placing orders in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Effective Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in the listed scrips must adjust their trading strategies and order placements according to the new price band limits.
