## Summary

Trading members are informed that Siyaram Silk Mills Ltd has fixed a record date pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement approved by the Hon’ble National Company Law Tribunal, Mumbai Bench, for the issuance of bonus Non-Convertible Redeemable Preference Shares (NCRPS).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Siyaram Silk Mills Ltd. (Scrip Code: 503811)
- Record Date: 21/08/2026
- Ex-Entitlement From Date & Sett. No.: 21/08/2026, DR-699/2026-2027
- Issue Details:
  - (i) 4 (Four) Preference Shares - Series I of Face Value of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up for every 1 (One) Equity Share of Rs. 2/- each fully paid up.
  - (ii) 3 (Three) Preference Shares - Series II of Face Value of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up for every 1 (One) Equity Share of Rs. 2/- each fully paid up.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of NCLT approved Scheme of Arrangement involving bonus issue of preference shares utilizing general reserves.

## Compliance Requirements

- Company to make necessary applications to the Exchange for listing and trading of the newly allotted NCRPS subject to compliance with requisite formalities.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 21/08/2026
- Ex-Entitlement Date: 21/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Eligible shareholders holding equity shares on the record date will receive bonus preference shares (NCRPS) as per the specified ratio, impacting company capital structure and shareholder holdings.
