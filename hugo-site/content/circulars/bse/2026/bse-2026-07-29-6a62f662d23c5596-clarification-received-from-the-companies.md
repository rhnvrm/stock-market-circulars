## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice listing companies from which clarifications were sought regarding significant price and volume movements in their respective scrips. The notice distinguishes between companies that have submitted their replies and those whose replies remain awaited.

- **Companies with Replies Received:**
  1. Darjeeling Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 539770)
  2. Mapro Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 509762)
  3. Indian Link Chain Manufacturers Ltd (Scrip Code: 504746)
  4. Arihant Foundations & Housing Ltd (Scrip Code: 531381)
  5. Affle (India) Ltd / Affle 3I Ltd (Scrip Code: 542752)
  6. Advance Agrolife Ltd (Scrip Code: 544562)
  7. Pankaj Polymers Ltd (Scrip Code: 531280)

- **Companies with Replies Awaited:**
  1. CMX Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 532217)
  2. Crazy Snacks Ltd (Scrip Code: 544807)
  3. Gconnect Logitech and Supply Chain Ltd (Scrip Code: 544156)
  4. Kinetic Trust Ltd (Scrip Code: 531274)
  5. Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 59117)
  6. Spenta International Ltd (Scrip Code: 526161)
  7. Justo Realfintech Ltd (Scrip Code: 544542)

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; part of routine stock exchange surveillance and price discovery monitoring.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities whose replies are awaited must submit clarifications promptly to ensure investors have access to relevant information.
