## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis effective from August 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Commercial papers listed for entities including DSP Finance Private Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited, Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, and The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd.
- Securities are traded in dematerialised form with standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- Tick size for the securities is 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard debt segment guidelines and dematerialised settlement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 5, 2026
- Allotment Dates: August 4, 2026
- Redemption Dates: November/December 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations for debt segment participants.
