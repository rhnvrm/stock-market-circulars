## Summary

MOLD-TEK Technologies Ltd has announced the record date for its bonus issue of equity shares. Trading members are advised that transactions in the company's equity shares will operate on an ex-bonus basis effective from October 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name & Code: MOLD-TEK TECHNOLOGIES LTD (Scrip Code: 526263)
- Bonus Ratio: 1 (One) Bonus Equity Share of Rs.2/- each for every 1 (One) Equity Share held.
- Record Date: October 9, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: October 9, 2026
- Projected Allotment: 28,805,118 equity shares to be allotted as of October 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must account for the ex-bonus date and record date stipulations in trade execution and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 9, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: October 9, 2026
- Expected Allotment Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders will receive additional shares in a 1:1 proportion, leading to stock price adjustment and expanded equity base.
