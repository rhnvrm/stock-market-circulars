## Summary

Fusion Klassroom Edutech Limited's equity shares are being shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) on the BSE platform, effective August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Fusion Klassroom Edutech Limited
- Scrip Code: 544856
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: August 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of equity shares from MT Group to M Group following previous exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to note the change in segment and group for dealings in the company's equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Improves liquidity and trading efficiency as shares move from Trade for Trade to the regular Rolling segment.
