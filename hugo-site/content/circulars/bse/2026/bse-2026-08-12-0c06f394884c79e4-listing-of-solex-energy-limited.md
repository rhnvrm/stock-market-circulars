## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission of 1,08,02,489 equity shares of Solex Energy Limited to dealings on the Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities. The listing is effective from Thursday, August 13, 2026. The shares have a face value of Rs. 10 each and will trade in a market lot of one share under Scrip Code 544862 and Scrip ID SOLEX.

## Key Points

- **Listing Date**: Effective from Thursday, August 13, 2026.
- **Company Name**: Solex Energy Limited.
- **Securities Details**: 1,08,02,489 Equity Shares of Rs. 10 each fully paid up (Distinctive Nos. 1 to 10802489).
- **Group/Segment**: Listed in 'B' Group under the Equity segment.
- **Trading Identifiers**:
  - Scrip Code: `544862`
  - Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name on BOLT: `SOLEX`
  - ISIN: `INE880Y01017`
- **Trading Form**: Compulsory dematerialized form only (as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000). Market lot is 1 share.
- **Lock-in and Physical Shares**: Physical certificates are enfaced with non-transferability, and dematerialized shares have appropriate lock-in periods marked in depository records.
- **Financial Details**: Financial year ending on March 31. Audited standalone and consolidated financial results for the year ended March 31, 2026, are accessible online. All shares rank pari-passu including dividend entitlement.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory rules are introduced. The listing is carried out under the standard SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, which mandate dematerialized-only trading for newly listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must ensure that dealings in the equity shares of Solex Energy Limited are executed solely in dematerialized form.
- **Share Transfer Agent / Registrar**: Skyline Financial Private Limited is appointed to handle all depository and registry operations.
- **Company Compliance**: Ms. Azmin Chiniwala is designated as the Company Secretary and Compliance Officer for any inquiries or disclosures.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date of Listing/Trading**: Thursday, August 13, 2026.
- **Notice Date**: Wednesday, August 12, 2026.
- **Financial Year-End**: March 31.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: This listing increases the number of tradable securities in the 'B' Group on the BSE, providing liquidity and trading opportunities in Solex Energy Limited. Operational systems, back offices, and brokers need to update their security master databases with the new Scrip Code `544862`, Scrip ID `SOLEX`, and ISIN `INE880Y01017` before trading commences on August 13, 2026.
