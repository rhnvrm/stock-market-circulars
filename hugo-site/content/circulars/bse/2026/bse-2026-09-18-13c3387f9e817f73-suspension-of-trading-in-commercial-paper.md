## Summary

Trading members are informed that Mirae Asset Capital Markets (India) Private Limited has fixed a record date for the redemption of its Commercial Paper. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified Commercial Paper from the record/ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mirae Asset Capital Markets (India) Private Limited
- Scrip Code: 732011
- Scrip ID: MACMPL-22-9-26-CP
- ISIN: INE0T2V14129
- Record Date: 21/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 21/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified Commercial Paper of the company starting from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 21/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 21/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specific Commercial Paper of Mirae Asset Capital Markets (India) Private Limited is suspended in preparation for its redemption.
