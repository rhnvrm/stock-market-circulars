## Summary

BREP Asia SG L&T Holding (NQ) Pte. Ltd., BREP Asia SG DRPL Holding (NQ) Pte. Ltd., BREP Asia II Indian Holding Co IV (NQ) Pte. Ltd. and BREP Asia II Indian Holding Co VII (NQ) Pte. Ltd. (the 'Sellers') and the sponsor group members of Knowledge Realty Trust propose to sell up to 74,00,00,000 Units (representing 16.69% of the unit capital) as the Base Offer Units, with an option to additionally sell up to 37,00,00,000 Units (representing 8.34%) as an oversubscription option. The floor price is set at Rs. 108/- per unit.

## Key Points

- **Base Offer Size:** Up to 74,00,00,000 Units (16.69% of unit capital).
- **Oversubscription Option:** Up to 37,00,00,000 Units (8.34% of unit capital).
- **Total Offer Size (if oversubscription exercised):** 1,110,000,000 Units (25.03% of unit capital).
- **Floor Price:** Rs. 108/- per unit.
- **Designated Stock Exchange:** BSE Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Undertaken to achieve the minimum public unitholding of the Trust as prescribed under regulation 14(2A) of the SEBI (Real Estate Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must adhere to revised guidelines for bidding in the Offer for Sale (OFS) segment as specified in previous exchange notices and SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- **T Day (Non-Retail Investors only):** August 31, 2026.
- **T+1 Day (Retail Investors and Non-Retail Investors carrying forward un-allotted bids):** September 01, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates liquidity and compliance with minimum public unitholding requirements for Knowledge Realty Trust.
