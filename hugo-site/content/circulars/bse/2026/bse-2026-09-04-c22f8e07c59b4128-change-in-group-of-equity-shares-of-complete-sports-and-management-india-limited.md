## Summary

 BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Complete Sports and Management India Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group), effective Monday, September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Complete Sports and Management India Limited
- Scrip Code: 544900
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The trading of equity shares is shifted from Trade for Trade to the normal Rolling segment, facilitating regular settlement cycles.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are required to note the change in segment and group for settlement and trading purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 04, 2026
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances liquidity and ease of trading for investors as shares move from the restrictive Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment.
