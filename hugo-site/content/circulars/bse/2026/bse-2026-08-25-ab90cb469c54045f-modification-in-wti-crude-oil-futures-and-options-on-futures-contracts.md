## Summary

BSE issued a notice regarding modifications to WTI Crude Oil Futures and Options on Futures contracts, effective from September 07, 2026. Changes include updating the symbol from WTICRUDE to CRUDEOIL for futures, revising the expiry day for options on futures, and updating strike price intervals.

## Key Points

- Symbol for WTI Crude Oil futures and options on futures revised from WTICRUDE to CRUDEOIL.
- Expiry day for Options on Futures revised to five business days prior to the expiry day of the underlying futures contract (previously two business days).
- Strike intervals for Options on Futures revised to 50-1-50 (ITM-ATM-OTM) from 25-1-25.
- No change in the expiry cycle of WTI Crude Oil Futures contracts.
- Trading members are requested to participate in mock trading scheduled for September 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Contract specification updates for WTI Crude Oil commodity derivatives.
- Revised launch calendar and contract master file availability scheduled for September 04, 2026 end of day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their trading systems and participate in the mandatory mock trading session on September 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading: September 05, 2026
- Contract Master File Generation: September 04, 2026 (End of Day)
- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects commodity derivatives trading members dealing in WTI Crude Oil contracts, requiring adjustments to order management systems, strike selections, and expiry tracking.
