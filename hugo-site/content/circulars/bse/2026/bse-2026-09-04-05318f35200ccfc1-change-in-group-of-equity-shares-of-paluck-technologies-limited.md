## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Paluck Technologies Limited (Scrip Code: 544897) are being transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Monday, September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Paluck Technologies Limited
- Scrip Code: 544897
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange’s Notice No. 20260903-35 dated September 03, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to take note of the segment change for dealings in the equity shares of Paluck Technologies Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables normal rolling settlement for the equity shares of Paluck Technologies Limited, improving trading liquidity and operational flow.
