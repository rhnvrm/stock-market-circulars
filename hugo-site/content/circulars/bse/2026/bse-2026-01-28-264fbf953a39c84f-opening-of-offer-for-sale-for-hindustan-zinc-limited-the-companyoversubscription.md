## Summary

Vedanta Limited announced the opening of the Offer for Sale (OFS) of its shares in Hindustan Zinc Limited, including the exercise of an oversubscription option due to strong institutional demand.

## Key Points

- Promoter Vedanta Limited offered equity shares in Hindustan Zinc Limited.
- An oversubscription option was exercised to accommodate additional demand.
- Non-retail bidding opened on January 28, 2026, followed by retail bidding.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to SEBI guidelines for Offer for Sale (OFS) mechanisms.

## Important Dates

- 2026-01-28: Non-Retail Bidding and Oversubscription Notice
- 2026-01-29: Retail Bidding

## Impact Assessment

- Increased public shareholding and liquidity for Hindustan Zinc Limited.
