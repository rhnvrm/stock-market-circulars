## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CPs) for various companies. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies as they have fixed record dates for redemption.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260824-42, dated August 24, 2026.
- Segment: Debt
- Department: Listing Operations
- Involves multiple companies fixing record dates for Commercial Paper redemption on September 1, 2026, and September 2, 2026.
- Trading members are instructed to refrain from trading in these specific CPs starting from the respective ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no transactions are executed in the specified Commercial Papers of the listed companies beyond the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: September 1, 2026, and September 2, 2026 (depending on the specific company instrument).

## Impact Assessment

Restricts secondary market trading for the specified Commercial Papers ahead of their redemption date to facilitate smooth settlement.
