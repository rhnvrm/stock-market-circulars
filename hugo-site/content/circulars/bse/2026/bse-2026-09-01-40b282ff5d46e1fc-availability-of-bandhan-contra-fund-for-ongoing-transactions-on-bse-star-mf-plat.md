## Summary

Bandhan Contra Fund is reopening for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and related facilities (SIP, STP, and SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform, pursuant to previous notice No. 20260807-38.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopens for ongoing transactions effective September 02, 2026.
- Covers multiple plans including Regular and Direct plans for Growth and IDCW options.
- Applicable ISINs include INF194KB1KU4, INF194KB1LA4, INF194KB1LB2, INF194KB1KX8, INF194KB1LC0, and INF194KB1LD8.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and participants are informed to note the availability of the scheme for transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and trading members to resume transactions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities for Bandhan Contra Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform.
