## Summary

The BSE has issued a surveillance notice observing significant price and volume movements across multiple scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors and trading members have access to relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the concerned companies, attaching replies received and noting pending responses.

## Key Points

- **Surveillance Action**: Exchange monitoring of unusual price/volume fluctuations in listed scrips.
- **Clarifications Received (11 Companies)**: Includes DCM Shriram Industries Ltd, Eurotex Industries and Exports Ltd, Farm Peace Ltd, Jauss Polymers Ltd, Nuvama Wealth Management Ltd, Palm Jewels Ltd, Shipping Corporation of India Ltd, Shree Rajasthan Syntex Ltd, Vellora Impact Ltd, Xelpmoc Design and Tech Ltd, and Mopshop Distribution Ltd.
- **Clarifications Awaited (12 Companies)**: Includes Bharat Immunologicals & Biologicals Corporation Ltd, Billwin Industries Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, El Forge Ltd, Fly-Hi Maritime Travels Ltd, M P K Steels (I) Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Rex Sealing and Packing Industries Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, Tamilnadu Telecommunications Ltd, and Symbiox Investment & Trading Company Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this represents standard surveillance disclosure and exchange monitoring activity.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities under surveillance must furnish timely clarifications regarding material price/volume movements to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: October 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market integrity and investor awareness by publicly identifying scrips experiencing abnormal trading activity and tracking company responses.
