## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) on certain securities pursuant to the revised LT-ASM framework. The measures include 100% margins, movement to higher stages, and price band revisions effective from early August 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins shall be applicable w.e.f. August 05, 2026, on all open positions as on August 04, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities are moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework along with lower price bands effective August 03, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective August 05, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework, and their price bands will be reinstated accordingly.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is a market surveillance measure, not an adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) guidelines including margin requirements and group transfers.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for shortlisted securities and account for price band modifications and group transfers.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Band / Higher Stages / Moving Out: August 03, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins / Group Transfers: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and surveillance measures for specific securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
