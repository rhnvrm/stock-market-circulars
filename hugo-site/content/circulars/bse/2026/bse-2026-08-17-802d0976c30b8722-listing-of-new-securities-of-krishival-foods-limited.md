## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 27,72,095 new equity shares of Krishival Foods Limited (Scrip Code: 505196, ISIN: INE0GGO01015) issued on a rights basis are permitted to trade with effect from Tuesday, August 18, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Krishival Foods Limited
- Scrip Code: 505196
- ISIN: INE0GGO01015
- Securities: 27,72,095 Equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid-up on a rights basis.
- Effective Date: August 18, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 8,34,258 shares (22295142 to 23129399) and 19,37,837 shares (23129425 to 25067261).
- Pari-passu: The new shares rank pari-passu with the existing old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for trading members regarding the expansion of paid-up equity capital for the company.
