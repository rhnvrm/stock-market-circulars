## Summary

BSE has announced that the ICICI Prudential Contra Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting October 21, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20260925-54.

## Key Points

- Schemes include Regular and Direct plans (Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options) with ISINs INF109K1A658, INF109K1A666, INF109K1A674, INF109K1A682, INF109K1A690, and INF109K1A708.
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026.
- Facilities Available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational notice for scheme availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the specified ISINs starting October 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables smooth ongoing transactions for investors and participants in ICICI Prudential Contra Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
