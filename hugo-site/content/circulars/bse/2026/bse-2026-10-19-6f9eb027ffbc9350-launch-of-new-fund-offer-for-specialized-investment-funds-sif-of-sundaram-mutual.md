## Summary

Sundaram Mutual Fund has announced the launch of its Specialized Investment Fund (SIF), 'SELECT EQUITY EX-TOP 100 LONG-SHORT FUND', across multiple regular and direct plans with growth and IDCW options on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The New Fund Offer (NFO) is available from October 21, 2026, to November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: SELECT EQUITY EX-TOP 100 LONG-SHORT FUND (Regular and Direct Plans - Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, IDCW Payout).
- NFO Period: October 21, 2026, to November 04, 2026 (extended till midnight for lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 06, 2026.
- SIP Facility: Available during the NFO period.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must reach the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 05, 2026, to be eligible for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: October 21, 2026
- NFO Close Date: November 04, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline / Clear Funds Availability: November 05, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform participants and investors with access to a new specialized equity long-short fund investment option on the BSE StAR MF platform with standard settlement workflows.
