## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion in the public issue of Leap India Limited, 4,67,68,854 equity shares have been successfully subscribed at a price of Rs. 159/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Leap India Limited
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investors Portion)
- Shares Subscribed: 4,67,68,854 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 159/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: August 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are to note the anchor allocation details for settlement and record purposes.
