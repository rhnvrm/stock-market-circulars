## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives segments on Saturday, September 26, 2026, from both the primary site (PR) and disaster recovery site (DR). Trading members using third-party trading platforms of empaneled vendors or in-house developed systems via ETI APIs can utilize this session to test their applications under various market conditions.

## Key Points

- **Mock Trading Date**: Saturday, September 26, 2026.
- **Segments Covered**: Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives.
- **Sites**: Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR).
- **Obligations**: Mock trading is strictly for testing and familiarization; trades do not attract any margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations and create no rights or liabilities.
- **Feedback**: Members are requested to share feedback on the session by 5:30 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational mock trading notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and system developers are encouraged to actively participate in testing their systems and provide necessary feedback to BSE technical support or helpdesk.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: September 25, 2026
- **Mock Trading Date**: September 26, 2026
- **Log-in**: 09:15 am - 10:00 am
- **Continuous Trading (PR)**: 10:00 am - 02:00 pm
- **Switchover to DR**: 02:00 pm - 02:45 pm
- **Continuous Trading (DR)**: 02:45 pm - 04:45 pm
- **Trade Modification**: 05:00 pm

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. The session provides an isolated environment for brokers and system developers to validate trading applications and ETI API connectivity without financial risk.
