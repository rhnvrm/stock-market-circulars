## Summary

Groma Global Limited has filed its SME IPO Draft Offer Document with BSE on September 29, 2026, in compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations. The document has been made available on the BSE SME website to invite public comments for at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Groma Global Limited
- Filing Date: September 29, 2026
- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Review Period: At least 21 days from the notice date
- Submission Channel: Email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com
- Host Website: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; processed in accordance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public may review the Draft Offer Document on the BSE SME platform and submit comments via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Public Comments Deadline: 21 days from notice date

## Impact Assessment

Marks the formal commencement of the public review and stakeholder consultation phase for Groma Global Limited's upcoming SME IPO listing on BSE.
