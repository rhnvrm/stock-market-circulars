## Summary

Trading members are informed that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Covers multiple companies including SRG Housing Finance Limited, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, Purva Oak Private Limited, Muthoot Capital Services Ltd., Moneyboxx Finance Limited, ECL Finance Limited, and Namdev Finvest Limited.
- Details specific scrip codes, ISIN numbers, record dates, ex-dates, and reduced face values per debenture.
- Trading will proceed with reduced face values as specified for each NCD.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the reduced face values and record/ex-dates when executing trades in the specified debentures.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates: August 9, 2026, to August 20, 2026 (varying by security)
- Ex-Dates: August 7, 2026, to August 20, 2026 (varying by security)

## Impact Assessment

Operational adjustment for trading members regarding the reduced face value and part redemption schedules of the listed debentures.
