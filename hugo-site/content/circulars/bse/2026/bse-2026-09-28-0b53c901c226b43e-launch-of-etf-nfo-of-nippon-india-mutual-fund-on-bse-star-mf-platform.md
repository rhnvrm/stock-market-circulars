## Summary

BSE has announced the launch of the NIPPON INDIA CRISIL-IBX FAR GILT ETF (including Direct Plan) NFO on the BSE StAR MF Platform, scheduled from September 29, 2026, to October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: NIPPON INDIA CRISIL-IBX FAR GILT ETF and its Direct Plan.
- Category: Debt.
- NFO Period: September 29, 2026, to October 12, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 12, 2026 for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 14, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 14, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled and reported with 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Payment initiation remains pending until 3:00 PM on the last day of funds pay-out or T+5 day, whichever is earlier.
- Clear funds must reach BSE Clearing Limited by 6:00 PM on October 14, 2026.
- Cheque, OTM, and switch transactions are subject to specific timeline rules.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: September 29, 2026
- NFO End Date: October 12, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 14, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: October 14, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates investment in the Nippon India CRISIL-IBX FAR Gilt ETF through the BSE StAR MF platform for market participants and investors.
