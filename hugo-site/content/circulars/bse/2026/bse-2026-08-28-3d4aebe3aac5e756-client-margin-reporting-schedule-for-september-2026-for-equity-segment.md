## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has released the client margin reporting schedule for the Equity Segment covering trading dates in September 2026. The circular outlines the corresponding last dates of margin reporting and penalty dates for each trade date during the month.

## Key Points

- Covers all trading days in September 2026 for the Equity Segment.
- Specifies trade dates alongside their respective last date for margin reporting and penalty dates.
- Issued by BSE Clearing Limited (formerly Indian Clearing Corporation Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; this is a routine monthly schedule issuance for operational compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the margin reporting timelines and avoid penalties by adhering to the specified last dates for margin reporting for each trade date.

## Important Dates

- First Trade Date in Schedule: 01-09-2026 (Margin Reporting: 08-09-2026, Penalty Date: 09-09-2026)
- Last Trade Date in Schedule: 30-09-2026 (Margin Reporting: 08-10-2026, Penalty Date: 09-10-2026)

## Impact Assessment

Operational schedule for clearing and settlement members to manage client margin reporting cycles smoothly throughout September 2026.
