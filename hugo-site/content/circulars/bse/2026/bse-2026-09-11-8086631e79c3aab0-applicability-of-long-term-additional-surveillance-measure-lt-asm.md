## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework on various securities. The measures include 100% margins, movement to higher stages, transfer to specific trade groups, and removal of certain securities from the framework.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable on all open and new positions from September 17, 2026, for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- List of securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands effective September 15, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Securities transferred to T / XT / MT / TS groups effective September 17, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- Securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective September 15, 2026 (Annexure II).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised LT-ASM framework rules involving margin requirements, price band restrictions, and segment/group transfers for identified scrips based on XBRL submissions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and note the price band and group changes for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Lower Price Band / Higher Stages / Moving Out: September 15, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins / Group Transfers: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience increased margin requirements, price band caps, and potential trading group restrictions, impacting liquidity and trading operations.
