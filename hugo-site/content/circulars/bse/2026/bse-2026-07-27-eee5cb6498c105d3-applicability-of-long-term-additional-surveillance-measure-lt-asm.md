## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Under this framework, specific securities are subjected to enhanced margin requirements, lower price bands, group transfers, or removal from the surveillance framework based on periodical reviews.

## Key Points

- 100% margins become applicable effective July 30, 2026, on all open positions as on July 29, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Lower price bands take effect from July 28, 2026, for securities moving to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework (Annexure I, Part B).
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T, XT, MT, or TS groups effective July 30, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective July 28, 2026, with their price bands reinstated.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) including 100% margin requirements and price band restrictions in accordance with the revised LT-ASM framework guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must ensure compliance with the enhanced 100% margin requirements for applicable open and new positions and account for price band restrictions or group transfers.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Band / Higher Stages / Removal: July 28, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins and Group Transfers: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for impacted scrips will be tightened, requiring participants to maintain higher capital allocations for open positions.
