## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities satisfying the criteria under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria under the IBC framework are listed in Annexure I.
- Annexure II contains the list of securities moving out of the IBC framework effective August 19, 2026.
- Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities under the framework.
- Annexure IV lists securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25%.
- The Price Band of a scrip moving out of the framework shall be reinstated to the pre-shortlisting price band, subject to no other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under the IBC framework for identified companies.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the inclusion and exclusion of securities under the IBC surveillance framework and adjust trading parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 19, 2026
- Notice Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced monitoring and potential trading restrictions for companies undergoing insolvency resolution processes.
