## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of Q&T Foods Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective Wednesday, September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Q&T Foods Limited
- Scrip Code: 544872
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: September 02, 2026
- Reference: Continuation of Exchange’s Notice No. 20260818-34 dated August 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard segment migration for trading securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the segment change for executing transactions in Q&T Foods Limited under the Rolling segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Allows regular rolling settlement for the equity shares of Q&T Foods Limited, improving trading flexibility compared to the Trade for Trade segment.
