## Summary

SBI Mutual Fund is offering the SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund NFO along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026 (extended till midnight for lumpsum purchases).

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- **NFO Period:** August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 31, 2026.
- **SIP Criteria:** Monthly frequency requires a minimum of 12 installments for amounts between Rs. 500 and Rs. 999, and 6 installments for amounts of Rs. 1,000 and above.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 31, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (August 24, 2026).

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** August 10, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for market participants and distributors using the BSE StAR MF platform regarding the SBI Balanced Hybrid Fund NFO.
