## Summary

SEBI vide its interim order no. WTM/KV/CFID/CFID-SEC1/32724/2026-27 dated September 17, 2026, has restrained noticee nos. 2 to 4 from buying, selling, or dealing in securities of Kore Digital Limited, directly or indirectly, until further orders.

## Key Points

- Interim order issued by SEBI in the matter of Kore Digital Limited.
- Noticee nos. 2 to 4 restrained from dealing in securities of the company.
- Order effective from September 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of interim restraint directions by SEBI against specified noticees.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the order and ensure strict compliance with the trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 17, 2026
- Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members must ensure that noticee nos. 2 to 4 do not execute transactions in the securities of Kore Digital Limited through their platforms.
