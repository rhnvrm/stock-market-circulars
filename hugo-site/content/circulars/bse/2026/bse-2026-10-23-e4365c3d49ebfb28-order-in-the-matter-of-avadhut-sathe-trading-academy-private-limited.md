## Summary

SEBI vide its order dated October 23, 2026, has issued directions debarring Avadhut Sathe Trading Academy Private Limited and associated noticees from accessing the securities market and prohibiting them from dealing in securities for specified periods.

## Key Points

- Noticees No. 1 and 2 are debarred and prohibited from buying, selling, or dealing in securities for a period of two (2) years or until compliance with specific directions.
- Noticee No. 3 is debarred and prohibited from accessing the securities market for a period of 6 months.
- Noticees with open derivative positions are permitted to square off/close out within 3 months or upon contract expiry, and settle prior transactions.
- The order is effective immediately from the date of the order.

## Regulatory Changes

SEBI enforcement action and debarment directions against entities involved in the matter.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the order and ensure compliance with the restrictions placed on the noticees.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 23, 2026
- Derivative Square-off Window: Within 3 months from the date of the order or contract expiry.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and safeguards market integrity by restricting penalized entities from participating in the securities market.
