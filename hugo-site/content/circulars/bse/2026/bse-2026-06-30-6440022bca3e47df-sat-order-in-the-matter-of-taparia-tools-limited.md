## Summary

BSE has issued a notice stating that pursuant to an email from SEBI dated June 30, 2026, all directions previously issued by SEBI against the Noticees in the matter of Taparia Tools Limited stand revoked, following the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated November 9, 2021.

## Key Points

- References previous Exchange Notice No. 20190626-33 dated June 26, 2019.
- Notes that the Hon’ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) vide its order dated November 9, 2021, quashed the WTM order dated June 26, 2019, and the interim order dated May 20, 2015.
- SEBI confirmed via email on June 30, 2026, that all directions issued against all Noticees stand revoked.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revocation of earlier directions issued by SEBI against the Noticees regarding Taparia Tools Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the revocation and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: June 30, 2026
- Original WTM Order: June 26, 2019
- SAT Order Date: November 9, 2021

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact as this resolves a long-standing legal proceeding involving specific parties.
