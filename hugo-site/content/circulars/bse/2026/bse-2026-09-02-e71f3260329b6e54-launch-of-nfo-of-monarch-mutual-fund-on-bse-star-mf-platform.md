## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Monarch Mutual Fund's 'Monarch Overnight Fund' on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from September 03, 2026, to September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Monarch Overnight Fund
- **Scheme Category:** Overnight
- **NFO Opening Date:** September 03, 2026
- **NFO Closing Date:** September 07, 2026 (extended till midnight for lumpsum purchases)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 09, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, with a 'N' flag reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 09, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted during the NFO period, subject to clear funds receipt by the designated deadline.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Period:** September 03, 2026 to September 07, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** September 09, 2026
- **Funds Pay-in Deadline:** September 09, 2026 by 6:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscription and trading of Monarch Overnight Fund units for members and investors through the BSE StAR MF platform.
