## Summary

BSE has announced that the ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031 will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and STP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting September 21, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20260825-40.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopens for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and STP facilities.
- Effective date: September 21, 2026.
- Affected ISINs include ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2031 - Regular Plan - Growth (INF109K1A591) and Direct Plan - Growth (INF109K1A609).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and participants on the BSE StAR MF platform are to take note of the availability of the scheme for ongoing transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute ongoing transactions and systematic transfer plans (STP) for the specified ICICI Prudential scheme on the BSE StAR MF platform.
