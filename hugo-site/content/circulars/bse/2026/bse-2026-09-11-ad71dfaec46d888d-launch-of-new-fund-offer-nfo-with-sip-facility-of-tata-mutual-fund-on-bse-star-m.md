## Summary

TATA Mutual Fund is launching its New Fund Offer (NFO) for the TATA CRISIL-IBX FINANCIAL SERVICES 3-6 MONTHS DEBT INDEX FUND along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The NFO period runs from September 15, 2026, to September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: TATA CRISIL-IBX FINANCIAL SERVICES 3-6 MONTHS DEBT INDEX FUND (Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- Subscription Window: September 15, 2026, to September 22, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 24, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 23, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: September 15, 2026
- NFO End Date: September 22, 2026
- Clear Funds Cut-off: September 23, 2026, by 6:00 PM
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to subscribe to the new debt index fund scheme and set up SIP facilities seamlessly through the BSE StAR MF infrastructure.
