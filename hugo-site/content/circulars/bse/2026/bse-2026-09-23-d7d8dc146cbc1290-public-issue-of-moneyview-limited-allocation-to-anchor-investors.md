## Summary

Trading members are informed that under the Anchor Investors (AIs) portion of the public issue of Moneyview Limited, 96,324,729 equity shares have been subscribed at Rs 34 per equity share on September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Moneyview Limited
- Segment: Equity
- Issue Type: Public Issue (Anchor Investor Portion)
- Shares Subscribed: 96,324,729 Equity Shares
- Issue Price/Allocation Price: Rs 34/- per equity share
- Date of Notice/Announcement: September 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the anchor investor allocation details for operational and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2026
- Announcement Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding institutional participation and anchor investor allotment ahead of the broader public issue opening.
