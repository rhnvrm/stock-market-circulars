## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding modifications in the pre-open call auction session for the equity derivatives segment, referencing previous circulars and a SEBI directive from January 2026. Trading members are instructed to review the updated FAQ document and ensure proper client-facing application integration.

## Key Points

- Refers to SEBI circular dated January 16, 2026, and BSE circular dated August 03, 2026.
- Encloses an updated FAQ document as an Annexure available on the Exchange website under Markets → Derivatives → Market Information → Pre-open Session.
- Members must ensure all fields and information related to the pre-open session are displayed appropriately in client-facing applications.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of modified procedures for the pre-open call auction session in the equity derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that client-facing applications appropriately display all fields and information related to the pre-open session.

## Important Dates

- FAQ availability on website: August 26, 2026
- Effective for live trading: Monday, September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational adjustments required for trading members and software vendors supporting client-facing derivatives trading applications.
