## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced the schedule for client margin reporting and associated penalty dates for the Equity Derivatives segment covering trade dates in October 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines specific trade dates, last dates of margin reporting, and penalty dates for October 2026.
- Covers trading dates from 01-10-2026 through 30-10-2026.
- Designed to assist trading members in adhering to timely margin reporting timelines and avoiding penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Routine operational schedule update with no new regulatory mandates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure client margins are reported within the prescribed deadlines corresponding to each trade date in October 2026 to prevent penalty charges.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 29 September 2026
- Schedule Period: October 2026 (Trade dates spanning 01-10-2026 to 30-10-2026)

## Impact Assessment

Members operating in the Equity Derivatives segment must integrate these reporting and penalty dates into their operational workflows and back-office systems for October 2026.
