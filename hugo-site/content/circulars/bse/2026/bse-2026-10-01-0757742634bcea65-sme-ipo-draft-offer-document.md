## Summary

BSE has published a notice announcing that Quality Enviro Engineers Limited has filed its Draft Offer Document on September 30, 2026, for its upcoming SME Initial Public Offering (IPO). In compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations, the document is made available on the stock exchange website to invite public comments.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Quality Enviro Engineers Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Regulatory Framework: Schedule VI of ICDR Regulations
- Public comment submission email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; filing is governed by existing ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Interested stakeholders and the public may submit comments on the Draft Offer Document within 21 days of the notice to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comment Deadline: 21 days from October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Limited direct impact on the broader market as part of standard SME primary market issuance procedures.
