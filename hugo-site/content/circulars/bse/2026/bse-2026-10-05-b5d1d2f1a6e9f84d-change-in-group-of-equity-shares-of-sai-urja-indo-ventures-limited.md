## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of SAI URJA INDO VENTURES LIMITED will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Monday, October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SAI URJA INDO VENTURES LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544958
- Segment Transition: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026
- Reference: Notice No. 20261005-3

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of trading group and settlement segment for the company's equity shares under the SME segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems and records regarding the change in trading segment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 5, 2026
- Effective Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates normal rolling settlement for the scrip, enhancing liquidity and operational efficiency for market participants.
