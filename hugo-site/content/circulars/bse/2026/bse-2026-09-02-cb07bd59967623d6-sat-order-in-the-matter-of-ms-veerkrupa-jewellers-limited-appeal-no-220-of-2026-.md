## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) issued a notice regarding the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order in the matter of M/s Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited (Appeal No. 220 of 2026) concerning noticee Rushabh Pradeep Shroff. SEBI confirmed that the appellant has paid the full penalty amount, fulfilling the condition for the stay of debarment.

## Key Points

- SAT vide its order dated July 20, 2026, directed a stay of debarment subject to the deposit of the full penalty to be kept in an interest-bearing account by SEBI.
- SEBI confirmed via email on September 02, 2026, that the appellant, Mr. Rushabh Pradeep Shroff, has successfully paid the penalty amount.
- Trading members are advised to take note and ensure compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of the SAT-directed stay of debarment following the deposit of the penalty amount.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and concerned parties must take note of the payment of the penalty and the subsequent status of debarment.

## Important Dates

- July 20, 2026: SAT order date directing stay of debarment upon penalty deposit.
- September 02, 2026: SEBI email confirming penalty payment; BSE notice issuance date.

## Impact Assessment

- Resolves the immediate debarment status condition for the individual noticee upon penalty deposit, ensuring adherence to regulatory and tribunal directives.
