## Summary

BSE has announced a revision in the price bands for 23 scrips under its equity segment, effective from August 25, 2026, as part of its ongoing market surveillance measures.

## Key Points

- Total scrips affected: 23
- Effective date: August 25, 2026
- Department: DOSS (Surveillance)

## Regulatory Changes

Price bands for specified scrips have been revised to new percentage limits ranging from 2% to 20% to manage stock price volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure their trading systems reflect the updated limits to prevent order rejections.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in the listed scrips will be subject to the new intraday price movement limits, impacting execution strategies for high-volatility orders.
