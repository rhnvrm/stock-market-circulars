## Summary

Midaas Fashions Ltd has announced the sub-division of its existing equity shares. The face value of equity shares will be sub-divided from Rs. 10/- each into two equity shares of Rs. 5/- each. The record date for the corporate action is October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Midaas Fashions Ltd
- Scrip Code: 530757
- Old ISIN: INE0OUS01011 (valid up to 15/10/2026)
- Existing Share Face Value: Rs. 10/-
- New Share Face Value: Rs. 5/-
- Ratio: 1:2 (One equity share of Rs. 10/- into two equity shares of Rs. 5/-)
- Record Date: 26/10/2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Stock split / sub-division processed under rolling settlement segment.
- Existing ISIN (INE0OUS01011) will become invalid for exchange transactions on or after 16/10/2026.
- New ISIN for the Rs. 5/- paid-up shares will be notified separately.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their records and trading systems regarding the ISIN invalidation date and record date.
- Transactions on or after 16/10/2026 cannot be executed under the old ISIN INE0OUS01011.

## Important Dates

- ISIN Invalidation Date: 16/10/2026
- Record Date: 26/10/2026
- Sub-Division / New ISIN Effective Date / W.E.F.: 26/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

The sub-division increases liquidity by increasing the number of shares outstanding while reducing the share price proportionally, affecting trading units and depository balances for shareholders.
