## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Parmax Pharma Limited. The bidding period concluded on August 12, 2026, with the revised obligation download scheduled for August 18, 2026, and the settlement date set for August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Parmax Pharma Limited
- **Settlement Type:** Takeover (Settlement No. 2026095)
- **Bidding Period:** 30/07/2026 to 12/08/2026
- **Obligation Download Date:** 18/08/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 19/08/2026
- **Demat Shares Delivery:** Must be delivered strictly through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement number 2026095.
- **Stamp Duty Charges:** Members are required to refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty details.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and custodians must use the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism for delivering demat shares and reference Notice No. 20200528-32 for compliance with the revised guidelines for acquisition through the stock exchange mechanism.
