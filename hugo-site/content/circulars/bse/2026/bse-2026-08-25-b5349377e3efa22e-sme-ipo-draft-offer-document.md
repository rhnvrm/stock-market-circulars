## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the Draft Offer Document filed by Dain Colour Chem Limited for its SME IPO. The document is hosted on the stock exchange website to seek public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Dain Colour Chem Limited
- Filing Date: August 21, 2026
- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Purpose: Seeking public comments under ICDR Regulations Schedule VI
- Hosting Website: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments on the draft offer document can be submitted via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days from the date of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Public Comment Deadline: Within 21 days of the notice

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and the public an opportunity to review and comment on the proposed SME IPO of Dain Colour Chem Limited.
