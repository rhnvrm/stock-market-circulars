## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in debt securities of JSW JFE Kalinga Steel Limited due to debt restructuring, effective from October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Affected Scrip Codes: 977660 and 977661.
- Associated ISINs: INE2QI808025 and INE2QI808033.
- Effective Date of Suspension: Wednesday, October 28, 2026.
- Reason for Suspension: Debt restructuring.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to note the suspension and contact designated exchange officials for further queries.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: October 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified debt securities of JSW JFE Kalinga Steel Limited will be halted, impacting holders and trading members dealing with these instruments.
