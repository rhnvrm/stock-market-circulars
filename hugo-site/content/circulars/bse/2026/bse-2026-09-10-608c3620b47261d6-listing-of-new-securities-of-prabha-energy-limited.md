## Summary

BSE has announced that trading members are permitted to trade in the new securities of Prabha Energy Limited (Scrip Code: 544379) effective from September 11, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Prabha Energy Limited
- Scrip Code: 544379
- ISIN: INE0I0M01023
- Securities: 95,13,903 equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up (Face Value: Re. 1/- each issued on rights basis).
- Distinctive Numbers: 332772522 to 342286424
- Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Pari-passu: The new shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can trade in the newly listed fully paid-up equity shares starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 10, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for market participants holding or trading the securities of Prabha Energy Limited, reflecting the complete capitalization of the rights issue shares.
