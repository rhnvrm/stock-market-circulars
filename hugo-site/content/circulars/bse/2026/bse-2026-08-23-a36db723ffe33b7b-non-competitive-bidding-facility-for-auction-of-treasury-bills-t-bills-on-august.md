## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Treasury Bills (T-Bills) scheduled for August 27, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated August 21, 2026. The auction includes 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day T-Bills.

## Key Points

- Three Treasury Bills are being auctioned: 91 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 091T261126, Issue Size: Rs. 9,000 Cr), 182 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 182T250227, Issue Size: Rs. 8,000 Cr), and 364 Day T-Bill (Symbol: 364T270827, Issue Size: Rs. 7,000 Cr).
- Bidding takes place through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange’s iBBS web-based system.
- Minimum lot and multiples are set at Rs. 10,000.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is conducted in accordance with RBI guidelines and Exchange circular no. 20180423-42 dated April 26, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: August 24, 2026, from 10:00 AM onwards (24 hours availability)
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investors): August 26, 2026, till 5:00 PM
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): August 27, 2026, till 8:00 AM
- Auction Date: August 27, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates non-competitive participation for investors in Government of India Treasury Bill auctions through the BSE iBBS platform.
