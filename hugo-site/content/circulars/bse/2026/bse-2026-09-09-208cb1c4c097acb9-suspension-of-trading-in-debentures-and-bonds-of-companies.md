## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures and bonds of specified companies on account of record dates fixed for payment of interest and redemption.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260909-10, dated September 09, 2026.
- Department: Listing Operations.
- Record Date for the listed NCDs is set for 10/09/2026.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of the listed companies during the suspension period.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance by not dealing in the specified non-convertible debentures and bonds of the mentioned companies around the record date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 09 Sep 2026
- Record Date / Ex-Date: 10/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of trading for specific NCDs to facilitate interest payment and redemption processing.
