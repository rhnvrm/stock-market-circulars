## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. 712 for Rolling Settlement No. DR-712/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on September 10, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on September 10, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Friday, September 11, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 10, 2026
- Pay-in Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine settlement operations for clearing and trading members involved in short deliveries.
