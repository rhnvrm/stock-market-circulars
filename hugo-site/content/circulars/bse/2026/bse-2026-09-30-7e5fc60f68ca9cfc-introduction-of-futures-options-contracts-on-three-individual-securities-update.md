## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of Futures & Options (F&O) contracts on three individual securities within the Equity Derivatives segment, effective October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Anand Rathi Wealth Ltd (ARWL)**: Scrip Code 543415, Market Lot: 250, Strikes: 7-1-7, Strike Price Interval: 20.
- **Siemens Energy India Ltd (SEIL)**: Scrip Code 544390, Market Lot: 175, Strikes: 7-1-7, Strike Price Interval: 50.
- **Ujjivan Small Finance Bank Ltd (USBL)**: Scrip Code 542904, Market Lot: 8000, Strikes: 7-1-7, Strike Price Interval: 1.
- Contract parameters and details are made available in the derivatives contract master file generated on September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Addition of derivative contracts for ARWL, SEIL, and USBL on the BSE exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refer to the derivatives contract master file available on the extranet and BSEIndia website for operational execution and contract specifications.

## Important Dates

- **Master File Release**: September 30, 2026 (End of Day)
- **Effective Date**: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances trading and hedging opportunities for market participants in the equity derivatives segment for the newly included securities.
