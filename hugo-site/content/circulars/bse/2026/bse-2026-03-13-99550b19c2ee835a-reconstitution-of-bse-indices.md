## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the periodic reconstitution of its various indices, specifying the companies being added and dropped across different sectoral indices such as BSE Commodities, BSE Consumer Discretionary, and BSE Financial Services.

## Key Points

- Effective date for the index reconstitution is March 23, 2026.
- Lists additions and drops for BSE Commodities, BSE Consumer Discretionary, and BSE Financial Services indices.
- New entrants include companies like Jsw Cement Ltd., Physicswallah Ltd., Urban Company Ltd., Piramal Finance Ltd., Pine Labs Ltd., and National Securities Depository Ltd.
- Exclusions include companies like Sadhana Nitrochem Ltd., Pvp Ventures Ltd., and Worth Investment Trading Co Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Index tracking funds and institutional investors need to rebalance their portfolios in accordance with the index reconstitution effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: March 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Stocks added to the indices may experience increased buying pressure from passive index funds, while dropped stocks may see outflows.
