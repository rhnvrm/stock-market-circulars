## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying criteria for inclusion, movement to lower stages, or exclusion. Effective October 06, 2026 (with certain changes effective October 05, 2026), affected securities will attract minimum 100% margins and/or be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 06, 2026, on open positions as of October 05, 2026, and new positions.
- Certain securities move to lower Stage I under the ESM framework w.e.f. October 05, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM shift from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. October 06, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II come under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 05, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework are listed in Annexure II, with price bands reinstated.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) for identified scrips.
- Mandatory 100% margin requirements on open and new positions.
- Migration of qualifying securities to the Trade-for-Trade settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements for affected securities from October 06, 2026.
- Market participants must note segment and price band adjustments for scrips moving across ESM stages.

## Important Dates

- October 05, 2026: Effective date for movement to lower stages, Stage II restrictions, and exits from the framework.
- October 06, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin requirements and shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

High operational and liquidity impact for traders and investors holding or trading securities placed under the ESM framework due to increased margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade settlement restrictions.
