## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of Minolta Finance Limited are permitted on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: MINOLTA FINANCE LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751127
- Scrip ID: MINOLT-RE
- ISIN No.: INE514C20018
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: July 30, 2026 to August 11, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: July 30, 2026 to August 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Rights Entitlements (REs) shall be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis.
- Trading shall be conducted in dematerialized form only.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes/bills for the purchase of REs must include a prominent disclaimer informing clients that purchase only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce the REs before closure.
- REs neither subscribed nor renounced by the issue closing date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- Trading Period Start Date: July 30, 2026
- Trading Period End Date (Renunciation): August 11, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: July 30, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides eligible shareholders the mechanism to trade their rights entitlements or apply for additional shares in the rights issue of Minolta Finance Limited.
