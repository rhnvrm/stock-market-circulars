## Summary

SEBI issued an order on July 31, 2026 (Order No. QJA/MN/CFID/CFID-SEC4/32566/2026-27) imposing debarment and restrictions on Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd. and key individuals including Mr. Punit Goenka and Mr. Subhash Chandra in the matter of unauthorized pledge of immovable property.

## Key Points

- Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd. is restrained from accessing the securities market for 2 months (PAN: AAACZ0243R).
- Mr. Punit Goenka is restrained from accessing the securities market for 12 months (PAN: AAEPG2529E).
- Mr. Subhash Chandra is restrained from accessing the securities market for 12 months (PAN: AACPC4004A).
- Open positions in exchange-traded derivative contracts can be squared off within 3 months or upon expiry, whichever is earlier.
- Pre-existing settlement obligations prior to the order can be settled normally.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of market debarment on the noticees for specified periods as per SEBI order dated July 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and listed entities must take note of the debarment and ensure compliance with SEBI directions.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: July 31, 2026
- Effective Date: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High governance and compliance impact on Zee Entertainment Enterprises Ltd. and restricted personnel regarding market access and trading activities.
