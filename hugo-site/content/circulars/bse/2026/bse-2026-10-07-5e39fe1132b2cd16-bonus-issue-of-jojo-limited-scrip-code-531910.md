## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that JOJO LIMITED has fixed the record date for a bonus issue of equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: JOJO LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 531910
- Bonus Ratio: 1:1 (One new bonus equity share of Rs. 5/- each for every one existing equity share held)
- Record Date: October 12, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: October 12, 2026
- Total Shares to be Allotted: 68,961,400 equity shares (as informed by the company as of October 08, 2026)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; standard corporate action processing under exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must ensure proper settlement and take note of the ex-bonus date and record date for transactions.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 12, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Eligible shareholders will receive additional equity shares in a 1:1 ratio, leading to a corresponding price adjustment on the ex-bonus date and an increase in total outstanding shares.
