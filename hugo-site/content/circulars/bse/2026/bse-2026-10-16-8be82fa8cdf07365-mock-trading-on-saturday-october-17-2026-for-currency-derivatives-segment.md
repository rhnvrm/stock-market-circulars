## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for its Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives segments on Saturday, October 17, 2026. Trading members utilizing third-party empaneled vendor platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their applications and functionalities under simulated market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 17, 2026.
- Applicable segments: Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives.
- Session Timings: Log-in from 09:15 AM to 10:00 AM; Continuous Trading from 10:00 AM to 01:00 PM.
- No new BOLT TWS Release associated with this session.
- Trades resulting from mock trading attract no margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations and create no rights or liabilities.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 02:00 PM on the day of the session.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively in the mock trading session and submit feedback to the BSE Helpdesk or Tech Support team by 02:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 17, 2026
- Feedback Deadline: October 17, 2026 (02:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Enables members to verify technical infrastructure readiness and application stability with zero financial or regulatory risk.
