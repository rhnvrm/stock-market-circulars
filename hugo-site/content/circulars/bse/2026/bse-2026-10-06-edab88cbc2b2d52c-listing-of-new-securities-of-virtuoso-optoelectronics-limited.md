## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of Virtuoso Optoelectronics Limited (Scrip Code - 543597) effective from Wednesday, October 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- 16,85,392 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 346/- to non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- Issue Price: Rs. 356/-
- ISIN: INE0I0T01010
- Date of Allotment: August 13, 2026
- Lock-in Details: 16,85,392 shares (Dist. Nos. 32565680 to 34251071) locked in up to April 14, 2027.
- These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members take note of the listing and trading permission of the new securities effective October 7, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: October 7, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: April 14, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard listing of preferential equity shares with no broader market or regulatory impact.
