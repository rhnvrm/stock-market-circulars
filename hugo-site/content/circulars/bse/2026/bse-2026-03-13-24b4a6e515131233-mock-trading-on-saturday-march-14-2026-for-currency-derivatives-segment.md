## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment on Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are required to test their connectivity to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site using the specified parameters for BOLT TWS, ETI APIs, and web-based applications.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Currency Derivatives segment.
- Details provided for connecting to the DR site of the BOLT Plus trading system.
- ETI API connection parameters: Primary DR IP 10.255.241.26, Port 13910; Secondary DR IP 10.255.241.27, Port 13910.
- Exchange web-based systems (RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, Extranet, etc.) accessible via designated URLs and IP configurations.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Operational mock trading notification only.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are encouraged to participate in the mock trading session to verify their systems and DR connectivity setup.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Zero impact on live market operations. Enables members to validate business continuity and disaster recovery readiness.
