## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the record date and payment of interest/part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) for several companies. Trading in the debentures of these companies will take place with reduced face value effective from the specified record/ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **SI Creva Capital Services Private Limited**: Scrip 978300 (ISIN: INE996U07438), Record Date: 29/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 8,333.37 per debenture.
- **U.P. Power Corporation Limited**: Scrip 955772 (ISIN: INE540P07103), Record Date: 30/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 2,50,000/- per debenture.
- **Fedbank Financial Services Limited**: Scrip 975918 (ISIN: INE007N07058), Record Date: 30/10/2026, Reduced Face Value: Rs. 43,750/- per debenture.
- **Satin Housing Finance Limited**: Scrip 976409 (ISIN: INE02YC07034), Record Date: 31/10/2026 (Ex-date: 30/10/2026), Reduced Face Value: Rs. 33,334/- per debenture.
- **Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited**: Scrip 977126 (ISIN: INE08XP07357) and Scrip 977127 (ISIN: INE08XP07365), Record Date: 01/11/2026 (Ex-date: 30/10/2026), Reduced Face Value: Rs. 45,450/- per debenture.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action notice communicating debt redemption schedules and reduced face values.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must take note of the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for debt trading and settlement.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date**: 21/10/2026
- **Record / Ex-Dates**: 29/10/2026 to 01/11/2026

## Impact Assessment

Directly impacts holders and traders of the specified Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) by updating face values and managing redemption payouts.
