## Summary

Alphagrep Mutual Fund is offering the Alphagrep Liquid Omni FOF New Fund Offer (NFO) along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility from August 04, 2026, to August 06, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Scheme Offered: Alphagrep Liquid Omni FOF (Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout options).
- NFO Window: August 04, 2026, to August 06, 2026 (extended till 6:00 PM on August 06, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 07, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on August 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (August 06, 2026).
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment at the AMC/RTA end.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: August 04, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: August 06, 2026 (6:00 PM for Lumpsum)
- SIP Authentication Deadline: August 06, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- Funds Pay-in Deadline: August 06, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for mutual fund distributors and investors participating in the Alphagrep Liquid Omni FOF NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
