## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has notified trading members that 8,35,000 new equity shares of ITCONS E-Solutions Limited (Scrip Code: 543806, ISIN: INE0O6A01019) have been listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, August 13, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants at an issue price of Rs. 71/- per share (including Rs. 61/- premium) with an allotment date of January 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Securities Listed:** 8,35,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each.
- **Effective Date:** Listed and permitted to trade from Thursday, August 13, 2026.
- **Issue Details:** Issued at Rs. 71/- per share (face value Rs. 10/- plus premium of Rs. 61/-) to promoters and non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.
- **Pari-Passu:** Newly listed shares rank pari-passu with the company's existing equity shares.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 8173327 to 9008326.
- **Lock-in Details:**
  - 6,84,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 8173327 to 8857326) are locked in until March 4, 2028.
  - 1,51,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 8857327 to 9008326) are locked in until March 4, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no broader regulatory framework changes introduced by this circular. It is a standard listing operations notice issued under existing BSE guidelines for the listing of new securities arising from corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members:** Update trading systems to include the newly listed shares under the existing ISIN (INE0O6A01019) and Scrip Code (543806) to enable trading from August 13, 2026.
- **For the Issuer:** Maintain coordination with depositories (NSDL and CDSL) to enforce lock-in periods on the designated distinctive numbers.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** January 30, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry for 1,51,000 Shares:** March 4, 2027
- **Lock-in Expiry for 6,84,000 Shares:** March 4, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact:** Low immediate market impact as 82% (6,84,000 shares) of the newly listed equity shares are locked in for two years, and the remaining 18% (1,51,000 shares) are locked in for one year. This prevents any immediate dilution or selling pressure in the open market.
- **Share Capital Impact:** The listing increases the total paid-up share capital of ITCONS E-Solutions Limited, and these shares enjoy equal voting and dividend rights alongside existing shares.
