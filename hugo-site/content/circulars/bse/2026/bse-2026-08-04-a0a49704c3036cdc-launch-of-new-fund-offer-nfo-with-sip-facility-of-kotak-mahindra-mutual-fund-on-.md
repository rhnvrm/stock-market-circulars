## Summary

Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund is offering the Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF NFO along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform from August 05, 2026, to August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Kotak Diversified Equity All Cap Omni FOF (Regular and Direct Plans for Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, and IDCW Payout).
- Subscription period: August 05, 2026, to August 19, 2026 (extended till midnight on August 19, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative allotment date: August 24, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: August 05, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: August 19, 2026
- Clear Funds Cut-off: August 21, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides platform users and investors an opportunity to subscribe to the new fund offer and set up SIP facilities through the BSE StAR MF platform.
