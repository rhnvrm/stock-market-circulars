## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the inclusion of certain securities, movement of securities to lower stages (such as Stage I), and securities moving out of the framework. Qualified securities will attract a minimum 100% margin and shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin effective September 25, 2026, on all open positions as of September 24, 2026, and new positions.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective September 24, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective September 25, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III of the notice.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance mechanisms, including mandatory margin requirements and trading segment shifts for shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the revised margin requirements, price bands, and settlement segment modifications for the affected securities to ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II and moving out/lower stage movements: September 24, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shift: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin blocked for securities included or shifted within the ESM framework will be impacted, requiring heightened risk management by market participants.
