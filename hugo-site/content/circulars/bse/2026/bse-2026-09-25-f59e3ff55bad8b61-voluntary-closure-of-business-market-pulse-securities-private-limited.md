## Summary

Market Pulse Securities Private Limited (Member No. 6845) has announced the voluntary closure of its business. The member will cease to operate in the Cash and Equity derivative segments of the BSE.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: Market Pulse Securities Private Limited
- Member Number: 6845
- Segments Affected: Cash and Equity Derivative segments
- Effective Date: September 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes introduced; routine notice regarding membership cessation.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and clients associated with Market Pulse Securities Private Limited must take note of the cessation of business and settle any pending matters accordingly. Queries can be directed to membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date of Closure: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact on the broader market as this is a routine voluntary closure of an individual trading member.
