## Summary

BSE has announced a change in the underlying asset name and symbol in the equity derivative segment for ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited. Pursuant to BSE Circular No. 20261021-20, the underlying symbol 'ICICIPRULI' is renamed to 'ICICILIFE' (ICICI Life Insurance Limited) effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Old Underlying Symbol: ICICIPRULI
- New Underlying Symbol: ICICILIFE
- Scrip Code: 540133
- Company Name Change: ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited to ICICI Life Insurance Limited
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Files generated for trade date October 22, 2026, will reflect the existing symbol ICICIPRULI, while files from October 23, 2026, onwards will incorporate the revised symbol ICICILIFE.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are required to update their trading and risk management systems to reflect the new underlying symbol and name from October 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 22, 2026
- Implementation Date / Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational and system update required for members trading equity derivatives with underlying ICICIPRULI / ICICILIFE to ensure seamless contract master alignment.
