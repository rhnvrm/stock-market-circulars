## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of trading hours for corporate bonds executed on the Request for Quote (RFQ) platform in the NDS-RST system. Effective October 21, 2026, the market timings for T+0 settlement are extended to close at 5:00 PM.

## Key Points

- RFQ trading hours for T+0 settlement in corporate bonds (excluding G-Sec, SDL, and T-Bills) are revised from 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
- T+1 settlement hours remain 09:00 AM to 05:00 PM for all eligible securities.
- Effective date of revision: October 21, 2026.
- Continuation of BSE Notice No. 20200127-41 dated January 27, 2020.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of the closing time for corporate bond T+0 settlement on the RFQ platform from 4:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the revised timings and make necessary operational and system arrangements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides participants with an additional hour of trading window for T+0 settlement of corporate bonds on the RFQ platform, enhancing execution flexibility.
