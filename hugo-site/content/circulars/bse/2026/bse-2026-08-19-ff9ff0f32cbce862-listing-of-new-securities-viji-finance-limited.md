## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Viji Finance Limited (Scrip Code - 537820) effective from August 20, 2026. A total of 4,90,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 1.80/- to non-promoters on a preferential basis upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Viji Finance Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 537820
- **ISIN:** INE159N01027
- **Security Details:** 4,90,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1.80/- on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion.
- **Effective Date:** August 20, 2026
- **Ranking:** These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Allotment Details

- **29/06/2026:** 3,04,00,000 shares (Distinctive Nos. 142500001 - 172900000)
- **10/07/2026:** 1,86,00,000 shares (Distinctive Nos. 172900001 - 191500000)

## Compliance Requirements

- **Lock-in Details:** 4,90,00,000 shares with distinctive numbers 142500001 to 191500000 are locked-in up to February 27, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 19, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** August 20, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** February 27, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate action reflecting capital expansion through preferential allotment and warrant conversion for Viji Finance Limited with no direct systemic market impact.
