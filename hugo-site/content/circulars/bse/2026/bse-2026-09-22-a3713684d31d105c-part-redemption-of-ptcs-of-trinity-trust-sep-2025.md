## Summary

MITCON Credential Trusteeship Services Ltd has informed the Exchange regarding the partial redemption and reduced face value of Pass-through Certificates (PTCs) of Trinity Trust Sep 2025, effective from September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TRINITY TRUST SEP 2025
- Scrip Code: 977166
- ISIN: INE2JU715017
- Scrip ID: TTS25-11.75%-21-9-27-PTC
- Purpose: Part Redemption of PTCs
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 1463.32 per PTC
- Effective Date: September 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the reduced face value for trading of the specified PTCs.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date for Reduced Face Value Trading: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members will trade the PTCs of Trinity Trust Sep 2025 with the updated reduced face value starting from September 23, 2026.
