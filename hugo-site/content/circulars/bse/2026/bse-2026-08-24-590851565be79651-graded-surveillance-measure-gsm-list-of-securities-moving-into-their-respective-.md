## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from August 25, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and take adequate precautions.

## Key Points

- Securities will move to higher stages of GSM effective August 25, 2026.
- Stage I: Applicable margin rate of 100% and price band of 5% or lower.
- Stage II: Trade-for-trade with a price band of 5% or lower and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value to be deposited by buyers.
- Stage III & IV: Trade-for-trade with a price band of 5% or lower, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and ASD of 100% of trade value.
- Settlement for these securities shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened surveillance measures in accordance with the provisions of the GSM Framework originally published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent updates.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, ensure compliance with margin and deposit requirements, and note that settlements will occur on a gross trade-to-trade basis without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date of Higher GSM Stages: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities are likely to be restricted due to mandatory trade-for-trade settlement, higher margin requirements, and Additional Surveillance Deposits.
