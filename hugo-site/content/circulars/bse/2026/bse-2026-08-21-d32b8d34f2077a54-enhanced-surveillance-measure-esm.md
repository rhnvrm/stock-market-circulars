## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the criteria for inclusion, movement of securities to lower stages, shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment, and revised margin requirements effective August 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the ESM criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin effective August 25, 2026, on open positions as of August 24, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities will be moved to lower stages (e.g., Stage I) under the ESM framework effective August 24, 2026.
- Qualifying securities will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be subject to Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective August 24, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III of the notice.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements, Trade-for-Trade settlement, and price band restrictions for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure adequate margins are maintained and note the shift in settlement segments and price bands for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **August 24, 2026:** Effective date for securities moving to lower stages, Stage II restrictions (Trade-for-Trade, 2% price band, Periodic Call Auction), and securities moving out of the framework.
- **August 25, 2026:** Effective date for minimum 100% margin requirements on open/new positions and shifting of qualifying securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading activity in the shortlisted securities will experience increased friction due to 100% margin requirements and Trade-for-Trade settlement restrictions, which are intended to curb excessive volatility and enhance market integrity.
