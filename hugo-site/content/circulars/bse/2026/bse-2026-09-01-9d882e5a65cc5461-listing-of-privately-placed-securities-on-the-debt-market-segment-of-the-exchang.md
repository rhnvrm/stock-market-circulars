## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed debt securities issued by REC Limited on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** REC LIMITED
- **Security Type:** Privately Placed Debt Instrument (NCD)
- **Scrip Code:** 978100
- **Scrip ID:** 757RECL36
- **ISIN Number:** INE020B08GJ1
- **Issue Size / Quantity:** 250,200 units at Face Value of Rs. 100,000 each
- **Interest Rate:** 7.57% p.a., payable annually
- **Date of Allotment:** 31/08/2026
- **Date of Redemption:** 31/10/2036

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialised form using the specified ISIN and adhering to the tick size of 1 paise.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** 31/08/2026
- **Listing Effective Date:** 01/09/2026
- **Interest Payment:** Annually from 31/10/2027 to 31/10/2036
- **Redemption Date:** 31/10/2036

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operation enabling liquidity and secondary market trading for the newly listed debt securities of REC Limited.
