## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the Draft Offer Document for the SME IPO of Bon Bon Ventures Limited, filed on September 30, 2026. The document is hosted on the SME website for public comments in compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Issuer: Bon Bon Ventures Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Public Comment Window: 21 days from the notice date
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com
- Hosting URL: https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx

## Regulatory Changes

None. The filing adheres to Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Public comments on the draft offer document must be submitted via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 5, 2026
- Document Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Comment Deadline: 21 days from October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard procedural disclosure for an SME IPO with no immediate market-wide impact.
