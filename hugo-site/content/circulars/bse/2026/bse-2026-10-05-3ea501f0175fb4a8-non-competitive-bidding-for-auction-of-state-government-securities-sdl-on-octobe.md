## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled on October 06, 2026, following the RBI press release. Trading members can participate through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Auction Date: October 06, 2026.
- Settlement Date: October 07, 2026.
- Bid Collection Start Date: October 05, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards (24 hours availability).
- Bid Collection End Date for Direct Investors: October 05, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- Bid Collection End Date for Members: October 06, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- Securities involved include re-issues and new issues of State Development Loans (SDLs) from various states such as AP, DL, GA, HP, JK, KA, MP, MH, ML, PB, RJ, TS, and WB.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit bids through the NCB-GSec module of the iBBS web-based system within the stipulated timelines.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start: October 05, 2026, 10:00 AM
- Direct Investor Bid End: October 05, 2026, 5:00 PM
- Member Bid End: October 06, 2026, 8:00 AM
- Auction Date: October 06, 2026
- Settlement Date: October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates participation of eligible investors and trading members in the non-competitive bidding auction of State Government Securities.
