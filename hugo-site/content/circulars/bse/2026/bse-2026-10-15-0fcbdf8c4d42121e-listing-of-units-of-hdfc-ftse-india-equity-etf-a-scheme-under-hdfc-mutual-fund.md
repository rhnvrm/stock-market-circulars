## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units of HDFC FTSE India Equity ETF under HDFC Mutual Fund, effective from October 16, 2026, in the 'B' Group.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: HDFC FTSE India Equity ETF (Open-Ended Scheme replicating/tracking FTSE India Equity Index (TRI)).
- Mutual Fund: HDFC Mutual Fund.
- Investment Manager: HDFC Asset Management Company Limited.
- Scrip Code: 544988, Scrip ID: HFTSEINDIA, ISIN: INF179KC1LA2.
- Number of Units: 2,25,03,202 units.
- Issue Price / Face Value: Rs. 30.34 per unit.
- Date of Allotment: October 12, 2026.
- Market Lot: 1 unit.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Listing conducted as per standard guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Units of the Mutual Fund will be traded strictly in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Trading will be in unit market lot (i.e. One unit).

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expands investment options for market participants by listing a new FTSE India Equity ETF scheme on the exchange.
