## Summary

Edelweiss Ultra Short Term Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and facilities like SIP, STP, and SWP on the BSE StAR MF Platform with effect from August 27, 2026, pursuant to notice no. 20260817-43.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopens for ongoing transactions effective August 27, 2026.
- Facilities available include subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Covers both Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard compliance and operational guidelines for mutual fund transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform apply.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to transact in various ISINs of Edelweiss Ultra Short Term Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
