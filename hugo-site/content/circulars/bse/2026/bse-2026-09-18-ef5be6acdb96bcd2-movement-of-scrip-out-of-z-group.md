## Summary

 BSE has announced the movement of Suraj Products Ltd (Scrip Code: 518075) out of the 'Z' group effective September 29, 2026, pursuant to SEBI Master Circular provisions.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Suraj Products Ltd
- Scrip Code: 518075
- Transfer Group: Moved out of 'Z' Group to 'X' Group
- Effective Date: September 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Action taken pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 (Chapter VII (A) - Penal Action for Non-Compliance).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and stakeholders are requested to note the category change for trading and settlement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances trading status and visibility for Suraj Products Ltd as it moves out of the non-compliant 'Z' group.
