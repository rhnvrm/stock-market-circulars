## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the Offer to Buy (OTB) acquisition window for the delisting of equity shares of AVADH RAIL INFRA LIMITED. The bidding is scheduled from Tuesday, August 18, 2026, to Monday, August 24, 2026. The company is listed on The Calcutta Stock Exchange Limited (CSE).

## Key Points

- Security Name: AVADH RAIL INFRA LIMITED
- Security Symbol: AVADHRIL
- ISIN: INE0LQ901017
- Scrip Code: 999438
- Offer Size: Up to 4,765 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1
- Face Value: Rs. 10.00
- Offer Price: Rs. 4660.00
- Opening Date: Tuesday, 18th August 2026
- Closing Date: Monday, 24th August 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None specific to regulations; routine operational notice for a delisting offer to buy window.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and Custodians must access platforms via prescribed URLs (iBBS, RTRMS, Extranet).

## Important Dates

- Opening Date: 18th August 2026
- Closing Date: 24th August 2026
- Upper Modification and Cancellation: 18th August 2026 to 21st August 2026
- Give-up / Take-up period: 18th August 2026 to 21st August 2026
- Give-up (last day of offer): 24th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- Take-up (last day of offer): 24th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 4.00 p.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the delisting process for Avadh Rail Infra Limited through the acquisition window on the Exchange, allowing eligible shareholders to tender their shares at the specified offer price of Rs. 4660.00.
