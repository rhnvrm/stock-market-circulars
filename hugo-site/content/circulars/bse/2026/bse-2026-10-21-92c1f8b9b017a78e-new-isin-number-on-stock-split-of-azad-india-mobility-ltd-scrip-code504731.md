## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of Azad India Mobility Ltd consequent to a stock split.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Azad India Mobility Ltd
- Scrip Code: 504731
- New ISIN: INE566M01025
- Stock Split Details: Sub-division of equity shares from face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-
- Ex-Date: 23-10-2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Allocation of new ISIN number following the corporate action of stock split.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their systems with the new ISIN for transactions in the company's equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 21 October 2026
- Ex-Date: 23 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for trading and settlement systems reflecting the new ISIN and face value change.
