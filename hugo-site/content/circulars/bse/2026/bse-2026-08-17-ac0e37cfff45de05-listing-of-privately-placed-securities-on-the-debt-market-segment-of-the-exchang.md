## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers on its debt market segment effective August 17, 2026. The listing covers issuances from companies including Redington Limited, Godrej Finance Limited, A.K. Capital Finance Limited, and Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Debt Market Segment
- **Effective Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Form:** Dematerialised form under specific ISINs
- **Trading Parameters:** Standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise.
- **Issuers Involved:** Redington Limited, Godrej Finance Limited, A.K. Capital Finance Limited, and Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard issuance and listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard dematerialized trading protocols and standard denominations for commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Allotment / Effective Dates:** August 14, 2026, and August 17, 2026 across various issues.
- **Redemption Dates:** Maturity dates range from November 12, 2026, to November 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for market participants dealing in debt instruments and commercial papers.
