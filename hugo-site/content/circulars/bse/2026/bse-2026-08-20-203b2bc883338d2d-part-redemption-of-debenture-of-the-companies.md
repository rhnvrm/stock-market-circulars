## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the record dates fixed for the payment of interest and part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) of specified companies. Trading in the debentures will be conducted with reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Companies involved: Share India Securities Limited and Kanakadurga Finance Limited.
- Purpose: Payment of Interest and Part Redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures.
- Trading with reduced face value effective from the specified ex-dates.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the record dates, ex-dates, and reduced face values for the respective NCDs to ensure accurate settlement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 20 Aug 2026
- Record Date: 26/08/2026 (for Share India Securities) and 31/08/2026 (for Kanakadurga Finance)
- Ex-Date: 25/08/2026 (for Share India Securities) and 31/08/2026 (for Kanakadurga Finance)

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification for market participants holding or trading the specified NCDs regarding face value reduction and redemption payouts.
