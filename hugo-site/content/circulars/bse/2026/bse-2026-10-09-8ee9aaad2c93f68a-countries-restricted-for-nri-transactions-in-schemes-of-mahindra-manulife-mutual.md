## Summary

BSE StAR MF has received communication from Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund regarding restrictions on NRI applications in its mutual fund schemes and Special Investment Funds (SIFs) on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect. Applications from NRI investors residing in specified restricted countries will not be accepted.

## Key Points

- Applies to mutual fund schemes and SIFs of Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Restricted countries list:
  1. Myanmar
  2. Pakistan
  3. Korea, Democratic Peoples Republic Of
  4. Iran, Islamic Republic Of
  5. Canada
- References previous notices No. 20200619-29 (June 19, 2020) and 20260929-41 (September 29, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

Mandatory restriction on acceptance of investment applications from NRI investors residing in the listed countries for Mahindra Manulife AMC schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors must ensure that new subscription or transaction applications from NRI investors residing in Myanmar, Pakistan, North Korea, Iran, and Canada are not submitted or processed for Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund schemes and SIFs.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 9, 2026 (Immediate effect)
- Notice Date: October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

NRI investors residing in the restricted jurisdictions will be unable to invest in or transact through Mahindra Manulife Mutual Fund schemes and SIFs on the BSE StAR MF platform, ensuring compliance with AMC directives and regulatory risk parameters.
