## Summary

BSE has announced the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities owing to their upcoming maturity dates. Trading members are instructed not to deal in these securities with effect from August 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three specific securities (T-bills) are reaching maturity.
- Affected Scrip Codes: 805176, 805102, 805211.
- Effective date for cessation of trading: August 04, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to execute any deals in the specified T-bills/Government Securities starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 03, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational impact limited to the maturity and settlement of the specified T-bills.
