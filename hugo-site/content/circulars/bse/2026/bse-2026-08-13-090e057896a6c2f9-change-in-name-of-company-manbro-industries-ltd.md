## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the change in the name of Manbro Industries Ltd to KD Green Industries Limited. The scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated accordingly effective August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Manbro Industries Ltd
- New Company Name: KD Green Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 512595
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): KDGREEN
- New Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus): KDGREEN

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their internal systems to reflect the new name and scrip identifiers for Manbro Industries Ltd.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating trading terminals and reference data systems with the new name and scrip identifiers.
