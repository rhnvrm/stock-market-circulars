## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for the UTI NIFTY 500 Exchange Traded Fund by UTI Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription period runs from August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund Offering: UTI NIFTY 500 Exchange Traded Fund and UTI NIFTY 500 Exchange Traded Fund - Direct Plan - Growth.
- NFO Period: August 10, 2026, to August 24, 2026 (extended till midnight on August 24, 2026, for lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 25, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: August 10, 2026
- NFO End Date: August 24, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors an opportunity to subscribe to the new index-based ETF through the BSE StAR MF platform.
