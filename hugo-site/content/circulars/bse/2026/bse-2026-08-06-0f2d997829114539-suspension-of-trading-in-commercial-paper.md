## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of Commercial Papers (CPs). Members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies as of the respective record and ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice covers multiple Commercial Papers scheduled for redemption in August 2026.
- Affected entities include ECL Finance Limited, Tata Steel Limited, Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited, ICICI Home Finance Company Limited, and others.
- Trading members are instructed to refrain from trading in these specific CPs starting from the respective ex-dates (ranging from 07/08/2026 to 19/08/2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable (Routine Corporate Action notification).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no dealings take place in the specified commercial paper securities after the respective record/ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 06/08/2026
- **Record / Ex-Dates:** 07/08/2026, 10/08/2026, 12/08/2026, and 18/08/2026 to 19/08/2026 depending on the specific company.

## Impact Assessment

- Prevents secondary market trading in commercial papers that are reaching maturity and undergoing redemption processes.
