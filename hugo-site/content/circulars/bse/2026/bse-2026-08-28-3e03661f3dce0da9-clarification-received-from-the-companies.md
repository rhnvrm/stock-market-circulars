## Summary

The Exchange has noted significant price and volume movements in certain scrips in the recent past. To ensure investors and trading members have access to the latest relevant information, clarifications were requested from the respective companies. Replies received have been attached, while responses from several other companies remain awaited.

## Key Points

- **Companies with Replies Received:**
  - 3M India Ltd (Scrip Code: 523395)
  - Advit Jewels Ltd (Scrip Code: 544803)
  - Alok Industries Ltd (Scrip Code: 521070)
  - Bombay Dyeing & Manufacturing Company Ltd (Scrip Code: 500020)
  - Genesys International Corporation Ltd (Scrip Code: 506109)
  - Jindal Worldwide Ltd (Scrip Code: 531543)
  - NMDC Steel Ltd (Scrip Code: 543768)

- **Companies with Replies Awaited:**
  - Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd (Scrip Code: 542176)
  - CMX Holdings Ltd (Scrip Code: 532217)
  - Deep Polymers Ltd (Scrip Code: 541778)
  - INOX India Ltd (Scrip Code: 544046)
  - Mac Hotels Ltd (Scrip Code: 541973)
  - Mahesh Developers Ltd (Scrip Code: 542677)
  - Mayur Leather Products Ltd (Scrip Code: 531680)
  - Moschip Technologies Ltd (Scrip Code: 532407)
  - Network People Services Technologies Ltd (Scrip Code: 544396)
  - PVP Ventures Ltd (Scrip Code: 517556)
  - Triumph International Finance India Ltd (Scrip Code: 532131)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to timely clarify significant market movements when requested by the Exchange to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 28 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding abnormal price and volume movements across specified listed scrips.
