## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors under the public issue of Paras Healthcare Limited. A total of 94,15,584 equity shares have been subscribed at a price of Rs. 693/- per equity share on October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Paras Healthcare Limited
- Anchor Investor Portion: 94,15,584 equity shares subscribed
- Issue Price: Rs. 693/- per equity share
- Notice Date: October 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported in this notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are informed of the anchor investor allocation status as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date / Allocation Date: October 19, 2026
