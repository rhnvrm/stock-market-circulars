## Summary

BSE has announced the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Sunrise Middle East Turn Tech Limited for its upcoming SME Initial Public Offering (IPO). The document is hosted on the exchange websites for public review and comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Sunrise Middle East Turn Tech Limited
- Filing Date: September 09, 2026
- Regulation Compliance: Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and the public can review the draft offer document and submit comments via email to equity.ipo@bseindia.com within 21 days from the notice date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 10, 2026
- Submission Deadline: Within 21 days of the notice

## Impact Assessment

Routine market activity facilitating public feedback for an upcoming SME IPO listing on the exchange.
