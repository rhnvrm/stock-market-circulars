## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding compliance with TRAI directions and guidelines on pre-tagging variable fields in SMS Content Templates. With the transition from logger mode to blocker mode scheduled, non-compliant SMS traffic will be rejected starting October 17, 2026. Members and Principal Entities must review and update their SMS templates promptly.

## Key Points

- TRAI mandated pre-tagging of variable fields in SMS Content Templates to prevent misuse.
- The variable-field scrubbing mechanism operated in logger mode since July 1, 2026, allowing delivery with error codes.
- TRAI is activating blocker mode to reject non-compliant SMS traffic.
- Members must complete the review and pre-tagging exercise by October 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition from logger mode (which generated delivery errors for non-compliant SMS) to blocker mode (which actively rejects non-compliant commercial SMS traffic).
- Mandatory pre-tagging of all variable fields in SMS Content Templates in accordance with TRAI frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Review all SMS Content Templates, prioritizing those used during the preceding 90 days.
- Identify and appropriately pre-tag all variable fields in the templates.
- Monitor delivery errors closely and coordinate with Access Providers (SMS template registrars).
- Complete the review and compliance exercise by October 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **18.11.2025**: TRAI Direction regarding pre-tagging of variable fields in SMS Content Templates.
- **01.07.2026**: Variable-field scrubbing mechanism initiated in logger mode.
- **06.10.2026**: Notice issued by BSE.
- **17.10.2026**: Deadline for completing template review and pre-tagging exercise before blocker mode activation.

## Impact Assessment

Failure to comply with TRAI's pre-tagging requirements before the activation of blocker mode will result in the rejection of commercial SMS messages, disrupting communications between members/principal entities and their clients.
