## Summary

BSE has announced the availability of the SME draft offer document for Mayur Electromeck Limited, filed on October 22, 2026. The document is open for public comments for at least 21 days in accordance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Mayur Electromeck Limited filed its SME Draft Offer Document on October 22, 2026.
- The document is hosted on the BSE SME website for public review and comments.
- Public comments can be submitted within 21 days of the notice date.
- Comments should be emailed to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

- No direct regulatory changes introduced; compliance with Schedule VI of ICDR Regulations for SME IPO public comments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stakeholders and public can review the draft offer document and submit comments within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 23, 2026
- Draft Filed Date: October 22, 2026
- Comment Submission Deadline: Within 21 days from the notice date

## Impact Assessment

- Routine public comment process for an SME IPO draft document, providing transparency and stakeholder feedback opportunity.
