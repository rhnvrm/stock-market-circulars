## Summary

BSE has announced the imposition and revision of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on various securities based on XBRL submissions. The measures include 100% margins on open and new positions, movement to higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price bands, and transfers to T / XT / MT / TS Groups, along with securities moving out of the framework.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. September 30, 2026, on all open positions as on September 29, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM and subjected to Lower Price Bands w.e.f. September 28, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in LT-ASM but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. September 30, 2026.
- Consolidated lists of securities under various annexures are published by the exchange.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions, not as an adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margin requirements for securities under Annexure I (Part A).
- Revision of price bands and movement to higher stages of LT-ASM for securities under Annexure I (Part B).
- Transfer of specific securities in LT-ASM to T / XT / MT / TS Groups under Annexure I (Part D).
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework (Annexure II), subject to other surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure 100% margin maintenance for affected securities from September 30, 2026.
- Adhere to revised price bands and trading group transfers effective from September 28 and 30, 2026.
- Members with clarifications can contact bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- September 28, 2026: Effective date for higher stages of LT-ASM, lower price bands, movement out of framework, and price band reinstatements.
- September 29, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- September 30, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin applicability and transfer of securities to T / XT / MT / TS Groups.

## Impact Assessment

The LT-ASM measures increase trading friction and margin requirements for listed securities placed under surveillance, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
