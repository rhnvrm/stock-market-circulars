## Summary

Pursuant to Notice No. 20260803-37, the Exchange has informed members that Alphagrep Liquid Omni FOF shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** ALPHAGREP LIQUID OMNI FOF
- **Effective Date:** August 10, 2026
- **Available Facilities:** Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP
- **Plans & ISINs:** Includes regular and direct plans across growth, IDCW reinvestment, and IDCW payout options.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can facilitate transactions in the specified ISINs through the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity and trading availability for investors and distributors on the BSE StAR MF platform for the listed debt fund schemes.
