## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of 4,38,463 new equity shares of Anand Rayons Limited (Scrip Code: 542721) issued on a preferential basis to promoters following the conversion of warrants. The shares will be permitted to trade with effect from Monday, August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Anand Rayons Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 542721
- **ISIN:** INE02GA01012
- **Security Details:** 4,38,463 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 68/- (Issue Price: Rs. 78/- per share).
- **Reason for Issuance:** Conversion of warrants issued to promoters on a preferential basis.
- **Effective Date:** August 31, 2026.
- **Ranking:** The new equity shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Distribution Nos.:** 21469925 to 21908387

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure adherence to the specified lock-in requirements and distribution numbers.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 20/07/2026
- **Listing / Trading Effective Date:** 31/08/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 29/04/2028 (for 4,38,463 shares)

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital of the company via warrant conversion with standard lock-in constraints.
