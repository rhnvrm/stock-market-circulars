## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of NCL RESEARCH AND FINANCIAL SERVICES LIMITED are permitted on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: NCL RESEARCH AND FINANCIAL SERVICES LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Trading Period for Renunciation: September 03, 2026 to September 15, 2026
- Scrip Code: 751139
- Scrip ID: NCL-RE
- ISIN No.: INE132F20020
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Rights Issue Period: September 03, 2026 to September 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation shall be settled on T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.
- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs, ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes/bills must include a prominent disclaimer informing clients that purchasing REs only grants the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before the issue closes.
- REs neither subscribed by making an application with requisite application money nor renounced on or before the Issue Closing Date shall lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Open Date: September 03, 2026
- RE Trading Close Date: September 15, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: September 03, 2026
- Rights Issue Close Date: September 18, 2026

Provides market participants and investors holding or wishing to trade rights entitlements of NCL Research and Financial Services Limited with operational timelines and settlement terms.
