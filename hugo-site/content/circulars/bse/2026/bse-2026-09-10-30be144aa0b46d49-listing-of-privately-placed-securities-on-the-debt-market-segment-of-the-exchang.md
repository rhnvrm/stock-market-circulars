## Summary

GMR Airports Limited has listed 150,000 privately placed debt securities (rated CARE AA-/Stable) on the debt market segment of the BSE effective September 10, 2026. The face value and paid-up value per security is Rs. 100,000.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** GMR Airports Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 978133
- **Scrip ID:** 5GMRAL29
- **ISIN Number:** INE776C08091
- **Quantity:** 150,000 units
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 100,064.40
- **Face Value / Paid-up Value:** Rs. 100,000.00 each
- **Rate of Interest:** 5% p.a., payable annually
- **Date of Allotment:** 09/09/2026
- **Date of Redemption:** 09/09/2029
- **Credit Rating:** CARE AA-/Stable

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialised form under the specified ISIN with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 9, 2026
- **Listing Effective Date:** September 10, 2026
- **Interest Payment Dates:** Annually from March 31, 2027 to September 9, 2029
- **Date of Redemption:** September 9, 2029

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading and liquidity for the newly listed privately placed debt securities of GMR Airports Limited on the BSE debt segment.
