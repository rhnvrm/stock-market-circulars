## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of 121 new equity shares of Lloyds Enterprises Ltd. (Scrip Code: 512463) with effect from October 26, 2026. These shares are issued pursuant to the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares of Re. 1/- each on a rights basis.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Lloyds Enterprises Ltd.
- **Scrip Code:** 512463
- **ISIN:** INE080I01025
- **Securities Details:** 121 equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid-up shares to fully paid-up (Re. 1/- each issued on rights basis).
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 1525368822 to 1525368942.
- **Ranking:** Pari-passu with existing equity shares of the company.
- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities with effect from October 26, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing of these shares is a routine corporate action resulting from the conversion of partly paid shares, with negligible financial or operational impact on market participants.
