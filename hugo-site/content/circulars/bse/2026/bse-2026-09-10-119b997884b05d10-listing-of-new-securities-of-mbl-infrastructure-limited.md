## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing and permission for trading of 14,50,000 fully paid-up equity shares of MBL Infrastructure Limited issued on a preferential basis pursuant to a Resolution Plan approved by the Hon'ble NCLT, Kolkata Bench.

## Key Points

- Company Name: MBL Infrastructure Limited
- Scrip Code: 533152
- ISIN: INE912H01013
- Number of Securities: 14,50,000 fully paid-up equity shares of Rs. 10/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 10/-
- Date of Allotment: May 30, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: September 11, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 154429257 to 155879256
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to October 31, 2027

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Ensure adherence to lock-in requirements up to October 31, 2027, for the specified distinctive numbers.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: May 30, 2026
- Trading Commencement Date: September 11, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: October 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Permits trading of newly allotted preferential equity shares on the exchange, ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
