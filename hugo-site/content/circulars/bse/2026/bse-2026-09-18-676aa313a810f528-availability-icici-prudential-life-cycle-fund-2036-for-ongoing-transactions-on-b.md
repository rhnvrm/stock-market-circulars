## Summary

BSE has announced the reopening of ICICI Prudential Life Cycle Fund 2036 for ongoing transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes will be available for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities.
- Applicable ISINs include INF109K1A575 (Regular Plan - Growth) and INF109K1A583 (Direct Plan - Growth).
- Reopening follows previous Notice No. 20260825-39 dated August 25, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors must note the availability of the scheme for client transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute ongoing transactions and facility setups for the specified ICICI Prudential scheme on the platform.
