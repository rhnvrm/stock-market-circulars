## Summary

BSE has issued the trading and settlement schedule for the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) segment covering the period from October 1, 2026, to October 31, 2026, detailing settlement numbers, trading dates, entry dates, and pay-in/pay-out timelines.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement cycles from settlement number SL-127/26-27 to SL-146/26-27.
- Specifies trading, entry, and confirmation dates alongside pay-in and pay-out schedules for each cycle.
- Pay-in is scheduled at 08:00 a.m. with members required to submit instructions to Depositories/Banks by 07:50 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must adhere to the scheduled pay-in and pay-out timelines for the SLB segment as outlined in the calendar.

## Important Dates

- Effective Start Date: October 1, 2026
- Effective End Date: November 2, 2026 (for final settlement cycle pay-out)

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational continuity for trading and settlement in the SLB segment.
