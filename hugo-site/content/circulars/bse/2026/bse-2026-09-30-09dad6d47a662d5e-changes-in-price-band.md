## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 27 scrips in the equity segment, effective from October 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised for 27 scrips to various limits (2%, 5%, 10%, and 20%).
- Affected scrips include Aastha Spintex Ltd, Aban Offshore Ltd, Reliance Infrastructure Ltd, and others.
- Effective date of the revision is October 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Price band adjustments implemented as part of surveillance mechanisms by the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading terminals and systems to reflect the revised price bands for the specified scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricts excessive price volatility and speculative trading in the listed scrips.
