## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 84,75,035 new equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 11.02/- by Vaxfab Enterprises Limited (Scrip Code - 542803) pursuant to the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from July 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Vaxfab Enterprises Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 542803
- **ISIN:** INE560T01015
- **Security Details:** 84,75,035 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 11.02/- (Issue Price: Rs. 21.02/-)
- **Effective Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Allottees:** Non-Promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the lock-in details and trading permissions for the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** 29/07/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 30/01/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion and listing of equity shares resulting from warrant conversions, with no direct adverse market impact.
