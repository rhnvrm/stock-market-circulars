## Summary

Franklin Templeton Mutual Fund is offering the Franklin India Short Term Fund NFO along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription period runs from August 05, 2026, to August 11, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Franklin India Short Term Fund (Regular and Direct plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- **NFO Window:** August 05, 2026, to August 11, 2026 (extended till 6:00 PM on August 11, 2026, for lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 12, 2026.
- **ISINs:** Regular Growth (INF090I01YT5), Regular IDCW Reinvestment (INF090I01YV1), Regular IDCW Payout (INF090I01YU3), Direct Growth (INF090I01YW9), Direct IDCW Reinvestment (INF090I01YY5), Direct IDCW Payout (INF090I01YX7).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 12, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (August 11, 2026).

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** August 05, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading members and investors to participate in the new fund offer and set up SIP facilities through the BSE StAR MF platform.
