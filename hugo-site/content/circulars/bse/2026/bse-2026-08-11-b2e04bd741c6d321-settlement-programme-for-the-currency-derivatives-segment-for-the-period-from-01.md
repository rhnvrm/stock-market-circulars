## Summary

BSE has released the settlement programme and schedule for the currency derivatives segment covering trading activities from September 1, 2026, to September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement schedules for currency derivatives transactions.
- Applicable period: 01.09.2026 to 30.09.2026.
- Issued by BSECL (BSE Clearing Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must adhere to the notified settlement dates and schedules.

## Important Dates

- Effective Start Date: September 1, 2026
- Effective End Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for currency derivatives during the specified period.
