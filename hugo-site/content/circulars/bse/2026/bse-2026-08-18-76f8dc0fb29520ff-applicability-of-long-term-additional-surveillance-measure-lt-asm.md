## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective dates for various measures range from August 19, 2026, to August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins shall be applicable effective August 21, 2026, on all open positions as on August 20, 2026, and new positions created from August 21, 2026, onwards for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework or being placed under lower price bands take effect from August 19, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective August 21, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective August 19, 2026, will have their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and is a market surveillance measure, not an adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision and imposition of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM), including margin requirements, price band revisions, and group transfers for listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on applicable securities and account for group transfers and price band restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for Lower Price Band & higher stages (Annexure I Part B): August 19, 2026
- Effective date for securities moving out of LT-ASM (Annexure II): August 19, 2026
- Effective date for 100% Margins (Annexure I Part A): August 21, 2026
- Effective date for group transfers (Annexure I Part D): August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in shortlisted securities will experience increased margin obligations (100% margins) and trading restrictions such as lower price bands and movement into specific trading groups.
