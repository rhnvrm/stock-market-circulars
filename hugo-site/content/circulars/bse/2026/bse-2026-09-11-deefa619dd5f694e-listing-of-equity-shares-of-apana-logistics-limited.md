## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of equity shares of Apana Logistics Limited effective from Tuesday, September 15, 2026. The scrip will be traded in the 'MT' Group of Securities and will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Apana Logistics Limited
- Scrip Code: 544910
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: APANA
- ISIN: INE1ET101019
- Market Lot: 2000 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 60/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 50/-)
- Total Securities: 17,510,000 Equity Shares
- Effective Date: September 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.
- Subject to Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days as per SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialised form and respect the minimum market lot of 2000 shares.
- Necessary corporate actions for lock-in marking have been completed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 10, 2026
- Listing / Effective Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading for Apana Logistics Limited shares on the BSE SME segment, providing liquidity to investors under standard exchange safeguards and trade-for-trade monitoring.
