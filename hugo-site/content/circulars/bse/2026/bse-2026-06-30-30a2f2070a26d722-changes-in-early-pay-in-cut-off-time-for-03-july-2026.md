## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (formerly known as Indian Clearing Corporation Limited) has announced revised final cut-off times for Early Pay-In (EPI) of funds and securities, collateral allocation, and margin report downloads specifically for July 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Revised timelines affect Early Pay-In of funds, securities, collateral allocation, and margin reports for July 3, 2026.
- EPI of Funds final cut-off time is revised to 07:00 PM.
- EPI of Securities final cut-off time is revised to 07:00 PM.
- Collateral Allocation cut-off time is revised to 10:00 PM.
- Margin reports download cut-off time is revised to 08:45 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Timelines adjusted in reference to ICCL circular dated August 29, 2020, and SEBI circular dated July 27, 2022, regarding Settlement of Running Account of Client’s Funds lying with Trading Members.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must adhere strictly to the revised EPI cut-off timelines for July 3, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Date of Changes: July 3, 2026
- Circular Date: June 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and participants must update their operational schedules and settlement workflows to align with the advanced cut-off timings on July 3, 2026.
