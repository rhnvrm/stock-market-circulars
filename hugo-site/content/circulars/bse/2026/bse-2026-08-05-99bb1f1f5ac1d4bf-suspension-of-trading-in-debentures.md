## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest or redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading members are advised not to deal in these specified debentures and bonds as per the schedule provided.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260805-40, dated August 5, 2026.
- Segment: Debt.
- Department: Listing Operations.
- Action: Suspension of trading in debentures/bonds due to upcoming record dates for interest payment and redemption.
- Affected Issuers include Edel Finance Company Limited, Marwadi Shares and Finance Limited, Muthoot Mercantile Limited, State Bank of India, Spandana Sphoorty Financial Limited, Edelweiss Financial Services Ltd., Power Finance Corporation Ltd, Avanse Financial Services Limited, Cholamandalam Investment and Finance Company Ltd, Poonawalla Fincorp Limited, and Bajaj Housing Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Temporary suspension of trading in specific debt securities to facilitate record date processing for interest payments and redemptions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure they do not deal in the specified debentures and bonds during the restricted periods associated with the record dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates range from August 12, 2026, to August 25, 2026, depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

- Temporary halt in secondary market liquidity for the affected NCDs and bonds around their respective record dates.
