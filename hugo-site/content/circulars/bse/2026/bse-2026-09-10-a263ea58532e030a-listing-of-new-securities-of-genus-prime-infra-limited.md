## Summary

BSE has informed market participants about the listing and permission for trading of new equity shares of Genus Prime Infra Limited with effect from September 11, 2026, pursuant to a scheme of arrangement.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 532425
- Security Details: 6,30,50,770 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each issued pursuant to the Scheme of Arrangement involving Genus Prime Infra Limited, Sansar Infrastructure Private Limited, Star Vaniya Private Limited, Sunima Trading Private Limited, Genus Power Infrastructures Limited, and Yajur Commodities Limited.
- Effective Date of Trading: September 11, 2026.
- ISIN Number: INE256D01014
- Distinctive Numbers: 14926441 to 77977210
- Date of Allotment: April 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

None

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: April 10, 2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Permits trading of newly allotted shares arising from corporate restructuring for Genus Prime Infra Limited.
