## Summary

BSE has announced the periodic review of securities trading in the periodic call auction mechanism based on trading activity from April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026, pursuant to SEBI guidelines. The changes will be effective from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Periodic review conducted based on trading activity between April 01, 2026, and September 30, 2026.
- List of scrips moving into the periodic call auction mechanism is provided in Annexure I.
- List of securities moving out of the periodic call auction mechanism is provided in Annexure II.
- Changes become effective from October 12, 2026.
- Trading members are advised to exercise additional due diligence while trading in these securities.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures based on SEBI circular no. CIR/MRD/DP/38/2013 dated December 19, 2013, and uniform stock exchange criteria for rationalized periodic call auction for illiquid scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise additional due diligence when trading in these securities either on their own account or on behalf of clients. Clarifications can be sought via email at bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Review Period: April 01, 2026, to September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026
- Notice Date: October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and trading mechanisms for specific scrips identified as illiquid or restored to normal trading, requiring market participants to update their trading systems and exercise due diligence.
