## Summary

Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund is launching the Motilal Oswal Quality Fund NFO along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 28, 2026, to September 11, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Motilal Oswal Quality Fund (Regular and Direct Plans across Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options).
- NFO Period: August 28, 2026, to September 11, 2026 (extended till midnight for Lumpsum purchases).
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on September 11, 2026.
- Tentative allotment date: September 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 18, 2026, for allotment.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 28, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: September 11, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscription, switch, and SIP transactions for members and investors participating in the Motilal Oswal Quality Fund NFO via the BSE StAR MF platform.
