## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) by Bamboo Hotel and Global Centre (Delhi) Private Limited. Trading in the debentures will be conducted with a reduced face value effective from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Bamboo Hotel and Global Centre (Delhi) Private Limited
- Security Details: 10.81% Non-Convertible Debentures (Scrip Code: 976378, ISIN: INE755L07015, Scrip ID: BHGCPL-10.81%-31-1-28-PVT)
- Record Date: 13/10/2026
- Ex-Date: 13/10/2026
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 50,000/- per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a corporate debt redemption notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and depository participants are required to note the reduced face value and record date for settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 13 October 2026
- Ex-Date: 13 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Debenture holders will receive part redemption payments as per the schedule, and subsequent trading will occur on the reduced face value basis.
