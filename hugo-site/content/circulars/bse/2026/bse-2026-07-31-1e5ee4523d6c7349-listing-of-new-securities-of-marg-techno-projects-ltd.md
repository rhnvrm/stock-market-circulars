## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Marg Techno Projects Ltd. with effect from August 3, 2026, following a rights issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Marg Techno Projects Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 540254
- Security Details: 6,39,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at par on a rights basis.
- ISIN: INE245H01018
- Distinctive Numbers: 14200001 to 78100000
- Date of Allotment: 29/07/2026
- Ranking: These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may note the listing and commencement of trading for the new securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 29/07/2026
- Effective Date for Trading: 03/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up equity base of Marg Techno Projects Ltd. through the listing of rights issue shares.
