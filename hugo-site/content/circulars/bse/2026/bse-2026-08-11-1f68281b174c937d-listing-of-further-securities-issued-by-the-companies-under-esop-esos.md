## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 16 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Wednesday, August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 16 companies have issued further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective date for trading of these securities: August 12, 2026.
- Includes prominent companies such as Bajaj Finserv Limited, RBL Bank Limited, Wipro Limited, and Yes Bank Limited.
- Lock-in status for all listed tranches is marked as Not Applicable (NA).

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are requested to take note of the listing of new securities for the mentioned companies.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 11, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity base of the respective companies with negligible direct market impact.
