## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding securities moving into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from October 14, 2026. Trading members are required to take adequate precautions as these securities attract enhanced surveillance actions and trade-to-trade settlement with no netting off.

## Key Points

- Effective date for movement to higher GSM stages: October 14, 2026.
- Applicable surveillance actions depend on the assigned GSM Stage (I to IV).
- Settlement shall be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in these securities.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities listed in the Annexure are subjected to stricter surveillance measures under the existing GSM framework established by the Exchange, including 100% margin rates, trade-for-trade settlement, restricted trading frequency, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adherence to the specific surveillance actions applicable to each GSM stage.
- Buyers must deposit the required Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) as mandated (50% or 100% of trade value depending on the stage).
- Ensure no netting off is applied during settlement for these securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 13, 2026
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity and liquidity in the affected securities may be restricted due to trade-for-trade settlement requirements, price bands, and additional margin/deposit obligations.
