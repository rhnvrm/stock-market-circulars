## Summary

BSE and other recognized stock exchanges, in consultation with SEBI, have issued a common Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for processing applications relating to further issues of securities by commonly listed entities. Under this mechanism, exchanges will allocate applications amongst themselves on an alternate basis as the "Lead Stock Exchange" to ensure a uniform, coordinated, and expeditious approval process.

## Key Points

- **Joint SOP**: Stock exchanges and SEBI have adopted a unified approach for processing further issue applications for commonly listed entities.
- **Lead and Recipient Exchanges**: Applications will be allocated among exchanges on an alternate basis. The designated "Lead Stock Exchange" handles direct communication and queries with the company, while "Recipient Stock Exchanges" share their observations with the lead exchange.
- **Scope**: Covers capital raising through Preferential Issues, Rights Issues, QIPs, FCCBs, ADRs, GDRs, ESOPs, ESPS, Sweat Equity, Bonus issues, and Schemes of Arrangement.
- **Single Point of Interaction**: Listed entities will submit applications simultaneously to all respective exchanges, but will submit responses to queries solely to the Lead Stock Exchange.
- **Applicability**: This mechanism applies exclusively to entities listed on more than one stock exchange. Exclusively listed entities follow existing procedures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Streamlined inter-exchange coordination for further issue approvals.
- Elimination of redundant or uncoordinated queries from multiple stock exchanges for commonly listed entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must continue to submit applications simultaneously to all respective stock exchanges on their portals.
- Companies will receive queries/concerns through and submit responses exclusively to the designated Lead Stock Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for applications submitted to Stock Exchanges.

## Impact Assessment

- Improves efficiency, reduces compliance friction, and ensures consistency in processing timeframes for capital-raising applications across multiple stock exchanges.
