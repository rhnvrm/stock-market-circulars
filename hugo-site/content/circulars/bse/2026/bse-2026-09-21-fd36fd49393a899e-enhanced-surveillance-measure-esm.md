## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin effective September 23, 2026. Certain securities will move to lower stages, shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment, or move out of the ESM framework entirely.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 23, 2026, on open positions as of September 22, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities will continue in the ESM framework but move to lower Stage I w.e.f. September 22, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 22, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM will shift from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 23, 2026.
- A list of securities moving out of the ESM framework w.e.f. September 22, 2026, is provided in Annexure II.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including 100% margins, shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment, and Periodic Call Auction mechanisms for shortlisted securities under the ESM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the increased margin requirements (100% margin) and note the segment and price band changes for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Stage II / Moving Out / Lower Stage:** September 22, 2026
- **Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade Shift:** September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and collateral requirements for securities listed under the ESM framework will be significantly impacted due to 100% margin requirements and movement to the Trade-for-Trade segment.
