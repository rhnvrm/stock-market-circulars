## Summary

The Exchange has informed Trading Members that SEBI has approved the conversion of the individual membership of Mr. Ramakant Ramniwas Kasat (Member No. 5165) into an LLP named Finnovative Share Broking LLP (Member No. 5165). Consequently, the trading member name has been updated effective July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Previous Member Name:** Mr. Ramakant Ramniwas Kasat (Member No. 5165)
- **New Member Name:** Finnovative Share Broking LLP (Member No. 5165)
- **Effective Date:** July 31, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Approval by SEBI for the conversion of individual membership to an LLP structure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update records to reflect the new entity name for Member No. 5165.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Effective Date:** July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update with no direct impact on general market liquidity or stock prices.
