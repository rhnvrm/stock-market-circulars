## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 12 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS). These securities become effective for trading from Monday, August 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 12 companies have issued further securities under ESOP/ESOS.
- Effective date for trading: August 03, 2026.
- Companies include Aditya Birla Capital Limited, BlueStone Jewellery and Lifestyle Limited, Eicher Motors Limited, Jagsonpal Pharmaceuticals Limited, Mahindra Lifespace Developers Limited, Nephrocare Health Services Limited, Network People Services Technologies Limited, Paramount Communications Limited, Poonawalla Fincorp Limited, Prime Securities Limited, Ramco Systems Limited, and S.J.S. Enterprises Limited.
- Lock-in details vary by company, with most marked as NA except Network People Services Technologies Limited (lock-in upto 27/11/2026).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the listing of new securities for the mentioned companies.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 31, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity base of the respective companies through employee stock option exercises.
