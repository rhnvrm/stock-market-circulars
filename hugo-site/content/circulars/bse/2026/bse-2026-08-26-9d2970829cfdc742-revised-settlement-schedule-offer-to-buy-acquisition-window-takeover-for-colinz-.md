## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd has issued a revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Colinz Laboratories Limited (Settlement No. 2026100).

## Key Points

- The takeover bidding period was conducted from 07/08/2026 to 20/08/2026.
- Revised Obligation Download Date is scheduled for 27/08/2026.
- Revised Settlement Date is scheduled for 28/08/2026.
- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must use Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026100 in the depository system.
- Members are required to refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: 07/08/2026
- Bid End Date: 20/08/2026
- Revised Obligation Download: 27/08/2026
- Revised Settlement Date: 28/08/2026
