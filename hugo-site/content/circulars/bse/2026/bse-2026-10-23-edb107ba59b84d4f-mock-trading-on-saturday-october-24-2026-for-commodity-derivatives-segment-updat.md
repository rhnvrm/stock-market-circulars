## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 24, 2026, updating previous notice no. 20261022-50. Trading members can test their trading applications, algorithms, and ETI APIs under exceptional market conditions.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, October 24, 2026, for the Commodity Derivatives segment.
- Members can test third-party trading platforms or in-house systems using ETI APIs (including risk-reduction mode and trading halt functionalities).
- No new BOLT TWS Release.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations.
- Feedback for the session is requested by 03:00 PM on the day of the mock trading session.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational mock trading update.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively in testing their systems and submit feedback via email or contact numbers provided by 03:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** October 24, 2026
- **Log-in Time:** 08:15 AM to 09:00 AM
- **Continuous Trading:** 09:00 AM to 06:30 PM
- **Feedback Submission Deadline:** 03:00 PM on October 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal and beneficial for ensuring system readiness and stability for commodity derivatives trading.
