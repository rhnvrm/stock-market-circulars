## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of new equity shares of Cemindia Projects Limited (Scrip Code: 509496, ISIN: INE686A01026) with effect from October 22, 2026. These shares were allotted to Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) pursuant to a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Cemindia Projects Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 509496
- **ISIN:** INE686A01026
- **Security Details:** 1,26,35,379 Equity shares of Re. 1/- each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 1,108/- per share
- **Date of Allotment:** October 19, 2026
- **Effective Date of Listing:** October 22, 2026
- **Distribution Nos.:** 171787585 to 184422963
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; standard listing of securities issued via QIP.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their systems and records regarding the increased share capital and distribution numbers.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** October 19, 2026
- **Notice Date:** October 21, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing expands the equity capital base of Cemindia Projects Limited through institutional participation, enhancing liquidity and market float.
