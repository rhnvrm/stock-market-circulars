## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members that the UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and related facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes involved: UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AG84) and UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF789F1AG92).
- Facilities available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP).
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate transactions in the specified index fund schemes for investors through the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 31, 2026
- Notice Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to resume trading and systematic transactions in the UTI Nifty 500 Index Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform without restrictions.
