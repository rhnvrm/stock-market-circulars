## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing the movement of certain securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The changes become effective from September 21, 2026, and entail strict surveillance actions to safeguard market integrity and investor interests.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached annexure will move to higher GSM stages effective September 21, 2026.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, including applicable margin rates of 100%, price bands of 5% or lower, trade-for-trade settlement, Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD), and restricted trading frequencies.
- No netting off shall be allowed as settlement will be done on a trade-to-trade basis.

## Regulatory Changes

The securities are subjected to enhanced surveillance provisions as per the GSM framework originally published on February 23, 2017, and subsequent updates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Buyers are required to deposit Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) and maintain applicable margin rates depending on the assigned GSM stage.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 18, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and price movement for the affected securities will be restricted due to stringent surveillance measures, mandatory trade-to-trade settlement, and cash deposit requirements for buyers.
