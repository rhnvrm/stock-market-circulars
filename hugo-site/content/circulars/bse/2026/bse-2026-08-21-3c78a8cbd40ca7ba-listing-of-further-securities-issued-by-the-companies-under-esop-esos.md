## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 10 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS). The trading for these newly issued securities will be effective from Monday, August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** August 24, 2026.
- **Scope:** Listing of further securities issued under ESOP / ESOS by 10 listed companies.
- **Companies Involved:**
  - Brigade Enterprises Limited (33,720 shares)
  - ITC Limited (313,800 shares)
  - Ivalue Infosolutions Limited (123,280 shares)
  - Keystone Realtors Limited (10,357 shares)
  - KFin Technologies Limited (25,784 shares)
  - Orbit Exports Limited (39,000 shares)
  - Rishabh Instruments Limited (500 shares)
  - Sai Life Sciences Limited (18,700 shares)
  - Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited (2,250 shares)
  - Sona BLW Precision Forgings Limited (337,775 shares)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard corporate action processing under existing listing guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the listed securities and corresponding lock-in details where applicable.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of equity capital base for the respective companies due to employee stock option exercises. Minimal direct impact on general market participants.
