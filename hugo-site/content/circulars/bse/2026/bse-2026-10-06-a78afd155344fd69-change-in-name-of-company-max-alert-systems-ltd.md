## Summary

Max Alert Systems Ltd has announced a change in the company name to Max Earth Resources Limited. Trading members are informed that the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated effective October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Max Alert Systems Ltd
- New Company Name: Max Earth Resources Limited
- Scrip Code: 534563
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): MAXERL
- Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus): MAXERL
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

The company has completed ROC formalities for the name change, prompting the Exchange to update trading symbol and identifiers.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that all trading terminals and back-office records reflect the new scrip ID (MAXERL) and company name from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Administrative update affecting trading displays and client systems. No financial or structural impact on securities.
