## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window - Takeover) for Aar Shyam India Investment Company Limited, covering the bidding period from October 19, 2026, to November 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- Settlement Type: Takeover
- Settlement Number: 2026126
- Bid Start Date: October 19, 2026
- Bid End Date: November 2, 2026
- Obligation Download Date: November 16, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 17, 2026
- Demat shares must be delivered through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type '(Takeover)' and settlement No. 2026126.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes the official acquisition window and operational settlement calendar under exchange takeover guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must ensure early pay-in of securities in the depository system sufficiently in advance before BSE OTB bidding and confirmation window closures.
- CDSL and NSDL early pay-in securities will be earmarked / blocked in client accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.
- Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- Bid Start Date: October 19, 2026
- Bid End Date: November 2, 2026
- Obligation Download Date: November 16, 2026
- Settlement Date: November 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates orderly processing and settlement of shares tendered during the takeover offer window for Aar Shyam India Investment Company Limited with clear procedural mandates for market participants.
