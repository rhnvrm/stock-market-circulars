## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of Acevector Limited in the 'B' Group of Securities effective from Monday, October 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544962
- ISIN: INE580P01029
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: ACEVECTOR
- Total Securities: 544,186,770 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 32/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 1/- and premium of Rs. 31/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities of the company will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.
- Lock-in details and shareholding pattern must comply with the provisions outlined in Annexure I and Annexure II.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 5, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and trading liquidity for the equity shares of Acevector Limited on the BSE platform.
