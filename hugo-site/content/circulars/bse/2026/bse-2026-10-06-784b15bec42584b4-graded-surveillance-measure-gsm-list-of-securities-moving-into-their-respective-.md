## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from October 07, 2026. Trading members are required to note the applicable surveillance actions and exercise caution.

## Key Points

- Securities are moving to higher stages of the GSM framework starting October 07, 2026.
- Applicable surveillance actions depend on the assigned GSM stage (Stages I, II, III, and IV).
- Measures include 100% margin rates, 5% or lower price bands, trade-for-trade settlement, Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD), and weekly trading restrictions for higher stages.
- No netting off is allowed for settlement in these securities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Movement of identified securities into higher GSM stages in accordance with the provisions of the GSM Framework established on February 23, 2017.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities.
- Ensure strict compliance with trade-to-trade settlement and deposit requirements (ASD) without netting off.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 07, 2026
- Notice Date: October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced surveillance on specific securities to protect investors and curb excessive volatility.
- Increased margin requirements and trading restrictions will affect liquidity and price movement for the securities listed in the annexure.
