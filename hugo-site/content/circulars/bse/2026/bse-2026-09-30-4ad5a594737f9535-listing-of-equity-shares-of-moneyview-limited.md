## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that the equity shares of Moneyview Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in due course. The scrip details have been allocated as part of the listing process.

## Key Points

- **Name of Company:** Moneyview Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544953
- **Symbol:** MONEYVIEW
- **ISIN No:** INE0PTN01011
- **Trading Status:** To be admitted to dealings in due course.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a standard corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the scrip code, symbol, and ISIN for trading and settlement operations once active.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 30, 2026
- **Listing Date:** To be announced via a separate notice.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables market participants to prepare trading terminals and systems for trading in Moneyview Limited equity shares upon the announcement of the formal trading date.
