## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of their commercial papers. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of these companies starting from the respective ex-dates / record dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20261013-38 dated October 13, 2026.
- Segment: Debt (Listing Operations / Suspension of Trading).
- Covers multiple commercial papers maturing / reaching record dates between October 19, 2026, and October 30, 2026.
- Affected issuers include Krazybee Services Ltd, REC Limited, A.K. Capital Finance Limited, Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited, Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, 360 ONE Prime Limited, Time Technoplast Limited, DSP Finance Private Limited, Muthoot Fincorp Limited, Vivriti Capital Limited, Axis Finance Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, and Capri Global Capital Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the Exchange are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies as of the respective record and ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: Between October 19, 2026, and October 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational suspension of trading in commercial papers due to redemption schedules, ensuring smooth settlement and preventing post-record-date trading.
