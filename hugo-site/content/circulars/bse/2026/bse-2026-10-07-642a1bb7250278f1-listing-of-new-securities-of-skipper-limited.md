## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 92,23,402 new equity shares of Skipper Limited issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters, effective from October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Skipper Limited
- Scrip Code: 538562
- ISIN: INE439E01022
- Number of Shares: 92,23,402 equity shares of Re. 1/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 470/- (including premium of Rs. 469/-)
- Allotment Date: 31/07/2026
- Effective Trading Date: October 8, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to note the lock-in and distribution details of the newly listed preferential shares.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: July 31, 2026
- Effective Date of Trading: October 8, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: April 15, 2027 (for Dist. Nos. 112937234 to 122160635)

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of equity base due to preferential allotment of shares to non-promoters, subject to specified lock-in restrictions until April 15, 2027.
