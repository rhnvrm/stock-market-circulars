## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 1,63,18,538 equity shares of Almondz Global Securities Limited issued to promoters on a preferential basis, effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Almondz Global Securities Limited (Scrip Code: 531400)
- Issue Type: Preferential allotment to promoters
- Number of Shares: 1,63,18,538 equity shares of face value Rs. 1/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 15.32/- per share (including premium of Rs. 14.32/-)
- ISIN: INE326B01035
- Distinctive Numbers: 174899757 to 191218294
- Date of Allotment: July 24, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 30, 2026
- Lock-in Period: Locked-in up to March 30, 2028

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard stock exchange listing approval for preferential issuance of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the listing and lock-in details of the newly issued securities and ensure compliance with trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: July 24, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: September 30, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: March 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

An increase in the paid-up equity capital of Almondz Global Securities Limited. The shares issued to promoters are under lock-in until March 30, 2028, restricting immediate liquidity for these specific shares.
