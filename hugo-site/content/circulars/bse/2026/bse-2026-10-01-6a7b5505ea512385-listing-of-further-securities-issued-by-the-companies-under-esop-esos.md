## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by eight companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) have been listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Monday, October 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Eight companies have issued further equity shares pursuant to ESOP / ESOS exercises.
- Effective date for trading of the new securities is October 05, 2026.
- Companies include Axis Bank, CG Power and Industrial Solutions, Keystone Realtors, Moschip Technologies, Polycab India, Shadowfax Technologies, Suprajit Engineering, and Vakrangee Limited.
- All listed securities rank pari passu with the existing equity shares of the respective companies.

Routine capital increase resulting from employee stock option exercises. The incremental equity has negligible dilutive impact on the overall equity base of the respective companies.
