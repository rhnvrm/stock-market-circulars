## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) issued a notice regarding the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated September 11, 2026, in the matter of Mauria Udyog Ltd. and 4 other scrips (Appeal No. 299 of 2026). SAT stayed the impugned order and directed that the penalty amount be placed in an interest-bearing account. SEBI confirmed that the appellants, including Ms. Shalini Sushil Jain & Ors., have paid the penalty amount in favor of SEBI.

## Key Points

- SAT Order Date: September 11, 2026 (Appeal No. 299 of 2026).
- Relief granted: Stay of the impugned order; penalty amount to be placed in an interest-bearing account.
- SEBI confirmation email dated September 17, 2026, confirms that appellants have paid the penalty amount in favor of SEBI.
- Appellants include Shalini Sushil Jain, Sayar S Bhandari, Vimala Anandraj Ostwal Bhandari, and Rekha Ravi Bhandari.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework change, but compliance with specific SAT directions and penalty payment confirmation in an ongoing appeal.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the SAT order and SEBI's confirmation regarding the penalty payment and ensure compliance.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: 11 September 2026
- SEBI Email / Notice Date: 17 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Specific to the listed appellants and scrips involved; general compliance reminder for trading members regarding ongoing SAT proceedings and penalty status.
