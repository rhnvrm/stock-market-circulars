## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the record dates and reduced face values for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) for several companies, effective on specified dates in August 2026.

## Key Points

- Record dates and ex-dates are set between August 17, 2026, and August 26, 2026, depending on the company and security.
- Trading in the debentures of the respective companies will take place with a reduced face value starting from the effective date.
- Companies involved include Andhra Pradesh State Beverages Corporation Limited, Aptus Value Housing Finance India Limited, Moneyboxx Finance Limited, Aptus Finance India Private Limited, Ananya Finance For Inclusive Growth Private Limited, Arman Financial Services Limited, Adani Airport Holdings Limited, and Krazybee Services Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of reduced face value trading for specified NCDs following part redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the reduced face values and record dates when executing trades in the mentioned debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: August 17, 2026, to August 26, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Directly affects trading parameters and valuation for the specified non-convertible debentures due to capital reduction through part redemption.
