## Summary

BSE has announced the application, stage revisions, group transfers, and exclusions of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margins, lower price bands, and transfers to T/XT/MT/TS groups effective October 21 and October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable effective October 23, 2026, on all open positions as on October 22, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands effective October 21, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Transfer of certain securities continuing in LT-ASM to T / XT / MT / TS Group effective October 23, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- List of securities moving out of LT-ASM framework effective October 21, 2026 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures in conjunction with existing exchange rules, imposing stricter margin requirements, price bands, and segment transfers.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements, revised price bands, and settlement group transfers for shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Bands & Exits: October 21, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects liquidity and margin requirements for specific listed securities placed under surveillance, while price bands for securities exiting the framework are reinstated.
