## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding process for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled for September 18, 2026. Trading members and direct investors can participate through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Securities available for auction include 7.06% GS 2041 (Issue Size: Rs. 17,000 Cr) and 7.43% GS 2076 (Issue Size: Rs. 11,000 Cr).
- Bid collection start date: September 11, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards.
- Bid collection end date for direct investors: September 17, 2026, till 5:00 pm.
- Bid collection end date for members: September 18, 2026, till 8:00 am.
- Settlement date: September 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module on the iBBS platform.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** September 11, 2026 (10:00 AM)
- **Direct Investor Bid End Date:** September 17, 2026 (5:00 PM)
- **Member Bid End Date:** September 18, 2026 (8:00 AM)
- **Auction Date:** September 18, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 21, 2026
