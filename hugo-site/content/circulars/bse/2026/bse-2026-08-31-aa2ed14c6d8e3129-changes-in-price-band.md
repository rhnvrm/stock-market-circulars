## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 15 scrips with effect from September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 15 scrips affected by price band revisions.
- Effective date of revision is September 01, 2026.
- Price bands revised to various percentages including 2%, 5%, 10%, and 20% depending on the scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of existing price bands for specified scrips as part of surveillance measures by BSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance in order execution.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Directly limits the maximum intraday price movement for the listed scrips to mitigate market volatility.
