## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE), as part of its surveillance and news verification mechanism, sought clarifications from ITI Ltd and Tata Consultancy Services Ltd regarding the accuracy of news articles appearing in media outlets. BSE has received and made public the replies from both companies.

## Key Points

- BSE sought verification from listed companies to ensure the accuracy of news reports published across various websites, newspapers, and television channels.
- **ITI Ltd (Scrip Code: 523610)** submitted its clarification in response to a news article on *CNBC-TV18*.
- **Tata Consultancy Services Ltd (Scrip Code: 532540)** submitted its clarification in response to a news article on *Business Standard*.
- The replies and clarifications submitted by these companies have been made available to members and investors.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes have been introduced. This notice is part of standard market surveillance and news verification procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

No general compliance requirements are imposed on other market participants. Listed companies are reminded of their obligation to provide prompt clarifications under LODR regulations when queried by the exchange.
