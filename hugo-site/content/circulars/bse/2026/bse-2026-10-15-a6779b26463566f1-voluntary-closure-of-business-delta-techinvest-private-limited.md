## Summary

BSE has informed all trading members that Delta Techinvest Private Limited (Member No. 6887) is voluntarily closing its business. The member will cease to do business in the Cash and Equity derivative segments effective October 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: Delta Techinvest Private Limited
- Member Number: 6887
- Affected Segments: Cash and Equity Derivative segments
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026
- Reason: Voluntary closure of business

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Clients and counterparties dealing with Delta Techinvest Private Limited should take note of the cessation of membership. Any queries or clarifications can be directed to membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricted impact limited to clients and stakeholders associated with Delta Techinvest Private Limited, with no wider systemic market implications.
