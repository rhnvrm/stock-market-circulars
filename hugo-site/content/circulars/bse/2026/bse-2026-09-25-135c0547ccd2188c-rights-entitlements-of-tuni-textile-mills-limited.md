## Summary

BSE has announced that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of TUNI TEXTILE MILLS LIMITED will be permitted on the Exchange starting September 28, 2026. The Rights Entitlements are traded under Scrip Code 751144, Scrip ID TUNTEX-RE1, and ISIN INE560D20027.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: TUNI TEXTILE MILLS LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751144
- Scrip ID: TUNTEX-RE1
- ISIN No.: INE560D20027
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: September 28, 2026 to October 19, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: September 28, 2026 to October 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of standard corporate action trading framework for rights entitlements renunciation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform all clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and that REs should not be mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Include a prominent disclaimer in contract notes and bills for RE purchases stating that RE purchase only gives the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce before the issue closes.
- REs neither subscribed with requisite application money nor renounced on or before the Issue Closing Date shall lapse and be extinguished.
- Trades shall be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.

## Important Dates

- Trading Period for Renunciation Start Date: September 28, 2026
- Trading Period for Renunciation End Date: October 19, 2026
- Rights Issue Opening Date: September 28, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders of TUNI TEXTILE MILLS LIMITED to trade, renounce, or apply for rights entitlements during the specified trading and issue periods.
