## Summary

Greaves Finance Limited has announced the record date for the payment of interest and part redemption of its non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading members are informed that trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Greaves Finance Limited
- Scrip Code: 977162
- Scrip ID: GFL-10.50%-29-9-27-PVT
- ISIN: INE1QWF07014
- Record Date: 13/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 11/09/2026
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 49,996 per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the reduced face value and ensure settlement procedures align with the revised debenture value.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 13/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 11/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact restricted to debenture holders receiving interest and part redemption along with adjusted trading face value.
