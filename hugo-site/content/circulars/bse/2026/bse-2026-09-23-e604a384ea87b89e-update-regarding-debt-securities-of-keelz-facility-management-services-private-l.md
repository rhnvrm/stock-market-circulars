## Summary

In continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260715-9 dated July 15, 2026, the Exchange has announced that debt securities of Keelz Facility Management Services Private Limited will be available for trading with revised terms on account of debt restructuring, effective from September 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 976986
- ISIN: INE1QV007010
- Effective Date: September 24, 2026
- Reason: Debt restructuring

## Regulatory Changes

Trading terms of the specified debt securities have been revised following debt restructuring.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the revised terms and scrip code availability for trading.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Revised Trading Terms: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading members and investors dealing in the debt securities of Keelz Facility Management Services Private Limited under the mentioned scrip code and ISIN.
