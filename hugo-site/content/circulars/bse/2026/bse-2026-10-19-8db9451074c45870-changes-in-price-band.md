## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of price bands for 16 scrips with effect from October 21, 2026, as part of surveillance and risk management measures.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for 16 scrips.
- Effective date: October 21, 2026.
- Revised limits range from 2% to 20%.
- Includes companies such as Comfort Intech Ltd, KRN Heat Exchanger And Refrigeration Ltd, and Trade Wings Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- Daily price operating ranges adjusted for specified scrips to prevent abnormal price volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trading systems and risk parameters reflect the updated price bands before market hours on October 21, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 19, 2026
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Limits extreme price fluctuations and safeguards market integrity for the listed scrips.
