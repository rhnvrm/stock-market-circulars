## Summary

Effective from Monday, September 21, 2026, the equity shares of Century Business Media Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Century Business Media Limited
- Scrip Code: 544928
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: CENTURYOOH
- ISIN No.: INE16VF01010
- Issue Price: Rs. 74/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 64/-)
- Number of Securities: 8,761,280 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1,600 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Corporate action executed for locking in shares in depository records as per the prospectus.
- Trading restricted to the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 17, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members can trade in the scrip starting September 21, 2026, subject to the mandated Trade-for-Trade segment restrictions and market lot size of 1,600 shares.
