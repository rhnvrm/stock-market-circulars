## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the criteria under the IBC surveillance framework are listed in Annexure I, taking effect from October 12, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the IBC framework are specified in Annexure II, with their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable measures.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- Securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) being less than 25% are detailed in Annexure IV.
- The shortlisting is purely a market surveillance action and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of additional surveillance measures for specified securities undergoing insolvency resolution.
- Periodic review of securities under the framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the surveillance measures applicable to the listed securities.
- Queries regarding the circular can be directed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading members and investors dealing in securities under the IBC framework will be subject to applicable surveillance restrictions and price band reinstatements as per exchange guidelines.
