## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the admission to dealings of Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of Indel Money Ltd effective from Tuesday, August 25, 2026, under the 'F GROUP' list.

## Key Points

- Securities: Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures of Indel Money Ltd.
- Effective Trading Date: August 25, 2026.
- Market Lot: Unit market lot (i.e. One Bond) as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000, in dematerialized form.
- Registrar and Share Transfer Agent: MUFG Intime India Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Bonds will only be traded in dematerialized form.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing announcement with no broad market or operational impact outside of normal debt security trading operations for Indel Money Ltd.
