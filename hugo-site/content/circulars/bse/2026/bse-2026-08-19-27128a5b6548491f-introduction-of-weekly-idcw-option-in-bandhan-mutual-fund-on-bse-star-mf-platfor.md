## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of the Weekly Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) option with a reinvestment facility for select Bandhan Mutual Fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- Weekly IDCW option with reinvestment facility introduced for Bandhan Mutual Fund.
- Applicable schemes include Bandhan Low Duration Fund, Bandhan Money Market Fund, and Bandhan Ultra Short Duration Fund.
- Available on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors can access the newly introduced weekly IDCW options for the specified schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 19 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with increased flexibility and frequent distribution options in low duration, money market, and ultra short duration funds through the BSE StAR MF platform.
