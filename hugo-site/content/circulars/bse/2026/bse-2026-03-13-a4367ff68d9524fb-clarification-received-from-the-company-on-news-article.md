## Summary

Orient Bell Limited submitted a clarification to the stock exchanges regarding a news article published in Business Standard titled 'Orientbell Embarks on a Digital Logistics Transformation Journey with Traqo.' The company clarified that it has no role in the publication and that Traqo was onboarded as a vendor in the normal course of business.

## Key Points

- The company onboarded 'Traqo' as a vendor in December 2024 in the normal course of business, valid until March 31, 2026.
- Transactions with Traqo are conducted at arm's length.
- There are no specific negotiations, events, or material developments requiring disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 regarding the news item.
- The news article has no material impact on the operations or financial position of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Timely disclosure and response to exchange queries under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Date of Letter: 12.03.2026
- News Item Date: 12.03.2026

## Impact Assessment

No material impact on the company's financial position or operations as the news pertains to routine vendor arrangements.
