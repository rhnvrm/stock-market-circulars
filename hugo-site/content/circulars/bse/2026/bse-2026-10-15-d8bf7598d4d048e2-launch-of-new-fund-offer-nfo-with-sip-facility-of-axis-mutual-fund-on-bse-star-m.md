## Summary

AXIS Mutual Fund is launching the AXIS Nifty EV & New Age Automotive Index Fund NFO along with SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window is open from October 16, 2026, to October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Schemes Offered:** AXIS Nifty EV & New Age Automotive Index Fund - Regular Plan (Growth) and Direct Plan (Growth).
- **ISINs:** INF846KA1622 (Regular), INF846KA1630 (Direct).
- **NFO Period:** October 16, 2026, to October 30, 2026.
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** November 05, 2026.
- **SIP Facility:** Available during the NFO period.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 05, 2026.
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (October 30, 2026).
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Opening Date:** October 16, 2026
- **NFO Closure Date:** October 30, 2026 (extended till midnight for lumpsum purchases)
- **SIP Authentication Deadline:** October 30, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** November 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with investment access to the EV and New Age Automotive theme via an index fund on the BSE StAR MF platform with standard operational and clearing guidelines.
