## Summary

Edelweiss Mutual Fund has notified BSE regarding the change in scheme names for two of its schemes available on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- EDELWEISS LOW DURATION FUND is renamed to EDELWEISS ULTRA SHORT TO SHORT TERM FUND.
- EDELWEISS GOVERNMENT SECURITIES FUND is renamed to EDELWEISS GILT FUND.
- Changes are applicable on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for future transactions and reporting.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Notice Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating nomenclature in trading and record systems.
