## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by multiple companies on the debt market segment effective August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are admitted to trading in dematerialised form under standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- Tick size for the securities is 1 paise.
- Issuers include Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, The Bombay Burmah Trading Corporation Ltd., Jio Credit Limited, Nuvama Wealth and Investment Limited, and Jindal Saw Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard commercial paper trading norms and dematerialised settlement.

## Important Dates

- Allotment/Effective Date: August 18, 2026 / August 19, 2026
- Redemption Dates: September and November 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations for debt segment participants.
