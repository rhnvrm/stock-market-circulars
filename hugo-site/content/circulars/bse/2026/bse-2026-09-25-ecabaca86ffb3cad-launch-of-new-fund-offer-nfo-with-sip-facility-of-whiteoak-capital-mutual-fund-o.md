## Summary

WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund is launching its New Fund Offer (NFO) for the WhiteOak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF (Regular and Direct Plans) along with a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from September 28, 2026, to October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: WhiteOak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active FOF (Regular Plan - Growth & Direct Plan - Growth)
- ISINs: INF1J2R01197 (Regular), INF1J2R01189 (Direct)
- NFO Period: September 28, 2026, to October 12, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 12, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 19, 2026
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational notice regarding a New Fund Offer launch and associated guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 16, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (October 12, 2026).
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: September 28, 2026
- NFO Close Date: October 12, 2026 (Midnight for Lumpsum; SIP/XSIP authentication by 3:00 PM)
- Funds Pay-in / Clear Funds Deadline: October 16, 2026 (by 6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and trading members with clear operational schedules, guidelines, and deadlines for participating in the WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform without any adverse market impacts.
