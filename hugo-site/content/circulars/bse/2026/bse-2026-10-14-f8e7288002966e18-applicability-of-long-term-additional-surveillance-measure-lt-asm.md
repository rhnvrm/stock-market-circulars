## Summary

BSE has announced the imposition, revision, and removal of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) across various securities pursuant to the revised LT-ASM framework.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable effective October 19, 2026 on all open positions as on October 16, 2026 and new positions created thereafter (Annexure I Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands effective October 15, 2026 (Annexure I Part B).
- Securities continuing in LT-ASM framework but transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group effective October 19, 2026 (Annexure I Part D).
- Securities moving out of LT-ASM framework effective October 15, 2026 with price bands reinstated (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and group shifts for securities identified under the LT-ASM framework based on XBRL submissions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and note price band changes and group transfers for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- October 15, 2026: Lower price bands applicable / securities moving out or to higher stages of LT-ASM.
- October 16, 2026: Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- October 19, 2026: 100% margins effective / group transfers effective.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in affected securities will face increased margin obligations, price band restrictions, or segment transfers, impacting liquidity and risk management for market participants.
