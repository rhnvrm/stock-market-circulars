## Summary

Madhav Jayesh Valia (Promoter and Seller) of East India Drums & Barrels Manufacturing Limited proposes to sell up to 29,07,200 equity shares representing up to 19.68% of the total issued and paid-up equity share capital through an Offer for Sale (OFS) via the designated BSE window. The floor price for the offer is set at Rs. 95/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- **Seller:** Madhav Jayesh Valia (Promoter and Seller)
- **Company:** East India Drums & Barrels Manufacturing Limited
- **Offer Size:** Up to 29,07,200 equity shares (representing up to 19.68% of total issued and paid-up equity share capital)
- **Floor Price:** Rs. 95/- per Equity Share
- **Objective:** To achieve the minimum public shareholding of the Company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with revised guidelines for Bidding in Offer for Sale (OFS) Segment referencing SEBI circulars SEBI/HO/MRD/DOPI/CIR/P/2018/159, SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-3/P/CIR/2023/10, and SEBI/HO/MRD/MRD-PoD-3/P/CIR/2024/82.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must adhere to the revised guidelines for bidding in the OFS segment.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 24, 2026
- **T Day (Non-Retail Investors only):** August 25, 2026
- **T+1 Day (Retail Investors and carry-forward Non-Retail bids):** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates dilution of promoter shareholding to meet regulatory minimum public shareholding norms and provides liquidity/trading opportunities in the company's shares.
