## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability, movement, and continuation of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective from March 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- New shortlisting of securities in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework w.e.f. March 16, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities moving to respective higher Stage ASM.
- Securities moving out of the Short Term ASM Framework (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under various stages of the ST-ASM framework (Annexure III).

## Regulatory Changes

Execution of surveillance measures under the Short Term ASM framework to protect market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages for the listed scrips and ensure appropriate margin and trading restrictions are followed.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: March 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities may be subject to enhanced margins or specific trading restrictions depending on their assigned ASM stage.
