## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice regarding the cancellation of enlistment for certain Research Analysts following orders by SEBI. The cancellations are a result of non-payment of prescribed renewal fees under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014.

## Key Points

- SEBI cancelled the Certificates of Registration of specified Research Analysts due to non-payment of renewal fees.
- Consequently, BSE Limited has cancelled the enlistment granted to these Research Analysts.
- Annexure A lists the affected entities, including Ajay Gupta, Dr. Ram Kumar Goyal, and Thanos Equity Research.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of registration cancellations under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 for fee default.

## Compliance Requirements

Affected research analysts are no longer permitted to operate under their previous SEBI registration or BSE enlistment.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 25, 2026
- Reference SEBI Circular Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The named research analysts lose their authorization to provide research services, protecting investors from non-compliant entities.
