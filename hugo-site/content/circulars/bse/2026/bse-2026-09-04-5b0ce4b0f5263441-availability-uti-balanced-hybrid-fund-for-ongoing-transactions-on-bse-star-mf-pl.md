## Summary

Following previous Notice No. 20260813-36 dated August 13, 2026, BSE has announced the availability of UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund for ongoing transactions, including subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective from September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes affected: UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund (Regular and Direct plans for Growth and IDCW Payout).
- Facilities available: Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective Date: September 7, 2026.
- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members need to note the availability of the scheme for client transactions starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless trading and investment operations in UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform for investors and distributors.
