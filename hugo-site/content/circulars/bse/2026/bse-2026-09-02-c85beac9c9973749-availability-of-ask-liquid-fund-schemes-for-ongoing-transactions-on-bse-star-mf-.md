## Summary

BSE has announced that pursuant to Notice No. 20260811-48 dated August 11, 2026, the ASK Liquid Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, including Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** ASK Liquid Fund
- **Scheme Category:** Liquid
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform
- **Effective Date:** September 03, 2026
- **Available Facilities:** Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, and SWP

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational notice regarding scheme availability.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors utilizing the BSE StAR MF platform can facilitate transactions in the mentioned scheme for investors starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 02, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity for investors and trading members looking to transact in ASK Liquid Fund schemes via the BSE StAR MF platform.
