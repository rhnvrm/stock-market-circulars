## Summary

ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund is launching the ICICI Prudential BSE Power ETF NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective from October 26, 2026, to October 30, 2026. The tentative allotment date for the scheme is November 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ICICI Prudential BSE Power ETF & ICICI Prudential BSE Power ETF - Direct Plan
- NFO Period: October 26, 2026, to October 30, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 04, 2026
- Extended NFO Period: The NFO schemes remain extended till midnight on October 30, 2026, only for Lumpsum purchases on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 03, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted subject to receipt of clear funds by 6:00 PM on November 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: October 26, 2026
- NFO End Date: October 30, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline / Clear Funds Availability: November 03, 2026 (up to 6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables members and investors to subscribe to the ICICI Prudential BSE Power ETF NFO seamlessly via the BSE StAR MF platform with standard operational guidelines.
