## Summary

Shellz India Limited's equity shares are listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE SME segment effective October 23, 2026. A total of 12,067,104 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each, issued at Rs. 179/- per share, will be traded in the 'MT' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Listing Effective Date: October 23, 2026.
- Scrip Code: 544998; Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SHELLZ.
- ISIN: INE0Z7301016.
- Market Lot: 800 Equity Shares.
- Issue Price: Rs. 179/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 169/-).
- Segment: Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.
- Market Maker: Mansi Share and Stock Broking Private Limited.
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading governed by SEBI circular No. CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, requiring securities to remain in the trade-for-trade segment for the initial 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that securities will trade exclusively in dematerialised form in minimum market lots of 800 shares.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.
- Necessary corporate actions executed to mark the lock-in period in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 21, 2026.
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: October 23, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and liquidity for Shellz India Limited shares on the BSE SME platform, with initial trade-for-trade safeguards ensuring price stabilization.
