## Summary

Suruchi Properties Private Limited has announced a partial redemption of its debentures, resulting in a reduced face value per debenture effective from August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Suruchi Properties Private Limited
- Purpose: Part Redemption of Debentures
- Scrip Code: 976775
- ISIN: INE217T07027
- Scrip ID / Description: SPPL-10%-11-6-28-PVT
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 73,436/- per Debenture

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised that the debentures will be traded with the aforementioned reduced face value starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 10, 2026
- Effective Date: August 11, 2026
- DR Number: 691/2026-2027

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors holding the affected debentures must take note of the reduced face value for ongoing settlement and valuation purposes.
