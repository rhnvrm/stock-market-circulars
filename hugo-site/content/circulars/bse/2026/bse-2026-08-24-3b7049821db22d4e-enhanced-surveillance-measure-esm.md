## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the inclusion of certain securities, movement to lower stages, and securities moving out of the framework. These measures include a minimum 100% margin requirement and shifting affected securities to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM and will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 26, 2026, on all open positions as on August 25, 2026 and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but are moved to lower Stage I w.e.f. August 25, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. August 26, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 25, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ESM framework restrictions including 100% margins and Trade-for-Trade settlement for shortlisted scrips.
- Price band reinstatements for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (minimum 100%) for open and new positions in securities specified under Annexure I.
- Monitor settlement mechanisms as affected securities shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage II adjustments / movement to lower Stage I / moving out: August 25, 2026.
- Effective Date for 100% margin requirement and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment: August 26, 2026.
- Open positions reference date: August 25, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading flexibility for the impacted securities will be restricted due to the 100% margin requirement and Trade-for-Trade settlement constraints, alongside periodic call auctions and 2% price bands for Stage II scrips.
