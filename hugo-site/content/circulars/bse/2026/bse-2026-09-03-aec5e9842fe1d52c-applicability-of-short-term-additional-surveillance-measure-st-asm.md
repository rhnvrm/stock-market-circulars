## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on September 04, 2026, and new positions created from September 07, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision and enforcement of surveillance margins under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for applicable open and new positions starting September 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 07, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: September 04, 2026
- Notice Date: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements may affect liquidity and leverage for trading participants holding or taking positions in the short-listed securities.
