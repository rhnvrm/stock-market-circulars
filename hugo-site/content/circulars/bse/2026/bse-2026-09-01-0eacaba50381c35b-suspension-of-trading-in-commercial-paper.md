## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in commercial papers for several companies due to fixed record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the respective record dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260901-3
- Department: Listing Operations
- Segment: Debt
- Action: Suspension of trading in commercial papers due to redemption.
- Affected entities include Can Fin Homes Ltd., Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited, Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited, Standard Chartered Capital Limited, Goldman Sachs (India) Finance Private Limited, Nuvama Wealth Finance Limited, Nuvama Clearing Services Limited, and GIC Housing Finance Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the listed companies on and from the specified record dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 01 Sep 2026
- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: 02/09/2026, 03/09/2026, 04/09/2026, 07/09/2026, 08/09/2026, and 09/09/2026 depending on the specific commercial paper.

## Impact Assessment

Restricts secondary market trading in specific commercial papers around their redemption record dates to ensure smooth settlement.
