## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding modifications in the Pre-open Call Auction Session in the Equity Segment, referencing prior SEBI circular No. SEBI/HO/47/11/11(3)2025-MRD-POD2/I/2765/2026 dated January 16, 2026, and BSE circular no. 20260803-35 dated August 03, 2026. Trading members are requested to review the updated FAQ document enclosed as an annexure.

## Key Points

- Updated FAQ document will be available on the Exchange website effective August 26, 2026 (under Markets → Equity → Market Information → Pre-open Session).
- Members must ensure all fields and information related to the Pre-open Session are appropriately displayed in client-facing applications.
- Changes become effective for live trading from Monday, September 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Modifications in the pre-open call auction session in the equity segment as mandated by SEBI and exchange circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that their client-facing applications appropriately display all fields and information related to the pre-open session.

## Important Dates

- FAQ Availability Date: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date for Live Trading: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational updates for trading members and developers of client-facing trading applications to align with pre-open session modifications.
