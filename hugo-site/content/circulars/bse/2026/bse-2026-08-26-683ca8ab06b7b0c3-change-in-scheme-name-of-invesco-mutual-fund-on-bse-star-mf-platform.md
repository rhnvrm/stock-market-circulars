## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing members about the change in scheme names for multiple schemes of Invesco Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Communication received from Invesco Mutual Fund regarding scheme name changes.
- Effective date: August 26, 2026.
- Affected schemes include various banking, PSU, and duration funds.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and participants must update their systems to reflect the revised scheme names for order placement and reporting.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update with minimal impact on trading workflows, ensuring accurate scheme identification on the BSE StAR MF platform.
