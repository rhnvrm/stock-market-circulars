## Summary

Trading members are informed that Power Grid Corporation of India Limited has fixed a record date for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures. Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Power Grid Corporation of India Limited
- Corporate Action: Part Redemption / Payment of Interest of Non-Convertible Debentures
- Affected ISINs & Scrip Codes:
  - Scrip Code 974593 | INE752E08676 | Reduced Face Value: Rs. 65,000/- per debenture
  - Scrip Code 974698 | INE752E08684 | Reduced Face Value: Rs. 67,500/- per debenture
  - Scrip Code 974737 | INE752E08692 | Reduced Face Value: Rs. 67,500/- per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the reduced face values and ensure trades are executed accordingly with effect from the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 01/08/2026
- Ex-Date: 31/07/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for debt segment trading members holding or trading the specified non-convertible debentures.
