## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Phychem Technologies Limited are being shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Phychem Technologies Limited
- Scrip Code: 544902
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer of trading segment from MT Group to Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members to take note of the segment change for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 07, 2026
- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in equity shares of the company will shift to the rolling segment, allowing normal rolling settlement.
