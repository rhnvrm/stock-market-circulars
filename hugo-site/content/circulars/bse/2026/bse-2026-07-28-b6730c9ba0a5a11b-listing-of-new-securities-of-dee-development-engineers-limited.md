## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of DEE DEVELOPMENT ENGINEERS LIMITED (Scrip Code - 544198) effective from Wednesday, July 29, 2026. A total of 59,76,096 equity shares were issued on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** DEE DEVELOPMENT ENGINEERS LIMITED
- **Scrip Code:** 544198
- **ISIN:** INE841L01016
- **Number of Shares:** 59,76,096 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 502/- per share (including premium of Rs. 492/-)
- **Date of Allotment:** 08/07/2026
- **Effective Date:** 29/07/2026
- **Distribution Nos.:** 74348234 to 80324329

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the lock-in details and distribution numbers for the newly listed equity shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** July 8, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto Dates:** February 28, 2027 (55,77,690 shares) and February 28, 2028 (3,98,406 shares)

## Impact Assessment

Increase in the paid-up equity capital of Dee Development Engineers Limited due to preferential allotment.
