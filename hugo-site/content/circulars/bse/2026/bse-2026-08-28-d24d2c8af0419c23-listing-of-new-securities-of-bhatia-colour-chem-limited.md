## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Bhatia Colour Chem Limited (Scrip Code: 543497) effective from August 31, 2026, following the conversion of warrants issued on a preferential basis to promoters.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Bhatia Colour Chem Limited (Scrip Code: 543497)
- **Securities Issued:** 1,98,400 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 124/-
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 134/- per share
- **Basis:** Preferential basis to promoters pursuant to conversion of warrants
- **Effective Date of Trading:** August 31, 2026
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares
- **ISIN:** INE0KQ001017
- **Distribution Nos.:** 14139348 to 14337747

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members note the lock-in requirements applicable to the shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 02/07/2026
- **Effective Date:** 31/08/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 31/08/2028

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital expansion via warrant conversion for the listed entity.
