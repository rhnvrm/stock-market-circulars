## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 13 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS). The trading for these newly issued securities commences with effect from Tuesday, August 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total Companies Involved: 13 listed companies.
- Effective Date: August 04, 2026.
- Segment: Equity.
- Department: Listing Operations.
- Included Companies: Anand Rathi Share And Stock Brokers Limited, Anthem Biosciences Limited, DCB Bank Limited, HDFC Bank Limited, L&T Finance Limited, Meesho Limited, Moschip Technologies Limited, Navin Fluorine International Limited, Polycab India Limited, Purple Finance Limited, Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited, South Indian Bank Limited, and Wipro Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the new securities admitted for trading and ensure correct processing of ISINs and lock-in requirements where applicable (e.g., Anand Rathi Share And Stock Brokers Limited has a lock-in period up to 13/07/2027).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 03, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase via ESOP allotments, leading to minor equity dilution and expanded float for the respective companies.
