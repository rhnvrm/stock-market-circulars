## Summary

Arunjyoti Bio Ventures Ltd has informed the Exchange about changing its name to Pasura Industries Limited. Consequently, the scrip ID and abbreviated name on the BOLT Plus trading system will be updated effective October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Arunjyoti Bio Ventures Ltd
- New Company Name: Pasura Industries Limited
- Scrip Code: 530881
- New Scrip ID (BOLT Plus): PASURA
- Abbreviated Name (BOLT Plus): PASURA
- Effective Date: October 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of trading identifiers and system records on the BSE BOLT Plus system to reflect the new corporate name.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their internal systems and records with the new name, scrip ID, and abbreviation for scrip code 530881.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date for Name Change / Scrip ID Update: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Purely administrative corporate action with no direct financial impact on existing shareholders, though trading terminals and client databases require updating.
