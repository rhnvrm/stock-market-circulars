## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, review, and revision of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. As per the framework, 100% margins, price band changes, and segment transfers apply to specified securities effective from September 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. September 08, 2026, on all open positions as on September 07, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moved to higher stages of LT-ASM, moving out of LT-ASM, or transferred to T / XT / MT / TS groups effective September 04/08, 2026.
- Lower price bands applicable to specified securities.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions, not an adverse action against companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework guidelines as detailed in annexures.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for scrips moving out of the framework, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure 100% margin maintenance for securities under Annexure I (Part A) starting September 08, 2026.
- Members and trading participants must adhere to revised price bands and segment transfers effective September 04 and September 08, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date (Higher Stages / Price Bands / Moving Out):** September 04, 2026
- **Effective Date (100% Margins / Group Transfers):** September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased capital requirements for trading in securities placed under 100% margin requirements.
- Potential reduction in liquidity and increased volatility for securities subjected to lower price bands and surveillance stage upgrades.
