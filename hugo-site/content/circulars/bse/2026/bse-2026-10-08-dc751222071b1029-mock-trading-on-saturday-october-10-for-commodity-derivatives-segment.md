## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Commodity Derivatives segment on Saturday, October 10, 2026. The session is designed to help trading members test their trading software, third-party vendor platforms, and in-house systems under simulated market conditions.

## Key Points

- **Date**: Saturday, October 10, 2026.
- **Segment**: Commodity Derivatives.
- **Purpose**: Software testing (including risk-reduction mode, trading halt, etc.) and fulfilling regulatory requirements under SEBI circular SEBI/HO/MRD1/DSAP/CIR/P/2020/234.
- **New Release**: New BOLT Pro TWS version 13.60 will be released separately.
- **Feedback**: Members are requested to provide feedback by 5:30 PM on the day of the session.

## Session Timings

- **Log-in**: 10:15 am to 11:00 am
- **Continuous Trading (PR)**: 11:00 am to 02:00 pm
- **Switchover to (DR)**: 02:00 pm to 02:45 pm
- **Continuous Trading (DR)**: 02:45 pm to 04:45 pm

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; fulfills routine software testing and regulatory mandates in accordance with SEBI circular dated November 24, 2020.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members can participate either in the mock trading session or the UAT environment to fulfill their regulatory requirements regarding software and risk management testing.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date**: October 10, 2026.
- **Feedback Deadline**: October 10, 2026 by 5:30 PM.

## Impact Assessment

Trades resulting from mock trading are for testing purposes only and do not attract any margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations, nor do they create actual rights or liabilities.
