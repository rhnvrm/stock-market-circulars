## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the allocation of a new ISIN number for the sub-divided equity shares of TCC Concept Ltd (Scrip Code: 512038) following a stock split.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TCC Concept Ltd
- Scrip Code: 512038
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares from face value of Rs. 10/- to Rs. 2/-
- New ISIN: INE887D01024
- Ex-Date: 04-09-2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the new ISIN for trades done on and from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 3, 2026
- Ex-Date: September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The new ISIN will be effective for trades starting from the ex-date, ensuring smooth operational transition for market participants.
