## Summary

In continuation of BSE Notice No. 20260929-39, trading in the equity shares of UNITEC FIBRES LIMITED (Scrip Code: 544948) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group).

## Key Points

- Company Name: UNITEC FIBRES LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 544948
- Segment Change: Transferred from Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Thursday, October 15, 2026
- Contact Person: Mr. Anurag Jain (Tel. No. 022-2272 8822)

## Regulatory Changes

Shift of equity shares from surveillance/trade-for-trade settlement (MT Group) to standard rolling settlement (M Group) on the BSE SME platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are required to note the change in segment and trading group for execution of transactions in UNITEC FIBRES LIMITED starting October 15, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Improves market liquidity and regularizes trading for equity shares of UNITEC FIBRES LIMITED as it moves out of the Trade for Trade segment into the Rolling segment.
