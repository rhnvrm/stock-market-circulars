## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new privately placed debt securities issued by Vivriti Capital Limited on the debt market segment effective September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Issuer Name:** Vivriti Capital Limited
- **Scrip Code / ID / ISIN 1:** 978171 / 96VCL27 / INE0P1407083 (VCL-9.6%-17-12-27-PVT), Coupon: 9.6% p.a., Maturity: 17/12/2027
- **Scrip Code / ID / ISIN 2:** 978173 / 98VCL28 / INE0P1407075 (VCL-9.8%-18-8-28-PVT), Coupon: 9.8% p.a., Maturity: 18/08/2028
- **Quantity:** 50,000 units for each tranche at a face value/issue price of Rs. 10,000 each.
- **Trading Form:** Dematerialised form only with a tick size of 1 paise.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure trades are executed in dematerialised form under the specified ISIN numbers.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 18/09/2026
- **Effective Listing Date:** 21/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and trading access for the newly listed privately placed debt instruments on the exchange debt segment.
