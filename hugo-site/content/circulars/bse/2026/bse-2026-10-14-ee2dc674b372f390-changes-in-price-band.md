## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that the price bands for 30 scrips have been revised from their existing levels, effective from October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 30 scrips affected by price band revisions.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is October 15, 2026.
- Price bands have been revised to various percentages (2%, 5%, 10%, 20%) depending on the scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of price bands for specified equity scrips as part of market surveillance and risk management.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure systems are updated with the revised price bands for the mentioned scrips before market hours on the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Limits the maximum intraday price movement for the affected scrips, mitigating excessive volatility and protecting investors.
