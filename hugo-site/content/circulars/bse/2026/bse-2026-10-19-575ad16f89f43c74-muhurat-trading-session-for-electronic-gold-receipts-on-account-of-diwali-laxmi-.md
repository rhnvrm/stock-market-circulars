## Summary

BSE has notified trading members regarding the timings of the special Muhurat Trading Session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment on account of Diwali Laxmi Pujan, scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Segment:** Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR)
- **Date:** Sunday, November 08, 2026
- **Log-in Period:** 01:15 pm to 02:05 pm
- **Order Entry Period (Pre-Open):** 02:05 pm to 02:13 pm (Random stoppage in last 1 minute)
- **Matching Period:** 02:13 pm to 02:20 pm
- **Continuous Trading:** 02:20 pm to 03:30 pm
- **Block Deal Session:** 02:35 pm to 02:50 pm
- **Closing Session:** 03:30 pm to 03:50 pm
- **Trade Modification End Time:** 04:15 pm
- **Trade Annulment End Time:** 04:20 pm

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a scheduled special trading session announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the special session schedule and coordinate with the BSE helpdesk or relationship managers for any queries.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Muhurat Trading Date:** November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact, providing participants with scheduled festive trading hours for the EGR segment.
