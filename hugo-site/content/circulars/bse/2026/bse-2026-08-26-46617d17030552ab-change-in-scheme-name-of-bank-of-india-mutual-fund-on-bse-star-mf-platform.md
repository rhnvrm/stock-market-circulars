## Summary

Bank of India Mutual Fund has notified the exchange regarding the change in scheme names for four of its mutual fund schemes available on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- Affected Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Changes include updates to ELSS, ultra-short duration, hybrid, and short-term income fund categories.

## Scheme Name Changes

1. **Existing:** BANK OF INDIA ELSS TAX SAVER -> **Revised:** BANK OF INDIA ELSS TAX SAVER FUND
2. **Existing:** BANK OF INDIA ULTRA SHORT DURATION FUND -> **Revised:** BANK OF INDIA ULTRA SHORT TERM FUND
3. **Existing:** BANK OF INDIA AGGRESSIVE HYBRID FUND -> **Revised:** BANK OF INDIA MID & SMALL CAP EQUITY & DEBT FUND
4. **Existing:** BANK OF INDIA SHORT TERM INCOME FUND -> **Revised:** BANK OF INDIA SHORT TERM FUND

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, distributors, and investors need to update their records and systems to reflect the revised scheme names for transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal and limited to name updates in transactional systems, portfolios, and reporting tools.
