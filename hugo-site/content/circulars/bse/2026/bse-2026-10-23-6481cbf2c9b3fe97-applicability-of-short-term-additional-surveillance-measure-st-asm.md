## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The applicable surveillance actions take effect from October 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, w.e.f. October 27, 2026.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, w.e.f. October 27, 2026.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on October 26, 2026, and new positions created from October 27, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened margin requirements under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin requirements (50% or 100%) on open and new positions for the shortlisted securities effective October 27, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 27, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants dealing in the shortlisted securities will experience higher capital/margin blockage due to the 50% or 100% margin requirements, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in those scrips.
