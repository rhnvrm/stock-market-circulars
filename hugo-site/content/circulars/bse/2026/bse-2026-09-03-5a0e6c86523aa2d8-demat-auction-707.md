## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 707 for Rolling Settlement No. DR-707/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of specified securities in auction. The circular outlines session timings for offer entry, matching, and reporting, alongside pay-in schedules.

## Key Points

- Auction offers for Rolling Settlement No. DR-707/2026-27 will be accepted on Thursday, September 03, 2026.
- Auction offer entry timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m.
- Auction matching timings: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted offers: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction will be held on Friday, September 04, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- Following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment, settlement is conducted through a Clearing Member of a Trading Member.
- The previous restriction preventing trading members from offering securities in auction for settlements where they experienced short delivery has been removed.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Members must exercise utmost care while punching security codes and rates, as no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- Members should verify cut-off rates on the "Auction Offer Entry Screen" and account for corporate actions (Ex-Rights, Ex-Bonus, Ex-Split, etc.).

## Important Dates

- **Auction Date:** September 03, 2026
- **Pay-in Date:** September 04, 2026 (08:00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Operational guidance for trading and clearing members participating in BSE demat auctions, ensuring smooth settlement execution and clearing operations.
