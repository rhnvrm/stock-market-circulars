## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the listing and permission to trade 2,92,001 new equity shares of DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Limited (Scrip Code - 543193) effective from September 22, 2026. These shares were issued at a premium upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Company Name: DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Limited
- Scrip Code: 543193
- ISIN: INE0B1K01014
- Number of Shares: 2,92,001 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 104/- (Issue Price: Rs. 114/- per share).
- Allottees: Non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants.
- Distribution Nos.: 34377852 to 34669852
- Date of Allotment: 10/06/2026
- Lock-in Details: 2,92,001 shares locked-in upto 25/03/2027.
- Effective Date of Trading: September 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure adherence to the lock-in periods and distribution numbers specified for the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 10/06/2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: 22/09/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 25/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

 Expands the paid-up equity capital of DJ Mediaprint & Logistics Limited following warrant conversion by non-promoters.
