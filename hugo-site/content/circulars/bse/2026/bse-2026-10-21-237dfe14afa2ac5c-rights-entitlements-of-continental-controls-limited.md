## Summary

BSE has announced that dealings in the Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of Continental Controls Limited will commence on Friday, October 23, 2026. The Rights Issue itself will remain open from October 23, 2026, to October 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Continental Controls Limited
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751147
- Scrip ID: CONTICO-RE
- ISIN No.: INE545B20014
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: October 23, 2026 to October 26, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: October 23, 2026 to October 30, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Dealings in Rights Entitlements (REs) permitted on the Exchange effective October 23, 2026.
- Settlement on T+1 Rolling Settlement basis on a trade-for-trade basis in dematerialized form only.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs mentioning the ISIN and ensuring they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Disclaimers must be included prominently in contract notes / bills informing clients that purchasing REs only gives the right to participate in the ongoing Rights Issue or renounce them before the closing date.
- REs neither subscribed nor renounced on or before the Issue Closing Date will lapse and be extinguished.

## Important Dates

- Trading Period for Renunciation Starts: October 23, 2026
- Trading Period for Renunciation Ends: October 26, 2026
- Rights Issue Opening Date: October 23, 2026
- Rights Issue Closing Date: October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders holding shares as of the record date receive Rights Entitlements which can be traded or renounced during the specified window. Investors must apply or renounce before expiration to avoid lapse and extinguishment of REs.
