## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that trading is permitted in the new securities of PVV Infra Ltd effective from August 18, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up shares.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 536659
- ISIN: INE428B01021
- Number of Securities: 75,28,384 Equity shares of Rs.5/- each issued on a rights basis.
- Distinctive Numbers: 130283746 to 137812129
- Effective Date: Tuesday, August 18, 2026
- Status: Pari-passu with the existing old equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the aforementioned securities from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational listing update for shareholders and market participants holding or trading PVV Infra Ltd shares.
