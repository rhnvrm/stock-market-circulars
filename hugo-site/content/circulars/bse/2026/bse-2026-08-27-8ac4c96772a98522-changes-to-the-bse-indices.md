## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding changes to various BSE indices effective Wednesday, September 02, 2026, on account of the demerger of India Glycols Ltd.'s manufacturing and production of Alcoholic, Non-Alcoholic Beverages, Bio-based Specialities, and Performance Chemicals businesses.

## Key Points

- India Glycols Ltd. (Exchange ticker: 500201) is demerging its key business segments into two different companies.
- Effective September 02, 2026, India Glycols Ltd. will be dropped from several BSE indices including BSE Smallcap, BSE Fast Moving Consumer Goods, BSE Allcap, BSE Midsmallcap, BSE 1000, BSE Next 500, BSE 250 Microcap, BSE 1000 Multicap Equal Size Weighted (25%), and BSE Smallcap 500.
- Other adjustments involve stocks such as GNG Electronics Ltd. and Sky Gold and Diamonds Ltd. entering or shifting across various indices like BSE Next 250 Microcap.
- Actions have been included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

## Regulatory Changes

- Reconstitution of index constituents resulting from corporate restructuring schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, index funds, and traders tracking BSE indices need to update their portfolios and tracking mechanisms in line with the revised index constituents.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Passive funds and index trackers holding or benchmarking against affected BSE indices will need to rebalance their holdings accordingly.
