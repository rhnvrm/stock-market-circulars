## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that Haldia Petrochemicals Limited has fixed a record date for the early redemption of its non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Consequently, trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of the company starting from the ex-date.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Haldia Petrochemicals Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 974026
- **Scrip ID:** HPL-8.75%-29-6-29-PVT
- **ISIN:** INE105U07014
- **Purpose:** Early Redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures
- **Record Date:** 23/10/2026
- **Ex-Date:** 23/10/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a corporate action notice regarding debt redemption and trading suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the Exchange are advised not to deal in the debentures of Haldia Petrochemicals Limited as of the specified ex-date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 21 October 2026
- **Record Date:** 23 October 2026
- **Ex-Date:** 23 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a standard operational notice reflecting the early redemption of debt instruments, affecting trading activity in the specific NCD scrip.
