## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-719/2026-27. The circular outlines the precise schedule for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and money statements, along with operational guidelines for clearing members and trading members.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement Number: DR-719/2026-27 (Auction 719).
- Offer entry session scheduled on Tuesday, September 22, 2026, from 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m.
- Auction matching will take place from 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction is scheduled for Wednesday, September 23, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.
- Restrictions on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they faced short delivery have been removed following the uniform membership structure implementation.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clarification that restrictions on trading members offering securities in auction in settlements where they had short delivered are now removed.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and complete securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Extreme care must be taken while punching security codes and rates; punching errors will not be permitted for correction.
- Members must verify cutoff rates on the Auction Offer Entry Screen and check corporate action notices (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 22, 2026
- Offer Entry Timing: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m.
- Auction Matching: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement & Report: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Auction Pay-in Date: September 23, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth execution of rolling settlement defaults and provides a clear operational timeline for clearing and trading members participating in BSE demat auctions.
