## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying criteria for inclusion, movement to lower stages, shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment, and price band revisions effective from October 07 and October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. October 08, 2026 (on open positions as of October 07, 2026, and new positions).
- Securities moving to lower Stage I under ESM framework w.e.f. October 07, 2026 are included in Annexure I.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. October 07, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the ESM framework w.e.f. October 07, 2026 are listed in Annexure II.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is given in Annexure III.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) criteria, 100% margin requirements, shifting to Trade-for-Trade segment, and price band restrictions (2% for Stage II) along with Periodic Call Auction.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions for applicable securities, and note the shift to Trade-for-Trade settlement and price band adjustments.

## Important Dates

- October 07, 2026: Effective date for movement to lower Stage I, movement to Stage II (Trade for Trade with 2% price band & Periodic Call Auction), and securities moving out of ESM.
- October 08, 2026: Effective date for 100% margin on open positions as of October 07, 2026 and new positions, and shifting of qualifying securities to Trade-for-Trade segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in securities listed under Annexures I, II, and III will experience increased margin requirements (100%), movement to Trade-for-Trade settlement, and price band/call auction constraints, impacting liquidity and trading mechanics for affected scrips.
