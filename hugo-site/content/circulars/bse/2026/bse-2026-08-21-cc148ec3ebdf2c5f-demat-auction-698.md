## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 698 in respect of Rolling Settlement No. DR-698/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of specified securities.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on August 21, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on August 21, 2026.
- Delivery Statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m. on August 21, 2026.
- Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. on August 21, 2026.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Monday, August 24, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- Restriction on trading members offering securities in auction in the settlement in which they have short delivered such securities is removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members should verify cutoff rates in the Auction offer Entry Screen and review corporate action notices.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 21, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: August 24, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading members involved in short deliveries for Rolling Settlement DR-698/2026-27, requiring adherence to strict auction entry and pay-in timelines.
