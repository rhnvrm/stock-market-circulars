## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Buyback) for BAJAJ AUTO LIMITED. The bidding is scheduled to be conducted from 01/07/2026 to 07/07/2026 under Settlement No. 2026079.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Bajaj Auto Limited
- **Notice No.:** 20260630-29
- **Notice Date:** 30 June 2026
- **Settlement Type:** Buyback
- **Settlement No.:** 2026079
- **Bid Start Date:** 01/07/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 07/07/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 13/07/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 14/07/2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operating procedure for stock exchange buyback acquisition window and settlement calendar.

## Compliance Requirements

- Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism available in both depositories (CDSL & NSDL).
- Market Type "(Buyback)" and Settlement No. 2026079 must be used by Members/Custodians.
- Members and custodians must ensure early pay-in reflection in BSE/ICCL systems prior to the bid acceptance/confirmation window closing.
- No EPN file needs to be uploaded by members and custodians for CDSL & NSDL early pay-in securities as they will be earmarked/blocked in the client's account.

- **Bid End Date:** 07/07/2026
