## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of Adroit Industries (India) Limited effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total Securities: 4,48,08,340 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up.
- Issue Price: Rs. 134/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 124/-).
- Scrip Code: 544950, Scrip ID: ADROITIND, ISIN: INE505M01015.
- Segment: Placed in 'T' Group of Securities and Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) applicable on September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading conducted in accordance with SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012, requiring a Trade-for-Trade segment for initial trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must execute trades exclusively in dematerialized form. Registrar to the issue is Bigshare Services Private Limited.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 28, 2026
- Listing & Trading Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and liquidity for shareholders of Adroit Industries (India) Limited with standard surveillance measures for newly listed scrips.
