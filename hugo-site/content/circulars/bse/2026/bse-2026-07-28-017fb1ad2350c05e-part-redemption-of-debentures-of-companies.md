## Summary

Trading members are informed that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260728-21
- Department: Listing Operations
- Segment: Debt
- Companies involved include IOT Utkal Energy Services Limited, Aadharshila Inratech Private Limited, Mizuho Capsave Finance Private Limited, Varthana Finance Private Limited, Muthoot Microfin Limited, and Greaves Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine corporate action processing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the reduced face values and record/ex-dates for the respective debentures.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates and Ex-Dates range across August 13, 2026, to August 16, 2026, depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal direct market impact; affects holders of the specified NCDs regarding cash flows and reduced face values.
