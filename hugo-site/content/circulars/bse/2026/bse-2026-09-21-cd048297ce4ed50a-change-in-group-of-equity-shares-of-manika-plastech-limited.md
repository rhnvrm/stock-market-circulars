## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Manika Plastech Limited (Scrip Code: 544929) will be transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment, shifting under the B Group with effect from Tuesday, October 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Manika Plastech Limited
- Scrip Code: 544929
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of equity shares from the Trade for Trade segment to the Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to note the change in segment and group for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 06, 2026
- Notice Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normalization of trading for the scrip as it moves to the rolling settlement segment.
