## Summary

Zerodha Mutual Fund is offering the NFO for ZERODHA BSE TOP 10 BANKS ETF on the BSE StAR MF platform from October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ZERODHA BSE TOP 10 BANKS ETF (Equity)
- NFO Period: October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 26, 2026 for Lumpsum purchases)
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 29, 2026
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag shall be reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 29, 2026, for allotment eligibility.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: October 12, 2026
- NFO End Date: October 26, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides market participants and investors with investment access to the Zerodha BSE Top 10 Banks ETF via the BSE StAR MF platform under standard mutual fund operating guidelines.
