## Summary

Effective Thursday, September 17, 2026, the equity shares of Manipal Payment and Identity Solutions Limited will be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: Manipal Payment and Identity Solutions Limited
- Scrip Code: 544916
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: MPIMANIPAL
- ISIN No.: INE241U01028
- Number of Securities: 231,804,528 Equity Shares of Rs.2/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 339/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 2/- and premium of Rs. 337/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Securities of the company will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that the scrip will be a part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, September 17, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 15, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members can trade in the equity shares of Manipal Payment and Identity Solutions Limited starting September 17, 2026, under the 'B' Group category.
