## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and admission to trading of new privately placed debt securities issued by Jio Credit Limited on the debt market segment effective September 02, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Jio Credit Limited
- **Security Details:** JCL-8.08%-31-08-29-PVT
- **Scrip Code:** 978101
- **Scrip ID:** 808JCL29
- **ISIN Number:** INE282H07109
- **Quantity:** 1,75,600
- **Face Value / Issue Price:** Rs. 1,00,000.00
- **Rate of Interest:** 8.08% p.a. (Payable annually from 31/08/2027 to 31/08/2029)
- **Date of Allotment:** 31/08/2026
- **Date of Redemption:** 31/08/2029
- **Credit Rating:** CARE AAA/Stable, CRISIL AAA/STABLE

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form under the specified ISIN.
- Tick size for the securities is 1 paise.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 31/08/2026
- **Listing Effective Date:** 02/09/2026
- **Redemption Date:** 31/08/2029

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt listing procedure with no wider systemic or market impact.
