## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the live activities schedule and operational parameters for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window/Takeover) of Cubical Financial Services Limited. The bidding period is scheduled from September 17, 2026, to September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name: CUBICAL FINANCIAL SERVICES LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 511710, ISIN: INE717D01023, Symbol: CUBIFIN
- Offer Size: Up to 3,77,44,200 Equity Shares
- Offer Price: Rs. 2.50 per share (Face Value: Rs. 2.00)
- Opening Date: Thursday, 17th September 2026
- Closing Date: Wednesday, 30th September 2026

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians should note operational guidelines outlined in previous Exchange notices (No. 20150930-1 and 20170203-29).

## Important Dates

- Offer Opening Date: 17th September 2026
- Offer Closing Date: 30th September 2026
- Give-up / Take-up Period: 17th September 2026 to 29th September 2026
- Last day Take-up: 4.00 p.m. on 30th September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly bidding, modification, and settlement processes for trading members and custodians participating in the takeover acquisition window of Cubical Financial Services Limited.
