## Summary

BSE Index Services Pvt. Ltd. has announced modifications to several BSE indices following a fast-track IPO review. Effective from the open of Tuesday, October 06, 2026, SBI Funds Management Limited and Manipal Health Enterprises Limited will be included in multiple benchmark and sectoral indices.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** October 06, 2026.
- **Index Additions:**
  - **SBI Funds Management Limited** (Ticker: 544829) added to BSE AllCap, BSE LargeCap, BSE LargeMidCap, and BSE Financial Services indices.
  - **Manipal Health Enterprises Limited** (Ticker: 544847) added to BSE AllCap, BSE LargeCap, BSE LargeMidCap, and BSE Healthcare indices.
- **Trigger:** Fast-track IPO review conducted for the index family.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; periodic index maintenance and fast-track IPO inclusion by BSE Index Services Pvt. Ltd.

## Compliance Requirements

Asset management companies, index funds, and market participants tracking BSE AllCap, LargeCap, LargeMidCap, Financial Services, and Healthcare indices must incorporate the constituent changes into their tracking portfolios ahead of the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Announcement Date:** September 24, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Passive funds and ETFs tracking the affected indices will adjust their portfolios to accommodate the newly listed entities, generating corresponding rebalancing activity.
