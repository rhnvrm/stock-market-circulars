## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the live activities schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) of Trio Mercantile Trading Ltd. The offer period runs from Wednesday, July 29, 2026, to Tuesday, August 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** TRIO MERCANTILE TRADING LTD
- **Security Symbol:** TRIOMERC
- **ISIN:** INE234G01022
- **Scrip Code:** 534755
- **Offer Size:** Up to 3,39,68,300 Equity Shares
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 1.25 per share
- **Opening Date:** Wednesday, 29th July 2026
- **Closing Date:** Tuesday, 11th August 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. Operational schedule issuance under existing takeover and acquisition window frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Bids can be placed for investors holding shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** 29th July 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** 11th August 2026
- **Bid Offering Period:** 29th July 2026 to 11th August 2026 (9.15 a.m. to 3.30 p.m.)
- **Give-up / Take-up Period:** 29th July 2026 to 10th August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates market participants and custodians in executing and settling the takeover offer for Trio Mercantile Trading Ltd within the stipulated timeline.
