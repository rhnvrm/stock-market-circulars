## Summary

BSE has announced the live activities schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) of Premier Explosives Ltd. The issue is scheduled to run from Monday, 12th October 2026, to Monday, 26th October 2026 on the Exchange.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Premier Explosives Ltd
- **Security Symbol:** PREMEXPLN
- **ISIN:** INE863B01029
- **Scrip Code:** 526247
- **Offer Size:** Up to 1,39,77,911 Equity Shares
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Face Value:** Rs. 2.00
- **Offer Price:** Rs. 705.65
- **Opening Date:** Monday, 12th October 2026
- **Closing Date:** Monday, 26th October 2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A (Standard operational schedule and guidelines circular for takeover offer-to-buy window).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading Members must ensure that the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can submit bids for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians must note that if no securities are accepted, no end-of-issue files will be generated on the Extranet.

## Important Dates

- **Opening Date / Bid Offering Start:** 12th October 2026 (9.15 a.m.)
- **Closing Date / Give-up Last Day:** 26th October 2026 (3.30 p.m.)
- **Take-up (Last day of the offer):** 26th October 2026 (up to 4.00 p.m.)
