## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are advised to participate and verify their technical connection parameters and disaster recovery (DR) site configurations.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment.
- Details provided for DR connection parameters of the BOLT Plus Trading system for Commodity Derivatives/EGR segment.
- Instructions for configuring BOLT TWS and 3rd party trading applications through ETI APIs.
- Exchange web-based systems (RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, Extranet, etc.) link details for DR site access provided.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should verify connectivity to the DR site using the specified primary and secondary IP and port settings.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact; designed to ensure system readiness and seamless failover capabilities for trading members.
