## Summary

Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund is reopening for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic facilities (SIP, STP, SWP) on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 2, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes involved: Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1572) and Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF846KA1580).
- Transactions permitted include ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Effective date of availability is September 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the aforementioned schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors ongoing liquidity and investment access to the Axis Nifty Energy Index Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform.
