## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities due to their redemption date. Trading members are instructed not to deal in the specified security effective from September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Details:** 182TB25926 (Scrip Code: 805197, ISIN: IN002025Y511)
- **Effective Date of Suspension:** September 23, 2026 (DR- 721/2026-2027)
- **Segment:** Debt
- **Department:** Listing Operations

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational suspension following the redemption schedule of government securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid T-bills/Government Securities with effect from September 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 22, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact as this is a routine suspension of trading following the maturity and redemption of a government treasury bill.
