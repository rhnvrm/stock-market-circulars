## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced a mock trading session for the Equity segment on Saturday, September 12, 2026. The session will test operations from both the Primary Site (PR) and the Disaster Recovery Site (DR).

## Key Points

- **Date:** Saturday, September 12, 2026.
- **Sites:** Primary Site (PR) and Disaster Recovery Site (DR).
- **Participants:** Trading members using third-party trading platforms can test their applications, including exceptional market conditions like call auction sessions, risk-reduction modes, trading halts, and block deals.
- **BOLT TWS:** There shall be no new BOLT TWS Release.
- **Switch Over to DR:** Scheduled between 12:00 pm and 12:45 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational mock session.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate to test their respective trading applications and connectivity with both primary and disaster recovery sites.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** September 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No direct impact on live market trading. Helps members ensure system readiness and resilience against disaster recovery scenarios.
