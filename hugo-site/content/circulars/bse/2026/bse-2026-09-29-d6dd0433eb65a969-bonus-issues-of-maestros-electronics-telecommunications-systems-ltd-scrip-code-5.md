## Summary

Maestros Electronics & Telecommunications Systems Ltd has announced a record date of October 1, 2026, for its 1:1 bonus issue. Trading members are informed that one new bonus equity share of Rs. 10/- each will be issued for every one existing equity share held as on the record date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Maestros Electronics & Telecommunications Systems Ltd (Scrip Code: 538401)
- Record Date: October 1, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: October 1, 2026 (Settlement No: DR- 727/2026-2027)
- Bonus Ratio: 1:1 (One bonus equity share for every one existing equity share held)
- Face Value: Rs. 10/- each
- Expected Allotment: 5,510,237 equity shares to be allotted as on October 5, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the ex-bonus date and record date for trade settlements and open positions in the scrip.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Record Date: October 1, 2026
- Ex-Bonus Date: October 1, 2026
- Allotment Date: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The 1:1 bonus issue will double the equity share capital of the company, resulting in a proportionate price adjustment on the ex-date and increasing liquidity in the company's shares.
