## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the temporary suspension of subscriptions and systematic investment/transfer plans in certain schemes of PGIM India Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Temporary suspension of subscription for Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) registrations under PGIM India Mutual Fund.
- Effective date: August 7, 2026.
- Affected schemes include:
  - PGIM INDIA GLOBAL EQUITY OPPORTUNITIES FUND OF FUND
  - PGIM INDIA EMERGING MARKETS EQUITY FUND OF FUND
  - PGIM INDIA GLOBAL SELECT REAL ESTATE SECURITIES FUND OF FUND
- Existing Systematic Registrations (SIP/XSIP/STPAMC) will be paused and not triggered or processed until further notice.
- Redemption, Switch-out, and STP-out transactions remain permitted without amount restrictions.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors must note the suspension of new subscriptions and systematic registrations for the specified PGIM India Mutual Fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding or planning fresh systematic investments in the affected PGIM India Fund of Funds will need to redirect their investment flows as existing SIP/STP requests are paused.
