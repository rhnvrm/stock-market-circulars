## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers for multiple companies that have fixed record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the respective record and ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260921-14
- Segment: Debt Segment
- Department: Listing Operations
- Subject: Suspension of Trading in Commercial Paper
- Affected entities include Standard Chartered Securities (India) Limited, Goldman Sachs (India) Finance Private Limited, IKF Finance Limited, SBICAP Securities Limited, Infina Finance Private Limited, KEC International Ltd., NLC India Limited, Avenue Supermarts Limited, Godrej Finance Limited, Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, ICICI Home Finance Company Limited, J.K. Cement Ltd, and Tata Steel Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the listed companies from their respective record dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 21 September 2026
- Record / Ex-Dates: 24/09/2026 and 25/09/2026 depending on the specific commercial paper.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in specified commercial papers is suspended temporarily or permanently as per redemption schedules, preventing further secondary market transactions in those instruments.
