The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Solitario Lab Grown Limited on September 30, 2026. The document contains disclosures specified under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations and is open for public comments for at least 21 days.

- Company Name: Solitario Lab Grown Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Segment: SME IPO
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice

- None. Standard compliance under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations for SME IPO Draft Offer Documents.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stakeholders and the public can review the Draft Offer Document on the SME website and submit comments within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Comments Deadline: Within 21 days from October 01, 2026
