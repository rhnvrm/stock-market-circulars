## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in commercial papers (CPs) for Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited and Tata Steel Limited due to the fixation of record dates for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies as per the announced record and ex-dates.
- Affected Issuers: Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited and Tata Steel Limited.
- Scrip Codes involved: 732259, 732257, and 731774.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure no transactions take place in the specified commercial paper ISINs on or after the respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Kotak Mahindra Prime Ltd (INE916D147E3): Record Date & Ex-Date: 20/08/2026
- Kotak Mahindra Prime Ltd (INE916D147D5): Record Date & Ex-Date: 24/08/2026
- Tata Steel Limited (INE081A14GZ9): Record Date: 26/08/2026, Ex-Date: 25/08/2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly redemption and settlement of commercial papers by restricting secondary market trading prior to maturity/redemption.
