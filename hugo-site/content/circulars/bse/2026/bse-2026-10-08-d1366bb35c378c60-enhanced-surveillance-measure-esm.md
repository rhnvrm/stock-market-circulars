## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, specifying securities subject to inclusion, stage movements, and exclusions effective October 09, 2026 and October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for inclusion under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin effective October 12, 2026 on open positions as on October 09, 2026 and new positions created from October 12, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective October 12, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II operate under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective October 09, 2026.
- Certain securities move to lower Stage I or move out of the ESM framework entirely effective October 09, 2026.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework are reinstated to their pre-shortlisting levels.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework, adjusting margin requirements, settlement segments, and price bands for specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure adherence to 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement rules for affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage Changes, Exclusions, and Stage II Price Bands: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Requirements & Trade-for-Trade Shifting: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities face heightened surveillance, increased margin requirements, trade-for-trade settlement restrictions, and price band adjustments, impacting liquidity and trading operations.
