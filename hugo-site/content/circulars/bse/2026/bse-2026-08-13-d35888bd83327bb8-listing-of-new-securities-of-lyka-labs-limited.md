## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission for trading of new equity shares of Lyka Labs Limited with effect from August 14, 2026, following a scheme of amalgamation.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Lyka Labs Limited (Scrip Code: 500259)
- Securities: 4,62,711 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each issued pursuant to the Scheme of Amalgamation of Lyka Exports Limited (Transferor Company) with Lyka Labs Limited (Transferee Company).
- Effective Date for Trading: August 14, 2026.
- ISIN Number: INE933A01014
- Distinctive Numbers: 35690001 to 36152711
- Date of Allotment: June 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; executed under Sections 230 to 232 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and depository participants need to note the credit and listing of the newly allotted shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: June 12, 2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital base of Lyka Labs Limited post amalgamation without any adverse market impacts.
