## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities qualifying under the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment, or move across different stages of the ESM framework effective July 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ESM will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. July 02, 2026, on all open positions as on July 01, 2026, and new positions created from July 02, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue in the ESM framework but move to lower Stage I w.e.f. July 01, 2026 (Annexure I).
- Securities qualifying under ESM will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. July 02, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. July 01, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is purely for market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the ESM framework, including 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlement shifts, and price band restrictions for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (minimum 100%) on open and new positions for securities listed under Annexure I, and account for segment shifts to Trade-for-Trade.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage Movements and Price Band Changes: July 01, 2026
- Effective Date for Margin Requirements and Trade-for-Trade Shifting: July 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for the affected securities will experience heightened restrictions as part of the exchange's ongoing surveillance measures.
