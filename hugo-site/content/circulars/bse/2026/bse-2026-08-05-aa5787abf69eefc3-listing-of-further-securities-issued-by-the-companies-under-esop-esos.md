## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by 14 companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) / Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Thursday, August 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 14
- Effective date for trading: August 06, 2026
- Companies include Affle 3i Limited, Ajanta Pharma Limited, Arvind Fashions Limited, CG Power and Industrial Solutions Limited, Federal Bank Limited, HDB Financial Services Limited, Karnataka Bank Limited, Mphasis Limited, Pearl Global Industries Limited, Regaal Resources Limited, Rubicon Research Limited, Senco Gold Limited, Vijaya Diagnostic Centre Limited, and Zensar Technologies Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are required to take note of the listing and admission of these securities for trading.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 05, 2026
- Effective Date: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity share capital for the respective listed companies following ESOP/ESOS exercises.
