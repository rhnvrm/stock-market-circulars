## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that the price bands for several scrips have been revised from their existing levels with effect from September 3, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260902-38
- Department: DOSS (Equity Segment)
- Effective Date: September 03, 2026
- Total scrips affected: 38 scrips with price band revisions ranging from 2% to 20%.

## Regulatory Changes

The price bands of specified scrips are adjusted to curb excessive price volatility and ensure orderly market operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure their trading systems and clients adhere to the updated limits.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 02 Sep 2026
- Effective Date: 03 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and traders dealing in the listed scrips must adjust their order placements according to the new price band limits.
