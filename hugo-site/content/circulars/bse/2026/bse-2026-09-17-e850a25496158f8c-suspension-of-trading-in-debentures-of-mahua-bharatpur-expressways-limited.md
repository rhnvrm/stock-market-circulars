## Summary

Mahua Bharatpur Expressways Limited has fixed a record date for the purpose of payment of interest and redemption of its Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). Consequently, trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of the company as per the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mahua Bharatpur Expressways Limited
- Scrip Code: 957131
- Scrip ID: MBEL-8.40%-5-10-26-PVT
- ISIN: INE835H07104
- Record Date: 20/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 18/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the debentures of Mahua Bharatpur Expressways Limited during the restricted period.

## Important Dates

- Ex-Date: 18/09/2026
- Record Date: 20/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified debentures of Mahua Bharatpur Expressways Limited is suspended temporarily in connection with the record date for interest payment and redemption.
