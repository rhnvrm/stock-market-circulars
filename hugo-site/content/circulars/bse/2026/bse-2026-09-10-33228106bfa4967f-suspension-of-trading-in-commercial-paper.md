## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in Commercial Papers (CP) of several companies due to fixed record dates for redemption. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading affects multiple commercial papers across various financial and corporate entities.
- Record dates and ex-dates are set for mid-September 2026.
- Affected entities include Adani Enterprises Ltd., Tata Steel Limited, Manappuram Finance Limited, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure they do not deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies on or after the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10/09/2026
- Ex-Dates: 11/09/2026 and 15/09/2026
- Record Dates: 11/09/2026, 14/09/2026, and 15/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in debt instruments and commercial papers must halt transactions in the listed ISINs to prevent failed settlements.
