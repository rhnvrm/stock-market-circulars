## Summary

Dexif Security Broking Private Limited (Member no. 6901) has submitted an application to surrender its membership of the BSE Exchange in accordance with the Rules, Bye-laws, and Regulations of the Exchange.

## Key Points

- Trading Member Name: Dexif Security Broking Private Limited
- Member Number: 6901
- Department: Membership Department

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members wishing to raise any objections to the surrender of membership must submit their objections in writing to the Membership Department on or before August 20, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 05, 2026
- Objection Deadline: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to the cessation of trading membership for Dexif Security Broking Private Limited upon completion of the surrender process.
