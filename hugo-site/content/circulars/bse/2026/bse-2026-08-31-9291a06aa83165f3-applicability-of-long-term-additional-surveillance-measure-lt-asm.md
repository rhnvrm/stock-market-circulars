## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the applicability, revisions, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. As per the revised guidelines, 100% margins and lower price bands apply to specified securities, and certain securities are transferred to T/XT/MT/TS groups or moving out of the framework entirely.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. September 03, 2026, on all open positions as on September 02, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM and lower price bands effective September 01, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in LT-ASM but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. September 03, 2026.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is attached as Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) including 100% margin requirements and lower price bands for shortlisted securities based on XBRL submissions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure adequate margin maintenance (100%) and adhere to lower price band restrictions for securities placed under the LT-ASM framework.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Price Band & Higher Stages: September 01, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and required capital allocations for affected securities will be significantly impacted due to 100% margin requirements and price band constraints.
