## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment scheduled for Saturday, August 29, 2026. Trading members using third-party platforms or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their applications, exceptional market conditions, and the new BOLT Pro TWS version 13.35.

## Key Points

- Scheduled date: Saturday, August 29, 2026.
- Segment: Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR).
- New software release: BOLT Pro TWS version 13.35.
- Divided into two distinct trading sessions with specific timings for log-in, pre-open, continuous trading, and trade modification.
- Trades resulting from mock sessions will not attract margin obligations, pay-in/pay-out, or create rights and liabilities.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an operational testing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to actively participate in the mock trading sessions.
- Members must provide feedback on the mock trading session via email or contact numbers by 12:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: August 29, 2026
- Feedback Submission Deadline: August 29, 2026, by 12:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

- Zero operational or financial risk to live market operations. Enables members to verify system stability and new terminal versions.
