## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in Auction with respect to Rolling Settlement No. DR-711/2026-27 on Wednesday, September 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on September 9, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted auction offers: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the auction will be held on Thursday, September 10, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard auction procedure reminder.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should exercise care while punching securities code and rate as no corrections are permitted in case of punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 9, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: September 10, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine operational notice affecting clearing and settlement members participating in demat auctions.
