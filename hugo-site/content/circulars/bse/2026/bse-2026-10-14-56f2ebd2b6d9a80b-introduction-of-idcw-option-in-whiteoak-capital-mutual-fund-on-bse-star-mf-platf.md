## Summary

BSE has announced that WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund has introduced the Income Distribution cum Capital Withdrawal (IDCW) option, featuring both reinvestment and payout facilities, on the BSE StAR MF platform effective October 15, 2026. This addition applies to five specified schemes of WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund.

## Key Points

- Introduction of IDCW option with Reinvestment & Payout Facility.
- Applicable platform: BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Effective date: October 15, 2026.
- Impacted Schemes:
  - WHITEOAK CAPITAL - BALANCED ADVANTAGE FUND
  - WHITEOAK CAPITAL - MULTI ASSET ALLOCATION FUND
  - WHITEOAK CAPITAL - BALANCED HYBRID FUND
  - WHITEOAK CAPITAL - EQUITY SAVINGS FUND
  - WHITEOAK CAPITAL - AGGRESSIVE HYBRID FUND

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational update regarding scheme options available on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors are advised to take note of the new IDCW option availability and facilitate transactions accordingly on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026
- Effective Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment and payout choices for investors participating in WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform with minimal operational impact.
