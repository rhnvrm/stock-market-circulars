## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units across five plans of Titanium Active Asset Allocator Long Short Fund under Tata Mutual Fund, effective from October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Mutual Fund:** Tata Mutual Fund
- **Asset Management Company:** Tata Asset Management Pvt Ltd
- **Effective Date of Listing:** October 19, 2026
- **Plans Listed:**
  - Regular Plan IDCW (Scrip Code: 544989, ISIN: INF277K30153)
  - Regular Plan IDCW Payout (Scrip Code: 544990, ISIN: INF277K30146)
  - Direct Plan IDCW (Scrip Code: 544991, ISIN: INF277K30187)
  - Regular Plan Growth (Scrip Code: 544992, ISIN: INF277K30138)
  - Direct Plan Growth (Scrip Code: 544993, ISIN: INF277K30161)
- **Face Value & Issue Price:** Rs. 10.00 per unit across all plans.
- **Date of Allotment:** October 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard listing procedure for mutual fund scheme units pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may note the listing and trading details of the mutual fund units admitted under Group "B".

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** October 12, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and trading availability on the exchange platform for investors holding units of Titanium Active Asset Allocator Long Short Fund under Tata Mutual Fund.
