## Summary

BSE has issued a notice outlining the timings of the Muhurat Trading Session on account of Diwali Laxmi Pujan for the Equity Derivatives Segment, scheduled for Sunday, November 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Log-in:** 01:15 pm to 02:05 pm
- **Pre-Open Order Entry (Limit and Market Orders):** 02:05 pm to 02:10 pm
- **Order Entry (Limit Orders Only):** 02:10 pm to 02:15 pm (with random stoppage in the last 2 minutes)
- **Order Entry Matching/Confirmation:** 02:15 pm to 02:17 pm
- **Buffer Period:** 02:17 pm to 02:20 pm
- **Continuous Trading:** 02:20 pm to 03:40 pm
- **Update of Future Contract Price Bands:** 03:15 pm to 03:20 pm
- **Trade Modification:** 02:20 pm to 04:15 pm
- **Trade Annulment Request:** Up to 04:20 pm

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a scheduled festive trading session announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the special session timings and ensure operational readiness.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Trading Session Date:** November 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal and localized operational impact, ensuring clarity for market participants during the festive trading session.
