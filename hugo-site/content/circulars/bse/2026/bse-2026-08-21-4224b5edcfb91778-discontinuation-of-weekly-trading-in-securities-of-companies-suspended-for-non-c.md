## Summary

BSE has announced the discontinuation of weekly trading in securities of companies suspended for non-compliance with Regulation 76 (Submission of Reconciliation of share capital audit report) of SEBI (Depositories & Participants) Regulations, 2018. The mandatory six months of trading on the first trading day of every week will be completed on September 07, 2026, leading to the discontinuation effective September 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Discontinuation of weekly trading in Z group on 'Trade for Trade' basis.
- Applies to companies suspended for non-compliance with Regulation 76 for two consecutive quarters (June 2025 and September 2025).
- Affected companies include Inditrade Capital Ltd, Mega Fin India Ltd, and Naturo Indiabull Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- Pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 issued on July 11, 2023 and updated on January 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Companies must ensure compliance with SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018, specifically regarding the submission of reconciliation of share capital audit reports.

## Important Dates

- Completion Date of Mandated Six Months: September 07, 2026
- Effective Date of Discontinuation: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading on the first trading day of every week in the specified securities will cease, affecting liquidity and market operations for these companies.
