## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of subscriptions in TATA GOLD ETF FUND OF FUND through lumpsum investments and switch-in transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Resumption applies to lumpsum investments (fresh and additional purchases) and Switch-In transactions.
- Previously restricted under Notice No. 20260608-36 dated June 08, 2026.
- Effective date of resumption is August 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational update based on communication from Tata Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to take note of the changes for executing transactions under the scheme without restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors can resume fresh and additional lumpsum investments as well as switch-in transactions in TATA GOLD ETF FUND OF FUND on the BSE StAR MF platform.
