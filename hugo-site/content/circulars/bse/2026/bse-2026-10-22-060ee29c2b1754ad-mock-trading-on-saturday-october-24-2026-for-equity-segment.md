## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Equity segment scheduled for Saturday, October 24, 2026. Trading members using third-party trading platforms and in-house systems can test their trading applications and various functionalities.

## Key Points

- Scheduled Date: Saturday, October 24, 2026.
- Segment: Equity.
- Covers testing of exceptional market conditions, call auction sessions, risk-reduction mode, trading halts, and block deals.
- No new BOLT TWS Release.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A (Routine operational mock trading session)

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are requested to actively participate and provide feedback by 03:00 PM on the mock trading day.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: October 24, 2026
- Feedback Submission Deadline: October 24, 2026, by 03:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact as this is a non-live testing session designed to ensure system robustness.
