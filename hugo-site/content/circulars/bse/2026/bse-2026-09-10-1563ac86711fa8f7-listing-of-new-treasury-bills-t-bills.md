## Summary

 BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under the G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS segment, effective from Friday, September 11, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three new Treasury Bills are being listed with different maturities.
- Scrip Code 805265 (ISIN: IN002026X230): 91TB101226 maturing on 10/12/2026.
- Scrip Code 805266 (ISIN: IN002026Y238): 182TB11327 maturing on 11/03/2027.
- Scrip Code 805267 (ISIN: IN002026Z235): 364TB09927 maturing on 09/09/2027.
- Market lot for all listed T-Bills is 1.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to note that the securities shall not be available for trading two (2) working days prior to the Maturity/Redemption Date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 11, 2026
- **Maturity Dates:** December 10, 2026; March 11, 2027; September 9, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations providing additional debt instruments for trading and investment.
