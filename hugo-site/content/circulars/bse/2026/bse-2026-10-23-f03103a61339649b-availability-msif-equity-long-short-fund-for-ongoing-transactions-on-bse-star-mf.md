## Summary

BSE has announced that the MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform with effect from October 26, 2026, pursuant to Notice No. 20260929-57 dated September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fund Name: MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund
- Effective Date: October 26, 2026
- Available Facilities: Subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP on BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Included ISINs:
  - Regular Plan - Growth: INF174V30019
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF174V30035
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout: INF174V30027
  - Direct Plan - Growth: INF174V30043
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF174V30068
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout: INF174V30050

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational availability notice for mutual fund transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the scheme through the BSE StAR MF Platform in accordance with existing operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date for Transactions: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute ongoing transactions, SIPs, STPs, and SWPs in various plans of the MSIF Equity Long-Short Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform seamlessly.
