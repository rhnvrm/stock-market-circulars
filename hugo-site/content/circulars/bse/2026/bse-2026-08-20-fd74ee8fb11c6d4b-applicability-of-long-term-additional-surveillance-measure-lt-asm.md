## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) issued a notice regarding the applicability, review, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective August 25, 2026, 100% margins will be applicable on open and new positions for securities in Annexure I (part A). Additionally, certain securities will be moved to higher stages, transferred to specific trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS), or moved out of the framework entirely.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. August 25, 2026, on all open and new positions for securities under Annexure I (Part A).
- Lower price bands applicable w.e.f. August 21, 2026, for securities moving to higher stages of LT-ASM.
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but transfer to T / XT / MT / TS groups w.e.f. August 25, 2026.
- Review and removal of securities from the LT-ASM framework effective August 21, 2026, as per Annexure II.
- Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance and XBRL submissions by listed companies, not as an adverse action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of 100% margins on open and new positions for shortlisted securities.
- Revision of price bands and transfer of securities to specialized trading groups (T, XT, MT, TS).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirements for applicable securities starting August 25, 2026.
- Members should refer to the annexures attached to the notice for the specific lists of securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 20 Aug 2026
- Effective Date for Price Bands & Movouts/Higher Stages: August 21, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins & Group Transfers: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements (100%) for affected securities will restrict leverage and liquidity.
- Price band restrictions and group transfers may impact trading volumes and price discovery for the short-listed scrips.
