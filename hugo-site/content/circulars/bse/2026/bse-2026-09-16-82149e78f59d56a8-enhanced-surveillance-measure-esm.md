## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the update to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 18, 2026, and will be shifted to the Trade-for-Trade segment. Specific securities are moving to lower stages or exiting the framework entirely based on periodical reviews.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM and will attract a minimum 100% margin on open and new positions.
- Affected securities will be shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction.
- Consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) involving higher margin requirements, movement to Trade-for-Trade settlement, and specific price band restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements and adjust trading parameters for securities listed under Annexure I and III.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade Shift: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage II restrictions and movement to lower stages: September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity in the shortlisted securities may be constrained due to 100% margin requirements and movement to the Trade-for-Trade settlement segment.
