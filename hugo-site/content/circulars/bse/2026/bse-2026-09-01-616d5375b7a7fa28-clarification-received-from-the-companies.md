## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in specific scrips. Clarifications have been received from several companies, while responses from others remain awaited.

## Key Points

- Exchange monitored significant price/volume movements in various scrips.
- Replies have been received and attached from 13 companies including AYM Syntex Ltd, Kopran Ltd, and Omaxe Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 12 companies including Aayush Wellness Ltd and Shah Alloys Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to clarify significant price/volume movements upon the Exchange's request to ensure investors have access to relevant information.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 01 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and protects investor interests by disclosing company responses or pending statuses regarding unusual market activity.
