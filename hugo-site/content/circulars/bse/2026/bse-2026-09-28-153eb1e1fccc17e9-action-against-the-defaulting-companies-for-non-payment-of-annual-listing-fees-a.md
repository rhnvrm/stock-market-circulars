## Summary

BSE has announced the revocation of suspension of trading in the equity shares of Geetanjali Credit and Capital Limited (Scrip Code: 539486) following compliance with the payment of outstanding Annual Listing Fees (ALF) under Regulation 14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Trading suspension for Geetanjali Credit and Capital Limited is revoked with effect from Thursday, October 01, 2026.
- Trading will be resumed from Group XT to Group X (Normal Segment).
- Action taken in continuation to the Exchange's previous notices dated March 03, 2020, and June 25, 2021.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure timely payment of Annual Listing Fees pursuant to Regulation 14 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 to prevent trading suspensions and regulatory actions.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 28, 2026
- **Effective Date of Revocation:** October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members can resume normal trading in the equity shares of Geetanjali Credit and Capital Limited starting October 01, 2026, as the scrip transitions back to the normal trading segment.
