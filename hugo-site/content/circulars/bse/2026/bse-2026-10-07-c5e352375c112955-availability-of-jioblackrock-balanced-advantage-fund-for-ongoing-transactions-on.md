## Summary

BSE has announced that the JioBlackRock Balanced Advantage Fund will be available for ongoing transactions, including subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- JioBlackRock Balanced Advantage Fund reopens for ongoing transactions.
- Facilities available include subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Applicable schemes include Regular Plan - Growth Equity (ISIN: INF22M001374) and Direct Plan - Growth Equity (ISIN: INF22M001366).
- Effective date for availability is October 8, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational circular regarding platform availability for mutual fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors can facilitate ongoing transactions for investors on the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 7, 2026
- Effective Date for Transactions: October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute ongoing transactions and systematic investment/withdrawal plans for the JioBlackRock Balanced Advantage Fund seamlessly through the BSE StAR MF platform.
