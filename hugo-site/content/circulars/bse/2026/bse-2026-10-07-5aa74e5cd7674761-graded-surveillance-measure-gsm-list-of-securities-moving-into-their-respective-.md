## Summary

BSE has announced the movement of certain securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective October 08, 2026. Trading members must take adequate precautions as settlements will be conducted on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Key Points

- Securities move to higher stages of GSM based on framework provisions.
- Effective date for higher stages: October 08, 2026.
- Settlements done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Surveillance actions vary by stage, including 100% margins, trade-for-trade restrictions, weekly trading limits, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the GSM framework across Stages I, II, III, and IV:
- **Stage I:** 100% applicable margin rate and price band of 5% or lower.
- **Stage II:** Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band and 50% Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) on trade value by buyers.
- **Stage III:** Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day), and 100% ASD by buyers.
- **Stage IV:** Trade for trade with 5% or lower price band, trading permitted once a week with no upward movement, and 100% ASD by buyers.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must exercise adequate caution while trading in these securities, account for trade-to-trade settlement without netting off, and ensure timely deposition of applicable margins and ASD.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 07, 2026
- **Effective Date:** October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading activity in affected securities may be restricted due to stringent margin requirements, trade-to-trade settlement, and mandatory ASD deposits.
