## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of Fischer Medical Ventures Limited (Scrip Code - 524743) effective from August 19, 2026, following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Security Type:** Equity shares issued on a preferential basis to promoters.
- **Quantity:** 60,00,000 equity shares of Re. 1/- each.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 23.40/- per share (including a premium of Rs. 22.40/-).
- **Effective Date:** August 19, 2026.
- **ISIN:** INE771F01041
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are to note the addition of these securities to the exchange trading system under the specified distribution numbers and lock-in parameters.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** 31/03/2026
- **Listing Date:** 19/08/2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** 27/02/2028 (for all 60,00,000 shares, Dist. Nos. 648515471 to 654515470)

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of Fischer Medical Ventures Limited through preferential warrant conversion with standard promoter lock-in requirements.
