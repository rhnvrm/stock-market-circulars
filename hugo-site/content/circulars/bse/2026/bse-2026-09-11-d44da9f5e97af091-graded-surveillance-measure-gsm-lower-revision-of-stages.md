## Summary

BSE has carried out a review of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework in coordination with other exchanges. Certain securities are eligible to move to lower stages of the GSM framework effective September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Review conducted in coordination with other exchanges under the GSM framework.
- Securities listed in the annexure will move to lower stages of surveillance.
- Effective date for the revision is September 15, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Movement of eligible securities to lower stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are advised to refer to the provided annexure for the list of securities moving to lower stages and take note of the revised surveillance requirements.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 15, 2026
- Notice Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reduced surveillance constraints for securities moving to lower stages, potentially normalizing trading conditions for the affected stocks.
