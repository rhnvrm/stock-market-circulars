## Summary

BSE has announced the transfer of equity shares of H. R. Hygiene Products Limited from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group).

## Key Points

- Company Name: H. R. Hygiene Products Limited
- Scrip Code: 544848
- Segment Shift: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Wednesday, August 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The trading category for the company's equity shares is being shifted, allowing normal rolling settlement instead of trade-for-trade.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members take note of the segment transfer effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 19, 2026
- Notice Date: August 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances liquidity and normalizes trading mechanics for the equity shares of H. R. Hygiene Products Limited.
