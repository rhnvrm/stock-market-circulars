## Summary

BSE has issued a settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for Duke Offshore Limited. The bidding window is scheduled to operate from August 6, 2026, to August 19, 2026, under settlement number 2026099.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Duke Offshore Limited
- **Settlement Type & No:** Takeover, Settlement No. 2026099
- **Bid Period:** August 6, 2026 to August 19, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** September 2, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 3, 2026
- **Delivery Mechanism:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adherence to revised guidelines for the acquisition of shares through stock exchanges as outlined in Notice No. 20200528-32.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must use Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026099 in the depository system.
- Early pay-in securities will be earmarked or blocked in clients' accounts in the requisite market type and settlement number.
- Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN files.
- Members should refer to the MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 06/08/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 19/08/2026
- **Obligation Download:** 02/09/2026
- **Settlement Date:** 03/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth processing, timely obligation downloads, and settlement of shares for the takeover offer of Duke Offshore Limited while maintaining compliance via early pay-in procedures.
