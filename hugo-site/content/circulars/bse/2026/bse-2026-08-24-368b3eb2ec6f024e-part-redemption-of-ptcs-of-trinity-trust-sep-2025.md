## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed about the reduced face value on partial redemption of Pass-through Certificates (PTCs) issued by TRINITY TRUST Sep 2025.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TRINITY TRUST SEP 2025
- Purpose: Part Redemption of PTCs
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 2851.78 per PTC
- Scrip Code: 977166
- ISIN: INE2JU715017
- Effective Date: July 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised that these PTCs will be traded with the above-mentioned reduced face value with effect from July 23, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 23, 2026
- Notice Date: July 22, 2026 / August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members must take note of the reduced face value for settlement and trading operations.
