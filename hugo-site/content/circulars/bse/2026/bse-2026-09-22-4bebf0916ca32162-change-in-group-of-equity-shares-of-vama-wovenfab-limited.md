## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Vama Wovenfab Limited will be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment effective Wednesday, October 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vama Wovenfab Limited
- Scrip Code: 544931
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: October 7, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares are moving out of the Trade for Trade restriction into regular rolling settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants should note the segment change for execution and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 22, 2026
- Effective Date: October 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Normalization of trading for Vama Wovenfab Limited shares under the rolling segment.
