## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Jattashankar Industries Ltd has fixed a record date for the sub-division of its equity shares. The existing equity shares of Rs. 10/- each are being sub-divided into ten equity shares of Re. 1/- each.

## Key Points

- Company: Jattashankar Industries Ltd
- Scrip Code: 514318
- Old Face Value: Rs. 10/- per share
- New Face Value: Re. 1/- per share (10 shares for every 1 existing share)
- Record Date: 19/10/2026
- Old ISIN (INE722N01014) ceases to be valid for transactions on or after 19/10/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Corporate action involving the sub-division of equity share capital.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure no transactions are executed using the old ISIN (INE722N01014) on or after 19/10/2026.
- The new ISIN for the Re. 1/- paid-up shares will be notified separately by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 09/10/2026
- Record Date: 19/10/2026
- Effective Date: 19/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders as of the record date will receive 10 shares of Re. 1/- each for every 1 share held, leading to a proportional adjustment in market price and liquidity.
