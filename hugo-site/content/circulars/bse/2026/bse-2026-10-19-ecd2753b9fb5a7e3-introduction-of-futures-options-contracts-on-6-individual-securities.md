## Summary

BSE Ltd has announced the introduction of futures and options (F&O) contracts on six individual securities in the Equity Derivatives segment, effective from October 30, 2026. The contract details will be available in the derivatives contract master file generated at the end of day on October 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective date for F&O trading availability: October 30, 2026.
- Contract master file availability: End of day on October 29, 2026 via extranet and BSE India website.
- Covers 6 underlying securities spanning agribusiness, banking, defense electronics, and specialty chemicals.

## Underlying Securities

1. **Coromandel International Ltd** (Scrip Code: 506395)
2. **City Union Bank Ltd** (Scrip Code: 532210)
3. **Data Patterns (India) Ltd** (Scrip Code: 543428)
4. **Karur Vysya Bank Ltd** (Scrip Code: 590003)
5. **Navin Fluorine International Ltd** (Scrip Code: 532504)
6. **Syrma SGS Technology Ltd** (Scrip Code: 543573)

## Regulatory Changes

Futures and options contracts for the specified underlying securities are made available for trading in the Equity Derivatives segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure their trading systems and terminals are updated with the derivatives contract master file released at the end of day on October 29, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Contract Master File Date:** October 29, 2026 (End of Day)
- **Effective Trading Date:** October 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances trading and hedging opportunities for market participants by expanding the derivative product suite to include six additional securities.
