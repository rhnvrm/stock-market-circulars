## Summary

BSE has released the trading and settlement schedule for the Corporate Debt Segment (ICDM T+0 and T+1) for the period from November 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines transaction dates, securities/fund pay-in dates, and securities/fund pay-out dates for ICDM T+0 and T+1 settlements.
- Covers settlement numbers ranging from 2627144 to 2627163 for November 2026.
- Includes special provisions such as Muhurat Trading on November 8, 2026 (Settlement No. 2627149 for T+1).

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine monthly operating schedule notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants active in the Corporate Debt Segment must adhere to the specified pay-in and pay-out schedules for ICDM T+0 and T+1.

## Important Dates

- Period of Schedule: 01.11.2026 to 30.11.2026
- First settlement date: 02-11-2026
- Last settlement date: 30-11-2026 (with pay-out extending to 01-12-2026 for T+1)
- Muhurat Trading: 08-11-2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement operations for participants trading in corporate debt instruments during November 2026.
