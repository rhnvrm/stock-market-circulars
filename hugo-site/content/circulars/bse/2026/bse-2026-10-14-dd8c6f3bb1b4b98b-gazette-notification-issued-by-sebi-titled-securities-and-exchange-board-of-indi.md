## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the Gazette Notification F. No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/323 dated October 08, 2026, issued by SEBI regarding the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026.

## Key Points

- Reference to SEBI Gazette Notification F. No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2026/323 dated October 08, 2026.
- Pertains to amendments under the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations.
- Trading members are advised to take note of the updated regulatory framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026 as notified by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are requested to review and take note of the regulatory amendments.
- For any clarifications, members can write to membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 14, 2026
- SEBI Gazette Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- General compliance and regulatory awareness update for all trading members operating on the exchange.
