## Summary

Trading members are informed that specific commercial papers and government securities are maturing pursuant to their redemption dates fixed by the companies/issuers. Trading in these securities is suspended effective from September 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code 805194 (182TB18926, ISIN: IN002025Y503) and Scrip Code 800496 (GS19SEP2026, ISIN: IN000926C047) are maturing.
- Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforementioned commercial papers and securities from September 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the specified commercial papers and securities starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2026
- Notice Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational measure reflecting the maturity and redemption of specific debt instruments.
