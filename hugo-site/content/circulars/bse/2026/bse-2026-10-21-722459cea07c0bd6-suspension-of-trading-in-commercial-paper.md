## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that specific companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of commercial papers. Members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of these entities from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- **Marwadi Shares and Finance Limited**: Scrip Code 731560 (ISIN: INE138I14032), Record & Ex Date: 22/10/2026.
- **Kotak Mahindra Prime Limited**: Scrip Code 732780 (ISIN: INE916D147L8), Record & Ex Date: 22/10/2026.
- **Dolat Algotech Limited**: Scrip Code 731576 (ISIN: INE966A14066), Record & Ex Date: 26/10/2026.
- **Tata Steel Limited**: Scrip Code 732769 (ISIN: INE081A14HS2, Ex Date: 27/10/2026) and Scrip Code 732206 (ISIN: INE081A14HI3, Ex Date: 29/10/2026).
- **Godrej Housing Finance Limited**: Scrip Code 732582 (ISIN: INE02JD14930), Record & Ex Date: 29/10/2026.
- **Godrej Finance Limited**: Scrip Code 732616 (ISIN: INE02KN14846), Record & Ex Date: 29/10/2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; standard corporate action processing for debt segment instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must refrain from dealing in the commercial papers of the listed companies on or after their respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 21 October 2026
- Record & Ex-Dates: 22, 26, 27, and 29 October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Low impact, restricted to participants holding or trading the specific commercial paper issuances undergoing redemption.
