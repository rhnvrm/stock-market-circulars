## Summary

BSE has scheduled a mock trading session in the Equity segment on Saturday, September 05, 2026. The session allows trading members to test their third-party trading applications, software, and risk management systems under simulated conditions, incorporating recent modifications in the Pre-open Call Auction Session.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, September 05, 2026, in the Equity segment.
- Incorporates modifications in the Pre-open Call Auction Session applicable for live trading starting Monday, September 07, 2026.
- Fulfils regulatory testing requirements in accordance with SEBI circular dated November 24, 2020, and Exchange Notice No. 20201201-22.
- Enables members using third-party trading platforms to test functionalities including block deals, trading halts, and risk-reduction modes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Testing and alignment with recent modifications in Pre-open Call Auction Session applicable from September 07, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members can participate in either the Mock Trading Session or UAT environment to fulfill regulatory testing requirements for software related to trading and risk management.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: Saturday, September 05, 2026
- Live Implementation Date for Pre-open Call Auction Modifications: Monday, September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness check for trading members and third-party platform providers with no direct impact on live market positions.
