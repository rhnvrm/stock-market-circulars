## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund is offering the UTI Annual Interval Fund - I during the Specified Transaction Period (STP) from September 07, 2026, to September 08, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Specified Transaction Period (STP): September 07, 2026, to September 08, 2026.
- Eligible transactions include Subscription and Redemption during normal business hours.
- Available plans include Regular and Direct plans covering IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment, and Growth options.
- MICR Cheque, Nodal, and OTM payment modes are not accepted during the STP.
- Clear funds must be received by the Exchange on the last date of the STP before 2:30 PM to avoid rejection.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members must ensure subscription and redemption requests are processed within the specified window and that clear funds reach the exchange prior to the 2:30 PM deadline on the final day of the STP.

## Important Dates

- STP Start Date: September 07, 2026
- STP End Date: September 08, 2026 (Cut-off time for funds: 2:30 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is limited to participating mutual fund distributors and investors transacting in UTI Annual Interval Fund - I during the specified window.
