## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The applicable margin requirements take effect from September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for ST-ASM Stage I will attract a margin rate of 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Securities under ST-ASM Stage II will attract a margin rate of 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher).
- Surveillance actions apply to all open positions as of September 3, 2026, and new positions created from September 4, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II frameworks with enhanced margin obligations in conjunction with prevailing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin collection (50% or 100%) for open and new positions on shortlisted securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 2, 2026
- **Effective Date:** September 4, 2026 (applicable on open positions as of September 3, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities under ST-ASM may lead to reduced leverage and heightened liquidity constraints for impacted scrips during the surveillance period.
