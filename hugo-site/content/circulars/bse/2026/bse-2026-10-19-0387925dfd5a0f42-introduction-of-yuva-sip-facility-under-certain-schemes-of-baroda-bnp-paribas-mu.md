## Summary

BSE StAR MF has announced the introduction of the YUVA SIP facility under select schemes of Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective October 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Facility**: YUVA SIP facility under Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund.
- **Eligible Schemes**: Baroda BNP Paribas Flexi Cap Fund and Baroda BNP Paribas Balanced Advantage Fund.
- **Investment Option**: Available exclusively under the Growth Option of the aforesaid schemes.
- **Investment Amount**: Minimum investment of Rs. 10/- and thereafter in multiples of Re. 1/-.
- **Tenure**: Minimum tenure of five years requiring a minimum of 1,300 instalments (early withdrawal subject to applicable exit loads).
- **Frequency**: Daily (on each Business Day).
- **Holding Mode**: Available for investments held in both Statement of Account (SOA) mode and Demat mode.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Members are advised to note the details of the YUVA SIP facility and ensure correct processing of transactions under the specified schemes.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors to participate in daily SIP investments with low ticket sizes (starting at Rs. 10) across select Baroda BNP Paribas mutual fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform.
