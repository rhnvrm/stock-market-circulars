## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) on certain securities based on XBRL submissions and market surveillance parameters. The measures include 100% margin requirements, transfer of securities to specific trading groups, and price band revisions effective August 11 and August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins are applicable effective August 13, 2026, on all open positions as of August 12, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities are moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework or transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups.
- Lower Price Band restrictions apply to specified securities effective August 11, 2026.
- Certain securities are moving out of the LT-ASM framework, and their price bands will be reinstated.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised LT-ASM framework requirements including margin hikes and trading segment adjustments as detailed in Annexures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements for open and new positions in securities under LT-ASM Stage I/II/etc. as specified.
- Members can direct clarifications to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for 100% Margins: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date for Higher Stages / Price Band / Group Transfers: August 11, 2026 and August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading activity in shortlisted securities may experience reduced liquidity due to 100% margin requirements and stricter price bands.
