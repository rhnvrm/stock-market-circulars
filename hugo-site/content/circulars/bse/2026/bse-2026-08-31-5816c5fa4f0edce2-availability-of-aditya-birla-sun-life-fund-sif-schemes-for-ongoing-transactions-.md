## Summary

Pursuant to Notice No. 20260807-42, BSE has announced that Aditya Birla Sun Life (SIF) schemes shall reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and SIP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform effective September 01, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes affected: Apex Equity Ex-Top 100 Long-Short Fund and Apex Equity Long-Short Fund (Equity category).
- Reopening date: September 01, 2026.
- Facilities available: Subscriptions, redemptions, and SIPs via BSE StAR MF.
- Redemption and switch-out restrictions: Accepted only twice a week (every Monday and Wednesday). If Monday or Wednesday is a non-business day, requests will be processed on the next business day.
- Redemption payouts: Released by AMC/RTA as per the payout TAT specified in the scheme master.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors must take note of the scheduled transaction availability and twice-weekly redemption/switch-out restrictions for the specified SIF schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to resume transactions and SIP investments in the specified Aditya Birla Sun Life SIF schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform, subject to specific weekday limits for redemptions and switches.
