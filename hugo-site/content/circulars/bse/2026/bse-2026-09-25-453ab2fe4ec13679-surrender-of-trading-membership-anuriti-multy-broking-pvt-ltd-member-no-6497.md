## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has notified trading members that SEBI has approved the surrender of trading membership of Anuriti Multy Broking Pvt. Ltd. (Member no. 6497).

## Key Points

- Member Name: Anuriti Multy Broking Pvt. Ltd.
- Member Registration/No: 6497
- Regulatory Approval: Approved by SEBI

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the surrender and ensure no further business dealings are undertaken through this membership ID.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 25 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is an administrative update regarding membership surrender and has no direct impact on general market operations or listed securities.
