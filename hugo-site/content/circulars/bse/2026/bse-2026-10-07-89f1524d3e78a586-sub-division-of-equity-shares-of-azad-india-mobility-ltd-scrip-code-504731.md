## Summary

Azad India Mobility Ltd has announced the sub-division of its equity shares. The existing equity share with a face value of Rs. 10/- each will be sub-divided into five equity shares with a face value of Rs. 2/- each, with the record date set as October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Azad India Mobility Ltd
- Scrip Code: 504731
- Corporate Action: Sub-Division of Equity Shares (Stock Split)
- Ratio: One equity share of Rs. 10/- each into five equity shares of Rs. 2/- each
- Record Date: October 23, 2026
- Old ISIN (INE566M01017) of Rs. 10/- paid-up will be invalid for transactions on or after October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a standard corporate action notice processed under rolling settlement segment rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised that transactions using the old ISIN (INE566M01017) will not be valid on or after October 23, 2026. The new ISIN for the Rs. 2/- paid-up shares will be notified separately by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Record Date / Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Existing shareholders will receive five shares of Rs. 2/- each for every single share of Rs. 10/- held as of the record date. The action enhances stock liquidity and affordability for retail investors.
