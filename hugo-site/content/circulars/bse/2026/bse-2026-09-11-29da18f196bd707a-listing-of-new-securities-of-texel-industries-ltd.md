## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for new equity shares of Texel Industries Ltd. with effect from September 15, 2026, following the conversion of warrants issued to promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Texel Industries Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 526638
- ISIN: INE594V01028
- Security Details: 7,84,312 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 28.25/-
- Effective Date of Trading: September 15, 2026
- Lock-in Details: Shares are under lock-in up to April 10, 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Standard compliance regarding the listing and lock-in of preferential shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 11, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: September 15, 2026
- Lock-in Upto: April 10, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action resulting in an increase in the paid-up equity capital of the company through warrant conversion.
