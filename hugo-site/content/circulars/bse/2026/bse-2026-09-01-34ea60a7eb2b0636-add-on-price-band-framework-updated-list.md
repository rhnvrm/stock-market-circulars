## Summary

BSE has released an updated list of securities placed under the Add-on Price Band Framework, following up on the exchange circular no. 20210811-6 dated August 11, 2021.

## Key Points

- Periodic review carried out for identification of securities under the Add-on Price Band Framework.
- Updated list of securities provided in Annexure I of the circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of price band restrictions as per the surveillance framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must verify the updated list in Annexure I for applicable securities and price band restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in securities specified in the annexure will be subject to the add-on price band limits to curb excessive volatility.
