## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Virtuoso Optoelectronics Limited with effect from September 2, 2026, following the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Virtuoso Optoelectronics Limited
- Scrip Code: 543597
- ISIN: INE0I0T01010
- Number of Securities: 7,32,600 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 445/- (Issue Price: Rs. 455/- per share)
- Effective Date of Trading: Wednesday, September 2, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 31833080 to 32565679
- Date of Allotment: 21/07/2026
- Lock-in Details: 7,32,600 shares locked-in upto 30/03/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the listing and locking-in details of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 21/07/2026
- Effective Trading Date: 02/09/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: 30/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

The listing expands the paid-up equity capital of the company through preferential allotment upon warrant conversion, with relevant lock-in restrictions applied as per regulatory norms.
