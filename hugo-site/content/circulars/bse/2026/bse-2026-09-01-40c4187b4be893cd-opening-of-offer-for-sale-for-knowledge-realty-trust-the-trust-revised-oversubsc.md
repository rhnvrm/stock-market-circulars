## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the Opening of Offer for Sale (OFS) for Knowledge Realty Trust, detailing the revised oversubscription option exercised by the sellers.

## Key Points

- Sellers have intimated the Stock Exchanges to exercise the Oversubscription Option for 37,00,00,000 Units (8.34% of the Unit capital).
- This is in addition to the 74,00,00,000 Units (16.69% of the Unit capital) forming part of the Base Offer Units.
- 11,10,00,002 Units (representing 10% in aggregate of the Base Offer Units and Oversubscription Option) are reserved for allocation to Retail Investors, subject to valid bids.

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to the revised allocation and oversubscription terms for the OFS.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 01, 2026
- Reference Dates: August 28, 2026 and August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Affects trading and allocation of units under the Offer for Sale for Knowledge Realty Trust.
