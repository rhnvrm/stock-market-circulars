## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction No. DR-732/2026-27, inviting offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction. The auction sessions and pay-in schedules are scheduled for October 12 and October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement No.: DR-732/2026-27.
- Auction Offer Entry: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on October 12, 2026.
- Auction Matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on October 12, 2026.
- Delivery Statement & Report: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m. on October 12, 2026.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m. on October 12, 2026.
- Auction Pay-in: Tuesday, October 13, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard demat auction operational notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings. Members must exercise utmost care while punching securities codes and rates as no corrections are permitted.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date / Offer Entry / Matching: October 12, 2026
- Auction Pay-in: October 13, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational process for clearing short-delivered securities in rolling settlement.
