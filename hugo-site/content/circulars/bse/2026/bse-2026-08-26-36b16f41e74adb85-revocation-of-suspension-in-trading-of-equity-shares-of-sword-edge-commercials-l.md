## Summary

BSE has announced the revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares of Sword-Edge Commercials Ltd (Scrip Code: 512359) effective September 03, 2026, pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026.

## Key Points

- Trading in equity shares of Sword-Edge Commercials Ltd will resume w.e.f. September 03, 2026.
- Securities will be resumed under the “XT” group.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) for IPOs & Relisted Scrips on September 03, 2026.
- Information Memorandum will be available on the Exchange's website under Corporates → Corporate Filings → Information Memorandum → Revocation.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to take note of the revocation and participation in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 26, 2026
- Effective Revocation Date / Relist Session: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables liquidity and trading resumption for shareholders of Sword-Edge Commercials Ltd under specialized surveillance group XT.
