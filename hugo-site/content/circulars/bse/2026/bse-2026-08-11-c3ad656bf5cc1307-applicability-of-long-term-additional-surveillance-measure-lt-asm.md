## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. As per the revised framework, specific surveillance measures, including 100% margins and transfer to specific trading groups, are imposed on listed securities based on XBRL submissions.

## Key Points

- 100% margins are applicable effective August 14, 2026, on open positions as of August 13, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework or subject to lower price bands effective August 12, 2026.
- Certain securities will continue in the LT-ASM framework but will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS groups effective August 14, 2026.
- Securities moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures (LT-ASM) based on the revised framework criteria.
- Application of 100% margin requirements and group transfers for identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure appropriate margin maintenance for securities listed under Annexure I (Part A).
- Members should note group transfers (T, XT, MT, TS) and adjust trading parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 11 Aug 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins: August 14, 2026
- Effective Date for Higher Stages / Price Bands / Movement Out: August 12, 2026
- Effective Date for Group Transfers: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for the shortlisted securities will be restricted, potentially increasing trading costs for market participants holding positions in these scrips.
