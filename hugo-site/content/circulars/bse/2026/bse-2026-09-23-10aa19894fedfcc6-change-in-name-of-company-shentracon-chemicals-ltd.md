## Summary

Shentracon Chemicals Ltd has informed the Exchange about a change in the name of the company to Midaas Fashions Limited. Consequently, the Scrip ID and Abbreviated Name on the BOLT Plus system will be updated.

## Key Points

- Existing Company Name: Shentracon Chemicals Ltd
- New Company Name: Midaas Fashions Limited
- Scrip Code: 530757
- New Scrip ID for BOLT Plus System: MIDAAS
- Abbreviated Name on BOLT Plus System: MIDAAS
- Effective Date: September 29, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must update their records to reflect the new name and scrip ID for scrip code 530757.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2026
- Effective Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact, limited to updating trading system nomenclature and internal records for the company.
