## Summary

Trading members are informed that new debt securities and commercial papers issued on a private placement basis have been listed and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are issued on a private placement basis.
- Trading takes place in dematerialised form under specific ISIN numbers.
- Standard market lot and denomination apply with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuers include Bajaj Finance Limited, DSP Finance Private Limited, InCred Financial Services Limited, Jio Credit Limited, and Axis Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions are conducted in dematerialised form using the prescribed ISINs and standard denominations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- Effective Date: July 31, 2026
- Allotment and Redemption dates vary by specific commercial paper issuance spanning July 2026 to July 2027.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice with minimal direct market impact beyond regular debt trading availability.
