## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-681/2026-27. The notice outlines the schedule for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and pay-in obligations for July 29 and July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction Rolling Settlement No: DR-681/2026-27 (Notice No. 20260729-5).
- Auction offer entry timings: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m. on July 29, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m. on July 29, 2026.
- Pay-in of the auction scheduled for Thursday, July 30, 2026, at 08.00 a.m.
- Restriction on trading members offering securities in auctions where they short-delivered has been removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of restrictions preventing trading members from offering securities in auction for settlements where they previously short-delivered those securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off times.
- Extreme care must be taken while entering security codes and rates, as punching errors cannot be corrected.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: July 29, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: July 30, 2026 (08.00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational process for clearing short deliveries and completing rolling settlement obligations on the exchange.
