## Summary

BSE has issued a surveillance notice (Notice No. 20260924-22) regarding significant price and volume movements in several scrips. The exchange sought clarifications from various companies to ensure investors have access to relevant and timely information.

## Key Points

- **Replies Received:** Clarifications have been submitted by 10 companies, including INDO-MIM Ltd, Intrasoft Technologies Ltd, PCS Technology Ltd, Premier Polyfilm Ltd, Protean eGov Technologies Ltd, Regaal Resources Ltd, RPG Life Sciences Ltd, Scoda Tubes Ltd, Trident Lifeline Ltd, and Suraj Estate Developers Ltd.
- **Replies Awaited:** Responses are still pending from 10 companies, including Billwin Industries Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Jhandewalas Foods Ltd, Mafia Trends Ltd, Manugraph India Ltd, Mopshop Distribution Ltd, S. M. Gold Ltd, Sailani Tours N Travels Ltd, and Vikram Aroma Ltd.
- **Department:** Surveillance (DOSS).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative surveillance disclosure enforcing transparency and timely disclosure by listed entities.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies experiencing abnormal price or volume movements are required to promptly clarify price-sensitive information to the exchange upon request.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and alerts investors regarding ongoing surveillance inquiries into scrips with unusual trading activity.
