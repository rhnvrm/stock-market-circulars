## Summary

BSE has issued a clarification regarding order settlement and processing on the BSE StAR MF platform for August 26, 2026, which is tentatively considered a banking holiday on account of “Id-E-Milad”. Subscription, redemption, and switch orders will be accepted with specific timelines for RTA/AMC forwarding and report date reflections.

## Key Points

- August 26, 2026, is tentatively a banking holiday due to Id-E-Milad.
- Subscription orders in liquid, debt, and equity schemes will be accepted on the BSE StAR MF platform on August 26, 2026.
- Liquid, overnight, and debt scheme subscriptions received on August 26 will be forwarded to RTA/AMC on the next working day (August 27, 2026), subject to funds confirmation.
- Equity scheme subscriptions will be reported to AMC registrars upon successful funds receipt/matching.
- Redemption and switch orders for equity, liquid, overnight, and debt schemes will be reported to respective RTA/AMCs on August 26, 2026.
- Redemption unit pay-in and pay-out for orders placed on August 26, 2026, shall be processed on the same day, but there will be no redemption funds pay-out on August 26, 2026.

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the adjusted order reporting dates, report date reflections, and fund settlement timelines for mutual fund transactions placed on August 26, 2026.

## Important Dates

- August 26, 2026: Banking holiday (Id-E-Milad) / Transaction placement date
- August 27, 2026: Forwarding date for liquid, overnight, and debt scheme subscriptions

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth processing and reporting of mutual fund orders on the BSE StAR MF platform despite the banking holiday on August 26, 2026.
