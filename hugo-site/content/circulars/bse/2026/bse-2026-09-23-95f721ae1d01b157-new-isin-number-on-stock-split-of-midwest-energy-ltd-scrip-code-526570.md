## Summary

Following the sub-division of equity shares, BSE has announced the allocation of a new ISIN number for Midwest Energy Ltd. The face value of the shares is changed from Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Midwest Energy Ltd
- Scrip Code: 526570
- New ISIN: INE519N01022
- Corporate Action: Sub-division of Equity Shares from Rs.10/- to Re. 1/-

## Regulatory Changes

- Assignment of a new ISIN number corresponding to the share split.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and depositories must update their records with the new ISIN for subsequent settlements and trades.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2026
- Ex-Date: September 25, 2026 (DR- 723/2026-2027)

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth trading and settlement operations under the new ISIN for the sub-divided equity shares.
