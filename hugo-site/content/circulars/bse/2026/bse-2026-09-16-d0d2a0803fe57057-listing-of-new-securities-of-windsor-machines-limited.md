## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Windsor Machines Limited (Scrip Code - 522029) effective from September 17, 2026, following the conversion of warrants issued to promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Windsor Machines Limited
- Scrip Code: 522029
- ISIN: INE052A01021
- Securities Issued: 17,32,405 equity shares of Rs. 2/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 189.85/-
- Issue Price: Rs. 191.85/-
- Distinctive Numbers: 101727013 to 103459417
- Allotment Date: 05/07/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: September 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing action for preferential warrant conversion.

## Compliance Requirements

Shares are subject to lock-in requirements up to 31/03/2028 as per regulatory guidelines for preferential issues.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 05/07/2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: 17/09/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 31/03/2028

## Impact Assessment

Minor increase in the paid-up equity capital of Windsor Machines Limited due to the conversion of warrants into equity shares by promoters.
