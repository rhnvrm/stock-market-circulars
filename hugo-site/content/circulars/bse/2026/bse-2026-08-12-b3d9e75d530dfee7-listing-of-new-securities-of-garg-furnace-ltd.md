## Summary

BSE has notified trading members that 18,00,000 new equity shares of Garg Furnace Ltd. (Scrip Code: 530615) are listed and permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Thursday, August 13, 2026. These shares were issued at an issue price of Rs. 195.00/- (Rs. 10/- face value + Rs. 185/- premium) to both Promoter and Non-Promoter groups on a preferential basis following the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Issuer:** Garg Furnace Ltd. (Scrip Code: 530615, ISIN: INE194E01015).
- **Listing & Trading Commencement Date:** Thursday, August 13, 2026.
- **Quantity of Shares:** 18,00,000 equity shares.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 195.00/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-, Premium: Rs. 185/-).
- **Allotment Date:** July 15, 2025.
- **Pari-Passu Status:** The newly listed shares rank pari-passu with the existing old equity shares of the company.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 5008701 to 6808700.
- **Lock-in Details:**
  - 12,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 5008701 to 6208700) are locked-in until February 20, 2028.
  - 6,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 6208701 to 6808700) are locked-in until February 21, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no direct regulatory changes introduced in this notice. It is a standard listing and trading approval notice following an existing allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

No general action or compliance requirements are mandated for standard investors or trading members. The issuer (Garg Furnace Ltd.) must adhere to BSE's listing regulations and ensure compliance with lock-in requirements for the specified shares.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** July 15, 2025
- **Effective Trading Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date (6,00,000 shares):** February 21, 2027
- **Lock-in Expiry Date (12,00,000 shares):** February 20, 2028

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a stock-specific listing notice. There will be no broader impact on the market indices or other securities.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Trading systems will enable trading for the new shares under Scrip Code 530615 starting August 13, 2026.
- **Liquidity Impact:** Increased liquidity for Garg Furnace Ltd. since an additional 18,00,000 shares (subject to the lock-in periods of the allotted portions) are now listed on the exchange.
