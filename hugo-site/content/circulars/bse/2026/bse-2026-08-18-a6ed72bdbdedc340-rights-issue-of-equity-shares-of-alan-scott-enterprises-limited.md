## Summary

Alan Scott Enterprises Limited has announced a rights issue of equity shares. The exchange has fixed the record date and ex-rights basis date as August 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name & Code:** Alan Scott Enterprises Limited (BSE Code: 539115)
- **Record Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Ex-Rights Basis Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Rights Ratio:** 1 (One) Equity Share for every 6 (Six) Equity Shares held.
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 75/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- each at a premium of Rs. 65/- per share).
- **Payment Structure:** Rs. 40/- per share payable on application and Rs. 35/- per share payable on first call.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the ex-rights date and ensure transactions in the equity shares of the company are conducted accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Ex-Rights Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Record Date:** August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders as of the record date are eligible for the rights issue entitlement. Market participants need to account for the price adjustment on the ex-rights date.
