## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealing of equity shares of Dudani Retail Limited on the SME segment effective from October 5, 2026, under the 'MT' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 5, 2026
- Scrip Code: 544961
- Scrip ID / Symbol: DUDANI
- ISIN: INE1DLN01012
- Total Securities: 1,03,90,000 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 29/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 19/-)
- Market Lot: 4,000 equity shares
- Trade-for-Trade Segment: Applicable for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI circular dated January 20, 2012
- Market Maker: Prabhat Financial Services Limited
- Registrar: Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory policy changes; standard SME IPO listing procedure and trade-for-trade surveillance placement for 10 trading days as per SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that trades are executed in dematerialized form only and adhere to the minimum market lot of 4,000 equity shares. Lock-in details and shareholding patterns are marked in the depository's records as per Annexure I and Annexure II.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): October 5, 2026
- Commencement of Trading: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and trading members to trade in the equity shares of Dudani Retail Limited on the BSE SME platform, with initial trade-for-trade surveillance measures.
