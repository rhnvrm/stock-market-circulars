## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, September 23, 2026, equity shares of Jindal Supreme (India) Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'T' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: JSIPL
- Scrip Code: 544935
- ISIN No.: INE1DLO01028
- Total Securities: 5,10,23,769 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 51023769).
- Issue Price: Rs. 93/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 83/-).
- Market Lot: 1 share.
- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted in the 'T' Group and subject to Trade-for-Trade segment restrictions for the first 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the demat-only trading requirement and participation in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS).
- Necessary corporate actions for lock-in marking have been executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 21, 2026
- Listing and Trading Commencement Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading and liquidity for equity shares of Jindal Supreme (India) Limited under standard IPO listing safeguards.
