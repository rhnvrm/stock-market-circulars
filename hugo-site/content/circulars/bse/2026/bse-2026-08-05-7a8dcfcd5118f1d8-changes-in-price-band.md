## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 18 scrips under the Equity segment, effective from August 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised for 18 different scrips.
- Revised price bands range from 2%, 5%, 10%, to 20% depending on the specific scrip.
- Changes become effective on August 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

The price limits within which stocks can fluctuate during the trading session have been updated by the Surveillance department.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance during order entry and execution.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05 Aug 2026
- Effective Date: 06 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity for the affected scrips will be constrained by the new percentage limits, preventing excessive price volatility.
