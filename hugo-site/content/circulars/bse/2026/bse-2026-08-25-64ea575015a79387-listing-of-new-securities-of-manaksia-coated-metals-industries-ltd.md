## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Manaksia Coated Metals & Industries Ltd (Scrip Code - 539046) effective from August 26, 2026. The issuance involves 8,00,000 equity shares resulting from the conversion of warrants issued to promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Manaksia Coated Metals & Industries Ltd
- Scrip Code: 539046
- ISIN: INE830Q01018
- Number of Shares: 8,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 65/- per share (including a premium of Rs. 64/-)
- Allottees: Promoters (preferential basis pursuant to conversion of warrants)
- Effective Date of Trading: August 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

New shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 30/06/2026
- Effective Date of Listing/Trading: 26/08/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 27/Feb/2028 for 8,00,000 shares (Distinctive Nos. 106334051 to 107134050)

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action representing equity expansion via warrant conversion for promoters, with standard lock-in requirements.
