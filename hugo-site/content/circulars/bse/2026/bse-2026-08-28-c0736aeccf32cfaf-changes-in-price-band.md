## Summary

 BSE has announced revisions to the price bands for several scrips under Notice No. 20260828-27. These changes modify the allowable daily price movement limits for the listed securities.

## Key Points

- Revised price bands apply to multiple equities including 7NR Retail Ltd, Bodal Chemicals Ltd, Kopran Ltd, and others.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is August 31, 2026.
- Price band percentages range from 2% to 20% depending on the specific scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of price band limits for listed scrips on the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure their trading systems reflect the updated limits to prevent order rejections.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Alters the maximum permissible intraday price volatility for the affected scrips, impacting algorithmic trading strategies and risk management parameters for market participants.
