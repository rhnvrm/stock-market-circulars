## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, August 25, 2026, the equity shares of Shankesh Jewellers Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'B' Group of Securities. The public issue comprised 147,031,420 equity shares at an issue price of Rs. 93/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shankesh Jewellers Limited
- Scrip Code: 544882
- ISIN No.: INE1WFC01025
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: SHANKESH
- Total Securities: 147,031,420 Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 93/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/- and premium of Rs. 88/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 21, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- The scrip will be a part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on August 25, 2026.
- Lock-in details and shareholding patterns are as per Annexure I and Annexure II of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 21, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of Shankesh Jewellers Limited equity shares on the BSE exchange, providing liquidity to investors.
