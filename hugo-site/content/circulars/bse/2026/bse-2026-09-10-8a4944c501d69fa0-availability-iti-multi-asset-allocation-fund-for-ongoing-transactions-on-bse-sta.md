## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants that the ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund has reopened for ongoing transactions, including subscriptions, redemptions, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund
- **Effective Date:** September 10, 2026
- **Available Facilities:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, SIP (Systematic Investment Plan), STP (Systematic Transfer Plan), and SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan).
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform

## Scheme Details & ISINs

- **ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Regular Plan - Growth:** INF00XX01DP2
- **ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout:** INF00XX01DQ0
- **ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment:** INF00XX01DR8
- **ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Direct Plan - Growth:** INF00XX01DS6
- **ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout:** INF00XX01DT4
- **ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment:** INF00XX01DU2

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and mutual fund distributors can process transactions for the aforementioned schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with existing exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless access for investors and members to execute transactions and systematic investment plans for the ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform.
