## Summary

BSE has announced a special Muhurat Trading session for the Request for Quote (RFQ) platform on NDS-RST on account of Diwali Laxmi Pujan, scheduled for Sunday, November 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Session Date:** Sunday, November 8, 2026.
- **Eligible Instruments:** Only Corporate Bonds (as defined under SEBI-Issue and Listing of Non-Convertible Securities Regulations).
- **Market Timings:** 02:05 PM to 03:00 PM and 03:00 PM to 03:30 PM (Confirmation, Modification, Cancellation).
- **Settlement Cycle:** T+1 Settlement.
- **Platform Availability:** NDS-RST other than the RFQ platform will not be available during the Diwali Muhurat trading session.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial modification to Exchange Notice No. – 20251223-12 dated December 23, 2025, regarding Trading Holidays for the Calendar Year 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- All trades executed during the Diwali Muhurat trading session shall result in settlement obligations.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Trading Session Date:** November 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants in the corporate bond segment can execute trades during the special trading window on NDS-RST RFQ.
