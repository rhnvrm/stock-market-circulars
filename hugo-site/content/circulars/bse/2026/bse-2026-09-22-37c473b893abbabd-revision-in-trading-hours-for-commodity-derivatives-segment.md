## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the revision of trading hours for the Commodity Derivatives segment, effective from November 02, 2026, to March 12, 2027, on account of the change in US daylight saving timings.

## Key Points

- Trading hours for the Commodity Derivatives segment are revised starting November 02, 2026.
- The revised timings remain in effect until March 12, 2027 (both days inclusive).
- Continuous trading for internationally referenceable Non-Agricultural Commodities is set from 09.00 AM to 11.55 PM.
- Trade modification timings are extended up to 00.10 AM.

## Regulatory Changes

Seasonal adjustment in trading hours to align with international market timings resulting from US daylight saving changes.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the revised timings and ensure operational readiness for the commodity derivatives segment during the specified period.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Start Date:** November 02, 2026
- **Effective End Date:** March 12, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to trading members and participants dealing in internationally referenceable non-agricultural commodity derivatives.
