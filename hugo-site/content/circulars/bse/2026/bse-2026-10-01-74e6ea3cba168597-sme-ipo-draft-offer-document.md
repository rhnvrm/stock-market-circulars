## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the filing of the Draft Offer Document by Gorantla Geosynthetics Limited on September 30, 2026. The document is hosted on the BSE SME website to invite public comments for a period of at least 21 days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Gorantla Geosynthetics Limited
- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Public Comment Period: 21 days from the date of the notice
- Submission Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

None. Compliance with Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations for SME IPO draft filing.

## Compliance Requirements

Public and stakeholders can review the Draft Offer Document on the BSE SME website and submit comments within 21 days to equity.ipo@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 30, 2026
- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Comments Deadline: 21 days from October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency and invites stakeholder feedback during the initial stage of Gorantla Geosynthetics Limited's SME IPO process.
