## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing market participants that SEBI has granted an extension of the timeline for enrolment with the Past Risk and Return Verification Agency ("PaRRVA"). The new deadline for enrolment is extended until September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Extension granted by SEBI vide Circular No. HO/38/14/(11)2026-MIRSD-POD1/I/18038/2026 dated August 03, 2026.
- Previous reference made to SEBI Circular dated April 29, 2026, and Exchange notice dated April 30, 2026.
- Enrolment deadline with PaRRVA is now extended to September 03, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Timeline for operationalisation and enrolment requirements regarding Past Risk and Return Verification Agency (PaRRVA) has been extended.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must take note of the extension and complete their enrolment with PaRRVA on or before the revised deadline.

## Important Dates

- **Original/Previous Deadline Reference:** April 29, 2026 / April 30, 2026
- **Extended Deadline:** September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides registered investment advisers additional time to comply with mandatory PaRRVA enrolment processes without immediate regulatory penalty.
