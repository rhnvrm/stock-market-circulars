## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, updating the list of securities satisfying criteria for inclusion, shifting stages, or moving out of the framework effective August 17/18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying for ESM shall attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 18, 2026, on all open positions as on August 17, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but are moved to lower Stage I w.e.f. August 17, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. August 18, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 17, 2026.
- Shortlisting is strictly on account of market surveillance and not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework, including 100% margins, trade-for-trade settlements, and price band restrictions for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the revised margin requirements and settlement segment shifts for affected securities and ensure compliance across all open and new positions.

## Important Dates

- **August 17, 2026:** Stage movements, price band changes, and periodic call auction implementation take effect.
- **August 18, 2026:** 100% margin requirement and shift to Trade-for-Trade segment take effect.

## Impact Assessment

Trading activity and liquidity in the affected securities may be impacted due to higher margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
