## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice requiring clarifications from several listed companies following observed significant movements in their share prices or trading volumes. Replies have been received from select companies, while others are still awaited.

## Key Points

- The Exchange monitors market activity to ensure investors have access to relevant and timely information.
- Replies have been received and attached from Shree Hari Chemicals Export Ltd and Deco Mica Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from 14 other companies including Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; executed under standard surveillance and disclosure frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to promptly clarify any material price or volume movements upon inquiry by the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 10 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures market transparency and helps investors make informed decisions by compelling timely disclosures from companies experiencing unusual market activity.
