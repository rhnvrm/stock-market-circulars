## Summary

BSE Clearing Ltd has issued a revised settlement schedule for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) of **RAMGOPAL POLYTEX LIMITED** (Notice No. 20261013-8). The bidding period was conducted from 25/09/2026 to 09/10/2026 under Settlement No. 2026116.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** RAMGOPAL POLYTEX LIMITED
- **Settlement Type & Number:** Takeover, Settlement No. 2026116
- **Bidding Period:** 25/09/2026 to 09/10/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download Date:** 14/10/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 15/10/2026
- **Share Delivery Mechanism:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026116.

## Regulatory Changes

No structural regulatory changes; this notice pertains solely to schedule modifications for an ongoing corporate takeover settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and Custodians must utilize Market Type "(Takeover)" and Settlement No. 2026116 in the depository system for early pay-in of shares.
- Members should check the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.
- Reference Notice No. 20200528-32 dated May 28, 2020, for settlement guidelines regarding share acquisition through Stock Exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** 25/09/2026
- **Bid End Date:** 09/10/2026
- **Revised Obligation Download:** 14/10/2026
- **Revised Settlement Date:** 15/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational timeline update for clearing members and custodians participating in the takeover offer for Ramgopal Polytex Limited, ensuring seamless settlement execution.
