## Summary

Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited has announced a buyback of its fully paid-up equity shares of face value Rs. 2/- each through the open market via stock exchanges, in accordance with SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018. The maximum buyback price is set at Rs. 500/- per share for an aggregate amount not exceeding Rs. 697,000,000/-.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Advanced Enzyme Technologies Limited
- Maximum Buyback Price: Rs. 500/- per equity share
- Maximum Buyback Size: Rs. 697,000,000/-
- Opening Date: August 14, 2026
- Last Date for Buyback: Earlier of November 20, 2026 (66 working days), deployment of maximum buyback size, or earlier closure by the Board.
- Company's Broker: Emkay Global Financial Services Limited (Clearing No. 185)
- Registrar: MUFG Intime India Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI (Buy-Back of Securities) Regulations, 2018 updated as of July 6, 2026, and Exchange Notice No. 20260731-9 dated July 31, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and the company must adhere to the stipulated timelines, payment obligations, and regulatory disclosure norms set forth under the SEBI Buyback regulations and exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 13, 2026
- Buyback Opening Date: August 14, 2026
- Last Date for Buyback: November 20, 2026 (or earlier upon completion/closure)

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit or liquidity opportunity for existing shareholders through the open market buyback route up to the specified financial limits.
