## Summary

Apollo Micro Systems Limited has announced an open offer to acquire up to 1,39,77,911 equity shares (representing 26.00% of the equity share capital) of Premier Explosives Limited at an offer price of INR 698/- per equity share along with applicable interest of INR 7.65, aggregating to INR 705.65 per share in cash. The acquisition window (takeover) opens on Monday, October 12, 2026, and closes on Monday, October 26, 2026 (excluding the SEBI holiday on Tuesday, October 20, 2026).

## Key Points

- **Acquirer:** Apollo Micro Systems Limited
- **Target Company:** Premier Explosives Limited
- **Offer Price:** INR 698.00 plus applicable interest of INR 7.65, totaling INR 705.65 per equity share payable in cash.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 1,39,77,911 equity shares representing 26.00% of the voting share capital of the target company.
- **Offer Period:** October 12, 2026, to October 26, 2026 (excluding October 20, 2026).
- **Designated Stock Exchange:** BSE Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with Regulation 18(11) and 18(11A), Regulation 3(1), and Regulation 4 of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 (SEBI SAST Regulations) and revised guidelines issued via SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must participate via the acquisition window on the designated stock exchange (BSE) in accordance with the Revised Guidelines of SEBI circulars (CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 and CFD/DCR2/CIR/P/2016/131).

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** October 12, 2026
- **SEBI Holiday (No Trading):** October 20, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Public shareholders of Premier Explosives Limited have the opportunity to tender their shares during the acquisition window at the specified offer price of INR 705.65 per share, providing significant liquidity and exit options.
