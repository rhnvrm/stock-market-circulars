## Summary

BSE has issued a notice reminding all active trading members to report cyber incidents for the quarter ending September 30, 2026, on or before October 15, 2026, through the member portal. Members registered with both BSE and NSE should submit their reports exclusively to NSE per technology-based sharing mechanisms.

## Key Points

- Reporting Period: Quarter ending September 30, 2026.
- Deadline: On or before October 15, 2026.
- Submission Portal: https://membersso.bseindia.com -> BEFS -> Cyber Incident Report -> Quarterly Incident Reporting.
- Dual-registered Members: Members registered with both BSE and NSE must submit their reports to NSE only. Exclusive BSE members use the BEFS portal.
- Disciplinary Action: Non-submission or delayed submission will attract disciplinary action as per Exchange Notice no. 20260417-45.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reinforces compliance with SEBI circular no. SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_CSC_EXT/P/CIR/2024/113 and Exchange circular no. 20240822-13.
- Implements technology-based sharing mechanism between exchanges (Exchange Notice No. 20250929-81).

## Compliance Requirements

- Active Trading Members must submit the Quarterly Cyber Incident Report via the BEFS portal (or NSE for dual-registered members) by October 15, 2026.
- Immediate cyber incident reporting must follow Exchange circular no. 20250108-42.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026.
- Submission Deadline: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational requirement for all active trading members to ensure timely cybersecurity reporting and avoid regulatory penalties or disciplinary actions.
