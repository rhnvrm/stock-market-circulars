## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) issued a notice regarding SEBI directions on Profit Your Trade.In (Proprietor: Mr. Patibandla Venkata Vasanta Kumar) concerning unregistered investment advisory activities. SEBI confirmed that the full amount was deposited on September 18, 2024, and the mandated one-year debarment period has elapsed.

## Key Points

- SEBI verified full payment deposition by the noticee on September 18, 2024.
- The one-year debarment period from accessing the securities market has officially elapsed.
- Trading members are advised to take note of the completion of the order terms.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; this is an administrative update confirming compliance with an existing SEBI order.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members should take note of the completion of the debarment period and ensure compliance records are updated.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 30, 2026
- Deposit Date: September 18, 2024

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market or operational impact as this relates to the fulfillment of previous enforcement actions against an individual entity.
