## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are advised to participate and test their disaster recovery (DR) connection parameters and web-based applications.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026.
- Focuses on the Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segment and associated Disaster Recovery (DR) site connection parameters.
- Outlines connection parameters for BOLT TWS and 3rd party trading applications via ETI APIs (Primary DR IP: 10.255.241.26, Port: 14910; Secondary DR IP: 10.255.241.27, Port: 14910).
- Provides specific URLs and IP addresses for exchange web-based systems like RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, and Extranet during DR testing.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine operational mock trading announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members participating in the EGR segment should verify their connectivity settings against the provided Disaster Recovery (DR) parameters.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational readiness check for trading members connecting to BSE systems during disaster recovery scenarios.
