## Summary

Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund has announced the reopening of the Motilal Oswal BSE Midcap 150 Momentum 30 Index Fund for ongoing transactions, including subscription, redemption, SIP, STP, and SWP facilities, on the BSE StAR MF platform effective July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme reopens for ongoing subscription and redemption from July 30, 2026.
- Facilities available include SIP, STP, and SWP.
- Applicable ISINs:
  - Direct Plan - Growth: INF247L01HE2
  - Regular Plan - Growth: INF247L01HF9

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and mutual fund distributors must note the availability of the scheme for ongoing transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to execute ongoing transactions and systematic investment plans for the specified index fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform.
