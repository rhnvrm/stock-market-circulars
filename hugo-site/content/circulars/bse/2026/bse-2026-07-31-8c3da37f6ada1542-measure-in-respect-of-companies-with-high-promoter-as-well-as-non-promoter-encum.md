## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding companies with high promoter and non-promoter 'encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011. Securities included in Annexure I will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective August 5, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I satisfy the inclusion criteria and will attract a minimum 75% margin.
- Securities listed in Annexure II are eligible to move out of the framework effective August 3, 2026.
- Annexure III provides a consolidated list of securities under the framework.
- Measures apply in conjunction with all other prevailing exchange measures and are subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of a minimum 75% margin on equity and equity derivatives for short-listed securities under the high encumbrance framework in accordance with SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure the application of a minimum 75% margin on all open positions as of August 4, 2026, and new positions created from August 5, 2026, for securities specified in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 31, 2026
- **Removal Effective Date (Annexure II):** August 3, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** August 4, 2026
- **Margin Effective Date (Annexure I):** August 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements on impacted securities may affect trading liquidity and positions in the equity and equity derivatives segments for market participants.
