## Summary

Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd. (ICCL) has issued the settlement schedule and guidelines for the Offer to Buy – Acquisition Window (Takeover) for JAI MATA GLASS LIMITED. The bidding window is scheduled to run from August 21, 2026, to September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Bidding Window:** August 21, 2026, to September 4, 2026.
- **Settlement Type & Number:** Takeover, Settlement No. 2026105.
- **Market Type & Depository Instructions:** Demat shares must be delivered exclusively via the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism using the Market Type '(Takeover)' and Settlement No. 2026105.
- **CDSL & NSDL Mechanism:** Securities will be earmarked/blocked in the client's account in the requisite market type and settlement number.
- **EPN File:** Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The process follows existing revised guidelines for the acquisition of shares through stock exchanges (referencing Notice No. 20200528-32).

## Compliance Requirements

- Participants must ensure early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/ICCL systems prior to bid closure timelines.
- Members must verify the `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise stamp duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Bid End Date:** September 4, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** September 17, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement execution and operational clarity for members and custodians participating in the takeover acquisition window for Jai Mata Glass Limited.
