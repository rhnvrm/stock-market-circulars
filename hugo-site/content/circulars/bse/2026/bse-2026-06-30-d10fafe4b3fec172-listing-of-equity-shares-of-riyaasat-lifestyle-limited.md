## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, July 01, 2026, the equity shares of Riyaasat Lifestyle Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: Riyaasat Lifestyle Limited
- Scrip Code: 544804
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: RIYAASAT
- ISIN No.: INE0KYI01012
- Market Lot: 1200 Equity Shares
- Issue Price: Rs. 106/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 96/-)
- Date of Allotment: June 29, 2026
- Number of Securities: 10,744,478 Equity Shares

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on July 01, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading in the scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days.
- Lock-in period marked in depository records as per company confirmation.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: July 01, 2026
- Date of Allotment: June 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants liquidity and trading access to Riyaasat Lifestyle Limited shares on the SME platform.
