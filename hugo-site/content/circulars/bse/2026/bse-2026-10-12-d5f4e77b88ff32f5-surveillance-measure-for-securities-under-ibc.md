## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the application of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective October 13, 2026, specific securities meeting the criteria are placed under or removed from the framework, with corresponding price band adjustments.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the IBC surveillance criteria are added or moved out effective October 13, 2026.
- Annexure I contains securities newly placed under the framework, Annexure II lists securities moving out, Annexure III provides the consolidated list, and Annexure IV lists securities classified under IBC post recommencement due to Non-Promoter Holding (NPH) below 25%.
- The shortlisting is purely a market surveillance measure and does not constitute adverse regulatory action against the companies.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework are reinstated to their pre-shortlisting bands, unless other surveillance measures apply.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of surveillance measures and price band restrictions for securities undergoing insolvency resolution.
- Reinstatement of original price bands for securities exiting the framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to the surveillance requirements and monitor operational changes for securities listed in the annexures.
- Queries can be addressed to bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 12, 2026
- Effective Date: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading participants holding affected securities will be subject to applicable surveillance monitoring and price band adjustments.
