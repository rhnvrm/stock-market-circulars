## Summary

Effective from Friday, October 9, 2026, the equity shares of TNA Solutions Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE in the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544983
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: TNA
- ISIN: INE10VL01011
- Market Lot: 2,000 Equity Shares
- Total Securities: 20,408,000 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 70/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- & premium of Rs. 60/-)
- Date of Allotment: October 7, 2026
- Market Maker: Pace Stock Broking Services Private Limited
- Registrar: Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 9, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action executed for lock-in period marking in depository records.
- Minimum market lot for trading is set at 2,000 shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 7, 2026
- Listing & Trading Effective Date: October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members and investors can actively trade in the SME IPO shares of TNA Solutions Limited starting October 9, 2026, subject to the initial 10-day trade-for-trade surveillance restriction.
