## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-734/2026-27. The notice outlines session timings for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, money statements, and pay-in schedules.

## Key Points

- Auction Offer Entry Timings: October 14, 2026, from 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m.
- Auction Matching Timings: October 14, 2026, from 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m.
- Delivery Statement & Report Timings: October 14, 2026, from 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: October 14, 2026, from 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Auction Pay-in: Thursday, October 15, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.
- Restriction on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they short delivered has been removed following the implementation of the Uniform Membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; follows standard settlement and auction operational procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations for securities and execute pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must take utmost care while punching securities code and rate; no corrections will be permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members should verify cut-off rates on the Auction Offer Entry Screen and account for corporate actions (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 14, 2026
- Auction Pay-in Date: October 15, 2026 at 08:00 a.m.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notice ensuring smooth clearing and settlement of short-delivered securities for trading members.
