## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the SAT order dated July 03, 2026, which stayed the impugned order against the appellants subject to the deposit of a 100% penalty amount. SEBI confirmed that Benzer Department Stores Private Limited has paid the penalty amount of Rs. 31,00,000 in favor of SEBI.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous Exchange Notice No. 20260427-33 dated April 27, 2026.
- SAT stayed the impugned order qua appellants subject to 100% penalty deposit within four weeks.
- SEBI verified via email dated July 29, 2026, that Benzer Department Stores Private Limited paid the Rs. 31,00,000 penalty.
