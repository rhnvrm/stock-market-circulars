## Summary

The Exchange has received communication from ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund regarding the discontinuation of fresh subscriptions in designated schemes through Lumpsum (including Switches), Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP–In) effective from July 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Discontinuation applies to fresh subscriptions and registrations for Lumpsum, Switches, SIP, and STP-In effective July 31, 2026.
- Affected schemes include:
  - ICICI PRUDENTIAL RETIREMENT FUND PURE EQUITY PLAN
  - ICICI PRUDENTIAL RETIREMENT FUND HYBRID AGGRESSIVE PLAN
  - ICICI PRUDENTIAL RETIREMENT FUND HYBRID CONSERVATIVE PLAN
  - ICICI PRUDENTIAL RETIREMENT FUND PURE DEBT PLAN
- Existing SIP/XSIP and STP-In registrations prior to the effective date will continue to be processed without change.
- Redemption, Switch-out, STP-out, and SWP transactions continue to be permitted without restrictions.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors must note that fresh registrations through Lumpsum, SIP, and STP-In were not accepted after the cut-off time on July 30, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 30 Jul 2026
- Effective Date for Discontinuation: 31 Jul 2026
- Cut-off Time for Last Registrations: 30 Jul 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding or wishing to invest in the specified ICICI Prudential Retirement Fund schemes can no longer register new systematic plans or make fresh lumpsum investments, while existing periodic instructions and exit options remain unaffected.
