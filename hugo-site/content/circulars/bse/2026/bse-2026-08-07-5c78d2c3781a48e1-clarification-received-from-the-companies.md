## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has sought clarification from multiple listed companies following observed significant price or volume movements in their scrips. Replies received from several companies have been attached, while responses from others are still awaited.

## Key Points

- **Notice Reference:** 20260807-25
- **Department:** Surveillance (DOSS)
- **Segment:** Equity
- **Companies with Replies Received:** Amit Securities Ltd, Apollo Techno Industries Ltd, Assam Entrade Ltd, Associated Coaters Ltd, Authum Investment & Infrastructure Ltd, Dc Infotech and Communication Ltd, PVP Ventures Ltd, Sobhagya Mercantile Ltd, Tata Technologies Ltd, and Walchand Peoplefirst Ltd.
- **Companies with Replies Awaited:** Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Crazy Snacks Ltd, Deco Mica Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Gconnect Logitech and Supply Chain Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Medico Remedies Ltd, Nexxus Petro Industries Ltd, RSD Finance Ltd, and Sujala Trading & Holdings Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory rule changes; routine surveillance action under exchange guidelines to ensure dissemination of relevant information to investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies experiencing significant price/volume variations are required to clarify and provide all relevant information to the exchange to maintain market integrity.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 07 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and ensures investors have access to timely disclosures regarding anomalous stock price and volume behavior.
