## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective from August 05, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- For ST-ASM Stage I securities, the applicable rate of margin is 50% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- For ST-ASM Stage II securities, the applicable rate of margin is 100% or the existing margin, whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- Surveillance actions apply to all open positions as on August 04, 2026, and new positions created from August 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of revised ST-ASM framework surveillance actions in conjunction with prevailing exchange measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100% depending on the stage) for all open and new positions in the shortlisted securities effective August 05, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 03, 2026
- Effective Date: August 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for shortlisted securities may impact trading liquidity and leverage for market participants holding positions in these scrips.
