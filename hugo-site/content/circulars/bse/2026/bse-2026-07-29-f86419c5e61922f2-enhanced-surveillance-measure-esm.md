## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the update on the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin effective July 31, 2026, and will be shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment. Specific securities are moved to lower stages or moved out of the framework entirely.

## Key Points

- Securities qualifying under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. July 31, 2026, on all open positions as on July 30, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Eligible securities are shifted from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. July 31, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II face a price band of 2% with Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. July 30, 2026.
- Shortlisting is strictly a market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance criteria including 100% margins and migration to the Trade-for-Trade segment for identified scrips.
- Price band restrictions and periodic call auction implementation for specific stages.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure appropriate margin collection (100%) for open and new positions on shortlisted securities.
- Trading participants must update systems for trade-for-trade settlement and applicable price bands.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for margins and trade-for-trade shift: July 31, 2026.
- Effective Date for stage modifications and price band changes: July 30, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for securities listed under the ESM framework may experience constraints due to 100% margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions.
