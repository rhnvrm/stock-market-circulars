## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Vishal Nirmiti Limited (Scrip Code: 544973) will be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (T Group) to the Rolling segment under B Group, effective October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vishal Nirmiti Limited
- Scrip Code: 544973
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (T Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (B Group)
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Equity shares are transferred from the restricted Trade for Trade segment to the regular Rolling segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the change in segment and update their systems accordingly for settlement.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The relocation to B Group eliminates Trade for Trade restrictions, improving liquidity and normal market participation for investors.
