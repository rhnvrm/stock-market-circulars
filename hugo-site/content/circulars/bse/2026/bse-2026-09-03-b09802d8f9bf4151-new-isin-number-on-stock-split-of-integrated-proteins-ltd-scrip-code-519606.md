## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the allotment of a new ISIN number for the equity shares of Integrated Proteins Ltd subsequent to its stock split/sub-division.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Integrated Proteins Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 519606
- **Old ISIN:** INE177M01021
- **Corporate Action:** Sub-division of equity shares from face value of Rs. 10/- to Re. 1/-
- **Ex-Date:** September 4, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Not applicable.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are informed to take note of the new ISIN for trades executed on and from the Ex-Date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 3, 2026
- **Ex-Date:** September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement and operational transition under the new ISIN following the stock split.
