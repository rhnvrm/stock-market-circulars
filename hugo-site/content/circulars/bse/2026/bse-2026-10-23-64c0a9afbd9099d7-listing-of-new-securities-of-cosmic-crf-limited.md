## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 18,600 new equity shares of Cosmic CRF Limited with effect from October 26, 2026, issued on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 543928
- ISIN: INE0ORA01015
- Number of shares: 18,600 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 1,604/- (Issue Price: Rs. 1,614/-)
- Allotment Date: March 26, 2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 9187644 to 9206243
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to May 1, 2028

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and trading permissions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: March 26, 2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: October 26, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry Date: May 1, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up equity capital base of Cosmic CRF Limited following warrant conversion.
