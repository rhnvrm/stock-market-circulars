## Summary

 BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of units of UTI BSE India Sector Leaders Exchange Traded Fund under UTI Mutual Fund, effective from Friday, September 18, 2026. The scheme replicates or tracks the BSE India Sector Leaders Total Return Index (TRI).

## Key Points

- Name of the Mutual Fund: UTI Mutual Fund
- Scrip Code: 544927
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: SECLEDBETA
- ISIN: INF789F1AH42
- Issue Price: Rs. 14.15 per unit (Face Value: Rs. 10.00 per unit)
- Date of Allotment: 15/09/2026
- Group: B (Market Lot: 1 Unit)
- Registrar & Transfer Agent: KFIN Technologies Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard listing procedure pursuant to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Units of the Mutual Fund will only be traded in dematerialized form as per SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 15, 2026
- Commencement of Trading: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides investors with an additional investment option in an exchange-traded fund tracking the BSE India Sector Leaders TRI index on the BSE platform.
