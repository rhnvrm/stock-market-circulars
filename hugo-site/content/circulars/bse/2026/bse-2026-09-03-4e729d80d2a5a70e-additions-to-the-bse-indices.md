## Summary

BSE has issued a notice announcing the addition of newly listed companies to its indices. Lumino Industries Limited is added to the BSE IPO index effective September 7, 2026, and Kwick Forensic Solutions Limited is added to the BSE SME IPO index effective September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Lumino Industries Limited (Ticker: 544894) added to the BSE IPO index.
- Kwick Forensic Solutions Limited (Ticker: 544895) added to the BSE SME IPO index.
- Actions included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Lumino Industries Limited: September 07, 2026
- Effective Date for Kwick Forensic Solutions Limited: September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard index inclusion for newly listed entities with minimal direct market impact beyond index tracking.
