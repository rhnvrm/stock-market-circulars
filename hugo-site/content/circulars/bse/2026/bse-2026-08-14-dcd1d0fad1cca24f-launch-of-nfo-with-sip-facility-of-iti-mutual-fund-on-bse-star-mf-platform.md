## Summary

ITI Mutual Fund is launching the ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund NFO (Regular and Direct plans, Growth and IDCW options) with an SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription window runs from August 17, 2026, to August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund (Regular and Direct Plans - Growth, IDCW Payout, IDCW Reinvestment).
- NFO Window: August 17, 2026, to August 31, 2026 (extended till midnight on August 31, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 07, 2026.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 04, 2026.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients by 3:00 PM on August 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled with a 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds for NFO subscriptions must reach the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 04, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Open Date: August 17, 2026
- NFO Close Date: August 31, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline / Clear Funds by: September 04, 2026 (6:00 PM)
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides members and investors with procedural guidelines for participating in the ITI Multi Asset Allocation Fund NFO via the BSE StAR MF platform.
