## Summary

Trading members of the BSE are informed that new commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by various companies have been listed and admitted to trading on the BSE Debt Segment effective August 24, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are traded only in dematerialised form under specified ISIN numbers.
- Standard trading denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof.
- Tick size for the securities is set at 1 paise.
- Listed entities include 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Sharekhan Limited, Muthoot Fincorp Limited, and CNH Industrial Capital (India) Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with dematerialised trading formats and standard denominations for debt market transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 24, 2026
- Allotment Dates: August 21, 2026 / August 24, 2026
- Redemption Dates: November 2026 to February 2027 depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update providing secondary market liquidity and trading availability for newly issued commercial papers.
