## Summary

UTI Mutual Fund is offering the UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund NFO along with a SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform from August 14, 2026, to August 28, 2026. The circular details four schemes under the regular and direct plans for growth and IDCW payout, specifying tentative allotment dates and processing instructions for members.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: UTI Balanced Hybrid Fund (Regular and Direct Plans for Growth and IDCW Payout).
- NFO Period: August 14, 2026, to August 28, 2026 (Extended till midnight on August 28, 2026, for lumpsum purchases).
- SIP / XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on August 28, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 02, 2026.
- ISINs: INF789F1AH00 (Regular Growth), INF789F1AH26 (Regular IDCW Payout), INF789F1AH18 (Direct Growth), INF789F1AH34 (Direct IDCW Payout).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions without client-initiated funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to the RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on September 02, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: August 14, 2026
- NFO Closure Date: August 28, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides seamless access for members and investors to subscribe to the new hybrid fund scheme via the BSE StAR MF platform with standard operational timelines.
