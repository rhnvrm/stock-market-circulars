## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Raconteur Global Resources Limited with effect from October 8, 2026, following a preferential allotment upon warrant conversion.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Raconteur Global Resources Limited (Scrip Code: 541703)
- **Security Details:** 24,99,999 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 4/- (Issue Price: Rs. 14/- per share).
- **Basis of Allotment:** Issued to Non-Promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to the conversion of warrants.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 10777845 to 13277843
- **Date of Allotment:** 02/07/2026
- **ISIN:** INE00WY01013
- **Effective Date:** Thursday, October 8, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the new securities and their lock-in status.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** July 2, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** May 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine expansion of paid-up equity capital through warrant conversion. The shares are ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares, subject to applicable lock-in restrictions.
