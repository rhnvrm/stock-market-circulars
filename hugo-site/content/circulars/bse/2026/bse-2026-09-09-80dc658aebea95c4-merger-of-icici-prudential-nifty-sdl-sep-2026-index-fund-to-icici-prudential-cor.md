## Summary

ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund has announced the merger of the ICICI Prudential Nifty SDL Sep 2026 Index Fund (Transferor Scheme) into the ICICI Prudential Corporate Bond Fund (Transferee Scheme). The merger takes effect after business hours on September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Effective date of merger: September 30, 2026 (after close of business hours).
- Exit load waiver period: Investors wishing to exit can redeem or switch without exit load from August 28, 2026, to September 30, 2026.
- Transferor scheme transaction timelines on BSE StAR MF Platform:
  - Demat mode: Up to September 24, 2026.
  - Non-Demat mode: Up to September 30, 2026.
- Systematic registrations (SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP) will be automatically migrated to the corresponding plan/option of the Transferee scheme after September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and investors must take note of the transaction cut-off dates and timelines for the transferor and transferee schemes.
- Investors wishing to cancel existing SIP/XSIP/STP/SWP facilities rather than migrating must submit cancellation requests within AMC-prescribed timelines.

## Important Dates

- Exit Load Free Window: August 28, 2026 to September 30, 2026
- Demat Transaction Cut-off: September 24, 2026
- Non-Demat Transaction Cut-off: September 30, 2026
- Merger Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Unitholders of the transferor scheme are provided a seamless exit window without exit load or automatic migration into the transferee scheme, ensuring investor choice and continuity of investments.
