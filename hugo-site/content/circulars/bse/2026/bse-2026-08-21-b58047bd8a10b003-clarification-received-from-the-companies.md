## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in certain scrips. Clarifications have been received from several companies, while replies from other listed entities are currently awaited.

## Key Points

- Companies that submitted replies regarding price/volume movements include Hubtown Ltd, Mangalam Cement Ltd, MMTC Ltd, Mysore Petro Chemicals Ltd, Network 18 Media & Investments Ltd, Prabha Energy Ltd, Premier Polyfilm Ltd, Sugs Lloyd Ltd, and Valencia Nutrition Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from Advance Petrochemicals Ltd, Apt Packaging Ltd, Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, Gconnect Logitech and Supply Chain Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, PVP Ventures Ltd, Shree Balaji (Mala) Textiles Ltd, and Triumph International Finance India Ltd.
- The exchange seeks these clarifications to ensure that trading members and investors have access to the latest relevant information.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies experiencing significant price or volume movements are required to clarify the reasons behind such movements to the exchange upon request.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market transparency and aids investors in making informed decisions by making company clarifications publicly available.
