## Summary

Bharat Coking Coal Limited (BCCL), a subsidiary of Coal India Limited, finalized its allocation of equity shares to anchor investors, raising ₹273.13 crore ahead of its initial public offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Total amount raised through anchor investors: ₹273.13 crore.
- Total equity shares allotted: 11,87,53,500 shares.
- Allocation price: ₹23 per share (upper end of the price band).
- Participation from major institutional investors including Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC), domestic mutual funds (Bandhan, Nippon India, UTI), and foreign portfolio investors.
