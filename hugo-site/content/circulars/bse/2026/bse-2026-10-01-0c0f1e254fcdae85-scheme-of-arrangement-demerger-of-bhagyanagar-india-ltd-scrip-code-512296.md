## Summary

Bhagyanagar India Ltd. has fixed a Record Date pursuant to the Composite Scheme of Arrangement for the demerger of its Copper Business into Tieramet Limited.

## Key Points

- Demerged Company: Bhagyanagar India Ltd. (Scrip code: 512296)
- Resulting Company: Tieramet Limited
- Record Date: 08/10/2026
- Share Entitlement Ratio: 1 (One) Equity Share of face value of Rs. 2/- each in Tieramet Limited for every 1 (One) Equity Share held in Bhagyanagar India Ltd.
- Group Transfer: Trading in equity shares transferred from 'B' Group to 'T' Group w.e.f. October 8, 2026.
- Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS): Subject to Call Auction in Pre-open Session on October 8, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer of scrip to 'T' Group and inclusion in Special Pre-Open Session (SPOS) pursuant to SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the record date, share entitlement ratio, and trading restrictions effective October 8, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 08/10/2026
- Effective Date / Transfer to 'T' Group & SPOS: 08/10/2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders as of the record date will receive equity shares in Tieramet Limited. Trading is subjected to trade-for-trade settlement and special pre-open session.
