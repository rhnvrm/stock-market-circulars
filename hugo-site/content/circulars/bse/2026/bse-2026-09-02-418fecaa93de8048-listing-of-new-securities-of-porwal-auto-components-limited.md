## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Porwal Auto Components Limited effective from September 3, 2026, following a preferential issue to non-promoters.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Porwal Auto Components Limited (Scrip Code: 532933, ISIN: INE386I01018)
- Securities: 17,54,384 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 47/- (Issue Price: Rs. 57/-)
- Allotment Date: 09/07/2026
- Distinctive Numbers: 15100001 to 16854384
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and trading permissions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Trading: 03/09/2026
- Allotment Date: 09/07/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 09/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate action resulting in a minor expansion of the company's paid-up equity base with shares subject to a 6-month lock-in period.
