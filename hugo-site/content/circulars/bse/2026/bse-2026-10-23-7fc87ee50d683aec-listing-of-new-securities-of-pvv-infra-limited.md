## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of 99,12,608 new equity shares of PVV Infra Limited (Scrip Code: 536659) resulting from the conversion of partly paid shares into fully paid-up shares issued on a rights basis. Trading in these securities will commence on Monday, October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** PVV Infra Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 536659
- **ISIN:** INE428B01021
- **Securities:** 99,12,608 Equity shares of Rs. 5/- each (conversion of partly paid to fully paid-up issued on rights basis)
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 149573337 to 159485944
- **Ranking:** Pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the new securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 26, 2026
- **Notice Date:** October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing notice for converted equity shares; standard operational update for market participants holding or trading PVV Infra Limited shares.
