## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the debt securities of Siskin Projects Private Limited, stating that the specified scrip code and ISIN will be available for trading with revised terms effective from September 29, 2026.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous Exchange Notice No. 20260406-10 dated April 06, 2026.
- Scrip Code: 977711
- ISIN: INE2C3E07017
- Security will trade under revised terms as specified in Annexure I.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading availability with revised terms for the debt securities of Siskin Projects Private Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the revised terms and scrip code availability for trading.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Notice Date:** September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impact is restricted to market participants and investors holding or trading the specific debt securities of Siskin Projects Private Limited.
