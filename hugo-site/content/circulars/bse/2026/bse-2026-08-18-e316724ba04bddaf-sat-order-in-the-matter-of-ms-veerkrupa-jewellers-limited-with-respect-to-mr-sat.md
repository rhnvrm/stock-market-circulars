## Summary

The Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) has directed a stay of debarment for Mr. Satish Vadilal Sheth in the matter of M/s Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited, subject to the deposit of the full penalty. SEBI confirmed that the appellant has paid the penalty amount of Rs. 20,00,000.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous Exchange Notice No. 20260601-36 dated June 01, 2026.
- SAT vide order dated July 20, 2026, directed a stay of debarment subject to deposit of the full penalty in an interest-bearing account by SEBI.
- SEBI confirmed via email on August 18, 2026, that Mr. Satish Vadilal Sheth paid the full penalty of Rs. 20,00,000.

## Regulatory Changes

- Stay of debarment granted by SAT upon fulfillment of penalty deposit conditions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to take note of the SAT order and SEBI confirmation regarding the stay of debarment and penalty payment.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order Date: July 20, 2026
- SEBI Confirmation Date: August 18, 2026
- Notice Date: August 18, 2026

- Operational status update for the concerned individual regarding trading restrictions and debarment relief upon compliance.
