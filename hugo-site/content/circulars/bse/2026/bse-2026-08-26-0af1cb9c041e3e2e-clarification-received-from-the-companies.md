## Summary

The Exchange observed significant price and volume movements in certain scrips and sought clarifications from the respective companies to ensure investors have access to the latest relevant information. Clarifications have been received from several companies, while replies from others are still awaited.

## Key Points

- The Exchange monitors unusual price and volume activity across listed scrips.
- Replies with clarifications have been received from 7 companies, including GlaxoSmithKline Pharmaceuticals Ltd and Unichem Laboratories Ltd.
- Replies are awaited from another 7 companies, including PVP Ventures Ltd and Super Tannery Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies are required to respond promptly to exchange queries regarding significant price and volume fluctuations to maintain market transparency.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 26 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and timely dissemination of material information to market participants regarding unusual stock price or volume movements.
