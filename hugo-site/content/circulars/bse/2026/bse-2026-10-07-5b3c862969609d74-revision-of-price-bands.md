## Summary

BSE has announced the revision of price bands for various scrips as part of a surveillance action review, effective from October 08, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands revised for various scrips as part of surveillance review.
- Effective date is October 08, 2026.
- Scrips in Trade for Trade segments continue to attract a 5% price band.
- Clarifications can be sought via email at bse.surv@bseindia.com.

## Regulatory Changes

Adjustment of price bands across multiple scrips under surveillance mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must update their trading systems and ensure adherence to the revised price bands effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date: October 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts intraday trading limits and order placements for the affected scrips.
