## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that 50,40,000 equity shares of SRIT India Limited have been allocated to anchor investors at a price of Rs 130/- per equity share under its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: SRIT India Limited
- Issue Segment: Equity / Public Issue (Anchor Investors Portion)
- Number of Shares Allocated: 50,40,000 Equity Shares
- Issue Price / Allocation Price: Rs 130/- per equity share
- Announcement Date: September 25, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to note the anchor investor allocation details for the public issue of SRIT India Limited.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 25, 2026
- Announcement Date: September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The anchor investor allotment provides institutional backing and price discovery confidence for the public issue of SRIT India Limited.
