## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the live activities schedule and operational parameters for the Offer to Buy (OTB) through the Acquisition Window for the delisting of equity shares of Elpro International Ltd. The fixed delisting price is set at Rs. 181.80 per equity share, with the bidding period scheduled from Tuesday, August 4, 2026, to Monday, August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** ELPRO INTERNATIONAL LTD
- **Security Symbol:** ELPROINTL
- **ISIN:** INE579B01039
- **Scrip Code:** 504000
- **Offer Size:** Upto 4,23,70,160 Equity Shares
- **Fixed Delisting Price:** Rs. 181.80 per equity share
- **Market Lot:** 1
- **Face Value:** Rs. 1.00

## Regulatory Changes

- The delisting process is conducted in accordance with SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021, utilizing the fixed price delisting process.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure the Unique Client Code (UCC) of the investor is registered and/or updated with the correct PAN number and category in the Exchange UCC database.
- Trading members can bid for investors holding shares in demat form.
- Members and custodians should note that if no securities are accepted, no end-of-issue files will be generated on the extranet.

## Important Dates

- **Opening Date / Bid Offering Period Start:** Tuesday, 4th August 2026 (9:15 a.m.)
- **Closing Date / Bid Offering Period End:** Monday, 10th August 2026 (3:30 p.m.)
- **Upper Modification and Cancellation Period:** 4th August 2026 to 7th August 2026
- **Take-up (Last day of the offer):** 10th August 2026 up to 4:00 p.m.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides clear timelines and platform accessibility details (iBBS, RTRMS, Extranet) for trading and custodian members participating in the delisting offer of Elpro International Ltd.
