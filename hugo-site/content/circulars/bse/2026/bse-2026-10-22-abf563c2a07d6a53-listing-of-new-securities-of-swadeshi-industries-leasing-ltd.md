## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 1,23,10,000 equity shares of Swadeshi Industries & Leasing Ltd. (Scrip Code - 506863) resulting from the conversion of warrants on a preferential basis, effective from October 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company: Swadeshi Industries & Leasing Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 506863
- ISIN: INE716M01034
- Security Details: 1,23,10,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at par to promoter and non-promoter categories upon warrant conversion.
- Allotment Date: March 5, 2026.
- Lock-in Details:
  - 46,25,590 shares (Dist. Nos. 10817951 - 15443540) locked-in up to April 31, 2028.
  - 76,84,410 shares (Dist. Nos. 15443541 - 23127950) locked-in up to April 30, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in periods and trading permissions for the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date: October 23, 2026
- Allotment Date: March 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing of shares issued via preferential warrant conversion, expanding the company's paid-up equity capital.
