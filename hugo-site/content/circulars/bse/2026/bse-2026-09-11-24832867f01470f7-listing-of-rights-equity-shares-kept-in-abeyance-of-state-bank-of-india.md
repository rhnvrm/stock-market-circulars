## Summary

State Bank of India has announced the listing and trading permission for 1,49,200 equity shares of Re. 1/- each, which were previously kept in abeyance and allotted on August 21, 2026. These shares will be permitted to trade starting from Tuesday, September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** State Bank of India
- **Scrip Code:** 500112
- **ISIN:** INE062A01020
- **Securities:** 1,49,200 Equity Shares of Re. 1/- each
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 9230617587 to 9230766786
- **Effective Date:** September 15, 2026
- **Ranking:** Pari passu with existing equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are informed about the listing and permission to trade in the newly released securities.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** August 21, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The listing of shares kept in abeyance expands the paid-up equity base for State Bank of India with no adverse market impacts.
