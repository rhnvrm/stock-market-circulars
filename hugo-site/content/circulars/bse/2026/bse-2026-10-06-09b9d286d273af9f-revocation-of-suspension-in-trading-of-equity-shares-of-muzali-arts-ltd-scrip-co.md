## Summary

BSE has announced the revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares of Muzali Arts Ltd (Scrip Code: 539410), effective from October 14, 2026. Trading will resume under the 'XT' group pursuant to SEBI Master Circular guidelines.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 539410
- Company Name: Muzali Arts Ltd
- Trading Resumption Date: October 14, 2026
- Trading Group: 'XT' group
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session for IPOs and Relisted Scrips on October 14, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading resumed pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026 (erstwhile SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/CFD/CMD/CIR/P/2020/12 dated January 22, 2020).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are advised to note that the scrip is part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) for relisted scrips.
- Information Memorandum of the company is available on the Exchange's website under Corporates → Corporate Filings → Information Memorandum → Revocation.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 06, 2026
- Revocation/Resumption Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables investors and trading members to resume trading in the equity shares of Muzali Arts Ltd after the revocation of suspension, subject to SPOS guidelines.
