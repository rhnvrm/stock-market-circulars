## Summary

Union Mutual Fund has communicated a change in the scheme names for three specific debt schemes available on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective immediately.

## Key Points

- Union Dynamic Bond Fund has been renamed to Union Dynamic Term Fund.
- Union Short Duration Fund has been renamed to Union Short Term Fund.
- Union Low Duration Fund has been renamed to Union Ultra Short To Short Term Fund.
- Changes apply to transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and distributors need to update their systems to reflect the revised scheme names for Union Mutual Fund.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 27 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to operational and record-keeping updates by distributors and investors reflecting the new scheme names.
