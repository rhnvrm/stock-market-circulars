## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that new securities of Quint Digital Ltd. (Partly Paid-up CCPS and Warrants issued on a rights basis) are permitted to trade with effect from Friday, September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Security 1:** 82,61,401 10% Partly paid-up non-cumulative non-participating Preference Shares (NCCPs) compulsorily convertible preference shares (CCPS) of Rs. 100/- each, partly paid up at Rs. 50/-.
  - Scrip Code: 710058
  - Scrip ID on BOLT System: QDLCCPS
  - ISIN No: IN9641R03011
  - Date of Allotment: 11/09/2026

- **Security 2:** 82,61,401 Partly paid-up detachable Warrants of Rs. 10/- each, partly paid up at Rs. 5/-.
  - Scrip Code: 961921
  - Scrip ID on BOLT System: QDLDW
  - ISIN No: INE641R13020
  - Date of Allotment: 11/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with the terms of trading for the newly listed partly paid-up securities.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** September 11, 2026
- **Commencement of Trading:** September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Permits investors and trading members to trade in the newly allotted partly paid-up rights issue securities of Quint Digital Ltd.
