## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the debt securities of Tyger Capital Private Limited, informing trading members about revised terms for its Series B Debentures effective from August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 975881
- ISIN: INE01EQ07111
- Company Name: Tyger Capital Private Limited
- Effective Date: August 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- The Series B Coupon Rate will not be stepped up by 0.25% p.a. as previously outlined in the Debenture Trust Deed.
- The existing coupon rate of 9.90% will continue to be applicable from the Series B Put Option Date / Series B Call Option Date and remain payable until the Series B Maturity Date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the revised coupon terms for trading and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 19, 2026
- Notice Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact as this is an administrative update to specific debt security terms for an individual issuer.
