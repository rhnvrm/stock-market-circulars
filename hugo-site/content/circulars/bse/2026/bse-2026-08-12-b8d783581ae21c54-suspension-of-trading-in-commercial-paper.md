## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has notified trading members regarding the suspension of trading in the Commercial Papers (CPs) of A.K. Capital Services Ltd. and Redington Limited. This suspension is a standard procedure aligned with the scheduled redemption of these instruments, for which record dates have been fixed.

## Key Points

- **A.K. Capital Services Ltd.**: Trading in Commercial Paper (Scrip Code: 731074, ISIN: INE701G14346) will be suspended. The record date and ex-date for redemption are fixed as August 19, 2026.
- **Redington Limited**: Trading in Commercial Paper (Scrip Code: 732112, ISIN: INE891D14C85) will be suspended. The record date and ex-date for redemption are fixed as August 20, 2026.
- **Trading Restriction**: Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of these companies starting from their respective ex-dates/record dates.

## Regulatory Changes

No major regulatory policy changes are introduced. This is a routine operational notice executed under the standard listing regulations for commercial paper/debt securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must ensure that no trades are executed or settled in the affected commercial papers (Scrip Codes: 731074 and 732112) on or after their respective ex-dates (August 19, 2026, and August 20, 2026).
- **Investors**: Holding these commercial papers will receive the redemption proceeds directly from the issuers based on the holdings as of the respective record dates.

## Important Dates

- **August 19, 2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for redemption of Commercial Paper of A.K. Capital Services Ltd. (INE701G14346).
- **August 20, 2026**: Record Date and Ex-Date for redemption of Commercial Paper of Redington Limited (INE891D14C85).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Extremely minimal/negligible. This suspension only affects the specific commercial paper tranches scheduled for redemption and does not impact the trading of equity shares of A.K. Capital Services Ltd. (AKCAPIT) or Redington Limited (REDINGTON).
- **Operational Impact**: Clearing and settlement systems will halt trading in these specific ISINs to prevent any transactional discrepancies prior to the redemption payment.
