## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, outlining the inclusion of certain securities, movement of specific securities to lower stages, and exclusion of others. Securities under the ESM framework are subject to heightened surveillance, margin requirements, and trading segment modifications effective August 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying inclusion criteria attract a minimum 100% margin effective August 06, 2026, on all open positions as on August 05, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but are moved to a lower Stage (Stage I) effective August 05, 2026.
- Qualifying securities are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective August 05, 2026.
- Shortlisting is purely on account of market surveillance and does not constitute an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) in conjunction with other prevailing exchange surveillance measures.
- Modification of price bands and settlement segments for shortlisted securities, with price bands reinstated to original levels upon exiting the framework (subject to other applicable measures).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements on open and new positions.
- Trading members should note the shift of applicable scrips to the Trade-for-Trade segment and Stage-specific price bands.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 04, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage II adjustments, movement to lower stages, and exits: August 05, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% margin and Trade-for-Trade segment shifts: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading activity in the affected securities may be restricted due to Trade-for-Trade settlement and 100% margin requirements.
- Enhanced monitoring protects market integrity and provides transparency on volatile or surveillance-tracked scrips.
