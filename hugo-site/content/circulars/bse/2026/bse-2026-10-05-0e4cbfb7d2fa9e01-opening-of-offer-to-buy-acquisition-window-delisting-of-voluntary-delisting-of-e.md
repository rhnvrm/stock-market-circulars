## Summary

Geetanjali Trading and Investments Private Limited ("Acquirer") has announced the opening of an Offer for Voluntary Delisting of Equity Shares of Hitech Corporation Limited ("Target Company"). The acquisition window operates through the Stock Exchange mechanism for tender offers under SEBI Delisting Regulations, running from October 07, 2026, to October 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** Hitech Corporation Limited
- **Acquirer:** Geetanjali Trading and Investments Private Limited
- **Offer Size:** 43,91,220 Equity Shares representing 25.57% of the paid-up equity share capital held by public shareholders.
- **Floor / Offer Price:** Rs. 252/- per Offer Share.
- **Indicative Price:** Rs. 353/- per Offer Share.
- **Total Consideration:** Aggregating up to Rs. 110.65 Crores.
- **Mechanism:** Reverse Book-Building Process (RBBP).

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI (Delisting of Equity Shares) Regulations, 2021, and SEBI circulars on mechanism for acquisition of shares through Stock Exchange pursuant to tender offers (CIR/CFD/POLICYCELL/1/2015 and subsequent updates).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the acquisition window schedule and tender shares on behalf of eligible public shareholders in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Offer Opening Date:** October 07, 2026
- **Offer Closing Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Notice Date:** October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Public shareholders of Hitech Corporation Limited have the opportunity to tender their shares through the acquisition window within the specified price discovery framework, leading to potential delisting of the company's equity shares from the stock exchange upon successful completion.
