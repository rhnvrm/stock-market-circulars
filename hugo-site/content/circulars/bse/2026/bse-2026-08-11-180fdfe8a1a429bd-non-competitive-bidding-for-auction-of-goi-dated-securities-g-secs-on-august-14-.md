## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of four Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled on August 14, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Four GoI dated securities (New GS 2029, New GS 2033, 7.24% GS 2055, and 7.50% GOI SGrB 2056) are offered through auction.
- Bidding takes place via the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.
- Bid collection for direct investors closes on August 13, 2026, at 5.00 pm.
- Bid collection for members closes on August 14, 2026, at 8.00 am.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Conducted pursuant to the RBI auction announcement and existing exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure bids are submitted within the prescribed timelines through the NCB-GSec module.

## Important Dates

- Bid Collection Start Date: August 11, 2026, from 10:00 am onwards
- Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor): August 13, 2026, till 5.00 pm
- Bid Collection End Date (Members): August 14, 2026, till 8.00 am
- Auction Date: August 14, 2026
- Settlement Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates retail and non-competitive participation in Government of India dated securities auctions through BSE trading members.
