## Summary

BSE has announced that trading in the equity shares of Anand Seamless Limited (SME IPO, Scrip Code: 544944) will be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from October 14, 2026, following Exchange Notice No. 20260928-37.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Anand Seamless Limited
- Scrip Code: 544944
- Segment Transfer: From Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: Wednesday, October 14, 2026
- Reference Notice: Continuation of Exchange’s Notice No. 20260928-37 dated September 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Equity shares are moving out of the Trade for Trade segment into the normal rolling settlement segment, enabling regular rolling settlement of trades.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the change in segment and group for settlement and trading operations starting October 14, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members will experience normal rolling segment trading for Anand Seamless Limited shares starting from the effective date, improving liquidity and trading efficiency compared to the Trade for Trade segment restrictions.
