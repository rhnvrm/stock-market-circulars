## Summary

Trading Members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Tuesday, October 6, 2026, the Equity Shares of Pind Hospitality Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the Exchange in the SME segment ('MT' Group of Securities). The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pind Hospitality Limited
- Scrip Code: 544966
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: PIND
- ISIN: INE0RNF01016
- Total Securities: 5,997,612 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Distinctive Number Range: 1 to 5,997,612
- Issue Price: Rs. 99/- per share (Face Value: Rs. 10/-, Premium: Rs. 89/-)
- Date of Allotment: October 01, 2026
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Market Maker: Bhansali Value Creations Private Limited
- Registrar: Bigshare Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form pursuant to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will participate in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing day (October 6, 2026).

## Compliance Requirements

- Trades must be executed in minimum market lots of 1,200 equity shares.
- Physical share certificates have been enfaced for non-transferability, and demat lock-in has been properly executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 01, 2026
- Commencement of Trading: October 06, 2026
- Trade-for-Trade Period: 10 trading days starting October 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public market trading and liquidity for Pind Hospitality Limited on the BSE SME platform while maintaining regulatory oversight through initial trade-for-trade settlement.
