## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision of stages, and removal of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and transfers to specific trading groups for affected securities based on periodic reviews and XBRL submissions.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable on open positions and new positions for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Movement of certain securities to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework along with lower price bands.
- Transfer of certain securities within the LT-ASM framework to T / XT / MT / TS Groups.
- Securities moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated, subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Imposition of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures as per the revised framework.
- Adjustments to margin requirements, price bands, and segment groupings for listed securities under surveillance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements for applicable securities effective August 20, 2026.
- Trading members and entities must track revised price bands and group transfers effective August 18, 2026 and August 20, 2026 respectively.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Lower Price Band / Movement to Higher Stages / Moving out: August 18, 2026
- Open Positions Cut-off Date: August 19, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margins / Group Transfers: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and leverage for the shortlisted securities will be restricted due to 100% margin requirements and lower price bands, aimed at enhancing market integrity and investor protection.
