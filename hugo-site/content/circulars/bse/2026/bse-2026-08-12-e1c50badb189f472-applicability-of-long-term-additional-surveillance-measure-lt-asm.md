## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of the revised Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The notice outlines the imposition of 100% margins on open and new positions of selected securities, the applicability of lower price bands, and the shifting of securities into or out of different LT-ASM stages and trading groups (T / XT / MT / TS).

## Key Points

- **100% Margins**: Imposed on all open positions as of August 14, 2026, and new positions created on or after August 17, 2026.
- **Stage Revision**: Securities moved to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework are effective from August 13, 2026.
- **Price Band Restriction**: A lower price band is applicable to shortlisted securities starting August 13, 2026.
- **Group Shifting**: Affected securities remaining under the LT-ASM framework will be transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Group w.e.f. August 17, 2026.
- **Framework Exclusions**: Securities moving out of the framework (w.e.f. August 13, 2026) will have their price bands reinstated, subject to any other active surveillance measures.
- **Non-Adverse Action**: Shortlisting is purely based on market surveillance criteria (utilizing XBRL submissions by listed companies) and is not punitive.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised LT-ASM surveillance rules under previous notice frameworks (dating back to March 21, 2018, and updated subsequently up to September 20, 2024).
- Re-classification of specific securities into T / XT / MT / TS trading groups.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members**: Must implement 100% margin controls for all open positions of shortlisted securities by August 14, 2026, and restrict leverage for new positions starting August 17, 2026.
- **Operational Systems**: Trading terminals and risk management systems must update price bands and group designations for affected securities according to the specified effective dates.

## Important Dates

- **August 13, 2026**: Implementation of higher stage movements, lower price bands, and exclusions from the LT-ASM framework.
- **August 14, 2026**: Calculation date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- **August 17, 2026**: Effective date for 100% margins on open and new positions; transfer of securities to T / XT / MT / TS groups.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: The imposition of a 100% margin and narrower price bands is designed to curb excessive volatility and speculative trading in shortlisted securities.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading members need to monitor the Annexures to ensure proper margin collection and system setup for the affected scrips. Clarifications can be requested via bse.surv@bseindia.com.
