## Summary

Centrum Broking Limited (Member No. 798) has voluntarily decided to cease its business operations across Debt, MFI, OBPP, and RTM segments on the BSE, effective Monday, October 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Trading Member: Centrum Broking Limited
- Member Number: 798
- Affected Segments: Debt, MFI, OBPP, and RTM segments
- Effective Date: October 26, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members or clients associated with Centrum Broking Limited in the affected segments should take note of the cessation of business and direct any queries to membership.ops@bseindia.com.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date of Closure: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Limited impact restricted to clients and counterparties dealing with Centrum Broking Limited in the specified segments (Debt, MFI, OBPP, RTM).
