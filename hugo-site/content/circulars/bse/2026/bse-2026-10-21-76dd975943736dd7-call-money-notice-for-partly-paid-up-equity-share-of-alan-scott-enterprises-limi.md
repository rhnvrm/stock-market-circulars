## Summary

BSE has announced the activation of the Call Money Module on the IBBS platform for Alan Scott Enterprises Limited. The company is raising the first and final call money of Rs. 35 per share on its partly paid-up equity shares (Security Symbol: ASCOTTCM).

## Key Points

- Issuer Name: Alan Scott Enterprises Limited
- Security Symbol: ASCOTTCM
- Type of Call: First and Final Call
- Call Money Amount: Rs. 35 per share
- Number of Partly Paid-up Equity Shares: 9,52,931
- Registrar: Purva Sharegistry (India) Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; executed in accordance with SEBI circular dated December 8, 2020, and exchange notice no. 20210104-37.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading Members, Self-Certified Syndicate Banks (SCSBs), and intermediaries must use the IBBS platform to collect and submit call money details during the designated window.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: Thursday, October 22, 2026
- Issue Closing Date: Thursday, November 05, 2026
- Bidding Period: 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. daily
- Issue Period: 15 days

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders holding partly paid-up shares of Alan Scott Enterprises Limited must pay the call money of Rs. 35 per share within the stipulated window to avoid default or forfeiture actions as per exchange and company guidelines.
