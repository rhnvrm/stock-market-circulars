## Summary

BSE has announced that certain securities will be moved to higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) effective from October 26, 2026, based on the framework established in previous notices.

## Key Points

- Securities move to higher GSM stages (Stages I, II, III, or IV) starting October 26, 2026.
- Applicable surveillance actions depend on the assigned stage, ranging from 100% margins and price band restrictions to trade-for-trade settlement and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD).
- Settlement for affected securities will be on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of stricter surveillance and margin requirements under the GSM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules, and deposit required ASD where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective Date: October 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and settlement for the specified securities will be restricted due to trade-for-trade requirements, mandatory ASD, and reduced price bands.
