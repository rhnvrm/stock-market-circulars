## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of Interest Rate Derivatives (IRD) contracts, comprising both Interest Rate Futures (IRF) and Options contracts, on three Government of India bonds with effect from September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Contracts are introduced for three Government of India bonds: 7.06% GS 2041, 6.57% GS 2033, and 6.53% GS 2031.
- Both Interest Rate Futures and Options Contracts will be available for each of the specified bonds.
- Effective date for the introduction of these contracts is September 21, 2026.
- Trading members are requested to refer to previous exchange notice 20150616-21 dated June 16, 2015, for general guidelines on Cash Settled Interest Rate Futures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Expansion of the exchange-traded interest rate derivatives segment to include new dated government securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must review product details and trading parameters provided in the respective annexures.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances liquidity and risk management options for market participants dealing in long-term Government of India securities.
