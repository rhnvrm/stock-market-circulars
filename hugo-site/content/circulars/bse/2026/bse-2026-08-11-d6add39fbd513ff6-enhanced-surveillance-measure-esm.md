## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities satisfying inclusion criteria, moving to lower stages (such as Stage I), shifting to the Trade-for-Trade segment, and attracting 100% margins effective August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I satisfy criteria for inclusion under ESM and attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. August 13, 2026, on open positions as of August 12, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Certain securities will continue in the ESM framework but move to a lower Stage (Stage I) w.e.f. August 12, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. August 13, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. August 12, 2026.
- A list of securities moving out of the ESM framework effective August 12, 2026, is provided in Annexure II, with price bands reinstated accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) under market surveillance guidelines.
- Segment transfer from Rolling Settlement to Trade-for-Trade for qualifying securities.
- Price band adjustments and periodic call auction enforcement for Stage II securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the 100% margin requirement on applicable open and new positions.
- Trading members should monitor securities moving across different stages and adjust trading parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage Changes / Price Bands / Moving Out: August 12, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade Shifting: August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading activity for affected scrips may be restricted due to Trade-for-Trade settlement and 100% margin requirements.
- Enhanced monitoring protects market integrity through preventive surveillance actions.
