## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that Haldia Petrochemicals Limited has fixed a record date for the early redemption of its Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs). Trading members are advised not to deal in these debentures effective from the ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Haldia Petrochemicals Limited
- Purpose: Early Redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs)
- Record Date: 30/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 30/09/2026
- Affected Scrips:
  - Scrip Code 974026 (ISIN: INE105U07014, Scrip ID: HPL-8.75%-29-6-29-PVT)
  - Scrip Code 974028 (ISIN: INE105U07030, Scrip ID: HPL-8.95%-1-7-27-PVT)

## Regulatory Changes

None. Operational notice regarding corporate debt action.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures of Haldia Petrochemicals Limited as of the record/ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 25 September 2026
- Record Date: 30 September 2026
- Ex-Date: 30 September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct impact on holders of the specified NCDs of Haldia Petrochemicals Limited due to early redemption and cessation of trading.
