## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice regarding the listing and admission for trading of further securities issued by 18 companies under their respective Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Schemes (ESOS), effective from Tuesday, September 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies involved: 18 listed entities.
- Effective date for trading of new securities: September 15, 2026.
- Covers issuance under ESOP/ESOS schemes.
- Included companies span diverse sectors, featuring C.E. Info Systems, CreditAccess Grameen, FSN E-Commerce Ventures (Nykaa), Marico, Tata Motors, and Yes Bank, among others.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members of the exchange are informed to take note of the listing of new securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 11, 2026
- Effective Trading Date: September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up capital of the respective companies through employee stock option exercises.
