## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade in 8,33,331 new equity shares of Remi Edelstahl Tubulars Limited (Scrip Code: 513043) effective from October 21, 2026, issued on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 513043
- ISIN: INE158G01015
- Security Details: 8,33,331 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 170/- (Issue Price: Rs. 180/-) on a preferential basis.
- Date of Allotment: 23/09/2026
- Distribution Nos.: 12614520 to 13447850
- Lock-in Details:
  - 6,66,666 shares (Dist. Nos. 12614520 to 13281185) locked-in up to 30/04/2028.
  - 1,66,665 shares (Dist. Nos. 13281186 to 13447850) locked-in up to 30/04/2027.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with lock-in periods and trading parameters for the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 23/09/2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: 21/10/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: 30/04/2027 and 30/04/2028

## Impact Assessment

Expands the paid-up equity capital of Remi Edelstahl Tubulars Limited with shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
