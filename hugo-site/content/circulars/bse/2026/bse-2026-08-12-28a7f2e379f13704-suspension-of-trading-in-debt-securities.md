## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued Notice No. 20260812-11 informing trading members that the debt securities (debentures) of IL&FS Financial Services Limited will be suspended from trading with effect from August 14, 2026. This suspension is a routine procedural step as these debt securities are scheduled to mature on August 16, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Affected Entity:** IL&FS Financial Services Limited.
- **Security Type:** Debt Securities / Debentures (ILFSFSL-8.75%-16-08-26-PVT).
- **ISIN:** INE121H07BP4.
- **BSE Scrip Code:** 954738.
- **Maturity Date:** August 16, 2026.
- **Trading Suspension Date:** August 14, 2026.
- **Action Required:** Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid debentures with effect from August 14, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes introduced by this notice. This is a routine operational procedure in compliance with existing listing regulations for debt securities reaching their maturity.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Restriction:** Trading members of the Exchange must ensure that they do not deal or execute transactions in the matured debentures with effect from August 14, 2026.
- **Systems Configuration:** Exchange brokers should update their trading and back-office terminals to prevent any order entry/matching for BSE Scrip Code 954738 (ISIN: INE121H07BP4) on or after the suspension date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 12, 2026
- **Trading Suspension Effective Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Maturity Date:** August 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine operational maturity process for existing debentures and does not indicate any distress or punitive action from regulators.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Limited to updating the trading status of BSE Scrip Code 954738 on trading platforms.
