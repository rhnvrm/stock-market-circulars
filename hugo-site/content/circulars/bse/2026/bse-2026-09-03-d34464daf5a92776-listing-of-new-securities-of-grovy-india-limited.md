## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and trading permission of 41,69,433 new equity shares of Grovy India Limited issued on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters, effective from September 4, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Grovy India Limited (Scrip Code: 539522)
- Securities: 41,69,433 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 26/-.
- Issue Price: Rs. 36/-
- ISIN: INE343C01012
- Effective Date of Trading: September 4, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the lock-in details and distribution numbers for the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 04/08/2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: 04/09/2026
- Lock-in Upto (16,66,664 shares): 31/03/2028
- Lock-in Upto (25,02,769 shares): 31/03/2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the equity capital base of Grovy India Limited with specific lock-in periods for promoters and non-promoters.
