## Summary

Indian Clearing Corporation Ltd. (BSECL) has issued the settlement programme and trade date schedules for the currency derivatives segment covering the period from November 01, 2026, to November 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines daily trade dates alongside their corresponding settlement dates for November 2026.
- Specifies November 26, 2026, as the expiry date (last trading day) for November 2026 currency future contracts.
- Sets November 30, 2026, as the final settlement date for November 2026 currency future contracts.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must align their operations and clearing cycles with the specified settlement calendar for currency derivatives.

## Important Dates

- Programme Period: 01.11.2026 to 30.11.2026
- Expiry Date (Nov 2026 Contracts): 26.11.2026
- Final Settlement Date: 30.11.2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth administrative scheduling and operational clarity for clearing and settlement activities in the currency derivatives segment.
