## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for Titanium Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund (SIF) under Tata Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The subscription period runs from September 23, 2026, to October 07, 2026, with a tentative allotment date of October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Fund House:** Tata Mutual Fund
- **Scheme Name:** Titanium Active Asset Allocator Long-Short Fund (SIF) - Regular and Direct Plans (Growth, IDCW Payout, and IDCW Reinvestment options)
- **NFO Window:** September 23, 2026, to October 07, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 07, 2026, for lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 12, 2026.
- **ISINs:** INF277K30138, INF277K30146, INF277K30153, INF277K30161, INF277K30179, INF277K30187.

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on the NFO closure date (October 07, 2026).
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on October 12, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Open Date:** September 23, 2026
- **NFO Close Date:** October 07, 2026
- **SIP Authentication Deadline:** October 07, 2026 (3:00 PM)
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants and investors access to a new specialized investment fund scheme via the BSE StAR MF platform with specific operational timelines.
