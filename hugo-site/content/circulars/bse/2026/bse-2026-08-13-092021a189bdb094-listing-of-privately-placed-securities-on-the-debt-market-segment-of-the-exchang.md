## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of privately placed commercial papers issued by multiple companies on the debt market segment of the Exchange, effective August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are traded only in dematerialised form under specified ISIN numbers.
- Standard trading denomination is Rs. 5 Lakhs and multiples thereof.
- Tick size for the securities is set at 1 paise.
- Issuers include Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, A.K. Capital Finance Limited, and Can Fin Homes Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard demat form and denomination rules for commercial papers.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 13, 2026
- Allotment and Redemption dates vary by issuer (ranging from August 2026 to November 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing notice with no direct broad market impact.
