## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the movement of specified securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from August 21, 2026. Trading members are required to note the heightened surveillance actions and margin requirements applicable to these securities.

## Key Points

- Securities are moved to higher GSM stages in accordance with the established GSM framework.
- Effective date for the surveillance actions: August 21, 2026.
- Settlement for the affected securities shall be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Specific surveillance actions vary by GSM stage (Stage I to Stage IV).

## Regulatory Changes

- **Stage I:** Applicable margin rate shall be 100% and price band of 5% or lower as applicable.
- **Stage II:** Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower as applicable and Additional Surveillance Deposit (ASD) of 50% of trade value to be deposited by the buyers.
- **Stage III:** Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower as applicable, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week), and ASD of 100% of trade value to be deposited by the buyers.
- **Stage IV:** Trade for trade with price band of 5% or lower as applicable, trading permitted once a week (every Monday/1st trading day of the week), ASD of 100% of trade value to be deposited by buyers with no upward movement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the listed securities.
- Ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements, trade-to-trade settlement rules, and Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD) where applicable.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 20 Aug 2026
- **Effective Date:** 21 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for securities entering higher GSM stages are expected to be constrained due to trade-to-trade settlement restrictions, weekly trading limits in higher stages, and heavy margin/deposit requirements.
