## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced constituent changes for the BSE 4–8-year G-Sec Index, effective Monday, August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- **BSE 4-8 Year G-Sec Index Changes:** 
  - **Add:** 6.54% GS 2032 (ISIN: IN0020210244, Maturity: 17-Jan-32)
  - **Drop:** 7.02% GS 2031 (ISIN: IN0020240076, Maturity: 18-Jun-31)
- **Unchanged Indices:** BSE 8-13 Year G-Sec Index, BSE India 10 Year Sovereign Bond Index, and BSE India 5 Year Sovereign Bond Index remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine index rebalancing with minimal impact on overall market operations.
