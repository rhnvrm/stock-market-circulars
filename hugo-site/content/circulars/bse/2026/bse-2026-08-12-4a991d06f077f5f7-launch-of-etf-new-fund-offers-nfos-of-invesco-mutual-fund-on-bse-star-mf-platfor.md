## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued an update (Notice No. 20260812-37) regarding the revised allotment and reopening dates for the Invesco Mutual Fund NFOs of INVESCO INDIA NIFTY BANK ETF and INVESCO INDIA BSE SENSEX ETF. Following a revised communication from Invesco Mutual Fund, the allotment date has been advanced from August 17, 2026, to August 14, 2026. Consequently, the reopening date has been revised from August 24, 2026, to August 21, 2026. Members must ensure clear funds are available in the ICCL account before the specified deadline to qualify for allotment.

## Key Points

- **NFO Schemes:** Clarifications apply specifically to the INVESCO INDIA NIFTY BANK ETF and INVESCO INDIA BSE SENSEX ETF.
- **Revised Dates:** Allotment is preponed to August 14, 2026; Reopening is preponed to August 21, 2026.
- **Funding Deadline:** Clear funds must be available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on August 14, 2026.
- **Payment Modes:** Cheques and OTM payments are accepted during the NFO period, but allotment remains contingent upon the receipt of clear funds in the ICCL account by the 6:00 PM August 14 deadline.
- **Precedent:** This notice updates Notice No. 20260727-25 dated July 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no structural regulatory changes introduced by this notice. It is a procedural operational schedule update.

## Compliance Requirements

BSE members must ensure the following:
- Customers' clear funds for these NFOs are in the Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL) account by 6:00 PM on August 14, 2026.
- Any cheque or OTM transactions are initiated early enough to achieve clearing before the specified cutoff time on August 14, 2026.
- Inform respective clients about the advanced allotment and reopening dates.

## Important Dates

- **Revised Allotment Date:** August 14, 2026 (advanced from August 17, 2026)
- **Revised Reopening Date:** August 21, 2026 (advanced from August 24, 2026)
- **ICCL Funds Cut-off Deadline:** August 14, 2026, by 6:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact:** Low. Requires back-office and treasury operations to align fund collection and transfer deadlines to the earlier date of August 14, 2026.
- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine operational scheduling change affecting only those market participants subscribing to these specific Invesco ETF NFO schemes.
