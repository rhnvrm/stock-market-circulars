## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the alignment of SEBI's Cyber Incident Reporting Portal with the FIRE format.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260826-30
- Notice Date: 26 Aug 2026
- Department: BSECL
- Subject: Alignment of SEBI’s Cyber Incident Reporting Portal with FIRE format

## Regulatory Changes

Adoption of the FIRE format for reporting cyber incidents to SEBI's portal.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and relevant entities must ensure their cyber incident reporting mechanisms comply with the updated FIRE format requirements on SEBI's portal.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 26 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures standardized and streamlined reporting of cyber incidents across market infrastructure institutions and regulated entities.
