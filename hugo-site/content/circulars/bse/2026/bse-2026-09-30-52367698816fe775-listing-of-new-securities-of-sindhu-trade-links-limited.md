## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade for 30,04,55,030 new equity shares (Scrip Code: 532029) of Sindhu Trade Links Limited with effect from October 1, 2026, following a preferential issue.

## Key Points

- Company: Sindhu Trade Links Limited (Scrip Code: 532029)
- Securities: 30,04,55,030 equity shares of Re. 1/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 22.20/- (Issue Price: Rs. 23.20/-)
- Allotment Date: August 13, 2026
- ISIN: INE325D01025
- Lock-in Details:
  - 20,60,53,398 shares (Dist. Nos. 1541928781 to 1747982178) locked-in up to March 30, 2028.
  - 9,44,01,632 shares (Dist. Nos. 1747982179 to 1842383810) locked-in up to March 30, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

New securities listed and permitted for trading pursuant to preferential allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the scrip code, ISIN, lock-in periods, and distribution numbers for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2026
- Allotment Date: August 13, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: October 1, 2026
- Lock-in Expiry: March 30, 2027 and March 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of equity capital base of Sindhu Trade Links Limited through preferential issue of shares to promoters and non-promoters.
