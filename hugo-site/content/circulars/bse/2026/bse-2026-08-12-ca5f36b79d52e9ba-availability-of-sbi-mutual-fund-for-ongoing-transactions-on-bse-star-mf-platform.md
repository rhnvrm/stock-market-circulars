## Summary

BSE has informed its members that pursuant to Notice No. 20260724-14 dated July 24, 2026, the **SBI NIFTY MIDCAP 150 MOMENTUM 50 ETF FOF** schemes will reopen for ongoing transactions on the BSE StAR MF Platform with effect from **August 12, 2026**. This reopening includes ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic facilities such as Systematic Investment Plan (SIP), Systematic Transfer Plan (STP), and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP).

## Key Points

- **Reopening Date**: August 12, 2026.
- **Schemes Reopened**:
  1. SBI NIFTY MIDCAP 150 MOMENTUM 50 ETF FOF - REGULAR PLAN - IDCW PAYOUT (ISIN: INF200KB1BS9)
  2. SBI NIFTY MIDCAP 150 MOMENTUM 50 ETF FOF - REGULAR PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF200KB1BR1)
  3. SBI NIFTY MIDCAP 150 MOMENTUM 50 ETF FOF - REGULAR PLAN - IDCW REINVESTMENT (ISIN: INF200KB1BT7)
  4. SBI NIFTY MIDCAP 150 MOMENTUM 50 ETF FOF - DIRECT PLAN - GROWTH (ISIN: INF200KB1BU5)
  5. SBI NIFTY MIDCAP 150 MOMENTUM 50 ETF FOF - DIRECT PLAN - IDCW PAYOUT (ISIN: INF200KB1BV3)
  6. SBI NIFTY MIDCAP 150 MOMENTUM 50 ETF FOF - DIRECT PLAN - IDCW REINVESTMENT (ISIN: INF200KB1BW1)
- **Scheme Type**: Classified as **DEBT** within the notice details.
- **SIP Frequency & Limits**:
  - For Monthly SIP amounts between **Rs. 500 and Rs. 999**, a minimum of 12 installments is required.
  - For Monthly SIP amounts of **Rs. 1,000 and above**, a minimum of 6 installments is required.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes are introduced. This is an operational update regarding mutual fund scheme availability on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Trading Members & Distributors**: Update operational procedures to facilitate subscriptions, redemptions, and systematic setups (SIP/STP/SWP) for the specified SBI Mutual Fund schemes.
- **System Configurations**: Ensure that validation for the updated SIP investment amounts and corresponding minimum installments is correctly configured on distributor platforms.

## Important Dates

- **July 24, 2026**: Date of prior Notice No. 20260724-14.
- **August 12, 2026**: Reopening date of the specified SBI Mutual Fund schemes for ongoing transactions on BSE StAR MF.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. Reopening these FOF schemes restores trading and subscription availability on the BSE StAR MF platform.
- **Operational Impact**: High convenience for distributors and investors utilizing BSE StAR MF, as it restores standard transactional capabilities for these six SBI Mutual Fund options.
