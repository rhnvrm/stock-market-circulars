## Summary

BSE has issued a notice communicating the change in scheme names for multiple schemes of DSP Mutual Fund available on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260824-20, dated August 24, 2026.
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026.
- Applies to several schemes of DSP Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform.
- Key name changes include DSP 10Y G-SEC FUND to DSP 10 YEAR CONSTANT MATURITY GILT FUND, DSP BOND FUND to DSP MEDIUM TERM FUND, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational update reflecting revised scheme names from the Asset Management Company (AMC).

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to take note of the revised scheme names for order placement and portfolio tracking on the platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 24, 2026
- Effective Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating scheme nomenclature on trading terminals and investor portfolios.
