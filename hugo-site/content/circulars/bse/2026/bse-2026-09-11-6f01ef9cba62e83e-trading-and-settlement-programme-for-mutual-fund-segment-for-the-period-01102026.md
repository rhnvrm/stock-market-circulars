## Summary

BSE has released the trading and settlement schedule for the mutual fund segment for the period from October 1, 2026, to October 31, 2026. The circular outlines settlement dates, fund pay-ins, and payouts for non-liquid and liquid mutual fund subscriptions as well as redemptions.

## Key Points

- Covers settlement cycles for non-liquid and liquid mutual fund subscriptions for October 2026.
- Specifies settlement dates ranging from settlement number 2627126 (October 1, 2026) to 2627145 (October 30, 2026).
- Funds pay-out to AMCs for non-liquid transactions will occur before 3:00 PM on T day.
- Funds pay-out for NFO (MF) schemes will happen on T day based on fund confirmations received by BSECL till 6:00 PM on T day.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and AMCs must adhere to the specified settlement dates and pay-in/pay-out timings for mutual fund transactions during October 2026.

- Period End: October 31, 2026
