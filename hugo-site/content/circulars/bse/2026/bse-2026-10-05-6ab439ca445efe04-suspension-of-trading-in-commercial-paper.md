## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of their commercial papers. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of these companies starting from the respective ex-dates and record dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Date: 05 October 2026
- Segment: Debt
- Department: Listing Operations
- Involves commercial papers from issuers including Paisalo Digital Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Capri Global Capital Limited, Muthoot Fincorp Limited, Nuvama Clearing Services Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, and 360 ONE WAM Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

[N/A]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the Commercial Papers of the specified companies on or after their respective record and ex-dates.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational suspension of trading in commercial papers during redemption record dates to facilitate orderly settlement and prevent secondary market transactions post-record date.
