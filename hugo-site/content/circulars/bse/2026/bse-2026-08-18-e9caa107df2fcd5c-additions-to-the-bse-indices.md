## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of newly listed companies to its indices. Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited will be added to the BSE IPO index effective August 20, 2026, and Sham Foam Limited will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited (Exchange ticker: 544868) is added to the BSE IPO index effective at the open of August 20, 2026.
- Sham Foam Limited (Exchange ticker: 544869) is added to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open of August 19, 2026.
- These actions are included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).

None.

## Important Dates

- August 18, 2026: Notice issuance date.
- August 19, 2026: Effective date for Sham Foam Limited index addition.
- August 20, 2026: Effective date for Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited index addition.

Standard index rebalancing/addition update with negligible market impact.
