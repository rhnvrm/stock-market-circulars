## Summary

Trading members are informed that effective from Wednesday, October 7, 2026, the equity shares of Black Opal Consultants Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE exchange in the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Black Opal Consultants Limited
- Scrip ID: BLACKOPAL
- Scrip Code: 544972
- ISIN No: INE0M3101012
- Number of Securities: 10,577,190 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 197/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 187/-)
- Market Lot: 600 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: October 5, 2026
- Market Maker: Share India Securities Limited
- Registrar: Skyline Financial Services Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of SME equity shares to dealing on the Exchange in the 'MT' Group of Securities.
- Scrip placed in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities will be traded strictly in dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Minimum market lot of 600 equity shares applies.
- Corporate action executed for locking in shares in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: October 5, 2026
- Listing and Effective Date: October 7, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): October 7, 2026
- Trade-for-Trade segment duration: October 7, 2026 onwards for 10 trading days

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity to public shareholders of Black Opal Consultants Limited through listing on the BSE SME platform, with initial trading subject to standard trade-for-trade surveillance measures.
