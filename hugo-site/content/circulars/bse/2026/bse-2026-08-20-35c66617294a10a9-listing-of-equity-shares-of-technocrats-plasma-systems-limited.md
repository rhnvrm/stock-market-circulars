## Summary

Effective from Friday, August 21, 2026, the equity shares of Technocrats Plasma Systems Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE SME platform under the 'MT' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544877
- ISIN No: INE19QK01022
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: TECHNOCRAT
- Issue Price: Rs. 132/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/- and Premium Rs. 122/-)
- Total Securities: 1,75,00,000 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1,000 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: August 19, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

As per SEBI circular dated January 20, 2012, the scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

Securities of the company will be traded only in dematerialised form. Corporate action for lock-in period has been executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 19, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: August 21, 2026
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Technocrats Plasma Systems Limited equity shares on the BSE SME segment with standard trade-for-trade surveillance for the initial 10 trading sessions.
