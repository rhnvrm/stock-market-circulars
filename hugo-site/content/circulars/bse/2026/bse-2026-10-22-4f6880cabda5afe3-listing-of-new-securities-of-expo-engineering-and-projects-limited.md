## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of 31,45,715 new equity shares of Expo Engineering and Projects Limited (Scrip Code: 526614) effective from October 23, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to promoters and non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants at an issue price of Rs. 70/- each (face value Rs. 4/- plus premium of Rs. 66/-).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Expo Engineering and Projects Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 526614
- **ISIN:** INE561D01025
- **Security Details:** 31,45,715 equity shares of Rs. 4/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 66/- (Issue Price: Rs. 70/-) pursuant to warrant conversion on a preferential basis.
- **Trading Date:** Permitted to trade on the Exchange with effect from Friday, October 23, 2026.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Allotment Details

- **Allotment Date 11/08/2026:** 13,32,856 shares (Dist. Nos. 22796401 - 24129256)
- **Allotment Date 20/08/2026:** 18,12,859 shares (Dist. Nos. 24129257 - 25942115)

## Lock-in Details

- 13,32,856 shares (Dist. Nos. 22796401-24129256): Locked-in up to 30/05/2028
- 1,41,429 shares (Dist. Nos. 24129257-24270685): Locked-in up to 31/05/2028
- 14,14,285 shares (Dist. Nos. 24270686-25684970): Locked-in up to 30/05/2027
- 85,715 shares (Dist. Nos. 25684971-25770685): Locked-in up to 31/05/2027
- 85,715 shares (Dist. Nos. 25770686-25856400): Locked-in up to 30/05/2027
- 85,715 shares (Dist. Nos. 25856401-25942115): Locked-in up to 31/05/2027

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard corporate listing notice for preferential shares.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to lock-in periods and trading parameters as specified by BSE.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 22, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** May 30, 2027 to May 30, 2028

## Impact Assessment

The listing of preferential shares increases the total paid-up equity capital of Expo Engineering and Projects Limited, expanding shareholder base according to the warrant conversion terms, with specific lock-in restrictions ensuring orderly market trading.
