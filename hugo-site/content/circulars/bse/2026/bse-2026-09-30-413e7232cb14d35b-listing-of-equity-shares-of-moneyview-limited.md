## Summary

Trading Members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Thursday, October 1, 2026, the equity shares of Moneyview Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealing on the Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544953
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: MONEYVIEW
- ISIN No: INE0PTN01011
- Number of Securities: 1,760,231,268 Equity Shares of Rs. 1/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 34/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 1/- and premium of Rs. 33/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 29, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities of the company will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- The scrip will be a part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Thursday, October 1, 2026.
- Lock-in details and shareholding patterns are maintained as per Annexure I and Annexure II.
- Corporate actions for demat lock-in have been executed in the depository's records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 29, 2026
- Listing and Trading Effective Date: October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates public market trading and liquidity for Moneyview Limited shares on the BSE platform.
