## Summary

The circular communicates updates regarding the implementation of Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967, reflecting amendments made by the UN Security Council Sanctions Committee to 22 entries on its sanctions list on 10 March 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Council Committee established pursuant to resolution 1988 (2011) enacted amendments to 22 entries on its sanctions list.
- Updates involve individuals and entities subject to assets freeze, travel ban, and arms embargo.
- Entails compliance obligations for market participants and intermediaries under Section 51A of UAPA, 1967.

## Regulatory Changes

- Incorporation of latest UNSC Sanctions List amendments into domestic compliance frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and intermediaries must screen their databases against the updated UNSC sanctions list entries and apply necessary asset freezes and restrictions where applicable.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 10 March 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures adherence to international counter-terrorism standards and statutory obligations under Indian law.
