## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding revised terms for debt securities of Mumbai Urja Marg Ltd, effective from Monday, August 17, 2026. The changes include a shift from unsecured NCDs to secured NCDs and an ISIN update.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 977069
- Old ISIN: INE0F6K08071 (Existing Terms: Unsecured NCDs)
- New ISIN: INE0F6K07024 (Revised Terms: Secured NCDs)
- Effective Date: August 17, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Mode of security changed from unsecured non-convertible debentures (NCDs) to secured NCDs.
- ISIN has been updated to reflect the revised terms.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are requested to note the revised ISIN and security terms for trading and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants must update their records with the new ISIN (INE0F6K07024) and secured status for Mumbai Urja Marg Ltd's debt securities.
