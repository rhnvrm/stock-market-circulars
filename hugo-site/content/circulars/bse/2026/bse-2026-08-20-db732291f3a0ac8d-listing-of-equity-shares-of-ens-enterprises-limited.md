## Summary

Effective from Friday, August 21, 2026, the equity shares of ENS Enterprises Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will operate in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days pursuant to SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code: 544876
- ISIN No: INE23ER01017
- Issue Price: Rs. 92/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 82/-)
- Total Securities: 13,594,912 Equity Shares
- Market Lot: 1,200 Equity Shares
- Date of Allotment: August 19, 2026
- Market Maker: ACME Capital Market Limited
- Registrar: Abhipra Capital Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines.
- Scrip placed in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure trades comply with the minimum market lot size of 1,200 shares.
- Necessary corporate actions for lock-in marking have been executed in depository records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 19, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enables public trading of ENS Enterprises Limited shares on the BSE SME platform with standard liquidity and settlement constraints for newly listed securities.
