## Summary

BSE has announced the revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares of Jiya Eco-Products Ltd (Scrip Code: 539225), effective from September 08, 2026. Trading will resume in the "XT" group pursuant to SEBI Master Circular guidelines.

## Key Points

- Suspension in trading of equity shares of Jiya Eco-Products Ltd is revoked w.e.f. September 08, 2026.
- Trading will be resumed in the "XT" group pursuant to SEBI Master Circular No. HO/49/14/14(7)2025-CFD-POD2/I/3762/2026 dated January 30, 2026.
- The Information Memorandum of the company will be available on the Exchange's website under Corporates → Corporate Filings → Information Memorandum → Revocation.
- The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) for IPOs and Relisted Scrips on September 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Resumption of trading executed in accordance with SEBI Master Circular on revocation of suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to take note of the resumption of trading and participation in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Revocation / Resumption of Trading: September 08, 2026
- Notice Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members can resume trading in the equity shares of Jiya Eco-Products Ltd under the XT group starting September 08, 2026, following participation in the Special Pre-open Session.
