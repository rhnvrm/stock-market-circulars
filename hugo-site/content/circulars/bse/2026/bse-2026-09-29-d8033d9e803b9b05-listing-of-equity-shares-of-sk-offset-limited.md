## Summary

Trading Members are informed that effective Wednesday, September 30, 2026, the Equity Shares of S.K. Offset Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE in the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** S.K. Offset Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544947
- **ISIN:** INE2MKZ01017
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 125/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 115/-)
- **Number of Securities:** 7,743,320 Equity Shares
- **Market Lot:** 1,000 Equity Shares
- **Segment:** SME (MT Group)
- **Date of Allotment:** September 28, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in terms of SEBI circular CIR/MRD/DP/02/2012 dated January 20, 2012.
- Trading will be conducted strictly in dematerialised form pursuant to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note participation in the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Wednesday, September 30, 2026.
- Corporate action has been executed for lock-in marking in depository records.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** September 28, 2026
- **Listing/Effective Date:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public market trading and liquidity for S.K. Offset Limited shares on the SME exchange platform.
