## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the revision of price bands for 22 scrips. These changes are effective from September 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 22 scrips affected by price band revisions.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is September 18, 2026.
- Price bands range from 2% to 20% depending on the specific security.

## Regulatory Changes

Price bands of specified scrips have been modified from their existing levels as part of routine surveillance measures by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of the revised price bands and ensure compliance while placing orders in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 17, 2026
- Effective Date: September 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading participants dealing in the listed scrips must update their trading systems to reflect the new price band limits, minimizing execution errors due to out-of-band orders.
