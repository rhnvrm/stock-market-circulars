## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for specified securities listed in Annexure I. The surveillance framework introduces enhanced margin requirements effective October 06, 2026, to manage market volatility and protect investors.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I attract an applicable margin rate of 50% or the existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage II attract an applicable margin rate of 100% or the existing margin (whichever is higher).
- Surveillance actions apply to all open positions as on October 05, 2026, and new positions created from October 06, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is strictly a market surveillance measure and not an adverse action against the companies.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of enhanced margin requirements under the revised Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% to 100%) for all open positions and new trades in the securities specified in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 01, 2026
- Effective Date: October 06, 2026 (applicable on open positions as of October 05, 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Market participants trading in securities listed under ST-ASM Stage I and II will experience increased capital requirements due to higher margin obligations, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes in those specific scrips.
