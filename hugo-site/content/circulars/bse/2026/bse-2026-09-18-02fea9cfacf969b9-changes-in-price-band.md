## Summary

BSE has announced revisions to the price bands of several scrips trading on the exchange, effective from September 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised for various equity scrips ranging from 2% to 20%.
- Effective date for the revised price bands is September 21, 2026.
- Affected scrips include ADC India Communications Ltd, Adcon Capital Services Ltd, Aptech Ltd, and numerous others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of price band limits for specified scrips to manage market volatility and prevent abnormal price fluctuations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must update their trading systems to reflect the revised price band percentages for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 18, 2026
- Effective Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified scrips will be subject to the newly established price limits, influencing intraday price movements and order execution.
