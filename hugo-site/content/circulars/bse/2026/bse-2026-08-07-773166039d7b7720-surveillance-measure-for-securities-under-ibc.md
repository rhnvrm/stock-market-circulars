## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the application of Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective August 10, 2026, specific securities satisfying the criteria have been placed under or moved out of the IBC Surveillance Framework.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the criteria under the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per IBC are placed under the Additional Surveillance Measure framework effective August 10, 2026.
- A separate list of securities moving out of the IBC Framework effective the same date is provided in Annexure II.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is available in Annexure III.
- The framework operates in conjunction with all other prevailing surveillance measures imposed by the exchange.
- Shortlisting is strictly a market surveillance measure and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities satisfying the IRP criteria under the IBC are subjected to surveillance actions and periodic reviews.
- Price bands for scrips moving out of the framework are reinstated to their original bands, subject to not being part of any other surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the list of securities added to or removed from the IBC surveillance framework and adjust trading parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 10, 2026
- Notice Date: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants dealing in affected scrips must account for surveillance restrictions and price band adjustments. Clarifications can be sought via email from the exchange surveillance department.
