## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific T-bills and Government Securities that are maturing on the redemption date. Trading members are advised not to deal in these securities starting August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice No: 20260817-8, dated August 17, 2026.
- Department: Listing Operations.
- Matured Securities:
  1. Scrip Code: 805108, Particulars: 364TB20826, ISIN: IN002025Z211
  2. Scrip Code: 805217, Particulars: 91TB20826, ISIN: IN002026X073

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational suspension due to the maturity of government debt instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are instructed to refrain from dealing in the specified T-bills and Government Securities effective from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date of Suspension: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact restricted to trading members holding or dealing in the specified maturing government securities.
