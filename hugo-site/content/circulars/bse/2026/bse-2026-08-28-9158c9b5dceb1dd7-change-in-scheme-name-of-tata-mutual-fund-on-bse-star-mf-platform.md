## Summary

TATA Mutual Fund has changed the scheme names for five of its schemes on the BSE StAR MF Platform with immediate effect, as communicated via Notice No. 20260828-34.

## Key Points

- TATA TREASURY ADVANTAGE FUND is renamed to TATA ULTRA SHORT TO SHORT TERM FUND.
- TATA SHORT TERM BOND FUND is renamed to TATA SHORT TERM FUND.
- TATA FLOATING RATE FUND is renamed to TATA FLOATING INTEREST RATES FUND.
- TATA ELSS FUND is renamed to TATA ELSS - TAX SAVER FUND.
- TATA GILT SECURITIES FUND is renamed to TATA GILT FUND.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors need to note the revised scheme names for future transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 28 August 2026
- Effective Date: With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact. Existing unitholders and investors must use the updated scheme names when placing orders on the BSE StAR MF platform.
