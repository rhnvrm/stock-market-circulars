## Summary

BSE has announced the addition of HD Fire Protect Limited (Exchange ticker: 544994) to the BSE IPO Index. The inclusion will be effective at the open of Friday, October 23, 2026, following its listing on the exchange on October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- HD Fire Protect Limited (Ticker: 544994) is listed on BSE effective October 21, 2026.
- The stock will be added to the BSE IPO Index effective at the open of October 23, 2026.
- This action is included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE).
