## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission for trading of 43,26,507 equity shares of Aditya Infotech Ltd with effect from September 29, 2026, following a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Aditya Infotech Ltd
- Scrip Code: 544466
- ISIN: INE819V01029
- Number of Shares: 43,26,507 Equity shares of face value Rs. 1/- each
- Issue Price: Rs. 3,467/- per share
- Date of Allotment: September 25, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: September 29, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 118300436 to 122626942

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard corporate listing procedure for securities allotted through QIP.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are informed about the admission of new shares ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 25, 2026
- Listing / Trading Commencement Date: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity share capital of Aditya Infotech Ltd and expands its institutional shareholding base following the QIP issuance at Rs. 3,467/- per share.
