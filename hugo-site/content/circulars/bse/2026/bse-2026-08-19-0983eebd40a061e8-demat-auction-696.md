## Summary

BSE Limited has announced Demat Auction 696 (Rolling Settlement No. DR-696/2026-27), inviting offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction. The auction sessions, matching, and associated reporting timelines are scheduled for August 19, 2026, with pay-in scheduled for August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Settlement Number:** DR-696/2026-27
- **Auction Offer Entry Timing:** 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on August 19, 2026
- **Auction Matching Timing:** 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on August 19, 2026
- **Delivery Statement & Report:** 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m. on August 19, 2026
- **Money Statement & Final Reports:** 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m. on August 19, 2026
- **Pay-in Date:** Thursday, August 20, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; standard auction procedure in accordance with exchange settlement guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should take utmost care while punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.
- Trading members must refer to the cutoff rate appearing in the "Auction offer Entry Screen" and account for any corporate actions (Ex-Rights/Ex-Bonus/Ex-Split).

## Important Dates

- **Auction Date:** August 19, 2026
- **Pay-in Date:** August 20, 2026 (08:00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

- Standard operational process for clearing short deliveries in rolling settlements. Affects only trading members and clients involved in settlement DR-696/2026-27.
