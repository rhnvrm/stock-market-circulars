## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The circular outlines securities satisfying inclusion criteria, moving to lower stages (Stage I), and transitioning to the Trade-for-Trade segment along with applicable margin requirements and price bands.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying inclusion criteria under ESM attract a minimum 100% margin effective October 07, 2026, on all open positions as on October 06, 2026, and new positions created from October 07, 2026.
- Certain securities continue in the ESM framework but move to lower Stage I effective October 06, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II are placed under Trade-for-Trade with a 2% price band and Periodic Call Auction effective October 06, 2026.
- Securities qualifying under ESM are shifted from the Rolling Settlement segment to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective October 07, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ESM is strictly for market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the concerned entities.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance framework updates including stage reclassifications, 100% margin requirements, and shifting eligible securities from rolling settlement to trade-for-trade.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with 100% margin obligations on open and new positions, adhere to trade-for-trade settlement rules, and respect applicable price bands for securities listed in the annexes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 05, 2026
- Effective Date for Stage Moves and Price Bands: October 06, 2026
- Effective Date for 100% Margin and Trade-for-Trade Settlement: October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Impacts market participants trading in securities listed under the ESM framework by enforcing tighter margin norms, trade-for-trade settlement, and price band restrictions.
