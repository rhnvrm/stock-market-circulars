## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the change in scheme names for seven schemes of Canara Robeco Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective from August 26, 2026.

## Key Points

- Canara Robeco Mutual Fund has updated the names of several of its mutual fund schemes.
- Changes take effect on the BSE StAR MF Platform starting August 26, 2026.
- Affected schemes include savings, short duration, income, dynamic bond, equity hybrid, infrastructure, and ELSS tax saver funds.

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable (administrative scheme naming update).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and distributors should update their systems with the revised scheme names to avoid transaction errors.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Notice Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal operational impact; ensures proper alignment with revised fund nomenclature on the BSE StAR MF platform.
