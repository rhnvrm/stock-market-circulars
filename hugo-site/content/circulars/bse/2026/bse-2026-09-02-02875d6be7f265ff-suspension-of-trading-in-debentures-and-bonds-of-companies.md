## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds of several companies. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified debentures and bonds as companies have fixed record dates for interest payment or redemption.

## Key Points

- Notice covers multiple companies including ICL Fincorp Limited, HDFC Bank Ltd., Aadhar Housing Finance Limited, Sammaan Finserve Limited, and others.
- Trading members are instructed not to deal in the specified debentures and bonds of the companies.
- Record dates and ex-dates span across early September 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure that they do not deal in the debentures and bonds of the listed companies on or after the respective ex-dates.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 02 Sep 2026
- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: 04/09/2026, 05/09/2026, 07/09/2026, 08/09/2026, and 09/09/2026 depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

Temporary suspension of trading in specific debt instruments for the purpose of interest payment and redemption processing.
