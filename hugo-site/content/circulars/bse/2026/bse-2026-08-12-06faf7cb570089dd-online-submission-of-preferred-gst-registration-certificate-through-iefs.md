## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited (BSECL) has introduced an online facility for clearing members to submit their Preferred GST Registration Certificates through the Electronic Filing System (IEFS). This transition from physical/manual processes to a digital portal is intended to improve administrative workflow and operational efficiency.

## Key Points

- **Online Submission**: Submission of Preferred GST Registration Certificates can now be completed online via the BSECL IEFS platform.
- **Portal Link**: The digital filing system can be accessed directly at https://iefs.bseindia.in/.
- **User Manual**: A comprehensive User Manual has been provided as an attachment to guide members through the portal's navigation and submission requirements.
- **Support Channels**: Clearing members requiring support can reach out via email at iccl.membership@icclindia.com or call 022-22725825, 022-22725671, or 022-22728365.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no changes to existing taxation or regulatory requirements under GST laws. The update only changes the operational process and mechanism for submitting these files to BSECL, digitizing an offline flow.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Process Transition**: Clearing members seeking to submit or update their Preferred GST Registration Certificates must use the IEFS portal.
- **Advisory**: Members must carefully review the attached User Manual to ensure correct navigation and compliance with standard filing requirements before submitting their request on the portal.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: August 12, 2026 (the online facility is active and available with immediate effect).

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: High administrative convenience for clearing members. Transitioning to online filing reduces physical documentation handling and accelerates certificate verification times.
- **Market Impact**: None. This is an administrative adjustment and does not affect security prices, trading, or market liquidity.
