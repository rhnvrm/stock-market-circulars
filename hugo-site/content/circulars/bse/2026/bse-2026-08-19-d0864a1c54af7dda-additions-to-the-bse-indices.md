## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of three newly listed companies to its indices. Alpine Texworld Limited and Shiprocket Limited will be added to the BSE IPO index effective at the open of August 21, 2026. Q&T Foods Limited will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open of August 20, 2026.

## Key Points

- Alpine Texworld Limited (Ticker: 544870) and Shiprocket Limited (Ticker: 544871) added to BSE IPO index effective August 21, 2026.
- Q&T Foods Limited (Ticker: 544872) added to BSE SME IPO index effective August 20, 2026.
- Actions included in the end-of-day corporate action file (*.SDE) on August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None specific to market participants beyond routine index tracking updates.

## Important Dates

- August 19, 2026: Notice issuance and inclusion in end-of-day corporate action file.
- August 20, 2026: Effective date for Q&T Foods Limited index addition.
- August 21, 2026: Effective date for Alpine Texworld Limited and Shiprocket Limited index additions.

## Impact Assessment

Routine index reconstitution update with minimal direct operational impact outside of index-tracking funds and portfolios.
