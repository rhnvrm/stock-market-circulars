## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 11,24,375 new equity shares of Shri Gang Industries & Allied Products Limited (Scrip Code - 523309) with effect from Tuesday, July 28, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters pursuant to the conversion of Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS).

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Shri Gang Industries & Allied Products Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 523309
- **Security Details:** 11,24,375 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 66/- (Issue Price: Rs. 76/-) on a preferential basis to non-promoters.
- **Reason:** Conversion of Compulsorily Convertible Preference Shares (CCPS).
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with the old equity shares of the company.
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 20114681 to 21239055
- **Date of Allotment:** 06/06/2026
- **ISIN:** INE241V01018

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members may note the listing and trading of the aforementioned securities effective from July 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** July 28, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** June 6, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate listing update regarding preferential allotment and conversion of CCPS for the company's equity segment.
