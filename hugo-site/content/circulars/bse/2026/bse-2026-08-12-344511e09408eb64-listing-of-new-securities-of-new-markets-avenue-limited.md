## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the listing and trading approval for 53,00,000 new equity shares of Rs. 10/- each of New Markets Avenue Limited (Scrip Code: 508867, ISIN: INE800K01014) with effect from Thursday, August 13, 2026. These shares were issued to non-promoter entities on a preferential basis upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Securities Listed**: 53,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each.
- **Issuer**: New Markets Avenue Limited (Scrip Code: 508867, ISIN: INE800K01014).
- **Allotment Date**: July 4, 2026.
- **Issue Price**: Rs. 10/- per share (at par).
- **Allottees**: Non-promoter entities, on a preferential basis, pursuant to the conversion of warrants.
- **Fungibility**: These shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company.
- **Distinctive Numbers**: 4940001 to 10240000.
- **Lock-in Details**: The 53,00,000 shares are subject to a lock-in period until February 27, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes introduced by this notice. It represents a routine listing operation under existing stock exchange and corporate governance rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Trading Members**: No active compliance action is required other than noting the listing of these shares and their eligibility for trading.
- **For Issuer**: Ensure adherence to ongoing disclosure and listing agreements with the stock exchange, specifically maintaining records of the locked-in shares until the specified expiry date.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment**: July 4, 2026
- **Trading Commencement Date**: August 13, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date**: February 27, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market/Operational Impact**: Low. The listing increases the total paid-up share capital of New Markets Avenue Limited. However, because these shares are subject to lock-in requirements until February 27, 2027, there will be no immediate increase in the tradeable public float, thereby mitigating any potential immediate dilution or downward pressure on the stock price.
