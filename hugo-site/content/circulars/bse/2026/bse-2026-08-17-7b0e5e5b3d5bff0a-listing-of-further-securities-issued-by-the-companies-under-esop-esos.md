## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a notice informing trading members about the listing and admission for trading of further new securities issued by 15 companies under their Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) or Employee Stock Option Scheme (ESOS). The trading for these securities is effective from Tuesday, August 18, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total companies issuing further securities: 15
- Effective date for trading: August 18, 2026
- Securities include issuances from companies such as HDFC Bank Limited, Dr. Lal Pathlabs Limited, Polycab India Limited, RBL Bank Limited, and others under ESOP / ESOS.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants are requested to take note of the listing of further securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Effective Date for Trading: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard capital increase and stock listing procedure resulting from employee stock option exercises across various listed entities.
