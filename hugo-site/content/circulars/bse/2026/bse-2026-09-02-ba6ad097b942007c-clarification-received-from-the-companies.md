## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a surveillance notice regarding significant price and volume movements observed in specific scrips. To ensure investors have access to relevant information, the Exchange sought clarifications from the respective companies.

## Key Points

- **Clarifications Received:** Replies have been received and attached from 9 companies, including 3C IT Solutions and Telecoms (India) Ltd, 7Seas Entertainment Ltd, Aayush Wellness Ltd, Godrej Agrovet Ltd, Identixweb Ltd, Kalyani Forge Ltd, National Fittings Ltd, Poojaa Precision Engg. Ltd, and Royal Sense Ltd.
- **Clarifications Awaited:** Replies are still pending from 12 companies, including Aryan Share and Stockbrokers Ltd, Bharat Global Developers Ltd, CMX Holdings Ltd, Deep Polymers Ltd, GS Auto International Ltd, Infobeans Technologies Ltd, Lead Financial Services Ltd, Mahesh Developers Ltd, Phaarmasia Ltd, PVP Ventures Ltd, Shah Alloys Ltd, and Triumph International Finance India Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Listed companies observed with significant price and volume movements are required to provide timely clarifications to the Exchange under surveillance guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 02 Sep 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to market participants regarding scrips experiencing unusual trading activity and highlights companies pending regulatory compliance responses.
