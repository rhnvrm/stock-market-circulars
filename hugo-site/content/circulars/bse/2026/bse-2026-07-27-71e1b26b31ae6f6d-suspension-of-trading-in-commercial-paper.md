## Summary

Trading members of the exchange are informed that multiple companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of commercial papers. Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of the specified companies starting from the respective ex-dates and record dates.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in commercial papers for various companies due to redemption record dates.
- Affected companies include Standard Chartered Securities (India) Limited, Vivriti Capital Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited, ONGC Petro Additions Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Network 18 Media & Investments Limited, 360 ONE Alternates Asset Management Limited, 360 ONE Prime Limited, 360 ONE WAM Limited, and Axis Finance Limited.
- Record dates and ex-dates span from July 30, 2026, to August 10, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard listing operations and trading suspension procedure for commercial paper redemption.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must avoid dealing in the specified commercial papers of the mentioned companies on and after the ex-date.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: July 30, 2026, August 7, 2026, and August 10, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Prevents trading in commercial papers that are approaching redemption, protecting market participants and ensuring orderly settlement.
