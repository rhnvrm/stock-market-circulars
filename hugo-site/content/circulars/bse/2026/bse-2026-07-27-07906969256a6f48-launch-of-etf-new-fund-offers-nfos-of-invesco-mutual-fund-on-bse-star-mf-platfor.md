## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the launch of New Fund Offer (NFO) schemes for Invesco Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from July 28, 2026, to August 11, 2026. The tentative allotment date for the schemes is August 17, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Schemes Included:** 
  - INVESCO INDIA NIFTY BANK ETF
  - INVESCO INDIA NIFTY BANK ETF - DIRECT PLAN
  - INVESCO INDIA BSE SENSEX ETF
  - INVESCO INDIA BSE SENSEX ETF - DIRECT PLAN
- **NFO Period:** July 28, 2026, to August 11, 2026 (extended till midnight on August 11, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled and reported with an 'N' flag to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on August 17, 2026, to be eligible for allotment.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Start Date:** July 28, 2026
- **NFO End Date:** August 11, 2026
- **Tentative Allotment Date:** August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors and members to participate in the new ETF fund offers of Invesco Mutual Fund through the BSE StAR MF platform with clear operational timelines.
