## Summary

BSE has issued a notice advising all registered intermediaries to ensure strict compliance with various directions and guidelines issued by the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) and communicated via SEBI. The notice covers mobile number revocation list integration, permitted usage of numbering series, pre-tagging of variable fields in SMS content templates, and consumer awareness campaigns.

## Key Points

- Integration with DoT's Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL) for periodic reconciliation and risk/authentication workflows.
- Strict usage of the 1600 numbering series exclusively for service and transactional calls, prohibiting promotional usage.
- Compliance with TRAI's Direction dated 18.11.2025 regarding pre-tagging of variable fields in SMS Content Templates before blocker mode activation.
- Execution of consumer awareness campaigns regarding 1600 and 140 numbering series.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of TRAI guidelines regarding telecom communication security, template scrubbing, and caller ID integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

1. Integrate with DoT's MNRL and establish periodic customer record reconciliation processes.
2. Ensure 1600 series is used strictly for service/transactional calls.
3. Review and pre-tag all variable fields in SMS content templates used during the preceding 90 days before blocker mode activation.
4. Undertake consumer awareness campaigns on 1600 and 140 numbering series.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 18 September 2026
- Reference to TRAI Direction Date: 18 November 2025

## Impact Assessment

Non-compliance will result in the non-delivery of legitimate SMS communications once blocker mode is activated by access providers, potentially disrupting critical service and transactional notifications.
