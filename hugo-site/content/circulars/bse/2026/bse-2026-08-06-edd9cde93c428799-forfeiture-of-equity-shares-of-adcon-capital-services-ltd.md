## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that Adcon Capital Services Ltd (Scrip Code: 539506) has forfeited 13,30,81,360 equity shares issued pursuant to a rights issue. The shares were partly paid-up (Re. 1/- each, distinctive numbers from 198354641 to 331436000) and were forfeited due to the non-payment of allotment and call monies.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Adcon Capital Services Ltd
- **Scrip Code:** 539506
- **Action:** Forfeiture of equity shares
- **Number of Shares Forfeited:** 13,30,81,360 equity shares
- **Details:** Re. 1/- each, partly paid-up, bearing distinctive numbers from 198354641 to 331436000
- **Reason:** Non-payment of allotment/call monies

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the share forfeiture and adjust their records/systems accordingly regarding the scrip.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 06 August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reduces the paid-up equity share capital base for the non-paying allottees and impacts the overall share distribution of Adcon Capital Services Ltd.
