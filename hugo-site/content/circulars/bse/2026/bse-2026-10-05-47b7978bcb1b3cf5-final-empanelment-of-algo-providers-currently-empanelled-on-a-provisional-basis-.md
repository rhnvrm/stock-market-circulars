## Summary

BSE Limited has granted final empanelment to eight algorithmic trading providers that were previously holding provisional empanelment status. These vendors are authorized to provide algorithmic trading solutions to BSE trading members.

## Key Points

- Eight algorithmic trading providers transitioned from provisional to final empanelment upon fulfilling exchange conditions.
- Listed vendors include AlgoBulls Technologies, Quantiply Technologies, Quick Algoplus, Simply Algo Fintech, Stratzy Fintech, Neutrino Trading, Multiicon Ideotechnology, and Options Scalping Private Limited.
- Applicable with immediate effect.
- The empanelled list is published on the BSE website.

## Regulatory Changes

- Finalization of empanelment status for algorithmic trading software and solution vendors offering services to exchange members.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members utilizing automated trading platforms must engage with empanelled vendors in compliance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05 Oct 2026
- Effective Date: Immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances regulatory oversight and standardizes algorithmic trading offerings available to BSE trading members through vetted technology providers.
