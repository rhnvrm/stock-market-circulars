## Summary

Equity shares of SJP Ultrasonics Limited are listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE SME platform effective October 8, 2026, under Scrip Code 544980 and Scrip ID SJP. The securities will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for the first 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** SJP Ultrasonics Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544980
- **Scrip ID / Symbol:** SJP
- **ISIN:** INE0S4601017
- **Listing Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 67/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 57/-)
- **Total Securities:** 12,940,000 Equity Shares
- **Market Lot:** 2,000 shares
- **Group:** MT (Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days)
- **Market Maker:** Aftertrade Broking Private Limited
- **Registrar:** Maashitla Securities Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard listing procedure under SEBI and BSE SME guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note that securities will be traded in dematerialised form only.
- Minimum market lot is 2,000 equity shares.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.
- Necessary corporate actions for lock-in periods have been executed in the depository records.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** October 7, 2026
- **Date of Allotment:** October 6, 2026
- **Listing Date:** October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides public market access and liquidity for SJP Ultrasonics Limited on the BSE SME platform, with initial trading subject to standard trade-for-trade surveillance measures.
