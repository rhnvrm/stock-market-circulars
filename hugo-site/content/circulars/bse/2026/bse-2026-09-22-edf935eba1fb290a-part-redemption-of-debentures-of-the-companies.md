## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about record dates and ex-dates for interest payment and part redemption of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) / Bonds for several companies. Trading in these debentures will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice covers multiple companies undergoing part redemption and interest payment on NCDs/Bonds.
- Trading will resume with reduced face values starting from the respective ex-dates.
- Affected entities include Power Grid Corporation of India Limited, Ugro Capital Limited, Keertana Finserv Limited, Mufin Green Finance Limited, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, VR Dakshin Private Limited, Niwas Housing Finance Limited, and Cube Highways Trust.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note the record dates, ex-dates, and reduced face values to ensure proper execution and settlement of trades in the affected debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 22/09/2026
- Record Dates: 26/09/2026, 27/09/2026, 28/09/2026, and 30/09/2026
- Ex-Dates: 25/09/2026, 28/09/2026, and 30/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the specified NCDs and bonds will receive part redemption payments, and subsequent trading will reflect the adjusted face values.
