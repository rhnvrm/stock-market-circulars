## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the availability of the Draft Offer Document filed by TNA Solutions Limited on August 08, 2026. The document is hosted on the SME website for public comments for a period of at least 21 days as specified under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TNA Solutions Limited
- Filing Date: August 08, 2026
- Notice Date: August 10, 2026
- Hosting Platform: BSE SME website (https://www.bsesme.com/PublicIssues/SMEIPODRHP.aspx)
- Public Comments Email: equity.ipo@bseindia.com

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance under Schedule VI of the ICDR Regulations for SME IPO Draft Offer Documents.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stakeholders and public can submit comments on the draft offer document within 21 days from the date of the notice.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 10, 2026
- Draft Filing Date: August 08, 2026
- Comment Deadline: Within 21 days from notice date

## Impact Assessment

- Provides transparency and invites public feedback on the proposed SME Initial Public Offering of TNA Solutions Limited.
