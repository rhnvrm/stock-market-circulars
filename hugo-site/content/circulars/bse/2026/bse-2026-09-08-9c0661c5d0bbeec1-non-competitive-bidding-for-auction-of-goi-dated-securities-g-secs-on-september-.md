## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding facility for the auction of Government of India (GoI) dated securities scheduled on September 11, 2026, based on the RBI press release dated September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Three dated securities are offered through the auction: 6.20% GS 2029 (Re-issue), 6.57% GS 2033 (Re-issue), and New GS 2056.
- Bidding will take place through the NCB-GSec module of the Exchange's iBBS web-based system.
- Bid collection for members starts on September 08, 2026 (24 hours availability) and ends on September 11, 2026, till 8.00 am.
- Bid collection for direct investors ends on September 10, 2026, till 5.00 pm.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must submit bids through the NCB-GSec module within the specified timelines and ensure adherence to minimum subscription units and amounts.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** September 08, 2026
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor):** September 10, 2026 (till 5:00 PM)
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** September 11, 2026 (till 8:00 AM)
- **Auction Date:** September 11, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** September 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates orderly participation of trading members and direct investors in the Government Securities primary auction market.
