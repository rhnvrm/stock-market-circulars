## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that the price bands for 16 scrips have been revised from their existing levels, effective August 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Total of 16 scrips affected by price band revisions.
- Price bands revised to specific limits (e.g., 2%, 5%, and 20%) depending on the scrip.
- Effective date of the change is August 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Revision of price limits for designated securities under surveillance/trading guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure orders placed in the affected scrips comply with the newly revised price bands.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 11, 2026
- Effective Date: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Limits daily price movement ranges for the specified equities to manage market volatility.
