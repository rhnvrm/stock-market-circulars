## Summary

BSE has notified a change in ISINs for the New Fund Offer (NFO) schemes of WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, updating details previously issued in Notice No. 20260925-52.

## Key Points

- WhiteOak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active Fof - Equity Regular Plan - Growth: Revised ISIN is INF03VN01AO0 (earlier INF1J2R01197).
- WhiteOak Capital - Diversified Equity Small Cap Active Fof - Equity Direct Plan - Growth: Revised ISIN is INF03VN01AN2 (earlier INF1J2R01189).
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 19, 2026.
- Members are required to execute all transactions using the revised ISINs with immediate effect.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of ISIN numbers for specific mutual fund NFO schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and distributors must ensure that all orders and transactions under the aforementioned NFO schemes use the updated ISINs immediately.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026.
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 19, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Purely operational update ensuring correct identification and settlement of mutual fund units on the BSE StAR MF platform.
