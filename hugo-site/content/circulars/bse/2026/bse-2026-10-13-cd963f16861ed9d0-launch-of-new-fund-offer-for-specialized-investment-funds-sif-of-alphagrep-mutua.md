## Summary

ALPHAGREP MUTUAL FUND is launching the New Fund Offer (NFO) for EMINENCE HYBRID LONG-SHORT FUND (SIF) along with SIP facility on the BSE StAR MF Platform from October 14, 2026, to October 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scheme: EMINENCE HYBRID LONG-SHORT FUND (SIF) across Regular and Direct plans (Growth, IDCW Reinvestment, IDCW Payout).
- NFO Period: October 14, 2026, to October 28, 2026 (extended till midnight on October 28, 2026, for Lumpsum purchases).
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 04, 2026.
- ISIN Details:
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF2VOA30052
  - Regular Plan - Growth: INF2VOA30045
  - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout: INF2VOA30060
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment: INF2VOA30029
  - Direct Plan - Growth: INF2VOA30011
  - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout: INF2VOA30037

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag shall be reported to the RTA/AMC.
- Transactions with initiated payment remain pending until 3:00 PM on the last day of funds pay-out or T+5 day, whichever is earlier.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on November 04, 2026.
- Cheque and OTM payments are accepted subject to clear funds receipt by 6:00 PM on November 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- NFO Start Date: October 14, 2026
- NFO End Date: October 28, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: November 04, 2026
- Funds Pay-in Deadline for Allotment: November 04, 2026 (6:00 PM)

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification providing members and investors with structural details, ISINs, and transaction guidelines for participating in the Alphagrep Mutual Fund NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
