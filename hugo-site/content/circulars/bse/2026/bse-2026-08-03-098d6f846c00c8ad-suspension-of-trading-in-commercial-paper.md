## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members about the suspension of trading in commercial papers of specific companies due to the fixation of record dates for redemption.

## Key Points

- Trading members are advised not to deal in the commercial papers of Nuvama Clearing Services Limited and DSP Finance Private Limited starting from the specified record/ex-date.
- Record Date and Ex-Date for both companies is set as 04-08-2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must refrain from executing trades in the commercial papers of the mentioned companies on and from the record date.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** 04-08-2026
- **Ex-Date:** 04-08-2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding these commercial papers will undergo redemption processes as scheduled, with secondary market trading suspended.
