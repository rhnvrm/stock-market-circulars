## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that trading is permitted in 1,14,034 new equity shares of TIL Ltd. (Scrip Code: 505196) with effect from Thursday, September 24, 2026, following the conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up on a rights basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TIL Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 505196
- ISIN: INE806C01018
- Securities: 1,14,034 Equity shares pursuant to conversion of partly paid shares to fully paid up of Re.10/- each issued on rights basis.
- Distinctive Numbers: 1,14,034 (82264460 to 82378493)
- Ranking: Pari-passu with the old Equity Shares of the Company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are permitted to trade in the newly listed securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2026
- Notice Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital for TIL Ltd. through the conversion of partly paid shares.
