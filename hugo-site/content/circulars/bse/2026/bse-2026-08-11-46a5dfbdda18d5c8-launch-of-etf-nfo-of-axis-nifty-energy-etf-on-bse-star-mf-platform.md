## Summary

AXIS Mutual Fund is offering the New Fund Offer (NFO) for AXIS NIFTY ENERGY ETF and AXIS NIFTY ENERGY ETF - DIRECT PLAN from August 12, 2026, to August 21, 2026, on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The tentative allotment date is August 27, 2026.

## Key Points

- Schemes offered: AXIS NIFTY ENERGY ETF and AXIS NIFTY ENERGY ETF - DIRECT PLAN (Category: Equity).
- NFO Period: August 12, 2026, to August 21, 2026.
- Lumpsum purchases on the BSE StAR MF Platform remain extended till midnight on August 21, 2026.
- Tentative allotment date: August 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions for which clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day shall be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the BSE Clearing Limited account by 6:00 PM on August 27, 2026.
- Cheque, OTM, and switch payments are subject to standard NFO guidelines and timely fund realization.

## Important Dates

- NFO Opening Date: August 12, 2026
- NFO Closing Date: August 21, 2026
- Tentative Allotment Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants and investors to subscribe to the new energy sector ETF via the BSE StAR MF platform with clear operational guidelines.
