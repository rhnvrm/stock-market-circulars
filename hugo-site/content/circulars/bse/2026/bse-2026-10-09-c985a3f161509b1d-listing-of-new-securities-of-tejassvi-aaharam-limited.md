## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade 5,11,62,204 new equity shares of TEJASSVI AAHARAM LIMITED (Scrip Code: 531628) issued on a preferential basis, effective from October 12, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: TEJASSVI AAHARAM LIMITED
- Scrip Code: 531628
- ISIN: INE173E01019
- Security Details: 5,11,62,204 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at par on a preferential basis to Promoters and Non-Promoters.
- Date of Allotment: 20/07/2026
- Effective Date of Trading: October 12, 2026
- Shares rank pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Various lock-in periods apply up to April 2027 and April 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the lock-in details and distribution numbers for the newly listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 09/10/2026
- Date of Allotment: 20/07/2026
- Listing / Trading Effective Date: 12/10/2026
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: 29/04/2027 and 29/04/2028

## Impact Assessment

Expands the equity capital base of the company with specific lock-in restrictions on preferential shares ensuring orderly trading.
