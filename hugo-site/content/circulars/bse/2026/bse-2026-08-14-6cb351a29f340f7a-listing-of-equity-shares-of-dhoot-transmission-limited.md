## Summary

Effective from Monday, August 17, 2026, the equity shares of Dhoot Transmission Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: Dhoot Transmission Limited
- Scrip Code: 544867
- Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name: DHOOTTRANS
- ISIN No.: INE01NH01023
- Number of Securities: 20,45,48,057 Equity Shares of Rs. 2/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 871/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 2/- and premium of Rs. 869/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 13, 2026
- Market Lot: 1
- Special Pre-open Session (SPOS): Applicable on Monday, August 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities of the company will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository's records for shares in demat form.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: August 13, 2026
- Listing and Commencement of Trading Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Market participants can trade in the equity shares of Dhoot Transmission Limited starting August 17, 2026, under the 'B' Group of Securities.
