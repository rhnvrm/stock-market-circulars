## Summary

BSE Index Services Private Limited has announced the addition of ARDEE INDUSTRIES LIMITED and G V ELECTRICALS LIMTED to the BSE IPO and BSE SME IPO indices respectively, following their listings on BSE.

## Key Points

- **ARDEE INDUSTRIES LIMITED (Exchange ticker - 544860):** Listed on BSE effective Wednesday, August 12, 2026. It will be added to the BSE IPO index effective at the open of Friday, August 14, 2026.
- **G V ELECTRICALS LIMTED (Exchange ticker - 544859):** Listed on the SME platform of BSE effective Wednesday, August 12, 2026. It will be added to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open of Thursday, August 13, 2026.
- **Corporate Action File:** These index additions are included in the end-of-day corporate action file (`*.SDE`) on August 12, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational index maintenance activity.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance requirements for general market participants, but index funds, portfolio managers, or passive strategies tracking the BSE IPO or BSE SME IPO indices will need to adjust their portfolios accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **August 12, 2026:** Listing of both ARDEE INDUSTRIES LIMITED and G V ELECTRICALS LIMTED on BSE/SME platform.
- **August 13, 2026:** Addition of G V ELECTRICALS LIMTED to the BSE SME IPO index effective at the open.
- **August 14, 2026:** Addition of ARDEE INDUSTRIES LIMITED to the BSE IPO index effective at the open.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact. The additions affect the BSE IPO and BSE SME IPO indices, which are tracked primarily by specialized index-based funds or strategies. There are no broader system-wide trading halts or changes.
