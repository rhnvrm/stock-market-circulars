## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Currency Derivatives segment (PR & DR) scheduled for Saturday, September 12, 2026. Trading members using third-party platforms, empaneled vendors, or in-house systems via ETI APIs can test their applications under various conditions, including exceptional market scenarios.

## Key Points

- Mock trading is scheduled for Saturday, September 12, 2026, covering both primary (PR) and disaster recovery (DR) sites.
- Applicable to Currency Derivatives and Cross Currency Derivatives segments.
- No new BOLT TWS Release is associated with this session.
- Trades resulting from mock trading carry no margin, pay-in, or pay-out obligations and create no rights or liabilities.
- Members are requested to provide feedback by 4:00 PM on the day of the mock session.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are encouraged to participate actively in the mock trading session to test systems and functionalities, and submit feedback via the designated contact channels before 4:00 PM.

## Important Dates

- Mock Trading Date: September 12, 2026
- Log-in Time: 09:15 AM to 10:00 AM
- Continuous Trading (PR): 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM (Currency), 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM (Cross Currency)
- Switchover to DR: 12:00 PM to 12:45 PM
- Continuous Trading (DR): 12:45 PM to 02:45 PM (Currency), 12:45 PM to 02:45 PM (Cross Currency)
- Trade Modification: 03:00 PM

## Impact Assessment

This is a voluntary/testing exercise designed to ensure system robustness and efficiency. There is no direct financial impact or regulatory obligation for market participants.
