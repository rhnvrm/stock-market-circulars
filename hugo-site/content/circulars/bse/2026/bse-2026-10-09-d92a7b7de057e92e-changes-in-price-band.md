## Summary

 BSE has announced the revision of price bands for multiple scrips effective from October 12, 2026, as per Notice No. 20261009-47.

## Key Points

- Price bands have been revised for 47 scrips across various percentages (ranging from 2% to 20%).
- Effective date for the revised price bands is October 12, 2026.
- Trading members are requested to take note of the changes.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of price limits for specified scrips to manage market volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure orders placed adhere to the newly specified price bands.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 9, 2026
- Effective Date: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants trading in the affected scrips must adjust their order execution strategies to account for the new price band limits.
