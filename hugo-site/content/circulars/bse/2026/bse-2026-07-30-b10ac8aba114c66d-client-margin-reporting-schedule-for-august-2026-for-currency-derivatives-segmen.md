## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has issued the schedule for client margin reporting and associated penalty dates for the Currency Derivatives Segment covering the month of August 2026.

## Key Points

- Outlines trade dates along with their corresponding last date of margin reporting and penalty dates for August 2026.
- Covers the entire trading month starting from August 3, 2026, through August 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a routine schedule issuance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members must ensure client margins are reported within the specified deadlines for each trade date to avoid penalties.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Period:** August 3, 2026, to August 31, 2026 (Trade Dates).
- **First Reporting Deadline:** August 10, 2026 (for August 3, 2026 trade date).
- **First Penalty Date:** August 11, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures operational clarity and transparency for clearing members regarding margin reporting obligations and penalty timelines for currency derivatives during August 2026.
