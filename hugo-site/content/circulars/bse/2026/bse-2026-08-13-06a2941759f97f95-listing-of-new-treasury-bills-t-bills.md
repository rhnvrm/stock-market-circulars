## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to dealings of new Treasury Bills (T-Bills) under the G GROUP - DEBT INSTRUMENTS segment effective from Friday, August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Scrip Code 805253, ISIN IN002026X198, Symbol 91TB121126, Maturity Date: 12/11/2026
- Scrip Code 805254, ISIN IN002026Y196, Symbol 182TB11227, Maturity Date: 11/02/2027
- Scrip Code 805255, ISIN IN002026Z193, Symbol 364TB12827, Maturity Date: 12/08/2027
- Market lot for all listed T-Bills is 1.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must note that securities will not be available for trading two working days prior to the maturity/redemption date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Listing Date: August 14, 2026
- Maturity Dates: November 12, 2026 (91-day), February 11, 2027 (182-day), August 12, 2027 (364-day)

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations update facilitating trading in newly issued government debt instruments.
