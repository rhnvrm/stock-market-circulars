## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Mopshop Distribution Limited shall be shifted from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) effective Wednesday, September 9, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mopshop Distribution Limited
- Scrip Code: 544883
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: September 9, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of equity shares from MT Group to M Group following previous exchange notices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members are to note the segment change and execute dealings accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 26, 2026
- Effective Date: September 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased trading flexibility for investors as the stock moves to the rolling segment.
