## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by companies including Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited, UGRO Capital Limited, Mizuho Capsave Finance Private Limited, Can Fin Homes Ltd., and Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, effective September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Listing of commercial papers with a standard denomination of Rs. 5 Lakhs and 1 paise tick size.
- Securities traded exclusively in dematerialised form under designated ISINs.
- Issuances include companies across real estate, financial services, and housing finance sectors.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure compliance with BSE debt segment trading regulations and dematerialisation norms.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: 25/09/2026
- Listing Date: 28/09/2026
- Redemption Dates: December 2026 to March 2027

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates secondary market trading and liquidity for privately placed debt instruments.
