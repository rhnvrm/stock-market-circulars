## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-694/2026-27. The notice outlines the schedule for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and pay-in.

## Key Points

- Auction offer entry timings: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on August 17, 2026.
- Auction matching timings: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on August 17, 2026.
- Delivery statement and report of accepted offers: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Money statement and final reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Tuesday, August 18, 2026, at 08:00 a.m.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must check their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in in depositories within depository cut-off timings. Members must exercise care while punching security codes and rates as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: August 17, 2026
- Pay-in Date: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine settlement operations for trading members participating in the demat auction process.
