## Summary

Effective from Wednesday, September 16, 2026, the equity shares of Prasol Chemicals Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the BSE Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities. The public issue price is Rs. 676/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Prasol Chemicals Limited
- Scrip Code: 544912
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: PRASOLCHEM
- ISIN No.: INE455U01024
- Total Securities: 59,18,3431 Equity Shares of Rs.2/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 676/- per share (Face Value Rs. 2/-, Premium Rs. 674/-)
- Date of Allotment: September 11, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Securities of the company will only be traded in Dematerialised form as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on Wednesday, September 16, 2026.
- Necessary corporate action has been executed to mark the lock-in period in the depository’s records.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 11, 2026
- Commencement of Trading: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading members can trade in the equity shares of Prasol Chemicals Limited starting September 16, 2026, under the 'B' Group of Securities.
