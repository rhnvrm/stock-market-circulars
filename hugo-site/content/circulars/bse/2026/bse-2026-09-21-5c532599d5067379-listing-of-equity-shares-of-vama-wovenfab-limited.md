## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, September 22, 2026, the equity shares of Vama Wovenfab Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the 'MT' Group of Securities. The scrip will remain in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Vama Wovenfab Limited
- Scrip Code: 544931
- ISIN No: INE1G7R01010
- Issue Price: Rs. 341/- per share (Face Value Rs. 10/-, Premium Rs. 331/-)
- Market Lot: 400 Equity Shares
- Total Securities: 5,188,964 Equity Shares
- Segment: SME (Trade-for-Trade for 10 trading days)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Securities will be traded only in dematerialised form. The scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on September 22, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: September 18, 2026
- Listing Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Vama Wovenfab Limited equity shares on the BSE SME platform.
