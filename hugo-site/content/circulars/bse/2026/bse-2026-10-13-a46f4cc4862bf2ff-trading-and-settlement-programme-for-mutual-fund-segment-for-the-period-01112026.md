## Summary

BSE has released the trading and settlement programme for the Mutual Fund segment covering the period from November 1, 2026, to November 30, 2026. The schedule outlines settlement numbers, fund pay-in, and payout dates for mutual fund subscriptions (both non-liquid and liquid) and redemptions.

## Key Points

- Covers mutual fund subscription and redemption schedules for November 2026.
- Details specific settlement numbers (SETM_NO), transaction dates, fund pay-in, and payout dates.
- Funds pay-out to AMCs for non-liquid transactions processed will happen before 3:00 PM on T day.
- Funds pay-out for NFO (MF) schemes will happen on T day based on funds confirmation received by BSECL till 6:00 pm on T day.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine operational calendar announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, AMCs, and distributors must adhere to the specified settlement dates, pay-in timings, and fund payout schedules for mutual fund transactions during November 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Period:** 01.11.2026 to 30.11.2026
- **First Settlement Date:** 02-11-2026 (Settlement No. 2627146)
- **Last Settlement Date:** 30-11-2026 (Settlement No. 2627164)

## Impact Assessment

Operational guidance for market participants and AMCs operating on the BSE mutual fund platform, ensuring smooth execution and clearing of subscription and redemption orders throughout November 2026.
