## Summary

Effective Thursday, October 8, 2026, the equity shares of Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited will be listed and admitted to dealing on the BSE under the 'T' Group of Securities. The scrip will be in the Trade-for-Trade segment for 10 trading days in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 544974
- **Scrip ID / Abbreviated Name:** NITYAS
- **ISIN:** INE211501023
- **Total Securities:** 57,593,248 Equity Shares of Rs. 5/- each fully paid up (Distinctive Range: 1 to 57593248)
- **Issue Price:** Rs. 75/- per share (Face Value Rs. 5/-, Premium Rs. 70/-)
- **Date of Allotment:** October 6, 2026
- **Market Lot:** 1 share

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded exclusively in dematerialised form pursuant to SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on October 8, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must note the 10-day Trade-for-Trade restriction.
- Corporate action executed for lock-in period marking in depository records.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Listing & Trading Effective Date:** October 8, 2026
- **Special Pre-open Session (SPOS):** October 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Investors and trading members can trade shares of Nityas Gems and Jewellery Limited starting October 8, 2026.
- Initial 10 trading days settlement under Trade-for-Trade segment mitigates speculative volatility.
