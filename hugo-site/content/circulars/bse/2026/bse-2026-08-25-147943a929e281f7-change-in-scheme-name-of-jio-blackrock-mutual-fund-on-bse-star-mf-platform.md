## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the change in scheme names for Jio BlackRock Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective from August 26, 2026, following communication received from the fund house.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026.
- JIOBLACKROCK SHORT DURATION FUND has been renamed to JIOBLACKROCK SHORT TERM FUND.
- JIOBLACKROCK LOW DURATION FUND has been renamed to JIOBLACKROCK ULTRA SHORT TO SHORT TERM FUND.
- The Notice-cum-Addendum issued by JIO BLACKROCK AMC is attached to the circular.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and distributors need to update their systems with the revised scheme names on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 26, 2026
- Notice Date: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to updating nomenclature across trading and platform systems for the affected schemes.
