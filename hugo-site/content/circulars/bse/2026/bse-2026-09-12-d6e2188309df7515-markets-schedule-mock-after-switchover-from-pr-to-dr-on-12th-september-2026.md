## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the mock trading schedule following the switchover of trading systems from the Primary (PR) location to the Disaster Recovery (DR) location on September 12, 2026. Trading operations for Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and Electronic Gold Receipts (EGR) segments commenced in the pre-open session at the DR location starting from 12:45 PM onwards.

## Key Points

- Switchover of trading systems from PR location to DR location initiated on 12th September 2026.
- Pre-open session at DR location commenced at 12:45 PM for Equity, Equity Derivatives, Currency Derivatives, Commodity Derivatives, and EGR segments.
- Detailed schedules covering order entry, continuous trading, closing sessions, and trade modifications were provided for various market segments.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operational transition and disaster recovery drill execution in accordance with exchange protocols.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the revised timings and ensure their systems align with the DR mock schedule.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 12th September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures readiness and business continuity of trading members and exchange systems by testing disaster recovery failover mechanisms.
