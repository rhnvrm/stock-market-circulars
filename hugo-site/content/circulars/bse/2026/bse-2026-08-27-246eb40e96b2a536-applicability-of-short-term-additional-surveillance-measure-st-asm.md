## Summary

BSE has announced the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for certain securities based on market surveillance criteria, effective August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities meeting the criteria are included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective August 31, 2026, on open positions as on August 28, 2026, and new positions created from August 31, 2026.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective August 31, 2026, on open positions as on August 28, 2026, and new positions created from August 31, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with the increased margin requirements for the shortlisted securities in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 27, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins: August 31, 2026 (on open positions as on August 28, 2026 and new positions)

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for specified securities may impact trading liquidity and position sizing for market participants holding open positions or trading in these scrips.
