## Summary

Pursuant to Notice No. 20260911-35 dated September 11, 2026, the HDFC BSE REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund schemes will reopen for ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facilities on the BSE StAR MF platform effective September 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** September 28, 2026.
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform.
- **Available Facilities:** Ongoing subscriptions, redemptions, and SIP facility.
- **Covered Schemes & ISINs:**
  - HDFC BSE REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund - Regular Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF179KC1KU2)
  - HDFC BSE REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund - Regular Plan - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF179KC1KV0)
  - HDFC BSE REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund - Regular Plan - IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF179KC1KW8)
  - HDFC BSE REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund - Direct Plan - Growth (ISIN: INF179KC1KX6)
  - HDFC BSE REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund - Direct Plan - IDCW Payout (ISIN: INF179KC1KY4)
  - HDFC BSE REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund - Direct Plan - IDCW Reinvestment (ISIN: INF179KC1KZ1)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational notice for mutual fund scheme transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants can execute transactions, subscriptions, redemptions, and set up SIPs for the specified ISINs through the BSE StAR MF platform starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 25, 2026
- **Previous Notice Date:** September 11, 2026
- **Effective Date / Reopening Date:** September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless trading, ongoing investments, and redemptions for market participants and investors in the HDFC BSE REITs and Commercial Real Estate Index Fund across all regular and direct plan variants.
