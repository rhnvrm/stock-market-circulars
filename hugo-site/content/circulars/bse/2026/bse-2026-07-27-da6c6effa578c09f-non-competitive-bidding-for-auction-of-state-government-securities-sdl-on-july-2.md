## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the non-competitive bidding process for the auction of State Government Securities (SDL) scheduled for July 28, 2026, following the RBI press release and exchange guidelines. Trading members can participate through the NCB-GSec module of the iBBS web-based system.

## Key Points

- Non-competitive bidding for State Government Securities (SDL) auction scheduled for July 28, 2026.
- Bidding takes place via the NCB-GSec module of the BSE iBBS platform.
- Covers multiple re-issued and new SDL securities from states including Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana.
- Bid collection start date begins on July 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Operates under RBI guidelines for SDL auctions and Exchange circular no. 20191122-28.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and direct investors must submit bids within the specified timelines through the NCB-GSec module.
- Settlement and pay-in obligations must be adhered to via ICCL operations.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Collection Start Date:** July 27, 2026, 10:00 AM onwards
- **Bid Collection End Date (Direct Investor):** July 27, 2026, till 5:00 PM
- **Bid Collection End Date (Members):** July 28, 2026, till 8:00 AM
- **Auction Date:** July 28, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** July 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates primary market participation in state debt securities for eligible investors through non-competitive bidding platforms.
