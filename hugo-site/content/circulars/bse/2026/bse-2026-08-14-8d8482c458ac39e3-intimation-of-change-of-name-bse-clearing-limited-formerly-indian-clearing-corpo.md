## Summary

BSE Clearing Limited has announced its change of name from Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL). All existing arrangements, services, and ongoing business activities continue without interruption.

## Key Points

- Entity name changed from "Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL)" to "BSE Clearing Limited".
- All existing arrangements and services remain unaffected and continue without interruption.
- Stakeholders are required to update internal records and use the new entity name.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Stakeholders and investors must update their internal records and mention the new entity name, "BSE Clearing Limited", while initiating fund transfers through Cheque, RTGS, or NEFT (Virtual Account Code).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 14 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is minimal. Market participants and investors must update payment instructions and beneficiary details to reflect the new name.
