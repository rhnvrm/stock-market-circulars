## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the payment of interest and part redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and bonds. Trading in these debentures/bonds will take place with a reduced face value effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260925-17, dated 25 Sep 2026.
- Segment: Debt.
- Companies involved include Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd, Vistaar Financial Services Pvt Ltd, IKF Finance Limited, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, Vivriti Capital Limited, U.P. Power Corporation Limited, and AAVAS Financiers.
- Record dates and ex-dates are set around early October 2026 (01/10/2026 to 05/10/2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading in debentures/bonds will be conducted with reduced face values as per the schedule provided for each scrip.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must take note of the record dates, ex-dates, and reduced face values for smooth settlement of transactions.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: October 1, 2026, to October 5, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding these debentures will receive part redemption payments and interest as applicable, with trading reflecting the reduced face values.
