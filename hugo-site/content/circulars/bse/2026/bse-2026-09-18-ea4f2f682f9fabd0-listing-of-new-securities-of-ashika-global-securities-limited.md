## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading permission for 2,00,000 new equity shares of Ashika Global Securities Limited (Scrip Code: 543766) effective from September 21, 2026. These shares were issued on a preferential basis to non-promoters upon the conversion of warrants.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Ashika Global Securities Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 543766
- **ISIN:** INE094B01013
- **Securities Issued:** 2,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 599/- (Issue Price: Rs. 609/-)
- **Allottees:** Non-promoters on a preferential basis pursuant to warrant conversion
- **Ranking:** Pari-passu with existing equity shares
- **Effective Date of Trading:** September 21, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the company must ensure compliance with lock-in requirements and depository procedures.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** June 29, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** September 21, 2026
- **Lock-in Upto:** March 21, 2027 (for 2,00,000 shares, Dist. Nos. 85083732 to 85283731)

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase via preferential warrant conversion with standard lock-in provisions. No broad market impact.
