## Summary

BSE has issued notice no. 20260929-46 regarding the movement of securities into their respective higher stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in the attached Annexure move to higher stages of the GSM framework.
- Effective date for the surveillance measures is September 30, 2026.
- Settlement for affected securities will be done on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off allowed.
- Surveillance actions vary across stages I, II, III, and IV.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the GSM framework based on the review of securities, introducing stricter margin requirements, price bands, Additional Surveillance Deposits (ASD), and trading frequency limits.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take adequate precautions while trading in the affected securities, noting that settlement occurs on a trade-to-trade basis with no netting off.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 29, 2026
- Effective Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High operational and liquidity impact for investors and trading members holding or trading securities moving into higher GSM stages, due to stringent margin requirements, trade-to-trade settlement, and restrictions on trading frequency.
