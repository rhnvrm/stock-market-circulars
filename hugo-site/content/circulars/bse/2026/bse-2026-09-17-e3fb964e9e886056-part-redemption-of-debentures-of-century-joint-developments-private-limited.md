## Summary

Century Joint Developments Private Limited has fixed a record date for the payment of interest and part redemption of its non-convertible debentures (NCDs). Trading in the debentures will continue with a reduced face value effective from the specified ex-date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Century Joint Developments Private Limited
- Scrip Codes: 976196, 976527
- Scrip IDs: CJDPL-10%-25-11-28-PVT
- ISINs: INE050R07091, INE050R07109
- Record Date: 18/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 18/09/2026
- Reduced Face Value: Rs. 85,000/- per debenture

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are requested to note the reduced face value and settlement details for trading in the debentures.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: 18/09/2026
- Ex-Date: 18/09/2026

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the debentures of the company will proceed with the reduced face value of Rs. 85,000/- per debenture.
