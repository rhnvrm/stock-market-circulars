## Summary

An open offer has been made by Mr. Rakesh Ramanlal Shah and Komal Infotech Private Limited to acquire up to 14,37,420 fully paid-up equity shares (representing 26% of the total paid-up/voting share capital) of ECS Biztech Limited at a price of Rs. 10.50 per share. The acquisition window is open from September 17, 2026, to September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- Acquirers: Mr. Rakesh Ramanlal Shah (“Acquirer-1”) and Komal Infotech Private Limited (“Acquirer-2”).
- Target Company: ECS Biztech Limited (EBL).
- Offer Price: Rs. 10.50/- per fully paid-up Equity Share payable in cash.
- Offer Size: Up to 14,37,420 Equity Shares (26% of Total Paid up/Voting Share Capital).
- Offer Period: September 17, 2026 (Thursday) to September 30, 2026 (Wednesday).

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is conducted pursuant to Regulations 3(1) & 4 of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, and relevant SEBI/Exchange guidelines on the acquisition of shares through the stock exchange mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and custodians must note the acquisition window schedule and guidelines for tendering shares under the takeover offer.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 15, 2026
- Offer Opening Date: September 17, 2026
- Offer Closing Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Public shareholders of ECS Biztech Limited can tender their shares through the stock exchange acquisition window during the specified open offer period.
