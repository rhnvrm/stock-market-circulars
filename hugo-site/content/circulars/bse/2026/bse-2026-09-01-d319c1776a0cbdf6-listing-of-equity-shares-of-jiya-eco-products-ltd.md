## Summary

Effective from Tuesday, September 08, 2026, the equity shares of Jiya Eco-Products Ltd. shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange under the XT Group. Trading will take place in the Trade-to-Trade segment.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Jiya Eco-Products Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 539225
- ISIN: INE023S01024
- Group: XT (Trade-to-Trade segment)
- Total Securities: 1,06,314 fully paid-up equity shares of Rs. 100/- each.
- Breakdown: 5,314 new equity shares on reduction and 1,01,000 new equity shares on preferential basis to the Successful Resolution Applicant.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities admitted to dealings under XT Group in accordance with the resolution plan under the provisions of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must note the lock-in details and trading segment restrictions (Trade-to-Trade).

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 01, 2026
- Effective Date of Listing: September 08, 2026
- Lock-in Release Date: September 21, 2027 (for 1,01,000 shares)

## Impact Assessment

- Resumption of trading for Jiya Eco-Products Ltd. under strict monitoring (XT group, trade-to-trade) following corporate restructuring via IBC.
