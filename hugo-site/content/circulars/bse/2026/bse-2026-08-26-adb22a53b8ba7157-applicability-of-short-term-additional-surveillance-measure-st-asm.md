## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Securities meeting the criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I or Stage II, attracting enhanced margin requirements effective from August 28, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria for ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II are shortlisted as per the attached annexure.
- Applicable margin rate for ST-ASM Stage I is 50% or existing margin (whichever is higher), capped at 100%.
- Applicable margin rate for ST-ASM Stage II is 100% or existing margin (whichever is higher).
- Surveillance actions apply to all open positions as on August 27, 2026, and new positions created from August 28, 2026.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and is not an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of enhanced surveillance measures under the ST-ASM framework to curb excessive price volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 28, 2026
- Applicable on open positions as of: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased capital requirements for trading participants holding positions in securities shortlisted under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II due to higher margin rates.
