## Summary

Trading members of the Exchange are informed that effective from Monday, August 24, 2026, the equity shares of Horizon Industrial Parks Limited shall be listed and admitted to dealings on the Exchange in the 'B' Group of Securities.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Horizon Industrial Parks Limited
- Scrip Code: 544880
- Scrip ID on BOLT System: HORIZONIND
- ISIN No.: INE685T01010
- Total Securities: 2,882,902,834 Equity Shares of Rs. 10/- each fully paid up
- Issue Price: Rs. 60/- per share (Face Value of Rs. 10/- and premium of Rs. 50/-)
- Date of Allotment: August 20, 2026
- Market Lot: 1

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities will be traded in dematerialised form only as per SEBI guidelines dated February 16, 2000.
- Scrip will be part of the Special Pre-open Session (SPOS) on the listing date.

## Compliance Requirements

- Necessary corporate action has been executed for locking-in shares in depository's records as per Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Listing: August 24, 2026
- Date of Allotment: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables public trading of Horizon Industrial Parks Limited shares on the BSE exchange, providing liquidity to investors who participated in the public issue.
