## Summary

Trading members are informed that the equity shares of Gulf Lloyds (India) Limited (Scrip Code: 544834) are being transferred from the Trade for Trade segment (MT Group) to the Rolling segment (M Group) with effect from Monday, August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Gulf Lloyds (India) Limited
- Scrip Code: 544834
- Previous Segment: Trade for Trade segment (MT Group)
- New Segment: Rolling segment (M Group)
- Effective Date: August 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of securities from the Trade for Trade segment to the normal rolling settlement segment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to note the segment change for execution and settlement purposes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: July 28, 2026
- Effective Date: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Improved liquidity and normal rolling settlement applicability for investors trading in the equity shares of the company.
