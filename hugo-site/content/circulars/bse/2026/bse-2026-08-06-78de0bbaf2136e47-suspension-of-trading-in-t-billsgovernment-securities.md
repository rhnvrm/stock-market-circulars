## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the suspension of trading in specific T-bills/Government Securities due to their maturity. Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified security effective August 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- Security Name/Particulars: 624MAHSDL26
- Scrip Code: 810762
- ISIN: IN2220210214
- Effective Date of Suspension: August 07, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal with the aforesaid T-bills/Government Securities with effect from August 07, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 06, 2026
- Effective Date: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational impact limited to the maturity and cessation of trading for the specified debt security.
