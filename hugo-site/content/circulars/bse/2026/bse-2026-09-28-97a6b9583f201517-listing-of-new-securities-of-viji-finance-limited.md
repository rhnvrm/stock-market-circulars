## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and permission to trade new equity shares of Viji Finance Limited (Scrip Code - 537820) with effect from Tuesday, September 29, 2026. A total of 2,95,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each were issued at a premium of Rs. 1.80/- to non-promoters on a preferential basis.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Viji Finance Limited
- Scrip Code: 537820
- ISIN: INE159N01027
- Issue Size: 2,95,00,000 equity shares of Rs. 1/- each at an issue price of Rs. 2.80/- (including premium of Rs. 1.80/-).
- Allottees: Non-promoters on a preferential basis.
- Ranking: Ranking pari-passu with existing equity shares.
- Effective Trading Date: September 29, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine corporate listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

Ensure adherence to lock-in requirements as specified:
- 1,05,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 191500001 to 202000000) locked-in upto 30/03/2027.
- 40,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 202000001 to 206000000) locked-in upto 31/03/2027.
- 1,50,00,000 shares (Dist. Nos. 206000001 to 221000000) locked-in upto 31/03/2027.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 28, 2026
- Trading Effective Date: September 29, 2026
- Allotment Dates: July 24, 2026 (1,45,00,000 shares) and August 12, 2026 (1,50,00,000 shares)
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: March 30, 2027 and March 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of paid-up equity capital base of Viji Finance Limited through preferential allotment to non-promoters with standard lock-in stipulations.
