## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by Navi Finserv Limited, Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited, and Bajaj Financial Securities Limited on the debt market segment effective October 21, 2026.

## Key Points

- Navi Finserv Limited listed three tranches of commercial papers totaling 1,400 units (Scrip Codes: 732799, 732800, 732801; ISINs: INE342T14IB2, INE342T14ID8, INE342T14IE6) with credit rating CARE A1+.
- Tata Realty and Infrastructure Limited listed 6,400 units of commercial papers (Scrip Code: 732802; ISIN: INE371K14EB9) with credit ratings CARE A1+, ICRA A1+.
- Bajaj Financial Securities Limited listed 3,000 units of commercial papers (Scrip Code: 732803; ISIN: INE01C314IH3) with credit ratings CRISIL A1+, IND A1+.
- Trading takes place in standard denominations of Rs. 5 Lakhs with a tick size of 1 paise in dematerialised form.

[N/A - Routine listing of debt securities]

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to standard debt segment norms, dematerialised holding, and standard lot sizes of Rs. 5 Lakhs.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 19, 2026
- Listing / Effective Date: October 21, 2026
- Maturity / Redemption Dates: Ranging from December 2026 to October 2027 depending on the specific tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operation facilitating debt instrument trading and liquidity for issuing corporate entities.
