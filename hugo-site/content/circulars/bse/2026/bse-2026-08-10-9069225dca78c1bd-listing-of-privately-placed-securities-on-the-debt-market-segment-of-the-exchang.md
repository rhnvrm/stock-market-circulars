## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and admission to trading of commercial papers issued on a private placement basis by multiple entities on the debt market segment effective August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities are issued on a private placement basis and traded in dematerialised form.
- Standard market lot is 1 with a tick size of 1 paise.
- Issuing companies include Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited, Navi Finserv Limited, Infina Finance Private Limited, and Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure transactions adhere to the specified ISINs, market lots, and standard trading conventions for debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 10, 2026
- Allotment Dates: August 7, 2026
- Redemption Dates: November 2026 to June 2027 depending on the specific commercial paper tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Routine market operations providing liquidity and trading access for newly issued commercial papers on the debt segment.
