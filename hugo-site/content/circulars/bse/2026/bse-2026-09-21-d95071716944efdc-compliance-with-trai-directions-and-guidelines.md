## Summary

BSE has issued a notice advising Research Analysts to ensure compliance with TRAI directions and guidelines, specifically regarding the Department of Telecommunications' (DoT) Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL), the strict use of the 1600 numbering series for transactional/service calls, SMS content template pre-tagging, and consumer awareness campaigns.

## Key Points

- Integration with DoT's Mobile Number Revocation List (MNRL) for periodic customer record reconciliation and updating.
- Restriction of the 1600 numbering series strictly for service and transactional calls, excluding promotional calls.
- Compliance with TRAI's direction dated 18.11.2025 on pre-tagging of variable fields in SMS Content Templates prior to blocker-mode activation.
- Conducting consumer awareness campaigns on 1600 and 140 numbering series.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory integration of MNRL checks into risk, authentication, and communication workflows.
- Mandatory review and pre-tagging of variable fields in SMS Content Templates (specifically those used in the preceding 90 days) before blocker mode activation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research Analysts must integrate with MNRL and establish periodic customer record reconciliation processes.
- Entities must review and rectify deficiencies in SMS Content Templates during logger-mode scrubbing to avoid delivery disruptions after blocker-mode activation.
- Use 1600 series strictly for non-promotional communication.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 21 September 2026
- Reference TRAI Direction Date: 18 November 2025

## Impact Assessment

Entities failing to comply with SMS variable-field validation before blocker-mode activation will face non-delivery of legitimate SMS communications.
