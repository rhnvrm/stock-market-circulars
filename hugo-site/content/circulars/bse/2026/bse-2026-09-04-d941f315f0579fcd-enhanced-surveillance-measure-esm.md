## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Securities satisfying the criteria will attract a minimum 100% margin, move to lower or higher stages, and shift to the Trade-for-Trade segment effective from September 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities in Annexure I attract a minimum 100% margin w.e.f. September 08, 2026.
- Selected securities move to lower Stage I under the ESM Framework w.e.f. September 07, 2026.
- Securities shifting to Stage II will be under Trade-for-Trade with a price band of 2% and Periodic Call Auction w.e.f. September 07, 2026.
- Qualifying securities shift from Rolling Settlement segment to Trade-for-Trade segment w.e.f. September 08, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the ESM framework in conjunction with existing exchange measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (minimum 100%) on all open and new positions for shortlisted securities and account for segment shifts.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Stage Changes / Price Bands: September 07, 2026
- Effective Date for Margins and Trade-for-Trade Shift: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trade-for-trade settlement restrictions for shortlisted scrips may impact liquidity and trading volumes for affected securities.
