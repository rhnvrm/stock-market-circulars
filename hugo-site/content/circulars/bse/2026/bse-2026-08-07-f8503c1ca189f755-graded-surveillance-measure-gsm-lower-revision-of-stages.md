## Summary

BSE has carried out a review of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework in coordination with other exchanges. Certain securities are eligible to move to lower stages of the GSM framework effective August 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- Periodic review of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework conducted in coordination with other exchanges.
- Eligible securities will move to lower stages of GSM effective August 10, 2026.
- Specific security details are provided in the attached Annexure_GSM_Lower.

## Regulatory Changes

- Movement of eligible securities to lower stages of the GSM framework, reducing trading restrictions for those specific entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members are advised to check the annexure for specific securities moving to lower stages and adjust trading parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 10, 2026
- Notice Date: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reduced surveillance restrictions for securities transitioning to lower GSM stages, potentially improving liquidity and normal trading activity for those stocks.
