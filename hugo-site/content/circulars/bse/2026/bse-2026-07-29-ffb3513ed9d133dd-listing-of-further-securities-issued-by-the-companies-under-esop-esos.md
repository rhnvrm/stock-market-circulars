## Summary

BSE has informed trading members that further new securities issued by six companies under their Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange effective from Thursday, July 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** July 30, 2026.
- **Companies Involved:**
  1. Gulf Oil Lubricants India Limited (ISIN: INE635Q01029, Scrip Code: 538567, Shares: 38,751)
  2. ICICI Bank Limited (ISIN: INE090A01021, Scrip Code: 532174, Shares: 948,479)
  3. ICICI Prudential Life Insurance Company Limited (ISIN: INE726G01019, Scrip Code: 540133, Shares: 1,948)
  4. OnMobile Global Limited (ISIN: INE809I01019, Scrip Code: 532944, Shares: 33,335)
  5. SBI Cards And Payment Services Limited (ISIN: INE018E01016, Scrip Code: 543066, Shares: 27,984)
  6. Tech Mahindra Limited (ISIN: INE669C01036, Scrip Code: 532755, Shares: 26,848)
- **Lock-in:** All listed shares are marked as 'NA' for lock-in under the current tranche.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine administrative listing notice for securities issued under employee stock option schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are advised to take note of the new securities listed and admitted for trading.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** July 29, 2026
- **Trading Effective Date:** July 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up equity capital for the respective companies due to ESOP exercises, with negligible direct impact on broader market operations.
