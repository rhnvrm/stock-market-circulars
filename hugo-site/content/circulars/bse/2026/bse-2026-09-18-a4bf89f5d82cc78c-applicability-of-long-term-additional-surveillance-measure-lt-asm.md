## Summary

The BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, stage revisions, continuation, and exclusion of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective dates for various measures span September 21, 2026, to September 23, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins are applicable effective September 23, 2026, on all open positions as of September 22, 2026, and new positions created thereafter for securities in Annexure I (Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework and those subject to Lower Price Bands are effective September 21, 2026 (Annexure I, Part B).
- Certain securities continue in the LT-ASM framework but are transferred to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective September 23, 2026 (Annexure I, Part D).
- Securities moving out of the framework will have their price bands reinstated subject to other applicable surveillance measures.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under the revised LT-ASM framework based on XBRL submissions by listed companies.
- Imposition of stringent margin requirements and price band restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements for open and new positions on applicable securities starting September 23, 2026.
- Members should refer to the annexures attached to the circular for specific security lists.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Lower Price Bands and Higher Stages: September 21, 2026
- Effective Date for Group Transfers and 100% Margins: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced surveillance measures may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the shortlisted securities due to higher margin obligations and price band constraints.
