## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has announced the suspension of trading in the Commercial Paper of Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited due to its maturity on August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- Entity: Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited
- Scrip Code: 732373
- ISIN: INE975F14D40
- Particulars: KMIL-31-8-26-CP

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised not to deal in the aforesaid Commercial Paper of the company effective from August 28, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Suspension: August 28, 2026
- Redemption Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational procedure for maturing debt instruments; no broader market impact.
