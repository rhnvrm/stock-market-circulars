## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability, revision, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The measures include 100% margin requirements, lower price bands, and group transfers effective from August 27, 2026, and August 31, 2026.

## Key Points

- 100% margins applicable w.e.f. August 31, 2026, on all open positions as on August 28, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Lower price bands applicable to specified securities w.e.f. August 27, 2026.
- Transfer of certain securities to T / XT / MT / TS Groups w.e.f. August 31, 2026.
- Movement of securities into higher stages of LT-ASM or out of the LT-ASM framework w.e.f. August 27, 2026.
- Shortlisting is based on XBRL submissions by listed companies and market surveillance parameters, not an adverse regulatory action.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised LT-ASM framework guidelines as per previous exchange notices.
- Imposition of stricter surveillance controls on shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure compliance with 100% margin requirements for open and new positions in applicable securities.
- Trading members and investors must adhere to revised price bands and segment group transfers.

## Important Dates

- **August 27, 2026:** Lower price bands effective; securities moved to higher stages or moving out of LT-ASM framework.
- **August 28, 2026:** Cut-off date for open positions subject to 100% margins.
- **August 31, 2026:** 100% margins applicable; transfer of securities to T / XT / MT / TS Groups effective.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased capital requirements for traders holding open positions in securities placed under LT-ASM.
- Potential reduction in liquidity and increased volatility for impacted scrips due to lower price bands and strict margin norms.
