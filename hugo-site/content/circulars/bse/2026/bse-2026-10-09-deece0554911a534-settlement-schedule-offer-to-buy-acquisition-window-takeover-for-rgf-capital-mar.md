## Summary

BSE has announced the settlement schedule and operational guidelines for the Offer to Buy (Acquisition Window - Takeover) of RGF Capital Markets Limited. The bidding window is scheduled to run from October 13, 2026, to October 27, 2026, under settlement number 2026121 and Market Type "Takeover".

## Key Points

- **Target Company:** RGF Capital Markets Limited.
- **Bidding Window:** October 13, 2026 to October 27, 2026.
- **Settlement Number:** 2026121 (Market Type: Takeover).
- **Obligation Download:** November 9, 2026.
- **Settlement Date:** November 11, 2026.
- **Demat Shares Delivery:** Must be delivered exclusively through the Early Pay-in of Shares mechanism in CDSL and NSDL depositories, earmarked/blocked in client accounts.
- **EPN File:** Members and custodians are not required to upload any EPN file.

## Regulatory Changes

- Execution of takeover acquisition window adhering to revised exchange guidelines for stock exchange mechanism acquisitions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must ensure early pay-in of securities is reflected in BSE/BSECL systems before bidding and confirmation window timelines.
- Use market type "(Takeover)" and settlement No. 2026121 in depository systems.
- Refer to `MBRSTD_OTB_CM_mmm_SettNo` file for client-wise Stamp Duty charges.

## Important Dates

- **Bid Start Date:** October 13, 2026
- **Bid End Date:** October 27, 2026
- **Obligation Download:** November 9, 2026
- **Settlement Date:** November 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures orderly settlement and smooth execution of the takeover offer for RGF Capital Markets Limited with standardized early pay-in requirements for members and custodians.
