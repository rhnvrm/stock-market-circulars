## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II for specified securities. The surveillance actions include applicable margin rates of 50% or 100% depending on the stage, effective from August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying criteria are placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin is 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable margin rate is 100% or existing margin whichever is higher.
- Measures apply to all open positions as on August 18, 2026, and new positions created from August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework in conjunction with existing exchange surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure appropriate margin maintenance (50% to 100%) for open positions and new positions in the shortlisted securities as specified in Annexure I.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 19, 2026
- Applicability on Open Positions: As of August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for securities under ST-ASM Stage I and II may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
