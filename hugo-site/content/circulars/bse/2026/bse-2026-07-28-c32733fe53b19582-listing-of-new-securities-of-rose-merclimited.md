## Summary

BSE has informed trading members about the listing and permission to trade new securities of Rose Merc. Limited (Scrip Code: 512115) effective from July 29, 2026, following the conversion of warrants into equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Rose Merc. Limited
- Scrip Code: 512115
- ISIN: INE649C01012
- Securities Issued: 1,74,445 equity shares of Rs. 10/- each issued at a premium of Rs. 80/- (Issue Price: Rs. 90/-) on a preferential basis to non-promoters upon conversion of warrants.
- Effective Date: July 29, 2026
- Distribution Nos.: 6244186 to 6418630
- Lock-in Details: Locked-in up to 29/04/2027 for all 1,74,445 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants take note of the listing and lock-in details of the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 05/06/2026
- Effective Date of Listing: 29/07/2026
- Lock-in Upto: 29/04/2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine capital increase via preferential allotment and warrant conversion with standard lock-in restrictions.
