## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of subscriptions through lump sum investments and switch-in transactions for PGIM India Global Select Real Estate Securities Fund of Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform, effective October 8, 2026.

## Key Points

- Fresh and additional purchases through lump sum and switch-in transactions are accepted without any amount restrictions starting October 8, 2026.
- Fresh subscriptions through Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) and Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) remain temporarily suspended.
- Existing systematic registrations (SIPs/XSIPs) will continue to be processed at the RTA/AMC end.
- Redemption, switch-out, and STP-out transactions are permitted without amount restrictions.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an operational notice regarding scheme subscription updates by the asset management company.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors must take note of the resumption of lump sum transactions and the continued suspension of fresh SIP/STP registrations for the scheme.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 8, 2026
- Notice Date: October 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors can resume lump sum investments in PGIM India Global Select Real Estate Securities Fund of Fund via the BSE StAR MF platform while SIP/STP options remain paused.
