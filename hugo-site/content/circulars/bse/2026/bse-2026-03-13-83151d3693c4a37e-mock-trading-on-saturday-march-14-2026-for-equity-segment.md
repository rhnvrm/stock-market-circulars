## Summary

BSE has announced a mock trading session for the Equity segment scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026. Trading members are required to test their connectivity to the Disaster Recovery (DR) site using the provided technical parameters for BOLT Plus, ETI APIs, and web-based applications.

## Key Points

- Mock trading session scheduled for Saturday, March 14, 2026, for the Equity segment.
- Members can connect to the DR site of the BOLT Plus trading system using BOLT TWS or 3rd party trading applications via ETI APIs.
- DR IP and Port parameters provided for primary and secondary connections.
- Instructions and URLs included for web-based systems such as RTRMS, EBOSS, LEIPS, and Extranet.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; operational drill for disaster recovery preparedness.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should participate in the mock trading session to verify system connectivity and ensure seamless operations during contingencies.

## Important Dates

- **Mock Trading Date:** March 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Helps brokerages and trading members validate disaster recovery setups and network readiness with BSE systems.
