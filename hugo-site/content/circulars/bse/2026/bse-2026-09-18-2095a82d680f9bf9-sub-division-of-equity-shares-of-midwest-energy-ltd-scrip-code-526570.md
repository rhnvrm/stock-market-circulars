## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the sub-division of equity shares of Midwest Energy Ltd (Scrip Code: 526570) from a face value of Rs. 10/- each into ten equity shares of Re. 1/- each, effective from September 25, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Midwest Energy Ltd
- Scrip Code: 526570
- Record Date: 25/09/2026
- Sub-division: Existing equity shares of Rs. 10/- each sub-divided into 10 equity shares of Re. 1/- each.
- Effective Date: 25/09/2026

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Old ISIN No. INE519N01014 (Rs. 10/- paid up) will not be valid for transactions done on the Exchange on or after 25/09/2026.
- New ISIN Number for the sub-divided shares (Re. 1/- paid up) will be communicated via a separate notice.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 25, 2026
- Effective Date (W.E.F.): September 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders holding shares as of the record date will receive sub-divided shares, increasing liquidity and adjusting the market price per share accordingly.
