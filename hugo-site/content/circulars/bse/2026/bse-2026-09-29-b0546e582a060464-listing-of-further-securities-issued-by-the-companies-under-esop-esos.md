## Summary

BSE Limited has informed trading members that further new securities issued by eight companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS) are listed and admitted for trading on the Exchange with effect from Wednesday, September 30, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2026.
- **Scope:** Listing and admission for trading of equity shares issued pursuant to ESOP/ESOS exercises.
- **Companies Involved:** Cipla Limited, CreditAccess Grameen Limited, Cyient Limited, Fractal Analytics Limited, ITC Hotels Limited, JM Financial Limited, SBI Cards And Payment Services Limited, and Xelpmoc Design and Tech Limited.
- **Lock-in Status:** ITC Hotels Limited has shares subject to lock-in as per Annexure_I; all other listed issuances have 'NA' for lock-in under this notice.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine corporate action processing.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members should note the admission of new securities for trading and ensure correct settlement parameters.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 29, 2026
- **Effective Trading Date:** September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Marginal increase in paid-up equity capital and liquidity for the respective companies with negligible direct market impact.
