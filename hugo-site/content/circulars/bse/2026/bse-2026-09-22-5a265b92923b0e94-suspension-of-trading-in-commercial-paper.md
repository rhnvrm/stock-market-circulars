## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice informing trading members that several companies have fixed record dates for the redemption of their Commercial Papers (CPs). Consequently, trading members are advised not to deal in the specified Commercial Papers of these companies starting from the respective ex-dates.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 20260922-38, dated September 22, 2026.
- Segment: Debt.
- Department: Listing Operations.
- Reason: Fixing of Record Date for Redemption of Commercial Papers.
- Action Required: Trading members are advised not to deal in the specified CPs of the listed companies.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must immediately halt trading activities in the specified Commercial Papers of the listed issuers starting from their respective ex-dates to ensure smooth processing of redemption payments.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates / Ex-Dates: September 28, 2026, and September 29, 2026, corresponding to the specific Commercial Paper issuances.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures orderly redemption and prevents secondary market transactions in Commercial Papers that are close to maturity and redemption.
