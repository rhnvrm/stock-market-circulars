## Summary

BSE has issued notice number 20260812-13 announcing the listing and trading approval of 6,50,500 new equity shares of Sobhagya Mercantile Ltd. (Scrip Code: 512014, ISIN: INE754D01018). These shares, of face value Rs. 10 each, were allotted at an issue price of Rs. 674.49 per share (inclusive of a premium of Rs. 664.49) to non-promoters on a preferential basis following the conversion of warrants. The newly listed shares rank pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the company and will be permitted to trade with effect from Thursday, August 13, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Company Name**: Sobhagya Mercantile Ltd.
- **Scrip Code / ID**: 512014 / SOBME (ISIN: INE754D01018).
- **Number of Shares**: 6,50,500 equity shares.
- **Issue Price**: Rs. 674.49 per share (Face Value of Rs. 10 and premium of Rs. 664.49).
- **Allotment Date**: July 13, 2026.
- **Distinctive Numbers**: 9748501 to 10399000.
- **Lock-in Period**: All 6,50,500 shares are locked-in until March 4, 2027.
- **Trading Commencement**: Thursday, August 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory framework changes are introduced. This is a routine listing and trading approval issued in accordance with BSE's Listing Operations and SEBI guidelines for the listing of new securities arising from preferential warrant conversions.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members are advised to take note of the new listing, trading commencement date, and lock-in details for clearing and settlement purposes. The company must ensure that the lock-in status of these distinctive numbers (9748501 to 10399000) is updated and maintained with the depositories (NSDL/CDSL) until March 4, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date**: July 13, 2026
- **Circular/Notice Date**: August 12, 2026
- **Trading Commencement Date**: August 13, 2026
- **Lock-in Expiry Date**: March 4, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Minimal. This is a routine individual corporate action with zero systemic or broader market-wide impact.
- **Operational Impact**: Trading members can commence trading in these shares from August 13, 2026. Clearing and settlement will handle these shares pari-passu with the existing listed shares of Sobhagya Mercantile Ltd., subject to lock-in check protocols where applicable.
