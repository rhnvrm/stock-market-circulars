## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance. Specific securities satisfying the inclusion criteria will attract a minimum 75% margin in the Equity and Equity Derivatives segments effective from August 19, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities listed in Annexure I attract a minimum 75% margin in Equity and Equity Derivatives segments w.e.f. August 19, 2026, on all open positions as on August 18, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Securities listed in Annexure II are eligible to move out from the framework effective from August 17, 2026.
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.
- The measure applies in conjunction with all other prevailing exchange measures and is subject to periodic review.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of a stringent minimum 75% margin requirement on securities identified with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure compliance with the 75% margin requirement for open and new positions on the specified effective dates for impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Exit (Annexure II):** August 17, 2026
- **Open Positions Cut-off Date:** August 18, 2026
- **Effective Date for Inclusion / Margin Applicability (Annexure I):** August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for listed securities with high encumbrance levels may impact leverage and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
