## Summary

BSE has issued a notice informing trading members about the trading in Rights Entitlements (REs) for Renunciation of DUCON INFRATECHNOLOGIES LIMITED starting September 03, 2026.

## Key Points

- Name of the Company: DUCON INFRATECHNOLOGIES LIMITED
- Type of Security: Rights Entitlements (REs)
- Scrip Code: 751137
- Scrip ID: DUCON-RE1
- ISIN No.: INE741L20034
- Market Lot: 1
- Group: R
- Trading Period for Renunciation: September 03, 2026 to September 07, 2026
- Rights Issue Period: September 03, 2026 to September 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

- Rights Entitlements shall be traded in dematerialized form only.
- Trades shall be settled on a T+1 Rolling Settlement basis and trade-for-trade basis.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must inform clients about the commencement of trading in REs and ensure they are not mistaken for ordinary shares.
- Contract notes must include a prominent disclaimer explaining that purchasing REs only provides the right to participate in the Rights Issue or renounce them before the closing date.
- Unsubscribed or unrenounced REs will lapse and be extinguished after the Issue Closing Date.

## Important Dates

- RE Trading Start Date: September 03, 2026
- RE Trading End Date: September 07, 2026
- Rights Issue Open Date: September 03, 2026
- Rights Issue Close Date: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables eligible shareholders and market participants to trade or renounce the rights entitlements of Ducon Infratechnologies Limited within the specified window.
