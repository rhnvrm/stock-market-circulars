## Summary

BSE has announced the application of Short-Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Stage I and Stage II to certain securities based on market surveillance criteria. The applicable surveillance actions, including revised margin rates, take effect from August 04, 2026.

## Key Points

- Securities satisfying the criteria have been included under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II.
- ST-ASM Stage I: Applicable rate of margin shall be 50% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective August 04, 2026, on all open positions as on August 03, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- ST-ASM Stage II: Applicable rate of margin shall be 100% or existing margin whichever is higher, capped at 100%, effective August 04, 2026, on all open positions as on August 03, 2026, and new positions created thereafter.
- Shortlisting under ASM is purely on account of market surveillance and should not be construed as an adverse action against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of stricter margin requirements for securities placed under ST-ASM Stage I and Stage II in conjunction with existing surveillance measures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and market participants must ensure compliance with the enhanced margin rates (50% or 100%) for open and new positions in the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 31 Jul 2026
- Effective Date for Open Positions (as of Aug 03, 2026) and New Positions: August 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements may affect liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities due to higher capital commitments required from market participants.
