## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the extension of the New Fund Offer (NFO) for HDFC Gold Silver Passive FoF on the BSE StAR MF platform. The NFO period is extended until midnight on August 27, 2026, for lumpsum purchases, with the revised allotment date scheduled for September 01, 2026, and the reopening date set for September 07, 2026.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Extended:** HDFC Gold Silver Passive FoF NFO extended to August 27, 2026.
- **Allotment Date:** Revised to September 01, 2026.
- **Reopening Date:** Revised to September 07, 2026.
- **SIP Authentication:** SIP/XSIP registrations must be authenticated by clients on or before 3:00 PM on August 27, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Transactions where clients do not initiate funds by 9:00 AM on T+1 day will be cancelled, and CC with 'N' flag reported to RTA/AMC.
- Clear funds must be available in the ICCL account by 6:00 PM on September 01, 2026.
- Switch-out funds received on or after the allotment date will not be considered for allotment.

## Important Dates

- **NFO Closure Date:** August 27, 2026
- **Allotment Date:** September 01, 2026
- **Reopening Date:** September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides operational clarity and extended timeline for investors and members participating in the HDFC Gold Silver Passive FoF NFO on the BSE StAR MF platform.
