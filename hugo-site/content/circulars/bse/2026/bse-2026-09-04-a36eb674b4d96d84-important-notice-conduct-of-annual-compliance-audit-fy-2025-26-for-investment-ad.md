## Summary

All Investment Advisers (IAs) are informed that the Annual Compliance Audit for FY 2025-26 must be conducted on or before September 30, 2026, in continuation of Exchange Notice No. 20260504-20.

## Key Points

- Deadline for conducting the Annual Compliance Audit for FY 2025-26 is September 30, 2026.
- The submission portal is expected to be live by the end of September 2026 with a separate intimation to follow.
- Two submission options will be provided: appointment of an auditor through the system or direct submission by the IA.
- Only submissions made via the Exchange portal will be considered valid; email or other modes are not accepted.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Investment Advisers must ensure the annual compliance audit is conducted by the specified deadline and submitted exclusively through the official Exchange portal once the window opens.

## Important Dates

- Audit Completion Deadline: September 30, 2026
- Portal Availability: End of September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational requirement for all registered Investment Advisers to ensure timely audit completion and valid online reporting to avoid non-compliance.
