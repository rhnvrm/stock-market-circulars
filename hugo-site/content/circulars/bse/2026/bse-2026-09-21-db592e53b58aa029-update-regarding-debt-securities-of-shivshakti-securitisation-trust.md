## Summary

Trading members are informed that the debt securities (NCDs) of Shivshakti Securitisation Trust will be available for trading with revised terms effective from Tuesday, September 22, 2026, following restructuring.

## Key Points

- Continuation of previous notice number 20250917-26 dated September 17, 2025.
- Scrip Code: 977121
- ISIN: INE2I7G15010
- Number of NCDs: 8,000
- Effective Date for revised terms: September 22, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Restructuring of debt securities terms as per Annexure 1.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members to note the revised terms for trading in the specified ISIN.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact limited to holders and traders of the specified NCDs of Shivshakti Securitisation Trust.
