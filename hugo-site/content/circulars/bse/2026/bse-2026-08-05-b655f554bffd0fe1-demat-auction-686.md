## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-686/2026-27. The notice outlines the schedule for auction offer entry, matching, delivery statements, and final reports on August 05, 2026, followed by the pay-in on August 06, 2026.

## Key Points

- Auction Notice Number: 20260805-3
- Rolling Settlement No: DR-686/2026-27
- Session Timings (Aug 05, 2026):
  - Auction offer entry: 02.00 p.m. to 02.45 p.m.
  - Auction Matching: 02.45 p.m. to 03.00 p.m.
  - Delivery Statement and report: 03.00 p.m. to 03.30 p.m.
  - Money Statement & final reports: 03.35 p.m. to 05.30 p.m.
- Pay-in Date: Thursday, Aug 06, 2026 at 08.00 a.m.
- Settlement is processed through Clearing Members following the uniform membership structure in the Equity Cash Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Restriction on trading members offering securities in auction for settlements where they faced short delivery has been removed following the implementation of the uniform membership structure.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and custodians must check auction pay-in obligations and execute pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members must exercise care while entering security codes and rates; corrections will not be permitted for punching errors.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: 05 Aug 2026
- Auction Session Date: 05 Aug 2026
- Pay-in Date: 06 Aug 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational circular affecting only trading members participating in the specific rolling settlement auction session with no broader market impact.
