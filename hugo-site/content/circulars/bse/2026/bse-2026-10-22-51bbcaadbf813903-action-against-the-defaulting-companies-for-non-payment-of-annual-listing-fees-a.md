## Summary

BSE has announced the revocation of suspension of trading in the equity shares of N2N Technologies Limited following compliance with the payment of outstanding Annual Listing Fees (ALF) under Regulation 14 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. Trading will resume effective October 27, 2026, and will continue in Trade to Trade for other reasons.

## Key Points

- Suspension of trading in equity shares of N2N Technologies Limited (Scrip Code: 512279) is revoked.
- Effective date of revocation: October 27, 2026.
- Trading will continue in the Trade to Trade segment for other reasons.
- Action taken pursuant to non-payment of Annual Listing Fees under Regulation 14 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement and compliance action regarding Annual Listing Fees.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and the concerned company must note the revocation and ensure compliance with ongoing listing obligations and disclosure requirements.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 22, 2026
- Effective Date of Revocation: October 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Investors and trading members dealing in N2N Technologies Limited can resume trading in the company's equity shares starting October 27, 2026, subject to Trade to Trade segment restrictions.
