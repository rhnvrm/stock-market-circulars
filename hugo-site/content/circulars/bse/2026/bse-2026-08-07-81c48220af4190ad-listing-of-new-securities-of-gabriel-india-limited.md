## Summary

BSE has informed market participants about the listing and permission for trading of 3,35,86,083 new equity shares of Gabriel India Limited with effect from August 10, 2026. These shares were issued pursuant to a composite scheme of arrangement involving Anchemco India Private Limited, Asia Investments Private Limited, and Gabriel India Limited.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Gabriel India Limited
- **Scrip Code:** 505714
- **Security Details:** 3,35,86,083 Equity Shares of Re.1/- each issued pursuant to the Composite Scheme of Arrangement.
- **Ranking:** Ranking pari-passu with the existing equity shares of the Company.
- **ISIN Number:** INE524A01029
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 150826138 – 184412220
- **Date of Allotment:** June 09, 2026
- **Effective Date of Trading:** August 10, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; administrative listing of securities pursuant to a corporate restructuring scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and depository participants need to note the ISIN and distinctive number range for settlement and trading.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allotment:** June 09, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 07, 2026
- **Listing/Trading Effective Date:** August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Expands the total listed equity base of Gabriel India Limited following the implementation of the composite scheme of arrangement.
