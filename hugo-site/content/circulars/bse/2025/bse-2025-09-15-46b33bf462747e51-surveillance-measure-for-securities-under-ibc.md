## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding surveillance measures for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective September 16, 2025, specific securities have been shortlisted under ASM Stage I or excluded from the framework based on corporate insolvency status.

## Key Points

- **Gensol Engineering Ltd** (Scrip Code: 542851, ISIN: INE06H201014) is shortlisted in Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for Companies as per IBC Stage 1 w.e.f. September 16, 2025.
- **Gayatri Projects Ltd** (Scrip Code: 532767, ISIN: INE336H01023) is excluded from ASM for Companies as per IBC w.e.f. September 15, 2025.
- A consolidated list of securities under various stages of the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) has been provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance stages under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework to monitor trading activity and protect investors in companies undergoing insolvency resolution processes.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages applicable to the listed securities and apply appropriate trading restrictions and margins as mandated.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date for Inclusions:** September 16, 2025
- **Effective Date for Exclusions:** September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in securities placed under ASM Stage I for IBC will attract heightened surveillance and specific margin requirements, impacting liquidity and trading mechanisms for the affected scrips.
