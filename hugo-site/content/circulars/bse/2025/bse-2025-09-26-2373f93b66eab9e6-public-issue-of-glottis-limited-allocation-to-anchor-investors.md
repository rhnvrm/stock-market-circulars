## Summary

BSE has announced the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors for the public issue of Glottis Limited. The company has finalized the allocation of 42,83,755 equity shares at an upper price band of ₹129 per share, successfully raising ₹55.26 crore from 7 anchor investors.

## Key Points

- **Total Amount Raised:** ₹55.26 crore
- **Total Shares Allotted:** 42,83,755 equity shares
- **Allotment Price:** ₹129 per equity share
- **Number of Anchor Investors:** 7 institutional entities (including Foreign Portfolio Investors, AIFs, and NBFCs)
- **Key Anchor Allocations:**
  - Meru Investment Fund PCC - CELL 1: 11,62,790 shares (₹15.00 crore)
  - LC Pharos Multi Strategy Fund VCC: 7,75,193 shares (₹10.00 crore)
  - VPK Global Ventures Fund - Scheme I: 7,75,193 shares (₹10.00 crore)
  - Abans Finance Private Limited: 3,92,638 shares (₹5.065 crore)
  - Sunrise Investment Opportunities Fund: 3,92,645 shares (₹5.065 crore)
  - M7 Global Fund PCC - Amaltas India Opportunities Fund: 3,92,648 shares (₹5.065 crore)
  - The Asio Fund VCC - The Asio Fund: 3,92,648 shares (₹5.065 crore)

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 regarding anchor investor bidding, allocation, and lock-in requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- 50% of the shares allotted to anchor investors are locked in for 30 days.
- Remaining 50% of the shares allotted to anchor investors are locked in for 90 days.

## Important Dates

- **Allotment Date:** September 26, 2025
- **Anchor Lock-in (30 Days):** Ends on October 30, 2025
- **Anchor Lock-in (90 Days):** Ends on December 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides strong institutional backing signals for Glottis Limited's initial public offering, establishing benchmark demand and price discovery prior to the main public bidding window.
