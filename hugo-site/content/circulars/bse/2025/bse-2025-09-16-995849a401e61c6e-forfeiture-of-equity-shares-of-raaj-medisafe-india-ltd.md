## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the forfeiture of equity shares of Raaj Medisafe India Limited, detailing the list of shareholders holding partly paid-up equity shares with unpaid call money amounts.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Raaj Medisafe India Limited
- Details partly paid-up equity shares with an amount paid-up of Rs. 5.00 per share.
- Lists folio numbers, shareholder names, certificate numbers, distinctive numbers, number of shares, and due amounts.
- Last reminder for payment was sent on 25.03.2025.
