## Summary

BSE has published the demat auction list for Settlement No. 725 dated September 30, 2025. The auction covers multiple securities subject to auction due to delivery shortfalls in the normal settlement cycle.

## Key Points

- Settlement Number: 725
- Auction Date: September 30, 2025
- Purpose: Settlement of demat delivery shortfalls through auction mechanism

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard exchange settlement operations under BSE market regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and participants involved in delivery shortages must participate in the demat auction process as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Routine market operations ensuring timely settlement of securities and mitigation of delivery default risks.
