## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the updation of correct and valid FATCA/CRS information, detailing the revised Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) ID type master codes and descriptions.

## Key Points

- Revised TIN ID type master has been published.
- Includes 10 specific ID types ranging from Student Visa to Tax Payer Identification Number (TIN).
- Essential for accurate FATCA/CRS reporting and client onboarding compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Update to the recognized ID types for FATCA/CRS compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Reporting entities and intermediaries must ensure valid and correct FATCA/CRS information is updated using the revised ID type master.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects client onboarding, KYC validation, and regulatory reporting databases for FATCA/CRS compliance.
