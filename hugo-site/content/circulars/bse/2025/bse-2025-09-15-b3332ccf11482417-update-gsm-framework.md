## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, detailing securities that are subject to enhanced surveillance measures, applicable price bands, and trading groups.

## Key Points

- Lists numerous securities with their respective Scrip Codes, Full Names, ISIN Codes, Applicable Price Bands, and Applicable Groups.
- Securities include 7NR Retail Ltd, Aarcon Facilities Ltd, Amalgamated Electricity Company Ltd, and many others.
- Price bands range from 2% to 20% depending on the specific security's classification under the GSM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance actions via the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework to protect investors and curb excessive volatility in specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the revised price bands and surveillance categories applicable to the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective date corresponding to the circular release on September 15, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified scrips will be restricted by the assigned price bands and surveillance stages, impacting liquidity and trading mechanisms for these equities.
