## Summary

Groww Mutual Fund has issued a notice cum addendum regarding revisions to the Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) features across its schemes (except ETFs), effective October 6, 2025.

## Key Points

- Weekly STPs can now be initiated on any day from Monday to Friday, with Friday as the default.
- No minimum balance is required in the transferee scheme for registering an STP or SWP.
- Registration and cancellation requests require a minimum notice of 7 calendar days.
- The default SWP date is set to the 15th of every month if unspecified.

## Regulatory Changes

- Update to Scheme Information Document (SID), Key Information Memorandum (KIM), and Statement of Additional Information (SAI) terms for Groww Mutual Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors and distributors must take note of the revised operational timelines and default parameters for STP/SWP transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 6, 2025
- Notice Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Provides greater flexibility to investors for structuring periodic transfers and withdrawals across Groww Mutual Fund schemes.
