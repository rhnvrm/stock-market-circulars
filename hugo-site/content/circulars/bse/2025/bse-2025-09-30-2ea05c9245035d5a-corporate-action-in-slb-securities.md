## Summary

This circular provides details regarding corporate actions such as dividends, stock splits, and bonus issues for various securities participating in the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) scheme, specifying record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods for October 2025.

## Key Points

- **ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited**: Dividend, Record Date: 23-October-2025, Ex-Date: 23-October-2025, Shut Period: 21-Oct-2025 to 23-Oct-2025.
- **Rashtriya Chemicals & Fertilizers Limited**: Dividend, Record Date: 10-October-2025, Ex-Date: 10-October-2025, Shut Period: 09-Oct-2025 to 10-Oct-2025.
- **Tata Investment Corporation Ltd.**: Stock Split, Record Date: 14-October-2025, Ex-Date: 14-October-2025, Shut Period: 13-Oct-2025 to 14-Oct-2025.
- **Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd**: Dividend, Record Date: 03-October-2025, Ex-Date: 03-October-2025, Shut Period: 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.
- **Shilpa Medicare Ltd.**: Bonus Issue, Record Date: 03-October-2025, Ex-Date: 03-October-2025, Foreclosure Date: 03-Oct-2025, Shut Period: 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine corporate action notification for market participants and SLB operations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, custodians, and trading members must take note of the specified record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods to manage obligations and settlements appropriately.

## Important Dates

- **October 3, 2025**: Ex-date and record date for Glenmark Pharmaceuticals and Shilpa Medicare.
- **October 10, 2025**: Ex-date and record date for Rashtriya Chemicals & Fertilizers.
- **October 14, 2025**: Ex-date and record date for Tata Investment Corporation.
- **October 23, 2025**: Ex-date and record date for ICICI Lombard General Insurance.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth processing of corporate actions within the SLB segment and prevents settlement discrepancies during shut periods.
