## Summary

Jinkushal Industries Limited has finalized the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors ahead of its initial public offering (IPO). The company allocated 28,78,500 equity shares to anchor investors at an allocation price of ₹121 per share, successfully raising ₹34,82,98,500.

## Key Points

- Anchor Investor Bidding Date: September 24, 2025.
- Allocation Price: ₹121 per equity share.
- Total Shares Allocated: 28,78,500 equity shares.
- Total Amount Raised: ₹34.83 crore.
- Key Anchor Investors included Nomura Singapore Limited ODI, HDFC Bank Limited, Viney Growth Fund, Steptrade Revolution Fund, Santosh Industries Limited, and Swyom India Alpha Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must adhere to SEBI ICDR regulations concerning anchor investor lock-in periods and subsequent public issue disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 24, 2025
- Issue Opening Date: September 25, 2025
- Issue Closing Date: September 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

The successful anchor book build demonstrates strong institutional interest, setting a positive tone for the public subscription phase of the IPO.
