## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a member user manual detailing the process for Cyber Incident Reporting (CIR) through the BSE Electronic Filling System (BEFS). The manual covers both quarterly incident reporting and immediate incident reporting requirements for trading members.

## Key Points

- **Quarterly Incident Reporting**: Designated Officers must submit quarterly reports within 15 days after the end of the respective quarter via the BEFS portal.
- **Immediate Incident Reporting**: All cyber-attacks, threats, incidents, and breaches must be reported within 6 hours of receipt of information.
- **Template Usage**: Members are required to download macro-enabled Excel templates from the BEFS portal, fill out the required details, validate the sheets, and upload the completed forms.
- **Digital Signing**: Uploaded Excel forms get converted to PDF, which must be digitally signed by the designated officer and re-uploaded to complete submission.
- **Acknowledgements**: System-generated acknowledgement emails are sent to Compliance Officers upon successful submission.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clarifies mandatory timelines and procedures for reporting cyber incidents via BEFS.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure designated officers utilize the BEFS portal for downloading templates, validating data, and uploading digitally signed reports within the stipulated timelines (15 days for quarterly reports, 6 hours for immediate incidents).

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for ongoing member compliance and incident reporting.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances operational risk management and ensures timely disclosure of cybersecurity events to the exchange.
