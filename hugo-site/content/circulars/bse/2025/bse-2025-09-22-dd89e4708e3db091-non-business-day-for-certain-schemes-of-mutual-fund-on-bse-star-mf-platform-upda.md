## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding non-business days for certain mutual fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform, specifically affecting schemes managed by Nippon India Mutual Fund and ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund for the date 2025-09-23.

## Key Points

- **Nippon India Mutual Fund**: NIPPON INDIA JAPAN EQUITY FUND has a non-business day on 2025-09-23.
- **ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund**: ICICI Prudential Global Advantage Fund has a non-business day on 2025-09-23.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors should take note of the non-business day declarations while executing transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: 2025-09-23

## Impact Assessment

Transactions for the specified schemes will not be processed on the declared non-business day.
