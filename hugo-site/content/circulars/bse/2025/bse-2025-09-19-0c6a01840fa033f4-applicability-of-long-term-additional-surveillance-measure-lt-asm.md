## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective September 22, 2025, several securities are shortlisted, moved between stages, or excluded from the LT-ASM framework.

## Key Points

- **Part A:** 6 securities are shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework (e.g., Golden Crest Education & Services Ltd, IEC Education Ltd).
- **Part B:** Nil securities moving to higher stages of the LT-ASM framework.
- **Part C:** 1 security (Stallion India Fluorochemicals Ltd) is moving to a lower stage (Stage I).
- **Part D:** Nil securities shortlisted in Direct stage IV LT-ASM framework.
- **Annexure II:** 22 securities are moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework, with several moving into the Trade for Trade framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the LT-ASM framework to monitor volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must note the surveillance status changes and apply corresponding trading restrictions, such as applicable margins or trade-for-trade settlement where specified.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affects liquidity and trading mechanics for the listed securities due to entry, movement across stages, or exit from the surveillance framework.
