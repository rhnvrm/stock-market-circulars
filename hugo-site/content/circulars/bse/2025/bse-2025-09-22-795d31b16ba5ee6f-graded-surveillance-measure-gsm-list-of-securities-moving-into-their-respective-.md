## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the movement of securities into respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages. The list includes ten securities moving across GSM stages I, II, III, and IV.

## Key Points

- Worldwide Aluminium Ltd, Sybly Industries Ltd, Galaxy Agrico Exports Ltd, and Shivansh Finserve Ltd have moved to GSM Stage I.
- Rajkamal Synthetics Ltd, Prashant India Ltd, Artson Ltd, and Golechha Global Finance Ltd have moved to GSM Stage II.
- Nagarjuna Agri Tech Ltd has moved to GSM Stage III.
- Tirth Plastic Ltd has moved to GSM Stage IV.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework for identified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors are advised to note the surveillance measures applicable to the respective securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in these securities will be subject to surveillance constraints such as separate trading margins or trade-for-trade settlement depending on the assigned GSM stage.
