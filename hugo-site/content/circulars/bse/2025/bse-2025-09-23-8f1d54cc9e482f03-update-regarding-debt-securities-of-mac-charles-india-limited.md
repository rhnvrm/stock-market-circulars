## Summary

This circular provides updates regarding the terms and security structures associated with the debt securities of MAC Charles (India) Limited, specifying modifications to existing charges, mortgages, hypothecation, and pledges.

## Key Points

- Details the release of certain initial continuing debentures and guarantees.
- Outlines revised terms involving first ranking exclusive charges by way of deeds of hypothecation.
- Specifies equitable mortgages and pledges executed by Hub SPV, SDPL, and the Company.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of security cover and asset hypothecation terms for listed debt instruments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and trustees must ensure proper execution and registration of modified charges and hypothecation deeds as specified.

## Important Dates

- Effective as per the relevant agreements and dates mentioned in the annexure (e.g., 23 August 2022 amendments and restatements).

## Impact Assessment

- Affects the security backing and collateral structure for debenture holders of MAC Charles (India) Limited.
