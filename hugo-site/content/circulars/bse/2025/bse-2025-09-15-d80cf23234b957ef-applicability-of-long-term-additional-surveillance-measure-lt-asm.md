## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective September 16, 2025. The circular details the list of securities shortlisted under various parts of the ASM framework, including new entries and consolidated lists.

## Key Points

- Five securities are shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework (Part A) effective September 16, 2025.
- Companies included feature Dynamic Portfolio Management & Services Ltd, Odigma Consultancy Solutions Ltd, Sharat Industries Ltd, Shilp Gravures Ltd, and Wardwizard Innovations & Mobility Ltd.
- A comprehensive consolidated list of securities continuing under the ASM Stage I and other stages is provided for market participant compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures to monitor volatility and protect investors.
- Application of applicable ASM margin requirements and trading controls on the shortlisted scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance status and ensure appropriate risk management controls and margin collections for transactions in the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and potential trading restrictions for the shortlisted securities, enhancing market integrity and cautioning investors regarding price volatility.
