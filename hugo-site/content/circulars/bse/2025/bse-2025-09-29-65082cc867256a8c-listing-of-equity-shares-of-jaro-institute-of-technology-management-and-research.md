## Summary

BSE has issued a circular detailing the listing of equity shares and associated lock-in particulars for Jaro Institute of Technology Management and Research Limited.

## Key Points

- Details various categories of equity shares under lock-in and those not under lock-in.
- Specifies lock-in dates ranging from August 22, 2025, to March 29, 2027.
- Highlights specific exemptions and conditions for ESOP shares, Alternative Investment Funds (AIF), and Anchor Investors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with Regulation 17(c) of SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018 regarding lock-in requirements for specified funds.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and depositories must adhere to the specified lock-in periods and distinct numbers/demat details for the listed shares.

## Important Dates

- Prospectus Date: 25-Sep-2025
- Various lock-in commencement and expiration dates spanning August 2025 to March 2027 (e.g., Anchor investor lock-ins up to 26-Oct-2025 and 25-Dec-2025).

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures transparency regarding tradable vs. locked-in equity shares post-IPO for Jaro Institute of Technology Management and Research Limited.
