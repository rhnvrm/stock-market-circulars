## Summary

BSE has announced the revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares of Mahip Industries Ltd. (Scrip Code: 542503) pursuant to the company's fulfillment of compliance requirements and payment of pending dues.

## Key Points

- Trading suspension for Mahip Industries Ltd. has been officially revoked.
- The company cleared outstanding listing fees and SOP fines.
- Trading members and investors are notified of the resumption of trading in the scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

- Removal of trading restrictions previously imposed due to non-compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company must continue to maintain ongoing compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations and BSE listing norms.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity restored for shareholders of Mahip Industries Ltd. as trading resumes on the exchange platform.
