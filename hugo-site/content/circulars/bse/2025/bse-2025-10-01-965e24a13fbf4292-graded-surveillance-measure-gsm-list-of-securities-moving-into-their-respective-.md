## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, detailing securities moving into their respective GSM stages or being adjusted due to inclusion in ESM or IBC frameworks.

## Key Points

- Mudunuru Ltd (Security Code: 538743) moved to GSM Stage 0.
- Bijoy Hans Ltd (Security Code: 524723) moved to GSM Stage II.
- City Online Services Limited (Security Code: 538674) moved to GSM Stage II.
- Adjia Technologies Limited (Security Code: 543269) moved to GSM Stage II.
- Charms Industries Ltd (Security Code: 531327) moved to GSM Stage IV.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of trading restrictions and surveillance monitoring under the GSM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must take note of the surveillance stages applicable to these scrips.

## Important Dates

Effective as per the circular release date.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in affected securities will be subject to heightened surveillance and margin requirements corresponding to their assigned GSM stage.
