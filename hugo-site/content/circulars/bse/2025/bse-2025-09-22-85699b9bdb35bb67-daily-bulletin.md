## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 120/2025-2026 outlines upcoming book closures, record dates, interest payments, and corporate actions scheduled for various companies spanning commercial papers, non-convertible debentures, and equity shares for late September through November 2025.

## Key Points

- **Adani Enterprises Ltd.:** Record date set for September 26, 2025, for commercial paper redemption.
- **Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd.:** Fixed October 3, 2025, as the record date for interim dividend.
- **India Glycols Ltd.:** Record date on September 23, 2025, for final dividend of Rs. 5.00 per share (100%).
- **Mahindra Rural Housing Finance Limited & Manba Finance Limited:** Scheduled multiple record dates for NCD part redemptions and interest payments through October and November 2025.
- **Haldia Petrochemicals, Healthy Life Agritec, and Hiren Wahen Buildtech:** Announced record dates for bond interest payments and rights issues.

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure timely processing of corporate actions, update member registries according to the specified record dates and book closure periods, and fulfill payment obligations to security holders.

## Important Dates

- **23/09/2025:** India Glycols Ltd. (Record Date - Final Dividend)
- **24/09/2025:** E.I.D. Parry (India) Ltd. (Record Date - CP Redemption)
- **26/09/2025:** Adani Enterprises Ltd. & Healthy Life Agritec Limited (Record Date)
- **27/09/2025:** Haldia Petrochemicals Limited (Record Date)
- **28/09/2025:** E.I.D. Parry (India) Ltd. & Mahindra Rural Housing Finance Limited (Record Date)
- **30/09/2025:** Hiren Wahen Buildtech private Limited (Record Date)
- **03/10/2025:** Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd. (Record Date - Interim Dividend)
- **05/10/2025 to 07/11/2025:** Various dates for Manba Finance and Moneyboxx Finance NCD interest and part-redemption payments.

## Impact Assessment

Market participants holding commercial papers, debentures, or equities of the listed companies must note the respective record dates and book closures to ensure eligibility for dividends, interest payouts, and redemption proceeds.
