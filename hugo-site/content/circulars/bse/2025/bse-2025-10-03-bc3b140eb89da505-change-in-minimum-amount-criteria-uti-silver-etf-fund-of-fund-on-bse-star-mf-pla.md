## Summary

UTI Asset Management Company Limited has issued an addendum announcing a revision to the minimum investment, SIP, and redemption amounts for the UTI Silver ETF Fund of Fund. The changes become effective from October 3, 2025, lowering the financial barrier for investors entering or transacting in the scheme.

## Key Points

- **Initial Investment:** Revised from Rs. 5,000/- to Rs. 100/- and in multiples of Re. 1/- thereafter.
- **SIP Amount:** Daily, weekly, and monthly SIP minimum amounts reduced from Rs. 500/- to Rs. 100/-.
- **Subsequent Investment:** Minimum amount under a folio lowered from Rs. 1,000/- to Rs. 100/-.
- **Redemption Amount:** Minimum redemption amount reduced from Rs. 1,000/- to Rs. 100/-.

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable (Scheme addendum under standard mutual fund guidelines).

## Compliance Requirements

- The addendum serves as an integral part of the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) and must be read in conjunction with them.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 3, 2025
- **Notice Date:** September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- **Investor Impact:** Lower thresholds increase accessibility for retail investors participating through systematic investment plans (SIPs) or smaller lump-sum allocations.
