## Summary

BSE has released the Tender Period Margin (TPM) Tracker for October 2025 applicable to the Commodity Derivatives Segment ("BCX"). The circular outlines the progressive escalation of tender period margins and minimum delivery margins for futures contracts including Gold, GoldM, Silver, SilverKG, and SilverM expiring in October 2025.

## Key Points

- Covers commodity futures contracts including GOLD, GOLDM, SILVER, SILVERKG, and SILVERM maturing in October 2025.
- Details daily tender period margin rates ramping up from 0.05% initially to 0.25% as expiration approaches.
- Highlights holidays during the tender period where margins are marked accordingly.
- Specifies a minimum delivery margin of 0.25% for the applicable contracts.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of scheduled tender period margins and delivery margins for commodity derivatives as per exchange risk management norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must maintain adequate margins as specified for respective tender period days to avoid shortfall penalties.

## Important Dates

- Effective dates span across September and October 2025 depending on contract expiries (e.g., October 3, 2025 and October 31, 2025).

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding positions near expiry must fund increased margin requirements during the tender period.
