## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the inclusion and retention of securities in the Trade-to-Trade ("T", "XT", "MT", "TS") segment with a price band of 5% or lower, effective from September 18, 2025.

## Key Points

- Annexure-I details the list of securities being shifted from the Rolling segment to the Trade for Trade segment (including SME segment) with a price band of 5% or lower.
- Annexure-II lists the securities retained in the Trade for Trade segment (including SME segment).
- Changes become effective on September 18, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

Transfer of specified rolling segment securities into the trade-for-trade category, imposing stricter settlement and price band restrictions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with trade-to-trade settlement rules for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity for the affected scrips may be reduced due to the removal of netting off in intraday trades and the imposition of a 5% price band.
