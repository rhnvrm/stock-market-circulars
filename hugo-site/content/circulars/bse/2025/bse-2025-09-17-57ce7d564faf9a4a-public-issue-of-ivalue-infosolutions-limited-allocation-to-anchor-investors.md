## Summary

Ivalue Infosolutions Limited has finalized the allocation of 56,21,686 equity shares to anchor investors at an allocation price of Rs. 299 per equity share (including a share premium of Rs. 297 per equity share) for its public offer.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares allocated to anchor investors: 56,21,686 shares.
- Anchor Investor allocation price: Rs. 299 per equity share (face value Rs. 2, premium Rs. 297).
- Meeting date for Board of Directors and Selling Shareholders: September 17, 2025.
- Major anchor investors include ICICI Prudential Innovation Fund, Unifi Bcad Fund, UTI Small Cap Fund, and Nuvama Crossover Opportunities Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with standard public issue and anchor investor allocation guidelines under SEBI and exchange regulations.

## Important Dates

- Allocation Date: September 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Initiates the public offer process with successful anchor investor participation, signaling institutional interest in Ivalue Infosolutions Limited's public issue.
