## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of Interest Rate Derivatives (IRD) contracts, covering both Futures and Options, on the underlying Government of India (GOI) security 6.01% maturing on July 21, 2030 (Symbol: 601GS2030).

## Key Points

- **Underlying Security**: 6.01% GOI Maturing on July 21, 2030 (Symbol for spot market feeds from CCIL NDS: 601GSEC2030).
- **Futures Contract Specifications**:
  - Symbol: FUTIRD
  - Trading Hours: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (aligned with NDS-OM).
  - Contract Unit: 1 contract = 2,000 bonds * 100 face value = INR 200,000.
  - Tick Size: 0.0025.
  - Settlement: Cash settled in INR on T+1 day.
- **Options Contract Specifications**:
  - Symbol: OPTIRD
  - Option Type: Premium Style European Call and Put Options.
  - Strike Price Intervals: INR 0.25.
  - Trading Hours: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.

## Regulatory Changes

- Contracts are introduced in accordance with SEBI circular guidelines, including position limits as per SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/MRD/CIR/P/2019/103.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adherence to position limits, order quantity limits (maximum quantity limit of 1250 orders/lots), and price bands stipulated for the newly introduced IRD contracts.

## Important Dates

- Effective for trading as per exchange schedule following the circular announcement.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants with enhanced tools for interest rate risk management and hedging using government security derivatives.
