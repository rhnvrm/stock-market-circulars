## Summary

Solarworld Energy Solutions Limited has issued lock-in details for its equity shares following its IPO, specifying lock-in periods for anchor investors, promoter holdings, and general equity allocations.

## Key Points

- Details various tranches of equity shares under lock-in and free categories.
- Specifies 30-day (upto 25th October, 2025) and 90-day (upto 24th December, 2025) lock-in periods for shares allocated to Anchor Investors.
- Clarifies that shares held by Value Quest Scale Fund (Alternative Investment Fund - Category II) are not under lock-in in terms of Regulation 17(c) of SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018.

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and investors must adhere to the specified lock-in dates and conditions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: 28.09.2025
- Anchor Lock-in Expiry (30 days): 25-Oct-2025
- Anchor Lock-in Expiry (90 days): 24-Dec-2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding share availability and lock-in expirations for trading in the equity segment.
