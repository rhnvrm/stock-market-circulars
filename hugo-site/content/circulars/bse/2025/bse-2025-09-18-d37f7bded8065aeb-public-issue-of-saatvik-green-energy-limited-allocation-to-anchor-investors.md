BSE has issued a notification regarding the public issue and allocation of equity shares to anchor investors of Saatvik Green Energy Limited. The notice outlines the details of the anchor investor bidding, allocation price, and shares allotted ahead of the main public issue opening.

- Corporate Action: Public Issue / Anchor Investor Allocation
