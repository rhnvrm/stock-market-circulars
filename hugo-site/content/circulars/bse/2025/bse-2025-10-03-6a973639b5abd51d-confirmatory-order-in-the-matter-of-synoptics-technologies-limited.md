## Summary

SEBI issued a confirmatory order under sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992, in the matter of Synoptics Technologies Limited, following preliminary findings of irregularities in its SME IPO bidding process.

## Key Points

- Examination conducted following complaints regarding irregularities in the SME IPO bidding process of Synoptics Technologies Limited in June-July 2023.
- Interim directions restrained the company, its promoters, and the lead manager (First Overseas Capital Limited) from certain market activities.
- Company raised INR 54.04 Crore through the public issue.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of restraint orders and monitoring requirements issued in the interim order.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees remain restrained from buying, selling, or dealing in securities directly or indirectly until further orders.
- Lead manager restricted from taking up new assignments relating to merchant banking until further directions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Interim Order: May 06, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on the involved company, promoters, and lead manager due to ongoing market restrictions.
