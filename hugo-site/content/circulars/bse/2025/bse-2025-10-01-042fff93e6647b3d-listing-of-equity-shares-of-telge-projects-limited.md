## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Telge Projects Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The circular provides a detailed breakdown of security quantities, distinctive numbers, lock-in periods, and whether the securities are fully paid and held in demat form.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Telge Projects Limited
- Event: IPO Listing of Equity Shares
- Total securities include various tranches with different lock-in expiry dates ranging from 2025 to 2028.
- Includes anchor investor allocations and free shares as part of the IPO structure.
- All listed securities are in dematerialized (DEMAT) form and fully paid.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and depository participants must ensure adherence to the specified lock-in dates and trading restrictions for the respective tranches of shares.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in expiry dates range across September and October 2025, as well as subsequent years up to 2028 depending on the tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Telge Projects Limited shares on the exchange platform while enforcing regulatory lock-in constraints on promoter and anchor holdings.
