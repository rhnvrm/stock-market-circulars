## Summary

DCM Shriram Ltd. has submitted a clarification letter to BSE in response to an inquiry regarding significant volume movement in the company's shares on September 18, 2025.

## Key Points

- The company confirmed that all necessary disclosures required under Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 have been duly and timely made.
- There are no pending or undisclosed events, information, or announcements that would explain the recent volume movement.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Continued compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 regarding timely disclosures of material events.

## Important Dates

- **Letter Date:** 18th September 2025

## Impact Assessment

No direct operational or financial impact; standard procedural compliance response to exchange surveillance.
