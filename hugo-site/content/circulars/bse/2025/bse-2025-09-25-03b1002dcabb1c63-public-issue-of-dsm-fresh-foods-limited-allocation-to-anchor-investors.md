## Summary

DSM Fresh Foods Limited has finalized the allocation of 16,66,800 equity shares to anchor investors at an allocation price of ₹101 per equity share (including a share premium of ₹91) as part of its public issue.

## Key Points

- Total Equity Shares Allocated to Anchor Investors: 16,66,800 shares.
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: ₹101 per share.
- Total Anchor Portion Amount: ₹16,83,46,800.
- Key Anchor Investors include Viney Growth Fund, Craft Emerging Market Fund, J4S Venture Fund, and Narnolia India Opportunity Fund among others.

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- In case the Offer Price discovered through the book building process is higher than the anchor investor allocation price, anchor investors are required to pay the difference by the pay-in specified in the revised CAN.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allocation / Board Meeting: September 25, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Signals institutional participation and sets the baseline pricing context ahead of the public issue opening.
