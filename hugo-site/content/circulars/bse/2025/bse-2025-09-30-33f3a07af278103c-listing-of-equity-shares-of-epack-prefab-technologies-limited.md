## Summary

EPACK PREFAB TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED has submitted its shareholding pattern and listing details for its equity shares following a post-issue filing under Regulation 31(1)(a)/Reg.31(1)(b)/Reg.31(1)(c).

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: EPACK PREFAB TECHNOLOGIES LIMITED
- Total number of shares: 100,451,997 fully paid-up equity shares.
- Promoter and Promoter Group hold 64,834,088 shares (64.54%).
- Public shareholders hold 35,617,909 shares (35.46%).
- Total number of shareholders: 120,927.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Dissemination of shareholding pattern pursuant to SEBI Listing Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Report for quarter/allotment ending: 30-09-2025

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational disclosure with no direct market impact.
