## Summary

BSE has issued guidelines and procedural instructions for members regarding the online submission of the Annual Audited Networth Certificate, Computation of Networth, Auditor's Report, and Audited Annual Accounts for the financial year ended March 31, 2025, via the BSE electronic portal.

## Key Points

- Submissions must be completed online through the BSE portal (https://befs.bseindia.com).
- Requires specific browser settings (Internet Explorer or compatible trusted sites configuration with ActiveX enabled).
- Members must select the yearly mode for financial year 31/03/2025 and specify whether networth is calculated on a standalone or consolidated basis.
- Mandatory inclusion of statutory auditor details, including PAN of the CA firm and partner/director.

## Regulatory Changes

- Strict adherence to online portal submission standards for annual financial disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members are required to upload scanned documents and enter audited computation details directly into the BSE portal.
- If any field is not applicable, members must enter '0'.
- Submissions undergo exchange scrutiny; status updates are reflected on the portal as 'Submitted', 'Networth Approved', or 'Networth Rejected'.

## Important Dates

- Financial Year Ended: March 31, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures regulatory compliance and verification of financial strength for exchange members through standard reporting mechanisms.
