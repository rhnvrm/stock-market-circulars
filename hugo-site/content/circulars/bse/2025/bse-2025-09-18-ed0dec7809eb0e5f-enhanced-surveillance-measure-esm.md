## Summary

BSE has issued a circular detailing updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Effective September 19, 2025, new securities have been added to the framework, while others have been moved to higher stages of ESM.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities in the ESM Framework effective September 19, 2025, including Global Capital Markets Ltd, Maitri Enterprises Ltd, and Ravinder Heights Ltd.
- Movement of existing securities to higher Stage ESM, including Bhatia Colour Chem Ltd, Miven Machine Tools Ltd, Navkar Urbanstructure Ltd, Sanblue Corporation Ltd, Sunrise Efficient Marketing Ltd, and Tecil Chemicals & Hydro Power Ltd.
- Provision of a consolidated list of securities under the ESM framework indicating respective stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework to monitor and curb excessive price volatility in specific securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the ESM framework stages and apply applicable trading restrictions and margin requirements for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, potentially impacting liquidity and margin requirements for investors.
