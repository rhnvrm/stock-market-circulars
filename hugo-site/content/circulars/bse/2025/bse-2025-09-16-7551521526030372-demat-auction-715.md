## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding Demat Auction - 715, detailing the auction schedule, participating guidelines, and settlement procedures for securities falling under the auction mechanism.

## Key Points

- Notice details auction schedule for demat securities.
- Trading members and clearing participants must adhere to the specified timelines and obligations.
- Applicable to designated scrips under shortage or auction settlement.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard exchange operational procedures for auction settlement of securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members involved in delivery shortages must ensure necessary funds and securities are available as per the auction schedule.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: As specified in the circular schedule.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth clearing and settlement of trades facing delivery shortages on the exchange platform.
