## Summary

SEBI has issued a consultation paper to review the existing framework for addressing technical glitches in stock brokers' electronic trading systems, originally established in November 2022. The proposed changes aim to ease compliance burdens by excluding smaller stock brokers, simplifying reporting requirements, and clarifying the definition of technical glitches.

## Key Points

- Definition of technical glitch is proposed to be modified to exclude occurrences outside trading hours and incidents outside brokers' control.
- The framework will apply to stock brokers providing IBT/STWT trading platforms with more than 10,000 registered clients.
- Approximately 457 smaller stock brokers will be excluded from the framework, reducing compliance burdens.
- Reporting of technical glitches is streamlined to a common reporting platform to avoid multiple submissions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revised eligibility criteria focusing exclusively on brokers with active client bases exceeding 10,000.
- Rationalization of the financial disincentive and reporting structure.
- Introduction of a centralized common reporting mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock brokers meeting the criteria must adhere to simplified reporting timelines and procedures via the common reporting platform.
- Smaller brokers falling below the 10,000 client threshold are exempted from these specific framework requirements.

## Important Dates

- Consultation paper issued based on reviews conducted through stakeholder and stock broker ISF recommendations.

## Impact Assessment

- Significantly lowers compliance costs and operational overhead for smaller stock brokers while maintaining robust oversight on larger technology-dominant trading platforms.
