## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding a non-business day on September 23, 2025, for specific schemes offered by Nippon India Mutual Fund and ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: 2025-09-23
- Affected Fund Houses: Nippon India Mutual Fund, ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund
- Affected Schemes: Nippon India Japan Equity Fund and ICICI Prudential Global Advantage Fund

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Members and investors should note that regular transactions for the specified schemes will not be processed on the declared non-business day.

## Important Dates

- Non-Business Day: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Transactions in the specified mutual fund schemes will remain restricted on the mentioned date, affecting subscription, redemption, and switch orders routed through the BSE StAR MF platform.
