## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Austere Systems Limited along with the submission of the shareholding pattern under Regulation 31.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Austere Systems Limited
- Total No. of Shares: 10,478,134
- Promoter & Promoter Group holding: 6,999,485 shares (66.80%)
- Public holding: 3,478,649 shares (33.20%)
- Locked-in shares: 1,448,649 shares for public category

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Submission of holding of specified securities in accordance with Regulation 31(1)(a)/31(1)(b)/31(1)(c).

## Important Dates

- Report for Quarter Ending: 10/09/2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate listing update with no broad market impact.
