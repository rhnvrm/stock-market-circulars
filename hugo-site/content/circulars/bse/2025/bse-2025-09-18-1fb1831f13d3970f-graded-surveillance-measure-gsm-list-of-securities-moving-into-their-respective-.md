## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the list of securities moving into their respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, detailing changes for various listed companies.

## Key Points

- Maitri Enterprises Ltd moved to GSM Stage 0 (marked # due to inclusion in ESM Framework).
- Companies including Chennai Meenakshi Multispeciality Hospital Ltd, Promact Impex Ltd, and Scintilla Commercial & Credit Ltd moved to GSM Stage I.
- Lynx Machinery & Commercials Ltd, Span Divergent Ltd, and Kiran Print Pack Ltd moved to GSM Stage II.
- Peoples Investments Ltd, Integra Switchgear Ltd, and Shivamshree Businesses Ltd moved to GSM Stage III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the Graded Surveillance Measure framework for specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must note the specific GSM stage transitions and applicable trading restrictions for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, margin requirements, or trading frequency restrictions as per the respective GSM stage.
