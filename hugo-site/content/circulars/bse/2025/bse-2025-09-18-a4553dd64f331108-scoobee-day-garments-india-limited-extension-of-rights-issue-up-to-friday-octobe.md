## Summary

Scoobee Day Garments (India) Limited has announced an extension of its ongoing Rights Issue period by 21 days. The revised closure date for the issue is now Friday, October 10, 2025, shifting all subsequent timelines including allotment, credit, and listing dates accordingly.

## Key Points

- Issue period extended by 21 days.
- New Issue Closing Date: Friday, October 10, 2025 (previously September 19, 2025).
- Issue Opening Date remains unchanged at Friday, September 12, 2025.
- Last Date for On Market Renunciation of Rights Entitlements remains unchanged at Tuesday, September 16, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Extension carried out in compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company will publish the revised schedule advertisement in newspapers and submit the same to the stock exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** September 12, 2025 (Unchanged)
- **Last Date for On Market Renunciation:** September 16, 2025 (Unchanged)
- **Issue Closing Date:** October 10, 2025 (Revised)
- **Finalisation of Basis of Allotment:** October 17, 2025 (Revised)
- **Date of Allotment:** October 20, 2025 (Revised)
- **Date of Credit:** October 24, 2025 (Revised)
- **Date of Listing:** October 29, 2025 (Revised)

## Impact Assessment

- Provides extended participation window for eligible shareholders to subscribe to the rights issue.
