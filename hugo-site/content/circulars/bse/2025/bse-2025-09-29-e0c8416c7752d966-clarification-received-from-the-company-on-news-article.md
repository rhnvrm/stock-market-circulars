## Summary

Sunteck Realty Limited submitted a clarification to BSE and NSE regarding a news article titled "Sunteck Realty shares zoom nearly 9%: Firm to launch ₹20,000 crore worth of ultra-luxury homes in Mumbai, Dubai". The company confirmed that the planned launches are within the previously disclosed total Gross Development Value (GDV) of ~₹39,800 crores from its Q1FY26 investor presentation and do not constitute new unpublished price-sensitive information.

## Key Points

- Addressed news report concerning ₹20,000 crore worth of upcoming project launches in Mumbai and Dubai.
- Clarified that the ₹20,000 crore launch figure is part of the previously disclosed total Gross Development Value (GDV) of ~₹39,800 crores.
- Confirmed that share price movement is market-driven with no pending unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Continued adherence to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Date of Letter: 29th September, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No operational or financial impact; the disclosure serves to clarify existing public information and dispel speculative reports.
