## Summary

Rubicon Research Limited has finalized the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors, successfully raising ₹619.09 crore ahead of its initial public offering (IPO). A total of 1,27,64,691 equity shares were allotted to 32 qualified institutional anchor investors at the upper price band of ₹485 per share.

## Key Points

- Total amount raised from anchor investors stands at ₹619.09 crore.
- 1,27,64,691 equity shares allocated at a price of ₹485 per share.
- Participation from 32 marquee domestic mutual funds and foreign portfolio investors (FPIs), including HDFC Mutual Fund, ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund, Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund, Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund, Goldman Sachs, and Fidelity Funds.
- Part of the larger public issue consisting of a fresh issue and an offer for sale (OFS) by promoter entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standard compliance with SEBI ICDR Regulations concerning anchor investor allocation and disclosures in public issues.

## Compliance Requirements

- Lock-in restrictions apply to the anchor investor portion: 50% of the shares are locked in for 30 days, and the remaining 50% are locked in for 90 days from the date of allotment.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date: October 8, 2025
- IPO Opening Date: October 9, 2025
- IPO Closing Date: October 13, 2025
- Basis of Allotment / Listing Date: Mid-October 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Indicates strong institutional demand and market confidence in Rubicon Research Limited's public offering, setting a positive tone for the primary market subscription.
