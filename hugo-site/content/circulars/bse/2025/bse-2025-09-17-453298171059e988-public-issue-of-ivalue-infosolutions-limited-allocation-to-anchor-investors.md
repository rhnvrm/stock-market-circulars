## Summary

The Board of Directors of Ivalue Infosolutions Limited, in consultation with the Book Running Lead Managers, has finalized the allocation of 56,21,686 equity shares to Anchor Investors at an allocation price of Rs. 299 per equity share (including a share premium of Rs. 297 per share).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Ivalue Infosolutions Limited
- Issue Size / Allocation: 56,21,686 Equity Shares allocated to Anchor Investors
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: Rs. 299 per Equity Share (Face Value: Rs. 2, Share Premium: Rs. 297)
- Date of Board Meeting / Finalization: September 17, 2025
