## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding corporate actions in Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) securities, outlining upcoming dividend payments, book closures, ex-dates, and shut periods for various listed companies for September 2025.

## Key Points

- Lists corporate actions (primarily dividends) for companies including 63 Moons Technologies, Parag Milk Foods, DCW, LT Foods, Aurionpro Solutions, and others.
- Provides exact book closure, record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods for each listed security.
- Helps market participants manage SLB positions and corporate action entitlements.

## Regulatory Changes

- Not applicable (routine market operations notice).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the specified shut periods and ex-dates for smooth settlement and corporate action processing.

## Important Dates

- **12-September-2025 to 23-September-2025:** Various book closure and ex-dates depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational notice ensuring transparency in corporate action processing for participants in the SLB and equity segments.
