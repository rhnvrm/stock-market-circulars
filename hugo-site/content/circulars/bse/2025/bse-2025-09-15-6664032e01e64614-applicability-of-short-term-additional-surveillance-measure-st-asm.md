## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective September 16, 2025. The circular provides lists of securities newly shortlisted under the ST-ASM framework, securities moving out of the framework, and a consolidated list of securities currently under various stages of ST-ASM.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities into the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective September 16, 2025.
- Inclusion of securities such as BDH Industries Ltd, Consolidated Construction Consortium Ltd, Indong Tea Company Ltd, Newtime Infrastructure Ltd, and Sanrhea Technical Textiles Ltd in ST-ASM Stage I.
- Movement of specific securities out of the ST-ASM framework, including Karan Woo Sin Ltd, KBS India Ltd, Lakhotia Polyesters (India) Ltd, Moschip Technologies Ltd, Parmeshwari Silk Mills Ltd, and Shilp Gravures Ltd.
- Consolidated list detailing scrip codes, ISINs, security names, and their respective ST-ASM stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the Short Term ASM framework to monitor excessive price volatility or abnormal trading patterns in specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the listed securities will be subject to additional surveillance margins or trading safeguards as mandated under the ST-ASM framework, affecting liquidity and short-term trading dynamics for the affected scrips.
