## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted for ESM, securities moving to higher or lower stages, and securities moving out of the framework effective September 29, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of various securities into the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Reclassification of existing securities into higher or lower Stage ESM categories.
- Removal of specified securities from the ESM framework effective September 29, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures in accordance with regulatory guidelines to monitor volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the stage changes and apply appropriate trading restrictions and margins for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance controls, margin requirements, or trading restrictions depending on their respective ESM stage.
