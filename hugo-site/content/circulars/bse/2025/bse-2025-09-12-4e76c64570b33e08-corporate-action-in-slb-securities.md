## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding corporate actions (dividends) in Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) securities, specifying record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods for companies including BEML Ltd, Sanghvi Movers Ltd., and Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited.

## Key Points

- **BEML Ltd**: Dividend corporate action with Ex-Date and Record Date on 22-September-2025; Shut Period from 19-Sep-2025 to 22-Sep-2025.
- **Sanghvi Movers Ltd.**: Dividend corporate action with Ex-Date and Record Date on 12-September-2025; Shut Period from 11-Sep-2025 to 12-Sep-2025.
- **Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited**: Dividend corporate action with Ex-Date and Record Date on 22-September-2025; Shut Period from 19-Sep-2025 to 22-Sep-2025.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational market notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants engaging in SLB transactions must take note of the corporate actions, ex-dates, and shut periods to ensure proper settlement and adjustments.

## Important Dates

- **12-September-2025**: Ex-Date and Record Date for Sanghvi Movers Ltd.
- **22-September-2025**: Ex-Date and Record Date for BEML Ltd. and Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact; standard administrative notice for trading and settlement participants regarding corporate actions in the SLB segment.
