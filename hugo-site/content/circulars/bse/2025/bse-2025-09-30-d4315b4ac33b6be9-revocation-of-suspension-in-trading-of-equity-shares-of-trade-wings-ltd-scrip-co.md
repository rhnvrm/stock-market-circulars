## Summary

This circular provides the company profile and operational details of Trade Wings Limited in connection with the revocation of suspension in trading of its equity shares under scrip code 509953 (ISIN: INE961E01017).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Trade Wings Limited
- Scrip Code: 509953, ISIN: INE961E01017
- Registered Office: Panjim, Goa; Corporate Address: Vashi, Navi Mumbai
- Managing Director: Mr. Shailendra Mittal
- Nature of Business: Pioneer in Indian tourism and hospitality industry, offering foreign exchange, cargo, ticketing, and hotel accommodation facilities.
- Operational Status: Fully Operational

## Regulatory Changes

None specified other than the administrative and corporate disclosures required for revocation of suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

Company must continue to maintain compliance with listing regulations and submit required disclosures through designated channels.

## Important Dates

- Date of Filing/Circular: 11th August 2025

## Impact Assessment

Enables resumption of trading activities for investors holding equity shares of Trade Wings Limited, enhancing liquidity.
