## Summary

Pidilite Industries Limited has issued an intimation regarding the record date and allotment details for its bonus issue of equity shares. A total of 50,88,57,016 bonus equity shares will be allotted with a deemed date of allotment on 24th September, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Stock Code:** 500331
- **ISIN:** INE318A01026
- **Number of Bonus Shares:** 50,88,57,016
- **Record Date:** 23rd September, 2025
- **Deemed Date of Allotment:** 24th September, 2025
- **Effective Listing Date:** 25th September, 2025

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Bonus shares to be credited in the depository system by Wednesday, 24th September, 2025 by 12 P.M.
- Ensuring availability for trading on the beginning of the day (BOD) of T+2 day, i.e., Thursday, 25th September, 2025.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** 23rd September, 2025
- **Allotment Date:** 24th September, 2025
- **Listing / Trading Date:** 25th September, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders will receive additional equity shares in proportion to their holding as of the record date, expanding the company's paid-up equity base.
