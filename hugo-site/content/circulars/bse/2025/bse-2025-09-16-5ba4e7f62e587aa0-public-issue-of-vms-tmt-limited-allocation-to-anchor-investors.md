## Summary

VMS TMT Limited has announced the successful allocation of 27,00,000 equity shares to anchor investors at a price of ₹99 per share (including a premium of ₹89 per share), aggregating to ₹26.73 crore, ahead of its initial public offering.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares allocated to anchor investors: 27,00,000 shares.
- Issue price / Allocation price: ₹99 per share.
- Total amount raised from anchor investors: ₹26.73 crore.
- Major anchor investors include Saint Capital Fund, Chanakya Opportunities Fund I, Maybank Securities Pte, Astorne Capital VCC - Arven, and Vbcube Ventures Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shares allotted to anchor investors are subject to lock-in provisions as per SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- 50% of the shares allotted to anchor investors are locked in for 30 days from the date of allotment.
- Remaining 50% of the shares are locked in for 90 days from the date of allotment.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date / Allocation Date: September 16, 2025.
- 30-day lock-in release date: October 22, 2025.
- 90-day lock-in release date: December 21, 2025.
