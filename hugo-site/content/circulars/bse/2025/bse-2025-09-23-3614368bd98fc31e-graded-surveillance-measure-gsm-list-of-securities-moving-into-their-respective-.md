## Summary

BSE has released a circular detailing the list of securities that are moving into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. A total of 33 securities have been identified for movement into GSM Stage III and Stage IV.

## Key Points

- Securities moving to GSM Stage III include India Radiators Ltd., Mathew Easow Research Securities Ltd., Artson Limited, and Advik Laboratories Ltd.
- Securities moving to GSM Stage IV include Rama Petrochemicals Ltd., Kartik Investments Trust Ltd., Deccan Bearings Ltd., Oswal Leasing Ltd., and several others.
- Certain securities are noted with caveats regarding movement due to inclusion in ESM or IBC frameworks, or as per NSE classifications.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of additional trading restrictions under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework to protect investors and maintain market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and apply necessary trading curbs and margin requirements as mandated by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to heightened surveillance, enhanced margin requirements, or restricted trading frequencies depending on their assigned GSM stage, directly impacting liquidity and short-term market activity for these counters.
