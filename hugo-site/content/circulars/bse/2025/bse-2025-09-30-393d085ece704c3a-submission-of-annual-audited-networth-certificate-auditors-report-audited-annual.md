## Summary

BSE has issued guidelines outlining the procedure for online submission of the Annual Audited Networth Certificate, Computation of Networth, Auditor’s Certificate, Audited Annual Accounts, and Financial Strength details for the financial year ended March 31, 2025.

## Key Points

- Submissions must be made through the online BSE portal (BEFS URL: https://befs.bseindia.com).
- Members need to configure Internet Explorer settings (ActiveX controls and trusted sites) as specified for portal access.
- Details to be provided include mode (Yearly), financial year (31/03/2025), standalone or consolidated basis, and statutory auditor details.
- Submissions undergo exchange scrutiny; status updates to 'Networth Approved' upon successful verification or 'Networth Rejected' in case of deficiencies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Annual compliance requirement for members to submit audited networth and financial statements via the dedicated online portal.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must upload scanned documents and fill in computation details through the BSE portal.
- If any field is not applicable, members are required to enter '0'.
- Provide accurate statutory auditor details (individual or audit firm, PAN, etc.).

## Important Dates

- Financial Year ended: March 31, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures regulatory oversight of member financial strength and capital adequacy compliance through standard digital workflows.
