## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing and lock-in details of equity shares of iValue Infosolutions Limited following its initial public offering (IPO), effective from September 23, 2025.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares under various lock-in categories as well as public free float are detailed.
- Anchor investor shares are subject to lock-in periods of 30 days (up to October 22, 2025) and 90 days (up to December 21, 2025).
- Promoter and pre-IPO shares carry lock-in durations extending up to March 2027.
- 1,31,17,272 equity shares are available under public category (not under lock-in).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to lock-in periods as specified in the company's prospectus and exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Listing / Lock-in Commencement Date: 23-Sep-2025
- Anchor Lock-in Expiry (30 Days): 22-Oct-2025
- Anchor Lock-in Expiry (90 Days): 21-Dec-2025
- Extended Lock-in Expiry: 23-Mar-2027

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification for market participants concerning security availability and lock-in status.
