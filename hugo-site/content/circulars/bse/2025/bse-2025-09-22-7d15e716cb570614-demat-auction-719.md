## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding Demat Auction - 719, detailing the schedule and procedures for auctioning securities due to short deliveries by market participants.

## Key Points

- Notice details auction participation for short deliveries in the settlement cycle.
- Provides timeline and operational guidelines for members.
- Ensures smooth clearing and settlement of trades on the exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard operating procedure under exchange bylaws.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members with short deliveries must adhere to the auction schedule and ensure necessary obligations are met.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified in the circular.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is restricted to trading members involved in short deliveries for the specific settlement period.
