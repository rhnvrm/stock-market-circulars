## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) of Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform. The circular outlines platform functionalities, investor eligibility rules, minimum investment requirements for accredited and non-accredited investors, and guidelines for updating accredited investor details via member logins.

## Key Points

- New AMC activation for SIF schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform as confirmed by RTAs.
- Minimum investment for Non-Accredited Investors is ₹10 lakh in any SIF scheme for first-time investments.
- Accredited Investors must invest the minimum amount set by the AMC in Scheme Information Documents (SID).
- SIF client AUM must not fall below ₹10 lakh due to redemptions.
- Distributors must pass the NISM XIII examination and register with AMFI to distribute SIFs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Activation of separate AMC creation for SIF functionality on the platform.
- Rules established for handling different modes of holding (Single vs. Joint) to verify Accredited Investor status.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must update accredited investor details manually or via bulk upload through Admin >> client details >> client master upload.
- Accredited investor registration certificates must be renewed prior to expiry.
- Members must ensure correct details are uploaded at order entry to avoid transaction rejections by AMC/RTA.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular release date.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the distribution and investment process for Specialized Investment Funds while enforcing strict investor qualification and holding criteria on the BSE StAR MF platform.
