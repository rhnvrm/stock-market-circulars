## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing the prescribed strike price intervals for various scrips in the Equity Derivatives segment.

## Key Points

- Lists multiple scrips along with their Scrip Codes, Scrip Names, Derivative Asset Codes, and corresponding Strike Price Intervals.
- Assists traders and market participants in placing derivative orders according to exchange norms.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Informational listing of derivative contract specifications.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to the specified strike price intervals when trading options in the respective scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for the equity derivatives segment.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational clarity for derivatives trading and order placement.
