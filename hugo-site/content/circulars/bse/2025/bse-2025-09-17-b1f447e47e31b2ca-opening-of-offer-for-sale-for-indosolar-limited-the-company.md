## Summary

Waaree Energies Limited (the "Seller") has issued a notice for an Offer for Sale (OFS) of up to 61,00,000 equity shares of face value ₹10 each, representing 14.66% of the total paid-up equity share capital of Indosolar Limited (the "Company"). The offering is aimed at achieving the minimum public shareholding requirements.

## Key Points

- **Seller:** Waaree Energies Limited (Promoter)
- **Company:** Indosolar Limited
- **Total Sale Shares:** Up to 61,00,000 equity shares (14.66% of paid-up equity share capital)
- **Dates:** September 18, 2025 (T-day for Non-Retail Investors) and September 19, 2025 (T+1 Day for Retail Investors)
- **Purpose:** Achieving minimum public shareholding under Rule 19A(5) of SCRR, 1957 and Regulation 38 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI master circular on OFS and stock exchange operational guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Bidders and investors must review the entire notice and applicable guidelines before participating in the OFS window.

## Important Dates

- **T-Day (Non-Retail Investors):** September 18, 2025
- **T+1 Day (Retail Investors):** September 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Alters promoter holding structure and increases public shareholding float for Indosolar Limited in compliance with regulatory mandates.
