## Summary

BSE Limited sought clarifications from various listed companies regarding recent fluctuations in price or trading volume. The responding companies confirmed that they do not possess any unpublished price-sensitive information or pending announcements that would explain the recent market movements, attributing them entirely to market conditions.

## Key Points

- Trigyn Technologies Limited clarified regarding recent price movement on September 23, 2025.
- Zuari Industries Limited addressed queries concerning an increase in trading volume on September 24, 2025.
- Bajaj Healthcare Limited received a surveillance query regarding recent volume increases.
- All reporting companies reaffirmed their commitment to timely disclosure under SEBI LODR Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must promptly respond to exchange inquiries regarding unusual market behavior and confirm the presence or absence of unpublished price-sensitive information.

## Important Dates

- Date of Clarifications: September 23–24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard compliance communication with no direct operational or financial impact on investors or market structures.
