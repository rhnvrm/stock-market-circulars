## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a circular detailing securities that are moving into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.

## Key Points

- Munoth Capital Markets Ltd, Maitri Enterprises Ltd, Revati Media Ltd, Suncity Synthetics Ltd, Omkar Pharmachem Ltd, and Adjia Technologies Ltd have moved to GSM Stage I.
- Pasupati Fincap Ltd has moved to GSM Stage III.
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower under the GSM framework due to inclusion in the ESM framework.
- Securities marked with ($) are moving lower under the GSM framework due to inclusion in the IBC framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Graded Surveillance Measures to monitor abnormal price volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and apply appropriate trading restrictions and margin requirements as mandated by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange surveillance mechanism.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, including possible trade-for-trade settlement or additional deposit requirements, potentially impacting liquidity.
