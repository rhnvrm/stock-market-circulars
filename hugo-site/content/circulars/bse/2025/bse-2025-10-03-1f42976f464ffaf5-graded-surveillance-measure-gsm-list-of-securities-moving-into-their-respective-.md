## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM), announcing that Stanpacks India Ltd (Security Code: 530931, ISIN: INE457D01018) has been moved to GSM Stage I effective immediately or as per exchange directives.

## Key Points

- **Security Name:** Stanpacks India Ltd
- **Security Code:** 530931
- **ISIN:** INE457D01018
- **Moved to:** GSM Stage I

## Regulatory Changes

The securities identified under the GSM framework are subject to enhanced monitoring and surveillance measures to protect investors and maintain market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance action and apply appropriate trading restrictions as mandated by the exchange for securities under GSM Stage I.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** As specified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in securities placed under GSM Stage I typically attracts additional margin requirements or trading curbs, which may impact liquidity and short-term price movement for the affected stock.
