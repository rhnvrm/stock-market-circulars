## Summary

BSE has published clarifications received from listed companies regarding a significant increase in the volume of their securities across exchanges. Companies including ITI Limited and Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers Limited have confirmed that they are in compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations and have no undisclosed, price-sensitive information that would explain the recent volume or price behavior.

## Key Points

- ITI Limited confirmed compliance with SEBI LODR Regulations and stated no pending events/matters affect price or volume.
- Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers Limited clarified that volume changes are purely market-driven and not due to any undisclosed company developments.
- Companies reiterated adherence to regulatory disclosure requirements under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must respond to stock exchange queries regarding unusual volume or price movements in accordance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations.

- Clarification Date: September 16, 2025

Informational notice indicating standard market surveillance procedures and company responses regarding stock volatility.
