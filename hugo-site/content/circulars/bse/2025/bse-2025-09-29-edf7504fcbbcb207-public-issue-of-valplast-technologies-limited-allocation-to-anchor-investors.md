## Summary

Valplast Technologies Limited has finalized the allocation of 14,70,000 Equity Shares to Anchor Investors at an allocation price of ₹54/- per Equity Share for its public issue.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares allocated to anchor investors: 14,70,000.
- Anchor Investor allocation price: ₹54/- per Equity Share (including a share premium of ₹44/-).
- Total amount raised from anchor investors: ₹7,93,80,000/-
- Key anchor investors include Rajasthan Global Securities Private Limited, Strategic Sixth Sense Capital Fund, and Viney Growth Fund.

## Compliance Requirements

In case the Offer Price discovered through the book building process is higher than the Anchor Investor allocation price, anchor investors are required to pay the difference by the pay-in as specified in the revised CAN.
