## Summary

SEBI has issued circular SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131 on September 25, 2025, detailing compliance guidelines and reporting mechanisms for the digital accessibility circular under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. These guidelines apply to all Regulated Entities (REs) to ensure digital platforms are accessible to persons with disabilities.

## Key Points

- Clarifies applicability and reporting structures for different categories of Regulated Entities.
- Outlines phased compliance milestones from September 2025 to July 2026.
- Mandates the appointment of IAAP certified accessibility professionals as auditors.
- Specifies email submission mechanisms to digital_acc@sebi.gov.in for applicable entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes a formal timeline and reporting mechanism for compliance with the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 for SEBI-regulated entities.
- Defines distinct reporting authorities for Stock Brokers, Depository Participants, Investment Advisors, Research Analysts, and MIIs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated entities must submit a list of digital platforms by September 30, 2025.
- Appoint IAAP certified accessibility professionals as auditors by December 14, 2025.
- Conduct accessibility audits by April 30, 2026.
- Remediate audit findings and ensure full compliance by July 31, 2026.
- Submit annual accessibility audit reports starting April 30, 2027.

## Important Dates

- September 30, 2025: Submission of list of digital platforms.
- December 14, 2025: Appointment of IAAP certified auditor.
- April 30, 2026: Conduct initial accessibility audit.
- July 31, 2026: Remediation and final compliance submission.
- April 30, 2027: First annual compliance report submission.

## Impact Assessment

Regulated entities must dedicate technical and compliance resources to audit and upgrade all customer-facing digital platforms to meet rigorous accessibility standards within strict deadlines.
