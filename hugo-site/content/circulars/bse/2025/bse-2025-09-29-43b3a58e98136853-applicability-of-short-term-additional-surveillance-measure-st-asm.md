## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework effective September 30, 2025. The notification includes lists of securities newly shortlisted, moved to higher stages, and moving out of the ST-ASM framework.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of securities in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective September 30, 2025.
- Movement of existing securities to higher or lower stages of the ST-ASM framework where applicable.
- Complete list of securities moving out of the ST-ASM framework, including those transitioning to other surveillance frameworks such as LT-ASM, Trade for Trade, or GSM.
- Consolidated list providing the current ST-ASM stage for applicable securities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under the ST-ASM framework for specified scrips to monitor price volatility and safeguard investors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance controls, higher margin requirements, or trading frequency limitations as mandated by the ST-ASM framework.
