## Summary

The BSE circular details upcoming corporate actions including dividends, stock splits, and bonus issues for various securities under the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) segment for October 2025.

## Key Points

- **ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited**: Dividend, Book Closure/Record Date: 23-October-2025, Shut Period: 21-Oct-2025 to 23-Oct-2025.
- **Rashtriya Chemicals & Fertilizers Limited**: Dividend, Book Closure/Record Date: 10-October-2025, Shut Period: 09-Oct-2025 to 10-Oct-2025.
- **Tata Investment Corporation Ltd.**: Stock Split, Book Closure/Record Date: 14-October-2025, Shut Period: 13-Oct-2025 to 14-Oct-2025.
- **Glenmark Pharmaceuticals ltd**: Dividend, Book Closure/Record Date: 03-October-2025, Shut Period: 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.
- **Shilpa Medicare Ltd.**: Bonus issue, Book Closure/Record Date: 03-October-2025, Foreclosure Date: 03-Oct-2025, Shut Period: 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.
