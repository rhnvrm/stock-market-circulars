## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding securities shortlisted, moving out of, and the consolidated list under the measure for companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011, effective October 01, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Annexure I (Shortlisted Securities):** Nil securities shortlisted under the framework.
- **Annexure II (Moving Out):** Tourism Finance Corporation of India Ltd (Scrip Code: 526650, ISIN: INE305A01023) is moving out of the framework.
- **Annexure III (Consolidated List):** Includes DJS Stock & Shares Ltd (511636), Future Market Networks Ltd (533296), and Thyrocare Technologies Ltd (539871).

## Regulatory Changes

Measures implemented under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 regarding high promoter and non-promoter encumbrances.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities and market participants must take note of the revised status of securities under the encumbrance framework.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding encumbered shares and affects trading restrictions or monitoring associated with the listed securities included or excluded from the framework.
