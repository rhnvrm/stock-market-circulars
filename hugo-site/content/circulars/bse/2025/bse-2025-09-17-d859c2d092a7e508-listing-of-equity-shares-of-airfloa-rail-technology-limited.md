## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Airfloa Rail Technology Limited, detailing various tranches of fully paid-up shares, lock-in periods, and free shares.

## Key Points

- Total listing includes multiple tranches of fully paid-up equity shares with varying lock-in periods and free categories.
- Lock-in start and end dates are specified for promoter/pre-IPO and anchor investor shares.
- Free shares amounting to 4,655,000 equity shares are made available for trading under demat mode.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and depositories must ensure adherence to the specified lock-in periods and release dates.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in start dates begin from 10.09.2025 and 16.09.2025.
- Lock-in end dates range from 15.10.2025 to 25.09.2028.

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading for the free component of equity shares while maintaining regulatory lock-ins for promoter and anchor allocations.
