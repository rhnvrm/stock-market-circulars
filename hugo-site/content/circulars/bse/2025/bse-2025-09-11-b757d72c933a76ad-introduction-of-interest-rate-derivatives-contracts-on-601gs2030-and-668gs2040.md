## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of Interest Rate Derivatives (Futures and Options) contracts on the underlying Government of India (GOI) security 6.01% GOI Maturing on July 21, 2030 (Symbol: 601GS2030).

## Key Points

- **Underlying Security**: 6.01% GOI Maturing on July 21, 2030.
- **Contracts Introduced**: Interest Rate Futures (FUTIRD) and Interest Rate Options (OPTIRD).
- **Trading Hours**: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, aligned with the NDS-OM platform.
- **Unit of Contract/Trading**: 1 contract = 2,000 units (Face Value Rs. 2 Lakhs).
- **Settlement**: Cash-settled contracts in INR on T+1 day.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishment of contract specifications, trading parameters, price bands, and settlement mechanisms for interest rate futures and options as per SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to position limits and risk management frameworks specified under relevant SEBI and RBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Effective date and contract activation as per exchange notification schedule.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides enhanced hedging and trading tools for market participants dealing in government securities and interest rate exposures.
