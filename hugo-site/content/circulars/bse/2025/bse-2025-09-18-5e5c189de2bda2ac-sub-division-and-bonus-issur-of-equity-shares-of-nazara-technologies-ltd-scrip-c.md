## Summary

Nazara Technologies Limited has released information regarding the sub-division and bonus issue of its equity shares, specifying distinctive numbers, effective listing dates, and the new ISIN allocation.

## Key Points

- Effective Listing Date: Tuesday, September 30, 2025
- Deemed Date of Allotment: Monday, September 29, 2025
- Number of Bonus Shares: 18,52,32,512
- New ISIN: INE418L01021 (New ISIN to be allotted)
- Distinctive Numbers Range: From 185232513 to 370465024

- Date of Notice: September 17, 2025
- Deemed Date of Allotment: September 29, 2025
- Effective Listing Date: September 30, 2025
