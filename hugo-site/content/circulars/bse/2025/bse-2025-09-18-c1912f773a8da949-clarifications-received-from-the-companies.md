## Summary

BSE has published clarifications received from various listed companies regarding significant volume movements in their scrips. The responding companies have confirmed that they are in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, and that any price or volume behavior is purely market-driven based on investor perceptions of publicly available information.

## Key Points

- PC Jeweller Limited confirmed timely disclosure of all material events and price-sensitive information, noting that price/volume variations are market-driven.
- Prozone Realty Limited stated that trade volume increases are based on investor perceptions of information already disseminated to the exchanges.
- Radhika Jeweltech Limited affirmed that all events with a significant bearing on operations have been duly intimated to the exchange.
- None of the companies possess undisclosed price-sensitive information requiring disclosure under Regulation 30.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; routine compliance check under SEBI LODR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must continue to monitor volume/price movements and promptly disclose any material events or price-sensitive information as mandated by Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- No direct operational or financial impact on the market or investors; confirms transparency and adherence to continuous disclosure norms.
