## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of Interest Rate Derivatives contracts, including Interest Rate Futures (IRF) and Interest Rate Options, on the underlying Government of India security 6.01% maturing on July 21, 2030 (Symbol: 601GS2030).

## Key Points

- **Underlying Security**: 6.01% GOI Maturing on July 21, 2030.
- **Futures Contract Specifications**: 
  - Symbol: FUTIRD
  - Trading Hours: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
  - Unit of Contract: 1 contract = 2,000 bonds * 100 Face Value = INR 200,000/-
  - Tick Size: 0.0025
  - Settlement: Cash settled on T+1 day.
- **Options Contract Specifications**:
  - Symbol: OPTIRD
  - Option Type: Premium Style European Call and Put Options
  - Unit of Trading: One contract denotes 2,000 units (Face Value Rs. 2 Lacs)
  - Strike Price Intervals: INR 0.25

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of new derivative instruments on specified government securities as per regulatory frameworks and SEBI circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to the specified position limits, order quantity limits (maximum 1250 orders/lots), and price bands.

## Important Dates

- Effective for trading as per exchange schedule following the circular issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides enhanced hedging tools and trading opportunities for market participants dealing in Indian government securities and interest rate derivatives.
