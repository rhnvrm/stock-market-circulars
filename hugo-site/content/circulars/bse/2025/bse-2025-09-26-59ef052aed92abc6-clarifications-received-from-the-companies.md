## Summary

BSE sought clarification from Ushakiran Finance Limited and Machino Plastics Limited regarding recent movements in their share prices. Both companies responded stating that the price movements are purely market-driven and that they have no undisclosed price-sensitive information.

## Key Points

- Ushakiran Finance Limited confirmed there is no pending price-sensitive information and that share price behavior is market-driven.
- Machino Plastics Limited stated that automotive demand may have surged due to the introduction of GST 2.0 (effective September 22, 2025), influencing market sentiment.
- Machino Plastics also noted that its low floating stock can cause small buy quantities to surge prices to upper ceilings.
- Both companies reaffirm adherence to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Continuous disclosure of material events and price-sensitive information under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Date of Clarification: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No operational or financial impact on the companies; standard disclosure process for stock exchange surveillance.
