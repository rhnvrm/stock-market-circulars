## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding securities moving into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The circular lists companies being placed into GSM Stages I, II, III, and IV, imposing specific trading restrictions and surveillance mechanisms on these scrips.

## Key Points

- Wardwizard Healthcare Limited and Pratik Panels Ltd. have moved to GSM Stage I.
- Woodsvilla Ltd., Manraj Housing Finance Ltd., and Purohit Construction Limited have moved to GSM Stage II.
- Neelkanth Limited, Futuristic Securities Ltd., and Golechha Global Finance Ltd. have moved to GSM Stage III.
- Garware Synthetics Ltd. has moved to GSM Stage IV.
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower under the GSM framework due to inclusion in the ESM Framework, and ($) due to inclusion in the IBC Framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened surveillance and trading restrictions as per the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) guidelines to protect market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must comply with the applicable trading restrictions, additional margin requirements, and settlement conditions corresponding to the respective GSM stages assigned to these securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

Heightened surveillance measures restrict speculative trading and enhance scrutiny on the listed securities, potentially affecting short-term liquidity and trading volumes for the affected stocks.
