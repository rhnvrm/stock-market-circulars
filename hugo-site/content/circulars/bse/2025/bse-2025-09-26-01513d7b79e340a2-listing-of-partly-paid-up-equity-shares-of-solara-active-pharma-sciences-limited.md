## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing and trading approval of 25,000 partly paid-up equity shares of Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited following the receipt of call money.

## Key Points

- Total partly paid shares for which reminder notice for first call was sent: 6,08,988.
- Total shares on which call money received as of August 16, 2025: 3,71,779.
- Shares available for trading on resumption: 25,000.
- Paid-up value per share available for trading: Rs. 7/-.
- ISIN for shares available for trading: IN9624Z01022.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Ensure proper crediting and adherence to depository guidelines for the listed partly paid-up shares.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 25, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact as this is a routine listing of partly paid-up shares subsequent to call money collection.
