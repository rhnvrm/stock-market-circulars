## Summary

The Central Processing Centre of the Ministry of Corporate Affairs has issued a Certificate of Incorporation pursuant to a change of name. The company formerly known as ITD Cementation India Limited has officially changed its name to Cemindia Projects Limited with effect from August 19, 2025.

## Key Points

- Previous Company Name: ITD Cementation India Limited
- New Company Name: Cemindia Projects Limited
- Original Incorporation Name: Cemindia Company Limited
- Corporate Identification Number (CIN): L61000MH1978PLC020435
- Effective Date: August 19, 2025
- Registered Office/Mailing Address: 9th Floor, Prima Bay, Tower - B, Gate No. 5, Saki Vihar Road, Powai, Mumbai, Maharashtra - 400072

## Regulatory Changes

The official company name has been updated in the records of the Registrar of Companies, Central Processing Centre.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company is obligated to display its old name along with its new name for a period of two years at all required places in terms of Section 12 of the Companies Act.
- Stakeholders are advised to verify the latest status and public documents on the MCA21 portal.

## Important Dates

- Certificate Date: August 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

This change affects all public records, trading symbols, and stakeholder references associated with ITD Cementation India Limited, requiring updates across operational and financial systems to reflect the new entity name, Cemindia Projects Limited.
