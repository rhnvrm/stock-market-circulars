## Summary

This circular outlines the framework and verification parameters for the submission of the Internal Audit Report for the half-year ended September 30, 2025, via the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS). It details verification criteria for client registration, anti-money laundering compliance, and UCC database updates.

## Key Points

- Covers verification standards for client registration documentation and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance.
- Mandates that client registration details match KRA and C-KYC databases.
- Requires verification that no unauthorized or restrictive clauses are included in client agreements.
- In-person verification must be conducted exclusively by authorized employees or approved persons.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clarifies audit sampling criteria and verification metrics for client data updates during the audit period.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members registered for any segment must ensure compliance with client registration and KYC norms.
- Promptly update any changes in client details across back-office records, UCC Database, KRA, and CKYCR.

## Important Dates

- Audit Period / Reference Date: Half year ended September 30, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures high standards of operational compliance and data integrity across trading members through structured internal audits.
