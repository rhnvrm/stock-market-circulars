## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, detailing securities newly shortlisted, moved to higher/lower stages, and the consolidated list effective from September 25, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted and included in the Long Term ASM Framework (Part A) effective September 25, 2025.
- Incon Engineers Ltd (Scrip Code: 531594) is moved to Stage II of the framework.
- A consolidated list of securities under various stages of the LT-ASM framework is provided.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including margin requirements and price bands for securities identified under the LT-ASM framework to safeguard market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages, additional margin requirements, and trading restrictions on the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities may experience modified margin requirements and volatility controls as per the surveillance framework stages.
