## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a circular regarding the official change in name of a listed company. Market participants, trading members, and investors are informed about the updated corporate name, effective for all market operations and trading records.

## Key Points

- Official update of the company's name on the BSE trading platform.
- Necessary updates to scrip codes, short names, and system records.
- Compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Name change processed in accordance with provisions of the Companies Act and SEBI LODR regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must ensure that all official documents, websites, and stationery display the new name along with the former name as mandated by regulatory guidelines.
- Trading members should update their internal systems with the new corporate name and identifiers.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified in the circular details.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational update for trading terminals, clearing corporations, and depository participants to reflect the new corporate name.
