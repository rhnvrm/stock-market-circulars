## Summary

BSE has released information regarding the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors as part of the public issue of L. T. Elevator Limited.

## Key Points

- Allocation of 14,36,800 equity shares to anchor investors.
- Issue price fixed at ₹78 per share.
- Total funds raised from anchor investors amount to approximately ₹11.21 crore.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Observance of statutory lock-in periods for anchor investor shares as per SEBI regulations (30 days for 50% of the shares and 90 days for the remaining).

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 11, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Reflects institutional investor demand and participation prior to the opening of the main public issue.
