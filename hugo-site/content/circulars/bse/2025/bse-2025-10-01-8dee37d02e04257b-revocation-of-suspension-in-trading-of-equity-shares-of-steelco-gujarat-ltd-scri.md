## Summary

This circular announces the revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares of Steelco Gujarat Limited (Scrip Code: 500399).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Steelco Gujarat Limited
- Scrip Code: 500399
- Registered Office: Plot No. 2, G.I.D.C. Estate, Palej, Dist. Bharuch – 392 220, Gujarat
- Nature of Business: Manufacturing of cold rolled steel products

## Regulatory Changes

Trading in the equity shares of the company has been restored following the revocation of suspension.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must adhere to the standard trading and listing guidelines prescribed by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange for revocation of suspension.

## Impact Assessment

Enables market participants to resume trading in the equity shares of Steelco Gujarat Limited on the BSE platform.
