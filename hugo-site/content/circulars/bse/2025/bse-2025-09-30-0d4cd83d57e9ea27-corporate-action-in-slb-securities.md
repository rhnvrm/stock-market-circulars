## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding corporate actions in SLB (Securities Lending and Borrowing) securities scheduled for October 2025. The circular details book closure dates, record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods for corporate actions including dividends, stock splits, and bonus issues across several prominent companies.

## Key Points

- **ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited**: Dividend, Record/Ex-Date: 23-October-2025, Shut Period: 21-Oct-2025 to 23-Oct-2025.
- **Rashtriya Chemicals & Fertilizers Limited**: Dividend, Record/Ex-Date: 10-October-2025, Shut Period: 09-Oct-2025 to 10-Oct-2025.
- **Tata Investment Corporation Ltd.**: Stock Split, Record/Ex-Date: 14-October-2025, Shut Period: 13-Oct-2025 to 14-Oct-2025.
- **Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd**: Dividend, Record/Ex-Date: 03-October-2025, Shut Period: 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.
- **Shilpa Medicare Ltd.**: Bonus Issue, Record/Ex/Foreclosure Date: 03-October-2025, Shut Period: 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational circular detailing scheduled corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, custodians, and trading members must take note of the respective shut periods, ex-dates, and record dates for executing trades and settling obligations in the SLB and normal segments.

## Important Dates

- **October 3, 2025**: Ex-date/Record date for Glenmark Pharmaceuticals and Shilpa Medicare.
- **October 10, 2025**: Ex-date/Record date for Rashtriya Chemicals & Fertilizers.
- **October 14, 2025**: Ex-date/Record date for Tata Investment Corporation.
- **October 23, 2025**: Ex-date/Record date for ICICI Lombard General Insurance.

## Impact Assessment

Traders and investors engaging in securities lending and borrowing or holding positions in these equities must adjust their strategies according to the specified ex-dates and shut periods to manage corporate entitlements effectively.
