## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective September 15, 2025.

## Key Points

- **A.F. Enterprises Ltd** (Scrip Code: 538351, ISIN: INE663P01015) and **Visa Steel Ltd** (Scrip Code: 532721, ISIN: INE286H01012) have been shortlisted under IBC Stage 1.
- **Gayatri Projects Ltd** (Scrip Code: 532767, ISIN: INE336H01023) has been excluded from ASM for companies under IBC.
- Provides a consolidated list of securities under various stages of the IBC surveillance framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Updates to the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies undergoing insolvency proceedings under the IBC, adjusting the surveillance stages for specific scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages applicable to the listed securities and ensure compliance with respective margin and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, margins, and scrutiny as dictated by their respective IBC stages.
