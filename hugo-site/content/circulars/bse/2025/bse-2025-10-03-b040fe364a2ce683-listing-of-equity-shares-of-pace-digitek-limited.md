## Summary

Pace Digitek Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern under Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, in connection with the listing of its equity shares.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: Pace Digitek Limited
- Filing Requirement: Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- Class of Security: Equity Shares.
- As on Date: 01.10.2025.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine compliance filing under existing listing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure timely submission of shareholding patterns and required disclosures in accordance with SEBI LODR guidelines.

Standard regulatory disclosure with no direct material impact on market operations.
