## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability, continuation, and removal of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective September 25, 2025. The circular provides lists of securities newly shortlisted, moving to higher/lower stages, moving out of the framework, and a consolidated list.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of securities in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective September 25, 2025.
- Inclusion of companies such as Deepak Builders and Engineers India Ltd, GACM Technologies Ltd, Gujarat Mineral Development Corporation Ltd, and others in the ST-ASM framework.
- Movement of specific securities out of the ST-ASM framework due to various reasons including inclusion in other frameworks like LT-ASM, ESM, or GSM.
- Consolidated list detailing the securities currently under various stages of the Short Term ASM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short Term ASM framework to monitor price volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are required to note the list of securities under the ST-ASM framework and apply necessary margin requirements and trading restrictions as mandated.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance margins and monitoring, potentially impacting liquidity and short-term price movement.
