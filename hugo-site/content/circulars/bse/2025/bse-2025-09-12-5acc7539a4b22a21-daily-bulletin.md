## Summary

BSE Bulletin No. 114/2025-2026 dated September 12, 2025, details upcoming book-closure dates, record dates, dividend distributions, and non-convertible debenture (NCD) interest and partial redemption payments for multiple listed companies.

## Key Points

- Lists corporate actions including dividend declarations and debt security payments.
- Features companies such as AAVAS Financiers Limited, Accel Limited, Adani Green Energy (UP) Limited, and various others.
- Specifies record dates ranging from September 2025 through December 2025.

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must ensure adherence to the specified record dates and book closures for corporate actions and debt servicing.

## Important Dates

- Bulletin Date: 12/09/2025
- Record/Book Closure Dates: 15/09/2025 to 16/12/2025

Operational notification for market participants and investors regarding corporate actions and debt servicing schedules.
