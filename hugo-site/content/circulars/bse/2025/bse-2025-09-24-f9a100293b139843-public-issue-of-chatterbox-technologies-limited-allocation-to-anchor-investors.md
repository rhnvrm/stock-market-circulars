## Summary

Chatterbox Technologies Limited announced the finalization of its anchor investor share allocation for its public issue. A total of 1,059,600 equity shares were allocated to anchor investors at an allocation price of ₹115 per share (including a share premium of ₹105 per share), raising an aggregate amount of ₹121,854,000.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Chatterbox Technologies Limited
- **Total Anchor Shares Allocated:** 1,059,600 Equity Shares
- **Anchor Investor Allocation Price:** ₹115 per share (Face Value: ₹10/-, Premium: ₹105/-)
- **Total Amount Raised from Anchors:** ₹121,854,000
- **Key Anchor Investors:** Sanshi Fund I (24.58%), Meru Investment Fund PCC-Cell (24.58%), Viney Growth Fund (17.78%), Finvesta First (16.42%), Mint Focused Growth Fund PCC Cell 1 (8.27%), and Moneywise Financial Services Pvt Ltd (8.27%).

## Regulatory Changes

- Allocations carried out in accordance with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirement) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- If the final offer price discovered through the book building process exceeds the anchor investor allocation price, anchor investors are required to pay the difference by the pay-in specified in the revised CAN.

## Important Dates

- **Meeting Date:** September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Marks a critical milestone in the public issue process for Chatterbox Technologies Limited, establishing institutional backing ahead of the broader share offering.
