## Summary

Nelco Ltd. has issued a clarification to BSE regarding a news item published on 'hindi-cnbctv18.com' titled 'Tata Group's Nelco to enter satellite broadband business, shares jump'. The company stated it cannot comment on media speculation and rumors, reiterating its commitment to disclosing material events under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- The company responded to an inquiry by BSE surveillance regarding recent media speculation about entering the satellite broadband business.
- Nelco declined to comment on rumors while noting that it routinely evaluates strategic opportunities as part of its ongoing corporate strategy.
- Confirmed that no material information or price-sensitive events have been withheld from the stock exchange.
- Clarified that movement in the price and volume of the company's securities is purely market-driven.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Continued compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 regarding timely disclosure of material developments.

- Date of Letter: 18th September, 2025
- BSE Inquiry Date: 18th September, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Neutral impact on operations; serves as a standard compliance clarification regarding stock price volatility driven by news reports.
