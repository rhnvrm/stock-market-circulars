## Summary

Urban Company Limited has announced the listing of its equity shares on the exchange, detailing various lock-in periods for pre-IPO shareholders, anchor investors, and alternative investment funds.

## Key Points

- Listing of equity shares of Urban Company Limited.
- Specific lock-in periods defined for different categories of shareholders and equity tranches.
- Anchor investors are subject to lock-ins of 30 days (up to 15th October, 2025) and 90 days (up to 14th December, 2025) for respective allocations.
- Alternative Investment Fund (Acrobat Capital Fund I) shares under lock-in till 04th March, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Complies with Regulation 17(c) of the SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018 regarding promoter and alternative investment fund lock-ins.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to lock-in periods and distinctive number segregations as specified in the prospectus.

## Important Dates

- Prospectus Date: September 12, 2025
- Listing / Effective Date: September 16, 2025
- Anchor Lock-in Expiry Dates: October 15, 2025 and December 14, 2025
- AIF Lock-in Expiry Date: March 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Establishes tradability for non-locked-in shares while restricting liquidity for locked-in pre-IPO and anchor allocations as per regulatory mandates.
