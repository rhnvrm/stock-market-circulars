## Summary

SEBI has released a consultation paper to review the framework addressing 'technical glitches' in stock brokers' electronic trading systems. The proposed review aims to streamline eligibility criteria, exclude smaller stock brokers to ease compliance, simplify reporting requirements, clarify technical glitch definitions, and rationalize financial disincentives.

## Key Points

- The definition of technical glitch is proposed to be modified to exclude incidents occurring after trading hours and those not under the control of stock brokers.
- The framework will apply to stock brokers providing IBT/STWT trading platforms with more than 10,000 registered clients as of March 31 of the previous financial year.
- Approximately 457 smaller stock brokers will be exempted from the framework, reducing compliance burdens for low-clientele entities.
- Reporting requirements are eased regarding timelines and trading holidays, utilizing a Common Reporting Platform to avoid multiple reporting.

## Regulatory Changes

- Exclusion of post-trading hours glitches and external uncontrollable issues from the technical glitch definition.
- Introduction of a client threshold (more than 10,000 registered clients) for framework applicability.
- Centralization of technical glitch reporting onto a Common Reporting Platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock brokers meeting the threshold must comply with streamlined reporting and compliance rules.
- Smaller brokers with 10,000 or fewer clients are relieved from specific technical glitch framework obligations.

## Important Dates

- Based on consultation paper feedback and data analysis from previous frameworks issued in November and December 2022.

## Impact Assessment

- Significantly reduces compliance overhead for over 450 smaller stock brokers while maintaining rigorous oversight on larger technology-dominant brokers.
