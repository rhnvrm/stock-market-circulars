## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Shringar House of Mangalsutra Limited, detailing various lock-in periods, distinctive numbers, and quantities of securities under lock-in and free categories following the company's IPO.

## Key Points

- Details distinct share brackets under lock-in ranging from fully paid-up shares under lock-in to shares not under lock-in.
- Specifies lock-in expiration dates extending through 2026 and 2027.
- Covers physical and demat security types (predominantly demat).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and depositories must adhere to the specified lock-in periods and distinctive number ranges.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in start date: 15-Sep-2025
- Lock-in end dates: 13-Dec-2025, 26-Mar-2026, 26-Mar-2027, 14-Oct-2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard administrative listing notice with no broader market impact.
