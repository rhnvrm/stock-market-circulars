## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) effective from September 12, 2025. The circular details securities shortlisted under the ST-ASM framework, securities moving out of the framework, and a consolidated list of securities currently in the framework.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted under the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective September 12, 2025.
- Specific securities are moving out of the ST-ASM framework due to various criteria including inclusion in other surveillance frameworks.
- A consolidated list of securities under various stages of the ST-ASM framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM surveillance parameters on listed equities to monitor abnormal price movements and volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must apply appropriate surveillance margins and trading checks for the shortlisted securities as per exchange norms.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced monitoring and potential trading restrictions (such as applicable higher margins) for the securities included in the ST-ASM framework.
