## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of surveillance measures in respect of companies with high 'Encumbrance' as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011, effective from October 06, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Annexure I**: Lists securities shortlisted in the surveillance measure for companies with high encumbrance w.e.f. October 06, 2025, including MSP Steel & Power Ltd, Nuvama Wealth Management Ltd, SAR Auto Products Ltd, Share India Securities Ltd, and Tilaknagar Industries Ltd.
- **Annexure II**: Lists securities moving out of the surveillance measure, including Indo Tech Transformers Ltd, Marathon Nextgen Realty Ltd, RattanIndia Power Ltd, and Steel Exchange India Ltd.
- **Annexure III**: Provides the consolidated list of securities under the high encumbrance framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions on companies exhibiting high promoter or non-promoter encumbrance under SEBI (SAST) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the shortlisted securities and apply appropriate trading restrictions or monitoring as mandated by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: October 06, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading restrictions and monitoring for specified scrips under the high encumbrance surveillance framework.
