## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares of Mahip Industries Ltd. (Scrip Code: 542503), allowing trading to resume following the company's fulfillment of compliance requirements and payment of applicable reinstatement fees and fines.

## Key Points

- Revocation of trading suspension for Mahip Industries Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 542503
- Compliance achieved with Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) and listing requirements.
- Settlement of dues including reinstatement fees and statutory fines.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The circular applies specifically to the operational status and trading permission of the listed entity.

## Compliance Requirements

The company has successfully cleared its outstanding dues and fulfilled filing requirements as mandated by BSE and SEBI regulations.

## Important Dates

- In-principle approval and revocation announced per exchange guidelines.
- Trading resumption effective as per exchange notification.

## Impact Assessment

Enables liquidity and trading access for shareholders of Mahip Industries Ltd. on the exchange platform.
