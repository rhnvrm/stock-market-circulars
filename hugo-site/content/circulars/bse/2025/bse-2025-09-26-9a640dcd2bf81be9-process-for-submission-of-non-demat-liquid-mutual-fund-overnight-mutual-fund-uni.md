## Summary

This circular outlines the lien application and consent process for Investment Advisers (IAs) to submit Non-Demat Liquid Mutual Fund or Overnight Mutual Fund Units toward their deposit requirement with BSE Limited (IAASB).

## Key Points

- Investment Advisers can pledge non-demat liquid or overnight mutual fund units to meet BSE deposit requirements.
- Unitholders must submit a lien application cum consent letter to the respective RTA or AMC.
- Units must be free of prior encumbrances, lock-in periods, or tax-saving schemes (such as Section 54EA/EB of the Income Tax Act).
- BSE Limited holds the right to redeem or transfer the pledged units in the event of default on dues emanating from arbitration and conciliation proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes a standardized procedure for recognizing lien/pledge on mutual fund units as required by SEBI Regulations for Investment Advisers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers and unitholders must correctly fill out the lien application form providing details of the mutual fund scheme, folio number, ISIN, units, and NAV.
- Ensure units are free from prior charges and are available as a free balance before applying for the lien.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as specified by the regulatory guidelines.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates regulatory compliance for Investment Advisers by allowing liquid mutual fund investments to serve as required security deposits.
