## Summary

The BSE has issued a notification detailing upcoming corporate actions, specifically dividend payments, book closure dates, record dates, and ex-dates for multiple securities trading in the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) segment.

## Key Points

- Lists corporate actions (primarily dividends) for companies such as 63 Moons Technologies Limited, Parag Milk Foods Limited, DCW Ltd., LT Foods Limited, and others.
- Provides essential dates including Book Closure/Record Date, Ex-Date, and Shut Period.
- Affects market participants engaged in the SLB segment requiring accurate tracking of corporate entitlements.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Informational notice regarding scheduled corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the ex-dates and shut periods for the listed securities while executing transactions in the SLB segment.

## Important Dates

- Effective dates range from September 12, 2025, to September 23, 2025, depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal direct operational impact; ensures transparency and smooth processing of corporate benefits for borrowed and lent securities.
