## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability, continuation, and removal of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective September 17, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework.
- List of securities moving out of the Short Term ASM Framework.
- Consolidated list of securities under various stages of the ST-ASM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures intended to protect investors and curb excessive price volatility in specific scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance margins and monitoring mechanisms.
