## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding Demat Auction - 726 for the settlement of short deliveries of securities in the market.

## Key Points

- Conducted as part of the exchange's rolling settlement process to fulfill delivery obligations resulting from short deliveries.
- Trading members holding the required securities can participate by offering them within the specified price and time window.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members involved in short deliveries or participating as sellers in the auction must ensure adherence to the auction schedule and ensure securities availability for pay-in.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 1, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth clearing and settlement cycles and mitigates counterparty risk from short deliveries.
