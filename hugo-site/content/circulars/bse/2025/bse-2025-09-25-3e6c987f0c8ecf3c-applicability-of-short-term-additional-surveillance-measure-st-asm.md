## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability, continuation, and removal of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework effective from September 26, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective September 26, 2025.
- Details on securities moving out of or continuing within the ST-ASM framework.
- Consolidated list of securities under various stages of the Short Term ASM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including price bands and margin requirements for shortlisted securities under the ST-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance status of the securities and adjust trading and margining parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to stricter surveillance controls, potential margin requirements, and price band restrictions.
