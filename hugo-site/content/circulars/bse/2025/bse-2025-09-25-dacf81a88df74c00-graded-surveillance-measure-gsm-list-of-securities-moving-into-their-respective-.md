## Summary

BSE has released a circular detailing the list of securities that are moving into various Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, effective as per the surveillance framework.

## Key Points

- Wardwizard Healthcare Limited and Pratik Panels Ltd. moved to GSM Stage I.
- Woodsvilla Ltd., Manraj Housing Finance Ltd., and Purohit Construction Limited moved to GSM Stage II.
- Neelkanth Limited, Futuristic Securities Ltd., and Golechha Global Finance Ltd. moved to GSM Stage III.
- Garware Synthetics Ltd. moved to GSM Stage IV.
- Securities marked (#) are moving lower of GSM Framework due to inclusion in ESM Framework.
- Securities marked ($) are moving lower of GSM Framework due to inclusion in IBC Framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of stricter surveillance and trading restrictions as per the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework for specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors need to take note of the stage movements and associated trading constraints (such as additional surveillance margins or trade-for-trade settlement where applicable).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for the affected securities will be directly impacted based on their assigned GSM stage.
