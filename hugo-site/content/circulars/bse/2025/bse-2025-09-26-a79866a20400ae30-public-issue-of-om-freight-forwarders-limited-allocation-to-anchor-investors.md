## Summary

Om Freight Forwarders Limited finalized its anchor investor allocation ahead of its Initial Public Offering (IPO), raising ₹15.90 crore by allocating 11,77,710 equity shares to three anchor investors at the upper price band of ₹135 per share.

## Key Points

- Total funds raised from anchor investors: ₹15.90 crore.
- Total equity shares allocated: 11,77,710 shares.
- Issue price / Upper price band: ₹135 per share.
- Investors included Craft Emerging Market Fund PCC – Citadel Capital Fund, Rajasthan Global Securities Pvt. Ltd., and Abundantia Capital VCC.

## Compliance Requirements

Lock-in requirements apply to the anchor investor shares pursuant to SEBI ICDR Regulations (50% lock-in for 30 days, remaining 50% for 90 days).

## Important Dates

- Anchor Allocation Date: September 26, 2025
- IPO Bidding Period: September 29, 2025 – October 3, 2025
- Listing Date: October 8, 2025
