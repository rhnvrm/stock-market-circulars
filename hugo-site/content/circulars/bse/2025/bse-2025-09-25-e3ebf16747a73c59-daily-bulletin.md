## Summary

The BSE Daily Bulletin dated September 25, 2025 (Bulletin No: 123/2025-2026) outlines scheduled corporate actions, including book closures, record dates, interest payments, redemptions, and dividend distributions for various commercial papers (CPs), non-convertible debentures (NCDs), and equity shares.

## Key Points

- Lists corporate actions for multiple issuers including Adani Enterprises Ltd., Aditya Birla Capital Ltd., Berar Finance Limited, Finstars Capital Limited, Five-Star Business Finance Ltd., ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited, and IOT Utkal Energy Services Limited.
- Details record dates (RD) for commercial paper redemptions, NCD interest payments, partial NCD redemptions, and interim equity dividends.
- Specifies trading flags and security types for each listed corporate action.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; this is a routine market operations bulletin.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and market participants must ensure compliance with the specified record dates and book closure schedules for corporate benefits and debt servicing.

## Important Dates

- **28/09/2025**: Record date for Finstars Capital Limited (NCD Redemption).
- **01/10/2025**: Record date for Adani Enterprises Ltd. (CP Redemption).
- **06/10/2025 to 23/10/2025**: Various record dates for Aditya Birla Capital, Five-Star Business Finance, ICICI Lombard, and IOT Utkal Energy Services.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notices ensuring transparency and timely execution of debt servicing, redemptions, and corporate distributions for market participants.
