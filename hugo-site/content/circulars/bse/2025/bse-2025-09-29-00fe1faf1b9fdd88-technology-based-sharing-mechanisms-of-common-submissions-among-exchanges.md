## Summary

This circular outlines the technology-based sharing mechanisms for common submissions among stock exchanges, specifying various compliance particulars, frequencies or due dates, and relevant notice references for trading members.

## Key Points

- Standardized timelines for holding statements (T+1 day).
- Reporting requirements for bank and demat accounts within 1 week of opening or closure.
- Submission of UCC-wise settlement of client funds details within 10 trading days post-quarterly settlement.
- Event-based and periodic compliance reporting regarding insurance cover, PAN details of KMPs/directors, change in non-designated/designated directors, change in control, and restructuring events.

## Regulatory Changes

Consolidation and referencing of historical notices and SEBI guidelines regarding common exchange submissions and member compliance requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must ensure timely submissions of holding statements, bank/demat account changes, UCC-wise client funds details, and event-based disclosures as per the specified frequencies and due dates.

## Important Dates

- Holding Statement: T+1 day
- Bank/Demat Account Reporting: Within 1 week of opening/closure
- UCC Client Funds Details: Within 10 trading days post quarterly settlement
- Insurance Cover Proof: May 31st or July 31st based on validity period

## Impact Assessment

 Streamlines operational reporting and ensures synchronized compliance across stock exchanges for member intermediaries.
