## Summary

BSE Limited has released a circular detailing the process and a standard lien application/consent letter format for Research Analysts (RAs) to submit Non-Demat Liquid Mutual Fund or Overnight Mutual Fund Units as part of their deposit requirements to RAASB (BSE Limited).

## Key Points

- Research Analysts can utilize Non-Demat Liquid or Overnight Mutual Fund Units to meet regulatory deposit requirements.
- Requires submission of a formal Lien Application cum Consent Letter to the respective RTA or AMC.
- The lien remains in force until lifted or vacated by RAASB (BSE Limited).
- Deposits can be utilized if the RA fails to pay dues arising from arbitration and conciliation proceedings under the Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) Mechanism.
- Dividend plans, reinvestment options, and bonus accretions are excluded from the lien.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes a standardized framework for marking a lien on non-demat mutual fund units in favor of BSE Limited for regulatory compliance by Research Analysts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research Analysts must submit the duly filled Lien Application cum Consent Letter containing scheme details, folio numbers, ISIN, unit count, and valuation.
- Ensure units are free from prior encumbrances, lock-in periods, or tax-saving provisions (e.g., Section 54EA/EB of the Income Tax Act).

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for ongoing compliance under SEBI Regulations pertaining to Research Analysts.

## Impact Assessment

- Simplifies the collateral and deposit submission process for Research Analysts using liquid and overnight mutual fund holdings while protecting exchange interests in case of default.
