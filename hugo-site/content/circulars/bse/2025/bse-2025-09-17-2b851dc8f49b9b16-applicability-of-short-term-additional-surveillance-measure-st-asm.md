## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Effective September 18, 2025, specific securities are shortlisted under the ST-ASM framework, while others are moving out or continuing with revisions.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of securities under the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective September 18, 2025.
- Inclusion of companies such as Abram Food Ltd, Escorp Asset Management Ltd, Parker Agrochem Exports Ltd, Rushil Decor Ltd, Shri Keshav Cements And Infra Ltd, and TIL Ltd in ST-ASM Stage I.
- Movement of several securities out of the ST-ASM framework due to various criteria or inclusion in other surveillance frameworks.
- Consolidated list provided detailing all securities currently under the ST-ASM framework along with their respective stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance measures under the Short Term ASM framework to monitor price volatility and safeguard investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements (often 100% or applicable higher margins) for securities placed under the ST-ASM framework, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
