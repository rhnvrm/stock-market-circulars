## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular regarding the framework on the Social Stock Exchange ("SSE"), incorporating amendments to the ICDR Regulations, 2018 and LODR Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Modifications are made pursuant to amendments to the ICDR Regulations and LODR Regulations notified in September 2025.
- Updates minimum registration criteria for Not for Profit Organizations (NPOs) seeking registration with the SSE.
- Specifies eligible legal structures for NPOs, including charitable trusts and section 8 companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Replaces the specific table row in paragraph 1, sub-paragraph A of the September 19, 2022 circular with updated entity registration criteria under various acts (Indian Trusts Act 1882, Public Trust Statutes, Societies Registration Act 1860, and Companies Act 2013).

## Compliance Requirements

- NPOs must ensure their registration certificates are valid for at least the next 12 months at the time of registration and are registered under the specified legal frameworks.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 19, 2025
- Gazette Notification Dates: September 08, 2025 and September 09, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Clarifies and aligns the SSE registration framework with recent amendments to SEBI ICDR and LODR regulations.
