## Summary

An open offer is being made by Vasantkumar Lavjibhai Mangroliya and four other acquirers to the public shareholders of Yash Trading and Finance Limited to acquire up to 26,00,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹10/- each, representing 26% of the expanded share capital of the target company at an offer price of ₹12/- per share.

## Key Points

- **Acquirers:** Vasantkumar Lavjibhai Mangroliya, Madhubhai Nanjibhai Vekaria, Pavankumar Dhirajlal Trivedi, Vinubhai Nanjibhai Vekaria, and Manan Pavankumar Trivedi.
- **Target Company:** Yash Trading and Finance Limited.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 26,00,000 equity shares (26% of the expanded share capital).
- **Offer Price:** ₹12/- per equity share.
- **Regulatory Framework:** In accordance with Regulations 3(1) and 4 read with Regulation 15(1) and Regulation 13(2A)(i) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 concerning open offers and public shareholding disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders may tender their equity shares in accordance with the terms specified in the Letter of Offer and Form of Acceptance-cum-Acknowledgement.

## Important Dates

- Refer to the detailed tendering period schedule provided in the official Letter of Offer documents.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects the shareholding structure and liquidity of equity shares of Yash Trading and Finance Limited during and post the open offer period.
