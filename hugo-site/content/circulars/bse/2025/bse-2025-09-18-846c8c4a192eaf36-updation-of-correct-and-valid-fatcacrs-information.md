## Summary

This circular outlines the uniform implementation of KYC requirements, ultimate beneficial ownership details, and FATCA/CRS information updation for mutual fund investors, following AMFI Best Practice Guidelines Circular No. 62/2015-16.

## Key Points

- Mandatory provision of Beneficial Ownership and additional KYC details (gross annual income, net worth, etc.) for all new mutual fund investors effective November 1, 2015.
- Purchase and switch transactions for 'KYC – On Hold' cases are restricted unless missing information is provided.
- Existing investors must update missing KYC information, beneficial ownership, and FATCA/CRS details by December 31, 2015, to continue making additional subscriptions or switches from January 1, 2016.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of AMFI guidelines on uniform KYC and FATCA compliance for mutual funds.
- Stricter validation rules applied by AMCs and RTAs based on KRA KYC statuses.

## Compliance Requirements

- Mutual Fund Intermediaries (MFIs) and Distributors (MFDs) must ensure new and existing investors provide complete beneficial ownership and additional KYC details.
- AMCs and RTAs must restrict transactions on non-compliant or 'On Hold' KYC accounts.

## Important Dates

- November 1, 2015: Effective date for new investor KYC and beneficial ownership mandates.
- December 31, 2015: Deadline for existing investors to update missing KYC information.
- January 1, 2016: Effective date restricting additional subscriptions/switches for existing investors with incomplete KYC.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and regulatory compliance across mutual fund investments while potentially restricting transactions for accounts with incomplete KYC or FATCA details.
