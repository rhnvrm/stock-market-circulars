## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares of Steelco Gujarat Limited (Scrip Code: 500399). The circular includes the company profile, registered and corporate address details, board of directors, and operational status.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Steelco Gujarat Limited
- Scrip Code: 500399
- Nature of Business: Manufacturing of cold rolled steel products
- Registered Office: Plot No. 2, G.I.D.C. Estate, Palej, Dist. Bharuch – 392 220, Gujarat

## Regulatory Changes

Trading suspension of the company's equity shares has been revoked by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

The company must continue to comply with all listing regulations and disclosure requirements stipulated by BSE.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange for revocation of suspension.

## Impact Assessment

Investors can resume trading in the equity shares of Steelco Gujarat Limited on the BSE platform.
