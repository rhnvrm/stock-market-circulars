## Summary

This BSE circular compiles responses from multiple listed companies—Gujarat Pipavav Port Limited, Punjab Chemicals and Crop Protection Ltd., and ANG Lifesciences India Limited—providing clarifications regarding recent significant movements in trading volume or share price across exchanges. All responding companies confirmed that they have no undisclosed information or events required under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, and attribute the market movements to purely market-driven factors.

## Key Points

- Gujarat Pipavav Port Limited clarified that it has no information or updates required under Regulation 30 explaining its recent volume increase.
- Punjab Chemicals and Crop Protection Ltd. reported that share price and volume movements are market-driven and it holds no undisclosed material information.
- ANG Lifesciences India Limited confirmed the absence of any price-sensitive information or announcements in relation to its recent price movement.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; issued under standard surveillance communication requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must respond to stock exchange surveillance queries regarding unusual volume or price fluctuations in a timely manner pursuant to SEBI LODR regulations.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: 15th September 2025

## Impact Assessment

Neutral market impact as companies have explicitly denied the presence of any material internal developments driving the recent trading activity.
