## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Justo Realfintech Limited under the SME IPO segment, detailing the distribution of securities and their respective lock-in periods.

## Key Points

- Total securities listed include 18,798,142 equity shares.
- Various tranches of shares are subject to lock-in periods extending from October 20, 2026, to October 20, 2028.
- Anchor investor shares are subject to shorter lock-in periods ending in October and December 2025.
- 3,551,000 shares are fully paid free shares (IPO FREE).

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and depositories must ensure that lock-in restrictions are strictly enforced in accordance with the specified dates.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in Start Date: September 29, 2025
- Various lock-in expiry dates: October 20, 2026; October 20, 2027; October 20, 2028; October 28, 2025; December 27, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of Justo Realfintech Limited equity shares on the BSE SME platform while maintaining regulatory lock-ins for promoter and anchor holdings.
