## Summary

Sundaram Trustee Company Limited has approved changes in the fundamental attributes and scheme name of Sundaram Diversified Equity Fund, changing it to Sundaram Value Fund. The changes follow a 'No Objection' letter from SEBI and become effective from September 12, 2025.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name Change: Sundaram Diversified Equity Fund is renamed to Sundaram Value Fund.
- Type of Scheme: Transitions from an open-ended equity linked saving scheme with a statutory lock-in of 3 years to an open-ended equity scheme following a value investment strategy.
- Investment Objective: Modified from achieving capital appreciation with a 3-year ELSS lock-in to providing long-term capital growth through a value investment strategy.
- Asset Allocation Changes: Maximum allocation for equity and equity-related instruments adjusted from 100% (min 80%) to 100% (min 65%), and debt/money market instruments allocation adjusted to max 35%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Approval received from SEBI via email dated July 8, 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors and unitholders are advised to take note of the fundamental attribute changes and the revised scheme features.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Existing unitholders in the scheme experience a shift in investment objective, strategy, and risk-return profile.
