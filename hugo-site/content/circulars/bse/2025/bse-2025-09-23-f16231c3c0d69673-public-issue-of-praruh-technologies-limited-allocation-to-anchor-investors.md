## Summary

Praruh Technologies Limited has announced the finalization of equity share allocation to anchor investors for its upcoming public issue. A total of 10,62,000 equity shares were allocated at an anchor investor allocation price of Rs. 63 per equity share (including a share premium of Rs. 53), raising a total amount of Rs. 6,69,06,000.

## Key Points

- **Total Equity Shares Allocated:** 10,62,000 shares
- **Anchor Investor Allocation Price:** Rs. 63 per share (Face Value: Rs. 10, Share Premium: Rs. 53)
- **Total Amount Invested:** Rs. 6,69,06,000.00
- **Key Anchor Investors:** 
  - Persistent Growth Fund – Varsu India Growth Story Scheme - 1 (3,82,000 shares, 35.97%)
  - Moneywise Financial Services Private Limited (4,80,000 shares, 45.20%)
  - Viney Growth Fund (2,00,000 shares, 18.83%)
- **Mutual Funds:** No applications were received from domestic mutual funds.
