## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing the application of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Effective September 24, 2025, specific securities are shortlisted for ESM, moved to higher stages, or maintained in the consolidated list.

## Key Points

- New securities shortlisted in the ESM framework effective September 24, 2025, include Anlon Healthcare Ltd, Emmforce Autotech Ltd, Gujarat Investa Ltd, IEL Ltd, Intense Technologies Ltd, and Synthiko Foils Ltd.
- Securities moving to higher stages under the ESM framework include Bisil Plast Ltd, India Lease Development Ltd, Kaya Ltd, Midwest Gold Ltd, Precision Electronics Ltd, Ramchandra Leasing & Finance Ltd, Saptak Chem And Business Ltd, Sattrix Information Security Ltd, Senthil Infotek Ltd, and Trustedge Capital Ltd.
- No securities are moving to lower stages or moving out of the ESM framework in this circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the ESM framework stage applicability and margin requirements for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the listed securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance controls, including potential margin requirements or trading frequency restrictions depending on their assigned ESM stage.
