## Summary

Mrs. Gitaben Nitinbhai Patel has issued a Letter of Offer to acquire up to 52,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each of Antariksh Industries Limited at an offer price of Rs. 96.00 per share, representing 26.00% of the voting share capital, pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: Mrs. Gitaben Nitinbhai Patel.
- Target Company: Antariksh Industries Limited (CIN: L74110MH1974PLC017806).
- Offer Size: Up to 52,000 equity shares (26% of Voting Share Capital).
- Offer Price: Rs. 96.00 per equity share payable in cash.
- Manager to the Offer: D & A Financial Services (P) Limited.
- Registrar to the Offer: Purva Sharegistry (India) Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is made pursuant to Regulations 3(1) and 4 of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.
- The offer is not conditional upon any minimum level of acceptance and is not a competing offer.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders wishing to tender their shares should consult their stock broker, investment consultant, or the Manager/Registrar to the Offer.
- Acceptance forms and transfer deeds must be properly filled and submitted through the member of the stock exchange.

## Important Dates

- Last date for upward revision/withdrawal: Tuesday, September 30, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit opportunity for existing public shareholders of Antariksh Industries Limited at the specified offer price.
