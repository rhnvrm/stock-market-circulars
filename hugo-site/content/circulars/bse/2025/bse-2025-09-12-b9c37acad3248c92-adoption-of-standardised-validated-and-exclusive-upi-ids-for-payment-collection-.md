## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular regarding the adoption of standardised, validated, and exclusive Unified Payment Interface (UPI) IDs for payment collection by SEBI-registered intermediaries from investors. The initiative aims to enhance safety, accessibility, and transparency in securities market transactions by ensuring funds are transferred directly to verified bank accounts.

## Key Points

- Structured UPI address mechanism introduced for SEBI-registered investor-facing intermediaries.
- Standardized format: Username (alphanumeric + intermediary segment suffix) followed by a unique handle '@valid' combined with the self-certified syndicate bank name (allocated by NPCI).
- Use of the structured UPI mechanism is optional for investors but mandatory for intermediaries to obtain and make available.
- Intermediaries are encouraged to actively promote the adoption of this mechanism.
- A visual 'thumbs-up inside a green triangle' icon will appear for verified intermediary UPI handles to assure investors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandatory implementation of structured UPI IDs for payment collections by registered intermediaries.
- Integration with NPCI and Self-Certified Syndicate Banks (SCSBs) for uniform handle allocation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Intermediaries must obtain and provide the structured UPI address to investors.
- Intermediaries must use the designated utility for generating valid Usernames according to their intermediary segment.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: June 11, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances investor confidence and prevents fraudulent transactions by providing a verified payment channel to recognized market intermediaries.
