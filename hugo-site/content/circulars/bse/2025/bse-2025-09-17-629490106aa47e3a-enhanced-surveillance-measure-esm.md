## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing newly shortlisted securities, securities moving to higher stages, and a consolidated list of securities under surveillance effective September 18, 2025.

## Key Points

- New securities shortlisted in the ESM framework w.e.f. Sep 18, 2025 include Digispice Technologies Ltd, Hilltone Software and Gases Ltd, JITF Infralogistics Ltd, Kavveri Defence & Wireless Technologies Ltd, and Tulasee Bio Ethanol Ltd.
- Securities moving to higher stages of the ESM framework include Avi Products India Ltd, CHPL Industries Ltd, and Oxford Industries Ltd.
- A comprehensive consolidated list of securities currently under the ESM framework (Stages I and II) has been published.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework to deter excessive volatility and speculation in specific scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the ESM stages and apply appropriate trading restrictions, margin requirements, and settlement conditions as mandated by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance controls, potentially affecting liquidity, price movement, and required trading margins.
