## Summary

BSE has published clarifications received from various listed companies in response to exchange inquiries regarding significant movements in their share price or trading volume in recent periods. The companies have confirmed that they do not possess any unpublished price-sensitive information or reasons for the market-driven movements.

## Key Points

- **Gujarat Pipavav Port Limited**: Clarified that it has no information or updates required under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, regarding the recent increase in volume.
- **Punjab Chemicals and Crop Protection Limited**: Stated that all material events have been timely disclosed and the recent movement in price and volume is purely market-driven.
- **ANG Lifesciences India Limited**: Confirmed the absence of any additional price-sensitive information or announcements under Regulation 30 influencing its recent price movement.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; responses are governed under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must respond to stock exchange surveillance queries regarding abnormal price/volume behavior within the stipulated timelines.

## Important Dates

- **Clarification Date**: September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Routine compliance disclosure with negligible direct impact on market operations or stock valuations.
