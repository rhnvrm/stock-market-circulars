## Summary

BSE has issued a circular detailing the application of additional exposure margins on securities that have crossed the prescribed Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL) thresholds. The circular includes an annexure specifying the affected scrip and security details.

## Key Points

- Additional exposure margin applied to securities exceeding MWPL limits.
- Annexure lists RBL Bank Limited (Scrip Code: 540065, ISIN: INE976G01028).

## Regulatory Changes

- Enhanced margin requirements triggered due to high open interest relative to market-wide position limits.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must maintain adequate margins for positions in the affected security as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective September 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased capital requirements for market participants holding positions in RBL Bank Limited derivatives.
