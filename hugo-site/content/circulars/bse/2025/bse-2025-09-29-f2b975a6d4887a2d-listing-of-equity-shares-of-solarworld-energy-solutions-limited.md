## Summary

Solarworld Energy Solutions Limited has issued a circular regarding the listing of its equity shares along with detailed lock-in arrangements, specifying various categories of shares under lock-in and those exempt from lock-in as per SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018.

## Key Points

- Details various tranches of equity shares under lock-in with specific start and end dates ranging from September 2025 to September 2028.
- 31,41,025 equity shares allocated to Anchor Investors are locked-in for 30 days (up to 25th October 2025), and 31,41,026 equity shares for 90 days (up to 24th December 2025).
- Certain shares held by Alternative Investment Funds (AIF Category II) are exempt from lock-in in accordance with Regulation 17(c) of SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with Regulation 17(c) of SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to the specified lock-in periods and distinct categories for equity shares as outlined in the company's prospectus.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: 28-Sep-2025
- Anchor Investor Lock-in Expiry (30 days): 25-Oct-2025
- Anchor Investor Lock-in Expiry (90 days): 24-Dec-2025

## Impact Assessment

- Establishes official trading eligibility and lock-in constraints for shares of Solarworld Energy Solutions Limited post-IPO.
