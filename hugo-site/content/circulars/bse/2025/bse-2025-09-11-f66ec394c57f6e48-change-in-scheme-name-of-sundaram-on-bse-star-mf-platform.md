## Summary

Sundaram Mutual Fund has issued a notice cum addendum regarding the change in fundamental attributes and scheme name of Sundaram Diversified Equity Fund. The scheme is being renamed to Sundaram Value Fund, transitioning from an ELSS scheme to an open-ended equity scheme following a value investment strategy, effective September 12, 2025.

## Key Points

- Scheme name changed from Sundaram Diversified Equity Fund to Sundaram Value Fund.
- Category changed from an open-ended equity linked saving scheme with 3-year lock-in and tax benefit to an open-ended equity scheme following value investment strategy.
- Investment objective modified to provide long-term capital growth with a value investment strategy.
- Asset allocation revised: Equity and equity-related instruments allocation changed from (Min 80%, Max 100%) to (Min 65%, Max 100%), and Debt/Money Market securities adjusted accordingly.
- Effective date of changes: September 12, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- Changes approved by Sundaram Trustee Company Limited following the "No Objection" letter issued by SEBI on July 8, 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

- Unitholders are notified of the changes in fundamental attributes and have been provided options/exit terms as per regulatory guidelines if they do not agree with the proposed changes.

## Important Dates

- SEBI Email Date: 08-July-2025
- Effective Date: 12-Sep-2025

## Impact Assessment

- Existing investors holding units in Sundaram Diversified Equity Fund will see their scheme attributes and investment strategy updated to reflect the value investment focus.
