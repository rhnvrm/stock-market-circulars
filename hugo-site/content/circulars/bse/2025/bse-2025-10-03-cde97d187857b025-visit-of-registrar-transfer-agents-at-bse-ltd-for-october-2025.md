## Summary

BSE Ltd. has released the schedule and mapping of Registrar & Transfer Agents (RTAs) visiting BSE Ltd. for October 2025. The circular details the scrip codes, long names of companies, and their corresponding RTA names such as Adroit Corporate Services Pvt. Ltd.

## Key Points

- Lists various listed companies alongside their assigned Registrar & Transfer Agents (RTAs).
- Facilitates coordination between listed entities, RTAs, and the exchange.
- Covers multiple scrip codes managed by Adroit Corporate Services Pvt. Ltd. and other designated RTAs.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities and RTAs are required to coordinate as per the scheduled visits and ensure compliance with transfer and registry operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 2025

## Impact Assessment

Operational update with minimal direct impact on general market participants, primarily intended for issuer companies and their RTAs.
