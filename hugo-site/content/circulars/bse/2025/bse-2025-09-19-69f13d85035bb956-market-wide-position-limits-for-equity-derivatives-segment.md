## Summary

Indian Clearing Corporation Limited (ICCL) has specified the security-wise market wide position limits (MWPL) and position limits for Trading Members, FII/FPI (Category I & II), and Mutual Funds for Futures and Options contracts on individual securities for the month of October 2025.

## Key Points

- Details of position limits are provided in Annexure I.
- Applicable for Futures and Options contracts on individual securities for October 2025.
- Incorporates updates to the header format as per SEBI circular guidelines.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with ICCL's Master Circular for Transactions in Equity Derivatives Segment and SEBI measures for enhancing trading convenience and risk monitoring.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, trading members, FIIs/FPIs, and mutual funds must adhere to the specified position limits for October 2025.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 19, 2025
- Applicable Month: October 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures market stability and compliance with risk monitoring parameters in the equity derivatives segment.
