## Summary

Stock Exchange BSE has issued a circular regarding updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Effective September 16, 2025, specific securities are shortlisted, moved to higher stages, or maintained under the ESM framework.

## Key Points

- New securities shortlisted in the ESM Framework effective September 16, 2025 include Bafna Pharmaceuticals Ltd, Karan Woo Sin Ltd, Lakhotia Polyesters (India) Ltd, Longspur International Ventures Ltd, and Parmeshwari Silk Mills Ltd.
- Securities moving to higher stages under the ESM framework include Ekennis Software Service Ltd and Trident Texofab Ltd.
- Annexure III provides a comprehensive consolidated list of securities currently under the ESM framework along with their respective stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for identified securities to protect market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the ESM framework applicability and stage changes for the listed securities when executing trades.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance controls, margin requirements, or trading frequency restrictions associated with their respective ESM stages.
