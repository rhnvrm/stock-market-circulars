## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a notification regarding securities moving into various Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, including Stage I, II, III, and IV.

## Key Points

- Polo Hotels Ltd has moved to GSM Stage I.
- Worldwide Aluminium Ltd and Omkar Pharmachem Ltd have moved to GSM Stage II.
- Garware Synthetics Ltd and Prashant India Ltd have moved to GSM Stage III.
- Artson Ltd has moved to GSM Stage IV.
- Framework adjustments account for inclusions in ESM or IBC frameworks where applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance and margin requirements for securities placed under specific GSM stages.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must comply with the trading restrictions and additional deposit requirements associated with the respective GSM stages.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange surveillance notifications.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities may be restricted due to the surveillance measures imposed.
