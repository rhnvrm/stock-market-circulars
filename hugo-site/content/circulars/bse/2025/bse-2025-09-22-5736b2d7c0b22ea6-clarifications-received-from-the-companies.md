## Summary

BSE Limited sought clarifications from Adani Green Energy Limited, Adani Total Gas Limited, and Adani Power Limited regarding recent movements in the volume of their equity shares. In their respective responses, the companies stated that the volume movements were purely due to market conditions and were entirely market-driven, with the management having no control over or knowledge of the reasons for the increase.

## Key Points

- BSE issued surveillance queries to multiple Adani group companies concerning equity share volume movements.
- Adani Green Energy Limited, Adani Total Gas Limited, and Adani Power Limited confirmed that volume changes are market-driven and independent of management control.
- All three companies reaffirmed compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, regarding disclosure of material events and price-sensitive information.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must continue to promptly disclose all material events and price-sensitive information in compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No operational or financial impact on the companies. This is a standard disclosure procedure triggered by exchange surveillance mechanisms.
