## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notification regarding corporate actions in Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) securities, detailing dividend record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods for specified companies.

## Key Points

- **BEML Ltd**: Dividend corporate action with Book Closure/Record Date and Ex-Date on 22-September-2025; Shut period from 19-Sep-2025 to 22-Sep-2025.
- **Sanghvi Movers Ltd.**: Dividend corporate action with Book Closure/Record Date and Ex-Date on 12-September-2025; Shut period from 11-Sep-2025 to 12-Sep-2025.
- **Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited**: Dividend corporate action with Book Closure/Record Date and Ex-Date on 22-September-2025; Shut period from 19-Sep-2025 to 22-Sep-2025.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational schedule update for corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and custodians must take note of the respective ex-dates, record dates, and shut periods for settlement and SLB operations.

## Important Dates

- **Sanghvi Movers Ltd. Ex-Date / Record Date**: 12-September-2025
- **BEML Ltd. & Gujarat Fluorochemicals Ltd. Ex-Date / Record Date**: 22-September-2025

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to market participants engaged in trading or lending/borrowing activities for the specified securities during their respective shut periods.
