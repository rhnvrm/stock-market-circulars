## Summary

BSE sought clarification from listed companies regarding significant trading volume movements across exchanges. In their responses, companies such as Vascon Engineers Limited and Expleo Solutions Limited confirmed that they have no undisclosed developments or pending announcements under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- BSE issued volume movement queries to listed entities following unusual trading activity.
- Vascon Engineers Limited and Expleo Solutions Limited confirmed that the trading volume increase is purely market-driven.
- Both companies verified timely compliance with all disclosure requirements under SEBI regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; routine compliance verification under existing SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must respond to stock exchange surveillance queries regarding unusual volume or price movements in a timely and transparent manner.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact; standard procedure ensuring transparency and investor protection against speculative volatility.
