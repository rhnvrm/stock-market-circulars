## Summary

BSE Limited sought clarifications from various listed companies regarding significant movements in their stock prices. In response, the companies have confirmed that they have disclosed all material events and information pursuant to Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and that the price movements are purely market-driven.

## Key Points

- Exchanges routinely seek clarification on significant stock price variations.
- Companies (including TVS Electronics Limited, Dynamin Industries Limited, and Nettlinx Limited) confirmed no pending disclosures or unannounced price-sensitive information.
- All companies reaffirmed compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard compliance verification under surveillance mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must continue to promptly disclose any material events or price-sensitive information to the exchange as mandated under Regulation 30.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Request Date: 26th September 2025
- Response Date: 29th September 2025

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct operational impact; provides transparency to investors regarding stock price volatility.
