## Summary

Systematic Industries Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern and post-issue details under Regulation 31 as of September 30, 2025, detailing promoter and public shareholdings.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: Systematic Industries Limited
- Filing Date: 30-SEP-2025
- Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 73.41% (1,63,94,442 shares)
- Public Shareholding: 26.59% (59,36,800 shares)
- Total Shares: 2,23,31,242 shares

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with Regulation 31(1) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Reporting Date: 30-SEP-2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard compliance disclosure with no direct market impact.
