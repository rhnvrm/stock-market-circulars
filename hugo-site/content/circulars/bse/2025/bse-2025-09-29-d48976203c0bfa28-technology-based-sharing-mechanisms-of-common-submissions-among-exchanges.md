## Summary

The circular outlines technology-based sharing mechanisms for common submissions among stock exchanges. It provides a comprehensive schedule of periodic and event-based submissions required from members, including holding statements, bank/demat account reporting, UCC settlement details, insurance cover proofs, and net worth certificates.

## Key Points

- Details reporting frequencies and notice references for various regulatory and operational filings.
- Covers recurring filings such as T+1 holding statements, weekly bank/demat account reporting, and quarterly UCC-wise client funds settlement details.
- Outlines event-based disclosures including changes in KMP/Directors, shareholding, control, and compliance officers.
- Specifies timelines for submitting half-yearly net worth certificates and insurance cover proofs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Harmonization and standardization of submission frequencies and tracking through relevant notice references.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure timely submissions within specified due dates (e.g., T+1 day for holding statements, weekly reporting for account openings/closures, quarterly reporting for UCC client funds details).
- Event-based changes such as changes in directors, control, or constitution must be intimated within stipulated timelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per reference notices.
- Specific frequencies range from T+1 day, weekly, event-based, to half-yearly intervals.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines regulatory reporting processes for trading members by clarifying common submission schedules and references across exchanges.
