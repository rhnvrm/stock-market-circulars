## Summary

BSE sought clarification from Jagsonpal Finance & Leasing Limited and Tirupati Foam Ltd regarding recent price movements in their shares. Both companies officially responded confirming that they are in compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and have no pending announcements or unpublished price-sensitive information that would impact share prices.

## Key Points

- Jagsonpal Finance & Leasing Limited clarified that share price movements are purely market-driven and outside the company's control or knowledge.
- Tirupati Foam Ltd confirmed there are no pending announcements under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- Both companies reiterated their commitment to keeping the stock exchange informed of any future material events.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Continuous compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 regarding prompt disclosure of material events and price-sensitive information.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: 29th - 30th September 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact; standard procedural response to exchange inquiries regarding market volatility.
