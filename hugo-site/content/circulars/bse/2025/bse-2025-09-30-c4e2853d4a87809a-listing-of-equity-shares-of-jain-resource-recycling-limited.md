## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and lock-in details for the equity shares of Jain Resource Recycling Limited following its initial public offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- 22,21,89,242 shares are under lock-in from 29-Sep-2025 to 30-Mar-2026.
- 6,90,17,263 shares are under lock-in from 29-Sep-2025 to 30-Mar-2027.
- Anchor investor shares (1,21,22,841 shares) are locked-in until 28-October-2025, and another 1,21,22,848 shares are locked-in until 27-December-2025.
- 2,96,33,620 shares are not under lock-in (IPO public issue).

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to lock-in periods as specified in the company prospectus.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice/Filing: 29-Sep-2025
- Lock-in expiry dates ranging from October 2025 to March 2027.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine listing update for market participants regarding share availability and lock-in restrictions.
