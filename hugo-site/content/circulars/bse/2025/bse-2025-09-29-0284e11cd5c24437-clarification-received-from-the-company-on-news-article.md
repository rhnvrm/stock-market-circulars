## Summary

Sunteck Realty Limited has issued a clarification in response to a news article published on Upstox on September 29, 2025, titled "Sunteck Realty shares zoom nearly 9%: Firm to launch ₹20,000 crore worth of ultra-luxury homes in Mumbai, Dubai." The company clarified that there is no new negotiation or undisclosed information, and the planned launches are well within the previously disclosed total Gross Development Value (GDV) of ~₹39,800 crores from its Q1FY26 investor presentation.

## Key Points

- Addressed media reports regarding ₹20,000 crore luxury housing launches in Mumbai and Dubai.
- Confirmed that the reported GDV of ₹20,000 crores is part of the overall ~₹39,800 crores GDV already disclosed in the Q1FY26 investor presentation.
- Stated that recent share price movements are market-driven with no pending Unpublished Price Sensitive Information (UPSI).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Continued compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Date of Clarification / Letter: September 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides market transparency and resolves queries raised by the stock exchanges regarding recent media reports and share price movements.
