## Summary

Pidilite Industries Ltd. has announced the intimation of the record date and allotment details for the issuance of bonus equity shares. The deemed date of allotment is 24th September, 2025, with a total of 50,88,57,016 bonus shares to be issued.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Pidilite Industries Limited
- **Stock Code:** 500331
- **ISIN:** INE318A01026
- **Number of Bonus Shares:** 50,88,57,016
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 513857017 - 1022714032

## Regulatory Changes

None. Standard corporate action compliance reporting.

## Compliance Requirements

Bonus shares will be credited in the depository system latest by Wednesday, 24th September, 2025 by 12 P.M. (next working day of the record date).

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** 23rd September, 2025 (Tuesday)
- **Deemed Date of Allotment:** 24th September, 2025 (Wednesday)
- **Effective Listing Date:** 25th September, 2025 (Thursday)
- **Trading Availability:** Beginning of Day (BOD) on T+2 day i.e., 25th September, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Shareholders eligible as of the record date will receive bonus shares, increasing the total paid-up equity base of Pidilite Industries Ltd. Trading in the bonus shares will commence on 25th September, 2025.
