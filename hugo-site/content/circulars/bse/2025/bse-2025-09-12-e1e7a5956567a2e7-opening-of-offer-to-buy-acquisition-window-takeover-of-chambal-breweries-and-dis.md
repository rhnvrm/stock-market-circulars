## Summary

M/s. Invade Agro Limited (the Acquirer) has announced an open offer to acquire up to 19,47,077 fully paid-up equity shares, representing 26% of the voting share capital of Chambal Breweries and Distilleries Limited, at an offer price of ₹6.00 per share payable in cash, pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: M/s. Invade Agro Limited.
- Target Company: Chambal Breweries and Distilleries Limited.
- Offer Size: 19,47,077 equity shares (26% of equity and voting share capital).
- Offer Price: ₹6.00 per share payable in cash.
- Regulatory Compliance: Pursuant to Regulation 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 and Regulation 31(A)(5) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is compliant with SEBI Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public shareholders wishing to tender their shares should consult their stock broker, investment consultant, or manager to the offer and submit the Form of Acceptance-cum-Acknowledgement.

## Important Dates

- Refer to the detailed public statement and letter of offer for specific schedule dates regarding the tendering period.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit or participation opportunity for public shareholders of Chambal Breweries and Distilleries Limited through the acquisition window.
