## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares and the shareholding pattern disclosure of VMS TMT Limited under Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: VMS TMT LIMITED
- Scrip details: Equity shares
- Quarter ending: 24/09/2025
- Total shareholding of Promoter and Promoter Group stands at 67.18%, while Public shareholding stands at 32.82%.

None.
