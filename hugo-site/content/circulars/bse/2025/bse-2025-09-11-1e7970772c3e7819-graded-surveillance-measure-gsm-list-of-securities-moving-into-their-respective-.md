## Summary

The BSE has released a notification regarding securities moving into various Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages. The affected companies face heightened surveillance and trading restrictions as per regulatory frameworks.

## Key Points

- Futuristic Securities Ltd (Security Code: 523113, ISIN: INE425D01015) moved to GSM Stage II.
- High Street Filatex Ltd (Security Code: 531301, ISIN: INE319M01011) moved to GSM Stage III.
- Space Incubatrics Technologies Ltd (Security Code: 541890, ISIN: INE797Z01010) moved to GSM Stage III.
- Specific symbols indicate movements due to inclusion in ESM or IBC frameworks.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Graded Surveillance Measures to monitor and curb excessive price volatility in specific securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must adhere to the trading restrictions and margin requirements applicable to securities placed in respective GSM stages.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the listed securities will be subject to additional safeguards, including differential margin requirements and trading frequency restrictions, impacting liquidity and market participation.
