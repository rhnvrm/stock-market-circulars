## Summary

SEBI has issued circular SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131 on September 25, 2025, detailing compliance guidelines and reporting mechanisms for Regulated Entities (REs) regarding digital accessibility under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

## Key Points

- REs must submit a list of digital platforms provided for investors by September 30, 2025.
- Appointment of IAAP certified accessibility professionals as auditors by December 14, 2025.
- Accessibility audits for digital platforms must be conducted by April 30, 2026.
- Remediation of audit findings and full compliance must be achieved by July 31, 2026.
- Annual accessibility audits and reporting are required starting April 30, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

Establishes explicit compliance milestones and reporting formats for all REs subject to the digital accessibility mandate originally issued on July 31, 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

Regulated entities must submit their compliance data via email to digital_acc@sebi.gov.in or through their designated reporting authorities (Stock Exchanges, Depositories, or BSE Ltd. for IAs and RAs) following the specified timelines.

## Important Dates

- September 30, 2025: Submission of digital platforms list
- December 14, 2025: Appointment of IAAP certified auditor
- April 30, 2026: Conduct of Accessibility Audit
- July 31, 2026: Remediation and final compliance
- April 30, 2027: First annual compliance report

## Impact Assessment

Regulated entities need to allocate resources promptly to audit and remediate their digital platforms to ensure full accessibility for persons with disabilities within the stipulated deadlines.
