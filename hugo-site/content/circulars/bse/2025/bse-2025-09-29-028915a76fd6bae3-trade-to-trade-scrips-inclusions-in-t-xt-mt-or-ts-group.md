## Summary

BSE has issued a circular detailing the shifting of various securities from the rolling segment to the Trade-to-Trade (T, XT, MT) segment, along with a list of securities being retained in the Trade-to-Trade segment with a price band of 5% or lower effective from October 03, 2025.

## Key Points

- Securities are shifted from the rolling segment to the Trade-to-Trade segment with a price band of 5% or lower.
- Includes regular and SME segment securities moving to groups like XT, T, and MT.
- Annexure-II lists securities retained in the Trade-to-Trade segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Transfer of specified securities into Trade-to-Trade settlement mechanisms.
- Application of a 5% or lower price band on the affected securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with Trade-to-Trade settlement rules for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 03, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified scrips will be restricted to the Trade-to-Trade segment, potentially impacting liquidity and intraday trading capabilities.
