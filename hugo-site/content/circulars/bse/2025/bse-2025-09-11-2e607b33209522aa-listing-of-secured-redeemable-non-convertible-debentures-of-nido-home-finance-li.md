## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of secured redeemable non-convertible debentures (NCDs) issued by Nido Home Finance Limited across multiple series with varying coupons, frequencies, and maturity dates ranging from 2027 to 2035.

## Key Points

- Issuer: Nido Home Finance Limited
- Security Type: Secured, Redeemable, Non-Convertible Debentures
- Face Value / Issue Price: ₹1,000.00 per NCD
- Allotment Date: September 10, 2025
- Total Series Listed: Series I to X with distinct scrip codes, ISINs, coupon rates (ranging up to 10.50%), and maturity dates (10-Sep-2027 to 10-Sep-2035).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and market participants must ensure adherence to standard depository, trading, and settlement guidelines for newly listed debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: September 10, 2025
- Maturity Dates: Staggered between September 10, 2027, and September 10, 2035 depending on the series.

## Impact Assessment

Provides liquidity and official exchange trading access for the newly issued debt securities of Nido Home Finance Limited.
