## Summary

True Colors Limited has successfully finalized its anchor investor allocation ahead of its SME Initial Public Offering (IPO). The company allocated 19,05,600 equity shares to 24 anchor investors at an issue price of ₹191 per share, successfully raising ₹36.40 crore.

## Key Points

- **Total Amount Raised:** ₹36.40 Crore
- **Total Shares Allotted:** 19,05,600 equity shares
- **Allotment Price:** ₹191 per share
- **Number of Anchor Investors:** 24 institutional and corporate participants
- **Listing Platform:** BSE SME

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard IPO anchor allotment compliance under SEBI ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Lock-in requirements apply to anchor investor shares in accordance with SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Anchor Investor Allotment Date:** September 22, 2025
- **IPO Opening Date:** September 23, 2025
- **IPO Closing Date:** September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Demonstrates robust institutional participation and strong pre-issue demand for True Colors Limited's SME IPO.
