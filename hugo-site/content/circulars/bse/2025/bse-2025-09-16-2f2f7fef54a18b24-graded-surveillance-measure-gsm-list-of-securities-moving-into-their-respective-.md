## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a circular detailing securities that are moving into various Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages (Stage I, II, III, and IV), effective as part of ongoing market surveillance mechanisms.

## Key Points

- KLG Capital Services Ltd moved to GSM Stage I.
- Pasupati Fincap Ltd and Integra Switchgear Ltd moved to GSM Stage II.
- P.M. Telelinnks Ltd moved to GSM Stage III.
- SW Investments Ltd moved to GSM Stage IV.
- Notes indicate securities marked (#) or ($) may be moving lower in the GSM framework due to inclusion in the ESM or IBC frameworks respectively.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities subjected to enhanced trading restrictions and surveillance stages to safeguard market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and associated trading restrictions for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective date corresponding to circular release: September 16, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trading surveillance for the listed scrips, directly impacting liquidity and trading activities for these securities.
