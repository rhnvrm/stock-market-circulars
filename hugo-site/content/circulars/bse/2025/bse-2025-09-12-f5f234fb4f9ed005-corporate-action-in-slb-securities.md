## Summary

The BSE has released a circular detailing corporate actions, specifically dividends, record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods for securities operating within the Stock Lending and Borrowing (SLB) segment.

## Key Points

- **BEML Ltd**: Dividend corporate action with Record Date and Ex-Date set for 22-September-2025. Shut period spans from 19-Sep-2025 to 22-Sep-2025.
- **Sanghvi Movers Ltd.**: Dividend corporate action with Record Date and Ex-Date set for 12-September-2025. Shut period spans from 11-Sep-2025 to 12-Sep-2025.
- **Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited**: Dividend corporate action with Record Date and Ex-Date set for 22-September-2025. Shut period spans from 19-Sep-2025 to 22-Sep-2025.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action schedule announcement.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the specified ex-dates, record dates, and shut periods when executing trades and managing obligations in the SLB segment.

## Important Dates

- Sanghvi Movers Ltd. Ex-Date / Record Date: 12-September-2025
- BEML Ltd. Ex-Date / Record Date: 22-September-2025
- Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited Ex-Date / Record Date: 22-September-2025

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact, affecting only settlement and borrowing/lending positions relative to the corporate action timelines for the specified equities.
