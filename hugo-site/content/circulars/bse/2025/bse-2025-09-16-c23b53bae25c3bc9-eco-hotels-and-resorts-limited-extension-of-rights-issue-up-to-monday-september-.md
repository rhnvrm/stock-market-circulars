## Summary

Eco Hotels and Resorts Limited has announced an extension of its ongoing rights issue. The issue, which originally was scheduled to close on Wednesday, September 17, 2025, has been extended to Monday, September 22, 2025, as approved by the Rights Issue Committee on September 16, 2025.

## Key Points

- Original Issue Closing Date: Wednesday, September 17, 2025.
- Revised Issue Closing Date: Monday, September 22, 2025.
- Scrip Code/ID: 514402/ECOHOTELS.
- ISINs: INE638N01012 (Ordinary Shares), INE638N20012 (RE), IN9638N01010 (PP).

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a corporate action timeline modification.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders must ensure the duly filled in CAF along with application money is submitted by the revised closure date of Monday, September 22, 2025.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: September 08, 2025
- Revised Issue Closing Date: September 22, 2025
- Finalization of Basis of Allotment: September 25, 2025
- Date of Allotment: September 26, 2025
- Date of Credit: September 30, 2025
- Date of Listing/Trading: October 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Provides additional time and opportunity for eligible shareholders to exercise their rights under the Rights Issue.
