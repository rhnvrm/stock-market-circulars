## Summary

TruAlt Bioenergy Limited has finalized the allocation of 50,76,289 equity shares to anchor investors at the upper price band of ₹496 per equity share, successfully raising ₹251.78 crore ahead of its initial public offering.

- Total Amount Raised: ₹251.78 crore
- Number of Anchor Investors: 20 institutional investors
- Shares Allocated: 50,76,289 equity shares
- Allocation Price: ₹496 per equity share (upper end of price band)
