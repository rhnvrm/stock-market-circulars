## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted, moved to higher stages, or continuing under the framework effective September 26, 2025.

## Key Points

- New securities have been shortlisted under the ESM framework (Annexure I, Part A).
- Securities continuing in the ESM framework have been moved to respective higher stages (Annexure I).
- A comprehensive consolidated list of securities under the ESM framework along with their respective stages is provided (Annexure III).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the ESM framework to safeguard market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must note the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the listed securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, including applicable margin requirements and trading restrictions depending on their assigned stage.
