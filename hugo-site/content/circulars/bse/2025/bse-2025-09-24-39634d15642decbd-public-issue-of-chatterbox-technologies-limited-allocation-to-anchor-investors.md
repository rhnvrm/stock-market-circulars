## Summary

Chatterbox Technologies Limited has finalized the allocation of 1,059,600 equity shares to anchor investors at an anchor investor allocation price of ₹115 per equity share (including a share premium of ₹105) for its upcoming public issue.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares allocated to anchor investors: 1,059,600.
- Anchor Investor allocation price: ₹115 per share.
- Total amount raised from anchor investors: ₹121,854,000.
- Key anchor investors include Sanshi Fund I, Meru Investment Fund PCC-Cell 1, Viney Growth Fund, Finvesta First, Mint Focused Growth Fund PCC, and Moneywise Financial Services Pvt Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- As per SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018, if the final Offer Price discovered through the book building process is higher than the anchor investor allocation price, anchor investors are required to pay the difference.

## Compliance Requirements

- Anchor investors must pay any potential price difference by the pay-in date specified in the revised CAN.

## Important Dates

- Allocation Date: September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Marks a key milestone in the public offering process for Chatterbox Technologies Limited, establishing anchor investor participation and pricing benchmarks.
