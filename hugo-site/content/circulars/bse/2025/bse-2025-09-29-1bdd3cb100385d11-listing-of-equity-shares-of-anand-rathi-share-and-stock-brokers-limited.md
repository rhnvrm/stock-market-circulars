## Summary

Anand Rathi Share & Stock Brokers Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern and details regarding the listing of its post-issue equity shares under Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 as of September 29, 2025.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: Anand Rathi Share & Stock Brokers Limited (Post Issue).
- Filing Date: 29.09.2025.
- Compliance standard: Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- Promoter and Promoter Group shareholding stands at 43,845,408 equity shares, representing 69.90% of total shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Disclosures are submitted in accordance with existing SEBI LODR norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities are required to regularly submit their specified securities holding patterns and declarations regarding partly paid shares, depository receipts, locked-in shares, and promoter encumbrances.

## Important Dates

- As of Date: 29.09.2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Informational filing for market participants regarding the post-issue shareholding structure and promoter holding of the company.
