## Summary

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs has issued a Certificate of Incorporation pursuant to a change of name for Pacheli Industrial Finance Limited to Grand Oak Canyons Distillery Limited, effective from April 3, 2025.

## Key Points

- Old Company Name: PACHELI INDUSTRIAL FINANCE LIMITED
- New Company Name: GRAND OAK CANYONS DISTILLERY LIMITED
- Original Company Name: DHOOT FINANCE COMPANY PRIVATE LIMITED
- Effective Date: April 3, 2025
- CIN: L74110MH1985PLC037772

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

The company is required to display its old name for a period of two years along with its new name at all required places in terms of Section 12 of the Companies Act, 2013.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Name Change: April 3, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate update with minimal direct market impact, though stakeholders should note the new entity name for future reference.
