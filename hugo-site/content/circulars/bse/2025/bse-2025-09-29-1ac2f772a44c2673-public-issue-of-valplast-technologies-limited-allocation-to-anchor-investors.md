## Summary

Valplast Technologies Limited has announced the allocation of 14,70,000 equity shares to anchor investors at an allocation price of ₹54 per share (including a share premium of ₹44 per share), raising a total amount of ₹7,93,80,000.

## Key Points

- Total Equity Shares allocated to Anchor Investors: 14,70,000 shares.
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: ₹54 per share.
- Total Amount Raised through Anchor Portion: ₹7,93,80,000.
- Key Anchor Investors include Rajasthan Global Securities Private Limited, Strategic Sixth Sense Capital Fund, Viney Growth Fund, Moneywise Financial Services Private Limited, Vikasa India EIF I Fund - Incube Global Opportunities, and Steptrade Revolution Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (issued pursuant to SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018).

## Compliance Requirements

- In case the Offer Price discovered through the book building process is higher than the anchor investor allocation price, anchor investors are required to pay the difference by the pay-in as specified in the revised CAN.

## Important Dates

- Board Meeting Date / Allocation Date: September 29, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Marks a successful preliminary milestone for Valplast Technologies Limited's public issue by securing anchor investor commitments.
