## Summary

KFin Technologies Limited has issued a clarification regarding a news article appearing on scanx.trade dated September 22, 2025, which claimed that General Atlantic planned to sell 10-15% of its stake at a discount. The company categorically denied the rumors, stating that the promoter group has not indicated any intention to sell their stake.

## Key Points

- **News Item Clarification:** Addressed reports on scanx.trade regarding General Atlantic planning a 10-15% stake sale.
- **Promoter Status:** General Atlantic (promoter and promoter group) has not indicated any intention to sell their stake to the management or Board of Directors.
- **No Negotiations:** The company is not aware of any ongoing negotiations regarding the mentioned transaction.
- **Price Movement:** The company has no information linking the 5.79% decrease in share price on September 22, 2025 (from ₹1149.40 to ₹1082.85) to the news development.
- **Regulatory Status:** No significant regulatory or legal proceedings are pending against the company.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Fulfillment of disclosure requirements under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Clarification:** September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Reassures investors and market participants by officially refuting speculative news regarding significant promoter stake divestment.
