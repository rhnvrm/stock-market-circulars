## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the applicability, continuation, and removal of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective from October 03, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted and included in the Long Term ASM Framework (Annexure I).
- Securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework are detailed in Annexure II.
- A consolidated list of securities continuing in the framework with respective ASM stages is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures such as LT-ASM to protect investors and curb excessive price volatility in specific scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance categories and apply appropriate margin requirements as mandated for scrips under the ASM framework.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Securities included in the LT-ASM framework may experience additional trading safeguards, including higher margin requirements and surveillance monitoring.
