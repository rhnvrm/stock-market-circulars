## Summary

Mr. Vivek Kumar Singhal, Mr. Kshitij Agrawal, M/s Nivesh Mandi Private Limited, and M/s Stock Mandi have announced an open offer for the acquisition of up to 15,60,000 equity shares, representing 26.00% of the voting capital of GSB Finance Limited, at an offer price of ₹21.44 per share payable in cash, pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Acquirers: Vivek Kumar Singhal, Kshitij Agrawal, Nivesh Mandi Private Limited, and Stock Mandi.
- Target Company: GSB Finance Limited.
- Offer Size: Up to 15,60,000 equity shares (26.00% of voting capital).
- Offer Price: ₹21.44/- per equity share (including interest).
- Regulatory Compliance: Pursuant to Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

- Acquisition of control and management accompanied by mandatory open offer requirements under SEBI SAST Regulations.
- Prior approval from the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) was obtained on September 12, 2025, as required for non-banking financial companies (NBFCs).

## Compliance Requirements

- Public shareholders of GSB Finance Limited may tender their shares in accordance with the letter of offer and acceptance-cum-acknowledgement form.
- Intermediaries and stockbrokers must facilitate the tendering process through the acquisition window.

## Important Dates

- RBI Approval Date: September 12, 2025.
- Refer to the detailed schedule in the letter of offer for specific tender period dates and payment schedules.

## Impact Assessment

- Results in a change in management and control of GSB Finance Limited.
- Provides an exit opportunity for public shareholders at the specified offer price.
