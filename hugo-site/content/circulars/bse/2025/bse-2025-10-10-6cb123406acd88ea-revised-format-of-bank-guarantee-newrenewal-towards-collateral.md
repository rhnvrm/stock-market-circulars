## Summary

BSE has issued the revised operational format and guidelines regarding the submission of new and renewal Bank Guarantees (BGs) used by trading members towards collateral and margin requirements with the clearing corporation.

## Key Points

- Specifies the updated text and covenants for Bank Guarantees (New/Renewal).
- Mandates compliance with electronic transmission via SFMS (Structured Financial Messaging System).
- Outlines conditions for acceptable scheduled commercial banks and claim periods.

## Regulatory Changes

- Integration of revised RBI and SEBI norms concerning collateral valuation and bank guarantee issuance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure that any new or renewed Bank Guarantees submitted to ICCL strictly adhere to the revised format.
- BGs must be issued by eligible scheduled commercial banks with proper SFMS confirmation.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for all subsequent BG submissions and renewals.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the processing and verification of collateral bank guarantees, reducing administrative friction and ensuring legal compliance.
