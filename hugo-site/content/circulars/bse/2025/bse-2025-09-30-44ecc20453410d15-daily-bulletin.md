## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 126/2025-2026, dated September 30, 2025, outlines upcoming book closures, record dates, and payment/redemption schedules for privately placed non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and commercial papers across multiple issuers.

## Key Points

- Lists record dates and corporate action details for non-convertible debentures and commercial papers.
- Features multiple entries for issuers including Adani Green Energy (UP) Limited, Annapurna Finance Private Limited, Bajaj Financial Securities Limited, Bank of Baroda, Berar Finance Limited, and Birla Group Holdings Private Limited.
- Specifies dates for payment of interest, partial redemption of NCDs, and redemption of commercial papers.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and market participants must take note of the specified record dates and book closure periods for corporate actions and debt servicing.

## Important Dates

- Bulletin Date: 30/09/2025
- Record Dates / Book Closure Dates range from October 2025 through December 2025 (e.g., Annapurna Finance on 07/10/2025, Adani Green Energy on 16/12/2025).

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification for bondholders, debenture trustees, and market participants regarding debt servicing schedules.
