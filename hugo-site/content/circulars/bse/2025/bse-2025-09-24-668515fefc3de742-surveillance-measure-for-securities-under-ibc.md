## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a notice regarding the Surveillance Measure for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective September 25, 2025. This outlines the shortlisted securities under Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) stages related to the Insolvency Resolution Process.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted under ASM for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) Stage 1 w.e.f. September 25, 2025 include Rajeswari Infrastructure Ltd and Sical Logistics Ltd.
- Consolidated lists of securities under various stages of IRP as per IBC are updated and detailed in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shortlisting of companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process into specific surveillance stages to increase market transparency and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and applicable trading restrictions for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced monitoring and potential trading restrictions (such as applicable margins or trade-for-trade mechanisms) for securities listed under the IBC surveillance framework.
