## Summary

KFin Technologies Limited has issued a clarification regarding a news article published on scanx.trade claiming that General Atlantic plans to sell 10-15% of its stake at a discount. The company categorically denied the reports as inaccurate and speculative.

## Key Points

- Media report claimed General Atlantic planned to sell 10-15% stake at a 5-8% discount.
- Company stock experienced a 5.79% decrease on September 22, 2025 (from ₹1149.40 to ₹1082.85).
- KFin Technologies confirmed that the promoter group has not indicated any intention to sell their stake.
- The company is unaware of any ongoing negotiations or developments linked to the share price drop.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, fulfilled by responding to exchange clarification requests.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Clarification:** September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to investors regarding speculative news reports and reaffirms the stance of the promoter group.
