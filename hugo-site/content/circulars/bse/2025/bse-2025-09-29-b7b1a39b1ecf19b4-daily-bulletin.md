## Summary

BSE Bulletin No. 125/2025-2026 dated September 29, 2025, outlines upcoming book closures, record dates, interest payments, partial redemptions, and dividend distributions for various listed securities, including non-convertible debentures (NCDs), commercial papers (CPs), and equity shares.

## Key Points

- Lists corporate actions including record dates for debt redemption, interest payments, and equity dividends across multiple issuers.
- Adani Airport Holdings Limited and Akara Capital Advisors schedule multiple NCD record dates for partial redemptions and interest payments through October to December 2025.
- Adani Enterprises Ltd. announces a record date of 06/10/2025 for Commercial Paper redemption.
- Ashnisha Industries Limited and Cybertech Systems and Software Ltd. announce equity corporate actions including a rights issue and a special dividend of Rs. 20.00 per share (200%).
- Bank of Baroda sets record dates for early redemption and interest payments on privately placed non-convertible debentures.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and market participants must ensure compliance with record dates and trading suspensions/flags as indicated for corporate actions.

## Important Dates

- **Bulletin Date:** 29/09/2025
- **Record Dates:** Spanning across October 2025 to December 2025 (e.g., 01/10/2025, 06/10/2025, 26/12/2025).
- **Payment/Allotment Date (Cybertech):** 05/11/2025.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification for investors and depositories regarding corporate actions, dividend payouts, and debt redemption schedules.
