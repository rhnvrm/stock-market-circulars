## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability, continuation, and removal of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework effective September 24, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted and added to the Short Term ASM framework (Annexure I).
- Certain securities will continue in the ST-ASM framework but are moved to higher stages (Annexure I).
- Selected securities are moving out of the ST-ASM framework effective September 24, 2025 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under various stages of the ST-ASM framework is provided (Annexure III).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures in accordance with SEBI and exchange guidelines to protect market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and surveillance monitoring apply to the short-listed securities, potentially affecting trading volume and volatility for these scrips.
