## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-716. The circular outlines the entry and matching timings for auction offers along with the subsequent pay-in schedule.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement No.: DR-716
- Auction Offer Entry and Matching Timings as per exchange schedule.
- Members must check auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should exercise care when punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine settlement and auction notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off times. Members must check the cut-off rate on the Auction offer Entry Screen and review corporate action notices.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 17, 2025
- Auction Pay-in Date: As specified in the settlement schedule.

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification impacting trading members participating in Demat Auction DR-716 with no broader market-wide structural changes.
