## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 128/2025-2026 dated October 3, 2025, outlines upcoming book-closure and record dates for various securities including commercial papers and privately placed non-convertible debentures (NCDs).

## Key Points

- Lists corporate actions including interest payments, part redemptions, and full redemptions for various issuers.
- Covers Commercial Papers in DMAT for companies like Aarti Industries Ltd.
- Covers privately placed Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) for issuers such as Achievers Finance India Limited, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, Ambit Finvest Private Limited, and Arka Fincap Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers must ensure compliance with record dates and book closure schedules for interest payouts and redemptions.

## Important Dates

- Bulletin Date: 03/10/2025
- Record/Book Closure Dates span across October and November 2025 (e.g., 06/10/2025, 16/10/2025, 17/10/2025, 27/10/2025, 04/11/2025).

## Impact Assessment

Relevant for bondholders and institutional investors tracking debt maturities, interest payments, and commercial paper redemptions.
