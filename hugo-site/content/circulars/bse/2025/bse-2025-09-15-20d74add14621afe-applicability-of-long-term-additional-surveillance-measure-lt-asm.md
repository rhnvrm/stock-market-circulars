## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective September 16, 2025, detailing shortlistings, stage movements, and consolidated security lists.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. September 16, 2025 (Annexure I, Part A).
- Includes companies such as Dynamic Portfolio Management & Services Ltd, Odigma Consultancy Solutions Ltd, Sharat Industries Ltd, Shilp Gravures Ltd, and Wardwizard Innovations & Mobility Ltd.
- Annexure III provides a consolidated list of securities under various ASM stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance measures under the Long Term ASM framework to ensure market integrity and safeguard investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to surveillance controls, including applicable margin requirements and price bands.
