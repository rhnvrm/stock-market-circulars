## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted, moving to higher stages, or continuing in the framework effective September 12, 2025.

## Key Points

- New securities shortlisted in the ESM Framework effective September 12, 2025 (e.g., ADC India Communications Ltd, Wardwizard Foods and Beverages Ltd).
- Securities moving to higher Stage ESM (e.g., Flora Corporation Ltd, GHV Infra Projects Ltd).
- Consolidated list of securities currently under various stages of the ESM framework provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the ESM framework to maintain market integrity and curb excessive volatility in specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable ESM stages and associated trading restrictions for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities may experience modified trading conditions and margin requirements in accordance with their assigned ESM stage.
