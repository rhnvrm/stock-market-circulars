## Summary

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited has issued a notice-cum-addendum announcing the inclusion of a Daily Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) frequency for select schemes, effective September 15, 2025.

## Key Points

- Daily SIP frequency is introduced for Aditya Birla Sun Life Gold Fund and Aditya Birla Sun Life Silver ETF Fund of Fund.
- Effective date for the new frequency is Monday, September 15, 2025.
- All other features, terms, and conditions of the Schemes' SID and KIM remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Existing and prospective investors opting for Daily SIP need to ensure sufficient fund availability as per the new frequency.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 15, 2025
- **Notice Date:** September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with added flexibility and frequent investment options in gold and silver fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform.
