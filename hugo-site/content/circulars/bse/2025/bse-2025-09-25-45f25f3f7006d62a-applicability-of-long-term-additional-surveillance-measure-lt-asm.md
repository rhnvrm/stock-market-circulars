## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective September 26, 2025. The circular outlines securities newly shortlisted in the framework, securities moving to higher stages, and securities moving out of the framework.

## Key Points

- **Annexure I - Part A**: Lists new securities shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. September 26, 2025 (e.g., Athena Constructions Ltd, Bil Vyapar Ltd, Chandrima Mercantiles Ltd, Karma Energy Ltd, Nimbus Projects Ltd, Setco Automotive Ltd, Shivam Chemicals Ltd, Triton Corp Ltd).
- **Annexure I - Part B**: Lists securities continuing in the Long Term ASM Framework but moved to higher Stage II (Jayabharat Credit Ltd, Pankaj Polymers Ltd).
- **Annexure II**: Lists securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework due to various reasons such as inclusion in Trade for Trade, GSM, ESM, or IBC frameworks.
- **Annexure III**: Provides the consolidated list of securities under the ASM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance measures such as trading restrictions and applicable margins on shortlisted securities to safeguard market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must comply with the trading restrictions, higher margin requirements, and collateral norms applicable to securities placed under the respective ASM stages.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date**: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity in the affected scrips may be constrained due to increased margin requirements or surveillance stage transitions.
