## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the list of securities shortlisted under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, those moving to higher or lower stages, and those moving out of the framework effective September 22, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Shortlisted Securities:** 7 securities (including SME scrips) are newly shortlisted in the ESM framework.
- **Movement to Higher Stage:** 1 security (Inducto Steel Ltd) is moved to a higher stage within the ESM framework.
- **Movement to Lower Stage:** 15 securities are moved to lower stages within the ESM framework.
- **Exit from Framework:** Multiple securities, including Akar Auto Industries Ltd, Archies Ltd, Asian Granito India Ltd, and Hindustan Motors Ltd, are moving out of the ESM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

The circular updates the surveillance status of various scrips in accordance with SEBI and BSE guidelines for the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must note the revised stage classifications and applicable trading restrictions for the listed securities effective September 22, 2025.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading margins and surveillance restrictions for the affected securities will be adjusted according to their respective new ESM stages or removal status.
