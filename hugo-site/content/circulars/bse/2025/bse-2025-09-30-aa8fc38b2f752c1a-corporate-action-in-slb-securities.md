## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding corporate actions in SLB (Securities Lending and Borrowing) securities, outlining upcoming book closure dates, record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods for various companies in October 2025.

## Key Points

- **ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited**: Dividend corporate action with Record Date and Ex-Date on 23-October-2025. Shut period from 21-Oct-2025 to 23-Oct-2025.
- **Rashtriya Chemicals & Fertilizers Limited**: Dividend corporate action with Record Date and Ex-Date on 10-October-2025. Shut period from 09-Oct-2025 to 10-Oct-2025.
- **Tata Investment Corporation Ltd.**: Stock Split corporate action with Record Date and Ex-Date on 14-October-2025. Shut period from 13-Oct-2025 to 14-Oct-2025.
- **Glenmark Pharmaceuticals ltd**: Dividend corporate action with Record Date and Ex-Date on 03-October-2025. Shut period from 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.
- **Shilpa Medicare Ltd.**: Bonus issue corporate action with Record Date, Ex-Date, and Foreclosure Date on 03-October-2025. Shut period from 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.
