## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 115/2025-2026, dated 15/09/2025, provides comprehensive notices regarding upcoming book closures, record dates, dividend payments, NCD part-redemptions, interest disbursements, and commercial paper redemptions for various listed entities.

## Key Points

- **63 Moons Technologies Limited**: Record date set for 17/09/2025 for final dividend of Rs. 1.20 per share (60%).
- **Apex Frozen Foods Limited**: Record date set for 19/09/2025 for final dividend of Rs. 2.00 per share (20%).
- **Aries Agro Limited**: Record date set for 19/09/2025 for dividend of Rs. 1.20 per share (12%).
- **A.K. Capital Finance Ltd. & Akara Capital Advisors**: Record dates announced for part-redemption of NCDs along with interest payments.
- **Adani Enterprises Ltd.**: Record date set for 19/09/2025 for the redemption of commercial papers in DMAT.
- **Multiple Issuers (e.g., Asirvad Microfinance, AU Small Finance Bank)**: Schedule of interest payment record dates for privately placed non-convertible debentures spanning September to November 2025.

- **Bulletin Date**: 15/09/2025
- **Book-Closure / Record Dates**: Varying dates from 17/09/2025 through 30/11/2025 depending on the company and security type.
