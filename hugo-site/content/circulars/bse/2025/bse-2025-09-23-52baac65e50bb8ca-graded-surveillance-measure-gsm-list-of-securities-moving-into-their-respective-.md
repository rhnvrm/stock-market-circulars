## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a notification regarding the list of securities moving into respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) Stages III and IV, detailing companies subject to enhanced surveillance and trading curbs.

## Key Points

- Securities such as India Radiators Ltd., Mathew Easow Research Securities Ltd, Artson Limited, and Advik Laboratories Ltd. have moved to GSM Stage III.
- Numerous securities including Rama Petrochemicals Ltd., Kartik Investments Trust Ltd., Deccan Bearings Ltd., and Gayatri Projects Ltd. have been moved to GSM Stage IV.
- The framework aims to alert market participants and curb excessive volatility or abnormal price rise in specific scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

- Affected securities face stricter trading controls, additional surveillance deposits, or trade-for-trade monitoring depending on their assigned GSM stage.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, brokers, and investors must take note of the surveillance stage changes and ensure compliance with respective margin requirements and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective date as per circular release on 25 July 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities are likely to be constrained due to stringent surveillance measures.
