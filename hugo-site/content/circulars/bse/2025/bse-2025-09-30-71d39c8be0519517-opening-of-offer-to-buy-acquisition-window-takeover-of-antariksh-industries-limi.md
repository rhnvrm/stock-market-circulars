## Summary

Mrs. Gitaben Nitinbhai Patel has issued a Letter of Offer to acquire up to 52,000 fully paid-up equity shares of Antariksh Industries Limited at an Offer Price of Rs. 96.00 per share, representing 26.00% of the Voting Share Capital pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: Mrs. Gitaben Nitinbhai Patel.
- Target Company: Antariksh Industries Limited.
- Offer Size: Up to 52,000 equity shares (26.00% of Voting Share Capital).
- Offer Price: Rs. 96.00 per equity share payable in cash.
- Manager to the Offer: D & A Financial Services (P) Limited.
- Registrar to the Offer: Purva Sharegistry (India) Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is made pursuant to Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Shareholders wishing to tender their shares should consult their stock broker, investment consultant, or the Manager/Registrar to the Offer and complete the Form of Acceptance cum Acknowledgement.

## Important Dates

- Last date for upward revision/withdrawal: Tuesday, September 30, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit opportunity for public shareholders of Antariksh Industries Limited at the specified offer price.
