## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing changes under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The circular provides lists of securities newly shortlisted in the ESM framework, securities moving to higher or lower stages, and securities moving out of the framework effective September 15, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities into the ESM framework effective September 15, 2025.
- Movement of specific securities to higher or lower stages of ESM.
- Removal of specified securities from the ESM framework effective September 12, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance measures, including additional trading restrictions or margin requirements for shortlisted securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must note the changes in surveillance stages and adjust trading strategies and margin provisions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for ESM inclusions and stage changes: September 15, 2025.
- Effective date for securities moving out of ESM: September 12, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

Stocks listed under the ESM framework may experience altered liquidity and trading volume due to enhanced margins or surveillance safeguards.
