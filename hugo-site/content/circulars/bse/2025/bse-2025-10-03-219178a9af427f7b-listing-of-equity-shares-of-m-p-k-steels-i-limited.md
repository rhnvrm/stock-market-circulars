## Summary

M P K Steels (I) Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern under Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, in connection with the listing of its equity shares post-IPO.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: M P K Steels (I) Limited
- Scrip Code/Name: Equity Shares
- Filing Date: October 3, 2025 (Post issue of IPO)
- Regulatory compliance under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 regarding shareholding pattern disclosures and ongoing listing requirements.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: 2025-10-03

## Impact Assessment

Standard market listing of new equity shares following an initial public offering.
