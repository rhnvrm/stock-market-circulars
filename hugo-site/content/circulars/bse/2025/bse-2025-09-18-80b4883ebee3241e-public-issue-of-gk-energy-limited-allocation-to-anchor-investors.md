## Summary

GK Energy Limited has finalized the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors ahead of its public issue, raising ₹139.28 crore by allocating 91,03,136 equity shares at the upper price band of ₹153 per share to 12 anchor investors.

## Key Points

- Total amount raised from anchor investors: ₹139.28 crore
- Total equity shares allocated: 91,03,136 shares
- Issue/Allocation Price: ₹153 per share
- Number of Anchor Investors: 12 institutional investors including Pinebridge India Equity Fund, 360 ONE Flexicap Fund, HSBC Flexi Cap Fund, and 3P India Equity Fund 1M.
