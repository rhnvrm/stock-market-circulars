## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the applicability, continuation, movement to higher/lower stages, and exclusion of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective September 24, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities are shortlisted and moved into the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective September 24, 2025.
- Certain securities will continue in the ST-ASM framework but are moved to respective higher Stage ASM.
- Multiple securities are moving out of the ST-ASM framework due to various criteria including inclusion in other surveillance frameworks such as LT-ASM or ESM.
- A consolidated list of securities under the ST-ASM stages has been published in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the ST-ASM framework to safeguard investors and curb excessive volatility in specific scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified scrips will be subject to heightened surveillance and applicable margin requirements as per the ST-ASM framework guidelines.
