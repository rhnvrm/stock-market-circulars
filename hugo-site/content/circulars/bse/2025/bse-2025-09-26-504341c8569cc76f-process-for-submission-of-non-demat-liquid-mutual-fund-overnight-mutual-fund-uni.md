## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a circular regarding the process for submission of Non-Demat Liquid Mutual Fund or Overnight Mutual Fund Units towards deposit requirements by Research Analysts (RAs) via a lien application cum consent letter.

## Key Points

- Details the format and requirements for creating a lien in favor of BSE Limited (RAASB) on mutual fund units.
- Specifies conditions regarding free balance, absence of lock-in periods, and non-coverage of dividend/bonus accruals under the lien.
- Empowers BSE Limited to realize the deposit in case of unpaid dues under the Online Dispute Resolution Mechanism or SEBI regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of standard lien application procedures for Research Analysts and Investment Advisers meeting deposit mandates under SEBI regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research Analysts must submit the duly completed lien application cum consent letter through their RTA or AMC to mark a pledge in favor of BSE Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per circular issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the compliance process for Research Analysts fulfilling deposit requirements using non-demat liquid and overnight mutual fund holdings.
