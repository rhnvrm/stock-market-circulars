## Summary

Eco Hotels and Resorts Limited has announced an extension of its rights issue closing date from Wednesday, September 17, 2025, to Monday, September 22, 2025. The extension was approved by the Rights Issue Committee on September 16, 2025, to provide eligible shareholders additional time to exercise their rights.

## Key Points

- Original Issue Closing Date: Wednesday, September 17, 2025
- Revised Issue Closing Date: Monday, September 22, 2025
- Scrip Code/ID: 514402/ECOHOTELS
- ISIN: INE638N01012

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Eligible shareholders must submit their filled Common Application Forms (CAF) along with application money by the revised closing date.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: September 8, 2025
- Revised Issue Closing Date: September 22, 2025
- Basis of Allotment: September 25, 2025
- Date of Allotment: September 26, 2025
- Date of Credit: September 30, 2025
- Listing / Trading Date: October 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides extended timeline and opportunity for investors to participate in the rights issue.
