## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted, moved to higher stages, or continuing within the framework effective September 19, 2025.

## Key Points

- New securities shortlisted in the ESM framework w.e.f. September 19, 2025 (e.g., Global Capital Markets Ltd, Maitri Enterprises Ltd, Ravinder Heights Ltd).
- Securities moving to higher stages of the ESM framework w.e.f. September 19, 2025 (e.g., Bhatia Colour Chem Ltd, Miven Machine Tools Ltd, Navkar Urbanstructure Ltd, Sanblue Corporation Ltd, Sunrise Efficient Marketing Ltd, Tecil Chemicals & Hydro Power Ltd).
- Consolidated list provided covering various securities across stages I and II.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework to monitor abnormal price movements and volatility in designated scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable ESM stages and associated trading restrictions or margin requirements for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trading restrictions for securities included in or moved up within the ESM framework, impacting liquidity and volatility for the affected scrips.
