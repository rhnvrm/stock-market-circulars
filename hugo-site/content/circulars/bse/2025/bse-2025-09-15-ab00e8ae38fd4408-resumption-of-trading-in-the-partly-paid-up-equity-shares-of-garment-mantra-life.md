## Summary

BSE has announced the resumption of trading in the partly paid-up equity shares of Garment Mantra Lifestyle Limited following the receipt of call money on a specified number of shares effective from September 2025.

## Key Points

- Total Partly Paid Shares issued as on 10.09.2025: 39,14,97,795
- Total Partly Paid shares on which call money received: 37,34,26,591
- Total shares available for trading on resumption: 37,34,26,591
- Paid-up value per share available for trading: Rs. 0.50
- ISIN: IN9653501026

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with standard BSE trading rules for partly paid-up equity instruments.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 10.09.2025

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the partly paid-up shares under ISIN IN9653501026 can resume trading activities for 37,34,26,591 shares.
