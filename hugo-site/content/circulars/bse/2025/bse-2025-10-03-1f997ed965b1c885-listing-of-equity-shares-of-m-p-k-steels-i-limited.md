## Summary

M P K Steels (I) Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern under Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, following the post-issue of its IPO.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: M P K Steels (I) Limited
- Filing pertains to Reg. 31(1)(a) post issue of IPO dated 3-OCT-2025.
- Details regarding promoter and public shareholding structures, lock-in status, and declarations on partly paid shares and depository receipts are recorded.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for shareholding pattern submission.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: 03-OCT-2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard procedural listing update with no direct broad market impact.
