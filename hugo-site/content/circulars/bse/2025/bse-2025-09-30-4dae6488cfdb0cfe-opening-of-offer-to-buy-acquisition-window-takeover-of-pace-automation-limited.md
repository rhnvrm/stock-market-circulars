## Summary

An open offer is being made by M/s U G Patwardhan Services Private Limited, M/s Agri One India Ventures LLP, and Mr. Ravidutt Parikh (collectively the Acquirers) to acquire up to 7,86,077 equity shares representing 26.00% of the voting share capital of Pace Automation Limited at an offer price of ₹13.00 per share payable in cash, pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Target Company: Pace Automation Limited.
- Acquirers: M/s U G Patwardhan Services Private Limited, M/s Agri One India Ventures LLP, and Mr. Ravidutt Parikh.
- Offer Size: Up to 7,86,077 equity shares (26.00% of the voting share capital).
- Offer Price: ₹13.00/- per equity share payable in cash.
- Regulations: Pursuant to Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 including subsequent amendments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public shareholders of the target company may participate in the open offer by submitting their acceptance-cum-acknowledgement and transfer deeds through their stockbrokers.

## Important Dates

- Last Date for Upward Revision of Offer Price/Size: Wednesday, October 01, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates change in control and management of the target company alongside acquisition of public shareholding.
