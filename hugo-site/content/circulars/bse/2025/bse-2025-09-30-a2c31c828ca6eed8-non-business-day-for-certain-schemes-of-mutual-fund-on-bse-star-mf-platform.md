## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding non-business days for certain schemes of mutual funds available on the BSE StAR MF platform for the month of October 2025. The circular lists specific dates where transactions for designated schemes across fund houses such as Axis, Kotak, Edelweiss, Nippon, DSP, Invesco, and Bandhan will not be processed due to non-business days.

## Key Points

- Lists non-business days for October 2025 across multiple mutual fund houses.
- Affected fund houses include Axis, Kotak, Edelweiss, Nippon, DSP, Invesco, and Bandhan.
- Applies to specific international, thematic, and specific fund-of-fund schemes.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors should note the non-business days for placing subscription, redemption, or switch orders for the specified mutual fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective Month: October 2025

## Impact Assessment

Operational guidance for investors and distributors trading in specific mutual fund schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform to avoid transaction failures or delays.
