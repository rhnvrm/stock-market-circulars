## Summary

BSE has announced the launch and operational guidelines for Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) of Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform. The circular specifies AMC creation requirements, investor eligibility criteria for accredited and non-accredited investors, NISM XIII certification requirements for distributors, client detail upload procedures, and validation rules for various modes of holding.

## Key Points

- Separate AMC creation and activation on BSE StAR MF platform for SIF schemes.
- Minimum investment for non-accredited investors is ₹10 lakh; accredited investors follow limits set in Scheme Information Documents (SID).
- SIF client AUM must not fall below ₹10 lakh due to redemptions.
- Distributors must pass the NISM XIII examination and register with AMFI to distribute SIFs.
- Facility provided for manual and bulk update of accredited investor details via Member Logins (Admin >> Client Details >> Client Master Upload).
- Mode of holding rules define whether accredited investor exemptions apply based on joint or single holdings.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of SIF-specific guidelines and validations on the BSE StAR MF platform for Edelweiss Mutual Fund.
- Mandatory NISM XIII certification and AMFI registration for members distributing SIF schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure correct and accurate updating of accredited investor details (PAN, Accredited No., Validity) via pipe-separated text file uploads or manual entry to prevent rejections.
- Renewal of registration certificates prior to expiry for accredited investors.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately upon release for SIF transactions on the platform.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines trading and onboarding for Specialized Investment Funds while enforcing strict validation checks on investor accreditation status and folio holdings.
