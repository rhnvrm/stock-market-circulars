## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, outlining the list of securities newly shortlisted, moved to higher/lower stages, or continuing under the framework effective September 23, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities in the ESM Framework effective September 23, 2025, includes companies such as Caspian Corporate Services Ltd, Empower India Ltd, Neo Infracon Ltd, Transchem Ltd, and Virinchi Ltd.
- Securities moving to higher Stage ESM include BCC Fuba India Ltd, Rainbow Foundations Ltd, Tera Software Ltd, Vaxfab Enterprises Ltd, and Wardwizard Foods and Beverages Ltd.
- A consolidated list of securities under various ESM stages is provided in Annexure III of the circular.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Enhanced Surveillance Measures (ESM) to specified scrips to monitor volatility and protect investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable ESM stages and trading restrictions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance controls, margin requirements, and stage-specific trading restrictions as mandated by the ESM framework.
