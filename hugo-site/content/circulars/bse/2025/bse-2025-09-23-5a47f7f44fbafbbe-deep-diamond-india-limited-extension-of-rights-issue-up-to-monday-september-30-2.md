## Summary

Deep Diamond India Limited has announced a further extension of the closing date for its ongoing Rights Issue. Originally scheduled to close on September 19, 2025, and previously extended to September 24, 2025, the closing date is now extended to Tuesday, September 30, 2025. This decision was approved by the Rights Issue Committee to grant shareholders additional time and opportunity to exercise their rights.

## Key Points

- **New Issue Closing Date:** Tuesday, September 30, 2025.
- **Scrip Code / ID:** 539559 / DDIL
- **ISIN:** INE005G01026 | **RE ISIN:** INE005G20018
- **Revised Schedule:** Post-issue activities including allotment and listing dates have been rescheduled accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. The terms of the original Letter of Offer (LOF) dated August 29, 2025, along with previous addendums, remain unchanged except for the revised timeline.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders wishing to participate must submit their duly filled Composite Application Forms (CAF) along with application money on or before September 30, 2025.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** September 09, 2025
- **Last Date for Market Renunciation:** September 18, 2025
- **Revised Issue Closing Date:** September 30, 2025
- **Finalization of Basis of Allotment:** October 06, 2025
- **Date of Allotment:** October 06, 2025
- **Date of Credit:** October 09, 2025
- **Date of Listing / Trading:** October 13, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Provides flexibility to eligible shareholders to subscribe to the rights issue within the extended timeframe without altering the underlying structure of the offering.
