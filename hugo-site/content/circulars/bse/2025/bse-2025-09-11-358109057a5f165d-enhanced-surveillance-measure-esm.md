## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing the application of the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework effective September 12, 2025. The circular provides lists of securities newly shortlisted under the ESM framework, securities moving to higher stages, and a comprehensive consolidated list of securities currently under the ESM framework.

## Key Points

- New shortlisting of securities under the ESM framework effective September 12, 2025, including ADC India Communications Ltd and Wardwizard Foods and Beverages Ltd.
- Securities moving to higher stages in the ESM framework include Flora Corporation Ltd and GHV Infra Projects Ltd.
- A consolidated list of securities under various stages of the ESM framework is provided for market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures in accordance with regulatory guidelines to deter excessive volatility and speculative trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the ESM stages and apply appropriate margin and trading restrictions for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trading controls for securities listed under the ESM framework, affecting liquidity and price discovery for the impacted scrips.
