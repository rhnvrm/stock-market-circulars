## Summary

Sampat Aluminium Limited has submitted its post-issue shareholding pattern statement pursuant to Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, detailing its specified securities and shareholding distribution as of September 23, 2025.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: Sampat Aluminium Limited
- Filing Date: September 23, 2025
- Declaration confirms no partly paid-up shares, convertible securities, warrants, or depository receipts were issued.
- Indicates presence of locked-in shares without promoter encumbrances.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Submission of shareholding pattern under Regulation 31(1)(a)/(b)/(c) of SEBI LODR Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Report Date / Allotment Date: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Routine compliance filing ensuring transparency in shareholding structure.
