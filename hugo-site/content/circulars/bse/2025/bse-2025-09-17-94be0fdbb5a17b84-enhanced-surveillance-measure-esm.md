## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the list of securities newly shortlisted, moved to higher stages, and the consolidated list of securities under the framework effective September 18, 2025.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in the ESM framework w.e.f. Sep 18, 2025 include Digispice Technologies Ltd, Hilltone Software and Gases Ltd, JITF Infralogistics Ltd, Kavveri Defence & Wireless Technologies Ltd, and Tulasee Bio Ethanol Ltd.
- Securities moving to higher stages of the ESM framework include Avi Products India Ltd, CHPL Industries Ltd, and Oxford Industries Ltd.
- A consolidated list of securities currently under various stages of the ESM framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions and stage migrations under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework for identified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance measures and applicable trading restrictions for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the shortlisted and migrated securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance controls and margin requirements as stipulated by the exchange under the ESM framework.
