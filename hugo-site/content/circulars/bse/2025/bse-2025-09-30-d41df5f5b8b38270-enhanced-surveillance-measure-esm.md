## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted, moved to higher stages, or continuing under surveillance effective October 01, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities into the ESM framework w.e.f. October 01, 2025 (Annexure I, Part A).
- Movement of existing securities to respective higher stages within the ESM framework (Annexure I).
- Consolidated list of securities under various ESM stages provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework to deter excessive volatility and protect investors in specific short-listed scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply appropriate surveillance margins and trading checks as mandated for securities in respective ESM stages.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in shortlisted securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance controls, potentially impacting liquidity and margin requirements for investors holding or trading these scrips.
