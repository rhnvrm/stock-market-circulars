## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted, moved to higher stages, and the consolidated list effective from September 23, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities under the ESM framework w.e.f. Sep 23, 2025 (e.g., Caspian Corporate Services Ltd, Empower India Ltd, Neo Infracon Ltd, Transchem Ltd, Virinchi Ltd).
- Movement of existing securities to higher Stage ESM (e.g., BCC Fuba India Ltd, Rainbow Foundations Ltd, Tera Software Ltd, Vaxfab Enterprises Ltd, Wardwizard Foods and Beverages Ltd).
- Consolidated list provided covering various securities and their respective ESM stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework rules, including specific trading restrictions and margin requirements on shortlisted and upgraded securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the ESM stages and apply appropriate trading controls and margins for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience tighter trading controls, increased margin requirements, or surveillance measures impacting liquidity.
