## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the movement of securities into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The circular lists companies moving into GSM stages 0, I, II, and III, along with notes regarding concurrent frameworks such as ESM and IBC.

## Key Points

- Polo Hotels Ltd (Security Code: 526687) moved to GSM Stage 0.
- RR Securities Ltd (Security Code: 530917) and LE Lavoir Ltd (Security Code: 539814) moved to GSM Stage I.
- Wardwizard Healthcare Ltd (Security Code: 512063) and Charms Industries Ltd (Security Code: 531327) moved to GSM Stage II.
- Purohit Construction Ltd (Security Code: 538993) moved to GSM Stage III.
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower in the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the ESM Framework.
- Securities marked with ($) are moving lower in the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the IBC Framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of stricter surveillance controls and monitoring on specified scrips to protect market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance margins and trading restrictions for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange exchange regulations.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, potentially impacting liquidity and price discovery for the short term.
