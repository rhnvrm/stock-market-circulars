## Summary

Roadway Solutions India Infra Limited along with Persons Acting in Concert (PAC 1: Ameet Harjinder Gadhoke and PAC 2: Teja Ranade Gadhoke) has announced the opening of an open offer to acquire up to 82,64,942 equity shares, representing 25.02% of the voting share capital of Soma Textiles & Industries Limited, at an offer price of INR 47.14/- per equity share payable in cash.

## Key Points

- **Acquirer:** Roadway Solutions India Infra Limited along with PACs.
- **Target Company:** Soma Textiles & Industries Limited.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 82,64,942 equity shares (25.02% of voting share capital).
- **Offer Price:** INR 47.14/- per equity share payable in cash.
- **Regulatory Framework:** In accordance with SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory rules introduced; compliance with SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 for open offers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public shareholders wishing to tender their shares must follow the acquisition window mechanism specified by the stock exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Tendering Period Commencement:** Tendering period details and schedule as per the Letter of Offer.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit opportunity for public shareholders of Soma Textiles & Industries Limited and results in potential change in management and control.
