## Summary

Mr. Amay Vatsalya has released a Letter of Offer to acquire up to 29,49,500 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹10 each, representing 26.00% of the voting share capital of CMX Holdings Limited (formerly SIEL Financial Services Limited), at an Offer Price of ₹9.70 per share payable in cash in compliance with SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: Mr. Amay Vatsalya.
- Target Company: CMX Holdings Limited (formerly known as SIEL Financial Services Limited).
- Offer Size: Up to 29,49,500 equity shares (26.00% of voting share capital).
- Offer Price: ₹9.70 per equity share payable in cash.
- Manager to the Offer: Mark Corporate Advisors Private Limited.
- Registrar to the Offer: MAS Services Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Made pursuant to Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 for substantial acquisition of shares and takeovers.
- The offer is not conditional on any minimum level of acceptance and is not a competing offer.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders may tender their equity shares through their stockbroker during the tendering period in accordance with the specified acquisition window mechanism.
- Shareholders who have recently sold their shares should hand over the Letter of Offer and accompanying form of acceptance to the buying member of the stock exchange.

## Important Dates

- Refer to the detailed public statement and announcements for the exact schedule of the tendering period and offer opening/closing dates.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit opportunity for eligible equity shareholders of CMX Holdings Limited up to the specified percentage through the stock exchange acquisition window.
