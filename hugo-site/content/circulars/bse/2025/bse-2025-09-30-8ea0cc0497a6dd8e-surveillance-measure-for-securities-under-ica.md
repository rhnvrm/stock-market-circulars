## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the list of securities under the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies relating to the Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA), effective October 01, 2025. The update covers securities continuing in ASM with revisions to their respective stages.

## Key Points

- Effective date for the revised ASM measures is October 01, 2025.
- Sadbhav Engineering Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532710) shall continue in ASM but will be moved to a respective lower stage.
- Consolidated list includes entities such as Mcleod Russel India Ltd, Sadbhav Engineering Limited, and Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy Ltd under the ICA framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies undergoing restructuring or proceedings related to the Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stage revisions and apply appropriate risk management and margin requirements as mandated by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in securities listed under the ICA surveillance framework may be subject to enhanced monitoring, margin requirements, or price bands, impacting short-term liquidity and volatility for the affected scrips.
