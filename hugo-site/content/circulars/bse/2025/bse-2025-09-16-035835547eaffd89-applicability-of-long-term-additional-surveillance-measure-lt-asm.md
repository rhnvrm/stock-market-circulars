## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, listing securities shortlisted for surveillance effective from September 17, 2025.

## Key Points

- Part A outlines securities newly shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. September 17, 2025, including companies such as 7Seas Entertainment Ltd, ACE Software Exports Ltd, and Global Capital Markets Ltd.
- Part B and Part C specify securities moving to higher or lower stages of the ASM framework, indicating nil movements in this notification.
- Part D details securities shortlisted in Direct Stage IV of the Long Term ASM Framework (nil).
- Annexure II details securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework (nil).
- Annexure III provides a consolidated list of securities under the ASM framework along with their respective stages.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities listed in Annexure I are subjected to enhanced surveillance and margin requirements under the Long Term ASM Framework effective September 17, 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must comply with applicable margin requirements and trading restrictions for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified securities will be subject to additional surveillance measures, potentially impacting liquidity and margin requirements for investors holding or trading these scrips.
