## Summary

iValue Infosolutions Limited has announced the listing of its equity shares on the exchange following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The circular outlines the lock-in details for various tranches of shares including shares held by anchor investors and promoters.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares listed/under lock-in details provided for iValue Infosolutions Limited.
- Anchor investor shares are locked-in for 30 days (up to 22nd October, 2025) and 90 days (up to 21st December, 2025).
- Public equity shares of 1,31,17,272 are not under lock-in.
- ISIN and distinctive numbers details are specified for demat form settlement.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to lock-in periods as specified in the company's prospectus and regulatory guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Listing Date / Lock-in Start Date: 23-Sep-2025
- Anchor Lock-in End Date (30 Days): 22-Oct-2025
- Anchor Lock-in End Date (90 Days): 21-Dec-2025
- Promoter/Other Lock-in End Date: 23-Mar-2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard corporate listing update with no direct market-wide systemic impact.
