## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the change in Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA) for Taurus Mutual Fund, detailing the scheme code updates for existing and migrated schemes.

## Key Points

- Details the mapping of existing BSE scheme codes to new migrated scheme codes.
- Covers various schemes under Taurus Mutual Fund including equity, direct, and normal plans.
- Affects scheme identification codes used on trading and mutual fund platforms.

## Regulatory Changes

- Administrative updates for mutual fund scheme mapping under the new RTA.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, intermediaries, and investors must update their systems and records with the new scheme codes for ongoing and future transactions.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact on order placement and scheme identification for Taurus Mutual Fund products on trading platforms.
