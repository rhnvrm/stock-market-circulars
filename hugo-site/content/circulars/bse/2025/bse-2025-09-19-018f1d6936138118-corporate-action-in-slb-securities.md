## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding corporate actions in Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) securities. The schedule outlines key dates including book closure, record dates, ex-dates, foreclosure dates, and shut periods for several companies undertaking corporate actions such as dividends, bonus issues, amalgamations, and stock splits.

## Key Points

- **Fertilizers and Chemicals Travancore Limited**: Dividend with Record Date and Ex-Date on 19-September-2025; Shut Period from 18-Sep-2025 to 19-Sep-2025.
- **Pidilite Industries Ltd.**: Bonus issue with Record Date, Ex-Date, and Foreclosure Date on 23-September-2025; Shut Period from 22-Sep-2025 to 23-Sep-2025.
- **Piramal Enterprises Limited**: Amalgamation with Record Date, Ex-Date, and Foreclosure Date on 23-September-2025; Shut Period from 22-Sep-2025 to 23-Sep-2025.
- **Sandur Manganese & Iron Ores Ltd.**: Bonus issue with Record Date, Ex-Date, and Foreclosure Date on 22-September-2025; Shut Period from 19-Sep-2025 to 22-Sep-2025.
- **Nazara Technologies Limited**: Bonus issue and Stock Split with Record Date, Ex-Date, and Foreclosure Date on 26-September-2025; Shut Period from 25-Sep-2025 to 26-Sep-2025.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational schedule update for corporate actions in SLB securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must take note of the specified record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods to ensure proper settlement and adjustments in SLB transactions.

## Important Dates

- **18-Sep-2025 to 19-Sep-2025**: Shut Period for Fertilizers and Chemicals Travancore Limited.
- **19-September-2025**: Record Date & Ex-Date for Fertilizers and Chemicals Travancore Limited.
- **19-Sep-2025 to 22-Sep-2025**: Shut Period for Sandur Manganese & Iron Ores Ltd.
- **22-September-2025**: Record Date, Ex-Date & Foreclosure Date for Sandur Manganese & Iron Ores Ltd.
- **22-Sep-2025 to 23-Sep-2025**: Shut Period for Pidilite Industries Ltd. and Piramal Enterprises Limited.
- **23-September-2025**: Record Date, Ex-Date & Foreclosure Date for Pidilite Industries Ltd. and Piramal Enterprises Limited.
- **25-Sep-2025 to 26-Sep-2025**: Shut Period for Nazara Technologies Limited.
- **26-September-2025**: Record Date, Ex-Date & Foreclosure Date for Nazara Technologies Limited.

## Impact Assessment

Affects market participants trading in the SLB segment for the specified scrips, requiring adherence to revised settlement schedules and corporate action adjustments.
