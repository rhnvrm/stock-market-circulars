## Summary

The IPO Committee of Tata Capital Limited, in consultation with book running lead managers, finalized the allocation of 14,23,87,284 Equity Shares to Anchor Investors at an allocation price of ₹ 326 per Equity Share (including a share premium of ₹ 316 per Equity Share).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Tata Capital Limited
- Offer Type: Initial Public Offering (IPO) of equity shares of face value ₹ 10 each
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: ₹ 326 per Equity Share
- Total Equity Shares Allocated to Anchor Investors: 14,23,87,284 Equity Shares
- Major Anchor Investors include Life Insurance Corporation of India, ICICI Prudential Mutual Fund, and HDFC Trustee Company Ltd.
