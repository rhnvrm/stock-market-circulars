## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the shifting and retention of various securities in the Trade-to-Trade ("T", "XT", "MT", or "TS") segments. Effective September 18, 2025, specified securities will be moved from the rolling segment to the trade-for-trade segment with a price band of 5% or lower, while others are retained in the segment.

## Key Points

- Multiple securities across normal and SME segments are shifted to the Trade-to-Trade (T, XT, TS) groups.
- Price bands for affected securities are set at 5% or lower.
- Annexure-I outlines newly included securities shifting from the rolling segment to the Trade-to-Trade segment.
- Annexure-II lists securities retained in the Trade-to-Trade segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Shift of specified equities and SME securities to Trade-to-Trade settlement.
- Imposition of a 5% price band on the affected scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure compliance with Trade-to-Trade settlement norms and restricted price bands for the listed scrips starting from the effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity and trading mechanisms for the listed securities will be restricted due to compulsory delivery-based trading and 5% price bands.
