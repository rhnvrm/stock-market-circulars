## Summary

 Gujarat Peanut and Agri Products Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern under Regulation 31(1)(b) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, detailing total paid-up equity shares and promoter holdings.

## Key Points

- Total paid-up equity shares: 10,262,000
- Promoter & Promoter Group holding: 7,286,000 shares (71.00%)
- Public shareholding: 2,976,000 shares (29.00%)
- Total number of shareholders: 752

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance under Regulation 31(1)(b) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- Submission of periodic shareholding pattern disclosures by listed entities.

## Important Dates

- Date of report / filing: 01-10-2025

## Impact Assessment

- Standard corporate compliance update with no direct market-wide trading or operational impact.
