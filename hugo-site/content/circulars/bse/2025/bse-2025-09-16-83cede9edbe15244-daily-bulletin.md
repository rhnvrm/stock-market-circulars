## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 116/2025-2026 details upcoming book closures, record dates, and payment dates for corporate actions including dividends, interest payments on non-convertible debentures (NCDs), and NCD redemptions scheduled by various listed companies for September, October, November, and December 2025.

## Key Points

- Lists corporate actions including book closure and record dates for companies like 360 One Portfolio Managers Limited, Adani Enterprises Ltd., Amba Enterprises Ltd., Bajaj Holdings & Investment Limited, Dev Information Technology Limited, and ECL Finance Limited.
- Details interim and final dividend announcements with respective per-share amounts.
- Specifies dates for interest payments and partial/full redemptions of Privately Placed and Secured Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure timely adherence to record dates and proper execution of interest disbursements and dividend distributions to eligible security holders.

## Important Dates

- Bulletin Date: 16/09/2025
- Record Dates span from 22/09/2025 through 08/12/2025 depending on the specific company and security.

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification for market participants, investors, and depositories to track corporate actions, entitlement dates, and cash flow schedules.
