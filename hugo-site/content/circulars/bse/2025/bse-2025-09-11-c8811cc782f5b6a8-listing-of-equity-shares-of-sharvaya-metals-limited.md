## Summary

Sharvaya Metals Limited has announced the listing of its equity shares following its IPO on the BSE, specifying various lock-in categories, distinctive numbers, and quantities totaling 10,030,000 shares.

## Key Points

- Total Securities: 10,030,000 equity shares listed.
- Includes fully paid shares, offer-for-sale (OFS) shares, and anchor lock-in periods ranging across multiple dates up to September 2028.
- All securities are in dematerialized (DEMAT) form.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to the specified lock-in periods and release dates for promoters and investors as per the IPO terms.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in expiry and effective dates range from September 2025 through September 2028.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading and settlement of Sharvaya Metals Limited shares on the exchange while enforcing regulatory lock-ins for specified holdings.
