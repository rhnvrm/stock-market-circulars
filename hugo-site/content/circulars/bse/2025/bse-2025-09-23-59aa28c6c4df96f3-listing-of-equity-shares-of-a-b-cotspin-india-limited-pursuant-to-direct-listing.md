## Summary

Stock exchange BSE has announced the listing and trading of equity shares of A B Cotspin India Limited on the BSE Mainboard platform, following its direct listing and migration from the Emerge Platform of the National Stock Exchange (NSE).

## Key Points

- Company Name: A B Cotspin India Limited
- Corporate action: Migration and listing from NSE SME (Emerge) platform to BSE Mainboard platform.
- Trading Availability: Equity shares are available for trading on the BSE Mainboard.
- Scrip Code: 544522

## Regulatory Changes

- Transition of platform category from SME exchange segment to Mainboard segment in accordance with applicable regulatory frameworks for migration.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entity must comply with continuous listing obligations, disclosure norms, and corporate governance standards applicable to Mainboard listed companies on BSE.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 2025 (Reference ID: 20250923-59)

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances stock liquidity, broadens investor participation, and transitions the company from SME to Mainboard status, raising regulatory disclosure and compliance visibility.
