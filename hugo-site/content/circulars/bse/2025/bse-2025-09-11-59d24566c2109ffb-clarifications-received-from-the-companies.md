## Summary

BSE Limited sought clarifications from three listed companies—Dilip Buildcon Limited, Elecon Engineering Company Limited, and Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd—regarding a significant increase in the volume of their stocks across exchanges on September 11, 2025. All three companies responded confirming that they are in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and stated that there are no material events, impending announcements, or price-sensitive information pending dissemination that would explain the volume movement.

## Key Points

- Dilip Buildcon Limited confirmed prompt disclosure of all material events under Regulation 30 and stated it has no comments on the stock volume increase.
- Elecon Engineering Company Limited confirmed compliance with SEBI LODR regulations and clarified that the company is in no way connected with the recent price/volume movement.
- Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd noted its ongoing compliance with disclosure requirements and regular reporting of events having a bearing on operations or performance.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard surveillance inquiries under SEBI LODR framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must continue to promptly disclose any material events, information, or price-sensitive developments to the stock exchanges in accordance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Inquiry Date: September 11, 2025
- Response Date: September 11, 2025

## Impact Assessment

The clarifications indicate normal market activity with no undisclosed fundamental developments or material corporate actions pending at the evaluated companies.
