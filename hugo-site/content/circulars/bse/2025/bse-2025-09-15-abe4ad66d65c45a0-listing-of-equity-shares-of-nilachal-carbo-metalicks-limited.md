## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Nilachal Carbo Metalicks Limited, detailing the number of securities, distinctive number ranges, lock-in status, and lock-in expiry dates.

## Key Points

- Total securities issued by the company: 24,927,000 equity shares.
- 4,985,400 shares under lock-in up to 16.09.2028.
- 6,670,770 shares under lock-in up to 16.09.2027.
- 6,670,820 shares under lock-in up to 16.09.2026.
- 6,600,000 fully paid shares without lock-in restrictions.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to the specified lock-in periods and depository procedures for demat securities.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in expiry dates: 16.09.2026, 16.09.2027, and 16.09.2028.

## Impact Assessment

Standard listing update with no broad market impact.
