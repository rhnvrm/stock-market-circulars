## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the launch and availability of new fund offer (NFO) schemes of The Wealth Company Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform. The circular provides a complete list of available liquid fund schemes along with their respective ISINs and tentative allotment dates.

## Key Points

- Platform: BSE StAR MF Platform
- Fund House: The Wealth Company Mutual Fund
- Schemes Introduced: Various regular and direct plans of The Wealth Company Liquid Fund
- Tentative Allotment Date: October 14, 2025

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and distributors must ensure orders are placed in accordance with the specified ISINs and scheme categories within the operational timelines of the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Tentative Allotment Date: 2025-10-14

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates seamless subscription and transactions in the newly launched liquid fund schemes for investors and members through the BSE StAR MF platform.
