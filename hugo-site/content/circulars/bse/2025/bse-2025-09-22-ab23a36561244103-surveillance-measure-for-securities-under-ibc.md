## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding surveillance measures for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective September 23, 2025. The circular details securities shortlisted under Stage 0 for receipt of corporate announcements or disclosures, along with a consolidated list of securities under various stages of the IRP/IBC framework.

## Key Points

- Lists securities shortlisted w.r.t. receipt of corporate announcements/disclosures under Stage 0 w.e.f. September 23, 2025 (e.g., Reliance Home Finance Ltd).
- Provides a consolidated list of securities under the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per IBC across different stages.
- Outlines framework updates including T+0 scrips and recommencement scrips where applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures and stage transitions for companies undergoing insolvency resolution processes under the IBC framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and applicable trading restrictions for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2025
- Circular Date: September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in affected securities is subject to enhanced surveillance measures, margin requirements, or monitoring as stipulated under the IBC framework.
