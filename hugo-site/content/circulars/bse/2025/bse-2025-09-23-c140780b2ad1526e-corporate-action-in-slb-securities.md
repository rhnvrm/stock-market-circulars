## Summary

This BSE circular provides details regarding corporate actions (dividends), book closure dates, ex-dates, and shut periods for securities participating in the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) mechanism.

## Key Points

- **India Glycols Ltd**: Dividend with Ex-Date and Book Closure/Record Date on 23-September-2025 (Shut Period: 22-Sep-2025 to 23-Sep-2025).
- **Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd**: Dividend with Ex-Date and Book Closure/Record Date on 03-October-2025 (Shut Period: 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025).
- **Tata Consultancy Services Ltd.**: Dividend with Ex-Date and Book Closure/Record Date on 15-October-2025 (Shut Period: 14-Oct-2025 to 15-Oct-2025).

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate action schedule update.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the specified ex-dates, record dates, and shut periods for proper settlement and SLB operations.

## Important Dates

- **23-September-2025**: India Glycols Ltd Ex-Date & Record Date
- **03-October-2025**: Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd Ex-Date & Record Date
- **15-October-2025**: Tata Consultancy Services Ltd. Ex-Date & Record Date

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to routine operational adjustments for lending and borrowing positions around the respective corporate action dates.
