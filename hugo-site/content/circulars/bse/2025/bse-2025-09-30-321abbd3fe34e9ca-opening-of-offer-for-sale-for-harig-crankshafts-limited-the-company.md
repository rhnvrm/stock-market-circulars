## Summary

Palika Towns LLP, the promoter of Harig Crankshafts Limited, has issued a notice for an Offer for Sale (OFS) of 5,25,000 equity shares (representing 5% of the company's share capital) through the stock exchange mechanism to comply with Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) norms.

## Key Points

- **Seller:** Palika Towns LLP (Promoter)
- **Company:** Harig Crankshafts Limited (Scrip Code: 500178, ISIN: INE905C01034)
- **Number of Shares Offered:** 5,25,000 Equity Shares (5% of share capital)
- **Purpose:** To comply with Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) norms under Rule 19A(5) of Securities Contracts (Regulation) Rules, 1957 to reach 10% public shareholding.
- **Clearing Member:** NSE Clearing Limited

## Regulatory Changes

- Undertaken pursuant to SEBI OFS circulars and amendments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) requirements.

## Important Dates

- **Non-Retail Investors Bidding:** October 01, 2025 ("T") from 9:15 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.
- **Retail Investors and Carry Forward Bidding:** October 03, 2025 ("T+1")

## Impact Assessment

- Adjusts promoter holding and increases public shareholding in Harig Crankshafts Limited to meet regulatory standards.
