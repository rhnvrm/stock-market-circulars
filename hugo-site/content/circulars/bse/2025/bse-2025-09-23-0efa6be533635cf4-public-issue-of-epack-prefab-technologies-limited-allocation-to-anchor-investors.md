## Summary

Epack Prefab Technologies Limited has finalized the allocation of 74,11,764 equity shares to anchor investors at an allocation price of Rs. 204 per equity share (including a share premium of Rs. 202 per share) as part of its public issue of equity shares.

## Key Points

- Company: Epack Prefab Technologies Limited
- Total Equity Shares Allocated to Anchor Investors: 74,11,764 shares
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: Rs. 204 per share (face value Rs. 2 each)
- Key Anchor Investors include various funds managed by Whiteoak Capital, 360 One Flexicap Fund, AL Mehwar Commercial Investments L.L.C., and LC Pharos Multi Strategy Fund Vcc.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with standard listing and public issue guidelines stipulated by BSE and NSE.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allocation / Notice: September 23, 2025

Indicates institutional demand and sets the stage for the public issue subscription phase.
