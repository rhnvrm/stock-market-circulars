## Summary

Om Freight Forwarders Limited has announced the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors ahead of its initial public offering (IPO). The company raised ₹15.89 crore by allocating 11,77,710 equity shares to anchor investors at the upper price band of ₹135 per share.

## Key Points

- Total amount raised from anchor investors: ₹15.89 crore.
- Total equity shares allocated: 11,77,710 shares.
- Issue price / upper price band: ₹135 per equity share.
- Key anchor investors include Craft Emerging Market Fund (Citadel Capital Fund), Rajasthan Global Securities, and Abundantia Capital VCC.

Compliance with SEBI ICDR Regulations regarding anchor investor lock-in periods (50% of shares locked for 30 days and the remaining 50% locked for 90 days).
