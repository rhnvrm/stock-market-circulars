## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of new equity securities of Epuja Spiritech Limited issued to various allottees, detailing the number of shares, post-issue holding, lock-in periods, and distinctive numbers.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Epuja Spiritech Limited
- Allotment of new equity shares to 17 allottees including Priyadarshi Srivastav, Mohanlal Ramavtar Mandhana, Hitesh Oberoi, and others.
- Lock-in periods specified up to 30/03/2026 and 01/04/2026 for respective allottees.
- Distinctive numbers ranging from 81579040 to 82817278.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with lock-in requirements as specified for the respective allottees until the completion of the lock-in period.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in Expiry Dates: 30/03/2026 and 01/04/2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine increase in paid-up capital and listing of shares for trading following regulatory guidelines.
