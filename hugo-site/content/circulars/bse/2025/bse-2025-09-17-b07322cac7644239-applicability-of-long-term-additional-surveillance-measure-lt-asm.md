## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the applicability and updates to the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective September 18, 2025, several securities have been shortlisted, moved to higher/lower stages, or removed from the framework.

## Key Points

- Multiple securities shortlisted under the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. September 18, 2025 (Annexure I, Part A).
- Securities moving to higher Stage ASM include Vintron Informatics Ltd (Annexure I, Part B).
- Securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework include Hilltone Software and Gases Ltd, Kavveri Defence & Wireless Technologies Ltd, and Maitri Enterprises Ltd (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities currently under various ASM stages provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of heightened surveillance mechanisms including potential margin requirements or trading restrictions as mandated by SEBI and exchange guidelines for securities under the LT-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, trading members, and investors must take note of the applicable ASM stages for the listed securities and adjust trading and margining strategies accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities may experience modified margin requirements or surveillance controls, impacting liquidity and volatility for the short-listed scrips.
