## Summary

BSE has issued a procedural user manual regarding the mandatory submission of the Margin Trading Compliance Certificate through the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS). All members who have obtained approval for providing the Margin Trading Facility (MTF) from the Exchange must submit the certificate, regardless of whether they have commenced operations.

## Key Points

- Members must log in to the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS) and navigate to Margin Trading Module > MT Compliance Certificate.
- Members offering MTF select "Yes"; those not offering it select "No".
- Scanned copy of the certificate must be uploaded in PDF format using the naming convention `ClgNo_MTFCERT.pdf` (e.g., `1232_MTFCERT.pdf`).
- The uploaded document must be digitally signed or physically signed and stamped by the Compliance Officer on the member's letterhead.
- Mandatory undertaking checkbox must be ticked before final submission.

## Regulatory Changes

Mandatory reporting requirement for all members approved for Margin Trading Facility to confirm compliance status via BEFS.

## Compliance Requirements

- All members with BSE approval for MTF must submit the certificate mandatorily.
- Members approved for MTF but not yet commenced operations must submit a certificate signed by the Compliance Officer stating the same.
- Submission is only complete upon receiving an acknowledgment email from the Exchange through BEFS.
- Physical submissions or emails sent directly will not be considered valid.

## Important Dates

- Applicable for the half-year ended September 30, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

Trading members registered for MTF must complete the electronic filing via BEFS to avoid regulatory non-compliance and ensure adherence to BSE reporting standards.
