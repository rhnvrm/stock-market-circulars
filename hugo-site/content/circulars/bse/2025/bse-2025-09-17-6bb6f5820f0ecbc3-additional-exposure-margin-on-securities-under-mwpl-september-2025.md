## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the application of Additional Exposure Margin on securities under the Market-Wide Position Limit (MWPL) framework.

## Key Points

- Additional exposure margins are applied to securities exceeding specified MWPL thresholds.
- Security affected: RBL BANK LIMITED (Scrip Code: 540065, ISIN: INE976G01028).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of additional margin requirements for derivative segment participants trading in the specified security.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure adequate margin maintenance for positions held in the impacted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective date: September 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased capital requirements for market participants maintaining open positions in RBL BANK LIMITED derivatives.
