## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a notification regarding the movement of securities into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.

## Key Points

- Polo Hotels Ltd (Security Code: 526687, ISIN: INE084C01012) moved to GSM Stage I.
- Worldwide Aluminium Ltd (Security Code: 526525, ISIN: INE550D01010) moved to GSM Stage II.
- Omkar Pharmachem Ltd (Security Code: 526167/532167, ISIN: INE501C01015) moved to GSM Stage II.
- Garware Synthetics Ltd (Security Code: 514400, ISIN: INE340D01016) moved to GSM Stage III.
- Prashant India Ltd (Security Code: 519014, ISIN: INE100E01012) moved to GSM Stage III.
- Artson Ltd (Security Code: 522134, ISIN: INE133D01023) moved to GSM Stage IV.

## Regulatory Changes

Applicable surveillance actions corresponding to the respective GSM stages are imposed on the listed securities to safeguard market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and ensure adherence to applicable trading restrictions and margin requirements for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

Effective immediately as per the circular release date.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, including possible trading restrictions, higher margin requirements, or transfer to trade-for-trade segment depending on the assigned GSM stage.
