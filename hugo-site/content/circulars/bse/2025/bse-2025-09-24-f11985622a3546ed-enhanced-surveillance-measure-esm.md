## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, listing securities that are shortlisted, moving to higher stages, or continuing in the framework effective September 25, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been freshly shortlisted in the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Specific securities are moving to higher stages within the ESM framework.
- A consolidated list of securities under the ESM framework is provided for market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework to monitor volatility and protect investors in designated scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the ESM stages and associated trading restrictions for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the shortlisted securities may be subject to additional surveillance margins or trading safeguards as mandated by the ESM framework.
