## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted, moved across stages, or exiting the framework effective September 12, 2025.

## Key Points

- New securities shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework (Part A) effective September 12, 2025.
- Securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework (Annexure II), such as Wardwizard Foods and Beverages Ltd.
- Consolidated list of securities under various ASM stages provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance and margin requirements for specified scrips under the LT-ASM framework to safeguard market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply applicable surveillance margins and check trading restrictions for the shortlisted scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affects liquidity and margin requirements for the shortlisted securities trading on the exchange.
