## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective September 24, 2025. The circular provides lists of securities newly shortlisted under the framework, securities moving across stages, and those moving out of the LT-ASM framework.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted and added to the Long Term ASM Framework (Annexure I, Part A) w.e.f. September 24, 2025.
- Lists for securities moving to higher or lower stages of ASM are also maintained.
- Specific securities are moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework due to inclusion in other frameworks such as Trade for Trade, GSM, ESM, or IBC (Annexure II).
- A comprehensive consolidated list of securities under various ASM stages is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance measures like LT-ASM to monitor price volatility and safeguard investors in specific scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply the requisite surveillance margins and trading restrictions as mandated by the exchange for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements (often 100% applicable margin) for securities under LT-ASM, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
