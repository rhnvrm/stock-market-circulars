## Summary

Adani Green Energy Limited, Adani Total Gas Limited, and Adani Power submitted formal clarifications to BSE regarding recent volume movements in their equity shares, confirming that the activity is entirely market-driven and independent of any company action or undisclosed material information.

## Key Points

- BSE sought clarification from Adani Group entities regarding unusual volume/price movements under surveillance guidelines.
- Companies confirmed that the volume fluctuations are purely due to prevailing market conditions.
- Management of the respective companies declared no knowledge or control over the reasons for increased trading volumes.
- Reiteration of compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 regarding disclosure obligations.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Continued adherence to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for prompt disclosure of price-sensitive information.

## Important Dates

- Date of Letters: September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No material impact on operations, financials, or corporate governance of the listed entities.
