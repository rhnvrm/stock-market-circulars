## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted, moving out, and consolidated lists effective from September 26, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted under the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective September 26, 2025.
- Securities moving out of the ST-ASM framework due to various criteria or inclusion in other frameworks have been identified.
- A consolidated list of securities under the ST-ASM framework is maintained for market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of ST-ASM framework updates as per exchange surveillance guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance applicability and ensure compliance with trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified securities will be subject to surveillance margins and applicable controls.
