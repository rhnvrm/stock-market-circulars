## Summary

Pace Digitek Limited has announced the finalization of the allocation of 1,11,93,807 equity shares to Anchor Investors at an anchor investor allocation price of ₹219 per equity share (including a share premium of ₹217 per equity share) for its public issue.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pace Digitek Limited
- Issue Details: Public issue of equity shares of face value ₹2 each.
- Total Anchor Shares Allocated: 1,11,93,807 Equity Shares.
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: ₹219 per Equity Share (Face Value ₹2 + Share Premium ₹217).
- Board Meeting Date: September 25, 2025.
