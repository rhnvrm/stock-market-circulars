## Summary

BSE has announced the application, revision, and removal of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective October 6, 2025. The circular details shortlists in Part A, upward stage shifts in Part B, downward stage shifts in Part C, and exclusions in Annexure II.

## Key Points

- **Part A:** New securities shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. October 06, 2025 (e.g., Karnavati Finance Ltd, Nyssa Corporation Ltd, Shiva Granito Export Ltd, Vineet Laboratories Ltd).
- **Part B:** Securities continuing in the framework but moving to higher Stage ASM (e.g., Stallion India Fluorochemicals Ltd moved to Stage II).
- **Part C:** Securities continuing in the framework but moving to lower Stage ASM (e.g., Gujarat Hy-Spin Ltd, Incon Engineers Ltd, Marsons Ltd).
- **Annexure II:** List of 27 securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. October 06, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance measures including margin requirements and price bands as mandated under the Long Term ASM Framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages, margins, and trading restrictions for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 6, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience changes in trading margins, price bands, or complete removal of surveillance restrictions, impacting liquidity and trading behavior.
