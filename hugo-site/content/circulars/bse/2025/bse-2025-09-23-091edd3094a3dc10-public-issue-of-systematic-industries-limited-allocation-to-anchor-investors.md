## Summary

Systematic Industries Limited has finalized the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors ahead of its public issue. A total of 16,87,800 equity shares were allocated to anchor investors at a price of ₹195 per equity share, raising approximately ₹32.91 crore.

## Key Points

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 23, 2025
- Total Shares Subscribed: 16,87,800 equity shares
- Allocation Price: ₹195 per share
- Total Amount Raised: ~₹32.91 crore
- Anchor portion accounted for approximately 28.47% of the total issue size of 59,28,000 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Lock-in requirements apply to the anchor investors' shares:
- 50% of the shares allocated to anchor investors are locked in for 30 days (ending October 29, 2025).
- The remaining 50% are locked in for 90 days (ending December 28, 2025).

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 23, 2025
- Issue Opening Date: September 24, 2025
- Issue Closing Date: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

The successful anchor book allocation indicates robust institutional participation, providing positive momentum and price discovery validation for Systematic Industries Limited's SME IPO on the BSE platform.
