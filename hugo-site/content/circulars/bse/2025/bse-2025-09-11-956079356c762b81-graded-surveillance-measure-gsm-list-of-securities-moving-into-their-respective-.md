## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a circular detailing the list of securities moving into respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages. The update includes specific scrips placed under stages II and III of the surveillance framework.

## Key Points

- Futuristic Securities Ltd (Security Code: 523113, ISIN: INE425D01015) has moved to GSM Stage II.
- High Street Filatex Ltd (Security Code: 531301, ISIN: INE319M01011) has moved to GSM Stage III.
- Space Incubatrics Technologies Ltd (Security Code: 541890, ISIN: INE797Z01010) has moved to GSM Stage III.
- Provisions are outlined for securities moving lower in the GSM framework due to inclusion in ESM or IBC frameworks.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of heightened surveillance mechanisms (GSM Stages II and III) on specified listed entities to safeguard market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must adhere to the trading restrictions, additional margin requirements, and settlement conditions applicable to securities under the respective GSM stages.

## Important Dates

Effective immediately as per the circular release date.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities are likely to be restricted due to the tighter surveillance norms and increased margin requirements.
