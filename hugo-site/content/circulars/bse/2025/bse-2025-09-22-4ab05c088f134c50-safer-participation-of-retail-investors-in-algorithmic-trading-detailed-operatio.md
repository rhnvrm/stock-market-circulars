## Summary

BSE has released detailed operational modalities and an undertaking framework for algo providers enabling safer participation of retail investors in algorithmic trading.

## Key Points

- Algo providers must execute undertakings on stamp paper and get them duly notarized.
- Providers must comply with regulatory requirements laid down by BSE and SEBI.
- Products and Risk Management Systems must adhere to standards formulated by the Broker's Industry Standards Forum.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formalization of the empanelment process and undertakings for algorithm providers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Algo providers need to submit the prescribed undertaking and ensure their products comply with Exchange and SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular guidelines.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances accountability and safety for retail participants engaging in algorithmic trading through empanelled providers.
