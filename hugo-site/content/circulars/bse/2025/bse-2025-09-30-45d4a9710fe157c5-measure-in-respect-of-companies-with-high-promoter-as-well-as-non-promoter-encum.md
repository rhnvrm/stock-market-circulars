## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the measures applicable to companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011, effective October 01, 2025. The notification provides lists of securities shortlisted, moving out of, and the consolidated list under the framework.

## Key Points

- **Annexure I:** No new securities were shortlisted under the measure with high promoter/non-promoter encumbrance effective October 01, 2025.
- **Annexure II:** Tourism Finance Corporation of India Ltd (Scrip Code: 526650, ISIN: INE305A01023) is moving out of the framework.
- **Annexure III:** Provides the consolidated list of securities under the framework including DJS Stock & Shares Ltd, Future Market Networks Ltd, and Thyrocare Technologies Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of surveillance and monitoring measures for companies with high share encumbrance levels under SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the revised status of securities under the high encumbrance framework effective from October 01, 2025.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced transparency and monitoring for investors regarding encumbered promoter shares, impacting trading and risk management for listed entities meeting the criteria.
