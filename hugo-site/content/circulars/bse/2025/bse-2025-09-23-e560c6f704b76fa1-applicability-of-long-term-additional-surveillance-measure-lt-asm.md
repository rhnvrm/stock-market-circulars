## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, listing securities shortlisted for the framework, securities moving out, and consolidated listings effective from September 24, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities are shortlisted and added to the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. September 24, 2025, including Alstone Textiles (India) Ltd, Bijoy Hans Ltd, and others.
- Securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework include Emmforce Autotech Ltd, Gujarat Investa Ltd, and IEL Ltd.
- A consolidated list of securities under various ASM stages is provided for market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measures to designated scrips to deter excessive volatility and speculation.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance measures, higher margin requirements, or trading restrictions on the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, potentially impacting liquidity and requiring higher margin deposits for market participants.
