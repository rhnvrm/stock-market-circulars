## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the revision in market lot sizes of derivatives contracts on individual stocks and indices, detailing the updated lot specifications for various scrips.

## Key Points

- Revised market lot sizes are provided for numerous individual stock derivatives (e.g., APL Apollo Tubes, ABB Ltd, Adani Enterprises).
- Updated market lot sizes are also specified for index derivatives including BSE SENSEX, BSE BANKEX, and BSE SENSEX 50.
- Trading members and participants must take note of the revised lot sizes for future contract months.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of market lot parameters for derivative contracts on individual stocks and indices in accordance with exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must update their trading and risk management systems to reflect the revised market lot sizes.

## Important Dates

- Effective date aligns with the applicable derivative expiry cycles as notified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

- Market participants holding or trading derivatives in the listed scrips and indices will need to adjust order quantities and margin calculations according to the new lot sizes.
