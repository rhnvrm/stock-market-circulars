## Summary

Multiple listed companies have submitted formal clarifications to BSE Limited and the National Stock Exchange of India in response to exchange inquiries regarding sudden increases in trading volume or price movements of their respective securities. The companies have confirmed that all material information has been duly disclosed in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- **Sandhar Technologies Limited**: Clarified that the recent increase in trading volume is entirely market-driven with no unpublished price-sensitive information pending.
- **Vaghani Techno-Build Limited**: Confirmed that price movements are based on external factors and investor sentiment beyond the company's control.
- **ASK Automotive Limited**: Responded to exchange queries regarding recent volume surges in compliance with SEBI LODR regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard execution of surveillance and disclosure requirements under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must continue to promptly disclose any material events or information that may affect price or volume behavior.

## Important Dates

- **Clarification Date**: September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Neutral impact on markets as these are routine stock exchange surveillance clarifications confirming no hidden corporate developments.
