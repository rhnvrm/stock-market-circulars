## Summary

Jaro Institute of Technology Limited has finalized the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors as part of its initial public offering (IPO) under the book building process.

## Key Points

- Anchor investor allocation details finalized prior to the opening of the public issue.
- Allocation made to institutional and anchor investors in accordance with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.
- Details include number of shares allocated, price per share, and total amount raised from anchor investors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI ICDR Regulations regarding anchor investor allocation in public issues.

## Compliance Requirements

- Anchor investors are required to pay any differential amount if the final issue price is higher than the anchor allocation price.
- Lock-in periods apply to shares allotted to anchor investors as per regulatory guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Allocation Date: September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Anchor investor participation provides initial market momentum and signals institutional confidence in the public issue.
