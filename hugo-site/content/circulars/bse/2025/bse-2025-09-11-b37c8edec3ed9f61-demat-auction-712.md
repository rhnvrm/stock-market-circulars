BSE has published the demat auction list for Settlement No. 712 dated September 11, 2025. The circular lists securities subject to auction settlement due to delivery shortfalls in the market.

- Auction Date: September 11, 2025
- Covers various securities requiring auction due to settlement delivery failures.
