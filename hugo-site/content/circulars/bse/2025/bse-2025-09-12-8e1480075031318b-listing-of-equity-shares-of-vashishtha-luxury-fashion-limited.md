## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Vashishtha Luxury Fashion Limited pursuant to its initial public offering (IPO), detailing the lock-in periods, distinctive numbers, and quantities of securities.

## Key Points

- Total securities listed include both fully paid lock-in shares and free shares under the IPO.
- Lock-in dates range from September 2025 up to September 2028 depending on the tranche of shares.
- Distinctive numbers and physical/demat status have been specified for all listed tranches.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with exchange listing norms and depository guidelines regarding demat shares and lock-in restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in Start Date: 03/09/2025
- Lock-in Upto Dates: 12/09/2026, 12/09/2027, and 12/09/2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing event with no broader market-wide impact.
