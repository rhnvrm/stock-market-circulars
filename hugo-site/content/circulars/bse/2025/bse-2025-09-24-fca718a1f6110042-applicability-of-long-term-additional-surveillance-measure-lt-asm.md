## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The circular outlines securities shortlisted in the framework, securities moving to higher or lower stages, and the consolidated list of securities under surveillance effective September 25, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Part A:** Lists securities newly shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework (e.g., ACS Technologies Ltd, Brand Concepts Ltd, Family Care Hospitals Ltd, Incap Ltd, Padmanabh Industries Ltd, Risa International Ltd).
- **Part B:** Lists securities continuing in the framework but moving to a higher Stage (e.g., Incon Engineers Ltd moving to Stage II).
- **Part C & D:** Lists securities moving to lower stages or direct stage IV, if applicable.
- **Annexure III:** Provides the consolidated list of securities under the LT-ASM framework across various stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including appropriate margin requirements and trading restrictions on shortlisted securities to safeguard market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply the stipulated surveillance margins and monitor trading activities in the specified securities as per the exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified scrips will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, potentially impacting liquidity and margin requirements for investors holding or trading these securities.
