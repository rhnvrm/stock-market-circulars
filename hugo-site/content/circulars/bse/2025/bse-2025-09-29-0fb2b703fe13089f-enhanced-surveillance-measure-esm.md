## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the list of securities newly shortlisted, moved to higher stages, or continuing under the framework effective September 30, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been freshly shortlisted under the ESM framework (Annexure I, Part A).
- Securities such as Shikhar Consultants Ltd are moved to respective higher stages within the ESM framework.
- A comprehensive consolidated list of securities under various ESM stages is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures in accordance with regulatory guidelines to safeguard market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the ESM stages and ensure compliance with the applicable trading restrictions and margin requirements for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the shortlisted securities will be subject to enhanced monitoring and specific trading restrictions (such as applicable margins and trading frequency limits) depending on their assigned ESM stage.
