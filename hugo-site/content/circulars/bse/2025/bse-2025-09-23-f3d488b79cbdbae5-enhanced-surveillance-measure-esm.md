## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted, securities moving to higher stages, and the consolidated list of securities under the ESM framework effective September 24, 2025.

## Key Points

- New securities shortlisted in the ESM Framework effective September 24, 2025 (e.g., Anlon Healthcare Ltd, Emmforce Autotech Ltd, Gujarat Investa Ltd, IEL Ltd, Intense Technologies Ltd, Synthiko Foils Ltd).
- Securities continuing in the ESM framework but moving to respective higher stages (e.g., Bisil Plast Ltd, India Lease Development Ltd, Kaya Ltd, Midwest Gold Ltd, Precision Electronics Ltd, Saptak Chem And Business Ltd, Sattrix Information Security Ltd, Senthil Infotek Ltd, Trustedge Capital Ltd).
- Comprehensive consolidated list of securities under various stages of the ESM framework provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures in accordance with regulatory guidelines for Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the ESM stages and associated trading restrictions for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance monitoring and applicable margin requirements or trading restrictions corresponding to their assigned ESM stage.
