## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the movement of select securities into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, aimed at enhancing market integrity and protecting investors.

## Key Points

- Bijoy Hans Ltd (Security Code: 524723, ISIN: INE491D01017) has moved to GSM Stage I.
- Integrated Capital Services Ltd (Security Code: 539149, ISIN: INE682B01023) has moved to GSM Stage I.
- Polo Hotels Ltd (Security Code: 526687, ISIN: INE084C01012) has moved to GSM Stage II.

## Regulatory Changes

Applicable surveillance actions corresponding to GSM Stages I and II will be enforced on the listed securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are required to take note of the surveillance measures and apply necessary trading restrictions as mandated by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced monitoring and surveillance controls, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volumes.
