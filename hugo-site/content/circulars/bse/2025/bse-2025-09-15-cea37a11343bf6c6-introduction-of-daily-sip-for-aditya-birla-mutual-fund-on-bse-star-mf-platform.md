## Summary

Aditya Birla Sun Life AMC Limited has issued a notice-cum-addendum to introduce the Daily Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) frequency for select schemes—Aditya Birla Sun Life Gold Fund and Aditya Birla Sun Life Silver ETF Fund of Fund—effective September 15, 2025.

## Key Points

- Daily SIP frequency added to Aditya Birla Sun Life Gold Fund and Aditya Birla Sun Life Silver ETF Fund of Fund.
- Effective date of implementation: Monday, September 15, 2025.
- All other features, terms, and conditions of the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational update regarding mutual fund scheme features.

## Compliance Requirements

Investors and distributors should note the inclusion of the new SIP frequency for ongoing transactions under the respective schemes.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 12, 2025
- Effective Date: September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides increased flexibility for investors participating in mutual fund schemes through the BSE StAR MF platform via daily systematic investment options.
