## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a list of securities moving into their respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, effective as per the regulatory framework for market surveillance.

## Key Points

- Several companies including Lcc Infotech Ltd., Manraj Housing Finance Ltd., Charms Industries Ltd., Simplex Mills Company Ltd., Dhenu Buildcon Infra Ltd., Advik Laboratories Ltd., Cistro Telelink Ltd., and Purohit Construction Limited are shifted to various GSM stages (I, III, and IV).
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower in the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the ESM Framework.
- Securities marked with ($) are moving lower in the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the IBC Framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of stricter surveillance measures and trading controls for the listed securities to safeguard market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange surveillance release.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in these securities will be subject to surveillance monitoring, additional deposits, or trading frequency restrictions depending on their assigned GSM stage.
