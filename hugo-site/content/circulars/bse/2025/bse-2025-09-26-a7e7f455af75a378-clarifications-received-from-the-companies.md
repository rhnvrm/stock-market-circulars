## Summary

BSE sought clarification from Ushakiran Finance Limited and Machino Plastics Limited under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, regarding recent movements in their share prices. Both companies responded that they have no pending price-sensitive information or announcements, attributing price movements entirely to market conditions and speculative trading.

## Key Points

- **Ushakiran Finance Limited (Scrip Code: 511507):** Clarified that there is no pending information or price-sensitive announcement; price movement is purely market-driven.
- **Machino Plastics Limited (Scrip Code: 523248):** Clarified that the price movement is due to market conditions, potential sentiment around GST 2.0 impacting automotive demand, and low floating stock.
- Both companies confirmed adherence to all compliance requirements under the Listing Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 regarding timely disclosures of material events.

- **Date of Clarification Letters:** September 26, 2025

- No operational or financial impact; standard regulatory disclosure confirming normal market behavior.
