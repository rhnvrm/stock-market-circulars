## Summary

BSE has published the demat auction list for Settlement No. 723 dated September 26, 2025. The auction includes various securities subject to auction due to delivery shortages in the normal settlement process.

## Key Points

- Settlement Number: 723
- Auction Date: September 26, 2025
- Routine auction mechanism for handling delivery failures and settlement shortages in the demat system.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and participants must comply with BSE demat auction settlement procedures.
- Ensure proper fulfillment of delivery obligations.

- Auction Date: September 26, 2025
