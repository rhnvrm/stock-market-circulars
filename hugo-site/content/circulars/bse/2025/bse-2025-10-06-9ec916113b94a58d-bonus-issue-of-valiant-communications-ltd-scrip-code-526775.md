## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the bonus issue of equity shares by Valiant Communications Ltd. (Scrip Code: 526775). The company announced a bonus issue in the ratio of 1:2, allotting 1 new fully paid-up equity share of ₹10 each for every 2 existing equity shares held by eligible shareholders as of the record date.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Valiant Communications Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 526775
- Bonus Ratio: 1:2 (1 new share for every 2 existing shares held)
- Face Value: ₹10 per share
- Total Shares Issued: Up to 38,14,030 equity shares

## Regulatory Changes

Complies with SEBI guidelines regarding corporate actions, bonus issuances, and demat credit timelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and depositories must ensure that share credits and trading permissions are updated according to the specified schedule.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: October 10, 2025
- Ex-Bonus Date: October 10, 2025
- Deemed Date of Allotment: October 13, 2025
- Commencement of Trading: October 14, 2025

## Impact Assessment

The bonus issue increases the paid-up share capital of the company from ₹7,62,80,600 to ₹11,44,20,900, adjusting stock liquidity and per-share market price accordingly.
