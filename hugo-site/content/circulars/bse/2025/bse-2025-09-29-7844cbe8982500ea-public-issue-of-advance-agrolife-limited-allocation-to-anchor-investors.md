## Summary

Advance Agrolife Limited finalized its anchor investor allocation on September 29, 2025, ahead of its public issue opening. The company successfully raised approximately ₹57.77 crore through the anchor book.

## Key Points

- Total Shares Allocated: 57,76,716 Equity Shares
- Allocation Price: ₹100 per share
- Total Amount Raised: ₹57,76,71,600 (~₹57.77 crore)
- Anchor Portion: Approximately 29.95% of the total issue size

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with SEBI ICDR Regulations regarding anchor investor lock-in periods.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Allocation Date: September 29, 2025
- Anchor Lock-in (50%): November 4, 2025 (30 days)
- Anchor Lock-in (Remaining 50%): January 4, 2026 (90 days)
