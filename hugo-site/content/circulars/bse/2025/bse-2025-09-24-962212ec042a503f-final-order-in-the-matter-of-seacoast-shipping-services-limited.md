## Summary

SEBI issued a final order under sections 11 and 11B of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 in the matter of Seacoast Shipping Services Limited and multiple connected noticees, addressing issues including the misrepresentation of financial statements across multiple financial years (FY 2020-21, FY 2021-22, FY 2022-23) and preliminary retractions of statements.

## Key Points

- Proceedings involve Seacoast Shipping Services Limited and 22 noticees including directors, promoters, and corporate entities.
- Addresses preliminary issues regarding the retraction of statements by Noticee No. 2.
- Evaluates allegations and findings concerning the misrepresentation of financial statements for FY 2020-21, FY 2021-22, and FY 2022-23.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement actions and directions issued pursuant to violations under SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees and entities involved are subject to the directions, restrictions, or penalties outlined in the final order by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Order Date / Release: 2025-07-25

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on the listed entity and associated noticees regarding compliance, governance, and trading restrictions.
