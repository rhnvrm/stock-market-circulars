## Summary

Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited announced details regarding the listing and trading approval for 25,000 partly paid-up equity shares following the receipt of first call money.

## Key Points

- Total partly paid shares for which reminder notice for first call was sent on July 01, 2025: 6,08,988.
- Total shares on which call money received as on August 16, 2025: 3,71,779.
- Total shares for which listing and trading approval was received on August 29, 2025: 3,45,923.
- Total shares to be available for trading on resumption: 25,000.
- Balance shares that could not be credited due to pledge: 856.
- Paid-up value per share available for trading: Rs. 7/-
- ISIN for shares available for trading: IN9624Z01022.
- Lock-in period: Nil.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Ensure proper credit and availability of partly paid-up shares for trading as per depository details.

## Important Dates

- Reminder Notice Date: July 01, 2025
- Call Money Received Date: August 16, 2025
- Listing and Trading Approval Date: August 29, 2025
- Circular Date: September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading for 25,000 newly listed partly paid-up shares of the company.
