## Summary

Palika Towns LLP, a promoter of Harig Crankshafts Limited, has announced an Offer for Sale (OFS) of 5,25,000 equity shares (representing 5% of the company's share capital) through the stock exchange mechanism to comply with Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) norms.

## Key Points

- Seller: Palika Towns LLP (Promoter)
- Company: Harig Crankshafts Limited (Scrip Code: 500178, ISIN: INE905C01034)
- Total Shares Offered: 5,25,000 Equity Shares (5% of paid-up capital)
- Objective: To achieve 10% public shareholding and comply with Rule 19A(5) of Securities Contracts (Regulation) Rules, 1957.
- Non-Retail Investors Bidding Date: October 01, 2025 ("T")
- Retail Investors and Carry Forward Bidding Date: October 03, 2025 ("T+1")

## Regulatory Changes

Conducted pursuant to SEBI OFS Circulars and Rule 19A(5) of Securities Contracts (Regulation) Rules, 1957 regarding Minimum Public Shareholding compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

Sellers, brokers, and prospective buyers must adhere to SEBI guidelines on Offer for Sale through stock exchange mechanisms. Non-retail investors bid on T day, while retail investors participate on T+1 day.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 30, 2025
- Non-Retail Bidding: October 01, 2025
- Retail & Carry Forward Bidding: October 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the promoter's stake dilution to meet regulatory public shareholding thresholds for Harig Crankshafts Limited.
