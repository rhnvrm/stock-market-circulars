## Summary

BSE has released the Tender Period Margin (TPM) Tracker for the Commodity Derivatives Segment (BCX) for October 2025 contracts, covering commodities such as Gold, GoldM, Silver, SilverKG, and SilverM.

## Key Points

- Outlines tender period margin rates and minimum delivery margins for October 2025 expiry contracts.
- Specifies daily progressive margin increments leading up to contract expiry.
- Accounts for market holidays during the tender periods.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants dealing in BCX commodity derivatives must maintain adequate margins as per the specified schedule during the tender periods.

## Important Dates

- Effective for October 2025 expiry contracts starting from September 26, 2025, through October 31, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and preparedness for clearing members and traders regarding margin obligations during the tender and delivery phases.
