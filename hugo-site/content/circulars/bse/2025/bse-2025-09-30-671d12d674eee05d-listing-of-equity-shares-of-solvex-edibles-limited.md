## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Solvex Edibles Limited, comprising fresh IPO shares and securities subject to various lock-in periods and distinctive number ranges.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Solvex Edibles Limited
- Total securities include free shares (fresh IPO) and fully paid-up shares under lock-in.
- Distinctive number ranges and lock-in expiry dates are specified for different tranches of shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and depositories must ensure adherence to the specified lock-in periods and distinctive number ranges for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in upto dates: 30/09/2026, 30/09/2027, and 30/09/2028 depending on the tranche.

## Impact Assessment

Standard listing update for market participants regarding newly issued equity and lock-in releases.
