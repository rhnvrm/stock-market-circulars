## Summary

Pacheli Industrial Finance Limited has officially changed its corporate name to Grand Oak Canyons Distillery Limited, effective April 3, 2025, following approval from the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (Central Processing Centre).

## Key Points

- Former Name: Pacheli Industrial Finance Limited
- Original Incorporation Name: Dhoot Finance Company Private Limited
- New Name: Grand Oak Canyons Distillery Limited
- Corporate Identification Number (CIN): L74110MH1985PLC037772
- Effective Date: April 3, 2025

## Regulatory Changes

- The official company name has been updated in the Registrar of Companies records pursuant to Rule 29 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014.

## Compliance Requirements

- In accordance with Section 12 of the Companies Act, the company is required to display its former name alongside its new name for a period of two years at all required places.

## Important Dates

- Certificate Issue Date: April 3, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- This is a administrative change of name and does not affect the rights, liabilities, or ongoing operations of stakeholders.
