## Summary

Karbonsteel Engineering Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern pursuant to Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, following the post-issue of its Initial Public Offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Filing corresponds to Regulation 31(1)(a) post-IPO issue status as of September 15, 2025.
- Declares details regarding promoter and public non-promoter shareholding, locked-in shares, and nil partly paid-up shares, warrants, or depository receipts.
- Provides Table I summary statement holding of specified securities.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard disclosure pursuant to listing requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entity must ensure timely and accurate submission of shareholding patterns under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Informational filing providing transparency on post-IPO shareholding distribution.
