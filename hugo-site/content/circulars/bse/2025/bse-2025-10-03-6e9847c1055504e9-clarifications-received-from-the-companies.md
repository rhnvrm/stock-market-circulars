## Summary

BSE Limited sought clarifications from various listed companies regarding recent significant movements in trading volume or price. In response, the companies confirmed that they have complied with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and have not withheld any material information that could impact price or volume behavior.

## Key Points

- **TVS Srichakra Limited**: Clarified regarding volume movement on October 3, 2025, confirming no undisclosed material information.
- **Borosil Scientific Limited**: Clarified regarding volume movement, stating that price/volume behavior is completely market-driven.
- **Dhanlaxmi Cotex Limited**: Acknowledged exchange inquiry regarding price movement and confirmed adherence to disclosure norms.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; routine enforcement of Regulation 30 of SEBI LODR.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must promptly respond to exchange queries regarding unusual market behavior and confirm compliance with material event disclosures.

## Important Dates

- **Clarification Dates**: October 3, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Neutral impact on market operations; standard compliance procedure for market surveillance.
