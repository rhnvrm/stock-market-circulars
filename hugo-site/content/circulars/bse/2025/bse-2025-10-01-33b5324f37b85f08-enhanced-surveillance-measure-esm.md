## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, listing securities that have been shortlisted or subjected to specific surveillance actions effective October 3, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of securities under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Effective date for the applicable measures is October 3, 2025.
- Includes companies such as Mudunuru Ltd, Rotographics (India) Ltd, and Sarthak Global Ltd under Annexure I (Part A).
- Provides a consolidated list of securities under various ESM stages in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Enhanced Surveillance Measures to targeted scrips to monitor volatility and protect market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and investors must note the inclusion of specific scrips under the ESM framework and comply with the applicable trading restrictions, margins, and deposit requirements.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 3, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and potential trading restrictions for securities included in the ESM framework, impacting liquidity and price movement for the affected scrips.
