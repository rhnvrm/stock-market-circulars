## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, listing securities that are shortlisted, moving out, or continuing under various stages of the framework effective September 25, 2025.

## Key Points

- Inclusion of securities in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework (Annexure I).
- Movement of specific securities out of the ST-ASM framework due to various reasons including inclusion in other surveillance frameworks like LT-ASM, ESM, or Trade for Trade (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities currently under the ST-ASM framework along with their respective stages (Annexure III).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ST-ASM framework for listed securities to monitor abnormal price movements and volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must note the inclusion and exclusion of securities from the ST-ASM framework and apply appropriate margin requirements as mandated.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, higher margin requirements, or specific trading controls depending on their assigned stage.
