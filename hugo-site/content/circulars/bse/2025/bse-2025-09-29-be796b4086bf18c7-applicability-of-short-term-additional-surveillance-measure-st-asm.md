## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework effective September 30, 2025. The circular outlines securities newly shortlisted, securities moving to higher or lower stages, securities moving out of the framework, and a consolidated list of securities under the ST-ASM framework.

## Key Points

- Effective date for the surveillance changes: September 30, 2025.
- Includes new inclusions in the ST-ASM framework (such as Jagsonpal Finance & Leasing Ltd, Magnus Steel and Infra Ltd, Tirupati Foam Ltd, and Vashishtha Luxury Fashion Ltd).
- Includes securities moving to higher/lower stages and securities moving out of the ST-ASM framework.
- Provides a comprehensive consolidated list of securities currently under various stages of the ST-ASM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance actions under the Short Term ASM framework to protect investors and curb excessive volatility in specific scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the applicable surveillance stage for the listed scrips and ensure compliance with respective margin requirements and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trading surveillance for securities placed under the ST-ASM framework, potentially impacting their liquidity and price movement.
