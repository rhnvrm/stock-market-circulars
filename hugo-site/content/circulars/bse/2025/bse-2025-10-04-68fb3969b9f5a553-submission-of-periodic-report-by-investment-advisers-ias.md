## Summary

This circular outlines the format and information requirements for the submission of periodic reports by Investment Advisers (IAs). It covers various mandatory disclosures including general entity details, legal structure, branch network, social media handles, and dedicated bank accounts used for receiving advisory fees.

## Key Points

- Mandates reporting of general details including registered name, trade name, and SEBI registration number.
- Requires disclosure of legal structure (Individual, Sole Proprietor, Partnership, LLP, or Body Corporate).
- Requires information on whether the Investment Adviser engages in non-advisory activities or acts as a part-time/research analyst.
- Details operational information such as branch network addresses, website details, and social media handles.
- Collects specific details of bank accounts utilized for receiving advisory fees.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standardized reporting structure for Investment Advisers to ensure consistent compliance tracking and oversight by regulatory bodies.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must accurately fill out and submit the periodic report format containing Table-1 general details, branch office addresses, social media links, and bank account details for advisory fees.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for periodic reporting compliance.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances transparency and regulatory oversight over Investment Advisers by streamlining the collection of operational and financial account data.
