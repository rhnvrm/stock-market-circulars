## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of 18,75,000 equity shares of Solvex Edibles Limited effective from subsequent dates, comprising fully paid-up shares, lock-in shares, and fresh IPO shares.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** Solvex Edibles Limited
- **Total Securities Listed:** 18,75,000 equity shares
- **Security Types:** Fully Paid up & Lock In, Fully Paid up - IPO (Free Shares)
- **Lock-in Details:** Specific lock-in periods apply up to September 30, 2028, September 30, 2027, and September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with standard BSE listing guidelines and depository requirements for demat settlement.

## Important Dates

- **Lock-in Effective Date:** 16/09/2025
- **Lock-in Expiry Dates:** 30/09/2026, 30/09/2027, 30/09/2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing update for market participants and investors regarding the availability and lock-in status of Solvex Edibles Limited shares.
