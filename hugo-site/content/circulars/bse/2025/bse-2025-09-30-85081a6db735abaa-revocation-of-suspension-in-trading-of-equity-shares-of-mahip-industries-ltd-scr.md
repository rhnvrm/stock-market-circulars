## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares of Mahip Industries Ltd. (Scrip Code: 542503). Trading in the securities of the company is resumed after the company complied with regulatory requirements and cleared pending obligations.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Mahip Industries Ltd.
- Scrip Code: 542503
- Action: Revocation of suspension in trading of equity shares.
- Effective Date: September 30, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

Trading restrictions previously imposed on the equity shares of Mahip Industries Ltd. have been lifted, permitting normal trading activities on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants are advised to take note of the resumption of trading and ensure all clearing and settlement instructions adhere to the updated exchange status.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Investors and shareholders regain liquidity and the ability to trade equity shares of Mahip Industries Ltd. on the BSE platform.
