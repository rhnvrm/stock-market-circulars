## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of True Colors Limited on the exchange, detailing the lock-in periods, distinctive numbers, and categories such as IPO, OFS, and anchor portions.

## Key Points

- Total securities listed: 24,654,600 fully paid equity shares.
- Includes various lock-in schedules for promoters and pre-issue shareholders.
- Contains allocations for IPO offer for sale (OFS), anchor investors, and free shares.

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must adhere to the specified lock-in periods and trading restrictions as per the distinctive numbers schedule.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in start dates begin from 22-09-2025 and 23-09-2025, with expiries extending through 2026, 2027, and 2028.

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of True Colors Limited equity shares on the exchange platform under the specified terms.
