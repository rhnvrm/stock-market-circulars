## Summary

L. T. Elevator Limited has submitted its post-issue shareholding pattern statement under Regulation 31(1)(b) / 31(1)(c) dated 18-SEP-2025, detailing the distribution of equity shares among promoters and the public.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: L. T. Elevator Limited
- Class of Security: Equity
- Date of Report / Allotment: 18-SEP-2025
- Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 12,017,328 shares (62.71%)
- Public Holding: 7,145,727 shares (37.29%)
- Total Shares: 19,163,055 shares

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 regarding the submission of shareholding patterns.

## Important Dates

- Event Date / Allotment Date: 18-SEP-2025

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational listing update for market participants and investors tracking shareholding distributions.
