## Summary

SEBI has mandated a structured Unified Payment Interface (UPI) address mechanism for all registered investor-facing intermediaries to collect funds securely from investors. The framework aims to enhance accessibility, safety, and transparency by providing investors with verified and validated UPI handles featuring distinct suffixes and handles allocated by NPCI.

## Key Points

- **Structured UPI Address:** Comprises a custom username based on intermediary segment abbreviations and a unique UPI handle (e.g., `@valid` combined with self-certified syndicate banks).
- **Visual Trust Indicator:** A "thumbs-up inside a green triangle" icon will be associated with verified intermediary UPI handles to assure investors of authenticity.
- **Mandatory for Intermediaries:** While optional for investors, it is mandatory for intermediaries to obtain, make available, and actively promote these structured UPI IDs.
- **Applicability:** Covers recognized stock exchanges, clearing corporations, depositories, investor-facing intermediaries, RTAs, and SCSBs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of a formalized and structured UPI address format for fund collections.
- Utilization of an authorized utility for generating intermediary usernames.

## Compliance Requirements

- Intermediaries must adopt the designated utility to generate compliant UPI usernames.
- Must display the structured UPI ID and associated verification icon clearly to investors.

## Important Dates

- Issued on June 11, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances investor confidence and reduces the risk of fraudulent fund transfers by ensuring payments are routed exclusively to verified and registered market intermediaries.
