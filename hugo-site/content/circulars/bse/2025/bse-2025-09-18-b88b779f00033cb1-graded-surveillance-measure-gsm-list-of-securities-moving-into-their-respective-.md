## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the movement of securities into various Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages effective from the specified date. Ten securities have been identified for migration across GSM Stages 0, I, II, and III.

## Key Points

- Maitri Enterprises Ltd moved to GSM Stage 0.
- Chennai Meenakshi Multispeciality Hospital Ltd, Promact Impex Ltd, and Scintilla Commercial & Credit Ltd moved to GSM Stage I.
- Lynx Machinery & Commercials Ltd, Span Divergent Ltd, and Kiran Print Pack Ltd moved to GSM Stage II.
- Peoples Investments Ltd, Integra Switchgear Ltd, and Shivamshree Businesses Ltd moved to GSM Stage III.
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower in the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the ESM Framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Applicability of surveillance measures and trading restrictions under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework for listed companies.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must note the revised GSM stages applicable to the listed securities and trade accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to heightened surveillance, additional margin requirements, or trading frequency restrictions depending on their respective GSM stage.
