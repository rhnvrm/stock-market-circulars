## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted, moved across stages, or removed from the framework effective October 03, 2025.

## Key Points

- Annexure I lists securities newly shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. October 03, 2025, including Accedere Ltd, Netweb Technologies India Ltd, Priya Ltd, Roni Households Ltd, Stanpacks India Ltd, Sunita Tools Ltd, Uravi Defence and Technology Ltd, and Visa Steel Ltd.
- Annexure II details securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. October 03, 2025 (such as Rotographics (India) Ltd).
- Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities under various ASM stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the LT-ASM framework to monitor price volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply appropriate surveillance margins and trading checks for the listed securities as mandated by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities may be subject to enhanced surveillance, additional margins, or other trading restrictions as per exchange norms.
