## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Effective October 03, 2025, specific securities have been shortlisted, moved across stages, or consolidated under the framework to monitor market volatility and protect investors.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of securities into the ESM Framework effective October 03, 2025 (e.g., Mudunuru Ltd, Rotographics (India) Ltd, Sarthak Global Ltd).
- Consolidated listing of securities continuing under various stages of the ESM framework.
- Implementation of trading safeguards applicable to securities under the ESM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Application of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) stages (such as Stage I and Stage II) to specified scrips to manage volatility and speculative trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must adhere to the applicable trading restrictions, higher margin requirements, and settlement conditions specified for securities under the ESM framework.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the affected scrips may experience restricted liquidity and higher margin requirements due to surveillance monitoring.
