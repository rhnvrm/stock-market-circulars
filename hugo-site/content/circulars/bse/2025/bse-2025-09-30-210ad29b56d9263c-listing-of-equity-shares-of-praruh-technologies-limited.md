## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Praruh Technologies Limited under various distinctive number ranges, including fully paid-up shares and free IPO shares, along with specified lock-in periods.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Praruh Technologies Limited
- Various tranches of fully paid-up equity shares are listed with demat settlement.
- Specific lock-in dates ranging from October 2025 to October 2028 apply to respective security ranges.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to the specified lock-in periods and trading restrictions as per the exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in Upto Dates: 30/10/2025, 30/12/2025, 05/10/2026, 05/10/2027, 05/10/2028

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing update for market participants tracking equity issuances and lock-in expirations.
