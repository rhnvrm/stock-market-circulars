## Summary

Palika Towns LLP, a promoter of Harig Crankshafts Limited, has announced an Offer for Sale (OFS) of 5,25,000 equity shares (representing 5% of the total equity share capital) to comply with Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) norms.

## Key Points

- **Seller:** Palika Towns LLP (Promoter)
- **Company:** Harig Crankshafts Limited (Scrip Code: 500178, ISIN: INE905C01034)
- **Shares Offered:** 5,25,000 Equity Shares (5% of share capital)
- **Purpose:** To reach 10% public shareholding and comply with Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) norms under Rule 19A(5) of Securities Contracts (Regulation) Rules, 1957.
- **Bidding Dates:** 
  - Non-Retail Investors: October 01, 2025 ("T" day)
  - Retail Investors and Non-Retail Investors carrying forward bids: October 03, 2025 ("T+1" day)

## Regulatory Changes

None. The OFS is conducted pursuant to SEBI OFS Circulars and guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Sellers, brokers, and prospective buyers must adhere to the BSE trading platform guidelines and SEBI OFS regulatory frameworks during the bidding process.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 30, 2025
- **Non-Retail Bidding:** October 01, 2025
- **Retail Bidding & Carry Forward:** October 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates promoter stake reduction to satisfy regulatory Minimum Public Shareholding requirements for Harig Crankshafts Limited.
