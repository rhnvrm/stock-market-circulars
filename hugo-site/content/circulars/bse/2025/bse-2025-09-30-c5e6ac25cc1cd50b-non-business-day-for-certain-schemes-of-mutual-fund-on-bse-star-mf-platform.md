## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing the non-business days for various mutual fund schemes across multiple fund houses (including Axis, Kotak, Edelweiss, Nippon, DSP, Invesco, and Bandhan) for the month of October 2025 on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Lists non-business dates for specific fund schemes for October 2025.
- Covers fund houses such as Axis, Kotak, Edelweiss, Nippon, DSP, Invesco, and Bandhan.
- Transactions in these specific schemes will not be processed on the respective non-business days.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and distributors must take note of the non-business days when placing orders on the BSE StAR MF platform for the affected schemes.

## Important Dates

- Effective dates span across October 2025 as specified for individual fund schemes.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures accurate processing timelines and prevents transaction failures or miscommunications regarding NAV applicability for specific international and thematic mutual fund schemes.
