## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Rukmani Devi Garg Agro Impex Limited, detailing the number of securities, distinctive numbers, security type, and lock-in periods following its IPO.

## Key Points

- Total securities listed include fully paid equity shares and IPO shares.
- Lock-in periods vary across tranches up to 1, 2, and 3 years, ending on October 3, 2026, October 3, 2027, and October 3, 2028 respectively.
- Distinctive numbers range from 1 to 8,876,000.

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Companies must adhere to the specified lock-in periods and share distribution parameters as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Listing / Effective Date: October 3, 2025
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: October 3, 2026; October 3, 2027; October 3, 2028

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of the company's equity shares on the exchange platform under the specified terms and lock-in restrictions.
