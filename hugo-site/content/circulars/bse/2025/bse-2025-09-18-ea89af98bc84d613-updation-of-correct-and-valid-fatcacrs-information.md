## Summary

This circular outlines the uniform implementation of KYC requirements, ultimate beneficial ownership details, and FATCA/CRS information for mutual fund investors based on AMFI Best Practice Guidelines No. 62/2015-16. It details mandatory requirements for new investors effective November 1, 2015, and for existing investors effective January 1, 2016.

## Key Points

- Mandatory provision of 'Beneficial Ownership' details for all new mutual fund investors.
- Mandatory submission of additional KYC details such as gross annual income and net worth for new investors.
- Purchase and switch transactions for 'KYC - On Hold' cases are restricted until missing info is updated in the KRA system.
- Existing investors must update beneficial ownership and additional KYC details by December 31, 2015, to avoid restrictions on additional subscriptions from January 1, 2016.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of AMFI Best Practice Guidelines regarding KYC, FATCA, and ultimate beneficial ownership.
- Clear categorization of actions required by AMCs and RTAs based on customer KYC status (Registered, On Hold, Under Process, Rejected, Not Available, Deactivated).

## Compliance Requirements

- AMFs, MFDs, AMCs, and RTAs must ensure new and existing clients fulfill KYC, FATCA, and beneficial ownership criteria before processing fresh or additional purchases/switches.
- Focused efforts must be deployed to collect missing KYC information from existing investors.

## Important Dates

- **November 1, 2015:** Effective date for new mutual fund investor KYC/FATCA restrictions.
- **December 31, 2015:** Deadline for existing investors to complete missing KYC and IPV requirements.
- **January 1, 2016:** Effective date for restrictions on existing mutual fund folios with incomplete KYC or beneficial ownership details.

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact on mutual fund distributors, AMCs, and RTAs requiring enhanced verification steps, leading to possible transaction rejections for non-compliant folios or 'KYC - On Hold' accounts.
