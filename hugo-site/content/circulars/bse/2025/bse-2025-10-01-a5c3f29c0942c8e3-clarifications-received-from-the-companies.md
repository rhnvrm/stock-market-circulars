## Summary

This circular compiles responses from multiple listed companies—Wonder Electricals Ltd., Banaras Beads Ltd., and IB Infotech Enterprises Ltd.—providing clarifications to BSE and NSE concerning significant movements in their share price or trading volume.

## Key Points

- **Wonder Electricals Ltd.**: Clarified that the significant increase in trading volume is market-driven and beyond management control, confirming compliance with all disclosure requirements under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- **Banaras Beads Ltd.**: Clarified regarding significant price movement, noting recent property purchase financed by a bank loan and confirming no insider transactions or undisclosed material information.
- **IB Infotech Enterprises Ltd.**: Acknowledged receipt of clarification request regarding price movement and noted ongoing compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must respond promptly to exchange queries concerning unusual market activity in compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- **Clarification Date**: October 1, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No direct operational or financial impact; purely informational disclosures affirming regular market-driven dynamics.
