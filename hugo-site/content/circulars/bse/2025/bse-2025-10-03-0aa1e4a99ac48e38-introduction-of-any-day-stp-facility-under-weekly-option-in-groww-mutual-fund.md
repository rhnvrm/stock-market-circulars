## Summary

Groww Asset Management Ltd. has issued a notice cum addendum announcing revisions to the Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) for all schemes of Groww Mutual Fund (except ETFs), effective from October 6, 2025.

## Key Points

- Weekly STPs can now be initiated on any day from Monday to Friday based on the investor's choice.
- If no day is selected for Weekly STP, Friday will be the default day.
- If an STP date falls on a holiday, the transaction will be processed on the next working day.
- No minimum balance is required in the transferee scheme for registering an STP or SWP.
- The minimum number of days required for registration and cancellation of STP/SWP is 7 calendar days from the request date.
- The default SWP date is set to the 15th of every month if no date is specified by the investor.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an operational scheme feature update.

## Compliance Requirements

Unitholders and investors need to note the revised timelines and options for STP and SWP registrations.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 30, 2025
- **Effective Date:** October 6, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with greater flexibility regarding weekly STP executions and standardizes SWP processing rules across Groww Mutual Fund schemes.
