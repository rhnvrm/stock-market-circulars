## Summary

This circular compiles clarifications received from listed entities—Waaree Energies Ltd. and Balmer Lawrie & Co. Ltd.—in response to news articles published in media outlets regarding ongoing US investigations and strategic business plans.

## Key Points

- Waaree Energies Ltd. clarified its stance on the news item titled "US probes Waaree Energies for alleged duty evasion on solar imports", noting its ongoing compliance and operations in the United States, including expansions in Texas and asset acquisitions.
- Balmer Lawrie & Co. Ltd. addressed inquiries regarding media reports about its plans to exit the refinery and oil services field.
- Both companies affirmed their commitment to complying with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a disclosure of clarifications sought by the exchange and provided by the companies under Regulation 30(11).

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must respond to exchange queries regarding material news items in a timely manner pursuant to Regulation 30.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and helps investors gauge the accuracy and official company position regarding circulating market rumors or news reports.
