## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability, continuation, and removal of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective October 06, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisted new securities in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective October 06, 2025.
- Listed securities moving out of the Short Term ASM Framework due to various reasons, including migration to other surveillance frameworks.
- Consolidated list of securities currently remaining in the ST-ASM framework along with their respective stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short Term ASM framework to monitor price volatility and safeguard investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance margins and restrictions for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 06, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities may experience additional trading margins (such as 100% margin requirement) or trading restrictions as per the applicable ST-ASM stage.
