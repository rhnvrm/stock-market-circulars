## Summary

Trualt Bioenergy Limited has finalized the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors ahead of its initial public offering (IPO). The company successfully raised approximately ₹251.78 crore by allocating 50,76,289 equity shares to 19 anchor funds (via 17 application forms) at an issue price of ₹496 per share.

## Key Points

- Total Anchor Allocation: 50,76,289 equity shares.
- Issue Price: ₹496 per equity share.
- Total Funds Raised: ₹251.78 crore.
- Number of Anchor Investors: 19 funds (17 application forms).
- Prominent institutional investors participating in the anchor round include major mutual funds and foreign portfolio investors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Complies with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 pertaining to anchor investor bidding and allotments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must note the lock-in periods applicable to anchor investor shares as per SEBI ICDR guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 24, 2025
- IPO Opening Date: September 25, 2025
- IPO Closing Date: September 29, 2025
- Basis of Allotment: September 30, 2025
- Listing Date: October 3, 2025
