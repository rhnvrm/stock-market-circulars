## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Sampat Aluminium Limited, detailing the post-issue shareholding pattern pursuant to Regulation 31(1)(a)/(b)/(c) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: Sampat Aluminium Limited
- Class of Security: Equity
- Date of Report / Allotment: 23-SEP-2025
- Disclosure indicates that the entity has locked-in shares but no partly paid-up shares, convertible securities, warrants, or encumbered promoter shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard compliance reporting under listing regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI LODR regarding shareholding pattern disclosure.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 23-SEP-2025

## Impact Assessment

- Routine corporate action with minimal direct market impact beyond standard transparency.
