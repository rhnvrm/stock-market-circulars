## Summary

BSE sought clarifications from various listed companies regarding recent price or volume movements in their scrips. In response, companies such as Trigyn Technologies Limited, Zuari Industries Limited, and Bajaj Healthcare Limited confirmed that they do not possess any unpublished price sensitive information (UPSI) or impending announcements that would explain the recent market movements.

## Key Points

- **Trigyn Technologies Limited:** Confirmed no UPSI, impending announcements, or corporate actions requiring disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- **Zuari Industries Limited:** Clarified that the recent increase in volume is not tied to any undisclosed corporate announcements and appears to be purely market-driven.
- **Bajaj Healthcare Limited:** Responded to exchange queries regarding volume fluctuations, affirming compliance with prompt disclosure obligations.
- **Compliance Commitment:** All responding entities reiterated their commitment to timely and transparent disclosures under SEBI LODR Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must promptly respond to stock exchange inquiries regarding unusual market activity and clarify whether any undisclosed price-sensitive information exists.

## Important Dates

- **Submission Dates:** September 23–24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard compliance communication ensuring market transparency without any fundamental or operational impact on the companies.
