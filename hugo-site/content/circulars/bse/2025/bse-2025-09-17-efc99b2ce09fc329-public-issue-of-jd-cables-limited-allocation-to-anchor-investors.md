## Summary

 Bombay Stock Exchange has released information regarding the public issue of equity shares of JD Cables Limited, detailing the finalization of 17,80,000 equity shares allocated to anchor investors at an allocation price of Rs. 152 per equity share.

## Key Points

- Company: JD Cables Limited
- Issue Details: Public issue of equity shares of face value of Rs. 10 each.
- Anchor Allocation: 17,80,000 Equity Shares allocated to Anchor Investors.
- Price: Rs. 152 per Equity Share (including share premium of Rs. 142 per Equity Share).
- Meeting Date: September 17, 2025.
