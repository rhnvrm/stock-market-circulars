## Summary

BSE has issued contract specifications and launch details for WTI Crude Oil Options on Futures contracts under the symbol WTICRUDE.

## Key Points

- Underlying: WTI Crude Oil Futures contract traded on BSE.
- Option Type: European Call & Put Options.
- Trading Unit: One BSE WTI Crude Oil futures contract.
- Strike Price Intervals: Rs. 50 (51 CE and 51 PE strikes).
- Tick Size: Rs. 0.10.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes standardized parameters and margin requirements for WTI Crude Oil Options on Futures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must adhere to the specified SPAN margining system, daily price limits, and trading session timings.

## Important Dates

- Contracts will be available as per the Contract Launch Calendar.

## Impact Assessment

- Expands commodity derivatives offerings on the exchange, providing additional hedging and trading instruments for market participants.
