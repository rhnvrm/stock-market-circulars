## Summary

Tamrock Great Britain Holdings Limited, a promoter group entity of Eimco Elecon (India) Limited, has announced the exercise of its oversubscription option to sell an additional 7,00,000 equity shares in addition to the base offer size of 7,23,875 equity shares. The total offer size now stands up to 14,23,875 equity shares, representing approximately 24.68% of the total issued and paid-up equity share capital of the company.

## Key Points

- Promoter Group entity Tamrock Great Britain Holdings Limited exercises the oversubscription option.
- Additional 7,00,000 equity shares offered alongside the base offer of 7,23,875 equity shares.
- Total combined offer size is up to 14,23,875 equity shares (24.68% of the total issued and paid-up equity share capital).
- 1,42,388 equity shares are reserved for allocation to Retail Investors, subject to valid bids.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with guidelines and circulars issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India and Stock Exchanges regarding Offers for Sale (OFS).

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: September 24, 2025
- T Day (Non-Retail Investors): September 24, 2025
- T+1 Day (Retail Investors and carrying forward unallotted non-retail bids): September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides an expanded window and increased liquidity for the sale of promoter stakes in Eimco Elecon (India) Limited, accommodating higher investor demand through the stock exchange mechanism.
