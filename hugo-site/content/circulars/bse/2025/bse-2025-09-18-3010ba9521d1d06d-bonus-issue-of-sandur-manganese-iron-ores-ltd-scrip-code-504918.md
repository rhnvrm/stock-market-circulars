## Summary

The Sandur Manganese & Iron Ores Limited has announced the listing and allotment of 32,40,69,876 bonus equity shares following a bonus issue. Pre-bonus share capital stood at 16,20,34,938 shares, expanding to a total post-bonus share capital of 48,61,04,814 equity shares.

## Key Points

- Pre-Bonus Shares: 16,20,34,938 (Distinctive Nos: 1 to 16,20,34,938)
- Bonus Shares Issued: 32,40,69,876
- Post-Bonus Shares: 48,61,04,814 (Distinctive Nos: 1 to 48,61,04,814)
- ISIN: INE149K01016
- Scrip Code: 504918

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; standard corporate action compliance for bonus issue listing.

## Compliance Requirements

Ensure proper crediting of bonus shares to shareholder demat accounts in accordance with depository guidelines under ISIN INE149K01016.

## Important Dates

- Deemed Date of Allotment: 23 September 2025
- Effective Listing Date: 24 September 2025

## Impact Assessment

Expansion of the company's equity base enhances liquidity and trading volumes for existing shareholders.
