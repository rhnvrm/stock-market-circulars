## Summary

Jaro Institute of Technology Management and Research Limited raised ₹134.99 crore from anchor investors ahead of its initial public offering (IPO). The company allocated a total of 15,16,853 equity shares to 19 anchor investors at the upper price band of ₹890 per share.

## Key Points

- Total anchor book size: ₹134.99 crore.
- Number of equity shares allocated: 15,16,853 shares.
- Number of anchor investors: 19 institutional investors.
- Issue price / upper price band: ₹890 per share.
- Key institutional participants included domestic mutual funds and global funds such as Citigroup Global Markets Mauritius, Nomura Singapore, Societe Generale, WhiteOak Capital Mutual Fund, and 360 ONE Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI ICDR Regulations regarding anchor investor allocation, lock-in periods, and public issue disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to mandatory lock-in periods for anchor investor shares (30 days for 50% of the shares and 90 days for the remaining portion).

## Important Dates

- Anchor Investor Allocation Date: September 22, 2025.
- IPO Bidding Open Date: September 23, 2025.
- IPO Bidding Close Date: September 25, 2025.
- Listing Date: September 30, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- High institutional participation demonstrates strong market confidence in the edtech sector and the company's fundamentals prior to public subscription.
