## Summary

BSE Limited has issued guidelines and a lien application cum consent letter format for Investment Advisers (IAs) to submit Non-Demat Liquid Mutual Fund or Overnight Mutual Fund Units towards their mandatory deposit requirements.

## Key Points

- Details the procedure for creating a lien on mutual fund units in favor of BSE Limited.
- Requires coordination between the unitholder, RTA/AMC, and IAASB (BSE Limited).
- Specifies conditions ensuring units are free of encumbrance and not subject to lock-in periods.
- Grants BSE the right to redeem or transfer units in case of failure to pay dues under arbitration and conciliation proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes standardized documentation for utilizing mutual fund units as deposits under SEBI Regulations pertaining to Research Analysts and Investment Advisers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must submit the duly filled Lien Application cum Consent Letter through their RTA or AMC to mark a lien in favor of BSE Limited.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular issuance date.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational clarity for Investment Advisers to meet deposit obligations using liquid or overnight mutual fund investments.
