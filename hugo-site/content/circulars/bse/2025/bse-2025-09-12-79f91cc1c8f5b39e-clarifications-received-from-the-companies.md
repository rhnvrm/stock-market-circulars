## Summary

BSE has published clarifications received from various listed companies in response to exchange inquiries regarding significant fluctuations in the price and volume of their securities.

## Key Points

- **Prithvi Exchange (India) Limited**: Clarified that the movement in the price of the company's securities is entirely market-driven and due to prevailing market conditions. The company confirmed compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, and stated there is no undisclosed or price-sensitive information.
- **Rushil Decor Limited**: Clarified regarding a significant increase in trading volume, confirming no specific material events or undisclosed price-sensitive information exist that would impact operations beyond regular disclosures.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must continue to promptly disclose any material events, information, or price-sensitive actions in compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- **Clarification Date**: September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard compliance communication; no direct operational or financial impact on market participants.
