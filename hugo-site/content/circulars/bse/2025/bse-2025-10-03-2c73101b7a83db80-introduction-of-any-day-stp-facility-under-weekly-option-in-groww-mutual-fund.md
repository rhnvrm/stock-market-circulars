## Summary

Groww Mutual Fund has issued a notice cum addendum regarding changes in frequency, eligibility criteria, processing timelines, and default dates for Systematic Transfer Plan (STP) and Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) across its schemes (except ETFs), effective October 6, 2025.

## Key Points

- Weekly STPs can now be initiated on any day from Monday to Friday, with Friday as the default day.
- No minimum balance is required in the transferee scheme for registering an STP or SWP.
- The minimum number of days required for registration and cancellation of STP/SWP requests is 7 calendar days.
- The default SWP date is set to the 15th of every month if no date is selected by the investor.

## Regulatory Changes

- Update to Scheme Information Documents (SID), Key Information Memorandum (KIM), and Statement of Additional Information (SAI) for Groww Mutual Fund schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors and unitholders are notified of the revised operational parameters for STP and SWP.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 30, 2025
- **Effective Date:** October 6, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Provides greater flexibility for investors setting up weekly STP frequencies and streamlines operational processing timelines.
