## Summary

Gujarat Peanut and Agri Products Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern under Regulation 31(1)(b) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, showing a total of 10,262,000 equity shares.

## Key Points

- Total Paid-up Equity Shares: 10,262,000 across 752 shareholders.
- Promoter & Promoter Group holding: 7,286,000 shares (71.00%).
- Public holding: 2,976,000 shares (29.00%).
- No partly paid-up shares or depository receipts issued.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard disclosure pursuant to Reg. 31(1)(b).

## Compliance Requirements

- Filing of shareholding pattern in accordance with SEBI LODR Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Report Date: 01-10-2025

## Impact Assessment

- Standard regulatory filing with no broader market impact.
