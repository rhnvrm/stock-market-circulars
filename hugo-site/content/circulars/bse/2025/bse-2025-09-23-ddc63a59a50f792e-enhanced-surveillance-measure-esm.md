## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the list of securities newly shortlisted, securities moving to higher stages, and the comprehensive consolidated list of securities under ESM effective September 24, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities are newly shortlisted under the ESM Framework w.e.f. September 24, 2025 (e.g., Anlon Healthcare Ltd, Emmforce Autotech Ltd, Gujarat Investa Ltd, IEL Ltd, Intense Technologies Ltd, Synthiko Foils Ltd).
- Securities moving to higher Stage ESM include Bisil Plast Ltd, India Lease Development Ltd, Kaya Ltd, Midwest Gold Ltd, Precision Electronics Ltd, Ramchandra Leasing & Finance Ltd, Saptak Chem And Business Ltd, Sattrix Information Security Ltd, Senthil Infotek Ltd, and Trustedge Capital Ltd.
- Annexure III provides the full consolidated list of securities subject to the ESM framework along with their respective stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework stages to specified securities to monitor volatility and protect market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are advised to note the applicable ESM stages for the listed securities and ensure adherence to relevant margin and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to heightened surveillance measures, potentially impacting liquidity and margin requirements for investors holding or trading these scrips.
