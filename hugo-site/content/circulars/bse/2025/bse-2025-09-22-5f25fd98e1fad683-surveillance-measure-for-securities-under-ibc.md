## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding surveillance measures for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective September 23, 2025. The circular details lists of securities shortlisted under various stages of the IBC framework, including Stage 0 and consolidated listings.

## Key Points

- Reliance Home Finance Ltd (Scrip Code: 540709, ISIN: INE217K01011) shortlisted under Stage 0 w.e.f. September 23, 2025.
- Consolidated list includes various companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per IBC.
- Specifies securities moving across different stages of the IBC surveillance framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) for companies undergoing insolvency resolution processes as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must note the surveillance status and trading restrictions applicable to the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in affected securities is subject to specific surveillance monitoring and margins as mandated by the exchange under the IBC framework.
