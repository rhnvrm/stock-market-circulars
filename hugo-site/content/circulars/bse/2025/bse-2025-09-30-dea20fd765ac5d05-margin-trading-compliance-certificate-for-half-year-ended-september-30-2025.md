## Summary

BSE has issued a user manual and procedural guidelines for trading members to submit the mandatory Margin Trading Compliance Certificate for the half-year ended September 30, 2025, through the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS).

## Key Points

- All members who have obtained approval for Margin Trading Facility (MTF) from the Exchange must mandatorily submit the compliance certificate.
- Submissions must be made via the BEFS portal under Margin Trading Module > Select M T Compliance Certificate.
- Members must select whether they are offering MTF to clients or not, and upload a digitally signed or physically signed and stamped certificate in PDF format using the naming convention `ClgNo_MTFCERT.pdf`.
- Members approved for MTF who have not commenced operations must submit a certificate stating the same on their letterhead.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiteration of mandatory electronic submission of the Margin Trading Compliance Certificate through BEFS.
- Physical or email submissions will not be considered valid submissions by the Exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Log in to BEFS with user credentials and navigate to the Margin Trading Module.
- Complete the undertaking checkbox and attach the digitally or physically signed copy of the certificate with the proper naming convention.
- Ensure submission is completed and an acknowledgment email is received from the Exchange.

## Important Dates

- Applicable for the half year ended September 30, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures regulatory compliance and supervision of Margin Trading Facilities offered by trading members across the exchange.
