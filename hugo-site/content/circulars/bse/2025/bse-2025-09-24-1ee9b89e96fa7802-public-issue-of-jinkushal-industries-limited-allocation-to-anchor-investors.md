## Summary

Jinkushal Industries Limited has finalized the allocation of 28,78,500 equity shares to anchor investors at an allocation price of ₹121 per equity share. Through this anchor placement, the company successfully mobilized ₹34,82,98,500 (approx. ₹34.83 crore) ahead of its public issue.

## Key Points

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 24, 2025
- Total Shares Allocated: 28,78,500 Equity Shares
- Anchor Allocation Price: ₹121 per Equity Share
- Total Amount Raised: ₹34,82,98,500
- Prominent institutional investors participated, including Nomura Singapore Limited ODI, HDFC Bank Limited, Viney Growth Fund, Steptrade Revolution Fund, Santosh Industries, and Swyom India Alpha Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with SEBI ICDR Regulations and BSE listing guidelines regarding anchor investor lock-in periods and public issue disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 24, 2025
- Public Issue Opening Date: September 25, 2025
- Public Issue Closing Date: September 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

The successful anchor allocation demonstrates robust institutional appetite and sets a strong valuation benchmark for Jinkushal Industries Limited's public offering.
