## Summary

SEBI has announced an extension of timelines for the implementation of the framework on 'Safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading'. A structured glide path with key milestones has been introduced to assist stock brokers who require additional time for system changes, while brokers ready by October 01, 2025, may go live immediately.

## Key Points

- The initial circular issued on February 04, 2025, had its timeline extended to October 01, 2025, and now provides a glide path for brokers needing further system modifications.
- Brokers ready with required systems can go live effective October 01, 2025.
- A specific milestone-based glide path is established for brokers who are not yet fully ready.
- Non-compliant brokers will be barred from onboarding new retail clients for API-based algorithmic trading starting January 05, 2026.
- Full applicability for all stock brokers comes into effect on April 01, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of a tiered milestone-based timeline for the implementation of retail algorithmic trading controls and infrastructure.
- Mandatory participation in mock trading sessions and exchange application registrations for retail algo products.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Milestone 1 (October 31, 2025):** Retail algo products coming through API and at least one algo strategy must be applied for registration with the exchange.
- **Milestone 2 (November 30, 2025):** Completion of registration of retail algo products and strategies with the exchange.
- **Milestone 3 (January 03, 2026):** Stock broker participation in at least one mock session with the new complete functionality.
- Stock brokers failing to meet these milestones shall be barred from onboarding new retail clients for API-based algo trading from January 05, 2026.
- Brokers not going live on October 01, 2025, must provide existing client counts as of September 30, 2025, to exchanges.

## Important Dates

- **October 01, 2025:** Go-live date for ready stock brokers.
- **October 31, 2025:** Milestone 1 deadline (Application for strategy/product registration).
- **November 30, 2025:** Milestone 2 deadline (Completion of product registration).
- **January 03, 2026:** Milestone 3 deadline (Mock session participation).
- **January 05, 2026:** Restriction on onboarding new retail API algo clients for non-compliant brokers.
- **April 01, 2026:** Full mandatory implementation of the framework for all stock brokers.

## Impact Assessment

This extension provides necessary breathing room for stock brokers and algorithmic vendors to adapt their infrastructure to SEBI's enhanced risk-mitigation and compliance standards, preventing abrupt market disruptions while ensuring structured adherence to safer trading practices.
