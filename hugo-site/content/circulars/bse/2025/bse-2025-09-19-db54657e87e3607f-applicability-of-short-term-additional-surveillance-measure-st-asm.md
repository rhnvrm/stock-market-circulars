## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. The circular outlines securities newly shortlisted, those moving out, and the consolidated list of securities under the ST-ASM framework effective from September 22, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of multiple securities under the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework w.e.f. September 22, 2025 (Annexure I).
- Movement of several securities out of the Short Term ASM Framework due to various reasons including shifting to other surveillance frameworks (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under different stages of the ST-ASM framework (Annexure III).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions on specified scrips to monitor volatility and protect investors under the ST-ASM guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and apply necessary margin and trading restrictions for the shortlisted scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities may experience heightened margin requirements or surveillance controls as per the applicable ST-ASM stage.
