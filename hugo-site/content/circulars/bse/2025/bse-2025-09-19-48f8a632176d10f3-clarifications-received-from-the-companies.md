## Summary

BSE sought clarification from several companies regarding a significant increase in trading volume across exchanges on September 19, 2025. Sonata Software Limited, Tata Investment Corporation Limited, and Uniparts India Limited responded confirming that they have made all necessary disclosures under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 and are not aware of any undisclosed information or impending announcements that would explain the volume movement.

## Key Points

- Sonata Software Limited confirmed compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 and denied knowledge of any price-sensitive information.
- Tata Investment Corporation Limited stated it has no information or announcements to share that would impact the price or volume behaviour of its scrip.
- Uniparts India Limited reaffirmed that all required disclosures have been duly made and no information has been withheld.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must continue to promptly disclose any material events or information to the stock exchanges in accordance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No direct operational or financial impact; standard surveillance clarification confirming standard market compliance.
