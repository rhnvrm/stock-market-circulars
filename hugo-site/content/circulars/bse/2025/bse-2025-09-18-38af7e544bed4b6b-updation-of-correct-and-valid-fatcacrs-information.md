## Summary

This circular outlines the uniform implementation of KYC requirements, ultimate beneficial ownership details, and FATCA/CRS compliance for mutual fund investors, following AMFI Best Practice Circular No. 62/2015-16.

## Key Points

- Mandatory provision of beneficial ownership details for all new mutual fund investors effective November 1, 2015.
- Mandatory collection of additional KYC details such as gross annual income and net worth for new investors.
- Purchase and switch transactions for 'KYC – On Hold' cases are restricted until missing information is provided and updated in the KRA system.
- Existing investors are required to update beneficial ownership and additional KYC details by December 31, 2015, to continue making additional subscriptions or switches from January 1, 2016.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of AMFI guidelines regarding uniform KYC and FATCA/CRS compliance across all mutual fund intermediaries and AMCs.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMCs and intermediaries must reject fresh/additional purchase and switch transactions for non-compliant or 'On Hold' KYC accounts.
- Existing investors must furnish missing KYC information and In-Person Verification (IPV) requirements.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for New Investors: November 1, 2015
- Deadline for Existing Investors to Update KYC: December 31, 2015
- Enforcement Date for Existing Investors: January 1, 2016

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures stringent compliance with KYC and tax reporting norms (FATCA/CRS), temporarily restricting non-compliant accounts from executing subscription and switch transactions.
