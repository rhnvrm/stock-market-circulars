## Summary

This circular announces the merger of Baroda BNP Paribas Banking and PSU Bond Fund into Baroda BNP Paribas Short Duration Fund, detailing the transferor and transferee scheme options along with their respective ISIN mappings.

## Key Points

- Transferor Scheme: Baroda BNP Paribas Banking and PSU Bond Fund (Regular and Direct plans, including Growth, Monthly IDCW, and Quarterly IDCW options).
- Transferee Scheme: Baroda BNP Paribas Short Duration Fund (Corresponding Regular and Direct plans, including Growth, Monthly IDCW, and Quarterly IDCW options/reinvestments).
- Complete ISIN-wise mapping provided for all affected unit holders.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Asset Management Company (AMC) and RTA to ensure seamless transition and unit allotment according to the specified ISIN mapping table.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As per operational schedule in the notice (Reference ID: 20250926-69).

## Impact Assessment

Existing investors in Baroda BNP Paribas Banking and PSU Bond Fund will have their holdings transferred to equivalent plans of Baroda BNP Paribas Short Duration Fund.
