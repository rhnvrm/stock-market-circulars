## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Jay Ambe Supermarkets Limited, detailing the number of securities, distinctive number ranges, paid-up status, and lock-in details.

## Key Points

- Total securities listed include various tranches with different lock-in expiry dates ranging from October 2025 to September 2029, alongside free shares.
- All listed shares are fully paid and in demat mode.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to the lock-in periods specified for respective share tranches.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in expiry dates range from 14/10/2025 to 16/09/2029.

## Impact Assessment

Standard listing update with no broader market impact.
