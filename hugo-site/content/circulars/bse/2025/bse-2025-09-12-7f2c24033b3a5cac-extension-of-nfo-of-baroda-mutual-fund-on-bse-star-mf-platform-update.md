## Summary

Baroda BNP Paribas Asset Management India Private Limited has announced an extension of the New Fund Offer (NFO) period for the Baroda BNP Paribas Business Conglomerates Fund. The closure date has been extended from September 15, 2025, to September 16, 2025.

## Key Points

- Scheme Name: Baroda BNP Paribas Business Conglomerates Fund
- Extension: NFO closure date extended by one day.
- Previous Closing Date: September 15, 2025
- Revised Closing Date: September 16, 2025
- All other terms and conditions of the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Investors and distributors should take note of the revised NFO closing date for subscription applications.

## Important Dates

- Revised NFO Closure Date: September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an additional day to subscribe to the open-ended equity scheme investing in business conglomerates.
