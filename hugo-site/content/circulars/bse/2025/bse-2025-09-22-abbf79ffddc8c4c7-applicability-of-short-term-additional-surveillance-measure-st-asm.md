## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Effective September 23, 2025, several securities have been shortlisted under the ST-ASM framework, moved to different stages, or excluded based on surveillance reviews.

## Key Points

- New securities added to the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework (Annexure I).
- Securities moving to lower/higher stages or continuing under the framework.
- Securities moving out of the ST-ASM framework due to inclusion in other frameworks like LT-ASM, Trade for Trade, GSM, or ESM (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities currently under the ST-ASM framework (Annexure III).

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities identified under ST-ASM are subject to enhanced surveillance margins and trading checks to protect investor interests and curb excessive volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened margin requirements and surveillance checks for affected scrips may impact short-term liquidity and trading volumes.
