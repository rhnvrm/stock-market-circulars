## Summary

BSE Limited issued a circular regarding the process for the submission of Non-Demat Liquid Mutual Fund or Overnight Mutual Fund Units towards deposit requirements by Investment Advisers (IAs). It includes the standard format for the Lien Application cum Consent Letter to be submitted to the RTA or AMC for creating a lien in favor of BSE Limited.

## Key Points

- Investment Advisers (IAs) can pledge non-demat liquid or overnight mutual fund units towards their deposit requirements with BSE Limited (IAASB).
- A Lien Application cum Consent Letter must be submitted to the respective Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA) or Asset Management Company (AMC).
- The units must be free of any prior pledge, charge, lien, or encumbrance and not held under Section 54EA/EB of the Income Tax Act, 1961.
- BSE Limited holds the right to redeem or transfer the units in case of default on dues emanating from arbitration and conciliation proceedings under the ODR mechanism or SEBI regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes the operational framework for marking a lien on non-demat mutual fund holdings by Investment Advisers to satisfy regulatory deposit mandates.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must accurately fill out and submit the Lien Application cum Consent Letter with correct scheme details, folio numbers, ISINs, and unit values to their respective AMC/RTA.
- Ensure units are unencumbered and available as a free balance before applying for the lien.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per circular instructions.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the compliance process for Investment Advisers by allowing them to utilize liquid and overnight mutual fund investments to fulfill deposit requirements without liquidating holdings.
