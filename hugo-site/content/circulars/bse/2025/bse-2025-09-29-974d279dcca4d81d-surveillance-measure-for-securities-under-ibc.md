## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding surveillance measures for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective September 30, 2025. The circular details lists of securities shortlisted under various stages of the IBC framework, including entries, stage movements, exclusions, and a comprehensive consolidated list.

## Key Points

- **IBC Stage 1 Additions:** Polo Hotels Ltd and Tiaan Consumer Ltd have been shortlisted under Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies relating to IRP as per IBC Stage 1 w.e.f. September 30, 2025.
- **Exclusions:** Visa Steel Ltd has been excluded from ASM for companies as per IBC w.e.f. September 30, 2025.
- **Consolidated List:** Includes various companies under different stages of IRP/IBC, such as A.F. Enterprises Ltd, Aban Offshore Ltd, AGS Transact Technologies Ltd, Alchemist Ltd, Alps Industries Ltd, Ambition Mica Limited, Ankit Metal & Power Ltd, Ansal Properties & Infrastructure Ltd, Arshiya Limited, Axis Solutions Ltd, Baron Infotech Ltd, Birla Cotsyn (India) Ltd, and Bloom Dekor Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance and monitoring frameworks for companies undergoing corporate insolvency resolution processes as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC).

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and applicable trading restrictions for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2025
- **Circular Date:** September 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in specified securities will be subject to heightened surveillance and margin requirements corresponding to their assigned IBC/ASM stage.
