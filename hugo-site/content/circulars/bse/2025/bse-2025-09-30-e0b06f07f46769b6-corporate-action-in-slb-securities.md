## Summary

This BSE circular provides a schedule of corporate actions—such as dividends, stock splits, and bonus issues—affecting various securities in the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) segment for October 2025.

## Key Points

- **ICICI Lombard General Insurance Company Limited**: Dividend, Record/Ex Date: 23-October-2025, Shut Period: 21-Oct-2025 to 23-Oct-2025.
- **Rashtriya Chemicals & Fertilizers Limited**: Dividend, Record/Ex Date: 10-October-2025, Shut Period: 09-Oct-2025 to 10-Oct-2025.
- **Tata Investment Corporation Ltd.**: Stock Split, Record/Ex Date: 14-October-2025, Shut Period: 13-Oct-2025 to 14-Oct-2025.
- **Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd**: Dividend, Record/Ex Date: 03-October-2025, Shut Period: 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.
- **Shilpa Medicare Ltd.**: Bonus Issue, Record/Ex Date & Foreclosure Date: 03-October-2025, Shut Period: 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and intermediaries must account for the specified shut periods, record dates, and ex-dates for SLB transactions involving the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- **October 3, 2025**: Ex/Record/Foreclosure date for Glenmark Pharmaceuticals and Shilpa Medicare.
- **October 10, 2025**: Ex/Record date for Rashtriya Chemicals & Fertilizers.
- **October 14, 2025**: Ex/Record date for Tata Investment Corporation.
- **October 23, 2025**: Ex/Record date for ICICI Lombard General Insurance.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures smooth settlement and operational adjustments for participants engaged in securities lending and borrowing during corporate action events.
