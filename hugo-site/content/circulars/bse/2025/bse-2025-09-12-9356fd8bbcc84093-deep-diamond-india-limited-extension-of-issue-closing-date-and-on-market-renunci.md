## Summary

Deep Diamond India Limited has announced a revision to the schedule of its ongoing Rights Issue. The Rights Issue closing date has been extended from September 19, 2025, to September 24, 2025. Additionally, the on-market renunciation period for Rights Entitlements (RE) has been extended to September 18, 2025.

## Key Points

- Rights Issue closing date extended to Wednesday, September 24, 2025.
- Last date for On-Market Renunciation of Rights Entitlements extended to Thursday, September 18, 2025.
- Issue originally opened on September 9, 2025.
- Revised post-issue schedule includes Basis of Allotment on September 29, 2025, and Listing/Trading on October 7, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; purely a schedule extension approved by the Rights Issue Committee.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders wishing to participate or renounce their rights must adhere to the updated timeline ending on September 24, 2025, for application submission.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: September 9, 2025
- Last Date on Market Renunciation: September 18, 2025
- Issue Closing Date: September 24, 2025
- Basis of Allotment: September 29, 2025
- Initiation of Allotment: September 30, 2025
- Date of Credit: October 6, 2025
- Date of Listing / Trading: October 7, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Provides extended flexibility and additional time for shareholders to exercise their rights and trade entitlements under the Rights Issue.
