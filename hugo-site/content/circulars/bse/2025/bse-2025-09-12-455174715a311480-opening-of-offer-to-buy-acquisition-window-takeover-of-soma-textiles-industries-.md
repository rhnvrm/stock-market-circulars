## Summary

Roadway Solutions India Infra Limited (Acquirer) along with PAC 1 (Ameet Harjinder Gadhoke) and PAC 2 (Teja Ranade Gadhoke) is making an open offer to acquire up to 82,64,942 equity shares, representing 25.02% of the voting share capital of Soma Textiles & Industries Limited, at a cash offer price of INR 47.14 per share pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- **Acquirer:** Roadway Solutions India Infra Limited
- **Persons Acting in Concert (PACs):** Ameet Harjinder Gadhoke and Teja Ranade Gadhoke
- **Target Company:** Soma Textiles & Industries Limited
- **Offer Size:** Up to 82,64,942 equity shares (25.02% of voting share capital)
- **Offer Price:** INR 47.14/- per equity share payable in cash
- **Purpose:** Substantial acquisition of shares/voting rights accompanied with change in control and management of the Target Company under Regulations 3(1) and (4) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is conducted in accordance with the provisions of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011, and subsequent amendments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public shareholders wishing to tender their shares must follow the instructions detailed in the Letter of Offer and submit the Form of Acceptance cum Acknowledgement through their respective stockbrokers.

## Important Dates

- **Detailed Public Statement Date:** Tuesday, July 15, 2025 (Published on Wednesday, July 16, 2025)
- **Commencement of Tendering Period (Prior to):** Tuesday, September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Significant corporate action leading to potential change in management and control of Soma Textiles & Industries Limited, providing an exit opportunity for public shareholders at the specified offer price.
