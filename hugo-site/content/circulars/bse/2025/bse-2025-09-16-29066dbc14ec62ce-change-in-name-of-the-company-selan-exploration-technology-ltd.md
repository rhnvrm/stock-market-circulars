## Summary

Selan Exploration Technology Ltd has issued a notice regarding the change of its company name pursuant to a certificate of incorporation.

## Key Points

- Company name changed as per official certificate.
- Statutory requirement to display the old name along with the new name for a period of two years.

## Regulatory Changes

- None specific other than corporate identity change.

## Compliance Requirements

- The Company must display the old name for a period of two years along with its new name at all places in terms of Section 12 of the Act.

## Important Dates

- Certificate date: August 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Informational notice for stakeholders and market participants regarding corporate identity.
