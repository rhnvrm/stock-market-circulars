## Summary

Chandra Prabhu International Limited has announced the details regarding its bonus equity shares issuance approved in the 40th Annual General Meeting held on September 10, 2025. The company issued 92,45,000 bonus equity shares in the ratio of 1:2.

## Key Points

- Bonus Ratio: 1:2 (one bonus equity share of face value Rs. 2/- for every two fully paid-up equity shares held).
- Number of Bonus Shares: 92,45,000 equity shares.
- Distinctive Numbers: From 18490001 to 27735000.
- ISIN: INE368D01025.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Issued in compliance with SEBI Circular No. CIR/CFD/PoD/2024/122 dated September 16, 2024.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company must ensure credit of bonus shares in the depository system by 12:00 P.M. on the deemed date of allotment.
- The bonus shares must be made available for trading at the beginning of the day (BOD) on the effective listing date.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 26, 2025
- Deemed Date of Allotment: September 29, 2025
- Effective Listing Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity share capital of the company, enhancing liquidity and equity base distribution among eligible shareholders.
