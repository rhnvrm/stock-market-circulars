## Summary

SEBI issued the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2025, introducing key amendments regarding dematerialized issuance of securities following schemes of arrangement, sub-divisions, splits, or consolidations, alongside revised annual disclosure timelines for entities registered on Social Stock Exchanges.

## Key Points

- Mandates that listed entities issue securities strictly in demat form following any scheme of arrangement, sub-division, split, or consolidation.
- Separate demat accounts must be maintained by the listed entity for investors who do not hold demat accounts.
- Updates disclosure timelines and requirements for not-for-profit organizations and designated securities listed on Social Stock Exchanges.
- Modifies annual impact report guidelines and verification standards.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Regulation 39(2A Added):** Listed entities executing a scheme of arrangement, sub-division, split, or consolidation must issue securities only in demat form, creating separate accounts for non-demat holders.
- **Regulation 91C(1) Substituted:** Revised annual filing disclosures for Social Stock Exchange entities regarding financial and non-financial information.
- **Regulation 91E Amended:** Updated terminology and self-certification requirements for annual impact reports.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must ensure all post-scheme or split issuances comply strictly with demat-only rules.
- Social Stock Exchange listed entities must submit annual disclosures within specified timelines (by October 31 or income tax return due dates).

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately from the date of publication in the Official Gazette (September 8, 2025).

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines digital holding practices across corporate restructuring activities.
- Enhances transparency and governance for social enterprises listed on Social Stock Exchanges.
