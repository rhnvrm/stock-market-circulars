## Summary

The Registrar of Companies, Central Processing Centre, has issued a Certificate of Incorporation pursuant to the change of name for ITD Cementation India Limited to Cemindia Projects Limited, effective from August 19, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Old Name:** ITD Cementation India Limited
- **New Name:** Cemindia Projects Limited
- **Original Incorporation Name:** Cemindia Company Limited
- **Corporate Identification Number (CIN):** L61000MH1978PLC020435
- **Effective Date:** August 19, 2025
- **Registered Office / Mailing Address:** 9th Floor, Prima Bay, Tower - B, Gate No. 5, Saki Vihar Road, Powai, Mumbai, Mumbai City - 400072, Maharashtra, India

## Regulatory Changes

- The company's official corporate name has been legally updated on the records of the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA).

## Compliance Requirements

- As per Section 12 of the Companies Act, the company is statutorily obligated to display its old name along with its new name for a period of two years at all required places.
- Stakeholders and market participants must update their records and databases to reflect the new name for the company.

## Important Dates

- **Certificate Date:** August 19, 2025
- **Effective Date:** August 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal direct operational impact, but all future disclosures, trading systems, and documentation must reference Cemindia Projects Limited.
