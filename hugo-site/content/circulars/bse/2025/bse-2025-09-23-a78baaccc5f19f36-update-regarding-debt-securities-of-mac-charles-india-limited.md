## Summary

This circular outlines the updates regarding the terms and security charges associated with the debt securities and debentures of MAC Charles (India) Limited, detailing changes to charges over account assets, project immovable assets, and guarantees.

## Key Points

- Details updates on the nature of instruments and security arrangements for debentures.
- Replaces certain existing second-ranking equitable mortgages and guarantees with revised exclusive charges.
- Outlines new security features including deeds of hypothecation and first-ranking exclusive equitable mortgages by Hub SPV and SDPL.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification of terms relating to debenture security, release of prior guarantees, and creation of new exclusive charges by Hub SPV, SDPL, and the Company.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and relevant parties must ensure compliance with the updated terms and executed deeds of hypothecation/pledge agreements.

## Important Dates

- Effective as per the revised terms and relevant dates specified in the agreements (e.g., deeds dated 23 August 2022 and subsequent amendments).

## Impact Assessment

- Impacts the security structure and collateral backing of the debentures issued by MAC Charles (India) Limited.
