## Summary

Vashishtha Luxury Fashion Limited has announced the listing of its equity shares pursuant to an Initial Public Offering (IPO). The circular details the security categories, distinctive number ranges, lock-in periods, and demat status for the issued shares.

## Key Points

- Total issued and listed securities include various tranches of fully paid equity shares under IPO.
- Distinctive numbers range from 1 to 2,356,758 total.
- Specified lock-in periods apply to promoter and pre-IPO shares ranging from September 2025 through September 2028.
- 799,200 shares are listed as free (unlocked) under the IPO.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and depository participants must ensure proper maintenance of lock-in instructions and demat settlement terms as per exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in start dates begin from 03/09/2025 with expiration dates up to 12/09/2028.

## Impact Assessment

Routine listing update for market participants and investors regarding share availability and lock-in expirations.
