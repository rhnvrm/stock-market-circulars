## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Jain Resource Recycling Limited, detailing various lock-in periods for promoters, pre-IPO, and anchor investor shares following its public issue.

## Key Points

- Listing of equity shares of Jain Resource Recycling Limited.
- Specifies lock-in periods ranging from 30 days to multiple years for various tranches of shares.
- Anchor investor shares (1,21,22,841 shares) locked in for 30 days up to October 28, 2025.
- Anchor investor shares (1,21,22,848 shares) locked in for 90 days up to December 27, 2025.
- 2,96,33,620 shares categorized as Fully Paid-up and Not Under Lock-in (IPO Public).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must adhere to the lock-in periods specified for respective equity shares under the IPO guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Circular / Allotment:** 29-Sep-2025
- **Anchor Lock-in (30 Days):** 28-Oct-2025
- **Anchor Lock-in (90 Days):** 27-Dec-2025
- **Other Lock-in Expirations:** Up to 30-Mar-2027

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding the free float and locked-in shares of Jain Resource Recycling Limited post-IPO.
