## Summary

BSE sought clarification from various companies regarding a significant increase in the volume of their securities across exchanges. The companies have responded confirming that the volume movements are purely market-driven and that they are in strict compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Exchanges sought clarification on unusual volume movements for companies including Tinna Rubber And Infrastructure Limited and AWL Agri Business Limited.
- Companies confirmed they have disclosed all necessary events and price-sensitive information under Regulation 30.
- No pending announcements or material developments exist that would explain the price or volume movements.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Continued adherence to disclosure obligations under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Date of Clarification: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No operational or financial impact on the companies; purely routine compliance disclosures.
