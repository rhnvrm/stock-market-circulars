## Summary

Eco Hotels and Resorts Limited has announced an extension of its ongoing rights issue closing date from Wednesday, September 17, 2025, to Monday, September 22, 2025. The decision was approved by the Rights Issue Committee to offer shareholders more time to exercise their rights under the offering.

## Key Points

- Original Issue Closing Date: Wednesday, September 17, 2025
- Revised Issue Closing Date: Monday, September 22, 2025
- Scrip Code/ID: 514402/ECOHOTELS
- ISINs: INE638N01012 (Equity), INE638N20012 (RE), IN9638N01010 (PP)

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Eligible shareholders must submit their duly filled Composite Application Forms (CAF) along with the application amount by the revised closing date of Monday, September 22, 2025.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: September 8, 2025
- Revised Issue Closing Date: September 22, 2025
- Finalization of Basis of Allotment: September 25, 2025
- Date of Allotment: September 26, 2025
- Date of Listing / Trading: October 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides flexibility and extended timeline for eligible investors to subscribe to the rights issue of Eco Hotels and Resorts Limited.
