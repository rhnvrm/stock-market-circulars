## Summary

SEBI issued circular SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131 on September 25, 2025, detailing compliance guidelines for the earlier circular on digital accessibility under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. The guidelines outline submission mechanisms, timelines, and reporting authorities for various Regulated Entities (REs).

## Key Points

- Applicable to all Regulated Entities (REs) required to directly submit compliance under the digital accessibility mandate.
- Establishes a clear timeline for listing digital platforms, appointing certified accessibility professionals, conducting audits, and remediating findings.
- Specifies reporting authorities: Stock Brokers/Depository Participants report to Stock Exchanges/Depositories; Investment Advisors (IAs) and Research Analysts (RAs) report to BSE Ltd.; MIIs and other REs report directly to SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of compliance milestones related to digital platform accessibility for persons with disabilities.
- Standardized reporting via email (`digital_acc@sebi.gov.in`) and respective supervisory bodies using specific formats.

## Compliance Requirements

- REs must submit a list of digital platforms provided for investors.
- Appointment of IAAP certified accessibility professionals as auditors.
- Conduct initial and annual accessibility audits, remediate findings, and submit consolidated reports within specified windows.

## Important Dates

- **September 30, 2025:** Submission of list of digital platforms provided by REs.
- **December 14, 2025:** Appointment of IAAP certified accessibility professionals as Auditor.
- **April 30, 2026:** Conduct of Accessibility Audit for digital platforms.
- **July 31, 2026:** Remediation of audit findings and ensuring full compliance.
- **April 30, 2027:** Submission of annual accessibility audit reports (within 30 days of each financial year end).

## Impact Assessment

Regulated entities must immediately audit their digital interfaces, engage certified professionals, and establish tracking mechanisms to meet strict regulatory timelines for digital accessibility compliance.
