## Summary

SEBI issued an order against Milani Techno Engineering Limited (MTEL) and its directors regarding unauthorized fund mobilization amounting to ₹ 4,12,20,000/- through the issue of Redeemable Cumulative Preference Shares (RCPS) during FY 2011-12 without complying with relevant provisions of the Companies Act and SEBI Act.

## Key Points

- MTEL raised ₹ 4,12,20,000/- via RCPS from the public during FY 2011-12.
- The offer was made to more than 49 persons without complying with public issue norms.
- Proceedings under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11A, and 11B(1) of the SEBI Act, 1992 were initiated against the company and its directors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of regulations concerning illegal public issuances and collective investment/fund mobilization schemes under the SEBI Act and Companies Act.

## Compliance Requirements

- Companies must comply with public issue norms and private placement rules under the Companies Act and SEBI regulations.
- Issuers must provide refund options with applicable interest where offerings violate investor protection norms.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: 2025-09-30 (corresponding to BSE dissemination date/fiscal period parameters)
- Financial Year of Violation: 2011-2012

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on unlisted companies engaging in unauthorized public fund mobilization through preference shares or similar instruments.
