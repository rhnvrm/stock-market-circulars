## Summary

SEBI has issued guidelines specifying the mechanism for monitoring compliance with the Minimum Investment Threshold under Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs). In the event of an active breach—where an investor's aggregate investment falls below INR 10 lakh due to investor-initiated transactions—all units held across strategies will be frozen for debit, followed by a 30-day notice to rebalance. Failure to rebalance within the notice period results in automatic redemption of the frozen units.

## Key Points

- Active breach is defined as a drop below the INR 10 lakh threshold due to investor-initiated transactions (redemption, transfer, sale, etc.).
- Upon an active breach, all units of the investor across strategies of the SIF are frozen for debit.
- Investors are given a 30-calendar-day notice period to rebalance their investments.
- If un-rebalanced after 30 days, frozen units are automatically redeemed by the AMC on the next immediate business day.
- Provisions come into force with immediate effect from the date of the circular (July 29, 2025).

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduces standardized monitoring protocols and operational workflows for AMCs, RTAs, and Depositories regarding SIF minimum investment thresholds.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMCs, RTAs, and Depositories must implement necessary system updates to track daily SIF investments, apply debit freezes upon active breaches, trigger 30-day notifications, and execute automatic redemptions if unaddressed.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** July 29, 2025
- **Rebalancing Notice Period:** 30 calendar days from notice issuance

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances regulatory compliance enforcement for Specialized Investment Funds.
- Protects fund structure integrity while introducing strict operational timelines for investors holding below-threshold balances.
