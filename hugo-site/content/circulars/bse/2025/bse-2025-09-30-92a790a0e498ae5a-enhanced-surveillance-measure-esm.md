## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, outlining securities newly shortlisted, moved to higher stages, and the consolidated list effective from October 01, 2025.

## Key Points

- New shortlisting of securities under the ESM framework (Annexure I, Part A) effective October 01, 2025.
- Movement of existing securities to higher stages within the ESM framework.
- Consolidated list of securities under various stages of the ESM framework provided for market participant reference.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities identified under Annexure I are subjected to enhanced surveillance and trading restrictions as per the ESM framework guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, trading members, and investors must take note of the ESM stages and associated trading safeguards for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced surveillance measures increase margin requirements and restrict trading frequency or leverage for the affected securities, potentially impacting their liquidity and short-term price discovery.
