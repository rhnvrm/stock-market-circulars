## Summary

BSE has issued contract specifications and parameters for the launch of WTI Crude Oil Options on Futures contracts. The contract will be available under the symbol WTICRUDE, offering European Call and Put options with underlying WTI Crude Oil Futures traded on BSE.

## Key Points

- **Symbol:** WTICRUDE
- **Option Type:** European Call & Put Options
- **Trading Unit:** One BSE WTI Crude Oil futures contract
- **Strike Prices:** 25 In-the-money, 25 Out-of-the-money, and 1 Near-the-money (51 CE and 51 PE strikes with Rs. 50 intervals)
- **Tick Size:** Rs. 0.10
- **Margin System:** Computed using SPAN software with a Price Scan Range of 3.5 standard deviations (3.5 sigma) and Volatility Scan Range (VSR) of minimum 5%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishment of standard contract parameters, pricing models (Black 76 option pricing model), and risk management rules for WTI Crude Oil Options on Futures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing members and trading members must ensure compliance with ICCL margin requirements, including upfront premium blocking and real-time SPAN margin computation across multiple daily intervals.

## Important Dates

- Contracts will be available as per the Contract Launch Calendar starting from the business day immediately after the expiry of the near month futures contract.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants with expanded hedging and trading tools within the energy commodities derivatives segment.
