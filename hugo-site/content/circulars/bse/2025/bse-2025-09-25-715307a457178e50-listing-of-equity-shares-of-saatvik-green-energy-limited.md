## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Saatvik Green Energy Limited, detailing the lock-in periods and categories for various share allocations following its initial public offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Total equity shares listed: 12,71,05,004.
- Various lock-in schedules specified for promoter shares, pre-IPO shares, and anchor investors.
- Anchor investor shares are locked-in for 30 days (up to 23rd October 2025) and 90 days (up to 22nd December 2025).
- Certain shares have lock-in periods extending up to 25th September 2028.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to the lock-in periods specified for respective equity share categories as per the company's prospectus.

## Important Dates

- Listing / Allotment effective dates starting from 24-Sep-2025.
- Anchor lock-in expiry dates: 23-Oct-2025 and 22-Dec-2025.
- Final lock-in expiry date: 25-Sep-2028.

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public trading of Saatvik Green Energy Limited shares while ensuring compliance with regulatory lock-in requirements for pre-IPO and anchor investors.
