## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 121/2025-2026 outlines upcoming book-closure dates, record dates, and payment schedules for interest and redemption of non-convertible debentures (NCDs) for various issuers including Aethon Developers Private Limited and Annapurna Finance Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Covers book-closure and record dates spanning from October 2025 through December 2025.
- Details private placed non-convertible debentures (NCDs) payment of interest and principal redemptions.
- Applicable to issuers such as Aethon Developers Private Limited and Annapurna Finance Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and depositories must ensure accurate record-keeping and timely processing of interest payments and redemptions based on the stated record dates.

## Important Dates

- Record/Book Closure Dates: Between 07/10/2025 and 30/12/2025 depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification for debt market participants regarding scheduled interest payments and part/full redemptions.
