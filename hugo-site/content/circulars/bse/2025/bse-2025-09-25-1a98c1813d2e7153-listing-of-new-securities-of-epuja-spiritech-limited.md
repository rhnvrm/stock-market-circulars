## Summary

BSE has notified the listing of new equity shares of Epuja Spiritech Limited issued to various allottees under preferential allotment, detailing the number of shares, post-issue holding, lock-in periods, and distinctive numbers.

## Key Points

- Company: Epuja Spiritech Limited
- Details allottees, new shares allotted, post-issue holding, and lock-in expiration dates up to April 2026.
- Specifies distinctive number (DN) ranges for the newly allotted securities.

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to lock-in periods specified for the respective allottees.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in expiry dates: 30/03/2026 and 01/04/2026.

## Impact Assessment

Routine equity issuance and listing for the company, increasing its paid-up capital.
