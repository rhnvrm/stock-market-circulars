## Summary

BSE sought clarifications from listed companies regarding significant increases in the volume of their securities. In response, Tinna Rubber And Infrastructure Limited and AWL Agri Business Limited confirmed that they have complied with all disclosure requirements under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, and that the volume movement is purely market-driven.

## Key Points

- Exchanges sought clarification on unusual volume movements for Tinna Rubber And Infrastructure Limited and AWL Agri Business Limited.
- Both companies confirmed adherence to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- Companies stated there are no pending announcements or material developments that could impact security prices or volumes.
- Volume and price movements are reported to be purely market-driven.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must respond to exchange queries regarding market movements in a timely manner under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No operational or financial impact on the companies or markets; standard compliance disclosure.
