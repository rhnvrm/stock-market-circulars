## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, outlining securities shortlisted for ESM, securities moving to higher or lower stages, and securities moving out of the framework effective September 29, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities into the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework.
- Movement of existing securities to respective higher or lower stages within the ESM framework.
- Removal of specified securities from the ESM framework.
- Effective date for all listed changes is September 29, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) guidelines to ensure market integrity and investor protection.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance status changes and apply appropriate trading restrictions and margin requirements as mandated by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities will experience modified trading conditions, margins, and surveillance controls corresponding to their respective ESM stages.
