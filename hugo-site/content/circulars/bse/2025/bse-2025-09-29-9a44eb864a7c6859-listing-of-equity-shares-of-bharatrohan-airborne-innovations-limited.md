## Summary

Bharatrohan Airborne Innovations Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern disclosure pursuant to Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, following the listing of its equity shares on the exchange.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: Bharatrohan Airborne Innovations Limited
- Scrip Code/Name: BHAI (Equity Shares)
- Total No. of Shareholders: 863
- Total No. of Equity Shares: 19,923,020
- Promoter & Promoter Group Holding: 8,022,000 shares (40.26%)
- Public Holding: 11,901,020 shares (59.74%)

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a standard statutory disclosure of shareholding structure.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to submit shareholding patterns under Regulation 31 specifying details of promoter holdings, public shareholding, locked-in shares, and pledged shares as applicable.

- Filing Date: 25/07/2025
