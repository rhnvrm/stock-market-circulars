## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding measures for companies with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance pursuant to Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011, effective September 23, 2025.

## Key Points

- Annexure I lists securities shortlisted under the encumbrance measure (Nil for this revision).
- Annexure II details securities moving out of the framework, including Tulsyan NEC Ltd (Scrip Code: 513629), noting transitions due to inclusion in the LT ASM framework.
- Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities under the framework, including DJS Stock & Shares Ltd, Future Market Networks Ltd, Hindusthan National Glass & Industries Ltd, Thyrocare Technologies Ltd, and Tourism Finance Corporation of India Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Updates to the list of securities under high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance as per SEBI (SAST) Regulation 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and listed entities must take note of the inclusion and exclusion of securities from the encumbrance measure framework.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading and surveillance monitoring for specified scrips subject to high encumbrance reporting and framework controls.
