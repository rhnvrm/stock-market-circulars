## Summary

SEBI has extended the implementation timeline and introduced a milestone-based glide path for the framework on 'Safer participation of retail investors in Algorithmic trading', allowing stock brokers more time to update their systems while establishing strict compliance deadlines.

## Key Points

- Stock brokers ready with required systems go live from October 01, 2025.
- A structured glide path with explicit milestones is provided for brokers requiring more time.
- Milestone 1 (October 31, 2025): Application for registration of retail algo products and at least one strategy.
- Milestone 2 (November 30, 2025): Completion of registration for retail algo products and strategies.
- Milestone 3 (January 03, 2026): Participation in at least one mock session with complete functionality.
- Non-compliance will result in being barred from onboarding new retail clients for API-based algo trading from January 05, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of the implementation timeline from the previous October 01, 2025 deadline through a milestone-based glide path.
- Full applicability of the framework for all stock brokers set for April 01, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock brokers must adhere to the defined milestones for strategy registration and mock session participation.
- Brokers not ready by October 01, 2025, must provide client count information as of September 30, 2025.
- Stock exchanges are directed to monitor compliance, update rules, and notify members.

## Important Dates

- October 01, 2025: Initial go-live date for ready brokers.
- October 31, 2025: Milestone 1 deadline (strategy application).
- November 30, 2025: Milestone 2 deadline (registration completion).
- January 03, 2026: Milestone 3 deadline (mock session participation).
- January 05, 2026: Penalty enforcement (bar on onboarding new API algo clients for non-compliant brokers).
- April 01, 2026: Full and mandatory applicability for all stock brokers.

## Impact Assessment

Provides significant operational relief to stock brokers and algo vendors by allowing a phased rollout, reducing immediate technical compliance pressure while setting firm accountability checkpoints.
