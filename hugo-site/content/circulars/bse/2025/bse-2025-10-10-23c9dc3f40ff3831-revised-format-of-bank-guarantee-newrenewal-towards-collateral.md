## Summary

BSE has released a revised format for Bank Guarantees (New/Renewal) submitted towards collateral by members.

## Key Points

- Revised standard documentation format for bank guarantees.
- Applicable for new submissions and renewals towards member collateral.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the acceptable legal wording and clauses for bank guarantees issued by scheduled commercial banks in favor of the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure that all future bank guarantees (new or renewed) are executed strictly in accordance with the revised format provided by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for new bank guarantees and renewals.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational alignment for members and their issuing banks to adopt the updated format.
