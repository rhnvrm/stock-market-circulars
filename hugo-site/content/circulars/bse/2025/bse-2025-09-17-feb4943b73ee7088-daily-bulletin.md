## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 117/2025-2026 dated September 17, 2025, outlines upcoming book closures, record dates, dividend declarations, interest payments, and redemption schedules for various listed entities including equities, privately placed non-convertible debentures (NCDs), and commercial papers.

## Key Points

- **3i Infotech Limited**: Announced a record date of 26/09/2025 for a Rights Issue of Equity Shares.
- **Atam Villages Limited**: Declared a final dividend of Rs. 0.8500 per share (8.5%) with a record date of 18/09/2025.
- **Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited**: Scheduled record dates for interest payments on privately placed non-convertible debentures.
- **Ambium Finserve Limited**: Multiple record dates listed for interest payments and bond redemptions across various tranches through 2025 and 2026.
- **Axis Securities Limited**: Commercial paper redemptions scheduled for October 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a routine operational bulletin issued by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure timely corporate action execution and accurate record date submissions in compliance with exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **18/09/2025**: Record Date for Atam Valves Limited dividend.
- **26/09/2025**: Record Date for 3i Infotech Limited rights issue.
- **06/10/2025 & 07/10/2025**: Record Dates for Akara Capital Advisors NCD interest payments.
- **23/10/2025 & 24/10/2025**: Record Dates for Axis Securities Commercial Paper redemptions.

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to investors regarding corporate actions, interest payments, and debt redemptions, ensuring proper settlement and trading suspension flags.
