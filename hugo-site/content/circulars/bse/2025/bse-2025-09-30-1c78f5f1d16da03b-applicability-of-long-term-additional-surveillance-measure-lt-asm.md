## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective October 01, 2025, several securities have been shortlisted under the framework or have had their surveillance stages modified.

## Key Points

- **Part A:** Lists securities newly shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework effective October 01, 2025, including Capital Trade Links Ltd, Gourmet Gateway India Ltd, Mangalam Industrial Finance Ltd, Natura Hue Chem Ltd, and Ratnabhumi Developers Ltd.
- **Part B & C:** Securities moving to higher or lower stages of ASM (Nil for this notice).
- **Part D:** Direct stage IV shortlisting details.
- **Annexure III:** Provides the consolidated list of securities continuing in the LT-ASM framework with their respective stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance measures and trading controls on specified listed entities to protect market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable margin requirements and surveillance constraints for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements (often 100% applicable margin) for securities listed under the LT-ASM framework, which may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
