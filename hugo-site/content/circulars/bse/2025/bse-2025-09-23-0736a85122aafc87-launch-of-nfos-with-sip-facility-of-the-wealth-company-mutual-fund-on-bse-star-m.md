## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the launch of New Fund Offers (NFOs) with Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facilities for schemes offered by The Wealth Company Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform. The tentative allotment date for these schemes is October 14, 2025.

## Key Points

- Availability of Arbitrage Fund and Ethical Fund schemes under The Wealth Company Mutual Fund.
- Includes both regular and direct plans with growth and IDCW (payout/reinvestment) options.
- Tentative Allotment Date for all listed schemes is set for October 14, 2025.
- Accessible via the BSE StAR MF Platform with SIP facility enabled.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors must ensure correct ISIN usage and adherence to platform guidelines for NFO and SIP processing.

## Important Dates

- Allotment Date: October 14, 2025 (Tentative)

## Impact Assessment

Expands investment choices for participants and investors utilizing the BSE StAR MF platform by incorporating new fund offerings from The Wealth Company Mutual Fund.
