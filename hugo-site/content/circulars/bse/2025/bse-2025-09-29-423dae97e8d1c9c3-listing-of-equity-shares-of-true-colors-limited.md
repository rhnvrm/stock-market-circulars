## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of equity shares of True Colors Limited pursuant to its Initial Public Offering (IPO), detailing the distribution, lock-in periods, and security categories.

## Key Points

- Total securities listed include various tranches of fully paid equity shares under IPO, OFS, and anchor investor categories.
- Lock-in dates range from September 2025 through September 2028 depending on the tranche.
- All securities are issued in dematerialized (DEMAT) form.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and participants must ensure adherence to the specified lock-in periods and trading restrictions for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Listing and lock-in commencement dates start from September 22, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

Standard market admission for new equity securities with no broader systemic impact.
