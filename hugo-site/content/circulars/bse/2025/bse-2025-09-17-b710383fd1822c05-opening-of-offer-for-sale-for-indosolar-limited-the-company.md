## Summary

Waaree Energies Limited (the Promoter/Seller) has issued a notice for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of up to 61,00,000 equity shares of face value ₹10 each of Indosolar Limited, representing 14.66% of the total paid-up equity share capital. The OFS will take place on September 18, 2025, for non-retail investors and September 19, 2025, for retail investors.

## Key Points

- **Seller:** Waaree Energies Limited (Promoter)
- **Company:** Indosolar Limited
- **Total Sale Shares:** Up to 61,00,000 equity shares (14.66% of total paid-up equity share capital)
- **T-Day (Non-Retail Investors):** September 18, 2025
- **T+1 Day (Retail Investors):** September 19, 2025
- **Objective:** Achieving the minimum public shareholding in the Company as prescribed under Rule 19A(5) of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Rules, 1957 and Regulation 38 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The OFS is conducted in compliance with SEBI master circulars and stock exchange operational guidelines for Offer for Sale.

## Compliance Requirements

Bidders, prospective investors, and their brokers must review the entire notice and abide by SEBI and Stock Exchange OFS guidelines before participating.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 17, 2025
- **T-Day (Non-Retail):** September 18, 2025
- **T+1 Day (Retail):** September 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

This corporate action impacts the shareholding distribution of Indosolar Limited by increasing public shareholding to meet regulatory compliance thresholds.
