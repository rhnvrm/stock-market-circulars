## Summary

BSE has issued a list of securities moving into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, detailing specific security codes, ISINs, and their respective new GSM stages.

## Key Points

- Pasupati Fincap Ltd., Integra Switchgear Ltd., Span Divergent Limited, City Online Services Limited, and Dalmia Industrial Development Limited have moved to GSM Stage I.
- Gayatri Projects Ltd. has moved to GSM Stage III.
- Framework notes detail rules for securities moving lower due to inclusion in ESM or IBC frameworks.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework for listed entities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must note the trading restrictions and additional surveillance margins applicable to securities placed under various GSM stages.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities may experience restrictions due to enhanced surveillance measures.
