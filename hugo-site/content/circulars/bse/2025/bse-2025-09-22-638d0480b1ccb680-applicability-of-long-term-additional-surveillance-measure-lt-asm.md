## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The circular lists securities shortlisted under the framework, those moving to higher stages, and those moving out of the framework effective September 23, 2025.

## Key Points

- Part A lists securities newly shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework effective September 23, 2025, including Axiscades Technologies Ltd, Gallops Enterprise Ltd, Megasoft Ltd, Sikozy Realtors Ltd, Softrak Venture Investment Ltd, SSPN Finance Ltd, TCM Ltd, Triton Valves Ltd, TruCap Finance Ltd, and Tulsyan NEC Ltd.
- Part B details securities continuing in the framework but moving to higher Stage ASM (e.g., Gujarat Hy-Spin Ltd moving to Stage II).
- Part II notes securities moving out of the LT-ASM Framework, such as Caspian Corporate Services Ltd due to inclusion in the ESM Framework.
- Part III provides a consolidated list of securities currently under various ASM stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including margin requirements and price bands for shortlisted securities under the LT-ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable ASM stages and corresponding margin requirements for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affects liquidity and trading dynamics of the listed scrips due to increased surveillance and margin obligations.
