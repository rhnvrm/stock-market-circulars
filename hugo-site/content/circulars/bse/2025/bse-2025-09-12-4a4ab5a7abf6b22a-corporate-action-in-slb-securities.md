## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding corporate actions (dividends) in Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) securities, specifying book closure dates, ex-dates, and shut periods for companies including BEML Ltd, Sanghvi Movers Ltd., and Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited.

## Key Points

- **BEML Ltd**: Dividend corporate action with Ex-Date and Book Closure/Record Date on 22-September-2025; Shut Period from 19-Sep-2025 to 22-Sep-2025.
- **Sanghvi Movers Ltd.**: Dividend corporate action with Ex-Date and Book Closure/Record Date on 12-September-2025; Shut Period from 11-Sep-2025 to 12-Sep-2025.
- **Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited**: Dividend corporate action with Ex-Date and Book Closure/Record Date on 22-September-2025; Shut Period from 19-Sep-2025 to 22-Sep-2025.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard operational schedule update for corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the specified shut periods and record dates for SLB transactions.

## Important Dates

- **11-Sep-2025 to 12-Sep-2025**: Shut period for Sanghvi Movers Ltd.
- **12-September-2025**: Record Date / Ex-Date for Sanghvi Movers Ltd.
- **19-Sep-2025 to 22-Sep-2025**: Shut period for BEML Ltd and Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited.
- **22-September-2025**: Record Date / Ex-Date for BEML Ltd and Gujarat Fluorochemicals Limited.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal operational impact limited to adjustments in the SLB segment for the listed securities during their respective corporate action windows.
