## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the imposition of additional exposure margins on securities that have crossed the prescribed threshold under the Market Wide Position Limit (MWPL).

## Key Points

- Additional exposure margins are applied to securities exceeding MWPL limits to manage market risk and volatility.
- Security affected: RBL Bank Limited (ISIN: INE976G01028, Scrip Code: 540065).

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of additional exposure margins for specified securities under MWPL surveillance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must maintain adequate margin collateral for positions in the affected security in accordance with the revised requirements.

## Important Dates

- Effective date: September 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements for trading in RBL Bank Limited derivatives or applicable segments, potentially impacting position sizing and leverage.
