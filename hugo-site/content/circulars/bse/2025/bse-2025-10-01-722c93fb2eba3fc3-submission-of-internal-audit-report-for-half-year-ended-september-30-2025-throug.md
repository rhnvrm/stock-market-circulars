## Summary

Submission of Internal Audit Report for the half year ended September 30, 2025, through BEFS (BSE Electronic Filing System), outlining verification areas such as client registration documentation, Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance, and UCC database updates.

## Key Points

- Mandates verification of client registration documents and AML compliance.
- Requires correct and updated KYC details to be uploaded in the UCC database of the exchange matching with KRA/C-KYC databases.
- Prohibits clauses in client documents that dilute member responsibility or conflict with mandatory rules.
- Verification of in-person verification procedures carried out by employees or authorized persons.
- Periodic tracking of changes in client information (address, bank, demat accounts) and updates in back office and databases.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standardized internal audit guidelines and reporting formats for trading members regarding client registration and KYC maintenance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members must ensure all client registration files, KYC documents, and financial information are properly verified and retrievable.
- Changes in client details must be duly notified in writing and updated across exchange UCC, KRA, and CKYCR databases.

## Important Dates

- Audit Period covers the half year ended September 30, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures robust compliance with SEBI and exchange guidelines regarding client onboarding, verification, and database hygiene.
