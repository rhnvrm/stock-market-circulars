## Summary

Telge Projects Limited has successfully completed its anchor investor allocation, securing ₹7.70 crore by allocating 7,33,200 equity shares at an issue price of ₹105 per share to 6 anchor investors prior to the opening of its public issue.

## Key Points

- Total amount raised from anchor investors: ₹7.70 Crore
- Total equity shares allotted: 7,33,200 shares
- Anchor allocation price: ₹105 per share
- Number of anchor investors: 6
- Allocation represents approximately 28.26% of the total issue size.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard procedures for SME public issue anchor allocation under SEBI (ICDR) Regulations were followed.

## Compliance Requirements

Anchor investors are subject to a statutory lock-in period, where 50% of the allocated shares are locked for 30 days and the remaining 50% are locked for 90 days from the date of allotment.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 24, 2025
- Public Issue Opening Date: September 25, 2025
- Public Issue Closing Date: September 29, 2025
- Expected Listing Date: October 3, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Successful anchor allocation signals healthy institutional interest and provides baseline market support ahead of the full public issue opening on the BSE SME platform.
