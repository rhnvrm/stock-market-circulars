## Summary

Trade Wings Limited has provided its company profile and details in connection with the revocation of suspension in trading of its equity shares on the BSE (Scrip Code: 509953, ISIN: INE961E01017).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Trade Wings Limited
- Scrip Code: 509953
- ISIN: INE961E01017
- Registered Address: 1st Floor, Naik Building, Opposite Don Bosco High School, M. G. Road, Panjim, Goa 403001
- Operational Status: Fully Operational
- Nature of Business: Pioneer in the Indian tourism and hospitality industry, facilitating travel conveniences, foreign exchange, cargo, domestic/international ticketing, and hotel accommodation.

## Regulatory Changes

- Suspension of trading in the equity shares of the company has been revoked, bringing the scrip back to active trading status.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company continues to adhere to listing regulations and statutory compliance, maintaining current auditors and corporate governance structures.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice/Submission: 11th August 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders and investors can resume buying and selling equity shares of Trade Wings Limited on the BSE exchange platform.
