## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Effective September 26, 2025, several securities have been shortlisted for the ESM framework or moved to higher/lower stages accordingly.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities into the ESM framework effective September 26, 2025.
- Movement of existing securities to respective higher stages within the ESM framework.
- Provision of a comprehensive consolidated list of securities under the ESM framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of heightened surveillance controls and trading restrictions on specified scrips as per regulatory guidelines governing the Enhanced Surveillance Measure framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members are required to note the list of securities under the ESM framework and ensure compliance with applicable trading margins and settlement conditions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, potentially impacting liquidity and requiring higher margin deposits from investors.
