## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Euro Pratik Sales Limited, detailing lock-in quantities, distinctive numbers, security types, lock-in dates, and depository status (Demat).

## Key Points

- Details total equity shares and corresponding lock-in periods under various distinctive number ranges.
- Specifies fully paid-up shares under lock-in as well as shares not under lock-in (IPO Public).
- Lock-in expiry dates range from October 2025 to March 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Administrative listing notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuing company and depositories must ensure compliance with lock-in restrictions and release schedules.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in start dates begin from 19-Sep-2025.
- Lock-in end dates extend through 18-Oct-2025, 17-Dec-2025, and 20-Mar-2026/2027.

## Impact Assessment

- Routine equity listing and lock-in setup with no direct market-wide impact.
