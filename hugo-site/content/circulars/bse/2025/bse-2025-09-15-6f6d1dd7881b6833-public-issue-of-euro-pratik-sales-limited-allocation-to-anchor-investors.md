## Summary

Euro Pratik Sales Limited has finalized the allocation of 54,64,554 Equity Shares to anchor investors at an Anchor Investor Allocation Price of ₹247 per share (including a share premium of ₹246 per share) for its upcoming initial public offering.

## Key Points

- Total Equity Shares allocated to anchor investors: 54,64,554.
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: ₹247 per Equity Share.
- Total amount allocated: ₹1,34,97,44,838.
- Out of the total allocation, 14,17,080 Equity Shares (25.93%) were allocated to 2 domestic mutual funds through 2 schemes.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with SEBI ICDR Regulations regarding anchor investor allocations and lock-in periods.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allocation: September 15, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

Provides visibility into institutional demand and anchor investor participation for the Euro Pratik Sales Limited IPO.
