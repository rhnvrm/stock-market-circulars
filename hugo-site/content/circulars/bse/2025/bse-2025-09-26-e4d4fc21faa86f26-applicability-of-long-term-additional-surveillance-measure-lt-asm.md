## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective September 29, 2025. The circular details short-listed securities, stage migrations, and companies moving out of the LT-ASM framework.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of securities in the Long Term ASM Framework (Part A) effective September 29, 2025, including Natural Capsules Ltd, Raj Packaging Industries Ltd, and Unishire Urban Infra Ltd.
- Movement of existing securities to higher or lower Stage ASM (Parts B & C).
- Removal of multiple securities from the Long Term ASM Framework (Annexure II), some of which transitioned to GSM, ESM, or Trade for Trade frameworks.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are subjected to enhanced surveillance and margin requirements under the Long Term ASM framework to protect market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable ASM stages and corresponding margin requirements for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trading restrictions apply to securities listed in the framework, affecting liquidity and short-term trading for the specified scrips.
