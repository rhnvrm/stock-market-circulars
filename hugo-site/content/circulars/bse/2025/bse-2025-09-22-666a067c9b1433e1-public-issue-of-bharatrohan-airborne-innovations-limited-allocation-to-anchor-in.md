## Summary

BharatRohan Airborne Innovations Limited has announced the successful allocation of 14,75,200 equity shares to anchor investors at the upper price band of ₹85 per share, successfully raising ₹12.54 crore ahead of its public issue opening.

## Key Points

- Total funds raised from anchor investors: ₹12.54 crore.
- Total shares allotted: 14,75,200 equity shares.
- Anchor allotment price: ₹85 per share (upper price band).
- Participating anchor funds include Mavira Growth Opportunities Fund, Nova Global Opportunities Fund PCC – Touchstone, Zeal Global Opportunities Fund, Aarth AIF Growth Fund, and Eminence Global Fund PCC – Eublia Capital Partners.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard procedures for SME IPO anchor investor allocations were followed in compliance with SEBI ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Lock-in restrictions apply to the anchor investor shares: 50% of the shares are locked in for 30 days, and the remaining 50% are locked in for 90 days from the date of allotment.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 22, 2025
- Listing Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Reflects healthy institutional participation and demand for the agritech sector SME public issue.
