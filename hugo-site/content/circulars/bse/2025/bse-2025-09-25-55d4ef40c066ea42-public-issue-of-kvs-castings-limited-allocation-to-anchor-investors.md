## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors as part of the public issue of KVS Castings Limited. The circular outlines the details of the anchor investor bidding, number of shares allocated, issue price, and lock-in requirements.

## Key Points

- Allocation of equity shares to anchor investors for the public issue of KVS Castings Limited.
- Details regarding institutional investor participation, number of shares allotted, and price per share.
- Compliance with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Adherence to existing SEBI ICDR regulations regarding anchor investor allocations in public issues.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and merchant bankers must ensure proper lock-in periods and statutory disclosures for anchor investors as per SEBI guidelines.
- Designated stock exchange reporting requirements.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Investor Bidding Date: As per issue schedule
- Issue Opening/Closing Dates: As specified in the prospectus

## Impact Assessment

- Provides transparency on institutional demand and anchor book subscription for KVS Castings Limited's public issue.
