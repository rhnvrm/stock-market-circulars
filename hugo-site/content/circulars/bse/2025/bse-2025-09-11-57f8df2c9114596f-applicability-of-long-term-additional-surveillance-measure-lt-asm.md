## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The circular lists securities shortlisted in the framework, those moving across stages, and securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework effective September 12, 2025.

## Key Points

- New securities shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework (Annexure I, Part A) effective September 12, 2025, include Chennai Meenakshi Multispeciality Hospital Ltd, Concord Control Systems Ltd, Jindal Leasefin Ltd, Promact Impex Ltd, and Vama Industries Ltd.
- Securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework (Annexure II) include Wardwizard Foods and Beverages Ltd.
- Consolidated lists of securities under various stages of the LT-ASM framework are detailed in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures aimed at enhancing market integrity and safeguarding investors from excessive price volatility in specific scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements (such as 100% margins or trade-for-trade settlement where applicable) for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified scrips will be subject to enhanced surveillance mechanisms, which may affect liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities.
