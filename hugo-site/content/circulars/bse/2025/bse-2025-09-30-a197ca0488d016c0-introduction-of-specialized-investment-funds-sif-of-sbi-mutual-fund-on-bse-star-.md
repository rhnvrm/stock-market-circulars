## Summary

SEBI has issued guidelines specifying the mechanism for monitoring compliance with the Minimum Investment Threshold under Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs). In the event of an active breach where an investor's aggregate investment falls below the INR 10 lakh threshold, all units across investment strategies of the concerned SIF are frozen for debit, followed by a 30-day rebalancing window. Failure to rebalance results in automatic redemption by the AMC.

## Key Points

- Active Breach Definition: Aggregate value of an investor's total investment across all investment strategies of a SIF falling below INR 10 lakh due to investor-initiated transactions (redemption, transfer, sale, etc.).
- Freeze Mechanism: Upon active breach, all units of the investor in the concerned SIF are frozen for debit.
- Notice Period: A 30-calendar-day notice is provided to the investor to rebalance their investments.
- Resolution & Auto-Redemption: Units are unfrozen if rebalanced within 30 days. If the investor fails to rebalance, frozen units are automatically redeemed by the AMC on the next immediate business day after the 30-day notice period.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandates AMCs to monitor compliance with the Minimum Investment Threshold on a daily basis without active breaches.
- Introduces standardized processing rules for active breaches involving debit freezes and mandatory automatic liquidation upon non-compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMCs, RTAs, and Depositories must implement systems and processes to monitor SIF investment thresholds daily and execute freezes and auto-redemptions as specified.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: July 29, 2025
- Effective Date: With effect from the date of the circular (July 29, 2025)

## Impact Assessment

Impacts mutual fund investors in Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs), AMCs, RTAs, and Depositories by enforcing strict minimum holding thresholds and automated penal redemption mechanisms for non-compliance.
