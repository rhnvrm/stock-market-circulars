## Summary

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (Central Processing Centre) has issued a Certificate of Incorporation pursuant to the change of name of Sagility India Limited to Sagility Limited, effective August 18, 2025. The company was originally incorporated as Berkmeer India Private Limited.

## Key Points

- **Old Name:** Sagility India Limited
- **New Name:** Sagility Limited
- **Original Name:** Berkmeer India Private Limited
- **CIN:** L72900KA2021PLC150054
- **Effective Date:** August 18, 2025

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal recognition of name change under Rule 29 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014.

## Compliance Requirements

- The Company is required to display its old name along with its new name at all required places for a period of two years in terms of Section 12 of the Companies Act.
- Stakeholders are advised to verify the latest status and documents on the MCA website.

## Important Dates

- **Certificate Date:** August 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal direct operational or financial impact; purely administrative change requiring standard compliance regarding display of names.
