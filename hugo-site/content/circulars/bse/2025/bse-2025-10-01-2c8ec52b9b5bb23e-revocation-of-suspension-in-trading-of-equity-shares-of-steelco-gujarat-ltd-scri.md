## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the revocation of suspension in the trading of equity shares of Steelco Gujarat Limited (Scrip Code: 500399). The circular includes the company profile, registered and corporate address details, board of directors, and operational status.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Steelco Gujarat Limited
- Scrip Code: 500399
- Nature of Business: Manufacturing of cold rolled steel products
- Registered Office: Plot No. 2, G.I.D.C. Estate, Palej, Dist. Bharuch – 392 220, Gujarat
- Operational Status: Commercial production yet to commence

## Regulatory Changes

- Trading suspension on the equity shares of the company has been revoked by the exchange.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company must adhere to ongoing listing obligations and disclosure requirements stipulated by BSE.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by BSE for trading revocation.

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders and investors regain the ability to buy and sell equity shares of Steelco Gujarat Limited on the BSE platform.
