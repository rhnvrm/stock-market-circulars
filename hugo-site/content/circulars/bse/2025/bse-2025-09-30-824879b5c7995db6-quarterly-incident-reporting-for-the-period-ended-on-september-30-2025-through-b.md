## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a member user manual outlining the procedure for Cyber Incident Reporting (CIR) through the BSE Electronic Filing System (BEFS). The manual details the steps for both Quarterly Incident Reporting and Immediate Incident Reporting for cyber-attacks, threats, and breaches.

## Key Points

- Two types of reporting mechanisms: Quarterly Incident Reporting and Immediate Incident Reporting.
- Quarterly reports must be submitted within 15 days after the end of the respective quarter via the BEFS portal.
- Immediate incident reports (for cyber-attacks, threats, and breaches) must be reported within 6 hours of receipt of information.
- Templates require macro enablement, data validation, and digital signing of the converted PDF output before final submission.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clarification of timelines and documentation steps for cyber incident reporting through BEFS.

## Compliance Requirements

- Designated Officers must download the CIR templates from the BEFS portal, fill out the required data (with or without incidents), validate all sheets, and upload the validated files.
- Generated PDFs must be digitally signed and uploaded along with the submission prompt, triggering a system-generated acknowledgement email to the Compliance and Designated Officers.

## Important Dates

- Quarterly reports: Within 15 days after the end of the respective quarter.
- Immediate reports: Within 6 hours of receipt of information regarding any cyber incident.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures structured and timely compliance for members regarding cyber incident management and reporting to the exchange.
