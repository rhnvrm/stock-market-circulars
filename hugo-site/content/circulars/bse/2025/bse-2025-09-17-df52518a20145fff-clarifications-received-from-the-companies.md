## Summary

BSE Limited sought clarification from various listed companies regarding significant volume movements in their securities. The companies have confirmed that they have no undisclosed information or developments requiring disclosure under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Vascon Engineers Limited confirmed no pending undisclosed information and stated the volume movement is purely market-driven.
- Expleo Solutions Limited confirmed compliance with Regulation 30 and stated that volume increases are based entirely on market conditions.
- Kirloskar Oil Engines Limited also addressed the BSE surveillance inquiry regarding trading volume.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes reported; standard surveillance inquiries under SEBI Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must respond to stock exchange inquiries regarding market volume or price movements in a timely manner under Regulation 30 of SEBI LODR.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No direct operational or financial impact; standard administrative disclosure ensuring market transparency.
