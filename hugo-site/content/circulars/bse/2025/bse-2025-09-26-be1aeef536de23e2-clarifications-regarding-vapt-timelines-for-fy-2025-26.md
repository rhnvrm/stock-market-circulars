## Summary

This circular provides clarifications and detailed scope requirements regarding Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) timelines and frameworks for the financial year 2025-26. It mandates comprehensive evaluation of IT environments, including infrastructure, applications, cloud deployments, and networks.

## Key Points

- Scope of VAPT must be transparent to SEBI and Exchanges, covering all critical assets and infrastructure.
- Includes VA/PT for servers, storage, network systems, databases, applications, APIs, Wi-Fi, and cloud environments.
- Testing methodology must adapt from recognized standards such as SEBI CSCRF, NCIIPC, CERT-In, NIST SP 800-115, ISO 27001, PCI-DSS, OSSTMM, and OWASP.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandates adherence to specific testing frameworks and broad coverage of internal and external infrastructure components.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities must ensure complete VAPT coverage as per the prescribed scope and document testing methodologies with supporting evidence and relevant records.

## Important Dates

- Applicable for FY 2025-26.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances the overall cybersecurity posture and ensures compliance with standardized regulatory frameworks across regulated entities.
