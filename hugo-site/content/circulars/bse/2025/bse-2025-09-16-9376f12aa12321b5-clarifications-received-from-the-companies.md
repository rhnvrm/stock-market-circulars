## Summary

BSE sought clarification from listed companies regarding a significant increase in the volume of their security across exchanges. Companies such as ITI Limited and Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers Limited responded stating that they have disclosed all material information pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 and that the volume movement is purely market-driven.

## Key Points

- BSE requested volume movement clarifications from listed entities.
- ITI Limited confirmed no pending undisclosed material events/matters affecting price or volume.
- Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers Limited stated that trading volume is market-driven and compliant with disclosure regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must respond to stock exchange inquiries regarding significant price or volume movements in accordance with listing regulations.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Informational notice indicating standard market surveillance queries and company compliance responses.
