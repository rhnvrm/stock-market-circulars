## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the introduction of Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) for Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform. The circular outlines key functionalities, investor category validations (Accredited vs. Non-Accredited), minimum investment thresholds, distributor requirements, and manual/bulk upload procedures for accredited investor details.

## Key Points

- A separate AMC creation is required for SIF, and a new AMC has been activated on the BSE StAR MF platform.
- Non-Accredited Investors can invest a minimum of ₹10 lakh for first-time investments in any SIF scheme, and their AUM must not fall below this threshold.
- Accredited Investors must invest amounts set by the AMC in Scheme Information Documents (SID).
- Mode of Holding (MOH) rules dictate whether AI exemptions apply based on whether holders are accredited or non-accredited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Activation of new SIF-specific AMC creation rules and integration on the BSE StAR MF platform.
- Strict rules governing joint and single mode of holding regarding Accredited Investor (AI) status and exemptions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members and distributors must pass the NISM XIII examination and register with AMFI to distribute SIFs.
- Accredited investor details must be updated via member logins (Admin >> client details >> client master upload) either manually or via bulk upload (pipe-separated file format: Member Code | Client Code | PAN Number | Accredited No. | Validity).
- AI certificates must be renewed prior to expiry with the issuing authorities.

## Important Dates

- Effective for SIF scheme transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform corresponding to circular date 2025-09-30.

## Impact Assessment

- Distributors and members must ensure proper updating of accredited investor details prior to order entry to avoid rejections and refunds from AMC/RTA.
