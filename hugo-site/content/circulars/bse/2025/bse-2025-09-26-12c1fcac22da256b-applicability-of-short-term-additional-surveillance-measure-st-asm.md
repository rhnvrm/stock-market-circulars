## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) effective from September 29, 2025. The circular details securities newly shortlisted in the ST-ASM framework, those moving to higher or lower stages, and securities moving out of the framework.

## Key Points

- Lists securities shortlisted in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework w.e.f. September 29, 2025 (e.g., Dynamic Industries Ltd, Evoq Remedies Ltd, GTT Data Solutions Ltd, etc.).
- Identifies securities continuing in the framework but moving to higher or lower stages (e.g., Mercury Trade Links Ltd moving to higher stage, Zodiac Ventures Ltd moving to lower stage).
- Outlines securities moving out of the ST-ASM framework (e.g., Epsom Properties Ltd, Karnavati Finance Ltd, Last Mile Enterprises Ltd, etc.).
- Provides a consolidated list of securities under the ST-ASM framework including Adani Power Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short Term ASM framework for designated scrips to monitor abnormal price movements.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and applicable margin requirements for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, such as applicable margins and trading restrictions, impacting liquidity and volatility.
