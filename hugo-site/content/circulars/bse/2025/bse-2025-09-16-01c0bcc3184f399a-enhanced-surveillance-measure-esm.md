## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted, moved to higher stages, and the consolidated list effective from September 17, 2025.

## Key Points

- New securities shortlisted in the ESM Framework w.e.f. Sep 17, 2025 (e.g., Mrugesh Trading Ltd, Newtime Infrastructure Ltd).
- Securities moving to higher Stage ESM (e.g., Amalgamated Electricity Company Ltd, MRC Agrotech Ltd, Nectar Lifesciences Ltd, Premier Energy and Infrastructure Ltd, RDB Real Estate Constructions Ltd, Yuranus Infrastructure Ltd).
- Consolidated list of securities under various ESM stages provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ESM framework to deter excessive volatility and speculative trading in identified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply appropriate trading safeguards, surveillance margins, and monitor trading limits for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance deposits, margin requirements, or trading frequency restrictions as mandated by the ESM framework.
