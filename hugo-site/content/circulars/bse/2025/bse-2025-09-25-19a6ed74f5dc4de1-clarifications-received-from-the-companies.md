## Summary

Stock exchanges sought clarifications from various listed companies regarding recent observed movements in share prices or trading volumes. In response, companies including Sandhar Technologies Limited and Vaghani Techno - Build Limited submitted formal clarifications stating that the movements are entirely market-driven and that they have not withheld any material, price-sensitive information under Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- Exchanges sought clarifications on recent volume and price spikes across multiple entities.
- Sandhar Technologies confirmed compliance with Regulation 30 and noted no undisclosed developments affecting share behavior.
- Vaghani Techno - Build attributed recent price changes to external factors and investor sentiment beyond company control.
- Companies reiterated commitment to timely disclosure of material information.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Responses adhere to existing disclosure requirements under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must promptly respond to exchange inquiries regarding unusual market behavior and confirm compliance with Regulation 30.

## Important Dates

- Date of Submissions: September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal market impact. These are standard disclosures ensuring transparency and addressing exchange surveillance queries.
