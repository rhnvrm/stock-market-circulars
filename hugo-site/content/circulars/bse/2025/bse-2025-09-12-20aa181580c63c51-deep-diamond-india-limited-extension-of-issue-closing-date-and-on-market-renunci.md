## Summary

Deep Diamond India Limited has announced an extension of its Rights Issue closing date from September 19, 2025, to September 24, 2025. Additionally, the trading period for Rights Entitlements (RE) has been extended to September 18, 2025, to provide shareholders additional time to exercise their rights.

## Key Points

- Rights Issue Closing Date extended to Wednesday, September 24, 2025.
- On-Market Renunciation Date for Rights Entitlements (RE) extended to Thursday, September 18, 2025.
- Revised post-issue schedule includes Allotment on September 30, 2025, and listing/trading on or about October 7, 2025.
- Scrip Code/ID: 539559 / DDIL, ISIN: INE005G01026, RE ISIN: INE005G20018.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes; routine timeline modification for an ongoing corporate action approved by the Rights Issue Committee.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders must submit their Composite Application Forms (CAF) along with application amounts by September 24, 2025.
- Market participants must take note of the revised dates for trading of Rights Entitlements.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: September 9, 2025
- Last Date on Market Renunciation: September 18, 2025
- Issue Closing Date: September 24, 2025
- Finalization of Basis of Allotment: September 29, 2025
- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2025
- Date of Credit: October 6, 2025
- Date of Listing / Trading: October 7, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders receive an extended window to subscribe to the rights issue and trade their rights entitlements, reducing potential forfeiture due to short-term liquidity or operational delays.
