## Summary

BSE issued letters to various listed companies seeking clarification regarding a significant increase in the volume of their securities across exchanges. In response, companies such as ITI Limited and Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers Limited submitted clarifications confirming compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations, stating that all material information has been disclosed and that the volume movement is purely market-driven.

## Key Points

- BSE surveillance sought clarification on significant volume/price movements in scrips.
- ITI Limited confirmed adherence to SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015, with no pending price-sensitive disclosures.
- Shree Pushkar Chemicals & Fertilisers Limited confirmed that the volume increase is purely market-driven and not due to any undisclosed company events.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must respond to stock exchange surveillance inquiries regarding unusual market activity in a timely manner under SEBI (LODR) Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard procedural disclosure with negligible direct impact on market operations or stock valuations.
