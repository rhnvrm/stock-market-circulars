## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The circular provides lists of securities newly shortlisted, moved to higher or lower stages, moving out of the framework, and the consolidated list of securities under LT-ASM effective from September 23, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework effective September 23, 2025.
- Gujarat Hy-Spin Ltd (Scrip Code: 540938) is moved to ASM Stage II.
- Caspian Corporate Services Ltd has moved out of the Long Term ASM Framework due to inclusion in the ESM Framework.
- Consolidated list includes multiple companies across various ASM stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Long Term ASM framework to monitor price volatility and safeguard investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply appropriate surveillance margins and trading restrictions as mandated by the framework for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to heightened surveillance margins and specific monitoring mechanisms, impacting liquidity and trading behavior.
