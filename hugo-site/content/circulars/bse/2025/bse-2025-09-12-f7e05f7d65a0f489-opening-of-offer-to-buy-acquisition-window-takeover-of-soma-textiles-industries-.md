## Summary

Roadway Solutions India Infra Limited along with Persons Acting in Concert (PAC 1: Ameet Harjinder Gadhoke, PAC 2: Teja Ranade Gadhoke) has released the Letter of Offer for the acquisition of up to 82,64,942 equity shares, representing 25.02% of the voting share capital of Soma Textiles & Industries Limited, at an offer price of INR 47.14 per share payable in cash.

## Key Points

- **Acquirer:** Roadway Solutions India Infra Limited
- **PACs:** Ameet Harjinder Gadhoke and Teja Ranade Gadhoke
- **Target Company:** Soma Textiles & Industries Limited
- **Offer Size:** Up to 82,64,942 equity shares (25.02% of voting share capital)
- **Offer Price:** INR 47.14 per equity share payable in cash
- **Regulations:** Pursuant to Regulations 3(1) and (4) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011

## Regulatory Changes

None. The offer is being conducted under the existing framework of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Public shareholders of the Target Company wishing to tender their shares must follow the procedures outlined in the Letter of Offer and submit forms through their respective stock brokers within the tendering period.

## Important Dates

- **Detailed Public Statement Date:** July 16, 2025
- **Commencement of Tendering Period / Upward Revision Deadline:** Tuesday, September 16, 2025 (1 working day prior)

## Impact Assessment

This open offer involves a substantial change in control and management of Soma Textiles & Industries Limited, providing an exit opportunity for public shareholders at the specified offer price.
