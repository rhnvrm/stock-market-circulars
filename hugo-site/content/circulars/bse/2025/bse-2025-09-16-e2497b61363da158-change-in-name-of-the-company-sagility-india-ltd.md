## Summary

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs has issued a Certificate of Incorporation pursuant to the change of name for Sagility India Limited to Sagility Limited, effective from August 18, 2025. The company was originally incorporated as Berkmeer India Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Previous Name: Sagility India Limited
- New Name: Sagility Limited
- Original Incorporation Name: Berkmeer India Private Limited
- Corporate Identification Number (CIN): L72900KA2021PLC150054
- Effective Date: August 18, 2025

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal recognition of the company name change by the Central Processing Centre, Registrar of Companies.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company is obligated to display its old name along with its new name at all required places for a period of two years in terms of Section 12 of the Companies Act.

## Important Dates

- Certificate Date: August 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Stakeholders are advised to take note of the new name for all future references and verify public documents on the MCA portal.
