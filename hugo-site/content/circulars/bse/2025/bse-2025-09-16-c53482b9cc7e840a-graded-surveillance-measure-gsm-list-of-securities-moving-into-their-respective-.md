## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a circular regarding the movement of securities into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. A total of 5 securities have been shifted to GSM Stages I, II, III, and IV.

## Key Points

- KLG Capital Services Ltd (Security Code: 530771, ISIN: INE929C01018) moved to GSM Stage I.
- Pasupati Fincap Ltd (Security Code: 511734, ISIN: INE527C01010) moved to GSM Stage II.
- Integra Switchgear Ltd (Security Code: 517423, ISIN: INE0IPL01018) moved to GSM Stage II.
- P.M. Telelinnks Ltd (Security Code: 513403, ISIN: INE092C01015) moved to GSM Stage III.
- SW Investments Ltd (Security Code: 503659, ISIN: INE948K01011) moved to GSM Stage IV.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities placed under GSM stages are subjected to enhanced monitoring and trading restrictions to protect investors and curb abnormal price movements.
- Specific conditions apply for securities marked with symbols indicating movement due to inclusion in ESM or IBC frameworks.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must verify applicable surveillance margins and restrictions before executing trades in the listed securities.
- Clients trading in these securities need to maintain required margin deposits as mandated by the exchange's GSM guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity for the affected securities is expected to tighten significantly due to the imposition of trading restrictions and higher margin requirements associated with higher GSM stages.
