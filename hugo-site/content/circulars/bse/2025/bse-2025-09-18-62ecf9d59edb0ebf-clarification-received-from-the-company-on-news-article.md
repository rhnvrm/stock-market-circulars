## Summary

BSE sought clarification from Nelco Ltd. regarding a news item titled 'Tata Group's Nelco to enter satellite broadband business, shares jump'. The company stated it is unable to comment on media speculation and confirmed that all material information under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations has been disclosed.

## Key Points

- Clarification sought on news article published on 'hindi-cnbctv18.com' regarding satellite broadband business entry.
- Company declined to comment on media speculation and rumors.
- Confirmed no withheld material information or price-sensitive events affecting operations.
- Stated that the movement in share price and volume is purely market-driven.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Continued adherence to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 for prompt disclosure of material developments.

## Important Dates

- Date of Letter: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No direct operational or financial impact; standard procedural compliance response.
