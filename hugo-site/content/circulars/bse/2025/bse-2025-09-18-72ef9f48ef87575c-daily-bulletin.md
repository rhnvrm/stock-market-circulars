## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 118/2025-2026 dated September 18, 2025, details upcoming book-closure and record dates for interest payments and partial/full redemptions of privately placed non-convertible debentures (NCDs) issued by Aye Finance Limited.

## Key Points

- Lists multiple record dates ranging from September 30, 2025, to December 31, 2025.
- Covers various ISIN security codes for Aye Finance Limited non-convertible debentures.
- Specifies corporate actions including payment of interest and part-redemption of NCDs.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and depositories must ensure accurate record-keeping and processing of interest payments and redemptions based on the specified record dates.

## Important Dates

- Record Dates: Spanning between 30/09/2025 and 31/12/2025 for various debenture issues of Aye Finance Limited.

## Impact Assessment

Routine schedule notification for debenture holders concerning interest disbursements and redemptions.
