## Summary

BSE has issued an updated list of securities subject to the Add-on Price Band Framework, detailing scrip codes, ISIN numbers, and scrip names for affected companies.

## Key Points

- Lists 36 securities included in the updated Add-on Price Band Framework.
- Includes scrip codes ranging from 504746 to 544361 alongside their respective ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Application of add-on price bands to curb excessive volatility in specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure trading systems respect the updated price bands for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective as per the circular release date.

## Impact Assessment

- Limits intraday price volatility for the specified scrips, affecting trading strategies and liquidity.
