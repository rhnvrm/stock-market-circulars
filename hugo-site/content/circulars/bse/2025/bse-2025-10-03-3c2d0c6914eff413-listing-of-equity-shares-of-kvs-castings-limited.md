## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of equity shares of KVS Castings Limited following its initial public offering (IPO). The circular outlines the number of securities, distinctive number ranges, security types, lock-in details, and settlement types (Demat).

## Key Points

- Total equity shares listed: 18,753,324.
- 9,985,324 shares under fully paid up and lock-in until 07.10.2026.
- 3,798,000 shares under fully paid up and lock-in until 07.10.2028.
- 704,000 anchor shares under lock-in until 05.11.2025.
- 706,000 anchor shares under lock-in until 04.01.2026.
- 3,560,000 free shares under fresh IPO.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must note the lock-in periods and distinctive number ranges for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Listing / Lock-in effective dates starting from September 24, 2025, through October 7, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of equity shares of KVS Castings Limited on the BSE platform while maintaining necessary lock-in restrictions for specific allocations.
