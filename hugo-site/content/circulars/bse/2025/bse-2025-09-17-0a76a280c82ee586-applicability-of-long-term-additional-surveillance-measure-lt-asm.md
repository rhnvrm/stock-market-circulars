## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective September 18, 2025. The circular lists securities newly shortlisted in the LT-ASM framework, securities moving to higher or lower stages, securities moving out of the framework, and the consolidated list of securities under LT-ASM.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been newly shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework (Part A) effective September 18, 2025.
- Vintron Informatics Ltd has been moved to ASM Stage III (Part B).
- Hilltone Software and Gases Ltd, Kavveri Defence & Wireless Technologies Ltd, and Maitri Enterprises Ltd are moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework due to inclusion in other frameworks (ESM, GSM).
- A consolidated list of securities under various stages of the LT-ASM framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance measures under the Long Term ASM framework to monitor price volatility and safeguard investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must note the inclusion, movement, and exclusion of scrips under the LT-ASM framework and apply necessary margin requirements and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in securities listed under the LT-ASM framework may be subject to additional surveillance margins (such as 100% margin) and specific trading controls to curb excessive volatility.
