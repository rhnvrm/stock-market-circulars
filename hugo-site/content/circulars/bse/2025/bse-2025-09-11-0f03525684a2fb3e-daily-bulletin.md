## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 113/2025-2026 dated September 11, 2025, outlines upcoming book-closure and record dates for various corporate actions, including final dividends for equity shares, interest payments, and part/full redemptions for non-convertible debentures (NCDs) and commercial papers (CPs).

## Key Points

- Lists corporate actions including final dividends for companies such as A.D.S. Diagnostics Ltd., Aartech Solonics Limited, Akar Auto Industries Limited, and Apeejay Surrendra Park Hotels Limited.
- Details record dates for interest payments and redemptions on privately placed non-convertible debentures (NCDs) for issuers like Arohan Financial Services Ltd., Ashiana Housing Limited, Dvara Kshetriya Gramin Financial Services, and Earlysalary Services Private Limited.
- Specifies record dates for commercial paper redemptions for entities such as Axis Securities Limited, Capri Global Capital Limited, and ECL Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities and issuers must ensure compliance with the specified book-closure and record dates for corporate actions and payouts.

## Important Dates

- Bulletin Date: September 11, 2025
- Record Dates ranging from September 12, 2025, to December 9, 2025, depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

Investors holding the respective equity shares, NCDs, or commercial papers should note the record dates to determine eligibility for dividends, interest, or redemption proceeds.
