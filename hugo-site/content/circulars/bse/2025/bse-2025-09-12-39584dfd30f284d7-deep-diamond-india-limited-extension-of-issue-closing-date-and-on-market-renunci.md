## Summary

Deep Diamond India Limited has announced a revised schedule for its ongoing rights issue, extending the issue closing date to September 24, 2025, and the on-market renunciation date for rights entitlements (RE) to September 18, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Issue Opening Date:** September 9, 2025 (unchanged)
- **Revised Issue Closing Date:** September 24, 2025 (previously September 19, 2025)
- **Revised Rights Entitlement Renunciation Date:** September 18, 2025 (previously September 15, 2025)
- **Scrip Code/ID:** 539559 / DDIL
- **ISIN:** INE005G01026, **RE ISIN:** INE005G20018

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension approved by the Rights Issue Committee at its meeting held on September 11, 2025, to provide shareholders additional time to participate.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders must ensure submission of the Common Application Form (CAF) along with application money by September 24, 2025.

## Important Dates

- **Last Date of On-Market Renunciation:** September 18, 2025
- **Issue Closing Date:** September 24, 2025
- **Basis of Allotment:** September 29, 2025
- **Date of Allotment:** September 30, 2025
- **Date of Listing/Trading:** October 07, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Provides extended flexibility for existing shareholders of Deep Diamond India Limited to subscribe or trade their rights entitlements.
