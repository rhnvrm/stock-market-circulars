## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, effective September 24, 2025. The circular outlines securities newly shortlisted, those moving out of the framework, and a consolidated list of securities under various ASM stages.

## Key Points

- New securities shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. September 24, 2025 (Annexure I, Part A) include companies like Alstone Textiles (India) Ltd, Bijoy Hans Ltd, and others.
- Securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. September 24, 2025 (Annexure II) include Emmforce Autotech Ltd, Gujarat Investa Ltd, and IEL Ltd.
- Provides a consolidated list of securities currently under different ASM stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Long Term ASM framework to monitor stock price volatility and ensure market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors need to take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities may be subject to enhanced surveillance, additional margin requirements, or specific trading restrictions depending on their assigned ASM stage.
