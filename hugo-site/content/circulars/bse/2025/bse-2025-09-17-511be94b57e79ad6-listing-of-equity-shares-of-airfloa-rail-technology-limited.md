## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading approval for equity shares of Airfloa Rail Technology Limited, covering various categories including fully paid-up shares under lock-in, anchor lock-in shares, and free IPO shares.

## Key Points

- Total securities listed include various tranches of fully paid-up shares subject to different lock-in periods and free shares.
- Lock-in periods range from October 2025 to September 2028 depending on the category of securities.
- Includes anchor investor lock-in shares maturing in October and December 2025.
- Free shares available under IPO allotment.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and depositories must ensure adherence to the specified lock-in periods and demat settlement terms.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in start date: 10.09.2025 / 16.09.2025
- Lock-in end dates: 15.10.2025, 14.12.2025, 25.09.2026, 25.09.2027, 25.09.2028

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading and settlement of Airfloa Rail Technology Limited shares on the exchange according to the defined lock-in schedules.
