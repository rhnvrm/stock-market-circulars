## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of new equity shares of Epuja Spiritech Limited resulting from allotments made to multiple allottees. The shares carry specified lock-in periods up to April 1, 2026.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Epuja Spiritech Limited
- Details of new shares allotted to multiple individuals (e.g., Priyadarshi Srivastav, Mohanlal Ramavtar Mandhana, Hitesh Oberoi, etc.) have been processed.
- Distinctive numbers and lock-in details have been specified for the allotted securities.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the new securities listing and associated lock-in periods.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in upto dates: 30/03/2026 and 01/04/2026.

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action expanding the paid-up capital base of Epuja Spiritech Limited.
