## Summary

BSE Limited has issued a circular regarding the process for the submission of Non-Demat Liquid Mutual Fund or Overnight Mutual Fund Units towards deposit requirements by Investment Advisers (IAs), detailing the lien application and consent letter requirements to be processed with RTAs and AMCs.

## Key Points

- Lien application cum consent letter format specified for recognizing lien in favor of BSE Limited.
- Applicable to Non-Demat Liquid Mutual Fund / Overnight Mutual Fund Units held by Investment Advisers.
- Requires details including Mutual Fund name, AMC name, Scheme name, Folio No., ISIN, Number of units, and NAV.
- Units must be free balance without prior pledge, charge, lien, or encumbrance, and not subject to Section 54EA/EB lock-in.

## Regulatory Changes

- Outlines operational compliance for Investment Advisers to fulfill deposit requirements mandated by SEBI Regulations through mutual fund unit pledging.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must submit duly filled and signed lien application cum consent letters through their RTA or AMC.
- Ensure units are free of encumbrances and lock-in periods prior to creating the lien in favor of BSE Limited (IAASB).

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular issuance date.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the deposit creation and utilization mechanism for Investment Advisers using non-demat mutual fund units under SEBI regulations.
