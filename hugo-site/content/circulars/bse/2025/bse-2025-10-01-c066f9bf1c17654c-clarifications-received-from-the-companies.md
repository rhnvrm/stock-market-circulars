## Summary

This circular compiles responses from multiple listed companies providing clarifications to BSE and NSE regarding significant movements in their share price and trading volume.

## Key Points

- **Wonder Electricals Ltd.**: Clarified that the significant increase in trading volume is purely market-driven and that all mandatory disclosures under SEBI (LODR) Regulations have been duly met.
- **Banaras Beads Ltd.**: Clarified regarding significant price movement that promoters have not traded shares, recent property purchase and loan details were already disclosed, and no other unpublished price sensitive information exists.
- **IB Infotech Enterprises Ltd.**: Acknowledged receipt of the BSE query regarding price movement.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 regarding timely disclosures of material events and clarifications.

## Important Dates

- **Clarification Date**: October 1, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to investors regarding unusual trading activity without indicating any underlying undisclosed corporate actions.
