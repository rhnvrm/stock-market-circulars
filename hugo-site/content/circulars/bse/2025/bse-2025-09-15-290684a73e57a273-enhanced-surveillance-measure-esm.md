## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the list of securities newly shortlisted, moved to higher/lower stages, or continuing within the framework effective September 16, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities into the ESM framework effective September 16, 2025.
- Movement of existing securities to higher or lower stages within the ESM framework.
- Consolidated list of securities currently subject to Enhanced Surveillance Measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the ESM framework to safeguard market integrity and protect investors from excessive volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable ESM stages and trading restrictions for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities may experience modified trading conditions, higher margin requirements, or trading frequency limitations depending on their assigned ESM stage.
