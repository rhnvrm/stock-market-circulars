## Summary

This circular outlines the detailed operational modalities and the required undertaking for Algo Providers participating in the safer participation of retail investors in algorithmic trading on the BSE. It specifies the legal, financial, and compliance obligations for entities providing algorithms or decision support tools to trading members.

## Key Points

- Algo providers must execute an undertaking on non-judicial stamp paper (Rs. 1000/- or state applicable duty) and get it duly notarized.
- Providers must seek empanelment with BSE LTD by submitting true, complete, and correct application details.
- Products and Risk Management Systems (RMS) must comply with requirements laid down by BSE and/or SEBI at all times.
- Products must adhere to implementation standards formulated by the Broker’s Industry Standards Forum.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of a mandatory formal undertaking format for Algo Providers intending to supply algorithmic tools to trading members.
- Clarification of exchange liability limitations regarding third-party vendor products.

## Compliance Requirements

- Execution and submission of the Algo Provider Undertaking (Annexure 1).
- Ensuring continuous compliance of products and risk management systems with regulatory guidelines.
- Payment of applicable license fees, charges, or levies by regulatory authorities.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the issuance date of September 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances regulatory oversight over third-party algorithm providers and decision support tools.
- Increases accountability for algo providers catering to retail participants through trading members.
