## Summary

Justo Realfintech Limited has finalized the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors ahead of its initial public offering (IPO) on the BSE SME platform. The company successfully raised ₹17.91 crore from anchor investors.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares allocated to anchor investors: 14,10,000 shares.
- Anchor allocation price: ₹127 per share.
- Total anchor book size: ₹17,90,70,000 (approximately ₹17.91 crore).
- Anchor portion represents approximately 28.42% of the total issue size.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adherence to SEBI ICDR Regulations concerning anchor investor allocation, bidding timelines, and mandatory lock-in periods.

## Compliance Requirements

- Institutional anchor investors are subject to a statutory lock-in period on their allotted shares under SEBI guidelines.
- 50% of the anchor shares (7,05,000 shares) are subject to a 30-day lock-in period ending on October 28, 2025.
- Remaining 50% of the anchor shares (7,05,000 shares) are subject to a 90-day lock-in period ending on December 27, 2025.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bid Date: September 23, 2025
- Public Issue Period: September 24, 2025 – September 26, 2025
- Listing Date: October 1, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants and investors visibility into institutional demand and price discovery prior to the opening of the main public bidding window for Justo Realfintech Limited's SME IPO.
