## Summary

Advance Agrolife Limited has finalized its allocation of equity shares to anchor investors, successfully raising approximately ₹57.77 crore ahead of its initial public offering (IPO). A total of 5,776,716 equity shares were allotted to anchor investors at the upper price band of ₹100 per share.

## Key Points

- **Total Anchor Allocation:** 5,776,716 equity shares
- **Issue Price / Anchor Price:** ₹100 per share
- **Total Funds Raised:** Approximately ₹57.77 crore
- **Number of Anchor Investors:** 9 institutional investors, including Wealthwave Capital Trust, Meru Investment Fund PCC, Aidos India Fund Ltd., Astrone Capital VCC, and Venus Investments VCC.
- **Book Running Lead Manager:** Choice Capital Advisors Private Limited

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; complies with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 regarding anchor investor allocations.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and lead managers must ensure lock-in compliance for anchor investor shares as mandated under SEBI ICDR regulations (30 days for 50% of the shares and 90 days for the remaining).

## Important Dates

- **Anchor Investor Bid Date:** September 29, 2025
- **IPO Open Date:** September 30, 2025
- **IPO Close Date:** October 3, 2025
- **Basis of Allotment:** October 6, 2025
- **Listing Date:** October 8, 2025

## Impact Assessment

The successful anchor round indicates institutional appetite and sets the baseline pricing and market sentiment ahead of the main public issue opening.
