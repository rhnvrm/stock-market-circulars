## Summary

Sundaram Mutual Fund has issued a notice-cum-addendum regarding changes to the fundamental attributes and scheme name of Sundaram Diversified Equity Fund, which will be renamed to Sundaram Value Fund effective 12-Sep-2025 following SEBI's 'No Objection' observation.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Renaming:** Sundaram Diversified Equity Fund is changing its name to Sundaram Value Fund.
- **Investment Strategy Change:** The scheme transitions from an ELSS (open-ended equity linked saving scheme with a 3-year statutory lock-in) to an open-ended equity scheme following a value investment strategy.
- **Asset Allocation Updates:** Changes in indicative allocations across equities, debt, money market securities, and REITs/InVITs.
- **Effective Date:** The changes will be effective from 12-Sep-2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- Approval obtained from Sundaram Trustee Company Limited post receipt of SEBI's 'No Objection' email dated 08-July-2025.

## Compliance Requirements

- Unitholders are notified of the changes in fundamental attributes and are given appropriate options/exit routes as per SEBI regulations if they do not wish to continue.

## Important Dates

- **SEBI Approval Date:** 08-July-2025
- **Effective Date:** 12-Sep-2025

## Impact Assessment

- Existing investors holding units under the ELSS scheme will experience a shift in investment strategy and removal of the statutory 3-year lock-in requirement depending on their existing commitments, with exit option provisions as per regulations.
