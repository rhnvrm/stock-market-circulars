## Summary

Karbonsteel Engineering Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern under Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, following the post-issue of its IPO, regarding the listing of its equity shares.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: KARBONSTEEL ENGINEERING LIMITED
- Scrip Code/Name of Scrip/Class of Security: EQUITY SHARES
- Share Holding Pattern Filed under: Reg. 31(1)(a) post-issue of IPO.
- The company has locked-in shares as per regulatory requirements for IPOs.

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for post-IPO shareholding disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities are required to submit shareholding pattern disclosures and maintain compliance regarding locked-in shares and promoter shareholding.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: 15-SEP-2025

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates public trading and official market listing of equity shares for Karbonsteel Engineering Limited.
