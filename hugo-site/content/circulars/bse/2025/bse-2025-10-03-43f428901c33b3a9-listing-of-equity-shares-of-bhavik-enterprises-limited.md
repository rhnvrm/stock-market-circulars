## Summary

Bhavik Enterprises Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern under Regulation 31(1)(a) of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, for the quarter ending 02-10-2025.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Bhavik Enterprises Limited
- Share Holding Pattern Filed under: Regulation 31(1)(a)
- Quarter ending: 02-10-2025
- Declarations indicate no partly paid-up shares, convertible securities, warrants, or depository receipts, but include locked-in shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure timely submission of shareholding patterns as per Regulation 31 of SEBI LODR.

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: 03-10-2025
- Quarter Ending: 02-10-2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard compliance disclosure with negligible direct market impact.
