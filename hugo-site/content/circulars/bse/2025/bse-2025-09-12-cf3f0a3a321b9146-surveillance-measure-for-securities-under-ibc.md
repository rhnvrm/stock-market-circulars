## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Surveillance Measure for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Effective September 15, 2025, specific securities are shortlisted under Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) Stage 1, while others are excluded or maintained on the consolidated list.

## Key Points

- **Stage 1 Shortlisting:** Securities such as A.F. Enterprises Ltd (Scrip Code: 538351) and Visa Steel Ltd (Scrip Code: 532721) are shortlisted in ASM for Companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per IBC Stage 1 w.e.f. September 15, 2025.
- **Exclusions:** Gayatri Projects Ltd (Scrip Code: 532767) is excluded from ASM for Companies under IBC w.e.f. September 15, 2025.
- **Consolidated List:** The circular provides a comprehensive updated list of securities under various stages of the IBC surveillance framework, including those at Stage 1 and those under receipt of corporate announcements.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates to the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies undergoing insolvency proceedings under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and apply appropriate trading restrictions and margin requirements as mandated by the exchange for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, impacting liquidity, margin requirements, and risk management controls for brokers and investors.
