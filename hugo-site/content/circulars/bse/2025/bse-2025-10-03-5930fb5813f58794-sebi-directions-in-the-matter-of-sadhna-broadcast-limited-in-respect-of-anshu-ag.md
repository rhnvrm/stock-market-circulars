## Summary

The Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) heard an appeal regarding SEBI's impugned order that imposed a penalty of Rs. 5 Lakhs, directed a disgorgement of Rs. 11,97,561/-, and debarred the appellant (Anshu Aggarwal) from accessing the securities market for one year in connection with the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd. (SBL). Since the appellant offered to deposit the entire penalty and disgorgement amount, the Tribunal stayed the debarment direction (other than in respect of SBL scrip) subject to this deposit.

## Key Points

- SEBI imposed a penalty of Rs. 5 Lakhs, disgorgement of Rs. 11,97,561/-, and a one-year market debarment on the appellant.
- The appellant contended she was not connected to the price and volume manipulation of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd. except through her husband's associations.
- The appellant offered to deposit the entire penalty and disgorgement amount without prejudice to her rights.
- SAT directed that subject to the deposit of the entire penalty and disgorgement amount, the debarment from accessing the securities market (excluding SBL scrip) shall remain stayed.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes; the order pertains specific interim relief in an appeal against an adjudication order.

## Compliance Requirements

The appellant is required to deposit the entire penalty and disgorgement amount to avail of the stay on market debarment.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 16, 2025
- Date of Impugned Order: May 29, 2025 (with Corrigendum on May 30, 2025)

## Impact Assessment

Provides interim relief to the individual appellant pending final disposal of the appeal, conditional upon financial compliance with the deposit requirement.
