## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the listing of further securities issued by companies under Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP / ESOS), including lock-in details and share quantities.

## Key Points

- Details of share issuances and lock-in expiry dates under ESOP/ESOS schemes.
- Total shares accounted for in the schedule: 1,82,150 public shares.
- Lock-in expiration dates span across October 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Applicable companies must ensure compliance with listing regulations and depository procedures for the newly issued securities.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in expiry dates: October 16, 2025, and October 17, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update with no direct market-wide price impact.
