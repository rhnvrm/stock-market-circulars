## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the change in Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA) for Taurus Mutual Fund, providing a comprehensive mapping of existing BSE scheme codes to new scheme codes post-migration across equity schemes.

## Key Points

- AMC: Taurus Mutual Fund
- Involves multiple schemes including Taurus Banking and Financial Services Fund and Taurus Discovery (Midcap) Fund.
- Outlines migration from existing scheme codes (e.g., TRTBG1-GR) to new scheme codes (e.g., TUTBG1G-GR).
- Covers both direct and normal (regular) plans, growth options, IDCW payout, and IDCW reinvestment options.

## Regulatory Changes

- Update of scheme codes and identifiers on the exchange platform following the change in RTA.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, brokers, and investors must take note of the revised scheme codes for ongoing and future transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective date as per the circular issued on September 18, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures seamless transition of mutual fund scheme mapping and continuous transaction processing without disruption.
