## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM), announcing that Gujarat Investa Ltd. has been moved to GSM Stage I effective from the specified date.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Gujarat Investa Ltd.
- Security Code: 531341
- ISIN: INE373D01017
- Moved to GSM Stage: I

## Regulatory Changes

The security is subjected to enhanced surveillance and appropriate trading restrictions as mandated under the Graded Surveillance Measure framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors need to be aware of the trading restrictions and surveillance margins applicable to securities under GSM Stage I.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 2025-09-12

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected security may experience reduced liquidity and additional margin requirements due to surveillance monitoring.
