## Summary

SEBI passed an order concerning Equicom Financial Research Private Limited (Investment Adviser) and its directors, detailing ongoing proceedings due to failure to comply with earlier final orders regarding the resolution of investor complaints.

## Key Points

- Noticee No. 1 is Equicom Financial Research Private Limited, a registered Investment Adviser.
- Previous orders restricted the noticees from accessing the securities market and directed the resolution of complaints pending in SCORES.
- The current proceedings arose from failure to comply with the directions within the stipulated time frame.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of previous prohibitions and potential additional penalties under the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.

## Compliance Requirements

Intermediaries must strictly comply with SEBI orders, resolve investor grievances within specified timelines, and furnish compliance reports certified by Chartered Accountants when mandated.

## Important Dates

- Date of Interim Order: January 21, 2020
- Date of Final Order: April 19, 2021
- Enquiry Report Date: April 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight on investment advisers and ensures strict adherence to investor grievance redressal mechanisms.
