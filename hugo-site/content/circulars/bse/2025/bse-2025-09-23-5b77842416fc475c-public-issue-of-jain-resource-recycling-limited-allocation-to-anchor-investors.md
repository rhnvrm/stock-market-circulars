## Summary

The IPO Committee of Jain Resource Recycling Limited, in consultation with the Book Running Lead Managers, finalized the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors. A total of 24,248,689 equity shares were allocated to anchor investors at the upper price band of ₹232 per equity share, aggregating to approximately ₹562.57 crore.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares allocated to anchor investors: 24,248,689 shares.
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: ₹232 per share (at the upper end of the price band).
- Total amount raised from anchor investors: ₹562.57 crore.
- Prominent institutional investors participating include HDFC Mutual Fund, Axis Mutual Fund, Goldman Sachs Funds, and Abu Dhabi Investment Authority (ADIA).
- The anchor portion constitutes approximately 45% of the total Qualified Institutional Buyer (QIB) portion.
