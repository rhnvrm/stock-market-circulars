## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular regarding the framework on the Social Stock Exchange (SSE), incorporating amendments to the ICDR Regulations, 2018, and LODR Regulations, 2015. The circular modifies minimum eligibility criteria for Not for Profit Organizations (NPOs) registering with the SSE.

## Key Points

- Incorporates Board-approved amendments to ICDR and LODR Regulations following recommendations from the Social Stock Exchange Advisory Committee (SSEAC).
- Specifies updated legal structures and acts under which NPOs must be registered in India to seek registration with the SSE.
- Details eligible entities including charitable trusts, societies, and Section 8 companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Partial modification to circular SEBI/HO/CFD/PoD-1/P/CIR/2022/120 dated September 19, 2022.
- Updated registration categories under Indian Trusts Act 1882, Public Trust statutes, Societies Registration Act 1860, and Section 8 of the Companies Act 2013.

## Compliance Requirements

- NPOs seeking registration or raising funds through the SSE must ensure their registration certificate is valid for at least the next 12 months and conforms to the specified legal forms.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 19, 2025
- Gazette Notifications: September 08, 2025 and September 09, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates smoother onboarding and regulatory clarity for social enterprises and NPOs intending to list or raise capital on the Social Stock Exchange.
