## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the surveillance measures for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective September 30, 2025. The update outlines securities shortlisted in ASM Stage 1, exclusions, and the consolidated list of companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP).

## Key Points

- **ASM Stage 1 Additions:** Polo Hotels Ltd (Scrip Code: 526687) and Tiaan Consumer Ltd (Scrip Code: 540108) have been shortlisted in IBC Stage 1 effective September 30, 2025.
- **Exclusions:** Visa Steel Ltd (Scrip Code: 532721) has been excluded from the ASM IBC framework effective September 30, 2025.
- **Consolidated List:** Includes various companies under different stages of IRP / receipt of corporate announcements, such as A.F. Enterprises Ltd, Aban Offshore Ltd, AGS Transact Technologies Ltd, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are moved across various stages of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) surveillance framework based on corporate disclosures and insolvency proceedings.
- Stage 1 and Stage 0 designations apply specific surveillance and monitoring controls on trading activities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance status and apply necessary risk management and trading restrictions for the listed scrips.
- Listed companies undergoing IRP must ensure continuous disclosure compliance as mandated by regulatory authorities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, potentially impacting liquidity and margin requirements for investors holding these scrips.
