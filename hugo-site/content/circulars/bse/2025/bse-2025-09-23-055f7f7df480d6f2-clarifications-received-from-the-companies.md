## Summary

Multiple listed companies, including Tinna Rubber And Infrastructure Limited and AWL Agri Business Limited (formerly known as Adani Wilmar Limited), have issued clarifications to BSE and NSE concerning a significant increase in the volume of their securities in the recent past. The companies confirmed they are in strict compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and stated that the volume/price movements are purely market-driven.

## Key Points

- Exchanges sought clarification on the recent spike in trading volume of certain securities.
- Tinna Rubber And Infrastructure Limited confirmed no pending material events or unpublished price-sensitive information.
- AWL Agri Business Limited reiterated adherence to disclosure obligations under Regulation 30.
- Management of the respective companies has no control or specific knowledge regarding the volume increase.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies are required to respond to exchange inquiries regarding unusual market activity in compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI LODR Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No operational or financial impact on the companies; purely procedural compliance disclosures.
