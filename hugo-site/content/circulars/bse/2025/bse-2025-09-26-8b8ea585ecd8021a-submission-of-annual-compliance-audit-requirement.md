## Summary

BSE has issued a user manual outlining the step-by-step procedure for Investment Advisers (IA) and Research Analysts (RA) to submit their Audit Compliance Report (ACR) and Action Taken Report (ATR) through the BSE membership portal.

## Key Points

- Access the BSE portal via the designated membership link using existing credentials.
- Navigate to Post Registration / Annual Compliance reporting to complete the filing.
- Step 1 involves filling out the ACR form, saving/proceeding, uploading the auditor-signed audit report along with the annexure, and submitting.
- Step 2 involves submitting the Action Taken Report (ATR) by filling out points, generating a PDF, having it signed on letterhead, and uploading it.

## Regulatory Changes

Mandatory compliance reporting framework for IA and RA members regarding annual audit submissions.

## Compliance Requirements

- IA and RA members must submit their Audit Compliance Report (ACR) and Action Taken Report (ATR) through the BSE portal.
- Ensure reports are duly signed by the auditor and the individual IA/RA or authorized signatory.

Streamlines the regulatory compliance process for Investment Advisers and Research Analysts by providing clear workflow instructions for digital submissions on the BSE platform.
