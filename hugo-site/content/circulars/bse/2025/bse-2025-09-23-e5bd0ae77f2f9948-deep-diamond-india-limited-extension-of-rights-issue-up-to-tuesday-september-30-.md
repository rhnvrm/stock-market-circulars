## Summary

Deep Diamond India Limited has announced an extension of the closing date for its ongoing Rights Issue to Tuesday, September 30, 2025. The extension was approved by the Rights Issue Committee to provide shareholders with additional time and opportunity to exercise their rights.

## Key Points

- Original closing date was September 19, 2025, initially extended to September 24, 2025, and now further extended to September 30, 2025.
- Last date of submission of the duly filled in CAF along with application money is Tuesday, September 30, 2025.
- Revised schedule of post-issue activities updated accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Eligible shareholders must submit their Common Application Form (CAF) along with the application amount by September 30, 2025.

## Important Dates

- Issue Closing Date: September 30, 2025
- Basis of Allotment: October 06, 2025
- Initiation of Allotment: October 06, 2025
- Date of Credit: October 09, 2025
- Date of Listing / Trading: October 13, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides extended participation window for shareholders of Deep Diamond India Limited under the rights issue.
