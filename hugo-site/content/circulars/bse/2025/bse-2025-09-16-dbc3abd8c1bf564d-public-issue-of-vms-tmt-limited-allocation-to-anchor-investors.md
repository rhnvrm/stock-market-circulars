## Summary

VMS TMT Limited has finalized the allocation of 27,00,000 equity shares to anchor investors at an issue price of ₹99 per equity share (including a premium of ₹89 per share), successfully raising ₹26.73 crore ahead of its initial public offering.

## Key Points

- Total Equity Shares Allocated: 27,00,000 shares
- Anchor Investor Issue Price: ₹99 per share
- Total Amount Raised: ₹26.73 Crore
- Key institutional anchor investors include Saint Capital Fund, Maybank Securities Pte, Astorne Capital VCC, Chanakya Opportunities Fund I, and Vbcube Ventures Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with SEBI ICDR Regulations regarding anchor investor bidding, lock-in periods, and public issue disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

50% of the shares allocated to anchor investors are subject to a 90-day lock-in period, and the remaining 50% are subject to a 30-day lock-in period.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Investor Bidding Date: September 16, 2025
- IPO Opening Date: September 17, 2025
- IPO Closing Date: September 19, 2025
- Tentative Listing Date: September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides institutional backing and price discovery validation for VMS TMT Limited's public issue in the capital markets.
