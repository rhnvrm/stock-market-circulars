## Summary

 BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing securities newly shortlisted, securities moving to higher stages, and the consolidated list of securities under ESM effective from September 25, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities under the ESM Framework w.e.f. Sep 25, 2025 (e.g., Artificial Electronics Intelligent Material Ltd, Arunjyoti Bio Ventures Ltd, Emkay Global Financial Services Ltd, Explicit Finance Ltd, Integrated Industries Ltd).
- Movement of existing securities to higher Stage ESM (e.g., Achyut Healthcare Ltd, SAB Events & Governance Now Media Ltd, Sri Chakra Cement Ltd).
- Provision of a comprehensive consolidated list of securities under various ESM stages.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures in accordance with regulatory guidelines to monitor volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance margins and trading restrictions for the shortlisted and migrated securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Increased margin requirements and trading controls apply to securities placed under the ESM framework, potentially affecting trading volume and liquidity for the affected scrips.
