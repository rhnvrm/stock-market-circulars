## Summary

SEBI has issued guidelines specifying the mechanism for monitoring compliance with the Minimum Investment Threshold under Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs). In case of an active breach where an investor's aggregate investment falls below INR 10 lakh, the units will be frozen for debit and a 30-day notice period will be provided for rebalancing. Failure to rebalance within this period results in automatic redemption by the AMC.

## Key Points

- Active breach defined as total SIF investment falling below INR 10 lakh due to investor-initiated transactions.
- All units held across investment strategies of the concerned SIF are frozen for debit upon an active breach.
- Investors are given 30 calendar days to rebalance their investments.
- Unfreezing occurs if rebalanced within 30 days; otherwise, frozen units are automatically redeemed at the next business day's NAV.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandates daily monitoring of the Minimum Investment Threshold by AMCs with zero active breaches permitted.
- Introduces formal debit freeze and automatic redemption workflows for non-compliant SIF investors.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMCs, RTAs, and Depositories must implement necessary operational systems to track thresholds, apply debit freezes, issue notices, and execute automatic redemptions.

## Important Dates

- Date of circular / Effective date: July 29, 2025
- Notice period for rebalancing: 30 calendar days from notice

## Impact Assessment

Ensures strict adherence to SIF investment minimums, protecting market integrity while providing operational clarity for AMCs and RTAs managing specialized funds.
