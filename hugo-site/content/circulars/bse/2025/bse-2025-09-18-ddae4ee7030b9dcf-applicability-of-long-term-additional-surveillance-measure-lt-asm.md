## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability, stage revisions, and removal of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, effective September 19, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted and added to the Long Term ASM Framework (Annexure I, Part A).
- Securities continuing in the framework have been moved to higher stages (Annexure I, Part B).
- Specific securities are moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework effective September 19, 2025 (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under various ASM stages is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of heightened surveillance monitoring and trading restrictions (such as applicable margins) for securities falling under the Long Term ASM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable ASM stages and margin requirements for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to stricter surveillance controls, higher margin requirements, and potential liquidity adjustments.
