## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability, continuation, and removal of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective September 18, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisted new securities in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective September 18, 2025 (e.g., Abram Food Ltd, Escorp Asset Management Ltd, Parker Agrochem Exports Ltd, Rushil Decor Ltd, Shri Keshav Cements And Infra Ltd, TIL Ltd).
- Listed securities moving out of the Short Term ASM Framework effective September 18, 2025 (e.g., Adtech Systems Ltd, ANG Lifesciences India Ltd, ARCL Organics Ltd, Bartronics India Ltd, D.P. Wires Ltd, Flexituff Ventures International Ltd, Frontier Capital Ltd, Nagreeka Exports Ltd, Salasar Techno Engineering Ltd, Xtglobal Infotech Ltd).
- Provided a consolidated list of securities continuing in the framework or assigned specific stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures as per regulatory guidelines for short-term price movement monitoring.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance status of the specified scrips and apply appropriate margin and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the listed securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance margins and monitoring as mandated under the ST-ASM framework.
