## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the surveillance measures for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective September 16, 2025. The circular provides updates on securities shortlisted in Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) stages, exclusions, and a consolidated list of impacted securities.

## Key Points

- Gensol Engineering Ltd (Scrip Code: 542851, ISIN: INE06H201014) has been shortlisted in Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for Companies relating to the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per IBC Stage 1 w.e.f. September 16, 2025.
- Gayatri Projects Ltd (Scrip Code: 532767, ISIN: INE336H01023) has been excluded from ASM for Companies as per IBC w.e.f. September 15, 2025.
- Consolidated lists include multiple companies under various stages of the IRP and corporate disclosure requirements as per the IBC framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance stages under the IBC framework for identified listed entities to warn investors and manage market risk.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and apply appropriate trading restrictions and margin requirements as mandated for securities under the IBC ASM framework.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Inclusions/Stages: September 16, 2025
- Effective Date for Exclusions: September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in securities listed under the IBC ASM framework is subject to heightened surveillance, margin requirements, or trading restrictions, impacting liquidity and price movement for the affected scrips.
