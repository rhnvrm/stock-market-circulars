## Summary

Justo Realfintech Limited has announced the listing of its equity shares under an SME IPO on the BSE, detailing various lock-in periods and categories of shares.

## Key Points

- Total number of securities listed: 18,798,142 equity shares.
- Includes various lock-in tranches ranging from October 20, 2026, to October 20, 2028.
- Anchor investor shares included with lock-in expiration dates in October and December 2025.
- Free shares available under IPO: 3,551,000.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must adhere to the stipulated lock-in periods and depository procedures.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in start date: September 29, 2025
- Lock-in end dates: October 20, 2026; October 20, 2027; October 20, 2028; October 28, 2025; December 27, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard equity listing for the SME segment with no broader market impact.
