## Summary

This circular compiles clarifications received from multiple listed entities—Wonder Electricals Ltd., Banaras Beads Ltd., and IB Infotech Enterprises Ltd.—in response to inquiries from BSE and NSE regarding significant price or volume movements in their respective scrips.

## Key Points

- **Wonder Electricals Ltd.**: Clarified regarding a significant increase in trading volume, stating that the company has complied with all disclosure requirements under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015. The volume increase is market-driven and beyond management control.
- **Banaras Beads Ltd.**: Clarified regarding significant price movement, noting that promoters/directors have not bought or sold shares recently. The company highlighted a recent property purchase from VDA funded via a bank loan, duly disclosed previously.
- **IB Infotech Enterprises Ltd.**: Submitted a response to BSE regarding price movement, reiterating timely disclosures of all material events.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; routine compliance disclosure under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must promptly respond to stock exchange queries regarding unusual market activity and confirm compliance with disclosure obligations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Clarifications: October 1, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Provides transparency to investors regarding market activity with no fundamental corporate actions impacting valuation announced in these clarifications.
