## Summary

BSE Bulletin No. 124/2025-2026 outlines upcoming book-closure and record dates for various corporate actions scheduled by listed entities, including commercial paper redemptions, non-convertible debenture (NCD) part redemptions, interest payments, and dividend distributions effective from late September through November 2025.

## Key Points

- **Birla Corporation Ltd.**: Record date set for 19/12/2025 for commercial paper redemption.
- **Dhampur Sugar Mills Ltd.**: Record dates set for 08/10/2025 and 10/10/2025 for commercial paper redemptions.
- **Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd.**: Record date set for 03/10/2025 for an interim dividend of Rs. 2.5000 per share (250%).
- **Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd.**: Multiple record dates set for 29/09/2025 regarding NCD part redemptions.
- **L&T Finance Limited**: Scheduled various interest payment record dates for secured and privately placed NCDs through October and November 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes announced; this is a standard operational disclosure of corporate actions.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must ensure accurate record-keeping and timely processing of payments or redemptions as per the stated record dates.

## Important Dates

- **29/09/2025**: Kolte-Patil Developers Ltd. NCD Record Dates
- **01/10/2025 to 03/10/2025**: Edelweiss ARC and Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Record Dates
- **08/10/2025 to 31/10/2025**: Various CP redemptions and NCD interest payments
- **02/11/2025 to 10/11/2025**: L&T Finance debenture interest payment dates
- **19/12/2025**: Birla Corporation CP redemption record date

## Impact Assessment

Essential for debt holders, equity investors, and market intermediaries to track corporate payouts, redemption schedules, and eligibility for dividends or interest.
