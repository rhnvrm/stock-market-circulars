## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing the application, continuation, and removal of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective September 24, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted and added to the Short Term ASM Framework (Annexure I).
- Certain securities will continue in the ST-ASM framework but are moved to higher or lower stages.
- Securities meeting specific criteria are moving out of the ST-ASM Framework (Annexure II).
- A consolidated list of securities under various ST-ASM stages is provided under Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ST-ASM framework to monitor price volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must comply with applicable margin requirements and trading restrictions for securities placed under the ST-ASM stages.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance, higher margin requirements, or specific trading windows depending on their assigned stage.
