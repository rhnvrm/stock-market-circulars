## Summary

The IPO Committee of WeWork India Management Limited, in consultation with the book running lead managers, finalized the allocation of 20,806,548 Equity Shares to Anchor Investors at an Anchor Investor Allocation Price of ₹ 648 per Equity Share (including a share premium of ₹ 638 per Equity Share).

## Key Points

- Company Name: WeWork India Management Limited
- Total Equity Shares Allocated to Anchor Investors: 20,806,548 Equity Shares
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: ₹ 648 per Equity Share
- Book Running Lead Managers include JM Financial Limited, ICICI Securities Limited, Jefferies India Private Limited, Kotak Mahindra Capital Company Limited, and 360 ONE WAM Limited.
- Major anchor investors include ICICI Prudential Technology Fund, various HDFC Mutual Fund schemes, Ashoka Whiteoak ICAV funds, and Goldman Sachs Funds.
