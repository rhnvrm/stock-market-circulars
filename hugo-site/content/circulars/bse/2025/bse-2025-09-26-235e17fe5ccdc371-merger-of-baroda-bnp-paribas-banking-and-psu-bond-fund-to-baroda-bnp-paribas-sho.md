## Summary

This circular outlines the merger of various plans of Baroda BNP Paribas Banking and PSU Bond Fund into corresponding plans of Baroda BNP Paribas Short Duration Fund, detailing the transferor and transferee ISINs and scheme names.

## Key Points

- Merger of Baroda BNP Paribas Banking and PSU Bond Fund into Baroda BNP Paribas Short Duration Fund.
- Covers multiple plans including Direct and Regular Growth, Monthly IDCW Payout/Reinvestment, and Quarterly IDCW Payout/Reinvestment.
- Explicit mapping provided for transferor ISINs to transferee ISINs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Scheme amalgamation executed in accordance with mutual fund regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors holding units in the transferor schemes will receive units in the corresponding transferee schemes based on the approved ISIN mapping.

## Important Dates

- Effective as per the schedule specified by Baroda BNP Paribas Mutual Fund.

## Impact Assessment

- Existing unitholders of Baroda BNP Paribas Banking and PSU Bond Fund will be transitioned to Baroda BNP Paribas Short Duration Fund.
