## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the shift of specified securities from the Rolling segment to the Trade-to-Trade (T, XT, MT) segment, along with the list of securities retained in the Trade-to-Trade segment with a price band of 5% or lower, effective from October 03, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several scrips are shifted from the Rolling segment to the Trade-to-Trade segment with a price band of 5% or lower effective October 03, 2025.
- Includes both mainboard and SME segment securities.
- Annexure-II lists securities retained in the Trade-to-Trade segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Trade-to-Trade settlement for listed companies meeting specific surveillance or regulatory criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must ensure orders for affected scrips comply with Trade-to-Trade settlement restrictions and price band limits.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Liquidity for the affected scrips may be impacted due to the mandatory delivery-based trading requirement in the Trade-to-Trade segment.
