## Summary

M/s. Invade Agro Limited (Acquirer) has issued a Letter of Offer to acquire up to 19,47,077 fully paid-up equity shares representing 26% of the voting share capital of Chambal Breweries and Distilleries Limited at an offer price of ₹6.00 per share payable in cash, pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Acquirer: M/s. Invade Agro Limited
- Target Company: Chambal Breweries and Distilleries Limited
- Offer Size: Up to 19,47,077 equity shares (26% of equity and voting share capital)
- Offer Price: ₹6.00 per share in cash
- Regulation compliance: Regulation 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 and Regulation 31(A)(5) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public shareholders may consult their stock broker or investment consultant regarding participation in the open offer.
- Tendering period and acceptance procedures as outlined in the detailed public statement and letter of offer.

## Important Dates

- Reference Date: 2025-09-12

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit opportunity for public shareholders of Chambal Breweries and Distilleries Limited at the specified offer price and results in a change in substantial shareholding/control.
