## Summary

Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released information regarding the public issue of equity shares of face value of Rs. 10 each by JD Cables Limited. The Board of Directors, in consultation with Book Running Lead Managers, finalized the allocation of 17,80,000 equity shares to anchor investors at an allocation price of Rs. 152 per equity share (including a share premium of Rs. 142 per equity share).

## Key Points

- Company Name: JD Cables Limited
- Total Equity Shares Allocated to Anchor Investors: 17,80,000 shares
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: Rs. 152 per equity share (Face Value: Rs. 10, Share Premium: Rs. 142)
- Key Anchor Investors include Beacon Stone Capital VCC, Negen Undiscovered Value Fund, YR Investment Opportunities Fund, Mint Focused Growth Fund, and Moneywise Financial Services Pvt. Ltd.
