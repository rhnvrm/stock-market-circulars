## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability, continuation, and movement of securities under the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective from September 19, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted and added to the Long Term ASM Framework (Part A).
- Securities continuing in the framework but moving to higher or lower stages are specified (Part B & C).
- Securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework are listed in Annexure II along with reasons (such as movement to GSM, ESM, or Trade for Trade frameworks).
- A consolidated list of securities under the framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance measures like 100% margins or movement to specific ASM stages for shortlisted scrips to safeguard market integrity and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable ASM stages and maintain necessary margins for trading in the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and surveillance on listed scrips may temporarily impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected securities.
