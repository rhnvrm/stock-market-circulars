## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a circular detailing the list of securities shortlisted under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) Framework, as well as securities moving to higher or lower stages, or moving out of the framework entirely, effective September 19, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities are newly shortlisted in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework.
- Specific securities are moved to higher or lower stages of the ST-ASM framework.
- Certain securities are moving out of the ST-ASM framework due to various criteria including inclusion in other surveillance frameworks.
- A consolidated list of securities under the ST-ASM framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short Term ASM framework to monitor price volatility and speculation in designated scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 19, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhanced monitoring and potential higher margin requirements for securities placed under the ST-ASM framework, impacting trading liquidity and volatility.
