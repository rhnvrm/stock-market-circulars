## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a circular detailing updates to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. Effective September 30, 2025, several securities are newly shortlisted under the ESM framework, while others are moved to higher stages or maintained according to surveillance requirements.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities into the ESM framework effective September 30, 2025 (Annexure I, Part A).
- Securities moving to higher stages of the ESM framework (Annexure I, Part B).
- Consolidated list of securities currently under various stages of the ESM framework (Annexure III).

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance measures, including specific margin requirements and trading restrictions, to identified securities to ensure market integrity and investor protection.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants, trading members, and investors must take note of the applicable ESM stages for the listed securities and adjust trading and margining parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity and margin requirements for the affected scrips will be modified in accordance with the prescribed ESM framework stages.
