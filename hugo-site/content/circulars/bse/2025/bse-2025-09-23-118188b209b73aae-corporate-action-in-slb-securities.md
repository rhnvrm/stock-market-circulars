## Summary

This circular outlines corporate action details including book closure, record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods for securities participating in the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) segment.

## Key Points

- India Glycols Ltd: Dividend with Record Date, Ex-Date on 23-September-2025 and Shut Period from 22-Sep-2025 to 23-Sep-2025.
- Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd: Dividend with Record Date, Ex-Date on 03-October-2025 and Shut Period from 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.
- Tata Consultancy Services Ltd: Dividend with Record Date, Ex-Date on 15-October-2025 and Shut Period from 14-Oct-2025 to 15-Oct-2025.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine corporate action notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and SLB members must take note of the specified shut periods and ex-dates for the respective securities.

## Important Dates

- 23-September-2025: India Glycols Ltd Record / Ex Date
- 03-October-2025: Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd Record / Ex Date
- 15-October-2025: Tata Consultancy Services Ltd Record / Ex Date

Ensures transparency and smooth handling of corporate actions and lending/borrowing obligations during shut periods.
