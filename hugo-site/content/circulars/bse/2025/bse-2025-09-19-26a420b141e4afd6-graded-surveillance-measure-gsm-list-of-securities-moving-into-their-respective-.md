## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing the securities moving into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The circular lists companies shifting to GSM Stages II, III, and IV along with their respective security codes and ISIN numbers.

## Key Points

- Garware Synthetics Ltd (514400) and Jain Marmo Industries Ltd (539119) have moved to GSM Stage II.
- Lynx Machinery & Commercials Ltd (505320), Tirth Plastic Ltd (526675), and Mena Mani Industries Ltd (531127) have moved to GSM Stage III.
- Peoples Investments Ltd (501144) has moved to GSM Stage IV.
- Securities marked with (#) move lower in the GSM framework due to inclusion in the ESM framework, and those marked with ($) due to inclusion in the IBC framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance measures restricting normal trading and mandating additional trading safeguards for listed companies exhibiting abnormal price movements.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply the requisite surveillance margins, trading frequency restrictions, and deposit requirements as mandated for each GSM stage.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by exchange regulations for the current surveillance review.

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity for the affected securities is likely to be restricted due to enhanced surveillance stages, additional cash-lock-in requirements, and limitations on trading frequency.
