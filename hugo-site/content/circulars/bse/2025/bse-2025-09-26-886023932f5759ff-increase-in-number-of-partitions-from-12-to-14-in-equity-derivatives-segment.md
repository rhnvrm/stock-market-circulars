## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the increase in the number of partitions from 12 to 14 in the Equity Derivatives Segment, along with the release of Version 1.15 of the BOLTPLUS Connectivity Manual dated September 5, 2025.

## Key Points

- Number of partitions in the Equity Derivatives Segment increased from 12 to 14.
- BOLTPLUS Connectivity Manual updated to Version 1.15.
- Details interactive interfaces, market data streams (low and high bandwidth), reference data interfaces, routes, and specific production, disaster recovery, and simulation/test connection parameters.

## Regulatory Changes

- Infrastructure upgrade expanding the partition capacity for equity derivatives trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and market participants must review the updated BOLTPLUS Connectivity Manual (Ver. 1.15) and align their trading and market data systems accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Manual Date: September 5, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Affects algorithmic traders, brokers, and exchange participants connected to the BSE Equity Derivatives segment requiring updates to connection parameters and partition handlings.
