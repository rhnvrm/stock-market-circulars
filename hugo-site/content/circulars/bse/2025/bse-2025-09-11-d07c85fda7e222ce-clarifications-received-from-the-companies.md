## Summary

BSE sought clarifications from multiple companies regarding a significant increase in the volume of their stocks across exchanges. The responding companies (Dilip Buildcon Limited, Elecon Engineering Company Limited, and Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd) confirmed they are in compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, and have no undisclosed price-sensitive information.

## Key Points

- Dilip Buildcon Limited stated it promptly discloses all material events and has no pending price-sensitive information explaining the volume increase.
- Elecon Engineering Company Limited confirmed compliance with Regulation 30 and stated it is not connected with the recent volume movement.
- Himadri Speciality Chemical Ltd affirmed its compliance with listing regulations and timely reporting of material events.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must continue to promptly disclose all material events, information, and price-sensitive updates pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 11, 2025

## Impact Assessment

No direct operational or financial impact; standard administrative response to exchange surveillance queries.
