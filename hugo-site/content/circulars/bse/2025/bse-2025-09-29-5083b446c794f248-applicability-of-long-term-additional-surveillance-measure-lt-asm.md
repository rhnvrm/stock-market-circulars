## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. The notice details shortlistings, stage movements, and exclusions of various securities effective from September 30, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Part A:** Shortlists new securities into the Long Term ASM Framework (e.g., Chandra Prabhu International Ltd, Healthy Life Agritec Ltd) and moves existing securities to higher stages (e.g., Ambo Agritec Ltd to Stage II).
- **Part B:** Details securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework (e.g., Haria Exports Ltd, IEC Education Ltd) due to inclusion in other frameworks like ESM or GSM.
- **Part C & D:** Lists adjustments and direct stage movements under the framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Securities identified under LT-ASM are subjected to enhanced surveillance and margin requirements to safeguard market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages, higher margin requirements, and trading restrictions for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to heightened surveillance measures, potentially impacting intraday liquidity and leverage.
