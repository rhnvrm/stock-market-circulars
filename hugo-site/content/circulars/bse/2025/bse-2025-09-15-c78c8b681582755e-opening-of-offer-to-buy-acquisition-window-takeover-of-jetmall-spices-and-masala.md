## Summary

Mr. Raman Aggarwal, Mrs. Anju Aggarwal, and Mr. Shrey Aggarwal have issued a Letter of Offer to acquire up to 15,60,000 fully paid-up equity shares of face value ₹10 each, representing 26.03% of the voting capital of Jetmall Spices and Masala Limited. The offer price is set at ₹12.50 per equity share, payable in cash in accordance with SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- **Acquirers:** Mr. Raman Aggarwal, Mrs. Anju Aggarwal, and Mr. Shrey Aggarwal.
- **Target Company:** Jetmall Spices and Masala Limited.
- **Offer Size:** Up to 15,60,000 fully paid-up equity shares (26.03% of Voting Capital).
- **Offer Price:** ₹12.50 per Equity Share payable in cash.
- **Regulatory Basis:** Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.
- **Managers and Registrars:** Mark Corporate Advisors Private Limited (Manager) and Cameo Corporate Services Limited (Registrar).

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is made pursuant to SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.
- The offer is not conditional upon any minimum level of acceptance and is not a competing offer.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders may consult their stockbrokers, investment consultants, or the manager/registrar to the offer for clarifications regarding the tendering process.
- Form of Acceptance-cum-Acknowledgement and transfer deeds must be submitted appropriately through registered stock exchange members.

## Important Dates

- Dates are governed as per the detailed public statement and tendering period schedule published by the managers to the offer.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides an exit or participation opportunity for public shareholders of Jetmall Spices and Masala Limited through the acquisition window framework.
