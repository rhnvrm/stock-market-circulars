## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the movement of securities into respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages. Gujarat Investa Ltd. has been moved to GSM Stage I.

## Key Points

- Security Name: Gujarat Investa Ltd.
- Security Code: 531341
- ISIN: INE373D01017
- Moved to GSM Stage: I

## Regulatory Changes

The securities identified under the GSM framework are subjected to enhanced surveillance and trading restrictions to protect investors' interests and maintain market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors dealing in Gujarat Investa Ltd. must adhere to the trading restrictions and margin requirements applicable under GSM Stage I.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected security will be subject to surveillance measures such as trade-for-trade settlement or applicable margin restrictions, potentially impacting liquidity and trading volume.
