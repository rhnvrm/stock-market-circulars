## Summary

Deep Diamond India Limited has announced a revised schedule for its ongoing Rights Issue. The Rights Issue closing date has been extended from September 19, 2025, to September 24, 2025. Additionally, the trading period for Rights Entitlements (RE) has been extended to September 18, 2025.

## Key Points

- Rights Issue closing date extended to Wednesday, September 24, 2025.
- On-market renunciation date for Rights Entitlements (RE) extended to Thursday, September 18, 2025.
- The decision was approved by the Rights Issue Committee on September 11, 2025, to give shareholders more time.
- No changes to the original Letter of Offer (LOF) and Composite Application Form (CAF) dated August 29, 2025, other than schedule updates.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of corporate action timelines for the rights issue of Deep Diamond India Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders must submit their duly filled Composite Application Form (CAF) along with application amounts by September 24, 2025.
- Trading of Rights Entitlements must be completed within the revised renunciation window ending September 18, 2025.

## Important Dates

- Issue Opening Date: September 09, 2025
- Last Date on Market Renunciation of Rights Entitlements: September 18, 2025
- Issue Closing Date: September 24, 2025
- Finalization of Basis of Allotment: September 29, 2025
- Date of Allotment: September 30, 2025
- Date of Credit: October 06, 2025
- Date of Listing / Trading: October 07, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Provides extended flexibility for existing shareholders of Deep Diamond India Limited to evaluate, trade, and subscribe to the rights issue.
