## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the resumption of trading in the partly paid-up equity shares of Garment Mantra Lifestyle Limited following the receipt of call money.

## Key Points

- Total partly paid shares issued as of 10.09.2025: 39,14,97,795
- Total shares on which call money received: 37,34,26,591
- Shares available for trading on resumption: 37,34,26,591
- Paid-up value per share available for trading: Rs. 0.50
- ISIN: IN9653501026
- Distinctive Numbers: 200768101 to 574194691

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with BSE trading guidelines for partly paid-up shares.

## Important Dates

- Notice / Effective Date: 10.09.2025

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors holding the specified partly paid-up shares to resume trading on the exchange platform.
