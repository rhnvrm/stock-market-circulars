## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing and lock-in details of equity shares of Shringar House of Mangalsutra Limited. The circular outlines various categories of shares, distinctive numbers, paid-up status, lock-in periods, and whether the shares are held in physical or demat form.

## Key Points

- Company Name: Shringar House of Mangalsutra Limited
- Registered Office: Unit No. B-1, Lower Ground Floor, Jewel World (Cotton Exch Bldg), 175, Kalbadevi Rd, Bhuleshwar, Mumbai – 400 002, Maharashtra, India
- Details cover fully paid-up shares under lock-in as well as shares not under lock-in (IPO public issue).
- Lock-in end dates range across various milestones from October 2025 to March 2027.

None. This is an operational listing notification.
