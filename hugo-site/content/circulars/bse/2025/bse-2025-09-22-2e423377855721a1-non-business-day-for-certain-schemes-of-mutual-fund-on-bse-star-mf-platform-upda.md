## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding non-business days for certain mutual fund schemes available on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Key Points

- Effective date for non-business day: 2025-09-23
- Affected schemes include Nippon India Japan Equity Fund and ICICI Prudential Global Advantage Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors should take note of the non-business day designations for order processing on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Non-Business Day: 2025-09-23

## Impact Assessment

Transactions in the specified mutual fund schemes will not be processed on the declared non-business day.
