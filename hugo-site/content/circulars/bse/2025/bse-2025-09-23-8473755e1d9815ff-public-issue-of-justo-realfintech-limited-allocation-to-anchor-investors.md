## Summary

Justo Realfintech Limited has finalized its anchor investor allocation ahead of its public issue on the BSE SME platform, allocating 14,10,000 equity shares to 11 anchor investors at an issue price of ₹127 per share (including a premium of ₹117), successfully raising ₹17.91 crore.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares allocated to anchor investors: 14,10,000 shares.
- Issue price: ₹127 per share.
- Total funds raised from anchor investors: Approximately ₹17.91 crore.
- Number of anchor investors: 11 institutional and corporate investors.
- Prominent anchor investors include Mavira Growth Opportunities Fund, Invicta Continuum Fund I, and Finavenue Growth Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Fifty percent of the shares allocated to anchor investors are subject to a 30-day lock-in period.
- The remaining fifty percent of the anchor shares are subject to a 90-day lock-in period.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Allocation Date: September 23, 2025
- Public Issue Opening/Closing and Subsequent Listing Schedule as per BSE SME guidelines.

## Impact Assessment

Successful anchor book subscription indicates positive institutional interest prior to the opening of the public issue.
