## Summary

SEBI has issued guidelines specifying the mechanism for monitoring compliance with the Minimum Investment Threshold under Specialized Investment Funds (SIFs). In case of an active breach where an investor's total investment falls below INR 10 lakh, all units held across strategies of the concerned SIF are frozen for debit, and a 30-day notice period is provided to rebalance investments before automatic redemption occurs.

## Key Points

- Active breach is defined as a fall in the aggregate value of an investor's total investment across SIF strategies below INR 10 lakh due to investor-initiated transactions.
- Upon an active breach, all units of the investor in the concerned SIF are frozen for debit.
- Investors are given a 30-calendar-day notice to rebalance their investments.
- If un-rebalanced after 30 days, frozen units are automatically redeemed by the AMC at the applicable NAV of the next immediate business day.

## Regulatory Changes

- Detailed mechanism for monitoring minimum investment thresholds under SIFs introduced following industry feedback.
- Operational framework established for freezing, notice issuance, and automatic redemption of non-compliant SIF units.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMCs, RTAs, and Depositories must implement necessary systems to monitor thresholds on a daily basis.
- Issue 30-day notices to investors upon detection of active breaches and handle debit freezes and automatic redemptions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: July 29, 2025
- Effective Date: With effect from the date of the circular (July 29, 2025)

## Impact Assessment

Impacts mutual fund investors in Specialized Investment Funds by enforcing strict holding thresholds, requiring AMCs and RTAs to automate daily threshold monitoring, debit freezing, and auto-redemption workflows.
