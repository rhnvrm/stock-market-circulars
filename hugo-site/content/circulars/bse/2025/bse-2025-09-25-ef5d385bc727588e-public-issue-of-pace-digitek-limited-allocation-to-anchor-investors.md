## Summary

Pace Digitek Limited announced the finalization of the allocation of 1,11,93,807 equity shares to Anchor Investors for its public issue. The anchor allocation price is set at ₹219 per equity share (including a share premium of ₹217 per equity share).

## Key Points

- Company Name: Pace Digitek Limited
- Date of Board Meeting / Finalization: September 25, 2025
- Total Equity Shares Allocated to Anchor Investors: 1,11,93,807 shares
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: ₹219 per equity share (Face Value ₹2, Share Premium ₹217)
- Key anchor investors include Bandhan Small Cap Fund, SBI General Insurance Company Limited, Samsung India Small and Mid Cap Focus Securities Master Investment Trust, Taurus Mid Cap Fund, Taurus ELSS Tax Saver Fund, Saint Capital Fund, Societe Generale, Aarth AIF Growth Fund, Compact Structure Fund, Al Maha Investment Fund PCC, Necta Bloom VCC, Nexus Global Opportunities Fund, and Rajasthan Global Securities Private Limited.
