## Summary

Chandra Prabhu International Limited has announced the issuance of 92,45,000 bonus equity shares in the ratio of 1:2. Shareholders holding shares as of the record date will receive one (1) bonus equity share of Rs. 2/- each for every two (2) fully paid-up equity shares held.

## Key Points

- Bonus Ratio: 1:2 (1 bonus share for every 2 shares held).
- Total Number of Bonus Shares: 92,45,000 equity shares.
- Face Value: Rs. 2/- per share.
- Distinctive Numbers: From 18490001 to 27735000.
- ISIN: INE368D01025.

## Regulatory Changes

- The bonus issue complies with SEBI guidelines regarding the timelines for allotment, depository credit, and trading availability.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company must ensure the credit of bonus shares in the depository system latest by 12:00 P.M. on September 29, 2025.
- The company must ensure the bonus shares are available for trading at the beginning of the day on September 30, 2025.

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 26, 2025
- Deemed Date of Allotment: September 29, 2025
- Effective Listing / Trading Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the equity capital base of Chandra Prabhu International Limited and provides additional liquidity to existing shareholders through the distribution of bonus shares.
