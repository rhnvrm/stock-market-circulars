## Summary

BSE has released Version 1.15 of the BOLTPLUS Connectivity Manual, detailing an increase in the number of partitions from 12 to 14 in the Equity Derivatives Segment.

## Key Points

- Release of BOLTPLUS Connectivity Manual Version 1.15.
- Number of partitions in the Equity Derivatives segment increased from 12 to 14.
- Covers interactive interfaces, market data streams, routes, and connection parameters for production, disaster recovery, and simulation/test environments.

## Regulatory Changes

- Structural update to trading segment partitioning to support market scaling and data distribution.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and participants must update their connectivity configurations and trading applications in accordance with the updated BOLTPLUS manual specifications.

## Important Dates

- Manual Version Date: 5th September 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Moderate operational impact for trading members requiring system updates to accommodate the expanded partition count in the Equity Derivatives segment.
