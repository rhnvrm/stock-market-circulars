## Summary

BSE has released an updated list of securities shortlisted under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from September 22, 2025. The notification outlines multiple securities subject to enhanced monitoring and trading restrictions based on specific criteria.

## Key Points

- Lists companies moved into the GSM framework effective September 22, 2025.
- Specifies criteria (Criteria 1, Criteria 2, etc.) applicable to each listed security.
- Covers numerous scrip codes and names across various sectors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures on specified securities to safeguard investors and maintain market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the securities placed under the GSM framework and apply appropriate trading restrictions and margin requirements.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the listed securities may be subject to additional surveillance safeguards such as trade-for-trade settlement or other applicable restrictions.
