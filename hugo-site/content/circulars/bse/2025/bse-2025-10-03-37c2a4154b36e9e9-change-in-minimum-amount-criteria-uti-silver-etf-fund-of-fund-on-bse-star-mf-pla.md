## Summary

UTI Asset Management Company Limited has issued an addendum (No. 62/2025-26) announcing a revision in the minimum investment, additional purchase, systematic investment plan (SIP), and redemption amounts for the UTI Silver ETF Fund of Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, effective October 3, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Minimum Initial Investment:** Revised from Rs. 5,000/- to Rs. 100/- in multiples of Re. 1/- thereafter.
- **Minimum SIP Amount:** Revised from Rs. 500/- to Rs. 100/- for daily, weekly, and monthly SIP frequencies.
- **Subsequent Minimum Investment:** Revised from Rs. 1,000/- to Rs. 100/- under a folio.
- **Minimum Redemption Amount:** Revised from Rs. 1,000/- to Rs. 100/- in multiples of Re. 1/- thereafter.

## Regulatory Changes

- This addendum forms an integral part of the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) of the scheme.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors and distributors must take note of the revised monetary thresholds for transactions in the UTI Silver ETF Fund of Fund.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 3, 2025
- **Notice Date:** September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Low operational impact; makes the fund more accessible by lowering entry barriers and minimum transaction limits for investors.
