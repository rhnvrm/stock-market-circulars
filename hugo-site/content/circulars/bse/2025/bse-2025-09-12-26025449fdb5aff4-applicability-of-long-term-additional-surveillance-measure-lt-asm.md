## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective September 15, 2025, specific securities are shortlisted under the framework, moved to higher/lower stages, or excluded entirely based on surveillance reviews.

## Key Points

- Annexure I Part A: Lists securities newly shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. September 15, 2025 (e.g., Binny Mills Ltd, Maitri Enterprises Ltd, Olympic Oil Industries Ltd, Sharika Enterprises Ltd, Toyam Sports Ltd).
- Annexure I Part C: Lists securities moving to respective lower Stage ASM (e.g., Ambo Agritec Ltd, GEE Ltd, Indo Thai Securities Ltd).
- Annexure II: Lists securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework entirely (e.g., 3P Land Holdings Ltd, BPL Ltd, CCL Products (India) Ltd, Cochin Shipyard Ltd, Kitex Garments Ltd).

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance measures, margin requirements, and trading controls on shortlisted scrips in accordance with SEBI and exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable ASM stages and adjust margins and trading limits accordingly for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements or removal of restrictions for securities entering or exiting the LT-ASM framework, directly impacting liquidity and trading behavior for the specified scrips.
