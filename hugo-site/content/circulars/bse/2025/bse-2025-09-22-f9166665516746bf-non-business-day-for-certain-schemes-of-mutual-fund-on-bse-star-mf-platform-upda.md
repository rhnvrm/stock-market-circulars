## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding non-business days for specific schemes of mutual funds on the BSE StAR MF platform, affecting transactions for Nippon India and ICICI Prudential schemes on September 23, 2025.

## Key Points

- Declares non-business day status for Nippon India Japan Equity Fund on 2025-09-23.
- Declares non-business day status for ICICI Prudential Global Advantage Fund on 2025-09-23.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Participants should take note of the non-business day declarations while processing orders for the affected schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: 2025-09-23

## Impact Assessment

Transactions, subscriptions, or redemptions for the specified mutual fund schemes will not be processed on the declared non-business day.
