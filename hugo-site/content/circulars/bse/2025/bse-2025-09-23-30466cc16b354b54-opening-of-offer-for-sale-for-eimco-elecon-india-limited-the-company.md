## Summary

Tamrock Great Britain Holdings Limited, a member of the promoter group of Eimco Elecon (India) Limited, proposes to sell up to 7,23,875 equity shares (approximately 12.55% of the total issued and paid-up equity share capital) as the Base Offer Size, with an additional oversubscription option of up to 7,00,000 equity shares (approximately 12.14%). The total offer represents up to 24.68% of the paid-up equity share capital.

## Key Points

- Seller: Tamrock Great Britain Holdings Limited (Promoter Group).
- Company: Eimco Elecon (India) Limited.
- Base Offer Size: 7,23,875 equity shares (12.55%).
- Oversubscription Option: Up to 7,00,000 equity shares (12.14%).
- Total Offer Size (including oversubscription): 14,23,875 equity shares (24.68%).
- Dates: September 24, 2025 (T day for non-Retail Investors) and September 25, 2025 (T+1 day for Retail Investors and carrying forward unallotted non-Retail bids).
- Seller's Broker: Spark Institutional Equities Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI master circular reference SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 and stock exchange operational guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Execution through a separate designated window of BSE and NSE via the seller's broker in compliance with OFS guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: September 23, 2025
- T Day (Non-Retail Investors): September 24, 2025
- T+1 Day (Retail Investors & Unallotted Non-Retail Bids): September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Significant secondary market share transaction affecting promoter holding percentage and public float of Eimco Elecon (India) Limited.
