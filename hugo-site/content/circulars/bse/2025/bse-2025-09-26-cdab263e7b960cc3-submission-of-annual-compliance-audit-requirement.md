## Summary

BSE has released a user manual for Investment Advisers (IA) and Research Analysts (RA) detailing the process for submitting the Audit Compliance Report (ACR) and Action Taken Report (ATR) via the BSE membership portal.

## Key Points

- Access the BSE portal via the provided membership link using existing credentials.
- Navigate to Post Registration / Annual Compliance reporting -> Annual Compliance reporting -> Filling.
- Submission involves two steps: filling and submitting the ACR report, followed by uploading the ATR report.
- Support email IDs are provided for IA and RA membership queries.

## Regulatory Changes

- Standardized online submission workflow for annual compliance reporting for IAs and RAs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must fill out the ACR form based on the auditor's report, upload the signed audit report along with the annexure, and submit.
- Members must complete the Action Taken Report (ATR) submission under the designated sub-tab and upload a signed copy on official letterhead.

## Important Dates

- Published on September 26, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines compliance filings for registered Investment Advisers and Research Analysts on the BSE platform.
