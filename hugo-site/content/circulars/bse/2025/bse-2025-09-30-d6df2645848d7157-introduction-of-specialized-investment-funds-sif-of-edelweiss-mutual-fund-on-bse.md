## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the introduction of Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) for Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform. The circular details functionality, investor eligibility criteria for accredited and non-accredited investors, NISM XIII examination requirements for distributors, manual and bulk upload procedures for client master data, and mode of holding validation rules.

## Key Points

- Separate AMC creation required for SIF as confirmed by RTAs, now activated on the BSE StAR MF platform.
- Non-Accredited Investors can invest a minimum of ₹10 lakh for first-time investment in any SIF scheme.
- Accredited Investors must invest the minimum amount set by the AMC in Scheme Information Documents (SID).
- SIF client AUM must not fall below ₹10 lakh due to redemption or other reasons in AMC/RTA records.
- Members and distributors must pass the NISM XIII examination and register with AMFI to distribute SIFs.
- Facility provided to update Accredited Investor details manually or via bulk upload (pipe-separated file) under Admin >> client details >> client master upload.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of specialized investment fund rules distinguishing between accredited and non-accredited investor minimum thresholds and holding validations.
- Mandatory NISM XIII certification and AMFI registration for SIF distributors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure correct and accurate updating of Accredited Investor details to avoid rejections and refunds from AMC/RTA.
- Renewal of registration certificates prior to expiry for accredited investors.
- Strict adherence to mode of holding (MOH) validation rules for single and joint holdings when determining AI status application.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for Edelweiss Mutual Fund SIF schemes on the BSE StAR MF platform.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines operations for trading and managing Specialized Investment Funds on BSE StAR MF.
- Ensures proper verification and categorization of accredited versus non-accredited investors through mandated master updates and validation rules.
