## Summary

SEBI has issued operational guidelines and timelines regarding the compliance requirements for digital accessibility under the 'Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 and rules made thereunder- mandatory compliance by all Regulated Entities' initially dated July 31, 2025 (Circular No. SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/111). The circular outlines timelines for platform reporting, auditor appointments, accessibility audits, and remediation.

## Key Points

- Applicable to all Regulated Entities (REs) providing digital platforms for investors.
- Establishes clear reporting mechanisms and authorities across various categories of market participants.
- Mandates the appointment of IAAP certified accessibility professionals as auditors.
- Requires annual compliance reporting starting from April 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

- Clarification of reporting channels and designated authorities (Stock Exchanges/Depositories, BSE Ltd. for IAs and RAs, and SEBI for MIIs and others).
- Submission formats and consolidation requirements for REs managing multiple digital platforms.

## Compliance Requirements

- **September 30, 2025:** Submission of a list of digital platforms provided for investors.
- **December 14, 2025:** Appointment of IAAP certified accessibility professionals as Auditors.
- **April 30, 2026:** Conduct of Accessibility Audit for the digital platforms.
- **July 31, 2026:** Remediation of findings from the audit and ensuring full compliance.
- **April 30, 2027 (and annually thereafter):** Submission of final reports on annual accessibility audits.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** September 25, 2025
- **Initial Platform List Submission:** September 30, 2025
- **Auditor Appointment Deadline:** December 14, 2025
- **Initial Audit Completion:** April 30, 2026
- **Remediation Deadline:** July 31, 2026
- **Annual Compliance Date:** April 30, 2027

## Impact Assessment

Regulated entities must urgently review their digital touchpoints (websites, mobile apps, portals) to ensure compliance with digital accessibility standards, engage certified professionals, and establish systematic remediation workflows to avoid regulatory penalties.
