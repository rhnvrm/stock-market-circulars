## Summary

BSE issued an update regarding the safer participation of retail investors in algorithmic trading, specifying detailed operational modalities and an undertaking requirement for Algo Providers.

## Key Points

- Algo Providers must execute an undertaking on stamp paper and get it duly notarized.
- Providers warrant that all information provided in their application to BSE is correct and complete.
- Providers agree to pay applicable license fees, charges, and royalties levied by regulatory or statutory authorities.
- Products and Risk Management Systems must comply with requirements laid down by BSE and/or SEBI at all times.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of mandatory undertaking requirements for algorithm providers supplying tools to trading members.
- Strict compliance standards for algorithmic products and underlying risk management systems.

## Compliance Requirements

- Execution of the prescribed undertaking by authorized signatories of Algo Providers.
- Ensuring continuous compliance of algorithmic tools with BSE and SEBI norms.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular guidelines.

## Impact Assessment

- Increases accountability and governance for algorithmic trading providers catering to retail participants on the exchange.
