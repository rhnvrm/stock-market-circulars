## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Dev Accelerator Limited, including the submission of the shareholding pattern under Regulation 31(1)(a) post-issue.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Dev Accelerator Limited
- Class of Security: Equity Shares
- Date of Filing: 16.09.2025
- Regulation: Filed under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a standard corporate disclosure and listing notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure timely submission of shareholding patterns and disclosures as mandated under Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR).

## Important Dates

- Filing Date: 16.09.2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding post-issue shareholding structure for investors and regulators.
