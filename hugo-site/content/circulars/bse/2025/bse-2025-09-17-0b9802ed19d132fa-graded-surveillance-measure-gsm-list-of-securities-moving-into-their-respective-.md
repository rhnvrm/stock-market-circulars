## Summary

BSE has released a circular detailing the list of securities that are moving into their respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, effective for trading.

## Key Points

- Munoth Capital Markets Ltd, Maitri Enterprises Ltd, Revati Media Ltd, Suncity Synthetics Ltd, Omkar Pharmachem Ltd, and Adjia Technologies Ltd have moved to GSM Stage I.
- Pasupati Fincap Ltd has moved to GSM Stage III.
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower under the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the ESM Framework.
- Securities marked with ($) are moving lower under the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the IBC Framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of heightened surveillance and trading restrictions as per the Graded Surveillance Measure framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors need to take note of the applicable trading restrictions and margin requirements for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Liquidity in the affected securities may be constrained due to surveillance measures, affecting short-term trading dynamics.
