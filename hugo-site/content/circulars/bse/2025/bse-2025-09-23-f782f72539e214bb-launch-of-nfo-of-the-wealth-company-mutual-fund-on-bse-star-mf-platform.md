## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the launch and availability of New Fund Offer (NFO) for schemes of The Wealth Company Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform, including various plans under The Wealth Company Liquid Fund with a tentative allotment date of October 14, 2025.

## Key Points

- Launch of The Wealth Company Mutual Fund schemes on BSE StAR MF platform.
- Includes 12 variants of The Wealth Company Liquid Fund (Regular and Direct plans with daily, weekly, and monthly IDCW and growth options).
- Tentative allotment date for the schemes is October 14, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and distributors must ensure proper processing of transactions through the BSE StAR MF platform in accordance with existing guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Tentative Allotment Date: October 14, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Expands investment options available to investors and distributors on the BSE StAR MF platform.
