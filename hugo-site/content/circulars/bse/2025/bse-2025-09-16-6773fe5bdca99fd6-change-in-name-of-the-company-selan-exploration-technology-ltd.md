## Summary

Selan Exploration Technology Ltd has officially changed its name, as certified by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

- Company name change certificate issued.
- Stakeholders' rights and liabilities remain unaffected.

## Regulatory Changes

- None beyond corporate name modification.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company is required to display its old name alongside the new name for a period of two years as per Section 12 of the Companies Act.

## Important Dates

- Effective from the date of the certificate.
