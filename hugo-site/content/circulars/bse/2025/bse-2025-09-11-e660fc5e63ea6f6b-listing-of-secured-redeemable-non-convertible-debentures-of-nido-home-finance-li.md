## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and trading of Secured Redeemable Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) issued by Nido Home Finance Limited across multiple series with varying coupons, frequencies, and maturity dates, effective from September 2025.

## Key Points

- Issuer Name: Nido Home Finance Limited
- Security Type: Secured, Redeemable, Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs)
- Face Value: ₹1,000.00 per NCD
- Date of Allotment: 10-Sep-2025
- Multiple series issued with scrip codes ranging from 940645 to 940663.
- Coupons range from 9.00% to 10.50% per annum alongside certain non-coupon (NA) series.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must adhere to the listing agreements, terms of issuance, and periodic reporting requirements specified for debt securities.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allotment: 10-Sep-2025
- Redemption/Maturity Dates: Spanning from September 2027 to September 2035 depending on the series.
- First Interest Payment Dates: Various dates starting from November 2025 and September 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Standard debt listing expanding fixed-income investment options on the exchange platform.
