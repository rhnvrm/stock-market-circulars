## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a notification regarding securities moving into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. The circular lists specific scrips that have been transitioned to GSM Stage I and Stage II effective from the specified dates.

## Key Points

- Bijoy Hans Ltd (Security Code: 524723, ISIN: INE491D01017) has moved to GSM Stage I.
- Integrated Capital Services Ltd (Security Code: 539149, ISIN: INE682B01023) has moved to GSM Stage I.
- Polo Hotels Ltd (Security Code: 526687, ISIN: INE084C01012) has moved to GSM Stage II.
- Securities marked (#) move lower in the GSM framework due to inclusion in the ESM framework, while those marked ($) move lower due to inclusion in the IBC framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance monitoring and trading safeguards for identified scrips to protect investor interests and maintain market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply appropriate surveillance margins, trading restrictions, and monitoring mechanisms as mandated for securities placed under GSM Stage I and Stage II.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to heightened surveillance, additional deposits/margins, and restricted trading frequencies, potentially affecting short-term liquidity and price volatility.
