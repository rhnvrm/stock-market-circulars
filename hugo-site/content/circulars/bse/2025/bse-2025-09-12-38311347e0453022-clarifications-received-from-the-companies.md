## Summary

This BSE circular provides stock price and volume movement clarifications received from listed companies, specifically Prithvi Exchange (India) Limited and Rushil Decor Limited, in response to stock exchange queries.

## Key Points

- **Prithvi Exchange (India) Limited**: Clarified regarding significant price movement, stating the company is in compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations and has no undisclosed price-sensitive information.
- **Rushil Decor Limited**: Clarified regarding significant volume increase across exchanges, confirming no unpublished price-sensitive information exists that would impact operations.
- Both companies attributed recent market movements to prevailing market conditions and external factors beyond their control.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must continue to promptly disclose all material events and price-sensitive information in compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- **Clarification Date**: September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Routine compliance disclosures with no direct fundamental impact on the companies or broader market operations.
