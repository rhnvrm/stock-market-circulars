## Summary

Pidilite Industries Limited has announced the intimation of the record date for the issue of bonus equity shares. The deemed date of allotment is 24th September, 2025, with a total of 50,88,57,016 bonus shares to be allotted.

## Key Points

- **Scrip Code:** 500331 (BSE)
- **ISIN:** INE318A01026
- **Number of Bonus Shares:** 50,88,57,016
- **Record Date:** 23rd September, 2025
- **Deemed Date of Allotment:** 24th September, 2025
- **Effective Listing Date:** 25th September, 2025
- **Distinctive Numbers:** 513857017 to 1022714032

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

The company has undertaken that bonus shares will be credited in the depository system latest by Wednesday, 24th September, 2025 by 12 P.M. (next working day of the record date) and available for trading on the beginning of the day (BOD) of T+2 day, i.e., Thursday, 25th September, 2025.

## Important Dates

- **Record Date:** 23rd September, 2025
- **Deemed Date of Allotment:** 24th September, 2025
- **Effective Listing / Trading Date:** 25th September, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Existing shareholders of Pidilite Industries Limited will receive bonus shares in their depository accounts, expanding the company's equity capital base and liquidity on the exchange.
