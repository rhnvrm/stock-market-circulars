## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) effective September 29, 2025. The notification lists securities newly shortlisted under the ST-ASM framework, securities moving to higher or lower stages, and securities moving out of the framework entirely.

## Key Points

- New securities added to the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective September 29, 2025 (e.g., Dynamic Industries Ltd, Evoq Remedies Ltd, GTT Data Solutions Ltd, etc.).
- Securities continuing in the framework but moving to higher or lower stages (e.g., Mercury Trade Links Ltd moving to higher stage, Zodiac Ventures Ltd moving to lower stage).
- List of securities moving out of the ST-ASM framework due to various criteria including inclusion in other surveillance frameworks or exiting entirely.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance actions under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) to monitor price volatility and market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable ST-ASM stages and ensure necessary margining and trading restrictions are applied to the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities may experience altered liquidity and margin requirements as a result of being placed in, moved within, or removed from the ST-ASM framework.
