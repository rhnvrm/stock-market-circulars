## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the lower revision of stages for various securities under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. A total of 57 securities have been moved to lower surveillance stages (e.g., from Stage 4 to Stage 3, Stage 3 to Stage 2, Stage 2 to Stage 1, or Stage 1 to Stage 0).

## Key Points

- Lists multiple securities undergoing a downward revision in their GSM stages.
- Provides Security Codes, ISIN Numbers, Security Names, Groups, and the respective From and To Stages.
- Facilitates reduced trading restrictions for companies that have improved their compliance or surveillance metrics.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of securities into lower Graded Surveillance Measure stages as per exchange and SEBI guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must note the revised GSM stages for the listed securities and adjust trading parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective date corresponding to the circular issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Easing of trading restrictions for the affected securities, potentially improving liquidity and normal trading operations.
