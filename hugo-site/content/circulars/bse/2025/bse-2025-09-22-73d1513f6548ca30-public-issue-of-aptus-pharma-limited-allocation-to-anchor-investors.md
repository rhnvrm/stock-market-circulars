## Summary

Aptus Pharma Limited has announced the finalization of its equity share allocation to anchor investors as part of its public issue of 18,60,000 equity shares of face value Rs. 10/- each. A total of 5,28,000 equity shares have been allocated to anchor investors at an allocation price of Rs. 70/- per equity share.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares offered: 18,60,000 shares of face value Rs. 10/- each.
- Anchor investor portion allocated: 5,28,000 equity shares.
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: Rs. 70/- per equity share (including share premium of Rs. 60/-).
- Major Anchor Investors include 31 Degrees North Fund, Eminence Global Fund PCC, and Zeal Global Opportunities Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- As per SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2009, if the final offer price discovered through the book building process is higher than the anchor investor allocation price, anchor investors must pay the difference by the specified pay-in deadline.

## Compliance Requirements

- Anchor investors are required to fulfill any additional payment obligations if the discovered offer price exceeds the anchor allocation price, as per the revised CAN.

## Important Dates

- Allocation Date: September 22, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Marks a key milestone in the public issue process for Aptus Pharma Limited, establishing institutional interest and pricing baseline prior to full public bidding.
