## Summary

SEBI issued circular SEBI/HO/ITD-1/ITD_VIAP/P/CIR/2025/131 on September 25, 2025, providing compliance guidelines and reporting mechanisms for the digital accessibility mandates under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. All Regulated Entities (REs) must comply with specified submission dates, including listing digital platforms, appointing IAAP certified accessibility professionals as auditors, conducting audits, and remediating findings.

## Key Points

- REs must submit a list of digital platforms provided for investors.
- Mandatory appointment of IAAP certified accessibility professionals as auditors.
- Accessibility audits must be conducted for all applicable digital platforms with findings remediated within specified timelines.
- Annual compliance reports must be submitted within 30 days of each financial year starting April 30, 2027.

## Regulatory Changes

Clarifies the reporting authorities and submission timelines for various regulated entities under the Digital Accessibility circular originally issued on July 31, 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

- Submission of digital platforms list (Table-C1): September 30, 2025
- Appointment of IAAP certified accessibility auditor (Table-C2): December 14, 2025
- Conduct of Accessibility Audit (Table-C3): April 30, 2026
- Remediation of findings (Table-C4): July 31, 2026
- Annual compliance submission: April 30, 2027 onwards

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: September 25, 2025
- Initial Platform List Deadline: September 30, 2025
- Final Remediation Deadline: July 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Regulated entities must review their digital interfaces, engage certified accessibility auditors, and implement technical remedies to ensure compliance with digital accessibility standards for persons with disabilities.
