## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the measure in respect of companies with high promoter as well as non-promoter encumbrance as per Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011, effective September 23, 2025. The notification provides lists of securities shortlisted, moving out of, and a consolidated list under the framework.

## Key Points

- Effective date for the changes is September 23, 2025.
- Annexure I lists no new securities shortlisted under the measure.
- Annexure II details securities moving out of the framework, including Tulsyan NEC Ltd (Scrip Code: 513629), noting movement due to inclusion in the LT ASM framework.
- Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities currently under the encumbrance framework, including DJS Stock & Shares Ltd, Future Market Networks Ltd, Hindusthan National Glass & Industries Ltd, Thyrocare Technologies Ltd, and Tourism Finance Corporation of India Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Updates to the list of companies subject to high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance surveillance measures under Regulation 28(3) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and listed entities must take note of the revised status of securities under the high encumbrance framework for trading and surveillance monitoring.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading restrictions and monitoring for securities identified with high promoter and non-promoter encumbrance levels.
