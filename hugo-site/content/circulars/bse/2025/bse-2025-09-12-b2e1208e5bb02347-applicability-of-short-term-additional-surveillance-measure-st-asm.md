## Summary

BSE has issued a circular detailing the list of securities shortlisted under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, those moving to higher or lower stages, and those moving out of the framework effective September 15, 2025.

## Key Points

- Inclusion of several securities into the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework.
- Movement of specific securities to higher or lower stages of the ST-ASM framework.
- Removal of multiple securities from the ST-ASM framework due to various criteria including inclusion in LT-ASM, Trade for Trade framework, or normal market operations.

## Regulatory Changes

Surveillance measures applied to specified scrips to monitor volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affected securities may experience modified margin requirements and trading restrictions depending on their assigned ASM stage.
