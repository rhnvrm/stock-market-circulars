## Summary

Solarworld Energy Solutions Limited has finalized its anchor investor allocation ahead of its initial public offering (IPO), raising ₹220.50 crore by allotting 62,82,051 equity shares to 15 anchor investors at the upper price band of ₹351 per share.

## Key Points

- Total funds raised from anchor investors: ₹220.50 crore.
- Total number of anchor investors: 15.
- Allocation price: ₹351 per share.
- Total equity shares allotted: 62,82,051 shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Standard compliance procedures for IPO anchor investor allocation under SEBI guidelines apply.

## Compliance Requirements

- Lock-in periods apply to the shares allotted to anchor investors: 50% of the shares are locked in for 30 days, and the remaining 50% for 90 days.

## Important Dates

- Anchor allocation date: September 22, 2025.
- Public issue opening date: September 23, 2025.
- Public issue closing date: September 25, 2025.
