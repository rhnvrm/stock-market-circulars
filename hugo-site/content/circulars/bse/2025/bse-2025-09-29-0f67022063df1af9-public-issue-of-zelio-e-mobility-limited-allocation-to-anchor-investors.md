## Summary

Zelio E-Mobility Limited announced the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors as part of its public issue on the BSE SME platform. The company successfully allocated 16,39,000 equity shares at the upper price band of ₹136 per share, aggregating to approximately ₹22.29 crore.

## Key Points

- **Total Shares Allocated:** 16,39,000 equity shares to anchor investors.
- **Issue Price:** ₹136 per share (upper end of the price band).
- **Funds Raised:** Approximately ₹22.29 crore.
- **Anchor Investors:** Allocation made across 8 anchor investors, including Sanshi Fund-I, India Max Investment Fund Ltd, Carnelian AIF Cat I Trust, Viney Growth Fund, Finavenue Growth Fund, Rajasthan Global Securities Pvt Ltd, SB Opportunities Fund, and Sunrise Opportunities Fund.
- **Lead Manager:** Hem Securities Limited.
