## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Market Wide Position Limits (MWPL), Client Level Position Limits, and Position Limits for Clearing Members (CM) and Institutional Investors for various scrip codes and ISINs traded under the Securities Lending and Borrowing Scheme (SLBS) for October 2025.

## Key Points

- Details position limits for numerous ETFs and corporate securities under SLBS.
- Specifies Market Wide Position Limit (No. of Shares).
- Specifies Client Level Position Limit (No. of Shares).
- Specifies Position Limit for CM and Institutional Investor (No. of Shares).

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must adhere to the prescribed position limits for SLBS transactions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 2025

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and compliance in securities lending and borrowing activities across eligible stocks and ETFs.
