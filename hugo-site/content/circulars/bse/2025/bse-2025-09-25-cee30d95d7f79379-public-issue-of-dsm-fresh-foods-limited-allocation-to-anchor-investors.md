## Summary

DSM Fresh Foods Limited has finalized the allocation of 16,66,800 equity shares to anchor investors at an allocation price of ₹101 per equity share (including a share premium of ₹91) ahead of its public issue.

## Key Points

- **Company Name:** DSM Fresh Foods Limited
- **Total Anchor Shares Allocated:** 16,66,800 Equity Shares
- **Anchor Investor Allocation Price:** ₹101 per share (Face Value: ₹10, Share Premium: ₹91)
- **Total Anchor Amount:** ₹16,83,46,800
- **Key Anchor Investors:** Viney Growth Fund, Craft Emerging Market Fund PCC-Elite Capital Fund, Craft Emerging Market Fund PCC-Citadel Capital Fund, J4S Venture Fund – 1, Narnolia India Opportunity Fund, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Conducted under standard SEBI ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- In case the final offer price discovered through the book building process exceeds the anchor investor allocation price, anchor investors must pay the difference by the pay-in date specified in the revised CAN.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Allocation / Meeting:** September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Marks a key step in the book-building and IPO process for DSM Fresh Foods Limited, indicating institutional participation and anchor commitment prior to the public opening.
