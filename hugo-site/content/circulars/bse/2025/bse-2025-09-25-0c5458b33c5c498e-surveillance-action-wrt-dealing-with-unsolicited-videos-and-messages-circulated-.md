## Summary

BSE has updated its surveillance action concerning securities in which unsolicited videos and messages have been circulated on social media platforms, effective September 26, 2025. Silverline Technologies Ltd has been placed under surveillance with applicable trading restrictions.

## Key Points

- Silverline Technologies Ltd (Scrip Code: 500389, ISIN: INE368A01021) has been added to the list of scrips with unsolicited social media circulation effective September 26, 2025.
- The applicable group for the security is XT with a price band of 0.05.
- No securities are moving out of this surveillance list as per Annexure II.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of specific surveillance measures and price band restrictions on identified scrips to protect investors from misleading promotional content on social media.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must take note of the applicable price band and trading restrictions for the listed security.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected security will be subject to strict surveillance controls, limiting speculative volatility driven by unsolicited social media recommendations.
