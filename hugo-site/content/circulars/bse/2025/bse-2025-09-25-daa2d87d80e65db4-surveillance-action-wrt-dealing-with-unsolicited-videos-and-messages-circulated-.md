## Summary

BSE has issued a surveillance update regarding scrips in which unsolicited videos and messages have been circulated on social media platforms, placing specific securities under restrictions effective September 26, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Silverline Technologies Ltd** (Scrip Code: 500389, ISIN: INE368A01021) has been placed under surveillance with applicable Group XT and a price band of 0.05.
- The action is effective from September 26, 2025.
- No scrips are moving out of this surveillance category in this update.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of specific trading restrictions and price bands on securities identified as subjects of unsolicited social media campaigns.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors are advised to exercise caution while dealing in the specified scrip and verify information originating from social media channels.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected scrip will be subject to strict price band constraints, mitigating potential market manipulation risks arising from unsolicited promotional activities on social media.
