## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of equity shares of KVS Castings Limited following its fresh IPO issue, detailing various tranches of securities under lock-in and fully paid categories.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares include fresh IPO shares and securities under various lock-in periods.
- Specific lock-in expiry dates range from late 2025 to late 2028 depending on the category of shares (e.g., Anchor shares, fully paid lock-in).
- Total number of securities listed aggregates to 18,753,324 equity shares.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to lock-in periods as specified for respective distinctive number ranges.

## Important Dates

- Listing / Lock-in effective dates starting from 24.09.2025 and 03.10.2025.
- Lock-in expiry dates extend up to 07.10.2028.

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading and settlement of KVS Castings Limited shares on the exchange platform in the demat segment subject to applicable lock-in restrictions.
