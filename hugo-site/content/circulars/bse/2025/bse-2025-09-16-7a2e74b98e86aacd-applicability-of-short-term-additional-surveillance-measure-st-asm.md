## Summary

 BSE has announced the applicability, continuation, and exclusion of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework effective September 17, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted under the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework w.e.f. September 17, 2025.
- Multiple securities are moving out of the ST-ASM Framework due to various reasons including inclusion in other surveillance frameworks or regular criteria.
- A consolidated list of securities under the ST-ASM framework is provided for market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ST-ASM framework to monitor abnormal price movements and safeguard investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to surveillance parameters and applicable margin requirements as per the ST-ASM framework guidelines.
