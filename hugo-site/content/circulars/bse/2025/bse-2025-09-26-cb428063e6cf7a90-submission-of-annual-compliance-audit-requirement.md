## Summary

This circular outlines the requirements for Investment Advisers (IAs) regarding the submission of the Annual Compliance Audit Report (ACR), annual certificates for client-level segregation, and Action Taken Reports (ATRs) based on SEBI regulations and circulars.

## Key Points

- **Annual Audit Compliance Report (ACR):** IAs must conduct a yearly compliance audit from a member of ICAI, ICSI, or ICMAI and submit the required report.
- **Client Level Segregation:** As per SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/003, IAs must maintain an annual certificate confirming compliance with client-level segregation requirements within six months of the financial year end.
- **Action Taken Report (ATR):** Adverse audit findings, if any, must be submitted along with an action taken report approved by the individual IA or management of non-individual IA not later than October 31st of each year.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reinforces adherence to annual compliance audits, qualification/certification requirements, and principal officer duties under SEBI Investment Advisers regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must complete their annual compliance audit for FY 2024-2025.
- Obtain and maintain certificates for client-level segregation.
- Submit any adverse findings and Action Taken Reports within the prescribed timelines.

## Important Dates

- **Financial Year:** 2024-2025
- **ATR Submission Deadline:** Not later than October 31st of each year.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures operational transparency, strict adherence to client segregation mandates, and robust internal controls among registered investment advisers.
