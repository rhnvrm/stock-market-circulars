## Summary

BSE published clarifications received from listed entities regarding recent movements in their share prices. Both Jagsonpal Finance & Leasing Limited and Tirupati Foam Ltd confirmed they are in compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, noting that the price movements are market-driven and they have no undisclosed price-sensitive information.

## Key Points

- Jagsonpal Finance & Leasing Limited confirmed compliance with SEBI LODR Regulations and stated price movement is purely market-driven.
- Tirupati Foam Ltd confirmed no pending announcements or price-sensitive information under Regulation 30.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Companies must continue to promptly inform stock exchanges of any material events or price-sensitive information in accordance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: 29th - 30th September 2025

## Impact Assessment

Routine regulatory disclosure with no direct material impact on market operations or investors.
