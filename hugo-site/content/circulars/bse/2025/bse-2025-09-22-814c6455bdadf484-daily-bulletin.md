## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 120/2025-2026, dated September 22, 2025, provides corporate action notices including record dates, book closures, interest payments, dividend declarations, and commercial paper/non-convertible debenture redemptions across multiple listed companies.

## Key Points

- Details multiple commercial paper redemptions and record dates (e.g., Adani Enterprises Ltd., E.I.D. Parry (India) Ltd., Larsen & Toubro Limited).
- Announces dividend record dates and payments, including Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd. (Interim Dividend) and India Glycols Ltd. (Final Dividend).
- Lists interest payments and partial/full redemptions for Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) by entities such as Mahindra Rural Housing Finance Limited, Manba Finance Limited, and Moneyboxx Finance Limited.
- Includes equity corporate actions such as rights issues for Healthy Life Agritec Limited.
