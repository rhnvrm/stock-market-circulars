## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework. Effective from September 17, 2025, specific securities have been shortlisted under the Long Term ASM Framework as detailed in the annexures.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. September 17, 2025 (Annexure I, Part A):
  - 7Seas Entertainment Ltd (Scrip Code: 540874, ISIN: INE454F01010)
  - ACE Software Exports Ltd (Scrip Code: 531525, ISIN: INE849B01010)
  - Global Capital Markets Ltd (Scrip Code: 530263, ISIN: INE062C01042)
  - Gujarat Investa Ltd (Scrip Code: 531341, ISIN: INE373D01017)
  - Indong Tea Company Ltd (Scrip Code: 543769, ISIN: INE0KN201016)
  - Jainco Projects India Ltd (Scrip Code: 526865, ISIN: INE966C01010)
  - Kavveri Defence & Wireless Technologies Ltd (Scrip Code: 590041, ISIN: INE641C01019)
  - Lippi Systems Ltd (Scrip Code: 526604, ISIN: INE845B01018)
  - MIC Electronics Ltd (Scrip Code: 532850, ISIN: INE287C01037)
  - Moschip Technologies Ltd (Scrip Code: 532407, ISIN: INE935B01025)
  - Southern Infoconsultants Ltd (Scrip Code: 540174, ISIN: INE298B01010)
  - Suyog Gurbaxani Funicular Ropeways Ltd (Scrip Code: 543391, ISIN: INE07GA01011)
- No securities were moved to higher or lower stages or out of the framework in Parts B, C, D, or Annexure II for this effective date.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the LT-ASM framework to monitor volatility and protect investors in designated scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply applicable surveillance margins and trading checks for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements for trading in the shortlisted securities, potentially restricting speculative volume and influencing liquidity.
