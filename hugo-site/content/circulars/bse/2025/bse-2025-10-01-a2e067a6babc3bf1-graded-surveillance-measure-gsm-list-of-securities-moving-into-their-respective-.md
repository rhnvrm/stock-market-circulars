## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released a notification regarding the movement of securities into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.

## Key Points

- Mudunuru Ltd has been moved to GSM Stage 0.
- Bijoy Hans Ltd, City Online Services Limited, and Adjia Technologies Limited have been moved to GSM Stage II.
- Charms Industries Ltd has been moved to GSM Stage IV.
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower under the GSM framework due to inclusion in the ESM framework.
- Securities marked with ($) are moving lower under the GSM framework due to inclusion in the IBC framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of heightened surveillance monitoring and trading restrictions for specified securities as part of market integrity measures.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and apply necessary trading restrictions or margin requirements.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As per exchange applicability.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to surveillance constraints such as trade-for-trade settlement or additional margins depending on their assigned GSM stage.
