## Summary

BSE has announced the finalization of the anchor investor allocation for the public issue of Orkla India Limited. A total of 68,43,900 equity shares have been successfully allocated to 30 anchor investors at the upper price band of ₹730 per equity share, aggregating to ₹499.67 crores.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares allocated: 68,43,900 shares.
- Anchor allocation price: ₹730 per equity share (upper end of price band).
- Total funds raised from anchor investors: ₹499.67 crores.
- Total number of participating anchor investors: 30 institutional investors.
- Substantial participation from domestic mutual funds, including 13 schemes across 6 fund houses, accounting for approximately 42.4% of the anchor book.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and participating institutional investors must adhere to SEBI ICDR regulations regarding lock-in periods for anchor investor shares (30% of the anchor portion locked for 30 days, and the remaining 70% locked for 90 days).

## Important Dates

- Anchor Investor Allocation Date: October 28, 2025
- Public Issue Opening Date: October 29, 2025
- Public Issue Closing Date: October 31, 2025

## Impact Assessment

The successful anchor book allocation signals strong institutional interest in Orkla India Limited's public offering, setting a positive tone for the upcoming IPO subscription window.
