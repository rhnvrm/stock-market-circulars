## Summary

BSE has announced the listing and lock-in details for the equity shares of Telge Projects Limited following its Initial Public Offering (IPO). The circular details distinctive numbers, fully paid status, and lock-in expiration dates for various tranches of shares.

## Key Points

- Entity: Telge Projects Limited
- Event: IPO Equity Listing and Lock-in Schedule
- Security Type: Fully Paid Equity Shares
- Various lock-in periods ranging from October 2025 to October 2028 for promoter and anchor investor blocks.
- Certain tranches designated as IPO free shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- Official admission of Telge Projects Limited shares to dealing on the exchange platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and depositories must ensure adherence to the specified lock-in periods and distinctive number ranges.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in periods expire on various dates including 02/10/2026, 26/10/2025, 29/10/2025, 28/12/2025, and 02/10/2028 depending on the tranche.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading and settlement of eligible shares while maintaining prescribed lock-in constraints for pre-IPO and anchor portions.
