## Summary

Chandra Prabhu International Limited has announced a bonus issue of 92,45,000 equity shares in the ratio of 1:2 (one bonus equity share of Rs. 2/- each for every two fully paid-up equity shares held) as approved by members in the 40th Annual General Meeting held on September 10, 2025.

## Key Points

- Bonus Ratio: 1:2 (1 bonus share for every 2 shares held)
- Number of Bonus Shares: 92,45,000
- Distinctive Numbers: From 18490001 to 27735000
- ISIN: INE368D01025
- Record Date: Friday, September 26, 2025

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI Circular No. CIR/CFD/PoD/2024/122 dated September 16, 2024 regarding timelines for bonus issue allotment and listing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Deemed date of allotment: September 29, 2025 (T+1 day).
- Credit of bonus shares in the depository system latest by 12:00 P.M. on September 29, 2025.
- Bonus shares to be available for trading at the beginning of the day (BOD) on September 30, 2025 (T+2 day).

## Important Dates

- Record Date: September 26, 2025
- Deemed Date of Allotment: September 29, 2025
- Effective Listing Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Increases the paid-up equity share capital of the company and enhances liquidity for shareholders.
