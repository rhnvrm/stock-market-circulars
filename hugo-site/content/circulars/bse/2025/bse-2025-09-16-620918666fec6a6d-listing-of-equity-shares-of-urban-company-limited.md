## Summary

Urban Company Limited has announced the listing of its equity shares on the exchange, along with detailed lock-in requirements for various categories of shareholders, including anchor investors and alternative investment funds, effective from September 16, 2025.

## Key Points

- Listing of equity shares of Urban Company Limited.
- Specific lock-in periods defined for various share categories, including anchor investor shares locked up to 30 and 90 days (October 15, 2025, and December 14, 2025, respectively).
- Alternative Investment Fund Category II shares held by Acrobat Capital Fund I locked in until March 4, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with Regulation 17(c) of the SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018 regarding lock-in of specified securities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities holding shares under specific lock-in criteria must adhere to the defined restriction periods before trading or transferring securities.

## Important Dates

- Listing Date: September 16, 2025
- Anchor Lock-in Expiry (30 Days): October 15, 2025
- Anchor Lock-in Expiry (90 Days): December 14, 2025
- AIF Lock-in Expiry: March 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Governs the tradable float of Urban Company Limited post-IPO by enforcing mandatory holding periods for promoters, anchor investors, and specific fund categories.
