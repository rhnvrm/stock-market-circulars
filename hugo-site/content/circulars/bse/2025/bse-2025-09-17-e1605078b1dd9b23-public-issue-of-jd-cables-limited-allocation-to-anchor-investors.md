## Summary

Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a circular regarding the public issue of equity shares of JD Cables Limited. The Board of Directors, in consultation with Book Running Lead Managers, finalized the allocation of 17,80,000 Equity Shares to Anchor Investors at an allocation price of Rs. 152 per Equity Share.

## Key Points

- Company Name: JD Cables Limited
- Total Anchor Shares Allocated: 17,80,000 Equity Shares
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: Rs. 152 per Equity Share (including share premium of Rs. 142)
- Date of Meeting: September 17, 2025
- Includes allocations to various institutional investors and funds such as Beacon Stone Capital VCC, Negen Undiscovered Value Fund, and others.
