## Summary

BSE has issued a circular detailing changes to the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework. The updates include the inclusion of new securities, shifting of existing securities to higher or lower stages, and the removal of securities from the ESM framework effective September 12 and September 15, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities under the ESM framework w.e.f. September 15, 2025.
- Movement of certain securities to higher or lower stages within the ESM framework w.e.f. September 15, 2025.
- Removal of specified securities from the ESM framework w.e.f. September 12, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance measures such as trading restrictions, margin requirements, or monitoring as prescribed under the ESM framework guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the security classification changes and ensure appropriate risk management and compliance with trading constraints.

## Important Dates

- Effective date for removal of securities: September 12, 2025.
- Effective date for shortlisting and stage movements: September 15, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

Trading liquidity and margin requirements for the impacted securities will be adjusted according to their specific ESM stage placement.
