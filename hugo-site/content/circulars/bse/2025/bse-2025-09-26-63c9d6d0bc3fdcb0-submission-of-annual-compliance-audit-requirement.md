## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the submission of Annual Compliance Audit requirements for Investment Advisers for the financial year 2024-2025, detailing the requirement for an Annual Audit Compliance Report (ACR), Client Level Segregation certificate, and Action Taken Report (ATR) as specified by SEBI regulations.

## Key Points

- Annual Audit Compliance Report (ACR) must be conducted yearly by members of ICAI, ICSI, or ICMAI.
- Client Level Segregation annual certificate must be maintained and obtained within six months of the end of the financial year.
- Action Taken Report (ATR) for adverse findings must be submitted to IAASB/SEBI no later than October 31st of each year.

## Regulatory Changes

- Re-emphasizes compliance requirements under SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2025/003 dated January 08, 2025.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment advisers must submit the ACR, ATR, and client-level segregation certificates within the stipulated deadlines.
- Ensure appropriate management approval for any adverse audit findings.

## Important Dates

- Financial Year: 2024-2025
- ATR Submission Deadline: Not later than October 31st of each year

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures strict adherence to regulatory standards by investment advisers and promotes operational transparency.
