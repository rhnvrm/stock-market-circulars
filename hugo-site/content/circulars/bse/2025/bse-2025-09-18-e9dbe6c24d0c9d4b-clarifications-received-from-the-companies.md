## Summary

BSE Limited sought clarifications from various listed companies regarding significant volume movements in their shares. In response, companies such as PC Jeweller Limited, Prozone Realty Limited, and Radhika Jeweltech Limited clarified that all material events and price-sensitive information in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 have been timely disclosed. They confirmed that the movement in trading volume is purely market-driven based on investor perception.

## Key Points

- Stock exchanges issued letters inquiring about significant volume movements in the shares of respective companies.
- PC Jeweller Limited stated that price and volume behaviour is market-driven and based on investor perception of publicly disclosed information.
- Prozone Realty Limited confirmed no undisclosed price-sensitive information exists that warrants disclosure under Regulation 30.
- Radhika Jeweltech Limited reiterated its compliance with timely disclosures of all significant events and information affecting company operations.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard compliance communication under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must continue to monitor price and volume movements and promptly disclose any material price-sensitive information under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Date: September 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Neutral market impact as these are standard responses to exchange queries affirming adherence to ongoing disclosure norms without new fundamental catalysts.
