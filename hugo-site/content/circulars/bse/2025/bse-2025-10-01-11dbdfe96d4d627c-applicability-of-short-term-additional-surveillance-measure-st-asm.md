## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective from October 03, 2025. The circular outlines securities newly included in the ST-ASM framework, those moving out, and the consolidated list with their respective surveillance stages.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: October 03, 2025.
- Annexure I lists securities newly shortlisted in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework (such as Dhanlaxmi Cotex Ltd, Escorp Asset Management Ltd, etc.).
- Annexure II details securities moving out of the ST-ASM framework (such as Adani Power Ltd, Adani Total Gas Ltd, KIOCL Ltd, etc.).
- Annexure III provides the consolidated list of securities under various ST-ASM stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Applicability of trading restrictions and surveillance measures under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework for specified scrips to monitor market volatility and safeguard investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the inclusion and exclusion of securities under the ST-ASM framework and apply necessary margin requirements and trading safeguards as mandated by the exchange.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance margins and monitoring mechanisms as applicable under the ST-ASM framework stages.
