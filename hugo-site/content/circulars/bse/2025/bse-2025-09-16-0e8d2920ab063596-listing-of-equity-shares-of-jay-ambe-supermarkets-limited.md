## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of equity shares of Jay Ambe Supermarkets Limited, detailing the number of securities, distinctive number ranges, paid-up status, and lock-in expiry dates.

## Key Points

- Total securities listed include various tranches with different lock-in expiry dates ranging from October 2025 to September 2029.
- All securities are fully paid and in demat mode.
- Tranche details specify distinct number ranges and respective lock-in conditions.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and market participants must take note of the lock-in periods and distinctive number ranges for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Listing Date: 16/09/2025
- Lock-in expiry dates vary by tranche (e.g., 14/10/2025, 13/12/2025, 16/06/2026, 16/09/2026, 16/09/2029).

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading and settlement of the newly listed equity shares subject to applicable lock-in restrictions.
