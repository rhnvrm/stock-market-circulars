## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the launch of New Fund Offers (NFOs) with Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility for schemes offered by The Wealth Company Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform. The circular lists multiple plans across Arbitrage, Ethical, and Flexi Cap fund categories with a tentative allotment date of October 14, 2025.

## Key Points

- Launch of new fund schemes by The Wealth Company Mutual Fund on BSE StAR MF.
- Includes Arbitrage Fund, Ethical Fund, and Flexi Cap Fund in both regular and direct plans with growth and IDCW options.
- Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) facility is available for the NFOs.
- Tentative allotment date for all listed schemes is October 14, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors using the BSE StAR MF platform must ensure client orders and SIP registrations are processed within the stipulated NFO timelines.

## Important Dates

- Tentative Allotment Date: October 14, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors and distributors with expanded investment options in equity and hybrid categories via the BSE StAR MF platform.
