## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Euro Pratik Sales Limited, detailing lock-in periods, distinctive numbers, and share quantities.

## Key Points

- Listing of fully paid-up equity shares of Euro Pratik Sales Limited.
- Includes specific lock-in periods for promoters and pre-IPO shareholders, ranging from September 2025 to March 2027.
- Certain shares are marked as not under lock-in (IPO public shares).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to lock-in requirements as specified by regulatory guidelines for equity listings.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in start date: 19-Sep-2025
- Lock-in end dates: 18-Oct-2025, 17-Dec-2025, and 20-Mar-2026 / 20-Mar-2027

## Impact Assessment

Enables trading of the company's equity shares on the exchange platform while enforcing applicable lock-in restrictions.
