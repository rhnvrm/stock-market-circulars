## Summary

BSE has announced the listing of further equity shares issued by companies under Employee Stock Option Plans/Schemes (ESOP/ESOS). A total of 1,82,150 shares have been listed with varying lock-in periods expiring on October 16 and October 17, 2025.

## Key Points

- Total shares issued and listed: 1,82,150 equity shares.
- Lock-in expiration dates: October 16, 2025, and October 17, 2025.
- Allotment includes multiple tranches with distinct distinctive number ranges.
- Public category issuance under ESOP/ESOS frameworks.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; routine listing of securities pursuant to employee stock option exercises.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuing entities must ensure compliance with SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations and guidelines concerning lock-in periods for shares issued under ESOP/ESOS.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in Expiry: October 16, 2025, and October 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Routine corporate action resulting in minor capital expansion and change in the paid-up equity capital of the issuing entity.
