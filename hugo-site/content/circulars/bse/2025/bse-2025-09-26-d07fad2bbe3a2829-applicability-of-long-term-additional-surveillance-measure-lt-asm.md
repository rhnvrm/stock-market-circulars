## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the applicability of Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, detailing securities shortlisted, moved to higher/lower stages, or exiting the framework effective September 29, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Part A:** New securities shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework (e.g., Natural Capsules Ltd, Raj Packaging Industries Ltd, Unishire Urban Infra Ltd).
- **Part B:** Securities continuing in the framework but moving to higher Stage ASM (e.g., Indo Thai Securities Ltd, Shree Krishna Infrastructure Ltd, Vintron Informatics Ltd).
- **Part C:** Securities moving to lower Stage ASM (None listed).
- **Part D:** Securities shortlisted in Direct stage IV LT-ASM (Nil).
- **Annexure II:** Securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework (e.g., Aerpace Industries Ltd, Cosmo Ferrites Ltd, JSW Holdings Ltd, Yash Highvoltage Ltd).

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance measures including margin requirements and price bands as per the Long Term ASM Framework guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must note the revised stage classifications and surveillance applicability for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance monitoring, applicable margin requirements, and price restrictions effective from the specified date.
