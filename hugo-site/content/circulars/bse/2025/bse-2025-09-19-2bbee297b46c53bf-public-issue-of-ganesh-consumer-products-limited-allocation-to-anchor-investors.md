## Summary

Ganesh Consumer Products Limited has finalized the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors, raising approximately ₹122.33 crore ahead of its Initial Public Offering (IPO).

## Key Points

- Total anchor portion allocation: 37,99,362 equity shares.
- Price per share: ₹322 (upper price band).
- Number of anchor investors: 14 institutional investors.
- Major anchor participants include Subhkam Ventures Pvt Ltd, Bengal Finance and Investment Pvt Ltd, Samsung India Small & Mid Cap Securities Master Trust, Sanshi Fund-I, LC Pharos Multi Strategy Fund, and Rajasthan Global Securities Pvt Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Compliance with SEBI ICDR Regulations regarding anchor investor bidding and lock-in requirements.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to statutory lock-in periods for shares allotted to anchor investors as per regulatory guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Investor Bidding Date: September 19, 2025
- Public Issue Opening Date: September 22, 2025
- Public Issue Closing Date: September 24, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Strong institutional participation in the anchor round demonstrates robust market confidence in Ganesh Consumer Products Limited's public offering.
