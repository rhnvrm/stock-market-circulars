## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the securities moving into their respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, detailing the security codes, ISIN numbers, security names, and the corresponding GSM stages.

## Key Points

- Polo Hotels Ltd (Security Code: 526687) moved to GSM Stage 0.
- RR Securities Ltd (Security Code: 530917) and LE Lavoir Ltd (Security Code: 539814) moved to GSM Stage I.
- Wardwizard Healthcare Ltd (Security Code: 512063) and Charms Industries Ltd (Security Code: 531327) moved to GSM Stage II.
- Purohit Construction Ltd (Security Code: 538993) moved to GSM Stage III.
- Securities marked (#) move lower in the GSM framework due to inclusion in the ESM framework.
- Securities marked ($) move lower in the GSM framework due to inclusion in the IBC framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures by placing specified securities into appropriate GSM stages to safeguard market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance actions and margin requirements for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance measures, trading restrictions, and margin requirements corresponding to their assigned GSM stages.
