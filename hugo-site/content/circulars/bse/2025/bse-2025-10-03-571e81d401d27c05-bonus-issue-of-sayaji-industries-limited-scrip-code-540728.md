## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the bonus issue of equity shares by Sayaji Industries Limited. The company has announced a bonus issue in the ratio of 3:1 for eligible shareholders.

## Key Points

- Bonus Ratio: 3:1 (3 new fully paid-up equity shares of ₹5/- each for every 1 existing fully paid-up equity share of ₹5/- each).
- Scrip Code: 540728.
- Notice Number: 20251003-36.

## Regulatory Changes

- Capital restructuring to accommodate the issuance and allotment of bonus equity shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders as of the record date will receive the bonus shares credited to their demat accounts following customary settlement cycles.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 3, 2025
- Record Date: October 7, 2025
- Ex-Date: October 7, 2025
- Allotment Date: October 8, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders experience an increase in shareholdings in proportion to the 3:1 ratio, accompanied by a corresponding price adjustment on the ex-date.
