## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective October 01, 2025. The circular lists securities shortlisted under various parts of the LT-ASM framework, including new entries and consolidated lists.

## Key Points

- Several securities are shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. October 01, 2025, under Annexure I (Part A).
- Companies included include Capital Trade Links Ltd, Gourmet Gateway India Ltd, Mangalam Industrial Finance Ltd, Natura Hue Chem Ltd, and Ratnabhumi Developers Ltd.
- Annexure III provides a consolidated list of securities subject to different ASM stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures in accordance with the Long Term ASM Framework to monitor price volatility and market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must note the inclusion of specific scrips under the LT-ASM framework and adhere to the applicable trading restrictions and margin norms.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2025
- Circular Date: July 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the shortlisted securities may experience additional surveillance controls, higher margin requirements, or specific trading windows as mandated by the exchange's surveillance policy.
