## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP) as per the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), effective September 25, 2025. The circular details lists of securities shortlisted under various stages of the IBC surveillance framework.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of securities under the IBC surveillance framework effective September 25, 2025.
- Inclusion of securities in Stage 1 of the Additional Surveillance Measure for Companies relating to IRP under IBC, including Rajeswari Infrastructure Ltd (526823) and Sical Logistics Ltd (520086).
- Provision of a consolidated list of securities and their respective IBC stages.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates to the surveillance stages for companies under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance measures and trading restrictions applicable to the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Securities listed under the IBC surveillance framework may face heightened monitoring and specific trading restrictions to safeguard investor interests.
