## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability, continuation, and removal of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective September 23, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted and added to the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework.
- Securities moving out of the ST-ASM framework include those meeting criteria or transitioning into other frameworks like LT-ASM, ESM, or GSM.
- A consolidated list of securities under various stages of ST-ASM is provided for market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short Term ASM framework to protect investors and curb excessive price volatility in specific scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to specific surveillance margins and restrictions as per the ST-ASM guidelines.
