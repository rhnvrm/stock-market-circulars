## Summary

BSE Limited sought clarifications from multiple listed companies regarding a significant movement in the price of their securities. The companies have officially responded, stating that all material events, information, and price-sensitive announcements have been disclosed in a timely manner in compliance with Regulation 30 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- **TVS Electronics Limited:** Confirmed that all relevant events and price-sensitive information have been disclosed and no information is pending under Regulation 30.
- **Dynamin Industries Limited:** Clarified that the stock price movement is purely market-driven and that there are no pending announcements or undisclosed information affecting the stock price.
- **Nettlinx Limited:** Acknowledged receipt of the BSE query regarding price movement.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; standard compliance checks under surveillance mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must remain regular in disclosing material events and price-sensitive information under Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Clarification Request Dates: 26th September 2025
- Response Dates: 29th September 2025

## Impact Assessment

Routine market surveillance activity confirming transparency and continuous disclosure from the listed entities.
