## Summary

SEBI has released a consultation paper to review the existing framework for addressing technical glitches in stock brokers' electronic trading systems. The proposed changes aim to streamline compliance, exclude smaller stock brokers, clarify definitions, and rationalize reporting requirements.

## Key Points

- The framework will apply to stock brokers providing IBT/STWT trading platforms with more than 10,000 registered clients as of March 31 of the previous financial year.
- Approximately 457 smaller stock brokers will be exempted from the framework, easing their compliance burden.
- The definition of technical glitch is proposed to be modified to exclude occurrences outside trading hours and those beyond the control of stock brokers.
- Reporting requirements are streamlined to use a Common Reporting platform to avoid multiple reporting.

## Regulatory Changes

- Re-evaluation of the eligibility criteria for applicability of the technical glitch framework.
- Exclusion of technical glitches occurring outside trading hours and those not under stock brokers' control.
- Centralization of glitch reporting through a Common Reporting platform.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock brokers with more than 10,000 registered clients offering IBT/STWT platforms must adhere to the streamlined reporting and compliance guidelines once finalized.
- Smaller brokers falling below the threshold are relieved from these specific technical glitch framework requirements.

## Important Dates

- Consultation paper issued following representations and feedback from stakeholders and industry forums.

## Impact Assessment

- Significantly reduces compliance overhead for smaller stock brokers while maintaining rigorous oversight on larger technology-dominant brokers.
