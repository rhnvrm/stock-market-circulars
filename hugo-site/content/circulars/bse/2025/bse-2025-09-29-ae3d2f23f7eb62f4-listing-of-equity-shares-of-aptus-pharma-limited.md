## Summary

Aptus Pharma Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern disclosure under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, detailing the holding of specified securities for the quarter ending September 27, 2025.

## Key Points

- Listed Entity: Aptus Pharma Limited
- Filing under Regulation 31(1)(a)
- Report period for quarter ending: 27-09-2025
- Confirms no issuance of partly paid-up shares, convertible securities, warrants, or depository receipts.
- Indicates presence of locked-in shares with no promoter encumbrances or pledges.

## Regulatory Changes

- Routine compliance filing under SEBI LODR regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities are required to regularly submit shareholding patterns and disclosures regarding locked-in shares and capital structures as per regulatory timelines.

## Important Dates

- Quarter Ending: 27-09-2025
- Filing Date: 2025-07-25

## Impact Assessment

- Provides transparency to investors and regulators regarding the shareholding distribution and locked-in shares of the company without immediate market-moving impact.
