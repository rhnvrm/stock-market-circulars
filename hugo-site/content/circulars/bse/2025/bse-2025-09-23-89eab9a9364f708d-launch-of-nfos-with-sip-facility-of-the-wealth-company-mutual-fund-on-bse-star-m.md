## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the launch of New Fund Offers (NFOs) with SIP facility for multiple schemes under The Wealth Company Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF Platform.

## Key Points

- Covers arbitrage, ethical, and flexi cap schemes from The Wealth Company Mutual Fund.
- Includes both regular and direct plans with options for IDCW (Payout / Reinvestment) and Growth.
- Tentative allotment date for all listed schemes is October 14, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and distributors using the BSE StAR MF platform can facilitate client subscriptions and SIP registrations as per standard exchange guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **Tentative Allotment Date:** October 14, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides expanded investment choices for investors and distributors on the BSE StAR MF platform.
