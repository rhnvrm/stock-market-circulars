## Summary

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (Office of the Central Processing Centre) has issued a Certificate of Incorporation pursuant to the change of name for DMR Hydroengineering & Infrastructures Limited to DMR Engineering Limited, effective from August 22, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Previous Name:** DMR Hydroengineering & Infrastructures Limited (originally incorporated as DMR Hydroengineering & Infrastructures Private Limited).
- **New Name:** DMR Engineering Limited.
- **Corporate Identification Number (CIN):** L74900HR2009PLC039823.
- **Effective Date:** August 22, 2025.
- **Registered Office / Mailing Address:** H. No.- 473, Sector-30, NA, Faridabad- 121003, Haryana, India.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal recognition of the company's new name by the Registrar of Companies under rule 29 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company is obligated to display its old name for a period of two years along with its new name at all places wherever required in terms of Section 12 of the Companies Act.
- Stakeholders are advised to verify the latest status and public documents on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs website (www.mca.gov.in/MCA21).

## Important Dates

- **Certificate Date:** August 22, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative and branding update with no alteration to the underlying rights and liabilities of stakeholders.
