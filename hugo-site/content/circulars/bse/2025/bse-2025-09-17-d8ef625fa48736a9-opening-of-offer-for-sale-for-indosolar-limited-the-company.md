## Summary

Waaree Energies Limited (the "Seller") has issued a notice for the Offer for Sale (OFS) of up to 61,00,000 equity shares of face value of ₹10 each of Indosolar Limited, representing 14.66% of the total paid-up equity share capital of the company.

## Key Points

- **Total Shares Offered:** Up to 61,00,000 equity shares (14.66% of total paid-up equity share capital).
- **Seller:** Waaree Energies Limited (Promoter).
- **Target Company:** Indosolar Limited.
- **Dates of Offer:** September 18, 2025 (for Non-Retail Investors only) and September 19, 2025 (for Retail Investors).
- **Objective:** To achieve the minimum public shareholding in the company as prescribed under Rule 19A(5) of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Rules, 1957 and Regulation 38 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in accordance with SEBI master circular on OFS and stock exchange operational guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Bidders and prospective investors must read the entire notice along with SEBI OFS circulars before participating.

## Important Dates

- **T-Day (Non-Retail Investors):** September 18, 2025
- **T+1 Day (Retail Investors):** September 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Facilitates compliance with minimum public shareholding norms and adjusts promoter shareholding in Indosolar Limited.
