## Summary

BSE has issued a notice detailing the application of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework to various securities effective September 26, 2025. The circular lists securities shortlisted in the framework, those moving to higher or lower stages, and those moving out of the framework entirely.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been freshly shortlisted in the Long Term ASM Framework (Part A).
- Securities such as Jayabharat Credit Ltd and Pankaj Polymers Ltd are moving to higher Stage ASM II (Part B).
- Multiple securities are moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework due to inclusion in other frameworks like ESM, GSM, or Trade for Trade (Annexure II).
- A comprehensive consolidated list of securities under the ASM framework is provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Surveillance measures applied to specified scrips to monitor market volatility and safeguard investor interests under the LT-ASM guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply appropriate surveillance margins and trading checks for the listed securities effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities may be subject to enhanced surveillance margins or restrictions as per their respective ASM stages, impacting liquidity and trading parameters.
