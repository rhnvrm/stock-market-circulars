## Summary

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs has issued a Certificate of Incorporation pursuant to the change of name for Pacheli Industrial Finance Limited to Grand Oak Canyons Distillery Limited, effective from April 3, 2025.

## Key Points

- Former Name: Pacheli Industrial Finance Limited (originally incorporated as Dhoot Finance Company Private Limited)
- New Name: Grand Oak Canyons Distillery Limited
- Effective Date: April 3, 2025
- Corporate Identification Number (CIN): L74110MH1985PLC037772

## Regulatory Changes

- Company name change officially registered under Rule 29 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company is obligated to display its former name along with its new name at all required places for a period of two years in terms of Section 12 of the Companies Act.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Name Change: April 3, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Operational and market impact is minimal, requiring updates to records and stationery to reflect the new corporate name.
