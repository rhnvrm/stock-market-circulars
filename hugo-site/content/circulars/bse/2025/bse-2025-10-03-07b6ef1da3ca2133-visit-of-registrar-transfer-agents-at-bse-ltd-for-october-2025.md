## Summary

BSE has published the updated list of Registrar & Transfer Agents (RTAs) along with their corresponding scrip codes and long names for October 2025. This circular serves as an operational reference for market participants, investors, and listed entities to identify the official RTA handling share transfer, dematerialization, and investor services for specific scrips.

## Key Points

- Lists companies mapped to their respective Registrar & Transfer Agents (such as Adroit Corporate Services Pvt. Ltd.).
- Provides scrip codes and company long names for verification.
- Acts as a reference for corporate actions, investor grievances, and registrar services.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a periodic administrative disclosure regarding RTA mappings.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure that any changes in their Registrar & Transfer Agent are promptly updated with the exchange and communicated to shareholders.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 2025

## Impact Assessment

Minimal direct market impact; provides transparency and operational clarity for investors and intermediaries regarding investor servicing points.
