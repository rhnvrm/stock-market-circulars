## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2025, dated September 8, 2025. These amendments introduce mandatory demat-only issuance requirements for listed entities following schemes of arrangement or capital restructuring, update disclosure timelines for non-profit organizations on the Social Stock Exchange, and refine annual impact reporting guidelines.

## Key Points

- **Demat-Only Issuance:** Listed entities must issue securities exclusively in dematerialized form following any scheme of arrangement, sub-division, split, or consolidation.
- **Separate Demat Account:** For investors who do not have demat accounts, the listed entity must open a separate demat account to hold such securities.
- **Social Stock Exchange Disclosures:** Updated timelines and submission requirements for non-profit organizations listed on the Social Stock Exchange regarding financial and non-financial disclosures.
- **Impact Reports:** Enhanced clarity on annual impact reports submitted by listed projects and organizations operating on the Social Stock Exchange.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Amendment to Regulation 39:** Insertion of sub-regulation (2A) requiring demat-only issuance post-scheme of arrangement or capital adjustments, with provisions for a separate suspense/entity-held demat account for non-demat holders.
- **Amendment to Regulation 91C:** Replaced sub-regulation (1) prescribing precise disclosure timelines tied to income tax return due dates or statutory deadlines for non-profit organizations registered on the Social Stock Exchange.
- **Amendment to Regulation 91E:** Updates regarding annual impact reports, self-certification for non-listed projects, and expenditure disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities undergoing schemes of arrangement or capital restructuring must ensure all security issuances are routed exclusively through demat channels.
- Non-profit organizations and entities listed on the Social Stock Exchange must comply with revised financial and non-financial reporting schedules.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** Regulations come into force from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette (September 8, 2025).

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines capital issuance processes by eliminating physical certificate complications post-restructuring.
- Enhances transparency and compliance standards for entities listed on the Social Stock Exchange segment.
