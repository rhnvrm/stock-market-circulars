## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding surveillance measures for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). The notification outlines lists of securities shortlisted under various stages of the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) framework for companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process, effective October 01, 2025.

## Key Points

- Details securities shortlisted or moving into specific stages under the IBC surveillance framework.
- Includes companies moving to 'Receipt of corporate announcement / disclosure from the company' (Stage 0) as per IBC effective October 01, 2025.
- Provides a consolidated list of securities under various IRP/IBC stages including companies such as Axis Solutions Ltd., East India Drums And Barrels M, Eastern Silk Industries Ltd., Indosolar Ltd., Jaiprakash Associates Ltd., and others.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are subjected to additional surveillance measures owing to corporate insolvency proceedings under the IBC framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and apply necessary trading restrictions and risk management controls for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened trading restrictions and increased margin requirements or surveillance monitoring for listed companies undergoing corporate insolvency resolution processes.
