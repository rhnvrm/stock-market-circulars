## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. Effective October 03, 2025, several securities are shortlisted under the framework, while others are moving out or continuing with revisions.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework w.e.f. October 03, 2025 include Dhanlaxmi Cotex Ltd, Escorp Asset Management Ltd, Jay Ambe Supermarkets Ltd, Prism Medico and Pharmacy Ltd, Rajasthan Gases Ltd, Supershakti Metaliks Ltd, and Umiya Buildcon Ltd.
- Multiple securities such as Adani Power Ltd, Adani Total Gas Ltd, KIOCL Ltd, and others are moving out of the ST-ASM framework.
- Consolidated list of ongoing securities under the ST-ASM framework includes companies across various stages.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short Term ASM framework to monitor abnormal price volatility and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply the specified surveillance margins and trading restrictions on the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 03, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and surveillance checks may impact the short-term liquidity and trading volume of the affected securities.
