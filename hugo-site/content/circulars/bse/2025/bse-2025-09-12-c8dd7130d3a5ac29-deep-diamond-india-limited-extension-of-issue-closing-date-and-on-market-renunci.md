## Summary

Deep Diamond India Limited has announced a revision to the schedule of its ongoing Rights Issue. The Rights Issue Committee, at its meeting on September 11, 2025, approved the extension of the issue closing date to Wednesday, September 24, 2025, and the trading period for Rights Entitlements (RE) to Thursday, September 18, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Issue Closing Date Extended:** Extended from September 19, 2025, to September 24, 2025.
- **Rights Entitlement Trading Period:** Extended from September 15, 2025, to September 18, 2025.
- **Reason for Extension:** To provide eligible shareholders with additional time and opportunity to exercise their rights.
- **Application Submission:** Duly filled Composite Application Forms (CAF) must be submitted by September 24, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes reported; standard corporate action timeline adjustment.

## Compliance Requirements

- Eligible shareholders must ensure submission of application forms and payment of application amounts by the revised closure date.
- Market participants and trading members must take note of the revised dates for Rights Entitlement renunciation.

## Important Dates

- **Rights Entitlement Trading Close:** September 18, 2025
- **Issue Closing Date:** September 24, 2025
- **Basis of Allotment (On or About):** September 29, 2025
- **Date of Allotment (On or About):** September 30, 2025
- **Date of Credit (On or About):** October 06, 2025
- **Date of Listing / Trading (On or About):** October 07, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Shareholders gain more time to evaluate and participate in the rights issue.
- Post-issue activities including allotment, credit, and listing dates are shifted accordingly.
