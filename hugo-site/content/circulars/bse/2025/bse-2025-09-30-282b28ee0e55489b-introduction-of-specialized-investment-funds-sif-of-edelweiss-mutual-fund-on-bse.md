## Summary

BSE has announced the introduction of Specialized Investment Funds (SIF) for Edelweiss Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform. The circular outlines platform functionalities, minimum investment thresholds for accredited and non-accredited investors, mode of holding validation rules, and member login procedures for updating accredited investor details.

## Key Points

- Separate AMC creation and activation on BSE StAR MF platform for SIF schemes.
- Minimum investment for Non-Accredited Investors is ₹10 lakh for first-time investments; Accredited Investors follow AMC-set limits in SID.
- SIF client AUM must not fall below ₹10 lakh due to redemptions or other reasons.
- Members and distributors must pass the NISM XIII examination and register with AMFI to distribute SIFs.
- Facility provided for manual and bulk upload of accredited investor details via Member Logins (Admin >> client details >> client master upload).
- Specific folio mode of holding rules defined for applying AI exemptions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of SIF functionality supporting Accredited and Non-Accredited investor criteria.
- Mandatory NISM XIII certification and AMFI registration for SIF distributors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Members must ensure correct and accurate updating of accredited investor details via client master upload to avoid transaction rejections.
- Renewal of registration certificates for accredited investors prior to expiry.
- Strict adherence to mode of holding rules for applying AI status across folios.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for Edelweiss Mutual Fund SIF schemes on BSE StAR MF platform.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines distribution and onboarding of Specialized Investment Funds for eligible members while imposing strict validation and compliance checks on investor categories and AUM maintenance.
