## Summary

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (Central Processing Centre) has issued a certificate of incorporation pursuant to a change of name for Sagility India Limited to Sagility Limited, effective from August 18, 2025. The company was originally incorporated as Berkmeer India Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Old Name: Sagility India Limited (Originally incorporated as Berkmeer India Private Limited)
- New Name: Sagility Limited
- Corporate Identification Number (CIN): L72900KA2021PLC150054
- Date of Incorporation Change: August 18, 2025
- Registered Office/Mailing Address: No. 23 & 24, AMR Tech Park, Building 2A, First Floor, Hongasandra Village, Off Hosur Road, Bommanahalli, Bengaluru, Karnataka - 560068

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal recognition of the company name change by the Registrar of Companies, Central Processing Centre.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company is required under Section 12 of the Companies Act to display its old name along with its new name at all required places for a period of two years.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 18, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative update with no direct impact on stakeholders' existing rights and liabilities pursuant to the name change.
