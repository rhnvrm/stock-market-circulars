## Summary

Trading members of the exchange are hereby informed that equity shares of Atlanta Electricals Limited are listed and admitted to dealings on the exchange in the IPO segment.

## Key Points

- Name of the company: Atlanta Electricals Limited
- Security details and ISIN allocated for equity shares.
- Commencement of trading date on the exchange.
- Market lot and group classification as per exchange norms.

## Regulatory Changes

- Admission of securities to secondary market trading pursuant to successful completion of Initial Public Offering (IPO).

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must ensure proper client code modifications and adherence to settlement schedules.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date for Trading: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- High impact for investors and trading members participating in the primary and secondary market operations of Atlanta Electricals Limited.
