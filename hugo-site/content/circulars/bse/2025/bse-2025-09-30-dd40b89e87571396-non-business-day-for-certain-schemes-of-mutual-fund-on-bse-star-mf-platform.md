## Summary

This circular notifies trading members and mutual fund participants of the non-business days applicable to various schemes across multiple fund houses on the BSE StAR MF platform for the month of October 2025.

## Key Points

- Lists non-business days for schemes managed by Axis, Kotak, Edelweiss, Nippon, DSP, Invesco, and Bandhan.
- Affects specific fund-of-funds and international equity/debt schemes during October 2025.
- Helps investors and intermediaries plan subscriptions, redemptions, and switches accordingly.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members must take note of these non-business days while processing transactions on the BSE StAR MF platform to avoid execution errors.

## Important Dates

- Effective Period: October 2025

## Impact Assessment

Operational transparency is maintained for transactions involving specific international and specialized mutual fund schemes during holiday-heavy or specific non-business periods.
