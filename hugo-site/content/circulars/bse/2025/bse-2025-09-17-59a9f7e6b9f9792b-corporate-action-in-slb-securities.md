## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding corporate actions (dividends) in Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) securities, detailing record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods for multiple companies including 63 Moons Technologies, Parag Milk Foods, and others.

## Key Points

- Lists corporate actions (dividends) for fifteen companies.
- Specifies Book Closure/Record Dates, Ex-Dates, and Shut Periods for each security.
- Covers dates ranging from September 11, 2025, to September 23, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must take note of the ex-dates and shut periods for executing transactions and managing obligations under the SLB segment.

## Important Dates

- **Record Dates:** September 12, 2025, through September 23, 2025.
- **Ex-Dates:** September 12, 2025, through September 23, 2025.
- **Shut Periods:** Various periods between September 11, 2025, and September 23, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification ensuring transparency and smooth settlement for corporate actions in the SLB segment.
