## Summary

Baroda BNP Paribas Asset Management India Private Limited has announced an extension of the New Fund Offer (NFO) closing date for the Baroda BNP Paribas Business Conglomerates Fund. The NFO period, which was originally scheduled to close on September 15, 2025, has been extended to September 16, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Scheme Name:** Baroda BNP Paribas Business Conglomerates Fund
- **AMC:** Baroda BNP Paribas Asset Management India Private Limited
- **Original Closing Date:** September 15, 2025
- **Extended Closing Date:** September 16, 2025
- **Platform:** BSE StAR MF Platform

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

All other terms, conditions, and provisions of the Scheme Information Document (SID) and Key Information Memorandum (KIM) remain unchanged.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 12, 2025
- **Revised NFO Closing Date:** September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides investors with an additional day to subscribe to the open-ended equity scheme investing in business conglomerates in India.
