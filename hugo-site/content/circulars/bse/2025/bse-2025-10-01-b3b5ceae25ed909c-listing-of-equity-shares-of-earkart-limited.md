## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Earkart Limited pursuant to its SME IPO, specifying the distinctive number ranges, lock-in details, and security types.

## Key Points

- Earkart Limited has completed its SME IPO, resulting in the listing of equity shares.
- Various tranches of shares have specific lock-in expiration dates ranging from October 2026 to October 2028.
- Includes both demat and physical securities categorized into fully paid up, lock-in, and free shares under offer for sale and fresh IPO.

## Regulatory Changes

- Official admission to dealings on the exchange for Earkart Limited shares.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and depositories must ensure compliance with lock-in restrictions and distinctive number ranges specified.

## Important Dates

- Issue Date / Lock-in Start: 23.09.2025
- Lock-in Expiry Dates: 10.10.2026, 10.10.2027, 10.10.2028

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants with transparency regarding tradable vs. locked-in shares for Earkart Limited.
