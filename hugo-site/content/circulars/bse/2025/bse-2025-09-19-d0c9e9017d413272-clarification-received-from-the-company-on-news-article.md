## Summary

Vodafone Idea Limited issued a clarification in response to a mainstream media article titled “Vodafone Idea shares surge 12% after government says not opposing relief plea”. The company confirmed that it had challenged additional Adjusted Gross Revenue (AGR) demands raised by the Department of Telecommunications before the Hon’ble Supreme Court. The matter was listed on September 19, 2025, and the next date of hearing is fixed for September 26, 2025.

## Key Points

- Clarification issued regarding media reports on AGR relief plea.
- Company challenged additional AGR demands raised by the Department of Telecommunications before the Hon'ble Supreme Court.
- Supreme Court listed the matter on September 19, 2025, setting September 26, 2025, as the next date of hearing.
- Company is awaiting a copy of the official order.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes; disclosure made pursuant to Regulation 30 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Timely disclosure to stock exchanges regarding material developments and media clarifications under Regulation 30 of SEBI LODR.

## Important Dates

- **19 September 2025:** Date of hearing and clarification letter.
- **26 September 2025:** Next date of hearing at the Hon'ble Supreme Court.

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency to investors regarding the status of ongoing AGR-related legal proceedings and volatility surrounding media reports.
