## Summary

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs, Office of the Central Processing Centre, has issued a certificate of incorporation pursuant to the change of name for ITD Cementation India Limited to Cemindia Projects Limited with effect from August 19, 2025.

## Key Points

- Former Company Name: ITD Cementation India Limited
- New Company Name: Cemindia Projects Limited
- Original Incorporation Name: Cemindia Company Limited
- Corporate Identification Number (CIN): L61000MH1978PLC020435
- Date of Certificate: August 19, 2025

## Regulatory Changes

The official name change has been approved and registered by the Registrar of Companies, Central Processing Centre.

## Compliance Requirements

- The company is obligated to display its old name for a period of two years along with its new name at all places wherever a company is required to display its name under Section 12 of the Companies Act.
- Stakeholders are advised to verify the latest status and public documents on the Ministry's website (www.mca.gov.in/MCA21).

## Important Dates

- Effective Date of Name Change: August 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

The change of name does not affect the rights and liabilities of stakeholders pursuant to such change.
