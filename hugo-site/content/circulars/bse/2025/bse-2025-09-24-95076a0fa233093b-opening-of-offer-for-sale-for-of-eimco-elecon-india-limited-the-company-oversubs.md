## Summary

Tamrock Great Britain Holdings Limited (Promoter Group entity) of Eimco Elecon (India) Limited has announced the exercise of its Oversubscription Option to sell an additional 7,00,000 equity shares in addition to the Base Offer Size of 7,23,875 equity shares. Consequently, the total Offer for Sale (OFS) size reaches up to 14,23,875 equity shares, representing approximately 24.68% of the total issued and paid-up equity share capital of the company.

## Key Points

- **Offeror:** Tamrock Great Britain Holdings Limited (Promoter Group).
- **Company:** Eimco Elecon (India) Limited.
- **Base Offer Size:** 7,23,875 equity shares (~12.55% of paid-up capital).
- **Oversubscription Option:** Up to 7,00,000 equity shares (~12.14% of paid-up capital).
- **Total Offer Size:** Up to 14,23,875 equity shares (~24.68% of paid-up capital).
- **Retail Allocation:** 1,42,388 equity shares reserved for allocation to Retail Investors, subject to receipt of valid bids.
- **Bidding Dates:** September 24, 2025 (for non-Retail Investors) and September 25, 2025 (for Retail Investors and non-Retail Investors carrying forward unallotted bids).

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted through a separate, designated window of the Stock Exchanges in accordance with guidelines and circulars issued by SEBI and the Stock Exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to SEBI framework for Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares through the stock exchange mechanism.

## Important Dates

- **T Day (Non-Retail Investors):** September 24, 2025.
- **T+1 Day (Retail Investors & Carry-forward Bids):** September 25, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Increased supply of equity shares in the market through the promoter's secondary market share sale, impacting short-term liquidity and share price dynamics for Eimco Elecon (India) Limited.
