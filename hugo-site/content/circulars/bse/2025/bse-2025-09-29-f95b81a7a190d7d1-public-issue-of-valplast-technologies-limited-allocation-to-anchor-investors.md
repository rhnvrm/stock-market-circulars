## Summary

Valplast Technologies Limited has finalized the allocation of 14,70,000 equity shares to anchor investors as part of its public issue. The shares were allocated at a price of ₹54 per share (including a share premium of ₹44 per share), raising a total amount of ₹7,93,80,000.

## Key Points

- Total Equity Shares allocated to Anchor Investors: 14,70,000
- Anchor Investor Allocation Price: ₹54 per share
- Total Amount Raised: ₹7,93,80,000
- Key Anchor Investors include Rajasthan Global Securities Private Limited, Strategic Sixth Sense Capital Fund, Viney Growth Fund, and others.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

As per SEBI (ICDR) Regulations, 2018, if the final offer price discovered through the book building process is higher than the anchor investor allocation price, anchor investors must pay the difference by the pay-in date specified in the revised CAN.

## Important Dates

- Date of Allocation: September 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Provides transparency regarding institutional participation and capital raised from anchor investors prior to the public issue opening.
