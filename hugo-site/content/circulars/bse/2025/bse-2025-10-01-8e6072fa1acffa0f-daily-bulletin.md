## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 127/2025-2026 details upcoming book-closures, record dates, and payment/redemption schedules for commercial papers and privately placed non-convertible debentures (NCDs) for companies including Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited, Akara Capital Advisors Private Limited, Bajaj Finance Limited, Chaitanya India Fin Credit Private Limited, and CSA Investments Private Limited.

## Key Points

- Lists corporate debt actions including commercial paper redemptions and NCD interest payments.
- Specifies record dates ranging from October 2025 through September 2030.
- Covers multiple issuances by major financial entities like Bajaj Finance Limited and Aditya Birla Housing Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and depositories must ensure timely processing of corporate actions based on the specified record dates.

## Important Dates

- Record dates span from 14/10/2025 to 13/09/2030 across various debt instruments.

## Impact Assessment

Operational notice for market participants regarding scheduled interest payments and debt redemptions.
