## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) for companies relating to the Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA) effective October 01, 2025. The update covers securities continuing in surveillance with stage movements and provides a consolidated list of impacted securities.

## Key Points

- Sadbhav Engineering Ltd. (Scrip Code: 532710) shall continue in ASM under ICA but moves to a respective lower stage.
- Consolidated list includes Mcleod Russel India Ltd, Sadbhav Engineering Limited, and Sterling and Wilson Renewable Energy Ltd.
- Effective date for the surveillance changes is October 01, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Additional Surveillance Measures (ASM) for companies under the Inter-Creditor Agreement (ICA) framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must note the revised surveillance stages and associated trading restrictions for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affects trading margins and surveillance monitoring for the specific securities included in the ICA surveillance framework.
