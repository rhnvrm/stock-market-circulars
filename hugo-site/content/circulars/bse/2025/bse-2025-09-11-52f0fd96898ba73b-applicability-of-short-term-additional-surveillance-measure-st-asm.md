## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework. The circular provides lists of securities shortlisted under the framework, securities moving out of the framework, and a consolidated list of securities under various stages effective September 12, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework effective September 12, 2025.
- Specific securities are moving out of the ST-ASM framework due to various criteria including inclusion in other frameworks.
- A consolidated list of securities under the Short Term ASM stages is provided for market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short Term ASM framework for specified scrips to monitor price volatility and safeguard investors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the scrips subjected to ST-ASM and apply applicable surveillance margins and trading restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the shortlisted securities will be subject to surveillance controls such as applicable margins and price bands to mitigate market volatility.
