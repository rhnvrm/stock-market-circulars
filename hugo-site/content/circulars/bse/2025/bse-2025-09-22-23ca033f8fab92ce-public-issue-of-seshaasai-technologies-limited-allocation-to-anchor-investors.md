## Summary

Seshaasai Technologies Limited has finalized the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors ahead of its initial public offering (IPO). A total of 57,52,296 equity shares were allocated at the upper end of the price band of ₹423 per equity share, successfully raising ₹243.32 crore.

## Key Points

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 22, 2025.
- Total Shares Allocated: 57,52,296 Equity Shares.
- Issue Price / Allocation Price: ₹423 per Equity Share (Face Value: ₹10).
- Total Funds Raised: ₹243.32 Crore.
- Participation included prominent domestic mutual funds and institutional investors such as Nippon India Mutual Fund, UTI Mutual Fund, and Motilal Oswal Mutual Fund.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a standard primary market corporate action under SEBI ICDR Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Companies and book-running lead managers must adhere to mandatory lock-in periods for anchor investor shares as stipulated by SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 22, 2025
- Public Issue Period: September 23, 2025 – September 25, 2025
- Listing Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Strong institutional participation in the anchor book indicates robust market confidence, setting a positive tone for the subsequent public subscription phase and listing.
