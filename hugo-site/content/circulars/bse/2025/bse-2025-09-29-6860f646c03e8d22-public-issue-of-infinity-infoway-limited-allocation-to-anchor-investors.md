## Summary

Infinity Infoway Limited has announced the finalization of its anchor investor allocation for its upcoming public issue. A total of 4,08,000 equity shares have been allocated to anchor investors at an issue price of Rs. 155/- per share.

## Key Points

- Company: Infinity Infoway Limited
- Total Equity Shares Allocated to Anchor Investors: 4,08,000
- Anchor Investor Offer Price: Rs. 155/- per share
- Total Amount: Rs. 6,32,40,000
- Key Anchor Investors include Holani Venture Capital Fund I, Finavenue Growth Fund, Meru Investment Fund PCC, Aarthai Growth Fund, and VPX Global Ventures Fund - Scheme 1.
