## Summary

BSE has issued a circular detailing the list of securities shortlisted under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, securities moving to higher or lower stages, and securities moving out of the framework effective September 15, 2025.

## Key Points

- Securities shortlisted in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework w.e.f. September 15, 2025 (Annexure I).
- Securities moving to higher or lower stage ASM frameworks.
- Securities moving out of the Short Term ASM framework due to various criteria including inclusion in other surveillance frameworks or regular exits (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under various ST-ASM stages (Annexure III).

## Regulatory Changes

Application of surveillance measures to monitor price volatility and safeguard investors in specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must note the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the listed securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affected scrips may experience modified margin requirements and trading restrictions aimed at curbing excessive price volatility.
