## Summary

Tamrock Great Britain Holdings Limited, a member of the promoter group of Eimco Elecon (India) Limited, has announced an Offer for Sale (OFS) of up to 7,23,875 equity shares (representing approximately 12.55% of the total issued and paid-up equity share capital) as the Base Offer Size. Additionally, an oversubscription option of up to 7,00,000 equity shares (approximately 12.14%) is available, bringing the total potential offer size to 14,23,875 equity shares (approximately 24.68%).

## Key Points

- **Seller:** Tamrock Great Britain Holdings Limited (Promoter Group).
- **Company:** Eimco Elecon (India) Limited.
- **Base Offer Size:** 7,23,875 equity shares (~12.55% of paid-up capital).
- **Oversubscription Option:** Up to 7,00,000 equity shares (~12.14%).
- **Total Potential Offer Size:** 14,23,875 equity shares (~24.68%).
- **Seller's Broker:** Spark Institutional Equities Private Limited (Avendus Capital Private Limited subsidiary).

## Regulatory Changes

- The offer is conducted in accordance with paragraph 19.1.5.2 of Chapter 1 of the SEBI master circular for stock exchanges and clearing corporations (SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024) and updated operational guidelines from BSE and NSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Bidding will take place on a separate designated window provided by the Stock Exchanges (BSE and NSE) in accordance with the OFS guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **T Day (September 24, 2025):** Opening of Offer for non-Retail Investors only.
- **T+1 Day (September 25, 2025):** Opening for Retail Investors and non-Retail Investors carrying forward unallotted bids.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides liquidity and changes shareholding distribution through the dilution of promoter holding via the stock exchange mechanism.
