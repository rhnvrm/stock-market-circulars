## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the post-issue shareholding pattern and listing of equity shares of L. T. Elevator Limited as of September 18, 2025.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: L. T. Elevator Limited
- Class of Security: Equity
- Reporting Date: 18-SEP-2025
- Total Promoter and Promoter Group holding: 62.71% (12,017,328 shares)
- Total Public holding: 37.29% (7,145,727 shares)

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Submission of shareholding pattern under Regulation 31(1)(b) / 31(1)(c) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- Allotment / Reporting Date: 18-SEP-2025

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational update for market participants concerning the equity structure of L. T. Elevator Limited.
