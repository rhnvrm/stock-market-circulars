## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the periodic reconstitution of its indices, specifically detailing additions and drops for indices such as BSE Select IPO and BSE Commodities, effective from September 22, 2025.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 22, 2025.
- Index Reconstitution covers additions and drops for BSE Select IPO and BSE Commodities indices.
- Notable additions to BSE Select IPO include Niva Bupa Health Insurance Company Ltd, Ather Energy Ltd, and Belrise Industries Ltd, while SBI Cards and Payment Services Ltd, Clean Science and Technology Ltd, and NMDC Steel Ltd are dropped.
- Numerous additions made to the BSE Commodities index.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and fund managers tracking these indices must adjust their portfolios accordingly to align with the reconstitution effective date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Index funds and institutional portfolios benchmarking against BSE Select IPO and BSE Commodities will need to rebalance their holdings to reflect the additions and deletions.
