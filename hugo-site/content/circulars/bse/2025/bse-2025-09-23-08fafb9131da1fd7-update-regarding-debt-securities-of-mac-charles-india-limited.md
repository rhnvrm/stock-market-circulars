## Summary

This circular outlines the modifications regarding the debt securities of MAC Charles (India) Limited, specifically detailing the release of certain initial continuing debentures and the revised terms of security, including first ranking exclusive charges and equitable mortgages.

## Key Points

- Update regarding debt securities of MAC Charles (India) Limited.
- Modification of terms relating to existing charges, hypothecation, and mortgage releases.
- Specific revisions covering Hub SPV, SDPL, and company account assets.

## Regulatory Changes

- Replacement of existing secondary ranking mortgage and subservient financial immovable assets with first-ranking exclusive charges via deeds of hypothecation.
- Revisions to pledge agreements and exclusive equitable mortgages by Hub SPV and SDPL.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers must ensure all documentation regarding revised hypothecation and pledges are correctly executed and filed as per the updated terms.

## Important Dates

- Effective date as per the circular terms and agreements dated 23 August 2022 and subsequent amendments.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides clarity to debenture holders and stakeholders regarding the updated security cover and asset hypothecation structure.
