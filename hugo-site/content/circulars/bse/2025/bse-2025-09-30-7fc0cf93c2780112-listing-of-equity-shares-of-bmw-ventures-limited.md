## Summary

BSE has issued a notice regarding the listing of equity shares of BMW Ventures Limited, detailing fully paid-up shares, lock-in periods, distinctive numbers, and demat details.

## Key Points

- Listing of equity shares of BMW Ventures Limited.
- Includes fully paid-up shares and shares under lock-in.
- Lock-in periods extend up to April 12, 2026, and April 12, 2027.
- Free of IPO shares are also specified.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and depositories need to note the lock-in and listing details for demat crediting and trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in From: 22.09.2025
- Lock-in Upto: 12.04.2026 / 12.04.2027

## Impact Assessment

Standard operational update for depository participants and investors regarding share availability and lock-in status.
