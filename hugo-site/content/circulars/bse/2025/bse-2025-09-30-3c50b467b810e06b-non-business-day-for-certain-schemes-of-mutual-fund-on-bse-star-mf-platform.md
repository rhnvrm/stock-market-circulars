## Summary

The BSE has released a schedule of non-business days for October 2025 applicable to certain schemes of various mutual funds on the BSE StAR MF platform, including Axis, Kotak, Edelweiss, Nippon, DSP, Invesco, and Bandhan.

## Key Points

- Lists specific non-business dates for October 2025 across multiple mutual fund houses.
- Affects transactions, subscriptions, redemptions, and switches for specified schemes (such as international funds and fund of funds).
- Fund houses included: Axis, Kotak, Edelweiss, Nippon, DSP, Invesco, and Bandhan.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors should note the non-business dates to avoid transaction scheduling errors on the BSE StAR MF platform for the affected schemes.

## Important Dates

- October 2025 (Various dates including October 1, 2, 6, 7, 13, 20, 21, 22, 27, and 29, 2025 depending on the specific fund and scheme).

## Impact Assessment

Operational guidance for investors and distributors trading in specific international and thematic fund of funds schemes during October 2025.
