## Summary

Tamrock Great Britain Holdings Limited, a member of the promoter group of Eimco Elecon (India) Limited, has issued a notice for a proposed Offer for Sale (OFS) of up to 7,23,875 equity shares (representing approx. 12.55% of the total issued and paid-up equity share capital), with an additional oversubscription option of up to 7,00,000 equity shares (approx. 12.14%). The total potential offer size is up to 14,23,875 equity shares (approx. 24.68%).

## Key Points

- **Seller:** Tamrock Great Britain Holdings Limited (Promoter Group).
- **Company:** Eimco Elecon (India) Limited.
- **Base Offer Size:** Up to 7,23,875 equity shares (12.55%).
- **Oversubscription Option:** Up to 7,00,000 equity shares (12.14%).
- **Total Offer Size (if oversubscription exercised):** Up to 14,23,875 equity shares (24.68%).
- **Seller's Broker:** Spark Institutional Equities Private Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Undertaken pursuant to SEBI master circular for stock exchanges and clearing corporations bearing reference number SEBI/HO/MRD-PoD2/CIR/P/2024/00181 dated December 30, 2024, along with operational guidelines issued by BSE and NSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- Bidding to take place through the designated window of the Stock Exchanges via the Seller's Broker in accordance with the OFS Guidelines.

## Important Dates

- **T Day (Non-Retail Investors only):** September 24, 2025
- **T+1 Day (Retail Investors and carry-forward bids for Non-Retail):** September 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- The offer impacts the secondary market float and promoter shareholding structure of Eimco Elecon (India) Limited, providing liquidity and broader institutional/retail participation through the exchange-based OFS mechanism.
