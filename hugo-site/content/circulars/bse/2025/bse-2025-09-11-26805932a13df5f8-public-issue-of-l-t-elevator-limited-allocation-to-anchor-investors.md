## Summary

L. T. Elevator Limited has finalized the allocation of 14,36,800 equity shares to anchor investors at an allocation price of ₹78 per equity share (including a share premium of ₹68 per share), successfully raising ₹11,20,70,400.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares allocated to anchor investors: 14,36,800 shares.
- Allocation price: ₹78 per share (Face Value: ₹10, Premium: ₹68).
- Total amount raised from anchor investors: ₹11,20,70,400 (~₹11.21 crore).
- Major anchor investors include India Continuum Fund I, India Max Investment Fund Ltd, Saint Capital Fund, RMS Growth Fund Scheme I, and Fortune Hands Growth Fund Scheme I.

## Compliance Requirements
