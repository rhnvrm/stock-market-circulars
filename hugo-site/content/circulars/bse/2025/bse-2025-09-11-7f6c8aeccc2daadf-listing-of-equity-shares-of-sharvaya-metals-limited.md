## Summary

Sharvaya Metals Limited has announced the listing of its equity shares following its Initial Public Offering (IPO), detailing security numbers, lock-in periods, and types of securities including fully paid shares, offer-for-sale (OFS), and anchor lock-ins.

## Key Points

- Total securities listed: 10,030,000 equity shares.
- Includes fully paid shares with various lock-in expiration dates ranging from September 2026 to September 2028.
- Features offer-for-sale (OFS) and anchor lock-in portions.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to statutory lock-in periods specified for respective share categories.

## Important Dates

- Lock-in expiry dates range from September 11, 2025, to September 11, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

- Enables trading and market availability of Sharvaya Metals Limited equity shares on the exchange subject to applicable lock-in restrictions.
