## Summary

True Colors Limited has finalized the allocation of equity shares to anchor investors ahead of its public issue, raising approximately ₹36.40 crore from 24 anchor investors at the upper price band of ₹191 per share.

## Key Points

- Total anchor book size: ₹36,39,69,600 (~₹36.40 Crore).
- Number of anchor investors: 24.
- Anchor issue price: ₹191 per share.
- Total anchor portion allocated: 19,05,600 Equity Shares.

## Regulatory Changes

- Conducted in compliance with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- Standard lock-in requirements apply to anchor investor shares as per SEBI ICDR regulations (30 days for 50% of the shares and 90 days for the remaining 50%).

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 22, 2025.
- Public Issue Opening/Closing dates and subsequent listing follow the scheduled IPO timeline.

## Impact Assessment

- Indicates institutional interest and market appetite for the True Colors Limited SME IPO.
