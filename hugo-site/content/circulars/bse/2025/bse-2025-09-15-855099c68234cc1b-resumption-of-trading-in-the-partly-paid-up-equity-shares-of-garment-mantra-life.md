## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the resumption of trading in the partly paid-up equity shares of Garment Mantra Lifestyle Limited (Scrip Code: 890215, ISIN: IN9653501026) effective from September 15, 2025, following the receipt of first/second call money on 37,34,26,591 partly paid shares.

## Key Points

- Total Nos. of Partly Paid Shares issued as on 10.09.2025: 39,14,97,795
- Total Nos. of Partly Paid shares on which first/second call money received: 37,34,26,591
- Total Nos. of Partly Paid shares available for trading on resumption: 37,34,26,591
- Paid-up value per share available for trading: Rs. 0.50
- ISIN: IN9653501026
- Distinctive Nos.: From 200768101 to 574194691

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must ensure compliance with the specified ISIN and distinctive numbers for trading operations.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 10, 2025 (as of date for records)

## Impact Assessment

Enables investors holding the specified partly paid-up shares to resume trading on the exchange platform.
