## Summary

BSE has released an updated list of securities shortlisted under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, effective from September 22, 2025. The notification lists 52 companies subjected to surveillance criteria.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 22, 2025.
- Framework: Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM).
- Total Securities listed in Annexure I: 52 companies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities listed in the notification are moved into or maintained under the GSM framework based on predefined objective criteria relating to price movement and volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance measures applicable to the listed scrips, which may include additional trading margins, trade-for-trade settlement, or other restrictions depending on the specific stage.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the shortlisted securities may be subject to enhanced surveillance and margin requirements, impacting liquidity and trading mechanisms for investors in these scrips.
