## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework, outlining the list of securities shortlisted, moved to higher or lower stages, or moving out of the framework effective September 15, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Part A:** Shortlists new securities in the Long Term ASM Framework (e.g., Binny Mills Ltd, Maitri Enterprises Ltd, Olympic Oil Industries Ltd, Sharika Enterprises Ltd, Toyam Sports Ltd) w.e.f. September 15, 2025.
- **Part B:** Securities continuing in Long Term ASM but moving to higher stages (NIL).
- **Part C:** Securities moving to respective lower Stage ASM w.e.f. September 15, 2025, including Ambo Agritec Ltd, GEE Ltd, and Indo Thai Securities Ltd.
- **Part D:** Direct stage IV shortlistings (Nil).
- **Annexure II:** Lists numerous securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework w.e.f. September 15, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of enhanced surveillance controls and margin requirements for securities listed under the LT-ASM framework to safeguard market integrity and investor interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages, margin requirements, and trading restrictions imposed on the specified securities.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Impacts liquidity and margin requirements for trading in the specified scrips listed under various stages of the LT-ASM framework.
