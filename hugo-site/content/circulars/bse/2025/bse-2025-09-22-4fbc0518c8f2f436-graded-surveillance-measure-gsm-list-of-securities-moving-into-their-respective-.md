## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing securities that are moving into their respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, effective as per the surveillance framework guidelines.

## Key Points

- Multiple securities across different stages (Stage I, II, III, and IV) of GSM are identified.
- Affected companies include Worldwide Aluminium Ltd, Sybly Industries Ltd, Galaxy Agrico Exports Ltd, Shivansh Finserve Ltd, Rajkamal Synthetics Ltd, Prashant India Ltd, Artson Ltd, Golechha Global Finance Ltd, Nagarjuna Agri Tech Ltd, and Tirth Plastic Ltd.
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower in the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the ESM Framework.
- Securities marked with ($) are moving lower in the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the IBC Framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of heightened surveillance measures to monitor abnormal price movements and protect investors.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and investors must take note of the applicable trading restrictions and margin requirements associated with each GSM stage.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by exchange surveillance notifications.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in these securities will be subject to surveillance actions such as trade-for-trade settlement or additional margin requirements, potentially impacting their liquidity.
