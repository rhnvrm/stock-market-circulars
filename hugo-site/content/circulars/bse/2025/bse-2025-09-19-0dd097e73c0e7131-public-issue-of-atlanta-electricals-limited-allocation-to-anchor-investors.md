## Summary

Atlanta Electricals Limited has finalized the allocation of 27,14,885 equity shares to 15 anchor investors at an allocation price of ₹754 per share (the upper end of the price band), successfully raising ₹204.70 crore ahead of its public issue opening.

## Key Points

- Total Amount Raised: ₹204,70,23,290 (approx. ₹204.70 crore)
- Total Shares Allotted: 27,14,885 Equity Shares
- Allotment Price: ₹754 per Equity Share
- Number of Anchor Investors: 15 institutional investors including major mutual funds like HDFC, ICICI Prudential, Axis, and Kotak.

## Regulatory Changes

- Compliance with SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018 regarding anchor investor allocation and lock-in norms.

## Compliance Requirements

- 50% of the shares allotted to anchor investors are locked in for 30 days.
- Remaining 50% of the shares are locked in for 90 days.

## Important Dates

- Anchor Bidding Date: September 19, 2025
- Anchor Lock-in (30 Days): October 24, 2025
- Anchor Lock-in (90 Days): December 23, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Strong institutional participation indicates positive market sentiment and robust price discovery for the upcoming public issue of Atlanta Electricals Limited.
