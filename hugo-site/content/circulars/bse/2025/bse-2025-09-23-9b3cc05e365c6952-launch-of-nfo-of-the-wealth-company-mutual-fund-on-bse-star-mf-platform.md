## Summary

BSE has announced the launch and availability of New Fund Offer (NFO) schemes for The Wealth Company Mutual Fund on the BSE StAR MF platform. The circular details 12 schemes under The Wealth Company Liquid Fund with their respective ISINs and tentative allotment dates.

## Key Points

- Launch of New Fund Offer (NFO) for The Wealth Company Mutual Fund.
- Includes 12 variants of The Wealth Company Liquid Fund (Regular and Direct plans, covering daily/weekly/monthly IDCW and growth options).
- Tentative allotment date for all listed schemes is 2025-10-14.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and distributors must ensure proper processing of transactions through the BSE StAR MF platform adhering to the specified ISINs and dates.

## Important Dates

- Tentative Allotment Date: 2025-10-14

## Impact Assessment

Provides expanded investment choices for investors and trading members utilizing the BSE StAR MF platform for liquid fund subscriptions.
