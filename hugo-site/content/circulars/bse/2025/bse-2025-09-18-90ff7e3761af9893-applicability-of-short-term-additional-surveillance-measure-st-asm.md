## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework effective September 19, 2025. The circular details securities newly shortlisted, moved to higher/lower stages, and moving out of the ST-ASM framework.

## Key Points

- New securities shortlisted in the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework w.e.f. September 19, 2025 (e.g., Anlon Healthcare Ltd, Epsom Properties Ltd, GSB Finance Ltd, etc.).
- Securities continuing in the framework but moving to higher or lower Stage ASM (e.g., Anirit Ventures Ltd, Zodiac Ventures Ltd, Karnavati Finance Ltd).
- Securities moving out of the ST-ASM Framework (e.g., Bridge Securities Ltd, Dhanalaxmi Roto Spinners Ltd, Jtekt India Ltd, etc.).
- Consolidated list of securities under various ST-ASM stages provided in Annexure III.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the Short Term ASM framework to protect investor interests and curb excessive price volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and applicable margin requirements for the listed scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 19, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affected scrips will be subject to specific trading restrictions and margin requirements as dictated by their respective ST-ASM stages.
