## Summary

BSE has issued a user manual outlining the step-by-step procedure for Research Analysts (RAs) to submit their periodic half-yearly compliance reports using the BSE RAIA membership portal and the associated Excel utility.

## Key Points

- Access the BSE RAIA Membership Portal using existing login credentials.
- Navigate to the 'Half Yearly Compliance Reporting' section and select the appropriate period (e.g., 30-09-2025).
- Download, unblock, and configure the Excel utility (ensuring macro enablement and proper file location).
- Follow the four-step filing process: fill data, validate individual sheets, validate all sheets, and generate the XML file for submission.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Research Analysts must complete and submit their half-yearly compliance reporting through the designated BSE portal using the latest provided Excel utility, ensuring all sheets are successfully validated and XML output is generated without errors.

## Important Dates

- Reporting Period Referenced: 30-09-2025

## Impact Assessment

Ensures standardized compliance reporting and adherence to regulatory guidelines for Research Analysts registered with BSE.
