## Summary

BSE Daily Bulletin No. 122/2025-2026, dated September 24, 2025, details upcoming book-closures, record dates, and payment dates for commercial papers, non-convertible debentures (NCDs), and bonds across multiple issuers.

## Key Points

- Lists commercial paper redemptions for entities such as Bobcard Limited, Bombay Burmah Trading Corpn. Ltd., and Julius Baer Capital (India) Private Limited.
- Specifies record dates for interest payments and redemptions on secured NCDs and privately placed non-convertible debentures for ECL Finance Limited, Kanakadurga Finance Limited, and Kogta Financial (India) Limited.
- Includes bond interest payment dates for Jhajjar Power Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Issuers and market participants must take note of the specified record dates and book closures for processing corporate actions and debt servicing.

## Important Dates

- **Record Dates:** Spanning from September 30, 2025, to December 7, 2025, depending on the specific security.

## Impact Assessment

Routine operational notification ensuring timely processing of debt servicing and commercial paper redemptions for investors.
