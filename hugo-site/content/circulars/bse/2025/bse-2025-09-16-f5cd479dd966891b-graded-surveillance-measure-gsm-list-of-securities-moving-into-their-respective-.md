## Summary

BSE has issued a notification detailing securities that are moving into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework.

## Key Points

- KLG Capital Services Ltd (Security Code: 530771) has moved to GSM Stage I.
- Pasupati Fincap Ltd (Security Code: 511734) and Integra Switchgear Ltd (Security Code: 517423) have moved to GSM Stage II.
- P.M. Telelinnks Ltd (Security Code: 513403) has moved to GSM Stage III.
- SW Investments Ltd (Security Code: 503659) has moved to GSM Stage IV.
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower under the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the ESM Framework.
- Securities marked with ($) are moving lower under the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the IBC Framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of trading surveillance measures as per the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework for identified securities to safeguard market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must comply with the applicable trading restrictions, additional margin requirements, and deposit norms associated with each GSM stage.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by exchange regulations for the respective surveillance action.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to heightened surveillance, margin requirements, and trading frequency restrictions corresponding to their assigned GSM stages.
