## Summary

BSE Limited has invited offers from trading members of the Exchange for the purchase of securities in auction under Rolling Settlement No. DR-712. The circular outlines the entry and matching timings for auction offers on September 11, 2025, along with the subsequent pay-in schedule.

## Key Points

- Rolling Settlement No.: DR-712
- Auction Offer Entry Timing: 02:00 p.m. to 02:45 p.m. on September 11, 2025
- Auction Matching Timing: 02:45 p.m. to 03:00 p.m. on September 11, 2025
- Delivery Statement and Report Generation: 03:00 p.m. to 03:30 p.m.
- Money Statement & Final Reports: 03:35 p.m. to 05:30 p.m.
- Pay-in of the Auction: Friday, September 12, 2025, at 08:00 a.m.
- Members must check auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off timings.
- Trading members should exercise care when punching securities code and rate, as no corrections are permitted for punching errors.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Routine settlement and auction notification.

## Compliance Requirements

Trading members and custodians must verify their auction pay-in obligations and execute securities pay-in within depository cut-off times. Members must check the cut-off rate on the Auction offer Entry Screen and review corporate action notices.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: September 11, 2025
- Auction Pay-in Date: September 12, 2025 (08:00 a.m.)

## Impact Assessment

Operational notification impacting trading members participating in Demat Auction DR-712 with no broader market-wide structural changes.
