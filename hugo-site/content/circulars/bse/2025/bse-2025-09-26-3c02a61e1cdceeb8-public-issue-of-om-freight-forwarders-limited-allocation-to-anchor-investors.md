## Summary

Om Freight Forwarders Limited successfully completed its anchor investor allocation, raising approximately ₹15.90 crore by allocating equity shares to institutional investors ahead of its public issue.

## Key Points

- Total amount raised from anchor investors: ₹15.89 crore.
- Total equity shares allocated: 11,77,710 shares.
- Issue price / upper price band: ₹135 per share.
- Key anchor investors included Craft Emerging Market Fund PCC – Citadel Capital Fund, Rajasthan Global Securities Pvt. Ltd., and Abundantia Capital VCC.

## Regulatory Changes

- None.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to SEBI ICDR Regulations regarding anchor investor lock-in periods (30 days for 50% of the shares and 90-day lock-in for the remaining shares).

## Important Dates

- Anchor investor allocation date: September 26, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Indicates institutional interest and market participation ahead of the public issue opening.
