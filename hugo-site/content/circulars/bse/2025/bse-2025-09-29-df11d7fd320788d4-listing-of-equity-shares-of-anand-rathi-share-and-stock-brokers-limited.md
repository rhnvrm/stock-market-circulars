## Summary

Anand Rathi Share & Stock Brokers Limited has submitted its shareholding pattern under Regulation 31 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 post issue, detailing promoter and public shareholdings as of 29.09.2025.

## Key Points

- Name of Listed Entity: Anand Rathi Share & Stock Brokers Limited Post Issue
- Filing under Regulation 31(1)(a) of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.
- Promoter and Promoter Group hold 43,845,408 equity shares, representing 69.90% of total shares.
- Company has outstanding ESOPs and significant beneficial owners as per disclosures.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with Regulation 31 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 regarding the submission of shareholding patterns.

## Important Dates

- Date of Record / As on: 29.09.2025

## Impact Assessment

Standard statutory disclosure with no direct market impact on trading operations.
