## Summary

BSE has issued a notification regarding the list of securities moving into their respective Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, effective from upcoming trading sessions.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been moved into GSM Stage III and Stage IV.
- Securities affected include India Radiators Ltd., Mathew Easow Research Securities Ltd, Artson Limited, Advik Laboratories Ltd., and Rama Petrochemicals Ltd., among others.
- Trading in these securities will be subject to enhanced monitoring and surveillance safeguards.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework guidelines to deter market manipulation and excessive speculation in specified scrips.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must apply the requisite surveillance margins and trading restrictions specified for the respective GSM stages.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: As specified by the exchange.

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and restricted trading frequency for the listed securities, potentially leading to lower liquidity and higher volatility for impacted scrips.
