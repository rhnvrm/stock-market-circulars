## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the lower revision of stages for securities under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, detailing securities moving down from higher stages to lower stages or exiting the surveillance mechanism entirely.

## Key Points

- Lists multiple securities undergoing lower revision under the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM).
- Specifies changes in security stages, moving from stages like 4, 3, 2, 1 down to lower stages or 0.
- Includes security codes, ISIN numbers, and security names for all affected entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Adjustment of surveillance stages for listed entities exhibiting abnormal price movements or volatility under the GSM framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the revised GSM stages for order placement and risk management.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately as per the circular issuance date.

## Impact Assessment

- Eases trading restrictions for the listed securities undergoing a lower revision of their GSM stage, improving liquidity and normal market operations.
