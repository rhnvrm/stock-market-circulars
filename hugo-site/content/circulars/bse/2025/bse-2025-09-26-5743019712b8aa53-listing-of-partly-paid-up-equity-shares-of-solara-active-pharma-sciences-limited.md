## Summary

Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited has announced details regarding the first call money for its partly paid-up equity shares. A total of 25,000 partly paid-up shares are made available for trading upon resumption, with a paid-up value of Rs. 7/- per share under ISIN IN9624Z01022.

## Key Points

- Total partly paid shares for which reminder notice for first call sent: 6,08,988
- Total shares on which call money received as of August 16, 2025: 3,71,779
- Total shares for which listing and trading approval received on August 29, 2025: 3,45,923
- Total shares available for trading on resumption: 25,000
- Balance shares unable to be credited to depositories due to pledge: 856
- Paid-up value per share available for trading: Rs. 7/-
- ISIN: IN9624Z01022
- Lock-in period: Nil

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Company compliance with respect to distribution and listing of partly paid shares as per BSE operational guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 25, 2025
- Call Money Received Date: Up to August 16, 2025
- Listing and Trading Approval Date: August 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Minimal impact limited to shareholders holding partly paid shares of Solara Active Pharma Sciences Limited completing their call payment and receiving trading availability.
