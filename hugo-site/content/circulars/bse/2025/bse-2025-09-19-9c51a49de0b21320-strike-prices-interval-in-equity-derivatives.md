## Summary

BSE has issued a circular detailing the strike price intervals for equity derivatives across a comprehensive list of scrips. The notice outlines the precise strike price step sizes applicable for futures and options contracts of various listed companies.

## Key Points

- Specifies the derivative asset codes and strike price intervals for numerous companies.
- Covers major scrips such as Adani Enterprises, Axis Bank, Infosys, Reliance, State Bank of India, and many others.
- Helps market participants and traders determine valid strike price steps for order placement in the derivatives segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates and standardizes the strike price intervals for equity derivatives trading as per exchange guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and clearing members must configure their trading terminals and risk management systems in accordance with the specified strike price intervals.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for the applicable derivatives contract cycles.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures uniformity and clarity in option contract strike selections, minimizing pricing discrepancies and order rejections.
