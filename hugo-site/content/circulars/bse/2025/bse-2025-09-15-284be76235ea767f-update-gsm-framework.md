## Summary

BSE has issued an update regarding the Graduated Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework, detailing the list of securities placed under surveillance along with their applicable price bands and groups.

## Key Points

- Lists multiple scrips subjected to surveillance measures.
- Specifies applicable price bands ranging from 2% to 20% for listed entities.
- Identifies the corresponding trading groups (such as X, XT, Z, M, B, T) for each scrip.

## Regulatory Changes

- Securities are subjected to specific surveillance monitoring and restricted price bands to protect investors from excessive volatility.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable price bands and group classifications when executing trades in these scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective date: July 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the listed securities is subject to enhanced monitoring and restricted price movement limits.
