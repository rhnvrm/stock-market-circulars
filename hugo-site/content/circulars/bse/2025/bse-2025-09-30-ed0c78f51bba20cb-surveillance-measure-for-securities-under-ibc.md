## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the surveillance measures for securities under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC) effective October 01, 2025. The notification includes lists of securities moving to Stage 0 under the IBC framework, as well as consolidated lists of companies undergoing the Insolvency Resolution Process (IRP).

## Key Points

- Effective date for the surveillance measures is October 01, 2025.
- Securities moving to Stage 0 of Receipt of corporate announcement / disclosure include companies such as Axis Solutions Limited, East India Drums And Barrels M, Eastern Silk Industries Ltd., Indosolar Ltd., Jaiprakash Associates Ltd., and others.
- Consolidated list includes various scrips under different stages of the IRP as per IBC.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of Additional Surveillance Measure (ASM) stages for companies undergoing insolvency resolution processes under the IBC.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance status of the listed securities and apply necessary trading restrictions and margin requirements accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 01, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances market integrity by providing transparent surveillance updates for companies undergoing insolvency proceedings, protecting investors from heightened volatility.
