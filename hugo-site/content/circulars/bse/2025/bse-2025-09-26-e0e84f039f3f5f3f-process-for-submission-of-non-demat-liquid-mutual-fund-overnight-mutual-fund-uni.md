## Summary

BSE has outlined the process and provided a standard format for a lien application cum consent letter to facilitate the submission of non-demat liquid mutual fund or overnight mutual fund units towards deposit requirements by Research Analysts (RAs) and Investment Advisers.

## Key Points

- RAs and Investment Advisers can pledge non-demat liquid or overnight mutual fund units as part of their deposit requirements with RAASB (BSE Limited).
- Requires submission of a formal Lien Application cum Consent Letter to the respective RTA / AMC.
- Pledged units remain under lien until lifted or vacated by BSE Limited.
- Units must be free of prior encumbrances, locks, or tax-saving constraints (such as Section 54EA/EB).

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes standardized procedures for asset verification and lien marking through RTAs/AMCs for regulatory deposits.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research Analysts must ensure units are held as free balances and submit duly signed lien application forms to the respective AMC/RTA.
- Acknowledge that BSE Limited retains the right to redeem or transfer units in case of unpaid arbitration dues or conciliation outcomes under the Online Dispute Resolution Mechanism.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately upon issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides operational clarity for Research Analysts to fulfill regulatory deposit mandates using liquid and overnight mutual fund holdings without liquidation.
