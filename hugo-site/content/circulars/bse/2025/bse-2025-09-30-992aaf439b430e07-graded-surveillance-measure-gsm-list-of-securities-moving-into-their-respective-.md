## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding securities moving into various Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) stages, highlighting increased monitoring and trading restrictions for specific listed entities.

## Key Points

- Lcc Infotech Ltd. moved to GSM Stage I.
- Manraj Housing Finance Ltd., Charms Industries Ltd., and Simplex Mills Company Ltd. moved to GSM Stage III.
- Dhenu Buildcon Infra Ltd., Advik Laboratories Ltd., Cistro Telelink Ltd., and Purohit Construction Limited moved to GSM Stage IV.
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower in the GSM framework due to inclusion in the ESM framework, and those marked with ($) due to inclusion in the IBC framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Imposition of stricter surveillance stages and trading curbs on specified securities to safeguard market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable GSM stages and adhere to the corresponding trading restrictions and margin requirements.

## Important Dates

Effective date: As specified in the circular.

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the affected securities will be subject to surveillance controls, potentially impacting liquidity and price discovery.
