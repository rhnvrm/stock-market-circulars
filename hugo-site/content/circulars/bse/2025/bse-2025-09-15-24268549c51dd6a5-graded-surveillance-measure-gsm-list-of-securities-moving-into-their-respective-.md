## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notice regarding the movement of securities into various stages of the Graded Surveillance Measure (GSM) framework. Specific securities have been placed under GSM Stage I and Stage III effective from the specified date.

## Key Points

- Pasupati Fincap Ltd., Integra Switchgear Ltd., Span Divergent Limited, City Online Services Limited, and Dalmia Industrial Development Limited have moved to GSM Stage I.
- Gayatri Projects Ltd. has moved to GSM Stage III.
- Securities marked with (#) are moving lower under the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the ESM Framework.
- Securities marked with ($) are moving lower under the GSM Framework due to inclusion in the IBC Framework.

## Regulatory Changes

Applicable surveillance actions and trading restrictions associated with GSM Stages I and III are enforced on the listed securities to safeguard market integrity and investor interests.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the surveillance stages and ensure compliance with applicable margin requirements and trading curbs for the affected securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in these securities will be subject to enhanced surveillance, additional margin requirements, or trading restrictions depending on their respective GSM stage, potentially affecting liquidity and short-term price volatility.
