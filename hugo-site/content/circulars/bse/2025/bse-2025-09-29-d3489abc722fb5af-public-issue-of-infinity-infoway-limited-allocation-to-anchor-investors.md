## Summary

Infinity Infoway Limited announced the finalization of its anchor investor allocation for its upcoming public issue. In consultation with the Book Running Lead Managers, the company allocated 4,08,000 equity shares to anchor investors at an offer price of Rs. 155/- per share, aggregating to Rs. 6,32,40,000.

## Key Points

- Company: Infinity Infoway Limited
- Date of Board Meeting: September 29, 2025
- Total Equity Shares Allocated to Anchor Investors: 4,08,000 shares
- Anchor Investor Offer Price: Rs. 155/- per share
- Total Amount: Rs. 6,32,40,000
- Key Anchor Investors include Holani Venture Capital Fund I, Finavenue Capital Trust, Meru Investment Fund PCC, Aarthai F Growth Fund, and VPX Ventures Fund - Scheme 1.
