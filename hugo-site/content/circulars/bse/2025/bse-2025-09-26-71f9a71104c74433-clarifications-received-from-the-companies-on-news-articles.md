## Summary

BSE and NSE sought clarifications from Waaree Energies Limited and Balmer Lawrie & Co. Ltd. regarding recent news articles and media reports under Regulation 30(11) of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- **Waaree Energies Ltd.**: Responded to news regarding a US probe for alleged duty evasion on solar imports, highlighting its strong manufacturing presence and ongoing compliance in the US.
- **Balmer Lawrie & Co. Ltd.**: Addressed media speculation regarding its plans to exit the refinery and oil services field.
- Both companies confirmed adherence to Regulation 30 disclosure requirements and stated that share price movements are market-driven.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with Regulation 30 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 for timely disclosure of material events and information.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Clarification**: September 26, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Ensures market transparency by providing official corporate responses to circulating media reports, mitigating speculative impact on stock prices.
