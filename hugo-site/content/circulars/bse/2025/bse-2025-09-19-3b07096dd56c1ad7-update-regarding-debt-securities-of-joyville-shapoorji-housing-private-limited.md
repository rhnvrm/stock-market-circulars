## Summary

Joyville Shapoorji Housing Private Limited has announced proposed amendments involving the reclassification of its Series B Debentures into Series C Non-Convertible Debentures and Series D Non-Convertible Debentures. The circular outlines modified terms regarding IRR and redemption premiums based on specified periods and conditions.

## Key Points

- Series B Debentures are proposed to be reclassified into Series C and Series D Non-Convertible Debentures.
- Distributable amounts are allocated based on specific conditions and Event of Default clauses under the 2015 and 2022 DTDs.
- Specific IRR structures are established for Series C and Series D debenture holders across timeline intervals ending through 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reclassification of debenture series affecting terms of redemption and post-tax IRR.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to the conditions set out under the respective DTDs (2015 DTD and 2022 DTD) regarding Events of Default and distribution mechanisms.

## Important Dates

- Milestone dates include 31/12/2025, 01/01/2026, 31/03/2026, and 01/04/2026 for phased IRR applicability.

## Impact Assessment

- Directly affects debenture holders of Joyville Shapoorji Housing Private Limited by altering the payout timelines, categorization, and post-tax return conditions of their holdings.
