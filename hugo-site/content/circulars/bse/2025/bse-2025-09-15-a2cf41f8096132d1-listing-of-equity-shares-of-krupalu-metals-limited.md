## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the listing of equity shares of Krupalu Metals Limited pursuant to its SME IPO, detailing the share distribution, lock-in periods, and demat status.

## Key Points

- Total equity shares listed amount to 58,72,000 fully paid shares.
- Includes shares under various lock-in periods extending up to September 20, 2028.
- Free IPO shares are also part of the issuance.
- All shares are in demat form.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to lock-in requirements and depository guidelines as specified for SME IPO listings.

## Important Dates

- Listing / Lock-in effective from September 09, 2025.
- Lock-in expiry dates extend up to September 20, 2028.

## Impact Assessment

Standard commencement of trading for Krupalu Metals Limited shares on the exchange platform.
