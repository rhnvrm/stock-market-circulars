## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has released an updated list of securities subject to the Add-on Price Band Framework. This circular specifies the scrip codes, ISIN numbers, and scrip names for companies added to or maintained within the price band restrictions.

## Key Points

- Lists 36 securities subject to the Add-on Price Band Framework.
- Includes scrip details such as Scrip Code, ISIN No., and Scrip Name.
- Designed to mitigate abnormal price volatility in specified scrips.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of revised price band restrictions for the listed scrips to ensure market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and trading members must take note of the revised price bands while placing orders for the affected scrips.

## Important Dates

- Effective date aligns with the circular release date of July 25, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Trading in the specified securities will be restricted within the designated price bands, limiting extreme intraday price movements.
