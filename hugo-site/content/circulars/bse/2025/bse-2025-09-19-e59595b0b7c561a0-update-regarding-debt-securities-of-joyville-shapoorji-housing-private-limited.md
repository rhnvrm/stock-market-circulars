## Summary

Joyville Shapoorji Housing Private Limited has proposed amendments regarding its debt securities, specifically the reclassification of Series B Debentures into Series C and Series D Non-Convertible Debentures, outlining specific terms, IRR, and redemption premiums.

## Key Points

- Proposed reclassification of Series B Debentures into Series C and Series D Non-Convertible Debentures.
- Detailed schedules for IRR and Redemption Premium based on specific dates and conditions.
- Distributable amounts to be utilized as per the terms and conditions outlined in the respective DTDs.

## Regulatory Changes

- Terms of Series C and Series D Debentures updated with distinct IRR schedules ranging from 17% to 18.5% depending on the period.

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to the conditions under the 2015 DTD and 2022 DTD regarding Events of Default and distribution of amounts.

## Important Dates

- Applicable IRR periods extend through December 31, 2025, January 1, 2026, March 31, 2026, and April 1, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Affects holders of Series B Debentures by restructuring their holdings into Series C and Series D instruments with revised payout schedules and IRR terms.
