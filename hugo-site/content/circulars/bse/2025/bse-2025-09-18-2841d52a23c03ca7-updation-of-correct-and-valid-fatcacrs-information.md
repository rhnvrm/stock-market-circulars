## Summary

This circular references the AMFI Best Guidelines Circular No. 62/2015-16 concerning the uniformity of KYC requirements, provision of ultimate beneficial ownership information, and implementation of FATCA requirements for mutual fund investors.

## Key Points

- Mandatory provision of "Beneficial Ownership" details for all new mutual fund investors effective November 1, 2015.
- Mandatory provision of additional KYC details (such as gross annual income, net worth) for all new investors.
- Rejection of purchase or switch transactions for 'KYC – On Hold' cases if missing information is not provided.
- Requirement for existing investors to update missing KYC information, beneficial ownership, and complete IPV requirements by December 31, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of uniform KYC norms and FATCA reporting standards as guided by AMFI.
- Restrictions on fresh/additional subscriptions and switches for non-compliant or incomplete KYC folios.

## Compliance Requirements

- AMCs and intermediaries must not accept purchase transactions or switches for 'KYC-On Hold' or missing info accounts.
- Existing investors must update missing KYC and beneficial ownership details to ensure uninterrupted transaction capabilities in existing folios.

## Important Dates

- **November 1, 2015:** Effective date for new MF investor KYC and beneficial ownership rules.
- **December 31, 2015:** Deadline for existing investors to complete/update missing KYC information and IPV.
- **January 1, 2016:** Enforcement of restrictions on existing folios lacking required KYC/beneficial ownership details.

## Impact Assessment

Impacts mutual fund distributors, AMCs, and investors by enforcing stricter onboarding criteria and restricting transactions on non-compliant folios.
