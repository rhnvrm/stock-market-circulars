## Summary

Wealth First Portfolio Managers Limited has submitted its 23rd Annual Report for the financial year 2024-25 pursuant to Regulations 30 and 34 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. The Annual Report includes the notice of the 23rd AGM, Directors' Report, Management Discussion and Analysis, Corporate Governance Report, and Audited Standalone & Consolidated Financial Statements.

## Key Points

- Submission of the 23rd Annual Report for F.Y 2024-25.
- 23rd Annual General Meeting scheduled for Friday, 5th September, 2025, at 04:00 P.M. IST via VC/OAVM.
- Includes Standalone and Consolidated Financial Statements along with auditor reports.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Filing and electronic dispatch of Annual Reports to shareholders and stock exchanges pursuant to SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Report / Letter:** 13th August, 2025
- **AGM Date:** 5th September, 2025

Routine compliance disclosure with no direct market or operational impact.
