## Summary

The Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has issued a notification regarding corporate actions (dividends) and associated schedules—including record dates, ex-dates, and shut periods—for securities participating in the Securities Lending and Borrowing (SLB) segment.

## Key Points

- **India Glycols Ltd**: Dividend with Ex-Date and Record Date on 23-September-2025; Shut Period from 22-Sep-2025 to 23-Sep-2025.
- **Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd**: Dividend with Ex-Date and Record Date on 03-October-2025; Shut Period from 01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025.
- **Tata Consultancy Services Ltd.**: Dividend with Ex-Date and Record Date on 15-October-2025; Shut Period from 14-Oct-2025 to 15-Oct-2025.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a standard operational disclosure regarding corporate actions in the SLB segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the specified ex-dates, record dates, and shut periods when executing trades and managing positions under the SLB framework.

## Important Dates

- **22-Sep-2025 to 23-Sep-2025**: Shut period for India Glycols Ltd.
- **23-September-2025**: Ex-date and Record date for India Glycols Ltd.
- **01-Oct-2025 to 03-Oct-2025**: Shut period for Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd.
- **03-October-2025**: Ex-date and Record date for Glenmark Pharmaceuticals Ltd.
- **14-Oct-2025 to 15-Oct-2025**: Shut period for Tata Consultancy Services Ltd.
- **15-October-2025**: Ex-date and Record date for Tata Consultancy Services Ltd.

## Impact Assessment

Ensures transparency and orderly settlement processing for lenders and borrowers in the SLB segment during corporate action events.
