## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability, continuation, and movement of securities under the Short Term Additional Surveillance Measure (ST-ASM) framework, effective from September 16, 2025.

## Key Points

- Several securities have been shortlisted and added to the Short Term 5/15/30 Days ASM Framework w.e.f. September 16, 2025.
- Includes companies such as BDH Industries Ltd, Consolidated Construction Consortium Ltd, Indong Tea Company Ltd, Newtime Infrastructure Ltd, and Sanrhea Technical Textiles Ltd.
- Multiple securities are moving out of the Short Term ASM Framework, including Karan Woo Sin Ltd, KBS India Ltd, Lakhotia Polyesters (India) Ltd, Moschip Technologies Ltd, Parmeshwari Silk Mills Ltd, and Shilp Gravures Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures under the ST-ASM framework to monitor price volatility and market integrity.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the applicable surveillance stages and margin requirements for the shortlisted securities.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 16, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Trading in the specified securities will be subject to surveillance constraints such as applicable margins and price bands.
