## Summary

BSE has released the circular detailing the process and consent letter format for submitting non-demat liquid mutual fund or overnight mutual fund units towards the deposit requirements applicable to Research Analysts (RAs).

## Key Points

- Outlines the lien application cum consent letter format required for RTA/AMC.
- Details terms regarding the creation and maintenance of lien on mutual fund units in favor of BSE Limited (RAASB).
- Specifies conditions under which the deposit can be utilized, such as failure to pay dues arising from arbitration and conciliation proceedings.
- Clarifies that dividend re-investments and bonus units are excluded from the lien.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes a standardized framework for Research Analysts to fulfill deposit requirements using non-demat liquid or overnight mutual fund units via lien marking.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research Analysts must submit the duly filled and signed lien application cum consent letter to the respective RTA/AMC to mark the lien in favor of BSE Limited.
- Ensure units are free from prior encumbrances and not held under Section 54EA/EB of the Income Tax Act.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately for compliance with Research Analyst regulations.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the deposit fulfillment process for Research Analysts utilizing mutual fund investments as collateral or security deposits.
