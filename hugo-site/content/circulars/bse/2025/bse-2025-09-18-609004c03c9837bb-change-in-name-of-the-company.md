## Summary

The company formerly known as DMR Hydroengineering & Infrastructures Limited has officially changed its name to DMR Engineering Limited, pursuant to the Certificate of Incorporation issued by the Central Processing Centre (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) on August 22, 2025.

## Key Points

- **Previous Name:** DMR Hydroengineering & Infrastructures Limited (originally incorporated as DMR Hydroengineering & Infrastructures Private Limited).
- **New Name:** DMR Engineering Limited.
- **Effective Date:** August 22, 2025.
- **Corporate Identification Number (CIN):** L74900HR2009PLC039823.
- **Registered Office / Mailing Address:** H. No.- 473, Sector-30, Faridabad - 121003, Haryana, India.

## Regulatory Changes

- Official update of company name registered with the Registrar of Companies (ROC, CPC).

## Compliance Requirements

- The Company is obligated to display its old name along with its new name at all required places for a period of two years in terms of Section 12 of the Companies Act.

## Important Dates

- **Certificate Date:** August 22, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational and legal name change with minimal direct impact on market trading, pending ticker or ISIN symbol updates if applicable.
