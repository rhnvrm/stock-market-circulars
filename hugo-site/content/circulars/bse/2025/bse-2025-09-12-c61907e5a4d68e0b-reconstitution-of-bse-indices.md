## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the periodic reconstitution of its indices, specifically detailing additions and drops for indices including BSE Select IPO and BSE Commodities, effective from September 22, 2025.

## Key Points

- Effective Date: September 22, 2025.
- Index: BSE Select IPO includes additions such as Niva Bupa Health Insurance Company Ltd, Ather Energy Ltd, and Belrise Industries Ltd, while dropping entities like SBI Cards and Payment Services Ltd.
- Index: BSE Commodities includes multiple additions such as Jai Balaji Industries Ltd, Lloyds Enterprises Ltd, and Ddev Plastiks Industries Ltd alongside several drops.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants tracking BSE indices need to adjust their portfolios and benchmark weights in accordance with the reconstitution list effective from the specified date.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 22, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Reconstitution affects index tracking funds, ETFs, and institutional portfolios holding the added or dropped securities.
