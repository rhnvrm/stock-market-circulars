## Summary

Tamrock Great Britain Holdings Limited, a promoter group entity of Eimco Elecon (India) Limited, has issued an oversubscription notice regarding the offer for sale (OFS) of equity shares through the stock exchange mechanism. The offeror decided to exercise the oversubscription option for an additional 7,00,000 equity shares, bringing the total offer size up to 14,23,875 equity shares (approximately 24.68% of the total issued and paid-up equity share capital).

## Key Points

- **Base Offer Size:** Upto 7,23,875 equity shares (approx. 12.55% of paid-up capital).
- **Oversubscription Option:** Additional 7,00,000 equity shares (approx. 12.14% of paid-up capital).
- **Total Offer Size:** Upto 14,23,875 equity shares (approx. 24.68% of paid-up capital).
- **Retail Reservation:** 1,42,388 equity shares reserved for allocation to retail investors, subject to valid bids.
- **Timeline:** Offer scheduled for September 24, 2025 (non-retail investors) and September 25, 2025 (retail investors and non-retail carrying forward unallotted bids).

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with SEBI guidelines and stock exchange mechanisms governing the Offer for Sale (OFS) of shares by promoters.

## Important Dates

- **September 23, 2025:** Initial OFS Notice date.
- **September 24, 2025 (T Day):** Offer opens for non-retail investors.
- **September 25, 2025 (T+1 Day):** Offer opens for retail investors and non-retail investors carrying forward unallotted bids.

## Impact Assessment

The expansion of the total offer size through the exercise of the oversubscription option increases the liquidity and public float of Eimco Elecon (India) Limited shares while adjusting promoter shareholding accordingly.
