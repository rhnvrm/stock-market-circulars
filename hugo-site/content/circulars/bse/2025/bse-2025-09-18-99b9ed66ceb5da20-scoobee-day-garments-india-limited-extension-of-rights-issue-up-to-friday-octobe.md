## Summary

Scoobee Day Garments (India) Limited has announced an extension of its Rights Issue period by 21 days. The revised closure date is now Friday, October 10, 2025, extended from the original closure date of Friday, September 19, 2025.

## Key Points

- Issue period extended by 21 days, moving the closure date from September 19, 2025, to October 10, 2025.
- Issue Opening Date remains unchanged on Friday, September 12, 2025.
- Last Date for On Market Renunciation of Rights Entitlements remains unchanged on Tuesday, September 16, 2025.
- Subsequent timeline dates including Basis of Allotment, Date of Allotment, Date of Credit, and Date of Listing have been correspondingly postponed.

## Regulatory Changes

- N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Companies must publish advertisements regarding schedule changes in newspapers and submit the same to stock exchanges in compliance with SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Important Dates

- **Issue Opening Date:** September 12, 2025 (Unchanged)
- **Last Date for On Market Renunciation:** September 16, 2025 (Unchanged)
- **Issue Closing Date:** October 10, 2025 (Revised from September 19, 2025)
- **Finalisation of Basis of Allotment:** October 17, 2025
- **Date of Allotment:** October 20, 2025
- **Date of Credit:** October 24, 2025
- **Date of Listing:** October 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

- Investors holding rights entitlements have an extended window to subscribe to the rights issue up to October 10, 2025.
