## Summary

An open offer is being made by M/s U G Patwardhan Services Private Limited, M/s Agri One India Ventures LLP, and Mr. Ravidutt Parikh to acquire up to 7,86,077 equity shares (representing 26.00% of the voting share capital) of Pace Automation Limited from its public shareholders at an offer price of ₹13.00 per share payable in cash, pursuant to SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Key Points

- Target Company: Pace Automation Limited.
- Acquirers: M/s U G Patwardhan Services Private Limited, M/s Agri One India Ventures LLP, and Mr. Ravidutt Parikh.
- Offer Size: Up to 7,86,077 equity shares (26.00% of voting share capital).
- Offer Price: ₹13.00/- per share payable in cash.
- Regulations: Regulations 3(1) and 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

None. The offer is made in compliance with existing SEBI (SAST) Regulations for substantial acquisition of shares and change in control.

## Compliance Requirements

Public shareholders wishing to tender their shares must follow the procedure outlined in the Letter of Offer and submit their forms through their respective stockbrokers.

## Important Dates

- Last Date for Upward Revision of Offer Price/Size: Wednesday, October 01, 2025.

## Impact Assessment

Provides public shareholders an exit opportunity at the specified offer price and results in a change of management and control for Pace Automation Limited.
