## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the Enhanced Surveillance Measure (ESM) framework, detailing the list of securities newly shortlisted, moved to higher stages, and consolidated under the framework effective September 17, 2025.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of new securities into the ESM Framework w.e.f. Sep 17, 2025 (e.g., Mrugesh Trading Ltd, Newtime Infrastructure Ltd).
- Movement of existing securities to higher Stage ESM (e.g., Amalgamated Electricity Company Ltd, MRC Agrotech Ltd, Nectar Lifesciences Ltd).
- Consolidated list provided covering various securities subject to ESM stages I and II.

## Regulatory Changes

Application of Enhanced Surveillance Measures to listed companies meeting specific stock price movement and volatility criteria.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must adhere to trading restrictions, margin requirements, and settlement conditions applicable to securities under the ESM framework.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 17, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Increased margin requirements and trading surveillance for the listed securities may impact liquidity and trading volumes for the affected scrips.
