## Summary

BSE has issued a circular regarding the applicability of the Long Term Additional Surveillance Measure (LT-ASM) framework effective from September 30, 2025. The notice details shortlistings, stage movements, and securities moving out of the LT-ASM framework.

## Key Points

- Shortlisting of securities in the Long Term ASM Framework (Annexure I, Part A).
- Securities continuing in the framework but moving to higher/lower stages (Annexure I, Parts B and C).
- Securities moving out of the Long Term ASM Framework (Annexure II).
- Consolidated list of securities under various ASM stages (Annexure III).

## Regulatory Changes

Implementation of surveillance measures including margin requirements and trading restrictions as per the Long Term ASM framework guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and trading members must take note of the securities entering, moving within, or exiting the LT-ASM framework and adjust margins and trading parameters accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 30, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Affects liquidity and margin requirements for specific scrips listed under the surveillance framework.
