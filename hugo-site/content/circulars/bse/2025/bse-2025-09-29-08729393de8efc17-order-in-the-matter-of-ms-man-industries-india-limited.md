## Summary

SEBI issued a final order against M/s. MAN Industries (India) Limited (MIIL) and its key officials (Mr. Ramesh Mansukhani, Mr. Nikhil Mansukhani, and Mr. Ashok Gupta) following a forensic audit and detailed investigation that revealed financial misrepresentation, non-consolidation of subsidiary financials, non-disclosure of related party transactions, and fund misutilization during FY 2014-15 to FY 2020-21.

## Key Points

- Forensic audit revealed misrepresentation of consolidated financial statements due to non-consolidation of subsidiary accounts (MSPL).
- Non-recognition of interest income on loans given to related parties under Section 186(7) of the Companies Act, 2013.
- Unapproved conversion of loans into capital advances without audit committee approval or proper related-party transaction disclosures.
- Intra-year movement of funds between related parties disclosed on a net basis contrary to accounting standards.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement actions under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1), 11B(2) read with Sections 15HA and 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992.
- Violations identified under SEBI (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices Relating to Securities Market) Regulations, 2003 (PFUTP) and SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015 (LODR).

## Compliance Requirements

- Strict adherence to mandatory consolidation of subsidiaries and transparent reporting of related party transactions.
- Prior approval from the Audit Committee for loans and advances to related parties in compliance with the LODR Regulations and Companies Act.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 29, 2025 (Referencing investigation period FY 2014-15 to FY 2020-21 and SCN dated August 03, 2022).

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights regulatory scrutiny on corporate governance, related party transactions, and financial reporting integrity for listed entities.
