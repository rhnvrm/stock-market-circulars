## Summary

The Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7107 of 2026 filed by Deepali Singhal against the CPIO's response regarding RTI queries on IPO allotment transparency, system audits of IPO managers, and manipulation data for 2024-25 and 2025-26. The appeal was dismissed, upholding the respondent's decision that the queries were vague and not specific.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information and documents regarding the IPO allotment process, process documents issued to IPO managers, audit findings of IPO manager systems (2024-25 and 2025-26), and manipulation identified by SEBI.
- The CPIO stated that queries were vague, non-specific, and did not qualify as 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- The Appellate Authority concurred with the CPIO, citing precedence from the Central Information Commission (Mr. T. V. Sundaresan vs. CPIO, SEBI) that broad, multiple, general queries cannot be furnished under the RTI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes or guidelines were introduced; this order reinforces existing standards on the specificity required for RTI applications.

## Compliance Requirements

No new compliance obligations established for market participants.

This order has no direct operational or market impact, reaffirming the legal interpretation of specificity under the Right to Information Act, 2005.
