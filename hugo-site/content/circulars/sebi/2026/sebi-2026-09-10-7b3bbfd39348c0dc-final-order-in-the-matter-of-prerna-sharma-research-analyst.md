## Summary

SEBI issued a final order against Ms. Prerna Sharma, a registered Research Analyst (Registration No. INH000006819), following an onsite inspection that revealed multiple violations of the SEBI (Research Analyst) Regulations, 2014 and PFUTP Regulations. The violations included failure to use the term 'Research Analyst' in all correspondences, lack of material conflict of interest disclosures, omitting necessary information and definitions in recommendations, and misleading clients via YouTube videos.

## Key Points

- SEBI conducted a surprise onsite inspection of the Noticee between March 20, 2023, and March 22, 2023.
- The Noticee failed to use the mandatory designation 'Research Analyst' across all communications.
- Required disclosures under Regulation 19 of RA Regulations, such as material conflicts of interest with subject companies, were omitted.
- The Noticee did not provide information relied upon or define/disclose specific terms used in client recommendations.
- The Noticee utilized YouTube videos in a manner that misled and lured gullible investors, raising concerns under unfair trade practices.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of existing provisions under the SEBI (Research Analyst) Regulations, 2014, SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008, and SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003.

## Compliance Requirements

Research Analysts must strictly ensure full compliance with disclosure norms, appropriate use of professional designation, transparency in recommendations, and adherence to fair marketing and client communication practices.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2021 to March 22, 2023
- Show Cause Notice Date: January 05, 2026
- Enquiry Report Date: December 31, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's strict stance against unregistered or non-compliant research analysts and misleading promotional activities targeting retail investors.
