## Summary

An RTI appeal was filed by Vivek Tripathi as the legal heir of investors in Helios Plantation & Developers Limited, seeking certified copies of complaints, investment details, claims, investigation records, and related correspondence from SEBI.

## Key Points

- Appellant Vivek Tripathi sought information under the RTI Act, 2005 concerning investments made by his late family members in Helios Plantation & Developers Limited.
- The requested information includes complaints, investment details, investor lists, investigation records, correspondence with other authorities, and refund/distribution details.
