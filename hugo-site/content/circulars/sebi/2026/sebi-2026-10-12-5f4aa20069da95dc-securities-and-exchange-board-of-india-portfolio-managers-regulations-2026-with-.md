## Summary

SEBI has promulgated the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2026, establishing a comprehensive regulatory framework governing the registration, operations, duties, responsibilities, and obligations of portfolio managers. These regulations become effective on April 1, 2027.

## Key Points

- Notified by SEBI on October 7, 2026, and published in the Gazette of India on October 8, 2026.
- Effective date of implementation is April 1, 2027.
- Sets out precise definitions and legal frameworks for associates, body corporates, accredited investors, alternative investment funds (AIFs), and co-investment portfolio managers.
- Provides rigorous regulatory oversight on portfolio management services and operational conduct.

## Regulatory Changes

- Comprehensive enactment of the 2026 regulations replacing previous operational frameworks for portfolio managers.
- Updated definitions and statutory clarifications regarding control changes, corporate bodies, and specialized portfolio managers such as co-investment portfolio managers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Portfolio managers must align their operational structures, documentation, and compliance frameworks with the new 2026 regulations prior to the effective date.
- Ongoing adherence to registration conditions, duties, and responsibilities stipulated under the regulations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notification: October 7, 2026
- Effective Date: April 1, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Significant regulatory and operational impact on all portfolio managers and asset management entities operating in India, requiring thorough preparation and compliance overhaul ahead of April 1, 2027.
