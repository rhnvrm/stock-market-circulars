## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7709 of 2024 against M Sethujanani in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This enforcement action is directed towards the realization of outstanding dues, penalties, or interest arising from previous adjudication and final orders related to market irregularities.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7709 of 2024.
- Defaulter / Noticee: M Sethujanani (Noticee No. 37 in original proceedings).
- Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd. (ARL) investigation regarding financial irregularities, fund diversion, and fraudulent trading schemes.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992, for the recovery of unpaid penalties and disgorgement amounts ordered by SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are required to freeze bank accounts, lockers, and demat accounts of the noticee as specified in the attachment notice to prevent unauthorized transactions and facilitate debt recovery.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict regulatory enforcement and ongoing recovery measures against entities and individuals involved in historical financial misstatement and market manipulation cases.
