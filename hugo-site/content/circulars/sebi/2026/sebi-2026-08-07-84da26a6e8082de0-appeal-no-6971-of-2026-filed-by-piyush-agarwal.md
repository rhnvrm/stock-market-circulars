## Summary

An RTI appeal was filed by Piyush Agarwal against the CPIO of SEBI regarding an RTI application dated June 19, 2026. The appellant sought highly granular trade-wise order audit trail and execution details for the trading account of Mr. Sudhir Kumar Agarwal, managed by the defaulting broker BMA Wealth Creators Ltd. (now BRH Wealth Kreators Ltd.), covering the period from February 5, 2007, to December 3, 2019. The CPIO responded that such information is not maintained by SEBI in the normal course of regulation of the securities market, leading to this appeal.

## Key Points

- **Granular Request**: The appellant requested 12 distinct points of detailed transactional data, including Order IDs, millisecond timestamps, Buy/Sell indicators, Dealer/Terminal IDs, modification/cancellation histories, and NSE order audit trail records.
- **Defaulter Entity**: The trading member, BMA Wealth Creators Ltd. (now BRH Wealth Kreators Ltd.), has been declared a defaulter.
- **Regulatory Boundaries**: SEBI clarified that it does not maintain granular trade-level execution files and order audit trails in its normal regulatory operations, as these are typically maintained by the stock exchanges.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced by this order.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no new compliance actions or requirements for market participants as a result of this decision.

## Important Dates

- **June 19, 2026**: Filing of the original RTI application.
- **July 09, 2026**: Response from the CPIO, SEBI.
- **July 13, 2026**: Filing of the RTI appeal.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: None. This is an individual administrative adjudication confirming SEBI's data retention and maintenance boundaries regarding trade execution records.
- **Investor Impact**: Highlights that investors seeking order routing and millisecond-level execution history must obtain them from the respective Stock Exchange (NSE) rather than SEBI, particularly when dealing with defaulting brokers.
