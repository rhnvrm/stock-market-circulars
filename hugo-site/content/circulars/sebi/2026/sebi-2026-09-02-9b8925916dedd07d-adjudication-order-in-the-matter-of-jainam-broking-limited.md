## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order against Jainam Broking Limited following inspections covering periods from April 2021 to September 2022 and November 2022 to November 2023. The inspections revealed non-compliances regarding the misuse of client funds, incorrect reporting of margins utilized for credit balance clients, and client funding beyond permitted timelines.

## Key Points

- Comprehensive inspections of Jainam Broking Limited were conducted by SEBI for two inspection periods (IP1 and IP2).
- Violations included submitting incorrect details for margins utilized for credit balance clients under SEBI circular guidelines.
- Noticee was found to have funded clients beyond the permissible T+2+5 timeline.
- Adjudication proceedings were initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, and Section 23-I of the SCRA Act, 1956.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of existing norms under SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 1992, and relevant circulars concerning client fund segregation and margin utilization.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock brokers must accurately report margin utilization for credit balance clients.
- Stock brokers must strictly adhere to timelines regarding client funding and avoid unauthorized credit extension beyond T+2+5.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 2026
- Inspection Period 1: April 01, 2021 to September 30, 2022
- Inspection Period 2: November 01, 2022 to November 30, 2023

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on stock brokers regarding client fund handling and adherence to margin reporting standards.
