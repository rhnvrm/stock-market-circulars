## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has announced the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 9265 of 2026, which was originally dated 29.07.2026 and issued against Kavita Shah in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9265 of 2026 has been officially canceled.
- The certificate was issued against individual noticee Kavita Shah.
- The proceedings relate to Sadhna Broadcast Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes introduced; this is a specific administrative recovery order update.

## Compliance Requirements

Not applicable for general market participants.

## Important Dates

- Date of Original Certificate: 29.07.2026
- Action Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Localized impact restricted to the named individual and proceeding parties with no systemic market implications.
