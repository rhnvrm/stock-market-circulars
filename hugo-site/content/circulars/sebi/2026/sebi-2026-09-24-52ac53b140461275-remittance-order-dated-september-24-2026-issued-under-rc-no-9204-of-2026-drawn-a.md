## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated September 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9204 of 2026 drawn against Usha Devi. This action relates to recovery proceedings and enforcement measures in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9204 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Usha Devi.
- Matter: Trading activities and recovery of dues/penalties in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and disgorgement amounts arising from market manipulation and irregular trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated intermediaries are required to comply with remittance and attachment directions, ensuring funds or assets associated with the noticee are dealt with strictly in accordance with SEBI recovery orders.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects SEBI's ongoing rigorous enforcement and recovery mechanisms against entities involved in price manipulation and unauthorized trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Global Limited.
