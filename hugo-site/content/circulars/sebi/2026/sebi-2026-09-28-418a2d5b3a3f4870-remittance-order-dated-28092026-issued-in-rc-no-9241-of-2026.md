## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a remittance order dated September 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9241 of 2026. Banks and mutual funds holding accounts or folios of the defaulter, Angad Rathod, have been directed to remit outstanding dues amounting to Rs. 5,86,000/-.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter Name:** Angad Rathod (PAN: CIUPR1814B).
- **Recovery Certificate Number:** RC No. 9241 of 2026.
- **Outstanding Dues:** Rs. 5,86,000/- (including previous dues of 5,76,000/- plus further interest, costs, and charges).
- **Attachment Proceedings:** Nos. 15709 and 15710 of 2026.
- **Remittance Account:** A/c No. SEBIRRDPEN9241 with ICICI Bank, IFS code – ICICI0000106.
- **Intimation:** Remittance details must be emailed to kapilsankhla@sebi.gov.in / pankajs@sebi.gov.in.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an enforcement and recovery action issued under Section 28A and 28B of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with the Income Tax Act, 1961.

## Compliance Requirements

All banks and mutual funds in India maintaining accounts or folios for the named defaulter must remit the specified outstanding amount (up to Rs. 5,86,000/-) to SEBI's designated ICICI Bank account and submit the required transaction details via email. If no account or folio is held, no notification is required.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** September 28, 2026.
- **Notice of Demand / Attachment Dates:** July 27, 2026 and August 19, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

This order impacts only the specified financial institutions holding accounts/folios for the individual defaulter and has no broad market or systemic impact.
