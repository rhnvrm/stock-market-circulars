## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment of Bank Account dated August 19, 2026, under Recovery Proceeding No. 15711 of 2026 in Certificate No. 9248 of 2026 against Punjiben Babubhai Rathod (PAN: CABPR8593L). The total outstanding dues amount to Rs. 18,43,390.90, which includes disgorgement amounts directed by the QJA in the matter of trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited), along with interest and recovery costs.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9248 of 2026 was drawn up on July 27, 2026.
- Total dues amounting to Rs. 18,43,390.90 include Rs. 17,24,465.79 (disgorgement amount), Rs. 1,17,925.11 (interest up to July 27, 2026), and Rs. 1,000.00 (recovery cost).
- All bank accounts, lockers, and proceeds held by the defaulter singly or jointly across all banks in India are attached with immediate effect.
- Banks are ordered to enforce a 'No Debit' instruction on the attached accounts up to the total dues, while credits are permitted.
- Banks are directed to immediately submit account details, one-year account statements, confirmation of attachment, and loan/advance details to SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

- All banks in India must immediately attach all accounts and lockers held by Punjiben Babubhai Rathod.
- Ensure no debits are made from the said accounts up to Rs. 18,43,390.90.
- Provide account details, statements for the last one year, confirmation of attachment, and loan/advance details to SEBI.
- If no account or balance exists, inform SEBI via designated email addresses.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate: July 27, 2026
- Date of Notice of Attachment: August 19, 2026
- Interest Calculation Period: January 01, 2026 to July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures strict enforcement of recovery and refund orders by SEBI, preventing the dissipation of funds held by defaulters in banking institutions.
