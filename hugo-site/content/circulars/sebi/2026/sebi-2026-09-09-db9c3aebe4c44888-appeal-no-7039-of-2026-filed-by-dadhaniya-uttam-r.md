## Summary

An RTI appeal was filed by Dadhaniya Uttam R against the CPIO, SEBI, regarding information sought on the Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) - 2025 recruitment process. The appellant contested the responses provided for query numbers 1, 3, 4, and 5 of the RTI application.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought final merit marks of specific ranks, category-wise accepted/declined offers, and sanctioned/working/vacant strength for various streams.
- SEBI directed the appellant to the website for recruitment results (query 1).
- SEBI denied information regarding accepted and declined offers under Section 7(9) of the RTI Act due to disproportionate diversion of resources for collating data maintained across separate files (queries 3 and 4).
