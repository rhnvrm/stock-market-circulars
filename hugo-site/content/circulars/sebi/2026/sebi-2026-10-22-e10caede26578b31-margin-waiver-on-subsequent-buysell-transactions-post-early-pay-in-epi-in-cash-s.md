## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular regarding margin waivers on subsequent buy/sell transactions post Early Pay-In (EPI) of securities in the cash segment. Clearing Corporations (CCs) shall, on a real-time basis, not collect margin from Trading Members (TM) / Clearing Members (CM) on subsequent buy/sell positions of a client up to the amount of accepted EPI where the client has a net sell obligation and the EPI request via block mechanism has been successfully accepted.

## Key Points

- Applies when a client has a net sell obligation in a settlement and the EPI request for sold securities has been successfully accepted by the CC.
- CC shall not collect margin on a real-time basis from TM/CM on subsequent buy/sell positions of the client up to the amount of accepted EPI.
- On trade date, CC computes available EPI value as the lower of the value of EPI done or value of securities sold.
- If a client squares off the sale position for which EPI was availed, the EPI value is removed, and any resulting client-level margin shortfall is blocked from the proprietary collateral of the TM/CM by the CC (excluding Trade for Trade / TFT securities).
- TMs must ensure risk management allows clients to square off positions or take new positions only if requisite margins are available.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandates Clearing Corporations to implement real-time margin waivers on subsequent buy/sell positions up to accepted EPI values.
- Requires blocking of TM/CM proprietary collateral in case of margin shortfalls resulting from the square-off of EPI-backed sale positions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Clearing Corporations must amend relevant bye-laws, rules, and regulations, implement necessary system changes, and disseminate provisions on their websites.
- Trading Members must update risk management controls to verify client margin availability before allowing square-offs or new positions.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: October 22, 2026.
- Effective Date: Four months from the date of issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Improves trading liquidity and operational efficiency for cash segment participants by leveraging Early Pay-In value for subsequent transactions.
- Strengthens risk monitoring by ensuring collateral blocking on position square-offs while streamlining margin utilization.
