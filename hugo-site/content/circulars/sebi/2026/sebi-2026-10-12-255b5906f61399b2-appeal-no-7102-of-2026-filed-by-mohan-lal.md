## Summary

Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7102 of 2026 filed by Mohan Lal under the RTI Act, 2005, upholding CPIO SEBI's response that information regarding land allotment against PACL Certificate No. U301049xxx and specific refund stop records are not available with SEBI.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought records, allotment details, and file notings regarding land allegedly allotted against PACL Certificate No. U301049xxx.
- SEBI CPIO informed that the requested information is not available with SEBI and referred the appellant to PACL public notices, status reports, and FAQs on the SEBI website.
- The Appellate Authority cited the CIC decision in *Sh. Pattipati Rama Murthy vs. CPIO, SEBI*, noting that if information is not in possession, the CPIO cannot invent it.
- Regarding the appellant's request to transfer the RTI application, the Appellate Authority noted that the disposal of properties and repayment to investors is entrusted to the Justice (Retd.) R. M. Lodha Committee.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; reaffirms existing RTI principles regarding information availability.
