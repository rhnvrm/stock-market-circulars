## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7000 of 2026 filed by R N Rajan against the CPIO, SEBI, under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought information regarding whether companies can demand an affidavit-cum-indemnity bond on non-judicial stamp paper along with KYC documents to release unclaimed dividends for subsequent periods when earlier dividends were already released.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought clarification on whether companies can demand additional affidavits/indemnity bonds for releasing unclaimed dividends after KYC and ISR forms have already been submitted.
- The respondent stated that queries seeking clarification or opinion do not constitute 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- The respondent directed the appellant to the SEBI Master Circular for Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents dated February 06, 2026, and the Investor Charter dated May 14, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; the order refers to existing master circulars for RTAs.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities and RTAs must adhere to guidelines specified in the SEBI Master Circular for Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents.

## Important Dates

- Date of Application: June 10, 2026
- Date of Respondent Reply: July 21, 2026
- Date of Appeal: July 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact; reinforces existing RTI transparency norms and refers investors to established master circulars for RTA-related queries.
