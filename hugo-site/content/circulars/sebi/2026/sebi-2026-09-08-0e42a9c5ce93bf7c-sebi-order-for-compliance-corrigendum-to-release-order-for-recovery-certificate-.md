## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a corrigendum to the previously released order concerning Recovery Certificate No. 4185 of 2021 in the matter of Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited.

## Key Points

- Corrigendum addresses minor typographical or administrative corrections in the original Release Order for Recovery Certificate No. 4185 of 2021.
- Issued under SEBI's recovery proceedings framework to ensure accuracy in enforcement documentation.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; purely an administrative correction of an existing recovery/release order.

## Compliance Requirements

Not applicable; administrative rectification by the regulator.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issuance: September 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No direct impact on general market participants or listed entities as this is specific to recovery proceedings against Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited.
