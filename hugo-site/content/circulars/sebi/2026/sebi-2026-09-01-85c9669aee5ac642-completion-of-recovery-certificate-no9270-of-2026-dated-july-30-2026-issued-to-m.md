## Summary

SEBI has issued proceedings concerning Recovery Certificate No. 9270 of 2026 dated July 30, 2026, issued to the defaulter Magnesh Bala in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited. This highlights ongoing regulatory enforcement and recovery actions.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9270 of 2026 issued on July 30, 2026.
- Defaulter: Magnesh Bala (PAN: ABKPG9103H).
- Company involved: Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
- Action initiated by the Recovery Officer of SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Intermediaries and financial institutions must ensure compliance with asset attachment and freezing orders as mandated by SEBI's recovery officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate: July 30, 2026
