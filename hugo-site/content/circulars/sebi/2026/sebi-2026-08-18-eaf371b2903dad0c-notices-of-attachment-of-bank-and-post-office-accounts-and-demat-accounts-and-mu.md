## Summary

SEBI has issued Notices of Attachment (AP Nos. 15691 and 15692 of 2026) dated August 18, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9212 of 2026 against Rohit Bairwa (PAN: ELYPB0179Q). The attachment covers bank accounts, post office accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios in connection with unlawful trading activities and price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- **Notice Details:** AP Nos. 15691 and 15692 of 2026 under Recovery Certificate No. 9212 of 2026.
- **Date of Notice:** August 18, 2026.
- **Entity Involved:** Rohit Bairwa (PAN: ELYPB0179Q).
- **Company:** DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- **Assets Attached:** Bank accounts, post office accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to SEBI's earlier findings regarding systematic price manipulation and unauthorized trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, post offices, depositories, and mutual fund houses are required to freeze and attach the accounts and folios belonging to the named entity in compliance with SEBI's recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- **Attachment Date:** August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Strict enforcement of recovery certificates ensures compliance, deters market manipulation, and safeguards market integrity by recovering illegal gains from entities involved in fraudulent trading practices.
