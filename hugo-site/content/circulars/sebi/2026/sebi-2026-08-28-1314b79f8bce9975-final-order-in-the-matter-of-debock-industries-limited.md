## Summary

SEBI issued a final order in the matter of Debock Industries Limited and several other noticees following an investigation that revealed severe violations including migration to the main board through fictitious preferential issues, inflation of sales and purchases, submission of false bank statements, and diversion of rights issue proceeds.

## Key Points

- Migration to the main board of NSE was achieved through fictitious preferential issues of warrants and subsequent conversion to equity shares.
- Debock Industries Limited inflated its sales by approximately 72% in FY22 and 77% in FY23, and purchases by approximately 94% in both years through circuitous transactions.
- The company submitted fabricated bank statements to SEBI to conceal irregularities.
- Proceeds amounting to ₹49.50 crore raised through a rights issue were diverted.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities named in the order must comply with the directions and penalties imposed under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1), and 11B(2) of the SEBI Act, 1992.

Reinforces regulatory oversight and enforcement against fraudulent financial reporting, market manipulation, and misuse of capital raising mechanisms by listed entities.
