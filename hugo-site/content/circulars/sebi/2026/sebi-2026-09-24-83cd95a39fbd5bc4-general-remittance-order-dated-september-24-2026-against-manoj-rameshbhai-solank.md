## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 24, 2026, against Manoj Rameshbhai Solanki (PAN: GEMPS6325N) under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9213 of 2026. This action is part of ongoing recovery proceedings concerning unlawful trading activities and price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- **Defaulter:** Manoj Rameshbhai Solanki (PAN: GEMPS6325N).
- **Recovery Certificate:** RC No. 9213 of 2026 (following earlier demand and attachment notices).
- **Scrip:** DU Digital Technologies Limited (DU Digital Global Limited).
- **Context:** Arises from investigations revealing systematic price and volume manipulation, where shares surged abnormally without fundamental backing.
- **Enforcement Action:** Directs remittance of funds from accounts or assets previously subjected to attachment notices to satisfy outstanding dues, penalties, and disgorgement amounts.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings enforced under the SEBI Act, 1992, empowering recovery officers to issue general remittance orders to entities holding defaulter assets.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and entities holding accounts or assets of Manoj Rameshbhai Solanki are required to remit the specified amounts to SEBI towards the recovery certificate dues.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** September 24, 2026
- **Previous Milestones:** Demand notice on June 24, 2026; Attachment notice on July 17, 2026; Prior Remittance Order on August 20, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's rigorous recovery mechanism for enforcing monetary penalties and disgorgement orders against entities involved in market manipulation, ensuring accountability and deterring fraudulent trading practices.
