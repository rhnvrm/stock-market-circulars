## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 20, 2026, bearing Notice No. 15723 of 2026 under Recovery Certificate No. 9245 of 2026 against Virtual Business Solution Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAFCV0106J). This enforcement action relates to recovery of dues stemming from investigations into fraudulent trading schemes and stock recommendations disseminated via social media (YouTube) in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment Number: 15723 of 2026.
- Recovery Certificate Number: 9245 of 2026 (dated July 27, 2026).
- Noticee / Defaulter: Virtual Business Solution Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAFCV0106J).
- Scrip Involved: Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Action Taken: Attachment of bank and post office accounts to recover outstanding regulatory dues and penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures executed pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of monetary penalties and disgorgement amounts resulting from illegal stock promotion schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and post offices are directed to attach and freeze all accounts/holdings of the defaulter and prohibit any withdrawals, transfers, or debits until dues are fully settled or cleared by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Attachment Notice: August 20, 2026
- Date of Recovery Certificate: July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's rigorous enforcement stance against entities engaging in digital pump-and-dump operations, ensuring compliance and financial deterrence.
