## Summary

SEBI has officially announced the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 9243 of 2026 dated July 27, 2026, issued to Arpan Gupta (PAN: AMCPG5914F) as a defaulter. This enforcement action pertains to irregularities and fraudulent activities involving trading-based stock recommendations disseminated through social media platforms (specifically YouTube) in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9243 of 2026 issued on July 27, 2026.
- Defaulter: Arpan Gupta (PAN: AMCPG5914F).
- Matter involves fraudulent stock recommendations via YouTube regarding Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Recovery proceedings have now reached completion.

## Regulatory Changes

No new general regulatory rules introduced; this is an enforcement and recovery completion order under existing SEBI recovery mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals must strictly adhere to SEBI regulations prohibiting unauthenticated investment advice, pump-and-dump schemes, and stock manipulation via social media channels.

## Important Dates

- Recovery Certificate Date: July 27, 2026
- Notice / Publication Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

 Reinforces SEBI's rigorous enforcement stance against social media influencers and entities engaging in fraudulent stock recommendations and failing to pay monetary penalties.
