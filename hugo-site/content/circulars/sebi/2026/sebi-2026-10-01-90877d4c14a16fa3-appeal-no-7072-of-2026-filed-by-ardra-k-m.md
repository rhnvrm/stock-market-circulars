## Summary

The Appellate Authority under the RTI Act, 2005 disposed of Appeal No. 7072 of 2026 filed by Ms. Ardra K M against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant had sought clarification regarding eligibility criteria for SEBI Grade A recruitment under the October 30, 2025 notification specifically concerning PGDM equivalence by AIU and recognition of CFA qualifications.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information regarding the validity of a Post Graduate Diploma in Financial Engineering (PGDM) from Madras School of Economics and CFA qualifications for SEBI Grade A eligibility.
- The CPIO responded stating that the queries were in the nature of seeking clarification/opinion and could not be construed as 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- The respondent noted that Grade A qualifications are governed by the SEBI (Employees' Service Regulation), 2001 (SEBI ESR).
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's stance, concluding that seeking clarifications and opinions does not fall under the definition of 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
