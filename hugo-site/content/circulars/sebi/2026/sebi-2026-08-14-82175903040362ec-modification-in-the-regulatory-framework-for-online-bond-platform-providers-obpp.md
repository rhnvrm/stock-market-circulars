## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular modifying the regulatory framework for Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs) under the SEBI NCS Master Circular dated October 15, 2025. The changes permit OBPPs to offer products and services regulated by the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) as well as bonds issued under Section 54EC of the Income Tax Act, 1961 or Section 85 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.

## Key Points

- OBPPs are permitted to offer products, securities, or services regulated by the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA).
- OBPPs can offer bonds issued under Section 54EC of the Income Tax Act, 1961 or Section 85 of the Income-tax Act, 2025.
- IFSCA-regulated products must be offered in the manner specified for SEBI-registered stock brokers operating within GIFT-IFSC and comply with FEMA 1999, Overseas Investment Rules, and LRS limits.
- Products must be clearly labeled as international or overseas instruments to avoid confusion.

## Regulatory Changes

Clause 5.2 of Chapter XXI of the SEBI NCS Master Circular dated October 15, 2025, has been modified to explicitly include IFSCA-regulated offerings and Section 54EC/Section 85 bonds under the permissible list of products/services on Online Bond Platforms.

## Compliance Requirements

OBPPs offering IFSCA-regulated products or Section 54EC/Section 85 bonds must ensure they are hosted under a separate tab or platform, properly labeled, and fully compliant with relevant FEMA and regulatory guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This circular broadens the product suite available on Online Bond Platforms, enhancing ease of doing business and enabling retail and institutional investors to access international financial products and tax-saving bonds seamlessly.
