## Summary

SEBI carried out an inspection of IIFL Capital Services Limited, registered as a stock broker and operating as an Online Bond Platform Provider (OBPP), for the period from July 26, 2024, to March 31, 2025. Following replies to inspection findings, an Adjudicating Officer was appointed under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, and a Show Cause Notice was issued on April 29, 2026, regarding alleged failures such as not mandatorily routing orders for listed debt securities through the Request for Quote (RFQ) platform of a recognized Stock Exchange.

## Key Points

- SEBI inspected IIFL Capital Services Limited on June 26, 27, and 30, 2025, covering the inspection period July 26, 2024 to March 31, 2025.
- The Noticee operates as a Stock Broker in the Debt Segment and as an Online Bond Platform Provider (OBPP).
- Principal alleged violation involves failing to ensure all orders placed on its OBPP with respect to listed debt securities were mandatorily routed through the RFQ platform of a recognized stock exchange.
- Adjudication proceedings were initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Rule 5 of SEBI Adjudication Rules.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of existing norms under SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 1992, NCS Regulations, 2021, and SEBI Master Circulars relating to OBPP and RFQ routing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs) must strictly ensure that all orders for listed debt securities, municipal debt securities, and securitized debt instruments are mandatorily routed through the RFQ platform of a recognized stock exchange in accordance with SEBI Master Circular mandates.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: July 26, 2024 - March 31, 2025
- Inspection Dates: June 26, 27, and 30, 2025
- Appointment of Adjudicating Officer: April 13, 2026
- Show Cause Notice Date: April 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on OBPP operations and mandatory RFQ routing compliance for debt market intermediaries.
