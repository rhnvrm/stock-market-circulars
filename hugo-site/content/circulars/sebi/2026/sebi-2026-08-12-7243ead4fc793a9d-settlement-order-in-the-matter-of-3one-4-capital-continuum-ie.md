## Summary

SEBI issued a settlement order in respect of summary settlement applications filed by 3One4 Capital Trust, 3One4 Capital Advisors LLP, Pranav Mohan Pai, and Siddharth Mohan Pai in the matter of 3One4 Capital Continuum IE. The examination revealed non-compliances relating to the maintenance of continuing interest, pro-rata contribution requirements, and investment concentration limits under AIF Regulations.

## Key Points

- **Continuing Interest Shortfall:** The continuing interest of the Sponsor and Manager was 2.44% for the quarters ended March 2024 and June 2024, falling below the prescribed minimum requirement of 2.5%.
- **Pro-rata Contribution Non-compliance:** Contributions made by the Sponsor and Manager were not maintained on a pro-rata basis with funds drawn from other investors between March 2024 and June 2025.
- **Investment Concentration Limit:** The Scheme's investment in Darwinbox Digital Solutions Private Limited exceeded 50% of its investable funds during the period from March 2024 to September 2024.
- **Violated Provisions:** Regulation 10(d), 15(1)(c), and 20(1), 20(2), 20(5) of the AIF Regulations, 2012 read with Master Circular provisions and the Code of Conduct.

## Compliance Requirements

Alternative Investment Funds and their managers must strictly adhere to minimum continuing interest requirements, pro-rata commitment drawdowns, and portfolio concentration limits specified under the AIF Regulations and Master Circulars.

- **Order Date:** August 2026
- **Period of Observation:** March 2024 to June 2025
