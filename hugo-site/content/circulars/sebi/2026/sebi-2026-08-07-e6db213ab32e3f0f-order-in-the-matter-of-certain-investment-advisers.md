## Summary

On August 7, 2026, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) issued an order cancelling the registration certificates of 39 investment advisers. This action was taken through summary proceedings due to the entities' failure to pay the mandatory five-yearly renewal fees required to keep their registrations active under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

SEBI clarified that this cancellation is an administrative and documentary action resulting from a lapse in fee payment. It is not based on any finding of fraud, mis-selling, or misappropriation of investor funds. The formal cancellation is intended to prevent the potential misuse of expired registration certificates by these intermediaries to mislead unaware investors.

## Key Points

- **Registration Cancellation:** The registration certificates of 39 investment advisers (Noticees 1 to 39 in the order) have been cancelled with immediate effect.
- **Reason for Action:** The noticees failed to pay the mandatory five-yearly renewal fees required to maintain their registration under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.
- **Administrative Nature:** The action was taken via summary proceedings under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992, and Regulation 30A of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008. There is no finding of fraud or financial misappropriation.
- **Investor Protection:** The order serves to update the list of registered intermediaries and prevent investors from dealing with advisers operating under lapsed or expired licenses.
- **Notable Affected Entities:** Affected intermediaries include both corporate entities and individual proprietorships, such as The Equicom Financial Research Pvt. Ltd., Getclarity Fintech Services Private Limited, Prutech Financial Services Pvt. Ltd., N2 Capital Advisory LLP, Chirag Gokani (Wealthwiz Advisors), and Nishant Chopra (Dezire Research).

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory framework or rule changes were introduced. This is an enforcement/administrative order executing existing regulations under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, and SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Cease Operations:** The 39 affected investment advisers must immediately cease all investment advisory operations and must not hold themselves out as SEBI-registered intermediaries.
- **Record Preservation:** The affected entities are required to preserve all client and business records related to their advisory activities for a period of at least five years.
- **Redress Grievances:** The noticees must redress any pending investor grievances and refund/transfer any client funds or securities still held by them.
- **Investor Verification:** Investors are advised to verify the registration status of any adviser on the SEBI website or via the SCORES/IAASB portals before engaging their services.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** August 7, 2026 (the cancellation is effective immediately upon issuance of the order).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This order primarily impacts specific unlisted intermediaries and individuals. It does not directly affect listed equities, market operations, or trading volumes.
- **Industry Impact:** Medium. This signals SEBI's strict enforcement of registration maintenance requirements and compliance with fee payments for market intermediaries, encouraging other registered investment advisers to maintain active licenses and pay dues on time.
- **Investor Awareness:** High. The order underscores the importance of verifying intermediary registration status, as investors dealing with unregistered or lapsed advisers will not have access to SEBI-mandated dispute resolution mechanisms like SCORES.
