## Summary

An appeal was filed by Richa Kohli under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI's response regarding queries concerning M/s Paisalo Digital Ltd. and disclosures of NPA accounts under SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, stating that the queries were in the nature of seeking clarification/confirmation and did not constitute 'information' as defined under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information regarding whether M/s Paisalo Digital Ltd. disclosed the classification of certain accounts as NPAs and whether compliance under Regulations 30 and 51 of SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 was met.
- The CPIO responded that the queries sought clarification rather than concrete information under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- The Appellate Authority concurred that clarification or confirmation requests fall outside the scope of Section 2(f) of the RTI Act and that material disclosures under Regulation 30 of LODR are available on stock exchange websites.
