## Summary

Ankur Dwivedi filed an RTI application seeking details of land allotment and refund status for PACL bonds held by him. The CPIO responded that the information was not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that SEBI does not hold the information and that refund/disposal matters are handled exclusively by the Justice R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details and documents regarding land allotment against PACL bonds.
- SEBI CPIO confirmed the requested information is not available with SEBI.
- The Appellate Authority dismissed the appeal, citing precedents that CPIO cannot invent information not in possession.
- Investors were reminded that the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee handles all PACL refund matters pursuant to Supreme Court orders.
