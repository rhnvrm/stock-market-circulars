## Summary

SEBI has announced an extension of the timeline for existing Angel Funds (registered on or before September 10, 2025) to comply with the Accredited Investor mandate. The deadline has been extended from September 08, 2026, to March 31, 2027, based on representations from the Alternative Investment Fund (AIF) industry.

## Key Points

- Angel Funds registered on or before September 10, 2025, now have until March 31, 2027, to implement the Accredited Investor mandate.
- Such funds shall not offer investment opportunities to more than 200 non-Accredited Investors during the extended transitional period.
- Acceptance of contributions for investment in investee companies from non-Accredited Investors is prohibited post March 31, 2027.
- Existing investors can continue to hold their investments as per the terms of the Private Placement Memorandum (PPM) and fund documents.

## Regulatory Changes

- Para 8.1.2 of the SEBI Master Circular for AIFs dated June 03, 2026, stands modified to reflect the new deadline of March 31, 2027.

## Compliance Requirements

- Existing Angel Funds must transition their investor base to fully comply with the Accredited Investor requirement on or before March 31, 2027.
- Non-accredited contributions for investee companies must cease after March 31, 2027.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** September 07, 2026 (Immediate effect)
- **Extended Compliance Deadline:** March 31, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Alleviates immediate compliance pressure for older Angel Funds, giving them more time to onboard Accredited Investors and restructure their fund raising mechanisms.
