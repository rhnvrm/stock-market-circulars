## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Attachment of Bank Account under Recovery Proceeding No. 15713 of 2026 and Certificate No. 9249 of 2026 dated August 19, 2026, against Jagdish Chhanabhai Vaghela (PAN: AFPPV2562Q). The attachment is directed towards recovery of Rs. 6,53,117.93, which includes disgorgement amounts directed vide QJA Order No. QJA/MN/IVD/ID7/31947/2025-26 dated December 31, 2025, in the matter of trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited), along with interest and recovery costs.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter:** Jagdish Chhanabhai Vaghela (PAN: AFPPV2562Q).
- **Recovery Certificate:** No. 9249 of 2026 dated July 27, 2026 (Attachment Proceeding No. 15713 of 2026).
- **Total Dues:** Rs. 6,53,117.93 (including disgorgement of Rs. 6,10,378.10, interest of Rs. 41,739.83, and recovery cost of Rs. 1,000.00).
- **Action:** All bank accounts, lockers, and proceeds held by the defaulter singly or jointly with any bank in India are attached with immediate effect.
- **No Debit Instruction:** Banks are instructed to disallow any debits from the attached accounts up to the total dues amount, while credits are permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- All banks in India must immediately attach all accounts and lockers held by the defaulter.
- No debits shall be made in the accounts to the extent of total dues until further orders.
- Banks must provide details of accounts/lockers, 1-year account statements, confirmation of attachment, and loan/advance details to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** August 19, 2026
- **Recovery Certificate Date:** July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is an individual recovery enforcement action against a specific defaulter and does not have broader systemic market impacts.
