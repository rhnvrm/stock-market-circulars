## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Advice under Recovery Certificate No. 9250 of 2026 against Saurabh Gupta (PAN: BFEPG0692E). This action pertains to recovery proceedings arising from investigations into trading-based stock recommendations disseminated via social media (YouTube) in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9250 of 2026
- Defaulter / Noticee: Saurabh Gupta (PAN: BFEPG0692E)
- Matter: Unauthorized trading-based stock recommendations using YouTube in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Issued by: Recovery Officer, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
- Action Type: General Remittance Advice / Recovery Proceeding

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts pursuant to final adjudication and recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and designated intermediaries are required to comply with remittance instructions, ensure freezing/attachment of applicable accounts or assets, and facilitate recovery of dues as directed by SEBI's recovery officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issuance: October 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory follow-through and enforcement actions by SEBI to recover penalties and dues from individuals involved in social media-driven stock pump-and-dump schemes and market manipulation in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
