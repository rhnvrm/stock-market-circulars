## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Prohibitory Order under Recovery Certificate Nos. 6956 and 6968 of 2023 against Sudha Mahendra Agrawal in the matter of Kushal Limited. This enforcement action is taken to recover unpaid dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts pursuant to regulatory proceedings.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Order issued under Recovery Certificate Nos. 6956 and 6968 of 2023.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Sudha Mahendra Agrawal.
- Company involved: Kushal Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of SEBI to prevent transfer, alienation, or charge of assets.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions under the SEBI Act, 1992, concerning recovery certificates and attachment of properties/bank accounts for realization of monetary penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, debtors, and relevant entities are directed not to release or transfer funds, assets, or securities belonging to the noticee without prior permission from SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Notice: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates stringent regulatory oversight and recovery enforcement by SEBI regarding non-payment of dues in connection with Kushal Limited.
