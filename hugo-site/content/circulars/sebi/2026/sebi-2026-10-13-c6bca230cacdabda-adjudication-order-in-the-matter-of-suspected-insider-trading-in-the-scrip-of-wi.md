## Summary

On October 13, 2026, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) issued an Adjudication Order in the matter of suspected insider trading in the scrip of Wipro Ltd. The order imposes a monetary penalty of ₹10 lakh on Supriya Kothari for failing to comply with multiple summons and requests for information issued during the investigation.

## Key Points

- SEBI investigated trading activities of Supriya Kothari in the scrip of Wipro Ltd.
- Noticee failed to respond to initial requests and formal summons issued in September and December 2025.
- A monetary penalty of ₹10 lakh has been imposed under the provisions of the SEBI Act, 1992.
- Violation established for non-cooperation with investigating authorities rather than substantive insider trading charges at this stage.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to Sections 11C(3), 11C(6)(a), and 11C(6)(b) of the SEBI Act, 1992, emphasizing mandatory cooperation with regulatory investigations.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals under SEBI investigation are legally obligated to promptly furnish requested information, documents, and appear before investigating authorities when summoned.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 13, 2026
- Initial Summons / Information Requests: September - December 2025

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's strict stance against non-cooperation and disregard for investigative summons, underscoring severe penalties for withholding information during market surveillance and insider trading probes.
