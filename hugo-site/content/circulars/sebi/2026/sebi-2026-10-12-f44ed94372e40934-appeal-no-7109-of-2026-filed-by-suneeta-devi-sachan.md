## Summary

Suneeta Devi Sachan filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the response of the CPIO, SEBI regarding PACL Certificate U063744xxx. The appellant sought details on refund status, land allotment, verification records, possession status, and procedure for correcting name discrepancies.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought 8 specific points of information regarding PACL Certificate U063744xxx/U063744776, including refund non-processing reasons and land allotment details.
- CPIO informed the appellant that the requested information is not available with SEBI and directed her to public notices, press releases, and FAQs available on the SEBI website.
- The Appellate Authority perused the application, CPIO response, and appeal to dispose of the matter based on available records.
