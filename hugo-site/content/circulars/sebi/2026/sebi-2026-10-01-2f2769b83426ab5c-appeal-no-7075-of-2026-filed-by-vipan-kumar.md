## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority dismissed Appeal No. 7075 of 2026 filed by Vipan Kumar under the RTI Act, 2005. The appellant had sought slab-wise information on commission or brokerage paid to Mutual Fund Distributors (MFDs) for FY 2025-26. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that MFD regulation and supervision are handled by AMFI and such records are not maintained by SEBI.

## Key Points

- **Appellant's Request:** Sought information regarding paid commission/brokerage to Mutual Fund Distributors slab-wise in FY 25-26.
- **Respondent's Reply:** Stated that MFDs are regulated by AMFI, and the requested data is not under SEBI's purview or available in SEBI records.
- **Legal Precedent Cited:** Referenced the Hon'ble Supreme Court judgment in *Central Board of Secondary Education & Anr. vs. Aditya Bandopadhyay & Ors*, establishing that public authorities are not obligated to collect or collate information not part of their records.
- **Outcome:** The appeal was dismissed as devoid of merit.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; reiterates established RTI Act principles regarding non-available records.
