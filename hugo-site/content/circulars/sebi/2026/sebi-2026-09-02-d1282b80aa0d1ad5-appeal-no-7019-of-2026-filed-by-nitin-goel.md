## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7019 of 2026 filed by Nitin Goel against the CPIO of SEBI under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought copies of the SEBI consultation paper on simplification of documentation, received public comments, finalized rules, and proposed formats for indemnity bonds. SEBI responded that certain documents are publicly available, while information regarding internal comments and policies under active consideration is exempt under Sections 8(1)(a) and 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- Appellant requested information relating to SEBI's consultation paper on the simplification of documentation for the transmission of securities.
- Respondent directed the appellant to the publicly available consultation paper on the SEBI website for query A-1.
- Information regarding internal comments was denied under Section 8(1)(a) (internal functioning) and Section 8(1)(e) (fiduciary capacity) of the RTI Act.
- Queries regarding finalized rules and indemnity bond formats (A-3 and A-4) were stated to be under policy consideration and will be made public upon finalization.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes or new rules are introduced by this appellate order; it concerns the disclosure status of documents under the RTI Act.
