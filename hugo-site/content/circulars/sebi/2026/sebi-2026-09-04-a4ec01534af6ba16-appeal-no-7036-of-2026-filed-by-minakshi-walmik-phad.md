## Summary

The appellant, Minakshi Walmik Phad, filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO's response regarding refund claim status and certified copies of land allotment records for PACL. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the information was not available with SEBI and noted that PACL refund matters are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details and certified copies of records regarding land allotment for PACL refund claims.
- SEBI's CPIO responded that the requested information is not available with SEBI.
- The Appellate Authority noted that SEBI cannot invent information not in its possession, citing precedent.
- Refund and property disposal for PACL are managed exclusively by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee under Supreme Court direction.
