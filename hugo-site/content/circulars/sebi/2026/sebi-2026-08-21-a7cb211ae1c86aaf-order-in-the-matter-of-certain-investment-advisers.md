## Summary

SEBI has issued an order under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Regulation 30A of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 against several Investment Advisers for their failure to pay mandatory renewal fees as required under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

## Key Points

- Noticees No. 1 to 15 are registered Investment Advisers with SEBI.
- Under Clause 3 of the Second Schedule of SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, registered Investment Advisers must pay renewal fees every five years within three months before the expiry of the fee period.
- The listed Investment Advisers failed to make the required renewal payments from various dates ranging across multiple years.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of statutory obligations regarding registration renewal fees under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

## Compliance Requirements

- Registered Investment Advisers must ensure timely payment of renewal fees every five years to keep their certificates of registration in force.

## Important Dates

- Effective date of order: August 2026
- Renewal fee payment deadlines vary per Noticee starting from previous years (e.g., 2019, 2023, 2024).

## Impact Assessment

- Non-compliant investment advisers face regulatory scrutiny and potential suspension or cancellation of their registration certificates for failing to maintain active registration status.
