## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992 in the matter of Vedic Ayurveda Ltd (formerly known as KD Leisures Limited), addressing alleged violations of LODR Regulations (Regulations 31A(2), 31A(3), 31(1), and 31(4) read with Regulation 4(1)(e)) and SAST Regulations, 2011 (Regulations 29(1) and 29(2) read with 29(3)) by the company and associated noticees (Arminder Singh and Priyanka Jain).

## Key Points

- Adjudication proceedings initiated against Vedic Ayurveda Ltd (Noticee 1), Arminder Singh (Noticee 2), and Priyanka Jain (Noticee 3).
- Noticee 1 faced allegations regarding non-compliance with LODR Regulations.
- Noticees 2 and 3 faced allegations regarding non-compliance with disclosure obligations under SAST Regulations, 2011.
- Noticee 3 had earlier filed a summary settlement application which failed due to non-remittance of the settlement amount.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A - This is an adjudication order enforcing existing regulations rather than introducing new regulatory changes.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must strictly adhere to disclosure requirements under the SAST Regulations and listing obligations under the LODR Regulations to avoid penal actions under the SEBI Act.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 2026
- Previous settlement application date by Noticee 3: May 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight and enforcement on timely disclosures and listing compliance for listed entities and promoters.
