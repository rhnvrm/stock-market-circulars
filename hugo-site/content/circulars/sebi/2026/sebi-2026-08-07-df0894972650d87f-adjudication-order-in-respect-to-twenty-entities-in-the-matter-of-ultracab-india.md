## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) initiated adjudication proceedings against twenty promoter and promoter group entities (Noticees) of Ultracab (India) Limited. The proceedings follow an examination of shareholding changes during the quarters ended September 2023 and December 2023. SEBI observed that the promoter group offloaded approximately 30% of their shareholding during this period; however, the mandatory disclosures required under Regulations 29(2) and 29(3) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 (SAST Regulations) were not submitted on the stock exchange website. Adjudicating Officers were appointed to inquire into the violations and adjudge penalties under Section 15A(b) of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Key Points

- **Examination Period:** SEBI conducted an examination of shareholding changes in Ultracab (India) Limited for the quarters ended September 2023 and December 2023.
- **Alleged Violation:** The promoter and promoter group entities offloaded approximately 30% of their shareholding without submitting mandatory disclosures on the stock exchange website, in violation of Regulations 29(2) and 29(3) of the SAST Regulations.
- **Entities Involved:** The proceedings involve twenty promoter and promoter group entities (referred to collectively as Noticees or PACs), including Sangeetaben Niteshbhai Vaghasiya, Nitesh Parshottambhai Vaghasiya, Artiben Pankajkumar Shingala, and others.
- **Adjudication Process:** Formally initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Rule 5 of the SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995, seeking to levy penalties under Section 15A(b) of the SEBI Act.
- **AO Transitions:** Multiple Adjudicating Officers were sequentially appointed (March 4, 2025; September 19, 2025; and May 27, 2026) due to internal transfers, with the case currently assigned to the present office.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes introduced. This is an enforcement and adjudication action to penalize non-compliance with existing requirements under the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Takeover & SAST Disclosures:** Promoters and major shareholders must strictly comply with Regulations 29(2) and 29(3) of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.
- **Disclosure Timelines:** Any change in shareholding exceeding specified regulatory thresholds must be reported to stock exchanges and the target company within the mandated timelines (typically two working days).
- **Show Cause Notice Response:** The twenty promoter group entities are required to submit their responses to the Show Cause Notice (SCN) issued as part of the adjudication proceedings.

## Important Dates

- **September 2023 – December 2023:** Examination period of the alleged promoter shareholding changes and disclosure violations.
- **March 04, 2025:** Appointment of the initial Adjudicating Officer by SEBI.
- **September 19, 2025:** Appointment of the successor Adjudicating Officer.
- **May 27, 2026:** Transfer of the adjudication matter to the current Adjudicating Officer's office.
- **August 2026:** Issuance of the Adjudication Order (indicated by the PDF URL timestamp and date).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Transparency:** Failure by promoters to disclose the sale of a substantial 30% stake deprives public investors of crucial ownership transition details, impacting investment decisions and market transparency.
- **Regulatory Enforcement:** This proceeding highlights SEBI's strict enforcement actions against disclosure omissions by key company insiders and promoters.
- **Financial Penalties:** The Noticees face potential monetary penalties under Section 15A(b) of the SEBI Act, 1992, for their failure to fulfill statutory disclosure obligations.
