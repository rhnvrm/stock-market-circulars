## Summary

SEBI passed a settlement order regarding Kaizen Trust (Applicant) in the matter of Kaizen Domestic Scheme I, settling violations related to the delayed winding up of the scheme and liquidation of assets under SEBI (Venture Capital Funds) Regulations, 1996.

## Key Points

- Kaizen Trust launched Kaizen Domestic Scheme I on June 6, 2012, with an extended tenure ending June 5, 2022.
- The Fund failed to wind up the scheme and distribute proceeds within the stipulated 3 months (by September 5, 2022), completing liquidation only on March 13, 2025 (a delay of about 2 years and 6 months).
- The non-compliance pertained to regulation 23(1)(a) read with regulation 24(2) of the VCF Regulations.
- The applicant filed a suo-motu settlement application; the Internal Committee and High Powered Advisory Committee recommended a settlement amount of ₹10,87,500, which was approved by SEBI and remitted by the applicant on August 21, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None (Enforcement and settlement of existing VCF Regulations).

## Compliance Requirements

Alternative investment and venture capital funds must strictly adhere to statutory timelines for winding up schemes, liquidating assets, and distributing proceeds to investors.

## Important Dates

- June 05, 2022: End date of extended tenure.
- September 05, 2022: Deadline for completing winding up and distribution.
- March 13, 2025: Actual completion date of asset liquidation and distribution.
- August 21, 2026: Settlement amount remitted and confirmed by SEBI.

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory compliance and accountability regarding fund life cycles and timely distribution of proceeds to investors in venture capital funds.
