## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular amending the framework for the calculation of Net Distributable Cash Flows (NDCFs) for Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs). Based on recommendations from the Hybrid Securities Advisory Committee (HySAC) and industry requests, InvITs, HoldCos, and SPVs can now add back payments made towards major maintenance expenses for road projects to the extent they are funded by external borrowing.

## Key Points

- Updates Section F (Para 3.19) of Chapter 3 of the Master Circular for Infrastructure Investment Trusts dated July 11, 2025.
- Permits the add-back of payments for major maintenance expenses of road projects funded by external debt in NDCF calculations.
- Applies to calculations at both the HoldCo/SPV level and the Trust level.
- Substitutes certain notes (such as Note No. 4 and 6) under S. No. (III.) titled 'Notes/ Other Rules'.

## Regulatory Changes

- **HoldCo/SPV Level NDCF:** Added a line item allowing the addition of payments made towards major maintenance expense for road projects to the extent funded by external borrowing subject to specific notes.
- **Trust Level NDCF:** Added a corresponding line item allowing the addition of payments made towards major maintenance expense for road projects to the extent funded by external debt.
- **Notes/Other Rules:** Revisions to Note 4 and Note 6 regarding surplus cash and other provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

- InvIT managers and relevant parties must incorporate the revised NDCF calculation methodology for road projects when determining distributable cash flows.
- Ensure external debt funding for major maintenance expenses meets the criteria specified in the notes/other rules.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately upon issuance of the circular on August 14, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Provides greater cash flow flexibility for road-sector InvITs undertaking major maintenance activities via external debt funding, potentially optimizing distribution payouts.
