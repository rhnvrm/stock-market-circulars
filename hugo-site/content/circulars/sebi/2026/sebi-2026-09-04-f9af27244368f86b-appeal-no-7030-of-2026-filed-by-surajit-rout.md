## Summary

Surajit Rout filed an RTI appeal (Appeal No. 7030 of 2026) against SEBI's CPIO response concerning grievance routing regarding CapitalVia Global Research Limited. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that the grievance was duly forwarded to ILO by OIAE and that the complaint could not be processed on SCORES since CapitalVia's registration is no longer active. The appeal was accordingly dismissed.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details and forwarding communications regarding grievance no. DOEAF/E/2026/0000228 and related claims against CapitalVia Global Research Limited.
- The CPIO informed that the complaint was forwarded to ILO by OIAE.
- SEBI clarified that CapitalVia Global Research's registration is inactive, hence complaints cannot be filed on SCORES.
- The Appellate Authority found the response adequate and dismissed the appeal.
