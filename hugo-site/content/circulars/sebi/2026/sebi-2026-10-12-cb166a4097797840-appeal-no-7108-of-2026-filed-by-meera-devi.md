## Summary

This order relates to Appeal No. 7108 of 2026 filed by Meera Devi under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI, concerning PACL Certificate No. U011115xxx. The appellant sought comprehensive details regarding land allotment status, verification reports, rules for withholding refund claims on land allotment status, and procedures for correction.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought detailed land allotment particulars, certified copies of records, and verification reports related to PACL Certificate No. U011115xxx.
- The appellant requested rules, guidelines, and procedures for correcting incorrect 'Land Allotted' statuses that halt PACL refund processing.
- The CPIO responded that the requested information was not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to public notices, status reports, and FAQs available on the SEBI website.
- The appeal was filed on the ground of receiving incomplete, misleading, or false information.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes were introduced by this order; it is an individual RTI appeal adjudication.

## Compliance Requirements

No new compliance requirements are mandated for market participants.

This order has no direct impact on market operations, trading, or listed companies, as it pertains strictly to an individual RTI query regarding PACL investor refund records.
