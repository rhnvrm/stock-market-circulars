## Summary

B. Mohan filed an RTI appeal (Appeal No. 7040 of 2026) against the CPIO's response regarding refund information in the PACL matter. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's reply that the requested information is not available with SEBI and noted that all communications and processes regarding PACL refunds are managed by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information regarding refunds in the PACL matter under the RTI Act, 2005.
- The CPIO informed the appellant that the information is not available with SEBI and advised approaching the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- Citing precedents (Sh. Pattipati Rama Murthy vs. CPIO, SEBI), the Appellate Authority confirmed that the CPIO cannot invent information not in possession.
- The appeal was dismissed, noting that PACL-related public notices, status reports, and FAQs are accessible on the SEBI website.
