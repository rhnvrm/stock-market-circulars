## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 20, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9240 of 2026 against Shreya Gupta (PAN: AIMPJ4419J). The recovery proceedings arise from the matter of trading-based stock recommendations using social media (YouTube) in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9240 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Shreya Gupta (PAN: AIMPJ4419J).
- Matter: Trading-based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, post offices, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to attach and freeze the accounts, lockers, and holdings of the defaulter as specified in the notice and prevent any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights rigorous regulatory enforcement against entities involved in pump-and-dump schemes, misleading social media stock recommendations, and failure to pay SEBI-imposed dues.
