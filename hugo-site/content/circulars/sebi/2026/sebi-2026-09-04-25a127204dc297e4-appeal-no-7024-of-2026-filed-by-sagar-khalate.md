## Summary

Sagar Khalate filed an RTI appeal under the RTI Act, 2005, seeking question papers and answers for the last three recruitment exercises for IT Officers conducted by regulatory bodies including SEBI, RBI, NABARD, IRDAI, and IFSCA. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that SEBI does not possess these records as the recruitment examination is conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) under an MoU, and IBPS does not share these documents with SEBI.

## Key Points

- The appellant requested prelims and mains question papers along with answers for IT Officer recruitment exercises across regulatory bodies.
- The CPIO and Appellate Authority confirmed that SEBI does not hold or have access to these question papers and answer keys.
- Reliance was placed on previous CIC decisions (e.g., Lakshminarayanan R vs. SEBI and Abhijit Bhimgonda Bhikkappa Patil vs. CPIO, SEBI) establishing that information not existing or available in records cannot be provided under the RTI Act.
