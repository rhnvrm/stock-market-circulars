## Summary

An RTI appeal filed by Ebenezer R against SEBI under the Right to Information Act, 2005 was dismissed by the Appellate Authority. The appellant sought detailed information regarding books purchased for the SEBI library from January 2020 to May 2026, which the CPIO had stated was not maintained in the requested format, though year-wise expenditure details were provided.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought itemized details of books purchased for the SEBI library from January 2020 to May 2026, including titles, authors, publishers, purchase dates, copies, and costs, along with year-wise expenditures.
- The CPIO responded that information was not available in the specific format requested, but provided the year-wise expenditure on books.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, citing precedents that a CPIO is not obliged to create or furnish information in a specific format if not maintained as such, and noted that appellants cannot expand the scope of their RTI query at the appeal stage.
