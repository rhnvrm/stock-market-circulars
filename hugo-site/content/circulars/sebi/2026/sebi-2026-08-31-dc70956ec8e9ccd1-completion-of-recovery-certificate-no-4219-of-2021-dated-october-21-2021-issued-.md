## Summary

SEBI has issued a notification regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 4219 of 2021 dated October 21, 2021, issued to the defaulter Seema Khandelwal (PAN: ANSPK6624M) in the matter of Delta Leasing & Finance Limited. The recovery proceedings and associated attachments have been concluded upon satisfaction or resolution of the dues.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 4219 of 2021 dated October 21, 2021.
- Defaulter: Seema Khandelwal (PAN: ANSPK6624M).
- Matter: Delta Leasing & Finance Limited.
- Status: Completion/satisfaction of recovery proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery order conclusion under the SEBI Act, 1992 following the realization or settlement of dues from the defaulter.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and intermediaries holding any freeze or attachment orders on accounts or assets of the named defaulter pursuant to this specific recovery certificate are advised to lift the restrictions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Original Recovery Certificate Date: October 21, 2021
- Notice / Completion Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects the conclusion of regulatory recovery measures for the specific defaulter in Delta Leasing & Finance Limited, with negligible broader market impact.
