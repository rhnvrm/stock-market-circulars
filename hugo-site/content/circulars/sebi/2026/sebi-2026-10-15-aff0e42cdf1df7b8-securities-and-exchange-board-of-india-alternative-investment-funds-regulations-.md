## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Alternative Investment Funds) Third Amendment Regulations, 2026, effective October 15, 2026, introduce key updates to definitions and eligibility criteria for accreditation agencies and accredited investors.

## Key Points

- Revised criteria for accredited investors across individuals, HUFs, family trusts, sole proprietorships, limited liability partnerships (LLPs), and other bodies corporate.
- Inclusion of securities market exposure thresholds as an alternative or supplementary eligibility metric.
- Recognition of specified SEBI registered or regulated entities alongside accreditation agencies for granting accreditation.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amended Regulation 2(1)(ab) to permit accreditation by specified SEBI registered/regulated entities in addition to recognized accreditation agencies.
- Added securities market exposure criteria (e.g., at least 5 crore rupees for individuals/HUFs/family trusts/sole proprietorships; at least 20 crore rupees for LLPs and bodies corporate).

## Compliance Requirements

- Accreditation agencies and SEBI registered/regulated entities must verify investor eligibility against the updated net worth, annual income, and securities market exposure benchmarks.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances flexibility in investor onboarding for Alternative Investment Funds by expanding recognition channels and incorporating securities market exposure into accreditation standards.
