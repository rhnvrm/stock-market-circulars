## Summary

SEBI has issued an order for the remittance of attached amounts under Recovery Certificate No. 8927 of 2025 against Narayan Shivram Kotnis. This relates to the ongoing recovery proceedings in the matter of Royal Twinkle Star Club Private Limited regarding illegal collective investment schemes.

## Key Points

- Order issued for remittance of attached amounts under Recovery Certificate No. 8927 of 2025.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Narayan Shivram Kotnis.
- Matter: Royal Twinkle Star Club Private Limited (unauthorized collective investment schemes).
- Action taken by the Recovery Officer of SEBI to facilitate recovery and investor compensation.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery action under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues arising from illegal fund mobilization.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and holding entities must remit the attached funds as directed by SEBI's Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's rigorous enforcement and recovery mechanism to recover assets from defaulters of illegal collective investment schemes.
