## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 17, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 7163 of 2023 against Investmart (Proprietor: Mr. Anshuman Bhise, PAN: DYFPB1666H).

## Key Points

- **Defaulter Entity:** Investmart (Proprietor: Mr. Anshuman Bhise).
- **PAN:** DYFPB1666H.
- **Recovery Certificate:** No. 7163 of 2023.
- **Order Type:** General Remittance Order.
- **Date:** September 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative recovery action pursuant to prior adjudicatory orders.

## Compliance Requirements

The noticee is required to comply with the recovery certificate demands and remit the due amounts as ordered by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** September 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is an entity-specific recovery action and has no direct broader impact on the general market operations or listed securities.
