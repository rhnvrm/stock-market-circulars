## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7698 of 2024 drawn against K Manikandan. This action is part of enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated following investigations into financial irregularities and misstatements in Arvind Remedies Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7698 of 2024.
- Defaulter / Noticee: K Manikandan.
- Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of unpaid dues, penalties, and interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries are required to freeze accounts, shares, and assets of the noticee as specified in the attachment notice and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice of Attachment: October 13, 2026
- Previous Demand Notice: February 26, 2024

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against individuals and entities defaulting on dues related to market violations in Arvind Remedies Ltd.
