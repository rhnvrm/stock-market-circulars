## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding the release of attachment on immovable property under Recovery Certificate Nos. 4722 of 2022 and 4723 of 2022 drawn against Veera Kumar Das Surapanani in the matter of Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd.

## Key Points

- Release of attachment on immovable property belonging to noticee Veera Kumar Das Surapanani.
- Recovery Certificates involved: RC No. 4722 of 2022 and RC No. 4723 of 2022.
- Company involved: Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Formal lifting of statutory attachment on immovable assets following the resolution or completion of recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant authorities, financial institutions, and registrars are notified of the release of attachment, allowing the property owner to exercise clear title and unencumbered rights over the released immovable property.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order / Notice: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects the formal conclusion and settlement of recovery proceedings, restoring ownership rights to the individual concerning the previously attached immovable property.
