## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice of attachment of demat account and mutual fund folio(s) (Notice No. 15720 of 2026 dated August 20, 2026) against Shreya Gupta (PAN: AIMPJ4419J) under Recovery Certificate No. 9240 of 2026 in the matter of trading based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued against defaulter Shreya Gupta.
- Covers Demat Account and Mutual Fund Folio(s).
- Issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9240 of 2026.
- Relates to irregularities involving stock recommendations via YouTube in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Recovery and attachment proceedings enforced under SEBI Act and relevant regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

Depository participants and mutual fund institutions are required to attach the specified demat accounts and mutual fund folios, prohibiting any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Freezes assets and holdings of the defaulter to recover dues related to unlawful stock recommendations.
