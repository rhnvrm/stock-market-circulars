## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment of Bank Account dated August 19, 2026, under Recovery Proceeding No. 15709 of 2026 (Certificate No. 9241 of 2026) against Angad Rathod (PAN: CIUPR1814B) for the recovery of Rs. 5,76,000/- along with interest, costs, and expenses.

## Key Points

- Total dues amounting to Rs. 5,76,000/- comprise a penalty imposed by the WTM in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited, interest from May 2025 to July 2026, and recovery costs.
- All bank accounts and lockers held by the defaulter, singly or jointly, are attached with immediate effect.
- Banks are ordered not to permit any debits in the said accounts to the extent of total dues, though credits are allowed.
- Banks are directed to provide account details, statements for the latest one year, confirmation of attachment, and details of loan/advances accounts.

## Regulatory Changes

- Exercise of powers under section 28A (1), 11(2) (ia) of SEBI Act, 1992 read with section 226 and the second schedule of the Income-tax Act, 1961.

## Compliance Requirements

- All banks in India must immediately attach the accounts and lockers of the defaulter and submit required account details, statements, and loan/advance particulars to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 19, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Date: July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures enforcement of regulatory penalties and recovery of dues by attaching assets of defaulting entities/individuals.
