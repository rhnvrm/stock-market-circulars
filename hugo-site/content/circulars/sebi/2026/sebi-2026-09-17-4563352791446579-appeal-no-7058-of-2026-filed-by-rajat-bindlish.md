## Summary

Appeal No. 7058 of 2026 under the Right to Information Act, 2005 filed by Rajat Bindlish before the Appellate Authority, SEBI, seeking clarity and records on the prescribed mode of dispatch for statutory communications (such as Annual Reports via web-links) to shareholders under Regulation of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought certified copies of circulars, guidelines, internal instructions, or SOPs explaining or implementing the dispatch of web-links for Annual Reports to shareholders who have not registered their e-mail addresses.
- Queries include whether SEBI has prescribed any mandatory mode of dispatch (e.g., Speed Post, Registered Post, Ordinary Post, Courier, etc.) or proof of delivery requirements under LODR Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this document pertains to an RTI appeal seeking information on existing provisions under SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must adhere to existing provisions under SEBI LODR Regulations regarding shareholder communications and dispatch of Annual Report web-links.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: July 21, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: July 31, 2026
- Appeal Date: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No direct market or operational impact as this is an individual statutory appeal under the RTI Act, 2005.
