## Summary

An RTI appeal filed by Jeetendra Kumar regarding refund status in the PACL matter was dismissed by the Appellate Authority under the RTI Act, 2005. The order confirmed that SEBI does not possess the requested information, as all PACL refund operations are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information regarding refunds in the PACL matter via an RTI application dated June 26, 2026.
- SEBI's CPIO responded that the information is not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, citing precedent that a CPIO cannot invent information not in possession.
- Contact details, status reports, and FAQs regarding PACL Ltd. are available on the SEBI website.

## Compliance Requirements

Investors seeking refunds in the PACL matter must approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee rather than filing RTI applications with SEBI.
