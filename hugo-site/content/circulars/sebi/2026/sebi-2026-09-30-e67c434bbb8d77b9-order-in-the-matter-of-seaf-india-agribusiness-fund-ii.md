## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding SEAF India Agribusiness Fund II (Registration No. IN/AIF2/15-16/0156) due to its failure to file Quarterly Activity Reports (QARs) for the quarters ending March 2025, June 2025, September 2025, and December 2025. Despite substituted service via newspaper publications, the noticee failed to reply, leading to ex-parte proceedings.

## Key Points

- SEAF India Agribusiness Fund II is registered as an Alternative Investment Fund (AIF) with SEBI.
- The AIF failed to submit Quarterly Activity Reports (QARs) for four consecutive quarters (March 2025 to December 2025).
- Summary proceedings under Regulation 30A of SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 were initiated.
- Notice served via RPAD and email returned unserved; final service executed via publication in Hindustan Times, Nav Bharat, and Prahaar on August 26, 2026.
- No reply was received from the Noticee, resulting in the matter being processed ex-parte.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of statutory reporting obligations for AIFs under Regulation 28 of AIF Regulations and SEBI Master Circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

- Registered AIFs must file Quarterly Activity Reports (QARs) on the SEBI Intermediary Portal within 15 calendar days from the end of each quarter.

## Important Dates

- April 24, 2026: Show Cause Notice (SCN) issued.
- August 26, 2026: Notice published in newspapers for substituted service.

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights SEBI's strict enforcement of periodic reporting compliance for Alternative Investment Funds and willingness to proceed ex-parte against defaulting intermediaries.
