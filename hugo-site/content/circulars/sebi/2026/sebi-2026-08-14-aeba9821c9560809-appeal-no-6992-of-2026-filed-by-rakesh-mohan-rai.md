## Summary

The appellant, Rakesh Mohan Rai, filed an RTI appeal under Section 19 of the RTI Act, 2005, challenging SEBI CPIO's response regarding the refund status and claim details of PACL India Ltd investments. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, confirming that the requested information is not held by SEBI and that matters concerning PACL property disposal and investor repayments are exclusively handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought status and refund details for multiple registration numbers relating to PACL India Ltd investments.
- CPIO informed that information is not available with SEBI and advised the appellant to approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- Appellate Authority noted that SEBI cannot invent information not in its possession, citing precedent (Sh. Pattipati Rama Murthy vs. CPIO, SEBI).
- Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee was constituted pursuant to the Supreme Court order dated February 2, 2016, for asset disposal and investor repayment.
