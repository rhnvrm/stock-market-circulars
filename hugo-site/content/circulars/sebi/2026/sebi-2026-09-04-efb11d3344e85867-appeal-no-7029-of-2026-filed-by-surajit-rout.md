## Summary

An RTI appeal filed by Surajit Rout against the CPIO of SEBI regarding information on grievance closure and trading account records with Geojit Financial Services.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought certified copies of reports relating to an appeal against grievance closure and verification reports of trading ledgers.
- Respondent provided action history of the appeal and noted that third-party/unheld trading records are not available with SEBI.
- Appellate Authority reviewed the responses and upheld the CPIO's reply under the RTI Act, 2005.
