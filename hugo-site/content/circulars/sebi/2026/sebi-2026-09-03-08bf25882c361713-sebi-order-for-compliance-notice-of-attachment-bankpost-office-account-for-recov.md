## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice for the attachment of bank and post office accounts under Recovery Certificate No. 8511 of 2025 against Add Life Pharma Ltd in order to recover outstanding dues.

## Key Points

- Entity involved: Add Life Pharma Ltd
- Recovery Certificate No.: 8511 of 2025
- Action: Attachment of Bank and Post Office accounts
- Issuing Authority: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery proceedings through asset and account attachments under statutory provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

- Restricted operations on the designated bank and post office accounts of the defaulter entity until dues are cleared.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 2026 (as per SEBI publication records)

## Impact Assessment

- High operational and financial impact on the targeted entity due to freezing of bank and post office accounts.
