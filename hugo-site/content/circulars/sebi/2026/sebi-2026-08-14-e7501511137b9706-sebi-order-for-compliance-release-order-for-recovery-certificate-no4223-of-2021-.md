## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a release order concerning Recovery Certificate No. 4223 of 2021 against Ms. Pushpendra Singh Baghel in the matter of Sai Prakash Properties Development Ltd., following ongoing legal and settlement developments related to unauthorized collective investment schemes.

## Key Points

- Involves recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI against Sai Prakash Properties Development Ltd. and its promoter/director Pushpendra Singh Baghel.
- Relates to the mobilization of funds through unauthorized collective investment schemes (CIS).
- SEBI has issued a release order concerning Recovery Certificate No. 4223 of 2021.

## Regulatory Changes

[None - Enforcement and Recovery Action]

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals named in SEBI recovery proceedings must adhere to instructions regarding settlement committees and refund processes as directed by regulatory and judicial authorities.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Administrative and legal resolution concerning recovery certificates issued against defaulters in unauthorized investment schemes.
