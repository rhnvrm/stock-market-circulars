## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order in favor of Archit Jindal (PAN: AGNPJ5775G) in the matter of Delta Leasing & Finance Ltd. under Certificate No. 6199 of 2023.

## Key Points

- Defaulter Name: Archit Jindal
- PAN: AGNPJ5775G
- Matter: Delta Leasing & Finance Ltd.
- Recovery Certificate No.: 6199 of 2023
- Issuing Authority: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative release order issued pursuant to recovery proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

Not applicable as this order releases the defaulter from prior recovery attachment measures under the specified certificate.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order concludes specific recovery proceedings related to Certificate No. 6199 of 2023 for the named individual and has no broader market or systemic impact.
