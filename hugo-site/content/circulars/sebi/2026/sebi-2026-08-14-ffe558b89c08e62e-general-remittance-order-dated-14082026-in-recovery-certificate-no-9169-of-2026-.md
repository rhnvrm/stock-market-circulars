## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated August 14, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9169 of 2026 against Jigneshkumar Purshottamdas Patel (PAN: AQEPP8019J). The recovery proceedings arise from the trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited.

## Key Points

- **Authority:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- **Defaulter:** Jigneshkumar Purshottamdas Patel (PAN: AQEPP8019J).
- **Recovery Certificate No.:** 9169 of 2026.
- **Matter:** Trading activities and regulatory violations concerning the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited.
- **Order Date:** August 14, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action via attachment and remittance proceedings under SEBI recovery mechanisms.

## Compliance Requirements

Directives issued to relevant financial entities and banks to remit attached funds/assets in accordance with the recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Targeted enforcement action against an individual defaulter with negligible broader market impact.
