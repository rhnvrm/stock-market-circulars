## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice against Shreya Gupta (PAN: AIMPJ4419J), designated as a defaulter, under Recovery Certificate No. 9240 of 2026. The matter pertains to unlawful trading-based stock recommendations disseminated via social media platforms (YouTube) concerning the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Defaulter/Noticee: Shreya Gupta (PAN: AIMPJ4419J).
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9240 of 2026.
- Scrip Involved: Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Violation/Matter: Unauthorized trading-based stock recommendations using social media (YouTube).
- Action: General Remittance Advice issued by SEBI for recovery of dues/penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of monetary penalties imposed due to fraudulent stock pumping and dumping via social media channels.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are advised to monitor and restrict accounts of the defaulter as per recovery proceedings and ensure no unauthorized transfer of funds or assets occurs.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice/Advice: October 2026 (Recovery Certificate No. 9240 of 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Reiterates SEBI's rigorous enforcement against social media finfluencers and manipulators involved in pumping scrips like Sadhna Broadcast Ltd., ensuring accountability and recovery of illegal gains.
