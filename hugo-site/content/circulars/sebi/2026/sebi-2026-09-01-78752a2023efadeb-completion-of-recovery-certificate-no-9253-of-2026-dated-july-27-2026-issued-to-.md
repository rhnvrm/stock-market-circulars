## Summary

SEBI has issued a completion of recovery certificate (Recovery Certificate No. 9253 of 2026 originally dated July 27, 2026) and release order in respect of Paras Shah (PAN: DOKPS0203A) concerning illegal trading-based stock recommendations and stock manipulation via YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9253 of 2026 was issued against defaulter Paras Shah on July 27, 2026.
- The proceedings relate to fraudulent stock recommendations and market manipulation using social media platforms like YouTube involving Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- With the fulfillment of recovery requirements, SEBI issued the completion notice and release order, lifting prior attachment orders on bank accounts and assets.

## Regulatory Changes

- Finalization and closure of specific recovery proceedings for the named defaulter.

## Compliance Requirements

- Relevant banks, financial institutions, and authorities are instructed to lift attachments on the defaulter's accounts.

## Important Dates

- Original Recovery Certificate Date: July 27, 2026
- Completion/Release Date: September 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Concludes enforcement actions against the individual entity regarding this specific recovery certificate, with no broader systemic market impact.
