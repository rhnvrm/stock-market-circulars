## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment of Demat Account and Mutual Fund Folio(s) No. 15724 of 2026 dated August 20, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9245 of 2026 against Virtual Business Solution Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAFCV0106J) as a defaulter. This action pertains to the matter of trading based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9245 of 2026 (Attachment Order No. 15724 of 2026).
- Defaulter: Virtual Business Solution Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAFCV0106J).
- Scrip Involved: Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Matter: Trading based stock recommendations using social media YouTube.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions taken pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of dues, penalties, and disgorgement amounts arising from illegal stock recommendations via social media.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, depositories, mutual funds, and financial intermediaries are required to freeze all demat accounts, bank accounts, and mutual fund folios belonging to the defaulter and prevent any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's strict enforcement and recovery measures against entities violating securities laws through misleading social media-based stock pumping and dumping schemes in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
