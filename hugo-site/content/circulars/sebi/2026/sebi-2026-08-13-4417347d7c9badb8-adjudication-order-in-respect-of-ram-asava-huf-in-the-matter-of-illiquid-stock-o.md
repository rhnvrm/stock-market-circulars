## Summary

SEBI conducted an investigation into the trading activities in illiquid stock options at BSE between April 1, 2014, and September 30, 2015, which revealed large-scale reversal of trades creating artificial volumes. Ram Asava HUF was identified as having executed non-genuine trades resulting in artificial volumes, violating the PFUTP Regulations.

## Key Points

- SEBI observed large-scale reversal of trades in the stock options segment of BSE, creating artificial volume during the investigation period (April 1, 2014 to September 30, 2015).
- Ram Asava HUF allegedly executed 4 non-genuine trades in 2 stock options contracts, creating an artificial volume of 1,50,000 units.
- Proceedings were initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, and Rule 5 of the SEBI Adjudication Rules for alleged violations of Regulations 3(a), (b), (c), (d), 4(1), and 4(2)(a) of the PFUTP Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes introduced; this is a quasi-judicial adjudication order enforcing existing PFUTP regulations against market manipulation.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must refrain from executing non-genuine reversal trades or creating artificial volumes in stock options or any other securities segments.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: April 1, 2014 – September 30, 2015
- SCN Date: August 08, 2022
- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight and penal actions against entities involved in historical market abuse and illiquid stock options misuse.
