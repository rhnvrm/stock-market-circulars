## Summary

SEBI initiated adjudication proceedings against Puja Rungta for alleged execution of reversal trades and creation of artificial volumes in the stock options segment of BSE during the period from April 1, 2014, to September 30, 2015.

## Key Points

- Investigation revealed large-scale reversal of trades in stock options at BSE, creating artificial volumes.
- The Noticee allegedly indulged in non-genuine trades violating Regulations 3(a), (b), (c), (d), 4(1) and 4(2)(a) of PFUTP Regulations, 2003.
- Proceedings were initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992.
