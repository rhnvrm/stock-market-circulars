## Summary

SEBI issued a final order concerning Varanium Cloud Limited and various noticees following an investigation into misrepresentation, fraudulent practices, and violations of multiple SEBI regulations including the PFUTP Regulations, LODR Regulations, ICDR Regulations, and SAST Regulations.

## Key Points

- Preliminary examination revealed flagrant violations leading to interim and confirmatory orders in 2024 against Varanium Cloud Limited and its Managing Director, Mr. Harshawardhan Hanmant Sabale.
- Subsequent investigation covered detailed violations of the SEBI Act, PFUTP Regulations, LODR Regulations, ICDR Regulations, and SAST Regulations.
- Show Cause Notice (SCN) issued on October 20, 2025 outlined multiple charges including misrepresentation and fraud against the company and associated entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement actions under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1), and 11B(2) of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees are subject to directions, penalties, or restrictions as determined by SEBI under the final order for non-compliance and fraudulent trade practices.

## Important Dates

- Interim Order Date: May 10, 2024
- Confirmatory Order Date: October 21, 2024
- SCN Date: October 20, 2025
- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on corporate governance, misrepresentation, and market manipulation, ensuring stricter enforcement against fraudulent entities and associated intermediaries.
