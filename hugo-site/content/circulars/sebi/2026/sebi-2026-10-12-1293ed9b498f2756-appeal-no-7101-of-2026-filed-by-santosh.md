## Summary

Santosh filed Appeal No. 7101 of 2026 under the Right to Information Act, 2005 before the Appellate Authority of SEBI against the CPIO's response regarding PACL Certificate No. U130007xxx. The appellant sought detailed calculation sheets, verification records, and installment details pertaining to the refund amount determined by the PACL Refund Committee.

## Key Points

- **Appellant**: Santosh
- **Respondent**: CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai
- **Application Date**: September 06, 2026
- **Appeal Date**: September 23, 2026 (Reg. No. SEBIH/A/E/26/00357)
- **Core Issue**: Details and calculation methodology for PACL Certificate No. U130007xxx (55 installments of Rs 2,200 totaling Rs 121,000; claimed Rs 114,400; refunded Rs 92,399).
- **Respondent's Stand**: Information sought is not available with SEBI, directing the appellant to public notices and FAQs on the SEBI website.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an individual statutory appeal under the RTI Act, 2005.
