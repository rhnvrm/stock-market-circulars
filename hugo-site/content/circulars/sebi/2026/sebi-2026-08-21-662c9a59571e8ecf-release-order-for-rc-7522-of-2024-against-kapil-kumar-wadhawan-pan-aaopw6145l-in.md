SEBI has issued a release order regarding Recovery Certificate (RC) 7522 of 2024 against Kapil Kumar Wadhawan (PAN – AAOPW6145L). The proceedings relate to transactions involving shares held by DHFL in DHFL Pramerica Life Insurance Company Limited and related matters.

## Key Points

- Entity: Kapil Kumar Wadhawan (Former Promoter of DHFL)
- PAN: AAOPW6145L
- Recovery Certificate: RC 7522 of 2024
- Subject Matter: Transaction of shares held by DHFL in DHFL Pramerica Life Insurance Company Limited and related matters.
