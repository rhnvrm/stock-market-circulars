## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate number 9277 of 2026 dated August 10, 2026, to Mr. Kapil Wadhawan (PAN: AAOPW6145L), former promoter and CMD of Dewan Housing Finance Corporation Limited (DHFL), directing payment of outstanding dues amounting to ₹27 crore.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter:** Mr. Kapil Wadhawan (PAN: AAOPW6145L).
- **Recovery Certificate Number:** 9277 of 2026, dated August 10, 2026.
- **Demand Amount:** ₹27 crore (comprising penalties under SEBI Act and SCRA).
- **Originating Matter:** Investigation and final orders regarding fund diversion and misrepresentation of financial statements at DHFL.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable - Enforcement Action]

## Compliance Requirements

The notice requires the defaulter to pay the demanded sum within 15 days of receipt, failing which coercive recovery measures including attachment of bank accounts, properties, and arrest will be initiated under the SEBI Act.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Notice / Recovery Certificate:** August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's rigorous enforcement stance on recovering penalties and dues from violators involved in major corporate defaults and financial misrepresentations.
