## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9228 of 2026 drawn against Fulabhai Bhitor (PAN: DYLPB2813Q). This order follows earlier investigation and adjudication orders regarding fraudulent, synchronized, and circular trading activities in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited (AISL).

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9228 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Fulabhai Bhitor (PAN: DYLPB2813Q).
- Scrip Involved: ANI Integrated Services Limited (AISL).
- Purpose: Recovery of dues and penalties arising from manipulative trading activities and non-compliance during SEBI investigations.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed under the SEBI Act, 1992, directing the remittance of attached funds towards the satisfaction of outstanding penalty dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated intermediaries are required to remit the funds from attached bank, demat, and mutual fund accounts of the defaulter as ordered by the SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance Order: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's ongoing enforcement rigor in recovering monetary penalties from individuals involved in market manipulation in SME scrips such as ANI Integrated Services Limited.
