## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 14, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9252 of 2026 against Chiranggi Irish Shah. This enforcement action is taken in the matter of front running by Navnit Gadoya, Chiranggi Irish Shah, and Surbhi Aggarwal under AP No. 15683 & 15684 of 2026 to recover dues/penalties imposed.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9252 of 2026.
- Defaulter: Chiranggi Irish Shah.
- Associated Matters: AP No. 15683 & 15684 of 2026.
- Context: Front running by Navnit Gadoya, Chiranggi Irish Shah, and Surbhi Aggarwal.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of penalties and dues arising from fraudulent and unfair trade practices (front running).

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and depository participants are required to attach and freeze bank accounts, demat accounts, and other assets of the defaulter to prevent any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight and stringent enforcement against market abuse, specifically targeting illegal front running practices and ensuring recovery of illegal gains.
