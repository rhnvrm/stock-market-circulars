## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order under Certificate No. 9251 of 2026 concerning Neha Aggarwal (PAN: AQDPA1749D), designated as a defaulter in the matter of trading-based stock recommendations utilizing social media platform YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd. This release follows the completion of recovery proceedings and settlement of dues.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9251 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Neha Aggarwal (PAN: AQDPA1749D).
- Underlying Matter: Stock price manipulation and misleading stock recommendations via YouTube regarding Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Action signifies the lifting of asset attachments and conclusion of recovery proceedings following payment/recovery of dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery conclusions under the SEBI Act, 1992, detailing the formal release of attachment on bank accounts and financial assets upon due settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and intermediaries are notified to lift freeze orders and restore normal operations for the accounts of the concerned individual as per the release order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: September 1, 2026
- Original Recovery Certificate: Certificate No. 9251 of 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates the full enforcement cycle by SEBI, from freezing assets and recovery proceedings to the formal release upon settlement in high-profile social media stock manipulation cases.
