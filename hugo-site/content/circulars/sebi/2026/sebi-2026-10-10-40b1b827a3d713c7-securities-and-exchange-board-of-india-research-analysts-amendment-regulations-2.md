## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued the SEBI (Research Analysts) (Amendment) Regulations, 2026, amending the principal regulations of 2014. The amendments introduce a formal definition for "institutional investor" and provide an exemption from maintaining call recordings for client interactions involving institutional investors.

## Key Points

- **Definition of Institutional Investor**: Inserted clause 2(1)(ia) defining "institutional investor" with reference to clause (y) of sub-regulation (1) of regulation 2 of the SEBI (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018.
- **Exemption from Call Recordings**: Sub-regulation 25(1)(vii) is amended to exclude the requirement of maintaining call recordings of client interactions for clients who qualify as institutional investors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Insertion of clause 2(1)(ia) incorporating the ICDR Regulations, 2018 definition of institutional investor.
- Addition of a proviso under sub-regulation 25(1)(vii) stating that keeping call recordings of conversations with clients who are institutional investors shall not be mandatory.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research analysts are no longer obligated to store call recordings of discussions or interactions with clients classified as institutional investors.
- Record-keeping compliance frameworks must be updated to reflect the exemption for institutional client interactions.

## Important Dates

- **Notification Date**: October 8, 2026.
- **Effective Date**: Shall come into force on the thirtieth day from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette (published October 9, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Eases operational and compliance burdens for research analysts servicing institutional clients by waiving mandatory call recording retention requirements for those specific interactions.
