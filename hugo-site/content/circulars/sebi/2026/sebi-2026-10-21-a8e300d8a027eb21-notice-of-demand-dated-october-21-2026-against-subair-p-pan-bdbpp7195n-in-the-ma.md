## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Section 222 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, against Subair P (PAN: BDBPP7195N) for recovery of dues amounting to Rs. 97,000 in the matter of M/s Inditrade Capital Limited (formerly M/s JRG Securities Ltd.).

## Key Points

- **Noticee:** Subair P (PAN: BDBPP7195N)
- **Recovery Certificate No.:** 9306 of 2026
- **Total Dues:** Rs. 97,000 (includes original penalty of Rs. 50,000 imposed vide Order No. EAD-9/SM/227/2018-19 dated January 18, 2019, interest of Rs. 46,000, and recovery cost of Rs. 1,000).
- **Payment Window:** Within 15 days of receipt of the notice.
- **Payment Modes:** Direct credit via EFT/NEFT/RTGS to ICICI Bank A/c No. SEBIRRDPEN9306 (IFSC: ICIC0000106) or via online payment facility on the SEBI portal.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

The noticee is required to pay the total dues of Rs. 97,000 within 15 days of receipt of the notice and forward payment confirmation details to SEBI's Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Notice:** October 21, 2026
- **Original Order Date:** January 18, 2019
- **Payment Deadline:** Within 15 days of receipt of notice

## Impact Assessment

This is an individual enforcement and recovery action with no broader market impact.
