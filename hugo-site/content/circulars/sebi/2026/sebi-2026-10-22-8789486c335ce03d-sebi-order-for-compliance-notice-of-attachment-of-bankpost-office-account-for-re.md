## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment for Recovery Certificate No. 7137 of 2023 against Karan Subhashchandra Ravde in the matter of India Infraspace Limited. The order mandates the attachment of bank and post office accounts and other assets for the recovery of unpaid dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 7137 of 2023.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Karan Subhashchandra Ravde.
- Company involved: India Infraspace Limited.
- Action: Attachment of bank/post office accounts and assets for recovery of dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and post offices are required to freeze accounts and assets of the defaulter as specified in the attachment notice and prevent any unauthorized withdrawals or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Order / Notice Date: October 2026

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities and individuals failing to meet financial obligations or regulatory penalties.
