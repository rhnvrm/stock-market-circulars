## Summary

SEBI has launched the Cyber Suraksha Portal as part of its Cyber Security Initiative. The platform serves as a centralized hub for the securities market to access cybersecurity circulars, vulnerability warnings, and incident insights directly.

## Key Points

- Launch of the Cyber Suraksha Portal by SEBI.
- Acts as a comprehensive, centralized hub for knowledge sharing and cybersecurity information dissemination.
- Market participants can directly access latest cybersecurity circulars, vulnerability warnings, and incident insights.

## Regulatory Changes

Establishment of a centralized portal for cybersecurity information dissemination and monitoring across securities market participants.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants should utilize the Cyber Suraksha Portal to stay updated on the latest cybersecurity circulars, vulnerability warnings, and incident insights.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular / Launch: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances cybersecurity preparedness and awareness across securities market participants by streamlining access to critical security alerts and regulatory circulars.
