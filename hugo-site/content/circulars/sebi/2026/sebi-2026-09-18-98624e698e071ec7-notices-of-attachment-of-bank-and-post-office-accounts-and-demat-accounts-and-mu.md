## Summary

SEBI has issued Notices of Attachment under Attachment Proceedings (AP) Nos. 15805 and 15806 of 2026 dated September 18, 2026, against Harsha Ishvarbhai Solanki (PAN: DCXPS3726R) and 20 others. This action is taken in connection with recovery proceedings initiated for outstanding dues related to trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- **Target Entities:** Harsha Ishvarbhai Solanki and 20 other co-noticees.
- **Matter:** Trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- **Action Taken:** Attachment of bank accounts, post office accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios.
- **Reference Numbers:** AP Nos. 15805 and 15806 of 2026, following Recovery Certificate No. 9290 of 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery action executed under the SEBI Act, 1992, and relevant regulations concerning recovery of penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, depositories, and mutual funds are required to freeze and attach the accounts and folios of the named defaulters and prohibit any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Notice:** September 18, 2026
- **Notice of Demand Date:** September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

High impact on the designated defaulters as their liquid assets, bank accounts, securities holdings, and mutual fund investments are legally attached to satisfy SEBI recovery dues.
