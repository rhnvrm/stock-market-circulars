## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7020 of 2026 filed by Shamshad Ahmed under the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005. The appeal challenged the CPIO's response regarding action taken on an appointment/transfer request for physical shares, information on a specific circular, and a request for an appointment with a Ministry of Corporate Affairs committee.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details on action taken by the MIRSD department concerning a representation dated February 11, 2026, regarding physical share transfer.
- SEBI's CPIO informed the appellant that the grievance was uploaded on the SCORES portal under registration number SEBI/MH26/MUMB/008349/1 and could be tracked online.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that queries regarding seeking specific actions or appointments do not qualify as 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
