## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a notice for the attachment of bank accounts belonging to Bhavin Natwarlal Panchal (PAN: AJMPP8429P), designated as a defaulter in the matter of Gala Global Products Limited, under Recovery Certificate No. 5697 of 2022.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter Name:** Bhavin Natwarlal Panchal
- **PAN:** AJMPP8429P
- **Matter:** Gala Global Products Limited
- **Recovery Certificate Number:** 5697 of 2022
- **Action:** Attachment of bank accounts for recovery of outstanding dues.
