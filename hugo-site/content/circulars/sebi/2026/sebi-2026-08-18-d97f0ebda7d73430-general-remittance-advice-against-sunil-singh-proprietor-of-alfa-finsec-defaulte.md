## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice against Sunil Singh, Proprietor of Alfa Finsec (PAN: BDGPS0774K), as a defaulter in the matter of trading activities in the scrip of Delta Leasing and Finance Ltd. pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 9156 of 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice issued against defaulter Sunil Singh (Proprietor of Alfa Finsec).
- PAN: BDGPS0774K.
- Related to trading activities in the scrip of Delta Leasing and Finance Ltd.
- Issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9156 of 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities holding funds or assets related to the defaulter must comply with the directions specified in the recovery certificate and remittance advice.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issuance: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Targeted recovery action against a specific defaulter with no broader market-wide structural impact.
