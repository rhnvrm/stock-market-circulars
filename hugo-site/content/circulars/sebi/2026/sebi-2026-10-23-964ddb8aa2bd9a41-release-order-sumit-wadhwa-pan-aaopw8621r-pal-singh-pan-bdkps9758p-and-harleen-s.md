## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order regarding Sumit Wadhwa (PAN: AAOPW8621R), Pal Singh (PAN: BDKPS9758P), and Harleen Singh (PAN: ANKPS5827L) under Recovery Certificate No. 7867 of 2024. The order pertains to proceedings in the matter of unregistered investment advisers, releasing previous attachment notices.

## Key Points

- Noticees / Defaulters: Sumit Wadhwa, Pal Singh, and Harleen Singh.
- Recovery Certificate: No. 7867 of 2024.
- Matter: Unregistered Investment Adviser proceedings.
- Action: Release/withdrawal of attachment orders against accounts/assets.

## Regulatory Changes

Lifting of enforcement and recovery attachment measures pursuant to settlement or compliance with SEBI recovery directives.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are notified to unfreeze accounts or assets previously attached under the recovery certificate for the specified individuals.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 2026 (Ref: oct-2026/1792759502701_1.pdf)

## Impact Assessment

Administrative release of recovery attachments for individuals involved in unregistered investment advisory matters, with no direct impact on listed company stocks.
