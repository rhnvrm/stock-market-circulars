## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7081 of 2026 filed by Smita Shreenath Bhivandkar under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI. The Appellate Authority dismissed the appeal as time-barred since it was filed beyond the statutory 30-day period without any condonation request or valid explanation. On merits, the Appellate Authority noted that queries regarding direct investment AUM corpus under SEBI circular dated September 13, 2012, renaming of schemes, AMFI's regulatory status, and SEBI SCORES complaints were vague, non-specific, and did not qualify as 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The first appeal was filed on September 09, 2026, against the CPIO's response dated July 28, 2026, rendering it time-barred under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act.
- The appellant failed to make a request for condoning the delay or provide valid reasons for the delay.
- On merits, queries concerning direct investment AUM corpus, expense loads for direct plans, renaming of schemes as regular, AMFI's compliance status, and lodging complaints against AMFI on SCORES were found vague and not specific.
- The CPIO's response stating that the queries did not constitute 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act was upheld.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a quasi-judicial appellate order on an RTI application rather than a new regulatory circular or policy update.

## Compliance Requirements
