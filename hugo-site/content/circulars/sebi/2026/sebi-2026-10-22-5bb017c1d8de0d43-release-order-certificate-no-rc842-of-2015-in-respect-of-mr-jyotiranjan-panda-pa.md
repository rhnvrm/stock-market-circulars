## Summary

SEBI has issued a release order (Release Order - Certificate No. RC842 of 2015) dated October 22, 2026, lifting attachment and certificate recovery proceedings against Mr. Jyotiranjan Panda (PAN: BEAPP0928K) in the matter of Magnox Infraprojects Limited, following the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) decision setting aside the earlier SEBI order qua the appellant.

## Key Points

- Certificate of Recovery No. RC842 of 2015 initiated against Mr. Jyotiranjan Panda has been released and withdrawn.
- Action taken in accordance with the SAT decision setting aside the original SEBI recovery/adjudication order against Mr. Jyotiranjan Panda.
- Lifts all attachment proceedings on bank accounts, salary, or other assets of Mr. Jyotiranjan Panda related to Magnox Infraprojects Limited.
