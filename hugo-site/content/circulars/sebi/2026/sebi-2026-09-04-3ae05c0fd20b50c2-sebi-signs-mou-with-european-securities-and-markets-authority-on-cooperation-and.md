## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) and the European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) concerning cooperation and exchange of information in relation to Central Counterparties (CCPs) regulated and supervised by SEBI. This MoU replaces an earlier MoU entered into on June 21, 2017.

## Key Points

- Replaces the previous MoU signed between SEBI and ESMA on June 21, 2017.
- Enables cooperation regarding Central Counterparties (CCPs) in line with respective laws and regulations.
- Establishes a framework for ESMA to place reliance on SEBI's regulatory and supervisory activities while safeguarding the European Union’s financial stability.
- Demonstrates the importance of cross-border cooperation to facilitate international clearing activities.
- Signed by SEBI Chairman Mr. Tuhin Kanta Pandey and ESMA Chair Ms. Verena Ross.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates the cross-border supervisory cooperation framework between SEBI and ESMA for Central Counterparties.

## Compliance Requirements

- No direct operational compliance requirements specified for market participants; applicable to regulatory bodies and CCP oversight.

## Important Dates

- Signed on September 04, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances regulatory trust and cross-border oversight between Indian and European regulatory authorities for international clearing activities.
