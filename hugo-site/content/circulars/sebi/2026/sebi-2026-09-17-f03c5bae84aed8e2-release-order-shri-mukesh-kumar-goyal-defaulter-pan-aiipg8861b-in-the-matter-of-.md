## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order concerning Shri Mukesh Kumar Goyal (PAN: AIIPG8861B), categorized as a defaulter, under Recovery Certificate No. 3780 of 2021 in the matter of Global Depository Receipt (GDR) Issues of IKF Technologies Limited. This order signifies the formal closure or settlement of recovery proceedings initiated against the noticee.

## Key Points

- Noticee / Defaulter: Shri Mukesh Kumar Goyal (PAN: AIIPG8861B).
- Matter: GDR Issues of IKF Technologies Limited.
- Recovery Certificate: No. 3780 of 2021.
- Action: Release order issued by SEBI concluding the recovery proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal release from recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 following the resolution or compliance of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Notices and financial institutions are updated regarding the release of attachment and closure of recovery proceedings against the specified defaulter.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Indicates successful closure/settlement of dues related to past enforcement actions involving IKF Technologies Limited's GDR issue irregularities.
