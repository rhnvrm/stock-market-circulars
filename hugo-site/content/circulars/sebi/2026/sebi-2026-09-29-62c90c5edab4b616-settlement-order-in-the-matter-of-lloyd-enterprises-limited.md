## Summary

SEBI issued a settlement order disposing of suo-motu settlement applications filed by Lloyds Enterprises Limited, Rajesh Rajnarayan Gupta, and Viresh Shankar Sohoni. The proceedings originated from an investigation initiated after BSE Limited submitted an alert report on November 21, 2023, highlighting irregularities in advances reported by the company.

## Key Points

- **Applicants:** Lloyds Enterprises Limited, Rajesh Rajnarayan Gupta, and Viresh Shankar Sohoni.
- **Alleged Violations:** Non-compliance with provisions of the LODR Regulations (including Regulations 4(1), 4(2)(e), 33(1)(a), 33(1)(c), 34(3), and 48) and PFUTP Regulations (Regulations 4(1), 4(2)(f), 4(2)(k), and 4(2)(r)), along with Section 27 of the SEBI Act.
- **Origin of Proceedings:** BSE Limited alert report dated November 21, 2023, regarding advance reporting irregularities during FY 2016-17 to FY 2020-21.
- **Settlement Basis:** Applications filed under the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018, settling the matter without admitting or denying the findings of fact and conclusions of law.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory frameworks introduced; settlement executed under the existing SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and key managerial personnel must ensure strict adherence to disclosure, listing obligations, and fair trade practice standards, specifically concerning proper accounting and reporting of financial advances.

## Important Dates

- **Alert Date:** November 21, 2023
- **Order Date:** September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Concludes pending enforcement actions against the company and associated individuals upon completion of settlement terms, mitigating regulatory and legal uncertainty.
