## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 6988 of 2026 filed by Neeraj Sisodia under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against SEBI's CPIO response regarding information sought on notices, communications, orders, non-compliances, and penalties imposed on Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) under SEBI LODR Regulations for FY 2022-23, 2023-24, and 2024-25.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought detailed information regarding SEBI, NSE, and BSE communications, non-compliance notices under SEBI LODR Regulations (such as Regulations 17(1), 18, 19, 20, 60(2)), and fines/penalties imposed on HPCL.
- Requests included specifics on waiver applications, payment status of penalties, and board/committee reviews regarding HPCL's corporate governance.
- The appeal challenges the initial response provided by the CPIO under the RTI Act framework.
