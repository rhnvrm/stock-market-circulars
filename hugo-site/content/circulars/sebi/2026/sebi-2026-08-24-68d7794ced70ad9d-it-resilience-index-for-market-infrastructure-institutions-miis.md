## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular introducing the IT Resilience Index (ITRI) framework for Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs), including stock exchanges, clearing corporations, and depositories. The framework aims to measure the robustness, availability, security, and overall performance of critical IT systems through a standardized, system-driven computation model.

## Key Points

- **Scope:** Applies to all Stock Exchanges, Clearing Corporations, and Depositories (except AMC Repo Clearing Ltd.), covering Critical Systems and related feeder systems.
- **Parameters & Weightages:** The ITRI computation includes 9 key parameters:
  - Availability: 20
  - Security: 20
  - Integrity: 10
  - Governance: 10
  - Reliability and Monitoring: 10
  - Business Continuity: 10
  - Modularity and Flexibility: 10
  - Scalability: 5
  - Others (e.g., Incident handling): 5
  - **Total:** 100
- **Automation:** ITRI computation must be system-driven, automatic, non-discretionary, and foolproof without manual intervention unless exceptions are discussed with the Standing Committee on Technology (SCOT).
- **Reporting:** MIIs must compute ITRI half-yearly, submit comparative analyses of two consecutive half-years along with corrective actions to their SCOT and Governing Board within 60 days of the half-year end.

## Regulatory Changes

- Mandates the creation and adoption of the IT Resilience Index (ITRI) for all MIIs following consultations with SEBI’s Technical Advisory Committee (TAC).
- Requires the Industry Standards Forum (ISF) of MIIs to finalize sub-parameters and detailed measurement criteria by November 30, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- MIIs must establish automated, system-driven data extraction and ITRI computation mechanisms.
- Compliance with half-yearly calculation timelines (within 60 days from the end of each half-year) and submission of comparative analysis and action plans to SCOT and the Governing Board.
- Prior discussion of manual data retrieval exceptions with SCOT if automated computation is not feasible.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 24, 2026
- **ISF Sub-parameters Finalization Deadline:** November 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances governance, operational transparency, and systemic stability across Indian market infrastructure by enforcing standardized, automated tracking of IT system resilience and proactive remediation of emerging vulnerabilities.
