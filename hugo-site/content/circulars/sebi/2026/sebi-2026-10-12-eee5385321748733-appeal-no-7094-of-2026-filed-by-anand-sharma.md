## Summary

Anand Sharma filed an RTI appeal against SEBI regarding the refund process for PACL investment made by his father, Ashok Sharma. The appellant sought detailed calculations, rules, and documents concerning the determined eligible refund amount of Rs 47,399 against claimed investment receipts of Rs 65,600. SEBI's CPIO and the Appellate Authority noted that the requested information is not available with SEBI, as the disposal of properties and repayment to investors is entrusted to the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought breakup, calculation methodology, and rules applied for determining the PACL refund amount of Rs 47,399.
- SEBI respondent and Appellate Authority clarified that the information is not held by SEBI.
- Communication regarding PACL refund status and processing is exclusively handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The appeal was disposed of without finding any deficiency in the CPIO's response, citing precedents that CPIO cannot provide information not in possession.
