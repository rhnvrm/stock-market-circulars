## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order regarding Sanjay S. Achharya HUF for executing non-genuine reversal trades in the illiquid stock options segment on BSE during the period from April 1, 2014, to September 30, 2015, which allegedly created artificial volume and misled the market.

## Key Points

- SEBI observed large-scale reversal of trades in illiquid stock options on BSE, leading to artificial volume.
- The Noticee (Sanjay S. Achharya HUF) was among the entities identified for executing non-genuine reversal trades during the investigation period.
- Proceedings were initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, for alleged violation of PFUTP Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of prohibitions against fraudulent and unfair trade practices relating to illiquid stock options.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must avoid non-genuine trades, reversal transactions, and creation of artificial volumes in derivative segments.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: April 1, 2014 to September 30, 2015.
- Show Cause Notice Date: November 16, 2021.
- Appointment of Adjudicating Officer: June 01, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on historical misuse of stock options segments and penalizes manipulative trading behavior.
