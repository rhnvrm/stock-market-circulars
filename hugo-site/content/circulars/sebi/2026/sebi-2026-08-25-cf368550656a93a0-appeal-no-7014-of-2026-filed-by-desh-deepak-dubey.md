## Summary

The appellant, Desh Deepak Dubey, filed an RTI appeal against the CPIO, SEBI, seeking information regarding property restriction/prohibition and auction list removal concerning property in Dehradun related to the PACL matter. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the requested information is not available with SEBI and noted that disposal of properties and repayment to investors is handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought details regarding Khasra number 137 kha, Mauja Aamwala tarla, Pargana Parwadoon, Tehsil Dehradun, Uttarakhand, concerning registry prohibitions and PACL auction lists.
- CPIO stated that the information sought is not available with SEBI.
- SEBI clarified that all communication regarding refund status and property disposal is handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The appeal was disposed of as lacking merit since SEBI cannot invent or provide information not in its possession.
