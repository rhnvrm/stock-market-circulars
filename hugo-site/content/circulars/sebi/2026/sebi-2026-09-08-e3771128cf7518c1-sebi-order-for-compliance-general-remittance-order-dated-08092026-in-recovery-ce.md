## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 08, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 8484 of 2024 against the defaulter Narendrabhai Amin (PAN: AMPGPA3864Q) in the matter of M/s Austral Coke and Projects Limited- II, pursuant to statutory recovery powers for enforcing compliance and realizing dues.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter:** Narendrabhai Amin (PAN: AMPGPA3864Q)
- **Matter:** M/s Austral Coke and Projects Limited- II
- **Proceeding Type:** Recovery Certificate No. 8484 of 2024
- **Order Type:** General Remittance Order dated 08.09.2026

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and recovery proceedings executed under SEBI statutory provisions for realization of outstanding dues from the named defaulter.

## Compliance Requirements

Third parties, garnishees, or holding institutions maintaining accounts, funds, or properties belonging to the defaulter are required to comply with the remittance directions as specified in the recovery certificate and general remittance order.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures enforcement of regulatory penalties and dues recovery, safeguarding market integrity and investor protection framework.
