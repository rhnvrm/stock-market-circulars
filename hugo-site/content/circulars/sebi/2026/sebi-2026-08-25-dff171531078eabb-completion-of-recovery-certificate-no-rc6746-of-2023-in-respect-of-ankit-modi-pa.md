## Summary

SEBI has issued a communication regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. RC6746 of 2023 in respect of Ankit Modi (PAN: AENPM9852P) in the matter of Illiquid Stock Options.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: RC6746 of 2023
- Noticee / Defaulter: Ankit Modi (PAN: AENPM9852P)
- Matter: Illiquid Stock Options
- Regulatory Body: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings concluded pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

No further compliance requirements for market participants as the recovery certificate proceedings in respect of the named individual have reached completion.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular / Notice: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Administrative update regarding the resolution and completion of enforcement recovery proceedings against an individual in the illiquid stock options case.
