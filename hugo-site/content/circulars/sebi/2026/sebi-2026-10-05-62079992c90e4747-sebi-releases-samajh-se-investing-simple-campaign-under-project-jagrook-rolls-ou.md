## Summary

SEBI has released the 'Samajh Se Investing Simple' campaign under Project Jagrook at Panjab University, Chandigarh on October 5, 2026, marking World Investor Week (WIW) 2026. Additionally, SEBI rolled out the Mentoring of University/College Students by Trainers (MUST) initiative and announced its official presence on WhatsApp to enhance financial literacy and investor awareness.

## Key Points

- **Samajh Se Investing Simple Campaign**: Launched during the 10th edition of World Investor Week (October 5-11, 2026) in collaboration with Securities Market Partners (SMPs) including NISM, stock exchanges (BSE, NSE, MCX), depositories (CDSL, NSDL), and AMFI.
- **MUST Initiative**: Mentoring of University/College Students by Trainers (MUST) launched to train and certify faculty, staff, or senior students as Partner Trainers (PTs) in partnership with the Association of Indian Universities (AIU).
- **Official WhatsApp Presence**: SEBI established an official WhatsApp channel (accessible via 1800 22 7575 with a verified blue tick) to share investor awareness information.
- **Multi-channel Approach**: Integrates physical and digital pathways to promote financial literacy, informed investment practices, and fraud prevention.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory or compliance rule changes for listed entities; the circular details investor education and awareness initiatives.

## Compliance Requirements

None applicable to listed companies or market intermediaries.

## Important Dates

- **World Investor Week 2026**: October 05 to 11, 2026
- **Campaign Launch Date**: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Positive long-term impact on retail investor awareness, financial literacy, and fraud prevention through structured university programs and accessible digital channels like WhatsApp.
