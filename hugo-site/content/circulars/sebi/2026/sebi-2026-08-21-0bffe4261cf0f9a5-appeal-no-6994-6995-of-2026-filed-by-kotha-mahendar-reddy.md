## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal Nos. 6994 & 6995 of 2026 filed by Kotha Mahendar Reddy under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant had sought detailed information regarding the registration requirements, educational qualifications, work experience, NISM examinations, fees, net worth, and checklists for Individual Investment Advisers under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought 14 specific points of information regarding the registration of Individual Investment Advisers.
- The CPIO directed the appellant to Regulation 7 of SEBI (IA) Regulations, 2013 and the Master Circular for Investment Advisers dated February 06, 2026.
- Specific details regarding mandatory NISM examinations and links/paths for relevant information were provided by the respondent.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes or policy amendments were introduced through this appellate order.

## Compliance Requirements

- Applicants seeking registration as Individual Investment Advisers must refer to the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 and the relevant Master Circulars for qualification, examination, and fee details.

## Important Dates

- Date of Application: July 09, 2026
- Date of CPIO Response: July 20, 2026
- Date of Appeal: July 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No direct market or operational impact as this is a standard RTI statutory appeal disposal.
