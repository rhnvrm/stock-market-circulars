## Summary

This document is an Appellate Authority order under the Right to Information Act, 2005 regarding Appeal No. 6997 of 2026 filed by Rajeev Bakshi against SEBI. The appellant sought information regarding the official assigned to inquire into a complaint against Binny Ltd., copies of the inquiry file, and the current status of the inquiry.

## Key Points

- Appellant requested personal details of the official assigned to inquire into a complaint against Binny Ltd. and its directors.
- Appellant also sought copies of the complete inquiry file and current status of the inquiry.
- The First Appellate Authority (FAA) noted that complaint handling in SEBI is an institutional and collective process, and identities of officers involved constitute personal information.
