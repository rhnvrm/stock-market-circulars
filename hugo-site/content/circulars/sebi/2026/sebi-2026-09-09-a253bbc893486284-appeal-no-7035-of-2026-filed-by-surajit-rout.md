## Summary

The Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7035 of 2026 filed by Surajit Rout under the RTI Act, 2005, against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant had sought certified copies of the Action Taken Report, scrutiny reports, and final decisions regarding Complaint No. SEBIE/WB26/EAST/017375/1 concerning his holding in Uttam Value Steels Ltd. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response directing the appellant to access the SCORES portal for the Action Taken Report and noted that correctness of information provided under the RTI Act cannot be adjudicated in this appellate mechanism.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought detailed records, Action Taken Reports, and scrutiny notes regarding a complaint filed on SCORES pertaining to 4,230 shares of Uttam Value Steels Ltd. (ISIN: INE292A01023).
- SEBI's CPIO provided the Action Taken Report and directed the appellant to the SCORES portal and relevant FAQs.
- The Appellate Authority found the response adequate, noting that information regarding complaint status and ATR is accessible via SCORES.
- The appeal challenging the correctness of the information was dismissed referencing Delhi High Court precedent stating disputes on information correctness fall outside RTI Act grievance redressal.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a quasi-judicial appellate order under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements
