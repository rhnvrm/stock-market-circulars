## Summary

SEBI has issued the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026, introducing Chapter III-G which outlines the 'Common Advertisement Code' (सामान्य विज्ञापन संहिता) for all regulated intermediaries and entities within the regulatory purview.

## Key Points

- Introduces Chapter III-G governing advertisements by SEBI-regulated entities.
- Broadly defines 'Advertisement' to include any promotional communication across print, broadcast, digital, and outdoor media.
- Defines 'Celebrity' criteria, including rankings in public celebrity indexes, prominent roles in mainstream entertainment media, social media follower thresholds set by ASCI, national sports representatives, and TV program hosts/anchors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Insertion of Chapter III-G (Common Advertisement Code) into the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.
- Detailed definitions for advertising scope and celebrity endorsements across various media platforms.

## Compliance Requirements

- SEBI-regulated intermediaries must ensure all promotional content and advertising campaigns strictly adhere to the new Common Advertisement Code provisions.

## Important Dates

- Effective from the one hundred and eightieth day from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette (October 8/9, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Standardizes advertising practices across all SEBI-regulated intermediaries, ensuring greater transparency, accountability, and compliance in marketing and influencer/celebrity endorsements.
