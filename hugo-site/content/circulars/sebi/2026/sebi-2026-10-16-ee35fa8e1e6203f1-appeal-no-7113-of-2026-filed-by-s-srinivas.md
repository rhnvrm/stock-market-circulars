## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7113 of 2026 filed by S Srinivas under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought extensive information regarding Sahara India group court proceedings, case papers, arrest orders, and various regulatory enactments. SEBI directed the appellant to public domain resources and the RBI Sachet Portal.

## Key Points

- Appellant requested detailed case papers, counters, affidavits, and judgments regarding Sahara India vs. SEBI and other regulatory bodies.
- Queries included requests for findings, recommendations, implementation actions, and arrest/death certificate copies related to Sahara group leadership.
- Respondent (CPIO, SEBI) informed that orders concerning Sahara India Commercial Corporation Ltd. (SICCL), Sahara India Real Estate Corporation Ltd. (SIRECL), and Sahara Housing Investment Corporation Ltd. (SHICL) are available on the SEBI website and RBI Sachet Portal.
