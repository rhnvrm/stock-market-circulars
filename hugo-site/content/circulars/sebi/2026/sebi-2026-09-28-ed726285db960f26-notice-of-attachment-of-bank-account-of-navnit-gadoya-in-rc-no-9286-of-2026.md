## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a notice of attachment of bank accounts against Navnit Gadoya under Recovery Proceeding No. 15815 of 2026 (Certificate No. 9286 of 2026) for the recovery of Rs. 5,26,000/-.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9286 of 2026 was drawn up on August 19, 2026, for total dues of Rs. 5,26,000/-.
- Dues comprise a penalty of Rs. 5,00,000/- imposed for front running, interest of Rs. 25,000/-, and recovery costs of Rs. 1,000/-.
- All banks in India are ordered to attach all accounts, lockers, and proceeds held singly or jointly by Navnit Gadoya (PAN: AQNPG4727F).
- No debit shall be made in the defaulter's account/s up to the total dues amount until further orders.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an enforcement and recovery action issued under section 28A(1) and 11(2)(ia) of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

- Banks must immediately attach all accounts and lockers held by the defaulter.
- Banks must prohibit debits up to Rs. 5,26,000/- while allowing credits.
- Banks must provide account details, one-year account statements, confirmation of attachment, and details of loan/advances accounts to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate: August 19, 2026
- Date of Notice of Attachment: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricted to the specific individual defaulter; no broader market or systemic impact.
