## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has officially announced the completion of Recovery Certificate No. RC 722 of 2015 issued against Softrak Venture Investment Ltd. This marks the successful conclusion of recovery proceedings initiated to collect outstanding dues.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: RC 722 of 2015
- Entity Involved: Softrak Venture Investment Ltd.
- Action Taken: Completion of recovery proceedings and issuance of release orders for attached assets.

## Regulatory Changes

[None - Enforcement and recovery action completion]

## Compliance Requirements

Entities under recovery proceedings must satisfy all outstanding dues as mandated by SEBI to achieve formal closure and release of attached properties or accounts.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issuance/Notice: September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides resolution to long-pending regulatory dues and lifts restrictions on the entity's assets associated with this specific recovery certificate.
