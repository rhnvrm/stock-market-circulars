## Summary

SEBI conducted a thematic inspection of stock broker Rashi Equisearch Private Limited between April 1, 2024, and November 27, 2025. The inspection revealed violations including failure to maintain evidence of clients' pre-order confirmations or authorizations and failure to capture Internet Protocol (IP) addresses for Internet Based Trading (IBT) and STWT orders.

## Key Points

- Thematic inspection conducted by SEBI and NSE on November 27, 2025, covering the period from April 1, 2024, to November 27, 2025.
- Noticee failed to maintain evidence of pre-order confirmations or authorizations for 38 out of 50 sample clients.
- Noticee failed to capture IP addresses for Internet Based Trading (IBT) and Securities Trading using Wireless Technology (STWT) orders.
- Adjudication proceedings initiated under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of regulatory compliance under SEBI Circulars, Master Circulars, and Stock Brokers Regulations regarding order placement evidence and audit trails for digital trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock brokers must strictly maintain documented evidence of clients' pre-order confirmations and authorizations.
- Proper systems must be maintained to capture and store Internet Protocol (IP) addresses for all Internet Based Trading (IBT) and wireless trading (STWT) transactions.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2024 to November 27, 2025
- Inspection Date: November 27, 2025
- Appointment of AO: July 23, 2026
- Show Cause Notice Date: August 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights regulatory scrutiny on stock brokers regarding record-keeping of client authorizations and electronic trading audit trails, reinforcing operational compliance standards across the brokerage industry.
