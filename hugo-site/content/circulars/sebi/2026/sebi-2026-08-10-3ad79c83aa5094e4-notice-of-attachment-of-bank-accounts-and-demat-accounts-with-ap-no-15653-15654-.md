## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice of attachment of bank accounts and demat accounts with AP No. 15653 & 15654 of 2026 dated August 10, 2026 against Lataben Narotambhai Rangi [Defaulter] in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited, under Recovery Certificate No. 9227 of 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice of attachment issued against defaulter Lataben Narotambhai Rangi.
- Involves AP No. 15653 & 15654 of 2026 dated August 10, 2026.
- Relates to trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9227 of 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to SEBI Recovery Certificate provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and depositories are required to attach the specified bank and demat accounts of the defaulter.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct impact on the defaulter's assets through attachment of bank and demat accounts to recover dues.
