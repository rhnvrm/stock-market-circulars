## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment of Demat Accounts (AP No. 15755 of 2026) dated August 21, 2026, against Mr. Kapil Wadhawan (PAN: AAOPW6145L) under Recovery Certificate No. 9277 of 2026 in the matter of Dewan Housing Finance Corporation Ltd (DHFL).

## Key Points

- Issuance of Notice of Attachment of Demat Accounts against the defaulter Mr. Kapil Wadhawan.
- Action taken pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 9277 of 2026.
- Relates to outstanding dues and penalties imposed by SEBI in the DHFL matter.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action executed under SEBI's statutory recovery powers for non-payment of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Depository participants and relevant financial intermediaries are required to freeze and attach the demat accounts specified in the order.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures enforcement of regulatory penalties and recovery of dues, restricting the defaulter from operating or transferring securities held in demat accounts.
