## Summary

SEBI has issued a completion order for Recovery Certificate No. 5722 of 2022 against Jayesh Vallabhaji Madiyar in the matter of Excel Castronics Limited, marking the successful closure of recovery proceedings due to the fulfillment/satisfaction of dues.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 5722 of 2022 was originally issued against Jayesh Vallabhaji Madiyar.
- The proceedings relate to the matter of Excel Castronics Limited.
- SEBI has ordered the completion and withdrawal of recovery measures consequent to the settlement or recovery of the specified amounts.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative enforcement closure order under SEBI recovery proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

No further compliance action required under this specific recovery certificate as dues are satisfied.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact as this is an individual recovery completion proceeding.
