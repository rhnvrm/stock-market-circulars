## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated August 14, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 9172 of 2026 against Dhaval Girishbhai Parmar (PAN: EPZPP2397F). This action is part of ongoing recovery proceedings related to illegal gains and penalties imposed in the matter of coordinated trading activities and price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery proceedings initiated via Recovery Certificate No. 9172 of 2026.
- Issued against defaulter Dhaval Girishbhai Parmar (PAN : EPZPP2397F).
- Arises from enforcement actions concerning the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited.
- Directs attachment and recovery measures to recover disgorged amounts and penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed under SEBI Act, 1992 recovery mechanisms for non-payment of disgorgement and monetary penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulters and associated financial institutions must comply with attachment orders, freezing of bank accounts, and remittance of funds as directed by the Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight and strict enforcement against market manipulators, ensuring deterrence and recovery of unlawful gains in SME scrips.
