## Summary

SEBI has issued an ex-parte interim order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11B(1), and 11D of the SEBI Act, 1992, against Prrsaar Sampada Private Limited, Chaubara Eats Private Limited, and other connected noticees. The proceedings stem from abnormal trading patterns and cross-segment price manipulation involving derivative contracts of Kfin Technologies Ltd. (KFINTECH) and Swiggy Ltd. (SWIGGY) along with cash segment activities.

## Key Points

- Investigation revealed coordinated and synchronized trading among related entities across cash and derivatives segments.
- Noticees engaged in deceptive order placement and price manipulation to generate wrongful gains.
- Specific instances include trading in derivative contracts of Kfin Technologies Ltd. on December 03, 2025, and Swiggy Ltd. on June 16, 2026.
- Prrsaar Sampada Private Limited is registered with SEBI as a stock broker, Depository Participant, and Research Analyst.

## Compliance Requirements

Noticees and connected entities must comply with the directions issued in the ex-parte interim order, including restraint from buying, selling, or dealing in securities either directly or indirectly.
