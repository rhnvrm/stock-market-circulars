## Summary

SEBI issued a Recovery Certificate (No. 9286 of 2026) and Notice of Demand under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, and Section 222 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, to Navnit Gadoya for a total due amount of Rs. 5,26,000/- including penalty and interest.

## Key Points

- Notice issued to Navnit Gadoya (PAN: AQNPG4727F).
- Total amount due: Rs. 5,26,000/- (comprising Rs. 5,00,000 penalty, Rs. 25,000 interest, and Rs. 1,000 recovery cost).
- Originates from AO Order No. Order/AK/DS/2026-27/32398-32400 dated 29.04.2026 in the matter of Front Running by Navnit Gadoya, Chiranggi Irish Shah, and Surbhi Aggarwal.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

- Payment of the total sum within 15 days of receipt of the notice via direct credit/EFT/RTGS or online payment via the SEBI SI Portal.
- Prohibition on transferring or dealing with properties without prior permission of the Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: 29.04.2026
- Notice Date: 19.08.2026
- Payment Window: Within 15 days of receipt of the notice

## Impact Assessment

Individual enforcement action with no systemic market impact.
