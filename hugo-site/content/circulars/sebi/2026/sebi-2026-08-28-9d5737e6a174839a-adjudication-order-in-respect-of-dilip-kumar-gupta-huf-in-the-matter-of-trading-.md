## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order against Dilip Kumar Gupta HUF for indulging in non-genuine reversal trades in the stock options segment of the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) during the period between April 1, 2014, and September 30, 2015. The trades created artificial volumes and a misleading appearance of trading in illiquid stock options, violating Regulations 3(a), (b), (c), (d), 4(1) and 4(2)(a) of the SEBI (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices) Regulations, 2003.

## Key Points

- SEBI observed large-scale reversal of trades in stock options at BSE, comprising 81.40% of all trades during the investigation period.
- Dilip Kumar Gupta HUF was identified as one of the entities executing non-genuine reversal trades to create artificial volume.
- Proceedings were initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Rule 4 of the Adjudication Rules.
- SCN was issued to the Noticee under Rule 4(1) of the Adjudication Rules to answer charges of violating PFUTP Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants must avoid executing non-genuine transactions, reversal trades, or creating artificial volumes in stock options or any other securities segment.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: April 1, 2014 – September 30, 2015
- SCN Date: March 31, 2022
- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory scrutiny against market manipulation and misuse of the stock options segment through illiquid scrips.
