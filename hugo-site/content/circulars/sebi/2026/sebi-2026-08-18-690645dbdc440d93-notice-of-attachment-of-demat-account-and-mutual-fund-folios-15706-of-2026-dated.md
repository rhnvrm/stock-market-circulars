## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice for the attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s) under Recovery Certificate No. 9159 of 2026 dated August 18, 2026, against Sapna, proprietor of Stallion Trading Co. (PAN: DRUPS8079D), in the matter of trading activities in the scrip of Delta Leasing and Finance Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice issued pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 9159 of 2026.
- Target entity: Sapna (Proprietor of Stallion Trading Co.), PAN: DRUPS8079D.
- Covers attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folios.
- Related to trading activities in Delta Leasing and Finance Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

None (Enforcement/Recovery action).

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant depositories and mutual fund institutions are required to restrict debits and transfers from the attached demat accounts and folios belonging to the specified entity.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricted to the specific entity under recovery proceedings; no broad market impact.
