## Summary

This order addresses Appeal No. 7044 of 2026 filed by Devender Pawar under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant sought extensive details, certified lists, and records regarding immovable properties in Delhi, Haryana, and Punjab identified, attached, or sold by the Justice R.M. Lodha Committee for the recovery of money for PACL investors.

## Key Points

- The appellant filed an RTI application dated July 17, 2026, seeking seven categories of information relating to PACL property attachments, e-auctions, sales, realization amounts, disbursements, lawful transfers prior to committee constitution, and objections/claims received.
- The respondent replied to the application on July 21, 2026.
- The Appellate Authority reviewed the application, response, and appeal to dispose of the matter based on available records.

No direct market or operational impact as this is an individual statutory appeal under the RTI Act regarding historical recovery proceedings.
