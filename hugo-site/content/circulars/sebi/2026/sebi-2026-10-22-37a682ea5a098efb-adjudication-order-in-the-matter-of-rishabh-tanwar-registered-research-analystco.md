## Summary

SECURITIES AND EXCHANGE BOARD OF INDIA issued an adjudication order against Registered Research Analyst Rishabh Tanwar (Registration No. INH100009293) following an inspection for the period from April 1, 2023, to October 31, 2024. The proceedings stem from allegations that an associated entity operated a share trading call center in violation of SEBI Research Analyst Regulations.

## Key Points

- SEBI received an inter-governmental reference alleging that Mr. Govind Mohanrao Surnar, associated with Research Analyst Rishabh Tanwar, was running a share trading call center in possible violation of SEBI RA Regulations.
- An inspection of the Noticee was conducted for the period from April 1, 2023, to October 31, 2024.
- Findings revealed breaches of Regulations 13(i) and 24(2) read with Clauses 1, 2, and 7 of the Code of Conduct specified in the Third Schedule of RA Regulations.
- Violations were also identified under Clauses 5.3 and 10.5 of Master Circular No. SEBI/HO/MIRSD-PoD-1/P/CIR/2024/49 dated May 21, 2024, along with outsourcing principles (Paras 1, 2, 4, 5, and 6 of Annexure E).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and penal action under Section 15EB of the SEBI Act, 1992, for non-compliance with existing RA Regulations, Code of Conduct, and Master Circular guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

Research analysts must maintain strict oversight over associates, ensure absolute adherence to the Code of Conduct, comply with SEBI Master Circulars, and follow prescribed principles for outsourcing intermediary activities.

## Important Dates

- **Inspection Period:** April 1, 2023 – October 31, 2024
- **Appointment of Adjudicating Officer:** July 10, 2026
- **Show Cause Notice (SCN-I):** September 4, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Underscores SEBI's regulatory vigilance over research analysts regarding unauthorized third-party call center operations, outsourcing governance, and adherence to professional codes of conduct.
