## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7059 of 2026 filed by Keshav Goyal under the RTI Act, 2005. The appellant sought a copy of the SEBI Grade A 2025 Phase II question paper and his evaluated answer sheet. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that question papers for SEBI Grade A recruitment are held solely by IBPS as per their MoU and are not shared with SEBI. Furthermore, the request for the evaluated answer sheet introduced at the appeal stage was held to be an impermissible expansion of the scope of the original RTI application.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought copies of the SEBI Grade A 2025 Phase II question paper (Paper 1 and Paper 2) for the General Stream under the RTI Act.
- SEBI's CPIO stated that recruitment exams are conducted by IBPS, and copies of question papers are not shared with SEBI or held in SEBI's records.
- Citing CIC precedent (Lakshminarayanan R vs. SEBI), the Appellate Authority confirmed that information not existent or available in public authority records cannot be provided.
- The appellant's attempt to seek his evaluated answer sheet during the appeal stage was disallowed based on CIC precedent (Harish Prasad Divedi vs. BPCL) prohibiting the expansion of RTI query scope at the appeal stage.
