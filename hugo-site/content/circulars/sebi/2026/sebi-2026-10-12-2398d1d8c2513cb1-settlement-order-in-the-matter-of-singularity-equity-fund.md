## Summary

SEBI issued a settlement order in the matter of Singularity Equity Fund following a suo-motu settlement application filed by Singularity AMC LLP. The applicant settled enforcement proceedings regarding alleged violations of regulation 10(d) of the AIF Regulations read with clause 11.1.2 of the Master Circular for Alternative Investment Funds.

## Key Points

- **Applicant:** Singularity AMC LLP (Manager of Singularity Equity Fund - I).
- **Violation:** Failure to maintain continuing interest of sponsor/manager in the scheme (minimum of 5% of corpus or 10 crore rupees, whichever is lower) during the quarters from September 2023 to June 2024.
- **Settlement Amount:** ₹9,37,500/- paid by the Applicant upon approval by the Panel of Whole Time Members.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A (Individual settlement order under SEBI Settlement Regulations, 2018).

## Compliance Requirements

Managers and sponsors of Alternative Investment Funds must ensure continuous adherence to continuing interest requirements specified under regulation 10(d) of the AIF Regulations and relevant master circulars.

## Important Dates

- **IC Meeting:** March 11, 2026

- **HPAC Meeting:** May 27, 2026
- **Panel Approval:** July 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The settlement concludes proceedings against Singularity AMC LLP on agreed financial terms without admission or denial of guilt, having no broader market impact.
