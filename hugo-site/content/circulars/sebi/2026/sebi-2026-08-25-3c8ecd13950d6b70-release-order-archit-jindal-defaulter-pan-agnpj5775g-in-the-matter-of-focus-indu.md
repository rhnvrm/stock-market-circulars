## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order concerning Archit Jindal (PAN: AGNPJ5775G), designated as a defaulter in recovery proceedings under Certificate No. 3875 of 2021 in the matter of Focus Industrial Resources Limited.

## Key Points

- Entity involved: Archit Jindal (PAN: AGNPJ5775G).
- Recovery Certificate: No. 3875 of 2021.
- Matter: Focus Industrial Resources Limited.
- Action: Issuance of a formal SEBI release order following the conclusion or satisfaction of dues related to the specific certificate.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement and recovery action release specific to the named defaulter.

## Compliance Requirements

Archit Jindal and associated financial intermediaries are released from the attachment restrictions previously imposed under the specified recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 25, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Minimal systemic impact as this is an individual enforcement release order closing recovery proceedings under a specific certificate.
