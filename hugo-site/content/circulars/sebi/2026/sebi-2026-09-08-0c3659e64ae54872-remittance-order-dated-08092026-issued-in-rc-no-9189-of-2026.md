## Summary

SEBI issued a Remittance Order dated September 08, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9189 of 2026, directing all banks and mutual funds in India to attach bank/demat accounts and mutual fund folios of the defaulter Punjiben Babubhai Rathod (PAN: CABPR8593L) and remit outstanding dues amounting to Rs. 6,55,000/-.

## Key Points

- Defaulter Name: Punjiben Babubhai Rathod (PAN: CABPR8593L).
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9189 of 2026 (Attachment Proceeding Nos. 15614 and 15615 of 2026).
- Outstanding Dues: Rs. 6,55,000/- as of the date of the order.
- Direction: Banks and Mutual Funds are directed to remit the specified amount forthwith via EFT/NEFT/RTGS to SEBI's designated bank account.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable]

## Compliance Requirements

- All banks and mutual funds must check for accounts/folios belonging to the specified defaulter.
- If held, remit funds up to the outstanding amount to SEBI's ICICI Bank account (A/c No. SEBIRRDPEN9189, IFS Code: ICIC0000106).
- Intimate remittance details via email to the designated SEBI officers along with required payment particulars.
- If no account/folio exists with the institution, no communication is required to be sent to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 08, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Date: July 02, 2026
- Notice of Demand Date: July 02, 2026
- Notice of Attachment Date: July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Applies specifically to banks and mutual funds holding accounts or folios for the named defaulter to ensure recovery of statutory dues.
