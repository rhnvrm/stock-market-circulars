## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated September 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9299 of 2026, jointly and severally to Fingravy Wealth Creation Services Private Limited and six individuals: Dhiraj Gupta, Sumit Kumar, Hemanchal Singh, Ravindra Singh, and Ashutosh Sharma.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9299 of 2026 dated September 28, 2026.
- Issued by SEBI Recovery Officer.
- Noticees / Defaulters: Fingravy Wealth Creation Services Private Limited (PAN: AADCF2427Q), Dhiraj Gupta (PAN: CMVPG2518N), Sumit Kumar (PAN: CSIPK7343G), Hemanchal Singh (PAN: FPBPS3962E), Ravindra Singh (PAN: FOIPS5131B), and Ashutosh Sharma (PAN: CBVPS2287K).
- Liability: Joint and several.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action under the SEBI Act, 1992 for recovery of dues/penalties through recovery certificates.

## Compliance Requirements

Noticees are required to pay the demanded dues immediately to avoid further coercive recovery measures such as attachment and sale of movable/immovable property, bank accounts, etc.

## Important Dates

- Date of Demand Notice / Recovery Certificate: September 28, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Regulatory enforcement action directed at specific defaulting entities/individuals without direct market-wide impact on listed securities.
