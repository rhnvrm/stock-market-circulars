## Summary

SEBI announced the winners of the Securities Market TechSprint, launched in collaboration with Market Infrastructure Institutions and industry bodies under the theme 'Innovation in Action: Shaping Securities Market for Tomorrow'. The winners were felicitated at the Global Fintech Fest 2026 on September 10, 2026.

## Key Points

- The TechSprint attracted 512 applications, leading to 116 working solutions built from 149 shortlisted prototypes.
- A panel from Market Infrastructure Institutions and industry associations shortlisted 24 entries for jury evaluation, selecting seven winning entries.
- Top winning projects include 'NIYAMA' and 'SEBI ImpactOS' for Agentic Compliance, and 'IPO SHERPA' for SME IPO Offer Document Preparation.
- Other winning recommendations include solutions for AI-driven detection of synthetic media/phishing and unified multi-asset investing apps.
