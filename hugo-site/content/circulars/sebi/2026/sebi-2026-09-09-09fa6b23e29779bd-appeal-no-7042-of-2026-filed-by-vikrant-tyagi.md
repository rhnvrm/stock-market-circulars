## Summary

Appellant Vikrant Tyagi filed an appeal under the RTI Act, 2005 against SEBI's refusal to provide a certified copy of the forensic audit report (prepared by Ravi Rajan & Co LLP) and the investigation report concerning Omaxe Limited, referenced in SEBI's Final Order dated July 30, 2024. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's decision, citing exemptions under Section 8(1)(e) and Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought copies of the forensic audit report and investigation report related to Omaxe Limited.
- SEBI's CPIO denied the request on the grounds that the information is held in a fiduciary capacity, contains commercial confidential information and personal information, and could harm the competitive position of third parties.
- The Appellate Authority noted that the appellant was not among the noticees in the underlying final order.
- Reliance was placed on previous Central Information Commission (CIC) rulings (e.g., *Mr. Ashok Kumar Rajak vs. CPIO, SEBI*, *Arun Kumar Agrawal vs. The CPIO, SEBI*, and *Deepti Maheswari vs. CPIO, SEBI*) which established that investigation and forensic audit reports involve third-party information and are exempt from disclosure under Section 8(1)(e) and (j) of the RTI Act.
