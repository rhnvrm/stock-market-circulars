## Summary

SEBI has issued an order for the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 7475 of 2023, originally dated December 14, 2023. The recovery certificate was issued against Rashesh Purohit (PAN: ADQPP1270Q) as a defaulter in the matter of TV Vision Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 7475 of 2023 dated December 14, 2023 has been cancelled.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Rashesh Purohit (PAN: ADQPP1270Q).
- Matter: TV Vision Limited.
- Issued by SEBI Recovery Proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Withdrawal and cancellation of recovery proceedings and associated enforcement measures against the specified defaulter.

## Compliance Requirements

Relief for the named defaulter as the recovery certificate and related enforcement actions under the recovery proceedings stand cancelled.

## Important Dates

- Original Recovery Certificate Date: December 14, 2023
- Cancellation / Order Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides regulatory resolution and lifting of recovery measures for the individual defaulter in relation to TV Vision Limited, with negligible systemic market impact.
