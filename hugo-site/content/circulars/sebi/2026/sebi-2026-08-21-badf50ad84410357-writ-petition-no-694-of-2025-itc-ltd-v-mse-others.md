## Summary

This document represents the judgment of the Bombay High Court in Writ Petition No. 694 of 2025 filed by ITC Limited against the Metropolitan Stock Exchange (MSE), Jupitice Justice Technology Private Ltd., and SEBI, challenging communications issued under SEBI's Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) Master Circular dated 31st July 2023.

## Key Points

- Challenge to communications dated 24th October 2024 and 26th November 2024 under SEBI's ODR mechanism.
- Petitioner (ITC Ltd.) called upon to participate in arbitral proceedings and pay arbitral fees.
- Involves interpretation and applicability of SEBI Master Circular dated 31st July 2023 streamlining dispute resolution in the Indian securities market.

## Regulatory Changes

- Re-emphasizes the implementation and operational scope of the Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) mechanism introduced via SEBI's Master Circular.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and listed companies are subject to the ODR framework administered through stock exchanges and institutional ODR providers as per SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Judgment Date: 21st August 2026
- Reserved On: 22nd July 2026
- Impugned Communications: 24th October 2024 and 26th November 2024

## Impact Assessment

- Clarifies judicial oversight and procedural aspects relating to SEBI's institutionalized Online Dispute Resolution mechanism for securities market disputes.
