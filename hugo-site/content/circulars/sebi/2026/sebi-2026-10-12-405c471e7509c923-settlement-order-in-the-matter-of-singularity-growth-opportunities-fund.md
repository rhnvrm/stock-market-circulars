## Summary

SEBI issued a settlement order dated July 02, 2026, in respect of Singularity AMC LLP regarding a suo-motu settlement application for alleged violation of regulation 10(d) of SEBI (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012 read with clause 11.1.2 of the Master Circular for AIFs. The violation pertained to non-pro-rata drawdowns made by the Manager across three schemes of Singularity Growth Opportunities Fund.

## Key Points

- Singularity AMC LLP acts as the Manager for Singularity Growth Opportunities Fund, a Category II Alternative Investment Fund.
- The application covered three schemes: Singularity Growth Opportunities Fund II, Singularity Early Opportunities Fund, and Founders Collective Fund.
- Violation of regulation 10(d) of AIF Regulations and clause 11.1.2 of the Master Circular (mandating that continuing interest of sponsor/manager be pro-rata to amount raised from other investors) was observed in Quarterly Activity Reports.
- Non-pro-rata drawdowns occurred from quarter ended June 2024 to June 2025 for two schemes, and from quarter ended June 2024 to September 2024 for the Founders Collective Fund.
- The settlement amount was determined at ₹31,12,500/- based on recommendations by the Internal Committee and HPAC, and approved by the Panel of Whole Time Members on July 02, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Strict enforcement of pro-rata drawdown requirements for sponsor/manager continuing interest in AIF schemes under regulation 10(d) of AIF Regulations and Master Circular clause 11.1.2.

## Compliance Requirements

- Alternative Investment Fund managers must ensure that all capital drawdowns and continuing interest obligations are executed strictly on a pro-rata basis alongside other investors across all fund schemes.

## Important Dates

- March 11, 2026: Meeting between SEBI Internal Committee and Applicant.
- March 24, 2026: Submission of revised settlement terms by Applicant.
- May 27, 2026: HPAC recommendation meeting.
- July 02, 2026: Approval of settlement by Panel of Whole Time Members.

## Impact Assessment

- Emphasizes regulatory focus on compliance with capital contribution and drawdown norms for AIF sponsors and managers, reinforcing equitable treatment of fund investors.
