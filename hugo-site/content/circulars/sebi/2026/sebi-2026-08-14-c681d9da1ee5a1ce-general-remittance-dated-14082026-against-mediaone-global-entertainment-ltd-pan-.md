## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance dated August 14, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9137 of 2026 against Mediaone Global Entertainment Ltd. (PAN: AAACR0405M), designating the company as a defaulter in ongoing recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9137 of 2026 invoked against Mediaone Global Entertainment Ltd.
- General remittance notice issued to enforce recovery of outstanding dues and penalties.
- Proceedings stem from previous regulatory findings regarding financial irregularities and fund diversion.

## Regulatory Changes

[None - Enforcement Action]

## Compliance Requirements

Entities holding funds or assets belonging to the defaulter are subject to remittance obligations under the recovery certificate as per SEBI regulations.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Strict enforcement of recovery certificate underscores SEBI's crackdown on non-compliance, financial defaults, and unauthorized fund diversions by listed entities.
