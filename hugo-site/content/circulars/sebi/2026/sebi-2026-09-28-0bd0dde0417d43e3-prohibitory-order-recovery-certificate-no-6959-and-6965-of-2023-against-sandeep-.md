## Summary

SEBI has issued a prohibitory order under Recovery Certificate Nos. 6959 and 6965 of 2023 against Sandeep Tulsiram Agrawal in the matter of Kushal Limited. This action is part of enforcement proceedings initiated to recover outstanding dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Order issued under Recovery Certificate Nos. 6959 and 6965 of 2023.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Sandeep Tulsiram Agrawal.
- Company Matter: Kushal Limited.
- Directed by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of monetary penalties and disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to comply with the prohibitory order by freezing relevant accounts, lockers, and holdings of the noticee to prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Notice: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict regulatory enforcement and asset attachment mechanisms utilized by SEBI for recovery of dues in corporate non-compliance and insider trading matters.
