## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9302 of 2026 drawn against Arvind Shukla (PAN: ISZPS7481G). This action pertains to the recovery of unpaid monetary penalties imposed for market manipulation and dissemination of misleading recommendations using social media platforms in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice of Demand / Recovery Certificate issued under RC No. 9302 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Arvind Shukla (PAN: ISZPS7481G).
- Matter: Market manipulation and unverified stock recommendations via Telegram channels in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues resulting from adjudication orders against entities engaging in unfair trade practices.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and asset holders are required to comply with recovery notices, freeze specified accounts, and prevent unauthorized transfer of assets belonging to the defaulter as mandated by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Recovery Certificate: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects SEBI's stringent regulatory enforcement and proactive recovery mechanism targeting social media operators and individuals involved in pump-and-dump schemes in listed scrips.
