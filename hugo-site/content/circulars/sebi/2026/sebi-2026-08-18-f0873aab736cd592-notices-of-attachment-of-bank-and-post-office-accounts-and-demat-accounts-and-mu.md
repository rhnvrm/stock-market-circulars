## Summary

SEBI has issued notices of attachment under Recovery Proceedings (RC No. 9213 of 2026) against Manoj Rameshbhai Solanki (PAN: GEMPS6325N) covering bank accounts, post office accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios pursuant to AP Nos. 15693 and 15694 of 2026 dated August 18, 2026, relating to trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Defaulter: Manoj Rameshbhai Solanki (PAN: GEMPS6325N).
- Matter: Trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Recovery Proceeding No.: RC No. 9213 of 2026.
- Action: Attachment of bank accounts, post office accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios.
- Date of order: August 18, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an enforcement and recovery action against a specific defaulter.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, postal authorities, depositories, and mutual fund houses are required to freeze and attach the accounts/folios of the named defaulter as per SEBI's recovery directions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct regulatory enforcement action targeted at an individual defaulter with no broader systemic market impact.
