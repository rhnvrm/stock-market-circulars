## Summary

SEBI has initiated summary proceedings under Regulation 30A of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 read with Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 against seven Investment Advisers for failing to pay the mandatory renewal fees as prescribed under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

## Key Points

- Noticees include Capitalpi Advisors and Research, Neha Gupta Prop. Trade India Research, Quant Commodities Private Limited, Shabnam Khan Proprietor M/s Only Profit India, CAA Kwan Talent Management Agency Private Limited, Rahul Ray, and M/s. Vasisth Capital Private Limited.
- Registrations date back across various years from 2013 to 2016, with renewal fees remaining unpaid from their respective due dates.
- Notices were issued between February 2025 and August 2026 giving the Noticees 21 days to submit replies with documentary evidence.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of Clause 3 of the Second Schedule of SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 requiring renewal fees every five years.
- Utilization of summary proceedings under Regulation 30A of SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 for non-payment of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

- Registered Investment Advisers must pay renewal fees every five years within three months before the expiry of the period for which fees have been paid.
- Noticees were required to file replies showing cause against the potential suspension or cancellation of their registration certificates.

## Important Dates

- Notices issued on: 24th February 2025, 03rd March 2025, 04th April 2025, 02nd June 2026, 04th June 2026, and 13th August 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Non-compliant Investment Advisers face potential cancellation or suspension of their certificates of registration, ensuring regulatory compliance and accountability in the advisory space.
