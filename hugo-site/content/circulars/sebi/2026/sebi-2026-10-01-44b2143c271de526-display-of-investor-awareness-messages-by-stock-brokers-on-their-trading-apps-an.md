## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular mandating all recognized stock exchanges, depositories, and stock brokers to prominently display investor awareness messages—shared as part of Project Jagrook and World Investor Week 2026—on their websites and trading apps.

## Key Points

- Mandates stock brokers to display investor awareness messages provided by SEBI.
- **Websites:** From October 5 to October 31, 2026, brokers must display both investor awareness messages and risk disclosures. From November 1, 2026 onwards, specified messages must appear on the landing page.
- **Trading Apps:** From October 5 to October 31, 2026, display is voluntary (risk disclosures optional during this period). From November 1, 2026 onwards, mandatory alternate-day display of investor awareness messages and risk disclosures on app landing pages.
- Stock exchanges and depositories are instructed to disseminate the circular, display messages on their websites, and amend relevant bye-laws.

## Regulatory Changes

- Introduction of mandatory display obligations for investor awareness messages on broker websites and trading apps under Project Jagrook.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock brokers must update their web and mobile trading platforms to comply with the specified display schedules starting October 5, 2026, and November 1, 2026.
- Stock exchanges and depositories must ensure compliance among members and update regulatory frameworks.

## Important Dates

- **October 5, 2026:** Commencement of initial display period.
- **October 31, 2026:** End of initial transition window.
- **November 1, 2026:** Full mandatory enforcement date for landing page and alternate-day app requirements.

## Impact Assessment

- Promotes widespread investor education and safety in alignment with World Investor Week 2026. Requires minor technical integration and UI updates by stock brokers for websites and trading apps.
