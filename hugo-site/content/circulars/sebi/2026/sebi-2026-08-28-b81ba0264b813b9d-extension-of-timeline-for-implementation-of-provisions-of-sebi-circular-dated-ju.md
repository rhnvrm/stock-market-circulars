## Summary

SEBI has extended the timeline for implementing the provisions of its circular dated June 15, 2026, which outlines norms for base price, price bands, call auction in pre-open sessions, and close-out procedures for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs). The new effective date is September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Implementation timeline for ETF norms extended based on feedback from Stock Exchanges.
- Effective date revised from September 1, 2026, to September 7, 2026.
- All other provisions of the original June 15, 2026 circular remain unchanged.
- MIIs are instructed to update systems, make necessary byelaw amendments, and inform market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

- Postponement of the operational enforcement date for ETF trading norms (base price, price bands, pre-open call auction, and close-out procedures) by six days.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock Exchanges, Clearing Corporations, and AMCs must update their systems and operational workflows to align with the new September 7, 2026 effective date.
- Relevant byelaws, rules, and regulations must be amended as needed.
- Market participants and investors must be duly notified of the revised timeline.

## Important Dates

- **August 28, 2026:** Circular issued.
- **September 7, 2026:** Revised effective date for implementation.

## Impact Assessment

Provides market infrastructure institutions and market participants additional time to ensure smooth technical and operational readiness for the new ETF trading and close-out norms.
