## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued the Alternative Investment Funds (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2026, amending the principal SEBI (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012. The amendment updates terminology and revises the eligibility criteria, net worth thresholds, and securities market exposure requirements for accredited investors.

## Key Points

- Updates terminology across regulations regarding investors and accreditation.
- Revises eligibility criteria for accredited investors, including specific thresholds for individuals, Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs), and body corporates.
- Introduces alternative market exposure criteria in securities markets alongside traditional net worth requirements.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Individuals / Entities:** Introduction of a securities market exposure requirement of at least 5 crore rupees.
- **Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs):** Net worth requirement of at least 50 crore rupees, or securities market exposure of at least 20 crore rupees, or each partner meeting the prescribed accreditation eligibility criteria.
- **Body Corporates (excluding LLPs):** Net worth of at least 50 crore rupees, or securities market exposure of at least 20 crore rupees.

## Compliance Requirements

Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) and managers must update their investor onboarding, verification, and accreditation processes in alignment with the revised net worth and market exposure thresholds.

## Important Dates

- **Notification Date:** October 9, 2026
- **Effective Date:** Coming into force on the date of publication in the Official Gazette (October 14, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

Provides greater flexibility for high-net-worth individuals and corporate entities to qualify as accredited investors through securities market exposure metrics, expanding the potential investor base for Alternative Investment Funds.
