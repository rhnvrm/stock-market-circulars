## Summary

SEBI issued a final order against M/s Trade Nexa Research Investment Advisor (Proprietor: Minakshi Asavani) following an inspection that revealed violations of SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, and PFUTP Regulations, including assuring fixed returns to clients. Taking into account the enquiry report and parallel adjudication proceedings, SEBI issued a regulatory censure under Regulation 26(1)(vii) of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.

## Key Points

- SEBI conducted a surprise onsite inspection of Trade Nexa Research (INA000009083) covering the period from April 01, 2020 to December 31, 2023.
- The inspection revealed that the noticee misled clients by providing assurances of fixed returns.
- The Designated Authority concluded that violations of IA Regulations and PFUTP Regulations were established and recommended regulatory censure.
- Separate adjudication proceedings were also conducted resulting in a monetary penalty order.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Individual enforcement action).

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must strictly avoid assuring fixed or guaranteed returns to clients and comply with all statutory obligations under IA Regulations and PFUTP Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2020 to December 31, 2023
- Date of Enquiry Report: August 12, 2025
- Date of Order: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on investment advisers regarding misleading client assurances and compliance enforcement.
