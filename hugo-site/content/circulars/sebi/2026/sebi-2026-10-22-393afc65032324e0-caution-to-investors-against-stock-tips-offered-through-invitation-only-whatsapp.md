## Summary

SEBI has issued a press release cautioning investors against fraudulent invitation-only WhatsApp groups operated by unknown persons, often using overseas numbers, who offer unauthorized stock tips and trading calls under the guise of free investor education. These groups utilize astroturfing—where members act in concert to post false profit claims and praise—to induce unsuspecting investors.

## Key Points

- Unregistered persons are offering stock tips and trading calls via invitation-only WhatsApp groups, often posing as market experts using overseas numbers.
- Astroturfing techniques are employed, where members post false claims of profit and praise the operators to create a false impression of genuine support.
- When groups are banned by enforcement agencies, operators create new groups and invite members via new numbers, citing risk control measures.
- Investors are advised not to join such groups, not to be swayed by false profit claims, to treat group suspension/re-creation as a red flag, and to deal only with SEBI-registered intermediaries.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an investor awareness and cautionary press release.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investors must deal only with SEBI-registered intermediaries and use authentic trading apps.
- Verify entity registration status at https://www.sebi.gov.in/intermediaries.html and trading apps at https://investor.sebi.gov.in/Investor-support.html.
- Avoid opening accounts or transferring funds to unverified platforms suggested in such groups.

## Important Dates

- October 22, 2026: Press release issuance date.

## Impact Assessment

Enhances retail investor awareness and safeguards market integrity by curbing fraudulent social media trading schemes and unverified advisory services.
