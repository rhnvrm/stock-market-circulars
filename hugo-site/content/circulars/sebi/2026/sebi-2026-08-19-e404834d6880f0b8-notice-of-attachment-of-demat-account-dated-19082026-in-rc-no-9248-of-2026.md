## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment of Demat Account and Mutual Fund Folio(s) under Recovery Proceeding No. 15712 of 2026 (Certificate No. 9248 of 2026) against defaulter Punjiben Babubhai Rathod for the recovery of Rs. 18,43,390.90.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9248 of 2026 dated July 27, 2026, issued for a total sum of Rs. 18,43,390.90 against Punjiben Babubhai Rathod (PAN: CABPR8593L).
- Dues arise from disgorgement ordered by QJA Order No. QJA/MN/IVD/ID7/31947/2025-26 dated December 31, 2025, in the matter of trading activities in DU Digital Technologies Limited (Now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Directs NSDL, CDSL, and all mutual funds in India to attach all demat accounts and mutual fund folios held singly or jointly by the defaulter with immediate effect.
- Orders that no debit shall be made in the said accounts/folios until further orders, while credits are permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

- Depositories (NSDL/CDSL) and Mutual Funds must immediately attach the demat accounts and mutual fund folios of the defaulter and restrict debits.
- Provide account details, account statements, and confirmation of attachment to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 19, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Date: July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational directive impacting depositories and mutual fund houses regarding a specific defaulter account restriction.
