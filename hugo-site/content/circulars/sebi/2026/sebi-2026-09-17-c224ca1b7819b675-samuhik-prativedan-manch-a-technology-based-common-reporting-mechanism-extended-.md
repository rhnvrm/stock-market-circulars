## Summary

SEBI has extended the Samuhik Prativedan Manch—a technology-based common reporting mechanism initially introduced for stock brokers—to members of Clearing Corporations (CCs) who are also registered as stock brokers. This initiative eliminates the requirement of submitting identical compliance reports separately to multiple Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations, thereby reducing operational redundancy and compliance costs for around 1,066 Clearing Members.

## Key Points

- Extension of Samuhik Prativedan Manch platform to Clearing Corporation members who are also stock brokers.
- Eliminates multiple and redundant report submissions to various Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations.
- Phased implementation schedule starting September 30, 2026.
- Benefits approximately 1,066 Clearing Members with multiple memberships.
- Reduces compliance costs and fosters ease of doing business.

## Regulatory Changes

Clearing Members (CMs) who are registered with multiple Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations are no longer required to submit compliance reports separately to each institution. Reports submitted through the Samuhik Prativedan Manch will be centrally consumed by all relevant Clearing Corporations.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Phase 1 (Effective September 30, 2026):** Clearing Members who are also stock brokers must submit 14 compliance reports (representing approximately 60% of their reporting requirements) via the common reporting platform.
- **Phase 2 (Effective December 31, 2026):** Exploration of remaining report submissions and evaluation of extending the platform to Professional Clearing Members (PCMs) holding multiple Clearing Corporation memberships.

## Important Dates

- **September 30, 2026:** Phase 1 implementation date covering 14 compliance reports for CMs who are also stock brokers.
- **December 31, 2026:** Phase 2 proposed implementation for remaining reports and expansion feasibility for PCMs.

## Impact Assessment

This measure substantially reduces administrative burdens and compliance costs for approximately 1,066 dual-registered Clearing Members and stock brokers, streamlining operational efficiency across market infrastructure institutions.
