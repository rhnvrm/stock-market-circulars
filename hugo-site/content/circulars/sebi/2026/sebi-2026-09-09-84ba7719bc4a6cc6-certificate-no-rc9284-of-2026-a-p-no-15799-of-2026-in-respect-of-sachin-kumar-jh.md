## Summary

SEBI has issued a certificate of recovery and notice of attachment under Certificate No. RC9284 of 2026 (A. P. No 15799 of 2026) against Sachin Kumar Jhawar HUF (PAN: AALHS9592Q). This enforcement action relates to proceedings in the matter of Illiquid Stock Options on BSE.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: RC9284 of 2026 / A. P. No 15799 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Sachin Kumar Jhawar HUF (PAN: AALHS9592Q).
- Matter: Illiquid Stock Options.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of dues, penalties, and disgorgement amounts arising from fraudulent and manipulative trading practices in illiquid stock options.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries are instructed to attach and freeze accounts, properties, and assets of the noticee to prevent any alienation or transfer of funds.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Recovery Proceeding: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates continuous regulatory enforcement and recovery actions against entities involved in artificial volume generation and reversal trades in illiquid stock options segments.
