## Summary

SEBI initiated adjudication proceedings against Late Mr. Bhagwati Prasad Deora for allegedly executing non-genuine reversal trades in the illiquid stock options segment on BSE during the period between April 1, 2014, and September 30, 2015, violating provisions of the PFUTP Regulations.

## Key Points

- SEBI observed large-scale reversal of trades in illiquid stock options on BSE creating artificial volumes.
- The Noticee was identified among entities executing non-genuine reversal trades lacking basic trading rationale.
- Proceedings were carried out under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Rule 4 of SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Adjudication order enforcing existing regulations under PFUTP).

## Compliance Requirements

- Adherence to prohibition of fraudulent and unfair trade practices relating to securities market.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: April 01, 2014 - September 30, 2015
- SCN Date: August 05, 2022
- Appointment of AO: June 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The order reinforces regulatory enforcement against historical market abuse and artificial volume creation in derivatives segments.
