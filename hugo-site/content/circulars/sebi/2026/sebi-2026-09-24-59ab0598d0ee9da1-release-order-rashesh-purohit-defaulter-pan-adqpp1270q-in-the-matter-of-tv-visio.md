## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order dated September 24, 2026, withdrawing and cancelling Recovery Certificate No. 7449 of 2023 (originally issued on December 8, 2023) against Rashesh Purohit (PAN: ADQPP1270Q) in the matter of TV Vision Limited.

## Key Points

- SEBI Recovery Officer issued Release Order for Recovery Certificate No. 7449 of 2023.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Rashesh Purohit (PAN: ADQPP1270Q).
- Company: TV Vision Limited (Scrip: TVVISION).
- Action: Official cancellation of the recovery certificate and release of attachment/proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal conclusion and withdrawal of recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

No further freezing of assets or accounts under Recovery Certificate No. 7449 of 2023; financial institutions and banks are released from earlier attachment mandates regarding this specific certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of Original Recovery Certificate: December 8, 2023
- Date of Release Order: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Signals the resolution and closure of recovery actions against the specified individual in TV Vision Limited, removing prior enforcement restrictions associated with this certificate.
