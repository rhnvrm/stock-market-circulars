## Summary

Vikram Aggarwal filed an RTI application dated August 30, 2026, seeking information and records regarding SEBI's handling, routing, and processing of complaints submitted via SCORES and subsequently routed or referred to the MINE system. The complaints pertained to HDFC Bank Limited, Kotak Mahindra Bank Limited (successor to ING Vysya Bank), Punjab National Bank, and Indian Hotels Company Limited (IHCL). Following the CPIO's response, the appellant filed Appeal No. 7099 of 2026 on September 22, 2026.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought specific SCORES reference numbers, assignment details, internal file notes, and routing bases for complaints concerning HDFC Bank, Kotak Mahindra Bank, PNB, and IHCL.
- Requested copies of rules, circulars, SOPs, internal instructions, or operational workflows governing the transfer/routing of complaints from SCORES to the MINE system.
- Sought audit logs, transfer logs, and procedures available to complainants for tracking, responding, or appealing after complaints are routed to MINE.
- Inquired whether any complaints were treated as market intelligence rather than handled through standard SCORES processing.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes announced; this document is an individual appellate order under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

No direct compliance obligations for market participants arising from this order; relates to internal SEBI RTI handling and complaint routing documentation.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: August 30, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 21, 2026
- Appeal Date: September 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational and market impact is low as this pertains to an individual RTI query regarding internal grievance redressal routing mechanisms rather than a new regulatory mandate.
