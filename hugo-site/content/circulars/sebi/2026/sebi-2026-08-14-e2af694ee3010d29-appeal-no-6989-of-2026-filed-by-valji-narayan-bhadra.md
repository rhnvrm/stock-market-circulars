## Summary

An RTI appeal was filed by Valji Narayan Bhadra before the SEBI Appellate Authority seeking certified copies of records related to land allotment under PACL Registration No. U171356xxx. SEBI's CPIO and Appellate Authority confirmed that the requested information is not available with SEBI, noting that refund status and related matters are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought various records regarding land allotment, possession, and database entries for PACL Registration No. U171356xxx.
- SEBI responded that the requested information is not in its possession.
- SEBI advised the appellant to approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee, which handles all communication regarding PACL refund status.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, citing precedent that a CPIO cannot invent information not in possession.
