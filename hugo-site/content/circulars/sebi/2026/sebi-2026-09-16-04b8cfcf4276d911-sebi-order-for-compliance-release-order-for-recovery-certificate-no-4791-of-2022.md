## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order concerning Recovery Certificate No. 4791 of 2022 against Gaekwad Plantations Limited. This order marks the conclusion or fulfillment of recovery proceedings initiated due to earlier non-compliance regarding collective investment schemes.

## Key Points

- Entity involved: Gaekwad Plantations Limited.
- Regulatory action: Release order under SEBI recovery proceedings for Recovery Certificate No. 4791 of 2022.
- Context: Arises from historical non-compliance related to the company's Collective Investment Schemes (CIS).

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement/recovery release action.

## Compliance Requirements

- Release of previously attached bank accounts, properties, or assets as per the recovery certificate compliance.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 16, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Limited broader market impact as this is an isolated recovery release for a specific non-listed entity.
