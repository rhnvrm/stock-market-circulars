## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated October 01, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9261 of 2026 drawn against Sachinkumar Bhagwandas Sahu. This action is part of enforcement and recovery proceedings relating to market manipulation and fraudulent trading activities in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Limited (now Crystal Business System Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under RC No. 9261 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Sachinkumar Bhagwandas Sahu.
- Matter: Fraudulent trading activities and pump-and-dump schemes in Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and intermediaries are required to comply with remittance and attachment directives to ensure recovery of dues from the specified noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Remittance Order: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities penalized for stock manipulation and illicit trading practices.
