## Summary

SEBI has issued a press release cautioning investors against persons on social media platforms offering live trading strategies and real-time tips. Highlighting previous circular guidelines on market price data sharing, SEBI noted that unregistered entities are conducting live trading sessions with substantial viewers, offering unverified advice, buy/sell recommendations, and performance claims without regulatory registration.

## Key Points

- SEBI's earlier circular (May 8, 2026) permits market price data sharing strictly for investor education without monetary incentives, subject to a thirty-day lag.
- Live market data sharing is restricted to orderly market functioning or regulatory requirements.
- Unregistered individuals on social media are portraying themselves as market experts, conducting live trading sessions, showcasing real-time trade performance, and providing unauthorized investment advice.
- Investors are strongly advised to deal exclusively with SEBI-registered intermediaries and avoid trusting claims or investment tips from social media live streams.

## Regulatory Changes

- Reiteration of restrictions on sharing live market data and utilizing market data from the preceding 30 days for educational or advisory purposes without proper regulatory standing.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities and individuals must refrain from providing real-time trading strategies, live market data, or unregistered advisory services on social media platforms.

## Important Dates

- Press Release Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increases investor awareness and regulatory scrutiny on social media finfluencers and unverified live trading channels, protecting retail investors from fraudulent or misleading advisory services.
