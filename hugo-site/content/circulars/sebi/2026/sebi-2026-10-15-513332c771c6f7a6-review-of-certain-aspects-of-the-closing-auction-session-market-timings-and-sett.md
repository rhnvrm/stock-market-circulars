## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular reviewing and modifying provisions related to the Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the equity cash segment, market timings, and the methodology for determining settlement prices for stock and index derivatives contracts based on public consultation and SMAC recommendations.

## Key Points

- CAS is implemented as a 10-minute session from 3:30 p.m. to 3:40 p.m. on all trading days.
- Order entry structure includes reference price transition (3:30-3:31 p.m.), order entry for limit and market orders (3:31-3:36 p.m.), and order entry for limit orders only without modification/cancellation for market orders (3:36-3:40 p.m.).
- Random closure of the order entry session occurs between 3:38 p.m. and 3:40 p.m.
- Index derivatives trading continues until 3:45 p.m. on all trading days except expiry days (expiring index derivatives cease at 3:30 p.m.).
- Stock derivatives trading continues until 3:45 p.m. on all trading days, including expiry days.
- Cash segment post-close session operates between 3:50 p.m. and 4:00 p.m.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modifies provisions of SEBI circular dated January 16, 2026, and Chapter 5 of the Master Circular for Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations dated December 30, 2024.
- Updates timings, operational aspects, and transition rules for unexecuted orders.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations must operationalize the revised CAS timings, order matching frameworks, and derivatives settlement procedures.
- Trading participants must update their trading terminals and algorithmic systems to comply with the revised session timings and order restriction rules.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 15, 2026
- Effective as per exchange implementation schedules.

## Impact Assessment

- Improves price discovery and market integrity during market close.
- Brings clarity to trading hours and settlement price determination for cash and derivatives segments.
