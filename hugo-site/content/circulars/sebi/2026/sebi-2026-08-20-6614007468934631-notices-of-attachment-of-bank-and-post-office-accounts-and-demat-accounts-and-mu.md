## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued notices of attachment under Attachment Proceeding (AP) Nos. 15717 and 15718 of 2026 dated August 20, 2026, against Rohit Bairwa (PAN: ELYPB0179Q). The attachment covers bank accounts, post office accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 9168 of 2026 in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- **Defaulter Entity:** Rohit Bairwa (PAN: ELYPB0179Q).
- **Regulatory Authority:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- **Proceedings:** Attachment Proceeding Nos. 15717 and 15718 of 2026 under Recovery Certificate No. 9168 of 2026.
- **Assets Attached:** Bank accounts, post office savings accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios.
- **Scrip Involved:** DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

This is an enforcement action and recovery proceeding rather than a regulatory policy change. It underscores SEBI's rigorous enforcement against individuals involved in fraudulent and unfair trade practices.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, post offices, depositories (NSDL/CDSL), and mutual fund registrars/AMCs are required to immediately freeze the accounts, demat holdings, and mutual fund folios associated with the named entity and prevent any debits or transfers, transferring the attached funds towards the recovery dues as directed by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Notice:** August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Directly impacts the designated defaulter by freezing liquidity and investment assets. Reinforces market integrity and regulatory deterrence against entities engaging in manipulative trading in emerging scrips.
