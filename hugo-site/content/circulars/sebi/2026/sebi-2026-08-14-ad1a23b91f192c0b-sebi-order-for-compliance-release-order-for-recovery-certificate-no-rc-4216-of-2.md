## Summary

SEBI has issued a release order concerning Recovery Certificate No. RC 4216 of 2021 against Mr. Dheerendra Krishan Pratap in the matter of Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited.

## Key Points

- Relates to Recovery Proceedings initiated by SEBI.
- Issued against Mr. Dheerendra Krishan Pratap, associated with Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited.
- Involves the release/lifting of attachments on properties or bank accounts linked to Recovery Certificate No. RC 4216 of 2021.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative enforcement and recovery action.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals must adhere to SEBI recovery orders and settlement terms as applicable under the securities laws.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Administrative resolution of specific recovery proceedings with minimal direct impact on broader market operations.
