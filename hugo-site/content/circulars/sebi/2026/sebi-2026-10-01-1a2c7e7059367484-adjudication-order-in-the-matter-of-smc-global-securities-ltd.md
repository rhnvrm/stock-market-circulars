## Summary

SEBI conducted a thematic inspection of SMC Global Securities Ltd for the period from January 1, 2024, to September 30, 2024, focusing on unauthorized trading complaints and client order placement verification. The inspection revealed failures in maintaining client order placement records and lack of supervision over Authorized Persons (APs), leading to the initiation of adjudication proceedings under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Key Points

- Thematic inspection covered January 1, 2024, to September 30, 2024.
- The Noticee failed to maintain adequate records relating to client order placements.
- Observed lack of supervision and monitoring of Authorized Persons (APs) by the stock broker.
- Proceedings initiated under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Regulatory Changes

- Strict enforcement of existing provisions under SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 1992, Master Circulars, and Exchange guidelines governing order placement audit trails and AP supervision.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock brokers must ensure comprehensive and tamper-proof record maintenance for all client order placements.
- Enhanced supervisory frameworks must be enforced over Authorized Persons to prevent unauthorized trading and ensure operational compliance.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: January 1, 2024 to September 30, 2024
- Show Cause Notice Date: July 30, 2025
- Proceedings ongoing in 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on stock brokers regarding client order integrity and Authorized Person compliance, emphasizing strict adherence to inspection mandates and operational transparency.
