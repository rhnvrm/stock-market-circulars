## Summary

SEBI issued a remittance order dated September 08, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9188 of 2026, directing all banks and mutual funds in India to remit outstanding dues of Rs. 8,73,000/- from the bank accounts and mutual fund folios of the defaulter, Jagdish Chhanabhai Vaghela.

## Key Points

- Defaulter Name: Jagdish Chhanabhai Vaghela (PAN: AFPPV2562Q).
- Recovery Certificate: No. 9188 of 2026 dated July 02, 2026.
- Total Outstanding Dues: Rs. 8,73,000/-.
- Designated Bank Account for Remittance: A/c No. SEBIRRDPEN9188 with ICICI Bank (IFSC: ICIC0000106).

## Regulatory Changes

- Direction issued under Section 28A and 28B of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with relevant provisions of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

## Compliance Requirements

- Banks and mutual funds must check for accounts or folios belonging to the defaulter, remit available funds up to the outstanding amount to SEBI, and submit transaction details via email to designated SEBI officials.
- If the defaulter holds no accounts or folios with the institution, no communication is required.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 08, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Operational compliance requirement for banks and mutual funds to execute attachment and remittance proceedings as mandated by SEBI.
