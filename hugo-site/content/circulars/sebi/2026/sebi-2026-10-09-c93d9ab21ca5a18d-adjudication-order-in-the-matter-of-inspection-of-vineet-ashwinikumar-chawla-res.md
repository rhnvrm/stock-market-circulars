## Summary

SEBI conducted an inspection of Research Analyst Vineet Ashwinikumar Chawla for the period April 1, 2024 to November 30, 2025. Following the inspection and subsequent replies, SEBI identified multiple alleged violations concerning fees and charges, limitations on research analysts, research report disclosures, maintenance of books of accounts and records, investor grievance redressal, client onboarding process, designation of principal officer, and advertisement code under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 and SEBI Master Circulars.

## Key Points

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2024 to November 30, 2025.
- Noticee: Vineet Ashwinikumar Chawla (SEBI Regn. No.: INH000008190).
- Alleged violations span across fees & charges, research report disclosures, record maintenance, grievance redressal, client onboarding, principal officer designation, and advertising code.
- Proceedings initiated under Section 15A(c) and 15EB of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; enforcement of existing provisions under SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008, and SEBI Master Circulars.

## Compliance Requirements

Research analysts must strictly adhere to regulatory provisions regarding fee collection, comprehensive research report disclosures, maintenance of proper books of accounts and records, efficient grievance redressal mechanisms, compliant client onboarding processes, proper designation of principal officers, and adherence to the advertisement code.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Date: January 29, 2026
- Inspection Findings Communicated: March 18, 2026
- Noticee Replies: March 19, 2026 and March 24, 2026
- Adjudicating Officer Appointment: June 15, 2026
