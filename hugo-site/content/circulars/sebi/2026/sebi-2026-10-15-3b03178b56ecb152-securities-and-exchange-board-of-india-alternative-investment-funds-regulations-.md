## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued the Third Amendment Regulations, 2026, updating the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012, effective October 15, 2026. This amendment modifies the eligibility conditions and criteria for accredited investors across various entity types.

## Key Points

- Revised accreditation criteria for individuals, HUFs, family trusts, and sole proprietorships, including provisions for securities market exposure.
- Updated net worth and securities market exposure criteria for limited liability partnerships (LLPs) and bodies corporate.
- Inclusion of accreditation via specified SEBI registered or regulated entities in addition to accreditation agencies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amended regulation 2(1)(ab) to include accreditation by specified SEBI registered/regulated entities.
- Added new eligibility clauses allowing securities market exposure thresholds for individuals, LLPs, and bodies corporate to qualify as accredited investors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs) must adopt the updated accredited investor eligibility norms for onboarding and compliance verification.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines and broadens the pathways for investor accreditation in Alternative Investment Funds, facilitating easier participation for eligible market participants with appropriate securities market exposure.
