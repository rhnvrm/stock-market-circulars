## Summary

SEBI initiated adjudication proceedings against National Steel and Agro Industries Limited for alleged violations of the PFUTP Regulations, 2003, arising from trading activities in illiquid stock options on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) between April 1, 2014, and September 30, 2015. Investigation revealed large-scale reversal trades creating artificial volumes.

## Key Points

- SEBI observed substantial non-genuine reversal trades (81.40% of all stock options trades during the investigation period) creating false or misleading appearance of trading on BSE.
- National Steel and Agro Industries Limited (PAN: AAACN3548H) was identified as one of the entities indulging in these non-genuine transactions.
- Proceedings under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Rule 4 of the Adjudication Rules, were initiated for violations of Regulations 3(a), (b), (c), (d), 4(1), and 4(2)(a) of the PFUTP Regulations, 2003.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Enforcement action under existing regulations).

## Compliance Requirements

- Strict adherence to PFUTP Regulations prohibiting deceptive, fraudulent, or manipulative trading practices, including reversal trades in illiquid contracts.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: April 1, 2014 – September 30, 2015
- Show Cause Notice Date: August 10, 2022
- Order Transfer/Assignment Date: June 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory vigilance against past market abuse and artificial volume generation in derivative segments.
