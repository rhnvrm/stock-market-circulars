## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7025 of 2026 filed by Shri Yukteshwar Baranwal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act) against SEBI's CPIO response. The appellant had sought regulatory clarifications regarding NRO Demat/Trading account openings, investments in Indian IPOs/equities/mutual funds, and documentation requirements for minor children who are foreign citizens and OCI cardholders managed by resident Indian natural guardians. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, ruling that queries seeking clarifications, opinions, or advice do not constitute 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- **Appellant's Queries:** Sought regulatory positions and clarifications on NRO account operations, investment eligibility in Indian capital markets, and CKYC/KRA/FATCA requirements for minor OCI cardholders with resident Indian guardians.
- **Respondent's Position:** The CPIO stated that the queries sought clarification and opinion, which fall outside the definition of 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, while providing references to existing Master Circulars on KYC.
- **Appellate Ruling:** The Appellate Authority concurred with the CPIO, citing relevant Central Information Commission (CIC) precedents confirming that CPIOs are not obligated to provide analysis, explanations, or opinions under the RTI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. The order reinforces existing statutory interpretations under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Compliance Requirements

- None. This is an adjudicatory order on an RTI appeal.
