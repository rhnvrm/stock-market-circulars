## Summary

Following amendments to the SEBI (Issue and Listing of Municipal Debt Securities) Regulations, 2015 (ILMDS Regulations) notified on July 08, 2026, SEBI has specified operational aspects regarding the face value of privately placed municipal debt securities and introduced a two-step escrow account mechanism for issuers operating as pooled finance vehicles or Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs).

## Key Points

- **Face Value Options:** The face value of each privately placed municipal debt security must be either Rs. One Lakh or Rs. Ten Thousand.
- **Face Value Restrictions:** Municipal debt securities issued at a face value of Rs. Ten Thousand must have a fixed maturity and cannot contain any structured obligations.
- **Trading Lot Alignment:** The trading lot on Stock Exchanges for privately placed municipal debt securities must always equal the face value of the security.
- **Two-Step Escrow Mechanism:** For pooled finance vehicles or SPVs set up under the Pooled Finance Development Fund Scheme, constituent municipalities must create escrow accounts and comply with specified mechanisms.
- **SPV Account Buffers:** The SPV/pooled finance vehicle must maintain an Interest payment account and a Sinking fund account, transferring funds from constituent municipalities, and must maintain an amount equivalent to one year's interest obligation in the Interest payment account throughout the security's tenure.
- **Credit Enhancement:** SPVs/pooled finance vehicles are permitted to include credit enhancement features to bolster credit ratings and safeguard investor interests.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Regulation 22 Clarification:** Specifies operational rules for Regulation 22 of the ILMDS Regulations concerning the face value of privately placed municipal debt.
- **Amendment to 2019 Escrow Circular:** Amends SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/DDHS/CIR/P/134/2019 dated November 13, 2019, by inserting new paragraphs 4.1.5 and 4.1.6 to govern pooled finance vehicle escrow arrangements.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Municipal Debt Issuers:** Must align the face values of private placements to either Rs. 1 Lakh or Rs. 10,000, and ensure Stock Exchange trading lots match this value.
- **Constituent Municipalities of Pooled Finance Vehicles/SPVs:** Must establish and manage required escrow accounts.
- **SPVs/Pooled Finance Vehicles:** Must establish Interest payment and Sinking fund accounts, execute agreements with constituent municipalities, and maintain at least one year of interest obligations as a buffer in the Interest payment account.

## Important Dates

- **July 08, 2026:** Notification of the SEBI (Issue and Listing of Municipal Debt Securities) (Amendment) Regulations, 2026.
- **August 11, 2026:** Date of issue and effective date of the operational circular.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Access:** Allowing a lower face value of Rs. 10,000 (without structured obligations) for privately placed municipal debt could potentially attract a broader range of investors and enhance participation in municipal debt markets.
- **Investor Protection:** The two-step escrow account mechanism, coupled with the mandatory one-year interest buffer in the SPV's Interest payment account, significantly enhances security for investors by mitigating default risk from constituent municipalities.
- **Operational Procedures:** Issuers and constituent municipalities will need to adopt more rigorous fund tracking, management, and escrow reconciliation procedures to comply with the multi-tiered account requirements.
