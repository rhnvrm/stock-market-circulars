## Summary

The appellant filed an RTI application seeking information regarding refunds to investors in the matter of PACL Ltd. The CPIO informed that the information is not available with SEBI and that refunds are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response and dismissed the appeal, noting that SEBI cannot invent information it does not possess.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details on investor refunds related to PACL Ltd.
- SEBI stated that the requested information is not available with SEBI and that refund communications are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The Appellate Authority cited precedence that the CPIO cannot invent information not in possession and found no deficiency in the response.
- Public notices, status reports, and FAQs regarding PACL Ltd. remain available on the SEBI website.
