## Summary

SEBI has reviewed the provisions related to International Securities Identification Number (ISIN) for debt securities issued on private placement basis to enhance ease of fund raising and Asset Liability Management (ALM) by issuers. The circular updates the NCS Master Circular by increasing the maximum number of ISINs maturing in a financial year and providing detailed bifurcation for plain vanilla and structured debt instruments.

## Key Points

- **Overall ISIN Limit:** A maximum of 17 ISINs maturing in any financial year are allowed for an issuer of debt securities, plus an additional 6 ISINs for capital gains tax debt securities under Section 54EC of the Income Tax Act, 1961.
- **Plain Vanilla Debt:** Maximum of 12 ISINs maturing per financial year for secured and unsecured plain vanilla debt securities, with incremental ISINs permitted for every additional Rs. 3,000 Crore when outstanding amounts exceed Rs. 15,000 Crore.
- **Structured & Market-Linked Debt:** Maximum of 5 ISINs maturing per financial year for structured debt, market-linked debt, Floating Rate Bonds (FRBs), Zero Coupon Bonds (ZCBs), and Tier II debt capital instruments. If an issuer issues *only* these instruments, the limit is 12 ISINs.
- **Grandfathering:** Existing legacy ISINs for FRBs, ZCBs, and Tier II bonds issued prior to the circular are grandfathered, with specific rules if legacy ISINs equal or exceed five.
- **Exclusions:** ISINs pertaining to Government of India (GoI) serviced/Extra Budgetary Resources (EBR) bonds and ESG Debt Securities are excluded from the applicable ISIN limits.

## Regulatory Changes

- Replaces paragraphs 1.1, 1.2, and 1.3 of the SEBI Master Circular for Issue and Listing of Non-Convertible Securities, Securitised Debt Instruments, Security Receipts, Municipal Debt Securities and Commercial Paper dated October 15, 2025.
- Establishes clearer thresholds linking outstanding maturing amounts to allowed ISIN counts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers of listed debt securities must ensure that their private placement issuances adhere to the revised ISIN maturity caps per financial year.
- Stock Exchanges and Depositories must monitor and enforce the updated ISIN limits and exclusions accordingly.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides greater operational flexibility and better ALM alignment for corporate debt issuers, especially those issuing large volumes or specialized debt instruments on private placement basis.
