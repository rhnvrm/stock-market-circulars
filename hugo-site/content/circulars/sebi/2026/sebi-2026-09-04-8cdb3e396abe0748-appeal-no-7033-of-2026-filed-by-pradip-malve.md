## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7033 of 2026 filed by Shri Pradip Malve against the CPIO, SEBI, under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant had sought various documents including technical glitch records, KYC forms, MCX technical glitch reports, and audit reports concerning Angel One Pvt Ltd.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information regarding technical glitch records, KYC forms, and audit/inspection reports of Angel One Pvt Ltd from January 2022 to July 2026, as well as MCX technical glitch reports for April to May 2026.
- SEBI's CPIO and Appellate Authority clarified that technical glitch reporting and financial disincentive structures are managed by Stock Exchanges as first-level regulators in accordance with SEBI circulars.
- Information regarding individual KYC forms and certain commercial confidential reports received in a fiduciary capacity is not maintained by SEBI in its normal course of regulation.

## Regulatory Changes

- None

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock brokers are required to report technical glitches to Stock Exchanges as per SEBI circulars dated November 25, 2022, and January 09, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Application: July 03, 2026
- Date of CPIO Response: July 17, 2026
- Date of Appeal: August 10, 2026

- No direct market or operational impact as this is an individual RTI adjudication.
