## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance dated October 9, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9277 of 2026 drawn against Kapil Wadhawan (PAN: AAOPW6145L), designated as a defaulter in the matter of Dewan Housing Finance Corporation Ltd (DHFL). This order directs the remittance of recovered or attached funds toward the satisfaction of outstanding dues under the recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- General Remittance issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9277 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Kapil Wadhawan (PAN: AAOPW6145L), former promoter of DHFL.
- Matter: Dewan Housing Finance Corporation Ltd (DHFL) recovery proceedings.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and dues from defaulters.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and designated intermediaries are required to execute remittance instructions and transfer attached funds to SEBI's recovery account as mandated in the order.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance: October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects rigorous regulatory follow-through and asset realization in high-profile corporate default and market violation cases.
