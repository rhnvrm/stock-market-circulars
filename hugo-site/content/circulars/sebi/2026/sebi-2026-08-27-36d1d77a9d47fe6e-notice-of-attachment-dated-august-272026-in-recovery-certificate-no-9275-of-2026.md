## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 27, 2026, in connection with Recovery Certificate No. 9275 of 2026, concerning Kunnamkulam Paper Mills Ltd. This action is part of SEBI's regulatory enforcement mechanisms to recover dues or enforce orders.

## Key Points

- **Entity Involved:** Kunnamkulam Paper Mills Ltd
- **Regulatory Authority:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
- **Reference:** Recovery Certificate No. 9275 of 2026
- **Date of Notice:** August 27, 2026
- **Action Type:** Notice of Attachment

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an enforcement action specific to the named entity under existing statutory recovery powers.

## Compliance Requirements

Subject entity and associated parties must comply with the terms of the attachment notice and settle outstanding dues as mandated by the recovery officer.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enforcement proceedings restrict asset transfers by the defaulter entity to ensure recovery of dues owed under regulatory mandates.
