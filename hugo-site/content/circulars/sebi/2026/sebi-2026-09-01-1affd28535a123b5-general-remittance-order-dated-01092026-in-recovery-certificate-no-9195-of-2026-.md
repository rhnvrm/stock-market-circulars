## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 1, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 9195 of 2026 issued against Manjulaben Parmar. This action relates to recovery proceedings and remittance of dues in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9195 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Manjulaben Parmar (PAN: DKYPP5235B).
- Matter: Trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of outstanding dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, garnishees, and financial institutions are required to comply with the remittance order by transferring attached funds or dues as directed by the SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing regulatory enforcement and rigorous recovery measures by SEBI against defaulters involved in market irregularities concerning DU Digital Global Limited.
