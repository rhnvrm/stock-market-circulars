## Summary

SEBI's Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 6981 of 2026 filed by Sandeep Kumar under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought details regarding demat account maintenance charges, communication rules between DP clients and account holders, and approved procedures for closing a demat account. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that the requested information is publicly available in the SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018 and the SEBI Master Circular for Depositories dated December 03, 2024.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought information on demat account maintenance charges, communication occasions, and closure procedures.
- Respondent directed the appellant to the SEBI (Depositories and Participants) Regulations, 2018, and the SEBI Master Circular for Depositories dated December 03, 2024.
- The Appellate Authority cited judicial precedents establishing that information already available in the public domain is not considered 'held' by the public authority under the RTI Act.
- The appeal was dismissed as devoid of merit.

## Regulatory Changes

None. Reaffirms existing public availability of depository regulations and circulars.
