## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice for the attachment of bank and post office accounts under Recovery Certificate No. 9223 of 2026 against Mr. Rajesh Punia (PAN: AVNPP5342G) and Mrs. Savita Punia (PAN: AALPP0729L), jointly and severally, in the matter of investigation into the financial statements of Oriental Trimex Limited.

## Key Points

- Defaulters: Mr. Rajesh Punia and Mrs. Savita Punia.
- Matter: Investigation in the Financial Statements of Oriental Trimex Limited.
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9223 of 2026.
- Action: Attachment of bank and post office accounts.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement action taken pursuant to SEBI recovery proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Banks and post offices are required to attach the accounts of the specified defaulters and prevent any debits or withdrawals.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 11, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Restricts financial assets and operations of the named individuals in connection with SEBI recovery proceedings.
