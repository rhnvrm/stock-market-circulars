## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order in favor of Sai Refinery (PAN: ABRFS9439H), who was previously listed as a defaulter under Recovery Certificate No. 3901 of 2021. The proceedings relate to the past matter of trading in illiquid stock options on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

## Key Points

- Entity released from defaulter status under Recovery Certificate No. 3901 of 2021.
- Relates to historical enforcement actions concerning trading in illiquid stock options on BSE.
- PAN: ABRFS9439H.

## Regulatory Changes

- Concluding and releasing recovery proceedings initiated against the specified entity.

## Compliance Requirements

- No further compliance actions required under this specific recovery certificate as dues/proceedings have been resolved/released.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 31, 2026
- Original Recovery Certificate: July 1, 2021 (Certificate No. 3901 of 2021)

## Impact Assessment

- Entity-specific release with negligible broader market impact.
