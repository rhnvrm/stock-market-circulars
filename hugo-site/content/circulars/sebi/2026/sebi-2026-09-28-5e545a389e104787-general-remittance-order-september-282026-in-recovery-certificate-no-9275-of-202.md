## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9275 of 2026 drawn in the matter of Kunnamkulam Paper Mills Ltd, directing the remittance of recovered dues pursuant to enforcement and recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9275 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Entity: Kunnamkulam Paper Mills Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated entities are required to comply with remittance instructions regarding attached funds or assets.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI to ensure realization of dues and uphold regulatory compliance.
