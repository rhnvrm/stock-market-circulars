## Summary

An RTI appeal filed by Hawa Singh against SEBI regarding PACL refund calculations was dismissed by the Appellate Authority as time-barred under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act, 2005, and due to the absence of a condonation request. Additionally, the appellant was advised that all communication regarding refund status is handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- The appellant filed an RTI application seeking details and calculations regarding a PACL refund claim under Registration No. U101134xxx.
- The first appeal was received on August 25, 2026, beyond the 30-day statutory limit from the CPIO's response date of May 25, 2026.
- The appeal was dismissed as time-barred due to lack of a delay condonation request and valid justification.
- On merits, the respondent noted that all PACL refund communications and processing are managed by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (individual appellate order).

## Compliance Requirements

- Appellants must file RTI first appeals within the statutory 30-day window prescribed under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act.
- PACL investors must approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee for refund status and related grievances.

## Important Dates

- Date of CPIO response: May 25, 2026
- Date of first appeal receipt: August 25, 2026
- Date of Order: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No broader market or regulatory impact; reinforces standard RTI timelines and clarifies jurisdictional authority for PACL refunds.
