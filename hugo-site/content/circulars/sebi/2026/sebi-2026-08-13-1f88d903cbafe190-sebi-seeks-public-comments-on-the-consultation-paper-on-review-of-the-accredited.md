## Summary

SEBI has released a consultation paper for a comprehensive review of the existing Accredited Investor (AI) framework based on stakeholder feedback and recommendations from the Alternative Investment Policy Advisory Committee (AIPAC). Key proposals include introducing securities market assets as an eligibility criterion, simplifying onboarding through manager-led accreditation valid at a group level, streaming validity to 3 years, and expanding deemed accreditation to all Persons Resident Outside India (PROI).

## Key Points

- Proposed securities market assets eligibility threshold: INR 5 crore for individuals and INR 20 crore for body corporates.
- Estimated to expand the pool of eligible Accredited Investors from around 1 lakh to nearly 4 lakh.
- Introduction of manager-led accreditation valid at a group level, alongside the existing Accreditation Agency route.
- Validity of accreditation streamlined to 3 years based on latest documents.
- Inclusion of all Persons Resident Outside India (PROI) under deemed Accredited Investors without minimum thresholds.
- Benefits extend across AIFs, Specialised Investment Funds (SIFs) of Mutual Funds, Portfolio Management Services (PMS), and Angel Funds.

## Regulatory Changes

- Expansion of eligibility criteria to include securities market assets alongside income and net-worth.
- Expansion of deemed accreditation scope under FEMA, 1999 to cover all NRIs, OCIs, and other PROIs seamlessly.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and stakeholders are invited to submit comments on the consultation paper by September 03, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: August 13, 2026
- Comments Deadline: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Significantly broadens the investor base for Alternative Investment Funds and related products like PMS and Angel Funds by streamlining onboarding and easing participation for resident and non-resident investors.
