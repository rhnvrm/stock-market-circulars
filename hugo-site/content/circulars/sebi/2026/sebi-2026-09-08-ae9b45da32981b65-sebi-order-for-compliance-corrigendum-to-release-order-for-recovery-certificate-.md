## Summary

SEBI has issued a Corrigendum to the Release Order previously issued under Recovery Certificate No. 1931 of 2019 in the matter of Sai Prakash Organic Food Limited and its directors/promoters, correcting specific details of the release of attachment.

## Key Points

- Corrigendum issued regarding Recovery Certificate No. 1931 of 2019.
- Entity: M/s Sai Prakash Organic Food Limited and associated noticees (including Pushpendra Singh Baghel, Pushpanjali Singh Baghel, and others).
- Modifies/corrects details from the previous Release Order dated August 14, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal rectification and update to administrative/enforcement release documentation under SEBI recovery proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant financial institutions and authorities to note the corrected particulars regarding the lifting of attachments or recovery measures.

## Important Dates

- Date of Corrigendum: September 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Administrative correction with minimal direct market impact, ensuring legal and documentary accuracy in SEBI's recovery proceedings.
