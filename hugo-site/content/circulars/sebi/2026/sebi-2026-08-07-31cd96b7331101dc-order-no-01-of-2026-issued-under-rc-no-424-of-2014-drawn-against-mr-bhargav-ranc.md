## Summary

This order is issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) under Rule 11 of the Second Schedule of the Income Tax Act, 1961, read with Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992. The order is directed against Mr. Bhargav Ranchodlal Panchal (the Defaulter) in relation to recovery proceedings under Recovery Certificate No. 424 of 2014, initiated for the disgorgement of unlawful gains totaling Rs. 8,97,024/- plus interest in the matter of IPO irregularities.

## Key Points

- **Disgorgement Directive**: SEBI's WTM order dated February 25, 2011, ordered the Defaulter to disgorge unlawful gains of Rs. 8,97,024/- along with 10% simple interest per annum for the period 2005–2010 (amounting to Rs. 4,48,512/-) within 45 days.
- **Recovery & Attachment**: Due to non-payment, SEBI initiated recovery proceedings in July 2014, issuing a Notice of Demand and attaching the Defaulter's bank and demat accounts.
- **Defaulter's Representation**: The Defaulter claimed non-receipt of the certified copy of the 2011 order as the reason for delay in challenging it before the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT).
- **Payment Under Protest**: The Defaulter received the certified order on October 26, 2020, and subsequently deposited Rs. 13,45,536/- via demand drafts on November 10, 2020, under protest, requesting the release of his frozen demat accounts.

## Regulatory Changes

- There are no regulatory amendments introduced by this order. It is an individual enforcement and recovery action.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Defaulter Compliance**: The Defaulter has remitted the demanded disgorgement amount and interest under protest and petitioned for the de-freezing of demat accounts.
- **No Broader Action Required**: This order does not impose new compliance obligations on market intermediaries or the general investing public.

## Important Dates

- **February 25, 2011**: Original SEBI Whole Time Member (WTM) Disgorgement Order.
- **July 18, 2014**: Initiation of recovery proceedings and issuance of Notice of Demand (NoD).
- **October 26, 2020**: Receipt of the certified copy of the WTM order by the Defaulter.
- **November 10, 2020**: Submission of Demand Drafts totaling Rs. 13,45,536/- by the Defaulter under protest.

## Impact Assessment

- **Systemic Impact**: Low. This is an individual enforcement action and has no broader regulatory impact on market infrastructure, trading practices, or listing requirements.
- **Defaulter Impact**: High. The Defaulter was restricted from accessing the securities market and had bank and demat accounts attached, which are now subject to de-freezing requests following payment.
