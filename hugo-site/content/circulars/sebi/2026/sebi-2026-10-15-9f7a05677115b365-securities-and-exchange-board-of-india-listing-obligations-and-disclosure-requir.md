## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has promulgated the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2026, dated October 12, 2026. This amendment introduces key revisions to regulation 62A governing the listing obligations of non-convertible debt securities.

## Key Points

- Amends regulation 62A of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015.
- Substitutes sub-regulation (1) to mandate that any listed entity whose non-convertible debt securities are listed on or after the date of publication of these amendment regulations must list all subsequent issuances of non-convertible debt securities on stock exchange(s).
- Omits sub-regulations (2) and (3) of regulation 62A.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Regulation 62A(1) Substitution:** Requires listed entities with non-convertible debt securities listed on or after the publication date to list all future issuances of such securities on stock exchanges.
- **Deletion:** Sub-regulations (2) and (3) of regulation 62A have been removed.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities issuing non-convertible debt securities must ensure that all subsequent issuances of non-convertible debt securities are listed on recognised stock exchanges in compliance with the updated regulation 62A.

## Important Dates

- **Effective Date:** October 12, 2026 (came into force on the date of publication in the Official Gazette).

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances transparency and mandatory listing compliance for corporate debt issuers by streamlining rules around non-convertible debt securities.
