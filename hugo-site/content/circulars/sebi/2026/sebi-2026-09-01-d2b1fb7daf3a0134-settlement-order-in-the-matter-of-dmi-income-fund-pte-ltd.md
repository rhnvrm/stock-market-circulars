## Summary

SEBI passed a Settlement Order in the matter of DMI Income Fund Pte Ltd, a registered Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI), resolving proceedings related to alleged delayed disclosure of a new share class (Class K Series 3) and investments made without providing required beneficial owner details.

## Key Points

- Entity Involved: DMI Income Fund Pte Ltd (Foreign Portfolio Investor).
- Settlement Amount: ₹27,62,500.
- Alleged Violations: Failure to intimate SEBI/DDP within 7 working days regarding the addition of a new share class and making investments without required beneficial owner details under SEBI (Foreign Portfolio Investors) Regulations, 2019.
- Application and Approval Timeline: Application filed on December 1, 2025; HPAC recommendation on June 29, 2026; WTM approval on August 12, 2026; payment remitted on August 19, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Disposal of pending enforcement/adjudication proceedings under SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018 upon payment of settlement amounts without admission or denial of findings.

## Compliance Requirements

FPIs must ensure timely disclosures of share class additions within the stipulated seven working days and maintain complete transparency regarding beneficial ownership.

## Important Dates

- Settlement Order Date: September 1, 2026
- Payment Remitted: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory compliance and disclosure standards for Foreign Portfolio Investors operating in the Indian securities market.
