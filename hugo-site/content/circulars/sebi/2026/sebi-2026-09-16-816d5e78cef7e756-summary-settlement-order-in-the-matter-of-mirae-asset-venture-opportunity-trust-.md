## Summary

SEBI passed a summary settlement order in the matter of Mirae Asset Venture Opportunity Trust AIF, its manager Mirae Asset Venture Investments (India) Private Limited, and several key managerial personnel. The proceedings stemmed from the investment of ₹128.18 crore (representing 38.35% of investable funds as of June 30, 2023) in a single investee company, Supermarket Grocery Supplies Private Limited, thereby exceeding the 25% permissible limit under AIF Regulations.

## Key Points

- Mirae Asset Late Stage Opportunities Fund exceeded the 25% investment limit in a single investee company for the period between January 01, 2023, and September 30, 2023.
- The non-compliance violated Regulation 15(1)(c) of the AIF Regulations read with Regulation 20(1) and the Code of Conduct.
- SEBI issued a Notice of Summary Settlement offering resolution upon remittance of a settlement amount of ₹12,75,000 jointly by the applicants.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of investment concentration limits for Category II Alternative Investment Funds under SEBI (Alternative Investment Funds) Regulations, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

- AIFs and their managers must ensure adherence to single investee company investment limits and promptly address any inadvertent breaches or submit to settlement proceedings as applicable.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: June 09, 2026
- Order Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The settlement resolves the pending enforcement proceedings against the fund, manager, and key managerial personnel without escalating into formal adjudication or penalties beyond the agreed settlement amount.
