## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7699 of 2024 drawn against M Sasikala in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7699 of 2024.
- Defaulter/Noticee: M Sasikala.
- Company: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of outstanding dues, penalties, and interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and other entities are required to freeze accounts, lockers, and assets of the noticee as specified in the attachment notice and prevent any unauthorized transfer or debit.

## Important Dates

- Date of Attachment Notice: October 13, 2026
- Original Recovery Certificate: RC No. 7699 of 2024

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures taken by SEBI against entities and individuals involved in financial irregularities and non-payment of penalties.
