## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding multiple Research Analysts who failed to pay their mandatory renewal fees in accordance with the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014 and the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008. The order initiates actions against registered research analysts who have defaulted on keeping their registration in force.

## Key Points

- Noticees include 25 registered Research Analysts and corporate entities (such as Jhaveri Securities Ltd., Karvy Stock Broking Limited, and various individual analysts) who failed to pay renewal fees.
- Under Clause 3 of the Second Schedule to the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, registered Research Analysts must pay renewal fees every five years within three months before expiry.
- Proceedings are initiated under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Regulation 30A of SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of statutory renewal fee obligations for registered Research Analysts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Registered Research Analysts must ensure timely payment of renewal fees every five years prior to the expiry of the current registration period to avoid regulatory action.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened scrutiny and potential suspension or cancellation of registration for non-compliant Research Analysts, affecting their ability to provide research services.
