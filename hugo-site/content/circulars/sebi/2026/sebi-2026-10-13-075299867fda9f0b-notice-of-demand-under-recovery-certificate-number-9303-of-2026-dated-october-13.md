## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9303 of 2026 issued to Mr. Aviral Saxena (PAN: KVYPS1652D). This action stems from investigations into market manipulation using social media in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice of Demand issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9303 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Mr. Aviral Saxena (PAN: KVYPS1652D).
- Underlying Matter: Investigation into market manipulation using social media in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts arising from market manipulation.

## Compliance Requirements

The noticee is required to pay the demanded amount immediately under the recovery certificate, failing which further attachment of bank accounts, movable/immovable properties, and other coercive recovery measures will be enforced.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice of Demand: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's rigorous enforcement and recovery mechanisms against individuals engaging in social media-driven market manipulation and pump-and-dump schemes in listed scrips like Moksh Ornaments Limited.
