## Summary

The 215th meeting of the SEBI Board held on September 24, 2026, approved a comprehensive review of the SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2020, introducing the new SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2026. The key objectives are the development of the Portfolio Management Services (PMS) industry, ease of compliance, consolidation of regulatory provisions, and removal of redundant provisions.

## Key Points

- Approval of SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2026 in supersession of the 2020 regulations.
- Permitting investment in IPOs and primary market issuance in debt market.
- Enabling investment up to 10% of client AUM in investment-grade, non-convertible, unlisted debt securities under Discretionary PMS (DPMS) with client consent.
- Permitting greater flexibility for investment in Exchange Traded Derivatives up to 1.25 times of client’s AUM.
- Enabling investment in Foreign Securities under DPMS and Non-Discretionary PMS (NDPMS).
- Introduction of Portfolio Managers Route for Investing in Mutual Fund units (PRIM) with minimum ticket size of INR 25 Lakh.
- Permitting Eligible Fund Managers (EFM) and introducing Independent Fund Managers (IFM).

## Regulatory Changes

- Supersession of SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2020 by the new 2026 regulations.
- Expansion of permissible asset classes: IPOs, primary debt issuances, unlisted debt securities (up to 10% AUM), exchange-traded derivatives (up to 1.25x AUM), and foreign securities.
- Introduction of PRIM framework with a net worth requirement of INR 2 crore, fixed management fee capped at 1%, and specific qualification requirements for Principal Officers.
- Clear liability framework where registered portfolio managers hold full responsibility for activities of Independent Fund Managers (IFMs).

## Compliance Requirements

- Portfolio managers must adhere to the new structural, net worth, and qualification guidelines under the 2026 regulations.
- Compliance with prudential caps (e.g., 25% cap on investments in affiliated/group/associate AMCs under PRIM).
- Segregation of activities and clients between Mutual Fund Distributors (MFD) and PRIM for all clients except accredited investors.

## Important Dates

- Approved in the SEBI Board Meeting dated September 24, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

These comprehensive regulatory changes significantly enhance the operational flexibility and product offerings of Portfolio Managers in India, fostering industry growth while reinforcing investor protection and ease of compliance.
