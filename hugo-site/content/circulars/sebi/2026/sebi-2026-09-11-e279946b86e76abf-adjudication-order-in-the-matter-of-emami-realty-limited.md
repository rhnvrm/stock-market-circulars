## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) issued an adjudication order in the matter of Emami Realty Limited concerning alleged misstatements in financial statements and non-disclosures of related party transactions for the financial years FY14 to FY25.

## Key Points

- Emami Realty Limited (formerly Emami Infrastructure Limited) is listed on BSE and NSE.
- SEBI conducted an investigation into alleged financial statement misstatements and violations of the SEBI Act, SCRA, and LODR Regulations during the Investigation Period (FY14 to FY25).
- Allegations include non-disclosures regarding related entities such as Emami Agrotech Limited under Clause 32 of the Listing Agreement and Accounting Standard (Ind AS 18).
- Violations were also alleged under Regulation 23(2) of LODR Regulations and other provisions relating to corporate governance and disclosures.

## Regulatory Changes

[Not Applicable - Adjudication Order enforcing existing regulations]

## Compliance Requirements

Listed entities must ensure strict compliance with related party disclosure norms under Listing Agreements, Ind AS, and LODR Regulations, avoiding misstatements in financial statements.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 2026
- Investigation Period: FY14 to FY25

## Impact Assessment

 Reinforces regulatory scrutiny on corporate governance, accurate financial reporting, and transparent related party disclosures for listed companies.
