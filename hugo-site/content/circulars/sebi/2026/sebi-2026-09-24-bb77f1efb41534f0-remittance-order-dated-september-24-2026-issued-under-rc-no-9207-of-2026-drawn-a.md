## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated September 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9207 of 2026 drawn against Rathod Mahendrkumar. This action relates to investigations and recovery proceedings regarding trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9207 of 2026.
- Noticee/Defaulter: Rathod Mahendrkumar.
- Matter: Trading activities and price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and garnishees are required to remit funds as directed under the remittance order towards the recovery certificate dues.

## Important Dates

- Date of Remittance Order: September 24, 2026
- Recovery Certificate No.: RC No. 9207 of 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects rigorous regulatory enforcement and continuous recovery actions by SEBI against entities involved in market irregularities and price manipulation in DU Digital Global Limited.
