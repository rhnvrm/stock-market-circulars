## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order regarding the BAO Ltd. Group, involving BAO Value Fund, Sparrow Asia Diversified Opportunities Fund, and Davos International Fund, for breaching the 10% FPI investment limit in Lerthai Finance Limited.

## Key Points

- NSDL reported that aggregate holdings of FPI investor groups breached the 10% investment limit of paid-up equity capital in Lerthai Finance Limited (ISIN: INE347D01011).
- Noticees involved: BAO Value Fund, Sparrow Asia Diversified Opportunities Fund, and Davos International Fund.
- Proceedings initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Rule 5 of SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995.
- Alleged violations include provisions under FPI Regulations (2014 and 2019), SEBI Master Circular dated May 30, 2024, June 05, 2024, and Code of Conduct violations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement action under FPI Regulations and SEBI Master Circulars for investment limit breaches and code of conduct non-compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

- FPIs must strictly monitor aggregate investment limits (10% threshold) in investee companies and adhere to disclosure and reporting norms under FPI Regulations and SEBI circulars.

## Important Dates

- Appointment of Adjudicating Officer: April 21, 2026.
- Order issued in September 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights strict regulatory monitoring by SEBI and NSDL regarding FPI investment ceiling compliance and adherence to FPI regulations.
