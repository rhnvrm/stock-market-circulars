## Summary

 Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a notice regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 8899 of 2025 dated September 15, 2025, issued to Spectrum Technoprojects Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAOCS7016F) in the matter of dealing in Illiquid Stock Options at BSE. 

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 8899 of 2025 dated September 15, 2025.
- Defaulter: Spectrum Technoprojects Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAOCS7016F).
- Matter: Dealing in Illiquid Stock Options at BSE.
- Action: Completion / satisfaction of recovery proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties/dues related to illegal/illiquid stock options trading.

## Compliance Requirements

Lifting of restrictions or noting of recovery completion for the entity as specified in the certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate: September 15, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Reflects completion of recovery proceedings against entities involved in historical illiquid stock options trading cases at BSE.
