## Summary

SEBI has issued a remittance order under Recovery Certificate No. 9166 of 2026 against Manoj Rameshbhai Solanki (PAN: GEMPS6325N) in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Recovery proceedings initiated via Recovery Certificate No. 9166 of 2026.
- Targets Manoj Rameshbhai Solanki (PAN: GEMPS6325N).
- Relates to irregularities in trading activities within the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Remittance order directs the transfer/remittance of attached funds/assets towards dues owed to SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

[None - Enforcement Action]

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulters and associated garnishees are required to comply with the remittance directions and transfer specified amounts to SEBI's recovery account.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enforces compliance and recovery of penalties/dues arising from illegal or irregular trading practices, upholding market integrity.
