## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated October 12, 2026 (Certificate No. 9301 of 2026) under Section 28A of the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992 read with Section 222 of the Income-tax Act, 1961 against Amesh Surajlal Jaiswal (PAN: AFZPJ0067Q). The total demand amounts to Rs. 20,81,000/-, which includes a penalty of Rs. 20,00,000/- imposed by the Adjudicating Officer on July 30, 2026, interest of Rs. 80,000/-, and recovery costs of Rs. 1,000/- in the matter of market manipulation using social media in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited.

## Key Points

- Entity: Amesh Surajlal Jaiswal (PAN: AFZPJ0067Q), with addresses in Pune and Hingoli.
- Total Dues: Rs. 20,81,000/- comprising Rs. 20,00,000/- penalty, Rs. 80,000/- interest, and Rs. 1,000/- recovery cost.
- Underlying Matter: Market manipulation using social media in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited (Adjudication Order No. JS/YK/2026-27/32530-32542 dated July 30, 2026).
- Payment Deadline: Within 15 days of receipt of the notice.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is an enforcement and recovery action under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

The notice requires the entity to pay the total sum of Rs. 20,81,000/- within 15 days via direct credit through EFT/NEFT/RTGS to ICICI Bank (A/c No. SEBIRRDPEN9301, IFSC: ICIC0000106) or via the online payment facility on SEBI's portal. Failure to pay will result in recovery actions including property attachment, bank account attachment, and potential arrest.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: October 12, 2026
- Original Order Date: July 30, 2026
- Payment Due: Within 15 days of notice receipt

## Impact Assessment

Individual recovery action against a specific entity involved in market manipulation; no broad market-wide operational or compliance impact.
