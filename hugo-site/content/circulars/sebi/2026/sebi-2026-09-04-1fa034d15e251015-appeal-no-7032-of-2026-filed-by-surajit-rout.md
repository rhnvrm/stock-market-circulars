## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7032 of 2026 filed by Surajit Rout under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against SEBI's CPIO response regarding Grievance No. DOEAF/E/2026/0000528.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details on the forwarding of his grievance, status of refund/interest/compensation claims, and communications with ECG Trade.
- SEBI's CPIO claimed exemption under Section 8(1)(a) of the RTI Act for internal functioning and strategic procedures.
- SEBI maintained that examinations and investigations are conducted confidentially and neither confirmed nor denied any ongoing examination.
- Query relating to correspondence with ECG Trade was deemed vague and not specific under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
