## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 21, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9265 of 2026 drawn against Kavita Shah. This action forms part of recovery and enforcement proceedings in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited to realize outstanding dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9265 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Kavita Shah.
- Related Scrip / Entity: Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries are required to freeze accounts, demat accounts, and other assets of the noticee as specified in the attachment notice to prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities and individuals involved in regulatory breaches concerning Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
