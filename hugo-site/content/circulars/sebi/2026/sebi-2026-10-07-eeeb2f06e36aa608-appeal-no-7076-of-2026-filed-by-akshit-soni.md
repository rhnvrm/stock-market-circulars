## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7076 of 2026 filed by Mr. Akshit Soni under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against SEBI's CPIO response regarding a complaint submitted against Vikas Lifecare Limited and its promoter group for alleged violations of securities laws.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought certified copies of Action Taken Reports, file notings, movement sheets, daily progress reports, and communications related to a complaint against Vikas Lifecare Limited and its promoters.
- The CPIO denied the information under Section 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act, citing fiduciary relationship and absence of larger public interest.
- SEBI stated that it neither confirms nor denies the existence of examination in any specific matter until enforcement actions culminate in public orders.

No direct operational or market impact; reinforces SEBI's stance on maintaining confidentiality of ongoing examinations and third-party information under fiduciary exemptions of the RTI Act.
