## Summary

This order by the Appellate Authority under the Right to Information Act, 2005, addresses Appeal No. 7096 of 2026 filed by Mukesh Rajendra Parikh against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant sought certified copies of records, Action Taken Reports (ATRs), annexures, and email correspondence related to SCORES Complaint No. SEBIE/MH26/MUMB/031292/1 handled by CDSL Ventures Limited (CVL).

## Key Points

- The appellant sought certified copies of ATRs submitted by CDSL Ventures Limited, first-level reviews, email communications with SEBI officers, dashboard entries, and final disposal records.
- The respondent (CPIO, SEBI) stated that the complaint handling process on SCORES is electronic, and the ATR contains all relevant processing information.
- SEBI clarified that no separate file notings are maintained outside the system and all actions and observations are recorded in the action history on SCORES, accessible to complainants via login credentials.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory or policy changes; this is an individual RTI appellate order.

## Compliance Requirements

None. Reaffirms the accessibility of electronic complaint processing history through the online SCORES dashboard.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: August 20, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 15, 2026
- Appeal Date: September 18, 2026 (Received September 21, 2026)
- Complaint Final Disposal Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No direct operational or market impact. Clarifies the availability of electronic audit trails and ATRs within the SCORES platform for investor grievances.
