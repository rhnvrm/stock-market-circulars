## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand (Certificate No. 9304 of 2026) under section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, and section 222 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, against Jalaj Agrawal (PAN: AUDPA0226H) for a total sum of Rs. 15,61,000/-. The dues comprise a penalty of Rs. 15,00,000/- imposed by the Adjudicating Officer on July 30, 2026, along with interest of Rs. 60,000/- and recovery costs of Rs. 1,000/-, in the matter of market manipulation using social media in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited.

## Key Points

- **Notice Details:** Certificate No. 9304 of 2026 dated October 14, 2026.
- **Entity:** Jalaj Agrawal (PAN: AUDPA0226H).
- **Total Dues:** Rs. 15,61,000/- (Penalty: Rs. 15,00,000/-, Interest: Rs. 60,000/-, Recovery Cost: Rs. 1,000/-).
- **Matter:** Market manipulation using social media in the scrip of Moksh Ornaments Limited.
- **Payment Window:** Within 15 days of receipt of the notice via EFT/NEFT/RTGS or SEBI online payment portal.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement and recovery action under existing statutory provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

The notice requires payment of Rs. 15,61,000/- within 15 days of receipt. Failure to pay will result in coercive recovery measures, including attachment and sale of movable and immovable properties, bank account attachment, or arrest and detention.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** July 30, 2026
- **Notice Date:** October 14, 2026
- **Payment Deadline:** Within 15 days of receipt of the notice

## Impact Assessment

Individual recovery action with no broader systemic impact on the market, reinforcing SEBI's strict stance against market manipulation and social media pump-and-dump schemes.
