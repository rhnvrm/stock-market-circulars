## Summary

SEBI has issued a release order for Recovery Certificate No. 9155 of 2026 against M/s Chandrima Mercantiles Limited and 17 other individuals/entities in the matter of price and volume manipulation in the scrip of Quasar India Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery proceedings initiated under Recovery Certificate No. 9155 of 2026.
- Involves M/s Chandrima Mercantiles Limited along with 17 individuals including Pranav Kamleshkumar Trivedi, Nayan Mahendrabhai Thakkar, and others.
- Arises from past adjudication penalties imposed for orchestrating circular and synchronized trades to manipulate stock prices.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed under SEBI Act recovery provisions for unpaid monetary penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulters are required to clear outstanding dues and penalties as specified in the recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Order Date / Issuance: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight and strict enforcement against market manipulation and non-payment of statutory penalties.
