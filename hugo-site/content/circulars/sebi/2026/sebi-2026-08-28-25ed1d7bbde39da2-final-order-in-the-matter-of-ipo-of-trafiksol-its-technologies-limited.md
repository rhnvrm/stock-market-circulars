## Summary

SEBI issued a final order concerning irregularities, misrepresentations, and potential fund diversion in the Initial Public Offer (IPO) of Trafiksol ITS Technologies Limited on the SME Platform of BSE. The IPO raised ₹44.87 crores but faced severe scrutiny post-allotment regarding discrepancies in the proposed use of proceeds for procuring software and vendor capability.

## Key Points

- Trafiksol ITS Technologies Limited filed a DRHP and raised ₹44.87 crores through its SME IPO, oversubscribed 345.65 times.
- Promoters included Mr. Jitendra Narayan Das (CMD) and Ms. Poonam Das (Whole-Time Director).
- Post-allotment complaints and investigations revealed discrepancies regarding the utilization of ₹17.70 crores proposed for software procurement from a third-party vendor (Oasis Corpcare Pvt. Ltd.).
- Ekadrisht Capital Private Limited acted as the sole Merchant Banker / Book Running Lead Manager.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities involved are subject to directions and restrictions as specified under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1) and 11B(2) of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Important Dates

- DRHP Filing Date: May 31, 2024
- RHP Filing Date: September 04, 2024
- Final Prospectus Filing Date: September 13, 2024
- IPO Subscription Period: September 10, 2024 to September 12, 2024
- Allotment Date: September 16, 2024

## Impact Assessment

Enhances regulatory scrutiny on SME IPO disclosures, merchant banker due diligence, and utilization of issue proceeds.
