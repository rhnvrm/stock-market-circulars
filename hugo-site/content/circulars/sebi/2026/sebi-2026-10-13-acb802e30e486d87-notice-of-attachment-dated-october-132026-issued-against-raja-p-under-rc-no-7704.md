## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7704 of 2024 drawn against Raja P. This action relates to recovery proceedings and enforcement measures in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 7704 of 2024.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Raja P.
- Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to freeze accounts and assets as specified in the attachment notice and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against entities and individuals involved in market irregularities in Arvind Remedies Ltd.
