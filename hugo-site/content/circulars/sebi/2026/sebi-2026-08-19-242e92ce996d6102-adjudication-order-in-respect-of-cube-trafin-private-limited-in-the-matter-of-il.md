## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order regarding Cube Trafin Private Limited for alleged non-genuine trades and creation of artificial volume in the stock options segment of BSE between April 1, 2014, and September 30, 2015.

## Key Points

- SEBI observed large-scale reversal of trades in stock options at BSE, leading to artificial volumes.
- The Noticee (Cube Trafin Private Limited) allegedly executed 5 non-genuine trades across 2 stock options contracts, resulting in an artificial volume of 10,16,500 units.
- Proceedings were initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, and Rule 5 of the Adjudication Rules for alleged violations of PFUTP Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of PFUTP Regulations against entities engaging in fraudulent and manipulative illiquid stock options trading.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must avoid non-genuine trades, reversal trades, and any activities creating artificial volume or misleading appearances of trading in securities.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: April 1, 2014, to September 30, 2015.
- Show Cause Notice Date: August 11, 2022.
- Date of Appointment of Current Adjudicating Officer: June 1, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight and penal actions against entities involved in historical illiquid stock options misuse on stock exchanges.
