## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding the completion/release of Recovery Certificate No. 3858 of 2021 drawn against Mindvision Capital Limited (PAN: AAACK8154A). This order signifies the culmination or satisfaction of recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI's Recovery Officer.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 3858 of 2021.
- Entity Name: Mindvision Capital Limited.
- PAN: AAACK8154A.
- Nature of Order: Completion / Release order under SEBI recovery proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of dues, penalties, or interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries are notified of the completion/release of recovery proceedings against the specified entity.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects the resolution and closure of regulatory recovery proceedings for Mindvision Capital Limited.
