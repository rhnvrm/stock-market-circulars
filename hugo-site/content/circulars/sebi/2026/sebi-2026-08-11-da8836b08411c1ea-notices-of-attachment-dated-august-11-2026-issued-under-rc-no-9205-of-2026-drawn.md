## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 11, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9205 of 2026 drawn against Nayan Mahendrabhai Thakkar. This action relates to investigations into trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 9205 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Nayan Mahendrabhai Thakkar.
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues/penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to freeze accounts and assets as specified in the attachment notice and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against entities and individuals involved in market irregularities in DU Digital Global Limited.
