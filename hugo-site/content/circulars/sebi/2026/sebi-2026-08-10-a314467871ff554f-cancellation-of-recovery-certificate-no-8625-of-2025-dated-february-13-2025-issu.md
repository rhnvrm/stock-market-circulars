## Summary

On August 10, 2026, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) officially cancelled Recovery Certificate No. 8625 of 2025 (originally issued on February 13, 2025) against Sudhakar Anchan (PAN: AHWPA1468K) in the matter of Sharepro Services (I) Pvt. Ltd. This cancellation brings to an end the recovery and attachment proceedings initiated against Mr. Anchan to recover outstanding dues of ₹3,49,000.

## Key Points

- **Cancellation Order:** Recovery Certificate No. 8625 of 2025 has been formally cancelled by the Recovery Officer of SEBI.
- **Recovery Amount:** The certificate was issued to recover a total sum of ₹3,49,000, which consisted of a ₹3,00,000 penalty (imposed via Adjudication Order dated November 17, 2023, for failure to comply with SEBI summons), ₹48,000 in interest, and ₹1,000 in recovery costs.
- **Asset Unfreezing:** Asset attachment proceedings (Attachment Proceeding No. 14548 of 2025) initiated on April 15, 2025, which ordered the freezing of bank accounts and lockers of the defaulter, stand withdrawn and terminated.
- **Legal History:** Sudhakar Anchan had contested the recovery proceedings before the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) in Appeal No. 201 of 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes are introduced by this order. This is a case-specific administrative enforcement cancellation.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Banks & Financial Intermediaries:** Banks and other institutions that held or attached accounts/lockers belonging to Sudhakar Anchan under Attachment Proceeding No. 14548 of 2025 must release and unfreeze the assets.
- **No active compliance requirements** are imposed on the broader market or listed entities.

## Important Dates

- **November 17, 2023:** SEBI Adjudication Order imposing the penalty.
- **February 13, 2025:** Issuance of Recovery Certificate No. 8625 of 2025.
- **April 15, 2025:** Attachment Notice issued to freeze bank accounts and lockers.
- **August 10, 2026:** Cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 8625 of 2025.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Nil. The cancellation concerns an individual enforcement case and has no impact on general stock market operations or listing requirements.
- **Operational Impact:** Low. Confined to financial institutions releasing the individual's frozen accounts.
