## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate No. RC9282 of 2026 in respect of Atit Commercial Company Private Limited (transferee company of Ashirwad Tie Up Pvt. Ltd.) (PAN: AACCA5249E) in the matter of Illiquid Stock Options. The recovery proceeding follows adjudication orders penalizing non-genuine reversal trades and artificial volume generation in the stock options segment of BSE.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: RC9282 of 2026
- Noticee / Defaulter: Atit Commercial Company Private Limited (Transferee company of Ashirwad Tie Up Pvt. Ltd.)
- PAN: AACCA5249E
- Matter: Trading in Illiquid Stock Options at BSE (execution of non-genuine reversal trades)
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties imposed for violation of SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and transferee companies are required to fulfill outstanding monetary penalties and dues levied by SEBI. Non-compliance results in attachment of bank accounts, properties, and other coercive recovery measures.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice of Demand: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's continued rigorous enforcement and recovery pursuit in legacy illiquid stock options cases, enforcing liabilities onto transferee/merged entities.
