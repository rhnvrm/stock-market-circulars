## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has officially recorded the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 9251 of 2026 issued to Neha Aggarwal in the matter of trading-based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd. Consequently, all previous asset and bank account attachments related to this certificate have been released.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9251 of 2026 was originally issued on July 27, 2026, against Neha Aggarwal (PAN: AQDPA1749D).
- Proceedings stem from SEBI's investigation into fraudulent stock recommendations disseminated via YouTube concerning Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Full dues/penalties under the recovery certificate have been satisfied, prompting SEBI to issue a release order for the attached bank accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement and recovery completion action.

## Compliance Requirements

- Defaulter and associated financial institutions are notified of the lifting of asset and account attachments.

## Important Dates

- July 27, 2026: Issuance of Recovery Certificate No. 9251 of 2026.
- September 01, 2026: Completion of recovery and release of attachments.

## Impact Assessment

- Demonstrates effective recovery and enforcement closure by SEBI in social media stock manipulation cases, with no broader systemic market impact.
