## Summary

SEBI has relaxed the requirement of mandatory merchant banker appointment for debt securities and non-convertible redeemable preference shares issued through private placement by certain eligible listed issuers. This measure aims to enhance the ease of issuing debt and expand access of high-rated securities to retail investors.

## Key Points

- Clause 1.3 of Chapter V of the SEBI NCS Master Circular dated October 15, 2025, has been revised.
- Issuers may choose not to appoint a Merchant Banker subject to the strict fulfillment of specified eligibility conditions.
- Eligible issuers must be regulated by a financial sector regulator (SEBI, RBI, IRDAI, or PFRDA), listed for at least one year with no pending fines/penalties under LODR Regulations, have no repayment defaults in the last three financial years and current financial year, issue unsubordinated/senior secured debt (or unsecured for CPSEs/PSUs/Statutory Bodies), and maintain a credit rating of at least AA-.

## Regulatory Changes

- Para 1.3 of the NCS Master Circular is amended to allow qualifying issuers to bypass appointing a merchant banker for private placements of debt or non-convertible redeemable preference shares, provided statutory auditor certificates and stock exchange confirmations are obtained.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers must obtain statutory auditor certification confirming no defaults in debt repayment, commercial papers, deposits, or loans over the last three financial years and current financial year.
- Recognized stock exchanges must confirm compliance with LODR regulations and absence of pending fines at the time of granting in-principle approval.
- The issued debt security must be rated at least AA-.

## Important Dates

- Effective date: October 07, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Lowers issuance costs and streamlines private placement procedures for high-credit-quality and compliant listed corporations and public sector enterprises, while ensuring adequate safeguard mechanisms remain intact.
