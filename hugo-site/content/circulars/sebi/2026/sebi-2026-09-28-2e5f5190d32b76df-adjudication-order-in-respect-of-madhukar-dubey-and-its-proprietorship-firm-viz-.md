## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order following the Hon'ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated September 2, 2025, which remitted the matter back to the Adjudicating Officer. The case involves Madhukar Dubey and his proprietorship firm, Magnum Industrial Corporation, regarding violations in the IPO of Channel Nine Entertainment Ltd. The Noticee contended that his KYC credentials were misused by his employer, Vijay Jindal, without his awareness.

## Key Points

- An earlier Adjudication Order dated February 28, 2022, imposed a joint and several monetary penalty of Rs. 8,00,000/- on thirteen Noticees under Section 15HA of the SEBI Act, 1992 for violations of PFUTP Regulations in funding the IPO of Channel Nine Entertainment Ltd.
- The appellant (Noticee 2) argued before SAT that he worked as a driver under Vijay Jindal, who misused his credentials, and that he was unaware of the original proceedings.
- Noticee noted a similar precedent involving the IPO of HPC Biosciences Ltd. where SAT also remanded the matter to SEBI.
- SAT vide order dated September 2, 2025, remitted the matter to grant the appellant an opportunity to contest the case.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes introduced; this is an adjudicatory proceeding enforcing existing provisions under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992 and SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003.

## Compliance Requirements

Market participants and individuals must rigorously safeguard their KYC credentials, PAN, and identity documents to prevent unauthorized misuse by employers or third parties in fraudulent securities market operations.

## Important Dates

- February 28, 2022: Original Adjudication Order passed.
- September 2, 2025: SAT Order remitting the matter to the Adjudicating Officer.
- September 29, 2025: Directed appearance date before the Adjudicating Officer.

## Impact Assessment

This adjudication order is specific to the named individual noticee and has no broader regulatory or operational impact on general market participants.
