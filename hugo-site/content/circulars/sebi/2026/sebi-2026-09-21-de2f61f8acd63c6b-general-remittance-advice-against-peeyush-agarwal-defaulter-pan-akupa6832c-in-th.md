## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Advice under Recovery Certificate No. 9256 of 2026 against Peeyush Agarwal (PAN: AKUPA6832C) in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited. This is part of ongoing recovery and enforcement proceedings to collect dues and penalties stemming from previous regulatory orders.

## Key Points

- Issued by SEBI regarding recovery proceedings against Peeyush Agarwal (PAN: AKUPA6832C).
- Relates to Recovery Certificate No. 9256 of 2026 under the broader Sadhna Broadcast Limited market manipulation case.
- Focuses on the collection and remittance of dues ordered by regulatory enforcement.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery certificate procedures through administrative remittance advice.

## Compliance Requirements

- The named defaulter is required to remit the specified dues immediately to avoid further coercive attachment of bank accounts, demat accounts, and other financial assets.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures regulatory compliance and recovery of illegal gains/penalties in a prominent stock manipulation case, reinforcing investor protection mechanisms.
