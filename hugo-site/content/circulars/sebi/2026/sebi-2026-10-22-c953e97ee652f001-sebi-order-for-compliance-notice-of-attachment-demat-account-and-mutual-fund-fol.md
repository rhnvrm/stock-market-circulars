## Summary

SEBI has issued an order and notice of attachment dated October 22, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7137 of 2023 against Karan Subhashchandra Ravde in the matter of India Infraspace Limited. The order mandates the attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folios to recover outstanding dues.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 7137 of 2023
- Noticee / Defaulter: Karan Subhashchandra Ravde
- Matter: India Infraspace Limited
- Action: Notice of Attachment of Demat Accounts and Mutual Fund Folio(s)
- Issued by: Recovery Officer, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions initiated pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of penalties, disgorgement amounts, or dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Depositories (NSDL, CDSL) and Asset Management Companies (AMCs) / Mutual Funds are required to freeze and attach the demat accounts and mutual fund folios standing in the name of the noticee and prohibit any debits, transfers, or disposal of securities/units.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Notice: October 22, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Year: 2023 (RC No. 7137 of 2023)

## Impact Assessment

Ensures enforcement compliance and asset freezing for recovery of dues, safeguarding market integrity and regulatory compliance regarding listed scrips like India Infraspace Limited.
