## Summary

SEBI has issued Recovery Certificate No. 9254 of 2026 dated July 27, 2026, to Pooja Aggarwal (PAN: AGMPA6216C) as a defaulter in the matter of trading-based stock recommendations using social media (YouTube) in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9254 of 2026
- Date of Recovery Certificate: July 27, 2026
- Defaulter: Pooja Aggarwal (PAN: AGMPA6216C)
- Matter: Trading-based stock recommendations using social media (YouTube)
- Scrip: Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries must comply with recovery notices, freeze specified accounts, and prevent asset alienation as directed by SEBI's Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate: July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement by SEBI against entities involved in pump-and-dump schemes and unauthorized stock recommendations via social media channels.
