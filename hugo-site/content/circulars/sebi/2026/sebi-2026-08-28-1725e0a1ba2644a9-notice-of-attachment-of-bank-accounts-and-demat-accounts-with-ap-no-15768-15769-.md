## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice regarding the attachment of bank accounts and demat accounts with AP No. 15768 & 15769 of 2026 dated August 28, 2026, against Mr. Dheeraj Wadhawan (PAN: AAOPW4517G) as a defaulter in the matter of Dewan Housing Finance Corporation Limited (DHFL) under Recovery Certificate No. 9214 of 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice of attachment issued against Mr. Dheeraj Wadhawan.
- Covers bank accounts and demat accounts under AP No. 15768 & 15769 of 2026.
- Issued pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 9214 of 2026.
- Relates to proceedings involving Dewan Housing Finance Corporation Limited (DHFL).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed by SEBI under relevant statutory recovery certificates to secure dues from defaulters.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and depositories are required to comply with the attachment orders regarding the specified bank and demat accounts.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricts access and operations on the specified bank and demat accounts of the defaulter to facilitate recovery of dues in accordance with regulatory mandates.
