## Summary

SEBI has issued a settlement order in respect of True North Enterprise Private Limited (investment manager of True North Fund III-A) regarding the delayed winding up of the scheme and alleged violation of Regulation 23(1)(a) read with Regulation 24(2) of the VCF Regulations, 1996.

## Key Points

- True North Fund III-A is a scheme registered under the SEBI (Venture Capital Funds) Regulations, 1996.
- The tenure of the scheme ended on October 20, 2017, and winding up ought to have been completed within three months by January 20, 2018.
- The applicant extended the tenure beyond permissible limits under the Placement Memorandum and completed the winding up on March 29, 2025, resulting in a delay of around 7 years and 2 months.
- The applicant filed a suo-motu settlement application, and the Internal Committee of SEBI recommended a settlement amount of ₹10,87,500/-.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Enforcement and settlement action under existing VCF Regulations).

## Compliance Requirements

- Payment of the recommended settlement amount as per the terms of the Settlement Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Scheme tenure ended: October 20, 2017
- Winding up completed: March 29, 2025
- IC Meeting: April 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative and financial settlement for past compliance delays without broader market impact.
