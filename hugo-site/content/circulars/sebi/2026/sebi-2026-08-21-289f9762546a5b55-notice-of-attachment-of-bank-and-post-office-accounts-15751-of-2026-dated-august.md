## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 21, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9251 of 2026 against Neha Aggarwal (PAN: AQDPA1749D). The action is taken in the matter of trading-based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9251 of 2026.
- Defaulter: Neha Aggarwal (PAN: AQDPA1749D).
- Scrip: Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Matter: Trading-based stock recommendations using social media (YouTube).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, post offices, and financial institutions are directed to attach and freeze all accounts, lockers, and holdings of the noticee to prevent any withdrawal or transfer of funds.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous regulatory enforcement against entities involved in pump-and-dump schemes or unauthorized stock recommendations via social media platforms.
