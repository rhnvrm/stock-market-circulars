## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order under Certificate No. 9260 of 2026 regarding Naresh Kumar (PAN: ASPPK8654F), designated as a defaulter in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited, releasing the person from previous attachment/recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued under Certificate No. 9260 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Naresh Kumar (PAN: ASPPK8654F).
- Matter: Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Lifting of attachment and enforcement actions pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are notified to lift freezes on accounts and assets as specified in the release order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reversal of recovery restrictions for the specified individual in Sadhna Broadcast Limited, restoring normal financial operations.
