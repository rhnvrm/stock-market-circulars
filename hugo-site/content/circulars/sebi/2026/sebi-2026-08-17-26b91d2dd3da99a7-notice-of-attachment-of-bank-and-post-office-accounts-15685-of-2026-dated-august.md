## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice for the attachment of bank and post office accounts against NISHAA ANAND (PAN: AJWPA4527H) under Recovery Certificate No. 9271 of 2026 in the matter of ISO at BSE.

## Key Points

- Defaulter: NISHAA ANAND (PAN: AJWPA4527H)
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9271 of 2026
- Notice No.: 15685 of 2026
- Date: August 17, 2026
- Action: Attachment of bank and post office accounts

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and post offices are required to attach the accounts of the defaulter as per the recovery notice.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Individual enforcement action with negligible impact on broader market operations.
