## Summary

SEBI conducted an investigation into Omaxe Limited to ascertain compliance with Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) norms. The investigation revealed that Omaxe Limited allegedly utilized its own funds to finance the acquisition of its shares offered by promoters in the Offer for Sale (OFS) conducted to achieve MPS compliance, while counting these shares as public shareholding.

## Key Points

- Omaxe Limited transferred ₹46.50 crore collectively to DVM Realtors Pvt. Ltd. (DRPL) and Garv Buildtech Pvt. Ltd. (Garv).
- Funds were subsequently routed through Jeet Builders Pvt. Ltd. (JBPL) to seven first-level recipient entities before moving into stock-broking accounts or connected entities.
- The arrangement was designed to covertly fund promoter-backed share acquisitions to artificially meet MPS requirements.
