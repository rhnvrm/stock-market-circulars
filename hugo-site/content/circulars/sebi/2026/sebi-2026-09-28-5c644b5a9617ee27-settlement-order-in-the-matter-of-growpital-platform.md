## Summary

SEBI issued a settlement order in the matter of Growpital Platform regarding settlement applications filed by Nutrikosh India Private Limited (Applicant 1) and Alok Chaudhary (Applicant 2). The proceedings originated from an ex-parte interim order dated January 29, 2024, and confirmatory order dated April 26, 2024, alleging the operation of an unregistered Collective Investment Scheme (CIS) raising ₹192.88 crore. A show cause notice dated March 3, 2025, alleged that Nutrikosh aided and abetted co-noticees, acted as a conduit for transferring ₹7.03 crore, and promised assured tax-free returns. The applicants subsequently filed settlement applications on April 30, 2025, which were considered by SEBI's Internal Committee.

## Key Points

- **Entities Involved:** Nutrikosh India Private Limited (Applicant 1) and Alok Chaudhary (Applicant 2).
- **Underlying Matter:** Growpital Platform investigation into unregistered CIS raising ₹192.88 crore from the public.
- **Allegations:** Aiding and abetting fraudulent activities, acting as a conduit to transfer ₹7.03 crore, promising assured tax-free returns, and showing fake revenues.
- **Legal Violations:** Section 12A(a), (b), (c) of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Regulations 3(a), 3(b), 3(c), 3(d) and 4(1) of SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003.
- **Settlement Applications:** Filed on April 30, 2025 (Application Nos. 8545/2026 and 8546/2025).

## Regulatory Changes

- No new broad regulatory changes introduced; this is an adjudicatory settlement order under the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with terms and conditions agreed upon under the SEBI Settlement Proceedings Regulations, 2018, as recommended by SEBI's Internal Committee.

## Important Dates

- **January 29, 2024:** SEBI Interim Order passed in the matter.
- **April 26, 2024:** Confirmatory Order passed.
- **March 3, 2025:** Show Cause Notice (SCN) issued to 30 noticees including applicants.
- **April 30, 2025:** Settlement applications filed by Applicants 1 and 2.
- **February 03, 2026:** Meeting held with SEBI's Internal Committee.

## Impact Assessment

- Demonstrates SEBI's continued enforcement and settlement mechanism enforcement regarding illicit collective investment schemes and associated corporate conduits.
