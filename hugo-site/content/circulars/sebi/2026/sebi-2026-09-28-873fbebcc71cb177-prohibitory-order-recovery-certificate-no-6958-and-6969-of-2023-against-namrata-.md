## Summary

SEBI has issued Prohibitory Orders under Recovery Certificate Nos. 6958 and 6969 of 2023 against Namrata Sandeep Agrawal in the matter of Kushal Limited. The recovery proceedings are initiated to enforce earlier orders and recover outstanding dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Orders issued under Recovery Certificate Nos. 6958 and 6969 of 2023.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Namrata Sandeep Agrawal.
- Matter: Kushal Limited enforcement and recovery proceedings.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of monetary penalties and disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant entities must comply with the prohibitory orders by freezing accounts, shares, and assets of the noticee as specified in the recovery certificates.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Order / Notice: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's strict enforcement mechanisms and recovery powers against defaulters failing to pay regulatory dues.
