## Summary

SEBI has issued Notice of Attachment No. 15705 of 2026 dated August 18, 2026, for the attachment of bank and post office accounts of Sapna, Proprietor of Stallion Trading Co. (PAN: DRUPS8079D), under Recovery Certificate No. 9159 of 2026 in the matter of trading activities in the scrip of Delta Leasing and Finance Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 15705 of 2026
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9159 of 2026
- Defaulter: Sapna (Proprietor of Stallion Trading Co.)
- PAN: DRUPS8079D
- Scrip Involved: Delta Leasing and Finance Ltd.
- Action: Attachment of all bank and post office accounts.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an individual enforcement and recovery action.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and post offices are required to attach the accounts of the defaulter and prohibit any debits or transfers, subject to the recovery certificate amount.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct enforcement action restricted to the named entity's accounts with no broader systemic impact on the market.
