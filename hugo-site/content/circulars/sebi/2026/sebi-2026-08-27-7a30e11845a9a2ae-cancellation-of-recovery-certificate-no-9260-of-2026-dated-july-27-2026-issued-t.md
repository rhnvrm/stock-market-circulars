## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued an order cancelling Recovery Certificate No. 9260 of 2026, which was originally dated July 27, 2026, and issued to Naresh Kumar (PAN: ASPPK8654F) as a defaulter in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited.

## Key Points

- SEBI cancelled Recovery Certificate No. 9260 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Naresh Kumar (PAN: ASPPK8654F).
- Matter: Sadhna Broadcast Limited proceedings.
- Action entails the withdrawal of recovery proceedings and the release of previous attachments on bank accounts, demat accounts, and other assets.

## Regulatory Changes

Withdrawal of enforcement actions and recovery certificates previously issued under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and depository participants are notified to lift freezes and release attachments on the accounts of the concerned individual.

## Important Dates

- Date of Cancellation / Order: August 27, 2026
- Original Certificate Date: July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal market impact; relates specifically to the lifting of recovery proceedings and asset freezes for an individual linked to the Sadhna Broadcast Limited matter.
