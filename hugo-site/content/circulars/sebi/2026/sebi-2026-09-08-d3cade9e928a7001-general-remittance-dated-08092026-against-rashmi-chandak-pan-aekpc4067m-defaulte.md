## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance notice dated September 8, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9162 of 2026 against Rashmi Chandak (PAN: AEKPC4067M), designated as a defaulter, in the matter of ISO at BSE.

## Key Points

- General Remittance notice issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9162 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Rashmi Chandak (PAN: AEKPC4067M).
- Matter: ISO at BSE.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to comply with the remittance and attachment directions as specified in the notice, ensuring no unauthorized transfers are permitted from the defaulter's accounts.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: September 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates continuous regulatory enforcement and recovery actions by SEBI against defaulters in market operations.
