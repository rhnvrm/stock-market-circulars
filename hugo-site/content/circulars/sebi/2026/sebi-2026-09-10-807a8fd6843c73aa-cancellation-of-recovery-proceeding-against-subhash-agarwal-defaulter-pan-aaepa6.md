## Summary

SEBI has ordered the cancellation of recovery proceedings initiated against Subhash Agarwal (Defaulter) under Recovery Certificate No. 9296 of 2026 dated September 08, 2026. This action arises from the ongoing matter concerning trading-based stock recommendations using social media (YouTube) in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Recovery proceeding under Recovery Certificate No. 9296 of 2026 has been cancelled.
- The case involves unauthorized stock recommendations and market manipulation practices via YouTube regarding Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Defaulter concerned: Subhash Agarwal (PAN: AAEPA6699R).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Not applicable for general market participants.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate / Order: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Individual enforcement update with no systemic or broad market impact.
