## Summary

SEBI issued Prohibitory Order No. 1578/2026 on October 21, 2026, against S. Giridharan under Recovery Certificate No. 6598 of 2023 in the matter of Edserv Softsystems Ltd.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Order Number: 1578/2026
- Date of Order: October 21, 2026
- Noticee / Defaulter: S. Giridharan (Chairman and Managing Director of Edserv Softsystems Ltd)
- Recovery Certificate: No. 6598 of 2023
- Issued by: Recovery Officer, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures under the SEBI Act, 1992, restraining the noticee from transferring, alienating, or creating any charge on properties and bank or demat accounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and asset holders must enforce attachment and freeze accounts and assets of the noticee as directed in the recovery certificate proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and recovery mechanisms by SEBI against individuals and promoters failing to pay dues or penalties related to market violations.
