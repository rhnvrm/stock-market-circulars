## Summary

An RTI appeal filed by Swarnali Basu against the CPIO, SEBI, regarding details and claims for an old UTI scheme certificate (Rajlakshmi Unit Scheme), was dismissed by the Appellate Authority as time-barred under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act due to delayed filing without a condonation request. On merits, the authority upheld the CPIO's stance that queries seeking clarification or opinion do not qualify as 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, while pointing the appellant to the MITRA portal for tracing unclaimed mutual fund folios.

## Key Points

- The first appeal was filed beyond the statutory 30-day window without any request or reasons provided for condoning the delay.
- Queries seeking help to claim benefits or details regarding old physical mutual fund certificates are in the nature of clarification/opinion and fall outside Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- Investors can utilize the MITRA (Mutual Fund Investment Tracing and Retrieval Assistant) Portal to trace inactive and unclaimed mutual fund folios.
