## Summary

SEBI has issued a corrigendum to its settlement order dated August 12, 2026 (reference no. SO/2026-27/PSD/21-24) regarding summary settlement applications filed in the matter of 3One4 Capital Continuum IE.

## Key Points

- Correction pertains to the PAN of 3One4 Capital Trust listed in the first table at page 1 of the original settlement order.
- The correct PAN shall be read as “AAATZ1910G”.
- A copy of the corrigendum is to be sent to the applicants and published on the SEBI website.

## Regulatory Changes

No substantive regulatory changes; this is an administrative rectification of typographical/clerical details in an existing settlement order.

## Compliance Requirements

No additional compliance requirements for market participants; applicable only to the involved applicants.

## Important Dates

- Corrigendum Issued: October 2026
- Original Settlement Order Date: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Negligible market impact as it is purely an administrative correction of identification details (PAN) for the settlement applicant.
