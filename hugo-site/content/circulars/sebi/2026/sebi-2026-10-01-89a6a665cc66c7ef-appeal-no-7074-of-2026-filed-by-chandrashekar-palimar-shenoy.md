## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7074 of 2026 filed by Chandrashekar Palimar Shenoy under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought certified copies of the Action Taken Report (ATR) submitted by trading member Profit Mart Securities Pvt Ltd regarding complaints lodged on SCORES concerning pre-trade alerts for rights issue shares. Noting that certified copies were not provided, the Appellate Authority remitted the application to the CPIO for de novo consideration within 30 days.

## Key Points

- Appellant filed an RTI application seeking certified copies of ATRs submitted by trading member Profit Mart Securities Pvt Ltd (INZ000004737).
- Complaints on SCORES related to failure in providing pre-trade alerts for Rights Issue shares as per NSE circular CML69345.
- Respondent previously closed three complaints as duplicates and provided uncertified downloaded copies of ATRs from SCORES.
- Appellate Authority observed that certified copies were not provided and remitted the application for de novo consideration.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a quasi-judicial order under the RTI Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

- CPIO, SEBI to reconsider the RTI application de novo and provide an appropriate response along with certified copies of ATRs within 30 days of the order date.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: October 01, 2026
- Compliance Deadline: Within 30 days from the date of receipt of the order

## Impact Assessment

Individual investor grievance redressal matter reinforcing transparency and compliance with RTI Act requirements for certified records.
