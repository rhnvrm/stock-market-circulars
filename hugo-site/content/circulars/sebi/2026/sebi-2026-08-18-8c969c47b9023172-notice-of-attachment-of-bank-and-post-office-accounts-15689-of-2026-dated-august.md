## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment of Bank and Post Office Accounts (Notice No. 15689 of 2026) dated August 18, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 8876 of 2025 against Heena Khatoon, Proprietor of Heena Enterprises (PAN-FGXPK5721G). This action is part of recovery proceedings in the matter of investigation into the trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of Continental Seeds and Chemicals Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 8876 of 2025 (Notice No. 15689 of 2026).
- Defaulter: Heena Khatoon, Proprietor of Heena Enterprises (PAN-FGXPK5721G).
- Matter: Investigation in the trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of Continental Seeds and Chemicals Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, post offices, and financial institutions are required to attach and freeze the accounts, lockers, and holdings of the noticee to prevent unauthorized withdrawals or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects rigorous regulatory enforcement and asset attachment measures against non-compliant entities involved in market irregularities.
