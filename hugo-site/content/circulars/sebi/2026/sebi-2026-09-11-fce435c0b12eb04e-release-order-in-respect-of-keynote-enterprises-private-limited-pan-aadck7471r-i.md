## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order along with the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 7430 of 2023 in respect of Keynote Enterprises Private Limited (PAN: AADCK7471R) in the matter of TV Vision Limited.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued by SEBI concerning Recovery Certificate No. 7430 of 2023.
- Entity involved: Keynote Enterprises Private Limited (PAN: AADCK7471R).
- Context relates to enforcement and recovery proceedings in the matter of TV Vision Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Lifting of attachment/recovery measures previously ordered under Recovery Certificate No. 7430 of 2023 for the entity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Relevant financial institutions and intermediaries are notified of the cancellation of the recovery certificate and release of restrictions.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Clears the specific recovery proceedings and associated restrictions for Keynote Enterprises Private Limited in relation to the TV Vision Limited matter.
