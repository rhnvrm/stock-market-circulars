## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate Number 9298 of 2026, dated September 21, 2026, to Allied Financial Services Private Limited (PAN: AAACA2020K). The action is taken in connection with outstanding regulatory dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter Entity:** Allied Financial Services Private Limited
- **PAN:** AAACA2020K
- **Recovery Certificate Number:** 9298 of 2026
- **Date of Notice:** September 21, 2026
- **Regulatory Body:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes; this is an enforcement and recovery action directed at a specific entity for non-payment of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

The named defaulter is required to immediately pay the specified dues under the Recovery Certificate to avoid further coercive measures, such as attachment of bank accounts, properties, and demat accounts.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct impact is restricted to the defaulter entity and associated persons/directors subject to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992.
