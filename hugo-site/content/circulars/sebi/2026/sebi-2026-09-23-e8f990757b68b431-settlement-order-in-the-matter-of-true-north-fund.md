## Summary

SEBI passed a settlement order in respect of True North Enterprise Private Limited regarding the suo-motu settlement application filed for alleged violation of Regulation 23(1)(a) read with Regulation 24(2) of the SEBI (Venture Capital Funds) Regulations, 1996, relating to a delay in winding up True North Fund Scheme-B.

## Key Points

- True North Enterprise Private Limited is the Investment Manager of True North Fund Scheme B.
- The scheme's tenure and permitted extensions ended on December 19, 2014, but the winding up was completed with a delay of about 10 years, concluding distribution in March 2025.
- The applicant extended the tenure beyond limits permissible under the Placement Memorandum and failed to liquidate and distribute assets timely.
- The Internal Committee of SEBI recommended a settlement amount of ₹10,87,500/- under the Settlement Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with settlement terms including payment of the recommended settlement amount of ₹10,87,500/-.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: May 30, 2025
- IC Meeting Date: April 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is a specific settlement order with no broad market-wide operational or trading impacts.
