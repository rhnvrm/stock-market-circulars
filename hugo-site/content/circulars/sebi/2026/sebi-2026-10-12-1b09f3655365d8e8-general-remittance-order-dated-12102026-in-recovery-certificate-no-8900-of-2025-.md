## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated October 12, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 8900 of 2025 against Clifford Capital Partners AG SA (erstwhile Seazun Limited). This enforcement action relates to penalties imposed in the matter of Global Depository Receipts (GDR) issues of IKF Technologies Limited.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 8900 of 2025.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Clifford Capital Partners AG SA (Erstwhile Seazun Limited).
- Matter: Fraudulent GDR issues and market manipulation involving IKF Technologies Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of outstanding penalties and dues arising from illegal GDR manipulation schemes.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries are directed to comply with the remittance and attachment orders by freezing specified funds, accounts, or assets belonging to the noticee and remitting the realized amounts towards the recovery certificate dues.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 12, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Year: 2025 (RC No. 8900 of 2025)

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's continued and rigorous pursuit of recovery against foreign and domestic entities implicated in historical GDR manipulation, reinforcing market integrity and regulatory deterrence.
