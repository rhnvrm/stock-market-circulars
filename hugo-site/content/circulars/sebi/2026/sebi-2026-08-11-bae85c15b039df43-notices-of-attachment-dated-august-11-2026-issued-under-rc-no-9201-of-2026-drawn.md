## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 11, 2026, under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9201 of 2026 against Dhaval Vinodbhai Gadani. This enforcement action pertains to the investigation into the trading activities and price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 9201 of 2026.
- Target individual: Dhaval Vinodbhai Gadani.
- Matter relates to irregular trading activities and artificial volume/price inflation in DU Digital Technologies Limited.
- Enforcement action is part of SEBI's recovery proceedings to collect outstanding dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A - Enforcement action under existing recovery powers of SEBI.

## Compliance Requirements

The notice requires the defaulter and related financial institutions to comply with the attachment of assets, preventing transfer or withdrawal of funds/securities until dues are cleared.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight and strict enforcement against entities involved in stock manipulation on SME platforms.
