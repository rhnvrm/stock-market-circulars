## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 11, 2026, under RC No. 9198 of 2026 drawn against Madhu Kumari Bairwa in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 9198 of 2026.
- Target entity/individual: Madhu Kumari Bairwa.
- Scrip involved: DU Digital Technologies Limited (DU Digital Global Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals named in SEBI recovery proceedings must comply with attachment orders and settlement directives.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enforcement action concerning specific trading activities in the scrip.
