## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated October 5, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 9272 of 2026 drawn against Bhaumik Parmar (PAN: EQQPP8919H). This action relates to recovery proceedings and disgorgement of dues in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued on October 5, 2026.
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9272 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Bhaumik Parmar (PAN: EQQPP8919H).
- Matter: Trading activities and price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and recovery measures pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of disgorgement amounts, interest, and recovery costs.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, garnishees, and intermediaries are required to remit attached funds and comply with the remittance order issued by SEBI's recovery officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance Order: October 5, 2026
- Recovery Certificate No. 9272 of 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights rigorous enforcement and asset recovery actions by SEBI against entities involved in stock manipulation and illegal trading gains in DU Digital Global Limited.
