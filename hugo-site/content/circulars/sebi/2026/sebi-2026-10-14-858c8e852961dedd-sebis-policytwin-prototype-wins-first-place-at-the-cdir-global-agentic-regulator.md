## Summary

SEBI’s Team Pheonix secured first prize at the C:>DIR Global ‘Agentic Regulator’ Hackathon for their prototype, "PolicyTwin". Organized by Financial Innovation for Impact (Fii), the hackathon brought together global regulators and innovators to build practical agentic AI solutions.

## Key Points

- Team Pheonix (comprising Shreya Chaturvedi, Suchita Jain, Apoorva Singh Rathore, and Shahityaa D) won the first prize of USD 35,000.
- The team has been invited to present their solution at the Singapore FinTech Festival in November 2026.
- PolicyTwin is a virtual practice market prototype enabling financial regulators to test proposed policies using AI agents representing retail investors, institutions, brokers, and bad actors.
- The tool allows regulators to assess impacts on trading, settlement, investor protection, and market fairness before implementation, with plans for adoption by SEBI after refinement and testing.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This press release announces an award and highlights an exploratory prototype under development.

## Compliance Requirements

None. No immediate compliance obligations or reporting requirements are imposed on market participants.

## Important Dates

- **14 October 2026:** Date of announcement and felicitation by the Chairman, SEBI.
- **November 2026:** Presentation scheduled at the Singapore FinTech Festival.

## Impact Assessment

PolicyTwin represents a long-term strategic evolution toward agentic AI-driven regulatory oversight and proactive policy impact testing, though it currently remains at the prototype stage.
