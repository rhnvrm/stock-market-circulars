## Summary

Brijesh Sharma filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI's response denying information regarding settlement proceedings, committee minutes, and related correspondence involving JP Morgan Chase Bank N.A. (Settlement Application No. 8617/2025). The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's decision, noting that under Regulation 29 of the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018, settlement documents and discussions are held in a fiduciary capacity and their disclosure is exempted under Section 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act as it would prejudice the applicant, with no larger public interest warranted.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought certified copies of the settlement application, meeting minutes, internal committee records, HPAC deliberations, and remittance confirmations related to JP Morgan Chase Bank N.A. in Settlement Application No. 8617/2025.
- CPIO denied the information citing Regulation 29 of SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018 and Section 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act (fiduciary capacity).
- The Appellate Authority affirmed that information submitted during settlement proceedings is deemed to be held in a fiduciary capacity and disclosure would prejudice the applicant.
- The Authority found no larger public interest warranting the disclosure of the requested information.
