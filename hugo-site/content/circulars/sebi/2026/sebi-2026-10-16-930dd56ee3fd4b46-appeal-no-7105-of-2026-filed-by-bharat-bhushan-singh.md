## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority dismissed Appeal No. 7105 of 2026 filed by Bharat Bhushan Singh against CPIO, SEBI, upholding the non-disclosure of information regarding Mohammad Nasiruddin Ansari and Baap of Chart under Section 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act, 2005, as the information pertains to third parties held in a fiduciary capacity.

## Key Points

- Appellant Bharat Bhushan Singh filed an RTI application seeking certified copies of SEBI records, addresses, bank account freezing communications, and related proceedings concerning Mohammad Nasiruddin Ansari (associated with Baap of Chart and unregistered investment advisory activities).
- CPIO, SEBI denied the information under Section 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act, stating it is held in a fiduciary capacity with no larger public interest involved.
- The Appellate Authority perused the appeal and confirmed that requested information relating to third parties received in a fiduciary capacity is exempt from disclosure.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; this is an RTI appellate order upholding existing exemptions under Section 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

- None. Information concerning third-party regulatory proceedings held in fiduciary capacity remains protected under RTI Act provisions.

## Important Dates

- Date of RTI Application: September 04, 2026
- Date of CPIO Response: September 23, 2026
- Date of Appeal: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No direct impact on market operations or listed entities. Reinforces privacy and fiduciary protection of third-party regulatory records under the RTI Act.
