## Summary

SEBI issued an exemption order under Sections 11(1) and 11(2)(h) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Regulation 11(5) of SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 regarding the proposed direct acquisition of shares and voting rights in Allcargo Logistics Limited by the Shashi Kiran Shetty Family Trust.

## Key Points

- The Target Company is Allcargo Logistics Limited, whose equity shares are listed on BSE and NSE.
- The Acquirer Trust (Shashi Kiran Shetty Family Trust) sought exemption from regulations 3 and 4 of the SAST Regulations, 2011 for acquiring shares and voting rights as part of promoter group shareholding reorganization.
- Total promoter and promoter group shareholding stands at 40.28% as of June 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Exemption granted from the applicability of open offer obligations under Regulations 3 and 4 of the SAST Regulations, 2011 for the proposed transaction involving the Acquirer Trust.

## Compliance Requirements

- The proposed acquisition must be completed in accordance with the disclosures and representations made in the application, addendums, and corrigendums.
- Compliance with all applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and other securities laws remains mandatory.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: March 19, 2026
- Addendum Date: September 07, 2026
- Corrigendum and Addendum Date: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Internal restructuring of promoter holdings into a family trust does not alter public shareholding or control dynamics, ensuring smooth continuation of corporate governance without triggering open offer requirements.
