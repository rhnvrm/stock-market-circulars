## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 21, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9247 of 2026 drawn against Sulabh Dikshit (PAN: ANEPD9669B). This enforcement action is taken in the matter of trading based stock recommendations using social media (YouTube) in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment of Demat Account and Mutual Fund Folio(s) issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9247 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Sulabh Dikshit (PAN: ANEPD9669B).
- Matter: Trading based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts arising from illegal stock recommendations.

## Compliance Requirements

Depository participants, mutual fund houses, and financial intermediaries are required to attach and freeze the demat accounts and mutual fund folios of the noticee, prohibiting any debits, transfers, or withdrawals.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's regulatory oversight and strict enforcement against fraudulent stock recommendations and pump-and-dump schemes conducted via social media platforms.
