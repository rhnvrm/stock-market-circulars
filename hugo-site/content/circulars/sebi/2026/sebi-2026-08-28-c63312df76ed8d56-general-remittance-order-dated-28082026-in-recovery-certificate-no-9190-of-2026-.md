## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Order dated August 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9190 of 2026 against Girish Kantilal Parmar (PAN: AUDPP6127A). This action is part of ongoing recovery proceedings related to illegal trading activities and stock price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Entity involved: Girish Kantilal Parmar (PAN: AUDPP6127A).
- Recovery Certificate: No. 9190 of 2026.
- Date of Order: August 28, 2026.
- Scrip: DU Digital Technologies Limited / DU Digital Global Limited.
- Context: Recovery of dues stemming from unauthorized market practices and price manipulation.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes; this is an administrative recovery and enforcement order issued against a specific defaulter.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and custodians holding accounts or assets of the defaulter must comply with the remittance order as directed by the Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 28, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Direct impact is restricted to the named individual and associated entities. It reinforces SEBI's strict stance on recovering penalties imposed for market manipulation.
