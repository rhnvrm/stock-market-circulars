## Summary

SEBI announced plans to review the methodology for determining settlement prices of derivative contracts on expiry following stakeholder feedback regarding the newly implemented Closing Auction Session (CAS) in the Equity Cash Segment. A consultation paper proposing changes is expected to be issued in about a week.

## Key Points

- CAS was introduced in the Equity Cash Segment on August 3, 2026, to determine closing prices, which also served as the basis for derivative settlement prices.
- SEBI monitored market functioning during the first month and engaged extensively with stock exchanges, brokers, mutual funds, FPIs, and other stakeholders.
- Based on feedback concerning derivative settlement prices on expiry, SEBI is considering revisions to the methodology.
- A consultation paper detailing proposed changes will be released in approximately a week.

## Regulatory Changes

- Potential revision of the methodology for determining settlement prices for derivative contracts on expiry.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants and stakeholders should review the upcoming consultation paper and provide feedback during the consultation window.

## Important Dates

- Consultation paper release expected: In about a week (mid-September 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on derivative traders, brokers, and institutional investors relying on expiry settlement pricing mechanics aligned with the CAS framework.
