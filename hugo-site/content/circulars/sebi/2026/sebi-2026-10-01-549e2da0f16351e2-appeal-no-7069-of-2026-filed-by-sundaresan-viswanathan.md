## Summary

Sundaresan Viswanathan filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI's response dated August 21, 2026. The appeal deals with various queries regarding SEBI's action taken reports (ATRs), committee minutes (such as QARC, SCODA, IPEF, ACLOD), and lists of entities that tapped the capital market.

## Key Points

- Appellant filed an RTI application dated July 27, 2026, followed by an appeal dated September 02, 2026.
- Queries sought information on action taken reports (ATRs) on listed companies, insolvencies, and specific communication references.
- Appellant raised queries regarding the maintenance and accessibility of minutes of various SEBI committees (QARC, SCODA, IPEF, ACLOD, etc.).
- Requested a year-wise list of entities that tapped the capital market since 2003 with insolvencies.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes or new compliance mandates were introduced by this appellate order.

## Compliance Requirements

None. The order disposes of an RTI appeal based on records available with SEBI.

## Important Dates

- RTI Application Date: July 27, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: August 21, 2026
- Appeal Date: September 02, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order has no operational or market impact as it is an individual statutory appeal under the RTI Act, 2005.
