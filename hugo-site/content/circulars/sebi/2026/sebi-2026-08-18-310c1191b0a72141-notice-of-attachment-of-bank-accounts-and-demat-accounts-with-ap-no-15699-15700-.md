## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice for the attachment of bank accounts and demat accounts under Recovery Certificate No. 9229 of 2026 (along with AP No. 15699 & 15700 of 2026) dated August 18, 2026, against Rakeshbhai K Pandya (PAN: CUMPP3101K) as a defaulter in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Defaulter: Rakeshbhai K Pandya (PAN: CUMPP3101K)
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9229 of 2026
- Action: Attachment of bank accounts and demat accounts
- Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and depositories are required to attach the bank and demat accounts of the specified defaulter and prevent any debits or withdrawals.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures enforcement of recovery proceedings against entities defaulting on dues owed to the regulatory authority.
