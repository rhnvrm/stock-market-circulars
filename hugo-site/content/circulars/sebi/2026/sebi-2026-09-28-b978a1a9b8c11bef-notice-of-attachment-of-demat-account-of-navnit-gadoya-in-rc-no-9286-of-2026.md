## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a notice of attachment of all demat accounts and mutual fund folios held by Navnit Gadoya (PAN: AQNPG4727F) under Recovery Proceeding No. 15816 of 2026 (Certificate No. 9286 of 2026). The total recovery amount stands at Rs. 5,26,000/- along with interest, charges, and expenses, originating from a penalty imposed for front running.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter:** Navnit Gadoya (PAN: AQNPG4727F).
- **Recovery Amount:** Rs. 5,26,000/- comprising Rs. 5,00,000/- penalty, Rs. 25,000/- interest (April to August 2026), and Rs. 1,000/- recovery cost.
- **Underlying Matter:** Penalty imposed vide AO Order No. Order/AK/DS/2026-27/32398-32400 dated April 29, 2026, in the matter of front running.
- **Action Ordered:** Immediate attachment of all demat accounts and mutual fund folios held singly or jointly, with a strict prohibition on any debits while allowing credits.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery under Section 28A(1) and 11(2)(ia) of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Section 226 and the second schedule of the Income-tax Act, 1961.

## Compliance Requirements

- Depositories (NSDL, CDSL) and Mutual Funds must immediately attach the accounts and folios of the defaulter, ensuring no debits are permitted.
- Submit account details, account statements, and confirmation of attachment to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- **Recovery Certificate Date:** August 19, 2026
- **Notice of Attachment Date:** September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Restricts the defaulter from liquidating or transferring securities and mutual fund units until outstanding dues are fully recovered.
