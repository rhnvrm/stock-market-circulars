## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7696 of 2024 drawn against S Nagarajan in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This action is part of enforcement and recovery proceedings to realize outstanding dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7696 of 2024.
- Defaulter / Noticee: S Nagarajan.
- Company Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Date of Issuance: October 13, 2026.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of monetary penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, demat depositories, and other intermediaries are required to attach and freeze all accounts, assets, and holding facilities of the noticee as specified in the recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice of Attachment: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects rigorous SEBI enforcement actions to recover outstanding dues in cases involving financial irregularities and non-compliance by entities associated with Arvind Remedies Ltd.
