## Summary

SEBI issued a settlement order (SO/JS/RJ/2026-27/7795-7799) concerning adjudication proceedings against five Adani Group companies—Adani Enterprises Ltd., Adani Total Gas Ltd., AWL Agri Business Ltd. (formerly Adani Wilmar Ltd.), Adani Green Energy Ltd., and Adani Energy Solutions Ltd. (formerly Adani Transmission Ltd.). The proceedings examined allegations related to disclosures of Related Party Transactions (RPTs) and corporate governance issues highlighted in the Hindenburg Report, along with potential violations of LODR Regulations, Listing Agreements, and exchange bylaws.

## Key Points

- Examination conducted by SEBI into RPT disclosures and corporate governance based on the Hindenburg Report.
- Adjudication proceedings initiated against Adani Enterprises Ltd., Adani Total Gas Ltd., AWL Agri Business Ltd., Adani Green Energy Ltd., and Adani Energy Solutions Ltd.
- Alleged violations pertain to clause 32 of the Listing Agreement, regulation 33(1)(d) of LODR Regulations, clause 41(I)(h) of the Listing Agreement, and exchange bylaws.
- The current Adjudicating Officer was appointed pursuant to transfer of the earlier AO.

## Regulatory Changes

[None specified in this order excerpt; enforcement and adjudication of existing LODR Regulations and Listing Agreement provisions.]

## Compliance Requirements

Entities involved are subject to ongoing adjudication and settlement terms under SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995 and SCRA Rules, 2005.

High profile regulatory scrutiny and potential financial settlements impacting compliance practices and disclosure standards for major listed corporate groups.
