## Summary

SEBI has introduced measures to ease the onboarding process for Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) by permitting them to execute a Power of Attorney (PoA) through digital signatures in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000.

## Key Points

- FPIs can now use digital signatures for executing a Power of Attorney (PoA).
- Eliminates the requirement for notarisation, apostillisation, or consularisation of PoA.
- Aims to significantly reduce the time and physical documentation required for FPI onboarding.
- Part of SEBI's broader digitalisation and investor-friendly initiatives.

## Regulatory Changes

- Permitted the execution of Power of Attorney (PoA) via digital signatures for FPIs under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
- Removed traditional procedural bottlenecks such as notarisation and apostillisation.

## Compliance Requirements

- FPIs and custodians/intermediaries can accept digitally signed PoAs complying with the IT Act, 2000 during the onboarding process.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Substantially lowers onboarding friction and processing time for foreign investors entering the Indian securities market.
