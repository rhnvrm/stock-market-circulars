## Summary

The Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7073 of 2026 filed by Shri Ramesh B under the RTI Act, 2005. The appellant had sought various details regarding minor name mismatches, physical share KYC, dematerialisation rules, and timelines. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that the requested information is already publicly available via SEBI Master Circulars.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought details on KYC requirements, dematerialisation procedures, ISR forms (ISR-1, ISR-2, ISR-3, ISR-4, ISR-13), and timelines for RTAs.
- Respondent directed the appellant to the SEBI Master Circular for Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents dated February 06, 2026, and the SEBI Circular dated July 23, 2026, on Ease of Doing Investment and Business.
- The Appellate Authority upheld that information already available in the public domain does not need to be separately provided under the RTI Act, citing judicial precedents.

## Regulatory Changes

None (RTI appellate order affirming existing public domain availability of regulatory guidelines).

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and investors must refer to published SEBI Master Circulars for RTA operations, KYC updation, and dematerialisation standards.
