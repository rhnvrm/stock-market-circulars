## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated September 30, 2026, under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 4723 of 2022 in the matter of Veera Kumar Das Surapanani regarding Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd. This release order signifies the formal conclusion or lifting of attachment/recovery measures for this specific certificate following compliance or settlement of dues.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate (RC) No.: 4723 of 2022
- Defaulter / Noticee: Veera Kumar Das Surapanani
- Matter: Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd.
- Action: Release Order issued by SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Regulatory Changes

Lifting of enforcement and recovery actions initiated pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992, following resolution of recovery certificate dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Not applicable as this is a release order indicating resolution of recovery proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 30, 2026
