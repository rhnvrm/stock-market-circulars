## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7711 of 2024 drawn against S Sengolancy Beaula in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This action is part of SEBI's ongoing recovery proceedings to realize outstanding dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7711 of 2024.
- Noticee / Defaulter: S Sengolancy Beaula.
- Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) pursuant to non-payment of dues under enforcement orders.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and asset attachment measures executed under the SEBI Act, 1992, and relevant recovery rules for the realization of regulatory dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and depositories are instructed to freeze bank accounts, demat accounts, and other movable/immovable assets of the noticee to the extent of the certificate amount, preventing any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: October 13, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Year: 2024 (RC No. 7711 of 2024)

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's stringent enforcement and recovery mechanisms to penalize non-compliance and recover outstanding dues from individuals associated with irregularities in Arvind Remedies Ltd.
