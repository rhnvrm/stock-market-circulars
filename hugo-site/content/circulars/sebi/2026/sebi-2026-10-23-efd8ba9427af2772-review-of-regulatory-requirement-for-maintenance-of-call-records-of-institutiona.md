## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular relaxing the mandatory requirement for Research Analysts (RAs) and Research Entities (REs) to maintain call recordings of communications with institutional investor clients. This amendment to Regulation 25(1)(vii) of the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, aims to enhance the ease of doing business based on representations received from market participants.

## Key Points

- Relaxation applies specifically to call recordings of interactions with clients that are institutional investors.
- Master Circular for Research Analysts dated February 6, 2026, has been suitably modified.
- Paragraph 1.13 of Chapter I and paragraph 33 of Annexure I (FAQs) updated to reflect the exemption.
- Requirement to maintain other forms of interaction records (physical records, emails, SMS, etc.) and record-keeping for non-institutional clients remains unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Regulation 25(1)(vii) of the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, has been amended.
- Paragraph 1.13(b)(i) of Chapter I of the Master Circular for RAs now explicitly includes a proviso stating that the requirement to maintain call recordings of interactions with institutional investors shall not be mandatory.
- Paragraph 33 of Annexure I (FAQs) regarding client record maintenance has been updated accordingly.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research Analysts and Research Entities dealing with institutional investors are no longer strictly mandated to maintain call recordings for those specific interactions.
- All other KYC requirements, interaction record maintenance (for non-institutional clients), and the five-year retention period rule continue to apply as mandated.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 23, 2026
- Effective immediately upon issuance.

## Impact Assessment

- Reduces operational and compliance overhead for RAs and REs servicing institutional clients.
- Aligns regulatory framework with market realities while maintaining transparency and record-keeping standards for retail and other non-institutional clients.
