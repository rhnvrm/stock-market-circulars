## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7080 of 2026 filed by Saurabh Kumar under the Right to Information Act, 2005, challenging the response of the CPIO regarding complaints lodged against Choice Equity Broking Private Limited on the SEBI SCORES portal.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought comprehensive Action Taken Reports (ATR), file processing notes, inter-departmental correspondence between SEBI and NSE, officer details, call recordings, and broker settlement breakups.
- SEBI clarified that complaint handling on SCORES is entirely electronic and that all actions and observations are recorded directly as action history on SCORES without maintaining separate external file notes.
- Officer details are publicly accessible on the SEBI website and the SCORES ATR itself.
- Information regarding call recordings and certain broker submissions was stated to be unavailable with SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes were introduced by this appellate order.

## Compliance Requirements

No new compliance obligations or reporting mandates were established.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: August 16, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 08, 2026
- Appeal Date: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order reaffirms the electronic nature of grievance redressal tracking on SCORES and clarifies the scope of record maintenance under the RTI Act.
