## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7114 of 2026 filed by Shri A. Balamurugan under the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005. The appellant had sought information regarding whether certain individuals operating 'Anushiya Academy of Trading and Yield Company' and collecting funds held valid SEBI licenses or approvals.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought verification on whether specific individuals and the entity 'Anushiya Academy of Trading and Yield Company' held SEBI registrations or approvals to collect funds and offer investment returns.
- The CPIO responded that the queries sought clarification and opinion, which do not constitute 'information' as defined under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, while providing the public link to access recognized SEBI intermediaries.
- The Appellate Authority concurred with the CPIO, ruling that queries seeking clarifications or opinions fall outside the purview of Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes, policy modifications, or circular updates were introduced by this order.

## Compliance Requirements

None. This order is specific to an individual RTI appeal proceeding.

## Impact Assessment

This order reaffirms established jurisprudence under the RTI Act that authorities are not obligated under the Act to provide clarifications, opinions, or interpretations. It has no direct impact on market operations or compliance standards.
