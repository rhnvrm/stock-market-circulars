## Summary

SEBI has issued prohibitory orders pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 6956 and 6968 of 2023 against Sudha Mahendra Agrawal in the matter of Kushal Limited. This enforcement action is taken for the recovery of unpaid monetary penalties imposed for violations of securities laws.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificates: No. 6956 and 6968 of 2023.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Sudha Mahendra Agrawal.
- Listed Entity: Kushal Limited.
- Action: Prohibitory orders and attachment notices issued by SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are required to freeze accounts and assets of the noticee as directed under the prohibitory orders.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issuance: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous regulatory enforcement by SEBI for recovery of unpaid penalties from promoters of listed companies.
