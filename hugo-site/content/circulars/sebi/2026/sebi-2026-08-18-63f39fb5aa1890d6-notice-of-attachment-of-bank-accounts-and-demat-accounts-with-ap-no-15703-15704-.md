## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice for the attachment of bank accounts and demat accounts with AP No. 15703 & 15704 of 2026 dated August 18, 2026, against Sonalben Amitbhai Khalas (PAN: HVUPK8521G) under Recovery Certificate No. 9230 of 2026 in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Defaulter: Sonalben Amitbhai Khalas (PAN: HVUPK8521G)
- Matter: Trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited
- Action: Attachment of bank accounts and demat accounts under Recovery Certificate No. 9230 of 2026
- Reference: AP No. 15703 & 15704 of 2026 dated August 18, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and depositories are required to attach the accounts of the specified defaulter as per the recovery proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricted access to bank and demat accounts for the named defaulter to recover dues.
