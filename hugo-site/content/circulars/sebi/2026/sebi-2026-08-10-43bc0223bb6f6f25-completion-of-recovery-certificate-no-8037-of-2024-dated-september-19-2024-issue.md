## Summary

SEBI has officially recorded the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 8037 of 2024 dated September 19, 2024, issued against the defaulter Kamlesh Ahuja (PAN: ADFPA4706R) in the matter of dealings in illiquid stock options at BSE.

## Key Points

- Defaulter Name: Kamlesh Ahuja
- PAN: ADFPA4706R
- Recovery Certificate No.: 8037 of 2024 (Dated: September 19, 2024)
- Issue: Dealings in Illiquid Stock Options at BSE
- Action: Completion of recovery proceedings and associated release orders.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative enforcement and recovery completion action.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals subject to recovery proceedings must fulfill all dues as specified under SEBI recovery certificates; upon completion, attachment orders on accounts and assets are lifted.

## Important Dates

- Original Recovery Certificate Date: September 19, 2024
- Completion Notice Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Concludes the recovery proceedings against the specified defaulter with no broader market impact.
