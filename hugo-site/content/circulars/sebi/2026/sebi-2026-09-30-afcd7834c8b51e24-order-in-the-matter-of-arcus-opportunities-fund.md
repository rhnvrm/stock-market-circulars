## Summary

SEBI issued an ex-parte order against Arcus Opportunities Fund (Registration No. IN/AIF2/12-13/0021) under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Regulation 30A of SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008, for failing to submit Quarterly Activity Reports (QARs) for the quarters ending March 2025, June 2025, September 2025, and December 2025.

## Key Points

- Noticee Arcus Opportunities Fund is registered as an Alternative Investment Fund (AIF) with SEBI.
- The fund failed to file mandatory Quarterly Activity Reports (QARs) for four consecutive quarters (March 2025 to December 2025).
- Show Cause Notice (SCN) dated April 27, 2026, remained unserved via RPAD, email, and affixation.
- Substituted service was successfully executed via publication in Hindustan Times, Nav Bharat, and Prahaar on August 26, 2026.
- Due to lack of response, proceedings were conducted ex-parte.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement order reinforcing existing reporting mandates for AIFs.

## Compliance Requirements

Registered AIFs must strictly adhere to filing Quarterly Activity Reports (QARs) on the SEBI Intermediary Portal (SI Portal) within 15 calendar days from the end of each quarter.

## Important Dates

- April 27, 2026: Show Cause Notice (SCN) issued
- August 26, 2026: Notice published in newspapers

## Impact Assessment

This order impacts the registration status of Arcus Opportunities Fund for failure to maintain regulatory compliance, with minimal direct impact on the broader securities market.
