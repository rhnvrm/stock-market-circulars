## Summary

SEBI initiated summary proceedings under Regulation 30A of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 read with Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 against DAR Mentorcap Film Fund due to failure to submit mandatory Quarterly Activity Reports (QARs).

## Key Points

- **Noticee:** DAR Mentorcap Film Fund (Registration No. IN/AIF2/12-13/0011).
- **Violation:** Non-filing of QARs for four consecutive quarters: March 2025, June 2025, September 2025, and December 2025.
- **Service of Notice:** Show Cause Notice (SCN) sent via RPAD and email returned unserved, and affixation failed. Substituted service was executed via publication in prominent newspapers (The Hindustan Times, Nav Bharat, and Prahaar) on August 26, 2026.
- **Ex-Parte Proceedings:** As no reply or representation was received from the Noticee, service was deemed complete and the matter was processed ex-parte.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; enforces existing compliance requirements for Alternative Investment Funds under AIF Regulations, 2012.

## Compliance Requirements

Alternative Investment Funds are mandated to file Quarterly Activity Reports (QARs) within 15 calendar days from the end of each quarter on the SEBI Intermediary Portal.

## Important Dates

- **SCN Date:** April 27, 2026
- **Newspaper Publication Date:** August 26, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights SEBI's strict enforcement of periodic reporting obligations and readiness to proceed ex-parte against non-compliant intermediaries.
