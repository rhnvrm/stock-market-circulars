## Summary

The appellant, Vikas Jain, filed an RTI application seeking information regarding alleged violations of the 6-month lock-in period by employees of Indian Renewable Energy Development Agency Limited (IREDA) who were allotted shares under the employee quota during its IPO. SEBI's CPIO responded by stating that queries seeking clarification/opinion do not constitute 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, and that investigation details are confidential. The appellate authority reviewed the case.

## Key Points

- **Applicant:** Vikas Jain
- **Respondent:** CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai
- **Subject:** RTI appeal concerning alleged employee lock-in period violations during the IREDA IPO.
- **Queries Raised:** Information on cognizance of complaints, lists of violating employees, investigation reports, action taken reports, and directives to stock exchanges or depositories.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes were introduced through this order; it is an appellate decision under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

None specified for market participants; standard RTI response protocols apply.

## Important Dates

- **Application Date:** July 03, 2026
- **CPIO Response Date:** July 21, 2026
- **Appeal Date:** July 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No direct market or operational impact. This order addresses individual RTI disclosures regarding ongoing or confidential regulatory examinations.
