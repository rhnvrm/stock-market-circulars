## Summary

Prakash Singh filed an RTI application seeking details regarding vacancies, exam timelines, and syllabus changes for SEBI Grade A Legal Officer posts. The CPIO responded stating that the queries sought clarifications and opinions rather than concrete 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, confirming that queries requiring clarification or research do not fall under the definition of information under the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought information on vacancies, examination dates, and pattern changes for SEBI Grade A Legal Stream.
- Respondent and Appellate Authority noted that queries seeking clarifications, opinions, or predictions do not qualify as 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- Recruitment details and notifications are published periodically on the SEBI website.
