## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 11, 2026, under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9222 of 2026 against Ankit Ajitbhai Panchal. This action is part of ongoing recovery proceedings and enforcement measures in the matter of manipulative trading activities and artificial price/volume inflation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 9222 of 2026.
- Target Entity/Individual: Ankit Ajitbhai Panchal.
- Matter: Trading activities and price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Aim: Recovery of illegal gains and penalties imposed by SEBI pursuant to previous adjudication orders.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed under SEBI Recovery Proceedings framework to recover dues from defaulters involved in fraudulent and unfair trade practices.

## Compliance Requirements

Strict compliance with SEBI recovery notices, prohibiting the transfer, alienation, or charge of assets by the defaulter until dues are fully cleared.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight and stringent enforcement against market participants engaged in fraudulent trading practices, ensuring accountability and recovery of disgorged amounts.
