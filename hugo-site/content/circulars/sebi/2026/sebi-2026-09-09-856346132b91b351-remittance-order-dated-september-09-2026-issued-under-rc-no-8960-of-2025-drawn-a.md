## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated September 09, 2026, under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 8960 of 2025 against Rajvardhan Foundation. This action relates to recovery proceedings concerning non-genuine transactions and dealings in illiquid stock options on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

## Key Points

- **Authority:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- **Entity Involved:** Rajvardhan Foundation.
- **Matter:** Dealings in illiquid stock options at BSE.
- **Reference:** Recovery Certificate No. 8960 of 2025, Remittance Order dated September 09, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory framework changes; this is an enforcement and recovery action targeted at a specific defaulter.

## Compliance Requirements

Execution of the remittance order as directed by the Recovery Officer for the recovery of pending regulatory dues.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Limited to the specific entity under recovery proceedings with no broader market-wide implications.
