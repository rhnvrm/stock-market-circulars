## Summary

SEBI has extended the timeline for implementing the provisions of its circular dated June 15, 2026, which outlines norms for base price, price bands, call auction in pre-open sessions, and close-out procedures for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs). The new implementation date is September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Original implementation date was September 1, 2026.
- Extended implementation date is September 7, 2026.
- Extension granted based on representations received from Stock Exchanges to ensure smooth implementation.
- All other provisions of the circular dated June 15, 2026, remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Postponement of the effective compliance date for ETF trading norms, price bands, call auctions, and close-out procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock Exchanges and Clearing Corporations must bring the circular's provisions to the notice of member stock brokers and publish them on their websites.
- Exchanges and Clearing Corporations must make necessary amendments to relevant bye-laws, rules, and regulations.
- Status of implementation must be communicated to SEBI in Monthly Development Reports.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: August 28, 2026
- New Effective Date: September 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants, stock exchanges, and clearing corporations additional time to adapt their trading, surveillance, and operational systems to comply with the new ETF framework.
