## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order regarding Piyush Agarwal (PAN: APBPA7314M) in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited under Recovery Certificate No. 9263 of 2026. This order lifts the previous attachments and freezes on bank accounts and assets.

## Key Points

- SEBI issued a Release Order for defaulter Piyush Agarwal.
- Associated with Recovery Certificate No. 9263 of 2026.
- Relates to the ongoing enforcement actions and recovery proceedings in the Sadhna Broadcast Limited manipulation case.
- Lifts attachments previously placed on bank accounts, mutual funds, and other financial holdings.

## Regulatory Changes

- Withdrawal and cancellation of recovery attachment proceedings specific to the mentioned certificate.

## Compliance Requirements

- Financial institutions and banks are instructed to unfreeze the accounts and assets of the individual as specified in the release order.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operational relief for the individual entity; no direct systemic impact on the broader market.
