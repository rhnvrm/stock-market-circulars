## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 10, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9227 of 2026 against Lataben Narotambhai Rangi (PAN: AYRPR7467F). This action stems from unpaid financial penalties imposed following an investigation into manipulative trading activities, synchronized trading, and fraudulent practices in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9227 of 2026 was issued to recover outstanding dues and penalties from Lataben Narotambhai Rangi.
- The underlying matter relates to fraudulent and unfair trade practices (PFUTP) and artificial volume generation in the shares of ANI Integrated Services Limited between March 1, 2021, and August 31, 2021.
- Banks and financial institutions are directed to remit attached funds to SEBI's recovery account in compliance with the order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery certificates via general remittance orders targeting non-compliant entities failing to pay adjudicated penalties within the stipulated timeframe.

## Compliance Requirements

- Designated banks and financial institutions must freeze and remit funds held in accounts associated with the named defaulter directly to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 10, 2026
- Investigation Period: March 1, 2021, to August 31, 2021

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory deterrence against market manipulation and non-payment of statutory penalties levied by SEBI.
