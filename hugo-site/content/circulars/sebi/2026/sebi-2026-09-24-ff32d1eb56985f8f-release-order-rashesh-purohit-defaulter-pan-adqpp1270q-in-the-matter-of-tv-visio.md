## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated September 24, 2026, lifting attachment and recovery proceedings against Rashesh Purohit (PAN: ADQPP1270Q) under Recovery Certificate No. 7475 of 2023 in the matter of TV Vision Limited.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued by the Recovery Officer of SEBI regarding Recovery Certificate No. 7475 of 2023.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Rashesh Purohit (PAN: ADQPP1270Q).
- Subject company: TV Vision Limited.
- Official lifting of attachment on bank accounts, financial holdings, and assets previously attached under recovery proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal closure and withdrawal of recovery/enforcement measures initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992 following settlement or realization of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and intermediaries are instructed to unfreeze any accounts or assets previously placed under attachment pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 7475 of 2023.

## Important Dates

- Release Order Date: September 24, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Date: December 2023 (Certificate No. 7475 of 2023)

## Impact Assessment

Low operational impact on TV Vision Limited as the order resolves personal recovery proceedings against an individual defaulter associated with past insider trading investigations.
