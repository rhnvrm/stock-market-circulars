## Summary

The appellant, R K Singhal, filed an RTI appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against SEBI's response regarding physical share status of Industrial Credit and Investment Corporation of India Limited (ICICI), KYC requirements, and documents submitted to the RBI. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that SEBI does not maintain physical share records in the normal course of regulation and that clarification/opinion queries do not constitute 'information' under the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information regarding physical shares (Folio No. R064xxx) of Industrial Credit and Investment Corporation of India Limited.
- SEBI stated that physical share status and third-party regulatory submissions are not maintained by SEBI in the normal course of regulation.
- Queries regarding KYC document requirements were classified as requests for clarification/opinion rather than 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- The appellant was directed to refer to SEBI's Master Circular on KYC dated October 12, 2023, available in the public domain.
