## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated August 17, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9280 of 2026 drawn against Plastika Industries (PAN: AAAFP4737H). This action is taken for the recovery of dues/penalties in the matter of Trading in Illiquid Stock Options at BSE.

## Key Points

- Notice of Demand issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9280 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Plastika Industries (PAN: AAAFP4737H).
- Matter: Trading in Illiquid Stock Options at BSE.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 and related regulations for the realization of outstanding dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Noticee and concerned financial institutions/banks must comply with the demand notice for payment of dues and cessation of unauthorized asset transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 17, 2026
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9280 of 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement follow-through by SEBI regarding past irregular trading in illiquid stock options on BSE exchanges.
