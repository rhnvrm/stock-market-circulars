## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, establish the comprehensive regulatory framework governing listing obligations and disclosure requirements for entities with listed securities on recognized stock exchanges in India. This consolidated version incorporates amendments up to October 15, 2026.

## Key Points

- Establishes uniform listing obligations and corporate governance norms across recognized stock exchanges.
- Defines key terms and applicability criteria for designated securities, associates, holding companies, key managerial personnel, and fugitive economic offenders.
- Integrates provisions for various financial instruments including non-convertible debt, security receipts, zero coupon zero principal instruments, and mutual fund units.
- Incorporates statutory references under the Companies Act, 2013 and the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016.

## Regulatory Changes

- Incorporates ongoing updates and amendments up to October 15, 2026.
- Updates definitions including designated securities, fugitive economic offenders, and the Insolvency Code.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed entities must adhere to disclosure norms, corporate governance standards, and financial reporting requirements specified under the regulations.
- Compliance officers and key managerial personnel must ensure timely filings and disclosures to recognized stock exchanges.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: Ninetieth day from initial publication date (with specific provisions like regulation 23(4) and 31A effective from the date of notification).
- Last Amended: October 15, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures robust market transparency, investor protection, and high standards of corporate governance across all listed entities in Indian capital markets.
