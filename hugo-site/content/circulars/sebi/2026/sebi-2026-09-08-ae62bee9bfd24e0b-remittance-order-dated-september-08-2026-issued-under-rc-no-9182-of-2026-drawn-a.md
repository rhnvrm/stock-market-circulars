## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated September 08, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9182 of 2026 drawn against Ankit Ajitbhai Panchal. This action relates to recovery proceedings and remittance of dues in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under RC No. 9182 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Ankit Ajitbhai Panchal.
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, garnishees, and intermediaries are required to remit attached funds and comply with directions issued under the recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of Remittance Order: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing strict regulatory enforcement and asset recovery measures by SEBI against entities involved in market irregularities concerning DU Digital Global Limited.
