## Summary

This order issued by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Regulation 30A of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 involves thirty Noticees (Investment Advisers) who failed to pay the mandated renewal fees required to keep their registrations in force.

## Key Points

- Involves 30 registered Investment Advisers, including SEAF India Investment Advisors Private Limited, Anant Hasmukhlal Shah, Kumar Manish, and others.
- Focuses on the non-payment of renewal fees every five years as required under Clause 3 of the Second Schedule of SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.
- Initiated under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Regulation 30A of SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of mandatory five-year renewal fee payments for registered Investment Advisers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must pay renewal fees within three months before the expiry of the registration period to keep their certification in force.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures regulatory compliance and accountability among registered investment advisers operating in the securities market.
