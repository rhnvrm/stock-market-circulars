## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a release order in Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9246 of 2026 concerning 8 entities associated with Shubhlaxmi Jewel Art Limited, marking the successful conclusion of recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- Recovery proceedings under RC No. 9246 of 2026 have been officially completed.
- All previously issued attachment notices against bank accounts, demat accounts, and mutual funds of the 8 entities have been lifted.
- The action follows the realization and payment of the pending dues and penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

- Lifting of recovery and attachment orders previously imposed on the defaulter entities.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities have cleared all pending dues, resulting in the closure of the recovery file under the certificate.

## Important Dates

- Release Order Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Low impact on the broader market; resolves compliance and enforcement proceedings for the specified entities.
