## Summary

An RTI appeal (No. 7053 of 2026) filed by Prince Pravin Naiya against the CPIO of SEBI regarding information on Credila Financial Services Limited under SEBI LODR Regulations and Credit Rating Agency disclosures was disposed of by the Appellate Authority.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details regarding material event disclosures under SEBI LODR Regulations 2015 and compliance certificates for Credila Financial Services Limited, alongside inquiries into loan default data supplied to credit rating agencies.
- SEBI's CPIO and Appellate Authority noted that the queries lacked specifics regarding the listed security and that material event disclosures under Regulations 30 and 51 are filed with stock exchanges, not directly with SEBI.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the response that securitization transactions referred to did not fall under the purview of SEBI's relevant regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. The order reiterates existing RTI principles and reporting locations for LODR disclosures.

## Compliance Requirements

- Material event disclosures under SEBI LODR Regulations must be accessed directly from the website of the concerned stock exchanges where the securities are listed.
