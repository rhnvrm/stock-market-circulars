## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7103 of 2026 filed by Ashok Kumar Saini under the Right to Information Act, 2005, concerning refund claims for PACL Limited. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the requested information is not available with SEBI and noted that PACL refund matters are exclusively handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought refund status and payment details for PACL Limited registration no. U281080XXX (Om Prakash Saini).
- The CPIO informed that the requested information is not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to public notices, status reports, and FAQs available on the SEBI website.
- The Appellate Authority cited CIC precedent (Sh. Pattipati Rama Murthy vs. CPIO, SEBI) establishing that CPIO cannot invent information not in its possession.
- Reaffirmed that the responsibility for property disposal and investor repayment for PACL Ltd. rests with the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee constituted under Supreme Court orders.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

None for regulated entities or market participants. Investors seeking PACL refunds are advised to refer to the official public notices, FAQs, and status reports issued by the Justice R.M. Lodha Committee and SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: September 17, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 23, 2026
- Appeal Filing Date: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No market or operational impact. This order reiterates existing jurisdictional boundaries regarding legacy PACL investor refund claims.
