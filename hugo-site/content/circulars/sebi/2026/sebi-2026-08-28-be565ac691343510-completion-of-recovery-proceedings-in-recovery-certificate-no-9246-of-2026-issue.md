## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice regarding the completion of recovery proceedings in Recovery Certificate No. 9246 of 2026 issued against 8 entities in the matter of Shubhlaxmi Jewel Art Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Proceedings under Recovery Certificate No. 9246 of 2026.
- Target: 8 entities associated with Shubhlaxmi Jewel Art Limited.
- Status: Completion of recovery proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Procedural closure of recovery proceedings pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 following settlement or realization of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

No further compliance action required for the concluded recovery proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Date of Publication: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Concludes regulatory recovery actions against the specified entities in relation to Shubhlaxmi Jewel Art Limited.
