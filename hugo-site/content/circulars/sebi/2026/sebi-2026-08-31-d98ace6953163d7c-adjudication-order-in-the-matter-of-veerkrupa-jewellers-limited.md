## Summary

SEBI conducted an investigation into Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited (VJL) to ascertain potential violations of the SEBI Act, ICDR Regulations, LODR Regulations, and Merchant Bankers Regulations. Adjudication proceedings were initiated against multiple noticees, including Mr. Chirag Arvindbhai Shah, First Overseas Capital Ltd., Ms. Mala Soneji, Mr. Rushabh Pradeep Shroff, and Mr. Satyen Bhupendra Dalal, for various alleged regulatory infractions.

## Key Points

- Investigation by SEBI into Veerkrupa Jewellers Limited (VJL).
- Noticees include company representatives and merchant bankers (First Overseas Capital Ltd.).
- Alleged violations involve regulations under ICDR Regulations, Merchant Bankers Regulations, and LODR Regulations.
- Inquiry conducted under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Rule 5 of SEBI (Procedure for Holding Inquiry and Imposing Penalties) Rules, 1995.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of disclosure, merchant banking, and issue of capital compliance standards based on adjudication findings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Strict adherence to ICDR Regulations, Merchant Bankers Regulations, and LODR Regulations by issuers and merchant bankers during public issues and disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026 (based on attachdocs/aug-2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on merchant bankers and company promoters regarding compliance failures during public issuances.
