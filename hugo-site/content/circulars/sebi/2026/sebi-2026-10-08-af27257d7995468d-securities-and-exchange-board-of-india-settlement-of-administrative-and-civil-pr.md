## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has notified the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings) Regulations, 2026. These regulations provide a comprehensive framework governing the process, terms, and conditions for the settlement of administrative and civil proceedings initiated or to be initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992, the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956, and the Depositories Act, 1996.

## Key Points

- Notified under SEBI's powers derived from Section 30 of the SEBI Act, 1992, Section 31 of the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956, and Section 25 of the Depositories Act, 1996, along with specific penal and compounding sections.
- Replaces and updates the previous settlement mechanisms to streamline procedures for market participants and entities facing administrative or civil actions.
- Outlines clear guidelines for application, review, terms of settlement, and compliance monitoring.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishes a structured regulatory framework under Chapter I and subsequent provisions for handling settlements of administrative and civil proceedings.
- Specifies the applicability and authority of SEBI in processing settlement applications across different market segments.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants, intermediaries, and entities seeking settlement of ongoing or anticipated administrative and civil proceedings must adhere strictly to the filing procedures, timelines, and financial/non-financial terms stipulated under these regulations.

## Important Dates

- **Notification Date:** October 6, 2026
- **Publication Date:** October 7, 2026
- **Effective Date:** As specified in the regulations upon publication in the Official Gazette.

## Impact Assessment

Provides legal clarity and a predictable path for resolution of disputes and enforcement actions, reducing prolonged litigation while maintaining regulatory deterrence and market integrity.
