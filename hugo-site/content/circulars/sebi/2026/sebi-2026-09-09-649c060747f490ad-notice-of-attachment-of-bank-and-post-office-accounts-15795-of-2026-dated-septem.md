## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment of Bank and Post Office Accounts dated September 09, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9285 of 2026 against Vikram Anand HUF (Defaulter) with PAN AAJHV0926R. This action is taken in the matter of dealings in Illiquid Stock Options on BSE for the realization of outstanding dues.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9285 of 2026.
- Defaulter: Vikram Anand HUF (PAN: AAJHV0926R).
- Matter: Dealings in Illiquid Stock Options on BSE.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the recovery of penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, post offices, and financial institutions are required to attach and freeze the accounts of the defaulter as per the recovery certificate and prevent any debits or withdrawals.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing regulatory enforcement against entities involved in historical misuse of illiquid stock options on BSE.
