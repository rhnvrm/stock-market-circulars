## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated August 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9192 of 2026 against Sagarkumar Pravinchandra Dataniya (PAN: BJIPD7414F). This order is issued in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9192 of 2026.
- Noticee/Defaulter: Sagarkumar Pravinchandra Dataniya (PAN: BJIPD7414F).
- Scrip: DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant entities are directed to comply with the remittance order concerning funds and assets of the defaulter as stipulated by the SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities involved in irregularities concerning DU Digital Global Limited.
