## Summary

SEBI has reviewed the inclusion of historical scenarios in stress testing for the Commodity Derivatives Segment. Based on representations, recommendations of the Risk Management Review Committee (RMRC), and public comments, SEBI has decided to lower the Z-score threshold from 10 to 5. This change replaces extreme price movements beyond the Z-score threshold of 5 with those corresponding to a Z-score of 5 for calculating peak historical returns in standardized stress testing, aimed at facilitating Ease of Doing Business.

## Key Points

- **Regulatory Modification:** Modifies paragraph 22 of Annexure O (Standardized Stress Testing for Commodity Derivatives) of the SEBI Master Circular for Commodity Derivatives Segment dated August 04, 2023.
- **Z-Score Reduction:** The Z-score threshold used to replace extreme price movements beyond the threshold in peak historical returns is reduced from 10 to 5.
- **Underlying Calculations:** Mean and sigma of returns over the applicable MPOR (Margin Period of Risk) across 15 years will continue to be used to calculate the Z-score.
- **Target Audience:** The circular applies to all Recognised Clearing Corporations having a Commodity Derivatives Segment.

## Regulatory Changes

- **Previous Provision (Z-Score of 10):** Paragraph 22 of Annexure O of the Master Circular dated August 04, 2023, prescribed that price movements corresponding to a Z-score of 10 would replace extreme price movements beyond that threshold in peak historical returns of all commodities.
- **Revised Provision (Z-Score of 5):** Price movements corresponding to a Z-score of 5 will now replace extreme price movements beyond that threshold in peak historical returns of all commodities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Clearing Corporations:** Recognised Clearing Corporations with Commodity Derivatives Segments must update their standardized stress testing models and systems to implement the revised Z-score threshold of 5.
- **Risk Management Framework:** Adjust Core Settlement Guarantee Fund (Core SGF) stress-testing parameters in alignment with the updated Part A (Scenarios - Historical Scenarios) rules.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** August 12, 2026.
- **Effective Date:** With immediate effect.

## Impact Assessment

- **Risk Management:** Lowering the Z-score from 10 to 5 means that less extreme price movements (within 5 standard deviations instead of 10) will be used to cap peak historical returns for stress testing. This will likely reduce the simulated stress losses in testing scenarios.
- **Financial/Collateral Impact:** A reduction in simulated stress losses could lead to a lower required size for the Core Settlement Guarantee Fund (Core SGF) or lower margin/collateral requirements for members, thereby facilitating "Ease of Doing Business" and freeing up liquidity.
- **Operational Alignment:** Clearing corporations need to immediately update their automated risk management and stress testing software.
