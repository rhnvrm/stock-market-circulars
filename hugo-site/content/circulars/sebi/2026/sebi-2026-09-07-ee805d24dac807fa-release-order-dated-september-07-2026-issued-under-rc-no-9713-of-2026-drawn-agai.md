## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated September 07, 2026, under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9713 of 2026 (drawn against RC No. 9173 of 2026) against Padmavathi Darak (PAN: AFMPD1863K). The matter pertains to trading in illiquid stock options at the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

## Key Points

- **Entity Involved:** Padmavathi Darak (PAN: AFMPD1863K).
- **Regulatory Reference:** Release order issued under RC No. 9713 of 2026 (drawn under RC No. 9173 of 2026).
- **Matter:** Trading in illiquid stock options at BSE.
- **Action:** Official release order issued by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new broad regulatory changes; this is an enforcement/recovery action release order specific to the named individual.

## Compliance Requirements

- Applicable only to the named noticee regarding compliance with recovery proceedings and release terms.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- This order has no direct impact on overall market operations or listed stock prices as it is an individual-specific recovery release proceeding.
