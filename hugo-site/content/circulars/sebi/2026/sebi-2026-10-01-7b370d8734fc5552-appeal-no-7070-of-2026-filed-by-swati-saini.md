## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority dismissed Appeal No. 7070 of 2026 filed by Swati Saini under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought demat, trading, and mutual fund account details of her husband for a pending maintenance case. SEBI upheld the CPIO's response that the requested information is not maintained by SEBI.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought financial and demat account details for a maintenance case under Section 125 CrPC and Section 24 HMA.
- SEBI's CPIO stated that the information sought is not maintained by SEBI.
- Citing Supreme Court precedence in *Central Board of Secondary Education & Anr. vs. Aditya Bandopadhyay & Ors*, the Appellate Authority noted that public authorities are not obligated to collect or collate information not part of their records.
- The appeal was accordingly dismissed.
