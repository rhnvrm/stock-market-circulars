## Summary

An appeal filed by Samar Imran against SEBI under the Right to Information Act, 2005 regarding the acceptability of Legal Heir/Warisan Certificates and applicability of Section 13(2) of the Indian Securities Act, 1920 read with Section 66(B) of the Government Securities Manual was disposed of. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the queries sought clarification, opinion, or interpretation, which do not fall under the definition of 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The Appellant sought information regarding whether Legal Heir Certificates from Uttar Pradesh with specific endorsements are acceptable for the transmission of securities.
- The Appellant also questioned the continued applicability of provisions from the Indian Securities Act, 1920 after the enactment of the Government Securities Act, 2006.
- SEBI's CPIO responded that the queries were in the nature of seeking clarifications and opinions, not 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- The Appellate Authority confirmed that the CPIO is not required to provide clarifications, opinions, interpretations, or create information under the RTI Act, citing precedents from the Central Information Commission (CIC).

## Regulatory Changes

- None. The order reiterates established principles under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act regarding queries seeking opinions/clarifications.
