## Summary

During the Global Fintech Fest (GFF) 2026 in Mumbai, SEBI's Chairman delivered a keynote address titled “Growing Fast, Growing Safe: Regulating a Large and Technology-Intensive Securities Market”. The address focused on predictive supervision using SupTech and AI, the Demat 2.0 corporate bond tokenisation pilot, and enhanced cyber and quantum resilience for the securities market.

## Key Points

- Highlighted predictive supervision through SupTech and artificial intelligence.
- Discussed the Demat 2.0 corporate bond tokenisation pilot initiative.
- Emphasized the importance of cyber and quantum resilience to ensure innovation scales safely without increasing market risk.
- Participated in a high-level panel discussion on capital market regulation in the age of AI, tokenisation, and quantum technology alongside international regulators from IOSCO and AFM (Netherlands).
