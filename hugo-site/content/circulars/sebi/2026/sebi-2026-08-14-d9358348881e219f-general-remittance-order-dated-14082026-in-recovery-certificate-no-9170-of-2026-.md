## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated August 14, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9170 of 2026 against Girish Kantilal Parmar (PAN: AUDPP6127A). The recovery action stems from penalty dues related to fraudulent or unauthorized trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9170 of 2026 issued against Girish Kantilal Parmar.
- Relates to proceedings and penalties concerning trading activities in DU Digital Technologies Limited.
- Directs the remittance of attached funds from bank accounts and demat accounts to satisfy unpaid regulatory dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed under the SEBI Act, 1992, for realization of penalties imposed by quasi-judicial authorities.

## Compliance Requirements

Mandatory remittance of attached funds by garnishees/financial institutions holding accounts of the defaulter to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Routine regulatory enforcement targeting specific non-compliant entities with no broader market-wide impact.
