## Summary

Prem Kumar Gurusamy filed an RTI application seeking the official address and contact details of the secretariat or authority handling matters related to Pearls Green Forests Ltd (PGF Ltd) for the Justice Vikramajit Sen Committee. SEBI provided the available office address of Justice Vikramjit Sen but noted that specific names and designations of persons receiving communications were not available with SEBI. The Appellate Authority upheld that public authorities can only furnish information available in their records.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought contact details and office addresses for the Justice Vikramajit Sen Committee concerning PGF Ltd matters.
- SEBI provided the postal and physical office address of the Office of Justice Vikramjit Sen.
- SEBI stated that the specific name and designation of the officer responsible for receiving representations was not available in SEBI records.
- The Appellate Authority dismissed the appeal, noting that SEBI is only obligated to provide information available on record.
