## Summary

An RTI appeal filed by Ritesh Kumar regarding the SEBI Grade A Law Officer Exam Phase II was dismissed by the Appellate Authority. The appellant sought certified copies of evaluated answer scripts and model answer keys. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that evaluated descriptive answer copies are held exclusively by IBPS under an MoU and are not shared with or available in the records of SEBI.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought evaluated answer scripts for Paper 1 and Paper 2 of the SEBI Grade A Law Officer Exam Phase II (held on 21.02.2026).
- CPIO replied that online recruitment exams are conducted by IBPS (an autonomous body) and evaluated descriptive scripts are not shared with SEBI.
- CPIO provided response sheets and right answer keys for the 2025 Legal Stream Phase II exam.
- Appellate Authority noted that under the RTI Act, information can only be provided if it is existent and available in the records of the public authority, citing previous CIC precedents.
- The appeal was dismissed as devoid of merit.
