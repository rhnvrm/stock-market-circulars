## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated September 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9209 of 2026 drawn against Pranav Kamleshkumar Trivedi. This action relates to investigations and recovery proceedings regarding trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under RC No. 9209 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Pranav Kamleshkumar Trivedi.
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, or remittance directives.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, garnishees, and intermediaries are required to remit funds or adhere to directions specified in the remittance order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Remittance Order: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights ongoing regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities and individuals involved in market irregularities in DU Digital Global Limited.
