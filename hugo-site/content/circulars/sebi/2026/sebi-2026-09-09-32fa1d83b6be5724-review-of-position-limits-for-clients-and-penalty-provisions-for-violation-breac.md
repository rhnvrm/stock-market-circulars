## Summary

SEBI has reviewed the position limits for clients and the penalty provisions for violations or breaches of position limits in the commodity derivatives segment, based on representations from stakeholders, working group recommendations, and public comments.

## Key Points

- Revised penal provisions for position limit violations under Annexure J of Chapter 3 of the SEBI Master Circular dated August 4, 2023.
- Monetary penalties for client-level violations in open interest are linked to the quantum/value of the violation and charged daily.
- Where violation is >2% of prescribed limits: Limit exceeded x Closing price x number of days x 2% (0.02) or Rs. 2,00,000/-, whichever is lower.
- Where violation is up to 2% of prescribed limits: Limit exceeded x Closing price x number of days x 2% (0.02) or Rs. 10,000/-, whichever is lower.
- Mandatory reduction of position by the next trading day; otherwise, the exchange will square off excess positions without notice.
- Additional square-off and penalty provisions for repeated violations within a calendar month.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updates Annexure J under sub-para (ii) of Para 3.4.3 of the SEBI Master Circular for Commodity Derivatives Segment dated August 4, 2023.

## Compliance Requirements

- Trading members and clients must ensure adherence to open interest position limits in commodity derivatives.
- Excess positions must be brought within limits by the next trading day following a violation to avoid mandatory exchange square-off and monetary penalties.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides structured penalty caps and clarity for position limit violations in commodity derivatives, aligning with ease of doing business objectives while maintaining market integrity.
