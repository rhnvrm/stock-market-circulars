## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice against Sadhna Bio Oils Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAKCS4057N) [Defaulter] under Recovery Certificate No. 9244 of 2026. This action arises from proceedings related to trading-based stock recommendations using social media (YouTube) in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Noticee / Defaulter: Sadhna Bio Oils Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAKCS4057N).
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9244 of 2026.
- Matter: Unauthorized trading-based stock recommendations using social media YouTube.
- Scrip: Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Issuing Authority: Recovery Officer, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and disgorgement amounts arising from market manipulation and unauthorized stock recommendations.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries must comply with the remittance advice and attachment orders to ensure recovery of dues and prevent unauthorized transfer of funds.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: October 2026 (Recovery Certificate No. 9244 of 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's regulatory oversight and strict recovery enforcement against entities violating securities laws through social media-based pump-and-dump schemes.
