## Summary

SEBI has issued notices of attachments in Recovery Certificate No. 9162 of 2026 against Rashmi Chandak (PAN: AEKPC4067M) pursuant to enforcement and recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate Number: RC No. 9162 of 2026
- Notice against: Rashmi Chandak
- PAN: AEKPC4067M
- Issuing Authority: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an enforcement and recovery action specific to an individual defaulter.

## Compliance Requirements

The named individual and concerned entities/banks holding accounts or assets of the noticee must comply with the attachment directions as specified in the recovery certificate order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order/Notice: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Individual regulatory enforcement action with negligible impact on overall market operations or listed securities.
