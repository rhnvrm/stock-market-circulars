## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority dismissed Appeal No. 6999 of 2026 filed by Shri T. Velsankar under the RTI Act, 2005. The appellant sought reasons for his prohibition from accessing the securities market and procedures for de-freezing his account. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the information is already publicly available pursuant to SEBI Circular No. SEBI/HO/MRD/DSA/CIR/P/2017/92 dated August 01, 2017, and NSE Circular Ref. No. 1102/2017.

## Key Points

- **Appellant**: Shri T. Velsankar (PAN: ADYPV06xxx).
- **Respondent**: CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai.
- **Information Sought**: Specific reasons for securities market prohibition and remedial steps for account de-freezing.
- **Ruling**: Publicly available information cannot be mandated to be provided under the RTI Act, as supported by judicial and CIC precedents.
