## Summary

SEBI issued proceedings and orders stemming from an Interim Order cum Show Cause Notice against Avadhut Sathe Trading Academy Private Limited (ASTAPL), Avadhut Dinkar Sathe, and Gouri Avadhut Sathe for engaging in unregistered investment advisory and research analyst activities under the guise of stock market education. The entities were found to be publishing selective profitable trades, making false claims of extraordinary returns, and violating provisions of the SEBI Act and PFUTP Regulations.

## Key Points

- ASTAPL and associated trainers engaged in unregistered investment advisory and research analyst services.
- Published selective profitable trades and misleading advertisements portraying unrealistic returns from stock market activities.
- Administrative warnings were previously issued in March 2024, which were flouted by continued misleading promotions.
- Prima facie violations identified under Section 12(1) of the SEBI Act, IA Regulations, RA Regulations, and PFUTP Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and directions under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B, and 11D of the SEBI Act, 1992 against unregistered entities engaging in fraudulent market practices.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals operating educational or training academies must refrain from providing direct or indirect investment advice, stock recommendations, or research reports without obtaining proper registration under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 and SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014.

## Important Dates

- Interim Order cum Show Cause Notice Date: December 04, 2025
- Investigation Appointment Date: July 24, 2025
- Order Date: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight on financial educators and training institutes to curb unregistered advisory services and misleading marketing claims that induce retail investors into securities markets.
