## Summary

This order details the background of the National Spot Exchange Limited (NSEL) matter, outlining the history of the Forward Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1952 (FCRA) exemption granted to NSEL, the introduction of paired contracts in September 2009, the findings of the Forward Markets Commission (FMC), the subsequent merger of FMC with SEBI in 2015, and the initiation of enquiry proceedings against stock brokers under the NSEL Settlement Scheme, 2025.

## Key Points

- The Department of Consumer Affairs granted an exemption from FCRA provisions to one-day forward contracts on NSEL in June 2007, subject to conditions including no short sales and mandatory delivery.
- In September 2009, NSEL introduced paired contracts allowing simultaneous buying and selling in the same commodity at different prices and durations to guarantee profits for buy contracts.
- FMC investigated trading data and concluded in December 2013 that 55 contracts violated the FCRA and exemption conditions.
- FMC merged with SEBI in 2015, following which SEBI examined broker roles and initiated enquiry proceedings against 302 stock brokers.
- Out of 302 proceedings, the Designated Authority recommended no action for 129 entities, and 12 were disposed of without directions, leaving 161 brokers with orders containing specific directions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation and application of the NSEL Settlement Scheme, 2025 for addressing pending proceedings related to NSEL platform trades.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock brokers and entities involved in NSEL paired contracts are subject to the terms, applications, and resolutions provided under the NSEL Settlement Scheme, 2025.

## Important Dates

- June 05, 2007: Exemption Notification granted to NSEL.
- September 2009: Introduction of paired contracts on NSEL.
- December 17, 2013: FMC passes order regarding FCRA violations.
- 2015: Merger of FMC with SEBI.

## Impact Assessment

- Resolves legacy regulatory and legal overhangs concerning trading members who participated in NSEL platform transactions through structured settlement schemes.
