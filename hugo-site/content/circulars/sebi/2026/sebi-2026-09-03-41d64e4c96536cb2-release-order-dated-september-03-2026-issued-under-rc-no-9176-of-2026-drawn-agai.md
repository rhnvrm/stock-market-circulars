## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated September 03, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9176 of 2026 drawn against Nikunj Sureshchandra Shah. This order lifts the previous attachment regarding the matter of dealing in trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Release Order issued under RC No. 9176 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Nikunj Sureshchandra Shah.
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Withdrawal of enforcement action and lifting of attachment pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are notified to unfreeze accounts and assets previously subjected to the attachment notice under RC No. 9176 of 2026 for this noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Indicates closure or resolution of recovery proceedings for the specific noticee concerning past market irregularities in DU Digital Global Limited.
