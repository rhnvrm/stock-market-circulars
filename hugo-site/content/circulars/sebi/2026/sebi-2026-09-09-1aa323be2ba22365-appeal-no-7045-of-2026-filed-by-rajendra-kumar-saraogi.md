## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7045 of 2026 filed by Rajendra Kumar Saraogi under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO of SEBI, regarding information on arbitration awards, member defaults, security deposits, and the Settlement Guarantee Fund (SGF) of the Calcutta Stock Exchange (CSE).

## Key Points

- The appellant sought records concerning Arbitration Case No. 74540 (R. Saraogi & Co. vs. Girdhari Mal Choraria).
- Inquiries covered the operating membership status and default declarations of Girdhari Mal Choraria at the Calcutta Stock Exchange (CSE).
- Questions were raised regarding the withholding, release, or forfeiture of security deposits and membership units, as well as the application of the Settlement Guarantee Fund (SGF).
