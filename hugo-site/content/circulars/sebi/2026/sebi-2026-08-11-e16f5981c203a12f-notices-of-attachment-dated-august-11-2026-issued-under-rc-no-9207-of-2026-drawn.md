## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 11, 2026, under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9207 of 2026 against Rathod Mahendrkumar. This action is taken in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- **Authority:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
- **Notice Type:** Notice of Attachment under Recovery Proceedings
- **Recovery Certificate:** RC No. 9207 of 2026
- **Noticee:** Rathod Mahendrkumar
- **Scrip Involved:** DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited)

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement and recovery action against a specific entity.

## Compliance Requirements

The noticee and concerned financial institutions/entities are required to comply with the attachment orders as per SEBI's recovery directives.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Directly impacts the named noticee and ensures enforcement of regulatory dues and penalties related to unauthorized or irregular trading activities in the scrip.
