## Summary

An appeal was filed by Shri Anivesh Kumar Pandey under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the response of the CPIO regarding the SEBI Grade A 2025 recruitment drive. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that the requested information regarding marks, cut-offs, and merit lists is already publicly available on the SEBI website.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought individual marks, weighted scores, waiting list status, top 100 candidate details, and category-wise cut-offs for the SEBI Grade A 2025 recruitment drive.
- CPIO directed the appellant to the relevant links on the SEBI website where the recruitment information is published.
- The Appellate Authority confirmed that information already in the public domain need not be supplied separately under the RTI Act.
