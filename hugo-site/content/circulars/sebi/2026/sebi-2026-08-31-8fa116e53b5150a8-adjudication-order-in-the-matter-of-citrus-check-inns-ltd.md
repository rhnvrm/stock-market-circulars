## Summary

This adjudication order relates to the proceedings against noticees (directors of Citrus Check Inns Ltd.) following the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) order dated June 15, 2026, which set aside the earlier Adjudication Order dated December 28, 2018, and remanded the matter to SEBI for fresh consideration.

## Key Points

- An earlier penalty of INR 50,00,000/- was imposed jointly and severally on Citrus Check Inns Ltd. and its directors under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992.
- Noticees 2, 3, and 4 appealed to SAT contending lack of service of SCN and hearing notices while detained in Central Jail, Nasik.
- SAT set aside the order dated December 28, 2018, qua the appellants and remanded the matter back to SEBI for fresh consideration.
- SEBI assigned the proceedings to the Adjudicating Officer on June 23, 2026, to complete proceedings by December 31, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Remand of adjudication proceedings for fresh inquiry adhering to principles of natural justice and proper service of notice.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees are required to appear before SEBI and cooperate with the inquiry proceedings without seeking adjournment.
- Final proceedings must be completed by December 31, 2026.

## Important Dates

- SAT Order date: June 15, 2026
- SEBI assignment date: June 23, 2026
- Appellant appearance date: July 15, 2026
- Proceedings completion deadline: December 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Re-evaluation of liability and adherence to due process of law for directors in collective investment scheme violations.
