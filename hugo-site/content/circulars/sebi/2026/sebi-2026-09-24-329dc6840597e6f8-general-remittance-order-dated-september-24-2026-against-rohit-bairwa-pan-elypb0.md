## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9168 of 2026 against Rohit Bairwa (PAN: ELYPB0179Q). This action arises from investigations into price and volume manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9168 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Rohit Bairwa (PAN: ELYPB0179Q).
- Matter: Trading activities and price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of SEBI to recover outstanding dues and penalties resulting from unlawful gains.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of penalties and disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and garnishees are mandated to remit attached funds and dues to SEBI as specified in the remittance order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Remittance Order: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's strict regulatory stance and enforcement mechanisms in recovering dues from entities involved in market manipulation in SME scrips.
