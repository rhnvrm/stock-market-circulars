## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Attachment of Bank and Post Office Accounts dated August 19, 2026, bearing Notice No. 15715 of 2026 under Recovery Certificate No. 9264 of 2026. The attachment is initiated against the defaulter Surbhi Aggarwal (PAN: BNIPA1542D) in the matter of front running involving Navnit Gadoya, Surbhi Aggarwal, and Chiranggi Irish Shah.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter Name:** Surbhi Aggarwal
- **PAN:** BNIPA1542D
- **Case Matter:** Front running by Navnit Gadoya, Surbhi Aggarwal, and Chiranggi Irish Shah
- **Recovery Certificate No.:** 9264 of 2026
- **Notice No.:** 15715 of 2026
- **Date of Notice:** August 19, 2026
- **Action Taken:** Attachment of bank and post office accounts under applicable recovery provisions.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery action executed pursuant to SEBI's powers for recovery of dues under recovery certificates.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and post offices holding accounts in the name of the defaulter are directed to attach the accounts and prevent any debits or withdrawals up to the specified dues.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct regulatory and enforcement impact on the named defaulter regarding asset freezing and account attachment to recover dues.
