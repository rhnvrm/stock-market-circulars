## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has released the 21st edition of the Handbook of Statistics (HBS) for the year 2025-26. Prepared by SEBI's Department of Economic and Policy Analysis (DEPA), the handbook serves as a single-stop resource for historical time-series data on the Indian securities market.

## Key Points

- The HBS 2025-26 comprises 85 annual series tables and 68 quarterly/monthly series tables, totaling 153 tables.
- Covers growth data on primary markets, secondary markets, derivative segments across various instruments, and investments by different investment vehicles.
- Includes statistics on regulatory activities conducted by SEBI and Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs), along with global stock market reference indicators.
- Feedback and comments from stakeholders can be directed to hbs@sebi.gov.in.

## Impact Assessment

This is a statistical and reference publication intended for researchers, analysts, market participants, and the general public. It has no direct operational or compliance impact on listed companies or market intermediaries.
