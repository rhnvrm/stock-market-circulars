## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9236 of 2026 against Manish Mishra (PAN: AMPPM6823L) in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited. This order is directed towards the attachment and remittance of funds for the recovery of dues owed to SEBI.

## Key Points

- Defaulter Name: Manish Mishra
- PAN: AMPPM6823L
- Recovery Certificate Number: RC No. 9236 of 2026
- Matter: Sadhna Broadcast Limited
- Order Date: September 24, 2026
- Order Type: General Remittance Order

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of recovery under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, authorizing attachment of bank accounts, securities, and other assets to satisfy outstanding dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Garnishees and financial institutions holding funds or assets of the defaulter must immediately remit the attached amounts to SEBI's recovery account as per the directive.

## Important Dates

- September 24, 2026: Date of General Remittance Order

## Impact Assessment

Strengthens regulatory enforcement and expedites recovery of unlawful gains from entities penalized for stock manipulation.
