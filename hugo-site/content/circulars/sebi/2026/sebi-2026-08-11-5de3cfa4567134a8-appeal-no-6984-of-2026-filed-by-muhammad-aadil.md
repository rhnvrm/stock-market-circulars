## Summary

Muhammad Aadil filed an RTI application seeking detailed information (including survey numbers, GPS locations, and circle rates) regarding unsold immovable properties of PGF Limited and PACL Limited across five states (Uttarakhand, Uttar Pradesh, Delhi, Haryana, and Punjab). SEBI's CPIO responded that the information is not available with SEBI and advised the appellant to approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's reply, noting that SEBI cannot provide information not in its possession and that the disposal of properties and repayment to investors is entrusted to the Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought complete lists, GPS coordinates, and circle rates of unsold immovable properties of PGF Limited and PACL Limited.
- CPIO stated that the requested information is not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The Appellate Authority confirmed that SEBI cannot invent or provide information it does not possess, upholding the CPIO's response.
- Responsibility for property disposal and investor repayment for PACL/PGF rests with the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
