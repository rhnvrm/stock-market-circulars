## Summary

Appellant Keshaw Prajapati filed an RTI appeal under the RTI Act, 2005, seeking clarification on eligibility criteria for registration as a Research Analyst (including educational qualifications, mandatory certifications, and experience requirements). SEBI's CPIO and the Appellate Authority noted that the requested information is already publicly available via Regulation 7 of SEBI (RA) Regulations, 2014 and the Master Circular for Research Analyst dated February 06, 2026. Consequently, the appeal was dismissed.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought clarification regarding registration requirements for a Research Analyst under SEBI regulations.
- Respondent directed the appellant to Regulation 7 of SEBI (RA) Regulations, 2014 and the Master Circular for Research Analyst dated February 06, 2026.
- Appellate Authority upheld the response, citing established judicial precedents that public authorities are not obligated under the RTI Act to provide information already available in the public domain.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (appellate order upholding existing disclosure norms regarding publicly accessible regulations).

## Compliance Requirements

- Applicants seeking registration as Research Analysts must refer to Regulation 7 of SEBI (RA) Regulations, 2014 and the relevant Master Circular.

## Important Dates

- Date of Application: July 01, 2026
- Date of CPIO Response: July 22, 2026
- Date of Appeal: July 27, 2026
- Date of Appellate Order: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reaffirms that RTI applications seeking details already published in regulatory master circulars and regulations will not impose additional information-generation obligations on SEBI.
