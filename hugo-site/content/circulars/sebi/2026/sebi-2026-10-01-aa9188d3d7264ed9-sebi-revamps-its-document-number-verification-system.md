## Summary

SEBI has revamped its "Document Number Verification System"—originally launched in April 2025—to strengthen authentication credibility and integrate with SEBI's E-Office outward number format.

## Key Points

- "Subject Matter" has been added as a mandatory field for verifying physical letters, notices, and show-cause notices.
- The verification system is now aligned with the Outward Number format generated through the E-Office outward module of SEBI.
- Required verification details include the outward number, name of issuing SEBI official, date of the letter, subject matter, and name of the recipient.
- Communications issued prior to September 2026 can be verified by emailing dnvs@sebi.gov.in.

## Regulatory Changes

The Document Number Verification System has been modified to incorporate additional mandatory validation parameters ('Subject Matter') and standardise outward numbering with the E-Office module.

## Compliance Requirements

Recipients or authorised representatives receiving notices or letters from SEBI must use the revamped online portal on the SEBI homepage (Authenticate Document Number Issued by SEBI) by entering all mandatory details correctly.

## Important Dates

- Operationalization of Revamped System: September 2026
- Date of Press Release: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The enhancement prevents potential fraudulent communications by providing a robust, multi-parameter verification framework for all official SEBI letters and notices.
