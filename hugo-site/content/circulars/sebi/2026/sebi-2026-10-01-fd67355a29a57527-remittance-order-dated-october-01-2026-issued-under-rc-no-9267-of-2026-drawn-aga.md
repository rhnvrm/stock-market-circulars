## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Remittance Order dated October 01, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9267 of 2026 drawn against S S Traders (Proprietor Mr. Marvadi Parsotambhai). This regulatory enforcement action pertains to investigations into trading activities in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited (AISL).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9267 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: S S Traders (Proprietor Mr. Marvadi Parsotambhai).
- Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited (AISL).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

Execution of statutory recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the recovery of penalties/dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated entities are required to comply with the remittance and freezing orders as directed by the SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: October 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight and rigorous recovery enforcement against entities involved in market violations concerning ANI Integrated Services Limited.
