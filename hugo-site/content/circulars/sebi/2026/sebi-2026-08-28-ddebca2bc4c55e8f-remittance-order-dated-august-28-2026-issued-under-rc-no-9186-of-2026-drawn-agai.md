## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated August 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9186 of 2026 drawn against Pranav Kamleshkumar Trivedi in the matter of trading activities and price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9186 of 2026 against Pranav Kamleshkumar Trivedi.
- Relates to the fraudulent trading activities and artificial volume/price creation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited.
- Enforces recovery of outstanding monetary penalties imposed by SEBI for regulatory violations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery proceedings under SEBI Act provisions for unpaid monetary penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

- The entity must remit the specified dues immediately to avoid further attachment of bank accounts, properties, and other assets.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory strictness against entities failing to clear penalties associated with stock price manipulation and unauthorized market practices.
