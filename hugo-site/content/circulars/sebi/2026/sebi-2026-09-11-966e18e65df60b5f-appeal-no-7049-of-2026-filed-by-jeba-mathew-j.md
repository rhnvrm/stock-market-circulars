## Summary

An appeal was filed under the Right to Information Act, 2005 by Jeba Mathew J against the response of the CPIO, SEBI regarding information sought on sanctioned strength, present strength in Grade A to F for the Electrical Engineering Stream, and retirement details.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information regarding sanctioned strength, present strength in Grades A to F for the Electrical Engineering Stream, and upcoming retirements in the next 2 years.
- SEBI's CPIO and the Appellate Authority noted that the current sanctioned strength of SEBI Officers (Grades A to F) and Persons in Position as of June 30, 2026, are 1166 and 1026 respectively.
- Persons in position (Grade A to F) in the Electrical Stream as of June 30, 2026, stand at 10.
- Information regarding impending retirements was denied under section 7(9) of the RTI Act due to disproportionate diversion of resources as the data is maintained across separate files.
