## Summary

SEBI issued Prohibitory Order no. 1575/2026 on October 15, 2026, against Venkatachari Suresh under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 8710 of 2025 in the matter of Securekloud Technologies Ltd. The order is aimed at recovering outstanding penalties and dues through the attachment and prohibition of transfer of movable and immovable properties.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Order No. 1575/2026 issued by SEBI on October 15, 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Venkatachari Suresh (Chairman and CEO of Securekloud Technologies Ltd).
- Recovery Certificate: RC No. 8710 of 2025.
- Target: Prohibition against disposing of, transferring, or alienating movable or immovable properties to realize unpaid dues/penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for non-payment of regulatory penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are mandated to restrict asset transfers, freeze accounts, and assist the Recovery Officer in enforcing the prohibitory order against the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Prohibitory Order: October 15, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Reference: RC No. 8710 of 2025

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's strict enforcement and recovery measures against promoters and key managerial personnel defaulting on statutory penalties, ensuring accountability in corporate governance and financial compliance.
