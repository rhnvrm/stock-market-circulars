## Summary

SEBI has issued a settlement order under the chairmanship of Whole Time Member Shri Kamlesh Chandra Varshney in respect of Adani Group companies and associated key managerial personnel and promoters. The proceedings originated from complaints received in 2020 alleging non-compliance with Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) requirements, leading to Show Cause Notices (SCN) issued on September 27, 2024, and supplementary SCN on March 3, 2025.

## Key Points

- Entities involved include Adani Enterprises Limited, Adani Power Limited, Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Limited, Adani Transmission Limited (now Adani Energy Solutions Limited), and several key individuals/promoters.
- Allegations relate to non-compliance with MPS requirements under Rule 19A of SCRR, Clauses 35 and 40A of the Listing Agreement, and Regulations 31 and 38 of LODR Regulations.
- Settlement applications were filed by the respective companies and individuals under SEBI settlement regulations.
- Proceedings under Section 11(1), 11(4), 11B(1), 11B(2) of SEBI Act and Section 12A of SCRA addressed via the settlement mechanism.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal resolution of enforcement and adjudicatory proceedings through the SEBI settlement framework for alleged disclosure and shareholding norm infractions.

## Compliance Requirements

Adherence to settlement terms, payment of settlement amounts as determined, and continued compliance with minimum public shareholding and listing norms.

## Important Dates

- Initial Complaint Period: June - July 2020
- Investigation Initiation: October 23, 2020
- Show Cause Notice (SCN): September 27, 2024
- Supplementary SCN (SSCN): March 3, 2025
- Settlement Order Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Brings regulatory closure to long-standing investigations and proceedings concerning Adani Group companies, providing regulatory certainty regarding shareholding structures.
