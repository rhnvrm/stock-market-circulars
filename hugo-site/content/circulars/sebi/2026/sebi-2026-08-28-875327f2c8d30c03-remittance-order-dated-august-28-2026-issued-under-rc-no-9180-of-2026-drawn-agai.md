## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated August 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9180 of 2026 drawn against Sanjay Savjibhai Parmar. This follows previous attachment proceedings regarding investigations into manipulative trading activities and volume rigging in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under RC No. 9180 of 2026 on August 28, 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Sanjay Savjibhai Parmar.
- Matter: Trading activities and price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Directs the transfer/remittance of attached funds from bank/demat accounts towards the settlement of SEBI dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of disgorgement amounts, penalties, and associated interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and depository participants are required to remit funds from the attached accounts of the noticee as specified in the SEBI recovery order.

## Important Dates

- Remittance Order Date: August 28, 2026
- Prior Attachment Notice / Recovery Proceedings: 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's rigorous follow-through on recovery proceedings and execution of remittance orders against entities found guilty of market manipulation.
