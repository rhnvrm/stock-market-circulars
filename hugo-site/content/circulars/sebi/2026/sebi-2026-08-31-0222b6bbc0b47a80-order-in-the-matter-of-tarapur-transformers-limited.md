## Summary

This SEBI order addresses multiple violations by Tarapur Transformers Limited (TTL) and various noticees, including promoter group entities and connected parties. The core issues examined include the diversion of company funds, questionable trade receivables, corporate governance lapses, and non-compliance with statutory summons issued by SEBI under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Key Points

- Investigation uncovered significant diversion of funds involving TTL and connected promoter group entities/related parties.
- Issues related to trade receivables formed a major part of the findings spanning several financial years.
- Severe corporate governance failures and lack of adequate internal controls were identified within the company.
- Multiple noticees failed to comply with summons issued during the investigation.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement actions and directions under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1), 11B(2), and penal provisions under Section 15A(a), 15HA, and 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees and the company are subject to specific directions, penalties, and remedial measures as mandated in the final order sections.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened scrutiny on listed entities regarding fund flows, related party transactions, and compliance with regulatory summons.
