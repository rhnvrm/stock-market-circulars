## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 10, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 9226 of 2026 against Jigar Bhailalbhai Khalash (PAN: ESZPK4415E). This order relates to recovery proceedings initiated in the matter of fraudulent and unfair trading activities in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter:** Jigar Bhailalbhai Khalash (PAN: ESZPK4415E).
- **Recovery Certificate:** No. 9226 of 2026.
- **Matter:** Trading activities and creation of artificial volumes in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- **Action:** General Remittance Order directing the transfer of attached funds toward satisfying outstanding regulatory dues.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A (Individual enforcement and recovery action under SEBI Act, 1992).

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and custodians holding attached accounts or assets of the noticee are required to remit the specified funds to SEBI's recovery account in compliance with the order.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** September 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is an enforcement action targeted at a specific individual defaulter and has no broad operational impact on the general market or listed entities, reinforcing SEBI's recovery mechanism for unpaid monetary penalties.
