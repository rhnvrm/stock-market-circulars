## Summary

SEBI has issued Notices of Attachment (AP Nos. 15695 and 15696 of 2026) dated August 18, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9228 of 2026 against Fulabhai Bhitor (PAN: DYLPB2813Q). This action is taken in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited to recover outstanding dues.

## Key Points

- Notices of Attachment (AP Nos. 15695 and 15696 of 2026) issued under RC No. 9228 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Fulabhai Bhitor (PAN: DYLPB2813Q).
- Target Assets: Bank accounts, Post Office accounts, Demat Accounts, and mutual fund folios.
- Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, post offices, financial institutions, and intermediaries must freeze the specified accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios to prevent any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict regulatory enforcement and asset attachment for non-compliance and recovery certificates regarding trading in ANI Integrated Services Limited.
