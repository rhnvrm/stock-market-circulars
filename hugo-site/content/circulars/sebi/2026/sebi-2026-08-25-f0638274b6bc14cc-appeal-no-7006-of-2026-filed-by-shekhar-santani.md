## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7006 of 2026 filed by Shekhar Santani concerning query no. 1 of his RTI application. The appellant had sought internal guidelines or circular interpretations explaining the expression 'as may be available' under Paragraph 7(c) of SEBI Circular HO/38/13/1122026-MIRSD-POD/I/3750/2026 dated January 30, 2026. The Appellate Authority found the CPIO's invocation of Section 8(1)(a) of the RTI Act insufficiently justified and remitted the matter back for de novo consideration.

## Key Points

- The RTI appeal challenged SEBI CPIO's response exempting information under Section 8(1)(a) of the RTI Act.
- Query pertained to the operational meaning and legal effect of 'as may be available' in proof of purchase requirements under SEBI Circular dated January 30, 2026.
- The Appellate Authority noted that the CPIO failed to provide sufficient justification for invoking Section 8(1)(a).
- The query has been remitted to the respondent for de novo consideration.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an adjudicatory order under the RTI Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

- CPIO, SEBI is directed to re-evaluate query no. 1 and provide an appropriate response in terms of the RTI Act within 15 days.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 25, 2026
- Response Deadline: Within 15 days from the receipt of the order

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is limited to internal RTI processing and does not affect market participants directly.
