## Summary

A Remittance Order dated August 28, 2026 has been issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9183 of 2026 drawn against Nayan Mahendrabhai Thakkar in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9183 of 2026.
- Drawn against defaulter/noticee Nayan Mahendrabhai Thakkar.
- Relates to trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).
- Directs remittance of dues towards recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

[None]

## Compliance Requirements

Entities holding funds or accounts pertaining to the noticee must comply with the remittance directions as specified in the order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Individual enforcement action with no broad market-wide operational or structural impact.
