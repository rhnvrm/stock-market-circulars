## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated August 25, 2026, in respect of Ankit Modi (PAN: AENPM9852P) regarding Recovery Certificate No. RC6746 of 2023 in the matter of Illiquid Stock Options. This indicates the formal lifting of attachments or resolution of recovery proceedings for the individual.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued in respect of Ankit Modi (PAN: AENPM9852P).
- Certificate Number: RC6746 of 2023.
- Matter: Illiquid Stock Options.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Lifting of recovery proceedings and associated attachments pursuant to compliance or settlement under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions and banks are notified to release any frozen accounts or attached assets corresponding to the specified recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: August 25, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Concludes recovery proceedings for the individual involved in the illiquid stock options case with no broader market impact.
