## Summary

An RTI appeal filed by Sanjay Singh against SEBI's CPIO regarding statutory disclosures and internal incident reports concerning a fire outbreak at Tata Communications Limited's facility was disposed of. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that public filings are available on stock exchange websites and that SEBI does not possess the internal incident report requested.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought certified copies of statutory disclosures, material event alerts, and internal incident reports regarding a fire outbreak at the Next-Gen Tower facility of Tata Communications Limited.
- SEBI's CPIO informed the appellant that listed company filings are publicly available on stock exchange websites and that the internal incident report is not available with SEBI.
- The Appellate Authority affirmed that information already in the public domain or not held by a public authority cannot be demanded under the RTI Act.
