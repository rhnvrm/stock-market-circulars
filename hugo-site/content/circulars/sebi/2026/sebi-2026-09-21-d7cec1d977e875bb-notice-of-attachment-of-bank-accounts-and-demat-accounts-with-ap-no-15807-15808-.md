## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated September 21, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 2001 of 2019 against Chandrakant A. Parekh (PAN: AHXPP5708J) in the matter of IPO Irregularities 2003-2005. The attachment covers bank accounts and demat accounts under AP No. 15807 and 15808 of 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 2001 of 2019.
- Defaulter: Chandrakant A. Parekh (PAN: AHXPP5708J).
- Attachment Reference: AP No. 15807 & 15808 of 2026 dated September 21, 2026.
- Matter: IPO Irregularities 2003-2005.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of dues and penalties related to historical IPO irregularities.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and depositories are required to freeze the bank and demat accounts of the noticee as specified in the attachment notice and prevent any unauthorized withdrawals, transfers, or debits.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's ongoing regulatory rigor and persistence in enforcing recovery proceedings for historical market violations and IPO irregularities.
