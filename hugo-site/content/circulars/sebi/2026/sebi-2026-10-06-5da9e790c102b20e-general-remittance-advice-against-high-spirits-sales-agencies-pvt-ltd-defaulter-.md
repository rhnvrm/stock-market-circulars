## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice under Recovery Certificate No. 9274 of 2026 against High Spirits Sales Agencies Pvt. Ltd. (Defaulter, PAN: AACCH3216L) in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited, directing the remittance of recovered dues/amounts pursuant to recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9274 of 2026
- Defaulter: High Spirits Sales Agencies Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AACCH3216L)
- Subject Matter: Sadhna Broadcast Limited
- Issued by: Recovery Officer, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
- Purpose: General Remittance Advice for transfer/remittance of attached/recovered funds towards satisfaction of dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures executed under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and dues from defaulters.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated entities must comply with the remittance advice by transferring the specified attached funds to SEBI's recovery account.

## Important Dates

- Date of Advice / Order: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's active enforcement and recovery mechanism to enforce regulatory dues and protect market integrity in connection with Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
