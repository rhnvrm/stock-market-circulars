## Summary

An appeal filed under the Right to Information Act, 2005 by Aniket Singh against SEBI's CPIO response regarding the request for a certified copy of an evaluated answer script for the Phase-2 Online Examination for Recruitment of Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) General Stream, 2025 was dismissed by the Appellate Authority.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought a certified copy of his evaluated answer script for Paper 1 (Descriptive English) of the SEBI Grade A Phase-2 exam, along with any evaluation remarks or moderation notes.
- SEBI's CPIO responded that the recruitment exam is conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) under an MoU, and copies of evaluated descriptive papers are retained solely by IBPS and not shared with SEBI.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's stance, citing the CIC decision in *Lakshminarayanan R vs. SEBI*, which established that information sought under the RTI Act must be existent and available in the records of the public authority.
