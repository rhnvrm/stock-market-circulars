## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Advice against defaulter Santosh Kumar (PAN: AEKPK6751Q) under Recovery Certificate No. 9152 of 2026. This action arises from recovery proceedings connected to an investigation into the trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of Delta Leasing and Finance Ltd.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter Name:** Santosh Kumar
- **PAN:** AEKPK6751Q
- **Scrip Involved:** Delta Leasing and Finance Ltd.
- **Recovery Certificate No.:** 9152 of 2026
- **Issuing Authority:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative enforcement and recovery action.

## Compliance Requirements

The defaulter is required to remit the specified dues immediately pursuant to the recovery certificate and notice of demand issued by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Issuance:** August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This is an individual enforcement proceeding with negligible direct impact on broader market operations or liquidity of the scrip.
