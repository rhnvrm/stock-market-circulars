## Summary

SEBI issued an interim order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992, against Kore Digital Limited and its key management personnel (Ravindra Doshi, Chaitanya Doshi, and Kashmira Doshi). The examination revealed prima facie misrepresentation of financial statements, multifold inflation of revenue from operations through recently acquired subsidiaries, suspicious bank account entries, and diversion of preferential issue proceeds.

## Key Points

- Prima facie evidence of financial statement misrepresentation and revenue inflation in Kore Digital Limited following its SME IPO.
- Suspicious accounting entries and fund flows identified in the company's books and bank accounts.
- Alleged diversion of preferential issue proceeds.
- Directives issued against Noticees to protect investor interests and maintain market integrity.

## Regulatory Changes

- Interim directions restricting the company and noticees from accessing the securities market or dealing in securities until further orders.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees and the company must comply with all interim restrictions and directions specified in the SEBI order.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 2026
- Examination Period: April 1, 2023 to March 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High negative impact on Kore Digital Limited's stock and operations, reinforcing strict regulatory scrutiny on SME-listed firms exhibiting abnormal financial growth and fund utilization discrepancies.
