## Summary

SEBI has announced an extension of the implementation timeline for provisions regarding norms for base price, price bands, call auction in the pre-open session, and close-out procedures for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs). The implementation date has been moved from September 1, 2026, to September 7, 2026.

## Key Points

- Extension of implementation timeline for ETF trading norms.
- Revised effective date: September 7, 2026 (previously September 1, 2026).
- Covers base price, dynamic price bands, call auction in pre-open sessions, and close-out procedures.
- Decision made in consultation with stock exchanges to ensure seamless technical transition and operational readiness.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modified the effective implementation date for ETF trading norms to September 7, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Recognized Stock Exchanges, Clearing Corporations, and Asset Management Companies (AMCs) must align their systems and processes with the revised timeline.

## Important Dates

- **Original Implementation Date:** September 1, 2026
- **Revised Implementation Date:** September 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides market participants with additional days for system testing and operational alignment, reducing transition risks.
