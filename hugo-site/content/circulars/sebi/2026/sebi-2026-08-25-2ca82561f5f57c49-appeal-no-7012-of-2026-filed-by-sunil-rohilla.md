## Summary

Sunil Rohilla filed an RTI appeal against the CPIO, SEBI, seeking details of objections received against a property put for sale through e-auction by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee in the matter of PACL Limited. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response stating that the requested information is not available with SEBI and that such matters are handled directly by the Committee.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought details of objections and objectors for a property sold through e-auction under Tender No. JRMLC/DL/SED_1.
- The appellant is the auction purchaser holding a Sale Certificate issued by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- SEBI respondent and Appellate Authority noted that the requested information is not available with SEBI and advised approaching the Committee directly.
- The appeal was disposed of without finding any deficiency in the CPIO's response, citing precedent that CPIO cannot invent information not in possession.
