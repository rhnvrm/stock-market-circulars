## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 4722 of 2022 drawn against Veera Kumar Das Surapanani in the matter of Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 4722 of 2022
- Defaulter / Noticee: Veera Kumar Das Surapanani
- Company: Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd.
- Action: Notice of completion / satisfaction of recovery proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Closure of recovery proceedings and release of any applicable attachments or encumbrances upon fulfillment of recovery certificate requirements.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: September 30, 2026
