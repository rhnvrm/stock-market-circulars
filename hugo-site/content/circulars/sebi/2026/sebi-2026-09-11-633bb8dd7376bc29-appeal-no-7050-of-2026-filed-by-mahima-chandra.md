## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7050 of 2026 filed by Mahima Chandra under the RTI Act, 2005. The appellant had sought information regarding recruitment plans, calendars, and decisions for SEBI Officer Grade A (Information Technology Stream) for the calendar year 2026. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, stating that the queries were in the nature of seeking clarification, opinion, or confirmation, which do not fall under the definition of 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details on whether SEBI approved any recruitment plan, calendar, or vacancy document for Grade A (IT Stream) in 2026.
- The CPIO responded that such queries seek clarification and do not constitute 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, while noting that recruitment details are published on the SEBI website as vacancies arise.
- The Appellate Authority confirmed that the CPIO is not required to provide clarifications, opinions, or create/interpret information under the RTI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes introduced; order clarifies the scope of 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
