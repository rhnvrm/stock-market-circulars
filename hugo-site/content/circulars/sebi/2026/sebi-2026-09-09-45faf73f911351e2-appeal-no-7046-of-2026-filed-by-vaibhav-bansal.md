## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7046 of 2026 filed by Vaibhav Bansal under the RTI Act, 2005. The appellant sought information regarding candidates' post-qualification experience and roll numbers for the SEBI Grade A legal stream selection announced on June 23, 2026. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that collating the information from separate files would disproportionately divert SEBI's resources, exempting it under Section 7(9) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought candidate count and roll numbers for those possessing 2 years post-qualification experience in SEBI Grade A legal stream results announced on June 23, 2026.
- Appellant also requested detailed marks distribution for his own Phase II Paper 2 exam.
- Respondent provided marks for Phase II Paper 2 but claimed exemption under Section 7(9) for queries regarding experience and roll numbers due to voluminous records spread across multiple files.
- Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's stance, citing precedents on disproportionate resource diversion.
