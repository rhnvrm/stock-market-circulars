## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7100 of 2026 filed by Abhishek Gautam under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought information regarding PACL registration numbers, refund status, land allotment details, and refund timelines. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the requested information was not available with SEBI and noted that PACL asset disposal and investor repayments are managed by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- The appellant filed an RTI application seeking action taken, legal grounds, land allotment details, and refund timelines for PACL registration numbers U016273xxx and U016365xxx.
- SEBI CPIO stated that the requested information was not available and directed the appellant to public notices and status reports on the SEBI website.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, citing Central Information Commission (CIC) precedent that SEBI cannot invent information not in its possession.
- Reiterated that the responsibility for property disposal and investor repayment for PACL is entrusted to the Justice (Retd.) R. M. Lodha Committee.

No new regulatory changes or policy directives were introduced; this order addresses an individual RTI appeal under existing legal frameworks.
