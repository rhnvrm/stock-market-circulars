## Summary

Bharat Bhushan Sharma filed an appeal (Appeal No. 6973 of 2026) under the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005, seeking information on when his investment of Rs. 4,76,000/- in the Saradha Group would be refunded. SEBI responded that the disposal of assets and investor refunds for the Saradha Group of Companies was assigned to the Hon'ble Justice (Retired) S.P. Talukdar Committee ("One Man Committee") by the Hon'ble Calcutta High Court. Consequently, the requested information is not available with SEBI. The Appellate Authority upheld this response and dismissed the appeal, noting that the CPIO cannot invent information not in SEBI's possession.

## Key Points

- **Appellant:** Bharat Bhushan Sharma.
- **Query:** Timeline for refunding an investment of Rs. 4,76,000/- in Saradha Group.
- **SEBI Response:** The refund process is managed by the court-appointed Hon'ble Justice (Retired) S.P. Talukdar Committee (One Man Committee), not SEBI.
- **Ruling:** The Appellate Authority dismissed the appeal, stating that the CPIO cannot provide or generate information that SEBI does not possess.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an individual adjudication under the Right to Information Act, 2005, and does not introduce or modify regulatory policies.
