SEBI has issued a notice regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 3875 of 2021 dated June 28, 2021, issued against defaulter Archit Jindal PAN: AGNPJ5775G in the matter of Focus Industrial Resources Limited.

- Recovery Certificate No.: 3875 of 2021 dated June 28, 2021.
- Defaulter: Archit Jindal (PAN: AGNPJ5775G).
- Company Matter: Focus Industrial Resources Limited.
- Status: Recovery certificate completed/satisfied.

Formal closure of recovery proceedings by the SEBI Recovery Officer upon full satisfaction or resolution of dues under the recovery certificate.

## Compliance Requirements

Notices and freezing orders associated with this specific recovery certificate against the named defaulter stand withdrawn or satisfied.

- Original Recovery Certificate Date: June 28, 2021
- Completion Notice Date: August 2026

Demonstrates successful conclusion of regulatory recovery proceedings, removing encumbrances or restrictions related specifically to this recovery certificate for the individual.
