## Summary

Appellate Authority order dated September 22, 2026, on Appeal No. 7098 of 2026 filed by Bhupal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against CPIO, SEBI. The appellant sought certified copies of records and details regarding land alleged to be allotted against PACL Certificate No. U016261xxx. The respondent stated that the requested information is not available with SEBI, and the Appellate Authority upheld this response citing precedence that CPIO cannot invent information not in its possession.

## Key Points

- Appellant is the holder of PACL Certificate No. U016261xxx and sought details regarding land allotment shown against the certificate.
- Requested documents included records provided by PACL Ltd., allotment letter, possession documents, sale deed, and database entries.
- CPIO, SEBI responded that the requested information is not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to SEBI website for PACL public notices and status reports.
- Appellate Authority noted that SEBI cannot provide information not in its possession or invent records, citing CIC decision in Sh. Pattipati Rama Murthy vs. CPIO, SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes; reaffirmation of RTI Act provisions regarding non-availability of records.

## Compliance Requirements

- None applicable to market entities; reiterates statutory limitations on information availability under RTI Act.
