## Summary

Jetha Global Master Fund filed a suo-motu settlement application with SEBI under the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018, to settle potential enforcement proceedings regarding non-compliance with Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPI) regulations, including delays in intimating changes in non-resident Indian (NRI) control and beneficial ownership.

## Key Points

- The Applicant is registered with SEBI as a Foreign Portfolio Investor (FPI) since October 23, 2021.
- The Applicant was under NRI control between March 14, 2025, and October 08, 2025, violating FPI eligibility provisions.
- The Applicant failed to intimate material changes to the Designated Depository Participant (DDP) within the prescribed timelines.
- The settlement application proposes to resolve the proceedings without admitting or denying the findings of facts and conclusions of law.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulations introduced; enforcement action settled under the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- FPIs must strictly adhere to timelines for intimating material changes, including changes in control, beneficial ownership, and eligibility criteria to DDPs.

## Important Dates

- Registration Date: October 23, 2021
- Period of Non-Compliance: March 14, 2025, to October 08, 2025
- Date of Order: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- The settlement has a localized impact on the applicant entity and reinforces SEBI's enforcement of disclosure and reporting timelines for FPIs.
