## Summary

SEBI has issued a Certificate of Completion dated August 31, 2026, under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9101 of 2026 in favor of Nilesh Pramodbhai Shah (HUF). The proceedings pertain to earlier regulatory actions regarding dealing in illiquid stock options at the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

## Key Points

- Entity involved: Nilesh Pramodbhai Shah (HUF).
- Recovery Certificate No.: RC No. 9101 of 2026.
- Issuance Date: August 31, 2026.
- Matter: Dealing in illiquid stock options at BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory policy changes; this is an administrative completion of recovery proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

Concludes the recovery proceedings for the named entity under the specified recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of Certificate of Completion: August 31, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

This is an individual enforcement closure with negligible broader market impact.
