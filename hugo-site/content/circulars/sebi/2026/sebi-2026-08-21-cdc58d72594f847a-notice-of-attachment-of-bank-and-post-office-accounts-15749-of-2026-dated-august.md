## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment under Recovery Certificate No. 9250 of 2026 dated August 21, 2026, against Saurabh Gupta (PAN: BFEPG0692E), attaching his bank and post office accounts to recover dues related to unauthorized stock recommendations using YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Defaulter: Saurabh Gupta (PAN: BFEPG0692E).
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9250 of 2026.
- Matter: Trading based stock recommendations using social media YouTube regarding Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Action: Attachment of bank and post office accounts for dues recovery.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement action via attachment of financial accounts under SEBI recovery proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Banks and post offices are prohibited from permitting any debits or withdrawals from the attached accounts of the defaulter without SEBI's prior permission.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 21, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Restricts financial liquidity of the specified defaulter to ensure compliance with SEBI recovery orders.
