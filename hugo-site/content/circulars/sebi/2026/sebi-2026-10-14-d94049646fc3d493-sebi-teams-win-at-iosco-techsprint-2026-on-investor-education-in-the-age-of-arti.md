## Summary

Two teams representing the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) won three awards at the prestigious IOSCO TechSprint 2026 held in Madrid on October 8, 2026. Organized by the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) in collaboration with the UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) AI Lab, the TechSprint focused on developing interactive investor-education resources to combat AI-enabled fraud and navigate capital markets AI risks.

## Key Points

- SEBI shortlisted two teams—TrustLens Shield and SwipeWise—out of 65 global applicant teams to participate in the IOSCO TechSprint 2026.
- Team SwipeWise won Problem Statement 2 ("Navigating Capital Markets and AI-Related Risks") and received the Pilot Pathway Award.
- Team TrustLens Shield received two awards: the Global Transferability Award and the Accessible Investor Education and Protection Award for an AI-driven investor protection solution.
- TrustLens Shield allows retail investors to verify suspicious financial messages, images, videos, and links in real-time across WhatsApp, web, and mobile apps, cross-checking claims against SEBI's regulatory databases and SEBI Check.
