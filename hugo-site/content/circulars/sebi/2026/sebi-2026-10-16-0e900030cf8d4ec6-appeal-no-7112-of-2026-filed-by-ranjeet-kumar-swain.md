## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7112 of 2026 filed by Ranjeet Kumar Swain under the Right to Information Act, 2005, regarding asset attachment, recovery proceedings, and refund status in the matter of Micro Finance Limited.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information regarding asset attachment, auction status, recovered amounts, refund timelines, and Nodal Officer details for Micro Finance Limited (CIN: U65923OR1983PLC001232).
- SEBI had previously issued an order on October 27, 2016, directing Micro Finance Limited and its directors to refund money collected under schemes to investors.
- Due to non-compliance by the company and its directors, SEBI initiated recovery proceedings and attached bank accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios for a sum of Rs. 88,92,00,000/- plus interest and expenses.
- SEBI recovered approximately Rs. 1.98 Crores from the defaulters, but no refund process has been initiated yet in this matter.
- Information regarding specific officer names and contact numbers was exempted under Section 8(1)(g) and 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act, while the contact email for the Recovery Division of ERO was provided.
