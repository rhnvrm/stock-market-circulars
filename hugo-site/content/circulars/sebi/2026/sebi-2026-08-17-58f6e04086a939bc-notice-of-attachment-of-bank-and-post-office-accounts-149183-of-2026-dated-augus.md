## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 17, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 8858 of 2025 against Heena Khatoon, Proprietor of Heena Enterprises (PAN-FGXPK5721G), in the matter of Continental Seeds and Chemicals Limited. The attachment covers bank and post office accounts for the realization of dues.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 8858 of 2025.
- Defaulter: Heena Khatoon, Proprietor of Heena Enterprises (PAN-FGXPK5721G).
- Matter: Continental Seeds and Chemicals Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, post offices, and financial institutions are required to attach the specified accounts and prevent any debits or withdrawals by the defaulter.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates continuous regulatory enforcement and strict recovery measures against defaulters associated with listed entities.
