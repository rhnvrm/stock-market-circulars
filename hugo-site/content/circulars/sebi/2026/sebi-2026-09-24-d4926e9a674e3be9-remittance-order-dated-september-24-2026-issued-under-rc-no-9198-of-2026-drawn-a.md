## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated September 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9198 of 2026 drawn against Madhu Kumari Bairwa. This action relates to investigations into trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9198 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Madhu Kumari Bairwa.
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, garnishees, and intermediaries are required to remit/transfer funds as specified in the remittance order towards realization of dues under RC No. 9198 of 2026.

## Important Dates

- Date of Remittance Order: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights rigorous regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities and individuals involved in market irregularities in DU Digital Global Limited.
