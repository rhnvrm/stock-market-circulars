## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance dated September 24, 2026, against Sonalben Amitbhai Khalas (PAN: HVUPK8521G) under Recovery Certificate No. 9230 of 2026. This action relates to recovery proceedings and remittance of dues arising from fraudulent trading activities in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Defaulter / Noticee: Sonalben Amitbhai Khalas (PAN: HVUPK8521G).
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9230 of 2026.
- Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Order Type: General Remittance / Recovery Proceeding pursuant to unpaid penalties.
- Issuing Authority: Recovery Officer, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed under the SEBI Act, 1992, directing the remittance of realized/attached funds towards satisfying penalties imposed for manipulative trading practices.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated intermediaries must ensure compliance with remittance orders and fund transfers as directed by SEBI's Recovery Officer under the recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance: September 24, 2026
- Date of Recovery Certificate: 2026 (RC No. 9230 of 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's continued rigor in enforcing monetary penalties and recovering dues from entities involved in market irregularities and manipulative trading in ANI Integrated Services Limited.
