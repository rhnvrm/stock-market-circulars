## Summary

Shantabai Ambu Rathod filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO of SEBI regarding PACL Certificate No. U18236xxx, seeking details on land allotment records, survey numbers, and reasons for refund application rejection.

## Key Points

- Appellant is a holder/legal claimant of PACL Certificate No. U18236xxx.
- Refund application status indicated that land has been allotted, though the appellant claims no possession or allotment letter was received.
- Appellant sought 12 specific points of information regarding the alleged land allotment, internal notings, and verification records.
- SEBI CPIO responded that information is not available with SEBI and advised approaching the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
