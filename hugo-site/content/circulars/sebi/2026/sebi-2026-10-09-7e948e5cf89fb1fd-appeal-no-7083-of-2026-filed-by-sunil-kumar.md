## Summary

Sunil Kumar filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act) against SEBI's CPIO response regarding query nos. 4 to 9 in his application dated August 29, 2026. The queries related to records, file notings, and examination details concerning Appeal No. SEBII/E/A/26/0000245 regarding the SMART ODR process and grievance handling related to NSE.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought information regarding records, correspondence, file notings, and examination details for CPGRAMS appeal no. SEBII/E/A/26/0000245 concerning SMART ODR and NSE.
- CPIO informed that the CPGRAMS appeal was disposed of on August 03, 2026, and provided a copy of the Action Taken Report (ATR) received from NSE.
- The Appellate Authority reviewed the application, response, and appeal, concluding that the respondent adequately addressed the queries.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes or policy updates introduced; this is an individual RTI appeal disposal order.

No compliance requirements for market participants as this is an administrative RTI appeal order.
