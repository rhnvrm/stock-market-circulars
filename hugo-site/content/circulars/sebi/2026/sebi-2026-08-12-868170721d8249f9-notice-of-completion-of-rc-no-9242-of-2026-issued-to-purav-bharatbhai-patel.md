## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Completion for Recovery Certificate No. 9242 of 2026, dated July 27, 2026, issued against **Purav Bharatbhai Patel** (PAN: ANWPP1802G) in the matter of **Sadhna Broadcast Limited**. The recovery certificate was originally drawn for non-payment of a penalty of Rs. 5,76,000/- (Rupees Five Lakh Seventy-Six Thousand Only) imposed by the Whole Time Member (WTM) via Order No. WTM/AB/ISD/ISD-SEC-5/31442/2025-26 on May 29, 2025. Following full receipt of the outstanding dues, interest, and costs, the recovery proceedings have been officially closed.

## Key Points

- **Subject Individual:** Purav Bharatbhai Patel (PAN: ANWPP1802G).
- **Subject Entity:** Sadhna Broadcast Limited (renamed to Crystal Business System Limited).
- **Recovery Certificate Details:** RC No. 9242 of 2026 was drawn on July 27, 2026, for a sum of Rs. 5,76,000/- along with interest, costs, charges, and expenses.
- **WTM Order Reference:** Penalty was imposed under Order No. WTM/AB/ISD/ISD-SEC-5/31442/2025-26 dated May 29, 2025.
- **Resolution:** Full dues (including interest and costs) were recovered, and recovery proceedings are officially completed under Section 28A(1) of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Sections 224 and 225 of the Income-tax Act, 1961.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no broader regulatory changes or policy amendments introduced by this circular. It is an administrative notification concerning the resolution of individual enforcement action.

## Compliance Requirements

No compliance or reporting actions are required from market intermediaries, listed companies, or the general public.

## Important Dates

- **WTM Penalty Order Date:** May 29, 2025
- **Recovery Certificate Issued:** July 27, 2026
- **Notice of Completion Date:** August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Entity Impact:** Neutral. Resolves the outstanding recovery proceedings concerning the individual in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited with no ongoing threat of further attachment or recovery action against the entity or this specific person.
- **Market Impact:** None. This administrative update does not impact the financial market, trading systems, or sector dynamics.
