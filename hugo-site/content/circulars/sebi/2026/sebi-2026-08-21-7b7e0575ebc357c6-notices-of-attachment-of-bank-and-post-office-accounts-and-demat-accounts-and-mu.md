## Summary

SEBI has issued Notices of Attachment (AP Nos. 15739 and 15740 of 2026) dated August 21, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9173 of 2026 against Padmavathi Darak. The action is taken to recover outstanding dues and penalties related to fraudulent trading and reversal trades in illiquid stock options on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

## Key Points

- Notices of Attachment: AP Nos. 15739 and 15740 of 2026.
- Recovery Certificate: RC No. 9173 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Padmavathi Darak.
- Scope of Attachment: Bank accounts, post office accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios.
- Underlying Matter: Trading in illiquid stock options at BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

Strict enforcement action under the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of dues arising from adjudication orders against manipulation in the illiquid stock options segment.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, post offices, depositories (NSDL/CDSL), and mutual fund asset management companies are required to freeze the specified accounts and holdings of the noticee and prohibit any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notices: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's ongoing rigorous recovery mechanism and enforcement measures against entities violating regulations through illiquid stock options trading.
