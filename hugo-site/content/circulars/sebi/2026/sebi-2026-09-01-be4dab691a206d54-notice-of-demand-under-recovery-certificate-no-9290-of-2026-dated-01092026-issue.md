## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice of demand under Recovery Certificate No. 9290 of 2026 dated September 1, 2026, pursuant to section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with section 222 of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The recovery action is directed jointly and severally against Harsha Ishvarbhai Solanki and 20 others in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9290 of 2026 issued by SEBI.
- Involves 21 entities, including individuals and corporate bodies (e.g., Harsha Ishvarbhai Solanki, B B Commercial Ltd).
- Matter pertains to trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.
- Action taken under section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 and section 222 of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; enforcement of existing recovery provisions under the SEBI Act.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities listed in the recovery certificate are required to clear the demanded dues jointly and severally as specified by the recovery officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Certificate / Notice: 01.09.2026

## Impact Assessment

Enforcement action against specific entities involved in irregular trading activities in ANI Integrated Services Limited, reinforcing regulatory compliance and recovery mechanisms.
