## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued an order for the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 8362 of 2024 dated November 21, 2024, issued to Sanbun Investments and its proprietor, Mr. Nishaan Singh (PAN: HCCPS6377H).

## Key Points

- **Defaulter Entity:** Sanbun Investments and proprietor Mr. Nishaan Singh.
- **Recovery Certificate:** Certificate No. 8362 of 2024, originally drawn on November 21, 2024.
- **Action:** Formal cancellation of the recovery certificate by SEBI following review and compliance/resolution status.
- **Background:** Relates to prior enforcement actions concerning unauthorized portfolio management services and direction for refunds/penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal withdrawal and cancellation of ongoing recovery proceedings under the specified certificate number.

## Compliance Requirements

- No further actions required under this specific recovery certificate as proceedings are formally cancelled.

## Important Dates

- **Original Recovery Certificate Date:** November 21, 2024
- **Cancellation Date:** September 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Concludes the specific recovery enforcement action tied to Certificate No. 8362 of 2024 for the named entity, reflecting resolution of dues or adjustment under regulatory orders.
