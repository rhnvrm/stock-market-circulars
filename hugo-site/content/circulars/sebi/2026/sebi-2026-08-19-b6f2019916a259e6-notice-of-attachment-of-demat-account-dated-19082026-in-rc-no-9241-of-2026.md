## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice for the attachment of all Demat accounts and mutual fund folios held by Angad Rathod, either singly or jointly, to recover unpaid dues amounting to Rs. 5,76,000 under Recovery Certificate No. 9241 of 2026.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9241 of 2026 dated July 27, 2026, issued against defaulter Angad Rathod (PAN: CIUPR1814B).
- Total dues include a penalty of Rs. 5,00,000 imposed by WTM order in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited, interest of Rs. 75,000, and recovery costs of Rs. 1,000.
- Directs NSDL, CDSL, and all mutual funds in India to attach all demat accounts and mutual fund folios of the defaulter with immediate effect.
- Imposes a total ban on debits from the attached accounts/folios, while credits are permitted.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery proceedings via attachment of securities and mutual fund investments under Section 28A(1) and 11(2)(ia) of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

- Depositories and Mutual Funds must immediately attach the accounts/folios of the defaulter, prohibit debits, and submit account details, statements, and confirmation of attachment to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 19, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Date: July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures compliance and recovery of dues enforced by regulatory authorities through asset freezing mechanisms.
