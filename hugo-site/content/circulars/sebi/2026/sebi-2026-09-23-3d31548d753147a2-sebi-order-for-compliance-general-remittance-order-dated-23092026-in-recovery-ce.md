## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 23, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 8933 of 2025 drawn against Pinal Rajesh Mehta, who is the legal representative of the deceased Rajesh Rajnikant Mehta (Defaulter), in the matter of Jay Energy and S. Energies Limited. The order directs the remittance of attached funds/assets towards satisfying the dues under the recovery certificate.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued on September 23, 2026.
- Recovery Certificate No.: 8933 of 2025.
- Defaulter: Pinal Rajesh Mehta (Legal representative of deceased Rajesh Rajnikant Mehta).
- Target Scrip / Matter: Jay Energy and S. Energies Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions under the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992, for the realization of outstanding dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, garnishees, and financial institutions holding funds or assets of the defaulter are required to remit the attached amounts to SEBI as specified in the general remittance order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and recovery follow-through by SEBI against defaulters and their legal representatives, ensuring recovery of regulatory dues.
