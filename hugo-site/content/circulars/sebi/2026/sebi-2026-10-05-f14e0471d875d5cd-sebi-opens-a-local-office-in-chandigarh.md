## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) inaugurated a new Local Office in Chandigarh on October 5, 2026. The office aims to decentralize operations and enhance regional accessibility for investors and issuers across Punjab, Haryana, and the Union Territory of Chandigarh.

## Key Points

- **Office Location**: SCO No. 20-25, 2nd Floor, Jeevan Prakash Building, Sector 17B, Chandigarh, India-160017.
- **Jurisdiction**: Covers the States of Punjab and Haryana, and the Union Territory of Chandigarh.
- **Administrative Control**: Functions under the jurisdiction of SEBI’s Northern Regional Office at New Delhi.
- **Core Functions**: Facilitating investor grievance redressal against listed companies and market intermediaries, spreading financial literacy, and engaging with issuers including SMEs, Social Stock Exchanges, and bond issuers.

## Regulatory Changes

- Expansion of SEBI's physical regional infrastructure to better serve local market participants and investors.

## Compliance Requirements

- Local listed companies and market intermediaries interact with the Chandigarh office for regional grievance resolution and stakeholder engagement as required.

## Important Dates

- **Inauguration Date**: October 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Improves regional grievance redressal efficiency and boosts investor education and financial literacy initiatives in Northern India.
