## Summary

SEBI has issued a Final Order dated September 28, 2026, under Sections 11(1), (4), (4A) and Sections 11B(1) and (2) of the SEBI Act, 1992 in the matter of Growpital Platform. The order examines charges against Rituraj Sharma, Krishnna Joshi, Yotta Agro Venture Pvt Ltd, and other noticees for operating unauthorized collective investment schemes and mobilizing funds from the public.

## Key Points

- Final order issued by SEBI regarding Growpital Platform.
- Involves 30 noticees including individuals, companies, and LLPs.
- Proceedings for Noticee Nos. 15 and 16 (Nutrikosh India Private Limited and Alok Chaudhary) settled via separate settlement order dated September 28, 2026.
- Addresses preliminary objections on SEBI's jurisdiction and adjudication process.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and remedial directions pursuant to investigations into unauthorized collective investment schemes under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities involved must cease and desist from unauthorized fund mobilization, refund investor funds as directed, and comply with debarment and penalty measures.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous regulatory enforcement against illegal collective investment schemes and pooling of funds in agricultural and commercial ventures.
