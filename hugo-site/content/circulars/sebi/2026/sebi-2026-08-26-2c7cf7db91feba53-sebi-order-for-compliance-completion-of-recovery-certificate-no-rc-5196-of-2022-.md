## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. RC 5196 of 2022 drawn against Tanna Tusharbhai Hiralal in the matter of Excel Castronics Limited. The order confirms the successful fulfillment/resolution of the recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate (RC) Number: RC 5196 of 2022.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Tanna Tusharbhai Hiralal.
- Matter: Excel Castronics Limited.
- Action: Completion of recovery proceedings and lifting of attachment where applicable.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and recovery measures executed under Section 11A and 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Notices, banks, and concerned authorities are informed about the completion of recovery proceedings against the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates the finalization and closure of recovery actions against entities involved in market violations concerning Excel Castronics Limited.
