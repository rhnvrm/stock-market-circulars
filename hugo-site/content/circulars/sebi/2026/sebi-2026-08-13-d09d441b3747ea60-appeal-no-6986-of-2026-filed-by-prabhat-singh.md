## Summary

An appeal was filed by Prabhat Singh under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against SEBI's CPIO response regarding SEBI Phase 2 Grade A Assistant Manager Examination 2025 (Legal Stream) documents and recruitment likelihood for 2026.

## Key Points

- The appellant requested response sheets for objective and subjective legal papers and the English paper from the SEBI Grade A Examination 2025, along with information on future recruitment notifications and vacancies.
- The CPIO stated that response sheets/question papers are held by IBPS under an MoU and not shared with SEBI, and that recruitment queries seek clarifications/opinions rather than information under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- The Appellate Authority found that query no. 1 regarding the response sheet warrants reconsideration by the respondent, while query no. 2 properly falls outside the definition of 'information' under Section 2(f).
