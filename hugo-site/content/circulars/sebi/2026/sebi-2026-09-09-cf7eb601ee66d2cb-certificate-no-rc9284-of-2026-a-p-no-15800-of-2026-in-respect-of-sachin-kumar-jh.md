## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued Recovery Certificate No. RC9284 of 2026 along with Attachment Proceeding A.P. No. 15800 of 2026 against Sachin Kumar Jhawar HUF (PAN: AALHS9592Q). These enforcement actions are initiated in the matter of dealings in illiquid stock options.

## Key Points

- **Noticee:** Sachin Kumar Jhawar HUF (PAN: AALHS9592Q).
- **Regulatory Authority:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).
- **Reference Numbers:** Certificate No. RC9284 of 2026, A.P. No. 15800 of 2026.
- **Subject Matter:** Regulatory dues/penalties arising from irregular trading activities in illiquid stock options.

## Regulatory Changes

- Implementation of coercive recovery measures, including attachment of bank accounts, demat accounts, and other movable/immovable properties pursuant to SEBI Recovery Rules.

## Compliance Requirements

- The noticee is required to immediately clear all outstanding dues and penalties specified in the recovery certificate to lift attachment orders.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Issuance:** September 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures strict enforcement of regulatory penalties and deters entities from non-compliance regarding historical illiquid stock options violations.
