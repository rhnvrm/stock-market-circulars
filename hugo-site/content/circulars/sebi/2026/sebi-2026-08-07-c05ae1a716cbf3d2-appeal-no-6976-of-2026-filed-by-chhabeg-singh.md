## Summary

In Appeal No. 6976 of 2026, the Appellate Authority of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) considered an appeal filed by Chhabeg Singh under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant challenged the response of the CPIO regarding queries seeking PACL Limited property sale deeds, the procedure for overriding a false "Land Allotted Yes" status in SEBI's refund database, and expected timelines for direct bank refunds. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's decision, reiterating that SEBI does not possess the requested information and that all PACL refund operations are managed by the Supreme Court-appointed Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- **Appellant's Queries:** The appellant sought certified copies of physical registered sale deeds or allotment letters submitted by PACL Limited for specific registration numbers, along with official protocols to override a false "Land Allotted Yes" flag in the refund database and timelines for bank account rectifications.
- **SEBI's Response:** The CPIO informed the appellant that the requested information is not available with SEBI, advising them to approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee which handles all PACL refund status and communications.
- **Precedent Cited:** The Appellate Authority cited the Central Information Commission (CIC) decision in *Sh. Pattipati Rama Murthy vs. CPIO, SEBI*, which held that if SEBI does not have information in its possession, the CPIO cannot invent it.
- **Jurisdiction over PACL Refunds:** The disposal of PACL properties and investor repayments are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee, constituted pursuant to the February 2, 2016 order of the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced. This order reinforces established legal principles regarding the scope of information disclosure under the RTI Act, 2005 and clarifies organizational jurisdictions.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no compliance requirements for SEBI-regulated entities or market intermediaries.

## Important Dates

- **July 10, 2026:** Date of the original RTI application.
- **July 14, 2026:** Date of the CPIO's response.
- **July 15, 2026:** Date of the appellant's appeal.
- **August 12, 2026:** Date of the Appellate Authority order.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** None. This order represents an individual administrative case.
- **Investor Impact:** Clarifies that PACL investors seeking data corrections or refunds must contact the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee directly, as SEBI does not maintain or modify individual PACL refund database flags.
