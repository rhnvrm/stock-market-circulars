## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated October 12, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7948 of 2024 against Muse Traders (Proprietor - Baljit Singh), Pure Traders (Proprietor - Bhavna Jethvani), and Taj Enterprises (Proprietor - Taj Khan) jointly and severally for the realization of outstanding dues.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued by SEBI on October 12, 2026.
- Recovery Certificate No. 7948 of 2024.
- Defaulters: Muse Traders (Baljit Singh, PAN: BERPR1999P), Pure Traders (Bhavna Jethvani, PAN: BBOPJ6519H), and Taj Enterprises (Taj Khan, PAN: BWRPK1312F).
- Liability: Joint and several liability for remittance of dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and dues from defaulting entities.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and garnishees holding funds or assets of the noticees are required to remit the specified amounts to SEBI pursuant to the recovery certificate and remittance order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: October 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI to recover dues from entities failing to comply with regulatory directions.
