## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7009 of 2026 filed by Kumari Devi (Public Grievance Forum) under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant sought information regarding Aadhaar requirements for demat account renewals and SEBI policies concerning the removal of companies undergoing compulsory liquidation.

## Key Points

- The appellant requested information on whether SEBI or NSDL mandates Aadhaar cards for demat account renewals.
- The appellant sought details on SEBI's policy and process for removing companies from stock exchanges following High Court-ordered compulsory liquidation.
- The First Appellate Authority noted that personal hearings under the RTI Act are discretionary and decided the appeal based on records as the queries were self-explanatory.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes or policy updates were introduced through this order.

## Compliance Requirements

None. The order pertains strictly to the disposal of an RTI appeal.
