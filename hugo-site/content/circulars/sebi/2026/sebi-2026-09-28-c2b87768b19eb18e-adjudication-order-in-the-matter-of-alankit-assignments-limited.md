## Summary

SEBI conducted an inspection of Alankit Assignments Limited, a registered registrar to an issue and share transfer agent (RTA), for the period from September 1, 2024, to December 31, 2025. The inspection uncovered multiple violations regarding service delivery timelines, code of conduct, and non-compliance with the Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF) for QRTAs.

## Key Points

- Noticee: Alankit Assignments Limited (PAN: AAACA9483E, SEBI Reg. No.: INR000002532).
- Inspection Period: September 1, 2024 to December 31, 2025.
- Violations cited include breaches of RTA service timelines, Schedule III Code of Conduct of RTA Regulations, and CSCRF requirements covering access controls, password policies, network security, and data security.
- Adjudication proceedings initiated under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory policy introduced; strict enforcement of existing RTA Regulations, Master Circulars, and CSCRF guidelines for QRTAs.

## Compliance Requirements

RTAs must ensure strict adherence to prescribed service timelines, maintain robust password and access control policies, secure network infrastructure, and comply fully with SEBI's cyber resilience mandates.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Dates: February 24–27, 2026 and March 9, 2026.
- Appointment of Adjudicating Officer: August 12, 2026.
- Show Cause Notice (SCN) Date: August 13, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Highlights regulatory focus on operational efficiency and stringent cybersecurity compliance for market infrastructure institutions and RTAs.
