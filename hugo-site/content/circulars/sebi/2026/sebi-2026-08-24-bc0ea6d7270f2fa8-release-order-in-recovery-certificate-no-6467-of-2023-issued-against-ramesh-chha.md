## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order regarding Recovery Certificate No. 6467 of 2023, which was previously issued against Ramesh Chhallani HUF (PAN: AALHR9880C) in the matter of Kelvin Fincap Limited. The order orders the release and lifting of attachments on accounts/properties as dues have been satisfied or proceedings resolved.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 6467 of 2023.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Ramesh Chhallani HUF (PAN – AALHR9880C).
- Matter: Kelvin Fincap Limited.
- Action: Lifting/release of previously ordered attachments on bank accounts, lockers, and other assets.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal release of recovery attachment pursuant to compliance/satisfaction of dues under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and authorities holding frozen accounts or assets are instructed to lift the freeze/attachment immediately.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restores operational status of accounts and assets for the noticee following settlement of recovery proceedings.
