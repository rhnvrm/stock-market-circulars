## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate No. 9296 of 2026 dated September 08, 2026, jointly and severally to Manish Mishra, Gaurav Gupta, Subhash Agarwal, Pooja Aggarwal, and Rakesh Kumar Gupta. The recovery proceedings arise from the matter of trading-based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Demand issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9296 of 2026.
- Date of Notice: September 08, 2026.
- Defaulters (Jointly and Severally): Manish Mishra (PAN: AMPPM6823L), Gaurav Gupta (PAN: AKHPG5185D), Subhash Agarwal (PAN: AAEPA6699R), Pooja Aggarwal (PAN: AGMPA6216C), and Rakesh Kumar Gupta (PAN: AAEPG2752R).
- Matter: Trading based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions initiated pursuant to SEBI orders regarding fraudulent trading practices and misleading stock recommendations via social media platforms.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulters are required to pay the demanded dues immediately under the Recovery Certificate, failing which further coercive attachment and recovery measures will be enforced by SEBI's Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate / Notice: September 08, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory crackdowns on fraudulent social media stock manipulation schemes and enforces recovery of illegal gains from defaulters.
