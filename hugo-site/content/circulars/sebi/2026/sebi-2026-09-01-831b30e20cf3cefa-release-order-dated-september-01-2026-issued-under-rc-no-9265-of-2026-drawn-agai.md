## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated September 01, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9265 of 2026 drawn against Kavita Shah in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited. This order releases the attachment or concludes specific recovery proceedings with respect to the noticee.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9265 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Kavita Shah.
- Matter: Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement order modifying or withdrawing recovery proceedings and attachments pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries are notified of the release of attachment on accounts or assets as specified in the order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: September 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Relates to the resolution and closure of specific recovery proceedings against individuals tied to past regulatory actions in Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
