## Summary

SEBI has issued Recovery Certificate No. 9255 of 2026 dated July 27, 2026, to Yogesh Kumar Gupta (PAN: ABJPG8977G) in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited, enforcing disgorgement and penalties related to earlier regulatory orders concerning fraudulent stock manipulation.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9255 of 2026 issued against Yogesh Kumar Gupta.
- Noticee PAN: ABJPG8977G.
- Relates to proceedings in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
- Enforcement of dues following non-compliance with final SEBI orders regarding illegal gains.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of rigorous enforcement and recovery mechanisms by SEBI against entities failing to pay disgorgement amounts ordered under securities laws.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees and entities subject to SEBI disgorgement orders must clear all dues promptly to avoid coercive recovery actions including attachment of bank accounts and properties.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate: July 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Strengthens regulatory deterrence against market manipulators and reinforces SEBI's capability to recover illegal gains.
