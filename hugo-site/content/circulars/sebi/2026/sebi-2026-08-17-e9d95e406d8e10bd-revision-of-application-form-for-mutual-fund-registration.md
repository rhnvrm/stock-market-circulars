## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular regarding the revision and consolidation of application forms for mutual fund registration. In view of the recent overhaul of the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026 and SEBI Intermediaries Regulations, the existing multi-stage application forms (such as Form A for in-principle approval and Forms C and D for final registration) have been consolidated into a single comprehensive application form for registration of mutual funds.

## Key Points

- Consolidates existing mutual fund registration forms into a single application form.
- Replaces separate application stages for in-principle approval and final registration with a unified format.
- Issued following the recent overhaul of SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026 and SEBI Intermediaries Regulations.
- All other conditions specified in the Master Circular dated March 20, 2026, remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of Form A and consolidation of application requirements for mutual fund sponsors, Asset Management Companies (AMCs), and trustee companies under the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities desirous of becoming sponsors of mutual funds, AMCs, and trustee companies must use the revised and consolidated application format for mutual fund registration.

## Important Dates

- Effective from August 17, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the regulatory onboarding and registration process for new mutual fund sponsors and asset management companies by removing redundant multi-form submissions.
