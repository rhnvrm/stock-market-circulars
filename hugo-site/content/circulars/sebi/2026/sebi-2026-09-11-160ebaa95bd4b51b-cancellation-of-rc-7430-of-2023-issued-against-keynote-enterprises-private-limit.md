## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 7430 of 2023 issued against Keynote Enterprises Private Limited (PAN: AADCK7471R) in the matter of TV Vision Limited. The recovery proceedings initiated under the certificate have been formally cancelled/withdrawn by the Recovery Officer.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate Number: RC 7430 of 2023
- Noticee / Defaulter: Keynote Enterprises Private Limited (PAN: AADCK7471R)
- Matter: TV Vision Limited
- Action: Cancellation of Recovery Certificate

## Regulatory Changes

Formal withdrawal of enforcement and recovery measures under the specified recovery certificate as dues are settled, addressed, or the certificate is otherwise set aside.

## Compliance Requirements

Intermediaries, banks, and financial institutions holding accounts or assets under previous attachment/recovery notices related to RC 7430 of 2023 are required to lift the freeze or restrictions upon receipt of the cancellation order.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Relieves Keynote Enterprises Private Limited from the recovery proceedings under RC 7430 of 2023, clearing liabilities/attachments associated with that specific recovery certificate in the TV Vision Limited matter.
