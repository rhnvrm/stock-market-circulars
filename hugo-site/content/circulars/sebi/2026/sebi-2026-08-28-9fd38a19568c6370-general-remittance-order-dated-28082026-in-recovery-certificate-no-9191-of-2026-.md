## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated August 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9191 of 2026 drawn against Dhaval Girishbhai Parmar (PAN: EPZPP2397F). This action relates to investigations into trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under RC No. 9191 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Dhaval Girishbhai Parmar (PAN: EPZPP2397F).
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for remittance and realization of dues/penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and garnishees are required to remit funds and assets as specified in the remittance order towards the recovery certificate dues.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against entities and individuals involved in market irregularities in DU Digital Global Limited.
