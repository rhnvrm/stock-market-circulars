## Summary

The appellants, Mukesh Arora and Sonia Narang, filed an RTI application dated August 13, 2026, seeking information and certified copies of documents regarding a complaint dated July 17, 2025, concerning the inclusion or continuation of a property in the PACL auction and recovery process (MR No. 7379-16). SEBI responded stating that the information sought is not available with SEBI and that all communications regarding refund status and PACL matters are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- **Appellants:** Mukesh Arora and Sonia Narang.
- **Respondent:** CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai.
- **Subject:** RTI application seeking details, action taken reports, and committee consideration regarding complaint dated July 17.07.2025 and MR No. 7379-16 under PACL recovery.
- **SEBI Stand:** Information not available with SEBI; matters regarding refund status and PACL recovery are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes were introduced through this order.

## Compliance Requirements

Applicants seeking information on PACL refund status or property recovery proceedings are advised to approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Important Dates

- **Application Date:** August 13, 2026
- **Respondent Reply Date:** August 24, 2026
- **Appeal Date:** September 03, 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order has no direct impact on market operations or listed securities, reaffirming the jurisdictional handling of PACL recovery matters by the designated committee.
