## Summary

SEBI has issued a release order regarding Recovery Certificate No. RC 4228 of 2021 initiated against Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited and related individuals, including Mr. Pushpendra Singh Baghel, Ms. Pushpanjali Singh Baghel, Mr. Shahendra Singh Baghel, Mr. Ranvajay Pratap Singh Baghel, Mr. Mragendra Singh Baghel, and Mr. Dheerendra Krishan Pratap.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. RC 4228 of 2021 was previously issued in the matter of Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited.
- The order pertains to the release/discharge of recovery proceedings or attached properties/accounts for the named entities.
- Part of SEBI's ongoing enforcement and recovery actions related to illegal collective investment schemes (CIS).

## Regulatory Changes

- Execution of statutory recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992, followed by formal release orders upon compliance or resolution.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities and individuals must adhere to ongoing stipulations and refund directions mandated by SEBI regarding mobilization of funds.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026 (corresponding to SEBI release notices).

## Impact Assessment

- Demonstrates progressive resolution or adjustment of recovery certificates issued against defaulters in collective investment scheme cases.
