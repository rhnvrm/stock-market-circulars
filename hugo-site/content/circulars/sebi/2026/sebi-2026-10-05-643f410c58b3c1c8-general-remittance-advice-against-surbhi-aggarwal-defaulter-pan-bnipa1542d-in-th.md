## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice dated October 5, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9264 of 2026 against Surbhi Aggarwal (PAN: BNIPA1542D). This proceeding arises from the adjudication order imposing monetary penalties for non-cooperation and front-running in the scrip of Rajesh Exports Ltd.

## Key Points

- Defaulter/Noticee: Surbhi Aggarwal (PAN: BNIPA1542D).
- Recovery Certificate No. 9264 of 2026.
- Matter: Front-running and non-cooperation with the Investigating Authority in Rajesh Exports Ltd.
- Action Type: General Remittance Advice issued by the SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions initiated under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of unpaid monetary penalties imposed by Adjudicating Officers.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and relevant intermediaries must comply with recovery directives, remittance advices, and asset/account freeze orders issued pursuant to the recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance Advice: October 5, 2026
- Original Adjudication Order: April 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's rigorous enforcement framework and recovery mechanisms against entities failing to pay penalties or cooperate with market surveillance and investigation proceedings.
