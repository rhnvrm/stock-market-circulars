## Summary

SEBI has issued a completion notice and release order for Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 8924 of 2025 against Yuvika Tradewing LLP (PAN: AABFY4620R). This marks the formal conclusion of recovery proceedings initiated due to non-payment of penalties stemming from disclosure violations under SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 in the matter of Cressanda Solutions Limited.

## Key Points

- Formal completion and closure of Recovery Certificate No. 8924 of 2025 against Yuvika Tradewing LLP.
- Originates from adjudication penalties for failing to make timely disclosures regarding share acquisitions in Cressanda Solutions Limited.
- Consequent release of attachments on bank and demat accounts of the noticee.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 concluded upon full settlement of dues/penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions and banks are notified to lift restrictions/freezes on the bank and demat accounts of Yuvika Tradewing LLP as dues have been settled.

## Important Dates

- Completion Date: October 15, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates effective regulatory recovery enforcement and subsequent relief upon compliance and settlement by entities involved in market violations.
