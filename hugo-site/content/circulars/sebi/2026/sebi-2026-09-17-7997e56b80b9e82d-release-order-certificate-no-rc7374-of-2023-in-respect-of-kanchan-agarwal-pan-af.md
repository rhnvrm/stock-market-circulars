## Summary

SEBI has issued a release order in respect of Certificate No. RC7374 of 2023 concerning Kanchan Agarwal (PAN: AFMPA8305D) in the matter of Illiquid Stock Options, signifying the successful realization of dues and conclusion of recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- Recovery proceedings initiated under Certificate No. RC7374 of 2023 have been officially released.
- The matter pertains to SEBI's investigation into non-genuine transactions and reversal trades in illiquid stock options.
- The dues associated with the noticee have been fully satisfied.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative recovery release order for a specific individual.

## Compliance Requirements

No further compliance actions are required under this recovery certificate as the matter is closed.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

This order has no broader market or operational impact as it is an isolated case closure.
