## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand under Certificate No. 9300 of 2026 dated October 08, 2026, against Aahuti Rasik Mistry and Manish Mishra, jointly and severally, for a total sum of Rs. 84,63,813.66 in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited.

## Key Points

- Total dues of Rs. 84,63,813.66 comprise disgorgement of Rs. 57,83,641/-, interest of Rs. 26,79,172.66 (from Nov 30, 2022 to Oct 08, 2026 @ 12% p.a.), and recovery costs of Rs. 1,000/-.
- Dues arise from WTM Order No. WTM/AB/ISD/ISD-SEC-5/31442/2025-26 dated 29.09.2025 in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
- Payment is required within 15 days of receipt of the notice via EFT/NEFT/RTGS, online payment portal, or designated payment links.
- Failure to pay will result in recovery actions including attachment and sale of movable/immovable property, bank account attachment, arrest, and appointment of receivers.
- Direct or indirect transfer of property or monies on or after May 29, 2025, to spouse, minor child, son's wife or minor child without adequate consideration is deemed void for recovery purposes.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of recovery proceedings under section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with the Income-tax Act, 1961.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must pay the total outstanding dues of Rs. 84,63,813.66 within 15 days and submit e-payment confirmation to the Recovery Officer and designated email addresses.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 08, 2026
- Payment Deadline: Within 15 days of receipt of notice

## Impact Assessment

Ensures strict recovery of disgorged amounts and compliance with regulatory orders, impacting the designated entities' financial assets and properties.
