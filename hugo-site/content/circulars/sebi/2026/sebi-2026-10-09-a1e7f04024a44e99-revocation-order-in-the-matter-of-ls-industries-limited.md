## Summary

SEBI examined the matter of LS Industries Limited (LSIL) following Interim and Confirmatory Orders regarding a manipulative scheme to defraud investors. The order highlights that BSE had revoked trading suspension based on company submissions, but subsequent surprise site visits confirmed the complete absence of operational business, plant, machinery, raw materials, and sufficient workforce.

## Key Points

- Interim Order passed by SEBI on February 11, 2025, and Confirmatory Order on May 30, 2025, against LSIL and its promoters/shareholders for PFUTP violations.
- Trading in LSIL was originally suspended on December 30, 2013, due to listing agreement non-compliances.
- BSE revoked trading suspension on July 23, 2024, after initial submissions and a site visit showed rental income but no active manufacturing.
- A subsequent surprise site visit by BSE on June 11, 2025, verified that no operational textile business, plant, machinery, or raw materials existed at the registered office.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enhanced regulatory scrutiny on stock exchanges revoking trading suspension for companies undergoing active investigations or lacking genuine operational substance.

## Compliance Requirements

- Listed companies must maintain verifiable operational assets and business activities in compliance with exchange revocation norms and listing agreements.

## Important Dates

- December 30, 2013: Trading suspended by BSE.
- July 23, 2024: Trading suspension revoked by BSE.
- February 11, 2025: SEBI Interim Order passed.
- May 30, 2025: SEBI Confirmatory Order passed.
- June 11, 2025: Surprise site visit conducted by BSE.

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures stricter gatekeeping by stock exchanges preventing speculative or non-operational companies from resuming trading amidst ongoing regulatory investigations.
