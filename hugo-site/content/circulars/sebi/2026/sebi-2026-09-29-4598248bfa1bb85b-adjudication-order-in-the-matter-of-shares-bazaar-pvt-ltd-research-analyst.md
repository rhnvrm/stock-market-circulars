## Summary

SEBI conducted an examination of Shares Bazaar Private Limited (Noticee 1), a registered research analyst, for the period between March 16, 2021, and December 16, 2022. The examination revealed that the entity was operating a Ponzi/MLM scheme (MMFF) offering assured returns to investors while falsely giving the impression that their monies were being invested in the securities market.

## Key Points

- Noticee 1 (Shares Bazaar Private Limited) and its directors (Bhupal Nanavath, Prasanna Lakshmi Atluri, Naresh Mitta, Tirumala Lakshmi Venkata Ramesh) were found to be involved in unauthorized portfolio management and investment advisory services.
- The entity operated a Ponzi/MLM scheme promising assured returns without actual investments in the securities market.
- Violations include Regulation 13(i) and Regulation 24(2) read with clauses 1 and 2 of Schedule III of the RA Regulations, 2014, Clause 3(a) of Schedule II of the Intermediaries Regulations, 2008, and Regulation 16.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement action and adjudication order under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Intermediaries and registered research analysts must strictly adhere to the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, and Intermediaries Regulations, 2008, refraining from operating Ponzi or MLM schemes, offering assured returns, or engaging in unauthorized portfolio management.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: March 16, 2021 to December 16, 2022
- Order Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights SEBI's continued crackdown on fraudulent entities misusing research analyst registrations to run unauthorized investment schemes and MLM models, protecting retail investors from deceptive assured return promises.
