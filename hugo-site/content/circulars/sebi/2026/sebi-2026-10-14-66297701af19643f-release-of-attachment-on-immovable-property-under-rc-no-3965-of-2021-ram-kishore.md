## Summary

SEBI has issued a release order regarding the attachment of immovable property under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 3965 of 2021 drawn against Ram Kishore Taparia (PAN: ABJPT1453C) in the matter of Cosboard Industries Ltd. This indicates the conclusion or satisfaction of recovery proceedings for this specific certificate.

## Key Points

- Release of attachment on immovable property issued by SEBI Recovery Officer.
- Recovery Certificate No.: 3965 of 2021.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Ram Kishore Taparia (PAN: ABJPT1453C).
- Company involved: Cosboard Industries Ltd.
- Indicates completion/satisfaction of recovery proceedings under the specified RC.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings closure under the SEBI Act, 1992, removing encumbrances/attachments on the specified immovable property.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant authorities, banks, and registrants are notified of the release of attachment on the property, restoring normal title and ownership rights.

## Important Dates

- Order / Release Date: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Removes legal/regulatory encumbrances on the attached property of the individual, signifying resolution of recovery dues related to Cosboard Industries Ltd.
