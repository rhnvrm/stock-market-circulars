## Summary

An RTI appeal (Appeal No. 7005 of 2026) was filed by Gnaneeswar Adhi before the Appellate Authority under the RTI Act, 2005, against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant sought certified copies of documents, reports, and records pertaining to the Initial Public Offering (IPO) allotment process for both Mainboard and SME platforms, including lottery processes, algorithm details, vendor information, maker-checker controls, PAN level allotment audits, and fuzzy logic matching parameters.

## Key Points

- Appellant filed an RTI application dated July 06, 2026, seeking detailed technical documentation, SOPs, algorithms, and audit reports related to IPO share allotments.
- The CPIO responded on July 28, 2026, leading to the filing of the appeal on July 30, 2026.
- Queries span across lottery process algorithms, vendor development details, system audits, rigging prevention mechanisms (maker-checker concepts), PAN-level audits, and name matching parameters.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes or policy directives were introduced through this order.

None applicable to market participants from this order.

- Date of CPIO Response: July 28, 2026
- Date of Appeal: July 30, 2026

No direct market impact; administrative response under the RTI Act concerning transparency in IPO allotment mechanisms.
