## Summary

The Appellate Authority of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has dismissed an appeal (Appeal No. 6979 of 2026) filed by Jagadish against the Central Public Information Officer (CPIO), SEBI. The appeal arose from a CPIO response dated December 02, 2025, which addressed the appellant's RTI application (transferred from the Central Vigilance Commission on November 04, 2025). The Appellate Authority dismissed the appeal on two grounds: first, as time-barred under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act since it was filed after a substantial delay without any request for condonation or explanation; and second, on its merits, finding that the CPIO had adequately addressed the appellant's query regarding the status of complaints.

## Key Points

- **Time-Barred Appeal**: The CPIO's response was dated December 02, 2025, and the appeal was received on July 14, 2026—well past the statutory thirty-day limit under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act. No request for condonation of delay or explanation was submitted.
- **Complaint Status Provided**: The CPIO informed the appellant that Complaint number 12091/2023 and other CVC Portal complaints received by SEBI's Vigilance Department were examined and closed, as no vigilance angle was found.
- **Jurisdiction of RTI Proceedings**: Citing the Delhi High Court judgment in *Narendra Tyagi vs. Assistant Director (CPIO)* (December 06, 2023), the Appellate Authority reiterated that disputes regarding the correctness of information provided under the RTI Act cannot be adjudicated in proceedings under the RTI Act. The CPIO's role is strictly to provide accessible documents/information.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes were introduced. The order reinforces established procedural rules and timelines under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act, 2005, and clarifies the scope of CPIO responsibilities.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Statutory Timelines**: RTI appellants must file first appeals within thirty days of receiving the CPIO's response. Any delay must be accompanied by a formal request for condonation explaining the valid reasons for the delay.
- **CPIO Obligations**: The CPIO is only required to supply available information and documents within their access, and does not have the mandate to resolve disputes regarding the correctness of such information.

## Important Dates

- **November 04, 2025**: RTI application transferred by CVC to SEBI.
- **December 02, 2025**: Respondent (CPIO, SEBI) responded to the RTI application.
- **July 14, 2026**: Appellant's first appeal received by the Office of the Appellate Authority (Reg. No. SEBIH/A/P/26/0044).
- **August 12, 2026**: Date of the Appellate Authority's order dismissing the appeal.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: None. This is an administrative matter concerning an individual's right to information request.
- **Operational/Corporate Impact**: None. The order reinforces the administrative bounds of the RTI Act without introducing any operational compliance requirements for listed entities or market intermediaries.
