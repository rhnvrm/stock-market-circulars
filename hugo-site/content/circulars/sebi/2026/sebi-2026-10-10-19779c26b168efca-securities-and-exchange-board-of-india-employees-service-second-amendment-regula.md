## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has notified the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Employees' Service) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026, amending the SEBI (Employees' Service) Regulations, 2001 with respect to recruitment eligibility criteria, educational qualifications, minimum percentage marks, and grading conversion scales.

## Key Points

- **Effective Date:** These regulations come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette (October 9, 2026).
- **Educational Qualifications Update:** Replaces previous qualification criteria with updated requirements including a bachelor's degree with at least 60% marks (50% for SC/ST/PwBD candidates) spanning at least 3 years after class XII, or a master's degree/postgraduate diploma (at least 2 years duration) obtained after graduation, or recognized professional qualifications such as Chartered Accountant, Chartered Financial Analyst, Company Secretary, or Cost Accountant.
- **Grading Scale Conversion:** Specifies clear guidelines and percentage equivalents for conversion of CGPA/OGPA/CPI into percentage marks on a 10-point scale where 6.75 CGPA equals 60% and 5.75 CGPA equals 50%.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amends Schedule, Table, Item 6, Column 3 of the SEBI (Employees' Service) Regulations, 2001.
- Updates eligibility criteria for recruitment to align with modern academic standards and grading systems.

## Compliance Requirements

- Internal applicability for SEBI HR and recruitment processes.

## Important Dates

- **Publication/Enforcement Date:** October 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Purely administrative and internal circular concerning SEBI employee service regulations and recruitment eligibility. No direct impact on listed companies, market participants, or stock prices.
