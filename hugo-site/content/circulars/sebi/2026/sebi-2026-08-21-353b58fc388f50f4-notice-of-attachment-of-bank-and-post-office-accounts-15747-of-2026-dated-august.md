## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 21, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9247 of 2026 against Sulabh Dikshit (PAN: ANEPD9669B) as a defaulter. This action is taken in the matter of trading based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9247 of 2026.
- Defaulter: Sulabh Dikshit (PAN: ANEPD9669B).
- Matter: Trading based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues/penalties arising from market manipulation and unauthorized stock recommendations.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, post offices, and financial institutions are required to attach and freeze accounts, lockers, and holdings of the defaulter to prevent any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and recovery actions by SEBI against entities involved in pump-and-dump schemes and misleading social media stock recommendations.
