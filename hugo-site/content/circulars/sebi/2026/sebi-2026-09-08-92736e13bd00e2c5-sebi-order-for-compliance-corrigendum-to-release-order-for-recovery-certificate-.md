## Summary

SEBI has issued a corrigendum to the release order associated with Recovery Certificate No. 4228 of 2021, originally issued in the matter of Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited and its directors/associates.

## Key Points

- Corrigendum modifies technical details, typographical errors, or clerical particulars in the earlier release order.
- Pertains to recovery proceedings initiated for unauthorized mobilization of funds through Collective Investment Schemes (CIS).
- Ensures legal and administrative accuracy in the execution and release process of the recovery certificate.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory policy changes; administrative correction of an existing recovery/release order.

## Compliance Requirements

- Relevant entities and authorities must take note of the rectified particulars for formal record-keeping and compliance.

## Important Dates

- Date of Corrigendum: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Limited to the specific entities and proceedings under Recovery Certificate No. 4228 of 2021 with no wider market impact.
