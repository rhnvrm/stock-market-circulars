## Summary

SEBI has announced the completion of Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7522 of 2024 issued against Kapil Kumar Wadhawan in the matter concerning the transaction of shares held by DHFL in DHFL Pramerica Life Insurance Company Limited and related disclosure violations.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 7522 of 2024 was previously initiated against Kapil Kumar Wadhawan for dues related to adjudication penalties.
- The matter stems from historical non-disclosures of share transfers concerning DHFL Pramerica Life Insurance Company Limited.
- The completion order signifies the successful conclusion of the recovery proceedings or settlement of the outstanding dues under this certificate.

## Regulatory Changes

- Formal release from recovery proceedings under RC 7522 of 2024.

## Compliance Requirements

- No further compliance requirements for this specific recovery certificate as proceedings are completed.

## Important Dates

- Date of Completion/Release: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Limited market impact as this is an isolated enforcement recovery closure involving past promoters.
