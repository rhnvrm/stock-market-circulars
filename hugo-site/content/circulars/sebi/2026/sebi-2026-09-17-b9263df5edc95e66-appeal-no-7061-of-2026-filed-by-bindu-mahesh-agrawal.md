## Summary

Bindu Mahesh Agrawal filed an appeal under the RTI Act, 2005 against SEBI's CPIO response regarding PACL investment details and refund claim status. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's reply, noting that SEBI does not possess the requested information and that refund management and asset disposal for PACL Ltd. are handled exclusively by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought confirmation of investments under registration numbers U126104xxx and U12616xxx, refund eligibility, and timelines for any future claim windows regarding PACL Ltd.
- The CPIO stated that the information is not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The Appellate Authority affirmed that SEBI cannot be expected to furnish information not in its possession, citing precedent from the Central Information Commission (CIC).
- Public notices, status reports, and FAQs regarding PACL are available on the SEBI website.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Investors seeking refunds or status updates regarding PACL Ltd. must approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee and refer to official updates published on the SEBI website.
