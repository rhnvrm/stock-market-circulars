## Summary

SEBI has issued a Confirmatory Order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992, in the matter of Nirman Agri Genetics Limited (NAGL). This order confirms the ad-interim directions issued in the Interim Order dated October 14, 2025, which restrained the company and its Promoter and Managing Director, Mr. Pranav Kailas Bagal. The enforcement actions were initiated following an investigation into the utilization of IPO proceeds, which revealed systematic diversion and siphoning of approximately 93% of the funds raised through conflicting vendor profiles, non-existent entities, and shell layers.

## Key Points

- **Entity Involved:** Nirman Agri Genetics Limited (NAGL) and its promoter/MD, Pranav Kailas Bagal.
- **Siphoning Allegations:** Investigation showed a systematic diversion of approximately 93% of IPO proceeds through conflicting vendor profiles and shell layers to benefit the promoter group.
- **Market Access Restraint:** NAGL is restrained from accessing the securities market in any manner until further orders.
- **Halt on Corporate Actions:** NAGL must continue to halt all corporate actions relating to its bonus issue, stock split, and proposed company name change.
- **Trading Prohibition:** Promoter Pranav Kailas Bagal remains restrained from buying, selling, or dealing in NAGL scrip, directly or indirectly.
- **Lack of Written Submission:** Despite multiple extensions for inspecting documents and personal hearings, the Noticees did not file written submissions, prompting SEBI to proceed based on oral arguments and existing findings.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no broad regulatory changes to the market structure; this is an entity-specific enforcement and confirmatory order reinforcing existing SEBI anti-fraud and investor protection regulations (Sections 11, 11(4), and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992).

## Compliance Requirements

- **Nirman Agri Genetics Limited (Noticee 1):** Must continue to comply with the ban on market access and maintain the halt on its corporate actions (bonus issue, stock split, and name change).
- **Pranav Kailas Bagal (Noticee 2):** Must remain restrained from buying, selling, or dealing in NAGL securities, either directly or indirectly.

## Important Dates

- **October 14, 2025:** Date of the initial Interim Order issuing the ad-interim directions.
- **May 13, 2026:** Original date scheduled for a personal hearing (adjourned upon Noticees' request).
- **July 16, 2026:** Personal hearing conducted via video conferencing.
- **August 12, 2026:** Date of the Confirmatory Order confirming the interim directions.

## Impact Assessment

- **Company Impact:** Highly negative. The company is barred from accessing the securities market and is forced to freeze all key corporate actions (bonus issue, stock split, name change), which severely impacts its capital structure and operational expansion plans.
- **Promoter Impact:** The promoter cannot trade or transact in the company's stock, restricting any liquidity or exit routes.
- **Market Impact:** Reinforces SEBI's strict stance on the misuse of IPO proceeds, enhancing investor protection and market integrity, especially in the SME segment (NSE Emerge).
