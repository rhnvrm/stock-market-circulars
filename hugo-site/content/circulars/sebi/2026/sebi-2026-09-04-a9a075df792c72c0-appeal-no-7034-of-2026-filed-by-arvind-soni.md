## Summary

Arvind Soni filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI's response regarding queries concerning PACL land holdings in Jaisalmer, disposal of land, objections on specific khasra numbers, and CBI court order copies. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that the requested information is not available with SEBI and that matters concerning PACL property disposal and investor repayment are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee, which is not a public authority under section 2(h) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information regarding PACL land holdings in Jaisalmer, land disposal procedures, specific khasra numbers, and CBI court orders via an RTI application dated July 21, 2026.
- The CPIO responded that the requested information was not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's position citing precedent that SEBI cannot invent information not in its possession and confirmed that the Justice Lodha Committee is not a public authority under the RTI Act.
