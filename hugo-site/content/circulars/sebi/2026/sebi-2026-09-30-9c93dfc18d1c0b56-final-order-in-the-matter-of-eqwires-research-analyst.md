## Summary

SEBI issued a final order dated September 2026 against M/s Eqwires Research Analyst (Registration No. INH000007465) under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Regulation 27 of SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008. The proceedings emanate from a surprise onsite inspection conducted in November 2021, which revealed violations of Research Analyst (RA) Regulations and PFUTP Regulations, specifically regarding misleading clients with assurances of fixed returns.

## Key Points

- SEBI conducted a surprise onsite inspection of M/s Eqwires Research Analyst from November 22 to 25, 2021.
- Inspection observations revealed violations of RA Regulations, PFUTP Regulations, and IA Regulations.
- The Designated Authority (DA) submitted an enquiry report on August 13, 2025, recommending regulatory censure.
- Noticee misled clients by giving assurances regarding fixed returns and failed in statutory compliance obligations.
- SEBI issued regulatory censure under Regulation 26(1)(vii) of the SEBI Intermediaries Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory policy changes; enforcement action against a registered research analyst for non-compliance and misleading practices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research analysts must strictly adhere to SEBI (Research Analyst) Regulations, 2014 and PFUTP Regulations.
- Prohibition of promising fixed, assured, or guaranteed returns to clients.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2020 to November 25, 2021
- Date of Inspection: November 22-25, 2021
- Enquiry Report Date: August 13, 2025
- SCN Date: January 05, 2026
- Final Order: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces SEBI's strict enforcement against research analysts making false promises of fixed returns and violating intermediary compliance standards.
