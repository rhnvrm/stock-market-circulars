## Summary

The appellant, Sunil Rohilla, filed an appeal under the Right to Information (RTI) Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI's response stating that the requested copy of the Valuation cum Inspection Report and vacant property images regarding a property purchased via auction (PACL matter) was not available with SEBI. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's reply, noting that SEBI cannot invent information not in its possession and that the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee—tasked with property disposal and investor repayment—is not a public authority under Section 2(h) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- **Subject:** RTI appeal regarding property inspection and valuation report in a PACL auction matter.
- **SEBI Stand:** The requested information is not available with SEBI; property disposal and refund matters are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- **Appellate Ruling:** Upheld the CPIO's response citing precedent that CPIO cannot invent information not in possession, and reiterated that the Justice Lodha Committee is not a public authority under the RTI Act.
