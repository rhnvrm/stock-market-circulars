## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a confirmatory order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992, in the matter of Rajesh Exports Limited (REL) and its promoter Rajesh Mehta. The order confirms the ex-parte interim order dated June 3, 2026, following prima facie findings of non-cooperation, non-disclosure of material consolidated financial information, misrepresentation of standalone and consolidated financial statements, incorrect consolidation, incorrect adjustment of trade receivables against payables, and fund misutilization.

## Key Points

- **Confirmation of Interim Directions:** SEBI confirmed the interim directions issued on June 3, 2026, against Rajesh Exports Limited and Rajesh Mehta.
- **Non-Cooperation:** The noticees failed to cooperate adequately with SEBI's investigation and inspection proceedings.
- **Financial Misrepresentation:** Serious discrepancies and misrepresentations were identified in both consolidated and standalone financial statements of REL.
- **Fund Misutilization:** Findings indicate misutilization of funds and incorrect adjustment of trade receivables against trade payables.
- **Claims on Gold Mines:** Unsubstantiated and misleading claims regarding investments in gold mines in Africa.

## Regulatory Changes

- Continuation of existing interim restrictions, prohibitions, and impounding measures against the noticees as specified in the interim order to protect investor interests and the integrity of the securities market.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees must adhere strictly to all directions and restrictions imposed under the interim and confirmatory orders.
- Listed entities must ensure accurate financial disclosures and complete cooperation with regulatory investigations.

## Important Dates

- **Interim Order Date:** June 3, 2026
- **Confirmatory Order Date:** October 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on financial disclosures and corporate governance among listed companies.
- Maintains market confidence by penalizing financial misrepresentation and non-cooperation by promoters and listed entities.
