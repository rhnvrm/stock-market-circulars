## Summary

SEBI has notified the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Certification of Associated Persons in the Securities Markets) (Amendment) Regulations, 2026, updating terminology, definitions, statutory references under the Companies Act, and certification pathways involving NISM programs.

## Key Points

- Replacement of the term 'associated person' with 'designated person' throughout the 2007 regulations.
- Broadened definition of 'designated person' to include intermediaries' principals, employees, agents, distributors, and employees of foreign portfolio investors operating in India.
- Replacement of references to the Companies Act, 1956 with the Companies Act, 2013.
- Addition of NISM courses or programs, determined in consultation with the Board, as valid qualification routes under Regulation 4.

## Regulatory Changes

- Replaced 'associated person' (and variants) with 'designated person' (धारित / निर्धारित व्यक्ति).
- Updated Regulation 2(1) definitions, inserting a comprehensive definition for 'designated person'.
- Updated statutory references in Regulation 2(2) from Companies Act, 1956 to Companies Act, 2013.
- Amended Regulation 4 to include NISM-certified courses/programs established in consultation with the Board alongside existing examination and CPE routes.

## Compliance Requirements

- Intermediaries and covered market participants must ensure their principals, employees, agents, and distributors obtain required certifications under the revised designated person framework within prescribed timelines.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 7, 2026 (Date of publication in the Official Gazette).

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines regulatory terminology, modernizes legal references, and provides flexible certification avenues via NISM courses for market participants.
