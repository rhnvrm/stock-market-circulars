## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7695 of 2024 against Varalakshmi P. This enforcement action is taken in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. for the recovery of outstanding dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued by SEBI Recovery Officer under Recovery Certificate No. 7695 of 2024.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Varalakshmi P.
- Associated Entity / Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Date of Notice: October 13, 2026.
- Action involves attachment of bank accounts, movable and immovable properties to realize unpaid regulatory dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated pursuant to the provisions of the SEBI Act, 1992, for realization of penalties and disgorgement amounts resulting from prior investigations into financial misstatements and market irregularities.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and other intermediaries are mandated to freeze all accounts, shares, and assets held by the noticee as specified in the attachment notice and prevent any unauthorized transfer or withdrawal.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issuance / Attachment: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects SEBI's continued and rigorous enforcement measures to recover unpaid regulatory dues from individuals involved in corporate and financial violations related to Arvind Remedies Ltd.
