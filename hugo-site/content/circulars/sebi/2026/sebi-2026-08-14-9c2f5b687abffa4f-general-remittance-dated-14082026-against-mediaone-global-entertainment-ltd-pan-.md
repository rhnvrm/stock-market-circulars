## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance notice dated August 14, 2026, against Mediaone Global Entertainment Ltd. (PAN: AAACR0405M), identified as a defaulter under Recovery Certificate No. 9136 of 2026.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: Mediaone Global Entertainment Ltd.
- PAN: AAACR0405M
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9136 of 2026
- Date of Notice: August 14, 2026
- Issuing Authority: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an enforcement and recovery action under existing statutory powers.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities holding funds or assets pertaining to the defaulter are required to comply with the remittance and attachment directions as specified under the recovery certificate proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular/Notice: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct legal and financial impact is restricted to the named defaulter and entities holding its assets, ensuring recovery of dues as per SEBI regulations.
