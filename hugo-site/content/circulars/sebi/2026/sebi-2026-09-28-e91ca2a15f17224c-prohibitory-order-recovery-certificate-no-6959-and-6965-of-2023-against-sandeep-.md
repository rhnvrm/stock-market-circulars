## Summary

SEBI has issued Prohibitory Orders dated September 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate Nos. 6959 and 6965 of 2023 against Sandeep Tulsiram Agrawal in the matter of Kushal Limited. This action relates to recovery proceedings initiated for the realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts arising from regulatory investigations into trading activities in the scrip of Kushal Limited.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Orders issued under Recovery Certificate Nos. 6959 and 6965 of 2023.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Sandeep Tulsiram Agrawal.
- Matter: Kushal Limited (formerly Kushal Tradelink Ltd).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992, for the recovery of unpaid penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, depository participants, and other intermediaries are required to prohibit any transfer, debits, or withdrawal from bank accounts, lockers, and demat accounts held by the noticee as specified in the order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Prohibitory Order: September 28, 2026
- Recovery Certificates: 2023 (Nos. 6959 and 6965)

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and asset attachment measures by SEBI against promoters and key managerial personnel for non-compliance with monetary penalties.
