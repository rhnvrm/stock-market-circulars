## Summary

Appeal No. 7078 of 2026 was filed by Mukesh Arora and Sonia Narang under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI's response dated August 21, 2026. The appellants sought detailed information, file notings, action taken reports, and correspondence regarding a complaint dated July 17, 2025 submitted to the Chief Vigilance Officer of SEBI concerning the wrongful inclusion or continuation of a property in the PACL auction process where sale or transfer documents had already been executed by PACL, as well as details of similar complaints regarding other PACL properties.

## Key Points

- Appellants: Mukesh Arora and Sonia Narang.
- Respondent: CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai.
- RTI Application Date: August 13, 2026.
- CPIO Response Date: August 21, 2026.
- Appeal Date: September 03, 2026 (received by Appellate Authority on September 08, 2026).
- Subject Matter: Request for vigilance inquiry records, action taken reports, file notings, officer details, and inter-departmental/external correspondence (including with PACL and the Justice R.M. Lodha Committee) regarding PACL property auctions.
- Scope: Seeks details on the specific complaint dated July 17, 2025, as well as records of similar complaints objecting to PACL property auction inclusion.
