## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 3780 of 2021 dated June 04, 2021, issued against Shri Mukesh Kumar Goyal (Defaulter, PAN: AIIPG8861B) in the matter of GDR Issues of IKF Technologies Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 3780 of 2021 was issued on June 04, 2021.
- Defaulter involved: Shri Mukesh Kumar Goyal (PAN: AIIPG8861B).
- Matter pertains to Global Depository Receipts (GDR) issues of IKF Technologies Limited.
- Confirms completion or status update regarding the recovery proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery certificate provisions under SEBI Act/Regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Execution of recovery measures as directed by the Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Recovery Certificate Date: June 04, 2021
- Notice Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative and legal enforcement action specific to the named defaulter; minimal direct impact on general market operations.
