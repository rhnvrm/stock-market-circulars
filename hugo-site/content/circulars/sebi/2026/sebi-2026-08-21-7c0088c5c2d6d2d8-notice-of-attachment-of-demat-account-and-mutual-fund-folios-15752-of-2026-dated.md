## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment of Demat Account and Mutual Fund Folio(s) No. 15752 of 2026 on August 21, 2026, against Neha Aggarwal (PAN: AQDPA1749D). The action is taken pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 9251 of 2026 in the matter of trading-based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 15752 of 2026
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9251 of 2026
- Defaulter / Noticee: Neha Aggarwal (PAN: AQDPA1749D)
- Matter: Trading-based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Action: Attachment of Demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s) for recovery of dues/penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI under the SEBI Act, 1992 to recover dues resulting from illegal trading schemes and market manipulation.

## Compliance Requirements

Depository participants and mutual fund registrars/transfer agents (RTAs) are required to freeze the specified demat accounts and mutual fund folios of the defaulter to prevent any debits, transfers, or redemptions.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous regulatory enforcement against individuals involved in pump-and-dump schemes and social media-driven stock manipulation.
