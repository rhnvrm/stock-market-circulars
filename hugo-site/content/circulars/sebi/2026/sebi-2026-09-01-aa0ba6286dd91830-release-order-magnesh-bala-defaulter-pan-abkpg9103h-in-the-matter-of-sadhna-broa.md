## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order under Recovery Certificate No. 9270 of 2026 regarding Magnesh Bala (PAN: ABKPG9103H) in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited. This order releases the attachment previously placed on assets and accounts following the fulfillment of recovery requirements.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9270 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Magnesh Bala (PAN: ABKPG9103H).
- Matter: Sadhna Broadcast Limited pump-and-dump investigation and subsequent recovery proceedings.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action concluded pursuant to the realization of dues under recovery proceedings of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are instructed to lift the freeze/attachment on accounts and assets of the noticee as specified in the release order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: September 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Marks the successful resolution and lifting of asset attachments following the compliance and settlement of dues in the Sadhna Broadcast Limited enforcement matter.
