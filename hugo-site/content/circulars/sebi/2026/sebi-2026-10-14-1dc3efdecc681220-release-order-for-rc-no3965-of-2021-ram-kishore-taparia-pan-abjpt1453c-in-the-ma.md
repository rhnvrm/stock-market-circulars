## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order in respect of Recovery Certificate No. 3965 of 2021 drawn against Ram Kishore Taparia (PAN – ABJPT1453C) in the matter of Cosboard Industries Ltd. This order signifies the release/lifting of attachments or conclusion of recovery proceedings against the noticee.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued for Recovery Certificate No. 3965 of 2021.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Ram Kishore Taparia (PAN: ABJPT1453C).
- Scrip / Matter: Cosboard Industries Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of SEBI upon satisfaction or conclusion of recovery measures.

## Regulatory Changes

Official release of recovery proceedings and lifting of attachment orders pursuant to SEBI's enforcement and recovery mechanism.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and relevant authorities are notified of the release of attachment on assets/accounts of the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects conclusion of specific recovery proceedings against the individual, with minimal direct market impact on the broader scrip.
