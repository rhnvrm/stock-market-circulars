## Summary

SEBI has issued an Order for Compliance confirming the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 4473 of 2022. This recovery proceeding was initiated against Mr. Girishkumar Prabhudas Ruparel (PAN: AIPPR8860G) in the matter of Atlanta Infrastructure and Finance Limited. Following full compliance and recovery of the dues, SEBI has officially wound up the recovery certificate and issued a corresponding Release Order for any attached bank or demat accounts.

## Key Points

- **Completion of Recovery:** Recovery Certificate No. 4473 of 2022 against Mr. Girishkumar Prabhudas Ruparel is completed and closed.
- **Dues Settled:** The completion indicates that the outstanding regulatory penalties/dues in the matter of Atlanta Infrastructure and Finance Limited have been fully recovered or settled.
- **Asset Release:** SEBI has issued a Release Order to lift any previous attachment orders on the bank accounts, demat accounts, or other assets of the noticee.
- **Corporate Context:** The original action arose from investigations into manipulative or fraudulent trading in the scrip of Atlanta Infrastructure and Finance Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes introduced by this compliance order. It is an administrative order completing recovery proceedings and releasing attachments for a specific individual under existing SEBI recovery guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

The concerned banks, depositories (NSDL, CDSL), and financial institutions are required to act upon the accompanying Release Order to lift attachments on the bank accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund holdings of Mr. Girishkumar Prabhudas Ruparel (PAN: AIPPR8860G). No active compliance action is required from the general market or other market intermediaries.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** August 11, 2026 (Effective date of the completion of recovery and asset release).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** None. The order is specific to an individual and relates to a legacy enforcement case, having no wider impact on market operations or the trading of other securities.
- **Company Impact (Atlanta Infrastructure and Finance Ltd):** Neutral/Minimal. While the underlying case involved trading in the company's shares, this specific order completes recovery for an individual and has no operational or financial impact on the company itself.
- **Individual Impact:** Highly positive for Mr. Girishkumar Prabhudas Ruparel as his previously attached bank accounts and securities holdings are now released.
