## Summary

SEBI has issued a formal corrigendum regarding the Release Order for Recovery Certificate No. 4223 of 2021 in the matter of Sai Prakash Properties Development Ltd. The order corrects minor clerical and typographical errors present in the original Release Order dated August 14, 2026.

## Key Points

- Pertains to Recovery Certificate No. 4223 of 2021 concerning Sai Prakash Properties Development Ltd.
- Corrects clerical/typographical mistakes in the previous release order.
- Ensures proper legal and administrative alignment with recovery proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

No substantive regulatory changes or new compliance mandates; this is solely an administrative correction.

## Compliance Requirements

No additional compliance actions are required by market participants or entities, as this is an administrative document rectification.

## Important Dates

- Corrigendum Date: September 8, 2026
- Original Release Order Date: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Zero market or operational impact. The correction is purely procedural in the context of ongoing recovery proceedings related to illegal Collective Investment Schemes (CIS).
