## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order regarding Aastha Ruia Beneficial Trust for alleged execution of non-genuine reversal trades in the stock options segment of BSE between April 1, 2014, and September 30, 2015, creating artificial volumes in violation of PFUTP Regulations.

## Key Points

- SEBI observed large-scale reversal of trades in stock options at BSE leading to artificial volumes.
- The Noticee (Aastha Ruia Beneficial Trust) allegedly executed 6 non-genuine trades across 3 stock options contracts, resulting in artificial volumes of 2,58,000 units.
- Proceedings were initiated under Section 15HA of the SEBI Act for alleged violation of Regulations 3(a), (b), (c), (d), 4(1), and 4(2)(a) of PFUTP Regulations.

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must comply with prohibitions against manipulative, deceptive, and non-genuine trading in derivative segments.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: April 1, 2014 – September 30, 2015
- SCN Date: August 02, 2022
- Date of Order: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enforcement action reinforcing regulatory oversight on historical illiquid stock options misuse without broader systemic market impact.
