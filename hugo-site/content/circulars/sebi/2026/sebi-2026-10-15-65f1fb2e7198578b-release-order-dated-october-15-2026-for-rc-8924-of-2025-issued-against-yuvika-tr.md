## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order dated October 15, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 8924 of 2025, directing the release of attachment on the bank and demat accounts of Yuvika Tradewing LLP in the matter of Cressanda Solutions Limited.

## Key Points

- Order Type: Release Order for Recovery Proceedings.
- Recovery Certificate (RC) Number: RC 8924 of 2025.
- Entity Involved: Yuvika Tradewing LLP (PAN: AABFY4620R).
- Scrip Involved: Cressanda Solutions Limited.
- Action: Lifting/release of previously ordered attachment on bank and demat accounts following resolution/satisfaction of recovery dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action concluding recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992, restoring normal status to the entity's attached accounts upon compliance.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and depository participants are instructed to lift the freeze/attachment on the accounts of Yuvika Tradewing LLP as specified in the release order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: October 15, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Year: 2025 (RC 8924 of 2025)

## Impact Assessment

Marks the successful closure of recovery proceedings for the specified noticee, enabling normal banking and operational access for the entity.
