## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice for the attachment of Demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s) (No. 15690 of 2026 dated August 18, 2026) against Heena Khatoon, Proprietor of Heena Enterprises (PAN: FGXPK5721G) as a defaulter, pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 8876 of 2025 in the matter of an investigation into the trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of Continental Seeds and Chemicals Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice issued against: Heena Khatoon, Proprietor of Heena Enterprises (PAN-FGXPK5721G).
- Action: Attachment of Demat Account and Mutual Fund Folio(s) 15690 of 2026.
- Authority: Recovery Certificate No. 8876 of 2025.
- Context: Investigation in the trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of Continental Seeds and Chemicals Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an enforcement and recovery action against a specific defaulter.

## Compliance Requirements

Depository participants and mutual fund registrars/transfer agents are required to restrict operations and attach the specified Demat accounts and folios belonging to the defaulter.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 18, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Confined to the named defaulter and has no broad market or sectoral impact.
