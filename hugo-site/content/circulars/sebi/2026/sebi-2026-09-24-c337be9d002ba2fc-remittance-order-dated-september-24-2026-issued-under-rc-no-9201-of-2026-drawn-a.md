## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated September 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9201 of 2026 drawn against Dhaval Vinodbhai Gadani. This order relates to recovery proceedings and enforcement actions arising from investigations into trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9201 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Dhaval Vinodbhai Gadani.
- Matter: Trading activities and market irregularities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures executed pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of penalties and dues resulting from unauthorized trading activities and price manipulation.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated entities holding attached funds or assets are directed to remit the realized amounts to SEBI's designated recovery account as per the remittance order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Remittance Order: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's continued stringent enforcement and active recovery mechanisms against individuals found guilty of stock price manipulation in SME scrips.
