## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 9258 of 2026 dated July 27, 2026, issued to Neha Agarwal (PAN: BLJPA5504J) as a defaulter in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited. This administrative action typically follows the settlement or satisfaction of dues or resolution under recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate Number: 9258 of 2026
- Original Date of Certificate: July 27, 2026
- Defaulter / Noticee: Neha Agarwal (PAN: BLJPA5504J)
- Matter: Sadhna Broadcast Limited enforcement and recovery proceedings
- Action: Cancellation of Recovery Certificate and subsequent release of asset/account attachments

## Regulatory Changes

Formal closure and withdrawal of recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992 against the specified individual.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are required to lift any restrictions, freezes, or attachments placed on the accounts or assets of the noticee pursuant to the cancelled recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Original Certificate Date: July 27, 2026
- Cancellation / Order Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects individual resolution of recovery dues in the broader Sadhna Broadcast enforcement case, lifting encumbrances on the concerned person's assets without broader systemic market impact.
