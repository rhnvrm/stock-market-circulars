## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7056 of 2026 filed by Rabindra Nath Sahay under the RTI Act, 2005. The appeal was directed against the CPIO's response concerning refund status details for deposits made in Golden Forest India Ltd under the Justice R.N. Agrawal Committee records. The appeal was found to be time-barred and also lacking merit as the requested information was not available with SEBI.

## Key Points

- The appellant filed an RTI application seeking details on deposit account status and refund phases concerning Golden Forest India Ltd.
- The CPIO responded stating that the requested information is not available with SEBI.
- The first appeal was filed beyond the statutory 30-day period without any request for condonation of delay.
- On merits, the Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that public authorities are only required to provide information available in their records.
