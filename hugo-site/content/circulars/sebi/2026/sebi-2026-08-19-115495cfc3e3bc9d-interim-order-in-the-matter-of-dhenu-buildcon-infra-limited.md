## Summary

SEBI passed an interim order pursuant to a reference from the Serious Fraud Investigation Office (SFIO) regarding potential violations of the SEBI (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices relating to Securities Market) Regulations, 2003 (PFUTP Regulations) in the matter of Dhenu Buildcon Infra Limited (DBIL) and associated noticees. The examination uncovered schemes involving purported loans, round-tripping of funds, preferential allotment of equity, and severe interconnections between the noticees.

## Key Points

- SEBI initiated an examination following a reference from SFIO dated April 20, 2026, under PFUTP Regulations.
- DBIL (formerly The Hingir Rampur Coal Company Limited) was an NBFC listed on BSE, whose Certificate of Registration was cancelled by the Reserve Bank of India on September 12, 2025.
- The order highlights extensive round-tripping of funds, conversion of purported loans into equity, preferential allotments, and coordinated actions among multiple entities and individuals including Surendra Kumar Jain and Virendra Jain.
- WhatsApp messages and financial analysis revealed a lack of commercial substance and physical existence for several participating entities.

## Compliance Requirements

Noticees and associated entities must comply with the interim directions issued by SEBI under Sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B of the SEBI Act pending further investigations and final orders.

## Important Dates

- **Reference Date from SFIO:** April 20, 2026
- **RBI Cancellation Date:** September 12, 2025

## Impact Assessment

The order restricts fraudulent market practices and safeguards market integrity by penalizing entities involved in round-tripping and artificial inflation of capital through preferential allotments.
