## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate No. RC9307 of 2026 against Sri Salasar Suppliers Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AADCS8457R) in the matter of Decillion Finance Limited. This action is part of SEBI's regulatory enforcement and recovery proceedings to realize dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Notice of Demand issued under Recovery Certificate No. RC9307 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Sri Salasar Suppliers Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AADCS8457R).
- Underlying Matter: Decillion Finance Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of outstanding dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant entities are required to comply with attachment and demand notices, ensuring freezing of accounts and assets of the noticee as directed by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: October 2026 (Recovery Certificate No. RC9307 of 2026)

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory compliance and rigorous recovery enforcement against entities involved in violations related to Decillion Finance Limited.
