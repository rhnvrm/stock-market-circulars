## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated August 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9184 of 2026 drawn against Dhaval Vinodbhai Gadani. This action relates to investigations into trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under RC No. 9184 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Dhaval Vinodbhai Gadani.
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues/penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to remit funds and assets as specified in the remittance order and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against entities and individuals involved in market irregularities in DU Digital Global Limited.
