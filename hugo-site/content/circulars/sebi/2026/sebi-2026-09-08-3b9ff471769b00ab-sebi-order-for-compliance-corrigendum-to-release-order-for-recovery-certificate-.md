## Summary

SEBI has issued a corrigendum to the Release Order pertaining to Recovery Certificate No. 4216 of 2021, updating and rectifying administrative details concerning the earlier recovery and release proceedings.

## Key Points

- Corrigendum issued for Release Order under Recovery Certificate No. 4216 of 2021.
- Rectification of administrative or clerical details in the original release order.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Administrative updates and corrections to ongoing recovery certificate proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant banks, financial institutions, and authorities are notified of the corrected details in the release order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Corrigendum: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational clarification regarding recovery and release orders with minimal direct market impact.
