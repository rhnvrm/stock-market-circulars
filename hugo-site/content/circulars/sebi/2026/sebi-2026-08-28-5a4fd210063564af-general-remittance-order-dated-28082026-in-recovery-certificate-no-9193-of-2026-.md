## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated August 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9193 of 2026 drawn against Jigneshkumar Purshottamdas Patel (PAN: AQEPP8019J). This order arises in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9193 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Jigneshkumar Purshottamdas Patel (PAN: AQEPP8019J).
- Scrip: DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated pursuant to the provisions of the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant entities are required to comply with the remittance and attachment directions specified in the order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates continuous regulatory follow-up and enforcement measures by SEBI against entities linked to market irregularities in DU Digital Global Limited.
