## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice for the attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s) (Notice No. 15722 of 2026 dated August 20, 2026) against Sadhna Bio Oils Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAKCS4057N) as a defaulter under Recovery Certificate No. 9244 of 2026. The recovery action is part of the ongoing investigation and enforcement in the matter of unauthorized trading-based stock recommendations using YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Defaulter Entity: Sadhna Bio Oils Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAKCS4057N).
- Recovery Certificate: No. 9244 of 2026.
- Action Taken: Attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folios.
- Context: Matter of trading-based stock recommendations via YouTube regarding Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement and recovery action against a specific entity.

## Compliance Requirements

Depository participants, mutual fund houses, and financial intermediaries are required to freeze and prohibit any debits, transfers, or transactions concerning the attached demat accounts and mutual fund folios of the defaulter.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures enforcement of recovery orders and restricts asset alienation by entities found violating securities regulations through illegal stock recommendations.
