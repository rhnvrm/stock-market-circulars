## Summary

SEBI initiated adjudication proceedings under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992 against two noticees, Mr. Ramakant Gupta and Mr. Manish Kumar Sowatia (Designated Persons of Jindal Steel & Power Limited), for allegedly trading in the scrip of the company during a trading window closure period and without obtaining pre-clearance, in violation of Regulations 9(1) read with clauses 4 and 6 of the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.

## Key Points

- The case arises from trading activity in the scrip of Jindal Steel & Power Limited around the disclosure of financial results for the quarter ended June 30, 2023.
- The company had closed its trading window from July 01, 2023 to August 13, 2023.
- Noticee No. 1 (Mr. Ramakant Gupta) and Noticee No. 2 (Mr. Manish Kumar Sowatia) executed trades during this closure period without obtaining the mandatory pre-clearance from the company.
- The actions violate Regulations 9(1) read with clauses 4 and 6 of the PIT Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A (Adjudication order enforcing existing regulations).

## Compliance Requirements

- Designated persons must strictly adhere to trading window closures declared by listed entities.
- Mandatory pre-clearance must be obtained from the company prior to conducting any trades in securities beyond prescribed thresholds.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period (IP): June 01, 2023 to August 31, 2023
- Financial Results Disclosed: August 11, 2023
- Trading Window Closure: July 01, 2023 to August 13, 2023
- Appointment of Adjudicating Officer: July 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory compliance regarding insider trading norms, serving as a deterrent against unauthorized trading by designated persons during sensitive periods such as financial result disclosures.
