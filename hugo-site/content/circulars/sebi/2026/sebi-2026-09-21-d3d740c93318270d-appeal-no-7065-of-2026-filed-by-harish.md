## Summary

The appellant, Harish, filed an RTI appeal against the CPIO, SEBI, regarding information sought about the Motilal Oswal Microcap 250 Index Fund being kept on hold due to category issues. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that appropriate information regarding SID filing and observations was provided, and that queries seeking clarifications and opinions do not constitute 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought information regarding prior approvals required by AMCs for launching mutual fund schemes, specifically concerning Motilal Oswal Microcap 250 Index Fund.
- CPIO informed that AMCs file draft Scheme Information Documents (SID) with SEBI for observations before launch, and that the concerned AMC had completed this process.
- CPIO noted that queries regarding how the fund was launched if categories cannot be maintained and future regulatory measures are in the nature of clarifications/opinions, falling outside Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- The Appellate Authority concurred with the CPIO's response and dismissed the appeal.
