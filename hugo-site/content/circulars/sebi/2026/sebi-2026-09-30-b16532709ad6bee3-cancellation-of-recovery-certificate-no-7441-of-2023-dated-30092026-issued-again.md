## Summary

SEBI has issued an order dated September 30, 2026, cancelling Recovery Certificate No. 7441 of 2023 issued against Keynote Enterprises Pvt Ltd in the matter of TV Vision Ltd.

## Key Points

- Cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 7441 of 2023.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Keynote Enterprises Pvt Ltd.
- Matter: TV Vision Ltd.
- Issued by SEBI Recovery Proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Withdrawal and cancellation of enforcement recovery action under SEBI framework.

## Compliance Requirements

Release of any attachment or recovery measures initiated pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 7441 of 2023 against Keynote Enterprises Pvt Ltd.

## Important Dates

- Date of Cancellation / Order: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides formal relief to Keynote Enterprises Pvt Ltd with the withdrawal of the SEBI recovery certificate in the TV Vision Ltd matter.
