## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has entered into a tripartite Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Rashtriya Raksha University (RRU) and National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM) on August 17, 2026, to foster long-term collaboration in capacity building, research, education, and technological innovation.

## Key Points

- Tripartite MoU signed between SEBI, RRU, and NISM on August 17, 2026.
- Focuses on capacity building, research, training, policy support, and knowledge exchange.
- Joint design and delivery of training programs, workshops, and certification courses for government officials, law enforcement, market participants, and the general public.
- Specialized training covering secure software development, API security, cybersecurity exercises, SOC, VAPT, AI, and quantum computing.
- Establishment of a Technical Innovation Unit (TIU) as a Command Centre for the Securities Market.

## Regulatory Changes

No direct regulatory changes or mandates for listed companies; rather, an institutional framework for training and technical cooperation.

## Compliance Requirements

Regulated entities and market participants may participate in upcoming specialized training and certification programs offered under the MoU.

## Important Dates

- **MoU Signing Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Press Release Date:** August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enhances market intelligence, cybersecurity readiness, and skill development across the Indian securities market ecosystem through institutional partnerships and advanced technological training.
