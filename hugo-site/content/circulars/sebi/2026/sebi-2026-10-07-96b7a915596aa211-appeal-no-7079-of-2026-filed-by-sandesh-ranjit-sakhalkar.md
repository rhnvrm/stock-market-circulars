## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7079 of 2026 filed by Shri Sandesh Ranjit Sakhalkar under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appeal pertained to information requested regarding SEBI orders and recovery proceedings in the matter of M/s Networth Marketing Limited, specifically concerning the investor refund status of the investor Ranjit Shrikrishna Sakhalkar.

## Key Points

- **Appellant:** Sandesh Ranjit Sakhalkar seeking information on behalf of his father, an investor.
- **Respondent:** CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai.
- **Matter:** SEBI orders dated August 28, 2017, and July 30, 2018, along with Recovery Certificate No. 1895 of 2019 regarding M/s Networth Marketing Limited.
- **Delay Condonation:** The appeal was filed beyond the stipulated 30-day window under Section 19(1) of the RTI Act, but was considered on merit by the Appellate Authority.
- **Confirmation:** The CPIO confirmed that the investor's name (Ranjit Shrikrishna Sakhalkar) is present in the investor list submitted by M/s Networth Marketing Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a quasi-judicial appellate order under the RTI Act addressing an individual information request.

## Compliance Requirements
