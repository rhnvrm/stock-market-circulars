## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate No. 9279 of 2026 against OnePaper Research Analysts Private Limited (PAN: AADCO2642Q) for a total sum of Rs. 30,91,000/-. The dues comprise a penalty imposed by the Adjudicating Officer, accrued interest, and recovery costs.

## Key Points

- **Entity Name:** OnePaper Research Analysts Private Limited (PAN: AADCO2642Q)
- **Address:** 74, Techno Park, 74/II “C” Cross Road, Opposite Gate No. 2, MIDC, Speez, Andheri east, Mumbai - 400093
- **Total Dues:** Rs. 30,91,000/-
- **Penalty Amount:** Rs. 30,00,000/- (vide Order No. Order/AK/DS/2026-27/32446 dated 16.06.2026)
- **Interest & Costs:** Rs. 90,000/- (Interest from June 2026 to August 2026 @ 1% p.m.) and Rs. 1,000/- (Recovery cost).

## Regulatory Changes

- Notice issued under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Section 222 of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

## Compliance Requirements

- The entity is directed to pay the total amount within 15 days of receipt of the notice via direct credit through EFT/NEFT/RTGS, online payment via the SEBI SI Portal, or the Recovery Payment module.
- Payment confirmation must be sent to the Recovery Officer and via email to designated SEBI officials.
- Prohibition on transferring, mortgaging, or dealing with properties without permission from the Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** June 16, 2026
- **Payment Window:** Within 15 days of receipt of the notice.

## Impact Assessment

- Enforcement of recovery proceedings against the defaulting entity, with potential attachment of movable/immovable properties and bank accounts in case of non-compliance.
