## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice under Recovery Certificate No. 9257 of 2026 against Gaurav Gupta (PAN: AKHPG5185D) as a defaulter in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery proceedings initiated via Recovery Certificate No. 9257 of 2026.
- Defaulter identified as Gaurav Gupta (PAN: AKHPG5185D).
- Relates to enforcement and disgorgement actions involving Sadhna Broadcast Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery certificates through attachment and remittance advice as per SEBI regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Designated financial institutions and intermediaries must comply with remittance and attachment directions issued by the Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures strict enforcement of regulatory penalties and disgorgement orders in market manipulation cases.
