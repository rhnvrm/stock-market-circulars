## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Attachment of Demat Account and Mutual Fund Folio(s) (Notice No. 15686 of 2026 dated August 17, 2026) against Nishaa Anand (PAN: AJWPA4527H) under Recovery Certificate No. 9271 of 2026 in the matter of Illiquid Stock Options (ISO) at BSE.

## Key Points

- Defaulter Name: Nishaa Anand
- PAN: AJWPA4527H
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9271 of 2026
- Matter: Illiquid Stock Options (ISO) at BSE
- Notice Details: Notice of Attachment of Demat Account and Mutual Fund Folio(s) issued under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to non-payment of dues or penalties levied by SEBI in connection with irregular trading practices in Illiquid Stock Options.

## Compliance Requirements

Depositories, brokers, and mutual fund RTAs must freeze all demat accounts and mutual fund folios held singly or jointly by the named defaulter and prevent any debits, transfers, or redemptions without prior approval from SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 17, 2026
- Recovery Certificate: No. 9271 of 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricts the defaulter from accessing securities markets, liquidating holdings, or withdrawing mutual fund investments until dues are fully cleared.
