## Summary

SEBI has issued a final order under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 and SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 against Capital Stroke Investment Services Pvt. Ltd. (Investment Adviser, Registration No. INA000001316). The order addresses multiple inspection findings revealing widespread non-compliance, fraudulent practices, promising assured returns, submission of forged documents, and failure to act in a fiduciary capacity.

## Key Points

- Noticee engaged in promising assured returns and misrepresenting research team performance and accuracy.
- Failure to act in a fiduciary capacity and adhere to the Code of Conduct for Investment Advisers.
- Submission of forged documents to SEBI during the inspection process.
- Procedural and substantive failures regarding risk-profiling questionnaires, weightages, supporting documents, and call recordings.
- Charging arbitrary/illogical fees, multiple times before tenure completion, and exceeding website-specified fees.
- Involvement in unregistered advisory activities through associated entities like Green Wealth.
- Failure to redress investor complaints.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and binding directions issued under the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 and PFUTP Regulations against the defaulting investment adviser.

## Compliance Requirements

Investment advisers must strictly comply with IA Regulations, maintain accurate risk-profiling records, avoid guaranteed/assured returns, charge only specified fees, and maintain call recordings and supporting documents.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 2026
- Inspection Period: November 03, 2020 to November 06, 2020
- SCN Date: January 05, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight over registered investment advisers, protecting retail investors from fraudulent advisory practices and unregistered entities.
