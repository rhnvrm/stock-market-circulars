## Summary

An appeal was filed by Amit Gangrade under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO's response concerning the status of a representation dated March 29, 2026, related to Recovery Certificate No. 8971 of 2025. The Appellate Authority observed that the CPIO had not adequately addressed the query regarding the representation's status and remitted the application back for de novo consideration within 15 working days.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought the status of a representation dated March 29, 2026, concerning Recovery Certificate No. 8971 of 2025.
- CPIO's initial response only provided details regarding the exclusion of Rs. 15,000/- due to an NEFT failure from the remaining amount, leaving the representation status unaddressed.
- The Appellate Authority found the response inadequate and ordered a de novo consideration.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a quasi-judicial appellate order under the RTI Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

- SEBI CPIO is required to provide a de novo response to the appellant regarding the representation within 15 working days from the date of the order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 22, 2026
- Response Deadline: Within 15 working days from the receipt of the order

## Impact Assessment

- Operational impact is restricted to the procedural compliance of the RTI application and has no broader market or systemic implications.
