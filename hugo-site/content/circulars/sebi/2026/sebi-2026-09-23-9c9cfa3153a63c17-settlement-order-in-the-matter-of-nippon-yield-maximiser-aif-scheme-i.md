## Summary

SEBI has issued a settlement order pursuant to a suo-motu settlement application filed by Nippon Life India AIF Management Limited regarding the non-compliance of regulation 29(1)(a) read with regulation 29(7) of the AIF Regulations concerning the Nippon India Yield Maximiser AIF Scheme I.

## Key Points

- Nippon Life India AIF Management Limited acted as the manager for the Nippon India Yield Maximiser AIF Scheme I.
- The scheme failed to wind up by the end of its extended tenure on March 08, 2020, and did not liquidate investments and distribute proceeds within 1 year.
- The applicant filed a suo-motu settlement application under the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018.
- The High Powered Advisory Committee (HPAC) and Panel of Whole Time Members approved a settlement amount of ₹10,87,500/-

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Payment of the recommended settlement amount of ₹10,87,500/- in accordance with the Settlement Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Scheme Extended Tenure End Date: March 08, 2020
- SEBI Circular Flexibility Date: April 26, 2024
- IC Meeting Date: June 04, 2026
- Revised Terms Submission: June 05, 2026
- HPAC Meeting Date: June 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The settlement resolves enforcement proceedings against the AMC for the specified violation without admission or denial of guilt, with negligible market impact.
