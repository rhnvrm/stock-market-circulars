## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7706 of 2024 against Solomon Y. This action is part of ongoing recovery proceedings and enforcement measures in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. to realize outstanding dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7706 of 2024.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Solomon Y.
- Company in matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action executed pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of unpaid dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to freeze accounts, lockers, and assets of the noticee as specified in the attachment notice and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice of Attachment: October 13, 2026.
- Initial Demand Notice / Recovery Certificate: February 26, 2024 (RC No. 7706 of 2024).

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery actions by SEBI against individuals and entities associated with financial irregularities and non-compliance in Arvind Remedies Ltd.
