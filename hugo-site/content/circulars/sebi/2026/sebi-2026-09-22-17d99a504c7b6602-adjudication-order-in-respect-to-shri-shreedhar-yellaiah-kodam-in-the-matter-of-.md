## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order concerning Shri Shreedhar Yellaiah Kodam in the matter of Well Pack Papers and Containers Limited, following an SAT order setting aside a previous penalty due to violations of natural justice and directing reconsideration.

## Key Points

- An original penalty of Rs. 5,00,000/- was imposed on February 16, 2015, under Section 15HA of the SEBI Act for alleged PFUTP violations.
- The Noticee appealed to the Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT), which set aside the order and directed SEBI to reconsider the case after giving an opportunity of defense.
- Proceedings were resumed under a newly appointed Adjudicating Officer in 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (individual quasi-judicial adjudication).

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance directed specifically to the Noticee to participate in the adjudication proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Date of SAT Order: February 13, 2026
- Date of SEBI AO Appointment: February 24, 2026
- Hearing Proceedings: March-April 2026 onwards

## Impact Assessment

- Isolated impact limited to the individual noticee and company proceedings.
