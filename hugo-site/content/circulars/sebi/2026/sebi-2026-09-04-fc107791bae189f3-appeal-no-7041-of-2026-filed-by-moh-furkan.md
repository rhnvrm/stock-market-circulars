## Summary

An RTI appeal filed by Moh Furkan against the CPIO of SEBI regarding PACL refund status and land allotment details was disposed of by the Appellate Authority. The Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the requested information is not available with SEBI and that refund matters are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought information regarding land allotment and refund status under PACL Certificate No. U199128xxx.
- CPIO and Appellate Authority confirmed that SEBI does not possess the requested records.
- Communication regarding PACL refund status is managed exclusively by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The appeal was dismissed as devoid of merit with no deficiency found in the CPIO's reply.
