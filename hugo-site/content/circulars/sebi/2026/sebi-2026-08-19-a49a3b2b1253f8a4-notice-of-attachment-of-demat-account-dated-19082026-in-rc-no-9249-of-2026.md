## Summary

 Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Attachment under Recovery Proceeding No. 15714 of 2026 (Certificate No. 9249 of 2026) dated August 19, 2026, against Jagdish Chhanabhai Vaghela. The attachment is ordered to recover total dues amounting to Rs. 6,53,117.93, which includes disgorgement amounts directed vide Order No. QJA/MN/IVD/ID7/31947/2025-26 dated 31.12.2025 in the matter of trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (Now DU Digital Global Limited), along with interest and recovery costs.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter Name:** Jagdish Chhanabhai Vaghela (PAN: AFPPV2562Q)
- **Recovery Certificate No.:** 9249 of 2026
- **Attachment Proceeding No.:** 15714 of 2026
- **Total Dues:** Rs. 6,53,117.93
- **Entities Addressed:** National Securities Depository Ltd. (NSDL), Central Depository Services (I) Ltd. (CDSL), and all Mutual Funds in India.
- **Action Ordered:** Immediate attachment of all Demat accounts and Mutual fund folios held singly or jointly by the defaulter, with a strict prohibition on any debits.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A (Individual enforcement and recovery action)

## Compliance Requirements

- Depositories (NSDL/CDSL) and all Mutual Funds must immediately attach all Demat accounts and mutual fund folios held by the defaulter (singly or jointly).
- No debits shall be permitted in the said accounts/folios until further orders, while credits are allowed.
- Provide details of accounts/folios, account statements, and confirmation of attachment to SEBI.

## Important Dates

- **Certificate Date:** July 27, 2026
- **Notice Date:** August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricted to the specific defaulter's accounts; no broader market impact.
