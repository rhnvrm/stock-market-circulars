## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9275 of 2026 in the matter of Kunnamkulam Paper Mills Ltd. This order directs the remittance of attached funds or assets toward the recovery of outstanding dues.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9275 of 2026.
- Defaulter: Kunnamkulam Paper Mills Ltd.
- Date of order: September 28, 2026.
- Focuses on recovery of investor funds and dues arising from past regulatory violations.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures under the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of outstanding penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated entities are required to remit attached funds as per the directions of the SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's rigorous enforcement stance in recovering unpaid dues and enforcing compliance orders against defaulting entities.
