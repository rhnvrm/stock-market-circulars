## Summary

SEBI has notified the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Mutual Funds) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026, updating cross-references regarding intermediaries and omitting the Fifth Schedule.

## Key Points

- Amends regulation 28 (Chapter V) and regulation 81(l) (Chapter XVI) of the SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026.
- Replaces references to the 'Fifth Schedule' with 'Chapter IIID of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008'.
- Omits the Fifth Schedule from the Mutual Funds Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Chapter V, regulation 28: References to the Fifth Schedule replaced with Chapter IIID of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.
- Chapter XVI, regulation 81(l): References updated similarly.
- Fifth Schedule: Omitted entirely.

## Compliance Requirements

- Mutual funds and asset management companies must align compliance references with Chapter IIID of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.

## Important Dates

- Notification Date: October 8, 2026.
- Effective Date: On the one hundred eightieth day from the date of publication in the Official Gazette.

## Impact Assessment

- Administrative restructuring of regulatory provisions with negligible operational impact on market participants.
