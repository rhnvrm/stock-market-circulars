## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Prohibitory Order dated September 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 6955 of 2023 issued against Mr. Manoj Tulsiram Agrawal in the matter of Kushal Limited. This action is part of SEBI's enforcement and recovery proceedings to realize outstanding dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 6955 of 2023.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Mr. Manoj Tulsiram Agrawal.
- Listed Entity: Kushal Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of SEBI to attach assets and freeze accounts.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for the recovery of monetary penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to comply with the prohibitory order by freezing accounts, mutual funds, demat accounts, and other movable/immovable properties of the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Notice: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against individuals associated with compliance failures or market irregularities in Kushal Limited.
