## Summary

On August 7, 2026, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) issued an administrative order (Reference No. CGM/SM/MIRSD/MIRSD-RAC-2/102/2026) cancelling the certificates of registration of 39 investment advisers. This enforcement action, passed by Chief General Manager Soma Majumder, was taken because these advisers failed to pay the mandatory five-yearly renewal fees required under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013. SEBI clarified that this cancellation is an administrative cleanup measure to maintain an updated registry and prevent the misuse of expired certificates, and does not carry any findings of fraud, dishonesty, or operational misconduct.

## Key Points

- **Registration Cancellation:** Formal cancellation of registration certificates for 39 investment advisers with immediate effect.
- **Core Cause:** Failure to pay the mandatory five-yearly renewal fees as required by Regulation 13 of the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.
- **Affected Entities:** Key entities affected include Getclarity Fintech Services Private Limited, The Equicom Financial Research Pvt. Ltd., N2 Capital Advisory LLP, Prutech Financial Services Pvt. Ltd., and Tacterial Consulting Private Ltd., along with various individual proprietors.
- **Administrative Nature:** Explicitly clarified by SEBI as an administrative housekeeping measure without any findings of fraudulent activities.
- **Investor Protection:** Investors are cautioned against dealing with these entities for any investment advisory services for a fee, as they are no longer registered with SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

- There are no changes to existing regulations. This order represents the enforcement of existing provisions under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, which mandate the payment of renewal fees every five years to keep a certificate of registration active.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Immediate Cessation:** All 39 listed investment advisers must immediately cease offering or undertaking any investment advisory operations.
- **Grievance Redressal:** Affected advisers remain liable to redress any outstanding investor complaints or grievances.
- **Transfer of Records:** Affected advisers must facilitate the transfer or return of client records, funds, or securities according to customer instructions.
- **Public Representation:** These entities are no longer permitted to represent themselves, display logos, or use designations indicating SEBI registration.

## Important Dates

- **Order Issuance Date:** August 7, 2026
- **Effective Date:** August 7, 2026 (Immediate effect)

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Low. This is a routine administrative housekeeping action to prune inactive or non-compliant registrations from the register and does not affect compliant active intermediaries.
- **Operational Impact:** High for the 39 affected investment advisers, as they must completely shut down their advisory operations and manage client wind-downs.
- **Investor Impact:** Medium. Serves to protect investors from dealing with unregistered/non-compliant entities and advises active clients of these firms to transition to registered alternatives.
