## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated September 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9205 of 2026 drawn against Nayan Mahendrabhai Thakkar. This action pertains to recovery proceedings and remittance of funds in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9205 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Nayan Mahendrabhai Thakkar.
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated garnishees are required to remit attached funds or assets in accordance with the remittance order issued by SEBI's Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of Remittance Order: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing regulatory enforcement and rigorous recovery actions by SEBI to enforce compliance and realize dues in connection with irregularities in DU Digital Global Limited.
