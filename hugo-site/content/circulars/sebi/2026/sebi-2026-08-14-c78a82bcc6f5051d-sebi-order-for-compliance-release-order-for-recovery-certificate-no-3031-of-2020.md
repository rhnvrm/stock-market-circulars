## Summary

SEBI has issued a release order regarding Recovery Certificate No. 3031 of 2020 against M/s Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited, along with its directors and promoters including Mrs. Pushpanjali Singh Baghel, Mr. Ranvajay Pratap Singh, Mr. Dheerendra Krishan Pratap, Mr. Mragendra Singh Baghel, Mr. Pushpendra Singh Baghel, and Mr. Shahendra Singh Baghel, in connection with unauthorized collective investment schemes.

## Key Points

- Involves recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI under Recovery Certificate No. 3031 of 2020.
- Targets M/s Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited and multiple associated individuals.
- Relates to non-compliance with orders directing refunds to investors from illegal collective investment schemes.

## Regulatory Changes

[None - Enforcement and Recovery Action]

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and individuals named must adhere to the ongoing recovery and settlement framework established under relevant regulatory and judicial oversight.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Facilitates the enforcement and asset recovery process associated with refunds owed to investors of illegal collective investment schemes.
