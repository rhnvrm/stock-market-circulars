## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7026 of 2026 filed by Samar Imran against the CPIO, SEBI under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought certified copies of records and confirmation regarding whether a Family Member Certificate issued by the Andhra Pradesh Government is acceptable as equivalent to a Legal Heirship Certificate. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that queries seeking clarifications, opinions, or interpretations do not qualify as 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant filed an RTI application seeking details and file notings related to the term 'equivalent certificate' used for proof of legal heirship.
- The appellant specifically enquired whether the Family Member Certificate issued in Andhra Pradesh is accepted as an equivalent to a Legal Heirship Certificate.
- The CPIO responded that the queries were in the nature of seeking clarifications or opinions, which do not fall under the definition of 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- The Appellate Authority confirmed that the CPIO is not obligated to provide interpretations, clarifications, or create new analysis under the RTI Act, citing relevant Central Information Commission precedents.

## Impact Assessment

This order reaffirms existing jurisprudence regarding the scope of Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, clarifying that statutory authorities are not required to provide opinions, legal interpretations, or clarifications on documents under the Act.
