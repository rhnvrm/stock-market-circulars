## Summary

SEBI issued a settlement order in the matter of PMC Projects (India) Private Limited concerning settlement applications filed by Mr. B. Ravi (CFO of APSEZL) and Mr. Karan Adani (CEO of APSEZL) regarding alleged violations of regulation 17(8) read with paragraph B of Part B of Schedule II of LODR Regulations and section 21 of SCRA.

## Key Points

- Investigation conducted into banking transactions and inter-corporate security deposits amongst PMC Projects (India) Private Limited, APSEZL, and its subsidiaries.
- Alleged violations against Mr. B. Ravi and Mr. Karan Adani regarding LODR Regulations and SCRA.
- Applicants filed settlement applications under the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018.

[None specified]

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must adhere to LODR Regulations and disclosure requirements regarding inter-corporate transactions and banking disclosures.
