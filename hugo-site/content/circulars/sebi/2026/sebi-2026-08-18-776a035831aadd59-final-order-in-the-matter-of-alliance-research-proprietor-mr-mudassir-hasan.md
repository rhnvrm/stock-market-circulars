## Summary

SEBI issued a final order regarding an inspection of Alliance Research and its sole proprietor, Mr. Mudassir Hasan, an investment adviser registered since April 2015. The inspection revealed multiple violations including carrying on unregistered advisory activities, improper KYC and risk profiling, charging unfair fees, and promising assured returns to clients in violation of the SEBI Act, IA Regulations, and PFUTP Regulations.

## Key Points

- SEBI conducted an inspection of Alliance Research during February 2020 covering the period from April 2018 to February 2020.
- Identified violations include unregistered investment advisory activities, improper risk profiling and product suitability assessment, exorbitant and unfair fee collection, and promising assured returns.
- Interim and confirmatory orders were previously passed to restrain the Noticee from accessing the securities market and acting as an investment adviser.
- The Noticee subsequently filed settlement applications for related proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of statutory directions under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1), and 11B(2) of the SEBI Act against the Noticee for regulatory breaches.

## Compliance Requirements

- Investment Advisers must strictly adhere to the IA Regulations, maintain proper KYC and risk profiling records, avoid promising assured returns, and refrain from charging unfair fees.
- Depositories and financial institutions must maintain debits and credits freezes on accounts as directed by SEBI orders.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 1, 2018 – February 15, 2020
- Interim Order Date: January 6, 2021
- Confirmatory Order Date: July 29, 2022

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights SEBI's stringent regulatory oversight on Investment Advisers and strict penal action against unauthorized or fraudulent investment advisory practices.
