## Summary

SEBI has issued Notice(s) of Attachment dated August 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 8927 of 2025 in the matter of Royal Twinkle Star Club Private Limited. The recovery proceedings are directed towards attaching bank accounts, post office accounts, and other assets of the company and its defaulter directors to recover outstanding dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Notice(s) of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 8927 of 2025.
- Entity Involved: Royal Twinkle Star Club Private Limited (RTSCL).
- Defaulters/Noticees include key directors and associated entities involved in illegal fundraising.
- Purpose: Recovery of dues stemming from unauthorized Collective Investment Schemes (CIS).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and investor refunds.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and asset holders are required to freeze accounts and assets as specified in the attachment notice and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice of Attachment: August 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous regulatory enforcement and asset recovery measures by SEBI to protect investors and execute refunds in fraudulent collective investment schemes.
