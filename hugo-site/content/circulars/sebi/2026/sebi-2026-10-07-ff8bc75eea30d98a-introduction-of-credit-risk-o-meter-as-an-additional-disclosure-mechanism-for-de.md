## Summary

SEBI has introduced the 'Credit Risk-o-Meter' as a mandatory additional disclosure mechanism for debt securities to assist investors in assessing credit risk through colour-coded visualization prior to investing. This requirement applies to offer documents, abridged prospectuses, private placement memorandums (PPM), all issuer/OBPP advertisements, and web/mobile platforms of Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs).

## Key Points

- Introduction of Credit Risk-o-Meter providing colour-coded visualization of credit risk for debt securities.
- Applicable to Non-Convertible Securities (NCS), Commercial Papers (CPs), Securitised Debt Instruments (SDIs), Security Receipts (SRs), and Structured Debt / Market Linked Debentures (MLDs).
- Mandatory across offer documents, abridged prospectuses, PPMs, advertisements, and OBPP web/mobile platforms.
- Inserts a new Chapter II-C into the NCS Master Circular dated October 15, 2025.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inserts Chapter II-C titled “Disclosure of Credit Risk-o-Meter for debt securities” into the NCS Master Circular.
- Inserts Clause 14 in Annexure-XXIB under Chapter XXI of the NCS Master Circular requiring Credit Risk-o-Meter details for securities offered on Online Bond Platforms.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and OBPPs must incorporate the Credit Risk-o-Meter in pictorial format displaying risk levels across all specified documents, advertisements, and digital platforms.
- Stock exchanges and depositories must put in place necessary systems, amend relevant bye-laws/rules, and disseminate circular provisions.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: October 07, 2026.
- Effective Date: Provisions come into force after 45 days from the date of issuance.

## Impact Assessment

Enhances investor protection and transparency by standardizing credit risk visualization for debt securities and online bond platforms.
