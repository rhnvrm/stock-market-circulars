## Summary

SEBI Recovery and Refund Department has issued a remittance order dated September 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9248 of 2026, directing all banks and mutual funds in India to remit outstanding dues amounting to Rs. 18,79,108.60 from the defaulter Punjiben Babubhai Rathod.

## Key Points

- Defaulter Name: Punjiben Babubhai Rathod
- PAN: CABPR8593L
- Outstanding Dues: Rs. 18,79,108.60
- Direction to all Banks and Mutual Funds in India holding accounts or folios of the defaulter to remit the balance.
- Remittance Mode: EFT/NEFT/RTGS to A/c No. SEBIRCIS9248 of Bank of India (IFSC: BKID00VAN04).

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued under section 28A and 28B of the SEBI Act, 1992 r/w Income Tax Act provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

All banks and mutual funds must verify if the specified defaulter holds any account or folio with them and remit available funds to SEBI while emailing the transaction details to designated officials.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Targeted recovery action directed at specific financial institutions for a single defaulter; no broad market impact.
