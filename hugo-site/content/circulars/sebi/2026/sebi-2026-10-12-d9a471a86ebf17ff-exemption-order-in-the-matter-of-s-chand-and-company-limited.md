## Summary

SEBI has issued an exemption order under Section 11(1) and 11(2)(h) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Regulation 11(5) of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 regarding the proposed direct acquisition of shares and voting rights in S Chand and Company Limited by DKJ Ownership Trust, an irrevocable discretionary private trust representing members of the promoter group.

## Key Points

- Target Company: S Chand and Company Limited, listed on NSE and BSE.
- Proposed Acquirer: DKJ Ownership Trust (settled April 08, 2025), represented by trustee Mrs. Neerja Jhunjhnuwala.
- Application sought exemption from open offer obligations under Regulation 3(1) and Regulation 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.
- The proposed transaction involves direct acquisition of shares and voting rights within promoter/family entities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Exemption granted from the strict compliance of open offer requirements under Regulation 3(1) and Regulation 4 of SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 subject to adherence to specified safeguards and conditions.

## Compliance Requirements

- The acquirer and target company must comply with all standard disclosures, reporting requirements, and conditions stipulated in the SEBI exemption order and relevant provisions of the SAST Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Application Date: June 17, 2026
- Order Reference: WTM/KCV/CFD/12/2026-27 (September 2026)

## Impact Assessment

- Minimal impact on public shareholders since the acquisition represents internal restructuring within the promoter group without altering the aggregate promoter holding or public float.
