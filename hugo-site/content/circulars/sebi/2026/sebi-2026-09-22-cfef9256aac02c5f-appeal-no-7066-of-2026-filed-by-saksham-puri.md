## Summary

This order relates to Appeal No. 7066 of 2026 filed by Shri Saksham Puri against the CPIO of SEBI under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought certified copies of evaluated descriptive answer scripts, question-wise marks, annotations, response sheets, and breakup of marks for the SEBI Grade A Officer recruitment examination (Phase II). SEBI's CPIO responded that evaluated descriptive papers are held solely by the conducting body, IBPS, pursuant to an MoU and are not shared with SEBI. The Appellate Authority reviewed the application and response.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought RTI information regarding his candidature and evaluation in SEBI Grade A Officer recruitment (Phase II Paper I and Paper II - Legal Stream).
- Respondent (CPIO, SEBI) stated that online examinations are conducted by IBPS (an autonomous body) under an MoU, and copies of question papers and evaluated descriptive papers are maintained by IBPS and not shared with SEBI.
- Relevant recruitment results, published marks, response sheets for the Legal Stream, and answer keys were previously provided or made accessible by the respondent.
