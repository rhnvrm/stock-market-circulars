## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a revocation order under sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B(1) of the SEBI Act, 1992 in the matter of Bharat Global Developers Limited (BGDL). The order addresses specific noticees named in the interim order dated December 23, 2024, and confirmatory order dated March 26, 2025, following a detailed investigation by SEBI into an alleged manipulative scheme.

## Key Points

- SEBI had previously passed an Interim Order on December 23, 2024, followed by a Confirmatory Order on March 26, 2025, against Bharat Global Developers Limited and various noticees for prima facie violations of the SEBI Act and PFUTP Regulations.
- A detailed investigation was subsequently conducted by SEBI's investigating team to examine the role of noticees and other entities involved in the alleged manipulative scheme.
- The investigating team concluded its detailed investigation and recommended specific actions regarding the directions made in the Interim Order.

## Regulatory Changes

- Modification and revocation of interim/confirmatory directions for specified noticees based on the findings of SEBI's detailed investigation.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees listed in the order must adhere to the updated directives issued by SEBI pursuant to the conclusion of the investigation.

## Important Dates

- Interim Order Date: December 23, 2024
- Confirmatory Order Date: March 26, 2025
- Revocation Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Clears or modifies regulatory restrictions for specific entities exonerated or subject to revised findings upon completion of SEBI's detailed investigation into Bharat Global Developers Limited.
