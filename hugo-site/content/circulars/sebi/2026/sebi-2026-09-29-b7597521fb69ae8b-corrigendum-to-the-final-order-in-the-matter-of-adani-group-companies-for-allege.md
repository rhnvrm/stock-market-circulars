## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a corrigendum dated September 29, 2026, to the final order dated September 28, 2026 (reference number WTM/KV/IVD-3/ID2/32734/2026-27) concerning Adani Enterprises Limited, Adani Transmission Limited, Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Limited, and Adani Power Limited.

## Key Points

- The corrigendum rectifies a clerical placeholder in the original final order dated September 28, 2026.
- At page 2, first line after the table, the placeholder words "[Insert Order date]" are officially replaced with "September 28, 2026".
- A copy of the corrigendum is required to be served upon all Noticees.

## Regulatory Changes

No substantive regulatory changes or new compliance burdens; this order solely provides a clerical correction to a prior SEBI adjudication order.

## Compliance Requirements

Noticees to note the corrected reference date in the final order records.

## Important Dates

- Corrigendum Date: September 29, 2026
- Original Order Date: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Negligible operational or market impact as this is purely a clerical correction to an existing order document.
