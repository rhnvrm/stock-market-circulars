## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Real Estate Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014, amended up to October 15, 2026, outline the statutory framework for the establishment, registration, and regulation of Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) in India. This document details preliminary guidelines, commencement terms, and precise definitions of key legal and financial terms.

## Key Points

- Establishes the formal title and commencement terms for REIT regulations.
- Defines critical statutory terms including Act, associate, Board, body corporate, bonus issue, certificate, and change in control.
- Aligns the definition of 'associate' with the Companies Act, 2013 and applicable accounting standards, incorporating control criteria and exclusions.
- Clarifies provisions for 'change in control' for both listed and unlisted corporate bodies.

## Regulatory Changes

- Updated definitions and amendments reflecting regulatory adjustments up to October 15, 2026.
- Refined scope of 'associate' and 'change in control' to align with modern corporate governance standards.

## Compliance Requirements

- REIT sponsors, managers, and trustees must ensure compliance with updated statutory definitions and registration mandates.
- Adherence to governance, control disclosure, and associate classification standards as stipulated under the Act.

## Important Dates

- Original Regulations enacted: 2014
- Latest Amendment incorporated: October 15, 2026
- Effective Date: As notified in the Official Gazette

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances regulatory clarity and legal certainty for REIT structures, investors, and market participants by harmonizing definitions with the Companies Act, 2013.
