## Summary

Kamal Kumar filed an RTI appeal (Appeal No. 7052 of 2026) before the SEBI Appellate Authority challenging the CPIO's response regarding queries on the SEBI circular dated July 23, 2026, concerning the transmission of securities of deceased shareholders where valuation exceeds 10 lakh rupees. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's stance that queries seeking clarifications, opinions, or interpretations do not qualify as 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought clarification regarding whether RTAs can transmit physical shares above 10 lakh rupees without a court document (like a succession certificate) if a legal heir certificate and NOCs are provided, based on the SEBI circular dated July 23, 2026.
- The CPIO stated that the queries sought clarification/opinion rather than specific 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, while pointing to SEBI Circular No. HO/38/13/11(14)2026-MIRSD-POD/1/17111/2026 dated July 23, 2026.
- The Appellate Authority confirmed that seeking advice or interpretation of circulars falls outside the scope of Section 2(f) of the RTI Act, citing precedents from the Central Information Commission (CIC).

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; the order reinforces existing principles regarding the definition of information under the RTI Act.
