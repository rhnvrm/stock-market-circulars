## Summary

An Adjudication Order was passed in respect of Madhukar Dubey and his proprietorship firm Magnum Industrial Corporation following an order by the Hon'ble Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) dated September 2, 2025. SAT remitted the matter back to the Adjudicating Officer to provide the Noticee an opportunity to contest the case, noting that his credentials were allegedly misused by his employer in connection with the IPO of Channel Nine Entertainment Ltd.

## Key Points

- An initial Adjudication Order dated February 28, 2022 imposed a joint and several monetary penalty of Rs. 8,00,000/- on thirteen Noticees under Section 15HA of the SEBI Act, 1992 for violations of SEBI Act and PFUTP Regulations regarding fraudulent funding of the IPO of Channel Nine Entertainment Ltd.
- Noticee Madhukar Dubey filed an appeal before SAT contending he worked as a driver under Mr. Vijay Jindal, who allegedly misused his KYC and credentials without his awareness.
- The Noticee noted a similar case regarding the IPO of HPC Biosciences Ltd. where SAT had remitted the matter to SEBI for fresh consideration.
- SAT vide order dated September 2, 2025, remitted the matter to the Adjudicating Officer to grant an opportunity of being heard.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is a quasi-judicial adjudication proceeding.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with directions for personal appearance and submission of defense before the Adjudicating Officer.

## Important Dates

- Original Order Date: February 28, 2022
- SAT Remand Order Date: September 2, 2025
- Appearance Date directed by SAT: September 29, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Individual quasi-judicial order with no direct systemic market impact.
