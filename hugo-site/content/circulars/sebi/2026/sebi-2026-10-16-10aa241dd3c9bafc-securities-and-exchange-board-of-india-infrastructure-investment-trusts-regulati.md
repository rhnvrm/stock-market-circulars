## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations, 2014 provide the comprehensive legal and regulatory framework for the establishment, registration, governance, and operation of Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) in India. This version incorporates amendments up to October 16, 2026, outlining definitions, trustee and sponsor responsibilities, investment conditions, and continuous disclosure norms.

## Key Points

- Governs the establishment and registration of Infrastructure Investment Trusts (InvITs) with SEBI.
- Defines key legal terms including 'Act', 'associate', 'Board', 'body corporate', and 'bonus issue'.
- Specifies detailed criteria for associates, controlling entities, and corporate structures related to InvIT participants.
- Establishes ongoing compliance, governance, and reporting obligations for trust managers, trustees, and sponsors.

## Regulatory Changes

- Incorporates amendments up to October 16, 2026, updating definitions such as 'associate' and 'change in control' to align with the Companies Act, 2013 and modern regulatory standards.

## Compliance Requirements

- InvIT sponsors, trustees, and investment managers must adhere to registration criteria, holding limits, disclosure standards, and governance norms specified under these regulations.
- Periodic filings and disclosures must be submitted to SEBI and unitholders as mandated.

## Important Dates

- Original Notification Date: 2014
- Last Amended Date: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulatory certainty and robust governance standards for infrastructure investments through InvITs, protecting unitholders while facilitating capital formation in the infrastructure sector.
