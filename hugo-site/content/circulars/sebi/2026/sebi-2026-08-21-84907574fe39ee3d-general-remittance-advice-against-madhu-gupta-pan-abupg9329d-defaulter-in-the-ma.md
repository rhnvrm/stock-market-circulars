## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Advice against Madhu Gupta (PAN: ABUPG9329D), designated as a defaulter, pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 9154 of 2026. The recovery proceedings relate to violations involving non-genuine reversal trades in Illiquid Stock Options on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

## Key Points

- **Defaulter Name:** Madhu Gupta
- **PAN:** ABUPG9329D
- **Recovery Certificate No.:** 9154 of 2026
- **Underlying Matter:** Dealing in Illiquid Stock Options on BSE resulting in artificial volume creation and PFUTP regulations violations.
- **Issuing Authority:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes; this is an enforcement and recovery action against a specific entity.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and custodians holding accounts or assets of the named defaulter are required to comply with remittance and asset-freezing directives as specified in the recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Issuance:** August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's ongoing enforcement mechanisms and strict recovery processes against entities penalized for fraudulent trading practices in the illiquid stock options segment.
