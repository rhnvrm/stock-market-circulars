## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7071 of 2026 filed by Dhanashree Deshpande under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought detailed information regarding a SEBI employee's investigation history, including case counts, case names, final order dates, ongoing investigations, and specific status on Brightcom Group Limited. SEBI's CPIO and Appellate Authority upheld that such details constitute personal information and their disclosure is exempt under Sections 8(1)(j) and 8(1)(g) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant requested information on investigations handled by a specific SEBI official (Mr. Krishna R.M.) formerly in CFID and currently in AFD.
- Information sought included total cases investigated, case names, final order dates, pending cases, and status regarding Brightcom Group Limited.
- Respondent and Appellate Authority denied the request citing exemption under Section 8(1)(j) (personal information / invasion of privacy) and Section 8(1)(g) (endangering life or physical safety) of the RTI Act.
- Precedents cited include H. E. Rajashekarappa vs. State Public Information Officer and Prerit Misra vs. CPIO, SEBI regarding personal information of public officials and RTI scope.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; reinforces existing RTI exemptions regarding personal information of public officials and ongoing investigations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Public authorities are not obligated to disclose personal information of officials or sensitive investigation details under the RTI Act if it violates privacy or safety exemptions.
