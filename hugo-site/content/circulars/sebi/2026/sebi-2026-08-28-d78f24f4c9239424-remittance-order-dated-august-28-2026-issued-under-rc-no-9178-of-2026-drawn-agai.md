## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated August 28, 2026 under Recovery Certificate No. 9178 of 2026 drawn against Madhu Kumari Bairwa in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9178 of 2026
- Noticee / Defaulter: Madhu Kumari Bairwa
- Scrip: DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited)
- Issuing Authority: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action via recovery proceedings and remittance orders under SEBI's statutory powers for recovery of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

The noticee is required to comply with the remittance order and clear the dues as specified in the recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Individual enforcement action with no direct material operational impact on the listed company itself, but part of regulatory surveillance and recovery mechanisms.
