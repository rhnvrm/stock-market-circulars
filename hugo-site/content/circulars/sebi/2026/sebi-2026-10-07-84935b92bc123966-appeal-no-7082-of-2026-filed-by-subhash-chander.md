## Summary

Subhash Chander filed an RTI application seeking detailed information regarding his PACL certificate (No. U038100xxx), specifically about land allotted against the certificate, registration details, claim status, deposit amounts, and refund rules. SEBI CPIO responded stating that the requested information is not available with SEBI and advised the appellant to approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee which handles all refund matters. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response citing precedent that information not in possession cannot be furnished.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought details of land allotted against PACL Certificate No. U038100xxx and the status of refund claims.
- SEBI CPIO stated that the requested information is not available with SEBI.
- All communications and disposal regarding PACL refund status are managed by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's reply, noting that SEBI cannot furnish information it does not possess.
