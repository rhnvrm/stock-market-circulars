## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued Prohibitory Order No. 1555 of 2026 in the matter of HBN Dairies & Allied Limited under Recovery Certificate No. 1216 of 2017. This enforcement action is part of ongoing recovery proceedings to realize dues and protect investors affected by illegal collective investment schemes operated by the company and its promoters/directors.

## Key Points

- **Order Details**: Prohibitory Order No. 1555 of 2026 issued by SEBI.
- **Entity Involved**: HBN Dairies & Allied Limited along with its promoters and directors (including Harmender Singh Sran, Amandeep Singh Sran, Manjeet Kaur Sran, and Jasbeer Kaur).
- **Recovery Certificate**: Issued under Recovery Certificate No. 1216 of 2017.
- **Date of Order**: October 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures implemented pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992, to execute recovery certificates and attach assets for unpaid investor refunds and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and asset holders are mandated to prohibit debits, transfers, or disposals of attached bank accounts, lockers, and properties belonging to the defaulters to ensure funds are available for investor restitution.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Order**: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and asset recovery actions against entities defaulting on Supreme Court-mandated or SEBI-ordered refunds related to illegal collective investment schemes.
