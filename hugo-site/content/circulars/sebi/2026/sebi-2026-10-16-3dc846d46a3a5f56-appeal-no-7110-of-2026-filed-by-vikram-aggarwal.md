## Summary

Appellant Vikram Aggarwal filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against SEBI's CPIO response regarding information sought on SCORES complaint reference SEBIE MH25 MUMB 018205_1 against The Indian Hotels Company Limited.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought extensive details, audit history, action history notes, review requests, and ATR attachments relating to a SCORES complaint filed on May 12, 2025 against The Indian Hotels Company Limited.
- The CPIO responded on September 23, 2026, enclosing the action history and Action Taken Report (ATR) provided by the company.
- The Appellate Authority perused the application, CPIO response, and appeal to dispose of the matter based on available records.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes or new policy frameworks introduced; this is a quasi-judicial RTI appellate order.

## Compliance Requirements

No new compliance requirements imposed on listed entities.

No direct market or operational impact; pertains solely to individual RTI disclosures regarding grievance redressal tracking.
