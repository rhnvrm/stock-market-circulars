## Summary

SEBI has issued a recovery notice of demand under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with the Income-tax Act, 1961, against Rajeev Chandak HUF for an amount of Rs. 5,31,000. The dues comprise a penalty imposed by the AO for dealings in illiquid stock options on BSE, along with interest and recovery costs.

## Key Points

- **Notice Details:** Certificate No. 9287 of 2026, File No. HO/50/11/11(226)2026-RRD-DIV2.
- **Entity:** Rajeev Chandak HUF (PAN: AAMHR6180G).
- **Total Dues:** Rs. 5,31,000 (includes Rs. 5,00,000 penalty, Rs. 30,000 interest from March 2026 to August 2026, and Rs. 1,000 recovery cost).
- **Underlying Matter:** Penalty imposed vide Order No. Order/JS/RJ/2025-26/32303 dated 27.03.2026 regarding dealings in illiquid Stock Options on BSE.

## Compliance Requirements

- The notice recipient is directed to pay the total amount within 15 days of receipt of the notice via direct credit through EFT/NEFT/RTGS to ICICI Bank account or through the online payment facility on the SEBI website.
- Non-payment may lead to recovery modes including attachment and sale of movable/immovable property, bank account attachment, or arrest and detention.
- Properties or monies transferred to spouse, minor child, son's wife, or son's minor child on or after 27.03.2026 without adequate consideration shall be deemed as the defaulter's property for recovery.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** 19.08.2026
- **Payment Window:** Within 15 days of receipt of the notice
