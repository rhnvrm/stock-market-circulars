## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Advice dated October 7, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9247 of 2026 drawn against Sulabh Dikshit (PAN: ANEPD9669B). This enforcement action relates to recovery of dues and penalties arising from unauthorized trading-based stock recommendations disseminated through YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9247 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Sulabh Dikshit (PAN: ANEPD9669B).
- Underlying Matter: Illegal stock recommendations and pump-and-dump operations using social media (YouTube) in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Action Type: General Remittance Advice issued by SEBI Recovery Officer for realization of dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings executed pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 and recovery regulations for collection of unpaid penalties and disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are instructed to facilitate remittance of attached funds and ensure compliance with recovery orders issued by SEBI's Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Date of General Remittance Advice: October 7, 2026
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9247 of 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and recovery tracking by SEBI against entities involved in social media-based stock manipulation schemes.
