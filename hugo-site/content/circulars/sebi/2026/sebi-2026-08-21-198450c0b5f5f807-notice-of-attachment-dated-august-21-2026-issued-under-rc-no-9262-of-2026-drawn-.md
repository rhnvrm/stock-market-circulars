SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 21, 2026 under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 9262 of 2026 against Aahuti Rasik Mistry in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited, for the recovery of dues.

- Notice issued under RC No. 9262 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Entity: Aahuti Rasik Mistry.
- Associated Company: Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
- Action involves attachment of properties/assets as per statutory recovery powers.

None.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 21, 2026

Individual-specific recovery action with negligible direct operational impact on the broader market or the company's normal business operations.
