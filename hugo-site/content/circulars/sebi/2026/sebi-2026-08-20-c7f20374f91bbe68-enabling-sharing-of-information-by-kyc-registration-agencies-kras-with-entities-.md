## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular specifying the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) under Regulation 16A(1) of the SEBI KRA Regulations, 2011. This enables interoperability and sharing of information between SEBI-registered KYC Registration Agencies (KRAs) and entities regulated by IFSCA for undertaking client KYC.

## Key Points

- IFSCA is specified as an authority under Regulation 16A(1) of SEBI KRA Regulations, 2011.
- Allows IFSCA-regulated entities to access the system of KRAs to undertake KYC of their clients engaging them for financial services.
- Entities accessing the KRA system must comply with SEBI's Master Circular on KYC norms dated October 12, 2023.
- For clients registered as Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPI), entities must follow data security guidelines specified in SEBI's Master Circular dated May 30, 2024.

## Regulatory Changes

- Inclusion of IFSCA-regulated entities under the scope of KRA system access for KYC sharing pursuant to Regulation 16A of SEBI KRA Regulations, 2011.

## Compliance Requirements

- Entities accessing KRA systems must adhere to SEBI's Master Circular on KYC norms.
- FPI-related client onboardings must comply with specific data security guidelines for FPIs, DDPs, and EFIs.

## Important Dates

- Effective date: Immediate effect (August 20, 2026).

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the KYC process across financial sector regulators by enabling cross-regulatory interoperability between SEBI KRAs and IFSCA-regulated entities.
