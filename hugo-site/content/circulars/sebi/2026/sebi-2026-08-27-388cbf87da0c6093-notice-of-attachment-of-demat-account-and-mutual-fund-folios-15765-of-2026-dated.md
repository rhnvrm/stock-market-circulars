## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Attachment of Demat Account and Mutual Fund Folio(s) dated August 27, 2026, against Daksh Share Brokers Private Limited (PAN: AACCD9973A) [Defaulter] under Recovery Certificate No. 8880 of 2025. The action arises from matters related to dealing in Illiquid Stocks Options at BSE.

## Key Points

- Defaulter: Daksh Share Brokers Private Limited
- PAN: AACCD9973A
- Regulatory Body: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)
- Action: Attachment of Demat Account and Mutual Fund Folio(s)
- Reference: Recovery Certificate No. 8880 of 2025
- Context: Dealing in Illiquid Stocks Options at BSE
- Date of Notice: August 27, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Initiation of formal attachment proceedings under the SEBI Act for recovery of outstanding dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant depositories, banks, and mutual fund institutions are required to freeze the demat accounts and mutual fund folios of the named defaulter to prevent any transfer or withdrawal of assets.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures enforcement of regulatory penalties and restricts the defaulter from liquidating assets until dues are cleared.
