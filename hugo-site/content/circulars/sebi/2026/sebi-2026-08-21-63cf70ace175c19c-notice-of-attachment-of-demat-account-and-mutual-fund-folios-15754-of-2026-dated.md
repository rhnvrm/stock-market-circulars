## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice for the attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s) (Notice No. 15754 of 2026 dated August 21, 2026) against Paras Shah (PAN: DOKPS0203A) under Recovery Certificate No. 9253 of 2026 in the matter of trading-based stock recommendations using YouTube in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Defaulter: Paras Shah (PAN: DOKPS0203A)
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9253 of 2026
- Notice No.: 15754 of 2026
- Matter: Trading based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Action: Attachment of demat account and mutual fund folio(s)

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions taken pursuant to SEBI Act and Recovery Certificate provisions for non-payment of dues/penalties arising from illegal stock recommendations.

## Compliance Requirements

Depository participants and mutual fund institutions are required to attach the specified demat accounts and mutual fund folios and prevent any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Freezing of assets and accounts of the defaulter to recover dues related to illegal stock pumping schemes.
