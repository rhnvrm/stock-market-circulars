## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice under Recovery Certificate No. 9259 of 2026 against Rakesh Kumar Gupta (PAN: AAEPG2752R) in the matter of Sadhna Broadcast Limited. This document provides instructions for the remittance of recovered amounts and asset attachments pursuant to recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Advice issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9259 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Rakesh Kumar Gupta (PAN: AAEPG2752R).
- Matter: Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer / Competent Authority of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and remittance of recovered funds.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, garnishees, and designated authorities are required to remit funds and comply with directions relating to the attachment and recovery of dues as specified in the remittance advice.

## Important Dates

- Date / Period: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights ongoing regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against entities involved in market violations and defaults concerning Sadhna Broadcast Limited.
