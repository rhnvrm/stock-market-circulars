## Summary

SEBI issued a Notice of Attachment (Bank) under Notice No. 15675 dated August 12, 2026, in pursuance of Recovery Certificate No. 9231 of 2026 against S R Traders (Proprietor Maheshkumar Jayantilal Chauhan).

## Key Points

- Notice Number: 15675
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9231 of 2026
- Defaulter: S R Traders (Prop. Maheshkumar Jayantilal Chauhan) (PAN/AHKPC1715D)
- Date: August 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

None

## Compliance Requirements

All banks and financial institutions are required to attach the accounts/holdings of the named defaulter as per SEBI's recovery directives.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Direct recovery and asset freezing measures against the specified defaulter.
