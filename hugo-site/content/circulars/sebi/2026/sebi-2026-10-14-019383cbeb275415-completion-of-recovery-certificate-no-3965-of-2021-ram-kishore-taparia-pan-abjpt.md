## Summary

SEBI has issued a completion notice and release order regarding Recovery Certificate No. 3965 of 2021 drawn against Ram Kishore Taparia (PAN: ABJPT1453C) in the matter of Cosboard Industries Ltd. This marks the successful conclusion or settlement of recovery proceedings.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate Number: 3965 of 2021.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Ram Kishore Taparia (PAN – ABJPT1453C).
- Company: Cosboard Industries Ltd.
- Action: Completion of recovery certificate and issuance of release order by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings concluded pursuant to dues recovery under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Lifting of freezing orders or attachment notices on assets/accounts of the noticee as applicable upon completion of recovery.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issuance: October 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Resolves outstanding recovery proceedings against the individual in relation to Cosboard Industries Ltd.
