## Summary

SEBI has announced the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 6665 of 2023 dated June 13, 2023, issued to Shubhang Exports Ltd. (Defaulter, PAN: AAJCS1526R) in the matter of Sulabh Engineers & Services Limited. The recovery proceedings have been successfully concluded upon realization of the required dues.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate Number: 6665 of 2023 (dated June 13, 2023).
- Defaulter: Shubhang Exports Ltd. (PAN: AAJCS1526R).
- Matter: Sulabh Engineers & Services Limited (share price manipulation case).
- Status: Completed/Closed following realization of dues by SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and conclusion of recovery proceedings pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of monetary penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

No further compliance required for this specific closed recovery certificate; serves as formal notification of recovery completion.

## Important Dates

- Original Certificate Date: June 13, 2023
- Completion / Notice Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates effective enforcement and closure of recovery actions regarding market manipulation in Sulabh Engineers & Services Limited.
