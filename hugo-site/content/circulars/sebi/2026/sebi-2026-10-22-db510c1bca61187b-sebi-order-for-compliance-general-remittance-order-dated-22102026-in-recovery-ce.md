## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a General Remittance Order dated October 22, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 3883 of 2021. The order is issued against Arrow Asia Stock Broking Limited in the matter of Kausambi Vanijya Ltd. (now known as Golden Bull Research and Growth Ltd) for realization of dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 3883 of 2021.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Arrow Asia Stock Broking Limited.
- Underlying Matter: Kausambi Vanijya Ltd. (now known as Golden Bull Research and Growth Ltd).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures undertaken pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992, directing garnishees or financial institutions to remit funds towards satisfaction of recovery certificates.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated intermediaries must comply with the general remittance order by freezing/remitting funds and assets held on behalf of the defaulter as mandated by the Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 22, 2026
- Recovery Certificate No.: 3883 of 2021

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory compliance, accountability, and stringent recovery enforcement mechanisms against brokers and entities defaulting on SEBI dues.
