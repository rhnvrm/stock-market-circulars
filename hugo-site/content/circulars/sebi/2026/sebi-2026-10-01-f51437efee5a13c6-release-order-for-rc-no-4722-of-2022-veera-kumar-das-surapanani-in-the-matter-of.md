## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order for Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 4722 of 2022 drawn against Veera Kumar Das Surapanani in the matter of Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd. This indicates the satisfaction or resolution of the recovery proceedings and the lifting of related attachments.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued for RC No. 4722 of 2022.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Veera Kumar Das Surapanani.
- Matter: Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd.
- Action: Lifting of attachment and completion of recovery proceedings by SEBI Recovery Officer.

## Regulatory Changes

Conclusion of recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 following the satisfaction of dues or settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and authorities holding attached accounts or assets of the noticee are instructed to release the same as per the order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Marks the successful closure of recovery actions against the specified individual in relation to Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd.
