## Summary

SEBI issued an order under Section 15-I(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 concerning suspected insider trading activity in the scrip of Indian Oil Corporation Ltd. (IOCL) around the financial results and interim dividend announcement for the quarter ended September 2023. The investigation focused on a designated employee of IOCL and connected in-laws who traded during the investigation period.

## Key Points

- Investigation into trading in the scrip of Indian Oil Corporation Ltd. (IOCL) from October 3, 2023 to November 15, 2023.
- Noticee No. 1 (Arun Kumar Somani) identified as a designated employee involved in the finalization of Q2 2023-24 financial results for IOCL.
- Noticee No. 2 (Ramesh Chand Gupta) and Noticee No. 3 (Tara Devi Gupta) are in-laws of Noticee No. 1 and executed trades during the investigation period.
- Examination of potential violations of the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015.

## Compliance Requirements

- Strict adherence to trading restrictions and designated person obligations during unpublished price sensitive information (UPSI) periods.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: October 3, 2023 to November 15, 2023.
- Date of Financial Results / Dividend Announcement: October 31, 2023.

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights regulatory vigilance on insider trading by designated persons and connected entities around corporate earnings announcements.
