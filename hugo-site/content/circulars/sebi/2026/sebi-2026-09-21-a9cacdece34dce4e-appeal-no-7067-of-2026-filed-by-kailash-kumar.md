## Summary

Appellant Kailash Kumar filed an RTI application under the RTI Act, 2005, seeking information regarding the inclusion of his land/property in the PACL Ltd. property list/portal and related verification records. The respondent CPIO stated that the information sought was not available with SEBI and noted that communications regarding refund status are handled separately.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought 10 specific queries regarding land survey numbers and inclusion in PACL Ltd. property lists.
- The CPIO responded stating the information is not available with SEBI.
- The Appellate Authority perused the appeal and noted that refund status communication procedures apply.
