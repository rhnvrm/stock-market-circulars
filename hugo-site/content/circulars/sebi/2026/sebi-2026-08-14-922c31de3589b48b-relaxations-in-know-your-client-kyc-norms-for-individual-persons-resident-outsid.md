## Summary

SEBI has issued a consultation paper proposing relaxations in Know Your Client (KYC) norms for individual Persons Resident Outside India (PROI), including Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), Overseas Citizens of India (OCIs), and foreign nationals, aiming to simplify digital onboarding and cross-intermediary portfolio portability.

## Key Points

- Elimination of physical presence requirements in India for PROI clients from FATF-compliant countries via digital KYC.
- Intermediaries permitted to rely on KYC undertaken by entities regulated by other Financial Sector Regulators.
- Mandatory collection of email IDs from PROI clients to streamline communications.
- Expanded list of authorised document-certifying officials to include overseas bank officials having relationships with Indian banks.
- Portability of KYC records across intermediaries in the securities market.
- Stipulation of safeguards for Video In Person Verification (VIPV), such as prevention of spoofing IPs, concurrent audits, and cybersecurity compliance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amendments proposed to streamline KYC requirements and remove physical attestation/presence bottlenecks for overseas individual investors.
- Recognition of KYC executed through other financial sector regulators.

## Compliance Requirements

- Intermediaries onboarding PROI clients must collect email IDs and adhere to specified VIPV security and audit safeguards.
- Public and stakeholders are invited to submit comments on the consultation paper by September 04, 2026.

## Important Dates

- **Consultation Release Date:** August 14, 2026
- **Last Date for Public Comments:** September 04, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Expected to significantly reduce onboarding friction and turnaround times for NRIs, OCIs, and foreign nationals looking to invest in Indian capital markets.
