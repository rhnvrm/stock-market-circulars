## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular removing the requirement for Foreign Portfolio Investors (FPIs) investing exclusively in Government Securities to furnish investor group details, following a similar withdrawal of concentration limits by the Reserve Bank of India.

## Key Points

- Previous regulations required FPIs investing in Government Securities under the Fully Accessible Route to be exempt from furnishing investor group details.
- The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) withdrew the concentration limit requirement for FPIs investing in Government Securities through the General Route on June 05, 2026.
- Consequently, SEBI has updated its Master Circular to completely remove the requirement of identifying investor groups for FPIs investing only in Government Securities.

## Regulatory Changes

- Sub-paragraph under Para 1 of Part A of the Master Circular for FPIs, DDPs, and Eligible Foreign Investors is modified: "FPIs investing only in Government Securities shall not be required to furnish investor group details."

## Compliance Requirements

- Depositories, Custodians, and Designated Depository Participants (DDPs) must update their systems to implement this exemption immediately.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: September 07, 2026
- Effective Date: With immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

- Reduces compliance burden and paperwork for foreign investors focusing strictly on Indian Government Securities.
- Streamlines onboarding and operational processes across custodians and depositories.
