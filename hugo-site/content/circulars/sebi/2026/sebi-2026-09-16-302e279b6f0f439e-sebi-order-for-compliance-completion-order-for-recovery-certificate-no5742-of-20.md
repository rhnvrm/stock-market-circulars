## Summary

SEBI has issued a completion order for Recovery Certificate No. 5742 of 2022 against Payal Jayeshbhai Madiyar in the matter of Excel Castronics Limited. The order signifies the completion or resolution of the recovery proceedings initiated under the certificate.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 5742 of 2022
- Noticee / Defaulter: Payal Jayeshbhai Madiyar
- Matter: Excel Castronics Limited
- Action: Completion / satisfaction / closure of recovery proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Completion of recovery certificate indicates fulfillment or resolution of specified dues under the recovery proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates closure of specific enforcement and recovery actions, updating status of compliance for the concerned individual and scrip.
