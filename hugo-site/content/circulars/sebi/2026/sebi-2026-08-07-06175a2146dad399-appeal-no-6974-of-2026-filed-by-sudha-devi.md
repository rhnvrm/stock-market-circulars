## Summary

The Appellate Authority under the Right to Information Act, 2005, heard an appeal filed by Sudha Devi (Appeal No. 6974 of 2026) against the response of the Central Public Information Officer (CPIO), SEBI. The appellant sought records and details of land allotment asserted against her PACL certificate (No. U06352xxx), which had resulted in her refund claim status being kept unprocessed. The CPIO responded that SEBI does not possess this information and advised approaching the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee. The Appellate Authority upheld this decision, stating that SEBI cannot produce information it does not possess.

## Key Points

- **Appellant's Queries:** Requested certified copies of PACL land allotment records, plot/survey numbers, allotment/possession letters, and criteria for rejecting or withholding her refund application.
- **CPIO Response:** Confirmed the requested information is not available with SEBI and directed the appellant to the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee, which manages PACL refund status communications.
- **Precedent Cited:** The Appellate Authority cited the Central Information Commission (CIC) ruling in *Sh. Pattipati Rama Murthy vs. CPIO, SEBI*, confirming that CPIOs cannot invent or provide non-existent information.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes are introduced by this order.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no compliance requirements for standard SEBI-registered intermediaries. Investors seeking PACL refund updates or land allotment clarifications are advised to consult the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Important Dates

- **July 11, 2026:** Receipt of the appellant's RTI application by SEBI.
- **July 15, 2026:** CPIO's response and subsequent filing of the first appeal.
- **August 12, 2026:** Date of the Appellate Authority's decision.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** None. This order addresses an individual's specific RTI query.
- **Investor Impact:** Reinforces that SEBI does not maintain individual PACL land allotment records, which are managed by the specialized Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
