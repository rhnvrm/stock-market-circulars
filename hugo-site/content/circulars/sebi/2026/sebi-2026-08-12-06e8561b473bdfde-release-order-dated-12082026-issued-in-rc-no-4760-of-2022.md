## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order (Release Order No.: RRD/RD-3/1411/4760/2026) to lift the attachment on bank accounts, lockers, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios belonging to Mr. Rajkumar Yadav. This action follows a Securities Appellate Tribunal (SAT) ruling on August 10, 2026, which quashed the SEBI Whole-Time Member (WTM) order dated February 15, 2018, specifically in relation to Mr. Yadav, and remitted the matter back to SEBI for a fresh decision. Recovery proceedings and attachment notices against the other eight defaulters associated with Recovery Certificate No. 4760 of 2022 remain active and unchanged.

## Key Points

- **Release of Attached Assets:** Banks, mutual funds, and depositories are instructed to immediately release all attached bank accounts, lockers, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios of Mr. Rajkumar Yadav (PAN: AHWPY1598A).
- **SAT Order Influence:** Mr. Rajkumar Yadav successfully appealed the SEBI WTM order dated February 15, 2018. The SAT quashed the order qua the appellant on August 10, 2026, leading to the cancellation of recovery proceedings against him.
- **Remaining Defaulters Unaffected:** The attachment orders issued on May 12, 2026, against the remaining eight defaulters (including Real Sunshine Corp Limited, Abdul Wahid, Pradeep Ojha, Anil Kumar, Bipin Singh Yadav, Atendra Kumar, Siba Prosad Ghosh, and Jitendra Singh Yadav) to recover a total sum of Rs. 25,19,98,029/- along with interest and costs remain in full force.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no broader regulatory changes introduced by this document, as it is a case-specific execution of a SAT judicial directive.

## Compliance Requirements

- **For Banks, Mutual Funds, and Depositories (NSDL/CDSL):**
  - Immediately release all lockers, bank accounts, demat accounts, and mutual fund folios belonging to Mr. Rajkumar Yadav.
  - Ensure that the attachment notices dated May 12, 2026, against the other eight defaulters listed under Recovery Certificate No. 4760 of 2022 are maintained without interruption.

## Important Dates

- **February 15, 2018:** Date of the SEBI WTM order (now quashed qua Rajkumar Yadav).
- **May 13, 2022:** Recovery Certificate No. 4760 of 2022 drawn up.
- **May 12, 2026:** Issuance of the original Notices of Attachment.
- **August 10, 2026:** SAT order quashing the WTM order against Rajkumar Yadav.
- **August 12, 2026:** Date of the SEBI Release Order (effective immediately).

## Impact Assessment

- **Individual Impact:** Restoration of financial assets and bank/demat operations for Mr. Rajkumar Yadav.
- **Institutional Impact:** Operations and compliance teams at banks, mutual funds, and depositories in India must act quickly to lift the attachment on the specified individual's folios/accounts while ensuring other attachments under the same certificate are kept active.
- **Market Impact:** No general market impact.
