## Summary

An appeal was filed by Sanamdeep Singh Bakshi under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO's response regarding query nos. 8 to 12 concerning GT Jewellers and Swarna Sangraha Yojana. The Appellate Authority disposed of the appeal, directing reconsideration of query no. 9 while upholding the responses to other queries.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought information regarding GT Jewellers (A unit of GreenTouch Projects Ltd) and Swarna Sangraha Yojana.
- Respondent stated that Recovery Cell - ERO had not received any recovery proposal with respect to the entity/scheme.
- Query no. 9 regarding orders, directions, notices, and investigation reports was found to be unaddressed by the respondent and remanded for reconsideration.
- Query no. 10 regarding confirmation of inclusion in proceedings was held to be seeking clarification/confirmation rather than 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
