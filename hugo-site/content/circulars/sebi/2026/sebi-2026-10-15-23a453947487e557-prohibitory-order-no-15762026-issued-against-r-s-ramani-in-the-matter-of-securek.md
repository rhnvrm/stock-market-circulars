## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued Prohibitory Order No. 1576/2026 on October 15, 2026, against R S Ramani (promoter and former CFO of Securekloud Technologies Ltd) under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 8709 of 2025. This action is part of ongoing recovery proceedings to realize unpaid monetary penalties imposed for financial irregularities and market violations.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Order Number: 1576/2026.
- Recovery Certificate: RC No. 8709 of 2025.
- Defaulter / Noticee: R S Ramani (Promoter/Former CFO, Securekloud Technologies Ltd).
- Action: Attachment of movable and immovable properties and prohibition on disposing, transferring, or alienating assets.
- Underlying Matter: Non-payment of dues and penalties arising from prior SEBI adjudication orders regarding financial misstatements and insider trading.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992, for the recovery of unpaid penalties and regulatory dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and third parties are mandated to freeze accounts and restrict transfers of assets belonging to the noticee to ensure realization of dues.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: October 15, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Reference: RC No. 8709 of 2025

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict enforcement by SEBI in pursuing recovery of penalties from defaulting individuals associated with listed companies, heightening compliance scrutiny on corporate governance and financial integrity.
