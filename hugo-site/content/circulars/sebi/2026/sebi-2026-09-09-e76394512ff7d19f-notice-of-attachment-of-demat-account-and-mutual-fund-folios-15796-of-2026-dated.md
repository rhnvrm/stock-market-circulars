## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated September 09, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9285 of 2026 against VIKRAM ANAND HUF (PAN: AAJHV0926R). This action relates to the recovery of dues in the matter of dealings in Illiquid Stock Options on BSE.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9285 of 2026.
- Defaulter: VIKRAM ANAND HUF (PAN: AAJHV0926R).
- Matter: Dealings in Illiquid Stock Options on BSE.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties related to illiquid stock options misuse.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, depositories, and mutual fund houses are required to attach and freeze demat accounts, bank accounts, and mutual fund folios as specified in the notice to prevent any transfer or withdrawal of funds.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: September 09, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing regulatory enforcement against entities involved in fraudulent or manipulative trading via illiquid stock options.
