## Summary

SEBI has issued a corrigendum to the earlier Release Order related to Recovery Certificate No. 3031 of 2020. The order corrects minor technical or clerical particulars regarding the release of attachment proceedings for M/s Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited and its associated noticees/directors.

## Key Points

- Corrigendum issued for Recovery Certificate No. 3031 of 2020.
- Involves M/s Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited and associated individuals including Pushpanjali Singh Baghel, Ranvajay Pratap Singh, Dheerendra Krishan Pratap, Mragendra Singh Baghel, Pushpendra Singh Baghel, and Shahendra Singh Baghel.
- Modifies and rectifies clerical errors from the original release order dated August 14, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an administrative enforcement order correcting a prior recovery/release record.

## Compliance Requirements

No new compliance actions are mandated for general market participants. Relevant financial institutions and authorities are notified of the corrected release details.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 8, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No direct impact on general market operations or listed securities as this pertains strictly to specific recovery proceedings against a non-listed entity.
