## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice under Recovery Certificate No. 8876 of 2025 against Heena Khatoon, Proprietor of Heena Enterprises (PAN: FGXPK5721G), as part of recovery proceedings and investigations into the trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of Continental Seeds and Chemicals Limited.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Advice issued under Recovery Certificate No. 8876 of 2025.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Heena Khatoon, Proprietor of Heena Enterprises (PAN: FGXPK5721G).
- Matter: Investigation in the trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of Continental Seeds and Chemicals Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of dues, penalties, and disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are required to remit amounts or maintain freezes on accounts/assets as directed under the recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Recovery Certificate No.: 8876 of 2025
- Date: 2025-07-25

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing rigorous enforcement by SEBI to recover dues and enforce penalties related to market manipulation and unauthorized trading activities in Continental Seeds and Chemicals Limited.
