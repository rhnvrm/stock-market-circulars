## Summary

SEBI issued an adjudication order against Fatmabibi Yusufbhai Rangwala for alleged execution of non-genuine reversal trades in the stock options segment of BSE, creating artificial volumes during the period from April 1, 2014 to September 30, 2015.

## Key Points

- SEBI observed large-scale reversal of trades in stock options at BSE leading to artificial volumes.
- Noticee allegedly executed 2 non-genuine trades in 1 stock options contract resulting in an artificial volume of 1,40,000 units.
- Proceedings initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Rule 5 of Adjudication Rules for alleged violation of PFUTP Regulations.
