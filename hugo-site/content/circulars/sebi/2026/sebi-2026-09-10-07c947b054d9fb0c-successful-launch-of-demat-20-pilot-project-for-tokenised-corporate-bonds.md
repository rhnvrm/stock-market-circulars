## Summary

SEBI and the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) have successfully launched "Demat 2.0", a pilot project for the tokenisation of corporate bonds. Announced at the Global Fintech Fest in Mumbai, Demat 2.0 introduces a new market infrastructure where corporate bonds are created as digital tokens on a distributed ledger maintained by depositories. Connected to RBI's wholesale CBDC (e₹) through the Unified Market Interface (UMI), the system enables instantaneous atomic settlement and automated asset servicing via smart contracts.

## Key Points

- **Technology Foundation:** Utilises Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT) for digital token creation, owned and maintained by statutory depositories.
- **Atomic Settlement:** Connected to RBI's wholesale CBDC (e₹) via UMI, ensuring simultaneous, instantaneous exchange of bonds and funds.
- **Automated Servicing:** Interest payments and redemption are handled automatically through smart contracts, depositing e₹ directly into bondholders' CBDC wallets on the due date.
- **Initial Issuances:** Three companies have raised an aggregate of ₹1,025 crore: REC Limited (₹500 crore on Sept 7, 2026), L&T Limited (₹500 crore on Sept 9, 2026), and IIFL (₹25 crore on Sept 9, 2026).
- **Global Uniqueness:** India is the first country to issue corporate bonds natively on a distributed ledger with ownership records on statutory depositories and funds settled in CBDC within a regulated framework.

## Regulatory Changes

- Establishment of a new regulatory and market infrastructure framework for native tokenised corporate bond issuances, trading, and settlement.

## Compliance Requirements

- Issuers and market participants participating in the pilot must integrate with the distributed ledger and UMI infrastructure established by depositories and RBI.

## Important Dates

- **Launch Date:** September 2026
- **REC Limited Issuance:** September 7, 2026
- **L&T Limited & IIFL Issuances:** September 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Efficiency:** Eliminates settlement risk entirely via atomic settlement, reduces issuance and servicing costs, and drastically cuts down manual file sharing and reconciliation.
- **Liquidity:** Funds are received by issuers and secondary market investors immediately (same day), freeing up capital deployment.
