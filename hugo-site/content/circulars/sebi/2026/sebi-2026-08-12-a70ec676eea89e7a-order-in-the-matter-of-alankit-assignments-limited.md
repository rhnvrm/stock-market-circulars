## Summary

SEBI has issued an order in the matter of Alankit Assignments Limited (the "Noticee"), a registered Registrar to an Issue and Share Transfer Agent (RTA) categorized as a Qualified RTA (QRTA). The proceedings arose from an inspection of the Noticee for the period of April 1, 2021, to September 19, 2022, which revealed multiple regulatory violations. Based on the inspection findings and subsequent Enquiry Report, the Designated Authority alleged that the Noticee employed associated persons without valid NISM certifications, failed to execute required tripartite agreements with client companies, and did not maintain signature cards.

## Key Points

- **NISM Certification Lapses**: A total of 39 associated persons engaged by the Noticee did not have mandatory NISM certification, and 10 associated persons did not possess valid NISM certification during the inspection period.
- **Missing Tripartite Agreements**: The Noticee failed to execute tripartite agreements with 7 client companies as required under the RTA Regulations and LODR Regulations.
- **Missing Signature Cards**: The Noticee failed to maintain signature cards for 4 client companies, in violation of Schedule I of the RTA Regulations.
- **Regulatory Violations**: The actions violated Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Regulation 27 of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008, the CAPSEM Regulations, 2007, and the SEBI (Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents) Regulations, 1993.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory rules were created; rather, the order enforces existing compliance mandates under:
- SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 (Regulation 27)
- SEBI (Certification of Associated Persons in the Securities Market) Regulations, 2007 (CAPSEM Regulations)
- SEBI (Registrars to an Issue and Share Transfer Agents) Regulations, 1993
- SEBI Circular CIR/MIRSD/7/2012 dated July 05, 2012

## Compliance Requirements

- **Mandatory Certifications**: Market intermediaries, specifically RTAs, must ensure that all associated persons involved in compliance and regulatory activities possess valid and active NISM certifications.
- **Execution of Tripartite Agreements**: RTAs must execute formal tripartite agreements with all corporate clients to satisfy the mandates of Regulation 9A of the RTA Regulations and Regulation 7(4) of the LODR Regulations.
- **Signature Verification Recordkeeping**: Ensure strict maintenance of signature cards for all client companies to safeguard share transfer verification and maintain internal operational controls.

## Important Dates

- **Inspection Period**: April 1, 2021, to September 19, 2022
- **Inspection Findings Letter**: January 12, 2023
- **Noticee's Response**: February 07, 2023
- **Enquiry Report Date**: February 28, 2025
- **Order Date**: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Operational Impact**: Emphasizes the need for registered intermediaries, especially QRTAs handling large investor bases, to maintain robust compliance calendars and personnel auditing mechanisms to avoid regulatory action.
- **Market Impact**: Reinforces SEBI's ongoing commitment to supervise and audit key market infrastructure intermediaries, protecting shareholder interests and ensuring high data integrity standards across listed corporate issuers.
