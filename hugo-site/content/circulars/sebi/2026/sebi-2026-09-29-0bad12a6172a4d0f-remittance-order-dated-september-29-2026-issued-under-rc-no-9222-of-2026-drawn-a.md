## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated September 29, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9222 of 2026 drawn against Ankit Ajitbhai Panchal. This action relates to investigations and recovery proceedings concerning trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9222 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Ankit Ajitbhai Panchal.
- Matter: Trading activities and market manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to Recovery Certificates issued under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated entities are required to comply with the remittance order, direct transfers/payments as mandated, and freeze specified accounts or assets of the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Remittance Order: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and recovery follow-through by SEBI to enforce monetary penalties and disgorgement against entities involved in stock price manipulation of DU Digital Global Limited.
