## Summary

SEBI issued a final enquiry order under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Regulation 27 of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 in respect of Modex International Securities Ltd (MISL), a registered stock broker. Forensic audits revealed severe irregularities including non-availability of client securities, shortfall in client funds, improper use of client funds, misrepresentation of books and accounts, and periodic payments resembling returns on investments, leading to a total shortfall of Rs. 125.92 Crore.

## Key Points

- NSE observed that MISL falsified the Register of securities of clients by passing false entries of transfer of securities.
- Forensic audit by KPMG revealed a massive shortfall of client funds and securities to the tune of Rs. 125.92 Crore.
- SEBI previously passed an ex-parte ad-interim order on April 30, 2020, followed by a confirmatory order on September 11, 2020.
- Multiple violations regarding client fund segregation, non-availability of securities, and misrepresentation of accounts were established.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of strict intermediary regulations and penalties against fraudulent stock brokers misusing client assets.

## Compliance Requirements

- Stock brokers must strictly maintain segregation of client funds and securities, avoid unauthorized usage, and ensure accurate reporting in books and accounts.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Report submitted by NSE: December 27, 2019
- Forensic Auditor appointed: January 24, 2020
- SEBI Ad-Interim Order: April 30, 2020
- Confirmatory Order: September 11, 2020
- Final Enquiry Order Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- High impact on market integrity and investor confidence, reinforcing SEBI's zero-tolerance policy towards misappropriation of client securities and funds by intermediaries.
