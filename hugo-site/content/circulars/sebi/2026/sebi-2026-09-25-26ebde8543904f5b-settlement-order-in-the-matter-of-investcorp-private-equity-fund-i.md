## Summary

SEBI passed a settlement order in the matter of Investcorp Private Equity Fund I (a scheme of Investcorp Infrastructure Fund I) concerning the delayed winding up of the scheme and liquidation of assets beyond its extended tenure end date of September 26, 2020.

## Key Points

- Investcorp Infrastructure Fund I registered with SEBI under VCF Regulations launched the scheme 'Investcorp Private Equity Fund I' with an initial 10-year tenure ending September 26, 2020.
- The Fund failed to wind up the Scheme before the tenure end and completed asset liquidation and proceeds distribution on June 30, 2025 (a delay of ~4.5 years), violating regulation 23(1)(a) read with regulation 24(2) of VCF Regulations.
- The Applicant filed a suo-motu settlement application under SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018.
- The Internal Committee (IC) and High Powered Advisory Committee (HPAC) recommended a settlement amount of ₹11,09,250/-, which was approved by the Panel of Whole Time Members.
- Upon remittance of the settlement amount by the Applicant, the proceedings were settled.

## Regulatory Changes

- None (Enforcement action settled via monetary payment under Settlement Regulations, 2018).

## Compliance Requirements

- Funds must strictly adhere to tenure limits and timely winding up / liquidation timelines specified under applicable VCF/AIF regulations.

## Important Dates

- September 26, 2020: Original extended tenure end date of the Scheme.
- June 04, 2026: IC meeting with Applicant representatives.
- June 29, 2026: HPAC meeting considering settlement terms.
- August 12, 2026: Approval by Panel of Whole Time Members.
- August 14, 2026: Notice of Demand issued and remittance of settlement amount.
- June 30, 2025: Completion of liquidation and distribution.

## Impact Assessment

- Resolves regulatory enforcement proceedings against the Fund without admission or denial of guilt, establishing precedent for handling delayed scheme wind-ups through the settlement mechanism.
