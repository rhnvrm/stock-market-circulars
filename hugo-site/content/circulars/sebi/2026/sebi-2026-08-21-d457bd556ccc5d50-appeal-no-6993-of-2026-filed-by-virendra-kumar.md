## Summary

An RTI appeal (No. 6993 of 2026) filed by Virendra Kumar against the CPIO, SEBI, regarding information sought on action taken on a SCORES complaint against Bonanza Portfolio Ltd. (SEBIE/UC26/HAR/019942/1) concerning unauthorized F&O trades and related grievances.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details and file notings of action taken by SEBI on a SCORES complaint against Bonanza Portfolio Ltd.
- The respondent stated that the information sought does not pertain to the appellant and directed access to the Action Taken Report (ATR) via the SCORES portal.
- SEBI noted that complaint handling on SCORES is electronic, and the ATR contains all processing information without separate file notings maintained outside the system.
