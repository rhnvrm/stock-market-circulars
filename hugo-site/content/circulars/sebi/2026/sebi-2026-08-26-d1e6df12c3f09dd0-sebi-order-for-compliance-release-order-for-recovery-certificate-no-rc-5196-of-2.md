## Summary

SEBI has issued an order regarding Recovery Certificate No. RC 5196 of 2022 against Tanna Tusharbhai Hiralal in the matter of Excel Castronics Limited, addressing compliance and attachment/release procedures under statutory recovery rules.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: RC 5196 of 2022
- Defaulter: Tanna Tusharbhai Hiralal
- Matter: Excel Castronics Limited
- Regulatory Body: Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of recovery proceedings and subsequent compliance or release directives in accordance with SEBI's statutory powers.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant financial institutions and the noticee must adhere to the attachment or release directives specified in the recovery order.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures regulatory compliance and enforces recovery processes in cases involving market violations and unpaid dues.
