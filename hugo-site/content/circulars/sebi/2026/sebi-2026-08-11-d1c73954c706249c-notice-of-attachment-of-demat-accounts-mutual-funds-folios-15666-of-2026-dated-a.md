## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice for the attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s) of Mr. Rajesh Punia (PAN: AVNPP5342G) and Mrs. Savita Punia (PAN: AALPP0729L) jointly and severally under Recovery Certificate No. 9223 of 2026, in the matter of investigation into the financial statements of Oriental Trimex Limited.

## Key Points

- **Defaulters:** Mr. Rajesh Punia and Mrs. Savita Punia.
- **Associated Entity:** Oriental Trimex Limited.
- **Action:** Attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s).
- **Recovery Certificate No.:** 9223 of 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery proceedings via asset attachment under SEBI's statutory powers.

## Compliance Requirements

- Depository participants and mutual fund institutions are directed not to allow any debit or transfer of securities/units from the attached accounts and folios without prior approval from SEBI.

## Important Dates

- **Date of Notice:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Restricts financial mobility and asset liquidation for the named defaulters pending recovery proceedings.
