## Summary

SEBI has initiated summary proceedings under Regulation 30A of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 against 12 Research Analysts for failing to pay mandatory registration renewal fees as required by the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014.

## Key Points

- SEBI initiated summary proceedings against 12 registered Research Analysts.
- The action stems from non-payment of mandatory five-year registration renewal fees.
- Affected entities include Dr. Stock Research Advisory, Mitesh Thacker, Religare Capital Markets Limited, and others.
- Proceedings are conducted under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Regulation 30A of SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of renewal fee obligations under Clause 3 of the Second Schedule to the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014.

## Compliance Requirements

- Registered Research Analysts must ensure timely payment of renewal fees every five years within the prescribed window (three months before expiry).
- Noticees are subject to summary proceedings for outstanding dues.

## Important Dates

- Various renewal due dates ranging from May 2024 to August 2025 across the respective Noticees.

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights strict regulatory enforcement regarding compliance and fee payments for intermediaries, potentially leading to suspension or cancellation of registration for non-compliant research analysts.
