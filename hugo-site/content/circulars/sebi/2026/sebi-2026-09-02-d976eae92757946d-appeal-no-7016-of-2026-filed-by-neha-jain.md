## Summary

This order disposes of Appeal No. 7016 of 2026 filed by Neha Jain against the CPIO, SEBI under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant had sought information regarding the status of investigation, asset freezing, refund procedures, and investigation reports concerning The Good Choice Enterprises Pvt. Ltd. (CIN: U52190RJ2007PTC024622), an entity reported for illegal money collection. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's stance that the queries sought clarification and opinion rather than 'information' as defined under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details on SEBI's investigation into The Good Choice Enterprises Pvt. Ltd. and actions taken against its directors.
- The appellant claimed a family loss of ₹6,30,000 across seven accounts due to alleged fraudulent money collection.
- The CPIO and Appellate Authority concluded that the queries sought clarifications and opinions, falling outside the definition of 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a quasi-judicial order under the RTI Act, 2005.
