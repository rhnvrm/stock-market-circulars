## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7694 of 2024 against Sathish Kumar N in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This enforcement action is part of SEBI's ongoing recovery proceedings for realization of dues and penalties related to investigations into financial irregularities and misstatements by Arvind Remedies Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7694 of 2024.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Sathish Kumar N.
- Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Date of Notice: October 13, 2026.
- Previous Notice of Demand was issued on February 26, 2024 under the same Recovery Certificate.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of outstanding dues, penalties, and settlement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are required to freeze accounts, demat accounts, and other movable/immovable assets of the defaulter as specified in the attachment notice and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Attachment Notice: October 13, 2026
- Previous Demand Notice: February 26, 2024

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against individuals involved in market irregularities and financial misstatements in Arvind Remedies Ltd.
