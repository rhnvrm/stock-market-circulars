## Summary

SEBI initiated adjudication proceedings under section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992 against Mr. Bhavik Indravadan Shah, Mrs. Jayshree Bhavik Shah, and Bhavik Indravadan Shah HUF for alleged front-running activities of the trades of the 'Big Client' Kaushik Shah Shares & Securities Pvt. Ltd. during the period from October 01, 2021, to January 15, 2025.

## Key Points

- Noticee No. 1 (Mr. Bhavik Indravadan Shah) acted as a dealer for Kaushik Shah Shares & Securities Pvt. Ltd. and abused his position by accessing impending order information.
- Trades were executed in his own account as well as in the accounts of related entities (Noticee Nos. 2 and 3).
- Violations alleged include sections 12A(a), 12A(b), and 12A(c) of the SEBI Act, and regulations 3(a), 3(b), 3(c), 3(d), 4(1), and 4(2)(q) of the PFUTP Regulations.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement action under Section 15HA of the SEBI Act for fraudulent and unfair trade practices.

## Compliance Requirements

- Strict adherence to market integrity rules, prohibition of front-running, and prevention of misuse of non-public impending order information by dealers and related entities.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: October 01, 2021, to January 15, 2025
- Date of Appointment of Adjudicating Officer: April 13, 2026
- Date of Show Cause Notice (SCN): April 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on market intermediaries and dealers to deter fraudulent front-running practices and protect investor interests.
