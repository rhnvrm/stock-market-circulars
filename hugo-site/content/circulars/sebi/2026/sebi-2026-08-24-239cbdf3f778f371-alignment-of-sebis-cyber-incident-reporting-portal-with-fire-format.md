## Summary

SEBI has issued a circular regarding the alignment of its Cyber Incident Reporting Portal with the FIRE (Financial Incident Reporting and Escalation) Format. This applies to all regulated entities including AIFs, clearing corporations, depositories, stock brokers, and other intermediaries.

## Key Points

- Alignment of SEBI's Cyber Incident Reporting Portal with the FIRE Format.
- Applicable to Alternative Investment Funds (AIFs), Bankers to an Issue, Clearing Corporations, Collective Investment Schemes, Credit Rating Agencies, Custodians, Debenture Trustees, Depositories, DDPs, Research Analysts, KRAs, Merchant Bankers, and other market participants.

## Regulatory Changes

- Integration and standardization of cyber incident reporting mechanisms using the FIRE format.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulated entities must ensure their reporting mechanisms and technical integrations comply with the aligned FIRE format on SEBI's portal.

## Important Dates

- Date of Circular: August 24, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances cyber resilience and streamlines incident reporting across the Indian securities market.
