## Summary

Subhanarayan Mishra filed an appeal under the RTI Act, 2005 challenging CPIO's response regarding examination centre mapping and state-wise candidate distribution for Phase II examinations. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that public authorities are not obligated to create, collect, or collate information not available in their records or desired format.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought mapping of examination centre codes, state-wise distribution of candidates called for interviews, and finally selected candidates.
- CPIO stated the requested information is not maintained in the desired format.
- Appellate Authority cited precedents confirming that public authorities are not required to create or process information to format it as requested.
