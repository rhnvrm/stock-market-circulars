## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment (Demat) under Notice No. 15676 dated August 12, 2026, pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 9231 of 2026 against S R Traders (Proprietor: Maheshkumar Jayantilal Chauhan, PAN: AHKPC1715D).

## Key Points

- Entity Name: S R Traders
- Proprietor: Maheshkumar Jayantilal Chauhan
- PAN: AHKPC1715D
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9231 of 2026
- Notice of Attachment (Demat): No. 15676 dated August 12, 2026

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of recovery proceedings through the attachment of demat accounts for pending dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities and depositories must comply with the attachment notice by freezing/attaching the relevant demat accounts as directed by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 12, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restricts operations and transactions in the specified demat accounts until dues are fully settled or further orders are passed by SEBI.
