## Summary

SEBI issued a final order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992 in the matter of Adani Enterprises Limited, Adani Transmission Limited, Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Limited, and Adani Power Limited concerning alleged violations of Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) requirements and associated entities.

## Key Points

- Adjudication of alleged Minimum Public Shareholding (MPS) violations by Adani Group entities.
- Involves multiple noticees including promoter entities, key managerial personnel, and foreign portfolio/investment entities such as Mr. Vinod Adani.
- Examines compliance with statutory public float norms under securities regulations.
- Issues binding directions under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), and 11B of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and regulatory directives issued to the noticees and listed companies to rectify or penalize non-compliance with public shareholding thresholds.

## Compliance Requirements

Noticees and listed entities must comply with the specific directions, remedial actions, or penalties stipulated in the final order.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 2026
- Effective immediately upon issuance.

## Impact Assessment

High significance for corporate governance, regulatory compliance, and market sentiment regarding Adani Group listed equities.
