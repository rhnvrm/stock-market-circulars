## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 19, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9264 of 2026 against Surbhi Aggarwal (PAN: BNIPA1542D). This action is taken in the matter of front running by Navnit Gadoya, Surbhi Aggarwal, and Chiranggi Irish Shah to recover dues and penalties.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9264 of 2026.
- Defaulter: Surbhi Aggarwal (PAN: BNIPA1542D).
- Matter: Front running by Navnit Gadoya, Surbhi Aggarwal, and Chiranggi Irish Shah.
- Assets targeted: Demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions initiated by SEBI under the Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, depositories, and mutual fund houses/RTAs are required to attach and freeze the demat accounts, bank accounts, and mutual fund folio(s) of the noticee to prevent any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and asset recovery by SEBI against individuals found guilty of front running and market abuse.
