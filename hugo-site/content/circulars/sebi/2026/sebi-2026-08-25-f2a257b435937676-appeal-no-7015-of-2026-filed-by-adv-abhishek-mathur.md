## Summary

Adv Abhishek Mathur filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI's response stating that information regarding pending refunds and claim amounts for investors of PACL Ltd. is not available with SEBI, as such matters are handled entirely by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details on the total number of unrefunded PACL Ltd. investors, pending claim amounts, reasons for delay, and action plans.
- SEBI's CPIO and the Appellate Authority confirmed that the requested information is not held by SEBI.
- The responsibility for property disposal and investor repayment for PACL Ltd. rests with the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee constituted under Supreme Court orders.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, citing relevant precedents from the Central Information Commission (CIC).

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a quasi-judicial appellate order under the RTI Act.

## Compliance Requirements

- None for market participants. Information requests regarding PACL refunds must be directed to the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Application: July 29, 2026
- Date of CPIO Reply: August 3, 2026
- Date of Appeal: August 3, 2026
- Date of Order: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- No direct market or operational impact; reinforces standard RTI procedures regarding information not held in a public authority's custody.
