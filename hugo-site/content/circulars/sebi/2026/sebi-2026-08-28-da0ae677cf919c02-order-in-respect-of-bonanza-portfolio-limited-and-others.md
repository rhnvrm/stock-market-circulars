## Summary

SEBI issued an order under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Regulation 27 of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 in respect of Bonanza Portfolio Limited (merchant banker) and its directors. The Designated Authority recommended disposing of the enquiry proceedings without any adverse action.

## Key Points

- Bonanza Portfolio Limited is registered as a merchant banker, stock broker, depository participant, portfolio manager, and research analyst.
- SEBI conducted an inspection of the company for the period from April 01, 2022 to April 30, 2024.
- The Designated Authority submitted a report dated March 18, 2026 recommending that proceedings be disposed of without adverse action under Regulation 26(1) of the Intermediaries Regulations.
- Noticee No. 2 (Satya Prakash Goel) passed away prior to the issuance of the SCN, as confirmed by a death certificate.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

No adverse action or compliance penalty imposed on the noticees in this matter.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2022 to April 30, 2024
- Date of DA Report: March 18, 2026
- Date of SCN to Noticee 1: May 29, 2026
- Date of Common SCN to Noticees 2–7: July 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No operational or market impact as proceedings were disposed of without adverse action.
