## Summary

The appellant, Santosh, filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the response of the CPIO, SEBI regarding information related to investments made in PACL Ltd, customer code LP0001 LK 622 Etawah, deposit amounts, property allocation status, and refund procedures under the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought PACL registration details, total deposited amounts, allocated plot status, refund claim status, and claim procedures for lost original certificate bonds.
- The respondent stated that the requested information is not available with SEBI and that all PACL refund communications are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The appellant was advised to approach the Committee directly and refer to public notices and FAQs available on the SEBI website.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is an appellate order under the RTI Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

None specified for market participants.
