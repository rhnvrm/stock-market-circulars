## Summary

SEBI has issued an exemption order under Section 11(1) and 11(2)(h) of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with Regulation 11(5) of the SEBI (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations, 2011 regarding the proposed acquisition of shares and voting rights in NGL Fine-Chem Limited by Ajita Nachane Family Trust and Rahul Nachane Family Trust.

## Key Points

- Target Company: NGL Fine-Chem Limited (listed on BSE and NSE).
- Acquirers: Ajita Nachane Family Trust and Rahul Nachane Family Trust, acting through trustees Mr. Rahul Nachane and Mrs. Ajita Nachane.
- Proposed Transaction: Acquisition of shares and voting rights in the Target Company by the acquirer trusts as part of promoter restructuring/succession planning.
- Exemption Sought: Exemption from the applicability of Regulation 3 and Regulation 4 of the SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011.

## Regulatory Changes

Grant of exemption from mandatory open offer requirements under SEBI (SAST) Regulations, 2011 for internal share transfers among promoter family trusts.

## Compliance Requirements

Acquirer trusts and the Target Company must comply with disclosures, reporting requirements, and conditions specified in the SEBI order.

## Important Dates

- Application fee remittance date: August 06, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enables smooth internal reorganization and succession planning for the promoter group of NGL Fine-Chem Limited without triggering open offer obligations.
