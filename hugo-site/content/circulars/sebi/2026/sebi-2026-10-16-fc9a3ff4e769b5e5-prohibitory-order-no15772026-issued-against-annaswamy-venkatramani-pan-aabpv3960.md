## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued Prohibitory Order No. 1577/2026 dated October 16, 2026, against Annaswamy Venkatramani (PAN: AABPV3960F). This action is taken under Recovery Certificate Nos. 2976 of 2020 (Sanraa Media Limited matter), 3197 of 2020 (G.V. Films Limited matter), and 6606 of 2023 for the realization of outstanding dues, penalties, and interest.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Order Number: 1577/2026
- Date of Order: October 16, 2026
- Noticee / Defaulter: Annaswamy Venkatramani (PAN: AABPV3960F)
- Recovery Certificates: No. 2976 of 2020, No. 3197 of 2020, and No. 6606 of 2023
- Associated Entities: Sanraa Media Limited, G.V. Films Limited

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and dues arising from past regulatory violations.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant entities are required to comply with the prohibitory order by freezing specified assets, bank accounts, and securities holdings of the noticee to prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Prohibitory Order: October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's continued stringent enforcement and recovery measures against defaulters who have failed to pay regulatory penalties.
