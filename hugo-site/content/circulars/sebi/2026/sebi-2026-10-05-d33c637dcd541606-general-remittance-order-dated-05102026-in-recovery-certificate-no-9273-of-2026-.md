## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated October 5, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9273 of 2026 drawn against Manjulaben Parmar (PAN: DKYPP5235B). This action relates to investigations into trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under RC No. 9273 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Manjulaben Parmar (PAN: DKYPP5235B).
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues/penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to comply with the general remittance order as specified in the recovery proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: October 5, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against entities and individuals involved in market irregularities in DU Digital Global Limited.
