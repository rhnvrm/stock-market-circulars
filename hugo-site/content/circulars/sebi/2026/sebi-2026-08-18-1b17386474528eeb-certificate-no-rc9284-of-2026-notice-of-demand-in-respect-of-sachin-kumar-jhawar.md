## Summary

SEBI has updated the regulatory framework for Online Bond Platform Providers (OBPPs) to enhance the ease of doing business. The modifications include allowing OBPPs to offer IFSCA-regulated products and Section 54EC tax-saving bonds, alongside updated compliance officer norms.

## Key Points

- OBPPs are permitted to offer products, securities, or services regulated by the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA).
- Inclusion of Section 54EC tax-saving bonds under the permitted offerings.
- Realignment of compliance officer qualifications with stock broker norms.

## Regulatory Changes

- Relaxation and streamlining of product offerings and compliance standards for OBPPs.
- Introduction of mandatory labeling requirements for overseas/international products.

## Compliance Requirements

- OBPPs offering IFSCA-regulated products must adhere to FEMA guidelines and ensure clear labeling.
- Provision of specific disclosures and grievance redressal clarifications for 54EC bond investors.
- Appointment of a compliance officer meeting the certification standards under the SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately from August 14, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances product diversification for OBPPs while ensuring robust investor protection and compliance alignment.
