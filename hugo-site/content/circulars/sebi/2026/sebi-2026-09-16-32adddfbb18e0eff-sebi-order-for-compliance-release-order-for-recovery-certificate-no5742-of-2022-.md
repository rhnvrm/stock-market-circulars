## Summary

SEBI has issued a release order regarding Recovery Certificate No. 5742 of 2022 drawn against Payal Jayeshbhai Madiyar in the matter of Excel Castronics Limited. The order formally releases/withdraws proceedings or attachments previously initiated under the recovery certificate following resolution or compliance.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 5742 of 2022.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Payal Jayeshbhai Madiyar.
- Matter: Excel Castronics Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Formal release and withdrawal of recovery/enforcement measures pursuant to the satisfaction of dues or settlement under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and relevant intermediaries are notified of the release/withdrawal of attachment on accounts or assets as specified in the order.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects conclusion of recovery proceedings for the specific noticee in relation to Excel Castronics Limited, with minimal broader market impact.
