## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate Number 9285 of 2026 dated August 19, 2026, to VIKRAM ANAND HUF (PAN: AAJHV0926R). This recovery proceeding stems from previous adjudication findings concerning non-genuine reversal trades and manipulative dealings in Illiquid Stock Options on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

## Key Points

- Notice of Demand issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9285 of 2026.
- Date of Recovery Certificate: August 19, 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: VIKRAM ANAND HUF (PAN: AAJHV0926R).
- Subject Matter: Dealings in Illiquid Stock Options on BSE involving non-genuine and reversal trades.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions initiated pursuant to SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of monetary penalties and dues arising from fraudulent trading practices.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulter entities must clear all outstanding dues immediately under the recovery certificate to avoid further coercive measures, such as the attachment of bank accounts, demat accounts, and properties by SEBI recovery officers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate & Demand Notice: August 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects SEBI's continued rigorous pursuit of recovery against entities involved in historical misuse of the stock options segment for artificial volume generation and tax/regulatory infractions.
