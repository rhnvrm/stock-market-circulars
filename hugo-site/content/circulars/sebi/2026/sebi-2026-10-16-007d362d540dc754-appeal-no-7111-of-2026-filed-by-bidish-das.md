## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7111 of 2026 filed by Bidish Das under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant challenged the CPIO's denial of soft copies of the Sahara-SEBI Refund Account from FY 2021-2022 to August 2026. The Appellate Authority upheld the denial under Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act, citing protection of third-party personal and financial privacy.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought soft copies of the Sahara-SEBI Refund Account for the period spanning FY 2021-2022 to August 2026.
- CPIO denied the request under Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act on the grounds that the information contains financial transactions of third parties constituting personal information.
- The Appellate Authority concurred with the CPIO, referencing Supreme Court precedent (CPIO, Supreme Court of India Vs. Subhash Chandra Agarwal) protecting personal and financial records from unwarranted invasion of privacy.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this order reinforces established RTI Act exemptions regarding third-party personal and financial data.

## Compliance Requirements

None applicable for market participants.

## Important Dates

- RTI Application Date: August 30, 2026
- CPIO Response Date: September 22, 2026
- Appeal Date: September 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

No direct market or operational impact; reiterates privacy safeguards for third-party financial records under the RTI framework.
