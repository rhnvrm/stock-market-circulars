## Summary

SEBI has issued a remittance order dated September 28, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 9249 of 2026, directing all banks and mutual funds in India to remit attached amounts towards outstanding dues of Rs. 6,65,760.28 from defaulter Jagdish Chhanabhai Vaghela (PAN: AFPPV2562Q).

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9249 of 2026.
- Defaulter Name: Jagdish Chhanabhai Vaghela (PAN: AFPPV2562Q).
- Outstanding Dues: Rs. 6,65,760.28.
- Designated Bank Account: A/c No. SEBIRCIS9249 with Bank of India (IFSC: BKID00VAN04).

## Regulatory Changes

None. Issued under Section 28A and 28B of the SEBI Act, 1992 r/w Income Tax Act provisions.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and mutual funds must remit the available attached amount to SEBI's designated bank account and email remittance details if accounts/folios exist for the defaulter. If no accounts/folios exist, no communication is required.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: September 28, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

Operational directive applicable to banks and mutual funds holding accounts or folios of the specified individual defaulter.
