## Summary

SEBI conducted a theme-based inspection of VCK Share & Stock Broking Services Limited covering the period from April 01, 2024 to April 30, 2025. The inspection revealed violations of Stock Brokers Regulations, including terminals being operated by unapproved users who were not registered with the stock exchange.

## Key Points

- Noticee is registered with SEBI as a stock broker (Reg. No. INZ000215030).
- Theme-based inspection conducted jointly by SEBI and NSE for the period April 01, 2024 to April 30, 2025.
- Alleged violations include Clause (xix) of regulation 26 of SEBI (Stock Brokers) Regulations, 1992 and clauses A(1) and A(2) of the code of conduct.
- Observation of Computer to Computer Link (CTCL) terminals being operated by unapproved individuals (e.g., family members/directors not registered as approved users).

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes introduced; enforcement of existing Stock Brokers Regulations and SEBI norms.

## Compliance Requirements

Stock brokers must ensure that all trading terminals (CTCL) are exclusively operated by authorized and approved users registered with the stock exchanges in compliance with SEBI guidelines.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2024 to April 30, 2025
- SCN Date: February 17, 2026
- Appointment of AO: July 07, 2026
- Order Date: October 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory scrutiny on stock brokers regarding Authorised Person governance, terminal operations, and strict adherence to approved user registries.
