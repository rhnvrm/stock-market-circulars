## Summary

An appeal filed under the Right to Information Act, 2005 by Shashidhar against the CPIO of SEBI regarding the recruitment of Officers Grade A (Assistant Manager) 2025 was disposed of. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that category-wise roll number lists of candidates appearing in interviews in specific requested formats are not maintained by SEBI, noting that relevant merit list rankings and selected candidates' details are published on the SEBI website.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought category-wise roll number lists of candidates who appeared for interviews in the General Stream (Unreserved, Unreserved-PWD, OBC-NCL) and category-wise numbers for medical examinations under the RTI Act.
- The CPIO responded that information in the specific format requested is not maintained, while noting that relevant merit list ranks are available on the SEBI website via candidate login credentials.
- The Appellate Authority observed that the CPIO is only expected to provide information available and existing in records, citing previous Central Information Commission precedents.
- The Appellate Authority noted that marks secured by the 135 selected candidates along with their categories are published on the SEBI website.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is an adjudicatory order under the RTI Act, 2005.
