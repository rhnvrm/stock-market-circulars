## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7027 of 2026 filed by Samar Imran under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant had sought certified copies of records and clarifications regarding whether a 'Parivarjan Praman Patr' issued in Uttar Pradesh is acceptable as equivalent to a Legal Heirship Certificate. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the queries sought clarification, opinion, or confirmation and therefore did not qualify as 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details and certifications regarding documents considered equivalent to a Legal Heirship Certificate.
- The respondent CPIO stated that the queries were in the nature of seeking clarification/opinion and fell outside Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- The Appellate Authority confirmed that the CPIO is not obliged to provide clarifications, interpretations, or create/analyze information under the RTI Act.
