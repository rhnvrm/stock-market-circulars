## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7707 of 2024 drawn against Murali Prakash Naidu. This action relates to recovery of dues and penalties stemming from investigations into financial irregularities, inflated profits, and fund diversion in Arvind Remedies Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7707 of 2024.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Murali Prakash Naidu.
- Company involved: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of outstanding dues, penalties, and interest.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries are mandated to freeze accounts, lockers, and holdings of the noticee as specified in the attachment notice to prevent asset alienation.

## Important Dates

- Date of Attachment Notice: October 13, 2026
- Original Recovery Certificate: RC No. 7707 of 2024

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's rigorous enforcement stance and recovery mechanisms against promoters and individuals associated with major corporate frauds and financial misstatements.
