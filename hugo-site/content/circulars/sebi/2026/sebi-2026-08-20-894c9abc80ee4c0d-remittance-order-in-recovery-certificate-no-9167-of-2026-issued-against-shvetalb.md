## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a remittance order under Recovery Certificate No. 9167 of 2026 against Shvetalben Sagarbhai Dataniya (PAN: CQGPD5278K). This action is part of ongoing recovery and enforcement proceedings related to unauthorized trading activities and price manipulation in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 9167 of 2026 targets Shvetalben Sagarbhai Dataniya.
- Relates to investigations into trading activities in DU Digital Technologies Limited.
- Directs the transfer of attached funds to satisfy outstanding regulatory dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Regulatory Changes

[None - Enforcement action under existing regulatory frameworks]

## Compliance Requirements

- Concerned financial institutions and banks must remit the attached funds as specified in the recovery order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issuance: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures enforcement of regulatory penalties and recovery of dues in market manipulation cases.
