## Summary

The Rose Valley Assets Disposal Committee (ADC) has issued a public notice requiring all depositors who have submitted claims on the ADC website (www.rosevalleyadc.com) to update their active Aadhaar-linked mobile numbers, upload their Aadhaar cards, and update their Aadhaar-linked bank accounts. Additionally, depositors who have not yet filed claims must do so before the final deadline.

## Key Points

- Depositors must update their active and Aadhaar-linked mobile numbers in their existing claims.
- Claimants are required to upload their Aadhaar Card and update their Aadhaar-linked bank account on the ADC website.
- The final deadline for uploading new claims is fixed for 30.11.2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Procedural update mandating Aadhaar and bank account verification details for claim disbursements by the Rose Valley ADC.

## Compliance Requirements

- Existing claimants must update their mobile numbers, Aadhaar cards, and bank account details on www.rosevalleyadc.com between 15.10.2026 and 15.11.2026.
- Unregistered depositors must upload their claims immediately on the ADC website before the final cutoff date.

## Important Dates

- Start Date for Updating Claims: 15.10.2026
- End Date for Updating Claims: 15.11.2026
- Last Date for Uploading New Claims: 30.11.2026

## Impact Assessment

- Ensures smooth communication via SMS and facilitates secure and direct disbursements of funds to verified depositors through Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.
