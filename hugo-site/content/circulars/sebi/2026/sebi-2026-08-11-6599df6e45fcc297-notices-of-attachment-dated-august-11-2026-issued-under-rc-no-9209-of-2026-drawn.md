## Summary

SEBI has issued Notice(s) of Attachment dated August 11, 2026 under RC No. 9209 of 2026 against Pranav Kamleshkumar Trivedi in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 9209 of 2026.
- Defaulter: Pranav Kamleshkumar Trivedi.
- Matter relates to trading activities in DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with recovery proceedings and attachment orders issued by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enforcement action against specific individual involved in recovery proceedings.
