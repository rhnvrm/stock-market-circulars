## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated August 13, 2026 under Recovery Certificate No. 9161 of 2026 against Rupee Services Private Limited concerning dealings in illiquid stock options at BSE.

## Key Points

- Issued under RC No. 9161 of 2026.
- Target entity: Rupee Services Private Limited.
- Context: Dealing in Illiquid Stocks Options at BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement action via recovery certificate proceedings.

## Compliance Requirements

- Compliance with recovery orders and remittance instructions as directed by the Recovery Officer.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Restrictive enforcement action against the named entity for past violations involving illiquid options.
