## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 6985 of 2026 filed by Narendran K under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appeal challenged CPIO's response concerning information on vacancies for Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) in the General Stream and planned recruitment for 2026.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought information on current vacancies for Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) in the General Stream and tentative vacancies for planned recruitment in 2026.
- CPIO stated that query regarding future recruitment is in the nature of seeking clarification/opinion and does not constitute 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
- CPIO noted that vacancies arise due to promotions, resignations, and retirements, and recruitment details are published on the SEBI website periodically.
- Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, citing relevant precedents that the RTI Act does not require authorities to create, interpret, or provide opinions and clarifications.
