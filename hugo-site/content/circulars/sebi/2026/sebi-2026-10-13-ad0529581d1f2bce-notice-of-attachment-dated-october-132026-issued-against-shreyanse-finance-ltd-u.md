## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7708 of 2024 against Shreyanse Finance Ltd in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under RC No. 7708 of 2024.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Shreyanse Finance Ltd.
- Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues/penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to freeze accounts and assets as specified in the attachment notice and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice / Attachment: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against entities involved in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd.
