## Summary

The Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 6996 of 2026 filed by Savita under the RTI Act, 2005. The appellant sought details regarding her PACL Limited refund amount credited to an incorrect bank account, total approved amount, transaction references, and remedial procedures. SEBI's CPIO had responded that the requested information was not available with SEBI and advised the appellant to approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought RTI information regarding wrongly credited PACL Limited refund amounts and remedial procedures.
- SEBI CPIO stated that the information was not available with SEBI and that PACL refund communications are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee.
- The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that SEBI cannot invent information not in its possession, citing CIC precedent.
