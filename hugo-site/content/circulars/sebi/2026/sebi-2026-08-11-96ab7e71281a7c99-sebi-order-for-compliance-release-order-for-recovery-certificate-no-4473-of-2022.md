## Summary

On August 11, 2026, the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) issued a Release Order and a Notice of Completion regarding Recovery Certificate No. 4473 of 2022. This certificate was originally issued against Mr. Girishkumar Prabhudas Ruparel (PAN: AIPPR8860G) to recover penalty dues arising from manipulative and fraudulent trading activities in the scrip of Atlanta Infrastructure and Finance Limited during the period 2010–2015. 

Following the full recovery or payment of the outstanding dues (including interest and recovery costs), SEBI has officially completed the recovery proceedings. Consequently, all previous attachments placed on Mr. Ruparel's bank accounts, demat accounts, and other assets under this recovery certificate are revoked and released with immediate effect.

## Key Points

- **Completion of Recovery:** SEBI has formally closed and marked as completed the recovery proceedings against Mr. Girishkumar Prabhudas Ruparel.
- **Asset Release:** All frozen bank accounts, demat accounts, locker accounts, and other attached financial assets belonging to Mr. Ruparel are released.
- **Underlying Cause:** The recovery proceedings stemmed from a penalty of ₹5,00,000 imposed on Mr. Ruparel for PFUTP violations in the matter of Atlanta Infrastructure and Finance Limited.
- **Full Recovery:** The release order indicates that all outstanding dues, interest, and costs associated with the recovery certificate have been fully recovered.

## Regulatory Changes

- No regulatory changes or policy amendments are introduced by this order. This is a standard individual enforcement closure under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

- **Banks and Financial Institutions:** All banks, locker operators, and depositories (NSDL & CDSL) holding attached accounts of Mr. Girishkumar Prabhudas Ruparel must immediately lift the attachment and restore normal operations on the accounts.
- **Noticee:** No further actions are required from Mr. Ruparel as the recovery case is officially concluded.

## Important Dates

- **Trading Violations Period:** 2010 – 2015
- **Adjudication Penalty Order:** February 2021
- **Recovery Certificate Issued:** 2022
- **Release Order and Completion Date:** August 11, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Individual Impact:** High impact on Mr. Ruparel, whose bank and demat accounts are now un-frozen and accessible.
- **Market Impact:** Low/Nil impact on the general securities market, as this is an isolated individual recovery proceeding.
- **Company Impact:** Low/Nil impact on Atlanta Infrastructure and Finance Limited (BSE: 530479) since the action is against a historical trader and not the company's current management or operations.
