## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 7095 of 2026 filed by Eshwar Singh under the Right to Information Act, 2005. The appellant sought historical details regarding a company from 1987 in Ahmedabad. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response that the information was not available with SEBI, citing the Supreme Court judgment in Central Board of Secondary Education & Anr. vs. Aditya Bandopadhyay & Ors.

## Key Points

- Appeal No. 7095 of 2026 filed by Eshwar Singh against CPIO, SEBI.
- Appellant sought historical information regarding a 1987 company in Ahmedabad and its subsequent amalgamation or share price.
- CPIO and Appellate Authority confirmed that the requested information is not available with SEBI.
- Appeal dismissed based on the Supreme Court ruling that public authorities are not required to collect or collate non-available information.
