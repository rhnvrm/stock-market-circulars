## Summary

SEBI conducted an investigation into the trading activities in illiquid stock options on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) for the period between April 1, 2014, and September 30, 2015. The investigation revealed large-scale reversal of trades leading to artificial volume creation. Kedar Mal Poddar HUF was identified as one of the entities that indulged in these non-genuine trades, allegedly violating Regulations 3(a), (b), (c), (d), 4(1), and 4(2)(a) of the PFUTP Regulations, 2003.

## Key Points

- Investigation period: April 1, 2014, to September 30, 2015.
- Substantial non-genuine trades (reversal trades) constituted 81.40% of all trades in the BSE stock options segment during the investigation period.
- Noticee: Kedar Mal Poddar HUF (PAN: AACHK5074Q).
- Alleged violations of Regulations 3(a), (b), (c), (d), 4(1), and 4(2)(a) of SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003.

## Regulatory Changes

None.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities must adhere to market regulations and refrain from executing non-genuine or reversal trades that create artificial volumes.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: April 1, 2014 - September 30, 2015
- SCN Date: August 02, 2022
- Assignment Date to Current AO: June 01, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Individual entity adjudication order with no broader systemic market impact.
