## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance dated September 29, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9214 of 2026 drawn against Dheeraj Wadhawan (PAN: AAOPW4517G) as a defaulter. This action pertains to investigations and recovery proceedings regarding trading activity in the scrip of Dewan Housing Finance Corporation Limited (DHFL).

## Key Points

- General Remittance issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9214 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Dheeraj Wadhawan (PAN: AAOPW4517G).
- Subject Matter: Trading activity in the scrip of Dewan Housing Finance Corporation Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and remittance of recovered amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, garnishees, and relevant entities are required to remit funds or comply with directions specified in the general remittance and recovery order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Remittance: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against defaulters in high-profile financial cases.
