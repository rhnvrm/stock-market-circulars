## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Demand dated October 16, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9305 of 2026 against Amesh Surajlal Jaiswal, Arvind Shukla, and Jalaj Agrawal, jointly and severally, in the matter of Akash Infra Projects Limited for the realization of dues.

## Key Points

- Notice of Demand issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9305 of 2026.
- Noticees / Defaulters: Amesh Surajlal Jaiswal (PAN: AFZPJ0067Q), Arvind Shukla (PAN: ISZPS7481G), and Jalaj Agrawal (PAN: AUDPA0226H).
- Liability: Jointly and severally liable for payment of dues.
- Company involved: Akash Infra Projects Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated under the SEBI Act, 1992 and related regulations for the recovery of penalties, fines, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and other intermediaries are required to comply with attachment and demand directives, ensuring that accounts and assets of the noticees are frozen and no unauthorized transfers are permitted.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: October 16, 2026
- Recovery Certificate No: 9305 of 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and recovery action by SEBI against individuals failing to pay dues associated with market violations in Akash Infra Projects Limited.
