## Summary

Parivesh Shukla filed an appeal under the RTI Act, 2005 against SEBI's CPIO response regarding a complaint lodged against Shashank Traders Limited. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's stance, noting that details regarding ongoing investigations and internal correspondence are exempt under Section 8(1)(h) of the RTI Act to preserve confidentiality and prevent market speculation.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details, action taken reports, notices, and investigation files concerning a complaint against Shashank Traders Limited.
- SEBI's CPIO provided the complaint registration number (SEBIE/MH26/MUMB/031706/1) but declined queries 2-7 citing the exemption of ongoing examinations/investigations.
- The Appellate Authority confirmed that maintaining the confidentiality of investigations is vital to prevent unwarranted market speculation and protect third-party interests.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. Reinforces existing RTI Act exemptions.
