## Summary

This document is a SEBI final order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), 11(4A), 11B(1) and 11B(2) of the SEBI Act, 1992, in the matter of Max Financial Services Limited, Max Life Insurance Company Limited, Axis Bank Ltd, Axis Capital Ltd, Axis Securities Ltd, and various individual noticees regarding transactions and corporate restructuring.

## Key Points

- Proceedings emanate from an SCN dated October 24, 2024, issued pursuant to a SEBI investigation.
- Involves entities including Max Financial Services Limited (MFSL), Max Life Insurance Company Limited (MLIC), and Axis Bank Limited.
- Details historical corporate restructurings, demergers, and equity stake sales involving the entities.
