## Summary

SEBI conducted an inspection of Investowryght Research Analytics Private Limited (Research Analyst, Registration No. INH000012157) for the period from April 01, 2024, to August 31, 2025. Following findings of various violations under the SEBI Act, PFUTP Regulations, RA Regulations, and SEBI Master Circulars, an Adjudication Officer was appointed under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act to inquire and adjudge under Sections 15HA and 15EB of the SEBI Act.

## Key Points

- Noticee is a registered research analyst holding registration number INH000012157 (PAN: AAGCI9545K).
- Inspection conducted by SEBI for the period between April 01, 2024 and August 31, 2025 revealed multiple regulatory lapses.
- Alleged violations include provisions of Regulations 3(a), 3(b), 3(d), 4(1), 4(2)(k), 4(2)(o) and 4(2)(s) of PFUTP Regulations read with Section 12A(a), (b) and (c) of SEBI Act and Code of Conduct specified under RA Regulations.
- Further violations alleged under RA Regulations read with SEBI Master Circular dated February 06, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of RA Regulations, PFUTP Regulations, and Master Circular provisions against non-compliant research analysts.

## Compliance Requirements

- Research analysts must strictly adhere to the Code of Conduct, disclosure norms, and operational guidelines specified under RA Regulations and relevant SEBI Master Circulars.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Period: April 01, 2024 to August 31, 2025
- Inspection Report / Findings Communication: April 01, 2026
- Appointment of AO: August 12, 2026
- SCN Issued: August 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on research analysts to protect investors from fraudulent and unfair trade practices and ensure compliance with operational standards.
