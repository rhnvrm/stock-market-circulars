## Summary

Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued an order regarding the cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 7449 of 2023 dated December 08, 2023, which was previously issued to Rashesh Purohit (Defaulter, PAN: ADQPP1270Q) in the matter of TV Vision Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No. 7449 of 2023 originally dated December 08, 2023.
- Defaulter: Rashesh Purohit (PAN: ADQPP1270Q).
- Matter: TV Vision Limited.
- Action: Cancellation of the recovery certificate by SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

Formal withdrawal and cancellation of recovery proceedings and attachment measures initiated under the Recovery Certificate against the named defaulter.

## Compliance Requirements

No further compliance actions or asset freezes required under this specific recovery certificate; relevant financial institutions and authorities are notified of the cancellation.

## Important Dates

- Date of Original Recovery Certificate: December 08, 2023
- Date of Cancellation / Notice: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Relief to the individual from specific recovery proceedings under this certificate, reflecting closure or settlement of dues related to the matter.
