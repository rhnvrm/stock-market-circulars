## Summary

SEBI issued an order in the matter of certain Investment Advisers, canceling the certificates of registration of multiple entities and individuals for failing to pay the mandatory five-yearly renewal fees and maintain compliance under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

## Key Points

- Cancellation of certificates of registration for non-payment of mandatory renewal fees.
- Actions taken against various individuals and corporate entities operating as investment advisers.
- Reinforces regulatory adherence and accountability among market intermediaries.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to Regulation 30A and relevant provisions of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 and SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

## Compliance Requirements

Investment advisers must ensure timely payment of renewal fees and adherence to continuous disclosure and compliance standards. Canceled entities must address any pending investor grievances and liabilities.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures investor protection by weeding out non-compliant investment advisers and maintaining market integrity.
