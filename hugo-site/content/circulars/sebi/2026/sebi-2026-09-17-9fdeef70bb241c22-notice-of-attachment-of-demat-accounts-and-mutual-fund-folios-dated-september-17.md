## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a notice for the attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folios belonging to Bhavin Natwarlal Panchal (PAN: AJMPP8429P) under Recovery Certificate No. 5697 of 2022 in the matter of Gala Global Products Limited.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter:** Bhavin Natwarlal Panchal (PAN – AJMPP8429P).
- **Matter:** Gala Global Products Limited.
- **Recovery Certificate:** No. 5697 of 2022.
- **Action:** Attachment of all securities held in demat accounts and units in mutual fund folios.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions executed under SEBI Act provisions to secure pending dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Depositories, brokers, and mutual fund institutions are required to freeze and prohibit any transfer, disposal, or dealing with the attached demat accounts and mutual fund folios.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** September 17, 2026
- **Original Recovery Certificate:** September 16, 2022

## Impact Assessment

Ensures compliance and restricts the defaulter from liquidating assets until outstanding dues under the recovery certificate are fully settled.
