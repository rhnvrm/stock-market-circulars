## Summary

SEBI has issued a Remittance Order dated August 28, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9181 of 2026 drawn against Rathod Mahendrkumar in the matter of trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- Remittance Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9181 of 2026.
- Target Entity/Individual: Rathod Mahendrkumar.
- Related Scrip: DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Proceedings Officer / Competent Authority at SEBI.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Compliance with the remittance order and recovery certificate instructions as mandated by SEBI's recovery proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enforcement action directed against specific defaulters/entities, ensuring regulatory compliance and recovery of dues without broad market-wide impact.
