## Summary

SEBI has issued a compliance notice regarding the attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s) pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 8511 of 2025 against Add Life Pharma Ltd. The action is taken under the statutory recovery powers of the Securities and Exchange Board of India.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 8511 of 2025
- Defaulter: Add Life Pharma Ltd
- Action: Notice of attachment of demat account and mutual fund folio(s)
- Issued by: Recovery Officer, SEBI

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Depositories, stock brokers, and mutual fund institutions are required to freeze the specified demat accounts and mutual fund folios and prevent any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Notice: July 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures against entities failing to meet statutory dues and penalties.
