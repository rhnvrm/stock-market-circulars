## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment for bank and post office accounts under Recovery Certificate No. 8932 of 2025 against M/s. Jay Energy and S. Energies Limited. This regulatory enforcement action is aimed at recovering outstanding dues, penalties, or fines pursuant to orders passed by the Securities and Exchange Board of India.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 8932 of 2025.
- Defaulter/Noticee: M/s. Jay Energy and S. Energies Limited.
- Action involves attachment of bank accounts, post office accounts, and other financial holdings.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of unpaid penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and post offices are required to freeze the accounts and assets of the noticee as specified in the attachment notice and prevent any debits or unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate / Notice: July 25, 2025

## Impact Assessment

Highlights strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures taken by SEBI against defaulting entities to enforce compliance and realize dues.
