## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7712 of 2024 against Subhalakshmi M S in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd. This enforcement action is taken to recover outstanding dues and disgorgement amounts ordered by SEBI following investigations into financial misstatements and fraudulent activities by Arvind Remedies Ltd. and connected entities.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7712 of 2024.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Subhalakshmi M S.
- Associated Entity: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of SEBI on October 13, 2026.
- Aims to recover unpaid disgorgement amounts and statutory penalties ordered in previous SEBI proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery measures implemented pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of monetary penalties and disgorgement of wrongful gains.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and market intermediaries are required to freeze accounts, lockers, and holdings of the noticee to prevent transfer or withdrawal of assets as mandated by the attachment notice.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice of Attachment: October 13, 2026
- Recovery Certificate Number: RC No. 7712 of 2024

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's rigorous pursuit of monetary recoveries and enforcement actions against individuals connected to financial fraud and market manipulation cases.
