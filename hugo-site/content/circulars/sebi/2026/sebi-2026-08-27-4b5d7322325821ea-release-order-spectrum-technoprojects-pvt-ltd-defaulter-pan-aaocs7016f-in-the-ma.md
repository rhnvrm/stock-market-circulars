## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order in favor of Spectrum Technoprojects Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAOCS7016F) in the matter of dealing in illiquid stock options at BSE, withdrawing or concluding Recovery Certificate No. 8899 of 2025.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued by SEBI Recovery Officer.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Spectrum Technoprojects Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAOCS7016F).
- Recovery Certificate No.: 8899 of 2025.
- Context: Dealing in illiquid stock options at the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).

## Regulatory Changes

Withdrawal of recovery proceedings and lifting of attachment notices upon satisfaction of dues under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions and banks are notified of the release of attachment on accounts or properties of the entity under the specified recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 27, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates the conclusion of enforcement and recovery actions for the specified entity regarding past illiquid stock option trading violations on BSE.
