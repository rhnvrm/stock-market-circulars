## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice dated September 30, 2026, regarding the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 4723 of 2022 drawn against Veera Kumar Das Surapanani in the matter of Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd.

## Key Points

- Completion of Recovery Certificate No. 4723 of 2022.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Veera Kumar Das Surapanani.
- Matter: Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Formal conclusion of recovery proceedings pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 following the settlement or realization of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Release of any previously attached bank accounts, properties, or assets of the noticee corresponding to the completed recovery certificate, as per SEBI regulations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Completion Notice / Order: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates the resolution and closure of recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI in the matter of Venus Power Ventures (India) Ltd.
