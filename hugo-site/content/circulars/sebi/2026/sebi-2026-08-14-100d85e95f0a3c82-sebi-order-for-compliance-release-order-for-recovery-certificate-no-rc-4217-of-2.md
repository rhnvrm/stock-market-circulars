## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order concerning Recovery Certificate No. 4217 of 2021 drawn against Mr. Mragendra Singh Baghel in the matter of Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited. This follows ongoing recovery proceedings and enforcement actions related to illegal collective investment schemes operated by the company.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued regarding Recovery Certificate No. 4217 of 2021.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Mr. Mragendra Singh Baghel.
- Company involved: Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Administrative enforcement and recovery order update under the SEBI Act, 1992 pursuant to recovery proceedings and asset attachment/release procedures.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant financial institutions and authorities are advised of the status update regarding the recovery certificate and attachment release.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects ongoing administrative and legal proceedings managed by SEBI's recovery division regarding entities associated with Sai Prakash Properties Development Limited.
