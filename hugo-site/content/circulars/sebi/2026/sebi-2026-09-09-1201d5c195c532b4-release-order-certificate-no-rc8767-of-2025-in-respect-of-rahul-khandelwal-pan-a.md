## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order bearing Certificate No. RC8767 of 2025 in respect of Rahul Khandelwal (PAN: AWPPK7085E) associated with Getrise Infotech Pvt Ltd. The order formally lifts the recovery proceedings and attachment measures initiated earlier.

## Key Points

- **Noticee:** Rahul Khandelwal (Director/Entity linked to Getrise Infotech Pvt Ltd).
- **Certificate Reference:** RC8767 of 2025.
- **Action:** Release of attachment/recovery proceedings by SEBI following clearance or satisfaction of dues.
- **Underlying Matter:** Dealings in illiquid stock options at BSE.

## Regulatory Changes

- No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement-related administrative closure order.

## Compliance Requirements

- No further compliance actions required under this specific recovery certificate as dues/proceedings stand released.

## Important Dates

- **Order Date:** September 9, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** Nil, as this is an isolated individual recovery release order.
- **Operational Impact:** Concludes the specific recovery case for the concerned individual.
