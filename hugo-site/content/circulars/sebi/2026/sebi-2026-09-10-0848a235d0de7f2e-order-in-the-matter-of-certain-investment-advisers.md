## Summary

SEBI has issued an order under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Regulation 30A of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008 against 16 Investment Advisers for failing to pay their mandatory renewal fees as required under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013.

## Key Points

- Noticees No. 1 to 16 are registered Investment Advisers with SEBI.
- Under Clause 3 of the Second Schedule of SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, registered Investment Advisers must pay renewal fees every five years within three months before the expiry of the period.
- The listed Noticees failed to pay these renewal fees starting from various due dates ranging from 2019 to 2023.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of statutory renewal fee provisions for registered Investment Advisers under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Regulation 30A of the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.

## Compliance Requirements

- Registered Investment Advisers must ensure timely payment of renewal fees every five years as mandated by the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 to keep their registrations active and in force.

## Important Dates

- Individual renewal fee default dates span from April 29, 2019, through April 10, 2023, depending on the original grant of registration.

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights regulatory strictness regarding ongoing compliance and fee payments by intermediaries, potentially leading to suspension or cancellation of registration for non-compliant entities.
