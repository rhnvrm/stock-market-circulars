## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate number 9297 of 2026, dated September 9, 2026, to Jatin Manubhai Shah (PAN: AEGPS5807M). This action is part of ongoing enforcement proceedings related to regulatory violations and illegal gains concerning Sadhna Broadcast Limited.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate Number: 9297 of 2026.
- Date of Issuance: September 9, 2026.
- Notice Recipient: Jatin Manubhai Shah (PAN: AEGPS5807M).
- Matter: Sadhna Broadcast Limited stock manipulation investigation and recovery of dues/penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of recovery proceedings pursuant to established regulatory violations and disgorgement/penalty orders.

## Compliance Requirements

- The notice recipient is required to immediately clear the outstanding dues as specified in the recovery certificate to avoid further coercive legal measures by SEBI's recovery officers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: September 9, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces SEBI's rigorous pursuit of entities involved in fraudulent activities and pump-and-dump schemes, ensuring accountability and deterrence in the securities market.
