## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated October 1, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 8932 of 2025 issued against M/s. Jay Energy and S. Energies Limited. This order directs the remittance of funds recovered from attached accounts toward the satisfaction of dues owed under the recovery certificate.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued on October 1, 2026.
- Recovery Certificate No.: 8932 of 2025.
- Defaulter/Noticee: M/s. Jay Energy and S. Energies Limited.
- Issued by SEBI Recovery Proceedings for realization of outstanding penalties and dues.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions under the SEBI Act, 1992, empowering recovery officers to issue general remittance orders for attached funds.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and designated custodians are required to comply with the remittance order by transferring attached funds and balances to SEBI's designated recovery account.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: October 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces SEBI's rigorous enforcement framework and execution mechanisms for recovering unpaid regulatory dues and penalties.
