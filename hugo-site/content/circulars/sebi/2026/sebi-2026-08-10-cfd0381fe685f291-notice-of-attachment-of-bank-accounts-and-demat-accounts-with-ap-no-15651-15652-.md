## Summary

SEBI has issued a notice of attachment of bank accounts and demat accounts under Recovery Certificate No. 9226 of 2026 dated August 10, 2026, against Jigar Bhailalbhai Khalash (Defaulter) in relation to trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Attachment notices (AP No. 15651 & 15652 of 2026) issued against defaulter Jigar Bhailalbhai Khalash.
- Initiated under Recovery Certificate No. 9226 of 2026.
- Relates to trading activity in ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of recovery proceedings and attachment of financial assets pursuant to statutory powers under SEBI Act.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and depositories are required to attach the specified bank and demat accounts and prohibit any debits or transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures recovery of dues and enforces regulatory compliance against defaulters involved in market violations.
