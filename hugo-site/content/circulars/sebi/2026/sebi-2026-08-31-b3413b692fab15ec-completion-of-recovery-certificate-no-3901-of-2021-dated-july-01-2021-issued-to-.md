## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has officially recorded the completion of Recovery Certificate No. 3901 of 2021 issued to Sai Refinery (PAN: ABRFS9439H) in the matter of trading in illiquid stock options on BSE.

## Key Points

- Recovery proceedings initiated via Recovery Certificate No. 3901 of 2021 dated July 01, 2021, have been completed.
- Relates to violations involving trading in illiquid stock options on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE).
- SEBI has issued a release order corresponding to the recovery certificate.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an enforcement and recovery completion order specific to an entity.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulter entity and associated financial institutions are notified of the completion of recovery proceedings and the release of attached assets.

## Important Dates

- Original Certificate Date: July 01, 2021
- Completion Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational closure of an enforcement action with negligible broader market impact.
