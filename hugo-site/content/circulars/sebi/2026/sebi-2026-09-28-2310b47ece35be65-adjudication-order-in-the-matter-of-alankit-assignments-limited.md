## Summary

SEBI conducted an inspection of Alankit Assignments Limited, a registered Registrar to an Issue and Share Transfer Agent (RTA), for the period from September 1, 2024, to December 31, 2025. Based on the inspection findings, adjudication proceedings under Section 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992 were initiated for alleged non-compliance with RTA service timelines, code of conduct provisions, and various clauses of the SEBI Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework (CSCRF) for QRTAs.

## Key Points

- Noticee: Alankit Assignments Limited (PAN: AAACA9483E, SEBI Reg. No.: INR000002532).
- Inspection Period: September 01, 2024 to December 31, 2025.
- Alleged Violations: Breach of RTA service timelines, RTA Regulations Code of Conduct, and CSCRF guidelines (Access Controls, Password Policy, Network Security, Data Security).
- Legal Basis: Section 15HB of the SEBI Act, 1992 read with RTA Regulations and CSCRF circular.

## Regulatory Changes

- Strict enforcement of operational timelines and robust IT/cybersecurity infrastructure standards for RTAs under the CSCRF guidelines.

## Compliance Requirements

- RTAs must maintain strict adherence to prescribed service delivery timelines, statutory code of conduct, and comprehensive cybersecurity controls including secure access management, password policies, network security, and data protection.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Dates: February 24–27, 2026 and March 09, 2026.
- Appointment of Adjudicating Officer: August 12, 2026.
- Show Cause Notice (SCN) Date: August 13, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on share transfer agents and emphasizes mandatory compliance with digital security and operational benchmarks.
