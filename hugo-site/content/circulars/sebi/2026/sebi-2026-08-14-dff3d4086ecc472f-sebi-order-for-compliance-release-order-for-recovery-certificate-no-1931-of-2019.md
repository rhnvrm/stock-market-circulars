## Summary

SEBI has issued a release order concerning Recovery Certificate No. 1931 of 2019 against M/s Sai Prakash Organic Food Limited and several associated individuals including Mr. Pushpendra Singh Baghel, Mrs. Pushpanjali Singh Baghel, Mr. Ranvajay Pratap Singh, Mr. Mragendra Singh Baghel, Mr. Dheerendra Krishan Pratap, and Mr. Shahendra Singh Baghel in the matter of unauthorized collective investment schemes.

## Key Points

- Involves Recovery Certificate No. 1931 of 2019 issued against M/s Sai Prakash Organic Food Limited and its directors/promoters.
- Details the release or modification of recovery proceedings in line with ongoing legal/regulatory compliance or developments.
- Part of SEBI's continuous enforcement actions against entities mobilizing funds without proper regulatory registration.

## Regulatory Changes

- Administrative enforcement update concerning asset attachment and recovery certificate execution under SEBI regulations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Affected entities and designated defaulters must adhere to ongoing stipulations set by regulatory authorities and Supreme Court-appointed committees regarding investor claim settlements.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Primarily affects the entities named in the recovery certificate and contributes to the ongoing resolution and restitution process for investors affected by unregistered collective investment schemes.
