## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a notice of attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s) pursuant to Recovery Certificate No. 8932 of 2025 against M/s. Jay Energy and S. Energies Limited.

## Key Points

- Issuance of Recovery Certificate No. 8932 of 2025 by SEBI.
- Attachment notice targeting demat accounts and mutual fund folios.
- Directed against M/s. Jay Energy and S. Energies Limited for failure to pay outstanding dues/penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action in accordance with SEBI recovery procedures for realization of dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulters and financial intermediaries (depositories, mutual funds, RTAs) must comply with the attachment orders and freeze relevant demat accounts and mutual fund folios as specified in the notice.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order/Notice: As per SEBI recovery proceedings (Reference Date: 2025-07-25).

## Impact Assessment

Restricts operations of specified demat accounts and mutual fund folios held by the defaulter until dues are cleared.
