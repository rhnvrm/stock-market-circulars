## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Demand under Recovery Certificate No. RC9281 of 2026 against LCI India Limited (PAN: AAACL5741Q) in the matter of illiquid stock options.

## Key Points

- Issuance of Recovery Certificate No. RC9281 of 2026 by SEBI.
- Target Entity: LCI India Limited (PAN: AAACL5741Q).
- Context involves past violations and non-genuine reversal trades in the illiquid stock options segment.
- Initiation of formal recovery proceedings due to outstanding dues/penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement action pursuant to failure to pay penalties levied under previous adjudication orders regarding illiquid stock options misuse.

## Compliance Requirements

- The entity must immediately clear the outstanding dues as specified in the demand notice to avoid further attachment of bank accounts, properties, or other coercive recovery measures by SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights SEBI's ongoing rigorous enforcement and recovery mechanisms for unpaid penalties arising from historical illiquid stock option market manipulation cases.
