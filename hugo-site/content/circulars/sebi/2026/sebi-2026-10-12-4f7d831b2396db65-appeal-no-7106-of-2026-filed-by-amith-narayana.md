## Summary

An appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 was filed by Amith Narayana against CPIO, SEBI, seeking calculation worksheets, itemized deductions, deficiency remarks, and master database records regarding PACL Certificate No. U267029xxx. SEBI's CPIO stated that the information is not available with SEBI. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's response, noting that SEBI cannot invent information not in its possession and that property disposal and investor repayment for PACL are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee under Supreme Court directions.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought calculation worksheets, itemized breakup, disallowance remarks, and master ledger entries for PACL refund under Certificate No. U267029xxx.
- SEBI stated the requested information is not available with SEBI.
- Appellate Authority upheld the response, citing precedent that CPIO cannot invent information not in possession.
- Repayment and property disposal for PACL are managed by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee pursuant to Supreme Court orders.
