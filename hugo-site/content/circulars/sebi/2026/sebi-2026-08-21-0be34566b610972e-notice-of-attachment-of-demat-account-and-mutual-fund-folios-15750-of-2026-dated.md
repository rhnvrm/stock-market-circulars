## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 21, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9250 of 2026 (arising out of order no. WTM/AN/IVD/ID13/30528/2023-24 dated January 11, 2024) against Saurabh Gupta (PAN: BFEPG0692E). This action pertains to recovery of dues in the matter of trading-based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9250 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Saurabh Gupta (PAN: BFEPG0692E).
- Matter: Trading-based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and disgorgement amounts resulting from illegal stock promotion schemes via social media.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, custodians, depositories, mutual fund AMCs, and financial intermediaries are required to attach and freeze all demat accounts, bank accounts, and mutual fund folio(s) of the defaulter, preventing any debits, transfers, or withdrawals.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing strict regulatory enforcement against entities involved in pump-and-dump stock manipulation schemes propagated via social media platforms.
