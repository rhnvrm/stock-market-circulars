## Summary

The appellant, Sanjay Shah, filed an RTI appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005, challenging SEBI CPIO's refusal to provide certified copies of communications and documents exchanged between SEBI and Gujarat Narmada Valley Fertilizers & Chemicals Limited (GNFC) regarding a grievance (Registration No. PMOPG/E/2024/0044124). The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's decision, stating that information provided by regulated entities to the regulator is held in a fiduciary relationship and is exempt under Section 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- Appellant requested records relating to grievance PMOPG/E/2024/0044124 concerning GNFC, including internal notes, correspondence, and notices between SEBI and GNFC.
- CPIO denied the request under Section 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act, citing fiduciary relationship and regulatory inputs.
- Appellate Authority concurred, noting that regulatory authorities receive documents from entities in a fiduciary capacity.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. The order reinforces existing exemptions under Section 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act regarding information received in a fiduciary capacity.

## Compliance Requirements

- Regulators are not required to disclose confidential regulatory inputs and correspondence provided by regulated entities under RTI Act exemptions.
