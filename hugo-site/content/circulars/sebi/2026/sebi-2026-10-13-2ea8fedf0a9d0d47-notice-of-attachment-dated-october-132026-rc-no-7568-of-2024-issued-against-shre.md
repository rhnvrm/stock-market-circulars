## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated October 13, 2026, under Recovery Certificate (RC) No. 7568 of 2024 (and RC No. 7708 of 2024) drawn against Shreyanse Finance Ltd. This action forms part of ongoing recovery proceedings in the matter of Arvind Remedies Ltd.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 7568 of 2024 and RC No. 7708 of 2024.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Shreyanse Finance Ltd.
- Matter: Arvind Remedies Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) on October 13, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of outstanding dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and relevant intermediaries are required to freeze bank accounts, shares, and other movable/immovable assets of the noticee as specified in the attachment notice.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice of Attachment: October 13, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict regulatory enforcement and asset attachment measures by SEBI to recover dues from entities involved in financial irregularities and violations in the Arvind Remedies Ltd case.
