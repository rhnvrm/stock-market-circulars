## Summary

This order by the Appellate Authority under the Right to Information Act, 2005, addresses Appeal No. 7097 of 2026 filed by Surendra Champalalji Jain against the CPIO, SEBI. The appellant sought certified copies of internal notings, BSE responses, communications, file movement records, and documents regarding the examination and closure of his complaints dated January 31, 2025, and July 8, 2025, specifically concerning the issue of written consent given for acting as Company Secretary and Compliance Officer for a rights issue.

## Key Points

- The appellant filed an RTI application dated August 19, 2026, seeking extensive internal records, file notings, and communications between SEBI and BSE.
- SEBI's CPIO responded on September 16, 2026, leading to the current appeal filed on September 21, 2026.
- The core dispute involves the appellant's complaint regarding whether he provided written consent to act as Company Secretary and Compliance Officer in the draft Letter of Offer dated December 2, 2024.
- The appeal requests certified copies of examination notes, BSE response documents, file movement details, and the alleged written consent.
