## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice against Virtual Business Solution Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAFCV0106J) [Defaulter] in the matter of trading-based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd. under Recovery Certificate No. 9245 of 2026.

## Key Points

- General Remittance Advice issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9245 of 2026.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Virtual Business Solution Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAFCV0106J).
- Matter: Trading-based stock recommendations using social media (YouTube) in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Issued by SEBI Recovery Proceedings.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action and remittance advice pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Financial institutions, banks, and designated intermediaries are advised regarding the remittance and realization of attached funds under the recovery proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issuance / Remittance Advice: October 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing regulatory enforcement, recovery of penalties, and strict action against entities involved in fraudulent stock recommendations and market manipulation in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
