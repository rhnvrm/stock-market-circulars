## Summary

Sunil Kumar filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 (RTI Act) against the CPIO, SEBI's response dated September 23, 2026. The appellant sought information regarding whether SEBI holds records of any monetary payment, financial consideration, or compensation offered to investors concerning the incorrect display of Face Value for debt security ISIN INE583D07448 on NSE interfaces over approximately 18 months. The CPIO had responded that the queries were in the nature of seeking clarification and did not constitute 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- **Appellant:** Sunil Kumar
- **Respondent:** CPIO, SEBI, Mumbai
- **Subject:** RTI application concerning records of financial compensation or settlement related to incorrect Face Value display of debt security ISIN INE583D07448 on NSE interfaces.
- **Grievance Context:** Face value of ISIN INE583D07448 remained unupdated on NSE interfaces for ~18 months following bond price adjustments after redemption/reduction of principal, raised earlier via SCORES complaint nos. SEBIE/UP25/JHAN/046465/1 and SEBIE/UP26/JHAN/016683/1.
- **CPIO Stance:** Information sought is in the nature of clarification and not 'information' u/s 2(f) of the RTI Act.
