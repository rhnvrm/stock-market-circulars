## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order dated September 29, 2026, concerning Recovery Certificate No. 9252 of 2026 drawn against Chiranggi Irish Shah (PAN: CACPS1824G). This relates to enforcement proceedings in the matter of front running involving Navnit Gadoya, Chiranggi Irish Shah, and Surbhi Aggarwal in the scrip of Rajesh Exports Ltd.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9252 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Chiranggi Irish Shah (PAN: CACPS1824G).
- Associated entities: Navnit Gadoya and Surbhi Aggarwal.
- Matter: Front running investigation and recovery proceedings.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Administrative and enforcement update reflecting the release/completion status under SEBI recovery proceedings pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Informs relevant financial institutions, banks, and intermediaries regarding the release status of the recovery certificate and attached accounts/assets of the noticee.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: September 29, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects ongoing resolution and procedural updates in SEBI's recovery actions against individuals involved in market irregularities.
