## Summary

SEBI has issued Notice of Attachment of Bank and Post Office Accounts (Notice No. 15721 of 2026 dated August 20, 2026) against Sadhna Bio Oils Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAKCS4057N) under Recovery Certificate No. 9244 of 2026. This action is taken in the matter of trading-based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Defaulter: Sadhna Bio Oils Pvt. Ltd. (PAN: AAKCS4057N)
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9244 of 2026
- Notice No.: 15721 of 2026
- Date: August 20, 2026
- Context: Investigation involving trading-based stock recommendations via YouTube in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions initiated under SEBI Act, 1992 for recovery of dues arising from illegal market practices.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and post offices are directed to attach all accounts standing in the name of the defaulter and prohibit any debits or withdrawals.

## Important Dates

- Notice Date: August 20, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Freezing of assets and accounts of the defaulter entity to recover illegal gains or penalties associated with fraudulent stock manipulation schemes.
