## Summary

SEBI issued an ex-parte interim order under Sections 11(1), 11(4), and 11B(1) of the SEBI Act, 1992, in the matter of manipulative trades during the Closing Auction Session (CAS) on SENSEX expiry at BSE on August 13, 2026. The order examines the trading activities and patterns of Copthall Mauritius Investment Limited and Mansi Share and Stock Broking Private Limited, identifying coordinated and manipulative trading behavior designed to influence closing prices and impact derivative positions.

## Key Points

- SEBI introduced the Closing Auction Session (CAS) framework via circular dated January 16, 2026, effective August 3, 2026.
- The order details abnormal price spikes during the CAS on SENSEX expiry on August 13, 2026, at BSE.
- Noticees involved include Copthall Mauritius Investment Limited and Mansi Share and Stock Broking Private Limited.
- Analysis reveals correlation between cash segment trading during CAS and Futures & Options (F&O) positions to generate wrongful gains.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement of strict monitoring and surveillance on Closing Auction Session (CAS) trades across stock exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- Noticees and market participants must comply with the interim directions issued by SEBI, preventing them from accessing the securities market or buying/selling/dealing in securities directly or indirectly until further orders.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 13, 2026
- CAS Framework Effective Date: August 3, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Heightened regulatory scrutiny on closing auction trades and derivative expiry positioning, ensuring market integrity and deterring price manipulation during market close.
