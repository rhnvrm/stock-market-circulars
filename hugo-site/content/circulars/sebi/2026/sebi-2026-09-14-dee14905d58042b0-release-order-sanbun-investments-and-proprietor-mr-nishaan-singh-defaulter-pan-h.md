## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order regarding Certificate No. 8362 of 2024 concerning Sanbun Investments and its proprietor, Mr. Nishaan Singh (PAN: HCCPS6377H), lifting prior attachment or recovery restrictions as per the order.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 8362 of 2024.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Sanbun Investments and Proprietor Mr. Nishaan Singh (PAN: HCCPS6377H).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Official release of attachment / recovery proceedings pursuant to compliance or settlement under the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are notified to lift any freezing of accounts or assets previously enforced under the certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

Restores operational status of accounts and assets for the concerned entity following the resolution or closure of recovery proceedings under Certificate No. 8362 of 2024.
