## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9212 of 2026 against Rohit Bairwa (PAN: ELYPB0179Q). This action relates to investigations and recovery proceedings regarding trading activities of certain entities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now known as DU Digital Global Limited).

## Key Points

- General Remittance Order issued under RC No. 9212 of 2026.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Rohit Bairwa (PAN: ELYPB0179Q).
- Matter: Trading activities in the scrip of DU Digital Technologies Limited (now DU Digital Global Limited).
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of dues, penalties, and remittance of attached funds.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and garnishees are required to remit attached funds and comply with SEBI recovery officer directives.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights rigorous regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities involved in market violations in DU Digital Global Limited.
