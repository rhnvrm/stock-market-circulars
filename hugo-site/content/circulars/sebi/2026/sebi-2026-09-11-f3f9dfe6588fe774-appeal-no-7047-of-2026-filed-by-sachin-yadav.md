## Summary

An appeal was filed by Sachin Yadav under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI, regarding the disclosure of candidate marks, response sheets, and evaluated answer scripts related to the SEBI Recruitment Exercise 2025 (Legal Stream, Officer Grade A). The Appellate Authority disposed of the appeal, noting that relevant recruitment details, marks, response sheets, and answer keys had been adequately provided or were accessible via the SEBI website.

## Key Points

- Appellant sought certified copies of candidate-wise scores, phase-wise marks, response sheets, and evaluated answer scripts for the SEBI Recruitment Exercise 2025.
- Respondent previously furnished website links for general recruitment results, specific marks for Paper 2 (Legal Objective and Descriptive), and the candidate's response sheet along with answer keys.
- The Appellate Authority held that queries regarding marks and response sheets were adequately addressed and required no further intervention.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. This is a quasi-judicial order under the RTI Act, 2005 pertaining to an individual information request.
