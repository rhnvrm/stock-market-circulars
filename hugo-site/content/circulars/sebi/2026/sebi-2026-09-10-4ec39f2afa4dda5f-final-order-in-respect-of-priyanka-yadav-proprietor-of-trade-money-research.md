## Summary

SEBI conducted a joint onsite inspection of Ms. Priyanka Yadav, Proprietor of Trade Money Research (Research Analyst Registration No. INH000010991), along with BSE in October 2024. The inspection revealed various violations of the SEBI (Research Analyst) Regulations, 2014, including operations being carried out by a person previously debarred by SEBI, failure to comply with fit and proper criteria, non-disclosure to clients regarding the involvement of a debarred person, and failure to conduct annual audits.

## Key Points

- Noticee: Ms. Priyanka Yadav - Proprietor of Trade Money Research (INH000010991).
- Joint onsite inspection conducted by SEBI and BSE on October 8-9, 2024.
- Key findings include business operations managed by a debarred individual (Sandeep Yadav), violation of fit and proper criteria, lack of transparency with clients, and missing annual audits.
- Proceedings conducted under the SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement action against registered intermediaries violating conditions of registration and employing debarred individuals.

## Compliance Requirements

- Strict adherence to fit and proper criteria under Intermediaries Regulations.
- Prohibition against associating with persons debarred from the securities market.
- Mandatory annual audits and clear client disclosures.

## Important Dates

- Inspection Date: October 08-09, 2024
- Pre-Enquiry SCN Date: April 16, 2025
- Enquiry Report Date: January 09, 2026
- Final Order Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight on research analysts and third-party involvement, ensuring market integrity and protection of investor interests.
