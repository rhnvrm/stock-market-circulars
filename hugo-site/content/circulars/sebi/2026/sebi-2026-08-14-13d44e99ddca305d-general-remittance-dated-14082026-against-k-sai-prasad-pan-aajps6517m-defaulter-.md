## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance notice dated August 14, 2026, against K. Sai Prasad (PAN: AAJPS6517M), designated as a defaulter, under Recovery Certificate No. 9139 of 2026 in the matter of Mediaone Global Entertainment Limited.

## Key Points

- General Remittance notice issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9139 of 2026.
- Defaulter: K. Sai Prasad (PAN: AAJPS6517M).
- Matter: Mediaone Global Entertainment Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions initiated pursuant to the SEBI Act, 1992 for the realization of outstanding dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries must comply with the general remittance and attachment directives regarding the defaulter's assets and funds.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 14, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates ongoing regulatory enforcement and recovery proceedings by SEBI against defaulters connected to Mediaone Global Entertainment Limited.
