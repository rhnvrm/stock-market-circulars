## Summary

SEBI has issued a Prohibitory Order dated September 28, 2026 under Recovery Certificate No. 6955 of 2023 against Mr. Manoj Tulsiram Agrawal. This action relates to recovery of unpaid penalties and dues arising from previous adjudging proceedings in the matter of Kushal Limited.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Order issued under Recovery Certificate No. 6955 of 2023.
- Defaulter / Noticee: Mr. Manoj Tulsiram Agrawal.
- Matter: Enforcement and recovery of dues following investigation and final orders in Kushal Limited.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of outstanding penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, depositories, and market intermediaries are required to freeze accounts, demat accounts, and assets of the noticee as specified in the prohibitory order and prevent unauthorized transfers.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Notice: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates strict regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against entities and individuals defaulting on penalties in connection with violations in Kushal Limited.
