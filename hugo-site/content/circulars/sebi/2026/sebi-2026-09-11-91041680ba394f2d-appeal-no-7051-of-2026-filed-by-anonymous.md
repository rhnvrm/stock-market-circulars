## Summary

An RTI appeal filed against SEBI regarding the availability of official contact details of the Chairman and CEO of KFin Technologies Ltd was disposed of by the Appellate Authority. The Authority upheld the CPIO's response that SEBI does not maintain or possess such information and is not required under the RTI Act to collect or collate non-available information.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought the official email address, phone number, and address of the Chairman and CEO of KFin Technologies Ltd via an RTI application.
- SEBI's CPIO responded that the requested information was not available with SEBI, while providing general contact details for KFin Technologies Ltd.
- Citing Supreme Court and High Court precedents, the Appellate Authority reiterated that the RTI Act only provides access to available and existing information in records and does not obligate public authorities to collect external information.

## Regulatory Changes

- None. The order reinforces established legal principles regarding the scope of information accessible under the RTI Act, 2005.
