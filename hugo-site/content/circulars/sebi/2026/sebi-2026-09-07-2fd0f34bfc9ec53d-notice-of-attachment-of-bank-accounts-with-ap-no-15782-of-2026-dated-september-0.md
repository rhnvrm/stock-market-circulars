## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment of Bank Accounts (Attachment Petition No. 15782 of 2026) dated September 07, 2026, against Mr. Kapil Wadhawan (PAN: AAOPW6145L) in the matter of Dewan Housing Finance Corporation Ltd (DHFL) under Recovery Certificate No. 9277 of 2026.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment of Bank Accounts issued by SEBI.
- Defaulter: Mr. Kapil Wadhawan (PAN: AAOPW6145L).
- Matter: Dewan Housing Finance Corporation Ltd (DHFL).
- Reference: Recovery Certificate No. 9277 of 2026 and Attachment Petition No. 15782 of 2026.
- Date of Notice: September 07, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery proceedings initiated by SEBI to realize outstanding dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks and financial institutions holding accounts in the name of the defaulter are required to attach/freeze the accounts and prohibit any withdrawals or transfers up to the specified recovery amount.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: September 07, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Ensures recovery of dues related to the DHFL collapse, reinforcing regulatory oversight and strict action against financial defaulters.
