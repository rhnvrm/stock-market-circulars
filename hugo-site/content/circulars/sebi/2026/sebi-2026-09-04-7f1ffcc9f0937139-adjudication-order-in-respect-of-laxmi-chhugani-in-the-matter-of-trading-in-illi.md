## Summary

SEBI conducted an investigation into the trading activities in illiquid stock options at BSE for the period between April 1, 2014, and September 30, 2015, which revealed large-scale reversal of trades leading to artificial volumes. Laxmi Chhugani was identified as one of the entities that executed non-genuine trades in stock options contracts, thereby violating provisions of the SEBI (Prohibition of Fraudulent and Unfair Trade Practices Relating to Securities Market) Regulations, 2003 (PFUTP Regulations).

## Key Points

- SEBI observed that 81.40% of all trades in the stock options segment of BSE during the investigation period were non-genuine reversal trades creating artificial volume.
- The Noticee (Laxmi Chhugani) allegedly executed 6 non-genuine trades across 2 stock options contracts, resulting in an artificial volume of 8,60,000 units.
- Proceedings were initiated under Section 15-I of the SEBI Act, 1992, read with Rule 5 of the SEBI Adjudication Rules and Section 15HA.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement action under SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations, 2003, for fraudulent and unfair trade practices relating to securities markets.

## Compliance Requirements

- Market participants must avoid executing non-genuine reversal trades, wash trades, or any transactions that create artificial volume or false appearance of trading in the securities market.

## Important Dates

- Investigation Period: April 1, 2014 to September 30, 2015
- Show Cause Notice Date: August 03, 2021
- Order Date: September 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Reinforces regulatory oversight against market manipulation and artificial volume generation in illiquid derivatives segments.
