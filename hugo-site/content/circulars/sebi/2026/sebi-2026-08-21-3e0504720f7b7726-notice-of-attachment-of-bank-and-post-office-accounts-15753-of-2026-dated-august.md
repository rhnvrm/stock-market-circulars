## Summary

SEBI has issued a Notice of Attachment dated August 21, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9253 of 2026 against Paras Shah (PAN: DOKPS0203A) in the matter of trading based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd. The recovery officer has ordered the attachment of bank and post office accounts to recover dues.

## Key Points

- Notice of Attachment issued under Recovery Certificate No. 9253 of 2026.
- Defaulter: Paras Shah (PAN: DOKPS0203A).
- Matter: Trading based stock recommendations using social media YouTube in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Action: Attachment of bank and post office accounts for recovery of dues under SEBI Act, 1992.

## Regulatory Changes

Strict enforcement action and recovery measures pursuant to SEBI orders against fraudulent stock recommendations and market manipulation via social media.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, post offices, and financial institutions are required to prohibit any debits or transfers from the attached accounts of the defaulter except with the explicit permission of SEBI.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight and punitive measures against individuals engaged in digital pump-and-dump schemes and unlawful stock recommendations.
