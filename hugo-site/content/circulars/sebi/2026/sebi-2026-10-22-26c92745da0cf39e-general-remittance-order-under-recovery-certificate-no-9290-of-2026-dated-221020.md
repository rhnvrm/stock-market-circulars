## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated October 22, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9290 of 2026 against 21 noticees/defaulters (including Harsha Ishvarbhai Solanki, Khushbu Arjunbhai Padhiyar, Nitaben Babulal Khalas, B B Commercial Ltd, and others) in the matter of trading activity in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited. The order directs banks and financial institutions to remit/attach funds and assets to recover dues under the Recovery Certificate.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9290 of 2026 dated October 22, 2026.
- Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited (Ticker: AISL).
- Noticees / Defaulters: 21 entities including Harsha Ishvarbhai Solanki, Khushbu Arjunbhai Padhiyar, Nitaben Babulal Khalas, Nimeshkumar Baldevbhai Parmar, Taraben Kirankumar Padhiyar, Ravi Dipakbhai Barupal, Rakeshbhai Kishanbhai Pandya, Jayesh Ganpatlal Pansal, Rajaniben Jayeshbhai Pansal, Bhavika Prakash Rathod, Rita Arvindbhai Rangi, Badamilal Banshilal Garg, Lataben Narotambhai Rangi, Navin Shankarlal Tank, Vishnu Shantibhai Parmar, Komal Vaghaji Chauhan, Dayaben Marvadi, B B Commercial Ltd, Bhargaviben Shamalbhai Raval, Prakash Chunilal Mudethiya, and Ramnath Sharma.
- Action: General Remittance Order issued by SEBI Recovery Officer for realization of dues and penalties.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement and recovery actions pursuant to Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992, for the recovery of penalties, disgorgement amounts, or dues arising from market irregularities and trading violations.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, garnishees, and designated entities are required to immediately attach, freeze, and remit funds, bank accounts, or financial holdings of the specified noticees up to the demanded recovery amount.

## Important Dates

- Date of Recovery Certificate and Order: October 22, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates SEBI's active enforcement posture in executing recovery proceedings and attaching assets of entities involved in irregular trading activities in listed scrips like ANI Integrated Services Limited.
