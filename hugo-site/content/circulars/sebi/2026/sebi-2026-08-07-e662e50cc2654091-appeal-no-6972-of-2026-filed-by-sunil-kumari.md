## Summary

The SEBI Appellate Authority, in its order dated August 07, 2026, dismissed Appeal No. 6972 of 2026 filed by Sunil Kumari against the Central Public Information Officer (CPIO), SEBI. The appellant had requested calculations, status, and timelines of her refund application for PACL (Pearls Agrotech Corporation Ltd.). The CPIO advised the appellant to approach the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee, which is tasked with disposal of properties and repayment to investors. The Appellate Authority upheld the CPIO's decision, citing established precedent that the Lodha Committee is not a public authority under the RTI Act.

## Key Points

- **RTI Application**: Filed on May 29, 2026, seeking details about the computation, current status, and timeline of the appellant's PACL refund application.
- **CPIO Response**: Dated June 12, 2026, advising the appellant that refund matters are handled by the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee, with public notices and status reports available on SEBI's website.
- **Appeal Grounds**: The appellant claimed that since the RTI application was addressed to the Nodal Officer of the Justice Lodha Committee, directing her to approach the Committee directly was incorrect.
- **Legal Precedents**: The Appellate Authority noted that the Committee was constituted under Supreme Court orders on February 2, 2016. Furthermore, the Central Information Commission (CIC) in *M Shanmugam v. CPIO, Pearls Agrotech Corporation Ltd. & Or.* (March 14, 2024) held that the Justice Lodha Committee is not a public authority under Section 2(h) of the RTI Act.
- **Outcome**: The appeal was dismissed.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no regulatory changes. The order reaffirms established legal and procedural limits under the Right to Information Act, 2005.

## Compliance Requirements

There are no action points or compliance requirements for regulated market entities. The order relates to individual RTI processing.

## Important Dates

- **May 29, 2026**: Date of the appellant's RTI application.
- **June 12, 2026**: Date of CPIO's reply.
- **July 06, 2026**: Date of the RTI appeal.
- **August 07, 2026**: Date of the Appellate Authority's order dismissing the appeal.

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: None. This is an administrative adjudication under the RTI Act.
- **Investor Impact**: PACL investors seeking refund details must interact directly with the Justice (Retd.) R.M. Lodha Committee's official help channels and portals, as queries cannot be handled through SEBI RTI applications.
