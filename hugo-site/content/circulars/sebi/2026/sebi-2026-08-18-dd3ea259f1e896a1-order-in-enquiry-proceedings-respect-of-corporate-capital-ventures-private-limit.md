## Summary

SEBI conducted an inspection of Corporate Capital Ventures Private Limited, a registered merchant banker, covering the period from April 01, 2021 to January 31, 2023, and initiated enquiry proceedings regarding multiple alleged regulatory violations including submission of forged documents, non-updation of track records, and NISM certification non-compliances.

## Key Points

- Noticee: Corporate Capital Ventures Private Limited (SEBI Registration No. INM000012276).
- Period of inspection: April 01, 2021 to January 31, 2023.
- Allegations include submission of forged/fake certificates during inspection, non-updation of track record, non-compliance with NISM certification requirements, and failure to submit correct information related to previous inspections.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement and proceedings under Section 12(3) of the SEBI Act, 1992 and Regulation 27 of SEBI (Intermediaries) Regulations, 2008.

## Compliance Requirements

- Merchant bankers must ensure strict adherence to ICDR Regulations, MB Regulations, PFUTP Regulations, and Code of Conduct stipulations.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: August 2026.
- Inspection Period: April 01, 2021 to January 31, 2023.

## Impact Assessment

- Highlights regulatory scrutiny on merchant banking operations, due diligence, and maintenance of accurate track records and compliance documentation.
