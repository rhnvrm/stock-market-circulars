## Summary

SEBI Appellate Authority disposed of Appeal No. 6975 of 2026 filed by RM Nagappan under the RTI Act, 2005, regarding queries on the status of his SCORES complaint and CPGRAMS appeals.

## Key Points

- The appellant sought details regarding the receipt date, status, delay reasons, responsible officers, and internal file notings for his second request/review concerning SCORES Complaint No. SEBIE/TN26/TIRU/012360/1.
- SEBI's CPIO informed that the SCORES complaint was assigned for second-level review on 06.04.2026, while certain CPGRAMS appeals were either not received or under examination.
- The CPIO noted that complaint details and history are accessible to the complainant directly via the SCORES platform.
- Information regarding officers' names and designations was held to be exempt under Section 8(1)(j) of the RTI Act as personal information relating to internal functioning.
- Queries seeking reasons for delay were considered clarifications and thus fell outside the definition of 'information' under Section 2(f) of the RTI Act.
