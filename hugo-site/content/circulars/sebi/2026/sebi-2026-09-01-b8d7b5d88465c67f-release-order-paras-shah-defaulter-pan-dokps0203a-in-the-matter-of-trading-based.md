## Summary

SEBI has issued a Release Order under Certificate No. 9253 of 2026 against Paras Shah (PAN: DOKPS0203A) in the matter of trading-based stock recommendations using social media (YouTube) in the scrip of Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.

## Key Points

- Release Order issued under Certificate No. 9253 of 2026.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Paras Shah (PAN: DOKPS0203A).
- Matter: Trading-based stock recommendations using YouTube in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI).

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement actions and subsequent release/resolution under recovery proceedings of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

Relief and vacation of attachment orders on accounts and assets as applicable upon the issuance of the release order.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: September 1, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Reflects the conclusion or adjustment of specific recovery proceedings against individuals involved in social media-based stock pumping schemes in Sadhna Broadcast Ltd.
