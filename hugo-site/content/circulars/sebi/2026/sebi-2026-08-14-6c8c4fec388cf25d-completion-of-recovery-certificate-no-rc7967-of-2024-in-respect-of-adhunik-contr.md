The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a completion of Recovery Certificate No. RC7967 of 2024 and release order in respect of Adhunik Contractors Private Limited (PAN: AAGCA9909H) in the matter of Illiquid Stock Options.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: RC7967 of 2024
- Entity Name: Adhunik Contractors Private Limited
- PAN: AAGCA9909H
- Matter: Illiquid Stock Options proceedings and dues recovery
- Status: Recovery proceedings completed and dues settled/resolved

No new regulatory changes introduced; this is an enforcement and recovery completion action.

Company-specific resolution with negligible broader market impact.
