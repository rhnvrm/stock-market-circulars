## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a notice of attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s) 14919(3) dated August 17, 2026, against Heena Khatoon, Proprietor of Heena Enterprises (PAN: FGXPK5721G) [Defaulter], under Recovery Certificate No. 8858 of 2025 in the matter of Continental Seeds and Chemicals Limited.

## Key Points

- **Defaulter Entity:** Heena Khatoon, Proprietor of Heena Enterprises (PAN: FGXPK5721G).
- **Matter:** Continental Seeds and Chemicals Limited (CSCL).
- **Action Taken:** Attachment of demat accounts and mutual fund folio(s) for non-payment of penalties/dues.
- **Recovery Certificate:** No. 8858 of 2025.
- **Date of Notice:** August 17, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement of recovery proceedings under SEBI Act, 1992, empowering the Recovery Officer to attach movable and immovable properties, including demat accounts and mutual fund folios, to recover outstanding dues and penalties.

## Compliance Requirements

Defaulters and financial intermediaries (depositories, depository participants, and mutual fund houses) are required to comply with the attachment orders and freeze debits/transfers from the specified demat accounts and mutual fund folios.

## Important Dates

- **Notice Date:** August 17, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Strict enforcement against entities involved in fraudulent trading practices, ensuring recovery of penalties and upholding market integrity.
