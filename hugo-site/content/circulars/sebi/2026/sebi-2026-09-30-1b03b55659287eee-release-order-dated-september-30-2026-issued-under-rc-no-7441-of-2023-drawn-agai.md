## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Release Order dated September 30, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 7441 of 2023 drawn against Keynote Enterprises Pvt Ltd in the matter of TV Vision Ltd. This order officially concludes/releases the recovery proceedings initiated under the said certificate.

## Key Points

- Release Order / Cancellation of Recovery Certificate No. 7441 of 2023.
- Defaulter/Noticee: Keynote Enterprises Pvt Ltd.
- Matter: TV Vision Ltd.
- Issued by the Recovery Officer of the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) on September 30, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

Withdrawal and release of recovery enforcement measures previously instituted under the SEBI Act, 1992 for the specified recovery certificate.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant banks, financial institutions, and authorities are notified of the release of attachment and withdrawal of recovery proceedings under RC No. 7441 of 2023 against the entity.

## Important Dates

- Date of Release Order: September 30, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides legal relief and resolution of specific recovery proceedings for Keynote Enterprises Pvt Ltd in relation to TV Vision Ltd.
