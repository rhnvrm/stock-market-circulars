## Summary

SEBI issued a final order in the matter of Modex International Securities Ltd. (MISL), a registered stock broker, along with its whole-time executive directors Mr. Dharmendra Kumar Arora and Mr. Pavan Kumar Sachdeva. Investigations and forensic audits revealed serious violations, including the falsification of the Register of Securities (RoS), diversion and misappropriation of client securities and funds, misrepresentation of books and accounts, and periodic payments to certain clients in the nature of returns on investments.

## Key Points

- Forensic audit and offsite supervision by NSE and SEBI uncovered massive falsification of the Register of Securities (RoS) by MISL.
- Identified significant diversion, misappropriation, and shortfall of clients' securities and funds.
- Uncovered misrepresentation of books and accounts through incorrect adjustments in RoS and client financial ledgers.
- Payments resembling returns on investments were periodically made to certain clients.

## Regulatory Changes

[Enforcement and penalty actions pursuant to SEBI Act, 1992 and SCRA, 1956 provisions]

## Compliance Requirements

Stock brokers must strictly maintain accurate segregation of client funds and securities, avoid falsifying registers of securities, and comply with all SEBI circulars regarding client account integrity.

## Important Dates

- Order Date: August 2026
- Forensic Audit Period: April 01, 2017 to January 24, 2020
- Interim Order Date: April 30, 2020

## Impact Assessment

Reinforces regulatory oversight and strict enforcement against stock brokers engaging in misappropriation of client assets and falsification of financial records.
