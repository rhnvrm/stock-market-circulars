## Summary

SEBI has announced the completion of Recovery Certificate No. RC9281 of 2026 issued in respect of LCI India Limited (PAN: AAACL5741Q) in the matter of Illiquid Stock Options.

## Key Points

- Entity Name: LCI India Limited
- PAN: AAACL5741Q
- Recovery Certificate Number: RC9281 of 2026
- Matter: Illiquid Stock Options proceedings and recovery of dues.

## Regulatory Changes

No general regulatory changes; this is an enforcement and recovery completion action against a specific defaulter/entity.

## Compliance Requirements

Entities involved in past recovery proceedings must ensure full clearance of dues as specified by SEBI recovery officers.

## Important Dates

- Certificate/Notice Date: August 2026
- Completion Date: August 31, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Minimal systemic impact as this is an isolated recovery completion for an individual entity under regulatory scanner for illiquid stock options.
