## Summary

SEBI issued a summary settlement order regarding Harbourfront India Trust (a Category II AIF), its manager Harbourfront India Investment Advisory LLP, and key managerial personnel (Rohit Chokhani, Sho Nakagawa, and Masaki Misono) for failing to maintain the requisite sponsor continuing interest of 2.5% of the fund corpus between September 12, 2023, and April 11, 2025. The applicants settled the enforcement proceedings upon payment of ₹12,75,000 jointly and severally.

## Key Points

- Harbourfront India Trust maintained a sponsor commitment of 2.44% (₹2 crore against an ₹82 crore corpus) instead of the mandated 2.5% from September 12, 2023, to April 11, 2025.
- The sponsor commitment was enhanced to ₹2.05 crore (2.5% of the corpus) via an addendum on April 11, 2025.
- Violations were identified under Regulation 10(d) of the AIF Regulations, Regulation 20(1) and 20(5), and the Code of Conduct for Managers.
- Applicants settled the matter under Chapter VII of the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018 by remitting ₹12,75,000.

## Regulatory Changes

- Enforcement proceedings disposed of pursuant to the settlement application under SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018.

## Compliance Requirements

- Sponsors of Alternative Investment Funds must continuously maintain the minimum prescribed continuing interest percentage throughout the fund's lifecycle.

## Important Dates

- September 12, 2023: Final close of the Fund / commencement of non-compliance period
- April 11, 2025: Contribution agreement addendum executed enhancing sponsor commitment to 2.5%
- April 29, 2026: SEBI issued Notice of Summary Settlement
- May 28, 2026: Applicants filed settlement application and remitted settlement amount

## Impact Assessment

- Concludes regulatory action for past non-compliance regarding sponsor commitment without protracted legal proceedings.
