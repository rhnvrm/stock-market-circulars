## Summary

SEBI has issued a general remittance notice dated September 24, 2026, under Recovery Certificate No. 9229 of 2026 against Rakeshbhai K Pandya (PAN: CUMPP3101K). This enforcement action pertains to recovery proceedings initiated regarding trading activities in the scrip of ANI Integrated Services Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice Type: General Remittance / Recovery Proceeding
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9229 of 2026
- Defaulter / Noticee: Rakeshbhai K Pandya (PAN: CUMPP3101K)
- Scrip: ANI Integrated Services Limited (AISL)
- Issuing Authority: Recovery Officer, Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992, for the realization of dues, penalties, or disgorgement amounts.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and intermediaries are required to comply with remittance directions, freeze accounts/assets as specified, and prevent unauthorized transfer of funds or securities belonging to the defaulter.

## Important Dates

- Date of Remittance / Notice: September 24, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Highlights stringent regulatory enforcement and recovery measures by SEBI against individuals involved in market violations or unpaid dues in ANI Integrated Services Limited.
