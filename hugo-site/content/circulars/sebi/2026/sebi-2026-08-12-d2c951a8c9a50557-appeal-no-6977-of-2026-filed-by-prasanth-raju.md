## Summary

The Appellate Authority of SEBI considered Appeal No. 6977 of 2026 filed by Prasanth Raju, challenging the response of SEBI's Central Public Information Officer (CPIO). The appellant had sought a copy of SEBI's letter dated May 29, 2026, which imposed a financial disincentive of Rs 6.04 crore on the National Stock Exchange (NSE) for a technical glitch occurring in April 2024. The CPIO had denied the information, citing exemptions under Sections 8(1)(d) and 8(1)(e) of the RTI Act. The Appellate Authority upheld this denial, concurring that the correspondence contains sensitive information regarding systemic vulnerabilities and is held in a fiduciary capacity.

## Key Points

- **Information Sought**: Copy of SEBI's letter dated May 29, 2026, to the National Stock Exchange (NSE) imposing a financial disincentive of Rs 6.04 crore due to a technical glitch in April 2024.
- **Refusal Grounds**: The information was classified as confidential and sensitive as it pertains to systemic vulnerabilities. It is held in a fiduciary capacity, and its disclosure could harm the competitive position of market participants.
- **Exemptions Applied**: Sections 8(1)(d) and 8(1)(e) of the Right to Information Act, 2005.
- **Appellate Finding**: SEBI receives references, investigations, and other documents from market entities in a fiduciary relationship, which are exempt from disclosure to protect market integrity.
- **Precedents Cited**:
  - *Mr. Ashok Kumar Rajak vs. CPIO, SEBI* (December 21, 2021) regarding the exemption of investigation reports and third-party correspondences under Section 8(1)(e) & (j).
  - *Mehmood Mehboob Shaikh vs. CPIO, SEBI* (November 11, 2025) upholding denial of copies of notices served on third parties.

## Regulatory Changes

There are no new regulatory changes introduced. This decision reinforces existing practices regarding non-disclosure of critical exchange operations, penalties, and systemic vulnerability reports under the RTI framework.

## Compliance Requirements

No compliance action is required for market participants. The order establishes a protective precedent for exchanges and intermediaries regarding the confidentiality of technical audits and regulatory correspondences concerning systemic failures.

## Important Dates

- **April 2024**: Technical glitch on NSE in a stock.
- **May 29, 2026**: SEBI letter imposing a financial disincentive of Rs 6.04 crore on NSE.
- **June 17, 2026**: Date of the initial RTI application.
- **July 13, 2026**: Response from CPIO, SEBI denying the request.
- **July 15, 2026**: Filing date of the appeal (Reg. No. SEBIH/A/E/26/00254).

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact**: Low. It reassures market infrastructure institutions (MIIs) that sensitive technical and operational reviews shared with the regulator will remain confidential.
- **Operational Impact**: Maintains confidentiality over the root cause analysis and technical details of system failures, minimizing the risk of exposing security or operational vulnerabilities to the public.
