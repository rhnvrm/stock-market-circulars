## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Order dated September 16, 2026, in Recovery Certificate No. 9252 of 2026 against Chiranggi Irish Shah (PAN: CACPS1824G) as part of enforcement and recovery proceedings in the matter of front running involving Navnit Gadoya, Chiranggi Irish Shah, and Surbhi Aggarwal.

## Key Points

- Recovery Certificate No.: 9252 of 2026.
- Noticee/Defaulter: Chiranggi Irish Shah (PAN: CACPS1824G).
- Related Matter: Front Running by Navnit Gadoya, Chiranggi Irish Shah and Surbhi Aggarwal.
- Order Type: General Remittance Order.

## Regulatory Changes

N/A

## Compliance Requirements

Entities holding funds, assets, or dues payable to the noticee are required to comply with the remittance directions as specified in the recovery certificate.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order: September 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enforcement action directed towards recovery of dues associated with market malpractice and fraudulent trading practices (front running).
