## Summary

SEBI has issued Prohibitory Orders under Recovery Certificate Nos. 6958 and 6969 of 2023 against Namrata Sandeep Agrawal in the matter of Kushal Limited. The order restrains the noticee from transferring, alienating, or dealing with assets and bank accounts to recover dues owed under the recovery certificates.

## Key Points

- Prohibitory Orders issued under Recovery Certificate Nos. 6958 and 6969 of 2023.
- Noticee / Defaulter: Namrata Sandeep Agrawal.
- Matter: Kushal Limited.
- Objective: Recovery of dues through attachment and prohibitory orders on bank accounts and properties.

## Regulatory Changes

Enforcement action pursuant to recovery proceedings under the SEBI Act, 1992 for realization of penalties and dues.

## Compliance Requirements

Banks, financial institutions, and persons holding assets or funds belonging to the noticee are prohibited from releasing or transferring them without SEBI's prior permission.

## Important Dates

- Date of Order / Issuance: September 28, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Demonstrates rigorous enforcement and recovery mechanisms employed by SEBI against defaulters in listed company matters.
