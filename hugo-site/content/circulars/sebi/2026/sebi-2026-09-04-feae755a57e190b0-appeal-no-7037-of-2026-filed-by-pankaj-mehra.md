## Summary

Pankaj Mehra filed an appeal under the Right to Information Act, 2005 against the CPIO, SEBI regarding refund claims submitted under the SEBI PACL Refund Process for PACL certificates (Certificate Nos. U105181xxx and U105187xxx). The appellant sought status updates, date-wise movement history, recorded reasons, and supporting documents relating to land allotment claims and illegibility/incorrectness statuses assigned to the certificates.

## Key Points

- The appellant's mother submitted refund applications under the SEBI PACL Refund Process which remained unresolved or faced issues.
- Claim 1 status indicated that land had been allotted, but the appellant stated no allotment letter or documents were provided.
- Claim 2 status was updated indicating the uploaded PACL certificate was incorrect or illegible without prior deficiency notice or opportunity to clarify.
- The RTI application sought 11 specific items of information including current status, file notings, reasons, date-wise action taken, and copies of communications.
