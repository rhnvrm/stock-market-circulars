## Summary

SEBI has issued a General Remittance Advice under Recovery Certificate No. 9159 of 2026 against Sapna, proprietor of Stallion Trading Co. (PAN: DRUPS8079D), in connection with trading activities and regulatory dues related to the scrip of Delta Leasing and Finance Ltd.

## Key Points

- Defaulter: Sapna (Proprietor of Stallion Trading Co.)
- PAN: DRUPS8079D
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9159 of 2026
- Matter: Trading activities and violations concerning Delta Leasing and Finance Ltd.
- Action: General Remittance Advice issued for recovery of dues.

## Regulatory Changes

None. This is an enforcement and recovery action against an individual entity.

## Compliance Requirements

Relevant financial intermediaries, banks, and institutions must comply with attachment and remittance directions as specified under the recovery proceedings.

## Important Dates

- Issue Date: September 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Operational impact is restricted to the entities and accounts directly linked to the named defaulter.
