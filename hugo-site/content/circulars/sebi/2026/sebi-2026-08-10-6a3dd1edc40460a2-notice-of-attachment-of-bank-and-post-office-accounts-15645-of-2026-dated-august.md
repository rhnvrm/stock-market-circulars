## Summary

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has issued a Notice of Attachment of Bank and Post Office Accounts (Notice No. 15645 of 2026, dated August 10, 2026) against Mr. Vivek Seth (PAN: AACPS3800Q), designated as the Defaulter, under Recovery Certificate No. 9217 of 2026 in the matter of Oriental Trimex Limited.

## Key Points

- Notice issued by SEBI Recovery and Refund Department.
- Target: Mr. Vivek Seth (PAN: AACPS3800Q).
- Connected Matter: Oriental Trimex Limited.
- Recovery Certificate No.: 9217 of 2026.
- Action: Attachment of bank and post office accounts to recover outstanding dues.

## Regulatory Changes

No new regulatory changes; enforcement action executed under existing statutory recovery powers pursuant to Section 28A of the SEBI Act, 1992.

## Compliance Requirements

All banks, post offices, and financial institutions holding accounts in the name of the defaulter are prohibited from permitting any debits or withdrawals from the attached accounts.

## Important Dates

- Date of Notice: August 10, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Enforcement action ensuring recovery of pending dues and penalties associated with financial misstatements and regulatory non-compliance in Oriental Trimex Limited.
