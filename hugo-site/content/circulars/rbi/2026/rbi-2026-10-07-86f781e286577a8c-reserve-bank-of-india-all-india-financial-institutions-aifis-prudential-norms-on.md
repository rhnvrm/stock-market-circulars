## Summary

Reserve Bank of India has issued the Reserve Bank of India (All India Financial Institutions (AIFIs) – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Fifth Amendment Directions, 2026, amending the framework for clearing member financial institutions to align with international standards.

## Key Points

- Amends paragraph 77(6)(i) of the RBI (AIFIs - Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Directions, 2025.
- Prescribes a 2 per cent risk weight for AIFI trade exposures to a QCCP for own purposes in OTC derivatives, exchange traded derivatives, and SFTs.
- Extends the 2 per cent risk weight to client clearing services where the clearing member AIFI is obligated to reimburse clients for losses upon QCCP default.

## Regulatory Changes

Sub-paragraph (a) of paragraph 77(6)(i) has been replaced to clearly define the 2 per cent risk weight application on trade exposures to QCCPs for both proprietary and qualifying client-clearing transactions.

## Compliance Requirements

All India Financial Institutions acting as clearing members of QCCPs must adopt the revised 2 per cent risk-weighting methodology for eligible trade exposures across OTC derivatives, exchange-traded derivatives, and SFTs.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issue: October 07, 2026
- Effective Date: October 07, 2026 (came into effect from the date of issue)

## Impact Assessment

Enhances the regulatory framework for AIFIs by aligning domestic capital adequacy norms for central clearing exposures with international standards.
