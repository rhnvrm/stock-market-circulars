## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has released the auction results for 91-Day, 182-Day, and 364-Day Treasury Bills. The notified face values were ₹8,000 Crore, ₹8,000 Crore, and ₹7,000 Crore respectively, with total face value accepted recorded as NIL across all tenors.

## Key Points

- 91-Day T-Bill Notified Amount: ₹8,000 Crore; Accepted: NIL
- 182-Day T-Bill Notified Amount: ₹8,000 Crore; Accepted: NIL
- 364-Day T-Bill Notified Amount: ₹7,000 Crore; Accepted: NIL
- Cut-off prices and implicit yields were reported as NA due to nil acceptance.
