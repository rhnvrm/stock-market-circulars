## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has issued the Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks - Miscellaneous) Amendment Directions, 2026, deleting Section C on 'Limits for Non-Option Derivative Contracts' of Chapter IV from the 2025 Directions.

## Key Points

- Section C on 'Limits for Non-Option Derivative Contracts' (paragraph 37) of Chapter IV of the Reserve Bank of India (Small Finance Banks - Miscellaneous) Directions, 2025 stands deleted.
- Issued under section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
- Instructions come into effect immediately upon issuance.

## Regulatory Changes

- Paragraph 37 (Section C: Limits for Non-Option Derivative Contracts) of Chapter IV of the Small Finance Banks - Miscellaneous Directions, 2025 has been removed entirely.

## Compliance Requirements

- Small Finance Banks should take note of the deletion of non-option derivative contract limits and adjust their risk management and compliance frameworks accordingly.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 19, 2026 (Immediate effect)

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines regulatory requirements regarding derivative contracts for Small Finance Banks.
