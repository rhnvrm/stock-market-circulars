## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued the Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Miscellaneous) Second Amendment Directions, 2026, dated October 19, 2026. These directions come into effect immediately upon issuance and formally delete Section C concerning 'Limits for Non-Option Derivative Contracts' from Chapter IV of the Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Miscellaneous) Directions, 2025.

## Key Points

- Issued under Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
- Circular reference: RBI/2026-27/294 (DOR.MRG.REC.No.248/00-00-020/2026-27).
- Effective immediately upon issuance on October 19, 2026.
- Deletes paragraph 37 (Section C on 'Limits for Non-Option Derivative Contracts') of Chapter IV of the 2025 Directions.

## Regulatory Changes

- Section C on 'Limits for Non-Option Derivative Contracts' of Chapter IV of the Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks - Miscellaneous) Directions, 2025 (paragraph 37) stands completely deleted.

## Compliance Requirements

- Commercial banks must update their internal policies, risk management guidelines, and operational frameworks to reflect the removal of limits on non-option derivative contracts under the 2025 Directions.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date / Issuance Date: October 19, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Streamlines the regulatory framework governing derivative contracts for commercial banks by removing specific restrictions on non-option derivative contracts, potentially providing greater operational flexibility or aligning with updated prudential norms.
