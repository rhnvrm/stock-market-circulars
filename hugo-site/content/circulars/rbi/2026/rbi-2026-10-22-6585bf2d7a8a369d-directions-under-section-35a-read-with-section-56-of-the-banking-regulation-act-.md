## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has extended the operational period of directions issued to The Suri Friends’ Union Co-operative Bank Ltd., Suri, West Bengal, by another three months from the close of business on October 22, 2026, to January 22, 2027, subject to review.

## Key Points

- Directions were originally issued under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 on July 21, 2022.
- The extension is implemented in the public interest.
- The extension does not signify any official certification or satisfaction by RBI regarding the financial health of the bank.
- All other terms and conditions of the original directive remain unchanged.

## Regulatory Changes

- Extension of supervisory directions under Section 35A for an additional three months up to January 22, 2027.

## Compliance Requirements

- The bank must continue to comply with all restrictions and instructions outlined in the RBI directive.

## Important Dates

- Extension Effective Date: October 22, 2026
- Extension End Date: January 22, 2027

## Impact Assessment

- Impact is confined to the customers and depositors of The Suri Friends’ Union Co-operative Bank Ltd., with no broader systemic impact on the banking sector.
