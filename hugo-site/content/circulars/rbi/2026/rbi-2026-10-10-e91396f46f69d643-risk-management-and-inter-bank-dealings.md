## Summary

Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued circular RBI/2026-27/291 (A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 25) dated October 10, 2026, introducing stringent guidelines on risk management and inter-bank dealings concerning foreign exchange derivative contracts involving INR. Key measures include prohibiting the rebooking of cancelled derivative contracts, slashing the threshold limit for taking positions without establishing underlying exposure from USD 100 million to USD 5 million, and mandating additional documentation and undertakings.

## Key Points

- **Prohibition on Rebooking:** Authorised Dealers (ADs) are barred from permitting users to rebook any cancelled foreign exchange derivative contracts involving INR (deliverable or non-deliverable).
- **Rollovers Allowed:** Rollover of foreign exchange derivative contracts on maturity continues to be permitted subject to existing master directions.
- **Threshold Reduction:** The threshold limit for taking positions without establishing underlying exposure (for hedging contracted exposure or across recognized stock exchanges) is reduced from USD 100 million to USD 5 million equivalent of notional value outstanding at any point in time.
- **Additional Undertaking:** ADs must obtain and retain an undertaking from users that the underlying exposure has not been hedged with any other AD, or clearly indicate parts hedged across multiple ADs.
- **Record Retention:** ADs are responsible for ensuring compliance and retaining necessary documents for at least two years.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amendment to Master Direction - Risk Management and Inter-Bank Dealings dated July 05, 2016.
- Cancellation and rebooking/rollovers: Rebooking of cancelled FX derivative contracts is strictly prohibited.
- Threshold limits: Reduced from USD 100 million to USD 5 million equivalent across all ADs and recognized stock exchanges.

## Compliance Requirements

- Authorised Dealers must cease permitting rebooking of cancelled foreign exchange derivative contracts.
- ADs must enforce the new USD 5 million threshold limit for unbacked foreign exchange derivative positions.
- ADs must obtain and retain proper undertakings regarding underlying exposures from users and preserve records for a minimum of two years.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 10, 2026
- **Effective Date:** Immediate effect

## Impact Assessment

- **Market Impact:** High impact on corporate hedgers, traders, and Authorized Dealers. Lowering the unbacked position limit to USD 5 million significantly restricts speculative or large unbacked hedging positions on stock exchanges and ADs. Prohibition on rebooking increases hedging costs and operational rigor for managing foreign exchange risk.
