## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has issued the Small Finance Banks – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy Sixth Amendment Directions, 2026, amending paragraph 75(5)(i) regarding risk weights for clearing member banks' trade exposures to Qualified Central Counterparties (QCCPs) to align with international standards.

## Key Points

- Applies a 2 per cent risk weight to a bank's trade exposure to a QCCP when acting as a clearing member for its own purposes in OTC derivatives, exchange-traded derivatives, and SFTs.
- Extends the 2 per cent risk weight to clearing services offered to clients where the clearing member bank is obligated to reimburse client losses in the event of QCCP default.
- Comes into effect immediately from the date of issue (October 7, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

- Replaces sub-paragraph 75(5)(i)(a) of the 2025 Master Directions on Capital Adequacy for Small Finance Banks.
- Clarifies risk weight treatment for QCCP exposures for both proprietary and client clearing services involving guarantees/reimbursement obligations.

## Compliance Requirements

- Small finance banks acting as clearing members must update their capital adequacy computation models and risk-weighted assets calculations for QCCP exposures according to the revised 2 per cent risk weight criteria.

## Important Dates

- Date of Issue / Effective Date: October 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Provides regulatory clarity and ensures alignment with international standards for small finance banks operating as clearing members, standardizing risk weight calculations for QCCP exposures.
