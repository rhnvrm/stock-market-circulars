## Summary

RBI has issued circular RBI/2026-27/290 announcing an increase in the minimum daily Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) maintenance requirement under Section 42(1) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934. Effective from the reporting fortnight beginning October 16, 2026, scheduled banks must maintain at least 99% of the required CRR on all days.

## Key Points

- Minimum daily CRR maintenance requirement increased from 90% to 99% of the required CRR.
- Applicable to all scheduled banks in India.
- Effective from the reporting fortnight beginning October 16, 2026.

## Regulatory Changes

- Revision of daily minimum cash reserve maintenance norms under Section 42(1) of the RBI Act, 1934.
- Tightening of daily liquidity compliance by raising the daily lower threshold from 90 percent to 99 percent of the requirement.

## Compliance Requirements

- All scheduled banks must ensure that their daily maintained CRR is not less than 99% of the prescribed CRR on every single day during the reporting fortnight, starting from October 16, 2026.

## Important Dates

- Circular Date: October 09, 2026
- Effective Date: Fortnight beginning October 16, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Increased operational focus and precision required for daily liquidity management and cash reserve deployment by scheduled banks.
