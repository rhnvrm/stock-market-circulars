## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) announced the results of the overnight Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction held on October 21, 2026. A total notified amount of ₹50,000 crore was offered, receiving robust participation with total bids amounting to ₹93,241 crore. The RBI allotted ₹50,007 crore at a cut-off rate and weighted average rate of 5.52%.

## Key Points

- **Tenor:** 1-day
- **Notified Amount:** ₹50,000 crore
- **Total Bids Received:** ₹93,241 crore
- **Amount Allotted:** ₹50,007 crore
- **Cut-off Rate:** 5.52%
- **Weighted Average Rate:** 5.52%
- **Partial Allotment Percentage:** 87.99% for bids received at the cut-off rate
- **Press Release:** 2026-2027/1357

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes; this is a routine liquidity injection operation conducted under the RBI's liquidity management framework.

## Compliance Requirements

No specific compliance actions required by market participants beyond standard settlement obligations for successful bids.

## Important Dates

- **Auction Date:** October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

The auction successfully injected liquidity into the banking system to manage short-term cash flow mismatches, with strong demand indicating adequate participation by market participants at the 5.52% rate.
