## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the auction results for Government Stocks, specifically the 6.53% Government Stock 2031 and 7.71% Government Stock 2066. The auction determined cut-off prices, implicit yields, and total accepted amounts.

## Key Points

- **6.53% GS 2031**: Notified amount of ₹19,000 crore; Cut-off price of ₹96.65 with an implicit yield of 7.3589%; Full amount accepted (₹19,000 crore); Nil devolvement on primary dealers.
- **7.71% GS 2066**: Notified amount of ₹14,000 crore; Cut-off price of ₹97.30 with an implicit yield of 7.9339%; Full amount accepted (₹14,000 crore); Nil devolvement on primary dealers.
