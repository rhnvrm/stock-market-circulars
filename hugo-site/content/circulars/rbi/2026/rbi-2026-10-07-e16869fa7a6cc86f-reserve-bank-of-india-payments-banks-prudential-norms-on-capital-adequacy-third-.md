## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued the Reserve Bank of India (Payments Banks – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Third Amendment Directions, 2026, updating the regulatory framework for payments banks acting as clearing members of Qualified Central Counterparties (QCCPs) to align with international standards.

## Key Points

- Replaces sub-paragraph 52(5)(i)(a) of the Reserve Bank of India (Payments Banks - Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Directions, 2025.
- Establishes a 2 per cent risk weight on a bank's trade exposure to a QCCP for its own purposes in respect of OTC derivatives, exchange-traded derivatives, and SFTs.
- Extends the 2 per cent risk weight to client clearing services where the clearing member bank is obligated to reimburse client losses upon QCCP default.
- Comes into effect immediately from the date of issue (October 7, 2026).

## Regulatory Changes

Paragraph 52(5)(i)(a) is updated to clearly delineate that a 2 per cent risk weight applies to a bank's trade exposure to a QCCP for proprietary transactions (OTC derivatives, exchange-traded derivatives, and SFTs) as well as client clearing obligations where the bank provides loss reimbursement guarantees in case of QCCP default.

## Compliance Requirements

Payments banks operating as clearing members of QCCPs must review and adjust their capital adequacy calculation models and risk-weighted asset computations to incorporate the revised 2 per cent risk weight specifications.

## Important Dates

- Effective Date: October 7, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Provides regulatory clarity and consistency for payments banks interacting with QCCPs, ensuring capital adequacy requirements accurately reflect risk exposures in alignment with global benchmarks.
