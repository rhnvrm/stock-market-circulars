## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has announced the conduct of an overnight Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction under the Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) for a notified amount of ₹50,000 crore on October 22, 2026, in view of current and evolving liquidity conditions.

## Key Points

- Auction Type: Variable Rate Repo (VRR) (1-day tenor)
- Notified Amount: ₹50,000 crore
- Window Timing: 09:30 AM to 10:00 AM on October 22, 2026
- Date of Reversal: October 23, 2026 (Friday)
- Operational Guidelines: As per RBI Press Release 2021-2022/1572 dated January 20, 2022.

## Regulatory Changes

No regulatory changes introduced; this is a tactical liquidity management operation.

## Compliance Requirements

Eligible counterparties participating in the LAF auction must adhere to standard bidding protocols and timings.

## Important Dates

- Auction Date: October 22, 2026
- Reversal Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Helps absorb or inject liquidity as needed, smoothing out short-term rate volatility in the interbank market.
