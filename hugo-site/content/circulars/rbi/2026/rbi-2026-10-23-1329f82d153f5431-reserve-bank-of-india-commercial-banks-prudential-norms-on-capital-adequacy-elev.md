## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued the Commercial Banks – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy Eleventh Amendment Directions, 2026, amending Chapter VII (Leverage Ratio framework) of the 2025 Directions to align with the latest Basel Committee on Banking Supervision 'leverage ratio 2017 standard'.

## Key Points

- Updates the Basel III leverage ratio definition (capital measure numerator divided by exposure measure denominator).
- Sets the minimum leverage ratio at 4 per cent for domestic systemically important banks (D-SIBs).
- Sets the minimum leverage ratio at 3.5 per cent for other commercial banks.
- Mandates G-SIB branches in India to maintain a leverage ratio of 3.5 per cent plus their specific leverage ratio buffer requirement.
- Specifies that the scope of application of the leverage ratio shall be on a consolidated group level basis.

## Regulatory Changes

- Chapter VII ('Leverage Ratio framework') of the Reserve Bank of India (Commercial Banks – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Directions, 2025 has been substituted/amended via Paragraph 262 to incorporate updated Basel III standards.

## Compliance Requirements

- Commercial banks, D-SIBs, and G-SIB branches operating in India must ensure compliance with the revised minimum leverage ratios and consolidated calculation methodologies as stipulated under the amended framework.

## Important Dates

- Issuance Date: October 23, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Enhances capital adequacy discipline and alignment with international regulatory standards for Indian commercial banks and systemically important financial institutions.
