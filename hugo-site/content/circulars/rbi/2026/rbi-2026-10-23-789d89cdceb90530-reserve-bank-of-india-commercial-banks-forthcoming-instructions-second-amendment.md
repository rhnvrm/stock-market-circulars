## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has issued the Commercial Banks – Forthcoming Instructions Second Amendment Directions, 2026, introducing key modifications to the minimum capital requirements for operational risk. These amendments refine the Business Indicator (BI) constituents, financial statement schedule mappings, Internal Loss Multiplier (ILM) calculations, and the valuation methodology for operational losses arising from damaged or destroyed assets.

## Key Points

- Chapter IV of the directions concerning Minimum Capital Requirements for Operational Risk becomes effective on April 1, 2027.
- Revisions to Table 8 under Paragraph 29 clarify the components, descriptions, typical sub-items, and schedule mappings (Schedules 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 13, 14, 15, and 16) for Interest, Lease and Dividend Component (ILDC), Services Component (SC), and Financial Component (FC).
- Updated rules for the Loss Component (LC) permit banks lacking 10 years of loss data to utilize high-quality historical loss data spanning 5 years and above.
- New explicit guidelines detail the calculation of operational losses when bank assets are damaged or destroyed under scenarios involving no replacement/repair, full repair/replacement, or partial repair.

## Regulatory Changes

- Paragraph 29 and Table 8 modified to update inclusions/exclusions for operating leases, rental incomes/expenses, and derivative asset fair valuations.
- Paragraph 31 substituted to refine the Loss Component (LC) formula (15 times average annual operational risk losses net of recoveries).
- Paragraph 32 updated to formalize the transition rule allowing 5+ years of loss data for the ILM calculation when 10 years are unavailable.
- Insertion of detailed explanations under Paragraph 39 specifying exact operational loss valuation formulas for damaged or destroyed assets.

## Compliance Requirements

- Commercial banks must ensure that their measurement, classification, and presentation of items used for computing Operational Risk Capital (ORC) strictly adhere to these revised Directions and Table 8 definitions.
- Banks must align their internal operational risk loss data collection and asset damage accounting frameworks ahead of the April 1, 2027 implementation date.

## Important Dates

- **Circular Date:** October 23, 2026
- **Effective Date:** April 1, 2027 (Chapter IV on Minimum Capital Requirements for Operational Risk)

## Impact Assessment

The amendments significantly impact commercial banks' capital adequacy computations, regulatory reporting structures, and internal risk management systems, requiring operational adjustments to align with revised business indicator mappings and loss data collection standards.
