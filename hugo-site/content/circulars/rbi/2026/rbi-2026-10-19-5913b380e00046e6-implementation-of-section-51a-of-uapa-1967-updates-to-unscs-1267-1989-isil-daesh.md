## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has issued a circular informing regulated entities about updates to the UNSC's 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List. Pursuant to Section 51A of the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967, and Ministry of External Affairs communications regarding UNSC press release SC/16480 dated October 15, 2026, amendments have been made to three entries (two individuals and one entity).

## Key Points

- Updates enacted for three entries on the UNSC ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida Sanctions List (Individuals: Mounir El Moutassadeq, Noor Wali Mehsud; Entity: Tehrik-e Taliban Pakistan).
- Regulated entities must verify that no accounts are maintained in the name of listed individuals or entities.
- Mandates adherence to Chapter IX of the RBI - Know Your Customer (KYC) Directions, 2025 and the UAPA Order dated February 02, 2021 (amended April 22, 2024).
- De-listing requests received by banks must be forwarded electronically to Joint Secretary (CTCR), MHA.

## Regulatory Changes

Incorporation of amendments specified in UNSC press release SC/16480 dated October 15, 2026, updating details, addresses, and associations for listed individuals and entities under the UN sanctions framework.

## Compliance Requirements

- Screen existing and new customer databases against the updated UNSC Sanctions List.
- Ensure immediate asset freezes and compliance with restrictions for any matching accounts or entities.
- Follow procedural guidelines set forth in Chapter IX of RBI KYC Directions, 2025 and the UAPA Order.

## Important Dates

- UNSC Press Release Date: October 15, 2026
- Circular Issuance Date: October 19, 2026

## Impact Assessment

Regulated entities must update their transaction monitoring and name-screening systems with the revised UNSC sanction entries to ensure strict adherence to statutory AML/CFT obligations and mitigate regulatory risks.
