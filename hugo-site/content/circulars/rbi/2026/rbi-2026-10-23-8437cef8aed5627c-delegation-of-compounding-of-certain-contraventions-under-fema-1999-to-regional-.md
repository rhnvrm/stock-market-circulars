## Summary

To promote customer service and operational efficiency, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has delegated powers for compounding contraventions under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999—specifically pertaining to Overseas Investments, Borrowing and Lending, and Guarantees—from the Central Office to select Regional Offices.

## Key Points

- Powers to compound contraventions under specified Overseas Investment regulations and rules are delegated to designated Regional Offices (Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Kolkata, New Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai) based on the Unique Identification Number (UIN) prefix of the foreign entity, joint venture, or wholly owned subsidiary.
- Compounding applications with no UIN allotted must be submitted to the Cell for Effective implementation of FEMA (CEFA) at the Central Office in Mumbai.
- Powers to compound contraventions under Borrowing, Lending, and Guarantees regulations are delegated to Regional Offices having jurisdiction over the registered office or place of domicile of the Indian company/entity.
- Contraventions involving lending or guarantees undertaken towards a foreign entity where the applicant has made Overseas Direct Investment (ODI) follow the Overseas Investment submission rules.
- Compounding applications involving multiple categories of transactions governed by different sets of rules/regulations with powers delegated to different offices shall be submitted to CEFA, Central Office, Mumbai.

## Regulatory Changes

- Amendment of procedures outlined in Master Directions on Compounding of Contraventions under FEMA, 1999 dated April 22, 2025, and A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 17/2024-25.
- Decentralization of FEMA compounding authority for foreign borrowings, lending, guarantees, and overseas investments to regional levels.

## Compliance Requirements

- Authorised Dealer (AD) Category-I banks and Authorised Persons must inform their constituents about the revised submission procedures and jurisdiction guidelines.
- Applicants must submit compounding applications to the correct Regional Office based on UIN prefixes for overseas investments or registered office jurisdiction for borrowings, lending, and guarantees.

## Important Dates

- Effective immediately with the issuance of circular RBI/FED/2026-27/298 on October 23, 2026.

## Impact Assessment

- Reduces processing time and administrative overhead for entities seeking compounding of FEMA contraventions by enabling localized submissions at designated Regional Offices across India.
