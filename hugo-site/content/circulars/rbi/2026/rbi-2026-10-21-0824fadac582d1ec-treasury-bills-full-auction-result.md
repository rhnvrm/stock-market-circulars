## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India has announced the full auction results for Treasury Bills across 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day tenors under Press Release 2026-2027/1360.

## Key Points

- **91-Day Treasury Bills**: Notified amount of ₹8,000 crore. Competitive bids received: 67 amounting to ₹15,366.600 crore. Non-competitive bids received: 10 amounting to ₹12,028.759 crore.
- **182-Day Treasury Bills**: Notified amount of ₹8,000 crore. Competitive bids received: 63 amounting to ₹18,925.500 crore. Non-competitive bids received: 5 amounting to ₹1,712.416 crore.
- **364-Day Treasury Bills**: Notified amount of ₹7,000 crore. Competitive bids received: 75 amounting to ₹11,352.500 crore. Non-competitive bids received: 4 amounting to ₹1,683.571 crore.

None. This is a routine periodic auction result notification.
