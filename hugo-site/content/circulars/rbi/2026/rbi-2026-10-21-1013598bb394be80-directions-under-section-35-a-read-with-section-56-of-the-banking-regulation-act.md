## Summary

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued specific directions to Puducherry Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, effective from the close of business on October 21, 2026. These measures restrict the bank from granting loans, making investments, incurring liabilities, or disbursing payments without prior written approval.

## Key Points

- Effective from the close of business on October 21, 2026, the bank cannot grant or renew loans, make investments, incur liabilities, or accept fresh deposits without prior RBI approval.
- Depositors are permitted to withdraw an amount not exceeding ₹5,000/- across all accounts, subject to certain set-off conditions against loans.
- Essential expenditures such as employee salaries, rent, and electricity bills are permitted.
- Eligible depositors can claim deposit insurance up to ₹5,00,000/- through DICGC as per applicable provisions.
- These directions remain in force for a period of six months and are subject to review.

## Regulatory Changes

- Supervisory directions imposed under Section 35A read with Section 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 due to lack of concrete efforts by the bank to address supervisory concerns.

## Compliance Requirements

- The bank must display a copy of the RBI directions on its website and premises for public perusal.
- Depositors seeking deposit insurance claims must submit their willingness and complete verification formalities through DICGC.

## Important Dates

- **Directive Date:** October 19, 2026
- **Effective Date / Restrictions Begin:** Close of business on October 21, 2026
- **Validity Period:** 6 months from October 21, 2026

## Impact Assessment

- Operations of Puducherry Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd are severely restricted, impacting customer withdrawals and credit disbursement while safeguarding depositor interests through DICGC coverage.
